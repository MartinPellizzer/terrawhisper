Actions

Browse All Actions

antioxidant

Antioxidant herbs protect cells from damage by neutralizing harmful free radicals.

anti-inflammatory

An anti-inflammatory medicinal herb works to reduce swelling, redness, and pain by suppressing the body's inflammatory responses.

antimicrobial

Antimicrobial refers to the ability of medicinal herbs to inhibit the growth of or destroy disease-causing microorganisms such as bacteria, viruses, fungi, and parasites.

antibacterial

Antibacterial herbs function by inhibiting the growth of or directly destroying harmful bacteria.

anticancer

Anticancer medicinal herbs function by inhibiting tumor growth, inducing apoptosis in malignant cells, or preventing the formation and spread of cancer.

cytotoxic

Cytotoxic refers to the ability of medicinal herbs to induce cell death or inhibit the growth of harmful cells, such as cancer cells.

antifungal

Antifungal medicinal herbs possess the ability to inhibit the growth of or destroy pathogenic fungi.

antiviral

Antiviral medicinal herbs contain bioactive compounds that inhibit the ability of viruses to infect, replicate, or spread within a host.
