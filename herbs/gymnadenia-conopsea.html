Gymnadenia conopsea

Marsh fragrant orchid

Gymnadenia conopsea, commonly known as the Broad-leaved Fragrant Orchid, is a rare and delicate woodland species. While primarily admired for its exquisite, nectar-rich blooms, it holds significant ecological value. Understanding its medicinal potential and conservation status is essential for preserving the biodiversity of its specialized, ancient forest habitats.

  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Europe
