Hymenodictyon flaccidum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the order Gentianaales and the family Rubiaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Hymenodictyon, and its unique species designation, flaccidum.
