Isatis

Isaatis, commonly known as Tansy, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine for its potent aromatic properties. Renowned for its bright yellow, button-like flowers, this plant has historically been used to treat digestive issues and respiratory ailments, though its high thujone content requires careful and expert handling.

  • Scientific name: Isatis
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales. Finally, it resides in the family Brassicaceae and is identified by the genus Isatis.
