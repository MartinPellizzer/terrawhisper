Cymbopogon khasianus

Cymbopogon khasianus ex

Cymbopogon khasianus, commonly known as Khasi lemongrass, is a fragrant perennial grass native to the northeastern regions of India. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this medicinal herb is widely used in traditional practices to treat digestive issues, skin ailments, and respiratory infections, offering potent antimicrobial and antioxidant benefits.

  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
