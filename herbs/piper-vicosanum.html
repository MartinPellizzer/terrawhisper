Piper vicosanum

Piper vicosanum, commonly known as a species of pepper, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse phytochemical properties. Found in tropical regions, it contains bioactive compounds with potential antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory effects. This plant serves as a significant subject in ethnobotanical research for developing natural therapeutic agents.
