Lepidium latifolium

Broadleaved pepperweed

Lepidium latifolium, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb, belongs to the Brassicaceae family. Widely distributed across various ecological zones, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties. It serves as a vital resource for exploring therapeutic applications, particularly in addressing diverse health conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Lepidium latifolium
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lepidium, specifically as the species Lepidium latifolium.
