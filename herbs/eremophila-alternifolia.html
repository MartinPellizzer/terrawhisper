Eremophila alternifolia

Narrow-leaved poverty bush

Eremophila alternifolia, commonly known as the desert bush or alternative-leaved eremophil, is a resilient shrub native to arid Australian landscapes. Valued in traditional medicine, its unique leaves possess significant bioactive properties. This hardy species serves as a vital botanical resource, offering potential therapeutic benefits through its diverse phytochemical composition.

  • Scientific name: Eremophila alternifolia
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Australasia
