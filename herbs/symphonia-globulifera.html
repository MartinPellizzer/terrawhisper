Symphonia globulifera

Symphonia globulifera, commonly known as the globuli tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical rainforests. Valued in traditional medicine, its bark and leaves possess potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds for their potential in treating various ailments and developing new therapeutics.

  • Scientific name: Symphonia globulifera
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic progression places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. It is a member of the Clusiaceae family and is specifically identified by the genus Symphonia.

This plant is primarily distributed across the landscape of West Tropical Africa. Within this region, its presence is documented in the country of Ghana.
