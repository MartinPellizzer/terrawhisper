Euphorbia sororia

Euphorbia sororia, commonly known as serpentine spurge, is a perennial herb native to North American limestone and serpentine soils. Characterized by its milky sap and distinctive floral structures, this plant plays a vital role in its specific ecosystems, though its caustic latex requires careful handling during medicinal or botanical study.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia sororia
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Malpighiales and belongs to the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Euphorbia.
