Ipomoea pes-caprae

Beach morning glory/goat's foot

Ipomoea pes-caprae, commonly known as Beach Morning Glory, is a resilient creeping vine found along tropical coastlines. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties. It is widely utilized for treating skin inflammations, wounds, and gastrointestinal issues, offering a natural therapeutic approach to various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea pes-caprae
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Ipomoea.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Ipomoea pes-caprae, is a creeping perennial herb where the whole plant is often utilized for its various therapeutic properties. Its sprawling stems act as runners that creep along the sand, allowing the vegetation to spread rapidly across coastal terrains. The lush, fleshy leaves are distinctively shaped like a goat's foot and serve as a primary source of bioactive compounds. Periodically, the plant produces vibrant, trumpet-shaped flowers that add aesthetic beauty to the shoreline. Because of its diverse chemical profile, the entire plant is frequently employed in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues.
