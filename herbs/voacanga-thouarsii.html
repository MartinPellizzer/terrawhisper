Voacanga thouarsii

Voacanaga thouarsii, commonly known as the Voacança, is a medicinal shrub native to the tropical rainforests of South America. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, this plant is traditionally utilized for its therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and psychoactive effects, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and modern medicine.
