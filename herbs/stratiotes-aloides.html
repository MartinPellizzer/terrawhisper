Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Stratiotes aloides, is a floating aquatic herb characterized by its unique structural adaptations. Unlike many terrestrial plants, it does not possess traditional roots for nutrient uptake from soil; instead, it utilizes fine, thread-like submerged structures that function similarly to roots to aid in stability and nutrient absorption from the water column. The most prominent part of the plant is its rosette of floating leaves, which are thick, fleshy, and often feature serrated margins that help the plant maintain its position on the water's surface.
