Stratiotes aloides

Stratiotes aloides, commonly known as water velvet, is a distinctive floating aquatic plant found in freshwater ecosystems. Characterized by its rosette of velvety, fringed leaves, it plays a vital role in providing habitat for aquatic organisms. While ecologically significant, it can become invasive, altering nutrient cycles and local biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Stratiotes aloides
  • Family: Hydrocharitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Alismatales. Finally, it is classified under the family Hydrocharitaceae within the genus Stratiotes.
