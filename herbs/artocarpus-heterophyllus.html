Artocarpus macrocrapon, scientifically known as Artocarpus heterophyllus, is the botanical name for the Jackfruit, a versatile tropical powerhouse. Renowned for its massive fruit, this plant offers significant medicinal value. Its leaves, bark, and fruit are traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory skin conditions.
