Polyalthia suberosa

Corky debbar tree

Polyalthia suberosa, commonly known as the "False Annona," is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This botanical specimen plays a significant role in promoting holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Polyalthia suberosa
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Polyalthia.
