What's the taxonomical classification of Teucrium bidentatum?

Teucrium bidentatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further classified under the order Lamiales. It is a member of the family Lamiaceae, which places it within the genus Teucrium, and it is specifically identified by the species name bidentaatum.
