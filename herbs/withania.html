Withania

Withania, commonly known as Ashwagandha, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine. Often referred to as "Indian Ginseng," this powerful adaptogen helps the body manage stress, boosts energy, and enhances cognitive function. Renowned for its rejuvenating properties, it supports immunity and vitality, making it a vital herb in holistic healing.
