Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Ashwagandha, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for the therapeutic properties found within its root. The root is the most significant part of the Withania somnifera plant, containing a high concentration of bioactive compounds such as withanolides. These medicinal components are typically harvested by drying the fleshy root system, which is then used to create various powders, extracts, and tinctures to promote relaxation and reduce stress.
