Cretan yarrow, scientifically known as Achillea cretica, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its delicate, feathery foliage and clusters of small flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various cultures for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory medicinal properties.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout Southeastern Europe and Western Asia. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Greece, specifically on the island of Kriti. Moving into Western Asia, the species is also present in Cyprus and across Türkiye. Additionally, its range extends to the East Aegean Islands.
