Strobocalyx arborea

Strobocalyx arborea, commonly known as a significant medicinal tree in tropical ecosystems, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is traditionally utilized in various healing practices. Understanding its phytochemical properties is essential for exploring its potential applications in modern pharmacology and natural therapeutics.

  • Scientific name: Strobocalyx arborea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Strobocalyx.
