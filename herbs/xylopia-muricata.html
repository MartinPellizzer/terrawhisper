Xylopia muricata

Xylopia muriata, commonly known as the Wild Pepper tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues, infections, and inflammation, offering valuable bioactive compounds for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Xylopia muricata
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Xylopia and the specific species Xylopia muricata.
