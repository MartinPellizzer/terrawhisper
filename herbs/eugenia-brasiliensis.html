Brazil-cherry

Eugenia brasiliensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (240 studies) · ★★★★★

Brazil-cherry, scientifically known as Eugenia brasiliensis, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to South America. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich, edible fruits, it offers significant medicinal potential. This plant is traditionally valued for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and digestive benefits, making it a subject of interest in modern ethnopharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution spanning several distinct tropical regions across the globe. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its presence extends to the Western Indian Ocean, where it inhabits the Comoros archipelago. The species also reaches the North-Central Pacific in Hawaii and the Caribbean within Trinidad and Tobago. Finally, a significant portion of its range is located in Brazil, particularly throughout the Northeast region.
