Actaea vaginata

Actaea racemosa, commonly known as black cohosh, is a perennial herb native to North America. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it is primarily utilized to support hormonal balance and alleviate symptoms associated with menopause. This potent botanical serves as a cornerstone in natural wellness for managing various reproductive health concerns.

  • Family: Ranunculaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Actaea vaginata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta.
