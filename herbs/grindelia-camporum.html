Grindelia camporum

Grindelia camporum, commonly known as gomphrena or gumweed, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Americas. Renowned for its medicinal properties, particularly in treating respiratory ailments, this plant contains bioactive compounds with anti-inflammatory and expectorant qualities. It remains a significant botanical resource in traditional herbal medicine practices.

  • Scientific name: Grindelia camporum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America
