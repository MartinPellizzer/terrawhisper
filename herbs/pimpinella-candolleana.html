Pimpinella candolleana

What's the taxonomical classification of Pimpinella candolleana?

Pimpinella candolleana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Apiales and is a member of the family Apiaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Pimpinella, and its species designation, candolleana.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is endemic to the high-altitude regions of the Andes mountains in South America, specifically within the territories of Peru and Bolivia. It is typically found growing in the puna ecosystem at elevations ranging from 3,500 to 4,500 meters above sea level. These populations are restricted to specific alpine tundra habitats characterized by rocky soils and intense solar radiation. Because its range is so localized to these montane environments, the species is geographically isolated from other similar flora. Its distribution remains closely tied to the unique climatic conditions of the high Andes.

What parts of this plant are used medicinally?

This plant Pimpineilla candolleana utilizes its entire herb for medicinal purposes. The leaves and stems are frequently harvested to create decoctions or infusions for therapeutic use. Specific phytochemical compounds found throughout the vegetative structure contribute to its biological activities. Practitioners often focus on the aerial parts of the herb to extract active constituents. This holistic approach ensures that the diverse chemical profile of the plant is fully utilized in traditional remedies.

According to a study published by "Zhongguo Zhong yao za zhi = Zhongguo zhongyao zazhi = China journal of Chinese materia medica", the herb of Pimpinella candolleana was analyzed to investigate components of its volatile oil using SPME-GC-MS. A total of 65 compounds were identified within the volatile oil, which accounted for 92.17% of the total. The main constituents found in the essential oil included alpha-zingiberene at 24.82% and pregeijererene at 16.27%. Other identified components were beta-bisabolene at 4.82%, 2-isopropyl-5-methyl-9-methylene-bicyclo [4.4.0] dec-1-ene at 4.03%, and beta-sesquiphellandrene at 3.98%. Additionally, the oil contained trans-beta-farnesene at 3.68% and ar-curcumene at 3.54%.
