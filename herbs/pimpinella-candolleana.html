Pimpinella candolleana

Pimpinella candolleana, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, is a distinguished species within the Apiaceae family. Found primarily in specific high-altitude ecosystems, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Researchers are increasingly exploring its unique phytochemical properties, aiming to uncover its potential therapeutic benefits for modern pharmacological applications.
