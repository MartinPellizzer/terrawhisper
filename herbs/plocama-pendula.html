Distribution

This plant is a specialized species characterized by its very limited geographical range. It is found exclusively within the Macaronesian region, specifically native to the Canary Islands. Because its habitat is restricted to these volcanic archipelagos, it is considered an endemic treasure of the area. This narrow distribution means the species relies on the unique microclimates provided by the island ecosystems. Consequently, protecting these specific habitats is essential for the continued survival of the species.
