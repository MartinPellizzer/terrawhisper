Pilocarpus pennatifolius, scientifically known as Pilocarpus pennatifolius, is a medicinal shrub native to South America. Renowned for its alkaloid content, particularly pilocarpine, this plant plays a vital role in pharmacology. It is primarily utilized to treat glaucoma and manage various conditions related to excessive sweating and salivation.
