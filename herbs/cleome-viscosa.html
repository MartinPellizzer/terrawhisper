Asian spiderflower, scientifically known as Cleome viscosa, is a versatile annual herb widely distributed across tropical regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its unique floral structure and bioactive compounds make it a significant botanical resource.

This plant, Cleome viscosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Brassicales and the family Cleomaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Cleome.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Egypt. Its presence extends to Macaronesia, specifically inhabiting the islands of Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Within this tropical zone, it is documented in Benin, Burkina, and Gambia.
