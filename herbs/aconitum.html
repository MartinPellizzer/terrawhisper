wolf's bane

Aconitum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (101 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Wolf's bane, scientifically known as Aconitum, is a genus of striking flowering perennials renowned for their beauty and extreme toxicity. While historically utilized in traditional medicine for its potent alkaloids, its high level of poison requires immense caution, as even minor contact can lead to severe, life-threatening systemic effects.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Aconitum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the northern regions of Europe. It can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Denmark and Finland.
