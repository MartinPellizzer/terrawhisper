Wolf's bane, scientifically known as Aconitum, is a genus of striking flowering perennials renowned for their beauty and extreme toxicity. While historically utilized in traditional medicine for its potent alkaloids, its high level of poison requires immense caution, as even minor contact can lead to severe, life-threatening systemic effects.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Aconitum, is a diverse flowering plant that is highly valued in traditional medicine despite its extreme toxicity. The most pharmacologically significant parts of the species are the roots, which are often harvested for their potent alkaloids. Specifically, the plant develops fleshy, starch-rich tubers that grow beneath the soil surface. These tuberous roots serve as the primary storage organs for the plant's energy and chemical compounds, making them the central focus for medicinal extraction.
