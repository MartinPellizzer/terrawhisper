Dendrophthoe falcata

Dendrophthoe falcata, commonly known as the "Sandalwood Mistletoe," is a parasitic shrub deeply rooted in traditional medicine. Found across parts of Asia, it is highly valued for its potent bioactive compounds. This plant is frequently utilized in folk remedies to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin disorders, and infections.

  • Scientific name: Dendrophthoe falcata
  • Family: Loranthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
