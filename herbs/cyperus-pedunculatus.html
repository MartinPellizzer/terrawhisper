Cyperus pedunculatus

Cyperus pedunculatus, commonly known as the pedunculate sedge, is a perennial wetland plant belonging to the Cyperaceae family. Thriving in marshes and damp habitats, this species is distinguished by its unique flowering structure. It holds significant botanical interest and is studied for its potential ecological and medicinal properties.
