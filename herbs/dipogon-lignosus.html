Dipogon lignosus, is a climbing perennial herb belonging to the Allium family. Often referred to as Saltpeter plant, it is highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, this medicinal plant is primarily used to treat various ailments, including respiratory and digestive issues.
