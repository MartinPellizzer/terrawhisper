Drosera capensis

Drosera capensis, commonly known as the Cape Sundew, is a captivating carnivorous plant native to South Africa. Characterized by its delicate, glandular leaves, it utilizes sticky mucilage to ensnare insects. Beyond its botanical beauty, this species offers fascinating potential in ethnobotany and studies regarding unique bioactive plant compounds.

  • Scientific name: Drosera capensis
  • Family: Droseraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, Drosera capeensis is a member of the Droseraceae family within the genus Drosera.

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution across several distinct global regions. In its native habitat, it is found within the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa.
