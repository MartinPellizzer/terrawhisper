Thonningia sanguinea

Thonningia sanguinea, commonly known as the Bloodvine, is a climbing shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its vibrant red flowers, this medicinal plant is deeply rooted in traditional healing. It is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections, respiratory issues, and digestive disturbances through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Thonningia sanguinea
  • Family: Balanophoraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Santalales, it is further categorized into the family Balanophoraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Thonningiia.
