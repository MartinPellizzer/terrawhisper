Delphinium davisii

Delphinium davisii, commonly known as Davis's larkspur, is a rare perennial wildflower native to the mountainous regions of California. While primarily valued for its striking blue blossoms and ecological significance, members of the Delphinium genus contain potent alkaloids, requiring careful study regarding their potential medicinal and toxicological properties.
