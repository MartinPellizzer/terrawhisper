Saxifraga

Saxifraga, commonly known as saxifrage, is a diverse genus of flowering plants thriving in rocky, alpine environments. Renowned for their resilience, these perennials often feature striking rosette patterns and delicate blooms. While primarily ornamental, certain species hold traditional medicinal value, traditionally used to support kidney health and bladder function.

  • Scientific name: Saxifraga
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Saxifraga.
