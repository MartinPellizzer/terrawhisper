What's the taxonomical classification of Cimicifuga simplex?

Cimicifuga simplex belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated under the order Ranunculales. It is further classified within the family Ranunculaceae, specifically falling under the genus Cimicifuga, with its unique identification as the species simplex.

According to a study published by "Bioorganic & medicinal chemistry", the roots of Cimicifuga simplex are used as a medicinal herb with a wide range of biological activities. Researchers isolated 7 9,19-cycloartenol glycosides from these roots. Among these, G3 exhibited the strongest inhibitory effect on immune responses, such as suppressing the differentiation of CD4+ T cells. In an IL-23-induced mouse ear model of skin disease, G3 repressed disease development by inhibiting pro-inflammatory mediators. G3 also affects the maturation of DCs in vitro to increase the Treg phenotype and decrease the Th17 phenotype. This process may ameliorate chronic inflammatory skin diseases by suppressing the IL-17+RORγt+/IL-10+FoxP3+ ratio.
