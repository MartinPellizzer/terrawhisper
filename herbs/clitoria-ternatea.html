Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Clitoria ternatea, possesses several distinct botanical components that are utilized in various medicinal and culinary applications. The most striking feature is the vibrant blue flowers, which are often used to create herbal teas due to their antioxidant properties. Within these blooms, the reproductive cycle is completed through the production of small seeds, which must be handled with care as they can be toxic if consumed raw. The plant also features elegant, pinnate leaf structures that contribute to its overall photosynthetic health. Whether examining the delicate flower in its budding stage or the fully matured, deep-colored Flower in full bloom, each part of this legume contributes to its unique identity in traditional medicine.
