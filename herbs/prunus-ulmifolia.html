Prunus ulmifolia

Prunus ulmifolia, commonly known as the elm-leaf cherry, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its distinctive foliage, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this species is frequently studied for its ability to support immune health and reduce systemic inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Prunus ulmifolia
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling into the family Rosaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Prunus.
