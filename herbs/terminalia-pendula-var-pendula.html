Terminalia pendula var. pendula

Terminalia pendula var. pendula, commonly known as the Weeping Terminalia, is a majestic evergreen tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its elegant, drooping branches and dense canopy, it serves both aesthetic and medicinal purposes. Its various parts are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat diverse ailments effectively.

  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
