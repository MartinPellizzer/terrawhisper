Giant hogweed

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Heracleum mantegazzianum, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Iceland, it is called Tröllahvönn, while in Slovakia, it is known as boľševník obrovský. In the Walloon language, it is identified as Paxhnåde do Cocaze, and in Poland, it is termed Barszcz Mantegazziego. Additionally, in Bosnia and Herzegovina, it is commonly called Gigantski svinjski korov.
