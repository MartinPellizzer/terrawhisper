Giant hogweed, scientifically known as Heracleum mantegazzianum, is a formidable perennial known for its massive size and potent phototoxicity. While historically studied for various bioactive properties, it is primarily recognized as a dangerous invasive species. Its sap can cause severe skin burns and long-term scarring upon sun exposure.
