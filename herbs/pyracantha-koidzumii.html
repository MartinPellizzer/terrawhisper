What's the taxonomical classification of Pyracantha koidzumii?

Pyracantha koidzumii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pyracantha and is specifically designated as the species koidzumii.
