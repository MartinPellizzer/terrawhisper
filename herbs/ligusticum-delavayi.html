Ligusticum delavayi

What's the taxonomical classification of Ligusticum delavayi?

Ligusticum delavayi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its hierarchical progression, the plant is placed in the order Apiales and is a member of the family Apiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Ligusticum, resulting in its unique scientific name, Ligusticum delavayi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ligusticum delavayi has a perennial herbaceous structure characterized by hollow, ribbed stems that can reach heights of up to two meters and large, compound leaves with serrated leaflets. During its flowering period, the plant produces numerous small, white flowers clustered in dense, flat-topped umbels.
