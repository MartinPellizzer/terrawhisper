Sophora denudata

Sophora denudaata, commonly known as Ku Shen, is a potent medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, this legume contains bioactive alkaloids used to treat skin conditions, inflammation, and parasitic infections. Its therapeutic properties make it a vital component in various topical and internal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Sophora denudata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Sophora.
