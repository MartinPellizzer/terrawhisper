Sophora denudata, scientifically known as Sophora denudata, is a traditional medicinal plant highly valued in East Asian medicine. Rich in matrine and oxymatrine alkaloids, it is widely utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antiviral, and anti-tumor properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research and holistic healing practices.
