Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Nigella damascena or Love-in-a-mist, utilizes its seeds as the primary component for medicinal and culinary applications. These small, dark, and angular seeds contain concentrated essential oils that possess various therapeutic properties, often utilized in traditional practices for their potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. While the plant is celebrated for its delicate blue flowers, it is the potent chemical composition found within the seeds that holds the most significant interest for those seeking its medicinal benefits.
