Alectra parasitica subsp. chitrakutensis

Alectra parasitica subsp. chitrakutenis, commonly known as Chitrak, is a unique parasitic medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, it is highly valued for its potent bioactive compounds. This subspecies is primarily utilized to enhance digestive health, stimulate appetite, and support metabolic detoxification processes.

  • Scientific name: Alectra parasitica subsp. chitrakutensis
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Orobanchaceae and the genus Alectra. Specifically, this organism is identified as the subspecies chitrakutensis of the species Alectra parasiticus.
