Biophytum sensitivum

Biophytum sensitivum, commonly known as the sensitive plant or "touch-me-not," is a unique medicinal herb prized for its rapid leaf movement. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties, offering therapeutic potential in treating skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Biophytum sensitivum
  • Family: Oxalidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Biophytum sensitivum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Oxalidales. Finally, it is a member of the family Oxalidaceae and is situated within the genus Biophytum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Its presence extends to the island of Taiwan, which is part of the broader Eastern Asia region. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the state of Assam. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various subtropical environments.
