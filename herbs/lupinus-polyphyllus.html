Lupinus polyphyllus

Lupinus polyphyllus, commonly known as the Large-leaf Lupine, is a striking perennial herb native to western North America. Renowned for its tall, majestic spikes of blue or purple flowers, this plant plays a vital role in nitrogen fixation, enhancing soil fertility and supporting diverse ecosystems through its unique biology.

  • Scientific name: Lupinus polyphyllus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it falls under the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Lupinus, with its species designation being polyphyllus.
