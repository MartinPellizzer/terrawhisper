Hypoxis hemerocallidea

Hypoxis hemecalcoidea, commonly known as African Potato, is a medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to boost immunity and treat various ailments, including inflammation and cancer. This remarkable botanical species continues to undergo significant scientific research for its therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Hypoxis hemerocallidea
  • Family: Hypoxidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Hypoxis hemerocallidea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales. It is further categorized into the family Hypoxidaceae and the genus Hypoxis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several regions in the southern portion of the continent. In South Tropical Africa, its habitat extends through the territories of Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving into the Southern Africa region, it can be found growing within the borders of Botswana. The species is also documented throughout the Cape Provinces. Additionally, its geographical range encompasses the mountainous terrain of Lesotho.
