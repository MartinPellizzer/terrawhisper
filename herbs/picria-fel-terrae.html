Picria fel-terrae

Picria fel-terrae, commonly known as Picric acid or bitterwood derivative, is a potent botanical compound historically utilized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its intense bitterness, it serves as a powerful tonic, digestive stimulant, and antimicrobial agent, playing a significant role in various pharmacological applications and historical herbal remedies.

  • Family: Linderniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
