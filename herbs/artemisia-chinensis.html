Artemisia chinensis

Artemisia chinensis, commonly known as Chinese wormwood, is a versatile perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, this aromatic plant contains bioactive compounds with potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. It is frequently utilized to support digestive health and treat various skin conditions through herbal applications.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia chinensis
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several major regions. In Eastern Asia, it can be found growing in Japan and the Kazan-retto area. Its presence extends into China, specifically within the South-Central and Southeast regions. Additionally, the species is distributed within the Western Indian Ocean, notably in Mauritius. These various locations highlight the widespread natural habitat of Artemisia chinensis.
