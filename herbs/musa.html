Banana genus, scientifically known as Musa, is a diverse group of herbaceous flowering plants celebrated for their nutritional and medicinal properties. Beyond providing essential fruits, various Musa species are utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, skin inflammations, and wounds, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as banana, offers various medicinal benefits through its different components. The peel is often used topically to soothe insect bites and reduce skin inflammation, while the leaf extracts are frequently utilized in traditional medicine for their antioxidant properties and ability to aid in wound healing. The fruits themselves are a vital source of essential vitamins and minerals that support digestive health, and the soft, nutritious flesh provides immediate energy and helps maintain electrolyte balance in the body.
