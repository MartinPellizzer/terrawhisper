Banana genus, scientifically known as Musa, is a diverse group of herbaceous flowering plants celebrated for their nutritional and medicinal properties. Beyond providing essential fruits, various Musa species are utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, skin inflammations, and wounds, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Spain. It can also be found in the Macaronesia region, particularly within the Canary Islands. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed across West Tropical Africa. This African range includes specific locations such as Burkina, Gambia, and Guinea-Bissau.
