Musa

Musa, commonly known as banana or plantain, is a versatile tropical plant valued for both nutrition and medicine. Beyond its edible fruit, various parts of the Musa plant, including leaves, stems, and peels, possess significant therapeutic properties, offering anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and digestive benefits in traditional healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Musa
  • Family: Musaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales and the family Musaceae, it is classified under the genus Musa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Spain. It can also be found in the Macaronesia region, particularly within the Canary Islands. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed across West Tropical Africa. This African range includes specific locations such as Burkina, Gambia, and Guinea-Bissau.
