What's the taxonomical classification of Bombax buonopozense?

Bombax buonopozense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is part of the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized under the genus Bombax, with its specific designation being the species buonopozense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Bombax buonopozense has a large deciduous tree habit characterized by a thick trunk often covered in conical prickles and spreading branches that terminate in large, fleshy, cup-shaped flowers. The leaves are imparipinnate with several pairs of leaflets that possess an elliptical shape and smooth margins. Its flowers are typically large, solitary, and colored in shades of red or pink, emerging prominently from the woody stems. The fruit develops into a large, woody capsule that eventually splits to release numerous seeds embedded in a soft, fibrous pulp.
