Mentha × villosa

Mentha villosa

Mentha × villosa, commonly known as hairy mint, is a versatile perennial herb prized for its aromatic properties. Characterized by its soft, velvety foliage, this hybrid offers a refreshing scent used in traditional remedies. It is valued in herbal medicine for supporting digestive health and providing soothing, calming effects.

  • Scientific name: Mentha × villosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, Mentha × villosa is identified under the genus Mentha.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented within the territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the central part of the continent, the species can also be found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of Austria and Belgium. The concentration of this essential oil within the plant's foliage makes it the most significant component for applications in aromatherapy and herbal remedies.
