Ruscus

Ruscus, commonly known as Butcher's-broom, is a versatile genus of evergreen shrubs valued for their medicinal properties. Renowned for treating venous insufficiency and hemorrhoids, its extracts help improve circulation and reduce swelling. This resilient plant has long been utilized in traditional herbal medicine to support vascular health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Ruscus
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, falling within the family Asparagaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is placed in the genus Ruscus.
