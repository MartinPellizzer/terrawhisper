Butcher's-broom, scientifically known as Ruscus, is a genus of evergreen shrubs prized in herbal medicine for centuries. Renowned for their unique, leaf-like stems called cladodes, these resilient plants offer potent therapeutic benefits. They are primarily utilized to support venous circulation, alleviate leg heaviness, and reduce swelling effectively.
