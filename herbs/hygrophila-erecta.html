Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its distribution spans the South-Central areas and the Southeast provinces. It is also documented as being native to the tropical island of Hainan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found specifically in the state of Assam.
