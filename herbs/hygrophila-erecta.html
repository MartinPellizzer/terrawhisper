Hygrophila erecta

Hygrophila erecta, commonly known as the upright water plant, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical wetlands. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, reduce inflammation, and support digestive health. This resilient aquatic species offers significant pharmacological potential for modern healing.

  • Scientific name: Hygrophila erecta
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Hygrophila.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Hygrophila erecta, is referred to by several common names depending on the linguistic context. In Chinese, it is called 小叶水蓑衣, a name that is consistently used across the different language codes including zh, zh-hans, and zh-cn.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its distribution spans the South-Central areas and the Southeast provinces. It is also documented as being native to the tropical island of Hainan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found specifically in the state of Assam. Additionally, the plant is widely distributed throughout Bangladesh.
