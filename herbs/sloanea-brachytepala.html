Sloanea brachytepala

Sloanea brachytepala, commonly known as a member of the Elaeocarpaceae family, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical forest ecosystems. Renowned for its unique botanical characteristics, this species holds immense potential in ethnopharmacology. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds for developing novel treatments in traditional medicine.
