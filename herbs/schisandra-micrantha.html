Schisandra micrantha

Schisandra micrantha, commonly known as the small-flowered magnolia, is a climbing shrub native to East Asian forests. Valued in traditional medicine, its berries and bark contain bioactive compounds used to support vitality, reduce fatigue, and enhance cognitive function, making it a significant botanical resource in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra micrantha
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Austrobaileyales, it is classified under the family Schisandraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Schisandra.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Schisandra micrantha, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across several Asian regions. In China, it is primarily found throughout the South-Central territories. Its distribution extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species is documented across the Indo-China region, particularly in Myanmar. These locations define the natural habitat where the medicinal plant can be sourced.
