Schisandra micrantha, scientifically known as Schisandra micrantha, is a perennial woody vine native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this plant is celebrated for its adaptogenic properties. It is primarily used to enhance vitality, reduce fatigue, and support cognitive function through its potent bioactive compounds.
