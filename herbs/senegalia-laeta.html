Senegalia laeta

Senegalia laeta, commonly known as the "smiling acacia" in some regions, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to arid African landscapes. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin infections, offering significant potential for modern therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Senegalia laeta
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is found within the genus Senegalia, specifically as the species Senegalia laeta.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Senegalia laeta, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Arabic, it is called سنط سعيد, while in Swedish, it is known as Acacia laeta. It is also identified as سنگالیا لائتا in Persian and שיטה רעננה in Hebrew.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is noted within the territories of Algeria and Egypt. Moving toward the western regions, it can be found throughout West Tropical Africa. Specifically, the species thrives in the countries of Burkina and Mali. Additionally, its range extends into the West Tropical African nation of Mauritania.
