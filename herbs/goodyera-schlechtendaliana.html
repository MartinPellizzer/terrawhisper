Goodyera schlechtendaliana

Goodyera schlechtendaliana, commonly known as the Spotted Orchid, is a captivating terrestrial orchid native to the tropical rainforests of Africa. Renowned for its distinctive foliage and delicate blooms, this plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem, offering unique insights into biodiversity and traditional botanical studies within its habitat.

  • Scientific name: Goodyera schlechtendaliana
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Goodyera schlechtendaliana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Orchidaceae and is placed within the genus Goodyera.
