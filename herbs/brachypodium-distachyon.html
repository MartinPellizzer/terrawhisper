Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Brachypodium, with the species name distachyon.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several regions of Southern and Southeastern Europe. It can be found throughout Southwestern Europe, specifically inhabiting the territories of France, Portugal, and Spain. Within the Spanish region, its presence extends to the Balearic Islands. Additionally, the species is recorded in the Balkan region, specifically in Albania. Consequently, its range spans a diverse Mediterranean landscape stretching from the Atlantic coast to the Southeast.
