Eremophila glabra

Eremophila glabra, commonly known as the desert broom or smooth eremophil, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid Australian landscapes. Renowned for its bioactive properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research into its unique therapeutic compounds.

  • Scientific name: Eremophila glabra
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Australasia
