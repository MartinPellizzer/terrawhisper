Helianthella quinquenervis

Helianthella quinquenervis, commonly known as the five-leaf helianthus, is a resilient perennial native to North American mountains. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism. It is often studied for its unique phytochemical properties and its role within diverse high-altitude medicinal ecosystems.

  • Family: Asteraceae
