Drymaria

Drymaria, commonly known as creeping chickweed, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient herb offers a natural approach to wellness, making it a significant botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Drymaria
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Caryophyllaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is the genus Drymaria.
