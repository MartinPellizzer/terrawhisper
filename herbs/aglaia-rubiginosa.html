Aglaia rubiginosa

Aglaia rubiginosa, commonly known as a member of the Meliaceae family, is a tropical medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic potential. Found in lush forest ecosystems, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant interest for modern pharmacological research and drug development.

  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
