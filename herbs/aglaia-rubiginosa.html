Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China zone, it can be found specifically in Thailand. Its presence extends significantly into the Malesia region, where it inhabits the island of Borneo. Furthermore, the species is recorded throughout the Lesser Sunda Islands and the Malay Peninsula. Finally, its range includes the large island of Sumatera, completing its diverse regional footprint.
