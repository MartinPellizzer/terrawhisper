Diplotaxis erucoides

Diplotaxis erucoides, commonly known as the mustard-like herb, is a distinctive medicinal plant found in specific arid regions. Valued in traditional practices, it possesses unique phytochemical properties. Research explores its potential therapeutic applications, particularly for its bioactive compounds, highlighting its importance in ethnobotany and natural pharmacology studies.

  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
