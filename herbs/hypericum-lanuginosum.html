Hypericum lanuginosum

Hypericum lanuginosum, commonly known as woolly St. John's wort, is a resilient perennial herb native to mountainous regions. Distinguished by its soft, hairy foliage and vibrant yellow blooms, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its soothing properties and potential therapeutic applications in treating various skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum lanuginosum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Hypericum.
