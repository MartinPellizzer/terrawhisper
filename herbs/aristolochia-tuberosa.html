Aristolochia tuberosa

Aristolochia tuberosa, commonly known as Wild Aristolochia, is a perennial herb native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds, it has historically been used to treat inflammation and digestive ailments. However, modern research highlights its significant toxicity, requiring cautious study regarding its medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia tuberosa
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Piperales and the family Aristolochiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic classification places it within the genus Aristolochia.
