Aglaia perviridis

Aglaia perviriidis, commonly known as a valuable medicinal plant in Southeast Asian traditional medicine, belongs to the Meliaceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is utilized for its bioactive compounds, which offer significant potential in treating various ailments, including inflammatory conditions and bacterial infections.

  • Scientific name: Aglaia perviridis
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is further categorized into the family Meliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Aglaia.
