Aloysiia trifida, scientifically known as Aloysia trifida, is a fragrant, shrubby plant native to South America, most famously recognized as the source of lemon verbena. Prized for its aromatic leaves, it offers diverse medicinal benefits, including digestive support, calming effects, and antioxidant properties used in traditional herbal remedies.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Lemon Verbena, is highly valued for its aromatic properties derived from its distinct botanical components. The most prominent feature used in medicinal and culinary applications is the leaves, which possess a bright, citrus-like scent. Contained within these foliage structures are the plant's potent essential oils, which serve as the primary source of its therapeutic fragrance and flavor. When processed, the extraction of this essential oil provides a concentrated essence used to soothe digestion and reduce stress.
