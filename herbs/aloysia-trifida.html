Aloysia trifida

Aloysiia trifida, commonly known as Lemon Verbena, is a fragrant perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic, citrus-scented leaves. Widely utilized in traditional medicine and culinary arts, it offers soothing properties for digestive health, anxiety relief, and inflammation. This versatile plant remains a staple in herbal teas and essential oils.

  • Scientific name: Aloysia trifida
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Aloysiia trifida, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Verbenaceae and the genus Aloysia.
