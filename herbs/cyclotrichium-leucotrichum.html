Cyclotrichium leucotrichum

Cyclotrichium leucotrichum, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, is highly valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Research highlights its bioactive compounds, which contribute to its diverse pharmacological applications, making it a subject of great botanical interest.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, specifically within the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cyclotrichium.
