The morphology of Combretum sundaicum is characterized by its growth habit as a woody liana, functioning as an obligatory climber. While it can exhibit shrub-like qualities in certain contexts, it is primarily distinguished by its climbing nature, reaching a significant height with a mean measurement of approximately 3 meters and a maximum potential height of up to 30 meters. As a terrestrial species, it does not function as an epiphyte, instead establishing itself firmly in the ground to support its climbing structure. The plant's development is defined by its woody texture, which provides the necessary structural integrity for its extensive liana growth form.
