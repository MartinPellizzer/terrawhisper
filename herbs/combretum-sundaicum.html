Combretum sundaicum

Combretum sundaicum, commonly known as the tropical climber, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is traditionally utilized in various ethnobotanical practices to treat inflammation and infections. Modern research continues to explore its significant therapeutic potential in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Combretum sundaicum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Combretaceae and is identified by the genus Combretum.
