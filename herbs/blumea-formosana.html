Blumea formosana

What's the taxonomical classification of Blumea formosana?

Blumea formosanana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae, placing it within the genus Blumea. Finally, the plant is identified at the species level as Blumea formosana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Blumea formosana has perennial herbaceous stems that support large, ovate to lanceolate leaves characterized by serrated margins and a distinctly pubescent, hairy texture. The foliage often exhibits a rugose surface with prominent venation that creates a textured appearance. Its inflorescence consists of small, yellowish flower heads arranged in dense, terminal clusters known as capitula. These flower heads are typically surrounded by specialized bracts that protect the developing florets. The overall structure of the plant is defined by its soft, felt-like coating of fine hairs covering both the leaves and stems.
