Glaucium flavum

Glaucium flavum, commonly known as Yellow Saw-wort, is a striking perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Distinguished by its fleshy, glaucous leaves and vibrant yellow composite flowers, this plant holds significant botanical interest. It is valued for its unique morphology and potential medicinal properties within traditional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Glaucium flavum
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Glaucium.
