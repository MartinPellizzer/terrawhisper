Yellow horned poppy, scientifically known as Glaucium flavum, is a striking perennial herb native to coastal regions. Characterized by its deeply lobed, blue-green foliage and brilliant yellow flowers, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine, though its potent alkaloids require careful handling and expert knowledge for therapeutic use.
