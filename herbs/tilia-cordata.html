Small-leaved lime, scientifically known as Tilia cordata, is a deciduous tree cherished for its fragrant, heart-shaped leaves and delicate blossoms. Historically used in traditional medicine, its flowers yield a soothing herbal tea renowned for its calming properties, effectively easing anxiety, promoting restful sleep, and reducing mild inflammatory symptoms.
