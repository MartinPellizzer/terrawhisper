Fritillaria delavayi

Fritillaria delaayi, commonly known as the Delavay's fritillary, is a rare medicinal plant native to the high-altitude regions of China. Renowned in traditional medicine, its bulbs contain bioactive alkaloids used to treat respiratory ailments and soothe coughs. This precious botanical species remains a significant subject of pharmacological research today.

  • Scientific name: Fritillaria delavayi
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Liliales, it is further nested within the family Liliaceae.
