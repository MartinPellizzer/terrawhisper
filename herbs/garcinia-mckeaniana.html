Garcinia mckeaniana

Garcinia mckeaniana, commonly known as the Jamaican mangosteen, is a tropical evergreen tree prized for its edible, tangy fruits. Beyond its culinary use, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Its leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds, offering potential medicinal properties used in traditional practices to treat various ailments.
