Ferula penninervis

Ferula penninervis, commonly known as Indian Asafoetida, is a perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, its resinous gum possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties. This aromatic plant is highly valued for treating respiratory ailments, gastrointestinal issues, and various inflammatory conditions throughout traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Ferula penninervis
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is situated in the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Ferula, under which the species Ferula penninervis is categorized.
