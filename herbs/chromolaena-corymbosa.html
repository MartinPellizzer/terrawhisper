This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Chromolaena.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across multiple regions in Asia and the Caribbean. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is commonly found throughout India and specifically in the state of Assam. Its presence is also documented in the mountainous terrain of the West Himalaya. Moving across the ocean to the Caribbean, the species can be found growing in the Bahamas. Additionally, it is distributed within the islands of Cuba.
