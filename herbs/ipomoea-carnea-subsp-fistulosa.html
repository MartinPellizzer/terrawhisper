Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Guinea and the Ivory Coast. The species also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Burundi. Furthermore, it is known to inhabit the territory of Cameroon within the same central region. Finally, its geographical reach includes Rwanda, completing its distribution across these tropical zones.
