This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Zingiberales and placed in the family Costaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Costus as Costus spiralis.

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions across the Caribbean and Northern South America. Within the Caribbean, its range includes the island of Cuba as well as the Venezuelan Antilles. Moving toward the mainland, the species is also found throughout Northern South America. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of French Guiana and Guyana.
