Calycophyllum spruceanum

Calycollyphum spruceanum, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical ecosystems, holds great promise in ethnobotanical research. Native to South American rainforests, this plant is valued for its diverse bioactive compounds. Current studies explore its potential pharmacological applications, particularly for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties.
