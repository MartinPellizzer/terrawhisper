Acacia implexa

Acacia implexa, commonly known as the Impleza Wattle, is a resilient shrub native to specific African ecosystems. Renowned for its hardiness, this medicinal plant is utilized in traditional healing practices to treat various ailments. Its unique chemical properties offer significant potential for pharmacological research and diverse therapeutic applications.

  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Acacia. Ultimately, its scientific designation is Acacia implexa.
