Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several distinct regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is widespread, spanning from the North-Central provinces to the South-Central areas. It can also be found growing within the tropical landscapes of Hainan. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout the Southeast region of China. Beyond mainland China, its habitat extends eastward to include the islands of Japan.

Plant Parts

This plant, Thelypteris acuminata, is a species of fern characterized by its specific morphological structures, most notably its underground stem known as the rhizome. The rhizome serves as the primary vegetative organ, functioning as a storage unit for nutrients and allowing the plant to spread and establish itself within its moist, shaded habitat. From this creeping or erect rhizome, the plant develops its characteristic fronds and roots, forming the foundational architecture necessary for its growth and survival in the wild.
