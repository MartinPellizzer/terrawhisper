Maesa japonica

Maesa japonica, commonly known as the Japanese cherry blossom, is a revered medicinal plant in traditional East Asian practices. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to reduce inflammation, support skin health, and promote overall wellness through its nutrient-rich blooms and restorative botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Maesa japonica
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Primulaceae and is placed in the genus Maesa.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Maesa japonica, is recognized by various common names across different regions and languages. In Chinese, it is referred to as 杜茎山 or 水光钟, while in Taiwan, the specific name 山桂花 is used. Additionally, in Japanese, it is commonly known as イズセンリョウ.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Eastern Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central and Southeast areas. Its range extends further into the East Asian territories of Japan and Korea. Additionally, the species is documented in the Nansei-shotō islands. These locations collectively define the natural geographical footprint of Maeisa japonica.
