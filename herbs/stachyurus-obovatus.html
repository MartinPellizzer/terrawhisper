What's the taxonomical classification of Stachyurus obovatus?

Stachyurus obovatus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Crossosomatales. This plant is a member of the family Stachyuraceae and is characterized by its placement in the genus Stachyurus, eventually reaching its specific identification as the species obovatus.
