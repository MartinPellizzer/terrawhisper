Gaultheria

Gaultheria, commonly known as wintergreen, is a versatile genus of flowering plants prized for their medicinal properties. Renowned for containing methyl salicylate, these plants offer potent analgesic and anti-inflammatory benefits. Frequently used in topical ointments and essential oils, Gaultheria serves as a natural remedy for soothing muscle aches.
