Rhodiola chrysanthemifolia subsp. sacra

Rhodiola chrysanthemifolia subsp. sacra, commonly known as a sacred variety of roseberry, is a potent adaptogen native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its ability to combat fatigue and enhance mental clarity, this medicinal plant offers profound restorative properties, supporting the body's resilience against physical and emotional stress.

  • Scientific name: Rhodiola chrysanthemifolia subsp. sacra
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifragales, it is a member of the family Crassulaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Rhodiola as the subspecies sacra.

Common Names

This plant, Rhodiola chrysanthemifolia subsp. sacra, is known by several distinct regional names depending on the language and cultural context. In English, it is commonly referred to as Sacred Rhodiola, while in French, it is known as Rhodiole sacrée. In Spanish, the plant is called Rhodiole sacrée, and in Arabic, it is identified as Rhodiola sacra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, its presence is primarily documented across the provinces of Qinghai and Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is found throughout the East Himalaya range. Its habitat also extends significantly into the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Consequently, its range is defined by these distinct alpine territories in both China and the Indian Subcontinent.
