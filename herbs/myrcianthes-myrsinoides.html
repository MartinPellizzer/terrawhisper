Myrcianthes myrsinoides

Myrcianthes myrsinoiides, commonly known as a valuable medicinal shrub, is native to the tropical regions of Southeast Asia. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various infections, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
