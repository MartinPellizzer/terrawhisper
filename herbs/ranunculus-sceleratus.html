Ranunculus sceleratus

Celery-leaf buttercup

Ranunculus sceleratus, commonly known as celery-leaved buttercup, is a toxic perennial herb native to various regions. Characterized by its distinctive deeply lobed foliage, this plant contains potent protoanemonin. While historically significant in traditional herbalism, its high toxicity requires extreme caution, as ingestion can cause severe irritation and injury.

  • Scientific name: Ranunculus sceleratus
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Ranunculus.
