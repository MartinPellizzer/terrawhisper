celery-leaf buttercup, scientifically known as Ranunculus sceleratus, is a perennial herb native to North America. Characterized by its deeply divided, celery-like foliage, this plant contains potent protoanemonin. While historically used in folk medicine, its high toxicity requires extreme caution, as contact can cause severe skin irritation and blistering.
