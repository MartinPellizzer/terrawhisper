Alibertia patinoi

Alibertia patinoi, commonly known as the "pau de rã," is a significant medicinal plant native to the Brazilian Atlantic Forest. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, infections, and skin ailments, offering promising potential for modern therapeutic drug development.

  • Scientific name: Alibertia patinoi
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Alibertia patinoi, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling into the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rubiaceae and is identified by the genus Alibertia.
