What's the taxonomical classification of Lonicera longiflora?

Lonicera longiflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It is further categorized under the family Caprifoliaceae, which places it within the genus Lonicera, and is specifically identified by the species epithet longiflora.
