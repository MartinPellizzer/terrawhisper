Abutilon theophrasti

Abutilon theophrasti, commonly known as velvetleaf, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Recognized by its soft, velvety foliage, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Often utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, this plant serves as a crucial botanical resource in various ethnopharmacological studies worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Abutilon theophrasti
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe
