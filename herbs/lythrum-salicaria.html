Lythrum salicaria

Lythrum salicaria, commonly known as purple loosestrife, is a striking perennial herb characterized by its vibrant spikes of magenta flowers. While prized for its ornamental beauty in wetlands, it is an aggressive invasive species. In traditional medicine, its astringent properties have been utilized to treat various digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Lythrum salicaria
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it is specifically positioned in the family Lythraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed by its designation in the genus Lythrum.
