Phytolacca americana var. americana

Phytolacca americana americana

Phytolacca americana var. americana, commonly known as American pokeweed, is a perennial herb valued in traditional ethnobotany. While containing potent compounds requiring careful preparation, its roots and berries have historically been utilized for their bioactive properties. This variety serves as a significant subject in studying natural pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Phytolacca americana var. americana
  • Family: Phytolaccaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Caryophyllales, it is situated in the family Phytolaccaceae. It is further identified by the genus Phytolacca, specifically as the variety americana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions within the European continent. In Northern Europe, its distribution includes both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the center of the continent, the species is also well-established in Middle Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Austria and Germany. Additionally, the plant's range extends through the territories of Czechia-Slovakia.
