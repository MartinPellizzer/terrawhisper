Phytolacca americana americana, scientifically known as Phytolacca americana var. americana, is a perennial herb native to the Americas. Renowned for its dark berries and distinctive roots, this plant has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries to treat various ailments, though its potent compounds require careful, expert handling.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions within the European continent. In Northern Europe, its distribution includes both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the center of the continent, the species is also well-established in Middle Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Austria and Germany. Additionally, the plant's range extends through the territories of Czechia-Slovakia.
