Distribution

This plant is native to several distinct regions across East and Southeast Asia. In mainland China, its presence is primarily noted throughout the South-Central and Southeast provinces. The species also extends its range into the island of Taiwan. Beyond the Chinese mainland, it can be found growing in Japan within Eastern Asia. Finally, its geographical reach includes the Indo-China region, specifically in Vietnam.
