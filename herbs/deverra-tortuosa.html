Deverra tortuosa

Deverra tortuosa, commonly known as the twisted deverra, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to the arid regions of North Africa. Renowned for its unique morphological features, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance, traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its diverse bioactive properties and potential therapeutic applications in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Deverra tortuosa
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
