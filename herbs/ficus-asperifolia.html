Distribution

This plant, Ficus asperifolia, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is notably documented within the borders of Benin. Furthermore, the species can be found growing throughout Burkina Faso. The geographical range also extends to include the nations of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, this medicinal plant is also recorded as being present in Guinea.
