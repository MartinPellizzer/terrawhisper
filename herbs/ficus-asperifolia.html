Ficus asperifolia

Ficus asperifolia, commonly known as the rough-leaved fig, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Widely used in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots possess potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. This plant plays a crucial role in treating various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Ficus asperifolia
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Rosales and the family Moraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ficus, specifically as the species Ficus asperifolia.

Distribution

This plant, Ficus asperifolia, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is notably documented within the borders of Benin. Furthermore, the species can be found growing throughout Burkina Faso. The geographical range also extends to include the nations of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, this medicinal plant is also recorded as being present in Guinea.
