Parastrephia quadrangularis

Parastrephia quadrangularis, commonly known as the "creeping bush" or "mora," is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive ailments, and skin irritations, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Parastrephia quadrangularis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized under the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Parastrephia.
