Juniperus rigida

Juniperus rigida, commonly known as the rigid juniper, is a resilient evergreen conifer native to the rugged landscapes of the Mediterranean and Middle East. Highly valued for its hardiness, this species plays a vital ecological role and is frequently studied for its unique phytochemical properties and medicinal potential.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus rigida
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Following the order Pinales, it is classified under the family Cupressaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Juniperus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted in the areas of Primorye and Sakhalin. Moving into China, the species can be found within the territories of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Additionally, the distribution extends into the China North-Central region. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse northern and central habitats.
