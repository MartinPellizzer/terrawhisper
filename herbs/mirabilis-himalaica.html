Mirabilis himalaica

Mirabilis himalaica ex

Mirabilis himalaica, commonly known as the Himalayan Marvel, is a resilient medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally used in holistic healing to alleviate inflammation, support respiratory health, and enhance overall vitality through its unique properties.

  • Scientific name: Mirabilis himalaica
  • Family: Nyctaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Mirabilis himalica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Nyctaginaceae and the genus Mirabilis.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several specific regions of Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central and North-Central territories. It also thrives in the high-altitude environment of Tibet. Moving to the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found throughout India. Additionally, it is documented to grow within the East Himalaya region.
