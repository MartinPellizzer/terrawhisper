Leptadenia lanceolata

Leptadenia lanceolata, commonly known as Vajradanti in Ayurveda, is a perennial climbing shrub valued for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned primarily for enhancing dental health and strengthening gums, this versatile plant also features in traditional remedies for treating skin ailments, promoting wound healing, and supporting overall digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Leptadenia lanceolata
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Leptadenia.
