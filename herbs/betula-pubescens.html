Betula pubescens

Downy birch

Betula pubescens, commonly known as the downy birch, is a resilient deciduous tree native to northern latitudes. Beyond its ecological significance, this species holds traditional medicinal value. Its bark and leaves are frequently utilized in herbal remedies to support skin health, reduce inflammation, and provide mild diuretic properties.

  • Scientific name: Betula pubescens
  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Betulaceae and is identified by the genus Betula.
