Diplomeris pulchella belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Asparagales and is a member of the family Orchidaceae. Ultimately, the plant is defined by its genus, Diplomeris, and its specific species designation, pulchella.
