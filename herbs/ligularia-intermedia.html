Ligularia intermedia, scientifically known as Ligularia intermedia, is a striking perennial herb native to the moist, mountainous regions of East Asia. Characterized by its large, heart-shaped leaves and vibrant yellow flower heads, this plant is prized in medicinal traditions for its diverse bioactive properties and restorative healing potential.
