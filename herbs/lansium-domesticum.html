Distribution

This plant, Lansium domesticum, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the Indo-China region. It can be found growing within the borders of Cambodia and Laos. Additionally, its natural range extends into the territories of Myanmar and Thailand. The species also populates various areas throughout Vietnam. Consequently, these five nations represent the primary locations for this medicinal plant in Southeast Asia.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as longkong or langsat, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties, most notably the bark. The bark of Lansium domesticum is frequently harvested and processed to extract bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as diarrhea and skin irritations. While the fruit is the most commercially significant part, the medicinal efficacy of the tree is deeply rooted in the chemical constituents found within its bark.
