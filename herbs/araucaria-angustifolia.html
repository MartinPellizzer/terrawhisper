Parana pine, scientifically known as Araucarica angustifolia, is a majestic conifer native to southern Brazil. Beyond its ecological importance, it holds significant medicinal value. Traditional uses include treating respiratory ailments and digestive issues, while its nutrient-rich seeds provide essential vitamins, showcasing the plant's remarkable synergy of nutrition and healing.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized into the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling under the family Araucariaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Araucararia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located in the Southeast region of Brazil. It also has a significant presence throughout the South of Brazil. Moving beyond Brazilian borders, its range extends into the Northeast of Argentina. Additionally, the species can be found within the territory of Paraguay. These areas collectively represent the natural geographical distribution of this medicinal species in Southern South America.
