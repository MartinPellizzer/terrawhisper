Olgaea tangutica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated within the order Asterales. It is further categorized under the family Asteraceae, which is a diverse group of flowering plants. Specifically, it is placed in the genus Olgaea, and its unique biological identity is defined by its species name, tangutica.
