Trautvetteria caroliniensis

Trautvetteria carolinienensis, commonly known as the Carolina trautvetia, is a distinctive moss species frequently found in moist, shaded forest environments. While primarily valued in botanical studies for its ecological role, it holds historical significance in traditional herbalism, where its unique properties have been explored for various medicinal applications.
