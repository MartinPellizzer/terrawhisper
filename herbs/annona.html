Annona

Annona, commonly known as custard apple or soursop, is a versatile genus of flowering plants celebrated in traditional medicine. Rich in bioactive compounds like acetogenins, these tropical fruits offer potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. They are widely studied for their potential therapeutic benefits in supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Annona
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Annonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Annona.
