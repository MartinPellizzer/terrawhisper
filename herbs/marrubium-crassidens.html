Marrubium crassidens

Marrubium crassidens, commonly known as White Horehound, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this plant is widely recognized for its potent expectorant qualities, frequently used to soothe respiratory ailments, alleviate coughs, and support overall pulmonary health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Marrubium crassidens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
