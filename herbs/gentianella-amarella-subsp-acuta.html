Gentianella amarella subsp. acuta

Gentianeella amarella subsp. acuta, commonly known as the Sharpless Gentian, is a distinctive medicinal herb found in specific montane habitats. Valued for its potent bitter principles, it has traditionally been used to stimulate digestion and support liver function, offering unique therapeutic properties within the broader Gentian family of plants.

  • Scientific name: Gentianella amarella subsp. acuta
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaecae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gentianeella, specifically the subspecies amarella subsp. acuta.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions within the Siberian landscape. It can be found growing in the Altai mountain range, which forms part of its native range. Additionally, the species is present in the Republic of Buryatia, extending its reach through the eastern parts of the territory. Its presence is also documented in the Chita region, contributing to its widespread occurrence in the area. Finally, the plant is distributed across the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions, covering significant portions of central and eastern Siberia.
