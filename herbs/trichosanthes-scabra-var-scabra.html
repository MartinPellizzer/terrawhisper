Trichosanthes scabra var. scabra

Trichosanthes scabra var. scabra, commonly known as Snake Gourd, is a versatile climbing vine valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is utilized to treat digestive issues, kidney ailments, and skin conditions. This nutrient-rich plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, serving as a vital herbal remedy globally.

  • Scientific name: Trichosanthes scabra var. scabra
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Cucurbitales, falling into the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Trichosanthes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions in Asia. In China, its distribution spans from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast coastal areas. It is also found specifically on the tropical island of Hainan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species thrives in the region of Assam. Additionally, its habitat extends into the mountainous terrain of the East Himalaya.
