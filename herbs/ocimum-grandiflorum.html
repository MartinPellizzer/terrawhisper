This plant is primarily found within the diverse landscapes of the African continent. Its distribution begins in Northeast Tropical Africa, where it can be located in Eritrea. It also grows across the highlands and plains of Ethiopia within the same northern region. Moving southward, the species is also prevalent throughout East Tropical Africa.
