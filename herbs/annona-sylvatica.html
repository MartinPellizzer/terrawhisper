Annona sylvatica

Annona sylvatica, commonly known as the wild custard apple, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, offering valuable bioactive compounds for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Annona sylvatica
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Annonaceae. Ultimately, Annona sylvatica is categorized under the genus Annona.
