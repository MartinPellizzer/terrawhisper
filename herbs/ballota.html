Horehound, scientifically known as Ballota nigra, is a perennial herb celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Traditionally used to soothe respiratory ailments, it serves as a natural remedy for coughs and congestion. This aromatic plant, characterized by its textured leaves, remains a vital component in many herbal healing practices worldwide.
