Ballota

Ballota, commonly known as White Horehound, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its soothing properties, it is traditionally used to alleviate respiratory ailments, including coughs and congestion. This aromatic plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its diverse bioactive compounds and calming herbal qualities.

  • Scientific name: Ballota
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ballota.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ballota nigra, is recognized by various common names depending on the language and region. In Esperanto, it is called Baloteo, while in Polish, it is referred to as mierznica. In Hebrew, the plant is known as גלונית, and in the Belarusian and Russian languages, it is commonly called Белакудранік and Белокудренник, respectively.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in Great Britain and Ireland.
