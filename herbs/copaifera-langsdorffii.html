Brazilian diesel tree

Copaifera langsdorffii · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (45 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Brazilian diesel tree, scientifically known as Copaifera langsdorffii, is a remarkable medicinal powerhouse native to the Amazon. Renowned for its resinous oleoresin, this species offers potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties. Used traditionally in folk medicine, it provides a natural resource for treating skin ailments and various systemic inflammations.

Plant Parts

This plant, Copaifera langsdorffii, is highly valued for its various therapeutic components derived from different botanical structures. The medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in the oleo-resin, a viscous substance extracted from the trunk, which is often referred to as an oil-resin due to its thick, lipid-rich consistency. Beyond this liquid exudate, the stem bark is frequently utilized in traditional applications, while the seeds and leaves also contribute to the plant's overall pharmacological profile. Together, these parts form the basis of its traditional use in treating inflammation and other ailments.
