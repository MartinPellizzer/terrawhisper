Copaifera langsdorffii

Copaifera langsloffii, commonly known as Copaiba, is a remarkable medicinal tree native to tropical landscapes. Renowned for its aromatic oleoresin, this plant serves as a powerhouse in traditional medicine. Its resin offers potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties, making it a vital natural resource for dermatological and systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Copaifera langsdorffii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling into the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Copaifera, specifically as the species Copaifera langsdorffii.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is noted specifically in Sri Lanka.
