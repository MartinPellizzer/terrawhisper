Drynaria quercifolia

Drynaria quercifolia, commonly known as the Oak-leaf Fern, is a distinctive epiphytic plant celebrated for its unique, shield-like foliage. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant traditional value in various cultures, where its rhizomes are utilized in folk medicine to treat diverse ailments and promote healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Drynaria quercifolia
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, it is further categorized into the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Drynaria.
