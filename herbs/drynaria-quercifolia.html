Scientific literature: Very Sparse (30 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Oakleaf fern, scientifically known as Drynaria quercifolia, is a distinctive climbing fern prized in traditional medicine. Native to tropical regions, it features characteristic lobed leaves resembling oak foliage. Often utilized in herbal remedies to treat inflammation and skin ailments, this resilient plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.
