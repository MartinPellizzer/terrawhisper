Sicyos edulis

Sicyo s eduulis, commonly known as the West Indian nettle, is a vigorous climbing shrub native to the Neotropics. While often viewed as an invasive weed, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Sicyos edulis
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Sicyos edulis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Sicyos.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans both Atlantic islands and African coastal regions. Within the Macaronesian archipelago, it can be found growing in the Azores, the Canary Islands, and Madeira. Its presence also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the territory of Cameroon. Furthermore, the species is documented inhabiting the islands of the Gulf of Guinea. Consequently, its habitat encompasses a diverse range of tropical and subtropical maritime environments.
