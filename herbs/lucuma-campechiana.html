Lucuma campechiana

Lucuma campechiana, commonly known as a medicinal fruit species, holds significant potential in traditional ethnobotany. Native to specific tropical regions, this plant offers unique bioactive compounds. Research suggests its leaves and pulp may possess antioxidant and therapeutic properties, making it a subject of growing interest for natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Lucuma campechiana
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, falling into the family Sapotaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed within the genus Lucuma.
