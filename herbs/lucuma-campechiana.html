eggfruit-tree, scientifically known as Lucuma campechiaana, is a tropical treasure renowned for its creamy, custard-like flesh. Beyond its delicious flavor, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, it is traditionally used to support digestive health and combat oxidative stress, making it a vital botanical resource.
