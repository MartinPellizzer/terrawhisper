Ornithogalum saundersiae

Ornithogalum saundersiae, commonly known as the Saunders' Star of Bethlehem, is a striking perennial bulb native to South Africa. Characterized by its elegant white, star-shaped blooms, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique botanical properties and adds significant aesthetic charm to medicinal and ornamental gardens alike.

  • Scientific name: Ornithogalum saundersiae
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ornithogalum.

Distribution

This plant is native to the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. Its natural range specifically includes the lush regions of KwaZulu-Natal. Additionally, it can be found growing within the borders of Eswatini. The species also extends its presence into the Northern Provinces of the region. Together, these locations define its primary geographical distribution.
