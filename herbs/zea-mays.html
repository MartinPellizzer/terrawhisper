Zea mays

Zea mays, commonly known as maize or corn, is more than just a global dietary staple. In traditional medicine, various parts of the plant are utilized for their therapeutic potential. From soothing urinary tract ailments with corn silk to leveraging its antioxidant properties, this versatile cereal offers significant medicinal value.

  • Scientific name: Zea mays
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Zea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical presence across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its distribution can be observed within the borders of Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward Middle Europe, the plant is also found throughout the country of Austria. Furthermore, its range extends into Belgium, which serves as another key location for its growth. Finally, the species is documented within the territories of Czechia-Slovakia.
