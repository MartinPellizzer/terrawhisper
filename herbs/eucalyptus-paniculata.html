Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is recorded in Rwanda. Moving toward the northeast, it can be found within the borders of Ethiopia. The species is also widely distributed across East Tropical Africa, specifically occurring in Kenya and Tanzania. Additionally, it extends its range into Uganda, completing its presence in the East Tropical African region.
