Boehmeria nivea

Ramie

Boehmeria nivea, commonly known as ramie, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its high-quality bast fibers. Beyond its industrial importance in textile production, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and promote wound healing through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Boehmeria nivea
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Urticaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Boehmeria.
