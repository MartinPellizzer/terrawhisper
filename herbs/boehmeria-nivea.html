Ramie, scientifically known as Boehmeria nivea, is a versatile perennial plant prized for its high-quality bast fibers. Beyond its industrial importance in textile production, it holds significant medicinal value. Traditionally used in various cultures, it is studied for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties in herbal medicine.
