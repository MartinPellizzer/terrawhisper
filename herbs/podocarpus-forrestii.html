What's the taxonomical classification of Podocarpus forrestii?

Podocarpus forrestii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Pinidae, following the taxonomic order Pinales. As a member of the family Podocarpaceae, it is placed within the genus Podocarpus, eventually reaching its specific designation as the species forrestii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Podocarpus forrestii has an evergreen habit characterized by linear-lanceolate leaves that are leathery, dark green, and arranged spirally along its branches. The foliage typically measures between 4 and 10 centimeters in length with distinct parallel venation. Its woody stems develop a rough bark as the tree matures in its natural forest habitat. The reproductive structures consist of small, fleshy, berry-like receptacles that surround a single seed. These morphological traits distinguish it from other members of the Podocarpaceae family.
