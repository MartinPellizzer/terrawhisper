Siparuna guianensis

Siparuna guianenesis, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in South American traditional medicine, is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive alkaloids and flavonoids, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive ailments, and various skin infections, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Siparuna guianensis
  • Family: Siparunaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant, Siparuna guianenensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Laurales. Finally, it is classified under the family Siparunaceae and the genus Siparuna.
