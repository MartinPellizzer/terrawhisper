Peppercorn Tree, scientifically known as Schinus areira, is a versatile evergreen native to South America. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it offers antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, this resilient species is highly valued in ethnobotany for its diverse therapeutic potential.
