Debregeasia saeneb

Debregeasia saeneb, commonly known as the forest coffee plant, is a vital medicinal species native to East Africa. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammatory conditions and infections. This resilient shrub offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Debregeasia saeneb
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Debregeasia saeneb, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Rosales. Finally, it is classified within the family Urticaceae under the genus Debregeasia.
