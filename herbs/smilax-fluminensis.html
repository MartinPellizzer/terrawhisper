Smilax fluminensis

Smilax fluellenis, commonly known as a species of Sarsaparilla, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, the plant's roots are traditionally harvested for their bioactive compounds, which are frequently utilized in herbal remedies to support detoxification and enhance overall blood health.

  • Scientific name: Smilax fluminensis
  • Family: Smilacaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Smilax fluminenis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Liliales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Smilacaceae and falls under the genus Smilax.
