Dianthera pectoralis

Dianthera pectoralis, commonly known as lungwort, is a medicinal herb valued for its respiratory benefits. Traditionally used to treat coughs, bronchitis, and asthma, this plant contains bioactive compounds that soothe the lungs. Its unique properties make it a significant subject in ethnobotany and natural pharmacology for respiratory health.

  • Scientific name: Dianthera pectoralis
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Northern America

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the southeastern region of the United States, with a notable presence in Florida. Its distribution extends significantly into Mexico, covering several diverse territories. It can be located throughout the central regions of Mexico as well as across the northeastern parts of the country. Additionally, the species inhabits the Gulf coastal areas of Mexico.
