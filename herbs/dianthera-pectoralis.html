Carpenter bush

Dianthera pectoralis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (288 studies) · ★★★★★

Carpenter bush, scientifically known as Dianthera pectoralis, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and digestive issues. This versatile plant offers significant pharmacological potential for developing modern treatments for various inflammatory conditions.
