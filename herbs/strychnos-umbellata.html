Strychnos umbellata

What's the taxonomical classification of Strychnos umbellata?

Strychnos umbellata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Gentianales and belongs to the family Loganiaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Strychnos and the species umbellata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Strychnos umbellata has woody, climbing liana stems that feature simple, alternate leaves with elliptic to oblong shapes and prominent pinnate venation. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in clusters, often appearing in the leaf axils. These blossoms eventually develop into fleshy, globose fruits that are characteristic of the genus.
