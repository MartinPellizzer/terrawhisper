Shiral

Microcos paniculata, commonly known as the paniculate microcos, is a resilient herbaceous plant valued in traditional herbal medicine. Belonging to the Rosaceae family, it thrives in diverse habitats. Renowned for its unique phytochemical properties, this species offers significant therapeutic potential in treating various ailments through its bioactive compounds.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
