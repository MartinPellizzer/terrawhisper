Ranunculus muricatus

Ranunculus muricatus, commonly known as the prickly buttercup, is a distinctive medicinal plant found in specific ecological niches. Characterized by its unique, textured foliage and delicate blooms, it has been traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in managing various localized inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Ranunculus muricatus
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Ranunculus.
