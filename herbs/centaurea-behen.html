Centaurea behen

Centaurea behen, commonly known as Behen, is a significant medicinal herb prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent astringent and restorative properties, this plant is primarily utilized to enhance reproductive health, support skin vitality, and promote overall wellness through its diverse bioactive compounds and healing benefits.

  • Scientific name: Centaurea behen
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Centaurea behen, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the Asteraceae family within the genus Centaurea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution that spans several key regions across Western and Central Asia. In Central Asia, its presence is documented within the borders of Turkmenistan. The species also extends into the Caucasus region, occurring in both the North Caucasus and the Transcaucasus areas. Moving into Western Asia, it can be found growing across the landscape of Iran. Finally, its range reaches further into Western Asia, specifically within the country of Iraq.
