Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents and distinct ecosystems. In the Atlantic region, its presence can be identified within the Macaronesia islands, specifically in Cape Verde. Moving to North America, the species is widely distributed throughout Western Canada. It can be found across the prairie provinces, including Alberta, Saskatchewan, and Manitoba. Furthermore, its habitat extends to the diverse landscapes of British Columbia.
