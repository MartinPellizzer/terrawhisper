Ziziphus nummularia

Ziziphus nummularia, commonly known as the jujube or small-leaf jujube, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid African regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its various parts—including leaves, roots, and bark—possess significant antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, making it a vital resource for treating diverse ailments in local communities.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphus nummularia
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Ziziphus.
