Helleborus

Hellebore, commonly known as Christmas Rose or Lenten Rose, is a striking genus of perennial flowering plants. While celebrated for their elegant, winter-blooming petals, these plants are highly toxic if ingested. Historically used in traditional medicine, they must be handled with extreme caution due to their potent chemical properties.
