What's the taxonomical classification of Adenophora rupincola?

Adenophora rupincola belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Campanulaceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Adenophora, with its unique identity defined by its species name, rupicola.
