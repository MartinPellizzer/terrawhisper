Berteroa incana

Berteroa incana, commonly known as silver berteroa, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant is recognized for its silvery foliage and therapeutic potential. It is frequently utilized in folk remedies to treat various ailments, offering a fascinating subject for pharmacological study.

  • Scientific name: Berteroa incana
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Berteroa.
