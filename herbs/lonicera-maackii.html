Lonicera maackii

Lonicera maackii, commonly known as Amur honeysuckle, is a versatile deciduous shrub valued in traditional herbal medicine. Known for its fragrant blossoms and medicinal berries, it is frequently utilized for its antioxidant properties and anti-inflammatory effects, offering significant potential in treating respiratory ailments and supporting overall immune health.

  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe
