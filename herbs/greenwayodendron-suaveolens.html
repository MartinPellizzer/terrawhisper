Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa. In West Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found in the Central African Republic and Cameroon. Its range further extends through the Congo to include the region of Cabinda. Altogether, these locations highlight the plant's prevalence across diverse West and West-Central African landscapes.

The reproduction of Greenwayodendron suaveolens is characterized by a highly flexible phenological cycle, with both flowering and fruiting periods being highly variable throughout the year, potentially occurring in any month from January through December. The plant produces fruit classified as a berry, which is characterized by an indehiscent nature, meaning it does not spontaneously split open at maturity to release its seeds.
