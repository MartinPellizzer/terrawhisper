Ilex cornuta

Chinese holly

Ilex cornuta, commonly known as the holly fern or prickly holly, is a resilient evergreen shrub prized for its glossy, spiny foliage. Beyond its ornamental beauty in landscaping, this plant holds traditional significance in various cultures, often utilized in herbal practices for its unique botanical properties and hardy nature.

  • Scientific name: Ilex cornuta
  • Family: Aquifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Aquifoliales, it is positioned in the family Aquifoliaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Ilex.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several specific regions within East Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central and North-Central territories. It can also be found growing in the Southeast regions of China as well as on the island of Hainan. Beyond mainland China, the species extends its range into the neighboring country of Korea. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Ilex cornuta.
