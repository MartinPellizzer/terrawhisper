Scientific literature: Very Sparse (2 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Lepidium sativum, scientifically known as Lucuma, is a nutritional powerhouse native to the Andean highlands. Often called "the gold of the Incas," this fruit offers a sweet, maple-like flavor. Rich in fiber, vitamins, and antioxidants, it serves as a versatile superfood in smoothies, desserts, and healthy dietary supplements.
