, scientifically known as Aconitum tanguticum, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of China. Often called the Tibetan monkshood, it is prized in traditional medicine for its potent alkaloids. However, due to its extreme toxicity, it must be handled with precision and expert pharmacological guidance.
