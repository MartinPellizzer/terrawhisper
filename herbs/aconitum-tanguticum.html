Aconitum tanguticum

Aconitum tanguticum, commonly known as Tangut monkshood, is a perennial herb native to high-altitude regions of China. Renowned for its striking blue flowers, this plant contains potent alkaloids. While used in traditional medicine for specific ailments, it requires expert handling due to its significant toxicity and complex pharmacological properties.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum tanguticum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Aconitum. Specifically, it is identified as the species Aconitum tanguticum.
