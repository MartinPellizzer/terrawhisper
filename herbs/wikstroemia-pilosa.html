What's the taxonomical classification of Wikstroemia pilosa?

Wikstroemia pilosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Malvales. This plant is a member of the family Thymelaeaceae, specifically belonging to the genus Wikstroemia, with its formal scientific designation being the species pilosa.
