Oxycoccus microcarpum, scientifically known as Vaccinium microcarpum, is a diminutive, creeping evergreen shrub native to North American wetlands. Often called small cranberry, this hardy plant produces tart, acidic berries. Traditionally valued in ethnobotany, its antioxidant-rich fruit offers potential therapeutic benefits, particularly in supporting urinary tract health and digestion.
