Distribution

This plant, Vitellaria paradoxa, is widely distributed across the landscape of West Tropical Africa. Plant Parts

This plant, Vitellaria paradoxa, commonly known as the shea tree, possesses several distinct parts that are utilized for various medicinal and economic purposes. The bark and specifically the stem bark are often harvested for their tannins and are sometimes used in traditional remedies to treat skin inflammations or gastrointestinal issues. The large, green leaves are frequently employed in topical applications to soothe ailments. Most notably, the plant produces large, fleshy fruits that contain hard nuts; within these nuts lie the seeds, which contain the rich, fatty kernels used to extract shea butter, a substance prized globally for its healing properties in treating skin conditions and reducing inflammation.
