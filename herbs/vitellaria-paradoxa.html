Vitellaria paradoxa

Vitellaaria paradoxa, commonly known as the Wild Marula, is a significant medicinal tree native to African savannas. Renowned for its versatile properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and skin inflammations. Its nutrient-rich fruit and bark offer profound therapeutic potential for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vitellaria paradoxa
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Ericales and placed in the family Sapotaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Vitellaria, specifically as the species Vitellaria paradoxa.
