Chinese snake gourd

Trichosanthes kirilowii · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (242 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese snake gourd, scientifically known as Trichosanthes kirilowii, is a traditional medicinal plant revered in East Asian herbalism. Characterized by its elongated, vine-grown fruit, it is widely utilized for its cooling properties. It is frequently employed to treat inflammation, promote digestion, and support overall detoxification within holistic wellness practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, Trichosanthes kirilowii, is primarily valued for its specialized underground structures which serve as the main source of its medicinal properties. The medicinal components are concentrated within the tubers, specifically in the form of fleshy root tubers that store essential nutrients. Whether referred to as a single root tuber or a cluster of root tubers, these organs are botanically classified as a tuberous root. Therefore, when harvesting for traditional medicine, practitioners focus on extracting these nutrient-rich tubers and the surrounding roots to ensure the highest therapeutic potency.
