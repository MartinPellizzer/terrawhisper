Astragalus gummifer, scientifically known as Astragalus gummifer, is a perennial legume native to East Asia, renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine, it is primarily utilized to bolster immune function, enhance vitality, and promote longevity through its rich concentration of bioactive polysaccharides and saponins.
