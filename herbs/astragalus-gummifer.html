Astragalus gummifer, scientifically known as Astragalus gummifer, is a perennial legume native to East Asia, renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine, it is primarily utilized to bolster immune function, enhance vitality, and promote longevity through its rich concentration of bioactive polysaccharides and saponins.

Distribution

This plant, Astragalus gummifer, exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the Western Asian region. It can be found growing naturally across the diverse landscapes of Iran. The species also extends its range into the territories of Iraq. Additionally, it is documented to inhabit the mountainous and arid regions of the Lebanon-Syria border. Finally, the plant is widely distributed throughout the country of Türkiye.
