Ferulago anguata, commonly known as the angular ferula, is a perennial herb native to specific arid regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant possesses unique phytochemical properties. Research explores its potential therapeutic applications, focusing on its bioactive compounds which may offer significant medicinal benefits for various health conditions.
