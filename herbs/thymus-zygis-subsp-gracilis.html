Thymus zygis subsp. gracilis

Thymus zygis subsp. gracilis, commonly known as a subspecies of wild thyme, is a resilient aromatic herb native to Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it possesses significant antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. This botanical treasure is increasingly studied for its therapeutic potential in traditional and modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Thymus zygis subsp. gracilis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, specifically within the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Thymus as the subspecies Thymus zygis subsp. gracilis.
