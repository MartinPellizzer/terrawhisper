Eucalyptus globulus

Eucalyptus globulus, commonly known as the blue gum, is a fast-growing evergreen tree native to Australia. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, its aromatic leaves are rich in cineole. This essential oil is widely utilized in aromatherapy and respiratory treatments to soothe coughs, reduce inflammation, and support congestion.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus globulus
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Eucalyptus.
