What's the taxonomical classification of Uraria crinita?

Uraria crinita belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Fabales and is a member of the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Uraria and its specific species name, crinita.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Uraria crinita has an erect, herbaceous habit characterized by slender, cylindrical stems that can reach heights of up to 1.5 meters. The leaves are arranged in an opposite pattern and consist of small, ovate to lanceolate leaflets with entire margins. Its inflorescences are typically terminal, composed of small, pale yellow flowers that emerge from dense, spike-like clusters. The fruit is a small, indehiscent legume that often contains a single seed. These botanical features are characteristic of the Fabaceae family and are adapted for its tropical habitat.
