Zygophyllum arabicum

Zygophyllum arabicum, commonly known as the Arabic stone plant, is a resilient succulent native to arid African regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its fleshy leaves contain bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. This hardy species exemplifies botanical adaptation, offering unique pharmacological potential for contemporary therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Zygophyllum arabicum
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zygophyllales. Finally, it resides in the family Zygophyllaceae and is identified by the genus Zygophyllum.
