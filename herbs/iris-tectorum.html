Plant Parts

This plant, Iris tectorum, is characterized by its distinct morphological features, most notably its thick, creeping rhizomes that serve as the primary storage organs for nutrients and medicinal compounds. Emerging from these underground rhizomes are its long, sword-shaped leaves, which are typically narrow and upright, providing the necessary surface area for photosynthesis. Together, the robust rhizomes and the slender leaves constitute the essential structural components of this medicinal species.
