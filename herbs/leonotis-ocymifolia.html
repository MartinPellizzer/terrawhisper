Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the tropical regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Burundi, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the northeast, the species also expands its range into Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Eritrea and Ethiopia. Consequently, these five nations represent the primary geographical locations for this medicinal species.
