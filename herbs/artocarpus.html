Artocarpus

Artocarpus, commonly known as jackfruit or breadfruit, belongs to the Moraceae family. Renowned for its versatile fruit and timber, this genus holds significant medicinal value. Various species are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for healing.

  • Scientific name: Artocarpus
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Moraceae, it is classified under the genus Artocarpus.
