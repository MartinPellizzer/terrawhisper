Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Jackfruit, utilizes various anatomical structures for its medicinal and nutritional properties. The sturdy stem provides the structural support necessary for the tree to reach immense heights, while the thick, protective bark is often harvested for its astringent qualities in traditional remedies. The large, fleshy fruit serves as a primary source of nutrients, and the large seeds contained within are frequently consumed or processed for their specific health benefits. Additionally, the broad, dark green leaves are highly versatile, often used in decoctions to treat various ailments.
