Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in both Gambia and Guinea. Moving into the West-Central Tropical Africa region, it can be found in the Central African Republic and Cameroon. Additionally, the species extends its reach to the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Together, these locations highlight a range centered primarily around the tropical belt of Africa.
