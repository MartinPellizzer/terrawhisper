Aconitum forrestii

What's the taxonomical classification of Aconitum forrestii?

Aconitum forrestii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales. This species is a representative of the family Ranunculaceae, specifically situated within the genus Aconitum, and is identified by its unique species name, forrestii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aconium forrestii has erect, herbaceous stems that reach heights of up to 1.5 meters and bear large, deeply lobed leaves with palmate venation. The plant produces striking, large flowers that are typically a deep blue or violet color, characterized by a prominent helmet-shaped uppermost sepal. These inflorescences are arranged in dense racemes that emerge from the upper axils of the foliage. Small, glandular hairs often cover the stems and pedicels, providing a distinct texture to the plant's surface. The root system consists of a sturdy tuberous rhizome which serves as the primary storage organ for the species.

    What side effects this plant can have?

Ingestion of its toxic alkaloids disrupts the electrical activity of the heart and nervous system. Patients often experience intense gastrointestinal distress followed by neurological symptoms like tingling and numbness in the extremities. As the toxins progress, they can lead to life-threatening conditions such as muscle paralysis and irregular heart rhythms. Ultimately, the systemic collapse may culminate in fatal respiratory failure.
