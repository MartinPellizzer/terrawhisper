Mammea suriga

Mammea suriga, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds profound value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Exploring its phytochemical properties offers promising insights into developing new therapeutic treatments for modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Mammea suriga
  • Family: Calophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Calophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Mammea.
