Mallotus peltatus

Mallotus peltatus, commonly known as the "Red-leafed Mallotus," is a distinctive evergreen shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant, reddish foliage and medicinal properties, it is used in traditional healing to treat skin ailments and inflammation. This plant plays a vital role in biodiversity and ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Mallotus peltatus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Mallotus.
