Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its presence is officially documented in the nation of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. Additionally, it can be found throughout the territories of Burkina and Ghana. The species also extends its range into Guinea-Bissau, contributing to its regional prevalence.
