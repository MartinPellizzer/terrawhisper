Thalia geniculata

Thalia geniusta, commonly known as the Water Hyacinth or Water Thalia, is a versatile aquatic perennial. Renowned for its striking appearance and rapid growth, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues through its bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Thalia geniculata
  • Family: Marantaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Thalia geniculata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Zingiberales. Finally, it is a member of the family Marantaceae within the genus Thalia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its presence is officially documented in the nation of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. Additionally, it can be found throughout the territories of Burkina and Ghana. The species also extends its range into Guinea-Bissau, contributing to its regional prevalence. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the country of Guinea, marking a significant portion of its western habitat.
