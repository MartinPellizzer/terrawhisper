What's the taxonomical classification of Dalbergia rimosa?

Dalbergia rimosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated within the order Fabales. It is a prominent representative of the family Fabaceae, specifically falling under the genus Dalbergia, with its unique biological identity defined by the species name rimosa.
