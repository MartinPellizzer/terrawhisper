Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of North and Central America. Within Mexico, it can be found spanning from the Northeast to the Southwest. It also inhabits the Gulf regions of Mexico, extending its presence toward the Southeast. Moving further south, its range continues into the Central American landscape. Specifically, the species has been documented growing within the borders of Belize.
