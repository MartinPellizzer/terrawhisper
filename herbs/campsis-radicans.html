Trumpet creeper, scientifically known as Campsis radicans, is a vigorous deciduous climbing vine celebrated for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped orange flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this plant holds significant roles in traditional medicine, often utilized for its perceived anti-inflammatory properties and its ability to support various systemic functions.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Lamiales and the family Bignoniaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Campsis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented within the borders of both Austria and Germany. Moving toward the western part of the continent, it can also be found throughout Southwestern Europe, specifically in France. The species extends its range into Southeastern Europe as well. Within this southeastern region, the plant is notably distributed across Bulgaria and Greece.
