Pavetta crassipes

Pavetta crassipes, commonly known as the forest pavetta, is a striking evergreen shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its fragrant, white star-shaped flowers, this plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem. It is valued in traditional medicine for treating various ailments, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Pavetta crassipes
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Pavetta crassipes, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family and falls under the genus Pavetta.
