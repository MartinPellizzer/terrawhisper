Oxyanthus speciosus

Oxyanthus speciosus, commonly known as the beautiful shrub or false jasmine, is a captivating medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its fragrant white blooms, it is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, including its potential antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant effects in various treatments.

  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
