Euphorbia forskaolii

Euphorbia forskaolii, commonly known as a medicinal succulent from Madagascar, is highly valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique chemical composition, this plant is utilized for its potential therapeutic properties. Exploring its bioactive compounds offers promising insights into developing new treatments for various inflammatory and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia forskaolii
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and is specifically categorized within the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the territories of Egypt and Libya. The species is also found in the Macaronesian islands, specifically inhabiting the Canary Islands and Cape Verde. Moving toward the coast of West Tropical Africa, it can be located in Benin. Collectively, these locations highlight the wide environmental range of Euphorbia forskaolii.
