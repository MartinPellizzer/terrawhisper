Primulina eburnea

Primulina eburnea, commonly known as the Ivory Primulina, is a captivating perennial herb native to China. Distinguished by its striking, creamy-white flowers and velvety foliage, this species belongs to the Gesneriaceae family. It is highly valued by collectors for its unique ornamental beauty and delicate, enchanting aesthetic in cultivation.

  • Scientific name: Primulina eburnea
  • Family: Gesneriaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Gesneriaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Primulina, of which it is the species eburnea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its distribution is primarily found across the South-Central regions. It also extends into the Southeast portions of the country. Beyond Chinese borders, the species can be located within the Indo-China region. Specifically, its presence is documented in the nation of Vietnam.
