Setaria italica

Foxtail millet

Setaria italica, commonly known as foxtail millet, is a versatile cereal grain cherished for its nutritional profile. Beyond its dietary importance, this resilient plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. Rich in antioxidants and essential minerals, it serves as a functional food supporting holistic wellness and metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Setaria italica
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Setaria.
