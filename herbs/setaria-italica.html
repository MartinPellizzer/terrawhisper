Foxtail millet, scientifically known as Setaria italicica, is a versatile annual grass widely cultivated for its nutrient-dense seeds. Beyond its role as a staple food source, it holds significant medicinal potential. Traditionally used in various cultures, it is valued for its antioxidant properties and ability to support digestive health.
