Elephantopus carolinianus

Elephantopus carolinianus, commonly known as the Carolinian elephantfoot, is a perennial herb native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. Its leaves are frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat various ailments, showcasing its profound ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Elephantopus carolinianus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Elephantopus.
