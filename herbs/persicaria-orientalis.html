Plant Parts

This plant is a perennial herb characterized by its striking ornamental features and unique botanical structure. The lush, heart-shaped leaves are often marked with a dark V-shaped blotch, providing a beautiful backdrop for the delicate pink inflorescence that rises in terminal spikes. As the flowering period concludes, the plant develops small, inconspicuous fruits that contain the seeds necessary for its reproduction. While the entire fruit may be small, it serves as a vital component of the plant's life cycle within its natural habitat.
