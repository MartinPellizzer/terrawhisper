Rudbeckia sullivantii

Rudbeckia sullivantii, commonly known as Sullivant's coneflower, is a resilient perennial native to North American prairies. Renowned for its striking golden petals and dark central cones, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its restorative properties, offering both aesthetic beauty and therapeutic potential within holistic and herbal traditions.

  • Scientific name: Rudbeckia sullivantii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. It is further identified by the genus Rudbeckia, specifically known as Rudbeckia sullivantii.
