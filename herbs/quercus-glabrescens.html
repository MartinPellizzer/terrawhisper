Quercus glabrescens

Quercus glabrescens, commonly known as the Smoothleaf Live Oak, is a resilient evergreen shrub or small tree native to the southeastern United States. Valued for its dense, leathery foliage, this hardy species offers ecological benefits and holds potential in traditional botanical studies for its unique chemical properties and durability.

  • Scientific name: Quercus glabrescens
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales and the family Fagaceae, it is classified under the genus Quercus.
