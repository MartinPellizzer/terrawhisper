Ferula orientalis

Ferula orientalis, commonly known as Oriental Ferula, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern regions. Renowned for its aromatic roots, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. This plant contains bioactive compounds used to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive issues, and various ailments through various extracts.

  • Scientific name: Ferula orientalis
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Ferula.
