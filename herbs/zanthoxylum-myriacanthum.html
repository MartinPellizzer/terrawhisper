What's the taxonomical classification of Zanthoxylum myriacanthum?

Zanthoxylum myriacanthum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant falls under the order Sapindales and is a member of the family Rutaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement within the genus Zanthoxylum, specifically as the species myriacanthum.
