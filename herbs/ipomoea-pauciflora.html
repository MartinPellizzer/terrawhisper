Ipomoea pauciflora

Ipomoea pauciflora, commonly known as a medicinal herb within the Convolvulaceae family, holds significant ethnobotanical interest. This species is recognized for its unique bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in traditional healing practices. Researchers are increasingly exploring its chemical constituents to uncover new applications in modern pharmacology and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea pauciflora
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Ipomoea.

Common Names

This plant, Ipomoea pauciflora, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is referred to as the few-flowered morning glory, while in Spanish, it is commonly called morning glory de pocas flores. In Portuguese, the plant is known as euforbia de poucas flores.
