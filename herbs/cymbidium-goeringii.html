Cymbidium goeringii

What's the taxonomical classification of Cymbidium goeringii?

Cymbidium goeringii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Asparagales. This plant is a member of the family Orchidaceae and is specifically classified under the genus Cymbidium, with its unique designation being the species goeringii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cymbidium goeringii has slender, upright, grass-like leaves that grow in fans from a creeping rhizome. The foliage is typically dark green and can reach lengths of up to 40 centimeters. During its flowering season, the plant produces delicate, fragrant blossoms on long, arching stalks. These flowers feature small, pale petals and a prominent, often patterned lip that serves to attract pollinators. The overall structure of the plant is graceful and compact, making it a staple in traditional orchid collections.
