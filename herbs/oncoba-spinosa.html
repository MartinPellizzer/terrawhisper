Oncoba spinosa

Oncoba spinosa, commonly known as the African Rosewood, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to various African regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
