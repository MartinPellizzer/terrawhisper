Lomatogonium carinthiacum

Lomatogonium carinariacum, commonly known as the Carinthian false moss, is a specialized bryophyte native to high-altitude alpine ecosystems. While not a primary source of pharmacology, it holds significant ecological value. Studying its unique physiological adaptations provides vital insights into plant resilience and survival within extreme, moisture-sensitive mountain habitats.

  • Scientific name: Lomatogonium carinthiacum
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Europe
