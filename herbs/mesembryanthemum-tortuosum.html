Mesembryanthemum tortuosum

Mesembryanthemum tortuosum, commonly known as the "Stone Plant" or "Ice Plant," is a succulent herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including skin issues and digestive disorders, offering significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Mesembryanthemum tortuosum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant is native to the Southern Africa region. Specifically, its presence is concentrated within the Cape Provinces. This unique geographical range highlights its adaptation to the local environment. Because of this limited distribution, it is considered an endemic species of the area. Understanding this specific location is essential for the conservation of the species.
