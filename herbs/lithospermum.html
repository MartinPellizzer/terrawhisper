Lithospermum

Lithospermum, commonly known as gromwell, is a genus of flowering plants in the Boraginaceae family. Renowned for their distinctive, often vibrant flowers, these perennials are valued in traditional medicine. Historically, certain species provided pigments for dyes and were utilized for their purported healing properties in various herbal remedies.
