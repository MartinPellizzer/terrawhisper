Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it is specifically documented within the South-Central territories. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be found in the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout the Indo-China region, including Laos and Myanmar. Finally, its presence is also confirmed within the borders of Thailand.

Plant Parts

This plant, Dendrobium cariniferum, is an epiphytic orchid that reproduces through the dispersal of its tiny, dust-like seed. Unlike larger flowering plants, the seed of this species lacks an endosperm, meaning it relies on a symbiotic relationship with specific mycorrhizal fungi in the environment to provide the necessary nutrients for germination and early growth. Once the seed successfully establishes itself, it develops into a complex structure of pseudobulbs, leaves, and specialized aerial roots.
