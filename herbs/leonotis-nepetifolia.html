Leonotis nepetifolia

Leonotis nepetifolia, commonly known as Lion's Ear, is a striking perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Native to parts of Africa, it is distinguished by its vibrant orange, tubular flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, used traditionally for its diverse medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Leonotis nepetifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is further classified under the genus Leonotis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Morocco. Its range extends to Macaronesia, where it can be found on the Canary Islands. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed across West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it inhabits the nations of Benin, Burkina, and Gambia.
