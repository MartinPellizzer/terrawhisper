Ambelania duckei

Ambelania duckei, commonly known as the "pau-ferro" in certain Brazilian regions, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Native to South American ecosystems, this species is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising pharmacological potential for developing new treatments in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Ambelania duckei
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Ambelania.
