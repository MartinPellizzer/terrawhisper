What's the taxonomical classification of Saussurea tangutica?

Saussurea tangutica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving through its hierarchical organization, the plant falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Saussurea, and its unique species designation, tangutica.
