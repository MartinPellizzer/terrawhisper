Casimiroa tetrameria

Casimiroa tetrameria, commonly known as "palo de mico" in certain regions, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional ethnobotany. Primarily used in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments and digestive issues, this plant offers significant pharmacological potential, warranting further scientific investigation into its bioactive compounds and therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Casimiroa tetrameria
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is categorized within the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Casimiroa, specifically as the species Casimiroa tetrameria.
