Croton membranaceus

Croton membranaceuus, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, holds profound importance in traditional healing practices. Native to various tropical regions, this plant is celebrated for its diverse pharmacological properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness.

  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
