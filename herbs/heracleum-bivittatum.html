Heracleum bivittatum

What's the taxonomical classification of Heracleum bivittatum?

Heracleum bivittatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression through the order Apiales, it is placed within the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, the plant is identified by the genus Heracleum and is specifically designated as the species bivittatum.

The root system typically develops into a stout taproot that provides stability for the tall, herbaceous structure.
