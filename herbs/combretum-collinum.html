Combretum collinum

Combretum collinum, commonly known as the "Bushwillow" or "Velvet Bushwillow," is a resilient woody shrub native to various African landscapes. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark are frequently utilized to treat diverse ailments, ranging from digestive issues to inflammation, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Combretum collinum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Combretum.
