Thalictrum

Thalictrum, commonly known as meadow rue, is a versatile genus of flowering plants within the Ranunculaceae family. Found in diverse habitats from woodland edges to moist meadows, these perennials are prized for their delicate, fern-like foliage and unique floral structures, adding elegant texture and charm to natural landscapes.
