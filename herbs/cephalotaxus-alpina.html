Cephalotaxus alpina

Cephalotaxus alpina, commonly known as the Alpine Plum Yew, is a resilient coniferous species native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its medicinal potential, this plant contains unique alkaloids that exhibit significant pharmacological properties, including anti-tumor and anti-inflammatory activities, making it a subject of great interest in phytotherapy.

  • Scientific name: Cephalotaxus alpina
  • Family: Cephalotaxaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, it is further categorized into the family Cephalotaxaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Cephalotaxus.
