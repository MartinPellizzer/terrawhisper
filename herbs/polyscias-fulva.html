Polyscias fulva

Polyscias fulva, commonly known as yellow sedge, is a resilient perennial herb native to tropical wetlands. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is valued for its bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Polyscias fulva
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Araliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Polyscias.
