Khaya senegalensis

Khaya senegalensis, commonly known as the African Mahogany, is a majestic evergreen tree prized for its medicinal properties and high-quality timber. Found across tropical Africa, various parts of this plant are traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant ethnobotanical value to local communities.

  • Scientific name: Khaya senegalensis
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Khaya senegalensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales and the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Khaya.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Its natural range also extends into the nation of Burkina. Furthermore, the species is documented to exist in Gambia and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, the geographical presence of this medicinal plant includes the country of Guinea.
