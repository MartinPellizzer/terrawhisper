Rosa xanthina

Rosa xanthina, commonly known as the Yellow Rose, is a deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant, golden-yellow blooms and aromatic fragrance, this medicinal plant is traditionally used in herbal practices to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote skin vitality through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Rosa xanthina
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Europe

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Middle Europe, its presence has been documented within Germany. The species is widely spread across China, specifically found in the South-Central regions. Additionally, it inhabits the North-Central parts of China. Finally, its range extends to the territories of both Inner Mongolia and Manchuria.
