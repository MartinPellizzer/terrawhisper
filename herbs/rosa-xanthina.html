Rosa xanthina, scientifically known as Rosa xanthina, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant, fragrant blooms, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its antioxidant properties, helping to support immune health and soothe various inflammatory conditions.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Middle Europe, its presence has been documented within Germany.
