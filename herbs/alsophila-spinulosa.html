Alsophila spinulosa

Alsophila spinulosa, commonly known as the prickly spinosa, is a resilient medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific arid regions, this species is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address various ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and natural wellness applications worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Alsophila spinulosa
  • Family: Cyatheaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Cyatheales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cyatheaceae, falling under the genus Alsophila.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the southeastern coastal areas. Its presence also extends to the tropical island of Hainan and the high-altitude terrains of Tibet. Beyond the Chinese mainland, the species is geographically established in Japan.
