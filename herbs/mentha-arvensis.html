Scientific literature: Moderate (123 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Corn mint, scientifically known as Mentha arvensis, is a perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Commonly found in damp habitats, this aromatic plant is distinguished by its small, serrated leaves and delicate purple flowers. It is valued in traditional medicine for its soothing, digestive, and antimicrobial properties.
