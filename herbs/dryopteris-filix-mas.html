Dryopteris filix-mas

Dryopteris felix-mas

Dryopteris filix-mas, commonly known as the Male Fern, is a perennial plant valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its historical use in treating parasitic intestinal infections, this resilient fern thrives in shaded, moist woodland environments. Today, it remains a subject of botanical interest for its unique medicinal properties.

  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
