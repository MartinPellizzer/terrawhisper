Platycladus orientalis

Oriental arbor-vitae

Platycladus orientalis, commonly known as the Oriental Arborvitae, is a resilient evergreen conifer valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it is frequently utilized to treat skin conditions, inflammation, and respiratory ailments. This versatile plant serves as a vital botanical resource in various holistic healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Platycladus orientalis
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, it is a member of the Cupressaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Platycladus.
