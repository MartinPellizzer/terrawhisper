Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Platycladus orientalis, possesses various components that are utilized in traditional medicine. The scale-like leaves are the most prominent feature, often serving as the primary source for medicinal preparations. From these, a potent leaf extract can be derived to harness its bioactive compounds. The plant also produces small, woody fruits that contain the reproductive elements of the species. Within these fruits, the seeds develop, eventually reaching a state where the mature seeds can be harvested for specific therapeutic applications.
