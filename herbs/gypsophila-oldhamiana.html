Gypsophila oldhamiana

Gypsophila oldhamiana, commonly known as Oldham's baby's breath, is a delicate perennial cherished for its ornamental beauty and potential medicinal properties. Native to specific Asian regions, this plant belongs to the Caryophyllaceae family. It is increasingly studied for its bioactive compounds, offering promising insights into traditional botanical healing.

  • Scientific name: Gypsophila oldhamiana
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Caryophyllaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by its genus, Gypsophila.
