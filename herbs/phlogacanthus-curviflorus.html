Phlogacanthus curviflorus

Phlogacanthus curviflorus, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional practices, holds remarkable therapeutic potential. Characterized by its unique floral structure, this plant is valued for its bioactive compounds. Research suggests its applications in treating various ailments, highlighting its growing importance in ethnobotany and modern pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Phlogacanthus curviflorus
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by its genus, Phlogacanthus, and its species name, curviflorus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is notably documented in the South-Central areas as well as throughout Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the lush landscapes of Assam. The species also extends its reach into the territory of Bangladesh. Finally, its distribution encompasses the rugged terrains of the East Himalaya region.
