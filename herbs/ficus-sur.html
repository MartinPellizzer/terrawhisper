Broom Cluster Fig, scientifically known as Ficus sur, is a versatile medicinal tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its unique fruiting habit, it is deeply embedded in traditional healing. Various parts, including the bark and leaves, are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive disorders.
