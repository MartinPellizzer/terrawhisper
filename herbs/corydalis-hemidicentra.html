Corydalis hemidicentra

What's the taxonomical classification of Corydalis hemidicentra?

Corydalis hemidicecentra belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its hierarchical lineage, it is placed under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Ranunculales and is a member of the family Papaveraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Corydalis and the species name hemidicecentra.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

The plant typically produces these blooms on slender, upright stalks that emerge from the leafy base. This morphology is specialized to facilitate pollination by specific long-tongued insects.
