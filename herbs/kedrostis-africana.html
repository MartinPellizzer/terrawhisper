Kedrostis africana

Kedrostis africana, commonly known as the "African Kedrostis," is a resilient climbing shrub native to Southern Africa. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this plant is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and digestive issues, showcasing its significant role in ethnobotanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Kedrostis africana
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Kedrostis africana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Cucurbitales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cucurbitaceae and is categorized under the genus Kedrostis.
