Gentiana scabra, scientifically known as Gynochthodes officiinalis, is a vital medicinal herb highly valued in traditional Eastern medicine. Renowned for its bitter properties, it is primarily used to clear heat, detoxify the body, and support liver health. This potent botanical serves as a cornerstone in treating various inflammatory conditions.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the Asian continent. It is primarily found in the southern regions of China. Specifically, its presence is documented in the tropical province of Hainan. Furthermore, the species is also distributed across China Southeast. These locations represent the core habitats where the plant can be naturally located.
