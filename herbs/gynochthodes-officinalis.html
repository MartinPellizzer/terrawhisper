Gynochthodes officinalis

Gynochthodes officiinalis, commonly known as Andrographis paniculata or King of Bitters, is a potent medicinal herb revered in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its intense bitterness, this plant possesses exceptional anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and immune-boosting properties, making it a vital natural remedy for treating fevers, infections, and various respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Gynochthodes officinalis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Gentianaales and falls under the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is taxonomically identified within the genus Gynochthodes.
