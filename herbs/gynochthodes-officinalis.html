Gentiana scabra, scientifically known as Gynochthodes officiinalis, is a vital medicinal herb highly valued in traditional Eastern medicine. Renowned for its bitter properties, it is primarily used to clear heat, detoxify the body, and support liver health. This potent botanical serves as a cornerstone in treating various inflammatory conditions.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the Asian continent. It is primarily found in the southern regions of China.
