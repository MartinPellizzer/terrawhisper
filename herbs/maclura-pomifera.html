Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Osage orange, is characterized by its large, bumpy, and bright green fruits that hang from the branches. These spherical fruits, which can grow to be several inches in diameter, are often described as having a texture similar to a hedge apple. While the fruits are the most prominent part of the plant, they are famously unpalatable to humans and most animals, though they have historically been studied for various medicinal and insecticidal properties.
