Maclura pomifera

Maclurura pomifera, commonly known as Osage orange, is a distinctive North American tree valued for its dense, resilient wood. While its large, bumpy fruit is inedible to humans, it holds historical significance in traditional medicine for treating skin conditions and parasitic infections through its various bioactive chemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Maclura pomifera
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Europe
