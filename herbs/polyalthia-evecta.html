Polyalthia evecta

Polyalthia evecta, commonly known as the Evecta or Evergreen Polyalthia, is a versatile medicinal shrub celebrated in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its bioactive properties, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is frequently utilized to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and provide antioxidant benefits within various holistic healing practices.

  • Family: Annonaceae
