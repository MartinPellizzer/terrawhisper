Trigonella caerulea

Blue fenugreek

Trigonella caerulea, commonly known as blue fenugreek, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional wellness practices. Renowned for its nutritional density, this plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is frequently utilized to support metabolic health, aid digestion, and provide essential micronutrients for overall holistic vitality.

  • Scientific name: Trigonella caerulea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Trigonella.
