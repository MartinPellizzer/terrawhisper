Blue fenugreek, scientifically known as Trigonella caerulea, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued for its unique blue-tinted seeds. Primarily used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits. This versatile plant serves as a natural remedy for managing metabolic health and promoting overall wellness.
