Amaranthus caudatus

Love-lies-bleeding

Amaranthus caudatus, commonly known as Love Lies Bleeding, is a striking annual plant celebrated for its cascading, deep-red flower tassels. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this nutritious pseudo-cereal has been vital in traditional medicine and diets, offering significant antioxidant properties and essential nutrients to support overall human health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Amaranthus caudatus
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Amaranthus caudatus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Amaranthus.
