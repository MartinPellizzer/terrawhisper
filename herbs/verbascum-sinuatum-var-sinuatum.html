Verbascum sinuatum var. sinuatum

Verbascum sinuatuum var. sinuatum, commonly known as wavy mullein, is a distinctive perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its unique undulating leaf margins, this species is frequently utilized for its soothing properties, particularly in treating respiratory ailments and supporting overall wellness through its bioactive botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Verbascum sinuatum var. sinuatum
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic hierarchy concludes with the genus Verbascum.

Common Names

This plant, Verbascum sinuatum var. sinuatum, is known by various names across different regions and languages. In English, it is frequently referred to as sinuate mullein, while in French, it is called molène sinuée. In Spanish, the species is commonly called molène sinuada, and in German, it is identified as sinuative Traubenkräuter.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Middle Europe, its presence is recorded in Germany. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it can be found in Southwestern Europe within the Baleares islands. The species also inhabits the island of Corse and the mainland of France. Finally, its range extends further west to include Portugal.
