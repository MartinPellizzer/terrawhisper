What's the taxonomical classification of Gymnema yunnanense?

Gymnema yunnanense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Gentianaales and falls under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific placement within the genus Gymnema, with its unique identity established as the species yunnanense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Gymnema yunnanense has a perennial climbing woody vine structure characterized by opposite, ovate to elliptic leaves that are typically entire with acute or obtuse apices. The stems are twining and often exhibit a slightly pubescent texture during early growth stages. Small, axillary inflorescences produce delicate flowers that are typically pale or white in color. The plant develops small, ovoid fruits that contain multiple seeds essential for its reproductive cycle.
