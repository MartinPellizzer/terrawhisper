Paeonia anomala

Paeonia anomala, commonly known as the anomalous peony, is a resilient perennial native to the rocky terrains of Central and Eastern Europe. Renowned for its striking blooms, this medicinal plant contains bioactive compounds traditionally used to treat inflammation, soothe digestive issues, and support overall neurological health in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia anomala
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Paeoniaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Paeonia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast territories of Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. Within Eastern Europe, its range extends through both North European Russia and East European Russia. Moving eastward, the species is well-established across several key regions of Siberia. Specifically, it can be found in the Altay mountains and throughout the Buryatiya region. Furthermore, its geographical presence continues into the Irkutsk area of Siberia.
