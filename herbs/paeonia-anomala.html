Anomalous peony, scientifically known as Paeoonia anomala, is a rare perennial herb native to the mountain ranges of Central Asia. Renowned for its striking pink blossoms and medicinal properties, it is traditionally used in herbal remedies to treat inflammation, promote circulation, and support overall vitality through its bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast territories of Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. Within Eastern Europe, its range extends through both North European Russia and East European Russia. Moving eastward, the species is well-established across several key regions of Siberia. Specifically, it can be found in the Altay mountains and throughout the Buryatiya region. Furthermore, its geographical presence continues into the Irkutsk area of Siberia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Paeonia anomala, possesses several components that are of significant botanical and medicinal interest. The most vital part is the thick, fleshy root, which is often harvested for its bioactive compounds and traditional therapeutic uses. While the plant is primarily herbaceous, the woody structure of the older rootstock provides essential support and storage for nutrients. Additionally, the bark of the older, more lignified parts of the root system can be examined for its texture and chemical composition, contributing to the overall profile of this medicinal species.
