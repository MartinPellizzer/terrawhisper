Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions in South and Southeast Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is specifically noted in Sri Lanka. The species is also widely distributed throughout the Malesia region, encompassing several distinct island groups and peninsulas. Specifically, it can be found in Borneo, Jawa, and the Maluku islands. Additionally, its habitat extends to the Malay Peninsula within the Malesian territory.
