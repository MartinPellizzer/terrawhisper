Asclepias subulata

Asclepias subulata, commonly known as the subulate milkweed, is a resilient perennial native to the Americas. Renowned for its lance-shaped leaves and delicate clusters of fragrant, star-shaped flowers, this plant serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators and plays a crucial role in supporting specialized monarch butterfly larvae.

  • Scientific name: Asclepias subulata
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Gentianales, it is further categorized into the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Asclepia.
