What's the taxonomical classification of Asarum wulingense?

Asarum wulingense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Piperales. As a member of the family Aristolochiaceae, this plant is further categorized under the genus Asarum, with its specific identity defined by the species name wulingense.
