Typhonium trifoliatum

What's the taxonomical classification of Typhonium trifoliatum?

Typhonium trifoliatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Aliasmaetales, it is categorized under the family Araceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Typhonium, and its unique species designation, trifoliatum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Typhonium trifoliatum has a subterranean corm and trifoliate leaves consisting of three distinct leaflets that are typically ovate to lanceolate in shape. The leaf blades feature prominent veins and can vary in size depending on the maturity of the plant. The petioles are long and fleshy, supporting the leaf structure from the base of the corm. Its inflorescence is a spadix enclosed by a spathe, which is a characteristic feature of the Araceae family. The plant's overall morphology is adapted for growing in moist, shaded environments.
