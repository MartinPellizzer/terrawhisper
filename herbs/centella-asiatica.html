Centella asiatica

Centella asiatica, commonly known as Gotu Kola, is a perennial herb revered in traditional Ayurvedic and Chinese medicine. Renowned for its remarkable skin-healing and cognitive-enhancing properties, this botanical powerhouse supports collagen production and improves circulation. It remains a vital ingredient in modern dermatological and neurological wellness formulations worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Centella asiatica
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Centella.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. Within Macaronesia, its presence can be noted in Cape Verde. The species is also widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is found in nations such as Benin, Burkina, and Gambia. Additionally, the plant's range extends into Ghana within the same West Tropical African zone.
