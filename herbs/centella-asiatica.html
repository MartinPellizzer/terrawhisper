Indian pennywort

Centella asiatica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1600 studies) · ★★★★★

Indian pennywort, scientifically known as Centella asiatica, is a perennial herb revered in traditional Ayurvedic and Chinese medicine. Renowned for its remarkable skin-healing properties and cognitive-enhancing benefits, it contains active triterpenoids. Today, this versatile botanical is a cornerstone ingredient in modern dermatological formulations and neurological health supplements.

The species is also widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is found in nations such as Benin, Burkina, and Gambia. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Gotu Kola, is a perennial herb that thrives in moist environments. The most therapeutically significant part of the species is the kidney-shaped leaf, which is often found growing in dense clusters. Each individual leaves structure is characterized by prominent veins and crenate margins, serving as the primary site for bioactive compounds. While the plant features a creeping stem known as a stolon that allows it to spread across the ground, researchers also utilize plant callus cultures in laboratory settings to study and mass-produce its medicinal constituents.
