Salvia santolinifolia

Salvia santolinifolia, commonly known as a distinctive sage species, is a medicinal plant native to specific Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it possesses significant therapeutic potential. Researchers study its bioactive compounds for their antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, highlighting its importance in traditional herbal medicine and modern pharmacology.
