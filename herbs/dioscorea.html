Yam, scientifically known as Dioscorea, is a diverse genus of flowering plants valued globally for both nutrition and medicine. Rich in bioactive compounds like diosgenin, these tubers offer significant therapeutic potential, particularly in regulating hormones, managing diabetes, and reducing inflammation, making them essential in traditional and modern pharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dioscoreales. Finally, it is a member of the family Dioscoreaceae within the genus Dioscorea.
