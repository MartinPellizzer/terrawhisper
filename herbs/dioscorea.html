Dioscorea

Yam

Dioscorea, commonly known as yam, is a diverse genus of flowering plants prized for both nutrition and medicine. Beyond being a global staple food, many species contain bioactive compounds like diosgenin. These phytochemicals are essential in pharmacology for synthesizing hormones, making Dioscorea a vital resource in modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dioscoreales. Finally, it is a member of the family Dioscoreaceae within the genus Dioscorea.
