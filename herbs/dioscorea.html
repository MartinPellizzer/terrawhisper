Plant Parts

This plant, belonging to the genus Dioscorea, is characterized by its complex storage structures used to survive dormant periods. The most significant medicinal components are found in its tubers, which serve as the primary nutrient reservoirs. These underground and/or aerial tubers can vary in shape and size depending on the specific species, though they are most commonly found as subterranean rhizomes or thickened root-like structures that store starch and bioactive compounds. In addition to these fleshy parts, the plant contains essential oil within its tissues, which contributes to its distinct aromatic properties and therapeutic efficacy.
