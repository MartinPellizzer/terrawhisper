The reproduction of Sonchus oleraceus is characterized by a sexual, bisexual reproductive strategy, as asexual reproduction is absent in this species. The flowering process occurs within a seasonal window starting in June and concluding in October, featuring flowers with a brown coloration arranged in a raceme inflorescence.
