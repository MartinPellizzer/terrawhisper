Sonchus oleraceus

Sow thistle

Sonchus oleraceus, commonly known as common sow thistle, is a versatile herbaceous plant valued in traditional medicine. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and minerals, it is widely utilized for its anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and digestive properties. This resilient perennial offers significant therapeutic potential for supporting overall health and detoxification.

  • Scientific name: Sonchus oleraceus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Sonchus.

Common Names

This plant, Sonchus oleraceus, is known by various names across different linguistic regions, although its nomenclature remains consistent in several Chinese dialects. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans, zh-cn) and Traditional Chinese (zh-hant), it is referred to as 苦苣菜. This same common name, 苦苣菜, is also utilized in Hong Kong (zh-hk) and Singapore (zh-sg).

Distribution

This plant, known as Sonchus oleraceus, exhibits a specific distribution across the regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the coastal and inland landscapes of Denmark. The species is also documented throughout the territories of Finland and Norway. Furthermore, its presence extends to the islands of Great Britain and Ireland.
