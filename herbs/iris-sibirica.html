Iris sibirica

Iris sibirica, commonly known as the Siberian Iris, is a resilient perennial valued for its striking blue or violet blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significance in traditional medicine, often utilized for its soothing properties to treat skin inflammations and support digestive health through its specialized rhizomes.

  • Scientific name: Iris sibirica
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Iridaceae and the genus Iris.
