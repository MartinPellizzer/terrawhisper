Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Siberian iris, possesses distinct morphological features that are central to its botanical identity. The most significant medicinal and structural component is the rhizomes, which are thick, creeping underground stems that serve as the plant's primary storage organ for nutrients. Above the ground, the plant is characterized by its long, slender, and sword-shaped leaves, which grow in a dense, upright fashion and provide the necessary surface area for photosynthesis.
