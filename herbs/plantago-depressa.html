Depressed plantain, scientifically known as Plantago depressa, is a low-growing perennial herb native to various regions. Characterized by its creeping habit and small, elliptical leaves, this resilient plant is often utilized in traditional herbal medicine for its soothing properties, particularly in treating minor skin irritations and supporting digestive health.
