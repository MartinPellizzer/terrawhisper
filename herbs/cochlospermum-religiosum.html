Cochlospermum religiosum

Cochlospermum religiosum, commonly known as the Yellow Silk Cotton tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammation to digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Cochlospermum religiosum
  • Family: Bixaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malvales and the family Bixaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cochlospermum.
