Trifolium alpestre

Trifolium alpestre, commonly known as Alpine clover, is a resilient perennial herb native to high-altitude alpine ecosystems. Distinguished by its delicate white or pinkish flowers, this plant thrives in rocky, subalpine terrains. It holds significant ethnobotanical interest due to its unique phytochemical properties and potential therapeutic medicinal applications.
