Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide and diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple continents. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Moving to Southern Africa, it can be found across the Cape Provinces, KwaZulu-Natal, and the Northern Provinces. Additionally, the species is located within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the region of Assam. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various tropical and subtropical environments.
