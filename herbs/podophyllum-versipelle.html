Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Mayapple, derives its primary medicinal value from its underground structures. The most significant part used in pharmacology is the rhizomes, which are often referred to as the rhizoma in botanical texts. These fleshy, horizontal structures function as the plant's storage centers, and when processed for therapeutic use, they are identified as the radix of the species. While the aerial stem supports the large leaves and fruit, the medicinal potency is concentrated within these subterranean roots, making the extraction of the active alkaloids from the underground organs essential for its application.
