Podophyllum versipelle

Podophyllum versipellis, commonly known as Mayapple, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to North American woodlands. Renowned for its large, umbrella-like leaves and distinctive yellow flowers, it contains podophyllotoxin. This potent compound is medicinally significant, serving as a critical precursor in the synthesis of various anticancer chemotherapy drugs.

  • Scientific name: Podophyllum versipelle
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Berberidaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Podophyllum.
