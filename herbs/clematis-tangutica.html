Clematis tangutica

Clematis tangutica, commonly known as Tangut Clematis, is a perennial climbing plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its delicate, bell-shaped violet flowers, it adds elegance to gardens. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its roots are utilized for various therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Clematis tangutica
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Clematis tangutica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and falls under the genus Clematis.

Common Names

In Czech, the species is called plamének tangutský, and in Estonian, it is identified as tanguutia elulõng. Furthermore, in Finnish, the plant is commonly called kiinankeltakärhö.
