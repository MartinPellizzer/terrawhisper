Golden clematis, scientifically known as Clematis tangutica, is a stunning perennial prized for its vibrant, sulfur-yellow blooms. Native to East Asia, this resilient climber features delicate, star-shaped flowers and lush foliage. It is widely used in ornamental gardens to add texture, color, and elegance to climbing structures and landscapes.
