Cenostigma pyramidale

Cenostima pyramidale, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in African traditional medicine, holds great pharmacological potential. Primarily used to treat various ailments, its bioactive compounds offer promising therapeutic properties. Understanding its ethnobotanical uses and chemical composition is essential for developing future treatments for diverse human health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Cenostigma pyramidale
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, making it a member of the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Cenostiigma.
