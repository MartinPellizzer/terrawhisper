Sebastiania klotzschiana

Sebastiania klotzschiana, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, holds great pharmacological potential. Native to diverse ecosystems, this plant is valued for its bioactive compounds. Researchers study its traditional uses in treating inflammation and infections, exploring how its unique chemical profile contributes to modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Sebastiania klotzschiana
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, specifically as a member of the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sebastiania, specifically the species Sebastiania klotzschiana.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast landscapes of Western South America, specifically within the borders of Bolivia. In the country of Brazil, its presence is widespread across several distinct ecological regions. It can be found throughout the West-Central part of Brazil, spanning diverse terrains. Furthermore, the species occupies the Northeast and Southeast regions of Brazil. Finally, its geographical range extends into the southern reaches of Brazil as well.
