Scrophularia frutescens

Scrophularia frutescens, commonly known as the shrubby figwort, is a resilient perennial herb native to Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its distinctive architectural structure and small, tubular flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various species within this genus have been utilized in traditional medicine for their therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Scrophularia frutescens
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe
