Renowned for its antioxidant properties, various parts of the plant are utilized to support detoxification, promote circulation, and enhance overall wellness.

The life history of Phyllostachys edulis is characterized by a perennial lifecycle, allowing the plant to establish long-term dominance within its ecosystem through a persistent underground rhizome system. The morphology of Phyllostachys edulis is characterized by a woody, graminoid growth form that functions as a self-supporting structure rather than a climber or liana.
