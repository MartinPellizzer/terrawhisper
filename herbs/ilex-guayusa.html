Guausa, scientifically known as Ilex guayusa, is a highly revered medicinal plant native to the Amazon rainforest. Traditionally consumed as a stimulating tea, it is celebrated for its antioxidant properties and ability to enhance mental clarity. This potent botanical serves as a vital cultural cornerstone for many indigenous communities.
