buffalo-ear, scientifically known as Blumea balsamifera, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic, camphoraceous leaves, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive complaints. This potent plant serves as a vital natural remedy in various cultures.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Sambong, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties. The large, aromatic leaves are the most frequently used part in traditional treatments, particularly for their diuretic effects. Additionally, the young shoots and young leaves are often harvested due to their high concentration of essential oils and active compounds. For certain preparations, the young stems are also employed, while the more mature stems provide the structural support for the plant's dense, bushy growth.
