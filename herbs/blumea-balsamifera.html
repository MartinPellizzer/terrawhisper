buffalo-ear, scientifically known as Blumea balsamifera, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic, camphoraceous leaves, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive complaints. This potent plant serves as a vital natural remedy in various cultures.
