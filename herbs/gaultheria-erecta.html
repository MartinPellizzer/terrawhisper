Gaultheria erecta

Gaultheria erecta, commonly known as Eastern snowberry, is a resilient creeping subshrub native to North American woodlands. Characterized by its striking, waxy white berries, this plant thrives in acidic, moist soils. While primarily valued for its ornamental beauty, it plays a vital role in supporting local forest ecosystems.
