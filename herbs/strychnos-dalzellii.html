Scientifically known as Strychnos dalzellii, is a significant medicinal shrub native to the Western Ghats of India. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, particularly strychnine, this plant plays a vital role in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. It is extensively studied for its neurotoxic properties and its potential applications in pharmacology.
