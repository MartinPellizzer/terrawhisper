Hairy Bergenia, scientifically known as Bergenia ciliata, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude Himalayan regions. Characterized by its distinctive hairy leaves and robust rhizomes, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional Ayurveda for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties used to treat various ailments.
