Bergenia ciliata

Bergenia ciliata, commonly known as the hairy bergenia, is a perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant is rich in bioactive compounds like tannins and flavonoids. It is primarily used to treat ailments involving inflammation, kidney issues, and various gastrointestinal disorders.

  • Scientific name: Bergenia ciliata
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized under the order Saxifragales and the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Bergenia, specifically as the species Bergenia ciliata.
