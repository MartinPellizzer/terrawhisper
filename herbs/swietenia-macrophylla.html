Swietenia macrophylla

Big-leaf mahogany

Swietenia macrophylla, commonly known as the Chinese Mahogany, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This species plays a crucial role in herbal medicine and regional biodiversity conservation.

  • Scientific name: Swietenia macrophylla
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Sapindales and the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Swietenia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Swietenia macrophyllia, is recognized by various common names across different languages and regions. In Thailand, it is called มะฮอกกานีใบใหญ่, while in Chinese, it is referred to as 大叶桃花心木. In Portuguese-speaking areas, it is known as Mogno, and in Vietnam, it is called Nhạc ngựa (cây gỗ). Additionally, in Esperanto, the species is identified as Grandfolia svitenio.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found inhabiting the islands of Comoros and the Seychelles. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is present within the Indo-China region, specifically located around the Andaman Islands. Finally, its presence reaches into Eastern Asia, with populations established in Taiwan.
