Vaccinium chengiae belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Ericales, the plant is placed in the family Ericaceae and falls under the genus Vaccinium. Finally, it is identified by its specific species name, chengiae.
