Thunbergia erecta, scientifically known as Thunbergia erecta, is a captivating evergreen shrub prized for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped purple blooms. Native to tropical Africa, this ornamental plant thrives in warm climates, offering lush foliage and striking floral displays that make it a stunning addition to any medicinal or decorative garden.

Distribution

This plant is native to several regions within West Tropical Africa. Its geographical range specifically includes the country of Ghana. Additionally, it can be found growing in Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. The distribution extends further into the Ivory Coast as well. Finally, this medicinal species is also documented within the borders of Liberia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as King's Mantle, is highly valued for its ornamental beauty, which is primarily derived from its striking flower. The blossoms are trumpet-shaped and typically feature a deep violet or royal purple color, often accented by a pale or white throat that attracts pollinators. These vibrant blooms are produced in profusion throughout much of the growing season, creating a lush canopy of color that makes the plant a centerpiece in many tropical gardens.
