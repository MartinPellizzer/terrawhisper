Dryobalanops aromatica

Indonesian kapur

Dryobalanops aromatica, commonly known as the aromatic olopan, is a significant medicinal tree native to the tropical forests of South Asia. Renowned for its resinous bark and aromatic properties, it is extensively utilized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and various inflammatory conditions.
