Crepis

Crepis, commonly known as hawksbeards, belongs to the Asteraceae family. These versatile herbaceous plants are characterized by their bright yellow, dandelion-like flowers and distinctive seed heads. While primarily studied in botanical research, various species hold potential in traditional medicine for their diverse bioactive compounds and antioxidant properties.
