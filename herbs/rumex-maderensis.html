Rumex maderensis

Rumex maderensis, commonly known as Maderen Dock, is a resilient perennial herb native to mountainous regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, its leaves and roots contain bioactive compounds with potential therapeutic properties. This plant serves as a fascinating subject for studying natural remedies and the diverse medicinal flora of high-altitude ecosystems.
