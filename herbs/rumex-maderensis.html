Madeira sorrel, scientifically known as Rumex maderensis, is a perennial herb native to the Macaronesian islands. Renowned for its distinctive tart flavor, it contains significant amounts of oxalic acid. Traditionally used in regional culinary practices, this plant serves as both a nutritious edible green and a botanical curiosity.
