Ancistrocladus tectorius

Ancistrocladus tectorius, commonly known as the climbing shrub, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments. Research explores its bioactive compounds, highlighting its potential role in developing natural treatments for diverse health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Ancistrocladus tectorius
  • Family: Ancistrocladaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
