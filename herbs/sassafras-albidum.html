Sassafras albidum

Sassafras officinale

Sassafras albium, commonly known as white sassafras, is a deciduous shrub prized for its aromatic properties. Native to various subtropical regions, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory qualities. Its fragrant leaves and roots offer unique therapeutic potential in various holistic healing practices.
