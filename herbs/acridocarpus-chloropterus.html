Acridocarpus chloropterus

Acridocarpus chloropterus, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds great pharmacological interest. Belonging to the Rhizophoraceae family, this species is valued for its bioactive compounds. Traditional healers utilize its extracts to treat various ailments, offering a promising natural resource for modern drug discovery and development.
