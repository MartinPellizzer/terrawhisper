Pinus halepensis

Pinus halepensis, commonly known as the Aleppo pine, is a resilient evergreen tree native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its adaptability to arid climates, it holds significant medicinal value. Its resin, needles, and bark are utilized in traditional remedies to support respiratory health and provide antimicrobial benefits.

  • Scientific name: Pinus halepensis
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Pinus halepensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is further organized into the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Pinus.

Distribution

This plant, known as Pinus halepensis, is widely distributed across several regions in Southwestern Europe. Its presence is notably documented within the Balearic Islands, which form part of its Mediterranean range. The species also thrives in the coastal and mountainous landscapes of Corsica. Furthermore, it can be found growing throughout various territories in mainland France. Finally, the plant's geographical reach extends to the island of Sardinia and the coastal areas of Portugal.
