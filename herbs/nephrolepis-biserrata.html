Large Sword Fern, scientifically known as Nephrolepis biserrata, is a versatile tropical plant valued in traditional medicine. Often used for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, it plays a significant role in treating skin ailments and respiratory issues. This hardy fern remains a vital resource in various ethnobotanical healing practices.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, falling into the family Polypodiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Nephrolepis.

Distribution

This plant, known as Nephrolepis biserrata, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Burkina. Furthermore, the species is documented to inhabit various areas of Ghana. Its presence extends to the territory of Guinea as well. Finally, this medicinal plant is also located in Ivory Coast and Liberia.
