Phoebe bournei

Phoebe bournei, commonly known as the Bourne's laurel, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments. Scientific interest continues to grow as researchers explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Phoebe bournei
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Laurales, and the family Lauraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Phoebe as Phoebe bournei.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Phoebe bournei, exhibits a specific geographical range within East Asia. Its distribution is primarily centered within the various regions of China. Specifically, it can be found growing in the South-Central part of the country. The species is also documented to inhabit the coastal areas of China Southeast. Additionally, its presence extends to the tropical island province of Hainan.
