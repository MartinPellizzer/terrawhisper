Baptisia australis

Baptisia australis, commonly known as Blue False Indigo, is a striking perennial native to North American prairies. Distinguished by its deep blue pea-like flowers and architectural foliage, this hardy legume improves soil health through nitrogen fixation, making it a resilient, multifunctional addition to any sustainable medicinal or pollinator garden.

  • Scientific name: Baptisia australis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is identified by the genus Baptisia.
