Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Blue False Indigo, is most valued for its underground structures, specifically its large, tuberous roots. These roots serve as the primary storage organs for the plant, allowing it to survive harsh conditions while concentrating various bioactive compounds. Historically, these roots have been the central focus of medicinal interest, as they contain alkaloids and other constituents that have been utilized in traditional herbal practices.
