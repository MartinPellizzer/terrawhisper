Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several regions across the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be identified within the borders of Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found in both Germany and the territories of Czechia-Slovakia. The distribution extends further south to Southwestern Europe, where it inhabits France. Additionally, this medicinal plant is located on the island of Corse within the same southwestern region.
