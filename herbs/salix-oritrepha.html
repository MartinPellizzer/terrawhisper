Salix oritrepha

Salix oritrepha, commonly known as the willow species, is a medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in salicin, it has historically been utilized to alleviate pain and reduce inflammation. This resilient shrub serves as a vital natural resource in traditional herbal medicine for treating various systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Salix oritrepha
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Malpighiales and the family Salicaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Salix.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of China. It can be found growing within the landscapes of China South-Central and China North-Central. Furthermore, its range extends into the northern territories of Inner Mongolia. The species is also documented to inhabit the high-altitude areas of Qinghai. Finally, its presence is noted throughout the vast plateau of Tibet.
