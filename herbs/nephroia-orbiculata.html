This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning both the Indian Ocean and various regions of East Asia. Within the Western Indian Ocean, its presence is specifically noted on the island of Réunion. In the vast territory of China, it can be found across multiple distinct climatic zones. Specifically, its range includes the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast regions of the country. Additionally, the species is documented to grow on the tropical island of Hainan.
