Nephroia orbiculata

Nephroia orbiculata, commonly known as the Rounded Nephroi, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specialized microclimates, this species contains potent bioactive compounds used traditionally to support kidney health and alleviate systemic inflammation, offering a fascinating subject for modern pharmacological research and study.

  • Scientific name: Nephroia orbiculata
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Menispermaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Nephroia.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning both the Indian Ocean and various regions of East Asia.
