Lathraea squamaria

Lathraea squamaria, commonly known as toothwort, is a unique holoparasitic plant that lacks chlorophyll, relying entirely on host trees for nutrients. Found in shaded woodlands, this fascinating species is studied for its distinctive biology and potential medicinal properties, offering intriguing insights into parasitic plant interactions within forest ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Lathraea squamaria
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls under the family Orobanchaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is completed by its placement in the genus Lathraea.
