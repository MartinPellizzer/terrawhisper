Clerodendrum serratum

What's the taxonomical classification of Clerodendrum serratum?

Clerodendrum serratum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Lamiales and is a member of the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Clerodendrum and is specifically designated as the species serratum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Clerodendrum serratum has an erect, woody shrubby habit characterized by opposite, ovate leaves with sharply serrated margins and a pubescent texture. The stems are often square in cross-section and covered in fine, soft hairs that give the plant a velvety feel. Its small, tubular flowers are typically white or pale lilac and are arranged in dense, terminal clusters. These blossoms emerge from a calyx that is similarly hairy and structurally prominent. The resulting fruit consists of small, fleshy drupes that transition in color as they mature.

According to a study published by "Microbial pathogenesis", the leaf extract of Clerodendrum serratum is used for its antimicrobial potential and anti-biofilm efficacy. This plant offers a rich source of bioactive compounds that target three Mycobacterium species, specifically M. smegmatis, M. fortuitum, and M. marinum. The anti-biofilm disruption of the extract was evaluated against pre-formed mature biofilms using the crystal violet microtiter plate assay and Atomic Force Microscopy. In silico analysis of five major phytoconstituents, including Hispidulin, Luteolin, Salvigenin, Pectolinarigenin, and Uncinatone, revealed strong binding affinities. These interactions with targets such as FadD32, InhA, and MmpL3 showed docking scores of up to -11.0 kcal/mol.

According to a study published by "Frontiers in pharmacology", Clerodendrum serratum possesses unique anti-inflammatoy and anti-arthritic properties. HPTLC analysis revealed the presence of ursolic acid with an Rf value of 0.38 and a quantified amount of 0.03% w/w. Further HPLC analysis showed an assay content for ursolic acid of 0.059% with a relative standard deviation of less than 2.5% and spike recovery between 95-110%. The aqueous extract exhibited COX-2 and TNF-alpha inhibition with significant results for both parameters where p < 0.0001. This plant extract showed reclamation of arthritis regarding paw edema, arthritic score, joint diameter, and body weight. Additionally, the plant extract exhibited in-vitro anti-inflammatoy activity.

What medicinal compounds this plant contains?

These diverse phytochemicals contribute to the significant therapeutic potential observed in Clerodendrum serratum extracts. The presence of salvigenin and uncinaone further enhances the bioactive profile of the species. Additionally, the saponins found within the plant contribute to its various biological activities and traditional medicinal uses.

According to a study published by "Microbial pathogenesis", Clerodendrum serratum leaf extract contains five major phytoconstituent compounds including Hispidulin, Luteolin, Salvigenin, Pectolinarigenin, and Uncinaone.
