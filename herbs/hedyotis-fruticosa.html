Hedyotis fruticosa

Hedyotis fruticosa, commonly known as the Indian Hedyotis, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and fever, this resilient plant offers promising therapeutic potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Hedyotis fruticosa
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several key regions within the Asian continent. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is commonly found growing in India. Its presence is also well-documented across the landscape of Sri Lanka. Moving further east into the Indo-China region, the species extends into Myanmar.
