Gardneria multiflora

What's the taxonomical classification of Gardneria multiflora?

Gardneria multiflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Gentianales and into the family Loganiaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Gardneria, and its unique species designation, multiflora.
