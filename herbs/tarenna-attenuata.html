Tarenna attenuata

Tarenna attenuata, commonly known as the slender tarenn, is a significant medicinal plant found across tropical Asia. Valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, it possesses diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits. This versatile species plays a crucial role in treating various ailments, offering profound potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Tarenna attenuata
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, it is specifically positioned within the Rubiaceae family. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tarenna, specifically the species Tarenna attenuata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found specifically in Assam. Its distribution also extends into the Indo-China region, where it is documented in Cambodia.
