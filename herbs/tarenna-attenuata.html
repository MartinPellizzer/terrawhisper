Tarenna attenuata

What's the taxonomical classification of Tarenna attenuata?

Tarenna attenuata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Gentianaales. This plant is a member of the family Rubiaceae, which is the specific group to which its genus, Tarenna, is assigned. Ultimately, the unique identity of this organism is defined by its species designation, attenuata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Tarenna attenuata has a woody climbing habit characterized by slender, twining stems and lanceolate to elliptic leaves with entire margins. The foliage typically displays an acute apex and a cuneate base, often arranged in an opposite or sub-opposite pattern along the branches. Its inflorescences consist of small, inconspicuous flowers that emerge from the leaf axils. The plant's reproductive structures eventually produce small, fleshy fruits that serve as a key identification feature.
