Allium paradoxum

Allium paradoxum, commonly known as the unexpected onion, is a perennial bulbous plant native to parts of Asia. Characterized by its distinctive nodding white flowers and edible, onion-like scent, it thrives in diverse habitats. While visually striking, its invasive potential requires careful management to protect local plant biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Allium paradoxum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaryllidaceae and falls under the genus Allium.
