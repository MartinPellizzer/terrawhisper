Dryopteris lepidopoda

What's the taxonomical classification of Dryopteris lepidopoda?

Dryopteris lepidopoda belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it is situated under the subclass Polypodiinae and falls within the order Polypodiales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Polypodiaceae, placing it in the genus Dryopteris, with its specific identification being the species lepidopoda.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dryopteris lepidopoda has large, bipinnate-pinnatifid fronds that are typically lanceolate in shape and characterized by a distinctively leathery texture. Each pinna is deeply lobed with serrated margins that contribute to a complex, feathery silhouette. The underside of the blades features prominent sori arranged in rows along the veins, often protected by specialized indusia.
