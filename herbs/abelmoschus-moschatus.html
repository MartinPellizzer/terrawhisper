Ambrette

Abelmoschus moschatus, commonly known as musk mallow, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its aromatic properties. Belonging to the Malvaceae family, it features distinctive fragrant flowers. Traditionally used in various herbal remedies, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly for its soothing effects and unique bioactive compounds.
