Ambrette, scientifically known as Abelmoschus moschatus, is a fragrant annual herb prized for its aromatic seeds. Often called "musk mallow," it produces a natural plant-based musk used extensively in high-end perfumery. Beyond its olfactory appeal, this versatile botanical holds significant value in traditional medicine and organic fragrance industries.
