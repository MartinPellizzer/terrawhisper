Grindelia squarrosa

Grindelia squarrosa, commonly known as gumweed, is a resilient perennial herb native to North American prairies. Renowned for its sticky, resinous foliage, this plant has long been utilized in traditional medicine. It is primarily valued for its potent expectorant properties, helping to soothe respiratory ailments and ease persistent coughing.

  • Scientific name: Grindelia squarrosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
