Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as gumweed, is utilized in traditional medicine primarily through its aerial parts. These above-ground structures, which include the stems, leaves, and flowering heads, are harvested to utilize the plant's characteristic sticky, resinous secretions. The aerial parts are often prepared as teas, tinctures, or decoctions to help soothe respiratory issues and support the immune system.
