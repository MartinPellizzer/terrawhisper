Citharexylum flexuosum

Citharexylum flexuosum, commonly known as the Jamaica Curat, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its fragrant flowers and dense foliage, this plant offers various therapeutic benefits, including antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a significant subject of study in ethnobotanical research and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Citharexylum flexuosum
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Citharexylum.
