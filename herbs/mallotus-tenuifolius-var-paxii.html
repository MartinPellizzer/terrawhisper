Mallotus tenuifolius var. paxii

Mallotus tenuifolius var. paxii, commonly known as a unique medicinal variant, is a significant botanical specimen valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its distinct morphological features, this plant offers diverse therapeutic potential. Research continues to explore its bioactive compounds for developing novel treatments in modern pharmacological applications.

  • Family: Euphorbiaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, taxonomically identified as Mallotus tenuifolius var. paxii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. It is further classified under the genus Mallotus.

This plant, known scientifically as Mallotus tenuifolius var. paxii, is referred to by several common names depending on the linguistic context. In Chinese, it is called 红叶野桐 (zh), which is also the specific designation used in mainland China (zh-cn) and in simplified Chinese script (zh-hans). In Taiwan, the name is written in traditional characters as 紅葉野桐 (zh-tw).
