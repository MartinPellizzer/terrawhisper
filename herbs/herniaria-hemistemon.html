Herniaria hemistemon

Herniaria hemistemon, commonly known as the "joint-repair herb," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat urinary tract issues, kidney stones, and inflammatory conditions. This potent botanical offers significant potential in holistic approaches to managing various ailments.

  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa
