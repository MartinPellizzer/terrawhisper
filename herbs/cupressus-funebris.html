Chinese Weeping Cypress, scientifically known as Cupressus funebris, is a graceful evergreen prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its pendulous foliage, this plant holds significant medicinal value, particularly in treating respiratory ailments and inflammation. Its unique botanical properties make it a vital component in various holistic healing traditions.
