Distephanus angulifolius

Distephanus anguifolius, commonly known as the Bitter Leaf, is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including malaria, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern healthcare applications.

  • Scientific name: Distephanus angulifolius
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it falls under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Distephanus, specifically as the species Distephanus anguifolius.
