Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. Within the Macaronesia archipelago, it can be found growing in Madeira. Moving to Northeast Tropical Africa, the species is documented in Ethiopia. Its range also extends across East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits both Kenya and Tanzania. Finally, the plant is recorded in the southern reaches of the continent within Malawi.
