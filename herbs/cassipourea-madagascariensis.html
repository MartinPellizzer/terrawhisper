Cassipourea madagascariensis

Cassipourea madagascariensis, commonly known as a vital medicinal plant in Madagascar, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices to treat various ailments. Understanding its chemical composition and pharmacological potential is essential for advancing modern natural medicine.
