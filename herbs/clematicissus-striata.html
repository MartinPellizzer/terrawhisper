Clematicissus striata

Clematissus striata, commonly known as the striped clematis, is a versatile medicinal climbing shrub native to parts of Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive ailments, and skin irritations, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Clematicissus striata
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Vitales and the family Vitaceae, it is classified under the genus Clematissus.
