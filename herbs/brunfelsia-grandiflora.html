Brunfelsia grandiflora

Brunfelsia grandiflora, commonly known as Yesterday-Today-and-Tomorrow, is a stunning evergreen shrub prized for its color-changing blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. While its flowers transition from violet to lavender and white, its chemical properties are studied for potential medicinal and toxicological applications.

  • Scientific name: Brunfelsia grandiflora
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Brunfelsia.

Common Names

This plant is known by various names depending on the region and language, including Брунфельсия крупноцветковая in Russian and Stambiažiedė brunfelsija in Lithuanian. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several different continents. In the Western Indian Ocean, its presence can be found within the island of Madagascar. Moving to the Americas, it is located in Northern South America specifically in Venezuela. Its range also extends significantly through Western South America. In this western region, the species is documented in Bolivia, Colombia, and Ecuador.
