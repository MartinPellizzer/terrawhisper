Dendrobium devonianum

Dendrobium devonianum, commonly known as the Devonian Dendrobium, is a captivating epiphytic orchid native to Southeast Asia. Beyond its striking, fragrant blooms, this resilient plant holds significant traditional value. It is frequently utilized in various herbal practices, valued for its unique bioactive properties and potential medicinal applications in wellness.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium devonianum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the orchid family, Orchidaceae, falling under the genus Dendrobium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central areas to the Southeast. Its presence also extends northward into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving towards the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in the state of Assam. Furthermore, it inhabits the mountainous terrain of the East Himalaya.
