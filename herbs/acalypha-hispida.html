Acalypha hispida

Chenille plant

Acalypha hispida, commonly known as caterpillars bushy plant, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, it is frequently used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and respiratory conditions. This plant offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Acalypha hispida
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Acalypha.
