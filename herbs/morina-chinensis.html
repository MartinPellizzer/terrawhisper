Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Morinda chinensis (often referred to in various texts as Morina chinensis), possesses medicinal properties that are frequently derived from its various components, most notably its roots. The roots of this species are highly valued in traditional herbal medicine for their bioactive compounds, which are often utilized to treat various ailments ranging from inflammatory conditions to digestive issues. By extracting the essential elements from these underground structures, practitioners can harness the plant's therapeutic potential to support systemic health.
