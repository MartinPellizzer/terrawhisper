Anoectochilus roxburghii

Roxburgh's jewel orchid

Anoectochilus roxburgii, commonly known as the Jewel Orchid, is a prized medicinal plant native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its striking foliage and therapeutic properties, it is used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, improve circulation, and boost vitality, making it a highly sought-after species in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Anoectochilus roxburghii
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Asparagales, falling into the family Orchidaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Anoectochilus.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Anoectochilus roxburgii, is widely recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is known as Roxburgh's Jewel Orchid, while in Chinese, it is commonly referred to as 金线兰 (jīn xiàn lán) or 金线莲 (jīn xiàn lián). Specifically, the names used in different writing systems include 金线兰 (zh), 金线兰 (zh-cn), and 金線蘭 (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across various regions of East and South Asia. Within China, its distribution spans from the South-Central provinces to the tropical island of Hainan. It is also documented occurring throughout the China Southeast region and the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found specifically in the state of Assam. Together, these locations highlight a broad ecological range for Anoectochilus roxburgii.
