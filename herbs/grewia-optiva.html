Plant Parts

This plant, Grewia optiva, commonly known as Bhimal, possesses various parts that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The stem bark is often employed in traditional medicine for its astringent qualities and is frequently used to treat inflammatory conditions. The roots are highly valued for their ability to act as a restorative tonic and are sometimes processed to alleviate digestive ailments. Additionally, the leaves are widely used in various preparations to manage skin issues and promote healing, making the entire botanical structure a significant resource in herbal medicine.
