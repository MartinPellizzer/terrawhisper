Grewia optiva

Grewia optiva, commonly known as Bhimji, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is extensively used in traditional Ayurveda to treat ailments like inflammation and skin disorders. Additionally, its bark and leaves are vital for producing high-quality, eco-friendly mucilage.

  • Scientific name: Grewia optiva
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Grewia and the specific species name optiva.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Grewia optiva, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context.
