Black cardamom, scientifically known as Amomum subulatum, is a pungent, aromatic spice native to the eastern Himalayas. Renowned for its smoky flavor profile, it is widely used in culinary traditions and traditional medicine to aid digestion, reduce inflammation, and alleviate respiratory issues, making it a vital botanical treasure.

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Black cardamom, is a perennial herb characterized by its distinctive anatomy used in traditional medicine. The most significant medicinal components are the dried fruits, which are large, fibrous capsules that house small, dark seeds known for their intense aroma and therapeutic properties. Additionally, the plant produces robust rhizomes that grow underground, often utilized in various herbal preparations for their unique bioactive compounds.
