Amomum subulatum

Black cardamom

Amomum subulatum, commonly known as a species of cardamom, is a medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Valued in traditional medicine, its aromatic rhizomes possess potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and digestive properties. This botanical treasure is widely utilized to treat gastrointestinal ailments and enhance overall metabolic wellness naturally.

  • Scientific name: Amomum subulatum
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, it is a member of the Zingiberaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Amomum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh.
