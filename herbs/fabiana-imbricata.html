Fabiana imbricata

Fabiana imbricata, commonly known as Fabiana, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude regions of South America. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat respiratory ailments and inflammatory conditions, offering valuable bioactive compounds that support holistic wellness and traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Fabiana imbricata
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Solanales, and the family Solanaceae. Finally, the species is identified by the genus Fabiana.
