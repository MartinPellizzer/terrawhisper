Piper amalago

Piper amalago, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in tropical regions, holds significant value in traditional ethnobotany. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and skin inflammations, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
