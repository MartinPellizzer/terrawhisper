Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several distinct Mediterranean regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing within the territory of Spain. Its presence extends into Southeastern Europe, where it inhabits both Italy and the island of Kriti. Moving toward the southern coast, the species is also located in Northern Africa, specifically in Tunisia. Finally, its range reaches the Macaronesia region, encompassing the Canary Is.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Catharanthus roseus, contains various parts that are studied for their pharmacological properties. The leaf is perhaps the most significant component, as it serves as the primary source for extracting potent alkaloids used in modern medicine. While the stem provides the structural support necessary for the plant's growth and helps transport nutrients throughout the organism, the root system functions to anchor the plant and absorb essential minerals from the soil. Together, these parts contribute to the complex chemical profile that makes this species a vital subject in medicinal research.
