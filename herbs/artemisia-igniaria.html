Artemisia igniaria

Artemisia igniaria, commonly known as a resilient member of the wormwood family, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing practices. Native to specific arid regions, this plant possesses unique phytochemical properties. Researchers study its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in treating various inflammatory and microbial conditions.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia igniaria
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artemisia and the specific species name Artemisia igniaria.
