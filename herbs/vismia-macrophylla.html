Vismia macrophylla

Vismia macrophylla, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. Scientific research continues to explore its potential for developing novel therapeutic agents.

  • Scientific name: Vismia macrophylla
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Vismia, specifically as the species Vismia macrophylla.
