Trollius chinensis

Trollius chinensis, commonly known as Chinese globeflower, is a striking perennial herb prized for its vibrant, ball-shaped yellow blooms. Native to East Asian montane regions, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its cooling properties, frequently used to address inflammation and various internal ailments in herbal formulations.

  • Scientific name: Trollius chinensis
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Trollius chinensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Trollius.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions of Northern Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found growing in Buryatiya, Chita, and Krasnoyarsk. Its range also extends into the Russian Far East, where it inhabits specific territories. Specifically, populations are documented within the Amur region. Furthermore, the species is also present in the Khabarovsk area.
