Stemona pierrei

Stemona pierrei, commonly known as Stemona, is a significant medicinal herb used extensively in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, this plant is primarily utilized to treat respiratory ailments, soothe coughs, and combat parasitic infections. Its bioactive compounds offer valuable therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Stemona pierrei
  • Family: Stemonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Pandanales. Finally, it resides in the family Stemonaceae under the genus Stemona.
