Uvaria narum

Uvaria narum, commonly known as a member of the Annonaceae family, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This plant serves as a vital resource in treating various ailments within indigenous medicine.

  • Scientific name: Uvaria narum
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Uvaria narum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is further categorized into the family Annonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Uvaria.
