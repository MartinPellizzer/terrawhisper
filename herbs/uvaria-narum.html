South-Indian Uvaria, scientifically known as Uvaria narum, is a climbing shrub prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Native to tropical regions, this plant features delicate flowers and nutritious fruits. It is widely utilized for its potent bioactive compounds, offering therapeutic benefits for digestive health, inflammation, and various skin conditions.
