Alpinia globosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Alpinia globosa?

Alpinia globosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is positioned in the order Zingiberales. It is a member of the Zingiberaceae family, which places it within the genus Alpinia, and its specific biological identity is defined by the species name globosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Alpinia globosa has large, lanceolate leaves that emerge from thick, fleshy rhizomes and terminal inflorescences bearing globose, bright red bracts. Each inflorescence features a dense cluster of flowers protected by these distinctive, rounded bracts that provide a striking visual contrast. The individual flowers are often smaller and more delicate compared to the prominent, spherical structures they emerge from.
