Lycopus

Lycopus, commonly known as Water Horehound, is a versatile perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Often found in wetland habitats, this resilient plant is celebrated for its cooling properties. It is frequently utilized to support digestive health, balance hormonal fluctuations, and soothe inflammation, offering a natural approach to wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lycopus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Lycopus.
