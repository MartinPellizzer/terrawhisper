Onobrychis viciifolia

Onobrychis viciifolia, commonly known as the fern-leaved umbellifer, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific Mediterranean regions. Renowned for its complex, finely divided foliage, this herbaceous perennial is valued in traditional herbalism. It contains bioactive compounds studied for their potential therapeutic properties in treating various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Onobrychis viciifolia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant, known scientifically as Onobrychis viciifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
