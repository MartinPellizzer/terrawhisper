What's the taxonomical classification of Potentilla fragarioides?

Potentiella fragarioides belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Rosales. It is further categorized under the family Rosaceae, which places it among a diverse group of flowering plants. Within this family, it is identified by the genus Potentiella, specifically designated as the species fragarioides.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Potentiella fragarioides has a low-growing, rhizomatous habit characterized by trifoliate leaves that resemble those of a strawberry, featuring three leaflets with serrated margins and distinctively veined surfaces. The foliage is typically covered in fine, soft hairs that provide a textured appearance to the plant. Its stems are relatively short and often spread across the ground through creeping rhizomes. The plant produces small, five-petaled flowers that are usually bright yellow in color. These blossoms sit atop slender stalks that rise slightly above the dense carpet of leaves.
