Skimmia arborescens

Skimmia arborescens, commonly known as the Japanese Skimmia, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Often used in traditional practices, its leaves and stems offer various therapeutic properties. This resilient plant thrives in shaded environments, adding lush texture and delicate seasonal charm to gardens.

  • Scientific name: Skimmia arborescens
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Sapindaales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Skimmia.

Common Names

This plant, Skimmia arborescens, is known by various names depending on the linguistic context. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central and Southeast territories, as well as in the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is specifically noted in the Assam region. Additionally, it thrives within the East Himalaya mountains. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to inhabit various mountainous and subtropical environments.
