American barberry, scientifically known as Berberis canadensis, is a deciduous shrub native to North America. Renowned for its bright yellow flowers and tart, edible berries, it holds significant medicinal value. Containing the alkaloid berberine, this plant is traditionally used to support digestive health and combat various inflammatory conditions.
