Berberis canadensis

American barberry

Berberis canadensis, commonly known as American Barberry, is a deciduous shrub native to North America. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots and stems contain berberine, a potent alkaloid. This plant is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits, making it a staple in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Berberis canadensis
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Berberidaceae and falls under the genus Berberis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Berberis canadensis, is recognized by various common names depending on the language and region. In Russian, it is called Барбарис канадский, while in German, it is referred to as Kanadische Berberitze. Its Bulgarian name is канадски кисел трън, and in Persian, it is known as زرشک کانادایی. Additionally, in Azerbaijani, the plant is called Kanada zirinci.

Distribution

This plant, known as Berberis canadensis, exhibits a specific range across several regions of the United States. In the North-Central U.S.A., its presence is documented in the states of Illinois and Missouri. Moving toward the Northeastern U.S.A., the species can also be found growing in Indiana. Additionally, its distribution extends into the Pennsylvania region within the Northeast. Finally, the plant is recorded as part of the natural flora in West Virginia.
