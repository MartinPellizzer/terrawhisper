Rhododendron latoucheae

Rhododendron latoucheae, commonly known as the Latouche's rhododendron, is a striking evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude forests of China. Renowned for its vibrant, bell-shaped blossoms, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in regional ethnobotany for its unique bioactive properties and its role in various holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron latoucheae
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Ericaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Rhododendron.
