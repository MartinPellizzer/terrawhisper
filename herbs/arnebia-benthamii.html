Arnebia benthamii, scientifically known as Arnebia benthamii, is a perennial herb belonging to the Boraginaceae family. Native to the mountainous regions of Central Asia, it is highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its deep red pigments, the plant is primarily utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties.
