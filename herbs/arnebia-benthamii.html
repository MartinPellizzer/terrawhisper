Arnebia benthamii

Arnebia benthamii, commonly known as Boraginaceae or "Arnebia," is a medicinal herb native to the mountainous regions of Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly shikonin, this plant is traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat skin inflammations, promote wound healing, and address various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Arnebia benthamii
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Boraginales, and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Arnebia.
