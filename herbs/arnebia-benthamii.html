Arnebia benthamii, scientifically known as Arnebia benthamii, is a perennial herb belonging to the Boraginaceae family. Native to the mountainous regions of Central Asia, it is highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its deep red pigments, the plant is primarily utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties.

Distribution

This plant, known as Arnebia benthamii, is primarily distributed throughout the Indian Subcontinent. Within this vast region, it can be found growing in the mountainous terrains of Nepal. It also has a significant presence in the diverse landscapes of Pakistan. Furthermore, the species is widely documented across the West Himalaya region.
