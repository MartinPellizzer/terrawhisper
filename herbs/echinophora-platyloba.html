Echinophora platyloba

Echinophora platyloba, commonly known as the Chinese Echinophora, is a significant medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, this plant is frequently utilized to support immune function and treat various ailments, offering a natural approach to promoting holistic wellness and systemic health.

  • Scientific name: Echinophora platyloba
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae and the genus Echinophora.

Common Names

This plant, Echinophora platyloba, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the broad-leaved echinophora, while in Chinese, it is known as 宽叶 Echinophora (kuān yè echinophora). In Korean, the plant is called 넓은잎 에키노포라 (neolbeunip ekinopora), and in Japanese, it is referred to as コマチノエキノフォラ (komachino ekinofora).
