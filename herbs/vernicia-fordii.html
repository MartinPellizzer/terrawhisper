Chinese wood-oil-tree

Vernicia fordii · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (135 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Chinese wood-oil-tree, scientifically known as Vernicia fordii, is a significant medicinal and industrial plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its oil-rich seeds, it serves as a vital source of tung oil. Beyond industrial use, its bioactive compounds offer therapeutic potential in traditional medicine for various healing applications.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across both African and Asian continents. In South Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving to Asia, the species is widely distributed throughout various regions of China. Specifically, it inhabits the areas of China North-Central and China South-Central. Additionally, its range extends to the tropical island province of Hainan.
