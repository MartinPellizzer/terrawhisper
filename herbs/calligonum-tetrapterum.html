Calligonum tetrapterum

Calligonum tetrapterum, commonly known as a desert shrub, is a resilient medicinal plant found in arid regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used in various folk medicines to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions. Its unique ability to thrive in harsh environments makes it biologically significant.

  • Scientific name: Calligonum tetrapterum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Calligonum tetrapterum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae and the genus Calligonum.

Common Names

This plant, Calligonum tetrapterum, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is often referred to as the four-winged calligonum, while in Arabic, it is commonly called al-shura or al-shuraa.
