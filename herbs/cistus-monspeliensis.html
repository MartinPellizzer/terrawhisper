Montpellier Cistus, scientifically known as Cistus monspeliensis, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant contains essential oils and polyphenols. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits, supporting skin health and overall wellness.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions of Southwestern Europe. Its distribution includes the islands of Baleares and Sardegna. Additionally, it can be located within the territory of Corse. The species also inhabits parts of France. Finally, its presence extends to the coastal regions of Portugal.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cistus monspeliensis, is a versatile medicinal shrub where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaves are particularly notable for their aromatic quality and are often collected for their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory potential. The fruits, which are small capsules, also contain bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's overall medicinal profile. Furthermore, the entire aerial parts of the plant, including the stems and flowers, are frequently harvested to create infusions or extracts used in traditional herbal remedies.
