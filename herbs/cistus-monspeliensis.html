Cistus monspeliensis

Cistus monspeliensis, commonly known as Rockrose, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its high concentration of polyphenols and antioxidants, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to support skin health, provide anti-inflammatory benefits, and protect cells against oxidative stress and aging.

  • Scientific name: Cistus monspeliensis
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Cistus monspeliensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malvales. Finally, it is classified under the family Cistaceae and the genus Cistus.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Cistus monspeliensis, is recognized by various regional names across different linguistic traditions. In German, it is referred to as the Montpellier-Ziastrose (de: Montpellier-Ziastrose), while in Arabic, it is known as قريضة متوسطية (ar: قريضة متوسطية). Its Mediterranean heritage is further reflected in the Catalan name estepa negra (ca: estepa negra), the Corsican Muchju biancu (co: Muchju biancu), and the Occitan Moja negra (oc: Moja negra).

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions of Southwestern Europe. Its distribution includes the islands of Baleares and Sardegna. Additionally, it can be located within the territory of Corse. The species also inhabits parts of France. Finally, its presence extends to the coastal regions of Portugal.
