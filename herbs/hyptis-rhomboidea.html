What's the taxonomical classification of Hyptis rhomboidea?

Hyptis rhomboidea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, eventually placing it in the order Lamiales. As a member of the family Lamiaceae, it is further categorized under the genus Hyptis, with the specific epithet rhomboidea denoting its unique species identity.
