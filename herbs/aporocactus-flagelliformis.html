Aporocactus flagelliformis

Aporocactus flagelliformis, commonly known as the Rat Tail Cactus, is a striking climbing succulent native to Mexico. Renowned for its long, pendulous stems and vibrant red tubular flowers, this plant is valued in ethnobotany. It serves as a fascinating subject for studying unique survival adaptations and traditional medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Aporocactus flagelliformis
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Cactaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Aporocactus.
