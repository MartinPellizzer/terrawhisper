Leucas aspera

Leucas aspera, commonly known as the Wild Aster or Rough-leaved Leucas, is a versatile medicinal herb found across tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties. This plant is frequently utilized in folk remedies to treat skin ailments, fevers, and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Leucas aspera
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Leucas.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range that spans several distinct regions across Asia and Africa. In the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam. Its presence extends to East Asia, specifically within China Southeast and the island of Hainan. Moving toward the African continent, the species is located in Northeast Tropical Africa within Eritrea. Finally, its distribution reaches the Western Indian Ocean, where it is documented in Mauritius.
