Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range primarily concentrated within South and Southeast Asia. In the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is noted in the regions of Assam and Bangladesh. Its distribution extends further into the Indo-China peninsula, where it can be found in Cambodia. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Laos. Finally, its habitat includes the tropical landscapes of Myanmar.
