Thapsia nitida subsp. meridionalis

Thapsia nitida subsp. meridionalis, commonly known as southern water willow, is a distinctive aquatic perennial native to the southeastern United States. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its rhizomes possess potent medicinal properties. Historically utilized to treat various ailments, this unique wetland plant remains a subject of significant ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Thapsia nitida subsp. meridionalis
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Thapsia as the subspecies meridionalis.
