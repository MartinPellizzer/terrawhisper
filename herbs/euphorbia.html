This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, the medicinal plant is identified by its genus, Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the northernmost regions of the European continent. Within Northern Europe, its presence can be identified in the territories of Denmark and Finland. The species also extends to the island nations of Iceland and the Førøya islands.
