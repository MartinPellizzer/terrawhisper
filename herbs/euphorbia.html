Euphorbia

Spurge

Euphorbia, commonly known as spurge, is a diverse genus of flowering plants spanning many environments worldwide. Renowned for their unique milky sap, these resilient species offer significant medicinal potential. While some varieties are used in traditional remedies, their potent chemical compounds require careful handling due to inherent toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, the medicinal plant is identified by its genus, Euphorbia.

Beneath the surface, the plant may develop specialized tubers or extensive roots that serve to store nutrients and anchor the organism.
