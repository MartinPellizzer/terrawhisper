Black Nightshade

Solanum nigrum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (555 studies) · ★★★★★

Black Nightshade, scientifically known as Solanum nigrum, is a versatile herbaceous plant widely used in traditional medicine across Asia and Africa. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its berries and leaves are carefully prepared to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations and various systemic infections.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is classified under the genus Solanum. Together, these taxonomic levels identify Solanum nigrum as a distinct member of its biological lineage.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Solanum nigrum, offers various therapeutic benefits through its different anatomical components. While the whole plant is often utilized in traditional medicine, specific attention is frequently given to the leaves, which are rich in bioactive alkaloids. These leaves can be processed into a potent leaf extract used for its various pharmacological properties. Additionally, the small, dark fruits are notable for their nutritional and medicinal value, though they must be handled with care due to varying toxicity levels. Even the seeds contain unique chemical compounds that contribute to the plant's overall medicinal profile.
