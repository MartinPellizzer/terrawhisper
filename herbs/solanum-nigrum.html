Solanum nigrum

Solanum nigrum, commonly known as black nightshade, is a versatile medicinal herb found worldwide. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and berries contain bioactive alkaloids used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. While potent, careful preparation is essential to ensure safety and harness its therapeutic benefits effectively.

  • Scientific name: Solanum nigrum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is classified under the genus Solanum. Together, these taxonomic levels identify Solanum nigrum as a distinct member of its biological lineage.

This plant exhibits a wide presence across several regions within Northern Europe. Specifically, its distribution includes the coastal and inland areas of Denmark.
