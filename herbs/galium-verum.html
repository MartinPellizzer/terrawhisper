bedstraw, scientifically known as Galium verum, is a perennial herb celebrated for its bright yellow, star-shaped flowers and sweet, honey-like fragrance. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it possesses diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient plant thrives in sunny meadows, offering both aesthetic beauty and historical therapeutic value.
