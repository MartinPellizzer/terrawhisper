What's the taxonomical classification of Rhododendron cavaleriei?

Rhododendron cavaleriei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed under the order Ericales. Within this order, the plant is a member of the family Ericaceae, which contains a diverse range of flowering shrubs. Finally, its specific identity is defined by the genus Rhododendron and its unique species designation, cavaleriei.
