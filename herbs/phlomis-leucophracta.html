Phlomis leucophracta

Phlomis leucophracta, commonly known as a member of the Lamiaceae family, is a resilient medicinal herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its unique structural leaves and therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and various systemic inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis leucophracta
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Phlomis, with its species name being leucophracta.
