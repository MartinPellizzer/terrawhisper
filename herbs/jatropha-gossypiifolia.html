Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa and its surrounding islands. In the Macaronesian region, it can be found growing in Cape Verde. Its presence extends significantly across West Tropical Africa, where it inhabits various territories. Specifically, it is documented in countries such as Burkina, Gambia, and Guinea-Bissau. Additionally, the species is well-established within the borders of Guinea.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Jatropha gossypiifolia, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The large, often reddish-tinted leaves serve as a primary source of medicinal compounds, and when prepared as a concentrated leaf extract, they can be applied to treat various ailments. Whether used in their whole form or processed, the leaf remains a central element in traditional medicine, while the small, oily seeds also contribute to the plant's diverse pharmacological profile.
