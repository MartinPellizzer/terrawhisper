Jatropha gossypiifolia

Bellyache bush

Jatrophia gossypiifolia, commonly known as bedstraw apple or gray elder, is a versatile medicinal shrub found across tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Jatropha gossypiifolia
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Jatropha.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa and its surrounding islands. In the Macaronesian region, it can be found growing in Cape Verde. Its presence extends significantly across West Tropical Africa, where it inhabits various territories. Specifically, it is documented in countries such as Burkina, Gambia, and Guinea-Bissau. Additionally, the species is well-established within the borders of Guinea.
