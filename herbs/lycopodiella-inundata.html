Lycopodiella inundata

Marsh clubmoss

Lycopodiella inundata, commonly known as the water clubmoss, is a unique medicinal plant found in wetland ecosystems. Renowned for its specialized bioactive compounds, it serves as a vital subject in ethnobotany. Researchers explore its potential therapeutic properties, particularly for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities within traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Lycopodiella inundata
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Lycopodiidae. Within the order Lycopodiales, it is classified under the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Lycopodiella.
