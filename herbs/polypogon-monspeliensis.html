Polypogon monspeliensis

Polypogon monspeliensis, commonly known as Montpellier beard grass, is a perennial herb valued in traditional ethnobotany. Native to Mediterranean climates, this plant features delicate, feathery inflorescences. While primarily studied for its ecological role, it holds significant interest in herbal medicine for its unique bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Polypogon monspeliensis
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Poaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Polypogon.
