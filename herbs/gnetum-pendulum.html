What's the taxonomical classification of Gnetum pendulum?

Gnetum pendulum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Gnetidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Gnetales and the family Gnetaceae, eventually reaching its specific designation within the genus Gnetum and the species pendulum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Gnetum pendulum has woody, climbing stems that feature small, ovate to elliptical leaves with distinct parallel venation and a somewhat leathery texture. The plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that eventually develop into fleshy, berry-like structures used for seed dispersal. Its leaves are typically arranged in an opposite or sub-opposite pattern along the twisting vines. The stems often exhibit a scrambling growth habit, allowing the plant to ascend through forest canopies. These morphological adaptations enable the species to thrive in tropical understory environments.

The researchers conducted two field visits, occurring in November 2017 and May 2018, to document these uses. The study involved interviewing 64 key informants ranging between 21 and 84 years old.
