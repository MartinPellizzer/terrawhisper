Clematis forsteri

Clematis forsteri, commonly known as the Forster's Clematis, is a captivating climbing vine native to the forest understories of Southeast Asia. Renowned for its delicate, star-shaped blooms and lush foliage, this medicinal plant offers unique botanical properties, making it a significant subject for both horticultural beauty and traditional healing.
