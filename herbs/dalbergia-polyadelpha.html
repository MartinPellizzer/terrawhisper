Dalbergia polyadelpha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its lineage further, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Dalbergia, with the unique species name polyadelpha.
