Common baobab, scientifically known as Adansonia digitata, is a multifunctional marvel of the African savannah. Often called the "Tree of Life," it provides nutrient-dense fruit, medicinal bark, and essential hydration. Renowned for its longevity and resilience, this iconic species offers profound ecological and therapeutic benefits to surrounding communities.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the baobab, utilizes various components for medicinal purposes. The leaves are frequently prepared as a decoction or crushed into a paste to treat inflammation and fever, while the individual leaf structure provides a rich source of nutrients. The stem bark is often used in traditional remedies to address digestive issues or skin ailments, and the thick, fibrous bark itself can be harvested for various therapeutic applications. Additionally, the hard shell of the fruit serves as a protective container for the nutrient-dense pulp, which is often processed into powders or juices for health benefits.
