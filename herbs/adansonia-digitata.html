Adansonia digitata

Adansonia digitata, commonly known as the African Baobab, is a legendary "Tree of Life." Renowned for its massive trunk and longevity, this remarkable species offers immense medicinal value. Its nutrient-dense fruit, bark, and leaves are utilized in traditional healing to treat diverse ailments, ranging from fever to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Adansonia digitata
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Adansonia digitata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae and is situated within the genus Adansonia.
