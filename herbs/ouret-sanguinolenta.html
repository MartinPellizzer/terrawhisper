Ouret sanguinolenta

Ouret sanguinolenta, commonly known as the Blood-veined Ouret, is a rare medicinal shrub prized for its potent bioactive compounds. Found in secluded tropical highlands, its crimson-patterned leaves contain unique alkaloids used traditionally to treat inflammatory ailments and boost circulation, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ouret sanguinolenta
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Ouret, specifically as Ouret sanguinolenta.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several distinct regions within Asia. In China, its distribution covers the South-Central areas as well as the Southeast provinces. It can also be found growing on the tropical island of Hainan. Beyond mainland China, the species is documented throughout Eastern Asia, specifically in Taiwan. Finally, its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is located in Assam.
