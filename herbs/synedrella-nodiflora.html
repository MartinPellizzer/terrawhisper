Synedrella nodiflora

Synedrella nodiflora, commonly known as Nodding Synedrella, is a versatile herbaceous plant found in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties. This resilient species is frequently utilized to treat skin infections, gastrointestinal issues, and various inflammatory conditions in diverse herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Synedrella nodiflora
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Synedrella, specifically as the species Synedrella nodiflora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range spanning several regions across the Atlantic and African continents. Within the Macaronesia region, its presence is noted in Cape Verde. Moving toward the mainland, it is widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found in the countries of Benin and Burkina. Furthermore, its distribution extends to the coastal West Tropical African nations of Guinea-Bissau and Guinea.
