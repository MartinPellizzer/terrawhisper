Alangium premnifolium

Alangium premnefolium, commonly known as the Alangium plant, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin disorders, and digestive ailments. This botanical specimen holds immense potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Alangium premnifolium
  • Family: Cornaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Alangium premnefolium, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Cornales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cornaceae and the genus Alangium.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is specifically noted throughout the Southeast portion of the country. Moving toward Eastern Asia, the species can be found in Japan as well as the Nansei-shotō islands. Its range also extends into the Indo-China peninsula, where it inhabits parts of Myanmar. Additionally, the plant is documented to grow within the borders of Vietnam.
