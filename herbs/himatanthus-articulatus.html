Himatanthus articulatus

Himatanthus articulatus, commonly known as the Madagascar Periwinkle or Madagascar Oleander, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various infections, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Himatanthus articulatus
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed within the genus Himatanthus.
