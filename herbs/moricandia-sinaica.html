Moricandia sinaica

Moriandica sinaica, commonly known as the Sinai Moriandica, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid regions. Valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. This hardy plant serves as a vital botanical resource for treating various ailments in diverse ecological landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Moricandia sinaica
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales, it falls into the family Brassicaceae. Finally, it is taxonomically identified under the genus Moricandia.
