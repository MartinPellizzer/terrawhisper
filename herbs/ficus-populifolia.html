Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Ficus populifolia, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence has been documented in the nation of Benin, where it grows within local ecosystems. Furthermore, the species can be found extending into the territory of Burkina. It also inhabits parts of the Gambia, contributing to its regional prevalence. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes Ghana and the Ivory Coast.

Morphology

The morphology of Ficus populifolia is characterized by its robust growth form, functioning primarily as a tree. As a woody species, it possesses a lignified structure that provides significant mechanical strength and stability. While it lacks the climbing habit of certain other members of the Moraceae family, it is strictly a self-supporting plant rather than an obligatory or facultative climber. Furthermore, the species is an independent organism that does not exhibit parasitic behavior, and it maintains a terrestrial life habit rather than functioning as an epiphyte.
