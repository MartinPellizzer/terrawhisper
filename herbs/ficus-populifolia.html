Ficus populifolia

Ficus populifolia, commonly known as the Poplar-leaf Fig, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient species remains a vital resource for ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Ficus populifolia
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Moraceae. Ultimately, Ficus populifolia is identified by its genus, Ficus.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Ficus populifolia, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence has been documented in the nation of Benin, where it grows within local ecosystems. Furthermore, the species can be found extending into the territory of Burkina. It also inhabits parts of the Gambia, contributing to its regional prevalence. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes Ghana and the Ivory Coast.
