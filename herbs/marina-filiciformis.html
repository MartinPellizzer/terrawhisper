Marina filiciformis

Marina filiciformis, commonly known as the fern-leaf sea lily, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its unique bioactive properties. Found primarily in coastal ecosystems, it offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. Traditionally used in folk medicine, modern research explores its potential in developing innovative therapeutic treatments for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Marina filiciformis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Marina.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across North America, spanning from the United States into several regions of Mexico. In the Southwestern United States, its presence is documented within the state of Arizona. Moving eastward, its distribution extends into the South-Central United States, specifically covering New Mexico. The species is also well-established throughout Mexico, occurring in the Central and Northeast regions. Additionally, the plant can be found inhabiting the coastal areas of Mexico's Gulf region.
