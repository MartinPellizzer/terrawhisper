Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across parts of Asia. In China, its distribution is noted in the South-Central regions as well as on Hainan island. Moving towards the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing within the borders of Bangladesh. The species is also widely documented across various parts of India. Finally, its presence extends further north into the mountainous terrain of Nepal.
