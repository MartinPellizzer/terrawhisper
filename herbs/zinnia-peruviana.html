Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the African continent and its surrounding islands. In the Macaronesian region, it can be found specifically within the Cape Verde archipelago. Its range extends to West-Central Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Moving toward the northeast, the species is documented in the tropical territories of both Eritrea and Ethiopia. Finally, its presence reaches as far south as Zimbabwe in South Tropical Africa.
