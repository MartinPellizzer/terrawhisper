What's the taxonomical classification of Damnacanthus angustifolius?

Damnacanthus angustifolius belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Gentianaales and is a member of the family Rubiaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized under the genus Damnacanthus, with its specific designation being the species angustifolius.
