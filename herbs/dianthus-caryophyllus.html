Dianthus caryophyllus

Carnation

Dianthus caryophyllus, commonly known as the carnation, is a cherished ornamental plant celebrated for its fragrant, ruffled blooms. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, this species holds significance in traditional herbalism. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is studied for its potential antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties in various medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Dianthus caryophyllus
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and the family Caryophyllaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dianthus.
