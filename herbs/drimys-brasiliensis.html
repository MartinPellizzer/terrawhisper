This plant, Drimys brasiliensis, commonly known as Canela or Winter's Bark, possesses medicinal properties that are primarily concentrated within its woody structures. The stem barks are highly valued for their therapeutic potential, often being harvested to extract aromatic compounds and essential oils used in traditional remedies.
