Decodon verticillatus

Decodon verticillatus, commonly known as the whorled decodon, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique leaf arrangement, this species offers potent bioactive compounds. It is widely studied for its therapeutic potential, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Decodon verticillatus
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant, known as Decodon verticillatus, is primarily distributed across several provinces in Eastern Canada. Its range includes the coastal and inland regions of New Brunswick and Nova Scotia. It can also be found throughout the province of Ontario. Additionally, the species is well-established within the territory of Québec. Finally, its presence is documented on the island of Prince Edward Island.
