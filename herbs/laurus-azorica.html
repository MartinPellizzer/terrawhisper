Azores laurel, scientifically known as Laurus azorica, is an endemic evergreen shrub native to the Macaronesian islands. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and glossy leaves, this resilient species plays a vital role in subtropical ecosystems. It is widely valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic and antioxidant properties.

Distribution

This plant is found exclusively within the Macaronesian archipelago. Specifically, its natural habitat is restricted to the islands of the Azores. This limited range makes it an important component of the local endemic flora. Because of this isolated distribution, the species is closely tied to the unique subtropical climate of the region. Consequently, its presence is a defining characteristic of the Azorean landscape.

Plant Parts

This plant, Laurus azorica, is a perennial evergreen shrub or small tree native to the Azores, and its structural integrity is provided by a sturdy, woody stem. The stem serves as the essential conduit for transporting water and nutrients from the root system to the aromatic foliage, while also providing the necessary support for its dense, leathery leaves. In addition to its physiological functions, the texture and composition of the stem are characteristic of the Lauralis vegetation found in Macaronesian laurel forests.
