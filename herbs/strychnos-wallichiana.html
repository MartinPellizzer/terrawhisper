The physiology of Strychnos wallichiana is characterized by complex metabolic pathways primarily focused on the biosynthesis of highly potent indole alkaloids, which serve as secondary metabolites for chemical defense. The plant's metabolic processes are geared toward the sequestration of nitrogenous compounds to facilitate the structural assembly of strychnine and related alkaloids within its tissues.
