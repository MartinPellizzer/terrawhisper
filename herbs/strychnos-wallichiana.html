Strychnos wallichiana

Strychnos wallichiana, commonly known as Wallichia's Strychnine tree, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, particularly strychnine, it has been utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Research continues to explore its pharmacological properties, including its impact on the nervous system.

  • Scientific name: Strychnos wallichiana
  • Family: Loganiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Loganiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Strychnos.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. In the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is noted in Assam, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka. It can also be found within the Indo-China region, specifically inhabiting the Andaman Islands. Furthermore, the species extends into the South-Central parts of China.
