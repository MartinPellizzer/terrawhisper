Ambrosia peruviana

Ambrosia peruvianas, commonly known as a vital medicinal herb in South American traditional practices, offers significant therapeutic potential. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and inflammation. Understanding its botanical properties is essential for exploring its ethnobotanical significance.

  • Scientific name: Ambrosia peruviana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, Ambrosia peruvians is categorized under the genus Ambrosia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ambrosia peruvians, is referred to by various names depending on the region and language.
