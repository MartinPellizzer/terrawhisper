Paeonia daurica

Paeonia daurica, commonly known as the Caucasian peony, is a perennial herb native to the Caucasus region. Renowned for its striking blooms, this medicinal plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies. It is studied for its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering significant value in ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia daurica
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifrages, it is further organized into the family Paeoniaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Paeonia.
