Crimean peony, scientifically known as Paeonia daurica, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Crimean Peninsula. Valued in traditional medicine, its roots contain bioactive compounds with potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. This striking plant is celebrated both for its ornamental beauty and its significant ethnobotanical importance in regional healing.

This plant, Paeonia daurica, is characterized by several distinct morphological features that are relevant to its study and medicinal use.
