Lactuca serriola

Prickly lettuce

Lactuca serriola, commonly known as serrated lettuce or small lettuce, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Found across various temperate regions, this plant is traditionally valued for its anti-inflammatory and digestive properties, offering a natural approach to wellness through its unique bioactive compounds and nutrients.

  • Scientific name: Lactuca serriola
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lactuca.
