What's the taxonomical classification of Spatholobus harmandii?

Spatholobus harmandii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further categorized under the order Fabales. It is a member of the family Fabaceae, which places it within the well-known legume group. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Spatholobus, with the unique species designation harmandii.
