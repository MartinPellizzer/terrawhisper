Bidens aurea

Bidens aurea, commonly known as Golden Bidens, is a vibrant medicinal herb prized for its therapeutic properties. Native to specific subtropical regions, this plant features bright, sun-like blooms. It is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote overall skin health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Bidens aurea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Bidens, with the species name aurea.
