The reproduction of Crotalaria retusa is characterized by a versatile phenological cycle, with flowering and fruiting periods that can occur throughout the year, beginning as early as April. The plant produces violet-colored flowers that typically measure between 1.7 cm and 2.4 cm in length. Following pollination, the plant develops fruit in the form of a pod, which ranges in length from 3 cm to a maximum of 4 cm. These pods are dehiscent, meaning they split open at maturity to facilitate seed release. Each fruit contains a significant number of seeds, typically ranging from 11 to 100 per pod.
