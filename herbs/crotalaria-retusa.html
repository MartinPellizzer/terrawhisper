Rattleweed, scientifically known as Crotalaria retusa, is a distinctive legume found across tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant yellow flowers and rattling seed pods, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various cultures utilize its bioactive compounds for traditional medicinal purposes, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and skin ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. Within the Macaronesian islands, it can be found specifically in Cape Verde. Its presence extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, where it inhabits multiple countries. Specifically, it is documented in Benin, Burkina, and the Gambia. Furthermore, its range includes the coastal territory of Ghana.
