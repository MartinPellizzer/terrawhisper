Crotalaria retusa

Crotalaria retusa, commonly known as the rattlepod, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in various folk medicines to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its unique chemical profile offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Crotalaria retusa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Crotalaria, with the species name retusa.
