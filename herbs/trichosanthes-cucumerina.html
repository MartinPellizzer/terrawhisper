Trichosanthes cucumerina

Ash gouard

Trichosanthes cucumerina, commonly known as Snake Gourd, is a versatile medicinal climber celebrated in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Rich in bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and diuretic properties. It is frequently utilized to support digestive health and manage various metabolic ailments naturally.

  • Scientific name: Trichosanthes cucumerina
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Cucurbitales and placed in the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Trichosanthes.
