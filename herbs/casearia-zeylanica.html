Casearia zeylanica

Casearia zeylanica, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, holds immense value in traditional pharmacopeia. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for modern pharmacological research and herbal medicine development.

  • Scientific name: Casearia zeylanica
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is situated in the family Salicaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Casearia, specifically as the species Casearia zeylanica.
