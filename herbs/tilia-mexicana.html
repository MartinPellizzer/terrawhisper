Tilia mexicana

Tilia mexicana, commonly known as Mexican Linden or American Linden, is a deciduous tree cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its fragrant, pale yellow flowers, it is widely utilized to create soothing infusions. These herbal teas are prized for their sedative, antispasmodic, and anti-inflammatory properties, promoting relaxation and calm.

  • Scientific name: Tilia mexicana
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Tilia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Mexican Linden, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, particularly those derived from its delicate inflorescences. These small, fragrant clusters of flowers are often accompanied by specialized, pale-yellow bracts that serve to attract pollinators. When harvested and dried, these floral structures are used to prepare soothing herbal teas, which are traditionally employed to alleviate symptoms of anxiety, promote relaxation, and reduce mild fever.
