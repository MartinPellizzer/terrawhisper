Tilia mexicana, scientifically known as Tilia mexicana, is a deciduous tree native to North America, celebrated for its medicinal properties. Often referred to as Mexican Linden, its fragrant flowers are traditionally harvested to create soothing infusions used to reduce anxiety, alleviate fever, and support digestive health through gentle relaxation.
