Sweet woodruff, scientifically known as Galium odoratum, is a fragrant perennial herb native to Europe. Renowned for its distinct vanilla-like scent, it contains coumarin, which offers medicinal properties including anti-inflammatory and sedative effects. Traditionally used in teas and flavoring, this woodland plant remains a fascinating subject in herbalism.
