Galium odoratum

Sweet woodruff

Galium odoratum, commonly known as sweet Woodruff, is a fragrant perennial herb native to European forests. Renowned for its delicate white flowers and vanilla-scented leaves, it has a long history in traditional medicine. It is frequently used for its calming properties and its role in flavoring various herbal infusions.

  • Scientific name: Galium odoratum
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Gentianales, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Galium.
