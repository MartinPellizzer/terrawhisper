Cyclopia intermedia

Kouga honeybush-tea

Cyclopia intermedia, commonly known as Honeybush, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South Africa. Renowned for its soothing properties, it is frequently used in herbal teas to alleviate anxiety and digestive issues. Rich in antioxidants, this fragrant plant offers a natural way to support relaxation and overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cyclopia intermedia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Fabales and categorized into the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Cyclopia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Cyclopia intermedia, is recognized by various common names depending on the language. In English, it is referred to as Kouga Honeybush-tea, while in Afrikaans, it is called both Kougabergtee and Bergtee. Additionally, in Arabic, the plant is identified as سيكلوبيا الوسطى.

Distribution

This plant is a species native to the southern portion of the African continent. Its natural occurrence is specifically concentrated within the Cape Provinces of South Africa. Within this region, it thrives in the unique environmental conditions provided by the local terrain. The geographical range of Cyclopia intermedia is therefore quite localized to these coastal and inland areas. Consequently, understanding its specific habitat in the Cape Provinces is essential for its conservation.
