Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, placing it in the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identification is within the genus Cassia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Egypt. Its presence extends to the Macaronesia archipelago, specifically in Cape Verde. The species is also well-established throughout West Tropical Africa. Within this West African zone, it is documented in Benin, Burkina, and Gambia.
