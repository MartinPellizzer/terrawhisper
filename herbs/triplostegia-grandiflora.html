What's the taxonomical classification of Triplostegia grandiflora?

Triplostegiia grandiflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Dipsacales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Caprifoliaceae, placing it within the genus Triplostegiia, and it is specifically identified by the species name grandiflora.
