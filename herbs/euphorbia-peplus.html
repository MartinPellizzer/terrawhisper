Petty spurge, scientifically known as Euphorbia pepulis, is a resilient herbaceous plant widely recognized for its milky, caustic sap. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat skin conditions and certain ailments, it requires cautious handling due to its irritant properties. This versatile plant holds significant interest in botanical studies.
