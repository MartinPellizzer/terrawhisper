Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat skin conditions and certain ailments, it requires cautious handling due to its irritant properties. Plant Parts

This plant, Euphorbia peplus, is a small annual herb where the medicinal properties are primarily derived from its aerial parts, which include the stems and leaves. In many traditional applications, the whole plants are utilized to harness their full therapeutic potential. A defining characteristic of this species is the presence of a milky, white latex that is contained within the plant's tissues. When the plant is damaged or cut, this caustic sap is released, serving as a potent substance often used in topical treatments, though it must be handled with extreme caution due to its irritant nature.
