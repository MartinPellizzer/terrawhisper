Pentapanax yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Pentapanax yunnanensis?

Pentapanax yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Apiales, it is situated within the family Araliaceae. The plant is specifically identified by its genus, Pentapanax, and its unique species designation, yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pentapanax yunnanensis has large, palmately compound leaves that typically consist of seven to nine leaflets arranged around a central point. These leaflets are elliptical or obovate in shape and possess finely serrated margins along their edges. The plant grows as a woody shrub or small tree with a sturdy stem structure. Its leaves are dark green and leathery, providing a dense canopy for the plant. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge in terminal clusters during the blooming season.
