Osmanthus matsumuranus

What's the taxonomical classification of Osmanthus matsumuranus?

Osmanthus matsumuranus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Lamiales. This plant is a representative of the family Oleaceae, specifically falling under the genus Osmanthus, and is identified by the species name matsumuranus.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Osmanthus matsumuranus has dark green, leathery, evergreen leaves that are elliptical or lanceolate in shape with entire margins and a glossy upper surface. The foliage typically measures between five and twelve centimeters in length and possesses a distinctively smooth texture. Small, fragrant, white or pale yellow flowers emerge from the axils of the leaves during the blooming season. These blossoms are characterized by their four-lobed corolla and a subtle, sweet scent. The plant also produces small, fleshy drupes that transition from green to a dark purple or black color upon ripening.

Within these areas, it is frequently found growing along rocky shores and in seaside forests. The species prefers the well-drained soils often present in these coastal habitats.
