Momordica balsamina

African cucumber

Momoirdica balsamina, commonly known as the balsam bitter melon, is a unique medicinal plant cherished in traditional healing. Characterized by its distinct aromatic properties, this species is valued for its bioactive compounds. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic benefits, including antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory effects in wellness.

  • Scientific name: Momordica balsamina
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cucurbitaleales, it is classified under the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Momordica.

Distribution

This plant, Momordica balsamina, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions in Africa. In Northern Africa, its presence has been documented specifically within Tunisia. Moving southward, the species is found throughout various parts of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of Benin, Burkina, and Mali. Additionally, its range extends to the West Tropical African nation of Mauritania.
