Distribution

This plant, Momordica balsamina, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions in Africa. In Northern Africa, its presence has been documented specifically within Tunisia. Moving southward, the species is found throughout various parts of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of Benin, Burkina, and Mali. Additionally, its range extends to the West Tropical African nation of Mauritania.
