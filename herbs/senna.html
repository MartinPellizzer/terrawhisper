Sennas, scientifically known as Senna, is a powerful medicinal plant belonging to the Fabaceae family. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, it contains sennosides that stimulate intestinal contractions. Widely used to treat constipation, this botanical remedy must be used cautiously to ensure safety and avoid digestive dependency.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as senna, is a medicinal shrub where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. While the entire structure including the stem can be studied, the primary medicinal value is often found in the leaves and the fruit. The plant produces distinctive senna pods that develop as it matures; each individual pod contains numerous small seeds that are frequently used in laxative preparations. Whether harvesting the dried pods or the foliage, understanding these specific plant parts is essential for traditional herbal medicine.
