Tanacetum bipinnatum subsp. bipinnatum

Tanacetum bipinnatum subsp. bipinnatum, commonly known as Tansy, is a perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic, yellow-flowered foliage. Traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat digestive issues and parasitic infections, this potent plant requires careful handling due to its strong essential oils and potential toxicity if misused.

  • Scientific name: Tanacetum bipinnatum subsp. bipinnatum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Tanacetum. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies Tanacetum bipinnatum subsp. bipinnatum.
