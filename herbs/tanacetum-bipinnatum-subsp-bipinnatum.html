Lake Huron Tansy, scientifically known as Tanacetum bipinnatum subsp. bipinnatum, is a resilient perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Renowned for its aromatic, fern-like foliage and bright yellow button flowers, this plant has historically been utilized in traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across the vast territories of Eastern Europe and northern Asia.
