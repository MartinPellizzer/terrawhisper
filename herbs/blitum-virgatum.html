Blitum virgatum

Blitum virgatum, commonly known as Good King Henry, is a nutritious leafy green celebrated in traditional herbal medicine. Belonging to the Amaranthaceae family, this versatile plant offers significant health benefits, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It serves as both a culinary staple and a potent medicinal remedy worldwide.
