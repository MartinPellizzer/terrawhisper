Phyllanthus obliquus var. obliquus

Phyllanthus obliquus var. obliquus, commonly known as the oblique leafflower, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions. This plant serves as a vital resource in various ethnobotanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus obliquus var. obliquus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Malpighiales, and the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus as the specific variety Phyllanthus obliquus var. obliquus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across various regions of East Asia. Within China, its distribution extends through the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range further expands into Japan, specifically including the Nansei-shotto islands. Additionally, the species is documented to grow natively in Taiwan. Together, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of Phyllanthus obliquus var. obliquus.
