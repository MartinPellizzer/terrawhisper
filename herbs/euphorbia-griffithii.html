Euphorbia griffithii

Euphorbia griffithii, commonly known as Griffith's spurge, is a resilient perennial valued for its unique medicinal properties. Native to specific subtropical regions, this plant contains bioactive compounds often studied for their therapeutic potential. Understanding its chemical profile is essential for exploring its traditional uses and modern pharmacological applications in healthcare.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia griffithii
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed by its placement in the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is found in the South-Central areas as well as the Tibet region. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically throughout Nepal and the East Himalaya. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indo-China region, notably in Myanmar. These varied locations highlight its presence across diverse high-altitude and subtropical landscapes.
