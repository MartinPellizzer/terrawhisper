Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Caryophyllales and falls under the family Talinaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Talinum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. Within the Macaronesian islands, it can be found growing in both Cape Verde and Madeira. Its range extends significantly into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the DR Congo. Furthermore, the species is prevalent in East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Kenya. Finally, its presence is also documented in Uganda, completing its diverse regional spread.
