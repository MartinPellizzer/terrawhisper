Orbea variegata

Stapelia planiflora jacq.

Orbea variegata, commonly known as the pig's ear plant, is a captivating succulent native to South Africa. Distinguished by its fleshy, patterned leaves and striking, carrion-scented flowers, it is prized by collectors. While primarily ornamental, its unique morphology makes it a fascinating study in botanical adaptation and desert survival.

  • Scientific name: Orbea variegata
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, specifically within the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Orbea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a fascinating and wide-ranging geographical distribution across several continents. In its natural habitat, it is found within the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. However, it has also become established in parts of Southwestern Europe, specifically in Spain. The species has expanded its reach to Australia, where it can be found in Queensland. Additionally, its presence extends to the southern regions of the continent in South Australia.
