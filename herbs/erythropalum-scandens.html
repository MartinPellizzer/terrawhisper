Erythropalum scandens

Erythropalum scandens, commonly known as the climbing erythropalum, is a remarkable medicinal climber native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally used in various herbal remedies to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Erythropalum scandens
  • Family: Olacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Santalales and the family Olacaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Erythropalum.
