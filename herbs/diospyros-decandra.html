Diospyros decandra

Diospyros decandra, commonly known as the ebony fruit or tualang, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, offering valuable bioactive compounds for holistic and modern pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Diospyros decandra
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
