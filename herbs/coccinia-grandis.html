Distribution

This plant, Coccinia grandis, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Egypt. Moving toward the western coastal regions, the species is widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Specific countries where it can be found include Benin, Gambia, and Ghana. Furthermore, its range extends into the West Tropical African nation of Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, Coccinia grandis, is a climbing vine where various components are utilized for their therapeutic potential. The lush green leaves are often processed to treat skin ailments, and each individual plant leaf can be applied topically or used in decoctions. The large, succulent leaf structure is characteristic of its vigorous growth pattern. Furthermore, the roots are frequently employed in traditional medicine to address various internal conditions, while the edible fruit is noted for its nutritional and medicinal properties.
