Perennial lupine, scientifically known as Lupinus perennis, is a striking herbaceous perennial native to North America. Renowned for its vibrant, upright flower spikes and lush, palmate foliage, this plant serves both ornamental and ecological purposes, providing vital nectar for pollinators and adding dramatic vertical interest to any garden landscape.
