Lupinus perennis

Lupinus perennis, commonly known as perennial lupine, is a striking perennial herb native to North America. Characterized by its upright spikes of vibrant blue or purple flowers, this plant thrives in well-drained soils. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it plays a vital ecological role in nitrogen fixation and supporting pollinators.

  • Scientific name: Lupinus perennis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it falls under the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by its genus, Lupinus.
