Cabbage tree, scientifically known as Cussonia spicata, is a distinctive, slow-growing evergreen shrub native to South Africa. Characterized by its iconic rosette of large, palm-like leaves, this hardy plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating skin ailments and respiratory issues.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Cabbage Tree, features distinctive leaves that are palmate in shape, meaning they spread out from a single point like the fingers of a hand. These compound leaves are composed of several large, green leaflets that provide a lush, architectural appearance to the tree. In medicinal contexts, the various components of the foliage are often studied or utilized, as the leaves serve as a primary structural and functional part of the plant's biology.
