Cabbage tree, scientifically known as Cussonia spicata, is a distinctive, slow-growing evergreen shrub native to South Africa. Characterized by its iconic rosette of large, palm-like leaves, this hardy plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating skin ailments and respiratory issues.
