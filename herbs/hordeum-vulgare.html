Hordeum vulgare

Barley plant

Hordeum vulgare, commonly known as barley, is a versatile cereal grain widely cultivated for food and brewing. Beyond its nutritional value, it holds significant medicinal potential. Rich in bioactive compounds like beta-glucan, it offers therapeutic benefits, including supporting digestive health, regulating blood sugar, and lowering cholesterol levels effectively.

  • Scientific name: Hordeum vulgare
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Hordeum vulgare, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Its specific classification is completed by its placement in the genus Hordeum.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Hordeum vulgare, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the coastal and inland landscapes of Denmark. The species is also documented throughout the territories of Finland. Furthermore, its presence extends to the islands of Great Britain and Iceland. Finally, the plant is well-established within the natural environments of Ireland.
