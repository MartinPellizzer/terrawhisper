Today, its extracts are studied for their potential to support digestive health and metabolic functions.

Plant Parts

This plant, Hordeum vulgare, offers various components that are utilized for their nutritional and medicinal properties. The most commonly consumed parts are the grains, which are technically the seed of the plant and serve as a fundamental dietary staple. In addition to the mature harvest, the young, tender barley grass leaf is highly valued in holistic wellness for its dense concentration of vitamins and enzymes. Whether one is consuming the hearty whole grains or the nutrient-rich green shoots, each part of the plant contributes to its overall therapeutic profile.
