Cape-honeysuckle, scientifically known as Tecoma capensis, is a versatile, evergreen shrub native to South Africa. Renowned for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped orange flowers, it is highly valued in traditional medicine. Its various parts are utilized for their potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, Tecomaria capensis, is characterized by its vibrant and showy flowers, which are central to its visual appeal and biological function. These trumpet-shaped blooms are typically bright orange or red and are arranged in dense clusters at the ends of the branches. Beyond their aesthetic beauty, the flowers serve as a vital nectar source for various pollinators, such as sunbirds, making them an essential component of the plant's reproductive cycle and its role within its ecosystem.
