Protium klugii

Protium klugii, commonly known as Breu Branco, is a remarkable medicinal tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its aromatic resin, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and healing properties. Traditionally used by indigenous communities, this botanical treasure offers significant therapeutic potential for treating skin wounds and respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Protium klugii
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Burseraceae. Specifically, Protium klugii is identified by its genus, Protium.

Distribution

This plant, known as Protium klugii, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the tropical regions of the Americas. Within Western South America, its presence is documented in the diverse landscapes of Colombia. It is also found spanning across parts of Peru, contributing to its regional availability. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the northern territories of Brazil. Together, these locations define the primary habitat where this medicinal species can be located.
