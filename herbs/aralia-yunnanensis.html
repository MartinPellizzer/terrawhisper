Aralia yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Aralia yunnanensis?

Aralia yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. At the class level, it is categorized as Equisetopsida, falling under the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is further organized within the order Apiales and the family Araliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic position is completed by its placement in the genus Aralia, specifically identifying it as the species yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aralia yunnanensis has a perennial woody habit characterized by compound leaves and large, terminal compound umbels of small white flowers. The leaves are typically pinnately compound with several pairs of leaflets that possess serrated margins. These leaflets are arranged alternately along the rachis and exhibit a distinct green coloration on their upper surfaces. The stems are sturdy and can develop a bark-like texture as the plant matures. Tiny, inconspicuous flowers cluster together to form broad, flat-topped inflorescences that attract various pollinators. Following pollination, the plant produces small drupes that serve as its primary method of seed dispersal.
