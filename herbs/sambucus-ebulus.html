Sambucus ebulus

Sambucus ebulus, commonly known as the European elderberry or dwarf elder, is a deciduous shrub native to North America. Distinguished by its creamy white flower clusters and dark berries, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its antioxidant properties and its potential to support immune system health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Sambucus ebulus
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Dipsacales and the family Viburnaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Sambucus.
