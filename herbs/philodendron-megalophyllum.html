Philodendron megalophyllum

Philodendron megalophyllum, commonly known as the Giant Philodendron, is a striking tropical plant valued for its massive, glossy leaves. Beyond its ornamental appeal, it holds significant botanical interest. Native to rainforest ecosystems, this species plays a vital role in its habitat, offering unique characteristics used in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Philodendron megalophyllum
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Southern America
