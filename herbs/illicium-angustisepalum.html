What's the taxonomical classification of Illicium angustisepalum?

Illicium angustisepalum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Austrobaileyales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Schisandraceae, which contains the genus Illicium. Ultimately, the specific identification of this plant is completed at the species level as angustisepalum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

These woody follicles eventually split open to reveal bright red seeds. The overall growth habit is that of a small evergreen shrub or subshrub.
