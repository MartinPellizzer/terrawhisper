Pueraria montana var. montana

Pueraria montana montana

Pueraria montana var. montana, commonly known as Kudzu, is a vigorous climbing legume celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant and sedative properties, this botanical powerhouse is frequently utilized to manage alcohol dependence, reduce anxiety, and alleviate various inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Pueraria montana var. montana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Pueraria, identified as the variety montana.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Pueraria montana var. montana, is recognized by several different common names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is referred to as Pueraria montana montana, while in Chinese, it is known by various names including 越南葛, 葛麻姆, 越南野葛, and 葛麻藤.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across various regions of China. It can be found throughout the South-Central areas of the country. Its distribution also extends to the island province of Hainan. In the northern territories, the species is documented in both Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Finally, the plant is known to grow within the China North-Central region.
