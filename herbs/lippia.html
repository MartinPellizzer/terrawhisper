Lippia

Lippia, commonly known as various local names depending on the species, is a versatile genus of flowering plants in the Verbenaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, many species offer significant medicinal properties, including antibacterial and anti-inflammatory benefits, making them vital resources in traditional herbal medicine worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Lippia
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Verbenaceae. Finally, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Lippia.
