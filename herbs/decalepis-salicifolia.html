Decalepis salicifolia

Decalepis salicifolia, commonly known as the willow-leaved decalepis, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to various African landscapes. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots possess significant therapeutic potential. Researchers study this plant for its bioactive compounds, which may offer antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory benefits for various ailments.
