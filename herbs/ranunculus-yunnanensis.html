Ranunculus yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Ranunculus yunnanensis?

Ranunculus yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Ranunculales. As a member of the family Ranunculaceae, it is categorized under the genus Ranunculus, with its specific identification being the species yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ranunculus yunnanensis has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by basal leaves that are cordate or reniform with crenate margins and cauline leaves that decrease in size along the stem. The plant produces bright yellow, actinomorphic flowers that typically feature five distinct petals and a central cluster of numerous stamens and carpels. Its stems are upright and often hairy, providing structural support for the inflorescence. The root system consists of a fleshy rhizome that allows the plant to persist through seasonal changes. Each reproductive structure is supported by a pedicel that emerges from the axils of the upper leaves.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is native to the high-altitude regions of southwestern China, specifically within the provinces of Yunnan and Sichuan. It thrives in the montane habitats and alpine meadows characteristic of these mountainous territories. Its range is largely defined by the rugged terrain of the Hengduan Mountains. Within these specific ecosystems, the species is typically found growing in moist soils at significant elevations. This restricted distribution makes it a specialist of the temperate, high-elevation environments found in this part of Asia.
