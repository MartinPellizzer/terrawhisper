Utricularia

Bladderwort

Utricularia, commonly known as bladderworts, are fascinating carnivorous plants found in aquatic and wetland habitats. Lacking true roots, they utilize specialized bladder-like traps to capture microscopic organisms. Beyond their predatory nature, certain species hold medicinal potential, traditionally used in various cultures for their unique bioactive properties and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Utricularia
  • Family: Lentibulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lentibulariaceae, it is classified under the genus Utricularia.

Common Names

This plant, belonging to the genus Utricularia, is known by various names across different Chinese linguistic regions. In Simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 狸藻属 (zh-hans) or 狸藻属 (zh-cn), and it is also known by the same designation in Singapore (zh-sg). In Traditional Chinese contexts, such as in Taiwan, it is identified as 狸藻屬 (zh-hant), while in Hong Kong, the name remains 狸藻屬 (zh-hk).
