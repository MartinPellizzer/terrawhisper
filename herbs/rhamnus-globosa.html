Rhamnus globosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Rhamnus globosa?

Rhamnus globosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Rosales. It is a member of the family Rhamnaceae, falling into the genus Rhamnus, and is specifically identified by its species name, globosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in clusters, which later develop into distinctive globular drupes. These fruits transition from green to a dark red or black color as they reach maturity. What side effects this plant can have?

These active compounds act as potent stimulant laxatives that can irritate the lining of the digestive tract. If consumed in large quantities or used excessively, the plant may lead to electrolyte imbalances or dehydration. Chronic ingestion could potentially result in more serious complications such as melanosis coli or dependency on laxatives.
