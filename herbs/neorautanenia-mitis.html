This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Within this region, its presence is officially documented in the country of Benin. It can also be found growing within the borders of Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species is known to inhabit parts of Ghana and the Ivory Coast.
