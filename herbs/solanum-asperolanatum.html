Solanum asperolanatum

Solanum asperolanatum, commonly known as the prickly nightshade, is a distinctive shrub native to Australia. Characterized by its woody stems and prickly foliage, this medicinal plant has been traditionally utilized by Indigenous communities. It holds significant ethnobotanical importance, offering potential insights into various therapeutic applications and pharmacological properties.

  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa
