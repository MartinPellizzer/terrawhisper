Devil's-fig, scientifically known as Solanum asperolanatum, is a resilient shrub native to Australian rainforests and coastal regions. Characterized by its distinctive prickly stems and small, white flowers, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally used by Indigenous communities to treat various skin ailments and systemic inflammations.
