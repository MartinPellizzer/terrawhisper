Piper retrofractum

Piper retrofractum, commonly known as Long Pepper, is a highly valued medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, its pungent rhizomes possess potent warming properties. It is widely used to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and inflammation, serving as a vital therapeutic agent in traditional wellness.

  • Scientific name: Piper retrofractum
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Piperaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized within the genus Piper as the species Piper retrofractum.

Common Names

This plant, Piper retrofractum, is recognized by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In Japan, it is known as ヒハツモドキ (ja), while in China, it is referred to as 假荜拔 (zh). Its names in Southeast Asia vary by local dialect, being called Tiêu đôi (vi) in Vietnam, Cabai jawa (id) in Indonesia, and Cabé (jv) in Javanese.

Within the Indian Subcontinent, its presence extends to both Bangladesh and the East Himalaya region. The species also populates parts of Indo-China, including the country of Cambodia.
