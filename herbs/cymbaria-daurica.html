Cymbaria daurica

Cymbaria daurica, commonly known as the forest fern, is a resilient medicinal plant native to specific subtropical ecosystems. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation and skin ailments. Its specialized bioactive compounds continue to attract significant interest in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Cymbaria daurica
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cymbaria daurica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Cymbaria.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across several specific regions within the vast territory of Siberia. Its distribution includes the mountainous areas of the Altai region. It is also widely documented throughout the Republic of Buryatia. Furthermore, the species can be located in the eastern part of Siberia within Chita. Finally, its range extends to the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions.
