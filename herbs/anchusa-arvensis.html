Anchusa arvensis

Anchus arvensis

Anchusa arvensis, commonly known as bugloss, is a resilient annual herb belonging to the Boraginaceae family. Often found in disturbed soils and agricultural fields, this plant is distinguished by its hairy stems and small, blue, funnel-shaped flowers. It plays a vital role in supporting local pollinator biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Anchusa arvensis
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae, it is classified under the genus Anchusa.
