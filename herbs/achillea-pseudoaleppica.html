Achillea pseudoaleppica

Achillea pseudoaleppica, commonly known as a unique member of the Asteraceae family, is a medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic potential. Native to specific Mediterranean landscapes, this plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to address inflammation and digestive issues, offering significant interest for modern pharmacological research and herbalism.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Achillea.
