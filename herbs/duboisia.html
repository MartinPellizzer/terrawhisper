Duboisia

Duboisia, commonly known as the duboisia shrub, is a genus of medicinal plants native to Australia. Renowned for its high alkaloid content, particularly scopolamine and hyoscyamine, it serves as a critical botanical source for pharmaceutical industries. These alkaloids are essential in developing treatments for various neurological and gastrointestinal conditions.

  • Scientific name: Duboisia
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Solanales, and the family Solanaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Duboisia.
