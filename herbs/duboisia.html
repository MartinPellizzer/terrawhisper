Corkwood trees, scientifically known as Duboisia, is a genus of shrubs native to Australia. Highly valued in pharmacology, these plants are a primary source of isoquinoline alkaloids, specifically apoatropine and deoxyapoatropine. These compounds are essential in producing medicinal agents used to treat muscle spasms and various neurological conditions.
