Zigzag caperbush, scientifically known as Capparis erythrocarpos, is a resilient, sprawling shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its striking red fruits and delicate white flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various indigenous communities utilize its roots and leaves in traditional remedies to treat diverse medicinal ailments.
