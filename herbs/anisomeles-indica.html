Indian Catmint, scientifically known as Anisomeles indica, is a versatile perennial herb renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Characterized by its aromatic foliage and distinctive purple flowers, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antipyretic properties, making it a valuable resource for treating various ailments naturally.
