Cynanchum

Cynanchum, commonly known as milkweed or swallow-wort, belongs to the Apocynaceae family. These climbing vines are celebrated in traditional medicine for their diverse bioactive compounds. Renowned for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, various species offer significant therapeutic potential in treating diverse ailments and supporting overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cynanchum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Cynanchum.
