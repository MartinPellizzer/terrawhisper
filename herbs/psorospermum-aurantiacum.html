Psorospermum aurantiacum

Psorospermum aurantiacum, commonly known as the orange-flowered sorrel, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various African landscapes. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize its unique chemical properties to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its vital role in regional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Psorospermum aurantiacum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Psorospermum.
