Detarium senegalense

Detarium senegalense, commonly known as the African Detarium, is a versatile tropical tree widely distributed across West and Central Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of the plant are used in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Detarium senegalense
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family and is identified by the genus Detarium. Consequently, its full scientific designation is Detarium senegalense.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. Its presence is notably recorded in the country of Benin. It can also be found growing throughout Burkina Faso. Additionally, the species inhabits parts of the Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends into Guinea-Bissau.
