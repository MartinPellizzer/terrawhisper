Androstachys johnsonii

Androstachys johnsonii, commonly known as the African Sandalwood, is a valuable medicinal plant native to various African rainforests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, infections, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Androstachys johnsonii
  • Family: Picrodendraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Androstachys johnsonii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Picrodendraceae and the genus Androstachys.
