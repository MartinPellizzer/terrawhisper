, scientifically known as Hedera sinensis, is an evergreen climbing vine native to East Asia. It serves as a vital natural remedy for managing respiratory ailments and coughs.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several diverse regions of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its distribution spans from the South-Central provinces to the North-Central territories. It is also commonly found throughout the Southeast regions of China and high within the Tibet plateau. Beyond Chinese borders, the species extends its range into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it can be identified growing within the tropical landscapes of Laos.

The morphology of Hedera sineensis is characterized by its growth habit as an obligatory climber, utilizing specialized adventitious roots to ascend and adhere to vertical surfaces or host structures. It functions as an independent organism rather than a parasite, deriving all necessary nutrients and moisture through its own root system from the substrate. Furthermore, it is a terrestrial species, establishing its primary root system in the soil to secure stability and access nutrients as it climbs.
