Distribution

This plant is native to the West Tropical Africa region. Its geographical range includes several distinct countries across this part of the continent. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Benin and Ghana. The distribution extends further to include Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Additionally, this medicinal species is documented to occur in the Ivory Coast.
