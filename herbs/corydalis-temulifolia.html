Corydalis temulifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Corydalis temulifolia?

Corydalis temulifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, the plant falls under the order Ranunculales and is a member of the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Corydalis, with the specific designation of the species temulifolia.
