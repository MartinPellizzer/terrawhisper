Ochna schweinfurthiana

Ochna schweinfurthiana, commonly known as the Wild Loquat, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various African regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat diverse ailments, including skin inflammations and digestive issues, offering significant potential for contemporary pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ochna schweinfurthiana
  • Family: Ochnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Malpighiales and the family Ochnaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement in the genus Ochna.
