Veronica anagallis-aquatica

Veronica anaogallis-aquatica, commonly known as water speedwell, is a delicate perennial herb found in wetland habitats. Traditionally valued in folk medicine, this aquatic species is recognized for its soothing properties. Its unique ability to thrive in saturated soils makes it a fascinating subject for both botanical study and herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Veronica anagallis-aquatica
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginacea, falling within the genus Veronica.
