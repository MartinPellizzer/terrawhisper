Telegraph plant, scientifically known as Codariocalyx motorius, is a fascinating sensitive herb renowned for its rapid seismonastic movements. When touched, its leaflets fold inward almost instantly. Beyond its captivating motion, this unique plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various cultures for its diverse medicinal properties.
