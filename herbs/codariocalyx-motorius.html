Codariocalyx motorius

Telegraph plant

Codariocalyx motorius, commonly known as the "dancing plant," is a fascinating medicinal species celebrated for its unique neuromuscular properties. Renowned in traditional pharmacology, it is primarily utilized to treat motor dysfunction and nerve sensitivity. This remarkable plant offers profound insights into neurological wellness through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Codariocalyx motorius
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Codariocalyx.
