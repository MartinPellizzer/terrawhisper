Tovomita longifolia

Tovomita longifolia, commonly known as the long-leaved Tovomita, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This resilient shrub offers valuable phytochemical compounds, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Tovomita longifolia
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Tovomita longifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is situated within the family Clusiaceae and the genus Tovomita.

Common Names

This plant, Tovomita longifolia, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Long-leaved Tovomita, while in French, it is called Tovomita à feuilles longues. In Malagasy, the native language of its primary habitat, it is known as Tovomita lava ravinina.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions in Northern South America. It can be found growing within the territories of French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname. In addition to these areas, its range extends significantly into the continent of Brazil. Specifically, the species is located in the Northeast region of Brazil. It is also documented to inhabit the northern parts of Brazil.
