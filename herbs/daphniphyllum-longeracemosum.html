Daphniphyllum longeracemosum

Daphniphyllum longeraceamosum, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb, belongs to the Daphniphyllaceae family. Native to specific Asian regions, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting holistic wellness.

  • Family: Daphniphyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
