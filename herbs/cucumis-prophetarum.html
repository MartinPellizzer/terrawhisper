Cucumis prophetarum

Cucumis prophetarum, commonly known as the prophet's cucumber, is a climbing medicinal vine native to tropical Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, its fruits and leaves are utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues. This botanical species offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Cucumis prophetarum
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Cucurbitaleales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cucumis.
