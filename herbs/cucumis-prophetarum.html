Wild cucumber, scientifically known as Cucumis prophetarum, is a climbing herbaceous vine native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive tendrils and distinctive fruits, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Traditionally, it has been utilized in various folk medicines to treat diverse ailments, though careful handling is essential.

Its metabolic pathways are centered on efficient photosynthesis and the translocation of photosynthates from the leaves to its developing fruits and specialized tissues.
