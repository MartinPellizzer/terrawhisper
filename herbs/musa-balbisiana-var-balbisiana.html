Musa balbisiana var. balbisiana

Musa balbisiana var. balbisiana, commonly known as wild banana, is a resilient perennial plant prized for its genetic importance. Beyond its starch-rich fruit, it serves as a vital medicinal resource. Its leaves, pseudostems, and roots are utilized in traditional practices to treat various ailments, offering significant bioactive potential.

  • Scientific name: Musa balbisiana var. balbisiana
  • Family: Musaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Zingiberales and the family Musaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement concludes within the genus Musa, specifically as the variety balbisiana.
