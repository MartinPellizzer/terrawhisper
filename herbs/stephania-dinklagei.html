Stephania dinklagei

Stephania dinklagei, commonly known as the "climbing jasmine" or "wild jasmine," is a medicinal woody climber native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical value.

  • Scientific name: Stephania dinklagei
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Africa
