Phyllanthus muellerianus

Phyllanthus muellerianus, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical ecosystems, holds profound pharmacological potential. Native to diverse forest habitats, this species is valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds. Research focuses on its antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, highlighting its importance in modern drug discovery.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus muellerianus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.
