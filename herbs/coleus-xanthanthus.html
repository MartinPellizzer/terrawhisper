Coleus xanthanthus

Coleus xanthanthus, commonly known as the Painted Nettle, is a striking ornamental plant valued for its vibrant, multicolored foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance, utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin disorders, and digestive issues due to its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Coleus xanthanthus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Coleus.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Coleus xanthanthus, exhibits a specific and localized geographical range. Its presence is primarily documented within the regional landscapes of China. Specifically, the species can be found growing throughout the South-Central part of the country. This concentration suggests that the environmental conditions in these particular provinces are ideal for its survival. Consequently, the distribution of this medicinal plant is closely tied to the unique climate of South-Central China.
