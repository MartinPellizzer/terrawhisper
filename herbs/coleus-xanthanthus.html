Life history

The life history of Coleus xanthanthus is characterized by its classification as a chamaephyte, a growth form where the plant's perennating buds are located close to the soil surface. Within the broader spectrum of life forms, it is specifically identified as a chamaephyte, distinguishing it from other strategies such as phanerophytes, hemicryptophytes, or therophytes. This life strategy allows the plant to persist through unfavorable environmental conditions by maintaining its regenerative structures at or just above the ground level, ensuring its ability to re-emerge during favorable growing seasons.
