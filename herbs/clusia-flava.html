Clusia flava

Clusia flava, commonly known as the Autograph Tree, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its thick, succulent leaves, this medicinal plant is utilized in traditional healing practices to treat various ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential through its unique bioactive compounds and diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Clusia flava
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Clusiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Clusia, specifically as the species Clusia flava.
