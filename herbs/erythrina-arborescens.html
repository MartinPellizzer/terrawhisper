Erythrina arborescens

Erythrina arborescens, commonly known as the coral tree, is a striking medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize its bark and leaves to treat various ailments, leveraging its potent bioactive compounds for their diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Erythrina arborescens
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Erythrina and the specific species Erythrina arborescens.

This plant, Erythrina arborescens, is known by several different common names across various linguistic contexts. In Chinese, it is frequently referred to as 鹦哥花, 鹦哥花 (simplified), or 鹦哥花 (in mainland China), as well as 刺桐. Additionally, it is sometimes called 红嘴绿鹦哥.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its distribution extends through the South-Central provinces and the tropical island of Hainan. It can also be found growing in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in the state of Assam. Finally, its presence is also noted within the borders of Bangladesh.
