Himalayan Coral Tree, scientifically known as Erythrina arborescens, is a striking deciduous tree renowned for its vibrant, coral-red blossoms. Native to the Himalayan foothills, this medicinal powerhouse is deeply embedded in traditional Ayurvedic practices, utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties across various botanical remedies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its distribution extends through the South-Central provinces and the tropical island of Hainan.
