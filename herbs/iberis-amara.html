Iberis amara

Iberis amara, commonly known as bitter candytuft, is a resilient perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Distinguished by its delicate white blooms and bitter profile, this plant offers unique therapeutic properties. Often used to support digestive health and skin vitality, it remains a fascinating subject for botanical studies.
