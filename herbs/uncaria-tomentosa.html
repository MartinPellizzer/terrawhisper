Uncaria tomentosa

Uncaria tomentoosa, commonly known as Cat's Claw, is a powerful medicinal vine native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this woody climber has been used traditionally to boost immunity, alleviate joint pain, and support overall wellness through its unique bioactive alkaloids and compounds.

  • Scientific name: Uncaria tomentosa
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Uncaria tomentosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Gentianales. Finally, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family and falls under the genus Uncaria.
