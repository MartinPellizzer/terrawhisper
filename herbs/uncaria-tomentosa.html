Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Cat's Claw, is a woody vine where various components hold significant importance in traditional medicine. The bark is the most widely utilized part, often harvested for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. While the leaves are sometimes studied for their chemical composition, the primary medicinal value is concentrated in the inner bark. Therefore, when preparing traditional remedies, practitioners focus on the therapeutic compounds found within the bark rather than the leaves.
