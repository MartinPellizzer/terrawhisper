Olea europaea subsp. cuspidata

Wild olive

Olea europaea subsp. cuspidata, commonly known as the African olive, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to arid regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory compounds. Traditionally used to support cardiovascular health and skin vitality, this subspecies offers significant therapeutic potential in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Olea europaea subsp. cuspidata
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, taxonomically known as Olea europaea subsp. cuspidata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the Oleaceae family and the genus Olea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the arid regions of Western Asia and the Arabian Peninsula. In Western Asia, its presence is recorded in the countries of Afghanistan and Iran. Moving toward the southern regions, the species can be found across the Arabian Peninsula. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of Oman and Saudi Arabia. Finally, its geographical range extends into the territory of Yemen.
