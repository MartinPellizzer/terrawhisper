Garcinia humilis

Achacha

Garcinia humilis, commonly known as the small garcinia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments and skin conditions, offering a natural approach to enhancing overall wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia humilis
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Malpighiales and the family Clusiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Garcinia.
