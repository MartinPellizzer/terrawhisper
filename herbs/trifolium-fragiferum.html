Trifolium fragiferum

Trifolium fragiferum, commonly known as strawberry clover, is a perennial legume valued for its unique, strawberry-scented blossoms. Beyond its aesthetic charm, this resilient plant serves as an important forage crop and medicinal herb, traditionally used to support digestive health and provide nutrient-rich sustenance in diverse ecological landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Trifolium fragiferum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Trifolium.
