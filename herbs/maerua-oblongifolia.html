Distribution

This plant, known as Maerua oblongifolia, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin and Burkina Faso. Additionally, its presence extends into the territory of Ghana. The species is also documented to inhabit parts of Mali. Finally, its geographical range reaches as far as Mauritania.
