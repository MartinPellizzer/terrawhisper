Maerua oblongifolia

Maerua oblongifolia, commonly known as a medicinal shrub, is a significant botanical species valued in traditional healing practices. Often found in tropical regions, this plant is recognized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Research focuses on its bioactive compounds, which offer potential applications in treating various ailments and advancing pharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
