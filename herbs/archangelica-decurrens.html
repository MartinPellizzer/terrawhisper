What's the taxonomical classification of Archangelica decurrens?

Archangelica decurrens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it in the order Apiales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Apiaceae, falling under the genus Archanjelica, and is specifically identified by the species name decurrens.
