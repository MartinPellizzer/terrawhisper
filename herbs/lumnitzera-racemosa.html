Lumnitzera racemosa

White-flowered black mangrove

Lumnitzera racemosa, commonly known as the "Bato-bato" in certain regions, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Native to tropical landscapes, this shrub is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising ethnobotanical insights into its potential bioactive compounds and healing capabilities.
