Artemisia atrovirens

Artemisia atrovirens, commonly known as a distinctive member of the wormwood family, is a medicinal shrub native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine for its antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, offering significant potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia atrovirens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Artemisia, within which the species Artemisia atrovirens is categorized.

Common Names

This plant, Artemisia atrovirens, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is referred to as 暗绿蒿, 白毛蒿, or 白艾蒿, with the specific orthography for Simplified Chinese being 暗绿蒿 and for Traditional Chinese being 暗緑蒿.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central areas to the North-Central provinces. Its presence also extends into the Southeast regions of China. Furthermore, the species reaches into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the territory of Thailand.
