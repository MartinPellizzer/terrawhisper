Parietaria judaica

Parietarica judaica, commonly known as Pellitory-la-reine, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Urticaceae family. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, this plant serves as a valuable natural resource in herbal pharmacology.

  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Europe
