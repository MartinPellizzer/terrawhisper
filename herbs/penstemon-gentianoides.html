Penstemon gentianoides

Penstemon gentianoides, commonly known as Gentian-leaf Beardtongue, is a striking perennial native to North American montane regions. While primarily celebrated for its elegant, trumpet-shaped blue flowers, this hardy plant holds ecological significance. It thrives in rocky soils, offering vital nectar for pollinators within diverse high-altitude alpine ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Penstemon gentianoides
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Plantaginaceae and is identified by the genus Penstemon.
