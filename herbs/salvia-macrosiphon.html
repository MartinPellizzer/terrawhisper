Salvia macrosiphon

Salvia macrosiphon, commonly known as long-stemmed sage, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean regions. Characterized by its elongated stems and aromatic foliage, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its potent bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic benefits in treating inflammation and supporting respiratory health.

  • Scientific name: Salvia macrosiphon
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Salvia.
