Euphorbia altissima

Euphorbia altissima, commonly known as the tall spurge, is a robust perennial celebrated for its striking architectural form and vibrant foliage. While primarily ornamental, this plant holds significant interest in traditional ethnobotany. Its unique chemical properties offer intriguing potential for pharmacological research and diverse applications in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia altissima
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Euphorbia.
