Creek bottlebrush, scientifically known as Melaleuca viminalis, is a versatile Australian riparian shrub renowned for its therapeutic properties. Characterized by delicate, feathery foliage and white, brush-like blooms, it is traditionally utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory qualities, offering natural healing benefits through its potent essential oils and extracts.
