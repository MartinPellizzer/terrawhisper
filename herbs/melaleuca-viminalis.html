Melaleuca viminalis

Melaleuca viminalis, commonly known as the Weeping Paperbark, is a versatile Australian native renowned for its distinctive peeling bark and medicinal properties. Rich in essential oils with potent antimicrobial and antiseptic qualities, it has long been utilized in traditional healing to treat skin ailments and support respiratory health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Melaleuca viminalis
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtoales, it is a member of the family Myrtaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Melaleuca.
