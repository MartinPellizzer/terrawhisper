This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Fabales and is situated in the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ticanto, specifically under the species Ticanto crista.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia and the Indian Ocean. Within the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found specifically in Mauritius. Its presence in China is widespread, occurring in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into Eastern Asia, specifically inhabiting Japan.
