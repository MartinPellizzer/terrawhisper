Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several specific regions in East and South Asia. In China, its distribution spans from the South-Central areas to the Southeast provinces. Additionally, it can be found growing on the tropical island of Hainan. The species also extends into Eastern Asia, specifically inhabiting the island of Taiwan. Finally, its range reaches the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in the Assam region.
