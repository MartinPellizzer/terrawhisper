Kadsura heteroclita

Kadsura heteroclita, commonly known as the evergreen Kadsura, is a significant medicinal climber found in the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its aromatic fruits and bark, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments like fever, indigestion, and inflammation, offering valuable bioactive compounds for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Kadsura heteroclita
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Austrobaileyales and the family Schisandraceae. Its scientific classification concludes with the genus Kadsura.
