Bidens gardneri

Bidens gardneri, commonly known as the garden bidens, is a versatile annual herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its vibrant, daisy-like flowers, this plant is prized in botanical studies and ornamental gardening. Its resilient nature and unique morphological features make it a significant subject in medicinal research.
