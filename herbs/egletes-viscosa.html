Egletes viscosa

Egletes viscosa, commonly known as sticky cudweed, is a resilient perennial herb native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive sticky, glandular hairs, this plant plays a vital role in traditional medicine. It is widely utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties across various cultural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Egletes viscosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Egletes, specifically the species Egletes viscosa.
