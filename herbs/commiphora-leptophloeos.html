Commiphora leptophloeos

Commiphora leptophloeos, commonly known as the "paperbark" tree, is a significant medicinal species native to East Africa. Renowned for its aromatic resin, this plant is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering valuable phytochemical properties for modern pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Commiphora leptophloeos
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Burseraceae and the genus Commiphora. Specifically, its scientific name is Commiphora leptophloeos.
