Saussurea stella

Saussurea stella, commonly known as the Starry Saw-wort, is a rare medicinal perennial found in high-altitude alpine ecosystems. Renowned for its unique floral morphology, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Traditional healers utilize its bioactive compounds to treat inflammatory conditions, making it a vital subject for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Saussurea stella
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Saussurea.
