Spurred mangrove, scientifically known as Ceriops tagal, is a significant coastal shrub valued in traditional medicine. Primarily found in tropical mangrove ecosystems, its bark and leaves possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. It is widely utilized to treat skin ailments, wounds, and various gastrointestinal issues across Southeast Asia.
