Lonicera nummulariifolia

Lonicera nummulariifolia, commonly known as the coin-leaved honeysuckle, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its unique foliage, this plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat inflammatory conditions, promote skin healing, and support overall wellness through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Lonicera nummulariifolia
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Dipsacales, it is a member of the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lonicera.
