Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in the Ivory Coast. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the borders of the Central African Republic. The species also inhabits the territories of Cameroon and Congo. Finally, its range extends through the country of Gabon.
