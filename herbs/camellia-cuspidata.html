Camellia cuspidata

What's the taxonomical classification of Camellia cuspidata?

Camellia cuspidata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Theaceae, falling into the genus Camellia, and is specifically identified by its species name, cuspidata.
