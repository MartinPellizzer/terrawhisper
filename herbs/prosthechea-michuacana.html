Prosthechea michuacana

Prosthechea michuacana, commonly known as a species of orchid, is a fascinating botanical treasure. While primarily celebrated for its stunning, star-shaped blooms and intricate patterns, this plant holds significant interest in ethnobotany. Exploring its unique biological properties offers valuable insights into the diverse potential of tropical flora for traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Prosthechea michuacana
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
