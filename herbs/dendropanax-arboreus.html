Dendropanax arboreus

Dendropanax arboreus, commonly known as the Golden Canopy Tree, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical rainforests. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, it has been used for centuries in traditional healing to treat respiratory ailments and skin infections, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Dendropanax arboreus
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales and the family Araliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dendropaunaex.
