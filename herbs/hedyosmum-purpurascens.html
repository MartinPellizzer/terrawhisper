Hedyosmum purpurascens

Hedyosmum purpuraescens, commonly known as the purple-flowered hedysarum, is a resilient perennial legume native to various regions. Renowned for its striking purple blossoms, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional practices to address diverse ailments, showcasing its unique bioactive properties and ecological importance.

  • Scientific name: Hedyosmum purpurascens
  • Family: Chloranthaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Chloranthales and the family Chloranthaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Hedyosmum.
