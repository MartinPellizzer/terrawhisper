Linum austriacum subsp. glaucescens

Linum austriacum subsp. glaucescens, commonly known as a subspecies of flax, is a distinctive perennial herb valued for its unique medicinal properties. Characterized by its glaucous, bluish-green foliage, this plant offers therapeutic potential in traditional herbalism, specifically supporting digestive health and providing essential fatty acids through its nutrient-rich seeds.

  • Scientific name: Linum austriacum subsp. glaucescens
  • Family: Linaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Linaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Linum, specifically as the subspecies glaucescens.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the Caucasus and Western Asia regions. Within the Caucasus, its presence is noted in both the North Caucasus and the Transcaucasus. Moving into Western Asia, the species is distributed across the territory of Türkiye. Its range further extends southward into the landscapes of Iraq. Additionally, the plant can be found growing within the borders of Iran.
