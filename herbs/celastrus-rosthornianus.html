Celastrus rosthornianus

Celastrus rosthornianus, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in traditional practices, holds immense botanical interest. Found primarily in tropical regions, this species is valued for its diverse phytochemical properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential bioactive compounds for developing novel therapeutic treatments and enhancing modern pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Celastrus rosthornianus
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Celastrales, it is further categorized under the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Celastrus.
