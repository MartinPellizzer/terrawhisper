Desert quandong, scientifically known as Santalum acuminatum, is a resilient Australian shrub prized for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Its vibrant red drupes are rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and essential fatty acids. Traditionally used by Indigenous Australians, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential in modern holistic wellness treatments.
