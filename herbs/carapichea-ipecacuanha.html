Carapichea ipecacuanha

Carapichea ipecacuanha, commonly known as ipecac, is a significant medicinal plant prized for its potent emetic properties. Derived from its roots, the plant contains emetine, a powerful alkaloid used historically to induce vomiting and treat amoebic dysentery. It remains a vital subject in pharmacological studies regarding amoebicidal treatments.

  • Scientific name: Carapichea ipecacuanha
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae and is identified by the genus Carapichea.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several tropical regions. In the Northwestern Pacific, its presence is noted within the Caroline Islands.
