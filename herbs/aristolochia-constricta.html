, scientifically known as Aristolochia constricta, is a perennial climbing herb native to various tropical and subtropical regions. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the diverse landscapes of Central America. Specifically, its range includes the tropical regions of Costa Rica and Honduras. It can also be found growing within the territories of Nicaragua and Panamá. Beyond the mainland, the species extends its reach into the Caribbean islands.
