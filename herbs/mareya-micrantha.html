Mareya micrantha

Mareya micrantha, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, holds significant importance in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from skin infections to digestive issues. Its unique chemical profile offers promising potential for modern therapeutic development.
