Solanum dulcamara · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (167 studies) · ★★★★☆

, scientifically known as Solanum dulcamara, is commonly called bittersweet nightshade. This climbing perennial is characterized by its distinctive bitter berries and creeping vines. Traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat skin ailments and joint pain, it must be handled with caution due to its inherent toxicity if ingested.
