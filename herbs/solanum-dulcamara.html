Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as bittersweet nightshade, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties derived from its aerial parts. These above-ground structures include the sprawling, twining stems, the ovate green leaves, and the distinctive small flowers that transition from yellow to purple. Additionally, the aerial parts encompass the berries, which change from green to a bright, striking red as they ripen. In herbal medicine, various components of these aerial parts are processed to extract bioactive compounds, though careful attention must be paid to their potent nature.
