Arnebia

Arnebia, commonly known as Bugloss, is a cherished medicinal herb prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its striking blue flowers, this plant contains unique naphthoquinones, such as shikonin. It is widely utilized in dermatological applications to promote skin healing, reduce inflammation, and treat various wounds and skin irritations.

  • Scientific name: Arnebia
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Boraginales and family Boraginaceae, it is classified under the genus Arnebia.
