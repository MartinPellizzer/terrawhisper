Trigonostemon flavidus

Trigonostemon flavidus, commonly known as a significant medicinal climber, belongs to the Menispermaceae family. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is valued for its diverse bioactive compounds. Researchers study its phytochemical properties to explore potential therapeutic applications, particularly for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and various pharmacological benefits in healing.

  • Scientific name: Trigonostemon flavidus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it resides in the family Euphorbiaceae and is identified by the genus Trigonostemon.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is specifically noted in the tropical province of Hainan. Its range extends into the Indo-China peninsula, where it can be found in Laos, Myanmar, and Thailand. Additionally, the species is distributed within the Malesia region, particularly in Malaya. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in different Southeast Asian ecosystems.
