Hypericum legrandii

Hypericum legrandii, commonly known as a unique member of the St. John's wort family, is a medicinal plant native to specific regional ecosystems. Renowned for its distinctive yellow blooms, it holds significant botanical interest. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to explore potential therapeutic applications in traditional and modern herbal medicine.
