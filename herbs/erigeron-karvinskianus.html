Erigeron karvinskianus

Erigeron karvinskianus, commonly known as Karvinsky's fleabane, is a resilient perennial cherished for its delicate, daisy-like blooms. While primarily ornamental, this hardy plant holds potential in traditional herbalism. Its unique chemical profile offers intriguing possibilities for medicinal applications, making it a fascinating subject for botanical and therapeutic study.

  • Scientific name: Erigeron karvinskianus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, it is specifically identified under the genus Erigeron.

This plant exhibits a notable presence across several regions in Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the landscapes of Great Britain and Ireland.
