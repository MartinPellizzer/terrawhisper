Meconopsis

Meconopsis, commonly known as the Himalayan poppy, is a striking medicinal plant native to the high altitudes of the Himalayas. Renowned for its vibrant, cup-shaped blooms, it contains unique alkaloids. Historically, these compounds have been studied for their potential therapeutic effects in treating various ailments and neurological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Meconopsis
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is found within the genus Meconopsis.
