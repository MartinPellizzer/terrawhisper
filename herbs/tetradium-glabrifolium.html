Tetradium glabrifolium

Tetradium glabrifolium, commonly known as the smooth-leaved tetradium, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Found in specific regional ecosystems, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and therapeutic potential is essential for modern pharmacological research and conservation.

  • Scientific name: Tetradium glabrifolium
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tetradium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Tetradium glabrifolium, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is called 賊仔樹 (zh) and 賊仔樹 (zh-tw), while in mainland China, it is known as 楝叶吴茱萸 (zh-cn). Among the indigenous languages, it is identified as paljaqic (pwn) and Plux (trv).
