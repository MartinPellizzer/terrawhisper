Mexican-indigo, scientifically known as Justicia spicigera, is a vibrant perennial shrub native to Mexico. Renowned for its striking orange-red flowers and lush foliage, it holds significant importance in traditional herbal medicine. Often used to treat inflammation and digestive issues, this versatile plant beautifully blends aesthetic charm with therapeutic potential.
