Cucumis sativus

Cucumis sativus, commonly known as the cucumber, is a versatile climbing vine highly valued for its cooling properties. Beyond its culinary uses, it serves significant medicinal purposes, offering hydration, skin-soothing benefits, and anti-inflammatory effects. Its high water content and essential nutrients make it a staple in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cucumis sativus
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cucurbitales, it is classified under the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its specific genus is identified as Cucumis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse presence across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its distribution includes specific territories such as Denmark, Great Britain, and Sweden. Moving toward the central part of the continent, the species can also be found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, it has been documented growing within the borders of Austria.
