Plant Parts

This plant, scientifically known as Cucumis sativus, utilizes various components for both nutritional and medicinal purposes. The most recognizable part is the fruits, commonly known as cucumbers, which are widely consumed for their hydrating properties. Within these fruits, the tiny seeds serve as a vital source of nutrients, and even a single seed contains concentrated bioactive compounds. Additionally, the cucumber leaf tissue can be utilized in various traditional applications, offering unique phytochemical benefits that complement the uses of the plant's reproductive and edible structures.
