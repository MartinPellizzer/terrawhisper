Oroxylum indicum

Oroxylum indicum, commonly known as Indian Camphor Tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly oxylipin derivatives, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various infections, offering vast potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Oroxylum indicum
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Oroxylum indicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales. It is further categorized into the family Bignoniaceae and the genus Oroxylum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it is primarily found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Additionally, the species can be located across the landscape of Bangladesh. Finally, it inhabits the mountainous regions of the East Himalaya.

Plant Parts

This plant, Oroxylum indicum, is highly valued in traditional medicine for the various therapeutic properties found within its different anatomical structures. The medicinal efficacy is often derived from the stem bark and the root bark, which are frequently processed to extract bioactive compounds. Additionally, the leaves are commonly utilized in herbal preparations, while the large, distinctive fruits serve as a significant component in traditional formulations. Beyond these specific areas, the general bark of the tree contains essential phytochemicals that contribute to its overall healing potential.
