Ligularia altaica

Ligularia altaica, commonly known as the Siberian Ligularia, is a resilient perennial herb native to East Asian montane regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots possess anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. This striking plant, characterized by large, heart-shaped foliage, offers significant therapeutic potential for treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Ligularia altaica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Asterales and placed in the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ligularia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the vast landscapes of northern and central Asia. In the Siberian region, its distribution includes the Altai mountains and the Tuva Republic. Its range extends southward into Central Asia, specifically within the borders of Kazakhstan. Moving eastward, the species is well-established in the Xinjiang province of China. Finally, its geographical footprint encompasses the territory of Mongolia.
