Aesculus assamica

Aesculus chuniana

Aesculus assamica, commonly known as the Assamese horse chestnut, is a rare medicinal plant native to the northeastern regions of India. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, this species is studied for its potential therapeutic applications, offering promising insights into traditional healing practices and modern pharmacological research developments.

  • Family: Sapindaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aesculus assamica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Aesculus.

Common Names
