Dunbaria rotundifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Dunbaria rotundifolia?

Dunbaria rotundifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated within the order Fabales. It is further classified under the family Fabaceae, which places it among the legumes. Ultimately, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Dunbaria, and its unique species name, rotundifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dunbaria rotundifolia has small, trifoliate leaves with rounded leaflets and tiny, pale yellow to white papilionaceous flowers arranged in axillary racemes. The stems are typically slender and can exhibit a trailing or decumbent growth habit. Each leaflet possesses a smooth margin and a distinctively circular shape, which gives the species its specific epithet. The inflorescence consists of several small blossoms that emerge from the leaf axils on short peduncles. Small, cylindrical pods develop from the flowers to facilitate seed dispersal.
