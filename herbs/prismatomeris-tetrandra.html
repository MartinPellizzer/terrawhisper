Prismatomeris tetrandra

Prismatomeris tetrandra, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb, holds profound value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique biochemical properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and therapeutic potential is essential for exploring its contributions to modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Prismatomeris tetrandra
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Prismatomeris, and species name, tetrandra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several regions across Asia. In China, it is found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Its range also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh. Together, these locations highlight the plant's presence across both Chinese and South Asian territories.
