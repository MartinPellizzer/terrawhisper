Cynanchum otophyllum

Cynanchum otophyllum, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional Eastern medicine, is a climbing vine celebrated for its therapeutic potential. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to support metabolic health, combat inflammation, and provide antioxidant benefits, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Cynanchum otophyllum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, falling into the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cynanchum.
