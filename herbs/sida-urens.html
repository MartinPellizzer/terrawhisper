Sida urens

Sida urens, commonly known as creeping sidewalk, is a versatile medicinal herb found across tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and analgesic properties. Often utilized to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and inflammation, it remains a vital resource in ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Sida urens
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Sida urens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae and is categorized under the genus Sida.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a distinct geographical distribution spanning across several key regions of the Atlantic and African continents. Within the Macaronesia archipelago, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. The species also extends into West Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Benin and Burkina. Furthermore, its range continues through the coastal and inland areas of Ghana. Finally, the distribution is completed by its occurrence in Guinea-Bissau.
