Pendulous Monkshood, scientifically known as Aconitum pendulum, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its distinctive, nodding bell-shaped flowers. While highly valued in traditional herbalism for its potent pharmacological properties, it contains powerful alkaloids, requiring extreme caution and expert handling due to its significant toxicity if mishandled.

Plant Parts

This plant, Aconitum pendulum, derives its significant medicinal and toxicological properties primarily from its underground structures. The most critical components are the roots and the rhizomes, which serve as the storage organs for the plant's complex alkaloids. These thickened, fleshy parts are often harvested for biochemical study, though they must be handled with extreme caution due to their high toxicity.
