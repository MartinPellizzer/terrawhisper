Copaifera salikounda

Copaifera salikounda, commonly known as a species of Copaiba, is a significant medicinal tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its resinous oleoresin, it is traditionally utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties. This plant remains a vital resource in ethnobotanical studies and natural pharmacology.
