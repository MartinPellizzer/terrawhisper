Betula pubescens var. pubescens

Betula pubescens var. pubescens, commonly known as the downy birch, is a resilient deciduous tree native to northern landscapes. Valued in traditional herbalism, its bark and leaves possess significant bioactive properties. This variety is frequently studied for its potential anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and antiseptic qualities in various medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Betula pubescens var. pubescens
  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Europe
