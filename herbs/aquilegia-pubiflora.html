Aquilegia pubiflora

Aquilegia pubiflora, commonly known as the hairy columbine, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Characterized by its delicate, spurred flowers and soft, pubescent foliage, this plant holds significant interest in ethnobotany. Its unique morphological features make it a vital subject for studying regional biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Aquilegia pubiflora
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
