Common jasmine, scientifically known as Jasminum officinale, is a fragrant, climbing evergreen shrub celebrated for its intoxicating aroma. Renowned in traditional medicine, its blossoms possess calming, antiseptic, and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used in aromatherapy and herbal infusions, this versatile plant offers profound therapeutic benefits for relaxation and wellness.
