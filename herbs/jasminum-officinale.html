Common jasmine, scientifically known as Jasminum officinale, is a fragrant, climbing evergreen shrub celebrated for its intoxicating aroma. Renowned in traditional medicine, its blossoms possess calming, antiseptic, and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used in aromatherapy and herbal infusions, this versatile plant offers profound therapeutic benefits for relaxation and wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Jasminum officinale, is most highly valued for its fragrant flower, which serves as the primary part used in traditional and modern medicine. These delicate, white blossoms are harvested to create essential oils, infusions, and teas, often utilized for their calming properties and ability to alleviate stress or anxiety. Because the aromatic compounds are concentrated within the petals, the flower remains the most essential component for therapeutic applications.
