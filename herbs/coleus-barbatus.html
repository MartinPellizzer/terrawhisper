Coleus barbatus

Coleus barbatus, commonly known as Blue Bush or Plectranthus barbatus, is a versatile medicinal herb widely used in traditional African and Caribbean medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is frequently employed to treat respiratory issues, digestive ailments, and various skin conditions through herbal infusions.

  • Scientific name: Coleus barbatus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Coleus as Coleus barbatus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In the Macaronesian islands, its presence can be specifically noted in Madeira. Moving to West-Central Tropical Africa, it is found within the borders of Burundi and Rwanda. The species also extends its reach into the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Finally, its range stretches toward Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Eritrea.
