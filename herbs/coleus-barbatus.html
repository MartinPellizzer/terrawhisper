Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In the Macaronesian islands, its presence can be specifically noted in Madeira. Moving to West-Central Tropical Africa, it is found within the borders of Burundi and Rwanda. The species also extends its reach into the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Finally, its range stretches toward Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Eritrea.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Coleus or West Indian Gumbo Limbo, utilizes various structures for its growth and medicinal properties. While some species in this genus are known to develop thickened, starchy tuberous roots for nutrient storage, the primary focus in herbal applications is often on the aerial parts. These above-ground structures consist mainly of the lush, aromatic leaves, which are frequently harvested and used in traditional remedies due to their specific bioactive compounds.
