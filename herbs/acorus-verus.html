Acorus verus, scientifically known as Acorus verus, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Often found in wetland habitats, its rhizomes contain potent bioactive compounds. These properties offer therapeutic benefits, particularly for digestive health and inflammation, making it a vital subject in ethnobotanical research.

Distribution

This plant, Acorus verus, exhibits a specific geographical range localized within the Russian Far East. It can be found growing in the Amur region, which forms part of its natural habitat. The species is also distributed across the Khabarovsk territory and the Kuril Islands. Additionally, its presence is documented within the Primorye area. Finally, the plant extends its reach to the island of Sakhalin.
