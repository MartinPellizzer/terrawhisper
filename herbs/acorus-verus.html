Distribution

This plant, Acorus verus, exhibits a specific geographical range localized within the Russian Far East. It can be found growing in the Amur region, which forms part of its natural habitat. The species is also distributed across the Khabarovsk territory and the Kuril Islands. Additionally, its presence is documented within the Primorye area. Finally, the plant extends its reach to the island of Sakhalin.

Plant Parts

This plant, Acorus verus, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its underground rhizome. This part of the plant contains a high concentration of volatile oil, which provides its characteristic aromatic properties and therapeutic potency. For medicinal applications, the dried rhizome is typically harvested and processed, as it serves as the main source for extracting active compounds used in various healing preparations.
