Taxus mairei · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (122 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Taxus mairei ex, scientifically known as Taxus mairei, is a significant evergreen conifer native to East Asia. Highly valued in pharmacology, this medicinal plant is a primary source of taxanes, complex compounds used extensively in cancer chemotherapy. Its alkaloids play a crucial role in modern oncology and botanical research.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In mainland China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central and North-Central territories. It can also be found growing in the Southeast regions of China. Furthermore, the species is located on the island of Hainan. Finally, its geographical range extends to the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia.
