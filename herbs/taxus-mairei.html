Taxus mairei

Taxus mairei, commonly known as Maire's yew, is a distinctive coniferous evergreen native to the mountainous regions of China. Renowned for its elegant, dense foliage and ornamental value, this species belongs to the Taxaceae family. It is studied for its potential pharmacological properties and its role in forest ecology.

  • Scientific name: Taxus mairei
  • Family: Taxaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is classified under the order Pinales and the family Taxaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Taxus.
