Phlomis orientalis

Phlomis orientalis, commonly known as Jerusalem Sage, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its architectural beauty and medicinal potential. Featuring velvety, sage-like foliage and dramatic yellow flower spikes, this hardy plant has traditionally been used in folk medicine to support respiratory health and soothe various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis orientalis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Phlomis.
