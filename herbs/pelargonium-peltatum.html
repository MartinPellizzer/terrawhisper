Pelargonium peltatum

Pelargonium peltatum, commonly known as the trailing geranium, is a versatile ornamental plant prized for its cascading habit and vibrant blooms. While primarily used for landscaping and hanging baskets, its lush foliage and resilient nature make it a staple in gardens, adding colorful, textured beauty to various outdoor spaces.

  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Africa
