Crossopteryx febrifuga

Crossopteryx febrifuga, commonly known as Fever Bark, is a potent medicinal plant prized for its antipyretic properties. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it is primarily utilized to reduce high fevers and combat systemic inflammation. This resilient botanical offers a natural approach to managing temperature regulation and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Crossopteryx febrifuga
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Crossopteryx.
