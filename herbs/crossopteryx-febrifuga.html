Sand Crown-berry, scientifically known as Crossopteryx febrifuga, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its potent antipyretic properties. Thriving in arid coastal regions, this resilient shrub offers traditional healing benefits, primarily used to reduce high fevers and combat systemic inflammation through its unique concentration of bioactive phytochemical compounds.
