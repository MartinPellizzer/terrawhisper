Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Benin and Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found in the Central African Republic and Cameroon. Additionally, its range extends to the territory of Cabinda within the same West-Central region. Collectively, these locations highlight a concentrated habitat within tropical African ecosystems.
