Lithocarpus litseifolius

Lithocarpus litseifolius, commonly known as a significant member of the Fagaceae family, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic potential. Found in specific forest ecosystems, it offers unique phytochemical properties. Research focuses on its bioactive compounds, which may provide antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits for various health applications.

  • Family: Fagaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Fagaceae and the genus Lithocarpus. Together, these taxonomic levels identify the specific biological lineage of Lithocarpus litseifolius.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is documented throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Additionally, the species can be found within the Indo-China region, particularly in Laos.
