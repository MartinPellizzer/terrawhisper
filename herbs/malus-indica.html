Malus indica

Malus indica, commonly known as the Indian Apple or Chinese Apple, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health, boost immunity, and reduce inflammation, offering a natural approach to promoting overall wellness and systemic balance.

  • Scientific name: Malus indica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Rosales, it is further classified under the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Malus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key Asian regions. In China, its presence is primarily noted within the South-Central areas. The Indian Subcontinent hosts the species in specific locations, including Assam, Nepal, and the East Himalaya. Furthermore, its range extends into the Indo-China region, specifically within Myanmar. Together, these locations highlight the plant's preference for diverse subtropical and montane environments.
