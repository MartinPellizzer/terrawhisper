Schisandra

Schisandra, commonly known as magnolia vine or five-flavor fruit, is a versatile medicinal plant revered in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its unique adaptogenic properties, it is used to support cognitive function, balance hormonal health, and reduce fatigue, offering a natural way to enhance vitality and overall resilience.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Austrobaileyales, it is a member of the family Schisandraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Schisandra.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the Russian Far East. Specifically, it can be found growing in the Amur and Khabarovsk territories. Its presence also extends to the Primorye and Sakhalin regions of Russia. Beyond the Russian borders, the species is widely distributed within China. More specifically, its range includes the China South-Central area.
