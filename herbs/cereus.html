Cereus

Cereus, commonly known as night-blooming cereus, is a striking genus of cacti prized for its spectacular, nocturnal blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, various species hold significant medicinal potential in traditional practices, offering antioxidant properties and therapeutic uses that continue to intrigue modern botanical researchers and herbalists alike.

  • Scientific name: Cereus
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Cactaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cereus.
