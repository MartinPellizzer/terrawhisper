Grubovia dasyphylla

Grubovia dasyphylla, commonly known as the "Espino de la Sierra," is a resilient medicinal shrub native to specific South American ecosystems. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to address various ailments, offering significant potential for future pharmacological research and therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Grubovia dasyphylla
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Grubovia, which includes the species Grubovia dasyphylla.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Middle Europe, its presence is specifically noted within Germany. Moving eastward, the species can be found growing in the Siberian region of Tuva. Its range extends significantly into Central Asia, where it inhabits the landscapes of Kazakhstan and Kirgizstan. Additionally, the distribution of this medicinal plant reaches further south into Turkmenistan.
