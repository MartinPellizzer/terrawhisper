Phanera aurea, scientifically known as Phanera aurea, is a magnificent climbing woody vine celebrated in tropical landscapes. Renowned for its vibrant, golden-yellow blooms, this botanical treasure is highly valued in traditional medicine. Its various parts are utilized for their therapeutic properties, offering significant potential in holistic healing practices.
