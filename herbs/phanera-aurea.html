Phanera aurea

Phanera aurea, commonly known as the Golden Shower tree, is a magnificent tropical legume celebrated for its cascading yellow blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional medicine utilizes various parts of the plant to treat ailments, highlighting its vital role in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Phanera aurea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Its specific taxonomic designation is found within the genus Phanera.

Common Names

This plant, Phanera aurea, is known by various common names across different linguistic contexts. In Chinese, it is frequently referred to as 牛蹄藤, a name that is consistently used across different regional standards including zh, zh-cn, and zh-tw. Additionally, the plant is sometimes identified by the names 黄麻藤 and 火索藤 in Chinese nomenclature.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range within the Asian continent. Its presence is notably documented across various regions of China. Specifically, it can be found inhabiting the China South-Central area. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout China Southeast. These locations define the primary natural habitats for this medicinal plant.
