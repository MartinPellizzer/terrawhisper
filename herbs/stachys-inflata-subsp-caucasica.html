Stachys inflata subsp. caucasica

Stachys inflata subsp. caucasica, commonly known as Caucasian Bugle, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Caucasus region. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. Its medicinal potential lies in its diverse phytochemical profile, offering therapeutic applications in treating inflammation and supporting various systemic wellness functions.

  • Scientific name: Stachys inflata subsp. caucasica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, this medicinal subspecies is situated within the genus Stachys.
