What's the taxonomical classification of Tephroseris kirilowii?

Tephroseris kirilowii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, specifically categorized within the genus Tephroseris, and is identified by its unique species name, kirilowii.
