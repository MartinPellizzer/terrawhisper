Colic root, scientifically known as Dioscorea villosa, is a perennial climbing herb native to North America. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains bioactive compounds like diosgenin. Traditionally used to alleviate menstrual cramps, digestive discomfort, and inflammatory conditions, this versatile plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical research.
