Dioscorea villosa

Dioscorea villosa, commonly known as wild yam, is a perennial climbing herb native to North America. Valued in traditional herbalism, this plant contains various steroidal saponins, including diosgenin. These compounds are frequently utilized in pharmacological research for their potential roles in hormone regulation and supporting overall reproductive health.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea villosa
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dioscoreales, it is a member of the family Dioscoreaceae. It is specifically classified under the genus Dioscorea.
