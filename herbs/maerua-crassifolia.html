Maerua crassifolia

Maerua crassifolia, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, is highly valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Maerua crassifolia
  • Family: Capparaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales, it is classified under the family Capparaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Maerua, with its species name being crassifolia.
