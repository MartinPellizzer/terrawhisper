Heliotropium ophioglossum

Heliotropium ophioglossum, commonly known as Snake Tongue, is a significant medicinal herb used extensively in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, this plant is frequently employed to treat various ailments, including skin disorders, inflammation, and neurological issues, offering valuable insights into natural healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Heliotropium ophioglossum
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Heliotropium.

Common Names

This plant, Heliotropium ophioglossum, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as snake tongue or viper's bugloss. Moving into Spanish, it is frequently called lengua de serpiente, while in Portuguese, it is known as língua de serpente. In French, the plant is identified as langue de serpent, and in Italian, it is referred to as lingua di serpente.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the Northeast Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its range extends to the territories of Djibouti, Somalia, and the island of Socotra. In addition to its African presence, it can be found across the Arabian Peninsula. Its distribution in this area includes the countries of Oman and Yemen.
