Frankenia pulverulenta

Frankenia pulverulenta, commonly known as the "Dusty Frankenia," is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Native to arid regions, it possesses significant therapeutic potential, particularly for treating skin ailments and inflammation. Its unique phytochemical profile offers promising insights for modern pharmacological research and natural medicine development.

  • Scientific name: Frankenia pulverulenta
  • Family: Frankeniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is positioned in the family Frankeniaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Frankenia.
