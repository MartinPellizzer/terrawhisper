Cedrelopsis grevei

Cedrelopsis grevei, commonly known as Madagascar Periwinkle or Ylang Ylang Madagascar, is a remarkable medicinal plant endemic to Madagascar. Renowned for its essential oil, it offers potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile shrub is highly valued in traditional medicine and modern aromatherapy for its therapeutic healing benefits.

  • Scientific name: Cedrelopsis grevei
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Cedrelopsis.
