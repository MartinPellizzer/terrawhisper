Euphorbia pulcherrima

Euphorbia pulcherrimas, commonly known as Poinsettia, is a striking ornamental plant celebrated for its vibrant, colorful bracts. While primarily grown for its aesthetic beauty during festive seasons, various cultures have explored its traditional medicinal uses, specifically for treating inflammation and skin ailments, though caution is advised due to toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia pulcherrima
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Euphorbia.
