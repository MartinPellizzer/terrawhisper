This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Achyranthes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the Mediterranean and beyond. In Middle Europe, its presence can be noted within the country of Austria. Moving toward the Mediterranean coasts, it is found in Southwestern Europe in both Spain and Sardegna. The species also inhabits Southeastern Europe, specifically within the territory of Sicilia. Finally, its range extends into Northern Africa, where it grows in Tunisia.
