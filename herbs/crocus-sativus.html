Crocus sativus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1465 studies) · ★★★★★

Saffron crocus, scientifically known as Crocus sativus, is a perennial plant celebrated for its precious, crimson stigmas. Harvested manually, these threads provide saffron, the world's most expensive spice. Beyond culinary brilliance, this remarkable plant offers significant medicinal benefits, including antioxidant, antidepressant, and anti-inflammatory properties used in traditional healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Crocus sativus, is a perennial herb highly valued for its unique reproductive structures. The most precious part of the plant comes from its purple flowers, which contain three long, crimson-colored stigmas. Each individual flower produces these distinct stigma threads, which are carefully hand-harvested to produce the world's most expensive spice, saffron. Once the dried stigmas are collected and processed, they are used globally for their intense flavor, vibrant coloring properties, and significant medicinal benefits.
