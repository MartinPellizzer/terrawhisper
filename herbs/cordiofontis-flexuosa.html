Cordiofontis flexuosa

Cordiofontis flexuosa, commonly known as a vital component in traditional herbal medicine, is a remarkable botanical species valued for its therapeutic properties. This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
