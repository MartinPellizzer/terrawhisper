Combretum comosum var. dolichopetalum

Combretum consum var. dolichopetalum, commonly known as a specialized variety of the Combretum genus, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its unique floral structure, this botanical specimen offers potent bioactive compounds, providing essential therapeutic properties used to treat various ailments in diverse ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Combretum comosum var. dolichopetalum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Myrtales, falling into the family Combretaceae. Specifically, it is a variety of the genus Combretum, known scientifically as Combretum consors var. dolichopetalum.
