Diospyros canaliculata

Diospyros canaliculata, commonly known as the Canela or Cinnamon Ebony, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its versatile therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits through its unique bark and fruit.

  • Scientific name: Diospyros canaliculata
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is a member of the family Ebenaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Diospyros.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Within this region, its presence has been documented in Ghana and the Ivory Coast. It also extends further into the coastal areas of Liberia. The species can be found stretching into Nigeria, representing a significant part of its West African range. Additionally, its distribution reaches into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the territory of Cabinda.
