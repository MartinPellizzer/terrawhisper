Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern that spans across both Atlantic islands and the African mainland. Within the Macaronesian region, its presence is noted in Cape Verde. Moving to the African continent, it is found throughout various parts of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its range includes the nations of Benin, Burkina, and Gambia. Furthermore, the geographical reach of this species extends into Ghana.
