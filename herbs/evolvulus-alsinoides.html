Evolvulus alsinoides

Evolvulus alsinoides, commonly known as Shankhapushpi, is a revered medicinal herb in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent nootropic properties, it enhances memory, cognitive function, and mental clarity. This small, creeping perennial is widely utilized to alleviate stress, anxiety, and neurological disorders, making it a cornerstone of traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Evolvulus alsinoides
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Evolvulus.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern that spans across both Atlantic islands and the African mainland. Within the Macaronesian region, its presence is noted in Cape Verde. Moving to the African continent, it is found throughout various parts of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its range includes the nations of Benin, Burkina, and Gambia. Furthermore, the geographical reach of this species extends into Ghana.
