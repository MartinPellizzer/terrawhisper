Krugiodendron ferreum

Krugiodendron ferreum, commonly known as Iron-Leaf, is a rare medicinal powerhouse celebrated for its potent antioxidant properties. Native to dense tropical rainforests, this resilient shrub contains unique bioactive compounds used traditionally to treat inflammation and bolster immune function, offering immense potential for modern pharmacological research and therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Krugiodendron ferreum
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Rhamnaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Krugiodendron, which includes the species Krugiodendron ferrum.
