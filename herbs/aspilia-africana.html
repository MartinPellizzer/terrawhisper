Aspilia africanica, scientifically known as Aspilia africanica, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Widely utilized in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties. Its various parts are frequently employed to treat skin infections, gastrointestinal issues, and diverse inflammatory conditions.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aspilia africana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the Asteraceae family and is categorized under the genus Aspilia.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its presence is also documented in Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species inhabits parts of the Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends into Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, Aspilia africana, is a versatile medicinal species where various components are utilized for healing purposes. The leaf is often prepared as a poultice, while the broader collection of leaves can be decocted to treat different ailments. In some traditional practices, a specific wild leaf may be sought out for its potent properties. Furthermore, the roots are frequently harvested for their therapeutic strength, and even a single, concentrated root extract can be used in traditional medicine to address various health concerns.
