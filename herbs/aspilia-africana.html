Aspilia africana

Aspilia africana, commonly known as the African fever grass, is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its potent bioactive properties, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat wounds, skin infections, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential in contemporary pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Aspilia africana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aspilia africana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the Asteraceae family and is categorized under the genus Aspilia.

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its presence is also documented in Burkina Faso.
