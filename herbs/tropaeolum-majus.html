Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as nasturtium, is a versatile herb where almost every component offers therapeutic potential. The vibrant, trumpet-shaped flowers are not only edible but are often used to infuse oils or teas, while the large, rounded leaves provide a rich source of vitamins and antioxidants. In various traditional medicinal practices, concentrated leaf extracts are utilized for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Additionally, the small, rounded fruits can be harvested, completing the botanical profile of this nutrient-dense species.
