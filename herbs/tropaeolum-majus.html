Tropaeolum majus

Tropaeoluum majus, commonly known as nasturtium, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its potent bioactive compounds. Rich in glucosinolates, it possesses remarkable antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. Traditionally used to support respiratory health and immunity, its edible flowers and leaves offer both nutritional benefits and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Tropaeolum majus
  • Family: Tropaeolaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Brassicales. Finally, it falls into the family Tropaeolaceae and is identified by the genus Tropaeolum.
