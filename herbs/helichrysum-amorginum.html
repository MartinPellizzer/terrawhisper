Helichrysum amorginum

Helichrysum amorginum, commonly known as the golden everlasting, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional remedies to support skin health, promote wound healing, and alleviate various inflammatory conditions through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum amorginum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Helichrysum amorginum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Helichrysum.
