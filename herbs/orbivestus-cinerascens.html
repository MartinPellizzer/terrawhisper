Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in the country of Senegal. Moving toward the Northeast Tropical Africa region, it can be found in Chad. The distribution continues through Djibouti and Eritrea within that same northeastern sector. Finally, the plant is also documented to grow in Ethiopia.
