Dendrolobium triangulare subsp. triangulare

Dendrolobium triangulare subsp. triangulare, commonly known as a prized orchid from the tropical forests of Southeast Asia, holds significant value in traditional medicine. Renowned for its restorative properties, this botanical species is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to enhance vitality, support immune function, and promote overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Dendrolobium triangulare subsp. triangulare
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Fabales, it is situated in the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dendrolobium, specifically as the subspecies triangulare.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. It is also found naturally in the island territory of Taiwan, which is part of Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species extends its range into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within Assam. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various subtropical environments.
