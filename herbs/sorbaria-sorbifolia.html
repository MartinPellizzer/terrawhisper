Sorbaria sorbifolia

Sorbaria sorbifolia, commonly known as the Mountain Sorbus, is a resilient medicinal shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, its berries and leaves are traditionally utilized to support digestive health and boost immunity. This versatile plant remains a vital resource in herbal medicine and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Sorbaria sorbifolia
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sorbaria.
