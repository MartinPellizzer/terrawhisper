Viburnum opulus

Viburnum opulus, commonly known as Guelder-rose or European Cranberrybush, is a versatile deciduous shrub celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bright red berries and medicinal leaves, it is frequently utilized to support cardiovascular health, soothe inflammation, and manage hormonal balance through its diverse bioactive phytochemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Viburnum opulus
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales, it is classified under the family Viburnaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Viburnum.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the northern regions of Europe. It can be found growing naturally throughout the various landscapes of Denmark.
