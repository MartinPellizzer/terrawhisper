Narrow Male-fern, scientifically known as Dryopteris affinis subsp. cambrensis, is a resilient fern prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its delicate, feathery fronds, this subspecies is often utilized in folk medicine for its purported anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, serving as a significant botanical resource in various historical healing practices.
