Miconia willdenowii

Miconia willdenowii, commonly known as the "palo de la lluvia," is a versatile medicinal plant native to South American forests. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This resilient shrub offers significant ethnobotanical potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Southern America
