Chinese parsnip

Saposhnikovia divaricata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (223 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese parsnip, scientifically known as Saposhnikovia divaricata, is a perennial herb deeply rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is primarily used to dispel wind and eliminate dampness. This potent botanical is frequently employed to treat ailments like skin itching, joint pain, and seasonal colds.
