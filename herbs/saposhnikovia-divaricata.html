Saposhnikovia divaricata

Chinese parsnip

Saposhnikovia divaricata, commonly known as Feng Yang Huo in Traditional Chinese Medicine, is a perennial herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Primarily utilized to expel wind and alleviate dampness, its roots contain bioactive compounds effective against skin ailments, joint pain, and respiratory issues, making it a cornerstone of herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Saposhnikovia divaricata
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Saposhnikovia.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Northern and Central Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found specifically within the areas of Buryatiya and Chita. Its range extends further east into the Russian Far East, spanning the Amur and Primorye regions. Additionally, the species is documented in Central Asia, specifically within Kazakhstan. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varying continental climates.
