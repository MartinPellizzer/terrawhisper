Kaempferia rotunda

Kaempferia rotunda, commonly known as Round-leaved Kaempferia, is a perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine. Native to Southeast Asia, this aromatic plant features lush, circular leaves and delicate flowers. Its rhizomes are highly valued for their potent bioactive compounds, offering significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and digestive therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Kaempferia rotunda
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, falling into the family Zingiberaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Kaempferia and the specific species rotunda.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence extends to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in Assam. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various subtropical environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Kaempferia rotundifolia, is a medicinal herb where the primary part utilized for its therapeutic properties is the tuberous rhizome. These underground, fleshy stems serve as the plant's storage organs, concentrating the essential oils and bioactive compounds that contribute to its healing potential. In traditional medicine, the tuberous rhizome is often harvested, dried, and processed into various forms to treat various ailments, making it the most vital component of the species.
