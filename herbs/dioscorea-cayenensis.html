Dioscorea cayenensis

Dioscorea cayenensis, commonly known as cassava yam, is a vital tuberous plant native to West Africa. Renowned for its nutritional density, it serves as a primary staple crop. Beyond sustenance, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance, utilized in traditional medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and therapeutic properties across various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea cayenensis
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Dioscoreales and the family Dioscoreaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Dioscorea.
