Cussonia zimmermannii

Cussonia zimmermannii, commonly known as the Droomoedbos, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its unique foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Indigenous communities utilize its various parts to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin conditions, showcasing its profound traditional therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Cussonia zimmermannii
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is further categorized under the family Araliaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Cussonia, specifically as the species Cussonia zimmermannii.

Common Names

This plant, Cussonia zimmermannii, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Cabbage Tree, while in Afrikaans, it is commonly called die Koolsboom. These names reflect its distinctive foliage, which resembles large cabbage leaves.
