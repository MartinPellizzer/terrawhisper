Black Plum, scientifically known as Vitex doniana, is a versatile multipurpose tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of the plant are used to treat ailments like malaria, inflammation, and digestive issues. Beyond medicine, its nutrient-rich fruits serve as a vital food source.

Distribution

This plant, Vitex doniana, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing naturally within the borders of Benin. Additionally, its presence is documented throughout the nation of Burkina. The species also inhabits various parts of Ghana and the Gambia. Finally, its range extends into the coastal territories of Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, Vitex doniana, possesses various anatomical components that are utilized for different therapeutic purposes. The leaves and individual leaf extracts are frequently employed in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, while the stem bark is often decocted to manage inflammatory conditions. Furthermore, the root serves as a vital medicinal source in many ethnobotanical practices. Beyond its internal medicinal uses, the plant also provides wooden chewing sticks derived from its branches, which are commonly used for oral hygiene and dental care.
