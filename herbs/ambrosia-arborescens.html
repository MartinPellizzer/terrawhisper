Ambrosia arborescens

Ambrosia arborescens, commonly known as the Divine Tree, is a rare medicinal plant revered for its potent healing properties. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, its bark and leaves contain unique bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments. This botanical treasure offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ambrosia arborescens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Ambrosia.
