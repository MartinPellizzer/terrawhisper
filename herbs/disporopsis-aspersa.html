Disporopsis aspersa

Disporopsis aspersa, commonly known as the "rough-leaved plant," is a significant medicinal herb utilized in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently employed to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin conditions, offering a natural approach to wellness through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Disporopsis aspersa
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
