Microtea debilis

Microtea debilis, commonly known as the creeping microtea, is a resilient, low-growing herb found in tropical wetlands. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, it remains a vital subject in ethnobotanical and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Microtea debilis
  • Family: Microteaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Microtea debilis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Microteaceae and the genus Microtea.
