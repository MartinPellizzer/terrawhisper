Beaumontia brevituba

Beaumontia brevituba, commonly known as the short-tubed beaumontia, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical regions. Renowned for its striking, fragrant bell-shaped flowers, this botanical specimen holds significant interest in traditional medicine. Its unique physiological properties offer potential therapeutic benefits, making it a subject of ethnobotanical study.

  • Scientific name: Beaumontia brevituba
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
