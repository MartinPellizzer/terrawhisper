Vogtia microphylla

Vogtia microphylla, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in certain tropical regions, is a significant botanical species valued for its bioactive properties. Often utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, this plant contains unique phytochemicals that offer potential therapeutic benefits, making it a subject of interest for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Vogtia microphylla
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is specifically classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Vogtia, with its specific epithet being microphylla.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range within the southwestern region of Europe. Its natural habitat is primarily concentrated in the Iberian Peninsula.
