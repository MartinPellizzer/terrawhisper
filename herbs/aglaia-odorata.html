Aglaia odorata

Chinese perfume tree

Aglaia odorata, commonly known as the fragrant Aglaias, is a tropical evergreen tree celebrated for its sweet, jasmine-like blossoms. Native to Southeast Asia, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in herbal practices for its aromatic properties and potential therapeutic benefits, offering a unique blend of beauty and botanical utility.

  • Scientific name: Aglaia odorata
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Sapindales, it is a member of the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Aglaiat.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Aglaiia odorata, is recognized by various common names across different linguistic and cultural contexts. In Simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 米仔兰 (zh-hans), while in Traditional Chinese (zh-hant) and the Cantonese-speaking regions of Hong Kong (zh-hk), it is known as 米仔蘭. In the context of the Bishnupriya Manipuri language, it is called Culan (bjn), and in Indonesia, it is commonly known as Pacar cina (id).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several specific regions in Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the Hainan province as well as throughout China Southeast. Moving into the Indo-China region, the species can be found growing in Cambodia. It also maintains a distribution within the borders of Laos. Finally, the plant is documented to inhabit various areas of Thailand.
