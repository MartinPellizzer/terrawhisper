Chinese perfume tree, scientifically known as Aglaia odorata, is a fragrant evergreen tree native to East Asia. Renowned for its intensely sweet, jasmine-like blossoms, it is highly valued in traditional medicine. Its various parts are utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues, blending beauty with therapeutic potential.
