Fumewort, scientifically known as Corydalis, is a diverse genus of perennial herbs within the Papaveraceae family. Renowned for their delicate, often arching foliage and striking, spurred flowers, these plants offer significant medicinal potential. Historically used in traditional medicine, they contain bioactive alkaloids with profound sedative and analgesic properties.
