Corydalis

Corydalis, commonly known as fumitory, is a genus of flowering plants in the Papaveraceae family. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various species contain alkaloids used in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues and respiratory ailments. These versatile plants are valued in pharmacology for their complex chemical compositions and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
