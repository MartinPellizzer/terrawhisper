Weeping elder, scientifically known as Sambucus pendula, is a striking ornamental cultivar prized for its dramatic, pendulous branches. While closely related to the medicinal elderberry, this weeping variety is primarily grown for its sculptural beauty in landscapes, offering lush, cascading foliage that adds elegance to any botanical collection.
