Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions of the South American continent. In Northern South America, its presence is noted within the borders of Guyana. Moving toward the western part of the continent, it can also be found in Bolivia. Within Brazil, the species occupies a broad range that includes the West-Central region. Furthermore, its geographical reach extends to both the Northeast and Southeast regions of Brazil.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as the soursop or guanábana, offers various components that are utilized for their therapeutic potential. The large, green leaves are frequently employed in traditional medicine, often prepared as infusions or topical poultices to address inflammation and various ailments. Additionally, the fruit peels contain bioactive compounds that are studied for their antioxidant properties and are sometimes utilized in specialized medicinal preparations.
