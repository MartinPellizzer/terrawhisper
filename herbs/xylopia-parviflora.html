Xylopia parviflora

Xylopia parviflora, commonly known as the small-flowered xylopia, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, offering valuable phytochemicals for modern pharmacological research.
