What's the taxonomical classification of Saxifraga imparilis?

Saxifraga imparilis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Saxifrages (Saxifragales) and is a member of the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, the plant is situated within the genus Saxifraga, specifically identified as the species imparilis.
