Croton grandivelum

Croton grandivelum, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, belongs to the Euphorbiaceae family. Native to tropical regions, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse pharmacological properties. It is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for developing new therapeutic treatments and natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Croton grandivelum
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Croton grandivelum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales. It is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and is specifically classified under the genus Croton.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several distinct regions of South America. Within the western part of the continent, it can be found growing in Bolivia. Its distribution extends significantly into Brazil, spanning the West-Central, Southeast, and South regions. Additionally, the species inhabits the southern reaches of the continent within Paraguay. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environmental range occupied by this medicinal species.
