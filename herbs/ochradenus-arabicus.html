Ochradenus arabicus

Ochradenus arabicus, commonly known as the Desert Amber plant, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid Arabian landscapes. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this botanical treasure is traditionally utilized to treat skin inflammations and digestive ailments, offering a unique chemical profile vital for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Ochradenus arabicus
  • Family: Resedaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ochradenus arabicus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Brassicales. Finally, it is a member of the Resedaceae family within the genus Ochradenus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution centered primarily within the arid regions of Western Asia and the Arabian Peninsula.
