Renowned for its distinct minty flavor, this plant offers significant health benefits, including potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable staple in holistic wellness practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Perilla frutescens, utilizes various anatomical structures for its medicinal and biological functions. The aromatic leaves are the most frequently harvested part, often used in traditional remedies for their antioxidant properties, while a single individual leaf can serve as a concentrated source of essential oils. The plant also produces small, nutrient-dense seeds that are often utilized in culinary and therapeutic applications. Structurally, the upright stems provide the necessary support for the foliage, and the underground roots help anchor the plant while absorbing vital nutrients from the soil.
