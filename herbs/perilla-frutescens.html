Perilla frutescens

Deulkkae

Perilla frutescens, commonly known as Shiso or Perilla, is a versatile aromatic herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Widely utilized in East Asian cuisine and traditional medicine, this plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It serves as a vital botanical resource for managing respiratory ailments and digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Perilla frutescens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Perilla.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Perilla frutescens, is recognized by various common names across different cultures and languages. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans), it is called 紫苏, while in Traditional Chinese (zh-hant), it is referred to as 紫蘇. In Poland, the plant is known as pachnotka bazyliowata (pl), and in Nepal, it is called सिलाम (ne). Additionally, in Korea, it is commonly known as 들깨 (ko).
