Physiology

The physiology of Perilla frutescens is characterized by a C3 photosynthetic pathway, which dictates its carbon fixation process and metabolic efficiency under varying environmental conditions. As a C3 plant, it utilizes the enzyme Rubisco to fix atmospheric carbon dioxide directly into a three-carbon compound during the Calvin cycle. Furthermore, the plant is not a nitrogen fixer; it lacks the specialized symbiotic relationships with nitrogen-fixing bacteria, such as rhizobia, required to convert atmospheric nitrogen into usable forms, and therefore relies on the uptake of available nitrates and ammonium from the soil to fulfill its nutritional requirements.
