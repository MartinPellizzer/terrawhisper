Sphaeropteris medullaris

Sphaeropteris medullaris, commonly known as the Tree Fern, is a remarkable medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its versatile therapeutic properties, its pith and sap are traditionally used to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin conditions, offering a rich natural resource for holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Sphaeropteris medullaris
  • Family: Cyatheaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic details place it in the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Cyatheales, specifically within the family Cyatheaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Sphaeropteris.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide and diverse geographical distribution across several distinct island regions. Within the Macaronesian archipelago, it can be found growing in Madeira. Its presence extends to the Southwestern Pacific, specifically documented in Fiji. In the region of New Zealand, the species inhabits both the North and South Islands. Finally, its range includes the remote Chatham Islands located near New Zealand.
