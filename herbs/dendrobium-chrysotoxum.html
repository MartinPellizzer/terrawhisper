Dendrobium chrysotoxum

Dendrobium chrysotoxuim, commonly known as the Golden Dendrobium, is a captivating orchid species prized for its vibrant blooms and therapeutic potential. Deeply rooted in traditional herbal practices, this medicinal plant is frequently utilized for its restorative properties, offering a unique intersection of botanical beauty and holistic wellness applications.
