Nepeta annua

Nepeta multifida

Nepeta annua, commonly known as annual catnip, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its calming properties, it is frequently used in traditional remedies to soothe anxiety, aid sleep, and reduce fever. Its aromatic leaves offer potent therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness and relaxation.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta annua
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Nepeta.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Northern and Central Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found inhabiting the Altai, Krasnoyarsk, and Tuva regions. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it is documented in Inner Mongolia. Furthermore, the species is also known to grow within the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environmental range of Nepeta annua.
