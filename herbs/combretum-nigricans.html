Combretum nigricans

Combretum nigricans, commonly known as the Black Combretum, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical African regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Combretum nigricans
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it falls into the family Combretaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Combretum and the species name nigricans.

Common Names

This plant, Combretum nigricans, is known by several distinct common names depending on the linguistic group. In the language of ha, it is referred to as Tsiriri, while in dag it is called Yuleŋga tabele, and in bm it is known as Samabali.

Distribution

This plant, Combretum nigricans, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Additionally, its presence is documented in the nation of Burkina. The species also extends its range into the territory of Gambia. Finally, this medicinal plant is distributed through Ghana and Guinea-Bissau.
