Astragalus

Astragalus, commonly known as huang qi in Traditional Chinese Medicine, is a powerful adaptogen used to bolster the immune system. Renowned for its ability to support vitality and fight fatigue, this resilient root helps the body manage stress, promotes longevity, and strengthens the body's natural defenses against illness.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Its specific taxonomic classification is defined by the genus Astragalus.
