Astragalus

Rattle-pod

Astragalus, commonly known as huang qi in Traditional Chinese Medicine, is a powerful adaptogen used to bolster the immune system. Renowned for its ability to support vitality and fight fatigue, this resilient root helps the body manage stress, promotes longevity, and strengthens the body's natural defenses against illness.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Its specific taxonomic classification is defined by the genus Astragalus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Astragalus, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic context and region. In Simplified Chinese, it is commonly called 黄芪属 (zh-hans) or 黄芪属 (zh-cn), while in Traditional Chinese contexts, it is identified as 黃芪 (zh-hant), 黄芪 (zh-hk), or 黄芪 (zh-sg).

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Astragalus, is highly valued in traditional medicine, with its medicinal properties primarily concentrated in its roots. Specifically, the root of the plant is harvested for its therapeutic benefits, though it is important to note that it does not utilize rhizomes in the same manner as other medicinal herbs. One of the most common traditional preparations involves using radix astragali mixfry with honey, a process where the dried root is prepared to enhance its efficacy. Whether referred to as radix astragali mix-fry with honey or simply as a honey-based decoction, this method of combining the root with honey is a staple in herbal practices to support the body's vital energy.
