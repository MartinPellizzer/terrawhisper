What's the taxonomical classification of Seseli delavayi?

Seseli delaavay belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Apiales and is a member of the family Apiaceae. Finally, the plant is placed within the genus Seseli, specifically identified as the species delaayi.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause severe skin irritation, blistering, and chemical burns upon contact due to the presence of furanocoumarins which trigger photosensitivity. When the sap touches the skin and is subsequently exposed to sunlight, a reaction known as phytophotodermatitis occurs. This process often results in redness, swelling, and painful lesions that may last for several days. If the plant is accidentally ingested, it can lead to significant gastrointestinal distress or more systemic toxicity. Users should handle the species with extreme caution and avoid any skin exposure to its juices.
