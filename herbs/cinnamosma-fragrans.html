Cinnamosma fragrans

Cinnamosmosa fragrans, commonly known as Cinnamon Bark, is a highly aromatic medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and antioxidants, it is widely used in traditional medicine to support digestive health, improve circulation, and reduce inflammation, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Cinnamosma fragrans
  • Family: Canellaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Canellales and the family Canellaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Cinnamosmum.
