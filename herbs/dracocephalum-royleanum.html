Dracocephalum royleanum

Dracocephalum royleanum, commonly known as Royle's dragonhead, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate blue flowers, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Dracocephalum royleanum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, specifically within the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Dracocephalum.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Dracocephalum royleanum, is known by various regional names across different languages. In English, it is commonly referred to as Royle's dragonhead, while in Hindi, it is known as Blue dragonhead or Neelkanth. In Nepali, the plant is called Neelkanth, and in Tibetan, it is referred to as Blue dragonhead.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. In the northern reaches of its range, it can be found growing in West Siberia. Moving southward, the species is widely distributed throughout Central Asia. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of Kazakhstan and Kirgizstan. Its presence further extends to the regions of Turkmenistan and Tadzhikistan.
