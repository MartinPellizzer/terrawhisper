This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. In the northern reaches of its range, it can be found growing in West Siberia. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Royle's dragonhead, contains various bioactive components distributed throughout its structure that contribute to its therapeutic properties. One of the key elements found within the plant is its essential oils, which are primarily concentrated in the aromatic leaves and flowering tops, providing the distinct scent and pharmacological potency required for medicinal use. Additionally, the plant contains seed mucilage, a viscous substance derived from the seeds that can be utilized for its soothing and demulcent qualities in traditional applications.
