Angophora hispida

Angophora hispida, commonly known as the Red Abelia, is a striking Australian native shrub celebrated for its vibrant, bell-shaped crimson flowers. While primarily ornamental, its unique botanical properties and resilient nature make it a subject of interest in ethnobotanical studies, showcasing the rich biodiversity of its natural habitat.
