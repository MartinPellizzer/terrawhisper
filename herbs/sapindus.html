Soapberry, scientifically known as Sapindu, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its unique saponin content. Often utilized in traditional healing, its berries and bark possess antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient species serves as a natural cleanser and a valuable resource in holistic pharmacology and herbal medicine.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Sapindus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Africa. In Northern Africa, its presence can be specifically noted in Tunisia. The species also extends to the Macaronesian region, with occurrences recorded in Cape Verde. Moving into West Tropical Africa, it is found in Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Additionally, the plant is distributed within the West Tropical African nation of Mali.
