Cordia oncocalyx

Cordia oncocalyx, commonly known as "Palo de Agua," is a significant medicinal shrub native to South American landscapes. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammatory conditions and digestive issues, offering valuable insights into natural ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Cordia oncocalyx
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed by the genus Cordia.
