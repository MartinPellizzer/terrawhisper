Crassocephalum bougheyanum

Crassocephalum bougheyanum, commonly known as the Button Bush, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues, owing to its potent bioactive compounds and antioxidant qualities.
