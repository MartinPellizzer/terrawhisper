Nigella sativa

Nigella sativa, commonly known as black cumin or black seed, is a highly revered medicinal plant used for centuries in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, these small black seeds offer diverse therapeutic benefits, supporting immune health, digestion, and metabolic function in various holistic practices.

  • Scientific name: Nigella sativa
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is further categorized under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Nigella.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is noted within the territories of Germany as well as the Czechia-Slovakia region. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it can be found growing throughout Southwestern Europe, specifically in France. The species also extends into Southeastern Europe, where it is established in Bulgaria. Finally, its range continues further south into the coastal and inland areas of Greece.
