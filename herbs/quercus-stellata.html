Quercus stellata

Post oak

Quercus stellata, commonly known as the Post Oak, is a resilient deciduous tree native to North American woodlands. Renowned for its rugged bark and distinctive lobed leaves, this species offers significant medicinal potential. Traditional practices utilize its tannins and bark extracts to treat various ailments, highlighting its profound ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Quercus stellata
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
