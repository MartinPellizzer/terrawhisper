Cachrys sicula

Cachrys sicula, commonly known as Sicilian cachrys, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its striking, deeply lobed foliage and yellow flowering heads, this plant holds significant botanical interest. It is studied for its unique phytochemical properties and its role within diverse ecological habitats.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
