Acalypha

Acalypha, commonly known as copperleaf or chenille plant, is a versatile genus within the Euphorbiaceae family. Renowned for its striking, colorful foliage, this medicinal herb is valued in traditional healing. It possesses significant anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, frequently used to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Acalypha
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Acalypha.
