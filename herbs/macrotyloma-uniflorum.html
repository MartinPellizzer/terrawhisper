Horse gram

Horse gram, scientifically known as Macrotyloma uniflorum, is a resilient legume highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its nutritional density, it serves as a potent therapeutic agent for managing diabetes, kidney stones, and respiratory ailments. This versatile plant offers significant bioactive properties, supporting overall holistic wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Guinea and Senegal. Moving toward the center of the continent, it can be found within West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, its range extends to Cameroon and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Additionally, the species is recorded in the West-Central Tropical African region of Rwanda.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as moth bean, possesses several components that are utilized for their nutritional and therapeutic properties. The most significant parts are the beans, which serve as a primary food source and are rich in protein. Within these beans are the seeds, which are the core reproductive units and the primary part harvested for medicinal and dietary use. Furthermore, the extraction of fixed oil from the seeds provides essential fatty acids that contribute to the plant's overall medicinal profile.
