Horse gram, scientifically known as Macrotyloma uniflorum, is a resilient legume highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its nutritional density, it serves as a potent therapeutic agent for managing diabetes, kidney stones, and respiratory ailments.
