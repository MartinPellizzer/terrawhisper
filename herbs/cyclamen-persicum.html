Cyclamen persicum

Cyclamen persicum, commonly known as the Persian cyclamen, is a versatile perennial cherished for its stunning, heart-shaped leaves and delicate blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its tuberous roots are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties and unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Cyclamen persicum
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is a member of the Primulaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cyclamen.
