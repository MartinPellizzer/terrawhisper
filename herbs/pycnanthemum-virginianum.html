Virginia mountain-mint, scientifically known as Pycnanthemum virginianum, is a versatile, aromatic perennial native to North American woodlands. Renowned for its minty fragrance and striking white flowers, it serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators. This hardy plant is highly valued in herbalism and ecological landscaping alike.
