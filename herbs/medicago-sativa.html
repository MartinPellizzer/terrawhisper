Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as alfalfa, possesses various components that are utilized for medicinal and nutritional purposes. The aerial parts of the plant, which include the stems and leaves, are frequently harvested for their high nutrient content and therapeutic potential. When processed, the alfalfa leaf juice serves as a concentrated source of vitamins and minerals, often used in health supplements. Furthermore, the root system plays a vital role in the plant's biology through the presence of nodules, which facilitate nitrogen fixation and support the overall vitality of the nodules and the entire organism.
