Caragana stenophylla

Caragana stenophylla, commonly known as the narrow-leafed peashrub, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to Central Asia. Valued for its ability to thrive in arid, harsh climates, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. It is studied for its potential medicinal properties, particularly regarding its antioxidant and protective capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Caragana stenophylla
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Caragana stenophylla, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling into the order Fabales. Ultimately, it is a member of the Fabaceae family within the genus Caragana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian territory of Siberia, its presence is specifically noted in the Chita region. Moving into China, it can be found within the Inner Mongolia and Manchuria territories. The species also inhabits the China North-Central area, showcasing its adaptability to diverse climates. Finally, its range extends further west into the Qinghai province of China.
