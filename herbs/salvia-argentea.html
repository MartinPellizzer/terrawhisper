Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Europe. In Middle Europe, its presence can be noted specifically within Germany. Its range extends into Southwestern Europe, where it is found in both Portugal and Spain. Furthermore, the species is also located in Southeastern Europe. Within this southeastern region, the plant is documented in both Albania and Bulgaria.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Silver Sage, is characterized by its striking foliage, which is the primary part used in various applications. The leaf of Salvia argentea is notably distinctive due to its dense covering of fine, silvery-white hairs, giving it a soft, velvety texture. These leaves are broad and ovate, serving not only as a key diagnostic feature for identification but also as the central component in traditional herbal preparations where its aromatic qualities are utilized.
