What's the taxonomical classification of Celtis bungeana?

Celtis bungeanana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic lineage further, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Cannabaceae. Ultimately, the plant is identified by its genus, Celtis, and its specific species name, bungeanana.
