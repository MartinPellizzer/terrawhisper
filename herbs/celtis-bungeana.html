Celtis bungeana

Celtis bungeanan, commonly known as the Chinese Hackberry, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to support respiratory health and soothe inflammation. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Celtis bungeana
  • Family: Cannabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling into the family Cannabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Celtis.

Common Names

This plant, Celtis bungana, is known by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In German, it is referred to as the Bunges Zürgelbaum, while in Chinese, it is called 小叶朴. It is identified in Russian as Каркас Бунге, and in Arabic, it is known as ميس بونجي. Additionally, in Hebrew, the plant is called מייש בונגה.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions within China. It can be found throughout the South-Central parts of the country, extending into the northern territories. Its distribution further reaches the provinces of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Additionally, the species is documented in the North-Central regions of China. Finally, its presence is also noted within the borders of Qinghai.
