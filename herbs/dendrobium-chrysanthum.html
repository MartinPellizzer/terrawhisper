Dendrobium chrysanthum

Golden flowered dendrobium

Dendrobium chrysanthum, commonly known as the Golden Dendrobium, is a prized medicinal orchid in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, this epiphytic plant is frequently used to nourish the yin, clear heat, and soothe the lungs, making it a vital ingredient in various restorative herbal formulations.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium chrysanthum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is further categorized into the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Dendrobium and the species name Dendrobium chrysanthum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central provinces and the Southeast coastal areas. Its range also extends to the tropical island of Hainan and the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Beyond China, the species is also documented within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in Assam. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environmental niches inhabited by this medicinal species.
