Scutellaria hastifolia

Scutelariia hastifolia, commonly known as spear-leaf scutellaria, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to specific temperate regions. Renowned for its unique lanceolate leaves, this plant has been traditionally utilized in herbal medicine for its potential calming and anti-inflammatory properties, offering a natural approach to supporting holistic wellness and balance.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria hastifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Scutellaria.
