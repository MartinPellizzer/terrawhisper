Polygonum macrophyllum, scientifically known as Bistorta macrophylla, is a perennial herb native to East Asian montane regions. Renowned for its large, lanceolate leaves and vibrant pink flower spikes, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. Its rhizomes are frequently utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties and healing potential.
