Black-hellebore, scientifically known as Veratrum nigrum, is a striking perennial herb native to mountainous regions of Europe and Asia. Renowned for its distinctive white, bell-shaped flowers, this plant contains potent alkaloids. While historically used in traditional medicine, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling and expert pharmacological knowledge.

Plant Parts

This plant, Veratrum nigrum, utilizes various underground structures for its survival and medicinal potency. The medicinal properties are often concentrated within the roots and the thick, fleshy rhizomes that extend horizontally through the soil. Because the root system is deeply integrated with these storage organs, practitioners must carefully harvest the entire subterranean portion to ensure the correct concentration of alkaloids is obtained.
