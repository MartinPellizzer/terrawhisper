Thymus pulegioides

Thymus pulegioides, commonly known as the Pulegoides Thyme, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Characterized by its aromatic foliage and delicate blooms, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional ethnobotany for its antimicrobial properties and its potential role in supporting respiratory and digestive health.
