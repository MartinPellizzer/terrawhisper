Wolffia arrhiza

Wolffia arrhiza, commonly known as a species of watermeal, is among the world's smallest flowering plants. These tiny, floating aquatic organisms are highly nutritious, packed with proteins and essential minerals. Often studied for their rapid growth and potential as a sustainable superfood, they offer significant medicinal and nutritional value.

  • Scientific name: Wolffia arrhiza
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Europe
