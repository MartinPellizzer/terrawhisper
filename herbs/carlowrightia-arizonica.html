Carlowrightia arizonica

Carlowrightia arizoniica, commonly known as Arizona Carlowrightia, is a rare desert wildflower native to the arid landscapes of the Southwestern United States. While primarily celebrated for its striking tubular blooms and ecological role, this resilient plant represents the unique botanical heritage of desert ecosystems, offering profound insights into xerophytic adaptation.

  • Scientific name: Carlowrightia arizonica
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Carlowrightia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across several key regions in the Southwestern United States. Specifically, its range includes the states of Arizona and California. Moving eastward, the species can also be located within the South-Central United States in Texas. The distribution extends significantly into Mexico, where it inhabits the Central region. Finally, the plant is also documented throughout Northeast Mexico.
