Angelica dahurica formosana

Angelica dahurica var. formosana, commonly known as Taiwan Angelica, is a precious medicinal herb valued in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its aromatic roots, it is widely utilized to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and improve blood circulation, serving as a vital natural remedy for various neurological and systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Angelica dahurica var. formosana
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae and the genus Angelica.
