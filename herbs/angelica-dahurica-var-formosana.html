Angelica dahurica formosana, scientifically known as Angelica dahurica var. formosana, is a specialized medicinal variety native to Taiwan. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this plant is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating skin conditions, inflammation, and respiratory ailments through its potent bioactive compounds and aromatic roots.
