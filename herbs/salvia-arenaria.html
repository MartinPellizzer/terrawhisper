Salvia arenaria

Salvia arenaria, commonly known as sand sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to sandy regions. Characterized by its aromatic foliage and delicate flowering spikes, this plant plays a vital role in stabilizing arid ecosystems. Its unique medicinal properties and hardiness make it a fascinating subject for ethnobotanical study.
