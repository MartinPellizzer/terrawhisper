East Indian kino

Pterocarpus marsupium · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (293 studies) · ★★★★★

East Indian kino, scientifically known as Pterocarpus marsupium, is a versatile medicinal plant highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its astringent and antiseptic properties, it is derived from the resinous gum of the tree. This botanical resource is widely used to treat various skin ailments and gastrointestinal issues.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the pouch kalk, utilizes various components for its traditional medicinal applications. The heartwood of Pterocarpus marsupium is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, often being utilized in treatments related to diabetes and inflammation. Additionally, the roots are frequently employed in folk medicine to address various ailments, showcasing the diverse utility of the plant's structural elements in herbal remedies.
