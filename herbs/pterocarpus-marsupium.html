East Indian kino

Pterocarpus marsupium · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (293 studies) · ★★★★★

East Indian kino, scientifically known as Pterocarpus marsupium, is a versatile medicinal plant highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its astringent and antiseptic properties, it is derived from the resinous gum of the tree. This botanical resource is widely used to treat various skin ailments and gastrointestinal issues.
