Distribution

This plant is distributed across several distinct regions throughout East and South Asia. In China, its presence is documented in both the South-Central and North-Central territories. It also inhabits the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found within the East Himalaya range. Finally, its geographical range extends into the country of Nepal.

Plant Parts

This plant, Smilax menispermoidea, utilizes various underground structures for its growth and survival. The medicinal properties are often concentrated in its subterranean components, specifically the rhizome and the root. While the rhizome functions as a horizontal stem that allows the plant to spread and store nutrients, the root system provides essential anchorage and absorbs water from the soil, making both parts vital to the plant's overall biology.
