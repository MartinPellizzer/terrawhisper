Ocotea notata

Ocotea notata, commonly known as “canela-preta,” is a significant medicinal tree native to South American forests. Renowned for its aromatic bark and essential oils, it is utilized in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This botanical species holds great potential for pharmacological research and natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ocotea notata
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Ocotea.
