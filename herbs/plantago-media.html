Hoary Plantain, scientifically known as Plantago media, is a resilient perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Characterized by its hairy leaves and distinctive seed spikes, this plant has been used extensively in traditional medicine to treat skin irritations, inflammation, and respiratory ailments due to its potent healing properties.
