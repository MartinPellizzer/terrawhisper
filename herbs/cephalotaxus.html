Plum-yew, scientifically known as Cephalotaxus, is an ancient evergreen conifer prized for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Beyond its lush, needle-like foliage, certain species are significant sources of taxanes, complex alkaloids used in advanced cancer therapies. This versatile plant remains a vital subject in both botany and pharmacology.
