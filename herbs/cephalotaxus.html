Cephalotaxus

Cephalotaxus, commonly known as plum yew, is a genus of coniferous evergreen trees prized for their ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Beyond their landscape value, these plants are significant biochemical sources, containing unique alkaloids that have garnered extensive scientific interest for their potential therapeutic properties in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Cephalotaxus
  • Family: Cephalotaxaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, it is further categorized into the family Cephalotaxaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Cephalotaxus.
