What's the taxonomical classification of Lysidice rhodostegia?

Lysidice rhodostegia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Fabales. This plant is a member of the family Fabaceae, falling into the genus Lysidice, and is specifically identified by the species name rhodostegia.

According to a study published by "Organic letters", three novel phloroglucinol derivatives known as lysidicins A-C (1-3) have been isolated from the roots of Lysidice rhodostegia. These specific derivatives, identified as 1 and 2, possess a spirocyclic benzodihydrofuran skeleton. The structures of these 3 compounds were elucidated through comprehensive NMR and MS spectroscopic analysis. Furthermore, the relative stereochemistries of 1 and 2 were assigned by NOE or NOESY experiments. A plausible pathway for the biosynthesis of 1-3 from 4 and a ketose derivative was also postulated.
