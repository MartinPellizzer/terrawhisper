Codonoopsis pilosula subsp. tangshen, commonly known as a specialized medicinal herb, is a unique botanical variety valued in traditional pharmacology. Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Asterales and falls into the family Campanulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Codonopsis as the subspecies tangshen.
