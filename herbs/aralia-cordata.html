Japanese-asparagus, scientifically known as Aralia cordata, is a versatile perennial valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, this plant features heart-shaped leaves and edible stems. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits for supporting overall wellness and digestive health.
