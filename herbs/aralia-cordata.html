Aralia cordata

Aralia cordata, commonly known as Heartleaf Aralia, is a resilient perennial prized for its striking, heart-shaped foliage. Native to East Asia, this woodland plant is celebrated in traditional herbalism for its diverse therapeutic potential. Its robust growth and ornamental beauty make it both a medicinal treasure and a garden staple.

  • Scientific name: Aralia cordata
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Araliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aralia, specifically the species Aralia cordata.
