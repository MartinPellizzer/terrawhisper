Allexis cauliflora

Allexis cauliflora, commonly known as the Stem-Flowering Bloom, is a rare medicinal plant prized for its unique reproductive habit. Native to tropical rainforest canopies, its specialized bark contains potent bioactive compounds. Traditional healers utilize its infusions to treat inflammatory ailments, marking it as a significant botanical treasure for pharmacology.
