Surinam quassia, scientifically known as Quassia amaram, is a tropical shrub prized for its intense medicinal properties. Native to the West Indies, its wood yields a vibrant yellow dye and potent bitters. Traditionally used to treat digestive ailments and fever, this plant remains a significant botanical resource in herbal medicine.
