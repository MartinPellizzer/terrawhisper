Arabian Boxthorn, scientifically known as Lycium shawii, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to the arid regions of the Middle East and North Africa. Renowned for its adaptability to harsh desert environments, this medicinal plant offers significant ethnobotanical value, providing various bioactive compounds used in traditional healing practices.
