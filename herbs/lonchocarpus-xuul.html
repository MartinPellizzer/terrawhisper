Lonchocarpus xuul

Lonchocarpus xuul, commonly known as the African rosewood, is a resilient medicinal plant native to arid African savannas. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of this tree are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Lonchocarpus xuul
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
