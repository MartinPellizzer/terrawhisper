Vitis davidii

Vitis davidii, commonly known as David's grape, is a resilient climbing vine native to East Asia. Valued in traditional practices, this botanical specimen offers unique pharmacological potential. Its leaves and fruits are studied for diverse bioactive compounds, highlighting its importance in ethnobotany and modern medicinal research for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vitis davidii
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Vitales, it is classified under the family Vitaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation concludes within the genus Vitis.
