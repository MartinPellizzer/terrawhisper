Polygonatum multiflorum

Polygonatum multiflorum, commonly known as Solomon's Seal, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bell-shaped flowers and rhizomatous roots, it is frequently used to nourish Yin, moisten the lungs, and soothe dryness. This versatile plant plays a vital role in holistic wellness and herbal formulations.

  • Scientific name: Polygonatum multiflorum
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is further categorized into the family Asparagaceae.
