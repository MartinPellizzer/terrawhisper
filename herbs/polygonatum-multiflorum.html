Convallaria multiflora L., scientifically known as Polygonatum multiflorum, is a perennial herb esteemed in traditional medicine. Often called Solomon's Seal, it features arching stems and bell-shaped flowers. Renowned for its tonic properties, the plant is frequently utilized to nourish Yin, calm the heart, and support overall vitality.
