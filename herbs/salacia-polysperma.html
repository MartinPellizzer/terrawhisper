Salacia polysperma belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic journey continues through the order Celastrales and the family Celastraeceae. Finally, the plant is placed within the genus Salacia, specifically identified by the species name polysperma.
