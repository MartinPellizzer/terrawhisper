What's the taxonomical classification of Polygonum lichiangense?

Polygonum lichiangense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows a distinct evolutionary lineage that places it under the order Caryophyllales. This plant is further categorized into the family Polygonaceae, a group known for its diverse characteristics. Within this family, it is identified under the genus Polygonum, eventually reaching its specific designation as the species lichiangense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?
