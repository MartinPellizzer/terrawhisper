Fragaria vesca

Fragaria vesca, commonly known as the wild strawberry, is a versatile woodland plant prized for its aromatic fruit. Beyond its culinary appeal, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Rich in antioxidants and Vitamin C, this species has been traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support digestive health and skin vitality.

  • Scientific name: Fragaria vesca
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, it is a member of the Rosaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Fragaria.
