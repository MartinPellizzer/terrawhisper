Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the wild strawberry (Fragaria vesca), utilizes various anatomical structures for both growth and therapeutic application. The roots consist of a shallow, creeping rhizomatous system that allows the plant to spread horizontally and anchor itself in nutrient-rich soil. The leaves are trifoliate, characterized by their serrated edges and distinct veining, often used in traditional herbalism for their astringent properties. Together, these components, along with the stems and runners, constitute the essential vegetative parts that support the plant's metabolic processes and facilitate its expansion across the forest floor.
