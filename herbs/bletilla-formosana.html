Bletilla formosana

Blettilla formosa, commonly known as the fringed orchid, is a highly valued medicinal plant in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent rhizomes, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, promote wound healing, and manage hematomas. Its unique pharmacological properties make it a vital resource in herbal therapeutics.

  • Scientific name: Bletilla formosana
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae and is identified by the genus Bletilla.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across various regions of East Asia. In China, its distribution spans from the South-Central areas to the North-Central provinces. It is also commonly found throughout the Southeast region of China and within Tibet. Beyond mainland China, the species extends its reach into the Nansei-shotō islands of Eastern Asia. Together, these locations define the specific natural range of Bletilla formosa.
