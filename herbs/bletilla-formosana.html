This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across various regions of East Asia. In China, its distribution spans from the South-Central areas to the North-Central provinces. It is also commonly found throughout the Southeast region of China and within Tibet. Beyond mainland China, the species extends its reach into the Nansei-shotō islands of Eastern Asia.
