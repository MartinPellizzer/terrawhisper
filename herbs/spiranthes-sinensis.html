Spiranthes sinensis

Spiranthes sinensis, commonly known as "Sheng Hua," is a revered medicinal herb in traditional Chinese medicine. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and promote blood circulation, this plant holds significant therapeutic value. Its unique properties make it a vital component in various herbal formulations aimed at restoring bodily balance.

  • Scientific name: Spiranthes sinensis
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Spiranthes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia and the Indian Ocean. Within China, it is found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range extends further into Eastern Asia, where it is specifically documented in Japan. Additionally, the species has a presence in the Western Indian Ocean, specifically on the island of Réunion.
