Potentilla flagellaris

What's the taxonomical classification of Potentilla flagellaris?

Potentiella flagellaris belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the evolutionary hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic lineage further, it falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Potentiella, with its specific identification being the species flagellaris.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

The leaves are trifoliate or occasionally five-foliate, featuring small, serrated leaflets that are often covered in a silvery pubescence.
