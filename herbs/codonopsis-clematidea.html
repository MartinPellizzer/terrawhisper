Codonopsis clematidea

Codonopsis clematidea, commonly known as the Clematis-leaved Codonoopsis, is a rare and captivating medicinal plant native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its striking foliage and unique botanical properties, this species offers intriguing potential in traditional healing practices, inviting researchers to explore its diverse bioactive compounds and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Codonopsis clematidea
  • Family: Campanulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it falls under the family Campanulaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized within the genus Codonoopsis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within Central Asia. It can be found growing in the territories of Kazakhstan and Kirgizstan. Additionally, its range extends into the mountainous landscapes of Tadzhikistan and Uzbekistan. Beyond Central Asia, the species also occupies parts of Western Asia. Specifically, its geographical presence includes the country of Afghanistan.
