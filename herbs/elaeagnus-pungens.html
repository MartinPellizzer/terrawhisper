Thorny olive, scientifically known as Elaeagnu pungens, is a resilient, evergreen shrub native to western North America. Characterized by its leathery, serrated leaves and sharp spines, this hardy plant thrives in coastal environments. It serves as a vital component of native landscapes, providing essential habitat and food for wildlife.

Plant Parts

This plant, Elaeagnus pungens, commonly known as silverthorn, is characterized by its distinctive foliage. The leaves are small, leathery, and oval-shaped, featuring a dark green upper surface and a unique silvery-scaled underside that gives the plant a shimmering appearance. In medicinal contexts, these leaves are the primary part of the plant studied for their potential bioactive properties.
