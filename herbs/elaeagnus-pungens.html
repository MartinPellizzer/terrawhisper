Elaeagnus pungens

Thorny olive

Elaeagnus pungens, commonly known as Silverberry or Beach Silverberry, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to coastal regions. Renowned for its shimmering, silvery foliage and small, edible fruits, this hardy plant plays a vital role in stabilizing dunes and providing nutritional support within diverse maritime ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Elaeagnus pungens
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Elaeagnaceae and the genus Elaeagnus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions. In Northern Europe, its presence can be found within the borders of Great Britain. Moving toward Southwestern Europe, the species is established in both France and Spain. It also extends into Southeastern Europe, specifically inhabiting the territory of Italy.
