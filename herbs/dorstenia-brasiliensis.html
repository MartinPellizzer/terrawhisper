Dorstenia brasiliensis

Dorsetnia brasiliensis, commonly known as the "pau-de-fogo," is a significant medicinal plant native to South American forests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation and infections. Scientific interest continues to grow as researchers explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in modern medicine.
