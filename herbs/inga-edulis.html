Ice Cream Bean, scientifically known as Inga edulis, is a versatile tropical tree prized for its sweet, creamy white pulp. Native to Neotropical regions, it offers nutritional benefits and plays a vital role in agroforestry by fixing nitrogen, enhancing soil fertility while providing delicious, edible fruit for local communities.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern focused primarily within the tropical regions of Africa. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is notably recorded in Nigeria. Its range extends further into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Equatorial Guinea and Gabon. Additionally, the species is distributed across the islands of the Gulf of Guinea. Finally, its geographical footprint reaches into the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
