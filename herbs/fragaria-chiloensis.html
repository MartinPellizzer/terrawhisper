Fragaria chiloensis

Beach strawberry

Fragaria chiloensis, commonly known as the Chiloe strawberry, is a native Chilean plant prized for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, it is traditionally used to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. This wild berry offers a potent natural remedy within South American traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Fragaria chiloensis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Fragaria.
