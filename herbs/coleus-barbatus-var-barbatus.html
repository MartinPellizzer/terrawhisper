Bearded Hullwort

Coleus barbatus var. barbatus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (153 studies) · ★★★★☆

Bearded Hullwort, scientifically known as Coleus barbatus var. barbatus, is a versatile medicinal herb prominent in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is widely utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin inflammations. Its therapeutic potential continues to attract significant pharmacological interest worldwide.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Coleus, specifically the variety barbatus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents and distinct ecological zones. In the Atlantic region, its presence is noted within the Macaronesian islands, specifically in Madeira. Moving toward the African continent, it is found in the West-Central Tropical region, including Rwanda. Its distribution also extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the territories of Eritrea and Ethiopia. Finally, the species can be located within the Horn of Africa in Somalia.
