Coleus barbatus var. barbatus

Coleus barbatus var. barbatus, commonly known as Blue Senna, is a significant medicinal herb used extensively in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its potent purgative and anthelmintic properties, this plant is frequently employed to treat intestinal parasites, constipation, and various digestive ailments, offering valuable therapeutic benefits in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Coleus barbatus var. barbatus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Coleus, specifically the variety barbatus.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents and distinct ecological zones. Moving toward the African continent, it is found in the West-Central Tropical region, including Rwanda. Its distribution also extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the territories of Eritrea and Ethiopia. Finally, the species can be located within the Horn of Africa in Somalia.
