Fumaria parviflora

Fumaria parviflora, commonly known as small-flowered fumitory, is a delicate annual herb belonging to the Papaveraceae family. Native to various regions, this plant is characterized by its tiny, inconspicuous flowers and fine, grayish-green foliage. It holds significant interest in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic potential and bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Fumaria parviflora
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Fumaria parviflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Papaveraceae and the genus Fumaria.
