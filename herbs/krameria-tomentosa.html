Krameria tomentosa

Krameria tomentoza, commonly known as the velvety kramer, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to arid regions. Valued in traditional herbalism, its unique foliage and bark possess therapeutic properties. This plant is frequently studied for its potential bioactive compounds, offering promising insights into natural treatments for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Krameria tomentosa
  • Family: Krameriaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Zygophyllales and the family Krameriaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Krameria.
