Pseudolachnostylis maprouneifolia

Pseudolachnostylis maprouneifolia, commonly known as Maprounei, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Brazil. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin conditions, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its unique bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Pseudolachnostylis maprouneifolia
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Pseudolachnostylis, which includes the species Pseudolachnostylis maprouneiifolia.
