Tabernanthe iboga

Tabernanthe iboga, commonly known as Iboga, is a perennial shrub native to Central Africa. Renowned for its potent psychoactive alkaloids, particularly ibogaine, it holds profound spiritual significance in Bwiti traditions. Beyond ritual use, researchers explore its unique pharmacological potential in treating addiction and managing various neurological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Tabernanthe iboga
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Tabernaemontana.
