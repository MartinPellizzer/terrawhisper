Tabernanthe iboga · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (78 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

bitter-grass, scientifically known as Tabernaemontana iboga, is a perennial shrub native to Central Africa. Renowned for its potent psychoactive alkaloids, particularly ibogaine, this plant holds profound spiritual significance in Bwiti traditions. Beyond ritual use, it is increasingly studied for its unique potential in treating addiction and neurological disorders.

Plant Parts

This plant, Tabernanthe iboga, is a shrub native to Central Africa that is primarily valued for its highly potent chemical properties found within its structure. While various parts of the plant may be studied, the most significant and pharmacologically active component is the root bark. This root bark contains high concentrations of ibogaine and other indole alkaloids, which are the primary constituents used in traditional ceremonies and scientific research for their psychoactive and medicinal effects.
