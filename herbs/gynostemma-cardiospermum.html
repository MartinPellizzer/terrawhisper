Gynostemma cardiospermum

Gymnema sylvestre, commonly known as Gurmar or "sugar destroyer," is a potent medicinal herb native to the tropical forests of India. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, it contains gymnemic acids that temporarily suppress sweet tastes and may help regulate blood sugar levels, making it a vital plant for metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Gynostemma cardiospermum
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gymnostemma, with the species name cardiospermium.
