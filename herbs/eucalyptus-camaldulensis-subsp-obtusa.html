Northern River Red Gum, scientifically known as Eucalyptus camaldulenensis subsp. obtusa, is a resilient Australian native prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its aromatic, antimicrobial essential oils, this subspecies offers significant medicinal potential, particularly in treating respiratory ailments and providing antiseptic benefits through traditional and modern applications.

Distribution

This plant is native to several distinct regions across the vast continent of Australia. Its geographical range includes significant portions of New South Wales where it thrives in specific environmental conditions. Additionally, the subspecies can be found growing within the Northern Territory. It is also widely distributed throughout the state of Queensland. Finally, its presence extends westward into the landscapes of Western Australia.
