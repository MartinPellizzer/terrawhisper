Scrophularia canina canina

Scrophularia caniina subsp. caniina, commonly known as canine figwort, is a perennial herb native to Mediterranean ecosystems. Characterized by its unique glandular flowers, this subspecies holds significant botanical interest. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic properties, making it a subject of ethnobotanical study.
