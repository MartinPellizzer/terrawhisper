Pueraria candollei

Pueraria candollei, commonly known as a versatile medicinal climber, is a significant species within the Fabaceae family. Widely distributed across tropical regions, this plant is prized in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects, making it a valuable resource in herbal pharmacology and ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Pueraria candollei
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is specifically situated in the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Pueraria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions within the Asian continent. In the Indian Subcontinent, its distribution is notably documented in Assam, Bangladesh, and the East Himalaya. Moving into the Indo-China region, the species can also be found throughout the Andaman Islands. Additionally, its range extends into the country of Laos.
