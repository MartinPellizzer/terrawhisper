Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific and limited geographical distribution within tropical and subtropical regions. In Eastern Asia, its presence is notably recorded in Taiwan. Moving further south into the Malesia region, the species can also be found in the Philippines. These two locations represent the primary known habitats for the genus.
