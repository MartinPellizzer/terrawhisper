What's the taxonomical classification of Rubus alexeterius?

Rubus alexeterius belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Rubus and the species alexeterius.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rubus alexeterius has arching, woody stems characterized by a dense covering of small, recurved prickles and a texture that transitions from glabrous to slightly pubescent. The leaves are typically trifoliate or occasionally pentafoliate, featuring sharply serrated margins and a distinctively rugose surface. Each leaflet displays a deep green color on the upper side while the underside often appears paler and more finely hairy. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the terminal ends of the branches, typically presenting white petals. The plant eventually develops aggregate fruits that transition from green to a deep red or black color upon ripening.
