Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Eastern Asia, its presence is noted in both the Nansei-shoto islands and Taiwan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found in Bangladesh. Its range extends into Indo-China, specifically covering the Nicobar Islands.
