Scutellaria

Scutellaria, commonly known as skullcaps, encompasses a diverse genus of flowering plants prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for their calming properties, various species are utilized to alleviate anxiety, insomnia, and inflammation. These herbaceous perennials offer significant therapeutic potential, bridging ancient herbal wisdom with modern pharmacological research and applications.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Scutellaria.
