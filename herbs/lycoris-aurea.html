Within these structures, the middle-layer bulb-scale provides a buffer of organic tissue, while the endothecium bulb-scale serves as a specialized internal layer that contributes to the bulb's structural integrity. These scales are anchored by a system of roots that facilitate nutrient uptake, and the entire structure is organized around a central stem plate, which acts as the vital connection point between the underground storage organs and the emerging aerial shoots.
