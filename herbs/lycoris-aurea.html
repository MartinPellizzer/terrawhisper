Lycoris aurea

Lycoris aurea, commonly known as the golden spider lily, is a striking perennial valued for its brilliant yellow blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant holds significant importance in traditional practices. It contains unique bioactive compounds that offer therapeutic potential, particularly for managing inflammation and supporting overall cellular health.

  • Scientific name: Lycoris aurea
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is further categorized under the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lycoris.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning multiple regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found distributed through the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Its presence extends beyond Chinese borders into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, the species is documented growing in the country of Laos. Additionally, its natural habitat includes the mountainous regions of Myanmar.
