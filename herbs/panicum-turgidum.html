Panicum turgidum

Panicum turgidum, commonly known as swollen panic, is a resilient perennial grass found across various tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting digestive health, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Panicum turgidum
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Panicum turgidum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy concludes within the genus Panicum.
