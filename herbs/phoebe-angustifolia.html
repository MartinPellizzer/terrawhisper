Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is specifically found throughout the South-Central areas. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be located in both Assam and Bangladesh. Furthermore, the species is distributed across the Indo-China region. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of Myanmar and Vietnam.
