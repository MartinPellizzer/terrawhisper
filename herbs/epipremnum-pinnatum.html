Native monstera, scientifically known as Epipremnum pinnatum, is a robust climbing aroid celebrated for its striking, fenestrated foliage. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this vigorous plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used in traditional practices, it offers intriguing potential for pharmacological research regarding its diverse bioactive phytochemical compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In China, its presence is recorded in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Beyond the mainland, it can also be found throughout the Nansei-shoto islands of Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is naturally distributed across the island of Taiwan. These combined locations highlight its significant range within the subtropical zones of the region.
