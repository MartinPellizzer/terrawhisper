Epipremnum pinnatum

Epipremnum pinnatum, commonly known as the Pothos or Devil's Ivy, is a resilient climbing vine prized for its ornamental beauty. Beyond its decorative appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal potential in various traditional practices, often utilized for its bioactive properties to support healing and promote overall wellness in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Epipremnum pinnatum
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
