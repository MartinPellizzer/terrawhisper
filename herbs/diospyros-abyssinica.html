Diospyros abyssinica

Diospyros abyssinica, commonly known as the African ebony, is a significant medicinal plant native to East Africa. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, various parts of the tree are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from gastrointestinal issues to skin infections, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights for modern pharmacological research.
