Potentiilla freyniana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving through its higher taxonomic ranks, the plant falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Potentiella and the unique species epithet freyniana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Potentiella freyniana has woody, perennial stems that arise from a rhizomatous rootstock and bear pinnately compound leaves with five to seven leaflets. The plant produces small, bright yellow flowers that are characterized by five distinct petals and numerous central stamens. These inflorescences are often held on slender pedicels that emerge from the axils of the foliage. After flowering, the plant develops small, aggregate dry fruits known as achenes.
