Podophyllum sinense, scientifically known as Podophyllum sinense, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its rhizomes contain potent podophyllotoxins. These compounds serve as critical precursors in synthesizing life-saving chemotherapy drugs, making this plant a cornerstone of modern pharmaceutical research and oncology.
