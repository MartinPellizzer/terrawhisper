Parthenium argentatum

Guayule

Parthenium argentatum, commonly known as Guayule, is a resilient desert shrub native to the southwestern United States and Mexico. Renowned for its high-quality natural rubber content, this hardy plant offers a sustainable alternative to synthetic materials. Beyond industrial use, it holds significant potential in traditional medicinal applications and biofuel production.

  • Scientific name: Parthenium argentatum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
