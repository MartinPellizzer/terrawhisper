Pulicaria arabica

Ladies' false fleabane

Pulicaria arabica, commonly known as Arabic Fleabane, is a versatile medicinal herb native to the arid regions of the Middle East. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Pulicaria arabica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Pulicaria.
