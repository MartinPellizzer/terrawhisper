Ox Knee, scientifically known as Achyranthes bidentaata, is a versatile medicinal herb widely utilized in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently employed to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its robust roots and leaves offer significant healing potential in herbal remedies.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is positioned in the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Achyranthes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across several distinct global regions. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically within Nigeria. Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, covering both Cameroon and the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, the species is also located in South Tropical Africa, specifically in Angola. Remarkably, its range reaches as far as the Russian Far East in the Primorye region.
