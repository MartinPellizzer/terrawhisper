Chilean Wineberry, scientifically known as Aristotelia chilensis, is a resilient shrub native to the temperate rainforests of Chile. Renowned for its vibrant, edible berries, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential antioxidant properties and its ability to support digestive health.
