Aristotelia chilensis

Aristotelia chilensis, commonly known as Maqui, is a remarkable medicinal shrub native to the temperate forests of Chile. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich berries, this plant is traditionally used to combat inflammation and support digestive health. Its potent bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential in modern natural medicine practices.

  • Scientific name: Aristotelia chilensis
  • Family: Elaeocarpaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Aristotelia chilensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae under the order Oxalidales. Finally, it is a member of the family Elaeocarpaceae and the genus Aristotelia.
