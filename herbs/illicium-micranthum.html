Illicium micranthum

What's the taxonomical classification of Illicium micranthum?

Illicium micranthum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the order Austrobaileyales and the family Schisandraceae. Finally, the plant is placed within the genus Illicium, with its specific designation being the species micranthum.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause severe neurological damage and death if consumed due to the presence of anisatin, a potent neurotoxin. When ingested, the toxin acts on the central nervous system to trigger intense seizures and convulsions. Victims often experience significant gastrointestinal distress, including nausea and vomiting, shortly after consumption. In many cases, the onset of these symptoms is followed by a loss of consciousness or coma. Because of these life-threatening risks, the plant should never be used for medicinal or culinary purposes.
