Micromeles caloneura

Micromeles caloneuron, commonly known as the Golden Berry tree, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Micromeles caloneura
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Micromeles, specifically the species Micromeles caloneuria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within the vast territory of China, it can be found growing in the South-Central areas. Additionally, its presence extends into the Southeast regions of China. Beyond Chinese borders, the species is also documented within the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, one of its notable locations in this region is Vietnam.
