Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of the African continent. Within the region of West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Burundi, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Additionally, its range extends into the East Tropical Africa region. In this eastern territory, the species can be found inhabiting parts of Kenya and Tanzania. Consequently, the geographical footprint of Dolichopentas longiflora spans multiple ecological zones across both central and eastern Africa.
