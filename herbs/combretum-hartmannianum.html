Combretum hartmannianum

Combretum hartmannianum, commonly known as the Hartmann's Combretum, is a versatile medicinal shrub indigenous to various African regions. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, various parts of this plant are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological interest for modern integrative medicine.

  • Scientific name: Combretum hartmannianum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Combretum hartmannianum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the family Combretaceae and the genus Combretum.
