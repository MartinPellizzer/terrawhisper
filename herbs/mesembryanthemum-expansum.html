Mesembryanthemum expansum

Mesembryanthemum expansum, commonly known as the expanding ice plant, is a resilient succulent valued for its unique medicinal properties. Native to arid regions, this plant contains bioactive compounds that offer antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. Its ability to thrive in harsh environments makes it a fascinating subject for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Mesembryanthemum expansum
  • Family: Aizoaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Aizoaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Mesembryanthemum.
