Croton oligandrus

Croton oligandrus, commonly known as a vital medicinal plant in various traditional practices, belongs to the Euphorbiaceae family. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it is utilized in ethnobotany for its potential therapeutic properties. Research continues to explore its efficacy in treating various ailments and its pharmacological importance.

  • Scientific name: Croton oligandrus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Croton.

Distribution

This plant is geographically distributed across several regions within West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the territory of Cabinda. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the country of Cameroon. Additionally, the species inhabits various areas within Congo. Finally, the natural range of this medicinal plant extends into Gabon.
