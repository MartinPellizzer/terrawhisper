Ethiopian-pepper

Xylopia aethiopica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (240 studies) · ★★★★★

Ethiopian-pepper, scientifically known as Xylopia aethiopica, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it serves as a vital component in traditional medicine. It is widely utilized for its antimicrobial, analgesic, and digestive properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in treating various ailments.
