Searsia pentaphyllos, scientifically known as Searsia pentaphyllos, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammation to digestive issues, making it a significant species in ethnobotanical studies and traditional healing.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across parts of Europe and Northern Africa. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Sicilia. Moving across the Mediterranean, it is widely distributed throughout Northern Africa. This distribution includes significant populations in Algeria, Morocco, and Tunisia. Furthermore, its habitat extends into the arid regions of Western Sahara.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the five-leaf karee, is characterized by its distinct compound leaf structure, which typically consists of five leaflets arranged along a central stalk. These leaflets are used in various traditional medicinal practices, often being prepared to treat ailments such as skin irritations or digestive issues. The arrangement and shape of the foliage are key identifying features that distinguish it from other species within the Anacardiaceae family.
