Silybum

Silybum, commonly known as milk thistle, is a resilient perennial herb celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned for containing silymarin, this plant serves as a powerful antioxidant and anti-inflammatory agent. It is most widely utilized in herbal medicine to support liver health and promote cellular regeneration.

  • Scientific name: Silybum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic progression places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is categorized under the genus Silybum.
