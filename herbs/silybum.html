Milk thistle

Silybum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (1 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Milk thistle, scientifically known as Silybum, is a remarkable perennial herb celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned primarily for its ability to support liver health, this plant contains silymarin, a powerful antioxidant. It has been used for centuries to protect the body against various toxins and inflammatory conditions.
