Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within the Indo-China region, it can be found inhabiting the Andaman Islands and the Nicobar Islands. Additionally, its presence is well-documented throughout Thailand. Moving into the Malesia region, the species is known to grow on the island of Jawa. Finally, its range extends across the Malay Peninsula, covering the area of Malaya.

Plant Parts

This plant, Curcuma mangga, is primarily valued for its underground structures known as rhizomes. The medicinal properties of the species are concentrated within the rhizome, which grows horizontally beneath the soil surface. These fleshy rhizomes are harvested and processed to extract their bioactive compounds, making the rhizome the most significant part of the plant for therapeutic use.
