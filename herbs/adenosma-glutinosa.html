Adenosma glutinosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Adenosma glutinosa?

Adenosma glutinosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Lamiales. This plant is a member of the family Plantaginaceae and is specifically identified by its genus, Adenosma, and its species name, glutinosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Adenosma glutinosa has glandular-viscid, lanceolate leaves, woody stems, and small, pinkish to reddish flowers arranged in dense, terminal panicles. Its stems are typically erect and develop a woody structure as the plant matures. The inflorescence consists of numerous small individual flowers that create a crowded appearance at the tips of the branches.
