Hyptis comaroides

Hyptis comaroides, commonly known as the "Mallow-leafed Hyptis," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, reduce inflammation, and support digestive health, making it a valuable botanical resource for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Hyptis comaroides
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Hyptis comaroides, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Hyptis.

Common Names

This plant, Hyptis comaroides, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the sea-mallow, while in Portuguese, it is commonly called malva-do-mar. In Spanish, the plant is known as malva-marinha, and in French, it is called malva de mer.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the southern regions of the South American continent. In Brazil, its presence is documented throughout both the Southeast and the South regions. Moving further south, the species can also be found in the northeastern parts of Argentina. Additionally, its range extends into the territory of Paraguay. Consequently, the geographical footprint of Hyptis comaroides is primarily defined by these interconnected southern territories.
