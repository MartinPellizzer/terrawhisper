Paulownia

Kiri

Paulownia, commonly known as the Empress Tree, is a fast-growing deciduous species celebrated for its stunning foliage and fragrant blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, various species offer medicinal potential, traditionally used in herbal practices to address inflammation and support respiratory health, making it a versatile plant in botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Paulownia
  • Family: Paulowniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Paulowniaceae and the genus Paulownia.
