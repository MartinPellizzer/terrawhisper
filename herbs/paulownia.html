Kiri, scientifically known as Paulownia, is a fast-growing deciduous tree celebrated for its remarkable medicinal properties. Often referred to as the "Empress Tree," its leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to reduce inflammation, support respiratory health, and promote overall vitality through their potent antioxidant effects.
