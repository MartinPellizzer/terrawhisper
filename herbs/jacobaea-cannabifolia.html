Aleutian Ragwort, scientifically known as Jacobaea cannabifolia, is a perennial herb native to the Aleutian Islands and coastal Alaska. Characterized by its distinctive cannabis-like foliage and bright yellow composite flowers, this plant belongs to the Asteraceae family and is an important component of its unique subarctic maritime ecosystem.
