Retama raetam

Retama raetam, commonly known as white broom, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its ability to thrive in arid environments, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is widely studied for its diverse bioactive compounds, offering potential applications in traditional and modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Retama raetam
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Retama raetam, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic position is completed within the genus Retama.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several Mediterranean regions. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Greece and Sicilia. Its range extends significantly into Northern Africa as well. Specifically, the species is documented in Algeria and Libya. Finally, it is also widely distributed throughout Egypt.
