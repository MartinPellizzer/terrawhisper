Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as silver wormwood, is characterized by its distinctive foliage, which serves as the primary source of its medicinal properties. The leaf of Artemia vestita is deeply lobed and covered in a dense layer of fine, silvery-white hairs, giving the plant a soft, velvety texture. These leaves are rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, making them the most significant part used in traditional herbal remedies.
