Artemisia vestita

Artemisia vestita, commonly known as the velvet wormwood, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Distinguished by its silvery, velvety foliage, this aromatic plant has long been valued in traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and insecticidal properties, making it a significant botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia vestita
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artemisia as the species Artemisia vestita.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence throughout various regions of China. It can be found growing in the areas of China South-Central and China North-Central. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the northern reaches of Manchuria. The species is also documented within the provinces of China Southeast and Qinghai. Consequently, these diverse locations highlight the wide ecological range of Artemisia vestita.
