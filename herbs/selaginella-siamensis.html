Selaginella siavensis, scientifically known as Selaginella siavensis, is a versatile clubmoss valued in traditional medicine. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, it is primarily found throughout the South-Central regions. Its presence extends significantly into the Indo-China peninsula, where it inhabits several distinct countries. Specifically, the species can be located within the borders of Cambodia and Laos.
