Selaginella siamensis

Selagineella siavensis, commonly known as a species of spike moss, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it is often utilized for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, offering promising therapeutic potential in treating various ailments and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Selaginella siamensis
  • Family: Selaginellaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Lycopodiidae. Within its taxonomic hierarchy, it is organized under the order Selaginellales and the family Selaginellaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Selaginella.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, it is primarily found throughout the South-Central regions. Its presence extends significantly into the Indo-China peninsula, where it inhabits several distinct countries. Specifically, the species can be located within the borders of Cambodia and Laos. Furthermore, its range includes the territories of both Thailand and Vietnam.
