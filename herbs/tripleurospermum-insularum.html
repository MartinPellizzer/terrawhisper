Tripleurospermum insularum

Tripleurospermum insularum, commonly known as the island chamomile, is a rare medicinal herb endemic to specific coastal regions. Renowned for its delicate white florets, this plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to soothe digestive discomfort and promote overall relaxation.
