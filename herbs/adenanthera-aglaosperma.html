Adenanthera aglaosperma, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, holds significant importance in traditional ethnobotany. Native to specific tropical regions, this plant is valued for its bioactive compounds. This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida.
