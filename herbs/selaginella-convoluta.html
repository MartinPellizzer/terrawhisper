Selaginella convoluta

Selaginella convoluta, commonly known as the spike moss, is a remarkable resurrection plant capable of surviving extreme dehydration. Renowned for its ability to revive after prolonged dormancy, this resilient species is studied for its unique physiological mechanisms, offering significant potential in biotechnological research and medicinal studies regarding cellular stress.

  • Scientific name: Selaginella convoluta
  • Family: Selaginellaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Lycopodiidae, it is organized under the order Selaginellales and the family Selaginellaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Selaginella.
