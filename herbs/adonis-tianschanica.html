Adonis tianschanica

Adonis tianschanica, commonly known as Tien Shan Adonis, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds. Renowned for its cardiac properties, this plant contains glycosides that support cardiovascular health. It thrives in high-altitude ecosystems, serving as a vital natural resource for therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Adonis tianschanica
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Adonis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the diverse landscapes of Central Asia. Specifically, it can be found growing in the natural habitats of Kazakhstan. Its distribution also extends into the mountainous regions of Kirgizstan. Furthermore, the species is native to the Xinjiang region of China.
