Solanum aureitomentosum

Solanum aureitomentosum, commonly known as the Golden Solanum, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, this species contains unique bioactive compounds. Researchers study its extracts for potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, highlighting its significant role in traditional healing practices.

  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa
