Job's Tears

Coix lacryma-jobi · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (486 studies) · ★★★★★

Job's Tears, scientifically known as Coix lacryma-jobi, is a versatile perennial grass valued in traditional medicine and culinary practices. Renowned for its hard, bead-like seeds, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and immune-boosting properties, making it a vital resource in holistic healing and nutritional wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Job's tears, is a versatile medicinal grain where various components offer unique benefits. The most notable part is the seed, which is harvested for its nutritional and therapeutic properties. These hard, pearl-like seeds are often used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation and support kidney health. Additionally, the roots of the plant are sometimes utilized in herbal preparations to address different physiological ailments, making the entire organism a valuable resource in holistic healing.
