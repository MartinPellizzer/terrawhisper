Job's Tears

Coix lacryma-jobi · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (486 studies) · ★★★★★

Job's Tears, scientifically known as Coix lacryma-jobi, is a versatile perennial grass valued in traditional medicine and culinary practices. Renowned for its hard, bead-like seeds, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and immune-boosting properties, making it a vital resource in holistic healing and nutritional wellness.
