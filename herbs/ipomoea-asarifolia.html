This plant exhibits a notable geographical distribution spanning both Atlantic islands and the African mainland. Within the Macaronesian region, it can be found growing in Cape Verde. Its presence extends significantly into West Tropical Africa across several different nations. Specifically, the species is documented in Burkina, Gambia, and Ghana. Plant Parts

This plant, Ipomoea asarifolia, is a climbing herbaceous species where the leaves serve as a primary component for medicinal application. The leaves are typically characterized by their cordate or sagittate shapes and are utilized in various traditional remedies to treat ailments such as skin inflammation and digestive issues. Because the leaves contain bioactive compounds, they are often prepared as poultices or infusions to harness their therapeutic potential.
