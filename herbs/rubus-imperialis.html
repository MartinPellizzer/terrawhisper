Rubus imperialis

Rubus imperialis, commonly known as the Imperial Raspberry, is a distinctive perennial shrub native to the montane regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its striking, large-leaved foliage and delicate flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering both nutritional benefits and traditional medicinal properties within its native ecosystem.

  • Scientific name: Rubus imperialis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling into the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rubus.
