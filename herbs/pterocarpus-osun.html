Distribution

This plant, Pterocarpus osuun, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it is notably found within the borders of Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species is widely distributed across Cameroon and Gabon. Its presence continues through the Congo region as well. Finally, the plant can be located within the vast territory of the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
