Koelreuteria paniculata

Koeleuruteria paniculata, commonly known as the Golden Shower tree, is a stunning ornamental species celebrated for its cascading yellow blossoms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this botanical marvel holds significant potential in traditional medicine, where its various parts are explored for their unique bioactive compounds and diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Koelreuteria paniculata
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Sapindaceae, falling within the genus Koelreuteria.
