Plant Parts

This plant, Koelreuteria paniculata, is characterized by distinct morphological features used in its botanical identification. The flowers are particularly striking, appearing in large, drooping panicles of bright yellow, bell-shaped blossoms that attract various pollinators during the summer months. As the plant matures, these blooms give way to unique, lantern-like seed pods that transition from green to a papery peach or salmon hue, housing the seeds within their delicate, inflated structures.
