Reproduction

The reproduction of Melianthus major is primarily a sexual process, as asexual reproduction is noted to be absent. The plant enters its flowering phase starting in August and continues through until November. The pollination process is characterized by a biotic syndrome, specifically utilizing birds as the primary pollinators.
