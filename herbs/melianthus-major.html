Honey bush, scientifically known as Melianthus major, is a striking perennial shrub native to South Africa. Characterized by its dramatic, dark green, serrated foliage and unique, crimson-red flower spikes, it is highly valued in landscaping. This hardy plant attracts pollinators, adding structural elegance and biodiversity to any garden collection.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans several continents and oceanic regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found specifically within the borders of Spain. Its presence is also notable in Southern Africa, particularly across the Cape Provinces. Furthermore, it occupies a remote location in the Middle Atlantic Ocean on the island of St. Helena. Finally, the species is distributed throughout the Indian Subcontinent, encompassing both India and the East Himalaya.
