Auriculate Dichrocephala, scientifically known as Dichrocephala integrifolia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to African forests. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across two major continental regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is documented in Italy and the European portion of Türkiye. Moving toward the African continent, it is widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Specifically, the species can be found within the borders of Benin, Ghana, and Guinea. This wide distribution highlights the plant's ability to thrive in varied ecological environments across these regions.
