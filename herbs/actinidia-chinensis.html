Actinidia chinensis

Actinidia chinensis, commonly known as the Chinese kiwi, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Rich in vitamin C, antioxidants, and essential nutrients, it supports immune function and digestive health. Beyond its nutritional benefits, its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic properties in modern pharmacological research and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Actinidia chinensis
  • Family: Actinidiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Actinidia chinensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Ericales.
