Spiraea japonica var. acuta

Spiraea japonica var. acuata, commonly known as Japanese Spiraea, is a resilient ornamental shrub valued for its dense foliage and vibrant blooms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, various species within this genus are studied for their traditional medicinal properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits through their unique bioactive chemical compounds.
