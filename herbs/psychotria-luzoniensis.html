Psychotria luzoniensis

Psychotria luzoniensis, commonly known as "Luzon Psychotria," is a significant medicinal shrub native to the Philippines. Renowned for its bioactive properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammation, offering valuable insights into Southeast Asian ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Psychotria luzoniensis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
