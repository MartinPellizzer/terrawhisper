Perilla frutescens var. frutescens

Perilla frutescens frutescens

Perilla frutescens var. frutescens, commonly known as Shiso or Perilla, is a versatile aromatic herb central to East Asian cuisine and traditional medicine. Renowned for its distinctive fragrance, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties used to support digestive and respiratory health.

  • Scientific name: Perilla frutescens var. frutescens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Perilla. Specifically, it is identified by the botanical name Perilla frutescens var. frutescens.
