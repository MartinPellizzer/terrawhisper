Thyme, scientifically known as Thymus, is a versatile aromatic herb celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Rich in thymol, this woody perennial offers powerful antibacterial, antifungal, and antioxidant benefits. Frequently used in traditional remedies to soothe respiratory ailments and aid digestion, it remains a cornerstone of holistic wellness.
