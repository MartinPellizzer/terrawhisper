Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the region of Southeastern Europe. Within this territory, it can be found growing in the country of Albania. Its range also extends across Bulgaria and throughout much of Greece. Additionally, the species is present in Italy, marking its presence in the Mediterranean area. Finally, its distribution includes the European portion of Türkiye.
