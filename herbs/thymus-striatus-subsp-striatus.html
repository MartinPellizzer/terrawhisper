Thymus striatus subsp. striatus

Thymus striatus subsp. striatus, commonly known as striped thyme, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Characterized by its distinctive linear leaves and delicate blooms, this medicinal plant is valued for its aromatic properties and potential therapeutic applications in traditional herbal medicine.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, specifically being a member of the family Lamiaceae. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the region of Southeastern Europe. Within this territory, it can be found growing in the country of Albania. Its range also extends across Bulgaria and throughout much of Greece. Additionally, the species is present in Italy, marking its presence in the Mediterranean area.
