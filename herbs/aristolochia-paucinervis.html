Aristolochia paucinervis

Aristolochia paucinervis, commonly known as a member of the birthwort family, is a medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its unique botanical features, it has historically been studied for its phytochemical properties. However, caution is essential due to the presence of potentially nephrotoxic aristolochic acids.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia paucinervis
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, falling into the family Aristolochiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Aristolochia as the species Aristolochia paucinervis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution primarily concentrated around the Mediterranean region.
