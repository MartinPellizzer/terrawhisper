Plant Parts

This plant, Sesbania herbacea, is a small herb characterized by a complex biological structure that supports its growth and nitrogen fixation. While its leaves, stems, and flowers are utilized in various traditional medicinal practices, the root system plays a vital physiological role through the presence of specialized root nodules. These small, bulbous growths house symbiotic bacteria that convert atmospheric nitrogen into a form the plant can use, thereby enhancing its nutritional profile and contributing to its overall medicinal potency.
