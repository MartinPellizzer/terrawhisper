Lindera aggregata var. aggregata

Lindra aggregata var. aggregata, commonly known as the Pink Sandhill, is a distinctive evergreen shrub native to Australia. Renowned for its vibrant, bell-shaped pink flowers and glossy foliage, this botanical gem is prized in traditional practices and ornamental gardening for its hardiness, unique aesthetic, and subtle medicinal potential.

  • Scientific name: Lindera aggregata var. aggregata
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lindra as the specific variety Lindra aggregata var. aggregata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Beyond mainland China, it can be found growing in the island territory of Taiwan. The species also extends its range into the neighboring country of Japan. Collectively, these locations highlight a significant presence throughout the Eastern Asian landscape.
