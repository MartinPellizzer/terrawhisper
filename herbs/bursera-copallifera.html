Bursera copallifera

Bursera copaillafera, commonly known as Copaifera, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional ethnobotany. Renowned for its resinous properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues. This plant serves as a vital natural resource, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Bursera copallifera
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification
