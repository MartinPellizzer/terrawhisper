Dwarf lilyturf, scientifically known as Ophiopogon japonicus, is a versatile evergreen perennial valued in both landscaping and traditional medicine. Often used as groundcover, this resilient plant holds significant therapeutic importance in East Asian practices, particularly for nourishing blood, calming the spirit, and supporting respiratory and kidney health.
