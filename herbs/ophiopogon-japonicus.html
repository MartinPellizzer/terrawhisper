Dwarf lilyturf, scientifically known as Ophiopogon japonicus, is a versatile evergreen perennial valued in both landscaping and traditional medicine. Often used as groundcover, this resilient plant holds significant therapeutic importance in East Asian practices, particularly for nourishing blood, calming the spirit, and supporting respiratory and kidney health.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Mondo Grass, is valued in traditional medicine primarily for its underground storage organs. The most significant medicinal component consists of the fleshy tuberous roots, which are harvested for their therapeutic properties. These thickened roots develop deep within the soil, forming a robust network of tubers that store essential nutrients. Whether referred to as a single tuber or a collection of many, the medicinal potency is concentrated within this specific root structure.
