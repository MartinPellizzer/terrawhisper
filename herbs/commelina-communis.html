Commelina communis

Commequina communis, commonly known as the common coffeeberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, its berries and leaves are utilized in traditional medicine to support digestive health, boost energy levels, and combat oxidative stress, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Commelina communis
  • Family: Commelinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Commelinales and the family Commelinaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its classification under the genus Commelina.
