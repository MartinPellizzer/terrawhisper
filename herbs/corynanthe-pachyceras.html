Corynanthe pachyceras

Corynanthe pachyceras, commonly known as the "tree of life" in some traditional contexts, is a significant medicinal plant native to West African tropical forests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently utilized in ethnomedicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation, infections, and various neurological disorders.

  • Scientific name: Corynanthe pachyceras
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. It is further identified by the genus Corynanthe, specifically as the species Corynanthe pachyceras.
