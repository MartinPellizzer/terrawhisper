Ventilago madraspatana

Ventilago madraspatanana, commonly known as Madras Vine, is a significant medicinal climber used extensively in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering a natural remedy rooted in deep ethnobotanical heritage.

  • Scientific name: Ventilago madraspatana
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ventilago madraspatana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Rosales. Furthermore, it is a member of the Rhamnaceae family and the genus Ventilago.
