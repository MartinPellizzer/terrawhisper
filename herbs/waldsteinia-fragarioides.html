Waldsteinia fragarioides

Geum fragarioides

Waldsteinia fragarioides, commonly known as Barren Strawberry, is a resilient perennial herb prized in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its creeping habit and delicate white flowers, this plant offers therapeutic potential through its bioactive compounds, often utilized for its astringent properties to support skin health and digestive wellness naturally.

  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe
