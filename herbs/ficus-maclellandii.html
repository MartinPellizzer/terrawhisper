Ficus maclellandii

Ficus maclellandii, commonly known as the Indian Laurel or Banyan fig, is a versatile medicinal plant highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, its leaves, bark, and fruit are utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations and respiratory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Ficus maclellandii
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and family Moraceae, it is classified under the genus Ficus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several key regions across Asia. Within China, its presence is primarily concentrated in the South-Central areas. The Indian Subcontinent serves as a major habitat, with specific populations located in Assam, Bangladesh, and the East Himalaya. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the Indo-China region, specifically within Cambodia. Together, these locations highlight the wide ecological spread of Ficus maclellandii.
