Magnolia lucida

Magnolia lucida, commonly known as the glossy magnolia, is a significant medicinal plant prized in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its bark and flowers are often utilized to treat inflammation, respiratory ailments, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to promoting holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia lucida
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, falling into the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Magnolia.

Common Names

This plant, Magnolia lucida, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the glossy magnolia, while in Chinese, it is known as lú lú mù. In Japanese, the species is called kōunmokū, and in Korean, it is identified as gwang-yul-mok.
