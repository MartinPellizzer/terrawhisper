Flat leaved vanilla, scientifically known as Vanilla planifolia, is a fragrant, climbing orchid native to Mexico. Renowned globally for its aromatic pods, this tropical plant produces the world's most sought-after spice. Beyond culinary uses, it offers significant medicinal potential, including antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties in traditional healing.
