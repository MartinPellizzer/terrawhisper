Birthwort, scientifically known as Aristolochia, is a genus of flowering plants valued historically in traditional medicine. While once used to treat inflammation and respiratory issues, modern science warns of its potent nephrotoxicity. Due to the risk of aristolochic acid, many species are now strictly regulated or avoided globally.
