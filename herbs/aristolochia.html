Aristolochia

Aristolochia, commonly known as birthwort, is a genus of flowering plants valued in historical herbalism. While once used for various ailments, modern science warns against its use due to aristolochic acid. This potent compound is nephrotoxic and carcinogenic, making many species strictly regulated or prohibited in medicinal practice today.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Piperales and the family Aristolochiaceae. Ultimately, this medicinal specimen is identified by the genus Aristolochia.
