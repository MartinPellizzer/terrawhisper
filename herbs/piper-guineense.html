West African Pepper

Piper guineense · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (61 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

West African Pepper, scientifically known as Piper guineense, is a versatile climbing shrub renowned for its profound medicinal properties. Widely utilized in traditional African medicine, its pungent fruits and leaves offer potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits, making it a cornerstone of ethnobotanical healing and culinary spice traditions.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Piper guineense, possesses various components that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The woody stem serves as the structural support for the climbing vine, while the vibrant green leaves are the most frequently harvested part for culinary and therapeutic uses. Each individual leaf contains essential oils that contribute to its distinct aroma and healing potential. Furthermore, the plant produces small, dark fruits that contain the seed, both of which are integral to the plant's reproductive cycle and can be processed for their bioactive compounds.
