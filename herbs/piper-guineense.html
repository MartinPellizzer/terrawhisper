West African Pepper, scientifically known as Piper guineense, is a versatile climbing shrub renowned for its profound medicinal properties. Widely utilized in traditional African medicine, its pungent fruits and leaves offer potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits, making it a cornerstone of ethnobotanical healing and culinary spice traditions.
