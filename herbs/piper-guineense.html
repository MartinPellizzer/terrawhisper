Piper guineense

Piper guineense, commonly known as West African black pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and seeds possess potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. It is widely utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Piper guineense
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Piper guineense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Piperales and the family Piperaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Piper.

Distribution

This plant, Piper guineense, exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally in the coastal and forest areas of Benin. Its distribution also extends to the West African nation of Gambia. Furthermore, the species is documented to inhabit the territories of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau.
