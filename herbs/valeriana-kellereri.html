Valeriana kellereri

Valeriana kellereri, commonly known as Kellerer's valerian, is a rare medicinal plant endemic to specific regions of Estonia. Distinguished by its unique floral characteristics, it has historically been valued in traditional herbalism. While less common than other valerian species, it remains a significant subject for botanical and pharmacological study.
