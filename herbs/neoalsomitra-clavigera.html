Neoalsomitra clavigera

Neoalsomitra clavigera, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant, belongs to the Rubiaceae family. Renowned for its unique botanical characteristics, this species holds immense potential in traditional medicine. This plant, Neoalsomitra clavigera, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Neoalsomitra.
