Buxus sinica

Buxus sinica, commonly known as Chinese boxwood, is a significant medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional practices, its various parts are utilized for their therapeutic properties. This plant is frequently studied for its potential bioactive compounds, offering valuable insights into natural pharmacology and holistic healing traditions.

  • Scientific name: Buxus sinica
  • Family: Buxaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic progression places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Buxales, specifically within the family Buxaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Buxus.

This plant, known scientifically as Buxus sinica, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Chinese, it is known as 黄杨 (zh), 黄杨 (zh-cn), 黃楊 (zh-tw), and 黄杨 (zh-hans), while in Macedonian, it is called кинеска зеленика (mk).

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions of East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Beyond mainland China, its range extends into the island nation of Japan. Furthermore, the species can be found growing throughout Korea. These diverse locations highlight the broad geographical footprint of Buxus sinica.
