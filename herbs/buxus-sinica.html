Chinese boxwood

Buxus sinica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (6 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Chinese boxwood, scientifically known as Buxus sinica, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Traditionally used in Chinese medicine, it is highly valued for its bark and roots. These parts are often utilized to clear heat, detoxify the body, and treat various inflammatory and respiratory conditions.
