This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across East Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central regions and across the Southeast. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Lady Palm, is primarily valued for its striking foliage rather than its roots or stems. The most significant part of Rhapis excelsa is its leaves, which are large, fan-shaped, and deeply divided into multiple segments. These dark green, leathery leaves are arranged in clusters atop slender stems, providing both ornamental beauty and a sense of lushness to indoor and shaded outdoor environments.
