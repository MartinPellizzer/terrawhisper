Rhapis excelsa

Rhapis excelsa, commonly known as the Lady Palm, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its elegant, fan-shaped foliage. Native to subtropical forests, this resilient plant thrives in low light, making it an ideal indoor ornamental. Beyond its decorative appeal, it is valued for purifying indoor air quality.

  • Scientific name: Rhapis excelsa
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Arecales and the family Arecaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Rhapis. As Rhapis excelsa, it represents a distinct species within this botanical hierarchy.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across East Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central regions and across the Southeast. It also grows naturally on the tropical island of Hainan. Moving further east, its distribution extends into the country of Japan. Finally, the species is also documented within the Nansei-shoto islands.
