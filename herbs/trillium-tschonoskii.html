Trillium tschonoskii

Trillium tschonoskii, commonly known as the rare woodland trillium, is a distinguished medicinal plant found in shaded forest ecosystems. Renowned for its unique botanical structure, it has been utilized in traditional healing practices to address inflammatory conditions. Understanding its ecological significance and therapeutic potential remains vital for conservation efforts.

  • Scientific name: Trillium tschonoskii
  • Family: Melanthiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Liliales, it is further categorized into the family Melanthiaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement concludes with the genus Trillium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found specifically on the Kuril Islands and Sakhalin. Its range extends significantly into China, covering various regional territories. Within China, it is documented in the South-Central regions as well as the North-Central areas. Furthermore, the species is also distributed throughout the Southeast portion of the country.
