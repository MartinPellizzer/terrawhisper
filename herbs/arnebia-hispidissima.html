Arabian primrose

Arnebia hispidissima · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (18 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Arabian primrose, scientifically known as Arnebia hispidula, is a perennial medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant blue flowers, it is highly valued in traditional medicine. The plant's roots contain potent naphthoquinones, offering significant anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and skin-healing properties used to treat wounds and infections.
