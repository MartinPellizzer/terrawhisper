Psidium guineense

Psidium guineense, commonly known as the Guinean guava, is a versatile medicinal shrub celebrated in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, its leaves and bark are frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin infections, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Psidium guineense
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Ultimately, its scientific designation is placed within the genus Psidium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several key regions across the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically within the Ivory Coast. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species is well-established in Congo, Gabon, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its range further extends southward into the South Tropical Africa region, specifically in Angola. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse ecological niches that Psidium guineense inhabits.
