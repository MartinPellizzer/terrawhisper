Brazilian Guava, scientifically known as Psidium guineense, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, its leaves and fruits are traditionally used to treat digestive ailments, skin infections, and inflammation, offering a rich natural resource for holistic healthcare practices.
