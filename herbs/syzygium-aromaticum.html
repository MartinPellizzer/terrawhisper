Syzygium aromaticum

Syzygium aromaticum, commonly known as clove, is a perennial evergreen tree native to the Maluku Islands. Renowned for its aromatic flower buds, this potent spice is prized in traditional medicine for its antiseptic, analgesic, and anti-inflammatory properties, offering a rich source of essential oils like eugenol for therapeutic use.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium aromaticum
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, scientifically known as Syzygium aromaticum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Syzygium.

This plant exhibits a specialized geographical distribution concentrated primarily within tropical maritime regions.
