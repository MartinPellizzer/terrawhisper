Baccharis concava

Baccharis concava, commonly known as coastal scrub or bitter bush, is a resilient shrub native to coastal regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. Its unique chemical profile makes it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America
