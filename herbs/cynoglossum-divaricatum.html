What's the taxonomical classification of Cynoglossum divaricatum?

Cynoglossum divaricatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Boraginales and is a member of the family Boraginaceae. Finally, its specific biological identity is defined by its placement in the genus Cynoglossum, with the specific epithet divaricatum.
