Plant Parts

This plant, known as Fraxinus, possesses various anatomical structures that are often studied for their medicinal properties. The medicinal compounds are frequently concentrated within the stem bark, specifically within the cortex, which serves as a vital layer of the plant's protective tissue. The foliage is characterized by compound leaves, where each individual leaflet features a prominent midrib that provides structural support and aids in nutrient transport. Additionally, the petiole acts as the essential stalk connecting these leaflets to the main stem, ensuring the efficient movement of water and minerals throughout the plant.
