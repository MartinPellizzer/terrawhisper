Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it resides in the family Oleaceae under the genus Fraxinus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific presence across several regions within Northern Europe. It can be found growing naturally in Denmark, spanning much of the Danish landscape. The species is also well-distributed throughout Great Britain and Ireland. Furthermore, its range extends into the northernmost territories of Norway. Finally, the plant is a documented part of the flora in Sweden.
