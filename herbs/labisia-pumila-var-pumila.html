Scientifically known as Labisia pumila var. pumila, is a perennial herb native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Commonly referred to as "Kacip Fatimah," this medicinal plant is highly revered in traditional medicine for its potent phytoestrogen properties, primarily used to support women's reproductive health and overall hormonal balance.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across Southeast Asia, spanning both the Indo-China and Malesia regions. Within the Indo-China territory, it can be found growing in Cambodia and Thailand. Moving into the Malesia region, the species is widely distributed across several key islands and peninsulas. Specifically, it inhabits the areas of Jawa, Malaya, and Sumatera. Consequently, its presence highlights a significant ecological footprint throughout the tropical landscapes of the region.
