What's the taxonomical classification of Viola formosana?

Viola formosana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Malpighiales and the family Violaceae. Finally, its specific identity is defined by the genus Viola and the species formosana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Viola formosana has an herbaceous perennial growth habit characterized by creeping rhizomes, ovate to cordate leaves with crenate margins, and zygomorphic flowers featuring five violet-colored petals. The foliage typically emerges from a basal rosette, displaying long petioles that support thin, green blades. Its inflorescence produces small, delicate blossoms that often feature a lighter or yellowish center to attract pollinators.
