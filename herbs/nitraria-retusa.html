Nitraria retusa

Nitraria retusa, commonly known as the Nitraria shrub, is a resilient medicinal plant native to arid regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. This hardy species holds significant ethnobotanical value in various traditional healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Nitraria retusa
  • Family: Nitrariaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the family Nitrariaceae and the genus Nitraria.
