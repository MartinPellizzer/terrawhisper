This plant, Gongronemopsis latifolia, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. The species also extends its range into Burkina Faso, contributing to the floral diversity of the area. Furthermore, it can be found throughout the territories of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau.
