Pulicaria jaubertii

Pulicaria jaubertii, commonly known as a member of the Asteraceae family, is a resilient medicinal herb found in various Mediterranean habitats. Valued for its aromatic properties, it contains essential oils with potential therapeutic benefits. This plant is often studied for its bioactive compounds and traditional uses in folk medicine.
