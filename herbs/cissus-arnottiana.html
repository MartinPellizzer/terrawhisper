Cissus arnottiana

Cissus arnottiana, commonly known as the creeping devil, is a versatile medicinal climber native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections, inflammation, and digestive issues, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical research and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Cissus arnottiana
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Vitales, falling within the family Vitaeaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cissus, specifically the species Cissus arnottiana.

Common Names

This plant, Cissus arnottiana, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the creeping vine or the medicinal creeper. In its native regions, it carries specific local identifiers such as Kanchana in Sanskrit, Kanchanam in Tamil, and Kanchana in Telugu. Furthermore, it is known as Kanchana in Kannada and Kanchana in Malayalam, reflecting its widespread traditional use across different southern Indian languages.
