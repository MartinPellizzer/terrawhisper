Saxifraga stolonifera

Saxifraga stolonifera, commonly known as Creeping Saxifrage, is a charming perennial celebrated for its trailing runners and lush foliage. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this resilient plant features traditional medicinal uses, often valued in herbal practices for its soothing properties and ability to support skin health and overall vitality.

  • Scientific name: Saxifraga stolonifera
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Saxifrages and the family Saxifragaecae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Saxifraga.
