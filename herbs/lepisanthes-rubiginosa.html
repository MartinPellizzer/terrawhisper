Lepisanthes rubiginosa

Lepisanthes rubiginosa, commonly known as the rusty-leaved Lepisanthes, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Native to tropical regions, it features unique phytochemical properties used to treat various ailments. Its leaves and bark are frequently utilized in decoctions to promote healing and support overall holistic wellness.
