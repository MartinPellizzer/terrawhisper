Orobanche connata

Orobanche connata, commonly known as a parasitic broomrape, is a specialized medicinal plant found in specific ecological niches. As an obligate parasite, it derives nutrients from host roots. It is valued in traditional medicine for its unique bioactive compounds, which are being increasingly studied for their potential therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Orobanche connata
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Orobanche.
