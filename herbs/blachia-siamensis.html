Blachia siamensis

Blachia siamensis, commonly known as Siamese ginger, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues. This potent botanical offers significant therapeutic potential through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Blachia siamensis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Blachia.
