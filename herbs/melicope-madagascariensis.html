Melicope madagascariensis

Melicope madagascariensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in East Africa, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Belonging to the Rutaceae family, this species is traditionally utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Local healers employ its leaves and bark to treat various ailments, including skin infections, inflammation, and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Melicope madagascariensis
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is further categorized into the family Rutaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Melicope.
