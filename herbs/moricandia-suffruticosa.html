Moricandia suffruticosa

Moricandia suffruticosa, commonly known as the "wild shrub," is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Found in diverse tropical landscapes, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Renowned for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial potential, this resilient species offers promising therapeutic applications in modern pharmacological research and natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Moricandia suffruticosa
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Brassicales and falls into the family Brassicaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Moriandcia.

Common Names

This plant, Morinda suffruticosa, is known by various common names across different linguistic and cultural landscapes. In English, it is frequently referred to as Indian Noni, while in Malay, it is known as Mengkuang Kelapa. In Indonesian, the plant is called Kemunting, and in the local context of certain regions, it is also identified as Morinda.
