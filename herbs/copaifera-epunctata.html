Copaifera epunctata

Copaifera epunctata, commonly known as Copaiba, is a significant medicinal tree native to tropical forests. Renowned for producing a viscous, aromatic oleoresin, it has been used traditionally for its potent anti-inflammatory, antiseptic, and healing properties. Today, it remains a vital botanical resource in various natural therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Copaifera epunctata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
