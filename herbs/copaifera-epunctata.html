Brazilian copaiba, scientifically known as Copaifera epunctata, is a remarkable medicinal tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its nutrient-rich oleoresin, this botanical treasure offers potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties. It remains a cornerstone of traditional medicine, providing natural relief for various skin and internal ailments.

Plant Parts

This plant, Copaifera epunctata, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its liquid exudates. The most significant component is the Copaiba oil, which is a fragrant, viscous substance harvested directly from the trunk. This liquid is technically an oleoresin, a complex mixture of resin and essential oils that possesses potent anti-inflammatory properties. To obtain medicinal preparations, practitioners often utilize the oleo-resin in its pure form or create a concentrated oleoresinous extract to enhance its therapeutic effects. While much of the medicinal value lies in these fluids, the bark of the tree also plays a role in the overall biological profile of the species.
