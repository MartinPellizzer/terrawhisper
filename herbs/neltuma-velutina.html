Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple continents. In Southern Africa, it can be found throughout the Cape Provinces, the Free State, and the Northern Provinces. Its presence extends across the ocean to the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within India. Additionally, the species has established itself in Australia, notably in New South Wales. This wide-ranging occurrence highlights the adaptable nature of its natural habitat.
