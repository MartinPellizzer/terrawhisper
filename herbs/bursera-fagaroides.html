Elephant Tree, scientifically known as Bursera fagaroides, is a distinctive succulent shrub native to the arid regions of East Africa. Renowned for its unique, swollen trunk and peeling bark, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for treating skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions throughout its habitat.
