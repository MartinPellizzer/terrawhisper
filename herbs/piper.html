Piper

Piper, commonly known as pepper, is a diverse genus of flowering plants celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive alkaloids and essential oils, various species offer anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and digestive benefits. Traditionally used in holistic healing, these versatile plants remain vital subjects in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Piper
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Piperaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Piper.
