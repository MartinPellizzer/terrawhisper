Phyllopentas schimperi

Phyllopentas schimperi, commonly known as the "stonecrop" or "living stone," is a remarkable succulent native to arid regions. Renowned for its unique physiological adaptations, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in various cultures for its potential therapeutic properties, offering significant interest for contemporary pharmacological research into its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Phyllopentas schimperi
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, it is specifically situated in the Rubiaceae family. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its classification within the genus Phyllopentas.

Common Names

This plant, Phyllopentas schimperi, is known by various regional names depending on the language spoken in its native habitats. In English, it is commonly referred to as Schimper's stonecrop, while in German, it is known as Schimpers Fetthenne. Localized names also include the Swahili term mti wa mshindo and the Arabic name al-khuzama.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West-Central Tropical Africa region. Within this area, its presence is documented in the country of Burundi. It can also be found throughout the Central African Republic and Cameroon. Additionally, its range extends to the various islands within the Gulf of Guinea. Finally, the species is recorded as growing in Rwanda.
