Cremastra appendiculata

Cremastra appendiculata, commonly known as the "appendiculate cremastra," is a distinctive climbing orchid native to East Asian forests. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is prized for its bioactive compounds. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and inflammation, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential within herbal pharmacology and botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Cremastra appendiculata
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cremastra, specifically as the species Cremastra appendiculata.
