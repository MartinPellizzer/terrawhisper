Cremastra appendiculata, scientifically known as Cremastra appendiculata, is a perennial herb native to East Asian forests. Highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly in China, it is primarily utilized to treat respiratory ailments, alleviate coughs, and reduce phlegm. Its therapeutic properties make it a significant botanical resource in herbal pharmacology.
