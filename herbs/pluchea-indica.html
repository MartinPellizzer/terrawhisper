Indian fleabane

Pluchea indica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (69 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Indian fleabane, scientifically known as Pluchea indica, is a versatile perennial shrub native to coastal regions of Asia. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and small purple flowers, this medicinal plant is widely used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues due to its potent bioactive properties.
