What's the taxonomical classification of Scutellaria tayloriana?

Scutelariia tayloriana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Lamiales. It is a member of the family Lamiaceae, which places it within the genus Scutellaria, and is specifically identified by its species name, tayloriana.
