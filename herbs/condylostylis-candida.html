Condylostylis candida

Condylostylis candida, commonly known as the White Condylostylis, is a rare medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its striking snowy blooms, this species offers unique therapeutic potential. Exploring its bioactive compounds reveals fascinating possibilities for natural healing, making it a subject of significant ethnobotanical interest today.

  • Scientific name: Condylostylis candida
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Condylostylis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range that spans across several distinct regions of the Americas. In North America, its presence is documented within the southeastern parts of Mexico. Moving southward into Central America, it can be found in the territories of Belize, Nicaragua, and Costa Rica. The distribution further extends into the northern reaches of South America. Specifically, this medicinal species has been identified within the country of Venezuela.
