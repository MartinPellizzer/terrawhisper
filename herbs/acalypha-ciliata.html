Acalypha ciliata

Acalypha ciliata, commonly known as Copperleaf, is a versatile medicinal herb found across tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin inflammations, respiratory ailments, and digestive issues. This plant holds significant value in ethnobotany for its diverse healing and antimicrobial properties.

  • Scientific name: Acalypha ciliata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
