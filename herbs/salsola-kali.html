Reproduction

The reproduction of Salsola kali is characterized by a sexual process involving bisexual flowers that typically feature a yellow coloration. The flowering period is relatively concentrated, beginning in July and concluding in August, with individual flowers measuring between 0.4 cm and 0.6 cm in width. The plant utilizes both abiotic and biotic pollination syndromes, specifically relying on wind and insects to facilitate fertilization; notably, self-fertilization is absent. Following successful pollination, the plant produces dry, indehiscent fruits categorized as achenes. These fruits are highly prolific, containing between 101 and 1000 seeds per fruit.
