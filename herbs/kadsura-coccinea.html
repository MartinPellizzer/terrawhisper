Kadsura coccinea

Kadsura coccineae, commonly known as the red kadsura, is a versatile medicinal climber prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its vibrant red fruits and aromatic leaves, this plant contains bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, digestive ailments, and respiratory issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural medicine practices.

  • Scientific name: Kadsura coccinea
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Kadsura coccineas, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Austrobaileyales and the family Schisandraceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Kadsura.

This plant, Kadsura coccinea, is known by several different common names depending on the region and language. In Chinese, it is referred to as 四川黑老虎 (zh/zh-cn/zh-tw), while in Vietnamese, it is commonly called Na rừng (vi). Additionally, in Arabic, the plant is known as كدسورة قرمزية (ar).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its distribution encompasses the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. The species also extends into the Indo-China region, where it can be found in Laos. Additionally, it is documented to grow within the borders of Myanmar. Together, these locations highlight the plant's presence across both Chinese provinces and Southeast Asian territories.
