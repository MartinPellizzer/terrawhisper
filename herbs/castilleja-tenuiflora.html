Castilleja tenuiflora

Castilleja tenuiflora, commonly known as a species of Indian paintbrush, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to specific high-altitude ecosystems. Renowned for its vibrant, colorful bracts, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various cultures have utilized its unique properties to treat diverse ailments, highlighting its profound pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Castilleja tenuiflora
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Castilleja.

Common Names

This plant, Castilleja tenuiflora, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the thin-flowered Indian paintbrush, a name that reflects its distinctive floral structure. In Spanish, it is frequently called pintura de indio or brocha india, which translates the "paintbrush" imagery used in English. These common names highlight the botanical characteristics and cultural perceptions of the species across different linguistic landscapes.
