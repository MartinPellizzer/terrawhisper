Santa Catalina Indian paintbrush, scientifically known as Castilleja tenuiflora, is a rare, endemic wildflower found only in the mountains of Baja California. Distinguished by its vibrant, specialized bracts, this unique species plays a vital role in its fragile ecosystem, offering significant interest for botanical researchers and conservationists alike.
