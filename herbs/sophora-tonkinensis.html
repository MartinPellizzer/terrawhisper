Vietnamese sophora, scientifically known as Sophora tonkinensis, is a medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. This potent botanical offers significant pharmacological potential for modern drug development and natural healing.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it falls under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Sophora, and its species name, tonkinenis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found inhabiting the South-Central areas. Additionally, its presence extends into the Southeast portions of China. The species also grows within the Indo-China region.
