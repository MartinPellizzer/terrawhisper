Prunus spinosissima

Thorny almond

Prunus spinosissima, commonly known as thorny rockrose or dwarf cherry, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to mountainous regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its bark and fruits possess antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This hardy species serves as a vital botanical resource for holistic healing and various therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Prunus spinosissima
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Rosales and falls into the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Prunus.

Common Names

This plant, Prunus spinosissima, is known by various regional names depending on the language and culture. In Uzbekistan, it is referred to as Bodomcha, while in Azerbaijan, it is called Tikanlı badam. In Persian, the plant is known as بادام پرخار, and in Tajikistan, it is commonly called Бодомча. Furthermore, in Chinese, it is identified as 刺扁桃.
