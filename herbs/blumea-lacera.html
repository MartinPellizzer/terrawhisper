Malay Blumea, scientifically known as Blumea lacera, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and skin inflammations. Its unique chemical profile offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the African continent and its surrounding islands. Within Macaronesia, its presence is noted in the archipelago of Cape Verde. Moving to West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found in both the Central African Republic and Cameroon. Its range extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the territory of Chad. Finally, the distribution reaches further east to include the nation of Djibouti.
