Vernonanthura brasiliana

Vernonanthura brasiliana, commonly known as "erva-de-santa-luzia," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Brazil. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, it is traditionally used to treat digestive issues, skin inflammations, and respiratory ailments. Modern research continues to explore its bioactive compounds for diverse therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Vernonanthura brasiliana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant, known scientifically as Vernonanthura brasiliana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
