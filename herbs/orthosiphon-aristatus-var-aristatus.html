Cat's Whiskers

Orthosiphon aristatus var. aristatus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (177 studies) · ★★★★☆

Cat's Whiskers, scientifically known as Orthosiphon aristatus var. aristatus, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its diuretic properties, it is traditionally used to treat kidney stones, urinary tract infections, and diabetes. Its distinctive, slender white flowers make it as beautiful as it is therapeutically potent.
