Cat's Whiskers

Orthosiphon aristatus var. aristatus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (177 studies) · ★★★★☆

Cat's Whiskers, scientifically known as Orthosiphon aristatus var. aristatus, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its diuretic properties, it is traditionally used to treat kidney stones, urinary tract infections, and diabetes. Its distinctive, slender white flowers make it as beautiful as it is therapeutically potent.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence extends to the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is recorded in the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the Assam region. Together, these locations highlight a wide geographical range spanning from Southeast China to parts of India.
