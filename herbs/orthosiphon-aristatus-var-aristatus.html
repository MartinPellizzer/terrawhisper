Orthosiphon aristatus var. aristatus

Orthosiphon aristatus var. aristatus, commonly known as Salam Panas, is a medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent diuretic properties, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat kidney stones, urinary tract infections, and diabetes by promoting fluid excretion and regulating blood sugar levels.

  • Scientific name: Orthosiphon aristatus var. aristatus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Orthosiphon. Specifically, the variety aristatus identifies this particular medicinal species within its taxonomic hierarchy.

Common Names

This plant, Orthosiphon aristatus var. aristatus, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as narrow-leafed Orthosiphon, while in Malay, it is widely recognized as Kerak nasi. In Indonesian, it is also known as Kerak nasi, and in Tagalog, it is called Salamag.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence extends to the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is recorded in the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the Assam region. Together, these locations highlight a wide geographical range spanning from Southeast China to parts of India.
