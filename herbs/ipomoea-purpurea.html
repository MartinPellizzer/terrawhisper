Ipomoea purpurea

Ipomoea purpurea, commonly known as morning glory, is a vigorous climbing vine celebrated for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal potential in traditional practices, often utilized for its diverse bioactive compounds. Understanding its botanical properties offers insights into its therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea purpurea
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Ipomoea.
