Common gorse, scientifically known as Ulex europaeus, is a hardy, evergreen shrub characterized by its vibrant yellow flowers and sharp, spine-like leaves. Traditionally used in herbal medicine for its diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties, this resilient plant thrives in coastal heathlands, playing a vital role in diverse ecosystems worldwide.
