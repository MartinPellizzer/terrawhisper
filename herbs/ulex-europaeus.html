Ulex europaeus

Ulex europaeus, commonly known as gorse, is a hardy, evergreen shrub native to Western Europe. Characterized by its dense, thorny structure and fragrant yellow blossoms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, its wood was used as fuel, while its various parts have featured in diverse folk medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Ulex europaeus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ulex.
