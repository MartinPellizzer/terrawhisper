African spurge

Euphorbia resinifera · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (33 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

African spurge, scientifically known as Euphorbia resinifera, is a perennial plant native to Morocco. Renowned for its milky, toxic latex, it is primarily valued in pharmacology for producing resiniferatoxin. This potent compound is used in medical research to study pain management and develop advanced treatments for chronic neurological conditions.
