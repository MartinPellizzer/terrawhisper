Japanese Larch, scientifically known as Larix kaempferi, is a versatile coniferous tree native to Japan. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its bark and needles are rich in bioactive compounds like antioxidants and flavonoids. These properties offer therapeutic potential in promoting skin health, reducing inflammation, and supporting overall wellness.
