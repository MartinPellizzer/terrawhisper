Larix kaempferi

Japanese larch

Larix kaempferi, commonly known as Japanese Larch, is a versatile coniferous tree valued for its medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like terpenoids and flavonoids, its bark and needles are utilized in traditional therapies to support respiratory health, reduce inflammation, and provide potent antioxidant benefits for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Larix kaempferi
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling into the family Pinaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Larix.
