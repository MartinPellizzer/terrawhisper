Sanicula graveolens

Sanicula graveolens, commonly known as sanicle, is a perennial herb belonging to the Sanicalaceae family. Native to woodland habitats, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive properties. Its roots and foliage are often studied for their potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Sanicula graveolens
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is specifically situated in the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Sanicula.
