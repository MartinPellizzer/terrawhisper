Senegalia rugata

Senegalia rugata, commonly known as Acacia concinna, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its rich bioactive compounds, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. This resilient plant serves as a vital natural resource for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Senegalia rugata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized into the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Senegalia, where it is known as Senegalia rugata.
