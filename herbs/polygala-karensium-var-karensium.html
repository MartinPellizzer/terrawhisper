Polygala karensium var. karensium

Polygala karensium var. karensium, commonly known as a medicinal variant of the Milkwort family, is a botanical treasure valued in traditional healing. This specific variety possesses unique phytochemical properties, often utilized for its purported neuroprotective and anti-inflammatory effects, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and holistic wellness treatments.

  • Scientific name: Polygala karensium var. karensium
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, placing it in the family Polygalaceae. Ultimately, the specimen is identified as a member of the genus Polygala.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central and Southeast territories, as well as in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Its distribution also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically appearing in the area of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented inhabiting the East Himalaya region. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environmental niches where the plant thrives.
