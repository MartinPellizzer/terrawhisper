Solanum incanum

Solanum incanum, commonly known as bitter apple or Sodom apple, is a versatile perennial shrub native to Africa and Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is utilized to treat various ailments, including skin diseases, inflammation, and gastrointestinal issues. However, its potent alkaloids require careful handling due to potential toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Solanum incanum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions in Africa. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented specifically within the country of Egypt. Moving towards the West Tropical Africa region, it can be found in Benin and Burkina. The species also inhabits the territories of Mali and Mauritania. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various West Tropical African environments.
