Paeonia rockii subsp. rockii

Paeonia rockii subsp. rockii, commonly known as the Tree Peony, is a majestic perennial celebrated for its spectacular, large blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this subspecies holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its roots are utilized for their anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties to treat various systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia rockii subsp. rockii
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Saxifragales. Finally, it resides in the family Paeoniaceae under the genus Paeonia.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across multiple distinct regions. In Europe, its presence can be noted within the Baltic States of Eastern Europe.
