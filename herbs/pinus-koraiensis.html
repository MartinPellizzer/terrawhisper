Pinus koraiensis

Pinus koraienensis, commonly known as the Korai pine, is a rare coniferous species endemic to the rugged mountains of Greece. Renowned for its unique ecological significance, this resilient plant offers potential medicinal value through its resin and essential oils, which possess antioxidant and antimicrobial properties used in traditional therapies.

  • Scientific name: Pinus koraiensis
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is classified under the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae. Ultimately, it is identified as a member of the genus Pinus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found spanning the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Its range extends southward into the Manchuria region of China. Additionally, the species is established within the island nation of Japan. Together, these locations define its primary geographical presence in the temperate forests of the East.
