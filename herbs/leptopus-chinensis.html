Leptopus chinensis

Andrachne colchica

Leptopus chinensis, commonly known as Chinese Leptopus, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional Eastern practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, promote healing, and support digestive health, offering a natural remedy backed by centuries of botanical wisdom.

  • Scientific name: Leptopus chinensis
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Phyllanthaceae and falls under the genus Leptopus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Leptopus chinensis, is referred to by various regional and linguistic names across different cultures. In Russian, it is called Лептопус китайский, while in Chinese, it is known as 雀儿舌头. Moving to Northern Europe, it is identified in Swedish as Andrakne, and in Bulgaria, it is called китайски лептопус. Furthermore, in the Ossetian language, the plant is referred to as Æхсымæрзæ.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Eurasia. In the Caucasus region, its presence is noted in both the North Caucasus and the Transcaucasus areas. Moving into Western Asia, the species can be found growing within the borders of Iran. Its range extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the South-Central provinces. Additionally, the plant is documented as being present on the island of Hainan.
