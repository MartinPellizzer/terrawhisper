Sinapis alba

White mustard

Sinapis alba, commonly known as white mustard, is a versatile annual herb belonging to the Brassicaceae family. Renowned for its pungent seeds and vibrant yellow flowers, it has long been utilized in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties, serving as a potent natural therapeutic agent.

  • Scientific name: Sinapis alba
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales, it is classified under the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Sinapis.
