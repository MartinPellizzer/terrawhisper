white mustard

Sinapis alba · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (585 studies) · ★★★★★

White mustard, scientifically known as Sinapis alba, is a versatile annual herb renowned for its pungent seeds and therapeutic properties. Widely cultivated for culinary and medicinal uses, it contains glucosinolates that offer anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits. This resilient plant remains a cornerstone in traditional herbal medicine and flavorful gastronomy.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as white mustard, is utilized in various traditional remedies through specific components of its reproductive structure. Both the seeds and the ripe seeds are of significant interest due to their concentrated chemical properties, particularly the glucosinolates that react to produce pungent oils. While the seeds are often harvested for their immediate therapeutic potential, the use of fully ripe seeds ensures a stabilized potency for medicinal preparations.
