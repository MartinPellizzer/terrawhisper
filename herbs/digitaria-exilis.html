Fonio, scientifically known as Digitaria exilis, is an ancient, resilient cereal grain native to West Africa. Renowned for its tiny, nutrient-dense seeds, this "superfood" is gluten-free and rich in essential amino acids like methionine. It plays a vital role in food security, thriving in poor soils where others fail.
