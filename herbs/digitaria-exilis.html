Digitaria exilis

Fonio

Digitaria exilis, commonly known as finger millet, is a nutrient-dense cereal grain widely cultivated across Africa and Asia. Renowned for its exceptional dietary fiber, minerals, and antioxidants, this resilient crop plays a vital role in food security and traditional medicine, offering significant health benefits for modern nutritional wellness.

  • Scientific name: Digitaria exilis
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and order Poales, it is further categorized under the family Poaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Digitaria.
