Star anise, scientifically known as Illicium, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its potent aromatic properties. Renowned for its unique star shape, this spice contains shikimic acid, a key component in antiviral medications. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to aid digestion, reduce inflammation, and boost immunity.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several distinct regions of East Asia. Within China, its distribution is quite extensive, spanning from the North-Central provinces to the Southeast. It is also documented to grow throughout the South-Central areas of the country. Furthermore, its range extends southward to include the island of Hainan. Finally, the geographical reach of Illicium encompasses Japan, marking its presence in Eastern Asia.
