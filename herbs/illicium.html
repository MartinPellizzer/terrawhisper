Illicium

Star anise

Illicium, commonly known as star anise, is a fascinating genus of aromatic shrubs and trees native to East Asia. Renowned for its unique star-shaped fruit, it is prized in traditional medicine and culinary arts for its potent essential oils, offering powerful antimicrobial, digestive, and antioxidant properties for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Illicium
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Austrobaileyales, it is situated in the family Schisandraceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Illicium.

It is also documented to grow throughout the South-Central areas of the country. Furthermore, its range extends southward to include the island of Hainan.
