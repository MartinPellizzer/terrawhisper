Distribution

This plant, known as Dioscorea cayenensis subsp. rotundata, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its presence can be documented within the borders of Benin. It also grows naturally throughout the territory of Burkina. Furthermore, the species is found in the coastal and inland areas of Gambia. Finally, its geographical range extends into Guinea-Bissau.
