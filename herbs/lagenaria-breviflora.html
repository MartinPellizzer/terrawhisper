Lagenaria breviflora

Lagenaria breviflora, commonly known as the bottle gourd, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently used to treat digestive issues, skin ailments, and inflammation. This nutrient-rich gourd offers significant health benefits, making it a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Lagenaria breviflora
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
