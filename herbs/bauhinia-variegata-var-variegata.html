Bauhinia variegata var. variegata

Bauhinia variegata var. variegata, commonly known as the Orchid Tree, is a striking ornamental plant celebrated for its vibrant, butterfly-shaped blooms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this medicinal powerhouse holds significant value in traditional healing, offering diverse therapeutic properties used to treat various ailments through its potent bark and flowers.

  • Scientific name: Bauhinia variegata var. variegata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
