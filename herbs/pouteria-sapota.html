Pouteria sapota

Mamey sapote

Pouteria sapota, commonly known as sapodilla or chikoo, is a versatile tropical evergreen tree prized for its sweet, malty fruit. Beyond its delicious edible flesh, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various parts utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations.

  • Scientific name: Pouteria sapota
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, specifically being a member of the family Sapotaceae. Within this family, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pouteria.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Pouteria sapota, is recognized by various common names across different languages and regions. In German, it is referred to as Große Sapote, while in Nahuatl, it carries the name Tezontzapocuahuitl.
