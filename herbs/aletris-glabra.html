Aletris glabra

What's the taxonomical classification of Aletris glabra?

Aletris glabra belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Dioscoreales and is a member of the family Nartheciaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Aletris, and its unique species designation, glabra.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aletris glabra has a basal rosette of long, linear, grass-like leaves that are smooth and lack hairs. These leaves grow from a slender rhizome and can reach several inches in length. A single, upright flowering stalk emerges from the center of the foliage to produce small, white, star-shaped flowers. Each flower features six tepals and is arranged in a terminal raceme. The plant typically stands between six and eighteen inches tall depending on its maturity.
