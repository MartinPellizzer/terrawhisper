Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of East and South Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the North-Central territories. Its presence further extends into the Southeast regions of China and high into the plateau of Tibet. Beyond China, the species is also located within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the Assam region. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse ecological range inhabited by Polygala arillata.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Polygala ariaretia (often referred to in traditional contexts related to Polygala species), utilizes its root as the primary medicinal component. The tuberous root contains the concentrated bioactive compounds responsible for its pharmacological properties, making it the most vital part for therapeutic extraction. In many traditional medicinal practices, these roots are harvested, dried, and processed to harness their specific healing qualities.
