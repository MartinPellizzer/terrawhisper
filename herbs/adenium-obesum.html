Desert rose, scientifically known as Adenium obesum, is a striking succulent native to the arid regions of Africa. Renowned for its sculptural, swollen caudex and vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms, this resilient plant thrives in harsh sunlight. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant traditional importance in various medicinal practices globally.
