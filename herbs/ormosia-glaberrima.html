Ormosia glaberrima

What's the taxonomical classification of Ormosia glaberrima?

Ormosia glaberrima belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated within the order Fabales. It further falls under the family Fabaceae, a group known for its diverse legumes. Within this family, the plant is categorized under the genus Ormosia, and its specific identity is defined by its species name, glaberrima.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause severe systemic toxicity due to the presence of abrin-like toxalbumins within its seeds. Ingestion of these toxic compounds typically triggers immediate gastrointestinal distress. Common symptoms include intense nausea and persistent vomiting shortly after consumption. Victims often suffer from debilitating abdominal pain and severe diarrhea as the toxins affect the digestive tract. These physiological reactions can lead to significant dehydration and other serious medical complications.
