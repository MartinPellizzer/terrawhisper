Garcinia yunnanensis

Garcinia yunnanensis, commonly known as Yunnan mangosteen, is a tropical medicinal plant prized in traditional practices. Rich in xanthones and bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-tumor properties. This versatile species continues to garner significant scientific interest for its diverse therapeutic potential.

  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
