Indian Tobacco

Lobelia inflata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (386 studies) · ★★★★★

Indian Tobacco, scientifically known as Lobelia inflata, is a perennial herb native to North America. Traditionally used by Indigenous peoples, it contains lobeline, an alkaloid with stimulant properties. While historically utilized for respiratory and digestive ailments, its potency requires cautious application due to potential toxicity in high doses.
