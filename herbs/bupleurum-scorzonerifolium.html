Bupleurum scorzonerifolium

Bupleurum scorzonerifolium, commonly known as Bupleurum, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, its roots contain bioactive compounds like saikosaponins. These constituents offer potent anti-inflammatory, hepatoprotective, and immunomodulatory properties, making it a vital botanical resource for supporting liver health and managing systemic inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum scorzonerifolium
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and order Apiales, it is a member of the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Bupleurum as Bupleurum scorzonerifolium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within several key regions of Siberia. It can be found growing in the Altai mountain area. Furthermore, the species is documented across the Buryatiya region. Its presence extends into the territory of Chita as well. Finally, the plant is distributed through the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions.
