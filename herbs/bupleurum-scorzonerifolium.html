Red Thorowax, scientifically known as Bupleurum scorzonerifolium, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and hepatoprotective properties, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is frequently utilized to support liver health, reduce systemic inflammation, and bolster the body's immune response.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within several key regions of Siberia. It can be found growing in the Altai mountain area. Furthermore, the species is documented across the Buryatiya region. Its presence extends into the territory of Chita as well. Finally, the plant is distributed through the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions.

Often, the dried roots are employed in decoctions or powdered forms to ensure stability and potency during treatment.
