Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Toona sinensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Meliaceae and falls under the genus Toona.

Plant Parts

This plant, Toona sinensis, is highly valued in traditional medicine for the various therapeutic properties found throughout its structure. The tender young buds and fresh leaves are frequently harvested for culinary and medicinal use, often providing a unique flavor and nutritional boost. The individual leaf structure is a key identifier for the species, while the sturdy stem bark is sometimes utilized in decoctions for its bioactive compounds. Furthermore, the dense woods of the tree are not only prized for their durability in construction but also contain chemical constituents that contribute to the plant's overall medicinal profile.
