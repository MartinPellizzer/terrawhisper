Myracrodruon urundeuva

Myraciadruon urundeuva, commonly known as Urundeuva, is a significant medicinal plant native to South American rainforests. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Modern pharmacological studies continue to explore its potential therapeutic applications and antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Myracrodruon urundeuva
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered within Sapindales and resides in the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Myracrodruon.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Myracrodruon urundeuva, is recognized by several different common names depending on the language and regional context. In Guaraní, it is referred to as Urunde'y mi, while in Latin nomenclature, it is associated with the names Brazilinis astronis, Astronium urundeuva, and Astronium juglandifolium. In Portuguese, the species is most commonly called Urundeúva or Astronium urundeuva.
