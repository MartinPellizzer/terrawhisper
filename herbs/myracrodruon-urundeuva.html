The reproduction of Myracrodruon urundeuva is characterized by the production of a drupe-type fruit, which is consistently indehiscent, meaning it does not split open at maturity to release its contents. The fruits exhibit a uniform size, with a recorded length of 1.4 cm (encompassing the minimum, mean, and maximum values). Within these fruits, the seeds are quite small and lightweight, possessing a mean mass of 0.016334 g, with individual seed weights ranging from a minimum of 0.01526 g to a maximum of 0.01696 g.
