Canthium horridum

Canthium horridum, commonly known as a thorny medicinal shrub, is a remarkable botanical species valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its defensive spines and potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Exploring its unique phytochemical properties provides vital insights into developing natural remedies for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Canthium horridum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central areas to the Southeast. Additionally, its presence is noted on the tropical island of Hainan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in Bangladesh. Finally, its range extends into the Indo-China region, specifically within Cambodia.
