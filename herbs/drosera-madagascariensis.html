Drosera madagascariensis

Drosera madagascariensis, commonly known as a species of sundew, is a captivating carnivorous plant native to Madagascar. Renowned for its glandular, sticky tentacles, it captures insects to supplement nutrient-poor soils. Beyond its botanical intrigue, this species holds significant interest in traditional medicine for its unique biochemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Drosera madagascariensis
  • Family: Droseraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Drosera madagascariensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Droseraceae and falls under the genus Drosera.
