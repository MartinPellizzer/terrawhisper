Gambier, scientifically known as Uncaria gambir, is a tropical shrub native to Southeast Asia. Traditionally prized for its tannins, it is widely used in herbal medicine for its astringent and antioxidant properties. Often utilized in wound healing and digestive health, this versatile plant remains a cornerstone of traditional pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct tropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends to the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Bangladesh. Within the Malesia region, the species is distributed across Borneo and Jawa. Finally, it is also native to the Malay Peninsula, known as Malaya.
