Uncaria gambir

Uncaria gambir, commonly known as gambier, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Extracted from the leaves of Uncaria tomentosa, it is renowned for its high tannin content. Traditionally used for its astringent and antimicrobial properties, it plays a vital role in both herbal medicine and industries.

  • Scientific name: Uncaria gambir
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Ultimately, its scientific designation is placed within the genus Uncaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct tropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends to the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Bangladesh. Within the Malesia region, the species is distributed across Borneo and Jawa. Finally, it is also native to the Malay Peninsula, known as Malaya.
