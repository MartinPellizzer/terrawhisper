Ligusticopsis wallichiana

Ligusticopsis wallichiana, commonly known as Wallich's Ligusticopsis, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional Ayurvedic practices. This plant serves as a vital natural resource for treating various ailments, offering valuable bioactive compounds for pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Ligusticopsis wallichiana
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Ligusticopsis wallichiaana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae and is situated within the genus Ligusticopsis.
