Dichapetalum gelonioides

Dichapetalum gelonioides, commonly known as the "shrubby dichapetalum," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Widely utilized in traditional medicine, its leaves and stems are prepared to treat ailments ranging from skin infections and inflammation to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical value in local healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Dichapetalum gelonioides
  • Family: Dichapetalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
