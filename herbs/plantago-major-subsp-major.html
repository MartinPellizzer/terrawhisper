common plantain

Plantago major subsp. major · Accepted scientific name

Common plantain, scientifically known as Plantago major subsp. major, is a versatile perennial herb found globally in disturbed soils. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is widely utilized in traditional healing to treat skin irritations, wounds, and inflammation. Its broad, ribbed leaves serve as a natural, soothing topical remedy.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Plantago as the subspecies Plantago major subsp. major.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several regions of Northern Europe. Its distribution includes the coastal and inland areas of Denmark and Finland. It is also documented growing within the Faroe Islands and the rugged terrain of Iceland. Furthermore, the species can be found extensively throughout Great Britain. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various Northern European climates.
