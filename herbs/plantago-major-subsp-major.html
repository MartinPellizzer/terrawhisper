Plantago major subsp. major

Plantago major subsp. major, commonly known as broadleaf plantain, is a resilient perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties, this versatile plant is frequently used to soothe skin irritations, stings, and minor abrasions, making it a staple in many herbal remedy kits.

  • Scientific name: Plantago major subsp. major
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Plantago as the subspecies Plantago major subsp. major.

This plant, known scientifically as Plantago major subsp. major, is recognized by various regional names depending on the language. In Sweden, it is commonly referred to as gårdsgroblad (sv), while in the Netherlands, it is known as grote weegbree (nl).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several regions of Northern Europe. Its distribution includes the coastal and inland areas of Denmark and Finland. It is also documented growing within the Faroe Islands and the rugged terrain of Iceland. Furthermore, the species can be found extensively throughout Great Britain.
