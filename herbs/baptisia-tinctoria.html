Yellow false indigo, scientifically known as Baptisia tinctoria, is a striking perennial native to North America. Renowned for its brilliant yellow blooms and deep blue dye properties, this hardy legume is highly valued in traditional herbalism. Its robust root system and nitrogen-fixing capabilities make it a resilient garden staple.
