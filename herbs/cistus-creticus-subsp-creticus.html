Cistus creticus creticus, scientifically known as Cistus creticus subsp. creticus, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub renowned for its therapeutic potential. Rich in polyphenols and essential oils, this plant is traditionally valued in herbal medicine for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant benefits for skin and holistic health.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across various regions of the Mediterranean. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing on the islands of Corse and Sardegna. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, its range extends into the coastal and mountainous areas of Albania. The species is also well-documented throughout the diverse landscapes of Bulgaria. Finally, it maintains a significant presence within the territory of Greece.
