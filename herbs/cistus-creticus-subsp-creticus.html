Cistus creticus subsp. creticus

Cistus creticus subsp. creticus, commonly known as the Cretan Rockrose, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Rich in polyphenols and essential oils, it is traditionally used for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial qualities, offering natural support for skin health and immune function.

  • Scientific name: Cistus creticus subsp. creticus
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, scientifically known as Cistus creticus subsp. creticus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales and the family Cistaceae. It is further identified by the genus Cistus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across various regions of the Mediterranean. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing on the islands of Corse and Sardegna. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, its range extends into the coastal and mountainous areas of Albania. The species is also well-documented throughout the diverse landscapes of Bulgaria. Finally, it maintains a significant presence within the territory of Greece.
