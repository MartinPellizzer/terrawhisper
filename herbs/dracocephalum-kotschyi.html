Dracocephalum kotschyi

Dracocephalum kotschyi, commonly known as Kotchyi's dragonhead, is a rare medicinal herb native to specific arid regions. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this plant is prized for its aromatic properties and potential therapeutic benefits. Research continues into its bioactive compounds, highlighting its significance in traditional and modern ethnopharmacology.
