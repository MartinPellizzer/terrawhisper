American red oak

Quercus rubra · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (440 studies) · ★★★★★

American red oak, scientifically known as Quercus rubra, is a majestic deciduous tree valued for its therapeutic potential. Historically used in traditional medicine, its bark contains tannins and compounds with antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and astringent properties, often utilized to treat skin irritations, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions.
