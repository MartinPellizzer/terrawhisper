Quercus rubra

American red oak

Quercus rubra, commonly known as the Northern Red Oak, is a majestic deciduous tree valued for its impressive stature and vibrant autumn foliage. Beyond its timber utility, it holds traditional medicinal significance, often utilized in herbal practices to address inflammatory conditions and support various aspects of systemic wellness.

  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Europe
