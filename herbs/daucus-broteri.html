Daucus broteri

Daucus muricatus

Daucus broteri, commonly known as wild carrot or tridax, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its delicate, white umbelliferous flowers and feathery foliage, this aromatic plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties within various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Daucus broteri
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae and the genus Daucus.
