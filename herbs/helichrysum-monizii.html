Helichrysum monizii

Helichrysum monizii, commonly known as the everlasting flower, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its potent bioactive compounds. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this species is used in traditional therapies to promote skin healing and support systemic wellness, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum monizii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
