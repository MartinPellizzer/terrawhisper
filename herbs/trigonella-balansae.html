Trigonella balansae

Trigonella balansae, commonly known as a rare member of the fenugreek family, is a unique medicinal plant native to specific ecological niches. Valued for its distinct phytochemical profile, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. Research into its bioactive compounds continues to uncover promising applications in traditional and modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Trigonella balansae
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Trigonella balansae, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling under the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Trigonella.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the Mediterranean and Western Asian regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Greece and on the island of Kriti. Moving into Western Asia, the species can be found inhabiting the island of Cyprus. It also extends to the East Aegean Is. within the same geographic zone. Finally, its range reaches further south into the territory of Palestine.
