Ranunculus pedatus

Ranunculus pedatus, commonly known as the footed buttercup, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Characterized by its unique leaf structure and delicate yellow blooms, this species plays a vital role in its local ecosystem, offering unique botanical interest for researchers and nature enthusiasts alike.
