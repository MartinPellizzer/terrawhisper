Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several distinct regions. In China, its presence is noted throughout both the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species also extends across Eastern Asia, where it can be found in Japan and Korea. Moving across the globe, its distribution reaches into North America. Specifically, it is documented within the province of New Brunswick in Eastern Canada.
