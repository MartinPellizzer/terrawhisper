Distribution

This plant, known as Manihot, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species is also documented to inhabit the territories of Burkina and Ghana. Furthermore, its presence extends into the coastal and inland areas of Guinea-Bissau. Finally, the plant completes its regional range by occurring throughout the country of Guinea.
