Valeriana laxiflora

Valeriana laxiflora, commonly known as the lax-flowered valerian, is a delicate perennial herb native to specific montane regions. Distinguished by its airy, loose flower clusters, this botanical specimen belongs to the Caprifoliaceae family. It holds significant interest in ethnobotany for its unique morphological traits and potential medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Valeriana laxiflora
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Valeriana laxiflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Dipsacales. Finally, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae within the genus Valeriana.
