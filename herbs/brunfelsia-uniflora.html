manac, scientifically known as Brunfelsia uniflora, is a captivating flowering shrub native to South America. Often called "Yesterday, Today, and Tomorrow" due to its color-changing blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. While prized for its ornamental beauty, it contains potent alkaloids requiring careful study for medicinal applications.
