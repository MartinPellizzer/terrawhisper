Eriodictyon angustifolium

Eriodictyon angustifolium, commonly known as Narrowleaf Yerba Santa, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to western North America. Renowned for its aromatic, resinous leaves, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional healing to treat respiratory ailments, soothe sore throats, and provide potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits.

  • Scientific name: Eriodictyon angustifolium
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Eriodictyon.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the arid regions of the Southwestern United States. Within this territory, it can be found growing in the states of Arizona, California, Nevada, and Utah. Its range extends southward beyond the American border into the nation of Mexico. Specifically, it is located throughout the Northwest region of Mexico. This combined distribution highlights its preference for the unique landscapes of the southwestern North American continent.
