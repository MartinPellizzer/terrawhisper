Calea oliveri

Calea oliveri, commonly known as a potent botanical from the Asteraceae family, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique bioactive compounds, it is frequently studied for its psychoactive and therapeutic potential, offering intriguing insights into natural pharmacology and ancient ethnobotanical wisdom.

  • Scientific name: Calea oliveri
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Calea oliveira, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is categorized under the genus Calea.
