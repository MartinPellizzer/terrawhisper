Crateva tapia

Crateva tapia, commonly known as the Sacred Temple Tree, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional healing, its various parts possess potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. It is frequently utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various systemic inflammatory conditions.

  • Family: Capparaceae
