Eryngium

Eryngium, commonly known as sea holly, is a diverse genus of flowering plants celebrated for its striking, architectural foliage and metallic blue blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, various species hold significant medicinal value, traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall systemic wellness.
