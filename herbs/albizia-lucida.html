Albizia lucida

Albizia lucida, commonly known as the Shining Albizia, is a majestic leguminous tree native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its lustrous, glossy foliage and delicate, pom-pom-like flowers, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. Various parts of the plant are utilized for their diverse therapeutic and healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Albizia lucida
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified as a member of the genus Albizia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across several regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast territories. It can also be found in Eastern Asia, specifically on the island of Taiwan. Moving towards the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in the state of Assam. Additionally, its distribution extends to the neighboring country of Bangladesh.
