Rhamnus petiolaris, scientifically known as Rhamnus petiolaris, is a deciduous shrub native to Eurasia. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its bark contains anthraquinone glycosides, which act as potent stimulant laxatives. While valued for treating constipation, careful application is essential due to its strong physiological effects on the digestive system.
