This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing in the country of Benin. Additionally, its presence extends to the nation of Burkina. Plant Parts

This plant, Guiera senegalensis, is a versatile medicinal shrub where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaves are frequently harvested for their bioactive compounds, and a concentrated leaves extract is often prepared to treat various ailments. Beyond the foliage, the leaf is sometimes used in traditional decoctions, while the stem bark is stripped to access medicinal constituents used in topical or oral applications. Additionally, the roots are highly valued in folk medicine, providing a potent source of healing agents that contribute to the plant's overall pharmacological profile.
