Guiera senegalensis

Guiera senegalensis, commonly known as the "desert tree," is a resilient shrub native to the Sahelian regions of Africa. Renowned for its profound ethnobotanical significance, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Guiera senegalensis
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Guiera, specifically as the species Guiera senegalensis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing in the country of Benin. Additionally, its presence extends to the nation of Burkina. The species is also documented in Gambia and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes Guinea.
