Peltophorum dubium var. dubium

Peltophorum dubium var. dubium, commonly known as the yellow flame tree, is a majestic tropical legume prized for its vibrant golden blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various parts utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Peltophorum dubium var. dubium
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, taxonomically classified as Peltophorum dubium var. dubium, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is placed into the genus Peltophorum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread and diverse geographical distribution across several continents. In South Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in regions such as Zimbabwe. Its presence extends to the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the borders of India. In the Western Hemisphere, the species inhabits the Southwest of Mexico and the Windward Islands of the Caribbean. Additionally, it is distributed within the Southeastern United States, particularly in Florida.
