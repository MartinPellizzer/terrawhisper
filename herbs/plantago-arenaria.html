Plantago arenaria, scientifically known as Plantago arenaria, is a resilient perennial herb native to sandy, coastal regions. Valued in traditional herbalism, this species belongs to the Plantaginaceae family. It is recognized for its distinctive leaf structure and potential therapeutic properties, often utilized in studies exploring its bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several distinct regions within the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is noted in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the center of the continent, it can be found within Middle Europe. Specifically, its range includes the countries of Austria and Belgium. Furthermore, the distribution extends through the Czechia-Slovakia region.
