Gardenia sootepensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Gardenia sootepensis?

Gardenia sootepenis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant follows the order Gentianaales and is a prominent member of the family Rubiaceae. At the most specific taxonomic levels, it is identified under the genus Gardenia and is distinguished as the species sootepenis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

These blooms are often accompanied by rounded, fleshy fruits that transition in color as they mature.
