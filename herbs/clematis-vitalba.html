Clematis vitalba, scientifically known as Clematis vitalba, is a vigorous, climbing deciduous shrub commonly found across Europe and Asia. Often called Old Man's Beard, this plant is characterized by its feathery seed heads. While historically used in folk medicine, it requires careful handling due to its potential toxicity.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Old Man's Beard, is a climbing woody vine that utilizes its various structures to spread across landscapes. While the mature plant features thick, twining stems, the most delicate and tender portions are the young shoots, which emerge rapidly during the growing season to seek sunlight. These flexible new growths are essential for the plant's upward mobility, allowing it to scramble over trees and shrubs. Throughout its lifecycle, the plant also develops complex leaf structures and clusters of feathery, white flowers that characterize its distinct appearance.
