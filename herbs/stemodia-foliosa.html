Stemodia foliosa

Stemodia foliosa, commonly known as the leafy stemodia, is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin inflammations. This plant holds significant potential for further pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Stemodia foliosa
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Stemodia foliosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
