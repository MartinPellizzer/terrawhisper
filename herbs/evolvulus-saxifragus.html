Evolvulus saxifragus

Evolvulus saxifragus, commonly known as Rock Bindweed, is a resilient creeping herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its delicate blue blooms, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including skin inflammation and digestive issues, owing to its unique bioactive phytochemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Evolvulus saxifragus
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Evolvulus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a notable geographical distribution across specific regions of South America. In Brazil, its presence is recorded within the diverse landscapes of the Northeast. It is also found inhabiting various areas throughout the Southeast of Brazil. Moving further south, the species extends its range into the Southern South American region. Specifically, its distribution includes the country of Paraguay.
