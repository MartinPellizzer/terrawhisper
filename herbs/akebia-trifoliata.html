Akebia trifoliata

Akebiia trifolia, commonly known as Akebia or Chocolate Vine, is a versatile climbing woody vine native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its fruits, roots, and leaves possess significant therapeutic properties, often utilized to treat inflammation, promote circulation, and support respiratory health through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Akebia trifoliata
  • Family: Lardizabalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it falls within the family Lardizabalaceae and is identified by the genus Akebiia.
