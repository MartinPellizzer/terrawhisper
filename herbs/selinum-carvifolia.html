Cambridge Milk-Parsley, scientifically known as Selinum carvifolia, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Native to mountainous regions, it is highly valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils, this plant is primarily utilized for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and digestive-enhancing medicinal benefits.
