Selinum carvifolia

Selinum carvifolia, commonly known as Caraway Selin, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Native to mountainous regions, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional medicine for its aromatic properties. It is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments, respiratory issues, and various inflammatory conditions through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Selinum carvifolia
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Selinum.

Common Names

This plant, Selinum carvifolia, is known by various regional names across Europe. In the Netherlands, it is referred to as karwijselie, while in Norway, it is called Krusfrø. In the Czech Republic, the species is known as olešník kmínolistý, and in Germany, it is commonly called Kümmelblättrige Silge. Additionally, in Sweden, it is identified by the name Krusfrö.
