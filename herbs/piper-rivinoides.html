Common Names

This plant, Piper rivinoides, is known by various local names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the pink pepper. In Hindi, it is known as laung kali, while in Bengali, it is called kanchal. Among the local populations in different parts of India, it also bears the names of kanchal and kanchali.
