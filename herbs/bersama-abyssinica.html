Bersama abyssinica

Bersama abyssinica, commonly known as the Bersama tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Renowned for its bioactive properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Bersama abyssinica
  • Family: Francoaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Bersama abyssinica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Geraniales and the family Francoaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Bersama.

Distribution

This plant, known as Bersama abyssinicca, exhibits a specific range within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence has been documented across several distinct nations throughout this part of the continent. Specifically, it can be found growing in the territories of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. The species also extends its distribution into the coastal and inland areas of Guinea and Ivory Coast. Finally, the geographical reach of this medicinal plant includes the country of Liberia.
