Weeping Paperbark, scientifically known as Melaleuca leucadendra, is a striking Australian shrub celebrated for its distinctive papery bark and delicate, weeping foliage. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains essential oils with potent antimicrobial benefits, traditionally used to treat skin ailments and support respiratory health in various cultures.
