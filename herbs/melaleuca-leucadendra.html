Scientific literature: Very Extensive (271 studies) · ★★★★★

Weeping Paperbark, scientifically known as Melaleuca leucadendra, is a striking Australian shrub celebrated for its distinctive papery bark and delicate, weeping foliage. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains essential oils with potent antimicrobial benefits, traditionally used to treat skin ailments and support respiratory health in various cultures.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and fragmented geographical distribution across multiple tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented specifically within Benin. Its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Gabon and throughout the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Moving toward the Indo-China region, the species is also located on the Andaman Islands. Finally, its distribution continues to the Nicobar Islands, marking its presence in the Indian Ocean.

Plant Parts

This plant, Melaleuca leucadendra, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its potent essential oils. These oils are extracted from the leaves and branches of the tree, containing bioactive compounds that possess significant antiseptic, antibacterial, and antifungal properties. Due to the high concentration of these aromatic oils, the plant is frequently utilized in therapeutic applications to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and various inflammatory conditions.
