Fritillaria ebeiensis

Fritillaria ebeiensis, commonly known as a rare medicinal bulb, belongs to the Liliales order. Indigenous to specific montane regions, this exquisite plant is valued for its unique phytochemical profile. Researchers explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications, highlighting its significant importance in traditional healing and modern ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Fritillaria ebeiensis
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Liliales and situated in the family Liliaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Fritillaria.

This plant is specifically found within the unique landscapes of China. Its geographical range is primarily concentrated in the South-Central region of the country. Within this area, the species occupies distinct ecological niches that support its growth. Because its distribution is so localized, it is considered a regional component of the South-Central Chinese flora.
