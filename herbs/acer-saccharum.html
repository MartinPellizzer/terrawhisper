This plant, known scientifically as Acer saccharum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Acer.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the sugar maple, is highly valued for its specific botanical components that offer both nutritional and traditional medicinal benefits. The sap is perhaps its most famous part, harvested in early spring to provide a nutrient-dense liquid rich in minerals like manganese and zinc. Additionally, the leaves are frequently utilized in various herbal preparations, often being brewed into teas to help manage blood sugar levels or reduce inflammation.
