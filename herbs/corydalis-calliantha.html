Corydalis calliantha

Corydalis calliantha, commonly known as the beautiful corydalis, is a captivating perennial herb valued for its striking, colorful blooms. Beyond its ornamental charm, this species belongs to a genus renowned for diverse phytochemical properties, containing various alkaloids that have long been studied for their potential therapeutic and medicinal applications.

  • Family: Papaveraceae

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Corydalis.
