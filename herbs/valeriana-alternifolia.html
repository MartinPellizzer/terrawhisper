Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Dipsacales, and the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Valeriana.

Distribution

This plant, Valeriana alternifolia, is primarily distributed across several key regions within the vast expanse of Siberia. Its presence is well-documented in the Republic of Buryatia, where it thrives in specific local habitats. The species also extends its range into the Transbaikal region, specifically within the area of Chita. Furthermore, it can be found growing throughout the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk oblasts. Finally, the geographical footprint of this medicinal plant includes the rugged terrain of Tuva.

Plant Parts

This plant, Valeriana alternifolia, derives much of its medicinal value from its underground structures, specifically the root and the rhizome. The thick, fragrant rhizome serves as a vital storage organ for the plant, containing the concentrated essential oils and chemical compounds often sought for therapeutic purposes. Additionally, the extensive root system provides the necessary stability and nutrient absorption required for the plant to thrive in its natural habitat.
