Myrciaria tenella

Myrciaria tenella, commonly known as the "nipa palm" in certain coastal regions, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its traditional healing properties. Found primarily in tropical wetlands, it offers therapeutic benefits through its various extracts, which are frequently utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation and digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Myrciaria tenella
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
