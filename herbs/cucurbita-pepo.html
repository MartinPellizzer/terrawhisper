Cucurbita pepo

Field pumpkin

Cucurbita pepo, commonly known as pumpkin, squash, or zucchini, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Cucurbitaceae family. Rich in vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants, its seeds and flesh offer therapeutic benefits, supporting digestive health, immune function, and heart wellness through their potent nutrient density and bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Cucurbita pepo
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cucurbitales, it is classified under the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cucurbita, which includes the species Cucurbita pepo.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cucurbita pepo, offers a diverse range of therapeutic components that can be utilized for various health benefits. The nutrient-dense seeds are highly valued for their ability to support prostate health and provide essential minerals, while the pumpkin peel can be processed to retain dietary fiber and antioxidants. From the plant's fats, pumpkin oils are extracted to serve as potent topical or dietary supplements rich in vitamin E and fatty acids. Furthermore, the reproductive biology of the plant includes pollen, which is often studied for its nutritional profile, and the leaf extract, which is frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties.
