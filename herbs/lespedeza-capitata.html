Lespedeza capitata

Lespedeza capitata, commonly known as the head-clover, is a versatile medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. This botanical treasure offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and holistic wellness practices.
