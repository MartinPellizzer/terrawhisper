Calophyllum brasiliense

Calophyllum brasiliense, commonly known as Cedro-rosa, is a versatile tropical tree valued for its medicinal and industrial properties. Native to Central and South America, its bark and leaves contain bioactive compounds with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory potential, offering significant therapeutic possibilities in traditional medicine and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Calophyllum brasiliense
  • Family: Calophyllaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Malpighiales and the family Calophyllaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Calophyllum.
