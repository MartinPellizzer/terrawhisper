Plant Parts

This plant, Calophyllum brasiliense, utilizes various components for its biological functions and traditional medicinal applications. The roots serve as a foundational anchor, absorbing essential nutrients and water from the soil to support the tree's growth. Its lush, evergreen leaves are characterized by prominent parallel veins and are often studied for their chemical properties. The stem bark is a significant part of the plant, frequently used in various traditional remedies due to its complex bioactive compounds. During its reproductive cycle, the tree produces fragrant, white flowers that attract pollinators, which eventually lead to the development of its distinctive fruits.
