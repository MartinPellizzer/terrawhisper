Drosera peltata

Crescent-leaved sundew

Drosera peltata, commonly known as the peltate sundew, is a captivating carnivorous plant native to specific wetland habitats. Characterized by its distinctive shield-shaped leaves and glistening, sticky mucilage, it captures insects to supplement its nutrient intake. This specialized adaptation allows it to thrive in nitrogen-poor, acidic soil environments.

  • Scientific name: Drosera peltata
  • Family: Droseraceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Drosera peltata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Droseraceae and falls under the genus Drosera.
