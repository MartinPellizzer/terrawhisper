Clematis loureiroana

Clematis loureiroana, commonly known as a medicinal climber, is a significant plant species found in South American ecosystems. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses unique bioactive properties used to treat various ailments. Understanding its chemical composition and therapeutic potential is essential for exploring its role in modern ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Clematis loureiroana
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Clematis.

Common Names

This plant, Clematis loureiroana, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic context. In Chinese, it is referred to by the names 丝铁线莲 (zh), 滑叶木通 (zh), and 紫木通 (zh). Specifically, in simplified Chinese characters, it is written as 丝铁线莲 (zh-hans), which is also the standard designation used in mainland China (zh-cn).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across parts of Southeast Asia and East Asia. Within China, its presence is documented in the tropical region of Hainan. It is also widely distributed throughout the China Southeast area. Furthermore, the species can be found within the Indo-China territory, specifically in Vietnam. These locations define the primary natural habitats where this medicinal species thrives.
