Smallanthus sonchifolius

Yacón

Smallanthus sonchiifolius, commonly known as Yacon, is a perennial herb native to the Andes. Renowned for its sweet, crunchy tubers, it is highly valued for its prebiotic properties and high fructooligosaccharide content. This medicinal plant is increasingly studied for its potential to support metabolic health and glycemic control.

  • Scientific name: Smallanthus sonchifolius
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling within the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Smallanthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several key regions within East Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and North-Central territories. Its distribution also extends to the southeastern parts of the country and the island of Hainan. Additionally, the species is documented as growing in the region of Taiwan. Together, these areas define its primary geographical range.
