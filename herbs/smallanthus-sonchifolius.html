Yacón

Smallanthus sonchifolius · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (257 studies) · ★★★★★

Yacón, scientifically known as Smallanthus sonchifolius, is a versatile perennial plant native to the Andes. Highly valued for its edible tubers, it serves as a nutritious, low-calorie food source. Beyond nutrition, it is prized in traditional medicine for its prebiotic properties and potential benefits in managing blood sugar levels.
