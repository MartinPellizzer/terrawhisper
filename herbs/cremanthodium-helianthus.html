What's the taxonomical classification of Cremanthodium helianthus?

Cremanthodium helianthus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Asterales. The plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, which places it within the genus Cremanthodium, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species helianthus.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cremanthodium helianthus has an erect, herbaceous habit characterized by slender, often branched stems and deeply pinnatisect leaves with narrow, linear lobes. Each individual flower head features ray florets with distinct, spreading ligules and central disc florets.
