Black walnut, scientifically known as Juglans nigra, is a powerful medicinal tree prized for its potent therapeutic properties. Renowned for its antimicrobial and antiparasitic effects, its hulls and bark offer natural remedies for fungal infections, digestive issues, and skin ailments, making it a cornerstone of traditional herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the black walnut, utilizes various components for medicinal purposes, with its leaves playing a significant role. The leaves of Juglans nigra are pinnately compound and contain bioactive compounds, such as juglone, which are often extracted for their antimicrobial and antifungal properties. In traditional herbalism, infusions made from these leaves are sometimes used topically to treat skin conditions or internally to support digestive health.
