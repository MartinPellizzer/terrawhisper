Juglans nigra

Juglans nigra, commonly known as Black Walnut, is a majestic deciduous tree revered for its potent medicinal properties. Its nutrient-rich hulls and bark contain unique compounds like juglone, historically utilized for their antiparasitic, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory effects. This versatile botanical remains a cornerstone in traditional herbal medicine worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Juglans nigra
  • Family: Juglandaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Juglans nigra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Fagales. Finally, it resides in the family Juglandaceae under the genus Juglans.
