Gonospermum fruticosum

Gonospermum fruticosum, commonly known as the climbing woody vine, is a medicinal plant significant in traditional healing practices. Belonging to the Menispermaceae family, it is widely utilized for its bioactive alkaloids. Research focuses on its potential therapeutic properties, particularly for treating inflammatory conditions and various neurological disorders.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it resides in the family Asteraceae under the genus Gonospermum.
