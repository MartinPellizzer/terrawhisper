Sambucus

Sambucus, commonly known as elderberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub cherished for centuries. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, these dark berries are frequently used to bolster the immune system and combat seasonal ailments. Whether brewed into syrups or tinctures, Sambucus offers a natural approach to holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Sambucus
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales and the family Viburnaceae, it is classified under the genus Sambucus.
