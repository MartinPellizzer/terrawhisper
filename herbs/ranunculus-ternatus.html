Catclaw buttercup, scientifically known as Ranunculus ternatus, is a distinctive perennial herb native to New Zealand. Often found in damp, shady environments, this plant is characterized by its deeply lobed, kidney-shaped leaves and bright yellow blooms. It holds significant cultural importance within traditional Māori herbal medicine practices.
