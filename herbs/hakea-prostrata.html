Hakea prostrata

Hakea prostrata, commonly known as the prostrate hakea, is a unique, ground-hugging shrub native to Western Australia. Characterized by its creeping habit and attractive, woody seed pods, this resilient plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem, providing essential habitat and nectar for local pollinators within its natural landscape.

  • Scientific name: Hakea prostrata
  • Family: Proteaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Proteales, it is a member of the family Proteaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Hakea, specifically as the species Hakea prostata.
