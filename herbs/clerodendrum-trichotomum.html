Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the harlequin glorybower, possesses several distinct morphological features that contribute to its medicinal profile. The roots are often utilized in traditional practices for their specific therapeutic properties, while the sturdy, woody stems provide the structural support necessary for its upright growth habit. The large, oppositely arranged leaves are notable for their distinct texture and are frequently studied for their bioactive compounds. During its blooming season, the plant produces striking, fragrant flowers that attract various pollinators, eventually giving way to the development of small, fleshy fruits that contain the seeds for the next generation.
