Rosa × polliniana

Rosa ×polliniana

Rosa × polliniana, commonly known as the Polliniana rose, is a captivating hybrid celebrated for its exquisite blooms and aromatic essence. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant place in traditional herbalism, where its petals are utilized for their soothing properties, skin-enhancing benefits, and gentle medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Rosa × polliniana
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Rosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range primarily centered within the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented across the territories of Germany and Hungary. The distribution also extends to the mountainous regions of Switzerland within the same central zone. Moving toward the southeast, the species can be found inhabiting the Northwestern Balkan Peninsula. Consequently, its habitat spans from central European landscapes to the rugged terrain of Southeastern Europe.
