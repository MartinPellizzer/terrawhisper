Satureja laxiflora

Satureja laxiflora, commonly known as the Loose-flowered Satereja, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Found in specific tropical ecosystems, this botanical species is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Satureja laxiflora
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further categorized under the family Lamiaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Satureja.
