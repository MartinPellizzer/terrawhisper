Diospyros virginiana

American persimmon

Diospyros virginiana, commonly known as the American persimmon, is a versatile deciduous tree valued for its medicinal and nutritional properties. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, its fruit and bark are traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammation, making it a significant botanical resource in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Diospyros virginiana
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Diospyros virginiana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ebenaceae and falls under the genus Diospyros.
