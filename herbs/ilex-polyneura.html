What's the taxonomical classification of Ilex polyneura?

Ilex polyneura belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic progression continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Aquifoliales. This species is a member of the family Aquifoliaceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Ilex, with the unique species designation polyneura.
