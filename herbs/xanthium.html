Cockleburs, scientifically known as Xanthium, is a genus of annual herbaceous plants recognized by their distinctive, prickly seed pods. While often viewed as agricultural weeds, various species hold significant ethnobotanical value. They are utilized in traditional medicine for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties across diverse global cultures.
