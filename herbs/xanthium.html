Xanthium

Xanthium, commonly known as cocklebur, is a genus of herbaceous plants recognized by their distinctive, prickly seed pods. While often viewed as a persistent weed in agricultural fields, various species hold significant medicinal value in traditional practices, utilized for their diverse bioactive compounds to treat inflammatory and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Xanthium
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Xanthium.
