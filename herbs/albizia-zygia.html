Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the country of Benin, where it is part of the local flora. Its presence also extends to Burkina Faso, contributing to the region's botanical diversity. Additionally, the species is distributed across Gambia and Ghana.
