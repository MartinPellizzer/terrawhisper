Anemone coronaria, scientifically known as Anemone coronaria, is a stunning perennial wildflower celebrated for its vibrant, cup-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds historical significance in traditional herbal practices, symbolizing both fragility and enduring strength.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be noted within the borders of Great Britain. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it is found throughout various parts of Southwestern Europe. Specifically, its distribution includes the islands of the Baleares and Corse. Furthermore, the species is widely established in both France and Portugal.
