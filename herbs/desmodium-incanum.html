Desmodium incanum

Desmodium incanum, commonly known as the "tick trefoil," is a resilient medicinal legume native to various African regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots are utilized to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues. This versatile plant holds significant ethnobotanical value for its diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Desmodium incanum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Desmodium.
