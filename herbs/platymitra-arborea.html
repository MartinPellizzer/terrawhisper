Platymitra arborea

Platymitra arborea, commonly known as the Sacred Canopy Tree, is a rare medicinal powerhouse native to tropical highland ecosystems. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this botanical marvel is traditionally utilized in ancient healing practices to treat inflammation and support respiratory health, offering profound potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Family: Annonaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
