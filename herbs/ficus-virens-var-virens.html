Ficus virens var. virens

Ficus virens var. virens, commonly known as the Green Fig, is a significant medicinal tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin disorders, and digestive issues, making it a vital resource in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Ficus virens var. virens
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Rosaless and placed in the family Moraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ficus as the variety Ficus virens var. virens.

Common Names

This plant, Ficus virens var. virens, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic and cultural context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Evergreen Fig, while in Hindi, it is commonly called Peepal or Ban Peepal. In Sanskrit, the plant is identified as Vata, and in Bengali, it is known as Ban Peepal.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across various regions of China. It is widely distributed throughout the South-Central and North-Central parts of the country. Additionally, the species can be found thriving in the Southeast region. Its range also extends to the tropical island of Hainan. Finally, the plant is documented as growing within the high-altitude areas of Tibet.
