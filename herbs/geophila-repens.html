This plant, Geophila repens, exhibits specific morphological characteristics across its vegetative structures that are often studied for their medicinal properties. The small, spreading leaves are arranged in a way that allows the plant to form a dense ground cover, supported by slender, creeping petioles that connect the foliage to the stem.
