Distribution

This plant is primarily found across several regions within the Australian continent. Its natural range extends throughout the state of New South Wales. Additionally, it can be located in the coastal and inland areas of Victoria. The species also makes an appearance in the southern landscapes of Tasmania. Therefore, its distribution is concentrated in the southeastern portions of Australia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as heath-leaved paperbark, utilizes its leaves as a primary component for its medicinal and aromatic properties. The leaves are small, narrow, and needle-like, which helps the plant conserve moisture in its native habitats. In traditional medicinal practices, these leaves are often harvested to extract essential oils that possess antiseptic and antimicrobial qualities, making them a significant part of the plant's therapeutic profile.
