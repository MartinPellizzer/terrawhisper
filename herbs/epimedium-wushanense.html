Epimedium wushanense

Epimedium wushanense, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, is a significant plant species valued in traditional pharmacology. Native to specific mountainous regions, it is prized for its unique bioactive compounds. Researchers study this plant to explore its potential therapeutic properties, particularly in developing natural treatments for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium wushanense
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Epimedium wushanense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Berberidaceae and the genus Epimedium.
