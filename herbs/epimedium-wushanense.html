Epimedium wushanense, scientifically known as Epimedium wushanense, is a perennial herb revered in traditional Chinese medicine. Native to mountainous regions, it is primarily cultivated for its bioactive flavonoids. These compounds are widely studied for their ability to enhance vitality, support kidney function, and promote overall reproductive health.
