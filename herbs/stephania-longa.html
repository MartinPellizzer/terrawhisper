Stephania longa

Stephanania longa, commonly known as Akarkara, is a perennial climbing herb prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is extensively used to treat neurological disorders, enhance libido, and reduce inflammation. This versatile plant serves as a vital natural remedy for various restorative health purposes.

  • Scientific name: Stephania longa
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ranunculales and the family Menispermaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Stephania, specifically as the species Stephania longa.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions within East Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range extends further to the island of Taiwan within the same general region. Additionally, the species can be found throughout the Nansei-shoto islands of Eastern Asia. These diverse locations highlight the specific geographical footprint of Stephanania longa.
