Pteris ensiformis

Pteris ensiformis, commonly known as the Brake fern, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments and respiratory issues. This resilient fern serves as a vital resource in natural healing and botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Pteris ensiformis
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, it is further categorized into the family Pteridaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pteris, specifically the species Pteris ensiformis.
