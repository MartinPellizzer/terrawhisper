Phyllotaenium lindenii

Phyllotaenium lindenii, commonly known as the Lindeni's Phyllotaenium, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Often utilized in traditional healing practices, this species contains bioactive compounds that offer potential antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits, making it a significant subject of interest in ethnobotanical research and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Phyllotaenium lindenii
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Phyllotaenium lindenii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Alismatales. Finally, it is situated within the family Araceae and the genus Phyllotaenium.
