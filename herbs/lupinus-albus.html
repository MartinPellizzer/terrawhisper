Gibto, scientifically known as Lupinus albus, is a prominent medicinal and nutritional plant native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its high protein content and nitrogen-fixing capabilities, it offers therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile legume serves as a valuable resource in both traditional herbalism and modern dietary science.
