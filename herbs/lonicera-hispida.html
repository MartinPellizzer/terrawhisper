What's the taxonomical classification of Lonicera hispida?

Lonicera hispida belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is categorized under the order Dipsacales and the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Lonicera, and its unique species designation, hispida.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lonicera hispida has arching, deciduous stems that are characterized by a dense covering of fine, spreading hairs and small, sharp prickles. The leaves are simple, opposite, and elliptical or oblong in shape with entire margins. Small, tubular flowers emerge from the leaf axils in pairs or small clusters, typically displaying a pale yellow or greenish-white coloration. These blossoms develop into fleshy, oblong berries that transition from green to a deep reddish-purple or black color when mature. The overall growth habit forms a spreading, low-growing shrub that can reach several feet in width.
