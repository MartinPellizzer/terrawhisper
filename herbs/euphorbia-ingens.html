Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the tropical regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the borders of Burundi and Rwanda. Its presence is also noted in the expansive territories of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward Northeast Tropical Africa, the species extends its range into Eritrea. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the highlands and lowlands of Ethiopia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Euphorbia ingenis, possesses distinct morphological features in its vegetative structures that are often studied for their medicinal properties. The stem is typically succulent and characterized by the presence of a milky, latex-like sap, which is a hallmark of the Euphorbia genus and is frequently the primary source of bioactive compounds. Complementing the stem are the leaves, which are generally arranged in a way that supports the plant's photosynthetic needs and contribute to its overall chemical profile. Together, the stem and leaf serve as the vital anatomical components used in various traditional applications of this species.
