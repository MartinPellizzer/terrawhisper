Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several significant regions of Eurasia. In Eastern Europe, its presence is documented throughout both Central European Russia and South European Russia. Moving into the Siberian region, it can be found in the mountainous area of Altay. The species also extends its range to the eastern territories of Chita. Furthermore, its habitat includes the remote southern region of Tuva.
