Clinopodium serpyllifolium subsp. brachycalyx

Clinopodium serpyllifolium subsp. brachycalyx, commonly known as short-calyx knotgrass, is a distinctive medicinal herb found in specific subtropical habitats. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this subspecies possesses unique phytochemical properties. It is primarily studied for its potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects, offering promising therapeutic applications in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Clinopodium serpyllifolium subsp. brachycalyx
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Clinopodium as the subspecies brachycalyx.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution centered within the Western Asian region. Its natural range is primarily documented across the territories of Lebanon and Syria. Additionally, the species can be found growing within the diverse landscapes of Türkiye. These specific locations highlight the plant's preference for the environmental conditions found in these Mediterranean and Middle Eastern areas.
