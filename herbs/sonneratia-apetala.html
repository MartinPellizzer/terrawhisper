Sonneratia apetala

Keora

Sonneratia apetala, commonly known as the "Apetala" or "Sonne" tree, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical forests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues and inflammatory conditions.

  • Family: Lythraceae

This plant, Sonneratia apetala, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Myrtales and is a member of the family Lythraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Sonneratia.
