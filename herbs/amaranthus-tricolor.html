Amaranthus tricolor

Edible amaranth

Amaranthus tricolor, commonly known as Joseph's Coat, is a striking ornamental plant prized for its vibrant, multi-colored foliage. Beyond its beauty, this species holds significant medicinal value in various cultures, utilized for its nutritional density and potential therapeutic properties to support wellness and treat various traditional ailments.

  • Scientific name: Amaranthus tricolor
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Amaranthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several different continents and climate zones. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Germany. Its presence extends into Southwestern Europe, where it is specifically documented in France. Moving toward Northern Africa, the species is also located in Egypt. Finally, its range reaches West Tropical Africa, with documented occurrences in both Benin and Sierra Leone.
