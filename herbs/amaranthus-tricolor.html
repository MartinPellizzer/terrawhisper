Edible amaranth, scientifically known as Amaranthus tricolor, is a vibrant, multi-colored leafy vegetable celebrated for its nutritional density. Highly valued in various cuisines, it offers a rich source of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants. This versatile plant serves as both a nutritious food source and a traditional medicinal herb.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several different continents and climate zones. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Germany. Its presence extends into Southwestern Europe, where it is specifically documented in France. Moving toward Northern Africa, the species is also located in Egypt. Finally, its range reaches West Tropical Africa, with documented occurrences in both Benin and Sierra Leone.
