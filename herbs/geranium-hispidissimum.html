What's the taxonomical classification of Geranium hispidissimum?

Geranium hispidissimum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae, eventually reaching its specific designation within the genus Geraniuim. Finally, the unique identity of this plant is established at the species level as hispidissimum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Geranium hispidissimum has deeply palmately lobed leaves characterized by dense, bristly hairs covering both the upper and lower surfaces. These leaves are typically arranged oppositely along a stem that is also covered in stiff, spreading trichomes. The plant produces small, five-petaled flowers that emerge from the axils of the hairy foliage. Its reproductive structures are supported by pedicels that exhibit the same rough, bristly texture as the vegetative parts. This morphological trait of intense hairiness is the defining feature that gives the species its name.
