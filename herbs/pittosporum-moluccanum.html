Distribution

This plant is native to the diverse tropical regions of the Malesia botanical zone. Its geographical range specifically includes the island of Jawa, where it thrives in local ecosystems. It is also widely distributed throughout the Lesser Sunda Islands within this region. Furthermore, the species can be found growing in the Maluku archipelago. Finally, its presence extends to the island of Sulawesi, marking a broad distribution across these Southeast Asian territories.
