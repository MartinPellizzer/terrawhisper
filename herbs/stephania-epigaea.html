Stephania epigaea

Stephania epigaea, commonly known as the "mistletoe vine," is a climbing woody plant native to the tropical regions of the Americas. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and respiratory issues, thanks to its unique bioactive phytochemical compounds.
