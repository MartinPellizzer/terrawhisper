Acalypha alopecuroidea

Acalypha alopecuroidea, commonly known as the hare's-tail copperleaf, is a versatile medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its unique pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Exploring its bioactive compounds offers promising insights into developing new natural therapeutic treatments.
