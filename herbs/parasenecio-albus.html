Parasenecio albus

Parasenecio albus, commonly known as White Senecio, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, it is frequently utilized in traditional herbal medicine to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions. Understanding its bioactive compounds is essential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Parasenecio albus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Parasenecio.
