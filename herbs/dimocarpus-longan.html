Dimocarpus longan

Dimocarpus longan, commonly known as longan, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its sweet, translucent flesh, it is used in various cultures to nourish the blood, calm the spirit, and improve sleep quality. This nutrient-dense botanical offers significant antioxidant and restorative health benefits.

  • Scientific name: Dimocarpus longan
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Dimocarpus longan, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Its taxonomic journey concludes within the genus Dimocarpus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions of East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is widespread, spanning from the South-Central provinces to the island of Hainan and throughout the Southeast regions. The species also has a significant distribution in Eastern Asia, specifically within Taiwan. Beyond the Asian continent, its range extends to the Western Indian Ocean. There, it can be found growing on the island of Réunion.
