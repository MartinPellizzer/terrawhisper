Longan, scientifically known as Dimocarpus longan, is a tropical evergreen tree celebrated for its sweet, translucent flesh. Originating in Southern China, this "dragon eye" fruit is prized in traditional medicine for its ability to nourish the blood, calm the nerves, and enhance overall vitality and sleep quality.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions of East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is widespread, spanning from the South-Central provinces to the island of Hainan and throughout the Southeast regions. The species also has a significant distribution in Eastern Asia, specifically within Taiwan. Beyond the Asian continent, its range extends to the Western Indian Ocean. There, it can be found growing on the island of Réunion.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as longan, is valued in traditional medicine primarily for its nutritional and therapeutic properties derived from its fruit. The fruit consists of a thin, brownish, leathery rind that protects a translucent, juicy white flesh surrounding a single dark, hard seed. In various medicinal practices, the dried fruit is used to nourish the blood, calm the nerves, and improve sleep quality, making it a significant component in herbal formulations aimed at addressing fatigue and palpitations.
