Virgilia oroboides subsp. oroboides

Virgilia oroboides oroboides

Virgilia oroboides subsp. oroboides, commonly known as Oroboides goldenrod, is a resilient perennial native to the arid landscapes of the American Southwest. Renowned for its striking yellow blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized by indigenous cultures for its diverse medicinal properties and restorative healing potential.
