Field Gladiolus, scientifically known as Gladiolus italicus, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its striking, funnel-shaped flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in folk medicine, its various parts are utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues.
