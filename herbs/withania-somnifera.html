Withania somnifera

Withania somnifera, commonly known as Ashwagandha, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned as a potent adaptogen, this perennial shrub helps the body manage stress and restore balance. Its roots contain bioactive withanolides, which offer neuroprotective, anti-inflammatory, and rejuvenating properties, making it a vital herb for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Withania somnifera
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Withania somnifera, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Withania.
