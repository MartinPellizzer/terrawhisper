Indian ginseng

Withania somnifera · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (2123 studies) · ★★★★★

Indian ginseng, scientifically known as Withania somnifera, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine. Often called Ashwagandha, this potent adaptogen helps the body manage stress, enhances cognitive function, and supports overall vitality. Renowned for its restorative properties, it serves as a powerful natural remedy for balancing energy and promoting wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Withania somnifera, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Withania.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the Mediterranean. Within Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in the Baleares and Sardegna. It is also documented to inhabit various areas of Spain. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species is present in Greece. Finally, its range extends to the island of Kriti.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as ashwagandha, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The most significant part is the root, specifically the dried roots, which are widely processed into powders for their adaptogenic benefits. The stem serves as the central structural support for the shrub, connecting the underground parts to the foliage. Additionally, the green leaves are also of interest in traditional practices, with each individual leaf contributing to the plant's overall biological functions and chemical profile.
