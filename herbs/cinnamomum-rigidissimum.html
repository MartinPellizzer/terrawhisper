Cinnamomum rigidissimum

What's the taxonomical classification of Cinnamomum rigidissimum?

Cinnamomum rigidissimum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it in the order Laurales. Furthermore, this plant is a member of the family Lauraceae, specifically falling under the genus Cinnamomum, with its specific designation being the species rigidissimum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cinnamomum rigidissimum has small, coriaceous, elliptic to oblong leaves that are characterized by an entire margin and a distinctively rigid, leathery texture. The leaf surfaces are typically dark green and lustrous on the upper side, while the undersides may appear slightly paler. These leaves are arranged in an opposite pattern along the woody stems of the plant. The plant also produces small, inconspicuous flowers that eventually develop into fleshy drupes. Its overall growth habit is that of a small tree or shrub with dense, evergreen foliage.
