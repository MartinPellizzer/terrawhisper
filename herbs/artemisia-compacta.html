Artemisia compacta

Artemisia compacta, commonly known as compact wormwood, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering a natural approach to supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia compacta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Artemisia.

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several distinct regions spanning from Eastern Europe to Central Asia. In the territory of Eastern Europe, it can be found growing within East European Russia. Its range extends deep into Siberia, specifically inhabiting the areas of Altay and Tuva. Moving into Central Asia, the species is also prevalent throughout Kazakhstan. Finally, its geographical presence is completed by its occurrence in Kirgizstan.
