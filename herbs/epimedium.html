Epimedium

Epimedium, commonly known as horny goat weed, is a versatile medicinal herb cherished in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its ability to support libido and hormonal balance, it contains bioactive icariin. This resilient perennial is widely used to promote circulation, enhance vitality, and support overall reproductive health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is categorized within the order Ranunculales and the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is classified under the specific genus Epimedium.
