Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several regions of Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, it can be found inhabiting the Andaman Islands. Its presence is also well-documented throughout mainland territories, specifically in Myanmar, Thailand, and Vietnam. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the Malesia region. One notable location within this area where the plant is found is Jawa.
