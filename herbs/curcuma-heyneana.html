Curcuma heyneana

Curcumina heyneana, commonly known as Wild Turmeric, is a versatile medicinal herb native to the tropical regions of India. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, this rhizomatous plant is widely used in traditional Ayurveda to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions through natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Curcuma heyneana
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Curcuma.
