Albizia schimperiana

Albizia schimperiana, commonly known as the Schimper's Albizia, is a versatile medicinal tree native to East Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant ethnobotanical value and healing potential.

  • Scientific name: Albizia schimperiana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is further identified by the genus Albizia, which encompasses the species Albizia schimperiana.

Additionally, the species extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the DR Congo.
