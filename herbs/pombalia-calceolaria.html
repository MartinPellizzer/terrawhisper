Pombalia calceolaria

Pombalia calceolaria, commonly known as the "white-flowered pombali," is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to South American rainforests. Renowned for its unique physiological traits, this plant is utilized in traditional healing practices to address various ailments, offering valuable insights into the rich biodiversity and pharmacological potential of tropical flora.

  • Scientific name: Pombalia calceolaria
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Pombalia calceolaria, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Violaceae and the genus Pombalia.
