Achillea wilhelmsii

Achillea wilhelmsii, commonly known as Wilhelm's Yarrow, is a rare medicinal herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Distinguished by its delicate flowering structures, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. It is valued in traditional herbalism for its potential therapeutic applications, offering intriguing possibilities for modern pharmacological research and wellness.
