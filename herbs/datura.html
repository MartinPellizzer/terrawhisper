jimsonweed

Datura · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (42 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Jimsonweed, scientifically known as Datura, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its beautiful, trumpet-shaped flowers, it contains powerful alkaloids like atropine and scopolamine. While historically used in traditional healing, its extreme toxicity requires cautious handling, as even small doses can be dangerous.
