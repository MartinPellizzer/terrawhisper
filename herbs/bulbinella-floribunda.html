Bulbinella floribunda

Bulbinella floribunda, commonly known as the Yellow Bulbine, is a resilient perennial herb native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its striking, star-shaped yellow blooms, this succulent-like plant is valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial properties and ability to support skin health and digestive wellness through its potent compounds.

  • Scientific name: Bulbinella floribunda
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Bulbinella floribunda, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asparagales and the family Asphodelaceae. It is further classified under the genus Bulbinella.
