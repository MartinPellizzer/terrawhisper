Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Nigeria and Sierra Leone. Moving toward the central regions, its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is documented in the countries of Burundi, Cameroon, and Congo.
