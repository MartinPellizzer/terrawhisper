Common Dodder

Cuscuta reflexa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (250 studies) · ★★★★★

Common Dodder, scientifically known as Cuscuta reflexa, is a parasitic, leafless climbing plant characterized by its bright yellow, thread-like stems. Often found intertwining with host vegetation, it lacks chlorophyll and relies entirely on its host for nutrients. In traditional medicine, it is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties.
