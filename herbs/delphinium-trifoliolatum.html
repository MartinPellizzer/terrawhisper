What's the taxonomical classification of Delphinium trifoliolatum?

Delphinium trifoliolatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It further falls under the order Ranunculales and is a member of the family Ranunculaceae. Ultimately, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Delphinium, and its unique species designation, trifoliolatum.
