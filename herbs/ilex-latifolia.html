Ilex latifolia

Ilex latifolia, commonly known as the broad-leaved holly, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to subtropical regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is utilized in traditional practices to address various ailments. Its antioxidant-rich leaves and berries offer significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ilex latifolia
  • Family: Aquifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Aquifoliales, falling into the family Aquifoliaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Ilex.
