Tarajo holly, scientifically known as Ilex latifolia, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Caribbean and subtropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and berries possess antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health and immune function.
