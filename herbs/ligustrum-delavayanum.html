Ligustrum delavayanum

Ligustrum delavayanum, commonly known as the Delavay privet, is a significant medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Historically utilized in traditional practices, it possesses diverse bioactive properties. This plant is valued for its potential therapeutic applications, particularly in addressing various ailments through its unique phytochemical profile and restorative qualities.

  • Scientific name: Ligustrum delavayanum
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Oleaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Ligustrum.
