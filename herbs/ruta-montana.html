Mountain Rue, scientifically known as Ruta montana, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Characterized by its distinctive blue-green, divided foliage and delicate yellow flowers, this species belongs to the Rutaceae family. It is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique aromatic and medicinal properties.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ruta, specifically as the species Ruta montana.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions of Southwestern Europe. Its distribution includes the islands of the Baleares and the mainland territories of France. It is also commonly documented throughout the Iberian Peninsula in both Spain and Portugal. Beyond the western reaches, the species extends into Southeastern Europe. Specifically, its natural habitat encompasses the mountainous landscapes of Greece.
