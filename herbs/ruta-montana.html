Ruta montana

Ruta montana, commonly known as mountain rue, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Characterized by its distinctive yellow flowers and aromatic foliage, this plant belongs to the Rutaceae family. Historically valued in traditional herbalism, it serves as a fascinating subject for botanical study.

  • Scientific name: Ruta montana
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ruta, specifically as the species Ruta montana.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ruta montana, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic context. In Catalan, it is called ruda de muntanya, while in Arabic, it is known as سذاب جبلي or سذاب جبلى in Egyptian Arabic. In Galician, the name used is Ruda de montaña, and in Spanish, it is commonly identified as Ruta montana.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions of Southwestern Europe. Its distribution includes the islands of the Baleares and the mainland territories of France. It is also commonly documented throughout the Iberian Peninsula in both Spain and Portugal. Beyond the western reaches, the species extends into Southeastern Europe. Specifically, its natural habitat encompasses the mountainous landscapes of Greece.
