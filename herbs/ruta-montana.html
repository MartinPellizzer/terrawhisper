Mountain Rue, scientifically known as Ruta montana, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Characterized by its distinctive blue-green, divided foliage and delicate yellow flowers, this species belongs to the Rutaceae family. It is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique aromatic and medicinal properties.
