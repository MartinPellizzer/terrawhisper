Phlomis lanceolata

Phlomis lanceolata, commonly known as Lance-leaved Phlomis, is a striking perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its upright, lance-shaped leaves and hooded, yellowish-white flowers, this resilient plant thrives in rocky, sunny habitats. It is valued in herbal medicine and as a beautiful ornamental for dry gardens.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis lanceolata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
