This plant, Phlomis lanceolata, is characterized by its distinct morphological features, specifically its lance-shaped leaves and its flowering aerial parts. The leaves are typically arranged in opposite pairs along the stem, possessing a textured, often hairy surface that helps the plant retain moisture.
