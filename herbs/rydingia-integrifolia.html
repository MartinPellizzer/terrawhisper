Rydingia integrifolia

Rydingia integrifolia, commonly known as the Smooth Rydingiia, is a versatile shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a subject of significant ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Rydingia integrifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Rydingia.

Common Names

This plant, Rydingia integrifolia, is known by several different common names across various languages and regions. In Persian, it is referred to as گل سرخ حبشی, while in Amharic, it is called ጥንጁት. In Chinese, the plant is commonly known as 阿比西尼亚玫瑰 (or 阿比西尼亚玫瑰 in simplified Chinese), and in Traditional Chinese, it is identified as 阿比西尼亞玫瑰.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions in Northeast Tropical Africa and the Arabian Peninsula. In the northeastern portion of the African continent, its presence is documented in Eritrea. It is also found within the borders of Ethiopia, extending its range across this East African nation. Moving across the Red Sea, the species can be located within the Arabian Peninsula. Specifically, its habitat includes the territory of Yemen, completing its recorded distribution pattern.
