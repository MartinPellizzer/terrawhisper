Blue sage, scientifically known as Salvia farinacea, is a stunning perennial native to the American Southwest. Renowned for its striking spikes of cobalt flowers and silvery foliage, this drought-tolerant plant thrives in sunny landscapes. It serves as a vital nectar source, attracting pollinators like butterflies and hummingbirds to gardens.
