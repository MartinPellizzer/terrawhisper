Wood-apple, scientifically known as Limonia acidissima, is a versatile deciduous tree native to South Asia. Renowned for its hard, woody shell and tangy pulp, it serves as both a nutritious food source and a potent medicinal herb, offering significant therapeutic benefits for digestive health and immune support.
