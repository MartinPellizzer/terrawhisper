Limonia acidissima

Limonia acidissima, commonly known as the wood apple, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South Asia. Renowned for its astringent properties, the fruit and bark are used to treat digestive ailments, dysentery, and fever. This nutrient-rich botanical serves as a vital natural remedy in traditional Ayurvedic medicine.

  • Scientific name: Limonia acidissima
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae, it is classified under the genus Limonia.
