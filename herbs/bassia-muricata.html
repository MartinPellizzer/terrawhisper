Bassia muricata

Bassia muriata, commonly known as Burning Bush, is a versatile annual herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its striking crimson foliage, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is frequently utilized for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support various health functions through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Bassia muricata
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed within the genus Bassia.
