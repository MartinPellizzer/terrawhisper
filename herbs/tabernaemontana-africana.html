Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions within West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence is documented in the nation of Gambia. It can also be found growing in the territories of Guinea and Guinea-Bissau. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into Ghana. Finally, its geographical range includes the coastal areas of the Ivory Coast.

Plant Parts

This plant, Tabernaemontana africana, is a medicinal shrub where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties, most notably the root. The root of the plant is frequently harvested and prepared in different ways, such as being decocted or crushed, to treat a variety of ailments ranging from skin infections to internal inflammatory conditions. In traditional medicine, the chemical constituents found within the root are believed to possess significant bioactive qualities that contribute to its healing potential.
