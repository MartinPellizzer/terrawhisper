Two-flower passionflower, scientifically known as Passiflora biflora, is a captivating climbing vine native to the Americas. Renowned for its delicate, twin-bloomed flowers, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional herbalism. It is frequently utilized for its calming properties, offering natural support for relaxation and soothing the nervous system.

This plant, Passiflora biflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Passifloraceae and the genus Passiflora.
