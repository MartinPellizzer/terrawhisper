Passiflora biflora

Passiflora biflora, commonly known as the two-flowered passionflower, is a captivating climbing vine native to the Americas. Renowned for its unique, delicate blossoms, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to promote relaxation, alleviate anxiety, and support sleep, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and tranquility.

  • Scientific name: Passiflora biflora
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Passiflora biflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Passifloraceae and the genus Passiflora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents and climate zones. In Eastern Asia, its presence is documented specifically within Taiwan. Its distribution extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be found in India. Within the Malesia region, the species is located in both Jawa and the Philippines. Finally, the plant has reached the Southeastern United States, specifically appearing in Florida.
