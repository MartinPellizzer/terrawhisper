Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse and fragmented geographical distribution spanning multiple continents. In the United States, its presence is noted across several states in the North-Central region, specifically within Iowa, Missouri, and Oklahoma. Additionally, it can be found in the Northeastern part of the country in Indiana. Moving beyond North America, the species also has a documented range in the Malesian region. Specifically, its occurrence is recorded on the island of Jawa.

Plant Parts

This plant, Helianthus angustifolius, is characterized by its distinct reproductive structures, specifically the flower, which plays a central role in its biological identity. As a member of the Asteraceae family, the flower is actually a composite head made up of numerous smaller florets that attract pollinators essential to its lifecycle. In the context of medicinal interest, these floral components are often the primary focus for studying the plant's specific bioactive compounds and therapeutic potential.
