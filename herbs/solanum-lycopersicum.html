Solanum lycopersicum

Fiaschello

Solanum lycopersicum, commonly known as the tomato, is more than a culinary staple. Rich in lycopene, vitamins, and antioxidants, this medicinal powerhouse supports cardiovascular health and combats oxidative stress. Beyond nutrition, its bioactive compounds offer significant anti-inflammatory properties, making it a vital subject in functional medicine and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Solanum lycopersicum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Solanum.
