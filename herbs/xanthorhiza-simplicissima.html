Xanthorhiza simplicissima

Xanthorhiza simplicissima, commonly known as Yellowroot, is a perennial herb native to western North America. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, its bitter rhizomes have long been utilized for their medicinal properties. Today, researchers study this plant for its potential bioactive compounds, exploring its applications in treating various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Xanthorhiza simplicissima
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe
