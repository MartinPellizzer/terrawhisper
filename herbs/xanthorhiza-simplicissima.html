Shrub yellowroot, scientifically known as Xanthorhiza simplicissima, is a perennial medicinal plant native to western North America. Renowned for its bright yellow rhizomes, it has historically been utilized in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, serving as a potent remedy for digestive and skin ailments.
