Wild wormwood, scientifically known as Parthenium hysterophorus, is a highly invasive, herbaceous annual plant native to the Americas. While utilized in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, it is globally recognized as a problematic weed that causes severe allergic reactions and disrupts diverse ecological systems worldwide.
