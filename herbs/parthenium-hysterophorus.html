Parthenium hysterophorus

Parthenium hysterophorus, commonly known as Parthenium weed or Congress grass, is a highly invasive annual herb native to the Americas. While it poses significant threats to biodiversity and human health through allergic reactions, it holds medicinal potential in traditional practices for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Parthenium hysterophorus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Parthenium.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans multiple continents and climate zones. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented in countries such as Germany and Poland.
