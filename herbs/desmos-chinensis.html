Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it is specifically documented in the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species can also be found growing within the borders of Bangladesh. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse habitats where Desmos chinensis thrives.
