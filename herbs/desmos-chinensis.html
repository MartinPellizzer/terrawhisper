Desmos chinensis

Desmos chinensis, commonly known as the Chinese Desmos, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and flowers possess potent bioactive compounds. These properties offer therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant effects, making it a valuable resource in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Desmos chinensis
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Annonaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Desmos.

Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it is specifically documented in the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species can also be found growing within the borders of Bangladesh.
