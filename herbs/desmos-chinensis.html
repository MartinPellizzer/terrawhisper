What's the taxonomical classification of Desmos chinensis?

Desmos chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Magnoliales. This plant is a member of the family Annonaceae, which is characterized by its specific floral and fruit structures. Further taxonomic refinement places it within the genus Desmos, ultimately identifying it by its specific species name, chinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Desmos chinensis has woody climbing stems with small, simple, entire-margined leaves arranged in an opposite or sub-opposite pattern. Its inflorescence consists of small, fragrant, yellowish-white flowers that emerge from the leaf axils. The fruit is a fleshy, berry-like drupe that is often green or yellowish when maturing.
