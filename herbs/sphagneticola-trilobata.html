Indian Daisy, scientifically known as Sphagneticola trilobata, is a resilient creeping perennial valued for its medicinal potential. Often found in tropical regions, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer promising therapeutic properties for modern pharmacological research.
