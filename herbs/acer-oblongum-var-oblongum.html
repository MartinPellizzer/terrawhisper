Acer oblongum var. oblongum

Acer oblongum var. oblongum, commonly known as the Oblong Maple, is a distinctive woody species valued for its unique morphological characteristics. Primarily found in tropical and subtropical regions, this variety plays a vital role in its ecosystem, offering significant botanical interest and potential medicinal properties within traditional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Acer oblongum var. oblongum
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Acer oblongum var. oblongum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Acer.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Its range also extends into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the state of Assam. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to inhabit diverse environmental landscapes.
