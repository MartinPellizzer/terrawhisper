Lysimachia pentapetala

Lysimachia pentapetala, commonly known as the five-petaled loosestrife, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic potential. Characterized by its vibrant blooms, this plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to address specific ailments, offering a unique intersection of botanical beauty and profound pharmacological significance in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Lysimachia pentapetala
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, falling into the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lysimachia.
