Phryma nana

Phrymna nana, commonly known as the dwarf primrose, is a delicate perennial herb native to specific subtropical regions. Characterized by its small, charming flowers and compact growth habit, this medicinal plant has long been valued in traditional herbalism for its unique bioactive properties and potential therapeutic applications in natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Phryma nana
  • Family: Phrymaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Phrymaceae and the genus Phryma.

Common Names

This plant, Phrymna nana, is known by several common names depending on the linguistic context, specifically within Chinese-speaking regions. In Mandarin Chinese, it is referred to as 透骨草 (tòu gǔ cǎo), a name that is consistently used across various regional dialects including simplified Chinese in mainland China (zh-cn) and traditional Chinese in Taiwan (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing within the Amur and Khabarovsk territories. Its presence extends further into the Kuril Islands and the Primorye region of the same country. Beyond Russia, the species is also documented in China, specifically across the South-Central areas. Together, these locations define the natural range of this medicinal species.
