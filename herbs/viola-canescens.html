Viola canescens

Viola canescens, commonly known as the cane violet, is a delicate perennial herb native to specific regions of Australia. Characterized by its charming violet blooms and heart-shaped leaves, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its soothing properties and its unique contribution to diverse botanical ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Viola canescens
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Violaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Viola.
