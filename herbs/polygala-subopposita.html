Polygala subopposita belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Its taxonomic journey continues through the family Polygalaceae, where it is placed within the genus Polygala, eventually reaching its specific designation as the species subopposita.
