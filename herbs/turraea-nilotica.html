Turraea nilotica

Turraea nilotica, commonly known as the African Rosewood or Umutuba, is a versatile medicinal tree native to Africa. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, its bark and resin are traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, serving as a vital resource in various ethnobotanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Turraea nilotica
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Turraea.
