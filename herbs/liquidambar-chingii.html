Distribution

This plant, known as Liquidambar chingii, exhibits a specific range of occurrence across East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its distribution is primarily concentrated in the South-Central regions. It can also be found throughout the Southeast portions of the country. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, Vietnam serves as a key geographical area where this medicinal plant is located.

Plant Parts

This plant, known for its aromatic properties, utilizes various anatomical structures for its medicinal value. The thick, resinous bark is perhaps the most significant part, as it is harvested to obtain the valuable styrax resin. The branches and sturdy stems support a dense canopy of fragrant leaves, which contribute to the plant's overall chemical profile. While the roots anchor the tree firmly into the soil, providing structural stability and nutrient uptake, the flowers may produce nectar to attract pollinators, completing the complex biological system of this medicinal species.
