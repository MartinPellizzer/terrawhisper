Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as wild garlic, is characterized by its lush, broad green leaves that emerge from the ground in early spring. These leaves are the primary part of the plant used for medicinal and culinary purposes, as they contain high concentrations of sulfur compounds and essential oils. When crushed or eaten, the leaves release a potent aroma reminiscent of garlic, making them a valuable resource for both flavoring food and supporting health through their antimicrobial properties.
