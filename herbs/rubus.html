Rubus

Rubus, commonly known as blackberries or raspberries, belongs to the Rosaceae family. Renowned for their delicious fruits, these plants offer significant medicinal potential. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and polyphenols, Rubus species possess anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and digestive benefits, making them valuable contributors to both traditional herbalism and modern nutrition.

  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe
