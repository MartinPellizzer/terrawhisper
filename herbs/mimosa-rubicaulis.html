Mimosa rubicaulis

Mimosa rubicaulis, commonly known as the creeping mimosa, is a versatile climbing shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its delicate foliage and medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally used in herbal remedies to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in folk medicine.

  • Scientific name: Mimosa rubicaulis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Mimosa. As a member of the Mimosa genus, Mimosa rubicaulis represents a specific lineage of medicinal flora within this complex taxonomic hierarchy.
