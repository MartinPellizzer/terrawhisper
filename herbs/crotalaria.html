Crotalaria

Crotalaria, commonly known as rattlepods, belongs to the Fabaceae family. These versatile plants are widely distributed across tropical and subtropical regions. Renowned for their medicinal properties, various species are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Crotalaria
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Crotalaria.
