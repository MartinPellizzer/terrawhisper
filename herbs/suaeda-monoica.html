Suaeda monoica

Suaeda monoica, commonly known as Sea Blite, is a succulent halophyte frequently found in coastal and saline environments. Renowned for its adaptability to high salinity, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine for its antioxidant properties and its role in treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Suaeda monoica
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaranthaceae and is situated within the genus Suaeda.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Egypt. Moving toward the Northeast Tropical Africa region, it can be found in the country of Chad. The species also extends into the territories of Djibouti and Eritrea. Finally, its geographical range encompasses the highlands and lowlands of Ethiopia.
