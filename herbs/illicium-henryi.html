Illicium henryi

Illicium henryi, commonly known as Henry's star anise, is a captivating evergreen shrub native to China. Distinguished by its glossy, leathery leaves and fragrant, star-shaped flowers, this ornamental plant is prized in landscaping. Beyond its beauty, it holds significant botanical interest for its aromatic properties and traditional medicinal potential.

  • Scientific name: Illicium henryi
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
