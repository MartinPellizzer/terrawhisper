Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Within this zone, it can be specifically located in the country of Ghana. Its presence is also documented across the borders of Guinea and the Ivory Coast. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the territory of Liberia.
