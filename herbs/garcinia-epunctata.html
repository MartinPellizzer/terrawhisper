Garcinia epunctata

Garcinia epunctata, commonly known as the "Wild Mangosteen," is a significant medicinal plant native to the tropical forests of South America. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues, offering vast potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia epunctata
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales and the family Clusiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Garcinia.
