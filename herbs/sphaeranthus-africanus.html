Plant Parts

This plant, Sphaeranthus africanus, is highly valued in traditional medicine, where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. Often, the whole plant is harvested to prepare decoctions, though many practitioners specifically focus on the whole aerial plant to capture all the bioactive compounds above the ground. The medicinal efficacy is frequently concentrated in the leaves, which are used in topical applications or infusions. Additionally, the distinct flower heads, often referred to as flowers in plural contexts due to their clustered nature, are integral parts of the plant's botanical profile and are sometimes included in traditional preparations to enhance its healing potential.
