Acer pictum

Acer pictum, commonly known as the painted maple, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique bark and foliage, it contains bioactive compounds utilized for their anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Acer pictum
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, specifically within the family Sapindaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed by the genus Acer.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range that spans across both Eastern Europe and the Russian Far East. In the western portion of its distribution, it can be found within the Baltic States. Moving eastward, the species is well-established in the Amur region of the Russian Far East. It also inhabits the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories within the same vast eastern expanse. Additionally, the plant's reach extends to the Kuril Islands, completing its diverse distribution pattern.
