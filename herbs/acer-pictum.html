Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range that spans across both Eastern Europe and the Russian Far East. In the western portion of its distribution, it can be found within the Baltic States. Moving eastward, the species is well-established in the Amur region of the Russian Far East. It also inhabits the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories within the same vast eastern expanse. Additionally, the plant's reach extends to the Kuril Islands, completing its diverse distribution pattern.
