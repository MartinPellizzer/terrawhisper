Salvia pachyphylla

Salvia pachyphylla, commonly known as a member of the sage family, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific arid regions. Characterized by its thick, fleshy leaves, this species is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique phytochemical properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits for various wellness applications and treatments.

  • Scientific name: Salvia pachyphylla
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae and falls within the genus Salvia.
