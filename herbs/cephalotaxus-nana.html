Cephalotaxus nana

Cephalotaxus nana, commonly known as the Dwarf Yew, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to East Asian forests. Renowned for its dense foliage and unique botanical characteristics, this medicinal plant is studied for its potential bioactive compounds, offering significant interest in pharmacological research and traditional herbal medicine applications.

  • Scientific name: Cephalotaxus nana
  • Family: Cephalotaxaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cephalotaxaceae and falls under the genus Cephalotaxus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range across several regions within East Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Moving further east, the species can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Japan. Its distribution also extends to the Korean Peninsula. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Cephalotaxus nana.
