Dryopteris goeringiana

What's the taxonomical classification of Dryopteris goeringiana?

Dryopteris goeringiana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Polypodiales and belongs to the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dryopteris and is specifically designated as the species goeringiana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dryopteris goeringiana has a creeping rhizome covered in brownish scales and produces pinnate fronds that typically reach lengths of 20 to 50 centimeters. The stipes are long and slender, often exhibiting a reddish or brownish hue near the base. Each frond consists of multiple pinnae that are arranged in a delicate, lace-like pattern. The pinnules are small, oblong, and feature slightly serrated margins that contribute to the plant's fine texture. This fern is characterized by its elegant, arching growth habit and its ability to form dense clumps over time.
