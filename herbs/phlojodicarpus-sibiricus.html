Phlojodicarpus sibiricus

Phlojodicarpus sibiricus, commonly known as Siberian Phlojodicarpus, is a unique medicinal plant native to East Asian regions. Valued in traditional herbal practices, it possesses diverse bioactive compounds. Researchers are currently exploring its potential therapeutic properties, particularly for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, to support modern pharmacological developments.

  • Scientific name: Phlojodicarpus sibiricus
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Phlojodicarpus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the vast expanse of Siberia. Its presence is documented across several key administrative regions including Buryatiya and Chita. Furthermore, the species can be found thriving in the territories of Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk. The distribution also extends further north into the remote areas of Yakutiya. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's affinity for the diverse Siberian landscape.
