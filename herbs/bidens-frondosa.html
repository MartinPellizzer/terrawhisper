Bidens frondosa

Beggar-tick

Bidens frondosa, commonly known as black-jack or beggarticks, is a resilient perennial herb native to North America. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this plant boasts significant medicinal properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. It is frequently utilized in natural remedies to support immune health and treat various skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Bidens frondosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Bidens.
