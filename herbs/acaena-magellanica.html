Greater Burnet, scientifically known as Acaena magellanica, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific coastal and subantarctic regions. Renowned for its delicate, fine-textured foliage and small, inconspicuous flowers, this plant is valued in traditional ethnobotany for its potential medicinal properties and its ability to thrive in harsh environments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across the southern regions of South America. Its distribution spans the northern and central parts of Chile, reaching all the way down to the southern coast. In Argentina, it is found in both the northwest territories and the southernmost provinces. This specific pattern shows that the species thrives in various climates within the southern cone. Consequently, its geographical range covers a significant portion of the southern tip of the continent.
