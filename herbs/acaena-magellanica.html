Acaena magellanica

Acaena magellanica, commonly known as Magellanic Acaena, is a resilient subantarctic herb native to the rugged landscapes of South America. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this low-growing plant offers unique bioactive properties. Exploring its medicinal potential provides vital insights into the natural healing traditions of extreme southern ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Acaena magellanica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Acaena. Specifically, the species is identified as Acaena magellanica.

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across the southern regions of South America.
