Stryphnodendron adstringens

Stryphnodendron adstringens, commonly known as Barbatimão, is a powerful medicinal plant native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its high tannin content, it possesses potent astringent, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used in wound healing and treating gastrointestinal issues, it remains a vital resource in contemporary phytotherapy.

  • Scientific name: Stryphnodendron adstringens
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is specifically situated in the family Fabaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Stryphnodendron.
