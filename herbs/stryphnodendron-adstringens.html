Stryphnodendron adstringens, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Commonly known as Barbatimão, its bark is renowned for its powerful astringent and antimicrobial properties. Traditionally used to treat skin inflammations, wounds, and gynecological issues, it remains a vital resource in both folk medicine and pharmacology.
