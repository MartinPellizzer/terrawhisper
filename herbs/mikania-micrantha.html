Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Mikania micrantha, utilizes its leaves as a primary component in various traditional medicinal applications. These leaves are typically characterized by their ovate shape and serrated margins, serving as the main site for the concentration of bioactive compounds. When processed, the leaves are often used in decoctions or topical applications to exploit their natural properties for treating skin ailments and inflammatory conditions.
