Bupleurum kaoi

Bupleurum kaoi, commonly known as Korean Bupleurum, is a highly valued medicinal herb in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and hepatoprotective properties, it is frequently utilized to support liver health and regulate physiological functions. This aromatic perennial remains a vital component in various herbal formulations.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum kaoi
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is specifically situated in the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Bupleurum.

Distribution

This plant possesses a very limited and specific geographical range. Its natural occurrence is concentrated entirely within the region of Eastern Asia. Specifically, the species is found growing natively on the island of Taiwan. Because it is restricted to this single location, it is considered an endemic species to the area. Consequently, its wild populations are localized to the unique habitats provided by the Taiwanese landscape.
