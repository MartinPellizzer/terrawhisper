Populus alba

Populus alba, commonly known as the white poplar, is a deciduous tree prized for its striking silvery foliage. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, its bark and various components have been utilized in traditional medicine for their potential anti-inflammatory and therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Populus alba
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling into the family Salicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Populus.
