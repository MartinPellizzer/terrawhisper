Silver poplar, scientifically known as Populus alba, is a deciduous tree valued for its distinctive shimmering foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this species holds significance in traditional medicine. Its bark and leaves are frequently utilized for their anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, offering natural remedies for various common ailments.

This plant, known as Populus alba, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of the European continent. This plant, Populus alba, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, particularly its leaves, leaf-buds, and bud exudates. The leaves are often harvested for their specific chemical constituents, while the leaf-buds serve as a concentrated source of active compounds used in traditional remedies. Additionally, the bud exudates, which are the resinous or sap-like substances released from the developing buds, are highly valued for their unique therapeutic qualities.
