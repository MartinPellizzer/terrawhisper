Curcuma kwangsiensis

Curcumma kwaangsiensis, commonly known as a precious medicinal herb, is a significant species within the Zingiberaceae family. Native to specific tropical regions, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds. It serves as a vital resource for exploring new therapeutic possibilities in modern pharmacology.
