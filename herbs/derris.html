Derris

Derris, commonly known as the rot rot plant, is a genus of climbing shrubs native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent insecticidal properties, extracts from its roots have long been used to control lice and mosquitoes. This versatile botanical offers significant value in both traditional medicine and natural pest control.

  • Scientific name: Derris
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, the medicinal specimen is categorized under the genus Derris.
