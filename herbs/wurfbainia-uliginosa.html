Wurfbainia uliginosa

Wurfbainia uliginosa, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in traditional herbalism, belongs to the Cornaceae family. Found primarily in East Asian regions, this species is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds for potential applications in treating various inflammatory and oxidative conditions.

  • Scientific name: Wurfbainia uliginosa
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is a member of the order Zingiberales and falls under the family Zingiberaceae. Taxonomically, it is identified by the genus Wurfbainia, with the specific species being Wurfbainia uliginosa.
