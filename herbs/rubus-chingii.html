Palm-leaf raspberry, scientifically known as Rubus chingii, is a perennial shrub native to China, highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant is primarily used to support cardiovascular health, regulate blood sugar, and combat oxidative stress, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.
