Palm-leaf raspberry, scientifically known as Rubus chingii, is a perennial shrub native to China, highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant is primarily used to support cardiovascular health, regulate blood sugar, and combat oxidative stress, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Chinquapin raspberry, is a medicinal species where various components hold significance. The most notable parts used in traditional medicine are the fruits, which undergo a distinct transformation during maturation. Initially, the plant produces green fruit that remains firm and acidic, but as they develop, they transition into the highly valued ripe fruit, which is celebrated for its nutritional and therapeutic properties. In addition to the edible components, the morphology of the plant includes characteristic prickly stems that protect the climbing vines, while the harvested fruit serves as the primary source for many medicinal preparations.
