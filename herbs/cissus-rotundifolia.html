Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa. In West-Central Tropical Africa, Cissus rotundifolia can be found in both Burundi and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends further east into the Northeast Tropical Africa region. Within this northeastern area, the species is documented in Djibouti and Eritrea. Finally, the plant is also known to grow throughout Ethiopia.
