Cissus rotundifolia

Cissus rotundifolia, commonly known as Indian grape or various local names, is a versatile climbing vine renowned in traditional medicine. Rich in bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and wound-healing properties, offering significant therapeutic potential for managing diverse health conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Cissus rotundifolia
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa. In West-Central Tropical Africa, Cissus rotundifolia can be found in both Burundi and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends further east into the Northeast Tropical Africa region. Within this northeastern area, the species is documented in Djibouti and Eritrea. Finally, the plant is also known to grow throughout Ethiopia.
