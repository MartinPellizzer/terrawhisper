Monarda citriodora var. austromontana

Monarda citriodora var. austromontana, commonly known as Lemon Bee Balm, is a fragrant perennial native to North American prairies. Renowned for its citrus-scented foliage and vibrant blossoms, this medicinal herb offers therapeutic properties, often used in traditional remedies to soothe digestive issues and support relaxation through its aromatic oils.

  • Scientific name: Monarda citriodora var. austromontana
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Monarda, specifically as the variety citriodora var. austromontana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across portions of North America. In the Southwestern United States, its presence is noted within the state of Arizona. Moving into the South-Central United States, it can also be found throughout New Mexico. Its distribution extends significantly into Mexico, covering the Northeast region of the country. Additionally, this variety occupies the Northwest and Southwest territories of Mexico.
