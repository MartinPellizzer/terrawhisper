Plant Parts

This plant, Vaccinium emarginatum, belongs to the Ericaceae family, and like many of its relatives in the genus Vaccinium, it maintains a specialized relationship with soil microorganisms. While the plant possesses various structural components such as stems, leaves, and berries, one of its most biologically significant features is the presence of the root nodule.
