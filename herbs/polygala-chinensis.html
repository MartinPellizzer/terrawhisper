Polygala chinensis

Polygala chinensis, commonly known as Jie Geng in Traditional Chinese Medicine, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in saponins, it is primarily used to soothe coughs, relieve phlegm, and support respiratory health. This potent botanical plays a vital role in holistic wellness practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Polygala chinensis
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Polygalaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Polygala.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout various regions of China, including South-Central, Southeast, and the island of Hainan. Its presence extends beyond the mainland into the island of Taiwan. Additionally, it can be found within the Nansei-shoto islands of Eastern Asia. These specific locations highlight its preference for the subtropical climates found across East Asia. Overall, the geographical range of Polygala chinensis is concentrated in these diverse coastal and southern territories.
