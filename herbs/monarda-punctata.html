Horsemint, scientifically known as Monarda punctata, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its unique, button-like flower clusters. Native to North America, this aromatic plant belongs to the mint family. It is valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial properties and serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators.
