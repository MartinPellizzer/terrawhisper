What's the taxonomical classification of Pittosporum rehderianum?

Pittosporum rehderianum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized under the order Apiales. This plant is a distinct representative of the family Pittosporaceae, falling under the genus Pittosporum, and is specifically identified by its species name, rehderianum.
