What's the taxonomical classification of Blumea clarkei?

Blumea clarkei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, which is characterized by its composite flower heads. More specifically, it is categorized within the genus Blumea, and its specific designation is the species clarkei.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Blumea clarkei has an erect, perennial herbaceous habit characterized by stems that are typically covered in a dense layer of glandular, sticky hairs. The leaves are arranged oppositely along the stem and possess an ovate to lanceolate shape with serrated margins. A defining feature of this species is the presence of numerous small, yellowish-white flower heads clustered in terminal corymbose panicles. These composite flowers are surrounded by involucral bracts that are often glandular and hairy, contributing to the plant's tactile texture. The overall morphology reflects its adaptation to humid environments through its specialized trichomes and leaf structure.
