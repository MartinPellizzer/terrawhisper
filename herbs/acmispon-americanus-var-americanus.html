Acmispon americanus var. americanus

Acmispon americanus var. americanus, commonly known as American broom, is a versatile medicinal plant native to various American landscapes. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it is frequently utilized for its potential therapeutic properties. This botanical study explores its unique chemical composition, ecological significance, and historical applications in folk medicine.

  • Scientific name: Acmispon americanus var. americanus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Acmispon. Specifically, it is identified by the botanical variety Acmispon americanus var. americanus.
