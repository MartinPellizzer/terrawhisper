Suaeda

Suaeda, commonly known as sea blite, is a versatile genus of succulent plants found in coastal and saline environments. Renowned for its salt-tolerance, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. Often used to treat inflammation and skin ailments, this resilient plant offers unique biochemical properties for therapeutic use.

  • Scientific name: Suaeda
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaranthaceae, specifically within the genus Suaeda.
