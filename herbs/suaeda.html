Sea-blites, scientifically known as Suaeda, is a genus of succulent halophytes adapted to saline environments. Found globally in coastal marshes and salt pans, these plants thrive in harsh conditions. Beyond their ecological significance, various Suaeda species offer medicinal potential, traditionally used for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and therapeutic properties.
