Lygodium flexuosum

Lygodium flexuosum, commonly known as the climbing fern, is a versatile medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and inflammation. This creeping species offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds and botanical properties.

  • Scientific name: Lygodium flexuosum
  • Family: Schizaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Schizaeales. Finally, it is a member of the family Schizaeaceae within the genus Lygodium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions in East and South Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence also extends to the Nansei-shotto islands within Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the Assam region. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive across diverse Asian landscapes.
