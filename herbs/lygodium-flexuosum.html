Maidenhair-creeper, scientifically known as Lygodium flexuosum, is a delicate climbing fern native to various tropical regions. Characterized by its slender, creeping rhizomes and fine, lace-like foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in diverse folk medicines for its purported healing and restorative properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions in East and South Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence also extends to the Nansei-shotto islands within Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the Assam region. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive across diverse Asian landscapes.
