Lantana xenica

Lantana xenica, commonly known as the white lantana, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its small, fragrant white blossoms, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Lantana xenica
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Lantana xenica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lantana.
