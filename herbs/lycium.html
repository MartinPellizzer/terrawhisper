Lycium

Lycium, commonly known as goji or wolfberry, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional Eastern medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich berries, it promotes longevity, enhances immunity, and supports eye health. This resilient shrub offers diverse therapeutic benefits, making it a cornerstone of holistic wellness and nutritional supplementation.

  • Scientific name: Lycium
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is classified under the genus Lycium.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Goji, utilizes various components for its medicinal and nutritional value. Each individual fruit is a small, bright red berry that is highly sought after in traditional medicine. These fleshy fruits are prized for their nutrient density and are often dried for long-term use. In addition to the berries, the bark of the shrub can also be utilized in certain traditional preparations to support overall health.
