Coleus grandidentatus

Coleus grandidentatus, commonly known as the large-toothed coleus, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves are frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and skin inflammations. This plant offers a rich source of bioactive compounds for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Coleus grandidentatus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls under the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Coleus.
