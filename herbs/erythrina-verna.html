Erythrina verna

Erythrina mulungu

Erythrina verna, commonly known as the Coral Tree, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its vibrant, crimson blossoms, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various cultures utilize its bark and leaves to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Erythrina verna
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. It is specifically identified by the genus Erythrina, where it is known as Erythrina verna.
