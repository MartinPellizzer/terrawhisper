Erythrina mulungu, scientifically known as Erythrina verna, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its sedative and anxiolytic properties, it is traditionally used to treat anxiety, insomnia, and hypertension. This potent botanical serves as a vital natural remedy for calming the nervous system and restoring balance.
