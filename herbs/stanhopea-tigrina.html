Stanhopea tigrina

Stanhopea tigrina, commonly known as the Tiger Stanhopea, is a stunning tropical orchid prized for its intricate, tiger-striped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds traditional significance in folk medicine, where its extracts are occasionally utilized for their unique bioactive properties and potential healing qualities in regional practices.

  • Scientific name: Stanhopea tigrina
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asparagales, it is a member of the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Stanhopea.
