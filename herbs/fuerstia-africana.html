Fuerstia africana

Fuerstia africana, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical Africa, is highly valued for its diverse pharmacological properties. Often utilized in traditional healing practices, this plant contains bioactive compounds believed to treat various ailments, ranging from inflammatory conditions to infections, making it a subject of botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Fuerstia africana
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant, Fuerstia africana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
