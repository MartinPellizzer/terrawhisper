Phyllanthus orbicularis

Phyllanthus orbicularis, commonly known as the Orbicular Phyllanthus, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its unique leaf structure, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, often utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and support overall metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus orbicularis
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it resides in the family Phyllanthaceae and is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.
