Hedychium flavum

Hedychium flavum, commonly known as Yellow Ginger Lily, is a fragrant perennial herb native to Asia. Renowned for its striking golden blooms, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is widely utilized for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties, offering diverse therapeutic benefits in herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Hedychium flavum
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Zingiberales and placed in the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Hedychium.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central and Southeast areas, as well as within the Tibet region. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is naturally occurring in Bangladesh. These diverse locations highlight its broad ecological range across both East and South Asia.
