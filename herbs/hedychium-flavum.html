Hedychium flavum, scientifically known as Hedychium flavum, is a perennial rhizomatous herb celebrated for its fragrant, yellow blossoms. Native to the tropical regions of Asia, this aromatic plant is deeply rooted in traditional medicine, where its rhizomes are utilized to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and inflammation.
