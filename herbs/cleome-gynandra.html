This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Brassicales and the family Cleomaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cleome.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several continents and diverse climatic regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in Spain. Its presence extends into Northern Africa, specifically within the territories of Egypt and Libya. Additionally, the species is located in the Macaronesian region, notably in Cape Verde. Finally, its range reaches West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Benin.
