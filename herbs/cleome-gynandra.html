Cleome gynandra

African cabbage

Cleome gynandra, commonly known as spider plant, is a nutritious leafy vegetable widely cultivated across Africa and Asia. Renowned for its exceptional medicinal properties, it is rich in vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants. This resilient plant is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including inflammation, anemia, and digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Cleome gynandra
  • Family: Cleomaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Brassicales and the family Cleomaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cleome.
