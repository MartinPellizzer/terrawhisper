Ampelopsis glandulosa var. glandulosa

Ampelopsis glandulosa var. glandulosa, commonly known as the glandular grape, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Characterized by its glandular structures, this variety is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential through its rich concentration of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Ampelopsis glandulosa var. glandulosa
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Vitales and the family Vitaceae, it is classified under the genus Ampelopsis. Specifically, the variety glandulosa represents the taxonomic designation for Ampelopsis glandulosa var. glandulosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in the state of Assam. Finally, the plant is also known to inhabit the country of Bangladesh.
