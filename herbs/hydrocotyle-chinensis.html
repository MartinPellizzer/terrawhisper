Hydrocotyle chinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Hydrocotyle chinensis?

Hydrocotyle chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Apiales. The plant is a member of the family Araliaceae, which places it within the genus Hydrocotyle, specifically identified by the species epithet chinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Hydrocotyle chinensis has small, kidney-shaped to orbicular leaves that measure between 5 and 25 millimeters in diameter and feature crenate or scalloped margins. These leaves are borne on long, slender petioles that emerge from a creeping rhizomatous stem. The plant displays a prostrate growth habit, forming dense mats through the spreading of these stoloniferous stems. Its flowers are inconspicuous, tiny, and white, typically arranged in small umbels. The overall foliage possesses a bright green color and a somewhat succulent texture.
