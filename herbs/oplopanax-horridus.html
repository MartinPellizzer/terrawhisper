Devil's club, scientifically known as Opoplanax horridus, is a formidable perennial shrub native to the Pacific Northwest. Renowned for its sharp spines and potent medicinal properties, this plant has been used for centuries by Indigenous cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to respiratory infections and skin conditions.

Plant Parts

This plant, Opoplanax horridus, is valued in traditional medicine for various therapeutic applications derived from its specific anatomical structures. The medicinal properties are primarily concentrated within the root bark and the inner stem bark, which contain essential bioactive compounds. Depending on the preparation method, practitioners may utilize the raw inner bark or processed root barks to extract healing elements. For more standardized or convenient administration, the plant is often processed into a fine dry root bark powder, ensuring that the concentrated essence of the species is easily accessible for medicinal use.
