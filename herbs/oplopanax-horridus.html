Devil's club, scientifically known as Opoplanax horridus, is a formidable perennial shrub native to the Pacific Northwest. Renowned for its sharp spines and potent medicinal properties, this plant has been used for centuries by Indigenous cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to respiratory infections and skin conditions.
