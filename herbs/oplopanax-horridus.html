Oplopanax horridus

Devil's club

Oplopanax horridus, commonly known as Devil's Club, is a formidable medicinal shrub native to the Pacific Northwest. Revered by Indigenous cultures, this thorny plant offers profound therapeutic potential. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and digestive issues, it remains a vital symbol of forest ecology and healing.

  • Scientific name: Oplopanax horridus
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Araliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Oplopanax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across the northern reaches of North America. Within the Subarctic American region, it can be found growing in both Alaska and the Yukon. Its presence extends into Western Canada, specifically throughout the provinces of Alberta and British Columbia. Additionally, the species is documented in the eastern part of the country within Ontario. These diverse locations highlight its ability to thrive across various Canadian and Alaskan landscapes.
