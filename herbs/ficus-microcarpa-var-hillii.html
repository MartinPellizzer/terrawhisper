Ficus microcarpa var. hillii

Ficus microcarpa var. hillii, commonly known as the Hillii Fig, is a versatile plant valued for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Traditionally used in various cultures, it is studied for its bioactive compounds, which may offer antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, making it a significant subject in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Ficus microcarpa var. hillii
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and family Moraceae, it is classified under the genus Ficus. Specifically, it is identified by the variety name hillii as Ficus microcarpa var. hillii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several distinct biogeographical regions. Within the Malesia realm, it can be found throughout the Lesser Sunda Islands. Its range extends into Papuasia, where it is native to New Guinea. In the Australian region, the species is specifically located in Queensland. Finally, the plant reaches the Southwestern Pacific, spanning as far as New Caledonia.
