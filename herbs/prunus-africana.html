Pygeum africanum

Prunus africana · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (233 studies) · ★★★★★

Pygeum africanum, scientifically known as Pruunus africanum, is a medicinal plant native to Africa, highly valued for its bark. It is most widely recognized for its therapeutic properties in treating benign prostatic hyperplasia. This botanical extract serves as a key natural remedy in managing prostate health and urinary function.
