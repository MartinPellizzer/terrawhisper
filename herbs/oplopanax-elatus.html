Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions within Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is notably concentrated in the Primorye territory. Moving south, it is widely found throughout the Manchuria region of China. The species also extends its natural range into the Korean Peninsula. Together, these areas define the core geographical footprint of Oplopanax elatus.
