Oplopanax elatus

Oplopanax elatus, commonly known as Devil's Club, is a sacred medicinal plant native to the Pacific Northwest. Deeply revered by Indigenous cultures, this potent shrub offers diverse healing properties. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin conditions, it remains a cornerstone of traditional botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Oplopanax elatus
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Araliaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Oplopanax.

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions within Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is notably concentrated in the Primorye territory. Moving south, it is widely found throughout the Manchuria region of China. The species also extends its natural range into the Korean Peninsula.
