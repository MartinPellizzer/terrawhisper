Tradescantia pallida

Tradescantia pallida, commonly known as Purple Heart, is a striking ornamental perennial celebrated for its deep violet foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this hardy plant holds significant medicinal potential. Traditionally used in various cultures, it is studied for its bioactive compounds, offering promising antioxidant and anti-inflammatory therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Tradescantia pallida
  • Family: Commelinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Commelinales and family Commelineae, it is classified under the genus Tradescantia. Specifically, its scientific name is Tradescantia pallida.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Spain.
