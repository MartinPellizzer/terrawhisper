Glycosmis pentaphylla

Gin berry

Glycosmis pentaphyllia, commonly known as Ashoka or False Ashoka, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned in traditional Ayurveda, its leaves, roots, and bark possess potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. It is widely utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin disorders, and inflammatory conditions in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Glycosmis pentaphylla
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Glycosmis pentaphyllla, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rutaceae and falls under the genus Glycosmis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across the Asian continent. Within the country of China, it can be specifically found in the South-Central regions. Its presence is also significant across the Indian Subcontinent, spanning from the state of Assam to the broader territories of India. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout the East Himalaya mountain range. Finally, its geographical reach extends into the neighboring nation of Bangladesh.
