Pteris insignis

What's the taxonomical classification of Pteris insignis?

Pteris insignis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Polypodiales and is a member of the family Pteridaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its genus, Pteris, and its specific species name, insignis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pteris insignis has pinnate fronds that measure between 30 and 60 centimeters in length and consist of a central rachis supporting multiple pairs of oblong to lanceolate pinnae. The texture of the foliage is generally thin and membranous, often appearing a bright green color when healthy. Small sori are arranged in distinct rows along the underside of the leaflets, which is a characteristic feature of this fern species. The stipe is typically dark and sturdy, providing structural support to the spreading leaf structure. These morphological traits distinguish the plant from other similar ferns found in its natural habitat.
