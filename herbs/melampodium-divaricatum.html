Melampodium divaricatum

Butter daisy

Melampodium divaricatum, commonly known as the White Mopane, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, making it a vital resource in indigenous healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Melampodium divaricatum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Melampodium.
