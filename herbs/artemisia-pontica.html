Pontic wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia pontica, is a perennial aromatic herb native to the Mediterranean and Caspian regions. Renowned for its bitter essence and silver-green foliage, it has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries to treat digestive ailments, skin conditions, and various inflammatory disorders through its potent compounds.
