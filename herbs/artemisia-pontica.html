Artemisia pontica

Artemisia pontica, commonly known as Roman wormwood, is a perennial herb distinguished by its aromatic, silvery-green foliage. Native to the Mediterranean and Central Asia, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potent essential oils, frequently utilized for their antimicrobial, digestive, and anti-inflammatory properties in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia pontica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Artemisia pontica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is placed within the genus Artemisia.
