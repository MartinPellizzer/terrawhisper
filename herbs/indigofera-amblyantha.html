Indigofera amblyantha

What's the taxonomical classification of Indigofera amblyantha?

Indigofera amblyantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Fabales. This plant is a member of the family Fabaceae and specifically falls within the genus Indigofera, with its unique identification being the species amblyantha.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Indigofera amblyantha has pinnately compound leaves consisting of several pairs of small, oblong leaflets and small, papilionaceous flowers that typically appear in axillary racemes. The stems are woody at the base and often exhibit a spreading or ascending growth habit. Each leaflet features a smooth margin and a distinct midvein that provides structural support. The flowers are characterized by a standard petal, two wings, and a keel, which is typical of the Fabaceae family. Small, indehiscent pods develop from the flowers following successful pollination.
