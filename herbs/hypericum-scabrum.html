Hypericum scabrum

Hypericum scabrum, commonly known as scabrous St. John's wort, is a perennial herb native to Europe. Characterized by its rough, textured leaves and bright yellow blossoms, this species belongs to the Hypericaceae family. While less studied than its relatives, it holds significant botanical interest for its unique chemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum scabrum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Hypericum scabrum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Hypericaceae and the genus Hypericum.
