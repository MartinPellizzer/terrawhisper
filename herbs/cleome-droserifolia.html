Cleome droserifolia

Cleome droserifolia, commonly known as the spider flower or cleome, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its unique botanical features, it possesses diverse bioactive compounds. Research suggests its potential applications in treating various ailments, offering promising therapeutic benefits for holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cleome droserifolia
  • Family: Cleomaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Brassicales, falling into the family Cleomaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Cleome.

Distribution

This plant, Cleome droserifolia, exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the African continent. In the region of Northern Africa, its presence is documented in both Egypt and Libya. Moving eastward, the species is also found throughout Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of Djibouti and Eritrea. Additionally, its distribution extends to the island of Socotra.
