Rheum hotaoense

Rheum hotaoense, commonly known as Japanese rhubarb, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, its roots contain potent anthraquinones, offering significant laxative and digestive benefits. Beyond its therapeutic uses, the plant is valued for its antioxidant properties and role in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Rheum hotaoense
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
