Searsia pentheri

Searsia pentheri, commonly known as the Penther's Karoo-thorn, is a resilient shrub native to Southern Africa. Valued for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments. This hardy plant thrives in arid environments, offering significant ethnobotanical importance for its unique chemical compounds and healing potential.

  • Scientific name: Searsia pentheri
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is situated in the family Anacardiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by its genus, Searsia.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence can be noted specifically within Mozambique. Moving into Southern Africa, it is found extensively throughout the Cape Provinces. Its range also extends into the provinces of KwaZulu-Natal and the Free State. Additionally, the species is located within the borders of Eswatini.
