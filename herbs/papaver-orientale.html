Papaver orientale

Papaver orientale, commonly known as the Oriental Poppy, is a stunning perennial celebrated for its large, papery blooms in vibrant shades of red, pink, and orange. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism, though its potent alkaloids require careful handling and expert medicinal application.

  • Scientific name: Papaver orientale
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Papaver, specifically the species Papaver orientale.
