Artemisia glauca

Artemisia glauca, commonly known as blue sagebrush, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the arid regions of North America. Characterized by its silvery-blue foliage and aromatic scent, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized by indigenous cultures for its diverse medicinal properties and ecological importance.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it falls under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Artemisia.

Common Names

This plant, Artemisia glauca, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly referred to as blue sagebrush or grey sagebrush, reflecting its distinct foliage color. In Spanish, it is frequently called artemisa azul or ajenjo azul, while in French, it is known as armoise glauque. These various common names highlight the plant's botanical characteristics and its shared identity across different cultures.
