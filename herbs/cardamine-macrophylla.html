What's the taxonomical classification of Cardamine macrophylla?

Cardamine macrophylla belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Cardamine, and its specific species name, macrophylla.
