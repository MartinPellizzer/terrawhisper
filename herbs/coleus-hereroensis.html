Coleus hereroensis

Coleus heucherifolia, commonly known as Coleus, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its vibrant foliage and therapeutic potential. Traditionally used in various herbal remedies, it possesses antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This hardy perennial is valued in ethnobotany for its ability to support wellness through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Coleus hereroensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Coleus heheroensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, following the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae within the genus Coleus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range across several regions of Southern and South Tropical Africa. Within the South Tropical Africa zone, it can be found in both Angola and Zimbabwe. Its presence extends into Southern Africa, where it is documented in Botswana. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout the Caprivi Strip. Finally, its geographical reach includes the nation of Namibia.
