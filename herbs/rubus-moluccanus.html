Rubus moluccanus

Rubus moluccanus, commonly known as the Moluccan raspberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich berries, this plant is traditionally used to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential in managing oxidative stress and promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Rubus moluccanus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Rubus.
