Butterfly-pea, scientifically known as Clitoria ternatea (often referred to as Clioria mariaana), is a vibrant climbing legume celebrated for its striking deep-blue flowers. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this versatile plant offers potent antioxidant properties, supporting cognitive health, reducing inflammation, and serving as a natural, mesmerizing food coloring.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions in Asia. In China, it can be found primarily within the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the areas of Assam and the East Himalaya. Additionally, the species is documented in the Indo-China region, particularly in Cambodia. These diverse locations highlight the wide ecological range inhabited by Clitoria mariana.
