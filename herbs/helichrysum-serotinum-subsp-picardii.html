Helichrysum serotinum subsp. picardii

Helichrysum serotinum subsp. picardii, commonly known as a subspecies of everlasting flower, is a precious medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory capabilities, it is frequently utilized in traditional remedies to support skin health, promote healing, and enhance overall cellular vitality.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum serotinum subsp. picardii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Within this framework, its specific taxonomic designation is the genus Helichrysum, specifically the subspecies picardii.
