Hairy basil, scientifically known as Ocimum americanum, is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive fuzzy leaves and aromatic scent, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ocimum americanum, is a versatile medicinal herb characterized by its distinct morphological features. The stem is typically square in cross-section and can become woody at the base as the plant matures. Its most prominent feature is the leaf, which grows in opposite pairs along the branches; these lush green leaves are highly aromatic and contain the essential oils used for therapeutic purposes. During its blooming stage, the plant produces small, delicate structures known as the flower, which are arranged in terminal racemes. Finally, although less frequently utilized in traditional preparations than the foliage, the root system provides the necessary stability and nutrient absorption to support the plant's vigorous growth.
