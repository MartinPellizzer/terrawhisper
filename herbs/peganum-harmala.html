Peganum harmala

Peganum harmala, commonly known as Syrian Rue, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and Middle East. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, particularly harmine and harmaline, it has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries. Today, it is studied extensively for its psychoactive, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Peganum harmala
  • Family: Nitrariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is further categorized into the family Nitrariaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Peganum, specifically known as Peganum harmala.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several European regions. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing in Hungary. Its presence extends into Southwestern Europe, where it inhabits the Baleares islands. Additionally, the species is located within the territories of France and Sardegna. Finally, it is also widely distributed throughout Spain.
