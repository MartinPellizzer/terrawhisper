Stapelia gigantea

Stapelia gigantea, commonly known as the Carrion Flower, is a stunning succulent native to South Africa. Renowned for its massive, star-shaped blooms, it attracts pollinators with a unique, fleshy appearance. While its striking aesthetic is captivating, its distinctive scent mimics decaying organic matter to entice flies for pollination.

  • Scientific name: Stapelia gigantea
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Stapelia.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in both Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, the species is also found throughout South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of Malawi and Mozambique. Additionally, its geographical range extends into the country of Zambia.
