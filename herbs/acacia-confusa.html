Formosa acacia, scientifically known as Acacia confusa, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the subtropical regions of Taiwan. Renowned for its ability to thrive in diverse environments, this plant offers significant medicinal potential, particularly in traditional practices for its unique phytochemical properties and its role in supporting wellness.
