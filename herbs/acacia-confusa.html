Acacia confusa

Formosa acacia

Acacia confusosa, commonly known as a medicinal shrub, is a significant botanical species valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical properties, this plant offers therapeutic potential in treating various ailments. Exploring its biological characteristics and pharmacological benefits provides crucial insights into its role in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Acacia confusa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Acacia, specifically as the species Acacia confusa.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Acacia confusa, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Southern Min, it is called Siuⁿ-si-á, while in Chinese, it is known as 相思树 (zh-hans), 相思樹 (zh-hant), and 相思樹 (zh-hk). Additionally, in Vietnamese, the plant is called Keo tương tư.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across both the Western Indian Ocean and several regions of China. Within the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in the island nations of Mauritius and the Seychelles. Moving to East Asia, the species is established in the South-Central part of China. It also maintains a presence in the Southeast regions of the country. Finally, its range extends to the tropical island province of Hainan.
