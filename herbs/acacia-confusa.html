Formosa acacia, scientifically known as Acacia confusa, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the subtropical regions of Taiwan. Renowned for its ability to thrive in diverse environments, this plant offers significant medicinal potential, particularly in traditional practices for its unique phytochemical properties and its role in supporting wellness.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across both the Western Indian Ocean and several regions of China. Within the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in the island nations of Mauritius and the Seychelles. Moving to East Asia, the species is established in the South-Central part of China. It also maintains a presence in the Southeast regions of the country. Finally, its range extends to the tropical island province of Hainan.
