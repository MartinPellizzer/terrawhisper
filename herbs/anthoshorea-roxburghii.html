Anthoshorea roxburghii

Anthoshorea roxburgii, commonly known as the Roxburghia tree, is a significant medicinal plant found in Southeast Asian tropical forests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights.

  • Family: Dipterocarpaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
