White Meranti, scientifically known as Anthoshorea roxburgii, is a significant timber tree native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Beyond its industrial value, this species holds traditional importance in ethnomedicine. Various parts of the tree are utilized in folk remedies to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its profound ecological and medicinal utility.
