Anchusa azurea var. azurea

Anchusa azurea var. azurea, commonly known as Bugloss, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its intense, sky-blue blossoms. Belonging to the Boraginaceae family, this resilient plant thrives in sunny meadows. Traditionally valued for its aesthetic charm, it also holds historical significance in herbalism and supporting local pollinators.

  • Scientific name: Anchusa azurea var. azurea
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Boraginales and falls under the family Boraginaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Anchusa and is classified as the variety azurea.
