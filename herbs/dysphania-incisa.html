Dysphania incisa

Dysphania inciosa, commonly known as the "jagged dysphania," is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This plant is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions across diverse cultures.

  • Scientific name: Dysphania incisa
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Amaranthaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Dysphania.
