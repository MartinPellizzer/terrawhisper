Kudzu, scientifically known as Pueraria, is a versatile medicinal climbing vine celebrated in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its bioactive isoflavones, it is frequently utilized to support cardiovascular health, alleviate alcohol withdrawal symptoms, and manage menopausal discomfort. This potent botanical offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Italy as well as throughout the Northwest Balkan Peninsula. Its presence extends into Eastern Europe, specifically within the territories of Crimea and Ukraine. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Kudzu, possesses a complex underground structure that is essential to its medicinal properties. It develops thick, starchy tuberous roots that function as a storage organ for nutrients. These specialized structures often take on the appearance of a large tuber, which is harvested for its therapeutic benefits. The entire underground network is comprised of a robust system of roots, including a primary root that anchors the plant firmly into the soil. Above the ground, the climbing stems provide the necessary support for the vines to spread across the landscape.
