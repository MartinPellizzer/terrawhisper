Pueraria

Pueraria, commonly known as Kudzu, is a versatile climbing legume celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is widely used to manage various ailments, including menopause symptoms, alcohol cravings, and inflammation. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for supporting holistic wellness and metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Pueraria
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is identified by the genus Pueraria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across several distinct regions. Its presence extends into Eastern Europe, specifically within the territories of Crimea and Ukraine.
