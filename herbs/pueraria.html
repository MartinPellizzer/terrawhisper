Kudzu, scientifically known as Pueraria, is a versatile medicinal climbing vine celebrated in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its bioactive isoflavones, it is frequently utilized to support cardiovascular health, alleviate alcohol withdrawal symptoms, and manage menopausal discomfort. This potent botanical offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Italy as well as throughout the Northwest Balkan Peninsula. Its presence extends into Eastern Europe, specifically within the territories of Crimea and Ukraine. Additionally, the species reaches the Atlantic islands of Macaronesia, notably inhabiting the Azores. Together, these locations highlight the wide distribution of the plant across various European landscapes.
