Hymenocallis latifolia var. latifolia

Hymenocallis latifolia var. latifolia, commonly known as the wide-leafed spider lily, is a captivating perennial valued for its striking white blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Traditional practices often utilize its unique chemical properties, exploring its potential medicinal applications within diverse regional pharmacopeias.

  • Scientific name: Hymenocallis latifolia var. latifolia
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Hymenocallis as the variety latifolia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found in the subtropical and tropical regions of the Caribbean and the southeastern United States. Within the Caribbean, its range includes the Bahamas, the Cayman Islands, Cuba, and the Dominican Republic. It also maintains a presence in the southeastern United States, specifically in the state of Florida. These various locations highlight its preference for warm, coastal, or island environments. Overall, its distribution is concentrated within a specific maritime corridor in the Atlantic and Caribbean basins.
