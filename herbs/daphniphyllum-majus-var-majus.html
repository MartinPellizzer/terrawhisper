Daphniphyllum majus var. majus

Daphniphyllum majus var. majus, commonly known as Maju, is a significant medicinal plant native to East Asian forests. Valued in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat inflammatory conditions and various ailments. This versatile shrub serves as a vital resource for pharmacological research and holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Daphniphyllum majus var. majus
  • Family: Daphniphyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Saxifragales and is a member of the family Daphniphyllaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Daphniphyllum as the specific variety Daphniphyllum maju s var. maju s.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions of East and Southeast Asia. In China, it is found within the South-Central provinces. Its range extends into the Indo-China peninsula, where it inhabits parts of Myanmar. Additionally, the species is documented as growing in Thailand. These specific locations define the natural geographical footprint of Daphniphyllum majus var. majus.
