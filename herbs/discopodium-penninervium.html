Discopodium penninervium

Discopodium penninervium, commonly known as the penninerved discopodium, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its distinct leaf venation, this species contains bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. Understanding its pharmacological properties is essential for exploring its potential in modern therapeutic developments.

  • Scientific name: Discopodium penninervium
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales, falling into the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Discopodium, specifically the species Discopodium penninervium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Benin and Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found in Burundi and Cameroon. Additionally, its distribution extends to the various islands located in the Gulf of Guinea. Together, these locations highlight the plant's occurrence across both mainland and insular environments.
