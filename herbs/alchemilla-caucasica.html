Alchemilla caucasica

Alchemilla caucasica, commonly known as Caucasian Lady's Mantle, is a perennial herb native to mountainous regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it contains tannins and flavonoids known for astringent and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to support hormonal balance and skin health, this resilient plant remains a significant botanical treasure.

  • Scientific name: Alchemilla caucasica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its classification through the subclass Magnoliidae and order Rosales, it is a member of the Rosaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Alchemilla.
