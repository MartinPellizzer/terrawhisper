Zebra plant, scientifically known as Tradescantia zebrina, is a striking ornamental species celebrated for its shimmering, striped foliage. Featuring deep green and silver hues with purple undersides, it thrives in indirect light. Beyond its beauty, it is studied for potential medicinal properties, including antimicrobial and antioxidant bioactive compounds.
