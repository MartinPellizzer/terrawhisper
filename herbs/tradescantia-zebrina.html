Tradescantia zebrina

Tradescantia zebrina, commonly known as Inch Plant or Wandering Dude, is a striking ornamental plant celebrated for its iridescent, purple-and-silver foliage. While primarily grown for its aesthetic beauty in indoor gardens, it holds traditional significance in various cultures, where its resilient nature and vibrant colors symbolize vitality and growth.

  • Scientific name: Tradescantia zebrina
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions within Northern Africa. Specifically, its presence is documented in the countries of Algeria and Morocco. Beyond the mainland, it can be found throughout the Macaronesian islands. This includes the Canary Islands, Madeira, and Cape Verde. Consequently, its natural range spans several distinct island and continental territories.
