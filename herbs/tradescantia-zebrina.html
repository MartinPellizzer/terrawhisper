Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions within Northern Africa. Specifically, its presence is documented in the countries of Algeria and Morocco. Beyond the mainland, it can be found throughout the Macaronesian islands. This includes the Canary Islands, Madeira, and Cape Verde. Consequently, its natural range spans several distinct island and continental territories.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Inch Plant or Wandering Dude, is primarily valued for its vibrant, multicolored foliage, and its medicinal potential is often explored through the use of leaf extracts. These extracts contain various bioactive compounds, such as flavonoids and phenolics, which are concentrated within the succulent tissues of the leaves. Researchers study these leaf extracts to investigate their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, aiming to harness the chemical complexity held within the plant's decorative greenery for therapeutic applications.
