Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the southeastern regions of Europe. Within this area, it can be found growing in Greece and on the island of Kriti. Its range also extends into the European portion of Türkiye. Moving into Western Asia, the species is present throughout the East Aegean Islands. Finally, the plant's distribution encompasses the broader territory of Türkiye.
