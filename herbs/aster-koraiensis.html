Aster koraiensis

Aster koraiensis, commonly known as the Korai aster, is a resilient perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Turkey. Valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Its unique phytochemical profile makes it a subject of interest for developing natural healing treatments.

  • Scientific name: Aster koraiensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aster koraiensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. It is further categorized into the family Asteraceae and the genus Aster.
