Black turmeric, scientifically known as Curcuma caesia, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Distinguished by its striking blue stems, this perennial herb offers profound therapeutic benefits. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is widely utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties in holistic healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Curcuma caesia or Black Turmeric, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties which are concentrated within its underground structures. The most significant plant parts utilized in traditional medicine are the rhizoomes, which possess a distinct dark or bluish-black color compared to common turmeric. Whether used in the form of a whole rhizome or processed into a powder from the dried rhizomes, these parts contain unique bioactive compounds that are essential for their therapeutic applications.
