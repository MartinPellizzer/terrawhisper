Curcuma caesia

Curcuma caesia, commonly known as Black Turmeric, is a unique medicinal plant prized for its striking blue rhizomes. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. This rare species is widely utilized to support digestive health, enhance immunity, and treat various skin and respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Curcuma caesia
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is a member of the family Zingiberaceae. It is specifically classified under the genus Curcuma.
