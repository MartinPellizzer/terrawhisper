Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its distribution includes the country of Benin. It can also be located throughout Burkina Faso. Additionally, the species is present in the Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its range extends to include Guinea-Bissau.
