Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Its presence extends to the coastal and inland areas of Ghana as well. Furthermore, the species is documented to inhabit the territories of Guinea-Bissau and Ivory Coast. Finally, its geographical range includes the nation of Liberia.
