Ansellia africana

Ansellia africana, commonly known as the African Sandbush, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to the arid landscapes of Southern Africa. Valued in traditional healing, its various parts are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, offering a vital natural resource for local communities.

  • Scientific name: Ansellia africana
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Orchidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ansellia, specifically as the species Ansellia africana.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Its presence extends to the coastal and inland areas of Ghana as well. Furthermore, the species is documented to inhabit the territories of Guinea-Bissau and Ivory Coast. Finally, its geographical range includes the nation of Liberia.
