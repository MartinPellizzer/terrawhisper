Michelia yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant is placed in the order Magnoliales and the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Michelia and the specific species yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?
