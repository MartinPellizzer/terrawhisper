Ziziphus mucronata

Buffalo thorn

Ziziphus mucronata, commonly known as the Buffalo Thorn, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to southern Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are used in traditional healing to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, offering significant ethnobotanical value in local communities.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphus mucronata
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Ziziphus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ziziphus mucronata, is referred to by various common names across different languages and regions. In Hausa, it is called Magaryar kura, while in Kinyarwanda, it is known as Umuganzacyaro (Ziziphus mucronata). The Afrikaans name for this species is Blinkblaar-wag-'n-bietjie, and in Persian, it is identified as خار گاومیش. Additionally, in Chinese, the plant is called 凸棗.
