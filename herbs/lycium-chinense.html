Chinese box-thorn

Lycium chinense · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (732 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese box-thorn, scientifically known as Lycium chinense, is a versatile medicinal shrub celebrated in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant produces nutrient-rich berries used to support immune function, enhance vitality, and promote longevity. It remains a cornerstone in holistic wellness and natural health therapies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented in Great Britain, Ireland, and Sweden. Moving toward the central part of the continent, it can also be found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, the species inhabits the territories of Germany as well as the Czechia-Slovakia region. Consequently, these locations map out a significant geographical range for the species across both northern and central latitudes.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as wolfberry or goji, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties. The most widely recognized part is the fruit, specifically the vibrant red fruits that are harvested for their high antioxidant content. In addition to the fruits, the leaves are sometimes used in traditional preparations, while the shoot tip is collected for specific therapeutic applications. Overall, every part of the fruit and the vegetative structures play a role in the plant's diverse pharmacological profile.
