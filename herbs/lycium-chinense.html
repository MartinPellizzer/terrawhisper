Chinese box-thorn

Lycium chinense · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (732 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese box-thorn, scientifically known as Lycium chinense, is a versatile medicinal shrub celebrated in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant produces nutrient-rich berries used to support immune function, enhance vitality, and promote longevity. It remains a cornerstone in holistic wellness and natural health therapies.

The reproduction of Lycium chinense is characterized by a bisexual sexual reproduction system, ensuring that individual flowers possess both male and female reproductive organs. The flowering period is highly flexible, with flowering start dates occurring as early as December and spanning through various months including January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, and November, indicating a variable seasonal cycle.
