Lycium chinense

Lycium chinense, commonly known as Chinese Goji berry, is a versatile medicinal shrub celebrated in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, these vibrant red berries support immune function, enhance vision, and promote longevity. This nutrient-dense superfood serves as a vital tonic for holistic health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Lycium chinense
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lycium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lycium chinense, is recognized by various names across different languages and cultures. In Poland, it is called Kolcowój chiński, while in Sweden, it is referred to as Bredbladigt bocktörne. In the Netherlands, the species is known as Chinese boksdoorn, and in Slovakia, it is identified as Kustovnica čínska. Additionally, in Persian, the plant is called لیسیوم چینی.
