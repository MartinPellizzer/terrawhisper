Dalbergia hainanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Dalbergia hainanensis?

Dalbergia hainanenensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated in the order Fabales. It is a member of the family Fabaceae and is further categorized under the genus Dalbergia, eventually reaching its specific designation as the species hainanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dalbergia hainanenensis has pinnately compound leaves consisting of several pairs of leaflets that are typically elliptic or oblong in shape. The leaflets feature entire margins and a smooth, slightly leathery texture on their surfaces. Its stems and branches exhibit a woody structure with a bark that may appear somewhat rough or fissured depending on the age of the specimen. The plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that are characteristic of the Fabaceae family. These flowers are arranged in clusters and eventually give rise to small, legume-style pods containing the seeds.

According to a study published by "Zhongguo Zhong yao za zhi = Zhongguo zhongyao zazhi = China journal of Chinese materia medica", the leaves of Dalbergia hainanensis were studied to identify its chemical constituents. Using chromatographic techniques on silica gel and polyamide column, researchers isolated 13 compounds from the plant for the first time. These identified compounds include 8-C-glucosylprunetin (1), 8-C-glucosylgenistein (2), 2-hydroxy-5-methoxy biochanin A (3), formononetin (4), and 3,5-dimethoxy-4-hydroxybenzaldehyde (5).
