Trefoil, scientifically known as Lotus, is a revered aquatic plant celebrated for its profound medicinal and spiritual significance. Renowned for its ability to thrive in murky waters, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and calming properties. Often used in traditional healing, the lotus symbolizes purity, resilience, and holistic wellness.
