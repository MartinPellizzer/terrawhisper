Plant Parts

This plant, Astragalus cicer, is a member of the legume family, which is characterized by its unique symbiotic relationship with nitrogen-fixing bacteria. A defining anatomical feature of this species is the presence of root nodules located along its root system. These specialized structures house bacteria that convert atmospheric nitrogen into a form the plant can utilize, playing a crucial role in its growth and survival in various environments.
