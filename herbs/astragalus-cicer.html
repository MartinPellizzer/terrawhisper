Astragalus cicer

Astragalus cicer, commonly known as the chickpea milkvetch, is a perennial herb native to parts of Asia and Europe. Valued in traditional medicine for its restorative properties, this plant contains bioactive compounds believed to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus cicer
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
