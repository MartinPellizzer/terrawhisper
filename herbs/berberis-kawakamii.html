Berberis kawakamii

What's the taxonomical classification of Berberis kawakamii?

Berberis kawakamii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Ranunculales. This plant is a member of the family Berberidaceae, falling under the genus Berberis, and is specifically identified by the species name kawakamii.
