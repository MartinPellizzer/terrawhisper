What's the taxonomical classification of Draba oreades?

Draba oreades belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Brassicales. This species is further categorized under the family Brassicaceae, falling into the genus Draba, and is specifically identified by the species name oreades.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Draba oreades has a low-growing, rosette-forming habit characterized by small, fleshy, spatulate leaves that are densely covered in fine, white hairs. These leaves are typically arranged in a tight basal cluster to withstand harsh alpine environments. The plant produces small, yellowish to white flowers that emerge on short, slender stalks from the center of the rosette. Each flower features four delicate petals and is part of a compact raceme. The siliques, or seed pods, are elongated and develop along the flowering stem after pollination. This compact structure helps the species conserve moisture and heat in its high-altitude habitat.
