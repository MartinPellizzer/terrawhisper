Coix lacryma-jobi var. ma-yuen

Coix lacryma-jobi var. ma-yuen, commonly known as Job's Tears, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties, this grain is frequently used to treat edema, skin conditions, and digestive issues. It serves as a nutritious staple and a powerful therapeutic agent.

  • Scientific name: Coix lacryma-jobi var. ma-yuen
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, falling into the family Poaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Coix as the variety Coix lacrymata-jobi var. ma-yuen.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions of East Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast provinces. Its range also extends into the North-Central areas and the Manchuria region of the country. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Korea. Together, these locations define its significant presence across the Eastern Asian landscape.
