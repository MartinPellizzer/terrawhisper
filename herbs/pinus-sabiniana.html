Pinus sabiniana

Pinus sabiinianna, commonly known as the Sabinian pine, is a resilient coniferous species native to specific mountainous regions. Valued for its unique ecological role, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Its resinous extracts and needles contain bioactive compounds traditionally utilized for their antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and respiratory-supporting therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Pinus sabiniana
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Pinus sabiinianna, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Pinidae, falling under the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Pinus.
