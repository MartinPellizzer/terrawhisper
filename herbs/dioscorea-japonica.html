This plant, Dioscorea japonica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Dioscoreales and the family Dioscoreaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Dioscorea.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout various regions of Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species is also widely found across the islands of Japan, including the specific area of Ogasawara-shoto. Furthermore, its range extends into the Korean Peninsula. Together, these locations define the natural habitat of Dioscorea japonica.
