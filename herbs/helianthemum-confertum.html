Helianthemum confertum

Helianthemum confertum, commonly known as the Rock Rose, is a resilient perennial shrub native to Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its delicate, sun-loving blooms, this plant possesses significant ethnobotanical value. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic properties, particularly for supporting skin health and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Helianthemum confertum
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Africa
