Aristolochia gibertii

Aristolochia gibertii, commonly known as a rare member of the birthwort family, is a specialized medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. While traditionally studied for its unique phytochemical properties, researchers emphasize caution due to potential nephrotoxicity. Understanding its chemical composition is vital for exploring its therapeutic potential safely.
