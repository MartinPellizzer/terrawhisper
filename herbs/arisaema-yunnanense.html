Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it is commonly found in the South-Central and Southeast regions. Its range extends into the Indo-China peninsula, where it inhabits several different countries. Specifically, it can be located in the mountainous or forested areas of Myanmar and Thailand. Additionally, the species is documented to grow naturally within the borders of Vietnam.

Plant Parts

This plant, Arisaema yunnanense, possesses a specialized morphology where the stem is modified into a fleshy, underground structure known as a corm. This thickened organ serves as a vital storage reservoir for nutrients and water, allowing the plant to survive dormant periods. From this central base, the aerial portion of the plant emerges, consisting of a short, erect scape that supports the inflorescence and the broad, spreading leaves characteristic of the species.
