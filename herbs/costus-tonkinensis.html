What's the taxonomical classification of Costus tonkinensis?

Costus tonkinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical structure, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Zingiberales and the family Costaceae. Ultimately, this plant is identified by the genus Costus and is specifically designated as the species tonkinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Costus tonkinensis has spiral, lanceolate leaves that emerge from thick, fleshy rhizomes and produce showy, terminal inflorescences with bright yellow bracts and tubular flowers. The plant features a distinctive phyllotaxy where the leaves rotate around the stem in a corkscrew pattern. These leaves are typically arranged in a dense, overlapping fashion to maximize light interception. The stems are succulent and robust, providing structural support for the heavy floral displays.
