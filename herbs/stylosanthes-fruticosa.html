Stylosanthes fruticosa

Stylosanthes fruticosa, commonly known as the "Bushy Desmodium," is a resilient perennial shrub native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments, promote wound healing, and support overall digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Stylosanthes fruticosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. It is specifically classified under the genus Stylosanthes.
