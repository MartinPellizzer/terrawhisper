Terminalia januarensis

Terminalia januarensis, commonly known as the Janwar Terminalia, is a significant medicinal tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant—including bark and leaves—are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as skin infections, inflammation, and digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia januarensis
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtales, and the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Terminalia as the species Terminalia januarensis.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the South American continent. It is primarily found inhabiting certain regions of Brazil.
