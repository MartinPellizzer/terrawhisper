Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtales, and the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Terminalia as the species Terminalia januarensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the South American continent. It is primarily found inhabiting certain regions of Brazil. Specifically, its presence is noted throughout the Northeast part of the country. Additionally, the species can be located within the Southeast region of Brazil as well. These two distinct areas represent the known natural range for this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

This plant, Terminalia januarensis, possesses various components that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The stem bark and the leaves are frequently harvested for their bioactive compounds, which contribute to the plant's therapeutic profile. Furthermore, the roots and the root bark serve as significant sources of traditional remedies, often being processed to extract essential healing elements. Whether using the entire root or specifically the root bark, each part plays a vital role in the ethnobotanical application of this species.
