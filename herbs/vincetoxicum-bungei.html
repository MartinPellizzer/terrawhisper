Vincetoxicum bungei

Vincetoxicum bungei, commonly known as the Bungé's vincetoxicum, is a climbing perennial herb native to various regions of Africa. Renowned for its delicate, tubular flowers, this medicinal plant is studied for its diverse phytochemical properties, offering significant potential in traditional healing practices and modern pharmacological research for therapeutic applications.
