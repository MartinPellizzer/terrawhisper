Gongronema napalense

Gongronema nepalense

Gongronema napalense, commonly known as Utazi in Nigeria, is a versatile medicinal plant highly valued in West African traditional medicine. Renowned for its bitter flavor, it possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-diabetic, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory effects. This nutrient-rich shrub serves as both a culinary staple and a potent herbal remedy.
