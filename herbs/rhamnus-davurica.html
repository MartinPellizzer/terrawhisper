Rhamnus davurica

Rhamnus davurica, commonly known as the Davurica Buckthorn, is a deciduous shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, its bark contains bioactive anthraquinones, which serve as potent natural laxatives. This resilient plant is studied for its digestive benefits and its potential role in holistic therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Rhamnus davurica
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Rhamnus.

This plant is widely distributed across specific regions of Northern Asia. Within Siberia, its presence is noted in both Buryatiya and Chita.
