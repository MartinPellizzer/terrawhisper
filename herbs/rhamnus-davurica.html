Dahurian buckthorn, scientifically known as Rhamnus davurica, is a deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, this resilient plant remains a vital subject of ethnobotanical research.
