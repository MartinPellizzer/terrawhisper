Delphinium potaninii var. potaninii

Delphinium potaninii var. potaninii, commonly known as a rare Himalayan perennial, is a distinguished member of the Ranunculaceae family. Renowned for its striking blue floral displays, this botanical treasure holds significant ethnobotanical interest. It is studied for its unique phytochemical properties and potential therapeutic applications in traditional medicine.

  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
