Scientific literature: Very Extensive (344 studies) · ★★★★★

Red mangrove, scientifically known as Rhizophora mangle, is a cornerstone of coastal ecosystems. Renowned for its intricate prop roots, this resilient species offers significant medicinal potential. Traditionally, its bark and leaves are utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions and gastrointestinal issues.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the red mangrove, utilizes its various components for both survival and medicinal potential. The leaves are thick, leathery, and evergreen, designed to withstand high salinity and intense sunlight while facilitating specialized salt excretion. Additionally, the bark of Rhizophora mangle is known to contain various tannins and bioactive compounds, which have been traditionally utilized in various cultures for their astringent and antimicrobial properties.
