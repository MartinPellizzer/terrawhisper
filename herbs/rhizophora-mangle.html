Rhizophora mangle

Rhizophora mangle, commonly known as the red mangrove, is a vital coastal species found in tropical intertidal zones. Renowned for its distinctive arching prop roots, it provides essential ecosystem services, including shoreline stabilization, nursery habitats for marine life, and protection against storm surges, playing a crucial role in biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Rhizophora mangle
  • Family: Rhizophoraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Rhizophora mangle, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rhizophoraceae and is situated within the genus Rhizophora.
