What's the taxonomical classification of Angelica likiangensis?

Angelica likiangensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this classification, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae, which is characteristic of many flowering plants with umbel-like inflorescences. Ultimately, this specific plant is defined by its membership in the genus Angelica, with its unique identity established at the species level as likiangensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Angelica likiangensis has an erect, perennial herbaceous habit characterized by hollow, ribbed stems that reach heights of up to 1.5 meters. Its inflorescence consists of broad, flat-topped umbels composed of numerous small, white flowers. The individual flowers possess five petals and are supported by long, slender pedicels radiating from a central point. This plant typically develops a robust taproot system that helps anchor its tall structure in its natural habitat.
