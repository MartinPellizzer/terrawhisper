Argan, scientifically known as Sideroxylon spinosum, is a resilient, slow-growing evergreen endemic to Morocco's semi-arid forests. Renowned for its nutrient-rich kernels, this remarkable plant produces precious oil used globally in cosmetics and nutrition. Beyond its economic value, it plays a vital role in combating desertification and supporting biodiversity.
