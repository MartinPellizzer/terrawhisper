What's the taxonomical classification of Idesia polycarpa?

Idesia polycarpa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further organized under the order Malpighiales. It is a constituent of the family Salicaceae, falling specifically under the genus Idesia, with its unique biological identity defined by its species designation, polycarpa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Idesia polycarpa has a woody shrub or small tree structure characterized by elliptical to lanceolate leaves that are typically glossy and arranged in an opposite or sub-opposite pattern. The leaf margins are often entire or slightly serrated, while the venation remains distinct across the blade surface. Its flowering structures consist of small, inconspicuous blooms that emerge from the axils of the leaves. Following fertilization, the plant produces small, fleshy drupes that serve as its primary fruit type. These morphological features are characteristic of many species within the Rutaceae family.

According to a study published by "Food & function", the leaves of Idesia polycarpa Maxim. are an excellent source of hydroxycinnamic acid derivatives with various biological activities. These leaves contain four hydroxycinnamic acid derivative isomers, specifically compounds 1, 2, 3, and 4. In HepG2 cells, treatment with these compounds showed that hepatocyte-free fatty acid accumulation decreased by 65.00%, 10.69%, and 47.00% following treatment with compounds 2, 3, and 4, respectively. Compound 1 presented no lipid-lowering activity. Furthermore, the bioactivities of compounds 2 and 4 were enhanced by 58.42% and 25.33% using the sun-drying method compared to freeze-drying.
