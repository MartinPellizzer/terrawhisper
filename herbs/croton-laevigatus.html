Croton laevigatus

Croton laevigatus, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, holds profound importance in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from skin inflammations to gastrointestinal issues. Exploring its pharmacological potential offers promising insights into modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Croton laevigatus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Croton, specifically as the species Croton laevigatus.

Common Names

This plant is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is referred to as 光叶巴豆 (zh), while in Arabic-speaking regions, it is called كروتون نايم (ar) and similarly identified in Egyptian Arabic as كروتون نايم (arz). Furthermore, in French, the plant is associated with the names Croton roxburghii (fr) and Croton boragatch (fr).

Distribution

This plant has a very specific and limited natural range. Its geographical distribution is primarily concentrated within the nation of China. More specifically, it is found natively on the island of Hainan. Because it is restricted to this single location, it is considered an endemic species to the region. Consequently, its survival is closely tied to the unique tropical environment of this particular island.
