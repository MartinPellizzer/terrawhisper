European violet willow, scientifically known as Salix daphnoides, is a distinctive deciduous shrub native to European wetlands. Characterized by its striking purplish-tinted leaves and slender stems, this species thrives in damp habitats. Historically, members of the Salix genus have been valued for their natural salicylic acid content in traditional medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Salix daphnoides, is a species of willow that holds significance in traditional herbalism, primarily through the use of its medicinal properties found within the bark. The bark contains salicin, a precursor to salicylic acid, which has historically been utilized for its analgesic and anti-inflammatory effects. When harvested carefully, this part of the plant serves as a natural source for treating various ailments related to pain and fever.
