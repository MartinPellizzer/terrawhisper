Black afara, scientifically known as Terminalia ivorensis, is a significant medicinal tree native to West African rainforests. Renowned for its versatile therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights.
