Black afara

Terminalia ivorensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (230 studies) · ★★★★★

Black afara, scientifically known as Terminalia ivorensis, is a significant medicinal tree native to West African rainforests. Renowned for its versatile therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Within this broad region, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its presence is also documented throughout Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the nation of Gambia. Finally, its geographical range includes the territories of both Ghana and Guinea.

Plant Parts

This plant, Terminalia ivorenii, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, particularly through its extensive use of the trunk barks and stem-bark. The bark of the tree is highly valued in traditional medicine, where different sections of the barks are harvested to prepare decoctions or extracts. Additionally, the leaves serve as a vital part of the plant's therapeutic profile, with each individual leaf containing specific bioactive compounds used in local treatments.
