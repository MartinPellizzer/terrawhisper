Mesosphaerum pectinatum

Mesosphaerum pectinatum, commonly known as the pectin-bearing shrub, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its unique chemical composition. Rich in bioactive polysaccharides, it plays a vital role in traditional healing practices. This study explores its pharmacological potential, focusing on its therapeutic applications in supporting digestive and immune health.
