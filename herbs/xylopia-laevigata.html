Xylopia laevigata

Xylopia laevigata, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Belonging to the Annonaceae family, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and inflammatory conditions, thanks to its potent bioactive compounds and aromatic properties.

  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
