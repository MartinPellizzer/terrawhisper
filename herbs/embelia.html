Scientifically known as Embelia, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Primulaceae family. Widely recognized in Ayurvedic traditions, it is primarily valued for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often harvested for its fruit, myrobalan, it offers significant therapeutic potential in treating digestive and neurological health issues.
