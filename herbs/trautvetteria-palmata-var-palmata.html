Trautvetteria palmata var. palmata

Actaea racemosa

Trautvetteria palmata var. palmata, commonly known as the palmate moss, is a distinctive bryophyte valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its fan-shaped leaves, this resilient species thrives in moist, acidic environments. It is often utilized in folk medicine for its potential antimicrobial properties and soothing applications in topical treatments.

  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant is native to several distinct regions within the Russian Far East. Its presence is well-documented across the Primorye territory and the Magadan region. Additionally, it can be found growing in the Khabarovsk area. The species also extends its range to the island territories of Sakhalin and the Kuril Islands. Together, these locations define the specific geographical distribution of this variety.
