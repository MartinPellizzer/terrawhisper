Plant Parts

This plant, Antiaris toxicaria, is highly valued in traditional practices due to the potent chemical compounds found within its specific components. The most critical part of the plant is the milky latex, which is extracted from the bark and stems to create a powerful arrow poison used by various cultures for hunting. Additionally, the seeds of the plant also contain significant concentrations of toxic alkaloids, making them a central element in the plant's pharmacological profile.
