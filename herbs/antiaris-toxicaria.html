Upas tree, scientifically known as Antiaris toxicaria, is a potent tropical plant renowned for its highly toxic latex. Historically used to create lethal arrow poisons, its chemical compounds pose significant dangers. This study explores its botanical characteristics, toxicological properties, and the complex role it plays in ethnobotanical history.
