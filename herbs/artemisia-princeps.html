Artemisia princeps

Artemisia princeps, commonly known as Japanese mugwort, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its aromatic, silvery foliage, this versatile plant holds significant medicinal value. It is traditionally used to treat digestive issues, inflammation, and respiratory ailments, serving as a cornerstone in various herbal healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia princeps
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artemisia, specifically as the species Artemisia princeps.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across both Europe and Asia. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing in regions such as Belgium, Germany, and the Netherlands. Its range extends significantly eastward into the vast territories of China. Specifically, it is documented within the China South-Central region. Additionally, the species is also known to inhabit the northern landscapes of Inner Mongolia.
