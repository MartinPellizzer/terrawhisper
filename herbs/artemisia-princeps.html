Korean mugwort, scientifically known as Artemisia princeps, is a versatile perennial herb deeply rooted in East Asian traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Widely used in culinary dishes and therapeutic poultices, this resilient plant remains a cornerstone of natural healing.
