Korean mugwort

Artemisia princeps · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (31 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Korean mugwort, scientifically known as Artemisia princeps, is a versatile perennial herb deeply rooted in East Asian traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Widely used in culinary dishes and therapeutic poultices, this resilient plant remains a cornerstone of natural healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Japanese mugwort, is primarily valued for its aromatic and medicinal properties which are concentrated within its leaves. These leaves are characterized by a silvery-green hue and a fine, velvety texture due to the presence of minute hairs on their surface. When crushed or dried, the leaves release a potent, camphoraceous scent, signaling the presence of essential oils that are frequently utilized in traditional herbal remedies and topical applications.
