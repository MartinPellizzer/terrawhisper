Syzygium siamense

Syzygium siamense, commonly known as the Siamese Rose Myrtle, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic leaves and antioxidant properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This botanical treasure offers significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium siamense
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Syzygium, specifically as the species Syzygium siamense.
