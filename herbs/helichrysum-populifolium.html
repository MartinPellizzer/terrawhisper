Helichrysum populifolium

Helichrysum populifolium, commonly known as the Poplar-leaf Everlasting, is a remarkable medicinal shrub native to diverse landscapes. Renowned for its vibrant, golden blooms, this plant offers potent therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized in traditional remedies to support skin health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum populifolium
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Specifically, it is identified by its genus, Helichrysum.

Common Names

This plant, Helichrysum populifolium, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as Everlasting or Paper daisy. In Spanish, it is known as Siempre viva, and in Portuguese, it is called Sempre-viva.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern primarily concentrated within the southern regions of the African continent. It is found extensively throughout the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. Additionally, its natural habitat extends further north into the KwaZulu-Natal province. These two distinct regions define the core geographical range where the species thrives. Consequently, its presence is closely tied to the diverse ecosystems of these specific Southern African territories.
