What's the taxonomical classification of Arthromeris wallichiana?

Arthromeris wallichiaana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its hierarchical lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. It further falls under the order Polypodiales and is a member of the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Arthromeris and the species wallichiaana.
