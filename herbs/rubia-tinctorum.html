Rubia tinctorum

Rubia tinctoria, commonly known as madder, is a perennial flowering plant celebrated for its potent roots. Historically prized as a source of vibrant red dye, it also holds significant medicinal value. In traditional practices, its bioactive compounds are utilized for their anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties to support various health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Rubia tinctorum
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Rubia.
