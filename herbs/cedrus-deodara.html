Himalayan cedar, scientifically known as Cedrus deoar, is a majestic evergreen conifer native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Himalayan Cedar or Deodar, offers various valuable components used in traditional and industrial applications. The bark is frequently utilized in medicinal preparations due to its astringent properties, often being processed for its therapeutic potential. From the tree itself, the wood oil is extracted for its distinct aromatic scent and is often employed in perfumery or topical treatments. Furthermore, the high-quality timber is highly prized not only for its durability and resistance to decay in construction but also for its historical significance in crafting essential tools and structures.
