Himalayan cedar, scientifically known as Cedrus deoar, is a majestic evergreen conifer native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its aromatic, rot-resistant wood, it holds profound significance in traditional medicine, offering therapeutic properties used to treat respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and various inflammatory disorders.
