Cedrus deodara

Himalayan cedar

Cedrus deoarara, commonly known as the Himalayan Cedar or Deodar, is a majestic coniferous evergreen native to the Himalayas. Renowned for its aromatic, resinous wood and impressive stature, this sacred tree holds significant medicinal value, offering potent anti-inflammatory, antiseptic, and sedative properties used in traditional healing practices worldwide.

  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe
