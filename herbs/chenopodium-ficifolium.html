Chenopodium ficifolium

Chenopodium ficifolium, commonly known as red goosefoot, is a resilient annual herb within the Amaranthaceae family. Distinguished by its striking reddish foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is valued in traditional medicine for its nutritional density and potential therapeutic properties, often utilized in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Chenopodium ficifolium
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Caryophyllales and placed in the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Chenopodium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Chenopodium ficifolium, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Estonia, it is called viigilehine hanemalts, while in Germany, it is known as Feigenblättriger Gänsefuß (or Feigenblättriger Gänsefuss in Switzerland). The Korean name for this species is 좀명아주, and in the Upper Sorbian language, it is identified as Figowcojta pólšica.
