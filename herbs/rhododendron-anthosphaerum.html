Rhododendron anthosphaerum

What's the taxonomical classification of Rhododendron anthosphaerum?

Rhododendron anthosphaerum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Ericaceae, falling within the genus Rhododendron, and is specifically identified by its species name, anthosphaerum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rhododendron anthosphaerum has evergreen, lanceolate leaves that are typically 5 to 15 centimeters long with a leathery texture and a dark green upper surface. The foliage often features a slightly revolute margin and a duller underside to minimize water loss. Its inflorescences consist of dense, spherical clusters of bell-shaped flowers that vary in color from pale pink to deep rose. The individual corollas are characterized by five fused petals that flare outward at the mouth. These flowering structures emerge from woody, terminal buds located at the ends of the branches.

What medicinal compounds this plant contains?

This plant contains grayanotoxins, which are a group of diterpenes that act as neurotoxins by affecting sodium channels in cell membranes. These specific compounds can cause significant physiological effects such as hypotension, bradycardia, and gastrointestinal distress if ingested.
