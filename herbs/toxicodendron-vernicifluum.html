Lacquer tree, scientifically known as Toxicodendron vernicifluum, is a remarkable species valued for its unique resin. While famous for producing high-quality traditional lacquer, it contains urushiol, a potent allergen. This plant offers fascinating insights into both industrial craftsmanship and the complex chemical defense mechanisms found within the botanical world.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Toxicodendron vernicifluum, is most valued for its specific botanical components used in traditional applications. The most significant part is the resin, which is extracted from the bark and is the primary source for producing high-quality urushi lacquer. Additionally, the heartwood of the tree is sometimes utilized, contributing to the plant's overall structural and medicinal profile in various traditional practices.
