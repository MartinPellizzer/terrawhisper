Aster poliothamnus, scientifically known as Aster poliothamnus, is a small, creeping perennial herb widely recognized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Often referred to as "Small Indian Daisy," this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, particularly for its digestive and anti-inflammatory benefits, making it a vital resource in herbal pharmacology.
