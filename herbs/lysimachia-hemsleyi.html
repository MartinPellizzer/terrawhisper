Lysimachia hemsleyi

What's the taxonomical classification of Lysimachia hemsleyi?

Lysimachia hemsleyi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, specifically placed within the genus Lysimachia, and is identified by its unique species epithet, hemsleyi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lysimachia hemsleyi has creeping, rhizomatous stems that produce whorled, lanceolate leaves with entire margins and acute tips. The foliage is typically bright green and grows in dense mats along the ground. Small, actinomorphic flowers emerge from the leaf axils, featuring five bright yellow petals that form a star-like shape. These blooms are supported by slender pedicels and emerge during the summer months. The overall structure of the plant creates a lush, ground-covering texture suitable for shaded environments.
