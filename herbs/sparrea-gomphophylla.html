Sparrea gomphophylla, scientifically known as Sparrea gomphophylla, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its diuretic and emmenagogue properties, it is frequently utilized to support kidney function and regulate menstrual cycles. This plant remains a significant botanical resource in various ethnobotanical practices worldwide.
