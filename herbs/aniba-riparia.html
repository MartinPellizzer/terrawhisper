Aniba riparia

Aniba riparia, commonly known as the riverbank herb, is a resilient medicinal plant found thriving in moist, riparian ecosystems. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it has long been utilized in traditional healing to treat inflammatory ailments and digestive issues, offering a natural remedy deeply rooted in botanical wisdom.

  • Scientific name: Aniba riparia
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aniba.
