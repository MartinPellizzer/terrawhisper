Moluccan ironwood, scientifically known as Intsia bijuga, is a versatile tropical timber tree valued for its remarkable durability and medicinal properties. Native to Southeast Asia and Oceania, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant ethnobotanical importance to local communities.
