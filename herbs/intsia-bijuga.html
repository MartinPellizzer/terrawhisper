Distribution

This plant, known as Intsia bijuga, exhibits a broad distribution across several key maritime regions. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found within the borders of Tanzania. Its presence extends significantly throughout the Western Indian Ocean, spanning multiple island territories. Specifically, it is located in the Chagos Archipelago, Mauritius, and Madagascar. Finally, the species completes its regional range by inhabiting the Seychelles.
