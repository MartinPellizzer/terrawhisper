Annona foetida

Annona foetida, commonly known as the foetid custard apple, is a tropical shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to various warm regions, it is recognized for its distinct, pungent aroma. Various parts of the plant are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Annona foetida
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Annona foetida, is a member of the kingdom Plantae and belongs to the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Magnoliales. Ultimately, it is a representative of the family Annonaceaae and the genus Annona.
