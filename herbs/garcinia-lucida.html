Garcinia lucida

Garcinia lucida, commonly known as the "forest gold" of parts of Africa, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its rich antioxidant content, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, offering immense potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia lucida
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Clusiaceae. It is further identified by its genus, Garcinia, which contains the species Garcinia lucida.
