Urera aurantiaca

Urera aurantiaca, commonly known as the orange nettle, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues. This potent botanical offers promising pharmacological potential for developing new natural therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Urera aurantiaca
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Urticaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Urera.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Urera auriculata, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently called stinging nettle, while in Spanish, it is known as ortiga. In Portuguese, the plant is commonly referred to as urtiga, and in French, it is called ortie.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions in Western South America. Specifically, its presence has been documented within the territories of Bolivia and Colombia. In addition to these western nations, it is widely found throughout various parts of Brazil. Its range extends into the West-Central and Northeast regions of the Brazilian landscape. Furthermore, the species can also be located within the Southeast portion of Brazil.
