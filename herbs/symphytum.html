Symphytum

Symphytum, commonly known as comfrey, is a perennial herb renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Historically used to promote healing, it contains allantoin, which accelerates tissue regeneration. While widely valued in traditional topical remedies for soothing bruises and sprains, its high alkaloid content necessitates cautious and responsible application.

  • Scientific name: Symphytum
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Symphytum.
