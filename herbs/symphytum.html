Comfrey

Symphytum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (16 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Comfrey, scientifically known as Symphytum, is a perennial herb renowned for its potent healing properties. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, its leaves and roots contain allantoin, which promotes cell regeneration and skin repair. Often applied topically for bruises and sprains, this versatile plant remains a cornerstone of traditional phytotherapy.
