Turraeanthus africanus

Turraeanthus africanus, commonly known as the African Sandalwood, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from respiratory issues to skin infections, serving as a vital resource in ethnobotanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Turraeanthus africanus
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Turraeanthus africanus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales and the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Turraeanthus.
