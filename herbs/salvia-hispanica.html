Chía, scientifically known as Salvia hispanica, is a nutritional powerhouse native to Central America. Renowned for its remarkable concentration of omega-3 fatty acids, fiber, and antioxidants, these tiny seeds offer significant health benefits. Often used in smoothies and baked goods, chía serves as a versatile superfood in modern diets.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a fragmented geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence can be identified within Great Britain.
