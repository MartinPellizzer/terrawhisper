Moquiniastrum polymorphum

Moquiniastrum polymorphum, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, holds significant value in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, this plant offers potential therapeutic benefits, particularly in treating inflammation and skin ailments. Understanding its botanical properties is essential for exploring its role in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Moquiniastrum polymorphum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Moquiniastrum, as part of the species Moquiniastrum polymorphum.
