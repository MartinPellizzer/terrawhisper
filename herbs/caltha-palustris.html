Marsh marigold, scientifically known as Caltha palustris, is a perennial herb celebrated for its vibrant, butter-yellow blooms. Thriving in wetland habitats, this resilient plant serves as an early spring signal. Historically used in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory properties, it remains a vital component of diverse aquatic ecosystems.
