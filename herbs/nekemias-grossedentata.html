Nekemias grossedentata

Nekemias grossedentata, commonly known as large-toothed nekemias, is a distinctive herbaceous perennial native to North American wetlands. Characterized by its serrated foliage and delicate blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Researchers study its unique chemical profile to explore potential therapeutic applications in traditional and modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Nekemias grossedentata
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Vitales, falling into the family Vitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Nekemias and the specific species Nekemias grossedentata.

This plant, Nekemias grossedentata, is known by several names across different linguistic contexts, specifically in Chinese. Plant Parts

This plant, Neemias grossedentata, possesses several distinct morphological features that are often utilized in traditional applications. The large, serrated leaves serve as a primary identifier, though the young leaves are frequently preferred for their potency and tenderness. The roots form a robust underground system, while the dried stems can be processed for various medicinal preparations. Notably, a characteristic white powder on the surface of certain parts can sometimes be observed, which is a key botanical marker for distinguishing this specific species.
