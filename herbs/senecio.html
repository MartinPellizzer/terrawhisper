Dendrosenecio, scientifically known as Senecio, is a diverse genus of flowering plants within the Asteraceae family. Renowned for their vibrant blooms, many species hold significant ethnobotanical value. While some varieties offer therapeutic potential in traditional medicine, others contain pyrrolizidine alkaloids, requiring careful study regarding their medicinal safety.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Senecio.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the northern regions of Europe. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Its presence also extends to the territories of Finland and the Faroe Islands, known locally as Føroyar. Furthermore, the species is documented across the landscape of Great Britain. Finally, its northern range reaches as far as the rugged terrain of Iceland.
