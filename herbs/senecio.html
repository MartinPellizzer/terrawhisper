Senecio

Dendrosenecio

Senecio, commonly known as groundsel or ragwort, is a diverse genus of flowering plants within the Asteraceae family. While many species are admired for their bright, daisy-like blooms, others contain pyrrolizidine alkaloids, making them toxic to livestock. Understanding their medicinal properties and toxicity is vital for botanical study.

  • Scientific name: Senecio
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Senecio.
