What's the taxonomical classification of Coriaria intermedia?

Coriaria intermedia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Cucurbitales. Within this order, the plant is a member of the family Coriariaceae and the genus Coriaria, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species intermedia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Coriaria intermedia has an erect, woody shrub reaching up to 2 meters in height with small, ovate, leathery leaves that are typically arranged in an opposite pattern. The foliage is characterized by a dark green upper surface and a lighter underside, often featuring distinct venation. Its flowers are small, inconspicuous, and typically creamy white or pale yellow, appearing in clusters along the branches. The plant produces small, fleshy, dark purple to black drupes that serve as its primary fruit type. These fruits are often found hanging in dense bunches from the stems during the fruiting season.
