Chamaelirium luteum

Chamaelirium luteum, commonly known as the Yellow Chamaeliurium, is a significant medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in ethnobotany to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Chamaelirium luteum
  • Family: Melanthiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Liliales and the family Melanthiaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Chamaelirium.
