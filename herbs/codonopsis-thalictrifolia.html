Codonopsis thalictrifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Codonopsis thalictrifolia?

Codonopsis thalictrifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant further falls into the order Asterales and is a member of the family Campanulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Codonopsis and the species thalictrifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Codonoopsis thalictrifolia has lanceolate to ovate leaves that grow in a basal rosette and feature distinctively serrated or wavy margins. These leaves are typically bright green and possess a smooth, somewhat fleshy texture. The plant produces upright flowering stalks that emerge from the center of the foliage. The flowers are small and tubular, often appearing in clusters with a delicate structure. This morphological arrangement allows the plant to thrive in shaded, moist environments.
