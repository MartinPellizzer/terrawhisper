Laburnum alpinum

Laburnum

Laburnum alpinum, commonly known as Alpine Laburnum, is a striking deciduous shrub native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Renowned for its cascading clusters of bright yellow flowers, this ornamental plant adds elegance to alpine gardens. While visually stunning, it contains toxic alkaloids, requiring careful handling and cultivation.

  • Scientific name: Laburnum alpinum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, situated within the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Laburnum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Laburnum alpinum, is recognized by various local names across different European languages. In Poland, it is referred to as Złotokap alpejski, while in Finland, it is called kaljukultasade. Its Scandinavian names include the Swedish Alpgullregn, the Norwegian Alpegullregn, and in Germany, it is commonly known as Alpen-Goldregen.
