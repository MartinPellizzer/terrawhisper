Laburnum, scientifically known as Laburnum alpinum, is a striking deciduous shrub native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Renowned for its cascading, golden-yellow blossoms, it offers significant ornamental value. While visually captivating, all parts of this plant contain toxic alkaloids, requiring careful handling and expert medicinal application.
