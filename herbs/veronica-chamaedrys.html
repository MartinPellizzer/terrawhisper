Veronica chamaedrys

Veronica chamaedrys, commonly known as Germander Speedwell, is a charming perennial herb characterized by its delicate blue flowers. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it possesses diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant thrives in meadows, offering both aesthetic beauty and historical significance in traditional European botanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Veronica chamaedrys
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Plantaginacea and the genus Veronica.
