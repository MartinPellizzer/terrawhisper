Vincetoxicum hirundinaria

Vincetoxicum hirundiinaria, commonly known as the golden shroud vine, is a potent medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its unique alkaloid profile, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. This study explores its phytochemical composition and therapeutic potential for developing modern pharmacological treatments and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vincetoxicum hirundinaria
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Vincetoxicum, and its species name, Vincetoxicum hirundinaria.
