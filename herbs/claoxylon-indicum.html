Claoxylon indicum

Claoxylon indicum, commonly known as the Indian Claoxylon, is a significant medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering valuable phytochemical compounds for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Claoxylon indicum
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Claoxylon. Ultimately, its scientific name is Claoxylon indicum.
