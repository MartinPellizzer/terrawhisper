Camphor Thyme, scientifically known as Thymus camphoratus, is a fragrant, aromatic shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its potent essential oils, this medicinal plant possesses significant antibacterial, antifungal, and antiseptic properties. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues.

This plant, Thymus camphoratus, is primarily valued for its chemical properties found within its essential oils.
