Thymus camphoratus

Thymus camphoratus, commonly known as camphor thyme, is a fragrant perennial herb native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its distinct aromatic profile, this plant contains high levels of camphor. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial, antiseptic, and soothing properties, supporting respiratory and digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Thymus camphoratus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Thymus, specifically as the species Thymus camphoratus.
