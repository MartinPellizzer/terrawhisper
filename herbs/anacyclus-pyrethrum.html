Anacyclus pyrethrum

Anacyclus pyrethrum, commonly known as Golden Pyrethrum, is a potent medicinal herb revered for its therapeutic properties. Rich in natural pyrethrins, this botanical powerhouse is widely utilized in traditional medicine to combat parasitic infections and reduce inflammation, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and systemic healing.

  • Scientific name: Anacyclus pyrethrum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Anacyclus and its specific species name, pyrethrum.
