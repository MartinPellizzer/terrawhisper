Dendrobium moniliforme

Dendrobium moniliiforme, commonly known as the beaded orchid, is a prized medicinal plant in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this orchid is frequently used to nourish the lungs, clear heat, and promote hydration. It remains a vital component in herbal formulations valued for their restorative benefits.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium moniliforme
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Dendrobium moniliforme, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales. It is a member of the Orchidaceae family, specifically positioned within the genus Dendrobium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Dendrobium moniliiforme, is referred to by various names depending on the language and region. In Japanese, it is commonly called セッコク, while in Sorbian, it is known as Zažna štomowa orchideja. Its names in Chinese vary slightly by script and region, being identified as 细茎石斛 in both mainland China (zh-cn) and general simplified Chinese (zh), as 細莖石斛 in Taiwan (zh-tw), and also as 细茎石斛 in traditional contexts (zh).

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the North-Central areas. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the Southeast region of China. Beyond the Chinese mainland, the species extends into the island nation of Japan. Finally, its geographical range includes the Korean Peninsula.
