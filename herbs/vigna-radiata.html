Vigna radiata

Mung bean

Vigna radiata, commonly known as mung bean, is a highly nutritious legume celebrated in traditional medicine. Rich in proteins, vitamins, and essential minerals, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and detoxifying properties. Often used to aid digestion and support metabolic health, this versatile plant serves as a nutritional powerhouse.

  • Scientific name: Vigna radiata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Vigna.
