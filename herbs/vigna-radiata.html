Mung bean, scientifically known as Vigna radiata, is a versatile legume celebrated both as a nutritional powerhouse and a medicinal marvel. Rich in proteins, antioxidants, and essential vitamins, it plays a vital role in traditional medicine for detoxifying the body, improving digestion, and promoting overall systemic wellness and vitality.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Vigna.
