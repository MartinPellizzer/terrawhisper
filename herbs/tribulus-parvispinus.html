Tribulus parvispinus

Tribulus parvispinus, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, belongs to the Zygophyllaceae family. Widely distributed in arid regions, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. It is frequently studied for its potential bioactive compounds, offering significant benefits for holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Tribulus parvispinus
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zygophyllales. Finally, it falls into the family Zygophyllaceae and is identified by the genus Tribulus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the arid regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Egypt. Its presence extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the landscapes of Djibouti and Eritrea. Furthermore, the species is documented across the territory of Somalia. Finally, its range continues through the expansive regions of both Sudan and South Sudan.
