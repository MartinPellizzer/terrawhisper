Harpagophytum procumbens

Devil's claw

Harpagophytum procumbens, commonly known as Devil's Claw, is a medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory properties, its tuberous roots contain iridoid glycosides like harpagoside. It is widely utilized in herbal medicine to alleviate joint pain, osteoarthritis, and various digestive ailments effectively.

  • Scientific name: Harpagophytum procumbens
  • Family: Pedaliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Pedaliaceae. It is further identified by the genus Harpagophytum.
