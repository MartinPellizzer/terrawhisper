Devil's claw

Harpagophytum procumbens · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (752 studies) · ★★★★★

Devil's claw, scientifically known as Harpagophytum procumbens, is a perennial herb native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory properties, it is widely used in traditional and modern medicine to alleviate joint pain, arthritis, and digestive discomfort. Its active compounds, particularly iridoid glycosides, drive its therapeutic efficacy.

Distribution

This plant is native to specific regions within the South Tropical Africa area, including Zimbabwe. Its natural habitat extends across several parts of Southern Africa, such as Botswana. Finally, this medicinal species is documented to inhabit parts of Namibia.
