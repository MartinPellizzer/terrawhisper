Geranium umbelliforme

What's the taxonomical classification of Geranium umbelliforme?

Geranium umbelliforme belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales, it is a member of the family Geraniaceae. Finally, its specific identity is defined by the genus Geranium and the species umbelliforme.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Geranium umbelliforme has a perennial, woody-based growth habit characterized by deeply lobed, palmately divided leaves and umbel-like inflorescences. The foliage consists of long-petioled leaves that often feature a velvety texture and distinctively serrated margins. Its flowering structure produces multiple small, five-petaled flowers that emerge from a single point on a common stalk. The stems are typically slender and can become somewhat woody at the base as the plant matures. This unique morphology allows the species to thrive in its specific montane habitats.
