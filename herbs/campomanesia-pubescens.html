Plant Parts

This plant, Campomanesia pubescens, is a type of red algae, which means its biological structure differs significantly from terrestrial plants that possess roots, stems, and leaves. Because it belongs to the division Rhodophyta, it does not produce a traditional fruit as defined by angiosperms; instead, its reproductive processes involve the production of spores and specialized reproductive cells within its thallus.
