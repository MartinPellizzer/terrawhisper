Tylosema esculentum

Tylosema esculentum, commonly known as the African yam bean, is a nutritious legume native to West Africa. Highly valued for its protein-rich seeds and drought-resistant nature, this versatile plant serves as a vital food security crop. It offers significant medicinal potential, traditionally used to support overall health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Tylosema esculentum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Tylosema esculentum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is specifically classified under the genus Tylosema.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence throughout various regions of the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its distribution includes the nations of Angola and Zimbabwe. Moving into Southern Africa, it can be found across the landscape of Botswana. The species also inhabits the Cape Provinces within the southern reaches of the continent. Additionally, its range extends into the arid environments of Namibia.
