Achillea grandifolia

Achillea grandifolia, commonly known as large-leaf yarrow, is a resilient perennial celebrated for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its ability to support wound healing and reduce inflammation, this botanical powerhouse has long been used in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues and promote overall systemic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Achillea grandifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it falls under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Achillea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several distinct regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Germany. Its distribution extends significantly into Southeastern Europe, where it inhabits the landscapes of Albania and Bulgaria. Furthermore, the species is widely established throughout the diverse terrain of Greece. Finally, the plant is also located in the European portion of Türkiye.
