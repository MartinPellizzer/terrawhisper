Paeonia obovata

Paeonia obovata, commonly known as the Chinese peony, is a revered medicinal herb in traditional Eastern medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, the plant's roots are frequently utilized to treat inflammation, alleviate menstrual pain, and promote blood circulation, offering a rich natural resource for holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia obovata
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
