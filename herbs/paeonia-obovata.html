What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Paeonia obovata has obovate to elliptical leaves that are typically 5 to 12 centimeters long, featuring a leathery texture and entire or slightly wavy margins. Its stems are robust and erect, supporting large, solitary flowers that can reach several centimeters in diameter. These blossoms are characterized by numerous delicate petals and a central cluster of prominent stamens. The overall structure of the plant is herbaceous and perennial, forming dense clumps of lush green vegetation.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant Paeonia obovata requires well-draining, fertile soil with a slightly acidic to neutral pH and a location that receives full sun to partial shade to thrive.

It is a deciduous perennial that performs best when planted in early spring or autumn to establish a strong root system. Deeply planting the tubers is crucial, as they should be positioned no more than two inches below the soil surface to prevent rot. Regular watering is necessary during the growing season, though the soil must be allowed to dry slightly between applications to avoid waterlogging.

Once established, this species is relatively hardy and requires minimal supplemental fertilization to produce its characteristic large, pink flowers.
