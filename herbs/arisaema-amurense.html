Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the temperate regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented in Khabarovsk, Primorye, and on the island of Sakhalin. The species also extends into northern China, where it can be found within Inner Mongolia. Furthermore, its range encompasses the broader Manchuria region. These specific locations define the natural geographical footprint of Arisaema amurense.
