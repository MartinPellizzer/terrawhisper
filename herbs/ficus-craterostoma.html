Ficus craterostoma

Ficus craterostoma, commonly known as the crater-mouth fig, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to arid regions. Renowned for its unique morphology, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional practitioners utilize various parts of this species to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its vital role in natural healing and biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Ficus craterostoma
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Rosales and the family Moraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ficus, which includes the species Ficus craterostoma.
