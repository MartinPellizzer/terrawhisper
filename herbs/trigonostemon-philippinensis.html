Trigonostemon philippinenis, scientifically known as Trigonostemon philippinenis, is a climbing woody vine native to the Philippines. Recognized for its distinct foliage and flowering patterns, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential therapeutic properties and bioactive compounds in regional healing practices.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central areas to the Southeast provinces. Its presence also extends into the Indo-China peninsula, covering multiple distinct nations. Specifically, it is documented growing in the territories of Laos and Myanmar. Additionally, the species is commonly distributed throughout Thailand.
