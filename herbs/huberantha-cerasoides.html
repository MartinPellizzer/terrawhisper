Cherry Ashok, scientifically known as Huberantha cerasoides, is a versatile medicinal tree celebrated in traditional Ayurveda. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to support reproductive health, regulate menstrual cycles, and manage inflammatory conditions. Its bark and leaves serve as vital components in various herbal remedies.
