Piper marginatum

Piper marginatum, commonly known as western pepperweed, is a perennial herb native to North America. Characterized by its distinctive serrated leaves and aromatic scent, this plant belongs to the Piperaceae family. While often considered a weed, it holds significant ecological value and traditional medicinal interest in various regional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Piper marginatum
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is a member of the family Piperaceae. It is specifically classified under the genus Piper.
