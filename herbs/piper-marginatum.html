Marigold pepper, scientifically known as Piper marginatum, is a perennial shrub native to the southeastern United States. Characterized by its distinctive serrated leaves and small, spike-like inflorescences, this plant thrives in moist, shaded habitats. While primarily recognized for its ornamental value, it holds significant ecological and traditional medicinal importance.
