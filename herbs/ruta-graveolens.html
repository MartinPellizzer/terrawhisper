Scientific literature: Very Extensive (630 studies) · ★★★★★

Rue, scientifically known as Ruta graveolens, is a perennial shrub prized for its aromatic, blue-green foliage. Historically used in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues and menstrual irregularities, it contains potent bioactive compounds. However, due to its toxicity in high doses, it must be handled with extreme caution.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as common rue, possesses several distinct morphological features that contribute to its medicinal properties. The leaves are highly characteristic, being deeply divided and blue-green in color, while the overall leaf structure provides a strong, pungent aroma. The stem is woody at the base and becomes more herbaceous toward the top, providing structural support for the foliage. Below the ground, the root system helps anchor the plant and absorb necessary nutrients from the soil. In various traditional applications, the dried leaf powder is often prepared and used for its therapeutic potential in folk medicine.
