Ruta graveolens

Ruta graveolens, commonly known as rue, is a perennial shrub celebrated in traditional medicine for its potent aromatic properties. Characterized by its bluish-green, pinnate leaves, this hardy plant has been long utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits, though its high potency requires careful and expert application.

  • Scientific name: Ruta graveolens
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ruta and the species graveolens.
