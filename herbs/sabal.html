Sabal

Sabal, commonly known as the Palmetto, is a versatile plant prized in traditional medicine for its healing properties. Often utilized to treat respiratory ailments and skin irritations, its various parts offer potent bioactive compounds. This resilient species continues to be a vital resource in holistic wellness and herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Sabal
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Arecales and placed in the family Arecaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sabal.
