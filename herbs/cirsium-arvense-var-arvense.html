Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as creeping thistle, can be utilized in its entirety, often referred to as the whole grass in various traditional preparations. The botanical structure begins with a robust underground rhizome system that allows the species to spread effectively through the soil. From this base, the aerial parts emerge, consisting of sturdy stems and distinctive prickly leaves. These aboveground parts bear the characteristic purple flower heads that are central to its medicinal identity. Ultimately, the aboveground vegetation serves as the primary source for harvesting the plant's therapeutic components.
