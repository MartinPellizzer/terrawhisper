, scientifically known as Cirsium arvense var. arvense, is a resilient perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Commonly called creeping thistle, this plant is distinguished by its prickly foliage and pinkish-purple flower heads. Traditionally, it has been utilized in various folk medicines for its potential therapeutic properties.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Cirsium.

Distribution

This plant is found across several regions within Northern Europe. Its documented distribution includes the territories of Denmark and Finland. It also grows within the islands of Føroyar and Iceland. Additionally, the species can be located throughout Great Britain.
