Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found in the nations of Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Its presence also extends to the West Tropical African countries of Mali and Nigeria. Beyond the western region, the species is documented within West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, its range includes the territory of Burundi.
