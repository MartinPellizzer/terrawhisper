Pulicaria undulata

Pulicaria undulata, commonly known as the wavy tansy, is a resilient perennial herb native to various regions of Africa and Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, often utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects to treat diverse ailments and promote overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Pulicaria undulata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Pulicaria, with its species designation being undulata.

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast landscapes of Northern Africa. Within this region, its presence is noted in the diverse terrains of Algeria.
