Toad's Herb, scientifically known as Eryngium carlinae, is a striking, thistle-like perennial native to European limestone grasslands. Renowned for its architectural, spiny bracts and silver-blue hues, this hardy plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine, long utilized for its purported restorative properties and unique botanical resilience in rugged terrains.

Plant Parts

This plant, known for its striking and architectural appearance, is primarily characterized by its robust and complex inflorescences. These inflorescences consist of dense, thistle-like heads surrounded by stiff, spiny bracts that serve as a protective layer for the tiny, individual flowers nestled within. The structural integrity of these inflorescences makes them a prominent feature of the plant's morphology, contributing to its distinctive silhouette in its natural habitat.
