Eryngium carlinae

Eryngium carlinae, commonly known as Carline Thistle, is a striking perennial native to European meadows. Distinguished by its spiny, architectural bracts, this resilient plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine. Renowned for its unique morphology, it serves as a fascinating subject for both botanical study and herbal folklore.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
