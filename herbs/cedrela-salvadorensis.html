Cedrela salvadorensis

Cedrela salvadorensis, commonly known as cedar or cedro, is a significant medicinal tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic timber, its bark and leaves possess potent bioactive compounds. Traditionally, it is utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, respiratory ailments, and skin conditions, offering diverse therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Cedrela salvadorensis
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is further categorized into the family Meliaceae. Ultimately, its scientific identity is defined by the genus Cedrela.
