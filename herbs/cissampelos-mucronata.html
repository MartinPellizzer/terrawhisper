Cissampelos mucronata

Cissampelos mucronata, commonly known as the woody climber or devil's claw, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa and Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots and leaves possess potent bioactive properties, used extensively to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive issues, and various skin ailments through natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Cissampelos mucronata
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Menispermaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cissampelos.
