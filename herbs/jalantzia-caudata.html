Jalantzia caudata

Jalantzia caudata, commonly known as the "long-tailed jalantzia," is a specialized medicinal herb native to specific mountain ecosystems. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support systemic health, offering potent bioactive compounds that serve as vital subjects for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Jalantzia caudata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is categorized under the genus Jalantzia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Justicia caudata, is recognized by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as tail-leaved Justicia, while in Spanish, it is commonly called Justicia de cola or Justicia caudada. In Portuguese, the plant is known as Justícia-de-cauda.

This plant, known scientifically as Jalanitzia caudata, possesses a very specific and localized natural range. Its geographical distribution is strictly confined to the Western Indian Ocean region. Within this maritime zone, the species is found exclusively on the island of Madagascar. This limited habitat suggests that the plant is endemic to this unique island ecosystem. Consequently, its survival is closely tied to the specific environmental conditions present in Madagascar.
