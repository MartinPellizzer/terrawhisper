Lannea velutina

Lannea velutinina, commonly known as velvet lannea, is a resilient climbing shrub native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its unique velvety foliage, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in ethnobotanical practices. Researchers continue to explore its bioactive compounds, investigating its potential therapeutic properties for various holistic healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Lannea velutina
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant, known as Lannea velutina, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Sapindales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Anacardiaceae and the genus Lannea.
