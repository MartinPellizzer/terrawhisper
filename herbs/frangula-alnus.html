Frangula alnus

Frangula alnus, commonly known as alder buckthorn, is a deciduous shrub native to Europe and North Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, its bark contains anthraquinone glycosides, which act as potent stimulant laxatives. Historically used to treat constipation, it remains a significant botanical subject in traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Frangula alnus
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Europe
