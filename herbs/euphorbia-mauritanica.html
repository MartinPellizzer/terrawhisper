This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Euphorbia, specifically as the species Euphorbia mauritanica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across both the African continent and the Indian subcontinent. Within Southern Africa, its presence is notably established throughout the various Cape Provinces. It can also be found growing in the arid regions of Namibia and across the landscapes of the Free State. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the subtropical areas of KwaZulu-Natal. Beyond Africa, the plant is also documented as occurring within the Indian subcontinent, specifically in India.
