Mentha satureioides

Mentha saturejoides

Mentha saturteioides, commonly known as Moroccan mint, is a fragrant perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its refreshing aroma and cooling properties, this plant is widely used in traditional herbal medicine and culinary practices. It offers potent antioxidant benefits, making it a versatile staple in natural wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Mentha.
