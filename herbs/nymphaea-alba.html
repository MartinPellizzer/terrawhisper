White water-lily, scientifically known as Nymphaea alba, is a perennial aquatic plant renowned for its striking white blooms and historical medicinal significance. Traditionally used to treat inflammation, anxiety, and digestive issues, its sedative properties stem from unique alkaloids. This elegant species remains a vital subject in ethnobotanical studies.

Plant Parts

This plant, Nymphaea alba, commonly known as the white water lily, is valued in traditional herbalism primarily for the therapeutic properties found within its botanical components. While various parts of the plant may be utilized in different preparations, the flower extract is particularly significant due to the presence of specific alkaloids and flavonoids contained within the petals. This extract is often studied for its potential sedative and calming effects, as the chemical constituents concentrated in the blossoms are believed to assist in soothing the nervous system.
