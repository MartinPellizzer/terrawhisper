Nymphaea alba

White water-lily

Nymphaea alba, commonly known as the European white water lily, is a majestic aquatic perennial. Renowned for its striking white blossoms and floating leaves, this plant has long been utilized in traditional herbalism. It possesses calming properties, often studied for its potential to soothe the nervous system and reduce inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Nymphaea alba
  • Family: Nymphaeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Nymphaeales, and the family Nymphaeaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement concludes within the genus Nymphaea.
