Ladeania juncea

Ladeania juncea, commonly known as the "creeping vine" or "juncea," is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and respiratory issues, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Ladeania juncea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is completed by the genus Ladeania.
