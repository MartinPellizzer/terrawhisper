Hippomane mancinella, scientifically known as Hippomane mancinella, is one of the world's most toxic plants. Often called the "Manchineel tree," it is notorious for its highly caustic sap. While historically explored for medicinal properties, its extreme toxicity poses severe risks, requiring extreme caution during any botanical study.
