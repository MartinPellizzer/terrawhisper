Varronia cylindristachya

Varronia cylindrostachya

Varronia cylindristachya, commonly known as a potent medicinal shrub, is a significant botanical species valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, offering natural bioactive compounds that contribute to holistic healing and supporting overall human wellness.

  • Scientific name: Varronia cylindristachya
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Ultimately, this medicinal species is identified by its genus, Varroniia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct tropical regions. Within the Malesia region, it can be found specifically in Malaya. Its range extends to the Caribbean, where it inhabits the Venezuelan Antilles. In Northern South America, the species is documented to grow in Venezuela. Finally, its presence is noted in Western South America within the countries of Bolivia and Colombia.
