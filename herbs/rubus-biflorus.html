Rubus biflorus

Rubus biflorus, commonly known as the two-flowered raspberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant is utilized in traditional healing to treat digestive ailments, reduce inflammation, and support skin health, offering a natural remedy rooted in botanical wisdom.

  • Scientific name: Rubus biflorus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Rubus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Rubus biflorus, is referred to by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is identified by the names 二花悬钩子 (zh), 二花悬钩子 (zh-cn), and 二花懸鉤子 (zh-tw). Additionally, it is known as 粉枝莓 (zh-hans) and is also called 大叶蛇泡簕 (zh).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across various regions of Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central and North-Central territories. It also extends its reach into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found growing in Assam. Finally, its distribution encompasses the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya.
