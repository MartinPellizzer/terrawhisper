Barbed skullcap, scientifically known as Scutelari barbata, is a perennial herb valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is frequently used to clear heat, resolve toxicity, and soothe inflammation. This potent botanical serves as a vital remedy for addressing respiratory issues and various febrile conditions.
