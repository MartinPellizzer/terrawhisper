Plant Parts

This plant, Tamarix tetragyna, possesses distinct morphological characteristics within its structure, most notably in its stem and leaves. The stem is typically woody and branched, providing a sturdy framework that allows the shrub to withstand arid environments. In contrast, the leaves are highly specialized and minute, appearing almost scale-like along the slender twigs. These diminutive leaves are an evolutionary adaptation designed to minimize water loss through transpiration, which is essential for the plant's survival in its native saline or semi-desert habitats.
