Alstonia boonei

Alstonia boonei, commonly known as the "tree of life" in certain African traditions, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is extensively utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions and digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Alstonia boonei
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, specifically residing in the family Apocynaceae. Its taxonomic identity is completed by its classification within the genus Alstona.

Distribution

This plant, known as Alstonia boonei, exhibits a specific distribution pattern throughout the West Tropical African region. Its presence is documented across several coastal and inland nations within this diverse ecological zone. Specifically, the species can be found growing in the territories of Benin and Gambia. Furthermore, its geographical range extends into the countries of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. The distribution is completed by its documented occurrence within the nation of Guinea.
