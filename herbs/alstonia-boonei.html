Alstonia, scientifically known as Alstonia boonei, is a significant medicinal tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, various parts of the plant are traditionally used to treat malaria, fever, and inflammation. It serves as a vital resource in ethnobotanical practices for managing various ailments.

Distribution

This plant, known as Alstonia boonei, exhibits a specific distribution pattern throughout the West Tropical African region.
