Rhynchosia viscosa

Rhynchosia viscosa, commonly known as the sticky rhynchosia, is a versatile medicinal herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive properties, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin disorders to digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Rhynchosia viscosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Rhynchosia viscosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Rhynchosia.
