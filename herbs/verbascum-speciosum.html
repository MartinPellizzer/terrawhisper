Verbascum speciosum

Showy mullein

Verbascum speciosum, commonly known as the Showy Mullein, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its architectural beauty and therapeutic potential. Characterized by tall, flowering spikes and velvety foliage, this plant offers significant medicinal properties, traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support respiratory health and soothe various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Verbascum speciosum
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant, known scientifically as Verbascum speciosum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
