Baillonella toxisperma

Moabi

Baillonella toxisperma, commonly known as Curare, is a woody climber native to South American rainforests. Historically significant, its bark contains potent alkaloids used by indigenous tribes to create arrow poisons. In modern pharmacology, its bioactive compounds have provided vital insights into neuromuscular blocking agents used during surgical anesthesia.

  • Scientific name: Baillonella toxisperma
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Africa
