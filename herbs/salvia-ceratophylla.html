Salvia ceratophylla

Salvia ceratophylla, commonly known as the Horned Sage, is a striking perennial herb native to the arid regions of Mexico. Distinguished by its unique, horn-like calyxes and vibrant purple blooms, this resilient plant is valued in traditional herbal practices for its potent antioxidant properties and diverse medicinal potential.

  • Scientific name: Salvia ceratophylla
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Salvia.
