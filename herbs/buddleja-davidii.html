Buddleja davidii

Buddleja davidii, commonly known as the Butterfly Bush, is a fast-growing deciduous shrub celebrated for its fragrant, cone-shaped flower spikes. While widely used in ornamental landscaping to attract pollinators, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest due to its traditional medicinal uses in treating inflammation, wounds, and various digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Buddleja davidii
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Buddleja.
