What's the taxonomical classification of Ficus ischnopoda?

Ficus ischnopoda belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic progression continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, eventually reaching the order Rosales. Within this order, the plant is a member of the family Moraceae and is situated under the genus Ficus, specifically identified by the species name ischnopoda.
