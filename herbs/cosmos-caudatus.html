Ulam Raja, scientifically known as Cosmos caudatus, is a vibrant medicinal herb native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its nutritional density, this leafy green is a staple in traditional diets. Rich in antioxidants and flavonoids, it is widely celebrated for supporting bone health, improving circulation, and promoting overall wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Celtuce or Wild Cosmos, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties found within its various components. The most significant medicinal part is the large, green leaves, which are rich in antioxidants and flavonoids. When a single leaf is processed, it can be utilized to create a potent medicinal extract used in traditional studies to support health and combat oxidative stress.
