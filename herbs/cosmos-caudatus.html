Cosmos caudatus

Ulam raja

Cosmos caudatus, commonly known as Wild Cosmos, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South America. Rich in antioxidants and essential nutrients, it is traditionally used to promote cardiovascular health, enhance bone density, and combat oxidative stress. This potent herb offers significant therapeutic potential in modern holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Cosmos caudatus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Specifically, its taxonomic classification is placed within the genus Cosmos.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. Within the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found on the island of Mauritius. Its presence also extends to Madagascar and the island of Réunion. Furthermore, it is documented in the territory of Rodrigues. Finally, the species is located within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the state of Assam.

This plant, commonly known as Celtuce or Wild Cosmos, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties found within its various components. When a single leaf is processed, it can be utilized to create a potent medicinal extract used in traditional studies to support health and combat oxidative stress.
