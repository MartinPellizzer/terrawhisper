Celosia argentea

Caudatus

Celosia argentea, commonly known as silver cock's comb, is a versatile annual herb cherished for its vibrant, ornamental blooms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is widely utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties across various healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Celosia argentea
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Celosia.
