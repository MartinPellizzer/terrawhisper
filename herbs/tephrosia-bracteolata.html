Tephrosia bracteolata

Tephrosia bracteolata, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Often utilized in traditional healing practices, this plant is valued for its potential therapeutic properties. Research continues to explore its bioactive compounds, aiming to uncover its full medicinal value for modern pharmacology.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Fabales, it falls under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically categorized under the genus Tephrosia.
