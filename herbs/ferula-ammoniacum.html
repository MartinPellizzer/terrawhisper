Ferula ammoniacum

Dorema ammoniacum

Ferula ammoniacum, commonly known as asafoetida, is a perennial plant prized for its pungent, resinous gum. Extracted from the roots, this medicinal herb has been used extensively in traditional Ayurvedic and Unani medicine. It is highly valued for its digestive, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering diverse therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Ferula ammoniacum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Specifically, Ferula ammoniacum is identified by the genus Ferula.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In Central Asia, it can be found growing within the borders of Turkmenistan. Its range extends into Western Asia, where it is native to both Afghanistan and Iran. Additionally, the species is present within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in Pakistan. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where this medicinal species thrives.
