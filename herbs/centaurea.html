Centaurea

Centaurea, commonly known as knapweed, is a diverse genus of flowering plants within the Asteraceae family. Renowned for their vibrant, thistle-like blooms, these resilient perennials play vital roles in supporting pollinators. Historically, various species have been utilized in traditional medicine for their diverse bioactive and therapeutic properties.
