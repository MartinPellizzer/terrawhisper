Knapweed, scientifically known as Centaurea, is a diverse genus of flowering plants renowned for their striking, thistle-like blooms. Historically valued in traditional medicine, various species offer therapeutic potential due to their rich antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. These resilient perennials play vital roles in supporting diverse pollinator ecosystems worldwide.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the northern regions of Europe. It can be found growing across the landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Its presence extends to the island territory of Føroyar as well. Furthermore, the species is established throughout the various regions of Great Britain. Finally, the plant is also documented within the natural habitats of Ireland.
