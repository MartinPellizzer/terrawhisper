Adenophora sinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Adenophora sinensis?

Adenophora sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage leading to the order Asterales. This plant is specifically situated within the family Campanulaceae, falling under the genus Adenophora, and is identified by its specific species epithet, sinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Adenophora sinensis has perennial herbaceous stems that typically reach heights of 20 to 60 centimeters and bear lanceolate to ovate leaves with serrated margins. The foliage is characterized by an opposite arrangement along the stem, often featuring distinct veining and a slightly pubescent texture. During its flowering period, the plant produces small, bell-shaped purple or violet corollas that emerge from terminal inflorescences. These tubular flowers are supported by slender pedicels and are clustered toward the upper portions of the stems. The root system consists of a thickened, fleshy rhizome that serves as the primary storage organ for the plant.
