What's the taxonomical classification of Terminthia paniculata?

Terminthia paniculata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Sapindales. This plant is specifically nested within the family Anacardiaceae, which places it in the genus Terminthia, and is identified by the specific epithet paniculata.

According to a study published by "Phytochemistry", Terminthia paniculata is widely used for treating inflammation and rheumatic arthritis in the folk areas of Yunnan province, China. The total extract of the plant was revealed with xanthine oxidase inhibitory activity in vitro and anti-hyperuricemic effect in vivo. Bioassay-guided separation on Fr. A5 yielded six chalcone-flavonone heterodimers known as termipaniculatones A-F. At a concentration of 20 mg/kg, termipaniculatone A showed anti-hyperuricemic effects and anti-inflammatory activity by alleviating paw swelling on MSU-induced mice.
