Trollius ledebourii

Trollius ledebourii, commonly known as the Ledebour's globeflower, is a striking perennial native to mountainous regions. Characterized by its bright, spherical yellow blooms, this plant holds traditional significance in herbal studies. Its unique morphology and resilient nature make it a fascinating subject for both botanists and medicinal researchers alike.

  • Scientific name: Trollius ledebourii
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Trollius.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Northern Asia. Within the vast territory of Siberia, its presence is specifically noted in the Chita region.
