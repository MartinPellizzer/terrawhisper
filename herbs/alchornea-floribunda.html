Alchornea floribunda

Alchornea floribunda, commonly known as the "pau-de-pau" in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical landscapes. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat skin inflammations, digestive ailments, and various infections, offering significant therapeutic potential within ethnobotanical studies and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Alchornea floribunda
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Alchornea floribunda, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malpighiales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and falls under the genus Alchornea.
