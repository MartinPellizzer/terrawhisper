Blechnum moorei

Blechnum moorei, commonly known as the Moorii fern, is a resilient, evergreen plant native to various regions of Africa. Valued in traditional medicine, it is often utilized for its perceived healing properties. This hardy fern thrives in shaded, moist habitats, offering unique botanical significance for both enthusiasts and herbalists.

  • Scientific name: Blechnum moorei
  • Family: Aspleniaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Polypodiinae and order Polypodiales, it is further categorized into the family Aspleniaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Blechnum.

Common Names

This plant, Blechnum moorei, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Moorii fern, while in Afrikaans, it is known as die Moorii-varen.

Distribution

This plant is characterized by a very specific and localized geographical range. Its distribution is primarily restricted to the region of the Southwestern Pacific. Within this vast ocean area, it can be found specifically in New Caledonia. Because of this narrow habitat, the species is considered an endemic element of the local flora. Consequently, its presence is uniquely tied to the particular environmental conditions of this island territory.
