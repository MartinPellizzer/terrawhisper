Asafoetida, scientifically known as Ferula assa-foeida, is a pungent resin derived from the roots of perennial plants in the Apiaceae family. Renowned in Ayurvedic and traditional medicine, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties. This aromatic substance serves as both a culinary staple and a therapeutic agent.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ferula assa-foeida, is a perennial herb highly valued for its complex chemical constituents harvested from various subterranean structures. The medicinal properties are primarily derived from the oleo-gum-resin that exudes from the plant's internal tissues. This substance, often referred to simply as oleo-gum, is collected from the roots and the thick, fleshy rhizome. Depending on the specific method of extraction and the maturity of the plant, the harvested oleo-gum-resin serves as the essential source for traditional and pharmacological applications.
