Ferula assa-foetida

Ferula assa-foeida, commonly known as asafoetida, is a pungent medicinal plant belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its distinct, sulfurous aroma, this resinous gum has been utilized in traditional Ayurvedic and Unani medicine for centuries to treat digestive ailments, respiratory issues, and various inflammatory conditions throughout history.

  • Scientific name: Ferula assa-foetida
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ferula, of which Ferula assa-foetida is a notable species.
