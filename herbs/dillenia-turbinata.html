Dillenia turbinata

What's the taxonomical classification of Dillenia turbinata?

Dillenia turbinata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Magnoliidae, which places it in the order Dilleniales. The plant is a member of the family Dilleniaceae and is further categorized into the genus Dillenia, with its specific identity defined by the species turbinata.
