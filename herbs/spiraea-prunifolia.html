Spiraea prunifolia

Spiraea pruinifolia, commonly known as Prunefolia Spirea, is a resilient medicinal shrub valued in traditional practices. Characterized by its delicate, fragrant blooms and unique leaf structure, this plant offers diverse therapeutic potential. Understanding its botanical properties is essential for exploring its role in natural healing and holistic wellness applications.

  • Scientific name: Spiraea prunifolia
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Spiraea.
