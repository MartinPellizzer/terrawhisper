Phanera strychnifolia

Phanera strychnifolia, commonly known as the Strychnos vine, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Renowned for its potent phytochemical properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including malaria and skin infections. Modern research continues to explore its potential in developing new therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Phanera strychnifolia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phanera.
