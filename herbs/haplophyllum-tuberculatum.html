Haplophyllum tuberculatum

Haplophyllum tuberculatum, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, holds significant ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues, offering a rich source of bioactive phytochemical compounds.

  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa
