Ziziphora

Ziziphora, commonly known as Lemon Balm or Mentha-like herb, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its aromatic properties. Renowned for its soothing scent, it possesses potent antibacterial, antifungal, and antioxidant qualities. Traditionally used to alleviate digestive discomfort and anxiety, this resilient herb remains a staple in natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphora
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is classified under the genus Ziziphora.
