What's the taxonomical classification of Peristrophe bicalyculata?

Peristrophe bicalyculata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It further falls into the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Acanthaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific taxonomic position within the genus Peristrophe as the species bicalyculaata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Peristrophe bicalyculata has a climbing or trailing habit characterized by slender stems, ovate to elliptical leaves with entire margins, and distinctive flowers featuring two separate calyx lobes. The foliage is typically arranged in an opposite pattern and possesses a smooth, somewhat fleshy texture. Its reproductive structures are defined by a tubular corolla that transitions into a spreading limb, often displaying pale colors. The calyx is particularly notable for its division into two distinct parts, which provides the species with its specific epithet. These morphological traits collectively distinguish it from other members of the Convolvulaceae family.
