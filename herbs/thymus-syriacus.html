Thymus syriacus

Origanum syriacum

Thymus syriacus, commonly known as Syrian thyme, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its potent essential oils, this medicinal herb possesses significant antibacterial and antioxidant properties. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health, boost immunity, and alleviate respiratory congestion.

  • Scientific name: Thymus syriacus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Thymus.

Plant Parts

This plant, Thymus syriacus, is highly valued for its medicinal properties, which are primarily concentrated within its small, aromatic leaves. These leaves serve as the main source of the plant's therapeutic potency, containing a rich concentration of essential oils. The extracted essential oil is the most critical component for medicinal use, providing the powerful antiseptic and antimicrobial effects characteristic of the thyme family. Whether utilized in whole form or as a refined essential oil, these botanical elements make the plant a vital resource in traditional herbal medicine.
