Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as winged bean, offers various nutritional and medicinal benefits through its diverse anatomy. The most distinctive feature is its large, green winged pod, which contains the edible contents and is often utilized in culinary applications. Beneath the soil, the plant develops fleshy tuberous roots that serve as energy storage organs. Additionally, the nutrient-dense seeds found within the pods are highly valued for their protein content and are frequently consumed as a vital source of sustenance.
