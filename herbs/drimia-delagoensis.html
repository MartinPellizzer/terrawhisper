Drimia delagoensis

Drimia delagoensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in Southern Africa, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties. Often utilized in traditional healing practices, this species is studied for its potential bioactive compounds. Understanding its botanical characteristics and ethnobotanical uses is essential for advancing modern pharmacological research and conservation.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Within these groupings, it is identified by its specific genus, Drimia.
