What's the taxonomical classification of Rotala rosea?

Rotala rosea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage leading to the order Myrtales. This plant is further categorized under the family Lythraceae, which defines its botanical relationships. Within this family, it is situated in the genus Rotala, with its specific identity being established as the species rosea.
