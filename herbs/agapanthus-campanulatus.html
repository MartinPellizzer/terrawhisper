Distribution

This plant is indigenous to the southern regions of the African continent. Its natural habitat is primarily found within the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. Additionally, the species can be discovered growing in the mountainous terrain of Lesotho. It also maintains a presence throughout the KwaZulu-Natal region. Finally, its distribution extends into the Free State and the Northern Provinces of South Africa.
