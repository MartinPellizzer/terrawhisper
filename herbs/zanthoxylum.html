Distribution

This plant, Zanthoxylum, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Furthermore, its presence extends to the coastal and inland territories of Gambia. Finally, the plant's range reaches into Guinea-Bissau, completing its distribution across these West African nations.
