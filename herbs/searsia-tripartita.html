Searsia tripartita

Searsia tripartiita, commonly known as the Three-leaf Sumac, is a versatile shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments and gastrointestinal issues. Its distinctive trifoliate leaves and hardy nature make it a significant botanical resource in traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Searsia tripartita
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
