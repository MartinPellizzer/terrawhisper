Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Sicilia. Its presence extends significantly into Northern Africa, where it is located in Algeria. Additionally, the species is documented in the North African nations of Libya and Egypt. Finally, its range reaches as far west as Morocco within the same continent.
