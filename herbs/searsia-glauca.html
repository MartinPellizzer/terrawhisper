Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the blue evenstar, utilizes its distinctive foliage for various medicinal applications. The leaf of Searsia glauca is characterized by its trifoliate structure, meaning each leaf is composed of three smaller leaflets that often possess a waxy, glaucous coating. In traditional medicine, these leaves are frequently harvested and prepared as infusions or decoctions to treat ailments such as skin irritations or digestive issues, making the leaf a vital component of the plant's therapeutic profile.
