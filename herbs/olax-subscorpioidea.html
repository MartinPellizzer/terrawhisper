Olax subscorpioidea

Olax subscorpioidea, commonly known as a medicinal shrub within the Olacaceae family, is highly valued in traditional ethnobotany. Native to tropical forest ecosystems, this plant is utilized for its diverse bioactive properties. Researchers are currently exploring its phytochemical potential to discover new therapeutic applications for various health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Olax subscorpioidea
  • Family: Olacaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Olax subscorpioidea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Santalales. It is a member of the family Olacaceae and falls under the genus Olax.
