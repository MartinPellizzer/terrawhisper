Clinopodium menthifolium subsp. ascendens

Clinopodium menthifolium subsp. ascendens, commonly known as climbing pennyroyal, is a distinctive medicinal herb within the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this subspecies is valued in traditional herbalism for its potent essential oils, which possess significant antimicrobial, digestive, and soothing qualities used in various natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Clinopodium menthifolium subsp. ascendens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
