Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found in Burundi and Rwanda. Its range also extends further into the Democratic Republic of the Congo within that same western-central zone. Moving toward Northeast Tropical Africa, the species is present in Djibouti. Finally, its distribution continues through the country of Eritrea.
