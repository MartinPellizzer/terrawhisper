Oxybasis urbica

Oxybasis urbica, commonly known as the urban nettle, is a resilient medicinal plant frequently found in disturbed soils and urban environments. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, it offers significant therapeutic potential. This versatile herb is traditionally utilized to support detoxification and promote overall wellness through its unique phytochemical profile.
