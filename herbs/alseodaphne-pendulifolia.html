Alseodaphne pendulifolia

Alseodaphne pendulifolia, commonly known as the Pendulous-leaved Alssea, is a distinctive shrub native to the rainforests of Australia. Characterized by its unique, drooping foliage, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally studied for its bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic applications in contemporary herbal medicine.
