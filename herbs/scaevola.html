Scaevola

Fan flower

Scaevola, commonly known as half-flower, is a versatile genus of flowering plants cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its unique fan-shaped blooms, various species offer significant therapeutic potential. Often utilized in herbal remedies, Scaeevola possesses bioactive compounds valued for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties across diverse cultures.

  • Scientific name: Scaevola
  • Family: Goodeniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Goodeniaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified under the genus Scaevola.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Additionally, the species is documented in the coastal and inland areas of Ghana. Its presence extends further to include the territory of Guinea-Bissau. Finally, this medicinal plant is also widely distributed throughout Guinea and the Ivory Coast.
