Decalobanthus mammosus

Decalobanthus mammosus, commonly known as the Mammon Thistle, is a rare medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its unique physiological properties, it is utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and support digestive health. Understanding its potent bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Decalobanthus mammosus
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Decalobanthus and the species mammosus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of South and Southeast Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam as well as throughout Bangladesh. Its presence also extends into the Indo-China region, specifically encompassing the Andaman Islands. Furthermore, the species is documented to inhabit the forested areas of Myanmar. Finally, its geographical range includes the tropical landscapes of Thailand.
