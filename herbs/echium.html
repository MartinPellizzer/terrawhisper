Echium

Echium, commonly known as viper's bugloss, is a striking flowering plant cherished for its vibrant blue blossoms and medicinal potential. Historically utilized in traditional herbalism, this resilient species offers various bioactive compounds. Today, it is valued in skincare and wellness for its unique antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties.

  • Scientific name: Echium
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It further descends through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Boraginales, falling into the family Boraginaceae. Ultimately, this medicinal specimen is identified by the genus Echium.
