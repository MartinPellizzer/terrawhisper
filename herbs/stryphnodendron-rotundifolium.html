Stryphnodendron rotundifolium

Stryphnodendron rotundifolium, commonly known as the round-leaf Stryphnodendron, is a resilient shrub native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive issues, offering significant ethnobotanical value for contemporary pharmacological research and herbal studies.
