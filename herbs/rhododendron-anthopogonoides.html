Rhododendron anthopogonoides

Rhododendron anthopogonoides, commonly known as the flower-leaved rhododendron, is a resilient shrub native to high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its striking blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize various parts of the species to treat ailments, highlighting its profound importance in mountain medicine.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron anthopogonoides
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is a member of the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is taxonomically situated within the genus Rhododendron.
