Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Gouldian cherry or pinkwood, possesses several components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The bark is often harvested for its astringent qualities, providing a traditional remedy for various ailments. Additionally, the leaf extract is frequently studied and used for its potential antimicrobial and antioxidant effects. Furthermore, the plant produces a resin that has been traditionally applied in folk medicine to treat skin irritations and wounds.
