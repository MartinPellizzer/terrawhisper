Red luster, scientifically known as Syncarpia glomulifera, is a distinctive Australian rainforest tree prized for its therapeutic potential. Known commonly as the Lollypop Gum, its bark and leaves contain bioactive compounds with potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant promise in traditional medicine and modern pharmacological research.
