Allium elburzense

Allium elburzense, commonly known as the Elburz onion, is a resilient medicinal plant native to the mountainous regions of Iran. Valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses unique bioactive properties. This plant is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic applications, particularly in supporting digestive health and providing antioxidant benefits.
