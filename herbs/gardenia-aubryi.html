Gardenia aubryi

Gardenia aubryi, commonly known as the Aubry Gardenia, is a captivating evergreen shrub prized for its lush foliage and fragrant, creamy white blooms. Native to specific tropical regions, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its therapeutic potential, offering unique botanical properties and aesthetic elegance to landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Gardenia aubryi
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Gentianales and is a member of the Rubiaceae family. Within this family, it is specifically categorized under the genus Gardenia.
