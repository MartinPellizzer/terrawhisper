Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions of the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is noted within the borders of Kenya. Moving toward South Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. The species also extends into the Southern African region, specifically inhabiting the Cape Provinces. Additionally, its geographical range includes the mountainous territory of Lesotho.
