Scutellaria multicaulis

Scutelariia multicaudis, commonly known as multiple-stemmed skullcap, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it serves as a potent agent in treating inflammation, fever, and respiratory ailments. This plant offers significant therapeutic potential for supporting neurological health and overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria multicaulis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls under the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Scutellaria.
