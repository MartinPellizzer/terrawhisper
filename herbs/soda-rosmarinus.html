Soda rosmarinus

Soda rosmarinus, commonly known as rosemary, is a fragrant, woody perennial herb prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential oils, it has been used for centuries to enhance memory, improve digestion, and boost circulation. This versatile medicinal plant offers powerful anti-inflammatory benefits for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Soda rosmarinus
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Amaranthaecae. Within this taxonomic hierarchy, it is specifically identified under the genus Soda.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Salsola rosmarinus, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Sweden, it is identified by its scientific designation used in local contexts as Salsola rosmarinus, while in Persian, it is known as اشنان, and in Arabic, it is called أشنان إكليل الجبل.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is noted within the borders of Egypt. Moving toward Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found on the island of Socotra. The species also extends into Central Asia, specifically inhabiting the territories of Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan. Finally, its range reaches into the mountainous landscapes of Tajikistan.
