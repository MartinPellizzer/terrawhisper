Meniocus linifolius

Meniocus linifolius, commonly known as the Flax-leaf Medic, is a resilient herbaceous plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits, this botanical species offers promising applications in modern herbal pharmacology, supporting holistic wellness through its unique bioactive chemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Meniocus linifolius
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Meniocus.

This plant, Minicous linifolius, is known by several common names depending on the language and regional dialect used. In Chinese, it is referred to as 条叶庭荠, a name that is consistently applied across the zh, zh-hans, and zh-cn language codes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Spain. Moving toward the east, it can be found in Southeastern Europe, spanning from Romania to the European portion of Türkiye. Furthermore, the species inhabits the Northwest Balkan Peninsula as part of its southeastern range. Finally, its distribution extends into Eastern Europe, where it is documented in the Krym region.
