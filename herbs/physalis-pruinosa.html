Physalis pruinosa

Strawberry groundcherry

Physalis pruinosa, commonly known as the groundcherry, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished for its nutritional and therapeutic properties. Native to the Americas, this small shrub produces succulent, golden fruits enclosed in papery husks. Traditionally, it has been utilized to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and bolster immunity.

  • Scientific name: Physalis pruinosa
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Solanales and placed in the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established under the genus Physalis.
