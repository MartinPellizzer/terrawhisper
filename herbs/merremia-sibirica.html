What's the taxonomical classification of Merremia sibirica?

Merremia sibirica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving through the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant is part of the order Solanales and falls within the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is defined by its specific biological classification as belonging to the genus Merremia and the species sibirica.
