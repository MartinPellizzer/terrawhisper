Picrorhiza kurrooa

Picrorhiza kurrooa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (323 studies) · ★★★★★

Picrorhiza kurroa, scientifically known as Picrorhiza kurroa, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the high-altitude Himalayas. Renowned in Ayurveda, it contains bioactive compounds like picroside. This potent plant is primarily valued for its hepatoprotective, cholagogue, and anti-inflammatory properties, serving as a vital remedy for liver ailments.

Plant Parts

This plant, Picrorhiza kurroa, is a perennial herb primarily valued for its medicinal properties found within its underground structures. The most significant part used in traditional medicine is the rhizome, which is often referred to in a singular sense as a rhizome when discussing the specific anatomy of an individual specimen. These fleshy rhizomes are harvested and processed to extract bioactive compounds, making the entire root system a critical component for therapeutic applications. While the roots are the primary focus of collectors, the plant also produces aerial shoots that emerge from the ground to facilitate photosynthesis and growth.
