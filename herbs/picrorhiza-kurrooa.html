Picrorhiza kurrooa

Picrorhiza kurroa, commonly known as Kutki, is a highly valued perennial herb native to the Himalayas. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, it is prized for its potent bitter principles, particularly picroside. This medicinal plant serves as a powerful hepatoprotective agent, effectively supporting liver health and treating various digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Picrorhiza kurrooa
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaeceae, it is classified under the genus Picrorhiza.
