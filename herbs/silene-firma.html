What's the taxonomical classification of Silene firma?

Silene firma belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, the plant is situated in the order Caryophyllales and the family Caryophyllaceae. Finally, it is defined by its specific classification within the genus Silene, reaching its unique designation as the species firma.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Silene firma has a perennial growth habit characterized by erect stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and feature opposite, lanceolate leaves with entire margins. Its flowers are small and actinomorphic, possessing five deeply notched petals that are usually white or pale pink in color. The calyx is tubular and features distinct longitudinal veins that provide structural support to the blooming structure.
