Isatis tinctoria

Dyer's woad

Isatis tinctoria, commonly known as Woad, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its potent antimicrobial properties. Rich in glucosinolates, it has been used traditionally to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and inflammation. Today, it remains a significant botanical resource in both herbal medicine and natural dye production.

  • Scientific name: Isatis tinctoria
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Isatis tinctoria, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Brassicales. Finally, it is a member of the family Brassicaceae and falls under the genus Isatis.
