Tropical Soda Apple, scientifically known as Solanum viarum, is a perennial shrub native to the Americas. Often considered an invasive weed, it produces distinctive yellow fruits. While it possesses traditional medicinal properties in certain cultures, its presence frequently disrupts local ecosystems, making it a significant concern for agricultural management.
