Climbing Orange

Zanthoxylum asiaticum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (103 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Climbing Orange, scientifically known as Zanthoxylum asiaticum, is a versatile medicinal climber native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic, citrus-like leaves, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, respiratory issues, and skin infections. This potent botanical serves as a vital natural remedy worldwide.
