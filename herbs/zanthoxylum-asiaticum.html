Zanthoxylum asiaticum

Zanthoxylum asiaticum, commonly known as Chinese Prickly Ash, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, skin ailments, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum asiaticum
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Zanthoxylum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Burundi, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the northeast, the species is also found within the tropical regions of Ethiopia. Furthermore, its range extends into the territory of Sudan and South Sudan. Together, these locations define the primary habitat for this medicinal species across Africa.
