Nekemias cantoniensis

Nekemias cantoniensis, commonly known as the "climbing nettle," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Nekemias cantoniensis
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Nekemias cantoniensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Vitales and the family Vitaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Nekemias.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several distinct regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is documented across South-Central, Southeast, and the island of Hainan. Furthermore, the species can be found in the high-altitude terrains of Tibet. Beyond mainland China, the plant's distribution extends to the island nation of Japan. Together, these locations highlight its wide ecological footprint throughout the Eastern Asian landscape.
