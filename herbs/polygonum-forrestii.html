Polygonum forrestii

What's the taxonomical classification of Polygonum forrestii?

Polygonum forrestii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales. This plant is further categorized into the family Polygonaceae, which places it within the genus Polygonum, and is ultimately identified by its specific species name, forrestii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Polygonum forrestii has slender, erect stems that reach heights of up to 60 centimeters and bear opposite, lanceolate leaves with entire margins. The leaf blades are typically acute at the apex and exhibit a smooth, glabrous surface texture. Small, inconspicuous flowers are arranged in axillary clusters, often appearing in shades of white or pale pink. These plants frequently feature ocreae, which are membranous sheaths surrounding the stem at the nodes. The reproductive structures culminate in small, dry fruits that characterize the genus.
