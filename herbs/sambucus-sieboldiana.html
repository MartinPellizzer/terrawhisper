Sambucus sieboldiana

Sambucus sieboldiana, commonly known as Siebold's elderberry, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its fragrant white blossoms and dark, nutrient-rich berries, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and alleviate symptoms of seasonal respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Sambucus sieboldiana
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Dipsacales and the family Viburnaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Sambucus as the species Sambucus sieboldiana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several distinct regions of the Northern Hemisphere. In Northern Europe, its presence can be noted within Great Britain. Moving toward the Russian Far East, it is found inhabiting both the Kuril Islands and Sakhalin. The species is also widely distributed throughout Eastern Asia, specifically throughout Japan. Finally, its range extends across the Korean Peninsula.
