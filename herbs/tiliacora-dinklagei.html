Tiliacora dinklagei

Tiliacora dinklagei, commonly known as "Kway Chai" in Southeast Asia, is a climbing woody vine highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this medicinal plant is frequently used to treat various ailments, including fever, skin issues, and digestive disorders through its bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Africa
