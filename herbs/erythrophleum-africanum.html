African blackwood, scientifically known as Erythrophleum africanum, is a highly prized timber species native to various African regions. Beyond its value in woodworking, this versatile plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its profound ecological significance.
