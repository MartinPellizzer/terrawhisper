Plant Parts

This plant, Marrubium astracanicum subsp. macrodon, is characterized by its distinct foliage, which serves as the primary source of its medicinal properties. The leaves are typically arranged in opposite pairs along the stem, exhibiting a rugose or wrinkled texture on the upper surface. They are often covered in fine, grayish-white hairs that give the plant a soft, pubescent appearance, and they possess a characteristic aromatic scent when crushed. In the context of herbal medicine, these leaves are the most vital part of the plant, containing the essential compounds used to treat various respiratory ailments.
