Aristolochia sempervirens

Aristolochia sempervirens, commonly known as green aristolochia, is a perennial climbing vine native to North America. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to respiratory issues, it contains potent alkaloids. However, caution is vital, as its compounds are linked to significant nephrotoxicity and carcinogenic risks.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia sempervirens
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Piperales and the family Aristolochiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Aristolochia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions in Europe. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in France, Spain, and the island of Sardegna. Moving toward the Balkan Peninsula, its presence extends into Southeastern Europe. Specifically, the species is documented within the borders of Albania. Finally, its geographical range also includes the country of Greece.
