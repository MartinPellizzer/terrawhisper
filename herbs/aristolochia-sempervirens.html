Long birthwort, scientifically known as Aristolochia sempervirens, is a perennial climbing vine native to North America. Characterized by its heart-shaped leaves and tubular flowers, this plant has a storied history in traditional medicine. However, modern science warns of its potent nephrotoxicity, necessitating extreme caution regarding its medicinal use.
