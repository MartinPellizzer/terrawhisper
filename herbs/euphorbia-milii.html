Crown of thorns, scientifically known as Euphorbia milii, is a captivating succulent shrub prized for its vibrant, long-lasting bracts. Native to Madagascar, this resilient plant features distinctive thorny stems and produces nectar-rich blooms. While popular in ornamental gardening, its milky sap requires careful handling due to its irritating properties.
