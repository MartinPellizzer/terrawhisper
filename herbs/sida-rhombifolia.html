Sida rhombifolia

Sida rhombifolia, commonly known as paddy manshade or arrowleaf sida, is a versatile medicinal herb found across tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and analgesic properties. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, this resilient plant remains a vital subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Sida rhombifolia
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Sida.

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several European regions. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in Belgium, Germany, and the areas encompassing Czechia and Slovakia.
