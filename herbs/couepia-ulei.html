Couepia ulei

Couepia ulei, commonly known as the "clove tree" in certain regions, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical rainforests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammation, offering promising potential for modern drug development and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Couepia ulei
  • Family: Chrysobalanaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant, known scientifically as Couepia ulei, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
