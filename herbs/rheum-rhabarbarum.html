Rheum rhabarbarum

Rheum rhabarbarum, commonly known as rhubarb, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its potent therapeutic properties. Primarily utilized in traditional medicine, its roots contain anthraquinone glycosides, which act as effective laxatives. Beyond digestive support, rhubarb offers antioxidant benefits, making it a significant botanical resource in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Rheum rhabarbarum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, its taxonomic lineage places it in the family Polygonaceae and the genus Rheum.
