rhubarb

Rheum rhabarbarum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (782 studies) · ★★★★★

Rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum rhabarbuum, is a perennial plant valued both culinarily and medicinally. Renowned for its potent rhizomes, it contains anthraquinone glycosides that serve as powerful natural laxatives. Traditionally used to stimulate digestion and alleviate constipation, this versatile plant remains a staple in herbal pharmacopoeia worldwide.

Moving eastward, the species can be found within the vast landscapes of Central European Russia.
