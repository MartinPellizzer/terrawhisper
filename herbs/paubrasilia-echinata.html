Paubrasilia echinata

Paubrasilia echinata, commonly known as Brazilwood, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its bioactive compounds. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional practices to support immune health and skin vitality, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and therapeutic healing.

  • Scientific name: Paubrasilia echinata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed by the genus Paubrasilia.
