Bupleurum salicifolium

Bupleurum salicifolium, commonly known as Chinese Thoroughwort, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, it contains bioactive compounds like saikosaponins, which exhibit potent anti-inflammatory, hepatoprotective, and immunomodulatory properties. This resilient plant serves as a vital botanical resource for managing various systemic inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum salicifolium
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Bupleurum as the species Bupleurum salicifolium.
