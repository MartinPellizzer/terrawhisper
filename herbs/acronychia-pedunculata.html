Acronychia pedunculata

Acronychia pedunculata, commonly known as the climbing milkweed, is a versatile medicinal climber found across tropical regions. Renowned for its milky latex, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential within ethnobotanical studies and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Acronychia pedunculata
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Acronychia.
