Matricaria chamomilla

Chamomile

Matricaria chamomilla, commonly known as German chamomile, is a cherished medicinal herb celebrated for its soothing properties. Renowned for its delicate, daisy-like flowers, this plant offers potent anti-inflammatory and sedative benefits. Widely used in teas and essential oils, it effectively eases digestive discomfort, reduces anxiety, and promotes restful sleep.

  • Scientific name: Matricaria chamomilla
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Matricaria chamomilla, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asterales. Finally, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Matricaria.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Matricaria chamomilla, is recognized by various traditional and regional names across the globe. In German-speaking or international contexts, it is often referred to by its botanical associations, but in specific languages, it carries unique identities: in Luxembourgish, it is called Kaméil, while in Chinese, it is known as 德國洋甘菊. Moving through Europe, it is referred to as rumianek pospolity in Polish, Kamamila in Basque, and orvosi székfű in Hungarian.
