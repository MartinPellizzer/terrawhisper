Chamomile, scientifically known as Matricaria chamomilla, is a beloved medicinal herb celebrated for its soothing properties. Renowned for its delicate, daisy-like flowers, this plant is widely used in herbal teas and essential oils to reduce anxiety, aid digestion, and promote restful sleep, making it a staple in natural wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Matricaria chamomilla, is primarily utilized for its therapeutic properties found within its various aerial parts. The most significant medicinal component is found in the flowers, which are characterized by a distinct structure featuring yellow disc florets surrounded by prominent white ray florets. These blossoms are borne upon slender, herbaceous stems that support the overall growth of the plant's shoots.
