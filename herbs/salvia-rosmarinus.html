Salvia rosmarinus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1838 studies) · ★★★★★

Rosmarinus officiinalis, scientifically known as Salvia rosmarinus, is a perennial aromatic shrub revered for its therapeutic properties. Native to the Mediterranean, this hardy herb is rich in antioxidants and essential oils. Traditionally used to enhance memory, aid digestion, and improve circulation, it remains a cornerstone of holistic herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Salvia rosmarinus, is highly valued in herbal medicine primarily for its aromatic aerial parts. The most significant component for therapeutic use is the leaf, as the dense, needle-like leaves contain a high concentration of bioactive compounds. When processed, these parts yield potent essential oils that are used for their stimulating and antiseptic properties. The extraction of this essential oil allows for a concentrated application of the plant's medicinal benefits, making the entire upper structure of the herb a vital resource in aromatherapy and traditional healing.
