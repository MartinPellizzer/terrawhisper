Echinacea tennesseensis

Tennessee purple coneflower

Echinacea tennesseensis, commonly known as Tennessee coneflower, is a rare perennial herb native to the southeastern United States. Distinguished by its striking purple blooms and prominent central cones, this medicinal plant belongs to the Asteraceae family, valued for its potential therapeutic properties and its significant role in regional biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Echinacea tennesseensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Echinacea tennesseensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Echinacea.
