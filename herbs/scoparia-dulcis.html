Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae, it is classified under the genus Scoparia. Specifically, it is known as Scoparia dulcis, a member of this distinct taxonomic lineage.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across multiple continents and climate zones. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented within the regions of Czechia and Slovakia. Moving toward the tropical belt, the species is widely found throughout West Tropical Africa. Specific occurrences have been recorded in the nations of Benin, Burkina, and Gambia. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the West Tropical African territory of Ghana.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Scoparia dulcis, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The green leaves are perhaps the most commonly used part, as each individual leaf contains bioactive compounds essential for traditional healing. Beyond the foliage, the roots are also harvested to prepare specialized decoctions. In modern pharmacology, researchers often focus on the potency of the plant extract derived from the entire organism, as well as the specific concentrated leaf extract, to study its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects.
