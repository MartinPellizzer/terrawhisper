Phaleria macrocarpa

Phaleria macrocarpa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (60 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Scientifically known as Phaleria macrocarpa, is a medicinal shrub native to Indonesia, commonly referred to as Mahkota Dewa. Renowned for its powerful antioxidant properties, the plant's fruits and leaves are traditionally used to treat various ailments, including inflammation, diabetes, and hypertension, offering significant potential in modern herbal pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning across parts of Southeast Asia and Oceania. Within the Indo-China region, its presence can be identified in the Nicobar Islands. Moving further east into the Papuasia region, the species is also found in New Guinea. These distinct locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse tropical island environments. Consequently, its natural range is characterized by these specific maritime and mainland territories.
