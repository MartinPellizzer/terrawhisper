Berberis koreana

Korean barberry

Berberis koreana, commonly known as Korean barberry, is a deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant seasonal foliage and delicate yellow blooms, this ornamental species is increasingly valued in medicinal traditions. It contains bioactive compounds that offer potential therapeutic benefits, particularly for supporting metabolic health and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Berberis koreana
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Berberis koreanana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Berberidaceae and falls under the genus Berberis.
