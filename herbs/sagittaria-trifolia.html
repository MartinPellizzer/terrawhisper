Chinese arrowhead, scientifically known as Sagittaria trifolia, is a perennial aquatic herb native to East Asia. Characterized by its distinctive arrow-shaped leaves, this plant grows in wetlands and marshes. Renowned in traditional medicine, its tubers are harvested for their nutritional value and various therapeutic applications in folk remedies.
