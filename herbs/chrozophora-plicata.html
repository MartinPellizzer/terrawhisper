Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Egypt. Moving towards the West Tropical African region, it can be found in Burkina, Mali, and Mauritania. Additionally, the species extends its range further south into Nigeria. Together, these locations define the primary geographical spread of Chrozophora plicata.
