Angel's-Trumpets, scientifically known as Datura ferox, is a potent medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its large, trumpet-shaped flowers, it contains powerful alkaloids like scopolamine. While utilized in traditional medicine for pain relief and sedation, its extreme toxicity requires cautious handling and expert pharmacological knowledge.

Plant Parts

This plant, Datura ferox, is known for its potent medicinal and toxic properties, which are primarily concentrated in its various anatomical structures. While the leaves, flowers, and roots all contain bioactive alkaloids, the seeds are particularly significant due to their high concentration of tropane alkaloids. These seeds, which are typically contained within spiny capsules, are the most frequently utilized part in traditional pharmacological applications, though they must be handled with extreme caution because of their intense physiological effects.
