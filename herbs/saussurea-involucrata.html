Saussurea involucrata

Saussurea involucrata, commonly known as Snow Lotus, is a rare medicinal herb flourishing in the high-altitude alpine regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this precious plant is highly valued for treating respiratory ailments, boosting immunity, and promoting overall vitality.

  • Scientific name: Saussurea involucrata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Saussurea.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the rugged high-altitude regions of northern and central Asia. In the vast expanse of Siberia, its presence is documented in the Altai and Tuva regions. Moving southward into Central Asia, the species occupies diverse mountainous landscapes. It can be specifically located within the territories of Kazakhstan and Kyrgyzstan. Additionally, its distribution extends into the unique environments of Uzbekistan.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Saussurea involucrata, is a remarkable species among high-altitude wild plants that thrives in extreme environments. When harvested for its medicinal properties, the whole grass is often utilized, though the most potent therapeutic compounds are concentrated within its specialized roots. Recent studies into its biological efficacy have focused on the complex polysaccharides found within its tissues, which contribute to its significant pharmacological value. Furthermore, because of its slow growth and ecological sensitivity, researchers are exploring the cultivation of regenerated plantlets to ensure a sustainable supply without depleting natural populations.
