Acer pseudoplatanus

Acer pseudoplatanoide, commonly known as the False Sycamore, is a deciduous tree valued for its striking foliage and architectural form. While primarily ornamental, various parts of the plant have historical roles in traditional medicine. This species is characterized by its rapid growth and adaptability to diverse environmental conditions.

  • Scientific name: Acer pseudoplatanus
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it falls under the family Sapindaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Acer.

Moving toward the center of the continent, its distribution reaches into Middle Europe. Specifically, this medicinal plant is documented to inhabit the mountainous and temperate regions of Austria.
