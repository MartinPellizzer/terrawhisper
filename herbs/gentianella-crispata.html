Gentianella crispata

Gentianeella crispata, commonly known as the curly-leaved gentian, is a resilient perennial herb native to alpine and subalpine regions. Characterized by its distinctively curled leaves and delicate bell-shaped flowers, this botanical species thrives in rocky terrains, playing a vital role in high-altitude ecosystems through its unique medicinal properties.

  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales. Finally, it resides in the family Gentianaceae under the genus Gentianella.
