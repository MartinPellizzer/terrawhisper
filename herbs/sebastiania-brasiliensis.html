Sebastiania brasiliensis

Sebastiania brasiliensis, commonly known as "erva-de-vassoura" in Brazil, is a significant medicinal shrub native to South America. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat skin inflammations, respiratory ailments, and digestive issues, offering valuable phytochemical compounds for contemporary pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Sebastiania brasiliensis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Sebastiania.
