Cnidium japonicum

Cnidium japonicum, commonly known as "Chuan Chuan Niu" in Traditional Chinese Medicine, is a perennial herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and coumarins, it is primarily used to alleviate pain, improve blood circulation, and treat various skin conditions, making it a vital botanical in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Cnidium japonicum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae and is identified by the genus Cnidium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Cnidium japonicum, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Chinese, it is known as 滨蛇床, with the specific forms 滨蛇床 used in mainland China (zh-cn) and 濱蛇床 used in Taiwan (zh-tw). In Japan, the plant is called ハマゼリ (ja), while in Korea, it is known as 갯사상자 (ko).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of Eastern Asia. Within China, it is primarily found growing in the Manchuria region. Its range extends through the Korean Peninsula, where it is also commonly documented. In the maritime areas of Eastern Asia, the species can be located in Japan. Furthermore, its geographical presence includes the remote Ogasawara-shoto islands.
