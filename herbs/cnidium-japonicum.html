Cnidium japonicum, scientifically known as Cnidium japonicum, is a versatile perennial herb highly valued in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, it is frequently used to treat skin conditions, joint pain, and circulatory issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of Eastern Asia. Within China, it is primarily found growing in the Manchuria region. Its range extends through the Korean Peninsula, where it is also commonly documented. In the maritime areas of Eastern Asia, the species can be located in Japan. Furthermore, its geographical presence includes the remote Ogasawara-shoto islands.
