Lungwort, scientifically known as Pulmonaria officinalis, is a perennial herb celebrated in traditional herbalism for its respiratory benefits. Named for its spotted leaves resembling lung tissue, it has historically been used to soothe coughs and support bronchial health, making it a cornerstone of ancient botanical medicine and apothecary traditions.
