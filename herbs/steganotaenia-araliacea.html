Steganotaenia araliacea

Steganotaenia araliacea, commonly known as the False Aralia, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Found primarily in tropical regions, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Often utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities, this plant offers promising therapeutic potential for treating various ailments and supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Steganotaenia araliacea
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
