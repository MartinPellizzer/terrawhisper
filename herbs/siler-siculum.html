Siler siculum

Siler siculum, commonly known as Sicilian Siler, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Valued in traditional herbalism, this plant is recognized for its unique flowering structure and potential therapeutic properties. It serves as an important subject for botanical study and regional biodiversity conservation efforts.

  • Scientific name: Siler siculum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Siler siculum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales. It is further categorized into the family Apiaceae and the genus Siler.

This plant exhibits a very restricted and specialized geographical range. Its presence is primarily limited to the specific region of Southeastern Europe. Consequently, its entire natural distribution is confined to these specific Mediterranean territories.
