What's the taxonomical classification of Itea macrophylla?

Itea macrophylla belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the order Saxifragales and is a member of the family Iteaecae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Itea, with its unique designation being the species macrophylla.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Itea macrophylla has an erect, deciduous shrub habit characterized by slender, woody stems and large, simple, elliptical leaves that measure up to 15 centimeters in length. During the flowering season, small, white, four-petaled flowers emerge in terminal racemes. These blooms are followed by small, globose capsules that serve as the plant's primary fruit structure.
