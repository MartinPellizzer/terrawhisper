Passiflora perfoliata

Passiflora perfoliata, commonly known as the leaf-shaped passionflower, is a climbing vine native to the tropical regions of the Americas. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, this medicinal plant is frequently utilized in traditional herbalism to alleviate anxiety, promote restful sleep, and support overall nervous system health through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Passiflora perfoliata
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Passiflora perfoliata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Passifloraceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Passiflora.
