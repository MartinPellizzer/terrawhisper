Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Acacia, is highly valued in traditional medicine due to the diverse therapeutic properties found within its various components. The leaves are often utilized for their soothing properties, while the herbs or entire aerial parts of certain species are processed to create medicinal infusions. One of its most significant contributions is the production of gums, specifically the thick, resinous gum known as gum arabic, which is widely used as a stabilizer and soothing agent. Furthermore, certain species yield precious seed oils that are extracted for their nourishing and healing applications in topical treatments.
