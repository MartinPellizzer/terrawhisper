Acacia

Wattles

Acacia, commonly known as wattle, is a diverse genus of trees and shrubs renowned for their resilience and medicinal properties. Found across various climates, these plants offer therapeutic benefits through their bark, leaves, and resins, which are traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Acacia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Acacia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Acacia, is highly valued in traditional medicine due to the diverse therapeutic properties found within its various components. The leaves are often utilized for their soothing properties, while the herbs or entire aerial parts of certain species are processed to create medicinal infusions. One of its most significant contributions is the production of gums, specifically the thick, resinous gum known as gum arabic, which is widely used as a stabilizer and soothing agent. Furthermore, certain species yield precious seed oils that are extracted for their nourishing and healing applications in topical treatments.
