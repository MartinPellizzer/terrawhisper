Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within Eastern Asia. It can be found growing in the mountainous areas of Japan and the Korean Peninsula. Additionally, its range extends to the island of Taiwan. In mainland China, the species is prevalent throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories.
