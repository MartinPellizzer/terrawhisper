Psidium cattleyanum

Psidium cattleianum

Psidium cattleyanum, commonly known as the Guava plant, is a versatile tropical species celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential vitamins and antioxidants, its leaves and fruits are utilized in traditional medicine to support digestive health, boost immunity, and provide anti-inflammatory benefits, making it a botanical powerhouse.

  • Scientific name: Psidium cattleyanum
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Psidium cattleyanum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification concludes within the genus Psidium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several distinct regions. In the Macaronesia archipelago, it can be found growing in the Azores and Madeira. Its distribution extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, specifically within the country of Senegal. Furthermore, the species is present in West-Central Tropical Africa, occupying territories such as Gabon. Finally, it is also documented throughout the Gulf of Guinea Islands within the same West-Central region.
