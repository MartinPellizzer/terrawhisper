Aloe chabaudii

Aloe chabaudii, commonly known as a rare succulent from the Aloe genus, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique botanical properties. Native to specific arid regions, this species offers intriguing potential in traditional healing practices, primarily utilized for its soothing skin benefits and restorative bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Aloe chabaudii
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asphodelaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Aloe.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving to East Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in Tanzania. The species also extends into South Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting the nation of Malawi. Furthermore, its range in South Tropical Africa includes the countries of Mozambique and Zambia.
