Corydalis diphylla subsp. diphylla

Corydalis diphylla subsp. diphylla, commonly known as Corydalis, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Rich in isoquinoline alkaloids, it is primarily utilized for its potent analgesic and sedative properties. This botanical species plays a crucial role in treating pain, inflammation, and various neurological discomforts.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis diphylla subsp. diphylla
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Papaveraceae. Specifically, it is identified as a member of the genus Corydalis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the diverse landscapes of the Indian Subcontinent. Its natural range includes the rugged and mountainous terrain of Nepal. Additionally, it can be found growing in parts of Pakistan. The species is also well-documented throughout the West Himalaya region. These specific locations define the primary geographical distribution of Corydalis diphylla subsp. diphylla.
