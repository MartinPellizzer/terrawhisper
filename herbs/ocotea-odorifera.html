Ocotea odorifera, scientifically known as Ocotea odorifera, is a highly aromatic evergreen tree native to the Brazilian Atlantic Forest. Renowned for its precious essential oil, extracted from its fragrant leaves, it possesses significant medicinal properties, including antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant activities, making it a vital botanical resource.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Canela or Brazilian Cinnamon, derives its medicinal potency primarily from its aromatic components. The leaves are highly valued for their essential oils, which are often harvested for their therapeutic and fragrant properties. Additionally, the branches contain significant concentrations of these bioactive compounds, making them a vital part of the plant used in traditional applications.
