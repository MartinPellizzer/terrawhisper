Ocotea odorifera, scientifically known as Ocotea odorifera, is a highly aromatic evergreen tree native to the Brazilian Atlantic Forest. Renowned for its precious essential oil, extracted from its fragrant leaves, it possesses significant medicinal properties, including antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant activities, making it a vital botanical resource.
