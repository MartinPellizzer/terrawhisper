Ocotea odorifera

Ocotea odorifera, commonly known as Canela-sassafras, is a precious aromatic tree native to Brazil's Atlantic Forest. Renowned for its fragrant essential oils, it possesses significant medicinal properties, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and sedative effects. This valuable species is widely utilized in traditional healing and the natural perfumery industry.

  • Scientific name: Ocotea odorifera
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Laurales, it is a member of the Lauraceae family and is specifically classified under the genus Ocotea.
