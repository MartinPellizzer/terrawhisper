Capsicum annuum

Capsicum annuum, commonly known as chili peppers or bell peppers, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its bioactive compounds. Rich in capsaicin, it offers potent anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and antioxidant properties. Widely used in traditional medicine, this species plays a crucial role in managing pain and supporting metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Capsicum annuum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Capsicum.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across various regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, it can be found in both Great Britain and Ireland.
