Calanthe discolor

Calanthe discolor, commonly known as Calathea, is a stunning tropical plant celebrated for its vibrant, patterned foliage. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant cultural importance. In various traditional practices, its leaves are utilized for their potential medicinal properties, offering a unique blend of aesthetics and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Calanthe discolor
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Calanthe, with its species name being discolor.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Calanthhe discolor, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Japanese, it is called エビネ (ebine), while in Korean, it is known as 새우난초 (saeu-nancho). In Chinese, the names used are 虾脊兰 (xiājǐlán) for general usage and simplified Chinese (zh-cn), and 蝦脊蘭 for traditional Chinese (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant is naturally distributed across several key regions within Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species also extends its range into the nation of Japan. Furthermore, it can be found growing within the Korean peninsula. Finally, its geographical reach includes the Nansei-shoto islands.
