Rhipsalis baccifera baccifera, scientifically known as Rhipsalis baccifera subsp. baccifera, is a unique epiphytic cactus native to tropical regions. Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions within West Tropical Africa. Its geographical range extends through the coastal and inland territories of Benin and Ghana. Furthermore, the species can be found growing within the borders of Guinea. The distribution continues southward into the lush environments of Ivory Coast. Finally, its presence is also documented within the country of Liberia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Rhipsalis baccifera subsp. baccifera, is a unique epiphytic cactus characterized by its pendulous, cylindrical stem segments. In various traditional medicinal applications, the whole plant is often utilized due to its diverse physiological properties. Practitioners may prepare a decoction using the whole herb to address specific ailments, ensuring that all succulent structures are included. For certain preparations requiring immediate potency, the use of fresh herbs is preferred to maintain the integrity of its active compounds. Additionally, while much of the plant consists of fleshy stems, the bark or outer epidermal layer of the stem segments can be specifically targeted for certain topical or systemic treatments.
