Rhipsalis baccifera subsp. baccifera

Rhipsalis baccifera subsp. baccifera, commonly known as the Mistletoe Cactus, is a unique epiphytic succulent native to tropical regions. While celebrated for its ornamental beauty and cascading stems, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Its specialized biology offers intriguing possibilities for medicinal research and traditional healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Rhipsalis baccifera subsp. baccifera
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is a member of the family Cactaceae. Specifically, it is identified as a member of the genus Rhipsalis, categorized under the subspecies baccifera.
