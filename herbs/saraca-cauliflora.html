Saraca cauliflora, is a significant medicinal shrub native to the tropical forests of South Asia. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this plant is primarily valued for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Its various parts are frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, wounds, and various inflammatory conditions.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Asian continent. Within the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is documented in India, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka. It also extends into the Indo-China region, where it can be found in Myanmar. Additionally, the species is known to grow in the country of Cambodia. These diverse locations highlight the broad geographical range of Saraca cauliflora.
