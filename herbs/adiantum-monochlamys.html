Adiantum monochlamys

Adiantum monochlamys, commonly known as the single-flowered maidenhair fern, is a delicate medicinal plant cherished in traditional healing practices. Valued for its unique botanical properties, it is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, offering a natural approach to wellness through its potent, bioactive herbal compounds.

  • Scientific name: Adiantum monochlamys
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Polypodiinae and order Polypodiales, it is a member of the family Pteridaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Adiantum.
