Fraxinus uhdei

Fraxinus uhdei, commonly known as the Uhde's ash, is a botanical species valued for its unique medicinal potential. Often utilized in traditional herbal practices, its bark and leaves contain bioactive compounds believed to possess anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering a natural approach to supporting holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Fraxinus uhdei
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Oleaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Fraxinus.
