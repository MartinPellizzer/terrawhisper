Fraxinus mandshurica

Fraxinus mandschurica

Fraxinus mandshurica, commonly known as the Manchurian ash, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, promote circulation, and alleviate pain. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential for various holistic health treatments and remedies.

  • Scientific name: Fraxinus mandshurica
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Oleaceae and the genus Fraxinus.

Distribution

This plant is naturally distributed across several key regions within the Russian Far East. It can be found growing in the Amur region, where it establishes itself in local ecosystems. Its range extends further into the Khabarovsk territory and throughout the Primorye area. Additionally, the species inhabits the islands of the Kuril archipelago. Finally, its geographical presence is also confirmed on the island of Sakhalin.
