Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of Central Asia. Within this region, it can be found growing in the territories of Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, and Uzbekistan. Its range extends further west into the mountainous terrain of Afghanistan. Additionally, the species reaches into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting parts of Pakistan.
