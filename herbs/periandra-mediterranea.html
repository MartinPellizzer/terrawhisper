Brazilian Licorice, scientifically known as Periandra mediterranea, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Atlantic Forest. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This botanical treasure offers significant pharmacological potential for modern natural medicine and wellness.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in South America. In the western part of the continent, it can be found within the borders of Bolivia.
