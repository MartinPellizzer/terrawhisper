Mexican Giant Horsetail, scientifically known as Equisetum myriochaetum, is a vigorous perennial celebrated for its unique, segmented structure. Rich in silica, this botanical wonder has been traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support bone health, skin integrity, and kidney function, offering a potent natural remedy within diverse ecosystems.

Plant Parts

This plant, Equisetum myriochaetum, is characterized by its unique morphology, where the medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in its aerial parts. These above-ground structures consist of specialized, photosynthetic stems that lack true leaves, instead featuring small, scale-like structures at the nodes. The aerial parts are known for their high silica content and distinctive jointed appearance, making them the primary component utilized in various traditional applications.
