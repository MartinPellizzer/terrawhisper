Equisetum myriochaetum

Equisetum myriochaetum, commonly known as a specialized variety of horsetail, is a resilient perennial valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its unique segmented stems and high silica content, this plant is frequently utilized for its astringent properties, supporting skin health, bone strengthening, and various restorative therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Equisetum myriochaetum
  • Family: Equisetaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Equisetidae and the order Equisetales, it falls under the family Equisetaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Equisetum as the species Equisetum myriochaetum.
