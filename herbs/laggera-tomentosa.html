Laggera tomentosa

Laggera tomentosa, commonly known as the velvet-leafed plant, is a significant medicinal species valued for its therapeutic properties. Often found in tropical regions, this plant is traditionally used to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a subject of interest in ethnobotanical research and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Laggera tomentosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Within this family, it is specifically categorized under the genus Laggera.
