Jasminum duclouxii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Lamiales and is a member of the family Oleaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Jasminum, with its specific designation being the species duclouxii.
