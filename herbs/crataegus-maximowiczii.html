What's the taxonomical classification of Crataegus maximowiczii?

Crataegus maximowiczii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It follows the order Rosales and falls under the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Crataegus, and its specific species name, maximowiczii.
