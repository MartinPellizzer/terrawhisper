Crataegus maximowiczii

Crataegus maximowiczii, commonly known as the Maximowicz hawthorn, is a resilient deciduous shrub valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this species is frequently utilized to support cardiovascular health, improve circulation, and manage blood pressure, making it a significant plant in various herbal healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus maximowiczii
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Crataegus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range that stretches across various regions of Northern Asia. In Eastern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within Central European Russia. Moving eastward into Siberia, the species can be found in the territories of Buryatiya, Chita, and Irkutsk. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the Russian Far East, particularly within the Amur region. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive across significant stretches of the Russian landscape.
