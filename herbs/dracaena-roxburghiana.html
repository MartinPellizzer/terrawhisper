Dracaena roxburghiana

Dracaena roxburgiana, commonly known as the Dragon Tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Asian forests. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its leaves and roots are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to skin disorders, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Dracaena roxburghiana
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
