This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within several key regions of East Asia. Plant Parts

This plant, Pericampylus glaucus, is a climbing shrub characterized by several distinct morphological features used in traditional medicine. The stems are slender and twining in nature, allowing the plant to ascend through surrounding vegetation. Its leaves are simple, alternate, and typically possess a leathery texture. The aerial parts of the plant, which include the foliage and reproductive structures, are often the focus of botanical study. The fruits are small, fleshy berries that develop from the flowering branches. Beneath the soil, the roots serve as the primary anchoring and nutrient-absorbing system, though in many medicinal preparations, the chemical properties of the various organs are carefully evaluated for their therapeutic potential.
