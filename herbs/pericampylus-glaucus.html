Native Grapes, scientifically known as Pericampylus glaucus, is a climbing woody vine primarily found in the tropical regions of Southeast Asia. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions through its unique bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within several key regions of East Asia. Furthermore, it can be found within the Nansei-shoto islands of Eastern Asia. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where Pericampylus glaucus thrives.
