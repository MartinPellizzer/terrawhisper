Eumachia montana

Eumachia montana, commonly known as mountain euomacha, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly in China, it is frequently utilized for its digestive benefits. Rich in bioactive compounds, this plant is studied for its potential to support liver health and metabolic functions.

  • Scientific name: Eumachia montana
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Gentianaales and is a member of the Rubiaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Eumachia, with its species name being Eumachia montana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical presence across several key regions in Asia. In China, its distribution is primarily concentrated within the Southeast territories. The species is also well-established in the Indian Subcontinent, specifically found in Assam, Bangladesh, and the East Himalaya regions. Furthermore, it can be located within the Indo-China area, including the Andaman Islands. Together, these locations highlight its wide ecological range across various subtropical landscapes.
