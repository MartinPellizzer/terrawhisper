Narcissus tazetta

Narcissus tazzetta

Narcissus tazetta, commonly known as the Bunching Daffodil, is a fragrant perennial bulb celebrated for its clusters of small, star-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant traditional value in various cultures, where its unique botanical properties are explored for potential medicinal applications and aromatic uses.

  • Scientific name: Narcissus tazetta
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Amaryllidaceae and the genus Narcissus.
