Plant Parts

This plant, Narcissus tazetta, is a perennial herb characterized by its distinct morphology, where the most significant medicinal and structural component is the underground bulb. During its blooming season, the plant produces clusters of delicate flowers, often arranged in an umbel-like structure atop a leafless stem. Each individual flower typically features a small central cup surrounded by petal-like segments, creating a striking visual display. Above the ground, the aerial parts consist primarily of long, green, strap-shaped leaves that facilitate photosynthesis and support the plant's overall growth cycle.
