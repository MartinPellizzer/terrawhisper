What's the taxonomical classification of Dichroa daimingshanensis?

Dichroa daimingshanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It is further categorized under the family Hydrangeaceae, falling into the genus Dichroa, with the specific designation of the species daimingshanensis.
