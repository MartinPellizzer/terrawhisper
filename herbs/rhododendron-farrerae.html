What's the taxonomical classification of Rhododendron farrerae?

Rhododendron farrerae belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Ericaceae, falling under the genus Rhododendron, and is specifically identified by its species name, ferrerae.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Its inflorescence consists of terminal clusters of bell-shaped flowers that transition in color from pale pink to deep crimson. The stems are sturdy and often develop a thickened, bark-covered trunk as the plant matures into a large shrub or small tree.
