Acacia mangium

Hickory wattle

Acacia mangium, commonly known as the brown paperbark acacia, is a versatile tropical tree valued for both forestry and medicinal purposes. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential in natural pharmacology and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Acacia mangium
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Acacia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Acacia mangium, utilizes its leaves as a primary component in various traditional medicinal applications. These bipinnate leaves are often harvested and processed to create decoctions or infusions, which are used in various cultures to treat ailments such as skin inflammations, digestive issues, or to reduce fever. Beyond their therapeutic properties, the leaves also play a crucial role in the plant's biological processes, contributing to the rapid growth and nitrogen-fixing capabilities that make this species so resilient in diverse environments.
