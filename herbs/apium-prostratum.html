Apium prostratum

Sea celery

Apium prostratum, commonly known as prostrate celery, is a unique herbaceous plant belonging to the Apiaceae family. Often found in coastal or wetland habitats, it is distinguished by its low-growing, spreading habit. This medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its diverse bioactive properties and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Apium prostratum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Its taxonomic placement is completed by its designation within the genus Apium.
