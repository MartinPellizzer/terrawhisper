Sea Celery, scientifically known as Apium prostratum, is a resilient coastal herb found in saline environments. Renowned for its unique nutritional profile, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution that spans across two different continents. In Southern Africa, it can be found growing within the Cape Provinces and throughout Namibia. Its presence extends to the Australian continent as well, specifically inhabiting the regions of New South Wales and Queensland. Additionally, the species is documented on the remote Norfolk Island.
