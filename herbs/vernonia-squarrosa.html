Vernonia squarrosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Vernonia squarrosa?

Vernonia squarrosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the taxonomic order Asterales. The plant is a prominent representative of the family Asteraceae, which is characterized by its composite flower structures. Within this family, it is categorized under the genus Vernonia, and its specific identification is determined by its species name, squarrosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Vernonia squarrosa has an erect, herbaceous habit characterized by stem heights ranging from 0.5 to 1.5 meters and leaves that are lanceolate to narrowly ovate with serrated margins. The stems are typically unbranched and may exhibit varying degrees of pubescence depending on the specific population. Its foliage is arranged oppositely along the length of the stem, often tapering toward the base. The inflorescence consists of numerous small, discoid flower heads clustered in terminal corymbiform cymes. These flowers are typically lavender to light purple in color and are supported by involucral bracts that are often spreading or squarrose.
