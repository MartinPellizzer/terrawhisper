Ziziphus abyssinica

Ziziphus abyssiniaca, commonly known as the Ethiopian jujube, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to East Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
