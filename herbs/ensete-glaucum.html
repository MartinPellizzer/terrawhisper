Ensete glaucum

Ensete glaucum, commonly known as the False Banana, is a vital perennial plant native to East Africa. Renowned for its large, paddle-shaped leaves and edible starchy corms, it serves as a critical food security crop. This resilient species plays a significant role in traditional agriculture and local medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Ensete glaucum
  • Family: Musaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Zingiberales and the family Musaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ensete, specifically as the species Ensete glaucum.
