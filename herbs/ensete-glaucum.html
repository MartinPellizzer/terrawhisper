Snow Banana, scientifically known as Ensete glaucum, is a resilient, large-leafed perennial native to the Indian subcontinent. Often mistaken for a true banana, this plant is prized for its edible corm and significant medicinal potential. It serves as a vital nutritional resource and traditional remedy in various tropical ecosystems.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions of Asia. Within China, it is specifically found throughout the South-Central areas. Its distribution extends into Eastern Asia, where it can be located in Taiwan. In the Indian Subcontinent, the species is present in Assam and Bangladesh.
