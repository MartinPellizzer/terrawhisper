Acacia falcata

Acacia falcata, commonly known as the falcate wattle, is a versatile medicinal plant native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Its unique foliage and bioactive compounds make it a significant botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Acacia falcata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Acacia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. Within the Malesia botanical zone, it can be found specifically on the island of Jawa. Its range extends significantly into Australia, covering substantial portions of the eastern coastline. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the state of New South Wales. Additionally, the species is widely distributed throughout the tropical and subtropical regions of Queensland.
