Pimpinella tirupatiensis

Pimpinella tirupatiensis, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, is an endemic species found within the unique ecosystems of the Tirumala hills. Renowned for its distinct phytochemical profile, this plant holds significant potential in traditional pharmacology, offering promising therapeutic properties for various ailments and contributing to biodiversity conservation efforts.
