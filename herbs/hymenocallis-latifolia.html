Hymenocallis latifolia

Hymenocallis latifolia, commonly known as the wide-leaf paperwhite, is a striking perennial valued for its ornamental beauty and potential medicinal properties. Found in tropical regions, this plant is often studied for its bioactive compounds, offering intriguing possibilities for traditional healing practices and modern pharmacological research into its therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Hymenocallis latifolia
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Amaryllidaceae and specifically within the genus Hymenocallis.

Common Names

This plant, Hymenocallis latifolia, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the large-leafed paperwhite, while in Spanish, it is known as lirio de papel or lirio de agua. In Portuguese, the plant is called lírio-do-papel, and in French, it is referred to as lys de papier.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several tropical and subtropical regions. In the Southeastern United States, its presence is specifically noted in the state of Florida. The species also extends throughout the Caribbean, where it can be found in the Bahamas and the Cayman Islands. Additionally, it is established within the larger island nations of the Caribbean, including Cuba and the Dominican Republic. Consequently, its range covers a diverse array of island and coastal environments.
