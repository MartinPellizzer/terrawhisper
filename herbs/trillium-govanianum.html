This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key mountainous regions in Asia. In Western Asia, its presence has been documented within the borders of Afghanistan. It can also be found in the high-altitude regions of Tibet in China. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species thrives in the East Himalaya and throughout Nepal. Additionally, its geographical range extends into the rugged terrains of Pakistan.

Plant Parts

This plant, Trillium govaniansum, is a medicinal species where various components are utilized for their therapeutic potential. The primary focus of traditional use often lies in the subterranean structures, specifically the rhizomes, which contain concentrated bioactive compounds. Whether harvesting a single rhizome or collecting multiple RhizoMEs from a healthy specimen, careful processing is required to ensure potency. In addition to these thickened stems, the roots play a vital role in the plant's overall physiological structure and nutrient storage. Ultimately, most medicinal applications are derived from a concentrated plant extract prepared from these gathered parts to facilitate healing.
