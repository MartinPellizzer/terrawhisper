Gnidia involucrata

Gnidia involucrata, commonly known as the Cape Gnidia, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. Characterized by its unique floral structures and leathery leaves, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various regional practices for its potent bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Gnidia involucrata
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Gnidia involucrata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Malvales and is a member of the family Thymelaeaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Gnidia.
