Aloe arborescens

Krans aloe

Aloe arborescens, commonly known as the tree aloe, is a striking succulent celebrated for its impressive stature and medicinal properties. Native to specific African landscapes, this robust plant features fleshy, water-storing leaves. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to soothe skin irritations and support overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Aloe arborescens
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aloe arborescens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales and the family Asphodelaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Aloe.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several regions of Southern Europe. Within Southwestern Europe, its presence is noted in the Balearic Islands, France, Portugal, and Spain. Additionally, the species can be found extending into Southeastern Europe, specifically within Italy. This range highlights its ability to thrive in the diverse Mediterranean climates found across these territories. Plant Parts

This plant, Aloe arborescens, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties which are concentrated within its distinct plant parts. The most significant components are the thick, fleshy green leaves, which serve as the primary source of its medicinal potency. Within these leaves, the internal gel acts as a reservoir for bioactive compounds, including specialized glycoprotein fractions that contribute to its healing effects. Furthermore, the leaf sap extract can be harvested to create various medicinal preparations, making the entire structure of the leaf an essential resource for pharmacological use.
