Eschscholzia

Eschscholzia, commonly known as California poppy, is a vibrant perennial herb native to western North America. Renowned for its brilliant orange blooms, this plant is highly valued in herbal medicine. Its alkaloids offer soothing sedative and anti-anxiety properties, making it a popular natural remedy for promoting restful sleep.

  • Scientific name: Eschscholzia
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the Papaveraceae family within the genus Eschscholzia.
