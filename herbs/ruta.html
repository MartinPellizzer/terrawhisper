Common rue, scientifically known as Ruta graveolens, is a perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic, blue-green foliage. Traditionally used in herbal medicine to support digestive health and menstrual regulation, it requires careful handling due to its potent properties. This versatile plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies worldwide.
