Ruta

Common rue

Ruta, commonly known as Rue, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant has historically been used to support digestive health, alleviate menstrual discomfort, and improve circulation. However, its high potency requires careful handling and expert guidance.

  • Scientific name: Ruta
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Ruta.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ruta, is referred to by several common names across different Chinese linguistic regions. In Simplified Chinese as used in mainland China and Singapore, it is known as 芸香属 (zh-cn, zh-sg) or 芸香属 (zh-hans), while in Hong Kong, it is specifically called 芸香屬 (zh-hk). In Traditional Chinese contexts, such as in Taiwan, it is identified as 芸香屬 (zh-hant).
