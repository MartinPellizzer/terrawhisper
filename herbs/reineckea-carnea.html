Reineckea carnea

Reineckea carnea, commonly known as the "African ginger" or "wild ginger," is a significant medicinal plant native to various tropical African regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights.

  • Scientific name: Reineckea carnea
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida.
