Colquhounia vestita

Colquhounia vestita, commonly known as the clothed colquhounia, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Characterized by its soft, velvety foliage, this plant is valued in traditional ethnobotanical practices for its potential medicinal properties and its resilience in rugged, mountainous ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Colquhounia vestita
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
