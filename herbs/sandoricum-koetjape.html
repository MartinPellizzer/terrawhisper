Sandoricum koetjape

Sandoricum koetjape, commonly known as santol, is a versatile tropical fruit tree native to Southeast Asia. Beyond its delicious, fleshy pulp, various parts of the plant hold significant medicinal value. Its leaves, bark, and seeds are traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Sandoricum koetjape
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa
