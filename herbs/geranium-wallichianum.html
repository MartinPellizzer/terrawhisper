Geranium wallichianum

Wallich geranium

Geranium wallichianum, commonly known as Wallich's Geranium, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant therapeutic properties, particularly for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource for treating various ailments in herbal pharmacology.
