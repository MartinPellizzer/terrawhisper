Tessaria absinthioides

Tessaria absinthioides, commonly known as bitterwood, is a medicinal shrub native to various African regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it has long been utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, fever, and inflammation. This plant offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and drug development.

  • Scientific name: Tessaria absinthioides
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Tessaria absinthioides, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Tessaria.

Common Names

This plant, Tessaria absinthioides, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic and geographical context. In English, it is commonly referred to as mare's tail, while in Spanish, it is frequently called yerba de la maza or yerba del monte. In Portuguese, the species is known as erva-de-santa-maria, and in the local context of certain regions, it may also be identified as yerba de santa maria.
