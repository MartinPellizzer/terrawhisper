Scutellaria arenicola

Scutellaria arenicola, commonly known as sand skullcap, is a distinctive perennial herb native to sandy habitats. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this plant is valued for its unique ecological niche and potential medicinal properties. Its subtle beauty and resilience make it a fascinating subject for botanical and pharmacological study.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria arenicola
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Scutellaria.
