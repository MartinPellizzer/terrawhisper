Myrtus nivellei

Myrtus nivellei, commonly known as Levellei's Myrtle, is a rare medicinal shrub prized for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Native to specific coastal microclimates, its aromatic leaves are traditionally harvested to treat respiratory ailments and skin irritations, offering a unique botanical treasure for modern holistic pharmacology.
