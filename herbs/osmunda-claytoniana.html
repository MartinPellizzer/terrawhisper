Osmunda claytoniana

Osmunda claytoniana, commonly known as the Interrupted Fern, is a resilient woodland plant valued for its distinct spore arrangement. While primarily ornamental, traditional herbal practices sometimes utilize its spores for topical applications. This fern thrives in moist, shaded habitats, serving as a vital component of diverse forest ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Osmunda claytoniana
  • Family: Osmundaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Osmundales. Finally, it is a member of the family Osmundaceae and falls under the genus Osmunda.
