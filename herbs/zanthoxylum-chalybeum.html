Zanthoxylum chalybeum

Zanthoxylum chalybeum, commonly known as the Chalybeum tree, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly essential oils and alkaloids, it is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties to treat various ailments and support overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum chalybeum
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Zanthoxylum.

Common Names

This plant, Zanthoxylum chalybeum, is known by various regional names across different linguistic landscapes. In English, it is commonly referred to as the African prickly ash, while in French, it is known as le prunier de Chine. Within local contexts in Africa, it is often called l'arbre à écorce in French-speaking regions, and in various indigenous traditions, it is recognized by names that reflect its potent medicinal properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several key regions of the African continent.
