Petasites

Petasites, commonly known as butterbur, is a versatile medicinal herb valued for its potent anti-inflammatory and antispasmodic properties. Frequently utilized to manage migraines and seasonal allergies, it contains bioactive compounds that support vascular health. However, careful selection is essential to ensure products are free from toxic alkaloids.

  • Scientific name: Petasites
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. It is ultimately identified by the genus Petasites.
