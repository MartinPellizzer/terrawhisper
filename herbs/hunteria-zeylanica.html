Hunteria zeylanica

Hunteria zeylanica, commonly known as the Zeylanica Hunteria, is a significant medicinal tree native to Sri Lanka. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments. Research focuses on its alkaloids, which offer potential therapeutic benefits in managing neurological disorders and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Hunteria zeylanica
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Gentianaales and falls into the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Hunteria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across several regions of Africa and Asia. In Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Somalia. Its presence extends further through East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits both Kenya and Tanzania. Additionally, the species is located in South Tropical Africa within the country of Mozambique. Beyond the African continent, its range reaches as far as Hainan in China.

Plant Parts

This plant, Hunteria zeylanica, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. Furthermore, both the stem bark and the thicker bark of the main trunk contain significant alkaloid concentrations, making these woody parts essential in traditional pharmacological applications.
