Jacob's-coat, scientifically known as Acalypha wilkesiana, is a striking tropical shrub celebrated for its vibrant, colorful foliage. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. In various traditional medicinal practices, its leaves are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations and wounds.
