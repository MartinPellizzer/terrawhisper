Acalypha wilkesiana

Acalypha wilkesiana, commonly known as Copperleaf or Jacob's Coat, is a striking ornamental shrub prized for its vibrant, multicolored foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this tropical plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its purported antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Acalypha wilkesiana
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Acalypha.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is noted specifically within Guinea. The distribution extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found throughout the Gulf of Guinea Islands, Rwanda, and the DR Congo. Furthermore, its reach continues into the Western Indian Ocean, specifically encompassing the Comoros. Together, these locations highlight the plant's widespread availability across different African landscapes.
