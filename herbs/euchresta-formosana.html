Euchresta formosana

Euchresta formosana, commonly known as the Formosan Euchresta, is a versatile medicinal plant native to East Asian forests. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and stems are utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This botanical specimen offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Family: Fabaceae
