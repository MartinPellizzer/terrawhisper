Chinese gooseberry, scientifically known as Actinidia, is a versatile climbing vine prized for its nutrient-dense fruit. Beyond its culinary appeal, it holds significant medicinal potential due to its high concentrations of vitamin C, antioxidants, and enzymes. These properties support immune function, digestion, and overall cardiovascular health in various traditional therapies.
