Actinidia

Actinidia, commonly known as kiwi, is a versatile genus of climbing vines prized for both culinary and medicinal benefits. Rich in vitamin C, antioxidants, and essential nutrients, it supports immune function and digestive health. Its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic properties, making it a valuable plant in natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Actinidia
  • Family: Actinidiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Actinidiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Actinidia.
