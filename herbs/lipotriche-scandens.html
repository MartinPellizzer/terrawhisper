Lipotriche scandens

Lipotriche scandens, commonly known as the woody climber or climbing Lipotriche, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its climbing habit, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds and restorative properties.
