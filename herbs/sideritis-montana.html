Mountain Ironwort, scientifically known as Sideritis montana, is a resilient perennial herb native to the rugged Mediterranean mountains. Renowned for its aromatic, silver-green foliage, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional herbalism to treat respiratory ailments, soothe digestive issues, and provide potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits.
