Sideritis montana

Sideritis montana, commonly known as mountain ironwort, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its aromatic, silver-leafed foliage, this plant has long been cherished in traditional medicine. It possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, frequently used to support digestive health and soothe respiratory discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Sideritis montana
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
