Allium longisepalum

Allium longisepalum, commonly known as the long-sepaled onion, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Belonging to the Amaryllidaceae family, it possesses unique phytochemical properties. Often utilized for its antimicrobial and antioxidant potential, this species offers significant therapeutic interest for managing various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Allium longisepalum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed by the genus Allium, specifically the species Allium longisepalum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in the Middle East. Within Western Asia, it is commonly found growing in the territories of Iran and Iraq. Its presence extends further north through the Lebanon-Syria border region and throughout Türkiye. Additionally, the species can be located across the various countries of the Arabian Peninsula. Collectively, these areas represent the primary natural habitats for Allium longisepalum.
