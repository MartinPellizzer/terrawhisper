Polygala caudata

Polygala caudata, commonly known as Mitanga, is a medicinal shrub native to Madagascar. Renowned in traditional healing, it is primarily utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat respiratory issues and skin ailments, this versatile plant offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
