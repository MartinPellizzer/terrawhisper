Polygala caudata

Polygala caudata, commonly known as Mitanga, is a medicinal shrub native to Madagascar. Renowned in traditional healing, it is primarily utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat respiratory issues and skin ailments, this versatile plant offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Polygala caudata
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Polygalaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Polygala.
