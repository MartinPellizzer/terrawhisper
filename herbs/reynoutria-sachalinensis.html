Reynoutria sachalinensis

Giant knotweed

Reynoutria sachalinensis, commonly known as Japanese knotweed or giant knotweed, is a robust perennial celebrated in traditional medicine. Rich in bioactive compounds like resveratrol and tannins, it offers significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential in treating metabolic disorders and promoting overall cellular health.

  • Scientific name: Reynoutria sachalinensis
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Reynoutria.

Common Names
