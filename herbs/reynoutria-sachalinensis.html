Giant Knotweed, scientifically known as Reynoutria sachalinensis, is a robust perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, particularly resveratrol and stilbenes, it offers significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient plant serves as a promising natural resource for developing modern therapeutic treatments and supplements.
