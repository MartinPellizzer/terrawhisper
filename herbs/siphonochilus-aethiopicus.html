Distribution

This plant, Siphochilus aethiopicus, is primarily distributed across the region of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Its presence is also documented in the country of Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species inhabits parts of Gambia and Ghana. Finally, the geographical range extends into Guinea-Bissau.
