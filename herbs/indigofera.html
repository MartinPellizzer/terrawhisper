Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several distinct regions within Northern Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the territories of Algeria, Egypt, and Libya. Its distribution also extends into the territory of Western Sahara. Beyond the mainland, the species is located within the Macaronesian islands of Cape Verde. This diverse range highlights the plant's adaptability to various North African environments.
