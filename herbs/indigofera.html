Indigofera

Indigofera, commonly known as indigo, is a diverse genus of flowering plants valued for both medicinal and industrial purposes. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in various cultures to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and wounds. Today, researchers explore its potential in developing potent pharmacological treatments.

  • Scientific name: Indigofera
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Within this family, it is identified by the genus Indigofera.
