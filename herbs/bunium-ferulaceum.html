Bunium ferulaceum

Bunium ferulaaceum, commonly known as Ferula, is a significant medicinal herb valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Apiaceae family, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer potential benefits in managing inflammation and supporting overall wellness through natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Bunium ferulaceum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Bunium, specifically as the species Bunium ferulaaceum.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within Southeastern Europe.
