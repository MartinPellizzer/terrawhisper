Balkan Pignut, scientifically known as Bunium ferulaceum, is a versatile medicinal herb native to the Balkan Peninsula. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. This plant offers valuable phytochemical properties, making it a subject of botanical interest.
