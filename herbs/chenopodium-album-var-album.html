Chenopodium album var. album

Chenopodium album var. album, commonly known as white goosefoot, is a versatile medicinal herb found globally. Renowned for its nutrient-dense leaves, it has been utilized in traditional medicine to support digestion and treat inflammation. This resilient plant offers significant antioxidant properties, making it a valuable resource in herbal wellness.

  • Scientific name: Chenopodium album var. album
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement in the genus Chenopodium.

Common Names

This plant, Chenopodium album var. album, is known by a variety of common names across different languages and regions. In English, it is frequently referred to as fat hen, white goosefoot, or lambs quarters. When looking at other languages, it is called weißer Gänsefuß in German, lamb's quarters in several dialects, and seiche in French. In Spanish, it is often called cenizo or quingombó, while in Italian, it is known as olivello.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several regions within Northern Europe. It is widely distributed throughout the various territories of Denmark and Finland. The species can also be found growing in the remote landscapes of Iceland and the Faroe Islands. Additionally, its range extends into the diverse habitats of Great Britain. Together, these locations highlight its ability to thrive in northern climates.
