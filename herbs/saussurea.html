Saussurea

Saussurea, commonly known as Snow Lotus, is a resilient medicinal plant flourishing in the high-altitude alpine regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly Tibetan pharmacology, it is highly valued for its potent antioxidant properties and its ability to treat inflammation, respiratory ailments, and various blood-related disorders.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
