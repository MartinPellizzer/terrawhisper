Selfheal

Prunella vulgaris · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (891 studies) · ★★★★★

Selfheal, scientifically known as Prunellla vulgaris, is a versatile perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its healing properties, it has long been utilized to treat wounds, respiratory issues, and inflammation. This resilient plant, characterized by its purple flowering spikes, remains a cornerstone of natural apothecary practices worldwide.

This plant, known scientifically as Prunella vulgaris, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as self-heal, is characterized by its unique morphological structures used in traditional medicine. The most distinctive feature is the dense, dark purple flowering spike that emerges from the center of the leaves. These terminal spikes are composed of numerous small, tubular flowers clustered tightly together. While some botanical texts might colloquially or incorrectly refer to these elongated structures as a spicca or spiicas, the correct botanical term remains the spike. Below the surface, the plant develops a sturdy, creeping root system that helps it spread through the soil and anchor itself firmly.
