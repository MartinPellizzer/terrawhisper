Prunella vulgaris

Prunella vulgaris, commonly known as self-heal, is a versatile perennial herb prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant has long been used to treat wounds, sore throats, and skin irritations. Its unique structure and healing potential make it a botanical treasure.

  • Scientific name: Prunella vulgaris
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Prunella vulgaris, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its classification is completed within the genus Prunella.

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several regions within Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Denmark and Finland.
