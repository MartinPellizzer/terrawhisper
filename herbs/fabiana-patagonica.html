Fabiana patagonica

Fabiana patagonica, commonly known as "puna" or "patagonian tea," is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the windswept landscapes of southern South America. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its aromatic foliage is frequently brewed into soothing herbal infusions to treat digestive ailments, respiratory issues, and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Fabiana patagonica
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification
