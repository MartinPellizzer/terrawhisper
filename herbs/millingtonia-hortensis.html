Millingtonia hortensis

Millingtonia hortensis, commonly known as the Indian Cork Tree, is a magnificent deciduous tree prized for its fragrant, creamy-white blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various parts used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Millingtonia hortensis
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Bignoniaceae and falls under the genus Millingtonia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in both India and Bangladesh. Its range extends further into the Indo-China region, specifically encompassing the Andaman Islands. In East Asia, the species is located throughout South-Central China. Additionally, the plant is distributed across the Arabian Peninsula, including the various Gulf States.
