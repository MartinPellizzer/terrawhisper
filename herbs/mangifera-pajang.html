Mangifera pajang

Bambangan

Mangifera pajang, commonly known as the Pahang mango, is a rare, wild relative of the cultivated mango native to Southeast Asia. Highly valued for its unique, intense flavor and aromatic qualities, this medicinal plant offers significant nutritional benefits and holds potential in traditional pharmacology for its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Mangifera pajang
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Mangifera pajang, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Sapindales and falls into the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Mangifera.

Common Names

Within this expansive tropical area, the species is specifically found on the island of Borneo. This restricted habitat suggests that the plant is endemic to the unique ecosystems of this particular island. Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Mangifera pajang and commonly referred to as the Bacang, is highly valued for its medicinal and nutritional properties, particularly through its use of the fruit.
