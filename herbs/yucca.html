Yucca, scientifically known as Yucca, is a resilient perennial plant revered for its diverse medicinal properties. Native to arid regions, its roots and leaves contain saponins, which possess anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments and joint pain, it remains a vital botanical resource.
