Jatropha curcas

Barbados nut

Jatrophas curcas, commonly known as physic nut, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the tropics. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin infections, inflammation, and digestive ailments. Beyond medicine, its seeds serve as a significant source of renewable biofuel and vegetable oil.

  • Scientific name: Jatropha curcas
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Jatropha.

Common Names

This plant, Jatropha curcas, is known by various names across different languages and regions. In Chinese, it is referred to as 麻疯树 (máfēngshù), while in the Pamonese language, it is called Galumbang. Its name in Javanese is Jarak Pagar, and in Polish, it is known as Jatrofa przeczyszczającą. Additionally, in the Yoruba language, this medicinal plant is commonly called Bòtújẹ̀.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Macaronesia, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. Moving toward the mainland, it is found within West Tropical Africa in several different nations. These locations include Benin, Burkina, and the Gambia. Additionally, the species can be located within the territory of Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, Jatropha curcas, possesses several components that are utilized for various medicinal and industrial purposes. The most notable part is the seed, which contains a high concentration of lipids. When the seeds are processed, they yield a potent oil that is frequently studied for its bioactive properties. Additionally, the plant produces a distinctive white latex that flows from its tissues when damaged; this latex serves as a vital source of secondary metabolites used in traditional healing practices.
