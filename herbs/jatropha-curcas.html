Barbados nut

Jatropha curcas · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (333 studies) · ★★★★★

Barbados nut, scientifically known as Jatrophas curcas, is a versatile, drought-tolerant shrub widely utilized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and purgative properties. While its seeds offer significant therapeutic potential, careful handling is essential due to the plant's inherent toxicity.

Plant Parts

This plant, Jatropha curcas, possesses several components that are utilized for various medicinal and industrial purposes. The most notable part is the seed, which contains a high concentration of lipids. When the seeds are processed, they yield a potent oil that is frequently studied for its bioactive properties. Additionally, the plant produces a distinctive white latex that flows from its tissues when damaged; this latex serves as a vital source of secondary metabolites used in traditional healing practices.
