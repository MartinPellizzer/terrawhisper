Barbados nut

Jatropha curcas · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (333 studies) · ★★★★★

Barbados nut, scientifically known as Jatrophas curcas, is a versatile, drought-tolerant shrub widely utilized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and purgative properties. While its seeds offer significant therapeutic potential, careful handling is essential due to the plant's inherent toxicity.
