Streblus asper

Siamese rough bush

Streblus asper, commonly known as Siamese rough bush, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its sandpaper-like leaves, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Streblus asper
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Rosales and placed in the family Moraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Streblus.
