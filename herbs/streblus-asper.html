Siamese rough bush, scientifically known as Streblus asper, is a versatile evergreen tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and roots possess potent anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and antioxidant properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions naturally.
