Redcurrant, scientifically known as Ribes rubrum, is a deciduous shrub prized for its translucent, tart berries. Beyond its culinary appeal, this medicinal plant is rich in vitamin C and antioxidants. Traditionally, it has been used to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality through its nutrient-dense profile.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the red currant, is valued for its edible and medicinal properties which are primarily found in its fruit. The most notable parts used are the small, translucent red currants that grow in drooping clusters. These tart berries are highly nutritious, containing significant amounts of vitamin C and antioxidants that contribute to their therapeutic benefits in supporting the immune system and overall health.
