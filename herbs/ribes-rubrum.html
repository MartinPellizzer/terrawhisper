Redcurrant, scientifically known as Ribes rubrum, is a deciduous shrub prized for its translucent, tart berries. Beyond its culinary appeal, this medicinal plant is rich in vitamin C and antioxidants. Traditionally, it has been used to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality through its nutrient-dense profile.
