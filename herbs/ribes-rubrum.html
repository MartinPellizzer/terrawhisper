Ribes rubrum

Ribes rubrum, commonly known as the red currant, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued for its potent nutritional profile. Rich in vitamins C and K, along with powerful antioxidants, it supports immune function and reduces inflammation. Traditionally used to treat fevers and digestive issues, it remains a functional superfood.

  • Scientific name: Ribes rubrum
  • Family: Grossulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Grossulariaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ribes.
