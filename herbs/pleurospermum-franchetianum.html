Pleurospermum franchetianum

What's the taxonomical classification of Pleurospermum franchetianum?

Pleurospermum franchetianum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further classified into the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is part of the order Apiales and falls under the family Apiaceae. Taxonomically, it is identified by the genus Pleurospermum, with the specific epithet franchetianum designating its species.

According to a study published by "Journal of Asian natural products research", four new glycosides known as pleurofranosides I-IV were isolated from the whole plants of Pleurospermum franchetianum Hemsl.
