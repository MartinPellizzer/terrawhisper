Arctostaphylos uva-ursi

Arctostaphylos uva-ursi, commonly known as bearberry, is a hardy, evergreen shrub native to northern temperate regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, the plant's leaves contain arbutin, a compound used traditionally to treat urinary tract infections. Its resilient nature makes it a vital component in herbal pharmacology and botanical studies.
