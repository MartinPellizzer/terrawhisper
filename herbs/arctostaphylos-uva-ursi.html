kinnikinnick

Arctostaphylos uva-ursi · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (801 studies) · ★★★★★

Kinnikinnick, scientifically known as Arctostaphylos uva-ursi, is a hardy, evergreen creeping shrub native to northern temperate regions. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its leaves contain arbutin, a compound frequently utilized for its potent antiseptic and diuretic properties, particularly in supporting urinary tract health and managing various bladder-related conditions.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as bearberry, utilizes various components for its therapeutic applications. The primary medicinal part used is the Folium uvae-ursi, which refers specifically to the dried leaves of the species. While the leaves are the most frequent source of active compounds like arbutin, the roots are also recognized as a constituent part of the plant that can possess medicinal properties. In pharmaceutical preparations, water extracts of these botanical elements are often utilized to create decoctions or standardized formulations for treating urinary tract infections.
