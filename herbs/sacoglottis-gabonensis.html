Distribution

This plant is distributed across several countries within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is specifically documented in Ghana, where it grows in local ecosystems. It can also be found throughout the coastal and inland areas of Guinea. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the Ivory Coast and Liberia. Finally, the geographical footprint of this medicinal plant includes Nigeria, completing its West African distribution.
