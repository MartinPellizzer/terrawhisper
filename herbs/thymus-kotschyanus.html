Plant Parts

This plant, Thymus kotschyanus, is primarily utilized for its therapeutic properties derived from its aerial parts, which include the leaves and flowering stems. These components are highly valued in pharmacology because they serve as the main source of its concentrated essential oils. The chemical profile of this essential oil is what provides the plant with its characteristic aromatic scent and its potent antimicrobial and antioxidant activities.
