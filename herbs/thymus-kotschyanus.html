Thymus kotschyanus

Thymus kotschyanus, commonly known as Kotchyan thyme, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to the rugged terrains of the Mediterranean and Middle East. Renowned for its essential oils, this medicinal plant offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, serving as a valuable natural remedy in traditional herbal medicine practices.

  • Scientific name: Thymus kotschyanus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Thymus.
