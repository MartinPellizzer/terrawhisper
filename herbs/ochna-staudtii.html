Ochna staudtii

Ochna staudtii, commonly known as the African Staudt's Ochna, is a resilient shrub native to various African regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots are frequently utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues, showcasing the profound botanical diversity found within its natural habitat.

  • Scientific name: Ochna staudtii
  • Family: Ochnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Ochnaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ochna, with its specific species being Ochna staudtii.

Common Names

This plant, Ochna staudtii, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic context of its native regions. In English, it is referred to as the Staudt's Ochna, while in German, it is known as Staudts Ochna. Additionally, in Afrikaans, the plant is called Staudts Ochna.

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several regions within the tropical landscapes of Africa. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Ghana, Ivory Coast, and Nigeria. The species also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Cameroon. Additionally, its geographical range includes the country of Gabon. Consequently, its habitat spans a significant portion of the western and central tropical belts.
