Alligator Weed, scientifically known as Alternanthera philoxeroides, is an invasive perennial herb native to South America. While primarily recognized as a problematic aquatic weed, it holds ethnobotanical significance. Traditionally, various cultures have utilized its properties in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various topical wounds.
