Alternanthera philoxeroides

Alligator weed

Alternanthera philoxeroides, commonly known as pigweed or alligator weed, is a resilient perennial herb found in wetlands globally. While often viewed as an invasive species, it possesses significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various cultures have utilized its leaves and stems for their purported medicinal properties and nutritional benefits.

  • Scientific name: Alternanthera philoxeroides
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Alternanthera.
