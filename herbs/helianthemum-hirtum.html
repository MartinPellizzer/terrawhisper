Helianthemum hirtum

Helianthemum hirtum, commonly known as the Rock Rose, is a resilient perennial shrub celebrated for its striking blooms and therapeutic potential. Native to diverse terrains, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional healing for its antioxidant properties and ability to support skin health and reduce inflammatory responses naturally.

  • Scientific name: Helianthemum hirtum
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Cistaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Helianthemum, which includes the species Helianthemum hirtum.
