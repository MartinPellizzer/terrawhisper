The sorrel genus, scientifically known as Rumex, is a diverse group of perennial herbs valued for their medicinal and culinary properties. Often found in wetlands, these plants contain oxalic acid and tannins. They are traditionally used to treat digestive issues, reduce inflammation, and support kidney health through their astringent qualities.
