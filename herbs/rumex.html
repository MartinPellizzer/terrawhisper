Rumex · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (27 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

The sorrel genus, scientifically known as Rumex, is a diverse group of perennial herbs valued for their medicinal and culinary properties. Often found in wetlands, these plants contain oxalic acid and tannins. They are traditionally used to treat digestive issues, reduce inflammation, and support kidney health through their astringent qualities.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions in Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the landscapes of Finland. Furthermore, the species inhabits the unique environments of the Føroyaar islands. Finally, its range extends to the rugged terrains of both Great Britain and Iceland.
