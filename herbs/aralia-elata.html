Aralia elata

Aralia elaata, commonly known as the Japanese angelica tree, is a perennial medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Native to East Asia, it features large, compound leaves and distinctive berries. Historically utilized for its anti-inflammatory and restorative properties, this botanical species offers significant potential for contemporary pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Aralia elata
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Araliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aralia.
