Catmint, scientifically known as Nepeta, is a versatile perennial herb cherished in both gardens and traditional medicine. Belonging to the mint family, it is famous for its aromatic foliage and soothing properties. Often used to reduce anxiety and promote sleep, this resilient plant offers both beauty and therapeutic benefits.
