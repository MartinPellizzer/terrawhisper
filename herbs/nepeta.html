Nepeta

Nepeta, commonly known as catmint, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic foliage and delicate lavender flowers. Belonging to the mint family, it is widely used in landscaping to attract pollinators and in traditional herbalism for its calming, soothing properties and mild medicinal benefits in wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is categorized under the genus Nepeta.
