Mammillaria geminispina

Mammillaria geminispina, commonly known as the twin-spined cactus, is a fascinating succulent native to Mexico. Renowned for its distinctive paired spines and globular shape, it serves more in ornamental horticulture than traditional medicine. However, its unique biological properties continue to intrigue researchers exploring the vast medicinal potential of cacti.

  • Scientific name: Mammillaria geminispina
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, falling into the family Cactaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Mammillaria.
