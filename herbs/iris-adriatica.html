Iris adriatica

Iris adriatica, commonly known as the Adriatic Iris, is a rare perennial herb native to the limestone cliffs of the Adriatic coast. Renowned for its striking violet blooms, this resilient plant thrives in rocky, sun-drenched habitats. While primarily ornamental, its unique ecological niche makes it a botanical treasure.

  • Scientific name: Iris adriatica
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asparagales and the family Iridaeceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Iris.
