Goniothalamus giganteus

Goniothaalamus giganteus, commonly known as the Giant Elephant Ear, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical rainforests. Renowned for its large, heart-shaped leaves, it holds immense ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize various parts of this plant to treat diverse ailments, including inflammation, skin infections, and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Goniothalamus giganteus
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Goniothalamus giganteus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Magnoliales. It is further categorized into the family Annonaceae and the genus Goniothalamus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Goniothalamus giganteus, is recognized by various regional common names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Giant Goniothalamus, while in Malay, it is known as Pokok Kuning. In the local dialects of the region, such as Tamil, it is called Karunthala, and in Malayalam, it is referred to as Karunthala.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Indo-China and Malesia botanical zones. In the Indo-China region, its presence is documented in both Myanmar and Thailand. Moving into the Malesia zone, the species can be found inhabiting the areas of Malaya. Additionally, its geographical reach extends to the island of Sumatera. Together, these locations define the specific tropical range where this medicinal species thrives.
