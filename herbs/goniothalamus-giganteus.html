Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Indo-China and Malesia botanical zones. In the Indo-China region, its presence is documented in both Myanmar and Thailand. Moving into the Malesia zone, the species can be found inhabiting the areas of Malaya. Additionally, its geographical reach extends to the island of Sumatera. Together, these locations define the specific tropical range where this medicinal species thrives.
