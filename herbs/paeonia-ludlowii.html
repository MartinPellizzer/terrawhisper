Paeonia ludlowii

Tibetan tree peony

Paeonia ludlowii, commonly known as Ludlow's peony, is a striking perennial native to China. Valued for its large, bowl-shaped blooms, this botanical treasure holds significant place in traditional practices. Beyond its ornamental beauty, its roots contain bioactive compounds often explored for their potential therapeutic and medicinal properties in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia ludlowii
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Paeonia ludlowii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Saxifrages and the family Paeoniaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Paeonia.
