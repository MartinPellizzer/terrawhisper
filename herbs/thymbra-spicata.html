Thymbra spicata

Spiked savoury

Thymbra spicata, commonly known as savory, is a fragrant perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Native to the Mediterranean region, this aromatic plant is prized for its essential oils. It serves various roles in traditional medicine and culinary practices, offering potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and digestive health benefits.

  • Scientific name: Thymbra spicata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Lamiaceae and is identified by the genus Thymbra.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across several distinct regions ranging from Southeastern Europe to Western Asia. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is well-documented in Greece and the European portion of Türkiye. Moving into Western Asia, the species can be found throughout the East Aegean Islands. Its range further extends eastward into the territories of Iran. Finally, the distribution concludes within the geographical borders of Iraq.
