Spiked savoury, scientifically known as Thymbra spicata, is a fragrant perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Native to the Mediterranean region, this aromatic plant is highly valued for its essential oils. It serves both culinary and medicinal purposes, offering potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties in traditional healing.
