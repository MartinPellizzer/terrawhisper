Thalictrum atriplex

Thalictrum atriplex, commonly known as the purple corydalis, is a perennial herb distinguished by its delicate, nodding flowers. Native to various temperate regions, this plant features deeply lobed foliage. While less frequent in modern pharmacopeias, its unique botanical characteristics make it a subject of interest in traditional herbal studies.

  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
