Alhagi graecorum

Alhagi graecorum, commonly known as camelthorn, is a resilient perennial legume found across arid regions of the Middle East and Mediterranean. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant therapeutic potential, offering antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties used to treat various ailments and support overall metabolic health.
