Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several regions of South America. In the western part of the continent, it can be found across Bolivia, Ecuador, and Peru. Moving toward the eastern side, the species is also well-established within the borders of Brazil. Specifically, it inhabits the West-Central region of the Brazilian territory.
