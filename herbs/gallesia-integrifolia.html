Gallesia integrifolia

Gallesia integrifolia, commonly known as the "Copaiba" or "Gallesia," is a significant medicinal shrub native to South American ecosystems. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues, offering valuable bioactive compounds for natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Gallesia integrifolia
  • Family: Petiveriaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Gallesia integrifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and is a representative of the family Petiveriaceae. Its specific classification is defined by the genus Gallesia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several regions of South America. In the western part of the continent, it can be found across Bolivia, Ecuador, and Peru. Moving toward the eastern side, the species is also well-established within the borders of Brazil. Specifically, it inhabits the West-Central region of the Brazilian territory. Additionally, its range extends into the Northeast region of Brazil.
