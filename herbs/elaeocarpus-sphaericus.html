Commonly referred to as the Soap Nut, scientifically known as Elaeocarpus sphaericus, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its high saponin content, it serves as a natural cleansing agent. Traditionally, it is utilized for treating skin ailments, respiratory issues, and promoting overall hair health.
