Elaeocarpus sphaericus

Elaeocarpus sphaericus, commonly known as the Tamarind Ball or Sea Tamarind, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical coastal regions. Renowned for its unique seed pods, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Traditionally used in folk medicine, its various components possess antioxidant and antimicrobial properties beneficial for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Elaeocarpus sphaericus
  • Family: Elaeocarpaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Oxalidales. Finally, it is a member of the family Elaeocarpaceae and the genus Elaeocarpus.
