Bistorta officinalis

Bistorta officinalis, commonly known as Common Bistort, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its astringent properties. Rich in tannins, its rhizomes are frequently utilized to treat gastrointestinal issues, skin inflammations, and wound healing. This resilient plant remains a vital component in various herbal therapeutic practices.

  • Scientific name: Bistorta officinalis
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Bistorta.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Bistorta officinalis or Common Bistort, relies primarily on its robust root system for its medicinal properties. The rhizomatous roots are thick, creeping, and possess a distinct reddish or brownish color, containing the concentrated tannins and astringent compounds that make the plant valuable in herbal medicine. While the leaves and stems are part of the plant's structure, it is the harvested root that serves as the principal part used in traditional remedies to treat various ailments.
