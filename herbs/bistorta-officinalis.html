Commonly called Bistort, scientifically known as Bistorta officinalis, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent astringent properties, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health and manage skin inflammations. This hardy plant offers a rich array of tannins, making it a versatile botanical remedy.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Bistorta officinalis or Common Bistort, relies primarily on its robust root system for its medicinal properties. The rhizomatous roots are thick, creeping, and possess a distinct reddish or brownish color, containing the concentrated tannins and astringent compounds that make the plant valuable in herbal medicine. While the leaves and stems are part of the plant's structure, it is the harvested root that serves as the principal part used in traditional remedies to treat various ailments.
