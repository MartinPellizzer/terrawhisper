Silver oak, scientifically known as Grevillea robusta, is a magnificent evergreen tree native to Australia. Renowned for its rapid growth and striking fern-like foliage, it serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. Often used in agroforestry, its extracts show potential in traditional healing for their various therapeutic properties and benefits.
