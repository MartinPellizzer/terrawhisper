Grevillea robusta

Grevillea robusta, commonly known as the Silky Oak, is a magnificent evergreen tree native to Australia. Renowned for its striking reddish-orange flowers and rapid growth, it holds significant value in forestry and ornamental landscaping. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, it offers diverse medicinal potential within traditional and contemporary herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Grevillea robusta
  • Family: Proteaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Proteales, it is classified under the family Proteaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Grevillea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence can be noted within the country of Italy. It also inhabits parts of Macaronesia, specifically the Canary Islands and Cape Verde. Moving toward the African continent, it is found in West Tropical Africa, notably in Gambia. Finally, its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is located in Cameroon.
