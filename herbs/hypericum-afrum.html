Hypericum afrum

Hypericum afrum, commonly known as African St. John's Wort, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various African landscapes. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat wounds, skin inflammations, and various ailments, offering a natural approach to holistic healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum afrum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Hypericaceae. It is ultimately identified by the genus Hypericum.
