Saxifraga brunonis

What's the taxonomical classification of Saxifraga brunonis?

Saxifraga brunonis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated under the order Saxifragales. The plant is further categorized into the family Saxifragaecae, which contains the genus Saxifraga, ultimately defining its specific identity as the species Saxifraga brunonis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Saxifraga brunonis has a perennial growth habit characterized by a basal rosette of fleshy, rounded, or spatulate leaves that are often tinged with red and possess dentate or crenate margins. These leaves are typically arranged in a tight cluster to conserve moisture and heat in its alpine habitat. The plant produces upright, slender flowering stems that emerge from the center of the rosette to bear small, inconspicuous flowers. The blossoms consist of five petals that are generally white or pale pink and are supported by a short pedicel. Small, capsule-like fruits develop following pollination to facilitate seed dispersal across rocky substrates.
