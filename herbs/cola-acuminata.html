True kola

Cola acuminata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (385 studies) · ★★★★★

True kola, scientifically known as Cola acuminata, is a perennial evergreen tree native to the tropical rainforests of West Africa. Renowned for its caffeine-rich nuts, it holds immense cultural and economic significance. Traditionally used in ceremonies and as a natural stimulant, it remains a vital component of regional heritage.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Malvales and placed in the family Malvaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cola.

Distribution

This plant, known as Cola acuminata, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is well-documented within the borders of Benin. Furthermore, the species can be found growing throughout the Ivory Coast. It also inhabits the tropical landscapes of Liberia and Sierra Leone. Finally, Nigeria serves as another significant part of its natural geographical range.
