Distribution

This plant exhibits a unique and disjunct geographical distribution across two different continents. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing on the island of Réunion. Additionally, the species has a significant presence in Australia, specifically within the state of Queensland. These distant locations highlight the wide range in which this medicinal species can thrive. Consequently, its habitat spans from tropical island environments to the coastal regions of the Australian continent.
