Vepris

Vepris, commonly known as a versatile medicinal herb, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is often utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues. Understanding its botanical characteristics and medicinal benefits is essential for holistic wellness.

  • Family: Rutaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is specifically situated in the family Rutaceae. It is further identified by its unique genus, Vepris.
