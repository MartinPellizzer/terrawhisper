What's the taxonomical classification of Pleurospermum benthamii?

Pleurospermum benthamii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Apiales. This plant is a member of the family Apiaceae, specifically categorized within the genus Pleurospermum, with its specific designation being the species benthamii.
