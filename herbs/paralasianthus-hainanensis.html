Paralasianthus hainanensis

Paralasianthus hainanenensis, commonly known as a rare medicinal shrub from Hainan, China, is a significant botanical species in traditional pharmacology. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers promising therapeutic potential. Research focuses on its bioactive compounds, aiming to unlock new treatments for various inflammatory and oxidative conditions.

  • Scientific name: Paralasianthus hainanensis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered within the Gentianales and belongs to the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Paralasianthus as the species Paralasianthus hainanensis.

Distribution

This plant is recognized for its highly specific and restricted geographical range. It is found exclusively within the territory of China. More specifically, its natural habitat is limited to the island province of Hainan. This localized distribution suggests that the species may be endemic to this particular tropical region. Consequently, its survival is closely tied to the unique environmental conditions found only in Hainan.
