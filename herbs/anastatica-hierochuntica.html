Anastatica hierochuntica

Anastatica hierochuntica, commonly known as the Rose of Jericho, is a unique desert plant famed for its remarkable resurrection abilities. Capable of surviving extreme dehydration, it curls into a tight ball before blooming upon contact with water, offering significant medicinal potential in treating respiratory issues and inflammatory skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Anastatica hierochuntica
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
