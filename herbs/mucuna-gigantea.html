Burny bean

Mucuna gigantea · Accepted scientific name

Burny bean, scientifically known as Mucuna gigantea, is a robust tropical legume valued for its potent medicinal properties. Rich in L-Dopa, it is traditionally used to support neurological health and hormonal balance. This powerful plant offers significant therapeutic potential in treating various conditions through its unique bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends into East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. Furthermore, the species is documented in the South Tropical Africa region, specifically in Mozambique. Together, these locations highlight the wide ecological range of this medicinal species.
