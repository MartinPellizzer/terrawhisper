Mucuna gigantea

Burny bean

Mucuna gigantea, commonly known as Velvet Bean, is a robust tropical legume celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its high L-dopa content, it is primarily used to support neurological health, boost dopamine levels, and manage Parkinson's symptoms. This potent botanical offers significant therapeutic potential for hormonal and cognitive balance.

  • Scientific name: Mucuna gigantea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Mucuna.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends into East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. Furthermore, the species is documented in the South Tropical Africa region, specifically in Mozambique. Together, these locations highlight the wide ecological range of this medicinal species.
