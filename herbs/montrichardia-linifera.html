Montrichardia linifera

Montrichardia linifera, commonly known as the Amazonian water plant, is a significant medicinal species found in South American freshwater ecosystems. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation and skin ailments. Scientific interest continues to grow regarding its bioactive compounds and potential pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Montrichardia linifera
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Alismatales and the family Araceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Montrichardia, specifically identified as the species Montrichardia linifera.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the northern regions of South America. It can be found growing within the territories of French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname.
