Strobilanthes alternata

Strobilanthes alternata, commonly known as Malabar Melastome, is a versatile medicinal shrub cherished in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it serves as a natural remedy for various ailments, including inflammation, skin disorders, and respiratory issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Strobilanthes alternata
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and order Lamiales, it is a member of the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is taxonomically identified under the genus Strobilanthes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found within the borders of Benin. Its presence extends to Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically in the country of Chad. Moving toward the ocean, the species is also located in the Chagos Archipelago within the Western Indian Ocean. Finally, it inhabits the Indo-China region, with recorded occurrences in both Myanmar and Thailand.
