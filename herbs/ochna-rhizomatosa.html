Ochna rhizomatosa

Ochna rhizomatosa, commonly known as a rhizomatous member of the Ochnaceae family, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Characterized by its unique root structure, this species is valued in traditional ethnobotany for its potential therapeutic properties and its significant role within its local ecosystem.

  • Scientific name: Ochna rhizomatosa
  • Family: Ochnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Common Names

This plant, Ochna rhizomatosa, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the rhizomatous ochna, while in Afrikaans, it is known as die rhizomatiese okna. Distribution

This plant, known as Ochna rhizomatosa, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented in several key countries, including Benin and Burkina Faso. Furthermore, it can be found growing within the borders of Ghana and Guinea. The species also extends its range into the territory of the Ivory Coast.
