Bistorta vivipara

Alpine bistort

Bistorta vivipara, commonly known as Viviparous Bistort, is a unique perennial herb native to alpine and subalpine regions. Distinguished by its remarkable ability to produce plantlets directly on its flower stalks, this resilient species thrives in moist, rocky habitats, playing a vital role in high-altitude mountain ecosystems worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Bistorta vivipara
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Bistorta.
