Alpine bistort, scientifically known as Bistorta vivipara, is a resilient perennial herb native to arctic and alpine tundra ecosystems. Characterized by its delicate pink flower spikes and robust rhizomes, this plant thrives in cold, damp environments. It plays a vital role in high-altitude biodiversity and traditional herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Bistorta vivipara, exhibits a specialized morphology characterized by its distinct vegetative and reproductive structures. The above-ground portion consists of upright stems and leaves that support the flowering spikes. Below the surface, the plant relies on complex root tissues for nutrient uptake, which are primarily connected to an extensive network of underground rhizomes. This creeping rhizome allows the species to spread vegetatively, ensuring survival in harsh alpine environments. In addition to vegetative growth, the plant produces small fruits that facilitate dispersal and the continuation of its life cycle.
