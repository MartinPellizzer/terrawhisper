Artemisia biennis

Artemisia biennis, commonly known as sweet wormwood, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Characterized by its aromatic foliage and delicate flowering stems, this plant offers potent bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial, digestive, and anti-inflammatory properties, serving as a vital botanical remedy worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia biennis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Artemisia.
