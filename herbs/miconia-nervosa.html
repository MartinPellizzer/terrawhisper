The ecology of Miconia nervosa is characterized by its broad adaptability to diverse tropical ecosystems and varying topographic positions. It inhabits a wide array of environmental settings, including Cerrado (latu sensu), Floresta de Terra Firme, Floresta de Várzea, Floresta Estacional Semi-decidual, Floresta Ombrófila (also known as Floresta Pluvial), and the Amazônica Savanna. This distribution allows the species to thrive in both seasonally flooded areas and stable upland forests. Furthermore, the species demonstrates a significant altitudinal plasticity, occupying a broad vertical gradient that extends from sea level (0 m AMSL) up to an elevation of 1450 m AMSL.
