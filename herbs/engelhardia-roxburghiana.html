Engelhardia roxburghiana

Engelhardia roxburgii, commonly known as the Engelhardia tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to South and Southeast Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, its various parts—including bark and leaves—possess potent bioactive properties. It is primarily utilized for treating skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, showcasing its remarkable therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Engelhardia roxburghiana
  • Family: Juglandaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Juglandaceae and falls under the genus Engelhardia.
