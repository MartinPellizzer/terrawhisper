Cigar flower, scientifically known as Cuphea ignea, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its vibrant, tubular blossoms. Native to Mexico, this ornamental plant features intense red or orange flowers that mimic small cigars. It is highly valued in landscaping for its compact growth and ability to attract pollinators.
