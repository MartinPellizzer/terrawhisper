Anemone chinensis

Pulsatilla chinensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (253 studies) · ★★★★★

Anemone chinensis, scientifically known as Pulsatilla chinensis, is a perennial herb deeply rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its striking purple blooms, this medicinal plant is primarily utilized to clear heat, resolve toxins, and regulate blood circulation, offering significant therapeutic potential for treating various inflammatory and gynecological conditions.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across parts of East Asia. Within the Russian Far East, its distribution includes the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central territories. Additionally, the species extends northward into the Inner Mongolia region of China. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varied continental environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, Pulsatilla chinensis, is highly valued in traditional medicine, primarily for its underground structures. The medicinal properties are concentrated within the roots, which are carefully harvested and processed for therapeutic use. Specifically, the root serves as the main source of its active compounds, and in pharmacological preparations, it is most commonly utilized in the form of dried radix.
