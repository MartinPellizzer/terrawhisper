Pulsatilla chinensis

Pulsatilla chinensis, commonly known as Chinese Pasque Flower, is a potent medicinal herb used in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, it effectively treats heat-related conditions, blood stagnation, and convulsions. While highly therapeutic for clearing toxins, it must be used with caution due to its natural toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Pulsatilla chinensis
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Pulsatilla.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across parts of East Asia. Within the Russian Far East, its distribution includes the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central territories. Additionally, the species extends northward into the Inner Mongolia region of China. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varied continental environments.
