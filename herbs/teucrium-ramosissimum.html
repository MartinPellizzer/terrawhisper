Teucrium ramosissimum

Teucrium ramosissimum, commonly known as the branching germander, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid regions. Renowned for its unique physiological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat various ailments. Its distinctive foliage and therapeutic potential make it a significant subject for botanical research.
