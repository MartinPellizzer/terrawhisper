Shrubby Cinquefoil, scientifically known as Dasiphora fruticosa, is a hardy, low-growing deciduous shrub native to arctic and alpine environments. Renowned for its fragrant, yellow flowers, this resilient plant is traditionally valued in herbal medicine for its astringent properties, often used to soothe digestive ailments and support skin health.
