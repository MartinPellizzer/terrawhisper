Dasiphora fruticosa

Dasiphora fruticosa, commonly known as Shrubsby or Golden Spiraea, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to Arctic and subarctic regions. Characterized by its vibrant yellow blooms, this hardy plant thrives in tundra environments. It holds significant ecological value, providing essential nectar for pollinators within its demanding, high-latitude habitats.

  • Scientific name: Dasiphora fruticosa
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Dasiphora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing throughout Great Britain and Ireland. Its range also extends further north to include the territories of Norway and Sweden. Additionally, the species is present within Middle Europe, specifically documented in Austria. These locations highlight the plant's preference for various northern and central European habitats.
