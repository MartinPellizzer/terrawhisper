Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across various regions of Mexico and Central America. Within Mexico, its presence is noted in the Gulf region, the Southwest, and the Southeast. Moving southward, the species can also be found throughout Belize. Furthermore, its range extends into the country of Costa Rica. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse habitats where the plant thrives.
