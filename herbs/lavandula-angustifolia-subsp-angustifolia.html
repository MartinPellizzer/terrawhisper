Lavandula angustifolia angustifolia, scientifically known as Lavandula angustifolia subsp. angustifolia, is a prized perennial herb renowned for its aromatic purple blooms. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this subspecies offers potent calming properties, frequently used to alleviate anxiety, improve sleep quality, and soothe skin irritations through its essential oils.
