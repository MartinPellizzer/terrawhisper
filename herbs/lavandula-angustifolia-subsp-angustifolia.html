Lavandula angustifolia subsp. angustifolia

Lavandula angustifolia angustifolia

Lavandula angustifolia subsp. angustifolia, commonly known as English lavender, is a highly prized perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic properties. Renowned for its calming scent, this subspecies is widely utilized in aromatherapy and traditional medicine to reduce anxiety, promote restful sleep, and soothe minor skin irritations and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Lavandula angustifolia subsp. angustifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Plant Parts

This plant, Lavandula angustifolia subsp. angustifolia, is primarily utilized for its therapeutic properties derived from its aerial parts, which include the fragrant flowering spikes and the leafy green stems. The most valuable medicinal component is the essential oil, which is extracted through steam distillation of these upper portions of the plant. This concentrated oil, captured from the blossoms and foliage, contains the complex aromatic compounds responsible for the plant's renowned calming and antiseptic effects.
