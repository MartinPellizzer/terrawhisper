Himalayan fleabane, scientifically known as Erigeron multiradiatus, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Characterized by its delicate, daisy-like blooms, this botanical treasure is valued in traditional medicine for its unique bioactive properties and its potential role in supporting various holistic healing practices.
