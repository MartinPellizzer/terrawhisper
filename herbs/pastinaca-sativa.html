Parsnip, scientifically known as Pastinaca sativa, is a biennial herbaceous plant belonging to the Apiaceae family. While primarily cultivated as a nutritious root vegetable, it holds historical significance in traditional medicine. Known for its distinct flavor, this plant requires careful handling due to its potential for skin photosensitivity.
