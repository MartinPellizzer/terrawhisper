Bauhinia variegata

Bauhinia variegata, commonly known as the Orchid Tree, is a stunning flowering plant prized for its ornamental beauty and medicinal properties. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, its various parts—including bark, leaves, and flowers—are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin disorders and inflammation to respiratory issues and digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Bauhinia variegata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is specifically positioned in the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Bauhinia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across several different continents and regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence can be documented specifically within Spain. Its distribution also extends to the Macaronesian islands, including Madeira. Moving toward the African continent, it is found throughout West Tropical Africa. Within this African region, it is specifically located in Ghana, Nigeria, and Sierra Leone.
