Quercus alba

Quercus alba, commonly known as the White Oak, is a majestic deciduous tree revered for its longevity and strength. Beyond its ecological importance, this species holds significant medicinal value. Historically, its bark has been utilized in traditional remedies to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various digestive issues through its tannins.

  • Scientific name: Quercus alba
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Quercus alba, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fagales and the family Fagaceae. Its taxonomic journey concludes within the genus Quercus.
