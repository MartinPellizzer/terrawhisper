Laggera crispata

Laggera crispata, commonly known as the "wild jasmine" of certain tropical regions, is a resilient medicinal shrub valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory potential, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering a natural approach to supporting holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Laggera crispata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Within this taxonomic hierarchy, it is identified under the genus Laggera.
