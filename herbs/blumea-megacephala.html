Blumea megacephala

Blumea megacephala, commonly known as Sambalang, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues. Its potent bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Blumea megacephala
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Blumea. Specifically, it is identified by the species name Blumea megacephala.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it is primarily found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into Eastern Asia, specifically within the Nansei-shoto islands and Taiwan. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indian Subcontinent, particularly in the Assam region. These varied locations highlight its broad ecological range across different Asian landscapes.
