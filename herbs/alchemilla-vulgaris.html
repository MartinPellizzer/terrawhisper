Alchemilla vulgaris

Alchemilla vulgaris, commonly known as Lady's Mantle, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its distinctive scalloped, velvety leaves that capture morning dew, this plant is renowned for its astringent properties. It is frequently utilized to support hormonal balance and promote healthy reproductive function.

  • Scientific name: Alchemilla vulgaris
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Alchemilla.
