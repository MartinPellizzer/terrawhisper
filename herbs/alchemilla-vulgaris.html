Lady's mantle, scientifically known as Alchemilla vulgaris, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Recognized by its scalloped, rosette-forming leaves that collect dew, this plant is widely used to support reproductive health. Its soothing properties make it a vital component in many natural remedies for women's wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Alchemilla vulgaris, commonly known as lady's mantle, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties derived from its leaves. These leaves are characterized by their distinctive rounded shape, scalloped edges, and a fine layer of soft hairs that give them a velvety texture. A unique feature of these leaves is their ability to collect small droplets of water through a process called guttation, which often makes them appear as if they are covered in morning dew. In herbal medicine, the leaves are often harvested and dried to create infusions or tinctures used for their astringent and anti-inflammatory qualities.
