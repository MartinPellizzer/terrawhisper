Disepalum plagioneurum

Disepalum plagioneurum, commonly known as the "leaf-veined disepalum," is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique morphological features, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This botanical species holds immense pharmacological potential, offering promising insights into natural therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Disepalum plagioneurum
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Disepalum plagioneurum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Magnoliales. It is further classified into the family Annonaceae and falls under the genus Disepalum.
