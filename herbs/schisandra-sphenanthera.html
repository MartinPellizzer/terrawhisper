Schisandra sphenanthera

Schisandra sphenanthera, commonly known as Schisandra, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine as an adaptogen, it is prized for its ability to reduce stress, enhance mental clarity, and support liver health. Its potent berries offer a unique blend of restorative properties.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra sphenanthera
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
