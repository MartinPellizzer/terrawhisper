Gypsophila perfoliata

Gypsophila perfoliata, commonly known as baby's breath in certain varieties, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Often utilized in traditional herbal medicine, it contains bioactive compounds believed to support respiratory health and soothe inflammation. This delicate botanical serves as a vital resource in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Gypsophila perfoliata
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales and the family Caryophyllaceae. It is further categorized into the genus Gypsophila, specifically known as Gypsophila perfoliata.

Common Names

This plant, Gypsophila perfoliaata, is known by several different common names depending on the language and region. In German, it is referred to as Durchwachsenblättriges Gipskraut, while in Chinese, its name is 钝叶石头花. In Bulgarian, it is called Текирска мишорка, and in Persian, it is known as گچ‌دوست ساقه محصور. Additionally, in Finnish, the plant is commonly called seporaunikki.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several parts of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in Germany, Hungary, and Poland. Moving toward the southeast, the species can also be found within the borders of Bulgaria. Furthermore, its range extends into Romania, marking another key location in Southeastern Europe. Collectively, these regions define the known natural habitat for Gypsophila perfoliata.
