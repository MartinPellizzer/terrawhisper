Anemone griffithii

What's the taxonomical classification of Anemone griffithii?

Anemone griffithii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is placed in the order Ranunculales. It is a member of the family Ranunculaceae, falling under the genus Anemone, and is specifically identified by the species name griffithii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Anemone griffithii has deeply lobed, palmate leaves with three to five distinct segments and actinomorphic flowers featuring five to seven white to pale pink tepals. The foliage is characterized by a basal rosette structure with long, slender petioles that elevate the leaves above the ground. Its flowers are centered around a prominent cluster of numerous yellow stamens that provide a bright contrast to the delicate petals. The plant typically grows from a creeping rhizome, which allows it to spread through its immediate habitat. Each blooming stem is relatively short and supports a single, large blossom at the apex.

The specific distribution is limited to these temperate montane zones, which provide the necessary moisture and cool temperatures for growth.
