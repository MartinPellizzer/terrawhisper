Prunus amygdalus

Almond tree

Prunus amygdalus, commonly known as the almond tree, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential fatty acids, antioxidants, and vitamins, its seeds and oils are utilized in traditional medicine to soothe skin, reduce inflammation, and support cardiovascular health through nutrient-dense applications.

  • Scientific name: Prunus amygdalus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Prunus.

Plant Parts

This plant, Prunus amygdalus, is highly valued for its various components, primarily centered around its nutritious seeds. The most common part used is the whole almond shelled seed, which is often consumed directly as the edible nut. These nuts are rich in essential nutrients and are frequently processed to extract almond oil, a versatile substance used in both culinary and medicinal applications. Additionally, the almond hulls, which are the fibrous outer coverings of the fruit, can also be utilized in various ways, though the primary medicinal and nutritional benefits are concentrated within the seeds themselves.
