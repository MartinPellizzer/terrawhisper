Vitis coignetiae

Vitis coignetiae, commonly known as the Coignet vine, is a specialized medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its unique bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to support holistic wellness. Understanding its botanical properties and therapeutic potential offers fascinating insights into the profound healing power of nature.

  • Scientific name: Vitis coignetiae
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Vitales and the family Vitaeaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Vitis, specifically as the species Vitis coignetiae.
