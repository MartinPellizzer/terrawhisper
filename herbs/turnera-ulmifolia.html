Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct oceanic regions. Within the Macaronesian islands, it can be found specifically on the Canary Islands. Its presence extends significantly into the Western Indian Ocean, where it inhabits the Aldabra atoll. Furthermore, the species is documented in the Chagos Archipelago and the Comoros. Finally, its geographical range includes the island of Mauritius, marking its presence in the Western Indian Ocean.
