What's the taxonomical classification of Parietaria micrantha?

Parietarica micrantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales. This plant is a representative of the family Urticaceae, which is a group known for its diverse herbaceous species. Within this family, it is categorized under the genus Parietaria, with the specific identifier being the species micrantha.
