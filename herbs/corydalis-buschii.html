Corydalis buschii

What's the taxonomical classification of Corydalis buschii?

Corydalis buschii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, eventually placing it under the order Ranunculales. As a member of the family Papaveraceae, this plant is further categorized into the genus Corydalis, with its specific designation being the species buschii.

What are the pharmacological activities of Corydalis buschii?
