Cochlospermum regium

Cochlospermum regium, commonly known as the Yellow Silk Cotton tree, is a striking medicinal plant native to Africa. Renowned for its brilliant golden blooms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the tree are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Cochlospermum regium
  • Family: Bixaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, it is further categorized into the family Bixaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cochlospermum.
