Gardenia gummifera

Gardenia gummifera, commonly known as the gum gardenia, is a distinctive shrub native to the arid regions of Western Australia. Renowned for its fragrant white blooms and resinous properties, this resilient species plays a vital role in its ecosystem, offering unique medicinal potential and botanical beauty to collectors.
