Montanoa tomentosa

Montanoa tomentoza, commonly known as the "fever plant," is a perennial shrub native to the mountainous regions of Mexico. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves are frequently utilized for their potent anti-inflammatory and antipyretic properties, helping to alleviate fevers, reduce swelling, and treat various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Montanoa tomentosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Montanoa.
