Paraphlomis chinensis

Paraphlomis chinensis, commonly known as Chinese Paraphlomis, is a significant medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Native to East Asia, this plant is primarily utilized for its therapeutic properties in treating respiratory ailments and inflammatory conditions. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for modern pharmacological research and herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Paraphlomis chinensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Paraphlomis. Therefore, its complete scientific designation identifies it as Paraphlomis chinensis.

This plant is primarily found across specific regions of Eastern Asia. Within China, its distribution is concentrated in the Southeast. It also has a recorded presence in the island nation of Japan.
