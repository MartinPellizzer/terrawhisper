Angelonia angustifolia

Angelonia angustifolia, commonly known as narrow-leaved angelica, is a perennial herb native to specific alpine and subalpine regions. Renowned for its delicate structure and aromatic properties, this medicinal plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional herbalism to support digestive health and provide soothing, restorative benefits for the body.
