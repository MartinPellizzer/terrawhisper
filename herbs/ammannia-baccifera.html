Ammannia baccifera

Amannnia baccifera, commonly known as the "bush medicinal" in certain African regions, is a significant botanical species utilized in traditional healing practices. Primarily recognized for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant plays a vital role in community wellness, offering potential pharmacological benefits for treating various ailments through natural extracts.
