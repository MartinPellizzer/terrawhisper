Coleus madagascariensis

Coleus madagascariensis, commonly known as Madagascar Coleus, is a versatile medicinal herb celebrated in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its vibrant foliage, this plant offers potent therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. It is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and various digestive disorders naturally.

  • Scientific name: Coleus madagascariensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Coleus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the southern regions of the continent. In South Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Mozambique. Its presence extends throughout Southern Africa, specifically within the Cape Provinces. Furthermore, the species is documented in the KwaZulu-Natal and Free State regions. Finally, its range includes the territory of Eswatini.
