Salicornia bigelovii

Salicornia bigeloii, commonly known as saltwort or glasswort, is a succulent halophyte thriving in saline coastal environments. Renowned for its adaptability, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. It contains bioactive compounds with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a subject of interest in traditional medicine and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Salicornia bigelovii
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Amaranthaecae. Within this hierarchy, it is specifically categorized under the genus Salicornia.
