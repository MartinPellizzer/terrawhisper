Rumex nervosus

Rumex nervosus, commonly known as Nervy Dock, is a perennial herb native to the Pacific Northwest. Distinguished by its prominent leaf venation, this plant thrives in moist, disturbed soils. While often considered a common weed, it holds ecological value and historical significance within traditional botanical studies and regional landscapes.
