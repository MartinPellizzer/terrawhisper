Adenaria floribunda

Adenaria floribunda, commonly known as the Coast Rose, is a distinctive climbing shrub native to South Africa. Characterized by its delicate, heart-shaped leaves and striking pinkish flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its healing properties, often utilized in folk remedies to treat various ailments and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Adenaria floribunda
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Adenaria floribunda, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the family Lythraceae and is situated within the genus Adenaria.
