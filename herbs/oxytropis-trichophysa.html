Oxytropis trichophysa

Oxytropis trichophysa, commonly known as a member of the legume family, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid mountainous regions. Renowned for its distinctive hairy foliage and vibrant pea-like flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally studied for its unique phytochemical properties and potential therapeutic applications in herbal medicine.
