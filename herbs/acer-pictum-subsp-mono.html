Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is a member of the family Sapindaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal subspecies mono is categorized under the genus Acer.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning several key regions across East Asia. Within the Russian Far East, its presence is documented in the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Its distribution also extends significantly into China, covering the South-Central areas. Furthermore, the species can be found as far north as Inner Mongolia. These diverse locations highlight the adaptable nature of its natural habitat.
