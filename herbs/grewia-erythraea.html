Grewia erythraea

Grewia erythraea, commonly known as the phalsa or phalsa shrub, is a resilient medicinal plant native to arid regions of Africa and Asia. Renowned for its nutrient-rich fruits, it is traditionally used to treat digestive issues, fever, and inflammation, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic wellness practices.
