Evening-primrose, scientifically known as Oenothera, is a versatile flowering plant cherished for its therapeutic potential. Renowned for its evening blooms, it is a rich source of gamma-linolenic acid. Traditionally used to support skin health and hormonal balance, this resilient herb remains a cornerstone in natural wellness and botanical studies.
