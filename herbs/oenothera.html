Oenothera

Oenothera, commonly known as evening primrose, is a versatile genus of flowering plants celebrated for its medicinal properties. Renowned for its oil-rich seeds, it is frequently utilized to support hormonal balance and skin health. This resilient herb offers a natural approach to managing inflammatory conditions and promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Oenothera
  • Family: Onagraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtoales, it is classified under the family Onagraceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Oenothera.

Distribution

It can also be found growing within the diverse landscapes of Finland. Furthermore, the species is distributed across the islands of Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, its geographical range extends into the rugged terrain of Norway.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as evening primrose, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties derived primarily from its reproductive components. While the leaves and roots are sometimes utilized, the most significant medicinal part is the oil seed. These small seeds are harvested to extract a potent oil that is rich in gamma-linolenic acid, an essential fatty acid used to support skin health and alleviate various inflammatory conditions.
