Leopard's bane, scientifically known as Arnica montana, is a perennial herb native to the mountain ranges of Europe. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this medicinal plant is widely utilized in homeopathic and topical treatments to alleviate bruising, swelling, and muscle aches through its potent anti-inflammatory properties.
