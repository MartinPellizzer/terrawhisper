Arnica montana · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (547 studies) · ★★★★★

Leopard's bane, scientifically known as Arnica montana, is a perennial herb native to the mountain ranges of Europe. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this medicinal plant is widely utilized in homeopathic and topical treatments to alleviate bruising, swelling, and muscle aches through its potent anti-inflammatory properties.

Plant Parts

This plant, Arnica montana, is characterized by its striking, daisy-like appearance, which is primarily defined by its vibrant yellow flowers. These large, composite flowerheads are composed of numerous individual florets that densely pack the structure to create a brilliant display. Within these complex flower heads, the medicinal properties are distributed throughout the various flower tissues, making the entire reproductive structure a key focus for botanical study and traditional use.
