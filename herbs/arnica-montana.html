Arnica montana

Arnica montana, commonly known as mountain arnica, is a perennial herb native to the alpine regions of Europe. Renowned for its bright yellow flowers, it is widely utilized in herbal medicine to alleviate bruising, swelling, and muscle aches. However, it must be used externally to avoid toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Arnica montana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Arnica montana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is situated within the genus Arnica.
