Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions of Eastern Asia. Within the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted in the Kuril Islands. The species is also extensively distributed throughout various parts of China, including the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Additionally, its habitat extends into the islands of Japan. Together, these locations define the natural spreading pattern of Chloranthus serratus.
