Strychnos pseudoquina

Strychnos pseudoquina, commonly known as pseudoquina, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Historically valued for its potent alkaloids, it has been utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Understanding its chemical properties and therapeutic potential remains crucial for exploring new developments in modern pharmacology.
