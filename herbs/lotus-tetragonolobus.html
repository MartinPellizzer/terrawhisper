Lotus tetragonolobus

Lotus tetragonolobus, commonly known as the four-angled lotus, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality, making it a significant botanical resource in various holistic wellness traditions.

  • Scientific name: Lotus tetragonolobus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Lotus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lotus tetragonolobus, is referred to by various regional names depending on the language and culture. In Polish, it is called Głąbigroszek szkarłatny, while in Lower Sorbian, it is known as Čerwjeny štyrjownik. The Dutch name for this species is Asperge-erwt, and in German, it is commonly called Rote Spargelbohne. Finally, in Chinese, it is identified as 翅荚百脉根.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several European regions. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented within the territories of Germany and the Czechia-Slovakia border area. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it is found throughout Southwestern Europe. Specifically, its range includes the islands of Baleares and Corse. Additionally, the plant is widely distributed across the mainland of France.
