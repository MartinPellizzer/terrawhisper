Distribution

This plant, Xanthosoma sagittifolium, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Benin, Guinea, and Guinea-Bissau. The species also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Gabon. Furthermore, its range reaches the coastal areas of the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's importance within these tropical ecosystems.
