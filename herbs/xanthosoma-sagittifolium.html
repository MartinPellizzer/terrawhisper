Xanthosoma sagittifolium

Xanthosoma sagittifolium, commonly known as the malanga or taro, is a versatile tropical plant valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in essential nutrients, its tubers are used in traditional medicine to support digestion, manage inflammation, and promote healing, making it a staple in various cultural dietary practices.

  • Scientific name: Xanthosoma sagittifolium
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Aliasmaetales and the family Araceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Xanthosoma.
