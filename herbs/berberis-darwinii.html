Berberis darwinii

Berberis darwinii, commonly known as Darwin's barberry, is a resilient, spreading evergreen shrub native to New Zealand. Characterized by its dense, fine-textured foliage and delicate white flowers, it adds architectural interest to gardens. This hardy species is highly valued for its ability to thrive in varied landscape settings.

  • Scientific name: Berberis darwinii
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it falls into the family Berberidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Berberis.
