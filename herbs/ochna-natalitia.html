Ochna natalitia

Ochna natalitia, commonly known as the Natal Bush Rose, is a striking evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its vibrant, rose-like yellow flowers and distinctive ornamental fruit, this plant is highly valued in landscaping. It serves as an important nectar source for local pollinators within diverse ecosystems.
