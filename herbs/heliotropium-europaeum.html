Heliotropium europaeum

Heliotropium europaeum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (27 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Scientifically known as Heliotropium europaeum, is a small, annual herb native to Europe. Often called European heliotrope, it features delicate, tubular blue flowers. While historically used in traditional medicine for its various botanical properties, it should be handled with caution due to the presence of toxic pyrrolizidine alkaloids.
