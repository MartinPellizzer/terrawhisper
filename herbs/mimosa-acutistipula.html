Mimosa acutistipula

Mimosa acutistipula, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, holds significant importance in traditional healing practices. Found primarily in tropical regions, this plant is celebrated for its diverse bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, offering promising natural therapeutic properties for holistic wellness and herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Mimosa acutistipula
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling into the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Mimosa.

Common Names

This plant, Mimosa acuatisipula, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across the diverse landscapes of Western South America. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Bolivia.
