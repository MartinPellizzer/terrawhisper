Solanum mammosum

Solanum mammosum, commonly known as nipplefruit or breast-shaped nightshade, is a distinctive perennial herb native to tropical regions. Characterized by its unique, bumpy fruit, this plant belongs to the Solanaceae family. While historically used in traditional medicine, its bioactive compounds require careful study due to potential toxicity levels.

  • Scientific name: Solanum mammosum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Europe

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several continents and climate zones. In Middle Europe, it can be found specifically within the borders of Germany. Its range extends significantly into the African continent, including West Tropical Africa in Ghana. Furthermore, it inhabits West-Central Tropical Africa, with notable presence in Burundi and the DR Congo. Finally, the species is also distributed throughout East Tropical Africa, particularly in Kenya.
