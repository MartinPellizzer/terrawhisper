Solanum mammosum, scientifically known as Solanum mammosum, is a distinctive perennial herb belonging to the Solanaceae family. Characterized by its unique, nipple-like fruit, this plant holds significant importance in traditional medicine. It is frequently studied for its diverse pharmacological properties, including potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial therapeutic applications.
