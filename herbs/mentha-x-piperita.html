Mentha piperita

Mentha × piperita · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (175 studies) · ★★★★☆

Mentha piperita, scientifically known as Mentha × piperita, is a refreshing hybrid mint widely celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned for its high menthol content, this aromatic herb is frequently utilized to soothe digestive issues, alleviate headaches, and provide cooling relief, making it a staple in natural wellness.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Mentha. As a hybrid species, its scientific name is Mentha × piperita.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as peppermint, utilizes various components for its therapeutic properties, though its primary medicinal value is concentrated in the foliage. The aromatic leaves are the most widely used part of the plant, containing high concentrations of active compounds. Within these leaves, as well as in the stems, are the essential oils that provide the characteristic cooling sensation and scent. While the plant may produce small, purple flowers during its blooming stage, the most potent medicinal source remains the extracted essential oil, which is harvested through distillation to be used in aromatherapy and various health applications.
