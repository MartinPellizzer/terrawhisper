Mentha × piperita

Mentha × piperita, commonly known as peppermint, is a sterile hybrid mint celebrated for its invigorating aroma and therapeutic properties. Rich in menthol, this versatile herb is widely utilized in herbal medicine to soothe digestive issues, alleviate headaches, and provide a refreshing sensory experience in teas, oils, and cosmetics.

  • Scientific name: Mentha × piperita
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Mentha. As a hybrid species, its scientific name is Mentha × piperita.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several regions in Europe. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in Denmark, Great Britain, and Ireland. Its presence extends into Middle Europe as well. Specifically, it is documented in countries such as Austria and Belgium. This variety of locations highlights its adaptability to different European climates.
