Cape jackalskos, scientifically known as Hydnora africana, is a fascinating, parasitic plant endemic to Southern Africa. Lacking leaves and chlorophyll, it survives by attaching to the roots of host plants. Traditionally used in various medicinal practices, this unique, subterranean organism offers intriguing insights into specialized botanical survival and ethnobotany.
