Rauvolfia

Rauvolfia, commonly known as Indian Snakeroot, is a significant medicinal shrub renowned for its potent alkaloids. Most famous for containing reserpine, it has been traditionally used to treat hypertension and mental disorders. This versatile plant remains a cornerstone in pharmacological research for developing essential cardiovascular and sedative medications.

  • Scientific name: Rauvolfia
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, falling under the family Apocynaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Rauvolfia.
