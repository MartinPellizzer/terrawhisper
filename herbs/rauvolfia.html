Devil peppers, scientifically known as Rauvolfia, is a versatile genus of medicinal plants renowned for their potent pharmacological properties. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, these shrubs are most famous for containing reserpine, a crucial alkaloid used to treat hypertension and psychiatric disorders, highlighting their significant role in modern pharmacology.
