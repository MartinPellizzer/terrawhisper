Astilbe rivularis

Astilbe rivularis, commonly known as the Rivularis Astilbe, is a captivating perennial prized for its delicate, plume-like flower spikes. Native to specific mountainous regions, this plant thrives in moist, shaded environments. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique physiological properties and soothing qualities.

  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Europe
