Beta vulgaris

Beta vulgaris, commonly known as beetroot, is a nutrient-dense root vegetable celebrated for its medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants, nitrates, and essential vitamins, it supports cardiovascular health, reduces blood pressure, and enhances athletic performance. This versatile plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, making it a staple in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Beta vulgaris
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Beta.
