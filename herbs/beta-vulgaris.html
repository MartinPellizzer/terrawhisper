Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Beta vulgaris, is highly valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties found throughout its anatomy. The most prominent part is the red beet, a nutrient-dense storage organ often referred to as the beet root, which is rich in antioxidants and nitrates. In addition to the tuber, the edible leaves provide a significant source of vitamins and minerals. Beyond whole consumption, various therapeutic applications utilize a concentrated leaf extract or a root derivative to harness its specific bioactive compounds for health benefits.
