Ephedra aphylla

Ephedra aphylla, commonly known as the leafless ephedra, is a resilient shrub native to the arid regions of North Africa and the Middle East. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains bioactive alkaloids used traditionally to treat respiratory ailments, fatigue, and various systemic conditions through its potent pharmacological effects.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra aphylla
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Gnetidae and order Ephedrales, falling into the family Ephedraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ephedra, specifically as the species Ephedra aphylla.
