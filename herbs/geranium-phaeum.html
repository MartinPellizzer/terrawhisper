Geranium phaeum

Black widow

Geranium phaeum, commonly known known as the Dusky Cranesbill, is a striking perennial valued for its deep purple blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds traditional medicinal significance. Often utilized in herbal remedies, it possesses unique bioactive properties that have historically supported various wellness practices and botanical treatments.

  • Scientific name: Geranium phaeum
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae, it is classified under the genus Geraunium.
