White Chebulic Myrobalan, scientifically known as Terminalia pallida, is a significant medicinal tree native to South and Southeast Asia. Widely used in Ayurvedic medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Its fruits and bark are traditionally utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin infections, and various systemic inflammations.
