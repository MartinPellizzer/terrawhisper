Monterey cypress, scientifically known as Hesperocyparis macrocarpa, is a coastal conifer native to California. While primarily valued for its durable timber and ornamental beauty, it holds traditional significance. Its resins and essential oils possess antimicrobial properties, offering potential therapeutic applications in folk medicine for addressing various skin and respiratory ailments.
