Hesperocyparis macrocarpa

Hesperocyparis macrocarpa, commonly known as the Monterey cypress, is a distinctive coniferous evergreen native to the California coast. While primarily celebrated for its ornamental beauty and ecological importance, this resilient tree holds traditional significance in coastal folk medicine, where its aromatic resins and wood are occasionally utilized for therapeutic purposes.
