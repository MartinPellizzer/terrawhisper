Sterculia

Sterculia, commonly known as cotton trees or snowball trees, comprises a diverse genus of tropical flowering plants. Renowned for their unique botanical structures and ornamental value, many species hold significant medicinal importance. They are frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various digestive disorders.

  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Sterculia.
