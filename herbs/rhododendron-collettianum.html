Rhododendron collettianum

Rhododendron collettianum, commonly known as the Himalayan Rhododendron, is a resilient shrub native to high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its vibrant blossoms, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its antioxidant properties and its ability to treat various ailments, including respiratory and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron collettianum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is part of the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Rhododendron.

Common Names

This plant, Rhododendron collettianum, is known by various regional names depending on the language and local culture. In English, it is commonly referred to as Collett's rhododendron, while in Turkish, it is known as kolektan. In the local dialects of the regions where it thrives, such as in parts of the Caucasus or surrounding areas, it may also be identified by specific vernacular terms used in traditional medicine.
