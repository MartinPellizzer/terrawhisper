Flower of the Andes, scientifically known as Chuquiraga jussieui, is a resilient shrub endemic to the high-altitude paramo ecosystems. Renowned for its striking orange blooms, this sacred plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional Andean healing, frequently used to treat respiratory ailments, kidney issues, and to boost overall immunity.
