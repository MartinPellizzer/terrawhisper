Pulmonaria mollis subsp. mollissima

Pulmonaria mollis subsp. mollissima, commonly known as Lungwort, is a perennial herb celebrated for its striking, spotted foliage and bell-shaped flowers. Historically valued in traditional medicine for treating respiratory ailments, this resilient plant thrives in shaded woodlands, offering both aesthetic charm and significant botanical interest for herbalists.

  • Scientific name: Pulmonaria mollis subsp. mollissima
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is part of the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Pulmonaria.
