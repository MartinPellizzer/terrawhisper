Aldama robusta

Aldama robusta, commonly known as a potent medicinal botanical, is celebrated for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to tropical regions, this remarkable plant offers significant bioactive compounds used in traditional healing. Its applications range from anti-inflammatory support to antimicrobial benefits, making it a vital subject in ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Aldama robusta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Aldama.

Distribution

This plant, Aldama robusta, exhibits a broad presence across several key regions of Brazil. Its natural habitat extends throughout the North of the country, covering vast tropical landscapes. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed within the West-Central and Southeast regions of Brazil. Its range also reaches down into the southern part of the nation, specifically the South region. Consequently, the geographical footprint of this medicinal plant spans a significant portion of the Brazilian territory.
