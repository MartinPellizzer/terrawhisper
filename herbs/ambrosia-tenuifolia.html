Ambrosia tenuifolia

Ambrosia tenuifolia, commonly known as the slenderleaf ragweed, is a distinctive herbaceous plant native to various regions. Characterized by its fine, delicate foliage, this species plays a unique role in its ecosystem. While often associated with pollen production, it remains a subject of botanical interest for its specific morphological traits.

  • Scientific name: Ambrosia tenuifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. It is further identified by the genus Ambrosia, specifically as the species Ambrosia tenuifolia.
