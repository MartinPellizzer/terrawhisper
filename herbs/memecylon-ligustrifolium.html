What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Memecylon ligustrifolium has elliptical to oblong leaves that are glossy, coriaceous, and typically measuring between five and twelve centimeters in length. These flowers are followed by small, globose berries that transition from green to a deep purple or black color when ripe.
