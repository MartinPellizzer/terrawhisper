Pulsatilla

Pulsatilla, commonly known as pasqueflower, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its striking, bell-shaped blooms, this plant is frequently utilized in homeopathic remedies to address emotional sensitivity and skin conditions. Its delicate beauty belies a potent botanical profile used to balance various systemic imbalances.

  • Scientific name: Pulsatilla
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae, it is classified under the genus Pulsatilla.
