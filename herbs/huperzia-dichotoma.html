Huperzia dichotomoma, scientifically known as Huperzia dichotoma, is a perennial clubmoss valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its neuroprotective properties, it contains huperzine A, a potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. This alkaloid is widely studied for enhancing cognitive function and potentially treating neurodegenerative disorders like Alzheimer's disease.
