Huperzia dichotoma

Huperzia dichotoma, commonly known as Huperzia or upright firmoss, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its neuroprotective properties. It contains huperzine A, a potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. This alkaloid is widely studied for its potential to enhance cognitive function and treat neurodegenerative conditions like Alzheimer's disease and dementia.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia dichotoma
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Lycopodiidae and the order Lycopodiales, falling into the family Lycopodiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Huperzia.
