Plant Parts

This plant, Ficus insipida, is characterized by the presence of a milky, white substance known as latex, which flows from the stems and leaves when the plant tissue is damaged. This latex serves as a vital component of the plant's biological makeup and is the primary part utilized in various traditional medicinal applications. In many cultures, this sticky secretion is harvested for its specific therapeutic properties, making the latex a central element in the plant's medicinal profile.
