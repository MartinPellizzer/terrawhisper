Pseudotaxus chienii

White-berry yew

Pseudotaxus chienii, commonly known as the Chinese Yew, is a rare evergreen conifer native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized for their bioactive compounds. This resilient plant holds significant pharmacological potential, particularly in researching natural therapeutic agents for diverse medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Pseudotaxus chienii
  • Family: Taxaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales, it is classified under the family Taxaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Pseudotaxus, with the species name chienii.
