Pentanema britannica

Pentanema britannica, commonly known as British Star-leaf, is a rare medicinal herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this plant is frequently utilized to support respiratory health and reduce systemic inflammation. Understanding its unique bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Pentanema britannica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Pentanema.
