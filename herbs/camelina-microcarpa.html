Camelina microcarpa

Camelina microcarpa, commonly known as small-fruited camelina, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Brassicaceae family. Valued for its nutrient-dense seeds, it serves as a potent source of essential fatty acids and antioxidants. Traditionally utilized in folk medicine, it offers significant therapeutic potential for promoting overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Camelina microcarpa
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Camelina microcarpa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Ultimately, it is placed within the genus Camelina.

Distribution

This plant, known as Camelina microcarpa, exhibits a specific distribution across various regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing naturally within the borders of Denmark. The species also inhabits the landscapes of Finland and Norway. Furthermore, its range extends to the islands of Great Britain and Ireland. Together, these locations define its primary geographical presence in the northern part of the continent.
