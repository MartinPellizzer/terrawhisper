What's the taxonomical classification of Euonymus nitidus?

Euonymus nitidus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Celastrales and is a member of the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is identified within the genus Euonymus and is specifically designated as the species nitidus.
