This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Eurasia. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Romania. The species also extends into Eastern Europe, where it can be found throughout Ukraine as well as across both Central and East European Russia. Plant Parts

This plant, Artemisia argyi var. argyi, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its various botanical components. The most significant portion used for therapeutic purposes is the mugwort leaf, which contains the active compounds necessary for its medicinal properties. More broadly, the medicinal efficacy of the species is often attributed to the use of its aerial parts, encompassing the leaves and stems that grow above the ground, ensuring that the most potent bioactive elements are harvested for treatment.
