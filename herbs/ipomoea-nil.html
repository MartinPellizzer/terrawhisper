Ipomoea nil

Ipomoea nil, commonly known as the water bind or morning glory, is a versatile medicinal plant frequently utilized in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions, promoting wound healing, and supporting digestive health across various global cultures.
