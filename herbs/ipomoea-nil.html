Ipomoea nil

Picotee morning glory

Ipomoea nil, commonly known as the water bind or morning glory, is a versatile medicinal plant frequently utilized in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions, promoting wound healing, and supporting digestive health across various global cultures.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea nil
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ipomoea nil, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ipomoea.

Common Names

This plant, Ipomoea nil, is widely recognized by several common names across different Chinese linguistic contexts. In Simplified Chinese, as used in mainland China, it is known as 牵牛花 (zh-hans and zh-cn), and this same nomenclature is also applied in Singapore (zh-sg). In Traditional Chinese, the name is written as 牽牛花, which is the standard form used in Hong Kong (zh-hk) and throughout other regions utilizing Traditional characters (zh-hant).
