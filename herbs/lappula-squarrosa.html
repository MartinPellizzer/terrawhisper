European stickseed, scientifically known as Lappula squarrosa, is a perennial herb belonging to the Boraginaceae family. Characterized by its tiny, hooked hairs that cling to clothing, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine. It is studied for its potential bioactive properties, particularly regarding its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities.
