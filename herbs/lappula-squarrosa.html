Lappula squarrosa

European stickseed

Lappula squarrosa, commonly known as rough beggarticks, is a resilient herbaceous plant belonging to the Boraginaceae family. Noted for its small, hairy flowers and sticky seeds, this species is valued in traditional ethnobotany. It is frequently studied for its potential bioactive compounds and therapeutic applications in diverse medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Lappula squarrosa
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe
