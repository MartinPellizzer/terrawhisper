What's the taxonomical classification of Canthium simile?

Canthium simile belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Gentianaales. This plant is further categorized under the family Rubiaceae, which places it within the genus Canthium, specifically identified by the species name simile.
