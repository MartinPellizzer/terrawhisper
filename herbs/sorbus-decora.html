Sorbus decora

Northern mountain ash

Sorbus decora, commonly known as the decorative mountain ash, is a resilient deciduous shrub prized for its ornamental beauty. Beyond its striking berries and foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Its unique chemical properties offer potential medicinal benefits, making it a subject of interest in traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Sorbus decora
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sorbus.
