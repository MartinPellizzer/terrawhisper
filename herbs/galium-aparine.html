Goose grass, scientifically known as Galium aparine, is a common annual herb characterized by its square stems and hooked hairs. Often found in disturbed soils, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties, frequently utilized to support skin health and kidney function naturally.
