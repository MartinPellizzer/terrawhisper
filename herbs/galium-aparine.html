Galium aparine

Galium aparine, commonly known as cleavers or bedstraw, is a versatile herbaceous plant characterized by its distinctive hooked hairs. Widely distributed across temperate regions, this climbing perennial has long been utilized in traditional herbalism for its diuretic, lymphatic, and skin-soothing properties, making it a staple in folk medicine.

  • Scientific name: Galium aparine
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Galium.
