Euphorbia ammak

African candelabra

Euphorbia ammak, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, is highly valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, particularly for skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. Exploring its phytochemical properties reveals its profound potential in modern natural pharmacology and wellness.
