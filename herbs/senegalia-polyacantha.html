Senegalia polyacantha

Senegalia polyacantha, commonly known as the many-spined acacia, is a resilient woody plant native to various African landscapes. Valued in traditional medicine, its bark and leaves possess significant therapeutic properties. Often used to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments, this species remains a vital component of regional ethnobotanical practices.
