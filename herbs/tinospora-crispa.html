Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Giloy, is a climbing shrub characterized by its vigorous growth habit where it utilizes woody vines to ascend surrounding vegetation. The structure of the plant is defined by its succulent yet sturdy stems, which serve as the primary conduit for nutrients and are often the most valued part for therapeutic use. The lush, green leaves are typically heart-shaped, providing a dense canopy for the climbing organism. In traditional medicine, practitioners often harvest and utilize the dried stems to prepare decoctions and powders, ensuring that the medicinal potency of the stem is preserved through careful processing.
