Tinospora crispa

Tinospora crispa, commonly known as Giloy or Guduchi in Ayurvedic medicine, is a versatile climbing shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its potent immunomodulatory and antioxidant effects, this medicinal plant plays a vital role in traditional healing, effectively managing fever, inflammation, and various metabolic disorders.

  • Scientific name: Tinospora crispa
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ranunculales, it is situated in the family Menispermaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Tinospora.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Giloy, is a climbing shrub characterized by its vigorous growth habit where it utilizes woody vines to ascend surrounding vegetation. The structure of the plant is defined by its succulent yet sturdy stems, which serve as the primary conduit for nutrients and are often the most valued part for therapeutic use. The lush, green leaves are typically heart-shaped, providing a dense canopy for the climbing organism. In traditional medicine, practitioners often harvest and utilize the dried stems to prepare decoctions and powders, ensuring that the medicinal potency of the stem is preserved through careful processing.
