Camphora migao

Camphora migao, commonly known as camphor, is a powerful medicinal plant prized for its aromatic properties. Extracted from various species, it offers potent antiseptic, analgesic, and anti-inflammatory benefits. Widely used in traditional healing and modern topical treatments, this versatile botanical serves as a cornerstone for respiratory and skin health.

  • Scientific name: Camphora migao
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cinnamomum (often referred to in specific contexts as Cinnamomum migao).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range within the diverse landscapes of China. It is primarily documented to grow across the South-Central regions of the country. Additionally, its natural habitat extends into the Southeast provinces of China. These specific areas provide the necessary environmental conditions required for its survival. Consequently, its distribution is concentrated within these particular southern and central territories.
