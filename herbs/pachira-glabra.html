The physiology of Pachira glabra is characterized by its reliance on the C3 photosynthetic pathway, a mechanism where carbon dioxide is fixed directly through the Calvin cycle without the specialized spatial or temporal separation found in C4 or CAM plants. This metabolic approach involves the initial fixation of CO2 by the enzyme RuBisCO to form a three-carbon compound, which is typical for many tropical woody species.
