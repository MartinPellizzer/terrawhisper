Pachira glabra

Pachira glabra, commonly known as the Money Tree, is a popular tropical ornamental plant celebrated for its braided trunk and lush, palm-like foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal in interior design, it holds cultural significance in Feng Shui, symbolizing prosperity, good fortune, and positive energy for those who cultivate it.

  • Scientific name: Pachira glabra
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Pachira glabra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae and is categorized under the genus Pachira.
