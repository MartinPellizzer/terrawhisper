Sphagneticola calendulacea

Morning flower

Sphagneticola calendulacea, commonly known as Bay Biscayne creeping oxeye, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it exhibits significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This resilient herb is increasingly studied for its potential therapeutic applications in treating various skin and systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Sphagneticola calendulacea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sphagneticola.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Sphagneticola calendulacea, is identified by various common names depending on the language and region. In English, it is referred to as the morning flower, while in Chinese, it is widely known as 蟛蜞菊. Depending on the specific regional orthography, its Chinese names include 黃花蜜菜 (zh and zh-tw) and 黄花蜜菜 (zh-cn).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of the Asian and European continents. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Italy. Moving toward the Asian continent, it can be found in the Manchuria region of China. Its range further extends into Southeast China, covering a significant portion of that territory. Additionally, the species is widely distributed throughout Eastern Asia, including both Japan and Korea.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Bay Biscayne creeping-herb or Wedelia, utilizes its leaves as a primary component for its medicinal properties. The leaves are characterized by their serrated edges and a bright green color, often growing in a dense, creeping pattern along the ground. In various traditional practices, these leaves are harvested and processed to extract bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's therapeutic potential.
