Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Bay Biscayne creeping-herb or Wedelia, utilizes its leaves as a primary component for its medicinal properties. The leaves are characterized by their serrated edges and a bright green color, often growing in a dense, creeping pattern along the ground. In various traditional practices, these leaves are harvested and processed to extract bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's therapeutic potential.
