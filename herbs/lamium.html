Deadnettle, scientifically known as Lamium, is a versatile genus of herbaceous plants within the Lamiaceae family. Often characterized by square stems and non-stinging leaves, these resilient perennials feature charming tubular flowers. While primarily ornamental, various species hold significant value in traditional herbalism for their diverse medicinal and therapeutic properties.
