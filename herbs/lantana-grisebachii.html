Lantana grisebachii

Lantana grisebachii, commonly known as Lantana, is a versatile perennial shrub celebrated for its vibrant, multi-colored flower clusters. Renowned for its ornamental beauty and hardiness, this medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential, utilized in various traditional practices to treat inflammation, fever, and skin ailments through its bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
