Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the Mediterranean. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in Portugal and Spain. Its presence is also noted on the islands of Corse and Sardegna within the same region. Additionally, the species extends into Southeastern Europe.
