Isodon rubescens

Isodon rubescens, commonly known as Redbean, is a deciduous tree native to North American forests. While primarily valued for its dense, durable timber, it holds traditional significance in ethnobotany. This species thrives in moist soils, playing a vital role in forest ecosystems and offering potential medicinal properties through its components.
