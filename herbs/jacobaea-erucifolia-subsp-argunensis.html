Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across several distinct regions of Northern and Central Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found specifically in the areas of Chita and Yakutiya. Moving eastward, the species is also documented within the Russian Far East, particularly in Primorye. Its distribution extends into China, where it is recorded in the South-Central region. Additionally, the plant inhabits the northern reaches of China within Inner Mongolia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Jacobaea erucifolia subsp. argunensis, is characterized by its distinct morphological features, most notably its leaf structure. The leaf is a primary component used in botanical identification, typically exhibiting a pinnatifid or deeply lobed shape that is characteristic of the Senecio group. These leaves are arranged along the stem and play a crucial role in the plant's metabolic processes, serving as the main site for photosynthesis and the accumulation of secondary metabolites that contribute to its medicinal profile.
