Pluchea

Pluchea, commonly known as camphorweeds, is a versatile genus of flowering plants within the Asteraceae family. Renowned for their aromatic properties, these resilient herbs are utilized globally in traditional medicine. They offer significant therapeutic potential, particularly for their anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant qualities used to treat diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Pluchea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Pluchea.
