Gentiana olivieri, scientifically known as Gentiana olivieri, is a perennial herb native to East Asian alpine regions. Characterized by its striking blue flowers and bitter roots, this medicinal plant is traditionally used in herbal medicine to stimulate digestion, alleviate gastrointestinal discomfort, and support overall metabolic health and vitality.
