Afghan Ash, scientifically known as Fraxinus xanthoxyloides, is a medicinal tree native to the mountainous regions of Central and South Asia. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, its bark and leaves are traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various digestive disorders effectively.
