Fraxinus xanthoxyloides

Fraxinus xanthoxyloides, commonly known as the Manilkara or Chinese Ash, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, its bark and leaves are frequently utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Fraxinus xanthoxyloides
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Oleaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Fraxinus, which includes the species Fraxinus xanthoxyloides.

This plant exhibits a broad natural range extending across several key regions of Asia. In Western Asia, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Afghanistan. Its distribution also reaches into the high-altitude regions of China, particularly throughout Tibet. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found growing in Pakistan. Additionally, the plant is widely distributed across the West Himalaya region.
