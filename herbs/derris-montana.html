Tuba merah, scientifically known as Derris montana, is a climbing woody vine native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent insecticidal properties, the plant contains rotenone, a natural compound used to control pests. Traditionally, it holds significant importance in ethnobotany for both ecological management and various medicinal applications.
