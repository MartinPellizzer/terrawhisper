Tuba merah, scientifically known as Derris montana, is a climbing woody vine native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent insecticidal properties, the plant contains rotenone, a natural compound used to control pests. Traditionally, it holds significant importance in ethnobotany for both ecological management and various medicinal applications.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically within the DR Congo. Its presence extends across Asia, where it grows in China Southeast and on the island of Hainan. The species is also situated within the Indian Subcontinent, notably in Bangladesh. Finally, its range reaches into Indo-China, particularly within the borders of Cambodia.
