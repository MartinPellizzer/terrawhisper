This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Alibertia and the species Alibertia edulis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across various regions of North and Central America. In Mexico, it can be found spanning the Gulf coast, the southwest, and the southeast portions of the country. Moving southward, its range extends into the tropical landscapes of Central America. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the borders of Belize. Finally, its geographical presence reaches as far south as Costa Rica.

Plant Parts

This plant, Alibertia edulis, is valued for its medicinal properties, particularly through the utilization of its leaves. The leaves are often harvested and processed to create tea leaves, which are then steeped to produce an infusion used in traditional remedies. By employing the tea leaves in this manner, practitioners can extract the beneficial compounds contained within the foliage to support various health practices.
