Sickle-Leaved Hare's-Ear, scientifically known as Bupleurum falcatum, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Featuring distinctive sickle-shaped leaves, this plant is primarily utilized for its adaptogenic properties. It is frequently used to support liver health, reduce inflammation, and alleviate stress, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness.
