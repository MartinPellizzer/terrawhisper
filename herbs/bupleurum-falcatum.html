Bupleurum falcatum

Bupleurum falcatum, commonly known as Chai Hu in Traditional Chinese Medicine, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Primarily used to regulate Liver Qi, it effectively relieves stagnation, reduces inflammation, and treats fever. This resilient plant remains a cornerstone in herbal formulations for restoring holistic balance.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum falcatum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Apiales and is a member of the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Bupleurum and the species falcatum.
