Leptadenia reticulata

Leptadenia reticulata, commonly known as Jivanti, is a perennial climbing shrub highly valued in Ayurveda. Renowned for its rejuvenating properties, this medicinal plant is used to enhance vitality, support reproductive health, and promote longevity. Its diverse phytochemical profile makes it a cornerstone in traditional treatments for strengthening the body.

  • Scientific name: Leptadenia reticulata
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, specifically within the family Apocynaceae. Ultimately, the species is identified under the genus Leptadenia.
