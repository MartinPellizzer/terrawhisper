False calumba, scientifically known as Coscinium fenestraatum, is a climbing woody vine native to South Asia. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this potent botanical is prized for its bitter principles. It is frequently utilized to treat digestive issues, skin disorders, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential in pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in both India and Sri Lanka.
