Hodgsonia heteroclita

Hodgsonia heteroclita, commonly known as the bladder plant, is a remarkable medicinal species native to Southeast Asian forests. Renowned for its unique botanical structure, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Research suggests its bioactive compounds may offer therapeutic potential in treating various ailments, highlighting its importance in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Hodgsonia heteroclita
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
