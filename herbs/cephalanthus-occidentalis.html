Cephalanthus occidentalis

Cephalanthus occidentalis, commonly known as buttonbush, is a versatile deciduous shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to wetlands, its fragrant, spherical white blooms and unique bark offer diverse therapeutic potential. Historically utilized for various ailments, this plant serves as a fascinating subject for both ethnobotanical study and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cephalanthus occidentalis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cephalanthus.
