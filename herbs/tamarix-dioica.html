Tamarix dioica

Tamarix dioica, commonly known as the Mediterranean tamarisk, is a resilient shrub valued for its adaptability to saline and arid environments. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this plant possesses diverse bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits through its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory compounds, making it a subject of botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Tamarix dioica
  • Family: Tamaricaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Tamaricaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tamarix, specifically as the species Tamarix dioica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions of Asia. In Western Asia, its presence is noted in both Afghanistan and Iran. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam. The species is also distributed throughout Bangladesh within the same subcontinent. Finally, its habitat extends into the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya.
