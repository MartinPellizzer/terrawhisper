Cardamine amara

Cardamine amarra, commonly known as bitter rocket, is a perennial herb belonging to the Brassicaceae family. Native to Europe and parts of Asia, this plant is distinguished by its pungent, bitter flavor and small white flowers. Traditionally, it has been utilized in folk medicine for its various therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Cardamine amara
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe
