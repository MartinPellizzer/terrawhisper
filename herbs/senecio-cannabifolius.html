Seneciio cannabifolius belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Asterales. This plant is a specific representative of the family Asteraceae, which is one of the most prominent families in the botanical world. Within this family, it is categorized under the genus Senecio, with its unique identity defined by its specific epithet, cannabifolius.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Senecio cannabifolius has deeply lobed, palmately divided leaves that resemble the serrated shape of cannabis foliage. The leaf margins are irregularly dentate, often featuring coarse teeth along the edges of the lobes. These leaves are arranged alternately along a stem that can grow quite robust and upright. The plant typically produces small, yellow flower heads clustered in corymbose inflorescences. Each flower head consists of numerous disc florets surrounded by a ring of ray florets.
