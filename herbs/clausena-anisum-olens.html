Clausena anisum-olens

Clausena anisum-olens, commonly known as the star anise relative, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it possesses potent antioxidant and antimicrobial qualities. Used to support digestive health and boost immunity, this botanical offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Clausena anisum-olens
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Sapindales and placed in the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Clausena as the species Clausena anisum-olens.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Clausena anisum-olens, is referred to by several different common names depending on the linguistic context. In Mandarin Chinese, it is commonly called 细叶黄皮 or 短柱黃皮, while in Traditional Chinese as used in Taiwan, it is specifically known as 短柱黃皮.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. In mainland China, it is found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends to Eastern Asia, specifically inhabiting the island of Taiwan. Moving into the Malesia region, the species can be located on the island of Borneo. Additionally, the plant's range encompasses the various islands of the Philippines.
