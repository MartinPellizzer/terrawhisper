Jasminum auriculatum

Jasminum auriculatum, commonly known as Arabian jasmine, is a fragrant, evergreen shrub prized for its intensely perfumed, white blossoms. Widely cultivated across tropical regions, this medicinal plant is celebrated in traditional practices for its calming aromatherapy properties, skin-soothing capabilities, and its ability to alleviate stress and promote relaxation.

  • Scientific name: Jasminum auriculatum
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification
