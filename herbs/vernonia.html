Vernonia

Vernonia, commonly known as ironweed, is a versatile genus of flowering plants prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, including sesquiterpene lactones, it is widely utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and fever. This resilient herb offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vernonia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Vernonia.
