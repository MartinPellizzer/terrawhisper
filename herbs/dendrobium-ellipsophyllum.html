Dendrobium ellipsophyllum

What's the taxonomical classification of Dendrobium ellipsophyllum?

Dendrobium ellipsophyllum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized under the order Asparagales. This plant is a member of the family Orchidaceae, falling specifically within the genus Dendrobium, and is identified by its unique species designation, ellipsophyllum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dendrobium ellipsophyllum has sympodial growth characterized by slender, reed-like pseudobulbs that are typically compressed and reach lengths of up to 20 centimeters. The leaves are distinctly elliptical or oblong in shape, possessing a leathery texture and an arrangement that often appears somewhat distichous. Small, white to creamy-colored flowers emerge from the upper nodes of the pseudobulbs, featuring a prominent three-lobed labellum. The inflorescence is relatively inconspicuous, often bearing only a few individual blossoms at a time. These morphological traits allow the orchid to adapt efficiently to its epiphytic lifestyle within shaded forest environments.
