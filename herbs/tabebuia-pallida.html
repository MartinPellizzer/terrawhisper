Tabebuia pallida

Cuban pink trumpet

Tabebuia pallida, commonly known as Palo Blanco, is a magnificent flowering tree native to Central America. Renowned for its striking white blossoms, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Tabebuia pallida
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa
