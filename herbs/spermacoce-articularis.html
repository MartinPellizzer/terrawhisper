Spermacoce articularis

Spermacoce articularis, commonly known as Joint-Relief Herb, is a potent medicinal plant valued for its anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used to soothe aching joints and reduce swelling, this botanical powerhouse contains unique bioactive compounds. It serves as a vital natural remedy for managing arthritis and promoting long-term musculoskeletal health.

  • Scientific name: Spermacoce articularis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is classified within the family Rubiaceae and the genus Spermacoce.
