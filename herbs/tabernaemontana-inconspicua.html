Tabernaemontana inconspicua

Tabernaemontana inconspicua, commonly known as a member of the Apocynaceae family, is a significant medicinal shrub found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering valuable biochemical compounds that hold great potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Tabernaemontana inconspicua
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae and the genus Tabernaemontana.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the lush landscapes of West-Central Tropical Africa. Its natural habitat includes the dense tropical regions of Cameroon and the Congo. Furthermore, the species can be found growing within the borders of Equatorial Guinea and Gabon. The geographical range also extends significantly into the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Collectively, these locations define the specific ecological niche where this medicinal species thrives.
