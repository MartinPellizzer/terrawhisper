Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is notably recorded within the borders of Mozambique. Moving into the Southern African region, it can be found inhabiting the KwaZulu-Natal area. The species is also documented as growing within the territory of Eswatini. Finally, its geographical range extends to the Northern Provinces of Southern Africa.
