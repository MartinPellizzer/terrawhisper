Viscum ovalifolium

Viscum ovalifolium, commonly known as the oval-leaved mistletoe, is a fascinating parasitic plant found primarily in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant therapeutic potential. Research suggests its bioactive compounds offer antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-tumor properties, making it a subject of great interest in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Viscum ovalifolium
  • Family: Santalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Santalales and the family Santalaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Viscum, specifically as the species Viscum ovalifolium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is widely documented in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically found in Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is known to inhabit the East Himalaya region. Together, these locations highlight the diverse habitats where Viscum ovalifolium thrives.
