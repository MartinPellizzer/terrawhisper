Manihot esculenta

Cassava

Manihot esculenta, commonly known as cassava or manioc, is a vital starchy tuberous crop native to South America. Renowned globally for its food security importance, it also possesses significant medicinal properties, offering bioactive compounds used in traditional practices to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various other ailments.

  • Scientific name: Manihot esculenta
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Manihot esculenta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Its specific taxonomic classification is completed by the genus Manihot.

Distribution
