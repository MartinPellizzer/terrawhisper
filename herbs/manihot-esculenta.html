Cassava, scientifically known as Manihot esculenta, is a vital starchy root vegetable essential to global food security. Primarily grown in tropical regions, it serves as a primary energy source for millions. Beyond nutrition, its various parts offer medicinal potential, demonstrating unique bioactive properties used in traditional healing practices.
