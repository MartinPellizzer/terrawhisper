Buxus macowanii

Buxus macowanii, commonly known as Macowan's boxwood, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to specific subtropical regions. Valued for its dense, lustrous foliage, this plant holds significant botanical interest. In traditional herbal practices, various species of the Buxus genus are studied for their unique phytochemical properties and medicinal potential.

  • Scientific name: Buxus macowanii
  • Family: Buxaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Buxales and the family Buxaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Buxus.
