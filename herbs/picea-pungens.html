Picea pungens

Blue spruce

Picea pungens, commonly known as the Blue Spruce, is a striking evergreen conifer celebrated for its distinctive silvery-blue needles. Native to the Rocky Mountains, this hardy species is highly valued in landscaping and forestry for its ornamental beauty, resilience to cold, and unique aesthetic appeal in diverse environments.

  • Scientific name: Picea pungens
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, falling into the family Pinaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Picea.

Common Names
