The physiology of Picea pungens is characterized by a metabolic framework typical of coniferous gymnosperms, utilizing a C3 photosynthetic pathway to facilitate carbon fixation. In this process, the plant captures atmospheric carbon dioxide through its stomata, where it is initially fixed by the enzyme RuBisCO into a three-carbon compound within the mesophyll cells.
