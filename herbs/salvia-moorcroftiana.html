Salvia moorcroftiana

Salvia moorcroftiana, commonly known as Moorcroft's sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Salvia moorcroftiana
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Salvia.
