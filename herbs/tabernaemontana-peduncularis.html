Tabernaemontana peduncularis

Tabernaemontana peduncularis, commonly known as the pinwheel flower or white rose laurel, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various plant parts are traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions, offering valuable insights into ethnopharmacology and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Tabernaemontana peduncularis
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Tabernaemontana, of which Tabernaemontana peduncularis is a member.

This plant, Tabernaemontana peduncularis, is known by various names depending on the region and language.
