Tagetes lucida

Pericón

Tagetes lucida, commonly known as Mexican marigold or Pericón, is a fragrant perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine. Rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, it is widely utilized for its digestive, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This versatile plant holds significant cultural importance across various herbal healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Tagetes lucida
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Tagetes.
