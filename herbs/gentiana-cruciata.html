Gentiana cruciata

Gentiana cruciata, commonly known as the cross-leaved gentian, is a perennial herb renowned for its bitter medicinal properties. Native to mountainous regions of Europe, it features distinctive cruciform leaf patterns. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it serves as a potent digestive tonic to stimulate appetite and support gastrointestinal health.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana cruciata
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Gentiana cruciata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianales. Finally, it is a member of the Gentianaecae family and falls under the genus Gentiana.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as the cross-leaved gentian, utilizes its underground parts as a primary source of its potent medicinal properties. The rhizomes and roots are the most significant components used in herbal medicine, as they contain a high concentration of bitter glycosides such as gentiopicrin. These subterranean structures are typically harvested, dried, and processed to create tinctures or infusions intended to stimulate digestion and improve appetite.
