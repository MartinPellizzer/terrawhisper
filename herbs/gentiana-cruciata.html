Cross Gentian, scientifically known as Gentiana cruciata, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Distinguished by the cruciform patterns on its leaves, it is highly valued in traditional herbalism. Its bitter roots are frequently used to support digestive health and stimulate appetite effectively.
