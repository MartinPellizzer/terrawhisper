Lilium davidii var. davidii

Lilium davidii var. davidii, commonly known as Davidii Lily, is a stunning perennial prized for its elegant, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant value in traditional practices. Its resilient nature and striking aesthetic make it a fascinating subject for both botanical study and medicinal exploration.
