Dwarf Labrador tea, scientifically known as Rhododendron tomentosum, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to North American wetlands. Renowned for its aromatic, fuzzy leaves, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional herbalism. It is frequently brewed into teas to soothe digestive issues and alleviate respiratory congestion.
