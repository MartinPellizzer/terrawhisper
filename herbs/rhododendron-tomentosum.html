Rhododendron tomentosum

Rhododendron tomeentum, commonly known as the velvet-leaf rhododendron, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to East Asian mountains. Renowned for its vibrant blooms and felt-like foliage, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its unique bioactive compounds to support various therapeutic wellness goals.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron tomentosum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rhododendron.
