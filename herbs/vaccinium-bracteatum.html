Vaccinium bracteatum

Vaccinium bracteatum, commonly known as the mountain cranberry, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its vibrant bracts and antioxidant properties, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits, offering a natural resource for holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vaccinium bracteatum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, placing it in the family Ericaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Vaccinium.
