Sea bilberry, scientifically known as Vaccinium bracteatum, is a resilient shrub native to the coastal regions of Southeast Asia. Renowned for its vibrant, antioxidant-rich berries, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potential to support ocular health, combat oxidative stress, and provide significant nutritional benefits.
