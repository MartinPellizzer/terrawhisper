Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the rugged landscapes of the Russian Far East. Its range extends through several distinct regions, including the Amur and Khabarovsk areas. It can also be found growing in the Magadan region, stretching across vast territories. Furthermore, its presence reaches the Kamchatka Peninsula, where it thrives in specific habitats. Finally, the species is documented inhabiting the Kuril Islands, completing its northern Pacific distribution.

Plant Parts

This plant, Fritillaria camschatcensis, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties which are concentrated within its underground structures. The most significant part used in traditional remedies is the bulbous root, which is harvested for its bioactive compounds. This root serves as the primary storage organ for the plant, containing the concentrated essence required for its therapeutic applications.
