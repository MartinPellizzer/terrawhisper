Bignonia nocturna

Bignonia nocturna, commonly known as the Night-blooming Bignonia, is a captivating medicinal plant prized for its fragrant, nocturnal blossoms. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it possesses unique bioactive properties used to treat various ailments. This enchanting climber offers a fascinating intersection of botanical beauty and potent therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Bignonia nocturna
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Bignoniaceae. Its specific biological designation is found within the genus Bignonia, where it is known as Bignonia nocturna.
