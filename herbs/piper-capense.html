African long pepper, scientifically known as Piper capense, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its pungent aroma and spicy flavor, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and inflammatory conditions, serving as a vital component in herbal healing.
