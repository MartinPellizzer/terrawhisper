Cajanus albicans

Cajaunus albicans, commonly known as the White Oleander, is a striking medicinal shrub prized for its potent phytochemical properties. Renowned in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds, it is studied for its potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects. However, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling and precise medicinal application.
