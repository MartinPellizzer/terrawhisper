Galphimia glauca

Galphimia glauca, commonly known as the "Sapo" plant, is a medicinal shrub native to the tropical regions of South America. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, it is traditionally used in indigenous healing practices to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for modern therapeutic research and bioactive compound discovery.

  • Scientific name: Galphimia glauca
  • Family: Malpighiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Galphimia glauca, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malpighiaceae and is situated within the genus Galphimia.
