Siberian Solomon's-seal

Polygonatum sibiricum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (114 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Siberian Solomon's-seal, scientifically known as Polygonatum sibiricum, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, its rhizomes contain saponins and polysaccharides used to nourish Yin and promote fluid production. It is frequently employed to support respiratory health, ease dryness, and bolster overall vitality.
