Albizia

Albizia, commonly known as the silk tree, is a versatile genus of flowering plants celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its calming properties, various species are used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and anxiety. Its diverse botanical characteristics make it a valuable resource in both herbalism and ecological restoration.

  • Scientific name: Albizia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Albizia.
