Saraca indica

Yellow ashok

Saraca indica, commonly known as Sara, is a significant medicinal plant in Ayurveda. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, it is primarily used to treat various ailments, including joint pain, skin disorders, and digestive issues. Its versatile leaves and bark offer valuable therapeutic benefits in traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Saraca indica
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the Fabaceae family, falling under the genus Saraca.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across multiple continents. In Northern Africa, its presence is recorded within the borders of Egypt. Moving toward the center of the continent, it can be found in the West-Central Tropical Africa region of the DR Congo. Its range extends into East Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting the territories of Tanzania and Uganda. Finally, the species reaches the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Bangladesh.
