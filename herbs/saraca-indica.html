Yellow Ashok, scientifically known as Saraca indica, is a versatile medicinal tree renowned in Ayurvedic medicine. Characterized by its vibrant yellow flowers and lush foliage, it offers profound therapeutic benefits. Its bark and leaves are frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin disorders, and digestive issues, promoting overall holistic healing.
