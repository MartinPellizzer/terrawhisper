Bauhinia bauhinioides

Bauhinia bauhinioides, commonly known as the orchid tree or mountain bauhinia, is a resilient medicinal shrub prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its unique bi-lobed leaves, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, making it a vital resource in botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Bauhinia bauhinioides
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is specifically nested within the family Fabaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Bauhinia.
