What's the taxonomical classification of Setaria viridis?

Setaria viridis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic progression continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, eventually reaching the order Poales. As a member of the family Poaceae, it is categorized under the genus Setaria, with its specific identification being the species viridis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Setaria viridis has an erect, annual growth habit characterized by slender, cylindrical stems that typically reach heights of 20 to 100 centimeters. The leaves consist of narrow, linear blades with parallel venation and a prominent midrib, arising from sheaths that wrap around the stem. Its reproductive structures are arranged in dense, bristly panicles that feature long, spreading hairs surrounding the spikelets. These spikelets are small and ovate, often containing a single grain that is adapted for efficient seed dispersal. The root system is fibrous, providing stability and nutrient uptake for its rapid lifecycle.
