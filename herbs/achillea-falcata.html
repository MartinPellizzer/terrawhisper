Achillea falcata

Achillea falcata, commonly known as the falcate yarrow, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Characterized by its uniquely curved, sickle-shaped leaves, this medicinal plant has long been valued in traditional ethnobotany for its potent bioactive compounds, offering therapeutic potential in treating inflammation and various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Achillea falcata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Achillea, specifically as the species Achillea falcata.
