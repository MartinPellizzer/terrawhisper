Distribution

This plant exhibits a varied geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted within Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found in Burundi and Rwanda. Additionally, it is located within the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Finally, the plant is also documented as growing in Cameroon.
