Astragalus spinosus

Astragalus spinosus, commonly known as the thorny milkvetch, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid Mediterranean and subtropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive spiny stems and delicate pea-like flowers, this plant plays a vital role in stabilizing soil and offers significant ethnobotanical value in traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus spinosus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is identified by the genus Astragalus.
