Salvia verbenaca

Wild clary

Salvia verbena, commonly known as Vervain sage, is a versatile medicinal herb celebrated for its restorative properties. Traditionally used to soothe nervous tension and support digestive health, this plant offers potent antioxidant benefits. Incorporating it into holistic wellness routines can promote calm, balance, and overall vitality through its unique botanical compounds.

  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Salvia.
