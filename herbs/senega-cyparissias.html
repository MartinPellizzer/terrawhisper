Senega cyparissia, scientifically known as Senega cyparis, is a perennial herb native to North America, cherished for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned primarily as an expectorant, its roots contain saponins that help soothe respiratory ailments, making it a historical cornerstone in treating coughs, bronchitis, and various lung conditions.
