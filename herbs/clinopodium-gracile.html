What's the taxonomical classification of Clinopodium gracile?

Clinopodium gracile belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae. The taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, eventually reaching its specific identification within the genus Clinopodium. Ultimately, the plant is defined by its unique species designation, gracile.
