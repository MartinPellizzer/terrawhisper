Salvia coccinea

Salvia coccinea, commonly known as scarlet sage, is a striking perennial celebrated for its vibrant, tubular red flowers. Native to specific montane regions, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in herbal practices for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support circulatory health and vitality through its potent phytochemicals.

  • Scientific name: Salvia coccinea
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Salvia.
