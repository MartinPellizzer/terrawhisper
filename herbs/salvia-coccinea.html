Texas sage, scientifically known as Salvia coccinea, is a stunning perennial native to the American Southwest. Renowned for its vibrant scarlet blooms and silvery foliage, this drought-tolerant plant is a magnet for hummingbirds and pollinators. It serves as a resilient, beautiful addition to any sun-drenched, water-wise landscape.
