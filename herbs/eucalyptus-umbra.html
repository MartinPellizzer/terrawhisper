Eucalyptus umbra

Eucalyptus umbra, commonly known as the Shadow Eucalyptus, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its potent therapeutic properties. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it offers powerful antiseptic and decongestant benefits. This resilient species serves as a vital natural resource in traditional healing, supporting respiratory health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus umbra
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Myrtales and falls into the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eucalyptus.
