What's the taxonomical classification of Oxytropis yunnanensis?

Oxytropis yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it is placed in the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Oxytropis, and its species designation, yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Oxytropis yunnanensis has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by pinnately compound leaves, small pea-like flowers, and elongated, slender seed pods.
