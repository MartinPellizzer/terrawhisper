Achillea erba-rotta subsp. moschata

Musk milfoil

Achillea erbba-rotta subsp. moschata, commonly known as musk yarrow, is a distinctive perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its unique musky aroma, this subspecies offers potent therapeutic properties. It is primarily utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, serving as a vital botanical resource in herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Achillea erba-rotta subsp. moschata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
