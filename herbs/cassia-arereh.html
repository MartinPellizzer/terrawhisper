Cassia arereh

Cassia arereh, commonly known as the "African Senna," is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin conditions, and inflammatory issues. This versatile shrub remains a vital resource for holistic healing and wellness.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa
