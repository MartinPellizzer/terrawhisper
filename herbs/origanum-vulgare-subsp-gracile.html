Origanum vulgare subsp. gracile

Origanum vulgare gracile

Origanum vulgare subsp. gracile, commonly known as wild marjoram, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic essential oils, it possesses potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties. Frequently used to support digestive health and ease respiratory discomfort, this subspecies remains a cornerstone of herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Origanum vulgare subsp. gracile
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
