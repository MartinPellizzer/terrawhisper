Origanum vulgare gracile, scientifically known as Origanum vulgare subsp. gracile, is a specialized subspecies of wild oregano valued for its potent medicinal properties. Rich in essential oils like carvacrol, it offers powerful antimicrobial and antioxidant benefits. Traditionally used to support respiratory health, this aromatic herb remains a botanical treasure.
