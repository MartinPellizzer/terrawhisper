Kleinia longiflora

Kleinia longiflora, commonly known as the Long-flowered Kleinia, is a striking succulent native to Southern Africa. Characterized by its fleshy leaves and delicate, trumpet-shaped white flowers, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties in treating various ailments across regional cultures.

  • Scientific name: Kleinia longiflora
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Kleinia, with its species designation being longiflora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, its range extends into South Tropical Africa. Specifically, this species is documented in the countries of Angola and Mozambique. Additionally, the geographical footprint of the plant includes the nation of Zimbabwe.
