Scientifically known as Kleinia longiflora, is a remarkable succulent shrub native to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Characterized by its fleshy leaves and striking white, tubular flowers, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties and its ability to thrive in harsh environments.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, its range extends into South Tropical Africa. Specifically, this species is documented in the countries of Angola and Mozambique. Additionally, the geographical footprint of the plant includes the nation of Zimbabwe.
