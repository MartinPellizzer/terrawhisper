This plant, Dendrobium amoenum, undergoes a fascinating developmental process that begins with the formation of specialized structures known as protocorms. These small, tuber-like structures serve as the initial stage of growth following seed germination, acting as essential storage organs that provide the necessary nutrients for the developing protocorm to transition into a functional seedling. As the plant matures, these protocorms eventually develop into the robust pseudobulbs and complex rhizome systems characteristic of this medicinal orchid species.
