Dendrobium amoenum

Lovely dendrobium

Dendrobium amoenum, commonly known as the "Golden Orchid," is a precious medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is highly valued for its restorative properties. This orchid is frequently utilized to nourish the body, relieve coughs, and enhance overall vitality and immune function.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium amoenum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Dendrobium amoenum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales. It is a member of the Orchidaceae family and is specifically categorized under the genus Dendrobium.
