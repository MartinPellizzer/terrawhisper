Cupania vernalis

Cupania vernalis, commonly known as a member of the Sapindaceae family, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to address various ailments, offering potential therapeutic benefits in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting overall systemic health and wellness.
