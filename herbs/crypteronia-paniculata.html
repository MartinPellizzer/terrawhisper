Crypteronia paniculata

Crypteronia paniculata, commonly known as the Wild Cryptoneria, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This plant offers valuable insights into natural pharmacology, highlighting the profound healing potential of biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Crypteronia paniculata
  • Family: Crypteroniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the family Crypteroniaceae and falls under the genus Crypteronia.
