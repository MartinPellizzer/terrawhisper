Terminalia avicennioides

Terminalia aviencinioides, commonly known as the mangrove terminalia, is a resilient tree species vital to coastal ecosystems. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its bark, leaves, and roots possess significant antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant plays a crucial role in treating various ailments within diverse indigenous medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia avicennioides
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it falls under the family Combretaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Terminalia.
