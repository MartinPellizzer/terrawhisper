Berberis dictyophylla

Berberis dictyophyllas, commonly known as the netted-leaf barberry, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains bioactive alkaloids like berberine. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential for addressing inflammatory conditions and supporting metabolic health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Berberis dictyophylla
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Berberidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Berberis.
