Clary sage, scientifically known as Salvia sclarea, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine and aromatherapy. Renowned for its aromatic essential oils, it is widely used to support hormonal balance, reduce stress, and enhance digestion. This fragrant botanical offers powerful therapeutic properties for holistic wellness enthusiasts.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Salvia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence can be noted within the borders of Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found in both Germany and the Czechia-Slovakia region. Its range extends further south into Southwestern Europe, specifically covering the Baleares islands. Additionally, the plant can be located in the Mediterranean territory of Corse.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as clary sage, utilizes various components for its medicinal and aromatic properties. While the roots are not the primary focus of its commercial use, they contribute to the plant's overall biological structure. The essential oils, extracted primarily from the leaves and flowering tops, are highly valued for their intense fragrance and therapeutic benefits. Additionally, the seeds are a significant part of the plant, often used in traditional practices and containing concentrated compounds that enhance its medicinal profile.
