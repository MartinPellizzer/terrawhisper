Convolvulus arvensis

Convolvulus arvensis, commonly known as field bindweed, is a resilient perennial herb frequently viewed as a nuisance in agriculture. Beyond its reputation as a persistent weed, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Historically, various cultures have utilized its roots and leaves in traditional medicine to treat diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Convolvulus arvensis
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae and the genus Convolvulus.
