Allium schoenoprasum

Chives

Allium schoenoprasum, commonly known as chives, is a versatile perennial herb prized for both culinary and medicinal uses. Rich in antioxidants and sulfur compounds, it offers anti-inflammatory and antibacterial properties. Traditionally used to support digestive health and blood circulation, this aromatic plant remains a staple in natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Allium schoenoprasum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Allium.
