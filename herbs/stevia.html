Stevia

Stevia, commonly known as sweet leaf, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its intense, natural sweetness. Derived from the Stevia rebaudiana herb, it serves as a calorie-free, non-glycemic alternative to sugar. Beyond culinary uses, it is valued in traditional medicine for supporting blood sugar regulation and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Stevia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Stevia.
