Sweet Leaf

Stevia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (11 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Sweet Leaf, scientifically known as Stevia, is a versatile plant native to South America, celebrated for its intense natural sweetness. Unlike sugar, its leaves provide a calorie-free alternative that does not impact blood glucose levels, making it a popular functional herb for managing diabetes and supporting healthy weight loss.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within Switzerland. Its presence extends to the Caucasus region, specifically throughout the Transcaucasus. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species is established in both Bangladesh and India. Additionally, the plant's range includes the island nation of Sri Lanka.
