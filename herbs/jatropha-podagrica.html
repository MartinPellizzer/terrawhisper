Jatropha podagrica

Jatrophia podagrica, commonly known as the Jatropha plant or Buddha belly plant, is a distinctive succulent native to Madagascar. Renowned for its unique, swollen stems and vibrant red flowers, it is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, including its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial applications.

  • Scientific name: Jatropha podagrica
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae within the genus Jatropha.
