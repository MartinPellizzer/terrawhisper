Phlomis persica

Phlomis persica, commonly known as Persian sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Middle East. Characterized by its striking, velvety silver-green foliage and vibrant yellow flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and soothing properties to support overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis persica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phlomis, which includes the species Phlomis persica.
