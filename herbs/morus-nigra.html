Black mulberry, scientifically known as Morus nigra, is a versatile deciduous shrub celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants, anthocyanins, and vitamins, its dark berries and leaves offer significant health benefits, including anti-inflammatory, anti-diabetic, and immune-boosting effects, making it a cornerstone in traditional herbal medicine.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Morus nigra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the family Moraceae and falls under the genus Morus.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Morus nigra, contains several components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaves are frequently employed in traditional remedies to treat various ailments, often prepared as teas or extracts. Additionally, the succulent fruits, which are dark and sweet, are consumed not only for nutrition but also for their antioxidant benefits. For more specific medicinal applications, the root bark is harvested to derive potent compounds, and in some pharmacological preparations, the root barks are processed to ensure high concentrations of active ingredients. Whether using the whole leaf or specific woody sections, each part of the plant contributes to its diverse medicinal profile.
