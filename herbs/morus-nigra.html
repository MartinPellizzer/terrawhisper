Morus nigra

Black mulberry

Morus nigra, commonly known as the Black Mulberry, is a deciduous tree cherished for its nutrient-dense, dark purple fruits. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and minerals, it has been used extensively in traditional medicine to support digestive health, manage blood sugar levels, and reduce inflammation throughout various healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Morus nigra
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Morus nigra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the family Moraceae and falls under the genus Morus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Morus nigra, is recognized by several common names across different Chinese linguistic contexts. In Simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 黑桑 (zh-hans), which is the same designation used in Mainland China (zh-cn), Singapore (zh-sg), and Hong Kong (zh-hk). Similarly, in Traditional Chinese, the common name is written as 黑桑 (zh-hant).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a varied geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be identified within the borders of Great Britain. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it is found throughout Southwestern Europe, specifically in Spain. The species also extends into Southeastern Europe, where it inhabits the landscapes of Albania and Bulgaria. Finally, its range reaches further south to include the country of Greece.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Morus nigra, contains several components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaves are frequently employed in traditional remedies to treat various ailments, often prepared as teas or extracts. Additionally, the succulent fruits, which are dark and sweet, are consumed not only for nutrition but also for their antioxidant benefits. For more specific medicinal applications, the root bark is harvested to derive potent compounds, and in some pharmacological preparations, the root barks are processed to ensure high concentrations of active ingredients.
