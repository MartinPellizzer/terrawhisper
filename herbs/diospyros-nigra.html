Distribution

This plant exhibits a remarkably wide geographical distribution spanning multiple continents and ecological zones. In the Western Indian Ocean, it is notably found throughout Madagascar. Its range extends across the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the borders of India. Within the Malesia region, the species is documented in Jawa. Finally, it reaches the Americas, appearing in Texas in the South-Central U.S.A. and in Florida in the Southeastern U.S.A.
