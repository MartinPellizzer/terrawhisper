Ficus natalensis

Ficus natalensis, commonly known as the Natal Fig, is a versatile medicinal plant native to various African regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues. Its leaves and bark hold significant potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Ficus natalensis
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, it is a member of the family Moraceae. Specifically, Ficus natalensis is categorized under the genus Ficus.
