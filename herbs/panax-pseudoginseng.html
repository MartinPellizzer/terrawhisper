Himalayan ginseng, scientifically known as Panax pseudoginseng, is a potent medicinal herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned in traditional medicine for its adaptogenic properties, it helps balance the body, reduce stress, and enhance vitality. This rare botanical is highly valued for boosting immunity and supporting overall cognitive function.
