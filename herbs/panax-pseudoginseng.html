Panax pseudoginseng

Panax pseudoginseng, commonly known as Siberian ginseng, is a powerful adaptogen prized in traditional medicine. Unlike true ginseng, it lacks saponins but offers unique bioactive compounds. It is widely used to boost immunity, reduce stress, and enhance vitality, serving as a natural remedy for fatigue and cognitive support.

  • Scientific name: Panax pseudoginseng
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Apiales, it is further categorized into the family Araliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Panax, specifically as the species Panax pseudoginseng.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, its presence is notably documented within the Tibet region. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it can be found growing in the mountainous terrains of Nepal.
