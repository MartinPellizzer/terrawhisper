Pistacia eurycarpa

Pistacia eurycarpa, commonly known as the broad-fruited mastic tree, is a resilient shrub native to arid Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, this species offers significant medicinal potential. Its bioactive compounds are being increasingly studied for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial effects in traditional and modern pharmacology.

  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
