Pteris cretica

Cretan brake

Pteris cretica, commonly known as the brake fern, is a resilient species widely distributed across Mediterranean and subtropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this fern possesses diverse bioactive properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. Understanding its botanical characteristics and therapeutic potential offers significant insights into natural pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Pteris cretica
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae. Within the order Polypodiales, it is classified under the family Pteridaceae and the genus Pteris.

Moving into Middle Europe, it is found in countries such as Hungary, the Netherlands, and Switzerland. Its range also extends into Southwestern Europe, specifically reaching the island of Corse.
