Enicostema axillare

Enicostema axillare, commonly known as "falso-cordão-de-frade," is a versatile medicinal plant native to Brazil. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and antimicrobial properties. Often used in herbal decoctions, this species plays a crucial role in folk healing practices, offering potential therapeutic benefits for various ailments.

  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Africa
