Pilea crenulata

Pilea crenulata, commonly known as the scalloped pilea, is a charming tropical herb valued for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Characterized by its distinctively wavy, textured foliage, this plant thrives in humid environments. It is traditionally utilized in various herbal practices to support wellness and promote healing.

  • Scientific name: Pilea crenulata
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Pilea crenulata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the family Urticaceae and is placed in the genus Pilea.
