Cistus × incanus

Cistus × incanus, commonly known as Rockrose hybrid, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub prized for its potent medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive polyphenols and essential oils, it is traditionally utilized for its powerful antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial effects, offering natural support for skin health and immune system function.

  • Scientific name: Cistus × incanus
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, falling into the family Cistaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation concludes with the genus Cistus.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning multiple continents. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing across both France and Spain. Its presence extends further south into Northern Africa, specifically within the territory of Morocco. Interestingly, the species also reaches across the Atlantic to the Southwestern United States. Within that North American region, its distribution is specifically noted in California.
