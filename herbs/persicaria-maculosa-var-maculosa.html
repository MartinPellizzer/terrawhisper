Persicaria maculosa var. maculosa

Persicaria maculosa var. maculosa, commonly known as Red Bistort or Spotted Smartweed, is a resilient perennial valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its distinctive spotted leaves, this plant contains bioactive compounds often utilized for their anti-inflammatory and astringent properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria maculosa var. maculosa
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Persicaria, specifically as the variety maculosa.
