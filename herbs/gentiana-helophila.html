Gentiana helophila

What's the taxonomical classification of Gentiana helophila?

Gentiana helophila belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Gentianaales. This plant is a member of the family Gentianaceae and is categorized under the genus Gentiana, specifically identified by the species name helophila.
