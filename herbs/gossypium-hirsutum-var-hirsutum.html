Gossypium hirsutum var. hirsutum

Gossypium hirsutum var. hirsutum, commonly known as upland cotton, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Beyond its commercial fiber, various plant parts are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern healthcare applications.

  • Scientific name: Gossypium hirsutum var. hirsutum
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
