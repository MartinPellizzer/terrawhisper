Callitropsis nootkatensis

Callitropsis nootkatensis, commonly known as the Nootka plant, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific coastal regions, this plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional healing practices to address various ailments, offering a fascinating subject for ethnobotanical research and natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Callitropsis nootkatensis
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, it is classified under the family Cupressaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Callitropsis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Callitropsis nootkatensis, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Estonian, it is called nutka kuldküpress, while in Czech, it is known as Cypřišek nutkajský. Norwegian speakers call it Nutkasypress, and in Croatian, it is identified as Nutkanski pačempres. Additionally, in Persian, the plant is known as سرو آلاسکا.
