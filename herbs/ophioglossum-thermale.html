Ophioglossum thermale

Ophioglossum thermale, commonly known as a specialized medicinal fern, is a rare botanical treasure found in specific thermal habitats. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential in traditional medicine, particularly for addressing inflammatory conditions and supporting metabolic health through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Ophioglossaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Ophioglossum thermale, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Ophioglossidae, it is categorized under the order Ophioglossales. Finally, it is classified within the family Ophioglossaceae and the genus Ophioglossum.
