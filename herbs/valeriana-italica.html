Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the southeastern regions of Europe. Its presence is well-documented across the Balkan Peninsula, specifically in Albania and Bulgaria. It can also be found extensively throughout Greece, including the island of Kriti. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the European portion of Türkiye. These specific locations highlight its regional prevalence within the Mediterranean and southeastern European landscapes.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Valerian, derives its primary medicinal value from its underground roots. The thickened rhizomes and roots are the specific parts harvested, dried, and processed to extract the essential volatile oils and valerenic acids responsible for its sedative properties. While the aerial portions of the plant may be present, it is the concentrated chemical compounds found within the root system that are utilized in herbal medicine to promote sleep and reduce anxiety.
