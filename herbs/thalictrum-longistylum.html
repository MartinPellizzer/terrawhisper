Thalictrum longistylum

Thalictrum longistylum, commonly known as Long-styled Fumitory, is a delicate perennial herb belonging to the Ranunculaceae family. Found in shaded, moist habitats, this botanical specimen is valued in traditional herbalism. Its unique floral structure and chemical properties make it a subject of interest for studying natural medicinal compounds.

  • Scientific name: Thalictrum longistylum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae within the genus Thalictrum.
