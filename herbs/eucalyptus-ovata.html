Plant Parts

This plant, Eucalyptus ovata, relies primarily on its leaves for its medicinal properties and physiological functions. The leaves are the most significant part used in traditional and modern medicine, as they contain high concentrations of essential oils, such as eucalyptol, which provide potent antiseptic and anti-inflammatory effects. These aromatic leaves serve not only as the main site for photosynthesis but also as the primary source of therapeutic compounds used to treat respiratory ailments and congestion.
