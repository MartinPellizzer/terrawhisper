Lebanon cedar, scientifically known as Cedrus libani, is a majestic coniferous evergreen native to the Mediterranean mountains. Renowned for its longevity and aromatic resin, this ancient tree holds significant medicinal value. Its essential oils are traditionally utilized for their antimicrobial, antiseptic, and soothing properties in various holistic treatments.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Cedar of Lebanon, possesses various parts that have historically been utilized for their unique properties. The aromatic wood is perhaps its most famous component, prized for its durability and fragrant resin, which has been used in traditional medicine and incense. The needle-like leaves, or foliage, are evergreen and contain essential oils that contribute to the plant's distinct scent. Additionally, the large, woody cones serve as the reproductive structures of the tree, housing the seeds and contributing to the overall chemical profile of the species.
