Cedrus libani

Lebanon cedar

Cedrus libani, commonly known as the Cedar of Lebanon, is a majestic coniferous tree renowned for its historical and medicinal significance. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, its fragrant resin and essential oils possess potent antimicrobial properties, making it a cornerstone in ancient herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cedrus libani
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, it is a member of the family Pinaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Cedrus.
