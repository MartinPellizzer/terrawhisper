Lebanon cedar, scientifically known as Cedrus libani, is a majestic coniferous evergreen native to the Mediterranean mountains. Renowned for its longevity and aromatic resin, this ancient tree holds significant medicinal value. Its essential oils are traditionally utilized for their antimicrobial, antiseptic, and soothing properties in various holistic treatments.
