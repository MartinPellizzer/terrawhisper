Clerodendrum bungei

Clerodendrum bungei, commonly known as the Bungei Clerodendrum, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues. This botanical specimen holds significant promise for ongoing pharmacological research and natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Clerodendrum bungei
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Clerodendrum, specifically the species Clerodendrum bungei.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within Great Britain.
