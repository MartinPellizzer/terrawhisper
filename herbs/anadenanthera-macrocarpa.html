Anadenanthera macrocarpa

Anadenanthera macrocarpa, commonly known as the large-pod acacia, is a significant tropical legume valued for its medicinal potential. Found across South American rainforests, this woody species is studied for its bioactive compounds. Traditionally, its various parts are utilized in folk medicine to address diverse ailments and promote healing.

  • Scientific name: Anadenanthera macrocarpa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further nested within the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Anadenanthera.
