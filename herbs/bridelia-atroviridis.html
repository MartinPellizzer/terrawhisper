Bridelia atroviridis

Bridelia atroviridis, commonly known as the "green bridelea," is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and digestive issues, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Bridelia atroviridis
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Bridelia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its presence is specifically documented within the borders of Benin and Burkina Faso. Additionally, the species can be found growing throughout the Ivory Coast. The geographical range further extends into the lush territories of Liberia. Finally, the plant is also widely recorded within the nation of Nigeria.
