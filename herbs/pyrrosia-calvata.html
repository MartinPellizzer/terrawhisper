Pyrrosia calvata

What's the taxonomical classification of Pyrrosia calvata?

Pyrrosia calvata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following the hierarchical structure of its taxonomy, it falls under the order Polypodiales and is a member of the family Polypodiaceae. Ultimately, its specific identity is defined by its placement within the genus Pyrrosia, with the species name being calvata.
