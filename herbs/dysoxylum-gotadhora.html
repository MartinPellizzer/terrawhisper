Dysoxylum gotadhora

Dysoxylum gotadhora, commonly known as Gota Kola, is a significant medicinal plant revered in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent neuroprotective properties, it is primarily used to enhance cognitive function, memory, and mental clarity. This versatile herb serves as a natural tonic for revitalizing the nervous system.

  • Scientific name: Dysoxylum gotadhora
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindaales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Dysoxylum and the species Dysoxylum gotadhora.

Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is noted in the state of Assam and the broader East Himalaya region. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various subtropical and montane environments.
