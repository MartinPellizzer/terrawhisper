Polygonum arenarium subsp. arenarium

Polygonum arenarium subsp. arenarium, commonly known as sand knotweed, is a resilient perennial herb found in sandy habitats. Valued in traditional medicine, this botanical specimen possesses diverse bioactive properties. It is often studied for its potential therapeutic applications, particularly regarding its antioxidant activities and its role in supporting wellness.

  • Scientific name: Polygonum arenarium subsp. arenarium
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Polygonum as the subspecies Polygonum arenarium subsp. arenarium.
