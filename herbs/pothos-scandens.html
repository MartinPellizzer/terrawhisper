Pothos scandens

Pothos scandens, commonly known as the Scindapsus or Golden Pothos, is a versatile climbing vine prized for its ornamental beauty. Beyond its lush foliage, this resilient plant is celebrated in traditional medicine for its potential antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, making it a fascinating subject for botanical study and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Pothos scandens
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Alismatales and the family Araceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pothos.
