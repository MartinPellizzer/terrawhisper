Hypoestes purpurea

Hypoestes purpurea, commonly known as the Purple Heart plant, is a striking ornamental species celebrated for its vibrant, deep violet foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, it is valued in traditional medicine for its potential healing properties, often utilized in various cultures to address skin ailments and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Hypoestes purpurea
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Hypoestes.

Common Names

This plant, Hypoestes purpurea, is known by various names across different linguistic contexts. In Chinese, it is commonly referred to as 六角英 (zh, zh-tw, zh-cn, zh-hans) and is also known as 枪刀药 (zh).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in Hainan and throughout China Southeast. It can also be found in Eastern Asia, specifically on the island of Taiwan. Moving into the Indo-China region, the species is distributed within Laos. Finally, its range extends into Malesia, where it is located in the Philippines.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the False Shamrock, is utilized in traditional medicine through its dried aerial part, which includes the leaves and stems above the ground. When these upper structures are harvested and dried, they are prepared for various therapeutic applications. The use of the dried aerial part allows for the preservation of the plant's bioactive compounds, making it easier to store and process for herbal remedies.
