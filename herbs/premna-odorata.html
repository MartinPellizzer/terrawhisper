Fragrant premna, scientifically known as Premna odorata, is a versatile medicinal shrub cherished in traditional healing. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, this plant offers potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. It is widely utilized to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and digestive issues, serving as a vital botanical remedy.
