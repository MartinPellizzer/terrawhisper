Buttontree, scientifically known as Terminalia anosperma (often referred to as Terminalia anosperma or anogeissiana), is a versatile medicinal powerhouse found across tropical Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of this tree—including the bark, leaves, and fruit—are traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.
