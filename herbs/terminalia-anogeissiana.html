Terminalia anogeissiana

Terminalia anosperma, commonly known as the Anogeissus or African Terminalia, is a versatile medicinal tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, its bark and leaves are traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant potential in ethnobotanical pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia anogeissiana
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Combretaceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Terminalia.
