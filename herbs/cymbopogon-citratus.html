Cymbopogon citratus

Lemongrass

Cymbopogon citratus, commonly known as lemongrass, is a versatile perennial grass celebrated for its aromatic qualities. Rich in citral, it offers potent antimicrobial, digestive, and anti-inflammatory benefits. Widely used in traditional medicine and culinary arts, this fragrant herb serves as a natural remedy for stress and various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Cymbopogon citratus
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales and the family Poaceae, it is classified under the genus Cymbopogon.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Cymbopogon citratus, is recognized by various regional names across the globe. In Poland, it is known as palczatkka cytrynowa, while in Ethiopia, it is referred to as harilik sidrunhein. In Indonesia, it is commonly called Serai, and in Bangladesh, it is known as গন্ধবেণা. Additionally, in Japan, this aromatic herb is called レモングラス.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented in the countries of Algeria, Egypt, and Morocco. The species also extends into the tropical zones of West Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the nation of Benin. Additionally, its range includes the West Tropical African country of Gambia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cymbopogon citratus, is highly valued in traditional medicine, with various components offering unique therapeutic benefits. The most commonly utilized parts are the long, slender leaves, which contain high concentrations of aromatic compounds. From these leaves, essential oils (or essential oil) are extracted through distillation, providing potent antimicrobial and insect-repellent properties. Furthermore, the water extract derived from the plant is frequently used in herbal teas to soothe digestive issues and reduce fever. Ultimately, while specific components are targeted for certain remedies, the whole Cymbopogon citratus plant serves as a rich source of bioactive constituents that contribute to its overall medicinal efficacy.
