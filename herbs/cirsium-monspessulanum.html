Cirsium monspessulanum

Cirsium montanum, commonly known as Montpellier thistle, is a resilient perennial herb native to Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its striking purple blooms, this plant possesses significant medicinal potential. Historically utilized in traditional herbalism, its bioactive compounds offer therapeutic properties, making it a subject of interest in ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Cirsium monspessulanum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Cirsium.
