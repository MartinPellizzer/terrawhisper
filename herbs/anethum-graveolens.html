Dill, scientifically known as Anethum graveolens, is a versatile aromatic herb celebrated for both its culinary and medicinal properties. Rich in essential oils, it is traditionally used to soothe digestive issues, alleviate bloating, and reduce inflammation. This fragrant plant serves as a potent natural remedy in holistic wellness practices.
