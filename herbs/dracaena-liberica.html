Dracaena liberica

Dracaena liberica, commonly known as the Liberica Dragon Tree, is a resilient medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its striking foliage, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in herbal remedies to treat inflammatory conditions, wounds, and diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Dracaena liberica
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asparagaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Dracaena.
