Mucuna birdwoodiana

Mucuna birdwoodiana, commonly known as a climbing legume, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical forests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to neurological issues, offering a rich potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
