Lousewort, scientifically known as Pedicularis, is a diverse genus of flowering plants within the Orobanchaceae family. Renowned for their unique, hooded blossoms, these hemi-parasitic perennials often thrive in alpine and meadow ecosystems. Historically valued in traditional medicine, they remain significant subjects in botanical studies and ecological research worldwide.
