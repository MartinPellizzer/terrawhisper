Pedicularis

Pedicularis, commonly known as lousewort, is a diverse genus of flowering plants within the Orobanchaceae family. Renowned for their unique parasitic nature, these perennials often attach to host roots to absorb nutrients. Their striking, tubular blossoms add vibrant colors to alpine meadows, making them a fascination for botanists worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Pedicularis
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Orobanchaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic designation is defined by the genus Pedicularis.
