Ribes nigrum

Ribes nigrum, commonly known as blackcurrant, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized for its nutrient-dense berries. Rich in vitamins C and K, alongside powerful antioxidants like anthocyanins, it offers significant therapeutic benefits. Traditionally used to support immune function and eye health, this medicinal plant remains a nutritional powerhouse.

  • Scientific name: Ribes nigrum
  • Family: Grossulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Grossulariaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ribes.
