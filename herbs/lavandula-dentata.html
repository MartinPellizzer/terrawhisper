French lavender, scientifically known as Lavandula dentata, is a fragrant, evergreen shrub celebrated for its serrated leaves and delicate purple blooms. Renowned in herbal medicine, it offers calming aromatherapy benefits, promotes relaxation, and possesses antiseptic properties, making it a versatile staple in holistic wellness and natural skincare traditions.
