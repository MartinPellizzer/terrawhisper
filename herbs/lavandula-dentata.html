Lavandula dentata

Lavandula dentata, commonly known as French lavender or dentate lavender, is a versatile perennial shrub prized for its aromatic foliage and delicate blue flowers. Renowned for its calming properties, this medicinal plant is frequently used in aromatherapy and traditional remedies to soothe anxiety, promote sleep, and support skin health.

  • Scientific name: Lavandula dentata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lavandula.
