Ferula fukanensis

Ferula fukanensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb within the Apiaceae family, holds great potential in traditional pharmacology. Endemic to specific ecological niches, this plant is studied for its bioactive compounds. Researchers are increasingly exploring its phytochemical properties to discover new therapeutic applications for treating various human ailments.

  • Scientific name: Ferula fukanensis
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, falling specifically into the family Apiaceae. Within this family, it is identified by the genus Ferula.

Distribution

This plant is recognized for its specific and limited geographical distribution. It is found within the vast territories of China, specifically concentrated in a single region. Within China, its occurrence is documented in the Xinjiang Uyghur Autonomous Region. This area provides the unique environmental conditions necessary for the species to thrive. Consequently, the plant's range is quite localized to these particular mountainous or arid landscapes.
