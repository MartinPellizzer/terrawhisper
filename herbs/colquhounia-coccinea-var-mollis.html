Colquhounia coccinea var. mollis

Colquhounia coccinea var. mollis, commonly known as soft colquhounia, is a resilient perennial herb native to East Asian highlands. Renowned for its vibrant crimson blooms, this botanical treasure is valued in traditional medicine for its potential antioxidant properties and its ability to support various wellness functions within herbal therapies.

  • Scientific name: Colquhounia coccinea var. mollis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Colquhounia.
