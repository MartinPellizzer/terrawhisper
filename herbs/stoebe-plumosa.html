Stoebe plumosa

Stoebe plumosa, commonly known as the feathery stoebe, is a distinctive shrub native to South Africa. Renowned for its fine, needle-like foliage and delicate texture, this resilient plant thrives in diverse ecosystems. Beyond its ornamental appeal, it holds significant botanical interest for its unique adaptations to coastal environments.

  • Scientific name: Stoebe plumosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Stoebe plumosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
