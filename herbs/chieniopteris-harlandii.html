Chieniopteris harlandii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. This fern is specifically categorized under the family Aspleniaceae, falling into the genus Chieniopteris, with the unique species designation harlandii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?
