Corymbia tessellaris

Corymbia tessellaris, commonly known as the Pilbara Bloodwood, is a resilient tree native to the arid regions of Western Australia. Renowned for its striking, mottled bark and vibrant blossoms, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering traditional medicinal applications for treating various ailments within indigenous cultural practices.

  • Scientific name: Corymbia tessellaris
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Corymbia, which encompasses this specific medicinal species.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a unique and widely separated geographical distribution across different continents. In the region of West Tropical Africa, its presence is noted specifically in Senegal.
