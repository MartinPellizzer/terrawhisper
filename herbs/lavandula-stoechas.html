Lavandula stoechas

Lavandula stoechas, commonly known as Spanish lavender or butterfly lavender, is a fragrant perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its striking purple bracts, this medicinal herb offers soothing properties. It is traditionally used to alleviate anxiety, promote sleep, and treat skin irritations through its aromatic essential oils.

  • Scientific name: Lavandula stoechas
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Lavandula.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the Mediterranean regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing naturally in the Balearic Islands, known as Baleares. Its presence extends to the island of Corsica and the coastal areas of France. Additionally, the species is widely distributed throughout Portugal. Finally, it is commonly located on the island of Sardegna.
