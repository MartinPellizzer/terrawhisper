Tragopogon

Tragopogon, commonly known as goat's beard or salsify, is a versatile genus of flowering plants in the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its striking, dandelion-like blooms, these resilient herbs offer both ornamental beauty and nutritional value, with certain species serving as edible roots rich in inulin for medicinal use.

  • Scientific name: Tragopogon
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, placing it in the family Asteraceae. Finally, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Tragopogon.
