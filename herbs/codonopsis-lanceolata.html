Codonopsis lanceolata

Deodeok

Codonopsis lanceolata, commonly known as the lance-leaved codonoopsis, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its unique lanceolate leaves, it holds significant value in traditional herbal medicine. Researchers continue to explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in treating diverse health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Codonopsis lanceolata
  • Family: Campanulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Asterales and the family Campanulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Codonoopsis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across several key regions within the Russian Far East. Specifically, its range includes the Amur and Khabarovsk regions. It also inhabits the Kuril Islands and the Primorye territory. Beyond the Russian borders, the species extends into China. Its distribution specifically covers the South-Central portion of the Chinese landscape.
