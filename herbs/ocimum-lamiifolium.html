Ocimum lamiiifolium, scientifically known as Ocimum lamiiifolium, is a potent medicinal herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Commonly referred to as "False Basil," this aromatic plant is celebrated in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, offering significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant benefits to support holistic wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Cameroon and Gabon. Its presence also extends to the Democratic Republic of the Congo in the same central region. Additionally, the species is located in Rwanda, further establishing its footprint in West-Central Tropical Africa. Finally, its range reaches into Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Eritrea.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Amchi or Indian Borage, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties found throughout its structure. The most significant medicinal component is the leaves, which are often harvested and processed for their essential oils. Whether used as a fresh leaf or in the form of dried plant parts, these elements provide potent aromatic and healing benefits. Additionally, the stems contribute to the plant's overall chemical profile, making the entire organism a versatile resource in herbal remedies.
