Berberis deinacantha

What's the taxonomical classification of Berberis deinacantha?

Berberis deinacantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales. The plant is a representative of the family Berberidaceae, situated within the genus Berberis, and is specifically identified by its species name, deinacantha.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Berberis deinacantha has woody, spreading shrubs characterized by stiff, spiny branches and small, leathery, elliptic leaves with entire margins. The foliage is typically dark green and arranged in clusters along the stems. Its most distinctive feature is the presence of sharp, needle-like spines that emerge from the axils of the leaves. Small, yellowish flowers emerge from these axils during the blooming season. These flowers eventually develop into small, fleshy berries that serve as a key identifier for the species.
