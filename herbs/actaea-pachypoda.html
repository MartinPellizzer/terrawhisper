Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Mayapple or Bloodroot, possesses various structural components, though its most significant medicinal and toxic elements are often concentrated in its subterranean structures. The roots of Actaea pachypoda consist of a thick, fleshy rhizome that grows horizontally beneath the soil surface. These rhizomes are highly potent and contain concentrated alkaloids, which historically have been used in traditional medicine but also pose a significant risk of toxicity if mishandled.
