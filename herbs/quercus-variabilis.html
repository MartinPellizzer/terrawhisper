Quercus variabilis

What's the taxonomical classification of Quercus variabilis?

Quercus variabilis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further organized under the order Fagales. It falls within the family Fagaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Quercus and the species variabilis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Quercus variabilis has elliptical leaves with wavy margins, a woody trunk with deeply furrowed gray bark, and small, nut-like acorns attached to short peduncles. The foliage typically displays a variable coloration ranging from deep green to bronze depending on the seasonal maturity. Its branching pattern is irregular and spreading, providing a dense canopy structure. Small, inconspicuous flowers appear in clusters during the spring growing season. The root system is extensive and tapers into a sturdy woody base to support its upright growth.
