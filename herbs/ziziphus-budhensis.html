Ziziphus budhensis

Ziziphus budhensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in South Asia, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties. Native to hilly regions, this plant is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation, digestive issues, and skin disorders, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphus budhensis
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant, Ziziphus budhensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales. Furthermore, it is a member of the Rhamnaceae family and falls under the genus Ziziphus.
