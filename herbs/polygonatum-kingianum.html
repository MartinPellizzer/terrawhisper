Polygonatum kingianum

Polygonatum kingianum, commonly known as King's Solomon's Seal, is a perennial herb native to China. Renowned in traditional medicine, its rhizomes possess significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. This elegant plant is valued for its ability to nourish the body and support overall systemic health.

  • Scientific name: Polygonatum kingianum
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Polygonatum kingianum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Ultimately, it is placed within the genus Polygonatum.
