Plant Parts

This plant, Polygonatum kingianum, is characterized by its distinct underground structures that are essential for its medicinal properties. The plant features a prominent rhizome system that serves as its primary storage organ. Specifically, it develops thick, creeping rhizomes that extend through the soil, creating a rhizomatous growth habit that allows the plant to spread effectively. While the entire underground portion is often referred to as the root system in a general sense, the medicinal value is concentrated in these fleshy rhizomes. For pharmacological use and traditional medicine, the harvested material is typically processed into dry rhizomes to ensure stability and potency.
