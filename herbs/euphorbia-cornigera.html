Horned spurge, scientifically known as Euphorbia cornigera, is a striking succulent native to Mexico. Characterized by its fleshy, cylindrical stems and unique, horn-like structures, this plant is prized in xeriscaping. While visually captivating, it contains a toxic milky latex, requiring careful handling to avoid skin and eye irritation.
