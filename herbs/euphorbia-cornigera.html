Euphorbia cornigera

Euphorbia cornigera, commonly known as the horn euphorbia, is a striking succulent native to Mexico. Characterized by its cylindrical, cactus-like stems and unique horn-shaped tubercles, this resilient plant thrives in arid environments. While visually captivating, it contains a toxic milky latex, requiring careful handling to avoid skin irritation.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia cornigera
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
