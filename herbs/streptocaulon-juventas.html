Streptocaulon juventas

Streptocaulon juventas, commonly known as the "Wild Ivy" or "Streptocaulo," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat skin ailments, promote wound healing, and manage inflammatory conditions through its natural properties.

  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
