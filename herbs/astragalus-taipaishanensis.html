Astragalus taipaishanensis

Astragalus taipaishanensis, commonly known as a rare medicinal legume, is an endemic species native to the high-altitude forests of Taiwan. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant holds significant potential in traditional medicine, offering promising bioactive compounds for immune support and antioxidant research in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus taipaishanensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling within the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Astragalus, specifically the species Astragalus taipaishanensis.
