American basswood, scientifically known as Tilia americana, is a versatile deciduous tree valued for its therapeutic properties. Often used in traditional herbalism, its fragrant flowers yield a soothing tea known to reduce anxiety, ease sleep disturbances, and alleviate digestive discomfort, making it a beloved staple in natural wellness practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the American Linden or Basswood, utilizes its leaves as a significant component of its physical structure and physiological function. The leaves of Tilia americana are typically heart-shaped with finely serrated margins and a soft, asymmetrical base, providing a broad surface area for photosynthesis. In medicinal contexts, while the flowers are most frequently harvested for herbal teas, the leaves are sometimes studied for their chemical composition and can contribute to the overall therapeutic profile of the plant.
