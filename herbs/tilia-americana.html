Tilia americana

Tilia americana, commonly known as American Linden or Basswood, is a deciduous tree prized for its fragrant, bell-shaped flowers. Historically used in traditional herbalism, its blossoms are steeped to create soothing teas known for their calming, sedative, and anti-inflammatory properties, offering natural relief from stress and mild anxiety.

  • Scientific name: Tilia americana
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae, it is classified under the genus Tilia.
