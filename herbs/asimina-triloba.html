Asimina triloba

Asimina triloba, commonly known as pawpaw, is a North American perennial prized for its unique tropical flavor and medicinal potential. Rich in acetogenins, antioxidants, and vitamins, this native species is studied for its bioactive properties, offering promising applications in supporting immune health and addressing various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Asimina triloba
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Magnoliales and the family Annonaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established under the genus Asimina.
