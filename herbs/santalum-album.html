Santalum album

Santalum album, commonly known as Indian sandalwood, is a highly revered evergreen tree prized for its aromatic heartwood. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antiseptic, and cooling properties. Today, it remains a cornerstone of holistic wellness, widely used in skincare, aromatherapy, and calming spiritual rituals.

  • Scientific name: Santalum album
  • Family: Santalaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Santalales, it is classified under the family Santalaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Santalum, specifically the species Santalum album.

Common Names

Distribution

Its presence extends into East Asia, specifically located in Taiwan. Additionally, it is documented to inhabit the area known as China Southeast. Plant Parts

This plant, Santalum album, commonly known as Indian sandalwood, derives its profound medicinal and aromatic value primarily from its heartwood. Unlike the softer sapwood, the dense, inner core of the tree is highly prized for its therapeutic properties and rich fragrance. Contained within this woody tissue are concentrated essential oils, which serve as the plant's primary bioactive components. These oils are extracted through distillation and are widely utilized in traditional medicine for their anti-inflammatory, antiseptic, and calming effects on the skin and nervous system.
