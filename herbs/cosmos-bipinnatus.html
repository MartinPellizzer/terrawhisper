Cosmos bipinnatus

Cosmos bipinnatus, commonly known as Garden Cosmos, is a vibrant annual herb celebrated for its striking, ray-like blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this species holds traditional significance in various cultures. Its resilient nature and diverse floral hues make it a staple for both pollinators and botanical enthusiasts.

  • Scientific name: Cosmos bipinnatus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, it is classified under the genus Cosmos.
