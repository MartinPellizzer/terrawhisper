Hylotelephium sieboldii

Hylotelephium sieboldii, commonly known as Orpine or Sedum sieboldii, is a charming perennial valued for both its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Native to East Asia, this succulent-like plant is traditionally used in herbal remedies to promote skin healing, reduce inflammation, and support overall vitality through its nutrient-rich properties.

  • Scientific name: Hylotelephium sieboldii
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Saxifrages and placed in the family Crassulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is found within the genus Hylotelephium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Hylotelephium sieboldii, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. Plant Parts

This plant, known as Hylotelephium sieboldii, is characterized by its distinctive succulent leaves that serve as a primary component of its medicinal profile. These fleshy leaves are typically arranged in a rosette pattern, possessing a thick, moisture-retaining texture that is characteristic of the Crassulaceae family. In traditional herbal applications, the leaves are the most significant part utilized, often valued for their specific chemical properties and their role in various therapeutic preparations.
