Hylotelephium sieboldii

Hylotelephium sieboldii, commonly known as Orpine or Sedum sieboldii, is a charming perennial valued for both its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Native to East Asia, this succulent-like plant is traditionally used in herbal remedies to promote skin healing, reduce inflammation, and support overall vitality through its nutrient-rich properties.

  • Scientific name: Hylotelephium sieboldii
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Saxifrages and placed in the family Crassulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is found within the genus Hylotelephium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the globe. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented within the borders of Germany. Moving toward Southeast Europe, the species can be found in Bulgaria, while it extends into the Baltic States of Eastern Europe. Its range also reaches into China, specifically throughout the South-Central regions. Finally, the plant is widely distributed across Eastern Asia, with a notable presence in Japan.
