Mitragyna diversifolia

Mitragyna diversifolia, commonly known as the wild kratom, is a medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Distinguished by its diverse leaf shapes, this botanical species contains bioactive alkaloids. Long utilized in traditional medicine, it offers significant pharmacological potential for addressing various ailments, making it a subject of intense scientific interest.

  • Scientific name: Mitragyna diversifolia
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Mitragyna.
