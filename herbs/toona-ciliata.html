Toona ciliata

Toona ciliata, commonly known as Indian Mahogany, is a large deciduous tree prized for its high-quality timber and medicinal properties. Native to South Asia, its leaves and bark are utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin diseases, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Toona ciliata
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Toona, where it is known as Toona ciliata.
