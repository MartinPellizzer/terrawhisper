Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Toona, where it is known as Toona ciliata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the islands within the Gulf of Guinea. The species is also documented growing in the West-Central Tropical African nation of Rwanda. Moving toward East Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in the countries of Kenya and Uganda. Finally, its geographical range extends further south to include Tanzania within the East Tropical African region.
