Penianthus zenkeri

Penianthus zenkeri, commonly known as a medicinal climbing plant, holds significant importance in traditional African ethnobotany. Native to specific tropical regions, this species is highly valued for its therapeutic properties. Researchers study its unique chemical constituents to explore potential applications in treating various ailments and advancing modern pharmacological developments.

  • Scientific name: Penianthus zenkeri
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Penianthus zenkeri, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Menispermaceae and is situated within the genus Penianthus.

Distribution

This plant is found exclusively within the region of West Tropical Africa. Its natural habitat spans across several key countries in this part of the continent. Specifically, its distribution includes the coastal and forest zones of Ghana and the Ivory Coast. It can also be located within the borders of Liberia and Sierra Leone. Finally, the range of this species extends into the territory of Nigeria.
