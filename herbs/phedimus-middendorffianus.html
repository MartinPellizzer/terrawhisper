Phedimus middendorffianus

Phedimus middendorffianus, commonly known as a vital medicinal plant, holds significant importance in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and pharmacological potential is essential for exploring its role in natural medicine and ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Phedimus middendorffianus
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Crassulaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Phedimus, identifying it as Phedimus middendorffianus.

Common Names

This plant, Phedimus middendorffianus, is known by various common names depending on the language and region. In Poland, it is referred to as Fedimus Middenndorfa, while in Finland, it is called mantšurianmaksaruoho. Its Chinese nomenclature includes several names such as 吉林景天, 吉林费菜, and 岩景天.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented within the borders of Germany. Moving eastward, the species is found in the vast Siberian territory of Yakutiya. It also extends into the Russian Far East, specifically inhabiting the Amur region. Furthermore, its distribution covers the Khabarovsk and Primorye areas within the same Far Eastern territory.
