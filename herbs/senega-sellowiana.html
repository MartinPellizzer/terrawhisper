Senega sellowiana

Senega selloiviana, commonly known as Senega, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its potent expectorant properties. Derived from its nutrient-rich rhizomes, this botanical powerhouse is primarily utilized to soothe respiratory ailments, effectively thinning mucus and easing persistent coughs, making it a cornerstone of herbal pharmacology.

  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
