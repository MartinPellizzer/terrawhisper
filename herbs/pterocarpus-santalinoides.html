Pterocarpus santalinoides

Pterocarpus santalinoides, commonly known as Santa Linoides, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and inflammation. Its unique phytochemical profile makes it a subject of significant pharmacological interest.
