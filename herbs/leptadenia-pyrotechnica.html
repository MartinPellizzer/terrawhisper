Leptadenia pyrotechnica

Leptadenia pyrotechnica, commonly known as the fire-stick plant, is a resilient climbing shrub native to arid Indian landscapes. Renowned in traditional medicine, this species possesses significant therapeutic potential. Its various parts are utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering valuable insights for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Leptadenia pyrotechnica
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Leptadenia.
