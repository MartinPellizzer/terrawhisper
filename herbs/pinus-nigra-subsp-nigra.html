Pinus nigra subsp. nigra

Pinus nigra subsp. nigra, commonly known as the Austrian Black Pine, is a resilient evergreen valued for its robust structure. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this subspecies possesses traditional medicinal qualities, often utilized in folk remedies for its resinous extracts, which exhibit potential antiseptic and anti-inflammatory properties in holistic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Pinus nigra subsp. nigra
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is classified under the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic hierarchy identifies it under the genus Pinus as the subspecies Pinus nigra subsp. nigra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern focused primarily across Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the natural landscapes of Denmark and Great Britain. The species also extends its range into the island of Ireland. Furthermore, its presence is noted throughout the Scandinavian regions of Norway and Sweden. Together, these locations define the northern territories where this subspecies is established.
