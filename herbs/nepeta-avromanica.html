Nepeta avromanica

Nepeta avromanica, commonly known as the Avroman catmint, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to specific Mediterranean regions. Valued for its aromatic properties, this perennial plant contains essential oils used in traditional remedies. It is primarily studied for its potential calming effects and its unique role in botanical wellness.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Nepeta.
