Napoleonaea imperialis

Napoleonaea imperialis, commonly known as the Imperial Napoleon, is a striking tropical shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its spectacular, large scarlet flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. In traditional medicine, various parts are utilized for their bioactive properties to treat diverse ailments and promote wellness.

  • Scientific name: Napoleonaea imperialis
  • Family: Lecythidaceae
  • Native range: Africa
