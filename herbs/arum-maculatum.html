Arum maculatum

Lords and ladies

Arum maculatum, commonly known as Lords-and-Ladies or Cuckoo-pint, is a striking perennial herb native to Europe. Characterized by its unique spadix and spathe, this plant is historically significant in folk medicine. However, caution is essential, as all parts contain calcium oxalate crystals, making it highly toxic if ingested.

  • Scientific name: Arum maculatum
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Alismatalees, falling into the family Araceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Arum.
