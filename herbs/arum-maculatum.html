Lords and ladies, scientifically known as Arum maculatum, is a captivating yet deceptive woodland perennial. Renowned for its striking spathe and spadix, this plant holds a complex place in ethnobotany. While historically used in traditional medicine, its potent calcium oxalate crystals require extreme caution due to their significant toxicity.
