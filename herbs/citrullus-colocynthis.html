Scientific literature: Very Extensive (732 studies) · ★★★★★

Colocynth, scientifically known as Citrullus colocynthis, is a desert-dwelling plant renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Often referred to as bitter apple, it contains bioactive compounds used traditionally to treat digestive ailments and skin conditions. While highly effective in specific doses, its intense bitterness demands careful, expert application.

The reproduction of Citrullus colocynthis is characterized by a bisexual sexual reproductive system where self-fertilization is absent, necessitating external agents for successful fertilization. These fruits contain a high volume of seeds, often exceeding 1,000 seeds per fruit.
