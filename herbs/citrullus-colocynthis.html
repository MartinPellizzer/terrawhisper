Citrullus colocynthis

Citrullus colocynthis, commonly known as bitter apple or colocynth, is a desert-dwelling plant valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent purgative properties, it contains bioactive cucurbitacins. While used historically to treat digestive ailments and skin conditions, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling and precise medicinal application.

  • Scientific name: Citrullus colocynthis
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cucurbitales, it is classified under the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Citrullus.
