Colocynth, scientifically known as Citrullus colocynthis, is a desert-dwelling plant renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Often referred to as bitter apple, it contains bioactive compounds used traditionally to treat digestive ailments and skin conditions. While highly effective in specific doses, its intense bitterness demands careful, expert application.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical presence across several regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its distribution can be observed specifically within Germany and Hungary. Moving toward the Mediterranean, the species is found in Southwestern Europe, inhabiting areas such as Spain and the island of Corse. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as bitter apple, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties. The most prominent part is the fruit, which grows as a large, ribbed, and bitter globose fruits that can be used in traditional remedies. Inside these fleshy structures are numerous small seeds, and each individual seed contains potent bioactive compounds often harvested for pharmacological study. Additionally, the roots of the plant are frequently employed in various herbal preparations to treat different ailments.
