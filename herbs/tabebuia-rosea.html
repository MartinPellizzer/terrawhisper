Tabebuia rosea

Tabebuia rosea, commonly known as Pink Trumpet Tree, is a stunning tropical flowering plant celebrated for its breathtaking pink blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally used in various cultures to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues due to its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Tabebuia rosea
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
