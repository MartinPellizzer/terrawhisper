Tabebuia rosea

Matilizhuate

Tabebuia rosea, commonly known as Pink Trumpet Tree, is a stunning tropical flowering plant celebrated for its breathtaking pink blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally used in various cultures to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues due to its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Tabebuia rosea
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Bignoniaceae and the genus Tabebuia. Specifically, Tabebuia rosea represents a distinct species within this taxonomic hierarchy.
