Glebionis coronaria

Garland chrysanthemum

Glebionis coronaria, commonly known as the Crowned Glebio, is a rare medicinal plant revered for its potent therapeutic properties. Found in remote mountainous regions, its vibrant blossoms contain unique bioactive compounds. Traditionally used to alleviate inflammation and support immune health, it remains a subject of intense botanical research today.
