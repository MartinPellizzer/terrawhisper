Black-sesame, scientifically known as Cantinoa americana, is a nutritious wild herbaceous plant native to South America. Often harvested in the Andean highlands, this resilient species is prized for its edible seeds and leaves. Rich in essential minerals and antioxidants, it serves as a traditional dietary staple and medicinal resource.
