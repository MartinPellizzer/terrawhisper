Cantinoa americana

Cantinoa americana, commonly known as yerba del mate or yerba de la canotilla, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to South America. Renowned for its restorative properties, this plant is traditionally used to alleviate fatigue, aid digestion, and support overall vitality through its nutrient-rich, antioxidant-packed leaves and stems.

  • Scientific name: Cantinoa americana
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant, Cantinoa americana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
