Gentiana veitchiorum

Gentiana veietschiorum, commonly known as Veitch's gentian, is a striking perennial wildflower native to high-altitude meadows. Renowned for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blue blooms, this resilient species thrives in alpine environments. Historically valued in herbal medicine, it serves as a fascinating subject for botanical study and mountain ecosystem conservation.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana veitchiorum
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Gentianaceae and falls under the genus Gentiana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key mountainous regions of Asia. In China, its range extends through the South-Central and North-Central provinces. It is also specifically documented within the high-altitude territories of Qinghai. Furthermore, its distribution encompasses the vast and rugged landscape of Tibet. Finally, the species can be found within the East Himalaya region of the Indian Subcontinent.
