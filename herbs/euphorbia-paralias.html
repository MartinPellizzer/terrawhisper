Sea spurge, scientifically known as Euphorbia paralias, is a resilient perennial herb found primarily in coastal sandy habitats. Characterized by its milky sap and distinctive yellow-green bracts, this plant plays a vital role in stabilizing dunes. Historically, various species of Euphorbia have been explored for their unique medicinal properties.
