Euphorbia paralias

Sea spurge

Euphorbia paralias, commonly known as a coastal spurge, is a resilient succulent found in saline environments. Renowned for its unique adaptations to sandy terrains, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Researchers study its bioactive compounds, exploring potential therapeutic applications in treating skin conditions and inflammatory ailments through traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia paralias
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclassification in Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Euphorbia.
