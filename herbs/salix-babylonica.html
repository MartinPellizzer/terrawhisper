Babylon willow

Salix babylonica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (2 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Babylon willow, scientifically known as Salix babylonica, is a graceful, weeping tree renowned for its medicinal heritage. Historically valued for its bark, it contains salicin, a natural precursor to aspirin. This plant has long been utilized in traditional remedies to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and manage fevers effectively.
