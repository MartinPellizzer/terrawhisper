Populus × canadensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (1 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Hybrid Black Poplar, scientifically known as Populus × canadensis, is a vigorous deciduous tree celebrated for its rapid growth and versatile applications. Beyond its ecological role, its bark contains salicin, a precursor to aspirin, offering traditional medicinal properties for managing pain and inflammation through its potent, natural chemical compounds.
