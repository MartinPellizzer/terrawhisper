What's the taxonomical classification of Adenophora khasiana?

Adenophora khasiana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the family Campanulaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized within the genus Adenophora, specifically identifying as the species khasiana.
