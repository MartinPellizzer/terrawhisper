Ageratum houstonianum

Mexican floss flower

Ageratum houstonianum, commonly known as Flossflower, is a vibrant perennial prized for its delicate, fuzzy blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in various cultures, it possesses diverse medicinal properties, often utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects in herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Ageratum houstonianum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ageratum houstonianum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Ageratum.
