Morinda

Morinda, commonly known as noni, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it has been used in traditional medicine for centuries to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote skin health. This potent botanical offers a rich source of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Morinda
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rubiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Morinda.

Common Names

This plant, belonging to the genus Morinda, is known by various names across different languages and regions. In Simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 木巴戟属 (zh-hans), while in Traditional Chinese, it is known as 巴戟天属 (zh-hant) and 巴戟天屬 (zh-hk). In the Udmurt language, it is called Моринда (udm), and in Nepali, it is identified as मोरिन्दा (new).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the nation of Benin. Furthermore, its presence extends to the coastal areas of Gambia. The species is also documented in Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, the distribution includes the territory of Guinea.
