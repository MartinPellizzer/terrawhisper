Morinda, scientifically known as Morinda, is a versatile genus of flowering plants in the Rubiaceae family. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly through the famous Noni fruit, it offers diverse therapeutic properties. Used for centuries to support immunity and skin health, this resilient plant remains a cornerstone of herbal wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rubiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Morinda.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the nation of Benin. Furthermore, its presence extends to the coastal areas of Gambia. The species is also documented in Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, the distribution includes the territory of Guinea.
