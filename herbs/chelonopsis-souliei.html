Chelonopsis souliei

Chelonopsis souliei, commonly known as the Golden Shell Fern, is a rare medicinal plant native to tropical rainforest understories. Renowned for its unique bioluminescent properties, it contains potent alkaloids used in traditional healing to soothe inflammation and promote rapid cellular regeneration, making it a cornerstone of ancient botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Chelonopsis souliei
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Chelonopsis souliei, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Chelonopsis.
