Tragopogon pratensis, scientifically known as Tragopogon pratensis, is commonly referred to as meadow salsify. Valued in traditional herbalism, it offers nutritional benefits and potential medicinal properties, making it a fascinating subject for botanical study.

Distribution

This plant, known as Tragopogon pratensis, exhibits a specific distribution across the northern regions of Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Denmark. Additionally, its presence extends to the Scandinavian countries of Norway and Sweden. The species is also documented throughout the landscape of Finland. Finally, its geographical range includes the territory of Great Britain.

This plant, Tragopogon pratensis, commonly known as meadow salsify, possesses a distinct morphological structure where the root plays a significant role in its survival and medicinal potential. The plant features a deep, fleshy taproot that extends vertically into the soil, serving as a primary storage organ for nutrients and water.
