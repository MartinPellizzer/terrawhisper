Pulicaria burchardii

Pulicaria burchardii, commonly known as a species of tansy, is a resilient herbaceous plant valued in traditional medicine. Often found in diverse landscapes, it possesses unique phytochemical properties. Renowned for its aromatic qualities, this plant is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic benefits in treating various inflammatory conditions.
