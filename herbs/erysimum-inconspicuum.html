Erysimum inconspicuum

Erysimum inconspicuum, commonly known as the inconspicuous wallflower, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. While primarily valued by botanists for its unique floral characteristics, it holds potential in traditional ethnobotany. This hardy plant thrives in rocky terrains, offering insights into specialized survival and medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Erysimum inconspicuum
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Erysimum.
