Colchicum palaestinum

Colchicum palaestinum, commonly known as the Palestinian autumn crocus, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Eastern Mediterranean. Renowned for its striking seasonal blooms, this medicinal plant contains bioactive alkaloids. Traditionally used in regional ethnobotany, it holds significant pharmacological interest for its potential therapeutic and cytotoxic properties.

  • Scientific name: Colchicum palaestinum
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Colchicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Colchicum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across parts of Northern Africa and Western Asia. In the North African region, its presence is documented within the territories of Egypt and Libya. Moving toward Western Asia, the species can be found spanning the area from Lebanon to Syria. It is also native to the region of Palestine. Finally, its distribution extends into the Sinai Peninsula, completing its localized habitat range.
