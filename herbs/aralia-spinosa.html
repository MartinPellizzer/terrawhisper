Devil's walking stick, scientifically known as Aralia spinosa, is a distinctive North American perennial shrub. Characterized by its formidable, spine-covered stems and large, compound leaves, this plant offers significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in folk medicine, it possesses various bioactive properties currently being studied for their therapeutic potential.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across both Europe and North America. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Albania. Moving to North America, the species is well-established throughout several states in the North-Central U.S.A. specifically within Illinois, Iowa, and Missouri. Additionally, its presence extends further south into the North-Central U.S.A. region of Oklahoma.
