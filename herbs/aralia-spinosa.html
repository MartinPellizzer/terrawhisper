Aralia spinosa

Aralia spinosa, commonly known as prickly angelica, is a resilient perennial native to North America. Characterized by its thorny stems and compound leaves, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used by various indigenous cultures, it offers diverse medicinal properties, particularly for addressing respiratory issues and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Aralia spinosa
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Apiales and the family Araliaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aralia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across both Europe and North America. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Albania. Moving to North America, the species is well-established throughout several states in the North-Central U.S.A. specifically within Illinois, Iowa, and Missouri. Additionally, its presence extends further south into the North-Central U.S.A. region of Oklahoma. Consequently, the habitat of Aralia spinosa covers diverse territories across these different continents.
