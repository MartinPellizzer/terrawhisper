What's the taxonomical classification of Calanthe whiteana?

Calanthe whiteana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It further falls under the order Asparagales and is a member of the family Orchidaceae. Ultimately, this specific plant is defined by its classification within the genus Calanthe, with its unique designation being the species whiteana.
