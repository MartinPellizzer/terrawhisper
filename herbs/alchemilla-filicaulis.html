Slender lady's-mantle, scientifically known as Alchemilla filicaulis, is a delicate perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its fine, lace-like foliage, this plant is often utilized for its astringent properties. It serves as a soothing botanical remedy, traditionally supporting reproductive health and aiding in various skin treatments.
