Alchemilla filicaulis

Alchemilla filicaulis, commonly known as Lady's Mantle, is a delicate perennial prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its soft, scalloped leaves that collect morning dew, this plant is historically used to support women's reproductive health, soothe skin inflammations, and act as a gentle astringent to promote healing and balance.

  • Scientific name: Alchemilla filicaulis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It falls into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, specifically within the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Alchemilla.
