Hypericum elodeoides

Hypericum elodeoides, commonly known as St. John's waterwort, is a perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its small, yellowish flowers and aquatic tendencies, this species belongs to the Hypericaceae family. While less studied than its cousin, it holds significant botanical interest for its unique ecological niche.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum elodeoides
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Hypericum.
