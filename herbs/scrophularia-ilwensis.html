Scrophularia ilwensis

Scrophularia ilwensis, commonly known as the Ilwens figwort, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific mountainous regions. Distinguished by its unique flowering structure, it has historically been studied for its potential therapeutic properties. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for exploring its traditional uses in natural medicine.
