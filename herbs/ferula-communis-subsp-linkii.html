Ferula communis subsp. linkii

Ferula communis subsp. linkii, commonly known as a variety of giant fennel, is a resilient medicinal herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it has been used in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern science.

  • Family: Apiaceae
