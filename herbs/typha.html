Typha

Reedmace

Typha, commonly known as cattails or bulrushes, is a versatile wetland plant prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its healing properties, it is used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin irritations. Beyond its medicinal value, every part of this resilient plant offers significant nutritional and ecological benefits.

  • Scientific name: Typha
  • Family: Typhaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Poales and falls into the family Typhaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Typha.
