Phlomis aurea

Phlomis aurea, commonly known as Golden Jerusalem Sage, is a striking perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Characterized by its architectural, velvety foliage and brilliant yellow flowering spikes, this hardy plant thrives in sunny, rocky soils. It is highly valued in landscaping for its drought tolerance and pollinator-friendly blooms.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis aurea
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Ultimately, it resides in the family Lamiaceae under the genus Phlomis.
