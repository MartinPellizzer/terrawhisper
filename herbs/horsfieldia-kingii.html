Horsfieldia kingii

Horsfieldia kingii, commonly known as the King's Horsfieldia, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to tropical rainforest ecosystems. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, this species is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies. Understanding its chemical composition and ecological role is essential for exploring its potential therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Horsfieldia kingii
  • Family: Myristicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, falling into the family Myristicaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Horsfieldia, to which the species Horsfieldia kingii belongs.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several specific regions in Asia. Within China, its distribution extends through South-Central China and the Southeast regions. It is also documented to grow natively on the island of Hainan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found in the state of Assam. Additionally, its range includes the neighboring country of Bangladesh.
