Ilex asprella

Ilex asprella, commonly known as the Holly plant, is a versatile evergreen shrub valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its glossy foliage and resilient nature, it is studied for its bioactive compounds. This medicinal plant offers unique therapeutic potential, particularly in supporting immune health and providing antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Ilex asprella
  • Family: Aquifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Aquifoliales, falling into the family Aquifoliaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Ilex.

Common Names

This plant, Ilex asprella, is known by various common names depending on the language and region. In Mandarin Chinese, it is referred to as 梅葉冬青 (zh), 梅叶冬青 (zh-cn), and 梅叶冬青 (zh-hans). In Taiwan, the specific name 燈稱花 (zh-tw) is used, while in Cantonese, it is known as 汀秤仔 (yue).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is primarily located in the Southeast portion of the country. Its range extends further into Eastern Asia, where it can be found growing in Taiwan. Additionally, the species reaches into the Malesia region, specifically inhabiting the Philippines. Together, these areas define the natural habitat of Ilex asprella.
