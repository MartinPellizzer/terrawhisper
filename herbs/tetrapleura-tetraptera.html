Tetrapleura tetraptera

Aridan

Tetrapleura tetraptera, commonly known as the African bush mango, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern healthcare and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Tetrapleura tetraptera
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Tetrapleura.

Distribution

This plant, known as Tetrapleura tetraptera, is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is specifically documented across several key nations within this tropical zone. In particular, it can be found growing in the territories of Benin and Ghana. The species also extends its reach into Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Furthermore, its geographical range encompasses the coastal and inland areas of the Ivory Coast.
