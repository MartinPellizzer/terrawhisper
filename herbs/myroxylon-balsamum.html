Scientific literature: Very Extensive (324 studies) · ★★★★★

Myroxylon balsamum, is a medicinal plant renowned for producing precious balsam. Historically valued in traditional medicine, its resin possesses significant antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and soothing properties. Widely used in aromatherapy and topical ointments, this botanical treasure offers diverse therapeutic benefits, supporting skin healing and respiratory wellness through its aromatic essence.
