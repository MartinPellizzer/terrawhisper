Myroxylon balsamum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (324 studies) · ★★★★★

Myroxylon balsamum, is a medicinal plant renowned for producing precious balsam. Historically valued in traditional medicine, its resin possesses significant antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and soothing properties. Widely used in aromatherapy and topical ointments, this botanical treasure offers diverse therapeutic benefits, supporting skin healing and respiratory wellness through its aromatic essence.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several regions across the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Ghana and Sierra Leone. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found within the territories of Gabon and the DR Congo. Additionally, its distribution extends into East Tropical Africa, specifically within Tanzania. Together, these locations highlight the broad ecological footprint of Myroxylon balsamum across various tropical zones.
