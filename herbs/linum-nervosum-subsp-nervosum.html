Linum nervosum subsp. nervosum

Linum nervosum subsp. nervosum, commonly known as Nervous Flax, is a specialized perennial herb native to specific North American habitats. Renowned for its delicate blue flowers and distinctive veined foliage, this subspecies holds significant botanical interest for its unique morphological traits and potential applications in traditional herbal medicine.
