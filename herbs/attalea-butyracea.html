Attalea butyracea

Attalea butyracea, commonly known as the butter palm, is a vital tropical plant native to South American ecosystems. Renowned for its nutrient-dense fruits, it serves both nutritional and medicinal purposes. Traditional practitioners utilize its oils and extracts to treat various ailments, highlighting its significant importance in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Attalea butyracea
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
