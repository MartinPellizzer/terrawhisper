Plant Parts

This plant, Attalea butyracea, is primarily valued for its large, oily fruit, which serves as the most significant medicinal and nutritional component. The fruit consists of a thick, fibrous outer layer and a fleshy mesocarp that contains a high concentration of lipids. These extracted oils are often utilized in traditional practices for their various therapeutic properties, making the fruit the central part of the plant used in medicinal applications.
