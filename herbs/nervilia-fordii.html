Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Nervilia fordii, is a terrestrial orchid that is typically utilized in its entirety for medicinal purposes. As a small, seasonal herb, it emerges from underground tubers to produce a single leaf and a flower before retreating during its dormant phase. In many traditional healing practices, the whole plant is harvested to extract its bioactive properties, ensuring that all therapeutic components—from the leafy structure to the specialized rhizomatous parts—are utilized in the preparation of remedies.
