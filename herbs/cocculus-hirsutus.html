Broom Creeper, scientifically known as Cocculus hirsutus, is a versatile climbing shrub native to the arid regions of India and Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, its hairy leaves and roots possess potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable botanical resource for treating various ailments and skin conditions.
