Garcinia cambogioides var. cambogioides

Garcinia cambogioides var. cambogioides, commonly known as Malabar gamboge, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Western Ghats. Renowned for its bioactive hydroxycitric acid, it plays a vital role in traditional Ayurveda. This species is highly valued for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-obesity therapeutic properties.
