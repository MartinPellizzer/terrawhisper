Eucalyptus gomphocephala

Tuart

Eucalyptus gomphocephala, commonly known as the Soap Tree or Soapbark, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Africa. Renowned for its high saponin content, its bark is traditionally used for cleansing. Beyond its soapy properties, it offers significant therapeutic potential in treating skin ailments and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus gomphocephala
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eucalyptus.
