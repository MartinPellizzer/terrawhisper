Rodgersia sambucifolia

Rodgersia sambucifolia, commonly known as Elder-leaved Rodgersia, is a striking perennial prized for its dramatic, architectural foliage. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its robust rhizomes, which possess various medicinal properties, including anti-inflammatory and astringent qualities used in various botanical remedies.
