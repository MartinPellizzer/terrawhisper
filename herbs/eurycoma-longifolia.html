Eurycoma longifolia

Eurycoma longifolia, commonly known as the long-leaved Eurycoma, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the tropical rainforests of Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healthcare advancements.

  • Scientific name: Eurycoma longifolia
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Eurycoma longifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindaales and the family Simaroubaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic placement is defined by its membership in the genus Eurycoma.
