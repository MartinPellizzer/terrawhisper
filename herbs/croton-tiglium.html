Croton tiglium

Croton tiglium, commonly known as the "milk bush," is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its milky latex, it is used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This versatile species holds great potential for pharmacological research and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Croton tiglium
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by its genus, Croton.
