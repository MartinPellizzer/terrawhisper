purging croton

Croton tiglium · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (173 studies) · ★★★★☆

Purging croton, scientifically known as Croton tiglium, is a potent medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its powerful purgative properties, it contains croton oil, traditionally used to treat intestinal ailments. While effective in controlled doses, its intense laxative effects require careful handling to avoid severe toxicity.
