Asimina angustifolia

Asimina angustifolia, commonly known as narrow-leaved asimina, is a specialized medicinal plant valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Found in specific ecological niches, it offers significant therapeutic potential in traditional healing practices. Understanding its botanical characteristics and bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its contributions to modern pharmacology.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Asimina angustifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae under the order Magnoliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Annonaceae and the genus Asimina.
