Angelica shikokiana

Angelica shikokiaana, commonly known as Angelica root or Korean Angelica, is a highly valued medicinal herb in East Asian traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this perennial plant is frequently utilized to support blood circulation, alleviate pain, and promote overall reproductive and cardiovascular health.

  • Scientific name: Angelica shikokiana
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae and falls under the genus Angelica.
