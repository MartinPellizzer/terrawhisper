Aquilaria crassna

Aquilaria crassna, commonly known as agarwood or gaharu, is a precious medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic, resinous heartwood, it is highly valued in traditional medicine and perfumery. This species plays a crucial role in treating various ailments, offering significant therapeutic and economic importance globally.

  • Scientific name: Aquilaria crassna
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant, known scientifically as Aquilaria crassna, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
