Zanthoxylum scandens

Zanthoxylum scandens, commonly known as climbing prickly ash, is a versatile medicinal climbing shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, thanks to its potent essential oils and antioxidant compounds.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum scandens
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Zanthoxylum.
