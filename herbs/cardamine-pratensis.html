The ecology of Cardamine pratensis is defined by its preference for consistently moist to wet environments, frequently colonizing the margins of lakes, marshes, and damp meadows. It thrives in areas characterized by high water tables, often establishing itself within the transition zones between open water and terrestrial landscapes, as well as among lush shrubberies where moisture is abundant. This species is well-adapted to nutrient-rich, alluvial soils and can withstand periodic inundation, allowing it to flourish in various wetland ecosystems and riparian buffer zones where soil saturation remains high throughout much of the growing season.
