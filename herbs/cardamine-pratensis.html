Cardamine pratensis

Cardamine pratensis, commonly known as cuckooflower or lady's smock, is a charming perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Thriving in damp meadows, its delicate pink blossoms signal spring. Traditionally used in herbal medicine for its diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties, it remains a significant botanical treasure in many ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Cardamine pratensis
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales, it is classified under the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Cardamine.
