Myrianthus arboreus

Myrianthus arboreus, commonly known as the Tree Myrtle, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to tropical regions, this woody species is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research due to its rich bioactive compound profile.

  • Scientific name: Myrianthus arboreus
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Rosales and categorized into the family Urticaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Myrianthus.
