Aralia dasyphylla

Aralia dasyphylla, commonly known as the wild sarsaparilla, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South America. Valued in traditional healing, its roots and bark contain bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This robust plant remains a significant botanical resource for natural wellness practitioners.

  • Scientific name: Aralia dasyphylla
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Araliaceae and falls under the genus Aralia.
