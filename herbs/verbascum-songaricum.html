Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the vast and diverse landscapes of Central Asia. Its geographical range specifically extends across the territory of Kazakhstan. Additionally, it can be found growing in the mountainous and arid regions of Kirgizstan. The distribution also includes significant populations within Turkmenistan and Tadzhikistan. Finally, this medicinal species is documented to inhabit the various terrains found throughout Uzbekistan.
