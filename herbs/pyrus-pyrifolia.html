Pyrus pyrifolia

Asian pear

Pyrus pyrifolia, commonly known as the Asian pear or Nashi pear, is a versatile fruit-bearing tree valued for both nutrition and traditional medicine. Rich in antioxidants and dietary fiber, it is utilized in various cultures to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall wellness through its medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Pyrus pyrifolia
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pyrus.

Common Names

Its presence in China is widespread, stretching from the South-Central regions to the Southeast. Furthermore, the species is documented as growing in the northern territory of Inner Mongolia. Finally, its range extends into Eastern Asia, where it is native to Japan.
