Coincya tournefortii

Coincya tournefortii, commonly known as the "silver ragwort" or "coin plant," is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its striking, silvery-felted foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Coincya tournefortii
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe
