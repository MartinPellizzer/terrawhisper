Juniperus squamata

Juniperus squamata, commonly known as the Flaky Juniper, is a resilient coniferous shrub native to high-altitude regions of China. Renowned for its distinctive peeling bark and scale-like foliage, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat respiratory ailments, promote circulation, and provide potent antioxidant benefits.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus squamata
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales, it is classified under the family Cupressaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Juniperus.
