Ferula cupularis

Ferula cupularis, commonly known as a member of the Apiaceae family, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to specific arid regions, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healing remedies.
