Passiflora suberosa

Passiflora suberosa, commonly known as the woody passionflower, is a climbing shrub native to tropical regions of the Americas. Distinguished by its unique floral structure and resilient woody vines, this species belongs to the Passifloraceae family and is valued in traditional medicine for its potential calming and therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Passiflora suberosa
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Passifloraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is found within the genus Passiflora.
