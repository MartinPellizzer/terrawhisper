Decalepis arayalpathra

Decalepis arayalpathra, commonly known as Arayalpathra, is a significant medicinal shrub deeply rooted in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments, skin disorders, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic healing and overall wellness enhancement.

  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
