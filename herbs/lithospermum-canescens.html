Lithospermum canescens

Lithospermum canescens, commonly known as the cane gromwell, is a perennial herb native to specific temperate regions. Valued in traditional herbalism, this plant features distinctive flowering characteristics and deep-reaching roots. Its unique botanical properties continue to intrigue researchers exploring its potential applications in natural medicine and skin health.

  • Scientific name: Lithospermum canescens
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Lithospermum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across both Canada and the United States. In Western Canada, its presence is recorded in the provinces of Manitoba and Saskatchewan.
