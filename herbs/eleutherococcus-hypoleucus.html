Eleutherococcus hypoleucus

Eleutherococcus hypoleucus, commonly known as Siberian ginseng, is a powerful adaptogen renowned for enhancing physical and mental resilience. This medicinal shrub helps the body manage stress, boosts immune function, and combats fatigue. Often used in traditional herbalism, it supports metabolic health and promotes overall vitality and sustained energy levels.

  • Scientific name: Eleutherococcus hypoleucus
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Moving through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is a member of the family Araliaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Eleutherococcus.

Common Names

This plant, Eleutherococcus hypoleucus, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as Siberian ginseng, while in Chinese, it is known as 西伯利亚刺五加 (Xībólìyà cì wǔjiā). In Russian, the plant is commonly called сибирский элеутерококк (sibirskiy eleuterokok).

Distribution

This plant is primarily recognized for its specific presence within the diverse ecosystems of Eastern Asia. Its natural habitat is notably concentrated in the unique landscapes found across Japan. Within this particular region, it thrives according to specific environmental requirements. Consequently, the geographical footprint of this species is relatively localized to these eastern territories. This limited range makes its occurrence in Japan a key characteristic of its distribution.
