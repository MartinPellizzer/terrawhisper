Crotalaria ochroleuca

Crotalaria ochroleuca, commonly known as the Golden Rattlepod, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, often applied to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various digestive concerns.

  • Scientific name: Crotalaria ochroleuca
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Crotalaria.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is specifically documented within the borders of Benin. Furthermore, it can be found growing across the landscape of Burkina. The species also inhabits various areas within Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends to Guinea and Guinea-Bissau.
