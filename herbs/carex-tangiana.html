What's the taxonomical classification of Carex tangiana?

Carex tangiana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Poales. Within this framework, the plant is a member of the family Cyperaceae and is categorized under the genus Carex, eventually reaching its specific designation as the species tangiana.
