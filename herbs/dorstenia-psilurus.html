Dorstenia psilurus

Dorstenia psilurus, commonly known as the Silver-Veined Medic, is a rare botanical treasure valued for its potent bioactive compounds. Renowned in traditional healing circles, this resilient plant offers unique therapeutic properties, particularly in supporting cellular regeneration and soothing inflammation, making it a subject of intense pharmacological interest today.

  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic hierarchy places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales. Finally, it is categorized under the family Moraceae and the genus Dorstenia.
