Morus acidosa

Morus acidosa, commonly known as the Sour Mulberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering a natural resource for holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Morus acidosa
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Rosales and placed in the family Moraeaecae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Morus.

Common Names

This plant, Morus acidosa, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic landscape. In English, it is commonly referred to as the sour mulberry, while in Chinese, it is known as sān zhú. Within its native habitats, it is also identified by the local names such as gāoxìngmǔ in Mandarin or simply as a species of mulberry in broader contexts.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is documented within the country of Spain.
