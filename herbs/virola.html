Virola

Virola, commonly known as a genus of important medicinal trees, holds a profound place in traditional ethnobotany. Native to tropical rainforests, various species are prized for their potent resinous extracts. These bioactive compounds are frequently utilized in indigenous healing practices for their diverse pharmacological properties and spiritual significance.
