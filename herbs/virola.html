Plant Parts

This plant is valued for its unique medicinal properties, which are primarily derived from specific biological products rather than structural tissues. The therapeutic efficacy of the species is often linked to the Virola resin, a thick, viscous substance that serves as a protective layer for the plant. Additionally, the medicinal value is found in the Virola exudate, which is the fluid released from the plant's surfaces during various physiological processes. These substances, characteristic of different spp. within the genus, contain the active compounds necessary for its traditional healing applications.
