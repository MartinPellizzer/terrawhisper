Delonix regia

Deloniox regia, commonly known as the Royal Poinciana or Flamboyant tree, is a breathtaking tropical species celebrated for its fiery orange-red blooms. Beyond its ornamental splendor, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its potential anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties, offering significant therapeutic possibilities in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Delonix regia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and specifically within the genus Delonix.
