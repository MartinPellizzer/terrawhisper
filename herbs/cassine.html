Spoonwoods, scientifically known as Cassine, is a genus of evergreen shrubs and trees native to tropical and subtropical regions. Renowned for their dense, durable wood, these plants also hold significant medicinal value. Various species are utilized in traditional remedies to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various systemic conditions.
