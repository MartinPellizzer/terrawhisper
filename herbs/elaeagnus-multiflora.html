Elaeagnus multiflora

Elaeagnus multiflora, commonly known as cherry silverberry, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized for its edible fruits and medicinal potential. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, it is traditionally used to support digestive health and skin vitality. This hardy plant serves as both a nutritional superfood and a therapeutic botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Elaeagnus multiflora
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Elaeagnaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Elaeagnus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across multiple continents. In Europe, its presence can be identified within the borders of Germany.
