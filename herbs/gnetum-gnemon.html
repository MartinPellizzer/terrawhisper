Gnetum gnemon

Gnetum

Gnetum gnemon, commonly known as Melinjo, is a versatile tropical plant prized for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Native to Southeast Asia, its seeds and leaves are rich in antioxidants and bioactive compounds, traditionally used to treat inflammation, improve digestion, and support overall metabolic health in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Gnetum gnemon
  • Family: Gnetaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Gnetidae and the order Gnetales, falling under the family Gnetaeae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gnetum, specifically as the species Gnetum gnemon.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several distinct Asian regions. Within China, its presence is documented in both the South-Central provinces and the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found specifically in the Assam region. Its distribution also extends into Indo-China, where it grows in the landscapes of Cambodia. Finally, the plant is also native to the forested territories of Myanmar.
