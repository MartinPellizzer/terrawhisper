Greigia sphacelata

Chupón

Greigia sphacelatas, commonly known as "paja" or "pajonales" in its native Andean habitats, is a fascinating bromeliad. Renowned for its dense, rosette-forming foliage, this plant plays a vital ecological role. In traditional medicine, its unique properties are often explored for their potential healing benefits within high-altitude ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Greigia sphacelata
  • Family: Bromeliaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Bromeliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Greigia.
