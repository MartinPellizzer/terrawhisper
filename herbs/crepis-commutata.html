The life history of Crepis commutata is characterized by its specific survival strategy as a hemicryptophyte, a classification that dictates its growth patterns and seasonal adaptations. As both a hemicryptophyte and a nanophanerophyte in terms of its structural development, the plant ensures its survival through overwintering mechanisms where its perennating buds are located at or just below the soil surface, protected by accumulated leaf litter or soil debris. This life form allows it to endure unfavorable seasonal conditions by maintaining dormant buds at the ground level, which then emerge to support rapid vegetative growth once favorable temperatures and moisture levels are restored.
