Spartium junceuum, scientifically known as Spartium junceum, is a striking perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Commonly called Spanish broom, it features slender, green stems and vibrant yellow flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this hardy plant holds significant botanical interest for its unique chemical properties and ecological resilience.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing within the territory of Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is established in both Belgium and Germany. The distribution extends to Southwestern Europe, where it inhabits the Baleares islands. Finally, it is also documented occurring in the Mediterranean region of Corse.
