Ephedra gerardiana

Ephedra gerardiana, commonly known as Somali Ephedra, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the arid regions of East Africa. Renowned for its potent alkaloid content, particularly ephedrine, this medicinal plant has been utilized traditionally to treat respiratory ailments, stimulate energy, and support metabolic functions in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra gerardiana
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Gnetidae and the order Ephedrales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ephedraceae and falls under the genus Ephedra.
