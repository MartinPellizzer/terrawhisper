Scientific literature: Very Extensive (748 studies) · ★★★★★

Chamaemelum nobile, scientifically known as Chamaemelum nobile, is a perennial herb prized for its fragrant, daisy-like flowers. Commonly called Roman chamomile, this medicinal plant is celebrated in aromatherapy and herbalism. Its soothing essential oils are widely used to alleviate anxiety, aid digestion, and reduce inflammation through calming infusions.
