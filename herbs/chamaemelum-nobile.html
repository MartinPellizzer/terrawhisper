Chamaemelum nobile, scientifically known as Chamaemelum nobile, is a perennial herb prized for its fragrant, daisy-like flowers. Commonly called Roman chamomile, this medicinal plant is celebrated in aromatherapy and herbalism. Its soothing essential oils are widely used to alleviate anxiety, aid digestion, and reduce inflammation through calming infusions.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Roman chamomile, possesses various components that contribute to its therapeutic profile. The aromatic character of the species is primarily derived from the essential oils extracted from the flowering heads, which contain bioactive compounds used in aromatherapy and herbal medicine. The inflorescence of the plant is composed of two distinct types of flowers: the delicate, petal-like ray florets that surround the center and the smaller, fertile disk florets located in the middle. Additionally, the plant features finely divided, feathery leaves that add to its characteristic texture, while the roots serve as the foundational anchoring system for the organism.
