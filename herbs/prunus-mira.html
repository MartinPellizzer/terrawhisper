Prunus mira

Tibetan peach

Prunus mira, commonly known as the Miraculous Cherry, is a rare medicinal plant revered for its potent restorative properties. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich bark and blossoms, it is traditionally used to alleviate inflammation and boost immune function. This botanical treasure offers promising therapeutic potential for modern holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Prunus mira
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized into the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Prunus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within certain regions of Asia. In China, it can be found growing across the South-Central territories. Additionally, its presence extends into the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. Beyond Chinese borders, the species is also distributed throughout the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it is documented as growing within the mountainous terrain of Nepal.
