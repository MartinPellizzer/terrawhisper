Sida orientalis

What's the taxonomical classification of Sida orientalis?

Sida orientalis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Malvales and is a member of the family Malvaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Sida, and its specific species name, orientalis.
