Tulbaghia

Tulbaghia, commonly known as garlic leek, is a versatile genus of perennial herbs native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its distinct onion-like aroma, these plants feature elegant, star-shaped flowers. Beyond their ornamental beauty in gardens, various species are utilized in traditional medicine for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties.

  • Scientific name: Tulbaghia
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe
