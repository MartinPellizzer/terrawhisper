Alangium salviifolium

Sage-leaved alangium

Alium salviifolium, commonly known as sage-scented garlic, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its aromatic properties. Characterized by its unique leaf structure, it offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant benefits. Traditionally used in herbal remedies, this plant serves as a vital resource in holistic wellness and natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Alangium salviifolium
  • Family: Cornaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Cornales and the family Cornaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Alangium.
