What's the taxonomical classification of Cynanchum thesioides?

Cynanchum thesioides belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianaales. This plant is a representative of the family Apocynaceae, which is characterized by its specific floral and vegetative traits. Within this family, it is categorized under the genus Cynanchum, and its specific biological identity is defined by the species name thesioides.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cynanchum thesioides has slender, twining stems with opposite, ovate to elliptical leaves that possess smooth margins and acute tips. These blossoms are typically white or pale in hue and feature a complex structure typical of the Apocynaceae family.
