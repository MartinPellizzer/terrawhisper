Tragopogon graminifolius

Tragopogon graminifolius, commonly known as Salsify or Grass-leaved Goat's Beard, is a versatile perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its nutrient-dense roots, it possesses anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource, offering both nutritional benefits and therapeutic potential in herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Tragopogon graminifolius
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Tragopogon graminifolius, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asterales. Finally, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Tragopogon.
