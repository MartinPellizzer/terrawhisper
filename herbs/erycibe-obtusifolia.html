Erycibe obtusifolia

Erycibe obtusifolia, commonly known as the blunt-leaved erythribe, is a fascinating medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical properties, it has been utilized in traditional healing practices to address various ailments. Understanding its bioactive compounds offers promising insights into modern pharmacological developments and natural therapies.

  • Scientific name: Erycibe obtusifolia
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales, it is further categorized into the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Erycibe.
