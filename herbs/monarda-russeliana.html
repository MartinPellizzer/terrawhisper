Monarda russeolana, scientifically known as Monarda russeolana, is a resilient perennial native to North American prairies. Often called wild bergamot, this aromatic herb features striking lavender blooms. Renowned for its antimicrobial properties, it is traditionally used in herbal medicine to support respiratory health and soothe digestive discomfort.
