Euphorbia hirsuta

Euphorbia hirsuta, commonly known as hairy spurge, is a versatile medicinal herb found across various tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its unique bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia hirsuta
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout the Mediterranean regions of Southwestern Europe. Its distribution includes specific locations such as the Baleares and Corse islands. Additionally, the species can be found growing within the mainland territory of France. It also inhabits the coastal and inland areas of Portugal. Finally, the geographical range extends to the island of Sardegna.
