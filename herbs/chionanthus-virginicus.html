Chionanthus virginicus

American fringetree

Chionanthus virginicus, commonly known as White Fringe Tree, is a deciduous shrub prized for its fragrant, fringe-like blooms. While primarily ornamental, it holds significance in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its astringent properties, the plant is often studied for its potential therapeutic applications in treating various skin and digestive conditions.

  • Scientific name: Chionanthus virginicus
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Lamiales and the family Oleaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Chionanthus.
