Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Cissus quadrangularis, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its unique anatomical structure. The most distinctive feature is its succulent, quadrangular stems, which are fleshy and serve as the primary storage for nutrients. While the plant possesses small, elliptical leaves, it is the specialized stem structure that is most frequently utilized in therapeutic applications. Various extracts derived from the plant are used to promote bone healing and joint health, often specifically utilizing a concentrated stem extract to ensure high potency of its active compounds.
