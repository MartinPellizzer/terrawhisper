Cissus quadrangularis

Cissus quadrangularis, commonly known as Adamant Creeper or Veldt Grape, is a succulent climbing shrub renowned in Ayurvedic medicine. Highly valued for its bone-healing properties, it promotes osteoblast activity. Rich in antioxidants, this versatile plant is also widely utilized to support joint health and manage weight effectively.

  • Scientific name: Cissus quadrangularis
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, scientifically known as Cissus quadrangularis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Vitales and the family Vitaceae. Ultimately, it is classified under the genus Cissus.
