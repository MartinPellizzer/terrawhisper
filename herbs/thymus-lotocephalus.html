Thymus lotocephalus

Thymus lotocephalus, commonly known as the Lotus-headed Thyme, is a distinctive aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its unique, rounded flower clusters, this medicinal herb offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. It is traditionally valued in herbal medicine for supporting respiratory health and soothing digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Thymus lotocephalus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Lamiaceae. It is specifically categorized under the genus Thymus.

Common Names

This plant, Thymus lotocephalus, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the lotus-headed thyme, while in Spanish, it is known as tomillo de cabecita de loto. In Portuguese, the species is called tomilho-de-botão-de-loto, and in French, it is identified as thym à tête de lotus.
