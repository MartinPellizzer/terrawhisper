Bitter kola

Garcinia kola · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (213 studies) · ★★★★★

Bitter kola, scientifically known as Garcinia kola, is a potent medicinal plant native to West Africa. Renowned for its unique bitter flavor, it possesses powerful antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Traditionally used to treat respiratory issues and boost immunity, this versatile nut serves as a vital natural remedy.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Clusiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Garcinia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Its distribution encompasses several specific countries within this diverse ecological zone. Specifically, it can be located in the nation of Benin. The species also grows within the territories of Guinea, Liberia, and Ivory Coast. Finally, its presence is well-documented across the landscape of Nigeria.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as bitter kola, possesses several components that are highly valued for their therapeutic properties. The medicinal potency is primarily concentrated within the seeds, particularly the rich and nutritious seed kernels found inside. In many traditional practices, the seed is chewed directly to alleviate various ailments. Beyond the fruit, other parts of the plant are utilized for health and hygiene; for instance, the stem bark is often employed in herbal preparations, while the wooden chewing sticks derived from its branches serve as an effective natural tool for oral care and dental hygiene.
