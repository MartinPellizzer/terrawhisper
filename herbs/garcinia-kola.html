Bitter kola

Garcinia kola · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (213 studies) · ★★★★★

Bitter kola, scientifically known as Garcinia kola, is a potent medicinal plant native to West Africa. Renowned for its unique bitter flavor, it possesses powerful antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Traditionally used to treat respiratory issues and boost immunity, this versatile nut serves as a vital natural remedy.

Physiology

The physiology of Garcinia kola is characterized by a standard C3 photosynthetic pathway, meaning it utilizes the Calvin cycle to fix carbon dioxide through the enzyme Rubisco, a trait typical of most tropical woody perennials. This metabolic process is optimized for the shaded and humid environments where the species often thrives, although it lacks the specialized adaptations of C4 or CAM metabolism. Furthermore, the plant does not function as a nitrogen fixer, as it lacks the symbiotic relationship with diazotrophic bacteria, such as Rhizobium, required to convert atmospheric nitrogen into usable forms, and instead relies on the uptake of nitrates and ammonium from the soil through its root system.
