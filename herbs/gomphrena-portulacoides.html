Gomphrena portulacoides

Gomphrena portulacoides, commonly known as a medicinal herb within the Amaranthaceae family, is a plant valued for its traditional therapeutic properties. Often found in specific tropical regions, it is utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant ethnobotanical interest for its potential bioactive compounds and healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Gomphrena portulacoides
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gomphrena, specifically as the species Gomphrena portulacoides.

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across the South American continent. In Northern South America, its presence is documented within the borders of Venezuela. Its distribution extends into Western South America, where it is found in Colombia. Within Brazil, the species occupies a significant territory spanning the Northeast region.
