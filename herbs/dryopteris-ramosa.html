Dryopteris ramosa

Dryopteris ramosa, commonly known as the branched wood fern, is a resilient pteridophyte native to temperate forest ecosystems. Characterized by its unique branching fronds, this medicinal plant has been traditionally utilized in various cultures for its bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic benefits in treating inflammation and various skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Dryopteris ramosa
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polypodiaceae and falls within the genus Dryopteris.
