Thyrocarpus glochidiatus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its specific taxonomic ranks, the plant is part of the order Boraginales and falls within the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Thyrocarpus, with its specific designation being the species glochidiatus.
