Soft knotweed, scientifically known as Koenigia mollis, is a versatile medicinal herb often found in moist, disturbed soils. Renowned for its astringent and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat skin irritations, wounds, and digestive ailments, offering a natural approach to holistic healing and wellness.
