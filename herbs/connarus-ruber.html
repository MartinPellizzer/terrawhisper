Connarus ruber

Connarus ruber, commonly known as a medicinal shrub, is a significant plant species native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, it contains unique bioactive alkaloids. Research explores its potential pharmacological properties, particularly for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory activities, making it a subject of interest in modern drug discovery.

  • Scientific name: Connarus ruber
  • Family: Connaraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Oxalidales and the family Connaraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Connarus.
