Podanthus mitiqui

Podanthus mitiqui, commonly known as the "guaco" plant, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions of the Americas. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and various infections, serving as a vital natural therapeutic resource.

  • Scientific name: Podanthus mitiqui
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Podanthus.
