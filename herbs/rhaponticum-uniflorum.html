Uniflower swisscentaury, scientifically known as Rhaponticum uniflorum, is a rare perennial herb native to the high-altitude alpine meadows of Switzerland. Renowned for its striking single yellow flower, this botanical treasure is highly valued in traditional herbalism for its potential therapeutic properties and its unique role in mountain ecosystems.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions of Northern Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, its presence is noted in Buryatiya, Chita, and Irkutsk. Moving eastward, the species can also be found within the Russian Far East. Specifically, it inhabits the Amur region and extends into Khabarovsk. Together, these locations define its specific ecological range across these territories.
