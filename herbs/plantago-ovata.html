Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant has been used traditionally to treat inflammation, respiratory ailments, and skin irritations. Plant Parts

This plant, Plantago ovata, is primarily valued for its unique botanical components used in traditional and modern medicine. The most significant part utilized is the seed, which is harvested in large quantities to produce a powerful natural laxative. When these seeds are processed, the outer layer is often separated to create the seed husk, a substance rich in mucilage. In many therapeutic applications, the entire seeds/husks are consumed to provide dietary fiber, though many preparations focus specifically on the finely ground seed-husk to ensure ease of ingestion and optimal hydration in the digestive tract.
