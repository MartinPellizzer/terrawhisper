Plantago ovata

Plantago ovata, commonly known as broadleaf plantain, is a versatile medicinal herb cherished for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its high mucilage content, it is widely used to soothe digestive inflammation and support gut health. Its cooling, anti-inflammatory effects also make it excellent for treating skin irritations and wounds.

  • Scientific name: Plantago ovata
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Plantago ovata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Plantaginaceae and the genus Plantago.
