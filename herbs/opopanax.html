Opopanax

Panax

Opopanax, commonly known as oshrak or storax, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine for its aromatic, resinous properties. Belonging to the Apiaceae family, this plant offers potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits. Its fragrant gum resin has been utilized for centuries to support digestive health and respiratory wellness.

  • Scientific name: Opopanax
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified in the family Apiaceae and is placed in the genus Panax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range primarily concentrated within the Mediterranean and Balkan regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in France as well as across the island of Sardegna. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the coastal landscapes of Spain. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species extends its reach into the territory of Albania. Finally, its distribution continues further east into the country of Bulgaria.
