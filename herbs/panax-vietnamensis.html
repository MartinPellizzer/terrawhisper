Panax vietnamensis

Panax vietnamensis, commonly known as Vietnamese ginseng, is a rare and highly prized medicinal plant native to Vietnam. Renowned for its potent adaptogenic properties, this species contains unique saponins that support immune function, reduce fatigue, and enhance cognitive vitality, making it a cornerstone of traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Panax vietnamensis
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Panax vietnamensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the Araliaceae family and the genus Panax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central areas to the North-Central provinces. Its presence is also noted within the Southeast regions of China. Moving beyond China, the species inhabits the East Himalaya region of the Indian Subcontinent. Finally, it is native to the Indo-China region, specifically within Vietnam.
