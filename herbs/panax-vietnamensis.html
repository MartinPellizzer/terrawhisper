Vietnamese ginseng, scientifically known as Panax vietnamensis, is a rare and highly prized medicinal herb endemic to Vietnam. Renowned for its potent adaptogenic properties, it contains unique saponins that help combat fatigue, boost immunity, and enhance cognitive function, making it a cornerstone of traditional restorative wellness and longevity.
