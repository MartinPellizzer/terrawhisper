Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Solanaceae and is a member of the genus Solanum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the tropical regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the territories of Burundi, Congo, and Rwanda. Its presence is also well-documented within the expansive borders of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Beyond the central regions, the species extends its range into Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, this northernmost part of its distribution includes the nation of Djibouti.
