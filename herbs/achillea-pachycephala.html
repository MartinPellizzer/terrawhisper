Achillea pachycephala

Achillea pachycephala, commonly known as the thick-headed yarrow, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Renowned for its distinctive flower clusters, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies for its potent bioactive compounds and diverse therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Achillea pachycephala
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified as part of the genus Achillea.
