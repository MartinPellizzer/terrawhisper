Rattlebox, scientifically known as Crotalaria spectabilis, is a perennial legume native to the Americas. While recognized for its distinctive seed pods that rattle in the wind, it is primarily studied for its phytochemical properties. Although used in traditional medicine, it contains toxic alkaloids requiring careful handling and scientific scrutiny.
