Crotalaria spectabilis

Crotalaria spectabilis, commonly known as rattlebox, is a distinctive herbaceous plant featured by its bright yellow flowers and distinctive seed pods. While valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive properties, it requires careful handling due to its potential toxicity, making it a subject of significant pharmacological and botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Crotalaria spectabilis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is identified by the genus Crotalaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Mali. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found in both the Central African Republic and the DR Congo. The species also extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, spanning across Sudan and South Sudan. Finally, its geographical range reaches into East Tropical Africa, specifically within Kenya.
