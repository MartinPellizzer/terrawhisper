Parahellenia tonkinensis

Parahellenia tonkinensis, commonly known as a valuable medicinal plant, holds significant importance in traditional healing practices. Found in specific tropical regions, this species is prized for its unique bioactive compounds. Researchers are increasingly exploring its therapeutic potential to discover new treatments for various ailments and support modern pharmacological advancements.

  • Scientific name: Parahellenia tonkinensis
  • Family: Costaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Parahellenia tonkinensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Zingiberales and the family Costaceae. Its taxonomic classification concludes within the genus Parahellenia.
