Chinese Quince, scientifically known as Chaenomeles speciosa, is a deciduous plant highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its fruit and bark are used to treat respiratory ailments, reduce inflammation, and soothe digestive issues. This versatile medicinal plant remains a staple in holistic wellness practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Chaenomeles speciosa, utilizes various components for medicinal purposes. The therapeutic properties can be found in the woody twigs, which are often processed into medicinal slices for decoctions. During its blooming period, the plant produces delicate flowers, which eventually develop into its characteristic fruits. In many traditional preparations, these dried fruits are specifically harvested and used to extract their healing benefits.
