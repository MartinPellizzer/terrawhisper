Chaenomeles speciosa

Chaenomeles speciosa, commonly known as the Chinese rose or prickly rose, is a versatile medicinal plant deeply rooted in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antibacterial properties, it is widely utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin infections, and various inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Chaenomeles speciosa
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, it is further categorized into the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Chaenomeles.
