Utania racemosa

Utania racemosa, commonly known as White Spikenard, is a perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine for its diverse healing properties. Native to various tropical regions, this plant is rich in bioactive compounds, frequently utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments through its potent, natural therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Utania racemosa
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Utania.

Distribution

This plant is native to the expansive Indo-China region. Its presence can be documented across several distinct nations within this territory. Specifically, it is found growing in the landscapes of Cambodia and Laos. The species also has a recorded distribution throughout Myanmar and Thailand. Finally, its geographical reach extends into the country of Vietnam.
