Phyllanthus fraternus

Phyllanthus fraternus, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb, holds a vital place in traditional healing practices. Found in various tropical regions, this plant is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Research explores its potential bioactive compounds, offering promising insights into its role in supporting holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus fraternus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across both Atlantic islands and the African mainland. Within the Macaronesian region, its presence is documented in Cape Verde. Moving to West Tropical Africa, the species can be found in the nation of Benin. Its distribution also extends to the landlocked territory of Burkina. Finally, the plant is recorded in the West African countries of Gambia and Ghana.
