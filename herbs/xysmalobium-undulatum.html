Xysmalobium undulatum

Milk bush

Xysmalobium undulatum, commonly known as the Wavy-leafed Medic, is a remarkable botanical species prized for its therapeutic potential. Native to dense tropical regions, this plant contains unique bioactive compounds. Traditional healers have long utilized its distinct, undulating foliage to treat inflammatory conditions and promote overall holistic wellness through herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Xysmalobium undulatum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Xysmalobium and the species Xysmalobium undulatum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Ethiopia. Its presence extends into East Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. Furthermore, the species reaches into the southern reaches of the continent. Specifically, it is also located within the territory of Angola in South Tropical Africa.
