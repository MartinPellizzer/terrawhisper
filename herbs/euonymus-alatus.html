Plant Parts

This plant, Euonymus alatus, commonly known as the burning bush, possesses distinct morphological features in its various structures. The twigs are particularly notable for their striking, corky, wing-like ridges that run along the stem, giving the plant its unique silhouette. Additionally, the leaves are simple, oppositely arranged, and become brilliantly colored in shades of red and orange during the autumn season, providing significant ornamental value.
