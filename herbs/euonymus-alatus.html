Euonymus alatus

Euonymus alatus, commonly known as the Winged Spindle, is a distinctive evergreen shrub prized for its unique, winged stem morphology. Native to East Asia, this resilient plant features glossy, leathery leaves and ornamental berries. While primarily used in landscaping, its unique botanical characteristics make it a subject of interest.

  • Scientific name: Euonymus alatus
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Celastrales, and the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Euonymus.
