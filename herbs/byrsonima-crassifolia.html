Byrsonima crassifolia

Byrsonima crassifolia, commonly known as the "Guaco" plant, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions of the Americas. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and bronchodilator properties, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and various insect bites or stings.

  • Scientific name: Byrsonima crassifolia
  • Family: Malpighiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
