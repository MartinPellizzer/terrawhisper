Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Ladybell, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its medicinal roots. The tuberous, fleshy roots are the most significant part of the plant used for therapeutic purposes, often being harvested, dried, and processed to treat various respiratory ailments. Because the essential active compounds are concentrated within this underground structure, the roots serve as the central component in many herbal formulations.
