Adenophora

Adenophora, commonly known as ladybells or Chinese ladybells, is a diverse genus of flowering plants within the Campanulaceae family. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly in East Asia, various species are utilized for their therapeutic properties, offering potential benefits in treating respiratory ailments, inflammation, and promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Adenophora
  • Family: Campanulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the Campanulaceae family and is categorized under the genus Adenophora.
