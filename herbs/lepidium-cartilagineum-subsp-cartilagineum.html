Lepidium cartilagineum subsp. cartilagineum

Lepidium cartilagineum subsp. cartilagineum, commonly known as cartilaginous pepperweed, is a resilient perennial herb found in coastal habitats. While primarily recognized for its ecological role, it holds potential in ethnobotanical studies. This subspecies is valued for its unique chemical profile, offering intriguing possibilities for traditional and modern medicinal research.

  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe
