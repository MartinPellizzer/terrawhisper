Dirca occidentalis

Dirca occidentalis, commonly known as the "Tree of Life," is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the arid regions of East Africa. Renowned for its resilience, it provides vital nutrients and traditional remedies. Its bark and leaves are frequently utilized in local medicine to treat various ailments and infections.
