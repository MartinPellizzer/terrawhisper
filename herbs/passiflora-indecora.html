Passiflora indecora

Passiflora indecora, commonly known as the beautiful passionflower, is a captivating climbing vine native to tropical regions. Renowned for its intricate, exotic blooms, this botanical wonder is prized both for its ornamental elegance and its traditional medicinal properties, often utilized in herbal practices to support relaxation and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Passiflora indecora
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Passifloraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Passiflora.

Distribution

This plant is specifically known for its limited geographical range within the continent. It is found natively in the western regions of South America. More precisely, its distribution is centered within the borders of Ecuador. Because of this localized presence, it is considered an endemic species to this specific area.
