Capparis brassii

Capparis brassii, commonly known as the wild caper, is a resilient shrub native to arid regions of Asia and Africa. Renowned for its nutritional value and therapeutic potential, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive ailments, and various skin disorders through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Capparis brassii
  • Family: Capparaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales. Finally, it is a member of the family Capparaceae and falls under the genus Capparis.
