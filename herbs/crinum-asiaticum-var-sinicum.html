Crinum asiaticum var. sinicum

Crinum asiaticum var. sinicum, commonly known as the spider lily, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this variety is extensively studied for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and cytotoxic properties, offering promising potential in modern pharmacological research and various therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Crinum asiaticum var. sinicum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, falling into the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Crinum, specifically as the variety sinicum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the regions of Eastern Asia. It can be found naturally growing across the Nansei-shoto islands. Furthermore, its presence is well-documented throughout Taiwan. Within the mainland, the species is specifically located in Southeast China. These areas collectively define the primary natural range for this medicinal variety.
