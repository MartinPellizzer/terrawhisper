Chinese Crinum, scientifically known as Crinum asiaticum var. sinicum, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its large, strap-shaped leaves, this plant contains bioactive alkaloids used to treat inflammation, swelling, and various skin conditions. It remains a significant botanical resource in diverse Asian healing practices.
