Scarlet bee balm, Oswego tea, scientifically known as Monarda didymia, is a vibrant perennial celebrated for its striking tubular flowers. Native to North America, this aromatic herb belongs to the mint family. It is prized in herbal medicine for its antimicrobial properties and serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators.
