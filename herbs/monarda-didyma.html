Monarda didyma

Scarlet beebalm, oswego tea

Monarda didyma, commonly known as Bee Balm or Oswego Tea, is a vibrant perennial celebrated for its striking, tubular red flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal herb is prized for its aromatic properties, offering soothing benefits for respiratory ailments and digestive health through its potent, essential oil-rich leaves.

  • Scientific name: Monarda didyma
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Monarda didyma, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed by its placement in the genus Monarda.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Monarda didyma, is recognized by various regional names across different languages. In Chinese, it is referred to as 美国薄荷 (zh-hans) or 蜂香薄荷 (zh-hant), while in Welsh, it is called Bergamot (cy). It is also known as Goldmelisse (de) in German and pysznogłówka szkarłatna (pl) in Polish.
