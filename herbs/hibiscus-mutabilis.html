Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide range of distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence can be noted in Gambia. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, it is found in Cameroon as well as throughout the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its reach extends further south to South Tropical Africa, specifically within Zimbabwe. Finally, the species is also documented in the Northern Provinces of Southern Africa.
