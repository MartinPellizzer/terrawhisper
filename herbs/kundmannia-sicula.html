This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae and falls under the genus Kundmannia.

Distribution

This plant, Kundmannia sicula, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing within the Balearic Islands, known as Baleares. Its presence is also documented on the island of Corsica. Furthermore, the species is located in parts of France and along the coast of Portugal. Finally, its geographical range extends to the island of Sardinia, or Sardegna.
