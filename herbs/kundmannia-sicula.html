Kundmannia sicula

Kundmannia sicula, commonly known as a rare endemic species, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific Mediterranean habitats. Characterized by its unique floral structure, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Researchers study its phytochemical properties to explore potential therapeutic applications, emphasizing the importance of preserving its fragile natural ecosystem.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae and falls under the genus Kundmannia.
