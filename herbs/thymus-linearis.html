Thymus linearis

Thymus linearis, commonly known as linear thyme, is a delicate perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Distinguished by its slender, needle-like leaves, this aromatic plant is valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, offering a fragrant addition to both therapeutic remedies and culinary applications.

  • Scientific name: Thymus linearis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Thymus, which defines its specific taxonomic placement.
