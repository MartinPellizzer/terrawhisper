Didymochlaena truncatula

Didymochlaena truncatula, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional healing practices, possesses unique bioactive properties. This plant is highly valued for its therapeutic potential in treating various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and pharmacological benefits is essential for exploring its role in modern integrative medicine and herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Didymochlaena truncatula
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Polypodiinae and order Polypodiales, it is categorized under the family Polypodiaceae. Ultimately, the species is identified by the genus Didymochlaena and the specific name truncatula.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across the diverse tropical landscapes of the Malesia region. Within this expansive area, it can be found growing on the island of Borneo. Its range also extends to the Indonesian island of Jawa. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the territory of Sulawesi. Finally, its geographical presence includes the large island of Sumatera.
