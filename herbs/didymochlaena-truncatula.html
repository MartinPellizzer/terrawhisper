Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across the diverse tropical landscapes of the Malesia region. Within this expansive area, it can be found growing on the island of Borneo. Its range also extends to the Indonesian island of Jawa. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the territory of Sulawesi. Finally, its geographical presence includes the large island of Sumatera.
