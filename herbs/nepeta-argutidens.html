Nepeta argutidens

Nepeta argutidentata, commonly known as silver catmint, is a perennial herb celebrated for its striking silvery foliage and fragrant blooms. Beyond its popularity in ornamental gardens, this plant holds significant medicinal value, often utilized in traditional practices for its calming, antimicrobial, and soothing properties to support overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta argutidens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Nepeta.
