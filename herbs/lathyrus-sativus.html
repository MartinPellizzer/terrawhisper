Grass pea, scientifically known as Lathyrus sativus, is a resilient legume prized for its ability to thrive in harsh, drought-prone environments. While historically used as a vital food security crop, it requires careful management due to potential neurotoxicity. This versatile plant remains a crucial subject in nutritional science.
