Lathyrus sativus

Grass pea

Lathyrus sativus, commonly known as the grass pea or chickling vetch, is a resilient legume valued for its ability to thrive in poor soils. Beyond its role in food security, this plant possesses significant medicinal potential, traditionally used to address nutritional deficiencies and various ailments across diverse cultures.

  • Scientific name: Lathyrus sativus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Lathyrus.
