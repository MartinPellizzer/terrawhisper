Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as bladdernut, is utilized in herbal practices primarily through its aerial parts. These above-ground components include the leafy green foliage, the woody stems, and the distinctive, papery bladder-like fruit capsules that give the species its name. When preparing medicinal extracts or infusions, these aerial parts are harvested to capture the various bioactive compounds contained within the leaves and reproductive structures.
