Viburnum ternatum

Viburnum ternatum, commonly known as blackhaw, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to eastern North America. Valued in traditional medicine, its bark and berries are utilized for their antioxidant properties and ability to support digestive health. This resilient plant thrives in woodland understories, offering both ecological and therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Viburnum ternatum
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Dipsacales and the family Viburnaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Viburnum.
