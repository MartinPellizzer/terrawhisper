Polemonium caeruleum

Jacob's-ladder

Polemonium caeruleum, commonly known as Jacob's Polemonium or Blue Jacob's Ladder, is a perennial wildflower native to North America. Renowned for its striking, bell-shaped blue blossoms, this plant thrives in moist, woodland settings. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds historical significance in traditional herbal practices for various medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Polemonium caeruleum
  • Family: Polemoniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Polemonium caeruleum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ericales and the family Polemoniaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Polemonium.
