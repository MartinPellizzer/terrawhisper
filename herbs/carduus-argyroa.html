Carduus argyroa

Carduus argyroa, commonly known as the Silver Thistle, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Characterized by its silvery, bract-covered flower heads, this plant is studied for its antioxidant properties and its traditional use in supporting digestive health and liver function in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Carduus argyroa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, placing it in the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Carduus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Carduus argyroa, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently called the silver thistle, while in Spanish, it is known as cardo plateado. In Portuguese, it is referred to as cardo-de-prata, and in French, it is called chardon argenté.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several Mediterranean regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is noted in France and the island of Sardegna. Its range extends into Southeastern Europe, where it can be found in Italy and Sicilia. Additionally, the species reaches into Northern Africa, specifically within Algeria. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in different coastal and continental environments.
