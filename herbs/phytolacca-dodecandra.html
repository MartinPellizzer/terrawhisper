African Soapberry, scientifically known as Phytolacca dodecandra, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Africa. Renowned for its saponin-rich roots, it serves both as a natural cleansing agent and a potent remedy. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments and respiratory issues, it remains a vital component of ethnomedicine.
