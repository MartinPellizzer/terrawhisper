Marrubium astracanicum

Marrubium astracanicum, commonly known as Astragan horehound, is a perennial medicinal herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat respiratory ailments, act as an expectorant, and soothe coughs, making it a valuable botanical resource in traditional phytotherapy.

  • Scientific name: Marrubium astracanicum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
