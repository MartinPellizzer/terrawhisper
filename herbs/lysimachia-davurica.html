Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the vast territory of Siberia. In the eastern reaches of the continent, it can be found growing in the areas of Buryatiya and Irkutsk. Its presence also extends into the remote and northern landscapes of Yakutiya. Furthermore, the species is documented in the southern Siberian regions of Tuva and Chita. Together, these locations define the specific geographical range where this medicinal species thrives.
