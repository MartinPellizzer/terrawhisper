Crataegus azarolus

Azarole

Crataegus azarolus, commonly known as the Azarole, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its small, fleshy fruits and thorny branches, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and cardioprotective therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus azarolus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Crataegus as the species Crataegus azarolus.
