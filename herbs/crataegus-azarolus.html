Azarole, scientifically known as Crataegus azarolus, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its small, edible fruits and striking blossoms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and cardioprotective properties across various cultures.
