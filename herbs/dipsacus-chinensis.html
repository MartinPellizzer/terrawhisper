Dipsacus chinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Dipsacus chinensis?

Dipsacus chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Dipsacales and is a member of the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized within the genus Dipsacus, with its specific identification being the species chinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dipsacus chinensis has an erect, herbaceous stem that typically reaches heights of 30 to 70 centimeters and features opposite, lanceolate leaves with serrated margins. The foliage is characterized by a distinct texture, often appearing slightly hairy or pubescent on both surfaces. Its inflorescence consists of dense, terminal heads composed of numerous small, tubular flowers arranged in a capitate manner. These flowers are usually white or pale pink and are supported by an involucre of bracts that protect the developing seeds. The plant eventually produces small, dry achenes that facilitate its dispersal in its native habitats.
