Paris bashanensis

Paris bashanenensis, commonly known as the Bashan Paris, is a rare perennial herb native to the shaded forest floors of China. Renowned for its distinctive dark, bell-shaped flowers, this botanical species is highly valued in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties and potential therapeutic applications in holistic healing.

  • Family: Melanthiaceae
