Madeira-vine, scientifically known as Anredera cordifolia, is a vigorous, perennial climbing plant native to South America. Renowned for its heart-shaped leaves and tuberous roots, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine. These nutrient-rich tubers offer various therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and immune-boosting properties for holistic wellness.
