Anredera cordifolia

Anredera cordifolia, commonly known as Madeira vine or Mallow vine, is a vigorous climbing perennial native to South America. Renowned in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, this resilient plant is frequently utilized to support respiratory health, boost immunity, and provide significant antioxidant benefits in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Anredera cordifolia
  • Family: Basellaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Basellaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Anredera.
