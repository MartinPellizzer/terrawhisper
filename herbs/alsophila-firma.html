Alsophila firma

Alsophila firma, commonly known as the firm-leafed plant, is a remarkable medicinal species valued for its potent therapeutic properties. Deeply rooted in traditional healing practices, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and promote overall wellness. This botanical treasure offers a unique blend of bioactive compounds for holistic health.

  • Scientific name: Alsophila firma
  • Family: Cyatheaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Alsophila firma, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae, it is categorized under the order Cyatheales. It is further classified under the family Cyatheaceae and falls within the genus Alsophila.

Common Names

This plant, Achillea millefolium, is known by a variety of common names across different languages and regions. In English, it is most widely recognized as yarrow, though it is also frequently called milfoil or soldier's woundwort. In German, the plant is called achillea, and in French, it is referred to as achillée millefeuille. Moving to Spanish, it is commonly known as milenrama, while in Italian, the name used is millefoglie. In Portuguese, it is called mil-folhas, and in Dutch, it is known as duizendblad.
