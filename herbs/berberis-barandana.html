Berberis barandana

Berberis barandana, commonly known as the Himalayan Barberry, is a resilient shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often utilized to treat digestive ailments and skin disorders, this medicinal plant remains a vital botanical resource.
