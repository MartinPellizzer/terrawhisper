Lathyrus palustris subsp. palustris

Lathyrus palustris subsp. palustris, commonly known as the marsh pea, is a specialized perennial herb found in wetland habitats. While primarily valued for its ecological role in nitrogen fixation, this subspecies holds historical significance in traditional herbalism, often studied for its unique biochemical properties and potential therapeutic applications.
