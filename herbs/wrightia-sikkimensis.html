What's the taxonomical classification of Wrightia sikkimensis?

Wrightia sikkimensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic descent through the order Gentianales, the plant is a member of the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Wrightia, specifically identifying as the species sikkimensis.
