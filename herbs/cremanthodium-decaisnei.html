Cremanthodium decaisnei

What's the taxonomical classification of Cremanthodium decaisnei?

Cremanthodium decaisnei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, falling under the genus Cremanthodium, and is specifically identified by the species name decaisnei.

How is this plant cultivated?
