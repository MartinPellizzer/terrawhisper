Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in both Algeria and Egypt. Its range extends into the Macaronesian islands, specifically within Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species is present in West Tropical Africa, occurring in countries such as Benin. Finally, the distribution continues inland to include the territory of Burkina.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the cluster fig, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, most notably its leaves and fruits. The leaves are frequently employed in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues due to their bioactive compounds. Similarly, the fruits serve as a significant part of the plant's therapeutic profile, often used to aid digestion and provide essential nutrients.
