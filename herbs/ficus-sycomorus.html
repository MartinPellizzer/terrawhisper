Ficus sycomorus

Sycamore fig

Ficus sycomorus, commonly known as the Sycamore Fig, is a majestic evergreen tree native to Africa and the Middle East. Renowned for its longevity, this versatile plant offers significant medicinal value, with various parts used traditionally to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations and respiratory distress.

  • Scientific name: Ficus sycomorus
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Moraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ficus.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in both Algeria and Egypt. Its range extends into the Macaronesian islands, specifically within Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species is present in West Tropical Africa, occurring in countries such as Benin. Finally, the distribution continues inland to include the territory of Burkina.
