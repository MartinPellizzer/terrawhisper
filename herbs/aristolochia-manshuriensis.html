, scientifically known as Aristolochia manshuriensis, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to East Asia. Traditionally utilized in herbal medicine, it features distinctive heart-shaped leaves and tubular flowers. While historically valued for various therapeutic properties, modern research cautions against its use due to the presence of nephrotoxic aristolochic acids.
