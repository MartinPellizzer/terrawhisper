Aristolochia manshuriensis

Aristolochia manshuriensis, commonly known as Manchurian birthwort, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to East Asia. Historically utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments, it contains bioactive compounds. However, caution is essential, as many species in this genus contain nephrotoxic and carcinogenic aristolochic acids.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia manshuriensis
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, it is further organized into the family Aristolochiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aristolochia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and varied geographical distribution across several key Asian regions. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye territory. Moving into China, the species is widely spread throughout the Manchuria region. Its range further extends into the central parts of the country, covering both North-Central and South-Central China. Additionally, the plant can be found inhabiting the diverse landscapes of Southeast China.
