Lemongrass, scientifically known as Cymbopogon, is a versatile, aromatic perennial grass cherished globally for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its refreshing citrus scent, it serves as a powerhouse in traditional medicine. Used to alleviate digestive issues, reduce inflammation, and combat infections, this plant is essential in wellness and aromatherapy.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Cymbopogon, offers various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The whole grass is often harvested to be processed into different forms, including the use of young sprouts in certain applications. Most notably, the plant is highly valued for its essential oils, which are extracted to provide a potent aroma and medicinal benefits. Whether used in its raw state or distilled to obtain pure essential oil, this botanical resource remains a staple in aromatherapy and natural healing.
