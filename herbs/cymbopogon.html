Cymbopogon

Cymbopogon, commonly known as lemongrass, is a versatile aromatic grass cherished in traditional medicine and culinary arts. Renowned for its refreshing citrus scent, it possesses potent antimicrobial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties. This essential plant serves as a natural remedy for digestive issues, fever, and skin infections worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Cymbopogon
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Poales, it is further categorized under the family Poaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus name, Cymbopogon.
