Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions. In the Malesia zone, it can be found throughout the Lesser Sunda Islands. Its presence extends significantly into Western South America, specifically within Peru, Bolivia, and the Galápagos. Additionally, the species is documented in the southern reaches of Brazil.
