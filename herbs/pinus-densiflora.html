Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across various regions of Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. Its range extends significantly into China, where it can be found in both Manchuria and the North-Central areas. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed across the Korean Peninsula.
