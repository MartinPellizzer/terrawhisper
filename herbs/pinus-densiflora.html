Pinus funebris, scientifically known as Pinus densiflora, is a majestic evergreen conifer native to East Asia. Renowned for its striking reddish bark and elegant form, this species holds significant medicinal value. Its needles and resin are traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to support respiratory health and promote wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling into the family Pinaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Pinus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across various regions of Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. Its range extends significantly into China, where it can be found in both Manchuria and the North-Central areas. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed across the Korean Peninsula. Finally, it is a common feature of the natural landscape throughout Japan.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Japanese red pine, utilizes its plant sap as a vital component in various traditional medicinal applications. The resinous sap, which flows from the bark and branches, is harvested for its potent aromatic and antiseptic properties. In many therapeutic practices, this sap is processed to create ointments or extracts used to treat skin conditions, promote wound healing, and alleviate inflammation.
