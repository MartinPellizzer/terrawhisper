Euphorbia garuana

Euphorbia garuana, commonly known as the Garuana spurge, is a resilient medicinal plant native to East Africa. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Scientific interest continues to grow as researchers explore its potent bioactive compounds for modern medicine.
