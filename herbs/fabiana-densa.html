Fabiana densa

Fabiana densa, commonly known as Fabiana or cushion bush, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Andean highlands. Renowned for its dense, fine-textured foliage, this plant thrives in high-altitude environments. In traditional medicine, various parts are utilized for their therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Fabiana densa
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Fabiana.
