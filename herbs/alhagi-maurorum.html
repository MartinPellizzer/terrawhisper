Persian manna

Persian manna, scientifically known as Alhagi maurorum, is a resilient desert shrub valued for its medicinal properties. Traditionally used in various cultures, it produces a sweet, resinous exudate utilized to treat respiratory issues, digestive ailments, and inflammation. This plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical and pharmacological research today.
