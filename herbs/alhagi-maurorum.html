Alhagi maurorum

Alhagi maurorum, commonly known as camelthorn, is a resilient perennial shrub found across arid regions of Asia and Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. It is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Alhagi maurorum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the Fabaceae family and falls under the genus Alhagi.
