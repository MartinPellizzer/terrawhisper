What's the taxonomical classification of Selaginella sinensis?

Selaginella sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Lycopodiidae. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Selaginellales and the family Selaginellaceae. Ultimately, the plant is identified by the genus Selaginella and is specifically designated by the species name sinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Selagineella sinensis has a creeping rhizomatous stem structure with small, scale-like leaves arranged in four rows along the branches. The leaves are microphyllous, meaning they are small and possess a single, unbranched vascular vein. These leaves are typically bright green and exhibit a flattened, overlapping pattern that covers the stem closely. The plant produces small, cone-like structures called strobili at the tips of the branches for reproduction. These strobili contain the sporangia where spores are produced and released.

What medicinal compounds this plant contains?

This plant contains various bioactive medicinal compounds including flavonoids, amentoflavone, and several types of phenolic acids. These phytochemicals contribute to the plant's significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Amentoflavone specifically serves as a major constituent that assists in protecting cells from oxidative stress. Researchers continue to study these specific molecules to understand their therapeutic potential in modern medicine.

According to a study published by "Biological & pharmaceutical bulletin", amentoflavone and three other flavonoids were isolated from the ethanol extract of Selaginella sinensis. Amentoflavone from this plant showed potent antiviral activity against respiratory syncytial virus with an IC50 of 5.5 microg/ml. The contents of amentoflavone in nine species of Selaginella were determined by reversed-phase HPLC. Selaginella sinensis showed a higher content of 1.13%.
