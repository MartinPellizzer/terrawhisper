Limonium algarvense

Limonium algarvense, commonly known as the Algarve sea lavender, is a rare, endemic coastal plant found along the southern shores of Portugal. While primarily valued for its ecological significance, it represents the unique biodiversity of saline habitats, offering potential pharmacological interest through its specialized secondary metabolites and medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Limonium algarvense
  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Plumbaginaceae and specifically assigned to the genus Limonium.
