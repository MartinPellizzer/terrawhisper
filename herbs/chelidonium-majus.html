Greater celandine

Chelidonium majus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (545 studies) · ★★★★★

Greater celandine, scientifically known as Chelidonium majus, is a perennial herbaceous plant renowned for its bright yellow, latex-rich sap. Traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat skin conditions and liver ailments, it contains potent alkaloids. However, its high toxicity requires cautious application and expert guidance during medicinal use.
