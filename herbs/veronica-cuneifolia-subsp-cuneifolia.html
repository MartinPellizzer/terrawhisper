Veronica cuneifolia subsp. cuneifolia

Veronica cuneifolia subsp. cuneliifolia, commonly known as wedge-leaved speedwell, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Renowned for its unique wedge-shaped leaves and delicate blooms, this subspecies holds significant botanical interest. Its traditional medicinal applications focus on its bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in herbalism.

  • Family: Plantaginaceae
