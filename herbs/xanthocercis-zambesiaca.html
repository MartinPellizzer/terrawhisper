Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the regions of South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Malawi and Mozambique. Its presence also extends into the territories of Zambia and Zimbabwe. Additionally, the species inhabits parts of Southern Africa, particularly in Botswana. These diverse locations highlight the plant's wide range across the southern portion of the continent.
