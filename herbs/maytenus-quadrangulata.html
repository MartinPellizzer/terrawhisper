Maytenus quadrangulata

Maytenus quadrangulata, commonly known as Celastrus or Celastrus quadrangulata, is a versatile medicinal shrub highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently used to treat digestive issues, inflammation, and skin ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Maytenus quadrangulata
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Maytenus.
