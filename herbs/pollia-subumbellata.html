Pollia subumbellata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized into the order Commelinales and the family Commelineaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed within the genus Pollia, with the specific designation of the species subumbellata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

These small flowers are often tucked within the foliage, eventually developing into striking, metallic-colored berries.
