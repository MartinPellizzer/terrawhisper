Sea Daffodil, scientifically known as Pancratium maritimum, is a striking perennial bulb found along Mediterranean coastlines. Renowned for its fragrant, white blooms, this resilient plant thrives in sandy habitats. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, it holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering diverse medicinal properties used in traditional healing practices worldwide.
