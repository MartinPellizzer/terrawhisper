Impatiens parviflora

Impatiens parviflora, commonly known as small-flowered touch-me-not, is a delicate annual herb native to Eurasia. Recognized by its petite, pale yellow blossoms, this plant thrives in shaded, moist environments. While often considered invasive, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest for its traditional medicinal properties in treating various skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Impatiens parviflora
  • Family: Balsaminaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is a member of the family Balsaminaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Impatiens.
