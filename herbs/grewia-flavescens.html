Donkey Berry, scientifically known as Grewia flavescens, is a resilient, deciduous shrub native to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Highly valued for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and digestive issues, offering significant ethnobotanical importance in desert ecosystems.
