Capsicum baccatum var. baccatum

Capsicum baccatum var. baccatum, commonly known as the ornamental chili, is a versatile plant valued for its striking aesthetic and medicinal properties. Rich in capsaicin, it offers potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits. Often used in traditional remedies, this variety serves as a powerful natural tool for managing pain and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Capsicum baccatum var. baccatum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa
