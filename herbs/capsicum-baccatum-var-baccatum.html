Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct tropical regions. In the Macaronesian islands, its presence is noted specifically within Cape Verde. Moving to West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found spanning the Gulf of Guinea Islands, the Congo, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Additionally, the species extends its range into Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically in Chad. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive across various African and Atlantic island environments.
