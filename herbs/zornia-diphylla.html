Zornia diphylla

Zornia diphylla, commonly known as the twinleaf, is a unique medicinal herb native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive foliage, this plant offers diverse therapeutic properties. Traditionally utilized in various healing practices, it serves as a vital botanical resource for addressing various ailments through its natural compounds.

  • Family: Fabaceae
