Uvaria boniana

Uvaria boniana, commonly known as a species within the Annonaceae family, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic potential. Native to tropical regions, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant pharmacological interest for its antioxidant and antimicrobial properties in herbal studies.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
