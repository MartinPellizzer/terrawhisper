Adenia

Adenia, commonly known as a genus of succulent climbing shrubs, belongs to the Passifloraceae family. Found across tropical Africa and Asia, these resilient plants are valued for their unique tuberous roots. Many species possess significant medicinal properties, offering therapeutic potential in traditional healing practices for various diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Adenia
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Passifloraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Adenia.
