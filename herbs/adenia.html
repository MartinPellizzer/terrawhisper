Elephantfoots

Adenia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (21 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Elephantfoot, scientifically known as Adenia, is a fascinating genus of succulent climbing plants found across tropical Africa and Asia. Renowned for their massive, tuberous caudexes used for water storage, these plants offer significant ethnobotanical value, providing various traditional medicinal applications for treating diverse ailments in diverse cultures.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Passifloraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Adenia.

Distribution

This plant is geographically distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing in the country of Benin. Additionally, its presence extends to the nations of Burkina and Ghana. The distribution also includes the territory of Guinea-Bissau. Finally, this medicinal species is also documented in Guinea.
