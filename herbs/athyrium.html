Athyrium

Athyrium, commonly known as lady ferns, belongs to a diverse genus of ferns prized for their delicate, feathery fronds. While primarily celebrated in ornamental gardening for their lush texture, certain species hold traditional significance in folk medicine, potentially offering soothing properties for skin ailments and various inflammatory conditions.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae, it is categorized under the order Polypodiales. Finally, it is classified under the family Aspleniaceae and the genus Athyrium.
