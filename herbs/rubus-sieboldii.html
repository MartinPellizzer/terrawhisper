Rubus sieboldii

Rubus sieboldii, commonly known as Siebold's raspberry, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asian forests. Valued in traditional medicine, its berries and foliage possess antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant is increasingly studied for its potential bioactive compounds and its role in supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Rubus sieboldii
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is further categorized under the genus Rubus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the regions of Eastern Asia. Within this part of the world, it can be found growing in Japan.
