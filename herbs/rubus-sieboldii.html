Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the regions of Eastern Asia. Within this part of the world, it can be found growing in Japan. Its range also extends to the Nansei-shoto islands located in the southern reaches of the archipelago. Additionally, the species has established a presence in the North-Central Pacific. Specifically, this geographical reach includes the islands of Hawaii.
