Chinese trumpet-creeper, scientifically known as Campsis grandiflora, is a vigorous, deciduous woody vine native to China. Renowned for its brilliant, trumpet-shaped orange flowers, this ornamental climber is also valued in traditional medicine. It is frequently used to treat various ailments, including inflammation, fever, and certain skin conditions.
