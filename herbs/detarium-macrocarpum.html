Detarium macrocarpum

Detarium macrocarpum, commonly known as the wild mango, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections, offering promising therapeutic potential.
