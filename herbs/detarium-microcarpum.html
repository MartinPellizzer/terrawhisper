Detarium microcarpum

Detarium microcarpum, commonly known as the African detarium, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Valued in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions. This botanical resource offers promising potential for pharmacological research and therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Detarium microcarpum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Detarium, with its species name being microcarpum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Detarium microcarpum, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Arabic, it is called كهبل صغير الثمار, while in Hausa, it is known as Taura. In Bambara, the species is called Ntamajalan, and in French, it is commonly referred to as Petit Detar.
