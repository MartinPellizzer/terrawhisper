Distribution

This plant is native to the vast tropical region known as Malesia. Within this broad area, its presence is specifically noted on the island of Borneo. Additionally, its geographical range extends to the island of Jawa. These locations represent the primary natural habitats where the species can be found growing. Consequently, its distribution is closely tied to the unique ecosystems of these Southeast Asian islands.
