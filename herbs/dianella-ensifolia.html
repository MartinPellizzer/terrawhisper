This plant, known scientifically as Dianella ensifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asparagales and the family Asphodelaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Dianella.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions in the Southern Hemisphere. Within South Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the countries of Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Its range also extends significantly into the Western Indian Ocean. In this maritime region, the species is documented on the island of Madagascar.
