Dianella ensifolia

Dianella ensifolia, commonly known as the sword-leafed Dianella, is a distinctive climbing shrub native to the rainforests of eastern Australia. Characterized by its narrow, lance-shaped foliage, this resilient plant plays a vital role in forest ecosystems, offering essential habitat and ecological connections within its lush, subtropical woodland environment.

  • Scientific name: Dianella ensifolia
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Dianella ensifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asparagales and the family Asphodelaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Dianella.
