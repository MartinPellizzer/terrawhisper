Phellodendron chinense var. glabriusculum

Phellodendron chinense glabriusculum

Phello-dendron chinense var. glabriusculum, commonly known as the "Chinese Bamboo," is a significant medicinal plant in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this variety is utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering a natural approach to managing various ailments within integrative wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Phellodendron chinense var. glabriusculum
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Phello dendron chinense var. glabriusculum, is recognized by various names across different linguistic contexts. In Chinese, it is referred to as 秃叶黄檗 (tūyè huángbò), with the specific regional variations being 秃叶黄檗 in simplified Chinese (zh-cn) and 秃葉黃檗 in traditional Chinese (zh-tw). In English, it is commonly identified by its botanical designation, Phello dendron chinense glabriusculum. Additionally, the term 黄檗皮 (huángbòpí) is frequently used in Chinese to refer to the medicinal bark derived from this species.
