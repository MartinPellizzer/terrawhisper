Camphora

Camphora, commonly known as camphor, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its aromatic properties. Derived from the wood of various trees, it serves as a potent remedy for respiratory issues, skin irritations, and muscle aches. Its cooling sensation and antiseptic qualities make it a staple in traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Camphora
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Ultimately, this medicinal species is identified by the genus Cinnamomum (often associated with the genus Camphora in botanical contexts).
