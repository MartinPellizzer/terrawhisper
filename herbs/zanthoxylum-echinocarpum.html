What's the taxonomical classification of Zanthoxylum echinocarpum?

Zanthoxylum echinocarpum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales. The plant is further categorized into the family Rutaceae, which contains the genus Zanthoxylum, and is specifically identified by the species name echinocarpum.
