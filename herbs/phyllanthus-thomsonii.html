Phyllanthus thomsonii

Phyllanthus thomsonii, commonly known as a unique member of the Phyllanthaceae family, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic potential. Found in tropical regions, it is studied for its bioactive compounds. Traditional healers often utilize its extracts to address various ailments, highlighting its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus thomsonii
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is situated in the family Phyllanthaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant, known as Phyllanthus thomsonii, exhibits a specific distribution across parts of Asia. In China, its presence is noted within the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam. Additionally, the species extends its range into the neighboring territory of Bangladesh.
