Erythronium sibiricum

Erythroium sibiricum, commonly known as Siberian Erythroium, is a resilient perennial herb native to the harsh Eurasian steppes. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, this plant possesses unique bioactive compounds. It is studied for its potential therapeutic properties, particularly in supporting systemic health and addressing various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Erythronium sibiricum
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Liliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Liliaceae and is situated within the genus Erythronium.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast and diverse landscapes of Siberia. Within this massive region, it can be found growing in the Altay mountains and throughout the Krasnoyarsk territories. Its presence extends further into the Tuva region and across the expansive plains of West Siberia. Beyond the borders of Russia, the species also populates parts of Central Asia. Specifically, its geographical range includes the territory of Kazakhstan.
