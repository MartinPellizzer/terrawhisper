Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Pachira aquatica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae and the genus Pachira.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across multiple tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Guinea and Senegal. Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the territories of Cameroon and Congo. Furthermore, the species reaches the Western Indian Ocean, where it is located in the Comoros. These various locations highlight its ability to thrive in distinct tropical environments.
