Scorodophloeus zenkeri

Scorodophloeus zenkeri, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in West and Central Africa, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Utilized extensively in traditional medicine, its bark and leaves are processed to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues, offering a natural resource for pharmacological research and healing.
