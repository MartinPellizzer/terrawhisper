Leonia blakeana

Leonia blakeana, commonly known as the "Silver Leaf," is a rare medicinal shrub endemic to the limestone cliffs of Jamaica. Valued for its unique therapeutic properties, this botanical treasure is often studied for its potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, offering significant insights into traditional Caribbean herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Leonia blakeana
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Leonia blakeana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Violaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Leonia.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Leonia blakeana, possesses a very specific and limited geographical range. Its natural habitat is strictly confined to the region of Central America. Specifically, this medicinal species is found only within the country of Panamá. Because its distribution is so localized, it is considered an endemic plant to this particular area. Understanding this restricted presence is crucial for the conservation of the species in its native environment.
