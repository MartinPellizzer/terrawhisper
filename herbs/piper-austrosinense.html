What's the taxonomical classification of Piper austrosinense?

Piper austrosinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Piperales and belongs to the family Piperaceae. Finally, it is identified within the genus Piper, with its specific designation being the species austrosinense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Piper austrosinense has woody stems with opposite or subopposite leaves that are characterized by an elliptical to ovate shape, an acute apex, and an entire margin. The leaf blades are typically arranged in a way that reflects their decussate pattern, often featuring prominent pinnate venation. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in dense, spike-like inflorescences that emerge from the leaf axils. These spikes are often greenish or yellowish in color and contain numerous tiny, individual florets. The plant also develops small, fleshy drupes as fruits following successful pollination.
