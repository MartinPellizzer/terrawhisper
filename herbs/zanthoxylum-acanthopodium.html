Andaliman, scientifically known as Zanthoxylum acanthopodium, is a unique medicinal plant native to Indonesia. Renowned for its intense, citrusy, and numbing flavor, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments and inflammation. This aromatic berry serves as both a culinary staple and a potent healing agent.
