Juniperus sabina

Juniperus sabina, commonly known as Savin juniper, is a coniferous evergreen shrub native to Europe and parts of Asia. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant contains potent essential oils. While historically used in traditional medicine, its high toxicity requires extreme caution during any therapeutic or practical application.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus sabina
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling into the family Cupressaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Juniperus as Juniperus sabina.
