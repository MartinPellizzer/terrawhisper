Hedera nepalensis

Hedera nepellenis, commonly known as the Himalayan ivy, is a resilient climbing shrub native to the moist forests of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and stems are utilized for their bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic benefits in treating respiratory ailments and various skin-related inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Hedera nepalensis
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Within the family Araliaceae, it is identified by the genus Hedera, specifically as the species Hedera nepalaensis.
