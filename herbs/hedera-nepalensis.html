Himalayan ivy, scientifically known as Hedera nepellenis, is a versatile evergreen climber native to the high altitudes of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and inflammation through its potent bioactive compounds.
