Himalayan ivy, scientifically known as Hedera nepellenis, is a versatile evergreen climber native to the high altitudes of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and inflammation through its potent bioactive compounds.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Within the family Araliaceae, it is identified by the genus Hedera, specifically as the species Hedera nepalaensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions in South and Central Asia. Within Western Asia, its presence is documented in the country of Afghanistan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam. The species also thrives throughout the East Himalaya and across the nation of Nepal. Finally, its geographical range extends further into the Indian Subcontinent to include Pakistan.
