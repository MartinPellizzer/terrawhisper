What's the taxonomical classification of Ligustrum henryi?

Ligustrum henryi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is a member of the order Lamiales and falls under the family Oleaceae. Within this family, it is categorized under the genus Ligustrum, specifically identified by the species name henryi.
