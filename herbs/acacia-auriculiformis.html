Acacia auriculiformis

Earleaf acacia

Acacia auriculiformis, commonly known as Earleaf Acacia, is a versatile nitrogen-fixing tree valued for its rapid growth and medicinal potential. Traditionally used in various cultures, it possesses bioactive compounds with antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. This resilient species plays a vital role in ecological restoration and herbal pharmacology worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Acacia auriculiformis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it falls under the family Fabaceae. Its specific scientific designation is found within the genus Acacia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the countries of Benin, Guinea, and Nigeria. Its presence extends further into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Additionally, the species is documented in the East Tropical Africa region, particularly in Tanzania. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse ecological range of Acacia auriculiformis across the tropics.
