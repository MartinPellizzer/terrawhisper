Veratrum lobelianum

Veratrum lobeleanum, commonly known as Lobelia Veratrum, is a potent medicinal herb native to various mountainous regions. Renowned for its complex alkaloids, this plant has historically been utilized in traditional medicine for its powerful physiological effects. However, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling and precise dosage in pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Veratrum lobelianum
  • Family: Melanthiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Liliales, it is further categorized under the family Melanthiaceae. Its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement in the genus Veratrum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented in both Finland and Norway. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species can also be found within the borders of Germany. Furthermore, it inhabits the territories of Austria and the Czechia-Slovakia region. These locations collectively define the known range for Veratrum lobelianum.
