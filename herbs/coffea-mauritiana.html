Coffea mauritiana

Coffea mauritiana, commonly known as the Mauritanian coffee, is a significant medicinal plant native to arid African regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and berries are utilized for their antioxidant properties and therapeutic benefits. This resilient species offers unique phytochemical compounds essential for studying natural pharmacological treatments.

  • Scientific name: Coffea mauritiana
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Coffea as the species Coffea mauritiana.

Distribution

This plant, known as Coffea mauritiana, exhibits a specific distribution across the Western Indian Ocean. It can be found naturally growing on the island of Mauritius. Additionally, its range extends to the large island of Madagascar. The species is also documented to inhabit the territory of Réunion. These locations represent the primary geographical areas where this medicinal plant is located.
