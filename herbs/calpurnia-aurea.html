Calpurnia aurea

Calpurnia aurea, commonly known as the Golden Calpurnia, is a striking perennial herb native to the moist, shaded forests of North America. Distinguished by its brilliant yellow blooms, this rare species plays a vital role in its ecosystem, often signaling healthy, undisturbed woodland habitats and supporting specialized pollinator species.
