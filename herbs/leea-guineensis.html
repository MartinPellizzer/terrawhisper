Leea guineensis

Leea guineensis, commonly known as the Guinea Leea, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical African regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Leea guineensis
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Leea guineensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Vitales and the family Vitaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Leea.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the region of West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing naturally in the territory of Benin.
