False jessemine, scientifically known as Gelsemium sempervirens, is a climbing evergreen vine native to East Asia. Renowned for its fragrant, star-shaped white flowers, it holds significant place in traditional medicine. While containing potent alkaloids used for neurological research, its extreme toxicity requires cautious handling and precise pharmacological application.
