Gelsemium sempervirens

False jessamine

Gelsemium sempervirgens, commonly known as jasmine vine or sempervirens, is a climbing evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its fragrant white flowers, this plant contains potent alkaloids. While historically used in traditional medicine, its bioactive compounds require careful handling due to their significant physiological effects and toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Gelsemium sempervirens
  • Family: Gelsemiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Gelsemiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Gelsemium.
