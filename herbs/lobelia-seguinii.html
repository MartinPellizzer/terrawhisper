Lobelia seguinii

What's the taxonomical classification of Lobelia seguinii?

Lobelia seguinii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving through its hierarchical classification, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the family Campanulaceae. Finally, the plant is situated within the genus Lobelia, specifically identified by its species name, seguinii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lobelia seguinii has an herbaceous structure characterized by erect stems that typically reach heights between 10 and 40 centimeters. Small, tubular flowers emerge from the leaf axils and exhibit a distinct zygomorphic symmetry common to the Lobeliaceae family. These blossoms often display a blue or violet coloration with a prominent lower lip. The root system is fibrous, providing stability to the slender plant within its moist habitats.
