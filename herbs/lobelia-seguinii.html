Lobelia seguinii

Lobelia seguinii, commonly known as "Xia Duan Cao" in traditional Chinese medicine, is a perennial herb valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its cooling and detoxifying effects, it is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments, reduce inflammation, and clear heat, playing a vital role in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Lobelia seguinii
  • Family: Campanulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Asterales and the family Campanulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lobelia.
