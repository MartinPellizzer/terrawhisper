Russian Knapweed, scientifically known as Rhaponticum repeps, is a resilient perennial herb native to Eurasia. Often found in meadows and disturbed soils, this plant is recognized by its striking purple flower heads. While primarily valued for its ecological role, it holds historical significance in traditional herbal studies and botanical research.
