Blackbean

Castañospermum australe, commonly known as the Brush Box, is a magnificent rainforest tree native to Australia. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers have long utilized its bark and leaves, which contain bioactive compounds with potential antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and medicinal properties for various ailments.
