Blackbean, scientifically known as Castañospermum australe, is a striking rainforest tree native to Australia. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including rheumatism and skin conditions. Beyond its healing potential, the tree is highly valued for its ornamental beauty and distinctive, large foliage.
