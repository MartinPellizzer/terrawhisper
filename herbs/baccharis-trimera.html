Baccharis trimera

Baccharis trimera, commonly known as Carqueja, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South America. Renowned for its bitter properties, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to support digestive health, liver function, and detoxification. This potent herb serves as a natural remedy for various gastrointestinal and metabolic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Baccharis trimera
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Baccharis.

This plant is widely distributed across several regions in Southern South America. It can be found in the northeast and northwest territories of Argentina.
