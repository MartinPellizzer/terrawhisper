Distribution

This plant, known as Artocarpus altilis, exhibits a specific distribution throughout the tropical regions of Africa. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Gambia and Guinea. Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the Central African Republic. Additionally, the species is documented in Cameroon, which is also part of the West-Central region. Finally, its range reaches the Gulf of Guinea Islands, completing its geographical profile in these areas.

Plant Parts

This plant, Artocarpus altilis, commonly known as breadfruit, contains various parts that possess unique medicinal and biological properties. The large, deeply lobed leaves are often utilized in traditional remedies for their topical applications, while the heartwood provides structural stability and contains bioactive compounds. The edible fruit serves as a vital nutritional source, but beyond sustenance, other components like the cortex of the roots are frequently studied for their potential pharmacological benefits. Additionally, the bud covers play a protective role in the plant's development, contributing to the overall chemical complexity of this versatile species.
