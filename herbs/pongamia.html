Pongamia

Pongamia, commonly known as Karanja, is a versatile medicinal tree valued in traditional Ayurveda. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it offers significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and insecticidal properties. Often used in skin treatments and oil production, this resilient plant serves as a vital resource in holistic healthcare and sustainable agriculture.

  • Scientific name: Pongamia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the Fabaceae family within the genus Pongamia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Pongamia or Karanja, is highly valued for its multi-purpose medicinal and industrial applications derived from various components. The most significant part is the seed oil, often referred to as Pongamia oil, which is extracted from the mature seeds and is widely used in traditional medicine and as a biofuel. After the oil extraction process, the remaining seed cake serves as a nutrient-rich organic fertilizer and a potent biopesticide. Additionally, the delicate flowers of the plant are sometimes utilized in herbal formulations, making the entire botanical structure a vital resource in holistic healing and sustainable agriculture.
