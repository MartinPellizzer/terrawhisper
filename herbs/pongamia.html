Scientifically known as Pongamia, is a versatile, multipurpose legume widely distributed across tropical regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains bioactive compounds with anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. Beyond healing, its seeds yield valuable non-edible oil, making this resilient tree a cornerstone of traditional medicine and sustainable biofuel development.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions within the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is noted specifically in Egypt. It also thrives in West-Central Tropical Africa, particularly within the DR Congo. The species can be found stretching across Northeast Tropical Africa, covering both Sudan and South Sudan. Finally, its range extends through East Tropical Africa, encompassing both Tanzania and Uganda.
