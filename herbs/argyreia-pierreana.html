What's the taxonomical classification of Argyreia pierreana?

Argyreiia pierreana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy down through its order, Solanales, it is placed within the family Convolvulaceae. Ultimately, this specific plant is identified by its genus, Argyreiia, and its unique species name, pierreana.
