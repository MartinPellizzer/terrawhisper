Cordia

Cordia, commonly known as various names like Schott or lacebark depending on the species, is a versatile genus of flowering plants. Renowned for its diverse medicinal properties, Cordia is utilized in traditional healing to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cordia
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cordia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Cordia, is recognized by various names across different languages and cultures. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans), it is referred to as 破布木属, while in Traditional Chinese (zh-hant) and the Cantonese dialect of Hong Kong (zh-hk), it is written as 破布木屬. It is also known in the Quechua language (qu) as Araña k'aspi, and in Russian (ru), it is called кордия.
