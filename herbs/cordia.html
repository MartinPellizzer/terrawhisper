Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cordia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical presence across several key regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Algeria, Egypt, and Libya. Its distribution also extends significantly into the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, the species is documented to inhabit the territories of Benin and Burkina. Consequently, these locations represent the primary areas where the medicinal plant is naturally located.
