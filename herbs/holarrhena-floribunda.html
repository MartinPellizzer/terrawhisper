Holarrhena floribunda

Holarrhena floribunda, commonly known as the flowering holly, is a resilient medicinal shrub cherished in traditional healing. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often utilized in herbal remedies to support immune health, it remains a vital resource in ethnobotanical studies and wellness.

  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa
