Plant Parts

This plant, Origanum compactum, is highly valued in traditional medicine for the therapeutic properties found within its specific plant parts. The most potent medicinal benefits are often derived from its essential oils, which are concentrated within the glandular trichomes of the leaves and flowers, providing strong antimicrobial and antioxidant effects.
