Origanum compactum

Origanum compactum, commonly known as compact oregano, is a resilient perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic leaves and medicinal properties. Rich in essential oils like carvacrol, it offers potent antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits. Widely used in traditional remedies, this plant supports digestive health and serves as a natural immune booster.

  • Scientific name: Origanum compactum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Origanum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific and somewhat limited geographical range across the Mediterranean region. In Europe, its presence is primarily concentrated within the southwestern territories, specifically in Spain. Moving across the sea, the species is also found throughout parts of Northern Africa. Within this African region, Morocco serves as a key location for its natural occurrence. Consequently, the distribution of Origanum compactum is defined by these two distinct coastal areas.
