Pyracantha

Pyracantha, commonly known as firethorn, is a versatile evergreen shrub celebrated for its vibrant, berry-laden clusters. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds traditional medicinal value. Historically, various parts of the plant have been used in folk remedies to address respiratory issues and inflammation, offering a blend of aesthetic and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Pyracantha
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Pyracantha.
