What's the taxonomical classification of Jasminum seguinii?

Jasminum seguinii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Lamiales, the plant is situated in the family Oleaceae. Within this family, it is identified under the genus Jasminum, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species seguinii.
