Strophanthus gratus, scientifically known as Strophanthus gratus, is a stunning tropical climber celebrated for its striking white flowers. Renowned in ethnobotany, it contains potent cardiac glycosides, primarilyOuabain. While used traditionally in medicine to treat heart conditions, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling and precise scientific application.

This plant, Strophanthus gratus, exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region.
