Macadamia nut species, scientifically known as Macadamia integrifolia, is a versatile evergreen tree native to Australian rainforests. Beyond its culinary fame, it holds significant medicinal potential. Rich in essential fatty acids and antioxidants, it is traditionally utilized for skin nourishment, promoting wound healing, and supporting overall cardiovascular health.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida.
