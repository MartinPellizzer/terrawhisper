Distribution

This plant is characterized by a highly localized geographical distribution. It is native to the tropical regions of China. Specifically, its presence is strictly confined to the island of Hainan. Because it is endemic to this single province, its natural habitat is quite limited. Consequently, the survival of this species depends heavily on the environmental stability of this specific island location.
