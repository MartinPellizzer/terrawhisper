Plant Parts

This plant, Aloe vera (often referred to in botanical contexts similar to Aloe welwitschia or specific Aloe species), is primarily valued for its thick, fleshy leaf. The leaf serves as the plant's primary storage organ, containing a translucent, mucilaginous gel that is rich in vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants. Within this succulent leaf, the inner parenchyma tissue holds the medicinal properties used to soothe skin irritations and promote healing, making the leaf the most significant part of the plant for therapeutic applications.
