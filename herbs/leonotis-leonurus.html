Leonotis leonurus

Leonotis leonurus, commonly known as Wild Dagga or Lion's Ear, is a striking perennial shrub native to South Africa. Renowned for its vibrant, orange tubular flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally used in various cultures for its calming properties, treating respiratory ailments, and serving as a potent herbal stimulant.

  • Scientific name: Leonotis leonurus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Leonotis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging natural distribution across several distinct African regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Burundi. The species is also extensively found throughout Southern Africa, including the Cape Provinces. Furthermore, it thrives in the KwaZulu-Natal region of the south. Finally, its geographical reach extends to the Free State and the Northern Provinces within Southern Africa.
