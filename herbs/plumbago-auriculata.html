Plumbago auriculata

Plumbago auriculata, commonly known as Cape Leadwort, is a versatile evergreen shrub celebrated for its profuse, pale blue to white flowers. Beyond its ornamental appeal in gardens, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various cultures utilizing its leaves and roots in traditional medicinal practices to treat diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Plumbago auriculata
  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Plumbaginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Plumbago, with the specific species being Plumbago auriculata.
