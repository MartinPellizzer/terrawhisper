Cape leadwort, scientifically known as Plumbago auriculata, is a versatile evergreen shrub renowned for its profuse, pale blue to white blossoms. Native to South Africa, this hardy plant is widely used in landscaping for its trailing habit and is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties.
