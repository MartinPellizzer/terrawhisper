Stauntonia

Stauntonia, commonly known as the vine vine or Stauntonia vine, is a unique medicinal plant native to North America. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat ailments like skin conditions and respiratory issues, this climbing woody vine offers fascinating botanical properties that continue to intrigue researchers and herbalists alike.

  • Family: Lardizabalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
