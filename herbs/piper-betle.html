betel

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (716 studies) · ★★★★★

Betel, scientifically known as Piper betle, is a perennial vine celebrated in traditional medicine across Asia. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, its aromatic leaves are used to treat digestive issues, oral infections, and respiratory ailments, serving as a cornerstone in Ayurvedic and Unani healing practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as betel, is a climbing vine characterized by several distinct morphological features. The most significant medicinal component is the leaf, specifically the aromatic betel leaf, which is widely used in traditional medicine for its antiseptic and stimulant properties. While the leaves are the primary part utilized by humans, the plant also produces a specialized inflorescence that serves its reproductive function. Supporting the vine's growth are the woody stems, which allow the plant to climb through surrounding vegetation.
