Piper betle

Piper betle, commonly known as betel leaf, is a perennial vine celebrated in traditional medicine across Asia. Rich in bioactive compounds, it possesses potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties. Often chewed with areca nut, this versatile plant is widely utilized to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and skin infections.

  • Scientific name: Piper betle
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Piperales and the family Piperaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Piper, specifically the species Piper betle.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is notably found in Assam. Its presence also extends significantly across the Western Indian Ocean. Specifically, it can be located in the island nations of Comoros and Madagascar. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout Mauritius and Réunion.
