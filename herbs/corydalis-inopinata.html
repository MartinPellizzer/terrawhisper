Corydalis inopinata

What's the taxonomical classification of Corydalis inopinata?

Corydalis inopinata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is positioned in the order Ranunculales. It is a member of the family Papaveraceae, falling under the genus Corydalis, and is specifically identified by the species name inopinata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Corydalis inopinata has deeply lobed, pinnatifid leaves that are arranged in a basal rosette and display a bright green coloration. The plant produces elongated, spurred flowers that vary in hue from pale yellow to soft pinkish tones. These floral structures feature a distinct nectar spur extending from the rear of the corolla. The stems are relatively slender and support a cluster of delicate blossoms during the spring blooming period. Small, capsule-like fruits develop following successful pollination to house the developing seeds.

How is this plant cultivated?
