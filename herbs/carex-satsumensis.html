Carex satsumensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Carex satsumensis?

Carex satsumensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Poales. It is a member of the family Cyperaceae, falling specifically within the genus Carex, and is identified by its unique species name, satsumensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Carex satsumensis has a perennial growth habit characterized by densely tufted, caespitose clumps of long, narrow, grass-like leaves that typically reach heights of 30 to 50 centimeters. The leaf blades are linear with acute tips and often exhibit a slightly lustrous or glaucous texture. Its reproductive structures consist of small, inconspicuous spikes arranged along the stem, featuring perigynia that are typically ovate to lanceolate in shape. The spikes are often clustered toward the top of the culm, providing a distinct textural contrast to the surrounding foliage. These morphological traits allow the plant to thrive in moist, shaded environments where it forms stable ground cover.
