Melilotus albus

Melilotus albus, commonly known as white sweet clover, is a perennial legume valued for its medicinal and forage properties. Rich in coumarin, it has historically been used to support circulation and digestive health. This versatile plant thrives in diverse habitats, offering significant botanical interest and therapeutic potential in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Melilotus albus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Melilotus.
