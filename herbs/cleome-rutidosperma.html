Cleome rutidosperma

Spider flower

Cleome rutidosperma, commonly known as the Spider Flower, is a versatile medicinal herb cherished in traditional systems like Ayurveda. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is widely utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and fever, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Cleome rutidosperma
  • Family: Cleomaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales. Finally, its taxonomic position is completed within the family Cleomaceae and the genus Cleome.
