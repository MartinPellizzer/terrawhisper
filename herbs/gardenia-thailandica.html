Gardenia thailandica

Gardenia thailandica, commonly known as the Thai Gardenia, is an exquisite evergreen shrub prized for its fragrant, creamy-white blossoms. Native to Southeast Asia, this ornamental plant is celebrated in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties, often used to soothe inflammation and support digestive health through its potent botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Gardenia thailandica
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant, known as Gardenia thailandica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Gentianaales. Ultimately, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family and the genus Gardenia.
