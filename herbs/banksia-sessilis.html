Banksia sessilis

Dryandra sessilis

Banksia sessilis, commonly known as Parrot Bush, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to Western Australia. Renowned for its striking yellow flower spikes and nutritious seeds, it plays a vital role in local ecosystems. Historically, Indigenous communities utilized its nectar and wood, highlighting its deep cultural and medicinal significance.

  • Scientific name: Banksia sessilis
  • Family: Proteaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Proteales and the family Proteaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Banksia.
