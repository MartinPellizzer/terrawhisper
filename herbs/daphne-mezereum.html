Mezereoon, scientifically known as Daphne mezereum, is a deciduous shrub renowned for its striking, fragrant pink flowers and bright red berries. While historically utilized in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds, it is highly toxic. Caution is essential, as all parts of this plant contain dangerous alkaloids.
