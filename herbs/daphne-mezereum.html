Daphne mezereum

Mezereon

Daphne mezereum, commonly known as February Daphne, is a striking deciduous shrub prized for its fragrant, pink blossoms. While its early spring blooms offer ornamental beauty, all parts of the plant are highly toxic. Historically used in traditional medicine, it requires extreme caution due to its potent chemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Daphne mezereum
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Thymelaeaceae and is identified by the genus Daphne. Specifically, its taxonomic designation is Daphne mezereum.
