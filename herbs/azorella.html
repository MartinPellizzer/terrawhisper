Scientifically known as Azorella, is a resilient shrub native to the high-altitude Andean ecosystems. Often referred to as "Yareta," this slow-growing plant forms dense, cushion-like mounds. Historically valued in traditional medicine, its unique properties are studied for their potential therapeutic benefits in treating various ailments within extreme environments.
