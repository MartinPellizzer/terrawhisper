Azorella

Azorella, commonly known as the "cushion plant," is a resilient perennial found in high-altitude Andean ecosystems. Renowned for its compact, woody structure, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk remedies to treat various ailments, leveraging its unique bioactive compounds to support health amidst harsh, alpine environments.

  • Scientific name: Azorella
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is specifically situated in the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Azorella.
