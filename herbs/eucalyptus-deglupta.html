Rainbow eucalyptus, scientifically known as Eucalyptus deglupta, is a striking, fast-growing tree renowned for its vibrant, multi-colored bark. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds medicinal potential. Its essential oils possess antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, traditionally used to support respiratory health and provide soothing relief from congestion.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the expansive region of Malesia. Within this zone, it can be found growing throughout the Philippines and the island of Sulawesi. Its presence also extends to the Lesser Sunda Islands and the Maluku archipelago. Additionally, the species reaches into the Papuasia region. Specifically, it inhabits the Bismarck Archipelago to complete its geographical range.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the rainbow eucalyptus, utilizes its leaves as a primary component in various traditional and medicinal applications. The leaves are characterized by their elongated, lanceolate shape and a glossy texture, containing essential oils that possess antimicrobial and decongestant properties. When harvested and processed, these leaves can be used to create infusions or extracts that help alleviate respiratory issues, making them a vital part of the plant's therapeutic profile.
