Eucalyptus deglupta

Eucalyptus deglupta, commonly known as the Rainbow Eucalyptus, is a striking tree prized for its multicolored bark. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, it possesses medicinal potential. Its essential oils offer antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and minor wounds through topical or aromatic applications.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus deglupta
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae, it is classified under the genus Eucalyptus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the expansive region of Malesia. Within this zone, it can be found growing throughout the Philippines and the island of Sulawesi. Its presence also extends to the Lesser Sunda Islands and the Maluku archipelago. Additionally, the species reaches into the Papuasia region. Specifically, it inhabits the Bismarck Archipelago to complete its geographical range.
