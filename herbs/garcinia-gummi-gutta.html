Garcinia gummi-gutta

Garcinia gummi-gutta, commonly known as Malabar tamarind or Kudampuli, is a tropical plant celebrated in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its sour fruit, it contains hydroxycitric acid, which aids weight management and digestion. This versatile botanical serves as both a culinary staple and a potent therapeutic agent in traditional wellness.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia gummi-gutta
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales and the family Clusiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Garcinia.
