What's the taxonomical classification of Asarum geophilum?

Asarum geophilum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Piperales. It is further categorized under the family Aristolochiaceae, which places it within the genus Asarum. Ultimately, its specific biological identity is defined by its designation as the species geophilum.
