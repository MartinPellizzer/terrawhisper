Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across several continents. In Europe, its presence is noted specifically within Germany. Moving toward the Atlantic, it can be found growing in the Macaronesian region of Madeira. The species also extends into the tropical and southern reaches of Africa, including Mozambique in South Tropical Africa. Finally, it is widely distributed throughout Southern Africa, specifically within the Cape Provinces and KwaZulu-Natal.
