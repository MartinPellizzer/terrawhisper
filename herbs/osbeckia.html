Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across both the Indian Ocean and East Asia. Within the Western Indian Ocean region, it can be found inhabiting the islands of Mauritius and Madagascar. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it is documented in the South-Central territories. Additionally, the species is located in the southern coastal province of Hainan. Finally, it is also distributed throughout the Southeast regions of China.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Osbeckia, features a distinct leaf structure that is essential to its botanical identification and medicinal properties. The leaf is typically characterized by an elliptical or oblong shape, often displaying prominent, longitudinal veins that run parallel from the base to the tip. These leaves are frequently covered in fine, soft hairs, giving them a slightly textured or pubescent feel, which is a common trait among many species in this genus. In traditional applications, the leaf is the primary part utilized for its bioactive compounds, which are often extracted for their various therapeutic benefits.
