Senna siamea

Siamese cassia

Sennas siamea, commonly known as the Siamese cassia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, it contains bioactive compounds that aid digestion. Beyond gastrointestinal health, traditional practices utilize its leaves and seeds to treat various ailments, making it a significant botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Senna siamea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It further falls into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, specifically as a member of the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Senna.
