Siamese cassia

Senna siamea · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (108 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Siamese cassia, scientifically known as Senna siamea, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent purgative properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat constipation. Beyond its digestive benefits, the plant offers significant pharmacological potential in addressing various inflammatory and metabolic conditions.
