Piper arboreum

Piper arborium, commonly known as the Tree Pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments. This aromatic shrub offers significant pharmacological potential for developing modern therapeutic treatments and natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Piper arboreum
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Piperaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Piper, specifically as the species Piper arborescens.

Common Names

This plant, Piper arboreum, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Long Pepper tree, while in its native India, it is commonly called Pimpali. In the Hindi language, it is known as Piphal, and in Bengali, it is referred to as Pipul.
