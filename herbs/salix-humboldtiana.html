Humboldt's willow

Salix humboldtiana · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (8 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Humboldt's willow, scientifically known as Salix humboldtiana, is a versatile riparian shrub native to North America. Renowned for its medicinal properties, its bark contains salicin, a precursor to aspirin. Historically used to alleviate pain and reduce inflammation, this resilient willow remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies today.
