Garlic plant, scientifically known as Allium sativum, is a versatile medicinal herb renowned for its potent health benefits. Rich in sulfur compounds like allicin, it possesses powerful antibacterial, antiviral, and antioxidant properties. Widely used to support cardiovascular health and boost immunity, garlic remains a cornerstone of natural wellness.
