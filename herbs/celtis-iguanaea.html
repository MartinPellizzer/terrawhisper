This plant, Celtis iguanaea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Rosalales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cannabaeae and the genus Celtis.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within North America. In the United States, its presence is specifically noted in the state of Florida. Its range extends significantly into Mexico, where it can be found throughout the central territories. Additionally, the species inhabits the northeastern and gulf coastal regions of Mexico.
