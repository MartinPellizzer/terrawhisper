Moringa stenopetala

Moringa stenopepalas, commonly known as the West African Moringa, is a resilient, multi-purpose tree native to the Sahelian regions. Renowned for its exceptional nutritional profile, every part of this plant—from nutrient-dense leaves to protein-rich seeds—offers profound medicinal benefits, serving as a vital tool against malnutrition and disease.

  • Scientific name: Moringa stenopetala
  • Family: Moringaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Brassicales and the family Moringaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Moringa.

This plant is primarily found within the specific regions of Northeast and East Tropical Africa. In the Northeast Tropical Africa zone, its presence is documented in Ethiopia. Moving toward the East Tropical Africa region, the species can also be located in Kenya. These two countries represent the key geographical areas where the plant naturally occurs. Consequently, its distribution is concentrated within these distinct African territories.
