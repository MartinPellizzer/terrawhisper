Cabbagetree, scientifically known as Moringa stenopepalta, is a highly nutritious medicinal plant native to West Africa. Renowned for its exceptional nutritional profile, it serves as a vital food security resource. Beyond nutrition, it offers significant therapeutic benefits, including antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it invaluable in traditional medicine.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the specific regions of Northeast and East Tropical Africa. In the Northeast Tropical Africa zone, its presence is documented in Ethiopia. Moving toward the East Tropical Africa region, the species can also be located in Kenya. These two countries represent the key geographical areas where the plant naturally occurs. Consequently, its distribution is concentrated within these distinct African territories.

Plant Parts

This plant, Moringa stenopepalta, is a versatile medicinal resource where almost every component offers unique therapeutic benefits. The nutrient-dense leaves are widely consumed for their high vitamin and mineral content, while concentrated leaf extracts are frequently utilized in pharmacological applications for their antioxidant properties. The seeds are also highly valued, often used for their oil and antimicrobial qualities. Furthermore, the roots provide essential medicinal compounds, and the root bark is specifically employed in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, showcasing the comprehensive utility of the entire plant.
