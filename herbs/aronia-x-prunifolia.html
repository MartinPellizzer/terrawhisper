Purple chokeberry, scientifically known as Aronia × pruinifolia, is a resilient deciduous shrub celebrated for its potent antioxidant properties. Rich in anthocyanins and vitamins, its dark berries offer significant health benefits, including anti-inflammatory effects and cardiovascular support. This versatile plant is highly valued in both traditional medicine and nutrition.
