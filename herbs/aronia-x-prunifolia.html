Aronia × prunifolia

Aronia × pruinifolia, commonly known as the chokeberry, is a resilient deciduous shrub celebrated for its potent antioxidant properties. Rich in anthocyanins and vitamins, its dark berries are utilized in traditional medicine to support cardiovascular health, reduce inflammation, and bolster the immune system through their exceptional nutrient density.

  • Scientific name: Aronia × prunifolia
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Aronia. Specifically, Aronia × pruiniifolia represents a hybrid within this unique taxonomic lineage.
