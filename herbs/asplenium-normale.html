Asplenium normale

What's the taxonomical classification of Asplenium normale?

Asplenium normale belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it resides in the order Polypodiales and is a member of the family Aspleniaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Asplenium and the specific species name normale.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Asplenium normale has large, glossy, dark green pinnate fronds that can reach lengths of up to 1.5 meters and are characterized by a prominent, dark, slightly curved stipe. The individual pinnae are lanceolate in shape and are arranged symmetrically along a central rachis. Each leaflet possesses finely serrated margins and a smooth, coriaceous texture that aids in moisture retention. The reproductive structures consist of small, dark sori located on the undersides of the leaflets, typically arranged in rows parallel to the veins. This fern species often exhibits a robust rhizome structure that allows it to anchor firmly in moist, shaded environments.
