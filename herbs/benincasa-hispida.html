Wax gourd, scientifically known as Benincasa hispida, is a versatile trailing vine prized in traditional medicine and culinary practices. Rich in bioactive compounds, this cucurbit is celebrated for its cooling properties, aiding digestion and supporting hydration. It serves as a vital natural remedy for promoting overall wellness and health.
