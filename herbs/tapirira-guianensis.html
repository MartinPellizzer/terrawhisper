Tapirira guianensis

Tapirira guianensis, commonly known as the "tapiira" in certain regions, is a medicinal shrub native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized by indigenous communities for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Tapirira guianensis
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Tapirira, as part of the species Tapirira guianenis.
