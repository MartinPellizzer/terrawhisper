Munronia pinnata

Munronia pinnata, commonly known as Black Lawng in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various infections, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Munronia pinnata
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Meliaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Munroonia.
