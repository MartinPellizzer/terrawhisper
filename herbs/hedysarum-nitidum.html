Hedysarum nitidum

Hedysarum nitidum, commonly known as the nitrogen-fixing legume, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, it is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat inflammation and promote healing. This resilient shrub offers significant pharmacological potential for modern botanical research.
