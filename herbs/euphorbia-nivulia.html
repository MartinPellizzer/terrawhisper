Euphorbia nivulia

Euphorbia nivulia, commonly known as the hill spurge, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities. This plant serves as a vital resource for treating various ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia nivulia
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it falls under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of India and its neighboring country, Bangladesh. The species also has a significant presence in the state of Assam. Furthermore, its geographical range extends into Pakistan to the west. Finally, the plant is also documented as being native to the island of Sri Lanka.
