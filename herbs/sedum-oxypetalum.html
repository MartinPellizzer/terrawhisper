Sedum oxypetalum

Sedum oxypetalum, commonly known as "Stonecrop" or "Jade Plant," is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is frequently used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This resilient succulent offers significant therapeutic potential through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Sedum oxypetalum
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Crassulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Sedum.
