Parkia filicoidea

What's the taxonomical classification of Parkia filicoidea?

Parkia filicoidea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further categorized under the order Fabales. It is a member of the family Fabaceae, a group known for its diverse range of flowering plants, and is specifically placed within the genus Parkia. Ultimately, its unique biological identity is defined by its specific classification as the species filicoidea.

According to a study published by "Plant foods for human nutrition (Dordrecht, Netherlands)", the study investigated the effects of various processing techniques on the nutrient composition and anti-nutritional factors of Parkia filicoidea. These methods of processing include boiling in water, acid, alkali, and fermentation. When using the water treated samples as controls, slight decreases in protein and carbohydrate contents were observed in the fermented and alkali-treated meals. An increase in extractable oil content was observed in the acid, alkali, and fermented samples. The alkali treatment appeared to be the most effective method for reducing trypsin inhibitor and tannin contents. This specific method also has the additional advantage of improving the protein digestibility.
