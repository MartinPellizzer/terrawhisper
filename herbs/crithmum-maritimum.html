Crithmum maritimum

Rock samphire

Crithmum maritimum, commonly known as sea samphire, is a resilient succulent perennial found clinging to rocky coastal cliffs. Thriving in salty, spray-drenched environments, this edible plant offers a unique, tangy flavor. Historically valued for its medicinal properties, it serves as a nutritious coastal herb rich in essential vitamins.

  • Scientific name: Crithmum maritimum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is a member of the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Crithmum.

Within these leaves, the plant produces a significant amount of volatile oil, which contains the essential aromatic compounds responsible for its therapeutic effects and characteristic scent.
