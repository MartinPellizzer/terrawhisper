Rock samphire, scientifically known as Crithmum maritimum, is a resilient coastal succulent found clinging to seaside cliffs. Renowned for its salty, intense flavor, this edible herb thrives in harsh, spray-swept environments. Historically prized in culinary traditions, it offers a unique, pungent profile that captures the essence of the ocean.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as sea plantain, possesses unique morphological features that contribute to its medicinal properties. Its fleshy, succulent leaves are the most prominent part of the plant, often growing in dense rosettes and exhibiting a distinct salt-tolerant structure. Within these leaves, the plant produces a significant amount of volatile oil, which contains the essential aromatic compounds responsible for its therapeutic effects and characteristic scent.
