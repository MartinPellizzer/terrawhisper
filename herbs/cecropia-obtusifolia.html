Cecropia obtusifolia

Cecropia obtusifolia, commonly known as the Colombian Cecropia or Emba, is a fast-growing tropical tree valued in traditional medicine. Native to Central and South America, its leaves are frequently utilized in decoctions to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance in regional healing.

  • Scientific name: Cecropia obtusifolia
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cecropia obtusifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Rosales. Finally, it is classified under the family Urticaceae and the genus Cecropia.
