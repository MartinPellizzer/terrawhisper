Ziziphus mauritiana

Ziziphus mauritiana, commonly known as the Indian jujube, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in traditional wellness. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its fruits, leaves, and bark are utilized to support digestive health, boost immunity, and promote skin healing, making it a cornerstone of herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphus mauritiana
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Rosales and the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Ziziphus, specifically as the species Ziziphus mauritiana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the African continent and its surrounding islands. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically documented within the territories of Algeria and Morocco. Moving toward the Atlantic, the species can also be found in the Macaronesian archipelago of Cape Verde. Its range extends further south into West Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the country of Benin. Additionally, the plant is recorded as growing within the borders of Burkina.
