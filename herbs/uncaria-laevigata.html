Uncaria laevigata

Uncaria laeviagata, commonly known as Cat's Claw, is a legendary woody vine native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, this botanical powerhouse is widely utilized to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote joint health, making it a cornerstone of traditional South American herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Uncaria laevigata
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Uncaria laevigata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales and the family Rubiaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Uncaria.

This plant, Uncaria laevigata, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic context, particularly within Chinese herbal medicine. In Mandarin Chinese, it is referred to by the names 平滑钩藤, 四楞通, 双钩藤, and 光叶钩藤.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is noted within the South-Central and Southeast territories. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in Assam and throughout Bangladesh. Additionally, the species extends into the Indo-China region, specifically within Laos. Collectively, these locations highlight the tropical and subtropical habitats where the plant thrives.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Uncaria laevigata and commonly referred to as Cat's Claw, derives its primary medicinal value from its stem barks. These woody tissues are harvested to extract bioactive alkaloids that are widely utilized in traditional and modern herbal medicine for their anti-inflammatory and immune-boosting properties. Because the therapeutic compounds are concentrated within the protective outer layers of the climbing vine, the quality and processing of the stem barks are essential for ensuring the efficacy of the resulting extracts.
