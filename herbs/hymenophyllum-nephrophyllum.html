Hymenophyllum nephrophyllum

Hymenophyllum nephrophyllum, commonly known as the kidney-leaved filmy fern, is a delicate vascular plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its translucent, paper-thin fronds, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in various medicinal practices, this unique fern offers fascinating insights into the diverse therapeutic potential of forest ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Hymenophyllum nephrophyllum
  • Family: Hymenophyllaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Hymenophyllales, specifically within the family Hymenophyllaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Hymenophyllum.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across several regions of New Zealand. It can be found growing in the North Island of the country. Additionally, its distribution extends to the South Island. The species also inhabits the Chatham Islands, marking a broader reach within the archipelago. Collectively, these locations define the known natural habitat for Hymenophyllum nephrophyllum.
