Rhus chinensis var. roxburghii

Rhus chinensis var. roxburghii, commonly known as Chinese Sumac, is a versatile medicinal plant deeply rooted in traditional pharmacopeia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is primarily utilized to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues. This resilient shrub offers significant phytochemical benefits, making it a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Rhus chinensis var. roxburghii
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Anacardiaceae and the genus Rhus.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. The species also extends its range to the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Furthermore, its geographical reach includes the Indo-China region, specifically within Laos. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where this medicinal variety can be found.
