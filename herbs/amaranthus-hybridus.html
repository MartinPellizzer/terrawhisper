Amaranthus hybridus

Smooth amaranth

Amaranthus hybridus, commonly known as green amaranth, is a versatile annual plant valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in vitamins and minerals, it serves as a vital food source globally. Traditionally, it is utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, promote healing, and support overall digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Amaranthus hybridus
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is specifically nested in the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, its scientific designation places it within the genus Amaranthus.
