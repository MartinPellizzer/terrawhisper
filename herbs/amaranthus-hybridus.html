Smooth amaranth, scientifically known as Amaranthus hybridus, is a versatile annual herb celebrated for its dual nutritional and medicinal value. Renowned for its nutrient-dense leaves and seeds, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, making it a vital component in traditional herbal medicine globally.
