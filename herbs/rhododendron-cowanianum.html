Rhododendron cowanianum

Rhododendron cowanianum, commonly known as the Cowan's Rhododendron, is a rare and striking evergreen shrub native to specific highland regions. Valued for its unique aesthetic and potential ethnobotanical properties, this species offers fascinating insights into plant biodiversity, requiring specialized care to thrive in its delicate, high-altitude ecological niche.
