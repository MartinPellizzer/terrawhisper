Carduus crispus

Carduus crispus, commonly known as crested milk thistle, is a distinctive perennial herb within the Asteraceae family. Native to the Mediterranean, this plant is recognized by its prickly, architectural foliage and striking purple blooms. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it is valued for its potential hepatoprotective and diuretic properties.

  • Scientific name: Carduus crispus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Carduus crispus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
