Linum album

Linum album, commonly known as white flax, is a perennial herb distinguished by its delicate white blossoms and slender stems. Found primarily in European meadows, this plant holds historical significance in textile production and traditional herbalism, valued for its resilient nature and its role within diverse botanical ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Linum album
  • Family: Linaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is situated in the family Linaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Linum.
