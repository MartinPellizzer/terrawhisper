Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. In Eastern Asia, its presence is noted within the Nansei-shoto islands and throughout Taiwan. Moving into the Malesia region, the species can be found inhabiting the island of Borneo. Its range further extends to the island of Jawa, illustrating its adaptability to various tropical environments. Finally, the distribution reaches the Lesser Sunda Islands, completing its presence across these maritime territories.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ficus septica, is a medicinal species where various components are utilized for their therapeutic potential. The structural integrity of the plant is provided by its sturdy stems, which support the overall growth of the organism. The most significant medicinal value is found in the leaves, which serve as the primary site for bioactive compound accumulation. Specifically, researchers often utilize methanolic leaf extracts to isolate and study the concentrated phytochemicals present within the leaves, demonstrating the importance of these foliage components in traditional and scientific healing practices.
