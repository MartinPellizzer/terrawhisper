Septic fig, scientifically known as Ficus septica, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots possess potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, this plant remains a vital resource in various herbal remedies.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. In Eastern Asia, its presence is noted within the Nansei-shoto islands and throughout Taiwan.
