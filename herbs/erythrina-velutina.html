Red Bean Tree, scientifically known as Erythrina velutina, is a striking medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive and skin disorders.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several continents and distinct ecological zones. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its presence extends to East Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Uganda. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented growing in Sri Lanka. Finally, it reaches the Caribbean region, where it is located in both Aruba and the Cayman Islands.
