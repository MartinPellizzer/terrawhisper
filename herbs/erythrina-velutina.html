Red Bean Tree, scientifically known as Erythrina velutina, is a striking medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant crimson blooms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive and skin disorders.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several continents and distinct ecological zones.
