Quercus obtusata

Quercus obtusata, commonly known as the bluntleaf oak, is a distinctive deciduous tree native to North American woodlands. Characterized by its unique, rounded leaf lobes, this species plays a vital role in forest ecosystems, providing essential habitat and food sources for various wildlife while contributing to regional biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Quercus obtusata
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Fagaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Quercus.
