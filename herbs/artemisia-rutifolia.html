Artemisia rutifolia

Artemisia rutifolia, commonly known as rutifolia wormwood, is a resilient perennial herb native to various high-altitude regions. Characterized by its distinct silvery foliage and aromatic properties, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Researchers study its unique chemical compounds for potential therapeutic applications in traditional and modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia rutifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Artemisia rutifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Artemisia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of Northern and Central Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found growing in the Altay and Buryatiya regions. Its distribution further extends through the Krasnoyarsk area and into the southern reaches of Tuva. Beyond the Siberian borders, the species also occupies significant habitats within Central Asia. Specifically, the plant is documented to thrive within the country of Kazakhstan.
