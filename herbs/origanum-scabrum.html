Origanum scabrum

Origanum scabrum, commonly known as wild marjoram or desert oregano, is a resilient perennial herb native to Mediterranean and arid regions. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it serves as a potent medicinal plant, valued in traditional healing for its powerful antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties used to support wellness.
