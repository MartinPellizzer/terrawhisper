Rhododendron arboreum

Burans

Rhododendron arboreum, commonly known as the tree rhododendron, is a majestic evergreen species native to the Himalayas. Renowned for its vibrant red blooms, this medicinal plant holds significant traditional value. It is frequently utilized in Ayurvedic practices to treat respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and to alleviate various inflammatory issues.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron arboreum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae, it is classified under the genus Rhododendron.

Its range extends across Asia, where it can be found in the high altitudes of Tibet in China. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species thrives in the lush landscapes of Assam. Furthermore, it is widely distributed across the East Himalaya and throughout various parts of India.
