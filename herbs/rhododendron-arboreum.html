Burans, scientifically known as Rhododendron arboreum, is a majestic Himalayan evergreen celebrated for its vibrant crimson blooms. Beyond its stunning beauty, this sacred plant holds profound medicinal value, traditionally used to treat ailments like hypertension and digestive issues. Its nutrient-rich nectar is also crafted into a refreshing, restorative tonic.
