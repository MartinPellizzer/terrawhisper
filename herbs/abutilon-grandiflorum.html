Abutilon grandiflorum

Abutilon grandiflorum, commonly known as the Flowering Abutilon, is a striking ornamental shrub prized for its large, bell-shaped blooms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is utilized in various traditional medicinal practices to address diverse health concerns, showcasing its unique pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Abutilon grandiflorum
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Abutilon grandiflorum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae within the genus Abutilon.
