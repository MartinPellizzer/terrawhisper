What's the taxonomical classification of Phyllostachys makinoi?

Phyllostachys makinoi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is further organized under the order Poales and is a member of the family Poaceae. Finally, its specific biological identity is defined by its placement in the genus Phyllostachys, with the species name makinoi.
