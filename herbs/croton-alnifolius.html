Croton alnifolius

Croton alnifolius, commonly known as the Alder-leaved Croton, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient plant serves as a vital resource in various ethnobotanical healing practices.
