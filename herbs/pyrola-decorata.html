Pyrola decorata

Pyrola decorata, commonly known as the decorated mountain heather, is a striking perennial herb native to specific high-altitude regions. Renowned for its delicate, bell-shaped blossoms, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in ethnobotanical practices for its unique bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in managing various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Pyrola decorata
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Ericaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Pyrola.
