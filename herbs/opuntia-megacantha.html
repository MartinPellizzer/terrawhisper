Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the prickly pear cactus, is characterized by its unique morphology where the functional "leaves" are actually modified into spines to reduce water loss. However, the primary photosynthetic organs are the flattened, succulent stems known as cladodes, which are often mistaken for leaves due to their green, fleshy appearance. While true leaves are highly reduced in this species to conserve moisture in arid environments, the specialized structure of the cladodes serves the vital biological purpose of capturing sunlight and performing photosynthesis.
