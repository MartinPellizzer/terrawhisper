Pulicaria glutinosa, scientifically known as Pulicaria glutinosa, is a perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Commonly called feverfew or pleasing feverfew, it grows in damp habitats. Historically valued in traditional medicine, this aromatic plant is recognized for its potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties in herbal remedies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of Africa and the Middle East. In Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Djibouti and Somalia. Moving toward the Arabian Peninsula, the species is also found throughout the Gulf States. Furthermore, it can be identified within the territories of Oman and Saudi Arabia.
