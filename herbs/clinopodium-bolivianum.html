Clinopodium bolivianum

Clinopodium bolivianum, commonly known as a member of the Lamiaceae family, is a medicinal herb native to South American landscapes. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering significant ethnobotanical value for natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Clinopodium bolivianum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Clinopodium.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the western and southern regions of the South American continent.
