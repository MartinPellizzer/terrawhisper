River Nettle, scientifically known as Laportea peduncularis, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Characterized by its stinging hairs, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various systemic ailments across cultures.
