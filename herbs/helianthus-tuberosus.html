Sunroot, scientifically known as Helianthus tuberosus, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its nutrient-dense tubers. Often used to support digestive health and manage blood sugar levels, it contains prebiotic inulin. This resilient perennial offers a unique blend of culinary potential and therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Helianthus tuberosus, is characterized by several distinct botanical structures that contribute to its medicinal properties. The most significant part used is the underground tubers, which are starchy swellings that store nutrients for the plant. Each individual tuber can be harvested for various applications, providing a concentrated source of bioactive compounds. Additionally, the aerial parts, which include the stems, leaves, and flowers above the ground, are often studied or utilized in traditional practices to complement the benefits derived from the roots.
