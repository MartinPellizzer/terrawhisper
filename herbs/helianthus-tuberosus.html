Helianthus tuberosus

Sunroot

Helianthus tuberosus, commonly known as Jerusalem artichoke, is a perennial sunflower valued for its edible tubers. Rich in inulin, this nutritious plant offers significant digestive benefits and serves as a sustainable food source. Beyond its culinary uses, it holds great potential in traditional medicine and modern functional nutrition.

  • Scientific name: Helianthus tuberosus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Helianthus, which includes the species Helianthus tuberosus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Helianthus tuberosus, is recognized by various common names across different languages and regions. In the Hong Kong region, the name 洋薑 (zh-hk) is used, and in Singapore, it is commonly known as 洋姜 (zh-sg). Additionally, in the Venetian language, it is referred to as Topinanbur (vec).
