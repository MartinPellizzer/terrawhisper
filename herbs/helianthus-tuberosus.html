Helianthus tuberosus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (464 studies) · ★★★★★

Sunroot, scientifically known as Helianthus tuberosus, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its nutrient-dense tubers. Often used to support digestive health and manage blood sugar levels, it contains prebiotic inulin. This resilient perennial offers a unique blend of culinary potential and therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness.
