Elaeocarpus floribundus

Elaeocarpus floribundus, commonly known as the Flowering Elaeocarpus, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Native to tropical regions, its leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds used in traditional healing to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural wellness applications.

  • Scientific name: Elaeocarpus floribundus
  • Family: Elaeocarpaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Oxalidales. Finally, it is a member of the family Elaeocarpaceae and the genus Elaeocarpus.
