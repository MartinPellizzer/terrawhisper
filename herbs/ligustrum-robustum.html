Ligustrum robustum

Ligustrum robustum, commonly known as the robust privet, is a resilient evergreen shrub valued for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Native to tropical regions, this plant features glossy foliage and fragrant white flowers. In traditional practices, various parts are utilized for their bioactive properties to support holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ligustrum robustum
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is a member of the Oleaceae family. Finally, it is classified under the genus Ligustrum as the species Ligustrum robustum.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across several distinct regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is noted in India, Bangladesh, and Assam.
