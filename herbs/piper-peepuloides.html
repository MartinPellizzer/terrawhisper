Piper peepuloides

Piper peepuloides, commonly known as the pepper leaf, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues. This aromatic shrub offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Piper peepuloides
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Piperaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Piper.
