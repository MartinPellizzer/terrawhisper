Lycopodium obscurum, scientifically known as Lycopodium obscurum, is a perennial herbaceous plant commonly referred to as flat-stemmed clubmoss. Found in North American forests, this ancient botanical species is valued in traditional medicine for its unique chemical properties, often utilized in various holistic treatments to support neurological and digestive health.
