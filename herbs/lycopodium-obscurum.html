Lycopodium obscurum

Lycopodium obscurum, commonly known as flat clubmoss, is a perennial spore-bearing plant found in temperate forests. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it contains unique alkaloids with diverse biological activities. This resilient species is studied for its potential therapeutic properties, offering intriguing insights into natural pharmacology and botanical wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lycopodium obscurum
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Lycopodiidae and order Lycopodiales, it is further categorized into the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lycopodium.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several northern regions. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted in the Primorye region.
