Epimedium pubigerum

Epimedium pubescens, commonly known as Horny Goat Weed, is a perennial herb prized in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compound, icariin, it is frequently utilized to support reproductive health, enhance vitality, and improve bone density. This versatile plant remains a cornerstone of herbal supplementation worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium pubigerum
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Epimedium pubigerum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the Berberidaceae family within the genus Epimedium.

Plant Parts

This plant, Epimedium pubigerum, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts. These above-ground structures, which include the leaves, stems, and sometimes the flowering components, contain the concentrated bioactive compounds necessary for its therapeutic effects. When harvesting the aerial parts, practitioners focus on the foliage, as it is the primary site for the accumulation of flavonoids and other medicinal constituents used to treat various ailments.
