The morphology of Tetraceras scandens is characterized by its growth as a woody liana, functioning as an obligatory climber. As a terrestrial plant, it is not epiphytic, instead establishing itself within the ground substrate. The plant's physical structure is defined by its woody nature, providing the necessary rigidity for its extensive climbing habit.

Its physiological profile is defined by its capacity for nutrient uptake, yet it does not possess the symbiotic mechanisms required for atmospheric nitrogen assimilation; specifically, it is not a nitrogen fixer.
