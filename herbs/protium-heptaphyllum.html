Protium heptaphyllum

Protium heptaphyllum, commonly known as Breu Branco, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its aromatic resin, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and healing properties. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments and respiratory issues, this botanical treasure offers significant potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Protium heptaphyllum
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Protium heptaphyllum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Burseraceae and falls under the genus Protium.
