Distribution

This plant exhibits a varied geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence can be noted in Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, it is found within the borders of both Austria and Germany. The species also extends into Southwestern Europe, specifically inhabiting the Baleares. Finally, its range in the Southwestern region includes the island of Corse.
