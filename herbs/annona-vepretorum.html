Annona vepretorum

Annona vepretorum, commonly known as a member of the custard apple family, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments. This species offers promising therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology due to its bioactive compounds.
