Saussurea pachyneura

What's the taxonomical classification of Saussurea pachyneura?

Saussurea pachyneura belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. As a member of the family Asteraceae, this plant is situated within the genus Saussurea, specifically identified by the species name pachyneura.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Saussurea pachyneura has thick, fleshy leaves arranged in a basal rosette that feature densely hairy, thickened margins and a distinctively coarse texture. These leaves are characterized by their robust structure and a dense covering of trichomes that serve as an adaptation to high-altitude environments. The plant typically develops a stout stem that supports a terminal inflorescence composed of numerous small, yellowish-white flower heads. Each individual flower head is surrounded by involucral bracts that possess a notable thickness and hairy surface consistent with the species name. The overall vegetative form is compact and adapted to withstand the harsh climatic conditions of its alpine habitat.
