Dicliptera paniculata

Dicliptera paniculata, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional practices, is a versatile plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Often found in tropical regions, it is utilized in various herbal remedies to address diverse ailments. Understanding its phytochemical profile is essential for exploring its potential pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Dicliptera paniculata
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dicliptera, specifically as the species Dicliptera paniculata.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Dicliptera paniculata, is referred to by various local names across different linguistic regions. In the Fula language, it is called Fura pucci (ff), while in Hausa, it is commonly known as Tubanin dawaki (ha). In Malayalam, the plant is identified as കാട്ടുപുഴുക്കൊല്ലി (ml), and in Tamil, it is referred to as காரக்காஞ்சிரம் (ta).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically recorded in Egypt. Its range extends to the Macaronesian islands, where it can be found in Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species is located within West Tropical Africa, including countries such as Benin and Burkina. Additionally, its distribution continues through the West Tropical African nation of Gambia.
