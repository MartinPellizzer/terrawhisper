Hesperozygis ringens

Hesperozygis ringens, commonly known as the "climbing madder," is a versatile medicinal shrub found throughout tropical Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, promote wound healing, and alleviate inflammation, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern herbal treatments.
