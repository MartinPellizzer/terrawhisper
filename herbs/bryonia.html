Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions in Northern Europe. It can be found growing naturally in the country of Denmark. Additionally, its presence extends to the northern territories of Finland. The species is also documented within the landscapes of Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, its geographical range includes the coastal and inland areas of Norway.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as white bryony, utilizes its extensive and fleshy root system as a primary storage organ for nutrients and medicinal compounds. The underground part of the plant is often characterized by its thick, tuberous structure, which contains concentrated alkaloids used in traditional herbal practices. Because the active constituents are highly potent within this specific area, the root is frequently the part harvested for pharmacological study and medicinal preparation.
