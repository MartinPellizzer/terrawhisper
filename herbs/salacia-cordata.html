Salaacia cordata, scientifically known as Salaacia cordata, is a climbing shrub native to the tropical forests of India and Sri Lanka. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, this potent plant is traditionally utilized for its anti-diabetic properties and its ability to support reproductive health and overall metabolic wellness.

Morphology

The morphology of Salacia cordata is characterized by its woody growth form, functioning as a tree-like structure that exhibits liana climbing habits. As a facultative climber, it utilizes other structures for support rather than being strictly obligatory or self-supporting. The plant is terrestrial in nature, neither aquatic nor epiphytic, and maintains an independent existence without parasitic tendencies. In terms of dimensions, the plant reaches a maximum height of approximately 7 meters, with an observed mean height of 17.5 meters depending on environmental conditions and developmental stage.
