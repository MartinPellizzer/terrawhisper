Halopeplis perfoliata

Halopepliis perfoliata, commonly known as the salt-tolerant halophyte, is a resilient medicinal plant found in saline coastal environments. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Research suggests its bioactive compounds may possess antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Halopeplis perfoliata
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Halopeplis and the species name Halopepliis perfoliata.
