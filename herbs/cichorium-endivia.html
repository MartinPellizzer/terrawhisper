Cichorium endivia

Cichorium endivia, commonly known as endive or chicory, is a versatile leafy green prized for its bitter flavor and nutritional density. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, this medicinal plant supports digestive health and liver function. It serves as both a culinary staple and a traditional herbal remedy in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Cichorium endivia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae, it is further classified under the genus Cichorium.
