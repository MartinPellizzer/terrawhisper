Sanguisorba officinalis

Sanguisorba officinalis, commonly known as sanguisorba or burnet, is a perennial herb valued for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned for its astringent qualities, this plant has been utilized traditionally to treat gastrointestinal issues and bleeding. Its nutrient-rich roots and leaves offer significant therapeutic potential in holistic herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Sanguisorba officinalis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Sanguisorbia.
