Great Burnet, scientifically known as Sanguisorbia officinalis, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism for its astringent properties. Characterized by its distinctive reddish flowers, this plant contains high tannin levels, making it a potent remedy for digestive issues, skin irritations, and supporting overall mucosal health and vitality.
