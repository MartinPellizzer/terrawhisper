Isodon parvifolius

Isodon parvifolius, commonly known as the small-leafed isodon, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional practices. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers significant therapeutic potential. This resilient species is studied for its bioactive compounds, which may provide various health benefits, ranging from anti-inflammatory to antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Isodon parvifolius
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Isodon parvifolius, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Isodon.
