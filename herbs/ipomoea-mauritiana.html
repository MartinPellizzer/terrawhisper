The physiology of Ipomoea Mauritiana is characterized by a C3 photosynthetic pathway, meaning it utilizes the Calvin cycle to fix carbon dioxide during the light-independent reactions, a common metabolic strategy for many members of the Convolvulaceae family. Consequently, its growth and physiological vigor are heavily dependent on the availability of exogenous nitrogen sources within the soil matrix.
