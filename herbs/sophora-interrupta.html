Sophora interrupta

Sophora interrupta, commonly known as the sandhill broom, is a resilient shrub native to the coastal dunes of Florida. Renowned for its striking yellow flowers and ability to thrive in nutrient-poor sandy soils, this medicinal plant has historically been utilized in traditional folk remedies for its unique biological properties.
