Pontic rhododendron, scientifically known as Rhododendron ponticum, is a hardy evergreen shrub native to Europe and North Africa. While valued for its fragrant, bell-shaped flowers, it contains toxic alkaloids like grayanotoxin. This potent plant is often studied for its medicinal properties, though careful handling is essential due to toxicity.
