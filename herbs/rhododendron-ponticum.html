Pontic rhododendron, scientifically known as Rhododendron ponticum, is a hardy evergreen shrub native to Europe and North Africa. While valued for its fragrant, bell-shaped flowers, it contains toxic alkaloids like grayanotoxin. This potent plant is often studied for its medicinal properties, though careful handling is essential due to toxicity.

Plant Parts

This plant, Rhododendron ponticum, possesses several distinct morphological features, though it is most notably recognized for its ability to produce copious amounts of sweet nectar within its bell-shaped, pinkish-white flowers. While the leaves are leathery, evergreen, and contain various toxic alkaloids, the nectar serves as a vital attractant for pollinators such as bees. Beyond the floral components, the plant features woody stems and branches that form dense, sprawling shrubs, making it a prominent feature in various woodland ecosystems.
