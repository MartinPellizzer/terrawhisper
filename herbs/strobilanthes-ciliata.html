Strobilanthes ciliata

Strobilanthes ciliata, commonly known as Karisalaksham in Ayurveda, is a significant medicinal shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Widely distributed across India, this plant is renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial activities, making it a vital component in traditional remedies for managing various systemic ailments and infections.

  • Scientific name: Strobilanthes ciliata
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Strobilanthes.
