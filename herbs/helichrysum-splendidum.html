Cape Gold, scientifically known as Helicrysum splendidum, is a breathtaking perennial herb native to South Africa's fynbos biome. Renowned for its vibrant, sun-kissed yellow blooms, this resilient plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its soothing properties and potential antioxidant benefits, making it a true botanical treasure.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. Moving into East Tropical Africa, the species is also documented within the borders of Tanzania. Its presence extends further south into the South Tropical Africa region, where it grows in Angola.
