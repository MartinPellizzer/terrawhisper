Helichrysum splendidum

Helichrysum splendidum, commonly known as the splendid everlasting, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic potential. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this species contains potent bioactive compounds. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support skin health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum splendidum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Helichrysum.
