Parkia timoriana

Parkia timoriana, commonly known as the Siamese broad bean, is a significant tropical legume valued for its medicinal and nutritional properties. Widely distributed across Southeast Asia, this versatile tree provides nutrient-rich pods and bark, traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation, digestive issues, and infections.

  • Scientific name: Parkia timoriana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae, specifically categorized under the genus Parkia.
