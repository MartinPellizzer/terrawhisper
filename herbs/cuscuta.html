Dodder, scientifically known as Cuscuta, is a genus of parasitic plants characterized by their leafless, thread-like stems. Lacking chlorophyll, these vines derive essential nutrients from host plants. Beyond its ecological role, Cuscuta holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, offering various bioactive compounds used to treat diverse ailments.
