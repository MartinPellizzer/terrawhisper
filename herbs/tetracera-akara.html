Tetracera akara, scientifically known as Tetracera akara, is a medicinal shrub native to the tropical regions of South Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly Ayurveda, it is prized for its potent bioactive compounds. This plant is frequently utilized to manage inflammation, treat skin ailments, and support overall vitality.
