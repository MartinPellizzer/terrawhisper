Yellow Azalea, scientifically known as Rhododendron luteum, is a striking deciduous shrub celebrated for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped yellow blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used in traditional medicine, it possesses unique bioactive compounds, though its potency requires careful handling and expert knowledge.
