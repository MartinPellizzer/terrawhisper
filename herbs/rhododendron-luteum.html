Rhododendron luteum

Rhododendron luteum, commonly known as the yellow azalea, is a striking deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its brilliant yellow blooms, this medicinal plant has historically been used in traditional practices. It contains various bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic benefits, though careful handling is required due to toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron luteum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Ericales and is a member of the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Rhododendron.
