Selaginella monospora

What's the taxonomical classification of Selaginella monospora?

Selaginella monospora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Lycopodiidae and is further organized into the order Selaginellales. This plant is a representative of the family Selaginellaceae and is situated within the genus Selaginella, specifically identified by its species name, monospora.
