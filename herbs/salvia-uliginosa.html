Salvia uliginosa

Salvia uliginosa, commonly known as the willow-leaved sage, is a distinctive perennial herb native to various tropical regions. Characterized by its narrow, lanceolate leaves and aromatic properties, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional ethnobotany for its potential therapeutic applications, including its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory qualities in herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Salvia uliginosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is a member of the Lamiaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Salvia.
