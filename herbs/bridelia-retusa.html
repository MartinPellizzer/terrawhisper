Bridelia retusa

Bridelia retusa, commonly known as the "Gatit" or "Wild Bridelia," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and roots are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Bridelia retusa
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Bridelia retusa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Phyllanthaceae and falls under the genus Bridelia.
