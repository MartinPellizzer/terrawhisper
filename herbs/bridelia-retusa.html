Bridelia retusa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. This plant is further categorized into the family Phyllanthaceae, within which it is placed in the genus Bridelia. Finally, its specific identification is completed by its species name, retusa.

What are the pharmacological activities of Bridelia retusa?

According to a study published by "Frontiers in microbiology", the leaf extracts of Bridelia retusa were used for the sustainable synthesis and characterization of biocompatible silver nanoparticles (AgNPs).
