Androsace filiformis

What's the taxonomical classification of Androsace filiformis?

Androsace filiformis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, specifically situated within the genus Androsace, with its unique designation as the species filiformis.
