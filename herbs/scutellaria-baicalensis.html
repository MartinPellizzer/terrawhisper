Scutellaria baicalensis

Scutellaria baicalensis, commonly known as Baikal skullcap, is a revered medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly baicalin, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine for its powerful anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and neuroprotective properties, offering significant potential in modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria baicalensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Scutellaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a notable geographical presence across diverse regions of the Eurasian continent. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Germany. Its distribution extends significantly into the vast territories of Siberia. Specifically, it is located in the regions of Buryatiya, Chita, and Irkutsk. Additionally, the plant's range reaches even further north into Yakutiya.
