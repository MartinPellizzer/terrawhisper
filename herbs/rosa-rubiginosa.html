R. rubiginosa

Rosa rubiginosa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (234 studies) · ★★★★★

R. rubiginosa, scientifically known as Rosa rubiginosa, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to the mountainous regions of Europe and Asia. Valued for its vibrant pink blossoms and nutrient-rich hips, this species is widely utilized in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and immune-boosting therapeutic properties.
