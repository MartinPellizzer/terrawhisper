Rosa rubiginosa

Rosa rubiginosa, commonly known as the rusty rose, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to South America. Valued for its vibrant blooms and nutrient-rich hips, this medicinal plant is traditionally used to boost immunity, support digestive health, and provide antioxidant benefits, making it a staple in natural herbal remedies.

  • Family: Rosaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is identified by the genus Rosa.
