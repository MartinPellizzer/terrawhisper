Ougeinia oojeinensis

Ougeinia oojeinensis, commonly known as the Oojein medicinal plant, is a rare species valued in traditional herbal medicine. Native to specific high-altitude regions, it possesses unique bioactive compounds. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential therapeutic properties, particularly for its antioxidant capabilities and its role in supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ougeinia oojeinensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Ougeinia, and its species name, Ougeinia oojeinensis.

Common Names

This plant, Ougeinia oojeinensis, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In Chinese, it is commonly referred to as 乌金木 (Wūjīnmù), a name that highlights its precious qualities, while in the local dialect of the region, it is called 乌金 (Wūjīn).

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing throughout the diverse landscapes of India. Additionally, its presence extends into the mountainous terrain of Nepal. The species is also documented within the specific ecological zones of the West Himalaya. Furthermore, its geographical range includes the territory of Pakistan and the state of Assam.
