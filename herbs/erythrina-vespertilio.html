Erythrina vespertilio

Erythrina vespertilio, commonly known as the Coral Tree, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant blossoms, it possesses significant therapeutic properties. Various parts of the tree are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive disturbances.
