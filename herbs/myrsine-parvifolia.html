Myrsine parvifolia

Myrsine parvifolia, commonly known as small-leaved myrsine, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to various subtropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties, including antioxidant and antimicrobial effects. Its leaves and stems are frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat various minor ailments and inflammations.

  • Scientific name: Myrsine parvifolia
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is a member of the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Myrsine.
