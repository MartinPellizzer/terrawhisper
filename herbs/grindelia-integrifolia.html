Puget Sound gumweed, scientifically known as Grindelia integrifolia, is a resilient perennial herb native to Western North America. Renowned for its sticky, resinous leaves, this plant has long been utilized in traditional medicine. It is primarily valued for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and expectorant properties in treating respiratory ailments.
