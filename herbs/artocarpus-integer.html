Artocarpus integer

Artocarpus integer, commonly known as Cempedak, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree native to Southeast Asia. Beyond its delicious, aromatic pulp, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its various therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized to support digestive health and possesses significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits.

  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
