Mitragyna parvifolia

Mitragyna parvifolia, commonly known as Katemfe or African Mitragyna, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally used to treat fever, inflammation, and digestive issues. Modern research continues to explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Mitragyna parvifolia
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Mitragyna parvifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Gentianales. Finally, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae and the genus Mitragyna.
