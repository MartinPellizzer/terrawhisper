Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions within the southern portion of the continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving toward the southern reaches, it can be found throughout Botswana. The species also extends into the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. Finally, its geographical range includes the mountainous terrain of Lesotho.

Plant Parts

This plant, Entada elephantina, is a woody climber that utilizes various botanical structures for its growth and medicinal properties. Its large, compound leaves are essential for photosynthesis and contribute to the plant's overall vitality. While some climbing species rely on complex underground systems, the medicinal value of this specific plant is often concentrated in the roots, which are harvested for traditional treatments. Unlike plants that spread through fleshy rhizomes, this species focuses its energy on its primary root system to anchor itself.
