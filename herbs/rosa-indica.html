Rosa indica, scientifically known as Rosa indica, is a celebrated medicinal plant renowned for its therapeutic versatility. Often referred to as the Indian rose, it boasts fragrant blooms used in traditional Ayurveda to soothe inflammation, improve skin health, and reduce stress, making it a cornerstone of holistic wellness and aromatherapy.

Distribution

Plant Parts

This plant, Rosa indica, offers various therapeutic benefits through its different anatomical components. The roots are often utilized in traditional medicine for their astringent properties and to help balance bodily functions. Additionally, the fragrant flower petals are widely harvested to create infusions, oils, and topical applications, valued for their soothing effects and ability to calm the nervous system.
