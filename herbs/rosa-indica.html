Rosa indica

Rosa indica, commonly known as the Indian rose, is a cherished medicinal plant celebrated for its therapeutic versatility. Renowned for its fragrant blooms, it is used in traditional medicine to soothe skin, reduce inflammation, and alleviate stress. Its petals and oils serve as powerful antioxidants in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Rosa indica
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions of Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species is also naturally distributed throughout Eastern Asia, specifically including Taiwan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the plant is commonly found in Bangladesh. Finally, its distribution extends across the vast landscape of India.
