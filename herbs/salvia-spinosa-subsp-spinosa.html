Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across several distinct regions. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented within the territories of Czechia and Slovakia. Its distribution extends into Northern Africa, where it can be found in both Egypt and Libya. Moving toward Central Asia, the species is also established in Kazakhstan. Finally, its reach continues through the mountainous landscapes of Kirgizstan.
