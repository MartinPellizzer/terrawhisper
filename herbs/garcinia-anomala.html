Garcinia anomala

Garcinia anomala, commonly known as the wild mangosteen, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Its fruits and bark contain bioactive compounds valued for their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory potential.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia anomala
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Garcinia anomala, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Clusiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Garcinia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range primarily concentrated within the Asian continent. In China, its distribution is noted in the South-Central and Southeast regions.
