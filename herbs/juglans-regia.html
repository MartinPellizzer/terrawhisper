Juglans regia

Juglans regia, commonly known as the Persian or English walnut, is a majestic deciduous tree prized for its nutrient-dense seeds. Beyond culinary uses, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, offering potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and neuroprotective properties that support cardiovascular health and cognitive function through its rich bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Juglans regia
  • Family: Juglandaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Juglandaceae. It is further identified by the genus Juglans, specifically as the species Juglans regia.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence is noted in Denmark and the islands of Great Britain and Ireland.
