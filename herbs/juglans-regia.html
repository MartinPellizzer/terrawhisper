common walnut

Juglans regia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1077 studies) · ★★★★★

Common walnut, scientifically known as Juglans regia, is a majestic deciduous tree revered for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Beyond its edible kernels, its leaves and hulls contain potent bioactive compounds, including tannins and polyphenols, offering significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits used in traditional and modern herbal medicine.

The reproduction of Juglans regia is characterized by a sexual process, as asexual reproduction is notably absent.
