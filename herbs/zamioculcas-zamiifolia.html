Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in the countries of Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern regions, the species also inhabits parts of South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in Malawi and Mozambique. Finally, its geographical range extends into the territory of Zimbabwe.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the ZZ plant, is characterized by its striking and glossy leaf structures that grow in a pinnate arrangement along thick, fleshy stems. Each individual leaf is composed of multiple small, dark green leaflets that possess a waxy coating, allowing the plant to retain moisture efficiently. These durable leaves are the primary site for photosynthesis and are highly valued for their aesthetic appeal in indoor environments.
