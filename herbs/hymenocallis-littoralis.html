Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Hymenocallis littoralis, is a bulbous perennial characterized by a distinct anatomy designed for survival in moist environments. Its primary energy storage organ is a fleshy bulb, which serves as the foundation from which the plant grows and also contains vital medicinal properties. From this bulb, a sturdy stem emerges, supporting the plant's structural integrity and lifting the foliage toward the light. The leaves are long, strap-like, and succulent, providing the necessary surface area for photosynthesis. Below the surface, a reliable root system anchors the plant firmly into the substrate. The most striking feature, however, is the flower, which displays elegant, trumpet-shaped blooms that are often used in various traditional applications.
