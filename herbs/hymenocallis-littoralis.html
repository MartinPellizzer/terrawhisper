Hymenocallis littoralis

Spider lily

Hymenocallis littoralis, commonly known as the Beach Spider Lily, is a stunning perennial prized for its fragrant, white blooms. While primarily ornamental, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest. In various traditional practices, its extracts are explored for potential medicinal properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities, showcasing its diverse biological utility.

  • Scientific name: Hymenocallis littoralis
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Amaryllidaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Hymenocallis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found within the borders of Tunisia. Its range also extends to the Macaronesian islands, specifically in Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species is widespread throughout West Tropical Africa. This tropical presence includes the nations of Gambia, Guinea-Bissau, and Guinea.

Plant Parts

The leaves are long, strap-like, and succulent, providing the necessary surface area for photosynthesis. Below the surface, a reliable root system anchors the plant firmly into the substrate.
