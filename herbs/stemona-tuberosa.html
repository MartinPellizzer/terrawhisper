Stemona, scientifically known as Stemona tuberosa, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, its tuberous roots contain potent alkaloids used to treat respiratory ailments, parasites, and inflammatory conditions. This medicinal plant remains a significant subject in pharmacological research for its diverse therapeutic properties.

Plant Parts

This plant, Stemona tuberosa, possesses several distinct anatomical features that contribute to its medicinal properties. The most significant part utilized for pharmacological purposes is the underground portion, which consists of thickened, fleshy roots that develop into specialized, starch-rich root tubers. These tuberous structures serve as the primary storage organs for the plant's active alkaloids. Above the ground, the plant also produces delicate flowers, which represent the reproductive component of its life cycle.
