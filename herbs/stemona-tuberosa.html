Stemona tuberosa

Stemona tuberosa, commonly known as Stemona, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Native to various Asian regions, its tuberous roots contain potent alkaloids. These bioactive compounds offer diverse therapeutic potential, ranging from anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties to applications in treating respiratory ailments and parasitic infections.

  • Scientific name: Stemona tuberosa
  • Family: Stemonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Pandanales and the family Stemonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Stemona, specifically the species Stemona tuberosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its distribution spans from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast regions. It is also found growing in the tropical environment of Hainan. Moving eastward, the species is documented across Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Additionally, its presence extends to the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the Assam region.
