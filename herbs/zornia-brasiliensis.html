Zornia brasiliensis

Zornia brasiliensis, commonly known as Brazilian zornia, is a versatile legume native to tropical regions. Renowned for its ecological significance, this plant serves as an excellent green manure and forage. Beyond agriculture, its unique phytochemical properties offer promising medicinal potential, particularly in supporting traditional healing practices and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Zornia brasiliensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Fabales and placed in the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Zornia, specifically identified as Zornia brasiliensis.
