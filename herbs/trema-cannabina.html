What's the taxonomical classification of Trema cannabina?

Trema cannabina belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the hierarchical structure of biological classification, it is placed in the order Rosales and belongs to the family Cannabaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific taxonomic designation within the genus Trema, resulting in its scientific name, Trema cannabina.
