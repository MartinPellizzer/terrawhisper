Dimetia scandens

Dimetia scandens, commonly known as the climbing shrub, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is often utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its unique chemical composition offers exciting potential for modern pharmacological research and holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Dimetia scandens
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Dimetia scandens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family and is categorized under the genus Dimetia.

Common Names

This plant, Dimentia scandens, is known by various names depending on the linguistic context. In Chinese (zh), it is commonly referred to as 凉喉茶, while in both Simplified Chinese (zh-cn) and Traditional Chinese (zh-tw), it is known as 藤耳草.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within parts of Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found across several distinct territories including India and Bangladesh. Furthermore, the species is distributed through the Assam region and the East Himalaya. Together, these locations define the natural habitat and spread of Dimitia scandens.
