Melicope pteleifolia

Melicope ptrileifolia, commonly known as the "false sandalwood," is a significant medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in various cultures to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its leaves and bark offer valuable pharmacological potential in modern herbal medicine.
