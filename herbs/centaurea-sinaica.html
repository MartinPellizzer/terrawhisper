Centaurea sinaica

Centaurea sinaica, commonly known as Sinai Centaury, is a rare medicinal herb native to the Sinai Peninsula. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, particularly for treating digestive ailments and inflammation. Its unique botanical profile makes it a subject of interest for pharmacological research and conservation.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa
