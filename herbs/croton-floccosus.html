Croton floccosus

Croton floccosus, commonly known as the "Pua" in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering a natural remedy rooted in deep ethnobotanical heritage and healing.

  • Scientific name: Croton floccosus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Croton.
