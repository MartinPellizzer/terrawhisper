Gliricidia maculata

Gliriciidia maculata, commonly known as the Spotted Gliriciidia, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient shrub offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic health and natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Gliricidia maculata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gliricidia.
