Tilia

Tilia, commonly known as Linden or Lime tree, is a revered medicinal plant celebrated for its soothing properties. Its fragrant blossoms are frequently brewed into calming teas, effective for easing anxiety, reducing fever, and improving sleep. This versatile botanical serves as a gentle, natural remedy for various stress-related ailments.

  • Scientific name: Tilia
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae, it is classified under the genus Tilia.
