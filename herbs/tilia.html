Basswood genus, scientifically known as Tilia, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its soothing properties. Often used in traditional herbalism, its fragrant flowers are brewed into calming teas to relieve anxiety, tension, and digestive discomfort. This gentle botanical serves as a natural remedy for promoting relaxation and sleep.
