Ceylon slitwort, scientifically known as Leucas zeylanica, is a versatile medicinal herb widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Primarily found in tropical regions, this plant is renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, making it a valuable natural remedy for treating various skin ailments and infections.

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in both Assam and Bangladesh. This plant, Leucas zeylanica, is a medicinal herb where the primary therapeutic value is often derived from its aerial parts. These above-ground components, which include the green stems, leaves, and small white flowers, are extensively utilized in traditional medicine to prepare various formulations. Because the active phytochemicals are concentrated within these aerial parts, they are frequently collected, dried, and processed to treat various ailments, making them the most significant portion of the plant for pharmacological applications.
