The mango genus, scientifically known as Mangifera, is a group of tropical evergreen trees celebrated globally for their nutritious fruits. Beyond culinary use, Mangifera species possess significant medicinal properties, utilized in traditional healing to combat inflammation, oxidative stress, and digestive issues through their bioactive leaves, bark, and fruit components.
