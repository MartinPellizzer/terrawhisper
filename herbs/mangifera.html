Mangifera

The mango genus

Mangifera, commonly known as the mango plant, is a tropical powerhouse celebrated for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Beyond its delicious fruit, various parts—including leaves, bark, and seeds—offer potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits. Traditionally used in diverse cultures, it remains a vital subject in modern herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Mangifera
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is part of the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Sapindales, and the family Anacardiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified under the genus Mangifera.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally in the nation of Burkina. Additionally, the species is documented in the coastal and inland areas of Gambia. Its presence extends further into Guinea-Bissau and the country of Guinea. Finally, the distribution includes the territory of Senegal, completing its regional range.
