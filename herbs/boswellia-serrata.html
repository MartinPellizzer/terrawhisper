Boswellia serrata

Boswellia glabra

Boswellia serrata, commonly known as Indian Frankincense, is a revered medicinal plant prized for its resinous gum. Rich in boswellic acids, it offers potent anti-inflammatory benefits, making it a cornerstone in traditional Ayurvedic medicine for managing joint health, arthritis, and various inflammatory conditions through natural, therapeutic healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Boswellia serrata
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Burseraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Boswellia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Indian frankincense, is highly valued for the therapeutic properties found within its various anatomical components. The primary medicinal interest lies in the oleo-gum resin, which is harvested from the stem and bark of the tree. When the bark is wounded, the tree produces a viscous gum resin exudate as a healing response. This substance, often referred to simply as resin, is collected in its crude form and can be processed into a purified gum resin used extensively in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions.
