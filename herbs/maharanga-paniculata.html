Maharanga paniculata

Maharanga paniculata, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from inflammation to digestive issues. Understanding its botanical characteristics and healing potential is essential for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Maharanga paniculata
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Maharanga, and species, paniculata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within the country of China, it is primarily found in the South-Central areas. Furthermore, its presence extends into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. In the Indian Subcontinent, the species is specifically located within the East Himalaya range. These distinct locations define the natural habitat where the plant can be discovered.
