Pittosporum senacia subsp. senacia

Pittosporum senacia senacia

Pittosporum senacia subsp. senacia, commonly known as the Cheesewood, is an evergreen shrub native to Australia. Valued for its fragrant, cream-colored flowers and glossy foliage, it holds traditional medicinal significance. Historically, various parts of the plant have been utilized for their bioactive properties in treating diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Pittosporum senacia subsp. senacia
  • Family: Pittosporaceae
  • Native range: Africa
