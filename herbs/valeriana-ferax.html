Valeriana ferax

Valeriana foetida, commonly known as stinking valerian, is a perennial herb renowned for its distinctive, pungent aroma. Native to mountainous regions, this plant has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries. It is primarily valued for its sedative and antispasmodic properties, helping to alleviate anxiety and promote restful sleep.

  • Scientific name: Valeriana ferax
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
