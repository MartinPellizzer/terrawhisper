Kingdonia uniflora

Kingdonia uniflora, commonly known as the single-flowered kingdonia, is a rare, perennial herb native to the high-altitude forests of China. Renowned for its delicate, solitary white flowers and distinctive rhizomes, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional practices for its potential therapeutic properties and unique botanical significance.

  • Family: Circaeasteraceae
