Papaver

Poppies

Papaver, commonly known as the poppy, is a genus of flowering plants renowned for its striking blooms and profound medicinal significance. Historically utilized for pain relief and sedation, its alkaloids serve as vital precursors in pharmacology. Beyond their aesthetic beauty, these plants remain central to modern anesthetic and analgesic studies.

  • Scientific name: Papaver
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Papaveraceae and falls under the genus Papaver.
