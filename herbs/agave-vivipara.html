Plant Parts

This plant, Agave vivipara, is characterized by its unique reproductive strategy, which often reduces the typical reliance on traditional fruits. While many members of the Agave genus produce capsules containing seeds, Agave vivipara is famously known for its viviparous nature, meaning it frequently produces small plantlets or offsets directly on its flowering stalk rather than developing mature, seed-bearing fruits. When the plant does follow a sexual reproductive cycle, the resulting structures serve as the vessel for seed dispersal, though the production of vegetative clones remains its most distinctive botanical feature.
