Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. In Central Asia, its presence is specifically noted within Kyrgyzstan. The species is widely found throughout China, including the South-Central regions, Southeast areas, and the island of Hainan. Additionally, its range extends into Eastern Asia, where it can be found in Japan. This diverse spread highlights the plant's ability to inhabit various ecological zones across the continent.

Plant Parts

This plant, Allium chinense, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties through its specialized underground structures. The therapeutic components are concentrated in the bulbs, which serve as the primary storage organs for the plant's nutrients and active compounds. When processed for traditional use, the plant is often prepared using the dry bulbs, ensuring that the concentrated essence is preserved for various healing applications.
