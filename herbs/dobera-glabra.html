Dobera glabra

Dobera glabra, commonly known as the smooth-leaved Dobera, is a significant medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation and infections. This botanical specimen offers valuable bioactive compounds essential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Dobera glabra
  • Family: Salvadoraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Brassicales and placed in the family Salvadoraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dobera, specifically the species Dobera glabra.

Common Names

This plant, Dobera glabra, is known by various names depending on the language and region. In Swedish, it is referred to as Garas, while in Arabic, it is called ضبر أجرد. Similarly, in the Arz dialect, it is identified as ضبر اجرد. In Chinese, the plant is commonly known as 苏丹香 (zh) or 苏丹香 (zh-hans).

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the Northeast Tropical Africa region. Its distribution specifically includes the nation of Djibouti. It is also naturally occurring in Eritrea and Ethiopia. Furthermore, the species can be located throughout Somalia. Finally, its range extends through both Sudan and South Sudan.
