Cereus fernambucensis

Cereus fernambucessis, commonly known as the fernambucan cereus, is a remarkable cactus species native to Brazil. Renowned for its impressive climbing habit and nocturnal blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its unique therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Cereus fernambucensis
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Cactaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cereus.
