Chenopodium quinoa

Quinua

Chenopodium quinoa, commonly known as quinoa, is a nutrient-dense pseudocereal celebrated globally for its exceptional nutritional profile. Beyond its dietary importance as a complete protein source, this resilient plant offers significant medicinal potential, containing bioactive compounds with antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties essential for holistic wellness and health.

  • Scientific name: Chenopodium quinoa
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae and the genus Chenopodium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Chenopodium quinoa, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. In German, it is referred to as Quinoa, while in Arabic, it is called كينوا. In the Aymara language, the plant is known as Jiwra, and it is widely called quinoa in Catalan. Additionally, in Czech, it is identified as merlík čilský.
