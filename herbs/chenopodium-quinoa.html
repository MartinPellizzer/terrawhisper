This plant, Chenopodium quinoa, is primarily valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties, which are concentrated in its seeds. Because the seed is so versatile, the plant is cultivated globally, with the harvested seeds serving as a staple food source that offers significant health benefits to consumers.
