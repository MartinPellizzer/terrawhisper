Carlina diae

Carlina diae, commonly known as the Dia's carline thistle, is a rare, perennial medicinal herb endemic to the high-altitude regions of the Pyrenees. Renowned for its unique botanical features, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties and potent bioactive compounds.
