Uncaria callophylla, scientifically known as Uncaria callophylla, is a significant medicinal plant belonging to the Rubiaceae family. Often recognized as a variety of cat's claw, it is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive alkaloids, which offer powerful anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and immune-boosting properties for holistic healing.
