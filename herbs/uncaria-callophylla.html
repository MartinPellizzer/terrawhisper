Uncaria callophylla

Uncaria callophylla, commonly known as a species of cat's claw, is a medicinal vine native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent bioactive alkaloids, it is traditionally used to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote joint health, serving as a vital resource in holistic botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Uncaria callophylla
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family. Its taxonomic classification concludes with its placement in the genus Uncaria.
