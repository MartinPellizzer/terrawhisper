Teucrium leucocladum

Teucrium leucocladum, commonly known as white germander, is a significant medicinal shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat respiratory ailments, inflammatory conditions, and skin disorders, offering valuable therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Teucrium.
