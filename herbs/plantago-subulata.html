Scientifically known as Plantago subulata, is a resilient perennial herb often called carpetweed. Native to various regions, this low-growing plant is characterized by its lance-shaped leaves and small white flowers. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, it is frequently utilized for its soothing properties and potential anti-inflammatory benefits.
