Plantago subulata

Plantaago subulata, commonly known as slingleaf or narrowleaf plantain, is a resilient perennial herb found in various habitats. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat skin irritations, minor wounds, and inflammation. Its small, lanceolate leaves contain bioactive compounds essential for natural healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Plantago subulata
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Plantago subulata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Plantago.
