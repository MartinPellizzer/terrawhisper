This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several key regions within the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its distribution includes the nation of Gambia as well as Sierra Leone. It is also documented to grow within the borders of Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found throughout the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Finally, its range extends further inland to encompass the Democratic Republic of the Congo.

Plant Parts

This plant, Bauhinia purpurea, utilizes various anatomical structures to support its medicinal properties. The extensive roots anchor the tree into the soil while absorbing essential nutrients and water. The sturdy stems provide a strong structural framework, branching out to support the heavy canopy. From these stem structures, numerous leaves emerge, characterized by their distinct two-lobed shape. Each individual leaf serves as a vital site for photosynthesis, and it is often from these green surfaces that many therapeutic compounds are harvested for traditional healing.
