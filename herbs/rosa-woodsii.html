Rosa woodsii

Rosa woodsii, commonly known as Woods' rose, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to western North America. Renowned for its delicate pink blossoms and fragrant petals, this hardy species thrives in diverse habitats. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, it holds significant traditional value for its nutritional and medicinal properties in herbalism.

  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Rosa.
