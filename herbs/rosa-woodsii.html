Woods' rose, scientifically known as Rosa woodsii, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to the arid regions of Western North America. Renowned for its delicate pink blooms and vitamin-rich hips, this hardy species holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering both aesthetic beauty and traditional medicinal applications in diverse ecosystems.
