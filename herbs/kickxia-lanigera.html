Kickxia lanigera

Kickxia lanigera, commonly known as woolly kickxia, is a charming, low-growing herbaceous plant native to various regions. Characterized by its soft, hairy foliage and delicate, trumpet-shaped flowers, it serves as a fascinating subject for botanical study, offering unique medicinal potential and aesthetic value within diverse ecological landscapes.
