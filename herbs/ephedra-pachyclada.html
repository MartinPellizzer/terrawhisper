Plant Parts

This plant, Ephedra pachyclada, is characterized by its unique morphology where the traditional concept of a leaf is highly modified. In this species, the leaf is reduced to tiny, scale-like structures that are tightly pressed against the green, photosynthetic stems. Because the leaves are so diminutive, they play a minimal role in photosynthesis, allowing the succulent-like branches to perform most of the plant's energy production.
