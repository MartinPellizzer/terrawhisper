Podanthus ovatifolius

Podanthus ovatifolius, commonly known as "Pau-de-alho" in Brazil, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Atlantic Forest. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, this aromatic plant is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and various digestive issues through diverse preparations.
