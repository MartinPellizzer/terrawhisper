Persicaria viscosa, is a creeping perennial herb belonging to the Polygonaceae family. Characterized by its sticky, glandular hairs and small pink flowers, this plant thrives in moist habitats. Traditionally used in various folk medicines, it is valued for its antimicrobial properties and its potential role in treating skin ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found specifically within the Khabarovsk and Primorye regions. Its distribution extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the Manchuria area. Furthermore, the species is widely recorded throughout China North-Central and China South-Central. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in different ecological zones across the continent.
