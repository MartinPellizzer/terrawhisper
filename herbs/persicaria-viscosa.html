Persicaria viscosa

Persicaria viscosa, commonly known as sticky knotweed, is a distinctive annual herb characterized by its viscid, glandular stems. Native to tropical regions, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Its unique adhesive texture and resilient growth make it a fascinating subject for ethnobotanical study.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria viscosa
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Persicaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found specifically within the Khabarovsk and Primorye regions. Its distribution extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the Manchuria area. Furthermore, the species is widely recorded throughout China North-Central and China South-Central. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in different ecological zones across the continent.
