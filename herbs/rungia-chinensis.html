Rungia chinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Rungia chinensis?

Rungia chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is defined by its specific classification within the genus Rungia, with the distinct species designation chinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rungia chinensis has perennial, creeping rhizomes that produce oppositely arranged, ovate to elliptic leaves with entire margins and acute tips. The leaf texture is typically slightly fleshy, and the surface can range from smooth to somewhat pubescent depending on the specific cultivar. During the flowering season, the plant produces small, tubular flowers that emerge from the leaf axils. These blossoms are often white or pale pink and are characteristic of the Asteraceae family. The overall growth habit remains low-growing, making it an effective groundcover for shaded environments.
