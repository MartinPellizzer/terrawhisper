Psiloxylon mauritianum

Psiloxylon mauritianum, commonly known as the Brazilian rosewood or pau-rosa, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Atlantic Forest. Renowned for its aromatic properties, its essential oils possess potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities, making it a valuable resource in traditional healing and modern pharmacological research for skin treatments.

  • Scientific name: Psiloxylon mauritianum
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa
