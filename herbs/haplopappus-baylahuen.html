Haplopappus baylahuen

Haplopappus baylahuen, commonly known as baylahuen, is a significant medicinal shrub native to Chile. Valued in traditional herbalism, it is primarily utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory and digestive properties. This aromatic plant serves as a vital natural remedy, offering therapeutic benefits for various ailments within South American folk medicine.
