White walnut

Juglans cinerea · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (424 studies) · ★★★★★

White walnut, scientifically known as Juglans cinerea, is a deciduous tree native to North America, valued for its medicinal properties. Historically, its bark has been utilized in traditional remedies to treat skin conditions and digestive issues. Today, it remains a significant subject of study for its complex chemical compounds.
