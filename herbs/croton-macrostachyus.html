Croton macrostachyus

Croton macrostachyus, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, offers a wealth of therapeutic potential. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments. Exploring its pharmacological properties provides vital insights into developing natural treatments for modern healthcare challenges.

  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Malpighiales and is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Croton, which includes the species Croton macrostachyus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Croton macrostachyus, is recognized by various regional names across different languages. In Rwanda, it is called Umurangara (rw), while in Bulgaria, it is referred to as широколистен кротон (bg). It is known as ብሳና (am) in Amharic, and in Greece, its name is Κροτών ο μακροστάχυος (el). Additionally, in the Kikuyu language, it is identified as Mũtũndũ wa Njora (Mũtĩ) (ki).

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its range includes the country of Ghana, where it can be found growing in native habitats. It is also distributed across the coastal and inland areas of Guinea. Furthermore, the species extends its presence into the Ivory Coast and Liberia. Finally, its geographical reach concludes with documented occurrences in Nigeria.
