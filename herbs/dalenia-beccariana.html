Dalenia beccariana

Dalenia beccariana, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, holds significant importance in traditional African medicine. Native to various tropical regions, this plant is frequently utilized for its potent therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Dalenia beccariana
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Dalenia beccariaana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the family Melastomataceae and the genus Dalenia.

Distribution

This plant is characterized by a very specific and restricted geographical range. It is primarily documented within the Malesia floristic region. Specifically, its presence is recorded on the island of Borneo. This limited distribution suggests that the species may be endemic to certain forest habitats in that area. Consequently, its survival is closely tied to the unique ecosystems found in this part of Southeast Asia.
