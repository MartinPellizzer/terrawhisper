Hydrocotyle moschata

Hydrocotyle moschata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (10 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

, scientifically known as Hydrocotyle moschata, is a creeping herbaceous plant valued in traditional medicine. Often referred to as musky pennywort, it thrives in moist, tropical environments. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this medicinal herb is utilized in various cultures to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the world. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented in both Great Britain and Ireland. Its range extends significantly to the Southern Hemisphere, specifically within the islands of New Zealand. Within this area, it can be found throughout New Zealand North as well as the remote Chatham Islands. Additionally, the species is recorded in the Kermadec Islands, completing its scattered global footprint.
