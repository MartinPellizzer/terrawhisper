Asplenium sarelii

What's the taxonomical classification of Asplenium sarelii?

Asplenium sarelii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. The plant is a member of the family Aspleniaceae, falling under the genus Asplenium, and is specifically identified by its species name, sarelii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Asplenium sarelii has large, pinnate fronds characterized by broadly elliptical to lanceolate pinnae that are arranged symmetrically along a dark, stipe-like rachis. The individual leaflets feature entire or slightly wavy margins and a distinctively leathery texture that aids in moisture retention. These fronds emerge from a short, creeping rhizome which provides the structural foundation for the plant's growth pattern. The underside of each leaflet typically hosts small, linear sori arranged in rows near the midrib for spore production. This combination of robust foliage and organized reproductive structures defines its unique morphological profile.
