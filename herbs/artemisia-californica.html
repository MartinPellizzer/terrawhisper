Coastal sagebrush, scientifically known as Artemisia californica, is a perennial shrub native to the California chaparral. Renowned for its aromatic, silver-green foliage, this resilient plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem. Traditionally used in indigenous medicine, it possesses potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties highly valued in herbalism.
