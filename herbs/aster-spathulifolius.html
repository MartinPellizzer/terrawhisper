Aster spathulifolius

Aster spatulifolius, commonly known as the spoon-leaf aster, is a resilient perennial native to various mountainous regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses unique bioactive compounds used to treat inflammatory conditions. Its distinctive foliage and seasonal blooms make it both a botanical interest and a therapeutic natural resource.

  • Scientific name: Aster spathulifolius
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aster spatulifolius, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Its taxonomic identity is finalized within the genus Aster.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is notably recorded within the Primorye territory. Moving further south, the species is also widely distributed throughout Japan. Additionally, its habitat extends into the Korean peninsula.
