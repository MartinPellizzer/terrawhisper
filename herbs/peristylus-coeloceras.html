Peristylus coeloceras

What's the taxonomical classification of Peristylus coeloceras?

Peristylus coeloceras belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Asparagales. This plant is a member of the Orchidaceae family, falling under the genus Peristylus, and is specifically identified as the species coeloceras.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Peristylus coeloceras has a succulent, leafless stem that is cylindrical, ribbed, and reaches heights of up to 30 centimeters. The stem surface is characterized by numerous longitudinal ridges that aid in water storage and structural stability. These stems emerge from a creeping rhizome, which allows the plant to spread across its substrate. Small, scale-like leaves are absent, as the photosynthetic processes are carried out entirely by the green stem tissue. The plant also produces delicate, white, tubular flowers that bloom from the stem segments.
