Spondias tuberosa

Spondias tuberosa, commonly known as the yellow mombin or hog plum, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its nutrient-rich fruit and therapeutic bark, it is traditionally used to treat digestive issues, skin ailments, and inflammation, offering significant pharmacological potential in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Spondias tuberosa
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Spondias, specifically as the species Spondias tuberosa.
