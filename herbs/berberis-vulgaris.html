Berberis vulgaris

Berberis vulgaris, commonly known as barberry, is a deciduous shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Rich in the alkaloid berberine, this plant offers potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant benefits. Historically used to support digestive health and metabolic function, its tart berries and roots remain staples in traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Berberis vulgaris
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is further categorized into the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Berberis.
