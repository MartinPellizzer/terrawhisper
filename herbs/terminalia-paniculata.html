Terminalia paniculata

Terminalia paniculata, commonly known as the terminalia tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, its bark, leaves, and fruits are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia paniculata
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Terminalia, specifically the species Terminalia paniculata.
