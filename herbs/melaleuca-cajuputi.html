Melaleuca cajuputi

Melaleuca cajuputi, commonly known as Cajuputi, is a resilient medicinal plant native to South American wetlands. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it possesses significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antiseptic properties. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and skin infections, this versatile species remains a vital resource in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Melaleuca cajuputi
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Melaleuca.
