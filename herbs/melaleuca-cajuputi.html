Melaleuca cajeputi, scientifically known as Melaleuca cajuputi, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Brazil. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it possesses significant antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and decongestant properties. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and skin infections, this plant remains a vital resource in natural pharmacology and aromatherapy.
