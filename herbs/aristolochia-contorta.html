Scientific literature: Very Sparse (23 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Northern Dutchman's Pipe, scientifically known as Aristolochia contorta, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Characterized by its unique, tubular, brownish-green flowers, it has historically been used in traditional medicine. However, due to potent nephrotoxic compounds, its use is strictly cautioned against in modern clinical practice.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing within the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the region of Inner Mongolia. Additionally, the species is documented throughout the Manchuria area of China. Finally, its range reaches as far as China North-Central, demonstrating its adaptability to diverse environments.
