Northern Dutchman's Pipe

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (23 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Northern Dutchman's Pipe, scientifically known as Aristolochia contorta, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Characterized by its unique, tubular, brownish-green flowers, it has historically been used in traditional medicine. However, due to potent nephrotoxic compounds, its use is strictly cautioned against in modern clinical practice.
