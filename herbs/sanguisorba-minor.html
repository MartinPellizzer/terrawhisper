Sanguisorba minor

Sanguisorbia minor, commonly known as Lesser Bloodroot, is a potent medicinal herb prized for its unique phytochemical properties. Native to temperate forest understories, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal pharmacology to support circulatory health and treat inflammatory skin conditions, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe
