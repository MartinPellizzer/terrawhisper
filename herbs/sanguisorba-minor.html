Salad Burnet

Sanguisorba minor · Accepted scientific name

Salad Burnet, scientifically known as Sanguisorba minor, is a perennial herb cherished for its delicate, trifoliate leaves and small, pinkish flowers. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it possesses astringent properties and has long been valued in culinary applications, offering a subtle, nutty flavor to fresh seasonal salads and garnishes.
