Spiranthera odoratissima

Spiranthera odoratissima, commonly known as the Fragrant Ladies' Tresses, is a delicate perennial herb valued for its exquisite, aromatic blooms. Beyond its aesthetic charm, this plant holds significant traditional importance in herbal medicine, where its unique properties are utilized to support various wellness practices and holistic healing rituals.
