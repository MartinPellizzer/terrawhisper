Neolitsea zeylanica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae, eventually reaching its specific designation within the genus Neolitsea. Ultimately, the plant is identified by its unique species name, zeylanica.
