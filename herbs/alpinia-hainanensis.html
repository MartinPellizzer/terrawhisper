Alpinia hainanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Alpinia hainanensis?

Alpinia hainanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is a member of the order Zingiberales and falls under the family Zingiberaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Alpinia, with its unique designation as the species hainanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Alpinia hainanenensis has large, elliptical leaves that grow in a lanceolate shape with prominent parallel venation and long, slender petioles. The plant develops from a robust rhizomatous stem system that spreads through the soil to support its upright growth. Its inflorescences consist of dense, terminal spikes that produce striking white or pale pink flowers. These blossoms are characterized by prominent, petal-like labella and a delicate, fragrant structure. The overall architecture of the plant is herbaceous and vigorous, typical of the Zingiberaceae family.
