Viscum articulatum

Viscum articulatum, commonly known as the segmented mistletoe, is a fascinating parasitic plant native to various tropical regions. Belonging to the Viscaceae family, it thrives by extracting nutrients from host trees. This species is notable for its unique growth patterns and holds significant interest in traditional ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Viscum articulatum
  • Family: Santalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Santalales, it is situated in the family Santalaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Viscum and the species Viscum articulatum.
