Adenia cardiophylla

What's the taxonomical classification of Adenia cardiophylla?

Adenia cardiophylla belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Malpighiales. This plant is a member of the family Passifloraceae, specifically positioned within the genus Adenia, and is identified by its unique species designation, cardiophylla.
