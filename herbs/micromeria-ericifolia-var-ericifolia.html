Micromeria ericifolia var. ericifolia

Micromeria ericifolia var. ericifolia, commonly known as the Erica-leaved Micromeria, is a resilient shrub native to tropical regions. Distinguished by its narrow, ericoid leaves and delicate flowering clusters, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional ethnobotany for its bioactive properties and potential therapeutic applications in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Micromeria ericifolia var. ericifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Micromeria, specifically as the variety ericifolia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a very specific and restricted geographical range within the Atlantic region. Its distribution is limited to the Macaronesian archipelago, specifically within the Canary Islands. Because it is endemic to this particular area, it plays a unique role in the local flora of the islands. This narrow ecological footprint means that its presence is highly dependent on the unique environmental conditions found in this territory. Consequently, the species is a key component of the natural biodiversity characteristic of the Canarian landscape.
