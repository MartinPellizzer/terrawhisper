Osmanthus fragrans var. aurantiacus

Osmanthus fragrans var. aurantiacus, commonly known as sweet osmanthus, is a highly fragrant evergreen shrub cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its golden blossoms, it is used to harmonize the stomach, clear heat, and soothe the lungs. Its aromatic essence offers both therapeutic benefits and sensory delight in wellness.

  • Scientific name: Osmanthus fragrans var. aurantiacus
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
