Osmanthus fragrans var. aurantiacus

Osmanthus fragrans var. aurantiacus, commonly known as sweet osmanthus, is a highly fragrant evergreen shrub cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its golden blossoms, it is used to harmonize the stomach, clear heat, and soothe the lungs. Its aromatic essence offers both therapeutic benefits and sensory delight in wellness.

  • Scientific name: Osmanthus fragrans var. aurantiacus
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Oleaceae and the genus Osmanthus. Specifically, this variety is identified as Osmanthus fragrans var. aurantiacus.

Common Names

This plant, Osmanthus fragrans var. aurantiacus, is widely recognized across East Asia by several distinct common names reflecting its fragrant orange blossoms. In Japan, it is known as キンモクセイ (kinmokusei), while in Korea, it is referred to as 금목서 (geummokseo). In Chinese-speaking regions, the plant is identified by various names depending on the specific terminology used: it is called 丹桂 (dān guì) in traditional contexts, and is commonly referred to as 金桂花 (jīn guì huā) in both simplified Chinese (zh-hans) and the standard simplified script used in mainland China (zh-cn).
