Teucrium viscidum

Teucrium viscidum, commonly known as sticky germander, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Distinguished by its sticky, glandular leaves and delicate flowering spikes, this plant belongs to the Lamiaceae family. It is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique botanical properties and ecological significance.

  • Scientific name: Teucrium viscidum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Teucrium.
