Adina pilulifera

Adina pilulifera, commonly known as the Pilulifera Adin, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource for developing natural healing treatments and remedies.

  • Scientific name: Adina pilulifera
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, it is further categorized into the family Rubiaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Adina, specifically the species Adina pilulifera.

Common Names

This plant, Adina pilulifera, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Mandarin Chinese, it is referred to as 水团花 (zh/zh-cn), while in Cantonese, it is known as 水團花 (yue). The specific traditional character variation used in Taiwan is 水圑花 (zh-tw), and in Japanese, the plant is called タニワタリノキ (ja).
