Distribution

This plant exhibits a highly disjunct geographical distribution spanning across the Southern Hemisphere. In the Australian region, its presence is specifically limited to the island of Tasmania. Moving to Southern South America, it can be found throughout central and southern Chile, as well as in southern Argentina. The species also extends its range to the Subantarctic Islands, specifically inhabiting the Falkland Islands. Consequently, its habitat is defined by cool, temperate climates across these distant landmasses.
