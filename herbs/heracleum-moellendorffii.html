Heracleum moellendorffii

Heracleum moellendorffii, commonly known as Moellendorff's hogweed, is a distinctive herbaceous perennial native to various Asian regions. Belonging to the Apiaceae family, this plant is characterized by its large, umbellate flower clusters. While often studied for its botanical significance, its specific medicinal applications remain a subject of ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Heracleum moellendorffii
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae and the genus Heracleum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. It can be found growing within the Russian Far East, specifically in the Primorye region. In China, its presence extends through the diverse landscapes of Manchuria and Inner Mongolia. Additionally, the species is widely distributed throughout the North-Central and South-Central parts of the country.
