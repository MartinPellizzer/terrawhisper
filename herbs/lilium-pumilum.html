Lilium pumilum

Lilium pumilum, commonly known as the dwarf lily, is a delicate perennial prized in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its compact stature and vibrant blooms, this medicinal plant is valued for its unique phytochemical properties. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic applications in soothing various systemic inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Lilium pumilum
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Liliales, it is further categorized into the family Liliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Lillium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of Northern Asia. In Siberia, its presence is documented across various territories including Buryatiya and Chita. It can also be found throughout the expansive landscapes of the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions. Beyond the Siberian heartland, the species extends into the Russian Far East. Specifically, its range reaches as far as the Amur region.
