Indian crowfoot grass

Eleusine indica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (36 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Indian crowfoot grass, scientifically known as Eleusine indica, is a resilient annual herb widely distributed across tropical regions. Often categorized as a common weed, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is utilized in various traditional medicine systems for its diverse pharmacological properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as goosegrass, features distinct leaves that are long, narrow, and linear in shape. These leaves grow in an alternate arrangement along the stem and are characterized by their prominent, parallel venation and a slightly rough texture. In medicinal applications, the leaves are often studied for their phytochemical properties and are sometimes used in traditional decoctions or topical preparations.
