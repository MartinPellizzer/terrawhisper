Dragon's blood tree

Dracaena cinnabari · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (13 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Dragon's blood tree, scientifically known as Dracaena cinnabari, is a rare, endemic species native to the Socotra archipelago. Renowned for its striking silhouette and crimson resin, this remarkable plant has been prized for centuries in traditional medicine for its potent antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties across various cultures.

Distribution

This plant is characterized by an extremely limited and specialized geographical range. It is found exclusively within the Northeast Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its entire natural habitat is restricted to the unique island of Socotra. This endemic species thrives only within this specific ecological niche. Consequently, its global distribution is entirely confined to this single, isolated location.

Plant Parts

This plant, Dracaena cinnabarii, is most highly valued for the production of its medicinal and aromatic exudates. The primary substance harvested from its trunk is a thick gum resin that oozes from the bark when the plant is wounded. Within this substance, the most prized component is the deep red resin, commonly known as dragon's blood, which has been used since antiquity for its various healing properties. This distinct resin is frequently utilized in traditional medicine and dyeing processes due to its intense color and unique chemical composition.
