Dragon's blood tree, scientifically known as Dracaena cinnabari, is a rare, endemic species native to the Socotra archipelago. Renowned for its striking silhouette and crimson resin, this remarkable plant has been prized for centuries in traditional medicine for its potent antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties across various cultures.
