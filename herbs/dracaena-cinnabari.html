Dracaena cinnabari

Dracaena cinnabarii, commonly known as the Dragon Blood Tree, is a striking succulent endemic to Socotra. Renowned for its unique umbrella shape, it produces a vivid red resin used traditionally in medicine and dyes. This resilient species symbolizes ecological heritage and remains a vital subject of botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Dracaena cinnabari
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae, it is further classified under the genus Dracaena.
