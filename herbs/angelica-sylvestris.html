Angelica sylvestris

Wild angelica

Angelica sylvestris, commonly known as wild angelica, is a perennial herb native to Europe and parts of Asia. Characterized by its large, umbel-shaped white flower clusters and aromatic foliage, this botanical species has long been valued in traditional herbalism for its diverse therapeutic properties and unique medicinal qualities.

  • Scientific name: Angelica sylvestris
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Angelica.
