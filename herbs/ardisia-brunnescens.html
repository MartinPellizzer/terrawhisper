What's the taxonomical classification of Ardisia brunnescens?

Ardisia brunnescens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Ericales. Within this order, the plant is a member of the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ardisia, with its specific designation being the species brunnescens.
