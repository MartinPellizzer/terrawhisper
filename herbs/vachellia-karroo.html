Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Karoo aloe, utilizes various anatomical structures for its medicinal properties. The thick, succulent leaves are often employed in traditional remedies to treat skin ailments or digestive issues due to their moisture-retaining properties and chemical composition. The roots are also highly valued and are frequently prepared as decoctions to address internal ailments and inflammatory conditions. Additionally, the bark of the plant can be harvested to create infusions used for its specific therapeutic effects, making the entire structure of Vachellia karroo a vital resource in indigenous medicine.
