Leptospermum petersonii

Leptospermum petersonii, commonly known as Peterson's Tea Tree, is a resilient shrub native to Australia. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it is utilized in traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial and antiseptic properties. This versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential in treating skin ailments and supporting respiratory health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Leptospermum petersonii
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by its genus, Leptospermum.
