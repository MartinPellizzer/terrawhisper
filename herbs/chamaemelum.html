Chamaemelum

Chamaemelum, commonly known as chamomile, is a revered medicinal herb celebrated for its calming properties. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, these delicate daisy-like flowers are widely used in herbal teas and essential oils. Renowned for soothing digestive issues and reducing anxiety, it remains a cornerstone of natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Chamaemelum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Chamaemelum.

Common Names

This plant, belonging to the genus Chamaemelum, is known by various names across different languages and cultures. In German, it is referred to as Römische Kamilien, while in Lithuanian, it is called Blezdingūnė. Swedish speakers use the term Romarkamomiller, and in Japanese, it is identified as カマエメルム属. Additionally, in Finnish, the plant is known as kampasauramot.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across various regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing within the territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Its distribution extends into Middle Europe, where it is documented in Austria and Belgium. Furthermore, the species is also present in the central regions of Czechia and Slovakia. Consequently, these locations represent the primary geographical range for this medicinal plant.
