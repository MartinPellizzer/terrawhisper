This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Chamaemelum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across various regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing within the territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Its distribution extends into Middle Europe, where it is documented in Austria and Belgium. Furthermore, the species is also present in the central regions of Czechia and Slovakia. Consequently, these locations represent the primary geographical range for this medicinal plant.
