Chinese hemlock-parsley, scientifically known as Conioselinum tataricum, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Often found in montane meadows, it belongs to the Apiaceae family. While historically studied in traditional contexts, it requires careful identification due to its striking resemblance to various toxic hemlock species found in nature.
