Supporting these is the stem, which provides the necessary structural integrity to elevate the plant. As the plant matures, it produces a striking flower cluster, typically arranged in an umbel, which marks the reproductive stage of its life cycle. Ultimately, the entire medicinal potency is concentrated within the fleshy bulb layers, making it a vital component in various traditional remedies.
