Cloudberry, scientifically known as Rubus chamaemorus, is a rare, amber-colored fruit found in Arctic and subarctic wetlands. Renowned for its exceptional vitamin C content and antioxidant properties, this hardy perennial offers potent anti-inflammatory benefits. Historically prized in Nordic cultures, it remains a valuable medicinal treasure for immune support.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as cloudberry, is highly valued for its nutritional and therapeutic properties, which are primarily concentrated in its fruits. These ripe, amber-colored berries are rich in essential vitamins, particularly Vitamin C, and various antioxidants that contribute to their medicinal efficacy. In traditional herbal medicine, the juices and extracts derived from these fruits are often used to support the immune system and promote overall wellness.
