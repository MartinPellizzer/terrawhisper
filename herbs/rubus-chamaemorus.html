Rubus chamaemorus

Cloudberry

Rubus chamaemorus, commonly known as cloudberry, is a resilient arctic plant prized for its golden, tart berries. Rich in vitamins C and E, these berries offer potent antioxidant properties. Traditionally used in northern cultures, cloudberries serve as a valuable medicinal resource for supporting immune health and reducing inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Rubus chamaemorus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Rubus chamaemorus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the Rosaceae family and falls under the genus Rubus.
