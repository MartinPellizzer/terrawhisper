Sideroxylon obtusifolium

Sideroxylon obtusifolium, commonly known as the West Indian Satinwood, is a precious tropical tree valued for its dense, durable timber. Beyond its woodworking utility, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance, with various parts utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin conditions to digestive issues and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Sideroxylon obtusifolium
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Sideroxylon obtusifolium, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Sapotaceae and falls under the genus Sideroxylon.
