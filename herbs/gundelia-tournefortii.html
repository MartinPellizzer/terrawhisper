Gundelia tournefortii

Gundelia tournefortii, commonly known as thistle or "Gundelia," is a resilient, thorny perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern regions. Valued for its nutritional density and medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including digestive issues, inflammation, and certain metabolic disorders.

  • Scientific name: Gundelia tournefortii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Gundelia tournefortii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. It is a member of the Asteraceae family and is specifically categorized under the genus Gundelia.
