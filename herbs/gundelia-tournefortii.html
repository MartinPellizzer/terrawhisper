Tumble Thistle, scientifically known as Gundelia tournefortii, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and Middle East. Renowned for its edible thistle heads and medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and act as a powerful antioxidant in various herbal remedies.
