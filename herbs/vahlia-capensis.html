Vahlia capensis

Vahlia capensis, commonly known as the Cape Vahlia, is a striking succulent plant native to South Africa. Distinguished by its fleshy, architectural leaves, this species thrives in rocky habitats. Beyond its ornamental appeal, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest, representing the unique biodiversity and potential medicinal properties of regional flora.

  • Scientific name: Vahlia capensis
  • Family: Vahliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Vahliales, it is classified under the family Vahliaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Vahlia, with its species name being Vahlia capensis.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several regions of Southern and South Tropical Africa.
