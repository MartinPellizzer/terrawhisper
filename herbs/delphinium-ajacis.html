Delphinium ajacis

Delphinium ajacis, commonly known as Larkspur, is a striking perennial valued for its tall, colorful spires. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant place in traditional medicine. Rich in alkaloids, it has been historically utilized for its potent sedative and analgesic properties, requiring careful handling due to its toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Delphinium ajacis
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, falling into the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Delphinium.

Furthermore, the species extends its range into the Scandinavian territories of Norway and Sweden.
