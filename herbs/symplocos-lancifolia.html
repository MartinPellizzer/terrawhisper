Symplocos lancifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Symplocos lancifolia?

Symplocos lancifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Ericales and belongs to the family Symplocaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Symplocos and the specific species epithet lancifolia.
