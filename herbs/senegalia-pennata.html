Climbing Acacia, scientifically known as Senegalia pennata, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its climbing habit and delicate foliage, this plant is deeply embedded in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections, respiratory issues, and inflammatory conditions.
