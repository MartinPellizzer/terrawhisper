Indian horse chestnut, scientifically known as Aesculus indica, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the Himalayan region. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This versatile species holds significant value in Ayurvedic medicine and holistic healing practices.
