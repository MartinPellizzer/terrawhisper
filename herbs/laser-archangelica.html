Plant Parts

This plant, known as Laserarchangelica (often referred to as garden angelica), is highly valued in traditional herbalism, particularly for the therapeutic properties found within its seeds. These small, fragrant seeds contain concentrated essential oils and bioactive compounds that are frequently harvested and used to support digestive health and alleviate gastrointestinal discomfort. While the roots and stems are also used in various preparations, it is the potent chemical profile of the seeds that makes them a primary focus for medicinal applications.
