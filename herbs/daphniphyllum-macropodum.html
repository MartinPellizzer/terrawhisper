Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In the northern reaches of its range, it can be found within the Kuril Islands of the Russian Far East. Its presence extends significantly into China, specifically throughout the South-Central and Southeast regions. The species is also well-established in Eastern Asia, including the territories of Japan and Korea.
