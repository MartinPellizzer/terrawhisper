Alpinia polyantha

What's the taxonomical classification of Alpinia polyantha?

Alpinia polyantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is positioned in the order Zingiberales. It is a member of the family Zingiberaceae, specifically falling under the genus Alpinia, and is identified by the species name polyantha.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Alpinia polyantha has erect, herbaceous stems that reach heights of up to two meters and feature large, lanceolate leaves arranged in a distichous pattern. The foliage consists of smooth, bright green blades that can grow significantly in length to capture sunlight in shaded environments. Its most striking feature is the terminal inflorescence, which produces dense, cone-like spikes of white bracts. These bracts protect the small, delicate flowers that emerge from within the structure. The plant also develops a sturdy rhizomatous root system that allows it to spread and establish itself in tropical soils.
