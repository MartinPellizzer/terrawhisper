Hairless Cat-mint, scientifically known as Nepeta nuda, is a perennial herb within the Lamiaceae family. Characterized by its smooth, leafless stems and delicate lavender flowers, this plant is prized for its aromatic properties. It serves as both an ornamental garden beauty and a traditional remedy in various medicinal practices.
