Euphorbia abyssinica

Euphorbia abyssinica, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in East Africa, holds profound ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammatory conditions. Understanding its phytochemical properties is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia abyssinica
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
