Phagnalon

Phagnalon, commonly known as the Golden Leaf, is a potent medicinal herb revered for its diverse healing properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, this resilient plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to alleviate inflammation and boost immune health, offering a natural remedy for various ailments throughout diverse global cultures.

  • Scientific name: Phagnalon
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Phagnalon.

Distribution
