Montanoa frutescens, scientifically known as Montanoa frutescens, is a perennial shrub native to Mexico and Central America. Commonly called "Tree Daisy," this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional folk medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory and uterine-stimulating properties, frequently used to treat various reproductive and inflammatory conditions.
