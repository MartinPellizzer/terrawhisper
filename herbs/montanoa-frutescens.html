Montanoa frutescens

Montanoa frutescens, commonly known as "Manzanilla de la Reina," is a medicinal shrub native to Central and South America. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments. This potent plant remains a cornerstone of folk medicine in many indigenous cultures.

  • Scientific name: Montanoa frutescens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae, it is formally categorized under the genus Montanoa.
