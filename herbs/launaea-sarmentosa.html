Launaea sarmentosa

Launaea sarmentoosa, commonly known as the creeping lau, is a medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
