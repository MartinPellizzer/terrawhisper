Buffel grass, scientifically known as Cenchrus ciliaris, is a resilient, perennial warm-season grass native to Africa and Asia. Renowned for its drought tolerance and rapid growth, it is widely used for forage and land reclamation. However, its invasive potential in certain ecosystems poses significant challenges to biodiversity and fire management.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across multiple continents. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Germany. Its presence extends into Southeastern Europe, specifically within the regions of Greece and Sicilia. Furthermore, the species is well-established in Northern Africa. Within this African territory, it is documented in both Algeria and Egypt.
