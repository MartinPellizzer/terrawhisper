Cenchrus ciliaris

Cenchrus ciliaris, commonly known as the fringed grass, is a resilient medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its distinctive delicate textures, this plant possesses unique bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized for its purported anti-inflammatory and restorative properties, serving as a vital resource in botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cenchrus ciliaris
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, situated within the family Poaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cenchrus.
