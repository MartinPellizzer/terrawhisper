Lonicera confusa

Lonicera confusa, commonly known as the confused honeysuckle, is a versatile climbing shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its fragrant, tubular flowers, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It serves as a vital component in various herbal remedies and ecological landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Lonicera confusa
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lonicera, specifically the species Lonicera confusa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In the Macaronesian region, its presence is noted in Cape Verde. It can also be found within the Western Indian Ocean, specifically inhabiting the islands of Mauritius and Réunion. Furthermore, the species is documented in China, occurring in the South-Central areas. Its range extends even further south to the island province of Hainan.
