Pleione yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Pleione yunnanensis?

Pleione yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales. As a member of the family Orchidaceae, it is further categorized into the genus Pleione, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pleione yunnanensis has an orchid morphology characterized by two basal, ovate-lanceolate leaves that emerge from a fleshy, ovoid pseudobulb. Each plant typically produces a single, erect inflorescence bearing multiple fragrant, labiate flowers ranging in color from white to pale pink or lavender. The flowers feature a prominent, three-lobed lip and often display delicate spotting or veining on the petals. The leaves are smooth and green, providing a seasonal contrast to the colorful blooms that appear before or during their growth. This terrestrial structure allows the plant to store nutrients within the pseudobulb for survival during dormant periods.

According to a study published by "Magnetic resonance in chemistry : MRC", the tubers of Pleione yunnanensis were used to isolate three new dihydrophenanthrofurans known as pleionesins A-C (1-3). These three new compounds were isolated alongside two known dihydrophenanthrenes (4-5) from the plant. The research involved complete (1)H and (13)C NMR spectra assignments for these specific substances. Scientists utilized 1D and 2D NMR experiments, including (1)H, (13)C, selective 1D NOE, HSQC, and HMBC, to analyze the compounds.

What medicinal compounds this plant contains?

This plant contains the medicinal compounds bibenzyls, dihydrophenanthrenes, pleionesins A-C, shanchigusin E, and shanchigusin F. These diverse secondary metabolites are primarily classified as stilbenoid derivatives. The pleionesins A-C group represents a unique set of structurally complex molecules found specifically within this species. Additionally, the presence of shanchigusin E and shanchigusin F contributes to the plant's overall chemical profile. These compounds are often studied for their potential biological activities in pharmacological research.

According to a study published by "Chemical & pharmaceutical bulletin", the tubers of Pleione yunnanensis contain four new bibenzyl derivatives known as shancigusins A-D (1-4). In addition to these new compounds, five known bibenzyls (5-9) were also isolated from the plant. The structures of these 9 compounds were determined through extensive analyses of their spectroscopic data.
