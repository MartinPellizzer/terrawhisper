Syringa vulgaris

Lilac

Syringa vulgaris, commonly known as lilac, is a beloved deciduous shrub celebrated for its fragrant, bell-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, various traditional medicinal practices have utilized its leaves and flowers to treat ailments such as inflammation, coughs, and skin irritations, highlighting its multifaceted value in botanical wellness.

  • Scientific name: Syringa vulgaris
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Oleaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Syringa.
