Allamanda schottii

Allamanda schottii, commonly known as the Schott's Allamanda, is a striking tropical shrub celebrated for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Researchers continue to explore its unique phytochemical properties, investigating its potential medicinal applications and therapeutic benefits in traditional healing.
