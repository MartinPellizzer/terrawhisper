Common hyacinth, scientifically known as Hyacinthus orientalis, is a fragrant perennial bulb prized for its striking, bell-shaped blooms. Historically utilized in traditional medicine for its calming properties, this plant is celebrated in aromatherapy. Its vibrant colors and intoxicating scent make it a cornerstone of both ornamental and therapeutic gardening.
