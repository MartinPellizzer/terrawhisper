Melaleuca rugulosa

Melaleuca rugulosa, commonly known as the wrinkled paperbark, is a resilient Australian shrub valued for its medicinal properties. Native to diverse ecosystems, its aromatic leaves contain potent essential oils with antimicrobial and antiseptic qualities. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments and respiratory issues, it remains a significant botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Melaleuca rugulosa
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Melaleuca.
