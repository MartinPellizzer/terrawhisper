Sinomarsdenia incisa

Sinomarsdenia inciosa, commonly known as the "incised Sinomarsdenia," is a significant medicinal herb native to East Asian forests. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant is often utilized for its unique bioactive compounds. Researchers study its pharmacological potential to explore therapeutic applications in treating various inflammatory and metabolic health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Sinomarsdenia incisa
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
