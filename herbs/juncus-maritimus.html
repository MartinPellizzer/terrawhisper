Juncus maritimus

Juncus maritimus, commonly known as the sea rush, is a resilient perennial plant found in coastal salt marshes and sandy habitats. Renowned for its ability to thrive in saline environments, this species plays a vital role in stabilizing shorelines and supporting local biodiversity within delicate maritime ecosystems worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Juncus maritimus
  • Family: Juncaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, falling under the family Juncaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Juncus.
