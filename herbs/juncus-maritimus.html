Sea Rush, scientifically known as Juncus maritimus, is a resilient perennial plant commonly found in coastal salt marshes and sandy habitats. Renowned for its ability to thrive in saline environments, it offers significant ecological value and traditional medicinal uses, particularly for its soothing properties in treating skin ailments.
