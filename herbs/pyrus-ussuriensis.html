Harbin pear, scientifically known as Pyrus ussuriensis, is a deciduous fruit tree native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its fruit is prized for its cooling properties, frequently used to soothe sore throats, relieve coughs, and reduce internal heat, offering a natural remedy for respiratory wellness.
