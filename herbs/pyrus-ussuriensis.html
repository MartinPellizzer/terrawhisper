Pyrus ussuriensis

Pyrus ussuriensis, commonly known as the Ussuri pear, is a resilient deciduous tree native to East Asian temperate forests. Renowned for its edible fruit and ornamental beauty, this species holds significant botanical interest. In traditional practices, its components are explored for various medicinal properties, highlighting its ecological and therapeutic value.

  • Scientific name: Pyrus ussuriensis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling within the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Pyrus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range stretching across significant portions of Northern Eurasia. In Eastern Europe, its presence is noted within both Central European Russia and East European Russia. Moving toward the Asian continent, the species is widely distributed throughout the Russian Far East. Specifically, it can be found in the Amur and Khabarovsk regions of this territory. Additionally, its habitat extends into the Primorye area, completing its extensive natural distribution.
