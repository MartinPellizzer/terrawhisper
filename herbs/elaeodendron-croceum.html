Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning multiple regions across Africa and the Atlantic. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted within Zimbabwe. The species is also widely distributed throughout Southern Africa, including the Cape Provinces, KwaZulu-Natal, and the Northern Provinces. Beyond the African continent, its reach extends into the Middle Atlantic Ocean. Specifically, it can be found growing on the remote island of St. Helena.
