Mallotus rhamnifolius

Mallotus rhamnifolius, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in traditional systems, holds immense therapeutic potential. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This botanical species offers promising avenues for pharmacological research and natural treatments in holistic medicine.

  • Scientific name: Mallotus rhamnifolius
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is further categorized into the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Mallotus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a fascinating geographical distribution that spans across two different continents. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of India. Additionally, its presence is noted within the tropical regions of Sri Lanka. Moving across the globe to Western South America, the species also inhabits the territory of Bolivia. Consequently, the range of Mallotus rhamnifolius connects distinct ecological zones from Asia to South America.
