Scientifically known as Thuja, is a genus of coniferous plants widely recognized for its aromatic foliage and medicinal properties. Often referred to as arborvitae, it has been traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support respiratory health, promote skin healing, and possess antiseptic qualities, making it a staple in holistic wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions in Northern Europe. Within this area, it can be found growing in Denmark and the Faroe Islands. Finally, its range extends further north into the territories of Norway.
