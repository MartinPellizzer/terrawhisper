Centella eriantha var. eriantha

Centella eriantha var. eriantha, commonly known as a specialized variety of Gotu Kola, is a medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, this plant is recognized in traditional medicine for its potential to enhance cognitive function, promote skin healing, and support overall circulatory health.

  • Scientific name: Centella eriantha var. eriantha
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified as a member of the genus Centella, categorized as the variety eriantha.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the specific ecological regions of Southern Africa. Its documented range is concentrated within the Cape Provinces of the South African territory. Within these areas, the species occupies particular niches that allow it to thrive in the local climate. This restricted distribution highlights the specialized habitat requirements necessary for its survival. Consequently, the geographical presence of this variety is highly localized to these southern coastal and inland provinces.
