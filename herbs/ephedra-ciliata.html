Ephedra ciliata

Ephedra ciliaeta, commonly known as the broom hemp or ephedra, is a perennial shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its significant pharmacological properties, it contains alkaloids like ephedrine. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this plant plays a vital role in treating respiratory issues and enhancing physical stamina.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra ciliata
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Gnetidae, it falls under the order Ephedrales and the family Ephedraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ephedra, with the specific species being Ephedra ciliata.
