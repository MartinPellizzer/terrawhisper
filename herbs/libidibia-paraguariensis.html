Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution throughout several regions of South America. In Western South America, its presence is noted within the borders of Bolivia. Its range extends into Brazil, specifically across the West-Central portion of the country. Within Southern South America, it can be found in both the Northeast and Northwest regions of Argentina. Finally, the species is also widely distributed throughout Paraguay.
