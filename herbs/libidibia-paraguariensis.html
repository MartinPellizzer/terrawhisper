Libidibia paraguariensis

Libidibia paraguariensis, commonly known as Pau Brasil, is a storied medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to support vascular health and combat oxidative stress, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Libidibia paraguariensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is a member of the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Libidibia, with its species name being paraguariensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution throughout several regions of South America. In Western South America, its presence is noted within the borders of Bolivia. Its range extends into Brazil, specifically across the West-Central portion of the country. Within Southern South America, it can be found in both the Northeast and Northwest regions of Argentina. Finally, the species is also widely distributed throughout Paraguay.
