Artemisia sieberi

Artemisia sieberi, commonly known as Sieber's wormwood, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional healing for its aromatic properties, often utilized to treat various ailments through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia sieberi
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Specifically, Artemisia sieberi is identified by its genus, Artemisia.
