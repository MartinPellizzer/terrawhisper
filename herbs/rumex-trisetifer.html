Trisetiferous Dock, scientifically known as Rumex trisetifer, is a distinctive perennial herb belonging to the Polygonaceae family. Characterized by its unique, three-lobed seed structures, this plant thrives in diverse habitats. While primarily recognized for its botanical characteristics, it holds potential interest in traditional herbal studies and ecological biodiversity.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is documented throughout the South-Central and North-Central territories.
