Maytenus obtusifolia

Maytenus obtusifolia, commonly known as "mora" or "mora de monte," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, it possesses significant bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, offering valuable therapeutic potential in treating various skin and systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Maytenus obtusifolia
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Maytenus.
