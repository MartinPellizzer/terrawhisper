Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout various regions of Southeastern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing in the countries of Bulgaria and Greece. Its range also extends into the European portion of Türkiye. Additionally, the species is located within the northwestern Balkan Peninsula. Finally, its geographical presence reaches into Eastern Europe, specifically within Crimea.
