Dicliptera chinensis

Dicliptera chinensis, commonly known as the Chinese Dicliptera, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Belonging to the Acanthaceae family, this plant is recognized for its bioactive properties, often used to treat inflammation, fever, and skin ailments. It remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Dicliptera chinensis
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. Specifically, it is a member of the genus Dicliptera, with its species name being chinensis.
