Ajuga forrestii

Ajuga forrestii, commonly known as Forrest's bugle, is a charming perennial herb native to the misty forests of East Asia. Characterized by its lush foliage and delicate, tubular flowers, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its unique bioactive compounds and its potential roles in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ajuga forrestii
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Ajuga. Specifically, this medicinal species is identified as Ajuga forrestii.
