Psiadia terebinthina

Psiadia terebinthina, commonly known as the "Terebinth" shrub, is a significant medicinal plant found across tropical Asia. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized in various folk medicines to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and respiratory issues, offering valuable therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Psiadia terebinthina
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
