Clinopodium nepeta subsp. spruneri

Clinopodium nepeta subsp. spruneri, commonly known as Spruner's catnip, is a distinctive perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Valued for its aromatic foliage, this subspecies possesses unique phytochemical properties. It is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine for its calming effects, digestive support, and potential antimicrobial therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Clinopodium nepeta subsp. spruneri
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls under the family Lamiaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Clinopodium as the subspecies spruneri.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found across the territories of Czechia and Slovakia. The distribution extends into Southwestern Europe, where it inhabits the islands of the Baleares and the island of Corse. Additionally, its range continues through the mainland landscape of France.
