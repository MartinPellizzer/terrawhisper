Dianella callicarpa

Dianella callicarpa, commonly known as the Callicarpa or Beautyberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued for its potent bioactive compounds. Rich in polyphenols and antioxidants, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation and skin ailments. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Dianella callicarpa
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asparagales and the family Asphodelaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dianella.
