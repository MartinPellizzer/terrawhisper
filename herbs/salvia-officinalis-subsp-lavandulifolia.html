Salvia officinalis lavanduliifolia, scientifically known as Salvia officinalis subsp. lavandulifolia, is a unique subspecies of common sage distinguished by its narrow, lavender-like foliage. Renowned for its potent aromatic oils, this medicinal herb offers enhanced therapeutic properties, traditionally used to support digestive health, boost immunity, and soothe inflammation.
