Salvia officinalis subsp. lavandulifolia

Salvia officinalis subsp. lavandulifolia, commonly known as Lavender-leaf Sage, is a fragrant perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Distinguished by its narrow, silvery-green leaves, this subspecies offers potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. It is widely used to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote relaxation through aromatherapy.

  • Scientific name: Salvia officinalis subsp. lavandulifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
