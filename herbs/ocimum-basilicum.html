Ocimum basilicum

Ocimum basilicum, commonly known as sweet basil, is a fragrant annual herb cherished in culinary and medicinal traditions. Renowned for its aromatic essential oils, it offers potent antioxidant, antibacterial, and anti-inflammatory properties. Beyond flavoring dishes, this versatile plant serves as a natural remedy for digestive health and stress relief.

  • Scientific name: Ocimum basilicum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ocimum basilicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ocimum.
