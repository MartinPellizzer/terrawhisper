Devil's Claw, scientifically known as Martynia annua, is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive, hooked seed pods, this plant is widely used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various infections, offering potent bioactive compounds for holistic healing and wellness.
