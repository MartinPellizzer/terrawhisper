Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in countries such as Burundi and Cameroon. It can also be found within the West-Central Tropical Africa region specifically around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Finally, its range extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Eritrea.
