Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its potential antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and sedative properties to treat various ailments and skin conditions.

Reproduction

The reproduction of Cestrum nocturnum is characterized by a specific inflorescence structure and specialized fruit development. The inflorescence is relatively compact, with lengths ranging from a minimum of 0.07 m to a maximum of 0.1 m. Following pollination, the plant produces fruit classified as a berry.
