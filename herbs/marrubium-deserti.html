Marrubium deserti

Marrubium deserti, commonly known as desert horehound, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid Mediterranean regions. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat respiratory ailments, soothe coughs, and reduce inflammation, offering a natural remedy rooted in ancient botanical wisdom.
