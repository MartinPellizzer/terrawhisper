Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as desert wormwood, is valued in traditional medicine primarily for its chemical composition rather than its structural anatomy. While the entire aerial part of the herb is often utilized in preparations, the most therapeutically significant component found within the plant is its essential oils. These volatile oils, concentrated within the glandular hairs of the leaves and stems, contain the active compounds responsible for the plant's antimicrobial and expectorant properties, making the extraction of these oils a crucial aspect of its medicinal application.
