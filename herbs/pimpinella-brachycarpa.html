What's the taxonomical classification of Pimpinella brachycarpa?

Pimpinella brachycarpa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its hierarchical lineage continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Apiales. As a member of the family Apiaceae, this plant is further categorized under the genus Pimpinella, with its specific identification designated as the species brachycarpa.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause allergic skin reactions such as contact dermatitis, redness, and itching when the sap or leaves touch sensitive skin. Because it belongs to the Apiaceae family, it may contain furocoumarins that induce photosensitivity in certain individuals. This condition makes the skin significantly more vulnerable to painful sunburns and blistering when exposed to ultraviolet light. Some people might also experience gastrointestinal distress or mild nausea if the plant is ingested accidentally. Always exercise extreme caution when handling this species to avoid these physiological complications.
