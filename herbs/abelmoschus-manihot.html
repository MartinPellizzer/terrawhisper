Abelmoschus manihot

Abelmoschus manihot, commonly known as the medicinal okra, is a versatile plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations. This botanical specimen holds significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
