Mansoa hirsuta

Mansoa hirsuta, commonly known as Clitoria or False Trumpet Vine, is a versatile climbing shrub prized in traditional medicine. Native to tropical regions, this plant is celebrated for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. It is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments and promote wellness.
