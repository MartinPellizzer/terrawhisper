Erigeron bonariensis

Erigeron bonarienensis, commonly known as the Bonariensis fleabane, is a resilient perennial herb native to South American landscapes. Renowned for its delicate, daisy-like blooms, this plant possesses diverse ethnobotanical properties. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities to support holistic healing.
