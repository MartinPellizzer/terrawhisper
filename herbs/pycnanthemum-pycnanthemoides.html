Pycnanthemum pycnanthemoides

Pycnanthemum pycnanthemoides, commonly known as bitter pycnanthemum, is a perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic, mint-family foliage, this plant offers significant ethnobotanical value. Historically utilized for its digestive and antimicrobial properties, it serves as a fascinating subject for studying natural compounds in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Pycnanthemum pycnanthemoides
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Pycnanthemum pycnanthemoides, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Pycnanthemum.

Common Names

This plant, Pycnanthemum pycnanthemoides, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is most commonly referred to as bitter pycnanthemum or mountain bushy mint. In German, it is known as Berg-Pycnanthemum, and in French, it is called pycnanthème amer.
