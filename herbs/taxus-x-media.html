Taxus × media

Taxus media

Taxus × media, commonly known as the hybrid yew, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized in ornamental gardening for its dense, dark foliage. While many yew species contain potent alkaloids, this hybrid is celebrated for its resilience and sculptural form, making it a staple in formal hedges and landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Taxus × media
  • Family: Taxaceae
  • Native range: Not available

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales, it is classified in the family Taxaceae and the genus Taxus. As a hybrid, Taxus × media represents a specific taxonomic intersection within this lineage.

Plant Parts

This plant, Taxus × media, is a hybrid cultivar often studied in biotechnological research for its ability to produce valuable secondary metabolites. In laboratory settings, researchers frequently focus on the development of its roots to study the biosynthesis of taxanes. Specifically, the induction of hairy roots through Agrobacterium rhizogenes-mediated transformation allows for the stable expression of medicinal compounds. By utilizing these hairy root cultures, scientists can efficiently produce high concentrations of bioactive molecules in a controlled environment, bypassing the long growth cycles required by the mature plant.
