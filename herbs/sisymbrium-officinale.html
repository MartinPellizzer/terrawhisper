Sisymbrium officinale

Sisymbrium officinale, commonly known as hedge mustard, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Brassicaceae family. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it is frequently used to support respiratory health and alleviate inflammation. Its potent bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential for treating various ailments throughout natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Sisymbrium officinale
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe
