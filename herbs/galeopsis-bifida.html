Galeopsis bifida

Galeopsis bifida, commonly known as hedge hemp, is a distinctive herbaceous plant belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Characterized by its serrated leaves and specialized spiked inflorescences, it thrives in disturbed soils. Historically utilized in traditional herbalism, this resilient species plays a vital role in local biodiversity and ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Galeopsis bifida
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
