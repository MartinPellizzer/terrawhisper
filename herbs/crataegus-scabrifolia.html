Crataegus scabrifolia

Crataegus scabrifolia, commonly known as the scabrous hawthorn, is a resilient medicinal shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive flavonoids and antioxidants, it is traditionally utilized to support cardiovascular health, regulate blood pressure, and alleviate digestive ailments, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus scabrifolia
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Crataegus, specifically as the species Crataegus scabrifolia.
