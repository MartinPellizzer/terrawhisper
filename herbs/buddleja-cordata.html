Buddleja cordata

Buddleja cordata, commonly known as the Cordate Butterfly Bush, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its heart-shaped leaves, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities, offering therapeutic potential in treating various ailments and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Buddleja cordata
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Buddleja cordata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the Scrophulariaceae family and falls under the genus Buddleja.

This plant, commonly known as the butterfly bush, utilizes its wild plant leaves as a key component of its biological structure. In the context of its medicinal properties, these wild plant leaves are often the primary part studied for their various bioactive compounds and traditional uses.
