Ranunculus ficaria

Lesser celandine

Ranunculus ficaria, commonly known as Lesser Celandine, is a perennial flowering plant valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its bright yellow blooms, it contains bioactive compounds used historically to treat skin ailments and inflammation. However, caution is essential, as its potent constituents can be toxic if improperly prepared.

  • Scientific name: Ranunculus ficaria
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic details place it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae within the genus Ranunculus.
