Lesser celandine, scientifically known as Ranunculus ficaria, is a perennial herbaceous plant characterized by its bright yellow, buttercup-like flowers. Native to Europe and parts of Asia, it thrives in moist, shady habitats. Historically used in folk medicine, its bioactive compounds require careful handling due to potential skin irritation.
