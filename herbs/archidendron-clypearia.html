Archidendron clypearia

Archidendroon clypearia, commonly known as the Linden or Lime tree, is a revered medicinal plant valued for its fragrant blossoms. Rich in flavonoids and essential oils, its infusions are traditionally used to soothe nervous tension, alleviate digestive discomfort, and support respiratory health through its gentle, calming sedative properties.

  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
