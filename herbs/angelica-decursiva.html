Common Hogfennel

Angelica decursiva · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (238 studies) · ★★★★★

Common Hogfennel, scientifically known as Angelica decursiva, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, this aromatic plant is prized for its bioactive compounds, which possess significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. It is frequently used to support circulation and promote overall wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found specifically within the Primorye region. Its presence extends significantly into China, covering various diverse territories. Specifically, it is documented in China South-Central, Inner Mongolia, and Manchuria. Furthermore, the species is also distributed throughout China North-Central.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Angelica decursiva, is highly valued in traditional medicine, primarily for its therapeutic properties found within its underground structures. The most significant medicinal component is the roots, which are harvested and processed to extract active compounds. Whether used in its fresh state or as a dry root, this part of the plant serves as the foundation for many herbal preparations. Specifically, the quality of the root is essential for its efficacy, as the concentration of medicinal constituents often depends on the maturity and careful drying of the botanical material.
