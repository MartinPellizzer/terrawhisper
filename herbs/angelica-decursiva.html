Angelica decursiva

Angelica decursiva, commonly known as Dang Gui in traditional medicine, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to support blood circulation, regulate hormonal balance, and alleviate menstrual discomfort. This potent botanical serves as a cornerstone in many traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Angelica decursiva
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Angelica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found specifically within the Primorye region. Its presence extends significantly into China, covering various diverse territories. Specifically, it is documented in China South-Central, Inner Mongolia, and Manchuria. Furthermore, the species is also distributed throughout China North-Central.
