Primula blattariformis

What's the taxonomical classification of Primula blattariformis?

Primula blattariformis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic progression continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Ericales. Within this order, the plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, which contains the genus Primula, and is specifically identified by its species name, blattariformis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Primula blattariformis has a rosette-forming habit characterized by long-petioled, broadly ovate to elliptical leaves with crenate or wavy margins and a distinctively rugose surface texture. The leaf blades are typically thin and display prominent venation patterns that contribute to their textured appearance. From the center of this vegetative rosette, an elongated, leafless scape emerges to support a terminal umbel of flowers. Each individual flower consists of a tubular corolla that opens into five spreading lobes, often displaying delicate color transitions. The overall structure reflects a typical primrose morphology adapted for low-growing ground cover.
