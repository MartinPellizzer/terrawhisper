Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across various regions of China. It can be found growing in the South-Central areas of the country. Additionally, its presence extends to the tropical landscapes of Hainan. The species is also documented within the northern territories of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Plant Parts

This plant, Dolomiaea costus, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its various components. The most significant part used is the root, specifically the dried and processed roots which are often ground into a fine powder for therapeutic applications. While the leaves may be present during its growth cycle, the primary medicinal potency is concentrated in the subterranean parts. Furthermore, the plant is a source of aromatic essential oil, which is extracted to harness its potent fragrance and chemical properties.
