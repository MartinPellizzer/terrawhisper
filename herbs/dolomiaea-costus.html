Dolomiaea costus

Dolomiaea costus, commonly known as Costus or Kust, is a revered medicinal plant belonging to the Costaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic roots, it has been used for centuries in traditional Ayurvedic and Unani medicine. It possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and digestive properties, making it a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Dolomiaea costus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
