Myricaria paniculata

Myricaria paniculata, commonly known as the water grass, is a perennial aquatic herb valued in traditional medicine. Thriving in wetlands, it is prized for its diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. This resilient plant plays a vital role in various folk remedies across tropical and subtropical regions.

  • Scientific name: Myricaria paniculata
  • Family: Tamaricaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Myricaria paniculata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Tamaricaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Myricaria.

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across various regions of China. It can be found growing in the South-Central parts of the country, as well as in the Southeast.
