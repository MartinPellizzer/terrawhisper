Scientific literature: Very Sparse (23 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its potent medicinal properties. Primarily recognized for its high anthraquinone content, it serves as a powerful natural laxative. Beyond digestive support, various species are utilized in traditional medicine to stimulate appetite and support liver function effectively.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in Great Britain and Norway. Moving toward the center of the continent, its presence is documented throughout Germany. The species also inhabits parts of Middle Europe, specifically within Austria. Finally, its geographical range extends into the Czechia-Slovakia region.
