Rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its potent medicinal properties. Primarily recognized for its high anthraquinone content, it serves as a powerful natural laxative. Beyond digestive support, various species are utilized in traditional medicine to stimulate appetite and support liver function effectively.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as rhubarb, is valued in traditional medicine primarily for its underground structures. The medicinal properties are concentrated in the root and the rhizome, which are harvested for their potent laxative effects. These parts of the plant contain high concentrations of anthraquinone glycosides, making them essential components in various herbal formulations used to treat digestive issues.
