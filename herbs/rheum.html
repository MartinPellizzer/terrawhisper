Rheum

Rheum, commonly known as rhubarb, is a versatile genus of perennial plants valued for both culinary and medicinal purposes. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, its roots contain anthraquinones, which act as potent laxatives. While celebrated in kitchens, its pharmacological properties require careful handling to ensure safe and effective use.

  • Scientific name: Rheum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rheum.
