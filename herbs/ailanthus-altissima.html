Renowned for its rapid spread, it possesses significant medicinal properties, particularly in traditional practices, where its bark and leaves are utilized to treat various inflammatory conditions and ailments.

The flowering cycle typically begins in April and concludes in July, featuring small green flowers that range in length from 0.3 cm to 0.4 cm.
