Ailanthus altissima

Ailanthus altiussima, commonly known as the Tree of Heaven, is a fast-growing deciduous species native to China. Despite its name, it is often considered an invasive weed. However, in traditional medicine, its bark and leaves possess significant bioactive properties, used historically to treat skin conditions and various inflammatory ailments.

  • Family: Simaroubaceae
