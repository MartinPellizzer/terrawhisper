Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Ailanthus altissima, possesses various components that are characteristic of its structure and biology. The bark is typically grayish and marked by prominent lenticels, providing a protective outer layer for the tree. The reproductive cycle is marked by the production of a specialized fruit, which is a dry, winged structure known as a samara. These winged seeds are designed to be dispersed effectively by the wind, allowing the species to spread rapidly across different environments. Additionally, the roots of the tree are extensive and highly vigorous, often spreading laterally to form a robust network that aids in its ability to thrive even in challenging urban conditions.
