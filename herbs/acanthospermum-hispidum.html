Bristly starburr, scientifically known as Acanthospermum hispidum, is a resilient herbaceous plant widely recognized for its hooked, prickly seeds. Primarily found in tropical regions, this botanical species is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, including its potential uses in treating inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues.
