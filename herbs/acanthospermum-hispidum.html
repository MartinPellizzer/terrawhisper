Acanthospermum hispidum

Bristly starburr

Acanthospermum hispidum, commonly known as burr salsify, is a versatile medicinal herb widely used in traditional folk medicine. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently employed to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for various holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Acanthospermum hispidum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Acanthospermum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution that spans across multiple continents and climate zones. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented in Belgium. Moving toward the African continent, it is found in Egypt within Northern Africa. Its range extends to the Macaronesian islands, specifically reaching Cape Verde. Finally, the species is established in West Tropical Africa, occurring in both Benin and Burkina.
