Santolina chamaecyparissus

Santolina chamaecyparissus, commonly known as Lavender Cotton, is a resilient evergreen shrub prized for its silvery foliage and bright yellow blooms. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it possesses significant antiseptic and carminative properties. This aromatic plant is frequently utilized in aromatherapy and topical remedies to soothe skin and digestion.

  • Scientific name: Santolina chamaecyparissus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Santolina.
