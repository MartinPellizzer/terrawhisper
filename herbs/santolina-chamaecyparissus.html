Widely used in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antiseptic and anti-inflammatory qualities. This hardy plant is often employed to soothe skin irritations and support respiratory health naturally.

Plant Parts

This plant, Santolina chamaecyparysso, is characterized by its distinctive foliage and aromatic properties. The small, silver-grey leaves are densely packed along the stems, creating a fine, textured appearance that is typical of many Mediterranean shrubs. Each individual leaf contributes to the plant's overall scent, which is highly concentrated within its essential oils. These essential oils, extracted from the plant material, are the primary component utilized in various medicinal and aromatic applications.
