Picramnia latifolia

Picramnia latifolia, commonly known as the "picramnia" or "pica," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Picramnia latifolia
  • Family: Picramniaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Picramnia latifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Picramniales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Picramniaceae and the genus Picramnia.
