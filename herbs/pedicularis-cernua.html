What's the taxonomical classification of Pedicularis cernua?

Pedicularis cernua belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Lamiales, the plant is a member of the family Orobanchaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy is completed by its placement within the genus Pedicularis, ultimately reaching its specific identification as the species cernua.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pedicularis cernua has an erect, unbranched stem that typically reaches heights of 10 to 30 centimeters and is characterized by a dense, terminal spike of bilabiate flowers. The leaves are mostly basal and pinnately lobed, while the stem leaves are smaller and alternate in arrangement. Each flower features a prominent, hood-like upper lip and a smaller, three-lobed lower lip that is often yellowish or pale pink in color. The inflorescence is compact and cylindrical, containing numerous individual florets that bloom sequentially. Fine hairs often coat the stems and calyces, providing a subtle texture to the plant's overall structure.
