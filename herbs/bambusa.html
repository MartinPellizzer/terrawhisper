This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Bambusa.

Distribution

This plant, known as Bambusa, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence can be identified within the borders of Libya. Moving toward the West Tropical Africa region, it is found in the country of Benin. The distribution extends further through Burkina and Ghana as well. Finally, the plant is also documented within the territory of Guinea.
