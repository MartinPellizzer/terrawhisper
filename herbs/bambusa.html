Bambusa

Bambusa, commonly known as bamboo, is a versatile genus of woody perennial grasses renowned for its rapid growth. Beyond its structural uses, various species offer significant medicinal potential. Rich in bioactive compounds, bamboo extracts are traditionally utilized for their anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties in diverse holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Bambusa
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Bambusa.
