, scientifically known as Vitex, is a versatile medicinal shrub widely valued for its therapeutic properties. Often referred to as Chasteberry, it is primarily used to support hormonal balance, alleviate menstrual irregularities, and manage symptoms of PMS. This potent botanical offers a natural approach to enhancing reproductive and emotional wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Middle Europe, it can be found specifically within Germany. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it is well-established in various parts of Southwestern Europe. This includes its presence on the islands of Baleares, Corse, and Sardegna. Additionally, the species is widely distributed throughout the mainland of France.
