Sorocea hilarii

Sorocea hilarii, commonly known as a versatile medicinal plant, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is often utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and ethnobotanical importance is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacological research.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Moraceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed within the genus Sorocea.
