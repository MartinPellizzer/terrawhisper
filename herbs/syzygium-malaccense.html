Syzygium malaccense

Malay rose apple

Syzygium malaccense, commonly known as the Malacca water apple, is a tropical evergreen tree prized for its medicinal properties. Often found in coastal regions, its fruits and bark possess antioxidant and antimicrobial qualities. Traditional practices utilize various plant parts to support digestive health and alleviate inflammation in diverse cultures.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium malaccense
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Syzygium.
