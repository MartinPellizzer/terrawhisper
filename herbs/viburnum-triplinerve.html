What's the taxonomical classification of Viburnum triplinerve?

Viburnum triplinerve belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Dipsacales. It is a member of the family Viburnaceae, falling within the genus Viburnum, and is specifically identified by its species name, triplinerve.
