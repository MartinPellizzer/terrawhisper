This plant, commonly known as Nauclea orientalis or the African peach, possesses various components that are utilized in traditional medicine, with the bark being one of the most significant parts. The bark is frequently harvested for its potent bioactive compounds, which are often processed into decoctions or powders to treat ailments such as fever, inflammation, and digestive issues. Beyond the bark, other parts of the tree, including the leaves and roots, are also studied for their potential therapeutic properties, but the bark remains a central element in the plant's medicinal application.
