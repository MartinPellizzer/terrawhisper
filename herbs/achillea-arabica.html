Arabian Milfoil, scientifically known as Achillea arabica, is a resilient perennial herb native to the arid landscapes of the Middle East. Renowned for its delicate, feathery foliage and clusters of small flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in folk medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties.
