What's the taxonomical classification of Myristica cagayanensis?

Myristica cagayanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and follows the order Magnoliales. This plant is a member of the family Myristicaceae, specifically falling under the genus Myristica, with its unique identification as the species cagayanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Myristica cagayanensis has an arborescent growth habit characterized by a woody trunk, elliptical to oblong leaves with entire margins, and small, inconspicuous flowers arranged in axillary clusters. The foliage is typically coriaceous in texture and features prominent pinnate venation on the underside. Its reproductive structures produce fleshy, ellipsoidal fruits that eventually split open to reveal a dark seed enclosed in a bright aril. The bark is generally smooth or slightly fissured depending on the age of the specimen.
