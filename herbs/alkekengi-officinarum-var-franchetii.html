Plant Parts

This plant, Alkekengi officinarum var. franchetii, is characterized by its unique and visually striking fruit, which develops within a bright, papery, bladder-like calyx. This inflated structure serves to protect the small, round, berry-like fruit contained inside, which typically transitions from green to a deep reddish color as it matures. In various traditional medicinal practices, these specialized reproductive structures are the primary focus for study and application.
