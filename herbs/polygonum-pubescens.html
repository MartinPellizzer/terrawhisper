Polygonum pubescens

What's the taxonomical classification of Polygonum pubescens?

Polygonum pubescens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Polygonaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Polygonum and is designated by the species epithet pubescens.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Polygonum pubescens has an erect, herbaceous stem that typically reaches heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and is characterized by a fine covering of soft, spreading hairs. The leaves are simple, alternate, and lanceolate in shape, featuring entire margins and distinct longitudinal veins. Small, inconspicuous flowers are arranged in clusters within the axils of the leaves or at the terminal ends of the stems. These blossoms are generally pink or white and possess a five-lobed corolla. The plant also develops small, ovoid fruits known as achenes that are often reddish or brownish upon maturity.
