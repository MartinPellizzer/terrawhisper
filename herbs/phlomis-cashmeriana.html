Phlomis cashmeriana

Phlomis cashmeriana, commonly known as Himalayan Phlomis, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic practices to treat respiratory ailments, inflammation, and skin disorders, offering a natural remedy rooted in mountain biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis cashmeriana
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
