Macaranga mappa

Macaranga mappa, commonly known as the Map Macaranga, is a distinctive tropical shrub native to Southeast Asia. Characterized by its large, uniquely lobed leaves, this species plays a vital role in forest ecosystems. Beyond its botanical beauty, it is studied for its potential medicinal properties and ecological significance.

  • Scientific name: Macaranga mappa
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Macaranga, specifically as the species Macaranga mappa.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Macaranga mappa, is referred to by various common names depending on the local language and region. In English, it is often called the map plant, while in Indonesian, it is known as pohon peta. In Malay, it is frequently referred to as pokok peta, and in Tagalog, it is called mapang.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several distinct tropical regions. Within the Malesia botanical zone, it can be found specifically in the Maluku islands. Its distribution also extends to the island of Sulawesi, further establishing its presence in Southeast Asia. Beyond the Indo-Pacific region, the species has reached the North-Central Pacific. Notably, it is also documented as growing in the remote archipelago of Hawaii.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Macaranga mappa, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its large, heart-shaped leaves. These expansive leaves are characterized by their distinct venation and are often harvested to prepare various topical applications or decoctions. In many ethnobotanical practices, the leaves are valued for their specific bioactive compounds, which are believed to contribute to the plant's therapeutic properties when applied to the skin or processed for medicinal use.
