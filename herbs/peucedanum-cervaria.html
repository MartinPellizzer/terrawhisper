Peucedanum cervaria

Peucedanum cervaria, commonly known as the deer's fennel, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Belonging to the Apiaceae family, this aromatic plant is characterized by its delicate white umbels. It holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various folk remedies for its unique medicinal properties.

  • Native range: Europe
