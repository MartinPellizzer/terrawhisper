Uncaria lancifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Uncaria lancifolia?

Uncaria lancifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its hierarchical classification, the plant is placed in the order Gentianales and belongs to the family Rubiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Uncaria and the species lancifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Uncaria lancifolia has woody, climbing stems equipped with curved, woody spines and lanceolate leaves that are typically opposite or sub-opposite in arrangement. The foliage consists of relatively thin, elongated blades with acute tips and entire margins. Its woody vines are adapted for climbing through dense forest canopies using specialized hooked structures. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the axils of the leaves to facilitate reproduction. The overall structure is characterized by a liana growth habit typical of the Rubiaceae family.
