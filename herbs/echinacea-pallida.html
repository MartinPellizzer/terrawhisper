Echinacea pallida

Echinacea pallida, commonly known as pale purple coneflower, is a resilient perennial native to the North American prairies. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to bolster the immune system and combat infections, offering a powerful natural ally for holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Echinacea pallida
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Echinacea as the species Echinacea pallida.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across parts of North America. In Eastern Canada, its presence is specifically noted in the province of Ontario. Moving into the North-Central United States, it can be found growing in Illinois and Iowa. The distribution continues southward through the states of Kansas and Missouri. Collectively, these locations define the primary natural habitat for Echinacea pallida.
