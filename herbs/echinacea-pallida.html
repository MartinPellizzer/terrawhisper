Pale coneflower, scientifically known as Echinacea pallida, is a resilient perennial native to North American prairies. Renowned for its distinctive drooping petals and prominent central cones, this medicinal herb is traditionally used to bolster immune function, combat infections, and reduce inflammation, serving as a cornerstone in herbal wellness.
