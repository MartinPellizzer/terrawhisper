Aristolochia baetica

Aristolochia baetica, commonly known as Spanish birthwort, is a perennial climbing herb native to the Mediterranean region. Historically valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant belongs to the Aristolochiaceae family. However, caution is essential due to the presence of nephrotoxic aristolochic acids within its tissues.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia baetica
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Piperales. Finally, it is a member of the family Aristolochiaceae and falls under the genus Aristolochia.
