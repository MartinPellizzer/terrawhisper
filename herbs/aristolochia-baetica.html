Andalusian Dutchman's Pipe, scientifically known as Aristolochia baetica, is a perennial climber native to the Mediterranean landscape. Characterized by its distinctive tubular flowers, this plant holds significant historical importance in traditional herbalism. However, modern science warns of its nephrotoxic properties, necessitating extreme caution regarding its medicinal applications.
