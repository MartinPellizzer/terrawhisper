Indian taro, scientifically known as Leucocasia gigantea, is a versatile tropical plant cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its large, heart-shaped leaves and edible tubers, it offers significant therapeutic benefits. This botanical powerhouse is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various skin ailments across diverse cultures.
