Leucocasia gigantea

Leucocasia gigantea, commonly known as the Giant Milky Vine, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various infections, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Leucocasia gigantea
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Alismatales, it is further categorized into the family Araceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Leucocasia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Leucocasia gigantea, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Vietnam, it is known as dọc mùng, while in Chinese, it is called 大野芋 (and specifically 大野芋 in simplified Chinese). In Japan, the plant is referred to as ハスイモ, and in Indonesia, it is commonly called Talas padang.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several regions across Asia. Within China, it is commonly found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be located in Bangladesh. Additionally, the distribution includes the Indo-China region, specifically within Cambodia.
