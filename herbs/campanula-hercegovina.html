Campanula hercegovina

Campanula hercegovina, commonly known as the Herzegovina Bellflower, is a rare and enchanting perennial native to the rugged Balkan mountains. Renowned for its striking blue blooms, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in regional folk remedies for its soothing properties and unique phytochemical composition.

  • Scientific name: Campanula hercegovina
  • Family: Campanulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Campanulaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Campanula.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Campanula hercegovina, possesses a very specific and restricted natural range. Its geographical distribution is primarily confined to the region of Southeastern Europe. Within this territory, it is specifically found located in the Northwest Balkan Peninsula. Because its habitat is so localized, it is considered an endemic species to this particular area. Consequently, understanding its presence in these mountainous Balkan landscapes is vital for its conservation.
