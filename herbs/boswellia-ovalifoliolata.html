Boswellia ovalifoliolata

Boswellia ovalifoiliolata, commonly known as a prominent member of the frankincense family, is a medicinal shrub native to arid regions. Renowned for its resinous bark, this species contains bioactive compounds with potent anti-inflammatory properties. It serves as a vital natural resource in traditional medicine for treating various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Boswellia ovalifoliolata
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Burseraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Boswellia, specifically as the species Boswellia ovalifoiliolata.
