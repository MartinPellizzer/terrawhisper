Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it falls into the family Linaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Linum and the variety usitatissimum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be identified in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the center of the continent, it is also found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, its range extends through Belgium and into the territories of Czechia and Slovakia. Finally, the geographical reach of this variety includes Germany, completing its presence in Middle Europe.
