Tecoma stans var. sambucifolia

Tecoma stans var. sambucifolia, commonly known as Yellow Bells or Yellow Elder, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, often utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties to treat various ailments in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Tecoma stans var. sambucifolia
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Bignoniaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tecoma as the variety sambucifolia.
