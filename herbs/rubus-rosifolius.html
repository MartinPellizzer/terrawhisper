Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse presence across several key regions within the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its range extends through Cameroon and the islands of the Gulf of Guinea. It is also documented within the borders of Rwanda in the same central region. Moving toward the northeast, the species can be found growing in Ethiopia. Finally, its distribution reaches into East Tropical Africa, specifically within Tanzania.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the raspberry, utilizes various components for its medicinal and nutritional properties. The stems are often characterized by the presence of small prickles, while the central stem provides the necessary structural support for the climbing or arching growth habit. The leaves are typically trifoliate or odd-pinnate and are frequently used in herbal preparations such as teas due to their astringent qualities. Finally, the aggregate fruits are the most well-known part of the plant, offering a rich source of antioxidants and vitamins.
