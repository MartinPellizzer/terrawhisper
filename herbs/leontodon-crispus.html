Leontodon crispus

Leontodoon crispus, commonly known as creeping hawkbit, is a resilient perennial herb found in diverse habitats. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. Its delicate yellow blooms and unique leaf structure make it a distinctive botanical specimen in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Leontodon crispus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Leontodon.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Switzerland. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it can be found throughout Southwestern Europe, including both France and Spain. The species also extends into Southeastern Europe, where it is documented in Albania. Finally, its range continues further east into the territory of Bulgaria.
