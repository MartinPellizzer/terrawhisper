Oryza sativa

Oryza sativa, commonly known as rice, is more than just a global dietary staple. Beyond its nutritional importance, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. Rich in antioxidants and essential minerals, various parts of the plant are utilized to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Oryza sativa
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Poaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Oryza, as part of the species Oryza sativa.

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several key regions within the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence can be found extending through the territories of Czechia and Slovakia. It also maintains a notable distribution within the borders of Hungary. Moving toward the coast, the plant is located in Southwestern Europe specifically within France. Finally, its geographical range reaches as far as the Southwestern European nations of Portugal and Spain.
