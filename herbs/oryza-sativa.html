Oryza molida

Oryza sativa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1050 studies) · ★★★★★

Oryza molida, scientifically known as Oryza sativa, is much more than a global dietary staple. Beyond its nutritional importance, this rice species possesses significant medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential minerals, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, provide energy, and offer soothing restorative benefits in various cultures.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Poaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Oryza, as part of the species Oryza sativa.

This plant is distributed across several key regions within the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence can be found extending through the territories of Czechia and Slovakia. Plant Parts

This plant, Oryza sativa, is primarily cultivated for its nutritional value, but various components offer diverse functional uses. The long, slender leaves are essential for photosynthesis and contribute to the plant's biomass, while the dried straw remains a valuable byproduct used in various traditional applications and as bedding. The reproductive cycle centers on the seeds, which are protected by a protective outer layer known as the rice husks. Once processed, the innermost part, the rice grains, serves as a primary global food source and is the most significant part of the plant for human consumption.
