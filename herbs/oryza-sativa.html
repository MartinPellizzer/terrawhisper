Oryza molida

Oryza sativa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1050 studies) · ★★★★★

Oryza molida, scientifically known as Oryza sativa, is much more than a global dietary staple. Beyond its nutritional importance, this rice species possesses significant medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential minerals, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, provide energy, and offer soothing restorative benefits in various cultures.
