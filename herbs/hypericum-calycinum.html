Hypericum calycinum

Hypericum calycinum, commonly known as the Calyx St. John's Wort, is a resilient perennial herb prized for its striking yellow blooms. Native to diverse landscapes, this plant belongs to the Hypericaceae family. It is valued in herbal traditions for its therapeutic potential and its ability to thrive in various environments.

  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe
