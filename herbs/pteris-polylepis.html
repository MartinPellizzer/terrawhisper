Pteris polylepis

Pteris polylepis, commonly known as the many-leaved brake fern, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Valued in traditional healing practices, it is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties. This fern offers promising pharmacological potential, particularly in treating various ailments through its unique bioactive phytochemical constituents.

  • Scientific name: Pteris polylepis
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Pteridaceae and is situated within the genus Pteris.
