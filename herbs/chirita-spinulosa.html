Chirita spinulosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Chirita spinulosa?

Chirita spinulosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Lamiales. This plant is a member of the family Gesneriaceae, specifically placed within the genus Chirita, and is identified by its unique species designation, spinulosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Chirita spinulosa has a compact, herbaceous growth habit characterized by stems and leaves covered in dense, stiff, spine-like hairs. These bristly trichomes provide a prickly texture to the entire plant structure. The leaves are typically small, ovate to lanceolate in shape, and arranged oppositely along the stem. Its flowers emerge from the leaf axils and feature a tubular corolla that is often pale or tinged with color. The presence of these specialized hairs is a defining morphological trait that distinguishes the species from its smoother relatives.
