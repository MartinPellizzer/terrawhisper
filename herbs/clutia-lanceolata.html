Clutia lanceolata

Clutia lanceolata, commonly known as the lance-leaved clutia, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to the tropical forests of Southeast Asia and the Pacific. Renowned for its glossy, elongated foliage, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its potent bioactive compounds and diverse therapeutic applications.
