Margyricarpus pinnatus

Margyricarpus pinnatus, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This species offers promising pharmacological potential, making it a subject of growing interest for scientific research.

  • Scientific name: Margyricarpus pinnatus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Margyricarpus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the South American continent. It can be found throughout several regions of Western South America, including Bolivia, Colombia, and Ecuador. Additionally, its range extends into Peru within the same western longitudinal belt. Beyond these western territories, the species is also present in the Brazil West-Central region. Consequently, its habitat spans both the Andean corridor and parts of the Brazilian interior.
