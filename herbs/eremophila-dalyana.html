Eremophila dalyana

Eremophila dalyana, commonly known as the Daly River shrub, is a resilient medicinal plant native to the arid regions of Northern Australia. Renowned for its unique pharmacological properties, this species is valued in traditional healing practices for its potential bioactive compounds, offering significant promise for future botanical research and medicine.
