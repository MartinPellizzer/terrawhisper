Garcinia oblongifolia

Garcinia oblongifolia, commonly known as the Wild Mangosteen, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia oblongifolia
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Garcinia oblongifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Clusiaceae and falls under the genus Garcinia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the tropical environment of Hainan.
