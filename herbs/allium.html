Ornamental onion, scientifically known as Allium, is a versatile genus of flowering plants prized for their striking, globe-shaped blooms. Beyond their aesthetic charm in gardens, many species possess potent medicinal properties. Renowned for their antimicrobial and antioxidant qualities, they have been used traditionally to support cardiovascular and immune health.
