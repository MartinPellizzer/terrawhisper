Allium

Ornamental onion

Allium, commonly known as garlic, onion, or leek, is a versatile genus of flowering plants prized for both culinary and medicinal purposes. Rich in organosulfur compounds like allicin, these plants offer potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making them essential staples in traditional wellness and modern nutritional science.

  • Scientific name: Allium
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, the medicinal specimen is identified by its specific genus, Allium.
