Plant Parts

This plant, Luffa aegyptiaca, is a climbing vine known for its diverse structural components, but its seeds are particularly noteworthy in various traditional applications. While the fibrous loofah sponge is derived from the dried, matured fruit, the small, flat seeds contained within the pulp are often utilized for their specific medicinal properties. These seeds are frequently studied or used in folk medicine due to their potential bioactive compounds, distinguishing them from the structural utility of the fruit or the vegetative functions of the leaves and stems.
