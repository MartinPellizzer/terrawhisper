Zinnia elegans

Youth-and-age

Zinnia elegans, commonly known as the common zinnia, is a vibrant annual celebrated for its brilliant, multi-colored blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this resilient plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used in traditional medicine, it possesses diverse bioactive properties that continue to inspire modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Zinnia elegans
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Zinnia.
