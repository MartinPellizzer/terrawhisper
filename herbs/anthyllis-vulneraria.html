Kidney vetch, scientifically known as Anthyllis vulneraria, is a resilient perennial herb native to Europe. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it was once used to treat wounds and skin ailments. Characterized by its vibrant yellow flower clusters, this plant thrives in rocky, coastal habitats, offering unique botanical interest.

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing naturally throughout the coastal and inland areas of Denmark.
