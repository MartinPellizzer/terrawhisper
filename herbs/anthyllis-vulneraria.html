Anthyllis vulneraria

Kidney vetch

Anthyllis vulneraria, commonly known as kidney vetch, is a perennial flowering plant native to Europe. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it was long used to treat wounds and various ailments. Today, this resilient species is recognized for its bright yellow blooms and its ecological role in supporting diverse pollinators.

  • Scientific name: Anthyllis vulneraria
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Anthyllis vulneraria, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is further categorized into the genus Anthyllis.
