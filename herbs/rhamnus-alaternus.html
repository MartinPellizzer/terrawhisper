Rhamnus alaternus

Rhamnus alaternus, commonly known as Italian buckthorn, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its medicinal properties, the plant contains emodin, which possesses significant laxative and antimicrobial qualities. Historically utilized in traditional herbalism, it remains a subject of interest for contemporary pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Rhamnus alaternus
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Rhamnus.
