Tagetes pauciloba

Tagetes pauiloba, commonly known as a species of marigold, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like flavonoids and essential oils, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern science.

  • Scientific name: Tagetes pauciloba
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Asterales and placed in the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Tagetes, specifically identified as the species Tagetes pauciloba.
