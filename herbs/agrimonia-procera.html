Agrimonia procera

Agrimonia procera, commonly known as the Great Agrimonia, is a resilient medicinal shrub valued for its potent bioactive compounds. Native to tropical forest ecosystems, this plant plays a vital role in traditional healing practices, offering therapeutic benefits for inflammation and immune support through its unique phytochemical profile and restorative properties.

  • Scientific name: Agrimonia procera
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe
