Cecropia telenitida

Cecropia telenitida, commonly known as the "Yarumo" in various tropical regions, is a fast-growing tree valued for its diverse medicinal properties. Historically used in traditional healing, its leaves and bark are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Cecropia telenitida
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Urticaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cecropia, specifically as the species Cecropia telenitida.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of South America. In the northern part of the continent, it can be found growing in Venezuela. Moving toward the western side of the continent, the species is also well-established in Colombia. Its presence continues through the western landscape into the nation of Ecuador. Finally, the distribution extends further south along the western coast to include Peru.
