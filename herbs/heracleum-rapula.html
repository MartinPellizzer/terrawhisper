Heracleum rapula, scientifically known as Heracleum rapula, is a perennial herb within the Apiaceae family. Commonly referred to as hogweed, this plant is characterized by its large, umbelliferous flower clusters. While historically used in traditional folk medicine, it requires careful handling due to its potent, skin-sensitizing phytochemical properties.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as hogweed, possesses various morphological features, but its roots are of particular interest in botanical studies.
