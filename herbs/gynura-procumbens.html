Longevity spinach, scientifically known as Gynura procumbens, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in Southeast Asian traditional medicine. Renowned for its nutrient-dense leaves, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-diabetic properties. This resilient plant serves as a natural remedy for promoting overall wellness and managing chronic metabolic conditions.

Plant Parts

This plant, Gynura procumbens, possesses several distinct morphological features that are utilized in both traditional medicine and biotechnological research. The most prominent parts are the broad, succulent leaves, which are widely recognized for their rich concentration of bioactive compounds. These leaves grow from elongated, somewhat fleshy stems that support the upright or sprawling shoots of the herb. Below the surface, a robust system of roots anchors the plant and facilitates nutrient uptake. In laboratory settings, specifically for plant tissue culture and micropropagation, regenerated leaf explants serve as a critical tool for producing clonal, disease-free plantlets, ensuring the preservation of the species' medicinal potency.
