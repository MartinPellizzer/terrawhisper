Gynura procumbens

Gynura procumbens, commonly known as the Viviparous Viviparous or Creeping Katuk, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its nutritional profile, it is widely used in traditional medicine to combat inflammation, manage blood sugar levels, and support overall kidney and cardiovascular health through its antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Gynura procumbens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gynura and the specific species name procumbens.

Common Names

This plant, Gynura procumbens, is recognized by various regional common names depending on the language and location. In Chinese, it is known as 平卧菊三七 (zh-hans), 平臥菊三七 (zh-hant), and 平臥菊三七 (zh-hk).
