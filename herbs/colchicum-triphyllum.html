Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse presence across several regions of the Mediterranean and Balkan peninsulas. In Southwestern Europe, its range extends into the country of Spain. Moving toward the Balkan region, it can be found throughout Southeastern Europe in Bulgaria and Romania. The species is also well-established in the coastal landscapes of Greece.
