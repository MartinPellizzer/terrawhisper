Colchicum triphyllum

Colchicum triphyllum, commonly known as autumn crocus, is a perennial herb distinguished by its striking three-leaflet structure. While highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent alkaloids, it contains colchicine, a compound that requires careful handling due to its high toxicity. This plant remains a significant subject in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Colchicum triphyllum
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Liliales, it is a member of the family Colchicaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Colchicum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse presence across several regions of the Mediterranean and Balkan peninsulas. In Southwestern Europe, its range extends into the country of Spain. Moving toward the Balkan region, it can be found throughout Southeastern Europe in Bulgaria and Romania. The species is also well-established in the coastal landscapes of Greece. Finally, its distribution reaches the island of Sicilia within Southeastern Europe.
