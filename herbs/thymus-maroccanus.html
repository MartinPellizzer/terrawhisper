Plant Parts

This plant, Thymus maroccanus, is highly valued in herbal medicine primarily for its aromatic properties, which are concentrated within its essential oils. These oils are extracted from the plant's volatile components, typically found in the leaves and flowering stems, and contain potent bioactive compounds such as thymol and carvacrol. These chemical constituents are responsible for the plant's strong medicinal efficacy, providing significant antimicrobial, antioxidant, and antiseptic qualities used in various therapeutic applications.
