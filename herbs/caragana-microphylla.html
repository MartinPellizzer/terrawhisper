Caragana microphylla

Caragana microphylla, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in South American biodiversity, offers remarkable therapeutic potential. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Understanding its phytochemical profile is essential for exploring new advancements in natural pharmacology and holistic healing.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling within the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Caragana.
