Ardisia sieboldii

Ardisia sieboldii, commonly known as the coral berry or Japanese ardisia, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its glossy leaves and vibrant red berries, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal practices to treat inflammation and support overall wellness through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Ardisia sieboldii
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ardisia, specifically as the species Ardisia sieboldii.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ardisia sieboldii, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Chinese, it is known as 多枝紫金牛, specifically called 多枝紫金牛 in mainland China (zh-cn) and 樹杞 in Taiwan (zh-tw). In Japan, the species is called モクタチバナ (ja), while in the Pwn language, it is identified as ljaludru (pwn).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of Eastern Asia. In mainland China, its distribution is specifically concentrated within the Southeast region. Its range extends significantly into Japan, encompassing various island territories. Specifically, it can be found inhabiting the Nansei-shoto and Ogasawara-shoto island chains. Finally, the species is also documented as growing in Taiwan.
