Canthium coromandelicum

Canthium coromandelicum, commonly known as the Indian Canteth, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. It serves as a vital natural resource for treating various ailments and promoting wellness.

  • Scientific name: Canthium coromandelicum
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae and belongs to the genus Cantium. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined as Cantium coroandelicum.
