Syzygium jambos

Rose apple

Syzygium jambos, commonly known as Rose Apple, is a versatile tropical evergreen celebrated for its aromatic flowers and edible fruit. Beyond its culinary appeal, this medicinal plant holds significant therapeutic value, traditionally used to treat digestive issues, fever, and inflammation, making it a cornerstone of various herbal healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium jambos
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Syzygium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. Within the Macaronesian islands, it can be found specifically in Madeira. Its presence extends into West Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Furthermore, the species is located in West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the Central African Republic. Finally, its range reaches the Gulf of Guinea Islands in the West-Central tropical zone.
