Pachysandra terminalis

Pachysandra terminalis, commonly known as the Terminalis plant, is a significant medicinal species valued in traditional herbal practices. Characterized by its unique botanical properties, it is frequently studied for its bioactive compounds. This plant offers promising potential in pharmacology, serving as a natural resource for developing diverse therapeutic treatments.

  • Family: Buxaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Buxales and the family Buxaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pachysandra.
