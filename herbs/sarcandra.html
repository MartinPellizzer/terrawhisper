Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast. Its presence also extends to the tropical island of Hainan. Beyond Chinese borders, the species is naturally occurring in Japan within Eastern Asia.
