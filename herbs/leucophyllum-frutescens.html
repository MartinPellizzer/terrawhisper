Texas sage, scientifically known as Leucophyllum frutescens, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the southwestern United States. Renowned for its striking purple blooms, it is valued in traditional medicine for its potential therapeutic properties. This hardy plant thrives in arid climates, offering both ornamental beauty and medicinal interest.
