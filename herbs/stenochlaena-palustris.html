Swamp vine fern, scientifically known as Stenochlaena palustris, is a versatile medicinal plant found in tropical wetlands. Renowned in traditional medicine, its rhizomes and leaves are utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues. This fern represents a vital resource in ethnobotanical healing practices.
