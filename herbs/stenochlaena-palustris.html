Stenochlaena palustris

Stenochlaena palustris, commonly known as the swamp fern, is a versatile medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its rhizomes and leaves are frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This resilient fern offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Stenochlaena palustris
  • Family: Aspleniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Aspleniaceae and is identified by the genus Stenochlaena.
