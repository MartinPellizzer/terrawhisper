Solanum lyratum

Solanum lyratum, commonly known as the lyre-leaf nightshade, is a versatile medicinal herb found primarily in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Solanum lyratum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and family Solanaceae, it is classified under the genus Solanum. Specifically, its scientific name is Solanum lyratum.
