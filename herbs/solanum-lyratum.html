lyreleaf nightshade, scientifically known as Solanum lyratum, is a perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its deeply lobed, lyre-shaped leaves, this plant belongs to the Solanaceae family. While it holds historical significance in traditional medicine, careful identification is essential due to the presence of alkaloids.

This plant, Solanum lyratum, is typically utilized in traditional medicinal practices through the use of its dried whole herbs. This comprehensive form of the plant includes the entire botanical structure—comprising the leaves, stems, and flowers—once they have been harvested and undergoes a careful drying process to preserve their therapeutic properties. By employing the dried whole herbs, practitioners ensure that the full spectrum of phytochemical compounds present throughout the plant is available for preparation into decoctions or infusions.
