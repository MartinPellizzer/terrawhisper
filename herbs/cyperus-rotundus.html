Cyperus rotundus

Cypterus rotundus, commonly known as Nut Grass, is a perennial sedge widely utilized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic tubers, this plant possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antispasmodic properties. It is frequently employed to treat digestive issues, menstrual discomfort, and various skin ailments across diverse global cultures.

  • Scientific name: Cyperus rotundus
  • Family: Cyperaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, it is a member of the family Cyperaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Cyperus, where it is known as Cyperus rotundus.
