Pelargonium graveolens

Pelargonium graveolens, commonly known as geranium, is a cherished medicinal plant prized for its aromatic foliage. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently used in aromatherapy to reduce anxiety and soothe skin irritations. This versatile herb offers powerful antioxidant benefits, making it a staple in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Pelargonium graveolens
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae, it is classified under the genus Pelargonium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence can be noted in the area of Corse. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, it is also found within Sicilia. The species extends into the Macaronesia region, specifically inhabiting the Canary Is. Furthermore, its distribution reaches into South Tropical Africa in Zimbabwe and extends to the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa.
