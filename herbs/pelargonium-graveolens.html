Rose-scent Storksbill

Pelargonium graveolens · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (701 studies) · ★★★★★

Rose-scented Storksbill, scientifically known as Pelargonium graveolens, is a highly aromatic perennial herb celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its exquisite floral fragrance, it is primarily cultivated for essential oil extraction. This versatile plant offers diverse medicinal benefits, including skin healing, stress relief, and potent antimicrobial applications.
