Rose-scent Storksbill

Pelargonium graveolens · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (701 studies) · ★★★★★

Rose-scented Storksbill, scientifically known as Pelargonium graveolens, is a highly aromatic perennial herb celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its exquisite floral fragrance, it is primarily cultivated for essential oil extraction. This versatile plant offers diverse medicinal benefits, including skin healing, stress relief, and potent antimicrobial applications.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence can be noted in the area of Corse. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, it is also found within Sicilia. The species extends into the Macaronesia region, specifically inhabiting the Canary Is. Furthermore, its distribution reaches into South Tropical Africa in Zimbabwe and extends to the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as geranium, is highly valued in aromatherapy and traditional medicine for its aromatic components. The most significant part used for therapeutic purposes is the essential oil, which is meticulously extracted from the aromatic leaves and stems. Within the specialized leaf tissues, various chemical compounds are synthesized, which are then concentrated to produce high-quality essential oils used in perfumes and healing salves.
