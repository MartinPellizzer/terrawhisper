Euterpe precatoria

Euterpe precatoria, commonly known as the solitary açaí palm, is a vital medicinal plant native to the Amazon rainforest. Unlike its multi-stemmed relatives, this species grows as a single trunk. It is highly valued for its nutrient-rich fruits and bark, which offer potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory therapeutic properties.
