Pyracantha angustifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Pyracantha angustifolia?

Pyracantha angustifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, following the evolutionary lineage of the order Rosales. As a member of the family Rosaceae, it is closely related to many other well-known flowering shrubs and trees. Ultimately, its specific biological identity is defined by its placement in the genus Pyracantha and its unique species designation, angustifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pyracantha angustifolia has narrow, linear, dark green leaves that measure between 1 and 5 centimeters in width and are arranged alternately along woody, thorny stems. These stems are characterized by sharp, woody spines that emerge from the axils of the foliage. Small, white, five-petaled flowers bloom in dense clusters during the spring season. Following the flowering period, the plant produces numerous small, fleshy berries that transition from green to bright orange or red. The overall growth habit is that of a spreading, densely branched shrub.
