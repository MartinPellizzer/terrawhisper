Kalanchoe laxiflora

Kalanchoe laxiflora, commonly known as the Laxiflora Kalanchoe, is a striking succulent native to Madagascar. Characterized by its fleshy, rosette-shaped leaves and bell-shaped, pendulous flowers, this resilient plant thrives in well-drained soil. It is highly valued by collectors for its unique architectural beauty and low-maintenance, ornamental appeal.

  • Scientific name: Kalanchoe laxiflora
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifragales, it falls under the family Crassulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Kalanchoe.

Distribution

Its distribution extends into Northern Africa, where populations are established in both Algeria and Morocco.
