Canarium odontophyllum

Canaarium odontophyllum, commonly known as the Canary Laurel, is a versatile tropical tree valued for its significant medicinal properties. Native to coastal forests, its bark and leaves are traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat inflammation, respiratory ailments, and skin infections, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Canarium odontophyllum
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is further categorized into the family Burseraceae. Ultimately, its scientific classification places it within the genus Canarium, specifically the species Canarium odontophyllum.
