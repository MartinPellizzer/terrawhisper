Bog rush, scientifically known as Juncus effusus, is a versatile perennial plant commonly found in wetlands and marshes. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it has been traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin irritations, and digestive issues. This hardy species offers significant therapeutic potential in various natural healing practices.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the coastal or wetland areas of Denmark and Finland. Furthermore, its presence extends to the islands of Føroyar and Iceland. The species is also widely documented throughout Great Britain. Together, these locations highlight the plant's preference for Northern European environments.
