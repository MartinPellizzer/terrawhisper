Bog rush, scientifically known as Juncus effusus, is a versatile perennial plant commonly found in wetlands and marshes. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it has been traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin irritations, and digestive issues. This hardy species offers significant therapeutic potential in various natural healing practices.
