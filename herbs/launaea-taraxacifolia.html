Launaea taraxacifolia

Launaea taraxacifolia, commonly known as the dandelion plant, is a versatile medicinal herb widely celebrated in traditional pharmacology. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health, detoxify the liver, and promote renal function, making it a cornerstone of natural wellness therapies.

  • Scientific name: Launaea taraxacifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Launaea taraxacifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is categorized under the genus Launaea.
