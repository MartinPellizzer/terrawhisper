Oenothera elata subsp. hookeri

Oenothera elata hookeri

Oenothera elata subsp. hookeri, commonly known as the Hooker's Evening Primrose, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this subspecies contains bioactive compounds often studied for their anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering significant potential in natural therapeutic applications and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Oenothera elata subsp. hookeri
  • Family: Onagraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, taxonomically known as Oenothera elata subsp. hookeri, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Myrtales and the family Onagraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Oenothera.
