Ribes himalense

Ribes himalense, commonly known as the Himalayan currant, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its nutrient-dense berries, it serves as a traditional remedy for various ailments. Its antioxidant properties and therapeutic extracts offer significant potential in supporting holistic wellness and treating systemic inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Ribes himalense
  • Family: Grossulariaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Grossulariaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ribes, specifically as the species Ribes himalense.
