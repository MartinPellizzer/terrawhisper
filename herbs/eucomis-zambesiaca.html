Eucomis zambesiaca

Eucomis zambesiaca, commonly known as the Pineapple Lily, is a striking medicinal plant native to Africa. Renowned for its unique rosette of flowers, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments such as digestive issues, skin inflammations, and even certain types of tumors through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Eucomis zambesiaca
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Eucomis zambesiaca, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Eucomis.
