What's the taxonomical classification of Pedicularis superba?

Pedicularis superba belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, the species is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Pedicularis, with its specific designation being the species superba.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pedicularis superba has a tall, erect stem that typically reaches heights between 20 and 60 centimeters and is characterized by dense, tubular, bilabiate flowers colored in shades of pink or red. Its leaves are primarily basal and deeply pinnatifid, often divided into narrow, linear segments that reduce surface area. The stem leaves are smaller and more widely spaced than the basal foliage, tapering as they ascend the plant.
