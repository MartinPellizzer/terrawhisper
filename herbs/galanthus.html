Galanthus

Snowdrops

Galanthus, commonly known as snowdrops, is a delicate perennial bulb celebrated for its early spring blooms. Beyond its aesthetic charm, this medicinal plant holds historical significance in traditional healing. While beautiful, it contains potent alkaloids, requiring careful study and precise application for any potential therapeutic or pharmacological use.

  • Scientific name: Galanthus
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Galanthus.
