Snowdrops, scientifically known as Galanthus, is a captivating genus of bulbous perennials celebrated for their early spring blooms. Beyond their delicate, bell-shaped white flowers, these plants hold significant medicinal potential. Rich in bioactive alkaloids like galantamine, they offer valuable pharmacological properties, particularly in treating neurological disorders and cognitive decline.
