Atriplex nummularia

Atriplex nummularia, commonly known as Silver Saltbush, is a resilient, woody shrub native to arid Australian landscapes. Renowned for its distinctive silvery foliage, this hardy plant thrives in saline soils. Beyond its ornamental value, it holds traditional significance, offering potential medicinal properties within its unique, drought-tolerant biological profile.
