False arnica, scientifically known as Heterotheca inuloides, is a resilient perennial herb native to North America. Often found in open woodlands, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Its bright, daisy-like blooms make it both a botanical interest and a medicinal resource.
