Heterotheca inuloides

Heterotheca inuloides, commonly known as golden aster, is a resilient perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. It is often utilized in herbal remedies to support skin health and address various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Heterotheca inuloides
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is categorized under the genus Heterotheca.
