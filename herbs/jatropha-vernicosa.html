Jatropha vernicosa

Jatrophas vernicosa, commonly known as the sea physic nut, is a resilient coastal shrub prized in traditional medicine. Found across tropical shorelines, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Its leaves and latex are frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering vast potential for modern pharmacological research.
