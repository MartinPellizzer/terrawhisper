Hypericum triquetrifolium

Hypericum triquetrifolium, commonly known as threecornered St. John's wort, is a distinctive perennial herb belonging to the Hypericaceae family. Characterized by its unique, three-angled stems and vibrant yellow blossoms, this plant is valued in ethnobotany for its potential therapeutic properties and its role within diverse natural ecosystems worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum triquetrifolium
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Hypericum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern focused primarily within the Mediterranean region. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is documented across Spain, France, and the Balearic Islands. Moving eastward, the species is also found throughout parts of Southeastern Europe. Specifically, its range extends into the territories of Albania and Greece. This distribution highlights a preference for the unique climates found along the southern European coastline.
