Pseudobombax ellipticum

Pseudobombax ellipticum, commonly known as the Elliptic Bombax, is a significant medicinal tree found in tropical forests. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant—including its bark and leaves—are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as inflammation, skin disorders, and digestive issues effectively.

  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Pacific
