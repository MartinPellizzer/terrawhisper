What's the taxonomical classification of Adenocaulon himalaicum?

Adenocauloon himalaicum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Adenocaulon, and its unique species designation, himalaicum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Adenocauloon himalaicum has a perennial habit characterized by a woody base, spreading stolons, and densely tomentose stems and leaves covered in white or grayish hairs. The leaves are typically lanceolate to ovate in shape and feature entire or slightly serrated margins. Its inflorescence consists of numerous small, white, discoid flower heads arranged in dense terminal clusters. These flower heads are supported by hairy involucral bracts that protect the developing florets. The overall vegetative structure is adapted to alpine environments through its thick, protective pubescence.
