Dolichandra unguis-cati

Cat's claw creeper

Dolichaandrast unguis-cati, commonly known as Cat's Claw, is a powerful woody vine native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned in traditional medicine, this botanical powerhouse contains bioactive oxindole alkaloids. It is widely utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, immune-boosting, and antioxidant properties, offering significant therapeutic potential for various health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Dolichandra unguis-cati
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa
