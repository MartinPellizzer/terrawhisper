Solanum aethiopicum

Solanum aethiopicum, commonly known as the Ethiopian eggplant, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Africa. Renowned for its nutritional profile, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This potent plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Solanum aethiopicum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is classified under the genus Solanum. Specifically, this medicinal species is identified by its scientific name, Solanum aethiopicum.
