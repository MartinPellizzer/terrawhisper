What's the taxonomical classification of Schoenoplectus lineolatus?

Schoenoplectus lineolatus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further organized under the order Poales. It is a representative of the family Cyperaceae, falling under the genus Schoenoplectus, and is specifically identified by its species name, lineolatus.
