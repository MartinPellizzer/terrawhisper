Croton discolor

Croton discolor, commonly known as the Copperleaf plant, is a striking ornamental shrub celebrated for its dramatic, bronze-tinted foliage. Native to tropical regions, it is frequently used in landscaping for its vibrant colors. Beyond its beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value in traditional medicine for various healing purposes.
