Rhus taitensis

Rhus taitensis, commonly known as the Taiyuan sumac, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional pharmacopoeia. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for developing new therapeutic agents in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Rhus taitensis
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, falling into the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rhus.
