Scientific literature: Very Extensive (263 studies) · ★★★★★

Ackee, scientifically known as Blighia sapida, is a tropical fruit tree native to West Africa. While its ripe fruit is a beloved culinary staple in Jamaica, the unripe seeds and leaves contain toxic hypoglycin. Understanding its medicinal properties and chemical potency is essential for safe consumption and traditional uses.

This plant is widely distributed across several specific regions within West Tropical Africa. In the southern part of this zone, it can be found growing in the countries of Benin and Ghana.
