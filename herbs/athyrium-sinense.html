Athyrium sinense

What's the taxonomical classification of Athyrium sinense?

Athyrium sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and is situated in the order Polypodiales. Taxonomically, it is categorized under the family Aspleniaceae, which contains the genus Athyrium, eventually reaching its specific designation as the species sinense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Athyrium sinense has large, bipinnate-pinnatifid fronds that typically measure between 30 and 90 centimeters in length and feature finely serrated, lanceolate leaflets. Each pinnule possesses a distinctively tapered shape with delicate, pointed margins that contribute to a feathery texture. The underside of the fronds is characterized by the presence of small, clustered sori located near the leaf margins.
