Bidens biternata

Bidens bipinnata, commonly known as Spanish parsley or beggarticks, is a resilient medicinal herb found in diverse habitats. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, this versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Bidens biternata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Bidens.
