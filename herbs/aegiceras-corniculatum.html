Aegiceras corniculatum

Aegiceras cornifolium, commonly known as the black mangrove, is a resilient shrub found in coastal mangrove ecosystems. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is utilized in traditional healing to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues. This plant plays a vital role in both ecological stability and ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Aegiceras corniculatum
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Aegiceras, specifically as the species Aegiceras corniculatum.
