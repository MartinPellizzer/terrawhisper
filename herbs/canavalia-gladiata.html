Scimitar-bean, scientifically known as Canavalia gladiata, is a robust, climbing legume native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive, sword-shaped pods, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is increasingly studied for its diverse bioactive compounds, offering promising therapeutic potential in traditional medicine and modern pharmacological research.
