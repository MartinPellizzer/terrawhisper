Iryanthera juruensis

Iryanthera juruensis, commonly known as the "tree of life" in certain Amazonian cultures, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical rainforests. Valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation and skin ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and holistic healing practices.

  • Family: Myristicaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
