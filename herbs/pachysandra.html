Mountain spurge, scientifically known as Pachysandra, is a resilient evergreen groundcover prized for its lush, waxy foliage. Thriving in shaded, woodland environments, this hardy perennial offers excellent soil coverage. Its ability to withstand diverse conditions makes it a popular choice for gardeners seeking low-maintenance, textured landscape accents.
