Amaranthus cruentus

Amaranthus cruentuus, commonly known as Red Amaranth, is a vibrant medicinal plant prized for its deep crimson foliage and nutritional density. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and essential minerals, it serves as a powerful functional food. Traditionally used to support blood health, it offers significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Amaranthus cruentus
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its classification within the genus Amaranthus.
