Amaranth, scientifically known as Amaranthus cruentuus, is a vibrant medicinal plant celebrated for its deep red foliage and nutritional density. Renowned in traditional healing, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This versatile herb serves as both a nutrient-rich leafy vegetable and a therapeutic remedy in various cultures.
