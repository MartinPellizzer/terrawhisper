Iris bungei

Iris bungei, commonly known as the Bungei Iris, is a striking perennial native to southern Africa. Renowned for its vibrant, bell-shaped blooms, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize various parts of the plant to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its profound importance in regional folk medicine.

  • Scientific name: Iris bungei
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Iris bunglei, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales and the family Iridaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes within the genus Iris.
