Indian soapberry

Indian soapberry, scientifically known as Sapindus mukorossi, is a versatile evergreen tree cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its saponin-rich fruit, it serves as a natural cleansing agent and therapeutic tool. Historically used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, this plant remains a vital botanical resource.
