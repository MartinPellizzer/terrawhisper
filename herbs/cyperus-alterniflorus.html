Cyperus alterniflorus

Cypterus alterniflorus, commonly known as the alternating-flower sedge, is a delicate perennial herb native to diverse wetland ecosystems. Characterized by its unique floral arrangement, this plant plays a vital role in supporting local biodiversity. Understanding its botanical features and ecological significance offers valuable insights into wetland conservation efforts.

  • Scientific name: Cyperus alterniflorus
  • Family: Cyperaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, falling into the family Cyperaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Cyperus.
