Excoecaria agallocha

Gewa

Excoecaria agallocha, commonly known as the Milky Euphorbe, is a versatile medicinal plant found in coastal mangrove ecosystems. Renowned for its milky latex, this species possesses significant therapeutic properties, including antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin diseases.

  • Scientific name: Excoecaria agallocha
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Excoecaria.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Eastern and Southern Asia. Within China, its presence is documented in both the Hainan province and the broader China Southeast area. It can also be found throughout the Nansei-shotto islands of Eastern Asia. The geographical range extends to the island of Taiwan, further establishing its presence in the East Asian region. Additionally, its distribution reaches into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within Bangladesh.
