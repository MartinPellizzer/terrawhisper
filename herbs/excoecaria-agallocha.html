Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Eastern and Southern Asia. Within China, its presence is documented in both the Hainan province and the broader China Southeast area. It can also be found throughout the Nansei-shotto islands of Eastern Asia. The geographical range extends to the island of Taiwan, further establishing its presence in the East Asian region. Additionally, its distribution reaches into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within Bangladesh.
