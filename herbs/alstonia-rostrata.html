Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central regions as well as on the island of Hainan. Its range extends into the Indo-China peninsula, where it is native to several countries. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the territories of Cambodia and Laos. Furthermore, the species is also distributed across Myanmar.
