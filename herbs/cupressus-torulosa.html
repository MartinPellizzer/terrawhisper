Himalayan cypress, scientifically known as Cupressus torulosa, is a majestic evergreen native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its resilience and aromatic foliage, this coniferous species holds significant ethnobotanical value, frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and restorative properties across various cultures.
