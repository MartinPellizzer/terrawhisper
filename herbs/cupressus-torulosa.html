Cupressus torulosa

Himalayan cypress

Cupressus torulosa, commonly known as the Torulosa Cypress, is a majestic evergreen conifer native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its aromatic, resinous wood, this resilient species holds significant ecological and medicinal value, traditionally utilized in various cultural practices for its unique bioactive properties and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Cupressus torulosa
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales, it is classified in the family Cupressaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cupressus.
