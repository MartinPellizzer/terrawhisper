Himalayan cypress, scientifically known as Cupressus torulosa, is a majestic evergreen native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its resilience and aromatic foliage, this coniferous species holds significant ethnobotanical value, frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and restorative properties across various cultures.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Cupressus torulosa, exhibits a specific distribution across high-altitude regions of Asia. Within China, its presence is primarily concentrated in the Tibet region. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the mountainous terrain of Nepal. The species is also distributed across various parts of Pakistan. Additionally, it thrives within the unique ecological zones of the West Himalaya.
