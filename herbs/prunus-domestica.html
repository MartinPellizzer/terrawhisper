Plum tree, scientifically known as Prunus domestica, is a versatile fruit-bearing shrub celebrated for both its culinary and medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and dietary fiber, its fruits and leaves offer therapeutic benefits, including aiding digestion, supporting heart health, and providing potent anti-inflammatory effects in traditional herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Prunus domestica, commonly known as the plum, is valued for its medicinal and nutritional properties, which are primarily derived from its fruits. The fruit is a fleshy drupe that contains a sweet pulp surrounding a single, hard stone or pit. In various traditional practices, the fruit is utilized for its high fiber content to aid digestion, while the dried fruit is often used to manage constipation and promote regular bowel movements.
