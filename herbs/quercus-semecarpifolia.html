Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of Asia. In Western Asia, it can be found growing within the territory of Afghanistan. Its range extends into China, specifically inhabiting the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in Assam and throughout the East Himalaya region. Finally, its presence is also well-established in the mountainous landscapes of Nepal.
