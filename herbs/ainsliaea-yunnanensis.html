Ainsliaea yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. This plant is further categorized into the family Asteraceae, which is the sunflower or daisy family, and specifically resides within the genus Ainsliaea. Its complete scientific designation is defined by its specific epithet, making it the species Ainsliaea yunnanensis.
