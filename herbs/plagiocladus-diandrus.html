Plagiocladus diandrus

Plagiocladus diandrus, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in certain tropical regions, holds great promise in ethnobotany. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this species is studied for its potential therapeutic properties. Understanding its biological activities offers vital insights into developing new treatments for various human health conditions.

  • Family: Phyllanthaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Plaigiocladus and the specific species name Plaigiocladus diandrus.
