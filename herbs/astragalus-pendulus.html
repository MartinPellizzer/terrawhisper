Astragalus pendulus

Astragalus pendulus, commonly known as the drooping milkvetch, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific rocky terrains. Characterized by its delicate, hanging pea-like flowers and fine foliage, this plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem, contributing to soil stability and supporting local pollinators through its nectar.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus pendulus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its scientific genus name, Astragalus.
