Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning across several key regions. In the Macaronesia archipelago, its presence is noted in Cape Verde. The species also extends into West Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Benin. Furthermore, it is distributed throughout the Gambia and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, its range includes the nation of Guinea within the West Tropical African zone.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Carica, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties. The broad green leaf is often harvested to prepare infusions or topical applications, while the fruit provides a concentrated source of nutrients and bioactive compounds. Within the fruit, the seed contains potent essential oils that are highly valued in traditional remedies. Additionally, the hardy root is frequently processed into decoctions, serving as a foundational element in many therapeutic treatments.
