Lippia macrophylla

Lippia macrophylla, commonly known as erva-cidreira in Brazil, is a resilient shrub valued for its potent therapeutic properties. Native to tropical regions, this medicinal plant is rich in essential oils. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, alleviate anxiety, and provide soothing anti-inflammatory effects.

  • Scientific name: Lippia macrophylla
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Verbenaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Lippia.
