Hippeastrum puniceum subsp. puniceum

Hippeastrum puniceum subsp. puniceum, commonly known as the Red Amaryllis, is a striking ornamental plant valued for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, various cultures utilize its bulbous roots in traditional medicine to address inflammatory conditions, promoting healing through its unique bioactive compounds and botanical properties.

  • Scientific name: Hippeastrum puniceum subsp. puniceum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asparagales, it is classified in the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Hippeastrum.
