This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across the African continent. In Northern Africa, it can be found specifically within the borders of Tunisia. Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the Gulf of Guinea Islands and the nation of Rwanda.
