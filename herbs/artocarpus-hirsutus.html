Artocarpus hirsutus

Artocarpus hirsutus, commonly known as the hairy jackfruit, is a versatile medicinal tree native to South Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant—including leaves, bark, and fruit—are utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Artocarpus hirsutus
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Rosales and the family Moraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artocarpus.
