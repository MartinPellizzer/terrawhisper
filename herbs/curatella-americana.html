Lixeira do cerrado, scientifically known as Curatella americana, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its profound therapeutic properties, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments, offering a natural remedy deeply rooted in regional biodiversity.
