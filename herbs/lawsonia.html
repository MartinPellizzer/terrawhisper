Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, it is widely documented to grow within the borders of Algeria and Libya. Its presence is also noted throughout the territories of Morocco and Tunisia. Moving toward the West Tropical Africa region, the species can be found in Benin. This range highlights the plant's ability to thrive in both Mediterranean and tropical environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as henna, relies primarily on its vibrant green leaf for its most significant medicinal and cosmetic applications. The leaf serves as the main source of lawsone, the natural dye responsible for the plant's characteristic staining properties. When harvested and dried, these leaves are ground into a fine powder used extensively in traditional medicine for its cooling effects and in beauty rituals for temporary skin and hair coloring.
