Senna cana

Sennat cana, commonly known as Senna or Alexandrian cassia, is a potent medicinal shrub prized for its powerful laxative properties. Rich in anthraquinone glycosides, it effectively stimulates intestinal motility to treat acute constipation. Widely utilized in traditional and modern herbal medicine, it remains a staple for digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Senna cana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
