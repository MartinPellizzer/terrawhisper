Senna cana, scientifically known as Senna cana, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, it contains anthraquinone glycosides that stimulate bowel movements. Beyond digestive health, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to support detoxification and skin wellness.
