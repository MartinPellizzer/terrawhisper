Rourea obliquifoliolata, scientifically known as Rourea obliquifoliolatta, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional herbal medicine. Native to tropical regions, this plant is widely recognized for its potent bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized in various folk remedies to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various skin conditions.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is recorded in Nigeria. Moving into the West-Central Tropical Africa zone, it can be found across several distinct territories. Specifically, its range extends through the Central African Republic, Cameroon, and Congo. Furthermore, the species is also documented within the enclave of Cabinda.
