What's the taxonomical classification of Tongoloa silaifolia?

Tongoloa silaifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Apiales. As a member of the family Apiaceae, it is categorized under the genus Tongoloa, with its specific designation being the species silaifolia.
