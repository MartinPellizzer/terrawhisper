Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical presence across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its distribution can be traced specifically to Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species is also found within Cameroon. The plant extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the territory of Chad. Finally, its range in Northeast Tropical Africa includes the nations of Djibouti and Eritrea.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Rudraksha or Draksha, features distinctive medicinal properties found within its various structures, particularly the flower. The flower of the Woodfordia species is typically small, often appearing in dense, terminal panicles that can give the plant a soft, feathery appearance. These blooms are essential to the plant's reproductive cycle and are frequently studied for their specific phytochemical compositions, which contribute to the overall therapeutic profile of the species.
