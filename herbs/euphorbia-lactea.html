Euphorbia lactea

Euphorbia lactea, commonly known as the Milk Bush, is a striking succulent prized for its unique, columnar growth and sculptural form. Native to tropical regions, this resilient plant features striking variegation and a distinctive milky sap. It serves as both a captivating ornamental centerpiece and a fascinating botanical specimen.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia lactea
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is further categorized into the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its scientific identity is defined by its placement within the genus Euphorbia.
