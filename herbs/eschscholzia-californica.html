Eschscholzia californica

California poppy

Eschscholzia californica, commonly known as California poppy, is a vibrant perennial native to western North America. Renowned for its striking orange blooms, this plant is highly valued in herbal medicine. It is traditionally utilized for its sedative and anxiolytic properties, helping to promote relaxation and support healthy sleep patterns.

  • Scientific name: Eschscholzia californica
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe
