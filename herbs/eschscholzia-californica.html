California poppy, scientifically known as Eschscholzia californica, is a vibrant perennial native to Western North America. Renowned for its striking orange blooms, this plant is highly valued in herbal medicine for its sedative and anxiolytic properties, offering a gentle, natural approach to promoting relaxation and improving sleep quality.
