Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range within the temperate regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is primarily concentrated throughout the Manchuria region. Furthermore, the distribution extends across the neighboring territories of Eastern Asia. Specifically, this subspecies can be found growing naturally in Korea. These locations define the natural habitat where the species is known to thrive.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Syringa oblata subsp. dilatata, is characterized by its prominent and fragrant flowers that serve as a key identifying feature. The flowers are typically arranged in dense, terminal panicles, displaying a beautiful array of colors ranging from white to various shades of lilac or purple. These blossoms are not only visually striking but also play a vital role in the plant's medicinal profile and its ability to attract pollinators to the garden.
