, scientifically known as Aristolochia macrocarpac, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to tropical regions. Often recognized by its distinctive, heart-shaped leaves and unique flowering structures, it has historically been utilized in traditional medicine. However, modern research warns of its potential nephrotoxicity and carcinogenicity due to aristolochic acids.

Distribution

This plant, Aristolochia macrocarpa, is primarily found throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Its natural habitat includes the lush landscapes of Ghana and Guinea. Additionally, the species can be located within the borders of the Ivory Coast. It is also known to grow across parts of Liberia. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant extends into Nigeria.
