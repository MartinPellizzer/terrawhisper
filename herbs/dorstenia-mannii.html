Dorstenia mannii

Dorsetnia mannii, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant, holds great importance in traditional healing practices. Found primarily in tropical regions, this species is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its phytochemical constituents to uncover potential applications in modern pharmacology and natural disease management.

  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, it is further categorized under the family Moraea and the genus Dorstenia.
