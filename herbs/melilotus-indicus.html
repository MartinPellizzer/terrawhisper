Yellow sweet clover, scientifically known as Melilotus indicus, is a versatile medicinal herb widely used in traditional healing. Renowned for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and wound-healing properties, it is frequently employed to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive concerns, offering a natural approach to various therapeutic wellness needs.
