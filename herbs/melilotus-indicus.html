Yellow sweet clover, scientifically known as Melilotus indicus, is a versatile medicinal herb widely used in traditional healing. Renowned for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and wound-healing properties, it is frequently employed to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive concerns, offering a natural approach to various therapeutic wellness needs.

The reproduction of Melilotus indicus is characterized by a sexual process involving bisexual flowers, with asexual reproduction being entirely absent. The plant exhibits a flowering period that can be highly variable, typically centered around April, and features small yellow flowers ranging in length from 0.2 cm to 0.25 cm. Following successful pollination, the plant develops fruit in the form of a dehiscence-capable pod.
