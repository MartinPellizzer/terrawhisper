Melilotus indicus

Yellow sweet clover

Melilotus indicus, commonly known as Indian Sweet Clover, is a versatile medicinal herb widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties, this plant serves as a vital remedy for treating skin disorders, respiratory issues, and various internal ailments through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Melilotus indicus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Melilotus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Melilotus indicus, is referred to by various regional names across different languages. In Estonia, it is called india mesiakas (et), while in Germany, it is known as Kleinblütiger Steinklee (de). It carries the name Hind xəşənbülü in Azerbaijan (az), and is identified as intianmesikkä in Finland (fi). Additionally, in Upper Sorbian, the plant is called Kwěćikaty komonc (hsb).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several key regions in the European continent. In Northern Europe, its distribution includes both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the central part of the continent, the species is also found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, it can be identified in countries such as Austria and Belgium. Furthermore, its geographical range extends into the Czechia-Slovakia region.
