What's the taxonomical classification of Asarum forbesii?

Asarum forbesii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the hierarchy of classification, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic ranks, this plant is organized under the order Piperales and is a member of the family Aristolochiaceae. Finally, it is classified within the genus Asarum, with the specific epithet forbesii identifying the individual species.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause neurological damage, gastrointestinal distress, and potential liver toxicity due to its high concentration of aristolochic acids. Consuming these compounds can lead to severe kidney damage and increased risks of bladder cancer. Some individuals may experience nausea, vomiting, or abdominal pain shortly after ingestion. Long-term exposure is particularly dangerous because the chemical components are known to be carcinogenic. Therefore, the medicinal use of this species carries significant and life-threatening health risks.
