What's the taxonomical classification of Lophatherum sinense?

Lophatherum sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized into the order Poales and is a member of the family Poaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Lophatherum and is distinguished as the species sinese.
