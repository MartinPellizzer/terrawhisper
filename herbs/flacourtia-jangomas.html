Flacourtia jangomas

Indian coffee plum

Flacourtia jangomas, commonly known as the Jangomas or Jangoma tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Flacourtia jangomas
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Malpighiales, falling within the family Salicaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Flacourtia, as part of the species Flacourtia jangomas.
