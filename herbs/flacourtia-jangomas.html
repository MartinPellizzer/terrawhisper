Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Africa and the Indian Ocean. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Kenya and Tanzania. Its presence extends further into the Western Indian Ocean islands. Specifically, it is documented in Mauritius and Réunion. Additionally, the species is also located within the Seychelles.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Jangol, utilizes various components for medicinal purposes, most notably its leaf and bark. The leaves are often employed in traditional remedies to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and inflammatory issues. Similarly, the bark is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, frequently used in decoctions to address digestive problems and fever, showcasing the diverse medicinal utility found within its different structural parts.
