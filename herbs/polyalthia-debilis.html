Polyalthia debilis

Polyalthia debilis, commonly known as the Weak Polyalthia, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is extensively utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin disorders to digestive issues, offering valuable phytochemical benefits for holistic health management.

  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
