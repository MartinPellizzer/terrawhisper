Gentianella turkestanorum

Gentianella turkesthanorum, commonly known as the Turkestan gentian, is a resilient medicinal herb native to the high-altitude steppes of Central Asia. Renowned for its bitter properties, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to stimulate digestion, support liver function, and alleviate various gastrointestinal ailments through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Gentianella turkestanorum
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Gentianaceae, specifically falling under the genus Gentianella.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of Central Asia. Its presence is documented within the borders of Kazakhstan and Kirgizstan. Additionally, it can be found growing in the territories of Uzbekistan and Tadzhikistan. Beyond the Central Asian region, its range extends into Western Asia. Specifically, the species is also known to inhabit the mountainous areas of Afghanistan.
