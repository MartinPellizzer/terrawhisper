Caragana opulens

Caragana opulens, commonly known as the Siberian Peashrub, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asian landscapes. Valued in traditional herbal practices, it possesses unique medicinal properties. Its woody stems and foliage are often explored for their bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic benefits in various folk remedies.

  • Scientific name: Caragana opulens
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Caragana.
