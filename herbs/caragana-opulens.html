Caragana opulens, scientifically known as Caragana opulens, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asian landscapes. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting overall systemic health.
