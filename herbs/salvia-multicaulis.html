Kurdish Basil

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (41 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Kurdish Basil, scientifically known as Salvia multicaulis, is a fragrant perennial herb deeply rooted in Middle Eastern traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, this plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is widely utilized in folk remedies to support digestive health and alleviate various respiratory ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated throughout the Western Asian region. It can be found growing within the borders of Iran, spanning across various local habitats. The species is also documented to inhabit parts of Iraq, extending its presence into the Levant. Furthermore, its range includes the territories of Lebanon-Syria and Palestine. Finally, the distribution reaches into the Sinai Peninsula, completing its regional footprint.
