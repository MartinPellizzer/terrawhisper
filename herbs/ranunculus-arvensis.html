Corn buttercup

Ranunculus arvensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (20 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Corn buttercup, scientifically known as Ranunculus arvensis, is a small, annual herb commonly found in disturbed soils and agricultural fields. Characterized by its delicate yellow flowers and finely divided leaves, this plant belongs to the Ranunculaceae family. While visually charming, it contains protoanemonin, making it toxic if ingested.
