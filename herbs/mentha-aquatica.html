water mint

Mentha aquatica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (551 studies) · ★★★★★

Water mint, scientifically known as Mentha aquatica, is a perennial herb native to wetlands and riverbanks. Characterized by its aromatic leaves and delicate purple flowers, this plant is prized in traditional medicine for its cooling properties, digestive support, and ability to soothe various ailments through its potent essential oils.
