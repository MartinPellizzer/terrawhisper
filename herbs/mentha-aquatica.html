Mentha aquatica

Mentha aquatica, commonly known as water mint, is a perennial herb native to wetland habitats across Europe and Asia. Renowned for its aromatic, minty fragrance, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine for its soothing properties, aiding digestion and providing relief from respiratory discomfort through its essential oils.

  • Scientific name: Mentha aquatica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Mentha.
