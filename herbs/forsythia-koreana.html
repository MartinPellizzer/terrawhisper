Forsythia koreana

Gaenari

Forsythia koreana, commonly known as Korean goldenbell, is a resilient deciduous shrub celebrated for its brilliant, bell-shaped yellow blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in East Asian herbalism to relieve inflammation, soothe respiratory ailments, and detoxify the body, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Forsythia koreana
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Oleaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Forsythia, specifically the species Forsythia koreana.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Forsythia koreana, is recognized by various regional names across East Asia. In Korea, it is widely known as 개나리 (ko), while in Japan, it is referred to as チョウセンレンギョウ (ja). The plant's name in Chinese varies slightly by script and region, being identified as 朝鮮連翹 (zh), 朝鲜连翘 (zh-hans), and 朝鲜连翘 (zh-cn).

Distribution

This plant is native to a very specific region within the continent of Eastern Asia. Its primary geographical distribution is centered exclusively within the country of Korea. Because of this limited range, it is considered an endemic species to the Korean peninsula. This localized habitat means that the plant thrives under the particular climatic conditions found in that area. Consequently, its natural occurrence is highly concentrated rather than being widespread across the globe.
