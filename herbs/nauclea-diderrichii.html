Nauclea diderrichii

Nauclea dideerrichii, commonly known as the Madagascar nutmeg, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Madagascar. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and roots are utilized to treat various ailments, including fever, inflammation, and digestive issues. This botanical species remains a significant subject in ethnopharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Nauclea diderrichii
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
