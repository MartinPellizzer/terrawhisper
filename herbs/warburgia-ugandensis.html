Scientific literature: Moderate (106 studies) · ★★★☆☆

East African greenbark, scientifically known as Warburgia ugandensis, is a vital medicinal shrub native to African montane forests. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and antifungal properties, this plant is deeply embedded in traditional medicine, frequently used to treat respiratory infections, digestive issues, and various skin ailments throughout the region.
