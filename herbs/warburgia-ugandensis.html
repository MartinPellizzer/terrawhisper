Warburgia ugandensis

Warburgia ugandensis, commonly known as the East African Greenheart, is a vital medicinal shrub native to African montane forests. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and antifungal properties, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues, making it a cornerstone of indigenous herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Warburgia ugandensis
  • Family: Canellaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Canellales, it is classified under the family Canellaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Warburgia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Rwanda and the DR Congo. Moving toward the Northeast, the species is also documented in Ethiopia. Its range extends into East Tropical Africa, where it is present in Kenya. Finally, the distribution continues southward to include Tanzania.
