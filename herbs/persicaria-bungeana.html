Persicaria bungeana

Persicaria bungeanana, commonly known as the Bunggan plant, is a versatile medicinal herb native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. This resilient plant continues to garner scientific interest for its diverse pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria bungeana
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae, specifically within the genus Persicaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions of the Eurasian continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within the territory of Great Britain. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species is also found growing in Germany. Its range extends significantly eastward into the Russian Far East, where it inhabits the Amur region. Furthermore, it is widely distributed throughout the Khabarovsk and Primorye areas of the Russian Far East.
