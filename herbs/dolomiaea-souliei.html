Dolomiaea souliei

Dolomiaea souliei, commonly known as the souliei, is a rare and striking medicinal plant native to the high-altitude rainforests of New Guinea. Renowned for its unique botanical characteristics, this species is studied for its potential therapeutic properties, offering a fascinating glimpse into the rich biodiversity of tropical ecosystems.

  • Family: Asteraceae
