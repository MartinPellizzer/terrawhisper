Plant Parts

This plant, Alkanna orientalis, is primarily valued for the medicinal properties found within its root. The root system is the most significant part of the plant for therapeutic use, as it contains specialized naphthoquinone compounds, such as alkannin, which give it a distinct reddish hue when processed. In traditional herbal medicine, these roots are often extracted or used in ointments to treat skin inflammations and promote healing.
