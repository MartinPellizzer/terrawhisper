Paepalanthus bromelioides

Paepalanthus bromelioides, commonly known as "pau-de-pau" or "pau-de-mico," is a significant medicinal plant native to Brazilian Atlantic forests. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat inflammation and infections, offering promising pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and natural drug development.
