Coccoloba alnifolia

Coccoloba alnifolia, commonly known as the sea grape, is a resilient coastal shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Indigenous to tropical shorelines, its leaves and fruits are utilized in traditional remedies to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering a natural treasure within its hardy, salt-tolerant foliage.

  • Scientific name: Coccoloba alnifolia
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae.
