Drypetes gossweileri

Drypetes gossweileri, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in various African regions, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, the plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions, offering promising potential for modern pharmaceutical research.
