Sitka spruce, scientifically known as Picea sitchensiss, is a majestic evergreen native to the Pacific Northwest. Beyond its towering presence, this conifer holds traditional medicinal value. Its resin and needles are historically utilized for their antiseptic and vitamin-rich properties, offering natural remedies for respiratory health and skin ailments.
