Picea sitchensis

Picea sitchensis, commonly known as Sitka spruce, is a majestic coniferous tree native to the Pacific Northwest. Beyond its timber value, it holds traditional medicinal significance. Often used in infusions, its needles and bark are valued for their rich vitamin C content and potential antimicrobial properties in natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Picea sitchensis
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling under the family Pinaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Picea.
