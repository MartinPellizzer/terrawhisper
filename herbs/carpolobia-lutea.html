Carpolobia lutea

Carpolobia lutea, commonly known as the yellow carpolo, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. This botanical treasure offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Carpolobia lutea
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Polygalaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Carpolobia, specifically the species Carpolobia lutea.

Distribution

This plant, known as Carpolobia lutea, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is specifically documented within the borders of Benin. Additionally, the species can be found growing throughout the country of Ghana. The geographical range extends further into Guinea, where it occupies local habitats. Finally, its distribution includes the coastal and inland areas of Ivory Coast and Liberia.
