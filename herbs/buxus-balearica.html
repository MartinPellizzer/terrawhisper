This plant exhibits a distinct geographical distribution concentrated around the Mediterranean basin. Within Southwestern Europe, it can be found in the Balearic Islands, Sardinia, and mainland Spain. Its range also extends significantly into Northern Africa. Specifically, the species inhabits parts of Algeria and Morocco. Consequently, its habitat is characterized by these specific coastal and Mediterranean regions.
