Allium victorialis

Victory onion

Allium victoriaulis, commonly known as Victoria's Garlic, is a rare medicinal herb prized for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Understanding its delicate ecology is vital for preserving its significant therapeutic potential.
