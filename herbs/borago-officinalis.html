Borage, scientifically known as Borago officinalis, is a cherished herb renowned for its striking blue flowers and medicinal properties. Historically used to boost mood and support kidney health, it is rich in gamma-linolenic acid. This versatile plant offers both culinary delight and therapeutic benefits in traditional herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Borago officinalis, is highly valued for its diverse medicinal properties derived from various components. The aerial parts of the plant, which encompass the stems and flowers, are often used in herbal preparations, while the lush green leaves are frequently utilized for their soothing qualities. Most notably, the plant is a significant source of fatty acids found within the seeds. From these, borage seed oil is extracted, a highly prized substance known for its high concentration of gamma-linolenic acid. This specialized seed oil is widely used in skincare and nutritional supplements to support skin health and reduce inflammation.
