Trichosanthes cucumerina subsp. cucumerina

Trichosanthes cucumerina subsp. cucumerina, commonly known as snake gourd, is a versatile climbing vine valued in traditional medicine. Beyond its nutritional benefits, this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and cooling effects. It is widely utilized in herbal remedies to treat various ailments and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Trichosanthes cucumerina subsp. cucumerina
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Trichosanthes.

Common Names

This plant, Trichosanthes cucumerina subsp. cucumerina, is known by various names across different languages and regions. In English, it is frequently referred to as the snake gourd, while in Hindi, it is commonly called kakrol or patola. In Bengali, the plant is known as chichinga, and in Tamil, it is called podalangai.

Distribution

This plant, Trichosanthes cucumerina subsp. cucumerina, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of West Tropical Africa. Its presence is documented within the borders of Guinea-Bissau, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species can also be found growing throughout the nation of Guinea. Furthermore, its range extends into the territory of Senegal. Finally, the geographical reach of this medicinal plant includes the coastal areas of Ivory Coast and Benin.
