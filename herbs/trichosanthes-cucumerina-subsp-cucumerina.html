Snake Gourd

Trichosanthes cucumerina subsp. cucumerina · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (318 studies) · ★★★★★

Snake Gourd, scientifically known as Trichosanthes cucumerina subsp. cucumerina, is a versatile climbing vine prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is used to treat digestive issues, kidney ailments, and inflammation. This nutrient-rich vegetable offers significant health benefits, supporting detoxification and overall wellness through its bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant, Trichosanthes cucumerina subsp. cucumerina, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of West Tropical Africa. Its presence is documented within the borders of Guinea-Bissau, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species can also be found growing throughout the nation of Guinea.
