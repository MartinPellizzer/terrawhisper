Pelargonium

Angel eyes

Pelargonium, commonly known as geranium, is a versatile genus of flowering plants celebrated for both ornamental beauty and therapeutic properties. Renowned for their aromatic foliage, many species are utilized in traditional medicine to support skin health, reduce inflammation, and promote emotional well-being through their calming, essential oil-rich leaves.

  • Scientific name: Pelargonium
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Geraniales, it is a member of the Geraniaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Pelargonium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Pelargonium, is widely recognized by various regional names across different linguistic contexts. In Simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 天竺葵属 (zh-hans) and 天竺葵属 (zh-cn), while in Singapore, the same designation 天竺葵属 (zh-sg) is used. In Traditional Chinese contexts, it is identified as 天竺葵屬 (zh-hant), and in Hong Kong, it is commonly known as 天竺葵屬 (zh-hk).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical presence across several distinct regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its range extends to include both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species can be found growing within the borders of Belgium. The distribution also reaches into Southwestern Europe, specifically covering the island of Corse. Finally, its habitat is further documented within the country of Spain.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as geranium, is highly valued in aromatherapy and traditional medicine primarily for its essential oil. This oil is most frequently extracted from the fragrant leaves and stems through a process of steam distillation, capturing the complex aromatic compounds that provide its therapeutic benefits. While the entire plant structure contributes to its biological functions, it is this concentrated oil that serves as the primary medicinal component used to promote relaxation and skin health.
