Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as geranium, is highly valued in aromatherapy and traditional medicine primarily for its essential oil. This oil is most frequently extracted from the fragrant leaves and stems through a process of steam distillation, capturing the complex aromatic compounds that provide its therapeutic benefits. While the entire plant structure contributes to its biological functions, it is this concentrated oil that serves as the primary medicinal component used to promote relaxation and skin health.
