Chrysobalanus icaco

Cocoplum

Chrysobalanus icaco, commonly known as Cocoplum, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to tropical coastal regions. Renowned for its edible, nutrient-rich fruits, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is increasingly studied for its potential therapeutic properties, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, making it a botanical treasure.
