Teff, scientifically known as Eragrostis tef, is a highly nutritious cereal grain native to Ethiopia. Beyond its role as a dietary staple, it possesses significant medicinal potential. Rich in essential minerals and antioxidants, teff is traditionally utilized to support digestive health, boost energy levels, and manage various inflammatory conditions.
