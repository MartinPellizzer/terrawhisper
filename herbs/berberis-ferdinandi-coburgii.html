What's the taxonomical classification of Berberis ferdinandi-coburgii?

Berberis ferdinandi-coburgii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy places it in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Ranunculales. Further classification identifies it as a member of the family Berberidaceae, specifically within the genus Berberis, with the unique species designation ferdinandi-coburgii.
