Japanese rodgersia, scientifically known as Rodgersia podophyllia, is a striking perennial prized for its dramatic, architectural foliage. Native to East Asia, this hardy plant features large, serrated leaves that add lush texture to woodland gardens. It thrives in moist, shady environments, making it an ideal choice for riparian landscapes.
