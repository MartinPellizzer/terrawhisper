Rodgersia podophylla

Rodgersia podophylla, commonly known as Rodgersia, is a striking perennial prized for its dramatic, large, architectural foliage. Native to moist, mountainous regions, it thrives in shaded, damp environments. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this robust plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its diverse bioactive properties and medicinal potential.

  • Scientific name: Rodgersia podophylla
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Saxifragales, falling within the family Saxifragaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rodgersia.

Common Names

This plant, Rodgersia podophylla, is known by various regional names across different East Asian languages. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans), Traditional Chinese (zh-hant), and the Cantonese context of Hong Kong (zh-hk), it is referred to as 鬼灯檠. In Japan, the species is commonly called ヤグルマソウ (yagurumasou), while in Korea, it is known as 도깨비부채 (dokkaebibuchae).
