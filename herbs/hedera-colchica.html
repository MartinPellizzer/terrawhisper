Hedera colchica

Hedera colchica, commonly known as Colchic ivy, is a perennial evergreen climber native to the Caucasus region. Distinguished by its large, glossy, lobed leaves, this robust species thrives in shaded woodland environments. While primarily ornamental, its unique botanical characteristics make it a subject of interest in traditional herbal studies.

  • Scientific name: Hedera colchica
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, specifically being a member of the family Araliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Hedera.
