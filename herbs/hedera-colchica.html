Persian Ivy, scientifically known as Hedera colchica, is a robust, evergreen climbing vine native to the Caucasus and Middle East. Renowned for its large, glossy leaves, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potential therapeutic properties, often utilized for its bioactive compounds in treating various ailments.
