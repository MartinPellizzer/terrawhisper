, scientifically known as Yucca brevifolia, is a resilient desert shrub native to the Mojave Desert. Commonly called Mojave Yucca, this striking plant is prized for its architectural rosette of stiff, sword-like leaves. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant ecological value and traditional importance in arid landscapes.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the arid regions of the Southwestern United States. Specifically, its range extends across the state of California. It can also be found growing throughout the landscapes of Nevada. Moving south across the border, the species is distributed within Mexico. Its presence is noted specifically in the Northwest portion of the Mexican territory.
