Hypericum richeri

Hypericum richeri, commonly known as a species within the St. John's Wort family, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific alpine or subalpine regions. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, it contains bioactive compounds studied for their potential therapeutic properties, particularly in supporting neurological health and skin recovery.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum richeri
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Hypericum richeri, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Hypericaceae and falls under the genus Hypericum.
