Rhodiola quadrifida

Rhodiola quadrifidida, commonly known as a lesser-known relative of Rhodiola rosea, is a resilient medicinal herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its adaptogenic properties, this plant is studied for its potential to combat fatigue, enhance mental clarity, and support the body's ability to manage physiological stress effectively.

  • Scientific name: Rhodiola quadrifida
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifrages, falling into the family Crassulaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rhodiola, specifically as the species Rhodiola quadrifida.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. In the European portion of the continent, it can be found throughout both East European Russia and North European Russia. Moving eastward into the Siberian region, the species establishes itself in the Altai mountains. Its presence continues across Siberia into the Republic of Buryatia. Finally, the distribution extends further east to include the Chitа region.
