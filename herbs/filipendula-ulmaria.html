mead wort

Filipendula ulmaria · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (519 studies) · ★★★★★

Mead wort, scientifically known as Filipendula ulmaria, is a perennial herb renowned for its historical medicinal value. Often found in damp meadows, it contains salicylic acid, making it a natural remedy for pain and fever. This fragrant, white-flowered plant has long been cherished in traditional herbalism for soothing inflammation.
