Platanus × hispanica

London plane

Platanus × hispanica, commonly known as the London plane, is a resilient deciduous tree prized for its ornamental beauty. While primarily valued in urban landscaping for its pollution tolerance, its bark and leaves have historically been explored in traditional medicine for their tannins and potential anti-inflammatory properties in topical applications.

  • Scientific name: Platanus × hispanica
  • Family: Platanaceae
  • Native range: Not available

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Proteales, it is situated in the family Platanaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Platanus and the specific hybrid name Platanus × hispanica.
