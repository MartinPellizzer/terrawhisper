Inga stipularis

Inga stipularis, commonly known as the inga or forest legume, is a versatile tropical tree valued for its medicinal and ecological benefits. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation and digestive issues. Its lush canopy also plays a vital role in nitrogen fixation and soil enrichment.

  • Scientific name: Inga stipularis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Inga, which includes the species Inga stipularis.

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions in Northern South America. Specifically, it can be found growing in the territories of French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname.
