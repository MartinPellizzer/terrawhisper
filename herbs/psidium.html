Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple regions across the Atlantic. Within the Macaronesian archipelago, it can be found inhabiting the Azores and the Canary Islands. Its presence further extends to the islands of Cape Verde and Madeira. Moving toward the African continent, the species is also located in West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its range includes the nation of Burkina.
