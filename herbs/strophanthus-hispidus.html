What's the taxonomical classification of Strophanthus hispidus?

Strophantus hispidus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Gentianales and is a member of the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its specific biological identity is defined by the genus Strophantus and the species hispidus.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Strophantus hispidus has a woody, shrubby habit characterized by stiffly hairy, lanceolate leaves and small, inconspicuous flowers arranged in axillary clusters. The flowers are relatively small and often possess a yellowish or greenish hue, blending into the dense canopy. What medicinal compounds this plant contains?

This plant contains the potent cardiac glycoside physignathin, along with other glycosides such as strophantin and various alkaloids. These medicinal compounds primarily function by inhibiting the sodium-potassium pump in cardiac muscle cells. Because of this mechanism, they are historically utilized in very controlled doses to increase the force of heart contractions. However, the high toxicity of these chemical constituents requires extreme caution during any therapeutic application. Researchers continue to study these specific molecules to better understand their potential impact on cardiovascular pharmacology.
