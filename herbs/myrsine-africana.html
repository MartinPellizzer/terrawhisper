African Boxwood, scientifically known as Myrsine africanica, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to various African landscapes. Renowned for its dense foliage, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, different parts of the plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, showcasing its profound importance in regional healing.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans multiple continents and climates. In the northern reaches of its range, it can be found in Great Britain within Northern Europe. Its presence extends to the Macaronesian islands, specifically documented in the Azores. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, the species is located in both Rwanda and the DR Congo. Finally, its distribution reaches into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is found in Eritrea.
