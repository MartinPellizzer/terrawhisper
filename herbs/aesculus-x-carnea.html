Aesculus × carnea

Red horse-chestnut

Aesculus × carnea, commonly known as the hybrid buckeye, is a striking ornamental plant valued for its lush foliage and vibrant flower clusters. While primarily grown for landscaping, its botanical relatives are studied for medicinal properties, offering a fascinating intersection of aesthetic beauty and potential therapeutic interest in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Aesculus × carnea
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Not available

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Aesculus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Aesculus × carnea, is referred to by various common names depending on the language. In Poland, it is called Kasztanowiec czerwony, while in Russia, it is known as Конский каштан мясо-красный. Moving to Hungary, the name used is hússzínű vadgesztenye, and in Finland, it is called punahevoskastanja. Finally, in Germany, this medicinal species is identified as Fleischrote Rosskastanie.
