Common Yellowwood, scientifically known as Afrocarpus falcatus, is a resilient evergreen tree native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its striking architectural form, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various indigenous communities have traditionally utilized its bark and leaves in medicinal preparations to treat ailments such as skin irritations and inflammation.
