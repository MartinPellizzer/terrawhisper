Ferula sinaica, scientifically known as Ferula sinaica, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family, native to the Middle East and North Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots and resins are utilized for their potent anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern healing.
