What's the taxonomical classification of Pedicularis chinensis?

Pedicularis chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic journey continues through the order Lamiales and the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Pedicularis, and its unique species designation, chinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pedicularis chinensis has an erect, perennial herbaceous habit characterized by narrow, linear to lanceolate leaves and tubular, bilabiate flowers that are typically pink or purple. The plant develops a basal rosette of leaves from which several upright flowering stems emerge, reaching varying heights depending on the environment. The inflorescence consists of a dense spike of zygomorphic flowers, where the upper lip is often hooded and serves to protect the reproductive organs. Each flower features a long, slender corolla tube that is specialized for specific pollinators. The stem structure is relatively slender yet sturdy enough to support the weight of the terminal floral cluster.
