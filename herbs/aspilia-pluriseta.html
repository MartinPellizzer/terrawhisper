Aspilia pluriseta

Aspilia pluriseta, commonly known as a member of the Asteraceae family, is a significant medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and various infections, offering promising potential for modern therapeutic developments.

  • Scientific name: Aspilia pluriseta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Aspilia. Specifically, the species is known as Aspilia pluriseta.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several distinct regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its range includes the Democratic Republic of the Congo. It can also be found in Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically within Sudan and South Sudan. Moving eastward, the species is distributed throughout East Tropical Africa, including Uganda. Finally, its southern reach extends into South Tropical Africa, where it is located in both Mozambique and Zambia.
