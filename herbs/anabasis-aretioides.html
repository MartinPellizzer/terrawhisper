Anabasis aretioides

Anabasis aretioides, commonly known as a halophytic medicinal plant, thrives in saline environments. This resilient species is valued in traditional ethnobotany for its unique biochemical properties. Often studied for its potential therapeutic applications, it offers significant interest in pharmacological research due to its specialized adaptations to extreme coastal habitats.
