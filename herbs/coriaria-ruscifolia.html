Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various island regions within the Oceania area. In the Papuasian region, it can be found growing in New Guinea and the Solomon Islands. Its range extends further into the Southwestern Pacific, where it is present in Fiji. Additionally, the species is documented in the archipelago of Samoa. Finally, the geographical presence of this medicinal plant includes the islands of Vanuatu.
