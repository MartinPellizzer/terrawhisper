Lepechinia chilensis

Lepechinia chilensis, commonly known as a native Chilean medicinal shrub, is a fascinating botanical species valued for its traditional healing properties. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this plant is frequently utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering a unique glimpse into the rich ethnobotanical heritage of South America.

  • Scientific name: Lepechinia chilensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its descent through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Lepechinia.

Common Names

This plant, Lepechinia chilensis, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Spanish, it is frequently referred to as chilena or chilena de la cordillera, while in Mapudungun, it is known as kofke.

Distribution

This plant is native to the specific regions of Southern South America. Its primary geographical distribution is concentrated within the central territory of Chile. Additionally, it can be found extending into the southern parts of Chile. This limited range indicates that the species is endemic to these specific coastal or mountainous zones. Consequently, understanding its habitat in central and southern Chile is essential for its conservation.
