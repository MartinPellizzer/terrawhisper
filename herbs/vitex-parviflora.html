Molave, scientifically known as Vitex parviflora, is a majestic hardwood tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its immense durability and strength, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional medicine utilizes its bark and leaves to treat various ailments, making it a vital resource in both ecological and therapeutic contexts.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct oceanic and island regions. Within the Malesia botanical zone, it can be found throughout the Lesser Sunda Islands and the Maluku archipelago. Its presence extends further north into the Philippines and across the island of Sulawesi. Additionally, the species reaches into the Northwestern Pacific region. Specifically, it is documented to inhabit the Caroline Islands within this area.
