Mullein, scientifically known as Verbascum thapsus, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its soft, velvety leaves, it is primarily utilized to support respiratory health. From soothing coughs to easing congestion, this resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and lung support.
