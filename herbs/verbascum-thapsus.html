Verbascum thapsus

Mullein

Verbascum thapsus, commonly known as Great Mullein, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its soothing mucilage, it is primarily used to support respiratory health, easing coughs and congestion. Its soft, velvety leaves and potent medicinal properties make it a staple in natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Verbascum thapsus
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is a member of the family Scrophulariaceae. It is specifically classified under the genus Verbascum.
