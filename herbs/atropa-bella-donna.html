Atropa bella-donna

Atropa bella-donna · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (219 studies) · ★★★★★

Atropa belladonna, scientifically known as Atropa belladonna, is a highly toxic perennial herb native to Europe. Renowned for its dark berries and bell-shaped flowers, it contains potent alkaloids like atropine. While historically used in cosmetics and medicine, its extreme toxicity demands cautious handling and strict pharmacological control.
