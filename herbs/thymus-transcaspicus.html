Thymus transcaspicus

Thymus transcaspicus, commonly known as Transcaspian thyme, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to the arid regions of Central Asia. Valued for its unique essential oils, this medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly for its antimicrobial, antioxidant, and soothing properties used in traditional and modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Thymus transcaspicus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, situated within the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Thymus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across specific regions of the Middle East and Central Asia. Its geographical range is quite localized, focusing on arid and semi-arid environments. In Central Asia, the species is notably distributed within the borders of Turkmenistan. Additionally, its presence extends into Western Asia, where it grows in the country of Iran. Therefore, its natural habitat is limited to these two distinct geographic territories.
