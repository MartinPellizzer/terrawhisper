Goldencup St. John's wort, scientifically known as Hypericum patulum, is a distinctive perennial herb native to western North America. Characterized by its bright yellow, star-shaped flowers and resinous foliage, this plant belongs to the hypericaceae family and is often celebrated for its unique aesthetic and potential medicinal properties.
