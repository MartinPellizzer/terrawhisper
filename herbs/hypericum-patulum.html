Hypericum patulum

Hypericum patulum, commonly known as the creeping St. John's wort, is a resilient perennial herb native to various regions. Distinguished by its spreading habit and yellow, star-shaped blossoms, this botanical species is valued in ethnobotany. It offers unique phytochemical properties, making it a subject of interest for traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum patulum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Hypericum, specifically the species Hypericum patulum.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions. In Southern Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the KwaZulu-Natal province. Its range extends significantly into China, where it can be found in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast regions.
