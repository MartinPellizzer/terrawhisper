Cuphea calophylla

Cuphea calophylla, commonly known as the Butterfly Bush or False Heather, is a striking ornamental shrub prized for its vibrant, tubular flowers. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this plant holds significant interest in ethnobotany, offering potential medicinal properties through its unique chemical composition, making it a subject of botanical study.

  • Scientific name: Cuphea calophylla
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the family Lythraceae and falls under the genus Cuphea.
