Euryops floribundus

Euryops floribundus, commonly known as the Starry Bloom, is a remarkable medicinal plant cherished in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to support immune function and reduce inflammation. This vibrant flowering species offers a natural remedy for restoring vitality and promoting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Euryops floribundus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is classified within the family Asteraceae under the genus Euryops.
