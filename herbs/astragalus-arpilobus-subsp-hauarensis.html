Astragalus arpilobus subsp. hauarensis

Astragalus arpilobus subsp. hauarensis, commonly known as a rare subspecies of milkvetch, is a specialized medicinal plant found in specific ecological niches. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it holds significant potential in traditional medicine, offering bioactive compounds that may support immune function and provide diverse therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus arpilobus subsp. hauarensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Astragalus as the subspecies Astragalus aprillus subsp. hauarensis.

Common Names

This plant, Astragalus arpilobus subsp. hauarensis, is known by several distinct common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is referred to as the Hauarens Astragalus, while in Arabic, it is commonly called Astragalus al-Hauarens.

Distribution

This plant is found exclusively within the diverse landscapes of Northern Africa. Its geographical range spans several key countries across this expansive region. Specifically, it can be located in the territories of Algeria and Egypt. The species also inhabits parts of Libya and Morocco. Finally, its distribution extends to the coastal and inland areas of Tunisia.
