Phlomoides medicinalis

Phlomoides medicinalis, commonly known as medicinal lungwort, is a perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to support respiratory health and soothe inflammation. Understanding its botanical properties and careful application is essential for leveraging its therapeutic potential in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Phlomoides medicinalis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phlomoides.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within certain regions of East Asia. It is primarily found growing in the South-Central parts of China. Additionally, the species can be located within the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. These two regions represent the core areas where the plant occurs naturally. Therefore, its habitat is limited to these particular mountainous and central territories.
