Gueldenstaedtia verna

Gueldenstaedtia verna, commonly known as the spring jasmine, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its fragrant white blossoms, this plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies. It possesses significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating respiratory ailments and supporting digestive health through its bioactive compounds.
