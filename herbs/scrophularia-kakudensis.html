Scrophularia kakudensis

Scrophularia kakudensis, commonly known as a specialized member of the figwort family, is a medicinal plant native to specific East Asian regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to address inflammatory conditions, supporting systemic balance and promoting overall wellness through its bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Scrophularia kakudensis
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Scrophularia kakudensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Scrophulariaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Scrophularia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. Its range extends significantly into China, where it can be found throughout Manchuria. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed across the Korean Peninsula. Finally, its geographical footprint encompasses various parts of Japan, completing its presence in Eastern Asia.
