Ixeridium dentatum

Ixeriom dentatum, commonly known as the tooth-edged Ixeriom, is a significant medicinal herb found in various tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address inflammatory conditions and skin ailments, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and therapeutic healing.

  • Scientific name: Ixeridium dentatum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the Asteraceae family, specifically categorized within the genus Ixeriidion.
