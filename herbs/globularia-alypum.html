Globularia alypum

Globularia alypum, commonly known as the globularia, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its striking blue flowers and distinctive foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally utilized in folk medicine, it is studied for its potential bioactive properties and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Globularia alypum
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Globularia.
