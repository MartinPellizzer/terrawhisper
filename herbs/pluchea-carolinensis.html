Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct oceanic regions. In the Atlantic, its presence is noted within the Macaronesian archipelago, specifically across the Canary Islands. Its range extends into Eastern Asia, where it can be found in both the Nansei-shotto islands and Taiwan. Moving toward the Southwestern Pacific, the species is documented in the Gilbert Islands. Finally, its distribution reaches as far as Tonga, demonstrating a wide maritime spread.
