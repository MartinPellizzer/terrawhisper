Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct oceanic regions. Its range extends into Eastern Asia, where it can be found in both the Nansei-shotto islands and Taiwan. Moving toward the Southwestern Pacific, the species is documented in the Gilbert Islands. Finally, its distribution reaches as far as Tonga, demonstrating a wide maritime spread.

Reproduction

The reproduction of Pluchea carolinensis is characterized by a flowering period that typically begins in March and concludes in May, though the timing can be variable throughout the year. The plant produces pink flowers arranged in a raceme inflorescence. Following pollination, it develops small, brown, indehiscent fruits classified as achenes. These fruits are quite minute, with a length ranging from a minimum of 0.06 cm to a maximum of 0.08 cm. Contained within these fruits are exceptionally lightweight seeds, with a mean seed mass of 1.5e-05 g, a minimum mass of 1e-05 g, and a maximum mass of 2e-05 g.
