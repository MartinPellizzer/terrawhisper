Spiraea chinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Spiraea chinensis?

Spiraea chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and is a member of the order Rosales. It is further categorized into the family Rosaceae, which hosts the genus Spiraea, and is specifically identified by its species name, chinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Spiraea chinensis has arching, deciduous stems that support serrated, lanceolate leaves arranged oppositely along the branches. The foliage typically displays a bright green hue during the growing season before transitioning to autumn colors. Large, terminal corymbs emerge from the tips of the branches, consisting of numerous small, five-petaled pink flowers. These dense floral clusters create a profuse blooming effect that covers much of the shrubby canopy. The plant generally reaches a height and spread of several feet, forming a rounded, multi-stemmed habit.
