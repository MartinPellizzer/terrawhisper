Achillea aleppica

Achillea aleppica, commonly known as Aleppo Yarrow, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant has been traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Achillea aleppica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Achillea, where its species name is aleprica.
