Indigofera linifolia

Indigofera linifolia, commonly known as a medicinal shrub, is a significant plant within the Fabaceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This resilient species offers valuable phytochemicals, making it a subject of growing interest in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Indigofera linifolia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Fabales and placed in the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Indigofera.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple regions across Africa and Asia. In Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Eritrea, Ethiopia, and the territory of Sudan-South Sudan. The species also extends its reach into the Western Indian Ocean, specifically appearing on the island of Réunion. Additionally, its range stretches into Western Asia, where it can be found in Afghanistan. Together, these locations highlight the broad ecological adaptability of the species.
