Sardinian buttercup, scientifically known as Ranunculus sardous, is a rare, endemic wildflower native to the rugged landscapes of Sardinia. Distinguished by its bright yellow blooms, this species holds significant ethnobotanical interest. While many buttercups contain toxic protoanemonin, studying its unique chemical profile offers insights into traditional Mediterranean medicinal practices.
