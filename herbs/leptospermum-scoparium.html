New Zealand teatree

Leptospermum scoparium · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (166 studies) · ★★★★☆

New Zealand teatree, scientifically known as Leptospermum scoparium, is a versatile evergreen shrub renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Widely utilized in traditional Māori healing, its leaves yield essential oils with powerful antibacterial and antifungal qualities. Today, it remains a cornerstone in natural wellness and skincare for its therapeutic benefits.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as manuka, is highly valued for its medicinal properties, which are primarily derived from the essential oil extracted from its leaves and small flowering parts. This oil is rich in bioactive compounds that possess potent antibacterial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory characteristics. Because the oil is concentrated within the plant's foliage, it is carefully distilled to create therapeutic products used in aromatherapy and topical treatments.
