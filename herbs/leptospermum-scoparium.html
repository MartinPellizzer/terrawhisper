New Zealand teatree

Leptospermum scoparium · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (166 studies) · ★★★★☆

New Zealand teatree, scientifically known as Leptospermum scoparium, is a versatile evergreen shrub renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Widely utilized in traditional Māori healing, its leaves yield essential oils with powerful antibacterial and antifungal qualities. Today, it remains a cornerstone in natural wellness and skincare for its therapeutic benefits.
