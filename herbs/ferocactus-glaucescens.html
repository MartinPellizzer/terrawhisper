Ferocactus glaucescens

Ferocactus glaucescens, commonly known as Glaucous Ferocactus, is a resilient succulent prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its distinctive blue-grey hue, this cactus contains unique bioactive compounds. Often utilized for its soothing properties and anti-inflammatory potential, it remains a significant botanical resource for holistic healing and dermatological applications.

  • Scientific name: Ferocactus glaucescens
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is a member of the family Cactaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Ferocactus, identifying it as Ferocactus glaucescens.
