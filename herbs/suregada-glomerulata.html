Suregada glomerulata

Suregada glomerulata, commonly known as the "Indian Coral Tree," is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its bark, leaves, and flowers are utilized to treat various ailments, including skin disorders, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for healing.

  • Scientific name: Suregada glomerulata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Suregada glomerulata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales. It is further categorized under the family Euphorbiaceae within the genus Suregada.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad natural distribution across several key regions of Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China zone, it can be found growing in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, and Vietnam. Its presence extends beyond the mainland into the Malesia region. Specifically, it is documented as growing on the island of Borneo. These diverse locations highlight its widespread ecological footprint across the tropical landscapes of the region.
