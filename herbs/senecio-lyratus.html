Senecio lyratus

Senecio lyratus, commonly known as Lyrate Groundsel, is a distinctive herbaceous plant characterized by its deeply lobed, lyre-shaped leaves. Often found in disturbed soils and coastal regions, this species plays a vital role in local ecosystems, offering unique botanical interest through its striking foliage and seasonal flowering patterns.

  • Scientific name: Senecio lyratus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Senecio.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa. In Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Eritrea and Ethiopia. Its presence extends further into the Horn of Africa, specifically within the borders of Somalia.
