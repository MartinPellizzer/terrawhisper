Hymenodictyon orixense

Hymenodictyon orixense, commonly known as a medicinal botanical treasure, is a significant species native to tropical ecosystems. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers promising therapeutic potential. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds to develop natural treatments for various ailments, highlighting its importance in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Hymenodictyon orixense
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Gentianaales and is a member of the Rubiaceae family. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Hymenodictyon, specifically as the species Hymenodictyon orixense.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of Asia, primarily concentrated within the Indian Subcontinent. Furthermore, its presence extends to the lush landscapes of Assam and the mountainous elevations of the East Himalaya. Beyond the Indian Subcontinent, the species is also documented in the South-Central region of China. Together, these locations define the natural range where this medicinal species thrives.
