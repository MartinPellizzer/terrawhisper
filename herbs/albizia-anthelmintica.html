Albizia anthelmintica

Worm cure albizia

Albizia anthelmintica, commonly known as the "earthworm tree" in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its potent anthelmintic properties, its bark and leaves are traditionally utilized to treat parasitic infections, digestive ailments, and various skin conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Albizia anthelmintica
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Albizia, specifically as the species Albizia anthelmintica.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within the tropical belt of Africa. In Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Eritrea and Ethiopia. It can also be found extending through the territories of Somalia and the combined regions of Sudan and South Sudan. Furthermore, its range reaches into East Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Kenya. Collectively, these locations highlight a specific geographical footprint concentrated in the eastern portions of the continent.
