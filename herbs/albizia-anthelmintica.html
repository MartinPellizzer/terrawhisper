Worm cure Albizia, scientifically known as Albizia anthelmintiica, is a medicinal tree prized in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its potent anthelmintic properties, its bark and leaves are frequently utilized to expel intestinal parasites. This versatile plant serves as a vital natural remedy for promoting gastrointestinal health and wellness.
