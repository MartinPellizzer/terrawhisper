Rockrose, scientifically known as Cistus, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub celebrated for its aromatic foliage and delicate blossoms. Renowned in herbal medicine, it possesses powerful antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used in essential oils and topical salves, this plant offers unique therapeutic benefits for skin healing and wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in both Belgium and Switzerland. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it can be found throughout various parts of Southwestern Europe. Specifically, its range includes the islands of Baleares and Corse. Additionally, the species is widely distributed across the mainland of France.
