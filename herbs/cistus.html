Cistus

Rockrose

Cistus, commonly known as Rock Rose, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its aromatic resins and potent antioxidants, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to soothe skin irritations, reduce inflammation, and support respiratory health, offering a natural essence of healing vitality.

  • Scientific name: Cistus
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales and the family Cistaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Cistus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in both Belgium and Switzerland. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it can be found throughout various parts of Southwestern Europe. Specifically, its range includes the islands of Baleares and Corse. Additionally, the species is widely distributed across the mainland of France.
