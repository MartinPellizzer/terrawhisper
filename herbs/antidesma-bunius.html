Antidesma bunius

Antidesma bunius, commonly known as Bignay, is a versatile tropical shrub prized for its nutrient-dense fruits. Widely utilized in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. From managing blood pressure to supporting digestive health, this botanical powerhouse offers significant therapeutic potential in holistic wellness practices.
