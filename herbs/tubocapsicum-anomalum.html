Tubocapsicum anomalum

Tubocapsicum anomalum, commonly known as the anomalous pepper, is a unique medicinal plant prized for its potent bioactive compounds. Often found in specialized ecological niches, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. Research highlights its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a valuable subject in ethnobotanical and pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Tubocapsicum anomalum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales. Finally, it is a member of the family Solanaceae and falls under the genus Tubocapsicum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Tubocapsicum anomalum, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Japan, it is known as ハダカホオズキ (ja), while in Korea, it is called 알꽈리 (ko). It is also recognized across several Chinese-speaking regions under the name 龙珠 (zh), which includes the traditional forms 龙珠 (zh-hans) and 龍珠 (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range extends further into Eastern Asia, where it is native to both Japan and Korea. Additionally, the species is documented in the Nansei-shotō islands. These diverse locations highlight the plant's presence across a significant portion of the eastern coastline.
