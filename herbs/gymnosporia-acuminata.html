Gymnosporia acuminata

Gymnosporia acuminata, commonly known as the thorny climber, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient plant serves as a vital resource in various ancient healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Gymnosporia acuminata
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Celastrales, falling into the family Celastraceae. Finally, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Gymnosporia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range extending across several distinct regions in Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the South-Central territories. The Indian Subcontinent hosts diverse populations, including those found in Assam, Bangladesh, and the East Himalaya. Furthermore, the species stretches into the Indo-China region, specifically inhabiting Myanmar. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's widespread distribution across various subtropical landscapes.
