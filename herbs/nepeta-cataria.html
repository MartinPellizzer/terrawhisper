Catnip, scientifically known as Nepeta cataria, is a perennial herb in the mint family celebrated for its aromatic properties. Renowned for its calming effects on humans and irresistible attraction to cats, it has been used traditionally to aid sleep, soothe digestion, and reduce anxiety through its gentle, medicinal qualities.
