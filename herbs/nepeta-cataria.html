Catnip, scientifically known as Nepeta cataria, is a perennial herb in the mint family celebrated for its aromatic properties. Renowned for its calming effects on humans and irresistible attraction to cats, it has been used traditionally to aid sleep, soothe digestion, and reduce anxiety through its gentle, medicinal qualities.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as catnip, contains various bioactive components distributed throughout its vegetative structure that contribute to its therapeutic properties. The medicinal efficacy of Nepeta cataria is often concentrated within its essential oils, which are primarily found in the glandular hairs of the leaves and stems. When researchers study the complex chemical profile of the herb, they frequently utilize a methanol extract to isolate a wide range of phytochemicals, including flavonoids and polyphenols. Furthermore, the specific volatile compounds that provide its characteristic aroma and sedative effects are derived directly from the plant's essential oil content, making these parts central to its traditional and pharmacological use.
