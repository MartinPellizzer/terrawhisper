Eugenia brejoensis

Eugenia brejoenensis, commonly known as the Brazilian guava or cerrado guava, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to South American ecosystems. Highly valued for its nutritional properties and medicinal potential, this plant offers antioxidant benefits and is increasingly studied for its role in traditional healing and sustainable biodiversity conservation.

  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtales, and the family Myrtaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Eugenia.
