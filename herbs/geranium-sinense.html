Geranium sinense

What's the taxonomical classification of Geranium sinense?

Geranium sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Geraniales. Furthermore, its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the family Geraniaceae and the genus Geranium, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species sinense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Geranium sinense has deeply lobed, palmate leaves that are typically bright green and characterized by serrated margins. The plant produces small, five-petaled flowers that often feature delicate veining and varying shades of pink or lavender. Its stems are slender and herbaceous, providing support for the spreading foliage and blooming stalks. A well-developed root system allows the plant to anchor itself effectively in well-drained soil. The overall structure of the plant remains relatively compact, making it suitable for garden borders or container planting.
