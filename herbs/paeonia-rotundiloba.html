Paeonia rotundiloba

Paeonia rotundiloba, commonly known as the round-leaf peony, is a significant medicinal herb prized in traditional pharmacology. Native to specific mountainous regions, this plant is valued for its bioactive compounds. Its roots are frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, promote circulation, and support various restorative health functions in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia rotundiloba
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Saxifrages and the family Paeoniaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Paeonia.
