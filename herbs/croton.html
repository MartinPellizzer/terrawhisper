Croton

Croton, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, belongs to the Euphorbiaceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various species are utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. While potent, careful application is essential due to the plant's highly reactive natural compounds.

  • Scientific name: Croton
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the specific genus Croton.
