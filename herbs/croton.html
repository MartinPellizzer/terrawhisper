Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Croton, possesses various components that are utilized for their distinct medicinal properties. The large, often colorful leaves are frequently studied for their bioactive compounds, while the twigs can be harvested to prepare decoctions. When the plant is wounded, it exudes a milky latex that is used in traditional applications. Additionally, the thick bark contains concentrated phytochemicals that serve as a vital source for extracting therapeutic substances.
