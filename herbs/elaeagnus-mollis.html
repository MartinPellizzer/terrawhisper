Elaeagnus mollis

Elaeagnus mollis, commonly known as soft olive, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its silvery foliage and fragrant white flowers, it serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. In traditional practices, its leaves and bark are utilized for their potential healing and restorative properties.

  • Scientific name: Elaeagnus mollis
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
