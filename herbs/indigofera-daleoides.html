Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions within the southern portion of the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in the countries of Angola, Zambia, and Zimbabwe. Moving toward the southern reaches, it can also be found within the borders of Botswana. Additionally, the species extends into the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. Together, these locations define the specific geographical range of this medicinal species.
