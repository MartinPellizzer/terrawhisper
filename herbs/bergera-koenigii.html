Bergera koenigii

Bergera koenigii, commonly known as the king's berry, is a significant medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers diverse therapeutic potential, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Exploring its phytochemical profile provides vital insights into developing natural treatments for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Bergera koenigii
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Bergera.

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several specific regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central provinces and the Southeast region.
