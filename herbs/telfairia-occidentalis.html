Telfairia occidentalis

Telfairiia occidentalis, commonly known as African spinach or ewedu, is a highly nutritious leafy vegetable native to West Africa. Renowned for its mucilaginous texture, it is a dietary staple used in traditional soups. Beyond culinary uses, it offers significant medicinal benefits, including antioxidant properties and support for digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Telfairia occidentalis
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Telfairia occidentalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Cucurbitales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cucurbitaceae and is categorized under the genus Telfairia.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Telfairiia occidentalis, is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Within this specific geographical zone, it can be found growing across several different nations. It is notably present in the coastal and inland areas of Benin and Guinea. Furthermore, the species maintains a significant presence in the Ivory Coast and Liberia. Its distribution also extends extensively through the diverse landscapes of Nigeria.
