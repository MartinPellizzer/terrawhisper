Fluted gourd, scientifically known as Telfairiia occidentalis, is a highly nutritious leafy vegetable native to West Africa. Widely consumed as "efo riro," its leaves are rich in essential vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants. This versatile plant plays a crucial role in traditional diets and offers significant medicinal benefits.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Telfairiia occidentalis, is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Within this specific geographical zone, it can be found growing across several different nations. It is notably present in the coastal and inland areas of Benin and Guinea. Furthermore, the species maintains a significant presence in the Ivory Coast and Liberia. Its distribution also extends extensively through the diverse landscapes of Nigeria.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the African locust bean, is a versatile medicinal and nutritional resource where various parts serve distinct purposes. The nutrient-dense leaf is frequently consumed for its high protein content and used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, while the stem provides the structural framework for the tree and can be utilized in specific herbal preparations. The fruit, which grows in pods, houses the highly valued seeds that are fermented to create traditional seasonings and are prized for their medicinal properties in boosting vitality. Additionally, the root of the tree is often employed in traditional healing practices to address various health concerns, ensuring that almost every part of Telfairiia occidentalis contributes to its significant ethnobotanical importance.
