Filipendula vulgaris

Filipendula vulgaris, commonly known as lady's bedstraw, is a perennial herb native to Europe. Historically valued in traditional herbalism, this plant belongs to the Rosaceae family. It is characterized by its delicate white flowers and aromatic foliage, often found in meadows and woodlands, traditionally used for its mild medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Filipendula vulgaris
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its classification through the subclass Magnoliidae and order Rosales, it is a member of the Rosaceae family. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Filipendula.
