Dracocephalum cuspidatum

Dracocephalum cuspidatum, commonly known as Spiked Dragonhead, is a resilient perennial herb native to Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and striking purple blooms, this medicinal plant offers diverse therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health and possess calming, antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Dracocephalum cuspidatum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, which characterizes its botanical lineage. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Dracocephalum.
