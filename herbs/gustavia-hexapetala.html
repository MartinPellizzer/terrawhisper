Gustavia hexapetala

Gustavia hexapetala, commonly known as the six-petaled Gustavie, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical ecosystems. Renowned for its striking floral displays, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Local traditional practices utilize its unique properties to treat various ailments, highlighting its vital role in natural pharmacology and biodiversity.

  • Family: Lecythidaceae

This plant, Gustavia hexapetala, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the Lecythidaceae family and the genus Gustavia.
