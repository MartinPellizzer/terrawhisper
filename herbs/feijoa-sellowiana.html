Feijoa, scientifically known as Feijoa sellowiaana, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its aromatic fruit and medicinal potential. Traditionally used in folk medicine, its leaves and fruit offer antioxidant properties. Often valued in herbal remedies for digestive support and wellness, this plant remains a fascinating botanical treasure.
