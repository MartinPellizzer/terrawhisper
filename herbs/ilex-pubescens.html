Ilex pubescens

Ilex pubescens, commonly known as the pubescent holly, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its medicinal and ornamental qualities. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich berries, it has been traditionally utilized in folk medicine to support immune health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Ilex pubescens
  • Family: Aquifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Aquifoliales, falling within the family Aquifoliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established under the genus Ilex.

Its presence also extends to the Southeast regions of the country. Additionally, the species can be located on the island of Hainan. Finally, its range reaches into Eastern Asia, specifically inhabiting Taiwan.
