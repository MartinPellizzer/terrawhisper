Pubescent holly, scientifically known as Ilex pubescens, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to Europe and Western Asia. Renowned for its glossy, serrated leaves and vibrant red berries, this plant holds significant botanical interest. Traditionally, its various parts have been explored for diverse medicinal properties and decorative landscaping uses.
