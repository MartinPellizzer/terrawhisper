Menispermum canadense, scientifically known as Menispermum canadense, is a perennial climbing vine native to North America. Commonly called Canada Moonseed, this plant is historically significant in traditional herbal medicine. While used for its various bioactive properties, it requires careful handling due to the presence of potent alkaloids.
