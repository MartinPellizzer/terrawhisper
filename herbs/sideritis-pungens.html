Sideritis pungens

Sideritis pungens, commonly known as prickly side-eye, is a distinctive medicinal herb found in rocky, Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat respiratory ailments and digestive issues, offering potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits through its unique chemical composition.

  • Scientific name: Sideritis pungens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Sideritis pungens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae and the genus Sideritis.

This plant, Sideritis pungens, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as pungent dittany, while in Greek, it is commonly called πικρή σιδερίτης (pikrí sideerítis). In Arabic, the species is known as السدير الحاد (al-sideer al-hād), and in Latin, it is referred to by its scientific nomenclature, Sideritis pungens.
