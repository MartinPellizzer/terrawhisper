Chenopodium giganteum

Chenopodium giganteum, commonly known as Giant Goosefoot, is a robust medicinal plant celebrated for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its nutrient-dense leaves and antioxidant capabilities, it serves as a vital natural remedy in traditional healing practices, offering significant benefits for digestive health and overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Chenopodium giganteum
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae. Its specific biological identity is defined by the genus Chenopodium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Chenopodium giganteum, is recognized by various common names across different linguistic regions. In Germany, the species is known as Baumspinat (de), while in Russia, it is called Марь гигантская (ru).
