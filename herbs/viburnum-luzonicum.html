Viburnum luzonicum

Viburnum luzonicum, commonly known as Sambong, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Philippines. Widely utilized in traditional healing, it serves as a potent natural diuretic and helps treat kidney stones and urinary tract infections. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic benefits for promoting overall renal health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Viburnum luzonicum
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Dipsacales and is a member of the family Viburnaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Viburnum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range extends into Eastern Asia, specifically within the island of Taiwan. Moving into the Indo-China region, the species is documented to grow in Vietnam. Finally, its presence reaches into the Malesia region, specifically located in the Maluku islands.
