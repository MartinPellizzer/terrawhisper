Anadenanthera colubrina

Vilca

Anadenanthera colubrina, commonly known as Cebíl or Vilca, is a significant medicinal tree native to South American forests. Renowned for its psychoactive properties, its seeds contain potent alkaloids used historically in sacred rituals. Beyond spiritual practices, it holds traditional importance in ethnobotany for its diverse pharmacological potential and cultural significance.

  • Scientific name: Anadenanthera colubrina
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Common Names

This plant, Anadenanthera colubrina, is known by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In Guaraní, it is referred to as Kurupa'y kuru (gn), while in Quechua, it is commonly called Wilkā (qu). In Lithuanian, it is known as Žaltinis karolmedis (lt), and in Chinese, it is identified as 蛇状柯拉豆 (zh/zh-hans).
