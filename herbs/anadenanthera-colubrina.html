Plant Parts

This plant, Adenanthera colubrina, offers various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The bark is frequently employed in traditional medicine for its astringent qualities, while the aerial parts, including the leaves and stems, are often harvested for decoctions. When the plant is injured, it produces a distinct gum which can be collected as a trunk exudate, a substance that has been historically noted for its specific medicinal applications in folk healing practices.
