Xanthosoma robustum

Xanthosoma robustum, commonly known as a species of giant taro, is a tropical perennial valued for its edible tubers. Belonging to the Araceae family, this plant thrives in moist, fertile soils. Beyond its nutritional importance, it holds significant potential in ethnobotany for its medicinal properties and cultural heritage.
