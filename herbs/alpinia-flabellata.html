Alpinia flabellata

Alpinia flabellata, commonly known as the Fan-leafed Galangal, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Alpinia flabellata
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, falling into the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Alpinia.
