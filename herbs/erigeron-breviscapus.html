Erigeron breviscapus

Erigeron brevicauspus, commonly known as short-cap erigeron, is a delicate perennial herb native to specific montane regions. Characterized by its small, composite flower heads and fine foliage, this plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem, offering unique botanical interest and potential medicinal properties within its natural alpine habitat.

  • Scientific name: Erigeron breviscapus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
