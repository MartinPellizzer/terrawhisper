What's the taxonomical classification of Chirita sinensis?

Chirita sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further organized into the order Lamiales. It is a member of the family Gesneriaceae, falling under the genus Chirita, with the specific species designation sinensis.
