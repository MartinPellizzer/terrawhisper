Canthium glaucum

Canthium glaucum, commonly known as blue-flowered milkwort, is a resilient perennial herb native to various sandy coastal regions. Valued in traditional medicine for its unique biochemical properties, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Understanding its botanical characteristics and medicinal applications provides vital insights into its role in natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Canthium glaucum
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Gentianaales and belongs to the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Canthium.
