Tanacetum vulgare subsp. vulgare

Tanacetum vulgare subsp. vulgare, commonly known as Tansy, is a perennial herb distinguished by its bright, button-like yellow flowers. Historically valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses diverse bioactive properties. While used for digestive and insecticidal purposes, its potency requires cautious application due to its significant therapeutic complexity.

  • Scientific name: Tanacetum vulgare subsp. vulgare
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Tanacetum.
