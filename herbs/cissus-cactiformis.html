Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Ethiopia and Somalia. Moving further south, it is also found throughout East Tropical Africa, specifically within Kenya and Tanzania. Additionally, its range extends into South Tropical Africa, where it can be located in Mozambique. Together, these locations highlight the diverse ecological spread of the species.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cissus cactiformis, exhibits specialized morphological adaptations characteristic of its succulent nature. The stem serves as a primary functional component, acting as a thickened, fleshy structure designed for efficient water storage and providing structural support for the climbing habit. In contrast, the leaf morphology is often modified or reduced to minimize transpiration, allowing the plant to thrive in arid environments while still performing necessary photosynthetic functions.
