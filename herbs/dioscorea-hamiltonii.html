Mountain yam, scientifically known as Dioscorea hamiltonii, is a medicinal climber native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally used in Ayurvedic medicine to treat various ailments. Its tubers are particularly valued for their therapeutic potential in managing inflammation and enhancing overall vitality.
