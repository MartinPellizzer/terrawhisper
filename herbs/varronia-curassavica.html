Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key tropical regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in both Mauritius and Madagascar. Its presence extends to the Indian Subcontinent, where it inhabits parts of India and Sri Lanka. Additionally, the species is located within the Malesia region, specifically on the island of Borneo.
