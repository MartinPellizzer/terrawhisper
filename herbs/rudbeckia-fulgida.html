Rudbeckia fulgida

Rudbeckia fulgida, commonly known as the shining coneflower, is a resilient perennial valued for its vibrant golden blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Historically used in traditional medicine, its bioactive compounds offer therapeutic potential, making it a subject of interest for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Rudbeckia fulgida
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
