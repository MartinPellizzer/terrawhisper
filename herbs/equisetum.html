Equisetum

The horsetail genus

Equisetum, commonly known as horsetail, is a resilient prehistoric plant belonging to the Equisetaceae family. Renowned for its high silica content, it has been used traditionally to support bone health, skin integrity, and kidney function. This ancient botanical offers unique therapeutic potential through its mineral-rich, structural properties.

  • Scientific name: Equisetum
  • Family: Equisetaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Equisetidae, it falls under the order Equisetales and the family Equisetaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Equisetum.
