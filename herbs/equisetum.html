The horsetail genus, scientifically known as Equisetum, is a group of ancient, non-flowering vascular plants with a prehistoric lineage. Renowned for its high silica content, it has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries to support bone health, skin integrity, and kidney function through its unique mineral properties.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Equisetidae, it falls under the order Equisetales and the family Equisetaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Equisetum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several regions in Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Additionally, its distribution extends to the unique environments of the Føroyaar islands. The species is also well-documented throughout the territory of Great Britain.
