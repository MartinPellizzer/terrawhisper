African yellowwood

African yellowwood, scientifically known as Annickia chlorantha, is a magnificent evergreen tree native to the tropical rainforests of West and Central Africa. Renowned for its striking, bright yellow flowers, this botanical treasure is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing therapeutic properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern primarily focused within the tropical regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Liberia and Nigeria. The species also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Cameroon and the Congo. Additionally, its range reaches the coastal region of Cabinda within the West-Central zone. Together, these locations highlight a concentrated geographical footprint across several diverse tropical nations.
