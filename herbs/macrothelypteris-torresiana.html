Macrothelypteris torresiana

Macrothelypteris torresiana, commonly known as a vital medicinal fern, holds significant importance in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and ethnobotanical uses is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacological research.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further organized into the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales.
