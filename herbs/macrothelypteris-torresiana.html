Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the southern hemisphere. Its presence extends significantly into the Western Indian Ocean, where it thrives in various island ecosystems. Specifically, it is documented in the Comoros and on the island of Mauritius.
