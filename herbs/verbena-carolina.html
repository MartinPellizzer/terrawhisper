Verbena carolina

Verbena carolina, commonly known as Carolina verbena, is a resilient perennial herb native to North America. Featuring delicate, tubular purple flowers atop slender stems, it thrives in sunny, moist habitats. This charming wildflower is highly attractive to pollinators like butterflies and bees, adding vibrant color to any natural landscape.

  • Scientific name: Verbena carolina
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Verbena carolina, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification is completed within the genus Verbena.
