Tournefortia mutabilis

Tournefortia mutabilis, commonly known as Barbasco, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent phytochemical properties, it is traditionally utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects. This plant serves as a vital resource in ethnobotany, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
