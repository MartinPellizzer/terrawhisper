Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of the South American continent. In Northern South America, its presence is documented within the borders of Venezuela. Moving toward the western part of the continent, it can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of Colombia and Ecuador. Together, these locations highlight the significant range this medicinal plant occupies throughout the Andean and northern regions.

Plant Parts

This plant, Minthostachys mollis, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for the chemical properties found within its leaves. These foliage components serve as the main source of the plant's therapeutic potential, as they contain a high concentration of aromatic essential oil. This oil, extracted from the leaf tissue, carries the distinct scent and the bioactive compounds responsible for the plant's antimicrobial and digestive benefits.
