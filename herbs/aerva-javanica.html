Aerva javanica

Aerva javaica, commonly known as Hydnora or Gunung Lawang, is a versatile medicinal herb found across tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. This resilient plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from skin infections to digestive and respiratory issues.

  • Scientific name: Aerva javanica
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Aerva.
