Justicia micrantha

Justicia micrantha, commonly known as the small-flowered Justicia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues, skin infections, and inflammatory conditions. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.
