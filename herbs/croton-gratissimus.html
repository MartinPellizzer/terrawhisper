Lavender croton, scientifically known as Croton gratissimus, is a striking ornamental shrub cherished for its vibrant, colorful foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various cultures utilize its leaves and extracts in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.

Plant Parts

This plant, Croton gratissimum, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly through the use of its foliage. The leaf serves as the primary medicinal component, often harvested to prepare a potent herbal tea. When these leaves are steeped, the resulting infusion is frequently used to treat various ailments, ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, making this part of the plant essential to its therapeutic application.
