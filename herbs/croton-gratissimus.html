Croton gratissimus

Croton gratissimus, commonly known as the "Wild Croton," is a versatile medicinal shrub found across tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and gastrointestinal issues, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Croton gratissimus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Croton.
