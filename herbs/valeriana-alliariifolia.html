Valeriana alliariifolia

Valeriana alliariifolia, commonly known as garlic valerian, is a distinctive perennial herb native to mountainous regions. Characterized by its unique, pungent garlic-like scent, this plant belongs to the Caprifoliaceae family. It is valued in ethnobotany for its aromatic properties and serves as a fascinating subject in alpine botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Valeriana alliariifolia
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales, it falls into the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with its placement in the genus Valeriana.
