Paeonia ostii

Paeonia ostii, commonly known as the Ostia peony, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic potential. Native to specific Asian regions, it contains bioactive compounds valued in traditional practices. This resilient species offers unique phytochemical properties, making it a significant subject for modern pharmacological research and herbal wellness.
