Asplenium ensiforme

What's the taxonomical classification of Asplenium ensiforme?

Asplenium ensiforme belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. This fern is a representative of the family Aspleniaceae, specifically situated within the genus Asplenium, with its unique biological identity defined by the species ensiforme.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Asplenium ensiforme has long, sword-shaped fronds that are dark green, glossy, and taper to a sharp point at the tip. These pinnate leaves consist of numerous narrow, lanceolate leaflets arranged along a central rachis. The texture of the foliage is typically leathery, which helps the plant retain moisture in its natural habitat. The underside of the fronds often displays small, circular sori that contain the reproductive spores. This morphological structure allows the fern to efficiently capture light while managing its structural integrity.
