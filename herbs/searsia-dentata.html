Searsia dentata

Nana-berry

Searsia dentata, commonly known as the Tooth-edged Karoo-thorn, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its distinctive serrated leaves, this hardy plant plays a vital role in local ecosystems. It is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties and its ability to thrive in arid environments.

  • Scientific name: Searsia dentata
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae, it is classified under the genus Searsia. Consequently, its full taxonomic identity is established as Searsia dentata.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across various regions of the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving into the Southern African region, it can be found inhabiting the Cape Provinces. Additionally, the species is recorded within the borders of Lesotho. Finally, its range extends through the lush landscapes of KwaZulu-Natal.
