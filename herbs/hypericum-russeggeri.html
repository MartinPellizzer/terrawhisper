Hypericum russeggeri

Hypericum russeggeri, commonly known as a rare member of the St. John's wort family, is a distinctive medicinal plant found in specific montane habitats. Characterized by its unique floral structure, it holds significant botanical interest. Research explores its potential bioactive compounds, offering promising insights into its traditional therapeutic applications.
