Hemidesmus indicus

Indian sarsaparilla

Hemidesmus indicus, commonly known as Indian Sarsaparilla, is a perennial climbing shrub revered in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its sweet, aromatic roots, it serves as a potent blood purifier, coolant, and rejuvenator. This versatile medicinal plant is widely utilized to treat skin disorders, fever, and various systemic inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Hemidesmus indicus
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hemidesmus.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Indian Sarsaparilla, is a climbing shrub where the most pharmacologically active components are the roots. Specifically, the medicinal properties are concentrated within the thick, fleshy root system that develops underground. While the slender, twining stem helps the plant climb through surrounding vegetation, it is the subterranean tuberous roots that are harvested for their therapeutic extracts. These large, starch-rich tuberous root structures are widely used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, making them the most vital part of the species.
