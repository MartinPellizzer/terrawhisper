Uvaria grandiflora, scientifically known as Uvaria grandiflora, is a climbing shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its fragrant, large blossoms, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It contains bioactive compounds studied for their potential antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic possibilities.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found in Hainan and throughout China Southeast. Its range also extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it is documented to grow in Cambodia and Laos. Furthermore, its presence is noted within the borders of Myanmar.
