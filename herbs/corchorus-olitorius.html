Corchorus olitorius

Corchorus olitorius, commonly known as jute mallow or saluyot, is a versatile medicinal plant widely used in traditional medicine. Renowned for its nutrient-dense leaves, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Often integrated into culinary practices, it serves as a vital natural remedy for promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Corchorus olitorius
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
