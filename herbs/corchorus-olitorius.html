Plant Parts

This plant, Corchorus olitorius, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components offer different benefits. The lush green leaves are the most commonly utilized part, often consumed as a nutritional vegetable, while a single leaf can be processed to extract mucilaginous properties used in traditional remedies. The slender, upright stems provide the structural framework for the plant's growth, supporting the development of its reproductive organs. For propagation and specific medicinal applications, the plant produces numerous small seeds, and each individual seed contains the genetic potential to grow a new specimen, ensuring the continuity of this valuable species.
