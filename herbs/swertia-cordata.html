Swertia cordata

Swertia cordata, commonly known as Swertia, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Native to the Himalayan regions, this plant is renowned for its potent bitter principles. It serves as a powerful digestive tonic, offering therapeutic benefits for liver health, metabolic regulation, and managing various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Swertia cordata
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Swertia cordata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianales. Finally, it is a member of the family Gentianaceae within the genus Swertia.
