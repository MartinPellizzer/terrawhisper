Saprosma fragrans

Saprosma fragrans, commonly known as the fragrant saprosma, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to the tropical forests of Africa. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Traditional healers utilize its various parts to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal distress and inflammation.
