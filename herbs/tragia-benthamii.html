Tragia benthamii

Tragia benthamii, commonly known as the nettle tree, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Belonging to the Euphorbiaceae family, it is recognized for its stinging hairs. Traditionally, various parts of this plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat skin irritations and inflammatory conditions effectively.

  • Scientific name: Tragia benthamii
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
