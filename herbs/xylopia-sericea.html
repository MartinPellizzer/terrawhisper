Xylopia sericea

Xylopia sericea, commonly known as the African Pepper tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic bark and seeds, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, offering significant phytochemical potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Xylopia sericea
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Xylopia sericea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Magnoliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Annonaceae and falls under the genus Xylopia.

Common Names

This plant, Xylopia seriacea, is known by several different names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Red Xylopia, while in Portuguese, it is known as Xylopia-vermelha. Within the local languages of its native habitat, it is called M'pinda in Lingala and Kimbundu.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across both Central and Northern South America. In the Central American region, its presence is documented in Costa Rica, Nicaragua, and Panamá. Moving into Northern South America, the species can also be found growing in the tropical landscapes of Guyana. Furthermore, its distribution extends eastward into the territory of Suriname. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's preference for specific neotropical environments.
