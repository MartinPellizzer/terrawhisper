Mad-dog weed, scientifically known as Alisma plantago-aquatica, is a resilient herbaceous plant found in wetland habitats. Traditionally utilized in various folk medicines, it is valued for its purported anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties. Understanding its botanical characteristics and therapeutic potential remains essential for exploring its role in natural pharmacology.
