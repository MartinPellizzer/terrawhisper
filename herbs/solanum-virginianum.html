Solanum virginianum

Solanum virginianum, commonly known as the Virginia Nightshade, is a medicinal herb native to specific regions. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it serves as a vital subject for pharmacological research into its bioactive compounds and healing properties.
