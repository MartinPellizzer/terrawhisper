Yellow-berried nightshade, scientifically known as Solanum virginianum, is a perennial herb native to North America. Characterized by its distinctive bright yellow berries and delicate white flowers, this plant belongs to the Solanaceae family. While often found in woodland settings, its chemical properties remain a subject of botanical interest.
