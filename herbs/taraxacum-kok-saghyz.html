Taraxacum kok-saghyz

Taraxacum kok-saghyz, commonly known as the Russian dandelion, is a resilient perennial prized for its medicinal properties. Renowned for its deep-reaching roots, it serves as a potent natural diuretic and liver tonic. Used traditionally to support digestion and detoxification, this versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Taraxacum kok-saghyz
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Taraxacum.
