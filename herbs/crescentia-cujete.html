Tzimah tree, scientifically known as Crescentia cujete, is a versatile tropical evergreen celebrated in traditional medicine. Known for its distinctive woody, gourd-like fruits, it is widely utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin conditions. This resilient plant offers profound therapeutic benefits through its various bioactive compounds.
