Polyspora ceylanica

Polyspora ceylanica, commonly known as Ceylon Polysporpora, is a significant medicinal herb native to Sri Lanka. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic medicine to treat various ailments. Its unique phytochemical profile offers promising therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Polyspora ceylanica
  • Family: Theaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Ericales and the family Theaceae. It is further identified by the genus Polyspora, specifically as the species Polyspora ceylanica.

This plant, Polyspora ceylanica, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In Sinhala, it is commonly referred to as Karunā. In Tamil, the plant is known as Karunā.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Polyspora ceylanica, exhibits a very specific and localized geographical distribution. Its presence is primarily concentrated within the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, the species is found inhabiting the island nation of Sri Lanka. Because its range is so restricted to this single region, it is considered an endemic species to that area. Consequently, the natural habitat of this medicinal plant is limited to the unique ecosystems found across Sri Lanka.
