Thymus caespititius

Thymus caespititius, commonly known as creeping thyme, is a resilient, low-growing perennial herb native to rocky, Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate blooms, this medicinal plant offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support respiratory health and soothe digestive discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Thymus caespititius
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Thymus.

This plant, known as Thymus caespititius, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across parts of Southwestern Europe and Macaronesia. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as creeping thyme, derives its significant therapeutic properties primarily from its essential oils. These volatile oils are concentrated within the glandular trichomes located on the leaves and flowering stems, containing key bioactive compounds such as thymol and carvacrol. When the plant material is harvested or crushed, these essential oils are released, providing the distinct aromatic profile and the potent antimicrobial and antiseptic qualities for which the species is medicinaly valued.
