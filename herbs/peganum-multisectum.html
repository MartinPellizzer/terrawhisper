Peganum multisectum

Peganum multisectum, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, is a significant plant in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, it is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile species offers promising therapeutic potential for managing various ailments through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Peganum multisectum
  • Family: Nitrariaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Nitrariaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Peganum.
