What's the taxonomical classification of Angiopteris yunnanensis?

Angiopteris yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and further categorized into the subclass Marattiidae. The plant follows the biological order Marattiales and is a member of the family Marattiaceae. Specifically, it is identified under the genus Angiopteris, with its unique designation being the species yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Angiopteris yunnanensis has a creeping rhizome that produces delicate, bright green, dichotomously branched fronds reaching lengths of up to 20 centimeters. The sterile segments of these fronds are finely divided into narrow, linear lobes that give the plant a feathery appearance. Fertile portions are characterized by specialized sporangia arranged in distinct, somewhat club-shaped or cylindrical structures at the tips of the branches. These reproductive structures are typically more compact and darker in color than the vegetative segments. The overall architecture of the plant is characterized by its intricate, lace-like spreading pattern across moist substrates.
