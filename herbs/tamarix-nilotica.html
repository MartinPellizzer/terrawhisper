Nile tamarisk, scientifically known as Tamarix nilotica, is a resilient, salt-tolerant shrub native to arid regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This versatile plant offers significant pharmacological potential for developing natural therapeutic treatments in desert ecosystems.
