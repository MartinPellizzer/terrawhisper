Chenopodiastrum hybridum

Chenopodiastrum hybridum, commonly known as the hybrid goosefoot, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique leaf structure, it possesses diverse bioactive compounds. This plant is frequently studied for its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering promising insights into natural therapeutic applications.
