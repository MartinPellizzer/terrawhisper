Pyrola japonica

Pyrola japonica, commonly known as Japanese wintergreen, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate bell-shaped flowers, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. Its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic benefits, particularly for addressing inflammation and supporting various respiratory wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Pyrola japonica
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Pyrola japonica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Pyrola.

Additionally, it can be located within the Primorye region of Russia. Moving into China, the species inhabits the areas of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Therefore, its range spans across diverse landscapes in both northern China and the Russian Far East.
