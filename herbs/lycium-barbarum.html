Lycium barbarum

Lycium barbarum, commonly known as goji berry, is a powerhouse of nutrition celebrated in traditional Chinese medicine. These vibrant red berries are packed with antioxidants, vitamins, and essential minerals. Renowned for supporting immune health, vision, and longevity, they serve as a versatile superfood in both culinary and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Lycium barbarum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Lycium.
