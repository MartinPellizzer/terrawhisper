Chinese wolfberry

Lycium barbarum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (550 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese wolfberry, scientifically known as Lycium barbarum, is a powerhouse of nutrition celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, these vibrant red berries support eye health, immune function, and longevity. Often used in teas and soups, they offer a potent natural remedy for promoting overall vitality.
