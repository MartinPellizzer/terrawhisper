Lycium barbarum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (550 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese wolfberry, scientifically known as Lycium barbarum, is a powerhouse of nutrition celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, these vibrant red berries support eye health, immune function, and longevity. Often used in teas and soups, they offer a potent natural remedy for promoting overall vitality.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Lycium barbarum, is a versatile medicinal shrub where various components are utilized for their health benefits. The vibrant red, oblong fruits, commonly referred to as goji berries, are the most widely consumed part of the plant and are prized for their high antioxidant content. Within these fruits, the tiny seeds are found, which play a vital role in the plant's reproduction. Additionally, the leaves of Lycium barbarum are sometimes harvested and used in traditional medicinal preparations, making the entire structure a significant subject in herbal studies.
