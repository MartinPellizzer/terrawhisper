Piper kadsura

Piper kadsura, commonly known as the Kadsur plant, is a significant medicinal herb frequently utilized in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Native to various tropical regions, it is highly valued for its potent bioactive compounds. This plant is primarily recognized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties in holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Piper kadsura
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Piper kadsura, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Piperales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Piperaceae and the genus Piper.

Common Names

This plant, Piper kadsura, is known by various common names depending on the language and region. In Mainland China (zh), the name is also given as 风藤, and in Japanese (ja), the plant is called フウトウカズラ.

Distribution

This plant is geographically distributed across several specific regions within Eastern Asia. Its range includes the islands of Japan and the Korean Peninsula. Within the Japanese archipelago, it can be found inhabiting the Kazan-retto area. Furthermore, its presence extends to the Nansei-shoto islands. Finally, the species is also documented occurring in the Ogasawara-shoto region.
