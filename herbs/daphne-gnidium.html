Flax-leaved Daphne

Daphne gnidium · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (26 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Flax-leaved Daphne, scientifically known as Daphne gnidium, is a highly toxic evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its glossy, lanceolate leaves and fragrant white flowers, it contains potent daphnane diterpene esters. Due to its extreme toxicity, it must be handled with extreme caution.
