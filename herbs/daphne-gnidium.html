Daphne gnidium

Daphne gnidium, commonly known as Spurge-laurel, is a perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its glossy evergreen leaves and fragrant white flowers, this plant is chemically potent. While historically studied for its medicinal properties, it contains toxic alkaloids, requiring cautious handling and expert pharmacological application.

  • Scientific name: Daphne gnidium
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Thymelaeaceae and specifically within the genus Daphne.

This plant is primarily distributed throughout several regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing in the Baleares islands and the island of Corse.
