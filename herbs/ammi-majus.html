Bishop's weed, scientifically known as Ammi majus, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Widely recognized in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat digestive issues and skin ailments, this aromatic plant remains a significant subject in ethnopharmacology.
