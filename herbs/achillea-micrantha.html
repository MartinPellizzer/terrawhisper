Achillea micrantha

Achillea micrantha, commonly known as the small-flowered yarrow, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, it possesses diverse bioactive compounds. Often utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, this plant plays a vital role in herbal remedies for promoting overall healing.

  • Scientific name: Achillea micrantha
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by its genus, Achillea.
