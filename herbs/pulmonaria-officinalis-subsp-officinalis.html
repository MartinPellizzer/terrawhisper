Pulmonaria officinalis subsp. officinalis

Pulmonaria officinalis subsp. officinalis, commonly known as Lungwort, is a perennial herb celebrated in traditional herbalism. Named for its spotted leaves resembling lung tissue, it has historically been used to support respiratory health. This medicinal plant offers unique bioactive compounds, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Pulmonaria officinalis subsp. officinalis
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is part of the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Pulmonaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is well-documented in Denmark, Great Britain, and Ireland. It can also be found extending further north into the landscapes of Sweden. Additionally, the species reaches into Middle Europe, specifically within the borders of Austria. Together, these locations define its primary geographical range across these diverse territories.
