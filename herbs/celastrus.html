Celastrus

Celastrus, commonly known as bittersweet, is a versatile genus of woody vines and shrubs valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently studied for its potential neuroprotective and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient plant offers a fascinating intersection of botanical beauty and significant therapeutic promise.

  • Scientific name: Celastrus
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Celastrales. Finally, it is a member of the family Celastraceae and falls under the genus Celastrus.
