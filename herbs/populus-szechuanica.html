Populus szechuanica

Populus szechuanica, commonly known as Sichuan Aspen, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Its bark and leaves contain bioactive compounds with potent anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, this species offers valuable therapeutic potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Populus szechuanica
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales and the family Salicaceae, it is further classified under the genus Populus.
