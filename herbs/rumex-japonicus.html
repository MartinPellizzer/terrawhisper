Japanese Dock, scientifically known as Rumex japonicus, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Often found in moist habitats, its leaves and roots possess anti-inflammatory and antibacterial properties. Frequently utilized in East Asian herbal practices, this resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for treating various skin and digestive ailments.
