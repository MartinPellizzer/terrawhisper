Japanese Dock, scientifically known as Rumex japonicus, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing in Kamchatka, Sakhalin, and Primorye. Its presence extends further through the Kuril Islands within the same Russian territory. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout China, specifically in the South-Central regions. Together, these locations define the diverse natural habitats where the plant thrives.
