Rumex japonicus

Rumex japonicus, commonly known as Japanese dock, is a perennial herb belonging to the Polygonaceae family. Found across Asia and North America, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, fever, and various digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Rumex japonicus
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing in Kamchatka, Sakhalin, and Primorye. Its presence extends further through the Kuril Islands within the same Russian territory. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout China, specifically in the South-Central regions.
