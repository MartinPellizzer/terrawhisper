Camelina, scientifically known as Camelina sativa, is a versatile oilseed crop valued for its exceptional nutritional profile. This hardy plant supports heart health and reduces inflammation, making it a powerful addition to modern wellness regimens.

Distribution

This plant, known as Camelina, exhibits a specific presence across several regions within Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the country of Denmark, where it adapts to the local climate. The species also extends its range into Finland, contributing to the botanical diversity of the area. Furthermore, its distribution reaches the islands of Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, the plant is also documented within the geographical borders of Norway.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Camelina sativa, is primarily cultivated for its nutrient-dense seed, which serves as the most significant medicinal and nutritional component. These small, oil-rich seeds are harvested to produce oil that is exceptionally high in alpha-linolenic acid, an essential omega-3 fatty acid known for its anti-inflammatory properties. Because the oil extracted from the seed can support cardiovascular health and skin integrity, it is frequently utilized in both dietary supplements and topical dermatological treatments.
