Persicaria capitata

Pink bubble persicaria

Persicaria capitata, commonly known as Capitata Knotweed, is a resilient perennial celebrated for its striking, rosette-forming foliage. Native to parts of Asia, this ornamental plant features dark, heart-shaped leaves. Beyond its aesthetic appeal in landscaping, it holds traditional significance in various herbal practices for its unique botanical properties.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria capitata
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Persicaria.
