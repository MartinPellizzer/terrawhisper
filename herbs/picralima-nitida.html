Picralima nitida

Picralima nitida, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in West African traditional medicine, holds immense therapeutic potential. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this species is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammatory conditions. Exploring its phytochemical properties offers promising insights for modern pharmacological advancements.

  • Scientific name: Picralima nitida
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae and is identified by the genus Picralima. Specifically, it is known as Picralima nitida.

Common Names

This plant, Picralima nitida, is known by several different names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as Bitter Leaf or African Timber Tree, while in French, it is commonly called la feuille amère. Within various West African linguistic groups, it is identified by names such as afitin in Yoruba and ebun in Igbo.

Distribution

This plant, Picralima nitida, is distributed across several nations within the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the natural habitats of Benin and Ghana. Its geographical range also extends into the territories of Guinea and Ivory Coast. Furthermore, the species is well-documented within the borders of Nigeria. Collectively, these locations define its primary ecological presence in West Africa.
