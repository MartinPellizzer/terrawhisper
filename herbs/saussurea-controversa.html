Saussurea controversa

Saussurea controversa, commonly known as the controversial sawtooth gentian, is a rare medicinal perennial native to high-altitude Himalayan regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses potent bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments. This precious botanical species requires careful conservation due to its limited natural habitat.
