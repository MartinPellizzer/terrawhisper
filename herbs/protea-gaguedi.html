Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it inhabits parts of Eritrea and Ethiopia. Furthermore, the species is located within the territories of Sudan and South Sudan. Together, these locations highlight the plant's significant range across both central and northeastern tropical zones.
