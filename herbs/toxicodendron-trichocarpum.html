Toxicodendron trichocarpum

Toxicodendron trichocarpum, commonly known as the hairy poison sumac, is a deciduous shrub native to North American wetlands. Distinguished by its distinctive fuzzy, hair-covered stems, this plant contains urushiol, a potent allergen. Contact with its foliage or bark can trigger severe, itchy skin reactions and intense dermatitis.

  • Scientific name: Toxicodendron trichocarpum
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
