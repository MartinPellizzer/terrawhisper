Mountain lacquer tree, scientifically known as Toxicodendron trichocarpum, is a significant medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its potent resin, it is traditionally used in various therapeutic applications. However, its high urushiol content requires careful handling due to its ability to cause severe allergic skin reactions.
