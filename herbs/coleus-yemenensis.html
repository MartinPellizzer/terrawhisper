Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further nested within the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Coleus as the species Coleus yemenenensis.

Distribution

This plant is characterized by a very specific and limited geographical range. It is native to the unique landscapes found within the Arabian Peninsula. Specifically, its natural habitat is restricted to the country of Yemen. Because of this localized presence, the species is considered endemic to this particular region. Consequently, its wild distribution is defined solely by the borders of the Yemeni territory.
