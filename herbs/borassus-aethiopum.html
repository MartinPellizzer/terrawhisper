Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the African Palmyra palm, offers various medicinal and nutritional benefits derived from its different anatomical structures. The root is often utilized in traditional practices for its various therapeutic properties, while the large, fan-shaped leaf can be processed for diverse uses. The inflorescences serve as the reproductive structures that eventually give rise to the edible components. Within the resulting fruit, the fruit pulp provides a nutritious substance, and the internal kernels are highly valued both as a food source and for their potential medicinal applications.
