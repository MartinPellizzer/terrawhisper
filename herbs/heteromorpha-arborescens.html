Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in the nations of Burundi and Rwanda. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, it is also found within Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, this species inhabits the territories of Eritrea and Ethiopia. Finally, its distribution extends further into the Northeast Tropical Africa region through Sudan and South Sudan.
