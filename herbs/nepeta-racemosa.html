Nepeta racemosa

Dwarf catnip

Nepeta racemosa, commonly known as catmint, is a versatile perennial herb beloved for its fragrant, lavender-blue blooms. Native to many regions, this resilient plant attracts pollinators like bees and butterflies. Beyond its ornamental charm, it possesses traditional medicinal properties, often utilized for its soothing, calming, and anti-inflammatory effects.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta racemosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is a member of the Lamiaceae family. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Nepeta.
