Zingiber roseum

Zingiber roseum, commonly known as the rose-scented ginger, is a rare and captivating perennial herb belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Distinguished by its delicate floral hues and aromatic rhizomes, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine, offering potent bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Zingiber roseum
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Zingiber roseum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Zingiber.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia.
