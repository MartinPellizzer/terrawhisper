Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Zingiber roseum, derives much of its medicinal value from its underground structures. Specifically, the rhizome serves as the primary part used in traditional remedies for its potent bioactive compounds. When harvesting the plant, practitioners focus on collecting the fleshy rhizoic structures that store nutrients and essential oils. Because the therapeutic properties are concentrated within these rhizomes, they are carefully processed to create extracts or powders used in various healing applications.
