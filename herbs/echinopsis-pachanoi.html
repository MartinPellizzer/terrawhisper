San Pedro

Echinopsis pachanoi · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (2 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

San Pedro, scientifically known as Echinopsis pachanoi, is a columnar cactus native to the Andes mountains. Renowned for its ritualistic significance in South American shamanism, it contains mescaline. This succulent is widely studied for its psychoactive properties and its profound role in traditional spiritual healing and ceremonial practices.

Distribution

This plant is native to several distinct regions across the globe. In Western South America, it can be found growing in Bolivia, Colombia, and Ecuador. Its distribution also extends to the Macaronesian islands, specifically within the Canary Islands. Additionally, the species has established a presence in Southwestern Europe, particularly in Spain. These varied locations highlight the wide geographical range of this medicinal species.
