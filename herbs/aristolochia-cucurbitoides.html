What's the taxonomical classification of Aristolochia cucurbitoides?

Aristolochia cucurbitoides belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Piperales and falls into the family Aristolochiaceae. Specifically, it is a member of the genus Aristolochia, with its unique designation being the species cucurbitoides.
