Salacia chinensis

Salaacia chinensis, commonly known as Salaak or Salaak Wood, is a medicinal climbing shrub native to South Asia. Widely utilized in Ayurvedic traditions, this plant is highly valued for its potent bioactive compounds. It is primarily recognized for its therapeutic properties in managing diabetes, improving joint health, and supporting kidney function.

  • Scientific name: Salacia chinensis
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Celastrales, belonging to the family Celastraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Salacia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Salaacia chinensis, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Vietnam, it is known as Chóc máu, while in Thailand, it is called กำแพงเจ็ดชั้น. It is also recognized as सप्‍तरंगी in Marathi. In Chinese, the plant is identified as 五层龙 (or 五层龙 in Simplified Chinese).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically throughout the Southeast portion of the country. Within the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is noted in the state of Assam and across various parts of India. It also grows within the unique ecological zone of the East Himalaya. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the neighboring territory of Bangladesh.

Plant Parts

This plant, Salacia chinensis, utilizes various anatomical structures for its medicinal properties, most notably its stems and leaves. The stems, which are often woody and climbing in nature, are frequently harvested to extract bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies for managing blood sugar levels. Additionally, the leaves serve as important vegetative components that contribute to the plant's overall physiological health and are sometimes studied for their specific chemical profiles.
