Epimedium pubescens

Epimedium pubescens, commonly known as Horny Goat Weed, is a perennial herb prized in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its bioactive flavonoids, particularly icariin, it is widely utilized to support reproductive health, enhance vitality, and promote bone density. This versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential for modern wellness.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium pubescens
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales and the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Epimedium.
