Epimedium pubescens, scientifically known as Epimedium pubescens, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine for its restorative properties. Commonly called Horny Goat Weed, this plant contains icariin, a bioactive compound utilized to support reproductive health, enhance vitality, and promote bone density through its unique pharmacological effects.
