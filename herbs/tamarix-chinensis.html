Tamarix chinensis

Tamarix chinensis, commonly known as Chinese tamarisk, is a resilient, salt-tolerant shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, it thrives in arid, saline environments. This plant is widely utilized for its bioactive compounds, offering significant therapeutic potential in treating inflammation, skin ailments, and various systemic conditions.

  • Scientific name: Tamarix chinensis
  • Family: Tamaricaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Tamaricaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Tamarix.
