Lantana canescens

Lantana canescens, commonly known as Lantana, is a vibrant medicinal shrub celebrated for its colorful, clustered blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Used in various traditional healing practices, its leaves and flowers possess bioactive properties often utilized for their soothing and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Lantana canescens
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it resides in the family Verbenaceae under the genus Lantana.
