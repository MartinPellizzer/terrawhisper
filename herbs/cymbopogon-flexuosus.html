Cochin lemongrass

Cymbopogon flexuosus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (233 studies) · ★★★★★

Cochin lemongrass, scientifically known as Cymbopogon flexuosus, is a perennial aromatic grass highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it offers significant antibacterial, antifungal, and digestive benefits. This versatile plant serves as a natural remedy for inflammation and stress, widely used in holistic wellness.
