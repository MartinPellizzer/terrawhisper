Cochin lemongrass

Cymbopogon flexuosus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (233 studies) · ★★★★★

Cochin lemongrass, scientifically known as Cymbopogon flexuosus, is a perennial aromatic grass highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it offers significant antibacterial, antifungal, and digestive benefits. This versatile plant serves as a natural remedy for inflammation and stress, widely used in holistic wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as citronella grass, derives its therapeutic value primarily from its fresh leaves, which are harvested to capture their potent aromatic properties. When these leaves undergo distillation, they yield a highly concentrated essential oil that serves as the plant's most significant medicinal component. This oil is widely utilized in aromatherapy and topical applications due to its strong insect-repellent qualities and its ability to soothe skin irritations.
