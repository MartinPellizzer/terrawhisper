Byrsonima coccolobifolia

Byrsonima coccolobifolia, commonly known as the Coccolobifolium, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant possesses diverse bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, serving as a vital natural resource in various folk healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Byrsonima coccolobifolia
  • Family: Malpighiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Malpighiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Byrsonima.
