Agrimonia pilosa

Hairy agrimony

Agrimonia pilosa, commonly known as hairy agrimonia, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Characterized by its fine, velvety foliage, this species plays a vital role in traditional healing practices. It is frequently studied for its bioactive compounds and potential applications in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Agrimonia pilosa
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaecae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Agrimonia.
