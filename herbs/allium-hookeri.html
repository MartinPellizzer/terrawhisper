East-Himalayan chives, scientifically known as Allium hookeri, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant is widely utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and various skin infections naturally.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. In China, its presence is documented within the South-Central provinces as well as the high-altitude terrains of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the lush landscapes of Assam. It also inhabits the rugged environments of the East Himalaya region. Finally, the species extends its range southward to include the island of Sri Lanka.
