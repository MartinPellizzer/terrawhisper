Croton cajucaru, scientifically known as Croton cajucara, is a medicinal shrub native to Brazil. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and gastroprotective properties, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat gastric ulcers and digestive disorders. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
