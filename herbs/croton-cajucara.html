Croton cajucara

Croton cajucara, commonly known as Cajucara, is a medicinal plant native to Brazil, highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently used to treat digestive issues, skin conditions, and metabolic disorders, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Croton cajucara
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is further categorized under the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its scientific designation is placed within the genus Croton.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of the South American continent. In Northern South America, its presence is documented in both Suriname and Venezuela. The species also extends into Western South America, specifically inhabiting parts of Bolivia and Peru. Furthermore, its range includes the West-Central region of Brazil.
