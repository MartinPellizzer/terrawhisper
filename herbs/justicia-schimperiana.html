Justicia schimperiana

Justicia schimperiana, commonly known as the wild jasmine or mountain justicia, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to East African highlands. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation, digestive issues, and skin infections through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Justicia schimperiana
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Justicia.

Common Names

This plant, Justicia schimperiana, is known by various names across different linguistic groups. In English, it is frequently referred to as Schimper's Justicia, while in Amharic, it is commonly called ወይነ ወይነ (Weyne Weyne).

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the northeastern and eastern regions of tropical Africa. Within Northeast Tropical Africa, its range extends across Eritrea, Ethiopia, and Somalia. It is also commonly found in the East Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its presence is documented in both Kenya and Tanzania. These locations collectively define the natural habitat where the species can be found.
