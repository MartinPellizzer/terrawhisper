Chinese lobelia, scientifically known as Lobelia chinensis, is a versatile medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional practices, it is primarily utilized for its respiratory benefits and anti-inflammatory properties. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions in East Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. It is also documented as growing on the island of Hainan. Moving beyond mainland China, the species is native to Japan in Eastern Asia. Finally, its geographical range extends to include the Korean Peninsula.
