Lobelia chinensis

Chinese lobelia

Lobelia chinensis, commonly known as Chinese Lobelia, is a versatile medicinal herb native to East Asia. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it is valued for its anti-inflammatory and respiratory benefits. This flowering plant serves as a vital resource in botanical studies, offering unique bioactive compounds for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lobelia chinensis
  • Family: Campanulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Campanulaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Lobelia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lobelia chinensis, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans), it is called 半边莲, while in Traditional Chinese (zh-hant) and the Cantonese dialect of Hong Kong (zh-hk), it is known as 半邊蓮. In Mandarin (zh), the name is 半边莲, and in Japanese (ja), it is commonly referred to as ミゾカクシ.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions in East Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. It is also documented as growing on the island of Hainan. Moving beyond mainland China, the species is native to Japan in Eastern Asia. Finally, its geographical range extends to include the Korean Peninsula.
