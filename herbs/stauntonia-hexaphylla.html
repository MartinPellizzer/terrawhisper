Stauntonia hexaphylla

What's the taxonomical classification of Stauntonia hexaphylla?

Stauntonia hexaphyllia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. The plant is a member of the family Lardizabalaceae, falling under the genus Stauntonia, and is specifically identified by its species name, hexaphyllia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Stauntonia hexaphyllia has woody, climbing vines that produce compound leaves typically composed of six to seven serrated leaflets arranged in a whorl. These leaves emerge from a central point on the stem, giving the foliage a distinct star-like appearance. The plant features small, yellowish-green flowers that grow in clusters along the stems. After flowering, it develops small, fleshy berries that transition from green to a bright red or orange color when mature. The stems are often twining and can become quite thick and woody as the plant ages.

According to a study published by "International journal of molecular sciences", the fruit of Stauntonia hexaphyllla is used medicinally and holds traditional importance in East Asia for treating rheumatism. Steam treatment is commonly applied to the fruit to enhance its medicinal properties. Steamed extracts of the fruit increase levels of 5-hydroxymethylfurfural, total flavonoids, and phenolics. These steamed extracts promote alkaline phosphatase activity, calcium nodule formation, and collagen synthesis in MC3T3-E1 osteoblasts. Furthermore, the extracts inhibit tartrate-resistant acid phosphatase activity in RAW 264.7 osteoclasts. The component 5-HMF is noted as playing a pivotal role in promoting alkaline phosphatase and inhibiting tartrate-resistant acid phosphatase activities.

Saponins from S. hexaphyllas leaves show a definite advantage for the development of oral medications to control inflammatory responses.

What are the pharmacological activities of Stauntonia hexaphylla?

This plant has analgesic, anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, diuretic, and sedative pharmacological activities. Its bioactive compounds work to alleviate physical pain and reduce systemic inflammation within the body. The antioxidant properties help neutralize harmful free radicals to prevent cellular damage. Additionally, the plant exhibits diuretic effects that promote increased urine production to assist in fluid regulation. Finally, its sedative qualities can be utilized to induce relaxation and assist with calming the nervous system.

According to a study published by "Natural product research", Stauntonia hexaphyllia is a medicinal plant used for its analgesic, sedative, and diuretic effects. Bioassay-guided fractionation of its ethanol extracts led to the isolation of seven compounds. The ethanol extract at 20 µg/mL, the DCM fraction at 20 µg/mL, and compound 1 at 10 µM significantly decreased COX-2 and iNOS expression in LPS-induced RAW264.7 cells. These results suggest that the leaves and compound 1 are useful candidates for treating inflammatory and other diseases.
