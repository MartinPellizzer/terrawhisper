Stauntonia hexaphylla

Stauntonia hexaphyllia, commonly known as the Six-leaf Stauntonia, is a perennial woody vine native to North America. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its roots are utilized for their potent medicinal properties, particularly in treating digestive ailments, skin conditions, and inflammatory issues, offering a unique natural remedy for holistic wellness.
