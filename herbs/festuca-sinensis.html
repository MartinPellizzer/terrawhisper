Festuca sinensis

Festuca sinensis, commonly known as Chinese fescue, is a resilient medicinal grass valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its unique therapeutic properties, this plant is often utilized to address various ailments, offering natural healing benefits. Its integration into holistic wellness highlights its significant role in botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Festuca sinensis
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales. Finally, it is a member of the family Poaceae and is situated within the genus Festuca.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Festuca sinensis, is referred to by several common names depending on the linguistic region: in Chinese, it is called 中华羊茅, specifically as 中华羊茅 in mainland China (zh-cn), and as 中華羊茅 in Taiwan (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions of China. It is notably found within the China South-Central area. Additionally, its presence extends into the China North-Central region. The species also inhabits the province of Qinghai. Together, these locations define the primary geographical range of Festuca sinensis.
