Murraya

Murraya, commonly known as Curry Leaf plant, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in Ayurvedic traditions. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it supports digestive health, manages blood sugar levels, and promotes hair vitality. This aromatic herb is an essential staple for both culinary delight and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Murraya
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. Finally, the medicinal species is categorized under the genus Murraya.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across diverse regions of Asia and the Indian Ocean. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found inhabiting the islands of Comoros, Mauritius, and Réunion. Furthermore, its presence extends to the tropical archipelago of the Seychelles. Moving toward the Asian continent, the species is also distributed throughout China. Specifically, it thrives within the South-Central region of China.
