Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across diverse regions of Asia and the Indian Ocean. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found inhabiting the islands of Comoros, Mauritius, and Réunion. Furthermore, its presence extends to the tropical archipelago of the Seychelles. Moving toward the Asian continent, the species is also distributed throughout China. Specifically, it thrives within the South-Central region of China.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Curry Leaf (Murraya koenigii), is highly valued in traditional medicine, with its leaves serving as the primary therapeutic component. These pinnate leaves are rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, such as carbazole alkaloids, which contribute to their antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties. In various healing practices, the leaves are utilized to aid digestion, manage blood glucose levels, and support neurological health, making them a versatile medicinal resource.
