Gynura japonica

Gynura japonica, commonly known as Sawtooth Treacle or Choy Sum, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its nutrient-rich leaves, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, often utilized to support detoxification, promote skin health, and manage various metabolic conditions naturally and effectively.

  • Scientific name: Gynura japonica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gynura, specifically as the species Gynura japonica.
