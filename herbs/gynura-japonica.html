Gynura japonica, scientifically known as Gynura japonica, is a versatile medicinal herb widely used in traditional Asian medicine. Often referred to as "flower cabbage," this nutrient-rich plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and detoxifying properties, making it a valuable resource for supporting overall health and wellness.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. The species also extends its range into the island nation of Japan. Additionally, it can be found growing within the diverse environments of Taiwan. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Gynura japonica.
