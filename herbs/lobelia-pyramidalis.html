Lobelia pyramidalis

What's the taxonomical classification of Lobelia pyramidalis?

Lobelia pyramidalis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant further falls into the order Asterales and is a member of the family Campanulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lobelia and the species name pyramidalis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lobelia pyramidalis has upright, slender stems that reach heights of up to one meter and bear densely packed, terminal pyramidal inflorescences of bright blue flowers. The leaves are lanceolate to linear in shape, arranged oppositely along the stem, and possess finely serrated margins. Each individual flower features a characteristic zygomorphic structure with a prominent two-lipped corolla. The upper lip is typically two-lobed while the lower lip consists of three lobes, creating a distinct visual profile. This species is easily identified by its striking conical flowering spikes that contrast sharply against its green foliage.
