Terminalia fagifolia

Terminalia fagifolia, commonly known as the "creeper terminalia," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This climbing shrub offers significant pharmacological potential for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia fagifolia
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Terminalia.
