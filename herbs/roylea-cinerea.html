Roylea cinerea

Roylea cinerea, commonly known as the Blue Roylea, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its vibrant blue flowers, this botanical species is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential through its unique phytochemical properties and healing capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Roylea cinerea
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, the species is categorized under the genus Roylea.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Roylea cinerea, is recognized by several different common names depending on the region and language. Within local contexts in India, it is known by its Hindi name, Neela Roylea, while in Bengali, it is called Neela Roylea as well.
