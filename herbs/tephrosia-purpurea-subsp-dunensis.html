Tephrosia purpurea subsp. dunensis

Tephrosia purpurea subsp. dunensis, commonly known as sand pea, is a resilient coastal shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Thriving in sandy environments, this subspecies contains bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to treat skin ailments and inflammation. Its unique ability to stabilize dunes makes it ecologically vital and pharmacologically significant.

  • Scientific name: Tephrosia purpurea subsp. dunensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Tephrosia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in Africa and the surrounding islands. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is recorded in both Kenya and Tanzania. Moving southward, the species is also found within South Tropical Africa, specifically in Malawi and Mozambique. Additionally, its range extends into the Western Indian Ocean, where it inhabits Madagascar. Together, these locations highlight the diverse habitats this medicinal subspecies occupies.
