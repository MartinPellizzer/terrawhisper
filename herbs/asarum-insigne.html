Asarum insigne

Asarum insigne, commonly known as Wild Ginger, is a perennial herb native to the moist, shaded forests of East Asia. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes and distinctive heart-shaped leaves, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial properties and its ability to soothe digestive and respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Asarum insigne
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Aristolochiaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Asarum, specifically the species Asarum insigne.
