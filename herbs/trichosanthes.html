Trichosanthes

Trichosanthes, commonly known as snake gourd or heart-leaved moonseed, is a versatile climbing vine celebrated in traditional medicine. Rich in bioactive compounds, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Often used to support digestive health and skin vitality, this botanical treasure serves as a vital therapeutic resource.
