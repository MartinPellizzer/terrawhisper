Avena sativa

Avena sativa, commonly known as oats, is a versatile cereal grain celebrated for both its nutritional value and medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like avenanthramides, it offers potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. Often used in skincare and dietary supplements, it supports digestive health and promotes overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Avena sativa
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Avena sativa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Poales. Finally, it is a member of the family Poaceae and is identified by the genus Avena.
