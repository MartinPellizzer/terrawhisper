This plant, known scientifically as Avena sativa, is a species of cereal grain that is primarily utilized for its nutritional and therapeutic properties. The most significant part of the Avena sativa herb is the grain itself, though the entire plant structure plays a role in its cultivation. The leaf is long, narrow, and blade-like, serving as the primary site for photosynthesis to support the plant's growth.
