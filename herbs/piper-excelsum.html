Piper excelsum

Piper excelsum, commonly known as the pepper tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its unique chemical profile offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Piper excelsum
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Piperales and the family Piperaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Piper.
