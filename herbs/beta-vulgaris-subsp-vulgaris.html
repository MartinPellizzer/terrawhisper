Beta vulgaris subsp. vulgaris

Beta vulgaris subsp. vulgaris, commonly known as beetroot, is a versatile biennial plant prized for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants, nitrates, and essential vitamins, it supports cardiovascular health, lowers blood pressure, and reduces inflammation. This nutrient-dense root serves as a powerful functional food in natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Beta vulgaris subsp. vulgaris
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Beta.
