Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the Russian Far East. Specifically, its presence is documented in the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye territories. Beyond Russia, the species extends its range into various parts of China. It can be found growing within the Inner Mongolia region of the country. Additionally, its distribution encompasses the broader area known as Manchuria.
