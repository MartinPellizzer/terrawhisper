Salvia trijuga

Salvia trijuga, commonly known as the three-cornered sage, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of East Asia. Characterized by its unique trifurcated structure, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its diverse therapeutic properties, offering potential benefits for various systemic health conditions.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
