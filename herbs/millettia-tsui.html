What's the taxonomical classification of Millettia tsui?

Millettia tsui belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Fabales. It is a member of the family Fabaceae, specifically falling within the genus Millettia, and is identified by its unique species name, tsui.
