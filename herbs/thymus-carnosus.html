Thymus carnosus

Thymus carnosus, commonly known as succulent thyme, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to arid regions. Characterized by its fleshy, moisture-retaining leaves, this resilient plant offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support respiratory health and alleviate various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Thymus carnosus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, the species is identified under the genus Thymus.

Plant Parts

This plant, Thymus carnosus, derives its significant therapeutic value primarily from its essential oils. These volatile oils are concentrated within the glandular trichomes located on the leaves and stems, containing complex chemical compounds like thymol that provide its characteristic aroma and medicinal potency. When harvested and processed, these essential oils serve as the primary active component used in various traditional and modern applications.
