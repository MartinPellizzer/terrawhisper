Gentiana lutea

Gentiana lutea, commonly known as Great Yellow Gentian, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Renowned for its intense bitterness, its roots are highly valued in traditional medicine and herbalism. It is widely used to stimulate digestion, support liver function, and enhance overall metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana lutea
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Gentiana.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout various regions of Middle Europe. Its presence is well-documented in the mountainous landscapes of Austria and Germany. It also inhabits the territories spanning across Czechia and Slovakia. Additionally, the species can be located within the alpine environments of Switzerland. Beyond the mainland, its distribution extends to the island of Corse in Southwestern Europe.
