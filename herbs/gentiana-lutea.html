Great Yellow Gentian

Gentiana lutea · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (646 studies) · ★★★★★

Great Yellow Gentian, scientifically known as Gentiana lutea, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Renowned for its bitter roots, it has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to stimulate digestion, support liver function, and treat various gastrointestinal ailments through its powerful tonic properties.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout various regions of Middle Europe. Its presence is well-documented in the mountainous landscapes of Austria and Germany. It also inhabits the territories spanning across Czechia and Slovakia. Additionally, the species can be located within the alpine environments of Switzerland. Beyond the mainland, its distribution extends to the island of Corse in Southwestern Europe.

Plant Parts

This plant, Gentiana lutea, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent therapeutic properties. The most significant part used for medicinal purposes is the roots, specifically the thick, bitter root system which contains high concentrations of bitter glycosides. In addition to the underground structures, the plant features elongated shoots that support the overall structure of the herb. The vibrant green leaves are also characteristic of the species, with each individual leaf contributing to the plant's ability to store the chemical compounds necessary for its medicinal efficacy.
