Great Yellow Gentian

Gentiana lutea · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (646 studies) · ★★★★★

Great Yellow Gentian, scientifically known as Gentiana lutea, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Renowned for its bitter roots, it has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to stimulate digestion, support liver function, and treat various gastrointestinal ailments through its powerful tonic properties.

It thrives in subalpine and alpine zones, often colonizing nutrient-rich, moist, yet well-drained soils that are characteristic of mountain pastures. These habitats provide the necessary light intensity and cool temperatures required for its long-term development, as the species follows a perennial life cycle that involves several years of vegetative growth before flowering. Because it occupies expansive meadow ecosystems, its survival is closely tied to the seasonal shifts of mountain climates, including snow cover that protects its rootstock during winter and the brief, intense growing seasons of the summer months.
