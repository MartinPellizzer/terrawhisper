Sambucus australis

Sambucus australis, commonly known as the Australian elderberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various regions. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional remedies to support immune health, combat inflammation, and soothe respiratory ailments through its nutrient-rich berries and therapeutic flowers.

  • Scientific name: Sambucus australis
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Dipsacales and the family Viburnaceae. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy concludes within the genus Sambucus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several regions of Southern South America. It can be found growing throughout Paraguay and Uruguay, extending into the northeastern parts of Argentina. Within the borders of Brazil, its presence is noted in both the Southeast and the South. These specific locations highlight its ability to thrive in diverse subtropical environments across the continent. Consequently, the geographical range of Sambucus australis spans multiple countries in the southern portion of the hemisphere.
