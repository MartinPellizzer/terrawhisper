Eriocaulon buergerianum

Eriocauloon buergerianum, commonly known as a medicinal herb in certain traditional practices, is a delicate plant often found in wetland habitats. Renowned for its unique botanical characteristics, it holds significant interest in ethnobotany. Researchers continue to explore its potential bioactive compounds and therapeutic applications for various health benefits.
