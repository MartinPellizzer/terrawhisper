Plant Parts

This plant, Polygonatum cyrtonema, possesses a distinct morphological structure that contributes to its medicinal value. The most significant part of the species is the fleshy rhizomes, which are often the primary focus of traditional pharmacological use. Each individual rhizome typically grows horizontally beneath the soil surface, forming a storage organ for nutrients. Rising from these underground structures is the stem, which serves as the central axis supporting the plant's upward growth. Finally, the leaf arrangement along the stem is essential for photosynthesis, providing the energy necessary for the development of both the foliage and the medicinal Rhizo-mes.
