Corydalis clarkei

Corydalis clarkei, commonly known as Clarke's Corydalis, is a perennial herb valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Native to East Asia, its tubers contain potent alkaloids used to alleviate pain and soothe the nervous system. This medicinal plant serves as a vital resource for treating various inflammatory and neurological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis clarkei
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is further situated in the family Papaveraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Corydalis.
