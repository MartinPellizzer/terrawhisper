Decalepis hamiltonii

Decalepis hamiltonii, commonly known as the climbing milkweed, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to African landscapes. Renowned for its bioactive properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and therapeutic applications.
