Clerodendrum volubile

Clerodendrum volubiile, commonly known as the climbing clerodendrum, is a vigorous woody climber celebrated in traditional medicine. Featuring fragrant, tubular flowers, this botanical treasure is prized for its diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Clerodendrum volubile
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further situated in the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Clerodendrum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its presence is also documented within the borders of Ghana. Furthermore, the species is known to inhabit parts of Guinea and the Ivory Coast. Finally, its geographical range extends into Liberia as well.
