Lonicera macrantha

Lonicera macrantha, commonly known as the large-flowered honeysuckle, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Rich in bioactive compounds, it possesses significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Often used in herbal remedies, this species plays a vital role in treating various ailments and supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lonicera macrantha
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Lonicera macrantha, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Dipsacales. Finally, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae and the genus Lonicera.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions within East and South Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast. Its range also extends into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Furthermore, the species is native to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia.
