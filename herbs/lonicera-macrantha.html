Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Lonicera macrantha, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Dipsacales. Finally, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae and the genus Lonicera.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions within East and South Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast. Its range also extends into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Furthermore, the species is native to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Finally, its geographical presence reaches the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam.

Plant Parts

This plant, Lonicera macrantha, possesses various parts that are utilized in traditional medicinal practices. The delicate flowers are often the most visually striking feature, emerging from distinct flower buds that develop along the woody stems. While a single flower may provide specific aromatic properties, the medicinal potency is frequently concentrated in the dried flower buds, which are harvested and preserved to be used in various herbal preparations and infusions.
