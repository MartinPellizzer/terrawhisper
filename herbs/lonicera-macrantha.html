What's the taxonomical classification of Lonicera macrantha?

Lonicera macrantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Dipsacales and is a member of the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, its specific biological identification is determined by its genus, Lonicera, and its species designation, macrantha.
