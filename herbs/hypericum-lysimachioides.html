Hypericum lysimachioides

Hypericum lysimachioides, commonly known as the creeping St. John's wort, is a resilient perennial herb native to various regions. Belonging to the Hypericaceae family, this plant is distinguished by its small, yellow flowers and spreading habit. It holds significant interest in ethnobotany for its diverse potential medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum lysimachioides
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
