Scirpus wichurae

What's the taxonomical classification of Scirpus wichurae?

Scirpus wichurae belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Poales, the plant falls under the family Cyperaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its assignment to the genus Scirpus, specifically within the species wichurae.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Scirpus wichurae has triangular stems that are typically compressed and reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters. The leaves are reduced to basal sheaths, while the culms are slender and smooth. Its inflorescence consists of a compact terminal cluster of small, brownish spikelets. Each spikelet contains multiple individual flowers protected by scale-like bracts. The plant's root system consists of creeping rhizomes that allow it to spread through wetland substrates.
