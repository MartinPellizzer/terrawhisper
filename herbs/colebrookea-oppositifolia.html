Colebrookea oppositifolia

Indian squirrel tail

Colebrookea oppositifolia, commonly known as the Colebrooke plant, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various African regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Colebrookea oppositifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Colebrookea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found in India, Nepal, and Bangladesh. Its presence also extends into the East Himalaya region of the subcontinent. Furthermore, the species is located in the South-Central part of China.
