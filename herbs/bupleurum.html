Bupleurum

Bupleurum, commonly known as Chai Hu in traditional Chinese medicine, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its ability to soothe the liver and regulate qi, it is frequently utilized to relieve stress, reduce inflammation, and support overall immune function and metabolic balance.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, the medicinal species is categorized under the genus Bupleurum.
