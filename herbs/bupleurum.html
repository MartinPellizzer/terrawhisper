Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly in East Asian practices, its roots are utilized to support liver health, reduce inflammation, and relieve stress. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Chai Hu, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices for its therapeutic properties. While the entire herb can sometimes be utilized, the primary medicinal component is the bupleurum root, which is harvested and processed for its bioactive compounds. Specifically, the dried roots are used to create decoctions and extracts aimed at regulating liver function and relieving stagnation.
