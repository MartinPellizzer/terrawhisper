hare's-ear, scientifically known as Bupleurum, is a versatile genus of flowering plants within the Apiaceae family. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly in East Asian practices, its roots are utilized to support liver health, reduce inflammation, and relieve stress. This resilient herb offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness.
