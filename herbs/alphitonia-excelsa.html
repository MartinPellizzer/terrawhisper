Soap Tree, scientifically known as Alphitonia excelsa, is a versatile evergreen tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its saponin-rich bark, it has long been used for traditional cleansing. Beyond its soap-like properties, this medicinal plant is valued in various cultures for treating skin ailments and digestive issues.
