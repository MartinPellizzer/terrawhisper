Zanthoxylum limoncello

Zanthoxylum limoncellum, commonly known as the Lemon Bark tree, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its potent therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues, offering a natural approach to holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum limoncello
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Zanthoxylum, specifically the species Zanthoxylum limonella.
