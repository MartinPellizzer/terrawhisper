Trichilia catigua

Trichilia catiagua, commonly known as Catiguá, is a medicinal tree native to South American forests. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly alkaloids and triterpenes, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This botanical powerhouse offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Trichilia catigua
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Trichilia.
