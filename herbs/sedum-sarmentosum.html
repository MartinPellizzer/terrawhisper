Sedum sarmentosum

Sedum sarmentosum, commonly known as stonecrop, is a resilient perennial valued for its succulent leaves and star-shaped flowers. Native to rocky terrains, it thrives in well-drained soils. Beyond its ornamental appeal in rock gardens, this plant is traditionally studied for its potential medicinal properties and drought-resistant capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Sedum sarmentosum
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Saxifragales and the family Crassulaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Sedum.
