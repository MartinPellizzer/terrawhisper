Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions in Northern Asia. Within Siberia, it can be found growing in the areas of Buryatiya and Chita. Its range also extends into the Russian Far East, covering the Amur region. Furthermore, the species is documented in the Khabarovsk territory. Finally, its presence is noted in the Primorye region, completing its known distribution.
