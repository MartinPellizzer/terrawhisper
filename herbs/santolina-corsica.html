Corsican lavender-cotton, scientifically known as Santolina corsica, is a charming evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its silvery-grey foliage and bright yellow blooms, this aromatic plant thrives in sunny, rocky soils. It is widely cherished in landscaping for its drought tolerance and unique, fragrant texture.

This plant is primarily found within the specific regions of Southwestern Europe. Its native range is quite restricted to a few Mediterranean islands.
