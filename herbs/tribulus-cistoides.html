Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct African regions. In the Macaronesian islands, it can be found specifically in Cape Verde. Its range extends into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Togo. Furthermore, the species is prevalent across Northeast Tropical Africa, spanning from Eritrea to Ethiopia. Finally, its presence is also confirmed within the borders of Somalia.
