Dryopteris barbigera belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following its placement in the order Polypodiales, the plant is assigned to the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is defined by the genus Dryopteris and the species barbigera.
