Stephanotis macrantha

Stephanotis macrantha, commonly known as Madagascar jasmine, is a fragrant, climbing evergreen shrub cherished for its waxy, white blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in floral arrangements, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and as a natural remedy for calming nerves.

  • Scientific name: Stephanotis macrantha
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Gentianaales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Stephanotis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across several regions of the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, its distribution includes the countries of Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern regions, it can also be located within South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of Angola and Malawi. Finally, its geographical range extends further into Mozambique.
