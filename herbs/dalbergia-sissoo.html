Indian rosewood, scientifically known as Dalbergia sissoo, is a versatile deciduous tree native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its high-quality timber, it also holds significant medicinal value. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections and inflammation to digestive issues and respiratory problems.
