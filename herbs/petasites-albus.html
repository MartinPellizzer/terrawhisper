Petasites albus

Petasites albus, commonly known as White Butterbur, is a perennial medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned primarily for its ability to alleviate migraines and seasonal allergies, it contains bioactive compounds that reduce inflammation. This plant plays a significant role in traditional herbal medicine and modern phytotherapy.

  • Scientific name: Petasites albus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae within the genus Petasites.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Petasites albus, is recognized by various regional names across Europe. In French, it is referred to as Pétasite blanc, while in Dutch, it is called Wit hoefblad. It is known as devětsil bílý in Czech, vitksråp in Swedish, and is called Weiße Pestwurz in German.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within Northern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Denmark and Norway. Its presence extends to the islands of the Faroe Islands, known locally as Føroyaar. Additionally, the species is established throughout Great Britain. Finally, its geographical range includes the lush terrain of Ireland.

Plant Parts

This plant, Petasites albus, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts. These above-ground structures include the large, heart-shaped leaves and the flowering stalks that emerge from the ground. When preparing herbal remedies, the aerial parts are often collected to harness the specific bioactive compounds contained within the foliage and blooms, which are used to address various therapeutic purposes.
