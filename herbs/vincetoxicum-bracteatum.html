Vincetoxicum bracteatum

Vincetoxicum bracteatum, commonly known as the vine of death, is a climbing medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its complex alkaloids, it is frequently studied for its potential pharmacological properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities, offering significant promise for future therapeutic developments in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Vincetoxicum bracteatum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Vincetoxicum bracteatum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae and the genus Vincetoxicum.
