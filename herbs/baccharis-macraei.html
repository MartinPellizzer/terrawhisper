Baccharis macraei

Baccharis macraei, commonly known as the Macrae's Baccharis, is a resilient shrub native to specific Australian landscapes. While less studied than other medicinal flora, it holds significant ethnobotanical potential. Researchers are increasingly investigating its bioactive compounds for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering promising avenues for future pharmacological developments.
