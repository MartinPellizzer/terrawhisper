Malus delavayi

Malus delavayi, commonly known as Delavay's apple, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to high-altitude regions of China. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional practices to support respiratory health and promote cellular vitality, offering a unique botanical resource for natural healing and wellness.

  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
