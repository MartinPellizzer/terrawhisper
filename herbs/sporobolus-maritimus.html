Sporobolus maritimus

Sporobolus maritimus, commonly known as sea heath, is a resilient coastal shrub native to sandy shorelines. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this maritime plant possesses unique bioactive properties. Its ability to thrive in saline environments makes it a fascinating subject for studying natural compounds with potential therapeutic and medicinal applications.
