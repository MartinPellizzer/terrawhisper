Phlomis lychnitis

Phlomis lychnitis, commonly known as Jerusalem sage, is a striking perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its architectural, velvety leaves and dramatic yellow flower spikes, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used in traditional remedies, it offers unique bioactive properties within the Lamiaceae family.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis lychnitis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed within the genus Phlomis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Phlomis lychnitis, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In French, it is called Phlomis lychnite, while in Catalan, it is known as salvió blener. Its names in Arabic include أذينة لزجة and the Sinatic variation اذينه لزجه. Finally, in Spanish, it is affectionately called Orejita de liebre.
