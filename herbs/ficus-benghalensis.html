Deeply rooted in Ayurvedic traditions, its leaves, bark, and sap are utilized to treat diverse ailments, including skin disorders, inflammation, and digestive issues, making it a cornerstone of traditional herbal healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ficus benghalensis, commonly known as the Banyan tree, possesses various parts that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaves are frequently used in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as skin inflammation and digestive issues. A distinctive feature of the species is its milky latex, which is often applied topically to soothe wounds or manage certain skin conditions. The bark is highly valued for its medicinal potency, often prepared as a decoction to treat dysentery, diarrhea, and even various forms of inflammation. Furthermore, the sturdy stem provides the structural foundation for its expansive canopy and serves as the vascular network that transports essential nutrients throughout the entire medicinal organism.
