Umbellularia californica

Umbellularia californica, commonly known as California bay laurel, is a versatile evergreen tree native to the West Coast. Renowned for its aromatic, pungent leaves, it serves as a potent culinary spice and traditional medicine. Its essential oils offer antimicrobial properties, making it a significant plant in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Umbellularia californica
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Umbellularia.
