Dodonaea lanceolata

Dodonaea lanceolata, commonly known as the lance-leaf hopbush, is a resilient shrub native to various regions of Australia. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. It is often utilized for its potential therapeutic benefits, particularly in treating skin ailments and addressing various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Family: Sapindaceae
