Dracontium spruceanum

Dracocontiun spruceanum, commonly known as Spruce Dragon's Breath, is a rare medicinal herb found in high-altitude montane forests. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this plant is traditionally used to alleviate respiratory ailments and reduce systemic inflammation, offering a unique botanical remedy for modern holistic wellness practitioners worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Dracontium spruceanum
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Dracontium spruceanum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Alismatales. Finally, it is a member of the family Araceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Dracontium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions of the Americas. In Central America, its presence is documented within the countries of Costa Rica and Panamá. Moving into Northern South America, the species can be found growing in Suriname and Venezuela. Additionally, its range extends into Western South America, specifically within the borders of Colombia. This diverse range highlights the plant's ability to thrive across various tropical territories.
