Elaeocarpus

Mendong tree

Elaeocarpus, commonly known as the Marang or Marang tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin conditions to digestive issues, showcasing its profound botanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Elaeocarpus
  • Family: Elaeocarpaceae
  • Native range: Africa
