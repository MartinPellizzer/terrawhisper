Albizia procera

Albizia procera, commonly known as the White Siris, is a fast-growing, deciduous tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its lush canopy and delicate flowers, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. Its leaves, bark, and roots are frequently utilized for their diverse therapeutic and healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Albizia procera
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Albizia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several diverse regions across the African continent.
