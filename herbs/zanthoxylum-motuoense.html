Zanthoxylum motuoense

Zanthoxylum motuoense, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in traditional practices, holds immense pharmacological potential. Belonging to the Rutaceae family, this species is valued for its bioactive compounds. Research focuses on its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties, highlighting its importance in developing novel natural therapeutic agents.
