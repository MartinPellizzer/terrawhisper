Asclepias

Milkweed

Asclepia, commonly known as milkweed, is a versatile genus of flowering plants celebrated for their unique nectar-rich blooms. Beyond their striking aesthetics, these plants hold significant medicinal value in traditional practices and serve as vital host plants for monarch butterflies, playing a crucial role in maintaining ecological balance.

  • Scientific name: Asclepias
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Gentianaales and the family Apocynaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by its genus, Asclepias.
