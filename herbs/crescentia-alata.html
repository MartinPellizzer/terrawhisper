Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the calabash tree, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties, which are primarily derived from the pulp of the fruits. This fleshy interior is frequently harvested and processed into syrups, infusions, or topical applications to treat various ailments such as respiratory issues, digestive disturbances, and inflammatory conditions. The bioactive compounds concentrated within the pulp make it the most significant medicinal component of the species.
