What's the taxonomical classification of Cirsium lidjiangense?

Cirsium lidjiangense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales. This plant is further categorized into the family Asteraceae, which is a large group of flowering plants, and is placed within the genus Cirsium. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is defined by the species epithet lidjiangense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cirsium lidjiangense has lanceolate to oblong leaves with coarsely serrated margins, a robust stem often covered in fine hairs, and flower heads composed of numerous pink to purplish tubular florets surrounded by involucral bracts. The foliage typically exhibits a distinct midrib and may possess small spines along the edges of the lobes. Its flowering stalks can reach significant heights, supporting several distinct capitula arranged in a corymb-like structure. The involucres are composed of multiple series of bracts that protect the developing seeds. These morphological traits are characteristic of the thistle family found in its high-altitude habitat.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

The distribution is restricted to these specific montane ecosystems which provide the necessary cool and moist climate.
