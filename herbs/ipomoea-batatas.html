Ipomoea batatas

Ipomoea batatas, commonly known as the sweet potato, is more than just a dietary staple. Beyond its nutritional value, this versatile plant possesses significant medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, boost immunity, and combat inflammation in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea batatas
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Ipomoea.

Distribution

This plant, Ipomoea batatas, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions. Within Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in both Portugal and Spain.
