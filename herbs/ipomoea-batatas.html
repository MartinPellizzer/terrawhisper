Reproduction

The reproduction of Ipomoea batatas involves both sexual and asexual processes, with asexual reproduction being notably present. The flowering cycle typically begins in May and extends through September, producing characteristic violet flowers. These flowers vary in size, with lengths ranging from 0.3 cm to 7 cm and widths spanning between 3 cm and 5 cm. Following pollination, the plant develops a capsule-type fruit that measures between 0.5 cm and 0.8 cm in length. Each fruit is dehiscent and contains a seed count typically ranging from 1 to 10 seeds. The dispersal of these seeds is characterized by an unspecialized syndrome, though it also exhibits anthropochorous patterns, indicating involvement by human activity.
