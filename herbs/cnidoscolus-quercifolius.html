Cnidoscolus quercifolius

Cnidoscolus quercifolius, commonly known as Chaya, is a nutrient-dense perennial shrub native to Mesoamerica. Revered in traditional medicine, this "spinach of the Maya" offers significant health benefits, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. When prepared correctly, it serves as a potent dietary staple rich in essential vitamins and minerals.

  • Scientific name: Cnidoscolus quercifolius
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
