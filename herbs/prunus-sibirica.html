Siberian apricot, scientifically known as Prunus sibirica, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its edible, nutrient-dense fruits, it offers significant medicinal potential. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, this hardy plant is traditionally utilized to support digestive health and bolster the body's immune system.
