Vernonanthura polyanthes

Vernonanthura polyanthes, commonly known as "erva-de-passarinho" in Brazil, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South American landscapes. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, respiratory ailments, and skin infections, offering significant therapeutic potential in ethnopharmacological studies.
