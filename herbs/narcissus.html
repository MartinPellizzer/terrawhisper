Daffodil, scientifically known as Narcissus, is a versatile perennial celebrated for its striking blooms and historical medicinal uses. While primarily ornamental, various species offer bioactive compounds with potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. However, caution is essential, as many parts contain toxic alkaloids that require expert handling and knowledge.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across various regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the central part of the continent, the species is also established in Middle Europe. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of Austria and Belgium. Finally, its geographical range extends through the regions of Czechia and Slovakia.
