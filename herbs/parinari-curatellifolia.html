Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence has been documented across several diverse nations, including Benin and Burkina. Furthermore, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Gambia and Ghana. The species also extends its reach into the territory of Guinea-Bissau. Collectively, these locations define the primary environmental range for Parinarum curatellifolia.
