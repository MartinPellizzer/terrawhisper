Parinari curatellifolia

Parinari curatellifolia, commonly known as a versatile medicinal herb, holds significant importance in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from skin conditions to digestive issues. Exploring its phytochemical profile offers promising insights into modern pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Parinari curatellifolia
  • Family: Chrysobalanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Common Names

This plant, Parinaricuriatellifolia, is known by various regional names depending on the language and location. In Rwanda, it is referred to as Umunazi, while in Afrikaans, it is called Grysappel. It is also recognized as Mpundu in Chichewa and known as luumumobola in Finnish. Additionally, in Chinese, the plant is commonly called 懷春李.
