Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Middle Europe, its presence is specifically noted within Germany. Moving eastward, the species can also be found throughout Ukraine in Eastern Europe. Its range extends significantly into the Russian Far East, where it inhabits the Kuril Islands and the Sakhalin region.
