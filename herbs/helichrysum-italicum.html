Helichrysum italicum

Curry plant

Helichrysum italicum, commonly known as Immortelle or Curry Plant, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub prized in herbal medicine. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it possesses remarkable anti-inflammatory and regenerative properties. Often used in skincare, this golden-flowered botanical promotes healing, revitalizes tissue, and supports skin health effectively.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum italicum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Helichrysum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Helichrysum italicum, is referred to by various names across different languages and cultures. In Corsica, it is called Maredda (co), while in Germany, it is commonly known as Italienische Strohblume (de). In other regions, its names often reflect its aromatic qualities, such as カレープラント (ja) in Japanese, Karryplante (da) in Danish, and Kerrieplant (nl) in Dutch.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be noted within Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found growing in Germany. The distribution extends significantly into Southwestern Europe, where it inhabits the Baleares. Additionally, this medicinal plant is commonly located throughout France and Corse.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Immortelle, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its aerial parts. The essential oils and therapeutic compounds are concentrated within the small, narrow leaves and the vibrant, everlasting flowers. These flowers are particularly notable for their ability to retain their color and shape even after being dried, making the entire above-ground portion of the plant highly useful in various herbal preparations.
