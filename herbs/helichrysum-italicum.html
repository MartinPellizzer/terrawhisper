Curry plant, scientifically known as Helicrysum italicum, is a fragrant Mediterranean perennial prized for its aromatic foliage. Beyond its culinary uses, this resilient shrub offers significant medicinal potential. Rich in essential oils and flavonoids, it is traditionally employed in herbal remedies to support skin health and soothe inflammatory conditions.
