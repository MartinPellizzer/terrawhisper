Mexican yew, scientifically known as Taxus globosa, is a distinctive evergreen conifer native to Mexico's mountainous regions. Renowned for its dense foliage and ornamental appeal, this species holds significant ethnobotanical interest. It is studied for its potential medicinal properties, particularly regarding the bioactive alkaloids found within its tissues.
