Senegalia nigrescens

Senegalia nigrescens, commonly known as the Black Wattle, is a versatile medicinal tree native to African savannas. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, showcasing its significant importance in ethnobotanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Senegalia nigrescens
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the Fabaceae family within the genus Senegalia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Tanzania. Moving toward the southern regions, the species is well-established in the country of Angola. It is also commonly documented throughout South Tropical Africa in Malawi and Mozambique. Finally, its presence extends further into the southern reaches of the continent within Zambia.
