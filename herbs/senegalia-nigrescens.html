Knobthorn, scientifically known as Senegalia nigrescens, is a distinctive, slow-growing tree native to African savannas. Renowned for its rugged, swollen thorns, this resilient species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.
