Geranium wilfordii

Geranium wilfordii, commonly known as Wilford's geranium, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Rich in bioactive compounds like geraniin, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This plant is increasingly studied for its potential in managing metabolic disorders and protecting cells against oxidative stress.

  • Scientific name: Geranium wilfordii
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae, it is classified under the genus Geranium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Middle Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Poland. Moving eastward, it can be found in the Russian Far East within the Amur and Primorye territories. The species also occupies significant portions of China, including the South-Central region. Additionally, its range extends northward into the Inner Mongolia area of China.
