Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as okra, offers various therapeutic benefits through its diverse components. The mucilaginous fruits are widely consumed and utilized for their nutritional and soothing properties, while the leaves serve as a traditional remedy in many cultures. Specifically, the young leaves are often prized for their higher concentration of bioactive compounds and are frequently used in dietary preparations to support health. Furthermore, a concentrated extract derived from different parts of the plant can be prepared to harness its medicinal potency for targeted therapeutic applications.
