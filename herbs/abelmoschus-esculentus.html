Abelmoschus esculentus

Abelmoschus esculentus, commonly known as okra or lady's finger, is a versatile flowering plant valued globally for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Beyond its culinary uses, it is prized in traditional medicine for supporting digestive health, managing blood sugar levels, and providing antioxidant benefits through its nutrient-rich mucilaginous pods.

  • Scientific name: Abelmoschus esculentus
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Abelmoschus esculentus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Abelmoschus.
