Lolium multiflorum

Italian rye grass

Lolium multiflorum, commonly known as Italian ryegrass, is a versatile annual grass widely utilized in forage production. While primarily valued in agriculture for livestock nutrition, it holds traditional significance in various medicinal practices. This resilient plant offers unique botanical properties that warrant exploration within the realm of natural therapeutics.

  • Scientific name: Lolium multiflorum
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Lolium multiflorum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Poales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Poaceae and falls under the genus Lolium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lolium multiflorum, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic region. In Simplified Chinese, it is called 意大利黑麦草 (zh-hans) and is also known as 意大利黑麦草 in mainland China (zh-cn) and Singapore (zh-sg). In Traditional Chinese, the name is written as 意大利黑麥草 (zh-hant), which is the same nomenclature used in Hong Kong (zh-hk).

Distribution

This plant is widely recognized for its presence across various regions of Northern Europe. Specifically, its distribution includes the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. It can also be found growing naturally throughout the landscapes of Finland. Furthermore, the species is well-established within the territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, its geographical reach extends further north into the rugged terrain of Norway.
