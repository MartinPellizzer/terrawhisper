Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Laurales, falling into the family Lauraceae. Finally, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Cinnamomum (often associated with the specific epithet parthenoxylon).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast territories. Additionally, its presence extends to the tropical island of Hainan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in the region of Assam. Finally, its range reaches the high-altitude areas of the East Himalaya.
