Mongolian Wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia mongolica, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties. Used to treat various ailments, this aromatic plant offers valuable therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and holistic wellness practices.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the vast expanse of Siberia. It can be found growing in the mountainous terrains of the Altai region. Additionally, the species is prevalent in the Republic of Buryatia. Its presence extends further into the Chitа area as well. Finally, the plant is commonly documented throughout the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions.
