Artemisia mongolica

Artemisia mongolica, commonly known as Mongolian wormwood, is a perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Native to the steppes of Central Asia, this aromatic plant is valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds, which exhibit significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties used to treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia mongolica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Artemisia, specifically as the species Artemisia mongolica.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the vast expanse of Siberia. It can be found growing in the mountainous terrains of the Altai region. Additionally, the species is prevalent in the Republic of Buryatia. Its presence extends further into the Chitа area as well. Finally, the plant is commonly documented throughout the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions.
