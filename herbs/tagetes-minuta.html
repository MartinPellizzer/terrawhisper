Tagetes minuta

Southern cone marigold

Tagetes minuta, commonly known as Wild Marigold or Minuta, is a versatile medicinal herb native to South America. Renowned for its potent essential oils, this aromatic plant offers significant antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat digestive issues and skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Tagetes minuta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Tagetes.
