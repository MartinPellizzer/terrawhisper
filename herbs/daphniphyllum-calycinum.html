Daphniphyllum calycinum

Daphniphyllum calycinum, commonly known as the "golden leaf" in certain traditional practices, is a significant medicinal shrub native to East Asian forests. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, promote circulation, and support overall vitality through its diverse pharmacological properties.

  • Scientific name: Daphniphyllum calycinum
  • Family: Daphniphyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Saxifragales and sits in the family Daphniphyllaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Daphniphyllum.

Common Names

This plant, Daphniphyllum calycinum, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic context and region. In Chinese, it is frequently referred to as 牛耳枫 (niú ěr fēng) or 南岭虎皮楠 (nán lǐng hǔ pí nán). Specifically, in Mainland China, the name used is 牛耳枫 (zh-cn), while in Taiwan, it is identified as 牛耳楓 (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across parts of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it is primarily located in the southeastern regions of the country. Its distribution also extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Vietnam. These combined areas represent the known natural habitat for this medicinal species.
