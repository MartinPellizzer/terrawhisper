Ajania fastigiata

Ajania fastigiata, commonly known as Tansy chrysanthemum, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional Eastern medicine. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it possesses potent antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, this resilient perennial offers significant therapeutic potential in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ajania fastigiata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Ajania.
