Leea glabra

What's the taxonomical classification of Leea glabra?

Leea glabra belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant is placed in the order Vitales and is a member of the family Vitaeaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Leea and the species glabra.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Leea glabra has opposite, simple, elliptic to oblong leaves that are glabrous, measuring roughly 5 to 15 centimeters in length with entire margins and acute apices. The stems are woody and typically display a reddish or brownish hue depending on the age of the growth. Small, fragrant white flowers are arranged in terminal cymes, which emerge from the leaf axils or the branch tips. Following pollination, the plant produces small, fleshy drupes that transition from green to a bright red or black color when ripe. These morphological features are characteristic of its shrubby growth habit within its tropical forest habitat.
