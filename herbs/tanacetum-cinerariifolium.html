Tanacetum cinerariifolium

Tanacetum cinerariifolium, commonly known as Tansy leaf, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its potent therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils, it has been traditionally utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits. Understanding its chemical composition is vital for exploring its diverse applications in herbal pharmacology.

  • Family: Asteraceae

Taxonomical Classification
