Pyrethrum, scientifically known as Tanacetum cinerariifolium, is a remarkable medicinal plant renowned for its potent insecticidal properties. Derived from its dried flower heads, the plant contains natural pyrethrins used widely in organic pest control. Beyond agriculture, it holds significant value in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic applications.
