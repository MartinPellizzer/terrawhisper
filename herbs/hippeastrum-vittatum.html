Hippeastrum vittatum

Hippeastrum vittatum, commonly known as the striped amaryllis, is a striking perennial valued for its vibrant, banded blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant traditional medicinal importance. It is frequently utilized in various cultures for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Hippeastrum vittatum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hippeastrum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the globe. In the Southwestern Pacific, its presence can be observed in Niue. The Caribbean region also hosts the species, specifically within the Dominican Republic, Haiti, and the Venezuelan Antilles. Additionally, its range extends to Western South America, where it is found in Bolivia. Together, these locations highlight the broad environmental adaptability of this medicinal plant.
