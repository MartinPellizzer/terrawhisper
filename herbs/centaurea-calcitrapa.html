Centaurea calcitrapa

Purple star thistle

Centaurea calcitrapa, commonly known as the limestone knapweed, is a resilient perennial herb native to Mediterranean rocky habitats. Characterized by its striking purple composite flowers, this botanical species holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Its unique phytochemical profile offers potential therapeutic properties, making it a subject of study in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Centaurea calcitrapa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement identifies it under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Centaurea.
