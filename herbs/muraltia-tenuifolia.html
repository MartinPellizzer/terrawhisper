Muraltia tenuifolia

Muraltia tenuifolia, commonly known as the False Ash, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Often found in tropical regions, its leaves and bark possess significant therapeutic properties, frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, making it a valuable resource in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Africa
