Gaultheria sinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Gaultheria sinensis?

Gaultheria sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the hierarchical structure of biological classification, it falls under the order Ericales and is a member of the family Ericaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Gaultheria, and its specific species epithet, sinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Gaultheria sinensis has evergreen, woody leaves that are elliptical or lanceolate in shape, featuring serrated margins and a leathery texture. The plant typically grows as a spreading shrub with dark green foliage that remains vibrant throughout the year. Small, bell-shaped white or pinkish flowers emerge from the leaf axils during the blooming season. These blossoms eventually give way to small, fleshy, berry-like fruits that are often red or dark in color. The overall structure of the plant is compact, making it well-suited for dense ground cover or ornamental hedging.

What side effects this plant can have?
