Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central and Southeast provinces, as well as within the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically documented in the region of Assam. Additionally, the species is known to inhabit parts of Bangladesh. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environments where this medicinal plant thrives.
