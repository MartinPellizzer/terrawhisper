Phyllanthus orbiculatus

Phyllanthus orbiculatus, commonly known as the orbicular phyllanthus, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Characterized by its unique rounded leaves, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is frequently utilized for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, playing a vital role in various herbal healing formulations.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus orbiculatus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Malpighiales and is a member of the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.

Common Names

This plant, Phyllanthus orbiculatus, is known by various names across different regions and languages. In English, it is commonly referred to as the orbicular phyllanthus, while in Chinese, it is known as qián bái chě (前白车). In Japanese, the plant is called kōrai-shiki (高来式), and in Vietnamese, it is known as cây diệp hạ châu.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions of the Americas. In the Caribbean, it can be found within the islands of Trinidad and Tobago. Its presence extends into Northern South America, where it inhabits French Guiana, Suriname, and Venezuela. Furthermore, the species reaches into Western South America, specifically documented in Bolivia. This broad range highlights the plant's ability to thrive in diverse tropical environments.
