Viscum cruciatum

Red-berry mistletoe

Viscum cruciatum, commonly known as the Cruciform Mistletoe, is a parasitic medicinal plant found in various subtropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating hypertension, inflammation, and neurological disorders. This plant offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Viscum cruciatum
  • Family: Santalaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Viscum cruciatum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Santalales. Finally, it is a member of the family Santalaceae and falls under the genus Viscum.
