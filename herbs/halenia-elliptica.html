Spurred gentian, scientifically known as Halenia ellipticia, is a rare, perennial herb native to the coastal wetlands of the southeastern United States. Distinguished by its unique, spurred flowers and elliptical leaves, this botanical treasure holds significant value in traditional herbalism and remains a subject of interest for biodiversity conservation.
