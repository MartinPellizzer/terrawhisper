Artemisia caruifolia

Artemisia caruifolia, commonly known as Caruiol's wormwood, is a perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Found primarily in Mediterranean ecosystems, this medicinal plant is valued for its aromatic properties. It contains diverse bioactive compounds, offering significant potential in traditional herbal remedies and modern pharmacological research for various therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia caruifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Asteraceae within the genus Artemisia.

This plant, known scientifically as Artemisia caruifolia, is referred to by various common names across different linguistic contexts. In Chinese, it is known as 青蒿, a name used in zh, zh-cn, and zh-tw; it is also identified as 黑蒿 and 白染艮.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central territories to the North-Central areas. Its presence is also noted in the Manchuria region of China. Additionally, the species extends into the China Southeast part of the country. Beyond mainland China, its geographical range includes Korea in Eastern Asia.
