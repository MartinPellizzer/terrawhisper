Terminalia dhofarica

Terminalia dhofarica, commonly known as the Dhofar terminalia, is a resilient tree native to the arid landscapes of the Arabian Peninsula. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its bark and resins offer potent therapeutic properties, often utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia dhofarica
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Terminalia.
