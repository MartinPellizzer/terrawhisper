What's the taxonomical classification of Liparis cespitosa?

Liparis cespitosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Asparagales. This plant is a distinguished representative of the family Orchidaceae, falling under the genus Liparis, and is specifically identified by the species epithet cespitosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Liparis cespitosa has a caespitose growth habit characterized by dense, tufted clusters of fleshy, lanceolate leaves that emerge from short, creeping rhizomes. Each leaf is typically bright green, somewhat succulent, and features parallel venation consistent with its orchid lineage. The plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that are often greenish or yellowish in hue, emerging from the base of the foliage on short stalks. These blooms possess a delicate structure with a distinctively shaped labellum and a small, fleshy column. The overall morphology is adapted for moist, shaded environments where its compact form helps retain moisture.
