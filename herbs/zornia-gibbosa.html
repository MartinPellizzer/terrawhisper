Zornia gibbosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Zornia gibbosa?

Zornia gibbosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is part of the order Fabales and is a member of the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Zornia and the species name gibbosia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Zornia gibbosa has a prostrate or ascending herbaceous habit characterized by slender, branched stems and small, ovate to lanceolate leaflets arranged in opposite pairs. The leaves are typically bright green with entire margins and a smooth texture. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in axillary clusters, often appearing in shades of yellow or white. The plant produces distinctive, slightly inflated or tuberculate legumes that are key to its identification. These seed pods are typically compressed and contain one to two small seeds.
