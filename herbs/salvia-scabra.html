Salvia scabra

Salvia scabra, commonly known as the rough sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to East Asian highlands. Characterized by its textured, serrated leaves and vibrant flowering spikes, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional practices for its diverse therapeutic properties, offering potential benefits for inflammation and digestive health.
