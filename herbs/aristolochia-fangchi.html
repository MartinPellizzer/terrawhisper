Aristolochia fangchi

Aristolochia fangchi, commonly known as fangji, is a traditional medicinal herb used in various herbal formulations. While historically utilized for its anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, modern science warns of its extreme nephrotoxicity and carcinogenicity due to aristolochic acid. Consequently, its use is strictly regulated or prohibited in many regions.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia fangchi
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Aristolochiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Aristolochia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within specific regions of East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central territories. It also maintains a significant distribution across the Southeast portion of the country. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it can be located within the borders of Vietnam.
