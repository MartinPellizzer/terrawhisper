Jatropha integerrima

Jatrophia integerima, commonly known as the "tree of life" in some regions, is a medicinal plant prized for its potent pharmacological properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and infections, offering significant potential for modern drug development and holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Jatropha integerrima
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Malpighiales and falls under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Jatropha.
