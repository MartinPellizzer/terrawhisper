Polyalthia obliqua

Polyalthia obliqua, commonly known as False Ashoka, is a versatile evergreen tree highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Native to South Asia, its leaves, bark, and fruits possess significant therapeutic properties, offering antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant benefits. It serves as a vital natural resource for diverse healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Polyalthia obliqua
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, it is specifically situated in the family Annonaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Polyalthia.
