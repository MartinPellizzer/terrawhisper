According to a study published by "Antioxidants (Basel, Switzerland)", the root of Chuanminshen violaceuam is used as an important edible and medicinal plant in China. While the leaves are generally considered byproducts, they contain phenolic compounds in methanolic extracts where chlorogenic acid ranged from 1.22 ± 0.03 to 2.87 ± 0.04 mg/g DW and rutin ranged from 2.25 ± 0.04 to 4.03 ± 0.05 mg/g DW. The results suggest the leaves of Chuanminshen violaceuam have potential to be developed as natural antioxidants.
