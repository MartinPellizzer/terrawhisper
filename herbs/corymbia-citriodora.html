Lemon eucalyptus

Corymbia citriodora · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (611 studies) · ★★★★★

Lemon eucalyptus, scientifically known as Corymbia citriodora, is a versatile Australian tree renowned for its invigorating citrus aroma. Highly valued in traditional and modern medicine, its essential oil possesses potent antimicrobial, insect repellent, and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a vital natural resource for topical treatments and aromatherapy applications.

Moving into West Tropical Africa, the species can be located in Burkina. Finally, its distribution continues through Gambia and Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the lemon-scented gum, possesses several parts that are utilized for their unique properties. The aromatic leaves are highly valued for their distinct citrus scent, which is primarily due to the high concentration of essential oil they contain. Furthermore, the tree produces a resinous substance known as kino, which can be extracted as a dark, reddish liquid or kino from trunks when the tree is wounded. Additionally, the bark of the Corymbia citriodorfa serves as a protective layer for the tree and is often studied in botanical contexts for its structural and chemical characteristics.
