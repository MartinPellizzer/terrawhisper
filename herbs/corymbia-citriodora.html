Corymbia citriodora

Corymbia citriodora, commonly known as the lemon-scented gum, is a magnificent Australian evergreen prized for its aromatic properties. Its essential oil, rich in citral, offers potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory benefits. Used in traditional and modern medicine, this fragrant tree serves as a vital resource for natural therapeutic healing.

  • Scientific name: Corymbia citriodora
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it is a member of the family Myrtaceae.
