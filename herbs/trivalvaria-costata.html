Trivalvaria costata

Trivalvaria costata, commonly known as the False Cinnamon, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the tropical forests of Asia. Renowned for its aromatic leaves and bark, it possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally, it is utilized in various herbal remedies to treat digestive ailments and skin infections.

  • Scientific name: Trivalvaria costata
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
