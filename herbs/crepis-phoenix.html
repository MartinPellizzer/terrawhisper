Crepis phoenix

What's the taxonomical classification of Crepis phoenix?

Crepis phoenix belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and is part of the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, which is further categorized into the genus Crepis, ultimately identifying it as the specific species Crepis phoenix.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Crepis phoenix has an erect, herbaceous stem that typically reaches heights between 10 and 40 centimeters, characterized by basal leaves that are often oblong or lanceolate and cauline leaves that are smaller and more sparsely arranged. The plant features a basal rosette of leaves with serrated or dentate margins that decrease in size as they ascend the stem. Its inflorescence consists of several yellow, ligulate flower heads arranged in a corymbiform cyme. Each flower head contains numerous individual florets, each possessing a distinct five-toothed corolla limb. The involucral bracts are arranged in multiple rows and provide protective coverage for the developing seeds.
