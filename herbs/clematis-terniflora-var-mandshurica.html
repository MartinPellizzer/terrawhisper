Clematis terniflora var. mandshurica

Clematis terniflora mandshurica

Clematis terniflora var. mandshurica, commonly known as the Manchurian Clematis, is a vigorous climbing vine prized for its fragrant, star-shaped white blooms. In traditional herbal practices, various parts of this plant are utilized for their bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in treating inflammation and various systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Clematis terniflora var. mandshurica
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Clematis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across Northeast Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented within the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Its distribution extends further south into various territories of China. Specifically, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Inner Mongolia. Additionally, the species is known to grow throughout the Manchuria region.
