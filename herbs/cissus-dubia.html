Cissus dubia

Cissus dubia, commonly known as "macaia" in Brazil, is a versatile medicinal climbing vine highly valued in traditional folk medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, it is frequently used to treat muscle aches, joint pain, and various inflammatory conditions, offering a natural approach to pain management.

  • Scientific name: Cissus dubia
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Vitales and the family Vitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cissus (noted as Cissus dubia).
