Artemisia mendozana

Artemisia mendozana, commonly known as a medicinal sage of the high Andes, is a resilient shrub valued in traditional folk medicine. Native to specific South American altitudes, this plant is prized for its aromatic properties and potential therapeutic applications, offering a unique glimpse into the botanical heritage of mountain ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia mendozana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling into the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artemisia and the specific species name mendozana.

Common Names

This plant, Artemisia mendozana, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is referred to as Mendoza's wormwood, while in Spanish, it is commonly called ajenjo de Mendoza. In Quechua, the plant is known as amancay.
