Bruguiera, scientifically known as Bruguiera, is a vital genus of mangrove trees essential to coastal ecosystems. Beyond ecological importance, various species offer significant medicinal potential, traditionally used for their diverse bioactive and therapeutic properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions in the African continent. In Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in both Djibouti and Somalia. Moving toward East Tropical Africa, the species can be found throughout Kenya and Tanzania. Furthermore, its range extends into South Tropical Africa, specifically within Mozambique. This pattern highlights a significant presence across both eastern and southern tropical zones.
