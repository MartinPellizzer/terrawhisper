Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales. Finally, it is a member of the family Musaceae within the genus Musa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct global regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence can be identified within the borders of Spain. Its range extends to the Macaronesia archipelago, specifically encompassing the Canary Islands. Within West Tropical Africa, the species is found in both Guinea and Senegal. Finally, its distribution reaches East Tropical Africa, where it is located in Tanzania.
