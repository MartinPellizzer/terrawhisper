Aeollanthus suaveolens

Aeollanthus suaveolens, commonly known as Sweet Basil or Wild Basil, is a versatile medicinal herb cherished in traditional healing. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant effects. It serves as a vital natural remedy for treating various respiratory and digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Aeollanthus suaveolens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Aeollanthus.

Common Names

This plant, Aeollanthus suaveolens, is known by various names across different linguistic and regional contexts. In English, it is frequently referred to as peppermint spike or aromatic aeollanthus, reflecting its distinctive scent. Within the Indian subcontinent, it is commonly called tulsi (specifically referring to the aromatic variety) in Hindi, while it is known as karu-valli in Tamil and kattu-tulsi in Telugu. In other local dialects, it may also be identified as amrit in Sanskrit, highlighting its traditional status in herbal medicine.

Distribution

This plant, Aeollanthus suaveolens, exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the countries of Benin, Ghana, and Nigeria. Its range also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Burundi. Additionally, the species is present within the borders of the Central African Republic. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various tropical environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, Aeollanthus suaveolens, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its aromatic properties, which are concentrated within its essential oil. This essential oil is extracted from the plant's volatile-rich foliage and serves as the main source of its therapeutic compounds. Due to the presence of this oil, the plant exhibits a strong, pleasant fragrance and possesses potent antimicrobial, antiseptic, and antispasmodic qualities that are utilized in various medicinal applications.
