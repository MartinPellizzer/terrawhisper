What's the taxonomical classification of Patrinia intermedia?

Patriina intermedia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic descent through the order Dipsacales, the plant is a member of the family Caprifoliaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Patrinia, with its specific identification being the species intermedia.
