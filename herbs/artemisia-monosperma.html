Artemisia monosperma

Artemisia monosperma, commonly known as single-seed wormwood, is a distinctive perennial herb native to arid regions. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, this plant is characterized by its aromatic foliage and unique reproductive structure. It holds significant ethnobotanical interest due to its potential therapeutic properties and ecological resilience.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia monosperma
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Artemisia, and its specific species name, Artemisia monosperma.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the regions of Northern Africa and Western Asia. In Northern Africa, Artemisia monosperma can be found growing in the territories of Egypt and Libya. Moving into Western Asia, the species is also documented throughout the Levant, specifically within Lebanon and Syria. Furthermore, its range extends to the Palestinian territories. Finally, the plant is known to inhabit the Sinai Peninsula, bridging the connection between these two major regions.
