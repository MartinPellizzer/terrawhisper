Andrographis echioides

Andrographis echioides, commonly known as the "False Andrographis," is a medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Belonging to the Acanthaceae family, it is valued in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds. Often used to address inflammatory conditions and respiratory ailments, this plant offers significant pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Andrographis echioides
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Andrographis.
