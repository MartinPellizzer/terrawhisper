Archidendron lucidum

Archidendroon lucidum, commonly known as the "shining archidendron," is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized to support immune function and metabolic health. This botanical treasure offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Archidendron lucidum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Archidendroon. Specifically, it is identified by the species name Archidendroon lucidum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout various regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. The species also extends its range to the island of Taiwan. Furthermore, it can be found within the Nansei-shoto islands of Eastern Asia. Collectively, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of Archidendron lucidum.
