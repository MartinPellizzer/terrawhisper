Chinese apes-earring, scientifically known as Archidendroon lucidum, is a highly valued medicinal plant in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its unique, ear-shaped fungi, it is prized for its ability to nourish the lungs, clear heat, and alleviate coughs. This potent herb remains a cornerstone of natural wellness practices.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout various regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. The species also extends its range to the island of Taiwan. Furthermore, it can be found within the Nansei-shoto islands of Eastern Asia. Collectively, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of Archidendron lucidum.
