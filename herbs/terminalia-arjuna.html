Terminalia arjuna

Terminalia arjuna, commonly known as Arjuna, is a majestic medicinal tree native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned in Ayurveda, its bark contains potent bioactive compounds used primarily to strengthen cardiac muscles and regulate blood pressure. This powerful botanical remains a cornerstone in traditional treatments for enhancing overall cardiovascular health.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia arjuna
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Combretaceae. Specifically, Terminalia arjuna is identified by the genus Terminalia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is commonly found in areas such as Assam and Bangladesh. Its presence also extends into East Asia, specifically throughout China Southeast. Moving toward the Western Indian Ocean, the species can be located on the island of Mauritius. Additionally, the plant is known to inhabit the island of Rodrigues within the same oceanic region.
