Bokbunja, scientifically known as Rubus coreanus, is a resilient Korean black raspberry prized for its deep crimson fruit. Renowned in traditional medicine, these nutrient-dense berries are rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and anthocyanins. Often used to support vitality and reproductive health, they offer a potent blend of wellness and flavor.

Plant Parts

This plant is highly valued for its medicinal and culinary properties, particularly through the use of the unripe Rubus coreanus (Korean black raspberry) fruit. While the fully ripened berries are widely consumed for their sweetness, the unripe stage of the fruit is often specifically targeted in traditional applications due to its distinct chemical composition. These immature berries are harvested to harness their potent bioactive compounds, which are believed to offer various health benefits before the fruit completes its maturation process.
