Colchicum szovitsii subsp. brachyphyllum

Colchicum szovitsii subsp. brachyphyllum, commonly known as a rare subspecies of autumn crocus, is a distinguished medicinal plant found in specific Asian habitats. Valued for its unique phytochemical profile, it contains bioactive alkaloids similar to colchicine, offering significant pharmacological potential in treating inflammatory conditions and various metabolic disorders.

  • Scientific name: Colchicum szovitsii subsp. brachyphyllum
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Liliales, it is a member of the family Colchicaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Colchicum as the subspecies brachyphyllum.

Distribution

This plant is geographically distributed across several key regions within Western Asia. Its presence can be documented throughout the territories of Lebanon and Syria. Furthermore, it inhabits specific areas within Palestine. The species also extends its range into the country of Türkiye. These locations collectively define the natural habitat for this particular subspecies.
