Picrasma quassioides

Picrasma quassioides, commonly known as Quassia, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its intense bitterness, it contains bioactive quassinoids used extensively in traditional medicine. These compounds offer potent antimicrobial, antimalarial, and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a significant subject in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Picrasma quassioides
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Simaroubaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Picrasma.
