Balloon flower, scientifically known as Platycodon grandiflorus, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Its roots contain bioactive compounds like platycodin D, known for soothing respiratory ailments, reducing inflammation, and expectorant properties. Widely used in East Asian remedies, this plant remains a vital natural resource for herbal healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Platycodon grandiflorus, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for its therapeutic properties found within the underground structures. The most significant part used is the roots, which are often harvested and processed for their medicinal potency. Specifically, the thick, fleshy root is dried and used to formulate various herbal remedies. While some medicinal plants rely on rhizoimes, in the case of this species, the primary focus remains on the specialized Roots that grow deep into the soil. Whether prepared as a whole Root or as a powdered extract, these parts serve as the foundation for its traditional applications.
