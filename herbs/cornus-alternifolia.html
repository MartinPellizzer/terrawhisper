Cornus alternifolia

Cornus alternifolia, commonly known as Pagoda Dog, is a distinctive deciduous shrub native to North American woodlands. Characterized by its unique horizontal branching pattern, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional practices. Its bark and berries offer diverse therapeutic potential, reflecting its significant role in botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cornus alternifolia
  • Family: Cornaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Cornus alternifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Cornales and the family Cornaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Cornus.
