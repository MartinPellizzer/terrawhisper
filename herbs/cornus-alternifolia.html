Distribution

This plant, known as Cornus alternifolia, exhibits a broad geographical range across several regions of Canada. In Western Canada, its presence can be found within the province of Manitoba. Moving toward the east, the species is widely distributed throughout Ontario. Its reach extends even further into the Atlantic provinces, including New Brunswick and Nova Scotia. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Pagoda Dog, is characterized by its distinctive foliage, as the leaves are arranged in an alternate pattern along the stems. These leaves are typically ovate or elliptical in shape, featuring smooth margins and a pointed tip, which contribute to the plant's lush appearance. In many medicinal contexts, the physiological properties of the leaves are studied, though they are most often recognized for their unique aesthetic role in the plant's structural growth.
