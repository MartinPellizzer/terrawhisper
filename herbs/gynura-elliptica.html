Gynura elliptica

Gynura ellipticia, commonly known as the Elliptic Gynura, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional wellness practices. Characterized by its distinct oval leaves, this plant offers diverse therapeutic potential. It is frequently studied for its bioactive compounds, which may support anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and various healing properties in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Gynura elliptica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Gynura.
