Viola arvensis

Field pansy

Viola arvensis, commonly known as field pansy, is a resilient annual herb native to Europe and parts of Asia. Characterized by its delicate violet flowers and small, notched leaves, this plant thrives in disturbed soils and grasslands, playing a subtle yet vital role in diverse meadow ecosystems worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Viola arvensis
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Viola arvensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, following the order Malpighiales. Finally, it resides in the family Violaceae under the genus Viola.
