Grewia bilamellata

Grewia bilobella, commonly known as the White Grewia, is a versatile medicinal plant native to various African regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its leaves, bark, and roots are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, offering significant pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Grewia bilamellata
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Malvales and falls under the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Grewia, specifically as the species Grewia bilamellata.
