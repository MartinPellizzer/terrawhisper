Polygala glomerata

What's the taxonomical classification of Polygala glomerata?

Polygala glomerata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Fabales and into the family Polygalaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Polygala, and its unique species designation, glomerata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Polygala glomerata has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by erect stems that reach heights of up to 30 centimeters and bear dense, terminal clusters of small, pinkish to purple flowers. The leaves are primarily basal and linear, while the cauline leaves are smaller, lanceolate, and arranged oppositely along the stem. Each flower features a distinctive keel petal that is fringed or slightly irregularly shaped, which is a hallmark of the genus. The inflorescence is highly compact, forming a head-like structure where individual flowers are crowded together. Small, inconspicuous bracts often subtend the flowering parts, adding to the complexity of the cluster.
