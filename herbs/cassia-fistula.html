golden shower

Cassia fistula · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (218 studies) · ★★★★★

Golden shower, scientifically known as Cassia fistula, is a striking medicinal tree celebrated for its drooping, bright yellow blossoms. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, its various parts—including bark, fruit, and flowers—offer potent therapeutic benefits, particularly as natural laxatives, anti-inflammatory agents, and treatments for skin conditions and digestive ailments.

Distribution
