Cassia fistula

Golden shower

Cassia fistula, commonly known as the Golden Shower tree, is a striking medicinal plant celebrated for its vibrant yellow blooms. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, its fruit pulp serves as a potent natural laxative. Beyond digestive health, its leaves and bark offer significant anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Cassia fistula
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Cassia fistula, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Cassia.

Common Names

This plant, Cassia fistula, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 阿勒勃 (zh-hans) or 腊肠树 (zh-cn), while in Traditional Chinese, it is called 阿勒勃 (zh-hant). Regionally, it is known as 豬腸豆 (zh-hk) in Hong Kong and 阿勃勒 (zh-tw) in Taiwan.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several distinct regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its range extends into the country of Egypt. It is also found within the Macaronesia region, specifically in Cape Verde. The distribution continues through West Tropical Africa, where it can be located in Gambia and Senegal. Finally, its presence is noted in West-Central Tropical Africa within Cameroon.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Golden Shower tree, possesses several parts that are utilized in various traditional medicinal practices. The stem bark is often employed for its therapeutic properties, while the leaves, including each individual leaf, are frequently used in decoctions to treat inflammatory conditions. Additionally, the twigs are sometimes harvested for their specific chemical constituents, and the seeds are highly valued for their potent laxative effects and role in treating various ailments.
