Java waterdropwort, scientifically known as Oenanthe javaica, is a perennial aquatic herb native to Southeast Asia. Traditionally utilized in various folk medicines, it is valued for its unique phytochemical properties. This plant thrives in marshy habitats and is often studied for its potential therapeutic applications in holistic wellness.
