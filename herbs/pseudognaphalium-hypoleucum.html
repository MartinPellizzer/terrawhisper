Pseudognaphalium hypoleucum

Pseudognaphalium hypoleucum, commonly known as the "false everlasting," is a resilient medicinal herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and respiratory issues, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Pseudognaphalium hypoleucum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and specifically resides in the genus Pseudognaphalium. As a member of this lineage, Pseudognaphalium hypoleucum shares the characteristic traits of its taxonomic group.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Western Asia, its presence is specifically noted within the country of Iran. Moving toward the East, it can be found in China, spanning both the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species also extends into Eastern Asia, where it is native to Japan. Finally, its range includes the Korean Peninsula, completing its presence across these diverse Asian landscapes.
