Lindernia micrantha

What's the taxonomical classification of Lindernia micrantha?

Lindernia micrantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales. This plant is a member of the family Linderniaceae and falls under the genus Lindernia, specifically identified by the species epithet micrantha.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lindernia micrantha has small, opposite, ovate to lanceolate leaves with entire margins and an acute apex that grow along slender, ascending stems. The plant features tiny, white to pale lavender flowers that are zygomorphic and emerge from the leaf axils. These blossoms possess a tubular corolla that flares into five distinct lobes, often displaying subtle markings on the lower lip. The reproductive structures include small stamens and a single style tucked within the floral tube. Tiny, globose capsules serve as the fruit, eventually releasing numerous minute seeds upon maturity.
