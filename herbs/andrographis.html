Andrographis

Andrographis, commonly known as King of Bitters, is a potent medicinal herb celebrated in Ayurvedic and traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its intense bitterness, it possesses powerful anti-inflammatory, antiviral, and immune-boosting properties. This versatile plant is widely used to support respiratory health and combat various systemic infections naturally.

  • Scientific name: Andrographis
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Lamiales, it is further categorized under the family Acanthaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is found within the genus Andrographis.
