Andrographis

Andrographis, commonly known as King of Bitters, is a potent medicinal herb celebrated in Ayurvedic and traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its intense bitterness, it possesses powerful anti-inflammatory, antiviral, and immune-boosting properties. This versatile plant is widely used to support respiratory health and combat various systemic infections naturally.

  • Scientific name: Andrographis
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Lamiales, it is further categorized under the family Acanthaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is found within the genus Andrographis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several specific regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found growing in the Assam region. Additionally, it is widely distributed throughout Bangladesh. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of the Andrographis species.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as king of bitters, is primarily valued for its therapeutic properties found within the aerial parts. These above-ground sections, which include the green stems, lanceolate leaves, and small white flowers, contain the concentrated bioactive compounds, such as andrographolides, necessary for its medicinal efficacy. When preparing herbal remedies, practitioners typically harvest these aerial parts to ensure the highest potency of the plant's healing constituents.
