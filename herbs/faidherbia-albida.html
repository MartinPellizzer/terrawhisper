Faidherbia albida

Faidherbiam albidia, commonly known as the white acacia, is a versatile medicinal plant native to African savannas. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its bark, leaves, and pods are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammation, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
