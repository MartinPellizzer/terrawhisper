Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Pinwheel Flower or Crape Jasmine, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties. The white, pinwheel-shaped flowers are often noted for their fragrance, while the dark green, glossy leaves are frequently used in traditional remedies. When the stem or leaf is damaged, the plant exudes a milky white latex, which is sometimes applied topically for its healing potential. Additionally, the root is recognized in various herbal practices for its specific therapeutic applications.
