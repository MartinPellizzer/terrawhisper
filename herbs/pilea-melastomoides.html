What's the taxonomical classification of Pilea melastomoides?

Pilea melastomoides belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Rosales. As a member of the family Urticaceae, it is further categorized into the genus Pilea, with its specific designation being the species melastomoides.
