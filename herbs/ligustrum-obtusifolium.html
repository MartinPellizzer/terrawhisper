Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Oleaceae and the genus Ligustrum. Specifically, the species is identified as Ligustrum obtusifolium.

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions across East Asia. Within China, its presence is documented in Manchuria, North-Central China, and Southeast China. Beyond the Chinese mainland, the species extends its reach into the neighboring territories of Korea. Its geographical range also encompasses the islands of Japan. Consequently, this medicinal plant follows a broad distribution pattern across the eastern parts of the continent.
