Gymnanthemum glaberrimum

Gymnanthemum glaberrimum, commonly known as bittercress, is a resilient perennial herb found in rocky, mountainous terrains. Renowned for its distinctively bitter flavor, this plant has been utilized in traditional medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and digestive benefits. It remains a fascinating subject for studying natural bioactive compounds in botany.

  • Scientific name: Gymnanthemum glaberrimum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gymnanthemum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Within this specific area, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its range also extends to the coastal and inland territories of Ghana. Additionally, the species is documented in the nation of Guinea. Finally, the geographical presence of this plant includes parts of Ivory Coast and Mali.
