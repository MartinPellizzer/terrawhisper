Dinebra retroflexa

Dinebra retroflexa, commonly known as the "Stitchwort" or "Hooked Dinebra," is a resilient medicinal herb native to various subtropical regions. Renowned in traditional healing, it is primarily utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antiseptic properties, offering natural remedies for skin ailments and digestive support through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Dinebra retroflexa
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Dinebra retroflexa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Dinebra.
