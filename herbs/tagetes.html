Tagetes

Tagetes, commonly known as marigolds, are vibrant medicinal plants valued for their diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential oils, they offer potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing benefits. Frequently used in traditional remedies to treat skin conditions and digestive issues, these golden blooms bridge beauty and healing.

  • Scientific name: Tagetes
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Tagetes.
