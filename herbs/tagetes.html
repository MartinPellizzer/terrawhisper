Marigold, scientifically known as Tagetes, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its vibrant blooms and therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential oils, it is widely used to treat skin inflammation, promote wound healing, and reduce swelling. Its natural compounds offer powerful anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits for wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Marigold, is widely utilized in traditional medicine, with its most potent therapeutic properties found within its vibrant flowers. These blossoms contain a rich concentration of essential oils, flavonoids, and carotenoids, which contribute to their anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. When processed into salves, teas, or extracts, the flowers serve as the primary source of healing compounds used to treat skin irritations and promote wound healing.
