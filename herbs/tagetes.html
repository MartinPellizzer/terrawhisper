Marigold

Tagetes · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (9 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Marigold, scientifically known as Tagetes, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its vibrant blooms and therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential oils, it is widely used to treat skin inflammation, promote wound healing, and reduce swelling. Its natural compounds offer powerful anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits for wellness.
