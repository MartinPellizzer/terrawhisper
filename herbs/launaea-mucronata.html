Launaea mucronata

Launaea mucronata, commonly known as "mumu" or "laukala" in various Pacific regions, is a versatile medicinal herb. Widely utilized in traditional healing practices, this plant is prized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, offering a natural remedy for skin ailments and various internal inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Launaea mucronata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa
