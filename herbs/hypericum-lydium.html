Hypericum lydium

Hypericum lydium, commonly known as a distinctive species of St. John's wort, is a perennial herb native to specific Mediterranean regions. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this plant holds significant botanical interest. It is studied for its unique phytochemical profile and potential therapeutic applications in traditional herbal medicine.

  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is a member of the Hypericaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Hypericum as the species Hypericum lydium.
