Mucuna macrocarpa

Mucuna macrocarpa, commonly known as the velvet bean, is a robust tropical legume valued for its diverse medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like L-dopa, it is traditionally used to support neurological health, balance hormones, and enhance vitality. This potent plant offers significant potential in holistic wellness therapies.

  • Scientific name: Mucuna macrocarpa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized under the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Mucuna, which encompasses the species Mucuna macrocarpa.

Common Names

This plant, known by several distinct names across different regions and languages, is referred to as 大果油麻藤 in Chinese (zh), while in Taiwan, it is commonly called 血藤 (zh-tw). In Vietnam, it is identified as Đậu mèo quả to (vi), and in Japan, it is known as ウジルカンダ (ja). Additionally, in the Tay language, it is called wahiy na kapalit (tay).

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. The species also extends its range to the islands of Japan within Eastern Asia. Additionally, it can be located throughout the Nansei-shotō archipelago. These specific locations highlight the plant's localized distribution within these subtropical environments.
