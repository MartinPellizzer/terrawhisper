Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. The species also extends its range to the islands of Japan within Eastern Asia. Additionally, it can be located throughout the Nansei-shotō archipelago. These specific locations highlight the plant's localized distribution within these subtropical environments.
