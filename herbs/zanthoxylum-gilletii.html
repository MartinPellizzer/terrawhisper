This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the West Tropical Africa region. Its specific distribution includes the country of Benin, where it grows in native habitats. It is also documented within the borders of Ghana, contributing to the local biodiversity. Furthermore, the species can be located throughout Guinea and the Ivory Coast. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant extends into Liberia.
