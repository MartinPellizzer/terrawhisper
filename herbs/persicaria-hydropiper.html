Water pepper, scientifically known as Persicaria hydropiper, is a creeping perennial herb native to tropical Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. Often used to treat digestive ailments and skin infections, this versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.
