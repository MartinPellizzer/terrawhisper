Persicaria hydropiper

Water pepper

Persicaria hydropiper, commonly known as Water Pepper, is a versatile medicinal herb native to moist habitats. Renowned for its pungent aroma, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. Traditionally used in various cultures, this plant serves as a natural remedy for treating skin infections, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria hydropiper
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Persicaria.
