Viburnum jucundum

Viburnum jucundum, commonly known as the delightful shrub, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to support digestive health and soothe inflammation. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a fascinating subject for both herbalists and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Viburnum jucundum
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Dipsacales and the family Viburnaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Viburnum.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution that spans across parts of North and Central America.
