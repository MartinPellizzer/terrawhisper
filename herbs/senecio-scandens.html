Senecio scandens

Senecio scandens, commonly known as the climbing groundsel, is a versatile climbing herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its creeping habit and delicate foliage, this plant is frequently utilized for its bioactive compounds. It plays a significant role in various herbal remedies, particularly for addressing diverse inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Senecio scandens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Senecio scandens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Senecio.
