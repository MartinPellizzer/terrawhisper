What's the taxonomical classification of Carex finitima?

Carex finitima belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Poales, the plant is nested within the family Cyperaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with its assignment to the genus Carex, ultimately identifying it as the specific species Carex finitima.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Carex finitima has a perennial growth habit characterized by slender, erect stems that typically reach heights of 5 to 20 centimeters. The leaves are narrow and linear, often appearing somewhat compressed or channeled in their structure. Its inflorescence consists of small, compact spikes that are arranged along the upper portion of the stem. The perigynia are notably small and possess a distinct shape that helps differentiate this species from similar sedges. These morphological traits allow the plant to thrive in specific niche habitats within its natural range.
