Distribution

This plant is primarily found across high-altitude regions of the Asian continent. In China, its distribution is specifically concentrated within the Tibet region. Moving toward the Indian subcontinent, it grows throughout the East Himalaya range. The species is also widely documented across the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Furthermore, its presence extends to the West Himalaya within the Indian subcontinent.
