Distribution

This plant is primarily found across high-altitude regions of the Asian continent. In China, its distribution is specifically concentrated within the Tibet region. Moving toward the Indian subcontinent, it grows throughout the East Himalaya range. The species is also widely documented across the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Furthermore, its presence extends to the West Himalaya within the Indian subcontinent.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Rhodiola or Golden Root, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties which are concentrated in its underground structures. The most potent part used in traditional and modern herbal medicine is the thick, fleshy rhizome, which often develops a distinctive yellowish color. In addition to the spreading rhizome, the entire root system is typically harvested to extract the active compounds responsible for its adaptogenic effects.
