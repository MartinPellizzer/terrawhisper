Rhodiola imbricata

Rhodiola imbricaata, commonly known as Roseroot, is a resilient perennial herb flourishing in high-altitude Himalayan environments. Renowned for its potent adaptogenic properties, this medicinal plant is traditionally used to enhance physical endurance, combat fatigue, and support mental clarity, offering significant therapeutic potential for managing modern physiological stress.

  • Scientific name: Rhodiola imbricata
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifrages, it is further classified under the family Crassulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rhodiola, specifically as the species Rhodiola imbricata.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across high-altitude regions of the Asian continent. In China, its distribution is specifically concentrated within the Tibet region. Moving toward the Indian subcontinent, it grows throughout the East Himalaya range. The species is also widely documented across the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Furthermore, its presence extends to the West Himalaya within the Indian subcontinent.
