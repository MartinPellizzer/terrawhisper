Carissa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (28 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Num-nums, scientifically known as Carissa, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for both ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Native to various African regions, it features glossy foliage and white flowers. Traditionally, its extracts are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across the African continent and its surrounding islands. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Egypt. Its distribution extends to the Macaronesian region, specifically found in Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species thrives throughout various parts of West Tropical Africa. This includes specific locations such as Benin, Burkina, and Ghana.
