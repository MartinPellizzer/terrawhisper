Carissa

Carissa, commonly known as Natal Plum, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its medicinal and ornamental qualities. Native to Africa, its berries are rich in antioxidants, while its leaves are traditionally used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues. This resilient plant offers both healing and aesthetic beauty.

  • Scientific name: Carissa
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Carissa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across the African continent and its surrounding islands. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Egypt. Its distribution extends to the Macaronesian region, specifically found in Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species thrives throughout various parts of West Tropical Africa. This includes specific locations such as Benin, Burkina, and Ghana.
