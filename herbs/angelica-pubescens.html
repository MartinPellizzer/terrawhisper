Angelica pubescens

Angelica pubescens, commonly known as Dong Qiang, is a highly valued medicinal herb in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its warming properties, it is primarily used to invigorate blood circulation and dispel stagnation. This potent botanical plays a crucial role in treating pain, menstrual irregularities, and various cardiovascular ailments.

  • Scientific name: Angelica pubescens
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, falling into the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Angelica.
