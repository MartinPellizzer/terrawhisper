Pubescent Angelica, scientifically known as Angelica pubescens, is a perennial medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, particularly in supporting cardiovascular health, regulating hormones, and reducing inflammation.
