Ajuga oblongata

Ajuga oblongata, commonly known as a medicinal herb in various traditional practices, is a fascinating plant species valued for its unique bioactive properties. Often found in specific ecological niches, it holds significant potential in ethnobotany, offering promising insights into natural treatments for diverse health conditions and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Ajuga oblongata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ajuga.

Common Names

This plant, Ajuga oblongata, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as bugleweed, while in Spanish, it is commonly called ajuga. In Portuguese, the plant is known as ajuga, and in French, it is referred to as ajuge.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across parts of Western Asia and the Caucasus region. Within the Caucasus, its presence is notably recorded in the Transcaucasus area. Moving toward Western Asia, the species can also be found in Iraq. This distribution highlights its adaptation to the diverse terrains found within these specific territories. Consequently, the habitat of Ajuga oblongata remains concentrated within these distinct geographical zones.

This plant, Ajuga oblongata, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts.
