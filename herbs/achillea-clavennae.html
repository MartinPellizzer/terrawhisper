Silvery yarrow, scientifically known as Achillea clavennae, is a resilient perennial native to coastal regions. Characterized by its distinctive silvery-gray foliage and delicate white flower clusters, this medicinal herb is valued for its soothing properties. It is traditionally used to support skin healing and promote overall wellness through its bioactive compounds.
