Achillea clavennae

Silvery yarrow

Achillea clavennae, commonly known as the clove-scented yarrow, is a distinctive perennial herb valued for its unique aromatic profile. Native to specific regions, this medicinal plant features fine, fern-like foliage and clusters of small flowers. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies for its soothing and antiseptic properties.

  • Scientific name: Achillea clavennae
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is classified under the genus Achillea.
