Echium creticum subsp. creticum

Echium creticum creticum

Echium creticum subsp. creticum, commonly known as Cretan Bugloss, is a resilient Mediterranean herb valued for its unique medicinal properties. Characterized by striking blue flowers, this plant is traditionally used in herbal remedies to support skin health and reduce inflammation, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds and nutrients.

  • Scientific name: Echium creticum subsp. creticum
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Boraginales, it is classified under the family Boraginaceae and the genus Echium. Specifically, this taxon is identified as the subspecies Echium creticum subsp. creticum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several European regions. In Northern Europe, its presence can be recorded in Sweden. Its range extends significantly into Southwestern Europe, where it inhabits the Baleares. Additionally, this subspecies is found within the territories of France and Corse. Finally, its distribution continues through the Mediterranean island of Sardegna.
