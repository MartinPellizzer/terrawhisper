Hedeoma drummondii

Hedeoma drummondii, commonly known as Drummond's pennyroyal, is a perennial herb native to North America. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, it is distinguished by its aromatic foliage and small, tubular flowers. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this plant possesses potent essential oils used for their various therapeutic properties.
