This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is noted within the borders of Angola. Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Nephelium lappaceum and commonly as rambutan, offers various components that are utilized in traditional and modern applications. The most visually striking part is the fruit, which is encased in a bright red, leathery rind covered in soft spines, containing a sweet, translucent pulp that is widely consumed. Within this fruit lie the seeds, which, despite being embedded in the fleshy aril, are noted in various medicinal studies for containing bioactive compounds that may possess antioxidant or anti-inflammatory properties.
