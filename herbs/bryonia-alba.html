Bryonia alba

White bryony

Bryonia alba, commonly known as White Bryony, is a climbing herbaceous vine native to Europe and parts of Asia. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this plant contains potent alkaloids. While historically used for treating inflammatory conditions and digestive issues, it must be handled with extreme caution due to its toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Bryonia alba
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Bryonia, which includes the specific species Bryonia alba.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Bryonia alba, is recognized by various regional names across different languages. Distribution

Moving toward Scandinavia, the species is well-established throughout Denmark. It also maintains a presence across the vast territories of Sweden and Norway.
