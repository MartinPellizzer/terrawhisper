White bryony, scientifically known as Bryonia alba, is a climbing deciduous vine native to Europe and parts of Asia. Traditionally used in homeopathic medicine, this plant contains potent alkaloids. While historically valued for treating respiratory and inflammatory conditions, its toxicity requires extreme caution, as all parts are highly poisonous.
