Aristida funiculata

Aristida funiculata, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional healing practices, belongs to the Poaceae family. Found primarily in tropical regions, this plant is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, showcasing its importance in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa
