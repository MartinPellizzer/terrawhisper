Bougainvillea glabra

Bougainvillea glabra, commonly known as paperflower, is a vibrant climbing shrub prized for its ornamental beauty. Beyond its striking bracts, this plant holds significant medicinal potential in traditional practices. It is frequently studied for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering promising applications in diverse natural therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Bougainvillea glabra
  • Family: Nyctaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales and the family Nyctaginaceae, it is classified under the genus Bougainvillea. Specifically, its scientific name is Bougainvillea glabra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several continents and distinct climate zones. In Europe, it can be found across the Mediterranean region, specifically in Spain within Southwestern Europe and Italy in Southeastern Europe. Its range extends southward into Northern Africa, where it is established in Algeria. The species also inhabits the Macaronesian islands, most notably the Canary Islands. Finally, its presence reaches into West-Central Tropical Africa, particularly within the nation of Cameroon.
