Eucomis autumnalis

Eucomis autumnalis, commonly known as the Pineapple Lily, is a striking perennial native to South Africa. Distinguished by its unique rosette of leaves and a central flower spike resembling a pineapple, this plant offers stunning ornamental value and medicinal potential, traditionally utilized for its diverse healing properties in botanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Eucomis autumnalis
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asparagaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eucomis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout the regions of Southern and South Tropical Africa. Within the South Tropical Africa zone, its distribution includes the countries of Malawi, Mozambique, and Zambia. It can also be located within the borders of Zimbabwe. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the Southern Africa region, specifically occurring in Botswana.
