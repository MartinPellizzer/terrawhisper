Autumn Pineapple Lily, scientifically known as Eucomis autumnalis, is a striking perennial native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its unique rosette of flowers resembling a pineapple, this plant offers both ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Traditionally, various parts are utilized in herbal remedies to treat diverse ailments and inflammation.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as the pineapple lily, is a perennial herb characterized by its striking appearance, where the whole plant features long, strap-like leaves and a unique inflorescence topped with a tuft of bracts. The most significant part for medicinal and botanical interest is the bulb, which serves as the plant's primary storage organ for nutrients and water. These underground bulbs are fleshy and bulbous in shape, allowing the species to survive dormant periods in its natural habitat.
