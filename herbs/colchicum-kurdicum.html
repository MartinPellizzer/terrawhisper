Colchicum kurdicum

Colchicum kurdicum, commonly known as Kurdish Autumn Crocus, is a resilient perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of the Middle East. Valued in traditional medicine, its bulbs contain bioactive alkaloids. While possessing significant therapeutic potential for inflammatory conditions, its high toxicity requires precise handling and expert pharmacological application.

  • Scientific name: Colchicum kurdicum
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Liliales, falling under the family Colchicaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Colchicum.
