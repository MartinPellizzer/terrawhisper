Dioscorea esquirolii

What's the taxonomical classification of Dioscorea esquirolii?

Dioscorea esquirolii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Dioscoreales and is a member of the family Dioscoreaceae. Specifically, it is identified under the genus Dioscorea, with its unique designation being the species esquirolii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

These stems are herbaceous and utilize twining mechanisms to ascend toward sunlight. Small, inconspicuous flowers are typically produced in clusters during the growing season.
