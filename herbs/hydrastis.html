Goldenseal, scientifically known as Hydrastis canadensis, is a perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial properties, it is primarily utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues and inflammation. Its distinctive yellow rhizome contains berberine, a powerful alkaloid widely used in various herbal remedies.
