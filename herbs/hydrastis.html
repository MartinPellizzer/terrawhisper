Hydrastis

Hydrastis, commonly known as Goldenseal, is a perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial properties, it contains berberine, which helps fight infections. Traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat digestive issues and skin inflammation, this botanical powerhouse remains a staple in natural wellness remedies today.

  • Scientific name: Hydrastis
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae within the genus Hydrastis.

In Croatian, it is called Narančin korijen, while in Finnish, it is known as Hurmejuuret. In Catalan, the name is hidrastis, and in Czech, it is called vodilka. Finally, in Spanish, the plant is commonly referred to as Hidrastis.
