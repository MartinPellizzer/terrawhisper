Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In the Macaronesian region, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. Moving to West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Benin. The species also inhabits the landscapes of Burkina and the Gambia. Finally, its range extends further along the coast to include Ghana.
