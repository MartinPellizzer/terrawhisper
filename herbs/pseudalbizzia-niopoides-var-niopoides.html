Pseudalbizzia niopoides var. niopoides

Pseudalbizzia niopoides var. niopoides, commonly known as the Niopo plant, is a significant medicinal species found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights.

  • Native range: Africa
