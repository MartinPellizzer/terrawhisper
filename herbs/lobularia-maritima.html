Lobularia maritima

Lobularia maritima, commonly known as sweet alyssum, is a versatile perennial herb cherished for its fragrant, honey-scented white blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal in gardens, it holds traditional medicinal significance. Renowned for its calming properties, this resilient plant is often utilized in herbal practices to soothe and restore balance.

  • Scientific name: Lobularia maritima
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe
