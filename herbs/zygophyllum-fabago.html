Zygophyllum fabago

Zygophyllum fabago, commonly known as the Sand Mustard, is a resilient succulent native to arid African regions. Characterized by its fleshy leaves and striking yellow blooms, this plant holds traditional significance in ethnobotany. It is frequently studied for its potential bioactive compounds and its role in traditional medicinal practices.

  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe
