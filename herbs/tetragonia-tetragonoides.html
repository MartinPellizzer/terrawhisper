Tetragonia tetragonoides

New zealand spinach

Tetragonia tetragonoides, commonly known as Warrigal greens, is a resilient, succulent leafy vegetable native to Australia. Renowned for its nutritional density, this edible plant offers essential vitamins and minerals. Often used in traditional diets, it serves as a versatile ingredient in modern cuisine, providing a salty, spinach-like flavor profile.

  • Scientific name: Tetragonia tetragonoides
  • Family: Aizoaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Aizoaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Tetragonia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Its distribution also extends to the territories of Great Britain and Sweden within the same northern reaches. Moving southward, the species is also documented throughout the Middle Europe region. Specifically, its geographical range includes the areas of Czechia and Slovakia.
