Cleome fimbriata

Cleome fimbriata, commonly known as Spider Flower, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is often utilized to support digestive health and skin vitality. Its unique floral structure and bioactive compounds make it a fascinating subject for ethnobotanical study.

  • Scientific name: Cleome fimbriata
  • Family: Cleomaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Brassicales and the family Cleomaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Cleome.
