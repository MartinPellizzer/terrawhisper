Casearia obliqua

Casearia obliqua, commonly known as erva-de-santa-luzia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Brazil. Renowned for its significant therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This plant holds immense value in ethnobotany, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and treatments.

  • Scientific name: Casearia obliqua
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Casearia obliqua, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Salicaceae and falls under the genus Casearia.
