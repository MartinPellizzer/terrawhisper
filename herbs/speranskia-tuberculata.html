Speranskia tuberculata

Speranskia tuberculata, commonly known as the tuberous speranskias, is a unique medicinal herb native to specific regions of Asia. Renowned for its distinctive tuberous roots, this plant is studied for its bioactive compounds. It holds significant potential in traditional medicine for treating various ailments through its natural healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Speranskia tuberculata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Speranskia tuberculata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Speranskia.
