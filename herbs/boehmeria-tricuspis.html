What's the taxonomical classification of Boehmeria tricuspis?

Boehmeria tricuspis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its hierarchical lineage continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Rosales. This plant is a member of the family Urticaceae, which is further narrowed down to the genus Boehmeria, ultimately identifying it as the specific species tricuspis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Boehmeria tricuspis has an erect, herbaceous stem that typically reaches heights of 30 to 100 centimeters and features oppositely arranged, ovate leaves with serrated margins and three prominent veins originating from the base. Small, inconspicuous flowers are clustered in axillary spikes or panicles, appearing in shades of green or pale white. These inflorescences are primarily unisexual, with staminate and pistillate flowers often separated on the same plant.
