Leonurus glaucescens, scientifically known as Leonurus glaucescens, is a perennial herb native to North America. Often called bluebugle, this medicinal plant belongs to the Lamiaceae family. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it contains bioactive compounds valued for their potential calming, uterine-stimulating, and anti-inflammatory properties in various folk remedies.
