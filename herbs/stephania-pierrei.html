Stephania pierrei

Stephania pierrei, commonly known as "erva-de-passarinho" in Brazil, is a climbing woody vine prized in traditional medicine. Rich in alkaloids, this botanical species is widely utilized for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic properties, playing a crucial role in folk remedies for treating various systemic ailments and infections.

  • Scientific name: Stephania pierrei
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
