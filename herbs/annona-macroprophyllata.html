Annona macroprophyllata

Annona macroprophylla, commonly known as the large-leafed custard apple, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, thanks to its rich concentration of bioactive phytochemicals.
