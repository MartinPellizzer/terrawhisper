Solanum pseudocapsicum

Solanum pseudocapsicum, commonly known as the Brazilian Nightshade, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its bright orange berries, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Solanum pseudocapsicum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, the species is categorized under the genus Solanum.
