Jerusalem-cherry, scientifically known as Solanum pseudocapsicum, is a perennial shrub native to the Americas. Distinguished by its bright orange, berry-like fruits, it holds significant historical importance in traditional medicine. While used to treat various ailments, its alkaloids require careful handling due to potential toxicity if consumed incorrectly.
