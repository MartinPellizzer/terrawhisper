Primula veris

Primula officinalis

Primula veris, commonly known as cowslip, is a perennial wildflower cherished for its bright yellow blooms and historical medicinal uses. Native to Europe, this gentle herb has long been utilized in traditional herbalism to soothe respiratory ailments, ease menstrual discomfort, and act as a mild sedative for calming nerves.

  • Scientific name: Primula veris
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Primulaceae and the genus Primula. Therefore, its full scientific designation identifies it as Primula veris.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Primula veris, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In Poland, it is called pierwiosnek lekarski, while in Belarus, it is referred to as Першацвет веснавы. Hungarian speakers call it tavaszi kankalin, and in Latvia, it is known as gaiļbiksīte. Finally, in Estonia, the plant is known by the name harilik nurmenukk.
