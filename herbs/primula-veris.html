Primula veris · Accepted scientific name

Primula officiinalis, scientifically known as Primula veris, is a perennial herb cherished for its bright yellow blossoms and therapeutic properties. Commonly called cowslip, this woodland plant has long been used in traditional medicine to soothe respiratory ailments, reduce inflammation, and support digestive health through its nutrient-rich, bioactive compounds.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the cowslip, is highly valued in traditional herbalism, where the most significant medicinal component is the flower. The colorful, bell-shaped blossoms are harvested to create infusions or tinctures, as they contain various bioactive compounds believed to possess soothing properties. Often used to alleviate respiratory ailments or digestive issues, the flower serves as the primary part of the plant utilized for its therapeutic potential.
