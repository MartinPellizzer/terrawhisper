Magnolia henryi

What's the taxonomical classification of Magnolia henryi?

Magnolia henryi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Magnoliales. This plant is a distinct representative of the family Magnoliaceae, falling under the genus Magnolia, and is specifically identified by its species name, henryi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Magnolia henryi has evergreen leaves that are broadly elliptic to obovate, measuring approximately 10 to 20 centimeters in length, with a leathery texture and a glossy dark green upper surface. The leaf margins are entire and often slightly revolute, while the undersides exhibit a paler green color with a fine pubescence. Its flowers are large and creamy white, emerging from terminal buds to reveal numerous prominent stamens and carpels. The plant typically develops into a small tree or large shrub characterized by a dense, spreading canopy. Its woody stems are sturdy and provide structural support for the heavy, coriaceous foliage.
