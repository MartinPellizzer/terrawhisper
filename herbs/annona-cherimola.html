Cherimoya, scientifically known as Annona cherimola, is a tropical fruit prized for its creamy texture and sweet, custard-like flavor. Native to the Andes, this "superfruit" is rich in essential vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants, offering significant medicinal benefits, including digestive support and anti-inflammatory properties that promote overall wellness.
