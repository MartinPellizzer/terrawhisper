Seseli praecox

Seseli praecox, commonly known as Early Seseli, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Native to rocky European habitats, this plant is characterized by its delicate white umbels and aromatic foliage. While primarily studied for its botanical uniqueness, it holds potential interest in traditional herbal medicine applications.
