Cryptocarya griffithiana

Cryptocarya griffithiana, commonly known as the Griffit's Crypto, is a significant medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this species is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and infections, offering promising potential for modern therapeutic research and botanical development.

  • Scientific name: Cryptocarya griffithiana
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Cryptocarya.

This plant is primarily found within the diverse tropical regions of Malesia. Its specific geographical range includes the large island of Borneo.
