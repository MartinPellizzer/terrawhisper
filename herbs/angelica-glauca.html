Angelica glauca

Angelica glauca, commonly known as glaucous angelica, is a perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this botanical species is recognized for its aromatic properties and bioactive compounds. It serves as a vital subject in ethnobotanical studies, highlighting the diverse therapeutic potential of alpine flora.

  • Scientific name: Angelica glauca
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement identifies it under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the Apiaceae family within the genus Angelica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several high-altitude regions. In Western Asia, it can be found growing within the borders of Afghanistan. Its range extends into China, specifically inhabiting the plateau of Tibet. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species is located in Pakistan. Additionally, the plant is distributed throughout the West Himalaya region of the Indian Subcontinent.
