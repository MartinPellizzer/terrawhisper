Smooth angelica, scientifically known as Angelica glauca, is a perennial herb native to North American alpine and subalpine regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and medicinal properties, this plant belongs to the Apiaceae family. It is traditionally valued in herbalism for its potential digestive and restorative therapeutic benefits.
