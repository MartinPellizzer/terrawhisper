Andira inermis

Andira inermis, commonly known as the "almond tree" or "andira," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its seeds and bark offer significant therapeutic potential in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Andira inermis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Andira and the species Andira inermis.

This plant, Andira inermis, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin and Burkina.
