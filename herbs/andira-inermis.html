Distribution

This plant, Andira inermis, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin and Burkina. Furthermore, the species is present in the coastal and inland areas of Gambia. Its range also extends through the territory of Ghana. Finally, the plant is documented as being native to Guinea-Bissau.
