Ferula tingitana

Ferula tingitana, commonly known as a medicinal giant fennel, is a distinctive perennial herb native to North Africa. Renowned in traditional Moroccan medicine, its roots and resins are utilized for their potent bioactive compounds. This plant holds significant pharmacological potential, particularly for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Ferula tingitana
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, falling into the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Ferula.
