Giant Tangier fennel, scientifically known as Ferula tingitana, is a robust perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its impressive stature and aromatic properties, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It contains bioactive compounds utilized for their potential anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and various therapeutic healing effects.
