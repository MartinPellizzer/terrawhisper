Berberis caroli

Berberis caroli, commonly known as Carol's barberry, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its thorny stems and vibrant seasonal blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic and antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Berberis caroli
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
