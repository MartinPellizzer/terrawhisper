Delphinium

Delphinium, commonly known as larkspur, is a striking perennial celebrated for its tall, spire-like blooms. While prized in ornamental gardens for their vivid blues and purples, these plants contain potent alkaloids. In traditional herbalism, they have been used cautiously, though their toxicity requires expert handling and extreme care.

  • Scientific name: Delphinium
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae, it is classified under the genus Delphinium.
