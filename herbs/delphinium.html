Larkspur, scientifically known as Delphinium, is a striking perennial celebrated for its tall, spire-like blooms in vibrant blues and purples. While admired in gardens for their architectural beauty, these plants contain potent alkaloids, requiring careful handling as they possess significant medicinal properties and potential toxicity if ingested improperly.
