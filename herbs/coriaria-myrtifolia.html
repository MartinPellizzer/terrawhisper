Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within the Mediterranean basin. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is documented across Spain and Portugal. It is also found within the Balearic Islands as part of its western range. Furthermore, the species extends into France to complete its Western European coverage. Finally, its distribution reaches into Southeastern Europe, specifically within Greece.
