Primulina longgangensis

Primulina longgangenuis, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, is a distinctive species belonging to the Gesneriaceae family. Endemic to specific karst landscapes, this plant is highly valued in traditional practices. Its unique bioactive compounds offer significant pharmacological potential, making it a subject of intense botanical and therapeutic interest.

  • Scientific name: Primulina longgangensis
  • Family: Gesneriaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Gesneriaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Primulina, and its species name, longgangensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within certain regions of Asia. It is primarily found in the Southeast part of China, where it inhabits local ecosystems. Additionally, its range extends into the Indo-China region. Specifically, the species has been documented growing within the borders of Vietnam. Consequently, its habitat is limited to these particular subtropical territories.
