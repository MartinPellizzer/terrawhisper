Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Tanzania. Its presence extends across the Western Indian Ocean, specifically within the Comoros and Madagascar. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout the Indian Subcontinent. This range includes specific populations located in both Bangladesh and India.
