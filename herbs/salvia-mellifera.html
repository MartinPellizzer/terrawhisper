Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as black sage, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties through the use of its aerial parts. These above-ground structures encompass the fragrant, serrated leaves, the square stems characteristic of the Lamiaceae family, and the flowering spikes that emerge during its growing season. Because the essential oils and bioactive compounds are concentrated within these leaves and stems, the aerial parts serve as the primary source for herbal preparations and extracts.
