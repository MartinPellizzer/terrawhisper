Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its range includes the Kuril Islands and Sakhalin. Moving into China, it can be found throughout the Manchuria region. The species also inhabits the South-Central areas of the country. Finally, its distribution extends as far south as the island of Hainan.
