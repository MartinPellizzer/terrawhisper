Garcinia cowa var. cowa

Garcinia cowa var. cowa, commonly known as the Malabar Gamboge, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Western Ghats of India. Renowned for its bioactive xanthones, this species is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering profound therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia cowa var. cowa
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
