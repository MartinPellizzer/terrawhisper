Plant Parts

This plant, Garcinia cowa var. cowa, is highly valued for its medicinal properties which are primarily derived from its reproductive structures. The fresh fruits are the most visible component, though the most potent therapeutic elements are found within the fruit rinds. In traditional and pharmacological applications, these rinds are often processed into dried fruit rind to concentrate the active compounds, ensuring stability and ease of use in various medicinal preparations.
