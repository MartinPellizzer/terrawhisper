Crotalaria sessiliflora

What's the taxonomical classification of Crotalaria sessiliflora?

Crotalaria sessiliflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Fabales and is a member of the family Fabaceae. Finally, this plant is identified by the genus Crotalaria and its specific species name, sessiliflora.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Crotalaria sessiliflora has an erect, herbaceous growth habit characterized by branched stems, trifoliate leaves with oblong to elliptical leaflets, and sessile yellow flowers that lack a distinct pedicel. The leaves are typically arranged alternately along the stem and feature entire margins with a smooth texture. Its floral structure consists of a bright yellow corolla that is slightly irregular in shape, typical of the Fabaceae family. The inflorescence emerges directly from the leaf axils, giving the plant its sessile characteristic. Following pollination, the plant produces distinctive, inflated legume pods that contain several small seeds.
