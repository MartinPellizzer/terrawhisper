Pepper bark tree

Pepper bark tree, scientifically known as Warburgia salutaris, is a medicinal evergreen tree native to Southern and Eastern Africa. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and antifungal properties, its bark is traditionally utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin infections, making it a cornerstone of indigenous pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as the Pepper-bark tree, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, with the most significant parts being the bark and the leaves. The medicinal bark, particularly the stem bark, is highly valued for its potent antimicrobial and antifungal qualities. In some traditional applications, the ground stem bark is processed into powders to be used in various remedies. Furthermore, the leaves, or simply the leaf, are often utilized in decoctions and infusions to treat respiratory and digestive ailments.
