Purple yam, scientifically known as Dioscorea alata, is a nutrient-dense tuber widely cultivated across tropical regions. Beyond its culinary versatility, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and minerals, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, manage blood sugar, and promote overall immune function.

Distribution

This plant, known as Dioscorea alata, exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence has been documented across several distinct nations in this part of the continent. Specifically, it can be found growing in the territories of Benin and Burkina. The species is also widely distributed throughout Guinea and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, the plant's range extends into the country of Mali.

Plant Parts

This plant, Dioscorea alata, commonly known as water yam, is primarily cultivated for its nutrient-rich and starchy tuber. This underground storage organ serves as the most significant medicinal and dietary part of the plant, containing various bioactive compounds that contribute to its therapeutic potential. While the vines and leaves are part of the plant's structure, it is the fleshy tuber that is harvested and utilized for its medicinal properties and nutritional value.
