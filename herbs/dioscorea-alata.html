Dioscorea alata

Dioscorea alata, commonly known as water yam, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to manage diabetes, reduce inflammation, and support digestive health. This nutrient-dense tuber plays a vital role in both holistic nutrition and herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea alata
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dioscoreales, falling into the family Dioscoreaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dioscorea, specifically as the species Dioscorea alata.

Distribution

This plant, known as Dioscorea alata, exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence has been documented across several distinct nations in this part of the continent. Specifically, it can be found growing in the territories of Benin and Burkina. The species is also widely distributed throughout Guinea and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, the plant's range extends into the country of Mali.
