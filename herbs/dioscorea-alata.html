Purple yam, scientifically known as Dioscorea alata, is a nutrient-dense tuber widely cultivated across tropical regions. Beyond its culinary versatility, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and minerals, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, manage blood sugar, and promote overall immune function.
