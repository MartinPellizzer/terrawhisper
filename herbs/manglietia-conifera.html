Manglietia conifera

What's the taxonomical classification of Manglietia conifera?

Manglietia conifera belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Magnoliales. It is further categorized under the family Magnoliaceae, specifically falling within the genus Manglietia, with its unique biological identity defined by the species name conifera.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?
