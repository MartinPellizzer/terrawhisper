Plinia trunciflora

Plinia trunciflora, commonly known as a rare species within the Jabuticaba family, is a significant medicinal plant native to South American ecosystems. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this botanical treasure offers therapeutic potential in treating inflammation and oxidative stress, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest worldwide.

  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
