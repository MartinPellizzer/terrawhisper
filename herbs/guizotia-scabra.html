This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its distribution includes the nations of Benin and Nigeria. Moving into the West-Central Tropical Africa region, the species can also be found in Burundi. Additionally, it is documented to grow within the Central African Republic. Plant Parts

This plant, Gichui or Guizotia scabra, is frequently utilized in traditional medicine, with its therapeutic properties primarily concentrated in the leaves. These leaves are often harvested and used in various forms, such as decoctions or infusions, to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to respiratory infections. The chemical constituents found within the leaf tissue are believed to provide the essential bioactive compounds necessary for its healing effects.
