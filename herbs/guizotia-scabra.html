Guizotia scabra

Guizotia scabra, commonly known as the "bitter herb" in some regions, is a versatile medicinal plant native to East Africa. Widely utilized in traditional medicine, it is prized for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, frequently used to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and various skin infections.

  • Scientific name: Guizotia scabra
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Guizotia scabra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Guizotia.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its distribution includes the nations of Benin and Nigeria. Plant Parts

This plant, Gichui or Guizotia scabra, is frequently utilized in traditional medicine, with its therapeutic properties primarily concentrated in the leaves. These leaves are often harvested and used in various forms, such as decoctions or infusions, to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to respiratory infections. The chemical constituents found within the leaf tissue are believed to provide the essential bioactive compounds necessary for its healing effects.
