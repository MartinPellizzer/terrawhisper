Coptis deltoidea

Coptis deltoidea, commonly known as Chinese goldthread, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bitter properties, it contains potent alkaloids like berberine. Frequently used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and infections, this plant serves as a vital natural remedy in East Asian pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Coptis deltoidea
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Coptis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Coptis deltoidea, is recognized by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context.
