Coptis deltoidea

What's the taxonomical classification of Coptis deltoidea?

Coptis deltoidea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is positioned in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic descent through the order Ranunculales, it falls into the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized under the genus Coptis, with its specific identity defined by the species deltoidea.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Coptis deltoidea has basal, palmately lobed leaves that typically feature three to five distinct, triangular-shaped leaflets with serrated margins. These leaves are arranged in a rosette pattern near the ground, often displaying a dark green color. The plant produces slender, upright stems that emerge from the center to bear small, yellowish-green flowers. Each flower consists of several delicate petals and sepals arranged around a central cluster of reproductive organs. The overall structure is characterized by its compact growth habit and fine, textured foliage.

What side effects this plant can have?

Careful monitoring is necessary to prevent these unpleasant systemic symptoms from worsening.
