Distribution

This plant, Cocos nucifera, exhibits a specific geographical presence across several regions of West Tropical Africa. Within this part of the continent, it can be found growing in the coastal and tropical landscapes of Benin. Its distribution also extends to the nation of Gambia, where environmental conditions support its growth. Furthermore, the plant is documented throughout the territories of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, its range reaches into Guinea, rounding out its presence in this West African stretch.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cocos nucifera, is a versatile species where almost every component serves a purpose. The massive leaves, often referred to as a single large leaf frond, are essential for photosynthesis and can be used for various traditional purposes. Surrounding the fruit, the thick husk provides protection, while the extracted husk fiber is highly valued for making ropes and mats. Below the surface, the extensive roots anchor the tree and absorb nutrients from the soil, ensuring the overall health of the coconut palm.
