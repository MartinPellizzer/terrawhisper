Sigesbeckia glabrescens

Sigesbeckia glabrescens, commonly known as "Galing Galing" in the Philippines, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, it is frequently used to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and various inflammatory conditions, serving as a vital natural remedy in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Sigesbeckia glabrescens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Sigesbeckia.
