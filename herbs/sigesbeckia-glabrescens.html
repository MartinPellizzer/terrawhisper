Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the northern reaches of its range, it can be found within the Russian Far East, specifically in the Primorye region. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it is documented across diverse territories such as Manchuria and Inner Mongolia. Furthermore, the species is established in the South-Central regions of China. Finally, its distribution reaches as far south as the tropical island of Hainan.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Sigesbeckia glabrescens or "Luyang Dingding," utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, most notably its roots and leaves. The leaves are often employed in traditional practices to treat inflammation and skin ailments, while the roots are frequently prepared as decoctions to address internal issues such as stomach pains or fever. Together, these plant parts serve as a vital resource in folk medicine for their therapeutic potential.
