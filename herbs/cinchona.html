Cinchona

Cinchona, commonly known as the quinine tree, is a legendary medicinal plant native to the Andes. Renowned for its alkaloid-rich bark, it serves as the historical foundation for treating malaria. This botanical powerhouse has played a transformative role in global medicine, bridging ancient indigenous knowledge with modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Cinchona
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is specifically classified within the order Gentianaales and the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Cinchona.
