Coptis japonica var. anemonifolia, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its high berberine content, this plant possesses potent antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. It is frequently utilized to treat gastrointestinal issues, skin infections, and metabolic disorders, serving as a vital therapeutic resource.
