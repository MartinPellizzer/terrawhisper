Cecropia pachystachya

Cecropia pachystachyia, commonly known as a member of the Cecropia genus, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical ecosystems. Valued in traditional medicine, its diverse chemical properties offer potential therapeutic benefits. This introduction explores its botanical characteristics, ecological importance, and the pharmacological applications of its bioactive compounds.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Rosales and the family Urticaceae. Within this botanical hierarchy, it is identified by the genus Cecropia.
