Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Verbena officinalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement in the genus Verbena.

Plant Parts

This plant, Verbena officinalis, is a medicinal species where the entire herba is utilized for its therapeutic properties. In traditional and modern medicine, the herb is often collected as the dried aerial part to ensure stability for long-term storage and preparation. Practitioners frequently utilize various herb extracts to harness its bioactive compounds, often specifically employing a potent leaf extract to treat various ailments.
