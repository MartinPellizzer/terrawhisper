Renowned for its long history of healing, this plant is frequently utilized to treat digestive issues, anxiety, and inflammation. Its versatile properties make it a staple in herbalism for promoting overall wellness.

The flowering process occurs via a spike inflorescence, with violet-colored flowers typically blooming from June through August. This reproductive cycle relies on biotic pollination, specifically utilizing an insect-based pollination syndrome where bees play a significant role.
