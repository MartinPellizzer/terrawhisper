Verbena officinalis

Verbena officinalis, commonly known as vervain, is a perennial herb steeped in historical folklore and traditional medicine. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant has been utilized for centuries to treat ailments ranging from anxiety to digestive issues, offering a potent natural remedy supported by modern herbal studies.

  • Scientific name: Verbena officinalis
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Verbena officinalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement in the genus Verbena.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across various regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its distribution includes the countries of Denmark, Great Britain, and Ireland. Moving toward the central part of the continent, the species is also well-established in Middle Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Austria. Furthermore, its geographical reach extends to include Belgium as part of its Middle European range.
