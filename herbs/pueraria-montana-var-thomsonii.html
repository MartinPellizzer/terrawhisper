The roots of the plant, particularly the tuberous root system, are frequently utilized in traditional medicine due to their specific bioactive compounds. The climbing habit is supported by its long, twining stems, which allow the plant to ascend through surrounding vegetation. Its foliage consists of compound leaves that are essential for photosynthesis, while the delicate flowers serve as the reproductive organs, often displaying characteristic colors that attract pollinators.
