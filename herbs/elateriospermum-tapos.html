Elateriospermum tapos

Elateriospermum tapos, commonly known as tapos, is a significant medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including skin inflammations and digestive issues, offering valuable phytochemical benefits for holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Elateriospermum tapos
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Elateriospermum tapos, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Malpighiales. Furthermore, it is a member of the Euphorbiaceae family within the genus Elateriospermum.

Common Names

This plant is known by various names depending on the region and language. In Malay, it is commonly referred to as Perah or Tapus, while in Sundanese, it is called Tapos. In Spanish, it is identified as Elateriospermeae, and in Vietnamese, its name is simply Elateriospermum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, it can be found growing in Thailand. Its presence extends significantly into the Malesia zone, where it is documented on the island of Borneo. Additionally, the species is distributed across the islands of Jawa and Sumatera. Finally, the plant is also native to the Malay Peninsula within the Malesia region.
