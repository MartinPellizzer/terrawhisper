Eurya acuminata

Eurya acuminata, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, belongs to the Theaceae family. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential in developing natural therapies for inflammation, oxidative stress, and antimicrobial applications.

  • Scientific name: Eurya acuminata
  • Family: Pentaphylacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
