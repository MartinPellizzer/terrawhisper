Rhamnus cathartica

Rhamnus catharticica, commonly known as buckthorn, is a deciduous shrub valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, its bark contains anthraquinone glycosides that stimulate intestinal motility. While effective for treating constipation, its high potency requires careful usage to avoid dehydration and severe abdominal cramping.

  • Scientific name: Rhamnus cathartica
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Rhamnaceae and the genus Rhamnus.
