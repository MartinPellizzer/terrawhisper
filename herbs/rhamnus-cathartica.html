Buckthorn

Rhamnus cathartica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (156 studies) · ★★★★☆

Buckthorn, scientifically known as Rhamnus cathartica, is a deciduous shrub prized for its potent medicinal properties. Traditionally used in herbalism, its berries contain anthraquinones, which act as powerful laxatives. While effective for treating constipation, its intense purgative effects require cautious application and careful dosage to ensure safe therapeutic use.
