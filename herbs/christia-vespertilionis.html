East Indian Island Pea, scientifically known as Christia vespertilionis, is a striking ornamental shrub prized for its unique, bat-like flowers. Beyond its aesthetic charm, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Native to tropical regions, it is studied for its potential medicinal properties and its role in diverse ecological ecosystems.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia and the Indian Ocean. Within the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found specifically on the island of Mauritius. Its presence in China extends from the tropical island of Hainan to the broader China Southeast region. Additionally, the species is distributed across the Indian Subcontinent, occurring in both Assam and Bangladesh. These diverse locations highlight the varied ecological range inhabited by Christia vespertilionis.
