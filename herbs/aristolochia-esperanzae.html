Aristolochia esperanzae

Aristolochia esperanzae, commonly known as the hopeful aristolochia, is a fascinating climbing vine native to tropical regions. Distinguished by its unique, tubular flowers, this medicinal plant has been used in traditional practices to treat various ailments. However, its potent bioactive compounds require careful study due to potential toxicity concerns.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia esperanzae
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Aristolochiaceae and the genus Aristolochia.
