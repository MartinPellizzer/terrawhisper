Echinops orientalis, is a striking perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family, celebrated for its unique, spherical blue thistle-like flowers. Native to the Mediterranean and Middle East, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering a fascinating blend of beauty and healing.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several distinct regions within the Eastern Hemisphere. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is noted specifically within the European portion of Türkiye. Its range extends into the Caucasus, where it can be found throughout both the North Caucasus and the Transcaucasus. Moving into Western Asia, the species is well-established in the territory of Iran. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the country of Iraq, completing its regional distribution.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Echinops orientalis, is characterized by its distinct morphology, though in traditional herbal practices, specific attention is often directed toward the underground structures. While the striking blue globe-shaped flower heads and spiny leaves are its most visible features, the root serves as a vital component of the organism's anatomy. In many medicinal contexts, the root is the part of the plant studied for its potential bioactive properties and its ability to store nutrients essential for the plant's survival in its natural habitat.
