Cretan Barberry, scientifically known as Berberis cretica, is a resilient shrub native to the Mediterranean landscapes of Crete. Renowned for its vibrant yellow flowers and tart berries, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its antioxidant properties and various therapeutic applications.
