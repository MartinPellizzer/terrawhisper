Berberis cretica

Berberis cretica, commonly known as Cretan barberry, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its vibrant yellow flowers and tart berries, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional herbalism for its antioxidant properties, digestive support, and potential anti-inflammatory effects in various natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Berberis cretica
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is specifically nested within the family Berberidaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Berberis, specifically as the species Berberis cretica.
