Chrysosplenium

Chrysosplenium, commonly known as a versatile medicinal herb, possesses remarkable therapeutic properties valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory capabilities, this plant serves as a natural remedy for various ailments. This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Saxifragales. Finally, it is a member of the family Saxifragaceae and the genus Chrysospelnium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Chrysospelnium, is referred to by various common names across different Chinese linguistic regions. In Simplified Chinese, it is identified as 金腰属 (zh-hans) or 金腰属 (zh-cn), while in Singapore, it is written as 猫眼草属 (zh-sg). In Traditional Chinese contexts, such as in Hong Kong and other regions, it is commonly known as 貓眼草屬 (zh-hk and zh-hant).
