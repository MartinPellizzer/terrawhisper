Phedimus hybridus

Phedimus hybridus, commonly known as the Golden Weaver, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, it serves as a natural remedy for inflammatory conditions. This resilient herb is widely studied for its ability to support immune function and promote overall vitality.

  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe
