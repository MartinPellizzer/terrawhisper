Saxifragas, scientifically known as Bergenia, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its thick, leathery leaves and charming pink blooms, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is primarily utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties to treat various ailments.
