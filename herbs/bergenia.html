Saxifragas

Bergenia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (22 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Saxifragas, scientifically known as Bergenia, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its thick, leathery leaves and charming pink blooms, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is primarily utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties to treat various ailments.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Saxifragales and the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Bergenia.
