Bergenia

Bergenia, commonly known as elephant's ears, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Himalayan region. Renowned for its thick, leathery leaves, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and diuretic properties, making it a vital resource for treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Bergenia
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Saxifragales and the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Bergenia.
