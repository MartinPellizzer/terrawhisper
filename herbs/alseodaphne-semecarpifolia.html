Alseodaphne semecarpifolia

Alseodaphne semecarpifolia, commonly known as the Mountain Daphne, is a distinctive evergreen shrub native to the rainforests of Eastern Australia. Renowned for its striking, fragrant white flowers and glossy foliage, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional ethnobotany for its unique chemical properties and its role in regional biodiversity.
