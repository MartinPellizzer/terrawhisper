evening primrose

Oenothera biennis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (719 studies) · ★★★★★

Evening primrose, scientifically known as Oenothera biennis, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for its striking yellow blossoms. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is most famous for its oil, which is rich in gamma-linolenic acid. This plant offers therapeutic benefits for skin health, hormonal balance, and inflammatory conditions.
