Alantsilodendron pilosum

Alantsilodendron pilosum, commonly known as the Velvet-Leafed Healer, is a rare medicinal plant prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Found primarily in high-altitude tropical forests, its soft, hairy leaves contain unique bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to reduce inflammation and support respiratory health through natural healing.
