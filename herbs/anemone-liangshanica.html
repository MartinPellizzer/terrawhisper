What's the taxonomical classification of Anemone liangshanica?

Anemone liangshanica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Ranunculales. This species is a member of the family Ranunculaceae, falling under the genus Anemone, and is specifically identified by its species name, liangshanica.
