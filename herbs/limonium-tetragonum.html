Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of the Eastern Hemisphere. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. Its range extends significantly through Eastern Asia, where it can be found in both Japan and Korea. Furthermore, the species inhabits the Nansei-shotō islands located within the Eastern Asian maritime area. Finally, its reach extends as far as the Southwestern Pacific, specifically documented in New Caledonia.
