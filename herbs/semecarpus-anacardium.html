Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the cashew, possesses several components utilized in various medicinal and culinary applications. The most recognizable parts are the cashew nuts, which are harvested from the accessory fruit, though the true botanical nut is found within the fleshy cashew apple. Beyond the fruit, the stem bark is often employed in traditional medicine for its astringent properties. The seeds contained within the shell are highly potent, and various leaf extracts are frequently studied for their bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic benefits.
