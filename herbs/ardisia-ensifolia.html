Ardisia ensifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Ardisia ensifolia?

Ardisia ensifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its hierarchical lineage continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Ericales. Specifically, this plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, falling under the genus Ardisia, with its specific designation being the species ensifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ardisia ensifolia has lanceolate leaves that are typically 5 to 15 centimeters long, arranged oppositely along the stem, and characterized by a coriaceous texture with entire margins. The foliage is deep green on the upper surface and often exhibits a slightly paler hue underneath. The plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that emerge from the leaf axils in clusters. Following pollination, it develops small fleshy fruits that are often used to distinguish the species. The overall growth habit is that of a woody shrub or small tree with a compact structure.
