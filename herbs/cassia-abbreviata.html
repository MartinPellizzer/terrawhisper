Long-tail Cassia, scientifically known as Cassia abbreviata, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its potent purgative properties, the plant's leaves and bark are traditionally used to treat digestive ailments. Modern research continues to explore its rich bioactive compounds for potential pharmacological applications worldwide.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. Moving toward the northeast, the species can be found in the tropical landscapes of Somalia. Its range extends further into East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits both Kenya and Tanzania.
