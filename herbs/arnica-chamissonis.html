Chamisso's arnica, scientifically known as Arnica chamissonis, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and parts of Europe. Renowned for its bright yellow, daisy-like blooms, this plant is highly valued in traditional herbalism for its potent anti-inflammatory properties, particularly when used topically to soothe bruises and swelling.
