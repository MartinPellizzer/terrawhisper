Arnica chamissonis

Arnica chamissonis, commonly known as Chamisson's arnica, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, it is prized in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory properties, frequently utilized in topical preparations to alleviate muscle aches, bruising, and swelling effectively.

  • Scientific name: Arnica chamissonis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Arnica.

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several northern regions of the world. In Europe, its presence can be found in Sweden and within the mountainous landscapes of Austria.
