Croton kongensis

Croton kongensis, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, holds significant importance in traditional ethnobotanical practices. Found primarily in tropical regions, this plant is valued for its bioactive compounds. Researchers are increasingly exploring its phytochemical properties to unlock potential therapeutic applications in treating various inflammatory and infectious ailments.

  • Scientific name: Croton kongensis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Croton kongensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Croton.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. In China, it can be found within the South-Central regions as well as on the island of Hainan. Its range extends further into the Indo-China peninsula, where it is native to Laos. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Myanmar. Finally, its presence is also confirmed in the country of Thailand.
