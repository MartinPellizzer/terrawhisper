Rubia manjith

Rubia manjith, commonly known as Indian Madder, is a cherished botanical in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent blood-purifying properties, this perennial herb features vibrant red roots used to treat skin disorders, inflammation, and detoxification. It remains a vital component in traditional healing for restoring systemic balance and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Rubia manjith
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Rubia.
