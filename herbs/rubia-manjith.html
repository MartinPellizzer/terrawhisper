Rubia cordifolia, scientifically known as Rubiia manjith, is a perennial climbing shrub highly valued in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent roots, it serves as a powerful blood purifier and anti-inflammatory agent. It is widely used to treat skin disorders, detoxify the liver, and promote overall blood health.
