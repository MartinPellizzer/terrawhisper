Veronica spuria

Veronica spuria, commonly known as spurry speedwell, is a delicate perennial herb native to temperate regions. Characterized by its small, bright blue flowers and creeping growth habit, this plant thrives in moist habitats. While primarily valued in botany, it plays a subtle role in supporting local meadow ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Veronica spuria
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Veronica spuria, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Plantaginae and falls under the genus Veronica.
