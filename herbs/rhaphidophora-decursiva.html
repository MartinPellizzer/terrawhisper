Rhaphidophora decursiva

Rhaphidophora decursiva, commonly known as the decussate leaf plant, is a significant medicinal species valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its unique growth patterns, this plant offers diverse therapeutic potential. Exploring its phytochemical properties provides vital insights into developing natural remedies for various ailments, highlighting its importance in ethnobotany.

  • Family: Araceae

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions of Asia. In China, it is widely distributed throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Furthermore, its range extends into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Beyond Chinese borders, the species is also found within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in Assam.
