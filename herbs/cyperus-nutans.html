Cyperus nutans

What's the taxonomical classification of Cyperus nutans?

Cyperus nutans belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It falls under the order Poales and is a member of the family Cyperaceae. Within this family, the plant is assigned to the genus Cyperus, and its specific identification is completed by its species name, nutans.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cyperus nutans has triangular stems that reach heights of up to 60 centimeters and bear terminal inflorescences consisting of many small, drooping spikelets. Each spikelet is composed of numerous individual florets that give the flower head a delicate, nodding appearance. The plant's reproductive structures are clustered in a way that allows the entire head to sway easily in the wind. This morphology is characteristic of many sedges found in wetland environments.
