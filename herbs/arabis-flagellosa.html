What's the taxonomical classification of Arabis flagellosa?

Arabis flagellosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Brassicales. This plant is further categorized into the family Brassicaceae, which places it within the genus Arabis. Ultimately, its specific biological identity is defined by its species designation, flagellosa.
