Stephania yunnanensis

Stephania yunnanensis, commonly known as a traditional medicinal climber, is a significant botanical species found in Southeast Asian regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is frequently utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising therapeutic potential for modern research into natural bioactive compounds.
