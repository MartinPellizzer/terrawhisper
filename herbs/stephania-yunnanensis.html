What's the taxonomical classification of Stephania yunnanensis?

Stephania yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is placed in the subclass Magnoliidae, which leads into the order Ranunculales. This plant is a member of the family Menispermaceae and is categorized under the genus Stephania, specifically identified as the species yunnanensis.

Stephania yunnanensis has a tuberous rootstock that serves as a storage organ, paired with climbing stems that feature opposite, cordate to ovate leaves with entire margins. Its inflorescences consist of small, delicate flowers that emerge from the leaf axils, often appearing in clusters. The study analyzed differences in the content of crebanine and several other BIAs across roots, stems, and leaves. These enzymes exhibit different activities toward (S)- and (R)-configured substrates and demonstrate strict stereoselectivity during the formation of aporprhine.
