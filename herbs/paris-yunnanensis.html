Paris yunnanensis

Paris yunnanensis, commonly known as Yunnan Paris, is a significant medicinal herb native to high-altitude regions of China. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive alkaloids used to treat inflammation, fever, and various infections. This potent plant plays a vital role in herbal pharmacology and holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Paris yunnanensis
  • Family: Melanthiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Melanthiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Paris, specifically as the species Paris yunnanensis.

Common Names

This plant, Paris yunnanensis, is known by several different names depending on the regional linguistic context. In Chinese, it is referred to by the common names 云南重楼 (zh), 云南重楼 (zh-hans), and 云南重楼 (zh-cn), as well as the traditional characters 雲南重樓 (zh-tw). Additionally, it is also frequently identified by the name 滇重楼 (zh).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found growing throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range also extends northward into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented within the state of Assam. Additionally, it is widely distributed throughout the East Himalaya region.
