Paris yunnanensis, scientifically known as Paris yunnanensis, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the high-altitude forests of China. Valued in traditional medicine, its rhizomes contain bioactive alkaloids used to treat inflammation, fever, and various systemic ailments. This rare plant remains a significant subject in modern ethnopharmacological research.
