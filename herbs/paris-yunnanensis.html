Paris yunnanensis, scientifically known as Paris yunnanensis, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the high-altitude forests of China. Valued in traditional medicine, its rhizomes contain bioactive alkaloids used to treat inflammation, fever, and various systemic ailments. This rare plant remains a significant subject in modern ethnopharmacological research.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found growing throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range also extends northward into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented within the state of Assam. Additionally, it is widely distributed throughout the East Himalaya region.
