Schisandra propinqua

Schisandra chinensis, commonly known as Magnolia Berry or Wu Wei Zi, is a powerful adaptogen native to East Asia. Renowned in Traditional Chinese Medicine, this versatile botanical helps balance bodily functions, reduces stress, and enhances cognitive focus. Its unique ability to support multiple organ systems makes it a vital medicinal powerhouse.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra propinqua
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Austrobaileyales. Finally, it is a member of the family Schisandraceae within the genus Schisandra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across various regions of East and South Asia.
