Onoclea struthiopteris

Onoclea struthiopteris, commonly known as ostrich fern, is a perennial plant prized for its edible fiddleheads and medicinal potential. Historically used in folk medicine to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments, this nutrient-rich fern offers significant bioactive compounds, making it a subject of interest in modern herbal pharmacology and nutrition.

  • Scientific name: Onoclea struthiopteris
  • Family: Aspleniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized into the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, falling within the family Aspleniaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed by its placement in the genus Onoclea.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Denmark. In addition to this, it is present within the territories of Finland and Norway. The species also occupies significant habitats across Great Britain. Finally, its geographical range extends to the island of Ireland.
