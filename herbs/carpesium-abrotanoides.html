(PCA) exerts anti-breast cancer activity by inhibiting the expression of glycolysis-related genes like glucose transporter 1, lactate dehydrogenase A, and hexokinase 2. These findings demonstrate that PCA has a noticeable anticancer effect against breast cancer in vitro by regulating glucose metabolism and PKM2 expression. This indicates that PCA is a promising agent for the development of functional food containing Carpesium abrotanoides L. root extracts.
