Primula elatior

Oxlip

Primula elatior, commonly known as the oxlip, is a delicate perennial wildflower native to Europe's damp grasslands. Renowned for its pale yellow blossoms, this species holds significant botanical interest. Historically utilized in traditional herbalism, it serves as a vital component in studying the therapeutic properties of the Primula genus.

  • Scientific name: Primula elatior
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Primula, and its specific species name, Primula elatior.
