Monarda lindheimeri · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (163 studies) · ★★★★☆

Monarda lindheimeri, scientifically known as Monarda lindheimeri, is a vibrant perennial herb celebrated for its striking, tubular flowers and aromatic foliage. Often called Bee Balm, this medicinal plant is traditionally used in herbalism to support digestive health, soothe respiratory issues, and provide antimicrobial benefits through its potent essential oils.
