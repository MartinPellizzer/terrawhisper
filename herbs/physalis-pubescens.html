Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as groundcherry, possesses several distinct morphological features that contribute to its medicinal profile. The stems are typically slender and branched, providing the necessary structural support for the plant's growth. Attached to these are the green, ovate leaves, which play a crucial role in photosynthesis. The most distinctive feature of Physalis pubescens is its fruit, which is enclosed within a papery, bladder-like calyx. Once matured, these individual fruits become fleshy and edible, representing the reproductive culmination of the plant's life cycle.
