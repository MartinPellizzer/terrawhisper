Physalis pubescens

Physalis pubescens, commonly known as groundcherry or husk tomato, is a versatile perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its edible, golden berries encased in papery calyxes, this plant offers significant nutritional value and traditional medicinal uses, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, making it a botanical treasure.

  • Scientific name: Physalis pubescens
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Solanales, and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Physalis.
