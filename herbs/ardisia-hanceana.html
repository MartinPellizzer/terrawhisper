Ardisia hanceana

What's the taxonomical classification of Ardisia hanceana?

Ardisia hanceana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further organized into the order Ericales. It is a member of the family Primulaceae, falling under the genus Ardisia, and is specifically identified by its species name, hanceana.

According to a study published by "Journal of ethnopharmacology", Ardisia hanceana Mez, known as Da-luo-san, is commonly used in folk medicine for the treatment of rheumatism. As part of the Luo-san group, this plant exhibits activity that was found to be better at 0.2 mg/mL. The activity of the Luo-san group was significantly stronger than that of the Zou-ma-tai group with a p<0.001. Within the 328 metabolites identified from the roots of the three plants, flavonoids were identified as main differentially accumulated metabolites. The relative contents of flavonoids were significantly higher in the Luo-san group, which may serve as key active ingredients for the treatment of rheumatic diseases.
