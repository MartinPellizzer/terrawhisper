Ligularia sagitta

Ligularia sagitta, commonly known as the arrowleaf ligularia, is a striking perennial herb native to East Asian wetlands. Characterized by its large, arrow-shaped leaves and vibrant yellow daisy-like flowers, this plant thrives in moist habitats. It is valued in traditional practices and as an ornamental for its dramatic foliage.

  • Scientific name: Ligularia sagitta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Ligularia.

Common Names

This plant, Ligularia sagittata, is known by several different common names across various Chinese linguistic contexts. In general Chinese nomenclature, it is referred to as 橐吾 (Tuó wú) or 卵叶橐吾 (Luǎn yè tuó wú). More specific regional and character variations include 箭叶橐吾 (Jiàn yè tuó wú), which is used in standard Chinese (zh), Simplified Chinese (zh-cn), and Traditional Chinese (zh-tw) as 箭葉橐吾 (Jiàn yè tuó wú).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions within China. It can be found growing in the landscapes of China South-Central and the northern stretches of Inner Mongolia. Additionally, its presence extends into the Manchuria region. The species is also documented in the areas of China North-Central. Finally, its range includes the high-altitude territories of Qinghai.
