Schwenckia americana

Schwenckia americana, commonly known as the woody ginger, is a fascinating tropical perennial native to the rainforests of Central and South America. Distinguished by its striking, large leaves and delicate blooms, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally used in various cultures to treat diverse ailments.

  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
