Limeberry, scientifically known as Micromelum minutum, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and fever. Its nutrient-rich berries and bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential.
