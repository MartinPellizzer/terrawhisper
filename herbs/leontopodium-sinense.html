What's the taxonomical classification of Leontopodium sinense?

Leontopodium sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical structure, the plant is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It further falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, the taxonomic identity is completed by its designation within the genus Leontopodium and the specific species sinese.
