Duboisia leichhardtii

Duboisia leihehardtii, commonly known as the medicinal duboisia, is a significant Australian shrub valued for its potent alkaloid content. Primarily utilized in pharmaceutical industries, it serves as a crucial source of scopolamine and hyoscine. These bioactive compounds are essential for developing treatments for motion sickness and various neurological conditions.
