Queensland duboisia, scientifically known as Duboisia leichhardtii, is a distinctive shrub native to the rainforests of Australia. Renowned for its high alkaloid content, particularly scopolamine and hyoscyamine, this medicinal plant serves as a vital biological source for pharmaceutical compounds used in treating various neurological and gastrointestinal conditions.
