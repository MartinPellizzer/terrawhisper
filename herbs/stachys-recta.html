Stachys recta

Perennial yellow woundwort

Stachys recta, commonly known as the upright bugle, is a perennial herb valued for its medicinal properties. Often found in rocky habitats, this plant is traditionally used in herbal remedies to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, owing to its unique bioactive compounds and restorative natural characteristics.

  • Scientific name: Stachys recta
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
