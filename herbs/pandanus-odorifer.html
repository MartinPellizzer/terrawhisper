Pandanus odorifer

Kewda

Pandanus odorifer, commonly known as the fragrant screwpine, is a distinctive tropical plant celebrated for its aromatic qualities. Native to lush, coastal environments, it features striking, sword-shaped leaves and unique prop roots. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its diverse therapeutic and healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Pandanus odorifer
  • Family: Pandanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Pandanus odorifer, is classified under the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it belongs to the order Pandanales and the family Pandanaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Pandanus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Pandanus odorifer, is recognized by various names across different languages and cultures. In Russian, it is referred to as пандан ароматнейший, while in Indonesia, it is commonly called Pandan laut. The traditional Chinese name is 露兜樹, and in the South Indian languages of Tamil and Kannada, it is known as கைதை and ಕೇದಿಗೆ, respectively.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across several key tropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted within the Congo. Moving toward East Tropical Africa, it can be found in Tanzania, while also inhabiting Mozambique in South Tropical Africa. Its distribution extends to the Western Indian Ocean, specifically on the island of Aldabra. Finally, the species reaches the Arabian Peninsula, where it is located in Yemen.
