Ribbon Bush, scientifically known as Hypoestes aristata var. aristata, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to South Africa. Renowned for its striking, narrow foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues.
