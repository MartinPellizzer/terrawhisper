Cape silver willow, scientifically known as Salix mucronata, is a distinctive riparian shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its shimmering, silvery foliage and slender form, it thrives in moist habitats. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this resilient species plays a vital role in stabilizing riverbanks and supporting local ecosystems.
