Salix mucronata

Salix mucronata, commonly known as the mucronate willow, is a resilient shrub native to diverse southern African landscapes. Renowned for its distinctive pointed leaf tips, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments like inflammation and pain.

  • Scientific name: Salix mucronata
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Salicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation concludes with the genus Salix.
