Cotoneaster integerrimus

Cotoneaster integerrimus, commonly known as the Smooth-leaved Cotoneaster, is a resilient evergreen shrub valued for its ornamental beauty. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this plant holds significance in traditional ethnobotany. Its dense foliage and hardy nature make it a fascinating subject for studying botanical properties and diverse ecological roles.
