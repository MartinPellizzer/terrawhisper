Grindelia scorzonerifolia

Grindelia scorzonerifolia, commonly known as gumweed, is a resilient perennial herb valued for its potent medicinal properties. Native to various North American regions, this plant is traditionally utilized for its expectorant qualities. It is frequently employed in herbal remedies to soothe respiratory ailments, alleviate coughs, and support overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Grindelia scorzonerifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling into the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Grindelia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the southern regions of South America. Its presence is specifically noted in the northeastern parts of Argentina. Furthermore, it can be found throughout the country of Paraguay. The species also inhabits the territory of Uruguay. Finally, its range extends into the southern portion of Brazil.
