Glinus lotoides

Glinus lotoides, commonly known as the creeping lotoides, is a resilient medicinal herb frequently found in tropical regions. Valued in traditional healing practices, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties used to treat various ailments. Its ability to thrive in diverse habitats makes it a significant subject for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Glinus lotoides
  • Family: Molluginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Caryophyllales and placed in the family Molluginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Glinus as Gliunus lotoides.
