Monarda media

Monarda media, commonly known as Bee Balm or Bergamot, is a resilient perennial prized for its vibrant, tubular flowers and aromatic foliage. Renowned in herbal medicine for its antiseptic and digestive properties, this plant offers both ecological beauty for pollinators and therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Monarda media
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Monarda.
