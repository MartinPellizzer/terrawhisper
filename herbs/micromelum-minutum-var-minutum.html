Micromelum minutum var. minutum

Micromelum minutum var. minutum, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Belonging to the Lauraceae family, this plant is valued for its bioactive compounds. Traditional healers utilize its leaves and stems to treat diverse ailments, highlighting its potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Micromelum minutum var. minutum
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Sapindales, specifically within the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Micromelum as the variety minutum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically appearing in the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh. These various locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse subtropical environments.
