Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically appearing in the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh.
