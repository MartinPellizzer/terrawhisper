Cyperus orthostachyus

What's the taxonomical classification of Cyperus orthostachyus?

Cyperus orthostachyus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Poales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Cyperaceae and is categorized under the genus Cyperus, eventually reaching its specific designation as the species orthostachyus.
