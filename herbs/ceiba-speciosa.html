Silk Floss Tree, scientifically known as Ceiba speciosa, is a striking tropical tree celebrated for its sculptural trunk and vibrant, bell-shaped flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various parts used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues.
