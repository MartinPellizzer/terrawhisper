Ferula szowitsiana, commonly known as a rare member of the Apiaceae family, is a medicinal plant indigenous to specific arid regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Research focuses on its bioactive compounds, which may provide valuable antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties for pharmacology.
