Morina nepalensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Morina nepalensis?

Morina nepalenis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is categorized under the order Dipsacales and is a member of the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific genus, Morina, and its unique species designation, nepalensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Morina nepalensis has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by erect, unbranched stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters. The leaves are primarily basal and arranged in a rosette, featuring an oblong to lanceolate shape with serrated or dentate margins. Small, tubular flowers are clustered into dense, terminal heads that often appear in shades of pink or purple. These inflorescences are supported by bracts that may possess fine hairs or glandular structures. The root system consists of a stout, woody rhizome that allows the plant to persist in its high-altitude Himalayan habitat.
