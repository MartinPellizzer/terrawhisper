Achillea abrotanoides

Achillea abrotanoides, commonly known as yellow yarrow, is a distinctive perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Characterized by its bright yellow flower heads, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its aromatic properties and therapeutic potential, often utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive and inflammatory health.

  • Scientific name: Achillea abrotanoides
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
