Indian-buttertree, scientifically known as Diploknema butyracea, is a versatile medicinal tree native to South Asia. Renowned for its nutrient-rich seeds and bark, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, this plant serves as a vital resource in Ayurvedic healing practices.
