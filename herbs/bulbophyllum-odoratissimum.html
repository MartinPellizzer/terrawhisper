Bulbophyllum odoratissimum

What's the taxonomical classification of Bulbophyllum odoratissimum?

Bulbophyllum odoratissimum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales. This plant is a member of the family Orchidaceae, specifically situated within the genus Bulbophyllum, and is identified by the unique species name odoratissimum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Bulbophyllum odoratissimum has creeping rhizomes with small, ovate pseudobulbs that bear single, leathery leaves at their apex. The inflorescence consists of a slender, arching scape that produces multiple small, fragrant flowers. Each flower features a distinctively shaped labellum and delicate, yellowish petals that contribute to its intense scent. These plants typically develop in clusters as the rhizomes spread across their substrate. The overall structure is compact and specialized for epiphytic growth in humid environments.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant requires high humidity levels above 70 percent, bright filtered light, and consistent warmth between 65 and 85 degrees Fahrenheit to thrive.

Growers should use a well-draining orchid medium like bark or sphagnum moss to prevent root rot. Water the plant frequently to keep the substrate moist, but ensure the potting container allows for adequate air circulation. Regular applications of a balanced orchid fertilizer during the growing season will support vigorous growth and future flowering.
