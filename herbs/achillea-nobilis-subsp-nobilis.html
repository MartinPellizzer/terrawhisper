Noble Yarrow, scientifically known as Achillea nobilis subsp. nobilis, is a distinguished perennial herb renowned for its fragrant, feathery foliage and delicate white flower clusters. Prized in traditional herbalism, this subspecies offers potent therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antiseptic qualities, making it a valuable asset in natural medicine.
