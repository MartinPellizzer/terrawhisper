Achillea nobilis subsp. nobilis

Achillea nobilis subsp. nobilis, commonly known as Noble Yarrow, is a distinguished perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic foliage and delicate white blooms. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this subspecies offers potent therapeutic properties, frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing capabilities within various natural medicinal formulations.

  • Scientific name: Achillea nobilis subsp. nobilis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Achillea.

Common Names

This plant, Achillea nobilis subsp. nobilis, is known by various common names across different linguistic traditions. In English, it is frequently referred to as noble yarrow, while in German, it is called edles Schafgarben. In French, the species is called yarrow noble, and in Spanish, it is known as milenrama noble.
