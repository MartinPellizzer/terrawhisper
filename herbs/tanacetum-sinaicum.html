Tanacetum sinaicum

Tanacetum sinaicum, commonly known as Chinese Tansy, is a versatile medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. This aromatic perennial is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and respiratory discomfort, offering a natural approach to holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Tanacetum sinaicum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Tanacetum.
