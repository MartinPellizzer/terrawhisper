Ranawara, scientifically known as Senna auriculata, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Widely used in Ayurveda, it is celebrated for its potent diuretic, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This vibrant yellow-flowered plant plays a crucial role in treating kidney ailments, skin disorders, and managing diabetes naturally.
