Stephania japonica

Stephanania japonica, commonly known as the Japanese Stephanania, is a climbing medicinal vine native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is valued for its unique bioactive compounds. Research explores its potential therapeutic properties, particularly in addressing various ailments, highlighting its significance in botanical pharmacology and herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Stephania japonica
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Menispermaceae within the genus Stephania.
