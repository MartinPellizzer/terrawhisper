Pandanus amaryllifolius, scientifically known as Pandanus amaryllifolius, is a versatile tropical plant celebrated for its aromatic, pandan-scented leaves. Widely used in Southeast Asian cuisine, it serves as a natural flavoring agent. Beyond culinary applications, this plant offers significant medicinal potential, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial therapeutic properties.
