Pandanus amaryllifolius

Pandanus amaryllifolius, commonly known as Pandan, is a versatile tropical plant prized for its aromatic, nutty fragrance. Beyond its culinary importance, it serves as a potent medicinal herb in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and diuretic properties, it is widely used to support metabolic and digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Pandanus amaryllifolius
  • Family: Pandanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Pandanus amaryllifolius, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Pandanales and the family Pandanaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic hierarchy places it within the genus Pandanus.
