Paeonia × suffruticosa

Paeonia × suffruticosa, commonly known as the tree peony, is a stunning perennial prized for its ornamental beauty and traditional medicinal properties. Renowned in various cultures, its roots are utilized in herbal formulations to address inflammation, promote blood circulation, and soothe nervous tension, blending aesthetic elegance with therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia × suffruticosa
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified in the family Paeoniaceae and the genus Paeonia.
