Moutan peony, scientifically known as Paeonia × suffruticosa, is a striking perennial celebrated for its ornamental beauty and potent medicinal properties. Renowned in traditional practices, its roots contain bioactive compounds used to address inflammation, promote blood circulation, and alleviate menstrual discomfort, making it a vital botanical in herbal medicine.
