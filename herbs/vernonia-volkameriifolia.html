What's the taxonomical classification of Vernonia volkameriifolia?

Vernia volkameriifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Asterales, the plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, which places it within the genus Vernonia. Ultimately, its specific biological identity is defined by its species designation, volkameriifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Vernonia volkameriifolia has lanceolate to elliptic leaves that are arranged oppositely along a woody, upright stem. The leaf margins are typically finely serrated or entire, depending on the specific maturity of the specimen. The foliage exhibits a deep green color with prominent venation that extends from the midrib toward the edges. Small, tubular flowers emerge from terminal heads, often displaying a purplish or pale hue. These floral structures are supported by bracts that protect the developing reproductive organs.
