Lancea tibetica, scientifically known as Lancea tibetica, is a significant medicinal shrub native to the high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional Tibetan medicine, it is widely utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, serving as a vital natural remedy for treating various ailments and promoting overall wellness.
