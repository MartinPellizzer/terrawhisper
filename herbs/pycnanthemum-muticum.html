Pycnanthemum muticum

Pycnanthemum muticum, commonly known as bitter pycnanthemum, is a perennial herb native to North America. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this aromatic plant is prized for its fragrant foliage and nectar-rich flowers. It serves as a vital pollinator magnet, supporting diverse beneficial insects within many ecological landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Pycnanthemum muticum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pycnanthemum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Pycnanthemum muticum, is referred to by several different common names depending on the region and language.
