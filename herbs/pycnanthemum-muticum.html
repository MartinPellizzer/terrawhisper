Blunt mountain-mint, scientifically known as Pycnanthemum muticum, is a versatile perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic, serrated leaves and white flower clusters, it serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators. This hardy plant is prized in medicinal traditions and ecological landscaping for its resilience.
