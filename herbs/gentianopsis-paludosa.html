Swamp gentian, scientifically known as Gentianopsis paludosa, is a striking perennial herb native to East Asian wetlands. Characterized by its brilliant, star-shaped blue flowers, this rare species thrives in acidic marshes. Renowned for its aesthetic beauty, it holds significant botanical interest and potential medicinal value in traditional practices.
