Gentianopsis paludosa

Gentianopsis paludosa, commonly known as the bog gentian, is a rare, perennial herb native to high-altitude wetlands. Distinguished by its striking, deep-blue bell-shaped flowers, this medicinal plant thrives in peat bogs. Historically valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses unique phytochemical properties used for their potent therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Gentianopsis paludosa
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Gentianopsis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Gentianopsis paludosa, is recognized by several different common names depending on the linguistic context. In Chinese, it is referred to as 湿生扁蕾, 沼生扁蕾, or 假斗格尔布. Specifically, in Mainland China, the name used is 湿生扁蕾 (zh-cn), while in Taiwan, it is identified as 濕生扁蕾 (zh-tw).
