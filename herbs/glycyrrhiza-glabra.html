Glycyrrhiza glabra · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (2665 studies) · ★★★★★

Liquorice, scientifically known as Glycyrrhiza glabra, is a perennial legume renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Derived from the plant's flavorful root, it has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to soothe digestive issues, alleviate coughs, and reduce inflammation, making it a versatile staple in herbal pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, Glycyrrhiza glabra, is a perennial herb primarily valued for its medicinal properties found within its underground parts. The most significant component is the root, which is harvested and processed to become the well-known licorice root used in various remedies. While the roots contain the highest concentration of glycyrrhizin, the plant also features distinctive green leaves that characterize its visible growth above the soil.
