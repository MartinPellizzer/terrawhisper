liquorice

Glycyrrhiza glabra · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (2665 studies) · ★★★★★

Liquorice, scientifically known as Glycyrrhiza glabra, is a perennial legume renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Derived from the plant's flavorful root, it has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to soothe digestive issues, alleviate coughs, and reduce inflammation, making it a versatile staple in herbal pharmacology.
