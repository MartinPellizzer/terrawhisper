Glycyrrhiza glabra

Glycyrrhiza glabra, commonly known as licorice, is a perennial legume celebrated in traditional medicine for centuries. Renowned for its sweet, distinctive root, it possesses powerful anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and soothing properties. Widely used to treat digestive issues and respiratory ailments, this versatile botanical remains a cornerstone of herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Glycyrrhiza glabra
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Glycyrrhiza glabra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Glycyrrhiza.
