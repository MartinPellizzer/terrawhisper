Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Boophone disticha, is a unique medicinal species characterized by its specialized underground structures. The most significant part of the plant is its large, perennial bulb, which serves as a storage organ for nutrients and is frequently utilized in traditional medicine. While some related species may possess smaller bulbs or offsets, the primary bulb of this plant is its most distinctive feature. In addition to its underground storage, the plant produces long, strap-like leaves that emerge directly from the base, forming a striking rosette pattern.
