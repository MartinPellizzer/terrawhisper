Garuga pinnata, scientifically known as Garuga pinnata, is a versatile medicinal tree native to the tropical regions of Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its bark and leaves are extensively utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections, inflammation, and gastrointestinal disorders, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical value.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range spanning parts of East and South Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast regions. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species is found in the state of Assam and across the East Himalaya. Additionally, its distribution extends into the neighboring country of Bangladesh. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where this medicinal species thrives.
