Maerua subcordata

Maerua subcordata, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in Southeast Asia, is a woody climber valued for its therapeutic properties. Utilized extensively in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark are often employed to treat various ailments, including fever and inflammation, offering valuable insights into natural pharmacological healing.

  • Scientific name: Maerua subcordata
  • Family: Capparaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Maerua subcordata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Brassicales. It is a member of the family Capparaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Maerua.
