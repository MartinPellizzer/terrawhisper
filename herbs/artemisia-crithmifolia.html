Artemisia crithmifolia

Artemisia crithmifolia, commonly known as the bitter wormwood, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and Middle East. Renowned for its aromatic, silver-green foliage, this plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, reduce inflammation, and act as a potent natural antimicrobial agent.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia crithmifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artemisia, specifically as the species Artemisia crithmifolia.
