Silene flos-cuculi

Silene flos-cuculi, commonly known as sticky catchfly, is a charming perennial wildflower characterized by its delicate pink blossoms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal in meadows, this plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism. It is studied for its unique phytochemical properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits within various medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Silene flos-cuculi
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Caryophyllaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic designation falls under the genus Silene.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Silene flos-cuculi, is referred to by various common names across different languages and regions. In the Netherlands, it is called echte koekoeksbloem (nl), while in Norway, it is known as Hanekam (nn). In the Czech Republic, it goes by the name kohoutek luční (cs), and in Slovenia, it is called kukavičja lučca (sl). Additionally, in North Macedonia, this species is recognized as обична руменка (mk).
