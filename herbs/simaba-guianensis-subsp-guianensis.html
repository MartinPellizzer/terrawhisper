Simaba guianensis subsp. guianensis

Simaba guianenensis subsp. guianenensis, commonly known as Guayacán, is a significant medicinal plant native to the tropical rainforests of South America. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering immense pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Simaba guianensis subsp. guianensis
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, taxonomically classified as Simaba guianenis subsp. guianenis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Sapindales and the family Simaroubaceae. It is specifically identified as a member of the genus Simaba.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of South America. In the northern part of the continent, it can be found inhabiting French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname. Its range extends into Western South America as well. Specifically, it is documented growing within the borders of Colombia. Furthermore, the species has been identified as part of the natural flora in Bolivia.
