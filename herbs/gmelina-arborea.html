Beechwood, scientifically known as Gmelina arborea, is a versatile, fast-growing deciduous tree prized in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it offers diverse benefits for skin health, digestion, and inflammation. Beyond its medicinal value, its timber and leaves serve essential roles in traditional ecological and holistic practices.
