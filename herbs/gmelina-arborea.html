Beechwood

Gmelina arborea · Accepted scientific name

Beechwood, scientifically known as Gmelina arborea, is a versatile, fast-growing deciduous tree prized in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it offers diverse benefits for skin health, digestion, and inflammation. Beyond its medicinal value, its timber and leaves serve essential roles in traditional ecological and holistic practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, Gmelina arborea, possesses several parts that are utilized for their various therapeutic properties. The large, green leaves are frequently used in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as inflammation and skin issues, while the roots are valued for their ability to act as a tonic and aid in digestive health. The small, fragrant flowers often serve as indicators of the plant's seasonal vitality and contribute to its overall medicinal profile. The sturdy stems provide the structural integrity necessary for the tree to reach significant heights, and the thick stem bark is particularly prized for its medicinal extracts, often used to treat fever, coughs, and various internal discomforts.
