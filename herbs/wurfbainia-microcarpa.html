Wurfbainia microcarpa

Wurfbainia microcarpa, commonly known as a species within the Magnoliaceae family, is a rare medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers potential therapeutic benefits. Exploring its pharmacological properties provides vital insights into developing natural treatments for various health conditions and ailments.

Taxonomical Classification

It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, falling within the family Zingiberaceae.
