Canarium album

Canarium album, commonly known as the White Kanarium, is a significant tropical tree valued for its versatile medicinal and economic properties. Native to Southeast Asian rainforests, it produces nutrient-rich resins and oils used traditionally to treat skin ailments, promote wound healing, and support overall wellness through natural bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Canarium album
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Burseraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Canarium, specifically the species Canarium album.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within Eastern Asia. In China, it can be found across the South-Central regions and throughout the Southeast. Additionally, its presence is noted on the island of Hainan. Beyond mainland China, the species extends to the island of Taiwan. Finally, its range includes the neighboring country of Japan.
