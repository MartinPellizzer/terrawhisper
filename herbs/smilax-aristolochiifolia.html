Mexican sarsparilla, scientifically known as Smilax aristolochiifolia, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its roots are prized for their tonic properties. Often used to support blood purification and digestive health, this resilient plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies worldwide.
