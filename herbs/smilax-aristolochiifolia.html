Smilax aristolochiifolia

Smilax aristolochiifolia, commonly known as Sarsaparilla, is a climbing woody vine prized for its medicinal properties. Historically used to treat skin ailments, rheumatism, and blood purification, its roots contain valuable saponins. This versatile plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotany, valued for its restorative benefits in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Smilax aristolochiifolia
  • Family: Smilacaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Liliales, it is further organized into the family Smilacaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Smilax, specifically as the species Smilax aristolochiifolia.
