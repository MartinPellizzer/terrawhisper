River bushwillow

River bushwillow, scientifically known as Combretum erythrophyllum, is a resilient deciduous tree native to African riverine ecosystems. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional healing to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, offering significant ethnobotanical value and therapeutic potential.
