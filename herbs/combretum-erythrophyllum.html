Combretum erythrophyllum

Combretum erythrophyllum, commonly known as the red-leafed Combretum, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to African landscapes. Renowned for its vibrant foliage, this species is deeply integrated into traditional healing practices. It is frequently utilized for its potent bioactive compounds to treat various ailments, showcasing significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Combretum erythrophyllum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Combretaceae and the genus Combretum. Specifically, the medicinal species is known as Combretum erythrophyllum.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within the southern part of the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving into the Southern Africa region, it can be found growing in Botswana. The species also extends its reach into the Cape Provinces. Finally, its geographical range includes the arid landscapes of Namibia.
