River bushwillow

Combretum erythrophyllum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (20 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

River bushwillow, scientifically known as Combretum erythrophyllum, is a resilient deciduous tree native to African riverine ecosystems. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional healing to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, offering significant ethnobotanical value and therapeutic potential.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within the southern part of the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving into the Southern Africa region, it can be found growing in Botswana. The species also extends its reach into the Cape Provinces. Finally, its geographical range includes the arid landscapes of Namibia.
