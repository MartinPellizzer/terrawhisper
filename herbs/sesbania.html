Sesbania

Sesbania, commonly known as the kaupil or vegetable sesbania, is a versatile leguminous plant valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is used to treat various ailments. This resilient species supports soil fertility while offering significant nutritional and therapeutic benefits in many tropical regions.

  • Scientific name: Sesbania
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and specifically categorized into the genus Sesbania.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several European regions. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in both Belgium and Germany. Moving towards the Mediterranean, it can be found within Southwestern Europe. Specifically, this species inhabits the islands of Corse and Sardegna. Furthermore, its range extends to include the mainland territory of France.
