Monotropa uniflora

Indian pipe

Monotropa uniflora, commonly known as Ghost Pipe or Indian Pipe, is a unique, non-photosynthetic perennial plant. Lacking chlorophyll, it derives nutrients from fungi rather than sunlight. This ethereal, translucent white plant thrives in shaded forest floors, offering a mysterious presence that has long fascinated herbalists and botanical enthusiasts alike.

  • Scientific name: Monotropa uniflora
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Monotropa uniflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Monotropa.
