Indian pipe, scientifically known as Monotropa uniflora, is a unique, ghostly white plant that thrives in shaded forest floors. Lacking chlorophyll, it survives through a parasitic relationship with fungi. Highly valued in traditional herbalism, this ethereal perennial is celebrated for its striking appearance and diverse medicinal applications.
