What's the taxonomical classification of Eurya nitida?

Eurya nitida belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Ericales. It is specifically situated within the family Pentaphylacaceae, falling under the genus Eurya, and is identified by the specific epithet nitida.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Eurya nitida has small, evergreen leaves that are oblong to ovate in shape, possessing a glossy, dark green upper surface and a paler underside with finely serrated margins. The leaf texture is typically coriaceous, meaning it feels leathery to the touch, which helps the plant retain moisture. These leaves are arranged alternately along the woody stems of the shrub or small tree. Tiny, white, actinomorphic flowers emerge from the axils of the leaves, often appearing in small clusters. Following pollination, the plant produces small, globose drupes that transition from green to a deep red or black color when mature.
