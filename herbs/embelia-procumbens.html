Embelia procumbens

What's the taxonomical classification of Embelia procumbens?

Embelia procumbens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and belongs to the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, specifically situated within the genus Embelia, and is identified by its unique species name, procumbens.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Embelia procumbens has a prostrate, creeping woody habit characterized by slender, branched stems that spread across the ground. The leaves are small, elliptic to lanceolate in shape, and possess entire margins with an alternate arrangement along the stems. Tiny, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the axils, typically presenting in small clusters or solitary units. The plant produces small, fleshy drupes that transition in color as they reach maturity. These structural features allow the species to thrive as a groundcover in its natural forest habitats.
