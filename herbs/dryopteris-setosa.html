Dryopteris setosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Dryopteris setosa?

Dryopteris setosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. This fern is a member of the family Polypodiaceae, falling under the genus Dryopteris, and is specifically identified by its species name, setosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dryopteris setosa has bipinnate-pinnatifid fronds that reach up to 60 centimeters in length, featuring distinctively bristly or setose stipes and rachises. The pinnae are lanceolate in shape and possess serrated margins that often taper toward the tips. A defining characteristic is the presence of stiff, hair-like scales located along the rachis and the underside of the segments. The texture of the foliage is relatively thin, yet the overall structure remains robust due to the supporting central axis. These morphological traits distinguish the species from other members of the Dryopteridaceae family.
