Ibicella lutea

Ibicella lutea, commonly known as the Golden Ibicella, is a vibrant medicinal herb prized for its bright yellow blooms and therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it is often utilized to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. This resilient plant serves as a vital resource in natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Ibicella lutea
  • Family: Martyniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Martyniaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Ibicella.

Common Names

This plant is known by various names depending on the language and region. In Portuguese and Spanish, it is referred to as Ibicella, while in Swedish, the common name is Ibicellor. It is also called Ibicella in Cebuano, and in Japanese, it is known as キバナツノゴマ.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several European regions. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing in Austria, Germany, and Belgium. Its presence extends into Southwestern Europe, where it is documented in France. Additionally, the species inhabits the island of Sardegna within the same southwestern territory. Together, these locations highlight the varied habitats where the plant thrives.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ibiceilla lutea, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts, which include the stems, leaves, and flowers. These above-ground structures contain the concentrated bioactive compounds necessary for its therapeutic properties. Because the medicinal efficacy is concentrated in these visible components, the aerial parts are typically harvested, dried, and processed to create various herbal remedies.
