Distribution

This plant, Eurycoma longifolia (commonly referred to as Eurycoma harmandiana in certain taxonomical contexts), exhibits a specific range within the Indo-China region. Its natural habitat is primarily concentrated in the tropical landscapes of Southeast Asia. Specifically, the species can be found growing across the country of Cambodia. It also maintains a significant presence throughout the various terrains of Thailand. Furthermore, its geographical distribution extends into the forested areas of Vietnam.
