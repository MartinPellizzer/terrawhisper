Eurycoma harmandiana

Eurycoma harmandiaana, commonly known as the "African Bitterwood," is a vital medicinal shrub native to the Sahelian regions of Africa. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, the plant is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including malaria, fever, and digestive issues, through the application of its bitter bark.

  • Scientific name: Eurycoma harmandiana
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is placed in the family Simaroubaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Eurycoma.

Distribution
