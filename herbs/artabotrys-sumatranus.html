Artabotrys sumatranus

Artabotrys sumatranus, commonly known as the Sumatran climbing vine, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned for its fragrant blossoms, this woody climber is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat various ailments, offering valuable bioactive compounds that hold great potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Family: Annonaceae
