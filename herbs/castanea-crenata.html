Japanese Chestnut

Japanese Chestnut, scientifically known as Castanea crenata, is a deciduous tree native to East Asia. Renowned for its edible nuts, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. Its leaves and bark are often utilized for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering diverse therapeutic potential in natural medicine.
