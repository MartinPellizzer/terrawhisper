Pfaffia glomerata

Pfaffia glomerata, commonly known as Sinapá or Brazilian Ginseng, is a powerful adaptogenic herb native to South America. Renowned for its revitalizing properties, it helps balance hormones, reduces fatigue, and boosts the immune system. This medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional herbalism for enhancing overall vitality and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Pfaffia glomerata
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, falling under the family Amaranthaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Pfaffia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Pfaffia glomerata, is recognized by various regional and common names depending on the language and local context. In Portuguese, it is frequently referred to as Ginseng-brasileiro, while in Persian, it is documented as پفافیا گلومراتا. Interestingly, in Spanish, the plant is associated with several different names, including Alternanthera glauca, Gomphrena dunaliana, and Gomphrena glauca.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of Northern South America. Specifically, it can be found growing within the territories of French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname. Its presence extends further into the region through the country of Venezuela. Additionally, the species reaches into Western South America, where it is documented in Bolivia. This broad range highlights the plant's ability to thrive in various tropical environments across the continent.
