Illigera aromatica

Illigera aromatica, commonly known as the aromatic illigera, is a climbing woody shrub native to Southeast Asian forests. Renowned for its distinct, fragrant foliage, this medicinal plant is utilized in traditional practices to treat various ailments, offering significant ethnobotanical value through its unique bioactive compounds and therapeutic potential.
