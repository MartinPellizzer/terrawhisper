Bulbine capitata

Bulbine capitata, commonly known as Bulbine or Bulbine plant, is a succulent perennial native to South Africa. Renowned for its fleshy, bright green leaves, this hardy species is highly valued in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties in various topical applications.

  • Scientific name: Bulbine capitata
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Bulbine capitaata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asphodelaceae and is situated within the genus Bulbine.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions within Southern Africa. It can be found growing in the landscapes of Botswana and Namibia. Additionally, its presence extends to the province of KwaZulu-Natal. The species is also documented within the Free State region. Finally, its geographical range includes the territory of Eswatini.
