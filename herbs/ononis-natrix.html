Ononis natrix

Yellow restharrow

Ononis natrix, commonly known as the common nettle, is a versatile medicinal herb found across various climates. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, it has been used traditionally to support kidney health, combat allergies, and enrich the body with essential vitamins, making it a staple in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Ononis natrix
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Ononis.
