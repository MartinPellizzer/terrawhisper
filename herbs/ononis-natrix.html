Yellow restharrow, scientifically known as Ononis natrix, is a perennial herb belonging to the Fabaceae family. Characterized by its bright yellow flowers and small leaflets, this plant thrives in dry, calcareous soils. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it possesses diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties used to support urinary health.
