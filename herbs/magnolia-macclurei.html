Magnolia macclurei

Magnolia maccluerei, commonly known as the Maclure's Magnolia, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific subtropical regions, it features fragrant blossoms and robust foliage. Traditionally used in holistic practices, this species offers significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia macclurei
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Magnoliales and the family Magnoliaceae. Ultimately, Magnolia maccluerei is identified by its genus, Magnolia.
