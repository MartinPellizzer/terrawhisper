Retama raetam subsp. gussonei

Retama raetam gussonei

Retama raetam subsp. gussonei, commonly known as a variant of the white broom, is a resilient shrub native to arid Mediterranean regions. Renowned for its medicinal potential, it contains bioactive alkaloids and flavonoids used in traditional therapies to treat respiratory issues, inflammation, and various ailments through its unique chemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Retama raetam subsp. gussonei
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Retama as the subspecies gussonei.
