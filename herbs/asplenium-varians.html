Asplenium varians

What's the taxonomical classification of Asplenium varians?

Asplenium varians belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Polypodiinae and follows the order Polypodiales. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues into the family Aspleniaceae, falling under the genus Asplenium, and is specifically identified as the species varians.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Asplenium varians has a creeping rhizome that produces pinnate fronds with lanceolate leaflets characterized by wavy or undulating margins. These leaves typically exhibit a variable coloration that transitions between shades of green and brownish hues depending on environmental conditions. The pinnules are arranged oppositely along the rachis and feature prominent midveins that extend toward the tips. Small sori are located on the underside of the leaflets, tucked neatly beneath the protective flaps of the leaf edges. This morphological variation in leaf shape and color is a defining trait of the species.
