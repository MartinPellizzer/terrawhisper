Astilbe chinensis

Astilbe chinensis, commonly known as Chinese Astilbe, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its delicate, plume-like flowers, this plant is utilized to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and inflammation, offering a unique blend of ornamental beauty and medicinal significance.

  • Scientific name: Astilbe chinensis
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Astilbe.
