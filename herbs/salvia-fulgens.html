Salvia fulgens

Salvia fulgens, commonly known as Scarlet Sage, is a vibrant perennial prized for its stunning, tubular red flowers. While primarily used in ornamental landscaping to attract pollinators, its botanical lineage suggests potential medicinal properties. This resilient plant adds breathtaking color to gardens while embodying the diverse heritage of Salvia species.
