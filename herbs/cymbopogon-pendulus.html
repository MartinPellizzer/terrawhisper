Jammu lemongrass, scientifically known as Cymbopogon pendulus, is a highly valued perennial grass celebrated for its potent therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils like citral, this aromatic herb is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat fever, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits for wellness.
