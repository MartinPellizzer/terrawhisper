Lonicera ligustrina

What's the taxonomical classification of Lonicera ligustrina?

Lonicera ligustrina belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Dipsacales. This plant is further categorized into the family Caprifoliaceae, which places it within the genus Lonicera, specifically as the species ligustrina.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lonicera ligustrina has deciduous, opposite leaves that are ovate to elliptical in shape, measuring 1 to 3 centimeters in length, with entire margins and obtuse or acute tips. The stems are woody and often arching, sometimes displaying a reddish hue as they grow. During the flowering season, it produces small, tubular, white to pale pink flowers that emerge from the leaf axils. These blossoms eventually transition into fleshy, oblong berries that change from red to black upon ripening. The overall structure of the shrub typically reaches heights of 1 to 2 meters.
