Pterygilia cylindrica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Lamiales, leading to its placement in the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Pterygella and the specific epithet cylindrica, forming its complete scientific name.
