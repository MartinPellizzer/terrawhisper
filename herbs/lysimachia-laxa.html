Lysimachia laxa

Lysimachia laxa, commonly known as the loose-flowered loosestrife, is a perennial herb distinguished by its delicate, yellowish blooms. Native to diverse habitats, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its potential therapeutic properties. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for identifying its unique role within medicinal ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Lysimachia laxa
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is a member of the family Primulaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Lysimachia.
