Asparagus taliensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Asparagus taliensis?

Asparagus taliensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Asparagales, the plant is a member of the family Asparagaceae. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy is completed by its designation within the genus Asparagus, specifically as the species taliensis.
