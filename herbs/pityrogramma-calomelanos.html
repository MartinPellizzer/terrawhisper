Pityrogramma calomelanos

Pityrogramma calomelanos, commonly known as the fern brake, is a medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it has been traditionally utilized for treating skin ailments and parasitic infections. Modern research continues to explore its diverse phytochemical properties and significant potential in pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Pityrogramma calomelanos
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, it is situated in the family Pteridaceae. Its taxonomic identification is completed by its placement in the genus Pityrogramma.
