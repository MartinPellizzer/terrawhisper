Salvia urmiensis

Salvia urmiensis, commonly known as a potent medicinal sage, is a rare plant native to the rugged terrains of Pakistan. Renowned in traditional herbal medicine, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Research explores its potential therapeutic applications, particularly for its antioxidant and antimicrobial activities, making it a subject of interest.

  • Scientific name: Salvia urmiensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Salvia. As a member of the Salvia urmiensis species, it represents a specific medicinal lineage within this diverse group.

Common Names

This plant, Salvia urmiensis, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context of its native habitats. In Persian, it is commonly referred to as sage or mirzam, while in Urdu, it is identified as salvia urmiensis.
