Atriplex centralasiatica

Atriplex centralasiatica, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in Central Asian flora, holds profound ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant is utilized in traditional remedies to address various ailments. Understanding its therapeutic properties offers promising insights into modern pharmacology and natural disease management.

  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Atriplex centralasiatica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae within the genus Atriplex.
