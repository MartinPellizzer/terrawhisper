Thalictrum orientale

Thalictrum orientale, commonly known as Oriental Meadow-rue, is a delicate perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Characterized by its airy, fern-like foliage and charming white flower clusters, this plant thrives in moist, shaded environments. It serves as a beautiful addition to shade gardens and supports local biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Thalictrum orientale
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized within the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Thalictrum, specifically as the species Thalictrum orientale.
