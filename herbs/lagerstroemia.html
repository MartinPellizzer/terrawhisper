This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found specifically within the Comoros. Its range extends into Central Asia, where it is located in Turkmenistan, Tajikistan, and Uzbekistan. Additionally, the species is present in China, specifically throughout the South-Central region.
