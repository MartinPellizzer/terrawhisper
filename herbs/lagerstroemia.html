Lagerstroemia

Crepe myrtles

Lagerstroemia, commonly known as Crape Myrtle, is a versatile genus celebrated for its vibrant, crinkled blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, various species hold significant medicinal value in traditional practices. They are frequently utilized for their anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties, offering therapeutic potential in treating diverse ailments.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, falling into the family Lythraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Lagerstroemia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lagerstroemia, is referred to by various names depending on the region and language. In German, it is called Lagerströmien, while in Indonesian and Javanese, it is commonly known as Bungur. In Komi, the names are written as Лагерстрёмия, which is also the designation used in Komi-Permyak.
