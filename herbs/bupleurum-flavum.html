Bupleurum flavum

Bupleurum flavum, commonly known as Golden Bupleurum, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds like saikosaponins. These constituents offer potent anti-inflammatory, hepatoprotective, and immunomodulatory properties, making it a significant plant for treating liver ailments and supporting immune health.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum flavum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Bupleurum, specifically as the species Bupleurum flavum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of Europe and Asia. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing within the territories of Bulgaria and Greece. It is also present in the European portion of Türkiye. Moving into Western Asia, the species inhabits the East Aegean Islands. Finally, the plant's range extends further into the main territory of Türkiye.
