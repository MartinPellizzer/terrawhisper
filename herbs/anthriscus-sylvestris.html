Anthriscus sylvestris

Cow parsley

Anthriscus sylvestris, commonly known as cow parsley, is a delicate biennial herb characterized by its lace-like white umbels. Often found in hedgerows and woodlands, this plant holds significant ecological value. While primarily ornamental, its historical use in traditional herbalism highlights its presence in various folk medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Anthriscus sylvestris
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Anthriscus.
