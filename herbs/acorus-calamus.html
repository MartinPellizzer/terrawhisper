Acorus calamus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (785 studies) · ★★★★★

Acorus roseau, scientifically known as Acorus calamus, is a perennial wetland plant prized for its aromatic rhizomes. Historically significant in traditional medicine, it is utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Often found in marshy habitats, this versatile species offers diverse applications in both herbal remedies and perfumery.
