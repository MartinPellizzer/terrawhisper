Commonly referred to as sweet marjoram, scientifically known as Clinopodium suaveolens, is a fragrant aromatic herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent essential oils, this plant offers significant antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. It is frequently utilized to support digestive health and alleviate various inflammatory conditions naturally.

Distribution

This plant, known as Clinopodium suaveolens, is primarily found throughout various regions of Southeastern Europe.
