Picea brachytyla

Picea brachytyla, commonly known as the short-needle spruce, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique botanical properties. Found in specific ecological niches, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to explore applications in traditional remedies, highlighting its importance in natural pharmacology and herbal medicine.
