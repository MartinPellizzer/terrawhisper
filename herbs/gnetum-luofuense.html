Gnetum luofuense

What's the taxonomical classification of Gnetum luofuense?

Gnetum luofuense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized under the subclass Gnetidae and the order Gnetales. Within this lineage, it is placed in the family Gnetaceae, falling under the genus Gnetum, and is specifically identified by the species name luofuense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Its leaves are typically ovate to lanceolate in shape and possess a relatively thin texture. The study focuses on the ripening of Gnetum seeds where many carbohydrates, proteins and fibers accumulate. Researchers assembled a full-length transcriptome from immature and mature G. luofuense seeds using PacBio sequencing reads. This analysis identified 5726 novel genes, 9061 alternative splicing events, 3551 lncRNAs, 2160 transcription factors, and 8512 genes possessing at least one poly(A) site. Gene expression comparisons of six transcriptomes showed that 14,323 genes were differentially expressed, with 7891 genes upregulated and 6432 genes downregulated. The expression of 14 differentially expressed transcription factors from the MADS-box, Aux/IAA and bHLH families was validated by qRT-PCR.
