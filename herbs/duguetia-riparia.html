Duguetia riparia

Duguetia riparia, commonly known as the riparian duguetia, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical forest ecosystems. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Its unique chemical profile continues to attract scientific interest for pharmaceutical development.

  • Scientific name: Duguetia riparia
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Magnoliales and the family Annonaceae. Within this botanical hierarchy, it is identified by the genus Duguetia.

Common Names

This plant, Duguetia riparia, is known by various common names depending on the region and local language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the riverine custard apple, while in Spanish, it is commonly called guanábana de monte or guanábana de río.
