Eugenia tinifolia

Eugenia tinifolia, commonly known as the tiniifolia or small-leafed Eugenia, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Eugenia tinifolia
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Eugenia.
