Phyllanthus graveolens subsp. benthamianus

Phyllanthus graveolens subsp. benthamianus, commonly known as various local names depending on the region, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this subspecies is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin conditions, and inflammatory issues, offering vital botanical benefits in ethnopharmacology.

  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
