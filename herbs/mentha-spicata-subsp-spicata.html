Spear Mint

Mentha spicata subsp. spicata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (3 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Spear Mint, scientifically known as Mentha spicata subsp. spicata, is a fragrant perennial herb celebrated for its refreshing aroma and versatile therapeutic properties. Widely used in culinary, cosmetic, and medicinal applications, this plant offers soothing relief for digestive issues and serves as a potent natural source of menthol.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Mentha. Specifically, the taxon is identified as the subspecies spicata.
