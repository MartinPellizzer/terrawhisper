Protium javanicum

Protium javanicuum, commonly known as Breu Branco, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its aromatic oleoresin, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and healing properties. Traditionally used by indigenous communities, this versatile resin offers significant potential in modern pharmacology for treating various skin and systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Protium javanicum
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Protium javanicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales. It is a member of the Burseraceae family and falls under the genus Protium.

Distribution

This plant is native to the expansive region of Malesia. Within this broad area, its specific presence is noted on the island of Jawa. It can also be found throughout the Lesser Sunda Islands. These locations represent the primary natural habitats where the species thrives. Consequently, its distribution is concentrated within these specific Southeast Asian archipelagos.
