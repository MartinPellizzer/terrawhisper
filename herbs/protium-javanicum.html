Distribution

This plant is native to the expansive region of Malesia. Within this broad area, its specific presence is noted on the island of Jawa. It can also be found throughout the Lesser Sunda Islands. These locations represent the primary natural habitats where the species thrives. Consequently, its distribution is concentrated within these specific Southeast Asian archipelagos.
