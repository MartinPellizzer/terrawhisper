Shiranji-tea

Woodfordia fruticosa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (122 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Shiranji-tea, scientifically known as Woodfordia fruticosa, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Widely utilized in Ayurvedic medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Renowned for treating digestive issues and respiratory ailments, this resilient plant serves as a vital natural remedy in traditional healing.
