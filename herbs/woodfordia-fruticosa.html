Woodfordia fruticosa

Woodfordia fruticosa, commonly known as Shirish or Fire-flame Bush, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South Asia. Renowned for its astringent properties, it is widely used in traditional Ayurveda to treat wounds, digestive issues, and skin ailments. Its vibrant red flowers also hold significant cultural and ecological importance.

  • Scientific name: Woodfordia fruticosa
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Africa
