Syzygium densiflorum

Syzygium densiflorum, commonly known as the pink clove or tropical rose apple, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, its leaves, bark, and fruits are traditionally used to treat digestive ailments, skin inflammations, and various systemic infections in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium densiflorum
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
