Acokanthera oblongifolia

Acokanthera oblongifolia, commonly known as the Poison Bush, is a significant medicinal plant native to African regions. Renowned for its potent cardiac glycosides, it holds a dual nature: while highly toxic if misused, its alkaloids are studied for pharmacological potential in treating heart conditions and managing complex biochemical processes.

  • Scientific name: Acokanthera oblongifolia
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Acokanthera.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within certain regions of the African continent.
