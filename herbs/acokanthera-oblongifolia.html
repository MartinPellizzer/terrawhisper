Acokanthera oblongifolia ex, scientifically known as Acokanthera oblongifolia, is a potent medicinal shrub native to East Africa. Renowned for its highly toxic cardiac glycosides, it has been used traditionally for hunting arrows and specialized medicine. While culturally significant, its extreme toxicity requires extreme caution and professional handling during use.
