Dioscorea oppositifolia

Dioscorea oppositifolia, commonly known as purple yam, is a versatile tuberous plant native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its nutritional density, it serves as a vital dietary staple. Beyond cooking, its bioactive compounds offer significant medicinal potential, particularly in supporting metabolic health and providing antioxidant protection.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea oppositifolia
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dioscoreales, it is classified under the family Dioscoreaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Dioscorea as Dioscorea oppositifolia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing throughout the diverse landscapes of India and its neighboring nation, Bangladesh. Its presence is also noted in the specific state of Assam, contributing to the local biodiversity. Furthermore, the species extends into the mountainous terrain of the East Himalaya. Finally, its geographical range reaches as far south as the island of Sri Lanka.
