Indian Yam

Dioscorea oppositifolia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (83 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Indian Yam, scientifically known as Dioscorea oppositifolia, is a perennial climbing herb valued for its medicinal properties. Native to various regions, this tuberous plant is rich in diosgenin, a precursor for hormone synthesis. It is traditionally used to support reproductive health, balance hormones, and manage various inflammatory conditions.

Its presence is also noted in the specific state of Assam, contributing to the local biodiversity. Furthermore, the species extends into the mountainous terrain of the East Himalaya.
