Calophyllum lanigerum

Calophyllum lanigerum, commonly known as a species within the Calophyllaceae family, is a notable medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Research focuses on its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, highlighting its growing importance in traditional and modern ethnopharmacology.

  • Family: Calophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, its taxonomic position is completed within the family Calophyllaceae and the genus Calophyllum.
