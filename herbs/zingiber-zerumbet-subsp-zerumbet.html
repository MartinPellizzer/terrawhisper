Zingiber zerumbet subsp. zerumbet

Zingiber zerumbet subsp. zerumbet, commonly known as shampoo ginger, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it contains bioactive compounds with potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. This versatile plant is widely utilized for treating skin ailments, digestive issues, and promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Zingiber zerumbet subsp. zerumbet
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Zingiber as the subspecies zerumbet.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found inhabiting the islands of Comoros and Madagascar. Its presence extends into Eastern Asia, where it is specifically located in Taiwan. Furthermore, the species is well-established within the various provinces of China. Specifically, it thrives throughout both South-Central and Southeast China.
