Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions of Eastern Asia. In the northern parts of China, it is notably found within the Manchuria region. Its range extends further east to include the islands of Japan. Additionally, the species is naturally occurring throughout Korea. Together, these locations define the natural habitat of Angelica acuutiloba.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Angelica acuutiloba, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its underground structures. The most significant medicinal component is the root, often referred to in herbal pharmacopoeias as radix or Angelica Root. While some species in this genus may feature a thickened rhizome, the therapeutic potency is concentrated within the extensive roots that develop deep within the soil. Whether referred to by its botanical name or its common medicinal designation, the quality of the root is essential for its pharmacological efficacy.
