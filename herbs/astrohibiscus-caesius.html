Astrohibiscus caesius

Astrohibiscus caesius, commonly known as the Blue Hibiscus, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, this shrub features striking blue blossoms. It is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and provide antioxidant benefits for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Astrohibiscus caesius
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae within the genus Astrohibiscus.
