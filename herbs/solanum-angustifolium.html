Solanum angustifolium

Solanum angustifolium, commonly known as the narrow-leaved nightshade, is a resilient medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its distinct foliage, this species contains bioactive compounds used to address various ailments. Understanding its botanical properties and therapeutic potential offers vital insights into natural pharmacology and ethnobotany.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several distinct regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its range extends through Germany and into the areas of Czechia and Slovakia. It can also be found growing within the borders of Hungary. Moving toward the Mediterranean, the species is distributed throughout Southwestern Europe, specifically in France.
