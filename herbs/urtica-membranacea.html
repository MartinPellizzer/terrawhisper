Mediterranean Nettle, scientifically known as Urtica membranacea, is a distinctive herbaceous perennial native to the Mediterranean basin. Renowned for its stinging hairs and nutrient-rich leaves, this plant has long been valued in traditional herbalism. It offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly for supporting digestive health and reducing systemic inflammation.
