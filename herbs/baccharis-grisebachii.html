Baccharis grisebachii

Baccharis grisebachii, commonly known as "chilca," is a resilient shrub native to South American landscapes. Renowned in traditional medicine, this versatile plant is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antiseptic, and healing properties. It serves as a vital natural remedy for treating skin ailments, wounds, and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Baccharis grisebachii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
