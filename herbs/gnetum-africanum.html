Gnetum africanum

Okazi

Gnetum africanum, commonly known as African nutmeg or afitin, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African rainforests. Renowned for its nutritional value and therapeutic properties, its seeds and leaves are extensively used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Gnetum africanum
  • Family: Gnetaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Gnetidae and the order Gnetales, it is classified under the family Gnetaeceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gnetum, specifically as the species Gnetum africanum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West-Central Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its range includes the Central African Republic and Cameroon. It can also be found growing within the borders of Congo and Gabon. Furthermore, the species is widely present in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. These specific locations define its primary natural habitat within the African continent.
