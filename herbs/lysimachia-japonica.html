Lysimachia japonica

Lysimachia japonica, commonly known as creeping loosestrife, is a perennial herbaceous plant valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its spreading habit and yellow blossoms, it is historically utilized for its anti-inflammatory and astringent properties. While often considered invasive, it remains a subject of interest in botanical medicine studies.

  • Scientific name: Lysimachia japonica
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Lysimachia.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In the Caucasus, its presence is specifically noted within the Transcaucasus area. Its distribution extends into Western Asia, where it can be found in Türkiye. Within China, the species inhabits both the Hainan region and China Southeast. Finally, it is a documented part of the flora in Eastern Asia, particularly in Japan.
