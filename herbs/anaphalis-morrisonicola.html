Anaphalis morrisonicola

Anaphalis morrisonicola, commonly known as the mountain daisy, is a resilient medicinal herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, reduce inflammation, and promote healing, making it a significant botanical resource in ethnopharmacology.
