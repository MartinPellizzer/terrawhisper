Spiked spiral-flag, scientifically known as Costus spicatus, is a striking tropical perennial celebrated for its vibrant, cone-like inflorescences. Native to the Americas, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties, offering a natural approach to supporting overall wellness and healing.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several distinct regions of Mexico. It can be found throughout the Northeast, Gulf, Northwest, and Southeast portions of the country. In addition to its widespread presence in Mexico, its habitat extends into the Caribbean. Specifically, the species is documented to grow within the island of Cuba. Plant Parts

This plant, known for its therapeutic properties, utilizes various components for medicinal use. The large, lanceolate leaves are frequently harvested for their bioactive compounds, and sometimes a single leaf or a specific leave is prepared in decoctions to treat ailments. Below the surface, the thick, fleshy rhizomes serve as a primary source of essential oils and nutrients, while the deeper root structure helps stabilize the plant and stores vital energy.
