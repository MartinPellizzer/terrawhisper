Huperzia fargesii

Huperzia fargesii, commonly known as Farges' clubmoss, is a medicinal lycopod valued in traditional Chinese medicine. It is primarily recognized for its high concentration of huperzine A, a potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. This compound is extensively researched for its potential to enhance cognitive function and treat neurodegenerative disorders.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia fargesii
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Lycopodiidae. It is further classified within the order Lycopodiales and the family Lycopodiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Huperzia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions in East and Southeast Asia. In China, it can be found primarily throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends further into Eastern Asia, where it is native to both Japan and Taiwan. Additionally, the species is distributed within the Indo-China region, specifically in Vietnam. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where this medicinal species flourishes.
