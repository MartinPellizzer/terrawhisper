Ipomoea hederacea

Ipomoea hederaacea, commonly known as Ivy-leaved Morning Glory, is a versatile creeping vine valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical properties, this plant is frequently utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, offering significant therapeutic promise in treating various ailments within diverse ethnobotanical practices globally.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea hederacea
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Ipomoea.
