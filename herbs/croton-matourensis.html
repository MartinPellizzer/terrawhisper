Croton matourensis

Croton matourensis, commonly known as Matourens, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, its leaves and bark possess significant bioactive properties. Often utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, this plant serves as a vital resource in community-based herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Croton matourensis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is specifically situated within the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Croton under the species name Croton matourensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution spanning across Central and Northern South America. In the Central American region, its presence is documented in Costa Rica and Panamá. Moving into Northern South America, it can be found inhabiting the tropical landscapes of French Guiana. The species is also widely distributed throughout the territories of Guyana. Finally, its range extends to include the nation of Suriname.
