Asystasia intrusa

Asystasia intrusa, commonly known as the creeping oxytropis, is a resilient herbaceous plant native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for exploring its potential medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Asystasia intrusa
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Asystasia intrusa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Asystasia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Within this broad region, its presence is documented in the nation of Benin.
