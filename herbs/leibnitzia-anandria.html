What's the taxonomical classification of Leibnitzia anandria?

Leibnitzia anandria belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, falling under the genus Leibnitzia, and is specifically identified by its species name, anandria.

According to a study published by "Yakugaku zasshi : Journal of the Pharmaceutical Society of Japan", Tu-er-feng is used in Sichuan province for the common cold with cough and rheumatism. The study compares the internal morphologies of the leaves and roots of several species, including G. anandria, which is also known as Leibnitzia anandria. Researchers examined G. piloselloides, G. delaavay, G. nivea, G. anandria, and G. jamesonii to clarify the botanical origin. Based on the analysis of Tu-er-feng obtained from 16 recent markets, G. piloselloides was determined as the botanical origin.
