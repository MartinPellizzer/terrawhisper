Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the vast regions of Siberia. It can be found growing in the mountainous terrain of the Altai region. Additionally, its presence is documented throughout the Republic of Buryatia. The species also inhabits the eastern territories of Chita. Finally, its geographical range extends into the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions.
