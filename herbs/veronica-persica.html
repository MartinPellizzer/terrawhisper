Persian speedwell, scientifically known as Veronica persica, is a low-growing annual herb characterized by its delicate blue flowers and small, oval leaves. Often found in gardens and disturbed soils, this resilient plant has been utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse healing properties and its calming, restorative effects.
