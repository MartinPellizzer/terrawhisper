Veronica persica

Veronica persica, commonly known as bird's-eye speedwell, is a versatile annual herb belonging to the Plantaginaceae family. Widely distributed across Europe and Asia, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties. Its small, blue flowers are distinctive markers of its widespread natural presence.

  • Scientific name: Veronica persica
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Plantaginaceae and is identified by the genus Veronica.
