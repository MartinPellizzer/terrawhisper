Acer griseum

Paperbark maple

Acer griseum, commonly known as the trident maple, is a distinctive deciduous tree prized for its striking, peeling bark and vibrant autumnal foliage. Beyond its ornamental value, it holds significance in traditional practices, offering unique phytochemical properties that continue to inspire contemporary research into its potential medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Acer griseum
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Sapindaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Acer.
