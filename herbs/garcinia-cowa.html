Garcinia cowa

Garcinia cowa, commonly known as the Malabar Gamboge, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Western Ghats of India. Renowned for its bioactive xanthones, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Traditionally used in folk medicine, this species holds immense potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia cowa
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Malpighiales and placed in the family Clusiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Garcinia, which includes the species Garcinia cowa.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing in the specific areas of Assam, Bangladesh, and the East Himalaya. Furthermore, its presence is well-documented throughout various parts of India. Beyond the subcontinent, the species also extends its range into South-Central China.
