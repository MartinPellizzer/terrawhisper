River Pumpkin, scientifically known as Gunnera perpensa, is a perennial herb native to arid regions of South America. Renowned for its large, fleshy leaves and medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat digestive issues and inflammation. This hardy plant thrives in seasonally wet soils, offering significant ethnobotanical value.

Plant Parts

This plant, Gunnera perpensa, is a medicinal herb where the primary therapeutic component is found within its underground structures. The most significant parts used for medicinal purposes are the rhizomes, which are thick, fleshy stems that grow horizontally beneath the soil surface. When prepared for traditional remedies, these dry rhizomes are often processed to extract their active compounds, providing the essential medicinal properties characteristic of the species.
