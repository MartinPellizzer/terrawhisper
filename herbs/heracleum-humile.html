Heracleum humile

Heracleum humile, commonly known as the dwarf hogweed, is a distinctive perennial herb native to East Asian mountain regions. While less celebrated than its larger relatives, this plant holds significant botanical interest. Its unique morphology and potential phytochemical properties make it a subject of study within traditional herbal medicine.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant, known scientifically as Heracleum humile, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Heracleum.
