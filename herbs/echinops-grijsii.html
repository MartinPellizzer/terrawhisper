Echinops grijsii

Echinops grijsii, commonly known as a unique member of the thistle family, is a medicinal plant distinguished by its striking spherical flower heads. Native to specific ecological niches, it is valued in traditional herbal practices for its potential therapeutic properties and its significant role within regional biodiversity and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Echinops grijsii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Echinops.

Common Names

This plant, Echinops grijsii, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 漏卢 (lòu lú), while in traditional Chinese, it is written as 漏盧. These same characters, 漏盧, are also used in the Lezheng dialect (lzh). Additionally, in some Taiwanese contexts (zh-tw), the plant is commonly known by the name 山防風.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across various regions of East Asia. Within China, its distribution is extensive, covering the South-Central and North-Central territories. It can also be found growing in the Manchuria region and throughout China Southeast. Furthermore, the species extends its range to the island of Taiwan. Together, these locations highlight a broad geographical footprint throughout the eastern part of the continent.
