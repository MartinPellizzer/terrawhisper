Berberis thunbergii

Berberis thunrBergii, commonly known as Indian Barberry, is a deciduous shrub prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its high berberine content, this plant offers potent antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. It is widely used to support metabolic health, improve digestion, and treat various skin and liver ailments.

  • Scientific name: Berberis thunbergii
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales and the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Berberis.
