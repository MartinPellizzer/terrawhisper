Japanese barberry, scientifically known as Berberis thunbergii, is a deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant red berries and ornamental appeal, it also holds significant value in traditional medicine. Its bark and roots contain berberine, a potent alkaloid used for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties.
