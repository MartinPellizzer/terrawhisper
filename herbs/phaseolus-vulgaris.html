String bean, scientifically known as Phaseolus vulgaris, is a versatile legume celebrated for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in essential proteins, vitamins, and minerals, it supports digestive health and blood sugar regulation. This widely cultivated plant serves as a vital dietary staple, offering significant antioxidant and therapeutic benefits.
