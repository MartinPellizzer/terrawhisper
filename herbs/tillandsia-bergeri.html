Tillandsia bergeri

Tillandsia bergeri, commonly known as the Berger's air plant, is a captivating epiphytic species native to Central America. Thriving without soil, it absorbs moisture and nutrients through specialized trichomes. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this resilient plant is studied for its unique ecological role and potential bioactive medicinal properties.

  • Family: Bromeliaceae
