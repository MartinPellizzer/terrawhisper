Dalbergia

Dalbergia, commonly known as rosewood, is a genus of medicinal plants renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like flavonoids and tannins, various species offer potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial benefits. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, Dalbergia plays a vital role in holistic healing practices worldwide.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, placing it in the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Dalbergia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as rosewood, is highly valued in traditional medicine and craftsmanship, with its therapeutic properties primarily concentrated in the heartwood. This dense, central core of the stem is prized for its rich chemical composition, which is often utilized in herbal formulations to address various ailments. Because the heartwood is more durable and chemically potent than the sapwood, it serves as the most effective part of the Dalbergia species for extracting the medicinal compounds necessary for its healing applications.
