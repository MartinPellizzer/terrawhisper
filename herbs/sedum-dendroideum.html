Tree Stonecrop, scientifically known as Sedum dendroideuum, is a resilient succulent valued in traditional medicine. Primarily used for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, it is frequently applied topically to treat skin irritations, wounds, and minor burns. This hardy perennial offers diverse therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and healing.
