Astianthus viminalis

Astianthus viminalis, commonly known as the "Red-flowered Astianthus," is a striking medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, it is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat inflammation and promote blood circulation. This versatile species remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical research today.

  • Scientific name: Astianthus viminalis
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Bignoniaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Astianthus.
