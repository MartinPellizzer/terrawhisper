Veronicastrum sibiricum

Veronicastrum sibiricum, commonly known as Siberian Speedwell, is a resilient perennial herb native to the temperate regions of Asia. Valued in traditional herbalism, this plant is recognized for its unique botanical characteristics and potential therapeutic properties, offering a fascinating subject for those exploring natural medicine and regional biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Veronicastrum sibiricum
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Veronicastrum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical presence across several key regions of Northern Asia. Within the vast expanse of Siberia, its habitat includes the areas of Buryatiya and Irkutsk. It can also be found thriving in the eastern Siberian territories of Chita and Yakutiya. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the Russian Far East. Specifically, its distribution encompasses the Amur region, marking a significant part of its natural range.
