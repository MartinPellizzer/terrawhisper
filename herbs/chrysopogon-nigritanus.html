Chrysopogon nigritanus

Chrysopogon nigritaunus, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from inflammatory conditions to infections. Understanding its phytochemical profile is essential for exploring its potential in modern medicine.

Distribution

This plant, Chrysopogon nigritanus, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin, where it contributes to the local medicinal flora. The species is also documented in Burkina, extending its presence further into the West African landscape. Furthermore, its geographical range includes the coastal and inland areas of Gambia and Ghana. Finally, the plant's distribution continues westward into the territory of Guinea-Bissau.
