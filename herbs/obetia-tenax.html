Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the southern part of the continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving into the Southern Africa region, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Botswana. Its range also extends to the Cape Provinces, showcasing its adaptability to different climates. Finally, the species is well-established within the KwaZulu-Natal area.
