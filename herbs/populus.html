Populus

The poplar and aspen genus

Populus, commonly known as the poplar tree, holds significant medicinal value across various cultures. Its bark is a renowned source of salicin, a natural precursor to aspirin, used historically to alleviate pain and reduce inflammation. This versatile plant offers profound therapeutic potential for treating fever and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Populus
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Malpighiales and the family Salicaceae. Ultimately, this medicinal species is classified under the genus Populus.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the poplar, offers therapeutic potential through its various components, specifically the cortex and the folia. Additionally, the folia, which are the leaves, are often utilized in traditional remedies for their astringent and anti-inflammatory properties.
