Balsam fir, scientifically known as Abies balsamea, is a versatile coniferous evergreen native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic, resinous needles and healing balsam, this species holds significant medicinal value. Its extracts are traditionally used to soothe respiratory ailments, promote wound healing, and provide calming aromatherapy benefits.
