Abies balsamea

Balsam fir

Abies balsamea, commonly known as balsam fir, is a majestic coniferous tree prized for its aromatic resins. Renowned in traditional medicine, its fragrant needles and sap offer soothing properties for respiratory ailments and skin healing. This resilient species remains a cornerstone of botanical wellness and natural forest ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Abies balsamea
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales and family Pinaceae, it is classified under the genus Abiés.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as balsam fir, provides valuable therapeutic substances primarily derived from its bark and its internal secretions. The most significant medicinal component is the oleoresin, a sticky, aromatic substance produced by the tree that is harvested for its potent antiseptic and healing properties. When extracted, this resin is often used in traditional remedies to treat skin irritations or respiratory issues, while the protective layers of the bark serve as the source for various chemical compounds used in botanical applications.
