Crinum moorei

Crinum moorii, commonly known as the Moorii Lily, is a striking perennial plant native to various African regions. Renowned for its elegant, white trumpet-shaped flowers and lush foliage, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its unique bioactive properties to treat diverse health ailments.

  • Scientific name: Crinum moorei
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Crinum moorei, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Asparagales. Furthermore, it is a member of the Amaryllidaceae family and the genus Crinum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a remarkably wide geographical distribution that spans several different continents and oceanic regions. In the Atlantic, its presence can be noted within the Macaronesian islands, specifically the Azores. Its range extends significantly into Southern Africa, where it is found in both the Cape Provinces and KwaZulu-Natal. Moving toward the East, the species is also documented in Eastern Asia, specifically in Korea. Finally, its reach stretches to the Southwestern Pacific, where it inhabits the island of Nauru.
