Ophiorrhiza nicobarica

Ophiorrhiza nicobarica, commonly known as the Nicobar climbing plant, is a significant medicinal herb native to the tropical regions of India. Valued in traditional medicine, its roots possess potent bioactive properties used to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, making it a vital subject for ethnobotanical research.

  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
