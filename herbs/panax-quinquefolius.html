American ginseng

Panax quinquefolius · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1270 studies) · ★★★★★

American ginseng, scientifically known as Panax quinquefolius, is a perennial herb native to eastern North America. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this adaptogen is renowned for its ability to reduce stress, enhance cognitive function, and bolster the immune system, making it a cornerstone of holistic wellness and herbal therapy.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as American ginseng, exhibits a distinct morphology where each part contributes to its botanical profile. The leaves are palmately compound, typically consisting of three to five leaflets that spread out from a central point. Emerging from the center of these leaves are the flowers, which are small, greenish-white, and arranged in umbels. Supporting the plant is a sturdy stem that rises above the ground to hold the foliage and blooms. Most importantly, the medicinal value is concentrated in the roots, specifically the fleshy, perennial root system that develops underground over many years, forming the characteristic branched structure used in traditional medicine.
