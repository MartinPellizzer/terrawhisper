Panax quinquefolius

Panax quinquefolius, commonly known as American ginseng, is a highly valued perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is prized for its adaptogenic properties, believed to enhance energy, reduce stress, and support immune function. This potent root serves as a cornerstone of herbal wellness.

  • Scientific name: Panax quinquefolius
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is categorized under the order Apiales and the family Araliaceae. Finally, its scientific classification is completed within the genus Panax.

Distribution

This plant, known as American ginseng, is found across several specific regions in North America. In Eastern Canada, its range includes the provinces of Ontario and Québec. Moving southward, the species extends into the North-Central United States. Within this region, it can be located in the state of Illinois.
