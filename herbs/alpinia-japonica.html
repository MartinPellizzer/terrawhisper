Alpinia japonica

Alpinia japonica, commonly known as Japanese cardamom or Japanese galangal, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its aromatic rhizomes possess potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. This versatile plant is widely utilized for treating digestive issues and enhancing overall wellness naturally.

  • Scientific name: Alpinia japonica
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Alpinia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout various regions of Eastern Asia. It can be found growing across the islands of Japan and Taiwan. The species is also widely documented throughout the Korean Peninsula. Within China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast territories. Together, these locations define the specific natural range of Alpinia japonica.
