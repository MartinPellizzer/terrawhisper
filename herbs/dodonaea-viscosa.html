Dodonaea viscosa

Hopseed bush

Dodonaea viscosa, commonly known as Hop Bush, is a versatile evergreen shrub widely distributed across tropical and subtropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This hardy plant is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and various inflammatory conditions globally.

  • Scientific name: Dodonaea viscosa
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dodonaea, specifically as the species Dodonaea viscosa.

Common Names

This plant, Dodonaea viscosa, is known by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In Indonesia, it is commonly referred to as Sikil (id), while in the Malayalam language of India, it is called വ്രാളി (ml). Among the Quechua-speaking communities, it is known as Ch'akatiya (qu), and in French-speaking regions, it is called bois de reinette (fr). Additionally, in South Africa, the plant is referred to as Sandolien (af).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several diverse regions across the globe. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is notably recorded in Spain. Moving toward Northern Africa, the species can be found within the territory of Tunisia. Its range extends into Macaronesia, where it inhabits both Cape Verde and Madeira. Finally, the plant is also documented in West Tropical Africa, specifically in Benin.
