Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammation to digestive issues, offering significant potential in modern ethnopharmacology.

The reproduction of Dodonaea viscosa is characterized by a specific flowering period that typically begins in February and concludes in May.
