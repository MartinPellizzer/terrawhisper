Hopseed bush, scientifically known as Dodonaea viscosa, is a versatile evergreen shrub widely distributed across tropical and subtropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammation to digestive issues, offering significant potential in modern ethnopharmacology.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dodonaea, specifically as the species Dodonaea viscosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several diverse regions across the globe. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is notably recorded in Spain. Moving toward Northern Africa, the species can be found within the territory of Tunisia. Its range extends into Macaronesia, where it inhabits both Cape Verde and Madeira. Finally, the plant is also documented in West Tropical Africa, specifically in Benin.
