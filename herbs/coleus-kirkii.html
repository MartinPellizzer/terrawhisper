Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several major regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted within Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can also be found in Cameroon. Its range extends to East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits parts of Tanzania. Finally, the plant is distributed throughout South Tropical Africa, occurring in both Malawi and Mozambique.
