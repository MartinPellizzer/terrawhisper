Crack Open, scientifically known as Casearia decandra, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this plant possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Casearia decandra, is primarily recognized for the medicinal properties found within its leaves. These leaves are characterized by their distinct shape and texture, serving as the central component in various traditional remedies used to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Because the bioactive compounds are concentrated in the foliage, the leaves are typically harvested, dried, or prepared as infusions to harness their therapeutic potential.
