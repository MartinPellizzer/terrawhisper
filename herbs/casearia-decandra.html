Casearia decandra

Casearia decandra, commonly known as the "sweet casearia," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark possess potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions in herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Casearia decandra
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Casearia decandra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales and the family Salicaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic classification places it within the genus Casearia.

Common Names

This plant, Casearia decandra, is known by several different common names depending on the region and local language. In English, it is often referred to as the cascarilla tree, while in Portuguese, it is commonly called cascarilha. In Spanish, it is known as cascarilla, and in French, it is called cascarille.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several tropical regions. In Central America, it can be found growing in Honduras and Panamá. Its presence extends into the Caribbean, where it is located in the Dominican Republic and Haiti. Additionally, the species is documented within the Leeward Islands. This widespread range highlights its adaptability across various island and mainland environments.
