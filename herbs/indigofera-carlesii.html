Indigofera carlesii

What's the taxonomical classification of Indigofera carlesii?

Indigofera carlesii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is classified under the order Fabales. It is a member of the family Fabaceae, which is further narrowed down to the genus Indigofera, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species carlesii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Indigofera carlesii has pinnate leaves composed of several pairs of small, oval-shaped leaflets and produces clusters of small, fragrant, pea-like flowers that typically range from pink to white. The plant features woody stems that often exhibit a spreading or scrambling growth habit. Its leaves are arranged alternately along the branches, providing a dense, feathery texture to the foliage. The flowers emerge from axillary racemes and are characteristic of the Fabaceae family. Small, flattened legumes form the seed pods following successful pollination.
