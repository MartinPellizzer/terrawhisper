Callicarpa

Callicarpa, commonly known as beautyberry, is a versatile genus of flowering shrubs celebrated for its striking, jewel-like purple berries. Beyond its ornamental appeal, many species hold significant medicinal value in traditional practices, offering antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties used to treat various skin conditions and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Callicarpa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Callicarpa.
