Chrozophora tinctoria

Chrozophora tinctoria, commonly known as Dyer's Chrozophora, is a versatile medicinal and dye-producing plant native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its rich pigment, it also possesses significant therapeutic properties. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this botanical resource offers valuable phytochemical compounds for various healing and coloring applications.

  • Scientific name: Chrozophora tinctoria
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling within the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Chrozophora.
