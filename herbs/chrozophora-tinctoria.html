Turn-sole, scientifically known as Chrozophora tinctoria, is a distinctive Mediterranean shrub valued for its historical medicinal and dye-producing properties. Traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, this resilient plant also yields a rich pigment, making it a significant botanical resource in ancient pharmacology.
