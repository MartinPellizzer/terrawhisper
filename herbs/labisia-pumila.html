Labisia pumila

Labisia pumila, commonly known as Kacip Fatimah, is a medicinal herb native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned in traditional herbalism, particularly in Malaysia, it is highly valued for its phytoestrogenic properties. This plant is widely used to support women's reproductive health, hormonal balance, and overall vitality and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Labisia pumila
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is placed in the family Primulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Labisia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Labisia pumila, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In Malaysia, it is widely known as Kacip fatimah (ms), while in Indonesia, it is referred to as Rumput fatimah (id). It is also called ว่านนางตัด in Thailand (th), and in Persian, it is identified as لابیسیا پامیلا (fa).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, it can be found growing in both Cambodia and Thailand. Its presence extends significantly into the Malesia zone, where it inhabits the island of Borneo. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout the islands of Jawa and the Malay Peninsula. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various tropical environments across the region.
