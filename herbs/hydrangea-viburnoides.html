Distribution

This plant is native to several distinct regions across East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central areas as well as the Southeast. It can also be found growing on the island of Hainan. Moving toward the broader Eastern Asian landscape, the species is distributed throughout Japan. Furthermore, its range extends to the Nansei-shoto islands.
