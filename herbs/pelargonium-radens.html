Pelargonium radens

Pelargonium radens, commonly known as the scented geranium, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its aromatic foliage. Rich in essential oils, it possesses potent antibacterial and antifungal properties. Often used in traditional remedies to soothe skin irritations and support respiratory health, this resilient plant offers significant therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Pelargonium radens
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales, it is classified under the family Geraniaceae. It is further identified by the genus Pelargonium, specifically as the species Pelargonium radens.
