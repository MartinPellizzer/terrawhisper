Plant Parts

This plant, Pelargonium radens, is highly valued in aromatherapy and herbal medicine primarily for its aromatic properties derived from its various components. The aerial parts of the plant, which include the stems and leaves, serve as the main source for harvesting its potent fragrance. Within these tissues, the plant produces concentrated essential oils that are extracted through steam distillation to create therapeutic products. While the essential oil is the most commercially significant element, the delicate flower petals also contribute to the plant's overall sensory profile and traditional uses.
