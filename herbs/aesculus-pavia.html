Aesculus pavia

Red buckeye

Aesculus pavia, commonly known as the Red Buckeye, is a striking deciduous shrub native to the southeastern United States. Renowned for its brilliant, upright clusters of scarlet flowers, it serves as a vital nectar source for hummingbirds. While visually stunning, its seeds contain toxins that require careful handling.

  • Scientific name: Aesculus pavia
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is found within the genus Aesculus.
