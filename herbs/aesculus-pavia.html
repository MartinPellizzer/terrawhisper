Aesculus pavia

Aesculus pavia, commonly known as the Red Buckeye, is a striking deciduous shrub native to the southeastern United States. Renowned for its brilliant, upright clusters of scarlet flowers, it serves as a vital nectar source for hummingbirds. While visually stunning, its seeds contain toxins that require careful handling.

  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe
