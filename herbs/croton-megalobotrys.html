Croton megalobotrys

Croton megalobotrys, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub within the Euphorbiaceae family, holds immense botanical interest. Found primarily in tropical regions, this species is valued for its diverse phytochemical profile. Researchers explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications, particularly in developing new treatments for various inflammatory conditions.
