Hoary mountain-mint, scientifically known as Pycnanthemum incanum, is a resilient perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its silvery, fuzzy foliage and clusters of white flowers, this plant serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators. It is highly valued in ecological landscaping for its hardiness and medicinal potential.
