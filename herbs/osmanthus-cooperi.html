Osmanthus cooperi

What's the taxonomical classification of Osmanthus cooperi?

Osmanthus cooperi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant follows a taxonomic lineage that leads to the order Lamiales. It is further categorized under the family Oleaceae, a group known for its fragrant properties. Within this family, the plant is placed in the genus Osmanthus, and its specific biological identity is defined by the species epithet cooperi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Osmanthus cooperi has elliptic to lanceolate leaves that are dark green, leathery, and possess finely serrated margins. These evergreen leaves grow in an opposite arrangement along woody stems that form a dense, spreading shrub. The plant produces small, tubular, fragrant white flowers that emerge from the axils of the foliage. Its fruit consists of small, fleshy drupes that transition from green to a dark color upon maturity. The overall structure of the plant is compact and maintains a lush appearance throughout the year.
