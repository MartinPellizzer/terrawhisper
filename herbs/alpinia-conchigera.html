Alpinia conchigera

Alpinia concinna, commonly known as Lesser Galangal, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Native to Southeast Asia, it is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties. Its aromatic rhizomes are frequently used to treat digestive ailments and skin infections.

  • Scientific name: Alpinia conchigera
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
