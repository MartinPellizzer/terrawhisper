Balanophora japonica

Balanophora japonica, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb in East Asia, is a distinctive plant valued for its unique biological properties. Often found in specific coastal or montane habitats, it is studied for its potential therapeutic benefits, contributing to traditional knowledge and modern pharmacological research into natural compounds.
