Plant Parts

This plant, Cistus laurifolius, is characterized by its distinctive foliage, which serves as a primary source of its medicinal properties. The leaves are typically evergreen, leathery in texture, and possess an elliptical or lanceolate shape with smooth margins. These leaves are densely covered in fine hairs, which help the plant retain moisture in its native Mediterranean habitats. In herbal medicine, the leaves are often harvested and used to prepare infusions or extracts, as they contain various bioactive compounds valued for their therapeutic potential.
