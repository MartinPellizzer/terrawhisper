Cistus laurifolius

Cistus laurifolius, commonly known as Gum Rockrose, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub celebrated for its aromatic foliage and vibrant pink blooms. Highly valued in traditional herbalism, this evergreen species possesses potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, making it a significant plant in the study of natural therapeutic compounds.

  • Scientific name: Cistus laurifolius
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Cistus laurifolius, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cistaceae and falls under the genus Cistus.
