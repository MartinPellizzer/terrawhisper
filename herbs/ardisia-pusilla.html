Ardisia pusilla

Ardisia pusilla, commonly known as a small shrubby member of the Primulaceae family, is a fascinating medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, it is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat inflammation and skin ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
