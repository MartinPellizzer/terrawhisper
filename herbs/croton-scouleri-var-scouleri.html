Distribution

This plant is a specific variety of the Croton genus found in a very restricted area. Its geographical distribution is limited to the unique island ecosystems of Western South America. Specifically, this botanical variety is endemic to the Galápagos archipelago. Because it is confined to these islands, its natural range is highly localized. Consequently, the survival of this plant is closely tied to the environmental conditions of the Galápagos region.
