Little-club-moss, scientifically known as Selaginaella tamariscina, is a resilient lycophyte prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its unique ability to survive extreme desiccation, this plant contains bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, skin disorders, and respiratory issues, offering significant pharmacological potential in modern therapeutic research and development.

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions throughout China. It can be found growing in the South-Central parts of the country as well as the Southeast. Plant Parts

This plant, Selaginella tamariscina, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through the application of its aerial parts, which include the stems and leaves of the lycopod. To harness its therapeutic potential and isolate its bioactive compounds, researchers frequently employ an ethanol extract derived from these above-ground structures. This specific method of extraction allows for the concentration of various phytochemicals found within the aerial parts, making it a vital component in studies focused on the plant's pharmacological properties.
