Selaginella tamariscina

Selagineella tamariscina, commonly known as the tamarisk spike-moss, is a remarkable resurrection plant capable of surviving extreme dehydration. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and neuroprotective properties. This resilient fern is increasingly studied for its potential to combat oxidative stress and support neurological health.

  • Scientific name: Selaginella tamariscina
  • Family: Selaginellaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Lycopodiidae, it is organized into the order Selaginellales and the family Selaginellaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Selaginella.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions throughout China. It can be found growing in the South-Central parts of the country as well as the Southeast. Additionally, its distribution extends to the island province of Hainan. In the northern reaches of the nation, it is documented within the Manchuria region. Finally, the species is also located in the North-Central areas of China.
