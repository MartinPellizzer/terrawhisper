Lappula redowskii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Boraginales and is a member of the family Boraginaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Lappula, with its unique classification completed at the species level as redowskii.
