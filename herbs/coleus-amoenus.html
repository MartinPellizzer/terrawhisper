Coleus amoenus

Coleus amoenus, commonly known as the pleasant coleus, is a vibrant ornamental plant celebrated for its strikingly patterned foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this species holds significance in traditional herbal practices. Its unique leaves, featuring intricate hues, make it a captivating addition to gardens and a subject of botanical interest.
