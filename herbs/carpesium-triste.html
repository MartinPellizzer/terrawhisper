Carpesium triste

Carpesium triste, commonly known as the somber carpel, is a rare medicinal plant prized for its potent bioactive compounds. Found primarily in dense, shaded woodland ecosystems, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to alleviate inflammatory conditions and support neurological health, making it a subject of significant pharmacological interest.
