Corydalis conspersa

Corydalis conspersa, commonly known as the spreading corydalis, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant contains various isoquinoline alkaloids. These bioactive compounds are studied for their potential analgesic and sedative properties, offering significant interest in pharmacological research for pain management and neurological health.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis conspersa
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Papaveraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Corydalis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Asia. Within China, its presence is documented in the South-Central and North-Central territories. It can also be found in the high-altitude landscapes of Qinghai and Tibet. Beyond the borders of China, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it has been recorded growing within the mountainous terrain of Nepal.
