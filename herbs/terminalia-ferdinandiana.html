Terminalia ferdinandiana

Terminalia ferdinandiana, commonly known as the Gulupa or Madagascar Almond, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Madagascar. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical value and importance.

  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Australasia
