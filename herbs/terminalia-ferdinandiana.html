Kakaadu plum, scientifically known as Terminalia ferdinandiana, is a powerhouse of nutrition native to Northern Australia. Renowned for having the world's highest natural concentration of Vitamin C, this remarkable fruit offers potent antioxidant properties, supporting immune health and skin vitality through its rich, bioactive nutrient profile.
