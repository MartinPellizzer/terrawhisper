Mimusops obtusifolia

Mimusops obtusifolia, commonly known as the "white-flowered mimuso," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical value.

  • Scientific name: Mimusops obtusifolia
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is further categorized into the family Sapotaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Mimusops, identifying it as Mimusops obtusifolia.
