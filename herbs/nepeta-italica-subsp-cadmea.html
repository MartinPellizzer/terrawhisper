Nepeta italica subsp. cadmea

Nepeta italicica subsp. cadmea, commonly known as a specialized subspecies of catnip, is a remarkable medicinal herb native to specific Mediterranean microclimates. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it possesses unique phytochemical properties used in traditional healing to soothe nervous tension, support digestive health, and provide gentle sedative effects.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta italica subsp. cadmea
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Nepeta, identifying it as the subspecies cadmea.
