Cytisus striatus

Cytisus striatus, commonly known as striped broom, is a resilient flowering shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blossoms, this plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism. It is often studied for its unique chemical composition and potential therapeutic properties within various folk medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Cytisus striatus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cytisus, specifically as the species Cytisus striatus.
