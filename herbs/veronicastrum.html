Veronicastrum

Veronicastrum, commonly known as speedwell, is a diverse genus of flowering plants celebrated for its delicate, blue-to-white blooms. Beyond its ornamental charm, various species hold significant roles in traditional herbal medicine, valued for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and soothing properties used to support respiratory and skin health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Veronicastrum
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Veronicastrum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of Northern Asia. Within the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found throughout Buryatiya and the Chitа region. Its distribution extends further into the Siberian landscapes of Irkutsk and the expansive areas of Yakutiya. Additionally, the species is documented in the Russian Far East, specifically within the Amur region. Collectively, these locations highlight a strong preference for the harsh, continental climates of these territories.
