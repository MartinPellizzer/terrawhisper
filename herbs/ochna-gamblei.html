Ochna gamblei

Ochna gamblei, commonly known as the Mickey Mouse plant, is a striking evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its ornamental fruit—black drupes nestled in bright red sepals—it is widely cultivated in subtropical gardens. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this resilient species holds significant value in traditional botanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Ochna gamblei
  • Family: Ochnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ochnaceae and falls under the genus Ochna.
