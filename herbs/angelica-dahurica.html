Dahurian angelica

Angelica dahurica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (461 studies) · ★★★★★

Dahurian angelica, scientifically known as Angelica dahurica, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its aromatic roots, it is primarily utilized to treat skin conditions, reduce inflammation, and alleviate pain. This potent botanical serves as a vital component in various restorative herbal formulas.
