Angelica dahurica

Angelica dahurica, commonly known as Dahurian Angelica, is a perennial herb highly valued in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin disorders, and headaches. Its potent bioactive compounds offer significant antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, making it a cornerstone of herbal therapy.

  • Scientific name: Angelica dahurica
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae within the genus Angelica.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the vast territories of Russia. In the Siberian region, it can be found growing in Buryatiya, Chitа, and Irkutsk. Its presence extends further north into the expansive landscapes of Yakutiya. Additionally, the species is documented within the Russian Far East, specifically in the Amur region. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various parts of the Russian wilderness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Angelica dahlica, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for its therapeutic properties derived from its underground structures. The primary medicinal component is the roots, which are harvested and often processed into dried roots for long-term storage and standardized use. While the botanical anatomy includes green leaves that facilitate photosynthesis, the pharmacological potency is concentrated in the root system and the fleshy rhizomes. These parts contain the essential volatile oils and compounds necessary for its medicinal applications.
