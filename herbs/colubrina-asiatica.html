Colubrina asiatica

Colubrina asiatica, commonly known as the Asian Colubrina, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark possess significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. This resilient plant serves as a vital natural resource for treating various skin and digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Colubrina asiatica
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Colubrina asiatica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Rosales and the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Colubrina.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Africa and the Indian Ocean. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting parts of Kenya and Tanzania. Its presence extends further south into the continent, specifically within Mozambique in South Tropical Africa.
