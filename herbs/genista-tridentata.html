Genista tridentata

Genista tridentata, commonly known as three-toothed broom, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the chaparral ecosystems of California. Characterized by its trifoliate leaves and vibrant yellow pea-like flowers, this nitrogen-fixing plant plays a vital role in soil enrichment and provides essential habitat for diverse local wildlife.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Genista.
