This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is specifically noted in Thailand. Moving into the Malesia zone, it can be found inhabiting the island of Borneo. The species also extends its range to the islands of Jawa and the Malay Peninsula. Furthermore, its distribution reaches as far as the Maluku archipelago.
