Callicarpa dolichophylla

Callicarpa dolichophylla, commonly known as a member of the beautyberry family, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Distinguished by its elongated leaves, this species offers diverse therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, showcasing the rich biodiversity of botanical remedies.

  • Scientific name: Callicarpa dolichophylla
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls under the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Callicarpa.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Callicarpa dolichophylla, is recognized by several different common names depending on the linguistic context and regional usage. In Chinese, it is referred to as 雪突 (zh), 雪突 (zh-hans), and 雪突 (zh-cn), while it is also commonly known as 穿骨枫 (zh) and 尖尾枫 (zh).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions in East Asia. Within China, its distribution spans the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Moving further east, the species is also documented as occurring in Japan. Additionally, its presence extends to the Nansei-shotō islands. Together, these locations define the specific ecological footprint of Callicarpa dolichophyllas.
