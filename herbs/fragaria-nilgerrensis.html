Fragaria nilgerrensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Fragaria nilgerrensis?

Fragaria nilgerrensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Fragaria, with its specific designation being the species nilgerrensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Fragaria nilgerrensis has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by creeping stolons, trifoliate leaves with serrated margins, and small white five-petaled flowers. The leaves typically feature long petioles and a distinctively textured surface with prominent venation. Its reproductive structures consist of aggregate fruits that develop from an inferior ovary, eventually forming small red achenes. The plant's stolons allow it to spread vegetatively across the soil surface to form dense mats. These morphological traits enable the species to colonize forest floors and open habitats effectively.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant requires well-drained, acidic soil with a pH between 5.5 and 6.5 and consistent moisture to thrive in temperate or subtropical climates.

Growers should plant the runners in partial shade to prevent the delicate fruit from scorching under intense sunlight. Regular applications of organic compost or balanced nitrogen-based fertilizers are necessary to support vigorous leaf and fruit development. Maintaining a consistent watering schedule is essential to prevent the soil from drying out completely between rainfalls.

Careful mulching helps suppress weeds and retains the soil humidity required for high yields.
