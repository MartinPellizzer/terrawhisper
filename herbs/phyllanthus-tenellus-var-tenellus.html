Phyllanthus tenellus var. tenellus

Phyllanthus tenellus var. tenellus, commonly known as Leafflower, is a small, herbaceous plant widely utilized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently employed to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This botanical specimen remains a vital component in various ethnobotanical healing practices globally.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus tenellus var. tenellus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus as the variety Phyllanthus tenellus var. tenellus.
