Dendrobium loddigesii

What's the taxonomical classification of Dendrobium loddigesii?

Dendrobium loddigesii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Asparagales. This plant is a specialized representative of the family Orchidaceae, falling under the genus Dendrobium, and is specifically identified by its species name, loddigesii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dendrobium loddigesii has elongated, pendulous pseudobulbs that are distinctly compressed and can reach lengths of up to 60 centimeters. These fleshy stems serve as primary water storage organs and are often densely clustered at the base of the plant. The foliage consists of relatively small, leathery leaves that emerge from the apex of each pseudobulb. Its inflorescence produces numerous small, fragrant flowers that typically display a pale yellow or cream coloration with subtle markings. The overall growth habit is epiphytic, allowing the plant to cling to tree branches in its natural habitat.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is native to the tropical regions of New Guinea, specifically inhabiting the lowland and montane forests of the island. It thrives within humid environments where it can grow as an epiphyte on trees or as a lithophyte on rocks. The distribution is largely confined to the moist, equatorial zones characterized by high rainfall and consistent temperatures. Localized populations are typically found in areas with significant canopy cover that provides filtered light. These specific ecological niches allow the species to flourish across its restricted geographic range.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant requires bright, filtered sunlight and high humidity levels between 60% and 80% to thrive in a warm tropical environment.

You should provide consistent moisture to the medium while ensuring excellent air circulation to prevent root rot. Using a coarse orchid bark or a mounted setup allows the roots to breathe effectively. Regular fertilization during the growing season will support the development of its characteristic colorful blooms.

Maintaining stable temperatures above 60 degrees Fahrenheit is essential for its long-term health.

What parts of this plant are used medicinally?

This plant Dendrobium loddigesii is utilized medicinally through the application of its laminae, lateral buds, and stems. The broad laminae are often processed to extract compounds that assist in reducing inflammation. Additionally, the lateral buds contain concentrated nutrients that are used in traditional topical treatments. The fibrous stems serve as a primary source for decoctions intended to treat various internal ailments. Together, these specific components provide a diverse range of therapeutic benefits for medicinal use.

According to a study published by "Zhong yao cai = Zhongyaocai = Journal of Chinese medicinal materials", the morphological characters and polysaccharides content of Dendrobium loddigesii from different habits were studied. The results showed remarkable differences in morphology from 5 areas, including stem height and diameter, laminae sizes and figures, amount of lateral bud, and texture of stem. The content of polysaccharides from samples of Xingyi and Anlong, Guizhou Province, were 22.62% and 20.96% respectively. These specific levels were higher than that of other areas. Consequently, it can be preferentially chosen as good material on commercial production.

What traditional systems uses this plant?

This plant Dendrobium loddigesii has been utilized within Traditional Chinese Medicine for its purported restorative and cooling properties. Practitioners historically applied various species of the Dendrobium genus to treat ailments related to yin deficiency and to nourish the body's fluids. While specific historical records for this exact species are rare, it falls under the broader classification of orchid-based remedies used to balance internal heat. These traditional applications often involve processing the rhizomes or stems to create decoctions for medicinal consumption. Such usage highlights the long-standing role of epiphytic orchids in ancient botanical practices.

According to a study published by "Yao xue xue bao = Acta pharmaceutica Sinica", Dendrobium loddigesii Rolfe is a Chinese medicine. Using IR, UV, MS and NMR spectra, 3 constituents were identified from the fresh stem of the plant. These constituents are shihunidine, shihunine and dendrophenol (4,4'- dihydroxy-3,3',5-trimethoxybibenzyl). Chemical reactions showed that shihunidine was derived from shihunine during the isolation process. Both shihunidine and shihunine were shown to be inhibitors of Na+, K+-ATPase of the rat kidney.

What are the pharmacological activities of Dendrobium loddigesii?

This plant has anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, antimicrobial, antitumor, and antipyretic pharmacological activities. These diverse biological properties suggest that the species contains potent bioactive compounds capable of modulating various physiological processes. The antioxidant effects help neutralize free radicals, while the antimicrobial actions provide defense against harmful pathogens. Furthermore, its antitumor potential offers a basis for cancer research, and its antipyretic qualities assist in reducing fever. Together, these characteristics highlight the significant therapeutic potential of Dendrobium loddigesii in medicinal applications.

According to a study published by "International journal of molecular sciences", Dendrobium loddigesii possesses active ingredients such as polysaccharides, alkaloids and phenols that provide anti-inflammatory, anti-tumour and immunity-boosting effects. The Dendrobium loddigesii Rolfe water extract (DLW) can upregulate the expression of aquaporins (AQP)5 protein to exert a repairing effect by promoting cell migration. Oral administration of DLW enhanced tear production in rats and exerted a protective effect on ocular surface damage. Furthermore, DLW can upregulate the expression of AQP5 and mucin (muc)5ac proteins in the lacrimal gland and reduce the inflammatory response. DLW also inhibited the activation of the corresponding mitogen-activated protein kinases (MAPK) and NF-KB pathway to improve dry eye symptoms.
