Glechoma hirsuta

Glechoma hirsuta, commonly known as hairy moneywort, is a low-growing perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Frequently found in moist, shaded environments, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, including its potential anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant effects used to treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Glechoma hirsuta
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls under the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized within the genus Glechoma.
