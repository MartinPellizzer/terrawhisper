Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, it can be found naturally in Myanmar, Thailand, and Vietnam. Its presence extends further into the Malesia zone, specifically across the island of Borneo. Additionally, the species is well-distributed throughout the Malay Peninsula. Together, these locations highlight the plant's significant ecological reach throughout the tropics.
