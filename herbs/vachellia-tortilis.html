Umbrella thorn, scientifically known as Vachellia tortilis, is a resilient, deciduous tree native to the arid landscapes of Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional healing to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant ethnobotanical value.

This plant is primarily distributed across the northern regions of the African continent. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Algeria and Egypt.
