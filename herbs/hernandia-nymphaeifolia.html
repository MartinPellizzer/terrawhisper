Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various islands within the Western Indian Ocean. Its presence can be noted in the unique ecosystems of the Aldabra atoll. Furthermore, it inhabits the remote stretches of the Chagos Archipelago. The species also grows throughout the Comoros and the island of Mauritius. Finally, its geographical range extends to the large and diverse landmass of Madagascar.
