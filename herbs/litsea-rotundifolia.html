Litsea rotundifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Litsea rotundifolia?

Litsea rotundifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Litsea, with the specific identifier of the species rotundifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Litsea rotundifolia has elliptical to ovate leaves that are typically 3 to 7 centimeters long, characterized by entire margins and an acute apex. The foliage is arranged in an alternate pattern along slender, woody stems. These leaves possess a smooth, somewhat leathery texture and a deep green color on the upper surface. Small, inconspicuous flowers often emerge from the leaf axils in clusters. The plant produces small, fleshy drupes that transition in color as they reach maturity.
