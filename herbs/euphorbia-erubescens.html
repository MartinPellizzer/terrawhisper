Euphorbia erubescens

Euphorbia erubescens, commonly known as a member of the diverse Euphorbiaceae family, is a medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique milky latex, this species is studied for its bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic applications in treating skin ailments and managing various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia erubescens
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
