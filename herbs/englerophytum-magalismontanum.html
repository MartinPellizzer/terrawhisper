Englerophytum magalismontanum

Englerophytum magalismontanum, commonly known as the mountain healer, is a rare medicinal plant endemic to high-altitude tropical forests. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it has been traditionally used to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments. Modern research continues to explore its significant pharmacological potential for developing new therapeutic treatments.
