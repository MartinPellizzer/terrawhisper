Dryopteris marginata

What's the taxonomical classification of Dryopteris marginata?

Dryopteris marginata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. This fern is a member of the family Polypodiaceae, falling under the genus Dryopteris, and is specifically identified by its species name, marginata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dryopteris marginata has bipinnate to tripinnate fronds that typically reach lengths of 30 to 90 centimeters and feature distinctive, irregularly serrated margins. The stipes are stout and often covered in brown scales near the base. Each pinna is divided into multiple smaller segments that give the foliage a delicate, lacy appearance. The overall texture of the fronds is somewhat leathery compared to other wood ferns. These fronds emerge from a short, creeping rhizome that stays partially buried in the forest floor.
