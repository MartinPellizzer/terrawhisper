Manyflower Garcinia, scientifically known as Garcinia multiflora, is a remarkable tropical plant prized in traditional medicine. Found primarily in Southeast Asia, this species is renowned for its bioactive compounds, including xanthones. It is widely utilized for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic potential.
