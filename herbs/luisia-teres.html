Luisia teres belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Asparagales, it falls under the diverse and well-known family Orchidaceae. Specifically, this plant is categorized within the genus Luisia, with its unique identity defined by its species name, teres.
