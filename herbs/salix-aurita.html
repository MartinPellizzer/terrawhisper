Salix cinerea, scientifically known as Salix aurita, is a resilient shrub native to European wetlands and moorlands. Historically valued for its high tannin content, it has been utilized in traditional medicine for its astringent and anti-inflammatory properties, offering natural remedies for skin irritations and various digestive ailments.
