Hooker's St. John's Wort, scientifically known as Hypericum hookerianum, is a perennial herb belonging to the Hypericaceae family. Native to parts of Asia, this plant is distinguished by its vibrant yellow blooms. While related to the well-known medicinal St. John's Wort, it possesses unique botanical characteristics and potential therapeutic applications.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it is specifically found growing within the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in India and the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species is located throughout the East Himalaya region. Finally, its range encompasses the territory of Bangladesh, marking its widespread occurrence in these diverse habitats.
