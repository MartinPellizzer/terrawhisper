Hypericum hookerianum

Hypericum hookerianum, commonly known as Hooker's St. John's Wort, is a perennial herb distinguished by its delicate yellow blossoms. Belonging to the Hypericaceae family, this plant is valued in ethnobotany for its diverse bioactive compounds, which offer potential therapeutic properties in traditional medicine for soothing various ailments and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum hookerianum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hypericum.
