Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Nigeria and Sierra Leone. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands and throughout the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Additionally, the species extends its reach into East Tropical Africa, specifically within Kenya. Consequently, the geographical range of Erythrina poepigiana spans from the western coast to the eastern highlands.
