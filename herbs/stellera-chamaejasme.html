Stellera chamaejasme

Stellaria chamaejasme, commonly known as starwort, is a delicate perennial herb native to Europe and parts of Asia. Characterized by its tiny white flowers and slender stems, this plant holds significance in traditional herbalism. It is often studied for its potential bioactive properties and its role in local ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Stellera chamaejasme
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae and order Malvales, it is classified under the family Thymelaeaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Stellera.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Northern and Central Asia. In the vast expanse of Siberia, it can be found specifically within the territories of Buryatiya and Chita. Its range extends further east into the Russian Far East, where it inhabits the Amur region. Within China, the species is established in the South-Central provinces. Additionally, the plant is documented as growing throughout the Inner Mongolia region of China.
