Jatropha dioica

Leatherstem

Jatrophia dioica, commonly known as Barren Desert Candlestick, is a resilient succulent native to arid regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant offers therapeutic potential for managing diabetes and inflammation. Its ability to thrive in harsh, water-scarce environments makes it a fascinating subject for both ethnobotany and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Jatropha dioica
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Jatropha.
