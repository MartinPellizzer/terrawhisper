Cissus repens

Cissus repens, commonly known as climbing creeper, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Characterized by its vigorous climbing habit, it possesses diverse therapeutic properties. Often utilized for its anti-inflammatory and restorative benefits, this botanical specimen offers significant potential in herbal medicine for supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cissus repens
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is specifically found in the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends to Eastern Asia, where it can be located in Taiwan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species is native to the Assam region. Additionally, the plant is also distributed throughout Bangladesh.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cissus repens, utilizes its various structures for growth, but in the context of traditional medicinal applications, the rootbark is a significant component. The rootbark, which is the protective outer layer of the plant's underground root system, is often the specific part harvested for its concentrated bioactive compounds. By extracting essence from this sturdy layer, practitioners aim to harness the therapeutic properties inherent in the plant's foundational anatomy.
