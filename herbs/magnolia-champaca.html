Champa, scientifically known as Magnolia champaca, is a magnificent flowering tree cherished in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its intensely fragrant, golden-yellow blossoms, it is used to treat respiratory ailments, skin disorders, and digestive issues. Beyond its therapeutic value, its essential oils are highly prized in traditional perfumery worldwide.
