Phyllanthus lawii

Phyllanthus lawii, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Belonging to the Phyllanthaceae family, this plant is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Research suggests its bioactive compounds may offer antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits, making it a subject of pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus lawii
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Phyllanthus.

This plant, known scientifically as Phyllanthus lawii, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is often known as Law's leaf-flower, while in its native botanical circles, it is frequently called Law's phyllanthus. In the local context of West Africa where it is often studied, it may be known by regional vernacular names, though its primary recognition remains tied to its specific scientific designation.
