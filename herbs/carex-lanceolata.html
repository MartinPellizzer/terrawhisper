Carex lanceolata

What's the taxonomical classification of Carex lanceolata?

Carex lanceolata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Poales and the family Cyperaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Carex, and its specific species designation, lanceolata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Carex lanceolata has lanceolate leaves that are typically 2 to 5 millimeters wide and up to 40 centimeters long, paired with upright, slender stems that reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters. The plant features narrow, pointed leaves that often exhibit a slightly channeled structure. Its inflorescence consists of several distinct spikes, where the male spikes are located at the top and the female spikes are situated below. The perigynia are small, lance-shaped, and possess a short beak which is characteristic of this species. This sedge often grows in tufted clumps, forming dense mats in its natural habitat.
