Black cincau, scientifically known as Platostoma palustre, is a versatile medicinal herb widely utilized in Southeast Asian traditional medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, this aromatic plant is frequently processed into jelly-like drinks. It is valued for its ability to alleviate body heat, aid digestion, and provide antioxidant benefits.
