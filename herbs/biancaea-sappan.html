Sappanwood, scientifically known as Biancaea sappan, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its vibrant red heartwood, it is rich in brazilin, a powerful antioxidant. Traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, this botanical treasure offers significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal medicine.
