Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across the African continent and its surrounding islands. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Egypt. The species also extends into Macaronesia, where it can be found in Cape Verde. Moving to West Tropical Africa, the plant is distributed across several nations, including Benin and Burkina. Furthermore, its geographical reach encompasses the West Tropical African country of Gambia.
