Chloranthus fortunei

Chloranthus fortunei, commonly known as Fortune's Chloranthus, is a versatile perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, this plant is prized for its medicinal properties, often used to treat inflammation and digestive issues. Its unique foliage and flowering patterns make it both therapeutic and aesthetically pleasing.

  • Scientific name: Chloranthus fortunei
  • Family: Chloranthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Chloranthales and the family Chloranthaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Chloranthus.

This plant, known scientifically as Chloranthus fortunei, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Chinese, it is known as 丝穗金粟兰 (zh), 絲穗金粟蘭 (zh-tw), 丝穗金粟兰 (zh-cn), and 丝穗金粟兰 (zh-hans), while in Japanese, it is called キビヒトリシズカ (ja).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the North-Central provinces to the Southeast regions. Its presence is also noted within the South-Central parts of the country. Furthermore, the species extends southward to include the tropical island of Hainan. Beyond mainland China, the plant is also naturally distributed within Japan.
