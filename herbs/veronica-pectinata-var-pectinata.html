Veronica pectinata var. pectinata

Veronica pectinata var. pectinata, commonly known as Combbed Speedwell, is a perennial herb distinguished by its characteristic serrated leaves. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, this plant contains bioactive compounds utilized for their potential anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, offering a unique botanical resource within the diverse genus Veronica.

  • Scientific name: Veronica pectinata var. pectinata
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae, it is classified under the genus Veronica. Specifically, this medicinal specimen is identified as the variety pectinata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within certain regions of Europe and Asia. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in the country of Bulgaria. Its range also extends into the European portion of Türkiye, located within the same southeastern region. Furthermore, the species is distributed across Western Asia, specifically within the borders of Türkiye. Consequently, its habitat spans across these interconnected parts of the Balkan and Anatolian landscapes.
