Gentiana filistyla

What's the taxonomical classification of Gentiana filistyla?

Gentiana filistyla belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the biological lineage of the order Gentianaales. The plant is further categorized under the family Gentianaceae, specifically within the genus Gentiana, and is identified by its unique species designation, filistyla.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Gentiana filistyla has linear to lanceolate leaves that are arranged oppositely along a stem and small, tubular blue flowers characterized by extremely thin, thread-like styles. The foliage typically exhibits a smooth texture and a bright green coloration throughout the growing season. Its corolla is typically funnel-shaped with distinct lobes that flare outward at the apex. The most defining morphological feature is the elongated, filamentous style that extends significantly beyond the floral tube.
