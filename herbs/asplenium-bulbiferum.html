Hen-and-chickens, scientifically known as Asplenium bulbiferum, is a unique fern recognized by the small plantlets, or "chicks," growing on its fronds. Native to New Zealand and Australia, this resilient species is valued in traditional herbal practices for its potential medicinal properties and its distinctive, evolutionary reproductive strategy.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the various regions of New Zealand, including the North Island and the South Island. Its presence extends further to the Antipodean Islands and the Chatham Islands, showcasing a broad reach within the New Zealand archipelago. Beyond these territories, the species also inhabits the Northwestern Pacific region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the Mariana Islands. Together, these locations define the unique geographical range of Asplenium bulbiferum.
