Pteridium caudatum

Pteridium caudatum, commonly known as the Caudate Brake Fern, is a distinctive medicinal plant recognized by its unique, tapering fronds. Traditionally utilized in various folk medicine practices, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Exploring its phytochemical composition offers promising insights into its potential therapeutic applications in modern botanical pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Pteridium caudatum
  • Family: Dennstaedtiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae. Within the order Polypodiales, it is classified under the family Dennstaedtiaceae and the genus Pteridium.

This plant, Pteridium caudatum, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across parts of North America and Mexico. In the Southeastern United States, its presence is specifically noted within the state of Florida. Moving into Mexico, the species can be found throughout the Central and Northeastern regions of the country. It also occupies territories along the Mexican Gulf coast.
