Lippia lupulina

Lippia lupulina, commonly known as yerba buena, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South America. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and inflammation. This potent plant offers a natural remedy through its rich bioactive compound profile.

  • Scientific name: Lippia lupulina
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lippia, specifically as the species Lippia lupulina.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of South America. In the western part of the continent, its presence is recorded in Bolivia. The species is also widely spread throughout Brazil, covering a vast range of territories. Specifically, it can be found in the North, South, and Southeast regions of Brazil.
