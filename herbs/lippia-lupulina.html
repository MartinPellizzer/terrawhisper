Lippia lupulina, scientifically known as Lippia lupulina, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South American wetlands. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and inflammation, offering a natural remedy rich in bioactive compounds and antioxidants.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lippia, specifically as the species Lippia lupulina.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of South America. In the western part of the continent, its presence is recorded in Bolivia.
