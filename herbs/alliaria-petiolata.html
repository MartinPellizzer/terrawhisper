Alliaria petiolata

Alliaria petiolata, commonly known as garlic mustard, is a biennial herb native to Europe but widely naturalized in North America. Distinguished by its garlic-scented leaves and small white flowers, this invasive species disrupts forest ecosystems by altering soil chemistry and suppressing the growth of native flora through allelopathy.

  • Scientific name: Alliaria petiolata
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe
