Myrceugenia obtusa

Raran

Myrceugenia obtusa, commonly known as the "pau-ferro" in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Its unique chemical profile offers promising potential for modern pharmacological research and herbal medicine.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
