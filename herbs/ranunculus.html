Buttercup, scientifically known as Ranunculus, is a diverse genus of flowering plants celebrated for their vibrant, waxy petals. While aesthetically striking in meadows, many species contain protoanemonin, a toxic compound. Historically used in traditional medicine, modern applications require extreme caution due to their potent skin-irritating and systemic effects.
