Aconitum cannabifolium

What's the taxonomical classification of Aconitum cannabifolium?

Aconitum cannabifoiliuim belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales. This plant is a specific representative of the family Ranunculaceae, situated within the genus Aconitum, and is identified by its unique species name, cannabifolium.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aconitum cannabifoliuim has deeply palmately lobed leaves that resemble those of the cannabis plant, featuring three to five distinct lobes with serrated margins. The plant typically develops an upright growth habit with sturdy stems that support its foliage and eventual floral structures. Its flowers are characterized by a specialized spurred shape, which is a defining feature of the Aconitum genus. These blooms often emerge in shades of blue, purple, or white depending on the specific environmental conditions. The root system consists of tuberous structures that allow the plant to persist in its native montane habitats.
