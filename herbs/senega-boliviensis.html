Senega boliviensis

Senega boliviensis, commonly known as Bolivian Senega, is a medicinal plant native to South American regions. Renowned for its potent expectorant properties, it is primarily used in traditional medicine to soothe respiratory ailments, alleviate coughs, and clear bronchial congestion, offering a natural approach to managing various pulmonary conditions.

  • Scientific name: Senega boliviensis
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it falls into the family Polygalaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Senega, specifically as the species Senega boliviensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of the South American continent. In Northern South America, its presence is documented within the borders of Venezuela. Moving toward the western part of the continent, the species can be found in Bolivia, Colombia, and Peru. Additionally, the plant extends its reach into the northeastern territories of Brazil. Consequently, its range spans multiple diverse ecological zones throughout the region.
