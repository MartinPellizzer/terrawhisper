What's the taxonomical classification of Dendrolobium triangulare?

Dendrolobium triangulare belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Fabales and falls under the family Fabaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Dendrolobium, and its unique species designation, triangulare.

From the apex of each pseudobulb, a single, leathery, lanceolate leaf emerges to facilitate photosynthesis. The inflorescence consists of multiple small, delicate flowers that often exhibit intricate patterns and varying hues.
