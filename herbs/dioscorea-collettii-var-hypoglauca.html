Dioscorea collettii var. hypoglauca

Dioscorea collettii var. hypoglauca, commonly known as a specialized variety of Chinese yam, is a medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this tuberous herb is utilized for its restorative properties, offering significant potential in supporting metabolic health and nourishing the body's vital energy.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea collettii var. hypoglauca
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Dioscoreales and the family Dioscoreaceae. Ultimately, it is identified as a member of the genus Dioscorea, specifically the variety hypoglauca.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions within East Asia. In mainland China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central territories. It can also be found growing in the Southeast regions of China. Furthermore, the species extends its geographical range to the island of Taiwan. These locations represent the known natural habitats for this particular variety.
