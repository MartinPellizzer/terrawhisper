Morning-glory, scientifically known as Ipomoea tricolor, is a striking plant renowned for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms. While celebrated in gardens for its aesthetic beauty, it holds significant historical importance in traditional medicine. Its seeds contain potent alkaloids, requiring careful study for their complex pharmacological and psychoactive properties.
