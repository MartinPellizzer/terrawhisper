Euonymus fortunei

Wintercreeper

Euonymus fortunei, commonly known as wintercreeper, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its ornamental foliage. Renowned for its ability to thrive in diverse conditions, it features glossy leaves often accented by colorful variegation. This hardy plant serves as an excellent groundcover or accent in many garden landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Euonymus fortunei
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Euonymus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Euonymus fortunei, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Simplified Chinese, it is known as 扶芳藤 (zh-hans) or 扶芳藤 (zh-cn), while in Traditional Chinese contexts such as Taiwan, it is called 常春衛矛 (zh-hant). Similarly, in Hong Kong, the name 常春衛矛 (zh-hk) is used, and in Singapore, it is identified as 常春卫矛 (zh-sg).
