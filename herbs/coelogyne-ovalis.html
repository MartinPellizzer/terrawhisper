What's the taxonomical classification of Coelogyne ovalis?

Coelogyne ovalis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales. This plant is a member of the family Orchidaceae, specifically falling within the genus Coelogyne, with its unique identity defined by its species name, ovalis.

According to a study published by "Gene", Coelogyne ovalis is an orchid of ornamental and medicinal importance. The CoCHS gene sequence from this plant was found to be 1445 bp and comprised an open reading frame of 1182 bp, which encodes for 394 amino acid residues. Quantitative polymerase chain reaction (qPCR) disclosed that CoCHS was expressed in all tissues examined. The highest transcript levels were found in the leaves, followed by the pseudobulb parts and the roots. A positive correlation existed between CoCHS expression and the content of flavonoids and anthocyanins within the in vivo and in vitro-raised plant parts.
