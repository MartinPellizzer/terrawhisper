Coelogyne ovalis

Oval coelogyne

Coelogyne ovalis, commonly known as a rare orchid species, is a captivating botanical treasure found in specific tropical habitats. While primarily celebrated for its delicate, ornamental blooms, this plant holds significant interest in ethnobotany. Exploring its unique biological properties offers fascinating insights into the diverse medicinal potential of orchidaceae.

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central areas as well as within the high-altitude terrain of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the lush landscapes of Assam. The species is also documented throughout the nation of Bangladesh.
