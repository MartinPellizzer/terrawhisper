Carduus hamulosus subsp. hamulosus

Carduus hamulosus subsp. hamulosus, commonly known as the hooked milk thistle, is a distinctive medicinal perennial native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its hooked spines and robust stature, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine for its potential hepatoprotective properties and its ability to support digestive health and detoxification.

  • Scientific name: Carduus hamulosus subsp. hamulosus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Asterales, falling within the family Asteraceae. Taxonomically, it is identified as a member of the genus Carduus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in Germany as well as throughout the Czechia-Slovakia region. It can also be found growing within the borders of Hungary. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species extends its range into Bulgaria. Finally, the geographical footprint of this subspecies reaches as far south as Greece.
