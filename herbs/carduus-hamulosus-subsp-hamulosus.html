Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Asterales, falling within the family Asteraceae. Taxonomically, it is identified as a member of the genus Carduus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in Germany as well as throughout the Czechia-Slovakia region. It can also be found growing within the borders of Hungary. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species extends its range into Bulgaria. Finally, the geographical footprint of this subspecies reaches as far south as Greece.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Carduus hamulosus subsp. hamulosus, is frequently utilized in traditional medicine in its whole plants form. When the whole plants are harvested, all morphological structures—including the prickly leaves, the sturdy stem, and the distinctive flower heads—are taken to prepare various herbal remedies. By using the whole plants, practitioners ensure that the diverse array of bioactive compounds distributed throughout the different tissues is captured, allowing for a comprehensive medicinal application.

Carduus hamulosus subsp. hamulosus has 11 reported phytochemicals identified across 1 scientific publications and several other databases.
