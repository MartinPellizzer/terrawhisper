Leonurus turkestanicus

Leonurus turkesstanicus, commonly known as Turkestan motherwort, is a medicinal herb native to Central Asia. Renowned for its sedative and antispasmodic properties, it is traditionally used to treat nervous disorders, hypertension, and menstrual irregularities. This potent botanical offers significant pharmacological potential in managing stress and cardiovascular health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Leonurus turkestanicus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Leonurus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast landscapes of Central Asia. Its natural habitat includes the diverse terrains found within Kazakhstan. Furthermore, it can be located throughout the mountainous and steppe regions of Kirgizstan. The species also extends its range into the territories of Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan. Finally, its geographical presence is completed by its distribution within Tajikistan.
