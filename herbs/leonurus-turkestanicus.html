Turkestan Motherwort, scientifically known as Leonurus turkesstanicus, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to Central Asian landscapes. Renowned for its calming properties, this perennial plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to alleviate anxiety, support cardiovascular health, and regulate menstrual cycles, offering a unique botanical approach to holistic wellness.
