Bryonia cretica

Bryonia cretica, commonly known as White Bryony, is a perennial climbing vine native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its distinctive tendrils and white flowers, this plant is highly significant in traditional ethnobotany. While historically used for medicinal purposes, its potent bioactive compounds require careful handling due to toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Bryonia cretica
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Cucurbitaleales, it is a member of the family Cucurbitaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Bryonia.
