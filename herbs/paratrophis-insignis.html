Paratrophis insignis

Paratropis insignis, commonly known as the African medicinal climber, is a significant botanical species valued in traditional healing practices. Primarily found in tropical regions, this plant is utilized for its potent bioactive compounds. Research continues to explore its therapeutic potential in treating various ailments and managing diverse inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Paratrophis insignis
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Paratropis insignis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Rosales and the family Moraea. It is specifically identified by its genus, Paratropis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several tropical regions in the Americas. In North America, its presence is specifically noted in Southeast Mexico. Moving south, it can be found throughout Central America, including Guatemala, Costa Rica, and Panamá. The species also extends into Northern South America, where it is located in Venezuela. Consequently, its range spans a diverse corridor from Mexico to the northern reaches of the South American continent.
