Pappea capensis

Jacket-plum

Pappea capensis, commonly known as the Cape Pepper, is a versatile evergreen tree native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, its bark, leaves, and fruit are traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from gastrointestinal issues to skin infections, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Pappea capensis
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Pappea capensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Sapindaceae and falls under the genus Pappea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several key regions of the African continent.
