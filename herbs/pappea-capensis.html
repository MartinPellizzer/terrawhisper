Jacket-plum, scientifically known as Pappea capensis, is a versatile evergreen tree native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional healing to treat ailments such as skin infections and gastrointestinal issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.
