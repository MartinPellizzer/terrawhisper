Astragalus rousseanus

Astragalus rousseanus, commonly known as a vital species within the Fabaceae family, is a medicinal plant highly valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its potent immunomodulatory properties, it is frequently utilized to enhance vitality, support respiratory health, and bolster the body's natural defenses against various physiological stressors.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus rousseanus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
