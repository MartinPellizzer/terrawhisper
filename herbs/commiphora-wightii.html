Commiphora wightii

Commiphora wightii, commonly known as African Myrrh, is a precious medicinal plant native to arid regions. Renowned for its aromatic resin, it possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used in holistic healing, this species offers significant therapeutic potential for treating skin ailments, respiratory issues, and promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Commiphora wightii
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Sapindales, it is a member of the Burseraceae family. Ultimately, it is classified under the genus Commiphora as the species Commiphora wightii.
