Zanthoxylum zanthoxyloides

Zanthoxylum zanthoxyloides, commonly known as Chinese Prickly Ash, is a versatile medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its bark and leaves possess potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic properties. This aromatic species is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin infections, and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum zanthoxyloides
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Sapindales, specifically within the family Rutaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed by the genus Zanthoxylum.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Zanthoxylum zanthoxyloides, is recognized by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Hausa, it is referred to as Fasa kwari, while in Bambara, it is known as Won. In Spanish-speaking regions, it is associated with multiple names, including Fagara senegalensis, Fagara zanthoxyloides, and Zanthoxylum senegalense.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the territory of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species is also documented across the borders of Burkina and the Gambia. Furthermore, its presence extends into the coastal areas of Ghana. Finally, its range reaches as far as Guinea-Bissau, marking its presence in these diverse West African environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Chinese Tuberous or Mahagandhi, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties. The roots and the root-bark are frequently harvested for their potent bioactive compounds, often used in traditional treatments for digestive and inflammatory issues. The stem bark also contains significant therapeutic elements and is a key part used in decoctions. Additionally, the aromatic leaves and the small, fleshy fruits contribute to the plant's overall pharmacological profile, making the entire structure a rich source of medicinal resources.
