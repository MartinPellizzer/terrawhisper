Ilex pernyi

Ilex pernyi, commonly known as the Perny holly, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and fruits possess potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in supporting cardiovascular health and managing oxidative stress.

  • Scientific name: Ilex pernyi
  • Family: Aquifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Aquifoliales and the family Aquifoliaceae, it is classified under the genus Ilex.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of China. It is commonly found throughout the South-Central parts of the country. Additionally, its presence extends into the North-Central provinces of the nation. The species is also distributed across the Southeast region of China. Finally, its habitat includes the high-altitude areas of Tibet.
