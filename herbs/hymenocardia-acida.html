Hymenocardia acida

Hymenocardia acida, commonly known as the "Acidic Hymenocardia," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient shrub offers significant pharmacological potential for developing modern therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Hymenocardia acida
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hymenocardia.
