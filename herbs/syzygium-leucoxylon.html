Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Syzygium leucoxylon, exhibits a specific distribution across the tropical regions of Southeast Asia. It is widely recognized as a member of the Malesia botanical zone. Within this region, the species can be found growing in the diverse habitats of Borneo. Additionally, its presence is well-documented throughout the landscapes of Malaya. The geographical range of this medicinal plant also extends to the various islands of the Philippines.
