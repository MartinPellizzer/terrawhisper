Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of Southern Europe. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found inhabiting the coastal areas of Portugal and Spain. Furthermore, its presence extends to the Balearic Islands within the same Southwestern European zone. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species is also established in Italy. Finally, its range reaches the island of Sicilia, completing its Mediterranean footprint.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as leucaena or subabul, possesses various components that contribute to its biological profile, but the seed is particularly significant in many medicinal and nutritional contexts. The seeds are small, flat, and contained within pods, and they are often studied for their high protein content and potential bioactive compounds. While the leaves are frequently used in traditional practices, the seed remains a vital part of the plant's reproductive cycle and a subject of interest for its chemical properties.
