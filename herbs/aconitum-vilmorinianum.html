Aconitum vilmorinianum

Aconitum vilmorinianum, commonly known as a member of the monkshood family, is a perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its striking blue flowers, this plant contains potent alkaloids. While historically utilized in traditional medicine, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling and expert knowledge to prevent accidental poisoning.

  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
