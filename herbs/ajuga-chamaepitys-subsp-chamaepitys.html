Ajuga chamaepitys subsp. chamaepitys

Ajuga chamaepitys subsp. chamaepitys, commonly known as Bugleweed, is a perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Characterized by its creeping habit and vibrant blue flowers, it is valued in traditional herbalism. This subspecies is studied for its potential bioactive properties, offering insights into its medicinal and ecological significance.

  • Scientific name: Ajuga chamaepitys subsp. chamaepitys
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified as a member of the genus Ajuga, specifically the subspecies Ajuga chamaepitys subsp. chamaepitys.
