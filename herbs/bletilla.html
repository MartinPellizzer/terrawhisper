Bletilla

Bletilla, commonly known as the fringed orchid, is a resilient perennial prized in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, its tubers contain glucomannan, a polysaccharide used to treat inflammation, coughs, and skin ailments. This elegant flowering plant offers a unique blend of aesthetic beauty and significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Bletilla
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is further classified under the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Bletilla.
