Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is further classified under the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Bletilla.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Furthermore, it can be found growing in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Beyond the Chinese mainland, its geographical range extends into the islands of Japan.
