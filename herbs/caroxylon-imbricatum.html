Caroxylon imbricatum

Caroxylon imbricaatum, commonly known as the imbricate caroxylon, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and support digestive health, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for natural healing.
