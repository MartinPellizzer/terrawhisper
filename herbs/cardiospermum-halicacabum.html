Cardiospermum halicacabum

Shepherd's vine

Cardiospermum halicacabum, commonly known as bladder pod or balloon vine, is a versatile medicinal climbing plant. Widely used in traditional Ayurvedic and Unani medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. This plant is highly valued for treating skin ailments, joint pain, and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Cardiospermum halicacabum
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified in the family Sapindaceae and is identified by the genus Cardiospermum.
