Plant Parts

This plant, Cardiospermum halicacabum, is a climbing vine known for its diverse therapeutic uses involving various components. The medicinal properties are often derived from the leaves, which can be used individually in herbal preparations. In many traditional practices, the leaf is harvested and processed to extract its beneficial compounds. Furthermore, the aerial parts of the vine, including the stems and foliage, are frequently utilized in decoctions. For convenience and potency, the whole plant powder is often prepared, allowing for the integrated consumption of all its active constituents.
