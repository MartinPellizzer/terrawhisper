Salvia deserta

Salvia deserta, commonly known as desert sage, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the arid landscapes of the southwestern United States. Characterized by its silvery foliage and delicate blue flowers, this aromatic plant thrives in dry soils, offering significant ecological value and traditional medicinal uses in desert environments.

  • Scientific name: Salvia deserta
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Salvia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of Northern and Central Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found in the Altai mountains and throughout the Krasnoyarsk region. Furthermore, its presence extends across the expansive landscapes of West Siberia. Moving southward into Central Asia, the species is well-established within the borders of Kazakhstan. Finally, its geographical range concludes with documented sightings in Kirgizstan.
