Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In Central Asia, its presence is documented within the borders of Turkmenistan. Moving toward the Caucasus, it can be found throughout both the North Caucasus and the Transcaucasus regions. Its range extends further into Western Asia, where it inhabits parts of Afghanistan. Additionally, the species is well-established within the territory of Iran.

This plant, Bryonia aspera, is a climbing herbaceous vine where the most significant medicinal component is the root. The root system is typically thick and fleshy, containing various bioactive compounds that are often utilized in traditional medicinal practices. Due to its potent chemical composition, the root must be handled with great care, as it serves as a concentrated source of the plant's therapeutic—and sometimes toxic—properties.
