Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In Central Asia, its presence is documented within the borders of Turkmenistan. Moving toward the Caucasus, it can be found throughout both the North Caucasus and the Transcaucasus regions. Its range extends further into Western Asia, where it inhabits parts of Afghanistan.
