Dorstenia contrajerva

Dorstenia contrajerva, commonly known as "contrajerba," is a significant medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it is frequently utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating digestive ailments and inflammatory conditions. Understanding its botanical characteristics and bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its healing potential.

  • Scientific name: Dorstenia contrajerva
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Dorstenia contrajerva, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Rosalales. Finally, it is a member of the family Moraceae and the genus Dorstenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a remarkably wide and fragmented geographical distribution across several global regions. In Eastern Asia, it can be found specifically within the Ogasawara-shoto islands. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in India, and into Indo-China, particularly in Vietnam. Within the Malesia region, the species is present in Jawa. Surprisingly, the plant also reaches as far west as the Southeastern United States, specifically in Florida.
