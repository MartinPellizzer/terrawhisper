Caralluma fimbriata

Caralluma fimbriata, commonly known as the desert succulent, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to arid regions of India. Renowned for its unique phytochemical properties, it is traditionally utilized for its potential anti-obesity effects, appetite suppression, and metabolic benefits, offering significant interest in modern pharmacological research and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Caralluma fimbriata
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Caralluma fimbriata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Gentianales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Apocynaceae and the genus Caralluma.
