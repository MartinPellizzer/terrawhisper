Plant Parts

This plant, Ziziphus lotus, is a hardy shrub whose medicinal properties are distributed across various components of its anatomy. While the leaves, bark, and fruits are frequently utilized in traditional remedies to treat different ailments, the reproductive cycle involves the production of fine pollen within its small, inconspicuous flowers. This pollen plays a critical role in the plant's pollination process, ensuring the development of the drupes that are often harvested for their therapeutic potential.
