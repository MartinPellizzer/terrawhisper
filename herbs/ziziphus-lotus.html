Lotus, scientifically known as Ziziphus lotus, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Valued for its ecological importance and traditional medicinal properties, it offers therapeutic benefits through its leaves and fruits. This hardy species plays a vital role in supporting local biodiversity and traditional wellness practices.
