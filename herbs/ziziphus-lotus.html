Ziziphus lotus

Ziziphus lotus, commonly known as the lotus jujube, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to North Africa and the Middle East. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is used in traditional healing to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering valuable bioactive compounds for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphus lotus
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ziziphus.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several key regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is notably recorded in Spain. This African coverage includes significant populations found in both Algeria and Egypt.
