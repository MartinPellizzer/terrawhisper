Calea urticifolia

Calea urticiifolia, commonly known as "Calea de conejo," is a remarkable medicinal shrub native to Mexico. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin inflammations. Its unique phytochemical profile continues to fascinate researchers exploring natural therapeutic alternatives.

  • Scientific name: Calea urticifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Calea urticifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is identified under the genus Calea.

Common Names

This plant, Calea urticifolia, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is referred to as nettle-leaved Calea, while in Spanish, it is commonly called Calea de ortiga. Additionally, in Latin, it is known by its botanical designation, Calea urticifolia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across two distinct continents. In Africa, its presence is noted within the West-Central Tropical region, specifically in the DR Congo. The species also thrives extensively throughout various regions of Mexico. Its Mexican range includes the central, northeastern, and northwestern territories.
