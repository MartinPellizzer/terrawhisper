Euphorbia kansui

Euphorbia kansui, commonly known as Kansui, is a perennial herb deeply rooted in traditional East Asian medicine. Valued for its potent diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties, it is primarily used to treat edema, hypertension, and various skin conditions. This medicinal plant requires careful handling due to its bioactive chemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia kansui
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Euphorbia.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Euphorbia kansui, is highly valued in traditional medicine for the specific components harvested from its underground structures. The most significant part of the organism is the root, which serves as the primary source of its medicinal properties. Practitioners often focus on the dried tuber or the dried roots to ensure stability and potency during processing. From these harvested elements, a specialized substance known as kansui is derived, which is essential for various therapeutic applications. Ultimately, the medicinal value of the plant is concentrated within these roots, making the careful collection and preparation of the subterranean parts vital to its use.
