Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Euphorbia kansui, is highly valued in traditional medicine for the specific components harvested from its underground structures. The most significant part of the organism is the root, which serves as the primary source of its medicinal properties. Practitioners often focus on the dried tuber or the dried roots to ensure stability and potency during processing. From these harvested elements, a specialized substance known as kansui is derived, which is essential for various therapeutic applications. Ultimately, the medicinal value of the plant is concentrated within these roots, making the careful collection and preparation of the subterranean parts vital to its use.
