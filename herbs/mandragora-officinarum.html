Mediterranean mandrake, scientifically known as Mandragora officiocarum, is a perennial herb steeped in ancient folklore and mystical significance. Renowned for its distinctive anthropomorphic root, this potent botanical has been utilized across various cultures for its sedative, analgesic, and hallucinogenic properties, serving as a cornerstone in historical pharmacopoeia and alchemy.
