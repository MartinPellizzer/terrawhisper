Nectandra angustifolia

Nectandra angustifolia, commonly known as "pau-ferro" or ironwood, is a medicinal shrub native to South American landscapes. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Nectandra angustifolia
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Nectandra, specifically as the species Nectandra angustifolia.

Common Names

This plant, Nectandra angustifolia, is known by various regional names depending on the language and local tradition. In Spanish, it is frequently referred to as muringa, while in Portuguese, it is commonly called muringa or muringa-do-campo. In English, it is sometimes known as the narrow-leaved muringa.
