Vincetoxicum indicum var. indicum

Vincetoxicum indicum var. indicum, commonly known as Indian Vincetoxicum, is a climbing perennial herb native to South Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant contains diverse bioactive alkaloids. It is primarily studied for its potential therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities, offering significant promise in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Vincetoxicum indicum var. indicum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa
