Alsophila nilgirensis

Alsophila nilgirensis, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, is an endemic species native to the Nilgiri Hills. Valued in traditional healing practices, this plant possesses unique phytochemical properties. Research continues into its potential therapeutic applications, highlighting its significance for biodiversity conservation and the development of natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Alsophila nilgirensis
  • Family: Cyatheaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Alsophila nilgirensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae, it is classified under the order Cyatheales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cyatheaceae and is categorized under the genus Alsophila.
