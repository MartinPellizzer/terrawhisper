Dimorphandra mollis

Dimorphandra mollis, commonly known as the soft-leaf dimorphandra, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to support digestive health and reduce inflammation, offering a fascinating bridge between ancient botanical wisdom and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Dimorphandra mollis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is identified by the genus Dimorphandra.
