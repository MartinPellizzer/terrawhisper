Loxogramme chinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Loxogramme chinensis?

Loxogramme chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following its taxonomic hierarchy through the order Polypodiales, it is placed in the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Loxogramme and its specific species name, chinensis.
