Commelina paludosa

Commelina paludosa, commonly known as the marshline shrub, is a resilient medicinal plant native to wetland ecosystems. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it has been traditionally utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments. Understanding its therapeutic potential offers promising insights into modern pharmacology and natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Commelina paludosa
  • Family: Commelinaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Commelina paludosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Commelinales and the family Commelinaceae. Ultimately, it is situated within the genus Commelina.
