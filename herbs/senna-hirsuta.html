Woolly senna, scientifically known as Senna hirsuta, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive hairy foliage, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine for its potent purgative and laxative properties, often serving as a natural remedy to support digestive health and regularity.
