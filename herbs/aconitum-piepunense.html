Aconitum piepunense

Aconitum piepunense, commonly known as a member of the potent monkshood family, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its complex alkaloids, it requires expert handling due to its toxicity. When used traditionally, it serves as a powerful tool in specialized pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum piepunense
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aconitum piepunense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling specifically under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and is situated within the genus Aconitum.
