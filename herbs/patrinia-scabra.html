Patrinia scabra

Patrinia scabra, commonly known as "Baishishen" in traditional Chinese medicine, is a perennial herb valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is extensively used to treat inflammation, coughs, and skin ailments. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource in holistic healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Patrinia scabra
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae and is identified by the genus Patriina. Specifically, the species is known as Patriina scabra.
