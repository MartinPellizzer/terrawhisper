Lemna minor

Lemna minor, commonly known as duckweed, is a tiny, floating aquatic plant found in freshwater ecosystems worldwide. Despite its diminutive size, it holds significant medicinal potential, offering antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This resilient species serves as a valuable resource in traditional healing and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Lemna minor
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, commonly known as common duckweed, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Alismatales and the family Araceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lemna.
