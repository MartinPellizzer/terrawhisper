Lizard Tail

Houttuynia cordata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (991 studies) · ★★★★★

Lizard Tail, scientifically known as Houttuynia cordata, is a versatile perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its heart-shaped leaves and potent bioactive compounds, it offers significant anti-inflammatory, antiviral, and antibacterial properties. This resilient plant is increasingly studied for its potential in supporting immune health and holistic wellness.
