Chinese Plum-yew, scientifically known as Cephalotaxus fortunei, is a significant medicinal evergreen native to East Asia. Renowned for its alkaloid content, this plant serves as a vital source for developing anti-cancer compounds. Its bioactive properties make it a subject of intense pharmacological interest in modern drug discovery and oncology.
