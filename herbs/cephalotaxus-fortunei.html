What's the taxonomical classification of Cephalotaxus fortunei?

Cephalotaxus fortunei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Pinidae, falling further into the order Pinales. The plant is a member of the family Cephalotaxaceae, which contains the genus Cephalotaxus, ultimately leading to its specific identification as the species fortunei.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cephalotaxus fortunei has evergreen, lanceolate leaves that are approximately 2 to 5 centimeters long, dark green on the upper surface, and slightly paler underneath. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the leaf axils, eventually developing into fleshy, bright red or orange drupe-like arils. These arils surround a single, hard seed which is the primary reproductive structure of the species. The overall texture of the foliage is fine and dense, making it a popular choice for ornamental landscaping.
