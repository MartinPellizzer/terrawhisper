Cephalotaxus fortunei

Cephalotaxus fortunei, commonly known as Fortune's Yew, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its complex alkaloids. Found in East Asia, this evergreen shrub is researched extensively for its potential anti-tumor properties. Its bioactive compounds offer promising pathways in pharmacological studies, particularly in developing advanced cancer treatments.

  • Scientific name: Cephalotaxus fortunei
  • Family: Cephalotaxaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
