Ayapana triplinervis

Ayapana triplinervis, commonly known as the Golden Leaf Herb, is a potent medicinal plant revered in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its exceptional anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health and boost immune function, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Ayapana triplinervis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
