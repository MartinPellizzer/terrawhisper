Lychnis fulgens

What's the taxonomical classification of Lychnis fulgens?

Lychnis fulgens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Caryophyllales. This plant is a member of the family Caryophyllaceae, specifically belonging to the genus Lychnis, and is identified by its unique species name, fulgens.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lychnis fulgens has erect, slender stems that typically reach heights of 30 to 60 centimeters and feature lanceolate, opposite leaves with smooth margins. The foliage is primarily green and arranged in a way that supports the upward growth of the flowering stalks. Brightly colored, funnel-shaped flowers emerge from the upper portions of these stems, characterized by their vibrant scarlet or red hues. Each bloom possesses five distinct petals that often appear slightly notched at the tips. The plant also produces small, rounded seed capsules that develop once the flowering period concludes.
