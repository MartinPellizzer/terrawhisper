Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Specifically, it is part of the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Rosales, falling into the family Rhamnaceae. It is identified by the genus Scutia and the species Scutia myrtiina.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Burundi and Rwanda. Its presence is also well-documented within the vast territory of the DR Congo. Moving toward Northeast Tropical Africa, the species is located within the borders of Ethiopia. Finally, its range extends through the Sudan and South Sudan regions.
