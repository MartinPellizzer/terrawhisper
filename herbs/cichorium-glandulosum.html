Cichorium glandulosum

Cichorium glandulosum, commonly known as glandular chicory, is a distinctive perennial herb native to Europe. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains bioactive compounds used traditionally to support digestive health and liver function. This plant is characterized by its glandular hairs and bright yellow flowers, making it botanically unique.

  • Scientific name: Cichorium glandulosum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it falls into the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cichorium.
