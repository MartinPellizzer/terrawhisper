Distribution

This plant is found across several regions in Northern Europe. Specifically, its range includes the country of Denmark. It is also widely distributed throughout the Scandinavian peninsula, occurring in both Norway and Sweden. Furthermore, the species can be located in Finland. Finally, its geographical presence extends to the islands of Great Britain.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as fragrant Solomon's seal, possesses a specialized underground structure known as a rhizome. This fleshy, creeping stem serves as a vital storage organ, allowing the plant to persist through dormant seasons and spread horizontally through the soil. In medicinal applications, it is often this thickened rhizome that is harvested and processed, as it contains the concentrated bioactive compounds valued for their therapeutic properties.
