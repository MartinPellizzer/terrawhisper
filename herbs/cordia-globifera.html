Cordia globifera

Cordia globifera, commonly known as the "palo de sombra," is a versatile tropical tree prized for its dense shade and medicinal properties. Traditionally used in folk medicine, its various parts are utilized to treat skin conditions and inflammatory ailments, making it a significant botanical resource in many warm ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Cordia globifera
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Cordia globifera, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Cordia.
