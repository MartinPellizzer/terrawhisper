Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as tansy ragwort, derives much of its therapeutic value from its chemical composition, specifically the essential oil extracted from its foliage and flowering tops. This volatile oil contains various bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's traditional use in folk medicine. Because the essential oil is highly concentrated, it is often utilized in minute quantities for its aromatic and medicinal properties, though careful dosage is required due to its potency.
