Bangalay, scientifically known as Eucalyptus botryoides, is a versatile Australian native tree prized for its medicinal properties. Renowned for its aromatic, oil-rich leaves, it offers potent antimicrobial and decongestant benefits. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, this resilient species serves as a vital resource in natural healing practices.
