What's the taxonomical classification of Canarium pimela?

Canarium pimela belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized into the order Sapindales and falls under the family Burseraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Canaarium and the specific species designation pimela.
