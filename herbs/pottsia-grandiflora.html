Pottsia grandiflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic lineage further, it falls under the order Gentianaales and is a member of the family Apocynaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Pottsia, and its species designation, grandiflora.
