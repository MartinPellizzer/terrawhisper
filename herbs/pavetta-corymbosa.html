Pavetta corymbosa

Pavetta corymbosa, commonly known as the white jasmine bush, is a fragrant shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its striking white flowers, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its diverse therapeutic properties, often utilized to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and inflammatory issues.

  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
