Corydalis cava

Hollowroot

Corydalis cava, commonly known as Lungwort or Earthroot, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine, its rhizomes contain various alkaloids with potent sedative, analgesic, and antispasmodic properties. This plant is widely researched for its potential therapeutic effects on the nervous system.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis cava
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the Papaveraceae family and falls under the genus Corydalis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Corydalis cava, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Poland, it is called Kokorycz pusta, while in France, it is known as Corydale creuse. In Frisian, the plant is called Holwoartel, and in Hungarian, it is identified as odvas keltike. Additionally, in Ukrainian, it is commonly known as Ряст порожнистий.
