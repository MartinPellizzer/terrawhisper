Allium ochotense

Allium ochotense, commonly known as the Mongolian onion, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its culinary and medicinal properties. Native to East Asia, it is rich in bioactive compounds like organosulfur, offering potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. This plant is widely used to support digestive and cardiovascular health.

  • Scientific name: Allium ochotense
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Allium ochotense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaryllidaceae and falls under the genus Allium.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several specific regions within the Russian Far East. Its presence is documented throughout the Amur region and the Kamchatka Peninsula. Additionally, it can be found growing in the Khabarovsk area. The species also inhabits the Kuril Islands. Finally, its geographical range extends into the Primorye territory.
