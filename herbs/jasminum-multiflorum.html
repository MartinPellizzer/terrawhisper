Jasminum multiflorum

Jasminum multiflorum, commonly known as many-flowered jasmine, is a fragrant, climbing shrub celebrated for its delicate, star-shaped blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this botanical treasure holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its essential oils and extracts are utilized for their calming, antiseptic, and restorative therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Jasminum multiflorum
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic hierarchy places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the Oleaceae family within the genus Jasminum.
