Alepidea amatymbica

Alepidea amatymbica, commonly known as the Cape Alepidia, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its unique botanical features, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize various parts of this plant to treat diverse ailments, highlighting its profound importance in regional folk medicine.

  • Scientific name: Alepidea amatymbica
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
