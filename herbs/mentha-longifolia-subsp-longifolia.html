Common Horse Mint

Mentha longifolia subsp. longifolia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (21 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Common Horse Mint, scientifically known as Mentha longifolia subsp. longifolia, is a perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Renowned for its aromatic, serrated leaves, this vigorous plant offers therapeutic benefits, including digestive support and antimicrobial properties. It thrives in moist habitats, serving as a versatile botanical remedy.
