Angelicas, scientifically known as Angelica, is a genus of aromatic herbs valued for centuries in traditional medicine. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is used to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and bolster the immune system. Its potent essential oils make it a staple in herbal wellness.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified in the family Apiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Angelica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the northern reaches of Europe. In the region of Northern Europe, it can be found growing in Denmark and Finland. Its presence is also noted in the Faroe Islands, which are referred to as Føroyar. Additionally, the species is documented throughout Great Britain. Finally, the plant's range extends to the island of Iceland.

Plant Parts

This plant is highly valued in traditional medicine, with various components offering unique therapeutic benefits. Most commonly, the root is harvested for its potent aromatic properties, and in many pharmacopoeias, the dried radix is specifically used to treat digestive and respiratory issues. While some preparations utilize the roots collectively to ensure a high concentration of active compounds, others may call for the whole Angelica to capture the full spectrum of its medicinal essence. Additionally, the seeds are sometimes employed for their specific strengthening effects, making the entire organism a versatile resource in herbal healing.
