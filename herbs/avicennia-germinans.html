Plant Parts

This plant, Avicennia germinans, commonly known as the black mangrove, utilizes its specialized leaves to thrive in harsh, saline environments. These leaves are typically thick and leathery, featuring a distinctively smooth, dark green upper surface and a paler underside. To manage high salt concentrations, the leaves possess specialized salt glands that actively excrete excess salt, often leaving visible white crystals on their surface. Beyond salt regulation, the leaves are essential for photosynthesis and serve as a primary source of medicinal compounds used in various traditional applications.
