What's the taxonomical classification of Solanum pittosporifolium?

Solanum pittosporifolium belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Solanum, and its unique species name, pittosporifolium.
