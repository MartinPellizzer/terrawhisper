Bombax ceiba

Bombax ceiba, commonly known as the Red Silk Cotton tree, is a magnificent deciduous species prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its vibrant crimson flowers, every part of this plant—from its bark to its seeds—offers therapeutic properties, used widely to treat inflammation, fever, and various digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Bombax ceiba
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Bombax ceiba, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Bombax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its distribution also extends to the tropical island of Hainan within Chinese borders. Moving further east, the species is naturally occurring in Taiwan. Finally, its range reaches into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the Assam region.
