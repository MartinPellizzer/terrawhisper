Pulsatilla sulphuridea, scientifically known as Pulsatilla alpina subsp. apiifolia, is a resilient alpine perennial celebrated for its striking bell-shaped flowers. Native to high-altitude meadows, this botanical treasure is valued in traditional herbalism for its potent properties, though it must be handled with extreme care due to its toxicity.
