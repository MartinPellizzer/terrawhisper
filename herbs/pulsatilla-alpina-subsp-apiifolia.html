Pulsatilla sulphuridea, scientifically known as Pulsatilla alpina subsp. apiifolia, is a resilient alpine perennial celebrated for its striking bell-shaped flowers. Native to high-altitude meadows, this botanical treasure is valued in traditional herbalism for its potent properties, though it must be handled with extreme care due to its toxicity.

Plant Parts

This plant, Pulsatilla alpina subsp. apiifolia, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties through its flowering aerial parts. These above-ground structures consist of the stems, leaves, and highly distinctive blooms that emerge during the growing season. The flowering aerial parts contain the concentrated secondary metabolites responsible for the plant's therapeutic effects, making them the essential component harvested for pharmacological use.
