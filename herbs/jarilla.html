Jarilla, scientifically known as Larrea divaricata, is a resilient shrub native to the arid landscapes of South America. Renowned for its profound medicinal properties, this plant is widely utilized in traditional healing to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and respiratory ailments, offering a powerful natural remedy from the desert.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Jarilla, utilizes various structures for its medicinal purposes, though its small and delicate flower is one of its most distinctive botanical features. While many of its healing properties are often associated with the aromatic leaves and woody stems, the blossoms play a vital role in the plant's reproductive cycle and contribute to the overall chemical profile used in traditional remedies.
