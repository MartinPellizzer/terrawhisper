Huperzia phlegmaria

Huperzia phlegmaria, commonly known as the tropical clubmoss, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent neuroprotective properties, it contains the alkaloid huperzine A. This compound is widely studied for its ability to enhance memory and treat cognitive impairments like Alzheimer's disease.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia phlegmaria
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Lycopodiidae and order Lycopodiales, it is further categorized into the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Huperzia.

Moving further east, the species is also documented within West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found inhabiting the territories of Cameroon and Equatorial Guinea. Plant Parts

This plant, Huperzia phlegmaria, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts. As a lycopod species, its medicinal properties are concentrated in the above-ground structures, which include the photosynthetic stems and the small, scale-like leaves that characterize its growth habit. Because the bioactive alkaloids are found within these aerial components, the stems and foliage are the specific parts harvested and processed for therapeutic applications.
