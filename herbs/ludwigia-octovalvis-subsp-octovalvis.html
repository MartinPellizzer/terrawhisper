Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the West-Central Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the country of Burundi, extending its range across various territories. The species is also documented in the Cabinda enclave and throughout the Central African Republic. Furthermore, its presence continues through the nation of Congo. Finally, the geographical reach of this subspecies includes the forested areas of Gabon.
