Combretum molle

Combretum molle, commonly known as the Fluff-pod or Soft Combretum, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to subtropical Africa. Renowned for its striking, pendulous red inflorescences, this botanical treasure holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various traditional medicinal practices utilizing its leaves and bark to treat diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Combretum molle
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, falling into the family Combretaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Combretum.
