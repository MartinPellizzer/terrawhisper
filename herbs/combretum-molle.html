Velvet bushwillow, scientifically known as Combretum molle, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to African savannas. Renowned for its soft, velvety foliage and vibrant pink flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts are traditionally utilized in medicinal practices to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues.

This plant, Combretum molle, possesses various anatomical components that contribute to its medicinal profile. The stem bark is a particularly significant part, frequently harvested for its bioactive compounds used in various therapeutic applications.
