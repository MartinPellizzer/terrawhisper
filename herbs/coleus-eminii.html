Coleus eminii

Coleus eminii, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional healing, belongs to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and pharmacological potential is essential for exploring its role in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Coleus eminii
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Coleus, specifically the species Coleus eminii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Burundi, Cameroon, and Rwanda. Its presence also extends significantly into the Democratic Republic of the Congo within the same central belt. Additionally, the species reaches into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Ethiopia. Together, these locations define the primary geographical range of this medicinal species.
