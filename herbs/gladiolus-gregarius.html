Gladiolus gregarius

Gladiolus gregarius, commonly known as the common gladiolus, is a resilient perennial herb native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its striking, sword-shaped foliage and vibrant floral displays, this medicinal plant offers significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in diverse healing practices for its unique bioactive properties and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Gladiolus gregarius
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Gladiolus gregarius, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Iridaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Gladiolus.
