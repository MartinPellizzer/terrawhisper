Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions of the African continent. Within South Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving into the Southern African region, it can be found throughout the Cape Provinces. Additionally, the species is known to inhabit the mountainous terrain of Lesotho. Its range also extends to the coastal and inland areas of KwaZulu-Natal.
