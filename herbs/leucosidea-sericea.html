Leucosidea sericea

Oldwood

Leucosidea sericea, commonly known as the Silver-leafed Leucosidea, is a significant medicinal shrub native to the moist forests of South Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research and development.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Leucosidea.
