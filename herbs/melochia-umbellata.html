Melochia umbellata

Melochia umbellaata, commonly known as Umbrella Melochia, is a versatile tropical shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to various warm regions, it is celebrated for its medicinal properties, particularly in treating inflammation and skin ailments. This resilient plant offers significant ethnobotanical potential for modern pharmacological research and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Melochia umbellata
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its taxonomic descent through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, it is a member of the family Malvaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Melochia as Melochia umbellata.

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of South and Southeast Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in Bangladesh, India, and Sri Lanka.
