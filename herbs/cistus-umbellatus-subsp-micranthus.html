Cistus umbellatus subsp. micranthus

Cistus umbellatus subsp. micranthus, commonly known as a subspecies of Rockrose, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub valued for its medicinal potential. Rich in polyphenols and essential oils, it possesses antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used in herbal remedies, this plant offers promising bioactive compounds for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Cistus umbellatus subsp. micranthus
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, falling into the family Cistaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Cistus, specifically as the subspecies umbellatus micranthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a very specific and limited geographical range within the natural world. Its distribution is primarily concentrated in the region of Southeastern Europe. Specifically, the subspecies micrantus can be found growing in the landscape of Greece. Because its habitat is so localized, it does not occur widely across other continents. This narrow presence makes it a distinct botanical feature of the Greek territory.
