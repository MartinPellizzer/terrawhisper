Ziziphora clinopodioides

Ziziphora clinopodioides, commonly known as a member of the mint family, is a resilient aromatic herb found in rocky, mountainous terrains. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties, including antimicrobial and antioxidant effects, making it a subject of interest for natural therapeutic applications and ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphora clinopodioides
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ziziphora, specifically as the species Ziziphora clinopodioides.

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Eastern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within South European Russia.
