Didissandra sesquifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Didissandra sesquifolia?

Didissandra sesquifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving through its specific hierarchy, the plant is part of the order Lamiales and falls under the family Gesneriaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Didissandra, and its unique species designation, sesquifolia.
