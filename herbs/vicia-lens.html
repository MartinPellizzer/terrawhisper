Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the lentil, is primarily valued for its nutrient-dense seeds, which serve as the principal medicinal and nutritional component. These small, lens-shaped seeds are packed with essential proteins, fiber, and various micronutrients that contribute to overall health and wellness. In various traditional medicinal practices, the processed seeds are utilized to support digestive health and provide sustained energy, making them a vital part of a therapeutic diet.
