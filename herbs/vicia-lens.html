Vicia lens

Vicia lens, commonly known as the lentil, is a vital medicinal and nutritional plant valued for centuries. Beyond its dietary importance, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Rich in essential proteins, vitamins, and minerals, it supports metabolic health, provides antioxidant protection, and plays a crucial role in traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Vicia lens
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Vicia.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Vicia lens, is recognized by various names across different languages and cultures. In Chinese, it is commonly referred to as 兵豆 (including the variants 兵豆 in zh-hans and zh-cn), while in Azerbaijani, it is known as Mərcimək. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species can be found growing in the landscapes of Austria. It also maintains a significant presence within the territories of Czechia-Slovakia. Finally, the plant is commonly distributed throughout Germany, completing its regional range.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the lentil, is primarily valued for its nutrient-dense seeds, which serve as the principal medicinal and nutritional component. These small, lens-shaped seeds are packed with essential proteins, fiber, and various micronutrients that contribute to overall health and wellness. In various traditional medicinal practices, the processed seeds are utilized to support digestive health and provide sustained energy, making them a vital part of a therapeutic diet.
