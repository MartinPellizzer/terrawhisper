Aucuba himalaica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Garryales and the family Garryaceae, eventually reaching its specific designation within the genus Aucuba. As a member of the species himalica, this plant occupies a distinct niche within its botanical classification.
