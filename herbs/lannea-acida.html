Lannea acida

Lannea acida, commonly known as the "acid leaf," is a versatile shrub native to various African regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional healing to treat ailments such as digestive issues, skin infections, and inflammation, offering a natural resource for holistic wellness and recovery.

  • Scientific name: Lannea acida
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
