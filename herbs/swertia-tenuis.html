Swertia tenuis

Swertiia tenuis, commonly known as Swertia, is a perennial herb highly valued in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its bitter principles, it serves as a potent digestive tonic and liver stimulant. Often used to treat fevers and metabolic disorders, this plant plays a vital role in traditional holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Swertia tenuis
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Gentianales and falls into the family Gentianaecae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Swertia.
