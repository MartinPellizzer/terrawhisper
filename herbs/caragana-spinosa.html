Caragana spinosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Caragana spinosa?

Caragana spinosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Caragana, and its unique species designation, spinosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Caragana spinosa has a woody, shrubby growth habit characterized by stiff, branched stems that often terminate in sharp, woody spines. Small, inconspicuous flowers typically emerge from the axils of the leaves during the growing season. The plant's architecture is dense and spreading, providing a rugged structural appearance. Its bark is relatively smooth on younger stems but becomes more textured as the plant matures.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant, commonly known as the karoo pea, thrives in well-draining, sandy soils within semi-arid environments and requires full sun exposure for optimal growth.

It is a hardy legume that can withstand significant drought once its root system is established. Cultivation involves sowing seeds directly into the ground during the warmer months to ensure successful germination. Because it is nitrogen-fixing, it improves soil quality and requires minimal fertilization.
