Valeriana jatamansi

Nardostachys jatamansi (jones ex roxb.) dc.

Valeriana jatamansi, commonly known as Jatamansi or Spikenard, is a revered medicinal herb in Ayurveda. Renowned for its potent sedative and anxiolytic properties, this aromatic plant is widely used to alleviate stress, insomnia, and neurological disorders. Its calming essence offers a natural approach to restoring mental equilibrium and tranquility.

  • Scientific name: Valeriana jatamansi
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Valeriana.

Its presence in China is extensive, spanning from the north-central territories to the southeast regions. Furthermore, the species is naturally distributed throughout the high-altitude terrain of Tibet. Finally, within the Indian Subcontinent, the plant is specifically located in the state of Assam.

Plant Parts

This plant, Valeriana jatamansi, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its various therapeutic properties derived from different anatomical structures. The primary medicinal components are found within the underground rhizomes and the fleshy roots, which contain the essential volatile oils and alkaloids required for efficacy. In certain preparations, the rootlet structures are also harvested alongside the main body to ensure a complete profile of active constituents. While some herbal formulations utilize the whole plants to capture a broader spectrum of nutrients, most concentrated extracts focus specifically on the subterranean parts. Whether processed as the whole plant or isolated parts, the potency of this species relies heavily on the quality of its root system.
