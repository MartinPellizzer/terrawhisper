Calabar bean

Physostigma venenosum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (120 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Calabar bean, scientifically known as Physostiema venenosa, is a potent tropical climbing plant renowned for its bioactive alkaloids. Primarily recognized for containing physostigmine, it has historically been used as a powerful purgative and muscle relaxant. Today, its chemical properties remain vital in pharmacological research and neurological studies.

The life history of Physostigma venenosum is characterized by its classification as a phanerophyte, specifically manifesting as a nanophanerophyte, where its perennating buds are located well above the soil surface. As a perennial climber, it maintains an evergreen deciduousness, ensuring that its foliage remains functional throughout its growth cycles rather than shedding seasonally. This life form strategy, functioning primarily as a phanerophyte, allows the plant to establish a robust presence in its habitat by protecting its vital reproductive and vegetative buds above the ground level.
