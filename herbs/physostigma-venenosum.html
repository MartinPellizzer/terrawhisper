Physostigma venenosum

Calabar bean

Physostigma venenosum, commonly known as Calabar bean, is a tropical climbing vine native to West Africa. Renowned for its potent alkaloid, physostigmine, this plant has significant historical and medicinal importance. While used traditionally, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling, as it acts as a powerful acetylcholinesterase inhibitor.

  • Scientific name: Physostigma venenosum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Physostigma.
