Calabar bean, scientifically known as Physostiema venenosa, is a potent tropical climbing plant renowned for its bioactive alkaloids. Primarily recognized for containing physostigmine, it has historically been used as a powerful purgative and muscle relaxant. Today, its chemical properties remain vital in pharmacological research and neurological studies.
