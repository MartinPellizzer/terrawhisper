Clematis grata

Clematis gratas, commonly known as the Clematis vine, is a captivating medicinal plant celebrated for its diverse therapeutic properties. Often utilized in traditional herbalism, this climbing species offers unique bioactive compounds. Understanding its botanical characteristics and healing potential provides valuable insights into its role in holistic wellness and natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Clematis grata
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Clematis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution stretching across several distinct regions of Asia. In Western Asia, it can be found within the borders of Afghanistan. Its presence extends into China, specifically localized in the Tibet region. The species is also distributed throughout Eastern Asia, inhabiting both Taiwan and the Nansei-shoto islands. Finally, it populates the East Himalaya area within the Indian Subcontinent.
