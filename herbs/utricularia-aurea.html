What's the taxonomical classification of Utricularia aurea?

Utricularia aurea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic lineage further, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lentibulariaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Utricularia, and its specific species name, aurea.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Utricularia aurea has small, creeping, threadlike stolons that produce tiny, bladder-like traps for catching aquatic microorganisms. Each blossom features a distinct bilabiate structure with a prominent lower lip and a small spur at the back.
