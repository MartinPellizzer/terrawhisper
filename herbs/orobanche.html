Broomrape, scientifically known as Orobanche, is a genus of parasitic plants that lacks chlorophyll, making it unable to photosynthesize. Instead, it survives by attaching to the roots of host plants to extract essential nutrients. While often viewed as agricultural pests, certain species hold significant potential in traditional medicinal practices.
