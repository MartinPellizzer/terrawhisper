Orobanche

Broomrape

Orobanche, commonly known as broomrape, is a unique genus of parasitic plants that lack chlorophyll. Lacking the ability to photosynthesize, these specialized organisms derive all their nutrients and water from the roots of host plants. While often considered agricultural pests, certain species hold significant potential in traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Orobanche
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida within the subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Orobanche.
