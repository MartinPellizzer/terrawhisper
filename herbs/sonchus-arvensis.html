Sonchus arvensis

Common sow thistle

Sonchus arvensis, commonly known as annual sow thistle, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Characteristically identified by its jagged leaves and yellow flowering heads, this plant has been traditionally utilized for its diuretic, digestive, and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Sonchus arvensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling into the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Sonchus.
