Common Sow Thistle

Sonchus arvensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (29 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Common Sow Thistle, scientifically known as Sonchus arvensis, is a versatile perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Often found in disturbed soils and meadows, this plant features distinctive milky sap and yellow composite flowers. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it possesses diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties used for digestive health.
