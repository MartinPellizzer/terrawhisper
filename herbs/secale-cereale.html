The rye plant, scientifically known as Secale cereale, is a resilient cereal grain valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive issues and inflammation, it contains bioactive compounds that support metabolic health, making it a significant botanical subject in historical pharmacology.
