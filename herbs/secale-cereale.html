The rye plant, scientifically known as Secale cereale, is a resilient cereal grain valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive issues and inflammation, it contains bioactive compounds that support metabolic health, making it a significant botanical subject in historical pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant, Secale cerealum, exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several European regions. In Northern Europe, it is commonly found throughout Great Britain and Ireland. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is well-established in Austria. It also maintains a significant presence in Belgium within the same central region. Furthermore, its range extends through the territories of Czechia and Slovakia.
