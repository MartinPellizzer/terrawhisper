Secale cereale

Secale cereale, commonly known as rye, is a resilient cereal grain valued for both nutrition and medicinal properties. Historically used in traditional remedies, it possesses antioxidant and anti-inflammatory qualities. Beyond its dietary importance, rye contributes essential fiber and bioactive compounds that support digestive health and metabolic functions in humans.

  • Scientific name: Secale cereale
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales and family Poaceae, it is classified under the genus Secale.
