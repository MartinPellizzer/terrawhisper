Lilium brownii var. brownii

Lilium brownii var. brownii, commonly known as Brown's Lily, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific high-altitude regions. Renowned for its unique morphological features, this variety holds significant value in traditional herbal practices. Its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic benefits, making it a subject of botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Lilium brownii var. brownii
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Liliaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lilium, specifically as the variety brownii.

Common Names

This plant, Lillium brownii var. brownii, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Brown's lily, while in Chinese, it is known as 棕色百合 (Zōngsè bǎihé). In Japanese, the species is called ブラウンリリー (Buraun rirī).

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions within China. It can be found growing in the South-Central parts of the country.
