Plant Parts

This plant, Atalantia monophylla, commonly known as the West Indian Lime, possesses various components that contribute to its traditional medicinal profile. While the leaves, fruit, and bark are frequently utilized in folk remedies for their aromatic and therapeutic properties, the heartwood represents a more internal structural element of the plant. In certain ethnobotanical applications, different segments of the woody tissue are examined for their chemical constituents, though the heartwood specifically serves as the dense, central core of the stem, providing necessary structural integrity to the plant during its growth.
