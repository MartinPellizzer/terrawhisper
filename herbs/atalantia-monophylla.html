Atalantia monophylla

Atalantia monophylla, commonly known as the West Indian Lime, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its aromatic leaves and citrus fruits, it offers potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. It is frequently utilized to support digestive health, boost immunity, and alleviate various skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Atalantia monophylla
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Atalantia.
