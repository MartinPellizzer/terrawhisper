Asian Wild Raspberry

Rubus ellipticus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (58 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Asian Wild Raspberry, scientifically known as Rubus ellipticus, is a versatile, thorny shrub native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned for its edible, succulent fruits, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. Often used to treat inflammation and digestive issues, this plant bridges the gap between nutrition and healing.
