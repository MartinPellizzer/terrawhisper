Asian Wild Raspberry

Rubus ellipticus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (58 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Asian Wild Raspberry, scientifically known as Rubus ellipticus, is a versatile, thorny shrub native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned for its edible, succulent fruits, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. Often used to treat inflammation and digestive issues, this plant bridges the gap between nutrition and healing.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several continents. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is noted specifically within Tanzania. It is also found in South Tropical Africa, occupying regions in both Malawi and Mozambique. Moving further south, the species can be located in the KwaZulu-Natal province of Southern Africa. Additionally, its distribution extends to the eastern hemisphere, specifically within China South-Central.
