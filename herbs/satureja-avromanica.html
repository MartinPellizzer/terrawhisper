Satureja avromanica

Satureja avromanica, commonly known as the golden healer, is a potent medicinal herb native to tropical highlands. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant contains unique bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and digestive ailments. Its rapid growth and resilience make it a vital resource in traditional medicine.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
