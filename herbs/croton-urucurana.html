Croton urucurana

Croton urucurana, commonly known as urucuana, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties. This plant offers significant pharmacological potential for developing natural treatments in modern integrative medicine and therapeutics.

  • Scientific name: Croton urucurana
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Croton.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across the diverse landscapes of Western South America. Its distribution includes significant populations found within the borders of Bolivia and Peru. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, it is widely documented throughout various regions of Brazil. Specifically, it can be located in the Brazil West-Central, Northeast, and Southeast areas. This wide range highlights the plant's adaptability to different ecological zones across these South American territories.
