Plant Parts

This plant, Croton urucuana, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties derived from its gum exudate. When the bark of the tree is wounded or cut, it releases a resinous substance that serves as the principal therapeutic component. This exudate is harvested and used in various traditional applications, often valued for its potential healing and antiseptic qualities in local medicinal practices.
