Caucasian Comfrey, scientifically known as Symphytuum caucasicum, is a perennial herb native to the Caucasus region. Renowned for its traditional medicinal properties, this plant contains allantoin, which promotes skin healing and tissue regeneration. It is frequently utilized in topical applications to soothe inflammation and support musculoskeletal recovery naturally.
