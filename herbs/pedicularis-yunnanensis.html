Pedicularis yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Pedicularis yunnanensis?

Pedicularis yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Lamiales and falls within the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pedicularis and is specifically designated as the species yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pedicularis yunnanensis has an erect, herbaceous stem that typically reaches heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and features small, lanceolate to linear leaves arranged oppositely along the length. The foliage is characterized by deeply lobed or pinnately divided margins that give the plant a fine, textured appearance. At the summit of the stem, it produces a dense, spike-like inflorescence consisting of tubular, bilabiate flowers. These blossoms exhibit a distinct color pattern, often ranging from pink to reddish-purple, with a prominent upper lip that serves to protect the reproductive organs. The overall structure is adapted to its high-altitude habitat, ensuring stability and efficient pollination through its specialized floral morphology.
