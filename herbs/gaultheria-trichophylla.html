Himalayan snowberry, scientifically known as Gaultheria trichophylla, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned for its striking white berries and leathery foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and analgesic therapeutic properties.
