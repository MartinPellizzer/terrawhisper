Himalayan snowberry

Himalayan snowberry, scientifically known as Gaultheria trichophylla, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned for its striking white berries and leathery foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and analgesic therapeutic properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range spanning parts of East Asia and the Indian subcontinent. In China, its distribution is concentrated within the South-Central regions as well as the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Moving into the Indian subcontinent, it can be found growing in the lush landscapes of Assam. The species also inhabits the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya. Additionally, its presence extends across the mountainous country of Nepal.
