Myrica gale

Bog-myrtle

Myrica gale, commonly known as sweet gale, is a resilient, aromatic shrub native to peat bogs and wetlands across Europe and North America. Renowned for its fragrant leaves and distinctive resinous scent, this medicinal plant has historically been utilized for its antimicrobial, digestive, and calming properties in traditional herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Myrica gale
  • Family: Myricaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Myrricaceae and is specifically identified as part of the genus Myrica.
