Bog-myrtle

Myrica gale · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (15 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Bog-myrtle, scientifically known as Myrica gale, is an aromatic evergreen shrub native to northern European wetlands. Traditionally used in brewing and herbal medicine, its fragrant leaves possess antimicrobial and insect-repellent properties. This hardy plant thrives in peat bogs, offering a unique blend of historical utility and botanical charm.
