Trifolium medium

Trifolium medium, commonly known as the medium clover, is a versatile medicinal herb prized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall wellness. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for exploring its pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Trifolium medium
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Trifolium.
