Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is specifically nested within the family Asteraceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Eupatorium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several key regions in East Asia. Within mainland China, its presence is widespread, spanning from the North-Central provinces to the South-Central territories. It is also commonly found throughout the Southeast regions of China and on the tropical island of Hainan. Beyond the Chinese mainland, its natural range extends significantly into Eastern Asia. Specifically, the species can also be identified growing in the natural landscapes of Japan.
