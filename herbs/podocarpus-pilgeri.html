Podocarpus pilgeri

Podocarpus pilgeri, commonly known as the Pilger's Podocarp, is a distinctive evergreen conifer native to specific montane regions. Valued for its ornamental beauty and unique needle-like foliage, this botanical treasure offers significant ecological importance. Exploring its medicinal potential reveals fascinating insights into the therapeutic properties of ancient forest flora.

  • Scientific name: Podocarpus pilgeri
  • Family: Podocarpaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, falling into the family Podocarpaceae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across East and Southeast Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the Hainan province as well as throughout the China Southeast region. Its range extends into the Indo-China peninsula, where it is native to various habitats. Specifically, the species is documented within the borders of Cambodia and Laos. Finally, the geographical reach of this medicinal plant includes the territory of Vietnam.
