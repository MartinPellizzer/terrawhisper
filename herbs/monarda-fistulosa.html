Beebalm, scientifically known as Monarda fistulosa, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for its striking lavender flowers and aromatic foliage. Renowned in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, this resilient wildflower also serves as a vital nectar source, attracting pollinators and enriching diverse natural ecosystems worldwide.

Plant Parts

This plant, Monarda fistulosa, commonly known as wild bergamot, utilizes its vibrant, tubular flowers as its primary medicinal component. These distinct, lavender-colored flowers are rich in essential oils, which are often collected to create a potent flower extract. This extract serves as a concentrated source of therapeutic compounds, frequently used in herbal preparations to provide antimicrobial and soothing properties.
