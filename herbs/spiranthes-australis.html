Spiranthes australis

Spiranthes australis, commonly known as the Southern Ladies' Tresses, is a delicate perennial orchid native to wetland habitats. Characterized by its elegant, spiraling spikes of small white flowers, this plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem, attracting various pollinators while adding aesthetic charm to marshy southern landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Spiranthes australis
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Spiranthes.
