Carya cathayensis

Carya cathayensis, commonly known as a rare species of hickory, holds significant potential in ethnobotanical studies. Native to specific subtropical regions, this plant features unique chemical profiles. While primarily studied for its ecological role, researchers explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in traditional and modern medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Carya cathayensis
  • Family: Juglandaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Carya cathayensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fagales and the family Juglandaceae. Finally, its taxonomic position is completed within the genus Carya.
