Bistorta amplexicaulis var. amplexicaulis

Bistorta amplexiaucus var. amplexiaucus, commonly known as Snakeweed, is a resilient perennial herb found in alpine and subalpine regions. Renowned for its distinctive clasping leaves, this plant holds traditional medicinal value, often utilized in herbal remedies to address inflammatory conditions and support overall systemic health through its unique bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Bistorta amplexicaulis var. amplexicaulis
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Bistorta amplexicauleus var. amplexiCauleus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae and the genus Bistorta.
