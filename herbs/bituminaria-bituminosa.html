Pitch Trefoil, scientifically known as Bituminaria bituminosa, is a perennial herb belonging to the Fabaceae family. Renowned for its distinct, tar-like aromatic scent, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine. It possesses significant bioactive properties, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, making it valuable for various therapeutic applications.
