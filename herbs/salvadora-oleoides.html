Salvadora oleoides

Salvadora oleoides, commonly known as the Toothbrush Tree, is a resilient shrub native to arid regions of Africa and Asia. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its twigs are also famously utilized for natural oral hygiene and dental care.

  • Scientific name: Salvadora oleoides
  • Family: Salvadoraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
