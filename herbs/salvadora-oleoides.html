Salvadora oleoides, scientifically known as Salvadora oleoides, is a resilient, deciduous shrub native to the arid regions of India and Pakistan. Often called the "Desert Toothbrush," this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties, particularly in treating oral and digestive ailments.
