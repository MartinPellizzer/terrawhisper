Hyphaene thebaica

Doum palm

Hyphaene thebaica, commonly known as the Golden Vein herb, is a rare medicinal plant prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Found primarily in tropical rainforest ecosystems, it contains unique bioactive compounds used in traditional healing to reduce inflammation and support immune function, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Hyphaene thebaica
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Africa
