Chamaecostus cuspidatus

Chamaecostus cuspidatus, commonly known as the Spiked Broom, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Found in diverse ecosystems, this plant possesses unique phytochemical properties. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic benefits, offering promising insights into natural treatments for various inflammatory and restorative health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Chamaecostus cuspidatus
  • Family: Costaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Zingiberales and the family Costaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Chamaecostus.
