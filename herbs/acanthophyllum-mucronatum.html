Acanthophyllum mucronatum

Acanthophyllum mucronatum, commonly known as a resilient member of the Boraginaceae family, is a specialized medicinal plant native to arid regions. Renowned for its unique morphological characteristics, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Research suggests its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic benefits, particularly in traditional medicine for diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Acanthophyllum mucronatum
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Caryophyllaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Acanthophyllum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions of Western and Central Asia. Within Central Asia, its presence is notably documented in Turkmenistan. Moving toward the Caucasus, the species can be found throughout the Transcaucasus region. Its range also extends significantly into Western Asia, specifically inhabiting the territories of Iran. Furthermore, the geographical footprint of this medicinal plant includes the country of Türkiye.
