Elephant Thistle, scientifically known as Silibum eburneum, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its striking white bracts and robust architecture, this plant is valued in traditional medicine. It shares botanical similarities with milk thistle, offering potential therapeutic properties for supporting liver health.
