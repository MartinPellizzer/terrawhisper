Scabiosa atropurpurea

Scabiosa atropurpurea, commonly known as the dark pincushion flower, is a striking perennial valued for its deep purple blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism, where its unique properties have been explored for potential therapeutic applications in treating various skin-related conditions.

  • Scientific name: Scabiosa atropurpurea
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales and the family Caprifoliaceae, it is classified under the genus Scabiosa.
