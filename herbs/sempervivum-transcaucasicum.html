Sempervivum transcaucasicum

Sempervivum transcaucasicum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (6 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Scientifically known as Sempervivum transcaucasicum, is a resilient perennial succulent native to the Caucasus Mountains. Often called "houseleeks," these hardy plants are valued in traditional medicine for their anti-inflammatory and healing properties. Their thick, fleshy leaves are frequently utilized to treat minor wounds, skin irritations, and various topical ailments.
