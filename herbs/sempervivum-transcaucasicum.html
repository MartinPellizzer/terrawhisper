Sempervivum transcaucasicum

Sempervivum transcaucasicum, commonly known as the Transcaucasian Houseleek, is a resilient perennial succulent native to the Caucasus Mountains. Valued in traditional medicine for its healing properties, this hardy plant is frequently utilized to treat skin inflammations, wounds, and various dermatological conditions due to its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Sempervivum transcaucasicum
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Crassulaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Sempervivum.
