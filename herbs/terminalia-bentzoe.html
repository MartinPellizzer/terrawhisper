Terminalia bentzoe

Terminalia bentzoe, commonly known as Benzoin, is a tropical tree prized for its fragrant resin. Extracted from the bark, this aromatic substance has been utilized for centuries in traditional medicine. It serves as a powerful antiseptic, expectorant, and anti-inflammatory agent, widely used to treat respiratory ailments and skin irritations.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia bentzoe
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Combretaceae. Specifically, Terminalia bentzoe is a member of the genus Terminalia.
