Scutellaria likiangensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Scutellaria likiangensis?

Scutelariia likiangensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic journey continues through the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, which is a group well-known for its diverse herbal characteristics. Ultimately, this specific plant is identified by its genus, Scutellaria, and its unique species designation, likiangensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Scutellaria likiangensis has a woody, shrubby growth habit characterized by small, lanceolate to ovate leaves that are typically arranged oppositely along the stems. The leaf margins are generally entire, and the surface texture can vary between smooth and slightly pubescent depending on the specific environmental conditions. The inflorescence consists of small, tubular flowers that emerge from the leaf axils in a compact arrangement. These blossoms often feature a distinct bilabiate structure typical of the Lamiaceae family, frequently displaying hues of blue, purple, or white. The overall architecture of the plant supports its survival in rocky or montane habitats through its compact and resilient form.

According to a study published by "Frontiers in plant science", Scutelariia likianensis is one of four substitute medicinal species for S. baiicalensis. Metabolomics and content determination analyses were performed to reveal the interspecific chemical diversity of these species. The study found that all parts of the substitute species showed high contents of baicalin, except for the roots of S. likianensis.
