Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in the territories of Algeria and Egypt. Moving toward the western part of the continent, its presence extends into West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is documented within the borders of Benin and Burkina. Additionally, the species is known to inhabit the region of Gambia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Senna or Cassia, utilizes its seed as a primary component in various traditional medicinal applications. The seeds are often harvested and processed to harness their natural laxative properties, which are frequently used to treat constipation and assist in digestive health. Because the concentration of active compounds can vary, the careful preparation of the seed is essential to ensure both efficacy and safety when used in herbal remedies.
