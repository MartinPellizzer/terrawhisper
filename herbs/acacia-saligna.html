Acacia saligna

Acacia saligna, commonly known as the Saligna Wattle, is a versatile medicinal plant native to various arid regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues. Its resilient nature makes it a vital resource for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Acacia saligna
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Fabales and categorized under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Acacia.
