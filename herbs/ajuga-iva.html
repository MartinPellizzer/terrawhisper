Ajuga iva

Ajuga iva, commonly known as Iva bugle, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is frequently utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. This plant plays a vital role in treating respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and various skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Ajuga iva
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ajuga, specifically as the species Ajuga iva.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ajuga iva, is referred to by various regional names depending on the language and locale. In German, it is commonly called Mittelmeer-Günsel, while in Catalan, it is known as fulla de creu. Its linguistic identity extends into Arabic-speaking regions, where it is called عجوقة عطريية, and specifically in the Maghreb, where it is known as شندڨورة in the Algerian Arabic dialect (aeb-arab) and as Lmesk iẓekwan in Kabyle (kab).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing in the Balearic Islands, which are part of the Baleares archipelago. Additionally, its presence is documented across the island of Corsica and within the mainland territory of France. The species also extends its range to the coastal landscapes of Portugal. Finally, its habitat includes the island of Sardegna, completing its localized European distribution.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ajuga iva, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for its therapeutic properties derived from its foliage. The medicinal efficacy is primarily concentrated in the leaves, which are harvested to prepare various remedies. When prepared as a leaves extract, the plant offers potent bioactive compounds that are utilized to treat ailments such as digestive issues and respiratory conditions. Ultimately, the quality and potency of the treatment depend heavily on the freshness and processing of the Leaves.
