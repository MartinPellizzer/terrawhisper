Neonauclea sessilifolia

Neonauclea sessilifolia, commonly known as the sessile-leaved neonauclea, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific regions of Australia. Renowned for its unique morphology, this species is valued in traditional ethnobotany. Research continues into its bioactive compounds, exploring potential therapeutic applications for various health conditions and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Neonauclea sessilifolia
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Neonauclea.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found primarily throughout the South-Central territories.
