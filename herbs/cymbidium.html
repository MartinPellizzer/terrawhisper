Cymbidium

Cymbidium, commonly known as boat orchids, is a diverse genus celebrated for its elegant, long-lasting blooms. While primarily prized as ornamental houseplants, various species hold significant roles in traditional medicine. They are often utilized in herbal practices to support respiratory health and balance vital energies within the body.

  • Scientific name: Cymbidium
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Cymbidium.

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across various regions of China. It is widely distributed throughout the South-Central and North-Central parts of the country. Finally, its geographical reach extends into the high-altitude areas of Tibet.
