Mundulea chapelieri

Mundulea chapelieri ex

Mundulea chapelieri, commonly known as the Mundulea tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to African savannas. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammation. Its seeds and bark offer significant pharmacological potential in natural medicine.
