Tephrosia luzoniensis

Tephrosia luzoniensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in tropical regions, belongs to the Fabaceae family. This resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. Often utilized to treat various ailments, it holds great potential for pharmacological research into its bioactive chemical constituents.

  • Scientific name: Tephrosia luzoniensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, specifically being a member of the Fabaceae family. Its taxonomic identification is completed by its placement within the genus Tephrosia.

Common Names

This plant, Tephrosia luzoniensis, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Luzon tephrosia, while in Tagalog, it is commonly called mambutong. Furthermore, in Hiligaynon, the plant is known as bultong.

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several key regions within the Malesia botanical zone. Its presence is notably recorded on the island of Jawa. Additionally, it can be found throughout the Lesser Sunda Islands. The geographical range further extends to the Philippines.
