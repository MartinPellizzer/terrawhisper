Rheum moorcroftianum

What's the taxonomical classification of Rheum moorcroftianum?

Rheum moorcroftianum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Caryophyllales. It is further categorized under the family Polygonaceae, which places it within the genus Rheum, ultimately identifying it by its specific species name, moorcroftianum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rheum moorcroftianum has a perennial growth habit characterized by a robust rhizome and large, broadly ovate to cordate leaves that feature wavy margins and prominent venation. The foliage is typically densely clustered in a basal rosette, providing a thick layer of green surface area near the ground. Long, sturdy peduncles emerge from the center of the rosette to support terminal inflorescences composed of numerous small flowers. These flowering structures are organized into dense panicles that can become quite prominent during the blooming season.
