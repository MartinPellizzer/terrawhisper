This plant is primarily found within the diverse landscapes of the Indo-China region. Its natural habitat extends across several distinct countries, including Cambodia and Laos. Plant Parts

This plant, known as Dalbergia cochinchiensis, derives its primary medicinal and commercial value from its specialized anatomical structures. While the stems provide the necessary structural framework and transport systems for the tree's growth, the most significant part is the dense, reddish heartwood. This heartwood is highly prized not only for its durability and color but also for its bioactive properties, which have been traditionally utilized in various medicinal applications.
