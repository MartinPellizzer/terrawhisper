Mexican bedstraw, scientifically known as Galium mexicanum, is a resilient herbaceous plant native to various regions of Mexico. Characterized by its delicate, whorled leaves and small white flowers, this species belongs to the Rubiaceae family. It holds significant interest in ethnobotany for its traditional medicinal applications and ecological importance.
