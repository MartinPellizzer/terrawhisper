Pinus caribaea

Pinus cariabaea, commonly known as the Caribbean pine, is a resilient coniferous species native to the coastal regions of the Caribbean. Renowned for its diverse medicinal properties, it offers therapeutic benefits through its resin and bark, playing a vital role in traditional healing practices and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Pinus caribaea
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. It is further classified under the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pinus, specifically as the species Pinus caribaea.
