Agastache

Agastache, commonly known as anise hyssop, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic foliage and striking wildflower spikes. Prized by pollinators and herbalists alike, it offers unique minty, licorice-like flavors. This resilient plant is frequently utilized in traditional wellness practices and as a fragrant garden centerpiece.

  • Scientific name: Agastache
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Agastache.
