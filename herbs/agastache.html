Giant-hyssop, scientifically known as Agastache, is a versatile perennial cherished in both ornamental gardens and traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and vibrant flower spikes, this resilient plant attracts pollinators while offering therapeutic benefits, including soothing digestive issues and reducing inflammation through its potent, essential oil-rich leaves.
