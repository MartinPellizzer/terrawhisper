Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of Central Asia. Specifically, it can be found growing in the territories of Kazakhstan, Kirgizstan, and Tadzhikistan. Its range also extends significantly into the country of China. Within China, the species is located in both the South-Central and North-Central regions. Consequently, its habitat spans a diverse geographical area throughout these mountainous areas.

Plant Parts

This plant, Rhodiola kirilowii, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for the bioactive compounds found within its underground structures. The medicinal potency is concentrated in its thick, fleshy roots and specialized rhizomes, which serve as the main storage organs for the plant. These parts are harvested and processed to extract the essential adaptogenic properties that characterize the species.
