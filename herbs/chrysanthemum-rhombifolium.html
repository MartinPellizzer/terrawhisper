Chrysanthemum rhombifolium

Chrysanthemum rhombifoliuom, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, belongs to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its unique rhombic leaves, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering therapeutic potential in treating various ailments and promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Chrysanthemum rhombifolium
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Chrysanthemum rhombifolium, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Chrysanthemum.
