Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. The species is widely distributed throughout China, spanning from the northern territory of Inner Mongolia to the tropical island of Hainan. It also thrives in the Manchuria region of China. Furthermore, the plant can be found throughout the South-Central parts of China.

Plant Parts

This plant, Gentiana manshurica, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties, which are concentrated in its root. The underground portion of the plant is harvested and processed to extract bitter compounds that are widely used in traditional medicine to aid digestion and reduce inflammation. While the leaves and stems are part of the plant's biological structure, it is the specialized root system that serves as the most significant therapeutic component.
