Sideritis pauli · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (209 studies) · ★★★★★

Scientifically known as Sideritis paulii, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Often referred to as ironwort, this plant is celebrated in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Its aromatic leaves are frequently brewed into soothing teas to support digestive and respiratory health.
