Berberis soulieana

Berberis soulieana, commonly known as the Soulieana Barberry, is a resilient shrub valued in traditional herbal medicine. Native to specific mountainous regions, its roots and stems contain bioactive alkaloids like berberine. These compounds offer potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, making it a significant subject for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Berberis soulieana
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Berberis soulieana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Berberidaceae and the genus Berberis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Berberis soulieana, is identified by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is referred to as 假豪猪刺, a name used consistently across different writing systems including zh (Traditional/Simplified), zh-cn (Simplified Chinese), zh-tw (Traditional Chinese), and zh-hans (Simplified Chinese).
