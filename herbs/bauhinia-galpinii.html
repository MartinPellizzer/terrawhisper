Pride of the Cape

Pride of the Cape, scientifically known as Bauhinia galpinii, is a striking ornamental shrub native to Southern Africa. Beyond its beautiful, butterfly-shaped blooms, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of the plant have been utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin conditions to inflammation.
