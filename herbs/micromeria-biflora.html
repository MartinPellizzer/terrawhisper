Micromeria biflora

Micromeria biflora, commonly known as the twin-flower plant, is a versatile medicinal herb found across tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This plant offers significant pharmacological potential for developing modern natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Micromeria biflora
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Microremia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several regions across Asia. In Western Asia, its presence has been documented within the borders of Afghanistan. Within China, it is specifically located in the South-Central regions of the country. The Indian Subcontinent serves as a significant habitat, with occurrences found in India and Nepal. Additionally, the species can be found growing in the East Himalaya region of the subcontinent.
