Senecio nutans

Senecio nutans, commonly known as Nodding Groundsel, is a distinctive perennial herb native to various regions. Characterized by its drooping, yellow flower heads, this plant belongs to the Asteraceae family. While visually striking in meadow ecosystems, it is primarily studied for its unique phytochemical properties and ecological significance.

  • Scientific name: Senecio nutans
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Senecio.
