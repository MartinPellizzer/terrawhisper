Muntingia calabura

Jamaican cherry

Muntigia calabura, commonly known as Jamaican cherry or Kerson fruit, is a versatile tropical shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, its leaves, bark, and fruits are traditionally used to treat inflammation, fever, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Muntingia calabura
  • Family: Muntingiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Muntingiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Muntigiia and the specific species Muntigia calabura.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the globe. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the countries of Kenya and Tanzania. Its presence extends into the Arabian Peninsula, specifically documented in Oman. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is well-established in both Bangladesh and India. This wide range demonstrates the adaptability of the species across diverse tropical environments.
