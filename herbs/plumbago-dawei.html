Plumbago dawei

Plumbago dawei, commonly known as Chinese Leadwort, is a significant medicinal herb in traditional East Asian practices. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various internal conditions. Recent studies continue to explore its potent bioactive compounds for modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Plumbago dawei
  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Plumbaginaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Plumbago.
