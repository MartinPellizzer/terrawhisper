Twin-flower daphne, scientifically known as Daphne pontica, is a charming, slow-growing evergreen shrub native to Eastern Europe and Asia. Renowned for its delicate, bell-shaped pink flowers and glossy foliage, it adds elegance to gardens. However, caution is essential, as all parts of this plant are highly toxic.

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions stretching from Europe to Western Asia. Moving eastward, the species is found within the Caucasus region, specifically in the North Caucasus and the Transcaucasus.
