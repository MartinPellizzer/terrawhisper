Polyscias guilfoylei

Polyscias guilfoylei, commonly known as the Aralia tree, is a versatile tropical plant valued for its ornamental beauty and therapeutic potential. Beyond its lush foliage, various traditional medicine systems utilize its leaves to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, highlighting its significant role in ethnobotany and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Polyscias guilfoylei
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, placing it in the family Araliaceae. Finally, it is identified under the specific genus Polyscias.
