Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution that spans across both Eastern Asia and North America. In its native Eastern Asian range, it can be found growing in Japan, Korea, and the Ogasawara-shotto islands. Its presence extends across the ocean to the North-Central United States, specifically within the state of Illinois. Additionally, the species has established itself in the Southeastern United States, notably in Alabama. This broad dispersal demonstrates the plant's ability to adapt to diverse environments across different continents.
