Gentiana scabrida subsp. scabrida

Gentiana scabrida subsp. scabrida, commonly known as a species of bitter gentian, is a resilient perennial herb native to high-altitude alpine regions. Renowned for its intense bitterness, it contains bioactive gentianin, making it a valuable botanical ingredient used traditionally in digestive tonics to stimulate appetite and support gastrointestinal health.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana scabrida subsp. scabrida
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Gentianaceae and the genus Gentiana. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies Gentiana scabrida subsp. scabrida.

Distribution

This plant, specifically the subspecies Gentiana scabrida scabrida, possesses a very restricted and localized geographical range. Its natural distribution is confined entirely to the region of Eastern Asia. Within this vast continent, the species is found specifically on the island of Taiwan. Because it is endemic to this single location, its survival depends heavily on the specific mountain ecosystems found there. Consequently, the plant's global presence is limited to this one specific island territory.
