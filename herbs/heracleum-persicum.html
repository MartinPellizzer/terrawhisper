Heracleum persicum

Persian hogweed

Heracleum persicum, commonly known as Persian Hogweed, is a potent medicinal herb native to the Caucasus region. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive issues. This aromatic plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its diverse phytochemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Heracleum persicum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. It is further identified by the genus Heracleum, which includes the specific species Heracleum persicum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Heracleum persicum, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In Persian, it is referred to as گلپر (Golpar), while in German, it is also commonly called Golpar. Its regional names in Europe include the Czech bolševník perský, the Dutch Perzische berenklauw, and the Finnish persianjättiputki.
