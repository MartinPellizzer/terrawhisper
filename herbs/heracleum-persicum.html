Persian hogweed

Heracleum persicum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (54 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Persian hogweed, scientifically known as Heracleum persicum, is a robust perennial herb native to the Middle East. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots and seeds are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties. However, caution is essential, as its sap can cause severe skin irritation upon contact with sunlight.
