Brucea mollis, scientifically known as Brucea mollis, is a medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark contain potent bioactive compounds, including bruceine alkaloids. These properties offer significant therapeutic potential, particularly for treating malaria, skin ailments, and various inflammatory conditions.
