Brucea mollis, scientifically known as Brucea mollis, is a medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark contain potent bioactive compounds, including bruceine alkaloids. These properties offer significant therapeutic potential, particularly for treating malaria, skin ailments, and various inflammatory conditions.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it is specifically found within the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it inhabits the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species can be located throughout the country of Bangladesh. Finally, its range encompasses the diverse landscapes of the East Himalaya.
