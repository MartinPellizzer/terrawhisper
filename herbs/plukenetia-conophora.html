This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Benin, Nigeria, and Sierra Leone. Moving toward the West-Central Tropical Africa zone, the species can also be found in the Central African Republic. Additionally, it is known to grow within the borders of Cameroon. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the African peach, offers various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaf is frequently used in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as inflammation, fever, and digestive issues due to its rich bioactive compounds. Furthermore, the seeds are highly valued for their nutritional and medicinal potential, often being processed to extract seed oil. This oil is prized for its high concentration of essential fatty acids and antioxidant properties, making it useful in both topical applications for skin health and as a dietary supplement to support overall wellness.
