Betula alnoides

Betula alnoides, commonly known as the alder birch, is a resilient deciduous tree valued for its unique medicinal properties. Traditionally used in herbalism, its bark and twigs offer anti-inflammatory and diuretic benefits. This hardy species serves as a vital natural resource for supporting holistic wellness and skin health.

  • Scientific name: Betula alnoides
  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Betulaceae and the genus Betula.
