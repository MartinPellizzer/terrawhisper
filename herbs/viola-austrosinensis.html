Viola austrosinensis

Viola austrosinensis, commonly known as the Austrian Violet, is a resilient perennial cherished for its delicate blooms and therapeutic potential. Often found in alpine meadows, this botanical treasure offers traditional medicinal benefits, including anti-inflammatory and soothing properties, making it a fascinating subject for both herbalists and nature enthusiasts alike.

  • Scientific name: Viola austrosinensis
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Malpighiales, it is further categorized into the family Violaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Viola.

Distribution

This plant, known as Viola austrosinensis, exhibits a specific and somewhat limited geographical distribution within Asia. It can be found growing in the southeastern regions of China, where its natural habitat is established. Furthermore, its presence extends to the tropical island province of Hainan. These two primary areas represent the known locations where this medicinal species thrives. Consequently, the plant is characteristic of the unique flora found in the Southeast Chinese landscape.
