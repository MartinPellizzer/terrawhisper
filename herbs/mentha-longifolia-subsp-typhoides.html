Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further organized into the family Lamiaceae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. Within Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Greece and specifically on the island of Kriti. Its presence also extends to the European portion of Türkiye. Moving toward North Africa, the species is documented to inhabit Egypt. Finally, its range reaches into Western Asia, where it is located on the island of Cyprus.
