Mentha longifolia subsp. typhoides

Mentha longifolia typhoides

Mentha longifolia subsp. typhoides, commonly known as Wild Mint, is a versatile aromatic herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its pungent scent and cooling properties, this subspecies is widely used to soothe digestive issues, alleviate respiratory discomfort, and reduce inflammation, making it a staple in herbal wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Mentha longifolia subsp. typhoides
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further organized into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Mentha as the specific subspecies Mentha longifolia subsp. typhoides.
