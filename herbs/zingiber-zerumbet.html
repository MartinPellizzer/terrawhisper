Zingiber zerumbet

Zingiber zerumbet, commonly known as pine ginger or shampoo ginger, is a perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it contains bioactive compounds with potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. This versatile plant is widely utilized in herbal remedies to support various healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Zingiber zerumbet
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is a member of the Zingiberaceae family. Its scientific classification concludes within the genus Zingiber.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of Asia and the Indian Ocean. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found inhabiting the islands of Comoros and Madagascar. Moving toward the mainland, its presence is noted throughout various parts of China, specifically in the South-Central and Southeast regions.
