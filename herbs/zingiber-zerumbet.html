Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as shuttlecock ginger, utilizes its underground structures primarily for storage and propagation. The most significant medicinal component is the rhizomes, which are thick, fleshy, and aromatic stems that grow horizontally beneath the soil surface. These rhizomes contain a concentrated array of essential oils and bioactive compounds, making them the most valued part of the plant for therapeutic applications. Whether harvested in their fresh or dried state, the rhizome serves as the primary source for extracting the plant's healing properties.
