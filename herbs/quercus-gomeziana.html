Quercus gomeziana

Quercus gomeziaana, commonly known as the Gomezia oak, is a distinctive medicinal plant prized for its unique bioactive properties. Native to specific tropical regions, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Research continues to explore its potential pharmacological benefits and its significant role in natural therapeutics.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Fagaceae and is identified by the genus Quercus.
