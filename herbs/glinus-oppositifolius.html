Gima, scientifically known as Glinus oppositifolius, is a versatile medicinal herb widely recognized in traditional healing practices. Often found in tropical regions, this creeping plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, serving as a vital natural remedy in ethnobotany.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the Baleares islands. Moving toward the African continent, it is widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found in the nations of Benin, Burkina, and Ghana.
