Glinus oppositifolius

Glinus oppositifolius, commonly known as the spreading g linen, is a resilient, low-growing medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Glinus oppositifolius
  • Family: Molluginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Caryophyllales and the family Molluginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Glinus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the Baleares islands. Moving toward the African continent, it is widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found in the nations of Benin, Burkina, and Ghana. Additionally, its habitat extends to the coastal regions of Guinea-Bissau.
