What's the taxonomical classification of Abroma augustum?

Abroma augustum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression through the order Malvales, it is placed within the family Malvaceae. Ultimately, the plant is defined by its specific genus, Abroma, and its unique species designation, augustum.

What parts of this plant are used medicinally?

This plant Abroma augustum provides medicinal value through its leaves, stem bark, and trichomes. The leaves are frequently prepared to treat various ailments and serve as a primary component in traditional remedies. Extracts from the stem bark are also utilized for their specific therapeutic properties. Additionally, the fine trichomes found on the plant surface contribute to its medicinal application. These different components work together to provide the chemical compounds necessary for healing.

According to a study published by "Antioxidants (Basel, Switzerland)", the stem bark powder of Abroma augustum was investigated for its medicinal potential. Phytochemical screening of the plant identified 50 distinct phytochemicals, including alkaloids, flavonoids, terpenes, phenolics, and coumarins. The extract contained a total phenolic content of 12.32 ± 0.01 mg/g and a total flavonoid content of 42.14 ± 3.5 mg/g. Antioxidant activity was demonstrated through IC50 values of 214.007 µg/mL for DPPH, 132.307 µg/mL for FRAP, and 45.455 µg/mL for ABTS. The methanolic extract also exhibited significant hepatoprotective properties by decreasing concentrations of ALT, AST, and LDH biomarkers.

What are the pharmacological activities of Abroma augustum?
