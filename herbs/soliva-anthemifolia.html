Soliva anthemifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Soliva anthemifolia?

Soliva anthemifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Asterales. As a member of the family Asteraceae, it is further categorized under the genus Soliva, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species anthemifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Soliva anthemifolia has prostrate, spreading stems that produce small, oval to lanceolate leaves and tiny, white, four-petaled flowers arranged in dense terminal clusters. The plant typically forms low-growing mats with leaves measuring between 5 and 15 millimeters in length. Its flowering structures consist of compact heads that lack prominent bracts, distinguishing them from other members of the genus. The foliage is often arranged oppositely along the creeping branches, providing a dense ground cover. These morphological features allow the species to thrive in disturbed or compacted soils.
