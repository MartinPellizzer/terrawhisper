Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several diverse tropical and subtropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically within the country of Guinea. Its range extends to the Western Indian Ocean, where it grows in the Comoros. Within China, the species is widely distributed across the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in multiple distinct environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the betel nut palm, is primarily utilized for its medicinal and psychoactive properties, which are concentrated within its fruit. The fruit is a drupe that consists of a fibrous outer husk surrounding a hard, stony endocarp that contains the valuable seed. It is this internal seed, often referred to as the areca nut, that is harvested, dried, and processed for various traditional uses, ranging from stimulating the digestive system to serving as a component in various herbal preparations.
