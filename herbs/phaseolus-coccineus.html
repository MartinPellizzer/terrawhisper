Phaseolus coccineus

Snap bean

Phaseolus coccineus, commonly known as the runner bean, is a versatile climbing legume prized for its nutritional and medicinal potential. Beyond its culinary uses, this plant contains bioactive compounds studied for their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, offering promising insights into natural therapeutic applications within traditional and modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Phaseolus coccineus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Phaseolus coccineus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Phaseolus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across various regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found in both Germany and the Czechia-Slovakia region. Its range extends into Southeastern Europe, specifically encompassing Bulgaria. Finally, the distribution reaches into Eastern Europe through Belarus.
