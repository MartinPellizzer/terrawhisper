Snap bean, scientifically known as Phaseolus coccineus, is a versatile climbing legume prized for its edible pods and nutritious seeds. Beyond its culinary value, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Rich in essential proteins and antioxidants, it serves as a vital nutritional resource in various traditional medicinal practices worldwide.
