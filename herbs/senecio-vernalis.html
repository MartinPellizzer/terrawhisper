Senecio vernalis

Eastern groundsel

Seneciio vernalis, commonly known as the Spring Groundsel, is a resilient herbaceous plant belonging to the Asteraceae family. Often found in disturbed soils and meadows, it is recognized by its bright yellow, daisy-like blooms. While valued in certain traditional practices, it requires careful study due to its botanical complexity.

  • Scientific name: Senecio vernalis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further organized into the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Senecio.
