Astrodaucus persicus

Astrodaucus persicus, commonly known as Persian fennel or wild fennel, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Western Asia. Renowned for its aromatic seeds and foliage, it has been utilized in traditional medicine to aid digestion, alleviate bloating, and support respiratory health through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Astrodaucus persicus
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Astrodaucus.
