Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Persian celery or Ajwain, is valued for its medicinal properties found throughout its various structures. While the aerial parts, including the leaves and stems, can be utilized, the most potent therapeutic components are typically derived from the roots. Specifically, the root system is harvested for its concentrated bioactive compounds, though the fruits also play a significant role in traditional applications. When the plant matures, the ripe fruits are collected and dried to be used as a powerful spice and medicine to aid digestion.
