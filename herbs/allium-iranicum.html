Allium iranicum

Allium iranicum, commonly known as a wild medicinal onion, is a resilient perennial native to the rugged landscapes of Iran. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential through its diverse bioactive compounds and antioxidant properties.
