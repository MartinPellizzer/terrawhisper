Castela texana

Castela texana, commonly known as Texas broomrape, is a specialized parasitic plant native to the southern United States. Lacking chlorophyll, it relies entirely on host roots for nutrients. This unique botanical species plays a vital role in its ecosystem, demonstrating fascinating evolutionary adaptations for survival in diverse prairie environments.

  • Scientific name: Castela texana
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Simaroubaceae and falls under the genus Castela.
