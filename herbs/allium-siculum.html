Allium siculum

Allium siculum, commonly known as the Sicilian garlic, is a distinctive wild plant native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its aromatic bulbs and elegant white flowers, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically utilized in traditional remedies, this species offers unique phytochemical properties worthy of scientific study and preservation.

  • Scientific name: Allium siculum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Allium siculum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaryllidaceae and the genus Allium.
