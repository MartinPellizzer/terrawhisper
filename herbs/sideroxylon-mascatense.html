Sideroxylon mascatense

Sideroxylon mascatense, commonly known as the Mascarene Sideroxylon, is a versatile tropical tree valued in traditional medicine. Its bark, leaves, and wood possess significant bioactive properties, often utilized for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects. This resilient species serves as a vital botanical resource for treating various ailments in coastal ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Sideroxylon mascatense
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Africa
