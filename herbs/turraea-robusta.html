Turraea robusta

Turraea robusta, commonly known as the African mahogany, is a significant medicinal tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its versatile therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant, including the bark and roots, are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as inflammation, infections, and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Turraea robusta
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Turraea robusta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Turraea.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Cameroon and the DR Congo. Its range also extends significantly into East Tropical Africa. Specifically, the species is documented within the borders of Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda.
