Typhonium flagelliforme

Typhonium flagelliforme, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in various traditional practices, belongs to the Araceae family. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological potential, this plant is often utilized in folk medicine to treat specific ailments. Understanding its chemical constituents is vital for exploring its therapeutic benefits and applications.
