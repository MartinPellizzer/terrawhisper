Aristolochia brevipes

Aristolochia brevipes, commonly known as the short-petaled aristolochia, is a distinctive climbing herb native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its unique, tubular flowers, this species holds significant botanical interest. While historically explored for medicinal properties, careful study is essential due to the presence of potent bioactive compounds.
