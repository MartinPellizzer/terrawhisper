Palicourea coriacea

Palicourtea coriacea, commonly known as “erva-de-rato,” is a medicinal shrub native to Brazil. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is primarily utilized for its potent diuretic and antihypertensive properties. Rich in alkaloids, this plant serves as a vital resource in ethnobotany for managing hypertension and promoting urinary health.

  • Scientific name: Palicourea coriacea
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Gentianales, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Palicourea.
