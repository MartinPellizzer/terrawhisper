Lycopodiella cernua

Staghorn clubmoss

Lycopodiella cernua, commonly known as the clubmoss fern, is a distinctive herbaceous plant valued in traditional herbal medicine. Known for its unique spore-bearing structures, it contains bioactive compounds studied for their therapeutic potential. This resilient species serves as a vital subject in ethnobotany and natural pharmacology research.

  • Scientific name: Lycopodiella cernua
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
