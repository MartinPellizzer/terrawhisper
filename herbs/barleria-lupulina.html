Hop-headed barleria, scientifically known as Barleria lupulina, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots possess potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various infections, showcasing its significant therapeutic potential.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning both African continents. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the countries of Benin and Burkina. Its presence also extends to the Western Indian Ocean region. Specifically, it is located within the island nations of Comoros and Mauritius. Finally, the species is well-established in the large island of Madagascar.
