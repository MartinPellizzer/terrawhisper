Barleria lupulina

Barleria lupulina, commonly known as the Yellow Barleria, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and flowers possess potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, wounds, and various inflammatory conditions through diverse herbal applications.

  • Scientific name: Barleria lupulina
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Barleria, specifically as the species Barleria lupulina.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning both African continents. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the countries of Benin and Burkina. Its presence also extends to the Western Indian Ocean region. Specifically, it is located within the island nations of Comoros and Mauritius. Finally, the species is well-established in the large island of Madagascar.
