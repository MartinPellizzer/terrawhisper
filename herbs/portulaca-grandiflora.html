Reproduction

The reproductive cycle of Portulaca grandiflora is characterized by a specific seasonal flowering period that begins in July and concludes in September. During this time, the plant produces flowers that vary in length from a minimum of 2.5 cm to a maximum of 4 cm. Dispersal of these seeds is notably anthropochorous, occurring through human activity.
