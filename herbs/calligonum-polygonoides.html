Calligonum polygonoides

Calligonum polygonoides, commonly known as Phog, is a resilient desert shrub integral to arid ecosystems. Renowned in traditional medicine, it offers therapeutic benefits for treating skin ailments, digestive issues, and respiratory conditions. This hardy plant serves as a vital resource, providing nutritional value and ecological stability in sandy terrains.

  • Scientific name: Calligonum polygonoides
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Calligonum polygonoides, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Caryophyllales and further categorized into the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Calligonum.
