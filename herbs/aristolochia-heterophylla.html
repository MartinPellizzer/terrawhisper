Aristolochia heterophylla

Aristolochia heterophyllla, commonly known as Wild Aristolochia or Dutchman's Pipe, is a perennial climbing herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine for its unique heart-shaped leaves, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and respiratory issues, though its potent alkaloids require careful, expert clinical consideration.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia heterophylla
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Aristolochiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Aristolochia.
