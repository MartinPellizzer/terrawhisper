, scientifically known as Aristolochia heterophyllia, is a climbing woody vine native to Southeast Asia. Often valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds, this species belongs to the Aristolochiaceae family. While historically used to treat various ailments, caution is essential due to the presence of nephrotoxic aristolochic acids.
