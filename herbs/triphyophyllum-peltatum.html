Triphyophyllum peltatum

Triphyophyllum peltatum, commonly known as the climbing peltate plant, is a unique woody climber native to Neotropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive shield-shaped leaves, this botanical marvel holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in various medicinal practices, it offers fascinating potential for exploring new therapeutic compounds and bioactive properties.

  • Family: Dioncophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa
