Terminalia bentzoe subsp. bentzoe

Terminalia bentzoe subsp. bentzoe, commonly known as benzoin, is a significant medicinal tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its fragrant oleoresin, it provides therapeutic benefits through its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and expectorant properties. Often used in traditional remedies and perfumery, this subspecies remains vital in global botanical applications.

  • Family: Combretaceae
