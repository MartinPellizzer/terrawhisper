Dendrophthoe pentandra

Dendrophthoe pentandra, commonly known as the "Harp of the Forest," is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions, showcasing its vital ethnobotanical importance.
