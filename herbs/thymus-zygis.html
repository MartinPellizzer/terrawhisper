Thymus zygis

Thymus zygis, commonly known as Zygis thyme, is a distinctive aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its woody stems and small, fragrant leaves, this medicinal plant is valued for its potent essential oils. It is traditionally utilized for its antimicrobial, antioxidant, and soothing therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Thymus zygis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Thymus.
