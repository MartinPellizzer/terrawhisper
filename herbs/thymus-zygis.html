Spanish thyme

Thymus zygis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (261 studies) · ★★★★★

Spanish thyme, scientifically known as Thymus zygis, is a fragrant, woody perennial native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its potent essential oils, this aromatic herb offers significant medicinal properties, including antimicrobial and antioxidant effects. It is traditionally used to support digestive health and alleviate respiratory congestion through its healing essence.
