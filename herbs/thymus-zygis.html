Thymus zygis

Thymus zygis, commonly known as Zygis thyme, is a distinctive aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its woody stems and small, fragrant leaves, this medicinal plant is valued for its potent essential oils. It is traditionally utilized for its antimicrobial, antioxidant, and soothing therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Thymus zygis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Thymus. As a member of this specific lineage, Thymus zygis represents a distinct medicinal species within its taxonomic group.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Thymus zygis, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Estonia, it is called Pürenee liivatee, while in German-speaking areas, it is known as Joch-Thymian. It goes by the name tomilhinha in Portuguese and is called Farigola salsera in Catalan. Additionally, in Arabic, it is identified as زيجس الزعتر.
