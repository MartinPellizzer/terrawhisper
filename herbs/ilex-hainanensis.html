Ilex hainanensis

Ilex hainanenensis, commonly known as the Hainan holly, is a rare evergreen shrub native to the tropical forests of southern China. While less documented in modern pharmacology than other species, this botanical treasure holds significant potential for traditional medicine, offering unique phytochemical properties for future therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ilex hainanensis
  • Family: Aquifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Aquifoliales in the family Aquifoliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Ilex.
