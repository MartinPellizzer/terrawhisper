Salvia tiliifolia

Salvia tiliifolia, commonly known as Linden-leaf Sage, is a distinctive medicinal herb prized for its unique botanical characteristics. Native to specific ecological niches, this plant offers potent therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to support wellness, providing natural compounds that serve as valuable holistic remedies.

  • Scientific name: Salvia tiliifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Salvia.
