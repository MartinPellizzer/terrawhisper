Eryngium heterophyllum

Eryngium heterophyllum, commonly known as the prickly eryngo, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Characterized by its unique leaf structure and resilient nature, this plant offers therapeutic potential. Research continues to explore its bioactive compounds, highlighting its importance in herbal medicine and natural healing traditions worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Eryngium heterophyllum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it falls under the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Eryngium, which includes the species Eryngium heterophyllum.
