Mexican thistle, scientifically known as Eryngium heterophyllum, is a resilient perennial herb native to Mexico. Renowned for its unique, spiny foliage and delicate blue flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally utilized in folk medicine, it is studied for its potential therapeutic properties and diverse bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across parts of North America, ranging from the southwestern regions to central Mexico. In the Southwestern United States, its presence is notably recorded in the state of Arizona. Moving into the South-Central United States, the species can be found throughout New Mexico and Texas. Its range extends significantly southward into Mexico, where it inhabits the central regions. Additionally, the plant is documented to grow within the northeastern parts of Mexico.
