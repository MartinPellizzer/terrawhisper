Feather fingergrass, scientifically known as Chloris virgata, is a widespread, annual grass found in tropical and subtropical regions. Often categorized as a common weed, it possesses significant medicinal potential. Various cultures utilize it to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues, highlighting its valuable ethnobotanical importance globally.
