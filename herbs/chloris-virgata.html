Chloris virgata

Feather fingergrass

Chloris virgata, commonly known as creeping panicgrass, is a resilient, creeping annual grass widely distributed across tropical and subtropical regions. While often viewed as a common weed, it holds significant ecological value and has been documented in various ethnobotanical studies for its potential medicinal properties and traditional healing uses.

  • Scientific name: Chloris virgata
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Poaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Chloris.

Moving southward, the species is also well-established across various parts of Northern Africa. Specifically, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Algeria and Egypt.
