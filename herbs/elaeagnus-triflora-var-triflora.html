Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions in Eastern Asia and Malesia. In Eastern Asia, its presence is specifically noted in Taiwan. Moving into the Malesian region, it can be found on the island of Borneo as well as in Jawa. Furthermore, its range extends through the Lesser Sunda Islands. Finally, the species is also documented within the territory of Malaya.
