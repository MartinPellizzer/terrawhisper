Centaurea cyanus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (453 studies) · ★★★★★

Cornflower, scientifically known as Centaurea cyanus, is a vibrant wildflower celebrated for its striking azure petals. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this botanical gem has long been utilized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its soothing properties, it is frequently employed to support eye health and reduce various inflammatory conditions.
