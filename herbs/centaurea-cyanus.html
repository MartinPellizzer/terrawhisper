Centaurea cyanus

Cornflower

Centaurea cyanus, commonly known as cornflower, is a striking annual herb celebrated for its vibrant blue blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this botanical treasure has long been utilized in traditional medicine. It possesses potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, making it a valuable ally for supporting digestive and ocular health.

  • Scientific name: Centaurea cyanus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Centaurea cyanus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Centaurea.
