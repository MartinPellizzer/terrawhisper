Sage glorybower, scientifically known as Clerodendrum phlomidis, is a versatile medicinal shrub celebrated in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is widely used to treat skin disorders, respiratory ailments, and digestive issues. Its aromatic leaves and berries offer significant therapeutic benefits in traditional healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Shankhapushpi, utilizes its leaves as a primary component in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. The leaves are characterized by their distinct shape and aromatic quality, often being harvested and processed to harness their therapeutic properties. In various medicinal preparations, these leaves are employed for their potential calming and neuroprotective effects, serving as a vital part of the plant's overall healing profile.
