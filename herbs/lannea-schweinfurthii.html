False Marula

False Marula, scientifically known as Lannea schweinfurthii, is a versatile climbing shrub native to various African regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections. This resilient plant holds significant ethnobotanical value in supporting community health and wellness.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. Its presence extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Ethiopia and Somalia. Additionally, the species inhabits the territories of Sudan and South Sudan.
