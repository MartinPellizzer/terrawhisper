Aconitum japonicum subsp. subcuneatum

Aconitum japonicum subsp. subcuneatum, commonly known as Japanese monkshood, is a potent perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. While its alkaloids possess significant therapeutic potential for pain relief and inflammation, the plant is highly toxic. Careful processing is essential to neutralize its lethal components before any medicinal application.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum japonicum subsp. subcuneatum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Aconitum. Specifically, its taxonomic designation is Aconitum japonicum subsp. subcuneatum.
