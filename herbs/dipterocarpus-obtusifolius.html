Dipterocarpus obtusifolius

Dipterocarpus obtusifolius, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in various tropical ecosystems, holds profound ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its unique bioactive compounds, this species is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices to treat diverse ailments, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and the development of therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Dipterocarpus obtusifolius
  • Family: Dipterocarpaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
