Chloranthus oldhamii

What's the taxonomical classification of Chloranthus oldhamii?

Chloranthus oldhamii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Chloranthales. It is a primary representative of the family Chloranthaceae, falling specifically under the genus Chloranthus, with its unique identity defined by the species name oldhamii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Chloranthus oldhamii has opposite, ovate to elliptic leaves with serrated margins and a distinctively long, tapering tip. These leaves are typically dark green and possess a smooth, somewhat leathery texture. The plant produces small, inconspicuous white or cream-colored flowers that emerge from the leaf axils. Its stems are woody at the base and can grow into a substantial shrubby form. The overall structure is characterized by a dense arrangement of foliage and a spreading growth habit.
