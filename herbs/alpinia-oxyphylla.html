Sharp-leaf galangal, scientifically known as Alpinia oxyphylla, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in both traditional and modern pharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast regions. It can also be found growing on the tropical island of Hainan. Moving into Southeast Asia, the species extends its range into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, Vietnam serves as a key geographical location for this medicinal plant.
