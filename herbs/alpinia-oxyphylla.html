Alpinia oxyphylla

Alpinia oxyphylla, commonly known as a member of the ginger family, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic potential. Native to tropical regions, it contains bioactive compounds with significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, offering traditional and scientific promise in treating various ailments and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Alpinia oxyphylla
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is classified under the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Alpinia, specifically as the species Alpinia oxyphylla.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast regions. It can also be found growing on the tropical island of Hainan. Moving into Southeast Asia, the species extends its range into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, Vietnam serves as a key geographical location for this medicinal plant.
