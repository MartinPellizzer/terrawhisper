Sharp-leaf galangal, scientifically known as Alpinia oxyphylla, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in both traditional and modern pharmacology.

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast regions.
