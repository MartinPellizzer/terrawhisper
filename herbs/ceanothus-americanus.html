New Jersey tea, scientifically known as Ceanothus americanus, is a versatile North American shrub cherished in traditional herbalism. Known for its delicate white flowers and striking red berries, this medicinal plant has historically been used to support reproductive health, alleviate inflammation, and promote overall vitality through its unique botanical properties.
