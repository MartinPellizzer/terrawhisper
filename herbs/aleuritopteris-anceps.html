What's the taxonomical classification of Aleuritopteris anceps?

Aleuritopteris anceps belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is organized into the subclass Polypodiinae and falls under the order Polypodiales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Pteridaceae and the genus Aleuritopteris, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species anceps.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aleuritopteris anceps has pinnate fronds characterized by linear-lanceolate pinnae that are densely arranged along a dark, slender rachis. The leaflets are typically thin and entire, often displaying a slightly revolute margin that curves inward toward the underside. On the abaxial surface, the sori are organized in distinct rows following the veins, protected by small, kidney-shaped indusia. The stipe is often dark or brownish, providing a sharp color contrast to the green foliage of the plant. These morphological features collectively define its identity within the Pteridaceae family.
