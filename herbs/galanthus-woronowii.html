Galanthus woronowii

Snowdrop

Galanthus woronowii, commonly known as Woronow's snowdrop, is a delicate perennial bulb celebrated for its early spring blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant botanical interest. Historically valued in traditional herbalism, its bioactive compounds offer intriguing potential for pharmacological research and the development of modern medicinal treatments.

  • Scientific name: Galanthus woronowii
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Galanthus, specifically as the species Galanthus woronowii.
