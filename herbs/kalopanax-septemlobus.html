Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several key regions across East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its range extends from the Kuril Islands to the Sakhalin region. It is also commonly found within the territory of Primorye in the same Russian district. Moving into China, the species is documented throughout the Manchuria region. Additionally, its presence reaches into the South-Central parts of China.
