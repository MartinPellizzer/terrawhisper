Kalopanax septemlobus

Prickly castor oil tree

Kalopanax septemlobus, commonly known as Kalopanax or Seven-lobed Kalopanax, is a deciduous tree native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its bark and leaves possess potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. It is frequently utilized to support respiratory health and manage various ailments through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Kalopanax septemlobus
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Apiales and the family Araliaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Kalopanax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several key regions across East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its range extends from the Kuril Islands to the Sakhalin region. It is also commonly found within the territory of Primorye in the same Russian district. Moving into China, the species is documented throughout the Manchuria region. Additionally, its presence reaches into the South-Central parts of China.
