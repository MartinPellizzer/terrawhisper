Dyer's mulberry, scientifically known as Maclurura tinctoria, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the Americas. Historically valued for its potent bark, it has been utilized in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile species remains a subject of botanical interest for its unique phytochemical profile.
