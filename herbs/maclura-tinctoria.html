Maclura tinctoria

Dyer's mulberry

Maclurura tinctoria, commonly known as the tincture tree, is a fascinating medicinal plant prized for its potent bioactive compounds. Traditionally used in herbal remedies, it offers diverse therapeutic potential, particularly in addressing inflammatory conditions. Understanding its unique chemical profile is essential for exploring its role in modern natural medicine.
