Cape chestnut, scientifically known as Calodendrum capense, is a striking evergreen tree native to South Africa. Renowned for its spectacular displays of bright orange flowers, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the plant are traditionally utilized in folk medicine for their potent antimicrobial and therapeutic properties.
