Cape chestnut, scientifically known as Calodendrum capense, is a striking evergreen tree native to South Africa. Renowned for its spectacular displays of bright orange flowers, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the plant are traditionally utilized in folk medicine for their potent antimicrobial and therapeutic properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. Its range extends further south into the region of South Tropical Africa. Specifically, the species is documented to inhabit Malawi and Zimbabwe. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive within various East and South Tropical African ecosystems.
