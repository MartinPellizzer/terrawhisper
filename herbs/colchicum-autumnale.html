Colchicum autumnale

Autumn crocus

Colchicum autumnale, commonly known as autumn crocus, is a perennial herb prized for its striking, bell-shaped flowers. While visually stunning, it contains colchicine, a potent alkaloid. This compound serves as a powerful medicinal agent for treating gout and certain cancers, though its extreme toxicity requires careful, professional administration.

  • Scientific name: Colchicum autumnale
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Liliales, falling into the family Colchicaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Colchicum.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as autumn crocus, possesses specialized structures that are central to its medicinal and botanical profile.
