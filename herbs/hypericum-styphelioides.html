Hypericum styphelioides

Hypericum styphelioides, commonly known as the stiff St. John's wort, is a resilient perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Distinguished by its upright, woody stems and bright yellow flowers, this plant belongs to the Hypericaceae family, offering unique botanical characteristics and potential medicinal properties within its specialized habitat.

This plant, Hypericum styphelioides, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Hypericaceae and falls under the genus Hypericum.
