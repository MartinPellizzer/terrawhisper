Agrimonia pilosa var. pilosa

Agriemonia pilosa var. pilosa, commonly known as hairy agriemonia, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique botanical characteristics. Renowned for its specialized healing properties, this variety is utilized in traditional herbal medicine to address various ailments, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Agrimonia pilosa var. pilosa
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Agrimonia. Specifically, the variety Agrimonia pilosa var. pilosa represents a distinct taxonomic designation within this group.
