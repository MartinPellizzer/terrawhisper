Corbichonia decumbens

Corbichonia decumbens, commonly known as a vital medicinal herb, is a plant celebrated for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional healing practices, it offers significant bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and medicinal applications is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacology.

This plant, Corbichonia decumbens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is classified under the family Lophiocarpaceae within the genus Corbichonia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence can be noted in both Mauritania and Niger.
