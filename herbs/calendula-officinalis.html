Calendula officinalis

Calendula officinalis, commonly known as pot marigold, is a versatile medicinal herb cherished for its healing properties. Renowned for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, it is widely used in topical salves to soothe skin irritations, promote wound healing, and calm inflammation, making it a staple in natural skincare.

  • Scientific name: Calendula officinalis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Calendula officinalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with the genus Calendula.
