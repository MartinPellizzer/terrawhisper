Plant Parts

This plant, Calendula officinalis, is highly valued in herbal medicine for the various therapeutic properties found within its different components. The bright, vibrant flowers are the most commonly utilized part, often processed into concentrated plant extracts that are used to soothe skin irritations and promote healing. These blossoms can also be pressed to produce a nutrient-rich seed oil, which serves as a nourishing base for many topical salves and cosmetic formulations. While the botanical profile of the plant is centered on its aerial parts, some traditional preparations may investigate the deeper structures, though the medicinal focus remains primarily on the petals rather than the roots or rhizomes.
