Chiococca alba

Chiococca alba, commonly known as white berry or white chiococca, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Chiococca alba
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Chiococca.
