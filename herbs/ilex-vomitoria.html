Cassina, scientifically known as Ilex vomitoria, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Americas. Renowned for its potent emetic properties, it has historically been used to induce vomiting in traditional medicine. Beyond its purgative effects, the plant offers unique phytochemical benefits, making it a significant botanical subject.
