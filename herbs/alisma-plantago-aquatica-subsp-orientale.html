Alisma plantago-aquatica orientale, scientifically known as Alisma plantago-aquatica subsp. orientale, is a versatile aquatic perennial valued in traditional medicine. Native to various wetland habitats, this subspecies is widely recognized for its diuretic, anti-inflammatory, and detoxifying properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in treating kidney and bladder-related ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions of Northern Asia. Within the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found inhabiting the areas of Buryatiya and Chita. Moving eastward, the species is also well-established throughout the Russian Far East. Specifically, its presence is documented in the Amur and Khabarovsk regions. Finally, the plant's range extends into the coastal territory of Primorye.
