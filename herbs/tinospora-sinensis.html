Chinese tinospora, scientifically known as Tinospora sinensis, is a versatile medicinal climber widely used in traditional Ayurvedic and Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antidiabetic properties, making it a vital herb for managing metabolic disorders and promoting overall immune health.

Plant Parts

This plant utilizes its stems as a primary component in traditional medicinal practices. The stems of Tinospora sinensis are characterized by a woody texture and a unique, somewhat rough surface, often exhibiting a characteristic grayish or brownish color. In many therapeutic applications, these stems are harvested, dried, and processed to extract bioactive compounds that are believed to possess various health-promoting properties.
