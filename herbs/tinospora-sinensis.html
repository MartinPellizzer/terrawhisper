Tinospora sinensis

Tinospora sinensis, commonly known as Heart-leaved Giloy, is a highly revered medicinal climber in Ayurvedic tradition. Renowned for its potent immunomodulatory and antipyretic properties, this versatile plant is used to manage fever, diabetes, and inflammation. Its nutrient-rich stems serve as a vital natural remedy for enhancing overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Tinospora sinensis
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Menispermaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tinospora, specifically as the species Tinospora sinensis.
