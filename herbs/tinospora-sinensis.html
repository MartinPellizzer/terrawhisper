Chinese tinospora, scientifically known as Tinospora sinensis, is a versatile medicinal climber widely used in traditional Ayurvedic and Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antidiabetic properties, making it a vital herb for managing metabolic disorders and promoting overall immune health.
