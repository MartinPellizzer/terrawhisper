European reed

Phragmites australis subsp. australis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (185 studies) · ★★★★☆

European reed, scientifically known as Phragmites australis subsp. australis, is a resilient perennial grass found in wetlands worldwide. Beyond its ecological role in water purification, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various parts have been utilized in traditional medicine for their potential anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties.
