What's the taxonomical classification of Dischidia nummularia?

Dischidia nummularia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Gentianales and is a member of the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dischidia and is specifically distinguished as the species nummularia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dischidia nummularia has small, succulent, orbicular leaves that are densely overlapping to form a compact, rosette-like cluster. The plant grows in a pendulous or creeping habit, often found clinging to tree trunks in its natural habitat. Its miniature size and tight structure allow it to function effectively as an epiphytic organism. This specific morphology helps the plant thrive in humid, shaded tropical environments.
