Amelanchier ovalis

Amelanchier ovalis, commonly known as the velvet leaf shrub, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to treat skin inflammations and digestive ailments. Its unique biochemical profile offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and therapeutic applications.
