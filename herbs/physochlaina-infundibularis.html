What's the taxonomical classification of Physochlaina infundibularis?

Physochlaina infundibularis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further categorized under the order Solanales. It falls within the family Solanaceae, specifically belonging to the genus Physochlaina, with its unique identity defined by its species name, infundibularis.

What are the pharmacological activities of Physochlaina infundibularis?

This plant has antispasmodic, antimicrobial, and antioxidant pharmacological activities. These therapeutic properties are primarily attributed to the diverse secondary metabolites found within its chemical profile. Research indicates that its ability to relax smooth muscle makes it effective for treating gastrointestinal spasms. Furthermore, the presence of specific bioactive compounds allows the plant to combat various bacterial strains and neutralize free radicals. These combined effects highlight the significant medicinal potential of Physochlaina infundibularis in herbal pharmacology.

According to a study published by "Analytical and bioanalytical chemistry", the plant Physochlaina infundibularis Kuang, known as Huashanshen (HSH) in China, is rich in tropane alkaloids (TAs). These TAs showed good effects on types of spasms. Through the use of UPLC-Q/TOF-MS, 29 TAs were efficiently profiled from the plant. Additionally, via virtual validation and molecular dynamic simulation, approximately a dozen alkaloids were found to have high selectivity for muscarinic receptors. One specific alkaloid, N-acetyl convolicine, was evaluated for its selectivity for M2 or M3 receptors.
