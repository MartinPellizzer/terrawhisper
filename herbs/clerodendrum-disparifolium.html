Clerodendrum disparifolium

Clerodendrum disparifolium, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, belongs to the Verbenaceae family. Native to various tropical regions, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Clerodendrum disparifolium
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Clerodendrum.
