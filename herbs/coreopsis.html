Coreopsis

Coreopsis, commonly known as tickseed, is a versatile flowering plant celebrated for its vibrant, sun-loving blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal in gardens, various species possess traditional medicinal properties. Used historically to support skin health and digestive wellness, this resilient perennial offers a beautiful blend of aesthetics and herbal utility.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
