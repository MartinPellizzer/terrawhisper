What's the taxonomical classification of Edgeworthia tomentosa?

Edgeworthia tomentosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. At the class level, it is categorized under Equisetopsida, which falls into the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the order Malvales and the family Thymelaeaceae. Finally, the plant is situated within the genus Edgeworthia, specifically identified by its species name, tomentosa.
