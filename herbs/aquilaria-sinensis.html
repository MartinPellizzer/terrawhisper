Incense tree, scientifically known as Aquilaria sinensis, is a prized medicinal plant celebrated for its aromatic resin. Deeply rooted in traditional Eastern medicine, its precious agarwood is utilized to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and respiratory distress, while also serving as a cornerstone in luxury perfumery and aromatherapy.
