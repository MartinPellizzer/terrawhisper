Hymenocallis

Hymenocallis, commonly known as the Paperwhite Lily, is a striking perennial celebrated for its fragrant, trumpet-shaped blooms. While primarily ornamental, various species possess traditional medicinal properties in folk healing. Often used for their soothing qualities, these elegant plants bridge the gap between botanical beauty and natural therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Hymenocallis
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Hymenocallis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence can be documented specifically within Tunisia. Moving toward the Atlantic islands, it is found within the Macaronesian region of Cape Verde. The species also extends into West Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Gambia and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, its range continues through the West Tropical African territory of Guinea.
