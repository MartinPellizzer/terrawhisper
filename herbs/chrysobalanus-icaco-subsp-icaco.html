Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the coastal or forested areas of Benin. Its range further extends through the territory of Gambia. Additionally, the species is documented to occur within the borders of Ghana. Finally, its presence is noted in both Guinea-Bissau and Guinea.
