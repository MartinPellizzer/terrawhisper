Periploca graeca

Periploca graeca, commonly known as the milky climber, is a perennial woody vine valued in traditional medicine. Native to tropical regions, it is characterized by its milky latex and small, fragrant flowers. This plant is frequently studied for its diverse pharmacological properties, including antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory potential.

  • Scientific name: Periploca graeca
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Periploca graeca, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae under the order Gentianales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Apocynaceae within the genus Periploca.
