Goniothalamus malayanus

Goniothalamus malayanus, commonly known as the Malayan Goniothalamus, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots possess potent bioactive compounds. Research highlights its remarkable antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering promising potential for developing new therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Goniothalamus malayanus
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Goniothalamus, and its species name, malayanus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is noted in Thailand and the Nicobar Islands. Moving into the Malesia region, it can be found throughout the Malay Peninsula. The species also inhabits the large islands of Borneo and Sumatera. Consequently, its range spans a diverse array of tropical environments across these distinct territories.
