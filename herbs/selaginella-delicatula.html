Selaginella delicatula

Selagineella delicatula, commonly known as a delicate spike moss, is a unique vascular plant prized in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its resilient nature and bioactive properties, this species is studied for its potential therapeutic effects, offering promising insights into natural treatments for various ailments through its phytochemical richness.

  • Scientific name: Selaginella delicatula
  • Family: Selaginellaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Lycopodiidae and the order Selaginellales, falling into the family Selaginellaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Selaginella.
