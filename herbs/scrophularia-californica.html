Scrophularia californica

Scrophularia californica, commonly known as California figwort, is a perennial herb native to western North America. Characterized by its distinctive spurred flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various cultures have utilized its roots and foliage in topical applications to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and swellings.

  • Scientific name: Scrophularia californica
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Scrophularia californica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Scrophularia.
