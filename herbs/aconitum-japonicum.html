Aconitum japonicum

Aconitum japonicum, commonly known as Japanese monkshood, is a potent medicinal plant native to East Asia. While highly toxic due to its alkaloid content, it is traditionally utilized in controlled doses within herbal medicine to treat pain and inflammation. Precise preparation is essential to harness its therapeutic potential safely.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum japonicum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Aconitum japonicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and is categorized under the genus Aconitum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the Manchuria region. The species also has a significant presence in Japan, which is part of the broader Eastern Asian territory. Furthermore, its geographical range extends to include Korea. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where this medicinal species can be found.
