Caucalis platycarpos

Caucalis platycarpos, commonly known as Broad-fruited White Carrot, is a distinctive herbaceous plant belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its unique morphological features, this species holds significant interest in ethnobotany. Research explores its potential bioactive compounds, offering promising insights into its traditional medicinal applications and pharmacological properties.

  • Scientific name: Caucalis platycarpos
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Caucalis, which includes the species Caucalis platycarpos.
