Phlomis aucheri

Phlomis aucheri, commonly known as Aucher's sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Western Asia. Distinguished by its velvety, silver-green foliage and striking upright flower spikes, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis aucheri
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Phlomis.

Common Names

This plant, Phlomis aucheri, is known by various names across different regions where it grows. In English, it is commonly referred to as Aucher's sage, while in Turkish, it is known as Aucher lavantası. In the local languages of its native habitats, it is called Aucher otu in Azerbaijani and Aucher murtu in Persian.
