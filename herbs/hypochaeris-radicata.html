Reproduction

The reproduction of Hypochaeris radicaata is primarily sexual, characterized by bisexual flowers that facilitate the plant's reproductive cycle. The flowering period typically commences in June and extends through September. These flowers are yellow in color and utilize biotic pollination syndromes, specifically attracting insects such as bees. While self-fertilization is present within the species, the reproductive process is driven by these insect interactions. Following pollination, the plant develops dry, brown, indehiscent fruits categorized as achenes. These fruits vary in size, with an average length of 1.2 cm, ranging from a minimum of 1.4 cm to a maximum of 1.9 cm. Each fruit is prolific, containing between 101 and 1000 seeds. The seeds themselves are quite small, possessing a mean mass of approximately 0.000814182 g (ranging from 0.00066 g to 0.0011 g) and a consistent volume of 1.8 mm³. Dispersal is achieved through an anemochorous syndrome, meaning the seeds are dispersed by the wind. Asexual reproduction is noted to be absent in this species.
