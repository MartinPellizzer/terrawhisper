Hypochaeris radicata

Hypochaeris radicata, commonly known as the creeping buttonweed, is a resilient perennial herb found in diverse tropical landscapes. Renowned for its small, delicate flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional medicine often utilizes its unique properties to address various ailments, highlighting its importance in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Hypochaeris radicata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hypochaeris.
