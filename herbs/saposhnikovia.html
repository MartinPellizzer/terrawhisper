Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Northern Asia. Within the vast expanse of Siberia, it can be found specifically in the areas of Buryatiya and Chita. Its distribution extends further east into the Russian Far East, covering the Amur and Primorye regions. Additionally, the species reaches into Central Asia, where it is documented in Kazakhstan. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varied continental environments.

This plant, commonly known in traditional medicine, primarily utilizes its root as the most significant medicinal component. The dried, tuberous root is harvested and processed to be used in various herbal formulations due to its specific therapeutic properties. While the aerial parts of the plant may exist, it is the underground root system that contains the concentrated active compounds required for its medicinal applications.
