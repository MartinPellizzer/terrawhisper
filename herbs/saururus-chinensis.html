Saururus chinensis

Saururus chinensis, commonly known as Asian lizard's tail, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, its aromatic leaves and rhizomes are utilized to treat inflammation, fever, and various skin ailments. This versatile plant plays a significant role in both herbal remedies and aquatic ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Saururus chinensis
  • Family: Saururaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Saururaceae, specifically categorized under the genus Saururus.
