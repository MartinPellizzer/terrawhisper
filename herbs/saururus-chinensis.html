Saururus chinensis · Accepted scientific name

Asian Lizard's Tail, scientifically known as Saururus chinensis, is a perennial aquatic herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it features distinctive whorled leaves and white, spike-like flowers. Often used to treat inflammation, fever, and skin ailments, this versatile plant holds significant value in herbal pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, Saururus chinensis, is a perennial herb characterized by its distinct morphological structure. The aerial parts of the species consist of slender stems and lanceolate leaves that rise above the soil to support its unique white flower spikes. Below the surface, the underground parts serve as the primary storage and stabilization organs. Specifically, the roots form an extensive rhizomatous system, where the thick, creeping root structures allow the plant to spread effectively through moist habitats. As a complete medicinal herbs species, all these components are often studied for their bioactive properties in traditional medicine.
