What's the taxonomical classification of Silene songarica?

Silene songarica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized into the order Caryophyllales and falls within the family Caryophyllaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Silene, with its specific designation being the species songarica.
