Cryptomeria japonica

Sugi

Cryptomeria japonica, commonly known as Japanese cedar or Sugi, is a majestic evergreen conifer native to Japan. Renowned for its towering stature and aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in East Asian practices to support respiratory health, reduce inflammation, and provide calming, therapeutic essential oils.

  • Scientific name: Cryptomeria japonica
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales, it is classified in the family Cupressaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cryptomeria.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cryptomeria japonica, is a coniferous evergreen that utilizes various structural components for its biological functions and medicinal potential. The needles are the primary photosynthetic organs, providing a distinct aromatic scent often used in traditional practices. The bark and the various layers of the bark serve as protective outer coverings for the trunk and branches, shielding the internal systems from environmental stress. Deep within the structure, the wood provides the essential rigid support required for the tree to reach impressive heights, and both the bark and wood are often studied for their unique chemical compounds.
