Strophanthus sarmentosus

Strophantus sarmentosus, commonly known as the climbing strophant, is a medicinal climbing shrub valued in traditional herbal medicine. Native to tropical regions, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Often utilized for its anti-inflammatory and purgative effects, this plant serves as a vital resource in treating various dermatological and gastrointestinal ailments.

  • Scientific name: Strophanthus sarmentosus
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa
