Arnica

Mountain tobacco

Arnica, commonly known as mountain arnica, is a perennial herb celebrated for its potent anti-inflammatory properties. Frequently used in topical salves and ointments, it excels at soothing muscle aches, reducing bruising, and easing swelling. This resilient wildflower remains a staple in natural remedies for supporting physical recovery and healing.

  • Scientific name: Arnica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, the medicinal specimen is categorized under the genus Arnica.
