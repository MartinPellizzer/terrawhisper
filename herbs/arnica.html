Mountain tobacco, scientifically known as Arnica, is a resilient perennial herb renowned for its vibrant, daisy-like golden blooms. Primarily used in topical remedies, it is celebrated for its potent anti-inflammatory properties, making it a natural staple for soothing muscle aches, reducing swelling, and accelerating the healing of bruises.
