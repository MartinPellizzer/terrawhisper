Ilex yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Ilex yunnanensis?

Ilex yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic descent through the order Aquifoliales, it is placed within the family Aquifoliaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Ilex, and its specific species name, yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ilex yunnanensis has evergreen, sclerophyllous leaves that are typically ovate to lanceolate in shape, measuring between 3 and 8 centimeters in length with serrated margins and a coriaceous texture. The leaf surfaces are dark green and lustrous on the upper side, while the undersides often exhibit a paler, more matte appearance. Its branches are woody and can develop a somewhat rugged bark as the shrub or small tree matures. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge in clusters, eventually giving way to small, globose drupes that change color during ripening. The overall growth habit is compact, forming a dense canopy of stiff, leathery foliage.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is native to the subtropical and temperate regions of southwestern China, primarily found within the provinces of Yunnan and Sichuan. It typically inhabits montane environments at elevations ranging from several hundred to over two thousand meters above sea level. These populations often thrive in moist, shaded areas such as forest margins, valleys, and thickets. The species is specifically adapted to the unique climatic conditions provided by the mountainous terrain of the Hengduan Mountains. Consequently, its distribution remains concentrated within these specific high-altitude ecological niches.
