This versatile botanical serves as a natural remedy for restoring harmony and tranquility.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Valerian, derives its primary medicinal properties from its underground structures. The most therapeutically active components are found within the roots and the fleshy rhizomes, which are harvested and dried for use in various herbal preparations. These parts of the plant contain the essential volatile oils and valerenic acids responsible for its calming and sedative effects.
