Valeriana

Valeriana, commonly known as valerian, is a perennial flowering plant celebrated for its potent sedative properties. Widely used in herbal medicine, its roots are prized for reducing anxiety and promoting restful sleep. This ancient botanical remedy serves as a natural remedy for insomnia and stress, supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Valeriana
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, the specific taxonomic designation for this medicinal species is the genus Valeriana.
