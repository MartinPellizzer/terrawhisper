What's the taxonomical classification of Cissus aralioides?

Cissus aralioides belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. At the class level, it is categorized under Equisetopsida, which falls into the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized under the order Vitales and the family Vitaeaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is defined by its membership in the genus Cissus and its designation as the species aralioides.

What traditional systems uses this plant?

This plant, Cissus aralioides, is utilized within Cameroon Traditional Medicine to treat various ailments including skin infections, stomach pains, and inflammatory conditions. Local practitioners often prepare decoctions from the leaves or roots to serve as medicinal tonics for internal health. In many communities, the plant is applied topically as a poultice to soothe localized swelling and promote wound healing. Beyond its specific medicinal applications, the plant holds cultural significance in regional folk practices related to holistic wellness. These historical uses demonstrate a deep-seated reliance on the botanical properties of the species for community healthcare.

According to a study published by "BMC complementary and alternative medicine", Cissus aralioides is one of five medicinal plant species from Cameroon traditional medicine. The study investigated the in vitro antimicrobial activity of its methanolic, ethylacetate (EtOAc) and hexanic fractions against 10 pathogenic microorganisms of the urogenital and gastrointestinal tracts. This investigation included 8 bacterial species, which consisted of 2 Gram positive species and 6 Gram negative species, as well as 2 fungal isolates. The researchers used the agar well-diffusion and the micro dilution methods to determine the inhibition diameters (ID) and Minimum inhibitory concentrations (MIC). Chemical composition analysis was performed according to Harbone (1976) to examine the extracts.
