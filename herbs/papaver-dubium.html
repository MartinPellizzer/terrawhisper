Papaver dubium

Long-headed poppy

Papaver dubium, commonly known as the long-headed poppy, is a resilient annual herb native to Europe and Asia. Recognized by its slender stems and delicate, pale pink petals, this species belongs to the Papaveraceae family. While often viewed as a weed, it holds significant botanical and ecological interest.

  • Scientific name: Papaver dubium
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the Papaveraceae family, falling under the genus Papaver.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Papaver dubium, is recognized by various local names across different regions. Additionally, in Estonia, this medicinal plant is commonly called põldmagun.
