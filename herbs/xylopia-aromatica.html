Xylopia aromatica

Xylopia aromatica, commonly known as aromatic xylopia, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and inflammation. Its aromatic properties make it a vital resource in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Xylopia aromatica
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Xylopia aromatica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Magnoliales and the family Annonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with the genus Xylopia.
