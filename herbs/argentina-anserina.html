Silverweed, scientifically known as Argentina anserina, is a perennial herb native to temperate regions. Characterized by its creeping rhizomes and serrated leaves, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism. It possesses mild astringent and anti-inflammatory properties, often utilized in folk remedies to support digestive health and skin healing.
