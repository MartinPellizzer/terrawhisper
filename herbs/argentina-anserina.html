Argentina anserina

Argentina anserina, commonly known as a valuable medicinal herb, is celebrated for its diverse therapeutic properties. Often utilized in traditional healing practices, this plant offers significant bioactive compounds that support holistic wellness. Understanding its botanical characteristics and medicinal applications provides essential insights into its role in natural healthcare remedies.

  • Scientific name: Argentina anserina
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Rosalales, specifically within the family Rosaecae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Argentina.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Furthermore, its presence extends to the northern landscapes of Finland. The species is also documented within the Faroe Islands, known as Føroyar. Finally, its geographical range includes Great Britain and the volcanic terrains of Iceland.
