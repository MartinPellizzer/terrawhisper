Cirsium arvense

Corn thistle

Cirsium arvense, commonly known as creeping thistle, is a resilient perennial herb found across much of the Northern Hemisphere. Renowned for its prickly foliage and vibrant purple flower heads, this plant holds traditional significance in herbal medicine, where its various parts are utilized for their potential therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Cirsium arvense
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cirsium.
