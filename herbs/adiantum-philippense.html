Adiantum philippense

Adiantum philippense, commonly known as the Walking Maidenhair fern, is a graceful, moisture-loving plant prized for its delicate, fan-shaped leaflets. Beyond its ornamental beauty in lush landscapes, this fern holds traditional significance in various cultures, where its unique botanical properties are studied for potential therapeutic and medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Adiantum philippense
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Adiantum philippense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae within the order Polypodiales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Pteridaceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Adiantum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions including Macaronesia and West Tropical Africa. Within the Macaronesian islands, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. Moving to the African mainland, it can be found throughout various West Tropical African nations. Its range includes the countries of Benin, Burkina, and Ghana. Additionally, the species extends its reach into Guinea-Bissau, completing its regional footprint.
