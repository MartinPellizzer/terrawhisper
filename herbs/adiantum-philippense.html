Adiantum philippense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is organized into the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the family Pteridaceae, leading to its placement within the genus Adiantum. Finally, the specific identification of this plant is concluded with its species designation, philippense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Adiantum philippense has delicate, fan-shaped fronds composed of small, rounded, dark green pinnae attached to thin, wiry, black stalks. The pinnules exhibit a characteristic papery texture and possess entire or slightly wavy margins. These leaflets are arranged in a bipinnate or tripinnate fashion, creating a lacy and intricate appearance. The dark, slender stipes provide a striking visual contrast against the bright green foliage. Sori are located on the underside of the leaflets, often protected by a distinctive, reflexed, kidney-shaped margin.
