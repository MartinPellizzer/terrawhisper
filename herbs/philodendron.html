Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several tropical regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in the Seychelles. Its range extends to the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the borders of Bangladesh. Moving into the Pacific, it is located in the North-Central Pacific region of Hawaii. Finally, it inhabits the South-Central Pacific islands, including both the Cook Islands and the Society Islands.

This plant, while primarily known for its ornamental value, possesses specific morphological structures that facilitate its growth and survival. The root system typically consists of adventitious roots that can also function as aerial roots, allowing the plant to climb surfaces and absorb moisture from the air. Supporting the lush foliage is a sturdy, succulent stem that serves as the main axis for leaf attachment and acts as a conduit for transporting water and nutrients throughout the organism.
