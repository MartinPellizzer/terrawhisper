What's the taxonomical classification of Callicarpa kwangtungensis?

Callicarpa kwangtungensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the hierarchical structure of plant taxonomy, it falls under the order Lamiales and belongs to the family Lamiaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Callicarpa, and its unique species designation, kwangtungensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Callicarpa kwangtungensis has woody stems with opposite, simple leaves that possess ovate to elliptical shapes and serrated margins. These inflorescences eventually give rise to small, globose drupes that turn a vibrant purple color when mature.
