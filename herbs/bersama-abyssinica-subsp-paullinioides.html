Bersama abyssinica subsp. paullinioides

Bersama abyssinica subsp. paullinioides, commonly known as a rare medicinal shrub, holds significant ethnobotanical value. Endemic to specific tropical forest ecosystems, this subspecies is prized in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to unlock potential therapeutic applications for various inflammatory and healing conditions.

  • Scientific name: Bersama abyssinica subsp. paullinioides
  • Family: Francoaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales, it is classified under the family Francoaceae and the genus Bersama. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies paullinioides.

Distribution

This plant is geographically distributed across several countries within the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its presence has been documented in the nation of Ghana. It can also be found growing within the borders of Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the territory of Ivory Coast. Finally, the distribution includes the coastal landscapes of Liberia.
