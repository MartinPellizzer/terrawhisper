Lannea humilis

Lannea humilis, commonly known as the climbing lantana or creeping lannaea, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Africa. Renowned for its climbing nature, this plant is traditionally used in various herbal remedies to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Lannea humilis
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, falling into the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Lannea.

Common Names

This plant, Lannea humilis, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic landscape of its habitat. In English, it is often referred to as the creeping lannaea, while in Zulu, it is commonly known as uMvumvumvu. Within the Xhosa language, the plant is identified as uMvumvumvu, and it is also recognized by the name uMvumvumvu in isiXhosa.
