Origanum onites

Origanum onitis

Origanum oniites, commonly known as wild oregano, is a resilient medicinal herb valued for its potent therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and antioxidants, this plant has long been utilized in traditional medicine to combat inflammation, support digestive health, and bolster the immune system through its natural antimicrobial effects.

  • Scientific name: Origanum onites
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Origanum, which includes the species Origanum onites.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across several continents. In Southeastern Europe, it is found growing in Greece and on the island of Kriti. Its distribution extends further within the Mediterranean to include Sicilia. Moving toward Northern Africa, the species is also documented in Tunisia. Finally, the geographical reach of the plant encompasses Western Asia, specifically in Cyprus.
