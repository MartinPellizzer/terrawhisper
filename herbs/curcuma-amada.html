Curcuma amada

Curcumma longa, commonly known as turmeric, is a vibrant perennial plant belonging to the ginger family. Renowned for its brilliant orange rhizomes, it has been a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine for centuries. Highly valued for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, this golden spice offers remarkable therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Curcuma amada
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is a member of the Zingiberaceae family. It is specifically classified under the genus Curcuma.
