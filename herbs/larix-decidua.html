European larch, scientifically known as Larix decidua, is a resilient coniferous tree native to mountain ranges across Europe. Valued for its rapid growth and timber quality, it also holds medicinal significance. Its needles and bark are traditionally used in herbal remedies to support respiratory health and reduce inflammation.

Plant Parts

This plant, Larix decidua, commonly known as the European larch, is valued for various therapeutic substances derived from its different anatomical structures. The medicinal potential of the species is often found in its resins, which are collected from the bark and stem to harness their antiseptic and anti-inflammatory properties. During certain environmental stresses or injuries to the tree, the plant produces various exudates that serve as a natural defense mechanism and can be utilized in traditional remedies. Furthermore, certain aromatic balm-like substances found within the needles and woody tissues contribute to the plant's characteristic scent and its holistic applications in herbal medicine.
