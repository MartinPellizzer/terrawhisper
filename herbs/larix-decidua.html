Larix decidua

European larch

Larix decidua, commonly known as the European larch, is a resilient deciduous conifer valued for its medicinal potential. Historically used in traditional practices, its needles and bark contain bioactive compounds with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant offers significant promise in contemporary herbal pharmacology and natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Larix decidua
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales and family Pinaceae, it is classified under the genus Larix.

Plant Parts

This plant, Larix decidua, commonly known as the European larch, is valued for various therapeutic substances derived from its different anatomical structures.
