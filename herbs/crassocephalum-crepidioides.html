Crassocephalum crepidioides

Gynura crepidioides

Crassocephalum crepidioides, commonly known as the "shaggy herb," is a versatile medicinal plant found across tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. This botanical specimen offers significant pharmacological potential for modern integrative medicine and herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Crassocephalum crepidioides
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus name Crassocephalum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Within this geographical zone, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its presence is also well-documented across the territories of Ghana and Guinea. Furthermore, the species extends its range into Ivory Coast. Finally, it is known to inhabit parts of Liberia.
