False Amaranth, scientifically known as Digera muriata, is a versatile annual herb widely recognized for its profound medicinal properties. Often found in tropical regions, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues, skin inflammations, and kidney disorders, thanks to its rich bioactive compounds.
