What's the taxonomical classification of Viburnum corymbiflorum?

Viburnum corymbiflorum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the biological lineage of the order Dipsacales. It is further categorized under the family Viburnaceae, placing it within the genus Viburnum, and is specifically identified by its unique species designation, corymbiflorum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Viburnum corymbiflorum has oppositely arranged, ovate to elliptical leaves with serrated margins and a woody, deciduous shrub habit. Its inflorescence consists of flat-topped or slightly rounded corymbs composed of numerous small, white flowers. These floral clusters are supported by branching peduncles that emerge from the leaf axils. The plant eventually produces small, fleshy drupes that transition in color as they mature.
