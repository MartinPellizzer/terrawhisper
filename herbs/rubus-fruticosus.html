Rubus fruticosus

Rubus fruticosus, commonly known as the blackberry, is a versatile perennial shrub valued for its medicinal and nutritional properties. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and polyphenols, its fruits and leaves are traditionally used to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and boost the immune system through various holistic remedies.

  • Scientific name: Rubus fruticosus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Rubus.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within Northern Europe. In the British Isles, it can be found growing throughout both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving further north, the species is also established in the country of Denmark. Its presence extends into the Scandinavian Peninsula, where it is common in Norway. Additionally, the plant is frequently documented across the landscapes of Sweden.
