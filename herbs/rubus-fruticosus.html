Bramble, scientifically known as Rubus fruticosus, is a versatile, thorny shrub renowned for its nutrient-dense fruits. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal value. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and tannins, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote skin healing and vitality.
