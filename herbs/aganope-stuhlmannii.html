Aganope stuhlmannii

Aganopetalum stuhlmannii, commonly known as the medicinal shrub, is a significant botanical species valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Utilized extensively in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds believed to possess anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities. Understanding its phytochemical profile is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Aganope stuhlmannii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its classification under the genus Aganope.

This plant, known scientifically as Aganope stuhlmannii, is recognized by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Within this geographic zone, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its range also extends northward into the territory of Burkina. Additionally, the species is documented in the coastal nations of Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its presence is noted within the borders of Guinea-Bissau.
