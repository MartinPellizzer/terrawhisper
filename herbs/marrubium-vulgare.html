Marrubium vulgare

Horehound

Marrubium vulgare, commonly known as white horehound, is a perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it has historically been used to treat respiratory ailments, such as coughs and bronchitis. This aromatic plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its soothing expectorant qualities.

  • Scientific name: Marrubium vulgare
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Marrubium. Consequently, its specific scientific name is Marrubium vulgare.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Marrubium vulgare, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. In Poland, it is referred to as Szanta zwyczajna, while in French-speaking regions, it is called Marrube blanc. In Russia, it is known as Шандра обыкновенная, and in Serbia, it goes by the name Očajnica. Additionally, in the Quechua language, it is identified as Yuraq itapallu.
