Horehound, scientifically known as Marrubium vulgare, is a perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant has long been utilized in traditional medicine as a potent expectorant. It is primarily valued for treating respiratory ailments, soothing coughs, and supporting overall bronchial health.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as white horehound, is a perennial herb where the entire plant can be utilized for various therapeutic purposes. Most medicinal applications focus on the aerial parts, which include the stems and flowers, but the most significant component is the leaves. These leaves are rich in bioactive compounds and contain specific essential oils that contribute to the plant's characteristic aroma and pharmacological potency. While the roots may also be harvested, the concentrated therapeutic value is most frequently derived from the foliage and the aromatic essences extracted from the above-ground structures.
