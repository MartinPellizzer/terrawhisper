Maiden silvergrass, scientifically known as Miscanthus sinensis, is a versatile perennial grass valued in traditional medicine. Primarily utilized for its cooling properties, it is often employed to treat inflammation, coughs, and fever. Beyond its therapeutic applications, this resilient plant also serves significant roles in ecological restoration and landscaping.
