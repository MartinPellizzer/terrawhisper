Miscanthus sinensis

Maiden silvergrass

Miscanthus sinensis, commonly known as Chinese Silvergrass, is a versatile perennial grass valued for its ecological and medicinal potential. While primarily recognized for its structural resilience and biomass production, traditional practices explore its bioactive properties. This introduction examines its botanical characteristics and emerging roles in natural therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Miscanthus sinensis
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Poaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Miscanthus.
