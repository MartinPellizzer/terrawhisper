Citrus medica

Citrus medica, commonly known as the citron, is a primitive citrus species valued for its aromatic rind and medicinal properties. Rich in essential oils and vitamin C, it is traditionally used to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and skin conditions. This ancient fruit remains a cornerstone in traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Citrus medica
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales and the family Rutaecae, it is classified under the genus Citrus. As Citrus medica, it represents a distinct medicinal species within this botanical hierarchy.

Distribution

This plant, Citrus medica, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in the territories of Algeria, Morocco, and Tunisia. Its range also extends into the Macaronesian islands located in the Atlantic Ocean. Specifically, it is documented in the Canary Islands as part of this island group. Additionally, the species is present in the Madeiran archipelago.
