This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant, Citrus medica, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in the territories of Algeria, Morocco, and Tunisia. Its range also extends into the Macaronesian islands located in the Atlantic Ocean. Specifically, it is documented in the Canary Islands as part of this island group. Additionally, the species is present in the Madeiran archipelago.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Citrus medica, offers various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The most prominent medicinal part is the fruit, which is often harvested for its nutritional and healing benefits. Specifically, the rind of the fruit is highly valued in traditional medicine due to its concentrated essential oils and bioactive compounds. In addition to the fruits, the leaves are also frequently employed in herbal preparations. Whether using the whole Leaf or the fleshy part of the fruit, each component of this citrus species contributes uniquely to its medicinal profile.
