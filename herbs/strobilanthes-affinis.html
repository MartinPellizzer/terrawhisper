Strobilanthes affinis

Strobilanthes affinis, commonly known as the Neelakurinji, is a remarkable shrub famous for its mass flowering every twelve years. Endemic to the Western Ghats, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts are utilized in indigenous medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin diseases to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Strobilanthes affinis
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae and is identified by the genus Strobilanthes. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined as Strobilanthes affinis.
