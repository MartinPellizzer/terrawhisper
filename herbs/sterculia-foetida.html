Bastard poo tree, scientifically known as Sterculia foetida, is a majestic deciduous tree native to South Asia. Renowned for its large, leathery leaves and fragrant blossoms, it holds significant medicinal value. Various parts of the tree are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin diseases to digestive issues.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its range extends across the Western Indian Ocean to include the Comoros. Within Asia, the species is documented in the Indian Subcontinent specifically in Assam, as well as in China Southeast. Finally, its presence is also noted in the Hainan province of China.
