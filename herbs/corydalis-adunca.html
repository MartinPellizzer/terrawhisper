Corydalis adunca, scientifically known as Corydalis adunca, is a delicate perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Belonging to the Papaveraceae family, this plant features distinctive, nodding yellow flowers. Historically valued in traditional medicine, its bioactive alkaloids offer significant pharmacological potential for studying neurological and sedative properties.
