Corydalis adunca

Corydalis adunca, commonly known as the hooked corydalis, is a delicate perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Belonging to the Papaveraceae family, this plant is distinguished by its unique, curved floral spurs. It thrives in moist, shady habitats, contributing to the rich biodiversity of riparian ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis adunca
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales and is a member of the Papaveraceae family. Finally, it is identified by the genus Corydalis.
