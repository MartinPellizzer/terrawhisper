Duhaldea cappa

Duhaldea cappa, commonly known as the Golden Ember, is a rare medicinal shrub prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Found deep within tropical highland ecosystems, its vibrant leaves contain unique alkaloids used traditionally to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Duhaldea cappa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
