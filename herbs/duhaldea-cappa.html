Elecampane, scientifically known as Duhaldea cappa, is a perennial herb revered for its powerful therapeutic properties. Renowned for its potent expectorant qualities, this medicinal plant has been utilized for centuries in traditional medicine to support respiratory health, soothe coughs, and promote overall wellness through its unique bioactive compounds.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Duhaldea, where it is identified as Duhaldea cappa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several specific regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central provinces and the Southeast regions. Its presence is also noted on the tropical island of Hainan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in the state of Assam. Finally, its habitat extends into the high-altitude areas of the East Himalaya.
