Dioscorea cirrhosa

Dioscorea cirrhosa, commonly known as a wild climbing yam, is a medicinal plant native to high-altitude Himalayan regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its tubers possess significant bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects, offering promising therapeutic applications in treating various physiological ailments.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea cirrhosa
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Dioscoreales and is a member of the family Dioscoreaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Dioscorea.

Common Names

This plant, Dioscorea cirrhosa, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is referred to as 薯莨 (zh), 薯茛 (zh-hans), and 薯茛 (zh-cn).
