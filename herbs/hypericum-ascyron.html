Hypericum ascyron

Hypericum ascryon, commonly known as St. John's Wort, is a perennial herb belonging to the Hypericaceae family. Renowned for its vibrant yellow flowers, this medicinal plant has been traditionally used to support emotional well-being and treat mild depression, offering a natural approach to enhancing mental clarity and mood balance.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum ascyron
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Hypericaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Hypericum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the diverse landscapes of Siberia. It can be found growing across the mountainous terrain of the Altai region. Furthermore, its presence extends into the eastern territories of Buryatia. The species is also documented within the geographical boundaries of Chita. Finally, its range encompasses the vast areas of both the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions.
