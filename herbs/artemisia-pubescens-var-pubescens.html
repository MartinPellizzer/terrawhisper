Artemisia pubescens var. pubescens

Artemisia pubescens var. pubescens, commonly known as pubescent wormwood, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific arid and montane regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this botanical variety possesses significant ethnobotanical importance, traditionally utilized in various medicinal preparations for its unique bioactive compounds and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia pubescens var. pubescens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Artemisia. Specifically, it is identified by the variety name pubescens.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of Eastern Europe and Siberia. In the European territories, its range extends through Central European Russia, East European Russia, and South European Russia. Moving eastward into the Siberian landscape, the species is also well-established in the Altay region. Furthermore, its distribution continues into the Republic of Buryatiya within Siberia. Together, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of Artemisia pubescens var. pubescens.
