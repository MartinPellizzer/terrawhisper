Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of Eastern Europe and Siberia. In the European territories, its range extends through Central European Russia, East European Russia, and South European Russia. Moving eastward into the Siberian landscape, the species is also well-established in the Altay region. Furthermore, its distribution continues into the Republic of Buryatiya within Siberia. Plant Parts

This plant, Artemisia pubescens var. pubescens, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts, which encompass everything above the soil surface. These aerial parts include the leafy stems, branches, and flowering structures that capture sunlight and produce the essential oils and bioactive compounds responsible for its therapeutic properties. When preparing medicinal extracts or teas, it is these harvested upper structures that are typically dried and processed to harness the plant's full medicinal potential.
