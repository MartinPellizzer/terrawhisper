Plectocephalus varians

Plectocephalus variaus, commonly known as the Blue Mistflower, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Native to tropical regions, this plant features delicate, sky-blue blossoms. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its soothing properties and diverse therapeutic applications in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Plectocephalus varians
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it falls under the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Plectocephalus.
