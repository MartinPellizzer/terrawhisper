Sorbus discolor

What's the taxonomical classification of Sorbus discolor?

Sorbus discolor belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further categorized under the order Rosales. It is a member of the family Rosaceae, falling into the genus Sorbus, with its specific designation being the species discolor.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Sorbus discolor has pinnately compound leaves that typically feature seven to fifteen leaflets with distinctively different colors on their upper and lower surfaces. The upper side of the leaflets is a deep, dark green, while the underside is a much paler, almost white-green color. These leaflets are generally elliptical or lanceolate in shape and possess finely serrated margins. The plant also produces clusters of small, white flowers in late spring which eventually mature into small, reddish-orange pomes. The overall structure of the foliage provides a stark visual contrast due to this leaf coloration.
