Dicliptera verticillata

Dicliptera verticillata, commonly known as the blue dicliptera, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This plant offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Dicliptera verticillata
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is specifically positioned in the family Acanthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Dicliptera.
