Plant Parts

This plant, Syringa pinnatifolia, utilizes various components for medicinal purposes, specifically focusing on the structural elements of its growth. The therapeutic potential is often derived from the stems, which serve as the primary support for the foliage. More specifically, the stem bark contains concentrated bioactive compounds that are harvested for traditional remedies. In many preparation processes, the peeled stem is carefully removed to access the inner tissues, and these peeled stems are frequently processed into decoctions or extracts to harness their full medicinal properties.
