Plant Parts

This plant, known as Phellodendron chinense, utilizes several of its anatomical structures for medicinal purposes. The therapeutic properties are primarily derived from the bark, which is often harvested as a dried, yellowish-brown material. While the stems serve as the structural framework for the plant's growth, the medicinal potency is most concentrated within the protective layers of the bark. Additionally, the underground system includes hairy roots that assist in nutrient absorption and stability within its natural habitat.
