Phellodendron chinense

Phelloodendron chinense, commonly known as Chuanlian, is a significant medicinal plant in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potent bitter properties, it is primarily used to clear heat, detoxify the body, and expel dampness. Its bioactive alkaloids make it a vital resource for treating inflammation and various febrile conditions.

  • Scientific name: Phellodendron chinense
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
