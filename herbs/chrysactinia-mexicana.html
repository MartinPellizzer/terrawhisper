Chrysactinia mexicana

Chrysactinia mexicana, commonly known as a member of the Asteraceae family, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Found primarily in arid regions, it contains bioactive compounds utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and modern natural healthcare applications.
