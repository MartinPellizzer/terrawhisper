Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern primarily concentrated across the Mediterranean region. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is documented within the territories of France and Portugal. It is also widely found throughout the Iberian Peninsula, specifically in Spain. Furthermore, its range extends to the island of Corse in the Mediterranean Sea. Finally, the species reaches into Southeastern Europe, where it can be located in Greece.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the silk cotton vine, utilizes its fruit as a key anatomical component for reproduction and seed dispersal. The fruit of Araujiia sericifera is a distinctive, elongated, and dehiscent capsule that develops from the fertilized ovary after flowering. Once the fruit matures and dries, it splits open to release numerous small seeds, which are enveloped in a mass of fine, silky hairs that aid in wind dispersal. In the context of its medicinal study, while various parts of the plant are investigated, the structure and lifecycle of the fruit remain essential to understanding the plant's botanical profile.
