Albizia myriophylla

Albizia myriophylla, commonly known as a rare medicinal legume, holds significant potential in traditional healing practices. Native to specific tropical regions, this plant is valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Research continues to explore its bioactive compounds, highlighting its promising role in developing natural treatments for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Albizia myriophylla
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Albizia. Specifically, the medicinal species is identified as Albizia myriophyllia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of South and Southeast Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in India and throughout the state of Assam. Its range also extends into Bangladesh and across the Eastern Himalayan region. Furthermore, the species stretches into the Indo-China territory, specifically appearing in Cambodia. This diverse presence highlights the plant's ability to thrive in multiple subtropical environments.
