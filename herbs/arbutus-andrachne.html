Arbutus andrachne

Arbutus andrachne, commonly known as the Strawberry Tree, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its striking red bark and fragrant white flowers, it offers more than aesthetic beauty. Historically, its fruits and leaves have been utilized in traditional herbal remedies for their diverse medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Arbutus andrachne
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Arbutus.

This plant is primarily found within the diverse landscapes of Southeastern Europe. Its natural range includes the coastal and mountainous regions of Albania and Bulgaria. It is also widely distributed across Greece, with a specific presence noted on the island of Kriti. Furthermore, the species can be found extending into the European territories of Türkiye.
