Lycopodium thyoides

Lycopodium thyoides, commonly known as False Clubmoss, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to North American wetlands. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its rhizomes contain alkaloids used for their purported medicinal properties. This unique spore-bearing plant thrives in acidic, moist soils, playing a vital role in forest ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Lycopodium thyoides
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
