Phenax angustifolius

Phenax angustifolius, commonly known as Narrow-leaf Phenax, is a resilient medicinal herb valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Characterized by its slender, elongated foliage, this plant plays a significant role in traditional healing practices, offering therapeutic potential for treating inflammatory conditions and supporting overall systemic wellness through natural compounds.

  • Scientific name: Phenax angustifolius
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, it is further categorized into the family Urticaceae. Finally, it is identified under the specific genus Phenax.

Common Names

This plant, Phenax angustifolius, is known by several different names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as narrow-leaved phenax, while in Spanish, it is known as phenax de hojas estrechas. In French, the species is called phenax à feuilles étroites, and in German, it is identified as schmalblättriger Phenax.
