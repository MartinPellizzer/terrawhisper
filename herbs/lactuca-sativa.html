Beyond its culinary popularity, it possesses therapeutic potential, containing bioactive compounds that offer antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. This ancient plant serves as both a dietary staple and a functional medicinal resource.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lactuca, which contains the species Lactuca sativa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of presence across several regions in the European continent. In Northern Europe, its distribution can be observed in Finland, Sweden, and Great Britain. Moving towards the center of the continent, the species is also found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, it is documented within the borders of Austria.
