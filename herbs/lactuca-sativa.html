Lactuca sativa

Lettuce

Lactuca sativa, commonly known as lettuce, is a versatile leafy green celebrated for its nutritional value and calming properties. Beyond culinary uses, it contains lactucarium, a milky substance historically utilized in traditional medicine for its mild sedative, analgesic, and anti-inflammatory effects, making it a fascinating subject of ethnobotanical study.

  • Scientific name: Lactuca sativa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lactuca, which contains the species Lactuca sativa.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lactuca sativa, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. In English, it is referred to as Leahtric, while in Chinese, it is called 萵苣 (zh). In Polish, the species is known as sałata siewna (pl), and in Basque, it is identified as Uraza (eu). Additionally, it is called Pilliyuyu in Quechua (qu).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of presence across several regions in the European continent. In Northern Europe, its distribution can be observed in Finland, Sweden, and Great Britain. Moving towards the center of the continent, the species is also found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, it is documented within the borders of Austria. Furthermore, its geographical reach extends to the territories of Czechia and Slovakia.
