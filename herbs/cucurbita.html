Cucurbita

Cucurbita, commonly known as squash, pumpkin, or gourd, belongs to the Cucurbitaceae family. Renowned for their nutritional density, these plants offer various medicinal benefits, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. From supporting digestive health to boosting immunity, Cucurbita species serve as vital dietary staples and therapeutic agents in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cucurbita
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Cucurbitales, it is a member of the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Cucurbita.
