Curcuma comosa

Curcumma comosa, commonly known as Tongkat Ali, is a powerful medicinal herb native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to enhance vitality, balance hormones, and boost energy levels. This natural adaptogen offers significant therapeutic potential for supporting overall strength and reproductive health.

  • Scientific name: Curcuma comosa
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Curcuma comosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Curcuma.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is specifically noted in the state of Assam. Moving into the Indo-China region, it can be found distributed throughout both Myanmar and Thailand. Additionally, the species extends into the Malesia region, specifically inhabiting Malaya. These locations highlight the widespread natural habitat of Curcuma comosa across Southeast Asia.
