Caragana manshurica

What's the taxonomical classification of Caragana manshurica?

Caragana manshurica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant's lineage continues through the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Caragana, and its species name, manshurica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Caragana manshurica has pinnately compound leaves consisting of 7 to 15 leaflets that are ovate or lanceolate in shape and possess serrated margins. The plant typically grows as a deciduous shrub or small tree reaching heights of up to 5 meters. Its bark is smooth and greyish, while the branches often feature small, inconspicuous lenticels. During the blooming season, it produces clusters of bright red, pea-like flowers that emerge from terminal or axillary positions. The fruit is a small, dry legume pod that eventually dehisces to release its seeds.
