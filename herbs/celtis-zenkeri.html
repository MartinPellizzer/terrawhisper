Celtis zenkeri

Celtis zenkeri, commonly known as the Zenker's nettle tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to African tropical forests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions, showcasing its profound ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Celtis zenkeri
  • Family: Cannabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Cannabaeaceae. Its taxonomic identification is completed by its placement within the genus Celtis.

Distribution

This plant is native to the lush landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its geographical presence is notably documented across several distinct countries in this region. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Benin and Guinea. The species also thrives in the tropical environments of Ivory Coast and Liberia. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the diverse ecosystems of Nigeria.
