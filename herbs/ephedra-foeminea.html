Plant Parts

This plant, Ephedra foeminea, is a medicinal shrub where the primary therapeutic components are derived from its specialized structures. As an evergreen herb, it possesses distinct, scale-like leaves that are highly reduced in size, serving to minimize water loss while the plant focuses its energy on its photosynthetic stems. These parts are frequently collected and utilized in traditional medicine to extract various alkaloids for their physiological effects.
