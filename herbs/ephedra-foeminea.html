Ephedra foeminea

Ephedra foeminea, commonly known as the foeminean ephedra, is a medicinal shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its alkaloid content, particularly ephedrine, it has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries to treat respiratory ailments, stimulate circulation, and enhance physical endurance through its potent stimulant properties.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra foeminea
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ephedra foeminea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Gnetidae, it is classified under the order Ephedrales and the family Ephedraceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Ephedra.

Distribution

This plant, Ephedra foeminea, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of Southeastern Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Albania and Bulgaria.
