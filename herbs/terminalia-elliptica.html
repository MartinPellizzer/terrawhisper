Indian-laurel, scientifically known as Terminalia elliptica, is a versatile evergreen tree native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its bark, leaves, and roots possess potent bioactive compounds. These elements are widely utilized for their antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties to treat various skin and digestive ailments.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing natively throughout various parts of India. Its presence also extends into the neighboring country of Bangladesh. Furthermore, the species is commonly documented in Nepal and the mountainous areas of the East Himalaya. Additionally, the plant is known to thrive in the specific environmental conditions of Assam.
