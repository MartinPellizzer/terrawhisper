Indian-laurel, scientifically known as Terminalia elliptica, is a versatile evergreen tree native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its bark, leaves, and roots possess potent bioactive compounds. These elements are widely utilized for their antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties to treat various skin and digestive ailments.
