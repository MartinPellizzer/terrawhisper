Epimedium diphyllum

Epimedium diphyllum, commonly known as Horny Goat Weed, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, it is primarily used to enhance vitality and support reproductive health. Its active compounds, including icariin, offer potential benefits for bone density and circulatory function.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium diphyllum
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Epimedium.
