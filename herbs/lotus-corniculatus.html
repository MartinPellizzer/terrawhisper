Plant Parts

This plant, Lotus corniculatus, is a perennial herb characterized by its distinct botanical structure, though much of its medicinal value and biological stability is anchored in its underground components. The roots of this species are typically slender and taproot-like, extending into the soil to provide essential stability and facilitate the absorption of nutrients and water. These roots play a vital role in the plant's ability to thrive in various habitats, ensuring the continuous growth of its yellow-flowered stems and trifoliate leaves.
