Senna obtusifolia

Senna obtusifolia, commonly known as the blunt senna, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, it contains anthraquinones that stimulate bowel movements. Beyond digestive health, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its diverse therapeutic and healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Senna obtusifolia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Specifically, it is classified under the genus Senna.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents and climatic zones. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented in Belgium, while it also grows within the Mediterranean landscapes of Spain in Southwestern Europe. Moving toward the African continent, the species is found in the regions of Libya within Northern Africa. Its distribution extends into the Atlantic through the Macaronesian islands of Cape Verde. Finally, the plant reaches the tropical reaches of West Africa, specifically within the borders of Benin.
