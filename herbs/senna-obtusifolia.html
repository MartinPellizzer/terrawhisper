American Sicklepod

Senna obtusifolia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (576 studies) · ★★★★★

American Sicklepod, scientifically known as Senna obtusifolia, is a versatile medicinal herb native to the Americas. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, this plant contains anthraquinone glycosides used to treat constipation. Beyond digestion, it holds significant value in traditional ethnobotany for treating skin conditions and various inflammatory ailments.
