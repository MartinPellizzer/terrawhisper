Rodgersia aesculifolia

Rodgersia aesculifolia, commonly known as Aesculifolia Rodgersia, is a striking perennial prized for its dramatic, maple-like foliage. Native to mountainous regions of China, this robust plant thrives in moist, shaded environments. Its large, textured leaves and towering panicles of creamy white flowers make it a stunning ornamental landscape feature.

  • Scientific name: Rodgersia aesculifolia
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Saxifragales, it is a member of the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Rodgersia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Rodgersia aesculifolia, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Poland, it is called Rodgersja kasztanowoliścienna, while in Finland, it is known as sormivaleangervo. Its Chinese name is 七叶鬼灯檠, and in Estonia, it is identified as hobukastanilehine rodgersia. Additionally, in Sweden, the plant is commonly called Kastanjerodgersia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Germany. Moving to Asia, it can be found throughout various parts of China, including the South-Central territories. Its range also extends to the northern reaches of the continent in Inner Mongolia. Finally, the species is also documented across the China North-Central and China Southeast regions.
