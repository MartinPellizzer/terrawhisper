Cephalaria gigantea

Cephalaeria gigantea, commonly known as the Giant Euphorbia, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its unique chemical profile offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Cephalaria gigantea
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cephalaeria.
