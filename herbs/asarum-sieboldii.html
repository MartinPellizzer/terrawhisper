Seonun wild Ginger, scientifically known as Asarum sieboldii, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it is primarily utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and pain relief. This potent botanical offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds.
