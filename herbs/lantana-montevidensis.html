Lantana montevidensis

Lantana montevidensis, commonly known as trailing lantana, is a versatile evergreen perennial prized for its vibrant purple blooms. While primarily ornamental, this hardy plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various cultures have utilized its leaves and stems in traditional remedies to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Lantana montevidensis
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Lantana.
