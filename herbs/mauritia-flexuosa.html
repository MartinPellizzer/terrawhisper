Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the moriche palm, possesses a robust anatomical structure where the primary stem acts as a central support for its massive fronds. Within its complex architecture, the individual leaf stalks, or stipe, connect the foliage to the main body, while the collective stipes of the surrounding canopy create a dense forest structure. Below the surface, an extensive network of roots stabilizes the palm in swampy terrains, with each individual root playing a vital role in absorbing nutrients and water to sustain its medicinal properties.
