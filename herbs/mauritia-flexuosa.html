Mauritia flexuosa

Ita palm

Mauritia flexuosa, commonly known as the Moriche palm or Buriti, is a vital multipurpose plant native to Amazonian wetlands. Renowned for its nutrient-dense fruit, it provides essential vitamins and antioxidants. Beyond nutrition, its leaves and fibers are indispensable for traditional crafts, medicine, and sustainable ecological restoration efforts.

  • Scientific name: Mauritia flexuosa
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Arecales and the family Arecaceae, it is classified under the genus Mauritia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Mauritia flexuosa, is referred to by various names across different languages and regions. In the Netherlands, it is called the Mauritiuspalm (nl), while in Portuguese-speaking areas, it is widely recognized as Buriti (pt). German speakers refer to it as the Buriti-Palm (de), and in Persian, it is known as موریچ (fa). Additionally, in Lithuania, the species is identified as Lanksčioji mauricija (lt).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence throughout several specific tropical regions. In the Caribbean, its range includes the islands of Trinidad and Tobago. Moving to northern South America, it can be found across the territory of French Guiana. The species also inhabits the vast landscapes of Guyana and Suriname. Finally, its geographical distribution extends into the nation of Venezuela.
