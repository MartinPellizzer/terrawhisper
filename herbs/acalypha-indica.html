Acalypha indica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (292 studies) · ★★★★★

Indian Copperleaf, scientifically known as Acalypha indica, is a versatile medicinal shrub widely used in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties. It serves as a vital natural resource for treating various skin and digestive ailments.
