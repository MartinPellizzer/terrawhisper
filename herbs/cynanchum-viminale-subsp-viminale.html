Common Caustic Vine, scientifically known as Cynanchum viminale subsp. viminale, is a climbing perennial native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this plant is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues through its potent bioactive compounds.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide range of habitats across several distinct regions of Africa and its surrounding islands. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is documented within the territories of Kenya and Tanzania. The species also extends into the Western Indian Ocean, where it can be found on the Aldabra archipelago. Furthermore, it inhabits the islands of the Comoros. Finally, the distribution of this subspecies includes the large island of Madagascar.
