Putranjiva, scientifically known as Putranjiva roxburghii, is a versatile medicinal shrub renowned in Ayurveda for its therapeutic properties. Often called the "child-protector," it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial qualities. Its various parts, including leaves and roots, are traditionally used to treat respiratory issues, skin ailments, and digestive disorders.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the Southeast region. Its distribution extends through Eastern Asia, where it can be found in Japan and the Nansei-shoto islands. Additionally, the species is documented to grow in Taiwan. Finally, its reach includes the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the area of Assam.
