Entandrophragma angolense

Entandrophragma angolense, commonly known as the African mahogany, is a significant medicinal tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to gastrointestinal issues, offering vast potential for modern drug discovery.

  • Scientific name: Entandrophragma angolense
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa
