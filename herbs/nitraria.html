Nitraria

Nitraria, commonly known as the nitraria or saltbush, is a genus of shrubs and herbs primarily found in arid regions. Renowned for its ability to thrive in saline soils, this resilient plant offers significant ethnobotanical value, providing nutritional resources and traditional medicinal applications across various diverse ecosystems globally.

  • Scientific name: Nitraria
  • Family: Nitrariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Nitrariaceae and falls under the genus Nitraria.
