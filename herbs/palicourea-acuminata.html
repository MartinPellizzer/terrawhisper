Palicourea acuminata, scientifically known as Palicourea acuminata, is a potent medicinal shrub native to the Brazilian Atlantic Forest. Renowned for its strong contraceptive properties, it contains bioactive alkaloids used traditionally in folk medicine. Today, it remains a significant subject of pharmacological research regarding reproductive health and hormonal regulation.
