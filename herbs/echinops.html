Echinops

Echinops, commonly known as globe thistle, is a striking perennial valued for its unique, spherical flower heads. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this resilient plant holds significant medicinal potential. Traditionally used in various cultures, its properties are being explored for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and various therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Echinops
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and specifically within the genus Echinops.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range within the northern regions of Europe. In the northernmost parts of the continent, it can be found growing in Norway and Sweden. Its distribution also extends across the British Isles, where it is present in both Great Britain and Ireland. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Collectively, these locations highlight a concentrated presence of Echinops throughout Northern Europe.
