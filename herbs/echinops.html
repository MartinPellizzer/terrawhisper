Globe-thistle, scientifically known as Echinops, is a striking perennial prized for its architectural, spherical flower heads. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is frequently utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and digestive properties, offering a natural approach to supporting overall wellness.
