Delphinium glandulosum

Delphinium glandulosum, commonly known as the glandular larkspur, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific montane regions. Renowned for its striking floral displays, this plant holds significant botanical interest. While used in traditional herbal practices, its potent alkaloids require careful handling due to their high toxicity and medicinal complexity.

  • Scientific name: Delphinium glandulosum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Delphinium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Delphinium glandulosum, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is typically called the glandular larkspur, while in German, it is known as die Drückende Rittersporn. In French, the species is identified as le Delphinium glanduleux, and in Latin, it is traditionally termed delphinium glandulosum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions of Western Asia and the Caucasus. Within the Caucasus, its presence is specifically noted in the Transcaucasus region. Moving towards Western Asia, it can be found spanning the area from Lebanon to Syria. Furthermore, the species is also distributed across Türkiye. Together, these locations define the primary natural range of Delphinium glandulosum.
