What's the taxonomical classification of Euonymus myrianthus?

Euonymus myrianthus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this classification, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the order Celastrales and is a member of the family Celastraeaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Euonymus and the species myrianthus.
