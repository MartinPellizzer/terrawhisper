Luculia pinceana

Luculia pinceana, commonly known as the Luculia or Pincean Luculia, is an exquisite evergreen shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its fragrant, bell-shaped white blossoms and lush foliage, this ornamental plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its soothing properties and its aesthetic elegance in tropical gardens.

  • Scientific name: Luculia pinceana
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rubiaceae, falling under the genus Luculia.

Common Names

This plant, Luculia pinceana, is known by several common names depending on the linguistic context, though they are consistent across different Chinese dialects. In Mandarin Chinese, it is referred to as 滇丁香 (zh), and this specific nomenclature is used interchangeably in Simplified Chinese (zh-cn and zh-hans) and Traditional Chinese (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central and Southeast territories, as well as in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is recorded growing throughout the East Himalaya region. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse mountain and subtropical environments.
