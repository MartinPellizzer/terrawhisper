Filago congesta

Filago congesta, commonly known as congested cudweed, is a small, annual herbaceous plant belonging to the Asteraceae family. Often found in sandy or disturbed soils, this resilient species is characterized by its dense, clustered flowering heads. In traditional medicine, it is valued for its diverse bioactive properties and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Filago congesta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Filago congesta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Filago.
