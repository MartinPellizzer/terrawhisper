Isatis minima

Isaatis minima, commonly known as Pennywort, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, this plant is frequently used to promote skin healing, support cognitive function, and boost immune health, making it a significant botanical resource in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Isatis minima
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Isatis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the vast landscapes of Central Asia. Its natural habitat includes the diverse territories of Kazakhstan and Kirgizstan. Additionally, its distribution extends into the regions of Turkmenistan and Tadzhikistan. The species can also be located within the borders of Uzbekistan. Consequently, these five nations represent the core geographical range for Isatis minima.
