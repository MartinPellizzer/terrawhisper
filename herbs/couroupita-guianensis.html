Couroupita guianensis

Couroupita guianensis, commonly known as the Cannonball tree, is a spectacular tropical evergreen valued for its unique flowers and medicinal potential. Beyond its ornamental beauty, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions, showcasing its diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Couroupita guianensis
  • Family: Lecythidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant, known scientifically as Couroupita guianensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
