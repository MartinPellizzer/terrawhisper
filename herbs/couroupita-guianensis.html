Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple tropical and subtropical regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in Bangladesh. Its presence extends to the Indo-China region, specifically inhabiting the Andaman Islands and the Nicobar Islands. Additionally, the species is located in the Northwestern Pacific area, including the Marshall Islands. Finally, its range reaches the Southeastern United States, where it is documented in Florida.
