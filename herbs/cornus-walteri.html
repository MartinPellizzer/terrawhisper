Cornus walteri

Walter's dogwood

Cornus walteri, commonly known as Walter's dogwood, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its striking foliage and delicate blooms, this plant has historically been utilized in traditional healing practices to address inflammatory conditions and support digestive wellness through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Cornus walteri
  • Family: Cornaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement includes the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Cornales. It is a member of the Cornaceae family and is specifically categorized under the genus Cornus.
