Adenophora himalayana

What's the taxonomical classification of Adenophora himalayana?

Adenophora himalayana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Campanulaceae, falling under the genus Adenophora, with the specific designation of the species himalayana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Adenophora himalayana has an erect, perennial herb structure characterized by ovate to lanceolate leaves with serrated margins and small, tubular, bell-shaped violet or purple flowers arranged in terminal racemes. The stems are typically slender and can reach heights of up to 60 centimeters depending on the environment. Its leaves are arranged oppositely along the stem and possess distinct venation patterns. The root system consists of a fleshy, tuberous rhizome that helps the plant persist through dormant seasons. The inflorescence features multiple flowers held on pedicels that emerge from the upper leaf axils.
