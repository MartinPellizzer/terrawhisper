Clinopodium menthifolium

Wood calamint

Clinopodium menthifolium, commonly known as minty polemint, is a versatile aromatic herb valued in traditional medicine. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, it possesses potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. Often utilized to soothe digestive ailments and reduce inflammation, this plant serves as a vital natural resource for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Clinopodium menthifolium
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
