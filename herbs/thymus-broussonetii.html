Thymus broussonetii

Thymus broussonetii, commonly known as Broussonet's thyme, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific Mediterranean regions. Valued for its aromatic properties, this plant contains essential oils with potential therapeutic benefits. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it offers unique phytochemical profiles that interest researchers studying natural antimicrobial and antioxidant compounds.
