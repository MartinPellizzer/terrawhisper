Geoffroea decorticans

Geoffroea decorticans, commonly known as the flowering geoffroea, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this plant contains bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin conditions. Its unique therapeutic properties continue to inspire modern pharmacological research and natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Geoffroea decorticans
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Geoffroea decoratica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with its placement in the genus Geoffroea.

Common Names

This plant is known by several different names depending on the language and botanical classification used. In Quechua, it is referred to as Chañar, while it is also identified by the name Gourliea decorticans in Quechua. In Hebrew, the plant is called ג'ופריאה מתקלפת, and in Spanish, it is commonly known as both Geoffroea spinosa and Gourliea decorticans.

Moving toward the southern part of the continent, its range extends significantly into Argentina. Within Argentina, the species is located in the northeast, the south, and the northwest regions.
