Myrcia chytraculia var. pauciflora

Myrcia chytraculia var. pauciflora, commonly known as a rare medicinal variant of the Myrtaceae family, holds significant ethnobotanical potential. Found in specific ecological niches, this plant is valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to explore new therapeutic applications in traditional and modern pharmacology.
