Vincetoxicum arnottianum

Vincetoxicum arnottianum, commonly known as the climbing milkweed, is a perennial climber native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant contains bioactive alkaloids used to treat various ailments, including skin diseases and inflammation. Its unique chemical properties continue to attract significant pharmacological research and interest.
