Lamium album album, scientifically known as Lamium album subsp. album, is a distinctive perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Commonly called white dead-nettle, this plant is characterized by its striking white flowers and serrated leaves. It holds significant value in traditional herbal medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties.
