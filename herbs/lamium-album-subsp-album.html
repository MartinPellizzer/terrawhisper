Lamium album subsp. album

Lamium album album

Lamium album subsp. album, commonly known as White Dead-nettle, is a perennial herbaceous plant belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Distinguished by its square stems and hooded white flowers, it thrives in disturbed soils. Traditionally, it has been utilized in herbal medicine for its anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Lamium album subsp. album
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
