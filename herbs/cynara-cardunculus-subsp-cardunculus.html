Cardoon, scientifically known as Cynara cardunculus subsp. cardunculus, is a versatile perennial plant celebrated for its culinary and medicinal properties. Closely related to the artichoke, it is rich in bioactive compounds like cynarin, which support liver health, aid digestion, and possess potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects in traditional medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the artichoke thistle, utilizes various components for medicinal purposes, most notably its leaf and root extracts. The leaves are highly valued in herbal medicine for their high concentration of cynarin and chlorogenic acid, which are frequently used to support liver function and promote bile production. Additionally, root extracts are often utilized in therapeutic formulations to aid digestion and provide antioxidant benefits, making the entire structural profile of the plant essential to its traditional healing properties.
