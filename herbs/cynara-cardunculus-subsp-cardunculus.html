Scientific literature: Sparse (71 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Cardoon, scientifically known as Cynara cardunculus subsp. cardunculus, is a versatile perennial plant celebrated for its culinary and medicinal properties. Closely related to the artichoke, it is rich in bioactive compounds like cynarin, which support liver health, aid digestion, and possess potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects in traditional medicine.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cynara as the subspecies cardunculus.
