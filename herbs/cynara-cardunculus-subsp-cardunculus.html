Cynara cardunculus subsp. cardunculus

Cynara cardunculus subsp. cardunculus, commonly known as the wild artichoke or cardoon, is a resilient Mediterranean perennial prized for its medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like cynarin, it is extensively utilized in traditional medicine to support liver health, stimulate bile production, and aid digestive function and detoxification.

  • Scientific name: Cynara cardunculus subsp. cardunculus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cynara as the subspecies cardunculus.
