Phalsa, scientifically known as Grewia asiatica, is a versatile deciduous tree native to the arid regions of India and Central Asia. Renowned for its medicinal properties, the plant offers various therapeutic benefits, including digestive support and anti-inflammatory effects, making it a significant component in traditional Ayurvedic healing practices.
