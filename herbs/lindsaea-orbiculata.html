What's the taxonomical classification of Lindsaea orbiculata?

Lindsaea orbiculata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae, it falls under the order Polypodiales. This plant is a representative of the family Lindsaeaceae, specifically positioned within the genus Lindsaea, with its unique identity defined by the species epithet orbiculata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lindsaea orbiculata has orbicular to broadly ovate fronds that measure approximately 5 to 15 centimeters in width and possess distinctively rounded lobes. The leaf margins are typically entire or slightly crenate, and the venation pattern consists of free veins that extend toward the edges. The stipe is relatively slender and often exhibits a dark pigmentation near the base. On the underside of the pinnules, the sori are arranged in rows following the vein structure and are protected by a thin, kidney-shaped indusium.
