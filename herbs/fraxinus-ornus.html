Manna ash, scientifically known as Fraxinus ornus, is a distinctive deciduous tree native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its ornamental beauty and fragrant white blossoms, it holds historical significance in herbal medicine. Traditionally, its sap was harvested to create "manna," a sweet substance used for its mild laxative properties.
