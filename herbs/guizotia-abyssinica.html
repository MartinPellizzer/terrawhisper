Plant Parts

This plant, Guisotia abyssinica, commonly known as Korarima, is a member of the Zingiberaceae family, and while various parts of the herb are utilized in traditional medicine, the seed is a particularly significant component. The seeds, often referred to as cardamom pods in a culinary and medicinal context, are harvested and dried for use in treating gastrointestinal issues and respiratory ailments. Beyond the seed, the plant's rhizomes and leaves are also frequently employed in herbal remedies, but the potent aromatic seeds remain a primary focus for their therapeutic properties.
