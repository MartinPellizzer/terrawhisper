Asplenium scolopendrium, scientifically known as Asplenium scolopendrium, is the iconic Hart's-tongue fern. Characterized by its long, undivided, glossy green fronds, this shade-loving perennial thrives in moist, limestone-rich environments. Historically valued in traditional herbalism, it serves as a fascinating subject for both botanical study and ancient medicinal folklore.

Distribution

This plant, known as Asplenium scolopendrium, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the northern regions of Europe.
