According to a study published by "Phytochemistry", Delphinium kamaonense Huth is a folkloric plant resource with medicinal values. Seven undescribed lycaconitine-type C19-diterpenoid alkaloids and ten known compounds were isolated from the plant. In vitro nitric oxide inhibitory activities assays showed that these lycaconitine-type C19-diterpenoid alkaloids possessed significant anti-inflammatoy inhibitory activities. Kamaonensine E was identified as the most potent compound with an activity of 0.9 ± 0.2 μM, which is stronger than the positive value of 9.0 ± 1.3 μM. Network pharmacology studies suggested the anti-inflammatoy mechanism might involve MAPK signaling pathways by binding to 3 key targets: MAPK8, MAPK14, and HSP90α.
