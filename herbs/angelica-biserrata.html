Angelica biserrata

Angelica biserrata, commonly known as "Kanchu" in traditional medicine, is a perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it has long been utilized in Ayurvedic practices to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues, digestive disorders, and inflammatory conditions, showcasing its significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Angelica biserrata
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Angelica, specifically as the species Angelica biserrata.

Plant Parts

This plant, Angelica biserrata, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its specific underground structures. The therapeutic properties are primarily concentrated in the root, which is harvested to obtain its various medicinal components. Specifically, practitioners often focus on the root bark and the inner root pith to extract active compounds. For medicinal preparation and storage, the plant is typically processed into dried roots, ensuring that the roots retain their potency for long-term use in various herbal formulations.
