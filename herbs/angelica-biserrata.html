Angelica biserata, scientifically known as Angelica biserata, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of China. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its roots are prized for their therapeutic properties, often used to treat respiratory ailments, alleviate pain, and promote blood circulation through various holistic healing practices.
