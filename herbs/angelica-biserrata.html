Angelica biserata, scientifically known as Angelica biserata, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of China. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its roots are prized for their therapeutic properties, often used to treat respiratory ailments, alleviate pain, and promote blood circulation through various holistic healing practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, Angelica biserrata, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its specific underground structures. The therapeutic properties are primarily concentrated in the root, which is harvested to obtain its various medicinal components. Specifically, practitioners often focus on the root bark and the inner root pith to extract active compounds. For medicinal preparation and storage, the plant is typically processed into dried roots, ensuring that the roots retain their potency for long-term use in various herbal formulations.
