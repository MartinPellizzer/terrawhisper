Elaeagnus loureiroi

Elaeagnus loureiroi, commonly known as Silverberry or Oleaster, is a versatile evergreen shrub valued for its medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to support digestive health and skin vitality. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential in various herbal and natural wellness applications.

  • Scientific name: Elaeagnus loureiroi
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
