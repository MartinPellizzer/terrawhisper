Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the vast landscapes of Siberia. It can be found growing within the Altay region, marking one part of its northern range. The species is also documented throughout the territory of Buryatiya. Furthermore, its presence extends into the Chita area of the Siberian wilderness. Finally, the plant is distributed across the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions, completing its regional profile.
