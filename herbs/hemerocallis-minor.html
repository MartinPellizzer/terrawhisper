What are the herbal preparations of this plant?

This plant is Hemerocallis minor, a species used in traditional medicine to create various therapeutic decoctions and extracts. Practitioners often employ these herbal extracts to address inflammatory conditions and soothe internal heat syndromes. The diverse applications of Hemerocallis minor underscore its importance in traditional pharmacopeias.
