Lactuca indica

Lactuca indica, commonly known as Indian Lettuce, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, this plant is recognized for its unique properties, often used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and fever. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Lactuca indica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Lactuca.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the Central African Republic. Its presence extends to Southern Africa, specifically in the KwaZulu-Natal region. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout the Western Indian Ocean. This includes notable populations located in Comoros, Mauritius, and Madagascar.
