Indian lettuce, scientifically known as Lactuca indica, is a versatile medicinal herb native to various Asian regions. Renowned for its cooling properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, fever, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing practices.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the Central African Republic.
