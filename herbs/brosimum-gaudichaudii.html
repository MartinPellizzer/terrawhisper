Brosimum gaudichaudii

Brosimum gaudichauidii, commonly known as Copal Blanco, is a significant medicinal tree native to the Neotropics. Renowned for its versatile resin, this species is deeply embedded in traditional folk medicine. It is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and healing properties, playing a vital role in community healthcare practices.
