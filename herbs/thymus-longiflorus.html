Thymus longiflorus

Thymus longiflorus, commonly known as the long-flowered thyme, is a distinctive aromatic herb native to specific Mediterranean climates. Renowned for its delicate, tubular blossoms and potent essential oils, this plant offers significant medicinal potential, traditionally utilized for its antiseptic, calming, and digestive properties within various holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Thymus longiflorus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus classification as Thymus.
