Austroflourensia fiebrigii

Austroflourensia fiebrigii, commonly known as the fever-leaf herb, is a potent medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, it contains unique bioactive compounds used to combat inflammation and reduce high fevers. This resilient species serves as a vital natural resource for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Austroflourensia fiebrigii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Austroflourensia.
