Ugni molinae

Ugni molinae, commonly known as Chilean guava, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to South America. Highly valued for its delicious, antioxidant-rich berries, it is also utilized in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial properties. This resilient plant is increasingly popular in gardens for both its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential.

  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Europe
