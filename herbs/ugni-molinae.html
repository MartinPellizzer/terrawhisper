Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Chilean guava, is highly valued for its edible fruit, which consists of small, fleshy berries that range in color from bright red to deep purple. These berries are the primary part of the plant used for medicinal and culinary purposes, often consumed fresh or processed into jams and syrups due to their high antioxidant content.
