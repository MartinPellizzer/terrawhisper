Guettarda viburnoides

Guettarda viburnoides, commonly known as the viburnum-leaf shrub, is a resilient medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Exploring its unique phytochemical profile reveals promising applications in treating various ailments, highlighting its importance in natural pharmacology and herbal medicine.

  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
