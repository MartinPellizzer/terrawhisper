Scrophularia ningpoensis

Scrophularia ningpoensis, commonly known as Ningbo Figwort, is a significant medicinal herb in traditional East Asian medicine. Valued for its cooling and detoxifying properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, swelling, and skin conditions. This plant plays a crucial role in holistic approaches to managing various systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Scrophularia ningpoensis
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Scrophularia, as part of the species Scrophularia ningpoensis.

Common Names

This plant, Scrophularia ningpoensis, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans), it is referred to as 宁波玄参, while in Traditional Chinese (zh-hant) and the Cantonese usage of Hong Kong (zh-hk), it is simply called 玄參. In mainland China (zh), the name 宁波玄参 is commonly used, and in Thailand (th), it is known as เอี้ยงเซียม.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several distinct regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the Inner Mongolia region in the north to the Southeast provinces. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the North-Central and South-Central territories of the country. Beyond the borders of China, the species extends its reach into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, this medicinal plant is also known to grow within the country of Vietnam.
