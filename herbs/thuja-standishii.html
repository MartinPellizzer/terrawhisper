Thuja standishii

Thuja standishii, commonly known as the Western Red Cedar, is a magnificent evergreen native to North America's Pacific coast. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this botanical treasure holds historical medicinal significance. Traditionally, its aromatic foliage has been utilized for its antimicrobial properties and to treat various skin and respiratory ailments.

  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
