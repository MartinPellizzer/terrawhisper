Thuja standishii

Thuja standishii, commonly known as the Western Red Cedar, is a magnificent evergreen native to North America's Pacific coast. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this botanical treasure holds historical medicinal significance. Traditionally, its aromatic foliage has been utilized for its antimicrobial properties and to treat various skin and respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Thuja standishii
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is categorized under the order Pinales and the family Cupressaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Thuja.
