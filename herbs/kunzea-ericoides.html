Kunzea ericoides

Kunzea ericoides, commonly known as kānuka, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to New Zealand. Renowned for its ability to thrive in diverse ecosystems, this resilient plant offers significant medicinal potential. Its aromatic leaves and bark are traditionally used for their antimicrobial, soothing, and healing properties in natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Kunzea ericoides
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic details place it in the subclass Magnoliidae and order Myrtales, falling under the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized within the genus Kunzea.
