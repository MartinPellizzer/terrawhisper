Burgan, scientifically known as Kunzea ericoides, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to New Zealand. Renowned for its profuse white flower clusters, it holds significant medicinal value. Traditionally used in Māori healing, its extracts possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering promising therapeutic potential in modern natural medicine.
