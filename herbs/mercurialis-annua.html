Mercurialis annua

Mercurialis annua, commonly known as annual glechoma or annual mercurial, is a versatile medicinal herb found in temperate regions. Renowned in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and respiratory issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern wellness.

  • Scientific name: Mercurialis annua
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
