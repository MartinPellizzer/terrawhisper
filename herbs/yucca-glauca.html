Soapweed yucca, scientifically known as Yucca glauca, is a resilient perennial native to North American prairies. Renowned for its saponin-rich roots, it has historically served as a natural cleanser. This hardy plant features striking white bell-shaped flowers and sword-like leaves, thriving in arid environments with minimal water.
