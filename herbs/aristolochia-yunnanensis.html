Aristolochia yunnanensis

Aristolochia yunnanensis, commonly known as Yunnan Aristolochia, is a significant medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, it is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties. While historically valued for treating various ailments, researchers closely monitor its usage due to potential nephrotoxicity risks.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia yunnanensis
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Aristolochia yunnanensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Piperales. Finally, it is a member of the family Aristolochiaceae and is situated within the genus Aristolochia.
