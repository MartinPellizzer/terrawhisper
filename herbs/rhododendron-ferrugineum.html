Rhododendron ferrugineum, scientifically known as Rhododendron ferrugineum, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude alpine zones of Europe. Renowned for its vibrant pink blossoms, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support respiratory and circulatory health in mountain ecosystems.
