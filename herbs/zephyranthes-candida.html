Zephyranthes candida

Zephyranthes candida, commonly known as the White Rain Lily, is a captivating perennial herb valued for its striking, snow-white blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest. It is utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in various healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Zephyranthes candida
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Africa
