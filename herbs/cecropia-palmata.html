Cecropia palmata

Cecropia palmata, commonly known as the palm-leaf Cecropia, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its significant bioactive properties, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat inflammation and infections. This study explores its phytochemical composition and potential therapeutic applications in modern pharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
