Cullen corylifolium

Cullen corylifolium, commonly known as Celery, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and alleviate stress. Its aromatic leaves offer both culinary delight and healing benefits.
