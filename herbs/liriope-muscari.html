Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Liriope muscari, possesses a unique underground structure that is central to its medicinal properties. The most significant part of the plant is its specialized tuberous roots, which are often referred to as liriope tubers or simply liropis tuber in various botanical contexts. These fleshy, storage tubers allow the plant to survive through different seasons, and the root system as a whole serves as the primary source for many traditional remedies. In addition to these underground structures, the plant also produces a small, dark fruit that contains its seeds, completing its life cycle.
