Jasmine, scientifically known as Jasminum, is a celebrated genus of shrubs and vines renowned for its intoxicating fragrance. Beyond its ornamental beauty, jasmine holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is widely utilized for its calming aromatherapy properties, skin-healing benefits, and its ability to reduce stress and anxiety.
