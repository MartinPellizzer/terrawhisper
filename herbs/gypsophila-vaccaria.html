Gypsophila vaccaria

Gypsophila vaccaria, commonly known as Vaccaria or annual gypsophila, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Eurasia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and skin inflammations, offering a natural approach to holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Gypsophila vaccaria
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Caryophyllaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Gypsophila, which includes the species Gypsophila vaccaria.
