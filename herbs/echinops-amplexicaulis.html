Echinops amplexicaulis

Echinops amplexiuscauis, commonly known as the Globe Thistle, is a striking perennial valued for its unique spherical flower heads. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized for its anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in various herbal remedies and traditional healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Echinops amplexicaulis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Echinops amplexicaulis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Echinops.

Common Names

This plant, Echinops amplexicaulis, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the nodding globe thistle, a name that reflects its characteristic drooping flower heads. In German, it is called die kriechende Kugeldistel, which describes its creeping growth habit and spherical blooms. Similarly, in Dutch, it is known as de kruipende pitbol, further emphasizing its unique botanical structure.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species is found in the Central African Republic and Cameroon. It also inhabits the East African highlands within the West-Central Tropical Africa classification, specifically in Burundi and Rwanda. Consequently, the range of Echinops amplexicaulis spans multiple diverse ecological zones throughout these central and western territories.
