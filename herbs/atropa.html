Atropa

Atropa, commonly known as deadly nightshade, is a highly toxic perennial herb belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its dark berries and bell-shaped flowers, it contains potent alkaloids like atropine. While historically significant in folklore and medicine, its extreme toxicity requires cautious handling and expert pharmacological application.

  • Scientific name: Atropa
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Ultimately, this medicinal species is identified by the genus Atropa.
