Caroxylon cyclophyllum

Caroxylon cyclophyllum, commonly known as the cycle-leaf plant, is a remarkable medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique phyllotaxy and potent bioactive compounds, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. It is widely studied for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Caroxylon cyclophyllum
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaranthaceae and falls under the genus Caroxylon.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across parts of Africa and Western Asia. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Egypt. Moving into Northeast Tropical Africa, the species can be found throughout Sudan and South Sudan. Its range extends into Western Asia, where it inhabits the territories of Iran and Iraq.
