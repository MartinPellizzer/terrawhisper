Euclea undulata

Euclea undulata, commonly known as the False Guarri, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its bark and roots are frequently used to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance and diverse therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Euclea undulata
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is further categorized into the family Ebenaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Euclea, specifically as the species Euclea undulata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Its presence extends into Southern Africa, where it is documented in Botswana and the Cape Provinces. Additionally, the species is known to inhabit the Caprivi Strip. Together, these locations highlight the diverse habitats occupied by Euclea undulata.
