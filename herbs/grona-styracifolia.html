Grona styracifolia

Grona styracifolia, commonly known as the Styrax-leafed Grona, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its unique leaf morphology, it contains bioactive compounds utilized in traditional healing practices. This species serves as a vital natural resource for treating various ailments and supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Grona styracifolia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy concludes within the family Fabaceae and the genus Grona.
