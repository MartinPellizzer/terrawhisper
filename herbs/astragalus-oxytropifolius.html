Astragalus oxytropifolius

Astragalus oxytropifolius, commonly known as the sharp-leaved milkvetch, is a resilient medicinal herb native to arid regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant and immunomodulatory properties. This versatile plant is studied for its ability to support cellular health and combat oxidative stress, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus oxytropifolius
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Astragalus, specifically as the species Astragalus oxytropifolius.

Common Names

This plant, Astragalus oxytrofolius, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the sharp-leaved milkvetch, while in Chinese, it is identified as 尖叶黄耆 (Jiān yè huáng qí). In Russian, the species is known as остролистный астрагал (ostrolistnyy astragal), and in Latin, its botanical designation remains Astragalus oxytrofolius.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Astragalus oxytropifolius, exhibits a specific and localized geographical distribution. Its presence is primarily documented within the region of Western Asia. Specifically, the species is native to the territory of Türkiye. This narrow range suggests that the plant is adapted to the particular environmental conditions found in this part of the world. Consequently, researchers focusing on its habitat concentrate their efforts within these specific Turkish landscapes.
