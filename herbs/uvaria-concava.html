Uvaria concava

Calabao

Uvaria concava, commonly known as a climbing shrub in various traditional medicine systems, belongs to the Annonaceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic potential, this plant is utilized in folk remedies to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant pharmacological interest for modern drug discovery efforts.

  • Scientific name: Uvaria concava
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Annonaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Uvaria.
