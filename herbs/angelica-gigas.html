Korean angelica, scientifically known as Angelica gigas, is a distinctive medicinal herb celebrated in traditional East Asian medicine. Distinguished by its deep purple stems and striking dark foliage, this potent plant is highly valued for its rich antioxidant properties and its ability to support hormonal balance and blood circulation.
