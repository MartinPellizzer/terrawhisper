Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Cat's Claw, is a woody vine characterized by its distinctive climbing mechanism. The climbing stem is supported by specialized, curved hooks that emerge from the nodes, allowing the plant to anchor itself firmly to surrounding vegetation. Its foliage consists of a pinnate leaf structure, which provides the surface area necessary for photosynthesis. During its reproductive phase, the vine produces small, yellowish-white flower clusters that emerge from the axils. While the entire plant grows vigorously, the medicinal properties are primarily derived from the bark and the woody stems of the vine.
