Linaria japonica

What's the taxonomical classification of Linaria japonica?

Linaria japonica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. At the class level, it is categorized under Equisetopsida, falling into the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized into the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Plantaginaceae. Within this family, it is placed in the genus Linaeria, with its specific identification being the species japonica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Linaria japonica has an erect, perennial growth habit characterized by slender, branched stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters. The leaves are mostly basal and linear or lanceolate in shape, while the cauline leaves are smaller and more sparsely distributed along the stem. Its flowers are zygomorphic and tubular, featuring a distinct two-lipped structure with a bright yellow corolla and purple markings on the throat. These blooms emerge from axillary positions and are supported by small, inconspicuous bracts. The plant also develops a woody base or rhizome that aids in its perennial survival.

What are the pharmacological activities of Linaria japonica?
