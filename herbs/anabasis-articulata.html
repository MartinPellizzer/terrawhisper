Anabasis articulata

Anabasis articulata, commonly known as the jointed anahesis, is a unique succulent shrub native to the coastal regions of Southern Africa. Thriving in saline, sandy environments, this resilient plant is distinguished by its segmented, leafless stems, playing a vital role in stabilizing coastal ecosystems and supporting local biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Anabasis articulata
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe
