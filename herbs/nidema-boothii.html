Nidema boothii

Nidema boothii, commonly known as a medicinal shrub native to tropical regions, is highly valued in traditional ethnobotany. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healing applications worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Nidema boothii
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Nidema boothii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the orchid family, Orchidaceae, and falls under the genus Nidema.

Common Names

This plant, Nidema boothii, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the False Spikenard, while in Spanish, it is commonly called Espiga Falsa. In Portuguese, the plant is known as Espiga Falsa, and in German, it is identified as Falses Spikenard.
