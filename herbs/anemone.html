Anemone

Anemone, commonly known as windflowers, is a versatile genus of flowering plants celebrated for their delicate beauty. Beyond their ornamental charm, various species hold significant roles in traditional medicine. Often used to treat skin conditions and inflammation, these plants offer a fascinating intersection of botanical elegance and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Anemone
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Anemone.
