Pedicularis plicata

Pedicularis plicata, commonly known as lousewort, is a perennial herb native to subarctic and alpine regions. Characterized by its distinctive pinkish-purple flower spikes, this plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem. While primarily known for its unique parasitic relationship with host plants, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Pedicularis plicata
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Pedicularis plicata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Orobanchaceae and the genus Pedicularis.
