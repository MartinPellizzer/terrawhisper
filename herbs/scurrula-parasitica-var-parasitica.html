Scurrula parasitica var. parasitica

Scurrula parasiticca var. parasiticca, commonly known as a hemi-parasitic mistletoe, holds significant value in traditional medicine. Often found growing on host trees, this unique plant is utilized in various cultures for its bioactive compounds, specifically targeted at treating inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and promoting overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Scurrula parasitica var. parasitica
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions within East Asia. In mainland China, its distribution spans from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast regions. It can also be found in the diverse landscapes of Tibet and on the tropical island of Hainan. Extending beyond the mainland, the species is documented in the eastern territory of Taiwan. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's wide range throughout much of the East Asian continent.
