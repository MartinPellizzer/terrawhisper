Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions within East Asia. In mainland China, its distribution spans from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast regions. It can also be found in the diverse landscapes of Tibet and on the tropical island of Hainan. Extending beyond the mainland, the species is documented in the eastern territory of Taiwan. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's wide range throughout much of the East Asian continent.
