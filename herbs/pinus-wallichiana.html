Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Blue Himalayan Cedar or Chir Pine, possesses distinct morphological features that are utilized in various medicinal applications. The leaves are needle-like, growing in pairs or clusters, and are often studied for their aromatic properties and essential oils. Additionally, the plant produces woody, cone-like fruits that contain the seeds, which are characteristic of the Pinaceae family and play a role in the plant's reproductive cycle and ecological significance.
