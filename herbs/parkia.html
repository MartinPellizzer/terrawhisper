Parkia

Parkia, commonly known as various names like locust bean or jamun, is a versatile genus of tropical trees. Renowned for its nutrient-dense pods, it serves as a vital food source and traditional medicine. Its seeds are often fermented into flavorful seasonings, playing a crucial role in diverse culinary cultures.

  • Scientific name: Parkia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Parkia.
