Thalictrum rhynchocarpum

Thalictrum rhynchocarpum, commonly known as a member of the Fumariaceae family, is a medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Often found in specific montane habitats, this plant possesses unique phytochemical properties. It is studied for its potential therapeutic applications, particularly in treating various ailments through its bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is a member of the Ranunculaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Thalictrum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West-Central Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of Burundi and Rwanda. The species is also documented across the territory of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Furthermore, its presence extends to Cameroon, highlighting its regional importance. Finally, the plant reaches the waters of the Gulf of Guinea Islands.
