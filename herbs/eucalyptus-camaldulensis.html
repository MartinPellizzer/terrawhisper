Eucalyptus camaldulensis

Eucalyptus camaldulensis, commonly known as the River Red Gum, is a resilient Australian native renowned for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils like eucalyptol, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, reduce inflammation, and provide antiseptic benefits, making it a cornerstone of herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus camaldulensis
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae, it is classified under the genus Eucalyptus.

Distribution

This plant, Eucalyptus camaldulensis, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of Southwestern Europe.
