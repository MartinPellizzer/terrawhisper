Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Curry leaf, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, most notably its leaves and fruits. The aromatic leaves are the most widely used part, frequently dried or crushed to extract essential oils that aid in digestion and provide antioxidant benefits. While the leaves are the primary focus of traditional medicine, the small, fleshy fruits also play a role in the plant's lifecycle and can be studied for their specific chemical constituents.
