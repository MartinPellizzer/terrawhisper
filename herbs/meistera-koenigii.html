Meistera koenigii

Meistera koeigii, commonly known as Curry Tree, is a highly valued medicinal plant native to India and Sri Lanka. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it is widely used in traditional Ayurveda to aid digestion, manage diabetes, and promote hair health, offering a unique blend of culinary and therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Meistera koenigii
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. It is formally identified by the genus Meistera, specifically as the species Meistera koeinigii.

Common Names

This plant, Meistera koenigii, is known by various names across different regions and languages. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Curry tree, while in Hindi, it is known as Kadi Patta. In Tamil, it is called Kariveppilai, and in Malayalam, it is referred to as Kariveppila.
