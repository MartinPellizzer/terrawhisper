Davallia formosana

Davalliia formosana, commonly known as the Formosan Rabbit's Foot Fern, is a resilient epiphytic plant prized for its soft, fuzzy rhizomes. While primarily enjoyed as an ornamental species, its unique biological properties offer intriguing potential in traditional ethnobotany, inviting further scientific exploration into its diverse medicinal applications.
