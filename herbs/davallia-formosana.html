What's the taxonomical classification of Davallia formosana?

Davalliia formosana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and is further organized into the order Polypodiales. This fern is a representative of the family Polypodiaceae, situated within the genus Davallia, and is specifically identified by the species name formosana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Davalliia formosana has a creeping rhizome covered in dense, brownish-to-tan scales and produces pinnate fronds that typically measure between 10 and 30 centimeters in length. The stipes are relatively short and possess a similar scaly texture that provides structural support to the foliage. Each leaf consists of multiple pairs of small, oblong to lanceolate pinnules arranged along a central axis. The margins of these segments are often slightly serrated or entire depending on the specific growth stage. Small, spore-bearing sori are located on the underside of the pinnules, which is characteristic of this epiphytic fern species.
