Taxillus sutchuenensis var. duclouxii

Taxillus sutchuenenensis var. duclouxii, commonly known as a rare medicinal variety of the mistletoe family, holds significant ethnobotanical value. Found in specific tropical habitats, this plant is prized in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties, potentially offering therapeutic benefits in treating various ailments through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Taxillus sutchuenensis var. duclouxii
  • Family: Loranthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Santalales. It is a member of the family Loranthaceae and specifically falls under the genus Taxillus.
