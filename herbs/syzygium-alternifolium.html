Syzygium alternifolium

Syzygium alternifolium, commonly known as the False Jamun or Indian Rose Myrtle, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Western Ghats. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, it is traditionally used in Ayurvedic medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions effectively.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium alternifolium
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by its scientific genus, Syzygium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Syzygium alitfolium, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as clove leaf or clove tree. Moving to local vernaculars, it is known as cengkeh in Indonesian and cengkeh putih in Malay. In Hindi, the plant is commonly called laung patta, while in Tagalog, it is often called dahon ng clove.
