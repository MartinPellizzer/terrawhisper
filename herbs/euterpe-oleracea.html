Açai palm, scientifically known as Euterpe oleracea, is a vital medicinal and nutritional powerhouse native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich berries, this palm offers significant health benefits, including anti-inflammatory properties and enhanced energy. It remains a cornerstone of traditional medicine and global superfood nutrition.
