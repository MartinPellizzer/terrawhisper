This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the tropical regions of the Americas. Plant Parts

This plant, Euterpe oleracea, commonly known as açaí, offers a variety of medicinal and nutritional components across its different structures. The most prominent part is the fruit, a small, dark purple drupe that is widely harvested for its antioxidant properties. Inside the fruit, the nutrient-dense fruit pulp serves as the primary source of healthy fats and vitamins, making it the most consumed element for health benefits. Within the center of the fruit lie the seeds, which are often discarded during processing but remain a significant part of the plant's biological structure. Furthermore, the plant is also valued for the oil extracted from its components, which is prized in both nutritional supplements and cosmetic applications due to its high concentration of essential fatty acids.
