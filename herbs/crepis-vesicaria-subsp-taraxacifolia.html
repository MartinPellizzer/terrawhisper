Crepis vesicaria subsp. taraxacifolia

Crepis vesicaria subsp. taraxacifolia, commonly known as dandelion hawk's-beard, is a versatile medicinal herb within the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its diuretic and digestive properties, this plant is traditionally used to support liver function and detoxification. Its nutrient-rich leaves offer significant antioxidant benefits for holistic wellness and herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Crepis vesicaria subsp. taraxacifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
