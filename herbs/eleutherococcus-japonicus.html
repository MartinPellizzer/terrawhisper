Eleutherococcus japonicus

Eleutherococcus japonicus, commonly known as Japanese Siberian ginseng, is a potent adaptogenic shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its ability to enhance physical stamina and mental resilience, this medicinal plant helps the body combat stress. It is widely utilized in traditional herbal medicine to support immune health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Eleutherococcus japonicus
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Araliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eleutherococcus, specifically as the species Eleutherococcus japonicus.

This plant is native to a specific region within the continent of Eastern Asia. Its primary geographical distribution is concentrated in the country of Japan. Within this island nation, it can be found growing in its natural habitat. This localized range defines its natural ecological presence. Consequently, its wild occurrence is strictly tied to the Japanese landscape.
