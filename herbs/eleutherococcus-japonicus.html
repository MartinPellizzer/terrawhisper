Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Japanese eleuthero, features distinctive leaves that are central to its botanical identification. The leaves are typically palmately compound, meaning they consist of several leaflets radiating from a single central point, similar to the shape of a hand. These leaflets are generally elliptical or ovate in shape with serrated margins, providing a lush, green appearance to the shrubby stems. While various parts of the plant may be studied, the structure and arrangement of these leaves are key characteristics used by botanists to distinguish this species within the Araliaceae family.
