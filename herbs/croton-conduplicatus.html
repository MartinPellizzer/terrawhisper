Croton conduplicatus

Croton conduplicatus, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, is valued for its diverse pharmacological properties. Often utilized in traditional healing practices, this plant contains bioactive compounds believed to possess anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, making it a significant subject of interest in ethnobotanical research and studies.

  • Scientific name: Croton conduplicatus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement identifies it as part of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is categorized under the family Euphorbiaceae within the genus Croton.
