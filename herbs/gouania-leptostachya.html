Gouania leptostachya

What's the taxonomical classification of Gouania leptostachya?

Gouania leptostachya belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales. More specifically, this plant is a representative of the family Rhamnaceae, situated within the genus Gouania, and is identified by its unique species name, leptostachyya.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Gouania leptostachyya has a woody, scrambling shrub habit characterized by slender, arching branches and small, elliptic to lanceolate leaves that are typically arranged alternately along the stems. Its reproductive structures consist of small, inconspicuous flowers that emerge from the leaf axils to produce distinct, woody pods. What parts of this plant are used medicinally?

This plant Gouania leptostachya is utilized medicinally through its aerial parts, specifically the leaves and the twigs. These components are harvested from above the ground to prepare various traditional remedies. The leaves are often processed into infusions or decoctions to treat specific ailments. Similarly, the twigs are collected and used in medicinal preparations to harness their bioactive properties. Together, these parts form the primary source of therapeutic value for the species.

According to a study published by "Natural product research", four new triterpenoid saponins known as gouaniasides VII-IX (1-3) and joazeiroside C (4) were isolated from the aerial parts of Gouania leptostachyas. One known triterpenoid (5) was also isolated from these same aerial parts. The anti-inflammatory potential of compounds 1-3 was evaluated based on their ability to inhibit nitric oxide (NO) production by RAW 264.7 macrophages. All of these compounds significantly inhibited NO production by macrophages in a concentration-dependent manner at noncytotoxic concentrations.

What health conditions is this plant used for?

This plant is used for treating gastritis, gastritis lesions, inflammation, and various inflammatory symptoms. It acts as a natural remedy to soothe the stomach lining and promote healing of damaged tissues. The botanical properties help reduce the intensity of painful inflammatory symptoms experienced by patients. By addressing internal inflammation, it assists in the recovery of gastric lesions. This traditional application focuses on calming the digestive tract and restoring gastrointestinal health.

According to a study published by "Phytotherapy research : PTR", Gouania leptostachya is a traditional medicinal plant used in Thailand for treating various inflammatory symptoms. The methanol extract from its leaves and twigs (Gl-ME) was studied for its ability to attenuate gastritis lesions in mice treated with HCl/EtOH. Research showed that Gl-ME dose-dependently diminished the secretion of nitric oxide (NO) and prostaglandin E2 (PGE2) from LPS-stimulated RAW264.7 cells and peritoneal macrophages. Specifically, the extract at doses of 50 and 300 µg/mL reduced the mRNA expression of iNOS and COX-2. The study also identified resveratol as a major component that suppressed NO production and HCl/EtOH-induced gastric symptoms.
