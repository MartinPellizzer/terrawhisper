This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range primarily concentrated within the vast territories of Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. In the European portion of its habitat, it can be found across East European Russia. Its distribution extends significantly into the Siberian region, covering several distinct areas. Specifically, it grows within the Altay, Buryatiya, and Chitá regions of Siberia. Furthermore, its presence is also documented throughout the Irkutsk area, highlighting its preference for these rugged landscapes.
