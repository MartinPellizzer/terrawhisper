Saussurea parviflora

Saussurea parviflora, commonly known as the small-flowered sawtooth herb, is a rare medicinal plant native to the high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains potent bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments. Its scarcity makes it a precious botanical resource for holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Saussurea parviflora
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Saussurea, specifically as the species Saussurea parviflora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range primarily concentrated within the vast territories of Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. In the European portion of its habitat, it can be found across East European Russia. Its distribution extends significantly into the Siberian region, covering several distinct areas. Specifically, it grows within the Altay, Buryatiya, and Chitá regions of Siberia. Furthermore, its presence is also documented throughout the Irkutsk area, highlighting its preference for these rugged landscapes.
