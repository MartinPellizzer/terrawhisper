Taxus chinensis

Taxus chinensis, commonly known as the Chinese yew, is a significant evergreen conifer prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its longevity and ornamental beauty, this species contains bioactive alkaloids. These compounds are crucial in modern pharmacology, particularly for synthesizing potent anticancer agents used in advanced chemotherapy treatments worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Taxus chinensis
  • Family: Taxaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, it is a member of the Taxaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Taxus as the species Taxus chinensis.
