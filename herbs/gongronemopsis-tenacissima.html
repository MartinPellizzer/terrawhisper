This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several distinct regions of Asia. In China, its distribution is specifically concentrated within the South-Central areas. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found extensively throughout India and the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species is documented in the neighboring country of Bangladesh.
