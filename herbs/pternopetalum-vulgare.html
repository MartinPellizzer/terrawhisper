Pternopetalum vulgare

What's the taxonomical classification of Pternopetalum vulgare?

Pternopetalum vulgare belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Apiales and is a member of the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, the plant is defined by its specific genus, Pternopetalum, and its unique species designation, vulgarie.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Its floral structure includes a central ovary surrounded by multiple stamens that aid in pollination. The leaf arrangement is often alternate along the stem, providing a layered appearance to the plant's overall architecture.
