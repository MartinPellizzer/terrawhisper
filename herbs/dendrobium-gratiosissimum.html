Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found specifically within the South-Central territories. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in the state of Assam. Furthermore, it is widely distributed throughout the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, this species inhabits the countries of Laos, Myanmar, and Thailand.
