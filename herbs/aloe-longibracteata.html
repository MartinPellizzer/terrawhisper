Aloe longibracteata

Aloe longibracteata, commonly known as a unique member of the succulent family, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific arid regions. Characterized by its elongated bracts, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. It is increasingly studied for its bioactive compounds, which may provide soothing and healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Aloe longibracteata
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is a member of the family Asphodelaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified under the genus Aloe.
