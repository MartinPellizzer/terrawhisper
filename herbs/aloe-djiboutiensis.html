Aloe djiboutiensis

Aloe djiboutiensis, commonly known as the Djibouti Aloe, is a resilient succulent native to the arid landscapes of the Horn of Africa. Characterized by its fleshy, pointed leaves and striking inflorescences, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its soothing properties and potential therapeutic applications in skincare.

  • Scientific name: Aloe djiboutiensis
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asphodelaceae and the genus Aloe.

Plant Parts

This plant, Aloe djiboutiensis, is primarily characterized by its succulent leaves, which serve as the main reservoir for its medicinal properties. These fleshy leaves are thick and elongated, storing the specialized gel and latex that are frequently utilized in traditional healing practices. Because the leave structure is designed to retain moisture in arid environments, the concentrated compounds found within the leaves are highly potent for topical and therapeutic applications.
