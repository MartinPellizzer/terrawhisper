Cymbopogon winterianus

Java citronella

Cymbopogon winterianus, commonly known as West Indian lemongrass, is a perennial aromatic grass prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in citral, it serves as a potent essential oil source used in aromatherapy and traditional medicine. Its invigorating scent and antimicrobial qualities make it invaluable for relaxation and skin health.

  • Scientific name: Cymbopogon winterianus
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Poales, it is a member of the family Poaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cymbopogon.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its distribution also extends significantly into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, the species is documented to grow in India. Additionally, it is found naturally within the island nation of Sri Lanka.
