Cymbopogon winterianus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (242 studies) · ★★★★★

Java citronella, scientifically known as Cymbopogon winterianus, is a highly aromatic perennial grass prized for its potent essential oil. Rich in citronellal, it serves as a powerful natural insect repellent and antimicrobial agent. Widely cultivated in tropical regions, this versatile plant is essential in perfumery and therapeutic aromatherapy.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as citronella grass, utilizes various anatomical structures to store and produce its characteristic aromatic compounds. The most pharmacologically significant components are the essential oils, which are concentrated within the plant to provide its distinct scent and medicinal properties. These oils are primarily extracted from the long, slender leaves and the protective leaf sheath that wraps around the stem. Below the surface, the plant is supported by a robust system of roots, where the specialized root tissue serves as a vital foundation for nutrient absorption and structural stability.
