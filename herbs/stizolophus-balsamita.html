Stizolophus balsamita

Stizolophus balsamita, commonly known as sweet balm, is a fragrant medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its soothing aromatic oils, it is frequently utilized in traditional remedies to aid digestion, alleviate respiratory discomfort, and reduce stress. This versatile plant remains a cornerstone in herbal wellness practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Stizolophus balsamita
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Stizolophus as Stizolophus balsamita.
