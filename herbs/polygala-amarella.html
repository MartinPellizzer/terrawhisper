Dwarf Milkwort, scientifically known as Polygala amarella, is a charming, low-growing perennial native to European woodlands and heaths. Characterized by its delicate pink flowers and small, serrated leaves, this hardy plant is historically valued in traditional herbalism for its purported soothing properties and its role in supporting respiratory health.
