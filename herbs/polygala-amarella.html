Polygala amarella

Dwarf milkwort

Polygala amarella, commonly known as Felwort, is a perennial herb native to European meadows and woodlands. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, this plant is noted for its bitter properties. It has historically been employed to support digestive health and act as an expectorant, though it must be used cautiously.

  • Scientific name: Polygala amarella
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, specifically being a member of the family Polygalaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Polygala.
