Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it is specifically found in the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends to the island of Taiwan, representing its range within Eastern Asia. Moving into the Indo-China region, the species can be located in the country of Laos. Additionally, it is distributed throughout Thailand, further establishing its regional footprint.
