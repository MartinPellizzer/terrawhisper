Amaranthus hypochondriacus

Amaranthus hypocondriacus, commonly known as Amaranth, is a highly nutritious pseudocereal celebrated for its medicinal properties. Rich in essential amino acids, antioxidants, and minerals, it supports immune function and cardiovascular health. This versatile plant serves as both a dietary staple and a natural remedy in traditional healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Amaranthus hypochondriacus
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Caryophyllales, it is a member of the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified under the genus Amaranthus.

Common Names

This plant, Amaranthus hypocondriacus, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic region, although in all the specified Chinese dialects, it is referred to by the same name. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans, zh-cn) and Traditional Chinese (zh-hant), as well as in the regional variations for Hong Kong (zh-hk) and Singapore (zh-sg), the common name is 千穗谷.
