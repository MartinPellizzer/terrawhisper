Astragalus ponticus

Astragalus ponticus, commonly known as Pontic milkvetch, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Pontic Mountains and surrounding regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant contains bioactive compounds believed to support immune function and promote longevity, making it a significant subject of ethnobotanical and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus ponticus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Astragalus. As a member of the Astragalus genus, Astragalus ponticus represents a specific lineage within these broader botanical groups.
