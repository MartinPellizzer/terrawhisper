Adonis ramosa

Forked-stem adonis

Adonis ramosa, commonly known as a member of the Ranunculaceae family, is a medicinal perennial valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Found in specific ecological niches, this plant contains bioactive compounds that offer potential therapeutic benefits, particularly in cardiovascular health and traditional herbal medicine applications throughout its natural habitat.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, falling into the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Adonis.
