Pinus ponderosa var. ponderosa

Pinus ponderosa var. ponderosa, commonly known as the Ponderosa Pine, is a majestic coniferous species native to western North America. Renowned for its fragrant, resinous bark and versatile timber, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various extracts have been utilized in folk medicine for their antiseptic and soothing properties.

  • Scientific name: Pinus ponderosa var. ponderosa
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling under the family Pinaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Pinus as the specific variety ponderosa.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions within the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented in Great Britain and Ireland.
