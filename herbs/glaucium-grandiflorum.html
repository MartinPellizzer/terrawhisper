Glaucium grandiflorum

Glaucium grandiflorum, commonly known as the large-flowered sea holly, is a striking medicinal plant native to coastal regions. Renowned for its distinctive blue hues, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies. This hardy perennial offers significant ethnobotanical value, providing unique therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Glaucium grandiflorum
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Glaucium.

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing within the country of Greece.
