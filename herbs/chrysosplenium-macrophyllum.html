Chrysosplenium macrophyllum

What's the taxonomical classification of Chrysosplenium macrophyllum?

Chrysospeltiun macrophyllum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Saxifrages. The plant is a member of the family Saxifragaceae, which contains its specific genus, Chrysospeltiun, and its defining species, macrophyllum.
