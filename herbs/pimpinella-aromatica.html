Pimpinella aromatica, is a fragrant perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic essential oils, this medicinal plant has been traditionally used to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. Its unique chemical profile offers significant therapeutic potential in holistic wellness and natural pharmaceutical research today.
