Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. Within the Macaronesian archipelago, it can be specifically found on the island of Madeira. Its range extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Ghana, Nigeria, and Sierra Leone. Furthermore, the species reaches into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically occurring in Burundi. This wide-reaching presence highlights its adaptability across various tropical and island environments.
