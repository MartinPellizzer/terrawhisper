Senna septemtrionalis

Sennai septemtrionalis, commonly known as Northern Senna, is a significant medicinal shrub native to specific temperate regions. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, it contains bioactive anthraquinones. This plant serves as a vital natural remedy in traditional herbalism, utilized primarily to stimulate intestinal motility and support digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Senna septemtrionalis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Senna as Senna septemtrionalis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. Within the Macaronesian archipelago, it can be specifically found on the island of Madeira. Its range extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Ghana, Nigeria, and Sierra Leone. Furthermore, the species reaches into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically occurring in Burundi. This wide-reaching presence highlights its adaptability across various tropical and island environments.
