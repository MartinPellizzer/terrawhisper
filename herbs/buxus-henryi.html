What's the taxonomical classification of Buxus henryi?

Buxus henryi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized into the order Buxales and the family Buxaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Buxus, reaching its specific taxonomic designation at the species level as Buxus henryi.
