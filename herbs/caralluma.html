Caralluma

Caralluma, commonly known as a traditional Ayurvedic herb, has gained global recognition for its unique appetite-suppressing properties. Native to arid regions of India and Africa, this succulent is widely utilized in natural wellness formulations to support weight management, boost energy levels, and promote metabolic health through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Caralluma
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Caralluma.
