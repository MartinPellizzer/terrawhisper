Karmal, scientifically known as Dillenia pentagyra, is a magnificent tropical tree celebrated for its large, striking flowers and robust medicinal properties. Native to South Asia, it is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic practices to treat various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds and restorative natural qualities.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across parts of Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central regions as well as on Hainan island.
