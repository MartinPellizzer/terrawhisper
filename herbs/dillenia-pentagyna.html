Dillenia pentagyna

Dillenia pentagyna, commonly known as the Elephant Apple, is a significant medicinal plant native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned for its large, acidic fruit, it possesses potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used in folk medicine, it serves as a vital natural remedy for digestive and inflammatory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Dillenia pentagyna
  • Family: Dilleniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dilleniales, specifically within the family Dilleniaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed by its classification under the genus Dillenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across parts of Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central regions as well as on Hainan island. Its distribution extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Bangladesh. Furthermore, the species is native to the Assam region within the same subcontinent. Finally, it can also be located throughout the East Himalaya range.
