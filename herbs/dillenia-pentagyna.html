Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across parts of Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central regions as well as on Hainan island. Its distribution extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Bangladesh. Furthermore, the species is native to the Assam region within the same subcontinent.
