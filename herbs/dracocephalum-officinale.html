Garden hyssop, scientifically known as Dracocephalum officiinale, is a resilient perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic, mint-like foliage and striking purple blooms, this medicinal plant offers traditional therapeutic benefits, including digestive support and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable addition to any apothecary garden.
