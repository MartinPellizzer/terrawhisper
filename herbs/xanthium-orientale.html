Plant Parts

This plant, Xanthium orientale, commonly known as Oriental cocklebur, is characterized by its distinct reproductive structures. The most notable medicinal and botanical feature is the fruit, which develops into a woody, oblong bur covered in hooked spines. These specialized fruits are designed for dispersal by clinging to the fur of animals or the clothing of humans, and in traditional medicinal practices, various components of the plant, including the seeds within these fruits, are studied for their bioactive properties.
