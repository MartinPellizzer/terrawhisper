Erythrostemon gilliesii

Erythrostemon gilliesii, commonly known as the Gillies' Erythrostemon, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, this plant is utilized in traditional healing practices to address various ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern research into natural therapeutic compounds and treatments.

  • Scientific name: Erythrostemon gilliesii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae, it is further identified by its genus, Erythrostemon.
