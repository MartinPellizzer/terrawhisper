Common Grape Vine

Vitis vinifera · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1756 studies) · ★★★★★

Common Grape Vine, scientifically known as Vitis vinifera, is a versatile medicinal and nutritional powerhouse. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich skins and seeds, it supports cardiovascular health, combats inflammation, and promotes skin vitality. Beyond its culinary fame, this ancient vine offers significant therapeutic benefits through its potent polyphenolic compounds.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the grapevine (Vitis vinifera), offers various components that are utilized for their diverse medicinal and nutritional properties. The leaves are often used in traditional remedies to help manage blood sugar levels and reduce inflammation. The fruits, which are the grapes themselves, are rich in antioxidants that support cardiovascular health. Within these grapes, the skin serves as a concentrated source of polyphenols and resveratrol, which are vital for fighting oxidative stress. Furthermore, the seeds (or seeds extracted as oil) contain potent bioactive compounds that contribute to skin health and cellular protection.
