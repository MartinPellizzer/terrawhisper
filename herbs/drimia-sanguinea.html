Drimia sanguinea

Drimia sanguinea, commonly known as the Blood Lily, is a captivating bulbous perennial native to Southern Africa. Distinguished by its striking, deep red blooms, this resilient plant thrives in various terrains. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest due to its unique chemical properties and traditional medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Drimia sanguinea
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Drimia sanguinea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asparagaceae and is categorized under the genus Drimia.
