Stachys grandidentata

Stachys grandidentata, commonly known as Large-toothed Woundwort, is a significant medicinal herb found in various tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat wounds, skin inflammations, and digestive ailments, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Stachys grandidentata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Stachys.
