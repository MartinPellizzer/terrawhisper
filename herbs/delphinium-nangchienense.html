What's the taxonomical classification of Delphinium nangchienense?

Delphinium nangchienense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Ranunculales and is a member of the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Delphinium, and its unique species designation, nangchienense.
