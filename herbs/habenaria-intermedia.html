Habenaria intermedia

Habenaria intermedia, commonly known as the intermediate rein orchid, is a delicate perennial herb native to various tropical regions. Characterized by its elegant white blooms and specialized pollination mechanisms, this species plays a vital role in its ecosystem, offering unique insights into the complex relationships between orchids and insects.

  • Scientific name: Habenaria intermedia
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asparagales and the family Orchidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Habenaria, specifically as the species Habenaria intermedia.

Common Names

This plant, Habeanaria intermedia, is known by several common names depending on the language and region. In Chinese, it is referred to as 大花玉凤花 (zh), and specifically as 大花玉凤花 in mainland China (zh-cn). In Taiwan, the name is written as 大花玉鳯花 (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the Tibet region. Moving to the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found in the lush areas of Assam and throughout the East Himalaya. Additionally, the species inhabits the mountainous terrains of Nepal. Finally, its geographical range extends further west into Pakistan.
