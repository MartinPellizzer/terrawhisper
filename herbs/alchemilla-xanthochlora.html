Alchemilla xanthochlora

Alchemilla xanthochlora, commonly known as Yellow Lady's Mantle, is a perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its distinctive scalloped leaves and pale yellow blooms, it is frequently utilized for its astringent and anti-inflammatory properties, supporting digestive health and skin vitality through its unique phytochemical composition.

  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe
