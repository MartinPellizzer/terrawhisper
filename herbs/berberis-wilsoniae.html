Berberis wilsoniae

Berberis wilsoniae, commonly known as Wilson's barberry, is a resilient shrub prized in traditional medicine. Native to mountainous regions, it contains bioactive alkaloids like berberine. These compounds offer potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making this plant a valuable resource for treating metabolic disorders and various skin infections.

  • Scientific name: Berberis wilsoniae
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ranunculales, it is further categorized into the family Berberidaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Berberis.
