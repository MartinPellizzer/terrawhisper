This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of Eastern Asia. It can be found growing in the Russian Far East, specifically on the island of Sakhalin. Its range extends significantly into Japan, where it is a well-known native species. Additionally, the plant is documented throughout the Korean peninsula.
