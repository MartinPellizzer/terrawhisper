Cuscuta epithymum

Cuscuta epithyma, commonly known as dodder, is a parasitic plant characterized by its leafless, thread-like stems. While primarily recognized for its ability to extract nutrients from host plants, it holds significant interest in traditional medicine. Research explores its complex chemical profile for potential therapeutic applications in various pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Cuscuta epithymum
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cuscuta, specifically as the species Cuscuta epithymum.
