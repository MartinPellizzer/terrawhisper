Capparis himalayensis

Capparis himalayensis, commonly known as the Himalayan Caper, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts possess potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic potential for treating diverse ailments and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Capparis himalayensis
  • Family: Capparaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales. Finally, it is a member of the family Capparaceae, falling under the genus Capparis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the vast stretches of the Indian Subcontinent. Within this region, it is specifically documented to grow in the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Its distribution also extends into the diverse landscapes of Pakistan. Furthermore, the species is commonly located throughout the West Himalaya region. These specific locations highlight its preference for the high-altitude environments of South Asia.
