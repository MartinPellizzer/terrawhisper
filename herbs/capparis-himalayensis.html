Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the vast stretches of the Indian Subcontinent. Within this region, it is specifically documented to grow in the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Its distribution also extends into the diverse landscapes of Pakistan. Furthermore, the species is commonly located throughout the West Himalaya region. These specific locations highlight its preference for the high-altitude environments of South Asia.
