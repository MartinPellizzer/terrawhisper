Premna herbacea

Premna herbacea, commonly known as the "herbaceous premna," is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its lush green foliage, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This botanical treasure offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Premna herbacea
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Premna.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically throughout the South-Central areas as well as on Hainan island. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it grows in the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented within the borders of Bangladesh. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes the East Himalaya region.
