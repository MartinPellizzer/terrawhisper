Rumex nepalensis

Rumex nepallenis, commonly known as Himalayan dock, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its multifaceted medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat digestive ailments, skin inflammations, and respiratory issues, serving as a vital resource in mountain ethnomedicine.

  • Scientific name: Rumex nepalensis
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Rumex nepalenis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae and falls under the genus Rumex.
