Sarcomphalus mistol

Mistol

Sarcomphalus mistol, commonly known as Mistol, is a resilient medicinal plant native to South American dry forests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat digestive ailments and inflammatory conditions. This versatile species plays a vital role in both local ecosystems and traditional folk medicine.
