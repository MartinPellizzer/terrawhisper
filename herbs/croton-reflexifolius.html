Croton reflexifolius

Croton reflexifolius, commonly known as the reflexed-leaf croton, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Croton reflexifolius
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
