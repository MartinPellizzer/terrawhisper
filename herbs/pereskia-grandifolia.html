Pereskia grandifolia

Pereskia grandifolia, commonly known as the Large-leaf Pereskia, is a remarkable climbing cactus native to Mexico. Characterized by its fleshy, succulent leaves and striking pink flowers, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potential healing properties, offering unique botanical benefits within the diverse Cactaceae family.

  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Africa
