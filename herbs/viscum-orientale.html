Viscum orientale, scientifically known as Viscum orientale, is a unique parasitic shrub native to East Asia. Often referred to as oriental mistletoe, this plant thrives by extracting nutrients from host trees. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, it is studied for its bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic properties in holistic healing.

Distribution

This plant, Viscum orientale, exhibits a specific distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Western Asia, its presence is documented within the borders of Afghanistan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found across a wide geographical range. Specifically, this species is native to India, Bangladesh, and Nepal. Additionally, its distribution extends to the northeastern region of Assam.
