Viscum orientale

Viscum orientale, commonly known as the Oriental mistletoe, is a unique parasitic plant native to East Asia. Thriving on host trees, it draws nutrients and water through specialized structures. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, this species is studied for its potential bioactive compounds and its fascinating ecological role.

  • Scientific name: Viscum orientale
  • Family: Santalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Santalales, it is classified under the family Santalaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Viscum as the species Viscum orientale.
