Ferula paniculata

Ferula paniculata, commonly known as a member of the Apiaceae family, is a perennial medicinal herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ferula paniculata
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Ferula, and its species name, paniculata.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ferula paniculata, is referred to by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly called panicle fennel, while in German, it is known as Ähren-Ferula. In its native or localized regions, it may be identified as panikelfenkel in Dutch or as panikelfenkel in German as well.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within Central and East Asia. In the territory of Central Asia, it can be found growing in Kazakhstan. Its range extends eastward into the Xinjiang region of China. Additionally, the species is native to the landscapes of Mongolia. These specific locations define the natural geographical footprint of Ferula paniculata.
