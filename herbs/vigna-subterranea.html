Vigna subterranea

Vigna subterranea, commonly known as Bambara groundnut, is a resilient, protein-rich legume native to West Africa. Renowned for its ability to thrive in poor soils, this nutritious crop serves as a vital food security tool. It offers significant nutritional value and plays a crucial role in sustainable agricultural systems.

  • Scientific name: Vigna subterranea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Vigna, specifically as the species Vigna subterranea.
