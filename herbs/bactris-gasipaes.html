Bactris gasipaes

Bactris gasipaes, commonly known as peach palm, is a versatile tropical plant prized for both its nutritious fruit and medicinal properties. Used extensively in traditional medicine, it helps manage inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This multifunctional palm serves as a vital resource for food security and holistic healing.
