Scientifically known as Neopicrorhiza, is a vital medicinal herb native to the Himalayan regions. This plant offers significant therapeutic potential, specifically for supporting liver health, reducing inflammation, and enhancing immunity.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several high-altitude regions in Asia. In China, its presence is documented within the South-Central areas as well as the Tibet region. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found thriving in the East Himalaya. The plant also occupies significant territories within the West Himalaya. Finally, its geographical range extends further into the mountainous landscapes of Nepal.
