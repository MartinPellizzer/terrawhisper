Vitex cymosa

Vitex cymosa, commonly known as Chasteberry, is a perennial shrub prized in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its ability to support hormonal balance, it is frequently used to alleviate symptoms of PMS and menopause. This potent botanical offers natural therapeutic benefits for regulating reproductive health and stabilizing mood cycles.

  • Scientific name: Vitex cymosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae within the genus Vitex.
