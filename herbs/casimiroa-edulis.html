White sapote

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (334 studies) · ★★★★★

White sapote, scientifically known as Casimiroa eduilis, is a tropical fruit native to the Neotropics. Renowned for its creamy, custard-like texture and sweet flavor, this plant offers significant nutritional value. Beyond its culinary appeal, it holds promising potential in traditional medicine and pharmacological research due to its unique bioactive compounds.
