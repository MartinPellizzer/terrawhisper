Casimiroa edulis

White sapote

Casimiroa edulis, commonly known as the mountain papaya, is a perennial shrub native to the Andean cloud forests. Highly valued for its nutritious, sweet fruit, it serves as a vital dietary supplement in South American highlands. This plant offers significant medicinal potential, containing bioactive compounds with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties.

  • Scientific name: Casimiroa edulis
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it falls under the family Rutaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Casimiroa, which includes the species Casimiroa edulis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Casimiroa edulis, is recognized by various regional and linguistic names across the globe. In French-speaking regions, it is referred to as Sapote blanche, while in Russia, it is known as Белая сапота. The plant holds specific cultural and linguistic identities as well, being called Cochitzapocuahuitl in Nahuatl and Kazimira jadalna in Polish.
