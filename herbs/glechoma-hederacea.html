Glechoma hederacea

Ground ivy

Glechoma hederacea, commonly known as ground ivy, is a creeping perennial herb native to East Asia. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it is valued for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Often found in moist, shaded habitats, this resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential in treating various skin and respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Glechoma hederacea
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Glechoma.
