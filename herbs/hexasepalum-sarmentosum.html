Hexasepalum sarmentosum

Hexasepalum sarmentosum, commonly known as the creeping hexasepalum, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation and skin ailments, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Hexasepalum sarmentosum
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, specifically being a member of the Rubiaceae family. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hexasepalum as the species Hexasepalum sarmentosum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented across several distinct nations, including Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Furthermore, the species can be found inhabiting the landscapes of both Ghana and the Ivory Coast. The botanical range also extends into the territory of Benin. Collectively, these locations define the primary environmental zone where this medicinal species thrives.
