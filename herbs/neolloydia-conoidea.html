Neolloydia conoidea

Neolloydia conoidea, commonly known as the "African Mahogany" or "Kanyaka," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, its bark, leaves, and roots are traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin infections, and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Neolloydia conoidea
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is a member of the family Cactaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with the genus Neolloydia, specifically the species Neolloydia conoidea.
