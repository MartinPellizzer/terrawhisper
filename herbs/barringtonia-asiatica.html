Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key maritime regions. In East Tropical Africa, its range extends to the coastal areas of Tanzania. Its distribution also covers significant portions of the Western Indian Ocean. Specifically, it can be found within the Chagos Archipelago, Comoros, and Mauritius. Finally, the species is notably present throughout the large island of Madagascar.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Fish Poison Tree, utilizes various components for both ecological and medicinal purposes, with a particular emphasis on its seed. The seed of Barringtonia asiatica contains high concentrations of saponins, which are natural chemical compounds that can be toxic if ingested but are utilized in traditional practices for their various biological activities. While the fruit is often discussed in terms of its buoyancy in marine environments, it is the chemical potency held within the seed that serves as a primary focus for studying its pharmacological potential.
