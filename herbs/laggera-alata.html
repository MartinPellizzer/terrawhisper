Laggera alata

Laggera alata, commonly known as the "Laga-laga" plant, is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient plant offers significant pharmacological potential for developing modern natural therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Laggera alata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is specifically nested within the family Asteraceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Laggera.

Common Names
