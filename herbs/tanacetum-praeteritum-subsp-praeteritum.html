Tanacetum praeteritum subsp. praeteritum

Tanacetum praeteritum subsp. praeteritum, commonly known as a subspecies of tansy, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its distinctive foliage and yellow composite flowers, this plant contains bioactive compounds studied for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant potential in various natural therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Tanacetum praeteritum subsp. praeteritum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Tanacetum. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies praeteritum of the species Tanacetum praeteritum.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Tanacetum praeteritum subsp. praeteritum, exhibits a specific geographical range within the Middle East. Its distribution is primarily concentrated in the region of Western Asia. Specifically, it can be found growing across the territories of Lebanon and Syria.
