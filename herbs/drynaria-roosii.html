Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of East Asia. In mainland China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast areas. Its range also extends to the tropical island of Hainan. Furthermore, the species is documented in the Nansei-shoto islands of Eastern Asia. Finally, its geographical presence is confirmed in the territory of Taiwan.

Plant Parts

This plant, Drynaria roosii, is valued in traditional medicine, where various underground structures are utilized for their healing properties. The medicinal preparation often involves the use of the rhizoide or the elongated rhizomes that anchor the plant to the substrate. Specifically, the rhizoma Drynariae is frequently cited in pharmacopoeias as the primary part used for decoctions. In some botanical descriptions, the detailed morphology includes the presence of small rhizome scales that protect the growing tips. When preparing dried extracts, practitioners may utilize the dry rhizoma to ensure the stability and potency of the active compounds required for treatment.
