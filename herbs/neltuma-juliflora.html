Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct ecological zones. Its range extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Benin. Furthermore, the species is known to grow within the territory of Burkina. Finally, its presence is also recorded in the West Tropical African nation of Nigeria.
