Neltuma juliflora

Neltuma juliflora, commonly known as the Crimson Sunshade, is a rare medicinal herb prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Found primarily in high-altitude tropical forests, its vibrant petals contain unique alkaloids used to soothe inflammation and support respiratory health, making it a cornerstone of traditional botanical healing practices.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa
