Found primarily in subtropical regions, this resilient species offers potent antioxidant benefits and anti-inflammatory compounds. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct ecological zones. Within the Macaronesian region, it can be found inhabiting the Canary Islands and the Cape Verde archipelago. Its range extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Benin. Furthermore, the species is known to grow within the territory of Burkina. Finally, its presence is also recorded in the West Tropical African nation of Nigeria.
