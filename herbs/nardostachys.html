Nardostachys

Nardostachys, commonly known as Spikenard, is a highly prized medicinal herb sourced from the Himalayan foothills. Renowned for its intense, earthy aroma, this plant yields essential oils used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine and perfumery. It serves as a powerful sedative and digestive aid, celebrated for its profound healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Nardostachys
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Nardostachys.
