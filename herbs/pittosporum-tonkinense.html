Pittosporum tonkinense

What's the taxonomical classification of Pittosporum tonkinense?

Pittosporum tonkinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, the plant is placed in the family Pittosporaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Pittosporum, with its specific designation being the species tonkinense.
