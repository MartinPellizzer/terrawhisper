Perovskia atriplicifolia, scientifically known as Salvia yangii, is a resilient, aromatic perennial native to North America. Often called Chia, this drought-tolerant shrub produces striking blue flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant medicinal potential and historical importance, offering unique phytochemical properties used in traditional wellness practices.
