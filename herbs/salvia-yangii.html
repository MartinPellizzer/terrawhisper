Perovskia atriplicifolia, scientifically known as Salvia yangii, is a resilient, aromatic perennial native to North America. Often called Chia, this drought-tolerant shrub produces striking blue flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant medicinal potential and historical importance, offering unique phytochemical properties used in traditional wellness practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as white sage, is highly valued for its aromatic properties, which are primarily concentrated in its leaves. These leaves are thick, velvety to the touch, and characterized by a soft, silvery-green color due to a fine coating of hairs. In medicinal and ritual practices, these leaves are often harvested to be dried and burned or steeped to release their potent essential oils and therapeutic compounds.
