Salvia caespitosa

Salvia caespitosa, commonly known as tufted sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific rocky terrains. Characterized by its compact, clumping growth habit and aromatic foliage, this plant offers unique phytochemical properties. It is valued in ethnobotany for its potential medicinal applications and its role in supporting biodiversity.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
