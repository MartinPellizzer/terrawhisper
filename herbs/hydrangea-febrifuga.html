Chinese-quinine, scientifically known as Hydrangea febribuga, is a vital medicinal herb in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its potent antipyretic properties, it is primarily used to reduce fever and clear heat. This plant serves as a natural remedy for inflammatory conditions, offering therapeutic benefits through its unique bioactive compounds.
