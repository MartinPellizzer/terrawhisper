False elder, scientifically known as Peronema canescens, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat fever, inflammation, and skin ailments. Its leaves and bark serve as vital resources in various holistic and herbal healing practices.
