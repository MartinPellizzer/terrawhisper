This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe. Plant Parts

This plant, known as Cistus albidus, utilizes its leaf as a significant component for medicinal applications. The leaves are typically characterized by a grayish-green hue and a fine covering of hairs, which help the plant retain moisture in its native Mediterranean habitat. In herbal practices, these leaves are often harvested to extract essential oils and bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's therapeutic properties.
