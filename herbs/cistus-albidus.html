Cistus albidus

Cistus albidus, commonly known as the Grey-leaved Rockrose, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant contains potent antioxidants and essential oils. It is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support skin health and provide anti-inflammatory benefits in various remedies.

  • Scientific name: Cistus albidus
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Cistaceae and the genus Cistus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe.
