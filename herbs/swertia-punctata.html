Swertia punctata

Swertia punctata, commonly known as Swertia, is a significant medicinal herb belonging to the Gentianaceae family. Widely used in Ayurvedic medicine, this plant is celebrated for its bitter principles. It possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and hepatoprotective properties, making it a vital natural remedy for enhancing digestive health and liver function.

  • Scientific name: Swertia punctata
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Swertia punctata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
