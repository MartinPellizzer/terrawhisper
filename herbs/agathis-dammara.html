Agathis dammara

Agathis alba

Agathis dammara, commonly known as dammar, is a significant resin-producing tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic oleoresin, this medicinal plant has been used traditionally to treat respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and inflammation. Its unique chemical properties make it a valuable resource in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Agathis dammara
  • Family: Araucariaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is classified under the order Pinales. Finally, it is a member of the family Araucariaceae and falls under the genus Agathis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Agathis dammara, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Indonesia, it is widely known as Damar, while in Poland, it is called Agatis damara. In Lithuania, the plant is identified as Baltasis agatmedis, and in Russia, it is referred to as Агатис даммара. Additionally, in Chinese, it is commonly called 贝壳杉.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the tropical regions of Malesia. Specifically, its presence is well-documented across the large island of Borneo. It also flourishes within the geographical boundaries of Malaya. Furthermore, the species can be found growing in the lush landscapes of Sumatera. These distinct locations highlight its specialized ecological range within Southeast Asia.
