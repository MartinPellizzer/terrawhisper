Agathis alba, scientifically known as Agathis dammara, is a majestic coniferous tree native to the rainforests of Indonesia. Renowned for producing high-quality dammar resin, it holds significant medicinal value. This aromatic substance is traditionally used for its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties, playing a vital role in holistic wellness.
