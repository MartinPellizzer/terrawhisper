Praxelis clematidea

Praxelis clematidea, commonly known as the Silver-Leaf Creeper, is a rare medicinal herb prized in traditional ethnobotany. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this climbing plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory ailments and skin conditions. Its unique biochemical profile continues to fascinate researchers studying natural therapeutic compounds.

  • Scientific name: Praxelis clematidea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Asterales, it is further classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is categorized within the genus Praxelis.
