Primrose Dendrobium, scientifically known as Dendrobium polyanthum, is a versatile medicinal orchid native to Southeast Asia. Highly valued in traditional practices, its stems are utilized to treat respiratory issues, fever, and inflammation. Beyond its therapeutic properties, this resilient plant is celebrated for its stunning, fragrant blooms and ornamental beauty.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the South-Central provinces. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found inhabiting the lush areas of Assam. The species also thrives throughout the East Himalaya and the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Finally, its distribution extends into the West Himalaya, completing its presence across these diverse landscapes.
