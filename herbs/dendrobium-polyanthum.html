Dendrobium polyanthum

Primrose dendrobium

Dendrobium polyanthum, commonly known as the many-flowered dendrobium, is a versatile medicinal orchid native to Southeast Asia. Widely used in Traditional Chinese Medicine, its stems are prized for nourishing the stomach, relieving coughs, and clearing heat. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium polyanthum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Asparagales and is a member of the Orchidaceae family. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Dendrobium as the species Dendrobium polyanthum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the South-Central provinces. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found inhabiting the lush areas of Assam. The species also thrives throughout the East Himalaya and the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Finally, its distribution extends into the West Himalaya, completing its presence across these diverse landscapes.
