Echinophora tenuifolia

Echinophora tenuifolia, commonly known as the "small echinacea," is a perennial herb native to the dry steppes of Central Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and adaptogenic properties. This resilient plant is frequently utilized to support immune function and promote overall metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Echinophora tenuifolia
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Echinophora, specifically as the species Echinophora tenuifolia.
