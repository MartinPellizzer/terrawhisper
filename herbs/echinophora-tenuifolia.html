Echinophora tenuifolia, scientifically known as Echinophora tenuifolia, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the arid regions of Central Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is primarily valued for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and neuroprotective properties, making it a significant subject in modern pharmacological research for brain health.
