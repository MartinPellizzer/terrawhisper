Millettia erythrocalyx

Millettia erythrocalyx, commonly known as the Red-calyx Milletia, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and infections, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Millettia erythrocalyx
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Millettia, where it is known as Millettia erythrocalyx.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of Asia. In China, its presence is noted within the South-Central regions. It is also widely spread throughout the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it can be found in the countries of Cambodia, Laos, and Myanmar.
