Vitis labrusca

Vitis labrusca, commonly known as the native American grape, is a versatile climbing vine prized for its medicinal and nutritional properties. Rich in polyphenols and antioxidants, it is traditionally used to support cardiovascular health, reduce inflammation, and combat oxidative stress, making it a valuable botanical in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vitis labrusca
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Vitales and the family Vitaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Vitis, specifically the species Vitis labrusca.
