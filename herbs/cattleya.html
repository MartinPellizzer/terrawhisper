Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several tropical regions. In Central America, its presence is documented in both Costa Rica and Panamá. Its distribution extends into the Caribbean, where it can be found in Trinidad-Tobago as well as the Windward Islands. Furthermore, the species reaches into Northern South America, specifically inhabiting French Guiana. Together, these locations highlight the widespread nature of Cattleya throughout these warm, humid environments.
