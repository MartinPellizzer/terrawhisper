Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing in the Amur and Khabarovsk regions. Its presence extends further into the Russian Far East through the territories of Primorye and Sakhalin. Additionally, the species is documented within the South-Central part of China. These locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse East Asian habitats.
