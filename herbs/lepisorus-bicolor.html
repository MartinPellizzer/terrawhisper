Lepisorus bicolor

What's the taxonomical classification of Lepisorus bicolor?

Lepisorus bicolor belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it is situated under the subclass Polypodiinae and falls within the order Polypodiales. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the family Polypodiaceae, leading to its placement in the genus Lepisorus, and it is ultimately identified by its specific species name, bicolor.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lepisorus bicolor has pinnate fronds characterized by a distinct bicolor appearance where the upper surface is dark green and the underside is a lighter, pale green. The pinnae are oblong to lanceolate in shape and are arranged symmetrically along the central rachis. Small, rounded sori are located on the underside of the pinnules, often protected by a thin indusium. The stipe and rachis are typically dark or brownish, providing a sharp contrast to the lighter green foliage. These morphological traits help distinguish the species within its specific forest habitat.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant requires high humidity, consistent moisture, and dappled or indirect sunlight to thrive in a cultivation setting.

You should place it in a container with well-draining soil that retains moisture without becoming waterlogged. Because it is an epiphytic fern, providing regular misting or a pebble tray will help maintain the necessary atmospheric humidity. Avoid exposing the foliage to direct afternoon sun, as this can quickly scorch the delicate fronds.

Keeping the ambient temperature between 60 and 80 degrees Fahrenheit will ensure stable growth throughout the year.
