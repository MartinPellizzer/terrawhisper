Chinese licorice

Glycyrrhiza uralensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1276 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese licorice, scientifically known as Glycyrrhiza uralensis, is a cornerstone of traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its multifaceted therapeutic properties, this potent root is widely used to soothe inflammation, support digestive health, and harmonize various herbal formulas. Its unique bioactive compounds offer significant antioxidant and restorative benefits for wellness.
