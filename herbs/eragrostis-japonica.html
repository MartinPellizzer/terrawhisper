Eragrostis japonica

What's the taxonomical classification of Eragrostis japonica?

Eragrostis japonica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Poales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Poaceae, specifically categorized within the genus Eragrostis, and is identified by its specific species name, japonica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Eragrostis japonica has annual, tufted, or creeping culms that reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and feature compressed, somewhat flattened stems. The leaves consist of narrow, linear blades that are typically 2 to 5 millimeters wide and possess a prominent midrib. Its inflorescence is a compact, spike-like panicle containing numerous small, single-flowered spikelets arranged in a dense cluster. Each spikelet is roughly 2 to 3 millimeters long and is characterized by a smooth, shiny lemma with a pointed tip. The plant typically produces small, inconspicuous seeds that allow it to colonize disturbed wetland habitats efficiently.
