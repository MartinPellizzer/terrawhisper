Lilyturf, scientifically known as Liropis graminifolia, is a resilient perennial prized in traditional medicine. Often used in East Asian practices, it is valued for its ability to nourish the lungs and clear heat. Beyond its medicinal properties, its lush, grass-like foliage makes it a popular choice for ornamental landscaping.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Liriope.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across various regions of East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is widespread, spanning from the South-Central regions to the North-Central areas. It is also commonly found throughout the Southeast part of China. Moving further east, the species can be identified in Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Additionally, its range extends southward into the Malesia region, specifically within the Philippines.
