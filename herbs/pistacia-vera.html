The pistachio tree, scientifically known as Pistacia vera, is a versatile perennial celebrated both for its nutritious kernels and medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants, essential fatty acids, and vitamins, it offers significant health benefits, including anti-inflammatory effects and support for cardiovascular health, making it a vital botanical resource.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the pistachio, offers various components that are utilized for their unique properties. The most well-known part is the seed, which is consumed globally for its nutritional value and medicinal potential in supporting heart health. Surrounding the kernel is the hull, the fleshy outer layer that protects the developing seed. Additionally, the tree produces an oleo gum resin, a substance extracted from the bark that has historically been used in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory qualities.
