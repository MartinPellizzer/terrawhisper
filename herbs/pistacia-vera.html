Pistacia vera

Pistacia vera, commonly known as the pistachio tree, is a highly valued medicinal and nutritional plant. Beyond its culinary fame, it offers significant health benefits, including potent antioxidant properties. Used in traditional medicine to support cardiovascular health and reduce inflammation, this resilient species remains a vital botanical resource worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Pistacia vera
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Pistacia vera, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Pistacia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in both France and Spain. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, its presence extends into Southeastern Europe. Specifically, it is located within the borders of Bulgaria and Greece. Furthermore, the species is also widely distributed across Italy in Southeastern Europe.
