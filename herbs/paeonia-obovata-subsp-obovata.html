Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout the diverse landscapes of the Russian Far East. Its range extends across the Amur region, where it inhabits specific local ecosystems. It can also be located within the Khabarovsk territory and the coastal areas of Primorye. Furthermore, the species is distributed across the islands of Sakhalin and the Kuril Islands. Collectively, these regions define the natural geographical boundaries for Paeonia obovata subsp. obovata.
