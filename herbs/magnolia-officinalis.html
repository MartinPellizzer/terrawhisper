Scientific literature: Very Extensive (611 studies) · ★★★★★

Magnolia-bark, scientifically known as Magnolia officinalis, is a potent medicinal herb derived from the bark of the evergreen magnolia tree. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, it contains bioactive compounds like magnolol and honokiol, which offer powerful antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and sedative properties to support mental wellness and digestive health.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad natural range across various regions of the Chinese landscape. It is widely documented to grow throughout China South-Central, thriving in many of its diverse climates. The species also maintains a presence in China North-Central, extending its reach into the heart of the country. Furthermore, its distribution includes the lush environments of China Southeast. Finally, the plant can be found inhabiting the unique high-altitude terrains of Tibet.

Plant Parts

This plant, Magnolia officinalis, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties derived from specific anatomical components. The primary medicinal substance is the Magnolia bark, which is specifically harvested as the cortex of the tree. This botanical material is obtained by stripping the stem bark from the mature branches and trunk to extract the potent compounds necessary for treatment. Additionally, while the bark is the most common part used, the leaf can also be utilized in various applications, though the concentrated essence of the plant's healing power is most famously found within its protective outer layers.
