Magnolia officinalis

Magnolia officinalis, commonly known as Magnolia bark, is a revered medicinal plant in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its bark contains bioactive compounds like magnolol. It is widely utilized to support digestive health, reduce anxiety, and bolster the body's immune response naturally.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia officinalis
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, falling into the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Magnolia, specifically the species Magnolia officinalis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad natural range across various regions of the Chinese landscape. It is widely documented to grow throughout China South-Central, thriving in many of its diverse climates. The species also maintains a presence in China North-Central, extending its reach into the heart of the country. Furthermore, its distribution includes the lush environments of China Southeast. Finally, the plant can be found inhabiting the unique high-altitude terrains of Tibet.
