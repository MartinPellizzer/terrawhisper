Jasminum sinense

What's the taxonomical classification of Jasminum sinense?

Jasminum sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Lamiales, it is situated within the family Oleaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized under the genus Jasminum, with its specific designation being the species sinese.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Jasminum sinense has woody, climbing stems that produce opposite, ovate to lanceolate leaves with entire margins and acute apices. The foliage is typically dark green and glossy, often featuring a slightly textured surface. Its flowers are salverform and white, emerging from the leaf axils in small clusters or solitary arrangements. These blossoms possess a distinct, sweet fragrance and five to seven fused petals that form a tubular base. The plant eventually develops small, fleshy drupes that transition from green to a dark color upon ripening.
