, scientifically known as Thymus atlanticus, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the rugged coastal regions of North Africa. Distribution

This plant exhibits a very specific and limited geographical distribution within the natural world. It is native to the northern reaches of the African continent. More precisely, its presence is documented within the borders of Morocco. Because it is localized to this single region, it is considered an endemic species to the area.
