Physalis coztomatl

Physalis coztomatl, commonly known as the Mexican groundcherry, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Mesoamerica. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, it possesses significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat digestive ailments and skin irritations, this botanical treasure offers promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
