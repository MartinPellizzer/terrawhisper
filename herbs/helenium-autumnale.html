Sneezeweed, scientifically known as Helenium autumnale, is a resilient perennial native to North American wetlands. Characterized by its vibrant, daisy-like golden blooms, this plant thrives in moist soils. Historically valued for its medicinal properties, it is often utilized in traditional remedies to address respiratory ailments and various seasonal allergies.
