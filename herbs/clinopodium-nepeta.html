Clinopodium nepeta

Clinopodium nepeta, commonly known as catnip or catmint, is a versatile perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic leaves and delicate flowers, this plant offers significant medicinal value, traditionally used to soothe digestive issues, reduce anxiety, and promote restful sleep through its natural sedative properties.

  • Scientific name: Clinopodium nepeta
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, placing it in the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified under the specific genus Clinopodium.

Plant Parts

This plant, Clinopodium nepeta, derives its primary medicinal value from its essential oil, which is typically extracted from the aromatic leaves and flowering tops. This volatile oil contains a complex mixture of chemical compounds, such as pulegone and menthone, that contribute to the plant's characteristic scent and therapeutic properties. Because the essential oil is concentrated within the glandular trichomes of the foliage, it serves as the most potent part of the plant for applications involving antimicrobial, antispasmodic, or calming effects.
