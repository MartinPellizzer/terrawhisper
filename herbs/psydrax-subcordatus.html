Psydrax subcordatus

Psydrax subcordata

Psydrax subcordatus, commonly known as Heart-leafed Drax, is a rare medicinal herb prized for its unique phytochemical profile. Traditionally used in holistic practices to support cardiovascular health and reduce systemic inflammation, this botanical species offers promising therapeutic potential. Understanding its bioactive compounds is essential for modern pharmacological research and integration.

  • Scientific name: Psydrax subcordatus
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Specifically, the species Psydrax subcordatus is categorized under the genus Psydrax.

This plant, Psydrax subcordatus, is found across several specific regions within West Tropical Africa. Its geographical range includes the nation of Benin, where it grows in local ecosystems.
