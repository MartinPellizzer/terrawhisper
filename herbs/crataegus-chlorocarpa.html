Crataegus chlorocarpa

Crataegus chlorocarpa, commonly known as the green-fruited hawthorn, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional phytotherapy. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is primarily utilized to support cardiovascular health. Its bioactive compounds help regulate blood pressure and strengthen heart muscle, making it a vital botanical for circulatory wellness.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus chlorocarpa
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Europe

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the Eurasian continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Poland. Moving eastward, it can be found throughout Central European Russia as well as in East European Russia. The species also extends into the Siberian region, particularly within the Altay mountains. Finally, its range reaches into Central Asia, where it is documented in Kazakhstan.
