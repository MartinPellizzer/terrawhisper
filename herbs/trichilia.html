Trichilia

Trichilia, commonly known as Sandpaper trees or Aloeswood, belongs to the Meliaceae family. These tropical plants are highly valued for their diverse medicinal properties. Various species are utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, often leveraging their potent bioactive compounds for therapeutic healing.

  • Scientific name: Trichilia
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Trichilia.
