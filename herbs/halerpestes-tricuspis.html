Halerpestes tricuspis

What's the taxonomical classification of Halerpestes tricuspis?

Halerpestes tricuspis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant falls under the order Ranunculales. It is further categorized within the family Ranunculaceae, specifically placed in the genus Halerpestes, with the species designation tricuspis.
