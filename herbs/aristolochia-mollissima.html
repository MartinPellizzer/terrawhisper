Aristolochia mollissima

Aristolochia mollissima, commonly known as soft-leaved aristolochia, is a distinctive climbing plant native to tropical regions. Historically utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse pharmacological properties, it contains potent alkaloids. However, modern science warns of its nephrotoxicity, emphasizing the need for cautious study regarding its medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia mollissima
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Piperales, it is a member of the family Aristolochiaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Aristolochia.
