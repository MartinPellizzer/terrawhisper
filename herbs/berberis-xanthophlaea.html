Berberis xanthophlaea

What's the taxonomical classification of Berberis xanthophlaea?

Berberis xanthophlaea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales. This plant is a representative of the family Berberidaceae, specifically situated within the genus Berberis, and is identified by the unique species name xanthophlaea.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Berberis xanthophlaea has erect, deciduous shrubs characterized by slender, arching stems that are armed with sharp, needle-like spines at the nodes. These blossoms eventually give way to oblong, fleshy berries that turn a deep blue or black color when fully ripe. It thrives in the puna ecosystem, typically found at elevations ranging from 3,000 to 4,500 meters above sea level. These shrubs are often localized to rocky slopes and montane grasslands where they can tolerate harsh, cold climates.
