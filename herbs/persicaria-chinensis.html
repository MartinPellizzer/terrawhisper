Persicaria chinensis

Persicaria chinensis, commonly known as Chinese Knotweed, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, reduce swelling, and support detoxification, offering a natural approach to promoting overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria chinensis
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed under the genus Persicaria.
