Chinese knotweed, scientifically known as Persicaria chinensis, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional East Asian practices. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and fever. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for supporting overall systemic health and wellness.
