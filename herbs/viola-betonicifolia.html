Viola betonicifolia

Viola betonicifolia, commonly known as the Betonica-leaf violet, is a distinctive medicinal herb prized for its unique therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Violaceae family, this plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to support respiratory health and soothe inflammation, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Viola betonicifolia
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Violaceae and falls under the genus Viola.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans several distinct regions across Asia. In Western Asia, its presence has been documented within the borders of Afghanistan. The species is also widely distributed throughout various parts of China. Specifically, it can be found in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast regions of the country. Additionally, its habitat extends to the island province of Hainan.
