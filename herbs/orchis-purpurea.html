Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be recorded in both Denmark and Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found within the borders of Austria. It also inhabits parts of Belgium, contributing to its central European range. Finally, the distribution extends through the Czechia-Slovakia region.

Plant Parts

This plant, Orchis purpurea, is characterized by several distinct morphological features that define its structure. The flower is perhaps its most striking feature, producing complex, hooded blooms with intricate patterns of purple and brown that serve to attract specific pollinators. Its leaf arrangement typically consists of several basal leaves that are lanceolate in shape and provide the necessary surface area for photosynthesis. While the plant lacks a prominent aerial stem, it utilizes a short, fleshy shoot to support the floral spike. Beneath the soil, the plant relies on a specialized root system consisting of fleshy, tuberous organs that store nutrients and allow the orchid to persist through dormant periods.
