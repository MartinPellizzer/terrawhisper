Grewia lasiocarpa

Grewia lasiocarpa, commonly known as Phalsa, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its small, tangy berries, it offers significant health benefits, including cooling properties and antioxidant support. Traditionally used in Ayurveda, it aids digestion, manages fever, and promotes overall wellness through its nutritional richness.

  • Scientific name: Grewia lasiocarpa
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, it is further categorized into the family Malvaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Grewia.
