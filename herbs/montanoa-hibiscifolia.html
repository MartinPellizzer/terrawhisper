Tree daisy, scientifically known as Montanoa hibiscifolia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Mexico and Central America. Renowned for its large, hibiscus-like leaves, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation, digestive issues, and skin irritations, offering significant ethnobotanical importance.
