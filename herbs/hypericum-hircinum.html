Hypericum hircinum

Hypericum hircinum, commonly known as St. John's Wort, is a perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Hypericaceae family, this plant is rich in bioactive compounds like hypericin, frequently utilized to support emotional well-being, alleviate mild depression, and promote skin healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum hircinum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hypericum.
