Eucalyptus bosistoana

Eucalyptus bosistoana, commonly known as Bosisto's Eucalyptus, is a prized medicinal plant native to Australia. Renowned for its high cineole content, this aromatic species is widely used in aromatherapy and topical treatments. Its potent essential oils offer powerful antiseptic, decongestant, and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a therapeutic staple.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus bosistoana
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Myrtyle and placed in the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Eucalyptus.
