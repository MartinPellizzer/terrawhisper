Arisaema flavum

Arisaema flavum, commonly known as the yellow jack-in-the-pulpit, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Characterized by its distinctive spadix and unique spathe, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices. It is frequently utilized for its bioactive properties to treat various inflammatory and neurological conditions.
