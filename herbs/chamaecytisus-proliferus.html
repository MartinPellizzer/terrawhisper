Chamaecytisus proliferus

Chamaecytisus proliferus, commonly known as the spreading broom, is a resilient shrub valued for its unique botanical properties. Native to specific temperate regions, this plant features vibrant yellow blooms and dense foliage. In traditional herbal practices, it is studied for its bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic applications in wellness.
