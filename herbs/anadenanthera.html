Anadenanthera

Anadenanthera, commonly known as Yopo or Vilca, is a genus of tropical trees renowned for their psychoactive seeds. Historically significant to indigenous cultures in South America, these plants contain potent alkaloids used in sacred rituals. Today, they are studied closely for their profound pharmacological properties and deep ethnobotanical importance.
