Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several key regions in Africa. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Tanzania. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, it is found in Malawi. The species also inhabits various areas throughout South Tropical Africa, including Mozambique and Zambia. Finally, its geographical range extends into the nation of Zimbabwe.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cissampelos torulosa, is characterized by its climbing habit, where the stem plays a vital role in its growth and medicinal application. The stem is typically woody and twining, allowing the plant to ascend through surrounding vegetation to reach sunlight. In traditional medicine, various parts of the vine are utilized, but the robust structure of the stem serves as the primary conduit for the plant's bioactive compounds, which are often concentrated in its roots and woody tissues.
