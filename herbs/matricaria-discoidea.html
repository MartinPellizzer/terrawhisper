Matricaria discoidea

Pineappleweed

Matricaria discoidea, commonly known as tansy chamomile, is a medicinal herb distinguished by its disc-like flower heads. Renowned in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory and antispasmodic properties, it is frequently used to soothe digestive upsets and skin irritations, offering a potent, natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Matricaria discoidea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Matricaria discoidea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. It is ultimately classified under the genus Matricaria.
