Helichrysum graveolens

Helichrysum graveolens, commonly known as Everlasting or Curry Plant, is a resilient perennial herb prized for its aromatic, resinous foliage. Renowned in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, this plant is widely utilized in skincare and essential oils to promote healing and skin rejuvenation.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum graveolens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Helichrysum.
