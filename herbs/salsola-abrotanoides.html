Salsola abrotanoides belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Salsola, with its unique biological classification completed by the species designation abrotanoides.
