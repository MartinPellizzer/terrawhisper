Triangular Dutchmanspipe, scientifically known as Aristolochia triangularis, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to the southeastern United States. Characterized by its distinctive, heart-shaped leaves and unique, tubular flowers, it holds significant historical interest in traditional medicine, though modern science cautions against its use due to potential nephrotoxicity.
