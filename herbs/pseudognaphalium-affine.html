Pseudognaphalium affine

Pseudognaphalium affine, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb, belongs to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Research continues to explore its bioactive compounds, highlighting its growing importance in pharmacological studies and natural healing practices.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. Within the Caucasus, it can be found specifically in the Transcaucasus area. Additionally, the plant's presence is noted as far south as the island of Hainan.
