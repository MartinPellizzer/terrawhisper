Nepeta ciliaris

Nepeta ciliaris, commonly known as a species of catmint, is a perennial herb distinguished by its delicate, hairy foliage and aromatic leaves. Native to specific mountainous regions, this plant belongs to the Lamiaceae family, offering unique phytochemical properties that have historically interested herbalists for their calming and medicinal potential.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta ciliaris
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Nepeta.
