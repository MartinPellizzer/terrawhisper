Christ's-thorn, scientifically known as Paliurus spina-christi, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to Mediterranean and Asian regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts—including leaves, bark, and roots—offer potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This versatile plant serves as a vital natural resource for diverse therapeutic applications.
