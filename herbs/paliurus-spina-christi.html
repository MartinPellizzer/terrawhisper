Paliurus spina-christi

Paliurus spina-christi, commonly known as Christ's Thorn, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to Mediterranean and Asian regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and fruits possess potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic potential for treating various ailments and supporting overall metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Paliurus spina-christi
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Paliurus.
