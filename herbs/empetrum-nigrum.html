Empetrum nigrum

Crowberry

Empetrum nigrum, commonly known as black crowberry, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to arctic and subarctic regions. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich berries, this hardy plant holds significant medicinal value. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health and provide anti-inflammatory benefits throughout various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Empetrum nigrum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Empetrum nigrum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Empetrum.
