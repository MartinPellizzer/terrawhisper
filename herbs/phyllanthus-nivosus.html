Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found within the borders of Gambia and Guinea. Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically around the Gulf of Guinea Islands.
