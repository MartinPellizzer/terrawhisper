Plant Parts

This plant, Tetraclinis articulata, possesses several distinct anatomical features that contribute to its biological profile. The leaves are scale-like and arranged in a dense, overlapping fashion along the branches, giving the foliage a characteristic textured appearance. Within these tissues, the plant produces a fragrant essential oil that serves as a key component for various medicinal and aromatic applications. As the plant matures, it develops woody cones that facilitate reproduction, while the structural integrity of the organism is provided by its sturdy woods. The durable quality of this wood has historically made it a valuable resource for both construction and traditional therapeutic uses.
