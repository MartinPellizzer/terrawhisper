Echinops kebericho

Echinops kebericho, commonly known as "Kibecha" in Ethiopia, is a vital medicinal shrub highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and infections, playing a crucial role in the herbal pharmacopoeia of East Africa.

  • Scientific name: Echinops kebericho
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Echinops.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Echinops kebericho, possesses a very specific and limited natural range. Its geographical distribution is primarily concentrated within the Northeast Tropical Africa region. More specifically, this medicinal species is endemic to the high-altitude areas of Ethiopia. Because it is found in such a localized manner, it is closely tied to the unique ecological conditions of the Ethiopian highlands. Consequently, its availability for medicinal use is inherently linked to this specific African territory.
