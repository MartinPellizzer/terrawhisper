Bokanga, scientifically known as Amphimas pterocarpoides, is a significant medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its potent psychoactive properties, it is traditionally used in ritual practices and as a stimulant. Its roots contain unique alkaloids, making it a subject of interest in ethnobotanical and pharmacological research.
