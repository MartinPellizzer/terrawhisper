What's the taxonomical classification of Codonopsis cardiophylla?

Codonopsis cardiophylla belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Asterales. This plant is a distinguished representative of the family Campanulaceae, falling under the genus Codonopsis, with its specific designation being the species cardiophylla.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Small, tubular flowers typically appear in clusters at the top of these stems. The overall growth habit is compact and herbaceous, making it a popular choice for indoor cultivation.
