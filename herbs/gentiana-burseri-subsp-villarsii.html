Gentiana burseri subsp. villarsii

Gentiana burseri subsp. villarsii, commonly known as a specialized variant of Bursar's Gentian, is a rare medicinal herb native to specific montane habitats. Renowned for its potent bitter principles, it has historically been utilized in traditional herbalism to support digestive health and stimulate various metabolic functions within the body.
