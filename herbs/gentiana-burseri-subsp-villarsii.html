Gentiana burseri villarsii, scientifically known as Gentiana burseri subsp. villarsii, is a rare, perennial herb native to specific high-altitude habitats. This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant, Gentiana burseri subsp. villarsii, exhibits a specific and limited geographical range within the European continent. Its distribution is primarily concentrated in the southern regions of Europe. In the western part of its range, it can be found growing within the borders of France. Moving toward the east, the species is also documented across Southeastern Europe. Specifically, this subspecies is located within the territory of Italy.
