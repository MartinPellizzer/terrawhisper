Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central areas as well as in Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it inhabits the East Himalaya region. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Nepal. Finally, its geographical range includes parts of Pakistan.
