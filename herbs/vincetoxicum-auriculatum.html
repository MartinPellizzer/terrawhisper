Vincetoxicum auriculatum

Vincetoxicum auriculatum, commonly known as the creeping vincetoxicum, is a perennial climbing herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic applications, particularly in managing inflammatory conditions and supporting diverse metabolic health processes.

  • Scientific name: Vincetoxicum auriculatum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianaales and is a member of the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Vincetoxicum, specifically as the species Vincetoxicum auriculatum.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central areas as well as in Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it inhabits the East Himalaya region. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Nepal. Finally, its geographical range includes parts of Pakistan.
