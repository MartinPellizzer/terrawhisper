Vicia

Vicia, commonly known as vetch, is a versatile genus of herbaceous plants prized for their medicinal and nutritional properties. Often used in traditional herbalism to support digestive health and skin vitality, these legumes also enrich soil quality, playing a vital role in ecological wellness and sustainable agricultural practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Vicia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Vicia.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the regions of Northern Europe.
