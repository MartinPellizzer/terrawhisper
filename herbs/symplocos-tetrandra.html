Symplocos tetrandra

Symplocos tetrandra, commonly known as the Yellow Soapwood, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South Asia. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its bark and leaves are traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic practices to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Symplocos tetrandra
  • Family: Symplocaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is a member of the family Symplocaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its classification under the genus Symplocos.
