Eupatorium perfoliatum

Boneset

Eupatorium perfoliatum, commonly known as Boneset, is a perennial herb renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Historically utilized to treat fevers and inflammation, this plant features unique, clasping leaves. Today, it remains a significant botanical remedy, valued in traditional herbalism for supporting immune function and easing systemic discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Eupatorium perfoliatum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Eupatorium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several provinces in Canada. In Western Canada, its presence is noted within the province of Manitoba. Moving to the eastern part of the country, it can be found in Ontario. The species also extends into the Atlantic provinces, specifically occupying New Brunswick and Nova Scotia. Additionally, its range includes the island province of Prince Edward Island.
