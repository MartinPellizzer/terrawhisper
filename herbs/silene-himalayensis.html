Silene himalayensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Silene himalayensis?

Silene himalayensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales. This plant is a member of the family Caryophyllaceae, specifically falling within the genus Silene, and is identified by the species name himalayensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Silene himalayensis has erect stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters, characterized by opposite leaves and a calyx featuring long, prominent teeth. The leaves are generally lanceolate to linear in shape and are arranged in opposing pairs along the length of the stem. Its flowers are produced in terminal cymes and possess five petals that are often white or pale pink in color. The calyx tube is distinctively inflated and often displays fine glandular hairs on its surface. These morphological traits allow the plant to thrive within its specific high-altitude montane environments.
