Dianthus superbus

Fringed pink

Dianthus superbus, commonly known as the superb pink, is a stunning perennial cherished for its vibrant, fringed blooms and elegant foliage. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant place in traditional herbalism, valued for its restorative properties and ability to support overall wellness through its unique botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Dianthus superbus
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Caryophyllaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dianthus.
