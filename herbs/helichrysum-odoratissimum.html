Kooigoed Everlasting, scientifically known as Helichrysum odoratissimum, is a resilient perennial herb native to South Africa. Renowned for its distinctively fragrant, golden blooms, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional healing. It possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, often used to support respiratory health and skin vitality.
