Helichrysum odoratissimum

Helichrysum odoratissimum, commonly known as the Everlasting Flower, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its intense, curry-like aroma, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties, offering diverse therapeutic benefits for skin and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum odoratissimum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Helichrysum as Helichrysum odoratisissimum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across multiple continents and diverse climates. In Northern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within Great Britain. The species also thrives in West Tropical Africa, with documented occurrences in Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found in Cameroon, the Gulf of Guinea Islands, and the DR Congo. Together, these locations highlight the wide range of environments where the plant is naturally situated.
