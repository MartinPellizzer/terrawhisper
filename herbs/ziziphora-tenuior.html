Ziziphora tenuior, scientifically known as Ziziphora tenuior, is a fragrant perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this medicinal plant is widely used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin irritations, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential through its bioactive compounds.
