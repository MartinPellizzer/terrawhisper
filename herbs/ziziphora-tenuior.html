Ziziphora tenuior

Ziziphora tenuior, commonly known as narrow-leaved ziziphora, is a fragrant perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Native to arid regions, this medicinal plant is prized for its essential oils. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties to treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphora tenuior
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It further falls into the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, Ziziphora tenuior is a member of the genus Ziziphora.
