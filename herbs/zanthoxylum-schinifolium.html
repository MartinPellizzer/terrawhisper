Zanthoxylum schinifolium

Zanthoxylum schinifolium, commonly known as "Pau-ferro," is a medicinal tree native to Brazil. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, its bark is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and various inflammatory conditions, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum schinifolium
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Zanthoxylum.
