Rhizanthes

Rhizanthes, commonly known as the underground orchid, is one of nature's most enigmatic botanical wonders. Found exclusively in Western Australia, this rare species lives its entire life cycle beneath the soil. Lacking chlorophyll, it relies on a symbiotic relationship with fungi to survive in its dark, subterranean habitat.

  • Family: Rafflesiaceae
