Pilocarpus racemosus, scientifically known as Pilocarpus racemosus, is a medicinal shrub native to South America. Renowned for its potent alkaloid, pilocarpine, this plant serves as a vital pharmaceutical source. It is primarily utilized in treating glaucoma and managing xerostomia by stimulating salivary and sweat gland secretions effectively.
