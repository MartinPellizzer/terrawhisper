Geranium sanguineum

Geranium sanguineum, commonly known as Bloody Cranesbill, is a resilient perennial prized for its striking, deep-red blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal herb has been traditionally used for its anti-inflammatory and astringent properties. It serves as a vital component in herbal remedies aimed at supporting skin health.

  • Scientific name: Geranium sanguineum
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae, it is classified under the genus Geranium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Geranium sanguineum, is recognized by various regional names across Europe. In Poland, it is referred to as bodziszek czerwony, while in Hungary, its common name is piros gólyaorr. Moving to the northern regions, it is called verikurjenpolvi in Finnish and verev kurereha in Estonian. Finally, in German-speaking areas, the species is known as Blutroter Storchschnabel.
