Plant Parts

This plant, known as Picea smithiana or the Smithian spruce, possesses various components that are utilized in traditional medicinal practices, most notably its knot wood. The knot wood, which develops at the junctions where branches meet the main trunk, is often collected and processed for its concentrated resinous properties. In certain herbal applications, these dense, woody sections are valued for their unique chemical compositions, which are believed to contribute to the plant's overall therapeutic potential.
