Trigonella foenum-graecum

Fenugreek

Trigonella foenum-graecum, commonly known as fenugreek, is a versatile medicinal herb prized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and bioactive compounds, it is widely used to support digestive health, regulate blood sugar levels, and boost lactation. This ancient botanical remains a cornerstone in traditional and modern wellness.

  • Scientific name: Trigonella foenum-graecum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Trigonella foenum-graecum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Trigonella.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as fenugreek, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components serve different purposes. The small, hard seeds are widely used in traditional medicine to regulate blood sugar and improve digestion. The fresh leaf is often consumed to add nutritional value to meals, while the dried leaves are utilized in various herbal preparations. In many culinary traditions, the plant is treated as a vegetable when harvested young, and the single seed represents the fundamental unit of its potent pharmacological properties.
