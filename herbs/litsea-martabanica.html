Litsea martabanica

Litsea martabanica, commonly known as Martaban Laurel, is a versatile evergreen tree native to Southeast Asian tropical forests. Valued in traditional medicine, its aromatic leaves and bark possess significant bioactive properties. These components are frequently utilized for their antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant effects in various holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Litsea martabanica
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification falls under the genus Litsea.
