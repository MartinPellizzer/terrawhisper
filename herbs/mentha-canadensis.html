Mentha canadensis

Mentha canadensis, commonly known as wild mint, is a versatile perennial herb cherished for its refreshing aroma and medicinal properties. Native to North America, this aromatic plant is rich in essential oils, offering soothing relief for digestive issues, respiratory congestion, and skin irritations through its potent, natural healing capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Mentha canadensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Mentha.
