Poison Squill, scientifically known as Drimia, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Hyacinthaceae family. Renowned for its high concentration of cardiac glycosides, it has historically been utilized in pharmacology to treat heart conditions. However, its extreme toxicity requires precise handling and strict dosage to avoid fatal consequences.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution concentrated within the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing across several key island territories, including the Baleares, Corse, and Sardegna. Additionally, its habitat extends to the mainland areas of the Iberian Peninsula. Specifically, the species is documented in both Portugal and Spain. Consequently, its geographical range is largely defined by these coastal and island environments.
