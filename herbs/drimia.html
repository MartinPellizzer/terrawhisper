Drimia

Drimia, commonly known as a genus of bulbous plants, holds significant interest in botanical and medicinal studies. Native to various regions, these resilient perennials are valued for their unique chemical compositions. Exploring their bioactive properties offers potential insights into traditional healing practices and modern pharmacological advancements in plant-based medicine.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified in the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Drimia.
