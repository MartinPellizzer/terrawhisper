Achyrantheres sessilis, scientifically known as Alternanthera sessilis, is a creeping perennial herb widely recognized in traditional medicine. Found in tropical regions, this versatile plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Rakta Rakti, is a creeping herb where almost every component holds therapeutic potential. While the whole plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicinal preparations, most treatments focus on the aerial parts which grow above the soil surface. The leaves are typically small, ovate, and serve as a vital part of the plant's medicinal profile, while the succulent stem provides the structural framework for its spreading growth habit.
