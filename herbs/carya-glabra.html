Carya glabra, scientifically known as Carya glabra, is commonly referred to as the Mock Hickory. Native to North America, this deciduous tree is valued for its resilient wood and distinctive bark. While often mistaken for true hickories, it holds significant ecological importance and potential interest in traditional botanical studies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide and diverse geographical distribution across several continents. In North America, it can be found throughout the North-Central United States, specifically within the states of Illinois and Iowa. Its range extends much further north into Eastern Canada, where it is documented in Ontario. Beyond the Americas, the species is also located within the Transcaucasus region of the Caucasus. Additionally, its presence is noted in Middle Europe, specifically within Germany.
