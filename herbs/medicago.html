Medicago

Medick

Medicago, commonly known as medic, is a versatile genus of legumes valued for their diverse medicinal and nutritional properties. Renowned in traditional practices, various species offer antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and restorative benefits. These resilient plants play a vital role in supporting holistic wellness through their unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Medicago
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and specifically within the genus Medicago.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern focused primarily within the Northern European region. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Its presence also extends to the northern territories of Finland and the rugged landscapes of Iceland. Furthermore, the species is documented across the islands of Føroyaar. Finally, the geographical range of Medicago includes the various regions of Great Britain.
