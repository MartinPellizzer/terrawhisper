Lonicera crassifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Lonicera crassifolia?

Lonicera crassifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic progression continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Dipsacales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Caprifoliaceae, specifically situated within the genus Lonicera, and is identified as the species crassifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lonicera crassifolia has opposite, simple, elliptical to oblong leaves that measure approximately 2 to 5 centimeters in length and possess entire margins with a thick, leathery texture. This deciduous shrub typically reaches heights of 1 to 2 meters and features arching branches with a somewhat woody structure. The foliage often displays a dark green color on the upper surface, while the undersides may appear slightly paler. During the flowering season, small, tubular, yellowish-white flowers emerge from the leaf axils. These blossoms eventually develop into small, fleshy, oblong berries that transition from green to a dark purple or black color upon ripening.
