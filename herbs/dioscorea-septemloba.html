Dioscorea septemloba

Dioscorea septemloba, commonly known as the seven-lobed yam, is a medicinal climbing plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its unique botanical structure, this species is valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds, which are often explored for their potential therapeutic properties in treating diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea septemloba
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Dioscoreales and the family Dioscoreaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Dioscorea.
