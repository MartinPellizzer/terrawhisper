Bulbophyllum retusiusculum

Bulbophyllum retusiusculum, commonly known as a unique member of the orchid family, is a specialized plant prized for its distinct morphological features. While primarily studied for its botanical rarity, its potential medicinal properties offer intriguing possibilities for ethnobotanical research, highlighting the diverse healing capabilities found within tropical ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Bulbophyllum retusiusculum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, it is identified by its genus, Bulbophyllum, and its species name, Bulbophyllum retusiusculum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Bulbophyllum retusiusculum, is referred to by several different common names depending on the regional language and specific classification. In Simplified Chinese, it is frequently called 藓叶卷瓣兰 (zh-hans, zh-cn, zh), while in Traditional Chinese, it is identified as 黃萼捲瓣蘭 (zh-tw). Additionally, the name 黄萼卷瓣兰 (zh) is also utilized to describe this species.

Distribution
