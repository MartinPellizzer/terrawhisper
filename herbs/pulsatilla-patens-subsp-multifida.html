Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated across parts of Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. In Eastern Europe, its presence is documented within the vast territories of East European Russia. Moving into the Siberian region, the species can be found throughout the Altay mountain area. It also inhabits significant portions of Siberia, including the regions of Buryatiya and Chitа. Finally, its range extends further across the Siberian landscape into the Irkutsk region.
