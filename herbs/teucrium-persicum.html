Teucrium persicum

Teucrium persicum, commonly known as Persian Germander, is a versatile aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean and Middle East. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and digestive issues. Its fragrant foliage and healing potential make it a significant botanical specimen.
