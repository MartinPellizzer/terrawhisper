Huperzia compacta

Huperzia compacta, commonly known as a species of firmoss, is a primitive vascular plant valued in ethnobotany for its bioactive properties. Primarily recognized for containing huperzine A, it serves as a significant natural source of acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, which are researched extensively for their potential cognitive-enhancing and neuroprotective medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia compacta
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Lycopodiidae and the order Lycopodiales, falling into the family Lycopodiaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Huperzia.
