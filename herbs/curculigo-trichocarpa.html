Curculigo trichocarpa

Curculigo trichocarpa, commonly known as the hairy-fruited curculigo, is a medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is valued for its bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and inflammation, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Curculigo trichocarpa
  • Family: Hypoxidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Hypoxidaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Curculigo.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast landscapes of the Indian Subcontinent. Within this region, it can be found growing in various parts of India. Its presence is also specifically noted in the northeastern state of Assam. Beyond the mainland, the species extends its range to the island nation of Sri Lanka. These diverse locations highlight the plant's significant ecological footprint within South Asia.
