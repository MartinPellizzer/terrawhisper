Dianthus sylvestris subsp. aristidis

Dianthus sylvestris aristidis

Dianthus sylvestris subsp. aristidis, commonly known as the wild pink, is a resilient perennial cherished for its delicate, fringed blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this subspecies holds traditional significance in herbal medicine, valued for its potential therapeutic properties and its ability to thrive in diverse, natural ecosystems.

  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Caryophyllaceae and falls under the genus Dianthus.
