Hesperis matronalis

Dame's-violet

Hesperis matronalis, commonly known as Dame's Rocket, is a fragrant perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Known for its striking violet blooms, this plant offers various therapeutic potential. While widely used in landscaping, it is studied for its unique phytochemical properties and its historical role in folk medicine practices.

  • Scientific name: Hesperis matronalis
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae, it is further classified under the genus Hesperis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Hesperis matronalis, is recognized by various colorful names across different languages and regions. In Japan, it is referred to as ハナダイコン (hanadaikon), while in Bulgaria, it is called вечерница (vechernitsa). French speakers know it as julienne des dames, and in Lithuania, it carries the name Vakarutė. Finally, in the Netherlands, it is commonly called the damastbloem.
