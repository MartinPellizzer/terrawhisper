Brucea antidysenterica, scientifically known as Brucea antidysenterica, is a medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its seeds contain potent bioactive compounds used primarily to treat dysentery, amoebiasis, and intestinal infections. This powerful botanical offers significant pharmacological potential for developing modern anti-parasitic treatments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several regions within the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Guinea and Nigeria. Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Burundi and the Central African Republic. Additionally, the species is known to inhabit the country of Cameroon.
