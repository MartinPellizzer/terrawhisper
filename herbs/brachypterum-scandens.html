Brachypterum scandens

Brachypterum scandens, commonly known as a climbing medicinal vine, is a versatile plant valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin conditions. Understanding its phytochemical profile is essential for exploring its significant pharmacological potential.
