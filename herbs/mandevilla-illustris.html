Mandevilla illustris

Mandevillea illustris, commonly known as the Brilliant Mandevilla, is a striking ornamental climber prized for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms. While primarily cultivated for its aesthetic beauty in tropical landscapes, its lush foliage and resilient nature make it a fascinating subject in ethnobotanical studies regarding traditional medicinal applications and herbal properties.

  • Scientific name: Mandevilla illustris
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant, Mandevilla illustris, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apocynaceae and is situated within the genus Mandevilla.
