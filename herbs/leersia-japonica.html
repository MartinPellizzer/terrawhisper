What's the taxonomical classification of Leersia japonica?

Leersia japonica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic descent, it falls under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Leersia and the specific species epithet japonica.
