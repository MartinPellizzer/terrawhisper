Acrocomia aculeata

Grugru palm

Acrocomia aculeaata, commonly known as the Macaw palm, is a versatile tropical plant valued for its medicinal and nutritional properties. Rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments and inflammation. Beyond medicine, its fruit and oil provide vital sustenance for many communities.

  • Scientific name: Acrocomia aculeata
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Arecales and the family Arecaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Acrocomia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Acrocomia aculeata, is recognized by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In the Netherlands, it is referred to as the Coyolpalm, while in Brazil, it is commonly called Coco-de-espinho. German speakers identify it as the Macauba-Palme, and in Haiti, it is known as Kòwòs. Additionally, in Traditional Chinese, it is called 格魯椰子.
