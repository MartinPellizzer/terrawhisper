Indian Belladonna, scientifically known as Atropa acuminata, is a perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Characterized by its toxic alkaloids, particularly atropine, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. While potent and dangerous if mishandled, its chemical compounds are essential in pharmacology for treating various neurological conditions.
