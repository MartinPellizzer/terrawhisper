Swertia angustifolia

Swertiia angustifolia, commonly known as Swertia or Kurchi, is a perennial herb highly valued in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its intense bitter properties, it serves as a potent therapeutic agent for digestive disorders, liver ailments, and fever. This botanical powerhouse plays a vital role in traditional holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Swertia angustifolia
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Gentianaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Swertia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence also extends significantly into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it is documented to grow in the Assam region and throughout the East Himalaya. Together, these locations highlight the diverse habitats where the species thrives.
