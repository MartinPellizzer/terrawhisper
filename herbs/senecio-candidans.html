Senecio candidans

Senecio candidans, commonly known as the White Groundsel, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific high-altitude regions. Characterized by its striking silvery-white foliage and delicate flowering heads, this plant holds significant botanical interest. It is frequently studied for its unique chemical properties and potential medicinal applications in traditional practices.

  • Scientific name: Senecio candidans
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Senecio.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the southern regions of South America. Its presence is notable in the southern reaches of Argentina, where it thrives in specific habitats. In Chile, the species can be found spanning across both the central and southern territories. Beyond the mainland, the plant also extends its range to the Subantarctic Islands. Specifically, it inhabits the Falkland Islands, completing its unique geographical footprint.
