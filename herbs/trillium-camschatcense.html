Trillium camschatcense

Trillium camschatense, commonly known as the Kamchatka trillium, is a striking perennial herb native to North American forests. Renowned for its elegant white blooms and lush green foliage, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in various cultures for its unique botanical properties and its role in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Trillium camschatcense
  • Family: Melanthiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Liliales and the family Melanthiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Trillium.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of the Russian Far East. Within this region, it can be found growing in the rugged terrain of Kamchatka. Its presence extends to the Khabarovsk area, where environmental conditions support its growth. Additionally, the species inhabits the Kuril Islands and the coastal reaches of Sakhalin. Finally, its geographical range includes the Primorye region, completing its spread across these northern territories.
