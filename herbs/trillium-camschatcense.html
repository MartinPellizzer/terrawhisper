What's the taxonomical classification of Trillium camschatcense?

Trillium camschatense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Liliales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Melanthiaceae, placing it within the genus Trillium, and it is specifically identified as the species camschatense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Trillium camschatense has three large, ovate to elliptical leaves that emerge from a single stem and three white, petaloid tepals that form a single whorl of flowers. These leaves are typically arranged in a single whorl at the top of the stem, creating a distinct three-part symmetry characteristic of its genus. The flowers bloom in the spring and feature upright pedicels that support the delicate blossoms. Each flower consists of six tepals that may vary slightly in shape but generally maintain a consistent white coloration.
