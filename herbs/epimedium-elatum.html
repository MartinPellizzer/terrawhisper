Epimedium elatum, scientifically known as Epimedium elatum, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Often called "horny goat weed," it is native to East Asia. Renowned for its bioactive flavonoids, particularly icariin, this plant is widely utilized to support vitality, hormonal balance, and overall reproductive health.
