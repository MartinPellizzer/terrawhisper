Epimedium elatum

Epimedium elatum, commonly known as Horny Goat Weed, is a perennial herb prized in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its bioactive flavonoids, particularly icariin, it is frequently used to support vitality, reproductive health, and bone density. This versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential for modern wellness and hormonal balance.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium elatum
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
