Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several specific regions within China. It can be found growing in the areas of China South-Central and China North-Central. Furthermore, its habitat extends to the tropical environment of Hainan. The species is also present in the lush landscapes of China Southeast.
