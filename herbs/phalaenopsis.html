Phalaenopsis

Phalaenopsis, commonly known as the moth orchid, is a captivating genus celebrated for its elegant, long-lasting blooms. Beyond its popularity in ornamental horticulture, various species hold traditional significance in herbal practices. This introduction explores its unique botanical characteristics and its emerging role within the diverse world of medicinal plants.

  • Scientific name: Phalaenopsis
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Phalaenopsis.
