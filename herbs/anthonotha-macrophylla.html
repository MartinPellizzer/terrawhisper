Anthonotha macrophylla

Anthonotha macrophylla, commonly known as the Large-leaf Anthonotha, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized in traditional herbal practices. Characterized by its expansive, vibrant foliage, this species contains unique bioactive compounds. It is primarily utilized for its potent healing properties, offering therapeutic potential in treating various inflammatory and systemic conditions.

  • Scientific name: Anthonotha macrophylla
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
