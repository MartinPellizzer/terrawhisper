Aeschynanthus mimetes belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Following the hierarchical order, it falls under the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Gesneriaceae. Specifically, this plant is identified by its genus, Aeschynanthus, and its unique species designation, mimetes.
