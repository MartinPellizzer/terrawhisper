Cinnamomum kotoense

Cinnamomum kotoense, commonly known as Koto Cinnamon, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its potent aromatic properties, it is utilized in traditional wellness practices. This species offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly through its bioactive compounds, which exhibit valuable antioxidant and antimicrobial characteristics.

  • Scientific name: Cinnamomum kotoense
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae, it is classified under the genus Cinnamomum.

This plant is characterized by a very specific and limited geographical range. It is found exclusively within the region of Eastern Asia.
