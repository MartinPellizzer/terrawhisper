Cimicifuga yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Cimicifuga yunnanensis?

Cimicifuga yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales. This species is a representative of the family Ranunculaceae, specifically situated within the genus Cimicifuga, and is identified by its unique specific epithet, yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cimicifuga yunnanensis has a perennial growth habit characterized by upright, rhizomatous stems that reach heights typically between 30 and 80 centimeters. The leaves are primarily basal and heart-shaped with serrated margins, while the cauline leaves are much smaller and distributed along the stem. During its flowering period, the plant produces dense, terminal racemes composed of numerous small, white to creamy-colored flowers. These blossoms emerge from the top of the stem to create a striking plume-like appearance. The overall structure is designed to support heavy floral displays amidst its native montane forest habitats.
