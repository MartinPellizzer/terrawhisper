Laggera

Laggera, commonly known as the "Tree of Life" in certain traditional practices, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its diverse healing properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory capabilities, this resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential in treating skin ailments and promoting overall wellness through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Laggera
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Laggera.

Distribution

Moving to West Tropical Africa, the species can be found across several different nations. Its range includes the territories of Benin, Burkina, and the Gambia. Additionally, the plant is documented to grow within the borders of Ghana.
