Finger-leaf morning-glory, scientifically known as Ipomoea digitata, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its deeply lobed leaves, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin inflammations, digestive issues, and respiratory discomfort, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.
