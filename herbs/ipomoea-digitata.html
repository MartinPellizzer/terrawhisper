Ipomoea digitata

Ipomoea digitata, commonly known as the finger-leaf morning glory, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, reduce inflammation, and manage digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea digitata
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales, falling into the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Ipomoea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide and diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the Americas. Within the Caribbean, it can be found growing in both the Dominican Republic and Haiti. Moving toward the mainland, its range extends into Northern South America, specifically in Guyana. The distribution further reaches deep into the continent, spanning as far as Paraguay in Southern South America. Together, these locations highlight the significant presence of Ipomoea digitata throughout the tropical and subtropical zones.
