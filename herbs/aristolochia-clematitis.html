Birthwort, scientifically known as Aristolochia clematitis, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to Europe and parts of Asia. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, it contains aristolochic acid, a potent compound now recognized for its significant toxicity and potential carcinogenic properties.
