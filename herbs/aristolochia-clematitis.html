Aristolochia clematitis

Aristolochia clematifis, commonly known as birthwort, is a perennial climbing plant native to Europe and parts of Asia. Historically utilized in folk medicine for various ailments, its use has drastically declined due to the presence of toxic aristolochic acids, which pose severe risks to human kidney and cellular health.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia clematitis
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, placing it in the family Aristolochiaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Aristolochia.
