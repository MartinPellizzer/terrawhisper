What's the taxonomical classification of Eragrostis nevinii?

Eragrostis nevinii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Poales. The plant is further categorized into the family Poaceae, which defines its botanical characteristics, and is specifically identified under the genus Eragrostis. Ultimately, its precise scientific designation is completed by its species name, nevinii.
