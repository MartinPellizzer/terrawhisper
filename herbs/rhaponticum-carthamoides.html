Distribution

This plant, Rhaponoticum carthamoides, is primarily distributed across various regions within the vast territory of Siberia. Its occurrence can be found in the mountainous landscapes of the Altai region. Additionally, the species is documented throughout the Republic of Buryatia. It further extends its range into the Irkutsk region, where specific environmental conditions support its growth.
