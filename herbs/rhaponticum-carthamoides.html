Plant Parts

This plant, Rhaponcium carthamoides, is a perennial herb where the primary medicinal value is concentrated in its underground structures. The roots and thick, creeping rhizomes serve as the main storage organs for the plant's bioactive compounds. When preparing traditional or clinical remedies, the root is typically harvested to obtain high concentrations of adaptogenic substances. Specifically, the medicinal efficacy is often derived from processed root extracts, which are used to standardize dosages in herbal supplements. In certain botanical contexts or regional variations, discussions may even extend to the specific qualities of the maral root, highlighting the importance of these subterranean parts in the plant's therapeutic profile.
