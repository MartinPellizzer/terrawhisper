What's the taxonomical classification of Cenocentrum tonkinense?

Cenocentrum tonkinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, the species is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It falls under the order Malvales and is a member of the family Malvaceae. Within this botanical framework, it is specifically categorized under the genus Cenocentrum, with the unique designation of the species tonkinense.
