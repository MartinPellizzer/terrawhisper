Filipendula ulmaria picbaueri, scientifically known as Filipendula ulmaria subsp. picbaueri, is a specialized subspecies of Meadowsweet. Renowned for its fragrant blooms, this botanical variety contains natural salicylates. Historically utilized in traditional herbal medicine, it offers potent anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, making it a significant subject for ethnobotanical study.
