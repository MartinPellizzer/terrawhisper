Iodes cirrhosa

Iodes cirrhosa, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, possesses remarkable therapeutic properties valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory capabilities, this plant offers significant potential in treating various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its diverse pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Iodes cirrhosa
  • Family: Icacinaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Icacinales, it is a member of the Icacinaceae family. Finally, it is classified under the genus Iodes, with the specific species name cirrhosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In China, its presence is noted within the South-Central and Southeast territories. The Indian Subcontinent also hosts the species, specifically within the Assam region and the East Himalaya. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the Indo-China area, encompassing the Andaman Islands. These various locations highlight the wide environmental adaptability of the species.
