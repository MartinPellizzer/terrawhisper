Nauclea latifolia

Nauclea latifolia, commonly known as the African apricot, is a versatile medicinal tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as malaria, inflammation, and fever, offering significant potential for modern therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Nauclea latifolia
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Nauclea, which includes the species Nauclea latifolia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Nauclea latifolia, is referred to by various common names across different languages and regions. In Japanese, it is called ナウクレア・ラティフォリア, while in Swedish, it is identified as Sarcocephalus latifolius. In Chinese, the plant is known as 幾內亞桃, and in the Hausa language, it is called Igiya. Additionally, in the Dagari language, it is commonly known as Guluŋguŋ.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its presence can be documented in the country of Benin, where it plays a role in the local ecosystem. It is also found within the borders of Burkina, contributing to the region's botanical diversity. Furthermore, the species extends its range into Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical reach includes the coastal and inland territories of Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, Nauclea latifolia, offers various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties in traditional medicine. The roots and the individual root systems are often decocted to treat ailments such as fever or inflammation. Additionally, the stem bark and the root bark are highly valued for their bioactive compounds, frequently being processed into extracts to address diverse health concerns. Furthermore, the leaves are commonly employed in medicinal preparations, showcasing the versatile utility of different plant parts in treating various conditions.
