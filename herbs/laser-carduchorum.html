Laser carduchorum

Laser carduchorum, commonly known as laser, is a potent medicinal plant revered in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its aromatic resin, it serves as a powerful digestive aid and anti-inflammatory agent. This versatile herb is frequently utilized to alleviate gastrointestinal distress and support overall metabolic health through its bioactive compounds.
