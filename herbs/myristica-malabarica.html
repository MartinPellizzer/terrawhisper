Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Malabar nutmeg, is valued for its medicinal properties, which are primarily derived from its fruit. The fruit is a fleshy, globose structure that contains a single seed, which is the most potent part used in traditional medicine. Within this fruit, the seed is enclosed in a fragrant, oily aril, both of which are utilized for their carminative and digestive benefits.
