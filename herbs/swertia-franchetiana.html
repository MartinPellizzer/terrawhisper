Swertia franchetiana

Swertiia franchetiaana, commonly known as Swertia or Chirata, is a potent medicinal herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, this bitter perennial plant possesses remarkable therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory, digestive, and antipyretic effects. It is widely utilized to treat fevers, jaundice, and various metabolic disorders.

  • Scientific name: Swertia franchetiana
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Swertia, specifically as the species Swertia franchetiana.
