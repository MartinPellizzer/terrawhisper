Callicarpa yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Callicarpa yunnanensis?

Callicarpa yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, this plant is identified by the genus Callicarpa and is distinguished as the species yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Callicarpa yunnanensis has opposite, ovate to lanceolate leaves with serrated margins and a woody, deciduous shrub-like growth habit. Small, fragrant white or pale pink flowers emerge in clusters from the leaf axils during the growing season. These blossoms eventually give way to distinctive, spherical clusters of small, fleshy drupes that turn a vibrant purple when ripe.
