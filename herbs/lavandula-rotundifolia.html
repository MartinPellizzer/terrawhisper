Lavandula rotundifolia

Lavandula rotundifolia, commonly known as Wild Lavender or Felty Lavender, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its aromatic, rounded leaves and fragrant purple blooms, this medicinal plant is prized in aromatherapy for its calming properties, ability to reduce anxiety, and soothing antiseptic qualities.

  • Scientific name: Lavandula rotundifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls under the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Lavandula.
