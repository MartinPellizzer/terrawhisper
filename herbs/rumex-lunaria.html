Canary Island Sorrel, scientifically known as Rumex lunaria, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Macaronesian islands. Characterized by its striking, moon-shaped leaves, this plant thrives in rocky, coastal habitats. Traditionally valued in regional folk medicine, it is recognized for its unique botanical features and ecological significance.
