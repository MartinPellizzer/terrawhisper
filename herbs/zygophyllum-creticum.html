Zygophyllum creticum

Zygophyllum creticum, commonly known as the Cretan Calotropis or Creosote bush, is a resilient succulent shrub native to arid Mediterranean regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is utilized in traditional healing to treat skin ailments and inflammation, showcasing remarkable adaptations to survive in harsh, dry desert environments.

  • Scientific name: Zygophyllum creticum
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zygophyllales, it is further categorized into the family Zygophyllaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Zygophyllum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several regions of the Mediterranean basin. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found throughout Spain and Portugal.
