Gomphrena globosa

Globe amaranth

Gomphrena globosa, commonly known as Globe Amaranth, is a vibrant medicinal plant celebrated for its striking, long-lasting blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally used in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, offering a rich blend of therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Gomphrena globosa
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the Amaranthaceae family, specifically falling under the genus Gomphrena.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several continents. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in countries such as Bulgaria and Romania. Its range also extends to the Macaronesian islands, specifically within Cape Verde. Moving toward West Tropical Africa, the species is documented in the nation of Benin. Additionally, its presence is noted in the landlocked country of Burkina Faso.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Gomphrena globosa, is primarily valued for its striking and ornamental flower heads. These globular, papery blossoms consist of numerous small, bract-like structures that create a dense, spherical shape, often appearing in vibrant shades of pink, purple, or white. In many traditional medicinal practices, it is the dried flower that is utilized to prepare infusions or decoctions, as the colorful blooms are believed to possess various therapeutic properties.
