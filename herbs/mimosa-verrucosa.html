Mimosa verrucosa

Mimosa verrucosa, commonly known as the Shrubby Mimosa, is a resilient medicinal plant native to arid regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues. Its unique phytochemical profile makes it a valuable subject for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Mimosa verrucosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Mimosa, within which the species Mimosa verricosa is situated.
