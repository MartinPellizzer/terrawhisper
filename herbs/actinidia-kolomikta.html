Kolomiktar, scientifically known as Actinidia kolomiktar, is a hardy, deciduous climbing vine native to East Asia. Renowned for its aromatic, pungent fruit, it is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant properties, ability to boost immunity, and its therapeutic potential in supporting respiratory and digestive health.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it falls into the family Actinidiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Actinidia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several distinct regions. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing in the Khabarovsk area. Its distribution also extends to the Kuril Islands and the Primorye region within the same territory. Additionally, the species is documented on the island of Sakhalin. Beyond the Russian Far East, its presence is noted as far south as Uzbekistan in Central Asia.
