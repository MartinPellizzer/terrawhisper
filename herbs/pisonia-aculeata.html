This plant, Pisonia acuata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales and the family Nyctaginaceae. It is further identified by the genus Pisonia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent and its surrounding islands. Within the Macaronesia archipelago, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. Moving to the mainland, it is widely spread throughout West Tropical Africa. This regional coverage includes significant populations found in Benin, Ghana, and the Ivory Coast. Finally, its geographical range extends further east to include Nigeria.
