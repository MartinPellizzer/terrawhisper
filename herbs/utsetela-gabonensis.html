Utsetela gabonenensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic progression, the plant is situated in the order Rosales and belongs to the family Moraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Utsetela and the specific species epithet gabonenensis.
