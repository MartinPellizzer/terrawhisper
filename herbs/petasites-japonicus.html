Fuki, scientifically known as Petasites japonicus, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Highly valued in Japanese cuisine, its tender young shoots are harvested in spring. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal potential, traditionally used for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and various therapeutic properties.
