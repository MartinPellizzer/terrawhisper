Petasites japonicus

Petasites japonicus, commonly known as Japanese butterbur, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine and East Asian cuisine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and allergies, this versatile plant bridges the gap between nutrition and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Petasites japonicus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is specifically situated in the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Petasites.
