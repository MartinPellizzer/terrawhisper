Lysimachia circaeoides

Lysimachia circaeoides, commonly known as the marsh loosestrife, is a perennial herb native to wetland ecosystems. Characterized by its delicate yellow flowers and moisture-loving nature, this plant plays a vital role in its habitat. Historically valued in traditional herbalism, it offers unique botanical properties within its specific ecological niche.

  • Scientific name: Lysimachia circaeoides
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Lysimachia.
