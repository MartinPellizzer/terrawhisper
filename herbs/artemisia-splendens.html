Artemisia splendens

Artemisia splendens, commonly known as the silver sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to diverse mountainous regions. Renowned for its aromatic, silvery foliage, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is frequently utilized for its potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties to support holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia splendens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Artemisia, specifically identified as the species Artemisia splendens.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated across several key regions of Western Asia and the Caucasus. Within the Caucasus, it can be found growing in the North Caucasus as well as throughout the Transcaucasus. Its presence extends significantly into Western Asia, where it inhabits the landscapes of Iran and Iraq. Additionally, the species is widely distributed across the territory of Türkiye. Together, these locations define the natural range where this medicinal plant thrives.
