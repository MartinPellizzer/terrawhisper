Bupleurum fruticosum

Shrubby hare's ear

Bupleurum fruticosum, commonly known as thorny bupleurum, is a perennial medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds like saikosaponins. These constituents offer potent anti-inflammatory, hepatoprotective, and immunomodulatory properties, making it a vital plant for addressing various inflammatory and liver-related conditions.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum fruticosum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Bupleurum.
