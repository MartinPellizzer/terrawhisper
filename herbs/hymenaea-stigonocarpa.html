Hymenaea stigonocarpa

Hymenaea stigonocarpa, commonly known as Jatobá, is a powerhouse of tropical biodiversity. Renowned for its dense timber and nutrient-rich fruit, this medicinal plant offers profound therapeutic potential. Traditional practices utilize its bark and resins to treat inflammation and digestive issues, highlighting its vital role in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Hymenaea stigonocarpa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Hymenaea.
