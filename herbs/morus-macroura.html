Morus macroura

Morus macroura, commonly known as the large-leaved mulberry, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from inflammatory conditions to digestive issues, offering promising therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research and herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Morus macroura
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Rosales and categorized under the family Moraeceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Morus.
