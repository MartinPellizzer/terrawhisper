Scorzonera albicaulis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, which is characterized by its distinct floral structure, and it is specifically identified under the genus Scorzonera and the species albicaulis.
