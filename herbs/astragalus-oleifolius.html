Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions of Western Asia and the Caucasus. Within the Caucasus, its presence is noted specifically in the Transcaucasus region. Its range extends further into Western Asia, where it can be found growing in Iran. Additionally, the species inhabits parts of Iraq and the territories spanning Lebanon and Syria. Finally, its geographical distribution includes the area of Palestine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Astragalus oleifolius, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for the therapeutic properties found within its roots. These subterranean structures serve as the primary source for extracting bioactive compounds used to support immune function and overall vitality. Because the medicinal potency is concentrated in the root system, careful harvesting and processing of these parts are essential to ensure the efficacy of the herbal remedies derived from the species.
