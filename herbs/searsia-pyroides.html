Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Fire Karoo bush, is characterized by its distinct foliage where the leaves are typically small, leathery, and arranged in a compound structure. Each individual leaf is part of a trifoliate arrangement, meaning they grow in groups of three, providing a dense, textured appearance to the shrub. These leaves play a crucial role in the plant's medicinal properties and its ability to thrive in arid environments.
