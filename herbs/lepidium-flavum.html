Lepidium flavum

Lepidium flavum, commonly known as yellow pepperweed, is a versatile medicinal herb found in diverse habitats. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant has traditionally been utilized in folk medicine to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. Its unique botanical properties make it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lepidium.
