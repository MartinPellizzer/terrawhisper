Delphinium savatieri

Delphinium savatieri, commonly known as the Savatier's larkspur, is a captivating medicinal plant native to specific high-altitude regions. Renowned for its striking floral displays, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Traditional practitioners utilize its unique chemical properties for various therapeutic purposes, though careful handling is essential due to its potency.

  • Scientific name: Delphinium savatieri
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it resides in the family Ranunculaceae and is identified by the genus Delphinium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within the country of China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central territories to the North-Central areas. Additionally, its presence extends into the Southeast regions of China. Beyond Chinese borders, the species is also documented within the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it has a notable distribution in the nation of Vietnam.
