Solanum muricatum

Solanum muricatum, commonly known as the prickly nightshade, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Research continues to explore its potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties for modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Solanum muricatum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Solanum.
