Plant Parts

This plant, Calea pinnatifida, is primarily recognized for the medicinal properties found within its leaves. These leaves are characterized by their distinct pinnatifid shape, featuring deep, irregular lobes that give them a feathered or dissected appearance. In traditional herbal applications, it is these specific leaf structures that are harvested and processed to extract the bioactive compounds necessary for therapeutic use.
