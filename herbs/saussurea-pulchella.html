Saussurea pulchella

Maiden saussurea

Saussurea pulchella, commonly known as the beautiful sawtooth, is a precious medicinal herb found in the high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient perennial is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments, skin disorders, and various internal inflammations effectively.

  • Scientific name: Saussurea pulchella
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Saussurea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Northern Asia. In Siberia, its presence is documented in the Chitá area. The species can also be found throughout the Russian Far East, specifically within the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Moving into China, it inhabits the territory of Inner Mongolia. Consequently, the range of Saussurea pulchella spans a significant stretch of the Eurasian continent.
