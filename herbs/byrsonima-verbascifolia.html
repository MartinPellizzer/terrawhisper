Byrsonima verbascifolia

Byrsonima verbascifolia, commonly known as Verbascum-leaved Byrsonia, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammations, and digestive issues, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for pharmacological study.

  • Scientific name: Byrsonima verbascifolia
  • Family: Malpighiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is situated in the family Malpighiaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Byrsonima, where it is known by the species name Byrsonima verbasciifolia.
