Woodland sage, scientifically known as Salvia nemorosa, is a charming perennial native to the shaded forest floors of Europe. Distinguished by its soft, velvety grey-green foliage and delicate violet flowers, this resilient herb thrives in dappled light, offering both aesthetic elegance and traditional medicinal value in herbal gardens.
