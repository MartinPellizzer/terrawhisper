Salvia nemorosa

Woodland sage

Salvia nemorosa, commonly known as the woodland sage, is a charming perennial native to the forest edges of Europe. Distinguished by its delicate, pale lavender flowers and soft, textured foliage, this plant thrives in dappled sunlight, offering both aesthetic beauty to gardens and traditional medicinal interest in herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Salvia nemorosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Salvia.
