Ligustrum expansum

Ligustrum expansum, commonly known as the expanding privet, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a significant subject for both ethnobotanical study and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Ligustrum expansum
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Oleaceae and the genus Ligustrum. Specifically, it is identified as the species Ligustrum expansum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted across the South-Central regions. It is also widely distributed throughout the Southeast portions of China. Beyond Chinese borders, the species extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it can be found growing within the country of Vietnam.
