Grindelia argentina

Grindelia argentina, commonly known as yerba de la mopa or mopa, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to South America. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and digestive issues, offering valuable natural healing benefits.

  • Family: Asteraceae
