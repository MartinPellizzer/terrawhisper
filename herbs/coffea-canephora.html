This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Coffea canephora, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of West Tropical Africa. Its presence is documented in the coastal and inland areas of Benin. Furthermore, the species can be found growing within the territories of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. It also maintains a significant distribution throughout the nation of Guinea. Finally, its range extends into the forested landscapes of the Ivory Coast.
