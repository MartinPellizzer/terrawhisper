Coffea canephora

Robusta

Coffea canephora, commonly known as Robusta coffee, is a resilient species native to central and western Africa. Renowned for its high caffeine content and robust flavor profile, it is a vital global commodity. Beyond its culinary use, it offers significant pharmacological potential due to its rich antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Coffea canephora
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Coffea, specifically as the species Coffea canephora.
