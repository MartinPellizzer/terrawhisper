Rhododendron microphyton

What's the taxonomical classification of Rhododendron microphyton?

Rhododendron microphyton belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the biological lineage of the order Ericales. Within this order, it is situated in the family Ericaceae, which is further categorized into the genus Rhododendron. Ultimately, its specific identity is defined by its species name, microphyton.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rhododendron microphyton has small, elliptical leaves that are typically less than 1 centimeter in length and possess a leathery texture. The plant produces small, bell-shaped flowers that are usually white or pale pink in color. Its branching structure is somewhat compact, reflecting its adaptation to specific microclimates. The overall morphology is characterized by these stunted, scale-like vegetative features.
