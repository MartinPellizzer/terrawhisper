Lipschitzia divaricata

Lipschitzia divaricata, commonly known as the African forest climber, is a versatile woody vine native to subtropical regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues, offering a rich source of bioactive natural compounds.

  • Scientific name: Lipschitzia divaricata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lipschitzia, with the species being Lipschitzia divaricata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In China, its presence is noted within the Inner Mongolia autonomous region as well as the China North-Central area. Its range extends further north into the vast landscapes of Mongolia. Additionally, the species can be found within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically located in the West Himalaya. These diverse locations highlight the adaptable nature of its natural habitat.
