Crepis rubra

Pink hawk’s-beard

Crepis rubra, commonly known as red hawk's-beard, is a distinctive perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Characterized by its vibrant reddish-orange flower heads and deeply lobed leaves, this plant thrives in rocky, montane habitats. It holds significant interest in botanical studies and traditional ecological knowledge within its regions.

  • Scientific name: Crepis rubra
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by its genus, Crepis.

Moving toward the center of the continent, it is also found throughout Middle Europe, specifically in Germany and Switzerland.
