Diospyros kaki

Kaki persimmon

Diospyros kaki, commonly known as the persimmon, is a versatile evergreen tree celebrated for its sweet, nutrient-dense fruit. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and fruit offer potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits. This resilient species serves as both a delicious dietary staple and a natural therapeutic remedy.

  • Scientific name: Diospyros kaki
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Diospyros kaki, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is further categorized into the family Ebenaceae. Ultimately, it is placed under the genus Diospyros.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence can be noted specifically within Bulgaria. The species also extends into the Caucasus region, particularly throughout the Transcaucasus area. Within China, it is found across diverse territories including China North-Central and China South-Central. Furthermore, its range reaches the southern island province of Hainan.
