Kaki persimmon, scientifically known as Diospyros kaki, is a versatile fruit-bearing tree valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in vitamins A and C, its antioxidant-packed flesh supports immune health. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation and digestive issues.
