Camellia sinensis sinensis, scientifically known as Camellia sinensis var. sinensis, is the fundamental plant source for all true teas. Renowned for its diverse antioxidant properties, this evergreen shrub is cultivated globally. Beyond its aromatic infusions, it offers significant medicinal benefits, including improved metabolic health and enhanced cognitive focus.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Theaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Camellia, specifically as the variety sinensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans several distinct regions across the African continent and its surrounding islands. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is noted specifically within the borders of Guinea. Its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Furthermore, the species is located in Northeast Tropical Africa within the country of Ethiopia. Finally, its distribution reaches the Western Indian Ocean, specifically encompassing the islands of Mauritius and Réunion.
