Erythrina excelsa

Erythrina excelsa, commonly known as the Red Coral Tree, is a magnificent tropical evergreen native to East Africa. Renowned for its striking scarlet blossoms, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional healing practices to treat various ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential in contemporary botanical research and medicine.

  • Scientific name: Erythrina excelsa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa
