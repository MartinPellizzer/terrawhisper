Taraxacum japonicum, scientifically known as Taraxacum japonicum, is a versatile perennial herb deeply rooted in traditional East Asian medicine. Often referred to as Japanese dandelion, this plant is highly valued for its anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in treating liver ailments and supporting digestive health.

Distribution

This plant is primarily characterized by a very specific and limited geographical range. Its natural occurrence is concentrated within the region of Eastern Asia. More specifically, the species is native to the islands of Japan. This localized distribution suggests that the plant is well-adapted to the particular climates found in this part of the world. Consequently, researchers looking for this medicinal species focus their efforts on the Japanese landscape.

Plant Parts

This plant, Taraxacum japonicum, utilizes its extensive root system as a primary component for medicinal purposes. The root is often harvested and processed to extract its bioactive compounds, which are valued in traditional practices for their various therapeutic properties. Because the root serves as the plant's main storage organ for nutrients and secondary metabolites, it contains a concentrated profile of the substances necessary for its medicinal efficacy.
