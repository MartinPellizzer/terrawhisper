Dillenia

Dillenia, commonly known as the Elephant Apple, is a versatile genus of flowering plants found in tropical regions. Renowned for its large, distinctive fruits and fleshy sepals, it holds significant medicinal value. Often used in traditional remedies, it possesses antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties essential for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Dillenia
  • Family: Dilleniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dilleniales, it is classified under the family Dilleniaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal plant is identified by its specific genus, Dillenia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Elephant Apple, is highly valued for its unique and fleshy fruits. Beyond their botanical structure, the fruits are widely utilized in various culinary and medicinal practices, often used to prepare tangy curries or traditional remedies.
