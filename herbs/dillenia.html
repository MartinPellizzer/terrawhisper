Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Elephant Apple, is highly valued for its unique and fleshy fruits. These large, acid-flavored fruits are characterized by their thick, fleshy sepals that surround a central core, making them a distinctive feature of the species. Beyond their botanical structure, the fruits are widely utilized in various culinary and medicinal practices, often used to prepare tangy curries or traditional remedies.
