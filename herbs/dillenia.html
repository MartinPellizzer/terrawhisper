Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several tropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its presence extends significantly into the Western Indian Ocean region. Within this ocean area, the species inhabits the islands of Comoros and Mauritius. Furthermore, it is widely distributed across the larger islands of Madagascar and Réunion.
