, scientifically known as Colchicum crocifolium, is a perennial bulbous plant renowned for its striking autumn blooms. While highly valued in pharmacology for producing colchicine, a potent alkaloid used to treat gout, it is also extremely toxic. Careful handling is essential due to its potential for severe systemic poisoning.
