Lolium perenne

Lolium perenne, commonly known as perennial ryegrass, is a widely cultivated grass valued primarily for forage and turf. While not traditionally used in mainstream herbal medicine, its high nutritional density supports livestock health. In ethnobotany, various grasses like this are studied for their potential bioactive and restorative properties.

  • Scientific name: Lolium perenne
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales. Finally, it is a member of the family Poaceae and falls under the genus Lolium.
