English ryegrass, scientifically known as Lolium perenne, is a versatile perennial grass valued globally. Beyond its primary role in forage and turf management, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic properties, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales. Finally, it is a member of the family Poaceae and falls under the genus Lolium.
