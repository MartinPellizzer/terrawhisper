What's the taxonomical classification of Spiraea trilobata?

Spiraea trilobata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its evolutionary path continues through the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is situated within the genus Spiraea, with its specific designation being the species trilobata.
