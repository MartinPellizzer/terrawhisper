Balsam poplar, scientifically known as Populus balsamifera, is a deciduous tree celebrated for its aromatic, resinous buds. Historically valued in traditional medicine, its soothing balsamic properties have been utilized to treat respiratory ailments and skin irritations. This resilient species remains a vital subject in ethnobotanical studies and forest ecology.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Populus balsamica, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, which are primarily concentrated in its specialized structures. The medicinal potency is often derived from the sticky, resinous exudate found on the leaf buds, which contains various balsamic compounds. Additionally, the buds themselves are harvested for their aromatic and healing qualities, serving as a key component in traditional remedies used to soothe respiratory issues and skin irritations.
