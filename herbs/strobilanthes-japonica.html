Strobilanthes japonica

What's the taxonomical classification of Strobilanthes japonica?

Strobilanthes japonica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Lamiales. It is further categorized into the family Acanthaceae, specifically falling under the genus Strobilanthes and the species japonica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Strobilanthes japonica has an erect, herbaceous habit characterized by opposite, ovate to lanceolate leaves with entire margins and a distinctly pubescent surface. Its inflorescence consists of dense, terminal clusters of tubular, bilabiate flowers that often exhibit a vibrant violet or bluish hue. The calyx is persistent and frequently possesses fine hairs, providing a structural base for the delicate corollas.
