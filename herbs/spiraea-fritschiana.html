What's the taxonomical classification of Spiraea fritschiana?

Spiraea fritschiana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within its evolutionary lineage, it is classified in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the hierarchical structure of its order, it falls under the Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its genus, Spiraea, and its specific species designation, fritschiana.
