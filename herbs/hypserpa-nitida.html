What's the taxonomical classification of Hypserpa nitida?

Hypserpa nitida belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the taxonomic order Ranunculales. Further classification places the plant in the family Menispermaceae, which contains its specific genus, Hypserpa. Finally, the unique biological identity of this plant is defined by its specific epithet, making its full scientific name Hypserpa nitida.
