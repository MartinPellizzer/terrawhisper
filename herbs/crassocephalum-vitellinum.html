Crassocephalum vitellinum

Crassocaphalum vitellinum, commonly known as the velvet leaf or bitter leaf, is a versatile medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive ailments, and skin infections, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Crassocephalum vitellinum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Crassocephalum.
