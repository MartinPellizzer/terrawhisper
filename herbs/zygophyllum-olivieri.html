Zygophyllum olivieri

Zygophyllum olivieri, commonly known as the Olive Calcrete, is a resilient succulent native to arid southern African regions. Renowned for its ability to thrive in harsh environments, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize its various parts to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Zygophyllum olivieri
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zygophyllales, it is classified under the family Zygophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Zygophyllum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Zygophyllum olivieri, is recognized by various common names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the olive-leaved zygophyllum, while in Afrikaans, it is commonly called olyfblaar-zygophyllum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the arid regions of Western Asia. Its presence can be documented throughout the various landscapes of Afghanistan and Iran. Additionally, it grows within the territories of Iraq and the Lebanon-Syria border region. The species also extends its range into the area of Palestine. Consequently, these specific countries define the known geographical footprint of Zygophyllum olivieri.
