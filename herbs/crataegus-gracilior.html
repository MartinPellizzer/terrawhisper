Crataegus gracilior

Crataegus gracilior, commonly known as the slender hawthorn, is a resilient medicinal shrub valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive flavonoids and antioxidants, it is traditionally used to support cardiovascular health, regulate blood pressure, and reduce inflammation. This plant remains a vital subject in ethnobotanical and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus gracilior
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. It is further identified by its genus, Crataegus.
