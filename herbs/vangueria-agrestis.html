Vangueria agrestis

Vangueria agrestis, commonly known as the Wild Coffee tree, is a resilient medicinal plant native to southern Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, its various parts are used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammation, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
