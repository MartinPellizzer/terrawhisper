Rubus caesius

Rubus caesius, commonly known as the European blackberry, is a deciduous shrub native to Europe and parts of Asia. Renowned for its edible, blue-tinted berries, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support digestive health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Rubus caesius
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Rosales and is a member of the Rosaceae family. It is specifically identified by the genus Rubus.
