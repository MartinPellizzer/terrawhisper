Dewberry, scientifically known as Rubus caesius, is a trailing, deciduous shrub native to Europe and North America. Renowned for its edible, bluish-black berries, it offers significant nutritional value. Historically utilized in folk medicine, this plant possesses antioxidant properties and has been studied for its potential anti-inflammatory and therapeutic benefits.
