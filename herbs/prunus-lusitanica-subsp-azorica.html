Prunus lusitanica subsp. azorica

Azores laurel cherry

Prunus lusitanica subsp. azorica, commonly known as the Azorean laurel, is a distinctive evergreen shrub endemic to the Azores. Valued for its lush foliage and ecological significance, this subspecies holds potential in traditional herbal practices, offering unique phytochemical properties that warrant further exploration for its diverse medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Prunus lusitanica subsp. azorica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Prunus as the subspecies Prunus lusitanica subsp. azorica.

Distribution

This plant is characterized by a highly restricted geographical range within the Atlantic islands. Specifically, its natural distribution is confined to the Macaronesian region. Within this territory, the subspecies is found exclusively on the Azores archipelago. This localized presence indicates that it is an endemic species to these specific volcanic islands. Consequently, its entire population is concentrated within this unique oceanic environment.
