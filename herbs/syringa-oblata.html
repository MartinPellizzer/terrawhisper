Syringa oblata

Syringa oblata, commonly known as the Oblata Lilac, is a resilient deciduous shrub valued for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Native to East Asia, this species is celebrated for its fragrant blooms and traditional uses in herbal remedies, offering unique therapeutic properties within the diverse Lilac genus.

  • Scientific name: Syringa oblata
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Oleaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Syringa, specifically as the species Syringa oblata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several diverse regions within China. Its distribution begins in the southern and central parts of the country, specifically within the China South-Central region. Moving toward the northern territories, it can also be found throughout Inner Mongolia and the Manchuria area. Additionally, the species extends into the China North-Central region. Finally, its geographical range reaches as far west as the province of Qinghai.
