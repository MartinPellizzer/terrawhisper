Brugmansia sanguinea

Brugmansia sanguinea, commonly known as Angel's Trumpet, is a striking ornamental shrub prized for its dramatic, pendulous red flowers. While visually enchanting, this plant contains potent tropane alkaloids. Such compounds possess significant medicinal potential but require extreme caution, as ingestion can lead to severe toxicity and hallucinations.

  • Scientific name: Brugmansia sanguinea
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Brugmansia, specifically as the species Brugmansia sanguinea.
