Red angel's-trumpet, scientifically known as Brugmansia sanguinea, is a striking tropical shrub celebrated for its pendulous, blood-red blossoms. While prized in ornamental gardens for its dramatic beauty and fragrant flowers, this plant contains potent tropane alkaloids, making it highly toxic and requiring extreme caution during handling.
