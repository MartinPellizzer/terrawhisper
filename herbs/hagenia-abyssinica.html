African Redwood, scientifically known as Hagenia abyssinica, is a majestic tree native to the high-altitude forests of East Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are traditionally used to treat ailments like inflammation, fever, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in ethnobotanical studies.
