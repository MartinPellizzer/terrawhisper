Hagenia abyssinica

Hagenia abyssinicica, commonly known as Kosso, is a majestic medicinal tree native to the high-altitude forests of East Africa. Renowned for centuries in traditional medicine, its bark is primarily utilized to treat intestinal parasites, specifically tapeworms, making it a vital ethnobotanical resource in Ethiopian highland ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Hagenia abyssinica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Hagenia abyssinica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement within the genus Hagenia.
