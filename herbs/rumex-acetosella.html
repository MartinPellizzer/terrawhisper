Sheep's sorrel, scientifically known as Rumex acetosella, is a perennial herb characterized by its distinctively tart, acidic flavor. Found primarily in acidic soils and woodland clearings, this plant contains oxalic acid. Historically used in traditional medicine, it serves as a notable botanical specimen in various ecological and herbal studies.
