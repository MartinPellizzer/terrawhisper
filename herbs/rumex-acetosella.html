Rumex acetosella

Rumex acetosella, commonly known as sheep sorrel, is a perennial herb characterized by its delicate, trifoliate leaves and reddish stems. Found in acidic soils, this plant contains oxalic acid, which imparts a sharp, sour taste. Traditionally, it has been used in folk medicine for its astringent and diuretic properties.

  • Scientific name: Rumex acetosella
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rumex, specifically as the species Rumex acetosella.
