Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae within the genus Triadica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. It is also found in the island territory of Taiwan, which is part of Eastern Asia. Furthermore, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. These varied locations highlight the diverse ecological range inhabited by Triadica cochinchinensis.
