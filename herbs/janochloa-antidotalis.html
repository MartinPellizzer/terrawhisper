Janochloa antidotalis

Janochloa antidotalis, commonly known as the Golden Weaver, is a remarkable medicinal plant revered in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this resilient herb is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory ailments and boost immune function, offering a natural remedy for various complex physiological conditions.

  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa
