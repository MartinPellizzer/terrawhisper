Mallotus, scientifically known as Mallotus japonicus, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark possess potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This plant is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions through its bioactive compounds.
