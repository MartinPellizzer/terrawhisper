What's the taxonomical classification of Meliosma yunnanensis?

Meliosma yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Proteales. This specific plant is a member of the family Sabiaceae, falling within the genus Meliosma, and is identified by the species name yunnanensis.
