Distribution

This plant is primarily found across specific regions of Western North America. In Western Canada, its range extends through the provinces of Alberta and British Columbia. Moving south into the Northwestern United States, it is commonly located in Idaho. The species also thrives within the mountainous landscapes of Montana. Finally, its geographical distribution includes the forested areas of Oregon.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as the western white pine, utilizes its leaf as a primary component for various medicinal and practical applications. The leaves are needle-like in structure, growing in clusters or fascicles, and they serve as a vital source of essential oils and vitamins. When harvested, these needles can be used in infusions or decoctions to provide antioxidant properties and support respiratory health.
