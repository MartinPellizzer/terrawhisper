Selenicereus undatus

Seleniceureus undatus, commonly known as the dragon fruit cactus, is a remarkable climbing epiphyte prized for its vibrant, nutritional fruit. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal potential, offering antioxidant properties and bioactive compounds that support wellness, making it a fascinating subject for ethnobotanical study and holistic healing.

  • Family: Cactaceae
