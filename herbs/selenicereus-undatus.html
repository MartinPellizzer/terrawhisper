Hylocereus undatus, scientifically known as Selenicereus undatus, is the tropical cactus species renowned for producing dragon fruit. Beyond its vibrant, nutrient-dense flesh, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and dietary fiber, it supports immune function, promotes digestive health, and aids in managing oxidative stress.

Plant Parts

This plant, Selenicereus undatus, commonly known as dragon fruit, utilizes various parts of its fruit for different medicinal and nutritional purposes. The flowers are large, nocturnal blooms that play a crucial role in the plant's reproductive cycle and are sometimes studied for their unique bioactive compounds. The pulp is the most widely consumed portion, being rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and fiber that support digestive health and immune function. Finally, the peel, while often discarded, contains significant amounts of polyphenols and can be processed for its potential therapeutic properties in managing oxidative stress.
