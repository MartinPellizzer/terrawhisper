Artemisia oliveriana

Artemisia oliverianana, commonly known as a medicinal wormwood species, is a resilient perennial valued for its therapeutic potential. Native to specific temperate regions, this plant contains bioactive compounds with significant antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. It holds growing interest in ethnobotany for its traditional applications in treating various ailments.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
