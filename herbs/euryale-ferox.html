Fox nut, scientifically known as Euryale ferox, is a nutrient-dense aquatic plant prized for its medicinal and nutritional benefits. Often consumed as makhana, these popped seeds are rich in antioxidants, magnesium, and proteins. They support heart health, aid digestion, and provide a gluten-free energy boost in traditional wellness practices.
