Fox nut

Euryale ferox · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (42 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Fox nut, scientifically known as Euryale ferox, is a nutrient-dense aquatic plant prized for its medicinal and nutritional benefits. Often consumed as makhana, these popped seeds are rich in antioxidants, magnesium, and proteins. They support heart health, aid digestion, and provide a gluten-free energy boost in traditional wellness practices.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. Within the Russian Far East, it can be found specifically in the Primorye region. Its presence is also widespread throughout China, spanning from the South-Central provinces to the island of Hainan. Additionally, the species inhabits the northern territories of China, including Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Collectively, these areas highlight the plant's ability to thrive across diverse northern and southern climates.
