What's the taxonomical classification of Euonymus oxyphyllus?

Euonymus oxyphyllus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Celastrales. The plant is further categorized into the family Celastraceae, within which it resides in the genus Euonymus, eventually reaching its specific identification as the species oxyphyllus.
