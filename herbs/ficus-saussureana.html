Ficus saussureana

Ficus saussureana, commonly known as the medicinal fig, is a versatile plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation and digestive ailments. This species offers significant therapeutic potential, serving as a vital resource in natural medicine and ethnobotany.

  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Moraceae, it is classified under the genus Ficus.
