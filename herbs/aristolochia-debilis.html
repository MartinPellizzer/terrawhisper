Slender Dutchman's pipe, scientifically known as Aristolochia debilis, is a climbing herbaceous plant native to parts of Asia. Characterized by its distinctive tubular flowers and heart-shaped leaves, it holds significant historical importance in traditional medicine, though its use is now strictly cautioned due to potential nephrotoxicity and carcinogenic properties.
