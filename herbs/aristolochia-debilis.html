Aristolochia debilis

Aristolochia debilis, commonly known as the debile birthwort, is a climbing medicinal herb native to various tropical regions. Historically utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse pharmacological properties, it belongs to the Aristolochiaceae family. However, its use is strictly cautioned due to the presence of toxic aristolochic acids.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia debilis
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Aristolochiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aristolochia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Aristolochia debilis, is identified by various common names depending on the region and language. In Chinese, it is referred to as 马兜铃 (zh), with the simplified form 马兜铃 used in mainland China (zh-cn) and the traditional form 馬兜鈴 used in Taiwan (zh-tw). In the Zhuang language, it is called 馬兜鈴 (lzh), while in Japanese, it is known as ウマノスズクサ (ja).
