Origanum vulgare subsp. vulgare

Origanum vulgare subsp. vulgare, commonly known as wild oregano, is a fragrant perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Renowned for its aromatic essential oils, this medicinal plant offers potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties. It has been traditionally utilized to support digestive health and bolster the immune system.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
