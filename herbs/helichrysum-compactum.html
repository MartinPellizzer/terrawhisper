Helichrysum compactum

Helichrysum compactum, commonly known as the everlasting flower, is a resilient perennial prized in herbal medicine. Renowned for its potent essential oils, this plant offers remarkable anti-inflammatory and skin-regenerating properties. Often used in dermatological treatments, it serves as a natural remedy for healing wounds and promoting cellular rejuvenation.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum compactum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is completed by the genus Helichrysum, of which Helichrysum compactum is a member.
