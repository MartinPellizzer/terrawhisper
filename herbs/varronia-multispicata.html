Varronia multispicata

Varroniia multispicata, commonly known as Varronia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential in developing natural treatments for skin ailments and various infectious diseases.

  • Scientific name: Varronia multispicata
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Varronia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of South America. In the northern part of the continent, it can be found growing within the territory of Guyana. Its distribution extends significantly into Brazil, covering a vast range of diverse environments. Specifically, it is documented throughout the North, Northeast, Southeast, and West-Central regions of the country. This broad coverage highlights the plant's adaptability to various Brazilian landscapes.
