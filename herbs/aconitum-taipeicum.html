Aconitum taipeicum

Aconitum taipeiacum, commonly known as the Taipei monkshood, is a striking perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. While prized for its ornamental beauty and unique floral structure, it contains potent alkaloids. In traditional practices, it is studied for medicinal potential, though extreme caution is required due to its toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum taipeicum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aconitum taipeicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Aconitum.
