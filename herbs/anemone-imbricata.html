Anemone imbricata

What's the taxonomical classification of Anemone imbricata?

Anemone imbricata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. This plant is a member of the family Ranunculaceae, falling under the genus Anemone, and is specifically identified by its species name, imbricaata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Anemone imbricaata has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by a creeping rhizome and leaves that are deeply divided into narrow, linear segments. The foliage typically emerges in basal clusters and exhibits a delicate, finely dissected structure. Its inflorescences consist of solitary flowers held atop slender, upright pedicels. The petals are generally white or pale pink, often featuring a central ring of yellow stamens. Small, scale-like bracts frequently surround the base of the flowering stalks.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

These specific habitats provide the cool, moist, and rocky environments necessary for its survival. The species is most frequently found in mountain ranges such as the Karakoram and the Hindu Kush.
