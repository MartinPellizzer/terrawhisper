Capsicum frutescens

Capsicum frutescens, commonly known as the wild chili or bird pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its intense heat, it contains high concentrations of capsaicin, offering potent anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and antioxidant properties used globally to support digestive health and pain management.

  • Scientific name: Capsicum frutescens
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Capsicum, which includes the species Capsicum frutescens.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Capsicum frutescens, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Simplified Chinese contexts, it is known as 灌木状辣椒, a name used in mainland China (zh-cn), Singapore (zh-sg), and other areas utilizing the simplified script (zh-hans). In Traditional Chinese contexts, it is identified as 灌木狀辣椒, which is the standard nomenclature in Hong Kong (zh-hk), Taiwan, and other regions using the traditional script (zh-hanth).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the northern subtropical regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing across the territories of Algeria, Morocco, and Tunisia. Its presence extends further west into the Macaronesian islands. Specifically, it is located within the Canary Islands. Additionally, the species is documented inhabiting the island of Madeira.

Plant Parts

This plant, Capsicum frutescens, is valued for its potent medicinal properties which are primarily derived from its vibrant fruits. These small, often spicy fruits contain high concentrations of capsaicin, the active compound responsible for their therapeutic effects. When processed into fruit extracts, these components become highly concentrated, making them effective for topical applications to relieve pain or as dietary supplements to stimulate metabolism and digestion.
