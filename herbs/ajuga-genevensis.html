Ajuga genevensis

Geneva bugle

Ajuga genevensis, commonly known as Bugleweed, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, promote wound healing, and support cardiovascular health. This resilient perennial offers significant therapeutic potential for modern herbal medicine and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ajuga genevensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further categorized under the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Ajuga.
