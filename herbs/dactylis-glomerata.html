Dactylis glomerata

Cock's-foot

Dactylis glomerata, commonly known as orchard grass, is a versatile perennial graminoid valued in both agriculture and traditional herbalism. Renowned for its high nutritional content, it serves as an essential forage crop. In medicinal contexts, it is studied for its potential bioactive compounds and restorative properties in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Dactylis glomerata
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Dactylis.
