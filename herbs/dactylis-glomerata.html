Cock's-foot

Cock's-foot, scientifically known as Dactylis glomerata, is a perennial grass valued for its traditional medicinal properties. Historically utilized in herbal remedies, it is believed to support digestive health and possess anti-inflammatory qualities. This resilient plant serves as a fascinating example of how common flora can offer therapeutic potential.
