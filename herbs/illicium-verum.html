Star anise

Illicium verum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (513 studies) · ★★★★★

Star anise, scientifically known as Illicium verum, is a fragrant, star-shaped perennial shrub native to southern China and Vietnam. Renowned for its distinct licorice-like aroma, this potent spice is widely used in culinary arts and traditional medicine for its antimicrobial, digestive, and antioxidant properties, including its source of shikimic acid.
