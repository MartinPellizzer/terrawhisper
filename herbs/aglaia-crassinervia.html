Aglaia crassinervia

Aglaia crassinervia, commonly known as a member of the Meliaceae family, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical forest ecosystems. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it offers potential therapeutic benefits in traditional medicine, particularly for its antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, making it a subject of pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Aglaia crassinervia
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Aglaia and the specific species Aglaiia crassinervia.
