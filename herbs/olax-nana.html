Olax nana

Sudiyo

Olax nana, commonly known as the dwarf ola, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and stems possess potent bioactive compounds. This plant is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, offering natural therapeutic benefits for diverse health conditions.

  • Family: Olacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Olax nana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Santalales. Finally, it is a member of the family Olacaceae and falls under the genus Olax.
