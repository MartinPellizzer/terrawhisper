Clinacanthus nutans

Clinacanthus nutans, commonly known as the nodding clinacanthus, is a rare medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Found in specific ecological niches, this botanical species offers significant potential in traditional healing practices. Understanding its bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its medicinal applications and contributing to modern pharmacology.
