Sabah Snake Grass, scientifically known as Clinacanthus nutans, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues. This plant continues to garner significant scientific interest for its diverse pharmacological potential and healing properties.
