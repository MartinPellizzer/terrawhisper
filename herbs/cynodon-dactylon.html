Cynodon dactylon, scientifically known as Cynodon dactylon, is a resilient, creeping perennial grass widely distributed across tropical and subtropical regions. Often called Bermuda grass, this versatile plant is esteemed in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties, frequently used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Bermuda grass, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components offer therapeutic benefits. The whole plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its cooling properties and ability to treat various ailments. Specifically, the leaves are often prepared as infusions to manage inflammation and fever, while the roots are valued for their diuretic and tonic qualities. The spreading rhizomes serve as the plant's primary means of propagation and contain concentrated bioactive compounds used in topical applications. Additionally, although the plant primarily spreads vegetatively, the seed can also be collected for cultivation and seasonal replanting.
