Cynodon dactylon, scientifically known as Cynodon dactylon, is a resilient, creeping perennial grass widely distributed across tropical and subtropical regions. Often called Bermuda grass, this versatile plant is esteemed in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties, frequently used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues.
