Plant Parts

This plant, Hydnora abyssinica, is a unique parasitic organism that lacks traditional leaves and stems, instead relying on specialized structures for survival. Because it lives entirely underground or partially submerged in the soil, its primary connection to the host plant is through its highly modified, fleshy roots that function as haustoria to absorb nutrients. The most striking part of the plant is its large, fleshy flowers, which emerge from the ground to attract pollinators with a distinct odor. Ultimately, the entire organism is centered around its massive, subterranean Root system, which serves as the structural foundation for its reproductive organs.
