Hydnora abyssinica

Hydnorah abyssinica, commonly known as a parasitic flowering plant, is a unique botanical species found in specific African habitats. Unlike green plants, it lacks chlorophyll, relying entirely on host roots for nutrients. This rare specimen is studied for its distinct biological properties and potential medicinal value in traditional practices.
