Coleus scutellarioides

Coleus scutellarioides, commonly known as the Plectranthus or Coleus plant, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional wellness practices. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating respiratory issues and inflammation. This resilient plant serves as a vital resource in herbal medicine globally.

  • Scientific name: Coleus scutellarioides
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Coleus.
