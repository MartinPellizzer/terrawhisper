Western karee, scientifically known as Searsia lancea, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its drought tolerance and aesthetic appeal, it serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. Traditionally, various parts of this resilient plant are utilized in folk remedies to treat ailments like skin infections.
