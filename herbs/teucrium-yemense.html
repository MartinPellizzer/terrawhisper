Teucrium yemense

Teucrium yemense, commonly known as Yemen Germander, is a striking evergreen subshrub prized for its silvery-grey foliage and delicate white flowers. Native to the Middle East, this ornamental plant thrives in sunny, well-drained environments, offering a unique texture and hardy resilience for Mediterranean-style gardens and diverse landscaping designs.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Teucrium.
