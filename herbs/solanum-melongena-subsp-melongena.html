Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is classified under the genus Solanum. Specifically, its taxonomic designation is Solanum melongena subsp. melongena.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across diverse climatic zones. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the Eurasian continent, the species is well-distributed throughout Central Asia. Specifically, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Kazakhstan, Turkmenistan, and Uzbekistan. Furthermore, the distribution extends into the mountainous regions of Tajikistan.
