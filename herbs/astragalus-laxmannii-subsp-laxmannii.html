Astragalus laxmannii subsp. laxmannii

Astragalus laxmannii subsp. laxmannii, commonly known as a vital component of traditional East Asian medicine, is a resilient perennial herb. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this subspecies is highly valued for its immunomodulatory and anti-inflammatory properties, playing a significant role in supporting holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus laxmannii subsp. laxmannii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Fabales, it falls under the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Astragalus and is taxonomically recognized as the subspecies laxmannii.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various specific regions within the vast expanse of Siberia. Its documented distribution includes the mountainous areas of the Altai region. It can also be located within the territory of Buryatia. Furthermore, the species is present in the Chitа region of the country. Finally, its range extends through the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions of Siberia.
