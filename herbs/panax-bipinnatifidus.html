Panax bipinnatifidus

Panax bipinnatifidus, commonly known as the double-pinnatifid ginseng, is a distinctive medicinal herb belonging to the Araliaceae family. Native to specific temperate regions, this species is valued in traditional herbalism for its bioactive compounds, which are believed to offer adaptogenic properties, supporting overall vitality and immune health.

  • Scientific name: Panax bipinnatifidus
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Araliaceae and falls under the genus Panax.

Common Names

This plant, Panax bipinnatifidus, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In Vietnamese, it is referred to as Trúc tiết nhân sâm, while in Arabic, it is called جنسنغ ريشي الأوراق. In Chinese, it is commonly known as 疙瘩七, a name that is consistently used across different regional variations including zh-cn and zh-tw.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central areas to the North-Central territories. Its presence is also well-documented within the Southeast regions of China. Furthermore, the species thrives in the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. Finally, its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the Assam region.
