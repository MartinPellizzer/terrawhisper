Feather-leaf bamboo ginseng, scientifically known as Panax bipinnatifidus, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued for its unique foliage and potent therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional wellness practices, this plant offers diverse bioactive compounds believed to support vitality, reduce stress, and promote overall physiological balance through its natural adaptogenic qualities.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Araliaceae and falls under the genus Panax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central areas to the North-Central territories. Its presence is also well-documented within the Southeast regions of China. Furthermore, the species thrives in the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. Finally, its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the Assam region.
