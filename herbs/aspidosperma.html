Aspidosperma

Aspidosperma, commonly known as various local names depending on the species, belongs to the Apocynaceae family. These tropical plants are highly valued in ethnobotany for their diverse alkaloid content. Often used in traditional medicine, their extracts demonstrate significant pharmacological potential, including antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and various neuroprotective therapeutic properties.
