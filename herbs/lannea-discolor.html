Lannea discolor

Lannea discolor, commonly known as the bushweed or forest climber, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various African regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic applications, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections and digestive issues to inflammatory conditions and respiratory distress.
