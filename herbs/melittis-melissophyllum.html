Melittis melissophyllum

Melittis melissophyllum, commonly known as the honey-leaved plant, is a remarkable medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its soothing qualities, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments. This plant offers a unique blend of botanical benefits, making it a vital resource in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Melittis melissophyllum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
