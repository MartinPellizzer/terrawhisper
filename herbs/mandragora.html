Mandragora

Mi polla

Mandragora, commonly known as mandrake, is a legendary medicinal plant steeped in folklore and mysticism. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, it has been used since antiquity for its anesthetic and sedative properties. While historically significant in traditional healing, its powerful effects require extreme caution and expert botanical knowledge.

  • Scientific name: Mandragora
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Mandragora.
