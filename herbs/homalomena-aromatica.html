Homalomena aromatica

Homalomena aromatica, commonly known as the aromatic homalomena, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its fragrant properties, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional healing practices. This species is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic applications in treating various ailments and supporting overall wellness.

  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
