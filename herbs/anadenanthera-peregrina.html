Anadenanthera peregrina

Anadenanthera peregrina, commonly known as Yopo, is a significant tropical tree valued for its psychoactive properties. Historically used by indigenous South American cultures, its seeds contain potent alkaloids. Beyond its ritualistic importance, this species offers valuable insights into ethnobotany and the complex biochemical interactions within traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Anadenanthera peregrina
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa
