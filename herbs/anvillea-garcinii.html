Anvillea garcinii

Anvillea garcinii, commonly known as the "tree of life" in certain traditional practices, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is widely utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various systemic conditions through its unique therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Anvillea garcinii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Anvillea garcinii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is placed in the genus Anvillea.
