Adenophora triphylla

Adenophora triphylla, commonly known as Ladybell or Nan Sha Shen, is a perennial herb highly valued in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its ability to nourish the lungs and clear heat, it is frequently used to treat coughs, thirst, and respiratory ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Adenophora triphylla
  • Family: Campanulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Campanulaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Adenophora.

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions in Northern Asia. Within Siberia, it can be found growing in the areas of Buryatiya, Chitа, and Irkutsk.
