Giant-bellflower, scientifically known as Adenophora triphylla, is a perennial herb valued extensively in traditional Chinese medicine. Native to East Asia, its roots are frequently utilized to treat coughs, bronchitis, and asthma. This medicinal plant serves as a vital remedy for respiratory ailments, offering natural therapeutic benefits for lung health.
