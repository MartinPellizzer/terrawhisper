Toothache plant, scientifically known as Acmella oleracea, is a remarkable medicinal herb renowned for its potent numbing properties. Primarily used to alleviate dental pain, this plant contains spilanthol, a natural compound that provides rapid relief. It serves as a vital traditional remedy for oral health and various inflammatory conditions.
