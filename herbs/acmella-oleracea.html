Acmella oleracea

Acmella oleracea, commonly known as the "toothache plant," is a vibrant medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique numbing properties, it is traditionally used to alleviate dental pain and oral inflammation. Its potent bioactive compounds make it a fascinating subject in ethnobotanical and pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Acmella oleracea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Acmella oleracea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. It is further categorized under the genus Acmella.
