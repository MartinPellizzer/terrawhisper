Aspilia

Aspilia, commonly known as the bushy sunflower, is a versatile medicinal plant widely used in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it possesses significant antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties. This resilient herb serves as a vital natural resource for treating various skin ailments and infections.

  • Scientific name: Aspilia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Aspilia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern centered primarily within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is well-documented across several key nations including Benin and Burkina.
