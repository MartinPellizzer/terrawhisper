Trapa natans

Water caltrop

Trapa natans, commonly known as the water caltrop or bladder nut, is an aquatic perennial valued for its medicinal properties. Traditionally used in various cultures, its nutrient-rich seeds and leaves offer potential therapeutic benefits, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, making it a subject of interest in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Trapa natans
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Trapa natans, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Myrtales and the family Lythraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Trapa.

Common Names

This plant, Trapa natans, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic region. In nan, it is referred to as Lêng-kak, while in several Chinese dialects and written forms, including zh-hans, zh-hant, zh-hk, and zh-cn, it is commonly called 菱角.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be observed in the country of Sweden. Moving into Middle Europe, it is found within the borders of Austria and Germany. The species also inhabits the territories of both Czechia and Slovakia. Finally, its geographical range extends further south into Hungary.
