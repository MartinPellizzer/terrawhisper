Lagotis clarkei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant further falls into the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is defined by the genus Lagotis and the species clarkei.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lagotis clarkei has small, delicate, pinnatifid fronds that typically measure only a few centimeters in length and are arranged in a spreading, somewhat procumbent habit. The plant lacks a prominent rhizome and instead features very thin, creeping stems that allow it to colonize damp substrates. Its reproductive structures are located on the undersides of the leaves in small, inconspicuous sori.
