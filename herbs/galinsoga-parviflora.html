Galinsoga parviflora

Galinsoga parviflora, commonly known as Gallant Soldier, is a resilient herbaceous plant found in tropical and subtropical regions. Often viewed as a weed, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin inflammations, wounds, and digestive issues across diverse cultures.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
