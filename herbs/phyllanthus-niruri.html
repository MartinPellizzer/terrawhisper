Medicinal Herbs, scientifically known as Phyllanthus niruri, is a versatile plant widely revered in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Often called "stonebreaker," it is celebrated for its potent hepatoprotective and diuretic properties. This small herb plays a vital role in supporting liver health and managing kidney stones through its bioactive compounds.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Phyllanthus niruri, is a versatile herb where nearly every component offers therapeutic value. While the whole plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, specific focus is often placed on the leaves due to their potent bioactive compounds. A single leaf can contain significant concentrations of lignans and flavonoids, making the whole plants highly effective when prepared as a decoction or a standardized medicinal plant extract for pharmacological use.
