Phedimus aizoon

Phedimus aizoon, commonly known as the medicinal herb of the hills, is a perennial plant valued in traditional medicine. Found in high-altitude regions, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Often used to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments, this plant remains a vital resource in regional ethnomedicine and pharmacological research.
