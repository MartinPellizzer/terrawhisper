Coniogramme intermedia

What's the taxonomical classification of Coniogramme intermedia?

Coniogramme intermedia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. This fern is further categorized into the family Pteridaceae, specifically within the genus Coniogramme, where it is identified by the species name intermedia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Coniogramme intermedia has large, once-pinnate to bipinnate fronds characterized by lanceolate to oblong segments with deeply incised, entire margins. The rhizomes are typically creeping and covered in dense, brownish scales that provide protection to the growing tips. Each pinna consists of several smaller segments that are often irregularly shaped and possess a thin, membranous texture. The underside of the fertile fronds is distinguished by prominent, dark soral patches that contain the reproductive spores. These morphological features allow the fern to thrive in shaded, humid environments where it can efficiently capture light.
