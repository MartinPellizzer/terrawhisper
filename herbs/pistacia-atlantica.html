Baneh, scientifically known as Pistacia atlantica, is a resilient tree native to the Middle East, particularly Iran. Renowned for its nutritious seeds, it serves as a cornerstone in traditional medicine and culinary arts. This versatile plant offers significant antioxidant properties, supporting digestive health and providing essential fatty acids.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across multiple continents and regions. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Greece and within the European portion of Türkiye. Its range extends further into Eastern Europe, specifically reaching the region of Krym. Moving toward Northern Africa, the species is established in Algeria. Finally, its presence is also noted within the territory of Libya.

Plant Parts

This plant, Pistacia atlanticica, offers various components that contribute to its medicinal profile. The leaf serves as a primary source for pharmacological studies, where concentrated leaf extracts are utilized for their bioactive properties. Beyond the foliage, the plant produces a complex resinous therapeutic oleoresin gum, a substance that integrates both resin and gum within its composition. These exudates are highly valued in traditional medicine, providing a potent blend of chemical constituents derived from the tree's natural defense mechanisms.
