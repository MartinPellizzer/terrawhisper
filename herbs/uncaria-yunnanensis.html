Uncaria yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Uncaria yunnanensis?

Uncaria yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant follows the taxonomic order Gentianaales. It is further categorized under the family Rubiaceae, which places it within the genus Uncaria, ultimately defining its specific identity as the species yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Uncaria yunnanensis has woody, climbing vines characterized by recurved, woody spines that emerge from the leaf axils to facilitate attachment to host trees. The leaves are typically pinnately compound with several pairs of leaflets that exhibit an elliptical or oblong shape. Small, yellowish flowers emerge from the leaf axils and possess a structure typical of the Rubiaceae family. The plant produces small, fleshy fruits that often turn red or orange upon maturity. Its stems are robust and develop a thick, bark-covered exterior as the liana matures in its forest habitat.

What parts of this plant are used medicinally?

The root is also carefully collected to prepare traditional decoctions used in herbal remedies. These specific components are valued for their unique chemical profiles and healing properties. According to a study published by "Yao xue xue bao = Acta pharmaceutica Sinica", chemical constituents were researched from the dried roots of Uncaria yunnanensis Hsia. C.C. Through modern chromatography, 7 compounds were isolated and identified. These constituents include 3 beta, 6 beta, 19 alpha-trihydroxyurs-12-en-28 oic acid (I), 23-nor-24-esomethylene-3 beta, 6 beta-19 alpha-trihydroxyurs-12-en-28 oic acid (II), 3-oxo-6 beta, 19 alpha-dihydroxyurs-12-en-28 oic acid (III), oleanic acid (IV), 5,7,3',4'-tetrahydroxy-flavan-3-ol (V), beta-yohimbine (VI) and diangoutengjian I (VII). All of these 7 compounds were isolated for the first time from the root of this plant. Among these, compound VII is a new one.

What are the pharmacological activities of Uncaria yunnanensis?

This plant has agonistic activity on 5-hydroxytryptamine receptors and agonistic activity on melatonin receptors. These specific pharmacological properties allow the plant's compounds to interact directly with key neurotransmitter systems in the brain. By stimulating these receptors, the plant may influence various physiological processes such as mood regulation and sleep patterns. Such biochemical actions highlight its potential for developing therapeutic agents targeting the central nervous system. Ongoing research continues to explore how these molecular interactions contribute to its overall biological effects.

According to a study published by "Natural products and bioprospecting", Uncaria yunnanensis is one of three Uncaria species that demonstrated agonistic activity. This activity was specifically observed on 4 neurotransmitter receptors, which are MT1, MT2, 5-HT1A, and 5-HT2C. The study utilized UFLC-IT/TOF-MS to characterize 57 compounds across seven Uncaria species. These compounds consisted of 35 indole alkaloids, 10 flavonoids, 5 triterpenoids, 5 chlorogenic analogues, and 2 other compounds.
