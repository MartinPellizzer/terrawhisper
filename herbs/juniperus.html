Juniperus

Juniperus, commonly known as juniper, is a diverse genus of coniferous plants celebrated for their aromatic berries and medicinal properties. Renowned for their antioxidant and antimicrobial qualities, these resilient shrubs are used in traditional remedies to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and provide therapeutic benefits in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, it is a member of the family Cupressaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Juniperus.
