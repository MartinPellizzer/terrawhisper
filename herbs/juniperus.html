Junipers, scientifically known as Juniperus, is a diverse genus of coniferous evergreen trees and shrubs celebrated for their aromatic qualities. Renowned in traditional medicine, these resilient plants offer potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, frequently utilized in herbal remedies and culinary infusions to support digestive and urinary health.
