Pseudosamanea guachapele

Pseudosamanea guachapele, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in regional ethnobotany, holds profound therapeutic potential. Found primarily in tropical ecosystems, this species is valued for its bioactive compounds. Traditional healers utilize its various parts to treat diverse ailments, offering a natural resource for pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Pseudosamanea guachapele
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Pseudosamanea, with the species name guachapele.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several regions across North and Central America. Within Mexico, its presence is noted in the Gulf regions, the Southwest, and the Southeast.
