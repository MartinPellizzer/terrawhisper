Otoba parvifolia

Otoba parvifolia, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, holds immense value in traditional pharmacopeia. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammatory conditions, offering a natural resource for holistic health and herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Otoba parvifolia
  • Family: Myristicaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Otoba parvifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, following the order Magnoliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Myristicaceae within the genus Otoba.
