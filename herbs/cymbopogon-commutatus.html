Cymbopogon commutatus

Cymbopogon commutatus, commonly known as lemongrass, is a versatile aromatic grass celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its refreshing citrus scent, it possesses significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. Widely used in herbal teas and essential oils, this plant offers therapeutic benefits for digestive health and relaxation.

  • Scientific name: Cymbopogon commutatus
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales and family Poaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Cymbopogon.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the territories of Mauritania and Senegal. Moving toward the eastern parts of the continent, its range extends into Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is documented in the countries of Chad and Djibouti. Finally, its presence is also confirmed within the borders of Eritrea.
