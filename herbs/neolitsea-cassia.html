Neolitsea cassia

Grey bollywood

Neolitsea cassia, commonly known as the Cassia Laurel, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to Southeast Asia. Valued in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds, it offers significant therapeutic potential. This plant is frequently studied for its antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a botanical treasure.

  • Scientific name: Neolitsea cassia
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Laurales, it is further categorized into the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Neolitsea, specifically as the species Neolitsea cassia.
