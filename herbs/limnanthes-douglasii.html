Limnanthes douglasii

Limnanthes douglasii, commonly known as meadowfoam, is a captivating perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its striking white blooms, this plant holds significant medicinal and industrial value. Traditionally used for its soothing properties, its unique oils are now vital in high-end skincare for deep, restorative hydration.

  • Scientific name: Limnanthes douglasii
  • Family: Limnanthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales, it is classified under the family Limnantheaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed by its genus, Limnanthes.
