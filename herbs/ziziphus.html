Ziziphus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (11 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Jujube, scientifically known as Ziziphus, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional medicine, its fruits, bark, and leaves offer calming, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory benefits. Often used to support sleep and digestive health, this resilient species remains a vital component of natural healing.
