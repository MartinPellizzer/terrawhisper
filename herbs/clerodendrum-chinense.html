Clerodendrum chinense

What's the taxonomical classification of Clerodendrum chinense?

Clerodendrum chinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant falls under the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Clerodendrum and the species chinense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Clerodendrum chinense has opposite, ovate to lanceolate leaves that measure up to 15 centimeters long and are characterized by serrated margins and prominent venation. The plant features woody stems that can reach heights of up to 3 meters in a shrubby growth habit. Its inflorescence consists of dense, terminal cymes containing small, white, tubular flowers with long exserted stamens. After flowering, the plant produces small, globose, fleshy berries that transition from green to a dark purple or black color when ripe. These morphological features collectively distinguish it from other species within the Verbenaceae family.
