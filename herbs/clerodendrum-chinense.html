Clerodendrum chinense

Clerodendrum chinense, commonly known as the Chinese Glory Bower, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its fragrant blossoms, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, making it a valuable resource in ethnobotanical studies and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Clerodendrum chinense
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Clerodendrum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Clerodendrum chinense, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Sweden, it is called Doftklerodendrum (sv), while in Vietnam, it is known as Ngọc nữ thơm (vi). In Chinese-speaking regions, it is identified as 重瓣臭茉莉 (zh) or 臭茉莉 (zh-tw), and in Indonesia, it is commonly called Bunga Melati Susun Papua (id).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several tropical and oceanic regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its presence extends to East Tropical Africa within the borders of Tanzania and into South Tropical Africa in Mozambique. Additionally, it is located in the Middle Atlantic Ocean on Ascension Island. Finally, the species inhabits the Western Indian Ocean, specifically within the Seychelles.
