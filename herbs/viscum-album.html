Viscum album

Viscum album, commonly known as European mistletoe, is a unique hemi-parasitic shrub that grows on various host trees. Traditionally used in folk medicine, it holds significant importance in herbalism. Modern research explores its bioactive compounds for potential applications in supporting the immune system and treating various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Viscum album
  • Family: Santalaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Santalales, falling into the family Santalaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Viscum, specifically known by the species name Viscum album.
