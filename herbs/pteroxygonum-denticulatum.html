Pteroxygonum denticulatum

Pteroxygonum denticulatum, commonly known as the "tooth-edged climber," is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues. Understanding its pharmacological potential remains vital for developing modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Pteroxygonum denticulatum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Caryophyllales and placed in the family Polygonaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Pteroxygonum, with the specific species being Pteroxygonum denticulaatum.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across certain regions of East Asia. It is primarily documented within the South-Central part of China. Consequently, its range is concentrated within these particular Chinese territories.
