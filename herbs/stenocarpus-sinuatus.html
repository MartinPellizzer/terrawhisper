Stenocarpus sinuatus

Stenocarpus sinuatus, commonly known as the Firewheel Tree, is a stunning evergreen native to Australia. Beyond its spectacular, star-shaped orange flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, its edible seeds provided vital nutrition, offering a unique intersection of ornamental beauty and historical medicinal and dietary importance.

  • Scientific name: Stenocarpus sinuatus
  • Family: Proteaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Proteales, it is situated within the family Proteaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Stenocarpus.
