American beautyberry

Callicarpa americana · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (249 studies) · ★★★★★

American beautyberry, scientifically known as Callicarpa americana, is a striking deciduous shrub native to the southeastern United States. Renowned for its vibrant, magenta-colored berry clusters, it serves as a vital food source for wildlife. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant possesses traditional medicinal properties used to soothe skin irritations.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as beautyberry, features distinct morphological characteristics across its various components. The leaves are typically large, oppositely arranged, and possess serrated edges that provide a lush, green canopy. Its woody twigs are sturdy and often serve as the structural base for the plant's vibrant displays, sometimes exhibiting a slightly reddish hue. Most notably, the plant produces striking clusters of small, spherical fruits that transition into a brilliant, metallic purple color, making them a highly recognizable feature of the species.
