Cytisus scoparius

English broom

Cytisus scoparius, commonly known as Scotch broom, is a deciduous shrub native to Europe. While primarily valued in landscaping for its vibrant yellow blooms, it holds historical significance in traditional medicine. Often used for its alkaloid content, it requires careful handling due to its potent chemical properties and invasive nature.

  • Scientific name: Cytisus scoparius
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is a member of the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae, falling under the genus Cytisus. Within this classification, the species is identified as Cytisus scoparius.
