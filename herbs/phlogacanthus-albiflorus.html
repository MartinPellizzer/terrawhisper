Phlogacanthus albiflorus

Phlogacanthus albiflorus, commonly known as the White Phlogacanthus, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its striking white blooms, this plant possesses diverse bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to address various ailments, showcasing its importance in ethnobotanical studies and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Phlogacanthus albiflorus
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Phlogacanthus, and its specific species name, albiflorus.
