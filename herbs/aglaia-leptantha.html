Aglaia leptantha

Aglaia leptantha, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in Southeast Asian traditional medicine, belongs to the Meliaceae family. Valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is often utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its phytochemical profile is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Aglaia leptantha
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Meliaceae, specifically falling under the genus Aglaia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China peninsula, it can be found growing in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, and Vietnam. Beyond the mainland, its range extends significantly into the Malesian region. Specifically, the species is known to inhabit the island of Borneo. Collectively, these locations highlight its presence in both mainland and maritime tropical environments.
