Cucumis maderaспаtanus, commonly known as the Madeira cucumber, is a climbing vine native to the Atlantic islands. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and skin inflammations. Its unique bioactive compounds offer significant potential for contemporary pharmacological research and development.
