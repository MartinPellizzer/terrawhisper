What's the taxonomical classification of Corydalis repens?

Corydalis repens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Ranunculales and into the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is defined by its specific classification within the genus Corydalis and the species repeps.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Corydalis repens has a basal rosette of deeply lobed, pinnatifid leaves that are bright green and often possess a slightly glaucous texture. The plant produces upright flowering stems that can reach heights of up to thirty centimeters. Its flowers are zygomorphic and characterized by a long, nectar-bearing spur that extends backward from the petals. The perianth typically features pale yellow or creamy white hues, sometimes accompanied by subtle violet markings. Small, capsule-like fruits develop from the flowers and eventually split to release numerous tiny seeds.
