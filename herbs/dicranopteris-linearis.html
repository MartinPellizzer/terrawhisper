Dicranopteris linearis

Dicranopteris linearis, commonly known as the linear fern, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to various subtropical regions. Valued in traditional healing practices, it possesses unique phytochemical properties. This fern is often studied for its potential therapeutic applications, offering promising insights into natural pharmacology and holistic wellness strategies.

  • Scientific name: Dicranopteris linearis
  • Family: Gleicheniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Gleicheniales. Finally, it is a member of the family Gleicheniaceae and falls under the genus Dicranopteris.
