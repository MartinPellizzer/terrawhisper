Geigeria alata

Geigeria alata, commonly known as the spreading Geigeria, is a resilient shrub native to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance within its natural ecosystem.

  • Scientific name: Geigeria alata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by its genus name, Geigera, and its species name, Geigera alata.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in the countries of Mali, Mauritania, and Niger. The species also extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it can be found growing in Chad. Additionally, its geographical range reaches as far east as Djibouti.
