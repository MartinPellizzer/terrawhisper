Swinglea glutinosa

Swinglea glutinosa, commonly known as the sticky-leaved shrub, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique adhesive properties, it has long been utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Exploring its bioactive compounds offers promising insights into developing new natural therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Swinglea glutinosa
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Swinglea glutinosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rutaceae and falls under the genus Swinglea.
