Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several distinct global regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is noted within the borders of Egypt. The species also extends into Macaronesia, where it can be found in both Cape Verde and Madeira. Moving toward West Tropical Africa, the plant is distributed through the territories of Benin and Burkina.
