Raulinoa echinata

Raulinoa echinata, commonly known as the "miracle berry" or "mountain berry," is a rare medicinal plant native to specific tropical rainforest regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Rutaceae
