Launaea nudicaulis

Launaea nudicaulis, commonly known as a medicinal herb in various traditional healing systems, is a plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Often utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, this species offers significant pharmacological potential for developing new treatments in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Launaea nudicaulis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Launaea.
