Retama sphaerocarpa

Retama sphaerocarpa, commonly known as the spherical broom, is a resilient shrub native to arid Mediterranean regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat respiratory ailments and inflammatory conditions. Its ability to thrive in harsh environments makes it a significant subject for ethnobotanical and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Retama sphaerocarpa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and is a member of the family Fabaceae. Finally, its scientific classification is completed by its placement in the genus Retama.
