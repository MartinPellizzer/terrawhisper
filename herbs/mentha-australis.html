Mentha australis

Mentha australis, commonly known as Australian mint bush, is a versatile aromatic shrub native to Australia. Renowned for its refreshing fragrance, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. It is traditionally utilized for its antimicrobial and soothing properties, making it a valuable botanical resource in both herbal remedies and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Mentha australis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Mentha under the species name Mentha australis.
