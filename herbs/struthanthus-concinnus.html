Struthanthus concinnus

Struthanthus concinnus, commonly known as the Velvet Bloom, is a rare medicinal plant prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Found in remote tropical highlands, its vibrant petals contain unique bioactive compounds used in traditional healing to reduce inflammation and support cellular regeneration, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Struthanthus concinnus
  • Family: Loranthaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Santalales, it is a member of the family Loranthaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Struthanthus, specifically identified by its species name concinuus.
