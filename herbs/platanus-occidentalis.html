Platanus occidentalis

American sycamore

Platanus occidentalis, commonly known as the American sycamore, is a majestic deciduous tree prized for its impressive size and distinctive peeling bark. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this resilient species plays a vital role in riparian ecosystems, providing shade and stabilizing soil along riverbanks while supporting diverse local wildlife.

  • Scientific name: Platanus occidentalis
  • Family: Platanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered within the Proteales and belongs to the family Platanaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Platanus.
