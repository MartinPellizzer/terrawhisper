Polypodium

Polypodium, commonly known as common polypody, is a resilient fern valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its soothing properties, it has long been used to support respiratory health and digestive function. This versatile plant offers a natural approach to wellness, bridging ancient botanical wisdom with modern holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Polypodium
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, falling under the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Polypodium.
