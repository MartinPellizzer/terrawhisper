Patrinia

Patrinia, commonly known as Greek herb or silver sage, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health and skin vitality. This resilient perennial offers a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Patrinia
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales, it is a member of the family Caprifoliaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Patrinia.
