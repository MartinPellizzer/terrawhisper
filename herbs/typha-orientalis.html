Bullrush, scientifically known as Typha orientalis, is a versatile aquatic perennial found in wetland ecosystems. Beyond its ecological role, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and astringent properties, offering natural remedies for various skin conditions and digestive ailments.
