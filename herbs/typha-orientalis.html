Bullrush, scientifically known as Typha orientalis, is a versatile aquatic perennial found in wetland ecosystems. Beyond its ecological role, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and astringent properties, offering natural remedies for various skin conditions and digestive ailments.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, it is further organized into the family Typhaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Typha.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing in both Primorye and Sakhalin. Its range extends significantly into China, where it is documented in the South-Central areas. Furthermore, the species is present in the northern Chinese territories of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. These diverse locations highlight the wide environmental adaptability of the species.
