Buddleja saligna

Witolien

Buddleja saligna, commonly known as the willow-leaved butterfly bush, is a versatile evergreen shrub valued for its medicinal properties. Native to various subtropical regions, it is traditionally used in herbal remedies to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, showcasing its significant role in ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Buddleja saligna
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further categorized under the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Buddleja, specifically as the species Buddleja saligna.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout several key regions of Southern Africa. It can be found growing within the Cape Provinces and the KwaZulu-Natal region. Additionally, its presence is noted in the Free State and the Northern Provinces. Beyond these areas, the species also extends into South Tropical Africa, specifically in Zimbabwe. Together, these locations define the diverse geographical range of Buddleja saligna.
