Egyptian clover

Egyptian clover, scientifically known as Trifolium alexandrinum, is a highly nutritious medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in phytoestrogens, it is traditionally used to support hormonal balance, alleviate menopause symptoms, and improve bone density. This potent legume offers significant nutritional value, making it a vital herbal remedy.
