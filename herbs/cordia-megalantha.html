Plant Parts

This plant, Cordia megalantha, possesses various parts that are often utilized in traditional medicinal practices. The small, fragrant white flowers are a distinctive feature of the species, while the fruit develops as a fleshy drupe following pollination. The broad, green leaves serve as the primary site for photosynthesis and are frequently the most common part used in herbal preparations. Structurally, the plant is supported by a sturdy stem that provides essential nutrients and water transport, all of which originate from the root system anchored deep within the soil.
