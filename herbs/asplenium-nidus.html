Asplenium nidus

Asplenium nidus, commonly known as the Bird's Nest Fern, is a striking epiphytic plant prized for its lush, rosette-shaped foliage. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this fern holds significant traditional medicinal value, often utilized in various cultures for its potential anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties within herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Asplenium nidus
  • Family: Aspleniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Polypodiinae and order Polypodiales, it is situated in the family Aspleniaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Asplenium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several tropical regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found within the Chagos Archipelago. Its range extends to the Indian Subcontinent, specifically including Bangladesh. Furthermore, it is widely distributed throughout Malesia, spanning from Borneo to Jawa. Finally, the species is also documented across the Lesser Sunda Islands.
