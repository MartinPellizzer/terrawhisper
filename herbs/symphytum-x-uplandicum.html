Symphytum × uplandicum, is a robust hybrid commonly referred to as Comfrey. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat skin inflammations and bone injuries. Rich in allantoin, it promotes rapid tissue regeneration, making it a vital staple in herbal healing and topical remedies.
