Cissus assamica

Cissus assamica, commonly known as the Assam Cissus, is a versatile medicinal climber native to the tropical regions of Northeast India. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research and herbal treatments.

  • Scientific name: Cissus assamica
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cissus assamica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Vitales and the family Vitaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Cissus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spread across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast.
