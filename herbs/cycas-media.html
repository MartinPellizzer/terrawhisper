Zamia palm

Cycas media, commonly known as the medium cycad, is a distinctive gymnosperm native to the rocky terrains of India. Characterized by its robust trunk and pinnate leaves, this slow-growing plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering traditional medicinal properties used in various regional practices to treat diverse health ailments.
