Crateva adansonii

Crateva adansonii, commonly known as the Sacred Crave, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, it is frequently used to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This flowering shrub holds significant cultural and healing value across tropical regions.

  • Scientific name: Crateva adansonii
  • Family: Capparaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales, it is a member of the family Capparaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Crateva.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its natural range also extends to Burkina and the Gambia. Additionally, the species is documented to occur within the borders of Ghana. Finally, its geographical presence includes the coastal nation of Guinea-Bissau.
