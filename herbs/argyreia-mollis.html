Argyreia mollis

Argyreiia mollis, commonly known as the Silver-leafed Argyreiia, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, this species is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices to treat various ailments, offering a natural approach to wellness through its potent, bioactive botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Argyreia mollis
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Argyreia, specifically as the species Argyreia mollis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted in the tropical environment of Hainan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing within Bangladesh. The Indo-China region also hosts the species, spanning across the Andaman Islands as well as the mainland territories of Cambodia and Laos. Together, these locations highlight the plant's wide range throughout Southeast Asia.
