Plant Parts

This plant, Xanthium spinosum, is characterized by its distinctive foliage, which includes both young and mature leaf structures. The leaves are typically arranged in an opposite pattern along the stem, providing a dense canopy that aids in photosynthesis. Each mature leaf is relatively large, often displaying a lanceolate or ovate shape with serrated margins, and serves as a key identifier for the species. These leaves play a vital role in the plant's physiological processes, contributing to the overall medicinal properties associated with its vegetative growth.
