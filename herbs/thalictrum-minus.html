Thalictrum minus

Thalictrum minus, commonly known as Lesser Celandine, is a perennial herbaceous plant characterized by its bright yellow, buttercup-like flowers. While often viewed as a garden escapee, it holds historical significance in traditional herbalism. This plant is studied for its unique phytochemical properties and potential medicinal applications in various cultures.
