Smyrnium cordifolium

Smyrnium cordifolium, commonly known as the heart-leaved burnet, is a distinctive perennial herb native to various Mediterranean and Asian regions. Renowned for its unique cordate leaves and umbelliferous flowers, this plant holds significant botanical interest, offering potential medicinal properties often explored in traditional herbal remedies and pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Smyrnium cordifolium
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Smyrnium cordifolium, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae and falls under the genus Smyrnium.
