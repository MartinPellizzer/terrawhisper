Shrubby Dillenia, scientifically known as Dillenia suffruticosa, is a versatile tropical shrub celebrated for its striking, large white flowers and edible fruits. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable natural resource for treating various ailments and promoting overall wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia and the Indian Ocean. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is commonly found growing in both India and Sri Lanka. Its range extends into the Indo-China region, specifically inhabiting the landscapes of Cambodia. In the Malesia region, the species can be located on the island of Borneo. Finally, its presence reaches the Western Indian Ocean, where it is established in the Seychelles.
