Madagascar corkwood, scientifically known as Commiphora madagascariensis, is a remarkable medicinal plant endemic to Madagascar. Renowned for its resinous bark, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of this species are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments such as skin infections, inflammation, and digestive issues.
