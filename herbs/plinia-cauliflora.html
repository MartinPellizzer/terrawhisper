Plinia cauliflora

Jabuticaba

Plinia cauliflora, commonly known as Jabuticaba, is a distinctive Brazilian fruit tree celebrated for its unique cauliflorous habit, where purple, fleshy fruits grow directly on the trunk. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, these sweet, grape-like berries are highly valued in gourmet culinary uses and traditional medicinal practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Plinia cauliflora
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by its genus, Plinia, and its specific epithet, cauliflora.

Common Names

This plant, Plinia cauliflora, is known by various regional names depending on the language and location. In Southern Min, it is referred to as Chhiū-phû-tô, while in Simplified Chinese, it is called 拟爱神木 and in Traditional Chinese as 擬愛神木. In the Cantonese-speaking regions of Hong Kong and Taiwan, it is commonly known as 嘉寶果.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across several tropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its range extends into Central America, specifically within the borders of Honduras. In Western South America, the species can be found in Bolivia. Finally, within Brazil, it is widely distributed throughout both the Northeast and Southeast regions.
