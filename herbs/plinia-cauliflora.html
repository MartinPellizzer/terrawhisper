Plinia cauliflora · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (254 studies) · ★★★★★

Jabuticaba, scientifically known as Plinia cauliflora, is a unique Brazilian fruit tree celebrated for its cauliflorous habit, where dark, succulent berries grow directly from the trunk. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, this medicinal plant offers significant health benefits, including anti-inflammatory properties and support for metabolic wellness.
