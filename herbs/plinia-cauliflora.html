Jabuticaba

Plinia cauliflora · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (254 studies) · ★★★★★

Jabuticaba, scientifically known as Plinia cauliflora, is a unique Brazilian fruit tree celebrated for its cauliflorous habit, where dark, succulent berries grow directly from the trunk. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, this medicinal plant offers significant health benefits, including anti-inflammatory properties and support for metabolic wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by its genus, Plinia, and its specific epithet, cauliflora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across several tropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its range extends into Central America, specifically within the borders of Honduras. In Western South America, the species can be found in Bolivia. Finally, within Brazil, it is widely distributed throughout both the Northeast and Southeast regions.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Jabuticaba, possesses several components that are utilized in traditional practices. The glossy, dark green leaves are often studied for their antioxidant properties and potential therapeutic benefits. The seed, found within the pulp, is also noted for its medicinal value due to its high concentration of bioactive compounds. Additionally, the fruit peels are frequently used in various preparations, as they are rich in anthocyanins and other phytochemicals that contribute to the plant's overall medicinal profile.
