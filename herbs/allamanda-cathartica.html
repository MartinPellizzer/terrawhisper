Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in Gabon as well as throughout the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Moving toward the eastern side of the continent, it can be found within East Tropical Africa, specifically in Tanzania. The species also extends into South Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Angola. Finally, its range continues southward into Zimbabwe, completing its spread across various tropical zones.

Plant Parts

This plant, Allamanda cathartica, is widely recognized for its various components that possess distinct pharmacological properties. The leaf part is frequently utilized in traditional medicine due to its specific chemical constituents that can influence digestive and physiological processes. Additionally, the plant contains a milky latex that flows from its stems and injured tissues, which is known to contain potent compounds that can act as powerful purgatives, though it must be handled with caution due to its high toxicity.
