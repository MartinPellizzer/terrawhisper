Allamanda cathartica

Golden trumpet

Allamanda cathartica, commonly known as Golden Trumpet, is a vibrant tropical shrub prized for its brilliant yellow, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally used in various cultures for its potent purgative properties to treat digestive ailments and promote overall gastrointestinal health.

  • Scientific name: Allamanda cathartica
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It falls into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, specifically being a member of the family Apocynaceae. Within this family, it is categorized under the genus Allamanda.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. Moving toward the eastern side of the continent, it can be found within East Tropical Africa, specifically in Tanzania. The species also extends into South Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Angola. Finally, its range continues southward into Zimbabwe, completing its spread across various tropical zones.
