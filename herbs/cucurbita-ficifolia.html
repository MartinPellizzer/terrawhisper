Cucurbita ficifolia

Fig-leaf gourd

Cucurbita ficifolia, commonly known as the fig-leaf gourd or tayoté, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Central and South America. Renowned for its nutritional density, it offers antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used to support kidney health and digestion, this unique squash provides significant therapeutic potential in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cucurbita ficifolia
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Cucurbita.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Cucurbita ficifolia, is recognized by various regional names across different languages. In German, it is referred to as the Feigenblatt-Kürbis, while in French, it is commonly called Courge de Siam. Its historical and cultural roots are reflected in the Nahuatl name Chilacayohtli. Moving to Europe, it is known as the Vijgenbladpompoen in Dutch and is called Dynia figolistna in Polish.
