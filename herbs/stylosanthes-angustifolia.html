Stylosanthes angustifolia

Stylosanthes angustifolia, commonly known as narrow-leafed stylosanthe, is a resilient medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Often found in tropical regions, this plant is recognized for its diverse pharmacological properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects, making it a significant subject of interest in ethnobotanical research and natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Stylosanthes angustifolia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Stylosanthes as the species Stylosanthes angustifolia.
