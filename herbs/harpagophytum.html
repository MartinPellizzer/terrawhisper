Harpagophytum

Harpagophytum, commonly known as Devil's Claw, is a medicinal plant native to southern Africa. Renowned for its powerful anti-inflammatory properties, it is primarily used to alleviate joint pain and osteoarthritis symptoms. Extracted from its hooked tuberous roots, this botanical powerhouse offers a natural approach to managing chronic inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Harpagophytum
  • Family: Pedaliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Pedaliaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Harpagophytum.
