Devil Claws

Harpagophytum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (24 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Devil'aws, scientifically known as Harpagophytum, is a medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory properties, it is primarily used to alleviate joint pain and arthritis symptoms. This remarkable herb contains harpagoside, a bioactive compound that makes it a cornerstone of traditional and modern herbal medicine.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of Southern and South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Angola and Mozambique. Its presence extends further into the territories of Zambia and Zimbabwe. Additionally, this medicinal species is located within the borders of Botswana. These diverse locations highlight its specific ecological niche within the southern portion of the continent.
