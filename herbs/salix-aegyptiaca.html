Salix aegyptiaca

Salix aegyptiaca, commonly known as the Egyptian Willow, is a distinctive medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for containing natural salicin, it has historically been utilized to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and treat fevers. This resilient shrub offers valuable insights into traditional botanical healing and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Salix aegyptiaca
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is further categorized under the family Salicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Salix.
