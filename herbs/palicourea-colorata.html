Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the tropical regions of northern South America. Its range includes several distinct territories such as French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname. Furthermore, it can be found extending across the landscapes of Venezuela. Moving toward the western portion of the continent, the species also inhabits parts of Bolivia. Consequently, its natural habitat spans a significant longitudinal stretch of the South American continent.
