Stachyurus chinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Stachyurus chinensis?

Stachyurus chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows a specialized evolutionary lineage. The plant is situated under the order Crossosomatales and is the sole representative of the family Stachyuraceae. Within this unique family, it is categorized under the genus Stachyurus, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species chinensis.
