Eucalyptus cladocalyx

Eucalyptus cladocalyx, commonly known as the sugar gum, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Australia. Renowned for its aromatic, oil-rich leaves, it offers potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used in traditional remedies to alleviate respiratory congestion, this resilient species remains a vital resource in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus cladocalyx
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa
