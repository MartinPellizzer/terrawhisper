This plant, Berberis diaphana, is utilized in traditional medicinal practices primarily through the application of its dried barks. When these bark segments are harvested and properly dried, they retain essential bioactive compounds that are harnessed for their therapeutic properties. The use of dried barks allows for easier storage and standardized dosing in various decoctions and herbal preparations aimed at treating specific ailments.
