Plant Parts

This plant, Eryngium campestre, is characterized by several distinct morphological features that contribute to its medicinal profile. The leaves are typically stiff, prickly, and deeply lobed, growing in a basal rosette or along the stem to capture sunlight efficiently. Beneath the soil, the roots form a sturdy system that anchors the plant and serves as a storage organ for nutrients. Finally, the plant produces small seeds housed within its spiny, globe-like flower heads, which are essential for the species' reproduction and dispersal across its natural habitat.
