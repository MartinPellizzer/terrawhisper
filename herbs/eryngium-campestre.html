Eryngium campestre

Eryngium campestre, commonly known as field eryngo, is a hardy, perennial herb distinguished by its spiny, bluish-green foliage and striking, thistle-like flower heads. Native to various temperate regions, this botanical species is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, including its potential anti-inflammatory and diuretic effects.

  • Scientific name: Eryngium campestre
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eryngium.
