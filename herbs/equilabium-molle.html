Equilabium molle

Equilabium molle, commonly known as the soft Equilabium, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its various parts possess bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Understanding its phytochemical properties is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Equilabium molle
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Equilabium.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing in the specific state of Assam as well as throughout India. Its range also extends into the neighboring country of Bangladesh. Furthermore, the species is present in the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya. Finally, its geographical presence is well-documented within the borders of Nepal.
