Quercus ilex, scientifically known as Quercus ilex, is the evergreen Holm oak, a resilient Mediterranean species renowned for its medicinal properties. Rich in tannins and polyphenols, its bark and leaves offer potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and astringent qualities, making it a valuable resource in traditional herbal remedies and pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the holm oak, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties. While various aspects of the tree may be studied, the young leaves are particularly significant due to their concentrated bioactive compounds. These tender, emerging leaves are often harvested to prepare decoctions or extracts, utilized primarily for their astringent and anti-inflammatory qualities in treating various ailments.
