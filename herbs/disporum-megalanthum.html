Disporum megalanthum

What's the taxonomical classification of Disporum megalanthum?

Disporum megalanthum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Liliales. It is further categorized under the family Colchicaceae, which places it within the genus Disporum. Finally, the specific identification of this plant is completed by its species name, megalanthum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Disporum megalanthum has rhizomatous stems with lanceolate, parallel-veined leaves that grow in a single whorl of approximately six to eight leaflets per node. The foliage is characterized by an entire margin and a distinctively bright green color. Its flowers are relatively large for the genus, featuring white tepals that are often marked with subtle purple or reddish veins. These blossoms emerge from the leaf axils and typically possess six tepals arranged in two whorls. Following pollination, the plant produces small, fleshy berries that transition from green to a dark color upon ripening.
