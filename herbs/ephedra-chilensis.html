Ephedra chilensis

Ephedra chilensis, commonly known as the Chilean jointfir, is a resilient shrub native to the arid landscapes of Chile. Valued in ethnobotany, this species belongs to a genus renowned for containing bioactive alkaloids. It offers significant potential for pharmacological research, particularly in developing treatments for respiratory and cardiovascular conditions.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra chilensis
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ephedra chilensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Gnetidae within the order Ephedrales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ephedraceae and falls under the genus Ephedra.
