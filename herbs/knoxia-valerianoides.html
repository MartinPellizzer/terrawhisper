Knoxia valeerianoides belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. This species is further categorized into the family Rubiaceae, specifically within the genus Knoxia, and is identified by its unique species name, valerianoides.

According to a study published by "Journal of Asian natural products research", five new 9,10-anthraquinones (1-5) were isolated from an ethanol extract of the roots of Knoxia valerianoides. Spectroscopic analysis was used to determine their structures, including the absolute configuration of 1. Compounds 4 and 5 exhibited moderate activity against nitrogen oxide production in macrophages induced by lipopolysaccharide at a concentration of 10(-5) M. The inhibition ratios for these compounds were 50.4 ± 3.6% for compound 4 and 41.7 ± 2.1% for compound 5.
