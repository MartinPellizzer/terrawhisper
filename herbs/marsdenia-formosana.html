What's the taxonomical classification of Marsdenia formosana?

Marsdenia formosana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. At the class level, it is categorized under Equisetopsida, falling into the subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized within the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Specifically, this plant is part of the genus Marsdenia, and its unique taxonomic designation is completed by its species name, formosana.
