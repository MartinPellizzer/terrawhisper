What's the taxonomical classification of Diplazium chinense?

Diplazium chinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is organized under the order Polypodiales and falls within the family Aspleniaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Diplazium and the species chinense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Diplazium chinense has long-creeping rhizomes that are woody, scaly, and often form dense mats on the forest floor. Each pinna is arranged along a central rachis that can vary in color from light green to a brownish hue. The margins of the pinnules often feature small, irregular teeth or lobes that contribute to the plant's intricate shape. Small sori are located on the undersides of the leaves, typically positioned near the margins and protected by a thin indusium.
