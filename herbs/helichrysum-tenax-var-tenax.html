Helichrysum tenax var. tenax

Helichrysum tenax var. tenax, commonly known as a species of everlasting flower, is a resilient medicinal herb prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and skin-healing capabilities, making it a vital resource in traditional herbal medicine and modern aromatherapy.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum tenax var. tenax
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa
