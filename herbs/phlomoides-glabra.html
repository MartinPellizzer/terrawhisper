Phlomoides glabra

Phlomoides glabra, commonly known as smooth lady's mantle, is a perennial herb native to Eastern Europe and parts of Asia. Characterized by its velvety, lance-shaped leaves and delicate yellow blossoms, this plant holds significant botanical interest for its unique morphological features and potential applications within traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Phlomoides glabra
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further classified under the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Phlomoides.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within several key regions of Western Asia and the Caucasus. Within the Caucasus region, it can be found growing throughout the Transcaucasus area. Its distribution also extends significantly into Western Asia, particularly across the territory of Iran. Furthermore, the species is widely documented in the country of Türkiye. These specific locations define the natural habitat where Phlomoides glabra is typically located.
