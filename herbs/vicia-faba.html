Broad Bean

Vicia faba · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1378 studies) · ★★★★★

Broad Bean, scientifically known as Vicia faba, is a versatile legume valued for both nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in proteins, vitamins, and minerals, it serves as a potent dietary supplement. Traditionally, it has been utilized to support digestive health and provide essential nutrients to aid bodily recovery.

This plant, known scientifically as Vicia faba, possesses several distinct morphological features that contribute to its botanical profile. The structural integrity of the organism is provided by its upright stems, which support the foliage and transport fluids throughout the system.
