Vicia faba

Broad bean

Vicia faba, commonly known as the broad bean or fava bean, is a versatile legume celebrated for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in essential proteins, vitamins, and minerals, it has historically been used to support digestive health, boost energy levels, and provide vital nourishment in various traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Vicia faba
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Vicia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Vicia faba, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found throughout the areas of Czechia and Slovakia. Its reach extends into Southwestern Europe, where it inhabits the islands of the Baleares. Additionally, this medicinal plant is located in the regions of Corse and France.
