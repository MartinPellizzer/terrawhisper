Pteris

Pteris, commonly known as various fern species, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat skin inflammations, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments. This resilient genus continues to inspire modern pharmacological research into its therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Pteris
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification
