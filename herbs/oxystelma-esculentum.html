Oxystelma esculentum

What's the taxonomical classification of Oxystelma esculentum?

Oxystelma esculentum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Gentianaales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Oxystelma, and its species name, esculentum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

What are the pharmacological activities of Oxystelma esculentum?

This plant has significant pharmacological activities including anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, anti-diabetic, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Research indicates that its bioactive compounds work effectively to neutralize free radicals and reduce oxidative stress in biological systems. These antioxidant qualities contribute to its potential in managing chronic inflammatory conditions throughout the body. Furthermore, the plant exhibits strong antimicrobial and antibacterial actions that can inhibit the growth of various pathogenic microorganisms. Its anti-diabetic effects also suggest a therapeutic role in regulating blood glucose levels and managing metabolic dysfunction.

According to a study published by "Scientific reports", Oxystelma esculentum (L.f.) Sm. extract was used for the green synthesis of zinc oxide nanoparticles which exhibited various biological activities. These nanoparticles showed antifungal inhibition rates of 72.3% against Aspergillus niger, 72.2% against Penicillium notatum, and 69.3% against Fusarium oxysporum at 4 mg/mL. The antibacterial activity peaked at a 13.9 mm inhibition zone at 8 mg/mL, while the highest antileishmania l activity was 57.33% at 160 µg/mL. They demonstrated 79% anti-inflammatory activity at 800 µg/mL and 63.11 ± 0.22% alpha-amylase inhibition at 400 µg/mL. Furthermore, the particles showed antioxidant activities including 51.7 ± 1.2% TAC, 42.4 ± 0.02% TRP, and 55.6 ± 1.4% FRSA at 200 µg/mL.

According to a study published by "Scientific reports", medicinal salt-tolerant Oxystelma esculentum (L.f.) Sm. extract was used for the green synthesis of zinc oxide nanoparticles. These phytofabricated ZnO-NPs contained zinc at 60.4% and oxygen at 22% and featured a particle size of 13 nm. The preparation demonstrated 79% anti-inflammatory activity at 800 µg/mL and 63.11 ± 0.22% alpha-amylase inhibition at 400 µg/mL. Furthermore, the nanoparticles showed 17.66 ± 0.55% α-glucosidase inhibition at 400 µg/mL and antioxidant activities including 51.7 ± 1.2% TAC, 42.4 ± 0.02% TRP, and 55.6 ± 1.4% FRSA at 200 µg/mL. The study also noted a low hemolytic activity of 1.41 ± 0.22% at 200 µg/mL.
