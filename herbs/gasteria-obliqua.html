Gasteria obliqua

Gasteria obliqua, commonly known as the oblique gastaria, is a fascinating succulent native to specific arid regions. Characterized by its unique leaf structure and resilient nature, this plant is valued in ethnobotany for its potential medicinal properties, offering intriguing insights into natural healing and traditional herbal remedies worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Gasteria obliqua
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asphodelaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gasteria, with the specific species being Gasteria obliqua.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several continents. In Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Spain in the southwestern region. Moving to the Southern Hemisphere, it can be found inhabiting the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. The species also has a significant presence in Mexico, where it is distributed across the central territories. Finally, its range extends to the northeast and southwest regions of Mexico, completing its broad global footprint.
