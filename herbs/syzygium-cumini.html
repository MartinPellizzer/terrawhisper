Syzygium cumini

Syzygium cumini, commonly known as the Cuminia or Cuminia tree, is a rare medicinal plant indigenous to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential through its unique bioactive compounds and antioxidant capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium cumini
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtales, and the family Myrtaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Syzygium.
