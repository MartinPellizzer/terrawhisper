Emory's barrel cactus, scientifically known as Ferocactus echidne, is a resilient succulent native to the arid Southwestern deserts. Renowned for its cylindrical shape and protective spines, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Historically, indigenous cultures utilized its moisture-rich interior and specialized properties for various traditional medicinal applications.
