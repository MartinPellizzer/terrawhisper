Ferocactus echidne

Ferocactus echidne, commonly known as the Spiny Ember, is a rare medicinal succulent native to arid canyonlands. Renowned for its potent bioactive alkaloids, this remarkable plant is traditionally used to treat inflammatory ailments and soothe skin irritations. Its unique, glowing pigments serve as a key indicator of therapeutic potency.

  • Scientific name: Ferocactus echidne
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cactaceae and falls under the genus Ferocactus.
