Samanea saman

Samanea saman, commonly known as the Rain Tree, is a majestic tropical legume prized for its expansive, umbrella-like canopy. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant medicinal value, with various parts utilized in traditional practices to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues through diverse bioactive compounds.
