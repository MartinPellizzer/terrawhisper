Astragalus thracicus subsp. thracicus

Astragalus thracicus subsp. thracicus, commonly known as a rare medicinal milkvetch, is a specialized plant indigenous to specific Balkan landscapes. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, it possesses unique bioactive compounds. This subspecies is increasingly studied for its potential therapeutic properties, offering promising insights into natural pharmacology and regional biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus thracicus subsp. thracicus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling within the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Astragalus as the subspecies Astragalus thracicus subsp. thracicus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions of Southeastern Europe and Western Asia. Within Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in the country of Greece. Additionally, the species inhabits the European portion of Türkiye. Its range also extends into the Western Asian territories of Türkiye. Consequently, its habitat is concentrated within these distinct Mediterranean and Anatolian landscapes.
