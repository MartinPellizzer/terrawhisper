Quercus spinosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Quercus spinosa?

Quercus spinosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Fagales. As a member of the family Fagaceae, it is categorized under the genus Quercus, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species spinosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

The plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that eventually develop into tiny, nut-like acorns nestled within cupules.
