Many-flowered marsh-pennywort, scientifically known as Hydrocotyle umbellata, is a creeping perennial herb found in wetlands globally. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its unique umbrella-like leaves and medicinal versatility make it a significant botanical specimen.
