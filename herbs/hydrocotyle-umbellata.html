Hydrocotyle umbellata

Hydrocotyle umbellata, commonly known as Umbrella Pennywort, is a versatile aquatic perennial valued in traditional medicine. Thriving in wetlands, this plant possesses diverse bioactive properties, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic applications in treating various ailments, offering significant botanical and pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Hydrocotyle umbellata
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Apiales and the family Araliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hydrocotyle, specifically as the species Hydrocotyle umbellata.
