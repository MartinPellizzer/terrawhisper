Heart-leaved globe daisy, scientifically known as Globularia cordifolia, is a charming perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Characterized by its distinct heart-shaped leaves and spherical blue flower heads, this resilient plant thrives in rocky terrains, offering unique aesthetic beauty and traditional medicinal potential in herbal studies.
