Hedyosmum racemosum

Hedyosmum racemosum, commonly known as the mountain berry, is a resilient shrub native to high-altitude regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. Its berries and foliage are studied for potential therapeutic applications, offering a fascinating glimpse into the medicinal richness of alpine ecosystems and biodiversity.

  • Family: Chloranthaceae
