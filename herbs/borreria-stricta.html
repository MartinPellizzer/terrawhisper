What's the taxonomical classification of Borreria stricta?

Borreria stricta belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Gentianales and is a member of the family Rubiaceae. Finally, its specific biological classification is defined by the genus Borreria and the species stricta.
