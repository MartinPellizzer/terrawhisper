Cakile maritima

Cakile maritima, commonly known as sea rocket, is a resilient succulent herb native to coastal sandy shores. Renowned for its ability to thrive in saline environments, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. It contains bioactive compounds often studied for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties in traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Cakile maritima
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales, falling within the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cakile.
