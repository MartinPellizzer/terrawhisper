Nuphar lutea · Accepted scientific name

Yellow water-lily, scientifically known as Nuphar lutea, is an aquatic perennial prized for its striking golden blooms. Historically used in traditional medicine, its rhizomes possess anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Often found in slow-moving waters, this resilient plant offers both ecological beauty and significant therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the yellow water-lily, possesses various components that are studied for their bioactive properties. While much of the medicinal interest lies in the complex chemical profile of the species, specifically the presence of alkaloids known as nupharidines, these compounds are distributed throughout different tissues of the plant. These nitrogenous substances are primarily concentrated in the rhizomes and roots, which are often the parts utilized in traditional preparations to exploit their pharmacological potential.
