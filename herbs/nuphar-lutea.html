Nuphar lutea

Yellow water-lily

Nuphar lutea, commonly known as the yellow water-lily, is a perennial aquatic plant found in slow-moving waters. Historically valued in traditional medicine, its rhizomes contain bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and digestive issues. This resilient species plays a vital role in wetland ecosystems and herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Nuphar lutea
  • Family: Nymphaeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Nymphaeaales and the family Nymphaeaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Nuphar, which includes the species Nuphar lutea.
