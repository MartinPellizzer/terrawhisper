What's the taxonomical classification of Tylophora kerrii?

Tylophora kerrii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Gentianaales. Specifically, this plant is a member of the family Apocynaceae, falling under the genus Tylophora, and is identified by the specific epithet kerrii.
