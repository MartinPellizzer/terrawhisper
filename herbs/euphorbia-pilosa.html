Euphorbia pilosa

Euphorbia pilosa, commonly known as hairy spurge, is a versatile annual herb found in tropical and subtropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is widely utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, serving as a valuable natural resource in folk remedies.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia pilosa
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and falls under the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a remarkably wide and fragmented geographical distribution across different continents. In the Western Indian Ocean, its presence is noted specifically around the Mozambique Channel Islands. Moving to the northern reaches of Asia, it can be found within the vast territories of Siberia. Within the Siberian region, its range includes the Altai mountains as well as the Irkutsk area. Furthermore, the species extends its reach to the Krasnoyarsk and Tuva regions.
