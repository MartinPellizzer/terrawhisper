Aster indicus

Aster indicus, commonly known as Indian Aster, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its striking composite flowers, this plant offers diverse therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits, serving as a vital natural resource in various herbal healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Aster indicus
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical presence across various regions of China. It can be found growing within the landscapes of China South-Central. Additionally, its distribution extends to the tropical environment of Hainan. The species is also documented in the northern reaches of China Inner Mongolia. Finally, its range encompasses the territories of China North-Central and China Southeast.
