Selaginella pulvinata

Selaginella pulvinaata, commonly known as the cushion moss spike-moss, is a resilient lycophyte prized for its unique desiccation-tolerance capabilities. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant is studied for its physiological mechanisms, offering profound insights into survival strategies that could potentially inspire advancements in botanical medicine and drought-resistant biotechnology.

  • Scientific name: Selaginella pulvinata
  • Family: Selaginellaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Selaginella pulvinaata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Lycopodiidae, it is classified under the order Selaginellales and the family Selaginellaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Selaginella.
