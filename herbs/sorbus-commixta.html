Sorbus commixta

Sorbus commixta, commonly known as the hybrid serviceberry, is a versatile deciduous shrub cherished for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Native to East Asia, its berries are rich in antioxidants. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in folk remedies to support overall wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Sorbus commixta
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Sorbus commixta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the Rosaceae family within the genus Sorbus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across various parts of Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its range extends to the Kuril Islands and Sakhalin. The species is also well-established within China, specifically in the South-Central and Southeast regions. Furthermore, its presence is noted throughout Japan, rounding out its regional coverage. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's significant footprint across the northeastern Asian landscape.
