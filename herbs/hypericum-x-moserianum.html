Hypericum × moserianum

Hypericum × moserianum, commonly known as Moserian's St. John's Wort, is a fascinating hybrid plant. While less famous than its parent species, it belongs to a genus renowned for therapeutic properties. This resilient perennial offers unique botanical interest, bridging the gap between ornamental beauty and traditional herbal medicinal applications.
