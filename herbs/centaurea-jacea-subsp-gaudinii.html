Centaurea jacea subsp. gaudinii

Centaurea jacea subsp. gaudinii, commonly known as a specialized subspecies of knapweed, is a rare botanical treasure. Renowned for its unique morphological traits, this medicinal plant holds significant ecological value. Its traditional uses highlight its potential pharmacological importance, offering a fascinating subject for studying natural compounds and biodiversity conservation.

  • Family: Asteraceae

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
