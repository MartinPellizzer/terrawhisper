What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Litsea veitchiana has elliptical to oblong leaves that are typically 5 to 12 centimeters long, featuring entire margins and prominent pinnate venation with distinctively curved secondary veins. Small, inconspicuous flowers are arranged in axillary panicles, which eventually give rise to small, fleshy drupes.
