Vicia japonica

What's the taxonomical classification of Vicia japonica?

Vicia japonica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Fabales. As a member of the family Fabaceae, it is further categorized into the genus Vicia, ultimately reaching its specific identification as the species japonica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Vicia japonica has slender, climbing stems that emerge from a taproot and support pinnately compound leaves with five to ten pairs of small, oval leaflets. Each leaflet is accompanied by a terminal tendril that facilitates climbing through nearby vegetation. The plant produces small, papilionaceous flowers that are typically pale purple or violet in color. These flowers are borne in small clusters or solitary along the stem. The fruit consists of small, cylindrical pods that contain several tiny, dark seeds.
