Plant Parts

This plant, Begonia urophylla, is primarily recognized for its distinctive and visually striking leaf, which serves as a key identifying feature of the species. The leaf structure is characterized by an asymmetrical, heart-shaped base and deep, rhythmic lobes that create a textured, almost sculptural appearance. Beyond its ornamental value, the leaf is the most significant part of the plant used in traditional medicinal applications, often utilized for its specific biological properties.
