Frankenia laevis

Frankenia laevis, commonly known as smooth frankenia, is a resilient halophytic shrub native to coastal regions. Characterized by its small, fleshy leaves and delicate flowers, this plant thrives in saline soils. It holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, utilized for its unique bioactive compounds and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Frankenia laevis
  • Family: Frankeniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Frankenia laevis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Caryophyllales. It is a member of the family Frankeniaceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Frankenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a scattered geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence can be identified within Great Britain. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it is found in various parts of Southwestern Europe. Specifically, it inhabits the islands of Baleares and Corse.
