Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct Asian regions. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the borders of India. Furthermore, the species is distributed across the Indo-China region.
