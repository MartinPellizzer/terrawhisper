Hawthorn, scientifically known as Crataeagus, is a resilient flowering shrub renowned for its profound cardiovascular benefits. Long utilized in traditional medicine, it is primarily celebrated for supporting heart health, regulating blood pressure, and improving circulation. This versatile botanical serves as a cornerstone in natural remedies for strengthening the heart.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as hawthorn, offers various components used in traditional medicine. The vibrant flowers often bloom in clusters during the spring, and observing the leaves with flowers can help in the correct identification of the species in the wild. While the botanical classification refers to the fleshy fruit produced by the tree, these are often colloquially described as small berries due to their appearance. Additionally, the medicinal properties of the plant can be found in the leaves, which are frequently studied for their bioactive compounds.
