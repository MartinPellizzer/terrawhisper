Crataegus

Hawthorn

Crataegus, commonly known as hawthorn, is a versatile medicinal shrub cherished for centuries in traditional herbalism. Renowned primarily for its profound cardiovascular benefits, it supports heart health by improving circulation and regulating blood pressure. Its nutrient-rich berries and flowers offer potent antioxidant properties, promoting overall vitality and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Crataegus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Crataegus, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Polish, it is called głóg, while in Scottish Gaelic, it is known as Sgitheach. Moving to different linguistic traditions, it is called Hodar in Lebanese, Долонно in Kyrgyz, and སྐྱུ་རུ་དམན་པ། in Tibetan.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within Northern Europe. Specifically, its presence can be noted throughout the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. It also grows naturally within the expansive landscapes of Finland. Furthermore, the species is commonly found across the islands of Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, its geographical range extends into the rugged terrains of Norway.
