Thaumatococcus daniellii

Thaumatococcus daniellii, commonly known as the Katemfe plant, is a versatile tropical shrub native to West Africa. Renowned for its miracle berries, it serves as a natural, intense sweetener. Beyond culinary uses, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering traditional medicinal properties used to treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Thaumatococcus daniellii
  • Family: Marantaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, it falls under the family Marantaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Thaumatococcus.
