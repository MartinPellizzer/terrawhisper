Arbutus menziei, scientifically known as Arbustus menziei, is commonly called Mandrake Laurel or Madrona. This evergreen shrub is native to the Pacific Coast, valued for its striking reddish bark. Traditionally, various parts of the plant have been utilized in folk medicine for their potential antiseptic and astringent properties.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as kinnikinnick, possesses various components that contribute to its medicinal profile, though the bark is a notable part of its structure. While the evergreen leaves are most frequently utilized in herbal preparations for their astringent properties, the bark and twigs can also be harvested for use in traditional remedies. The bark typically exhibits a reddish-brown hue and serves as a protective layer for the woody stems, containing various compounds that characterize the species.
