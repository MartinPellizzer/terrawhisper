Arbutus menziei, scientifically known as Arbustus menziei, is commonly called Mandrake Laurel or Madrona. This evergreen shrub is native to the Pacific Coast, valued for its striking reddish bark. Traditionally, various parts of the plant have been utilized in folk medicine for their potential antiseptic and astringent properties.
