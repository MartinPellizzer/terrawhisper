Ducrosia flabellifolia

Ducrosia flabellifolia, commonly known as the fan-leafed Ducrosia, is a remarkable medicinal shrub native to arid African landscapes. Renowned for its unique morphological features, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Its various parts are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat diverse ailments, offering promising therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Ducrosia flabellifolia
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ducrosia, specifically as the species Ducrosia flabellifolia.
