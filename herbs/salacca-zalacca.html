Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, it can be found growing in Vietnam. Its presence extends significantly into Malesia, where it inhabits the island of Borneo. It is also widely distributed throughout the Indonesian archipelago, specifically in Jawa and the Lesser Sunda Islands. Finally, the species is commonly documented across the Malay Peninsula in Malaya.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Salacca zalacca or salak, is highly valued for its unique medicinal and nutritional properties, particularly regarding its fruit. The fruit is characterized by its distinctive reddish-brown, scaly skin that resembles snake skin, protecting the sweet, acidic flesh inside. Beyond its culinary use, the various components of the fruit, including the pulp and the peel, are often utilized in traditional medicine for their antioxidant and astringent qualities, helping to support digestive health and overall wellness.
