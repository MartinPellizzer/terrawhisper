Sageretia thea

Sageretia thea, commonly known as the golden sage, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its potent therapeutic properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory capabilities, this botanical treasure is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices to support digestive health, boost immunity, and promote overall holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Sageretia thea
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Sageretia thea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rhamnaceae and the genus Sageretia.
