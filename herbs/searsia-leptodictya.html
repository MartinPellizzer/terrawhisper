Mountain karee, scientifically known as Searsia leptodictya, is a resilient, evergreen shrub native to South Africa. Renowned for its tough, leathery leaves, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts are utilized in herbal remedies to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues and inflammation.
