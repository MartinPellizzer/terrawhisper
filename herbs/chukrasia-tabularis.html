Chukrasia tabularis

Chukrasia tabularis, commonly known as the Himalayan Chukrasi, is a resilient medicinal herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and digestive ailments. This plant serves as a vital natural resource for holistic healing in mountainous ecosystems worldwide.
