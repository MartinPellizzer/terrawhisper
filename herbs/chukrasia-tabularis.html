Distribution

This plant, Chukrasiia tabularis, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central provinces and the Southeast regions. Its range also extends to the tropical island of Hainan and the high-altitude terrains of Tibet. Beyond China, the species is naturally distributed within the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it is known to grow in the lush landscapes of Assam.
