Rheum palmatum

Rheum palmatum, commonly known as Tibetan rhubarb, is a powerhouse of traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent purgative properties, its roots contain anthraquinones that effectively treat constipation and digestive disorders. Beyond digestion, it is valued for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects, making it a vital botanical in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Rheum palmatum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rheum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Europe, its presence is noted within the borders of Great Britain. Moving toward Asia, it is widely distributed throughout various parts of China. Specifically, it can be found in China South-Central, China North-Central, and the Qinghai province. Additionally, its range extends into the Inner Mongolia region of China.
