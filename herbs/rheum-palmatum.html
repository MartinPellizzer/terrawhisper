Rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum palmatum, is a potent medicinal herb prized in traditional practices for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its high anthraquinone content, it serves as a powerful natural laxative. Beyond digestive health, this perennial plant offers diverse applications in treating inflammation and supporting metabolic functions.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Rheum palmatum, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its underground structures. While many plants utilize various organs, the therapeutic focus for this species is concentrated on the roots. Specifically, practitioners often refer to the harvested material as rhubarb roots, which are harvested to extract key bioactive compounds. Although some species in the genus may produce seeds, it is the thick, fleshy rhizomes and the extensive root system that serve as the primary source for pharmacological applications.
