Hemionitis tenuifolia

Hemioniis tenuifolia, commonly known as slender-leaf hemionitis, is a distinctive medicinal fern valued in traditional herbal practices. Characterized by its delicate, fine-textured foliage, this plant possesses unique bioactive compounds. It is primarily studied for its potential therapeutic properties, offering promising insights into natural pharmacology and holistic wellness treatments.

  • Scientific name: Hemionitis tenuifolia
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Polypodiinae and order Polypodiales, it is situated in the family Pteridaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Hemionitis.
