What's the taxonomical classification of Asparagus myriacanthus?

Asparagus myriacanthus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Asparagales. Furthermore, this plant is a member of the family Asparagaceae, which contains the genus Asparagus, eventually leading to its specific designation as the species myriacanthus.
