Trifolium subterraneum

Subterranean clover

Trifolium subterraneum, commonly known as subterranean clover, is a versatile annual legume valued for its restorative properties. Beyond its role in soil enrichment through nitrogen fixation, it holds medicinal potential in traditional practices. Its bioactive compounds offer therapeutic possibilities, making it a subject of interest in botanical pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Trifolium subterraneum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is situated in the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Trifolium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Trifolium subterraneum, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Persian, it is called شبدر زیرزمینی, while in French, it is known as Trèfle souterrain. In Arabic, the name used is نفل مطمور, and in German, it is identified as Bodenfrüchtiger Klee. Finally, in Dutch, the plant is commonly called Onderaardse klaver.
