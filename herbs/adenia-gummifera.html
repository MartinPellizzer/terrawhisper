Adenia gummifera

Adenia gummifera, commonly known as the gum adenia, is a fascinating succulent climber native to arid regions of Africa. Characterized by its unique tuberous roots and distinctive climbing habit, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its bioactive compounds, which are studied for their diverse therapeutic and pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Adenia gummifera
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales and the family Passifloraceae, it is classified under the genus Adenia. Specifically, the species is identified as Adenia gummifera.

This plant is primarily distributed across the various regions of Northeast and East Tropical Africa.
