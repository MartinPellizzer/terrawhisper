This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Rosales, it falls into the family Moraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Morus and the variety macroura.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories, as well as in the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species is also documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh. Consequently, its habitat spans a diverse range of climates from Southeast Asian tropics to the Tibetan plateau.
