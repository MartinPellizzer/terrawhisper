Zingiber purpureum

Zingiber purpureum, commonly known as purple ginger, is a remarkable medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Renowned for its vibrant foliage and therapeutic properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This aromatic herb offers significant antioxidant potential, making it a valuable subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Zingiber purpureum
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
