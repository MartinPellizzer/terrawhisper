Cassumunar Ginger, scientifically known as Zingiber purpureum, is a perennial herb native to Southeast Asia. Distinguished by its striking purple bracts, this aromatic plant belongs to the Zingiberaceae family. Highly valued in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a significant botanical resource for wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Zingiber purpureum, is valued primarily for its medicinal properties derived from its underground structures. The most significant part used for therapeutic purposes is the rhizomes, which contain a concentrated amount of bioactive compounds. Whether a practitioner utilizes a single rhizome or a collection of rhizomes, the primary goal is often to extract the potent essential oil found within the tissue. This oil is responsible for much of the plant's aromatic and pharmacological efficacy.
