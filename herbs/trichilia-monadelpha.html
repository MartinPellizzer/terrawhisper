Trichilia monadelpha

Trichilia monadelpha, commonly known as the "Wild Mahogany," is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and roots possess potent bioactive properties. These components are frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from skin infections to complex inflammatory conditions.
