Euphorbia greenwayi

Euphorbia greenwayi, commonly known as a succulent member of the Euphorbiaceae family, is a fascinating medicinal plant valued for its unique biological properties. Native to specific arid regions, this species contains bioactive compounds that offer therapeutic potential, making it a subject of significant interest in traditional and modern pharmacology.
