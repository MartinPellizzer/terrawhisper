Woolly foxglove, scientifically known as Digitalis lanaata, is a striking perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Characterized by its densely pubescent, velvety leaves and bell-shaped flowers, this potent medicinal plant contains cardiac glycosides, historically utilized for treating heart conditions and regulating rhythmic palpitations.

Plant Parts

This plant, Digitalis lanata, is a significant medicinal species known for its therapeutic properties derived from specific components. The primary source of its active glycosides is the leaves, which are carefully harvested at the correct developmental stage to ensure potency. Whether used as a whole leaf or processed into a fine dried leaf powder, these parts are essential in the preparation of various cardiac medications. Using the dried leaf powder allows for more precise dosing and easier integration into pharmaceutical formulations, making the processed leaves indispensable in modern medicine.
