Veronica officinalis

Veronica officinalis, commonly known as Germander, is a perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its soothing effects on the respiratory and digestive systems, this plant has long been utilized to support mucosal health, offering natural remedies for coughs, inflammation, and various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Veronica officinalis
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae, it is classified under the genus Veronica.
