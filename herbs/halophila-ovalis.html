Halophila ovalis

Halophila ovalis, commonly known as seagrass, is a vital marine angiosperm found in tropical shallow waters. Beyond its ecological role in stabilizing seabed sediments and providing habitats, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. Research explores its bioactive compounds for their antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Halophila ovalis
  • Family: Hydrocharitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Alismatales, it is situated in the family Hydrocharitaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Halophila.
