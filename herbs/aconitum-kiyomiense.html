Aconitum kiyomiense

Aconitum kiyomiense, commonly known as the Kiyomi monkshood, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. While prized for its striking blue flowers, it contains potent alkaloids. In traditional medicine, it is carefully processed to mitigate toxicity, serving as a powerful agent for addressing pain and circulatory issues.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum kiyomiense
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Ranunculales, specifically within the family Ranunculaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Aconitum.
