Plant Parts

This plant, Aconitum kiyomiense, is a species known for its distinctive morphological features, particularly within its underground structures. The most significant medicinal part of the plant is its tuberous roots, which are thickened, fleshy organs used for nutrient storage. In many species of the Aconitum genus, these tuberous roots contain potent alkaloids, making them the primary focus of pharmacological interest and traditional medicinal use, though they require careful processing due to their high toxicity.
