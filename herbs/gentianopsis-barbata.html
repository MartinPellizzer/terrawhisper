Gentianopsis barbata

Gentianopsis barbaata, commonly known as the Bearded Gentian, is a striking perennial herb native to the high-altitude alpine meadows of the Himalayas. Renowned for its brilliant blue, star-shaped flowers, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional practices for its potent bitter compounds, used to support digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Gentianopsis barbata
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Gentianopsis, where it is specifically known as Gentianopsis barbaata.
