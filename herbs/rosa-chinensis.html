Rosa indica, scientifically known as Rosa chinensis, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential oils, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, reduce inflammation, and promote cardiovascular health. This resilient species remains a cornerstone in both herbal remedies and aromatherapy.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Rosa chinensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling within the family Rosaceae. Ultimately, this medicinal species is identified by its genus, Rosa.

Plant Parts

This plant, Rosa chinensis, is highly valued in traditional medicine, where the flower serves as a primary medicinal component. These blossoms are often harvested when they are in full bloom to capture their essential aromatic compounds and bioactive constituents. In various therapeutic practices, the flower is utilized to help regulate blood circulation, soothe the liver, and alleviate emotional stagnation, making it a versatile element in herbal formulations.
