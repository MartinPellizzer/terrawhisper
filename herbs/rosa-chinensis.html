Rosa indica, scientifically known as Rosa chinensis, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential oils, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, reduce inflammation, and promote cardiovascular health. This resilient species remains a cornerstone in both herbal remedies and aromatherapy.
