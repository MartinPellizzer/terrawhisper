Euphorbia drummondii

Euphorbia drummondii, commonly known as Drummond's spurge, is a resilient succulent native to arid regions. Distinguished by its unique milky latex and specialized structures, this medicinal plant has historically been utilized in traditional folk medicine. Understanding its chemical properties offers intriguing possibilities for future pharmacological research and therapeutic applications.
