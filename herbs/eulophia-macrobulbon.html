Eulophia macrobulbon

Eulophia macrobulbon, commonly known as the large-bulbous eulophia, is a terrestrial orchid species native to various tropical regions. While primarily valued by botanists for its distinct floral structure, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest. In traditional practices, parts of this plant are occasionally utilized for their potential medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Eulophia macrobulbon
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
