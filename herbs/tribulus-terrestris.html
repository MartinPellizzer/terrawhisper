Tribulus terrestris

Devil's thorn

Tribulus terrestris, commonly known as puncturevine or goat's head, is a resilient medicinal herb widely used in traditional Ayurvedic and Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potential to support hormonal balance, reproductive health, and vitality, this plant offers diverse bioactive compounds that make it a staple in modern herbal supplementation.

  • Scientific name: Tribulus terrestris
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zygophyllales, eventually placing it in the family Zygophyllaceae. Within this hierarchy, the medicinal species is identified under the genus Tribulus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Middle Europe, its presence is well-documented in countries such as Austria and Germany. It can also be found extending through the territories of Czechia-Slovakia and Hungary. Furthermore, the species reaches into Southwestern Europe, specifically inhabiting the Baleares. This widespread occurrence highlights the adaptability of the species across different European climates.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as puncture vine, exhibits distinct morphological features across its various structures. The stem typically grows prostrate along the ground, forming a spreading mat that allows the plant to colonize open spaces. From these stems, compound leaves emerge, consisting of several small, green leaflets that are arranged symmetrically. Each individual leaf is designed to maximize sunlight absorption in arid environments. As the plant matures, it produces small, fleshy fruits that are characterized by their hard, woody exterior covered in sharp spines. This unique structure of the fruit serves as a highly effective mechanism for seed dispersal, as the spines easily latch onto passing animals or human footwear.
