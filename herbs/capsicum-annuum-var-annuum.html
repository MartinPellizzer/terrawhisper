Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is also established within the borders of Germany. Its presence continues through the central regions of Austria. Finally, the distribution extends to the area encompassing Czechia-Slovakia.
