Quillaja saponaria

Quillaja saponaria, commonly known as the soapbark tree, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to Chile. Renowned for its high saponin content, the bark produces a natural foaming agent used in traditional remedies and modern pharmacology. It offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly for respiratory health and immune support.

  • Scientific name: Quillaja saponaria
  • Family: Quillajaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Fabales and the family Quillajaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Quillaja.
