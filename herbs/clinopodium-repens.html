Clinopodium repens

What's the taxonomical classification of Clinopodium repens?

Clinopodium repens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales. This plant is a member of the family Lamiaceae, falling under the genus Clinopodium, and is specifically identified by the species epithet repens.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Clinopodium repens has quadrangular stems that reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters, bearing opposite, ovate leaves with serrated margins and a slightly hairy texture. The foliage is typically dark green, while the small, tubular flowers are arranged in dense, terminal whorls. These blossoms are predominantly pink or purplish and emerge from the leaf axils in compact clusters. The plant's reproductive structures consist of tiny nutlets that develop after successful pollination. Overall, the morphology is characterized by its compact, creeping habit and aromatic qualities.
