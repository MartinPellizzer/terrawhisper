Albuca bracteata

Pregnant onion

Albuca bracteata, commonly known as the Fluff Cup, is a captivating ornamental plant prized for its unique, velvety blooms. While primarily grown for its aesthetic charm in tropical landscapes, its distinct botanical features offer fascinating study for enthusiasts exploring the diverse and beautiful world of specialized flowering plant species.

  • Scientific name: Albuca bracteata
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, falling into the family Asparagaceae. Ultimately, its scientific identity is defined by its placement within the genus Albuca.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Albuca bracteata, is referred to by various common names depending on the language. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several continents and oceanic regions. In Europe, its presence is noted within the Mediterranean landscapes of Spain. Its reach extends into Northern Africa, where it can be found growing in Morocco. Within the Southern African region, it inhabits both the Cape Provinces and the KwaZulu-Natal area. Finally, its range reaches the Middle Atlantic Ocean on the remote island of St. Helena.
