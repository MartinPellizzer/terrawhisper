Graptophyllum pictum

Graptophyllum pictum, commonly known as the Justicia or Aphelandra plant, is a striking ornamental shrub valued for its colorful, patterned leaves. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this tropical perennial holds significant medicinal importance in traditional practices, often utilized for its diverse bioactive properties and potential therapeutic applications in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Graptophyllum pictum
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further categorized under the family Acanthaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Graptophyllum, specifically as the species Graptophyllum pictum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the nations of Ghana, Guinea, and Sierra Leone. Its presence extends further into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Cameroon. Additionally, the species reaches the Western Indian Ocean, where it is documented in Mauritius. This wide range demonstrates the plant's ability to thrive in diverse tropical environments.
