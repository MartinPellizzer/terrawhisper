Plumeria obtusa

Singapore graveyard flower

Plumeria obtusa, commonly known as the Singapore plumeria, is a stunning tropical shrub celebrated for its fragrant, waxy blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this resilient plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its antiseptic and anti-inflammatory properties to treat skin ailments and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Plumeria obtusa
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Plumeria obtusata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apocynaceae and falls under the genus Plumeria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In the Middle East, it can be found throughout the Gulf States of the Arabian Peninsula. Its presence in China is extensive, spanning the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Furthermore, the species extends into the Southwestern Pacific, specifically inhabiting the Gilbert Islands. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse maritime and continental environments.
