Reproduction

Reproduction in Plumeria obtusa is facilitated through both sexual and asexual mechanisms, with its sexual cycle heavily reliant on specialized dispersal strategies. The plant produces fragrant, tubular flowers designed to attract pollinators, typically moths or butterflies, which aid in cross-pollination. Following successful fertilization, the plant develops elongated, slender seed pods that eventually split open to release numerous small, lightweight seeds.
