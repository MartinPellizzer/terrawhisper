Callicarpa japonica

Callicarpa japonica, commonly known as Japanese beautyberry, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its striking clusters of vibrant purple berries, this ornamental plant offers significant medicinal potential. Traditionally used in folk remedies, it possesses bioactive properties that support various therapeutic applications in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Callicarpa japonica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Lamiales and is situated in the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Callicarpa.
