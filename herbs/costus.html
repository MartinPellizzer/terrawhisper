Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the region of West Tropical Africa. Within this territory, it can be found growing in the nation of Benin. Its presence extends further to include the country of Burkina. Additionally, the species is documented throughout Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, the plant is also established within the borders of Guinea.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Costus, utilizes various structures for growth and survival, but the root is particularly significant in traditional herbal medicine. The rhizomatous root system serves as the plant's primary storage organ for nutrients and water, while also concentrating various bioactive compounds. In many therapeutic practices, these roots are harvested, dried, and processed to create decoctions or powders used to treat diverse ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions.
