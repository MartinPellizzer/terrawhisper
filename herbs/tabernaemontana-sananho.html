Tabernaemontana sananho

Tabernaemontana sananho, commonly known as the white rose periwinkle, is a medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in ethnobotanical practices to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
