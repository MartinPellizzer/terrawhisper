Galeola faberi

Galeola faberi, commonly known as the Faberi's Galeola, is a distinctive orchid species native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its intricate floral structures and unique aesthetic appeal, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices. Understanding its botanical characteristics and ecological role is essential for its conservation and study.

  • Scientific name: Galeola faberi
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Galeola faberi, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the Orchidaceae family, situated within the genus Galeola.
