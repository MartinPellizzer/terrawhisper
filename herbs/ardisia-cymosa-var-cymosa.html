Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, falling into the family Primulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ardisia, specifically as the variety cymosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted in both the South-Central and Southeast provinces. The species also extends into the Nansei-shoto islands within the Eastern Asia region. Furthermore, its range includes the island of Taiwan, marking another significant part of its Eastern Asian habitat. Finally, the distribution reaches into the Indo-China region, specifically documented in Thailand.
