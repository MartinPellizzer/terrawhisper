Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its natural range also extends into Burkina and the Gambia. Additionally, the plant is prevalent within the borders of Ghana. Finally, its distribution includes the nation of Guinea-Bissau.

This plant, commonly known as the Kola nut tree, is primarily valued for its medicinal and stimulant properties which are concentrated within its seed.
