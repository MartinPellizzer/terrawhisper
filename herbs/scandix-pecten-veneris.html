Shepherd's needle, scientifically known as Scandix pecten-veneris, is a delicate biennial herb characterized by its distinctive, comb-like seed pods. Often found in meadows and disturbed soils, this botanical specimen has been utilized historically in traditional medicine for its purported anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, though modern clinical research remains limited.
