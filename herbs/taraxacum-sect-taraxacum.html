Taraxacum sect. Taraxacum, is a prominent group of perennial herbs within the Asteraceae family, most famously represented by the common dandelion. Renowned for its bright yellow composite flowers, this botanical section holds significant medicinal value, utilized traditionally for its diuretic, digestive, and hepatic properties in various holistic healing practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Taraxacum sect. Taraxacum, possesses several distinct components that are utilized for various medicinal purposes. The deep, perennial root is often harvested for its bitter properties and tonic effects, while the vibrant yellow flowers are frequently used in infusions. The vegetative structure consists of a basal rosette of deeply lobed leaves, and each individual leaf contributes to the plant's overall nutrient profile. As the life cycle progresses, the plant produces a specialized seed dispersal mechanism, often referred to as a "clock," which allows the species to propagate effectively.
