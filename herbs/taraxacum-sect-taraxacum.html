Taraxacum sect. Taraxacum

Taraxacum sect. Taraxacum, commonly known as the common dandelion, is a resilient perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its multifaceted therapeutic properties, this plant serves as a potent diuretic, liver tonic, and digestive aid. Its versatile leaves, roots, and flowers offer significant nutritional and healing benefits worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Taraxacum sect. Taraxacum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Europe

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions within Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the landscapes of Denmark and Finland. The species also extends its reach to the islands of Føroyaar. Furthermore, its presence is documented throughout Great Britain. Finally, the plant is located within the unique environment of Iceland.
