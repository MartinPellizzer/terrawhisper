Syringa reticulata subsp. amurensis

Syringa reticulata subsp. amurensis, commonly known as the Amur lilac, is a resilient shrub valued for its ornamental beauty and potential medicinal properties. Historically used in traditional practices, its bark and leaves are studied for bioactive compounds that offer antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
