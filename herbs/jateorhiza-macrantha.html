Jateorhiza macrantha, scientifically known as Jateorhiza macrantha, is a potent medicinal plant widely used in traditional African medicine. Often referred to as "Devil's Claw" in certain regions, its roots contain bioactive compounds valued for their anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and digestive properties, making it essential for treating various ailments.
