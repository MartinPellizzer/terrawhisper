Rosa corymbifera

Rosa corymbifera, commonly known as the corymb rose, is a resilient deciduous shrub celebrated for its fragrant, pink blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its antioxidant properties and therapeutic potential, offering natural wellness benefits through its nutrient-rich hips and aromatic essence.

  • Scientific name: Rosa corymbifera
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling into the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rosa.
