Lamium album

White dead-nettle

Lamium album, commonly known as white dead-nettle, is a resilient perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Characterized by its square stems and hooded white flowers, this plant thrives in disturbed soils. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it possesses anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties valued for treating various skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Lamium album
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Lamium album, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. It is further categorized into the genus Lamium.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as white dead-nettle, is characterized by its distinctive morphological features used in medicinal preparations. The aerial parts of the specimen are typically collected for their therapeutic properties, encompassing the stems and leaves. Specifically, the flowering tops are highly valued, as they contain a high concentration of active compounds. The flowers themselves are small, white, and bilabiate, arranged in whorls along the upper nodes of the stem. When harvesting for herbal purposes, the entire upper portion of the plant, including the flower and the surrounding foliage, is often gathered to ensure maximum potency.
