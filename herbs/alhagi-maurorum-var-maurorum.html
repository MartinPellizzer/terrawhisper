Alhagi maurorum var. maurorum

Alhagi maurorum var. maurorum, commonly known as camelthorn, is a resilient perennial shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to arid regions, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Research suggests its extracts may offer anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant benefits, making it a subject of interest for treating various metabolic and inflammatory conditions.

  • Family: Fabaceae
