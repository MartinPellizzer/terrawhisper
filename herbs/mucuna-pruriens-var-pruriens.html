Cow-itch, scientifically known as Mucuna pruriens var. pruriens, is a climbing legume renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Distinguished by its irritating, hair-covered pods, this plant is highly valued for its high L-dopa content. It is primarily utilized to support neurological health, balance hormones, and enhance overall cognitive function.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct tropical regions. Within the Macaronesian islands, it can be specifically found in Cape Verde. Its range extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Benin. Furthermore, the species is present in the landlocked nation of Burkina. Finally, its presence is also noted in the coastal West Tropical African country of Guinea-Bissau and throughout Ghana.
