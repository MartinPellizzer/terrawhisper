Mucuna pruriens var. pruriens

Mucuna pruriens var. pruriens, commonly known as velvet bean, is a powerful medicinal legume prized in Ayurvedic traditions. Renowned for its high L-dopa content, it serves as a natural precursor to dopamine. This potent botanical is primarily utilized to support neurological health, balance hormones, and enhance overall vitality.

  • Scientific name: Mucuna pruriens var. pruriens
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is part of the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Mucuna, under the variety pruriens.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct tropical regions. Within the Macaronesian islands, it can be specifically found in Cape Verde. Its range extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Benin. Furthermore, the species is present in the landlocked nation of Burkina. Finally, its presence is also noted in the coastal West Tropical African country of Guinea-Bissau and throughout Ghana.
