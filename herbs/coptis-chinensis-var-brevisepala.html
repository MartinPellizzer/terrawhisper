Coptis chinensis var. brevisepala

Coptis chinensis var. brevisepala, commonly known as Goldthread, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its high berberine content, this plant possesses significant antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antipyretic properties. It is widely utilized in herbal formulations to treat gastrointestinal issues and various inflammatory conditions effectively.

  • Scientific name: Coptis chinensis var. brevisepala
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Ranunculales and falls into the family Ranunculaceae. Specifically, it is identified as a member of the genus Coptis, categorized as the variety brevisepala.

Common Names

This plant, Coptis chinensis var. brevisepala, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic context and specific variety. In Chinese (zh), it is frequently referred to as 黄连 (huánglián) or 鸡爪黄连 (jīzhuǎ huánglián). More specifically, the variety brevisepala is identified as 短萼黄连 (duǎn è huánglián), a name that is used in Simplified Chinese (zh-cn) as 短萼黄连 and in Traditional Chinese (zh-tw) as 短萼黃連.
