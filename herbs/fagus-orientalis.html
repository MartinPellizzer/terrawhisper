Fagus orientalis

Fagus orientalis, commonly known as the Oriental beech, is a majestic deciduous tree native to East Asia. Renowned for its dense, shade-providing canopy and hard timber, it holds significant ecological value. Historically, its bark and leaves have been utilized in traditional medicine for their diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Fagus orientalis
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Fagales and the family Fagaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Fagus, specifically as the species Fagus orientalis.

Moving toward the Balkan Peninsula, the species is well-established in Southeastern Europe, specifically within Bulgaria and Greece. Its presence also extends to the European portion of Türkiye.
