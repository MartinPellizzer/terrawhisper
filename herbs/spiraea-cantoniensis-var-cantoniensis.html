Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. Within Southwestern Europe, its presence is noted in both Portugal and Spain. Moving toward the east, it can also be found in Southeastern Europe, specifically in Bulgaria. The species extends into the Macaronesian islands, with populations located in the Azores. Finally, its range reaches as far as Siberia, where it is documented in the Chit Chita region.
