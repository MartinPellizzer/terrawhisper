What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause acute cyanide poisoning, nausea, vomiting, headaches, dizziness, and potentially fatal respiratory failure due to its high concentration of amygdalin. When consumed, the amygdalin in the plant is metabolized by enzymes in the digestive tract into hydrogen cyanide. This toxic release interferes with the body's ability to utilize oxygen at a cellular level. Symptoms of toxicity can escalate quickly from mild discomfort to severe neurological impairment. If large quantities are ingested, the resulting metabolic acidosis can lead to coma or death.
