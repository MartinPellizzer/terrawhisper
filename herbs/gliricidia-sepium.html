Quickstick

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (258 studies) · ★★★★★

Quickstick, scientifically known as Gliricidia sepium, is a versatile, nitrogen-fixing legume widely used in tropical agriculture. Renowned for its rapid growth, this hardy shrub serves as excellent fodder, organic fertilizer, and medicinal aid. Its leaves are traditionally utilized for managing parasites and promoting healing in various folk remedies.

Plant Parts

This plant, Gliricidia sepium, possesses various parts that are utilized for different medicinal and practical purposes. The green, pinnate leaves are frequently used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. The bark is often decocted to manage ailments such as digestive issues or fever, while the roots are valued for their potent properties in treating various internal infections. Internally, the sturdy heart wood provides structural integrity to the plant and is sometimes studied for its unique chemical composition. Additionally, the plant produces legume fruits that, while less commonly used in primary medicinal preparations, contribute to the overall biological lifecycle and nutrient cycling of the species.
