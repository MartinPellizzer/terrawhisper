Quickstick, scientifically known as Gliricidia sepium, is a versatile, nitrogen-fixing legume widely used in tropical agriculture. Renowned for its rapid growth, this hardy shrub serves as excellent fodder, organic fertilizer, and medicinal aid. Its leaves are traditionally utilized for managing parasites and promoting healing in various folk remedies.
