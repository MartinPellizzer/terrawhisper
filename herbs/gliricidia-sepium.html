Gliricidia sepium

Quickstick

Gliricidia sepium, commonly known as quickstick or madre de cacao, is a versatile tropical legume prized for its medicinal and agricultural benefits. Renowned for its nitrogen-fixing properties, it is also utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, parasites, and various inflammatory conditions, serving as a vital multipurpose resource.

  • Scientific name: Gliricidia sepium
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Gliricidia.

Plant Parts

Internally, the sturdy heart wood provides structural integrity to the plant and is sometimes studied for its unique chemical composition.
