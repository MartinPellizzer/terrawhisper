Physalis

Physalis, commonly known as groundcherry or cape gooseberry, is a versatile genus of flowering plants in the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its edible, lantern-like husks, these plants offer unique nutritional benefits. Beyond culinary uses, various species are valued in traditional medicine for their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties.
