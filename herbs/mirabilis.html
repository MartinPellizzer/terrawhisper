Mirabilis

Mirabilis, commonly known as the Four O'Clock flower, is a captivating ornamental plant prized for its evening blooms. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of the plant have been utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Mirabilis
  • Family: Nyctaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Nyctaginaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is found within the genus Mirabilis.
