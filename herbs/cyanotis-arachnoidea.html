Cyanotis arachnoidea

Cyanotis arachnoidea, commonly known as the spider spiderwort, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Native to tropical regions, this plant is prized for its diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. It is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat various ailments and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cyanotis arachnoidea
  • Family: Commelinaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cyanotis arachnoidea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Commelinales and the family Commelinaeaecae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cyanotis.
