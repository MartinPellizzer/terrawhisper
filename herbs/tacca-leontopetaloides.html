Distribution

This plant, Tacca leontopetaloides, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several nations in West Tropical Africa. Within this region, it can be found growing in the territory of Benin. Its presence is also documented in Burkina Faso, contributing to its regional spread. Additionally, the species extends its range into the coastal and inland areas of the Gambia. Finally, the plant is known to inhabit parts of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau.
