Distribution

This plant, Tacca leontopetaloides, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several nations in West Tropical Africa. Within this region, it can be found growing in the territory of Benin. Its presence is also documented in Burkina Faso, contributing to its regional spread. Additionally, the species extends its range into the coastal and inland areas of the Gambia. Finally, the plant is known to inhabit parts of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, Tacca leontopetaloides, commonly known as polomatoa or kuku, is a tropical perennial where the most significant medicinal and nutritional component is its tubers. These starchy, underground tubers serve as the primary part of the plant used for human consumption and traditional remedies. Because the tubers contain a high amount of starch, they are often processed into flour or used in various culinary applications, while their biological properties are explored for their potential therapeutic benefits in traditional medicine.
