Gomphrena

Gomphrena, commonly known as Globe Amaranth, is a vibrant medicinal herb prized for its resilient blooms and therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial qualities. Often used to support respiratory health and digestive wellness, this versatile plant bridges the gap between beauty and healing.

  • Scientific name: Gomphrena
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Caryophyllales, it is specifically situated in the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Gomphrena.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Germany. Its presence extends into Southeastern Europe, specifically documented in both Bulgaria and Romania. Furthermore, the species reaches the Macaronesian islands, including the Azores. Finally, its range continues across the Atlantic to the archipelago of Cape Verde.
