Polygonatum punctatum, scientifically known as Polygonatum punctatum, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Often referred to as spotted Solomon's seal, it is highly valued in traditional medicine. Its rhizomes are frequently used to nourish Yin, tonify fluids, and support respiratory and digestive health through various decoctions.
