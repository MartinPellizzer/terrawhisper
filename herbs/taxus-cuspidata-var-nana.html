Taxus cuspidata var. nana

Taxus cuspidata var. nana, commonly known as the dwarf Japanese yew, is a resilient coniferous plant valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its dense foliage, this variety contains unique alkaloids. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic properties, particularly in supporting cellular health and managing various physiological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Taxus cuspidata var. nana
  • Family: Taxaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, it is a member of the Taxaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Taxus as the variety cuspidata var. nana.
