Turmerics, scientifically known as Curcuma, is a genus of flowering plants in the ginger family, renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Primarily valued for the bioactive compound curcumin, these rhizomes offer powerful anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits, making them a cornerstone in traditional Ayurvedic medicine and modern holistic wellness.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized within the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic classification falls under the genus Curcuma.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern primarily centered within the tropical regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Guinea-Bissau and the Ivory Coast. Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is documented in the Congo. Additionally, the species inhabits the islands located within the Gulf of Guinea. Finally, the geographical range reaches further into the heart of the continent within the DR Congo.
