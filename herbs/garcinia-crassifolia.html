Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Garcinia crassifolia, possesses a very specific and limited natural range. Its geographical distribution is primarily centered within the region of Papuasia. Specifically, this medicinal species can be found growing in the Solomon Islands. Because of this localized presence, it is considered an endemic element of that particular island group. Understanding such a restricted habitat is essential for the conservation of the species.
