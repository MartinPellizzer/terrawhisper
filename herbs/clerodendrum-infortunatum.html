Clerodendrum infortunatum

Clerodendrum infortunatum, commonly known as the climbing clerodendrum, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin conditions. This robust shrub offers significant pharmacological potential for modern integrative medicine.

  • Scientific name: Clerodendrum infortunatum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Clerodendrum.
