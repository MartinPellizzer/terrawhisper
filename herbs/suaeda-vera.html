Shrubby Sea-blite, scientifically known as Suaeda vera, is a succulent halophyte flourishing in saline coastal environments. Renowned for its salt-tolerant properties, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices to treat skin ailments and inflammation, offering unique bioactive compounds for therapeutic use.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Suaeda vera, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts. These above-ground structures, which include the succulent stems and the fleshy, salt-tolerant leaves, contain the concentrated bioactive compounds necessary for its therapeutic effects. Because the plant thrives in saline environments, its aerial parts are particularly noted for their unique chemical composition, which is harvested to prepare various medicinal extracts and treatments.
