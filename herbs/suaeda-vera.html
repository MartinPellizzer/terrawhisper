Suaeda vera

Suaeda vera, commonly known as sea blite, is a succulent halophyte frequently found in coastal saline environments. Valued in traditional medicine, this resilient plant possesses significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Its ability to thrive in harsh, salty soils makes it a unique subject for pharmacological and nutritional research.

  • Scientific name: Suaeda vera
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Suaeda vera, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaranthaceae and the genus Suaeda.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within Great Britain. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species is also found growing in Germany. Its range extends significantly into Southwestern Europe, where it inhabits the Baleares and Corse. Additionally, the plant is widely distributed throughout the coastal areas of France.
