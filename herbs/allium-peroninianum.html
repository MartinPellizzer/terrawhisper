Allium peroninianum

Allium peroninianum, commonly known as a wild garlic variety, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, it supports cardiovascular health and immune function. This aromatic herb offers a natural approach to wellness, blending culinary utility with profound therapeutic benefits.

  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
