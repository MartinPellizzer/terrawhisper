Ainsliaea glabra

What's the taxonomical classification of Ainsliaea glabra?

Ainsliaea glabra belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, which places it within the genus Ainsliaea, ultimately leading to its specific identification as the species glabra.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ainsliaea glabra has woody, deciduous stems that support opposite, thin, membranous leaves with 3 to 5 shallowly lobed leaflets and finely serrated margins. The foliage typically displays a light green color and lacks significant pubescence on its surfaces. Small, inconspicuous flowers appear in clusters from the leaf axils during the spring months. These flowers possess five white petals and are supported by short pedicels. The plant's overall structure remains relatively low-growing and spreading along the forest floor.
