Descurainia sophia

Descurainia sophia, commonly known as the "furo-furo" plant, is a remarkable medicinal herb native to the Brazilian Amazon. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, it is traditionally used by indigenous communities to treat various ailments, particularly those involving inflammation, skin conditions, and systemic infections, offering significant therapeutic potential.
