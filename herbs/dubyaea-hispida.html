What's the taxonomical classification of Dubyaea hispida?

Dubyaea hispida belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and falls into the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, specifically placed within the genus Dubyaea, and is identified by its unique species name, hispida.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dubyaea hispida has an erect, herbaceous habit characterized by stems that are densely covered in coarse, spreading hairs and leaves that are primarily cordate or broadly ovate with serrated margins. During the flowering stage, the plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers arranged in terminal panicles. These inflorescences emerge from the upper portions of the stem, often accompanied by bracts. The overall structure is defined by its bristly texture and a somewhat spreading growth form.
