Chewstick, scientifically known as Gouania lupuloides, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Caribbean. Renowned for its woody stems, it is traditionally used as a natural toothbrush to promote oral hygiene. Beyond dental care, it offers significant therapeutic potential in treating digestive issues and reducing inflammation.
