Abies webbiana, scientifically known as Abies spectabilis, is a majestic fir species native to the high-altitude forests of the Himalayas. Renowned for its striking conical shape and fragrant needles, this evergreen holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices to treat respiratory ailments and promote wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specialized distribution across several high-altitude regions of Asia. In Western Asia, its presence can be recorded within the mountainous terrain of Afghanistan. It also thrives in the elevated regions of Tibet within China. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species is found across Nepal and throughout Pakistan. Furthermore, its range extends significantly into the West Himalaya region.

Plant Parts

This plant, Abies spectabilis, commonly known as the red fir, is a coniferous species where the leaves serve as a primary component for identification and medicinal study. The leaves are needle-like in shape, typically appearing dark green and glossy on the upper surface, while the underside often features two distinct white stomatal bands. In various traditional applications, these evergreen needles are utilized to extract essential oils and aromatic compounds that possess various therapeutic properties.
