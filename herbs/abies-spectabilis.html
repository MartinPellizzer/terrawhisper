Abies spectabilis

Abies webbiana

Abies spectabilis, commonly known as the East Himalayan fir, is a majestic evergreen conifer native to the high-altitude forests of the Himalayas. Beyond its ecological significance, this species holds traditional medicinal value, often utilized in folk remedies for its aromatic resins and essential oils to treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Abies spectabilis
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, falling into the family Pinaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Abies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specialized distribution across several high-altitude regions of Asia. In Western Asia, its presence can be recorded within the mountainous terrain of Afghanistan. It also thrives in the elevated regions of Tibet within China. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species is found across Nepal and throughout Pakistan. Furthermore, its range extends significantly into the West Himalaya region.
