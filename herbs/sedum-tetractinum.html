Sedum tetractinum

What's the taxonomical classification of Sedum tetractinum?

Sedum tetractinum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further classified into the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic hierarchy through the order Saxifragales, it is placed within the family Crassulaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Sedum, and its specific species name, tetractinum.

Its stems are often creeping or ascending, allowing the plant to form low-growing mats or cushions across rocky substrates. How is this plant cultivated?

This plant, Sedum tetractinum, is best cultivated in well-draining, sandy or gravelly soil under full sun to partial shade conditions.

It thrives in rocky crevices or alpine environments where moisture does not linger around its fleshy roots. Regular watering is necessary during the growing season, but the substrate must dry out completely between applications to prevent root rot. This succulent is highly drought-tolerant once established and requires minimal fertilization to maintain its compact form.
