Erica mannii

Erica mannii, commonly known as Mann's heath, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the fynbos biome of South Africa. Characterized by its delicate, needle-like foliage and bell-shaped flowers, this botanical treasure plays a vital role in its ecosystem, offering nectar to specialized pollinators within its unique habitat.

  • Scientific name: Erica mannii
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is a representative of the family Ericaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Erica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically in Nigeria. Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Burundi and Rwanda. Furthermore, the species is documented in Cameroon within the same central region. Finally, its range includes the islands located in the Gulf of Guinea.
