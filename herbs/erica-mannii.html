Erica mannii, scientifically known as Erica mannii, is a remarkable shrub native to the fynbos regions of South Africa. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic properties and its vital role in diverse ecosystems.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically in Nigeria.
