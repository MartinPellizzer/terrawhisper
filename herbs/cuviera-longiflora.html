Life history

The life history of Cuviera longiflora is characterized by its classification as a phanerophyte, a growth form where the perennating buds are located well above the ground on aerial shoots. Within this category, it exhibits specific variations in its life strategy, functioning both as a phanerophyte and a nanophanerophyte, which indicates that while its buds are elevated, the plant typically maintains a relatively low or shrubby stature compared to larger woody species. This life form allows the plant to persist through varying environmental conditions by protecting its vital regenerative structures on its stems, ensuring its survival across successive growing seasons.
