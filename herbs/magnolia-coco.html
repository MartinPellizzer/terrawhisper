The physiology of Magnolia coco is characterized by a highly efficient metabolic framework typical of many woody angiosperms, primarily operating through a C3 photosynthetic pathway. In this process, carbon dioxide is fixed directly from the atmosphere by the enzyme RuBisCO during the Calvin cycle, occurring within the mesophyll cells without the specialized spatial separation seen in C4 plants or the temporal separation found in CAM species.
