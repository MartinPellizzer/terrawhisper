Combretum zeyheri

Large-fruited bushwillow

Combretum zeyheri, commonly known as a versatile medicinal shrub, holds significant importance in traditional healing practices. Native to specific African regions, this plant is valued for its diverse phytochemical properties. It is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat various ailments, showcasing its profound therapeutic potential in ethnobotany.

  • Family: Combretaceae

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Combretum, and its species name, zehyeri.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends into East Tropical Africa, specifically within the territories of Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, the species is also located in South Tropical Africa. This southern distribution encompasses the countries of both Angola and Malawi.
