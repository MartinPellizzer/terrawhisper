Southwestern mock vervain, scientifically known as Glandularia bipinnatifida, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its delicate, pinkish-purple blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used in traditional medicine, it is studied for its potential antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial therapeutic properties.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across different continents and regions. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented within Germany. Moving to the Northwestern United States, it is found in both Colorado and Wyoming. Its range also extends into the North-Central United States, specifically appearing in Kansas. Finally, the species is also known to inhabit the state of Missouri.
