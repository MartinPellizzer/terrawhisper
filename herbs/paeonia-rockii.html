Paeonia suffruticosa var. papaveracea, scientifically known as Paeonia rockii, is a striking medicinal shrub prized in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, its roots contain essential compounds used to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and regulate blood circulation, offering significant therapeutic potential for various systemic health conditions.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Saxifragales. Finally, it is a member of the family Paeoniaceae and is identified by the genus Paeonia.
