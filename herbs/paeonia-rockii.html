Paeonia suffruticosa var. papaveracea, scientifically known as Paeonia rockii, is a striking medicinal shrub prized in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, its roots contain essential compounds used to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and regulate blood circulation, offering significant therapeutic potential for various systemic health conditions.

Morphology

The morphology of Paeonia rockii is characterized by a robust, perennial growth habit with a primarily woody structure, as it develops thick, woody stems and a substantial woody rootstock that facilitates long-term survival. The plant features large, compound leaves that are typically divided into several distinct, ovate to lanceolate leaflets with finely serrated margins. During its flowering period, it produces large, showy blossoms that can reach significant diameters, often exhibiting deep red or pink petals with prominent, contrasting stamens. The arrangement of these floral structures on the woody stems is central to its visual profile, while the overall architecture of the plant reflects its perennial, woody nature, allowing it to maintain a sturdy presence in its natural habitat.
