Paeonia rockii

Paeonia suffruticosa var. papaveracea

Paeonia rockii, commonly known as Rock's peony, is a stunning perennial celebrated for its large, fragrant blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant holds significant value in traditional practices, where its roots are utilized for their therapeutic properties, offering potential benefits in managing inflammation and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia rockii
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Saxifragales. Finally, it is a member of the family Paeoniaceae and is identified by the genus Paeonia.
