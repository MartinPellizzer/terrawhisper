Lycopus americanus, scientifically known as Lycopus americanus, is a perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Commonly called American bugleweed, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional herbalism to support reproductive health, alleviate menstrual discomfort, and soothe inflammatory conditions through its unique bioactive properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several regions of the Canadian landscape. In Western Canada, its range extends through the provinces of Alberta and British Columbia. It can also be found throughout the prairie landscapes of Manitoba and Saskatchewan. Moving toward the Atlantic coast, the species is documented in Eastern Canada. Specifically, its distribution reaches as far east as Newfoundland.
