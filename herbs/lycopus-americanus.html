Lycopus americanus, scientifically known as Lycopus americanus, is a perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Commonly called American bugleweed, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional herbalism to support reproductive health, alleviate menstrual discomfort, and soothe inflammatory conditions through its unique bioactive properties.
