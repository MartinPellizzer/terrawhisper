Distribution

This plant is found across several distinct regions within the South American continent. In Western South America, its presence is specifically noted in Bolivia. Its distribution extends significantly into Brazil, where it inhabits the North and Northeast regions. Additionally, the species can be found throughout the West-Central area of Brazil. Finally, it is also documented to grow within the Southeast part of the country.

This plant, known scientifically as Plumbago populnea (formerly Plumbago populnea or often referred to in similar contexts as Plumbago zinaica), possesses various morphological features, though the medicinal properties are often attributed to different components. While the leaves and flowers are frequently studied for their bioactive compounds, the root is a significant part of the plant that contains specific phytochemicals used in traditional medicine. The chemical constituents found within the root system are often utilized in various holistic practices to address different ailments.
