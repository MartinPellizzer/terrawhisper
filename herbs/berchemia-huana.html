What's the taxonomical classification of Berchemia huana?

Berchemia huaana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic hierarchy, the species falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rhamnaceae. It is specifically identified within the genus Berchemia, with the unique species designation huaana.
