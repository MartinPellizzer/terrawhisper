Syzygium cordatum

Syzygium cordatum, commonly known as the Water Gum, is a versatile evergreen tree native to tropical wetlands. Renowned for its medicinal properties, its leaves and bark are traditionally used to treat ailments like skin infections and digestive issues. This resilient species plays a vital role in supporting local ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium cordatum
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae and is situated in the genus Syzygium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several distinct regions of the African continent. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the countries of Burundi, Gabon, and Rwanda. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the vast territories of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving eastward, the species extends its reach into the landscapes of East Tropical Africa. Specifically, this distribution includes the country of Kenya, highlighting its diverse habitat.
