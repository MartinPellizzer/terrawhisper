Swertia speciosa

Swertiia speciosa, commonly known as Swertia or Chirata, is a perennial herb highly valued in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its intense bitterness, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and hepatoprotective properties. This medicinal plant is primarily utilized to treat digestive disorders, fever, and skin ailments, promoting overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Swertia speciosa
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Gentianales, it is a member of the Gentianaceae family. Finally, it is taxonomically identified under the genus Swertia.

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of the Asian continent. In China, its distribution is specifically noted within the high-altitude areas of Tibet.
