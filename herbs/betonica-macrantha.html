Betonica macrantha

Betonica macrantha, commonly known as the Large-flowered Betonica, is a remarkable medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to address various ailments, offering natural remedies that support holistic wellness and serve as a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Betonica macrantha
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
