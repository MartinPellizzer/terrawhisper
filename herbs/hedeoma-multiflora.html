Hedeoma multiflora

Hedeoma multiflora, commonly known as the "heart-shaped leaf" or "climbing milkweed," is a versatile medicinal climber native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including skin inflammation and digestive issues, offering significant potential in holistic and ethnobotanical healing practices.
