Mirabilis jalapa

Four o'clock flower

Mirabilis jalapica, commonly known as Jalap, is a perennial climbing plant native to Mexico. Renowned in traditional medicine, its tuberous roots contain potent purgative alkaloids. Historically valued for treating digestive ailments and skin conditions, this botanical specimen remains a significant subject of study in ethnopharmacology and natural therapeutics.
