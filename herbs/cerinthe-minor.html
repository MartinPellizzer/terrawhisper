Cerinthe minor

Cerinthe minor, commonly known as Honeywort, is a charming annual herb prized in pollinator gardens. Characterized by its distinctive blue-green foliage and cup-shaped, azure flowers, it acts as a magnet for bees and butterflies. This hardy plant adds unique texture and striking color to any cottage-style landscape.
