Balloon vine, scientifically known as Cardiospermum, is a climbing medicinal plant celebrated for its therapeutic potential. Renowned for its heart-shaped leaves, this versatile herb is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and inflammation, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and herbal healing.
