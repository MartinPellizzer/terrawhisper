Poison Ivy, scientifically known as Tragia volubiantis, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent urushiol oils, it causes severe allergic contact dermatitis upon touch. While traditionally studied for unique phytochemical properties, it remains primarily recognized as a hazardous irritant in many natural ecosystems.
