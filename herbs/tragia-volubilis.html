Tragia volubilis

Tragia volubiilis, commonly known as the stinging nettle vine, is a climbing medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive stinging hairs, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin inflammations, respiratory issues, and digestive disorders, offering significant therapeutic potential in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Tragia volubilis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Tragia volubilis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and falls under the genus Tragia.
