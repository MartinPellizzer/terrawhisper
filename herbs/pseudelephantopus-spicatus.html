Pseudelephantopus spicatus

Pseudelephantopus spicatus, commonly known as the Spicate Elephantopus, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions throughout herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Pseudelephantopus spicatus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pseudelephantopus and the specific species Pseudelephantopus spicatus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of Asia and Africa. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Guinea. Moving toward the Asian continent, it can be found in Southeast China and the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. The species also inhabits parts of the Indian Subcontinent, specifically documented in the Assam region. Additionally, its range extends through the Indian Subcontinent to include Bangladesh.
