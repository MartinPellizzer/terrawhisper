Ronabea emetica

Ronabea emetica, commonly known as the emetic vine, is a potent medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its powerful purgative properties, it has long been utilized in traditional medicine to induce vomiting and cleanse the digestive system, serving as a vital tool for treating various gastrointestinal ailments.

  • Scientific name: Ronabea emetica
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales and the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Ronabea, and its species name, emetica.
