Cucumis ficifolius

Cucumis ficifolius, commonly known as the melon pear or horned melon, is a unique medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and essential minerals, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, boost immunity, and provide skin hydration, offering significant benefits in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cucumis ficifolius
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Cucurbitales. Finally, it resides in the family Cucurbitaceae and is identified by the genus Cucumis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Cucumis ficifolia, is recognized by various common names across different linguistic and cultural contexts. In English, it is most frequently referred to as the melon pear or the sand melon. Within Spanish-speaking regions, it is widely known as melón sandía or melón de sandía. In French, it is often called melon poire, while in Portuguese, it is referred to as melão-pera. Additionally, it is sometimes called melon de sandía in various regional dialects, reflecting its unique botanical characteristics.
