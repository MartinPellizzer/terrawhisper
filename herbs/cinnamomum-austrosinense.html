Cinnamomum austrosinense

What's the taxonomical classification of Cinnamomum austrosinense?

Cinnamomum austrosinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Laurales. Within this order, it is situated in the family Lauraceae, specifically falling under the genus Cinnamomum. The complete scientific name, Cinnamomum austrosinense, identifies it as a distinct species within its respective taxonomic hierarchy.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cinnamomum austrosinense has simple, alternate, evergreen leaves that are elliptical to oblong in shape, measuring approximately 5 to 12 centimeters in length with entire margins and an entire or slightly wavy edge. The leaf surface is typically coriaceous and dark green on the upper side, while the underside may exhibit a paler coloration. Its woody stems and branches develop a smooth to slightly fissured bark as the plant matures. The reproductive structures consist of small, inconspicuous flowers arranged in axillary panicles. These flowers eventually develop into small, globose drupes that turn a dark color upon ripening.
