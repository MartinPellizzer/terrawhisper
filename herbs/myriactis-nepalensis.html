What's the taxonomical classification of Myriactis nepalensis?

Myriactis nepalensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, eventually reaching the order Asterales. As a member of the family Asteraceae, this plant is categorized under the genus Myriactis, with its specific designation being the species nepalensis.
