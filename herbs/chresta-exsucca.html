Chresta exsucca

Chresta exsucca, commonly known as the desert bone-dry shrub, is a resilient medicinal plant native to arid landscapes. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation and digestive ailments. This hardy species offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Chresta exsucca
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America
