Croton micans

Croton micaens, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, is native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for developing new pharmacological treatments and natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Croton micans
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Croton micaens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malpighiales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Croton.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several regions of the Americas. Within the Caribbean, its presence is noted in Jamaica, Trinidad-Tobago, and the Windward Islands. Moving toward the mainland, it can be found throughout parts of Northern South America, specifically in Venezuela. The species also extends its reach into Western South America, where it is located in Bolivia. Together, these locations highlight the widespread ecological range of Croton micaens.
