Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the Northeast Tropical Africa region. Its natural range includes several distinct countries within this geographical area. Specifically, it can be found growing in Djibouti, Eritrea, and Ethiopia. The species is also documented to exist within the borders of Somalia. Finally, its distribution extends through the territories of both Sudan and South Sudan.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Myrrh, possesses several parts that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaves are typically small and compound, providing a delicate structure to the shrub, while the stems serve as the primary framework for its growth. A key medicinal component is found within the stem bark, which protects the inner tissues of the plant. When the plant is wounded or cut, it releases aromatic resinous exudates that serve as a natural defense mechanism. It is this collected resin, which hardens upon exposure to air, that is most famously harvested and used globally in traditional medicine and perfumery.
