Ocotea paranaensis

Ocotea paranaensis, commonly known as canela-sassafrás, is a significant medicinal plant native to South American forests. Rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally valued for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This species plays a vital role in ethnobotanical practices and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Ocotea paranaensis
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Southern America
