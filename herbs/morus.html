Morus

Morus, commonly known as the mulberry tree, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional healing. Renowned for its nutrient-rich leaves and antioxidant-packed berries, it is frequently used to support blood sugar regulation, improve cardiovascular health, and reduce inflammation, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Morus
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Rosales, it is further categorized under the family Moraceae. Ultimately, the medicinal specimen is identified by its genus, Morus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is noted in countries such as Denmark, Great Britain, and Sweden. Moving toward the central part of the continent, the species can also be found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the borders of Austria. Furthermore, its range extends into the Middle European territories of Czechia and Slovakia.
