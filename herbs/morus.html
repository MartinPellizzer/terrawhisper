Mulberry, scientifically known as Morus, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for centuries in traditional medicine. Renowned for its nutrient-rich fruits, bark, and leaves, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-diabetic properties. This multipurpose botanical serves as a vital natural resource for supporting metabolic health and overall wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. Moving toward the central part of the continent, the species can also be found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the borders of Austria.
