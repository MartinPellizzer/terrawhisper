Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Mulberry (Morus), possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaf is perhaps the most widely recognized part used in traditional medicine to manage blood sugar levels and inflammation. Beyond the foliage, the branch can be harvested for certain extracts, while the root itself contains bioactive compounds. Specifically, the root bark or root-bark is often employed in traditional formulations to treat ailments ranging from coughs to digestive issues, making every part of the plant valuable in herbal medicine.
