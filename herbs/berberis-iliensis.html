Berberis iliensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Berberis iliensis?

Berberis iliensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the order Ranunculales and is a member of the family Berberidaceae. At a more specific taxonomic level, it is situated within the genus Berberis, and its unique identity is defined by its specific designation as the species iliensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Berberis iliensis has deciduous, obovate to elliptical leaves with entire margins and sharp, spine-tipped stipules that are characteristic of the Berberidaceae family. The stems are often woody and armed with small spines located at the nodes. Small, yellowish flowers typically emerge in clusters from the axils of the leaves during the flowering season. These flowers eventually develop into small, fleshy berries that serve as a primary reproductive structure. The overall growth habit is that of a spreading shrub with a complex branching pattern.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?
