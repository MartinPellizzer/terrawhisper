Elaeagnus bockii

Elaeagnus bockii, commonly known as Bocconia or Silverberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health and skin vitality. This resilient plant offers a rich profile of bioactive compounds essential for holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Elaeagnus bockii
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the family Elaeagnaceae and falls under the genus Elaeagnus.
