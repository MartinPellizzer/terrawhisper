Elaeagnus bockii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant further falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Elaeagnaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic position is defined by the genus Elaeagnus and the species bockii.

What parts of this plant are used medicinally?

This plant Elaeagnus bockii utilizes its rhizome for various medicinal applications in traditional practices. The underground stem serves as a primary source for extracting bioactive compounds. Practitioners often prepare decoctions from these roots to treat specific ailments. These preparations are valued for their potential therapeutic properties in regional medicine. Detailed studies continue to investigate the chemical makeup of these rhizomes.

According to a study published by "Zhongguo Zhong yao za zhi = Zhongguo zhongyao zazhi = China journal of Chinese materia medica", seven compounds were isolated from the rhizome of Elaeagnus bockii. These compounds were identified as angelicin (1), psoralen (2), vanillic acid (3), bakuchiol (4), oleanolic acid (5), ursolic acid (6) and beta-sitosterol (7). The chemical constituents were obtained from the rhizome of the plant using silica gel and Sephadex LH-20 chromatography. All 7 of these specific compounds were obtained from Elaeagnus bockii for the first time.
