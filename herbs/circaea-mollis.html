Circaea mollis

Circaea mollis, commonly known as Enchanter's Nightshade, is a delicate woodland perennial prized for its ethereal charm. Featuring soft, heart-shaped leaves and dainty white flowers, this plant thrives in shady, moist habitats. While primarily ornamental, it holds a mystical place in folklore, embodying the quiet beauty of forest ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Circaea mollis
  • Family: Onagraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Circaea mollis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the family Onagraceae and the genus Circaea.
