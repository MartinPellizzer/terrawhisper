Viburnum yunnanense

What's the taxonomical classification of Viburnum yunnanense?

Viburnum yunnanense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Dipsacales. This plant is a member of the family Viburnaceae, falling under the genus Viburnum, and is specifically identified by its species name, yunnanense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Viburnum yunnanense has large, opposite, elliptical leaves that measure up to 20 centimeters in length and feature serrated margins and prominent pinnate venation. The shrub typically grows to a height of two to three meters and displays a multi-stemmed, upright growth habit. During the flowering season, it produces dense, flat-topped cymes of creamy white flowers that emerge from the leaf axils. These blossoms eventually give way to small, fleshy drupes that transition from green to a dark reddish or black color when fully ripe. The bark is generally smooth and greyish, providing a subtle textural contrast to the lush foliage.
