Hydrangea macrophylla

Hydrangea macrophylla, commonly known as Bigleaf Hydrangea, is a popular ornamental shrub celebrated for its vibrant, color-changing blooms. While primarily grown for beauty, various cultures have historically explored its leaves for medicinal properties, including diuretic and anti-inflammatory uses, though it should be handled with caution and professional guidance.

  • Scientific name: Hydrangea macrophylla
  • Family: Hydrangeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Cornales and the family Hydrangeaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Hydrangea.
