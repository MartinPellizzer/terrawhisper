Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. Its range extends into China, where it can be found in both Manchuria and the North-Central parts of the country. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout the Korean Peninsula. Finally, the plant is also documented as growing within the various islands of Japan.
