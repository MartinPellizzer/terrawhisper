Passiflora nitida

Passiflora nitida, commonly known as the sparkling passionflower, is a striking climbing vine native to tropical regions. Characterized by its intricate, exotic blooms and lush foliage, this species is celebrated in ethnobotany. It holds significant potential in traditional medicine for its diverse bioactive compounds and calming therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Passiflora nitida
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Passifloraceae. Finally, Passiflora nitida is situated within the genus Passiflora.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Passiflora nitida, is recognized by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as nitid passionflower, while in Spanish, it is known as pasiflora nítida. In Portuguese, the species is called flor de maracujá nítida, and in French, it is identified as passiflore nitide.
