Limonium vulgare

Common sea-lavender

Limonium vulgare, commonly known as sea lavender, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal regions. Renowned for its delicate, airy clusters of violet flowers, this plant thrives in saline environments. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant botanical interest for its unique ecological role and potential medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Limonium vulgare
  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is further categorized into the family Plumbaginaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Limonium.
