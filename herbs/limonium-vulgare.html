Common sea-lavender, scientifically known as Limonium vulgare, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal marshes and saline environments. Renowned for its striking clusters of lavender-blue flowers, this hardy plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its potential anti-inflammatory properties and its unique ability to thrive in salty soils.
