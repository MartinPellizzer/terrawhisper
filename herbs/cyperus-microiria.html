Cyperus microiria

What's the taxonomical classification of Cyperus microiria?

Cyperus microiria belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Poales and falls within the family Cyperaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Cyperus, with its unique designation being the species microiria.
