What's the taxonomical classification of Amethystea caerulea?

Amethystea caerulea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Further taxonomic refinement places the plant in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its specific biological identity is defined by its placement within the genus Amethystea, with its unique designation being the species caerulea.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Amethystea caerulea has opposite, elliptic to lanceolate leaves that are dark green with distinctively serrated margins and a prominent midrib. The plant typically grows as a woody shrub reaching heights of up to two meters. Its flowers are small and tubular, often displaying a pale blue or violet hue that gives the species its name. The inflorescence is arranged in clusters that emerge from the leaf axils. Mature specimens develop a sturdy, branched stem structure suitable for its tropical habitat.
