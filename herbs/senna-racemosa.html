Limestone senna, scientifically known as Senna racemosa, is a perennial shrub native to the arid regions of North America. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, it contains anthraquinones, which act as powerful natural laxatives. This versatile plant is widely utilized in traditional herbal medicine to support digestive health.
