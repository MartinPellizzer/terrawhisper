Hymenidium amabile

Hymeniidum amabile, commonly known as the Amabile fern, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its unique therapeutic properties. Renowned for its traditional applications in treating skin ailments and promoting healing, this resilient species offers a fascinating glimpse into the profound intersection of botanical biodiversity and natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Hymenidium amabile
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Hymeniidion and the species Hymeniidion amabile.
