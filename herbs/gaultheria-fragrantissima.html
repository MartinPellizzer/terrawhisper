Gaultheria fragrantissima

Gaultheria fragrantissima, commonly known as Sweet Wintergreen, is a fragrant, evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its aromatic leaves and delicate bell-shaped flowers, this medicinal plant is highly valued for its essential oils, which possess potent anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties in traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Gaultheria fragrantissima
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is a member of the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gaultheria, specifically as the species Gaultheria fragrantissima.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. In China, it can be found growing throughout the South-Central territories as well as in Tibet. The species also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the region of Assam. Furthermore, it is widely distributed across the East Himalaya mountains. Finally, its presence is firmly established within the borders of India.
