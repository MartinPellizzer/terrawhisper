Gaultheria fragrantissima, is a fragrant evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its aromatic, sweet-scented foliage and delicate bell-shaped flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its soothing properties, often serving as a natural remedy for respiratory and inflammatory ailments.
