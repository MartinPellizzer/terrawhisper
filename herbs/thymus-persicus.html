Thymus persicus

Thymus persicus, commonly known as Persian thyme, is a fragrant perennial herb native to the Middle East. Valued for its aromatic essential oils, this medicinal plant offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. It is traditionally used in herbal remedies to support respiratory health and soothe digestive ailments effectively.

  • Scientific name: Thymus persicus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Thymus, specifically as the species Thymus persicus.
