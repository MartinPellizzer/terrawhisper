Spathodea campanulata

Spathodea campanulata, commonly known as the African Tulip Tree, is a striking tropical evergreen prized for its vibrant, bell-shaped orange flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various cultures utilizing its bark and leaves in traditional remedies to treat ailments like skin inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Spathodea campanulata
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Bignoniaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Spathodea.
