Lippia micromera, scientifically known as Lippia micromera, is a medicinal shrub native to the African savannah. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin infections. This aromatic plant holds significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic applications.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of Central America and the Caribbean. Within Central America, it can be found growing in the countries of Costa Rica, Honduras, and Nicaragua. Its presence also extends into the Caribbean region, where it is documented in Cuba. Additionally, the species is located in the Dominican Republic. Together, these regions define the natural range of this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

This plant, Lippia micromera, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties, which are largely concentrated in the leaf. These small, aromatic leaves contain the essential oils and bioactive compounds necessary for its therapeutic applications, often being used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Because the chemical potency of the species is most significant in its foliage, the leaves serve as the principal part harvested for decoctions, infusions, or essential oil extraction.
