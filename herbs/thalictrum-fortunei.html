Thalictrum fortunei

What's the taxonomical classification of Thalictrum fortunei?

Thalictrum fortunei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further classified into the subclass Magnoliidae and placed in the order Ranunculales. This plant is a member of the family Ranunculaceae, specifically falling under the genus Thalictrum, and is identified by the species name fortunei.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Thalictrum fortunei has herbaceous, clump-forming stems that rise to a height of approximately 60 to 90 centimeters and bear deeply lobed, trifoliate leaves with serrated margins. The foliage is a lush, medium green that provides a dense texture within the garden landscape. Small, delicate flowers emerge in late spring or early summer, typically appearing in clusters of pale yellow or cream-colored petals. These blossoms are characterized by their light, airy structure and often feature prominent stamens that add to their visual lightness. The plant's overall form is upright yet graceful, making it a structural element in shaded woodland settings.

    What side effects this plant can have?

    This plant can cause skin irritation, redness, and blistering if the sap comes into contact with human skin. The primary danger stems from the presence of protoanemonin, a chemical compound found in many plants of the Ranunculaceae family. If handled without gloves, individuals may experience localized dermatitis or allergic reactions. While direct ingestion is not common for this ornamental species, any contact with mucous membranes could lead to significant discomfort. It is always advisable to use protection when pruning or managing this plant in a garden setting.
