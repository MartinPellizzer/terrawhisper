Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the African continent and its islands. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented in both Algeria and Egypt. The species can also be found within the Macaronesia region, specifically in Cape Verde. Moving toward West Tropical Africa, the plant is naturally occurring in Benin. Finally, its range extends further inland to include the country of Burkina.
