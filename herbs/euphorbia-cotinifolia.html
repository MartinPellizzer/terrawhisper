Euphorbia cotinifolia

Euphorbia cotinifolia, commonly known as the leaf-shaped spurge, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic potential, this plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant promise for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia cotinifolia
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Malpighiales, and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct global regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is noted specifically within Tunisia. The species also extends to the Macaronesian islands, where it can be found in the Canary Islands. Moving toward the tropics, it inhabits West Tropical Africa, specifically in Benin, and reaches the Western Indian Ocean in the Comoros. Finally, its range reaches into East Asia, where it is documented on the island of Hainan in China.
