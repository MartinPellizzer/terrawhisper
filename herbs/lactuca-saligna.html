Lactuca saligna

Lactuca saligna, commonly known as wild lettuce or salad lettuce, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its sedative and analgesic properties, this plant has been traditionally used to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and treat insomnia, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lactuca saligna
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Lactuca.
