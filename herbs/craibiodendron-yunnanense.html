Craibiodendron yunnanense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ericales. This plant is further categorized into the family Ericaceae, specifically within the genus Craibiodendron, and is identified by the unique species epithet yunnanense.
