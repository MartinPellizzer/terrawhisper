Orobanche mutelii var. mutelii

Orobanche mutelii var. mutelii, commonly known as a parasitic broomrape, is a unique medicinal plant. Unlike most vegetation, it lacks chlorophyll, deriving nutrients directly from host roots. In traditional medicine, various extracts are utilized for their bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in treating inflammation and supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Orobanche mutelii var. mutelii
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, taxonomically classified as Orobanche mutelii var. mutelii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Orobanchaceae. It is further categorized into the genus Orobanche.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe. It can also be found inhabiting various parts of the Iberian Peninsula, specifically within Spain.
