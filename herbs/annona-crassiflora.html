Annona crassiflora

Annona crassiflora, commonly known as araticum, is a vital medicinal tree native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its nutrient-dense fruit, it also possesses significant therapeutic properties. Traditionally used to treat inflammation and digestive issues, this species plays a crucial role in both regional biodiversity and ethnomedicine.

  • Scientific name: Annona crassiflora
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Annona crassiflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Magnoliales. It is further categorized into the family Annonaceae and the genus Annona.
