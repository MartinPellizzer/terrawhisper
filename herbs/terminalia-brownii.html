Terminalia brownii

Terminalia brownii, commonly known as the Brown Terminalia, is a resilient medicinal tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Its bark and leaves contain bioactive compounds essential for promoting healing and overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia brownii
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Terminalia, specifically as the species Terminalia brownii.
