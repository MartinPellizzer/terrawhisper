What's the taxonomical classification of Sabia yunnanensis?

Sabia yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Proteales. This plant is further categorized under the family Sabiaceae and falls within the genus Sabia, eventually reaching its specific identification as the species yunnanensis.
