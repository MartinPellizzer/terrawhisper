Honey buchu, scientifically known as Agathosma betulina, is a fragrant, aromatic shrub native to South Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat urinary tract issues and digestive ailments. Its unique, minty essence makes it a valuable botanical resource in herbal medicine and perfumery.
