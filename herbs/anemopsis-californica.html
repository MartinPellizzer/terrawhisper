Anemopsis californica

Anemopsis californica, commonly known as California anemopsis, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the moist, shaded woodlands of the Pacific coast. Characterized by its delicate white flowers and lush foliage, this plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism, offering unique botanical properties within its native ecological niche.

  • Scientific name: Anemopsis californica
  • Family: Saururaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Piperales and categorized in the family Saururaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Anemopsis.
