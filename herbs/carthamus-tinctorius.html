This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as safflower, is a versatile medicinal and industrial resource where various components serve distinct purposes. The primary interest lies in its seeds, which function as a high-quality oilseed used for both nutritional and therapeutic applications. During biotechnological processes, somatic embryo regenerated shoots can be utilized to study plant development and enhance cultivation efficiency. Following the extraction of valuable lipids, the remaining seed cakes serve as a nutrient-rich byproduct often used in animal feed. Ultimately, the diverse parts of the Carthamus tinctorius contribute to its global importance in the fields of medicine and agriculture.
