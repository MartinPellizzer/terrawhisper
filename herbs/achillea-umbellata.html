Achillea umbellata

Achillea umbellata, commonly known as Umbellate Yarrow, is a resilient perennial herb native to various arid regions. Renowned for its unique umbrella-like flower clusters, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine for its antimicrobial properties and its ability to support digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Achillea umbellata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, it is identified under the genus Achillea as Achillea umbellata.
