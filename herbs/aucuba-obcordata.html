Aucuba obcordata

What's the taxonomical classification of Aucuba obcordata?

Aucuba obcordata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant follows the taxonomic order Garryales and is a member of the family Garryaceae. Its classification is completed within the genus Aucuba, specifically identifying it as the species obcordata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aucuba obcordata has elliptic to obcordate leaves that are leathery in texture, glossy on the upper surface, and arranged in an opposite pattern along its woody stems. The leaf margins are typically entire, though they can occasionally exhibit slight undulations. These evergreen leaves possess a deep green color and often feature distinct yellow spotting or variegation. The plant structure consists of an upright, shrubby growth habit with branching stems that support the foliage. Small, inconspicuous flowers may emerge from the leaf axils during the growing season.
