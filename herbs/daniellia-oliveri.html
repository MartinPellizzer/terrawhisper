African copaiba balsamtree, scientifically known as Daniellia olivevi, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the tropical rainforests of West and Central Africa. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, this versatile tree produces resins and extracts widely used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and infections.

This plant, Daniellia olivei, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the Fabaceae family under the genus Daniellia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its presence has been documented within the borders of Benin. It can also be found growing throughout the nation of Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species extends its range into Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical reach includes the coastal territory of Guinea-Bissau.
