Hedeoma nana nana, scientifically known as Hedeoma nana subsp. nana, is a diminutive, aromatic member of the mint family. Native to North American wetlands, this creeping perennial features tiny, pale lavender flowers. Its pungent, menthol-like scent makes it a fascinating subject for both botanical study and traditional herbalism.
