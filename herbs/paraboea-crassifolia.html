Paraboea crassifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Paraboea crassifolia?

Paraboea crassifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is a member of the order Lamiales and falls under the family Gesneriaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Paraboea, with its unique designation being the species crassifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Paraboea crassifolia has perennial, tufted, caespitose growth habits with leaves that are linear to lanceolate, relatively thick, and typically measure between 5 and 15 centimeters in length. The leaf blades are often folded or involute and possess a distinctively coriaceous texture. Its inflorescences are arranged in panicles that emerge from the terminal portion of the stems. The flowers are small and characterized by six tepals that are often tinged with pink or purplish hues. Each plant produces small, somewhat fleshy fruits that contain several tiny seeds.
