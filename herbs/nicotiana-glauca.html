Tree Tobacco

Nicotiana glauca · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (165 studies) · ★★★★☆

Tree Tobacco, scientifically known as Nicotiana glauca, is a perennial shrub native to South America. Characterized by its glaucous, blue-green foliage and tubular yellow flowers, this plant is highly toxic due to elevated nicotine levels. While used in traditional medicine, it requires extreme caution due to its potent alkaloids.

Plant Parts

This plant, Nicotiana glauca, exhibits distinct morphological features across its various structures. The leaves are typically large, lanceolate, and possess a characteristic glaucous, bluish-green hue that gives the species its name. From the central stems, which are sturdy and upright, numerous branches extend outward to create a bushy growth habit. These branches eventually support clusters of trumpet-shaped flowers, which are often pale yellow or creamy in color and serve as a vital component for attracting pollinators.
