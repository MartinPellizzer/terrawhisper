Phyllanthus pulcher

Flor escondida

Phyllanthus pulcher, commonly known as the "Beautiful Phyllanthus," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its striking foliage and flowering patterns, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic potential for various holistic health treatments.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus pulcher
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Phyllanthus.
