Valeriana tangutica

Valeriana tangutica, commonly known as Tangutica Valerian, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of China. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots possess calming and sedative properties. Often used to alleviate insomnia and anxiety, this botanical offers a natural approach to promoting relaxation and neurological balance.

  • Scientific name: Valeriana tangutica
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Dipsacales and the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Valeriana.
