Valeriana tangutica, scientifically known as Valeriana tangutica, is a perennial herb belonging to the Caprifoliaceae family. Native to the high-altitude regions of China, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional practices. It is primarily utilized for its calming properties, helping to alleviate insomnia, anxiety, and various neurological discomforts.
