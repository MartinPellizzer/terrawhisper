Aquilegia

Aquilegia, commonly known as columbine, is a captivating genus of flowering perennials celebrated for their unique, spurred petals. Beyond their ornamental beauty, various species have historical roles in traditional herbalism. While primarily used for aesthetic landscaping, they remain a fascinating subject of botanical study and cultural significance worldwide.

  • Family: Ranunculaceae

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Aquilegia.
