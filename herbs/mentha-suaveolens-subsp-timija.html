Atlas Mountain Mint, scientifically known as Mentha suaveolens subsp. timija, is a fragrant perennial herb native to the rugged Atlas Mountains. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, this botanical treasure is utilized in traditional medicine to soothe digestive ailments, reduce inflammation, and provide calming properties through its potent essential oils.
