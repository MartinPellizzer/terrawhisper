Rosa

The rose genus

Rosa, commonly known as roses, is a versatile genus celebrated for its exquisite blooms and profound medicinal properties. Beyond their beauty, various species offer therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. Often utilized in skincare and herbal remedies, rose extracts promote healing, soothe skin, and support overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Rosa
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales. Finally, it resides in the family Rosaceae and is identified by the genus Rosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Additionally, its presence extends to the island of Iceland and the territory of Føroyaar. The species is also well-documented throughout Great Britain. Together, these locations define the primary northern range for the genus Rosa.
