Lonicera angustifolia

Lonicera angustifolia, commonly known as Narrow-leaf Honeysuckle, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to North America. Renowned for its fragrant, tubular flowers and sweet nectar, this medicinal plant has historically been used in traditional herbalism to support respiratory health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall wellness through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Lonicera angustifolia
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Lonicera angustifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Dipsacales. Finally, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae within the genus Lonicera.

Distribution

This plant, Lonicera angustifolia, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Western Asia, its presence can be documented within the borders of Afghanistan. Moving eastward, it is found throughout South-Central China as well as the high-altitude regions of Tibet. The species also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the state of Assam. Finally, its range encompasses the rugged landscapes of the East Himalaya.
