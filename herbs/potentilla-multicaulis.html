What's the taxonomical classification of Potentilla multicaulis?

Potentiella multicaulis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, eventually placing it in the order Rosales. Within this order, the plant is a member of the family Rosaceae, which contains many well-known flowering species. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Potentiella, and its unique identity is defined by its specific epithet, multicaulis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Potentiella multicaulis has a perennial growth habit characterized by multiple stems arising from a woody rhizome, featuring pinnately compound leaves with five to seven leaflets. Each leaflet is typically ovate or lanceolate with serrated margins and a finely pubescent surface. The plant produces small, five-petaled yellow flowers that are borne on slender pedicels atop distinct stalks. These floral structures are clustered in terminal cymes that emerge from the leafy foliage. The overall morphology supports its ability to thrive in rocky or alpine environments.
