What's the taxonomical classification of Dunbaria circinalis?

Dunbaria circinalis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated within the order Fabales. It is a prominent representative of the family Fabaceae, a group known for its diverse botanical characteristics. Within this family, the plant is categorized under the genus Dunbaria, specifically identified by its scientific species name, circinalis.
