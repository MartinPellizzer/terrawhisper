Broad-leaved coral tree, scientifically known as Erythrina latissima, is a striking ornamental species native to South Africa. Renowned for its vibrant red flowers, this woody legume holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of the tree have been utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments like inflammation and infections.
