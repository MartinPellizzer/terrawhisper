Erythrina latissima

Erythrina latissima, commonly known as the Coral Tree, is a striking medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its vibrant red blossoms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the tree are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Erythrina latissima
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Erythrina, with the specific species name being latiessima.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving into the Southern Africa region, it can be found growing in Botswana. The species also extends its range into the Cape Provinces of South Africa. Additionally, it is known to inhabit the coastal and inland areas of KwaZulu-Natal.
