Psorospermum senegalense

Psorospermum senegalense, commonly known as the Senegalese bush, is a significant medicinal shrub native to African savannahs. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues, playing a vital role in ethnomedicine.

  • Scientific name: Psorospermum senegalense
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Psorospermum, specifically as the species Psorospermum senegalense.
