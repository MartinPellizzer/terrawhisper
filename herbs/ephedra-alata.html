Ephedra alata

Ephedra alata, commonly known as Alata Ephedra, is a medicinal shrub native to the arid regions of Central Asia. Renowned for its alkaloid content, particularly ephedrine, it has been used traditionally to treat respiratory ailments and boost energy. Today, it remains a significant subject in pharmacological research and ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra alata
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Gnetidae and the order Ephedrales. Finally, it resides in the family Ephedraceae and the genus Ephedra, specifically identified as the species Ephedra alatas.
