Ammopiptanthus nanus

Ammopiptanthus nanus, commonly known as the Dwarf Ammopiptan, is a resilient medicinal herb found in remote mountainous terrains. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this compact plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Traditional healers utilize its extracts to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments, highlighting its vital role in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Ammopiptanthus nanus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ammopiptanthus.
