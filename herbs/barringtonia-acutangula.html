Barringtonia acutangula

Hijal

Barringtonia acutangula, commonly known as the Freshwater Mangrove, is a striking tropical tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its distinctive, spiky fruits and ornamental flowers, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, showcasing its unique biochemical potential.
