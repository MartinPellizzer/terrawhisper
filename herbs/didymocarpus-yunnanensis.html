What's the taxonomical classification of Didymocarpus yunnanensis?

Didymocarpus yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Gesneriaceae. Ultimately, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Didymocarpus, and its unique species designation, yunnanensis.
