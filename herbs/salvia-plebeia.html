Australian sage, scientifically known as Salvia plebeia, is a resilient perennial herb native to Australia. Characterized by its aromatic, textured foliage and delicate flowering spikes, this plant is cherished in traditional medicine. It offers diverse therapeutic potential, often utilized for its soothing properties and its ability to support holistic wellness.
