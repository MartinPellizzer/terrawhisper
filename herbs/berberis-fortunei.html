Berberis fortunei

Berberis fortunei, commonly known as Fortune's barberry, is a resilient deciduous shrub prized for its ornamental beauty. Featuring arching branches and vibrant foliage, it often produces delicate yellow flowers and striking red berries. This hardy species thrives in diverse landscapes, making it a versatile addition to many ornamental gardens.

  • Scientific name: Berberis fortunei
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is specifically situated in the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Berberis.
