Chiantonthus retusus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. This species is a prominent representative of the family Oleaceae, situated within the genus Chionanthus, and is specifically identified by the species name retusus.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Chionanthus retusus has pinnately compound leaves with 5 to 12 pairs of ovate to elliptical leaflets that feature entire margins and obtuse apices. These leaflets are arranged oppositely along the rachis and typically possess a smooth, glabrous texture. The plant produces terminal panicles of small, creamy white, four-petaled flowers that bloom in late spring. Its woody structure includes spreading branches and a trunk covered in gray to brownish bark. During the autumn, the tree develops small, fleshy, dark purple drupes that serve as a primary dispersal mechanism.
