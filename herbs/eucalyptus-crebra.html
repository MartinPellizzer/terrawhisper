Ironbark, scientifically known as Eucalyptus crebra, is a resilient Australian tree distinguished by its rugged, deeply fissured bark. Renowned for its medicinal properties, its essential oils offer potent antimicrobial and decongestant benefits. This hardy species plays a vital role in traditional healing and supporting respiratory health through aromatherapy.
