Eucalyptus crebra

Eucalyptus crebra, commonly known as Narrow-leaved Red Gum, is a resilient Australian native tree. Renowned for its distinctive reddish bark and medicinal properties, it has long been valued in traditional healing. Its essential oils offer potent antimicrobial and decongestant benefits, making it a significant species in botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus crebra
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae, it is classified under the genus Eucalyptus.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as the Red Bloodwood, relies primarily on its leaves for its medicinal properties. The leaves of Eucalyptus crebra are characterized by their lanceolate shape and leathery texture, containing concentrated essential oils that are highly valued in traditional medicine. When crushed or distilled, these leaves release potent aromatic compounds often used to treat respiratory ailments, alleviate congestion, and provide antiseptic benefits.
