Styrax

Styrax, commonly known as storax or benzoin, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its aromatic resin. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antiseptic properties. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, this resinous powerhouse remains a vital component in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Styrax
  • Family: Styracaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Styracaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Styrax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several Mediterranean regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of France. Its presence extends into Southeastern Europe, where it is native to Albania. The species is also well-established in the country of Greece. Furthermore, it is located in Italy and on the island of Kriti.
