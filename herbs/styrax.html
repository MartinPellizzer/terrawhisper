Storax

Styrax · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (11 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Storax, scientifically known as Styrax, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its aromatic resin. Historically used in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and expectorant properties. Often derived from the bark, this resin serves as a valuable remedy for respiratory ailments and skin irritations across various cultures.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several Mediterranean regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of France. Its presence extends into Southeastern Europe, where it is native to Albania. The species is also well-established in the country of Greece. Furthermore, it is located in Italy and on the island of Kriti.
