Spatholobus

Spatholobus, commonly known as the "blood-circulating herb," is a significant medicinal plant in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent properties, it is primarily used to invigorate blood circulation, dispel stasis, and alleviate pain. This versatile botanical plays a crucial role in treating various gynecological and cardiovascular conditions.

  • Scientific name: Spatholobus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is the genus Spatholobus.
