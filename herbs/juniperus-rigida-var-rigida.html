Juniperus rigida var. rigida

Juniperus rigida var. rigida, commonly known as the rigid juniper, is a resilient evergreen conifer native to the high-altitude rocky slopes of East Asia. Renowned for its hardy nature, this botanical species possesses unique phytochemical properties, making it a subject of interest in traditional medicine and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus rigida var. rigida
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling into the family Cupressaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Juniperus as the variety Juniperus rigida var. rigida.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Juniperus rigida var. rigida, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly called the rigid juniper, while in Spanish, it is known as el enebro rígido. Furthermore, in the local context of its native regions, it is identified as el enebro rígido de las montañas.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. Moving into China, it can be found spanning across Inner Mongolia and the Manchuria area. The species also extends into China North-Central territories. Finally, its range reaches as far west as the Qinghai province.
