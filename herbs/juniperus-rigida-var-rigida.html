, scientifically known as Juniperus rigida var. rigida, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude regions of East Asia. Renowned for its ability to thrive in rocky, subalpine environments, this hardy juniper offers significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally used in various medicinal practices for its unique bioactive properties.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. Moving into China, it can be found spanning across Inner Mongolia and the Manchuria area. The species also extends into China North-Central territories. Finally, its range reaches as far west as the Qinghai province.
