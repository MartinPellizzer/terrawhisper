Euphorbia palustris

Euphorbia palustris, commonly known as Marsh Spurge, is a striking perennial valued for its dramatic architectural foliage and lime-green bracts. While primarily used in ornamental gardening to add texture to damp landscapes, its unique botanical properties continue to intrigue researchers studying the diverse chemical compounds within the Euphorbia genus.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia palustris
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Euphorbia. Consequently, its specific scientific name is Euphorbia palustris.
