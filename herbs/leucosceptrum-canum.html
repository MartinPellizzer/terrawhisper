Plant Parts

This plant, Leucosceptrum canum, possesses several distinct morphological and chemical features that contribute to its medicinal profile. The morphology is characterized by its prominent leaves and delicate flowers, which serve as the primary structures for identification. A key anatomical feature is the presence of glandular trichomes, which are specialized hair-like structures on the plant surface. These glandular trichomes play a crucial role in the plant's biology, acting as sites for the synthesis and storage of various bioactive compounds. When researchers analyze the chemical composition of these parts, they often focus on the total sesquiterpenoid extract, a significant secondary metabolite group derived from these specialized structures that contributes to the plant's therapeutic potential.
