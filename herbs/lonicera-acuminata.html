Lonicera acuminata

Lonicera acuminata, commonly known as the slender honeysuckle, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat respiratory ailments, inflammation, and skin conditions, offering a rich source of bioactive phytochemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Lonicera acuminata
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales, specifically within the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lonicera.
