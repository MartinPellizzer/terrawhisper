Distribution

This plant, known as Canavalia, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. It is also found throughout the territories of Burkina and the Gambia. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the nation of Ghana. Finally, its distribution includes the coastal and inland areas of Guinea-Bissau.
