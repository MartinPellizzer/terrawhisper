Kadsura angustifolia

Kadsura angustifolia, commonly known as the aromatic star anise vine, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it is traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Kadsura angustifolia
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Kadsura angustifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Austrobaileyales and the family Schisandraceae. It is further identified by the genus Kadsura.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Kadsura angustifolia, exhibits a specific range within East and Southeast Asia. Its primary geographical distribution includes the southeastern regions of China. Furthermore, the species can be found extending into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the country of Vietnam.
