Eleutherococcus brachypus

Eleutherococcus brachypus, commonly known as Siberian ginseng, is a potent adaptogen renowned for its ability to enhance physical and mental resilience. This medicinal shrub helps the body manage stress, boosts immune function, and increases energy levels. It is highly valued in traditional herbalism for promoting overall vitality and equilibrium.

  • Scientific name: Eleutherococcus brachypus
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Araliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Eleutherococcus.
