What's the taxonomical classification of Urceola rosea?

Urceola rosea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Gentianales, it falls under the family Apocynaceae. At the most specific taxonomic levels, the plant is identified by the genus Urceola and the species rosea.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Urceola rosea has opposite, ovate to elliptical leaves that are typically 3 to 10 centimeters long with entire margins and an acute apex. These leaves are arranged along woody, somewhat succulent stems that can reach heights of up to 3 meters. The plant produces trumpet-shaped, pink to rose-colored flowers that emerge from terminal or axillary clusters. Each blossom features a tubular corolla that flares into five lobes, often accompanied by small, fleshy fruits. The overall structure is that of a perennial shrub with a somewhat sprawling or upright growth habit.
