Rinorea subintegrifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Rinorea subintegrifolia?

Rinorea subintegrifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is positioned in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Malpighiales and is a member of the family Violaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Rinorea and its unique species designation, subintegrifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rinorea subintegrifolia has simple, elliptical to oblong leaves that are typically subentire along the margins, meaning the edges are nearly smooth but may possess very slight indentations. The leaf arrangement is opposite, with each petiole supporting a blade that features prominent pinnate venation. The plant exhibits a woody structure characteristic of its genus, often presenting as a shrub or small tree. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced, which are typically borne in clusters or along the stems.
