Rauvolfia micrantha

Rauvolfia micrantha, commonly known as the small-flowered Rauvolfia, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat hypertension, insomnia, and various neurological disorders. Its bioactive alkaloids offer profound potential for developing modern cardiovascular and sedative therapies.

  • Scientific name: Rauvolfia micrantha
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Rauvolfia.

Common Names

This plant, Rauvolfia micrantha, is known by various regional names across different linguistic groups.
