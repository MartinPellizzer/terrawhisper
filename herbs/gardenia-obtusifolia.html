Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of the Indo-China region. Within this area, its presence is documented throughout the country of Cambodia. It also grows naturally across the territories of Laos and Myanmar. Furthermore, the species can be found flourishing in the tropical environments of Thailand. Finally, its geographical range extends into the lush forests of Vietnam.
