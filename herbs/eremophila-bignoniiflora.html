Eremophila bignoniiflora

Eremophila bignoniiflora, commonly known as the desert sandbush, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid regions. Renowned for its unique aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments and skin irritations, offering a natural remedy rooted in its remarkable evolutionary adaptation.

  • Scientific name: Eremophila bignoniiflora
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic refinement places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. It is a member of the family Scrophulariaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Eremophilia.
