Geum aleppicum

Yellow avens

Geum aleppium, commonly known as Aleppian Avens, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, this plant has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries. It possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, often serving as a natural remedy for digestive and respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Geum aleppicum
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Geum, specifically as the species Geum aleppicum.
