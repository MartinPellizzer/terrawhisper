Palicourea nemorosa

Palicourea nemorosa, commonly known as "falsa" or "coronilla," is a medicinal shrub native to the Neotropics. Renowned for its potent biological activities, it is traditionally used for its emmenagogue and contraceptive properties. Modern research focuses on its alkaloids, exploring their significant potential in developing new pharmacological treatments.

  • Scientific name: Palicourea nemorosa
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Palicourea nemorosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, following the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rubiaceae within the genus Palicourea.
