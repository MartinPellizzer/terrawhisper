Astragalus bhotanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Astragalus bhotanensis?

Astragalus bhotanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Fabales. This plant is a member of the family Fabaceae and is further categorized into the genus Astragalus, with its specific identity defined by the species name bhotanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Astragalus bhotanensis has a perennial growth habit characterized by erect stems, trifoliate leaves with small leaflets, and dense, papilionaceous flowers. The plant typically features long-stalked peduncles that bear several small, pea-like blossoms. Its leaves are composed of three distinct leaflets that are often covered in fine, silvery hairs. The flowers are usually light purple or pinkish in color and emerge from the leaf axils. The root system is deep and woody, helping the species anchor itself in alpine environments.
