Saurauia roxburghii

Saurauia roxburghii, commonly known as the Saurauia plant, is a significant medicinal species valued in traditional healing practices. Native to tropical regions, it is frequently utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and skin ailments, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and herbal medicine.

  • Family: Actinidiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
