bashfulplant, scientifically known as Mimosa pigra, is a perennial shrub native to tropical regions. Often recognized by its delicate, feathery foliage and pink pom-pom flowers, it possesses medicinal properties used in traditional healing. However, it is also a notorious invasive species, rapidly transforming diverse wetland ecosystems into monocultures.
