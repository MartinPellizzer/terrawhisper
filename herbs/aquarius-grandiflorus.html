Aquarius grandiflorus

Aquarius grandiflorus, commonly known as the Great-Flowered Water Lily, is a remarkable medicinal aquatic plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this species contains potent bioactive compounds used in traditional healing to treat inflammation, soothe digestive ailments, and promote overall restorative wellness in diverse ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Aquarius grandiflorus
  • Family: Alismataceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Alismatales and the family Alismataceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Aquarius, specifically as the species Aquarius grandiflorus.
