Irvingia grandifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Irvingia grandifolia?

Irvingia grandifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Malpighiales and the family Irvingiaceae. Finally, the plant is situated within the genus Irvingia, with its specific designation being the species grandifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Irvingia grandifolia has large, broadly elliptic to oblong leaves that typically measure between 15 and 35 centimeters in length and possess a leathery texture with an entire margin. The foliage is characterized by an obtuse or rounded apex and a cuneate base, often presenting a deep green color on the upper surface. Its woody stem structure supports a canopy of dense branching, which is typical for many species within the Irvingia genus. The plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that eventually develop into fleshy, oval-shaped drupes. These fruits contain a single large seed that is highly valued for its nutritional content.

Its distribution follows the humid equatorial belt, which provides the specific environmental conditions required for its growth.
