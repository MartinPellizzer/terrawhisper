Herb-Robert, scientifically known as Geranium robertianum, is a charming perennial herb recognized by its delicate, fern-like foliage and vibrant pink flowers. Often found in shady, moist woodlands, this plant possesses diverse medicinal properties, traditionally used in herbal remedies to support respiratory health and alleviate various inflammatory conditions.
