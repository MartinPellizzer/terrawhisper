Geranium robertianum

Herb-robert

Geranium robertianum, commonly known as Herb Robert, is a delicate perennial herb distinguished by its finely divided, fern-like leaves and tiny pink flowers. Found in shaded, damp environments, this resilient plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its astringent properties and potential therapeutic uses in skin and digestive care.

  • Scientific name: Geranium robertianum
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Geranium.

Common Names

This plant, Geranium robertianum, is known by various names across different Chinese linguistic regions. In Simplified Chinese, as used in mainland China (zh-cn), Singapore (zh-sg), and general contexts (zh-hans), it is referred to as 汉荭鱼腥草. Similarly, in Traditional Chinese contexts such as those in Hong Kong (zh-hk), it is called 漢葒魚腥草, a naming convention that is also maintained in other Traditional Chinese regions (zh-hant).
