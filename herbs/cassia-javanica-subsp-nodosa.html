Cassia javanica subsp. nodosa

Cassia javaica subsp. nodosa, commonly known as Java Cassia, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this subspecies is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering a potent natural resource for holistic health and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Cassia javanica subsp. nodosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling into the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cassia as the specific subspecies nodosas.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several tropical regions. In the Indo-China zone, it can be found growing within the borders of Thailand. Moving into the Malesia region, its presence extends to the island of Jawa. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the area known as Malaya. These locations highlight the specific environmental niches where the subspecies thrives.
