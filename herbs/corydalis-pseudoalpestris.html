Corydalis pseudoalpestris

What's the taxonomical classification of Corydalis pseudoalpestris?

Corydalis pseudoalpestris belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales. This plant is further categorized within the family Papaveraceae, which contains the genus Corydalis, and is specifically identified by its species name, pseudoalpestris.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Corydalis pseudoalpestris has basal leaves that are deeply divided into narrow, linear segments and upright, nectar-producing flowers characterized by a long, spurred corolla. The foliage is typically glaucous in color and arranged in a rosette pattern at the base of the stem. Each flower features a distinctively elongated spur that extends backward from the petals to house nectar. The plant produces delicate, terminal inflorescences that rise above the basal leaf cluster. Its overall structure is adapted to the moist, rocky alpine environments where it grows.
