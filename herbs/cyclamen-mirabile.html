Cyclamen mirabile

Cyclamen mirabile, commonly known as the Miraculous Cyclamen, is a captivating perennial native to specific Mediterranean regions. Renowned for its striking, heart-shaped foliage and delicate, intricately patterned blooms, this plant offers both aesthetic elegance and historical interest, making it a prized specimen for botanical enthusiasts and medicinal plant researchers.

  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
