Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct tropical and Atlantic regions. Within the Macaronesian islands, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. Moving to the mainland, it is widely spread throughout West Tropical Africa. Its range includes several countries such as Benin, Burkina, and the Gambia. Additionally, the species can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Ghana.

The physiology of Ficus thonningsii is characterized by a standard C3 photosynthetic pathway, meaning it utilizes the Calvin cycle for carbon fixation without the specialized adaptations found in C4 or CAM metabolism. This metabolic process involves the initial fixation of atmospheric carbon dioxide by the enzyme RuBisCO to produce 3-phosphoglycerate.
