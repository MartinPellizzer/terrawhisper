Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of the Southern Hemisphere. In Brazil, its presence is specifically noted throughout the southern portion of the country. Moving into Southern South America, the species can be found in the northeast, south, and northwest regions of Argentina. Additionally, the plant extends its range into the neighboring territory of Paraguay. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Urtica spatulata.

Plant Parts

This plant, Urtica spatulaata, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties. The most common method of preparation involves using the green leaves and the broader aerial parts of the organism to extract bioactive compounds. In some traditional applications, the leaf itself is processed directly, while other treatments focus on the underground root system. Specifically, the dried roots are often harvested to provide a concentrated source of nutrients, ensuring that every part of the plant contributes to its therapeutic potential.
