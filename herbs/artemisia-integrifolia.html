Artemisia integrifolia

Slender-leaf wormwood

Artemisia integrifolia, commonly known as the desert wormwood, is a resilient perennial shrub native to arid regions of the Middle East and North Africa. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments, skin conditions, and various inflammatory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia integrifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the Asteraceae family, falling under the genus Artemisia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across specific regions of Eastern Europe and Siberia. In Eastern Europe, its presence can be documented within the borders of Belarus. Moving into the Siberian region, it is found in the republic of Buryatiya. The species also populates the areas surrounding Chitа and Irkutsk. Finally, its range extends further into the Krasnoyarsk region of Siberia.

When harvesting for medicinal purposes, practitioners focus on these aerial parts to ensure the effective collection of the plant's potent chemical constituents.
