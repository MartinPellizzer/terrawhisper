Plant Parts

This plant, Artemisia integrifolia, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its aerial parts. These above-ground components, which include the stems and the aromatic, silvery-green leaves, contain the essential oils and bioactive compounds necessary for therapeutic use. When harvesting for medicinal purposes, practitioners focus on these aerial parts to ensure the effective collection of the plant's potent chemical constituents.
