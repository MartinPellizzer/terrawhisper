Sideritis sipylea

Sideritis sipylea, commonly known as a medicinal shrub native to the Mediterranean, is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and inflammatory conditions. Its unique phytochemical profile makes it a subject of significant interest in ethnopharmacology.

Common Names

This plant, Sideritis sipylea, is known by various names depending on the local language and region. In Greek, it is commonly referred to as σιδηρίτης (sidiritis) or σιδηρίτης της Σπήλης (sidiritis tis Spilis), while in English, it is often called Sipylus ironwort. In Turkish, it is known as Sipylüs çayı, reflecting its traditional use in herbal infusions.
