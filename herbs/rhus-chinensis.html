Chinese gall, scientifically known as Rhus chinensis, is a medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its resinous galls are harvested for their unique therapeutic properties. Often used to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues, this plant remains a vital component of herbal pharmacopeias.
