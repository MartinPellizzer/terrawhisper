Triadica rotundifolia

Triadica rotundifolia, commonly known as the sea grape, is a versatile coastal shrub prized in traditional medicine. Native to tropical regions, its fleshy leaves and fruits are rich in bioactive compounds. Historically used to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to gastrointestinal issues, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Triadica rotundifolia
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
