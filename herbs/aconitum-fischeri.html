Azure Monkshood, scientifically known as Aconitum fischeri, is a striking perennial native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its vibrant blue, helmet-shaped flowers, this species belongs to a genus famous for potent alkaloids. While visually captivating, it requires extreme caution due to its high toxicity and complex medicinal properties.
