Cupu assu, scientifically known as Theobroma grandiflorum, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its nutrient-dense seeds, it offers potent antioxidant properties and bioactive compounds. Often utilized in traditional healing, this botanical treasure provides significant therapeutic potential for enhancing overall wellness and vitality.
