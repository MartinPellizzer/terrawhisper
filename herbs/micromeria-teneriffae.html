Distribution

This plant is characterized by a very specific and restricted geographical range. It is endemic to the Macaronesia region, which includes several volcanic archipelagos. Within this area, its distribution is primarily concentrated in the Canary Islands. Because of this localized presence, it is considered a key component of the local flora. Consequently, the species is a unique biological representative of the Canary Islands' natural landscape.
