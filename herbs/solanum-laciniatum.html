Kangaroo-Apple, scientifically known as Solanum laciniatum, is a distinctive Australian shrub renowned for its glossy, dark green foliage and striking orange berries. While visually captivating, it contains potent alkaloids, requiring careful handling. Traditionally used in indigenous medicine, this resilient species remains a significant subject in botanical and pharmacological studies.
