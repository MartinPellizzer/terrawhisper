Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Paris, is a perennial herb characterized by a distinct morphological structure. The medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in its underground organs, specifically within the thick, fleshy rhizomes and the fibrous roots that anchor it to the soil. Above the surface, the plant features broad, dark green leaves that grow in a characteristic whorl. These above-ground parts support the plant's photosynthetic processes, eventually giving rise to a single, solitary flower that emerges from the center of the foliage.
