Calystegia soldanella

Sea bindweed

Calystegia soldanella, commonly known as the sea purslane, is a resilient succulent native to coastal environments. Renowned for its ability to thrive in saline soils, this medicinal plant offers unique bioactive properties. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits in various treatments.

  • Scientific name: Calystegia soldanella
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Solanales and is a member of the Convolvulaceae family. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Calystegia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Calystegia soldanella, is characterized by its prostrate growth habit, where the stems play a crucial role in its survival in coastal environments. These stems grow horizontally along the ground, acting as stolons that allow the plant to spread across sandy substrates and establish new clones. By creeping along the surface, the stems help stabilize the plant against wind and shifting sands while connecting various nodes that can develop roots, ensuring the plant's persistence in its specialized habitat.
