Sea Bindweed, scientifically known as Calystegia soldanella, is a resilient coastal succulent found along sandy shorelines. Characterized by its fleshy leaves and delicate white flowers, this plant plays a vital role in stabilizing dunes. While primarily valued for ecological protection, it holds intriguing potential within traditional maritime herbal practices.
