Boronia

Boronia, commonly known as aromatic shrubs, belongs to the Rutaceae family and is native to Australia. Renowned for their delicate, fragrant flowers and striking foliage, these plants are highly prized in perfumery and horticulture. They offer a unique blend of visual beauty and intense, uplifting citrus-scented essential oils.

  • Scientific name: Boronia
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Boronia.

Distribution

This plant is native to the diverse landscapes across the Australian continent. Its geographical range extends through the coastal and inland regions of New South Wales and Queensland. It is also found within the tropical and arid environments of the Northern Territory. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout the southern territories of South Australia. Finally, its presence can be noted as far south as the island state of Tasmania.
