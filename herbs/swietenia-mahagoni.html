Swietenia mahagoni

Small-leaved mahogany

Swietenia mahagoni, commonly known as mahogany, is a majestic tropical tree valued for its durable timber and diverse medicinal properties. Historically utilized in traditional practices, its bark and leaves possess antioxidant and antimicrobial qualities. Researchers continue exploring its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Swietenia mahagoni
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Swietenia.
