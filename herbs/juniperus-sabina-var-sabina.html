Sabiina, scientifically known as Juniperus sabiina var. sabiina, is an evergreen coniferous shrub native to Europe and Western Asia. Valued in traditional medicine for its aromatic essential oils, it possesses antiseptic and diuretic properties. However, due to its high toxicity, it must be used with extreme caution and precision.

Plant Parts

This plant, Juniperus sabina var. sabina, is a coniferous species where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The needle-like leaves are a primary source of bioactive compounds, while the small, fleshy fruits—often referred to as a single berry or fruit depending on the context—contain concentrated medicinal elements. These fruits are particularly valued for their ability to yield high-quality essential oils, which are extracted to harness the plant's potent aromatic and chemical characteristics for medicinal use.
