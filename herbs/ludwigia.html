Ludwigia

Ludwigia pic, 1891 non bayle, 1878

Ludwigia, commonly known as primrose-willow, is a diverse genus of herbaceous plants found in wetland habitats worldwide. Renowned for their vibrant yellow flowers, these aquatic species play vital roles in local ecosystems. Beyond their aesthetic appeal, various Ludwigia species are studied for their diverse phytochemical properties and medicinal potential.
