Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several regions of East Asia. Within China, its range extends through the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Beyond the Chinese mainland, it is also widely distributed throughout Japan. Furthermore, the species can be found growing in Korea.
