What's the taxonomical classification of Pedicularis henryi?

Pedicularis henryi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. This plant is a member of the family Orobanchaceae, falling under the genus Pedicularis, and is specifically identified by its species name, henryi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pedicularis henryi has erect stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and are adorned with small, lanceolate leaves that are often deeply lobed or pinnately divided. Each flower features a prominent upper lip that is hooded and a smaller lower lip used to facilitate pollination.
