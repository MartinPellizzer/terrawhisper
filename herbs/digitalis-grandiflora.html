Digitalis grandiflora

Yellow foxglove

Digitalis grandiflora, commonly known as Large-flowered Foxglove, is a striking perennial native to the Caucasus Mountains. Renowned for its towering spikes of bell-shaped blooms, this botanical marvel is highly valued in ornamental gardening. However, it contains potent cardiac glycosides, requiring careful handling due to its significant medicinal toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Digitalis grandiflora
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Digitalis.
