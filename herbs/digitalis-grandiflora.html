Scientific literature: Very Sparse (12 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Yellow foxglove, scientifically known as Digitalis grandiflora, is a striking perennial prized for its tall spires of large, bell-shaped yellow flowers. Native to mountain regions, this botanical wonder is significant in pharmacology due to its potent cardiac glycosides, though it must be handled with extreme caution and expertise.
