Ziziphus xylopyrus

Ziziphus xylopyrus, commonly known as the wild jujube, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. Its unique fruit and bark offer significant bioactive compounds for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphus xylopyrus
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Rhamnaceae.
