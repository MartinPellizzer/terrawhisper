Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the globe. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is noted within the borders of Kenya. Its reach extends into China, specifically throughout the Southeast region of the country. Within Eastern Asia, the species can be found in both Taiwan and the Nansei-shotto islands.
