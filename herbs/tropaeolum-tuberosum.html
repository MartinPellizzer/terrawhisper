Mashua, scientifically known as Tropaeoluum tuberosum, is a resilient tuberous plant native to the high Andes. Valued for centuries in traditional Andean medicine, this pungent root offers significant therapeutic potential. It is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties, serving as a vital nutritional and medicinal resource.
