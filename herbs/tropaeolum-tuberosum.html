Tropaeolum tuberosum

Tropaeolum tuberosum, commonly known as nasturtium tuber, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its nutrient-rich edible tubers. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This hardy perennial offers a unique combination of culinary utility and therapeutic potential for promoting overall wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Tropaeolum tuberosum
  • Family: Tropaeolaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Tropaeoluum tuberosum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Brassicales and the family Tropaeolaeaceae. It is further categorized into the genus Tropaeoluum.
