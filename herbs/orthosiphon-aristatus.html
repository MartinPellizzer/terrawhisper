Orthosiphon aristatus

Cat's whiskers

Orthosiiphon aristatus, commonly known as Cat's Claw, is a powerful medicinal vine native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, this botanical powerhouse is widely utilized in traditional medicine to support immune function, combat infections, and alleviate joint pain and digestive issues naturally.

  • Scientific name: Orthosiphon aristatus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by its genus, Orthosiphon.

Common Names

This plant, Orthosiphon aristatus, is known by several distinct common names depending on the language and region. In Indonesia, it is widely referred to as Kumis kucing (id) and also as Brengos kucing (jv) in Javanese. The Sundanese language calls it Kumis ucing (su), while in Vietnamese, it is known as Cỏ râu mèo (vi). Additionally, in Malayalam, the plant is identified as പൂച്ചമീശ (ml).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence also extends to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the Assam region. Together, these locations highlight the diverse habitats where the plant thrives.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Cat's Whiskers, utilizes various components for its therapeutic properties. The lush, green leaves are the primary source of its medicinal value, often being harvested and dried to be used as tea leaves for infusion. Beyond the visible foliage, the complex biological tissues within the plant contain the essential phytochemicals necessary for healing. While the slender stems provide the structural support for the plant's growth, the most potent concentrated forms are often derived from a specialized leaf extract, which is used in various traditional and modern remedies.
