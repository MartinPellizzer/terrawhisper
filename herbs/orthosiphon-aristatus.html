Cat's whiskers

Orthosiphon aristatus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (46 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Cat's whiskers, scientifically known as Orthosiphon aristatus, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to Malaysia. Characterized by its unique, thread-like flowers, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It is primarily utilized for its potent diuretic properties, helping to support kidney health and treat various urinary tract ailments.

Beyond the visible foliage, the complex biological tissues within the plant contain the essential phytochemicals necessary for healing.
