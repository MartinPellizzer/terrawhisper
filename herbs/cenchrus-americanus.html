Indian millet

Cenchrus americanus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (11 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Indian millet, scientifically known as Cenchrus americanus, is a resilient annual grass valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Primarily grown as a staple cereal, it serves as a vital food source in various cultures. Beyond nutrition, it is recognized in traditional medicine for supporting overall health and wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, falling within the family Poaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Cenchrus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence is noted within Great Britain. Moving to Southwestern Europe, it can be found in both Spain and Sardegna. The species also extends into Southeastern Europe, specifically inhabiting Italy. Finally, its range reaches the European portion of Türkiye.
