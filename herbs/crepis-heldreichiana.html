Crepis heldreichiana

Crepis heldreichiana, commonly known as the Heldreich's hawkwood, is a resilient perennial herb native to the rocky terrains of the Balkans. Valued in traditional herbalism, this plant contains bioactive compounds with potential anti-inflammatory properties, offering a fascinating subject for pharmacological research into natural remedies for various healing processes.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
