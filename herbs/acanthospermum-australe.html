Acanthospermum australe

Paraguayan starburr

Acanthospermum australe, commonly known as spiny rosette or burr plant, is a resilient herbaceous annual found in tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. This plant is frequently studied for its potential bioactive compounds and its diverse applications in treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Acanthospermum australe
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Its scientific classification concludes with the genus Acanthospermum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Acanthospermum australe, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans) and standard Chinese (zh), it is called 南方刺苞果, while in Traditional Chinese (zh-hant) and the Cantonese dialect used in Hong Kong (zh-hk), it is commonly known as 刺苞果. Additionally, in the Guarani language (gn), the plant is identified as Tapekue.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented within the borders of Germany. Moving toward the equatorial regions, it is found throughout West-Central Tropical Africa in both Cameroon and the DR Congo. The distribution also extends into South Tropical Africa, specifically within the country of Angola. Finally, its reach continues further east to include the nation of Mozambique.
