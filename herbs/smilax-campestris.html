Smilax campestris

Smilax campestris, commonly known as the woody Sarsaparilla, is a climbing medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its restorative properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and blood purification. This resilient plant offers valuable bioactive compounds for holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Smilax campestris
  • Family: Smilacaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Smilacaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Smilax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of South America. In the western part of the continent, its presence has been documented in Bolivia. Its range extends extensively through Brazil, covering various distinct ecological zones. Specifically, it can be found throughout the North, Northeast, and Southeast regions of Brazil. Additionally, the species is distributed across the West-Central territories of the country.
