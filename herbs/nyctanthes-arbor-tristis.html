Nyctanthes arbor-tristis

Nyctanthes arbor-tristris, commonly known as Night-flowering Jasmine, is a fragrant shrub prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its intoxicatingly scented blooms, it possesses significant therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized in Ayurvedic practices to alleviate various ailments, including skin disorders, inflammation, and nervous system imbalances, offering holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Nyctanthes arbor-tristis
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, scientifically known as Nyctanthes arbor-tristris, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Oleaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with the specific genus Nyctanthes.
