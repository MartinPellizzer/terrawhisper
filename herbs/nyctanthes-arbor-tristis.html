Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Night-flowering Jasmine, possesses several parts utilized for their medicinal properties. The broad, green leaves are frequently employed in traditional remedies, and a single leaf can often be used to prepare topical applications. The fragrant, white flowers are perhaps the most distinctive feature, used for their calming effects, while the small seeds are also valued in various herbal formulations. Additionally, the stem bark is harvested to extract bioactive compounds used in treating different ailments.
