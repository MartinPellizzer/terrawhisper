Trachyspermum ammi

Carum, ajowan

Trachyspermum ammi, commonly known as ajwain or carom seeds, is a potent medicinal herb cherished in Ayurvedic traditions. Renowned for its pungent aroma and essential oils, it serves as a powerful digestive aid, offering relief from indigestion, bloating, and respiratory issues while providing significant antimicrobial and antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Trachyspermum ammi
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is a member of the Apiaceae family. It is scientifically classified under the genus Trachyspermum, with its specific species designation being ammum.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Ajwain, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its aromatic properties derived from its various components. The medicinal potency is primarily concentrated in the essential oils, which are extracted from the plant to treat digestive ailments. These essential oils are most abundantly found within the small, ridged fruit of the species. Within these fruits, the tiny seeds serve as the primary source of therapeutic value, specifically containing the zygotic embryos that house the concentrated chemical compounds necessary for its healing effects.
