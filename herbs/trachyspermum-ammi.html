Carum, Ajowan

Trachyspermum ammi · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (456 studies) · ★★★★★

Carum, Ajowan, scientifically known as Trachyspermum ammi, is a potent medicinal herb celebrated in Ayurvedic tradition. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it possesses powerful carminative, antiseptic, and digestive properties. Commonly used to alleviate bloating and indigestion, this versatile plant remains a cornerstone in natural wellness and traditional pharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Ajwain, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its aromatic properties derived from its various components. The medicinal potency is primarily concentrated in the essential oils, which are extracted from the plant to treat digestive ailments. These essential oils are most abundantly found within the small, ridged fruit of the species. Within these fruits, the tiny seeds serve as the primary source of therapeutic value, specifically containing the zygotic embryos that house the concentrated chemical compounds necessary for its healing effects.
