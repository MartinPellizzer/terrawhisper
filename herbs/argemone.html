Prickly-poppy, scientifically known as Argemone, is a resilient herbaceous plant belonging to the Papaveraceae family. Commonly found in disturbed soils, it is recognized by its distinctive bluish-green leaves and bright yellow flowers. While it holds significant ethnobotanical value for its medicinal properties, it requires careful handling due to toxicity.
