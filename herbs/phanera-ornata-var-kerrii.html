Phanera ornata var. kerrii

Phanera ornata var. kerrii, commonly known as a rare medicinal climber, is a specialized botanical variety valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique foliage and climbing habit, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, providing essential bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments within indigenous medicinal systems worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Phanera ornata var. kerrii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
