Annona stenophylla subsp. cuneata

Annona stenophylla subsp. cuneata, commonly known as the wedge-leaved custard apple, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its unique botanical characteristics, this subspecies is studied for its potential therapeutic properties, offering valuable insights into traditional healing practices and modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Annona stenophylla subsp. cuneata
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Magnoliales and the family Annonaceae. Finally, it is identified as a member of the genus Annona, specifically categorized as the subspecies Annona stenophylla subsp. cuneata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution limited to certain regions of the African continent. It can be found within West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically located in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Additionally, its range extends into South Tropical Africa, where it is native to Angola. These two distinct areas represent the primary habitats where this subspecies thrives. Consequently, its presence is concentrated within these specific tropical and subtropical zones.
