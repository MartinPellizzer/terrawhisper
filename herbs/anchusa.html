Alkanet, scientifically known as Anchusa, is a remarkable medicinal herb prized for its vibrant coloring properties and therapeutic potential. Belonging to the Boraginaceae family, this plant contains pyrrolizidine alkaloids and essential oils, traditionally used in topical salves to soothe skin irritations, promote healing, and reduce localized inflammation effectively.
