Cuphea glutinosa

Cuphea glutinosa, commonly known as the sticky cuphea, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique adhesive properties and vibrant foliage, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize its specific chemical compounds to treat various ailments, showcasing its vital role in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Cuphea glutinosa
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Myrtaleales and the family Lythraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cuphea as the species Cuphea glutinosa.
