Pluchea sagittalis

Pluchea sagittalis, commonly known as Arrowleaf Pluchea, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical wetlands. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin inflammations, and respiratory issues. This plant serves as a vital natural resource in various ethnobotanical healing practices.
