Phyllolobium chinense

Phyllolobium chinense, commonly known as Chinese Phyllolobium, is a significant medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to address various ailments. Modern research continues to explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in holistic healthcare.

  • Scientific name: Phyllolobium chinense
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is specifically nested within the family Fabaceae. Common Names

This plant, Phyllolobium chinense, is known by several different common names across various Chinese linguistic contexts. In standard Chinese (zh), it is frequently referred to as 蔓黄芪, 扁茎黄芪, or 背扁黄芪. These same names are utilized in Simplified Chinese (zh-hans) and specifically within mainland China (zh-cn) as 蔓黄芪.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across various regions of China. It can be found throughout the South-Central parts of the country. Additionally, its presence extends into the northern territories of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. The species also inhabits the North-Central regions of China. Finally, its range reaches as far west as the province of Qinghai.
