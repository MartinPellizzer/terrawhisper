Flat-stem milk-vetch, scientifically known as Phyllolobium chinense, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality, serving as a vital resource in herbal medicine.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across various regions of China. It can be found throughout the South-Central parts of the country. Additionally, its presence extends into the northern territories of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. The species also inhabits the North-Central regions of China. Finally, its range reaches as far west as the province of Qinghai.
