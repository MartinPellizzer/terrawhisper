Polygonum molliiforme

What's the taxonomical classification of Polygonum molliiforme?

Polygonum molliiforme belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated within the order Caryophyllales. It is further categorized under the family Polygonaceae, falling into the genus Polygonum, and is specifically identified by its species name, molliiforme.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Polygonum molliiforme has an erect, herbaceous growth habit characterized by slender, reddish-tinted stems and soft, pubescent leaves that are typically ovate or lanceolate in shape. The leaf margins are entire, while the surfaces exhibit a fine coating of hairs that give the foliage a soft texture. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the leaf axils in clusters, often appearing in shades of white or pale pink. These blossoms are accompanied by ocreae, which are membranous sheaths at the base of the petioles typical of the Polygonaceae family.
