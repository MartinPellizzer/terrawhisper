Heart-leaf arnica, scientifically known as Arnica cordifolia, is a perennial herb native to North America's montane forests. Distinguished by its distinctive heart-shaped leaves, this plant belongs to the Asteraceae family. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, it is frequently utilized for its topical anti-inflammatory properties to soothe bruising and swelling.
