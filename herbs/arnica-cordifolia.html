Arnica cordifolia

Heart-leaf arnica

Arnica cordifolia, commonly known as Heart-leaf Arnica, is a perennial herb native to North American forests. Characterized by its distinctive cordate leaves and vibrant yellow blooms, this botanical species is valued in traditional herbalism. It is frequently utilized for its soothing properties to alleviate minor aches and skin irritations.

  • Scientific name: Arnica cordifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Arnica cordifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Arnica.

Distribution

This plant is found across several distinct regions of North America. In the Subarctic regions, its range includes the Northwest Territories and the Yukon. Moving south, it is widely distributed throughout Western Canada. Specifically, it can be located within the provinces of Alberta and British Columbia. Additionally, its presence extends eastward into the province of Manitoba.
