Cassia ferruginea var. vellozoana

Cassia ferruginea var. vellozoana, commonly known as a specialized variety of the ferruginous cassia, is a significant medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in ethnobotanical practices to treat various ailments, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Cassia ferruginea var. vellozoana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Cassia. Specifically, it is identified as the variety vellozoaana of the species Cassia ferruginea.
