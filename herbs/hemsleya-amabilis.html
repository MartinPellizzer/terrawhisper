Hemsleya amabilis

Hemsleya amabilis, commonly known as the Lovely Hemsley, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to the lush, subtropical forests of East Asia. Renowned for its delicate foliage and aromatic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal practices to support digestive health and provide soothing, therapeutic benefits for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Hemsleya amabilis
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
