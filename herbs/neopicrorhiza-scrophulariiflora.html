Distribution

This plant, Neopicrorhiza scrophulariiflora, exhibits a specific distribution across several key mountainous regions. Within China, it is primarily found throughout the South-Central areas as well as the high altitudes of Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it thrives in the East Himalaya. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Nepal. Finally, it can also be located throughout the West Himalaya region.

Plant Parts

This plant, Neopicrorhiza scrophulariiflora, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties which are concentrated in its specialized underground parts. The most significant component for therapeutic use is the rhizome, which grows deep within the soil. These thick, fleshy rhizomes serve as the main storage organs for the plant's bioactive compounds. In botanical studies of this species, the term root is often used colloquially, but specifically, it is the tuberous rhizome system and the associated roots that contain the essential bitter principles required for its medicinal applications.
