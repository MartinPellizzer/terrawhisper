Neopicrorhiza scrophulariiflora

Neopicrorhiza scrophulariiflora, commonly known as the Himalayan bitterroot, is a potent medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its intense bitterness, it contains bioactive compounds like picroside, which offer significant anti-inflammatory, hepatoprotective, and antioxidant properties, making it vital in traditional Ayurvedic medicine.

  • Scientific name: Neopicrorhiza scrophulariiflora
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Neopicrorhiza.

Distribution

This plant, Neopicrorhiza scrophulariiflora, exhibits a specific distribution across several key mountainous regions. Within China, it is primarily found throughout the South-Central areas as well as the high altitudes of Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it thrives in the East Himalaya. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Nepal. Finally, it can also be located throughout the West Himalaya region.
