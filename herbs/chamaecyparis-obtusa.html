Hinoki Cypress

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (39 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Hinoki Cypress, scientifically known as Chamaecypares obtusa, is a revered evergreen tree native to Japan. Highly prized for its aromatic, therapeutic wood, it is widely used in traditional medicine and aromatherapy. Its essential oils offer calming, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory benefits, making it a cornerstone of holistic wellness practices.
