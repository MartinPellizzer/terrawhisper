Aristea ecklonii

Aristea ecklonii, commonly known as Ecklon's grass, is a resilient perennial species native to Southern Africa. Beyond its ecological role in stabilizing soil, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to inflammatory conditions and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Aristea ecklonii
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Europe
