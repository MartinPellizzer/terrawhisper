Tanacetum densum subsp. laxum

Tanacetum densum subsp. laxum, commonly known as the dense tansy, is a resilient perennial herb prized for its aromatic foliage. Historically utilized in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant offers unique bioactive compounds. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for exploring its potential therapeutic applications.
