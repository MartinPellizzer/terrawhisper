Straw Everlasting, scientifically known as Helicrysum kraussii, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its vibrant, paper-like blooms, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, often utilized in herbal teas and topical remedies for healing.
