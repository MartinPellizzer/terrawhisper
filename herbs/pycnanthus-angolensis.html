Pycnanthus angolensis

Pycnanthus angolensis, commonly known as African Nutmeg, is a versatile medicinal plant native to West Africa. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammation. This potent botanical offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Pycnanthus angolensis
  • Family: Myristicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Myristicaceae, falling under the genus Pycnanthus.
