Phlomoides bracteosa

Phlomoides bracteosa, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, is a distinctive plant species valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Found in specific ecological niches, it holds significant potential in traditional medicine. Research into its bioactive compounds continues to uncover promising applications for treating various health conditions and ailments.

  • Scientific name: Phlomoides bracteosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Phlomoides.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Phlomoides bracteosa, is referred to by several different common names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly called the bracteose phlomoides, while in Hindi, it is known as bhramari. Additionally, in the local Sanskrit terminology, it is identified as bhramari.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Western Asia, its presence is documented within the borders of Afghanistan. The species also thrives across the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the East Himalaya region. Furthermore, it can be found growing in the mountainous terrains of Nepal and the West Himalaya. Finally, its range extends into the neighboring territory of Pakistan.
