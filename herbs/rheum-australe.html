Himalayan Rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum australe, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Valued in traditional medicine, its roots contain potent bioactive compounds used to treat digestive ailments and inflammation. This hardy plant thrives in rocky terrains, offering unique therapeutic benefits.
