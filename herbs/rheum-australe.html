Rheum australe

Rheum australe, commonly known as Southern Rhubarb, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific alpine and subalpine regions. Distinguished by its robust leaves and characteristic rhizomes, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional ethnobotany for its bioactive compounds, often utilized for their digestive and anti-inflammatory therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Rheum australe
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Caryophyllales, it falls under the family Polygonaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rheum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, it is specifically found growing within the Tibet region. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it inhabits the East Himalaya and the West Himalaya. Furthermore, the species can be located throughout the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Finally, it is also documented to occur within the territory of Pakistan.
