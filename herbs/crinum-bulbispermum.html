Orange river lily, scientifically known as Crinum bulbispermum, is a striking perennial plant native to southern Africa. Renowned for its dramatic white blooms and strap-like leaves, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Traditionally used in various medicinal practices, it serves as a fascinating subject for pharmacological study and conservation.
