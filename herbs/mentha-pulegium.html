Mentha pulegium

Mentha pulegium, commonly known as pennyroyal, is a perennial herb within the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its potent essential oils, particularly pulegone, this aromatic plant has been utilized extensively in traditional medicine. While valued for its antimicrobial and digestive properties, its high toxicity requires cautious and careful application.

  • Scientific name: Mentha pulegium
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Mentha.
