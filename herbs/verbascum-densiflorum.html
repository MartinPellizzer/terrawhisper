Verbascum densiflorum

Denseflower mullein

Verbascum densiflorum, commonly known as the dense-flowered mullein, is a striking perennial herb prized for its towering spikes and velvety foliage. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this plant is frequently utilized for its soothing properties, particularly in supporting respiratory health and calming inflammatory responses through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Verbascum densiflorum
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Verbascum densiflorum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement within the genus Verbascum.
