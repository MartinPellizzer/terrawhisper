Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of Northern Europe. Its presence is documented in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark and Finland. Furthermore, it can be found growing within the islands of the Faroe Islands and Iceland. The species also extends its range into the diverse landscapes of Great Britain. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's preference for northern latitudes.
