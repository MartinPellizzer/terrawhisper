Daphne oleoides

Daphne oleoides, commonly known as the Oleander Daphne, is a resilient shrub native to various Asian regions. Renowned for its fragrant, delicate blossoms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional medicine utilizes its components to treat diverse ailments, though careful handling is essential due to its inherent toxicity.

  • Family: Thymelaeaceae

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
