Cymbidium floribundum

What's the taxonomical classification of Cymbidium floribundum?

Cymbidium floribundum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Asparagales. This plant is a member of the Orchidaceae family, which is further organized into the genus Cymbidium, ultimately identifying it as the specific species floribundum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cymbidium floribundum has long, leathery, elliptical leaves that grow from thickened, fleshy pseudobulbs arranged in a basal rosette. These pseudobulbs serve as critical water storage organs to support the plant during periods of drought. The inflorescences consist of elongated, arching racemes that can bear numerous large, fragrant flowers. Each individual flower features three sepals and three petals, with the labellum often displaying intricate patterns and distinct coloring. The root system is composed of thick, fleshy roots that are adapted for clinging to tree bark in epiphytic habitats.
