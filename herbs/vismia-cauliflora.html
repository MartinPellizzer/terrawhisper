Vismia cauliflora

Vismia cauliflora, commonly known as the "cauliflorous vismia," is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique flowering habit, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. This botanical species offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
