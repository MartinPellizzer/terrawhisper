Guilandina bonduc

Guilandinia bonduc, commonly known as a versatile medicinal plant, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this species is frequently utilized to address various ailments, ranging from inflammatory conditions to digestive issues. Exploring its phytochemical properties offers promising insights into modern therapeutic applications.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically noted within Egypt. Moving toward the Atlantic, it can be found in the Macaronesian islands of Cape Verde. The species also spans a significant portion of West Tropical Africa. Within this western belt, its range includes Benin, Burkina, and Gambia.
