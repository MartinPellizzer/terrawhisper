Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically noted within Egypt. Moving toward the Atlantic, it can be found in the Macaronesian islands of Cape Verde. The species also spans a significant portion of West Tropical Africa. Within this western belt, its range includes Benin, Burkina, and Gambia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Guilandina bonduc, possesses several components that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The roots of the plant are often harvested, with the individual root serving as a primary source for traditional remedies. Furthermore, the seeds are of significant importance; specifically, the seed kernels contained within the hard outer shells are frequently processed to obtain a potent seed extract used in various therapeutic applications.
