Holodiscus discolor

Holodiscus discolor, commonly known as the variegated disco plant, is a distinctive medicinal species prized for its unique botanical properties. Native to specific tropical ecosystems, this plant is valued in traditional healing practices for its therapeutic potential, offering intriguing insights into natural pharmacology and biodiversity within its native habitat.

  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement in the genus Holodiscus.
