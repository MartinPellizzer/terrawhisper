Parasenecio ambiguus

Parasenecio ambiguus, commonly known as the False Senecio, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued in traditional ethnobotany. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Understanding its chemical properties and ecological role is essential for exploring its applications in developing natural remedies and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Parasenecio ambiguus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, placing it in the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its unique genus, Parasenecio.

Distribution

This plant, Parasenecio ambiguus, exhibits a specific distribution pattern within the regional landscapes of East Asia. Its presence is documented across several distinct territories within the country of China. Specifically, the species can be found growing in the North-Central regions of China. Additionally, its habitat extends into the various provinces located in Southeast China. Consequently, the geographical range of this medicinal plant spans both northern and southeastern ecological zones.
