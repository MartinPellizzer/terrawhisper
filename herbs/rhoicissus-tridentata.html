Common Forest Grape, scientifically known as Rhoicissus tridentata, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation and skin ailments. Its small, fleshy fruits and glossy foliage make it a significant botanical resource in holistic wellness practices.
