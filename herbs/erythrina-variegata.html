Indian Coral Tree, scientifically known as Erythrina variegata, is a striking ornamental legume renowned for its vibrant scarlet blossoms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this versatile plant holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its various parts are utilized to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to skin disorders and digestive issues.
