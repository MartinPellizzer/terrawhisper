Cerbera odollam

Cerbera odollam, commonly known as the sea trumpet, is a coastal evergreen tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its striking white flowers and glossy foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse pharmacological properties and unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Cerbera odollam
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
