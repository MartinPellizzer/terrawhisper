Beach Vitex, scientifically known as Vitex rotundifolia, is a resilient, salt-tolerant shrub native to coastal regions. Widely recognized for its striking purple flower spikes, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing practices.
