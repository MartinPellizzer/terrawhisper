Salvia aucheri

Salvia aucheri, commonly known as Acher's sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to the rocky terrains of Central and Western Asia. Distinguished by its aromatic foliage and striking violet-blue flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in folk medicine for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Salvia aucheri
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, falling into the genus Salvia.
