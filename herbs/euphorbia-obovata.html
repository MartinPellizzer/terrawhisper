Euphorbia obovata

Euphorbia obovata, commonly known as the obovate spurge, is a resilient succulent native to arid regions. Characterized by its fleshy, paddle-like leaves and milky latex, this plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem. In traditional medicine, its unique chemical properties are explored for various healing and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia obovata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae, falling under the genus Euphorbia.

Common Names

This plant, Euphorbia obovata, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the obovate spurge, a name that highlights its distinct leaf shape. Within Spanish-speaking regions, it is commonly called euforbia obovata, while in Portuguese, it is known as euforbia obovata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a highly localized geographical distribution within the Middle East. It is specifically documented to occur in the Western Asia region. Within this territory, its presence is restricted to the Sinai Peninsula. This narrow range suggests that the species is adapted to the specific environmental conditions of this area. Consequently, the plant remains a specialized component of the Sinai landscape.
