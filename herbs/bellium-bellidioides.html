Bellium bellidioides

Bellium bellidioides, commonly known as spotted bellwort, is a charming perennial herb native to North America. Often found in woodland settings, this delicate plant features distinctive spotted leaves and bell-shaped flowers. While primarily valued by botanists and gardeners for its aesthetic beauty, it remains a fascinating subject in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Bellium bellidioides
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
