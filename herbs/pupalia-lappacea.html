Forest burr, scientifically known as Pupalia lappacea, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive hooked fruits, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized in various folk medicines to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, showcasing its profound botanical importance.

Distribution

This plant, known as Pupalia lappacea, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin, where it integrates into the local ecosystem. The species also extends its range into Burkina, contributing to the area's medicinal flora. Furthermore, its presence is documented in the countries of Gambia and Ghana. Finally, the distribution includes Guinea-Bissau, completing its footprint across these West African nations.
