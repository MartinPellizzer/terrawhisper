Tanacetum macrophyllum

Tanacetum macrophyllum, commonly known as large-leaf tansy, is a resilient perennial herb native to diverse landscapes. Valued in traditional herbalism, this plant is recognized for its aromatic foliage and distinct yellow blooms. Its unique phytochemical properties make it a subject of interest for exploring natural medicinal applications and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Tanacetum macrophyllum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Tanacetum.
