Rayed Tansy, scientifically known as Tanacetum macrophyllum, is a distinctive perennial herb native to Western North America. Characterized by its broad, serrated leaves and bright yellow button-like flowers, this botanical specimen is valued in traditional herbalism for its aromatic properties and potential therapeutic applications in holistic wellness practices.
