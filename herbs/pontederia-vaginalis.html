Pontederia vaginalis

Ponderia vaginalis, commonly known as Water Primrose, is a versatile creeping herb frequently found in wetland ecosystems. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. This resilient plant is widely utilized in various cultures to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions effectively.

  • Scientific name: Pontederia vaginalis
  • Family: Pontederiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Ponderia vaginalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Commelinales. It is further categorized into the family Pontederiaceae and the genus Ponderia.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In Western Asia, its presence is recorded within the borders of Afghanistan.
