Schimpera arabica

Schimpera arabica, commonly known as the Arabian Schimpera, is a resilient medicinal plant native to the arid landscapes of the Arabian Peninsula. Valued in traditional ethnobotanical practices, its unique chemical properties offer significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting overall wellness through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Schimpera arabica
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Schimpera.
