Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it can be found growing in the lush landscapes of Assam and throughout India. Its presence extends into the neighboring country of Bangladesh as well. Furthermore, the species is known to inhabit the rugged terrains of the East Himalaya.
