Selaginella bryopteris

Selagineella bryopteris, commonly known as the resurrection plant, is a remarkable pteridophyte capable of surviving extreme desiccation. Renowned for its ability to revive upon rehydration, this medicinal herb is rich in bioactive compounds. It holds significant therapeutic potential in treating various ailments, particularly focusing on anti-aging and neuroprotective properties.

  • Scientific name: Selaginella bryopteris
  • Family: Selaginellaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Lycopodiidae and the order Selaginellales, falling into the family Selaginellaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Selaginella and the specific species Selaginella bryopteris.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it can be found growing in the lush landscapes of Assam and throughout India. Its presence extends into the neighboring country of Bangladesh as well. Furthermore, the species is known to inhabit the rugged terrains of the East Himalaya. Finally, it is also documented to grow within the mountainous regions of Nepal.
