Siberian Violet-Willow

Salix acutifolia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (3 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Siberian Violet-Willow, scientifically known as Salix acuifolia, is a resilient shrub native to the harsh, subarctic regions of Northern Asia. Renowned for its adaptability, this species is historically valued in traditional medicine for its bark, which contains salicin, a natural precursor to aspirin used to alleviate pain and inflammation.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Salix acuifolia, derives its medicinal value primarily from its bark. The bark of various willow species is historically significant because it contains salicin, a compound that acts as a precursor to salicylic acid, which has natural analgesic and anti-inflammatory properties. When harvested correctly, this woody layer is processed to create infusions or extracts used to alleviate pain and reduce fever.
