Stellaria media

Little mouse-ear chickweed

Stellaria media, commonly known as chickweed, is a versatile annual herb frequently found in gardens and meadows. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this cooling plant is prized for its soothing properties. It is widely used topically to alleviate skin irritations, inflammation, and itching, offering natural relief for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Stellaria media
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Caryophyllales and the family Caryophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Stellaria.
