little mouse-ear chickweed, scientifically known as Stellaria media, is a common, low-growing annual herb found globally. Renowned for its small, star-shaped white flowers and delicate leaves, this resilient plant offers diverse medicinal benefits, particularly for soothing skin irritations, reducing inflammation, and supporting overall wellness through traditional herbal practices.
