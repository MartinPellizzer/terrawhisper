Dermatophyllum secundiflorum

Mountain laurel

Dermatophyllum secundiflorum, commonly known as a potent medicinal shrub, is highly valued in traditional pharmacology for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions, offering a natural approach to healing through its unique phytochemical profile and restorative essence.

  • Scientific name: Dermatophyllum secundiflorum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Dermatophyllum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Dermatophyllum secundiflorum, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as mountain laurel, Texas mountain laurel, or more abbreviated as Tx. mtn. laurel, while it is also sometimes called secundiflora. In Chinese, the plant is known as 侧花槐.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple continents. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically within the borders of Kenya. Its range extends to the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in both India and Pakistan. Moving to North America, the species is located within the South-Central United States. Specifically, its presence in this region includes the states of New Mexico and Texas.
