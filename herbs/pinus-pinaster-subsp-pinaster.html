Plant Parts

This plant, specifically Pinus pinaster subsp. pinaster, is highly valued in traditional and modern medicine for its therapeutic properties derived from its various structures, most notably its bark. The bark of this subspecies is harvested to extract bioactive compounds, such as proanthocyanidins, which are renowned for their potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. Beyond the bark, other parts of the tree, including the resin and needles, are also utilized in various applications, but the bark remains a primary source for the production of specialized extracts used to support vascular health and skin protection.
