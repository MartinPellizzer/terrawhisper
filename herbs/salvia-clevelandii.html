Cleveland Sage, scientifically known as Salvia clevelandii, is a fragrant, aromatic shrub native to the coastal sage scrub of California. Renowned for its striking purple flowers and intoxicating scent, it serves as a vital pollinator magnet. This hardy perennial is highly valued in xeriscaping for its drought tolerance.
