Beyond its value in fine woodworking, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to skin infections.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in countries such as Gambia and Guinea. Moving toward the center of the continent, it is found within West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically in Cameroon and the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its range also extends significantly eastward into the region of East Tropical Africa. Notably, its presence is confirmed in Kenya, demonstrating its ability to thrive in diverse tropical environments.
