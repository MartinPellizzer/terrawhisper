Polygala japonica

Polygala japonica, commonly known as Japanese Milkwort, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to support respiratory health and soothe inflammation. This versatile plant plays a significant role in herbal therapies, offering potential benefits for cognitive and digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Polygala japonica
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Polygalaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Polygala, specifically as the species Polygala japonica.

Common Names

This plant, Polygala japonica, is known by various regional names across East Asia. In Japan, it is referred to as ヒメハギ (ja), while in Korea, it is commonly called 애기풀 (ko). In Chinese-speaking regions, including mainland China (zh-cn), Taiwan (zh-tw), and broader Chinese contexts (zh), the plant is recognized by the name 瓜子金.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented in both the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Its range extends significantly into China, where it can be found throughout the Manchuria region. Furthermore, the species inhabits the North-Central parts of China. Finally, its geographical reach includes the South-Central areas of the country as well.
