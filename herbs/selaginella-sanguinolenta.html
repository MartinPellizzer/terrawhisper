What's the taxonomical classification of Selaginella sanguinolenta?

Selaginella sanguinolenta belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Lycopodiidae. The plant's taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Selaginellales and the family Selaginellaceae. Ultimately, it is placed within the genus Selaginella, with its specific identification being the species sanguinolenta.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant requires high humidity, consistent moisture, and indirect light to thrive in a well-draining, peat-based soil mix.

Because it is a resurrection fern, it can withstand periods of extreme dryness by curling into a ball, but it recovers quickly once watered. It prefers temperatures between 60 and 75 degrees Fahrenheit and should be kept away from direct sunlight to prevent leaf scorch. Using a terrarium or a pebble tray can help maintain the necessary ambient moisture levels.
