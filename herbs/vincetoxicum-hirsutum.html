Vincetoxicum hirsutum (Wall.) Kuntze, scientifically known as Vincetoxicum hirsutum, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apocynaceae family. Widely distributed across tropical Asia, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It is primarily recognized for its diverse pharmacological properties, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing capabilities.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast regions. Its presence is also noted on the tropical island of Hainan. Moving further east, the species is documented in Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Additionally, the plant extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the Assam region.
