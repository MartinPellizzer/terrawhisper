Vincetoxicum hirsutum

Vincetoxicum hirsutum (wall.) kuntze

Vincetoxicum hirsutum, commonly known as the hairy vincetoxicum, is a distinctive medicinal climbing vine found in tropical regions. Renowned for its complex alkaloids, this plant plays a significant role in traditional medicine. Researchers continue to explore its phytochemical properties for potential therapeutic applications in treating various neurological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Vincetoxicum hirsutum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast regions. Its presence is also noted on the tropical island of Hainan. Moving further east, the species is documented in Taiwan within Eastern Asia.
