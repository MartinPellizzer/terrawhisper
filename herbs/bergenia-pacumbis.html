Chinese Bergenia, scientifically known as Bergenia pacumbis, is a resilient perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Native to high-altitude regions, its thick, fleshy leaves contain potent bioactive compounds. Often used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments, this versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.
