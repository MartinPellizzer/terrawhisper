Bergenia pacumbis

Bergenia pacumbis, commonly known as the highland Bergenia, is a perennial herb native to the montane regions of East Africa. Renowned for its thick, fleshy leaves, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to respiratory inflammation through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Bergenia pacumbis
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Bergenia pacumbis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Saxifragales and the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Bergenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. In Western Asia, its presence can be identified within the borders of Afghanistan. Moving towards the east, it is found throughout South-Central China and the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species is specifically located in the state of Assam. Additionally, it thrives throughout the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya.
