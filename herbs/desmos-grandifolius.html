Desmos grandifolius

What's the taxonomical classification of Desmos grandifolius?

Desmos grandifolius belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Magnoliales. It is further categorized under the family Annonaceae, falling into the genus Desmos, and is ultimately identified by its specific species name, grandifolius.

According to a study published by "Zhongguo Zhong yao za zh𝘪 = Zhongguo zhongyao zazhi = China journal of Chinese materia medica", the root of Desmos grandifolius was investigated to find new compounds with anti-tumor activities.
