Cycas circinalis

Cycas circinalis, commonly known as the Queen Sago, is a magnificent cycad native to the tropical regions of India. Renowned for its ornamental beauty, this ancient plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Its various parts are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Cycas circinalis
  • Family: Cycadaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Cycadidae, it is ordered under Cycadales and falls into the family Cycadaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cycas.
