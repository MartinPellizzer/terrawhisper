Ocotea puberula

Ocotea pubeula, commonly known as a species of cinnamon or laurel, is a medicinal plant valued for its potent bioactive compounds. Found in tropical ecosystems, its leaves and bark contain essential oils with antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in traditional medicine and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Ocotea puberula
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Laurales, it is a member of the family Lauraceae and is classified under the genus Ocotea.
