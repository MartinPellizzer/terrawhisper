Cajanus cajan

Red gram

Cajanus cajan, commonly known as pigeon pea, is a versatile legume renowned for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Beyond its role as a vital protein source, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, making it a cornerstone of sustainable holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cajanus cajan
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Cajanus cajaen, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and the genus Cajanus.

Distribution

This plant, Cajanus cajan, exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, it is notably found within the borders of Egypt. Its presence extends to the Macaronesia region, specifically occurring in Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species is widespread throughout West Tropical Africa. Within this tropical zone, it is documented in Benin, Burkina, and Gambia.
