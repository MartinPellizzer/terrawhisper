Red gram, scientifically known as Cajanus cajan, is a versatile legume widely cultivated for its nutrient-dense seeds and ecological benefits. Renowned in traditional medicine, it offers therapeutic properties for digestive health and inflammation. This resilient plant serves as a vital protein source and a cornerstone of sustainable agriculture globally.

Distribution

This plant, Cajanus cajan, exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, it is notably found within the borders of Egypt. Its presence extends to the Macaronesia region, specifically occurring in Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species is widespread throughout West Tropical Africa. Within this tropical zone, it is documented in Benin, Burkina, and Gambia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Canavalia ensiformis (often referred to in various contexts by names like Caja), utilizes several distinct anatomical structures for its survival and medicinal properties. The lush, green leaves are often the most visible part of the plant, with each individual leaf providing the surface area necessary for photosynthesis. Supporting these structures are strong, upright stems that transport nutrients throughout the organism. The reproductive cycle is completed through the production of a seed, which is contained within a specialized pod. This pod often takes the shape of a large, fleshy bean, which serves as the primary vessel for the plant's genetic material.
