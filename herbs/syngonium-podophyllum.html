Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct ecological regions. Within the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found in both the Comoros and the Seychelles. Its range extends to the Indian Subcontinent, specifically documented in Bangladesh. Moving into the Malesia region, the species is present on the island of Borneo. Additionally, the plant is widely distributed across Jawa within the same Malesian territory.

Plant Parts

This plant, Syngonium podophyllum, is characterized primarily by its striking and versatile leaf structure, which serves as a key identifying feature. The leaves exhibit a fascinating morphological change as the plant matures; young leaves are typically simple and arrow-shaped, while older, climbing leaves develop deep lobes, eventually becoming highly divided and almost finger-like. These leaves are often heart-shaped or sagittate, possessing a glossy texture and a vibrant green hue that makes them highly valued in both medicinal studies and ornamental horticulture.
