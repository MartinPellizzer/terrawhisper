Bupleurum bicaule

Bupleurum bicaule, commonly known as Mithun, is a perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Valued in traditional medicine, particularly Ayurveda, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and hepatoprotective properties. This plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin disorders, fever, and digestive issues, through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum bicaule
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, falling into the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Bupleurum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions within the vast territory of Siberia. It can be found growing in the mountainous areas of the Altai region. Furthermore, its presence is well-documented throughout the Republic of Buryatia. The species also occupies significant portions of the Transbaikal territory, specifically around Chita. Finally, the plant is distributed across the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions, completing its extensive Siberian range.
