Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its presence has been documented within the borders of Benin. It also thrives in the coastal and inland areas of the Gambia. Furthermore, the species can be found growing throughout the landscapes of Ghana and Guinea.
