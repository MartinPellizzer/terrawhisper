Gentiana officinalis, scientifically known as Gentiana officinalis, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its intense bitterness. Renowned for its potent bitter principles, it is frequently utilized to stimulate digestion, enhance appetite, and support liver function, making it a cornerstone ingredient in various restorative herbal tonics.
