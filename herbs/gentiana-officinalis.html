Gentiana officinalis

Gentiana officinalis, commonly known as common gentian, is a perennial herb renowned for its intensely bitter roots. Historically prized in traditional medicine, it serves as a powerful digestive tonic. Its unique bitter compounds stimulate appetite and support gastrointestinal health, making it a staple in various herbal bitters and tonics.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana officinalis
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Gentiana officinalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Gentianales. Finally, it is classified under the family Gentianaceae and the genus Gentiana.
