Cynanchum corymbosum

What's the taxonomical classification of Cynanchum corymbosum?

Cynanchum corymbosum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Gentianaales. The plant is a member of the family Apocynaceae, specifically falling within the genus Cynanchum, and is identified by the unique species name corymbosum.
