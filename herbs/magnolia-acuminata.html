Magnolia acuminata

Cucumbertree

Magnolia acuminata, commonly known as the cucumber tree, is a majestic deciduous tree native to North American forests. Renowned for its large, fragrant white blossoms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various parts of the plant have been utilized in traditional medicine for their diverse therapeutic properties and healing potential.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia acuminata
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Magnoliales and the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, its specific genus is Magnolia, identifying it as Magnolia acuminata.

The bark of Magnolia acuminata contains various phytochemicals, including magnolols and honokiol, which are often the focus of medicinal research due to their potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. While many parts of the tree are structurally distinct, the bark remains a significant source of the bioactive compounds traditionally sought after in botanical studies.
