Phillyrea latifolia

Phillyrea latifolia, commonly known as Broad-leaved Phillyrea, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Valued for its dense foliage and ecological importance, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Research explores its bioactive compounds, which may provide antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties for traditional herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Phillyrea latifolia
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is a member of the Oleaceae family. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Phillyrea.
