Green Olive Tree, scientifically known as Phillyrea latifolia, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its leathery leaves and small white flowers, this hardy plant thrives in rocky soils. It holds significant ethnobotanical value, often utilized in traditional remedies for its diverse medicinal properties.

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing in the Balearic Islands, known as Baleares. Plant Parts

This plant, Phillyrea latifolia, is characterized by its distinctive foliage, which serves as a primary identifier for the species. The leaves are typically evergreen, possessing a leathery texture and a broad, elliptical shape that distinguishes them from other members of the Oleaceae family. These leaves are arranged oppositely along the stems and feature a dark, glossy upper surface with a lighter underside, providing a lush appearance to the shrub's dense canopy.
