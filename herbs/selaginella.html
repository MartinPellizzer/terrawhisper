Selaginella

Spikemosses

Selagineella, commonly known as spike moss, is a unique genus of primitive vascular plants. Often referred to as resurrection plants, they possess the extraordinary ability to survive extreme dehydration. Beyond their botanical fascination, various species are studied for their bioactive compounds, offering significant potential in traditional medicine and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Selaginella
  • Family: Selaginellaceae
  • Native range: Europe
