Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across different oceanic and continental regions. Within the Macaronesian islands, its presence is documented in the Azores, the Canary Islands, and Madeira. Moving toward the African continent, the species can also be found in West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its range extends through the country of Guinea. Additionally, the plant is recorded as being native to Nigeria.
