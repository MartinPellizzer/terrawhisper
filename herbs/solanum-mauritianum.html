Solanum mauritianum

Woolly nightshade

Solanum mauritianum, commonly known as the "wild eggplant" or "mountain nightshade," is a versatile medicinal shrub found across tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This plant serves as a vital resource in ethnobotanical studies and herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Solanum mauritianum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales, falling into the family Solanaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Solanum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across different oceanic and continental regions. Within the Macaronesian islands, its presence is documented in the Azores, the Canary Islands, and Madeira. Moving toward the African continent, the species can also be found in West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its range extends through the country of Guinea. Additionally, the plant is recorded as being native to Nigeria.
