Pseudocaryopteris foetida

Pseudocaryopteris foetida, commonly known as the stinking fern, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its potent, pungent aroma, this species is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies. It holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering potential pharmacological properties for treating diverse ailments in traditional medicine.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
