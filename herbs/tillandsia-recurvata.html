Tillandsia recurvata

Tillandsia recurvata, commonly known as Ball Moss, is a fascinating epiphytic plant native to the Americas. Unlike soil-bound species, it thrives by absorbing moisture and nutrients directly from the air through specialized scales. This resilient air plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem, providing habitat for various organisms.

  • Scientific name: Tillandsia recurvata
  • Family: Bromeliaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Bromeliaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Tillandsia and the specific species Tillandsia recurvata.
