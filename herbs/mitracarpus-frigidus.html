Mitracarpus frigidus

Mitracarpus frigidus, commonly known as the Frost-Hearted Vine, is a rare medicinal plant native to high-altitude alpine ecosystems. Renowned for its potent cooling properties, it is traditionally used in herbal remedies to reduce systemic inflammation and soothe fevers, offering a unique botanical solution for thermal regulation and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Mitracarpus frigidus
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
