Rumex acetosa

Rumex acetosa, commonly known as common sorrel, is a perennial herb valued for its distinctively sharp, acidic flavor. Rich in vitamin C and organic acids, it has been used traditionally in folk medicine to support digestion and reduce inflammation. This versatile plant is both a culinary staple and a medicinal treasure.

  • Scientific name: Rumex acetosa
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Rumex.
