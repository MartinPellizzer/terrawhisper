Garden sorrel, scientifically known as Rumex acetosa, is a perennial herb cherished for its bright, tangy leaves. Renowned for its distinctively tart flavor, it is a culinary staple in salads and soups. Beyond the kitchen, it offers traditional medicinal benefits, particularly in supporting digestive health and reducing inflammation.
