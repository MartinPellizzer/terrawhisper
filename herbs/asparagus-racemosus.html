Asparagus racemosus

Asparagus racemosus, commonly known as Shatavari, is a premier medicinal herb in Ayurveda. Renowned as a potent rejuvenator, this perennial climber is celebrated for its ability to balance hormones and support reproductive health. Its nutrient-rich roots offer profound nourishment, vitality, and restorative benefits for holistic physical wellness.

  • Scientific name: Asparagus racemosus
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Asparagus racemosus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asparagales. Furthermore, it is a member of the Asparagaceae family and is situated within the genus Asparagus.
