Shatavari

Asparagus racemosus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (236 studies) · ★★★★★

Shatavari, scientifically known as Asparagus racemosus, is a perennial herb deeply revered in Ayurvedic medicine. Often called the "Queen of Herbs," it is primarily celebrated for its potent adaptogenic properties and ability to support female reproductive health, hormonal balance, and vitality, making it a cornerstone of holistic wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Asparagus racemosus, is a highly valued medicinal herb where nearly every part offers therapeutic potential. While the whole plant is often utilized in traditional Ayurvedic preparations, the most significant medicinal components are found within the underground structures. Specifically, the plant develops thickened, fleshy tuberous roots that serve as a primary source of bioactive compounds. Whether prepared as a decoction of the root or processed into a concentrated root extract, these elements are used to treat various ailments. Ultimately, it is the potent properties found within the roots that define the plant's reputation as a powerful adaptogen and tonic.
