Dracaena trifasciata

Dracaena trifasciata, commonly known as the Snake Plant or Mother-in-law's Tongue, is a resilient succulent prized for its architectural foliage. Beyond its popularity as a hardy indoor air purifier, it holds significant traditional medicinal value, often utilized in various cultures for its potential antimicrobial and healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Dracaena trifasciata
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asparagaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dracaena.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is recorded specifically within Tunisia. Its range extends to the Macaronesian islands, where it can be found on the Canary Islands. The species is also well-represented across West Tropical Africa, occurring in Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Furthermore, its documented territory includes the West Tropical African nation of Nigeria.
