Canadian wild ginger

Canadian wild ginger, scientifically known as Asarum canadense, is a perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Characterized by its heart-shaped leaves and aromatic rhizomes, this plant has a long history in traditional medicine. It is primarily valued for its spicy properties and potential use in treating digestive ailments.
