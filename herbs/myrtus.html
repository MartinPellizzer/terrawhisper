Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, falling into the family Myrtaeae. Finally, the medicinal plant is identified by the genus Myrtus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe. Its presence can be found spanning several distinct island territories, including the Baleares and Corse. Additionally, the species is widely established throughout the mainland regions of France and Portugal. It also thrives in the specific coastal environments of Sardegna. Overall, these locations define the core geographical range for this medicinal species.
