Mucuna monosperma

Mucuna monospermma, commonly known as velvet bean, is a powerful medicinal legume renowned for its high L-dopa content. Widely utilized in Ayurvedic medicine, it serves as a potent natural remedy for managing Parkinson's disease, balancing hormones, and enhancing cognitive function, while supporting overall reproductive health and neurological vitality.

  • Scientific name: Mucuna monosperma
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Mucuna, specifically the species Mucuna monosperma.

Distribution

This plant, Mucuna monospermma, exhibits a widespread presence across various regions of the Indian Subcontinent. It is commonly documented throughout the vast territories of India and its neighboring country, Bangladesh. Within the subcontinent, its range extends into the unique ecological landscapes of Assam. Furthermore, the species can be found inhabiting the high-altitude environments of the East Himalaya. Finally, its geographical distribution reaches as far south as the island nation of Sri Lanka.
