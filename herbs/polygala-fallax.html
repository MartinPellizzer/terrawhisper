Polygala fallax

Polygala fallaex, commonly known as a versatile medicinal herb, holds a significant place in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to address various ailments, ranging from respiratory issues to inflammatory conditions, offering a natural approach to supporting overall human wellness.

  • Scientific name: Polygala fallax
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling into the family Polygalaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Polygala.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Polygala fallaex, is identified by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is commonly referred to as 黄花倒水莲 (zh) and 念健 (zh). Specifically, in Mainland China, the name used is 黄花倒水莲 (zh-cn), while in Taiwan, it is written as 黃花倒水蓮 (zh-tw). Additionally, the simplified Chinese designation is 黄花倒水莲 (zh-hans).
