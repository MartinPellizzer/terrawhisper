Artabotrys blumei

Artabotrys blumei, commonly known as the Dutchman's Pipe, is a climbing woody vine prized for its fragrant, sculptural flowers. Native to Southeast Asia, this botanical gem is valued in traditional medicine for its potential therapeutic properties, offering a unique blend of aesthetic elegance and significant ethnobotanical importance for researchers.
