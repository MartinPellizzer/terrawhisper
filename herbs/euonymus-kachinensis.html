Euonymus kachinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Euonymus kachinensis?

Euonymus kachinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Celastrales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Celastraeaceae, placing it within the genus Euonymus, and it is specifically identified by the species epithet kachinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Euonymus kacchinensis has opposite, simple, elliptic to lanceolate leaves that typically measure between 2 and 5 centimeters in length with serrated margins and acute apices. Small, inconspicuous flowers appear in clusters, often featuring four to five petals and numerous stamens. The fruit develops into a distinctive four-lobed capsule that transitions from green to a reddish or pinkish hue upon maturity. Within these capsules, small, fleshy seeds are enclosed, which serve as the primary means of dispersal.
