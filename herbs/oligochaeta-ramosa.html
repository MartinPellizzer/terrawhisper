Oligochaeta ramosa

Oligochaeata ramosa, commonly known as the branching quillwort, is a rare aquatic medicinal plant valued for its unique biochemical properties. Traditionally used in holistic remedies to support respiratory health and soothe inflammation, this delicate species offers significant potential for modern pharmacology through its diverse array of bioactive secondary metabolites.

  • Scientific name: Oligochaeta ramosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Oligochaeta, which includes the species Oligochaeta ramosa.

Common Names

This plant, Oligochaeata ramosa, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the branched wormweed, while in Spanish, it is known as la ramillete de oligoqueta. In French, the species is called l'oligochète ramifiée, and in German, it is identified as der verzweigte Oligochäta.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range extending across several distinct regions. In the Caucasus, its presence is specifically noted within the Transcaucasus area. Its distribution continues through Western Asia, where it can be found in both Türkiye and Iran. Furthermore, the species reaches into the Indian Subcontinent, inhabiting parts of India. Finally, its documented range extends into Pakistan, completing its spread across these diverse territories.
