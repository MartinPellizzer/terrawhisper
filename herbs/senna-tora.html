Java-bean

Senna tora · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (388 studies) · ★★★★★

Java-bean, scientifically known as Senna tora, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, it contains active anthraquinone glycosides used to treat constipation. Beyond digestive health, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine for treating skin ailments and inflammatory conditions.
