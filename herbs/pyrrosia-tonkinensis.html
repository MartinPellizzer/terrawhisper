Pyrrosia tonkinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its hierarchical lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. This species is further categorized under the order Polypodiales and the family Polypodiaceae. Within this taxonomic framework, it is identified by its specific genus, Pyrrosiia, and its unique species designation, tonkinensis.
