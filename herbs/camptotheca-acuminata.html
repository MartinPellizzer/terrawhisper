Camptotheca acuminata

Camptotheca acuminata, commonly known as a versatile medicinal herb, holds significant importance in traditional healing practices. Primarily utilized for its potent pharmacological properties, this plant offers therapeutic potential in treating various ailments. Exploring its bioactive compounds reveals its valuable role in ethnobotany and the ongoing development of natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Camptotheca acuminata
  • Family: Nyssaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cornales, it is classified under the family Nyssaceae. Its scientific designation is finalized within the genus Camptotheca, specifically as the species Camptotheca acuminata.
