Spearmint, scientifically known as Mentha spicata, is a perennial herb celebrated for its refreshing aroma and versatile therapeutic properties. Widely used in culinary, cosmetic, and medicinal applications, it offers soothing effects for digestive health and mental clarity. This aromatic plant remains a cornerstone in traditional herbal remedies worldwide.
