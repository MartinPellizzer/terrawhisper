Heteromorpha arborescens abyssinica

Heteromorpha arborescens var. abyssinica, commonly known as an Ethiopian medicinal shrub, is a rare botanical treasure valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Found in specific highland ecosystems, this variety is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern scientific research and herbal wellness.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Heteromorpha and the variety abyssinica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Burundi and Rwanda. Moving toward the northeast, the species can also be found throughout the tropical landscapes of Eritrea. Its range extends significantly into Ethiopia, which serves as a major part of its northeast tropical habitat. Finally, the plant is distributed across the vast territories of Sudan and South Sudan.
