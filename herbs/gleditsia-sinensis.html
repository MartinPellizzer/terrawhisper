Gleditsia sinensis

Gleditsia sinensis, commonly known as the Chinese honey locust, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its seeds and bark are often utilized to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and skin conditions, offering a natural approach to holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Gleditsia sinensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gleditsia.
