Santolina canescens

Santolina canescens, commonly known as Cotton Lavender, is a resilient evergreen shrub prized for its silvery, fine-textured foliage. Native to the Mediterranean, this aromatic plant offers drought tolerance and unique ornamental beauty. In traditional herbal practices, its essential oils are valued for their soothing, antiseptic, and therapeutic properties.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
