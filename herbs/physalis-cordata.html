Physalis cordata

Physalis cordata, commonly known as the heart-leaf groundcherry, is a resilient perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its cordate leaves and delicate bell-shaped flowers, it produces edible, golden berries encased in papery husks. This versatile plant offers both ornamental beauty and nutritional value in traditional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Physalis cordata
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Physalis.
