Annickia affinis

Annickia affinis, commonly known as the "yellow jasmine" or "annickia," is a fascinating medicinal plant native to African tropical forests. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin infections, and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Annickia affinis
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Annickia, specifically as the species Annickia affinis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across the tropical regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found in the enclave of Cabinda. It is also widely distributed across the central territories of Cameroon and the Republic of the Congo. Finally, its geographical range extends further into the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
