Fallopia dumetorum

Fallopia dumetorum, commonly known as knotweed, is a resilient medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it contains bioactive compounds like resveratrol. Often utilized to support cardiovascular health and combat oxidative stress, this versatile species remains a significant subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Fallopia dumetorum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae and is placed within the genus Fallopia.
