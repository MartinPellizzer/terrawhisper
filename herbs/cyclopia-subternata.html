Honeybush-tea, scientifically known as Cyclopia subterrata, is a unique medicinal shrub native to South Africa's Fynbos biome. Renowned for its aromatic, honey-like flavor, this plant offers antioxidant benefits and soothing properties. Traditionally used in indigenous medicine, it remains a popular, caffeine-free herbal infusion celebrated for its wellness qualities.
