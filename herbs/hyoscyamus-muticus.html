Henbane, scientifically known as Hyoscyamus muticus, is a powerful medicinal plant native to Africa and parts of Asia. Renowned for its high concentration of tropane alkaloids, it is primarily utilized in pharmacology for its potent anticholinergic properties, serving as a critical source for treating various gastrointestinal and neurological conditions.
