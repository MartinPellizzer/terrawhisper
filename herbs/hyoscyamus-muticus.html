Hyoscyamus muticus

Hyoscyamus muticus, commonly known as Henbane, is a powerful medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Native to Africa and parts of Asia, it contains potent alkaloids like hyoscyamine. While used traditionally to treat gastrointestinal spasms and respiratory issues, its extreme toxicity requires careful, expert handling and administration.

  • Scientific name: Hyoscyamus muticus
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Hyoscyamus.

Distribution

This plant is native to the diverse landscapes of Northern Africa. Its presence can be found spanning across the territory of Algeria. It also grows throughout various regions of Egypt and Libya. Furthermore, the species is distributed within the borders of Morocco. Finally, its geographical range extends into the territory of Western Sahara.
