Tephrosia sinapou

Tephrosia sinapou, commonly known as the "bush tea" or "scented leaf," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments, skin irritations, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal practices.
