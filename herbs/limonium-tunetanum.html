Limonium tunetanum

Limonium tunetanum, commonly known as a specialized coastal sea lavender, is a rare medicinal plant endemic to specific Mediterranean habitats. Valued for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Research focuses on its antioxidant and bioactive properties, highlighting its importance in traditional herbal medicine and modern pharmacology.
