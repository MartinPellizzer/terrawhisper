Plant Parts

This plant, Dioscorea zingiberensis, is characterized by several distinct morphological features used in its medicinal preparation. The most significant parts are the underground storage organs, which include both fleshy rhizomes and starchy tubers. Specifically, the thick rhizome contains the concentrated active compounds, while the rounded tubers serve as the primary energy reserves for the plant. Above the ground, the plant displays lush green foliage consisting of broad leaves, where each individual leaf is essential for photosynthesis to support the growth of the subterranean structures.
