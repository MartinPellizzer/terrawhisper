Macrosolen globobus, scientifically known as Macrosolen globosus, is a remarkable parasitic plant belonging to the Loranthaceae family. Often found in tropical ecosystems, this hemiparasite thrives by attaching to host trees. Renowned in traditional medicine, it holds significant ethnobotanical potential for its diverse bioactive compounds and therapeutic properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of the Asian continent. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found throughout the vast territories of India and specifically in the state of Assam. It is also documented in the neighboring country of Bangladesh, indicating a significant presence in the region. Furthermore, the species extends into the rugged terrains of the East Himalaya. Beyond the Indian Subcontinent, its range reaches into Indo-China, specifically within Myanmar.
