Bletilla ochracea

Bletilla ochracea, commonly known as a medicinal orchid, is a rare species valued in traditional herbal medicine. Native to specific subtropical regions, it contains unique bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and promote healing. Understanding its pharmacological properties is essential for exploring its potential in modern therapeutic applications.

  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, known as Bletilla ochracea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Orchidaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Bletilla.
