Castanea sativa

Sweet chestnut

Castaña sativa, commonly known as the sweet chestnut, is a versatile deciduous tree valued for its nutritious nuts and medicinal properties. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, its extracts possess antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and astringent qualities, making it a significant botanical resource for treating skin conditions and supporting vascular health.

  • Scientific name: Castanea sativa
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales and the family Fagaceae, it is classified under the genus Castanea.
