Petunia

Petunia, commonly known as a versatile ornamental, holds surprising medicinal potential. While primarily celebrated for its vibrant blooms, various species contain alkaloids and compounds with antioxidant properties. Historically used in traditional practices to treat inflammation and skin ailments, modern research continues to explore its therapeutic bioactive constituents for healing.

  • Scientific name: Petunia
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Petunia.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across various regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Germany.
