Ephedra altissima

Ephedra altissima, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, belongs to the Ephedraceae family. Native to specific arid regions, this plant is highly valued for its alkaloid content, particularly ephedrine. It has been traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat respiratory ailments, boost energy, and manage cardiovascular functions.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra altissima
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Gnetidae, it is situated in the order Ephedrales and the family Ephedraceae. Finally, Ephedra altissima is categorized under the genus Ephedra.
