Pimelea linifolia

Pimelea linifolia, commonly known as the flax-leaved pimelia, is a distinctive medicinal plant cherished for its unique botanical properties. Characterized by its slender, linear leaves, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. Exploring its bioactive compounds provides vital insights into traditional healing practices and modern pharmacological advancements in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Pimelea linifolia
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Malvales and is a member of the Thymelaeaceae family. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pimelea.
