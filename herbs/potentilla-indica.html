Potentilla indica

Indian strawberry

Potentiella indica, commonly known as Indian Cinquefoil, is a versatile medicinal herb native to various subtropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, often utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits. It plays a crucial role in herbal remedies aimed at promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Potentilla indica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling under the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Potentiella.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found within the borders of Austria and Belgium. Furthermore, it extends its range into the territories of Czechia and Slovakia. This pattern highlights the plant's ability to inhabit diverse environments across the continent.
