Indian strawberry

Potentilla indica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (255 studies) · ★★★★★

Indian strawberry, scientifically known as Potentiella indica, is a resilient, creeping perennial herb native to various tropical regions. Characterized by its delicate yellow flowers and trifoliate leaves, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties used to treat various ailments.
