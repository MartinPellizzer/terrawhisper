Annona mucosa

Annona mucosa, commonly known as the custard apple or sugar apple, is a tropical fruit-bearing plant cherished for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Renowned for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial qualities, it is widely used in traditional medicine to support digestive health and bolster the body's immune system naturally.

  • Scientific name: Annona mucosa
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. Finally, Annona mucosa is identified by its specific genus, Annona.
