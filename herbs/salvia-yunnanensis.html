Salvia yunnanensis

Salvia yunnanensis, commonly known as Yunnan Sage, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of Southwest China. Renowned in traditional herbal medicine, this plant contains bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Salvia yunnanensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Salvia yunnanensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Salvia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Salvia yunnanensis, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is frequently called 云南鼠尾草, a name also written as 云南鼠尾草 in Simplified Chinese (zh-hans) and specifically in Mainland China (zh-cn), while the Traditional Chinese script (zh-tw) renders it as 雲南鼠尾草. Additionally, in certain traditional medicinal contexts, it is identified by the name 朱砂理肺散.
