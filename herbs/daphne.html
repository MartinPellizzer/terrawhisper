Winter Daphne, scientifically known as Daphne, is a versatile evergreen shrub celebrated for its intoxicatingly sweet fragrance. Often blooming in late winter, its fragrant blossoms provide early color to gardens. While prized for beauty, it is important to handle carefully, as many parts of the plant are toxic.
