Daphne

Winter daphne

Daphne, commonly known as laurel, is a genus of flowering shrubs celebrated for its intoxicating fragrance and exquisite blooms. While many species grace ornamental gardens worldwide, it is essential to exercise caution; many varieties contain toxic compounds, making them beautiful yet dangerous if ingested by humans or pets.

  • Scientific name: Daphne
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Thymelaeaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Daphne.
