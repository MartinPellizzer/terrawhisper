Baccharoides anthelmintica

Baccharoides anthelmintica, commonly known as the "worm-expelling plant," is a medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is primarily utilized for its potent anthelmintic properties to treat intestinal parasites. Its bioactive compounds offer significant pharmacological potential in developing natural treatments for parasitic infections.

  • Scientific name: Baccharoides anthelmintica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Baccharoides.
