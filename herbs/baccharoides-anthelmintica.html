Vernonia anthelmintica, scientifically known as Baccharoides anthelmintica, is a medicinal plant valued in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its potent anthelmintic properties, it is primarily used to treat intestinal worms. This shrub offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in managing parasitic infections through its bioactive phytochemical constituents.
