Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution centered primarily within the vast territories of Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. In the European portion of its range, it can be found across East European Russia. The species is also widely distributed throughout several key regions of Siberia. Specifically, it inhabits the areas of Altay, Buryatiya, and Chita. Finally, its presence is confirmed within the Irkutsk region of Siberia.
