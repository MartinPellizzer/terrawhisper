Stachys

Stachys, commonly known as hedge nettles, is a versatile genus of flowering plants valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for their healing properties, various species are used to treat wounds, inflammation, and digestive issues. These resilient perennials offer a fascinating intersection of botanical beauty and ancient medicinal wisdom for natural wellness.
