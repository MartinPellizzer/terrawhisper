Kalanchoe mortagei

Kalanchoe mortagei, commonly known as the "Living Stone" or "Sacred Kalanchoe," is a fascinating succulent prized for its unique medicinal properties. Native to Madagascar, this resilient plant is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices to address various ailments, offering a natural approach to wellness through its bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Africa
