Nymphaea

Water lily genus

Nymphaea, commonly known as water lilies, is a captivating genus of aquatic plants celebrated for their stunning blooms. Beyond their aesthetic beauty, various species possess significant medicinal properties. Historically utilized in traditional practices, they offer therapeutic potential for soothing inflammation, reducing anxiety, and supporting overall neurological wellness through bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Nymphaeaceae
  • Native range: Europe
