Phlomoides laevigata

Phlomoides laevigata, commonly known as the smooth-leaved phlomoides, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Characterized by its smooth, lanceolate leaves and delicate flowering structures, this plant holds significant botanical interest. Researchers explore its unique phytochemical profile for potential therapeutic applications in traditional and modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Phlomoides laevigata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Phlomoides. Specifically, it is known by its scientific name, Phlomoides laeviagata.
