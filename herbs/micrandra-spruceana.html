Micrandra spruceana

Micrandra spruceana, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Exploring its pharmacological potential offers promising insights into developing new therapeutic agents for modern healthcare applications.

  • Scientific name: Micrandra spruceana
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Micrandra spruceana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is classified within the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Micrandra.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of South America. In the northern part of the continent, it can be found in Guyana, Suriname, and Venezuela. Moving toward the western side of the continent, its range extends into Colombia. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Peru. Together, these locations highlight a broad presence across both northern and western South American territories.
