Myrsine linearis

Myrsine linearis, commonly known as the "pau-de-fogo," is a resilient evergreen shrub native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional practices to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Its unique chemical composition offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Myrsine linearis
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Myrsine.
