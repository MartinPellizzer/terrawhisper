Helia alata

Heliia alatata, commonly known as the golden sun-leaf, is a potent medicinal herb cherished in traditional wellness practices. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, ranging from skin rejuvenation to anti-inflammatory support. Its unique bioactive compounds make it a vital resource in natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Helia alata
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Heliopsis alatata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Gentianales. Finally, its taxonomic classification places it in the family Gentianaceae and the genus Heliopsis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats stretching across the tropical regions of the Americas. In Central America, its presence is documented within the countries of Nicaragua and Panamá. Its distribution extends into the Caribbean, where it can be found in Trinidad and Tobago. Furthermore, the species inhabits parts of Northern South America, specifically throughout Guyana. Finally, its geographical reach includes the territory of French Guiana.
