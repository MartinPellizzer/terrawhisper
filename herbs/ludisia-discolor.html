This resilient species adds dramatic texture and elegance to any indoor collection or shaded garden setting.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several specific regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Its range also extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the borders of Cambodia. Furthermore, the species is also native to the tropical landscapes of Laos.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the jewel orchid, is primarily characterized by its striking and ornamental leaves. These foliage elements are the most distinctive part of Ludisia discolor, featuring a deep velvety texture and a dark, almost black base color that is beautifully contrasted by prominent, contrasting veins in shades of red or pink. While the plant may occasionally produce small, inconspicuous flowers, it is the lush and patterned arrangement of the leaves that provides its primary aesthetic value and serves as the main focus of its botanical interest.
