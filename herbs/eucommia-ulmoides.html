Eucommia ulmoides

Eucommia ulmoides, commonly known as Eucommia or cassia bark, is a cornerstone of Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its ability to strengthen bones and tendons, this resilient evergreen tree provides bark rich in latex. It is widely utilized to support kidney health, regulate blood pressure, and promote overall vitality.

  • Scientific name: Eucommia ulmoides
  • Family: Eucommiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Garryales, it is uniquely classified under the family Eucommiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Eucommia.
