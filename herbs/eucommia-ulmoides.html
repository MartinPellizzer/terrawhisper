Du Zhong

Eucommia ulmoides · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (826 studies) · ★★★★★

Du Zhong, scientifically known as Eucommia ulmoides, is a cornerstone of Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its bark, this medicinal plant is primarily used to strengthen the kidneys and bones. It plays a vital role in supporting cardiovascular health and managing joint discomfort, offering significant therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Eucommia ulmoides, is a deciduous tree where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The bark is perhaps the most significant part used in traditional medicine, often harvested for its unique rubbery, filamentous texture. The leaves are also frequently employed in herbal preparations, and each individual leaf contributes to the plant's overall medicinal potency. Furthermore, the seeds are gathered for specific applications, with each tiny seed containing concentrated compounds that are valued in pharmacological studies.
