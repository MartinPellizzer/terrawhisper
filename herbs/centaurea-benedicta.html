Centaurea benedicta

Carduus benedictus

Centaurea benedicta, commonly known as Blessed Thistle, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned primarily for supporting digestive health and easing gastrointestinal discomfort, this potent botanical has long been used to promote overall wellness and soothe the body's natural healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Centaurea benedicta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Centaurea, specifically as the species Centaurea benedicta.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its distribution includes both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species can be found growing in Belgium. Its range continues through the central territories of Germany. Finally, the plant is also documented within the Czechia-Slovakia region.
