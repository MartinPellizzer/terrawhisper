Carduus benedictus, scientifically known as Centaurea benedicta, is a perennial herb historically revered in traditional medicine. Often called Blessed Thistle, it has been long utilized to support digestive health, soothe inflammation, and bolster the immune system. This potent botanical remains a staple in herbal remedies for its restorative properties.
