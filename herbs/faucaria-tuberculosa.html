Faucaria tuberculosa

Faucaria tuberculosa, commonly known as the Zebra Cactus, is a striking succulent native to South Africa. Characterized by its distinctive white, transverse stripes and tooth-like marginal tubercles, this plant is prized by collectors. Beyond its ornamental appeal, it belongs to a genus studied for its unique biochemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Faucaria tuberculosa
  • Family: Aizoaceae
  • Native range: Africa
