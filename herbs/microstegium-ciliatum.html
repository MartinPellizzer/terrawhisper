What's the taxonomical classification of Microstegium ciliatum?

Microstegium ciliatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Poales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Poaceae, falling under the genus Microstegium, and is specifically identified as the species ciliatum.

Each spikelet contains tiny florets protected by specialized bracts that often display a subtle pubescence.
