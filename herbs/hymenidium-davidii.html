Hymenidium davidii

Hymeniidium davidii, commonly known as Davidii, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Historically utilized in traditional practices, this species offers significant bioactive compounds. Understanding its phytochemical profile is essential for exploring its potential applications in modern pharmacology and natural wellness treatments for diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Hymenidium davidii
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is classified under the genus Hymeniidum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Hymeniidum davidii, is identified by various common names depending on the language and region. In Chinese, it is referred to as 松潘滇藁本, a name that is consistently used across the language codes zh, zh-hans, and zh-cn.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions within Asia. In China, its presence is documented in the South-Central areas as well as the North-Central territories. It can also be found in the northern reaches of the country within Inner Mongolia. Additionally, its habitat extends to the high-altitude landscapes of Qinghai. Beyond China, the species is also distributed within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in Nepal.
