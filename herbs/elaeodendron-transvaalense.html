Elaeodendron transvaalense

Elaeodenro transvaalense, commonly known as the Transvaal Alder, is a significant medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, various parts of the plant are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, offering valuable insights into ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Elaeodendron transvaalense
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Elaeodendroon.
