Seseli foliosum

Seseli foliosum, commonly known as leafy seseli, is a delicate perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Native to specific mountainous regions, this botanical species is distinguished by its intricate foliage and fine textures. It holds significant interest in ethnobotany for its unique chemical properties and potential medicinal applications.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Seseli.
