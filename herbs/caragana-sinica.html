Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is widely documented across the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. It is also found further north within the Manchuria region of the country. Beyond the Chinese mainland, the species extends its range into Eastern Asia.
