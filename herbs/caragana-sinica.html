Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Caragana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is widely documented across the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. It is also found further north within the Manchuria region of the country. Beyond the Chinese mainland, the species extends its range into Eastern Asia. Specifically, it can be located growing naturally in Japan.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Caragana sinica or red pagoda bush, utilizes its specialized underground structures for both survival and medicinal purposes. The medicinal properties of the species are primarily concentrated in its roots, which are harvested to create traditional remedies. Specifically, the dried root is used in various decoctions, as the chemical compounds stored within the root system are believed to offer therapeutic benefits.
