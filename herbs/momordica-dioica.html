Spine gourd, scientifically known as Momordica dioica, is a nutritious cucurbitaceous vegetable widely cultivated in South Asia. Renowned for its unique spiny exterior, this medicinal plant offers significant health benefits, including antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-diabetic properties, making it a valuable dietary addition for holistic wellness and disease prevention.
