Eugenia capensis subsp. zeyheri

Eugenia capensis subsp. zehyheri, commonly known as the Cape Myrtle, is a rare medicinal shrub native to specific African ecosystems. Valued for its potent bioactive compounds, this subspecies is studied for its potential antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, offering promising insights into natural pharmacology and traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Eugenia capensis subsp. zeyheri
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it is further nested within the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Eugenia.

Distribution

This plant is specifically found within the southern reaches of the African continent. Its natural range is limited to the unique landscapes of the Cape Provinces. Within this region, it thrives in specific ecological niches characteristic of Southern Africa. This localized distribution makes it a distinctive part of the regional flora. Consequently, the species is most commonly encountered by researchers exploring these coastal and inland areas.
