Indian willow, scientifically known as Salix tetrasperma, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in salicin, it has been traditionally used to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and treat fevers. This resilient plant offers significant botanical interest for its bioactive compounds and potential in modern pharmacology.
