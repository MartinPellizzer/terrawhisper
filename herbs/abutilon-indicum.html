Abutilon indicum

Indian mallow/country mallow

Abutilon indicum, commonly known as Indian Mallow, is a versatile medicinal shrub widely used in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it serves as a natural remedy for digestive issues, skin ailments, and inflammatory conditions. This resilient plant offers significant pharmacological potential for modern holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Abutilon indicum
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Abutilon indicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement within the genus Abutilon.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Abutilon indicum, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, most notably its leaves and fruit. The leaves are often employed in traditional remedies to treat ailments such as inflammation and respiratory issues, frequently used in decoctions or poultices. Additionally, the fruit, which consists of small, schizocarpic capsules containing numerous seeds, is also recognized for its therapeutic potential in treating digestive disorders and other systemic conditions.
