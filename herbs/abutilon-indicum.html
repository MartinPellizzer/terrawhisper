Indian Mallow/Country Mallow, scientifically known as Abutilon indicum, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and diuretic properties. Various parts of this plant are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin disorders to urinary tract infections.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct global regions. In the Macaronesian islands, it can be found growing in Madeira. Plant Parts

This plant, known as Abutilon indicum, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, most notably its leaves and fruit. The leaves are often employed in traditional remedies to treat ailments such as inflammation and respiratory issues, frequently used in decoctions or poultices. Additionally, the fruit, which consists of small, schizocarpic capsules containing numerous seeds, is also recognized for its therapeutic potential in treating digestive disorders and other systemic conditions.
