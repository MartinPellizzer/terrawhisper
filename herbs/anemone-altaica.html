Anemone altaica Fisch. ex C.A.Mey., scientifically known as Anemone altaica, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to the mountainous regions of Central Asia. Renowned for its delicate white blooms, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance, traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its diverse bioactive properties and therapeutic potential.

Plant Parts

This plant, Anemone altaica, utilizes its underground structures for both survival and medicinal application. The primary part of interest is the rhizome, which serves as a nutrient storage organ that allows the plant to persist through dormant seasons. For pharmaceutical or traditional preparations, the dried rhizome is typically harvested and processed, as the dehydration process helps stabilize the bioactive compounds necessary for its therapeutic uses.
