Anemone altaica

Anemone altaica fisch. ex c.a.mey.

Anemone altaica, commonly known as the Altai anemone, is a resilient perennial wildflower native to the mountainous regions of Central Asia. Renowned for its delicate, star-shaped blossoms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally used in folk medicine for its unique bioactive properties and diverse therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Anemone altaica
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Anemone, specifically identified as the species Anemone altaica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across the vast landscapes of Eastern Europe and Siberia. In the European regions, it can be found spanning from Central European Russia to East European Russia. Its presence also extends further north into the territories of North European Russia. Moving eastward into Siberia, the species is specifically documented in the Altay region. Finally, its range continues into the Republic of Buryatiya within the Siberian expanse.
