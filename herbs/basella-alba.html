Scientific literature: Very Extensive (466 studies) · ★★★★★

Vine spinach, scientifically known as Basella alba, is a lush, climbing perennial celebrated for its nutritious, succulent leaves. Widely cultivated in tropical regions, this versatile plant serves as a dietary staple and a traditional medicinal remedy, valued for its cooling properties and ability to support digestive and skin health.

Plant Parts

This plant, Basella alba, utilizes various anatomical structures for its growth and medicinal properties. The lush, succulent leaves are the most prominent feature, often used in traditional remedies for their cooling effects. These leaves grow along climbing stems that are thick and fleshy, providing the necessary support for the vine to ascend. Below the surface, the roots anchor the plant firmly into the soil to absorb essential nutrients. Whether one utilizes the tender leaf for culinary purposes or the entire vine for healing, every part of the plant plays a vital role in its biological function.
