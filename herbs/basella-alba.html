vine spinach

Basella alba · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (466 studies) · ★★★★★

Vine spinach, scientifically known as Basella alba, is a lush, climbing perennial celebrated for its nutritious, succulent leaves. Widely cultivated in tropical regions, this versatile plant serves as a dietary staple and a traditional medicinal remedy, valued for its cooling properties and ability to support digestive and skin health.
