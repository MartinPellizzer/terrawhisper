Solanum mauense

Solanum mauense, commonly known as a lesser-known medicinal herb, belongs to the Solanaceae family. Found primarily in tropical regions, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its unique bioactive compounds. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential pharmacological properties, particularly for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Solanum mauense
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Solanales, falling within the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Solanum.

This plant, Solanum mauense, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In Spanish, it is frequently referred to as matalote, and in English, it is known as nightshade.

Distribution

This plant is native to specific regions within the East Tropical Africa zone. Its documented presence is primarily concentrated in the country of Kenya. Additionally, the species can be found growing within the borders of Tanzania. These two nations represent the key geographical areas where its natural habitat exists. Consequently, the distribution of Solanum mauense is closely tied to these East African territories.
