Buddleja sessiliflora

Rio grande butterfly-bush

Buddleja sessiliflora, commonly known as the sessile-flowered butterfly bush, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its dense, cluster-like blooms, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, leveraging its unique phytochemical properties to support wellness and promote healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Buddleja sessiliflora
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Buddleja, specifically as the species Buddleja sessiliflora.
