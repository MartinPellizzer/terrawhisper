Phlomoides umbrosa

Phlomoides umbrosa, commonly known as the Shadowed Phlomoides, is a specialized perennial herb native to specific montane ecosystems. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant possesses unique bioactive compounds. Understanding its phytochemical properties and ecological niche is essential for exploring its potential medicinal applications and ensuring its conservation in nature.

  • Scientific name: Phlomoides umbrosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Phlomoides.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several distinct regions of China. Its presence can be documented within the territories of China South-Central and China Southeast. Additionally, the species is found extending into the northern landscapes of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. The distribution further encompasses the expansive areas of China North-Central.
