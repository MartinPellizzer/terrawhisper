Zanthoxylum heterophyllum

Zanthoxylum heterophyllum, commonly known as Wild Lime or Lakacha, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional Ayurveda. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, its leaves and bark are utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive disorders, and skin infections, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum heterophyllum
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Sapindales and is a member of the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Zanthoxylum.
