, scientifically known as Jateorhiza palmata, is a powerful medicinal plant native to the tropical regions of Africa. Commonly referred to as "African Ginger," this rhizomatous herb is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and digestive properties, offering diverse therapeutic benefits for various ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its distribution includes the country of Ghana. Moving toward the eastern coast, it can be found in both Kenya and Tanzania within East Tropical Africa. Its reach extends further south into the regions of Malawi and Mozambique. Overall, these locations highlight the plant's diverse habitat spanning from west to south tropical Africa.
