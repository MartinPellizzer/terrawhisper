Longleaf, scientifically known as Falcaria vulgaris, is a distinctive perennial herb belonging to the Brassicaceae family. Often found in rocky, coastal habitats, it is characterized by its deeply lobed, long leaves and bright yellow flowers. Historically used in traditional medicine, this resilient plant offers unique botanical and therapeutic interest.

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across various regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is well-documented in Denmark and Sweden.
