Marsdenia griffithii

What's the taxonomical classification of Marsdenia griffithii?

Marsdenia griffithii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianaales. This plant is a representative of the family Apocynaceae, specifically situated within the genus Marsdenia, and is identified by its unique species designation, griffithii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Marsdenia griffithii has woody, twining stems that produce opposite, elliptical to oblong leaves with entire margins and acute or obtuse tips. The foliage typically exhibits a smooth, somewhat leathery texture and a deep green coloration. Small, tubular flowers emerge from the axils, often displaying a pale yellowish or creamy hue. These blossoms are frequently clustered in cymes, which are characteristic of the Apocynaceae family. The plant's overall structure is designed for climbing through dense vegetation using its flexible stems.
