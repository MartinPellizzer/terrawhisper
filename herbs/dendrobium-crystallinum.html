Morphology

The morphology of Dendrobium crystallinum is characterized by its growth habit as an obligatory epiphyte, relying on host trees for structural support within its natural habitat. It functions as a self-supporting climber, utilizing its specialized structures to maintain its position without the need for parasitic interaction, as it remains entirely independent of its host for nutrients. In terms of structural composition, the plant is non-woody, exhibiting a succulent or herbaceous texture rather than a rigid, lignified stem.
