Dendrobium crystallinum

Dendrobium crystallinum, commonly known as the Crystal Dendrobium, is a captivating epiphytic orchid native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its translucent, crystalline appearance and delicate blooms, this species is highly prized by collectors. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant interest in traditional botanical studies and specialized horticultural practices.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium crystallinum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, it is identified as a member of the genus Dendrobium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct Asian regions. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central provinces as well as on the island of Hainan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found inhabiting the territory of Assam and extending into Bangladesh. Its range also reaches into the Indo-China region, specifically within the borders of Cambodia. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse tropical and subtropical environments where the plant thrives.
