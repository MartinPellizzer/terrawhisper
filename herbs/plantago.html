Plantain, scientifically known as Plantago, is a versatile medicinal herb widely recognized for its healing properties. Often found in lawns and meadows, this resilient plant contains bioactive compounds that offer anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing benefits. It is traditionally used topically to soothe stings, scrapes, and minor skin irritations.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Plantaginaceae and falls under the genus Plantago.
