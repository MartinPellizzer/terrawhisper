Plantago

Plantago, commonly known as plantain, is a versatile medicinal herb valued for centuries in traditional healing. Renowned for its soothing properties, it is frequently used to treat skin irritations, minor wounds, and digestive ailments. This resilient plant offers natural anti-inflammatory and antibacterial benefits, making it a staple in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Plantago
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Plantaginaceae and falls under the genus Plantago.
