Korean mistletoe, scientifically known as Viscum coloratum, is a parasitic evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, it is frequently utilized to support cardiovascular health, balance internal energies, and reduce inflammation. This potent botanical offers unique therapeutic properties, making it a significant subject in herbal pharmacology.
