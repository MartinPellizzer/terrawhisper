Korean mistletoe, scientifically known as Viscum coloratum, is a parasitic evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, it is frequently utilized to support cardiovascular health, balance internal energies, and reduce inflammation. This potent botanical offers unique therapeutic properties, making it a significant subject in herbal pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found across the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye territories. Its range also extends significantly into China, where it is documented in the South-Central regions. Additionally, the species is present in the northern reaches of China within Inner Mongolia. Together, these areas define the specific environmental niches where the species thrives.
