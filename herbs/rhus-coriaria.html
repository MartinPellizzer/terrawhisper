Rhus coriaria

Rhus coriaria, commonly known as sumac, is a versatile shrub native to the Mediterranean and Middle East. Renowned for its vibrant berries, it serves as both a culinary spice and a potent medicinal herb. Rich in antioxidants, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Rhus coriaria
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Rhus.
