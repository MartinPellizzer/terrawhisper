Sumac

Rhus coriaria · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (385 studies) · ★★★★★

Sumac, scientifically known as Rhus coriaria, is a versatile Mediterranean shrub prized for its medicinal and culinary properties. Rich in antioxidants and tannins, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities. Traditionally used to treat digestive issues and skin ailments, this vibrant plant remains a vital element in herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as sumac, possesses various components that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The leaves, often referred to simply as the leaf, are frequently studied for their antioxidant content, while the fruits serve as a primary source of bioactive compounds. Specifically, the fruit epicarp is noted for its distinct texture and chemical profile, which contributes to the overall therapeutic value of the plant. Additionally, the oleo gum resin extracted from the plant is highly valued in traditional applications for its unique medicinal characteristics.
