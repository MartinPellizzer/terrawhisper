Arisaema dracontium

Arisaema dracontium, commonly known as Konnyaku, is a perennial plant native to East Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, its tubers are processed to create konjac, a versatile dietary staple. Beyond culinary uses, it offers unique medicinal properties, requiring careful preparation to manage its natural toxicity for safe consumption.

  • Scientific name: Arisaema dracontium
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Aliasmaetales, it is classified under the family Araceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Arisaema.
