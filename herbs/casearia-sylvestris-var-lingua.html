Casearia sylvestris var. lingua

Casearia sylvestris var. lingua, commonly known as "linguazinha" in Brazil, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional folk medicine. Native to South American forests, this plant is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues through various decoctions and extracts.

  • Scientific name: Casearia sylvestris var. lingua
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Malpighiales in the family Salicaceae. Specifically, the medicinal variety lingua is a member of the genus Casearia.
