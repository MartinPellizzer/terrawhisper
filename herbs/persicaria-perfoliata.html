Persicaria perfoliata

Mile-a-minute weed

Persicaria perfoliata, commonly known as climbing smartweed, is a distinctive climbing herbaceous plant recognized by its unique leaves that wrap around the stem. Native to various tropical regions, it holds significant traditional importance, often utilized in herbal medicine for its purported healing properties and its role in diverse ecological systems.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria perfoliata
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Persicaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented within the borders of Germany. The species also extends significantly into the Russian Far East, where it can be found in the Amur region. Furthermore, it inhabits the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories of the Russian Far East. Finally, its geographical range reaches the island of Sakhalin within the same Far Eastern area.
