Mile-A-Minute Weed, scientifically known as Persicaria perfoliata, is a vigorous, creeping perennial herb native to tropical regions. Characterized by its rapid growth and delicate, heart-shaped leaves, this plant is often viewed as a nuisance in gardens. However, it holds significant traditional value in various cultures for its medicinal properties.
