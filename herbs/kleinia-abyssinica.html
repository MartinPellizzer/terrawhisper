Kleinia abyssinica

Kleinia abyssinica, commonly known as the Abyssian Kleinia, is a significant medicinal plant native to East African highlands. Valued in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammation. This succulent species offers promising biochemical properties for modern ethnopharmacological research.

  • Family: Asteraceae
