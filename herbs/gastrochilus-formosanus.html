What's the taxonomical classification of Gastrochilus formosanus?

Gastrochilus formosanus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Within this family, the plant is categorized under the genus Gastrochilus, and its specific designation is the species formosanus.
