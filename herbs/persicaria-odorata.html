Vietnamese Mint, scientifically known as Persicaria odorata, is a pungent, aromatic herb widely celebrated in Southeast Asian cuisine. Characterized by its distinct peppery and citrusy flavor, this medicinal plant offers potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable addition to both culinary dishes and traditional wellness practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Vietnamese coriander, primarily utilizes its aromatic leaves for medicinal and culinary purposes. The leaves are the most significant part of the plant used in traditional practices, as they contain the essential oils responsible for its distinct scent and therapeutic properties. Whether used fresh or dried, the leaf serves as the primary source of the plant's bioactive compounds, which are often employed to aid digestion or provide antioxidant benefits.
