Nymphaea nouchali

Nymphaea nouchali, commonly known as the Blue Water Lily, is a sacred aquatic plant revered in many Eastern traditions. Renowned for its stunning azure blooms, it possesses significant medicinal properties, often used in traditional healing to reduce inflammation, soothe the nervous system, and promote overall mental well-being and tranquility.

  • Scientific name: Nymphaea nouchali
  • Family: Nymphaeaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Nymphaea nouchali, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, following the order Nymphaeaales. Within its taxonomic hierarchy, it is a member of the family Nymphaeaceae and the genus Nymphaea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range spanning across several regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Egypt. Moving toward the center of the continent, it is widely distributed throughout West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found in the nations of Burundi, Congo, and Rwanda. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the vast territory of the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
