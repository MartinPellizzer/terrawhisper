Star lotus

Star lotus, scientifically known as Nymphaea nouchali, is a magnificent aquatic perennial revered in traditional medicine. Renowned for its striking blooms, this plant offers potent therapeutic properties. It is widely utilized in Ayurvedic practices to soothe the nervous system, reduce inflammation, and promote holistic mental and physical well-being.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range spanning across several regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the borders of Egypt. Moving toward the center of the continent, it is widely distributed throughout West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found in the nations of Burundi, Congo, and Rwanda. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the vast territory of the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
