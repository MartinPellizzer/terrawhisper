Monolluma quadrangula

Mondia whitei (often referred to as Mondia quadrangularis), commonly known as White Mondia, is a powerful medicinal shrub native to Africa. Renowned for its potent aphrodisiac properties and tonic effects, this plant is widely used in traditional medicine to enhance vitality, support reproductive health, and balance hormonal functions.

  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
