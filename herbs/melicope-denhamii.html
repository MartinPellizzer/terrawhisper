Melicope denhamii

Euodia quercifolia

Melicope denhamii, commonly known as a member of the Rutaceae family, is a medicinal plant indigenous to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it contains bioactive compounds studied for their potential therapeutic properties, including antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects, offering significant promise in traditional and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Melicope denhamii
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It falls into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, specifically as a member of the family Rutaceae. Within this family, it is categorized under the genus Melicope.

Common Names

This plant is known by several different names depending on the language and region. In English, it is referred to as Euodia quercifolia, while in Indonesian, it is identified as both Melicope denhami and Euodia quercifolia. In Javanese, the name used is Melicope denhami, and in Madurese, it is commonly called Kol-kolan.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions within the Malesian and Papuasian botanical zones. Within the Malesia region, its presence is documented in Borneo, Maluku, and Sulawesi. Moving into the Papuasia region, it can be found throughout New Guinea. Its range also extends to the Bismarck Archipelago, further diversifying its geographical footprint. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's significant presence across the tropical islands of Southeast Asia and Oceania.
