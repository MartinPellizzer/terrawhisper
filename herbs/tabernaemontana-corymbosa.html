Tabernaemontana corymbosa

Tabernaemontana corymbosa, commonly known as the Pinwheel Flower, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive alkaloids, this plant is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat inflammation, fever, and digestive issues. Its therapeutic potential continues to inspire modern pharmacological research and botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Tabernaemontana corymbosa
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tabernaemontana.

Common Names
