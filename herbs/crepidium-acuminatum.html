Crepidium acuminatum

Crepidium acuminatum, commonly known as a specialized orchid species, is a fascinating medicinal plant found in tropical forest ecosystems. Renowned for its unique morphological characteristics, it holds significant potential in traditional healing practices. Exploring its phytochemical properties offers promising insights into developing new natural therapeutic treatments for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Crepidium acuminatum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Crepidium, with its species name being Crepidium acuminatum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its distribution spans from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast. It is also documented as growing in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found specifically in Assam. Additionally, its presence extends across the borders into Bangladesh.
