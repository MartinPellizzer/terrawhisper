Grape genus, scientifically known as Vitis, is a diverse group of woody climbing vines celebrated for their medicinal and nutritional properties. Renowned for high antioxidant content, particularly resveratrol, Vitis species are utilized in traditional medicine to support cardiovascular health, reduce inflammation, and combat oxidative stress throughout the body.
