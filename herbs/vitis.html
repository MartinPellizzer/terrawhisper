Vitis

Grape genus

Vitis, commonly known as grapevines, encompasses a diverse genus of climbing woody plants celebrated globally. Beyond their culinary fame, these vines possess significant medicinal value. Rich in polyphenols and antioxidants like resveratrol, Vitis extracts are widely utilized in traditional and modern medicine to support cardiovascular health and combat inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Vitis
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Vitales and family Vitaceae, it is classified under the genus Vitis.

Common Names

This plant, known as Vitis, is referred to by several different common names depending on the language.
