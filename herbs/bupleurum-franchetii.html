, scientifically known as Bupleurum franchetii, is a traditional medicinal herb widely utilized in East Asian pharmacology. Often referred to as Chai Hu, this perennial plant is prized for its ability to relieve stagnation, clear heat, and soothe the liver. It plays a crucial role in treating various inflammatory conditions.
