Dutchman's Pipe, scientifically known as Aristolochia trilobata, is a perennial climbing vine native to the southeastern United States. Characterized by its distinctive heart-shaped leaves and tubular flowers, this plant holds significant historical importance in ethnobotany, though its use is strictly cautioned due to potential nephrotoxic and carcinogenic properties.
