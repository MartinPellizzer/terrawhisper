Aristolochia trilobata

Aristolochia trilobata, commonly known as trilobate birthwort, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to East Asia. Characterized by its distinctive three-lobed leaves, it has historically been utilized in traditional medicine. However, modern science warns against its use due to the presence of nephrotoxic and carcinogenic aristolochic acids.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia trilobata
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aristolochia trilobata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Piperales. Finally, it is classified under the family Aristolochiaceae within the genus Aristolochia.
