Baccharis pentziifolia var. pentziifolia

Baccharis pentziifolia var. pentziifolia, commonly known as the shrubby Baccharis, is a resilient medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation and infections, offering significant potential for modern bioactive compound research and development.

  • Scientific name: Baccharis pentziifolia var. pentziifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling within the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is identified as a member of the genus Baccharis, specifically the variety pentziifolia.
