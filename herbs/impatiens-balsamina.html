Plant Parts

This plant, Impatiens balsamina, is characterized by a variety of distinct anatomical structures that contribute to its growth and medicinal potential. The plant features succulent, fleshy stems that support its upright posture, while its vibrant, colorful flowers are often the most striking feature, attracting pollinators with their unique shapes. The leaves are typically serrated and spread along the stem to maximize sunlight absorption for photosynthesis. Below the surface, the roots serve to anchor the plant and absorb essential nutrients and moisture from the soil.
