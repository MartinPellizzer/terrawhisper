Chinese necklace poplar, scientifically known as Populus lasiocarpa, is a versatile deciduous tree valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bark, which contains salicin, it offers potent anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties. This species serves as a natural remedy for pain relief, supporting holistic approaches to managing various ailments.
