Populus lasiocarpa

Populus lasiocarpia, commonly known as the Black Poplar, is a resilient deciduous tree native to East Asia. Beyond its ecological significance, various parts of the tree have been utilized in traditional medicine. Its bark and leaves are often studied for their bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic applications in herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Populus lasiocarpa
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales and the family Salicaceae, it is classified under the genus Populus.
