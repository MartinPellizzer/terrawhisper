What parts of this plant are used medicinally?

This plant Schisandra neglecta is used medicinally through the application of its leaves and stems. These specific components are often harvested to prepare traditional decoctions or topical treatments. The leaves contain bioactive compounds that contribute to the overall therapeutic properties of the species. Similarly, the stems are utilized in various herbal formulations to address different health concerns. Integrating both parts allows practitioners to utilize the full chemical profile of the plant.

According to a study published by "Bioorganic chemistry", schisdilactones K-U (1-11) were isolated from the leaves and stems of Schisandra neglecta A. C. Smith. These 16,17-secopreschisanartane-type schinortriterpenoids (SNTs) include schisdilactones K (1), O (5), and R (8) which were confirmed by single-crystal X-ray crystallographic analysis. Additionally, the configurations of schisdilactones T and U (10 and 11) were elucidated using quantum chemical calculation. Schisdilactones L-S (2-8) and U (11) exhibited moderate protective activities against corticosterone-induced apoptosis of PC12 cells at 20 μM. During these tests, cell viability was found to be in the range of 62.95-66.97%.
