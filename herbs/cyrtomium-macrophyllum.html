Cyrtomium macrophyllum

What's the taxonomical classification of Cyrtomium macrophyllum?

Cyrtomium macrophyllum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Polypodiales and the family Polypodiaceae, eventually reaching its specific designation within the genus Cyrtomium. Ultimately, the plant is identified by its unique species name, macrophyllum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cyrtomium macrophyllum has large, pinnately compound fronds that typically reach lengths of up to 60 centimeters and feature multiple pairs of broadly lanceolate or ovate leaflets. The texture of these leaflets is notably thin and membranous, often exhibiting a slightly glossy surface. Each individual pinna is characterized by finely serrated margins and prominent venation that extends toward the edges. The rhizomes of this fern are creeping and stout, providing a sturdy base for the emergent foliage. These morphological traits collectively define its presence in moist, shaded woodland habitats.
