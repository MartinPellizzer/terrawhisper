African Milk Bush, scientifically known as Euphorbia umbellata, is a succulent shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its umbrella-shaped canopy and striking appearance, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine. It is primarily utilized to treat various ailments, including skin conditions, inflammation, and digestive issues.
