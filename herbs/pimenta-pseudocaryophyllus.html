Pimenta pseudocaryophyllus

Pimenta pseudocaryophyllus, commonly known as Cravo-do-mato, is a potent medicinal shrub native to Brazil. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it contains essential oils with significant antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat respiratory issues and skin ailments, it remains a vital subject of pharmacological study.

  • Scientific name: Pimenta pseudocaryophyllus
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Pimenta.
