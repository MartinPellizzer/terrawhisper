Rubus pungens

What's the taxonomical classification of Rubus pungens?

Rubus pungens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Rosales and the family Rosaecae, eventually reaching its specific designation within the genus Rubus. Ultimately, this plant is identified by its unique species name, pungens.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rubus pungens has upright, arching stems that are densely covered in sharp, recurved prickles and feature small, serrated leaves arranged alternately along the branches. The leaves are typically trifoliate or five-foliate with ovate leaflets that possess distinctively pointed tips and deeply impressed veins. These leaves exhibit a dark green upper surface and a paler underside, often with a slightly rugose texture. The plant's woody canes can reach several feet in height, providing a structural framework for its sprawling growth habit. During the blooming season, small white flowers emerge from the axils of the leaves before transitioning into aggregate fruits.
