Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Central Asia, it can be found growing in the territories of Turkmenistan, Tajikistan, and Uzbekistan. Its presence is also significant throughout various parts of China, particularly in the South-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is documented as growing on the island of Hainan. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to inhabit multiple distinct environmental zones.
