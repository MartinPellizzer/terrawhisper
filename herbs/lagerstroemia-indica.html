Lagerstroemia indica

Crepe myrtle

Lagerstroemia indica, commonly known as Crape Myrtle, is a versatile ornamental plant celebrated for its vibrant, crepe-like blooms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. Various parts of the plant are utilized for their anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties to treat diverse ailments.

  • Scientific name: Lagerstroemia indica
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the Lythraceae family, specifically placed in the genus Lagerstroemia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lagerstroemia indica, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In Chinese, it is referred to as 紫薇 in simplified Chinese (zh-hans), traditional Chinese (zh-hant), and Cantonese (zh-hk). In Poland, it is known as Lagerstremia indyjska (pl), while in Russia, it is commonly called Лагерстрёмия индийская (ru).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Central Asia, it can be found growing in the territories of Turkmenistan, Tajikistan, and Uzbekistan. Its presence is also significant throughout various parts of China, particularly in the South-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is documented as growing on the island of Hainan. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to inhabit multiple distinct environmental zones.
