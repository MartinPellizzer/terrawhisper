Sideritis euboea

Sideritis euboea, commonly known as Sideritis, is a versatile medicinal herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and skin irritations. This plant serves as a valuable natural resource in herbal medicine and traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Sideritis euboea
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
