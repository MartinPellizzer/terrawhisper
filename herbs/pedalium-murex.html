Pedalium murex

Pedalium murium, commonly known as Punarnava or Sandakalli, is a resilient medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its powerful diuretic and rejuvenative properties, it is widely used in traditional medicine to support kidney health, reduce edema, and combat inflammation, offering significant therapeutic benefits for overall vitality.

  • Scientific name: Pedalium murex
  • Family: Pedaliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Lamiales and the family Pedaliaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pedalium.

Distribution

This plant, known as Pedalium murianum, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally within the borders of Benin. Additionally, the species is documented to occur throughout Ghana. Its presence is also noted in the Ivory Coast, highlighting its regional importance. Finally, the plant is distributed within the territories of both Nigeria and Togo.
