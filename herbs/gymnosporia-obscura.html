Gymnosporia obscura

Gymnosporia obscura, commonly known as the black gymnospore, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots possess significant therapeutic properties, often used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic health management.

  • Scientific name: Gymnosporia obscura
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Gymnosporia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted specifically in Burundi. Moving toward the northeast, it can be found within the territories of Ethiopia and the combined regions of Sudan and South Sudan. The species also extends into East Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Kenya. Finally, its geographical range continues southward into Tanzania.
