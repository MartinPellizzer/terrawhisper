Amur rose, scientifically known as Rosa davuricia, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its striking pink blooms and hardiness, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional practices. It is frequently utilized for its antioxidant properties, supporting skin health and promoting overall immune system vitality.
