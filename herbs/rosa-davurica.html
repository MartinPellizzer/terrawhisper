Amur rose, scientifically known as Rosa davuricia, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its striking pink blooms and hardiness, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional practices. It is frequently utilized for its antioxidant properties, supporting skin health and promoting overall immune system vitality.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Eurasia. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented within the borders of Poland. Moving eastward, it is widely distributed across various territories in Siberia. Specifically, its habitat includes the regions of Buryatiya, Chita, and Irkutsk. Finally, the species can also be found extending into the remote territory of Yakutiya.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Rosa davurica, utilizes its fruits as a significant component in traditional medicinal practices. These fleshy fruits, often referred to as rose hips, are harvested once they have fully ripened to ensure a high concentration of bioactive compounds. Within these fruits, various nutrients and medicinal properties are stored, making them a valuable part of the plant's overall therapeutic profile.
