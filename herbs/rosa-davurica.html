Rosa davurica

Amur rose

Rosa davurica, commonly known as the Davurica rose, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its delicate pink blooms and aromatic properties, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality through its nutrient-rich petals.

  • Scientific name: Rosa davurica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Rosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Eurasia. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented within the borders of Poland. Moving eastward, it is widely distributed across various territories in Siberia. Specifically, its habitat includes the regions of Buryatiya, Chita, and Irkutsk. Finally, the species can also be found extending into the remote territory of Yakutiya.
