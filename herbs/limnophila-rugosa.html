Limnophila rugosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Limnophila rugosa?

Limnopila rugosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further categorized under the order Lamiales. It is a representative of the family Plantaginaceae, specifically falling under the genus Limnopila, with its specific designation being the species rugosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Limnophila rugosa has elongated, lanceolate leaves that feature a distinctively wrinkled or rugose texture on their upper surfaces. These leaves are arranged in whorls of three to five around a slender, flexible stem. The leaf margins are typically smooth, though the overall appearance is defined by the deep, irregular veining. This plant often grows in dense clusters, with its submerged foliage providing significant surface area for aquatic life. Its growth habit allows it to sway easily with water currents in its natural habitat.
