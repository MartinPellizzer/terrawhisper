Cissampelos pareira

Cisampelos pareira, commonly known as Pareira Brava, is a climbing medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly in Ayurveda, it possesses potent astringent and anti-inflammatory properties. It is frequently utilized to treat digestive disorders, skin ailments, and various inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Cissampelos pareira
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ranunculales, it is further categorized into the family Menispermaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established under the genus Cissampelos.

Moving toward Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found spanning across Ethiopia, Somalia, and the territories of Sudan and South Sudan. Additionally, the species extends its reach into East Tropical Africa, specifically within Kenya.
