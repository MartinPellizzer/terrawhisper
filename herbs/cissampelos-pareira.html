This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Rwanda. Moving toward Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found spanning across Ethiopia, Somalia, and the territories of Sudan and South Sudan. Additionally, the species extends its reach into East Tropical Africa, specifically within Kenya. Collectively, these locations highlight a wide longitudinal spread throughout the tropical zones of Africa.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as pareira, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, though the most significant parts are the roots. The large, tuberous roots are the primary part used in traditional remedies, often prepared as a decoction to treat digestive issues. Even a single, potent root can provide concentrated therapeutic benefits, while the green leaf serves as a key identifying characteristic of the species during its growth phase.
