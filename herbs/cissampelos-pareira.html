The physiology of Cissampelos pareira is characterized by a C3 photosynthetic pathway, a common metabolic strategy among many dicotyledonous plants where carbon fixation occurs via the Calvin cycle without the specialized adaptations seen in C4 or CAM species. This pathway involves the initial fixation of atmospheric carbon dioxide into a three-carbon compound, facilitating the synthesis of carbohydrates necessary for the plant's growth and the production of its bioactive secondary metabolites.
