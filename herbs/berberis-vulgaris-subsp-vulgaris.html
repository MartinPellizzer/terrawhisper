Berberis vulgaris vulgaris, scientifically known as Berberis vulgaris subsp. vulgaris, is a deciduous shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Rich in the alkaloid berberine, this plant has been used traditionally to support digestive health, manage blood sugar levels, and combat infections, offering a potent natural remedy in herbal medicine.
