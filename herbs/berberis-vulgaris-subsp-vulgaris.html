Berberis vulgaris subsp. vulgaris

Berberis vulgaris vulgaris

Berberis vulgaris subsp. vulgaris, commonly known as barberry, is a deciduous shrub valued for its medicinal properties. Rich in the alkaloid berberine, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, manage blood sugar levels, and combat inflammation. Its tart berries and bark offer potent antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits.

  • Scientific name: Berberis vulgaris subsp. vulgaris
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Berberis, specifically as the subspecies vulgaris.
