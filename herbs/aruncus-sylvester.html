Aruncus sylvester

What's the taxonomical classification of Aruncus sylvester?

Aruncus sylvester belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical structure, it is placed under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Aruncus, and its species designation, sylvester.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aruncus sylvester has large, pinnately compound leaves measuring up to 30 centimeters in length and terminal panicles of creamy white flowers that bloom in midsummer. The foliage consists of several pairs of leaflets that are sharply serrated and deeply textured along their margins. These leaves grow on sturdy, upright stems that can reach heights of up to 1.5 meters. The flowering clusters are dense and plume-like, composed of numerous tiny individual blossoms that create a frothy appearance. Beneath the surface, the plant develops a vigorous rhizomatous root system that allows it to spread through woodland environments.
