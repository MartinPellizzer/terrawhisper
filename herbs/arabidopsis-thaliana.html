Arabidopsis thaliana

Arabidopsis thaliana, commonly known as thale cress, is a foundational model organism in plant biology. Despite its small size, this rapid-cycling crucifer provides invaluable insights into genetics, development, and molecular signaling. Its sequenced genome and ease of cultivation make it indispensable for advancing our understanding of fundamental botanical processes.

  • Scientific name: Arabidopsis thaliana
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales and family Brassicaceae, it is classified under the genus Arabidopsis.
