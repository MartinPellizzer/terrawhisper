Aletris spicata

Aletris spicata, commonly known as white maidenhair vervain, is a delicate perennial wildflower native to North American wetlands. Distinguished by its elegant, upright white flower spikes, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine. It is often valued for its purported soothing properties and its role in botanical ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Aletris spicata
  • Family: Nartheciaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
