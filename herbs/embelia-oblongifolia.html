Embelia oblongifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Embelia oblongifolia?

Embelia oblongifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant is part of the order Ericales and falls within the family Primulaceae. Finally, its specific biological identity is defined by its placement in the genus Embelia, with the distinct species name oblongifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Embelia oblongifolia has simple, opposite, oblong to elliptic leaves that are typically entire, leathery in texture, and possess an acute to obtuse apex. The stems are woody and often exhibit a scrambling or climbing growth habit to navigate surrounding vegetation. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the leaf axils and are characterized by their delicate structure. The fruit is a small, fleshy drupe that develops following successful pollination. These morphological traits collectively define the plant's physical appearance within its native habitat.
