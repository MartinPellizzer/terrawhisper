Yellow flax, scientifically known as Reinwardtia indica, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its vibrant purple blooms, this plant is deeply embedded in traditional Ayurvedic practices. It is widely utilized for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties to treat various ailments.
