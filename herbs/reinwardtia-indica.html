Reinwardtia indica

Reinwardtia indica, commonly known as Indian Reinwardtia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. This plant is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and respiratory conditions, offering valuable therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Reinwardtia indica
  • Family: Linaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Reinwardtia indica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Linaeaee. Its taxonomic journey concludes within the genus Reinwardtia.
