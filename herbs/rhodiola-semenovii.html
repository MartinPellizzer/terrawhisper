Rhodiola semenovii

Rhodiola semenovii, commonly known as Siberian Rhodiola, is a potent adaptogen native to the harsh, high-altitude regions of Central Asia. Renowned for its ability to enhance physical stamina and mental resilience, this resilient plant helps the body manage stress, combat fatigue, and support overall metabolic and cognitive health.

  • Scientific name: Rhodiola semenovii
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifragales, it is further categorized into the family Crassulaceae. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the rugged landscapes of Central Asia. It can be found growing in the territories of Kazakhstan, Kirgizstan, Tadzhikistan, and Uzbekistan. In addition to these Central Asian nations, its range extends into the eastern part of the continent. Specifically, it is documented within the North-Central regions of China.
