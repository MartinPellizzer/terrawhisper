Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Stemona kerrii, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its medicinal roots. The tuberous roots are the most significant part of the plant, as they contain the bioactive alkaloids responsible for its pharmacological properties. These roots are typically harvested, dried, and processed to be used in various herbal formulations to treat respiratory ailments and parasitic infections.
