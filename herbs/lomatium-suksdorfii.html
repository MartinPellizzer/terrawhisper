Lomatium suksdorfii

Lomatium suksdorfii, commonly known as Suksdorf's lomatium, is a delicate perennial herb native to Western North America. Belonging to the Apiaceae family, this plant features charming white flower clusters. Historically used by Indigenous communities, it holds significant ethnobotanical value for its potential medicinal properties and its role in diverse ecosystems.
