Ferula narthex

Ferula narthex, commonly known as the nard or aromatic fennel, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its aromatic rhizomes possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant has long been utilized for its essential oils in various therapeutic applications.
