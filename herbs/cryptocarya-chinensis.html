Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence also extends to the island nation of Japan. Furthermore, the species is documented within the Nansei-shotō archipelago.
