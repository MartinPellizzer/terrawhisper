Periploca angustifolia

Periploca angustifolia, commonly known as the "climbing milkweed," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various infections, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Periploca angustifolia
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, falling under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Periploca.
