Cassia surattensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Cassia surattensis?

Cassia surattensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Fabales. As a member of the Fabaceae family, it is placed in the genus Cassia, eventually reaching its specific identification as the species surattensis.

As the plant matures, it produces distinct woody legumes that hang from the branches and eventually split to release small seeds. According to a study published by "European review for medical and pharmacological sciences", the flower of Cassia surattensis is used medicinally and its methanolic extract exhibited a zone of inhibition of 15 mm. The extract showed a minimum inhibitory concentration of 6.25 mg/ml and an IC50 of 2.49 mg/ml on hyphal growth. Phytochemical screening of the flower extract revealed the presence of carbohydrate, tannins, saponins and phenols. The amount of tannin was estimated to be 55.14 ± 3.11 mg/g, while total phenolics were 349.87 ± 5.41 mg/g gallic acid equivalent. Additionally, the flavonoids were estimated to be 89.64 ± 3.21 mg/g catechin equivalent.
