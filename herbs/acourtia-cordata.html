Acourtia cordata

Acourtia cordata, commonly known as the heart-leafed acourtia, is a resilient perennial herb native to various tropical and subtropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant is recognized for its distinctive foliage and potential therapeutic properties, offering intriguing possibilities for natural medicine and sustainable pharmacological research in biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Acourtia cordata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Acourtia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Acourtia cordata, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently called heart-leaf acourtia, while in Spanish, it is commonly known as acourtia de hoja de corazón or acourtia de hoja de corazón.
