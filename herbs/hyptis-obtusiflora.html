Hyptis obtusiflora

Hyptis obtusiflora, commonly known as the blunt-leaf Hyptis, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its unique floral structure, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights into natural healing and biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Hyptis obtusiflora
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
