Glaucidium palmatum

Glaucidium palmatum, commonly known as the Palm-leaf Glaucidium, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and support digestive health. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
