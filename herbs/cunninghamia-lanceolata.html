Cunninghamia lanceolata

Cunninghamia lanceolata, commonly known as the Lancewood, is a versatile Australian medicinal plant. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it has traditionally been used by Indigenous communities to treat various ailments. This study explores its therapeutic potential, focusing on its antioxidant properties and its significant role in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Cunninghamia lanceolata
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, it is further categorized into the family Cupressaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cunninghamia, specifically as the species Cunninghamia lanceolata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans several distinct regions. In the Atlantic Ocean, its presence can be noted on the island of St. Helena. Within Asia, it is widely spread across various provinces of China. Specifically, it is found in the South-Central and North-Central regions of the country.
