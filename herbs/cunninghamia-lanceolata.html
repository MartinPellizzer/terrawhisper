Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans several distinct regions. In the Atlantic Ocean, its presence can be noted on the island of St. Helena. Within Asia, it is widely spread across various provinces of China. Specifically, it is found in the South-Central and North-Central regions of the country. Additionally, its range extends to the island of Hainan and the high-altitude areas of Qinghai.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cunninghamia lanceolata, is a coniferous species characterized by its robust structure, where the wood serves as a primary component for various industrial and medicinal applications. Within the trunk, the dense and durable heartwood is particularly notable for its distinct color and strength, often utilized in traditional practices due to its unique chemical properties. The quality and density of both the structural wood and the inner heartwood are essential indicators of the tree's maturity and its potential efficacy in botanical uses.
