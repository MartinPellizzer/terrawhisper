Aconitum carmichaelii

Aconitum carmichaelii, commonly known as Chinese Aconite or Fuzi, is a potent medicinal herb central to Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its warming properties, it is used to treat cold-deficiency syndromes and pain. However, due to its extreme toxicity, it requires rigorous processing to ensure safe therapeutic application.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum carmichaelii
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aconitum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several distinct regions in Asia and Europe. In China, its distribution is extensive, covering areas such as South-Central China and North-Central China. It is also found throughout the Manchuria region and the northern reaches of Inner Mongolia. Beyond Asia, the species extends its range into Middle Europe, specifically within Germany.
