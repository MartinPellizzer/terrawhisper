Aconitum carmichaelii · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (311 studies) · ★★★★★

Aconitum carmichaelii, scientifically known as Aconitum carmichaelii, is a potent medicinal herb widely used in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its warming properties, it treats pain and circulation issues. However, due to its high toxicity, it requires meticulous processing to ensure safety and efficacy in clinical applications.
