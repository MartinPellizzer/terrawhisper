Aconitum carmichaeli

Aconitum carmichaelii · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (311 studies) · ★★★★★

Aconitum carmichaelii, scientifically known as Aconitum carmichaelii, is a potent medicinal herb widely used in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its warming properties, it treats pain and circulation issues. However, due to its high toxicity, it requires meticulous processing to ensure safety and efficacy in clinical applications.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several distinct regions in Asia and Europe. It is also found throughout the Manchuria region and the northern reaches of Inner Mongolia. Beyond Asia, the species extends its range into Middle Europe, specifically within Germany. Plant Parts

This plant, Aconitum carmichaelii, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its underground structures. The most significant therapeutic component is found in the roots, which are often processed to reduce toxicity. These roots consist of a central tuberous body from which numerous smaller, fibrous rootlets extend into the soil. In addition to these primary roots, the plant develops lateral roots that help anchor it and absorb nutrients. Above the ground, the plant features various aerial parts, including the stems and leaves, which characterize its growth habit during the vegetative stage.
