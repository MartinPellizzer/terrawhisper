This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the vast territories of Siberia. It can be found growing in the mountainous regions of the Altai area. Additionally, its presence is documented across the Republic of Buryatia. The species also inhabits the eastern reaches of the region, specifically within Chita. Finally, its range extends through the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk oblasts.

Plant Parts

This plant, Artemisia gmeliinii var. gmelinii, is a medicinal herb characterized by its distinct botanical structures. The plant features numerous green leaves, with each individual leaf often displaying a fine texture typical of the Artemisia genus. These leaves are a primary source of the plant's therapeutic potency, as they contain concentrated essential oils that provide its characteristic aromatic properties. During its flowering stage, the plant produces inflorescences that serve as the reproductive centers. Additionally, the roots of the plant are an important component, often utilized in traditional applications to harness the full medicinal spectrum of the species.
