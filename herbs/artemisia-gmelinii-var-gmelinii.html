Belonging to the Asteraceae family, this plant is widely recognized in traditional medicine for its antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory potential. It serves as a vital resource in ethnobotanical studies for developing natural bioactive compounds.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further nested within the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Artemisia as the specific variety gmelinii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the vast territories of Siberia. It can be found growing in the mountainous regions of the Altai area. Additionally, its presence is documented across the Republic of Buryatia. The species also inhabits the eastern reaches of the region, specifically within Chita. Finally, its range extends through the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk oblasts.
