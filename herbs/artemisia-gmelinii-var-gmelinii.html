Artemisia gmelinii var. gmelinii

Artemisia gmelinii var. gmelinii, commonly known as Gmelin's wormwood, is a significant medicinal shrub native to various regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, offering vast potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Finally, it is identified under the genus Artemisia as the specific variety gmelinii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the vast territories of Siberia. It can be found growing in the mountainous regions of the Altai area. Additionally, its presence is documented across the Republic of Buryatia. The species also inhabits the eastern reaches of the region, specifically within Chita. Finally, its range extends through the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk oblasts.
