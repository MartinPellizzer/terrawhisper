Plains coreopsis, scientifically known as Coreopsis tinctoria, is a vibrant, sun-loving perennial native to North American prairies. Renowned for its striking golden blooms, this resilient plant is prized in traditional herbalism for its restorative properties and is frequently utilized as a natural, brilliant yellow dye in textile production.
