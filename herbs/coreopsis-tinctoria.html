Plains coreopsis, scientifically known as Coreopsis tinctoria, is a vibrant, sun-loving perennial native to North American prairies. Renowned for its striking golden blooms, this resilient plant is prized in traditional herbalism for its restorative properties and is frequently utilized as a natural, brilliant yellow dye in textile production.

This plant, commonly known as tansy mustard, is characterized by its distinct morphological features used in various medicinal applications. The most striking aspect of the plant is its vibrant flowers, which are presented in dense, yellow-colored capitulia. These composite heads are often harvested as flowering tops, a part of the plant that contains a concentrated amount of active compounds. Below these bright displays, the leaves are typically deeply divided or lobed, providing a lush green backdrop to the blooming structure. Each individual flower within the cluster contributes to the overall medicinal profile of the plant's aerial parts.
