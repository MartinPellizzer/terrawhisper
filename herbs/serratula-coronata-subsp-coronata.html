Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several regions across the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented within the territories of Germany and Switzerland. Moving toward the east, the species is also found in Southeastern Europe, specifically in Romania. Its range extends further into Eastern Europe, where it inhabits parts of Belarus. Finally, the distribution reaches into Central European Russia, completing its presence across these diverse landscapes.
