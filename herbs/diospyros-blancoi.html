Diospyros blancoi

Diospyros blancoi, commonly known as the white ebony, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments. Recent studies highlight its potential antioxidant and antimicrobial activities, making it a subject of growing scientific interest.
