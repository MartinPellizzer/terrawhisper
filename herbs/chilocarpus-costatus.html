Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China territory, it can be found growing in both Myanmar and Thailand. Its presence extends significantly into the Malesia region as well. Specifically, it is documented in the islands of Borneo and Sumatera. Additionally, the species is native to the Malay Peninsula.
