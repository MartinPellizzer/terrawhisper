Distribution

This plant is distributed across several key regions within the Caribbean and Northern South America. In the Caribbean, its range encompasses the Leeward Islands as well as the Windward Islands. It is also documented to grow within the nation of Trinidad and Tobago. Moving to the mainland, the species can be found throughout Guyana. Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Phyllanthus brasiliensis subsp. brasiliensis, is characterized by its specific morphological features used in traditional medicine. The most prominent components are its small, green leaves, which are arranged in a pinnate fashion along the stems to maximize sunlight absorption. When harvesting for medicinal purposes, practitioners often collect the entire aerial parts of the plant, including the stems and foliage, as these above-ground structures contain the concentrated bioactive compounds required for therapeutic use.
