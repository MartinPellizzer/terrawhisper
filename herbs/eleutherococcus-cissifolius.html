What's the taxonomical classification of Eleutherococcus cissifolius?

Eleutherococcus cissifolius belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Apiales. Further taxonomic refinement identifies the plant as a member of the family Araliaceae, specifically categorized under the genus Eleutherococcus, with the species designation cissifolius.
