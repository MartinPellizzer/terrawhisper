Aconitum ferox

Aconitum ferox, commonly known as Indian Aconite or Atis, is a potent medicinal plant native to the Himalayas. Renowned for its high alkaloid content, particularly aconitine, it is used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. While highly toxic if mishandled, it serves as a powerful remedy for neurological and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum ferox
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Aconitum ferox, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ranunculales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Aconitum.
