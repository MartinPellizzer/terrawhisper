Indian aconite

Indian aconite, scientifically known as Aconitum ferox, is a potent medicinal plant native to the Himalayas. Known for its extreme toxicity, it contains powerful alkaloids like aconitine. In traditional Ayurvedic medicine, carefully processed forms are utilized for their analgesic and anti-inflammatory properties, though it requires expert handling to avoid poisoning.
