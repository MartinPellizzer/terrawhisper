Pentaclethra macroloba

Pentaclethrha macroloba, commonly known as the "climbing tree," is a versatile medicinal plant widely utilized in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this species are employed to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and gastrointestinal issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
