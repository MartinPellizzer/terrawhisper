Ficus simplicissima

Ficus simplicissima, commonly known as the simplest fig, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its distinctive foliage, it possesses diverse bioactive properties. Often utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial potential, this species serves as a vital resource in ethnobotany and natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Ficus simplicissima
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and family Moraceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Ficus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh. Together, these locations highlight the plant's presence in both mainland China and parts of South Asia.
