Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh. Together, these locations highlight the plant's presence in both mainland China and parts of South Asia.
