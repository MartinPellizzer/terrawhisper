Camellia euphlebia

Camellia euphlebia, commonly known as the Veined Camellia, is a distinctive medicinal plant prized for its unique physiological properties. Renowned for its intricate leaf patterns, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. Traditional healers utilize its components to treat various ailments, highlighting its growing importance in modern botanical pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Camellia euphlebia
  • Family: Theaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Theaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Camellia, specifically as the species Camellia euphlebia.
