Papaver glaucum

Papaver glaucum, commonly known as the glaucous poppy, is a resilient perennial wildflower native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its striking blue-green foliage and delicate petals, this medicinal plant has historically been utilized in traditional practices to address various ailments, offering a fascinating glimpse into alpine botanical pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Papaver glaucum
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ranunculales, it is a member of the Papaveraceae family. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Papaver.
