Arabis paniculata

What's the taxonomical classification of Arabis paniculata?

Arabis paniculata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Brassicales. This plant is a member of the family Brassicaceae and is specifically identified within the genus Arabis, with its unique biological identity defined by the species paniculata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Arabis paniculata has a basal rosette of spatulate to lanceolate leaves that are typically entire or slightly dentate and a terminal panicle of small white flowers. The leaves are often hairy on both surfaces and possess long petioles that connect them to the central rosette. Its flowering stems are upright and branched, bearing numerous small four-petaled blossoms. The siliques, or seed pods, are slender and elongated, spreading outward from the stem as they mature. This structural arrangement allows the plant to remain low to the ground during its vegetative stage before elevating its reproductive organs.
