Rheum uninerve

What's the taxonomical classification of Rheum uninerve?

Rheum uninerve belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Polygonaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Rheum, and its unique species designation, uninerve.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rheum uninerve has a single-veined leaf structure characterized by a prominent midrib that lacks the secondary lateral venation common in other species of the genus. The leaves are typically lanceolate or oblong in shape and are often accompanied by a basal rosette that grows close to the ground. These leaves possess a somewhat leathery texture and may exhibit slightly wavy or entire margins depending on the specific environmental conditions. The plant also features a robust rhizome that serves as the primary underground storage organ for nutrients. Flowering stalks emerge from this central base, eventually producing small, inconspicuous flowers arranged in dense panicles.
