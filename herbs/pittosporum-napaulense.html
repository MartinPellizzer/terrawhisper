Pittosporum napaulense

Pittosporum napaulense, commonly known as Napaule, is a significant medicinal plant indigenous to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation and skin ailments. Exploring its phytochemical composition offers promising insights into developing new natural pharmacological treatments.

  • Scientific name: Pittosporum napaulense
  • Family: Pittosporaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Apiales and the family Pittosporaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pittosporum.
