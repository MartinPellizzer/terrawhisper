Musanga cecropioides

Musanga cecropioides, commonly known as the Musanga or African tree ivy, is a striking medicinal plant native to tropical African rainforests. Characterized by its massive, heart-shaped leaves, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and inflammatory issues, through its unique phytochemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Musanga cecropioides
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Africa
