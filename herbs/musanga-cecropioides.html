African Corkwood, scientifically known as Musanga cecropioides, is a striking, fast-growing evergreen tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its unique, large, heart-shaped leaves and distinctive bark, it plays a vital role in forest ecosystems, offering shade and habitat while holding significant medicinal potential in traditional healing practices.
