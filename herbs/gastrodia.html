Gastrodia

Gastrodia, commonly known as the jelly orchid, is a unique, holoparasitic medicinal plant native to East Asia. Lacking chlorophyll, it relies on symbiotic fungi for nutrients. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its tubers are prized for nourishing the body, enhancing cognitive function, and promoting overall vitality and longevity.

  • Scientific name: Gastrodia
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it falls into the family Orchidaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Gastrodia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across various regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Cameroon and Rwanda. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, the species is also found in South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of Malawi and Mozambique. Additionally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant extends into Zimbabwe.
