Scientifically known as Gastrodia, is a genus of holoparasitic orchids native to East Asia. Lacking chlorophyll, these plants derive nutrients from underground fungi. Highly valued in traditional medicine, especially Gastrodia elata, they are prized for enhancing cognitive function, boosting vitality, and supporting overall neurological health and longevity.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across various regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Cameroon and Rwanda. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, the species is also found in South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of Malawi and Mozambique. Additionally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant extends into Zimbabwe.
