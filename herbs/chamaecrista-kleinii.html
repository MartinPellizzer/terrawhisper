Chamaecrista kleinii

Chamaecrista kleinii, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, belongs to the Fabaceae family. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments. Scientific research continues to explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Chamaecrista kleinii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Chamaecrista.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in India and Sri Lanka. Additionally, its range extends into the specific environmental niche of the West Himalaya. Moving further east, the species is also documented within the Malesia region. Specifically, it has been identified as occurring on the island of Jawa.
