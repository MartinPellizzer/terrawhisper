Cottonweed, scientifically known as Achillea maritima, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal environments. Often found in sandy dunes, this plant features feathery foliage and clusters of white flowers. Traditionally valued for its medicinal properties, it contains bioactive compounds used to support skin health and digestive wellness.
