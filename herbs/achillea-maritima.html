Achillea maritima

Achillea maritima, commonly known as sea yarrow, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal regions. Thriving in sandy soils and salt spray, this plant features delicate, feathery foliage and clusters of small white flowers. It holds significant traditional value in folk medicine for its soothing and anti-inflammatory properties.

  • Scientific name: Achillea maritima
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it resides in the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Achillea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, it can be found inhabiting the coastal areas of Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species is also recorded in Belgium. Its presence extends into Southwestern Europe, specifically within the Baleares islands. Additionally, the plant is documented growing on the island of Corse.
