Sorbus aucuparia subsp. pohuashanensis

Sorbus aucuparia subsp. pohuashanensis, commonly known as a rare subspecies of the mountain ash, is a botanical treasure found in specific high-altitude regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant medicinal potential, providing traditional healing properties and serving as a vital subject for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Sorbus aucuparia subsp. pohuashanensis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Sorbus as the subspecies pohuashensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its distribution encompasses the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Moving into China, the species can be found spanning the territory of Inner Mongolia. Additionally, it is documented to grow throughout the Manchuria region. Together, these locations define the natural habitat for this particular subspecies.
