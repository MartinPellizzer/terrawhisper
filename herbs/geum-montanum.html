Geum montanum, scientifically known as Geum montanum, is a perennial herb native to mountainous regions of North America. Often called mountain avens, this resilient plant features delicate yellow flowers and aromatic foliage. Traditionally used in folk medicine, it holds significant botanical interest for its unique chemical properties and ecological importance.
