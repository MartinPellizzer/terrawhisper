Geum montanum

Geum montanum, commonly known as Water Avens, is a perennial herb native to mountainous regions of Europe and Asia. Characterized by its delicate yellow blossoms and serrated leaves, this resilient plant has long been valued in traditional herbalism for its astringent properties and its potential role in digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Geum montanum
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Geum.
