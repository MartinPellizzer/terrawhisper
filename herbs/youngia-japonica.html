Youngia japonica

Crepis japonica

Youngia japonica, commonly known as the Japanese hawkweed, is a resilient herbaceous plant belonging to the Asteraceae family. Native to East Asia, this small, leafy perennial features delicate yellow flowers. It is frequently studied in ethnobotany for its potential therapeutic properties and its role within various traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Youngia japonica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Youngia.

Common Names

This plant, Youngia japonica, is known by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In Japan, it is referred to as オニタビラコ (ja), while in Korea, it is commonly called 뽀리뱅이 (ko). In Chinese-speaking regions, it is identified as 黄鹌菜 (zh) and 黄鹌菜 (zh-cn), and in Vietnam, it is known as Hoàng đương Nhật Bản (vi).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide and scattered geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence can be noted specifically in Sicilia. Its range also extends to Macaronesia, where it is found on both the Canary Islands and Madeira. Moving toward the Southern Hemisphere, the species inhabits the KwaZulu-Natal region of Southern Africa. Finally, its distribution reaches the Western Indian Ocean, specifically within the Comoros.
