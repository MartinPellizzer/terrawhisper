Clematis gouriana, commonly known as a climbing medicinal vine, is a fascinating botanical species valued for its unique therapeutic potential. This plant, Clematis gouriana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Clematis.
