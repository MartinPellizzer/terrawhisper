Clematis gouriana

Clematis gouriana, commonly known as a climbing medicinal vine, is a fascinating botanical species valued for its unique therapeutic potential. Found in specific ecological niches, this plant is studied for its bioactive compounds. Understanding its traditional uses and chemical properties is essential for exploring its role in natural pharmacology.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Clematis gouriana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Clematis.
