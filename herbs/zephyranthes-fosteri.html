Zephyranthes fosteri

Zephyranthes fosteri, commonly known as Foster's rain lily, is a stunning perennial herb valued for its delicate, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant interest in ethnobotany. Its unique chemical properties offer potential therapeutic benefits, making it a subject of study in traditional medicinal practices.
