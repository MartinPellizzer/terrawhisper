Barleria grandiflora, scientifically known as Barleria grandiflora, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for developing modern pharmacological treatments.
