Thymus vulgaris

Thymus vulgaris, commonly known as thyme, is a perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, this medicinal plant is rich in essential oils like thymol. It offers potent antibacterial, antifungal, and antioxidant properties, making it a staple in traditional remedies for respiratory health.

  • Scientific name: Thymus vulgaris
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Thymus.
