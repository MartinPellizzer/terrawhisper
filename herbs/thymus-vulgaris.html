Thyme

Thymus vulgaris · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1604 studies) · ★★★★★

Thyme, scientifically known as Thymus vulgaris, is a perennial herb beloved for its aromatic leaves and culinary versatility. Beyond the kitchen, this medicinal powerhouse offers potent antibacterial, antifungal, and antioxidant properties. Often used in teas and essential oils, it serves as a natural remedy for respiratory issues and digestion.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Thymus vulgaris, is highly valued in herbal medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. Medicinally, the whole plants are often harvested to ensure maximum potency, though the most significant therapeutic compounds are concentrated within the aerial part of the organism. Specifically, the aromatic leaves serve as the primary source of its bioactive constituents. When processed, the plant yields potent essential oils that contain thymol, a powerful antimicrobial agent. These essential oils are frequently extracted from the vegetation to be used in various topical and aromatic applications.
