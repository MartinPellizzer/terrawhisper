Smilax havanensis

Smilax havenensis, commonly known as Sarsaparilla, is a climbing woody vine prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its restorative properties, the plant's roots are harvested to create decoctions used to treat skin conditions, inflammation, and blood purification. It remains a significant botanical resource in various ethnobotanical healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Smilax havanensis
  • Family: Smilacaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Smilax havanaensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Liliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Smilacaceae within the genus Smilax.
