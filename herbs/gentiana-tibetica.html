Gentiana tibetica

Gentiana tibetica, commonly known as Tibetan Gentian, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is highly valued for its bitter roots, which are utilized to treat digestive ailments, reduce inflammation, and support overall immune health.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana tibetica
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Gentianaceae and the genus Gentiana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, its presence is primarily documented across the vast plateau of Tibet. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found throughout the East Himalaya range. Furthermore, the species is also known to grow within the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Collectively, these locations define the specialized ecological niche where Gentiana tibetica thrives.
