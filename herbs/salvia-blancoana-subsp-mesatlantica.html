Salvia blancoana subsp. mesatlantica

Salvia blancoana subsp. mesatlantica, commonly known as a regional subspecies of the white sage lineage, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific Mexican habitats. Valued for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in traditional ethnobotanical practices for treating various ailments through its bioactive compounds.
