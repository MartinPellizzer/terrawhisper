Rytigynia senegalensis

Rytigynia senelegensis, commonly known as the African sandalwood, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical African regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for contemporary pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Rytigynia senegalensis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Rytigynia.
