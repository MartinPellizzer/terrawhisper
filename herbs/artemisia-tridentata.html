Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as big sagebrush, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties through the use of its aerial parts. These above-ground structures, which include the silver-gray leaves, woody stems, and flowering heads, contain the essential volatile oils and aromatic compounds necessary for therapeutic applications. When harvested, these aerial parts are often dried and used in infusions or topical preparations to harness the plant's potent chemical constituents.
