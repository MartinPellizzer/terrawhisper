Viburnum

Sweet viburnum

Viburnum, commonly known as wayfaring tree or highbush cranberry, is a versatile genus of flowering shrubs celebrated for their beauty and medicinal potential. Traditionally used in herbal remedies, various species offer anti-inflammatory and antispasmodic properties, providing valuable support for digestive health, menstrual relief, and overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Viburnum
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Europe
