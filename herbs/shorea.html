Shorea

Shorea, commonly known as Sal, is a vital genus of timber-producing trees native to tropical Southeast Asia. Renowned for its durable wood and resinous oleoresin, it plays a crucial role in traditional medicine. Its bark and leaves are utilized for treating various ailments, offering significant pharmacological benefits.

  • Scientific name: Shorea
  • Family: Dipterocarpaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Malvales and placed in the family Dipterocarpaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Shorea.
