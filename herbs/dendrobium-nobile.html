Noble Dendrobium

Dendrobium nobile · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (417 studies) · ★★★★★

Noble Dendrobium, scientifically known as Dendrobium nobile, is a highly prized orchid renowned in traditional Chinese medicine. Valued for its therapeutic properties, the pseudobulbs are used to nourish the yin, clear heat, and support respiratory health. This elegant plant bridges the gap between ornamental beauty and medicinal utility.
