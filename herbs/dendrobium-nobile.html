Noble Dendrobium

Dendrobium nobile · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (417 studies) · ★★★★★

Noble Dendrobium, scientifically known as Dendrobium nobile, is a highly prized orchid renowned in traditional Chinese medicine. Valued for its therapeutic properties, the pseudobulbs are used to nourish the yin, clear heat, and support respiratory health. This elegant plant bridges the gap between ornamental beauty and medicinal utility.

Plant Parts

This plant, Dendrobium nobile, is a highly valued medicinal orchid characterized by its unique morphological structures. The primary medicinal component is the stem, which functions as a specialized storage stem known as a pseudobulb, allowing the plant to retain moisture and nutrients. These elongated, succulent stems serve as the main reservoir for bioactive compounds used in traditional therapies. During its blooming period, the plant produces a beautiful flower that emerges from the nodes of the growth. Within the intricate anatomy of its reproductive organs, specialized structures such as nectar tubes can be found, which facilitate pollination. Together, these diverse plant parts contribute to the overall biological complexity and therapeutic potential of the species.
