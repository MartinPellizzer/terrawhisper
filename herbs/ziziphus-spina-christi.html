Christ's thorn, scientifically known as Ziziphus spina-christi, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to arid regions of Africa and the Middle East. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts—including leaves, bark, and roots—possess potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic potential for diverse ailments.
