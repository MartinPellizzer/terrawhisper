Aster likiangensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Aster likiangensis?

Aster likiangensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Asterales, the plant is categorized under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Aster, with its unique designation being the species likiangensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aster likiangensis has perennial, rhizomatous stems that produce lanceolate to ovate leaves with serrated margins and distinct venation. The foliage is typically arranged oppositely along the stem and exhibits a slightly pubescent texture. Its inflorescence consists of small, discoid flower heads that emerge in terminal clusters. Each flower head features numerous narrow ray florets surrounding a central disk of tubular florets. The overall plant structure remains compact, often forming dense mats through its spreading rhizome system.
