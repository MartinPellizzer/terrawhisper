Natal plum, scientifically known as Cariressa macrocarpa, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to South Africa. Valued for its glossy foliage and fragrant white flowers, it offers medicinal potential through its antioxidant-rich fruits. Traditionally, various parts of the plant have been utilized in folk remedies to support overall wellness.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends to East Tropical Africa, specifically located in Kenya. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout South Tropical Africa. This southern range includes significant populations in Mozambique, Zambia, and Zimbabwe.
