Carissa macrocarpa

Natal plum

Carissa macrocarpa, commonly known as Natal Plum, is a versatile evergreen shrub valued for its medicinal and ornamental properties. Beyond its striking white flowers and edible fruit, various cultures utilize its leaves and bark in traditional remedies to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions through herbal decoctions.

  • Scientific name: Carissa macrocarpa
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, it is further categorized into the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Carissa, specifically the species Carissa macrocarpa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends to East Tropical Africa, specifically located in Kenya. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout South Tropical Africa. This southern range includes significant populations in Mozambique, Zambia, and Zimbabwe.
