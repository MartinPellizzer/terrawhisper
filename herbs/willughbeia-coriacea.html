Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is documented in both Laos and Thailand. Moving into the Malesia zone, the species can be found spanning across the island of Borneo. It is also widely distributed throughout the islands of Jawa and the Malay Peninsula. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's significant range across diverse tropical habitats.
