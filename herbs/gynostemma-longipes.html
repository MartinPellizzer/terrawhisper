Gynostemma longipes

Gymnostemma longipes, commonly known as a relative of the famous Gymnema sylvestre, is a significant medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to support metabolic health, regulate blood sugar levels, and provide potent antioxidant benefits within various holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Gynostemma longipes
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cucurbitales, it is a member of the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Gymnostemma, specifically as the species Gymnostemma longipes.
