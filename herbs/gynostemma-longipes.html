Plant Parts

This plant, Gynostemma longipes, is a medicinal species characterized by its unique vegetative structure. In traditional applications, the whole plants are often utilized to harness the full spectrum of its therapeutic properties. The growth habit of the whole G. longipes plant is defined by its spreading nature, which relies on specialized structures like the stolon to navigate and colonize new areas. Beneath the surface, the plant utilizes rhizomes for nutrient storage and stability, while the more structural, woody elements can sometimes be compared to the strength found in rattan.
