Lactuca orientalis

Lactuca orientalis, commonly known as Oriental lettuce, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern regions. Renowned for its distinctive milky sap, this plant has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive ailments, and to promote overall sedative effects.

  • Scientific name: Lactuca orientalis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lactuca, specifically the species Lactuca orientalis.
