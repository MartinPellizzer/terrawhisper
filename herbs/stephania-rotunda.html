Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Stephania rotundata, is a medicinal climber that is primarily valued for its underground structures. The most significant parts utilized in traditional medicine are its large, fleshy tubers, which are often harvested for their therapeutic properties. In addition to these prominent tubers, the plant also develops a root system that supports its growth and nutrient uptake, though it is the specialized tuberous organs that serve as the main source of its bioactive compounds.
