Vaccinium pallidum

Hillside blueberry

Vaccinium pallidum, commonly known as wintergreen, is a perennial shrub native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it contains methyl salicylate, a natural compound used historically for its analgesic and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient plant thrives in acidic soils, offering valuable insights into traditional botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Vaccinium pallidum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Vaccinium pallidum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Vaccinium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Vaccinium pallidum, is referred to by several different common names depending on the language and region. In English, it is frequently called the hillside blueberry, the dryland blueberry, or the Blue Ridge blueberry. Similarly, in Chinese, it is known by the names 旱地蓝莓 and 银蓝越橘.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of North America. In Eastern Canada, its range extends into the province of Ontario. Moving into the North-Central United States, it can be found inhabiting Illinois. The species also grows within the territories of Missouri and Kansas. Finally, its presence continues further south into Oklahoma.
