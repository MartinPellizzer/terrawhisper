What's the taxonomical classification of Koenigia islandica?

Koenigia islandica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, eventually placing it under the order Caryophyllales. Within this order, the plant is a member of the family Polygonaceae. It is specifically categorized under the genus Koenigia, with its unique identity defined by the species epithet islandica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Koenigia islandica has a low-growing, perennial habit characterized by creeping rhizomes and small, opposite, lanceolate leaves that measure roughly 5 to 15 millimeters in length. The foliage is typically glabrous or slightly pubescent, featuring entire margins and acute tips. During the flowering stage, the plant produces inconspicuous, actinomorphic flowers arranged in small, terminal cymes. These tiny blooms lack true petals and instead consist of small, greenish sepals that surround the reproductive organs. The overall structure is adapted for survival in harsh, arctic or alpine environments where compact growth is essential.
