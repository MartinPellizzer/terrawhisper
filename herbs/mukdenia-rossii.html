Mukdenia rossii

Mukdenia rossii, commonly known as Mukdenia, is a perennial herb highly valued in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Its rhizomes contain bioactive compounds with potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and hemostatic properties. Frequently used to treat bleeding disorders and skin inflammations, this medicinal plant serves as a vital natural remedy in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Mukdenia rossii
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifragales, it is further categorized into the family Saxifragaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Mukdenia, which includes the species Mukdenia rossii.

This plant is primarily found within the specific regions of East Asia. In China, its natural habitat is concentrated in the Manchuria area. Furthermore, its geographical reach extends into the neighboring territory of Korea. These locations represent the core zones where the species can be discovered in the wild. Consequently, the distribution of Mukdenia rossii is closely tied to these specific East Asian landscapes.
