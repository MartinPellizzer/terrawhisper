Solanum villosum

Solanum villosum, commonly known as the yellow nightshade, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Widely distributed across tropical regions, it is prized in traditional medicine for its diverse pharmacological properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, making it a valuable resource for holistic healing practices.
