Rubia tibetica, scientifically known as Rubia tibetica, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional Tibetan medicine, its roots contain bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, promote blood circulation, and support detoxification, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural pharmacology.
