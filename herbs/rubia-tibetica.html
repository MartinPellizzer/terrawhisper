Rubia tibetica, scientifically known as Rubia tibetica, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional Tibetan medicine, its roots contain bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, promote blood circulation, and support detoxification, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural pharmacology.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Central and Western Asia. In Central Asia, its presence is specifically noted in Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan. Moving toward Western Asia, the species can be found within the borders of Afghanistan. The distribution also extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Additionally, it is documented to grow within the Xinjiang province of China.
