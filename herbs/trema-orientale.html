Trema orientale

Trema orientale, commonly known as the Oriental Trema, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Often found in tropical regions, it possesses significant therapeutic potential, particularly for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. Exploring its bioactive compounds offers promising insights into developing new natural pharmacological treatments.

  • Family: Cannabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Cannabaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Trema.
