Mandragora autumnalis, scientifically known as Mandragora autumnalis, is a perennial herb renowned for its mystical history and potent pharmacological properties. Native to the Mediterranean, this plant features distinctive fleshy roots and bell-shaped flowers. Traditionally used in ancient medicine, its alkaloids provide significant sedative and analgesic effects in herbalism.
