Mandragora autumnalis

Mandragora autumnalis, commonly known as autumn mandrake, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its distinctive tuberous roots, this plant holds significant historical importance in folklore and traditional medicine. It contains potent alkaloids, which have been studied for their sedative, analgesic, and hallucinogenic properties throughout history.

  • Scientific name: Mandragora autumnalis
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales. Finally, it is a member of the family Solanaceae and falls under the genus Mandragora.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the Mediterranean region. It can be found throughout Southwestern Europe, specifically within the territories of Spain and Portugal.
