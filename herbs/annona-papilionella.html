Annona papilionella

Annona papiillonella, commonly known as the butterfly custard apple, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, it possesses unique phytochemical properties used to treat various ailments. This study explores its therapeutic potential, focusing on its bioactive compounds and its role in natural pharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida.
