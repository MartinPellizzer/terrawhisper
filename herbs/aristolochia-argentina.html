Aristolochia argentina

Aristolochia argentina, commonly known as the Argentine birthwort, is a perennial climbing herb native to South American regions. Characterized by its distinctive heart-shaped leaves and tubular flowers, this species belongs to the Aristolochiaceae family. While historically studied for unique phytochemical properties, its use requires extreme caution due to toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia argentina
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Piperales and the family Aristolochiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Aristolochia.
