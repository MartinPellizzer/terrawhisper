Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the orchid tree, utilizes its distinctively shaped leaves for various medicinal purposes. The leaves, which are characterized by their unique two-lobed or butterfly-like appearance, are frequently harvested and used in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Due to their rich chemical composition, these leaves are often prepared as decoctions or poultices to harness their healing properties.
