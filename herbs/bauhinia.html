Bauhinia

Bauhinia, commonly known as the orchid tree, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated in traditional healing. Renowned for its striking, butterfly-shaped leaves, various species offer potent therapeutic properties. Used to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, Bauhinia serves as a vital natural resource in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Bauhinia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Bauhinia.

Its range extends into Northern Africa, specifically throughout the territory of Algeria. The species is also found in the Macaronesian islands, notably on Madeira. Finally, it occupies parts of West Tropical Africa, including the nations of Benin and Burkina.
