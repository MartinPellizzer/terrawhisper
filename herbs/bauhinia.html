Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple continents and climatic zones. In Southwestern Europe, its presence can be identified within the borders of Spain. Its range extends into Northern Africa, specifically throughout the territory of Algeria. The species is also found in the Macaronesian islands, notably on Madeira. Finally, it occupies parts of West Tropical Africa, including the nations of Benin and Burkina.
