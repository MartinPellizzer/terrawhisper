Aloe perryi

Aloe perryi, commonly known as a succulent medicinal plant, is a striking species native to specific African regions. Renowned for its fleshy, moisture-retaining leaves, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional healing. This hardy aloe is valued for its soothing properties, offering therapeutic potential in various dermatological applications.

  • Scientific name: Aloe perryi
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asparagales, it is classified within the family Asphodelaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Aloe.

Distribution

This plant is characterized by a highly restricted and specific geographical range. It is native to the Northeast Tropical Africa region, specifically localized to the Socotra archipelago. Within this unique island environment, the species has adapted to the distinct climatic conditions provided by the area. Because it is endemic to Socotra, its natural occurrence is limited to this single geographic location. Consequently, its entire global distribution remains confined to this specific part of the Indian Ocean.
