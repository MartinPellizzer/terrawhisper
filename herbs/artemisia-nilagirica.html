Artemisia nilagirica

Artemisia nilagirica, commonly known as Nilgiri wormwood, is a distinctive aromatic shrub native to the high-altitude grasslands of the Western Ghats. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, this medicinal herb contains bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to treat various ailments, offering significant pharmacological interest for modern scientific research.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia nilagirica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Asterales and is a member of the family Asteraceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with the genus Artemisia, identifying it specifically as Artemisia nilagirica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across parts of East Asia and the Indian subcontinent. In China, its distribution is notably found within the South-Central regions as well as the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian subcontinent, the species can be located in the state of Assam. Furthermore, it is widely recorded throughout the country of India. Finally, its habitat extends to the specific ecological zones of the West Himalaya.
