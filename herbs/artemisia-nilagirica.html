Nilgri wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia nilagirica, is an aromatic perennial herb endemic to the Western Ghats of India. Renowned for its distinctive fragrance and potent bioactive compounds, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
