Cinnamomum osmophloeum

Cinnamomum osmophloeum, commonly known as the fragrant cinnamon tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Valued for its aromatic bark and essential oils, it possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditional medicine utilizes its bioactive compounds to treat various ailments, highlighting its immense therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Cinnamomum osmophloeum
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Laurales, and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cinnamomum, specifically the species Cinnamomum osmophloeum.
