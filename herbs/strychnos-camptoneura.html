Strychnos camptoneura

Strychnos camptoneura, commonly known as a member of the Loganiaceae family, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its complex alkaloids, this species is studied for its potent pharmacological properties. Researchers explore its bioactive compounds for potential applications in treating neurological conditions and managing pain.

  • Scientific name: Strychnos camptoneura
  • Family: Loganiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Gentianaales and the family Loganiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Strychnos.

Common Names

This plant, Strychnos camptoneura, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is referred to as the Camptoneura Strychnos, while in Malay, it is commonly called pokok kutuk. In Indonesian, the plant is known as kutuk, and in the local language of Tagalog, it is referred to as kutyak.
