Scientifically known as Glycosmis, is a genus of flowering plants in the Rutaceae family. Widely distributed across tropical Asia, these shrubs are prized in traditional medicine for their diverse therapeutic properties. Various species are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections through their bioactive compounds.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is categorized under the genus Glycosmis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across multiple regions in Asia and Africa. In the continent of Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Angola in South Tropical Africa. Within China, the species occupies a wide variety of territories, including the South-Central and Southeast regions. Furthermore, it can be found in the tropical environment of Hainan and the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. These scattered locations demonstrate the plant's ability to adapt to different climatic zones.
