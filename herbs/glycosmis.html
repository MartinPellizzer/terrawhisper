Glycosmis

Glycosmis, commonly known as various local names depending on the species, belongs to the Rutaceae family. These evergreen shrubs or small trees are highly valued in traditional medicine. They contain diverse bioactive compounds, including alkaloids, which offer significant therapeutic potential for treating various ailments and promoting overall wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is categorized under the genus Glycosmis.
