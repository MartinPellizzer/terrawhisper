Sideritis raeseri

Sideritis raeseri, commonly known as Sideritis, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and common colds, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Sideritis raeseri
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
