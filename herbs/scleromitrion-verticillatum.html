Scleromitrion verticillatum

Scleromitrion verticillatum, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, holds profound importance in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacological applications.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
