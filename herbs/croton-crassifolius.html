Croton crassifolius

Croton crassifolius, commonly known as the thick-leaved croton, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, its leaves possess significant bioactive properties. Often utilized for their anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, this plant serves as a vital natural resource for treating various skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Croton crassifolius
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Croton, and its specific species name, Croton crassifolius.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across parts of Asia. Within China, it can be found in the Hainan province as well as throughout China Southeast. Its range extends into the Indo-China region, where it is naturally occurring in Laos. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Myanmar. Finally, the plant is also distributed across Thailand, completing its regional presence.
