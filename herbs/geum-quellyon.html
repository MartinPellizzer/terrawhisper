Geum quellyon

Geum quellyon, commonly known as the meadow burnet, is a resilient perennial herb native to various temperate regions. Characterized by its delicate yellow blossoms and serrated foliage, this plant holds significant value in traditional herbalism. It is often studied for its unique phytochemical properties and potential medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Geum quellyon
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Geum.
