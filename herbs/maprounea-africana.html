Maprounea africana

Maprounea africana, commonly known as the African Maprounea, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Widely utilized in traditional medicine, its various parts—including leaves, bark, and seeds—possess potent bioactive properties. It is frequently employed to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and various gastrointestinal disorders.

  • Scientific name: Maprounea africana
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and is identified by the genus Maprounea.
