Distribution

This plant is distributed across several distinct regions within the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Benin and Nigeria. Moving into the West-Central Tropical Africa zone, the species can be found in the Central African Republic. It also inhabits the territory of Cameroon within this same central region. Finally, its geographical range extends further into the Congo.
