Gentiana choanantha subsp. choanantha

Gentiana choanantha subsp. choanantha, commonly known as the Choanata Gentian, is a rare perennial herb native to specific high-altitude regions. Renowned for its striking blue blooms, this medicinal plant contains bitter glycosides used traditionally to stimulate digestion, support liver function, and alleviate various gastrointestinal discomforts through its potent properties.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana choanantha subsp. choanantha
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
