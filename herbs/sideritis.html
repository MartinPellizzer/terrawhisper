Sideritis

Sideritis, commonly known as Yellow Sideritis or Ironwort, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, this aromatic herb is frequently used in traditional medicine to support respiratory health, soothe digestive issues, and strengthen the body's natural immune defenses.

  • Scientific name: Sideritis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Sideritis.

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within Middle Europe. It can be found growing in the mountainous and hilly terrains of Austria.
