Mountain tea, scientifically known as Sideritis, is a revered herbal remedy native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this aromatic plant is traditionally brewed into a soothing infusion. It is widely used to support respiratory health, boost immunity, and alleviate digestive discomfort naturally.
