Eugenia stipitata

Eugenia stipitata, commonly known as the Surinam cherry, is a tropical evergreen shrub prized for its edible, fleshy fruits. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. It is traditionally utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, serving as a valuable resource in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Eugenia stipitata
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Eugenia.
