Pueraria montana

Pueraria montana, commonly known as Kudzu, is a perennial climbing vine native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to manage menopause symptoms, support liver health, and reduce alcohol cravings. This potent botanical offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Pueraria montana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Pueraria and the species montana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is noted in both Italy and the Northwestern Balkan Peninsula. Moving toward Eastern Europe, the species can be found within the territories of Crimea and Ukraine. Additionally, its range extends to the Atlantic region, specifically inhabiting the Azores. This wide-ranging dispersal highlights the plant's ability to thrive in various European and Macaronesian environments.
