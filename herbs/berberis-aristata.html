Berberis aristata

Berberis aristata, commonly known as Indian Barberry or Daruharidra, is a perennial shrub native to the Himalayas. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, its bright yellow stems and roots contain berberine, a potent alkaloid. This medicinal plant is widely utilized for its powerful antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and blood-purifying therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Berberis aristata
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Berberis.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Moving to the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found throughout the diverse landscapes of India and Assam. The species also thrives in the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya. Furthermore, its geographical range extends significantly into the mountainous regions of Nepal.
