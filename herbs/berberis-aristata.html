Indian Barberry

Berberis aristata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (106 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Indian Barberry, scientifically known as Berberis aristata, is a deciduous shrub native to the Himalayas. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, its roots and bark contain berberine, a potent alkaloid. It is widely utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties, serving as a vital natural remedy for various metabolic ailments.
