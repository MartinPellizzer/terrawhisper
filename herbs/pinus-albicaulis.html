Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the mountainous regions of Western Canada and the Northwestern United States. In Canada, its range extends through the provinces of Alberta and British Columbia. Moving south into the United States, it can be found within the state of Idaho. The species also inhabits the rugged landscapes of Montana. Additionally, its geographical presence continues into the state of Oregon.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Pinus albicaulis or the whitebark pine, utilizes its specialized leaf structure to thrive in harsh, high-altitude environments. Unlike many broad-leaved plants, the leaf of this species is modified into a needle-like shape, which is essential for minimizing water loss through transpiration in dry, windy alpine conditions. These slender, greenish needles are typically arranged in bundles and are highly efficient at capturing sunlight for photosynthesis while remaining resilient against heavy snow and freezing temperatures.
