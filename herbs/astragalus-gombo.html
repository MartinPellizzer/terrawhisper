Astragalus gombo

Astragalus gombo, commonly known as Astragalus, is a revered medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent immune-boosting properties, this plant serves as a vital adaptogen, helping the body manage stress, enhance vitality, and support overall longevity through its rich array of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus gombo
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
