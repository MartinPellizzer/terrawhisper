Garcinia indica

Garcinia indica, commonly known as Kokum, is a tropical evergreen tree native to the Western Ghats of India. Renowned for its deep red pericarp, it is widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine and culinary practices for its antioxidant properties, aiding digestion and managing cholesterol through its bioactive hydroxycitric acid.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia indica
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Clusiaceae and is identified by the genus Garcinia.
