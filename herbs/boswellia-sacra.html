Boswellia sacra

Boswellia sacrra, commonly known as sacred frankincense, is a majestic tree native to the arid landscapes of Oman. Renowned for producing aromatic resin, it has been cherished for centuries in traditional medicine. This potent botanical is highly valued for its powerful anti-inflammatory properties and its ability to support holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Boswellia sacra
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, order Sapindales, and family Burseraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Boswellia, specifically the species Boswellia sacra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution spanning across Northeast Tropical Africa and the Arabian Peninsula. In the African region, its presence is notably documented within Somalia. Moving into the Arabian Peninsula, it can be found throughout the various Gulf States. The species also maintains a significant range within the country of Oman. Finally, its geographical reach extends into the territory of Yemen.
