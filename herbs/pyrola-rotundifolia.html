Pyrola rotundifolia

Pyrola rotundifolia, commonly known as round-leaved wintergreen, is a perennial herb native to boreal forests and subarctic regions. Distinguished by its distinctive circular leaves and delicate bell-shaped flowers, this plant thrives in acidic, mossy habitats. It plays a vital role in forest ecosystems through its unique symbiotic relationships.

  • Scientific name: Pyrola rotundifolia
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe
