What's the taxonomical classification of Glochidion sphaerogynum?

Glochidion sphaerogynum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Malpighiales, the plant is further organized into the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Glochidion, and its unique species designation, sphaerogynum.
