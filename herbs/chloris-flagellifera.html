Chloris flagellifera

Wire grass

Chloris flagelliera, commonly known as whipgrass, is a resilient herbaceous plant valued in traditional medicine. Known for its unique botanical characteristics, it is frequently utilized across various cultures for its potential therapeutic properties. This introduction explores its medicinal applications, traditional uses, and the phytochemical compounds that define its healing potential.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales and the family Poaceae, it is classified under the genus Chloris. Consequently, its specific scientific name is Chloris flagellifera.
