Hebecarpa ovata

Hebecaarpia ovata, commonly known as the False Indian Hawthorn, is a perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. It is frequently utilized in herbal formulations to treat various ailments and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Hebecarpa ovata
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Fabales and the family Polygalaeaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Hebecaarpia, and species name, ovata.

Common Names

This plant, Hebecaarpia ovata, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Indian bellflower, while in Hindi, it is known as Kalmeghari. In Bengali, it is identified as Kalmegh, and in Sanskrit, it is traditionally called Kalmegh.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions in North and Central America. In Mexico, it can be found spanning from the Gulf region to the Southwest. Additionally, its presence extends into the Southeast portion of Mexico. Moving into Central America, the species is documented within the borders of El Salvador. Finally, its range continues southward into the country of Costa Rica.
