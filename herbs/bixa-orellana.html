Achiote, scientifically known as Bixa orellana, is a versatile tropical shrub celebrated for its vibrant, pigment-rich seeds. Beyond its use as a natural food coloring, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, offering antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties that support skin health and overall wellness.
