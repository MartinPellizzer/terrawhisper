Aloe nyeriensis

Aloe nyeriensis, commonly known as the Nyeri Aloe, is a striking succulent native to the Kenyan highlands. Distinguished by its large, fleshy leaves and vibrant flowering spikes, this species is prized both for its ornamental beauty and its traditional medicinal applications in treating various skin ailments and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Aloe nyeriensis
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is situated in the family Asphodelaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Aloe.
