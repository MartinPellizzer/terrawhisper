Astragalus fasciculifolius

Astragalus fasciolifolius, commonly known as the clustered-leaf milkvetch, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its medicinal potential, this plant contains bioactive compounds utilized in traditional practices to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus fasciculifolius
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by its genus, Astragalus, with the species name fasciifolius.
