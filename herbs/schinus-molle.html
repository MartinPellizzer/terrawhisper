Peruvian pepper

Schinus molle · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (397 studies) · ★★★★★

Peruvian pepper, scientifically known as Schinus molle, is a versatile evergreen tree native to the Andes. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and pink peppercorns, it offers significant medicinal value. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, this resilient plant remains a vital resource in ethnobotany.
