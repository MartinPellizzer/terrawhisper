Schinus molle

Schinus molle, commonly known as the Peruvian peppertree, is a versatile evergreen native to South America. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and distinctive red berries, this resilient plant offers significant medicinal value. It is traditionally utilized for its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties in various holistic healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Schinus molle
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Anacardiaceae and the genus Schinus.
