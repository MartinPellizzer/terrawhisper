Piper carpunya

Piper carpunya, commonly known as Long Pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its pungent, warming properties, it is frequently used to stimulate digestion, alleviate respiratory ailments, and boost metabolic health. This potent botanical serves as a vital ingredient in many therapeutic formulations.

  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
