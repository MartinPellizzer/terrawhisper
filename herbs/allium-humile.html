Allium humile

Allium humile, commonly known as the dwarf onion, is a resilient perennial herb native to various rocky terrains. Renowned for its compact stature and delicate white blooms, this medicinal plant offers unique phytochemical properties. It is traditionally valued in folk medicine for its antioxidant potential and various therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Allium humile
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Allium humile, is classified within the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It belongs to the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaryllidaceae and the genus Allium.
