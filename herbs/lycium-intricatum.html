Lycium intricatum

Lycium intricatum, commonly known as wolfberry or desert wolfberry, is a resilient shrub native to arid regions. Highly valued in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant properties. This hardy plant is frequently utilized to support immune health, combat oxidative stress, and provide therapeutic benefits in various holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Lycium intricatum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lycium.
