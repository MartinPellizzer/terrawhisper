Urtica pilulifera

Urtica pilulifera, commonly known as the stinging nettle, is a herbaceous perennial valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory and nutrient-rich profile, this plant plays a vital role in supporting kidney health and skin vitality through its potent bioactive compounds and minerals.

  • Scientific name: Urtica pilulifera
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Urticaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Urtica.
