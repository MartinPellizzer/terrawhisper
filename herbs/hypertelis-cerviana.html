Hypertelis cerviana

Hypertelis cerviana, commonly known as the Velvet Fern, is a rare medicinal plant prized for its unique bioactive compounds. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, this botanical species offers significant therapeutic potential. Understanding its chemical properties is essential for exploring its role in modern natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Hypertelis cerviana
  • Family: Molluginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Hypertelis cerviana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is classified under the family Molluginaceae and the genus Hypertelis.
