Sugarcane, scientifically known as Saccharum, is more than just a sweet dietary staple. Beyond its role in sugar production, this versatile perennial grass possesses significant medicinal properties. Traditionally used in various cultures, its extracts offer antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse presence across several Mediterranean regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in both Spain and Sardegna. Its range extends into Southeastern Europe, where it is widely distributed throughout Italy. The plant is also commonly located in the island of Kriti. Consequently, its geographical footprint covers multiple key areas of the southern European coastline.
