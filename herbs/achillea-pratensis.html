Achillea pratensis

Achillea pratensis, commonly known as meadowland yarrow, is a resilient perennial herb distinguished by its clusters of yellow flower heads. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, this plant possesses anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties. It thrives in moist meadows, serving as both a vital pollinator resource and a botanical healer.

  • Scientific name: Achillea pratensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Achillea.
