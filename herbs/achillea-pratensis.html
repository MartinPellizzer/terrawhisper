Achillea pratensis, scientifically known as Achillea pratensis, is a perennial herb renowned for its vibrant yellow flower clusters. Commonly called Meadow Tansy, this resilient plant has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries to treat digestive issues, skin inflammations, and respiratory ailments, offering a rich profile of bioactive compounds.
