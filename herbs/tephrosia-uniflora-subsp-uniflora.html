Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented within the countries of Algeria and Egypt. Moving toward the Atlantic, the subspecies can be found in the Macaronesian archipelago of Cape Verde. Its range extends further into West Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Burkina. Additionally, the plant is recorded as being native to the coastal and inland regions of Ghana.
