Brucea

Brucea, commonly known as Brucea javanica or the Brucea plant, is a medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains potent bioactive alkaloids used to treat various ailments, including intestinal parasites, malaria, and skin infections. Its unique pharmacological properties continue to inspire modern scientific research.

  • Scientific name: Brucea
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida.
