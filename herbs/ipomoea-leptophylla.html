Bush moonflower, scientifically known as Ipomoea leptophylla, is a resilient, woody shrub prized for its delicate, trumpet-shaped blooms. Native to arid regions, this plant offers significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in various medicinal practices, it is studied for its potential bioactive properties and its ability to thrive in harsh environments.
