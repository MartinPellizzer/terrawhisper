Ipomoea leptophylla

Ipomoea leptophylla, commonly known as the slender morning glory, is a resilient medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Often found in arid regions, this species is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address various ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern research into its bioactive natural compounds.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea leptophylla
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Solanales and placed in the family Convolvulaceae. It is further identified by the genus Ipomoea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the United States. In the Northwestern U.S.A., it can be found growing in the states of Colorado, Montana, and Wyoming. Moving toward the North-Central U.S.A., the species also inhabits the plains of Kansas. Additionally, its range extends into the state of Nebraska within the North-Central region. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Ipomoea leptophylla.
