Tephrosia candida

White hoary pea

Tephrosia candida, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in various traditional practices, is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, this plant is utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering potential pharmacological benefits through its unique chemical compounds and bioactive constituents.

  • Scientific name: Tephrosia candida
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is situated in the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Tephrosia, with the species being Tephrosia candida.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Tephrosia candida, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In English, it is called the White Hoary Pea, while in Bengali, it is known as বাঙ্গারা. Across different Chinese linguistic contexts, the names vary slightly: it is called 白花鐵富豆 in both Simplified and Traditional Chinese (zh and zh-tw), whereas in Mainland China (zh-cn), it is specifically referred to as 白灰毛豆.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several key regions in the African continent. Within West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Ghana and Sierra Leone. Its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is present in Cameroon and the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Additionally, the species is documented within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. These locations highlight its presence across both West and West-Central tropical zones.
