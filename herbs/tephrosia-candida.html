Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several key regions in the African continent. Within West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Ghana and Sierra Leone. Its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is present in Cameroon and the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Additionally, the species is documented within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. These locations highlight its presence across both West and West-Central tropical zones.
