Koompassia excelsa

Koompassia excelsa, commonly known as the Golden Canopy, is a revered medicinal plant celebrated for its potent healing properties. Found in lush tropical rainforests, its bark and leaves contain unique bioactive compounds used traditionally to reduce inflammation, support immune function, and promote overall vitality through natural herbal remedies.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
