Bromus inermis

Smooth brome

Bromus inermis, commonly known as smooth brome, is a perennial cool-season grass highly valued for its resilience and productivity. While primarily utilized in forage production and land reclamation, its nutritional properties and ability to thrive in diverse environments make it a significant species in managing sustainable grassland ecosystems worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Bromus inermis
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Poales, it is further categorized under the family Poaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Bromus.
