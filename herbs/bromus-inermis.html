Smooth Brome, scientifically known as Bromus inernis, is a perennial bunchgrass native to North American prairies. While primarily valued for soil stabilization and forage, it possesses traditional medicinal significance. Used in various ecological contexts, this hardy grass plays a vital role in maintaining biodiversity and supporting medicinal plant habitats.
