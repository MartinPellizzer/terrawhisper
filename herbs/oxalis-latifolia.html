Garden pink-sorrel, scientifically known as Oxalis latifolia, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for its delicate, clover-like foliage and vibrant blooms. Renowned for its tangy, citrus-like flavor, it is used both culinarily and medicinally. This resilient plant thrives in various conditions, offering both aesthetic beauty and therapeutic potential.

This plant, known as Oxalis latifolia, utilizes its distinct leaves as a primary component for medicinal applications. These leaves are typically characterized by their trifoliate structure, resembling small clovers, which serve as the main site for the accumulation of bioactive compounds. In various traditional practices, the leaves are harvested and prepared to harness their specific therapeutic properties, making them the most significant part of the plant for medicinal use.
