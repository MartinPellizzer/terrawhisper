Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Annona muricata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Magnoliaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Annonaceae and falls under the genus Annona.

Distribution

This plant, known as Annona muricata, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented in the nation of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species can also be found spanning across Gambia and Guinea-Bissau. Furthermore, it is distributed throughout the territory of Guinea. Finally, its range extends northward to include Mauritania.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as soursop, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties. The large, dark green leaves are frequently prepared as infusions or poultices to treat inflammation and infections, with each individual leaf containing potent bioactive compounds. The fleshy, heart-shaped fruits are widely consumed for their nutritional value and antioxidant effects, while the seeds are often processed for their specific antimicrobial qualities, despite requiring careful handling. Additionally, the woody stems and bark are sometimes incorporated into traditional remedies to address various ailments, demonstrating that almost every part of Annona muricata serves a purpose in herbal medicine.
