Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution stretching across parts of North and Central America. In Mexico, its presence is noted in both the Southwest and Southeast regions of the country. Moving south into Central America, the species can be found within the borders of Belize. Its range further extends to include the territory of El Salvador. Additionally, the plant is documented to grow within the tropical landscapes of Costa Rica.

Plant Parts

This plant, Strychnus orbicularis, is characterized by its distinct foliage, where the leaf serves as a primary component for both its biological function and its traditional medicinal applications. The leaves are typically arranged in an opposite pattern along the stem, providing a dense green canopy that facilitates photosynthesis. In various ethnobotanical practices, the leaf is often the specific part harvested and processed to extract bioactive compounds used in folk remedies.
