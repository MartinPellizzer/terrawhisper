Asteriscus graveolens, scientifically known as Asteriscus graveolens, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments, skin inflammations, and respiratory issues, offering a natural wealth of bioactive compounds for healing.
