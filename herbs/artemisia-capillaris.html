Artemisia capillaris

Artemisia capillaris, commonly known as Capillary Wormwood, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly in Korea, it is valued for its therapeutic properties. This plant is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant bioactive compounds for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia capillaris
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Artemisia. Specifically, it is identified by the scientific name Artemisia capillaris.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several diverse regions across Asia. In the northern reaches, its presence is noted in the Irkutsk region of Siberia. Moving toward the eastern coast, it can be found within the Primorye territory of the Russian Far East. Its range extends into Western Asia, specifically documented in Afghanistan. Finally, the species is located in China, specifically throughout the South-Central areas and on the island of Hainan.
