Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the southern portion of the African continent. It can be found growing throughout the diverse landscapes of the Cape Provinces in Southern Africa. The species also inhabits the mountainous terrain of Lesotho. Additionally, its range extends into the subtropical areas of KwaZulu-Natal and the central plains of the Free State. Finally, the geographical presence of this medicinal plant includes the territory of Esswatini.
