Thymus pallescens

Thymus pallescens, commonly known as pale thyme, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its delicate foliage and subtle fragrance, this medicinal plant offers therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health and provide soothing antimicrobial properties in wellness practices.
