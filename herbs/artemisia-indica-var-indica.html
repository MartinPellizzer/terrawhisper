Plant Parts

This plant, Artemisia indica var. indica, possesses various anatomical structures that contribute to its medicinal properties, though it is important to note that the term "smoke" does not refer to a biological component of the plant itself. Instead, the plant consists of a woody base, aromatic leaves with distinctive glandular hairs, and small flowering heads that contain the essential oils used in traditional remedies. In certain cultural practices, these dried aerial parts may be burned, producing a medicinal smoke that is inhaled or used for ritual purification, allowing the plant's volatile compounds to be released into the air.
