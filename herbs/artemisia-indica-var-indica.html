This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Artemisia. Specifically, this medicinal variety is identified as Artemisia indica var. indica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of China. It can be found growing in the South-Central part of the country as well as on the island of Hainan. Moving toward the northern territories, the species is also present in Inner Mongolia. Additionally, its range extends into the Manchuria region. Finally, the plant is recorded in the North-Central areas of China.
