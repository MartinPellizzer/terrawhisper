Astragalus polycladus

What's the taxonomical classification of Astragalus polycladus?

Astragalus polycladus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It falls under the order Fabales and is a member of the family Fabaceae. Within this family, the plant is categorized under the genus Astragalus, and its specific designation is given by the species name polycladus.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Astragalus polycladus has a perennial growth habit characterized by a woody taproot, trifoliate leaves with elliptical leaflets, and dense, cylindrical racemes of pea-like flowers. The leaves typically feature three distinct leaflets that are arranged along a central petiole to maximize light absorption. Its inflorescence consists of numerous small, papilionaceous blossoms that vary in color from white to pale pink or purple. The stems are often branched and covered in fine, matted hairs that provide protection against environmental stressors. These morphological adaptations allow the plant to thrive in its specific rocky or montane habitats.
