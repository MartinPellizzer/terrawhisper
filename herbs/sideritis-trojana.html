Sideritis trojana

Sideritis trojana, commonly known as Turkish Sideritis, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Valued in traditional herbal medicine, this aromatic plant is renowned for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, frequently utilized in soothing herbal teas to support respiratory health and overall wellness.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
