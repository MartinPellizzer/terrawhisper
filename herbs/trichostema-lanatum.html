Trichostema lanatum

Trichostema lanatum, commonly known as Cotton Bush or Woolly Trichosteuma, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized in topical applications to treat skin ailments, promote wound healing, and combat various parasitic infections naturally.

  • Scientific name: Trichostema lanatum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Trichostema.
