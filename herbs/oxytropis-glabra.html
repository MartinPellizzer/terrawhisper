Oxytropis glabra

Oxytropis glabra, commonly known as smooth oxytropic, is a perennial herb belonging to the Fabaceae family. Native to arid regions, this plant is distinguished by its smooth, hairless stems and delicate foliage. It holds significant ethnobotanical interest, often studied for its unique chemical properties and potential medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Oxytropis glabra
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Oxytropis, specifically as the species Oxytropis glabra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across the vast territories of Eastern Europe and Asia. Within Eastern Europe, it can be found growing throughout East European Russia. Its presence extends significantly into the Siberian region, where it inhabits several distinct areas. Specifically, it is documented in the Altay, Buryatiya, and Chitа regions of Siberia. Additionally, the species is known to thrive in the Irkutsk area, further marking its range across these landscapes.
