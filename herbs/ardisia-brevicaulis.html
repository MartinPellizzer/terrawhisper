Ardisia brevicaulis

What's the taxonomical classification of Ardisia brevicaulis?

Ardisia brevicaulis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, falling within the genus Ardisia, and is specifically identified by its species name, brevicaulis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ardisia brevicaulis has an erect woody habit characterized by short internodes, opposite lanceolate leaves with entire margins, and small axillary flowers. The foliage is typically dark green and glossy on the upper surface, while the undersides may exhibit a slightly different texture. Its stems are relatively stout for its size, supporting a compact branching pattern. The inflorescences emerge from the leaf axils, leading to small fleshy fruits that aid in seed dispersal. These morphological traits distinguish it from other species within the Rubiaceae family.

This plant Ardisia brevicaulis is utilized medicinally through the application of its leaves and roots. Similarly, the roots contain specific chemical compounds that are extracted for therapeutic purposes. These botanical components are frequently integrated into traditional remedies to address different health concerns. Practitioners rely on these specific parts to ensure the desired medicinal potency is achieved.

According to a study published by "Zhong yao cai = Zhongyaocai = Journal of Chinese medicinal materials", the leaves and roots of Ardisia brevicaulis contain different volatile oil constituents that should be utilized differently in clinical application. The leaves contain 38 identified compounds which make up 65.952% of the total essential oil. Main constituents of the leaves include Palmitic acid (43.329%), Phytol (3.142%), and Fitone (2.430%). The roots contain 46 identified compounds representing 54.890% of the total essential oil. Major constituents of the roots consist of gamma-Muurolene (14.227%), Caryophyllene (11.592%), cis-alpha-Bisabolene (5.222%), and Calamenene (2.913%).
