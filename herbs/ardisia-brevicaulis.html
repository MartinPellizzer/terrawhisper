Ardisia brevicaulis

Ardisia brevicaulis, commonly known as a small evergreen shrub, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical forest understories. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses diverse bioactive properties, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. Research continues to explore its potential pharmacological applications and its role in natural healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Ardisia brevicaulis
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ardisia brevicaulis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Primulaceae and falls under the genus Ardisia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of East and Southeast Asia.
