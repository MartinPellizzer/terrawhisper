Rheum turkesstanicum, scientifically known as Rheum turkesstanicum, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Central Asia. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, its roots are rich in anthraquinone glycosides, making it a vital component in traditional remedies for digestive health and various inflammatory conditions.
