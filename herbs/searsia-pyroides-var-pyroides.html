Searsia pyroides var. pyroides

Searsia pyroides var. pyroides, commonly known as the Karoo Anacotium, is a resilient, small-leaved shrub native to Southern Africa. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses diverse therapeutic properties. This hardy species is often utilized to treat various ailments, showcasing the profound medicinal importance of indigenous botanical biodiversity.
