Himalayan Fritillary

Scientific literature: Moderate (100 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Himalayan Friillary, scientifically known as Fritillaria cirrhosa, is a precious medicinal herb native to the high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly in Tibet and China, its bulbs are highly valued for treating respiratory ailments, coughs, and phlegm, serving as a vital component in many healing formulas.
