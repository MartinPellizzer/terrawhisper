Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across the rugged terrains of Northern Asia. Within the vast region of Siberia, it can be found growing in Buryatiya, Chita, and Yakutiya. Its presence extends further east into the diverse landscapes of the Russian Far East. Specifically, the species inhabits the Amur region and stretches as far as Kamchatka. Consequently, these varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in specific Siberian and Far Eastern ecosystems.
