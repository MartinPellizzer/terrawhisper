Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant's taxonomic progression continues through the order Rosales and the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Berchemia, with its specific identification being the species edgeworthii.
