Brosimum acutifolium

Brosimum acuifolia, commonly known as the "palo de sangre" or bloodwood, is a significant medicinal tree native to tropical forests. Renowned for its distinct reddish sap, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat wounds, skin ailments, and inflammatory conditions, showcasing its valuable ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Brosimum acutifolium
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Moraeaee and the genus Brosimum. Specifically, it is identified by the species name Brosimum acuifolium.
