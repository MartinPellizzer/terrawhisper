Ardisia thyrsiflora

Ardisia thyrsiflora, commonly known as a rare medicinal shrub, belongs to the Primulaceae family. Native to specific tropical forest ecosystems, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential bioactive compounds for their therapeutic applications in modern pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Ardisia thyrsiflora
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Ardisia thyrsiflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Ardisia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spread across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. It can also be found in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Beyond China, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Together, these locations define the natural habitat and distribution pattern of Ardisia thyrsiflora.
