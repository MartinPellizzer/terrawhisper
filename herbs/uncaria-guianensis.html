Uncaria guianensis

Uncaria guianensis, commonly known as Cat's Claw, is a powerful woody vine native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains unique oxindole alkaloids. These bioactive compounds offer significant anti-inflammatory and immune-boosting properties, making this medicinal plant a vital resource for supporting overall health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Uncaria guianensis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. Specifically, Uncaria guianensis is identified by the genus Uncaria.
