Cat's-claw, scientifically known as Uncaria guianensis, is a powerful woody vine native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned in traditional medicine, this botanical powerhouse contains unique alkaloids used to support immune function and reduce inflammation. It serves as a vital natural remedy for managing various inflammatory and autoimmune conditions.

Distribution

This plant, Uncaria guianensis, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions in South America. It can be found growing throughout the northern parts of the continent, including Venezuela. Its range also extends through the Guianas, specifically in Guyana, Suriname, and French Guiana. Additionally, this species is located within the Caribbean region, specifically on the islands of Trinidad and Tobago. Together, these locations highlight its presence within the tropical landscapes of northern South America and its nearby islands.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Uncaria guianensis, is a woody vine whose therapeutic properties are derived from various anatomical structures. The most significant medicinal components are found within the bark and the roots, which are frequently utilized in traditional pharmacology for their bioactive alkaloids. Additionally, the foliage, including both the individual leaf and the collective leaves of the climbing vine, contributes to the plant's overall biological profile, although the primary concentration of medicinal compounds is typically sought in the woody tissues and underground parts.
