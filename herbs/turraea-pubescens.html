Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of Asia. In China, it can be specifically found on the island of Hainan as well as throughout the China Southeast area. Its range extends further into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in India. Additionally, the species is present within the Indo-China region. Specifically, this includes locations such as Cambodia and Laos.
