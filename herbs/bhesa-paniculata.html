Bhesa paniculata

Bhesas paniculata, commonly known as the "climbing medicinal herb," is a versatile plant valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Native to tropical regions, it possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. This climbing shrub is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Bhesa paniculata
  • Family: Centroplacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Bhesa paniculata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Centroplacaceae and the genus Bhesa.
