Salvia limbata

Salvia limbata, commonly known as the limbata sage, is a resilient medicinal herb native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammation. Its unique chemical profile makes it a subject of significant ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Salvia limbata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Salvia limbata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Salvia.
