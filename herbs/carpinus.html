Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as hornbeam, is recognized for its distinctive botanical features, particularly its leaves. The leaves of Carpinus are characterized by their serrated margins and prominent, parallel venation that gives them a textured appearance. In many medicinal and ecological contexts, these leaves are studied for their specific chemical compositions and their role in the plant's overall biological functions.
