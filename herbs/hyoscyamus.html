Henbane, scientifically known as Hyoscamus, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its high concentration of tropane alkaloids, it has historically been used for its sedative and antispasmodic properties. However, due to its extreme toxicity, it requires precise handling and expert pharmacological guidance.
