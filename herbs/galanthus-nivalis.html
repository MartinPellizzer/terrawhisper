Galanthus nivalis

Snowdrop

Galanthus nivalis, commonly known as the Snowdrop, is a delicate perennial bulb celebrated for its early spring bloom. Renowned for its pure white, bell-shaped petals, this medicinal plant has been traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and various infections, though its potent chemical compounds require careful, expert handling.

  • Scientific name: Galanthus nivalis
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae.
