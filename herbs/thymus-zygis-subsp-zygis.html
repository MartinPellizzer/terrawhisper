Thymus zygis subsp. zygis

Thymus zygis subsp. zygis, commonly known as Zygis thyme, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its essential oils, this medicinal plant features small, woody stems and tiny leaves. It is traditionally utilized for its potent antimicrobial, antiseptic, and digestive properties in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Thymus zygis subsp. zygis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
