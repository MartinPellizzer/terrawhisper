What's the taxonomical classification of Itea ilicifolia?

Itea ilicifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized under the order Saxifrages, falling specifically into the family Iteaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with its placement in the genus Itea, ultimately identifying it as the species iliocifolia.
