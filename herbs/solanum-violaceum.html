Solanum violaceum

Solanum viaceum, commonly known as the violet nightshade, is a fascinating medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its distinct floral characteristics, it has historically been utilized in traditional healing practices. This introduction explores its botanical properties, phytochemical composition, and its significant role in ethnopharmacology and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Solanum violaceum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several distinct regions within the southern hemisphere. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Mozambique. The species also extends into the Western Indian Ocean, where it can be found in the Comoros. Furthermore, it inhabits the islands of Mauritius and Madagascar. Finally, its geographical range includes the island of Réunion within the same ocean region.
