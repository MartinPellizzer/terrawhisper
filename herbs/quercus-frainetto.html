Hungarian oak, scientifically known as Quercus frainetto, is a majestic deciduous tree native to Europe. Renowned for its robust timber and ecological significance, it holds traditional medicinal value. Often utilized in herbal practices, its bark and extracts are studied for their potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties in natural healing.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence can be specifically noted within the borders of Hungary.
