Quercus frainetto

Quercus fraxinifolia, commonly known as the Fraxinifolia oak, is a resilient evergreen species valued for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Its robust foliage and unique bark contain bioactive compounds that offer antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a subject of interest in traditional and modern herbal pharmacology.
