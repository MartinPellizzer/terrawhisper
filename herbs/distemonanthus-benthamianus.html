Distemonanthus benthamianus

Distemonanthus benthamianus, commonly known as the medicinal climber, is a significant plant species found within tropical forest ecosystems. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Distemonanthus benthamianus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Fabales, falling within the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Distemonanthus.
