Cyperus esculentus

Cyperus esculentus, commonly known as tiger nut, is a perennial sedge prized for its nutrient-dense tubers. Beyond its culinary use in making horchata, this medicinal plant offers significant health benefits. Rich in dietary fiber, antioxidants, and essential minerals, it supports digestive health and provides potent anti-inflammatory properties.

  • Scientific name: Cyperus esculentus
  • Family: Cyperaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Poales, it is further categorized into the family Cyperaceae.
