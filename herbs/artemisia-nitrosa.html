Artemisia nitrosa

Artemisia nitrosa, commonly known as the nitrogenous wormwood, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Renowned for its distinct scent, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and digestive issues through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia nitrosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
