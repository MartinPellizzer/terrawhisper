Cornelian-cherry, scientifically known as Cornus mas, is a deciduous shrub cherished for its vibrant yellow blooms and edible, crimson fruits. Renowned in traditional medicine, its antioxidant-rich berries and bark offer potent anti-inflammatory and digestive benefits, serving as a versatile botanical remedy for promoting overall wellness and vitality.
