Pseudobombax simplicifolium

Pseudobombax simplicifolium, commonly known as the Single-leaf Bombax, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this species are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Pseudobombax simplicifolium
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Malvales, and the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pseudobombax.
