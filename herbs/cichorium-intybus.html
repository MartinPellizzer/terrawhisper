Cichorium intybus

Cichorium intybus, commonly known as chicory, is a versatile medicinal herb valued for its bitter properties. Renowned for supporting digestive health, it acts as a powerful prebiotic and liver tonic. Often used to stimulate bile production and manage blood sugar, this resilient plant offers significant therapeutic benefits in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cichorium intybus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is situated within the genus Cichorium.
