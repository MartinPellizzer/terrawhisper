Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1101 studies) · ★★★★★

Chicory, scientifically known as Cichorium intybus, is a versatile perennial herb cherished for its medicinal and culinary properties. Renowned for supporting digestive health, it acts as a potent prebiotic. Often used to stimulate bile production and liver function, this resilient plant offers significant therapeutic benefits in traditional herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cichorium intybus, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its various therapeutic components. The roots are perhaps the most significant part used, often processed as a substitute for coffee or used to support digestive health. Beyond the primary root, the leaves can be consumed fresh in salads or used in decoctions to provide bitter tonic properties. The seeds are also utilized in certain preparations for their specific medicinal qualities. Ultimately, depending on the intended application, sometimes the whole plant is harvested to capture the full spectrum of its bioactive compounds.
