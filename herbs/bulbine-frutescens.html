Bulbine frutescens

Stalked bulbine

Bulbine frutescens, commonly known as Bulbine or Stalked Bulbine, is a succulent perennial native to South Africa. Renowned for its fleshy, bright green leaves and vibrant yellow flowers, this hardy plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and skin-healing properties, particularly in treating burns.

  • Scientific name: Bulbine frutescens
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales and is a member of the family Asphodelaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Bulbine.

Distribution

This plant is native to various regions throughout Southern Africa. Its primary distribution can be found across the diverse landscapes of the Cape Provinces. It also grows within the mountainous terrain of Lesotho. Additionally, the species is located in parts of Namibia. Finally, its range extends to the KwaZulu-Natal and Free State provinces.

Beneath the surface, the root system provides stability and nutrient uptake, while the roots themselves are often succulent in nature.
