Tambourissa leptophylla

Tambourissa leptophylla, commonly known as the wild jasmine or leaf-leaved tambourissa, is a unique medicinal shrub native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this botanical treasure is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential through its bioactive chemical compounds.

  • Family: Monimiaceae
