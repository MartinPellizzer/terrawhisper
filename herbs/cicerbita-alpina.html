Cicerbita alpina

Cicerbita alpina, commonly known as Alpine Cicerbita, is a perennial herb native to high-altitude European meadows. Characterized by its striking white flower heads, this plant thrives in moist, mountainous terrains. While primarily valued for its ecological role, it holds significance in traditional herbal studies for its unique botanical properties.

  • Scientific name: Cicerbita alpina
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Cicerbita.
