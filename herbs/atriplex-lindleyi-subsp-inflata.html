Atriplex lindleyi subsp. inflata

Atriplex lindleyi subsp. inflata, commonly known as bladder saltbush, is a resilient perennial shrub native to arid Australian regions. Valued for its unique bladder-like structures, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. It is traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices, offering potential therapeutic benefits through its specialized chemical properties.
