Actaea rubra, scientifically known as Actaea rubra, is a perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Often called Red Baneberry, this striking plant is characterized by its distinctive clusters of bright red berries. While historically used in traditional medicine, it is highly toxic and requires extreme caution.
