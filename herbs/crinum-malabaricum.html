Crinum malabaricum

Crinum malabaricum, commonly known as the Malabar Lily, is a perennial bulbous herb native to the tropical regions of India. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this striking plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, often utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and analgesic qualities in treating various skin and systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Crinum malabaricum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is situated in the family Amaryllidaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Crinum.
