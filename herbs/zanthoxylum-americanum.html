Prickly ash, scientifically known as Zanthoxylum americanum, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic, pungent bark, it has historically been used in traditional herbalism to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and skin irritations, offering potent antimicrobial and analgesic properties for holistic wellness.
