Adder's tongue, scientifically known as Ophioglossum petiolatum, is a unique fern species valued in traditional medicine. This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized into the subclass Ophioglossidae and the order Ophioglossales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ophioglossaceae within the genus Ophioglossum.
