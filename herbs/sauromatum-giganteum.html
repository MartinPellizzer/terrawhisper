Sauromatum giganteum

Sauromatum giganteum, commonly known as the Giant Sauromatum, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is extensively utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and infections, offering promising potential for modern drug development and therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Sauromatum giganteum
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Sauromatum giganteum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Alismatales and the family Araceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Sauromatum.
