Morphology

The morphology of Capparis micracantha is characterized by its growth as a woody shrub that functions as an obligatory climber, specifically taking the form of a liana or vine. As a terrestrial plant, it is not an epiphyte, growing directly in the soil rather than on other vegetation. The plant exhibits a robust, woody structure and can reach a maximum height of approximately 6 meters.
