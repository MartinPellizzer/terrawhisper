Amur barberry, scientifically known as Berberis amurensis, is a deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant autumn foliage and medicinal properties, it contains berberine, a potent alkaloid. This plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support digestive health, manage blood sugar, and combat inflammation.
