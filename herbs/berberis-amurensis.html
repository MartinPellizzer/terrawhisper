Amur barberry, scientifically known as Berberis amurensis, is a deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant autumn foliage and medicinal properties, it contains berberine, a potent alkaloid. This plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support digestive health, manage blood sugar, and combat inflammation.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the eastern reaches of the Asian continent. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing across several distinct regions, including Amur and Khabarovsk. Its presence extends further south into the Primorye territory and across the island of Sakhalin. Beyond the Russian borders, the species also inhabits parts of China, specifically within the Inner Mongolia region. Together, these locations define the natural geographical range of Berberis amurensis.
