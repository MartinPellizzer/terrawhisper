Viscum schimperi

Viscum schimperi, commonly known as African mistletoe, is a fascinating parasitic plant native to various African regions. Renowned for its unique ecological role, it serves as a vital medicinal resource. Traditional healers utilize its leaves and stems to treat diverse ailments, highlighting its significant ethnobotanical importance and therapeutic potential.
