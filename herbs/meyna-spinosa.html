Meyna spinosa

Meyna spinosa, commonly known as the spinous Meyna, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts possess significant therapeutic properties, including antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects. This resilient plant continues to be a subject of vital pharmacological research today.

  • Scientific name: Meyna spinosa
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, it is further nested within the family Rubiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Meyna, with its species name being Meyna spinosa.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of the Indian Subcontinent. Within this region, its presence is noted in several specific areas including Assam and India. It can also be found flourishing in the fertile terrains of Bangladesh. Additionally, the species inhabits the rugged elevations of the East Himalaya. Beyond the Indian Subcontinent, its range extends into the Indo-China region, specifically within Cambodia.
