Chrysanthemum

Chrysanthemum, commonly known as mums, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional practices. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is frequently used to soothe inflammation, reduce fever, and alleviate respiratory issues. Whether consumed as a calming tea or applied topically, this vibrant bloom offers profound holistic healing benefits.

  • Scientific name: Chrysanthemum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Chrysanthemum.

This plant exhibits a diverse presence across several regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within the territories of Germany and Poland.
