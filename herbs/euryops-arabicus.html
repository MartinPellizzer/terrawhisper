Euryops arabicus

Euryops arabicus, commonly known as a versatile medicinal plant, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this species is frequently utilized for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Understanding its botanical characteristics and therapeutic applications is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Euryops arabicus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Euryops, identifying it as Euryops arabicus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the arid regions of Northeast Tropical Africa and the Arabian Peninsula. Within Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically recorded in Djibouti, Somalia, and the island of Socotra. Moving toward the Arabian Peninsula, the species can also be found inhabiting parts of Oman. Furthermore, its range extends into the territory of Saudi Arabia. These specific locations highlight its preference for the unique environmental conditions found in these desert landscapes.
