Cymbopogon distans

Cymbopogon distans, commonly known as wild lemongrass, is a perennial grass valued for its aromatic properties. Native to various African regions, this medicinal plant is rich in essential oils. It is traditionally used to treat digestive issues, reduce inflammation, and combat infections, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cymbopogon distans
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales and the family Poaceae, it is classified under the genus Cymbopogon. Therefore, its full taxonomic identity is established as Cymbopogon distans.
