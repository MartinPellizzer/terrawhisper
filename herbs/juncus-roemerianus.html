Juncus roemerianus, scientifically known as Juncus roemerianus, is a resilient perennial rush primarily found in coastal salt marshes. While largely valued for its role in stabilizing ecosystems and preventing erosion, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance, traditionally utilized in various cultures for its unique structural and medicinal properties.
