Fallopia ciliinervis, scientifically known as Fallopia ciliinervis, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly resveratrol, it is frequently used to support cardiovascular health, reduce inflammation, and combat oxidative stress, making it a significant subject in modern pharmacological research.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across East Asia. In China, its presence is widely recorded throughout the South-Central and North-Central territories. It can also be found growing in the northern reaches of Manchuria and the high-altitude areas of Qinghai. Beyond the borders of China, the species extends its distribution into Korea. Together, these locations highlight a broad habitat across much of Eastern Asia.
