Arachis pintoi

Caracolera

Arachis pintoi, commonly known as Pinto Peanut, is a versatile, low-growing perennial legume prized for its lush green foliage and vibrant yellow flowers. Beyond its ornamental appeal, it serves as an excellent groundcover, enhancing soil fertility through nitrogen fixation and providing a resilient, eco-friendly solution for erosion control.

  • Scientific name: Arachis pintoi
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and is a member of the Fabaceae family. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Arachis.
