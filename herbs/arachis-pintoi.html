Caracolera, scientifically known as Arachis pintoi, is a versatile perennial legume valued in both landscaping and medicine. Renowned for its nitrogen-fixing capabilities and vibrant yellow blooms, it serves as an effective groundcover. Medicinally, it is utilized in traditional practices for its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties in herbal remedies.
