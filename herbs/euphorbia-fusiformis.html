Euphorbia fusiformis

Euphorbia fusiformis, commonly known as the Cotyledon or succulent euphorbia, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to arid regions. Valued in traditional healing practices, its specialized tissues contain unique bioactive compounds. This resilient succulent offers significant potential for pharmacological research, particularly in exploring its diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia fusiformis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Euphorbia.
