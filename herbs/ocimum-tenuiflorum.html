Ocimum tenuiflorum

Holy basil

Ocimum tenuiflorum, commonly known as Holy Basil or Tulsi, is a sacred perennial herb revered in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent adaptogenic properties, it helps the body manage stress, supports immune function, and combats inflammation. This versatile plant serves as a cornerstone in traditional healing and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ocimum tenuiflorum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ocimum tenuiflorum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ocimum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ocimum tenuiflorum, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several continents. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found in Gabon. Its presence extends to East Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Kenya. In the southern regions of the continent, the species is distributed through Malawi and Zambia. Finally, its range reaches Asia, where it is located in South-Central China.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Holy Basil, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The green leaves are the most frequently used part, where each individual leaf contains high concentrations of essential oils and bioactive compounds. Beyond the foliage, the stem provides structural support and also holds medicinal value, while the root system is often incorporated into traditional remedies. Furthermore, a concentrated leaf extract is frequently prepared to create potent infusions or tinctures used in holistic wellness practices.
