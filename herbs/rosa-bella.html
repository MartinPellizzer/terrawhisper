Rosa bella

Rosa bella, commonly known as the beautiful rose, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its soothing fragrance and antioxidant-rich extracts, it is frequently used in traditional remedies to promote skin health, reduce inflammation, and alleviate stress, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Rosa bella
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Rosa.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Rosa bella, is recognized by various regional and linguistic names across different cultures. In Swedish, it is referred to as Marmeladros, while in Chinese, both the standard form and the simplified version (zh-cn) are known as 美蔷薇. In the Arabic-speaking world, it carries the descriptive names ورد بيلا in Egyptian Arabic (arz) and ورد جميل in standard Arabic (ar).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across various regions of China. Specifically, it can be found growing in the northern territories of Inner Mongolia. Its presence also extends into the central parts of the country, particularly throughout China North-Central. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the areas of China Southeast. Together, these locations highlight the broad range of this medicinal plant within the Chinese landscape.
