Common Names

This plant, Fibigia clypeata, is known by various common names depending on the language and region. In Bulgarian, it is referred to as фибигия, while in Estonian, its name is kilpfibiigia. In Arabic, the species is called هيتمة جوبية. Interestingly, in Hebrew, it is associated with vision, being known as both משקפיים מצויים and משקפי הזקן.
