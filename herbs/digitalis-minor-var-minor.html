Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Digitalis minor var. minor, exhibits a very specific and restricted geographical distribution. Its presence is primarily confined to the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe. More specifically, this particular variety is documented within the Balearic Islands. Because of this limited range, the plant is considered an endemic feature of this island archipelago. Consequently, its survival is closely tied to the unique environmental conditions found in this specific territory.
