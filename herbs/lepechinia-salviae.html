Lepechinia salviae

Lepechinia salviae, commonly known as Salvias sage, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to Australia. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it has long been utilized in traditional healing practices. Its potent bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential, particularly for addressing inflammatory conditions and supporting overall wellness through natural herbal applications.
