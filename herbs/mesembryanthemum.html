Mesembryanthemum

Mesembryanthemum, commonly known as ice plants, is a diverse genus of succulent plants prized for their striking, jewel-like flowers. Beyond their ornamental beauty, various species possess significant medicinal properties, traditionally used in folk medicine to treat inflammation and skin ailments due to their unique chemical compositions and hydrating capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Mesembryanthemum
  • Family: Aizoaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Caryophyllales and the family Aizoaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is categorized under the genus Mesembryanthemum.
