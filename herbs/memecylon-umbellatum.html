Memecylon umbellatum

Memecylon umbellatum, commonly known as the Umbrella Tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues. Its leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds vital to ethnobotanical research.

  • Family: Melastomataceae
