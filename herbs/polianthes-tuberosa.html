Tuberose, scientifically known as Polianthes tuberosa, is a fragrant perennial herb celebrated for its intoxicatingly sweet scent. Primarily used in high-end perfumery, this plant also holds significant medicinal value. It is traditionally employed in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from nervous disorders to digestive issues and skin inflammations.
