Tarwi

Lupinus mutabilis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (202 studies) · ★★★★★

Tarwi, scientifically known as Lupinus mutabilis, is a high-protein Andean legume celebrated for its remarkable nutritional profile. Native to the Andes mountains, this versatile plant offers significant health benefits, including essential amino acids and fiber. Traditionally used in South American cuisine, it remains a vital superfood for sustainable agriculture.

This plant, known scientifically as Lupinus mutabilis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is further categorized into the genus Lupinus.
