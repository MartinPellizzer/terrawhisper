Indigofera trifoliata

What's the taxonomical classification of Indigofera trifoliata?

Indigofera trifoliata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Fabales. It is a member of the family Fabaceae, which is further categorized into the genus Indigofera, eventually reaching its specific designation as the species trifoliata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Indigofera trifoliata has a prostrate or ascending growth habit characterized by slender, woody stems and trifoliate leaves consisting of three small, ovate to elliptical leaflets. The foliage typically features fine pubescence on both the upper and lower surfaces, giving the plant a slightly textured appearance. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in axillary racemes, displaying a pale yellow or cream coloration. The fruit develops into small, oblong, or slightly curved pods that contain several tiny seeds. These morphological traits allow the plant to thrive in various disturbed or sandy habitats.
