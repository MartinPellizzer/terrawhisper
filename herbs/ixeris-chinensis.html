Ixeris chinensis

Ixeris chinensis, commonly known as the Chinese Ixeris, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional practices. Renowned for its cooling properties, this plant is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat inflammation, fever, and skin ailments, offering a natural approach to promoting holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Ixeris chinensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ixeris, specifically as the species Ixeris chinensis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ixeris chinensis, is recognized by various regional common names across East Asia. In Japan, it is referred to as タカサゴソウ, while in Korea, it is called 선씀바귀. The various Chinese linguistic traditions offer different names for the species, including 兔兒菜 and 山苦荬 in mainland China (zh and zh-cn), and 兔仔菜 in Taiwan (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the vast territories of Northern Asia. Within the expansive region of Siberia, it can be found in Buryatiya, Chita, and Irkutsk. Its range further extends into the remote northern reaches of Yakutiya. Additionally, the species is documented in the Russian Far East, specifically within the Amur region. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Ixeris chinensis.
