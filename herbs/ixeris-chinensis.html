Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the vast territories of Northern Asia. Within the expansive region of Siberia, it can be found in Buryatiya, Chita, and Irkutsk. Its range further extends into the remote northern reaches of Yakutiya. Additionally, the species is documented in the Russian Far East, specifically within the Amur region. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Ixeris chinensis.
