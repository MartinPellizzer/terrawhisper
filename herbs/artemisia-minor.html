Artemisia minor

Artemisia minor, commonly known as mugwort, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health, regulate menstrual cycles, and enhance dream lucidity. This versatile plant remains a staple in holistic herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia minor
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Artemisia minor, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. It is ultimately identified by its genus, Artemisia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several distinct regions of Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the North-Central areas as well as throughout the Qinghai province. It can also be found growing in the high-altitude terrains of Tibet and the remote stretches of Xinjiang. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species extends its reach into the East Himalaya region. Together, these locations highlight a distribution primarily centered around central and eastern mountainous territories.
