Azorella compacta

Azorella compacta, commonly known as Llareta, is a resilient, cushion-forming perennial native to the high-altitude Andes. This slow-growing marvel thrives in extreme, frigid environments. Valued for its unique longevity and dense structure, it holds significant ecological importance and traditional medicinal interest within its rugged, alpine mountain habitats.

  • Scientific name: Azorella compacta
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae and the genus Azorella.

This plant is distributed across several specific regions within the western and southern portions of South America.
