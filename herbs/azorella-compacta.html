Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Llareta, is a slow-growing cushion plant native to the high Andes, and its most distinctive medicinal component is the resin it produces. This aromatic resin is extracted from the plant's dense, compact structure and has been traditionally used in folk medicine for its various therapeutic properties. Often utilized in topical applications, the resin is valued for its ability to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions, serving as a vital resource in the traditional healing practices of the mountainous regions where it grows.
