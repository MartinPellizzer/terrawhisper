Trichilia dregeana

Trichilia dregeana, commonly known as the African Sandalwood, is a versatile medicinal tree native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Trichilia dregeana
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales. Ultimately, Trichilia dregeana is a member of the Meliaceae family within the genus Trichilia.
