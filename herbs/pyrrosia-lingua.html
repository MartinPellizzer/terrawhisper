Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Pyrrosia lingua, is valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties, particularly through the use of its leafy structures. In pharmacological preparations, the medicinal component is referred to as Pyrrosiae Folium or Folium Pyrrosiae, which denotes the dried leaves of the species. These folia are harvested and processed to extract the active compounds necessary for medicinal applications, ensuring that the specific botanical identity of the leaves is maintained for standardized herbal treatments.
