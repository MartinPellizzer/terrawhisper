Hymenaea

Hymenaea, commonly known as the Jatobá tree, is a versatile tropical powerhouse native to Central and South America. Renowned for its nutrient-dense fruit and aromatic resins, this medicinal plant offers diverse therapeutic benefits, ranging from anti-inflammatory properties to supporting respiratory health, making it a vital resource in traditional herbalism.
