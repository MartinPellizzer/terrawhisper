Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the Asian continent. In East Asia, it can be found growing across Southeast China. Its presence is also highly significant throughout the Indian Subcontinent, spanning multiple territories. Specifically, it inhabits the regions of India, Bangladesh, and Nepal. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the Assam region of the subcontinent.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Turmeric, utilizes different structures for its biological functions and medicinal applications. The leaf is characterized by its large, oblong shape and vibrant green color, serving as the primary site for photosynthesis to support the plant's growth. Beneath the soil, the most significant part for therapeutic use is the rhizome, a fleshy underground stem that stores essential oils and curcuminoids, which are harvested and processed for their potent healing properties.
