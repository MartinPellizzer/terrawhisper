Veratrum viride var. viride

Veratrum viride var. viride, commonly known as Green False hellebore, is a potent perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its high alkaloid content, it has historical medicinal uses in treating hypertension and cardiac issues. However, its extreme toxicity requires precise handling, as improper use can be fatal.

  • Scientific name: Veratrum viride var. viride
  • Family: Melanthiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Liliales, it is further categorized into the family Melanthiaceae. Finally, the specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Veratrum, specifically as the variety Veratrum viride var. viride.
