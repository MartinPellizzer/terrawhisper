American false-hellebore

Veratrum viride var. viride · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (5 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

American false-hellebore, scientifically known as Veratrum viride var. viride, is a perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, this plant has historically been used in traditional medicine to treat hypertension and cardiac arrhythmias, though its extreme toxicity requires cautious handling and professional medical supervision.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range across the northeastern regions of North America. In Eastern Canada, its presence can be found throughout Labrador, New Brunswick, and Québec. The distribution extends southward into the Northeastern United States as well. Within this American region, it is documented in both Maine and Connecticut.
