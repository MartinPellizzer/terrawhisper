Huperzia crassa

Huperzia crassa, commonly known as a species of clubmoss, is a primitive vascular plant found primarily in tropical regions. Renowned for its medicinal potential, it contains unique alkaloids that act as potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitors, making it a subject of significant interest in neuroprotective and cognitive enhancement research.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia crassa
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Lycopodiidae and order Lycopodiales, it is a member of the family Lycopodiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Huperzia.
