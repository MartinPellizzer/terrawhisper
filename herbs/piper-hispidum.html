Piper hispidum

Piper hispidum, commonly known as the hairy pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This shrub is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat various ailments, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Piper hispidum
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is a member of the family Piperaceae. It is classified under the genus Piper, specifically identified by the species name Piper hispidum.
