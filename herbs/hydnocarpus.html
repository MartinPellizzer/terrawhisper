Chaulmoogra trees, scientifically known as Hydnocarpus, is a genus of tropical evergreen trees renowned for their therapeutic oils. Extracted from their seeds, chaulmoogra oil has historically served as a vital treatment for leprosy and skin ailments. These resilient plants represent a significant bridge between traditional botanical medicine and modern pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central areas as well as across the Southeast. Additionally, its presence extends to the tropical island of Hainan. Moving to the Indian Subcontinent, the species is native to the region of Assam. Finally, it is also documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh.
