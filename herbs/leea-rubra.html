Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct tropical regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing within the Seychelles. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in both Assam and Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is present throughout parts of Indo-China. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of Cambodia and Laos.
