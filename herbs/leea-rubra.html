Leea rubra

Leea rubra, commonly known as the Red Leea, is a striking tropical shrub celebrated for its vibrant, crimson flower clusters. Native to various Pacific islands, this ornamental plant is prized in landscaping for its lush foliage and brilliant blooms, adding a splash of exotic color to warm, humid garden environments.

  • Scientific name: Leea rubra
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Vitales, it is classified under the family Vitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Leea, specifically as the species Leea rubra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct tropical regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing within the Seychelles. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in both Assam and Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is present throughout parts of Indo-China. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of Cambodia and Laos.
