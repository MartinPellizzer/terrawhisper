Adenocalymma nodosum

Adenocalymma nodosum, commonly known as "Catuaba," is a potent medicinal shrub native to the Brazilian rainforest. Renowned for its stimulating properties, it is traditionally used to enhance libido, boost energy, and treat fatigue. Its bark contains bioactive alkaloids that make it a vital component in herbal tonics worldwide.
