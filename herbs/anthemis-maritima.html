Anthemis maritima

Anthemis maritima, commonly known as sea chamomile, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal regions. Characterized by its delicate white daisy-like flowers and aromatic foliage, this plant thrives in sandy soils. Traditionally valued for its soothing properties, it serves as a vital component in various medicinal herbal preparations.

  • Scientific name: Anthemis maritima
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Anthemis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of Southwestern Europe. Its presence can be observed throughout the coastal areas of France and Portugal. Additionally, it is found inhabiting the island of Corse. The species also grows within the Mediterranean territories of Sardegna. Finally, its geographical range extends to the Baleares islands.
