Fagopyrum tataricum

Tartari buckwheat

Fagopyrum tataricum, commonly known as Tartary buckwheat, is a resilient medicinal plant valued for its unique nutritional profile. Rich in rutin and bioactive compounds, it offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used to support vascular health and manage blood sugar, this versatile plant remains a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Fagopyrum tataricum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae and the genus Fagopyrum.
