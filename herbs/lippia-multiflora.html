Lippia multiflora

Lippia multiflora, commonly known as Omweg or False Rosemary, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Africa. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin infections. Its potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties make it a vital resource in ethnomedicine.

  • Scientific name: Lippia multiflora
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lippia, specifically as the species Lippia multiflora.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several nations within the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Benin and Burkina.
