Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Lippia multiflora, possesses various parts that are utilized for its medicinal properties. The aromatic leaves are the most significant component, often harvested in their fresh state or processed into dried leaves to preserve their essential oils for infusions and treatments. These green leaf structures are held aloft by sturdy stalks, which support the plant's growth and allow the foliage to capture sunlight effectively.
