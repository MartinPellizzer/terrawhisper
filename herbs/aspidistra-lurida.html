What's the taxonomical classification of Aspidistra lurida?

Aspidistra lurida belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its evolutionary lineage continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, eventually placing it in the order Asparagales. Within this order, the plant is a member of the family Asparagaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Aspidistra, with its unique biological identity defined by its species name, lurida.
