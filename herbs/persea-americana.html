Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Persea americana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Laurales. Finally, it is a member of the Lauraceae family within the genus Persea.

Distribution

This plant, Persea americana, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across various regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Burkina, Gambia, and Guinea-Bissau. Moving into the West-Central Tropical Africa region, its presence extends into the Central African Republic. Additionally, the species is documented within the borders of Cameroon. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's presence in both West and West-Central tropical environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Persea americana, is a versatile medicinal resource where various components offer distinct benefits. The fruit, commonly known as the avocado, is widely consumed for its nutritional density, while the large seeds contained within are often utilized in traditional remedies for their bioactive properties. The leaves, which can be referred to in singular form as a medicinal leaf, are frequently prepared as decoctions to treat inflammation or hypertension. Additionally, the stem bark is sometimes harvested to extract compounds used in various herbal formulations, making the entire structure of the plant significant in ethnobotany.
