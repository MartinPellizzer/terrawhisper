Agrimonia eupatoria

Agriemonia eupatoria, commonly known as Eupatorium, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to support respiratory health, alleviate digestive discomfort, and reduce inflammation. Its therapeutic properties make it a significant subject in ethnobotanical research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Agrimonia eupatoria
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic division places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosaless, within the family Rosaecae. It is specifically identified under the genus Agrimonia, known by its species name eupatoria.
