Artemisia transiliensis

Artemisia transiliensis, commonly known as a species of wormwood, is a resilient medicinal herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it has been utilized in traditional medicine to address various ailments. This plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds and antioxidant capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia transiliensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further descending through the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Artemisia.
