Scopolia carniolica

Scopolia carniolica, commonly known as Carniolan scoparia, is a potent medicinal herb belonging to the Solanaceae family. Native to central Europe, this plant contains bioactive alkaloids like scopolamine. It is traditionally utilized for its sedative and antispasmodic properties, though its high toxicity requires careful, expert handling in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Scopolia carniolica
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Solanales, and the family Solanaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Scopolia.
