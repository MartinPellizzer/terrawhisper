European scopolia

Scopolia carniolica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (256 studies) · ★★★★★

European scopolia, scientifically known as Scopolia carniolica, is a perennial medicinal herb native to Central and Southeastern Europe. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, particularly scopoine, this plant has a long history in traditional medicine. It is primarily utilized for its sedative, analgesic, and antispasmodic properties in various herbal remedies.
