Polygonum ochotense

What's the taxonomical classification of Polygonum ochotense?

Polygonum ochotense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is a member of the order Caryophyllales and falls within the family Polygonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy is completed by its placement in the genus Polygonum and its specific designation as the species ochotense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Polygonum ochotense has erect, slender stems that are typically reddish or greenish and feature characteristic swollen nodes. The leaves are small, alternate, and lanceolate in shape with entire margins. Each leaf possesses a distinct ochrea, which is a membranous sheath surrounding the stem at the node. The plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that are often clustered in the upper leaf axils. These floral structures eventually develop into tiny, dry fruits used for reproduction.
