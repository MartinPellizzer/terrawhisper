Ruellia

Ruelliia, commonly known as Rue, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently used to support digestive health and soothe skin irritations. This aromatic herb remains a vital staple for those seeking natural wellness solutions.

  • Scientific name: Ruellia
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further categorized into the family Acanthaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ruellia.
