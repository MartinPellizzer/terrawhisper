Holmskioldia sanguinea

Holmskioldiia sanguinea, commonly known as the blood-leaf plant, is a striking tropical species valued for its deep red foliage. Beyond its ornamental appeal, it holds significant place in traditional medicine. Often used in herbal remedies, it is studied for its potential bioactive properties and various therapeutic applications in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Holmskioldia sanguinea
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Holmskioldia sanguinea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic position is completed within the genus Holmskioldia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct ecological regions. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is noted within the borders of Malawi. The species also reaches the Western Indian Ocean, specifically occurring on the island of Mauritius. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found in both Assam and Bangladesh. Finally, its range extends further into the mountainous terrain of the East Himalaya.
