Pachygone laurifolia

Pachygone laurifolia, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, belongs to the Euphorbiaceae family. Native to tropical regions, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse bioactive compounds. Research focuses on its potential pharmacological properties, particularly its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory activities used in holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Pachygone laurifolia
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Menispermaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pachygone, specifically the species Pachygone laurifolia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions of East Asia. In mainland China, its distribution is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast territories. Moving eastward, the species can be found naturally occurring in Japan. Its presence extends further south to include the Nansei-shoto islands. Finally, the plant is also documented as growing within the coastal regions of Taiwan.
