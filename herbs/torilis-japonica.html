Torilis japonica

Torilis japonica, commonly known as the Japanese Torilis, is a resilient herbaceous plant valued in traditional herbalism. Distinguished by its delicate white umbels, it thrives in diverse ecosystems. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this species is studied for its potential therapeutic properties and its role in holistic medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Torilis japonica
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Torilis.
