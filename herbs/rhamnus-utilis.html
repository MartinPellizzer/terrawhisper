What's the taxonomical classification of Rhamnus utilis?

Rhamnus utilis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized under the order Rosales and belongs to the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rhamnus and the species utilis.
