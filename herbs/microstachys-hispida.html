Microstachys hispida

Microstachys hispida, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, is highly valued in traditional ethnobotany. Rich in bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and skin ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Microstachys hispida
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is completed by its classification within the genus Microstachys.
