Zantedeschia aethiopica

Arum lily

Zantheschia aethiopica, commonly known as the African mahogany, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical African regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
