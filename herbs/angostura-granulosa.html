Angostura granulosa, scientifically known as Angostura granulosa, is a woody shrub native to the tropical Americas. Renowned for its potent bark, this plant has been used extensively in traditional medicine for its digestive and tonic properties, eventually becoming the essential, bitter foundation for the world-famous Angostura bitters.
