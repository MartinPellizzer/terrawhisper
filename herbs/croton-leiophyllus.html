Croton leiophyllus

Croton leiophyllus, commonly known as the "Leafy Croton," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent bioactive properties used to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues. Its leaves and bark offer significant therapeutic potential in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Croton leiophyllus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation concludes with the genus Croton.
