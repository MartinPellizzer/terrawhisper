What's the taxonomical classification of Pittosporum ovoideum?

Pittosporum ovoideum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Under the order Apiales, it is nested within the family Pittosporaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized under the genus Pittosporum, with the specific identifier being the species ovoideum.
