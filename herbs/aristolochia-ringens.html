Aristolochia ringens

Aristolochia ringens, commonly known as Moonflower or Wild Ginger, is a climbing perennial native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique, tubular blossoms, this plant has historically been used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation and digestive ailments. However, its use requires caution due to potential nephrotoxicity.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia ringens
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Aristolochia ringens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Piperales. Finally, it is a member of the family Aristolochiaceae and the genus Aristolochia.
