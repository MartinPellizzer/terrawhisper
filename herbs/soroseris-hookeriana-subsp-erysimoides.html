Soroseris hookeriana subsp. erysimoides

Soroseris hookerianan subsp. erysimoides, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, is a specialized botanical species native to high-altitude regions. Valued for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Understanding its traditional uses and chemical properties is essential for exploring its role in modern natural pharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
