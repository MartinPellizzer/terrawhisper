Elsholtzia ciliata

Elsholtzia ciliata, commonly known as the Chinese Hackberry, is a deciduous tree valued for its medicinal and ornamental properties. In traditional practices, various parts of the tree are utilized to treat ailments such as inflammation and skin conditions. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a significant species in ethnobotany.
