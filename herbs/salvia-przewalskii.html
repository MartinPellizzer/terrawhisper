Ganzi Danshen, scientifically known as Salvia przewalskii, is a potent medicinal herb deeply rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its ability to invigorate blood and clear heat, it is widely used to treat cardiovascular ailments, promote circulation, and reduce inflammation, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.
