Salvia przewalskii

Salvia przewalskii, commonly known as Przewalski's sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of China. Distinguished by its striking violet-blue flowers and aromatic foliage, this species is valued in traditional herbal practices for its unique bioactive compounds and its ability to thrive in harsh environments.

  • Scientific name: Salvia przewalskii
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Salvia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central areas and the North-Central provinces. Furthermore, the species is native to the high-altitude terrains of Tibet. Its range also extends beyond Chinese borders into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, this medicinal herb is documented to grow within the mountainous landscape of Nepal.
