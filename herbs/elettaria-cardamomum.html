Cardamom, scientifically known as Elettaria cardamomum, is a highly aromatic spice and medicinal plant native to the tropical forests of India. Renowned for its distinctive flavor, it serves various therapeutic purposes, including aiding digestion, relieving respiratory issues, and acting as a potent antioxidant within traditional Ayurvedic and holistic medicine.
