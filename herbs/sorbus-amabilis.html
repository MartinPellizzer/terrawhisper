Sorbus amabilis

What's the taxonomical classification of Sorbus amabilis?

Sorbus amabilis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the biological hierarchy, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its lineage through the order Rosales, it falls under the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Sorbus, and its unique species name, amabilis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Sorbus amabilis has pinnate leaves composed of several pairs of serrated leaflets and terminal clusters of white flowers that transition into bright red pomes. During the autumn season, the canopy undergoes a dramatic transformation as the leaves turn vibrant shades of orange and red. The fruit develops into dense, rounded clusters that remain visible on the branches long after the foliage has fallen.
