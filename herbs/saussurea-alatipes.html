Saussurea alatipes

What's the taxonomical classification of Saussurea alatipes?

Saussurea alatipes belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, the plant is placed in the order Asterales and falls within the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Saussurea, with its specific designation being the species alatipes.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Saussurea alatipes has lanceolate to ovate leaves that are characterized by prominent, wing-like margins extending along the midrib. The plant features a sturdy, upright stem that supports a dense inflorescence of yellowish or greenish flower heads. Each individual floret is small, but they are clustered together to form a prominent capitulum. The overall architecture of the plant is adapted for survival in high-altitude alpine environments.
