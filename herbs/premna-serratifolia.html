Herbal tree, scientifically known as Premna serratifolia, is a versatile medicinal plant widely used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Found across tropical Asia, it is celebrated for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Various parts, including leaves and bark, are utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and fever.
