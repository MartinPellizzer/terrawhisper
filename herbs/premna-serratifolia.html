Premna serratifolia

Premna serratifolia, commonly known as the serrate-leaved premna, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, its leaves and stems possess potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. It is widely utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Premna serratifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Lamiales, it is further categorized into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Premna.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent and its surrounding waters. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Kenya and Tanzania. Its presence extends further south into the region of South Tropical Africa, specifically within Mozambique. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout the Western Indian Ocean. This oceanic range includes the Aldabra islands as well as the remote Chagos Archipelago.
