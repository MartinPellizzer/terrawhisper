Thoroughwort, scientifically known as Chromolaena, is a versatile medicinal shrub widely utilized in traditional healing practices across tropical regions. Renowned for its potent antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties, this plant plays a crucial role in treating skin infections, cuts, and gastrointestinal ailments through its bioactive phytochemical compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution throughout various regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the nations of Benin, Guinea, and Ivory Coast. Its presence is also well-documented within the borders of Nigeria. Moving further south, the species extends into West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is known to inhabit the region of Cabinda.
