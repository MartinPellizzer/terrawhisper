Chromolaena

Chromolaena, commonly known as Siam weed, is a versatile medicinal plant widely used in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties, it is frequently applied to treat skin infections and minor cuts. This resilient shrub plays a vital role in diverse herbal medicine practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Chromolaena
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Chromolaena.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution throughout various regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the nations of Benin, Guinea, and Ivory Coast. Its presence is also well-documented within the borders of Nigeria. Moving further south, the species extends into West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is known to inhabit the region of Cabinda.
