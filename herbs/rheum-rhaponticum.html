Rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum rhaponticum, is a perennial herb prized for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its roots contain anthraquinones, which act as powerful laxatives. Beyond digestive support, this plant is studied for its antioxidant effects, offering a unique blend of therapeutic potential and history.
