Rheum rhaponticum

Rheum rhaponicum, commonly known as alpine rhubarb, is a perennial herb native to high-altitude regions of Europe and Asia. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, its roots contain anthraquinone glycosides, which serve as effective laxatives. This hardy plant is widely utilized in traditional herbal medicine to support digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Rheum rhaponticum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is further categorized into the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rheum, which includes the specific species Rheum rhaponticum.
