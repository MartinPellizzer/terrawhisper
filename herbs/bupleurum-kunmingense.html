Bupleurum kunmingense

Bupleurum kunmingense, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional practices, belongs to the Apiaceae family. Native to specific mountainous regions, it is highly valued for its bioactive compounds. This plant is primarily utilized in herbal formulations to support liver health, reduce inflammation, and restore vital energy.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum kunmingense
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, falling within the family Apiaceae.
