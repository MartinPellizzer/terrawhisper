Koelreuteria elegans subsp. formosana

Koelreuteria elegans subsp. formosana, commonly known as the Formosan Golden Shower tree, is a striking ornamental plant valued for its cascading yellow blooms. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, this subspecies holds significant botanical interest, offering potential medicinal properties rooted in traditional practices, though further scientific research is required to explore.
