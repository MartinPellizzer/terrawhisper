Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and varied geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Middle Europe, its presence can be noted within the borders of Germany. Moving toward Eastern Europe, the species is found inhabiting the Baltic States. Its range extends significantly into the Russian Far East, specifically covering the Kuril Islands and Sakhalin. Finally, the distribution reaches into Central Asia, where it is located in Tajikistan.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as the Manchurian walnut, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, most notably its fruit and leaf. The fruit, which contains a nutrient-dense kernel protected by a hard shell, is frequently harvested for its rich oils and bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies. Additionally, the leaf serves as another significant part of the plant, often utilized in herbal preparations due to its specific chemical constituents that contribute to the plant's overall therapeutic profile.
