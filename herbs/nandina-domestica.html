This plant, known scientifically as Nandina domestica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the Berberidaceae family within the genus Nandina.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as sacred bamboo, is frequently utilized in traditional medicine, with its leaves serving as a significant component in various preparations. These compound leaves are characterized by their delicate, lance-shaped leaflets that can transition from a vibrant green to striking shades of red or bronze depending on the season. In medicinal applications, the leaves are often harvested to create infusions or decoctions, valued for their specific chemical properties and therapeutic potential.
