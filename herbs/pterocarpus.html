Pterocarpus

Pterocarpus, commonly known as the Padauk tree, is a versatile medicinal plant highly valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties, various parts of the tree are utilized to treat skin ailments, promote wound healing, and support overall wellness through diverse therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Pterocarpus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Its taxonomic identification is completed by its classification within the genus Pterocarpus.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several nations within the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its presence has been documented in the country of Benin. It also thrives within the borders of Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species can be found growing in Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends to the coastal areas of Guinea-Bissau.
