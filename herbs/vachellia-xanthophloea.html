Fever tree, scientifically known as Vachellia xanthophloea, is a prominent African medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its distinctive yellowish bark, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including malaria and fever. This resilient species plays a vital role in both ecosystem stability and ethnobotany.

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across various regions of the African continent.
