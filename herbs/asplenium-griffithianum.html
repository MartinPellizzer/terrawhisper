What's the taxonomical classification of Asplenium griffithianum?

Asplenium griffithianum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. Taxonomically, this plant is a member of the family Aspleniaceae and falls under the genus Asplenium, specifically identified as the species griffithianum.
