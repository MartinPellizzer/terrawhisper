Aeginetia sinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Aeginetia sinensis?

Aeginetia sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further categorized under the order Lamiales. It is a member of the family Orobanchaceae, which places it within the genus Aeginetia, eventually reaching its specific identification as the species sinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aeginetia sinensis has an endophytic life cycle characterized by a lack of chlorophyll, possessing specialized root tubers that attach to the roots of host plants like Scrophularia. The plant lacks true leaves, instead featuring small, scale-like structures along its leafless, fleshy stems. These stems are typically upright and can reach several decimeters in height while maintaining a brownish or yellowish hue. Once matured, the plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that emerge from the stem to facilitate reproduction. The reproductive structures eventually develop into small capsules containing numerous tiny seeds.
