Huperzia selago

Huperzia selago, commonly known as fir clubmoss, is a perennial lycopod found in acidic, moist habitats. Renowned for its pharmacological potential, this plant contains huperzine A, a potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. It is studied extensively for its neuroprotective properties and its ability to enhance cognitive function and memory.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia selago
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Lycopodiidae, it is classified under the order Lycopodiales and the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Huperzia.
