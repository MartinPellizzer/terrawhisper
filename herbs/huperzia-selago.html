Huperzia selago · Accepted scientific name

Northern firmoss, scientifically known as Huperzia selago, is a perennial clubmoss found in acidic, damp habitats across the Northern Hemisphere. Renowned in pharmacology, it contains the potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor huperzine A, which is extensively studied for its potential to enhance cognitive function and treat neurodegenerative diseases like Alzheimer's.
