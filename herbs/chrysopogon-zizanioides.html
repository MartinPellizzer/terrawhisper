Chrysopogon zizanioides

Chrysopogon zizanioides, commonly known as Vetiver, is a perennial grass celebrated for its aromatic roots. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and sedative properties. Beyond its therapeutic uses, this resilient plant plays a crucial role in soil conservation and essential oil production worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Chrysopogon zizanioides
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales. Finally, it is a member of the family Poaceae within the genus Chrysopogon.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented across several countries including Ghana, Liberia, and Sierra Leone. It is also found within the West Tropical Africa zone in Togo. Furthermore, the species extends its range into West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, its occurrence in this central region is noted in Cameroon.
