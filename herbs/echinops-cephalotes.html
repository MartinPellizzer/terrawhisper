Headed globe thistle, scientifically known as Echinops cephalotes, is a striking perennial native to Southern Europe. Renowned for its dramatic, spherical blue flower heads, this hardy plant serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. It is traditionally valued in herbal practices for its potential properties in supporting overall wellness and vitality.
