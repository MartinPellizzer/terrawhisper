Tripolium pannonicum

Sea aster

Tripolium pannonicum, commonly known as Hungarian knotweed, is a resilient medicinal herb native to Europe. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive issues. Its unique phytochemical profile makes it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies and natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Tripolium pannonicum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tripolium as Tripolium pannonicum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Tripolium pannonicum, is referred to by various common names depending on the regional language. In Hungarian, it is called pozsgás őszirózsa, while in Catalan, it is known as cabridella. Its names also vary across other European languages, being called Sólnô astra in Czech (csb), Strand-Asters in Danish (da), and Strand-Aster in German (de).
