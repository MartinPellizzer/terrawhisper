Salvia aurea

Salvia aurea, commonly known as Golden Sage, is a captivating perennial cherished for its vibrant, sun-kissed blooms and aromatic foliage. Native to specific Mediterranean climates, this resilient plant offers both ornamental beauty and traditional medicinal potential, making it a versatile addition to any therapeutic garden or botanical collection.
