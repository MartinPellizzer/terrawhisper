Sinicuichi, scientifically known as Heimia salicifolia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Americas. Commonly called Sinicuichi, this plant is renowned in traditional folk medicine for its psychoactive properties and its ability to alleviate tension, support relaxation, and treat various physical ailments through its unique chemical compounds.
