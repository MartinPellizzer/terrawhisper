Thymus mandschuricus

Thymus mandschuricus, commonly known as Manchurian thyme, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it possesses significant antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties. This aromatic plant serves as a versatile therapeutic agent, supporting respiratory health and boosting immune function naturally.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
