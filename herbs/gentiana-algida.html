Gentiana algida

Gentiana algida, commonly known as the Arctic Gentian, is a resilient perennial wildflower native to the frigid tundra of the far north. Renowned for its striking pale blue blossoms, this hardy species thrives in alpine environments, playing a vital role in the delicate ecological balance of Arctic ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana algida
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Gentianaceae and specifically within the genus Gentiana.

Distribution

This plant, known as Gentiana algida, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across various regions of Siberia. It can be found growing within the Altay mountain range, which marks one of its key habitats. The species also populates the territory of Buryatiya, contributing to its regional presence. Furthermore, its range extends into the Chita region and the Krasnoyarsk area. Finally, the plant is documented to inhabit the southern Siberian region of Tuva.
