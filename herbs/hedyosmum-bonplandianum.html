Hedyosmum bonplandianum

Hedyosmum bonplandianum, commonly known as the "Maguey de monte," is a resilient shrub native to Mexico. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this species offers significant medicinal potential. Research focuses on its bioactive compounds, which may provide antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, making it a subject of great pharmacological interest.
