Plumbago

Plumbago, commonly known as Leadwort, is a versatile genus of flowering plants celebrated for its striking, often pale blue blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, various species hold significant medicinal value in traditional practices, utilized for their diverse bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic applications in treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Plumbago
  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Plumbaginaceae and the genus Plumbago.

Distribution

Its presence extends further into Middle Europe through the country of Germany. Moving toward the Mediterranean, the species is distributed throughout Southwestern Europe, specifically within the Baleares. Finally, its distribution includes the islands of Corse and the mainland regions of France.
