Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several regions across East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is recorded in the southeastern parts of the country as well as on the island of Hainan. The distribution extends further into Eastern Asia, specifically documented in Taiwan. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, the species can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Cambodia. Finally, its range includes the mountainous and forested areas of Laos.
