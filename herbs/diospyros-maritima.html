Diospyros maritima

Diospyros maritima, commonly known as the maritime ebony, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to coastal regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its various parts possess therapeutic properties used to treat diverse ailments. This botanical species plays a vital role in coastal ecosystems while offering significant potential for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Diospyros maritima
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ebenaceae and is categorized under the genus Diospyros.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In East Asia, its presence is noted within the Nansei-shoto islands and throughout Taiwan. Moving into mainland China, the species is specifically found within the South-Central region. Its range further extends into Indo-China, where it inhabits parts of Cambodia. Additionally, the plant can be located within the borders of Laos.
