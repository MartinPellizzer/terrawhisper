Broadleaf ebony, scientifically known as Diospyros maritima, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to coastal habitats. Renowned for its dense, durable timber and glossy foliage, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently studied for its potential medicinal properties, offering unique bioactive compounds for traditional and modern pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In East Asia, its presence is noted within the Nansei-shoto islands and throughout Taiwan. Moving into mainland China, the species is specifically found within the South-Central region. Its range further extends into Indo-China, where it inhabits parts of Cambodia. Additionally, the plant can be located within the borders of Laos.
