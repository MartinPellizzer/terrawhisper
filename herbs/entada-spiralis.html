Entada spiralis

Entada spiralis, commonly known as the sea bean or spiral climber, is a robust woody vine native to tropical regions. Renowned for its massive, coiled pods, this medicinal plant is utilized in traditional healing practices to treat various ailments, including skin conditions, inflammation, and digestive issues through its bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Entada spiralis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Entada, which includes the species Entada spiralis.

Common Names

This plant, Entada spiralis, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the spiralous entada, while in French, it is called l'entada spiralée. Within local contexts in different regions, it is also identified as espina de monte in Spanish and as spirale entada in Portuguese.
