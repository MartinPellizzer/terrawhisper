Harrisonia abyssinica

Harrisonia abyssinica, commonly known as the "African Harrisonia," is a significant medicinal shrub native to East Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots are utilized to treat various ailments, including malaria, stomach aches, and inflammation, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Africa
