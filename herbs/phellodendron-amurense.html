Amur cork-tree

Phellodendron amurense · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (457 studies) · ★★★★★

Amur cork-tree, scientifically known as Phellodendron amurense, is a deciduous tree prized in traditional medicine. Its bitter, yellow inner bark contains alkaloids like berberine, offering potent anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and digestive benefits. Widely utilized in East Asian therapies, it serves as a vital natural remedy for various systemic ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across distinct regions of Eurasia. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is documented within the territories of Bulgaria and Romania. Moving toward the Asian continent, the species is found extensively throughout the Russian Far East. Specifically, it inhabits the Amur and Khabarovsk regions of this vast area. Additionally, the plant can be located as far east as the Kuril Islands.
