What's the taxonomical classification of Docynia delavayi?

Docynia delaavay belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Docynia, with its specific designation being the species delaayi.

What are the pharmacological activities of Docynia delavayi?

This plant has antimicrobial, antioxidant, carbohydrate digestion enzyme inhibitory, and prebiotic pharmacological activities. Its antioxidant properties help neutralize free radicals, thereby reducing oxidative stress within biological systems. The plant also demonstrates antimicrobial effectiveness by inhibiting the growth of various pathogenic microorganisms. Furthermore, it exhibits carbohydrate digestion enzyme inhibitory activity, which can assist in managing postprandial glucose levels. Finally, its prebiotic potential supports gut health by promoting the growth of beneficial intestinal microbiota.

According to a study published by "Ultrasonics sonochemistry", Docynia delavayi polyphenols (DDP) exhibit excellent antioxidant activity with a DPPH radical scavenging IC50 of 3.75 μg/mL. These polyphenols also demonstrate inhibitory activity against carbohydrate digestion enzymes, specifically α-Glucosidase and α-Amylase. DDP shows inhibitory effects on Escherichia coli, Bacillus subtilis, Staphylococcus aureus, and Salmonella. Furthermore, DDP promotes species associated with short-chain fatty acid synthesis while reducing pathogenic bacteria like Shigella sonnei and Klebsiella pneumoniae. The substance also modulates the intestinal metabolic profile by enriching pathways involved in Fatty acid biosynthesis, Fatty acid metabolism, and Fatty acid elongation in mitochondria.
