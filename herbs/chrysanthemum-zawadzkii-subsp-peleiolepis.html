Chrysanthemum zawadzkii subsp. peleiolepis

Chrysanthemum zawadzkii subsp. peleiolepis, commonly known as a unique medicinal subspecies, is a rare botanical treasure. Valued in traditional practices, this plant possesses potent bioactive compounds with potential therapeutic benefits. Understanding its chemical profile and ecological significance is essential for exploring its future applications in natural pharmacology and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Chrysanthemum zawadzkii subsp. peleiolepis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Chrysanthemum, under the subspecies peleiolepis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the vast landscapes of the Siberian region. Its documented distribution includes specific occurrences in the Buryatiya area. It can also be located throughout the territories of Chita and Irkutsk. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the Krasnoyarsk region. Finally, this medicinal plant is known to inhabit the southern reaches of Tuva.
