Aster

Frost flowers

Aster, commonly known as starwort, is a versatile flowering plant cherished for its radiant, star-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, Aster holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is often utilized to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote skin healing, making it a botanical treasure.

  • Scientific name: Aster
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Aster.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented throughout Great Britain, Norway, and Sweden. Moving toward the central part of the continent, the species is also found in Middle Europe. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of both Austria and Belgium. These various locations highlight the wide range of habitats this medicinal plant can occupy.
