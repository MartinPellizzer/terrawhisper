Vaccinium

Vaccinium, commonly known as blueberries, cranberries, and lingonberries, comprises a diverse genus of shrubs prized for their nutrient-dense fruits. Renowned for their high antioxidant content, these plants offer significant health benefits, supporting immune function and cardiovascular wellness. They are widely cultivated globally for both culinary and medicinal purposes.

  • Scientific name: Vaccinium
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae, it is classified under the genus Vaccinium.
