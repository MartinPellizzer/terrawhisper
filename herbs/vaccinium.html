Blueberry, scientifically known as Vaccinium, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its profound antioxidant properties. Cultivated globally, this genus offers potent therapeutic benefits, making it a cornerstone in natural wellness and nutrition.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the northern reaches of the European continent.
