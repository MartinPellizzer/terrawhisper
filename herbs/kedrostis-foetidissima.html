Kedrostis foetidissima

Kedrostis foetidissima, commonly known as the foul-smelling kedrostis, is a perennial climbing shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties. It is primarily utilized for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant effects, offering diverse applications in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Kedrostis foetidissima
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
