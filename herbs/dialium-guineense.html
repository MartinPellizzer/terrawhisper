Plant Parts

This plant, Dialium guineense, is a versatile medicinal resource where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaves are often employed in decoctions to treat ailments such as fever or skin conditions, while the stem bark is frequently harvested to prepare infusions used for addressing gastrointestinal issues or inflammatory conditions. The fleshy pulp surrounding the fruit is not only edible but also contains bioactive compounds that contribute to its nutritional and medicinal value. Within the fruit, the seeds are sometimes processed for specific traditional remedies, and even the root is utilized in various herbal preparations to treat different systemic disorders.
