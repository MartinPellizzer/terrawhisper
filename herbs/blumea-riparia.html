Blumea riparia

Blumea riparia, commonly known as Sambilungan in the Philippines, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, fever, and inflammation, serving as a vital natural resource for various community healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Blumea riparia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Blumea, where it is known as Blumea riparia.

Common Names

This plant, Blumea riparia, is known by several different common names depending on the language and regional context. In Chinese (zh), it is referred to as 滇桂艾纳香 and 白花九里明. Specifically, in Simplified Chinese (zh-hans), it is called 假东风草, while in Mainland China (zh-cn), it is identified as 滇桂艾纳香, and in Traditional Chinese (zh-tw), the name is written as 滇桂艾納香.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions in East and South Asia. Within China, its distribution spans from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast coastal areas. It can also be found growing on the tropical island of Hainan. In Eastern Asia, the species is documented occurring in Taiwan. Finally, its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the Assam region.
