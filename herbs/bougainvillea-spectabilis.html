Great bougainvillea, scientifically known as Bougainvillea spectabilis, is a vibrant, woody climber celebrated for its stunning bracts. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. Traditional practices utilize its various parts to treat inflammation, wounds, and digestive issues, highlighting its profound therapeutic value in herbal medicine.
