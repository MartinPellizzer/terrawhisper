What's the taxonomical classification of Rubia schumanniana?

Rubia schumanniana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is placed in the order Gentianaales. It is a member of the family Rubiaceae, specifically situated within the genus Rubia, and is identified by the species epithet schumanniana.
