What's the taxonomical classification of Parnassia mysorensis?

Parnasssia mysorensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Celastrales. It is a member of the family Celastraceae, falling under the genus Parnassia, and is specifically identified by its species name, mysorensis.
