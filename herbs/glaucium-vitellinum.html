Glaucium vitellinum

Glaucium vitellinum, commonly known as the Blue Vitelline, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its unique therapeutic properties. Found in specific ecological niches, it offers significant bioactive compounds used in traditional healing. Understanding its botanical characteristics and medicinal applications is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Glaucium vitellinum
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Glaucium.
