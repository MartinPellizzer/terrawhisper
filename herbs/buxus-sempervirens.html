Plant Parts

This plant, Buxus sempervirens, utilizes various structures that can be significant in different contexts. The evergreen leaves are small, leathery, and elliptical, providing a dense texture to the shrub. Additionally, the slender twigs are known for their extreme hardness and density, which historically made them useful for fine woodworking. While the single leaf may appear simple, the collective arrangement of the foliage creates the characteristic lush appearance of this medicinal species.
