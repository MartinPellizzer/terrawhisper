Buxus sempervirens

Buxus sempervirens, commonly known as common box, is an evergreen shrub prized for its dense foliage and ornamental versatility. Beyond its use in landscaping, this plant holds significant historical importance in traditional medicine, where its chemical constituents have been explored for various therapeutic properties and botanical applications.

  • Scientific name: Buxus sempervirens
  • Family: Buxaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Buxus sempervirens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Buxales. Finally, it is a member of the family Buxaceae and the genus Buxus.
