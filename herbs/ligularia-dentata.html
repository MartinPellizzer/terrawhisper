Ligularia dentata, scientifically known as Ligularia dentata, is a striking perennial herb valued for its large, serrated leaves and vibrant yellow blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant traditional medicinal importance, particularly in East Asian practices, where its roots are utilized to treat inflammation and digestive ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in Great Britain and Sweden. Its presence extends into Middle Europe, specifically within the borders of Germany. Within Asia, the species is well-established across various parts of China. Specifically, it inhabits both the North-Central and South-Central regions of the country.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Leopard Plant, utilizes its underground root system as a primary component for its medicinal properties. The root of Ligularia dentata is often harvested and processed for use in traditional medicine, where it is valued for its bioactive compounds. In various herbal practices, the root is prepared to address specific health concerns, serving as a vital part of the plant's overall therapeutic profile.
