Conioselinum tenuissimum

Conioselinum tenuissiimum, commonly known as a rare member of the Apiaceae family, is a specialized medicinal plant valued for its unique phytochemical profile. Found in specific ecological niches, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to explore new advancements in natural pharmacology and traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Conioselinum tenuissimum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Apiales, falling into the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Conioselinum as the species Conioselinum tenuissimum.
