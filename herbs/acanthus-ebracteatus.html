Acanthus ebracteatus

Acanthus ebracteatus, commonly known as the thornless acanthus, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin conditions, offering a natural approach to wellness through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Acanthus ebracteatus
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is found within the genus Acanthus.
