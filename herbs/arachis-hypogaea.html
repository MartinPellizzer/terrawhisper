Arachis hypogaea

Peanut

Arachis hypogaea, commonly known as the peanut or groundnut, is a versatile legume valued for both nutrition and medicine. Beyond its protein-rich seeds, it possesses therapeutic properties, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. Traditionally, its oils and extracts are utilized to support skin health and cardiovascular wellness in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Arachis hypogaea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, the medicinal specimen is identified by its genus, Arachis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be observed within the borders of Great Britain. Moving toward the southeast, the species is also documented in Bulgaria. Its range extends into Eastern Europe, where it is found in Belarus and the Krym region.
