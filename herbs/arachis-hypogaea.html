Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, the medicinal specimen is identified by its genus, Arachis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be observed within the borders of Great Britain. Moving toward the southeast, the species is also documented in Bulgaria. Its range extends into Eastern Europe, where it is found in Belarus and the Krym region. Finally, the plant is distributed throughout Central European Russia.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Arachis hypogaea, is highly valued for its nutrient-dense components, particularly its groundnut seeds. These seeds serve as the primary source of both dietary fats and essential proteins, making them a staple in various global cuisines. Within the chemical composition of these seeds, seed storage globulins play a crucial role, acting as the main protein fraction that provides vital amino acids necessary for biological functions.
