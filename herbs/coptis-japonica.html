Japanese goldthread

Japanese goldthread, scientifically known as Coptis japonica, is a perennial herb renowned in traditional East Asian medicine. Characterized by its striking yellow rhizomes, it contains potent alkaloids like berberine. This medicinal plant is widely studied for its powerful antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties, offering significant therapeutic potential.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Coptis japonica, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties which are concentrated in its underground structures. The most therapeutically significant parts are the rhizomes, which are often harvested and processed to extract potent alkaloids. While the roots are often discussed in botanical descriptions, it is specifically the rhizome that serves as the main source of active compounds used in traditional medicine. For commercial and pharmaceutical preparation, the dried rhizome is typically utilized, as this form ensures stability and ease of use in various decoctions and herbal formulations.
