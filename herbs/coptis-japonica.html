Coptis japonica

Coptis japonica, commonly known as Japanese goldthread, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its high berberine content, this plant possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. It is widely utilized in herbal remedies to treat digestive ailments, skin infections, and various metabolic disorders.

  • Scientific name: Coptis japonica
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and is placed within the genus Coptis.
