Handroanthus heptaphyllus

Handroanthus heptaphyllus, commonly known as the seven-leaved Handroanthus, is a significant medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat various ailments. Its unique botanical characteristics and bioactive compounds make it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Handroanthus heptaphyllus
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Handroanthus heptaphyllus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Bignoniaceae and falls under the genus Handroanthus.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Handroanthus heptaphyllus, is recognized by several distinct common names across different languages and regions. In Portuguese, it is frequently referred to as Ipê-rosa or simply Ipê rosa, while in Guarani, it is known as Tajy hu. Its names also vary significantly in other parts of the world, such as the Lithuanian designation Septynlapis balamedis and the Chinese name 粉色小號風鈴木.

Moving into Brazil, the species is widely spread across multiple distinct climatic zones. It can be found inhabiting the West-Central and Northeast regions of the country.
