Handroanthus heptaphyllus

Handroanthus heptaphyllus, commonly known as the seven-leaved Handroanthus, is a significant medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat various ailments. Its unique botanical characteristics and bioactive compounds make it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Handroanthus heptaphyllus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Bignoniaceae and falls under the genus Handroanthus.
