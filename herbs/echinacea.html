Purple cone flower, scientifically known as Echinacea, is a renowned perennial herb prized for its therapeutic properties. Native to North America, this resilient plant is most famous for its ability to support the immune system. Often used to combat common colds, Echinacea remains a staple in natural wellness traditions worldwide.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Echinacea.

Distribution

This plant is found across several distinct regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in Austria, Germany, and Poland. Moving toward the west, the species can also be located in Southwestern Europe within France. Additionally, its range extends into the Baltic States located in Eastern Europe. This diverse distribution demonstrates the plant's ability to thrive in various European environments.
