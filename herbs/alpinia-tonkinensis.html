Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is recorded in the Hainan province. It can also be found throughout the broader China Southeast region. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, the species is known to inhabit Vietnam. These locations define the primary natural range where this medicinal plant grows.

Plant Parts

This plant, Alpinia tonkinenensis, is a species within the Zingiberaceae family that is valued for its therapeutic potential. The most significant medicinal component of this species is the rhizome, which serves as the primary underground storage organ. This thickened, horizontal stem is harvested and processed to extract bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies. Because the rhizome acts as a nutrient reservoir for the plant, it often contains a concentrated density of essential oils and phytochemicals, making it the most potent part for medicinal applications.
