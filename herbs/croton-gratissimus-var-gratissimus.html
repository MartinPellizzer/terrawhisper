Croton gratissimus var. gratissimus

Croton gratissimus var. gratissimus, commonly known as the "wild croton," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
