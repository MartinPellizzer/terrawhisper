Swertia bifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Swertia bifolia?

Swertia bifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the taxonomic lineage of the order Gentianales. It is further categorized under the family Gentianaceae, falling into the genus Swertia, with the specific designation of the species bifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Swertia bifolia has opposite, decussate leaves that are typically elliptical to oblong in shape and possess entire margins. The plant develops as a woody shrub or small tree, often reaching heights of up to three meters. Its leaves are characterized by a smooth texture and a somewhat leathery consistency. The flowers are small and typically appear in axillary clusters, featuring a tubular structure. The fruit develops into a dry, woody capsule that eventually splits to release the seeds.
