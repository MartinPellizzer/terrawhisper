Nervilia plicata

Nervilia pliata, commonly known as the folded-leaf jewel orchid, is a fascinating terrestrial orchid native to tropical forests. Renowned for its distinct, pleated foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, highlighting its importance in regional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Nervilia plicata
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Nervilia, which includes the species Nervilia plicata.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Nervilia plicata, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is frequently called 毛叶芋兰, 紫花脉叶兰, or 紫花脈葉蘭, while also being known as 毛葉芋蘭 and 青天葵.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions within East Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and North-Central provinces. Its presence also extends to the island of Hainan and the Southeast coastal regions of the country. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed in the island territory of Taiwan. These diverse locations highlight the specific environmental preferences of the species across the region.
