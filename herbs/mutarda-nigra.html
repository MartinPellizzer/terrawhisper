Mutarda nigra

Mutarda nigra, commonly known as black mustard, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its potent bioactive compounds. Renowned for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, it has been used extensively in traditional medicine to support digestion and respiratory health, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Mutarda nigra
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe
