Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Brassica nigra (formerly Mustard nigra), utilizes its small, dark, and pungent seeds as a primary source of medicinal properties. These seeds contain high concentrations of glucosinolates, which react to form allyl isothiocyanate when crushed, providing potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. In various traditional practices, the seeds are often ground into a paste or used in poultices to relieve muscle pain and congestion.
