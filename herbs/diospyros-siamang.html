The physiology of Diospyros siamang is characterized by a metabolic framework governed by the C3 photosynthetic pathway, a trait shared by most woody dicotyledonous species. Within this pathway, carbon fixation is initiated by the enzyme RuBisCO, which reacts atmospheric carbon dioxide with ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate to produce 3-phosphoglycerate. Because it utilizes the C3 mechanism rather than C4 or CAM pathways, the plant's physiological efficiency is highly dependent on environmental conditions such as light intensity, temperature, and water availability, as the process is susceptible to photorespiration when stomatal conductance decreases.
