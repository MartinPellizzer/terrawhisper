Paederia yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Paederia yunnanensis?

Paederia yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Gentianaales. This plant is a member of the Rubiaceae family, specifically within the genus Paederia, and is identified by its unique species name, yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Paederia yunnanensis has woody, climbing stems with opposite, ovate to lanceolate leaves that possess entire margins and acute apices. The foliage is typically dark green and can reach several centimeters in length depending on the maturity of the plant. Small, tubular flowers emerge in axillary cymes, often displaying a pale color palette. These inflorescences are supported by slender pedicels that arise from the leaf axils. The plant also produces small, fleshy fruits that develop from the fertilized ovaries.
