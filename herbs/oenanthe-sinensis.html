Oenanthe sinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Oenanthe sinensis?

Oenanthe sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Apiales. The plant is a member of the family Apiaceae, specifically placed within the genus Oenanthe, with the species designation sinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Oenanthe sinensis has perennial, rhizomatous stems that produce lanceolate to oblong leaves with entire margins and an alternate arrangement. The foliage typically exhibits a bright green color and can reach significant lengths depending on the growing environment. Small, actinomorphic flowers emerge from the axils of the leaves, often displaying a delicate structure. These blooms are frequently arranged in clusters that follow the progression of the stem. The plant's root system is characterized by creeping rhizomes that allow it to spread effectively through soil.
