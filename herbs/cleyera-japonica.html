Sakaki, scientifically known as Cleyerra japonica, is an evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Deeply revered in Shinto traditions, it holds significant spiritual importance. Beyond its ritualistic use, this resilient plant is valued for its dense foliage and potential medicinal properties, making it a botanical treasure in cultural landscapes.
