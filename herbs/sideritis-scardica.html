Sideritis scardica

Sideritis scardica, commonly known as Mursalski tea, is a potent medicinal herb native to the Balkan Peninsula. Renowned for its exceptional antioxidant properties, this aromatic plant is traditionally used to support cardiovascular health, boost immunity, and alleviate digestive issues, making it a cornerstone of natural Balkan wellness traditions.

  • Scientific name: Sideritis scardica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Sideritis scardica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Sideritis.

This plant is primarily found across the diverse landscapes of Southeastern Europe. Within this region, it is frequently documented growing in Bulgaria.
