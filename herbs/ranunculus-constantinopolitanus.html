Ranunculus constantinopolitanus

Ranunculus constantinopolitanus, commonly known as the Constantino-polis buttercup, is a distinctive medicinal herb found in specific Mediterranean ecosystems. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, this plant has historically been studied for its bioactive compounds. Its therapeutic potential offers fascinating insights into traditional herbalism and modern pharmacological research applications.

  • Scientific name: Ranunculus constantinopolitanus
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae, falling under the genus Ranunculus.
