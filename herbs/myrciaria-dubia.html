Myrciaria dubia

Myrciaria dubia, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in tropical regions, is a plant valued for its diverse therapeutic potential. Often utilized in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds believed to possess antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. This species offers significant interest for pharmacological research and natural healing practices.
