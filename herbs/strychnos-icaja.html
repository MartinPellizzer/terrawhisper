Strychnos icaja

Strychnos icaja, commonly known as Icaja, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, it has been traditionally used by indigenous communities for various therapeutic purposes. This botanical species holds immense pharmacological interest due to its complex chemical profile and unique bioactive properties.
