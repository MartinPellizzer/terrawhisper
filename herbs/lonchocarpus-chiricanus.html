Lonchocarpus chiricanus

Lonchocarpus chiricanus, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds profound ethnobotanical importance. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its chemical properties and therapeutic potential offers valuable insights into developing new natural pharmacological treatments.
