Khaki-gum, scientifically known as Eucalyptus alba, is a distinctive medicinal plant revered for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to alleviate respiratory ailments, reduce inflammation, and act as a natural antiseptic, offering significant healing benefits through its aromatic leaves.

Plant Parts

This plant, Eucalyptus alba, derives much of its therapeutic value primarily from its leaves. These foliage components are rich in essential oils, such as eucalyptol, which are harvested to create medicinal preparations used for respiratory relief. Because the leaves contain these concentrated bioactive compounds, they serve as the most significant part of the plant for both pharmacological applications and traditional healing practices.
