Eucalyptus alba

Eucalyptus alba, commonly known as the White Eucalyptus, is a majestic evergreen tree native to Australia. Renowned for its aromatic, medicinal leaves, it contains essential oils with powerful antiseptic and decongestant properties. This resilient species is highly valued in traditional herbal medicine for treating respiratory ailments and promoting wellness.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus alba
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification concludes with the genus Eucalyptus.
