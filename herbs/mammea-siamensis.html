Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Calophyllaceae and falls under the genus Mammea.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the diverse landscapes of the Indo-China region. Its natural range encompasses several Southeast Asian nations, including Cambodia and Laos. It is also widely distributed throughout the territories of Myanmar and Thailand. Additionally, the species can be located within the borders of Vietnam. Together, these countries form the core geographical area where this medicinal plant thrives.
