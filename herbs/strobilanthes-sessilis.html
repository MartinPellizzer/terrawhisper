Strobilanthes sessilis

Strobilanthes sessilis, commonly known as Karunthali in certain regions, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional Ayurveda. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments, respiratory issues, and skin disorders. This plant remains a vital resource in herbal pharmacology and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Strobilanthes sessilis
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Strobilanthes.
