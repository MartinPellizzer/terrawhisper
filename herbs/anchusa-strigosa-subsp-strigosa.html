Anchusa strigosa subsp. strigosa

Anchusa strigosa subsp. strigosa, commonly known as Bugloss, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic potential. Native to Mediterranean regions, this subspecies contains pyrrolizidine alkaloids and bioactive compounds. Traditionally used to support liver health and inflammation, it remains a subject of significant botanical and pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Anchusa strigosa subsp. strigosa
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Boraginales, falling into the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Anchusa as the subspecies Anchusa strigosa subsp. strigosa.
