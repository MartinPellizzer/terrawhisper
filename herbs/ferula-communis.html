Ferula communis

Giant ferrel

Ferula communis, commonly known as giant fennel, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its impressive stature and yellow umbels, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices for its bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic benefits in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Ferula communis
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Ferula.
