Arbutus unedo

Mamkamla

Arbutus unedo, commonly known as the strawberry tree, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its distinctive red, bumpy fruits, it holds significant medicinal value. Historically, its leaves have been utilized in traditional remedies to treat urinary tract infections and support overall kidney health.

  • Scientific name: Arbutus unedo
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Arbutus, specifically the species Arbutus unedo.

Common Names
