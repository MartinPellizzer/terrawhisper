Mamkamla, scientifically known as Arbutus unedo, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Commonly called the Strawberry Tree, it is valued for its distinctive red berries and medicinal properties. Its leaves are traditionally used in herbal remedies to support urinary tract health and provide antioxidant benefits.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the strawberry tree, is a versatile medicinal species where the entire plant is often utilized for its therapeutic properties. The glossy, evergreen leaves are frequently employed in infusions and tinctures due to their astringent qualities. During its growing season, the plant produces clusters of small, wild berries that eventually mature into edible, sweet-tasting fruits. These fruits, while often used for culinary purposes, represent a key part of the plant's life cycle and overall botanical profile.
