Agrimonia eupatoria subsp. asiatica

Agrimonia eupatoria subsp. asiatica, commonly known as Eupatorium, is a resilient medicinal herb native to various Asian landscapes. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this subspecies is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat respiratory ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering a rich natural resource for holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Agrimonia eupatoria subsp. asiatica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Agrimonia as the subspecies asiatica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several key regions of Eurasia. In Eastern Europe, its presence is noted throughout East European Russia. The species also extends deep into Siberia, specifically inhabiting the Altay and West Siberia regions. Moving into Central Asia, the plant can be found growing within the borders of Kazakhstan. Finally, its range reaches into Kirgizstan, completing its presence across these diverse territories.
