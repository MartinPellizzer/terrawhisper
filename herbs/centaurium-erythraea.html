Centaurium erythraea

Centaurium erythraea, commonly known as Erythraean Centaury, is a perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism for its bitter properties. Renowned for supporting digestive health, it acts as a potent bitter tonic to stimulate appetite and bile production, making it a significant botanical tool for enhancing gastrointestinal function and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Centaurium erythraea
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Gentianaceae and the genus Centaurium. Specifically, its scientific name is Centaurium erythraeum.

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented in Denmark, Great Britain, and Ireland.
