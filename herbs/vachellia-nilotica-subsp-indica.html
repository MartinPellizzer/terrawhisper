Babul

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (9 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Babul, scientifically known as Vachellia nilotica subsp. indica, is a resilient, thorny acacia native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its extensive therapeutic properties, this medicinal plant offers various parts—including bark, gum, and leaves—used traditionally to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions within Ayurvedic medicine.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the African continent and its surrounding islands. In Northern Africa, its presence is noted within the country of Egypt. Moving towards the Atlantic, it can be found in the Macaronesian archipelago of Cape Verde. The species also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Finally, its range reaches Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is documented in both Eritrea and Ethiopia.
