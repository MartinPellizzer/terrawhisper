Andean walnut, scientifically known as Juglans neotropica, is a remarkable medicinal tree native to the high-altitude forests of the Andes. Valued for its nutrient-dense nuts and therapeutic bark, it offers significant antioxidant properties. This versatile species plays a vital role in traditional medicine and regional ecological biodiversity.
