Juglans neotropica

Juglans neotropica, commonly known as the Neotropical walnut, is a remarkable medicinal tree native to Central and South America. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. Its leaves and hulls are traditionally utilized for their antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties in various healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Juglans neotropica
  • Family: Juglandaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Fagales, it is further organized into the family Juglandaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Juglans.

This plant is primarily distributed across several distinct regions of the South American continent. In Northern South America, its presence is noted within the borders of Venezuela.
