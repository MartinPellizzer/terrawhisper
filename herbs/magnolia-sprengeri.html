Magnolia sprengeri

Magnolia sprengeri, commonly known as the Sprenger's Magnolia, is an exquisite ornamental evergreen prized for its fragrant, creamy-white blossoms. Originating from East Asia, this resilient shrub features glossy, leathery foliage, making it a versatile choice for temperate gardens and providing both aesthetic beauty and a calming, aromatic sensory experience.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia sprengeri
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Magnolia.
