Dendrobium flexicaule

Dendrobium flexicaule, commonly known as a species of orchid, holds significant value in traditional herbal medicine. Often utilized in various cultural practices, this plant is prized for its unique bioactive compounds. Understanding its botanical properties and therapeutic potential offers fascinating insights into the intersection of natural biodiversity and holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium flexicaule
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is specifically situated in the family Orchidaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its membership in the genus Dendrobium.

This plant, Dendrobium flexicaule, is known by several common names depending on the regional language and script used. In Chinese, it is referred to as 曲茎石斛 (zh), and specifically as 曲茎石斛 (zh-cn) and 曲茎石斛 (zh-hans) in Simplified Chinese. In Traditional Chinese, the name is written as 曲莖石斛 (zh-tw).
