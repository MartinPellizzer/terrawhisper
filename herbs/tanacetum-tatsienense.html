Tanacetum tatsienense

Tanacetum tatsienense, commonly known as Tansy, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds. Renowned for its aromatic leaves and yellow discoid flowers, this plant offers diverse therapeutic potential, particularly in addressing digestive issues and inflammatory conditions through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Tanacetum tatsienense
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Tanacetum.
