Enantia polycarpa, scientifically known as Annickia polycarpa, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including malaria, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions. This plant remains a vital subject for pharmacological research and ethnobotanical studies.
