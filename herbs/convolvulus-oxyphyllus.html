Convolvulus oxyphyllus

Convolvulus oxyphyllus, commonly known as the narrow-leaved bindweed, is a resilient medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its delicate foliage and bell-shaped blooms, this plant possesses unique bioactive compounds. It is often studied for its potential therapeutic properties in treating various inflammatory and skin-related conditions.

  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
