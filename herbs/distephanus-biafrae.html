Distephanus biafrae

Distephanus biafrae, commonly known as the Biafra plant, is a significant medicinal herb native to West Africa. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including inflammatory conditions and infections. Exploring its phytochemical profile offers promising insights into modern natural drug development.
