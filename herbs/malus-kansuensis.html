Malus kansuensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Malus kansuensis?

Malus kansuensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, it falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement within the genus Malus, specifically as the species kansuenis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Malus kansuenensis has small, ovate to elliptical leaves with serrated margins and a woody, spreading growth habit characterized by multi-stemmed branches. During the flowering season, the plant produces delicate white to pale pink blossoms that emerge from short spurs. These flowers are followed by small, globose pomes that transition from green to a yellowish or reddish hue upon ripening. The bark on mature specimens becomes grayish and develops a somewhat rough texture over time.
