What's the taxonomical classification of Cardamine trifoliolata?

Cardamine trifoliolata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the hierarchical structure of plant taxonomy, it falls under the order Brassicales and is a member of the family Brassicaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Cardamine, and its specific species name, trifolioata.
