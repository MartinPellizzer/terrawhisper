Solanum aculeatissimum

Solanum aculeaatisimum, commonly known as the needle-leaved nightshade, is a distinctive medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its unique elongated leaves, this species is utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, offering potential benefits in treating various ailments through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Solanum aculeatissimum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Europe

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents and climatic zones. In Middle Europe, its presence is specifically noted within Germany. The species also extends to the Macaronesian region, where it can be found in Cape Verde. Furthermore, it is widely distributed across West Tropical Africa. Within this African region, its distribution includes the countries of Burkina, Ghana, and Guinea.
