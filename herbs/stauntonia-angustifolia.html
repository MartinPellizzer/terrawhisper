Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and North-Central territories. Its presence is also documented in the Southeast regions of China as well as the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Beyond the Chinese borders, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it is known to grow within the Assam region.

The root system is the most significant part of the plant used in traditional herbal preparations, often being harvested to create decoctions or extracts. Because the active compounds are concentrated within this underground structure, the root serves as the essential component for those seeking to utilize the plant's therapeutic potential.
