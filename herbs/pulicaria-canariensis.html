Pulicaria canariensis

Pulicaria canariensis, commonly known as Canary Fleabane, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Macaronesian islands. Renowned for its delicate yellow blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in regional folk medicine for its potential antiseptic properties and its ability to support digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Pulicaria canariensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Pulicaria.

This plant is a specialized species with a very restricted natural range. It is primarily found within the Macaronesia region, specifically occurring in the Canary Islands. Because of this limited scope, its presence is highly localized to certain volcanic landscapes. The distribution highlights its status as an endemic element of the Atlantic archipelago.
