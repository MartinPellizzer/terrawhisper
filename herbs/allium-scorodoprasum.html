Allium scorodoprasum

Sand leek

Allium scorodoprasum, commonly known as wild garlic or ramsons, is a perennial herb cherished for its potent aroma and medicinal properties. Rich in sulfur compounds and antioxidants, it supports cardiovascular health and immune function. This versatile plant serves as both a nutrient-dense culinary ingredient and a traditional natural remedy.

  • Scientific name: Allium scorodoprasum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it falls under the family Amaryllidaceae. It is scientifically identified by its genus name, Allium.
