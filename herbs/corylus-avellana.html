Plant Parts

This plant, Corylus avellana, commonly known as the hazel, offers various components that can be utilized for different purposes. The shells, which protect the nutritious kernels, are often valued for their high antioxidant content and can be used in traditional applications or as a source of tannins. Additionally, the leaves are frequently employed in herbal preparations, as they possess astringent properties that are often utilized to treat skin inflammations or digestive issues.
