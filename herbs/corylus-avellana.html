Corylus avellana

Corylus avellana, commonly known as the hazel shrub, is a versatile plant prized for both its nutritious nuts and medicinal properties. Historically utilized in traditional herbalism, it contains bioactive compounds with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory potential, offering therapeutic benefits for skin health, digestive support, and various systemic wellness applications.

  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Europe
