Bugle weed, scientifically known as Ajuga reptans, is a perennial groundcover prized for its lush, creeping habit and vibrant foliage. Traditionally used in folk medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties, this hardy plant serves as both an ornamental garden staple and a resilient botanical healer.
