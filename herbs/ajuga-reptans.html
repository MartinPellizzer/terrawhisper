Ajuga reptans

Ajuga reptans, commonly known as bugleweed, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its spreading habit and decorative foliage. Traditionally used in folk medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, this hardy plant thrives in moist environments, offering both aesthetic charm for gardens and historical significance in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Ajuga reptans
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ajuga, specifically as the species Ajuga reptans.
