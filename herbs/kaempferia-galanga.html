Kaempferia galanga

Kencur

Kaempferiia galanga, commonly known as aromatic ginger, is a perennial herb native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its pungent, spicy rhizomes, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Kaempferia galanga
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, specifically being a member of the family Zingiberaceae. Within this botanical hierarchy, it is categorized under the genus Kaempferia.
