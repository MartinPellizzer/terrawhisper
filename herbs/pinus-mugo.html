Pinus mugo

Pinus mugo, commonly known as the Mountain Pine, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude regions of Europe. Renowned for its hardiness, it produces nutrient-rich resin and essential oils. Traditionally, its extracts have been utilized in herbal medicine to support respiratory health and alleviate various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Pinus mugo
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales and family Pinaceae, it is classified under the genus Pinus. Specifically, Pinus mugo represents a distinct species within this botanical hierarchy.
