Glory Lily

Gloriosa superba · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (208 studies) · ★★★★★

Glory Lily, scientifically known as Gloriosa superbba, is a striking climbing plant renowned for its vibrant, flame-like petals. Native to tropical regions, it contains potent alkaloids like colchicine. While highly valued in traditional medicine for treating various ailments, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling and precise medicinal application.
