Gloriosa superba

Glory lily

Gloriosa superbba, commonly known as the Flame Lily, is a striking climbing perennial celebrated for its brilliant, fiery blooms. While highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine for treating various ailments, it contains potent alkaloids like colchicine, necessitating extreme caution due to its high toxicity if handled or consumed incorrectly.

  • Scientific name: Gloriosa superba
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Colchicaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gloriosa.
