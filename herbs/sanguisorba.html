Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the northern regions of Europe. Its presence is documented across several island nations, including Great Britain and Ireland. Furthermore, the species can be found in the northern territories of Denmark and Finland. The geographical range even extends to the remote landscapes of Iceland. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's preference for Northern European climates.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Sanguisorbia or Bloodwort, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its various structures, although the most significant component used in traditional remedies is typically the root or rhizome. While the plant produces a seed as part of its natural reproductive cycle to ensure the continuation of the species, these seeds are generally not the focus of its therapeutic applications. Instead, practitioners look to the chemical compounds stored within the plant's vascular tissues to achieve the desired healing effects.
