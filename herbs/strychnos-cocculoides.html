Corky-bark monkey-orange, scientifically known as Strychnos cocculoides, is a distinctive shrub native to African savannas. Renowned for its thick, textured bark and bright orange fruits, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices to treat ailments ranging from skin conditions to digestive issues.
