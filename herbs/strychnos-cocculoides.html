Strychnos cocculoides

Strychnos cocculoides, commonly known as the wild strychnine plant, is a woody climber native to various African tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized for their potent pharmacological properties, primarily targeting neurological and inflammatory conditions, though its high alkaloid content requires extremely cautious application.

  • Scientific name: Strychnos cocculoides
  • Family: Loganiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianales and the family Loganiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Strychnos.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found in Burundi, Congo, and Gabon. Its presence is also noted within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Additionally, the species extends its range into East Tropical Africa. Specifically, it has been documented growing in Kenya.
