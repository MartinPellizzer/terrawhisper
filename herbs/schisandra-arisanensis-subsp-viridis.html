Schisandra arisanensis subsp. viridis

Schisandra arisanensis subsp. viridis, commonly known as a rare subspecies of Schisandra, is a medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers potent antioxidant and adaptogenic properties. This botanical treasure is increasingly studied for its potential to support neurological health and stress resilience.
