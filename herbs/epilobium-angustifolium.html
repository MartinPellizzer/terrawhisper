Red fireweed, scientifically known as Epilobium angustifolium, is a resilient perennial renowned for its vibrant magenta blooms. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, this plant holds significant medicinal value, traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin irritations, and digestive issues. Its nutrient-rich properties make it a versatile treasure in herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as narrow-leaf willowherb, is utilized in herbal medicine primarily through its various structures. The leaves are typically narrow and lanceolate, containing the bioactive compounds sought for their therapeutic properties. During its blooming season, the plant produces delicate, often pink-hued flowers that attract pollinators and signify the plant's maturity. In many traditional preparations, the entire aerial part of the plant is harvested, encompassing the stems, leaves, and blossoms to ensure a complete profile of its medicinal constituents.
