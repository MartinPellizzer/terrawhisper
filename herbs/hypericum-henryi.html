Hypericum henryi

Hypericum henryi, commonly known as Henry's St. John's Wort, is a charming perennial shrub prized for its delicate, star-shaped yellow blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in lush gardens, this species belongs to a storied genus often studied for its bioactive properties and potential roles in traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum henryi
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is further categorized into the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Hypericum.

Its presence extends into the Indo-China region, where it can be found in Myanmar, Thailand, and Vietnam.
