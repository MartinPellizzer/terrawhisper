Labourdonnaisia glauca

Labourdonnaisia glauca, commonly known as the Blue-flowered Labourdonnaisia, is a distinctive evergreen shrub native to South Africa. Renowned for its striking, pale blue blossoms, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine. Its unique properties offer fascinating insights into the diverse botanical heritage of the Cape Floristic Region.

  • Scientific name: Labourdonnaisia glauca
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is classified within the family Sapotaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Labourdonnaisia.
