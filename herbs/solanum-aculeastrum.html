Solanum aculeastrum

Solanum aculeastrum, commonly known as the prickly nightshade, is a significant medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin infections. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for modern therapeutic developments and holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Solanum aculeastrum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Solanum. As Solanum aculeastrum, it represents a distinct species within this complex botanical hierarchy.
