Kaempferia

Kaempferia, commonly known as medicinal ginger, belongs to the Zingiberaceae family. These low-growing herbs are celebrated for their aromatic rhizomes and striking foliage. Rich in bioactive compounds, they offer significant therapeutic potential, particularly in traditional medicine for treating inflammation, digestive issues, and various ailments through their potent antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Kaempferia
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, it is specifically situated in the family Zingiberaceae. Its taxonomic identity is defined by its classification within the genus Kaempferia.
