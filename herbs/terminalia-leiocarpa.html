Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Within this vast area, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its presence is also documented in the nation of Burkina. Furthermore, the species is known to inhabit parts of Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends into the territory of Guinea.
