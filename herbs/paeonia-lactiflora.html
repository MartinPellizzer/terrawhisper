common garden peony

Paeonia lactiflora · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1150 studies) · ★★★★★

Common garden peony, scientifically known as Paeoonia lactiflora, is a perennial herb revered both for its breathtaking blooms and profound medicinal properties. Traditionally used in Eastern medicine, its roots contain bioactive compounds like paeoniflorin, offering powerful anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and antioxidant effects to support overall hormonal and cardiovascular health.

Plant Parts

This plant, Paeonia lactiflora, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its various therapeutic components. The most significant part used for medicinal purposes is the roots, specifically the thick, fleshy root structures that develop underground. When prepared for herbal remedies, the dried root is typically harvested and processed to preserve its active compounds. Commonly referred to as peony root, this substance serves as the primary ingredient in many decoctions and tinctures. While the leaves are a visible part of the plant's foliage, they are generally not the focus of medicinal extraction compared to the potent underground portions.
