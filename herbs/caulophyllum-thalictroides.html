Caulophyllum thalictroides

Blue cohosh

Caulophyllum thalictroides, commonly known as Blue Cohosh, is a perennial herb native to North America. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, it has been used to support reproductive health and regulate menstrual cycles. This botanical species is characterized by its distinct blue berries and delicate, compound foliage found in woodlands.

  • Scientific name: Caulophyllum thalictroides
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ranunculales, it is further nested within the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified under the genus Caulophyllum.
