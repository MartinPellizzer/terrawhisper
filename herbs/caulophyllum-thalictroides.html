Blue cohosh, scientifically known as Caulophyllum thalictroides, is a perennial herb native to North American forests. Often distinguished by its striking blue-tinted stems, this botanical species has been historically valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly regarding reproductive health and its potential anti-inflammatory effects.
