Scaevola spinescens

Scaevola spinescens, commonly known as the prickly scaevola, is a resilient coastal shrub prized in traditional medicine. Native to tropical regions, this plant possesses significant therapeutic potential. Its bioactive compounds are frequently utilized to treat various ailments, showcasing the remarkable healing properties found within its unique, thorny botanical structure.

  • Scientific name: Scaevola spinescens
  • Family: Goodeniaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Scaevola spinescens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Goodeniaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Scaevola.

Common Names

This plant, Scaevola spinescens, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Prickly Scaevola, while in Indonesian, it is commonly called Daun Jarak or Daun Jarak Sembung. Additionally, it is known as Scaevola berduri in Malay.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several different regions of Australia. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of New South Wales. Its presence extends northward into the tropical landscapes of the Northern Territory. Additionally, the species inhabits significant portions of Queensland. Finally, the plant's range reaches further south into South Australia and Victoria.
