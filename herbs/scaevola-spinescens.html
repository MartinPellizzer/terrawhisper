Spiny Fan-flower, scientifically known as Scaevola spinescens, is a resilient coastal shrub prized in traditional medicine. Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several different regions of Australia. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of New South Wales. Its presence extends northward into the tropical landscapes of the Northern Territory. Additionally, the species inhabits significant portions of Queensland.
