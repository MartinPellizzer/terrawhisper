Croton schiedeanus

Croton schiedeanus, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in Central American traditional medicine, holds remarkable therapeutic potential. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. Ongoing ethnobotanical research continues to explore its diverse pharmacological properties and potential pharmaceutical applications.
