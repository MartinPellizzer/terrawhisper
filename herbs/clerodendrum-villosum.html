Clerodendrum villosum

Clerodendrum villosum, commonly known as the "creeping clerodendrum," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and stems possess potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Clerodendrum villosum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic details place it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, specifically within the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Clerodendrum.
