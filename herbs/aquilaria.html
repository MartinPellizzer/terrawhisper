Lign-aloes trees, scientifically known as Aquilaria, is a genus of evergreen trees renowned for producing agarwood. This precious, fragrant resin forms when the tree responds to fungal infections. Highly valued in traditional medicine and perfumery, Aquilaria species are cornerstone elements of ancient healing practices and luxury fragrance industries.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central areas as well as the Southeast. It can also be found growing on the island of Hainan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in the state of Assam. Finally, its range extends into the neighboring territory of Bangladesh.
