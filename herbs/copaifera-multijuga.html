Brazilian copaiba, scientifically known as Copaifera multijuga, is a powerhouse of Amazonian biodiversity. Renowned for its oleoresin, this medicinal plant offers potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties. Deeply embedded in traditional medicine, it serves as a natural remedy for skin ailments and respiratory issues, bridging ancient wisdom with pharmacology.
