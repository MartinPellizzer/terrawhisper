Brazilian copaiba, scientifically known as Copaifera multijuga, is a powerhouse of Amazonian biodiversity. Renowned for its oleoresin, this medicinal plant offers potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties. Deeply embedded in traditional medicine, it serves as a natural remedy for skin ailments and respiratory issues, bridging ancient wisdom with pharmacology.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of South America. Plant Parts

This plant, Copaifera multijuga, is a significant source of valuable therapeutic substances, most notably derived from its trunk. The primary medicinal component used is the oleoresin, an exudate that is harvested by tapping the bark of the tree. This viscous, aromatic liquid is rich in bioactive compounds, including diterpenes and sesquiterpenes, which provide the plant with its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties.
