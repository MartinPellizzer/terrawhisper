Copaifera multijuga

Copaifera multijuga, commonly known as Copaiba, is a versatile medicinal tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its fragrant oleoresin, this plant provides a powerful natural remedy. Its oil is widely utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties, serving as a cornerstone in traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Copaifera multijuga
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Copaifera, with its species name being multijuga.

Plant Parts

This plant, Copaifera multijuga, is a significant source of valuable therapeutic substances, most notably derived from its trunk. The primary medicinal component used is the oleoresin, an exudate that is harvested by tapping the bark of the tree. This viscous, aromatic liquid is rich in bioactive compounds, including diterpenes and sesquiterpenes, which provide the plant with its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties.
