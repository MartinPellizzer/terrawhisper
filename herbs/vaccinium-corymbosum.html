Smooth highbush blueberry, scientifically known as Vaccinium corymbosum, is a versatile perennial shrub prized for its nutrient-dense fruit. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and dietary fiber, these berries offer significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory properties and improved cognitive function, making them a cornerstone of both culinary and medicinal traditions.
