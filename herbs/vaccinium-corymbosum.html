Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and dietary fiber, these berries offer significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory properties and improved cognitive function, making them a cornerstone of both culinary and medicinal traditions.

Plant Parts

This plant, scientifically known as Vaccinium corymbosum, is most widely recognized for its nutrient-dense fruit, which serves as the primary source for various health-promoting applications. While the leaves and stems possess botanical significance, the most common medicinal and nutritional derivative utilized by humans is the blueberry juice. Extracted from the ripe berries, this liquid is rich in anthocyanins and antioxidants, which are frequently studied for their ability to support cardiovascular health and reduce oxidative stress in the body.
