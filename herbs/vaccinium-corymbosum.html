Vaccinium corymbosum

Vaccinium corymbosum, commonly known as the highbush blueberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Rich in anthocyanins and vitamins, its berries support cardiovascular health, reduce inflammation, and enhance cognitive function. This nutrient-dense plant offers significant therapeutic potential in modern nutritional and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vaccinium corymbosum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, falling into the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Vaccinium.
