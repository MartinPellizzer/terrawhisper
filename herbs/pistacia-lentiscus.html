Lentisk

Pistacia lentiscus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (514 studies) · ★★★★★

Lentisk, scientifically known as Pistacia lentiscus, is an evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it has been used traditionally to treat digestive issues, skin inflammations, and respiratory ailments. Its aromatic resins and bioactive compounds offer significant antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits in herbal medicine.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant, known as Pistacia lentiscus, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing natively in the Baleares islands. Additionally, the species is present in the territories of Corse and France. Its range further extends to the coastal areas of Portugal. Finally, the plant is also well-distributed throughout Sardegna.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as mastic, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The most valued part of the plant is the aromatic resins, which are harvested as a thick, sticky substance known as mastic gum. Beyond these resin exudates, the medicinal profile of the species extends to its evergreen leaves, which are often studied for their antioxidant qualities. Additionally, the woody structures, including the twigs and the main stem, contribute to the plant's overall biological makeup and are sometimes integrated into botanical studies regarding its essential oil content.
