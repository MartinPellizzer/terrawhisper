Pistacia lentiscus

Pistacia lentiscus, commonly known as mastic tree, is an evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its aromatic resin, known as mastic, this plant has been used for centuries in traditional medicine. It possesses powerful antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, supporting digestive health and skin vitality.

  • Scientific name: Pistacia lentiscus
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Sapindaales and the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation concludes with the genus Pistacia.

Distribution

This plant, known as Pistacia lentiscus, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing natively in the Baleares islands. Additionally, the species is present in the territories of Corse and France. Its range further extends to the coastal areas of Portugal. Finally, the plant is also well-distributed throughout Sardegna.
