Lentisk

Pistacia lentiscus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (514 studies) · ★★★★★

Lentisk, scientifically known as Pistacia lentiscus, is an evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it has been used traditionally to treat digestive issues, skin inflammations, and respiratory ailments. Its aromatic resins and bioactive compounds offer significant antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits in herbal medicine.

Distribution

This plant, known as Pistacia lentiscus, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing natively in the Baleares islands. Additionally, the species is present in the territories of Corse and France. Its range further extends to the coastal areas of Portugal. The reproduction of Pistacia lentiscus is characterized by a dioecious sexual reproduction system, meaning individual plants are either male or female. Dispersal is achieved through a zoochorous syndrome, relying on animals for seed distribution.
