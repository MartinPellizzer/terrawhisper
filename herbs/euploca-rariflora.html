Euploca rariflora

Euploca rariflora, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional practices, belongs to the Acanthaceae family. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, this plant is frequently utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Its unique phytochemical profile makes it a subject of growing interest in pharmacological research.
