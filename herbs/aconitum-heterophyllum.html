Himalayan monkshood

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (49 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Himalayan monkshood, scientifically known as Aconitum heterophyllum, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine as "Atis," it possesses potent bioactive alkaloids. While highly toxic if mishandled, it is revered for its therapeutic potential in treating fevers and digestive ailments.
