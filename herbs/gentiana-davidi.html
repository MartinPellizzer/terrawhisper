Gentiana davidi

What's the taxonomical classification of Gentiana davidi?

Gentiana davidi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic descent, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Gentianales, it falls under the family Gentianaceae, which is characterized by its specific floral structures. Ultimately, the plant is categorized under the genus Gentiana, with its specific identification being the species davidi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Gentiana davidi has erect, perennial stems that reach heights of 10 to 30 centimeters and bear opposite, lanceolate leaves with entire margins. The foliage is typically a bright green color and possesses a smooth texture. Its most striking feature is the trumpet-shaped flowers which bloom in shades of deep blue or violet. These blossoms are characterized by five distinct corolla lobes and a prominent, pale throat. The plant also develops small, capsule-like seed pods following successful pollination.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is native to the high-altitude regions of western China, specifically within the provinces of Sichuan, Yunnan, and Tibet. It thrives primarily in alpine and subalpine meadows located at elevations ranging from 2,500 to 4,000 meters. The species prefers moist, rocky slopes and grassy habitats where sunlight is abundant. Its distribution is closely tied to the rugged terrain of the Hengduan Mountains and the eastern edge of the Tibetan Plateau. These specific environmental conditions dictate the limited geographic range of the species.
