Asparagus dumosus

Asparagus dumosus, commonly known as the dwarf asparagus, is a resilient, perennial shrub valued for its medicinal properties. Native to various African regions, this plant is traditionally used in herbal remedies to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Asparagus dumosus
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asparagaceae and the genus Asparagus.
