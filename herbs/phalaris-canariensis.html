Phalaris canariensis

Canary grass

Phalaris canarienensis, commonly known as Canary Canary grass, is a perennial plant native to the Canary Islands. While primarily valued in landscaping and forage, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Its unique chemical properties and traditional uses make it a subject of study within the diverse realm of medicinal flora.

  • Scientific name: Phalaris canariensis
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales. Finally, it is a member of the family Poaceae and falls under the genus Phalaris.
