This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found specifically within the Chita region. Its presence extends into the Russian Far East, where it inhabits the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye areas. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow within the Inner Mongolia region of China. Together, these locations define the natural range of this medicinal species.
