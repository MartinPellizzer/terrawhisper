Angel's Trumpet

Brugmansia arborea · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (25 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Angel's Trumpet, scientifically known as Brugmansia arborea, is a striking tropical shrub famed for its large, pendulous, bell-shaped flowers. While celebrated for its ornamental beauty and intoxicating fragrance, it is highly potent. Containing powerful tropane alkaloids, this plant requires extreme caution due to its significant toxicity and hallucinogenic properties.
