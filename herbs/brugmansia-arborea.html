Brugmansia arborea

Brugmansia arborea, commonly known as Angel's Trumpet, is a striking tropical shrub renowned for its large, pendulous, bell-shaped flowers. While celebrated for its ornamental beauty, it contains potent tropane alkaloids. These compounds make the plant highly toxic, requiring extreme caution as they possess powerful hallucinogenic and medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Brugmansia arborea
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Solanales and is a member of the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Brugmansia.
