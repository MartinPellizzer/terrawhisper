Vernonia britteniana

Vernonia brittenianana, commonly known as brittle ironweed, is a resilient perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Distinguished by its striking purple florets and sturdy stems, this plant plays a vital role in local ecosystems. Historically, various species of Vernonia have been utilized in traditional medicine for their therapeutic properties.
