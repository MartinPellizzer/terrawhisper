Arum palestinum, scientifically known as Arum palaestinum, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its unique spadix and spathe, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used in traditional medicine, it requires careful handling due to the presence of irritating calcium oxalate crystals.
