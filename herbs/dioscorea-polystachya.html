Chinese yam

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (41 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Chinese yam, scientifically known as Dioscorea polystachya, is a versatile medicinal plant revered in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its nutritional density, this tuberous root is used to tonify the spleen, nourish the kidneys, and replenish vital energy. It serves as a cornerstone in holistic wellness and dietary therapy.
