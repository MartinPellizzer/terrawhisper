Dioscorea polystachya

Chinese yam

Dioscorea polystachya, commonly known as the many-stemmed yam, is a versatile medicinal plant found in various tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this tuberous herb is used in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea polystachya
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Dioscoreales and the family Dioscoreaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Dioscorea as Dioscorea polysachya.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across several distinct regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is noted in both the Kuril Islands and the Primorye region. Moving into China, the species is widely distributed throughout the South-Central territories. Furthermore, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Inner Mongolia. Finally, its range extends into the North-Central parts of China, completing its diverse distribution pattern.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Dioscorea polysachya, is a climbing herb where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The most significant part is the starchy tuber, which is harvested and processed to obtain a potent extract used in traditional medicine. While the aerial parts, including the stems and leaves, are often studied for their botanical characteristics, it is the underground storage organ that holds the highest medicinal value. After the initial processing of the tubers, the remaining residue may be examined to ensure the efficiency of the extraction process.
