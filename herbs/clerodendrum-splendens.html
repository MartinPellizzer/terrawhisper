Life history

The life history of Clerodendrum splendens is characterized by its classification as a phanerophyte, specifically exhibiting the growth habits of both a nanophanerophyte and a general phanerophyte. As a phanerophyte, its perennating buds are elevated well above the ground surface, protected by its stem structure, which allows it to persist through varying environmental conditions. Depending on the specific ecological niche and the height of its woody structural development, it can function as a nanophanerophyte, maintaining a relatively low stature while still keeping its buds above the soil level, or as a more robust phanerophyte. This life form ensures that its regenerative organs remain safe from surface-level disturbances, facilitating its long-term survival and establishment within its native habitat.

The morphology of Clerodendrum splendens is characterized by its growth form as a woody shrub, exhibiting a robust and structured habit.
