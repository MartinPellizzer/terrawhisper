Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the mountain firethorn, is primarily valued for its decorative and functional qualities, particularly its vibrant fruit. These small, berry-like pomes grow in dense, colorful clusters that transition from green to brilliant shades of red or orange as they ripen. While the foliage and thorny stems are defining features of its structure, it is the fleshy fruit that serves as a significant focal point for both wildlife and ornamental landscaping.
