Ardisia mamillaata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving through the higher taxonomic ranks, it falls under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its genus, Ardisia, and its specific species designation, mamillata.

According to a study published by "Natural product research", triterpene saponins are identified in the extract of Ardisia mamillata Hance (AMH). The research utilizes a high-performance liquid chromatography coupled to electrospray ionisation and quadrupole time-of-flight mass spectrometry (HPLC-ESI-QTOF-MS/MS) method to separate and identify these components from the plant. Through the use of the predominant ion [M + HCOO]- in negative ion mode, 30 triterpene saponins were identified or tentatively identified in the plant extract. Out of the total identified, 17 triterpene saponins are classified as new compounds.
