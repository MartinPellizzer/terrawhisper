Ghaap, scientifically known as Hoodia gordonii, is a succulent plant native to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Historically used by San people to suppress hunger during long hunts, it has gained global attention for its potential appetite-suppressing properties, making it a significant subject in natural weight-management research.

Distribution

This plant is indigenous to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Its primary distribution can be found within the Cape Provinces, where it thrives in specific desert climates. Additionally, the species is widely spread across the landscape of Namibia. It also maintains a presence within the Free State region of the continent. Consequently, its habitat is concentrated in these specific southern territories.
