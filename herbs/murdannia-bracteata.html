What's the taxonomical classification of Murdannia bracteata?

Murdannia bracteata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it in the order Commelinaales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Commelinaceae, specifically falling under the genus Murdannia, with the species designation bracteata.
