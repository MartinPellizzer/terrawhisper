Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Trapa natans var. bispinosa, is characterized by its unique reproductive structures that serve various medicinal and nutritional purposes. The most prominent part used is the fruit, which is a woody, nut-like structure often equipped with spines for protection. Within this protective casing, the seeds develop into a highly nutritious water chestnut kernel. These fresh nuts are harvested during the growing season and are prized for their specific properties. Whether consumed as whole fruits or processed into the starchy water chestnut kernel, the different stages of the plant's reproductive cycle provide essential components used in traditional applications.
