Trapa natans var. bispinosa

Trapa natans var. bispinosa, commonly known as the water caltrop, is a unique aquatic perennial valued for its diverse medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential within the realms of herbal and traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Trapa natans var. bispinosa
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Africa
