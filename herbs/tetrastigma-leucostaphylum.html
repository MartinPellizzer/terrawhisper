Tetrastigma leucostaphylum

Tetrastigma leucostaphylum, commonly known as a vital medicinal climber, is an intriguing species found in tropical forest ecosystems. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological potential, this plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional healing.
