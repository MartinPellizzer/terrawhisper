Phytolacca

Pokeberry

Phytolacca, commonly known as pokeweed, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to North America. Renowned for its striking purple stems and berries, it holds a complex medicinal legacy. While used traditionally for various ailments, its potent compounds require careful handling due to the plant's inherent toxicity if consumed improperly.
