Ficus nervosa

Ficus nervosa, commonly known as the Nervosa Fig, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Moraceae family, this species is recognized for its robust foliage and bioactive compounds, which are traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to support overall wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Ficus nervosa
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales and the family Moraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Ficus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it is widely documented across the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Its presence also extends to the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Furthermore, the species can be found growing in the Assam region of the Indian Subcontinent. Collectively, these locations highlight its preference for specific subtropical habitats across the continent.
