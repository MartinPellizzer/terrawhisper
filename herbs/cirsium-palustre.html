Marsh thistle, scientifically known as Cirsium palustre, is a perennial herb found in damp, marshy habitats. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it possesses diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties. While primarily noted for its ecological role, its bioactive compounds offer intriguing potential for supporting digestive health and managing systemic inflammation.
