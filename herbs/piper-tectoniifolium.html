Piper tectoniifolium

Piper tectoniifolium, commonly known as the Teakleaf Pepper, is a versatile medicinal climber valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it possesses potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties. This plant serves as a vital natural resource for treating various ailments, particularly within Ayurvedic practices.

  • Scientific name: Piper tectoniifolium
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
