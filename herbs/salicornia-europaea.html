Common glasswort, scientifically known as Salicornia europaea, is a succulent halophyte thriving in saline coastal environments. Often used in traditional medicine to manage inflammation and edema, this resilient plant is rich in bioactive compounds. It serves as a vital component in studying salt tolerance and specialized coastal ecosystems.

Plant Parts

This plant, Salicornia europaea, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties through the collection of its aerial parts. These above-ground structures consist of succulent, jointed stems and small, scale-like leaves that are adapted to saline environments. Because the therapeutic compounds are concentrated within these fleshy green segments, the aerial parts are typically harvested to prepare various extracts or traditional remedies.
