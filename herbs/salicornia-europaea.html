Salicornia europaea

Common glasswort

Salicornia europaea, commonly known as glasswort or marsh samphire, is a succulent halophyte thriving in saline coastal environments. Renowned for its salt-tolerant properties, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its antioxidant potential and diuretic effects, offering unique therapeutic benefits derived from its specialized adaptation to extreme habitats.

  • Scientific name: Salicornia europaea
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is situated in the family Amaranthaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Salicornia.
