Erect prickly-pear, scientifically known as Opuntiya stricta, is a resilient succulent native to subtropical regions. Renowned for its fleshy, paddle-like cladodes and defensive spines, this plant offers diverse medicinal benefits. It is traditionally utilized for its antioxidant properties, aiding in skin healing, digestive health, and managing inflammation.
