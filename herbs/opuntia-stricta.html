Opuntia stricta

Erect prickly-pear

Opuntia stricta, commonly known as the prickly pear cactus, is a resilient succulent valued for its diverse medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, manage blood sugar levels, and reduce inflammation. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential in modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Opuntia stricta
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is specifically positioned in the family Cactaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Opuntia, specifically the species Opuntia stricta.
