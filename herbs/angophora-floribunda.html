Angophora floribunda

Angophora floribunda, commonly known as the Broad-leaved Angophora, is a distinctive Australian eucalypt prized for its striking, creamy-white flower clusters. Found primarily in coastal and sclerophyll forests, this resilient species serves as a vital ecological cornerstone, providing nectar for pollinators and structural beauty to its native woodland habitats.

  • Family: Myrtaceae
