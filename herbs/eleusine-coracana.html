Finger millet, scientifically known as Eleusine coracanha, is a highly nutritious cereal grain celebrated for its exceptional health benefits. Rich in calcium, dietary fiber, and essential antioxidants, it serves as a vital dietary staple in many tropical regions, offering significant medicinal properties to support bone health and manage diabetes.
