Physiology

The physiology of Eleusine coracana is characterized by a highly efficient metabolic framework, most notably its utilization of the C4 photosynthetic pathway. This specialized carbon fixation mechanism allows the plant to concentrate carbon dioxide within bundle sheath cells, significantly reducing photorespiration and enabling efficient biomass production even under high temperatures and intense light intensities. Furthermore, regarding its nutrient acquisition strategies, Eleusine coracana is not a nitrogen fixer; it lacks the symbiotic relationship with nitrogen-fixing bacteria required to convert atmospheric nitrogen into usable forms, meaning it relies primarily on the uptake of nitrates and ammonium from the soil to meet its nitrogen requirements for protein synthesis and growth.
