Eleusine coracana

Finger millet

Eleusine coracanana, commonly known as finger millet, is a highly nutritious cereal grain valued for its medicinal properties. Rich in calcium, iron, and essential amino acids, it supports bone health and manages diabetes. Traditionally used in various cultures, this resilient superfood offers significant dietary benefits and promotes overall metabolic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Eleusine coracana
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Poales, and the family Poaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Eleusine.
