Crataegus pinnatifida

Crataegus pinnatifida, commonly known as the Chinese Hawthorn, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Rich in bioactive flavonoids and organic acids, its fruit is widely used to support cardiovascular health, aid digestion, and regulate blood sugar, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus pinnatifida
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Crataegus, specifically identified by the species Crataegus pinnatifida.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across Northern Asia and parts of East Asia. In the region of Siberia, its presence is specifically noted within West Siberia. Moving toward the Russian Far East, the species can be found in the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Additionally, its habitat extends into China, where it is located in Inner Mongolia. These diverse locations highlight the broad ecological range of Crataegus pinnatifida.
