Mountain hawthorn, scientifically known as Crataegus pinnatifida, is a versatile medicinal plant deeply rooted in traditional Eastern medicine. Renowned for its cardioprotective properties, it is primarily used to support heart health, improve circulation, and manage blood pressure. Its nutrient-rich fruits offer significant antioxidant benefits for overall cardiovascular wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across Northern Asia and parts of East Asia. In the region of Siberia, its presence is specifically noted within West Siberia. Moving toward the Russian Far East, the species can be found in the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Additionally, its habitat extends into China, where it is located in Inner Mongolia. These diverse locations highlight the broad ecological range of Crataegus pinnatifida.

Plant Parts

This plant, Crataegus pinnatifida, is widely recognized in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties. The most significant part used for medicinal purposes is the fruits, which are often harvested when ripe to ensure maximum potency. When prepared for consumption or clinical use, the fruit extract is frequently utilized due to its high concentration of bioactive compounds like flavonoids and polyphenols. Whether consumed as a whole fruit or processed into a concentrated Fruit derivative, these components play a crucial role in supporting cardiovascular health. Therefore, the medicinal value of Crataegus pinnatifida is deeply rooted in the nutritional and chemical profile of its fleshy red berries.
