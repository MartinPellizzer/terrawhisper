Carambola, scientifically known as Averrhoa carambola, is a tropical fruit tree celebrated for its unique star-shaped slices. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its antioxidant properties and ability to support digestive health and immune function in various herbal remedies.

Its range extends to the Western Indian Ocean, specifically within the Comoros. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as starfruit, is characterized by several distinct components that contribute to its medicinal and culinary uses. The woody stem provides the structural support for the plant's growth, while the most prominent feature is the star-shaped fruit. When the plant produces ripe fruit, it offers a sweet and tangy flavor that is often used in various traditional remedies. Furthermore, the concentrated fruit extract is frequently utilized in therapeutic applications due to its rich bioactive compounds.
