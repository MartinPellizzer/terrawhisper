Averrhoa carambola

Averrhoa bilimbi, commonly known as bilimbi or cucumber tree, is a tropical plant valued for its medicinal properties. Characterized by its small, acidic fruits, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, kidney stones, and digestive issues. Its potent bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Averrhoa carambola
  • Family: Oxalidaceae
  • Native range: Africa
