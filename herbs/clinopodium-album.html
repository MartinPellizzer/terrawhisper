Clinopodium album, scientifically known as Clinopodium album, is a perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Often found in moist, grassy habitats, this aromatic plant is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. It contains essential oils frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments.
