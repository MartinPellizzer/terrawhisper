Maytenus vitis-idaea

Maytenus vitius-idaeae, commonly known as the climbing celastrus, is a versatile medicinal climber native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin conditions. Its unique phytochemical profile continues to intrigue modern pharmacological research.
