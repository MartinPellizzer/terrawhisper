What's the taxonomical classification of Eriophorum comosum?

Eriophorum comosum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this taxonomic hierarchy, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It falls under the order Poales and is a member of the family Cyperaceae. Finally, its specific biological identification is determined by its placement in the genus Eriophorum and its unique species designation, comosum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Eriophorum comosum has narrow, linear leaves that are typically basal and emerge from a rhizomatous root system. These bristles are actually modified perianth parts that aid in wind dispersal of the seeds. The stems are slender and erect, supporting the terminal clusters of cotton-like tufts.
