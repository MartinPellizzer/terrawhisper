Tocoyena bullata

Tocoyena bullata, commonly known as the "mountain fruit" or "puyas," is a medicinal shrub native to the high-altitude Andean regions. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Tocoyena bullata
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, taxonomically known as Tocoyena bullata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae within the genus Tocoyena.
