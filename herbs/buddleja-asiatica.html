Asiatic butterfly-bush, scientifically known as Buddlejia asiatica, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its fragrant blooms, it is traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential in contemporary pharmacological research and wellness.
