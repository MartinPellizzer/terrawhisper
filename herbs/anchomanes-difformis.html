Anchomanes difformis

Anchomanes difformis, commonly known as the "climbing anchomanes," is a fascinating medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its unique morphological features, it is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, often utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and support overall wellness through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Anchomanes difformis
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Anchomanes difformis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Alismatales. Finally, it is a member of the family Araceae and is categorized under the genus Anchomanes.

This plant, known scientifically as Anchomanes difformis, is primarily found across the West Tropical Africa region.
