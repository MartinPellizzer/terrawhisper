Grewia bulot

Grewia bulot, commonly known as the velvet leaf, is a resilient medicinal shrub prized in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments and skin inflammations. Its nutrient-rich leaves and bark offer valuable bioactive compounds for natural healing processes.
