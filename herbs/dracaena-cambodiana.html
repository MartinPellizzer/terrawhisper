Dracaena cambodiana

Dracaena cambodiana, commonly known as the Cambodian Dracaena, is a striking ornamental plant valued for its lush, architectural foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this species holds significant botanical interest. While primarily used in landscaping, its unique characteristics invite exploration into its potential traditional uses and medicinal properties within tropical ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Dracaena cambodiana
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dracaena, specifically as the species Dracaena cambodiania.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In China, its presence is specifically recorded within the tropical province of Hainan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found inhabiting the area of Assam. The Indo-China region also serves as a significant habitat, with populations located in Cambodia and Laos. Furthermore, its distribution extends through the neighboring country of Thailand.
