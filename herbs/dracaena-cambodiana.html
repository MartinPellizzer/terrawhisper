Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In China, its presence is specifically recorded within the tropical province of Hainan. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found inhabiting the area of Assam. The Indo-China region also serves as a significant habitat, with populations located in Cambodia and Laos. Furthermore, its distribution extends through the neighboring country of Thailand.

Plant Parts

This plant, Dracaena cambodiana, is highly valued for its unique botanical properties, particularly concerning its specialized anatomy. The primary source of its medicinal and traditional utility is found within the stems, which harbor a specialized substance known as dragon's blood. When the bark of the plant is wounded, it produces a distinctive red secretion that eventually hardens into a deep, pigmented red resin. This substance is widely harvested and used in various traditional practices due to its unique chemical composition.
