Aconitum apetalum

Aconitum apetalum, commonly known as a species of monkshood, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Ranunculaceae family. Renowned for its alkaloid-rich properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine for its sedative and analgesic effects, though its extreme toxicity requires precise handling and expert pharmacological knowledge.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum apetalum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Aconitum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions of Northern and Central Asia. In the northernmost reaches of its range, it can be found growing in the Altay region of Siberia. Moving southward, its presence extends into the vast territories of Kazakhstan within Central Asia. The geographical spread continues into the eastern portions of the continent, specifically reaching the Xinjiang province of China. Collectively, these locations define the unique natural habitat where the species thrives.
