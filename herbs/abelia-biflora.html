Abelia biflora

What's the taxonomical classification of Abelia biflora?

Abelia biflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales. It is a member of the family Caprifoliaceae, falling under the genus Abelia, and is specifically identified by the species name biflora.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Abelia biflora has opposite, elliptical to ovate leaves that measure one to three inches long and possess finely serrated margins with a glossy, dark green upper surface. The plant grows as a multi-stemmed, arching shrub that typically reaches heights of three to six feet. Small, tubular, white flowers emerge in clusters from the leaf axils throughout the summer months. These blossoms are characterized by their bell-like shape and are followed by distinctive, papery, reddish-pink sepals that persist into the autumn. The woody stems often exhibit a reddish tint, adding textural interest to the dense, spreading foliage.
