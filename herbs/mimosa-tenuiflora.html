Mimosa tenuiflora

Jurema

Mimosa tenuiflora, commonly known as Jurema Preta, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Brazilian Caatinga. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and healing properties, its bark is extensively used in traditional skincare and wound care. This remarkable plant continues to fascinate researchers for its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Mimosa tenuiflora
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Finally, it is classified under the genus Mimosa.
