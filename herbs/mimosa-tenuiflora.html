Jurema, scientifically known as Mimosa tenuiflora, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the northeastern regions of Brazil. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, it is widely utilized in traditional healing and skin treatments due to its high tannin content and remarkable ability to accelerate wound healing and regeneration.
