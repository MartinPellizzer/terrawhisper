Curculigo

Curculigo, commonly known as black curculigo or creeping devil, is a genus of medicinal herbs native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, its rhizomes possess potent bioactive compounds. These properties offer therapeutic benefits, including antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and adaptogenic effects, making it a valuable subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Curculigo
  • Family: Hypoxidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Hypoxidaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is the genus Curculigo.
