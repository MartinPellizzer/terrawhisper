Alder, scientifically known as Alnus, is a versatile genus of deciduous trees valued for their medicinal properties. Historically used in traditional remedies, its bark and leaves contain tannins and salicylic acid derivatives. These compounds offer anti-inflammatory and astringent benefits, supporting skin health and aiding in various digestive treatments.
