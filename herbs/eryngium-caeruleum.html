Eryngium caeruleum

Eryngium caeruleum, commonly known as Blue Sea Holly, is a striking perennial celebrated for its architectural beauty and medicinal potential. Native to various temperate regions, this plant features unique steel-blue bracts. Historically utilized in traditional herbalism, it offers intriguing pharmacological properties that continue to fascinate modern botanical researchers.

  • Scientific name: Eryngium caeruleum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the expansive landscapes of Central Asia. Its natural range includes several distinct nations within this specific region. Specifically, it can be located in the territories of Kazakhstan and Kirgizstan. The species also extends its distribution into Turkmenistan and Tadjikistan. Finally, its geographical presence is completed by its occurrence in Uzbekistan.
