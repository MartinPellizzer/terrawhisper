Phyllanthus lanceolarius

Phyllanthus lanceolarius, commonly known as the lance-leaved phyllanthus, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique lanceolate leaves, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus lanceolarius
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Phyllanthus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Phyllanthus lanceolarius, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is often identified as the Lance-leaf Phyllanthus, while in Spanish, it is known as Phyllanthus lanceolario. In Portuguese, the species is called Phyllanthus lanceolario, and in German, it is referred to as Lanzblättriger Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across parts of Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Its range also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented as growing in Bangladesh. These locations highlight the plant's presence in both East and South Asian ecosystems.
