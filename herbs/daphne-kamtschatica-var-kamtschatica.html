Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the diverse landscapes of the Russian Far East. Its natural range includes the rugged and volcanic terrain of the Kamchatka Peninsula. It also grows within the forested regions of Khabarovsk and the coastal areas of Primorye. Furthermore, the species can be located on the island of Sakhalin. Finally, its distribution extends to the remote Kuril Islands located in the Pacific Ocean.
