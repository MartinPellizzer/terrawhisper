Polycarpon prostratum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Caryophyllales. This plant is a member of the family Caryophyllaceae, specifically situated within the genus Polycarpon, and is identified by its unique species name, prostratum.
