Hawaiian baby woodrose

Argyreia nervosa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (45 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Hawaiian baby woodrose, scientifically known as Argyreiia nervosa, is a perennial shrub native to the Pacific Islands. Renowned for its striking white flowers and woody stems, this plant contains potent ergoline alkaloids. These compounds induce powerful hallucinogenic effects, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical and pharmacological studies.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the nations of Burkina, Guinea, and Nigeria. Its distribution also extends further west to include the country of Senegal. In addition to these western regions, it is present in West-Central Tropical Africa. Within this central zone, the species is documented in the Central African Republic.
