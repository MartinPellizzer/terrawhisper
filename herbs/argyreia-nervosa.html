Argyreia nervosa

Argyreiia nervosa, commonly known as the Silver Leaf plant, is a medicinal shrub native to parts of Asia. Renowned for its neuroprotective properties, it is traditionally used in herbal medicine to enhance cognitive function, alleviate anxiety, and support neurological health through its unique bioactive phytochemical compounds and antioxidants.

  • Scientific name: Argyreia nervosa
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Argyreiia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the nations of Burkina, Guinea, and Nigeria. Its distribution also extends further west to include the country of Senegal. In addition to these western regions, it is present in West-Central Tropical Africa. Within this central zone, the species is documented in the Central African Republic.
