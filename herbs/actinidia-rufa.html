Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within Eastern Asia. It can be found growing in the islands of Japan. Its natural range also extends to the Korean Peninsula. Additionally, the species inhabits the Nansei-shotō archipelago. Finally, this medicinal plant is also documented as occurring in Taiwan.
