Ulex gallii

Ulex gallii, commonly known as Atlantic gorse, is a resilient, evergreen shrub native to the coastal regions of Western Europe. Distinguished by its dense, thorny foliage and vibrant yellow blossoms, this hardy species plays a vital role in stabilizing sandy soils and providing essential habitats within unique maritime ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Ulex gallii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. It is specifically identified by its genus, Ulex.
