Western Gorse, scientifically known as Ulex gallii, is a hardy, evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean and Atlantic coasts. Characterized by its dense, thorny structure and vibrant yellow flowers, it thrives in acidic, sandy soils. Historically utilized in traditional remedies, this resilient plant plays a vital ecological role.
