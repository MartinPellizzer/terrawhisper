Capparis fascicularis

Capparis fascicularis, commonly known as the Fasciculate Caper, is a resilient shrub native to arid regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. Researchers explore its potential for treating inflammation and oxidative stress, highlighting its significance in ethnobotany and the development of natural therapeutic compounds.
