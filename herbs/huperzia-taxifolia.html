Huperzia taxifolia

Huperzia taxifolia, commonly known as Huperzia or Dragonwort, is a perennial clubmoss valued in pharmacology. It is the primary natural source of huperzine A, a potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. This alkaloid is widely researched for its neuroprotective properties and its potential to enhance memory and treat cognitive decline.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia taxifolia
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Lycopodiidae and the order Lycopodiales, falling into the family Lycopodiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Huperzia.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of Mexico. In the central part of the country, it can be found within the Mexico Central territory. Its presence extends to the eastern coast through the Mexico Gulf region. Furthermore, the species inhabits the diverse landscapes of Mexico Northeast. Finally, its geographical reach covers the expansive areas of both Mexico Northwest and Mexico Southwest.
