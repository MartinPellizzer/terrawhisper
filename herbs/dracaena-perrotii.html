Dracaena perrotii

Dracaena perrotii, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in certain tropical regions, is a unique botanical species valued for its therapeutic potential. Often studied for its bioactive compounds, this plant holds significant interest in ethnobotany, offering promising insights into natural remedies and the diverse pharmacological possibilities of forest flora.

  • Scientific name: Dracaena perrotii
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. It is further identified by the genus Dracaena, specifically as the species Dracaena perrotii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its range extends significantly into East Tropical Africa as well. Specifically, the species is documented to inhabit parts of Kenya. Additionally, the plant is located within the borders of Tanzania.
