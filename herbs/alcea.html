Alcea

Hollyhock

Alcea, commonly known as hollyhock, is a cherished perennial celebrated for its towering spikes of vibrant, bell-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental elegance in gardens, this medicinal plant has historically been utilized for its soothing properties, particularly in traditional remedies targeting respiratory ailments and skin inflammations through its mucilaginous qualities.

  • Scientific name: Alcea
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal plant is identified by its genus, Alcea.
