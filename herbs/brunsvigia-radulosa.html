Candelabra Flower, scientifically known as Brunsvigia radulosa, is a striking bulbous perennial native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its magnificent, globe-shaped umbels of white flowers, this plant adds dramatic elegance to gardens. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance in traditional medicinal practices across its natural habitat.

Distribution

This plant is native to the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. Its range specifically includes the vast territories found within Botswana. It can also be found growing across the various Cape Provinces. Additionally, its presence extends into the mountainous regions of Lesotho. Finally, the species is widely distributed through the Free State and KwaZulu-Natal.

Plant Parts

This plant, Brunsvigia radulosa, is characterized by its prominent underground structures known as bulbs. These bulbs serve as the primary storage organs for the plant, allowing it to survive through dormant periods and store the necessary nutrients to support its rapid growth and the emergence of its striking flowering stalks. In many medicinal applications involving species of this genus, the bulb is the most significant part of the plant due to its concentrated chemical properties.
