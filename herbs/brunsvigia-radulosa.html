Brunsvigia radulosa

Brunsvigia radulosa, commonly known as the Button Bulb, is a striking perennial bulb native to southern Africa. Characterized by its spectacular, globe-shaped white flower heads, this plant thrives in rocky terrains. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant traditional importance in various cultures for its unique medicinal properties and uses.

  • Scientific name: Brunsvigia radulosa
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is a member of the family Amaryllidaceae. Specifically, it is classified under the genus Brunsvigia as the species Brunsvigia radulosa.

Distribution

This plant is native to the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. Its range specifically includes the vast territories found within Botswana. It can also be found growing across the various Cape Provinces. Additionally, its presence extends into the mountainous regions of Lesotho. Finally, the species is widely distributed through the Free State and KwaZulu-Natal.
