Periploca forrestii

Periploca forrestii, commonly known as Forrest's Periploca, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Native to specific tropical regions, it contains bioactive compounds utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties. Understanding its chemical profile is essential for exploring its potential applications in modern pharmacological research and treatments.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Ultimately, the species is identified under the genus Periploca.
