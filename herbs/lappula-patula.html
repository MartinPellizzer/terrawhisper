Lappula patula

Lappula patula, commonly known as stickseed or slender lupine, is a small, annual herb belonging to the Boraginaceae family. Characterized by its tiny, prickly fruits that cling to clothing, this resilient plant thrives in disturbed soils. It holds significant interest in ethnobotany for its diverse traditional medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Lappula patula
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Boraginales, it is a member of the family Boraginaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lappula.
