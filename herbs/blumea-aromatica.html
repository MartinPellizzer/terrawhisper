Blumea aromatica, scientifically known as Blumea aromatica, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent aromatic properties, the plant is rich in essential oils. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues through its bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends to Eastern Asia, where it is notably distributed in Taiwan. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in the Assam region.
