Blumea aromatica, scientifically known as Blumea aromatica, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent aromatic properties, the plant is rich in essential oils. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues through its bioactive compounds.
