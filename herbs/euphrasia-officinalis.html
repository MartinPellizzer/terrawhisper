Eyebright, scientifically known as Euphrasia officinalis, is a cherished medicinal herb renowned for its ocular benefits. Traditionally used to soothe irritated, inflamed, or tired eyes, this small flowering plant contains compounds that support vision health. Beyond ophthalmology, it serves as a gentle tonic for respiratory and inflammatory conditions.
