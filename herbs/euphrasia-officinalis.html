Euphrasia officinalis

Eyebright

Euphrasiia officinalis, commonly known as Eyebright, is a cherished medicinal herb renowned for its ocular benefits. Traditionally used to soothe inflammation and treat eye irritations, this delicate flowering plant offers potent antioxidant properties. Its therapeutic applications extend to treating respiratory ailments, making it a cornerstone in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Euphrasia officinalis
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Euphrasia officinalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Orobanchaceae and the genus Euphrasia.
