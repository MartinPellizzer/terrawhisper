Schisandra pubescens

Schisandra pubevescens, commonly known as the hairy schisandra, is a valuable medicinal climber native to East Asia. Renowned for its adaptogenic properties, this plant is used in traditional medicine to enhance vitality, reduce fatigue, and support cognitive function. Its unique berries offer potent antioxidant benefits for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra pubescens
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Schisandra pubescens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Austrobaileyales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Schisandraceae and the genus Schisandra.
