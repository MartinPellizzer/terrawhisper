Iphiona grantioides

Iphiona grantioides, commonly known as the False Senecio, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments, offering a fascinating subject for ethnobotanical study and natural pharmacology.
