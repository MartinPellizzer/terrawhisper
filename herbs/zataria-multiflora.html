Zataria multiflora

Zataria multiflora, commonly known as Pentaphyllum or Spilanthol, is a versatile medicinal herb celebrated for its unique numbing properties. Widely used in traditional medicine to alleviate toothaches and throat discomfort, this aromatic plant contains bioactive compounds that offer potent analgesic, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory benefits for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Zataria multiflora
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Zataria. Specifically, its scientific name is Zataria multiflora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within Western Asia, it can be found growing in both Afghanistan and Iran. Its range extends into the Arabian Peninsula, specifically within the country of Oman. Furthermore, the species is present in the Indian Subcontinent, including parts of Pakistan. Finally, its habitat encompasses the mountainous terrain of the West Himalaya.
