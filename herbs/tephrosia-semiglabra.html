Tephrosia semiglabra

Tephrosia semiglabra, commonly known as the "smooth tephrasia," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This plant is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and respiratory conditions, offering promising therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Tephrosia semiglabra
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its classification through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is a member of the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Tephrosia.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions within Southern Africa. It can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of Botswana. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the Cape Provinces. Additionally, the species inhabits the mountainous terrain of Lesotho.
