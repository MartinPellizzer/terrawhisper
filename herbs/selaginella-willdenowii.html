Selaginella willdenowii

Selaginella willdenowii, commonly known as the resurrection plant, is a remarkable lycopod celebrated for its ability to survive extreme desiccation. This resilient species undergoes physiological dormancy during droughts, reviving instantly upon hydration. Beyond its biological wonder, it holds significant potential in pharmacological research for developing protective cellular treatments.

  • Scientific name: Selaginella willdenowii
  • Family: Selaginellaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Plant Parts

This plant, Selaginella willdenowii, is commonly known as the resurrection plant due to its remarkable ability to survive extreme dehydration. The primary morphological feature of this species is its small, scale-like leaves, which are arranged in a dense, overlapping fashion along the stems. These leaves play a crucial role in the plant's survival mechanism; when water becomes scarce, the leaves curl tightly inward to protect the delicate tissues from desiccation, and they unfurl once again when moisture is reintroduced, restoring the plant's lush, green appearance.
