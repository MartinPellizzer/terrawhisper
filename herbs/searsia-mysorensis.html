Searsia mysorensis

Searsia mysorensis, commonly known as the Mysore Sumac, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. This resilient plant offers significant bioactive compounds valuable to modern pharmacology.

  • Family: Anacardiaceae
