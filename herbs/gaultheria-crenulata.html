Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species also extends its range into Eastern Asia, specifically found in Taiwan. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, the plant is documented in Cambodia. Finally, its distribution includes the mountainous landscapes of Laos.
