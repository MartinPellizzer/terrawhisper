Gaultheria crenulata

Gaultheria crenulata, commonly known as crenulate wintergreen, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate bell-shaped flowers, this medicinal plant contains methyl salicylate. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to alleviate pain, reduce inflammation, and support respiratory wellness.

  • Scientific name: Gaultheria crenulata
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed within the genus Gaultheria.
