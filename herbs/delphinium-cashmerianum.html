Delphinium cashmerianum

Delphinium cashmerianum, commonly known as the Kashmiri Delphinium, is a striking perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its vibrant blue blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies for its unique bioactive compounds and diverse medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Delphinium cashmerianum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Delphinium.
