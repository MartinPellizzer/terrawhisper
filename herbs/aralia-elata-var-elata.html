Aralia elata var. elata

Aralia elaata var. elaata, commonly known as the Japanese Angelica Tree, is a deciduous shrub prized in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its adaptogenic properties, it is frequently utilized to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and alleviate fatigue, offering a natural approach to enhancing overall vitality and resilience.

  • Scientific name: Aralia elata var. elata
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aralia elaata var. elata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Araliaceae and the genus Aralia.
