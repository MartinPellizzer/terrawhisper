Chinese angelica-tree, scientifically known as Aralia elata var. elata, is a perennial medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds valued for their anti-inflammatory and adaptogenic properties. Often used to support immune health and vitality, this versatile plant remains a significant botanical resource.
