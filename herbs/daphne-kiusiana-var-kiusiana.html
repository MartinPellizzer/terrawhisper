Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range extends into the East Asian mainland, where it can be found growing in Korea. Furthermore, the species is established in the islands of Japan. Finally, its geographical reach includes the Nansei-shoto island chain.
