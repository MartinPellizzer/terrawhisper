Croton draco

Croton draco, commonly known as the Dragon's Blood tree, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its deep red resin. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat wounds, inflammation, and digestive issues, this species offers potent bioactive compounds. Its unique healing properties continue to fascinate modern botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Croton draco
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and falls under the genus Croton.
