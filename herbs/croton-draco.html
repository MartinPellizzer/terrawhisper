Weeping blood, scientifically known as Croton draco, is a remarkable plant renowned for its unique, crimson resin. Often called Dragon's Blood, this sap has been revered for centuries in traditional medicine. It possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties, making it a vital resource in holistic healing practices.
