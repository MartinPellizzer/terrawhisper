Parsley, scientifically known as Petroselinum crispum, is a versatile biennial herb cherished in both culinary and medicinal traditions. Renowned for its high concentrations of vitamins A, C, and K, it serves as a potent antioxidant. Often used to support kidney health and digestion, this aromatic plant offers remarkable therapeutic benefits.
