Papeda (citrus)

Citrus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (152 studies) · ★★★★☆

Papeda (citrus), scientifically known as Citrus, is a versatile genus of flowering plants celebrated for its aromatic leaves and nutritious fruits. Widely used in traditional medicine, these plants offer potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, making them essential for boosting immunity and supporting overall wellness in many cultures.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales and family Rutaceae, it is classified under the genus Citrus.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of Southern and Eastern Europe. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in both Portugal and Spain. Moving toward the Mediterranean, the species is also well-established in Italy. Its presence extends into Southeastern Europe, where it is common in Albania. Additionally, the plant thrives within the borders of Bulgaria.
