What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Impatiens apalophylla has a decumbent to ascending herbaceous habit characterized by succulent, hollow stems and an absence of true leaves on the stem. Its flowers are typically small and solitary, emerging from the axils of these reduced leaf structures. The reproductive organs consist of a nectar-producing spur and delicate petals that facilitate pollination. The root system is generally shallow and adapted to its specific ecological niche.
