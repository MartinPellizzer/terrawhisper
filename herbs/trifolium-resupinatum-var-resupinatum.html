Trifolium resupinatum var. resupinatum

Trifolium resupinatum var. resupinatum, commonly known as creeping clover, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its prostrate growth and trifoliate leaves, this plant is frequently studied for its bioactive compounds, which offer potential therapeutic benefits in supporting digestive health and reducing systemic inflammation.
