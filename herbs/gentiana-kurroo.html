Gentiana kurroo

Gentiana kurroo, commonly known as Kurroo, is a perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Highly valued in Ayurveda, its bitter roots possess potent medicinal properties. It is primarily utilized to treat digestive disorders, fever, and liver ailments, serving as a vital natural remedy for enhancing overall metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana kurroo
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Gentianales, it is a member of the family Gentianaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Gentiana.
