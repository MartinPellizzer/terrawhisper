Himalayan gentian

Gentiana kurroo · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (36 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Himalayan gentian, scientifically known as Gentiana kurroo, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, its bitter roots possess potent therapeutic properties, primarily used to stimulate digestion, treat liver ailments, and alleviate various gastrointestinal disorders through their restorative bioactive compounds.
