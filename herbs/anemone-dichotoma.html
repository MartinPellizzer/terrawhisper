What's the taxonomical classification of Anemone dichotoma?

Anemone dichotoma belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Ranunculales. The plant is a member of the family Ranunculaceae, falling under the genus Anemone, and is specifically identified by its species name, dichotoma.
