Purple prairie clover, scientifically known as Dalea purpurea, is a perennial legume native to North American prairies. Renowned for its striking violet flowers and nitrogen-fixing capabilities, this resilient plant supports biodiversity. Historically used in traditional medicine, it remains a vital component of healthy grasslands and restorative ecological landscapes.
