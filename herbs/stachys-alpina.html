Alpine Woundwort, scientifically known as Stachys alpina, is a resilient perennial herb native to high-altitude European meadows. Distinguished by its velvety, dark leaves and spiked violet flowers, this plant has historically been valued in traditional herbalism for its astringent properties and its ability to assist in healing minor wounds.
