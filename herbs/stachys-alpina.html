Stachys alpina

Stachyis alpina, commonly known as Alpine Woundwort, is a perennial herb native to mountainous regions. Valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses notable astringent and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to support skin healing and soothe digestive discomfort, this resilient plant remains a fascinating subject for both botanical study and natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Stachys alpina
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
