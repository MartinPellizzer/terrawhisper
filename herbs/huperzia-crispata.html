Huperzia crispata

Huperzia crispata, commonly known as the spiked firmoss, is a primitive clubmoss valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its high concentration of huperzine A, this plant serves as a potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. It is widely studied for its neuroprotective properties and potential in treating cognitive decline and Alzheimer's disease.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia crispata
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Lycopodiidae. Within the order Lycopodiales, it is classified under the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Huperzia, specifically as the species Huperzia crispata.
