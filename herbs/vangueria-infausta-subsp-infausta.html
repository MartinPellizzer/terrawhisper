Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several distinct regions within the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Rwanda and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, it can also be found in East Tropical Africa within Tanzania. The species extends further south into the South Tropical Africa region, specifically inhabiting Angola. Finally, its geographical range continues into Malawi, completing its spread across these diverse tropical zones.
