Cichorium pumilum

Cichorium pumilum, commonly known as small chicory, is a resilient herbaceous plant native to various temperate regions. Valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses significant therapeutic potential. Rich in bioactive compounds, this species is studied for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits, offering a natural approach to supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cichorium pumilum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cichorium pumilum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is situated in the genus Cichorium.
