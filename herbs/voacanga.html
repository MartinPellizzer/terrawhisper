Voacanca, scientifically known as Voacanga stellata, is a remarkable tropical plant native to the rainforests of Africa and Asia. Highly valued in ethnobotany, it contains potent indole alkaloids. These bioactive compounds are extensively researched for their potential pharmacological applications, particularly in developing new treatments for neurological and cardiovascular disorders.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. Within this area, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its presence also extends to the nation of Burkina. Furthermore, the species is documented in Gambia and Ghana. Finally, the distribution includes the coastal territory of Guinea-Bissau.

This plant, known scientifically as Voacanga africana, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, particularly its leaf and root. The leaves are often studied for their complex alkaloids, which contribute to the plant's bioactive profile, while the roots are frequently utilized in traditional applications due to their concentrated chemical compounds.
