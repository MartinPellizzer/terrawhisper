Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several distinct regions. In the Macaronesia region, its presence is noted specifically in Cape Verde. Moving to West Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the territory of Benin. The distribution further extends to the landlocked nation of Burkina. Additionally, the plant is documented throughout Gambia and Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Jatropha, possesses various parts that are utilized for their specific medicinal and biological properties. The leaf is frequently used in traditional medicine to treat skin inflammations and wounds due to its soothing properties. The root is often employed in various herbal preparations to address digestive issues or as an anti-inflammatory agent. Most notably, the seed contains a high concentration of oils, which, while requiring careful processing to manage toxicity, can be utilized for both industrial and therapeutic purposes.
