Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across both the Indian Ocean and parts of East Asia. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in Mauritius and Réunion. Its distribution continues into China, specifically within the South-Central and Southeast regions. Furthermore, the species is documented to occur in Eastern Asia, particularly in Japan. This wide dispersal highlights the plant's adaptability to different tropical and subtropical environments.
