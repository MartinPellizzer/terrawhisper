Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Roxburg's cardamom, is a member of the Zingiberaceae family that holds significant medicinal value through its specific anatomical components. The most therapeutically potent part of the plant is the rhizome, which is extensively harvested and used in traditional medicine for its warming properties and ability to treat digestive ailments. Additionally, the seeds contained within its capsules are utilized for their aromatic and carminative qualities, contributing to the plant's overall medicinal profile.
