Alpinia roxburghii

Alpinia roxburghii, commonly known as Roxburgh's cardamom, is a perennial herb belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Native to subtropical regions, this aromatic plant is prized in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds. It is widely utilized to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues through its therapeutic properties.

  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
