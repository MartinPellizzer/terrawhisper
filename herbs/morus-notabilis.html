Morus notabilis

Morus notabilis, commonly known as the White Mulberry, is a versatile deciduous tree cherished for its medicinal and nutritional properties. Native to East Asia, its leaves, bark, and fruits are rich in bioactive compounds, traditionally used to support metabolic health, reduce inflammation, and provide essential antioxidants to the body.

  • Scientific name: Morus notabilis
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, placing it in the family Moraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Morus, specifically the species Morus notaibilis.
