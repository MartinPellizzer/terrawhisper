Ornithogalum thyrsoides

Ornithogalum thyrsoides, commonly known as the Thyrsus Star-of-Bethlehem, is a striking perennial wildflower native to Europe. Characterized by its tall, elegant racemes of white, star-shaped blossoms, this plant holds significant botanical interest. While primarily ornamental, its unique morphology offers fascinating insights into the diverse world of medicinal flora.

  • Family: Asparagaceae

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
