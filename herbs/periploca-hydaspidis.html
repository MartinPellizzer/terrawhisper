Periploca hydaspidis

Periploca hydaspidis, commonly known as a climbing shrub from the Apocynaceae family, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in various folk remedies to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering promising potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
