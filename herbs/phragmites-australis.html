Phragmites australis

Common reed

Phragmites australis, commonly known as the common reed, is a robust perennial grass found in wetlands worldwide. While often viewed as an invasive species, it possesses significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various cultures have utilized its versatile stalks and rhizomes for traditional medicinal purposes, structural materials, and ecological filtration.

  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Phragmites.
