Common reed, scientifically known as Phragmites australis, is a versatile perennial grass found in wetlands globally. Beyond its role in ecosystem stabilization, it possesses significant medicinal properties. Traditionally used in various cultures, its extracts offer potential antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant benefits, making it a subject of botanical interest.
