Potamogeton lucens

Potamogeton lucens, commonly known as shining pondweed, is an aquatic perennial valued for its unique ecological properties. Found in freshwater habitats, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine. While primarily studied for its botanical characteristics, its bioactive potential offers intriguing possibilities for future pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Potamogeton lucens
  • Family: Potamogetonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Potamogeton luceus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Alismatales. Finally, it is a member of the family Potamogetonaceae and falls under the genus Potamogeton.
