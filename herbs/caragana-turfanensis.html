Caragana turfanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Caragana turfanensis?

Caragana turfanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated within the order Fabales. This plant is a representative of the family Fabaceae, falling under the genus Caragana, with its specific designation being the species turfanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Caragana turfanensis has a woody, perennial growth habit characterized by erect stems, lanceolate to elliptic leaves with serrated margins, and small, actinomorphic flowers arranged in terminal clusters. The foliage typically exhibits a leathery texture and a deep green color on the upper surface. Its floral structures feature delicate petals that often transition in hue during the blooming period. The root system is well-developed to support its upright structure in its native habitat. Small, woody fruits develop following successful pollination.
