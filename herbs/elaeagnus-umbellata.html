Autumn Olive, scientifically known as Elaeagnu umbellata, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized for its nutrient-dense berries. While often considered invasive, it offers significant medicinal potential. Historically used in traditional practices to support digestive health and reduce inflammation, its antioxidant-rich fruit provides a natural boost to overall wellness.

This plant, Elaeagnus umbellata, possesses several distinct morphological features that contribute to its medicinal and nutritional profile.
