Elaeagnus umbellata

Autumn olive

Elaeagnus umbellata, commonly known as Autumn Olive, is a versatile deciduous shrub valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, its fruits offer health benefits, while various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional remedies to support wellness and combat inflammation in holistic practices.

  • Scientific name: Elaeagnus umbellata
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Rosales. Finally, it is classified under the family Elaeagnaceae and the genus Elaeagnus.
