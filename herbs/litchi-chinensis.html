Litchi chinensis

Litchi chinensis, commonly known as lychee, is a tropical evergreen tree prized for its sweet, juicy fruit. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is frequently utilized for its antioxidant properties, helping to support immune function and combat oxidative stress in the body.

  • Scientific name: Litchi chinensis
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, falling within the family Sapindaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Litchi.

Distribution

This plant, Litchi chinensis, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in both Mauritius and Réunion. Its native range extends across China, specifically within the Hainan province and the China Southeast region. Additionally, the species is present within the Indian Subcontinent, particularly in Assam. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in different tropical and subtropical environments.
