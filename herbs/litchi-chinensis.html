Litchi, scientifically known as Litchi chinensis, is a tropical evergreen tree celebrated for its sweet, juicy fruit. Beyond its culinary appeal, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. Rich in antioxidants and vitamin C, it is utilized to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote overall vitality.

Distribution

This plant, Litchi chinensis, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in both Mauritius and Réunion. Its native range extends across China, specifically within the Hainan province and the China Southeast region. Additionally, the species is present within the Indian Subcontinent, particularly in Assam. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in different tropical and subtropical environments.
