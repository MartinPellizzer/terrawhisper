Citrus trifoliata

Citrus trifolia, commonly known as the trifoliate orange, is a unique evergreen shrub valued in traditional medicine. Distinguished by its three-leaflet structure, this plant offers diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, it is widely utilized for its antioxidant, antimicrobial, and digestive-supporting medicinal benefits.

  • Scientific name: Citrus trifoliata
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Citrus.

Moving eastward into Central Asia, the species can be found inhabiting Tajikistan and Uzbekistan. Furthermore, the plant has a significant distribution within China, specifically located in the South-Central region.
