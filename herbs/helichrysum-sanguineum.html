Helichrysum sanguineum

Helichrysum sanguineum, commonly known as blood-red everlasting, is a resilient perennial herb prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its vibrant, crimson blooms, this plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often utilized in skincare and holistic remedies, it serves as a powerful botanical ally for promoting skin vitality.
