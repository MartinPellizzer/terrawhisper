Gibraltar thyme, scientifically known as Thymus willdenowii, is a rare, endemic shrub found exclusively on the cliffs of the Rock of Gibraltar. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate pink blossoms, this resilient plant offers significant ethnobotanical interest, potentially providing unique bioactive compounds for traditional and modern medicine.
