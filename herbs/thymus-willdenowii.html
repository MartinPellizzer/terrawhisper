Thymus willdenowii

Thymus willdenowii, commonly known as Willdenow's thyme, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its essential oils, this medicinal herb possesses potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health and alleviate respiratory discomfort through its soothing qualities.

  • Scientific name: Thymus willdenowii
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
