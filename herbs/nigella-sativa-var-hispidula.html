Nigella sativa var. hispidula

Nigella sativa var. hispidula, commonly known as a variant of black cumin, is a potent medicinal herb prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like thymoquinone, it offers significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits. This botanical variety is increasingly studied for its potential in supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Nigella sativa var. hispidula
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
