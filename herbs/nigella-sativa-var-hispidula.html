Plant Parts

This plant, Nigella sativa var. hispidula, is primarily valued for its small, black, angular seeds, which are the most significant medicinal component of the species. Each individual seed is rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds that contribute to its therapeutic properties. When harvested, these seeds are collected and processed for use in various traditional remedies, making the seed the most crucial part of the plant for pharmacological applications.
