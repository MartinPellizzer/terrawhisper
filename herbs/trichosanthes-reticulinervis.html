What's the taxonomical classification of Trichosanthes reticulinervis?

Trichosanthes reticulinervis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Trichosanthes, and its specific species designation, retiulinervis.
