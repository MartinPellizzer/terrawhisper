Pueraria tuberosa

Pueraria tuberosa, commonly known as the tuberous kudzu, is a versatile medicinal climbing vine native to tropical Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its nutrient-rich tubers are utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. This botanical powerhouse offers significant therapeutic potential for supporting overall human wellness.

  • Scientific name: Pueraria tuberosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. It is scientifically classified under the genus Pueraria, with the specific species being Pueraria tuberosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across the diverse landscapes of the Indian Subcontinent. It is widely recognized as being native to various regions within India, including the specific area of Assam. Beyond these borders, the species can also be found flourishing in the neighboring country of Bangladesh. Its presence extends into the rugged terrains of the East Himalaya, showcasing its adaptability to different altitudes. Furthermore, the plant is well-established within the mountainous landscapes of Nepal.
