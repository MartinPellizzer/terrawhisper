Kniphofia uvaria

Red hot poker

Kniphofia uvaria, commonly known as Red Hot Poker, is a striking perennial prized for its torch-like blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds traditional medicinal significance. Often used in folk remedies, its properties are studied for their potential therapeutic benefits, making it both a garden gem and botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Kniphofia uvaria
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asphodelaceae and the genus Kniphofiia.
