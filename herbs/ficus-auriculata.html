Roxburgh fig, scientifically known as Ficus auriculata, is a versatile medicinal tree native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations. Its leaves, bark, and fruits offer significant bioactive compounds.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central areas as well as the Southeast provinces.
