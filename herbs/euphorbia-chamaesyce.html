Euphorbia chamaesyce

Euphorbia chamaesyce, commonly known as the Euphorbia spurge, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various arid regions. Renowned for its succulent properties, this plant contains unique phytochemicals utilized in traditional healing. It serves as a vital resource in ethnobotany, offering potential therapeutic applications in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia chamaesyce
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Euphorbia.
