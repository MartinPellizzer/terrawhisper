Milton

Malus domestica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1446 studies) · ★★★★★

Milton, scientifically known as Malus domestica, is the botanical name for the common apple tree. Renowned for its nutritional density, this plant provides essential vitamins, antioxidants, and dietary fiber. Beyond its culinary value, Malus domestica has been utilized in traditional medicine to support digestive health and boost immunity.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the apple tree (Malus domestica), utilizes its leaves primarily for photosynthesis to support its fruit production, but they also hold significant botanical interest. The leaves are characterized by an elliptical or ovate shape with finely serrated margins and a slightly pubescent texture on the underside. In various traditional medicinal contexts, leaf extracts have been studied for their potential antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, though the fruit remains the most prominent part of the plant used for consumption.
