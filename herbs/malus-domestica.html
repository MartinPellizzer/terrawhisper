Malus domestica

Malus domestica, commonly known as the apple tree, is a versatile fruit-bearing species valued globally for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in dietary fiber, antioxidants, and essential vitamins, apples support cardiovascular health, aid digestion, and possess anti-inflammatory qualities, making this plant a cornerstone of holistic wellness and nutrition.

  • Scientific name: Malus domestica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is a member of the Rosaceae family. It is scientifically classified under the genus Malus.
