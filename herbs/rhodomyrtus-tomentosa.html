Rhodomyrtus tomentosa

Rhodomyrtus tomentosa, commonly known as Rose Myrtle, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its medicinal and ornamental properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like flavonoids and tannins, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Rhodomyrtus tomentosa
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtalees, and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rhodomyrtus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions in Asia. In China, it is commonly found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its distribution extends into Eastern Asia, specifically encompassing Taiwan and the Nansei-shoto islands. Furthermore, the species can be located within the Indian Subcontinent, particularly in India. Together, these locations highlight the diverse range in which the plant thrives.
