Ceylon hill-cherry

Rhodomyrtus tomentosa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (96 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Ceylon hill-cherry, scientifically known as Rhodomyrtus tomentosa, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its edible, antioxidant-rich berries, this medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Its leaves and bark are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, highlighting its profound ethnobotanical importance.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions in Asia. In China, it is commonly found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its distribution extends into Eastern Asia, specifically encompassing Taiwan and the Nansei-shoto islands. Furthermore, the species can be located within the Indian Subcontinent, particularly in India. Together, these locations highlight the diverse range in which the plant thrives.
