Bergia ammannioides

Bergia ammannioides, commonly known as water hyssop, is a small, aquatic herb widely distributed across tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly Ayurveda, this plant possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin diseases, respiratory issues, and various internal ailments effectively.

  • Scientific name: Bergia ammannioides
  • Family: Elatinaceae
  • Native range: Africa
