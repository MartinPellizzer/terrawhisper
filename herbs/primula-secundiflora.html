Primula secundiflora

What's the taxonomical classification of Primula secundiflora?

Primula secundiflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized into the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Primulaceae and falls under the genus Primula, specifically identified by the species name secundiflora.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Primula secundiflora has a basal rosette of oblong to lanceolate leaves that are typically entire or slightly dentate with long petioles. The inflorescence consists of a single, terminal umbel held aloft by a slender, leafless scape. Each flower features five spreading corolla lobes that are often pale yellow or cream in color. The stamens are positioned within the floral tube, while the ovary is inferior as is characteristic of the genus. These morphological traits distinguish it from other primrose species found in similar alpine habitats.
