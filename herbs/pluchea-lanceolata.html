Pluchea lanceolata

Pluchea lanceolata, commonly known as Gamari in India, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Characterized by its lance-shaped leaves, this plant possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. It is widely utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions across diverse cultures.

  • Scientific name: Pluchea lanceolata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Pluchea.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Pluchea lanceolata, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Hindi, the plant is commonly referred to as kusha or kusha-patti, and in Telugu, it is identified as mardala.
