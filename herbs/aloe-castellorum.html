Distribution

This plant, known as Aloe castellorum, exhibits a specific and limited geographical range within the Middle East. Its natural habitat is primarily concentrated within the Arabian Peninsula. Specifically, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Saudi Arabia. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the neighboring territory of Yemen. Consequently, this species is characteristic of the unique arid environments found in these two specific countries.

Plant Parts

This plant, Aloe castellorum, is primarily valued for its succulent leaf, which serves as the main reservoir for its medicinal properties. Within the structure of the leaf, the plant produces a thick, clear gel and a bitter yellowish sap, both of which contain bioactive compounds used in traditional healing. Because the leaf is designed to store moisture and nutrients, it acts as the concentrated source of the plant's therapeutic alkaloids and polysaccharides.
