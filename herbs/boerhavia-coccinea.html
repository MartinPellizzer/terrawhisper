Hog Meat, scientifically known as Boerhavia coccinea, is a versatile medicinal herb renowned in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Often referred to as Punarnava, this plant is celebrated for its remarkable rejuvenating properties, particularly its ability to support kidney function, reduce inflammation, and promote overall detoxification within the human body.
