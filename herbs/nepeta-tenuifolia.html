Nepeta tenuifolia

Nepeta tenuifolia, commonly known as Narrow-leaf Catmint, is a versatile perennial prized for its delicate, silvery-green foliage and airy lavender blooms. Beyond its ornamental charm in gardens, this hardy plant holds traditional medicinal significance, often utilized for its calming properties and ability to soothe various minor ailments naturally.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta tenuifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Nepeta.

Common Names

This plant, Nepeta tenuifolia, is known by several common names in Chinese depending on the specific part or variety being referenced. It is frequently referred to as 荆芥 (jīngjiè) or 裂叶荆芥 (lièyè jīngjiè), the latter specifically highlighting its characteristic narrow or split leaves. Additionally, when the medicinal roots are the focus, it is called 荆芥根 (jīngjiè gēn), and in some botanical contexts, it may be listed as 裂叶荆芥(荆芥) to denote its relationship to the broader Nepeta cataria group.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across various regions within China. It can be found growing in the areas of China South-Central and China North-Central. Additionally, its distribution extends into the northern territories of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. The species also makes its presence known within the high-altitude landscapes of Qinghai. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse environmental niches inhabited by Nepeta tenuifolia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as slender-leaved catnip, is characterized by its delicate structure consisting of upright stems and slender shoots that support flowering spikes. The aromatic quality of the plant is primarily derived from its specialized peltate glandular trichomes, which are distributed across the foliage and contain essential oils. Once the flowering period concludes, the plant produces small seeds that serve as the primary means of reproduction.
