Nepeta tenuifolia, scientifically known as Nepeta tenuifolia, is a charming perennial herb within the Lamiaceae family. Commonly called thinleaf catmint, this hardy plant features delicate, narrow foliage and clusters of tiny lavender flowers. Prized for its ornamental beauty and aromatic properties, it thrives in sunny, well-drained garden spaces.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across various regions within China. It can be found growing in the areas of China South-Central and China North-Central. Additionally, its distribution extends into the northern territories of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. The species also makes its presence known within the high-altitude landscapes of Qinghai. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as slender-leaved catnip, is characterized by its delicate structure consisting of upright stems and slender shoots that support flowering spikes. The aromatic quality of the plant is primarily derived from its specialized peltate glandular trichomes, which are distributed across the foliage and contain essential oils. Once the flowering period concludes, the plant produces small seeds that serve as the primary means of reproduction.
