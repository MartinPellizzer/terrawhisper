Pulicaria gnaphalodes

Pulicaria gnaphaulodes, commonly known as Fleabane, is a resilient perennial herb valued for its diverse medicinal properties. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, this plant contains bioactive compounds used traditionally to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and digestive issues. Its aromatic foliage offers significant potential in modern ethnopharmacological research.
