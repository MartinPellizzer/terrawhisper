Caesalpinia

Caesalpina, commonly known as peacock flower or poinciana, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated in traditional healing. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Various species are utilized globally to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and infections, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Caesalpinia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is identified by the genus Caesalpinia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Egypt. Its presence extends to the Macaronesian islands, specifically in Cape Verde. The species is also widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Within this western region, it inhabits the countries of Gambia, Ghana, and Ivory Coast.
