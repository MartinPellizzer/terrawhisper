Erodium gruinum

Erodium gruinum, commonly known as stork's-bill, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Geraniaceae family. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is traditionally used in various cultures to treat wounds, skin irritations, and digestive ailments, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Erodium gruinum
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales, it is classified under the family Geraniaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Erodium, and its specific species name, Erodium gruinum.

Common Names

The Czech name for this species is pumpava jeřábí (cs), and in Turkey, it is commonly called kargadıdağı (tr). Additionally, in Hebrew, it is identified as מקור-חסידה גדול (he).
