Plant Parts

This plant, known as Tamarix, is characterized by its unique morphology where the functional leaf structures are highly specialized. Instead of broad, traditional foliage, the plant features minute, scale-like leaves that are tightly pressed against the stem. This reduction in leaf size is an evolutionary adaptation that helps the plant minimize water loss through transpiration, allowing it to thrive in arid and saline environments where its medicinal properties are often sought.
