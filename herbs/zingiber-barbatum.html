Zingiber barbatum

Zingiber barbaatum, commonly known as beard ginger, is a perennial herb belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties, making it a vital resource for treating various digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Zingiber barbatum
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
