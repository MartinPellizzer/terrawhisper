Yellow lotus, scientifically known as Nelumbo lutea, is a perennial aquatic plant native to North America. Renowned for its striking golden blooms and large floating leaves, it holds significant medicinal value. Traditionally, its rhizomes and seeds have been utilized to support digestive health and reduce inflammation in various cultures.
