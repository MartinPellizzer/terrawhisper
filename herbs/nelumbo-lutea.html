Nelumbo lutea

Nelumbo lutea, commonly known as the American lotus, is a majestic aquatic perennial prized for its medicinal properties. Traditionally used in various herbal practices, its leaves, seeds, and rhizomes offer potential therapeutic benefits. This resilient plant flourishes in freshwater habitats, embodying both botanical beauty and significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Nelumbo lutea
  • Family: Nelumbonaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Nelumbo lutea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Proteales. Finally, it is classified under the family Nelumbonaceae and the genus Nelumbo.
