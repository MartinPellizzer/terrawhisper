Cephalaria uralensis

Cephalaeria uraensis, commonly known as the "Chinese medicinal herb," is a significant plant in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. This versatile species plays a crucial role in treating various ailments and advancing modern pharmacological research.
