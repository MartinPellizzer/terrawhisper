Hedge taper, scientifically known as Saponaria officinalis, is a perennial herb valued for its unique soap-like properties. Derived from its saponin-rich roots and leaves, it has historically been used for cleansing. Beyond traditional washing, this plant offers therapeutic potential in treating skin conditions and addressing various inflammatory ailments.

Plant Parts

This plant, Saponaria officinalis, is utilized in various medicinal preparations through the use of its whole plants and its aerial parts. The aerial parts, which consist of the stems, leaves, and flowers growing above the ground, are particularly valued for their high concentration of saponins. These natural surfactants are found throughout the entire structure of the plant, making the collection of the whole plant an effective method for harvesting the bioactive compounds required for its traditional therapeutic uses.
