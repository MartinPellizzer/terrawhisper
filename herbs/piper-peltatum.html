Plant Parts

This plant, known as Piper peltatum, utilizes its large, heart-shaped leaves as its primary medicinal component. These leaves are characterized by their distinct peltate structure, where the petiole attaches to the center of the leaf blade rather than the margin. In traditional herbal practices, these leaves are often harvested and processed to harness their bioactive compounds, which are believed to possess various therapeutic properties.
