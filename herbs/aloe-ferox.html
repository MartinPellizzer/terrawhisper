Aloe ferox

Aloe ferox, commonly known as Cape Aloe, is a large, succulent plant native to South Africa. Renowned for its intense bitterness, it is primarily utilized in traditional medicine as a potent purgative. Its thick, fleshy leaves contain bioactive compounds highly valued for their powerful laxative and dermatological healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Aloe ferox
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Asparagales and placed in the family Asphodelaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Aloe, specifically as the species Aloe ferox.

Distribution

This plant is widely recognized for its presence within the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. Its primary distribution can be found throughout the various Cape Provinces. Additionally, the species is known to inhabit the mountainous regions of Lesotho. It also grows naturally across the expansive plains of the Free State. Consequently, these specific areas define the core geographical range of Aloe ferox.
