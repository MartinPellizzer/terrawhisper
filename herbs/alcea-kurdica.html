Alcea kurdica

Alcea kurdica, commonly known as the Kurdish Hollyhock, is a resilient medicinal herb native to the mountainous regions of the Middle East. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to address various ailments, offering unique phytochemical benefits that support overall wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Alcea kurdica
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Malvaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Alcea.
