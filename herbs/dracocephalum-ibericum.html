Dracocephalum ibericum, scientifically known as Dracocephalum ibericum, is a perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Native to the Caucasus and surrounding regions, this aromatic plant is prized for its essential oils. It holds significant medicinal potential, traditionally used to support digestive health and alleviate various inflammatory conditions.
