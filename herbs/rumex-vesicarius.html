Bladder dock, scientifically known as Rumex vesicarius, is a distinctive perennial herb characterized by its unique, bladder-like seed capsules. Often found in moist or marshy habitats, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its various therapeutic properties, particularly in treating skin ailments and supporting digestive health through its bioactive compounds.
