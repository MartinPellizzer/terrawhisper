Ocotea bullata

Ocotea bullata, commonly known as Canelo, is a majestic evergreen tree native to Chile. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, its bark is rich in tannins and essential oils. Traditionally used to treat inflammation and digestive ailments, this botanical treasure holds significant value in both folk medicine and pharmacology.

  • Native range: Africa
