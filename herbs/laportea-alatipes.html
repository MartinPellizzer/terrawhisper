Laportea alatipes

Laportea alatipes, commonly known as a stinging nettle species, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive stinging hairs, it possesses unique bioactive compounds. Traditionally utilized in folk medicine, this plant offers significant potential for treating inflammation, skin irritations, and various systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Laportea alatipes
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Rosales and the family Urticaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Laportea.

Its presence extends further into the heart of West-Central Tropical Africa within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Additionally, this species is documented in the East African region of Rwanda.
