Musa paradisiaca, scientifically known as Musa × paradisiaca, is a versatile tropical plant prized for its nutrient-dense fruit. Beyond its culinary importance, it offers significant medicinal potential. Rich in potassium and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to aid digestion, manage blood pressure, and support overall metabolic health effectively.

Distribution

This plant, Musa × paradisiaca, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions in Africa. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found within the borders of Burkina, Gambia, Guinea-Bissau, and Guinea. Moving further south into West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence extends into the Central African Republic. These locations highlight the plant's reliance on specific tropical climates found within these nations. Consequently, its range is concentrated within these distinct West and Central African territories.
