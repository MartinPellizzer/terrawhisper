Cymbaria mongolica

Cymbaria mongolica, commonly known as the Mongolian Star-herb, is a resilient perennial prized in traditional pharmacology. Native to the high-altitude steppes, this botanical specimen contains unique bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized to support respiratory health and reduce inflammation, offering a natural remedy rooted in ancient herbal wisdom.

  • Scientific name: Cymbaria mongolica
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Cymbariia.
