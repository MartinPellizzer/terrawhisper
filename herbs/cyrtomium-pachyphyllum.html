What's the taxonomical classification of Cyrtomium pachyphyllum?

Cyrtomium pachyphyllum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. Taxonomically, this plant is situated within the family Polypodiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Cyrtomium, with the species name pachyphyllum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cyrtomium pachyphyllum has thick, leathery, pinnately divided fronds that typically measure between 30 and 60 centimeters in length. The pinnae are broadly lanceolate with entire or slightly wavy margins and a distinctively succulent texture. These large leaves emerge from a creeping rhizome that facilitates spreading through the forest floor. The underside of the foliage contains small, rounded sori protected by indusia, which are characteristic of this fern species. Its overall structure provides a robust, lush appearance suited for shaded, moist environments.
