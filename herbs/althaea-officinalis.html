marsh mallow

Althaea officinalis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (825 studies) · ★★★★★

Marsh mallow, scientifically known as Althaea officinalis, is a perennial herb celebrated for its soothing mucilaginous properties. Traditionally used to calm irritated mucous membranes, it serves as a natural remedy for sore throats and digestive discomfort. This versatile botanical offers gentle, restorative support for respiratory and gastrointestinal wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Althaea officinalis, offers a variety of components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The whole herb contains a complex array of mucilaginous compounds and bioactive constituents that contribute to its overall medicinal profile. Specifically, the marshmallow root is the most highly valued part, prized for its high mucilage content which provides soothing effects on the digestive and respiratory tracts. In addition to the roots, the aerial parts, including the leaves and stems, are frequently used in herbal preparations to support systemic health. Furthermore, specialized flower extract can be derived to harness the specific phytochemicals found within the blossoms, offering a concentrated way to utilize the plant's natural essence.
