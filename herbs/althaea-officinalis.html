Althaea officinalis

Marsh mallow

Althaea officinalis, commonly known as Marshmallow, is a perennial herb cherished for its soothing medicinal properties. Renowned for its high mucilage content, it serves as a gentle remedy for soothing irritated mucous membranes. This plant is traditionally utilized to alleviate coughs, sore throats, and various digestive tract inflammations.

  • Scientific name: Althaea officinalis
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Malvales, and the family Malvacaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Althaea.

Plant Parts

This plant, Althaea officinalis, offers a variety of components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The whole herb contains a complex array of mucilaginous compounds and bioactive constituents that contribute to its overall medicinal profile. Specifically, the marshmallow root is the most highly valued part, prized for its high mucilage content which provides soothing effects on the digestive and respiratory tracts. In addition to the roots, the aerial parts, including the leaves and stems, are frequently used in herbal preparations to support systemic health. Furthermore, specialized flower extract can be derived to harness the specific phytochemicals found within the blossoms, offering a concentrated way to utilize the plant's natural essence.
