Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several distinct regions of the African continent. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, its range extends from the Democratic Republic of the Congo to the island territories of the Gulf of Guinea. It can also be found in the landlocked areas of Burundi and Rwanda within the same West-Central zone. Furthermore, the species reaches into Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically documented in Ethiopia. This distribution highlights the plant's ability to inhabit diverse ecological settings across both central and eastern regions.
