Chinese dandelion, scientifically known as Taraxacum sinicum, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its cooling and detoxifying properties, it is frequently utilized to support liver health, reduce inflammation, and promote urinary tract function, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and vitality.
