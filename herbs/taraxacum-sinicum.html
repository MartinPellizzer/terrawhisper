Taraxacum sinicum

Taraxacum sinicum, commonly known as the Chinese dandelion, is a revered medicinal herb in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, this plant is frequently utilized to support liver health, boost immunity, and clear toxins. Its versatile roots and leaves offer significant therapeutic potential in wellness.

  • Scientific name: Taraxacum sinicum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae, it is classified under the genus Taraxacum.

Distribution
