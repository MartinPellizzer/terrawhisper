Viburnum sympodiale

What's the taxonomical classification of Viburnum sympodiale?

Viburnum sympodiale belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. At the class level, it is categorized as Equisetopsida, falling under the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized into the order Dipsacales and is a member of the family Viburnaceaae. Within this family, it is placed in the genus Viburnum, and its specific designation is the species sympodiale.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Viburnum sympodiale has woody, spreading stems with rough, gray-brown bark and opposite, ovate-to-oblong leaves that measure roughly 5 to 15 centimeters in length. During the flowering season, the plant produces small, white or cream-colored flowers arranged in dense, flat-topped cymes. These inflorescences are followed by the development of fleshy, drupe-like fruits that transition in color as they mature.
