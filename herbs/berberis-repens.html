Berberis repens, scientifically known as Berberis repens, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to many temperate regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant contains bioactive compounds like berberine. It is traditionally used in herbal medicine to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall metabolic wellness.
