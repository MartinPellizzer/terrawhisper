Artemisia judaica, scientifically known as Artemisia judaica, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional folk medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues, respiratory distress, and inflammatory conditions.
