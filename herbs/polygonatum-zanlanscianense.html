Polygonatum zanlanscianense

Polygonatum zanlanscianense, commonly known as Solomon's Seal, is a highly valued medicinal herb in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Native to mountainous regions, its rhizomes are rich in polysaccharides and saponins. It is widely utilized to nourish Yin, replenish fluids, and support kidney health, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Polygonatum zanlanscianense
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
