Piper trinervium

Piper trinervium, commonly known as Three-nerved Pepper, is a significant medicinal herb native to the tropical regions of Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally used in Ayurvedic medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering valuable therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Piper trinervium
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Piperaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus name, Piper.
