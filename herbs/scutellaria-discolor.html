Scutellaria discolor, scientifically known as Scutellaria discolor, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of East Asia. Distinguished by its unique, patterned foliage, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its calming properties, often utilized to support neurological health and alleviate various inflammatory conditions.
