Japanese flowering-quince

Chaenomeles japonica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (230 studies) · ★★★★★

Japanese flowering-quince, scientifically known as Chaenomeles japonica, is a deciduous shrub prized in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant's fruits and leaves are frequently utilized to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall wellness through their rich concentration of bioactive compounds.
