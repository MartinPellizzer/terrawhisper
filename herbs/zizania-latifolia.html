Zizania latifolia

Zizania latifolia, commonly known as wild rice or Manchurian wild rice, is a versatile aquatic plant prized for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in essential vitamins and minerals, it is traditionally used in various cultures to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall metabolic balance and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Zizania latifolia
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Zizaniia latifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Poales, it is further categorized under the family Poaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Zizaniia.
