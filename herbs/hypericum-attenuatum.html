Hypericum attenuatum

Hypericum attenuatum, commonly known as the slender St. John's wort, is a perennial herb native to the Western United States. Distinguished by its delicate, yellow flowers and narrow leaves, this plant belongs to the Hypericaceae family. It thrives in montane environments, playing a vital role in local ecosystems.
