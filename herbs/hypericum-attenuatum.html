Hypericum attenuatum

Hypericum attenuatum, commonly known as the slender St. John's wort, is a perennial herb native to the Western United States. Distinguished by its delicate, yellow flowers and narrow leaves, this plant belongs to the Hypericaceae family. It thrives in montane environments, playing a vital role in local ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum attenuatum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its lineage through the subclass Magnoliidae and order Malpighiales, it is a member of the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Hypericum.
