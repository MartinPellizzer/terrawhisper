Tabernaemontana bovina

Tabernaemontana bovina, commonly known as the crown flower or milky vine, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its white, fragrant blossoms, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Tabernaemontana bovina
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
