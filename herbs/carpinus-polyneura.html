What's the taxonomical classification of Carpinus polyneura?

Carpinus polyneura belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the biological lineage of the order Fagales. This plant is a part of the family Betulaceae, which is characterized by its specific reproductive structures. Within this family, it is placed in the genus Carpinus, and its unique botanical identity is finalized by its specific designation as the species polyneura.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Carpinus polyneura has simple, ovate to elliptic leaves with serrated margins and prominent, multiple parallel venation patterns. The foliage typically exhibits a bright green color on the upper surface and a slightly paler underside. Its branches are woody and develop a rugged texture as the plant matures. The reproductive structures consist of small, inconspicuous flowers that eventually produce nutlets. Each nutlet is attached to a specialized, wing-like bract that facilitates wind dispersal.
