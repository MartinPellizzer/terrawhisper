What's the taxonomical classification of Lindera thomsonii?

Lindera thomsonii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It is further categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Laurales, it falls into the family Lauraceae. Specifically, this plant is a member of the genus Lindera, with its scientific designation completed by the specific epithet thomsonii.
