Plant Parts

This plant, Artemisia alba subsp. alba, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties which are concentrated within its various botanical components. The most significant therapeutic compounds are found in the essential oils, which are extracted from the plant's chemical profile to provide its characteristic aroma and biological activity. These oils are most abundantly distributed throughout the leaves, which serve as the primary site for secondary metabolite production. Consequently, when harvesting the specimen for medicinal use, practitioners typically collect the entire aerial parts, ensuring that the foliage and stems are gathered to maximize the potency of the bioactive constituents.
