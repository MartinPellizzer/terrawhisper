Solidago virgaurea

Solidago virgaurea, commonly known as European goldenrod, is a perennial herb prized for its vibrant yellow blooms. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this plant offers potent anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties. Often used to support kidney and urinary tract health, it remains a vital botanical staple in natural wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Solidago virgaurea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific biological grouping is defined by the genus Solidago.
