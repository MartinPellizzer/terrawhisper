Goldenrod, scientifically known as Solidago virgaurea, is a perennial herb prized in traditional herbalism for its vibrant yellow blooms. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is primarily utilized to support urinary tract health, reduce inflammation, and soothe digestive issues, making it a cornerstone of botanical medicine across Europe.
