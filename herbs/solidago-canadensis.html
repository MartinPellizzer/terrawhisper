Solidago canadensis

Solidago canadensis, commonly known as goldenrod, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for its vibrant, plume-like yellow flowers. Widely distributed across North America, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, often utilized to support urinary tract health and ease seasonal allergies.

  • Scientific name: Solidago canadensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Solidago canadensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the Asteraceae family and falls under the genus Solidago.
