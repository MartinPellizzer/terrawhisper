Canada goldenrod, scientifically known as Solidago canadensis, is a versatile perennial herb renowned for its vibrant, plume-like yellow flowers. Widely distributed across North America, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, often utilized to support urinary tract health and soothe skin irritations.
