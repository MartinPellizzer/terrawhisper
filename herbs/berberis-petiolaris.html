Berberis petiolaris

Berberis petiolaris, commonly known as the Petiolaria barberry, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its striking yellow blooms and thorny stems, this ornamental plant also holds ethnobotanical significance. Its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic properties, making it a subject of interest in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Berberis petiolaris
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is specifically situated in the family Berberidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Berberis.

Common Names

This plant, Berberis petiolaris, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the petiolate barberry, while in Chinese, it is known as 刺黄柏 (cì huáng bǎi).
