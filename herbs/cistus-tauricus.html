Cistus tauricus

Cistus tauricus, commonly known as the Taurus Rockrose, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub prized for its aromatic foliage and delicate flowers. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a valuable botanical resource for supporting skin health and holistic wellness through natural extracts.

  • Scientific name: Cistus tauricus
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cistus tauricus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cistaceae and the genus Cistus.
