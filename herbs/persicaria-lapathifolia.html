Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range across the northern regions of Europe. Within Northern Europe, its presence is documented in Denmark and Finland. The species can also be found in the Faroe Islands, known locally as Føroyar. Furthermore, its distribution extends to the island of Great Britain. Finally, it is recorded as being present in Ireland.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as large knotweed, is primarily characterized by its distinctive leaf structure, which serves as a key identification feature. The leaf is typically large, ovate to lanceolate in shape, and possesses a smooth margin, often displaying a characteristic dark, tear-shaped blotch in the center. In medicinal applications, the leaf is the part most frequently utilized, as it contains various bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's traditional therapeutic properties.
