Coptis chinensis

Coptis chinensis, commonly known as Goldthread, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its high berberine content, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This medicinal plant is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments, infections, and metabolic disorders through various therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Coptis chinensis
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Coptis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Coptis chinensis, is recognized by several different common names depending on the region and language. In simplified Chinese (zh-hans), it is called 黄连, while in traditional Chinese (zh-hant) and the Cantonese-speaking region of Hong Kong (zh-hk), it is referred to as 黃連. In mainland China (zh), the name is also written as 黄连, and in Vietnam (vi), it is known as Hoàng liên Trung Quốc.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions of China. Its distribution includes extensive areas within China South-Central and China North-Central. Additionally, it can be located throughout the China Southeast region. Beyond the borders of China, its presence extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the country of Vietnam.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Coptis chinensis, is a perennial herb where the most medicinally significant parts are the underground structures. The medicinal value is primarily concentrated in the rhizome, specifically the thick, yellowish-colored rhizomes that are harvested and dried for use in traditional remedies. While the root system provides structural support, it is the tuberous nature of the rhizome that contains the highest concentration of alkaloids. Above the ground, the plant features distinct green leaves, which are typically divided into multiple leaflets; each individual leaf is characterized by its shape and arrangement, though they are less commonly used for medicinal purposes than the underground parts.
