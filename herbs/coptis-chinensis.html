Chinese goldthread

Coptis chinensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (682 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese goldthread, scientifically known as Coptis chinensis, is a perennial herb revered in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its high berberine content, this plant offers potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. It is widely utilized to treat gastrointestinal issues, infections, and metabolic disorders, serving as a cornerstone in herbal pharmacology.
