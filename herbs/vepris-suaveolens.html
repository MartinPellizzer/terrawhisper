Vepris suaveolens

Vepris suaveolens, commonly known as the fragrant Vepris, is a significant medicinal shrub prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant contains bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. Its essential oils and botanical extracts offer therapeutic potential in managing inflammation and supporting overall wellness.

  • Family: Rutaceae

This plant, Vepris suaveolens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification is completed within the genus Vepris.
