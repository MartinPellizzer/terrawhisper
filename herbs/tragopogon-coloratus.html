Tragopogon coloratus

Tragopogon coloratus, commonly known as the Colorado goatsbeard, is a resilient perennial herb native to western North American prairies. Renowned for its striking, dandelion-like yellow blooms, this botanical treasure is valued in ethnobotany for its diverse therapeutic properties, offering potential medicinal benefits within its nutrient-rich, fibrous root systems.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
