Heliotropium crispum

Heliotropium crispum, commonly known as the Curly Heliotrope, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its unique foliage and delicate blooms, this plant contains bioactive compounds traditionally utilized in ethnobotany for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Heliotropium crispum
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Boraginales, it is classified under the family Boraginaceae. It is further identified by its genus, Heliotropium, specifically as the species Heliotropium crispum.
