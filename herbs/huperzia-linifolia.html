Huperzia linifolia

Huperzia linifolia, commonly known as the Linifolia Clubmoss, is a fascinating medicinal plant prized for its neuroprotective properties. Rich in the alkaloid huperzine A, it is traditionally utilized to enhance cognitive function and treat memory impairments. This ancient botanical offers significant potential in modern pharmacological research for neurological health.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia linifolia
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
