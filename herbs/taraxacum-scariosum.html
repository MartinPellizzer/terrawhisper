Taraxacum scariosum

Taraxacum scariosum, commonly known as a rare dandelion variant, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Characterized by its specialized leaf morphology, this species offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. Understanding its botanical nuances is essential for exploring its potential in traditional and modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Taraxacum scariosum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Taraxacum.

Distribution

This plant, Taraxacum scariosum, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the vast landscapes of Siberia. Its presence is documented within the mountainous regions of the Altai. Additionally, the species can be found spreading through the territory of Buryatiya. It also inhabits the eastern stretches of Siberia, specifically within the Chita region. Finally, the plant's range extends into the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk areas.
