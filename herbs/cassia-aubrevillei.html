Cassia aubrevillei

Cassia aubrevillei, commonly known as the African cassia, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues. Its leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds essential for healing.

  • Scientific name: Cassia aubrevillei
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
