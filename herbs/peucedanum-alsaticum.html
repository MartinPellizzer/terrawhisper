Peucedanum alsaticum

Peucedanum alsaticum, commonly known as Alsatian celandine, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Found in limestone-rich habitats across Europe, this plant is characterized by its delicate yellow umbels. While historically noted in ethnobotany, it is primarily valued today for its unique ecological presence in biodiversity studies.

  • Scientific name: Peucedanum alsaticum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is further nested within the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Peucedanum.
