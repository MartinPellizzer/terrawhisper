Pandanus tectorius

Pandanus tectorius, commonly known as the Screw Pine, is a versatile tropical plant cherished for its medicinal and practical uses. Found across Indo-Pacific coasts, its fragrant leaves and fruits offer therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits, while serving as a vital resource for traditional healing and crafts.

  • Scientific name: Pandanus tectorius
  • Family: Pandanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its descent through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Pandanales, it is a member of the family Pandanaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Pandanus.
