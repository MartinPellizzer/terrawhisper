Ligularia nelumbifolia

Ligularia nelumbifolia, commonly known as the giant ligularia, is a striking perennial celebrated for its massive, lotus-like leaves. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant traditional medicinal value in various cultures, often utilized for its potent bioactive compounds to treat inflammatory conditions and support overall digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ligularia nelumbifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
