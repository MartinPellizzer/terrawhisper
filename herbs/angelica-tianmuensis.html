Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted in both the North-Central and Southeast territories. Beyond the Chinese mainland, the species extends its range into the island nation of Japan. Additionally, it can be found growing within the borders of Korea. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Angelica tiaomuenensis.
