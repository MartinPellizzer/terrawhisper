Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted in both the North-Central and Southeast territories. Beyond the Chinese mainland, the species extends its range into the island nation of Japan. Additionally, it can be found growing within the borders of Korea. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Angelica tiaomuenensis.

Plant Parts

This plant, Angelica tianmuenensis, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties, which are concentrated in its underground structures. The most significant part used in traditional applications is the root, which is harvested and processed to extract its therapeutic compounds. These roots serve as the main source of bioactive constituents, making them the essential component for preparing various herbal remedies.
