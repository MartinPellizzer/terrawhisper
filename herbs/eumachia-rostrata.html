Eumachia rostrata

Eumachia rostrata, commonly known as Euchromyma pilosum, is a significant medicinal herb native to East Asian mountain regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this perennial plant is frequently utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in supporting digestive health, reducing inflammation, and promoting overall vitality through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Eumachia rostrata
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianaales and is a member of the family Rubiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Eumachia as Eumachia rostrata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is specifically noted in Thailand. It is also widely distributed throughout the Malesia zone, which includes several significant islands and peninsulas. Specifically, the species can be found growing in Jawa and across the island of Sumatera. Furthermore, its range extends into the Malay Peninsula, marking its presence in the Malay Peninsula area.
