Sorbus aucuparia

Sorbus aucuparia, commonly known as the mountain ash or rowan, is a resilient deciduous tree valued for its medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and vitamin C, its berries are traditionally used to support digestive health and immune function, offering a natural remedy rooted in ancient herbal wisdom and folklore.

  • Scientific name: Sorbus aucuparia
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sorbus.
