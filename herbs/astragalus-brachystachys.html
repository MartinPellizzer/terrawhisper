Astragalus brachystachys

Astragalus brachystachys, commonly known as a species of milkvetch, is a resilient medicinal plant native to specific arid regions. Valued in traditional herbalism, it is often studied for its potential immune-modulating properties. This hardy perennial offers unique phytochemical compounds that contribute to its significant role in natural pharmacology.
