Magnolia denudata

Yulan magnolia

Magnolia denudata, commonly known as the Denudata Magnolia, is a deciduous flowering plant cherished for its large, fragrant white blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its bark and flowers are utilized for their various therapeutic properties and bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia denudata
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, it is positioned in the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Magnolia, under the specific species name Magnolia denudata.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Magnolia denudata, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. Plant Parts

This plant, Magnolia denudata, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for the therapeutic properties found within its flower buds. These buds are harvested to extract concentrated essential oils, which contain the bioactive compounds responsible for the plant's calming and aromatic effects. The quality and potency of the essential oil derived from these buds are crucial for its medicinal application, making the careful collection of the floral structures a vital part of its use.
