Yulan magnolia

Magnolia denudata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (608 studies) · ★★★★★

Yuulan magnolia, scientifically known as Magnolia denudaata, is a deciduous flowering tree native to East Asia. Renowned for its large, fragrant white blooms, it holds significant value in traditional Chinese medicine. Its flower buds are widely utilized to treat respiratory ailments, regulate menstruation, and promote overall blood circulation.
