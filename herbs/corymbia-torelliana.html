Corymbia torelliana

Corymbia torelliana, commonly known as the tornillo, is a majestic eucalyptus species native to Australia. While primarily valued for its impressive timber and canopy, it holds significance in traditional ecological knowledge. This robust tree plays a vital role in supporting biodiversity and offers potential medicinal insights through its bioactive compounds.
