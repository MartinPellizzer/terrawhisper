Prunus cerasus

Sour cherry

Prunus cerasus, commonly known as the sour cherry, is a versatile fruit-bearing shrub valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and anthocyanins, it serves as a potent natural remedy for reducing inflammation, supporting cardiovascular health, and promoting restful sleep through its unique melatonin content.

  • Scientific name: Prunus cerasus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Prunus.
