Rhamnus ussuriensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Rhamnus ussuriensis?

Rhamnus ussuriensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Rosales and the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Rhamnus, and its specific species designation, ussuriensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rhamnus ussuriensis has an erect, deciduous shrub reaching heights of up to 3 meters, characterized by grayish-brown bark and oval-shaped leaves with serrated margins. The foliage is typically arranged oppositely along the slender, woody branches. During the flowering season, small, inconspicuous yellowish-green flowers emerge in clusters from the leaf axils. These blossoms eventually develop into small, fleshy drupes that turn from green to a deep black color upon maturity. The plant's overall structure is dense and spreading, providing a robust architectural form in its natural habitat.
