Thunder God Vine

Tripterygium wilfordii · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (482 studies) · ★★★★★

Thunder God Vine, scientifically known as Tripterygium wilfordii, is a deciduous climbing vine native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, it contains potent bioactive alkaloids. Modern research focuses on its significant anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive properties, making it a valuable subject for treating autoimmune diseases and rheumatoid arthritis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout various regions of China, including the South-Central and Southeast areas. Its presence extends into the high-altitude territories of Tibet as well. Beyond mainland China, the species can be found across Eastern Asia, specifically in Taiwan. Additionally, its range reaches into the Indo-China region, encompassing Myanmar. Together, these locations define the specific natural habitat of Tripterygium wilfordii.

Plant Parts

This plant, Tripterygium wilfordii, is a woody climber that contains various parts used in traditional medicine. The therapeutic properties are most commonly derived from the root bark, which is harvested and processed for its bioactive compounds. In addition to the bark, the root and the extensive underground roots system serve as primary sources for medicinal extraction. Above the ground, the plant features climbing stem structures that support its growth, as well as broad leaves that characterize its foliage.
