Lilium davidii

Lilium davidii, commonly known as Davidii Lily, is a striking perennial prized for its elegant, bell-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this species holds historical significance in traditional practices. Understanding its botanical characteristics and potential therapeutic properties offers fascinating insights into the intersection of horticulture and medicine.

  • Scientific name: Lilium davidii
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Liliales and the family Liliaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Lilium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across various regions of Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central territories to the North-Central provinces. Its presence also extends into the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented within the state of Assam. Additionally, it thrives throughout the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya.
