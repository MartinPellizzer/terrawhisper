Ipomoea corymbosa

Ipomoea corymbosa, commonly known as the morning glory, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. This plant offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea corymbosa
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Ipomoea, with the species name corymbosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In the Macaronesian region, its presence is noted specifically within the Canary Islands. Its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward South Tropical Africa, the species is also documented in Mozambique. Finally, it inhabits parts of the Western Indian Ocean, including Mauritius and Rodrigues.
