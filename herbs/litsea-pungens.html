Litsea pungens

Litsea pungens, commonly known as the pungent bay, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant contains essential oils with potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures to support digestive health and soothe skin irritations.

  • Scientific name: Litsea pungens
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Litsea.

Furthermore, it is widely distributed throughout the Southeast regions of China. The species also maintains a notable presence in the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Beyond China, its range continues into the Indo-China region, specifically within Myanmar.
