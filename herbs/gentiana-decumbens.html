Gentiana decumbens

Gentiana decumbens, commonly known as a member of the gentian family, is a resilient medicinal herb found in high-altitude terrains. Renowned for its potent bitter properties, it is traditionally utilized to stimulate digestion, support liver function, and alleviate gastrointestinal discomfort, offering valuable therapeutic potential in holistic herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana decumbens
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Gentianales and falls into the family Gentianaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gentiana, specifically as the species Gentiana decumbens.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout the vast regions of Eastern Europe, specifically within East European Russia. Its distribution extends significantly into the expansive territories of Siberia. Within this Siberian region, it can be located in the mountainous areas of Altay. Furthermore, the species is documented in the Republic of Buryatiya and the region of Chita. Finally, its presence is also confirmed in the Irkutsk oblast of Siberia.
