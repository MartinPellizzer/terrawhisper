East Indian screwtree, scientifically known as Helicteres isora, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned in traditional Ayurveda, its various parts—including leaves, bark, and roots—possess potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable resource for treating skin ailments and digestive disorders.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across parts of Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central regions as well as on Hainan island. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is native to Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is commonly documented throughout various parts of India. Finally, its natural habitat also includes the mountainous terrain of Nepal.
