Helicteres isora

Helicteres isora, commonly known as the "Sarpagandha" of certain regions, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional Ayurveda. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is primarily utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and fever, offering a natural approach to managing various systemic health concerns.

  • Scientific name: Helicteres isora
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Helicteres isora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. It is further categorized into the genus Helicteres.

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across parts of Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central regions as well as on Hainan island.
