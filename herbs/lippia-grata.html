Lippia grata

Lippia gratia, commonly known as gratia or gratia herb, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments and skin infections, offering a natural remedy rooted in deep botanical heritage.

  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
