Melaleuca linariifolia

Melaleuca linariifolia, commonly known as Snow-in-Summer, is a striking evergreen shrub native to Australia. Renowned for its profuse white floral displays, it serves more than just an aesthetic purpose. In traditional medicine, its aromatic oils possess potent antiseptic and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable botanical healer.

  • Scientific name: Melaleuca linariifolia
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Melaleuca.

Distribution

This plant, commonly known as tea tree, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions. In its native habitat, it is widely found throughout various parts of Australia. Specifically, it thrives within the coastal and inland areas of New South Wales. The species also populates significant portions of Queensland, where the climate supports its growth. Beyond its native range, it has been introduced to the Southeastern United States, particularly in Florida.
