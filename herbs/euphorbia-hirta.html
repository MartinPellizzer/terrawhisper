Common Spurge

Euphorbia hirta · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (640 studies) · ★★★★★

Common Spurge, scientifically known as Euphorbia hirta, is a widespread, herbaceous plant celebrated in traditional medicine. Often found in tropical regions, it is rich in bioactive compounds. Traditionally, it is used to treat respiratory ailments, gastrointestinal issues, and skin inflammations, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.
