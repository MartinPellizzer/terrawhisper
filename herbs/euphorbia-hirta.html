Euphorbia hirta

Common spurge

Euphorbia hirta, commonly known as asthma weed or pantotan, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and bronchodilatory properties. This resilient plant is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin infections throughout various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia hirta
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is documented across the countries of Egypt, Libya, and Morocco. The species also extends into the Macaronesia region, specifically found in Cape Verde. Furthermore, its range reaches into West Tropical Africa, where it is located in Benin. Together, these locations highlight the wide environmental adaptability of the species.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as asthma weed, is highly valued in traditional medicine, where the whole plant is often utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Each individual leaf contains bioactive compounds that contribute to its healing potential, and when the stems are broken, a milky latex is released, which is frequently used in topical applications. Practitioners often prepare decoctions using the whole plant excluding roots to treat respiratory and gastrointestinal ailments, ensuring that the most potent aerial portions are concentrated for medicinal use.
