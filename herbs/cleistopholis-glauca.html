Cleistopholis glauca

Cleistopholis glauca, commonly known as the "Kandis" tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Valued in traditional healing practices, its various parts possess potent bioactive compounds. Researchers study this species for its potential antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, highlighting its importance in modern pharmacological development.
