Machilus duthiei

Machilus duthiei, commonly known as the Dutheia tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues, offering valuable insights for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Machilus duthiei
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Laurales, it is classified under the family Lauraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Machilus.
