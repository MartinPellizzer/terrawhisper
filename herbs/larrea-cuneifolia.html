Larrea cuneifolia

Larrea cuniefolia, commonly known as the creosote bush, is a resilient shrub native to the arid landscapes of the Americas. Renowned for its ability to thrive in harsh environments, this plant possesses significant medicinal properties, traditionally used for its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant qualities in various healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Larrea cuneifolia
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zygophyllales, it is specifically positioned in the family Zygophyllaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Larrea.
