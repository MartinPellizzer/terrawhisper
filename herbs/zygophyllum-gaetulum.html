Zygophyllum gaetulum

Zygophyllum gaetulum, commonly known as the desert stone, is a resilient succulent native to arid African landscapes. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues. Its unique ability to thrive in harsh environments makes it ecologically significant.

  • Scientific name: Zygophyllum gaetulum
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zygophyllales, it is classified under the family Zygophyllaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Zygophyllum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located across several regions within Northern Africa. Specifically, its range includes the territories of Algeria and Morocco. It can also be found extending into the area of Western Sahara. Beyond the African mainland, its distribution reaches the Macaronesian islands. Within this island group, the species is documented in both the Canary Islands and Cape Verde.
