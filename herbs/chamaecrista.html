Chamaecrista

Chamaecrista, commonly known as part of the legume family, encompasses a diverse genus of herbaceous and shrubby plants. Renowned for their vibrant yellow flowers, these species are vital in traditional medicine. They possess significant bioactive compounds, offering therapeutic potential in treating inflammation, infections, and various metabolic ailments globally.

  • Scientific name: Chamaecrista
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In the Macaronesian archipelago, its presence is noted in both the Canary Islands and Cape Verde. Moving toward the African mainland, it can be found throughout parts of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, the species occurs in the nations of Benin and Burkina. Additionally, its range extends to include the territory of Gambia.
