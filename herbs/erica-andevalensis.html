Category:erica andevalensis

Erica andevalensis, commonly known as the Andevalensis Heath, is a distinctive shrub belonging to the Ericaceae family. Native to specific montane regions, this resilient plant thrives in acidic, nutrient-poor soils. It is valued for its unique floral structure and holds potential significance in traditional ethnobotanical practices and ecological studies.
