Blumea eriantha

Blumea eriantha, commonly known as the hairy Blumea, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves possess significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and skin infections, this plant remains a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Blumea eriantha
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
