What's the taxonomical classification of Scrophularia macrocarpa?

Scrophularia macrocarpa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the biological hierarchy, it is classified in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic ranks, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Scrophularia, and its unique species designation, macrocarpa.

Small, tubular flowers emerge from the upper leaf axils, displaying a characteristic reddish-brown or yellowish hue.
