Piper regnellii

Piper regnellii, commonly known as West African pepper or African black pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its pungent aroma, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and inflammation, serving as both a culinary spice and therapeutic agent.

  • Scientific name: Piper regnellii
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is a member of the family Piperaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Piper, with the specific species being Piper regnellii.
