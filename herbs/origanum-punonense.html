Origanum punonense

Origanum punonense, commonly known as Puno oregano, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Peruvian Andes. Renowned for its potent aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering unique therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Origanum punonense
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Origanum punonense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Origanum.
