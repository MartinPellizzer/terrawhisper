Rubber-hedge euphorbia, scientifically known as Euphorbia tirucaill, is a succulent shrub native to tropical regions. Characterized by its slender, leafless green stems, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its milky latex. Often used to treat skin ailments and inflammation, it remains a fascinating subject in ethnobotany.
