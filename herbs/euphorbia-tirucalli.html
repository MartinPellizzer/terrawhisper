Euphorbia tirucalli

Rubber-hedge euphorbia

Euphorbia tirucailli, commonly known as the Firestick Euphorbia, is a striking succulent native to Africa. Characterized by its slender, leafless green stems, this plant is prized for its ornamental beauty. In traditional medicine, its milky latex is carefully utilized for its potent bioactive compounds to treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia tirucalli
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and specifically identified by the genus Euphorbia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Firestick Euphorbia, is characterized by its unique succulent structure where the primary photosynthetic functions are carried out by its slender, cylindrical green stems. These stems are leafless and highly specialized, acting as the main body of the plant to store water and nutrients. When the stems are broken or injured, the plant immediately exudes a thick, milky sap. This viscous substance is actually a specialized latex that serves as a defense mechanism against herbivores. Because this latex can be highly irritating to the skin and eyes, it is a defining biological feature of the Euphorbia tirucalli species.
