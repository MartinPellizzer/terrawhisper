Sausage Tree, scientifically known as Kigelia africana, is a remarkable African species celebrated for its massive, pendulous fruits. Renowned in traditional medicine, this botanical wonder offers diverse therapeutic properties, ranging from anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects to skin-healing benefits, making it a vital subject for modern pharmacological research and ethnobotany.
