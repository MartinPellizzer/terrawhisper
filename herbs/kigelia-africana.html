Kigelia africana

Kigelia africana, commonly known as the sausage tree, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to sub-Saharan Africa. Renowned for its large, pendulous fruits, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat skin conditions, inflammation, and hormonal imbalances, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Kigelia africana
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Kigelia africana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Bignoniaceae and is categorized under the genus Kigelia.

Distribution

This plant, known as Kigelia africana, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin and Burkina Faso.
