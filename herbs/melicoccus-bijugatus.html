Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as the Bael tree, contains various components used in traditional medicine, but the fruit pulp is particularly significant. The fruit pulp is thick, aromatic, and possesses a unique sticky consistency that is highly valued for its therapeutic properties. In various medicinal practices, this pulp is utilized for its cooling effects and its ability to aid digestion, particularly in treating gastrointestinal issues such as diarrhea and dysentery.
