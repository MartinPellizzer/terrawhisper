Melicoccus bijugatus

Melicoccus bijugatus, commonly known as the Bijuga or Bijugá, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for healing.

  • Scientific name: Melicoccus bijugatus
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Sapindaceae. Specifically, Melicoccus bijugatus is a member of the genus Melicoccus.
