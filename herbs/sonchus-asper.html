Spiny sowthistle

Sonchus asper · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (12 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Spiny sowthistle, scientifically known as Sonchus asper, is a resilient perennial herb common across the Mediterranean. Characterized by its prickly, serrated leaves and bright yellow composite flowers, this plant has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various skin ailments through its bioactive compounds.
