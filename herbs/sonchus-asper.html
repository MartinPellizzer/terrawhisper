Sonchus asper

Spiny sowthistle

Sonchus asper, commonly known as the prickly sow thistle, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its jagged, spiny leaves, this plant is rich in bioactive compounds. It is widely utilized for its anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and digestive properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Sonchus asper
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sonchus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Sonchus asper, is recognized by various common names across different languages and regions. In Chinese, it is referred to as 花叶滇苦菜 (zh-hans), 花葉滇苦菜 (zh-hant), and 花葉滇苦菜 (zh-hk). In Polish, it is known as Mlecz kolczasty (pl), while in the Qinghai-Tibetan language, it is called Yuyu khana (qu).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the northern reaches of the European continent. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland regions of Denmark. Its presence extends further north to include the territories of Finland and Iceland. Additionally, the species is documented within the archipelago of the Førøyar. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant encompasses Great Britain.
