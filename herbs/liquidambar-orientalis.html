Liquidambar orientalis

Liquidambar orientalis, commonly known as the Oriental sweetgum, is a deciduous tree valued for its aromatic resin and medicinal properties. Historically utilized in traditional healing, its fragrant bark and leaves offer therapeutic potential. This species serves as a fascinating subject for studying plant-based compounds and their diverse pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Liquidambar orientalis
  • Family: Altingiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Saxifrages and the family Altingiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Liquidambar.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Liquidambar orientalis, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for the exudate produced from its bark. The most significant medicinal component is the styrax gummi, a fragrant, balsamic resin that is harvested by making incisions in the trunk. This gum is prized for its aromatic properties and is frequently utilized in various therapeutic applications, ranging from topical treatments to respiratory aids.
