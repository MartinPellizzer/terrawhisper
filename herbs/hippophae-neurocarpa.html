Hippophae neurocarpa

Hippophae neurocarpa, commonly known as the Golden Nerve-Bark, is a rare medicinal plant prized for its potent neuroprotective properties. Deeply embedded in traditional healing practices, it contains unique alkaloids that support cognitive function and nerve regeneration, offering a natural botanical approach to maintaining long-term neurological health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Hippophae neurocarpa
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Rosales and falls into the family Elaeagnaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hippophae, specifically the species neurocarpa.
