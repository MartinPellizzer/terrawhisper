The taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. Finally, this plant is situated within the genus Parnassia, specifically identified by its unique species designation, davidii.

The petals are typically white and are characterized by intricate vein patterns and small spots. The nectar scales are crucial for attracting pollinators and are located at the center of the bloom.
