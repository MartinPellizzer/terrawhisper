Jacquemont's Cobra-Lily, scientifically known as Arisaema jacquemontii, is a striking perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Characterized by its unique spathe and spadix, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest. While possessing bioactive compounds, it requires careful handling due to the presence of irritating calcium oxalate crystals.
