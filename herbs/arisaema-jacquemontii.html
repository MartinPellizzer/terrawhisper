Arisaema jacquemontii

Arisaema jacquemontii, commonly known as the Jack-in-the-pulpit relative, is a striking perennial herb native to high-altitude Himalayan regions. Characterized by its unique spathe and spadix, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. While used in traditional remedies, its potent bioactive compounds require careful handling due to inherent toxicity.

  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
