Leucas lavandulifolia

Leucas lavandulifolia, commonly known as Lavender-leaf Leucas, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its aromatic properties. Characterized by its distinct, lavender-scented foliage, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address various ailments, showcasing the profound botanical richness of its natural habitat.

  • Scientific name: Leucas lavandulifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, specifically falling into the family Lamiaceae. Within this botanical hierarchy, it is identified by the genus Leucas.
