Daucus carota

Daucus carota, commonly known as wild carrot or Queen Anne's lace, is a versatile biennial herb. While frequently recognized for its delicate white umbels, this plant holds significant botanical importance. It serves as a wild relative to the cultivated carrot, offering unique phytochemical properties used in traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Daucus carota
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Apiales, it is further organized into the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Daucus.
