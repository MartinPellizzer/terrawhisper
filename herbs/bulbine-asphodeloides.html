Plant Parts

This plant, known as Bulbine asphodeloides, is characterized primarily by its succulent foliage, which serves as a key medicinal component. The leaf is fleshy, elongated, and typically grows in a basal rosette, containing a high concentration of bioactive compounds such as alkaloids and polysaccharides. Because the leaf stores significant amounts of moisture and nutrients, it is the part most frequently utilized in traditional remedies for treating skin irritations, inflammation, and various topical ailments.
