Bulbine asphodeloides

Snake flower

Bulbine asphedeloides, commonly known as Bulbine or Bulbine lily, is a versatile succulent native to South Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is widely utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. This hardy plant is frequently applied topically to treat skin conditions, wounds, and various inflammatory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Bulbine asphodeloides
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asphodelaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Bulbine.
