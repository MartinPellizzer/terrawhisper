Crepidiastrum sonchifolium

Crepidiascrum sonchiifolium, commonly known as Sonchi or Sonchifolia, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties, this plant is widely used to treat digestive issues, skin ailments, and fever, offering a natural approach to holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Crepidiastrum sonchifolium
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Crepidiastrum.
