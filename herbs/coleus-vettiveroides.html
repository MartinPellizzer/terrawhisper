Shiva's Gift, scientifically known as Coleus vettiveroides, is a medicinal shrub native to the tropical regions of India. Renowned for its aromatic, minty fragrance, this plant is highly valued in traditional Ayurveda. It possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, making it essential for treating various skin and respiratory ailments.
