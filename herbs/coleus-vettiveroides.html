Coleus vettiveroides

Coleus vettiveroides, commonly known as Vetiver Coleus, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South Asia. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is widely used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. This perennial herb offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant activities, making it a valuable resource in natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Coleus vettiveroides
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Coleus.
