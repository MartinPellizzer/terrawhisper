Fritillaria anhuiensis

Fritillaria anhuiensis, commonly known as a rare medicinal lily, is an endemic species found in specific high-altitude regions. Valued in traditional practices, its bulbs contain unique alkaloids used to treat respiratory ailments. This precious botanical resource requires careful conservation due to its limited distribution and increasing medicinal demand.

  • Scientific name: Fritillaria anhuiensis
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Fritillaria anhuiensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Liliales. It is a member of the Liliaceae family and falls under the genus Fritillaria.
