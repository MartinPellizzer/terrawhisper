Corydalis bungeana

Corydalis bungarean, commonly known as the Bungaree Corydalis, is a perennial herb native to Australia. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant contains bioactive alkaloids with potential sedative and analgesic properties. Understanding its chemical composition and ecological role is essential for exploring its therapeutic applications in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis bungeana
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Corydalis.

It can also be found inhabiting the areas of Primorye and Sakalin. Furthermore, its range extends into China, specifically within the Inner Mongolia region.
