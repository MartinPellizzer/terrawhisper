Ashitaba, scientifically known as Angelica keiskei, is a revered medicinal herb native to the subtropical regions of Japan. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in East Asian medicine to support blood circulation, enhance vitality, and promote overall wellness through its unique antioxidant properties.

This plant exhibits a highly localized geographical distribution within the natural landscapes of Eastern Asia. Specifically, its occurrence is restricted to the islands of Japan. Plant Parts

This plant, Angelica keiskei, offers various components that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The primary therapeutic value is often found in the roots, which are harvested for their potent bioactive compounds.
