Angelica keiskei

Angelica keiskei, commonly known as a unique member of the Apiaceae family, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic potential. Characterized by its aromatic properties, this plant is studied for its bioactive compounds, which may offer diverse health benefits, including anti-inflammatory and digestive support in traditional practices.

  • Scientific name: Angelica keiskei
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is a member of the Apiaceae family. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Angelica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a highly localized geographical distribution within the natural landscapes of Eastern Asia. Specifically, its occurrence is restricted to the islands of Japan. Within this region, it thrives in unique habitats that support its specialized growth requirements. Because its range is so narrow, the species is considered an endemic element of the Japanese flora. Consequently, understanding its specific environmental needs is crucial for the conservation of this regional botanical treasure.
