Ashitaba, scientifically known as Angelica keiskei, is a revered medicinal herb native to the subtropical regions of Japan. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in East Asian medicine to support blood circulation, enhance vitality, and promote overall wellness through its unique antioxidant properties.
