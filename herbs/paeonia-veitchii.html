Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of China. It can be found growing in the landscapes of China South-Central and China North-Central. Additionally, the species extends its range northward into the territory of Inner Mongolia. Its presence is also documented within the high-altitude regions of Qinghai. Finally, the plant is distributed throughout the vast plateau area of Tibet.
