Paeonia veitchii

Paeonia veiitchii, commonly known as Veitch's peony, is a resilient perennial prized for its striking blooms and medicinal potential. Native to mountainous regions, this botanical treasure is valued in traditional practices for its bioactive compounds, which are studied for their therapeutic properties in supporting various holistic health applications.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia veitchii
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifrages, it is classified under the family Paeoniaceae and the genus Paeonia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Paeonia veiitchii, is highly valued in traditional medicine and carries distinct names across different regions. In Vietnam, it is commonly referred to as Xuyên xích thược, while in Chinese medicine, it is known by the name 川赤芍.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of China. It can be found growing in the landscapes of China South-Central and China North-Central. Additionally, the species extends its range northward into the territory of Inner Mongolia. Its presence is also documented within the high-altitude regions of Qinghai. Finally, the plant is distributed throughout the vast plateau area of Tibet.
