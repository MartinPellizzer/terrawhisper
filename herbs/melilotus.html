Melilot, scientifically known as Melilotus, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the legume family. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it contains coumarin, which offers anti-inflammatory and antispasmodic benefits. Traditionally used to support circulation and soothe digestive issues, this plant remains a significant subject in herbal pharmacology and studies.
