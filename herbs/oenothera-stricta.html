What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Its flowers consist of four large, bright yellow petals that surround a central cluster of numerous stamens and a prominent stigma. The fruit is a long, slender capsule that dehisces to release small, dark seeds. This plant often forms dense colonies through the use of creeping rhizomes.
