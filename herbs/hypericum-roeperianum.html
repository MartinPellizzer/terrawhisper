Hypericum roeperianum

Hypericum roeperianum, commonly known as a member of the St. John's Wort family, is a distinctive medicinal plant prized for its unique botanical properties. Characterized by its vibrant blooms, this species holds significant potential in traditional herbalism, offering intriguing possibilities for therapeutic applications and specialized pharmacological research and study.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum roeperianum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Hypericum roeperianum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling into the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Hypericaceae and is situated within the genus Hypericum.
