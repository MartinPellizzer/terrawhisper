Machilus thunbergii

Machilus thunbergii, commonly known as the Thunberg's Machilus, is a versatile evergreen tree native to East Asian forests. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark possess significant bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects, offering promising therapeutic potential in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Machilus thunbergii
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Machilus as Machilus thunbergii.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Machilus thunbergii, is recognized by various common names across different regions and languages. In Japan, it is called タブノキ (ja), while in Korea, it is referred to as 후박나무 (ko). In Chinese-speaking regions, the names vary slightly by location: it is called 红楠 (zh) and 红楠 (zh-cn) in mainland China, whereas in Taiwan, it is commonly known as 豬腳楠 (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions in East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in both the North-Central and Southeast territories. The species is also widely distributed throughout Eastern Asia, specifically occurring in Japan and Korea. Additionally, its range extends to the Nansei-shoto islands. Together, these locations define the diverse natural habitat of Machilus thunbergii.
