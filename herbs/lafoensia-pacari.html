Lafoensia pacari

Lafoensia pacari, commonly known as “pica-pica,” is a remarkable medicinal shrub native to the high-altitude Andean regions. Renowned for its potent aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering a rich tapestry of ethnobotanical significance.

  • Scientific name: Lafoensia pacari
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Lythraceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Lafoensia, identifying it as Lafoensia pacari.
