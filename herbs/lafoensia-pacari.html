Mangava-brava, scientifically known as Lafoensia pacari, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation and infections. This resilient shrub offers significant pharmacological potential, making it a subject of growing interest in ethnobotanical research.

Plant Parts

This plant, Lafoensia pacari, possesses various anatomical components that are utilized in traditional medicinal practices. The lush green leaves are often a primary focus for topical or decoction-based applications, while the sturdy branches provide the structural framework for the plant's growth. For more potent medicinal extractions, practitioners frequently turn to the bark, utilizing both the specialized stem bark from younger sections and the thicker, more protective bark of the trunk to derive essential bioactive compounds.
