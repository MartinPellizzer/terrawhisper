Aloe sabaea

Aloe sabaea, commonly known as the Sabaea Aloe, is a striking succulent native to the dry regions of East Africa. Renowned for its dramatic, upright rosette structure and medicinal properties, this plant is widely utilized in traditional healing to treat skin ailments, promote wound healing, and support digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Aloe sabaea
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asphodelaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Aloe.
