Primula cernua

What's the taxonomical classification of Primula cernua?

Primula cernua belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Ericales. The plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, specifically within the genus Primula, and is identified by the unique species designation cernua.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Primula cernua has a basal rosette of oblong to elliptical leaves that feature serrated margins and a slightly wrinkled texture. The foliage is typically dark green and grows closely to the ground to form a dense cluster. From the center of this rosette, an upright, leafless flowering stalk emerges to support the inflorescence. The flowers are characterized by five distinct petals that often display delicate color gradients. Each individual blossom is arranged in an umbel-like cluster at the apex of the stem.
