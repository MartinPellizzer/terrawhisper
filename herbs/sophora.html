Sophora

Sophora, commonly known as mountain laurel or brushbox, is a versatile genus of flowering plants valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical properties, various species are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin conditions to inflammation. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a subject of significant pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Sophora
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. It is further identified by the genus Sophora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range stretching across several distinct regions of the continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within the borders of Great Britain. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species is found in Romania as well as in the European portion of Türkiye. Its distribution extends further into Eastern Europe, specifically encompassing the territory of Krym. Finally, the plant reaches its easternmost limits within East European Russia.
