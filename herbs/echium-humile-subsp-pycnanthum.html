Echium humile subsp. pycnanthum

Echium humile subsp. pycnanthum, commonly known as a subspecies of the small bugloss, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific Mediterranean habitats. Renowned for its vibrant blue flowers, it contains bioactive compounds studied for their potential therapeutic properties, offering valuable insights into traditional herbal medicine and botanical pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Echium humile subsp. pycnanthum
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Echium, specifically as the subspecies pycnanthum.
