Ferula hermonis

Ferula hermonis, commonly known as a member of the Apiaceae family, is a medicinal plant native to specific arid regions. Valued in traditional healing practices, it contains bioactive compounds with potential therapeutic properties. Research continues to explore its pharmacological significance, particularly regarding its antioxidant and antimicrobial healing capabilities.
