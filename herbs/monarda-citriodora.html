Native to North America, this versatile herb is widely used in traditional medicine to support digestive health, soothe respiratory discomfort, and reduce inflammation, making it a valuable addition to natural wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Monarda citriodora, is highly valued for its aromatic properties, which are primarily concentrated in its lush, green leaves. The therapeutic potency of the foliage is derived from the presence of numerous glandular trichomes, which are tiny, hair-like structures located on the surface of the plant. These specialized trichomes serve as microscopic reservoirs that synthesize and store the plant's precious essential oil. When the leaves are crushed or bruised, these glands rupture, releasing the potent essential oil that provides the plant with its characteristic citrus-like scent and medicinal benefits.
