Monarda citriodora

Monarda citriodora, commonly known as Lemon Bee Balm, is a fragrant perennial prized for its citrusy aroma and therapeutic properties. Native to North America, this medicinal herb is rich in essential oils, traditionally used to support digestive health, ease respiratory congestion, and provide calming, antimicrobial benefits in herbal wellness.

  • Scientific name: Monarda citriodora
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Monarda citriodora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Monarda.

Plant Parts

This plant, Monarda citriodora, is highly valued for its aromatic properties, which are primarily concentrated in its lush, green leaves. The therapeutic potency of the foliage is derived from the presence of numerous glandular trichomes, which are tiny, hair-like structures located on the surface of the plant. These specialized trichomes serve as microscopic reservoirs that synthesize and store the plant's precious essential oil. When the leaves are crushed or bruised, these glands rupture, releasing the potent essential oil that provides the plant with its characteristic citrus-like scent and medicinal benefits.
