Mopane, scientifically known as Colophospermum mopane, is a resilient deciduous tree native to Southern Africa. Highly valued for its ecological significance, it serves as a vital medicinal resource. Various parts of the plant are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections and respiratory distress.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the region of South Tropical Africa. Within this broad area, its presence is specifically noted in the country of Angola.
