Colophospermum mopane

Mopane

Colophospermum mopane, commonly known as the mopane tree, is a resilient species native to Southern Africa. Beyond its ecological significance, it holds profound medicinal value in traditional healing. Various parts of the tree, including its bark and leaves, are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Colophospermum mopane
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and is placed in the genus Colophospermum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the region of South Tropical Africa. Within this broad area, its presence is specifically noted in the country of Angola. It also thrives within the borders of Malawi and Mozambique. Furthermore, the species can be found throughout much of Zambia. Finally, its geographical range extends into the nation of Zimbabwe.
