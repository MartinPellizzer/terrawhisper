Anisodus stramoniifolius

Anisoedus stramoniifolius, commonly known as the star-leafed medicinal shrub, is a versatile plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, respiratory ailments, and skin conditions. This study explores its therapeutic potential and pharmacological significance in modern herbal medicine.
