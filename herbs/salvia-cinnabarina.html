Cinnabar Sage, scientifically known as Salvia cinnabarina, is a striking perennial shrub native to Western Australia. Renowned for its vibrant, tubular crimson flowers, this hardy species thrives in sandy soils. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it serves as a vital nectar source for local pollinators within diverse bushland ecosystems.
