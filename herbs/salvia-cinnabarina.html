Salvia cinnabarina

Salvia cinnabarina, commonly known as the Cinnabar Sage, is a striking perennial native to Western Australia. Distinguished by its vibrant, tubular crimson flowers, this hardy shrub thrives in sandy soils. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it serves as a vital nectar source for local pollinators within its unique ecological landscape.
