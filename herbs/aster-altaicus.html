Aster altaicus

Aster altaicus, commonly known as the Altai Aster, is a resilient perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Central Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this botanical treasure possesses significant bioactive properties, offering therapeutic potential for various ailments through its unique chemical composition and restorative natural healing powers.

  • Scientific name: Aster altaicus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is specifically positioned in the family Asteraceae. It is further identified by the genus Aster, with the specific species name being altaicus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the vast territory of Siberia. Its presence is notably documented in the Altai region, where specific environmental conditions support its growth. Additionally, it can be found throughout the Republic of Buryatia and the area surrounding Chita. The species also extends its range into the Irkutsk region, highlighting its adaptability to various Siberian landscapes. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the Krasnoyarsk region, completing its broad distribution across central and eastern Siberia.
