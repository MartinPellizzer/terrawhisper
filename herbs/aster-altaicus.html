This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the vast territory of Siberia. The species also extends its range into the Irkutsk region, highlighting its adaptability to various Siberian landscapes. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the Krasnoyarsk region, completing its broad distribution across central and eastern Siberia.
