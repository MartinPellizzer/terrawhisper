Rubus wallichianus

Rubus wallichianus, commonly known as Himalayan raspberry or wild blackberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive ailments, and skin issues. This potent plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its nutrient-rich berries and leaves.

  • Scientific name: Rubus wallichianus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rubus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found stretching from the South-Central areas to the Southeast. Its range also extends significantly into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. In Eastern Asia, the species is specifically recorded in Taiwan. Finally, the plant is widely distributed across the East Himalaya within the Indian Subcontinent.
