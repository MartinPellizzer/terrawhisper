Symplocos racemosa

Symplocos racemosa, commonly known as Lodhra, is a significant medicinal plant in Ayurveda. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is primarily used to treat gynecological disorders, skin conditions, and inflammation. The bark is highly valued for its astringent qualities, playing a crucial role in promoting holistic reproductive health.

  • Scientific name: Symplocos racemosa
  • Family: Symplocaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Symplocos racemosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Symplocaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Symplocos.
