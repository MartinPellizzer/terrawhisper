Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range that spans across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the European portion of Türkiye. Moving into Eastern Europe, the species can be found inhabiting the Crimean Peninsula. Its distribution extends deeply into Siberia, where it thrives in the Altay mountain region. Furthermore, the plant is recorded in the Siberian territories of Buryatiya and Chita.
