Lonicera nigra

Lonicera nigra, commonly known as black honeysuckle, is a deciduous shrub native to North America. Renowned for its dark, fragrant berries and tubular flowers, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine. It is often studied for its antioxidant properties and potential applications in supporting overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lonicera nigra
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Dipsacales and the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Lonicera.
