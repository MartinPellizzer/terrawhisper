Coleus monostachyus

Coleus monostachyus, commonly known as the single-stalk coleus, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its vibrant foliage, this species is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to support wellness. Understanding its botanical characteristics and healing potential offers valuable insights into natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Coleus monostachyus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Coleus.

Common Names

This plant, Coleus monostachyus, is known by various names across different regions and languages. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Plectranthus or the Coleus, while in Spanish, it is commonly called mostaza de monte. In Portuguese, it is known as mostarda-de-monte, and in French, the plant is identified as la mostarda de monte.

Distribution

This plant, Coleus monostachyus, is primarily found throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Its geographical range specifically includes the nation of Burkina. Additionally, this medicinal species can be located within the borders of Ghana. It is also distributed across the coastal and inland areas of Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Finally, the plant's presence extends to the territory of Ivory Coast.
