Magnolia ovata

Magnolia ovata, commonly known as the deciduous magnolia, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its bark and flowers are utilized to treat digestive issues and anxiety. This resilient species offers a natural approach to promoting holistic wellness and balance.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia ovata
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is situated under the genus Magnolia, specifically identified as the species Magnolia ovata.
