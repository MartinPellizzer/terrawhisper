Orange-Ball-Tree, scientifically known as Buddlejia globosa, is a remarkable medicinal shrub prized for its striking, spherical orange inflorescences. Native to specific high-altitude regions, this plant is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.
