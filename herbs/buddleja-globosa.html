Buddleja globosa

Buddleja globosa, commonly known as the globe buddleja, is a striking evergreen shrub native to South America. Renowned for its unique, spherical clusters of violet flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its antioxidant properties and its potential role in treating inflammatory ailments and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Buddleja globosa
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Buddleja.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Buddleja globosa, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Chinese, it is known as 球花醉鱼草 (zh) or 球花醉鱼草 (zh-hans), while in Quechua, it is called China kiswar (qu). Other regional names include Bollbuddleja in Swedish (sv) and Coeden-fêl peli oren in Welsh (cy).

Furthermore, the species is established in New Zealand, appearing in both the North and South islands.
