Bengal ginger

Zingiber montanum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (27 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Bengal ginger, scientifically known as Zingiber montanum, is a potent medicinal herb renowned in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Characterized by its aromatic rhizomes, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. It serves as a vital natural remedy for digestive ailments and various systemic inflammatory conditions.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several specific regions in Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast coastal areas. Additionally, its presence is documented on the island of Hainan within Chinese territory. Moving into the Indo-China region, the species is also native to the country of Cambodia. Finally, the plant's range extends further into the neighboring nation of Laos.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Zingiber montanum, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its medicinal properties found within the rhizome. The underground rhizomes serve as the main storage organ for the plant, containing concentrated bioactive compounds that are harvested for therapeutic use. Whether used in its fresh or dried form, the rhizome is the essential component utilized to prepare various herbal remedies.
