Zingiber montanum

Zingiber montanum, commonly known as Mountain Ginger, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional Southeast Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it offers significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. This aromatic rhizome is widely utilized to support digestive health and alleviate various systemic inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Zingiber montanum
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, it is identified by its scientific genus, Zingiber.

Common Names

This plant, Zingiber montanum, is known by several different common names depending on the language and region. In Javanese, it is referred to as Bengle, while in Chinese, it is commonly called 山地姜, 山地姜, or 山地姜 (in simplified Chinese), and is also known as 光果姜.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several specific regions in Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast coastal areas. Additionally, its presence is documented on the island of Hainan within Chinese territory. Moving into the Indo-China region, the species is also native to the country of Cambodia. Finally, the plant's range extends further into the neighboring nation of Laos.
