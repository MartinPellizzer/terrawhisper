Water Lettuce

Water Lettuce, scientifically known as Pistia stratiotes, is a free-floating aquatic plant characterized by its rosette of velvety, light green leaves. Often found in freshwater habitats, it serves vital ecological roles in water purification and providing shelter for aquatic life, though it can become invasive in certain environments.
