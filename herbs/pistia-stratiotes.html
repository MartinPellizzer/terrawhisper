Pistia stratiotes

Water lettuce

Pistia stratiotes, commonly known as water lettuce, is a free-floating aquatic perennial characterized by its rosette of velvety, light green leaves. Beyond its striking appearance, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammation to digestive issues and kidney disorders.

  • Scientific name: Pistia stratiotes
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Alismatales and family Araceae, it is classified under the genus Pistia. This specific taxonomic lineage defines the biological identity of the medicinal species Pistia stratiotes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within the borders of Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is commonly found in both Germany and the Netherlands. Its range extends into Southeastern Europe, specifically reaching as far as Italy. Finally, the distribution continues into Eastern Europe, where it is located in Ukraine.
