Melampyrum caucasicum

Melampyrum caucasicum, commonly known as Caucasian Melampyrum, is a perennial herb belonging to the Orobanchaceae family. Characterized by its distinctive bracts and parasitic nature, this plant thrives in diverse mountain ecosystems. It holds significant interest in ethnobotany for its potential therapeutic properties and unique ecological role in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Melampyrum caucasicum
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
