Commiphora caudata

Commiphora caudata, commonly known as the tailed myrrh, is a resilient shrub native to the arid regions of the Horn of Africa. Renowned for its aromatic resin, this medicinal plant is utilized in traditional healing to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
