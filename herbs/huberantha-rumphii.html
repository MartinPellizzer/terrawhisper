Huberantha rumphii

Huberantha rumphii, commonly known as the "pua" or "mulu" in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Huberantha rumphii
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Annonaceae. Ultimately, its scientific classification places it within the genus Huberantha.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse regions of Malesia. Within this expansive area, it can be found growing on the island of Borneo. Its presence also extends to the Malay Peninsula, which is commonly referred to as Malaya. Furthermore, the species is documented in the Maluku Islands and throughout the Philippines. Finally, its geographical range includes the large island of Sumatera.
