Indigofera heterantha

Himalayan indigo

Indigofera heterantha, commonly known as a member of the Fabaceae family, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, this plant possesses significant antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. It serves as a vital botanical resource for developing natural remedies and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Indigofera heterantha
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
