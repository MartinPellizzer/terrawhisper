Himalayan indigo, scientifically known as Indigofera heterantha, is a perennial shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its deep blue pigment, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices for its potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties in herbal formulations.
