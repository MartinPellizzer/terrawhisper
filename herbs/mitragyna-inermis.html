Mitragyna inermis

Mitragyna inermis, commonly known as the African Mitragyna, is a medicinal shrub native to tropical African regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant contains bioactive alkaloids used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, fever, and pain, offering significant potential for modern drug development and therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Mitragyna inermis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Mitragyna inermis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Gentianales. Finally, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family and is situated within the genus Mitragyna.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Mitragyna inermis, is referred to by various traditional names depending on the linguistic group. In the Wolof language, it is commonly called Xoos, while in the Dagbani language, it is known as Shɛɣu, and in the Bemba language, it is referred to as Jun.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Mitragyna inermis, is primarily located within the West Tropical Africa region.
