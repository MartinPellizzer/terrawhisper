Fagopyrum

Fagopyrum, commonly known as buckwheat, is a versatile pseudocereal valued for its exceptional nutritional profile. Rich in dietary fiber, antioxidants, and essential minerals, it supports metabolic health and heart wellness. Beyond its dietary importance, various species in this genus offer significant medicinal potential in traditional and modern healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Fagopyrum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Fagopyrum.
