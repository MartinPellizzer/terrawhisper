Buckwheat, scientifically known as Fagopyrum, is a versatile pseudocereal celebrated for its exceptional nutritional profile. Beyond its culinary uses, it possesses significant medicinal properties, including antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-diabetic effects. Rich in rutin, Fagopyrum supports cardiovascular health, making it a vital plant in traditional and modern wellness practices.
