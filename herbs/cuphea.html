Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in Great Britain. Its range extends southward into Northern Africa, specifically within the borders of Morocco. The species is also present in Macaronesia, inhabiting the Azores islands.
