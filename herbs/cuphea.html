Cuphea

Cuphea, commonly known as various names including the Pipoca plant, is a versatile genus of flowering plants within the Lythraceae family. Renowned for their vibrant, colorful blooms and ornamental appeal, many species are studied for their diverse phytochemical properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in traditional and modern medicinal practices.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Lythraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Cuphea.
