Echium vulgare subsp. vulgare

Echium vulgare vulgare

Echium vulgare subsp. vulgare, commonly known as purple viper's bugloss, is a striking biennial herb native to Europe. Characterized by its vibrant blue flowers and bristly texture, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used in traditional medicine, it is studied today for its unique bioactive phytochemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Echium vulgare subsp. vulgare
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
