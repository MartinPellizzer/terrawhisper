Long-leaved arnica, scientifically known as Arnica longifolia, is a perennial herb native to Western North America. Renowned for its bright yellow, daisy-like blooms, this plant has a storied history in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory properties to soothe bruises, swelling, and muscle aches.

This plant is primarily distributed across the mountainous regions of Western Canada and the Northwestern United States. In Canada, its presence is noted within the provinces of Alberta and British Columbia. Moving southward into the United States, it can be found in the state of Idaho.
