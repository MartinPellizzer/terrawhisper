Glabrous Angelica, scientifically known as Angelica japonica, is a perennial herb native to East Asia, highly revered in traditional medicine. Known for its aromatic roots, it is a cornerstone of Kampo formulations. This botanical powerhouse is utilized for its anti-inflammatory, analgesic, and blood-regulating properties, supporting overall female hormonal health.

Distribution
