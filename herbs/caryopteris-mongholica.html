Caryopteris mongholica

Caryopteris mongholica, commonly known as the Mongolian Bluebeard, is a resilient perennial shrub prized for its striking blue flowers. Native to high-altitude regions, this medicinal plant offers significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional practices to treat various ailments, showcasing its unique therapeutic and ornamental potential.

  • Scientific name: Caryopteris mongholica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Caryopteris.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions in Northern and Central Asia. In the Russian Federation, its presence is specifically noted within the Buryatiya region of Siberia. It also inhabits parts of China, extending through Inner Mongolia into the China North-Central territory. Furthermore, the species can be found growing naturally within the borders of Mongolia. Together, these areas define the primary geographical range of Caryopteris mongholica.
