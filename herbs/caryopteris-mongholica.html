Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions in Northern and Central Asia. In the Russian Federation, its presence is specifically noted within the Buryatiya region of Siberia. It also inhabits parts of China, extending through Inner Mongolia into the China North-Central territory. Furthermore, the species can be found growing naturally within the borders of Mongolia.
