What's the taxonomical classification of Atriplex repens?

Atriplex repens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant further falls under the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Amaranthaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Atriplex, with the species designation repens.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Atriplex repens has prostrate, creeping stems with succulent, ovate to lanceolate leaves that measure approximately 1 to 3 centimeters in length and possess fleshy, somewhat thickened margins. The leaf surfaces are typically smooth and can range from a bright green to a slightly glaucous hue depending on environmental conditions. Small, inconspicuous flowers are arranged in dense, spike-like clusters along the stems. These flowers lack true petals and are characterized by their minute, somewhat fleshy structure. The plant often forms dense mats due to its sprawling growth habit and rooting capabilities at the nodes.
