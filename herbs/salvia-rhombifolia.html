Salvia rhombifolia

Salvia rhombifolia, commonly known as the rhombic-leaved sage, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Characterized by its distinctive diamond-shaped leaves and delicate flowering spikes, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, including its potential anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial effects in herbal treatments.

  • Scientific name: Salvia rhombifolia
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Southern America

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range across the South American continent. Its primary distribution can be found in Western South America, specifically within the country of Peru. Additionally, its habitat extends into the southern regions of the continent. More precisely, it is located in Northern Chile. Consequently, its geographical presence is concentrated along the western and southern stretches of the region.
