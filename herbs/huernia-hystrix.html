Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several regions within the southern portion of the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its range includes both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving toward the southern reaches, it can be found throughout the Cape Provinces. Its distribution also extends into the KwaZulu-Natal region. Finally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Esватини.
