Devil's tongue

Amorphophallus konjac · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (351 studies) · ★★★★★

Devil's tongue, scientifically known as Amorphophalus konjac, is a versatile tuberous plant valued in both culinary and medicinal contexts. Rich in glucomannan, a potent dietary fiber, it aids digestion, manages weight, and supports blood sugar levels. This remarkable plant offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic metabolic health management.

Beyond the borders of China, the species extends its range into the Korean Peninsula. Plant Parts

This plant, Amorphophallus konjac, is primarily valued for its large, starchy underground structure known as a tuber. This specialized organ serves as a storage reservoir for nutrients and water, allowing the plant to survive dormant periods between growing seasons. It is from this fleshy, bulb-like part that the glucomannan, a highly soluble dietary fiber, is extracted for various medicinal and culinary applications.
