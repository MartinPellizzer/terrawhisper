Devil's tongue

Amorphophallus konjac · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (351 studies) · ★★★★★

Devil's tongue, scientifically known as Amorphophalus konjac, is a versatile tuberous plant valued in both culinary and medicinal contexts. Rich in glucomannan, a potent dietary fiber, it aids digestion, manages weight, and supports blood sugar levels. This remarkable plant offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic metabolic health management.
