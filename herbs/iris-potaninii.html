Iris potaninii

What's the taxonomical classification of Iris potaninii?

Iris potaninii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales. This plant is a representative of the family Iridaceae, specifically situated within the genus Iris, and is identified by its unique species designation, potaninii.
