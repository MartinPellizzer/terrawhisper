Bristly luffa, scientifically known as Luffa echinata, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive spiny fruit, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various digestive and systemic disorders.
