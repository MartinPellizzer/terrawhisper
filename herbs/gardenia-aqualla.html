Distribution

This plant, known as Gardenia aquatica, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented in several countries, starting with its occurrence in Benin. The species also extends through the territories of Burkina Faso and Ghana. Furthermore, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Guinea. Finally, its geographical range includes the coastal and inland areas of the Ivory Coast.

This plant, Gardenia aquatica, utilizes various structures for its growth and medicinal properties, most notably its leaf and stem. The leaves are typically glossy, dark green, and elliptical in shape, serving as the primary site for photosynthesis and containing various bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies. Supporting the plant's structure is the stem, which provides the necessary framework to elevate the foliage toward sunlight and facilitates the transport of water and nutrients throughout the organism.
