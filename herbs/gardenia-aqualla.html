This metabolic strategy involves the initial fixation of CO2 into a three-carbon molecule, typically 3-phosphoglycerate, which dictates the plant's water-use efficiency and gas exchange patterns. Consequently, its physiological processes are highly sensitive to environmental factors such as light intensity, temperature, and moisture availability, as the C3 pathway lacks the specialized carbon-concentrating mechanisms found in C4 or CAM plants.
