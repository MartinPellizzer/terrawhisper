Triplaris

Tripali, commonly known as the "king of herbs" in traditional medicine, is a potent botanical renowned for its restorative properties. Often used to revitalize the body, it supports digestive health, enhances immunity, and combats fatigue. This powerful plant serves as a cornerstone in holistic wellness and natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Triplaris
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the Polygonaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Triplaris.
