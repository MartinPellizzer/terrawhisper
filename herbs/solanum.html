Solanum

Solanum, commonly known as nightshade, is a diverse genus containing hundreds of species used globally in medicine. While some, like bittersweet, offer therapeutic alkaloids for pain and inflammation, others are toxic. Understanding their complex chemical profiles is essential for harnessing their healing potential while ensuring safety in herbal applications.

  • Scientific name: Solanum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales. Finally, it is a member of the family Solanaceae and falls under the genus Solanum.
