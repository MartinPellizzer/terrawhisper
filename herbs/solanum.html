Nightshade

Solanum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (36 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Nightshade, scientifically known as Solanum, is a vast and diverse genus comprising hundreds of species essential to global agriculture and medicine. While some members provide nutritious staples like potatoes and eggplants, others contain potent alkaloids used in pharmacology to treat inflammation, pain, and various neurological conditions through specialized compounds.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales. Finally, it is a member of the family Solanaceae and falls under the genus Solanum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across various regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Denmark. Furthermore, its presence extends to the northern landscapes of Finland. The species is also documented throughout Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, its range encompasses the coastal and inland areas of Norway.
