Calendula arvensis

Calendula arvensis, commonly known as field marigold, is a resilient annual herb native to Europe. Distinguished by its bright yellow, daisy-like blooms, this plant thrives in disturbed soils. While less famous than its garden cousin, it holds traditional significance in herbal medicine for its soothing and anti-inflammatory properties.

  • Scientific name: Calendula arvensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Calendula.
