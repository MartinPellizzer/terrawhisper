Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls into the subclass Gnetidae and the order Ephedrales, specifically being a member of the family Ephedraceae. Within this taxonomic hierarchy, it is categorized under the genus Ephedra as the species Ephedra fragilis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions within Southwestern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing in the Baleares islands, Portugal, and Spain. Its range also extends into Southeastern Europe, where it inhabits certain territories. Within this southeastern region, the species is notably present in Italy. Finally, it is also documented growing on the island of Sicilia.
