This plant, Impatiens tinctoria, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Balsaminaceae and the genus Impatiens.
