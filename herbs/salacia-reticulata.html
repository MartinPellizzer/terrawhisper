Kothala himbutu, scientifically known as Salaacia reticulata, is a climbing shrub native to the tropical forests of Sri Lanka. Renowned in traditional Ayurveda, this potent medicinal plant is widely utilized for its remarkable anti-diabetic properties, aiding in blood sugar regulation and supporting overall metabolic health and vitality.

Plant Parts

This plant, Salacia reticulata, is a climbing shrub that utilizes various structural components for its growth and medicinal properties. The botanical identity of the species is characterized by its woody stems and slender twigs that allow it to scramble through forest vegetation. The thick, rugged bark covering the main stem is often the most significant part used in traditional medicine due to its bioactive compounds. Additionally, the green, lanceolate leaves serve as the primary site for photosynthesis, supporting the overall vitality of the climbing vine.
