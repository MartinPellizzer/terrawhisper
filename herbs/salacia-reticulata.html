Salacia reticulata

Salacia reticulata, commonly known as Salacia, is a medicinal shrub native to India, highly valued in Ayurveda. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is primarily used to manage blood sugar levels and support metabolic health. This natural remedy plays a vital role in traditional treatments for diabetes.

  • Scientific name: Salacia reticulata
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae, it is classified under the genus Salacia. Specifically, the species is identified as Salacia reticulata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within certain tropical regions. In the Indian Subcontinent, it is notably found growing in Sri Lanka. Furthermore, its range extends into the Indo-China region. Within this area, the plant can be located on the Andaman Islands.
