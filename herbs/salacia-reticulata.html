Kothala himbutu, scientifically known as Salaacia reticulata, is a climbing shrub native to the tropical forests of Sri Lanka. Renowned in traditional Ayurveda, this potent medicinal plant is widely utilized for its remarkable anti-diabetic properties, aiding in blood sugar regulation and supporting overall metabolic health and vitality.

Morphology

The morphology of Salacia reticulata is characterized by its growth habit as a vigorous climber, specifically functioning as a liana. As a woody plant, it possesses a robust, lignified structure that allows it to ascend and navigate through surrounding vegetation, utilizing its woody stems to provide the necessary support for its sprawling vine-like extension. This combination of a woody constitution and a climbing life form enables the species to effectively compete for light within its ecological niche.
