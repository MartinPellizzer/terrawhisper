What's the taxonomical classification of Pittosporum perglabratum?

Pittosporum perglabratum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Apiales. This plant is a member of the family Pittosporaceae and is specifically categorized within the genus Pittosporum, ultimately identified by the species name perglabratum.
