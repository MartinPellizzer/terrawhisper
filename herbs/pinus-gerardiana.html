Chilgoza Pine, scientifically known as Pinus gerardiana, is a precious coniferous species native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Plant Parts

This plant, Pinus gerardiana (commonly known as the Chilgoza pine), is highly valued for its reproductive structures, specifically its seeds. The seeds are contained within woody, oval-shaped cones that mature over several seasons. Once the cones open, they release nutrient-dense, edible seeds that are rich in essential fatty acids and proteins, making them a vital medicinal and nutritional resource for local communities.
