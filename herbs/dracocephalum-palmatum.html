Dracocephalum palmatum

Dracocephalum palmatum, commonly known as palmately-leaved dragonhead, is a resilient perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Native to the Mediterranean, this aromatic plant features distinctive lobed leaves and vibrant blue flowers. Highly valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses diverse bioactive properties, including antioxidant and antimicrobial medicinal qualities.
