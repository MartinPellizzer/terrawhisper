Picramnia sellowii

Picramnia sellowii, commonly known as "canela-de-fogo," is a medicinal shrub native to South American tropical forests. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat skin inflammations, wounds, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for developing modern therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Picramnia sellowii
  • Family: Picramniaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Picramniales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Picramniaceae and the genus Picramnia.
