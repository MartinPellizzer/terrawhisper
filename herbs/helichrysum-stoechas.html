Goldilocks, scientifically known as Helichrysum stoechas, is a resilient Mediterranean perennial prized for its vibrant, straw-like blooms. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this aromatic plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is widely utilized in skincare and wellness remedies to promote healing, soothe skin, and support respiratory health.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Immortelle, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts. These above-ground structures, which include the fragrant leaves and the small, golden-yellow flower heads, contain the essential oils and bioactive compounds responsible for its therapeutic properties. When harvested, these aerial parts are typically dried to be used in infusions, essential oil extraction, or topical preparations to exploit their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits.
