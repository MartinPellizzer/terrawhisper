Terminalia buceras

Terminalia buceras, commonly known as the Black Terminalia, is a versatile medicinal tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its leaves and bark offer significant pharmacological potential for modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia buceras
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Myrtales and falls into the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by its genus, Terminalia.
