Giant Himalayan Lily, scientifically known as Cardiocrinum giganteum, is a magnificent perennial herb native to the Himalayas. Renowned for its striking white blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, its bulbs are utilized in various medicinal practices to treat ailments ranging from skin disorders to digestive issues.
