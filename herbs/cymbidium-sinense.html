Cymbidium sinense

What's the taxonomical classification of Cymbidium sinense?

Cymbidium sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant further descends through the order Asparagales and is a member of the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Cymbidium, with its unique biological designation being the species sinese.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cymbidium sinense has long, leathery, lanceolate leaves that grow in a fan-like arrangement from a creeping rhizome. These evergreen leaves are typically dark green and can reach significant lengths depending on the maturity of the plant. The plant produces arching inflorescences that bear multiple fragrant, waxy flowers characterized by a prominent, colorful lip. Each flower features delicate petals and sepals that often display shades of green, white, or pink. The overall structure of the orchid is characterized by its robust, sympodial growth habit.
