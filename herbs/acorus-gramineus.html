Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range that extends across several distinct regions. In Northern Europe, its presence is notably recorded in Great Britain. Moving eastward, it can be found within the Russian Far East, specifically in the Primorye region. The species is also widely distributed throughout China, spanning from Inner Mongolia to the South-Central provinces. Additionally, its habitat reaches as far south as the island of Hainan.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Acorus gramineus, is a significant Chinese medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic properties. The most important part used in traditional treatments is the aromatic rhizome, which is harvested and processed for its medicinal potency. Additionally, the long, narrow leaf provides a distinct visual character to the plant, but it is the underground rhizomes that contain the concentrated active compounds essential to its use as a Chinese medicinal herb.
