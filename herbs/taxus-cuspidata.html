Japanese yew, scientifically known as Taxus cuspidata, is an evergreen conifer native to East Asia. Renowned for its striking foliage and ornamental value, this slow-growing species is highly valued in landscaping. While aesthetically beautiful, it contains potent alkaloids, requiring careful handling due to its significant medicinal and toxic properties.
