Lady's-mantle, scientifically known as Alchemilla mollis, is a versatile perennial celebrated for its frothy, lime-green blooms and scalloped foliage. Beyond its ornamental charm in gardens, this plant holds traditional medicinal significance, long used in herbal remedies to support digestive health and soothe skin irritations through its astringent properties.
