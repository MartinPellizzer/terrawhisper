Alchemilla mollis

Lady's-mantle

Alchemilla mollis, commonly known as Lady's Mantle, is a charming perennial celebrated for its scalloped, chartreuse foliage. Renowned for its ability to catch morning dew in its velvety leaves, this hardy plant thrives in partial shade, adding soft texture and ethereal beauty to any cottage garden or borders.

  • Scientific name: Alchemilla mollis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Rosales, and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Alchemilla.
