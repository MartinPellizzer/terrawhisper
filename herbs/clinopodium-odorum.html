Clinopodium odorum, scientifically known as Clinopodium odorum, is a fragrant perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Commonly referred to as wild basil, this aromatic plant is valued for its essential oils. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial, digestive, and anti-inflammatory therapeutic properties.
