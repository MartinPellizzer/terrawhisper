Juniperus drupacea

Juniperus drupacea, commonly known as the Drupaceous Juniper, is a distinctive evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its unique, berry-like cones, this resilient species is valued in traditional herbalism. Its aromatic foliage and phytochemical properties offer significant potential for therapeutic applications in modern medicinal studies.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus drupacea
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe
