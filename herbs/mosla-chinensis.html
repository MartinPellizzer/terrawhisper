Life history

The life history of Mosla chinensis is characterized by its classification as a therophyte, a life strategy defined by the completion of its entire biological cycle within a single growing season. As a therophyte, the plant survives unfavorable environmental conditions by producing specialized dormant seeds that persist in the soil until favorable moisture and temperature levels return. This life cycle is further specified through its growth habits, functioning both as a therophyte in its seasonal annual cycle and exhibiting characteristics consistent with the nanophanerophyte and phaeranophyte scales during its active vegetative phase. By prioritizing rapid growth, flowering, and seed production within a short temporal window, the species effectively utilizes transient resources to ensure the continuity of its lineage before the onset of seasonal dormancy.
