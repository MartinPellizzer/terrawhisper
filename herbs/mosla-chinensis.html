Mosla chinensis

Mosla chinensis, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, is highly valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique therapeutic properties, this plant serves as a natural remedy for various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer significant health benefits, making it a vital resource in botanical medicine and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Mosla chinensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae and falls under the genus Mosla.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Its range extends beyond the mainland to include the islands of Japan. Furthermore, the species can be found growing throughout the Korean peninsula.
