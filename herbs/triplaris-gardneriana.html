Triplaris gardneriana

Triplaris gardneriana, commonly known as the Gardners's Tripari, is a rare medicinal shrub native to the tropical forests of Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat inflammatory conditions, promote digestive health, and support overall systemic wellness.

  • Native range: Southern America
