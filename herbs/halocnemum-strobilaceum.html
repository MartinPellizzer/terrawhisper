Halocnemum strobilaceum

Halocnemum strobilaceum, commonly known as the saltwort, is a resilient succulent halophyte found in saline coastal environments. Renowned for its ability to thrive in extreme salt concentrations, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently studied for its unique phytochemical properties and potential therapeutic applications in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Halocnemum strobilaceum
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Halocnemum.
