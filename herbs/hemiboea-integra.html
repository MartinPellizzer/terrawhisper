What's the taxonomical classification of Hemiboea integra?

Hemiboea integra belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Gesneriaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Hemiboea, and its unique species designation, integra.
