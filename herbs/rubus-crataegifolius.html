Rubus crataegifolius

Rubus craataeifolius, commonly known as the Korean raspberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and boost immunity, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Rubus crataegifolius
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Rubus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Northern Asia. In Russia, its range extends through the Buryatiya region of Siberia to the Primorye area within the Russian Far East. Its presence is also well-documented across various parts of China, including the provinces of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Furthermore, the species can be found spreading into the China North-Central territories. Collectively, these locations define the specific ecological niche where the species thrives.
