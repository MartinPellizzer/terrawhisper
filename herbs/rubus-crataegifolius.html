Hawthorn-leaved Bramble, scientifically known as Rubus crataegifolius, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its distinctive lobed leaves and thorny stems, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to address digestive issues and support overall systemic health.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Northern Asia. In Russia, its range extends through the Buryatiya region of Siberia to the Primorye area within the Russian Far East. Its presence is also well-documented across various parts of China, including the provinces of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Furthermore, the species can be found spreading into the China North-Central territories. Collectively, these locations define the specific ecological niche where the species thrives.
