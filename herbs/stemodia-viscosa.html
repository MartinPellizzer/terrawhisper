Stemodia viscosa

Stemodia viscosa, commonly known as Sticky Stemodia, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
