Beach milkwood

Cerbera manghas · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (48 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Beach milkwood, scientifically known as Cerbera manghas, is a coastal evergreen tree native to the Indo-Pacific region. Characterized by its glossy leaves and white, fragrant flowers, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. However, its seeds contain potent cardiac glycosides, making the plant highly toxic if ingested by humans or animals.
