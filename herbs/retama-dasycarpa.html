Retama dasycarpa

Retama dasycarpa, commonly known as "retama," is a resilient shrub native to the arid regions of the Andes. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory issues and inflammation. This hardy plant plays a vital role in both traditional folk medicine and regional biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Retama dasycarpa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Retama. Specifically, it is identified by the species name Retama dasycarpa.
