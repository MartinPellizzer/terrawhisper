Helicia obovatifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Helicia obovatifolia?

Helicia obovatifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Proteales. As a member of the family Proteaceae, this plant is situated in the genus Helicia, specifically identified by its unique species designation, obovatifolia.
