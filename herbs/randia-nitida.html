Randia nitida

Randia nitiida, commonly known as Gardenia or Coffeeberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its fragrant blossoms, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential, particularly for supporting digestive health and reducing inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Randia nitida
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales. Ultimately, Randia nitida is a member of the family Rubiaceae within the genus Randia.

Common Names

This plant, Randia nitida, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the mountain hogplum, while in Spanish, it is commonly called mamey de monte or mamey de monte de montaña. Additionally, it is known as nance de monte in certain Spanish-speaking regions.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several distinct tropical regions. Within the Caribbean, its range includes the Windward Islands. Moving to Northern South America, it can be found growing in both Guyana and Venezuela. The species also extends into Western South America, specifically throughout Colombia.
