Moringa oleifera

Moringa oleifera, commonly known as the drumstick tree or miracle tree, is a nutrient-dense powerhouse celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its exceptional nutritional profile, every part of this plant offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antibacterial properties, making it a vital resource for enhancing global health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Moringa oleifera
  • Family: Moringaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Moringa oleifera, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Brassicales. Finally, it is a member of the family Moringaceae and is situated in the genus Moringa.

Distribution

This plant, Moringa oleifera, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several warm regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in the country of Libya. Its presence extends to the Macaronesian islands, specifically within Cape Verde. In West Tropical Africa, the species is established in Benin and Burkina. Additionally, the plant is widely distributed throughout the Gambia.
