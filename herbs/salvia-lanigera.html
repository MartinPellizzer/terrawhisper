Salvia lanigera

Salvia lanigera, commonly known as woolly sage, is a distinctive Australian perennial prized for its soft, felt-like foliage. Native to New South Wales, this aromatic shrub features delicate grey-green leaves and elegant purple blooms. It is widely used in medicinal practices and ornamental gardening for its soothing, therapeutic properties.
