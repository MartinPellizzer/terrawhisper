Qetetina, scientifically known as Verbascum sinaiticum, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to the rugged landscapes of the Sinai Peninsula. Renowned in traditional folk medicine, this plant is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating respiratory ailments and inflammatory conditions through its potent bioactive compounds.
