Lonchocarpus

Lonchocarpus, commonly known as a genus of flowering plants in the Fabaceae family, holds significant botanical interest. Found primarily in tropical African regions, these woody species are valued for their ecological roles and potential medicinal properties. Exploring their bioactive compounds offers promising insights into natural pharmacology and traditional healing practices.

  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Fabales and categorized under the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Lonchocarpus.

Additionally, the species extends its range into the coastal areas of Gambia and Guinea-Bissau. Its presence is also well-documented within the borders of Ghana.
