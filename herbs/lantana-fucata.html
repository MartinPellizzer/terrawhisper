The ecology of Lantana fucata is characterized by its remarkable adaptability to diverse ecosystems and a broad altitudinal distribution. It thrives across a wide variety of habitats, ranging from anthropized areas (Área Antrópica) and coastal restingas to complex biomes such as the Caatinga (strictu sensu), Cerrado (lato sensu), and Carrasco. The species also colonizes various forest types, including seasonal semi-deciduous forests (Floresta Estacional Semi-decidual), gallery forests (Floresta Ciliar ou Galeria), and ombrophilous forests, both ombrophilous dense forests (Floresta Ombrófila) and mixed ombrophilous forests (Floresta Ombrófila Mista).
