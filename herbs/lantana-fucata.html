Lantana fucata

Lantana fucata, commonly known as a medicinal shrub, is a versatile plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it offers therapeutic potential in treating various ailments. This introduction explores its botanical characteristics, traditional uses, and the growing scientific interest in its pharmacological properties.
