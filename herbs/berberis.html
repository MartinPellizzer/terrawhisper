Berberis

Berberis, commonly known as barberry, is a versatile genus of shrubs prized for both culinary and medicinal uses. Renowned for containing the alkaloid berberine, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This ancient remedy is frequently utilized to support metabolic health, digestion, and immune system function naturally.

  • Scientific name: Berberis
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Berberidaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Berberis.
