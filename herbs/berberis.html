Oregon-grape

Berberis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (125 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Oregon-grape, scientifically known as Berberis, is a versatile shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Renowned for its bright yellow roots and berries, it contains berberine, a potent alkaloid. Traditionally used to support digestive health, regulate blood sugar, and combat inflammation, this resilient plant remains a cornerstone of herbal medicine.
