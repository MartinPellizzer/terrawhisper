Collinsonia canadensis, scientifically known as Collinsonia canadensis, is a perennial herb native to North America, commonly called Stone Root. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it is primarily used to support lymphatic health, alleviate congestion, and address inflammatory conditions. Its distinctive yellow flowers make it a notable addition to woodland ecosystems.
