Medicago falcata

Sickle medick

Medicago falcata, commonly known as sickle medic, is a versatile legume species valued in traditional medicine. Belonging to the Fabaceae family, this plant is recognized for its unique morphological features and therapeutic potential. Research explores its bioactive compounds, highlighting its importance in addressing various health concerns through natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Medicago falcata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Medicago falcata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and the genus Medicago.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Medicago falcata, is referred to by various common names depending on the language. In Bulgarian, it is called сърповидна люцерна, while in Russian, it is known as Люцерна серповидная. Moving to Central Europe, the Czech name for this species is tolice srpovitá, and in Estonia, it is called sirplutsern. Finally, in Finland, the plant is commonly known as sirppimailanen.
