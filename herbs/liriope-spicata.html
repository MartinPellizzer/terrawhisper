Creeping lilyturf, scientifically known as Liropis spicata, is a versatile, shade-loving perennial prized in landscaping. Renowned for its dense, grass-like foliage and elegant purple flower spikes, this hardy plant offers excellent groundcover capabilities. It serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes, making it a functional addition to any garden.
