Liriope spicata

Liriope spicata, commonly known as creeping lily, is a resilient perennial herb valued for its lush, grass-like foliage and delicate purple blooms. Frequently used in landscape design for groundcover, this hardy plant thrives in shade, offering both ornamental beauty and practical ecological benefits in diverse garden environments.

  • Scientific name: Liriope spicata
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asparagaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Liriope.
