Jatropha multifida

Jatrophia multifida, commonly known as Coral Plant or Beach Coral, is a striking medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant red blooms, this plant offers diverse therapeutic potential. Traditionally, its various parts are utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Jatropha multifida
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Jatropha.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Africa. Within the Macaronesian islands, it can be found growing in Cape Verde. Its presence extends into West Tropical Africa, specifically within the territories of Gambia, Guinea, and Togo. Furthermore, the species reaches into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is located in the Central African Republic. Together, these locations highlight the plant's wide range across the tropical and subtropical zones of the continent.
