Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Africa. Within the Macaronesian islands, it can be found growing in Cape Verde. Its presence extends into West Tropical Africa, specifically within the territories of Gambia, Guinea, and Togo. Furthermore, the species reaches into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is located in the Central African Republic. Together, these locations highlight the plant's wide range across the tropical and subtropical zones of the continent.

Plant Parts

This plant, Jatropha multifida, possesses several components that are utilized in traditional medicinal practices. The entire structure, including the woody stems and the more slender stems of younger growth, contains specialized tissues. Within these parts, one can find a milky latex that is often characteristic of the genus. Furthermore, the plant's medicinal potential is often derived from its deeper structures, specifically through the use of the yellow rootbark and the dense rootwood, which are harvested for their therapeutic properties.
