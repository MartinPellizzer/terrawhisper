Rubus crassifolius

What's the taxonomical classification of Rubus crassifolius?

Rubus crassifolius belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Rubus, and its specific species epithet, crassifolius.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rubus crassifolius has large, broadly ovate leaves that are typically 10 to 25 centimeters long, featuring 3 to 5 coarse, serrated leaflets and a distinctively thick, leathery texture. The leaf margins are deeply and irregularly dentate, while the undersides often exhibit a dense coating of fine hairs. Its stems are woody, sprawling, and heavily armed with sharp, curved prickles that aid in climbing. The plant produces small, white to pale pink flowers that eventually develop into aggregate fruits. These berries are typically dark red or black when fully ripe and are surrounded by persistent calyx lobes.
