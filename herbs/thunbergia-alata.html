Distribution

This plant, known as Thunbergia alata, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally within the borders of Ghana. The species also inhabits the landscapes of Guinea and the Ivory Coast. Furthermore, its presence extends into the territories of Liberia. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes Nigeria.

This plant, Thunbergia alata, is widely recognized for its vibrant and ornamental flower, which serves as a key feature of its botanical identity. The blooms typically display a striking trumpet shape with deep purple or blue corollas and bright yellow centers, often accented by contrasting colored throats. In medicinal contexts, while various parts of climbing plants are sometimes studied, the visual characteristics and reproductive structures of the flower remain its most distinguishing morphological attribute.
