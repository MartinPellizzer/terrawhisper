Macaranga heterophylla

Macaranga heterophyllia, commonly known as the "Macaranga" plant, is a versatile medicinal species native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This study explores its phytochemical properties, therapeutic potential, and its significant role in ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Macaranga heterophylla
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus name Macaranga.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Macaranga heterophyllia, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently called the Macaranga tree, while in Malay, it is known as pokok kangkang ayam. In Indonesian, it is commonly referred to as pohon kangkang ayam, and in Tagalog, it is known as kangkang ayam.
