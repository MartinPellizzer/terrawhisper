Common Purple Mallow, scientifically known as Urena lobata, is a perennial shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant purple flowers and serrated leaves, this medicinal plant is deeply embedded in traditional healing. It is widely utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties in various cultures.
