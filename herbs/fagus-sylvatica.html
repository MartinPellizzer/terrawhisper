European beech, scientifically known as Fagus sylvatica, is a majestic deciduous tree deeply rooted in European landscapes. Beyond its ecological importance, it holds historical medicinal value. Traditionally, its bark and leaves have been utilized in folk remedies to address inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various digestive issues throughout history.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the European beech, possesses various anatomical structures, though its medicinal applications are most frequently associated with its protective outer layer. The bark of Fagus sylvatica is characterized by a smooth, grey texture during its youth, which eventually becomes more textured as the tree matures. In traditional herbal practices, extracts or preparations derived from the bark have been studied for their potential therapeutic properties, often utilized for their astringent qualities in topical applications.
