Fagus sylvatica

Fagus sylvatica, commonly known as the European beech, is a majestic deciduous tree integral to temperate forest ecosystems. Beyond its ecological significance, it holds historical medicinal value. Traditionally, its bark and leaves have been utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Fagus sylvatica
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Fagales and the family Fagaceae. Finally, its taxonomic position is completed within the genus Fagus, specifically identified as Fagus sylvatica.
