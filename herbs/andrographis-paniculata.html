Andrographis paniculata

Andrographis paniculata, commonly known as King of Bitters, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in Ayurvedic and traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its intense bitterness, this plant offers powerful anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and immune-boosting properties, making it a vital natural remedy for treating respiratory infections and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Andrographis paniculata
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Andrographis paniculata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Andrographis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range also extends significantly into the Indian Subcontinent. In this region, the species is documented to grow in Assam and throughout Bangladesh. Together, these locations highlight the diverse habitats where the plant thrives.
