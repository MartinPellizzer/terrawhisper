King of bitter

Andrographis paniculata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1177 studies) · ★★★★★

King of bitter, scientifically known as Andrographis paniculata, is a versatile medicinal herb widely used in Ayurvedic and traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its intense bitterness, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and immune-boosting properties, making it a powerful natural remedy for treating respiratory infections, fever, and various inflammatory conditions.

This plant, known scientifically as Andrographis paniculata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Andrographis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range also extends significantly into the Indian Subcontinent. In this region, the species is documented to grow in Assam and throughout Bangladesh. Together, these locations highlight the diverse habitats where the plant thrives.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as King of Bitters, is a versatile medicinal herb where multiple components hold therapeutic value. The most frequently utilized portion is the aerial parts, which encompass the stems and the foliage. Specifically, the green leaves are highly valued in traditional medicine, and even a single leaf can contain significant concentrations of bioactive andrographolides. While the whole plant is often harvested and dried for various preparations, the roots also contribute to the plant's medicinal profile, though they are used less commonly than the above-ground structures.
