Schisandra lancifolia

Schisandra lancifolia, commonly known as a member of the magnolia family, is a medicinal climbing shrub valued in traditional herbal practices. Primarily native to East Asian forests, this plant is recognized for its unique bioactive compounds, which are frequently utilized to support vitality, balance bodily functions, and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra lancifolia
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Austrobaileyales, it is further categorized into the family Schisandraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Schisandra.
