Stemona aphylla

Stemona aphylla, commonly known as the stemona plant, is a significant medicinal herb in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, it is primarily utilized for its anthelmintic and antitussive properties. This botanical species plays a crucial role in treating parasitic infections and various respiratory ailments effectively.

  • Scientific name: Stemona aphylla
  • Family: Stemonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Pandanales. Finally, it is a member of the family Stemonaeae and falls under the genus Stemona.
