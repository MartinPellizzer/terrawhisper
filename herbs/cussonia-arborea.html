Octopus Cabbage Tree, scientifically known as Cussonia arborea, is a distinctive South African shrub prized for its unique, hand-like foliage. Often used in landscaping, this hardy plant offers architectural beauty. Beyond its aesthetics, it holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various parts utilized in traditional medicinal practices for healing.
