Cussonia arborea

Cussonia arborea, commonly known as the Cabbage Tree, is a distinctive South African medicinal plant. Renowned for its large, rosette-like leaves, it serves various traditional purposes. Indigenous communities utilize its bark and roots to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin irritations, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Cussonia arborea
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Araliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cussonia.
