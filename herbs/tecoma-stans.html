Arizona yellow bells

Tecoma stans · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (382 studies) · ★★★★★

Arizona yellow bells, scientifically known as Tecoma stans, is a striking, drought-tolerant shrub native to the Americas. Renowned for its tubular, golden-orange blossoms, it attracts pollinators like hummingbirds and bees. This resilient plant thrives in sunny, arid landscapes, offering vibrant color and medicinal potential in traditional healing practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, Tecoma stans, possesses various parts that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The vibrant green leaves are often harvested to prepare traditional remedies, while the leaf extract serves as a concentrated source of bioactive compounds used in topical or oral applications. The sturdy stems and the textured barks are also significant, frequently decocted to treat inflammation or infections. Additionally, the roots are valued in many folk medicine practices for their specific therapeutic potencies, making the entire structure of the plant a vital resource in herbal healing.
