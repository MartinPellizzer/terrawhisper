Arizona yellow bells, scientifically known as Tecoma stans, is a striking, drought-tolerant shrub native to the Americas. Renowned for its tubular, golden-orange blossoms, it attracts pollinators like hummingbirds and bees. This resilient plant thrives in sunny, arid landscapes, offering vibrant color and medicinal potential in traditional healing practices.
