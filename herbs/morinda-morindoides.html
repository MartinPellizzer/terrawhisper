Morinda morindoides

Morinda morindoides, commonly known as the small-leaf noni, is a significant medicinal shrub native to South Asia. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Morinda morindoides
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, it is positioned in the Rubiaceae family. Specifically, Morinda morindoides is identified by the genus Morinda.
