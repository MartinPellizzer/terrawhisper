Strychnos myrtoides

Strychnos myrtoides, commonly known as the "moya" or "moya-moya" in certain regions, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical forests. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized for their bioactive alkaloids, offering potential therapeutic properties in treating diverse ailments through specialized phytochemical applications and ethnobotanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Strychnos myrtoides
  • Family: Loganiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Gentianales. Finally, it is a member of the family Loganiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Strychnos.
