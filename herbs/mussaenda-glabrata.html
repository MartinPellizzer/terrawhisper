Mussaenda glabrata

Mussaenda glabrata, commonly known as the Firecracker Plant, is a striking ornamental shrub prized for its brilliant, flame-like red bracts. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this tropical species holds significant botanical interest. Its unique floral structures and vibrant foliage make it a captivating addition to diverse medicinal and garden landscapes.
