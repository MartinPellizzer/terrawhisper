The life history of Mallotus resinosus is characterized by its classification as a phanerophyte, indicating a growth habit where the perennating buds are located well above the ground on woody branches. This life form allows the plant to persist through various environmental conditions by maintaining its structural integrity via elevated stems. While the species predominantly functions as a phanerophyte, its developmental plasticity within its ecosystem may occasionally exhibit characteristics associated with other life forms, such as the nanophanerophyte stage in smaller or more stunted individuals where the buds are held close to the ground. Throughout its life cycle, the plant focuses on the development of woody tissues and the production of specialized resins, transitioning from early vegetative growth to reproductive maturity where it utilizes its elevated stature to facilitate seed dispersal and optimize light interception.
