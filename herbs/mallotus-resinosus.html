Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Malpighiales, and the family Euphorbiaceae. Within this taxonomic hierarchy, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Mallotus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several key regions of Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in both India and Sri Lanka. Its range extends into the Indo-China region, where it is documented in Myanmar and Laos. Additionally, the species is present in the Andaman Islands. This wide dispersal highlights the plant's adaptability to various tropical environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Kamala tree, utilizes its various structures for medicinal purposes, with particular significance placed on its roots. While the most famous medicinal component is the resinous fruit, the roots of Mallotus resinosus are also recognized in traditional practices for their therapeutic potential. These underground organs are often processed to extract bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's overall pharmacological profile, supporting its use in diverse herbal remedies.
