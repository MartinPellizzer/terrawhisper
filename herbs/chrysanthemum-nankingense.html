Chrysanthemum nankingense

Chrysanthemum nankingense, commonly known as Nanking chrysanthemum, is a cherished medicinal herb in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is frequently used to alleviate inflammation, soothe sore throats, and clear heat. This aromatic flower serves as a vital botanical resource for holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Chrysanthemum nankingense
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
