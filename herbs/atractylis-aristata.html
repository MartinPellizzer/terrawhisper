Atractylis aristata

Atractylis aristata, commonly known as the Aristate Atractylis, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, this plant has been traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and skin conditions, owing to its unique bioactive compounds.
