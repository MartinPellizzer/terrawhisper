Feijoa, scientifically known as Acca sellowiana, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its aromatic, edible fruit. Often called pineapple guava, its unique flavor blends strawberry and guava notes. Beyond culinary uses, this hardy plant offers beautiful blossoms and dense foliage, making it an excellent ornamental garden addition.
