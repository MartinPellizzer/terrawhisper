Feijoa

Feijoa, commonly known as pineapple guava, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its unique, aromatic fruit. Boasting a delightful flavor profile of pineapple, strawberry, and guava, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its antioxidant properties and ability to support overall immune health and wellness.

  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Feijoa.
