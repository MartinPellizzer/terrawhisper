Dryopteris erythrosora

Dryopteris erythrosora, commonly known as the red-sorus fern, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Characterized by its vibrant, reddish spore clusters, this fern contains bioactive compounds with potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. It serves as a fascinating subject for both ethnobotanical study and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Dryopteris erythrosora
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae, falling within the order Polypodiales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Polypodiaceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Dryopteris.
