Distribution

This plant, Elaeis guineensis, is widely distributed across various regions within West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. The species is also documented to inhabit the landscapes of Burkina. Furthermore, its presence extends to the coastal and inland areas of Gambia. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, Elaeis guineensis, is widely recognized for its versatile and highly valued components. The most economically significant part is the African oil palm fruit, which contains a rich, oily pulp used extensively in food and medicinal applications. Within these fruits, one can find the nut, which serves as a vital source of lipids. Each individual nut is encased in a hard shell that protects the internal seed, often referred to as the nut in a singular context. Additionally, the large, pinnate leaves of the palm are frequently utilized in various traditional practices, showcasing the extensive utility of every part of this remarkable species.
