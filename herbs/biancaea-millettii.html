Biancaea millettii

Biancaea milletii, commonly known as the African mahogany or milletii, is a significant medicinal tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation and infections to digestive and skin disorders.

  • Scientific name: Biancaea millettii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is specifically situated in the family Fabaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Biancaea.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Biancaea millettii, is referred to by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is commonly called 小叶云实 (zh), while in Swedish (sv), Cebuano (ceb), Vietnamese (vi), and Waray (war), it is identified by its botanical synonym, Caesalpinia millettii.

Distribution

This plant is specifically found within a very localized geographic range. Its documented presence is limited to the southeastern region of China. Within this particular area, it thrives in the unique environmental conditions provided by the landscape. Because its distribution is so focused, it is considered a regional species rather than a widespread one. Consequently, understanding its habitat in Southeast China is essential for its conservation.
