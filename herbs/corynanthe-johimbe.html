Distribution

This plant, known as Corynanthe johimbé, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Nigeria. Its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is present in the province of Cabinda. Furthermore, the species is documented throughout Cameroon and the Republic of the Congo. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the territory of Equatorial Guinea.

Plant Parts

This plant, Corynanthe johimbé, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its roots. The bark of the roots is the most significant part used in traditional and pharmaceutical applications, as it contains high concentrations of indole alkaloids, most notably yohimbine. These bioactive compounds extracted from the root system are utilized for their physiological effects on the human body, particularly in treating certain circulatory and sexual health issues.
