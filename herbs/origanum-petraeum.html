Origanum petraeum

Origanum petraeum, commonly known as Rock Oregano, is a resilient perennial herb native to rocky, mountainous terrains. Distinguished by its aromatic foliage, this botanical species is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, offering natural therapeutic benefits for various ailments throughout herbal practices.
