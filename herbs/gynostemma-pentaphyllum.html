Gynostemma, scientifically known as Gynostemma pentaphyllum, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Asia. Often called "Jiaogulan," it is renowned for its adaptogenic properties, helping the body manage stress. Rich in saponins similar to ginseng, this plant supports metabolic health, boosts immunity, and promotes overall longevity and vitality.
