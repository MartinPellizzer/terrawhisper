Gynostemma, scientifically known as Gynostemma pentaphyllum, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Asia. Often called "Jiaogulan," it is renowned for its adaptogenic properties, helping the body manage stress. Rich in saponins similar to ginseng, this plant supports metabolic health, boosts immunity, and promotes overall longevity and vitality.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several distinct regions in East Asia. In the northern reaches of its territory, it can be found growing in the Kuril Islands within the Russian Far East. Its distribution extends significantly through China, where it is documented in the North-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is prevalent throughout South-Central China and the Southeast part of the country. Finally, its habitat reaches as far south as the island province of Hainan.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Gymnema pentaphyllum or Gurmar, is a climbing shrub where the most therapeutically significant components are its green leaves and the various aerial parts of the stem. In traditional medicine, the plant is frequently processed into a soothing herb tea made from its harvested foliage to help regulate blood sugar levels. Additionally, the plant can be utilized in the form of dry grass for topical applications or ground into a fine powder that is easily incorporated into capsules or dietary supplements for concentrated medicinal use.
