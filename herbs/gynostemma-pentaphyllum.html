Gynostemma pentaphyllum

Gymnostemma pentaphyllum, commonly known as Laparong in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional wellness. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is widely utilized to support metabolic health, enhance vitality, and balance physiological functions, offering a natural approach to holistic healing and long-term wellness.

  • Scientific name: Gynostemma pentaphyllum
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Gymnostemma.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several distinct regions in East Asia. In the northern reaches of its territory, it can be found growing in the Kuril Islands within the Russian Far East. Its distribution extends significantly through China, where it is documented in the North-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is prevalent throughout South-Central China and the Southeast part of the country. Finally, its habitat reaches as far south as the island province of Hainan.
