Artabotrys hexapetalus

Artabotrys hexapetalus, commonly known as the climbing ylang-ylang, is a versatile woody climber valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its fragrant, star-shaped flowers, this plant offers therapeutic potential, particularly for treating respiratory ailments and skin conditions. Its unique botanical properties make it a significant subject for ethnobotanical study.

  • Scientific name: Artabotrys hexapetalus
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artabotrys.
