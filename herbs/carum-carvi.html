Caraway plant, scientifically known as Carum carvi, is a fragrant herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it has been utilized for centuries in traditional medicine to aid digestion, reduce bloating, and alleviate gastrointestinal discomfort. Its potent essential oils offer significant therapeutic and culinary benefits.
