Carum carvi

Carum carvi, commonly known as caraway, is a fragrant annual herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it is widely used in culinary traditions and traditional medicine. It offers significant therapeutic benefits, particularly for aiding digestion, relieving bloating, and soothing various gastrointestinal discomforts naturally.

  • Scientific name: Carum carvi
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Apiales and sits within the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Carum, specifically as the species Carum carvi.

This plant exhibits a distinct geographical presence across several regions within Northern Europe. Its distribution can be traced through various territories, including Denmark and Finland.
