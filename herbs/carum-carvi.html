Caraway plant, scientifically known as Carum carvi, is a fragrant herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it has been utilized for centuries in traditional medicine to aid digestion, reduce bloating, and alleviate gastrointestinal discomfort. Its potent essential oils offer significant therapeutic and culinary benefits.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Carum carvi, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components offer distinct therapeutic benefits. The most commonly utilized parts are the small, aromatic fruits, which are technically known as schizocarps. These fruits contain the tiny, dark seeds that are widely used in culinary and medicinal applications to aid digestion. The lush, green leaves of the plant are also edible and can be used in various preparations. Furthermore, the plant is a rich source of essential oil, which is extracted from the seeds and fruits to provide its potent, characteristic aroma and concentrated medicinal properties.
