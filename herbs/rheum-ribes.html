Rheum ribes

Rheum ribes, commonly known as Ribes rhubarb, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Central and Western Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its roots contain potent anthraquinones, offering significant purgative and digestive benefits. This resilient plant remains a vital botanical resource for therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Rheum ribes
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed within the genus Rheum.
