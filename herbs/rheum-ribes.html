Syrian rhubarb

Rheum ribes · Accepted scientific name

Syrian rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum ribes, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of the Middle East. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots contain bioactive compounds with potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable natural resource for treating various digestive and systemic ailments.
