Squirting cucumber

Ecballium elaterium · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (38 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Squirting cucumber, scientifically known as Ecballium elaterium, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its unique seed-dispersal mechanism, it has historically been used in traditional medicine as a potent purgative. However, due to its high toxicity, it must be handled with extreme caution and expertise.
