Squirting cucumber

Ecballium elaterium · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (38 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Squirting cucumber, scientifically known as Ecballium elaterium, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its unique seed-dispersal mechanism, it has historically been used in traditional medicine as a potent purgative. However, due to its high toxicity, it must be handled with extreme caution and expertise.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as squirting cucumber, contains various components that are utilized in traditional medicine. The fruits are most distinctive due to their ability to eject seeds under pressure, and the fruit juice extracted from them is often used for its potent purgative properties. Additionally, the root is recognized for its medicinal value, particularly in treating digestive ailments, while the leaves can also be processed for their specific therapeutic applications.
