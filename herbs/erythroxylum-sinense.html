What's the taxonomical classification of Erythroxylum sinense?

Erythroxylum sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Malpighiales. The plant is a member of the family Erythroxylaceae, specifically categorized within the genus Erythroxylum, and is identified by the species name sinense.
