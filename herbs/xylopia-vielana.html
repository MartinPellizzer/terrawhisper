Distribution

This plant is found across several distinct regions within Southeast Asia and East Asia. In China, its distribution is specifically concentrated in the Southeast portion of the country. The species also extends into the Indo-China region, where it can be found in Cambodia. Additionally, it inhabits various parts of Laos and Thailand. Finally, its range includes the forested areas of Vietnam.
