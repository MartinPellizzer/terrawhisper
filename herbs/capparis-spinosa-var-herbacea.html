Capparis spinosa var. herbacea

Capparis spinosa var. herbacea, commonly known as the herbaceous caper, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this variety is traditionally used to support digestive health, manage metabolic functions, and promote skin healing through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Capparis spinosa var. herbacea
  • Family: Capparaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales, it is classified under the family Capparaceae and the genus Capparis.
