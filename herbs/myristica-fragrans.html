Nutmeg tree, scientifically known as Myristica fragrans, is an evergreen tropical tree prized for its aromatic seeds and bright red mace. Beyond its culinary importance, this plant offers significant medicinal value, possessing potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties used in traditional healing to support digestive and neurological health.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Myristica fragrans, is a fascinating species where multiple components are utilized for their distinct medicinal and culinary properties. The most prominent part is the seed, which is housed within a fleshy, reddish-orange outer covering known as the aril, commonly referred to as mace. While the whole fruit is harvested, the primary medicinal value is derived from the nutmegs themselves, which consist of the hard inner kernel. When these nutmeg seeds are processed, they release potent essential oils that are used in various traditional remedies to aid digestion and improve circulation.
