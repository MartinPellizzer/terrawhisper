Grindelia hirsutula

Hairy gumplant

Grindelia hirsuta, commonly known as balsamroot or balsamroot sunflower, is a resilient perennial native to western North America. Renowned for its bright yellow blooms, this medicinal plant has historically been used by Indigenous cultures to treat respiratory ailments, skin irritations, and digestive issues due to its potent antimicrobial properties.

  • Scientific name: Grindelia hirsutula
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Grindelia hirsutula, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. It is a member of the Asteraceae family and is specifically categorized within the genus Grindelia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several different continents and climate zones. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within Great Britain. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species is also found growing in Germany. The distribution extends to North America, where it inhabits the Northwest Territories of Subarctic America and the province of Alberta in Western Canada. Additionally, the plant reaches the Southern Hemisphere, specifically appearing in New South Wales, Australia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Grindelia hirsuta, commonly known as balsamroot, utilizes its various anatomical structures to facilitate both growth and medicinal potency. The whole plant is characterized by a basal rosette of hairy, often glandular leaves that serve as the primary site for photosynthesis and the storage of bioactive compounds. During its flowering season, the plant produces distinct reproductive structures, most notably the unopened composite flower heads, which consist of tightly packed involucral bracts protecting the developing florets. These developing heads are essential stages in the plant's lifecycle, housing the concentrated resins and essential oils that are frequently sought after for their therapeutic properties.
