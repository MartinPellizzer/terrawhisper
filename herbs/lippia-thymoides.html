Lippia thymoides

Lippia thymoides, commonly known as West African wild basil, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to sub-Saharan Africa. Renowned for its potent aromatic properties, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering significant antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits for holistic wellness.
