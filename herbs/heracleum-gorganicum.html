Heracleum gorganicum

Heracleum gorganicum, commonly known as Gorgan Hogweed, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Caspian Sea region. Renowned for its impressive stature and white umbel flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, containing bioactive compounds traditionally utilized in folk medicine for their diverse therapeutic and healing properties.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
