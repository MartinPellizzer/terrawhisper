Fumaria indica

Fumaria indica, commonly known as Indian Fumitory, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Fumariaceae family. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. This plant is widely utilized to treat skin disorders, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Fumaria indica
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Fumaria indica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the Papaveraceae family and falls under the genus Fumaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across parts of Western Asia and the Indian Subcontinent. In the Western Asian region, it can be found growing in Afghanistan and Iran. Its presence extends further into the Levant, specifically within the Lebanon-Syria area. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is well-established in the state of Assam. Additionally, the plant is commonly documented throughout Bangladesh.
