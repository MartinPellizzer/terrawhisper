Fumaria indica, is a medicinal herb belonging to the Papaveraceae family, commonly known as Indian Fumitory. Widely distributed across tropical Asia, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating skin ailments, digestive disorders, and inflammation through its potent bioactive compounds.
