Epimedium acuminatum, scientifically known as Epimedium acuminatum, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Often referred to as "Horny Goat Weed," its bioactive flavonoids are studied for their potential to support reproductive health, enhance vitality, and improve circulation, making it a cornerstone of many herbal wellness practices.
