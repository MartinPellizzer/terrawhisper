Epimedium acuminatum

Epimedium acuminatum, commonly known as the pointed epimedium, is a resilient perennial prized in traditional herbal medicine. Characterized by its unique lanceolate leaves and delicate flowering spikes, this plant is celebrated for its bioactive compounds, which are often utilized to support vitality, hormonal balance, and overall reproductive health.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium acuminatum
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Epimedium and the specific species acuminatum.
