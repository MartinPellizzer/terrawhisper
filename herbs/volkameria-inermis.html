Volkameria inermis

Volkameria inermis, commonly known as the Gliricidia or Gliricidia tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This hardy species remains a vital resource in ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Volkameria inermis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
