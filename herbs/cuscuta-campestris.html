Cuscuta campestris

Golden dodder

Cuscuta campesestris, commonly known as field dodder, is a parasitic plant characterized by its leafless, yellowish, thread-like stems. Lacking chlorophyll, it survives by extracting nutrients from host plants via specialized structures called haustoria. Beyond its ecological role, it holds significant interest in traditional medicine for its diverse bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Cuscuta campestris
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cuscuta, specifically as the species Cuscuta campestris.
