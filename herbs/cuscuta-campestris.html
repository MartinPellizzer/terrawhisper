Golden dodder, scientifically known as Cuscuta campestris, is a parasitic plant characterized by its leafless, thread-like stems. Lacking chlorophyll, it survives by extracting nutrients from host plants. Beyond its biological nature, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance, being utilized in various traditional medicinal practices for its unique therapeutic properties.
