Sesbania grandiflora

Sesbania grandiflora, commonly known as Hummingbird Tree or Agati, is a versatile leguminous shrub prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its nutritional density and therapeutic properties, it is utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This resilient plant serves as both a vital food source and medicine.

  • Scientific name: Sesbania grandiflora
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Sesbania and the species Sesbania grandiflora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans both Atlantic islands and the African mainland. Within the Macaronesia region, its presence is documented in Cape Verde. Moving to West Tropical Africa, the species is widely distributed across several key nations. It can be found growing in the territories of Benin, Ghana, and Guinea. Finally, its distribution extends further south into Nigeria, completing its regional footprint.
