Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans both Atlantic islands and the African mainland. Within the Macaronesia region, its presence is documented in Cape Verde. Moving to West Tropical Africa, the species is widely distributed across several key nations. It can be found growing in the territories of Benin, Ghana, and Guinea. Finally, its distribution extends further south into Nigeria, completing its regional footprint.

Plant Parts

This plant, Sesbania grandiflora, is a versatile medicinal species where almost every component offers therapeutic potential. The feathery, bipinnate leaves are frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation and various skin ailments. The bright, large flowers are not only edible but are also valued for their nutritional content and their ability to aid in treating digestive issues. The seeds are often employed in herbal formulations to address specific systemic conditions, while the stem bark is harvested for its potent properties in managing swelling and pain. Finally, the roots are utilized in various traditional practices to treat a range of ailments, including fever and certain inflammatory conditions, making the entire plant a comprehensive resource in ethnobotany.
