Trianthema portulacastrum

Trianthema portulacastrum, commonly known as purslane spines, is a versatile medicinal herb used extensively in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties, it plays a vital role in treating skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering significant therapeutic potential in various natural healing systems.

  • Scientific name: Trianthema portulacastrum
  • Family: Aizoaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it falls into the family Aizoaceae and is categorized under the genus Trianthema.
