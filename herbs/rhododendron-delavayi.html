Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Additionally, it is native to the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented within the state of Assam. Finally, its range extends across the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya.
