What's the taxonomical classification of Rhododendron delavayi?

Rhododendron delaavay belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is a member of the order Ericales and falls within the family Ericaceae. Ultimately, the plant is identified by its genus, Rhododendron, and its specific species designation, delaayi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rhododendron delavayi has leathery, elliptical leaves that are dark green on the upper surface and often display a fine, brownish indumentum on the undersides. The plant grows as an evergreen shrub with woody stems that can reach heights of several meters in its natural habitat. Its inflorescences consist of clusters of funnel-shaped flowers that typically exhibit a vibrant pink or rose-colored hue. Each individual blossom features five fused petals and prominent stamens that emerge from the center of the corolla. The texture of the foliage remains consistent throughout the seasons, providing a dense and structural appearance to the shrub.

According to a study published by "Zhong yao cai = Zhongyaocai = Journal of Chinese medicinal materials", the leaves of Rhododendron delavayi were studied to identify their chemical constituents. Through the use of column chromatography with silica gel and Sephadex LH-20, 8 compounds were isolated and elucidated. These compounds include 3',4',7-trihydroxy-3,5-dimethoxyflavone (1), 3',4',5,7-tetrahydroxy-3-methoxyflavone (2), quercetin-3-O-beta-D-arabino-pyranoside (3), catechin (4), epicatechin (5), epicatechin-(2beta-->O-->7,4beta-->8)-ent-epicatechin (6), (2S)-4-(3,4-dihydroxyphenyl)-2-butanol (7), and (3,4-dihydroxyphenyl)-2-ethanol (8). Compounds 2, 3, 6, 7, and 8 were isolated from this plant for the first time.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause severe physiological distress including nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, dizziness, confusion, arrhythmia, seizures, and respiratory depression. These toxic effects typically stem from the presence of grayanotoxins within its tissues. Once ingested, the compounds interfere with sodium channels in cell membranes, leading to neurological and cardiovascular instability. Such symptoms can escalate rapidly from mild gastrointestinal upset to life-threatening cardiac irregularities. Immediate medical attention is necessary if someone experiences these reactions after exposure.
