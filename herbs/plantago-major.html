Broadleaf plantain, scientifically known as Plantago major, is a resilient perennial herb found globally in disturbed soils. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties. Often used to soothe insect bites and skin irritations, this common plant is a versatile staple in natural medicine.
