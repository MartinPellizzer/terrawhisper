Geranium thunbergii

Geranium thuunbergii, commonly known as Thunberg's geranium, is a delicate perennial herb native to specific rocky terrains. Renowned for its vibrant blooms and unique foliage, this species holds significant potential in ethnobotany. Researchers continue exploring its bioactive compounds, investigating its traditional uses and therapeutic properties in modern herbal medicine.
