Gaultheria hispidula

Gaultheria hispidula, commonly known as the bristly wintergreen, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the forest understories of North America. Renowned for its glossy, leathery leaves and delicate bell-shaped flowers, this plant offers significant medicinal potential, particularly through its natural concentration of methyl salicylate for therapeutic use.

  • Scientific name: Gaultheria hispidula
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gaultheria.
