Sinkiang fritillaria, scientifically known as Friitillaria pallidiflora, is a precious medicinal herb native to China. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its bulbs are primarily utilized to treat respiratory ailments, such as coughs and asthma. This species belongs to the Lilium family and is prized for its therapeutic properties.
