Fritillaria pallidiflora

Fritillaria pallidiflora, commonly known as the pale fritillary, is a delicate medicinal wildflower native to specific montane regions. Renowned for its unique aesthetic and traditional healing properties, this plant offers significant botanical interest. Exploring its chemical constituents reveals its potential role in supporting wellness through natural, plant-based remedies.

  • Scientific name: Fritillaria pallidiflora
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Liliales and the family Liliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Fritillaria, specifically the species Fritillaria pallidiflora.

Furthermore, the distribution extends into the eastern reaches of the continent within China. Specifically, its documented occurrence includes the remote Xinjiang region.
