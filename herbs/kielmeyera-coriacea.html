Kielmeyera coriacea

Kielmeyera coriacea, commonly known as the leather-leaf Kielmeyer, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical forest ecosystems. Renowned for its robust foliage, this species is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. Research explores its potential bioactive compounds, highlighting its importance in ethnobotanical studies and pharmacology.
