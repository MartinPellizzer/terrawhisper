Nepal Monkshood, scientifically known as Aconiutum heterophylloides, is a potent medicinal herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its complex alkaloids, it is used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. However, due to its high toxicity, it must be handled with extreme caution by skilled practitioners.

This plant, Aconitum heterophylloides, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Aconitum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several high-altitude regions of Asia. Within China, its presence is primarily documented in the Tibet region. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found inhabiting the East Himalaya. Additionally, the species is widely distributed throughout Nepal. These locations highlight its preference for the mountainous terrains of the Himalayan belt.
