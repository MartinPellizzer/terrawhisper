Bloodwood, scientifically known as Brosimum rubescens, is a distinctive tropical tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its striking, deep-red sap that resembles blood, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Indigenous cultures utilize its medicinal properties to treat various ailments, ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.
