Brosimum rubescens

Brosimum rubescens, commonly known as Red Milk Tree, is a remarkable tropical species native to the Amazon rainforest. Characterized by its distinct reddish latex, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. It is widely studied for its potential medicinal properties, particularly regarding its bioactive compounds and traditional healing uses.

  • Scientific name: Brosimum rubescens
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, it is further organized into the family Moraeaee and the genus Brosimum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across the tropical regions of Central and South America. In Central America, its presence is documented within the country of Panamá. Moving into Northern South America, the species can be found spanning several distinct territories. It is native to the Guianas, specifically inhabiting French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname.
