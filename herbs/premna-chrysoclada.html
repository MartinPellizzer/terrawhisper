Premna chrysoclada

Premna chrysoclada, commonly known as "Gros Michel" in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical climates. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
