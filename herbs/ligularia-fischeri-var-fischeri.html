This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct northern regions. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within Great Britain. This plant, Ligularia fischeri var. fischeri, is characterized by its prominent and large leaf structure, which serves as a vital component for its medicinal properties. The leaves are typically broad, often featuring a cordate or heart-shaped base with serrated margins, providing a significant surface area for photosynthesis and the accumulation of bioactive compounds.
