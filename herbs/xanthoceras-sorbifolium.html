Yellowhorn, scientifically known as Xanthoceras sorbifolium, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, its seeds and leaves possess significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Beyond its medicinal potential, this resilient plant is increasingly recognized for its oil-rich seeds, offering both therapeutic and industrial benefits.
