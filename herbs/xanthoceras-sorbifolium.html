Xanthoceras sorbifolium

Xanthoceras sorbifolium, commonly known as Yellow Xanthoceras, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its various parts—including leaves, bark, and seeds—possess significant therapeutic properties. Research highlights its potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, making it a subject of great pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Xanthoceras sorbifolium
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Xanthoceras.
