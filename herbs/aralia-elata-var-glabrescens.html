Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the temperate regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing across the Amur and Khabarovsk regions. Its distribution extends further into the Primorye territory and onto the island of Sakhalin. Beyond the Russian borders, the species is also documented within the Manchuria region of China.
