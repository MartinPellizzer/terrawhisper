Eremophila longifolia

Eremophila longifolia, commonly known as the Long-leaved Eremophil, is a distinctive shrub native to the arid regions of Western Australia. Renowned for its striking, slender foliage and vibrant floral displays, this resilient plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized by Indigenous communities for various medicinal and practical purposes.

  • Scientific name: Eremophila longifolia
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eremophila and the specific species Eremophila longifolia.
