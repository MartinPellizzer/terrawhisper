Uncaria hirsuta

Uncaria hirsuta, commonly known as hairy cat's claw, is a climbing woody vine native to the tropical forests of Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this botanical powerhouse is rich in bioactive alkaloids, frequently utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and immune-boosting properties to support overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Uncaria hirsuta
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Gentianales and the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Uncaria.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Uncaria hirsuta, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Chinese, it is known as 台湾钩藤 (zh) or 毛钩藤 (zh), while specifically in Taiwan, it is called 臺灣鉤藤 (zh-tw) and in mainland China as 台湾钩藤 (zh-cn). Additionally, in Japanese, the plant is commonly called 毛鉤藤 (ja).

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions across East Asia. Within mainland China, its presence is primarily recorded in the South-Central areas. It can also be found growing throughout the Southeast portions of China. Additionally, the species is widely distributed across the island of Taiwan. Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Uncaria hirsuta, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts. These above-ground components, which include the stems, leaves, and climbing tendrils, contain the bioactive compounds necessary for its therapeutic effects. When harvesting the aerial parts, practitioners focus on the entire vegetative structure that grows above the soil to ensure the collection of the plant's medicinal properties.
