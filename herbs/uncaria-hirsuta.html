Uncaria hirsuta

What's the taxonomical classification of Uncaria hirsuta?

Uncaria hirsuta belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within its taxonomic hierarchy, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Gentianaales, it falls into the family Rubiaceae. Specifically, this plant is identified by its genus, Uncaria, and its distinct species name, hirsuta.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Uncaria hirsuta has woody climbing vines characterized by small, elliptical leaves and specialized hooked spines that facilitate upward growth. The stem is typically slender and green, eventually becoming brownish and woody as the plant matures. Its leaves are arranged alternately along the stem and possess a smooth or slightly textured surface. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the axils of the leaves, often presenting in small clusters. The plant is also distinguished by its hairy texture, as indicated by its specific epithet.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant Uncaria hirsuta grows best in tropical or subtropical environments with well-draining soil and partial shade.

Cultivators must ensure the substrate remains consistently moist but never waterlogged to prevent root rot. Regular fertilization with organic matter helps promote the dense foliage necessary for a healthy harvest.

According to a study published by "Natural product research", three new monoterpenoid alkaloids named hirsutaniines A-C (1-3) were isolated from the aerial parts of Uncaria hirsuta. These chemical constituents were investigated to elucidate the medicinal plant's structure. The structures and absolute configurations of these 3 compounds were determined using NMR, X-ray diffraction, and CD analysis. Notably, compound 3 represents the 1st dimeric monoterpenoid alkaloid obtained from the genus Uncaria.

Historical documentation suggests that various decoctions and preparations were made from its stems and leaves to address specific bodily discomforts. These long-standing customs form the basis for contemporary research into the plant's bioactive compounds.

According to a study published by "The American journal of Chinese medicine", research was conducted using an electron spin resonance (ESR) technique. This method was used to measure the superoxide radical (O2-) and hydroxyl radical (OH) scavenging activity from the plant Uncaria hirsuta Haviland. The study detected these radicals as spin adducts of the spin traps 5,5-dimethylpyrroline-N-oxide (DMPO). The scavenging potencies of water extracts of the crude drugs were evaluated by their ability to reduce the peaks of these spin adducts.

What are the pharmacological activities of Uncaria hirsuta?

This plant has anti-inflammatory, antiapoptotic, anticancer, antioxidant, and neuroprotective pharmacological activities. Its chemical constituents work to neutralize free radicals, which provides a robust antioxidant effect that protects cells from oxidative stress. These bioactive compounds also trigger pathways that inhibit apoptosis, making the plant effective in its anticancer and antiapoptotic properties. Furthermore, the plant helps reduce systemic inflammation and offers neuroprotective benefits that may shield the brain from degenerative processes. Such diverse biological actions highlight the significant medicinal potential of Uncaria hirsuta in various therapeutic applications.

According to a study published by "Journal of ethnopharmacology", Uncaria hirsuta Havil is one of the species belonging to the genus Uncaria that makes up Medical Uncariae Ramulus Cum Uncis. Modern pharmacological studies show that both the total extracts and the phytoconstituents isolated from this mixture possess neuroprotective, antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, anticancer, antibacterial, and autophagy-enhancer properties. These properties allow for benefits in treating diseases such as neurodegenerative diseases, cancer, cardiovascular diseases, diabetes, and migraine. Between 2015 and the present, 107 newly marked chemical compositions including alkaloids, terpenoids, flavonoids, and steroids have been identified from the mixture. The alkaloids within the mixture have the capability to self-assemble with other classes of components while remaining biologically active.

What health conditions is this plant used for?

This plant is used for treating Alzheimer's disease, Parkinson's disease, d-galactosase-induced learning and memory dysfunctions, neurodegeneration, and various other neurodegenerative diseases. Researchers utilize its bioactive compounds to target the underlying mechanisms that drive cognitive decline. The plant shows significant promise in mitigating the effects of neurodegeneration by protecting neural pathways. It is specifically studied for its ability to reverse memory impairment caused by d-galactosase induction in animal models. These medicinal properties make it a vital subject of study for managing long-term neurodegenerative diseases.

According to a study published by "Journal of food and drug analysis", Uncaria hirsuta is investigated for its potential to address neurodegeneration leading to conditions such as Alzheimer's and Parkinson's diseases. The 95% ethanol extract of U. hirsuta was shown to reverse learning and memory dysfunctions induced by d-galactose. Isolated compounds from the plant, 5β-carboxystrictosidine (1) and chlorogenic acid (2), protected PC12 cells against 6-OHDA induced toxicity. For intracellular ROS scavenging, the IC50 values were 24.5 μM for compound 1 and 19.7 μM for compound 2. These compounds reduced intracellular calcium levels with IC50 values of 46.9 μM and 27 μM, respectively. Additionally, compound 1 inhibited caspase 3 and 9 with IC50 values of 25.6 and 24.5 μM, while compound 2 showed values of 19.4 and 16.3 μM.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause significant health complications including epilepsy, fever, gastrointestinal illness, headache, and hypertension. Individuals consuming Uncaria hirsuta may experience sudden neurological issues such as seizures or episodes of epilepsy. Digestive disturbances often manifest as gastrointestinal illness, which can lead to discomfort or nausea. Systemic reactions like fever and persistent headaches are also reported side effects of its use. Furthermore, the plant has been linked to cardiovascular concerns specifically involving the onset of hypertension.
