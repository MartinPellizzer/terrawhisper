Uncaria hirsuta, scientifically known as Uncaria hirsuta, is a prominent medicinal climber belonging to the Rubiaceae family. Commonly referred to as "Hirsute Cat's Claw," this plant is highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and immune-boosting properties, playing a vital role in holistic healing.
