Fritillary, scientifically known as Fritillaria, is a diverse genus of bulbous perennials admired for their striking, bell-shaped flowers. Beyond their ornamental beauty, various species hold significant roles in traditional medicine, particularly in East Asian practices, where they are utilized to treat respiratory ailments and soothe persistent, dry coughs.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Fritillaria, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties which are concentrated within its underground bulbs. These bulbs are composed of fleshy, overlapping scales that serve as the storage organs for the plant's active compounds. In traditional medicine, the therapeutic application typically involves the use of dried bulbs, which are carefully prepared to preserve their efficacy. Once processed, these components are often ground into fine powders to be incorporated into various herbal formulations and treatments.
