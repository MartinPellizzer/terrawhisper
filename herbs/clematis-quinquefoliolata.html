Clematis quinquefoliolata

What's the taxonomical classification of Clematis quinquefoliolata?

Clematis quinquefoliolata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant further falls under the order Ranunculales and is a member of the family Ranunculaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Clematis and the specific species epithet quinquefolioata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Clematis quinquefoliolata has compound leaves composed of five distinct leaflets that are typically ovate to lanceolate in shape with entire or slightly serrated margins. These leaflets are arranged oppositely along the stem and exhibit a smooth or slightly pubescent texture on their surfaces. The plant features slender, twining stems that allow it to climb through surrounding vegetation using its specialized petioles. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the leaf axils, often appearing in shades of white or pale cream. The reproductive structures include central clusters of stamens that are characteristic of the Clematis genus.
