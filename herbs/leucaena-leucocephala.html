Leucaena leucocephala

Jumbay

Leucaena leucocephalha, commonly known as lead tree or river tamarind, is a versatile multipurpose legume. Renowned for its rapid growth, it serves as vital forage, soil improver, and fuelwood. Medicinally, various parts are utilized in traditional practices to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues and skin infections.

  • Scientific name: Leucaena leucocephala
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is scientifically identified by the genus Leucaena and the species leucocephala.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Leucaena leucocephala, is recognized by various local names across different linguistic traditions. In Chinese, it is referred to as 銀合歡 (yínhéhuān), while in Javanese, it is called pethèt. The Nahuatl language uses the name Huāxcuahuitl, and in Quechua, it is known as Yarawisqu. Additionally, in Malay, the plant is commonly called Petai belalang.
