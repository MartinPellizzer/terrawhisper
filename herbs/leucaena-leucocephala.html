Jumbay, scientifically known as Leucaena leucocephalha, is a versatile leguminous tree highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its nutritional density, it is utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. Beyond its medicinal properties, it serves as a vital source of protein for livestock worldwide.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is scientifically identified by the genus Leucaena and the species leucocephala.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as leucaena or river tamarind, possesses various components that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The leaves are frequently studied for their bioactive compounds, often being processed into leaf extracts to harness their therapeutic potential. The nutrient-rich pod serves as a significant source of sustenance and medicine, while the seed gum can be utilized for its thickening and medicinal qualities. Additionally, the roots are sometimes employed in traditional practices to address specific health concerns.
