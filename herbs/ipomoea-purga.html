Ipomoea purga

Ipomoea purga, commonly known as Hawaiian bindweed, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Pacific Islands. Renowned for its potent purgative properties, it has been used traditionally to treat digestive ailments. Today, researchers explore its bioactive compounds for potential applications in pharmacology and holistic wellness treatments.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea purga
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Ipomoea.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Ipomoea purga, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its specific botanical components. The most significant part used for therapeutic purposes is the jalap root, which is harvested and processed to extract potent purgative compounds. This root contains various alkaloids and glycosides that are utilized to stimulate bowel movements, making it a key ingredient in many historical medicinal preparations.
