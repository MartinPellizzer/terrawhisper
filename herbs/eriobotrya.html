Eriobotrya, scientifically known as Eriobotrya japonica, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its medicinal and nutritional properties. Commonly called the loquat, its leaves and fruits are utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering a rich source of vitamins and antioxidants.

Distribution

This plant, known as Eriobotrya, exhibits a specific distribution across various regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing in the Balearic Islands, which are part of the Baleares archipelago. Additionally, its presence is noted in the island of Corse and the coastal regions of France. The species also extends its reach to the territory of Portugal. Finally, its geographical range includes the Mediterranean island of Sardegna.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Eriobotrya japonica or the loquat, utilizes its large, leathery leaf as a primary component in traditional medicinal practices. The evergreen foliage is characterized by its elongated shape and serrated edges, and it is frequently harvested to prepare decoctions or poultices used to treat respiratory ailments such as coughs and asthma.
