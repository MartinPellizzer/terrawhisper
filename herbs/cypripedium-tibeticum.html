Cypripedium tibeticum

Cypripedium tibeticum, commonly known as the Tibetan Lady's Slipper, is a rare orchid species native to high-altitude Himalayan regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it is utilized for its unique bioactive properties. Conservation efforts are vital, as its specialized habitat and slow growth make it highly vulnerable to exploitation.

  • Scientific name: Cypripedium tibeticum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Cypripedium tibeticum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the Orchidaceae family and falls under the genus Cypripedium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several key Asian regions. In China, its presence is noted throughout the North-Central and South-Central territories. It is also prominently distributed within the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. Beyond Chinese borders, the species extends into the East Himalaya region of the Indian Subcontinent. Finally, its geographical reach includes the tropical landscapes of Myanmar within Indo-China.
