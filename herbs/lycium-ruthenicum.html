Black Goji Berry, scientifically known as Lycium ruthenicum, is a rare and potent medicinal fruit prized in traditional wellness practices. Distinguished by its deep purple hue, it boasts an exceptional concentration of anthocyanins. This antioxidant-rich superfruit supports eye health, boosts immunity, and promotes anti-aging benefits through its unique nutrient profile.
