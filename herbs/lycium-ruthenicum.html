Lycium ruthenicum

Lycium ruthenicum, commonly known as a specialized member of the Solanaceae family, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to address various ailments, highlighting its botanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Lycium ruthenicum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales, it is further categorized into the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Lycium as the species Lycium ruthenicum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across both Middle Europe and Central Asia. In Middle Europe, its presence is specifically documented within the borders of Germany. Moving eastward, the species is widely distributed throughout the vast landscapes of Central Asia. Its range includes significant populations found in Kazakhstan, Kirgizstan, and Turkmenistan. Finally, the distribution extends further into the mountainous regions of Tadzhikistan.
