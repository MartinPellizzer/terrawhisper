Aconitum bulleyanum

Aconitum bulleyanum, commonly known as the bullseye monkshood, is a striking perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. While prized for its unique botanical beauty, it contains potent alkaloids. This species requires careful handling, as its medicinal properties must be balanced against its significant toxicity in traditional herbal applications.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum bulleyanum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aconitum bulleyanum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Aconitum.
