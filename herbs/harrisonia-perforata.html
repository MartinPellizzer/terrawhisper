Harrisonia perforata

Harrisonia perforata, commonly known as the "perforated harrisonia," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various African regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Harrisonia perforata
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Harrisonia perforata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Harrisonia.
