Sarcomphalus joazeiro

Sarcomphalus joazeiro, commonly known as Juazeiro, is a resilient evergreen tree native to South America. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional practices to treat skin ailments and oral infections. Its bark and leaves contain potent bioactive compounds, making it a valuable botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Sarcomphalus joazeiro
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Sarcomphalus joazeiro, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling specifically within the order Rosales. Ultimately, its taxonomic lineage places it in the family Rhamnaceae and the genus Sarcomphalus.
