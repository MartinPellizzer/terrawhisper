Magnolia duclouxii

Magnolia duclouxii, commonly known as the Ducloux Magnolia, is a rare and exquisite ornamental species native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its striking, fragrant blooms, this plant holds significant interest in traditional herbalism. Its unique botanical properties offer potential medicinal benefits, making it a subject of scientific curiosity.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia duclouxii
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, it is further organized into the family Magnoliaceae. Ultimately, Magnolia duclouxii is identified by its specific genus, Magnolia.
