Barleria trispinosa, scientifically known as Barleria trispinosa, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to the arid regions of Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots and leaves possess potent bioactive properties, frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation, digestive issues, and skin infections, highlighting its significant ethnobotanical value.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Acanthaceae. Specifically, it is identified by its genus, Barleria.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the Northeast Tropical Africa region. Within this geographical area, it can be found growing in the country of Djibouti. Its range also extends through the nation of Eritrea and into Ethiopia. Additionally, the species is present throughout Somalia. Finally, its distribution encompasses the territories of both Sudan and South Sudan.
