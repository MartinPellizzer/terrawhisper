Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of Western Asia. Its natural range includes the mountainous and arid regions of Afghanistan. It can also be found growing throughout the vast territories of Iran. Furthermore, the species is native to parts of Iraq and the Levant region. Specifically, its distribution extends through Lebanon, Syria, and Palestine.
