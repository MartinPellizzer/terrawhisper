Aristolochia rigida

Aristolochia rigida, commonly known as the stiff aristolochia, is a perennial climbing herb native to subtropical regions. Characterized by its unique, tube-shaped flowers, this medicinal plant has historically been used in traditional practices. However, due to the presence of toxic aristolochic acids, its clinical use is strictly cautioned.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia rigida
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, it is further categorized into the family Aristolochiaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Aristolochia.
