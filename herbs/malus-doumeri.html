What's the taxonomical classification of Malus doumeri?

Malus doumeri belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Rosales. This plant is a member of the family Rosaceae, which characterizes its botanical lineage, and is further defined by its placement within the genus Malus. Ultimately, its specific identity is established by its unique species designation, doumeri.
