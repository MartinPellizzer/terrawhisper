Salvia canariensis, scientifically known as Salvia canariensis, is a striking perennial shrub native to the Canary Islands. Distribution

This plant is an endemic species primarily found within the Macaronesian region. Its natural habitat is specifically restricted to the Canary Islands. Within this archipelago, it occupies unique ecological niches that support its growth. Because of this limited range, the species is considered a local treasure of the island landscapes. Consequently, its entire geographical distribution is centered on these volcanic territories.
