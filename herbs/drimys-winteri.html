Drimys winteri

Winter's bark

Drimys winteri, commonly known as Winter's Bark, is a resilient evergreen tree native to the temperate forests of Chile and Argentina. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, its bark contains high concentrations of quinones, offering powerful antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic effects used extensively in traditional South American healing.

  • Scientific name: Drimys winteri
  • Family: Winteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Caneleales and categorized under the family Winteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Drimys and the specific species Drimys winteri.
