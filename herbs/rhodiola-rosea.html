Rhodiola rosea

Golden root

Rhodiola rosea, commonly known as Roseroot or Golden Root, is a powerful adaptogen native to cold, mountainous regions. Renowned for its ability to enhance stress resilience, this medicinal herb supports mental clarity, combats fatigue, and balances hormonal function, making it a vital botanical tool for physical and emotional wellness.

  • Scientific name: Rhodiola rosea
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Saxifragales, specifically within the family Crassulaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Rhodiola.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Rhodiola rosea, is recognized by various common names across different linguistic regions. In Simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 蔷薇红景天 (zh-hans) or 蔷薇红景天 (zh-cn), while in Traditional Chinese contexts, it is commonly called 紅景天 (zh-hant) and 紅景天 (zh-hk). Additionally, in Singapore, the name used is 红景天 (zh-sg).

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within Northern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing in the rugged landscapes of Finland. Its presence extends to the remote islands of Føroyaar and Iceland. Additionally, the species is native to the territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Together, these locations represent the key northern habitats for this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Arctic root, derives its primary medicinal potency from its specialized underground part. Specifically, the most bioactive components are concentrated within the thick, fleshy rhizomes that grow beneath the soil surface. While often referred to simply as the root in herbal literature, the medicinal extract is technically harvested from these creeping rhizomes rather than a singular tap root. Therefore, when preparing herbal remedies, it is the robust rhizome structure that serves as the essential source of its adaptogenic properties.
