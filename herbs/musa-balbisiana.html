Musa balbisiana

Musa balbisiana, commonly known as the wild banana, is a resilient species integral to tropical ecosystems. Valued for its starch-rich fruit and versatile leaves, it serves as a vital genetic ancestor for cultivated varieties. Beyond nutrition, its medicinal properties offer significant potential in traditional healing and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Musa balbisiana
  • Family: Musaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Musa balbisiana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Zingiberales and the family Musaecae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Musa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across both Africa and Asia. In the African continent, it can be found within the West-Central Tropical Africa region, specifically around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its presence in China is extensive, covering the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Additionally, the species reaches into the high-altitude areas of Tibet. These diverse locations highlight the broad ecological range inhabited by Musa balbisiana.
