Saussurea polycephala

What's the taxonomical classification of Saussurea polycephala?

Saussurea polycephala belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. As a member of the family Asteraceae, this plant is placed within the genus Saussurea, specifically identified by its species name, polycephala.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Saussurea polycephala has a perennial rosette of thick, leathery, oblong leaves that are often covered in dense, white, woolly hairs to protect against intense UV radiation. These basal leaves are arranged in a compact cluster, helping the plant conserve moisture and heat in its high-altitude alpine habitat. The flowering stem emerges from the center of the rosette, eventually bearing a large, head-like inflorescence composed of numerous small, yellowish florets. Each individual flower is tucked within protective bracts, creating a textured, thistle-like appearance typical of the genus. The overall structure is highly adapted to survive extreme temperature fluctuations and thin atmospheric conditions.
