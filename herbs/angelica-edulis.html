Angelica edulis

Angelica eduulis, commonly known as a precious medicinal herb, is a remarkable plant celebrated for its diverse therapeutic properties. Found in specific ecological niches, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, offering significant bioactive compounds. This introduction explores its botanical characteristics, traditional uses, and growing importance in pharmacology.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, falling into the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Angelica and the specific species name Angelica eduulis.
