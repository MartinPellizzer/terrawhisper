Oenothera rosea

Oenothera rosea, commonly known as Rosy Evening Primrose, is a resilient perennial herb celebrated for its delicate, pink-hued blooms. Native to North America, this botanical treasure is valued in traditional medicine for its soothing properties. Its unique ability to thrive in diverse environments makes it a fascinating subject of study.

  • Scientific name: Oenothera rosea
  • Family: Onagraceae
  • Native range: Europe
