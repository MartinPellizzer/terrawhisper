Voacanga africana

Voacanga africana, commonly known as the African Voacança, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments. Modern research explores its bioactive compounds, highlighting its potential in developing new treatments for complex human diseases.

  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Within this region, its presence is documented in the nation of Benin. It can also be found growing within the borders of Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the country of Gambia. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the territory of Ghana and the coastal area of Guinea-Bissau.
