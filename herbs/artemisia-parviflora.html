Artemisia parviflora

Artemisia parviflora, commonly known as the small-flowered wormwood, is a resilient perennial herb native to various regions. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, this medicinal plant is valued for its unique phytochemical profile. Traditionally, it has been utilized in folk medicine to treat diverse ailments through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia parviflora
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, Artemisia parviflora is identified by its genus, Artemisia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning the South-Central and North-Central territories. Its distribution extends further into the high-altitude landscapes of Qinghai and Tibet. Additionally, the species reaches into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the area of Assam. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varied regional environments.
