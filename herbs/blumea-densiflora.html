Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across parts of Asia, primarily concentrated within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing in the diverse terrains of Assam and throughout the East Himalaya region. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the neighboring countries of Bangladesh and Nepal. Beyond the Indian Subcontinent, the species is also documented in the South-Central regions of China. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's preference for specific subtropical and montane environments.
