The life history of Anthemis auriculata is characterized by its classification as a hemicryptophyte, a life form where the perennating buds are located at the soil surface or just beneath it, protected by the leaf litter or soil layer during unfavorable seasons. Within the spectrum of plant growth forms, it functions specifically as a hemicryptophyte, distinguishing it from other life forms such as phanerophytes, chamaephytes, cryptophytes, or therophytes. This biological strategy allows the plant to persist through seasonal environmental shifts by maintaining its vital structures at the ground level, enabling it to re-emerge and complete its reproductive cycle once favorable conditions return.
