Camphora longepaniculata

Camphora longepaniculata, commonly known as the "Long-leafed Camphor" plant, is a significant medicinal species valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its potent aromatic properties, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. This plant plays a crucial role in ethnobotany, offering therapeutic benefits for skin and respiratory health.

  • Scientific name: Camphora longepaniculata
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae, it is classified under the genus Camphora. Specifically, the species is identified as Camphora longepaniculata.
