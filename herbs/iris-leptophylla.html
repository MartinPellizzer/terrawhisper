Iris leptophylla

What's the taxonomical classification of Iris leptophylla?

Iris leptophylla belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asparagales. This plant is a member of the family Iridaceae, which contains the genus Iris, and is specifically identified by its unique species designation, leptophylla.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Iris leptophylla has narrow, linear leaves that are significantly thinner than those of many other iris species, typically measuring only a few millimeters in width. These slender green leaves emerge from a rhizomatous base and grow in a fan-like arrangement. The plant produces delicate flowers characterized by three upright standards and three drooping falls, which often display intricate veining and color patterns. The reproductive structures are centered within the perianth tube, supporting the development of seed capsules after pollination.
