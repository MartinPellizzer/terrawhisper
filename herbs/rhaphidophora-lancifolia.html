What's the taxonomical classification of Rhaphidophora lancifolia?

Rhaphidophora lancifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Alismatales. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the family Araceae, placing it within the genus Rhaphidophora, and it is ultimately identified by its specific species name, lancifolia.
