Smilax bockii

Smilax bockii, commonly known as Bocage's Sarsaparilla, is a perennial climbing vine native to North America. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this plant features woody stems and distinctive thorny leaves. Historically used to support vitality and skin health, it remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies and natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Smilax bockii
  • Family: Smilacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae and order Liliales, it is a member of the Smilacaceae family. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Smilax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In China, it is found throughout the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Its range extends further into Eastern Asia, specifically covering the Nansei-shoto islands. Additionally, the species is documented as growing in Taiwan. Collectively, these areas define the natural geographical footprint of Smilax bockii.
