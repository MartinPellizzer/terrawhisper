Smilax bockii, scientifically known as Smilax bockii, is a perennial climbing vine native to North America. Often referred to as Greenbrier, this robust plant features thorny stems and serrated leaves. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it is recognized for its various bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic properties in herbalism.
