Millettia caerulea

Millettia caerulea, commonly known as the Blue Milletia, is a striking tropical shrub celebrated for its vibrant, azure blooms. Native to various forested regions, this botanical gem is highly valued in traditional medicine and ornamental gardening, offering both aesthetic elegance and unique bioactive properties for holistic wellness and ecological balance.

  • Scientific name: Millettia caerulea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Fabales, and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Millettia.

Common Names

This plant, Millettia caerulea, is known by various names depending on the regional language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Blue Millettia, while in Hindi, it is called Neel Jati. In the local context of West Bengal, it is known as Nil Millettia, and in the Odia language, it is referred to as Nila Millettia.
