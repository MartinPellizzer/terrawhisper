Dioscorea panthaica

Dioscorea pantthaica, commonly known as a medicinal yam, is a significant plant species valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Found in tropical regions, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to address various ailments. This study explores its phytochemical composition, potential pharmacological benefits, and its growing importance in ethnopharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
