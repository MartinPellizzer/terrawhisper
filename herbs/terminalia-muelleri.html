Terminalia muelleri

Terminalia muelleri, commonly known as the Mueller's Terminalia, is a significant tropical tree valued for its medicinal and economic properties. Native to Southeast Asia, its bark, leaves, and roots are utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising bioactive compounds for modern pharmacological research and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia muelleri
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Terminalia.
