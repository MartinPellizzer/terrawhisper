Triclisia dictyophylla

Triclisia dictyophylla, commonly known as a significant medicinal climber, is a species valued in traditional herbal medicine. Found primarily in tropical forest ecosystems, it contains unique bioactive compounds. Researchers study this plant for its potential therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting overall holistic wellness through natural phytochemistry.

  • Scientific name: Triclisia dictyophylla
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Triclisia dictyophylla, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Menispermaceae and is situated within the genus Triclisia.
