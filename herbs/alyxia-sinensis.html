Bead Vine, scientifically known as Alyxia sinensis, is a climbing shrub native to Southeast Asia, celebrated for its striking ornamental berries. Beyond its beauty, this medicinal plant holds significant value in traditional practices, where its roots and bark are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties and bioactive compounds.
