Bead Vine, scientifically known as Alyxia sinensis, is a climbing shrub native to Southeast Asia, celebrated for its striking ornamental berries. Beyond its beauty, this medicinal plant holds significant value in traditional practices, where its roots and bark are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties and bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions across East Asia. Within China, its presence is notably recorded throughout the South-Central territories. It can also be found growing in the tropical environment of Hainan. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the Southeast regions of the Chinese mainland. Finally, the species is documented to inhabit the island of Taiwan.

Plant Parts

This plant, Alyxia sinensis, features woody stems that serve as a vital component in its medicinal application. These stems are characterized by their climbing or scandent growth habit, often functioning as lianas that wrap around other vegetation to reach sunlight. In traditional pharmacological practices, the bark and the woody material of the stems are harvested to extract various bioactive compounds used in various herbal remedies.
