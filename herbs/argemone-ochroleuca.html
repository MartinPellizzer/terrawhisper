Argemone ochroleuca

Argemone ochroleuca, commonly known as Yellow Prickly Poppy, is a distinctive medicinal plant found across tropical regions. Characterized by its bright yellow blooms and prickly leaves, it contains bioactive alkaloids. While traditionally utilized in folk medicine for various ailments, its potent chemical properties require careful scientific study and handling.

  • Scientific name: Argemone ochroleuca
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Argemone.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across two distinct major regions. Within the Macaronesia archipelago, its presence is documented in both the Canary Islands and Madeira. Moving toward the African continent, the species is widely distributed throughout Southern Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Botswana and the Cape Provinces. Furthermore, its natural habitat extends into the mountainous terrain of Lesotho.
