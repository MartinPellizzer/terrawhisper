Veronica austriaca jacquinii

Veronica austriaca subsp. jacquinii, commonly known as a subspecies of speedwell, is a resilient medicinal herb found in specialized habitats. Traditionally valued in ethnobotany, it possesses unique bioactive properties. This plant is studied for its potential therapeutic applications, offering promising insights into natural pharmacology and holistic wellness practices.

  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Plantaginaceae, it is classified under the genus Veronica. Specifically, the medicinal taxon is identified as Veronica austriaca subsp. jacquinii.
