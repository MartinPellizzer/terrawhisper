Vitis ficifolia

Vitis ficifolia, commonly known as the Figoon grape, is a unique evergreen climber native to Australia. Renowned for its luscious, fig-like fruit, this plant offers more than just sweetness. It holds significant medicinal potential, containing bioactive compounds valued for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and health-promoting properties in traditional wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Vitis ficifolia
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Vitis ficifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Vitales. Finally, it falls into the family Vitaceae and is situated within the genus Vitis.
