Eriobotrya japonica

Eriobotrya japonica, commonly known as the loquat, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Its leaves and fruits possess significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antibacterial properties. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and digestive issues, this resilient evergreen offers a rich source of vitamins and therapeutic compounds.

  • Scientific name: Eriobotrya japonica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Eriobotrya japonica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Eriobotrya.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Eriobotrya japonica, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing within the Balearic Islands, which are part of the Baleares archipelago. Its presence is also noted in the French territories and along the coastlines of Portugal. Additionally, this species inhabits the island of Corse. Finally, the plant is documented to grow in the Mediterranean region of Sardegna.
