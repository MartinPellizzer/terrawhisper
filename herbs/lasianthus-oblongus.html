Lasianthus oblongus

Lasianthus oblongus, commonly known as a member of the Rubiaceae family, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for developing new therapeutic treatments in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Lasianthus oblongus
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Rubiaceae. It is further identified by the genus Lasianthus, specifically as the species Lasianthus oblongus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lasianthus oblongus, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is typically known as the oblong lasianthus, while in Hindi, it is called kshudrasphota. Within regional Indian languages, it is identified as thirumathi in Tamil and munda in Malayalam.
