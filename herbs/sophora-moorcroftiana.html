Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it is specifically found inhabiting the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its presence is also widespread throughout the Indian Subcontinent, where it thrives in the East Himalaya. Within this subcontinent, the species is documented across the diverse landscapes of India and Nepal. Additionally, the plant's range extends into the mountainous terrains of Pakistan.
