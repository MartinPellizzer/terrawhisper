Brown hemp, scientifically known as Crotalaria juncea, is a versatile annual legume prized for its rapid growth and multipurpose utility. Beyond its significant role in sustainable fiber production for textiles and paper, this resilient plant serves as an essential green manure, enhancing soil fertility through vital nitrogen fixation processes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple continents and climatic zones. In Eastern Europe, its presence can be documented within the territory of Ukraine. Moving toward the African continent, it is found across Northern Africa, specifically in Libya. The species also thrives in West Tropical Africa, where it is native to Burkina. Furthermore, its range extends to other West Tropical African nations such as Ghana and Guinea-Bissau.
