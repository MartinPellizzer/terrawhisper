Crotalaria juncea

Brown hemp

Crotalaria juncea, commonly known as sunn hemp, is a versatile legume celebrated for its multifaceted medicinal and agricultural benefits. Rich in alkaloids and essential nutrients, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Beyond medicine, its rapid growth makes it an exceptional green manure crop.

  • Scientific name: Crotalaria juncea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Crotalaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple continents and climatic zones. In Eastern Europe, its presence can be documented within the territory of Ukraine. Moving toward the African continent, it is found across Northern Africa, specifically in Libya. The species also thrives in West Tropical Africa, where it is native to Burkina. Furthermore, its range extends to other West Tropical African nations such as Ghana and Guinea-Bissau.
