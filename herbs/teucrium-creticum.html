Teucrium creticum

Teucrium creticum, commonly known as Cretan Germander, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this plant is characterized by its small, aromatic leaves and delicate flowers. Traditionally, it has been utilized in herbal medicine for its various therapeutic and restorative properties.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
