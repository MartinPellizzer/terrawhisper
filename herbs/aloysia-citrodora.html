Aloysia citrodora

Aloysiia citrodora, commonly known as lemon verbena, is a fragrant perennial shrub native to South America. Renowned for its bright, citrusy aroma, this medicinal herb is widely used in herbal teas to promote relaxation, aid digestion, and reduce stress, offering both therapeutic benefits and a refreshing sensory experience.

  • Scientific name: Aloysia citrodora
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Verbenaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed within the genus Aloysiia.
