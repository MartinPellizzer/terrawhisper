Aloysiia citrodora, scientifically known as Aloysiia citrodora, is a fragrant shrub native to South America, widely celebrated for its lemon-scented leaves. Often called lemon verbena, this medicinal plant is prized in herbalism for its calming properties, digestive support, and ability to alleviate stress through its refreshing, citrusy aroma.
