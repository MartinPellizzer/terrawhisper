Buddleja lindleyana

Buddleja lindleyana, commonly known as the Lindley's Butterfly Bush, is a resilient shrub valued for its striking floral displays. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this species holds significant medicinal potential. Research explores its bioactive compounds, which may offer therapeutic properties, highlighting its importance in traditional healing and modern botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Buddleja lindleyana
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Buddleja lindleyana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Scrophulariaceae and the genus Buddleja.
