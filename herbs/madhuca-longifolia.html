Madhuca, scientifically known as Madhuca longifolia, is a versatile evergreen tree cherished in traditional medicine. Commonly found in tropical regions, its various parts—including bark, leaves, and flowers—possess significant therapeutic properties. It is widely utilized for treating skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, making it a botanical treasure.
