Curculigo latifolia

Curculigo latifolia, commonly known as Broad-leaved Curculigo, is a significant medicinal herb belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Native to various tropical regions, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly its ability to act as a potent tonic, rejuvenator, and anti-inflammatory agent.

  • Scientific name: Curculigo latifolia
  • Family: Hypoxidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asparagales and the family Hypoxidaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Curculigo as Curculigo latifolia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is documented in both the Hainan province and the Southeast regions of the country. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in Bangladesh. The species also extends into the Indo-China area, specifically inhabiting the Andaman Islands. Furthermore, its distribution includes the country of Cambodia.
