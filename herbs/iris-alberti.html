Iris alberti

Iris alberti, commonly known as the Albert's Iris, is a charming perennial native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its delicate, slender leaves and elegant violet blooms, this plant thrives in rocky, sunny habitats. While primarily valued for its ornamental beauty, it holds a unique place in botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Iris alberti
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Asparagales and placed in the family Iridaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Iris.
