Phanera roxburghiana

Phanera roxburghiana, commonly known as the Roxburgh Papilionaceous plant, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South Asia. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin disorders to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Phanera roxburghiana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized into the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phanera.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing naturally throughout the diverse landscapes of India. The species also extends its range into the neighboring territories of Pakistan and Nepal. Furthermore, it is well-documented in the lush environments of Bangladesh. Finally, its geographical presence includes the specific elevations of the East Himalaya region.
