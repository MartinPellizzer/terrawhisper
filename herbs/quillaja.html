Quillaja

Quillaja, commonly known as soapbark, is a remarkable medicinal tree native to South America. Renowned for its high saponin content, it has been utilized traditionally for centuries. Today, its unique chemical properties are valued in pharmacology and biotechnology for their potent surfactant, anti-inflammatory, and immune-boosting capabilities in modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Quillaja
  • Family: Quillajaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Fabales and the family Quillajaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal specimen is identified by the genus Quillaja.
