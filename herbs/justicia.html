Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans multiple continents and oceanic regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence can be identified within the island of Sicilia. Moving toward the African mainland, it is also found across Northern Africa, specifically in Algeria. The distribution extends into the Macaronesian islands, encompassing both the Azores and the Canary Islands. Finally, its reach continues into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Benin.
