Aquilaria yunnanensis

Aquilaria yunnanensis, commonly known as Yunnan agarwood, is a precious medicinal plant native to high-altitude forests. Renowned for its fragrant, resinous heartwood, it is highly valued in traditional medicine for its calming, analgesic, and antiseptic properties, serving as a cornerstone in holistic healing and aromatic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Aquilaria yunnanensis
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Aquilaria yunnanensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
