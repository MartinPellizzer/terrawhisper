Plant Parts

This plant, Aquilaria yunnanensis, is a species highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for the substances it produces within its tissues. While various parts of the tree may be utilized, the stem bark serves as a critical component in its medicinal profile. When the tree becomes stressed or infected, it produces a fragrant, resinous substance that accumulates within the bark and wood, which is often harvested for its therapeutic properties. The stem bark is frequently processed to extract these essential compounds, which are used in various pharmacological applications to treat diverse ailments.
