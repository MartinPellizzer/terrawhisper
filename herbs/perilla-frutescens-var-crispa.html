Shiso, scientifically known as Perilla frutescens var. crispa, is a versatile aromatic herb widely used in East Asian cuisine and traditional medicine. Renowned for its unique, peppery flavor, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including potent anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and antioxidant properties, making it a valuable botanical resource.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning both Eastern Asia and parts of North America. In its native Eastern Asian regions, it can be found growing in Japan and Korea. Its presence extends into the North-Central United States, specifically within the states of Illinois and Missouri. Additionally, the species has established itself in the Northeastern United States, notably in Connecticut. Together, these locations illustrate the wide range inhabited by this specific variety.
