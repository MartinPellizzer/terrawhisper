Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Perilla frutescens var. crispa, possesses distinct morphological features across its various components. The stems are typically erect and often exhibit a purplish tint, providing a sturdy structure for the plant's growth. The leaves are the most prominent feature, characterized by their serrated edges and deeply lobed shape, which can vary in color from green to deep purple depending on the cultivar. Each individual leaf contains a high concentration of aromatic essential oils, which are central to its medicinal properties. Additionally, the plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that grow in clusters, completing its reproductive cycle and adding to its complex botanical profile.
