Perilla frutescens var. crispa

Shiso

Periella frutescens var. crispa, commonly known as shiso or perilla, is a versatile medicinal herb celebrated in East Asian traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This nutrient-dense plant is widely utilized to support digestive health and enhance overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Perilla frutescens var. crispa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Perilla frutescens var. crispa, possesses distinct morphological features across its various components. The leaves are the most prominent feature, characterized by their serrated edges and deeply lobed shape, which can vary in color from green to deep purple depending on the cultivar. Each individual leaf contains a high concentration of aromatic essential oils, which are central to its medicinal properties.
