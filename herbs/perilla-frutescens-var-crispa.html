Perilla frutescens var. crispa

Periella frutescens var. crispa, commonly known as shiso or perilla, is a versatile medicinal herb celebrated in East Asian traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This nutrient-dense plant is widely utilized to support digestive health and enhance overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Perilla frutescens var. crispa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Perilla. Specifically, this variety is identified as Perilla frutescens var. crispa.
