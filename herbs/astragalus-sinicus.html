Chinese milk-vetch, scientifically known as Astragalus siniicus, is a vital medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its immune-boosting and adaptogenic properties, this perennial legume is primarily used to enhance vitality, support kidney function, and promote longevity through its potent, bioactive polysaccharides and flavonoids.
