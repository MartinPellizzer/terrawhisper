Plantago afra subsp. afra

Plantago afra subsp. afra, commonly known as African Plantain, is a versatile medicinal herb native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and healing properties, it has been used traditionally for centuries to treat skin irritations, wounds, and digestive ailments, serving as a cornerstone in indigenous pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Plantago afra subsp. afra
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, scientifically known as Plantago afra subsp. afra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaea. Finally, it is situated within the genus Plantago.

Common Names

This plant, Plantago afra subsp. afra, is known by a variety of local names across different linguistic regions. In English, it is commonly referred to as African plantain, while in Afrikaans, it is known as Afrikaanse plantain. Within South Africa, it holds significant traditional importance and is called kwaaihoek in isiXhosa and is also referred to as isizitshalo in isiZulu.
