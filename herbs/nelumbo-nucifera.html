Nelumbo nucifera

Nelumbo nucifera, commonly known as the sacred lotus, is a remarkable perennial aquatic plant revered in many cultures. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. Its roots, seeds, and leaves are utilized for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and calming properties to support overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Nelumbo nucifera
  • Family: Nelumbonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Proteales, it is classified under the family Nelumbonaceae. Finally, Nelumbo nucifera is identified by its genus, Nelumbo.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across multiple continents and distinct climate zones. In Southeastern Europe, its presence can be documented in both Italy and Romania. Moving toward Eastern Europe, the species is found within the territories of Ukraine and South European Russia. Additionally, the distribution extends to West Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Benin. This wide-reaching spread highlights the plant's ability to adapt to various environmental conditions.
