What's the taxonomical classification of Swertia delavayi?

Swertia delaavayi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage leading to the order Gentianaales. Within this order, it is placed in the family Gentianaceae, falling under the genus Swertia, and is specifically identified by its species name, delaayi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Swertia delavayi has opposite, decussate leaves that are ovate to elliptic in shape with entire margins and acute to acuminate apices. The plant typically presents as a small shrub or subshrub with woody stems that branch extensively. Its flowers are small, actinomorphic, and often white or pale pink, emerging from the axils of the foliage. The fruit consists of woody, dehiscent capsules that contain numerous small seeds. These morphological features are characteristic of the Gentianaceae family to which this species belongs.
