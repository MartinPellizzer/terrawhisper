Albizia adianthifolia

Albizia adianthifolia, commonly known as the White Siris, is a versatile medicinal tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections and inflammation to digestive issues and respiratory distress.

  • Scientific name: Albizia adianthifolia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it falls under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically categorized within the genus Albizia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the West Tropical Africa region. Its documented geographical range includes the nation of Benin. It is also widely distributed within the territory of Gambia.
