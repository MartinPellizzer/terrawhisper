Calocedrus formosana

Calocedrus formosana, commonly known as the Formosan Cedar, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to subtropical forest regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to address various ailments. Exploring its therapeutic potential offers promising insights into modern pharmacology and natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Calocedrus formosana
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales, it is classified under the family Cupressaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Calocedrus.

Distribution

This plant is a specialized species found within the specific geographic boundaries of Eastern Asia. Its natural habitat is strictly limited to the island of Taiwan. Within this region, it typically thrives in high-altitude montane environments. Because of this narrow range, the species is considered an endemic treasure of the Taiwanese landscape. Consequently, its survival is closely tied to the unique ecological conditions found only in this part of the world.
