Melon plant, scientifically known as Cucumis melo, is a versatile member of the Cucurbitaceae family celebrated for its nutritional and therapeutic properties. Beyond its delicious fruit, this plant offers significant medicinal benefits, including antioxidant support, skin healing, and anti-inflammatory effects, making it a vital resource in traditional herbal medicine.

Ecology
