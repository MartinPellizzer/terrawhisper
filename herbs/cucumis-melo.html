Cucumis melo

Melon plant

Cucumis melo, commonly known as melon, is a versatile plant celebrated for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants, it supports digestive health, skin vitality, and immune function. Beyond its culinary uses, various extracts are utilized in traditional medicine to combat inflammation and oxidative stress.

  • Scientific name: Cucumis melo
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cucurbitaleales and the family Cucurbitaceae, it is classified under the genus Cucumis.
