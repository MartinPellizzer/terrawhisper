Teijsmanniodendron hollrungii

Teijsmanniodendron hollrungii, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in Southeast Asian traditional medicine, holds great botanical interest. Native to tropical rainforests, this species is valued for its diverse pharmacological properties. Researchers continue to explore its bioactive compounds, seeking potential applications in treating various ailments and developing new therapeutics.

  • Scientific name: Teijsmanniodendron hollrungii
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Teijsmanniodendron.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the diverse regions of Malesia. Within this expansive area, it can be found growing in the tropical landscapes of Borneo. Its range also extends across the peninsula of Malaya and into the island of Sulawesi. Furthermore, the species is present in the Maluku archipelago. Finally, its geographical reach includes the islands of the Philippines.
