Ivory Coast raffia palm, scientifically known as Raphia hookeri, is a versatile medicinal plant native to West African tropical forests. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and digestive issues. Its leaves and stems provide essential bioactive compounds used in herbal remedies.
