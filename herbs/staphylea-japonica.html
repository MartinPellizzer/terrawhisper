Staphylea japonica

Staphylea japonica, commonly known as the Japanese bladdernut, is a deciduous shrub valued for its ornamental beauty and traditional medicinal properties. Native to East Asia, its distinctive inflated seed capsules are iconic. Historically, various parts of the plant have been utilized in folk medicine for their unique therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Staphylea japonica
  • Family: Staphyleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Crossosomatales. Finally, it is a member of the family Staphyleaceae and falls under the genus Staphylea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central and North-Central provinces. Its presence also extends to the southeastern coastal areas of China and the tropical island of Hainan. Furthermore, the species is native to the islands of Japan within Eastern Asia. Collectively, these locations define the specific natural habitat where this medicinal species thrives.
