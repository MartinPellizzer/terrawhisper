Galium

Bedstraw

Galium, commonly known as bedstraw, is a versatile genus of flowering plants spanning various medicinal and ecological roles. Renowned for its unique, whorled leaves, certain species offer traditional therapeutic benefits, including skin soothing and diuretic properties. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for exploring its diverse ethnobotanical applications.

  • Scientific name: Galium
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family. Its taxonomic classification concludes with the genus Galium.
