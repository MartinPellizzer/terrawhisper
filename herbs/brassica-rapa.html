Brassica rapa

Fast plants

Brassica rapa, commonly known as field mustard or turnip, is a versatile plant valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in glucosinolates and antioxidants, it supports detoxification and immune function. Traditionally used to treat respiratory issues and inflammation, this resilient species remains a vital component in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Brassica rapa
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales, falling into the family Brassicaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Brassica.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Brassica rapa, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Norwegian, it is called bondekål (nb), while in German, it is known as Rübsen (de), a name also used in Switzerland as Rübsen (de-ch). In Czech, the plant is called brukev řepák (cs), and in Azerbaijani, it is identified as Şalğam-turp (az).

Its presence extends further north to include the territories of Finland and the Faroe Islands. Additionally, the species is well-documented across the islands of Great Britain. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant reaches as far as Ireland.
