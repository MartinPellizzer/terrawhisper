Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across the South American continent. In Western South America, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Peru. The species is widely distributed throughout Brazil, spanning the Northern, Northeastern, and Southeastern regions. Additionally, it can be found extending into the Southern part of the continent in Paraguay. This broad geographical footprint highlights its adaptability to various regional environments.
