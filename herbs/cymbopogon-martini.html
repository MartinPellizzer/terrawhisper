Cymbopogon martinii, scientifically known as Cymbopogon martini, is a highly aromatic perennial grass belonging to the Poaceae family. Renowned for its intense lemony scent, this essential oil-bearing plant is widely cultivated for its therapeutic properties, offering significant antiseptic, antimicrobial, and calming benefits in traditional and modern herbal medicine.
