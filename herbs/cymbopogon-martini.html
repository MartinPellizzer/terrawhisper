Cymbopogon martinii, scientifically known as Cymbopogon martini, is a highly aromatic perennial grass belonging to the Poaceae family. Renowned for its intense lemony scent, this essential oil-bearing plant is widely cultivated for its therapeutic properties, offering significant antiseptic, antimicrobial, and calming benefits in traditional and modern herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cymbopogon martinii, is a highly valued medicinal grass known primarily for its fragrant properties. While the roots are part of the plant's structural foundation, the most significant parts for therapeutic use are the long, slender leaves. These leaves are the primary source from which various oils are extracted through distillation processes. Specifically, the plant is harvested to produce high-quality essential oils, which contain concentrated bioactive compounds. The most sought-after component is its essential oil, often referred to as citronella oil, which is widely used in aromatherapy and traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and calming effects.
