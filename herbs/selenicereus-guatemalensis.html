The morphology of Selenicereus guatemalensis is characterized by a growth habit that is primarily terrestrial, though it can transition through semiaquatic environments depending on localized moisture levels. Furthermore, while it exhibits certain climbing tendencies, it does not function as an obligatory epiphyte, maintaining a primarily terrestrial existence. Its structural composition is defined by non-woody tissue, which contributes to its flexible and succulent morphology.
