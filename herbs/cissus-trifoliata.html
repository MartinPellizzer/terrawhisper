Cissus trifoliata

Cissus trifoliata, commonly known as the three-leafed creeper, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its climbing habit and distinct foliage, it possesses significant bioactive properties. This botanical specimen is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and wound-healing capabilities in various herbal remedies worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Cissus trifoliata
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Northern America

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range across several regions of the United States. In the North-Central United States, it can be found growing in Kansas, Missouri, and Oklahoma.
