Limnophila aromatica

Rice paddy herb

Limnophila aromatica, commonly known as aromatic water-lily, is a unique aquatic medicinal plant valued for its potent essential oils. Renowned for its calming properties, it is traditionally used in herbal remedies to alleviate stress and soothe digestive discomfort, offering a fragrant, natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Limnophila aromatica
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Limnophila.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions in Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the Hainan province as well as throughout China Southeast. The species also extends into Japan, which is a significant part of its eastern range. Furthermore, it can be found across the Korean peninsula. Finally, its geographical reach includes the Nansei-shoto islands.
