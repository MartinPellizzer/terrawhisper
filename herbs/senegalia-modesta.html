Senegalia modesta

Senegalia modesta, commonly known as the "Desert Thorn," is a resilient deciduous tree native to the arid regions of Africa. Valued in traditional medicine, its bark, leaves, and roots possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering therapeutic potential for treating various ailments in diverse indigenous communities.
