Illicium griffithii

Illicium griffithii, commonly known as Griffith's star anise, is a distinctive evergreen shrub prized in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, its aromatic fruits and leaves contain bioactive compounds with potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This botanical species serves as a vital resource for natural healing and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Illicium griffithii
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification
