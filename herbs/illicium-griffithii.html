Himalayan star anise, scientifically known as Illicium griffithii, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the eastern Himalayas. Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Illicium griffithii, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties which are concentrated in its unique fruit. The fruit is a woody, star-shaped aggregate of follicles that serves as the most significant part of the plant for traditional and pharmacological applications. Within these star-shaped structures, essential oils are stored, making the fruit a vital source of bioactive compounds used in various therapeutic preparations.
