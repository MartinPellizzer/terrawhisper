What's the taxonomical classification of Desmodium zonatum?

Desmodium zonatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae, which characterizes it as a member of the legume group. Finally, it is placed within the genus Desmodium, specifically identified by its unique species name, zonatum.
