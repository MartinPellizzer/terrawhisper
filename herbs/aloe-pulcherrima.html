Aloe pulcherrima

Aloe pulcherrima, commonly known as the beautiful aloe, is a striking succulent prized for its ornamental appeal. Featuring distinctive, brightly colored bracts and architectural foliage, this plant thrives in arid environments. Beyond its aesthetic value, it holds significant botanical interest and potential medicinal applications within traditional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Aloe pulcherrima
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Asphodelaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aloe.

Distribution

This plant is primarily characterized by a very limited and specific range within the wild. Its known geographical distribution is centered in the Northeast Tropical Africa region. Within this broad area, the species is specifically documented to occur in Ethiopia. Because of this localized presence, it is considered a geographically restricted succulent. Consequently, its natural habitat is confined to these particular environmental conditions in the Ethiopian landscape.
