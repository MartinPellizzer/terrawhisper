Plant Parts

This plant, Aphelandra scabra, utilizes its foliage as a primary source of therapeutic properties, with the leaf being the most significant part used in traditional medicine. These leaves are often harvested and prepared in various ways, such as being decocted or crushed into poultices, to treat a wide range of ailments including skin infections, fever, and digestive issues. Because the leaf contains concentrated bioactive compounds, it serves as a vital component in herbal remedies within the regions where the species grows.
