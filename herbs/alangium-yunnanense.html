Alangium yunnanense

What's the taxonomical classification of Alangium yunnanense?

Aliangium yunnanense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Cornales. This plant is further categorized under the family Cornaceae, which defines its botanical characteristics. At the most specific levels of its classification, it is identified by the genus Aliangium and the specific epithet yunnanense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Alangium yunnanense has a perennial growth habit characterized by woody rhizomes and long, slender stems that often reach heights of up to 1 meter. The leaves are arranged in a pseudo-whorl at the apex of the stem and possess an elliptical to lanceolate shape with entire margins. Small, fragrant, white flowers emerge from the leaf axils in a branched inflorescence. These blossoms typically feature five petals and are accompanied by a tubular calyx. The plant produces small, fleshy fruits that serve as a key distinguishing feature of its reproductive morphology.
