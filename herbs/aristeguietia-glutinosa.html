Aristeguietia glutinosa

Aristeguietia glutinosa, commonly known as the sticky-leaved plant, is a medicinal herb valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its unique adhesive foliage, this species is studied for its bioactive compounds. It plays a significant role in folk remedies, offering potential therapeutic benefits for various ailments through its natural properties.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
