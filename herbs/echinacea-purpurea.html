Echinacea purpurea

Echinacea purpurea, commonly known as purple coneflower, is a resilient perennial herb celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Native to North American prairies, this vibrant flowering plant is widely utilized in herbal medicine to bolster the immune system, reduce inflammation, and help the body combat common respiratory infections naturally.

  • Scientific name: Echinacea purpurea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified in the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific biological identity is defined by the genus Echinacea.
