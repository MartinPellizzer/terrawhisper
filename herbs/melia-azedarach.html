Melia azedarach

Persian lilac

Melia azedarach, commonly known as Chinaberry or Persian Lilac, is a versatile deciduous tree prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it offers therapeutic potential in treating skin conditions, parasitic infections, and inflammation. While culturally significant, its potent properties require careful handling due to inherent toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Melia azedarach
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Meliaceae and falls under the genus Melia.
