Gymnanthemum amygdalinum

Gymnanthemum amygdalinum, commonly known as Bitter Leaf, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Africa. Renowned for its profound therapeutic properties, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to manage diabetes, enhance digestion, and boost immunity. This potent herb offers a natural approach to supporting overall metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Gymnanthemum amygdalinum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Gymnanthemum.

Distribution

This plant, Gymnanthemum amygdalinum, is primarily found within the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its natural habitat extends across several key nations, including Benin and Burkina Faso. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Guinea. The plant also maintains a presence in the Ivory Coast, contributing to its regional importance. Finally, its geographical range is completed by its distribution in Liberia.
