What's the taxonomical classification of Maesa indica?

Maesa indica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical structure, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is further organized under the order Ericales and falls within the family Primulaceae. At the most specific taxonomic levels, it is identified under the genus Maesa, with its unique designation being the species indica.
