Chinese ash, scientifically known as Fraxinus chinensis, is a deciduous tree native to East Asia, valued extensively in traditional medicine. Its bark and leaves contain bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, fever, and various ailments. This versatile plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical research and herbal pharmacology.
