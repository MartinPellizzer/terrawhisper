Heterophyllaea pustulata

Heterophyllaea pustulata, commonly known as the "pustulate heterophyllaea," is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to the rainforests of New Caledonia. Renowned for its unique leaf morphology, this plant is traditionally utilized in local ethnobotany for its therapeutic properties, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Heterophyllaea pustulata
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianaales and is a member of the Rubiaceae family. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Heterophyllaea.
