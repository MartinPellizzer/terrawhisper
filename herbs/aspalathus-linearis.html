Scientific literature: Very Extensive (738 studies) · ★★★★★

Rooibos, scientifically known as Aspalathus linearis, is a celebrated medicinal shrub native to South Africa's Cederberg mountains. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this caffeine-free herbal infusion offers soothing benefits for digestion and skin health. Traditionally used in wellness rituals, it remains a globally cherished superfood and therapeutic botanical.

Distribution

This plant is native to a specific region located within the southern portion of the African continent. Its natural habitat is primarily concentrated within the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. This localized distribution means that it thrives under the unique climatic conditions found in these coastal and inland areas. Because of this restricted range, the species is considered an endemic element of the local flora.
