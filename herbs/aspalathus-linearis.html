Aspalathus linearis

Rooibos

Aspalathus linearis, commonly known as rooibos, is a unique medicinal shrub endemic to South Africa's Cederberg mountains. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this caffeine-free infusion offers numerous health benefits, including anti-inflammatory and anti-aging effects. It remains a globally cherished herbal tea, valued for its soothing, nutrient-rich profile.

  • Scientific name: Aspalathus linearis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Aspalathus.

Distribution

This plant is native to a specific region located within the southern portion of the African continent. Its natural habitat is primarily concentrated within the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. This localized distribution means that it thrives under the unique climatic conditions found in these coastal and inland areas. Because of this restricted range, the species is considered an endemic element of the local flora. Consequently, the Cape Provinces serve as the essential environmental stronghold for its survival in the wild.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Rooibos, utilizes various components for its medicinal and commercial value. The primary source of its antioxidant properties is found in the needle-like leaves, which are harvested and fermented to develop their distinct profile. The structural integrity of the shrub is provided by its woody stems, which support the growth of the foliage. While the roots are less commonly used in commercial production, they remain an integral part of the plant's biological system. For therapeutic applications, the dried material is often processed into fine powders for topical or supplemental use, or steeped in water to create nutrient-rich liquids used in teas and health tonics.
