Michelia chapensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Michelia chapensis?

Michelia chapensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae, following the taxonomic order Magnoliales. As a member of the family Magnoliaceae, it falls under the genus Michelia, with its specific identification being the species chapensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Michelia chapensis has large, elliptical, coriaceous leaves that are dark green on the upper surface and slightly paler underneath. The leaf margins are entire and the venation is prominent, particularly on the abaxial side. This species produces fragrant, cream-colored flowers that emerge from the terminal ends of the branches. The reproductive structures include a central gynoecium surrounded by numerous stamens that eventually develop into aggregate follicles. These woody follicles split open to reveal bright red or orange seeds, which are often attached to fleshy arils.
