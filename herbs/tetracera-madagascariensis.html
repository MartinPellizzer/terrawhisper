Tetracera madagascariensis

Tetracera madagascariensis, commonly known as the Madagascar climber, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Native to tropical regions, its leaves and stems are frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammatory conditions, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Tetracera madagascariensis
  • Family: Dilleniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Dilleniales and the family Dilleniaceae. It is further identified by the genus Tetracera.
