Catharanthus roseus

Catharanthus roseus, commonly known as Madagascar periwinkle, is a resilient flowering shrub highly valued in modern medicine. While prized for its vibrant blooms, its true significance lies in its potent alkaloids. These compounds serve as the essential foundation for synthesizing life-saving chemotherapy drugs used to treat various cancers.

  • Scientific name: Catharanthus roseus
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Gentianaales and belongs to the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Catharanthus.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in Spain. Finally, its range reaches the Macaronesian islands, including the Canary Is.
