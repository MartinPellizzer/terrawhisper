Cratoxylum sumatranum

Cratoxylum sumatranum, commonly known as the "pulasari" tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Cratoxylum sumatranum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Hypericaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Cratoxylum.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across the diverse landscapes of the Malesia botanical region. It can be found growing within the tropical forests of Borneo. Its presence extends to the island of Jawa, contributing to the local biodiversity. The species also inhabits the various islands of the Lesser Sunda archipelago. Finally, its geographical range includes the territories of Malaya and the Philippines.
