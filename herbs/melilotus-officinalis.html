Melilotus officinalis

Field melilot

Melilotus officinalis, commonly known as sweet clover, is a versatile medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in coumarin, it is traditionally used to support circulation, reduce inflammation, and aid digestive health. This resilient plant serves as a vital natural resource in both traditional herbalism and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Melilotus officinalis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Melilotus.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as sweet clover, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties through the use of its aerial parts. These above-ground components include the slender, upright stems, the trifoliate leaves, and the fragrant, pale yellow flowers that bloom in dense racemes. When harvested, these aerial parts are typically dried to preserve the active coumarins, which are the key chemical constituents responsible for the plant's pharmacological effects.
