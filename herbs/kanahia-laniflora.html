Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within the tropical belts of Africa. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is recorded in Benin, Ivory Coast, and Nigeria. The species also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found in the Central African Republic. Additionally, it is documented as growing within the borders of Cameroon. Together, these locations highlight a specific range concentrated in the western and central tropical zones.
