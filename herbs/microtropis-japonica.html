Microtropis japonica

Microtropis japonica, commonly known as the Japanese Microtropis, is a climbing shrub native to East Asian forests. Valued in traditional medicine, this species contains unique bioactive alkaloids. Research explores its potential pharmacological properties, particularly regarding its effects on the nervous system, making it a subject of significant ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Microtropis japonica
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, known as Microtropis japonica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Microtropis.
