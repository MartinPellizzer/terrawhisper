What's the taxonomical classification of Abutilon sinense?

Abutilon sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Abutilon, and its species designation, sinense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Abutilon sinense has ovate to cordate leaves with serrated margins, woody stems, and tubular flowers that typically range in color from yellow to orange. The foliage is often characterized by a soft, pubescent texture due to fine hairs covering the surface. Its flowers emerge from axillary positions and feature a distinct long calyx tube that supports the spreading petals. The plant develops into a perennial shrub with an upright growth habit. Small, capsule-like seed pods form following successful pollination to facilitate reproduction.
