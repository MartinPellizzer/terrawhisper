Ipomoea squamosa

Ipomoea squamosa, commonly known as the swallow-wort or field bindweed, is a versatile medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
