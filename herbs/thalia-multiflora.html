Thalia multiflora

Thalia multiflora, commonly known as the many-flowered Thalia, is a striking perennial aquatic plant cherished for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Native to wetland habitats, it features elegant, tall stalks and delicate blossoms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, various cultures utilize its unique properties for traditional healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Thalia multiflora
  • Family: Marantaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is classified under the family Marantaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Thalia, specifically as the species Thalia multiflora.

Common Names

This plant, Thalia multiflora, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In Portuguese, the plant is called thalia de muitas flores, and in French, it is referred to as thalia à fleurs multiples.
