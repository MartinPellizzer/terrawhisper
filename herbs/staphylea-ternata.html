Staphylea ternata

Staphylea ternata, commonly known as Bladdernut, is a deciduous shrub prized for its unique, papery seed capsules. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its antioxidant properties. Its diverse chemical profile offers significant potential for pharmacological research and holistic wellness applications.

  • Scientific name: Staphylea ternata
  • Family: Staphyleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae and order Crossosomatales, it is placed in the family Staphyleaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Staphylea.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions within Eastern Asia. Its natural habitat includes the islands of Japan, where it can be found growing in various environments. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the Nansei-shotō archipelago. The geographical range also extends southward to include the island of Taiwan. Consequently, these three distinct areas define the known ecological presence of Staphylea ternata.
