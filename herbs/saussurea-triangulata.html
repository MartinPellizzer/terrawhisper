Saussurea triangulata

Saussurea triangulata, commonly known as Costus, is a precious medicinal herb found in the high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, it is extensively used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin disorders, and inflammatory conditions, making it a highly valued botanical resource globally.

  • Scientific name: Saussurea triangulata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Saussurea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the temperate regions of Northeast Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented across several distinct territories including Amur and Khabarovsk. It also extends further into the maritime regions of Primorye and the island of Saksaklin. Beyond the Russian borders, the species is found within the Manchuria region of China. Together, these locations define the primary natural range for this medicinal species.
