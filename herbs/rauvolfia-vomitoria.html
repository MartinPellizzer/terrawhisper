African serpentwood

Rauvolfia vomitoria · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (154 studies) · ★★★★☆

African serpentwood, scientifically known as Rauvolfia vomitoria, is a medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, particularly reserpine, it has been used traditionally to treat hypertension and mental disorders. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource in both ethnobotany and modern pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, Rauvolfia vomitoria, is highly valued in traditional medicine, with various components serving specific therapeutic purposes. The foliage and individual leaf structures are often studied for their chemical properties, though the most potent medicinal compounds are concentrated underground. Specifically, the roots contain high concentrations of alkaloids, and the root bark is frequently harvested for its pharmacological potency. Whether utilizing the raw root barks or processed extracts, each part contributes to the plant's significant role in treating various ailments.
