Rauvolfia vomitoria

Rauvolfia vomitoria, commonly known as poison firework, is a potent medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa and Asia. Renowned for its high concentration of reserpine, it has historically been used to treat hypertension and psychotic disorders. This powerful botanical remains a cornerstone in pharmacology for managing neurological and cardiovascular health.

  • Scientific name: Rauvolfia vomitoria
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rauvolfia.
