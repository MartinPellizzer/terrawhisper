African serpentwood

Rauvolfia vomitoria · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (154 studies) · ★★★★☆

African serpentwood, scientifically known as Rauvolfia vomitoria, is a medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, particularly reserpine, it has been used traditionally to treat hypertension and mental disorders. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource in both ethnobotany and modern pharmacology.
