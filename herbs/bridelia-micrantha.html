Bridelia micrantha

Bridelia micrantha, commonly known as the Lamprides, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and roots are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections. This plant serves as a vital natural resource for diverse community healthcare.

  • Scientific name: Bridelia micrantha
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, the medicinal species is identified by its genus, Bridelia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally in the country of Benin.
