Zanthoxylum integrifoliolum

Zanthoxylum integrifolium, commonly known as the prickly amadou, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Rich in bioactive alkaloids and essential oils, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic properties. This versatile species serves as a vital natural resource for treating various ailments in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum integrifoliolum
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Zanthoxylum.
