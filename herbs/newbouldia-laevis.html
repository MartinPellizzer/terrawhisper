Newbouldia laevis

Newbouldia laevis, commonly known as the smooth Newbouldi, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Native to specific subtropical regions, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to treat inflammation and digestive ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for its sustainable and effective application.

  • Scientific name: Newbouldia laevis
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Newbouldia laevis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Bignoniaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Newbouldia.

Distribution

This plant, Newbouldia laevis, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several nations in West Tropical Africa. Its presence is documented within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species is also found spreading through the landscapes of Burkina and the Gambia.
