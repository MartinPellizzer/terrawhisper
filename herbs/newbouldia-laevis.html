Distribution

This plant, Newbouldia laevis, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several nations in West Tropical Africa. Its presence is documented within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species is also found spreading through the landscapes of Burkina and the Gambia. Furthermore, its range extends into the coastal and inland regions of Ghana. Finally, the plant's documented distribution includes the territory of Guinea-Bissau.
