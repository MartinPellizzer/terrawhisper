Plant Parts

This plant, Pinellia ternata, possesses a complex underground structure that is essential to its medicinal properties. The primary storage organ is a fleshy, thickened tuber, which is actually a modified underground stem known as a rhizome. This central tuber serves as the main source of active compounds used in traditional remedies. From this base, the plant develops its functional aerial parts, most notably the broad, green leaves that facilitate photosynthesis. While the plant is characterized by this singular, prominent tuber, the overall anatomy relies on the connection between the nutrient-storing rhizome and the leafy canopy above.
