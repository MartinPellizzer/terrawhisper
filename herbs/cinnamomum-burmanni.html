Java cinnamon, scientifically known as Cinnamomum burmanni, is a highly aromatic spice native to Indonesia. Renowned for its warm, spicy flavor, this plant is widely utilized in culinary traditions and traditional medicine. It possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable resource in holistic healing.
