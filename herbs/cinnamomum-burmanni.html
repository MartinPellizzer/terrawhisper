Cinnamomum burmanni

Java cinnamon

Cinnamomum burmanni, commonly known as Indonesian cinnamon or Padang cinnamon, is a highly aromatic plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent, spicy bark, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine and culinary arts for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering diverse therapeutic benefits for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cinnamomum burmanni
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cinnamomum burmannii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Cinnamomum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Cinnamomum burmannii, is referred to by various names depending on the region and language. In Chinese, it is known as 阴香 (zh-hans), 陰香 (zh-hant), and 陰香 (zh-hk). In Indonesia, it is commonly called Cinnamomum burmannii (id), and in Arabic, it is referred to as قرفة إندونيسية (ar).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the globe. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its range extends to the Western Indian Ocean, where it is documented in Mauritius. Within China, the species is distributed across South-Central regions as well as the Hainan province. Additionally, it is present in the Southeast portion of China, completing its wide ecological footprint.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cinnamomum burmannii, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its various components that contain potent therapeutic properties. The most significant part used is the bark, specifically the stem bark, which is harvested and dried to be used as a powerful warming spice and digestive aid. In addition to the bark, the leaves and twigs are often utilized in herbal preparations due to their aromatic qualities. A crucial element found throughout these parts is the volatile oil, which provides the characteristic scent and serves as the primary source of the plant's medicinal efficacy.
