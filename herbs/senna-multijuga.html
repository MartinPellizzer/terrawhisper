Senna multijuga

Sennat multijuga, commonly known as Multijuga or Sanay, is a vital medicinal plant used extensively in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent purgative and laxative properties, it effectively treats constipation and digestive disorders. This botanical treasure plays a crucial role in detoxification and maintaining gastrointestinal health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Senna multijuga
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Senna.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the countries of Ghana and Sierra Leone. Its presence extends into East Tropical Africa as well. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of Kenya and Tanzania. Furthermore, the species is also documented to grow within the borders of Uganda.
