False sicklepod, scientifically known as Senna multijuga, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, it is primarily utilized for its potent purgative properties. The plant's leaves and fruits offer therapeutic benefits in treating digestive issues, skin ailments, and various inflammatory conditions.
