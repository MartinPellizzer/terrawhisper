Geranium limae

Geranium limae, commonly known as the Lima Geranium, is a distinguished medicinal plant celebrated for its therapeutic versatility. Renowned for its calming aromatic properties, it is traditionally utilized to soothe nervous tension and promote skin health. This resilient herb offers a unique blend of botanical healing and sensory delight.

  • Scientific name: Geranium limae
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Geranium.
