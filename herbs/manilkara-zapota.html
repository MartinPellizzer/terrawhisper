Sapodilla, scientifically known as Manilkara zapota, is a versatile tropical evergreen prized for both its sweet fruit and medicinal properties. Historically used in traditional medicine, its bark and sap possess antibacterial and anti-inflammatory qualities, offering therapeutic benefits for digestive health and wound healing across various cultural practices worldwide.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct tropical regions across the globe.
