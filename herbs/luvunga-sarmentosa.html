Distribution

This plant exhibits a distinct geographical distribution spanning several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is specifically noted in Vietnam. Moving toward the Malesia zone, the species is found inhabiting the island of Borneo. Additionally, it extends its range across the island of Sumatera within the same Malesian territory. Consequently, the distribution of Luvunga sarmentosa connects diverse ecological landscapes across the archipelago and mainland.
