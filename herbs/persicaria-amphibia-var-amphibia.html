Persicaria amphibia var. amphibia

Persicaria amphibia var. amphibia, commonly known as water smartweed, is a resilient aquatic perennial found in wetlands and slow-moving waters. Renowned for its ecological significance, this plant supports biodiversity. While primarily valued in environmental management, various traditional practices have historically explored its potential medicinal properties and restorative benefits.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria amphibia var. amphibia
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Persicaria, specifically as the variety amphibia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several northern European regions. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Its presence is also documented within the territories of Finland and Iceland. Additionally, the species is located in the Faroe Islands, known locally as Føroyar. Finally, its geographical range extends to include the various landscapes of Great Britain.
