Pilea cavaleriei

Pilea cavaleriei, commonly known as the Cavalry Pilea, is a captivating ornamental plant prized for its unique, textured foliage. While primarily grown for its aesthetic charm in tropical collections, its genus is often studied for potential medicinal properties, offering a fascinating intersection of botanical beauty and traditional herbal interest.

  • Scientific name: Pilea cavaleriei
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Pilea cavaleriei, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Rosales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Urticaceae and the genus Pilea.
