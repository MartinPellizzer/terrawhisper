Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of the Mediterranean basin. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing throughout the territories of France, Portugal, and Spain. Its presence also extends into Southeastern Europe, specifically within the country of Italy. Furthermore, the species is well-established in Northern Africa, particularly in Tunisia. These diverse locations highlight the plant's adaptability to the Mediterranean climate.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as prickly pear, is a unique species of cactuses that offers various medicinal and nutritional benefits through its different structures. The most visible parts are the flattened, succulent cladodes, which serve as the plant's primary photosynthetic stems. From these stems, the plant produces vibrant, nutrient-rich fruits that are often harvested for their antioxidant properties. Deep within these fruits lie the seeds, which can be processed to extract highly valuable seed oil, a substance prized in skincare for its healing and moisturizing capabilities.
