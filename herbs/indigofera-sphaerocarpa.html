Sonoran indigo, scientifically known as Indigofera sphaerocarpa, is a resilient desert shrub native to the Southwestern United States and Mexico. Renowned for its distinctive spherical seed pods, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in various medicinal preparations, it serves as a fascinating subject for studying desert biodiversity.
