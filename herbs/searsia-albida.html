Searsia albida

Searsia albiida, commonly known as the White Karee, is a resilient, evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Highly valued for its dense, silvery-green foliage, it serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. Its various parts are traditionally used in holistic remedies to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Searsia albida
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae, it is classified under the genus Searsia.
