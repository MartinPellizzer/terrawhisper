Silene fissipetala

Silene fissipepetala, commonly known as the split-petal campion, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific montane ecosystems. Valued for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers potential therapeutic properties. Understanding its traditional uses and bioactive compounds is essential for advancing botanical research and exploring its role in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Silene fissipetala
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Caryophyllales and the family Caryophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Silene.
