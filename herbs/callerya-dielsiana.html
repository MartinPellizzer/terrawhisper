Callerya dielsiana

Callerya dielsiana, commonly known as the Delsian's Callerya, is a remarkable tropical orchid prized for its stunning, fragrant blooms. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, this species holds significant interest in ethnobotany. While primarily ornamental, its unique biological properties offer intriguing potential for exploring traditional medicinal applications and natural bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Callerya dielsiana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Callerya dielaisia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification is completed within the genus Callerya.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical presence across several distinct regions in Asia. In China, its range extends through the South-Central provinces and reaches into the North-Central areas. It is also documented within the tropical landscapes of Hainan. Furthermore, the species can be found spreading through the Southeast regions of China. Beyond Chinese borders, its distribution continues into the Indo-China region, specifically within Myanmar.
