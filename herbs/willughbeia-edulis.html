Willughbeia edulis

Willughbeia eduilis, commonly known as the wild nutmeg, is a tropical climbing shrub valued for its edible fruits and medicinal properties. Indigenous to rainforest regions, it is traditionally utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin inflammations, and various internal infections, offering a rich source of nutrients and therapeutic potential.
