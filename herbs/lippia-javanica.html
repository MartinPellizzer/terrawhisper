Lippia javanica

Lippia javanica, commonly known as the Java Lippia, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various African regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat ailments such as digestive issues, respiratory infections, and skin irritations, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Lippia javanica
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Lippia javaica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Verbenaceae and falls under the genus Lippia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the African continent. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in both the Central African Republic and the DR Congo. Moving toward the northeast, the species is also found in Ethiopia. Its range extends further into East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Kenya. Finally, the plant is documented as being native to Tanzania.
