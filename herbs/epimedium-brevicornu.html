Epimedium brevicornu

Epimedium brevicornu, commonly known as Epimedium or Horny Goat Weed, is a perennial herb prized in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compound, icariin, this plant is widely used to support reproductive health, enhance vitality, and improve circulation, offering significant therapeutic potential for various wellness applications.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium brevicornu
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Epimedium brevicornu, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the Berberidaceae family and falls under the genus Epimedium.
