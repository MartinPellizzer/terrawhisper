Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Perilla, offers a diverse range of therapeutic components that are utilized in various medicinal applications. The aromatic leaves are highly valued for their unique properties, and when these leaf structures are processed, they can yield a potent essential oil. Specifically, Perilla essential oil is prized for its concentrated aromatic compounds, which are often used in holistic treatments. Furthermore, the extraction of seed oil from its seeds provides another significant source of healthy fatty acids, making the entire plant a versatile resource in traditional medicine.
