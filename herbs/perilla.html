Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is noted within the borders of Germany. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species can be found in both Italy and Romania. Its range extends further into Eastern Europe, specifically covering parts of South European Russia. Finally, the plant is also widely distributed throughout Ukraine.
