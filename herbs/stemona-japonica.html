STEMONA JAPONICA WHOLE, scientifically known as Stemona japonica, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional Eastern medicine. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, it is primarily utilized for its antitussive and anthelmintic properties. This plant serves as a vital natural remedy for treating coughs, respiratory issues, and parasitic infections.
