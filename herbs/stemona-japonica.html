Stemona japonica

Stemona japonica, commonly known as Japanese Stemona, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, its roots contain potent alkaloids used primarily for their anthelmintic and antitussive properties. This medicinal plant plays a crucial role in treating parasitic infections and soothing respiratory ailments naturally.

  • Scientific name: Stemona japonica
  • Family: Stemonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Pandanales and placed in the family Stemonaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Stemona.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the Eastern Asian region. In China, its presence is notably recorded across the South-Central provinces. It is also widely distributed throughout the Southeast regions of the country. Beyond mainland China, the species extends its reach to the island nation of Japan. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where Stemona japonica can be found growing.
