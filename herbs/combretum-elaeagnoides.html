Combretum elaeagnoides

Grey jesse bushwillow

Combretum elaeagnoides, commonly known as the Elaeagnoides Combretum, is a significant medicinal shrub native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a rich source of bioactive phytochemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Combretum elaeagnoides
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Combretum and the specific species Combretum elaeagnoides.
