Alpinia calcarata

Alpinia calcarrata, commonly known as the white cardamom or ginger lily, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, digestive, and antimicrobial properties. This vibrant perennial is widely utilized to support gastrointestinal health and alleviate various systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Alpinia calcarata
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Alpinia.
