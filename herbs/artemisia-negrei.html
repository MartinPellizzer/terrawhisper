Artemisia negrei

Artemisia negrei, commonly known as a rare member of the wormwood family, is a distinctive medicinal plant endemic to specific Mediterranean habitats. Valued for its unique phytochemical profile, it holds significant potential in ethnobotany. Research continues into its bioactive compounds, exploring their therapeutic properties for various traditional medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia negrei
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling into the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Artemisia.

Common Names

This plant, Artemisia negrei, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is referred to as Negrei's wormwood, while in Spanish, it is commonly called ajenjo de Negrei. In Portuguese, the species is known as artemísia de Negrei, and in French, it is called armoise de Negrei.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Artemisia negrei, exhibits a very specific and restricted geographical distribution. Its presence is primarily documented within the North African region. Specifically, the species is found growing in the territory of Morocco. Because it is tied to this particular area, it is considered an endemic element of the local flora. Consequently, its natural habitat remains confined to these unique Mediterranean landscapes.
