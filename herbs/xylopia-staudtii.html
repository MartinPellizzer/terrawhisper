Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Within this zone, its presence is recorded in the country of Ghana. It can also be found spanning across the borders of Guinea and Ivory Coast. Furthermore, the species is known to inhabit the forested areas of Liberia. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant extends into Nigeria.
