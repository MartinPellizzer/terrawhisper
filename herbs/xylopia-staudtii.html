Xylopia staudtii

Xylopia staudtii, commonly known as a valuable medicinal shrub in West Africa, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Xylopia staudtii
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Annonaceae. Specifically, it is classified under the genus Xylopia.
