Campomanesia xanthocarpa

Campomanesia xanthocarpa, commonly known as yellow-fruit campomanesia, is a specialized red macroalga valued for its unique bioactive properties. Found in specific marine ecosystems, this seaweed contains distinct pigments and polysaccharides. It serves as a promising subject in ethnopharmacology, offering potential therapeutic applications in modern medicinal research and development.

  • Scientific name: Campomanesia xanthocarpa
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Campomanesia.
