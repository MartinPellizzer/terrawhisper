Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across two major regions. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented specifically within the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Moving toward Central Asia, the species can also be found growing in Turkmenistan. Additionally, its range extends into the mountainous landscapes of Tajikistan. Finally, the plant completes its regional distribution with occurrences found in Uzbekistan.
