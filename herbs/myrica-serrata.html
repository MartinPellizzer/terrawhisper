Myrica serrata, scientifically known as Myrica serrata, is a prominent medicinal shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Commonly referred to as Takol, it is highly valued in Ayurveda for its potent antioxidant properties. Its bark and leaves are frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and inflammation.
