Pulsatilla vulgaris

Pasqueflower

Pulsatilla vulgaris, commonly known as pasqueflower, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its bell-shaped, violet blooms. Native to Europe, this plant holds significant historical importance in traditional herbalism. However, due to its potent bioactive compounds, it must be handled with extreme caution, as it is naturally toxic.

  • Scientific name: Pulsatilla vulgaris
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, falling within the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed under the genus Pulsatilla.
