Argemone subfusiformis

Argemone subfusiformis, commonly known as the subfuscent prickly poppy, is a distinctive medicinal herb belonging to the Papaveraceae family. Found primarily in tropical regions, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive alkaloids. Research focuses on its potential therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial applications.
