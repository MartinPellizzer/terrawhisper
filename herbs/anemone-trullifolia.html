Anemone trullifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Anemone trullifolia?

Anemone trullifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Ranunculales, it is further nested within the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific biological classification as part of the genus Anemone, with its unique identity designated as the species trullifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Anemone trullifolia has deeply divided, trifoliate leaves that feature narrow, linear lobes and a prominent central vein. These basal leaves are typically dark green and provide a distinct structural base for the plant's growth. The stems are slender and upright, supporting solitary flowers that emerge from the foliage. Each flower consists of delicate, petal-like sepals that surround a central cluster of numerous stamens. The overall architecture of the plant is characterized by its low-growing profile and fine-textured greenery.
