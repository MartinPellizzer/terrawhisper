Triaenophora rupestris

What's the taxonomical classification of Triaenophora rupestris?

Triaenophora rupestris belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Lamiales. At the family level, it is a member of the Orobanchaceae, which characterizes its biological lineage. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Triaenophora, and its species designation, rupestris.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Triaenophora rupestris has a low-growing, prostrate growth habit characterized by woody stems and small, opposite, elliptic leaves with entire margins. The foliage is typically dark green and can appear somewhat leathery to the touch. Its flowers are small and arranged in compact clusters along the stems. The plant often develops a dense, mat-like structure that allows it to cling to rocky surfaces. This morphological adaptation is specifically suited for survival in its natural rupicolous habitats.
