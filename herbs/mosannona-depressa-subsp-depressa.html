Mosannona depressa subsp. depressa

Mosannona depressa subsp. depressa, commonly known as the Hungarian marsh grass, is a specialized medicinal plant found in unique wetland ecosystems. Renowned for its distinct phytochemical profile, it offers significant therapeutic potential. This subspecies is increasingly studied for its bioactive compounds, providing valuable insights into natural pharmacological treatments.

  • Scientific name: Mosannona depressa subsp. depressa
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
