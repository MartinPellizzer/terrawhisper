Distribution

This plant, known as Harungana, is widely distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing in the country of Benin, contributing to the local medicinal flora. Its presence extends through Burkina and into the coastal areas of the Gambia. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the landscapes of Ghana. Finally, its geographical range includes the territory of Guinea-Bissau.
