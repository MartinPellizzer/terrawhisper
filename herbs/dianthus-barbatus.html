Dianthus barbatus

Dianthus barbatus, commonly known as Sweet William, is a charming perennial celebrated for its fragrant, clustered blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this species holds significance in traditional herbalism. Its vibrant colors and delicate scent make it a staple for both landscape design and historical botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Dianthus barbatus
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Caryophyllaceae and is situated in the genus Dianthus.
