Lippia integrifolia, scientifically known as Salimenaea integrifolia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and skin inflammations, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and development.
