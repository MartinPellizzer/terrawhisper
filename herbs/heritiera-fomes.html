Heritiera fomes

Sundri

Heritiera fomes, commonly known as the Golden Root, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its potent healing properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, it plays a crucial role in traditional herbal medicine. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential for supporting overall wellness and immune function.
