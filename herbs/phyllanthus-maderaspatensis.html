Phyllanthus maderaspatensis

Madras leaf-flower

Phyllanthus maderaspatensis, commonly known as the "false leaf-flower," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus maderaspatensis
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several distinct regions in Africa. Within the Macaronesian islands, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. Moving to the mainland, it is found throughout various parts of West Tropical Africa. This includes populations located in Benin, Burkina, and Ghana. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the Ivory Coast.
