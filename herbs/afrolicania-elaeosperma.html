Afrolicania elaeosperma belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical structure, the plant is situated in the order Malpighiales and is a member of the family Chrysobalanaeae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Afrolicania and is specifically recognized by its species name, elaeosperma.
