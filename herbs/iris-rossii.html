Iris rossii

Long-tail iris

Iris rossii, commonly known as the Rossi Iris, is a resilient perennial native to the high-altitude regions of Central Asia. Renowned for its striking, delicate blooms and elegant foliage, this medicinal plant has been traditionally utilized in folk remedies for its unique bioactive properties and restorative botanical qualities.

  • Scientific name: Iris rossii
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales. Within the family Iridaceae, it is specifically identified by the genus Iris.
