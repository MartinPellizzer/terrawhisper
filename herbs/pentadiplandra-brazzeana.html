Pentadiplandra brazzeana

Pentadiplandra brazzeaana, commonly known as the wild coffee plant, is a significant medicinal shrub native to East Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Pentadiplandra brazzeana
  • Family: Pentadiplandraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales. Finally, it is a member of the family Pentadiplandraceae and falls under the genus Pentadiplandra.
