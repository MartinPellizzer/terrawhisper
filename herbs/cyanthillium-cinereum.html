Cyanthillium cinereum

Cyanthillium cinereum, commonly known as the Azure Bloom, is a rare medicinal herb prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Native to high-altitude ecosystems, this vibrant plant contains unique bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to soothe inflammation, support immune function, and promote overall cellular regeneration and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Cyanthillium cinereum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Cyanthillium.
