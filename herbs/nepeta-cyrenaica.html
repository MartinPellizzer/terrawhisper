Nepeta cyrenaica

Nepeta cyrenaica, commonly known as Cyrenaic catmint, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its aromatic, silver-grey foliage and delicate violet blooms, this plant belongs to the Lamiaceae family. It is valued in traditional herbalism for its soothing properties and unique botanical charm.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta cyrenaica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Nepeta.
