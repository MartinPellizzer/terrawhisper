Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several key regions of Asia. In China, its presence is documented across the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found thriving within the East Himalaya region. Additionally, its range extends into Indo-China, specifically within the borders of Myanmar. Together, these locations highlight the broad environmental adaptability of Beesia calthiifolia.
