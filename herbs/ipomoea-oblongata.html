Wild morning glory, scientifically known as Ipomoea oblongata, is a resilient climbing vine native to arid regions. Renowned for its striking trumpet-shaped flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in various medicinal practices, it offers unique bioactive compounds that continue to intrigue modern pharmacological research and study.
