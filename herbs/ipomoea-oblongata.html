Ipomoea oblongata

Ipomoea oblongata, commonly known as the slender morning glory, is a versatile medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation and digestive ailments. This resilient climber offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea oblongata
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Ipomoea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several regions of the African continent. In Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Ethiopia. Its distribution continues into South Tropical Africa, where it is present in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving further south, the species is also recorded in Botswana. Finally, the plant reaches its southern limits within the Cape Provinces.
