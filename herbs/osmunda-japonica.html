Osmunda japonica

Asian royal fern

Osmunda japonica, commonly known as the Japanese royal fern, is a striking perennial plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its elegant, feathery fronds, it thrives in moist, shaded environments. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant cultural value and traditional medicinal uses in various Asian herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Osmunda japonica
  • Family: Osmundaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Osmundales, specifically within the family Osmundaceae. Finally, its scientific designation is placed within the genus Osmunda.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its range extends to the Kuril Islands and Sakhalin. Within China, it is widely spread across the North-Central territories. Furthermore, the species can be found throughout the South-Central regions of the country. Finally, its presence is also established in the Southeast portions of China.
