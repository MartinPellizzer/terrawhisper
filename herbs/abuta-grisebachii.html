Abuta grisebachii

Abuta grisebachii, commonly known as Abuta, is a medicinal climbing shrub native to the tropical rainforests of the Amazon basin. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, its roots contain potent alkaloids used to treat various ailments, including fever, inflammation, and digestive issues, making it a significant species in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Abuta grisebachii
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Menispermaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Abuta, specifically as the species Abuta grisebachii.
