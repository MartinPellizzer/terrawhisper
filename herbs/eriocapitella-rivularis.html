Himalayan windflower, scientifically known as Eriocapitella rivularis, is a resilient perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. Often found near mountain streams, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in folk medicine, it is prized for its potential therapeutic properties, offering a unique glimpse into Himalayan botanical heritage.
