Stauntonia obovata

What's the taxonomical classification of Stauntonia obovata?

Stauntonia obovata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is positioned in the order Ranunculales. It is a member of the family Lardizabalaceae, falling under the genus Stauntonia, and is specifically identified by the species name obovatata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Stauntonia obovata has woody, twining vines that produce obovate to elliptical leaves with entire or slightly serrated margins and a distinctively textured surface. These blossoms eventually give way to fleshy, bright red or orange berries that are roughly spherical in shape.
