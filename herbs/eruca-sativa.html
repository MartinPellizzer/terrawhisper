Eruca sativa

Eruca sativa, commonly known as rocket or arugula, is a versatile leafy green celebrated for its pungent, peppery flavor. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant offers significant medicinal benefits, containing bioactive compounds with antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties that support digestive health and overall wellness in traditional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Eruca sativa
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicaleales, it falls under the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Eruca.
