Heracleum sphondylium

Hogweed

Heracleum sphondylium, commonly known as hogweed, is a robust perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism. Found in diverse habitats, this plant contains bioactive compounds with potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. While used historically for various ailments, careful identification is essential due to its resemblance to toxic umbellifers.

  • Scientific name: Heracleum sphondylium
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Heracleum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Heracleum sphondylium, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. Additionally, in Hungary, the plant is called közönséges medvetalp.
