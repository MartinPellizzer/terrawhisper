Laurelia sempervirens

Laurelia sempervirens, commonly known as the Evergreen Laurel, is a resilient medicinal plant prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it is traditionally used to soothe inflammation and support respiratory health. This versatile evergreen offers a natural approach to wellness through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Family: Atherospermataceae
  • Native range: Southern America
