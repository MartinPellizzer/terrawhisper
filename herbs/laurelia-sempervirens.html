Chilean laurel, scientifically known as Laurelia sempervirens, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the temperate forests of Chile. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional healing practices to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and digestive issues through its potent bioactive compounds.
