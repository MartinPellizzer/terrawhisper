Laurelia sempervirens

Chilean laurel

Laurelia sempervirens, commonly known as the Evergreen Laurel, is a resilient medicinal plant prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it is traditionally used to soothe inflammation and support respiratory health. This versatile evergreen offers a natural approach to wellness through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Laurelia sempervirens
  • Family: Atherospermataceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Laurales, it is classified under the family Atherospermatae. It is specifically identified by the genus Laurelia, which includes the species Laurelia sempervirens.
