Prosopis farcta

Prosopis farcta, commonly known as desert broom or thorny mesquite, is a resilient, woody shrub native to the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern regions. Highly adapted to arid environments, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, offering significant potential in treating various ailments within traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Prosopis farcta
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Fabales, falling into the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Prosopis.
