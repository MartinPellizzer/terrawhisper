Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as sugarcane, is primarily valued for its succulent and fibrous stem. The stem serves as the main storage organ for the plant, accumulating high concentrations of sucrose within its parenchymatous tissues. As a medicinal and nutritional resource, the stem is harvested to extract its sweet juice, which is used in various traditional remedies and dietary applications.
