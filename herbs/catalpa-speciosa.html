Catalpa speciosa

Catalpa speciosa, commonly known as the Northern Catalpa, is a striking deciduous tree celebrated for its massive, heart-shaped leaves and fragrant, bell-shaped white flowers. While primarily ornamental in landscaping, its unique botanical characteristics and robust presence make it a fascinating subject for studying diverse North American woodland ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Catalpa speciosa
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Lamiales and the family Bignoniaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Catalpa.
