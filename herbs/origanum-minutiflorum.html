Distribution

This plant is characterized by a very specific and limited geographical range. Its presence is primarily confined to the region of Western Asia. Within this territory, the species is documented to grow in Türkiye. Because of this localized habitat, it is considered an endemic element of the local flora. Consequently, its natural distribution is much more restricted than many other common medicinal herbs.
