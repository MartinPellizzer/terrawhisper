Naranjilla, scientifically known as Solanum quitoense, is a vibrant tropical plant native to the Andean regions. Valued for its unique citrus-like flavor, this medicinal shrub offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often consumed as a refreshing juice, it serves as a natural remedy for supporting overall health and immunity.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct tropical regions. Within the Malesia zone, it can be found specifically on the island of Jawa. Its presence extends into Central America, where it is documented in Costa Rica, Nicaragua, and Panamá. Additionally, the species reaches the Caribbean region, specifically inhabiting Cuba. Together, these locations highlight the widespread nature of its natural habitat.
