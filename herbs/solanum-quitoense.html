Naranjilla, scientifically known as Solanum quitoense, is a vibrant tropical plant native to the Andean regions. Valued for its unique citrus-like flavor, this medicinal shrub offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often consumed as a refreshing juice, it serves as a natural remedy for supporting overall health and immunity.
