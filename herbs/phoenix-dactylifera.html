Phoenix dactylifera

Phoenix dactylifera, commonly known as the date palm, is a versatile medicinal plant revered for its nutritional and therapeutic properties. Beyond providing energy-rich fruit, its various parts offer antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits. Traditionally used in diverse cultures, it serves as a vital resource for holistic wellness and nourishment.

  • Scientific name: Phoenix dactylifera
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Arecales and the family Arecaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phoenix.
