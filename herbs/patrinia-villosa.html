Patrinia villosa

Patriina villosa, commonly known as hairy patriaria, is a perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its hairy stems and delicate yellow flowers, this plant belongs to the Rutaceae family. Traditionally utilized in herbal medicine, it offers unique phytochemical properties that continue to interest modern botanical researchers today.

  • Scientific name: Patrinia villosa
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Patrinia villosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Dipsacales. Finally, it is classified within the family Caprifoliaceae and the genus Patrinia.
