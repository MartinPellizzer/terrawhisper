Aquilegia brevistyla

Aquilegia brevistyla, commonly known as the Short-styled Columbine, is a delicate perennial wildflower native to specific mountainous regions. Renowned for its unique floral structure and intricate petal arrangements, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its specialized phytochemical properties and its distinct role in regional herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Aquilegia brevistyla
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Aquilegia brevistylia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Its specific classification is further defined by its placement in the genus Aquilegia.
