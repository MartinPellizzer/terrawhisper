Garden cress, scientifically known as Lepidium sativum, is a versatile annual herb celebrated for its pungent flavor and nutritional density. Often used as a culinary garnish, this tiny cruciferous plant is rich in vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants, offering significant therapeutic potential in traditional medicine for promoting overall wellness.

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented in both Great Britain and Ireland. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as garden cress, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components are utilized for their nutritional properties. The small, dark seeds of Lepidium sativum are perhaps the most well-known part, frequently consumed for their high mineral content. Whether used as whole seeds or ground into a powder, each individual seed provides a concentrated dose of nutrients. In addition to the seeds, the tender green leaves are also edible and can be incorporated into culinary dishes to enhance their health benefits.
