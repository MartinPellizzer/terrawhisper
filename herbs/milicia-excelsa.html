African teak, scientifically known as Milicia excelsa, is a majestic tropical tree renowned for its significant medicinal properties. Widely distributed across West and Central Africa, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as skin infections, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering valuable therapeutic potential.
