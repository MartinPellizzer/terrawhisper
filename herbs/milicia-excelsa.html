Milicia excelsa

African teak

Miliicia excelsa, commonly known as the Iroko tree, is a majestic timber species native to West African tropical forests. Beyond its immense commercial value, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Various parts are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to various infections and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Milicia excelsa
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant, known as Milicia excelsa, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its presence is documented within the borders of Benin. It can also be found growing in the landscapes of Burkina. Furthermore, the species is native to the territories of Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends to include Guinea-Bissau.
