What's the taxonomical classification of Fritillaria yuzhongensis?

Fritillaria yuzhongensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Liliales. This plant is a member of the family Liliaceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Fritillaria, with its unique designation as the species yuzhongensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Friillaria yuzhongensis has bulbous rhizomes with lanceolate leaves and nodding, campanulate flowers that feature dark purple or brownish spots on a greenish-yellow base. The plant typically grows to a height of 10 to 30 centimeters, supporting several thin, spreading leaves arranged in a whorl. Its perianth consists of six tepals that are often marked with distinct maculations and may exhibit a slight recurved shape. The reproductive structures include central stamens and a single style, which are tucked within the bell-shaped floral architecture. This species is distinguished from similar fritillaries by the specific pattern of its spotting and its relatively compact growth habit.
