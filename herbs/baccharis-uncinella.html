Baccharis uncinella

Baccharis uncinella, commonly known as a medicinal shrub within the Asteraceae family, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Native to specific South American regions, this plant is traditionally utilized for its potent bioactive compounds. Research continues to explore its therapeutic potential in treating inflammation and various skin conditions through natural extracts.
