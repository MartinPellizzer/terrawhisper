Chrysopogon aciculatus

Chrysopogon aciculatus, commonly known as needle-leaf chrysopogon, is a resilient medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique bioactive properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammatory conditions and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and herbal therapy.

  • Scientific name: Chrysopogon aciculatus
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, specifically as a member of the family Poaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Chrysopogon, with its specific species name being aciculatus.
