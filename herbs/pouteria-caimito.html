Pouteria caiquita, scientifically known as Pouteria caimito, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree native to Central and South America. Renowned for its sweet, dark-fleshed fruit, this species is valued in traditional medicine for its antioxidant properties and ability to support digestive health, offering significant potential for holistic wellness.
