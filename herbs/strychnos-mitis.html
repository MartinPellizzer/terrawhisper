Yellow Bitterberry

Strychnos mitis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (9 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Yellow Bitterberry, scientifically known as Strychnos mitis, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical African forests. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized for their potent bioactive compounds. Often used to treat ailments like malaria and skin infections, it remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical research.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Sierra Leone. Moving towards the center of the continent, it is found within the vast landscapes of the DR Congo in West-Central Tropical Africa. The species also extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting Ethiopia and the areas of Sudan and South Sudan.
