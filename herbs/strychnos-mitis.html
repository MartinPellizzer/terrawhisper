Strychnos mitis

Yellow bitterberry

Strychnos mitis, commonly known as a member of the Loganiaceae family, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical rainforests. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, this woody climber is traditionally utilized in various ethnobotanical practices. Research focuses on its bioactive compounds, which offer potential therapeutic applications in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Strychnos mitis
  • Family: Loganiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Loganiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Strychnos.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Sierra Leone. Moving towards the center of the continent, it is found within the vast landscapes of the DR Congo in West-Central Tropical Africa. The species also extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting Ethiopia and the areas of Sudan and South Sudan. Finally, its range stretches into East Tropical Africa, where it can be located in Kenya.
