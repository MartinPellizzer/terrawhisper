Yellow Bitterberry

Strychnos mitis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (9 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Yellow Bitterberry, scientifically known as Strychnos mitis, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical African forests. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized for their potent bioactive compounds. Often used to treat ailments like malaria and skin infections, it remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical research.
