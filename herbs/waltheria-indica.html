Waltheria indica

Waltheria indica, commonly known as the "Indian Waltheria," is a rare, medicinal herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic medicine to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and inflammation, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Waltheria indica
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae and is placed within the genus Waltheria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning both the Macaronesian islands and parts of West Tropical Africa. Within the Macaronesian region, it can be found growing in the Canary Islands and Cape Verde. Its range extends further into West Tropical Africa, where it is present in Benin. Additionally, the species is documented in the landlocked nation of Burkina. Finally, its distribution reaches the coastal area of Gambia, completing its spread across these distinct territories.
