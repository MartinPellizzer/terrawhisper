Prunus

The cherries

Prunus, commonly known as stone fruit, encompasses a diverse genus including cherries, plums, and apricots. Beyond their nutritional value, various species offer medicinal potential. Traditionally, their bark and leaves have been utilized for their anti-inflammatory, sedative, and antioxidant properties, providing a rich natural resource for holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Prunus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Prunus.
