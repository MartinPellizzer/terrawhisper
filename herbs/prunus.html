Distribution

This plant, known as Prunus, exhibits a specific distribution throughout the regions of Northern Europe. In the western reaches of the continent, it can be found growing in Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the Scandinavian Peninsula, the species is established in Norway. It also maintains a presence in the island nation of Denmark. Finally, its geographical range extends further east into the territory of Finland.
