Plant Parts

This plant, Maytenus aquifolium, commonly known as the "bitter leaf," utilizes its leaves as the primary component in traditional medicinal practices. The leaves are characterized by their small, ovate shape and serrated margins, containing various bioactive compounds that are extracted for their therapeutic properties. In many herbal remedies, these leaves are prepared as decoctions or infusions to address various ailments, making them the most significant part of the plant for medicinal use.
