Salvia chinensis

Salvia chinensis, commonly known as Chinese sage, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, this aromatic plant features delicate blue flowers and serrated leaves. It is widely utilized for its cooling properties, offering therapeutic benefits in treating inflammation, fever, and various respiratory ailments effectively.

  • Scientific name: Salvia chinensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Lamiales and belongs to the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Salvia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Salvia chinensis, is identified by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans, zh), it is referred to as 华鼠尾草, while in Traditional Chinese (zh-hant, zh-hk) and the standard Chinese (zh) designation, it is known as 華鼠尾草. Additionally, in Arabic (ar), the plant is called قصعين تشيني.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout specific regions of Eastern Asia. In mainland China, its presence is most notable within the South-Central territories. Additionally, it can be located across the Southeast regions of China. The species also extends its reach to the island of Taiwan. Together, these locations define the core geographical range of Salvia chinensis.
