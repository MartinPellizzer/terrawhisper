Clausena heptaphylla

Clausena heptaphylla, commonly known as the seven-leaf clausena, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat various ailments. Current research explores its bioactive compounds, highlighting its potential in developing natural therapeutic agents for modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Clausena heptaphylla
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is further categorized into the family Rutaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Clausena.
