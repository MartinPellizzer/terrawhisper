Eugenia

Eugenia, commonly known as clove or various species within the Myrtaceae family, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and antioxidants, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health, combat inflammation, and provide antimicrobial benefits, making it a cornerstone in traditional wellness.

  • Scientific name: Eugenia
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is categorized within the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae, specifically classified under the genus Eugenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In the Macaronesian islands, it can be specifically found within the Canary Islands. Its presence extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, where it inhabits various territories. Specifically, the species is documented in the nations of Benin, Burkina, and Ghana.
