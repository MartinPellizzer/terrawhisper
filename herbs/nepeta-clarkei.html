Nepeta clarkei

Nepeta clarkei, commonly known as Clary Nep, is a perennial herb valued for its aromatic, mint-like foliage. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this plant is prized in traditional herbalism for its calming properties and essential oils, offering a soothing botanical remedy for relaxation and mild stress relief.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
