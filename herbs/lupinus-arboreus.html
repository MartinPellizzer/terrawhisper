Lupinus arboreus

Lupinus arboreus, commonly known as the Tree Lupin, is a striking perennial shrub prized for its tall, architectural spikes of vibrant flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this resilient plant plays a vital role in nitrogen fixation, enhancing soil fertility and supporting diverse ecosystems within its natural montane habitats.

  • Scientific name: Lupinus arboreus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Lupinus, specifically as the species Lupinus arboreus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions in the Southern and Northern Hemispheres. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing within the territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Its presence extends to Australia, where it is specifically documented in Tasmania and Victoria. Additionally, the species is established in the northern parts of New Zealand. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse temperate environments.
