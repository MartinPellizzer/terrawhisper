Veronica polita

Veronica polita, commonly known as the polished speedwell, is a delicate perennial herb native to specific regions of Asia. Characterized by its glossy foliage and subtle blooms, this plant holds significance in traditional ethnobotany. It is often studied for its potential medicinal properties and its unique role in local ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Veronica polita
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is positioned in the family Plantaginaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Veronica.
