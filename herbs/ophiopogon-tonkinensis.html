Ophiopogon tonkinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Ophiopogon tonkinensis?

Ophiopogon tonkinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales. This plant is a part of the family Asparagaceae, situated within the genus Ophiopogon, and is specifically identified by its species name, tonkinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ophiopogon tonkinensis has narrow, linear, grass-like leaves that typically measure 2 to 4 millimeters in width and grow in dense, tufted clumps. These dark green foliage blades are somewhat fleshy and can reach lengths of up to 20 centimeters. The plant produces small, inconspicuous white to pale lavender flowers that emerge from the base of the leaf clusters. Following the flowering stage, it develops small, spherical, dark blue to black berries that are characteristic of the genus. The overall growth habit is low-spreading, creating a thick groundcover through its fibrous root system.
