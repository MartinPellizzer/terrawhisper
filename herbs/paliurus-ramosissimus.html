Thorny Wingnut, scientifically known as Paliurus ramosissimus, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its dense, thorny architecture, this hardy plant serves various traditional medicinal purposes. Its leaves and stems are frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive ailments.
