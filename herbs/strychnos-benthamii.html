Strychnos benthamii

Strychnos minor

Strychnos benthamii, commonly known as a medicinal woody climber, is a significant plant species within the Loganiaceae family. Found primarily in tropical African forests, it holds traditional importance due to its potent alkaloids. Researchers study its bioactive compounds for potential pharmacological applications, particularly in developing novel therapeutic agents.

  • Scientific name: Strychnos benthamii
  • Family: Loganiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Loganiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Strychnos.
