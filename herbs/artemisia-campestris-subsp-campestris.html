Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Artemisia as the subspecies campestris.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is well-documented in Denmark and Finland. It can also be found inhabiting the landscapes of both Sweden and Great Britain. Moving toward the center of the continent, the species extends its reach into Middle Europe. Specifically, its range includes the mountainous and temperate terrains of Austria.

Plant Parts

This plant, Artemisia campestris subsp. campestris, is primarily utilized for its therapeutic properties derived from its aerial parts, which include the stems, leaves, and flowering structures above the ground. These components are particularly valued because they serve as the primary reservoir for the plant's essential oil. This concentrated volatile oil, extracted from the foliage, contains the bioactive compounds responsible for the plant's distinct aromatic profile and its medicinal efficacy in traditional applications.
