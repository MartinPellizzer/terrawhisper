Qualea parviflora

Qualea parviflora, commonly known as "jacarandá-do-campo," is a prominent medicinal tree native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its bark is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern botanical research and treatments.

  • Scientific name: Qualea parviflora
  • Family: Vochysiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
