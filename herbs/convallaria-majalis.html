Lily-of-the-valley, scientifically known as Convallaria majalis, is a delicate perennial prized for its fragrant, bell-shaped white flowers. While widely used in perfumery, it is a potent medicinal plant containing cardiac glycosides. Historically utilized for heart ailments, it must be handled with extreme caution due to its high toxicity.
