Convallaria majalis

Convallaria majalis, commonly known as Lily of the Valley, is a delicate woodland perennial prized for its fragrant, bell-shaped flowers. Historically used in traditional medicine for its cardiac glycosides, it possesses potent properties that influence heart function. However, due to its high toxicity, it must be handled with extreme caution.

  • Scientific name: Convallaria majalis
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Convallaria majalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
