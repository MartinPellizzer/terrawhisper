Persicaria minor

Persicaria minor, commonly known as Small Knotweed, is a resilient herbaceous plant frequently found in damp, shady habitats. Valued in traditional medicine for its restorative properties, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and skin ailments. This versatile botanical offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and herbal remedies.

  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe
