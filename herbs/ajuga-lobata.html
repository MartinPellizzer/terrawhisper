Ajuga lobata

Ajuga lobata, commonly known as bugleweed, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is frequently utilized to support respiratory health and promote overall wellness, serving as a vital resource in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Ajuga lobata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae and falls under the genus Ajuga.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the Asian continent. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central territories and the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found extensively across India. Furthermore, its range extends into the specific regions of Assam and the East Himalaya. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various mountainous and subtropical environments.
