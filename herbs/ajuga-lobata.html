Ajuga lobata

What's the taxonomical classification of Ajuga lobata?

Ajuga lobata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the biological lineage that leads to the order Lamiales. It is further categorized under the family Lamiaceae, which places it within the well-known mint family. Ultimately, this plant is identified by its specific genus, Ajuga, and its unique species designation, lobata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ajuga lobata has a creeping growth habit characterized by stolons that produce basal rosettes of deeply lobed, ovate leaves with serrated margins. The leaves typically exhibit a textured surface and can vary in color from deep green to reddish tones depending on environmental conditions. As the plant matures, it sends up upright flowering spikes that bear small, tubular flowers arranged in dense verticillasters. These blossoms are often blue or violet and emerge from the center of the foliage clusters. The overall structure allows the plant to form dense mats through its spreading rhizomatous or stoloniferous network.
