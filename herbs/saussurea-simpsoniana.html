Saussurea simpsoniana

Saussurea simpsoniana, commonly known as a rare alpine medicinal herb, thrives in high-altitude Himalayan ecosystems. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties. Its specialized survival mechanisms in harsh climates offer significant potential for contemporary pharmacological research and development.
