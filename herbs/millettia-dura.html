Millettia dura

Millettia dura, commonly known as the Hardwood Milletia, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Found primarily in tropical regions, its bark, leaves, and roots are utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering potent bioactive compounds with potential antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits.

  • Scientific name: Millettia dura
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized into the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its scientific classification is defined by the genus Millettia, specifically the species Millettia dura.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the tropical regions of the African continent.
