American licorice

American licorice, scientifically known as Glycyrrhiza lepidota, is a perennial herb native to North American prairies. Highly valued for its sweet, aromatic root, it contains glycyrrhizin, offering potent anti-inflammatory and soothing properties. Historically used in traditional medicine, it remains a significant botanical resource for respiratory and digestive support.
