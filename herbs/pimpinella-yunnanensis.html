Pimpinella yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Pimpinella yunnanensis?

Pimpinella yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further categorized under the order Apiales. It is a member of the family Apiaceae, falling within the genus Pimpinella, and is specifically identified by the species name yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pimpinella yunnanensis has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by erect stems, pinnately compound leaves with serrated leaflets, and white umbels composed of small, five-petaled flowers. The leaves are typically arranged in an alternate pattern along the stem and possess distinctively textured surfaces. Each flowering umbel consists of multiple smaller umbellets that emerge from a common peduncle. The plant produces small, dry fruits known as schizocarps which contain longitudinal ribs. These structural features are diagnostic for distinguishing this species within the Apiaceae family.
