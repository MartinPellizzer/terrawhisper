Zygophyllum scabrum

Zygophyllum scabrum, commonly known as the scurvy bush, is a resilient succulent native to arid regions of Southern Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and skin irritations, making it a vital resource in ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Zygophyllum scabrum
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zygophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Zygophyllaceae and falls under the genus Zygophyllum.

Common Names

This plant, Zygophyllum scabrum, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the scabrous caltrop, while in Afrikaans, it is known as skraalstert. Additionally, in isiXhosa, the plant is called iintshatshane.

Distribution

This plant is found across several distinct regions within Northern Africa. Its distribution includes the expansive landscapes of Algeria and Egypt. Furthermore, it can be located throughout the territory of Libya. The species also inhabits parts of Morocco and Tunisia. Consequently, its presence is widespread across these five North African nations.
