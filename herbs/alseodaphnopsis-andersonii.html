Alseodaphnopsis andersonii

Alseodaphnopsis andersonii, commonly known as the Anderson's shrub, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this species is studied for its potential therapeutic properties. Understanding its botanical characteristics and traditional uses is essential for advancing natural pharmacology and conservation efforts.

  • Scientific name: Alseodaphnopsis andersonii
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Laurales, it is classified under the family Lauraceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Alséodaphnopsis.
