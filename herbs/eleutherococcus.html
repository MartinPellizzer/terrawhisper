Five-leaved aralia, scientifically known as Eleutherococcus, is a powerful adaptogen native to East Asia. Often called Siberian ginseng, this resilient shrub is celebrated in traditional medicine for enhancing physical stamina, reducing fatigue, and boosting immune function. It serves as a natural remedy to help the body manage physiological stress.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution primarily focused within the regions of the Russian Far East. It can be found growing across the Amur territory and throughout the Khabarovsk region. Furthermore, its presence extends to the coastal areas of Primorye and the island of Sakhalin. Beyond Russia, the species also inhabits parts of China, specifically within the South-Central region. Collectively, these locations define the natural habitat where this medicinal species thrives.
