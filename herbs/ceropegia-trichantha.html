What's the taxonomical classification of Ceropegia trichantha?

Ceropegia trichantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic descent further, it falls under the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Ceropegia, and its unique species designation, trichantha.

How is this plant cultivated?

Because it is a caudiciform species, it is essential to prevent moisture from sitting around the base of the swollen stem to avoid rot.
