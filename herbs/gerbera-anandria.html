What's the taxonomical classification of Gerbera anandria?

Gerbera anandria belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales. This plant is a specific representative of the family Asteraceae, a group known for its complex flower heads. Within this family, it is placed in the genus Gerbera, and its unique identity is defined by its specific designation as the species anandria.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

The overall structure of the plant is low-growing and herbaceous, focusing its energy on the striking floral display.
