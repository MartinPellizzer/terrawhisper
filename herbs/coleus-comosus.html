Coleus comosus

Coleus comosus, commonly known as Coleus or Mayana, is a vibrant medicinal plant celebrated for its striking foliage and therapeutic properties. Widely used in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory qualities, often utilized to treat respiratory issues, skin ailments, and digestive discomfort through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Coleus comosus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling within the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Coleus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the African continent, primarily concentrated in the tropical regions. In Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the territories of Eritrea and Ethiopia. Its presence extends further south into East Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Kenya. Additionally, the species is known to inhabit the landscapes of Tanzania. Finally, its geographical range includes the country of Uganda, completing its regional footprint.
