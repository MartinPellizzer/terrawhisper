Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of the Americas. In North America, its presence is documented in the Southeast region of Mexico. Moving into Central America, the species can be found throughout the territory of Belize. Its range further extends southward to include the country of Honduras. Finally, the distribution reaches into Costa Rica and Panamá, completing its regional footprint.
