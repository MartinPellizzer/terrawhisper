Psephellus sibiricus

Psephellus sibiricus, commonly known as Siberian thistle, is a resilient medicinal herb native to the harsh environments of Central Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, support digestive health, and promote overall systemic wellness through its phytochemical richness.

  • Scientific name: Psephellus sibiricus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Psephellus, specifically as the species Psephellus sibiricus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of Northern and Central Asia. In Eastern Europe, its presence is noted throughout East European Russia. The species is also well-established in Siberia, specifically within the Altay and West Siberia regions. Moving into Central Asia, it can be found growing in Kazakhstan. Finally, its geographical range extends into China, particularly within the Xinjiang province.
