The ecology of Simaba obovata is defined by its preference for specific humid forest ecosystems, occurring primarily within Floresta de Igapó, Floresta de Várzea, and Floresta Ombrófila (also known as Floresta Pluvial). This species inhabits low-lying tropical environments where water availability is a key factor, ranging from seasonally flooded areas to perennially moist rainforests. Its distribution is restricted to relatively low altitudes, with an elevational range situated between 60 m AMSL and a maximum of 200 m AMSL.
