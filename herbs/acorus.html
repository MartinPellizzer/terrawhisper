Acorus, scientifically known as Acorus calamus, is a versatile perennial herb valued in traditional medicine for centuries. Often called sweet flag, its aromatic rhizomes possess significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and sedative effects. Used widely in Ayurvedic practices, it serves as a potent remedy for digestive and neurological ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a distinct distribution across various regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Its presence also extends to the remote islands of Føroyar and Iceland. Furthermore, the species is well-established within the territory of Great Britain. Collectively, these locations define its primary geographical range in the northern part of the continent.
