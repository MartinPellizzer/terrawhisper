Jacobaea minuta

Jacobaea minuta, commonly known as Ragwort, is a perennial herbaceous plant belonging to the Asteraceae family. Characterized by its bright yellow, daisy-like flowers, it thrives in disturbed soils and grasslands. While ecologically significant, it contains pyrrolizidine alkaloids, making it toxic to livestock if ingested during grazing.

  • Scientific name: Jacobaea minuta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Jacobaea.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Jacobaea minuta, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently called small ragwort or dwarf ragwort, while in German, it is known as Kleines Greiskraut. In French, the plant is called petite séneçon, and in Spanish, it is referred to as senecio pequeño.
