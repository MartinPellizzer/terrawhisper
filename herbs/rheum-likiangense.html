Rheum likiangense

What's the taxonomical classification of Rheum likiangense?

Rheum likiangense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further classified under the order Caryophyllales. It is a member of the family Polygonaceae, falling specifically within the genus Rheum, with its unique designation as the species likiangense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rheum likiangense has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by thick, fleshy rhizomes and large, cordate to ovate leaves with prominent venation. The leaf blades are typically broad and often possess a slightly wavy or sinuate margin. During the flowering stage, the plant produces erect, branched inflorescences that emerge from the center of the rosette. These panicles consist of numerous small, inconspicuous flowers that are often clustered in dense cymes. The overall structure is robust, reflecting its adaptation to specific montane or alpine environments.
