Parkia speciosa

Parkia speciosa, commonly known as bitter bean or petai, is a tropical legume native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its pungent aroma and unique flavor, this plant is a dietary staple in many cultures. Beyond its culinary uses, it offers significant nutritional value and various traditional medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Parkia speciosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Parkia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is specifically noted in Thailand. Moving into the Malesia zone, it can be found throughout the island of Borneo. Its habitat also extends to Jawa and the Malay Peninsula. Finally, the species is distributed across the Philippine archipelago.
