Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as tarragon, is a versatile herb valued for both its culinary and therapeutic properties. The most recognizable part used in cooking is the aromatic tarragon spice, which is derived from the slender, green leaf structures of the plant. Beyond its use as a seasoning, the plant is a source of potent essential oil, which contains the concentrated aromatic compounds responsible for its distinct scent. In medicinal applications, leaf extracts are frequently utilized to harness the plant's bioactive components for various health benefits.
