tarragon, scientifically known as Artemisia dracunculus, is a perennial herb cherished for its aromatic culinary and medicinal properties. Renowned for its distinct anise-like flavor, it has been used historically to aid digestion, reduce inflammation, and support metabolic health. This versatile plant remains a staple in both kitchens and herbalism.
