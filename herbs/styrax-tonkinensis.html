Lao benzointree, scientifically known as Styrax tonkinensis, is a remarkable medicinal tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for producing high-quality benzoin resin, it offers significant therapeutic benefits. This resin is traditionally utilized in aromatherapy and topical treatments to soothe skin irritations, reduce inflammation, and promote respiratory wellness.
