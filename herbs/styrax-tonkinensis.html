Styrax tonkinensis

Styrax tonkinenensis, commonly known as the Tonkin storax tree, is a precious medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for producing fragrant resin, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine for its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and soothing properties, playing a vital role in both ancient remedies and modern aromatherapy practices.

  • Scientific name: Styrax tonkinensis
  • Family: Styracaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Ericales and the family Styracaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Styrax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across parts of Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central and Southeast regions. Its range extends further into the Indo-China peninsula. In this area, the species is documented in Cambodia and Laos. Additionally, the plant is commonly distributed throughout Thailand.

Plant Parts

This plant, Styrax tonkinensis, is primarily valued for its medicinal and aromatic properties derived from its resin. This viscous substance is exuded from the bark of the tree and is harvested through a process of tapping the trunk. Once collected and processed, the resin serves as the active component used in various traditional remedies, often utilized for its antiseptic and soothing qualities in topical or internal applications.
