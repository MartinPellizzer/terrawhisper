Silkrubber

Funtumia elastica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (26 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Silkrubber, scientifically known as Funtumia elastica, is a versatile evergreen tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned primarily for its high-quality latex used in rubber production, this medicinal plant also holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammation.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the lush landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Within this specific region, it can be found growing in the diverse ecosystems of Benin. Its natural range also extends through the territories of Ghana and Guinea. Furthermore, the species is well-established within the tropical forests of Ivory Coast. Finally, its geographical presence includes the coastal and inland areas of Liberia.
