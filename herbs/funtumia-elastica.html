Funtumia elastica

Funtumia elastica, commonly known as the rubber tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to African rainforests. Renowned for its latex, it also contains bioactive alkaloids with diverse therapeutic potential. Traditionally used to treat various ailments, modern research explores its pharmacological properties, particularly for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities.

  • Scientific name: Funtumia elastica
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Funtumia.

This plant is primarily distributed across the lush landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Within this specific region, it can be found growing in the diverse ecosystems of Benin.
