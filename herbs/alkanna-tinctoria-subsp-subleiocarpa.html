Alkanna tinctoria subsp. subleiocarpa

Alkaannna tinctoria subsp. subleiocarpia, commonly known as Alkanet, is a remarkable medicinal herb valued for its vibrant pigment and therapeutic properties. Traditionally utilized in herbalism, this subspecies is prized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities, often integrated into soothing topical salves and oils to support skin health.
