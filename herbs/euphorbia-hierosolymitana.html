Euphorbia hierosolymitana

Euphorbia hierosolymitana, commonly known as a rare Mediterranean spurge, is a distinctive medicinal plant endemic to specific North African regions. Characterized by its unique succulent structure, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Researchers study its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications, particularly in treating skin conditions and inflammatory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia hierosolymitana
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
