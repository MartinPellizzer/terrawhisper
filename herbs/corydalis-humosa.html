Corydalis humosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Corydalis humosa?

Corydalis humoza belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Ranunculales. This plant is a member of the family Papaveraceae, specifically falling under the genus Corydalis, with its unique designation as the species humosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Its reproductive organs are housed within specialized blossoms featuring a distinct nectar spur extending from the base of the petals.
