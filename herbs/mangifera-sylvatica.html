Mangifera sylvatica

What's the taxonomical classification of Mangifera sylvatica?

Mangifera sylvatica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Sapindales and is a member of the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Mangifera, and its unique species designation, sylvatica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Mangifera sylvatica has a medium to large tree habit characterized by an erect trunk, spreading crown, and lanceolate leaves that are typically coriaceous with an acute apex. The leaves are dark green on the upper surface and often exhibit a slightly paler underside. Its inflorescences consist of branched panicles that bear numerous small, yellowish flowers arranged in clusters. The fruits are drupes that vary in size and shape, typically featuring a fleshy mesocarp surrounding a single large seed. These morphological traits distinguish it from other members of the Anacardiaceae family.
