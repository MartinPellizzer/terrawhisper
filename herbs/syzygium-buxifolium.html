Boxleaf Eugenia, scientifically known as Syzygium buxifolium, is a versatile medicinal shrub cherished in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it possesses potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. Often utilized to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, this resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and healing.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. Within China, it is specifically found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas.
