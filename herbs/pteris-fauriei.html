Pteris fauriei

What's the taxonomical classification of Pteris fauriei?

Pteris fauriei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. This fern is a representative of the family Pteridaceae, specifically situated within the genus Pteris, with its specific designation being the species fauriei.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pteris fauriei has a creeping rhizome that produces pinnate fronds measuring approximately 20 to 40 centimeters in length with distinctively serrated margins. The pinnae are arranged in an alternate pattern along the rachis and exhibit a somewhat leathery texture. Small, dark sori are located on the underside of the leaflets, typically positioned near the margins under a thin protective flap called an indusium. The stipe or leaf stalk is often dark or brownish in color, providing a sharp contrast to the green blade. These morphological features collectively help identify the species within its specific forest habitat.
