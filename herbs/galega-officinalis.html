Galega officinalis

Galega officinalis, commonly known as Goat's Rue, is a perennial herb historically revered for its hypoglycemic properties. Long used in traditional medicine to manage blood sugar levels, this flowering plant belongs to the Fabaceae family. Today, it remains a subject of botanical interest for its potential anti-diabetic therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Galega officinalis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Galega.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Galega officinalis, is a perennial herb whose medicinal properties are primarily derived from its aerial parts. The Galega herb consists of upright, flowering stems that bear trifoliate leaves and characteristic pea-like blossoms, typically ranging in color from blue to purple. When preparing medicinal extracts or decoctions, it is these above-ground structures—including the leaves, stems, and flowers—that are harvested and utilized for their bioactive compounds.
