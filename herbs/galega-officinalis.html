Goat's-rue, scientifically known as Galega officinalis, is a perennial herb with a rich history in traditional medicine. Renowned for its historical use in managing blood sugar levels, it contains bioactive compounds that may support metabolic health. This versatile plant continues to be studied for its potential therapeutic properties.
