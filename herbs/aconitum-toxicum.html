Aconitum toxicum

Aconitum toxicum, commonly known as monkshood or wolfsbane, is a highly potent medicinal plant belonging to the Ranunculaceae family. While revered in traditional medicine for its powerful alkaloid properties, its extreme toxicity requires expert handling. This introduction explores its historical uses, chemical composition, and critical safety precautions.
