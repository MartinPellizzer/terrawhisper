Pseudocedrela kotschyi

Pseudocedrela kotschyi, commonly known as African mahogany or Kotchyi, is a versatile medicinal tree native to West African savannahs. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections and inflammation to digestive issues and respiratory discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Pseudocedrela kotschyi
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pseudocedrela.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Pseudocedrela kotschyi, is referred to by various local names depending on the linguistic group. In the Fulfulde language, it is commonly called Boodi, while in Bambara, it is known as Sensanfin.
