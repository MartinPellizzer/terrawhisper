Himalayan Musk Rose, scientifically known as Rosa brunonii, is a fragrant, climbing shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its intense, sweet aroma and delicate pink blooms, this resilient species is valued in traditional medicine for its soothing properties and its unique, enchanting scent.
