Rosa brunonii

Rosa brunonii, commonly known as the Brunoni rose, is a distinctive climbing shrub native to the subtropical regions of South Africa. Renowned for its delicate, fragrant blossoms and vibrant petals, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in folk medicine for its therapeutic properties and its significant ornamental beauty.

  • Scientific name: Rosa brunonii
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Rosa.
