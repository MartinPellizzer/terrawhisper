Podocarpus elongatus

Podocarpus elongatus, commonly known as the elongated yellowwood, is a resilient evergreen conifer native to South Africa. Valued for its striking architectural form and dense foliage, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various ethnobotanical practices, offering unique biochemical properties that hold significant interest for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Podocarpus elongatus
  • Family: Podocarpaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, falling into the family Podocarpaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Podocarpus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Podocarpus elongatus, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Afrikaans, it is called Breëriviergeelhout, while in Arabic, it is known as معلاقة متطاولة. In Chinese, the names vary slightly by script and region, including 好望角羅漢松果 (zh), 好望角罗汉松果 (zh-hans), and 好望角罗汉松果 (zh-cn).

Distribution

This plant is found across several distinct regions within the Southern and South Tropical Africa areas. Specifically, its range includes the countries of Malawi, Zambia, and Zimbabwe. It is also known to inhabit the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. Remarkably, the distribution extends far beyond the mainland to the middle of the Atlantic Ocean. There, it can be located on the remote island of St. Helena.
