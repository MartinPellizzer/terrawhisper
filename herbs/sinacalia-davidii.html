Sinacalia davidii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is further categorized under the order Asterales and falls within the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Sinacalia, and its species designation, davidii.
