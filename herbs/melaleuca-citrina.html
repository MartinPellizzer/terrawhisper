Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several continents and distinct ecological zones. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is noted within the northwestern Balkan Peninsula. Its range extends significantly into East Tropical Africa, specifically within the countries of Kenya and Tanzania. Furthermore, the species can be found in the Western Indian Ocean, particularly around the Comoros. Finally, the plant reaches the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in the region of Assam.
