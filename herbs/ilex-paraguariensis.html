Yerba mate, scientifically known as Ilex paraguariensis, is a stimulating medicinal plant native to subtropical forests in South America. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it serves as a traditional infusion used to boost energy, enhance mental focus, and support digestive health through its rich profile of bioactive compounds.
