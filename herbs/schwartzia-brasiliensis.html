Schwartzia brasiliensis

Norantea brasiliensis

Schwartzia brasiliensis, commonly known as pau-de-pau, is a medicinal tree native to Brazil. Widely utilized in traditional ethnobotany, its bark and leaves possess significant therapeutic potential. Research highlights its bioactive compounds, which offer promising antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Family: Marcgraviaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification
