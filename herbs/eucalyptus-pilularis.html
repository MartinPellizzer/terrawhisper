Blackbutt

Eucalyptus pilularis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (11 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Blackbutt, scientifically known as Eucalyptus pilularis, is a majestic evergreen native to Australia's eastern coasts. Renowned for its durable timber, it also holds significant medicinal value. Its leaves contain essential oils with potent antimicrobial and decongestant properties, traditionally used to alleviate respiratory ailments and soothe inflammatory skin conditions.
