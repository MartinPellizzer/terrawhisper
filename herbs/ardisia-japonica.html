Marlberry, scientifically known as Ardisia japonica, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its glossy leaves and small red berries, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties to treat various ailments and skin conditions.
