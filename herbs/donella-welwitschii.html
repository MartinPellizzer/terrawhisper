Donella welwitschii

Donella welwitschii, commonly known as the Bitterleaf plant, is a potent medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments, reduce inflammation, and manage fever. This resilient species serves as a vital natural resource for holistic wellness.
