Phyllanthus

Clambus

Phyllanthus, commonly known as Leafflower, is a diverse genus of flowering plants widely revered in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to support liver health, manage inflammation, and combat oxidative stress. This versatile botanical holds significant promise in modern pharmacological research and herbal wellness.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Phyllanthus, is a diverse genus of medicinal herbs where various tissues are studied for their therapeutic potential. While the leaves, roots, and seeds are the primary parts traditionally used in herbal remedies to treat liver ailments and inflammation, modern biotechnological research often focuses on the cellular level. In laboratory settings, scientists utilize tissue culture techniques to induce the formation of a callus, a mass of unorganized parenchyma cells, which serves as a vital medium for producing high concentrations of bioactive compounds like phyllanthin and euomylin.
