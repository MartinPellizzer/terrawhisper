Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Phyllanthus, is a diverse genus of medicinal herbs where various tissues are studied for their therapeutic potential. While the leaves, roots, and seeds are the primary parts traditionally used in herbal remedies to treat liver ailments and inflammation, modern biotechnological research often focuses on the cellular level. In laboratory settings, scientists utilize tissue culture techniques to induce the formation of a callus, a mass of unorganized parenchyma cells, which serves as a vital medium for producing high concentrations of bioactive compounds like phyllanthin and euomylin.
