Phyllanthus

Phyllanthus, commonly known as Leafflower, is a diverse genus of flowering plants widely revered in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to support liver health, manage inflammation, and combat oxidative stress. This versatile botanical holds significant promise in modern pharmacological research and herbal wellness.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found specifically within Sardegna.
