Scrophularia striata

Scrophularia striata, commonly known as striped figwort, is a distinctive perennial herb native to various temperate regions. Characterized by its unique, spotted flower patterns and upright stems, this medicinal plant has historically been utilized in traditional herbalism for its purported anti-inflammatory and lymphatic-supporting properties within various folk remedies.

  • Scientific name: Scrophularia striata
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is specifically placed in the family Scrophulariaceae. Ultimately, it is identified under the genus Scrophularia as Scrophularia striata.
