Carlina acanthifolia

Carlina acanthifolia, commonly known as the thistle-leaved carline, is a resilient perennial herb native to rocky European terrains. Highly valued in traditional herbalism, this plant features distinctive, spiky foliage and striking composite flowers. It is primarily studied for its potent bioactive compounds and diverse therapeutic applications in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Carlina acanthifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Carlina.
