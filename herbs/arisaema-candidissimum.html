Arisaema candidissimum

What's the taxonomical classification of Arisaema candidissimum?

Arisaema candidissimum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, the plant is placed in the order Alismatales and the family Araceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Arisaema, and its specific species designation, candidissimum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Arisaema candidissimum has a single, large, cordate leaf that emerges from a subterranean corm and features a prominent sheath-like structure known as a spathe. This spathe is typically creamy white or pale green, enclosing a fleshy, upright spike called a spadix. The spadix serves to attract pollinators through its specific color and texture. Small, inconspicuous flowers are nestled at the base of this spadix within the protected chamber of the spathe. As the plant matures, the leaf and spathe eventually wither to leave behind a fleshy berry.
