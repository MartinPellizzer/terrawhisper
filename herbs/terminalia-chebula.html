Terminalia chebula

Terminalia chebulu, commonly known as Haritaki in Ayurveda, is a cornerstone of traditional medicine. Often referred to as the "king of medicines," this versatile fruit is celebrated for its potent antioxidant and digestive properties. It plays a vital role in detoxification, supporting overall immunity and promoting long-term wellness.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia chebula
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Within these groups, it is identified by the genus Terminalia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia and Africa. In the Indian Subcontinent, it is notably found within the area of Assam. Its presence extends into Eastern Asia, where it can be located in Taiwan. The species is also widely distributed throughout China, specifically across the South-Central and Southeast regions. Additionally, it grows in West-Central Tropical Africa, particularly within the DR Congo.
