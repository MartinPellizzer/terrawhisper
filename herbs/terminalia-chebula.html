Chebulic myrobalan, scientifically known as Terminalia chebulla, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned as the "king of medicines," this potent fruit offers diverse therapeutic benefits, including digestive support, antioxidant properties, and immune boosting. It is highly valued for its ability to balance bodily doshas and promote holistic wellness.
