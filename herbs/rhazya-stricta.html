Rhazya stricta

Rhazya stricta, commonly known as the "Rhazy plant," is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Scientific interest focuses on its bioactive compounds, which show potential in pharmacological research and therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Rhazya stricta
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rhazya, specifically as the species Rhazya stricta.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution primarily focused within the arid regions of Western Asia and the Arabian Peninsula. In Western Asia, its presence is documented across Afghanistan, Iran, and Iraq. Moving toward the Arabian Peninsula, the species can be found throughout the various Gulf States.
