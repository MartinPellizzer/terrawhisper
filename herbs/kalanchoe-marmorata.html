Kalanchoe marmorata

Kalanchoe marmorata, commonly known as the Marble Kalanchoe, is a striking succulent prized for its distinctive, marbled foliage. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potential therapeutic properties. Exploring its unique biological characteristics offers fascinating insights into the diverse world of medicinal succulents.

  • Scientific name: Kalanchoe marmorata
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is a member of the family Crassulaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Kalanchoe as the species Kalanchoe marmorata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several key regions across the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence has been documented in Tunisia. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found in both Rwanda and the DR Congo. The plant also extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting the territories of Eritrea and Ethiopia.
