Combretum leprosum

Combretum leprosum, commonly known as the "wild marula" or "leprosy bush," is a significant medicinal shrub native to southern Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, showcasing its vital role in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Combretum leprosum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Myrtales and is a member of the family Combretaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Combretum, which includes the medicinal species Combretum leprosum.
