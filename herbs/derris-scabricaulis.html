What's the taxonomical classification of Derris scabricaulis?

Derris scabricaulis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its hierarchical progression, it is placed in the order Fabales and belongs to the family Fabaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific genus, Derris, and its unique species designation, scabricaulis.

What traditional systems uses this plant?

This plant, Derris scabricaulis, has been historically utilized by indigenous communities in Southeast Asia as a natural botanical insecticide to control various insect pests.

This method provided a reliable way to protect agricultural yields from infestations before the widespread availability of synthetic pesticides.

What side effects this plant can have?

The primary danger lies in the inhibition of mitochondrial respiration, which can lead to systemic cellular damage in humans. If accidental ingestion occurs, symptoms might include nausea, vomiting, or more serious neurological impairments. Because it is a potent natural pesticide, even small doses can be hazardous to both humans and local wildlife.
