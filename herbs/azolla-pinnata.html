Azolla pinnata

Water velvet

Azolla pinnata, commonly known as mosquito fern, is a tiny, free-floating aquatic fern celebrated for its remarkable nitrogen-fixing capabilities. Often used as a biofertilizer in rice paddies, this versatile plant plays a crucial role in sustainable agriculture, enhancing soil fertility and providing a nutrient-rich feed for livestock and fish.

  • Scientific name: Azolla pinnata
  • Family: Salviniaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

This plant, Azolla pinnata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae, it is classified under the order Salviniales and the family Salviniaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Azolla.
