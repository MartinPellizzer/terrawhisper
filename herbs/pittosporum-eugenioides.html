Pittosporum eugenioides

Pittosporum eugenioides, commonly known as the Cheesewood, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to various tropical regions. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal importance. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in treating diverse ailments and skin conditions.
