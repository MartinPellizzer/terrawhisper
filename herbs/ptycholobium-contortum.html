Ptycholobium contortum

Ptycholobium contortum, commonly known as the twisted acacia, is a resilient medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and digestive issues. Its unique chemical composition offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research.
