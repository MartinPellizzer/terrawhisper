Ethiopian Cardamom, scientifically known as Aframomum corrorima, is a highly aromatic medicinal plant native to the Ethiopian highlands. Valued for its unique spicy flavor, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, respiratory issues, and inflammation, serving as both a culinary staple and a potent herbal remedy.
