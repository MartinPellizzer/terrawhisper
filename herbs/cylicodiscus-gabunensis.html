Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Within this specific region, its presence has been documented in Ghana, Ivory Coast, and Nigeria. The species also extends into the West-Central Tropical Africa zone. In this central area, it can be found growing in the Central African Republic and Cameroon. Consequently, the geographical range of Cylicodiscus gabunenensis spans a significant portion of the African tropical belt.
