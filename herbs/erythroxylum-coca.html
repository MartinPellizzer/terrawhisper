Coca, scientifically known as Erythroxylum coca, is a perennial shrub native to the Andean regions of South America. Historically revered by indigenous cultures, its leaves have been used for centuries in traditional medicine to alleviate fatigue, hunger, and altitude sickness, serving as a profound cultural and botanical cornerstone.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Erythroxylum coca, is primarily valued for the chemical properties found within its foliage. The most significant part of the species is the coca leaf, which has been used for centuries in traditional Andean medicine to alleviate fatigue and altitude sickness. Specifically, the medicinal alkaloids are concentrated in the green leaves of the shrub. When processed or chewed, these coca leaves serve as a natural stimulant and anesthetic. Whether referred to in a botanical context or a cultural one, the use of Coca leaves remains a central element of indigenous healing practices in South America.
