Coca, scientifically known as Erythroxylum coca, is a perennial shrub native to the Andean regions of South America. Historically revered by indigenous cultures, its leaves have been used for centuries in traditional medicine to alleviate fatigue, hunger, and altitude sickness, serving as a profound cultural and botanical cornerstone.
