Swertia yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Swertia yunnanensis?

Swertia yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianales. This plant is a representative of the family Gentianaceae, specifically categorized under the genus Swertia, with the unique species designation yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Swertia yunnanensis has opposite, decussate leaves that are elliptic to lanceolate in shape with entire margins and acute apices. The plant typically grows as a small shrub or subshrub reaching heights of up to one meter. Its flowers are small, white, and tubular, arranged in dense axillary clusters. The fruit consists of woody, five-valved capsules that contain numerous tiny, winged seeds for dispersal. The stems are often woody at the base and can exhibit a slightly quadrangular structure.
