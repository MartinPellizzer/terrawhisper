Vicia cretica

Vicia cretica, commonly known as Cretan vetch, is a resilient herbaceous plant native to the Mediterranean region. Often found in rocky terrains, it plays a vital role in local ecosystems. Beyond its ecological significance, this species is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique bioactive properties and medicinal potential.

  • Scientific name: Vicia cretica
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized into the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Vicia.

This plant is primarily located within the region of Southeastern Europe. Specifically, its natural habitat includes various areas across Greece.
