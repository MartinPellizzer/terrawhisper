What's the taxonomical classification of Oreocnide obovata?

Oreocnide obovata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Rosales. This plant is a member of the family Urticaceae, which places it within the genus Oreocnide, ultimately identifying it as the specific species obovata.
