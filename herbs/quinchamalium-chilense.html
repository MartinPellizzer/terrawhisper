Quinchamalium chilense

Quinchamalium chilense, commonly known as "palo de sangre," is a medicinal shrub native to the arid regions of Chile. Renowned in traditional folk medicine, it is frequently used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its unique bioactive compounds offer significant potential for contemporary pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Quinchamalium chilense
  • Family: Schoepfiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Santalales, it is classified under the family Schoepfiaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Quinchamalium, with the species name chilense.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of Western and Southern South America. In the western regions, its presence is documented within the borders of both Bolivia and Peru. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, the species can be found throughout Southern Argentina. Additionally, its range extends into the Northwest region of Argentina. Finally, the distribution concludes in the Central part of Chile.
