Hemerocallis citrina

Hemerocallis citrina, commonly known as the Citron Daylily, is a resilient perennial valued for its vibrant blooms and potential medicinal properties. While primarily ornamental, various cultures utilize its rhizomes in traditional remedies to address inflammation and digestive issues, showcasing the fascinating intersection of botanical beauty and natural healing potential.

  • Scientific name: Hemerocallis citrina
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales. Finally, it falls within the family Asphodelaceae and is identified by the genus Hemerocallis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is noted in both the Amur and Primorye regions. Its distribution extends significantly into China, where it can be found throughout the South-Central territories. Additionally, the species occupies much of the North-Central part of the country. Finally, its range reaches further north into the Inner Mongolia region of China.
