Chloranthus multistachys

Chloranthus multistachys, commonly known as the "Scented Chloranthus," is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, digestive ailments, and respiratory issues. Its unique bioactive compounds make it a significant subject in modern ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Chloranthus multistachys
  • Family: Chloranthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Chloranthales, it is situated in the family Chloranthaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is completed by its placement in the genus Chloranthus.
