Entada leptostachya

Entada leptostachya, commonly known as the climbing bean or woody climber, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, various parts of this vine are traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering promising potential for modern therapeutic developments.
