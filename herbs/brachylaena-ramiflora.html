Brachylaena ramiflora

Brachylaena ramiflora, commonly known as the Silver Tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its striking silvery foliage, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize its leaves and bark to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues, skin inflammations, and gastrointestinal discomforts.

  • Scientific name: Brachylaena ramiflora
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Brachylaena.
