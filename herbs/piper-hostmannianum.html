Piper hostmannianum

Piper hostmannianum, commonly known as a member of the versatile pepper family, is a medicinal plant native to tropical rainforest regions. Renowned for its complex phytochemical profile, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and therapeutic development.

  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Piperales. Finally, it is a member of the family Piperaceae within the genus Piper.
