What's the taxonomical classification of Leontopodium conglobatum?

Leontopodium conglobatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Asterales, the plant is a member of the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Leontopodium and the species conglobatum.
