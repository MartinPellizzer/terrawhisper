Dimetia hedyotidea

Dimetia hedyotidea, commonly known as the Silver-leafed Heidy, is a rare medicinal plant prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Found in remote tropical highlands, its leaves contain unique bioactive compounds used in traditional healing to reduce inflammation and support immune health, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Dimetia hedyotidea
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Dimetia hedyotidea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rubiaceae and the genus Dimetia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted within the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species is also found in Eastern Asia, specifically on the island of Taiwan. Moving into the Indo-China region, it can be located within the borders of Cambodia. Finally, its geographical range extends into the country of Thailand.
