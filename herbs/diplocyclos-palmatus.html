Native briony, scientifically known as Diplocyclos palmatus, is a climbing medicinal vine revered in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Characterized by its distinct five-lobed leaves, this plant is primarily utilized for its potent therapeutic properties, specifically in treating skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and various digestive disorders through its diverse bioactive compounds.
