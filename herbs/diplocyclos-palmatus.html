Diplocyclos palmatus

Native briony

Diplocyclos palmatus, commonly known as the Snake gourd, is a versatile climbing vine prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is utilized to treat digestive ailments, skin disorders, and inflammation. Beyond its nutritional value, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Diplocyclos palmatus
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Diplocyclos.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West-Central Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its presence can be noted in the nation of Burundi. It is also found within the territories of Congo and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Furthermore, its range extends to include Rwanda. Finally, the species is documented to grow on the Gulf of Guinea Islands.
