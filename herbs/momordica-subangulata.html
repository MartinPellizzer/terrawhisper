What's the taxonomical classification of Momordica subangulata?

Momoerdica subangulata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Cucurbitales. It is a member of the family Cucurbitaceae, which places it within the genus Momordica, and is specifically identified by the species name subangularata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Momordica subangulata has climbing stems characterized by thin, twining vines and deeply lobed, palmate leaves that typically feature three to five distinct segments. The foliage is soft and green, providing a dense canopy as the plant climbs through various supports. Its inflorescences consist of small, yellowish flowers that appear in clusters, often displaying a subtle fragrance to attract pollinators. As the plant matures, it produces distinctively shaped, elongated fruits that transition in color as they ripen. These fruits eventually contain seeds encased in a fleshy pulp, which is a defining feature of the Cucurbitaceae family.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant, commonly known as spiny gourd, requires well-drained sandy loam soil with a pH between 6.0 and 7.0 and consistent organic matter to thrive.
