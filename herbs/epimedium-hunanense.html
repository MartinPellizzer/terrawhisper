Epimedium hunanense

Epimedium hunanense, commonly known as a rare medicinal shrub, is an endemic species highly valued in traditional herbal medicine. Found in specific high-altitude habitats, it possesses unique bioactive compounds. Researchers are currently exploring its potential pharmacological properties, particularly its ability to provide therapeutic relief for various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium hunanense
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Epimedium hunanense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Berberidaceae and falls under the genus Epimedium.
