Eupatorium lindleyanum

Eupatorium lindleyanum, commonly known as Bartram's boneset, is a versatile perennial herb native to North America. Renowned in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory and febrifuge properties, it has long been utilized to treat fevers, respiratory ailments, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Eupatorium lindleyanum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Eupatorium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range stretching across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented within the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Moving southward, the species is also widely distributed throughout South-Central China. Furthermore, its habitat extends to the tropical island of Hainan. Consequently, these locations highlight the diverse environmental niches occupied by the species.
