Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range stretching across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented within the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Moving southward, the species is also widely distributed throughout South-Central China. Furthermore, its habitat extends to the tropical island of Hainan. Consequently, these locations highlight the diverse environmental niches occupied by the species.
