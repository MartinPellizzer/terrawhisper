Helichrysum noeanum

Helichrysum noeanum, commonly known as the everlasting flower, is a resilient medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, it is frequently utilized in traditional remedies to support skin health and promote healing, making it a valuable botanical treasure in holistic wellness.
