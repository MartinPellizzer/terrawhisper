Scientifically known as Achillea borealis var. borealis, is a hardy perennial native to northern latitudes. A member of the Asteraceae family, this botanical variety is distinguished by its delicate white florets and aromatic foliage. Traditionally valued for its restorative properties, it remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.
