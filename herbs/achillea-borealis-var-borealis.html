Achillea borealis var. borealis

Achillea borealis var. borealis, commonly known as northern yarrow, is a resilient perennial native to northern latitudes. Renowned for its delicate, feathery foliage and clusters of tiny flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various cultures for its soothing antiseptic and anti-inflammatory medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Achillea borealis var. borealis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Achillea. Specifically, it is identified by the variety borealis.
