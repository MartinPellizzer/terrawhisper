Phonus lanatus

Phonus lanatus, commonly known as Woolly Fleece, is a remarkable medicinal herb celebrated for its unique therapeutic properties. Renowned for its soothing qualities, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional healing to alleviate inflammation and support skin health, offering a natural remedy rooted in ancient botanical wisdom and efficacy.

  • Scientific name: Phonus lanatus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, eventually placing it in the family Asteraceae. Within this botanical hierarchy, the medicinal specimen is identified by the genus Phonus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide presence across several key regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its range extends from the islands of Great Britain to the coastal areas of Norway. Moving into Middle Europe, the species can be found throughout the landscapes of Belgium. It also maintains a significant distribution within the territories of Czechia-Slovakia. Finally, the plant is commonly documented across the diverse habitats of Germany.
