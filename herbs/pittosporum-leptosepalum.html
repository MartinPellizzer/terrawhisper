What's the taxonomical classification of Pittosporum leptosepalum?

Pittosporum leptosepalum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Pittosporum and is specifically designated by its species name, leptosepalum.
