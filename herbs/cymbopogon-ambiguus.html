Cymbopogon ambiguus

Cymbopogon ambiguus, commonly known as a variety of lemongrass, is a perennial aromatic grass valued in traditional medicine. Rich in essential oils like citral, it possesses potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This versatile plant is widely utilized for its therapeutic potential in treating digestive issues and skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Cymbopogon ambiguus
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Poales, it is a member of the family Poaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Cymbopogon.
