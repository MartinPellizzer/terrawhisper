Lemon Grass, scientifically known as Cymbopogon ambiguus, is a versatile aromatic grass renowned for its refreshing citrus scent. Widely used in traditional medicine and culinary arts, it possesses powerful antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This potent botanical serves as a natural remedy for digestion, fever, and various skin ailments.
