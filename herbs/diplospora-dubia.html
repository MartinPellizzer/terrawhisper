This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several regions in Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is documented throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Beyond the mainland, it can also be found growing in the islands of Japan. Furthermore, the species is specifically recorded within the Nansei-shoto archipelago. Together, these locations define the primary geographical range of Diplospora dubia.
