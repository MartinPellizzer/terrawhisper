Chervil, scientifically known as Anthriscus cerefolium, is a delicate perennial herb celebrated in both culinary and medicinal traditions. Renowned for its subtle, anise-like flavor, it is traditionally used to support digestion, reduce inflammation, and promote relaxation. This versatile plant serves as a nutritious addition to many holistic wellness routines.
