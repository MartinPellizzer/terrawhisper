Juniperus excelsa var. polycarpos, scientifically known as Juniperus polycarpos, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the arid regions of the Middle East. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and berries, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory healing properties.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales, it is classified under the family Cupressaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Juniperus as Juniperus polycarpos.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of Central and Western Asia. In Central Asia, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Turkmenistan. The species also extends into the Caucasus region, inhabiting both the North Caucasus and the Transcaucasus areas. Its range continues through Western Asia, where it is found growing in Iran. Finally, the distribution reaches the Levant, covering the territory of Lebanon and Syria.
