Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as rose hip, is highly valued in herbal medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties found in its various components. The most prominent part used is the fruits, which are rich in vitamins and antioxidants. Specifically, the fleshy fruit serves as a potent source of nutrients that support skin health and immune function. Furthermore, many preparations involve a water extract derived from these parts, providing a gentle yet effective way to consume the plant's beneficial compounds in liquid form.
