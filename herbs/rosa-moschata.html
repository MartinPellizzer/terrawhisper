Rosa moschata

Rosa moschatta, commonly known as rosehip, is a powerhouse of natural wellness derived from the fruit of wild rose bushes. Renowned for its exceptional concentration of Vitamin C and essential fatty acids, it serves as a potent antioxidant, promoting skin rejuvenation, supporting immune function, and reducing systemic inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Rosa moschata
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Rosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in Spain. Its presence extends into Southeastern Europe, specifically within the borders of Bulgaria. The species is also well-established in Southeastern Europe through Italy and the island of Sicilia. Finally, its range reaches into Northern Africa, where it is located in Algeria.
