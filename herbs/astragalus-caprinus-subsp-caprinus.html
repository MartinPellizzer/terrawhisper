Astragalus caprinus subsp. caprinus

Astragalus caprinus subsp. caprinus, commonly known as goat milk astragalus, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific montane regions. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this subspecies is valued for its bioactive compounds, which are studied for their potential immunomodulatory, antioxidant, and adaptogenic properties in supporting overall human wellness.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus caprinus subsp. caprinus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
