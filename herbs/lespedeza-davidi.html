What's the taxonomical classification of Lespedeza davidi?

Lespedeza davidi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further categorized under the order Fabales. It is a member of the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified within the genus Lespedeza, with its unique designation being the species davidi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lespedeza davidi has trifoliate leaves with ovate to elliptical leaflets, pinnate flowers in dense terminal racemes, and small, brown, ovoid legumes. Its inflorescences are composed of numerous small, pea-like blossoms that vary in color from pale yellow to cream. The stems are woody at the base and can become quite sprawling or upright depending on the growing environment. These characteristic structures allow the plant to be identified easily among other members of the Fabaceae family.
