Azima tetracantha

Azyma tetracantha, commonly known as the thorny Azima, is a resilient medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Characterized by its distinctive defensive spines, it contains unique bioactive compounds. Used primarily for treating inflammatory conditions and skin ailments, this plant remains a vital subject of ethnobotanical study and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Azima tetracantha
  • Family: Salvadoraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales, it is further categorized into the family Salvadoraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Azima, specifically the species Azima tetracantha.
