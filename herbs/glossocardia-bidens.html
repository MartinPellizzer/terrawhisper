Glossocardia bidens

Glossocardia bidens, commonly known as the "two-toothed tongueleaf," is a distinctive medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant is recognized for its unique leaf morphology and potent bioactive compounds. Researchers study it for its potential therapeutic properties in treating various inflammatory and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Glossocardia bidens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling into the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Glossocardia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of Asia and Africa. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Tanzania. Within China, it can be found in the tropical Hainan province, the China Southeast region, and the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Furthermore, its range extends into Eastern Asia, specifically inhabiting the Kazan-retto area. Collectively, these locations highlight the wide ecological variety inhabited by Glossocardia bidens.
