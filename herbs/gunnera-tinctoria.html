Chilean rhubarb, scientifically known as Gunnera tinctoria, is a distinctive perennial herb native to South America. Renowned for its massive, textured leaves, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its medicinal properties, particularly in treating digestive ailments and skin conditions.
