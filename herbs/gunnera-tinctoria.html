Gunnera tinctoria

Gunnera tinctoria, commonly known as the "marmelada" or "tinturella," is a significant medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat digestive ailments, skin inflammations, and various infections, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Gunnera tinctoria
  • Family: Gunneraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gunnerales, it is classified under the family Gunneraceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Gunnera, specifically identified as Gunnera tinctoria.
