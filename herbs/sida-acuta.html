Morning Mallow

Sida acuta · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (45 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Morning Mallow, scientifically known as Sida acuta, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Widely used in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and diuretic properties. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for treating respiratory ailments, skin issues, and various inflammatory conditions worldwide.
