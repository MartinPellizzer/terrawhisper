Solanum guaraniticum

Solanum guaranitium, commonly known as a medicinal member of the nightshade family, is a plant native to South American tropical forests. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, it possesses unique bioactive compounds. Researchers study its phytochemical properties to explore potential therapeutic applications in treating inflammation and various systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Solanum guaraniticum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales, it is further categorized into the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Solanum as the species Solanum guaraniticum.
