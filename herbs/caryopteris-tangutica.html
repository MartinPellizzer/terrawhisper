Caryopteris tangutica

Caryopteris tangutica, commonly known as Blue Spreader, is a resilient perennial shrub prized for its striking cobalt-blue blooms. Native to East Asia, this hardy plant thrives in sunny, well-drained soils. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators, enhancing garden biodiversity and ecosystem health.

  • Scientific name: Caryopteris tangutica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Caryopteris. Specifically, Caryopteris tangutica represents a unique species within this taxonomic hierarchy.
