Withania frutescens

Withania frutescens, commonly known as a relative of the famous Ashwagandha, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid regions. Renowned for its bioactive alkaloids, this plant is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic practices to reduce stress, enhance vitality, and support immune health through its potent adaptogenic properties.

  • Scientific name: Withania frutescens
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Solanales, and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Withania.
