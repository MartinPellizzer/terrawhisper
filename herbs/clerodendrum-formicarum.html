Clerodendrum formicarum

Clerodendrum formicarum, commonly known as the ant-tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique scent and bioactive properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Clerodendrum formicarum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Clerodendrum formicarum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Clerodendrum.
