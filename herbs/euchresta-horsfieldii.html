Euchresta horsfieldii

Euchresta horsfieldii, commonly known as the "wild climber," is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations. This climbing shrub offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive phytochemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Euchresta horsfieldii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
