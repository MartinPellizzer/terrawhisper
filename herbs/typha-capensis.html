Typha capensis

Typha capensis, commonly known as the bulrush or cattail, is a versatile aquatic perennial found in wetland ecosystems. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is utilized in traditional healing to treat inflammation and digestive issues. This resilient plant offers significant ecological value and serves as a vital natural resource.

  • Scientific name: Typha capensis
  • Family: Typhaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Poales, it is classified under the family Typhaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Typha, with its species designation being capensis.
