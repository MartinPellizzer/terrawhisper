Wyethia sagittata

Arrowleaf balsamroot

Wyethia sagittata, commonly known as the arrowleaf vine, is a climbing woody plant native to the rainforests of Australia. Renowned for its traditional medicinal uses, particularly by Indigenous communities, this species contains unique bioactive compounds valued for their potential therapeutic properties in treating various ailments and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Wyethia sagittata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, it is identified by the genus Wyethia.
