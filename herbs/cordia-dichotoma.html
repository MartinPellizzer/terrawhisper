Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Cordia dichotoma, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Cordia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central and Southeast regions. It can also be found growing in the high-altitude areas of Tibet. The species extends its reach to the Nansei-shotto islands in Eastern Asia. Additionally, the distribution includes the island of Taiwan.

Plant Parts

This plant, known for its various therapeutic properties, utilizes multiple components for medicinal purposes. The leaves are often employed in traditional remedies to treat skin ailments or inflammation, while the leaf structure itself serves as a vital site for photosynthesis to sustain the organism. The sturdy stem provides structural support and can sometimes be utilized in decoctions, whereas the root is frequently harvested for its potent bioactive compounds used in various herbal preparations. Additionally, the sweet and nutritious pulp of the fruit is not only consumed for its nutritional value but also contributes to the plant's overall medicinal profile.
