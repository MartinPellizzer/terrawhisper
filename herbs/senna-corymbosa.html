Argentine Senna, scientifically known as Senna corymbosa, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized for its potent therapeutic properties. Primarily recognized for its powerful laxative effects, it contains bioactive sennosides that stimulate intestinal activity. This plant remains a cornerstone in traditional herbal medicine for managing acute constipation and digestive health.
