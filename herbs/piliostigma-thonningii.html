Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several countries within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented in Benin, where it plays a role in the local ecosystem. The species can also be found in the landscapes of Burkina and the Gambia. Furthermore, its geographical reach extends into the nation of Ghana. Finally, the distribution of this medicinal plant includes the coastal area of Guinea-Bissau.
