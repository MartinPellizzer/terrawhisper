Cissus gongylodes

Cissus gongylodes, commonly known as "Bailong Teng" in traditional Chinese medicine, is a climbing vine valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its ability to nourish the blood and strengthen bones, this medicinal plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from joint pain to nutritional deficiencies.

  • Scientific name: Cissus gongylodes
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Vitales, placing it in the family Vitaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Cissus.
