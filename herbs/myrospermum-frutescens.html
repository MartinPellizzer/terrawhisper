Myrospermum frutescens

Myrospermum frutescens, commonly known as "Palo de Agua," is a resilient shrub native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this medicinal plant is frequently utilized for its bioactive compounds. It is primarily recognized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, offering significant interest for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Myrospermum frutescens
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Fabales and is a member of the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Myrospermum.
