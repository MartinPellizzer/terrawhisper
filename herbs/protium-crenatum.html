Protium crenatum

Protium crenatum, commonly known as a member of the Burseraceae family, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical ecosystems. Renowned for its aromatic resin, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer promising therapeutic potential for anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial applications in pharmacology.
