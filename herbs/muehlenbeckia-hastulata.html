Muehlenbeckia hastulata

Muehlenbeckia hastulata, commonly known as koromiko, is a versatile New Zealand shrub cherished in traditional Rongoā Māori medicine. Renowned for its astringent properties, the plant's leaves are frequently used to treat digestive ailments, skin irritations, and wound healing, offering a natural, potent remedy deeply rooted in indigenous botanical wisdom.

  • Scientific name: Muehlenbeckia hastulata
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Muehlenbeckia.
