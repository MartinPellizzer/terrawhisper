Euryale

Euryale, commonly known as Gorgon nut or Fox nut, is a versatile aquatic plant revered in traditional medicine. Prized for its nutrient-dense seeds, it offers significant therapeutic benefits, including aiding digestion, improving sleep, and reducing inflammation. This resilient plant remains a vital staple in holistic wellness and nutritional practices.

  • Scientific name: Euryale
  • Family: Nymphaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and falls under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Nymphaeaales, it is classified under the family Nymphaeaceae. Specifically, this medicinal species is identified by the genus Euryale.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. The species is also widely spread throughout various parts of China, including the South-Central areas and the island of Hainan. Furthermore, its range extends to the northern reaches of China, encompassing Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Together, these locations define the primary natural habitats where Euryale can be found.
