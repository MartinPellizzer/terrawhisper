Hertia cheirifolia

Hertia cheirifolia, commonly known as the tea-leaved hertia, is a resilient shrub native to the tropical landscapes of Southeast Asia and Oceania. Renowned for its glossy foliage, this botanical species holds significant ethnobotanical value, often utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties and healing potential.

  • Scientific name: Hertia cheirifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hertia, specifically as the species Hertia cheirifolia.
