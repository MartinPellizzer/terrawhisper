Oxyria sinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Oxyria sinensis?

Oxyria sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it falls into the subclass Magnoliidae and is further categorized under the order Caryophyllales. This plant is a member of the family Polygonaceae, which places it within the genus Oxyria, eventually reaching its specific classification as the species sinensis.
