Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in East Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range extends further into Japan, where it is established as a native species. Additionally, the plant is documented to grow within the Nansei-shotō island chain. Overall, these locations highlight a significant presence of the species throughout Eastern Asia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Smilax bracteata, is a climbing woody vine that utilizes various anatomical structures for its growth and medicinal properties. While the foliage and stems are prominent features of its climbing habit, a critical part of the plant is the rhizome. This underground stem serves as a vital storage organ, allowing the plant to persist through unfavorable conditions and providing a concentrated source of bioactive compounds often sought in traditional herbal medicine.
