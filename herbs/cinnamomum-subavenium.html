Plant Parts

This plant, Cinnamomum subavenium, utilizes several of its components for medicinal purposes, most notably its aromatic parts. The leaves are highly valued for their ability to yield a potent leaf oil, which is often extracted for its therapeutic properties. Additionally, the plant's structural components, including the stems and the bark, contain bioactive compounds.
