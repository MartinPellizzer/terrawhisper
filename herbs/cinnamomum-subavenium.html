Cinnamomum subavenium

Cinnamomum subavenium, commonly known as a potent medicinal plant, holds significant value in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this species offers diverse therapeutic benefits, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. Exploring its chemical constituents reveals its profound potential for supporting holistic health and treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Cinnamomum subavenium
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae, it is classified under the genus Cinnamomum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout both the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species also extends into Eastern Asia, specifically inhabiting the island of Taiwan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing within the borders of Bangladesh. Finally, its range reaches into Indo-China, where it is documented in Cambodia.
