Lygodium microphyllum

Old world climbing fern

Lygodium microphyllum, commonly known as the small-leaf climbing fern, is a unique medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its traditional healing properties, it is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments and respiratory issues, offering a natural approach to wellness through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Lygodium microphyllum
  • Family: Schizaeaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Lygodium microphyllum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae, it is classified under the order Schizaeales. Finally, it falls into the family Schizaeaceae and is identified by the genus Lygodium.
