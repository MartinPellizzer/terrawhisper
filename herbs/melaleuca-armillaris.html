Bracelet honey myrtle, scientifically known as Melaleuca armillaris, is a resilient Australian shrub prized for its aromatic foliage. Native to coastal regions, this medicinal plant contains potent essential oils with antimicrobial properties. Often used in traditional healing, it offers therapeutic benefits for skin health and respiratory support through natural essences.
