Peucedanum medicum

What's the taxonomical classification of Peucedanum medicum?

Peucedanum medicum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Apiales. As a member of the family Apiaceae, it is further categorized under the genus Peucedanum, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species medicum.
