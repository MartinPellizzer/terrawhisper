Litsea pedunculata

Litsea pedunculata, commonly known as the wild laurel, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Lauraceae family. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Litsea pedunculata
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
