Astragalus brachycalyx subsp. brachycalyx

Astragalus brachycalyx subsp. brachycalyx, commonly known as Huang Qi in traditional Chinese medicine, is a vital medicinal herb. Renowned for its ability to tonify the "Qi," it is widely utilized to bolster the immune system, enhance vitality, and support overall longevity through its potent, nutrient-rich root properties.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus brachycalyx subsp. brachycalyx
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement situates it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, specifically within the family Fabaceae. It is identified as a member of the genus Astragalus, specifically the subspecies Astragalus brachycalyx subsp. brachycalyx.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the Western Asia region. It can be found growing naturally across the diverse landscapes of Iran. Additionally, its presence extends into the territories of Iraq. The species also inhabits various parts of Türkiye. Consequently, these three countries represent the primary range for this particular subspecies.
