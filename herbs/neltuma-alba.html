Neltuma alba

Neltuma alba, commonly known as the White Nettle, is a resilient medicinal herb prized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant capabilities, this plant has been used traditionally to support kidney health, soothe skin irritations, and boost overall vitality through its nutrient-dense botanical profile.

  • Scientific name: Neltuma alba
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Neltuma, with the specific species name Neltuma alba.
