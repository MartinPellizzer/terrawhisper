Antrophyum formosanum

What's the taxonomical classification of Antrophyum formosanum?

Antrophyum formosanum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. The plant further follows the order Polypodiales and is a member of the family Pteridaceae. Taxonomically, it is identified under the genus Antrophyum, with the specific designation of the species formosanum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Antrophyum formosanum has simple, undivided fronds that are typically oblong or lanceolate in shape and emerge from a creeping rhizome. The leaf blades are characterized by a leathery texture and a prominent midrib that extends to the apex. These ferns often exhibit a dark green color with a slightly wavy margin along the edges of the lamina. The reproductive structures, known as sori, are located on the underside of the leaves and are arranged in linear patterns following the veins. This specific arrangement of sori is a defining morphological feature of the genus.
