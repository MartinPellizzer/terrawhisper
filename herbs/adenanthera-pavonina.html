Adenanthera pavonina

Red sandalwood

Adenanthera pavonina, commonly known as the Bird of Paradise pea, is a resilient legume native to various tropical regions. Beyond its striking ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential bioactive properties, offering intriguing possibilities for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Adenanthera pavonina
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the coastal and inland regions of West Tropical Africa. Within this specific geographic zone, it can be found growing in the nation of Ghana. Its range also extends through the territories of Guinea and Guinea-Bissau. Furthermore, the species is documented to inhabit parts of Sierra Leone. Finally, the distribution of this medicinal plant includes the expansive landscapes of Nigeria.
