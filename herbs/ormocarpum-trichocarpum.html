Ormocarpum trichocarpum

Ormocarpum trichocarpum, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, holds great pharmacological potential. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Current research explores its antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, highlighting its importance in ethnobotany.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa
