Heptapleurum hypoleucum

Heptapleurum hypoleucum, commonly known as the Schefflera or Umbrella Tree, is a versatile tropical plant. While primarily celebrated as an ornamental indoor species, it holds significant place in traditional herbal medicine. Its leaves are frequently utilized in various cultures for their potential therapeutic properties and medicinal applications.
