Bauhinia variegata var. candida

Bauhinia variegata var. candida, commonly known as the White Orchid Tree, is a medicinal plant prized in traditional practices. Characterized by its elegant white blooms, it possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. It is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments and digestive issues.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe
