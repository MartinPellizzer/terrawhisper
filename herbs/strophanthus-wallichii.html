Strophanthus wallichii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Gentianaales and is a member of the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Strophanthus, with the unique identifier being the species wallichii.
