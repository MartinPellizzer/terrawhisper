Centaurea antitauri

Centaurea antitauri, commonly known as Centaury, is a perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism for its bitter properties. Characterized by its delicate white flowers, this plant is frequently utilized to support digestive health, stimulate appetite, and promote bile production, offering a natural approach to managing various gastrointestinal ailments.

  • Scientific name: Centaurea antitauri
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further organized into the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Centaurea, specifically identified as Centaurea antitauri.
