Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the Hainan province as well as throughout China Southeast. The species also extends its range into Japan, specifically within the Nansei-shotō islands. Furthermore, its geographical footprint includes the Korean Peninsula. Collectively, these areas define the natural habitat where Distylium racemosum can be found.

This plant, Distylium racemosum, is characterized by its distinctive foliage, where the leaf serves as a primary morphological feature. The leaves are typically arranged in an opposite pattern along the stem, often displaying an elliptical or oblong shape with smooth margins. In medicinal applications, the leaf is frequently the part of the plant studied for its bioactive compounds, contributing to the overall therapeutic profile of the species.
