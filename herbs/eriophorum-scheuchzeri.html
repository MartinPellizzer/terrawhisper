Eriophorum scheuchzeri

Eriophorum scheuchzeri, commonly known as Scheuchzer's cottongrass, is a distinctive perennial herb found in peatlands and acidic bogs. While primarily admired for its delicate, white tufted seed heads, this plant holds ecological significance, contributing to wetland biodiversity and traditional herbal practices within specialized botanical studies and regional ethnobotany.
