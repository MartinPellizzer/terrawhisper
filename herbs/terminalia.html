Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Combretaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed at the genus level as Terminalia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across different regions of Africa and its surrounding islands. In the Macaronesian region, it can be found specifically within Cape Verde. Moving to the mainland, it is widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Its presence is documented in several nations, including Benin and Burkina. Additionally, the species extends its reach to include the countries of Gambia and Ghana.
