Flemingia strobilifera

Flemingia strobilifera, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in traditional practices, holds immense therapeutic potential. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is primarily utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Ongoing research explores its antioxidant properties, highlighting its importance in developing modern natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Flemingia strobilifera
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Flemingia, where it is identified as Flemingia strobilifera.
