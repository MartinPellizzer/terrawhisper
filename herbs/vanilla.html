Vanilla

Vanilla, commonly known as the world's most coveted spice, originates from climbing orchids native to tropical regions. Beyond its enchanting aroma, this medicinal plant offers therapeutic benefits, including calming anxiety, aiding digestion, and reducing inflammation. Its complex flavor and soothing properties make it a versatile staple in wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vanilla
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within West Tropical Africa. In the western part of the continent, it can be found growing in Ghana. The species also inhabits the lush landscapes of Guinea and the Ivory Coast. Additionally, its presence extends into the tropical environments of Liberia. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes Nigeria.
