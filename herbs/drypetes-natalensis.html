Drypetes natalensis

Drypetes natalensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in West African traditional medicine, holds immense pharmacological potential. Often utilized by local healers to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammatory conditions, this plant is increasingly being studied for its bioactive compounds and diverse therapeutic applications in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Drypetes natalensis
  • Family: Putranjivaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Putranjivaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Drypetes.
