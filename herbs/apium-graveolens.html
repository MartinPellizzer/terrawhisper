Wild celery, scientifically known as Apium graveolens, is a versatile perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Historically prized for its aromatic leaves and stalks, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Rich in antioxidants and bioactive compounds, it is widely studied for its anti-inflammatory and diuretic medicinal properties.
