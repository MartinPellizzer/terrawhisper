Silene vulgaris

Bladder campion

Silene vulgaris, commonly known as Bladder Campion, is a resilient perennial herb distinguished by its inflated, bladder-like calyx. Found in diverse habitats across the Northern Hemisphere, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, though it remains primarily valued in botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Silene vulgaris
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Silene vulgaris, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Caryophyllaceae and falls under the genus Silene.
