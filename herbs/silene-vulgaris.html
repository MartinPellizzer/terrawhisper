Bladder campion, scientifically known as Silene vulgaris, is a hardy perennial herb native to much of the Northern Hemisphere. Recognizable by its delicate, bladder-like calyx, this wildflower thrives in meadows and rocky soils. Historically used in folk medicine, it serves as a vital nectar source for various pollinators.
