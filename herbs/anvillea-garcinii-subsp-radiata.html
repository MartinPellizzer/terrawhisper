Anvillea garcinii subsp. radiata

Anvillea garcinii subsp. radiata, commonly known as the "Anvillea" or "Indian Anvillea," is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this subspecies is traditionally utilized in various healing practices to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various digestive disorders through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Anvillea garcinii subsp. radiata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Anvillea garcinii subsp. radiata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Anvillea.
