Capparis pyrifolia, scientifically known as Capparis pyrifolia, is a resilient, perennial shrub widely distributed across tropical and subtropical regions. Distribution

This plant, known as Capparis pyrifolia, exhibits a significant presence across several regions in Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is notably distributed throughout India.
