Capparis pyrifolia, scientifically known as Capparis pyrifolia, is a resilient, perennial shrub widely distributed across tropical and subtropical regions. Commonly referred to as the Caper bush, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering diverse therapeutic benefits for various ailments.

Distribution

This plant, known as Capparis pyrifolia, exhibits a significant presence across several regions in Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is notably distributed throughout India. Its range also extends extensively into the Indo-China region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the countries of Cambodia, Laos, and Thailand. Additionally, the species is well-established within the borders of Vietnam.
