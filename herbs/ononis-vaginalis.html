Ononis vaginalis

Oonis vaginalis, commonly known as the Vaginal Oyster plant, is a rare medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique botanical characteristics, this plant is primarily studied for its potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering promising insights into natural treatments for various reproductive health concerns.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe
