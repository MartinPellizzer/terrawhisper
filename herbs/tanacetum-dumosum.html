Tanacetum dumosum

Tanacetum dumosum, commonly known as the bushy tansy, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Distinguished by its aromatic foliage and bright yellow button-like flowers, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties within various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Tanacetum dumosum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, placing it in the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tanacetum.
