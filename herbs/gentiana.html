Gentian, scientifically known as Gentiana, is a genus of perennial flowering plants prized for their intense bitterness. Renowned in traditional herbalism, these plants contain bitter glycosides that stimulate digestion and support liver function. Often used in liqueurs and bitters, gentian remains a cornerstone of botanical wellness and digestive health.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Gentianaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gentiana.

Plant Parts

This plant, known for its potent medicinal properties, is characterized by its distinct morphology, most notably its large, trumpet-shaped flowers that attract pollinators. While the vibrant blossoms are the most visible feature above ground, the true therapeutic value of the Gentiana is concentrated in its bitter roots. These underground roots are carefully harvested and processed to extract the essential compounds used in various herbal remedies.
