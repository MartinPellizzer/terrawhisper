Gentian, scientifically known as Gentiana, is a genus of perennial flowering plants prized for their intense bitterness. Renowned in traditional herbalism, these plants contain bitter glycosides that stimulate digestion and support liver function. Often used in liqueurs and bitters, gentian remains a cornerstone of botanical wellness and digestive health.
