Zygophyllum album

Zygophyllum album, commonly known as the White Calotropis or White Zygophyllum, is a resilient succulent shrub native to arid African regions. Renowned for its striking white flowers and drought tolerance, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Zygophyllum album
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zygophyllales, it is further organized into the family Zygophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Zygophyllum.
