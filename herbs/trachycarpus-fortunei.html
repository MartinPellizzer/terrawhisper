Trachycarpus fortunei

Trachycarpus fortunei, commonly known as Fortune's Trachycarpus, is a significant medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Primarily found in East Asian mountainous regions, this plant offers diverse therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized for its potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, serving as a valuable resource in holistic herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Trachycarpus fortunei
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Arecales and family Arecaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Trachycarpus.
