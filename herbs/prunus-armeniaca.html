Prunus armeniaca

Apricot

Prunus armeniaca, commonly known as the apricot, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Beyond its nutritious fruit, its seeds, leaves, and bark contain bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to support skin health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall wellness through various natural applications.

  • Scientific name: Prunus armeniaca
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Prunus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Prunus armeniaca, is recognized by various regional names across different languages. In Polish, it is called morela, while in the Silesian dialect (szl), it is referred to as aprikołza. The Chinese name for this fruit is 杏 (zh), and in Abkhaz, it is known as Аҷарам (ab). Additionally, in the Basque language, it is called abrikotondo (eu).
