Sinomenium acutum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (313 studies) · ★★★★★

Sinomenium acutum, scientifically known as Sinomenium acutum, is a perennial herb native to East Asia, highly valued in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its potent analgesic and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various types of pain, including migraines, neuralgia, and various rheumatic conditions effectively.
