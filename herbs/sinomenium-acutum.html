Sinomenium acutum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (313 studies) · ★★★★★

Sinomenium acutum, scientifically known as Sinomenium acutum, is a perennial herb native to East Asia, highly valued in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its potent analgesic and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various types of pain, including migraines, neuralgia, and various rheumatic conditions effectively.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Beyond mainland China, the species can be found growing in Japan. It also has a documented range within Korea. Consequently, its geographical footprint spans a significant portion of the Eastern Asian landscape.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Sinomenium acutum, is a climbing woody vine that utilizes its slender stems to ascend through surrounding vegetation. The medicinal properties of the species are primarily concentrated in the root and the woody stem, which are often harvested and processed for therapeutic use. While the plant produces lush green leaves that facilitate photosynthesis, the reproductive cycle involves the production of a small seed contained within its fruit.
