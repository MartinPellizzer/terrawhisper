Grammatophyllum speciosum

Grammatophyllum speciosum, commonly known as the Giant Orchid, is one of the world's largest orchid species. Native to Southeast Asia, this magnificent epiphyte is celebrated for its massive, pendulous inflorescences. Beyond its stunning aesthetic appeal, it holds significant cultural value and remains a fascinating subject in botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Grammatophyllum speciosum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
