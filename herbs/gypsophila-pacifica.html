Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Northern Asia. It can be found growing in the western portions of Siberia, specifically within West Siberia. Its range extends significantly into the Russian Far East, encompassing the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Additionally, the species is present in the Manchuria region of China. These varied locations highlight its ability to inhabit diverse landscapes across the eastern Eurasian continent.
