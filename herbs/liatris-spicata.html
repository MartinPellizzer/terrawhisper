Liatris spicata

Dense blazing star

Liatris spicata, commonly known as Blazing Star, is a striking perennial wildflower native to North American prairies. Renowned for its vibrant, purple spiked blooms, this resilient plant plays a vital role in supporting pollinators. Traditionally, it has been utilized in herbal medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Liatris spicata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Liatris.
