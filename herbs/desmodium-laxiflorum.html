Desmodium laxiflorum

What's the taxonomical classification of Desmodium laxiflorum?

Desmodium laxiflorum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant falls under the order Fabales and is a member of the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Desmodium, with its unique classification completed at the species level as laxiflorum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Desmodium laxiflorum has trifoliate leaves with oblong to elliptical leaflets and small, pale pink to white flowers arranged in loose, axillary racemes. The plant typically exhibits a decumbent or ascending growth habit with stems that may be slightly pubescent. Each leaflet possesses a smooth margin and a distinct petiole connecting it to the main stem. The inflorescence is characterized by its sparse arrangement, which distinguishes it from more densely flowering species in the genus.
