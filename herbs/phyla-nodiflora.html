Phyla nodiflora

Creeping lip plant

Phyla nodiflora, commonly known as Frogfruit or Turkey Tofield, is a resilient, creeping perennial herb found in tropical and subtropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, this versatile plant thrives in moist soils, offering significant therapeutic potential for managing various skin and digestive ailments.

  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Europe
