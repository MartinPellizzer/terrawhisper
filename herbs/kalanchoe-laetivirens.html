Kalanchoe laetivirens

Kalanchoe crenodaigremontiana

Kalanchoe laetivirens, commonly known as the bright Kalanchoe, is a striking succulent prized for its vibrant, variegated foliage. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. Researchers study its bioactive compounds, exploring its diverse pharmacological properties, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, within traditional and modern herbal medicine.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Kalanchoe laetivirens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Saxifragales. Finally, it is a member of the family Crassulaceae and the genus Kalanchoe.
