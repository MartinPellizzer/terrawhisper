Sargentodoxa cuneata

Sargentodoxa cuneata, commonly known as Sargentodoxa, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional systems like Ayurveda. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its roots are extensively utilized to treat various ailments, including skin disorders and infections, making it a vital subject in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Sargentodoxa cuneata
  • Family: Lardizabalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ranunculales, it is placed in the family Lardizabalaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sargentodoxa, specifically as the species Sargentodoxa cunearata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across various regions of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central and North-Central territories. It is also documented in the Southeast regions of China as well as on the island of Hainan. Beyond Chinese borders, its natural habitat extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, the species is known to occur naturally within the country of Laos.
