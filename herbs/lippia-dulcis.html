Aztec Sweet Herb, scientifically known as Lippia dulcis, is a fragrant, aromatic shrub native to Mexico and Central America. Renowned for its distinctively sweet, citrusy scent, this versatile plant is traditionally used in herbal medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and skin irritations, offering both therapeutic and culinary benefits.
