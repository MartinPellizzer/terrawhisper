Asystasia gangetica

Asystaisia gangetica, commonly known as the Gangesia or Gangesia plant, is a significant medicinal herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant is widely utilized to support respiratory health and enhance overall immune system function.

  • Scientific name: Asystasia gangetica
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
