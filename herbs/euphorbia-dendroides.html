Tree Spurge, scientifically known as Euphorbia dendroides, is a distinctive succulent shrub native to Mexico. Renowned for its striking, candelabra-like architecture, it is prized in xeriscaping. Beyond its ornamental beauty, various species of Euphorbia possess historical medicinal properties, though their potent, milky latex requires careful, expert handling.
