Green-stemmed forsythia, scientifically known as Forsythia viridissima, is a resilient deciduous shrub valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bright yellow blooms, its medicinal properties primarily reside in the bark and fruit. Often used to relieve inflammation and clear heat, it remains a vital component in various herbal remedies.
