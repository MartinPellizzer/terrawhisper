Muskroot

Ferula moschata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (76 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Muskroot, scientifically known as Ferula moschata, is a highly aromatic perennial plant belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its intense, musk-like fragrance, it yields a precious oleo-gum-resin. Historically prized in traditional medicine, it is utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties, including antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and sedative effects.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution centered primarily within the regions of Central Asia. It can be found growing naturally across the mountainous landscapes of Kyrgyzstan. Its habitat also extends into the territories of Tajikistan, where it is part of the local flora. Furthermore, the species is documented within the borders of Uzbekistan. Finally, its range stretches eastward into the Xinjiang region of China.
