Muskroot, scientifically known as Ferula moschata, is a highly aromatic perennial plant belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its intense, musk-like fragrance, it yields a precious oleo-gum-resin. Historically prized in traditional medicine, it is utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties, including antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and sedative effects.
