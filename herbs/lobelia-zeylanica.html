Lobelia zeylanica

What's the taxonomical classification of Lobelia zeylanica?

Lobelia zeylanica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy down through the order Asterales, the plant is a member of the family Campanulaceae. It is situated within the genus Lobelia, and its specific classification is defined by the species name zeylanica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lobelia zeylanica has an erect, herbaceous growth habit characterized by slender, oppositely arranged leaves that transition from ovate to lanceolate shapes. The foliage is typically bright green with entire margins and prominent venation visible on the leaf surfaces. Its inflorescence consists of small, tubular flowers that are zygomorphic and often exhibit a distinct blue or violet coloration. The floral structure features a prominent lower lip and a narrower upper lip, which is characteristic of the Campanulaceae family. Stems are generally smooth and can vary in height depending on the specific environmental conditions of its habitat.
