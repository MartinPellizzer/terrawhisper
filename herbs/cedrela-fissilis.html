Cedar, scientifically known as Cedrela fissilis, is a majestic tropical tree native to South America. Renowned for its aromatic, durable timber, it also holds significant medicinal value. Traditionally, its bark and leaves are utilized in various cultures to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive issues, and skin ailments through herbal remedies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning several regions across the Americas. In Central America, its presence is documented within the countries of Costa Rica, Honduras, and Panamá. Moving toward the Caribbean, it can also be found in Trinidad and Tobago. Its distribution extends further into Northern South America, specifically within French Guiana. Altogether, these locations highlight the diverse tropical environments where the species thrives.
