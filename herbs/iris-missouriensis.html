Distribution

This plant, known as Iris missouriensis, exhibits a specific range across North America. In Western Canada, its presence is documented within the provinces of Alberta and British Columbia. Moving into the Northwestern United States, the species can be found growing in the state of Idaho. It also maintains a significant distribution throughout the mountainous regions of Montana. Furthermore, its geographical reach extends southward into the state of Colorado.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the western blue flag, utilizes its roots as a vital component for both its survival and its historical medicinal application. The plant features thick, fleshy rhizomes that grow horizontally underground, serving as a storage organ for nutrients and water. In traditional herbal practices, these rhizomatous roots were often harvested and prepared for their purported therapeutic properties, although they must be used with caution due to their potent chemical composition.
