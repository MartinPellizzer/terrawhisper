Kalanchoe laciniata, scientifically known as Kalanchoe laciniata, is a captivating succulent native to Madagascar. Renowned for its distinctive, deeply lobed leaves and resilient nature, this plant is prized by collectors. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant interest in traditional herbal practices for its unique bioactive properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Rwanda. Moving toward the northeast, it can be found within the borders of Chad. The species also inhabits parts of Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically within Ethiopia and Eritrea. Finally, its range extends further into the horn of Africa to include Somalia.
