Eucomis bicolor

Pineapple flower

Eucomis bicolor, commonly known as the bicolor pineapple lily, is a striking perennial native to southern Africa. Characterized by its unique rosette of leaves and tufted flower spike, this ornamental plant offers exceptional visual appeal. While primarily valued in horticulture, it holds significant interest in traditional medicinal practices and biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Eucomis bicolor
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales and family Asparagaceae, it is classified under the genus Eucomis. Specifically, it is identified by its unique botanical characteristics as Eucomis bicolor.
