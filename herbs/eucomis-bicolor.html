Pineapple Flower, scientifically known as Eucomis bicolor, is a stunning perennial native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its distinctive rosette of small white blossoms atop a tall scape, it mimics the appearance of a pineapple. This resilient plant thrives in well-drained soil, adding exotic elegance to any botanical collection.

This plant, Eucomis bicolor, is characterized by its distinct morphological features, most notably its large, fleshy bulbs that serve as the primary storage organs for nutrients and water. Above the ground, the plant produces striking, strap-shaped leaves that grow in a basal rosette, creating a lush green base from which the flowering scape emerges.
