Ipomoea wightii

Ipomoea wightii, commonly known as the climbing morning glory, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this climbing vine offers significant therapeutic potential. Researchers are increasingly exploring its phytochemical properties to discover new applications in treating various ailments and promoting wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea wightii
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Ipomoea.
