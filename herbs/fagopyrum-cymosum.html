Fagopyrum cymosum

Fagopyrum cymosum, commonly known as African buckwheat, is a nutritious pseudocereal native to sub-Saharan Africa. Highly valued for its resilience in diverse climates, it offers exceptional dietary benefits, including high protein content and essential minerals. This versatile plant serves as a vital food security resource for many regional communities.

  • Scientific name: Fagopyrum cymosum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae and falls under the genus Fagopyrum.
