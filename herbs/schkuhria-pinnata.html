Schkuhria pinnata

Schkuhria pinnata, commonly known as the "bush tea" or "pinnate schkuhria," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat gastrointestinal ailments, reduce inflammation, and support overall wellness through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
