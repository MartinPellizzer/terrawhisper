Prunus persica

Prunus persica, commonly known as the peach tree, is a versatile deciduous plant valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Beyond its sweet fruit, various parts of the tree, including bark and leaves, have been used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and promote overall digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Prunus persica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Prunus, where it is known as Prunus persica.
