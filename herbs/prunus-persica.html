Plant Parts

This plant, Prunus persica, is a versatile medicinal species where various components offer distinct therapeutic properties. The root is often utilized in traditional medicine for its potential calming and anti-inflammatory effects. Most notably, the peach seeds, which contain the internal pit or kernel, are highly valued in various pharmacological applications; however, the seed must be handled with care due to the presence of amygdalin. By understanding the specific medicinal uses of the root and the seed, practitioners can better harness the natural benefits provided by the entire peach plant.
