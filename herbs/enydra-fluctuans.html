Enydra fluctuans

Water cress

Erythrina fluctuans, commonly known as the coral tree, is a striking medicinal plant valued in traditional pharmacology. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. This species plays a vital role in biodiversity, offering significant potential for developing new therapeutic treatments in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Enydra fluctuans
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its scientific name is derived from the genus Enydra.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Within this zone, its presence is documented in the country of Benin. It can also be found growing throughout Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species is native to parts of Ghana and Ivory Coast. Finally, its geographical range extends into Nigeria.
