R. afra, scientifically known as Rauvolfia afra, is a perennial shrub native to sub-Saharan Africa. Widely utilized in traditional African medicine, its leaves and roots possess significant therapeutic properties. It is primarily valued for treating hypertension, anxiety, and digestive ailments, offering a rich source of bioactive alkaloids.
