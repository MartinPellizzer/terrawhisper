Siberian Fir, scientifically known as Abies sibirica, is a majestic coniferous evergreen native to the dense taiga of Northern Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic essential oils, this resilient tree offers potent antibacterial and soothing properties, making it a cornerstone in traditional herbal medicine and modern aromatherapy applications.

Plant Parts

This plant, Abies sibirica, commonly known as the Siberian fir, is highly valued for its complex chemical composition, particularly the presence of various bioactive compounds found throughout its tissues. While the needles and resin are the most prominent sources of medicinal properties, the plant's therapeutic value is largely defined by its rich concentration of terpenes. These organic compounds, including monoterpenes like alpha-pinene and limonene, are found in high amounts within the essential oils of the needles and the sticky resin, providing the plant with its distinct aromatic scent and serving as potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory agents in herbal medicine.
