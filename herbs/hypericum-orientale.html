Hypericum orientale, scientifically known as Hypericum orientale, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern regions. Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hypericum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions within Western Asia and the Caucasus. Its presence is notably documented throughout the North Caucasus region. Additionally, the species can be found extending into the Transcaucasus area. A significant portion of its natural habitat also includes the territory of Türkiye. Together, these locations define the core geographical range of Hypericum orientale.
