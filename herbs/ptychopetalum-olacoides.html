Ptychopetalum olacoides

Ptychopetalum olacoidides, commonly known as "Agondole" in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments, offering a natural approach to wellness through its diverse pharmacological properties and healing potential.

  • Family: Olacaceae
