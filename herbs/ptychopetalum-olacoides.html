Ptychopetalum olacoides

Ptychopetalum olacoides · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (365 studies) · ★★★★★

Scientifically known as Ptychopetalum olacoidoides, is a medicinal plant native to South America, most famously recognized as the source of Muira Puama. Often called "potency wood," it is highly valued in traditional herbalism for its reputed ability to enhance cognitive function, boost libido, and support overall neurological health.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the diverse ecosystems of Northern South America. Its distribution includes the tropical regions of French Guiana and Guyana. Furthermore, the species can be identified throughout the territory of Suriname. In the vast expanse of Brazil, it is specifically located in the northern part of the country. This localized range highlights its presence in the humid landscapes of the northern continent.
