Hofariqun, scientifically known as Hypericum, is a versatile genus of flowering plants renowned for its medicinal properties. Most famous for St. John's Wort, this herb is widely utilized in herbal medicine to support emotional well-being, alleviate mild depression, and promote wound healing through its potent, bioactive phytochemical compounds.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Malpighiales and is a member of the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Hypericum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing across the landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Additionally, its presence extends to the islands of Føroyar. The species is also well-documented throughout the territory of Great Britain. Finally, its range reaches into the coastal areas of Ireland.
