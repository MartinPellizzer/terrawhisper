Jasminum grandiflorum

Spanish jasmine

Jasminum grandiflorum, commonly known as the large-flowered jasmine, is a fragrant climbing shrub celebrated for its stunning, creamy white blossoms. Native to tropical regions, this botanical treasure is prized in traditional medicine for its soothing properties, often utilized to calm the nervous system and enhance overall emotional well-being.

  • Scientific name: Jasminum grandiflorum
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Oleaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Jasminum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the country of Guinea. Moving toward the center of the continent, it can also be found in the West-Central Tropical Africa region of Rwanda. The species further extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Djibouti. Finally, its range in the northeast includes the territories of both Eritrea and Ethiopia.

Plant Parts

The large, white Flowers are not only aesthetically striking but are the most significant part of the plant used for therapeutic purposes, offering aromatic and healing properties.
