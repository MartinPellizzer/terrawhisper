Spanish jasmine, scientifically known as Jasminum grandiflorum, is a captivating evergreen shrub celebrated for its large, intensely fragrant white blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its soothing aromatic properties and potential therapeutic benefits in topical and holistic applications.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the country of Guinea. Moving toward the center of the continent, it can also be found in the West-Central Tropical Africa region of Rwanda. The species further extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Djibouti. Finally, its range in the northeast includes the territories of both Eritrea and Ethiopia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Jasminum grandiflorum, is highly valued for its various components used in traditional and modern applications. The lush green leaves provide a foundational structure for the shrub, but the primary medicinal interest lies in its fragrant flower and the potent flower extract derived from its blossoms. The large, white Flowers are not only aesthetically striking but are the most significant part of the plant used for therapeutic purposes, offering aromatic and healing properties.
