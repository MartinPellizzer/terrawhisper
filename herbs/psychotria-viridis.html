Psychotria viridis

Chacruna

Psychotria viridis, commonly known as Chacruna, is a perennial shrub native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its high concentration of N,N-Dimethyltryptamine (DMT), it is a vital component in the traditional brew Ayahuasca. This sacred plant holds profound spiritual and medicinal significance within indigenous Amazonian shamanic practices.

  • Scientific name: Psychotria viridis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Gentianaales and falls into the family Rubiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Psychotria, specifically as the species Psychotria viridis.

Common Names

Among the Quechua-speaking peoples, it holds the name Samiruka, and in Azerbaijani, it is identified as Yaşıl psixotriya.
