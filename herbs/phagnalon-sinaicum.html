Phagnalon sinaicum

Phagnalon sinaicum, commonly known as the Golden Leaf Herb, is a remarkable medicinal plant revered in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, it is widely utilized to support immune function and reduce inflammation. This versatile botanical offers promising therapeutic potential for modern holistic wellness and natural recovery.

  • Scientific name: Phagnalon sinaicum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Phagnalon sinaicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is categorized under the genus Phagnalon.
