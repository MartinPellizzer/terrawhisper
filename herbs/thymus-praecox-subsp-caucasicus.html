Thymus praecox caucasicus, scientifically known as Thymus praecox subsp. caucasicus, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Caucasus mountains. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this subspecies offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. It is traditionally valued in herbal medicine for supporting digestive health and soothing respiratory ailments.
