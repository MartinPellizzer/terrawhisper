Thymus praecox subsp. caucasicus

Thymus praecox subsp. caucasicus, commonly known as Caucasian creeping thyme, is a resilient perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of the Caucasus. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate blooms, this medicinal plant offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, making it a valuable resource in traditional herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Thymus praecox subsp. caucasicus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Thymus as the subspecies caucasicus.
