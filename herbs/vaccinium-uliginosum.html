Bog bilberry, scientifically known as Vaccinium uliginatum, is a resilient shrub native to North American bogs and forests. Renowned for its small, dark blue berries, this plant is rich in antioxidants and anthocyanins. Traditionally used in folk medicine, it offers potential therapeutic benefits for supporting eye and metabolic health.
