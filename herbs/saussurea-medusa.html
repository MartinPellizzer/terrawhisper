Saussurea medusa

Saussurea medusa, commonly known as the Medusa Saw-wort, is a rare medicinal herb native to the high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional Tibetan medicine, its roots possess potent bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, fever, and various ailments, making it a valuable botanical treasure in mountain ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Saussurea medusa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Saussurea, specifically as the species Saussurea medusa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions within the vast landscape of China. Its distribution is particularly notable in the South-Central and North-Central parts of the country. Furthermore, it can be found growing in the high-altitude terrains of the Qinghai province. The species also inhabits the rugged mountainous areas of Tibet. Finally, its geographical range extends into the remote and diverse environments of Xinjiang.
