Snow lotus, scientifically known as Saussurea medusa, is a rare, high-altitude medicinal herb found in the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. This resilient plant is highly valued for treating various ailments, ranging from respiratory issues to skin conditions, through its bioactive compounds.
