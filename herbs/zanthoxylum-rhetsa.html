Zanthoxylum rhetsa

Zanthoxylum rhetsa, commonly known as the Chinese Prickly Ash, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its pungent, aromatic properties, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and skin conditions. This robust shrub remains a vital resource in herbal pharmacology and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum rhetsa
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
