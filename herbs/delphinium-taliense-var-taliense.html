Delphinium taliense var. taliense

Delphinium taliense var. taliense, commonly known as a rare variety of the larkspur, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific high-altitude regions. Characterized by its striking floral structure, this botanical specimen is increasingly studied for its unique phytochemical properties and its potential role in traditional pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Delphinium taliense var. taliense
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Delphinium taliense var. taliense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling into the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Delphinium.
