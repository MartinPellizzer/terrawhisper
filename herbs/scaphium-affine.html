Scaphium affine

Scaphium affine, commonly known as a valuable medicinal plant in tropical regions, is highly regarded for its diverse therapeutic applications. Primarily used in traditional medicine, various parts of this species are utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and fever, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern healthcare developments.

  • Scientific name: Scaphium affine
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Scaphium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China peninsula, it can be found growing in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, and Vietnam. Additionally, its range extends into the Malesia region, specifically inhabiting the island of Borneo. This wide presence suggests that the species thrives in various tropical environments across these distinct territories. Consequently, the plant is well-established across both mainland and insular Southeast Asian landscapes.
