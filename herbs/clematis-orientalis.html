Clematis orientalis

Clematis orientalis, commonly known as Old Man's Beard, is a resilient climbing perennial celebrated for its enchanting, star-shaped blooms. Native to Europe and Asia, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional herbalism to soothe inflammation and support respiratory health, offering both aesthetic beauty and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Clematis orientalis
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Clematis orientalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it resides in the family Ranunculaceae and is categorized under the genus Clematis.
