Ardisia colorata

Ardisia colorata, commonly known as the coral ardisia, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its vibrant red berries and glossy foliage, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its potent bioactive compounds to treat various inflammatory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Ardisia colorata
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
