Bupleurum marginatum var. stenophyllum

Bupleurum marginatum var. stenophyllum, commonly known as a narrow-leaved bupleurum, is a specialized medicinal herb found in unique arid ecosystems. Renowned for its distinct morphological features, this variety is valued in traditional herbalism for its bioactive compounds, offering significant therapeutic potential in treating various inflammatory and systemic conditions.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum marginatum var. stenophyllum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae and the genus Bupleurum. Specifically, it is identified as the variety stenophyllum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct high-altitude regions. In China, its presence is noted within the South-Central areas and throughout the province of Qinghai. It is also documented growing within the vast terrains of Tibet. Furthermore, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the West Himalaya. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varied mountainous environments.
