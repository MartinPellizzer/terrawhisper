Plant Parts

This plant, Aloe davyana, is primarily valued for the therapeutic properties found within its succulent foliage, specifically the soothing leaf gel. This clear, mucilaginous substance is extracted from the inner parenchyma of the thick leaves and is widely utilized in traditional and modern medicine for its hydrating, anti-inflammatory, and healing capabilities. When applied topically, the leaf gel acts as a cooling agent for skin irritations and burns, making it the most essential medicinal component of the species.
