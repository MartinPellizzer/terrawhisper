Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is notably documented in the country of Ghana. It also grows naturally throughout the territory of Guinea. Additionally, the species can be found across the landscapes of Ivory Coast and Liberia. Finally, its distribution extends further east into Nigeria.

Plant Parts

This plant, Petersianthus macrocarpus, is utilized in traditional medicine through various components of its structure, most notably the stem bark and the general bark. The stem bark is frequently harvested for its bioactive compounds, which are believed to possess therapeutic properties when prepared as decoctions or extracts. The bark itself serves as a primary source of medicinal ingredients used to treat various ailments, making these specific parts essential to the plant's ethnobotanical value.
