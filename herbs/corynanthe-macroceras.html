Corynanthe macroceras belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. This plant is a representative of the family Rubiaceae, a diverse group of flowering plants, and is further categorized under the genus Corynanthe. Ultimately, its specific scientific identity is defined by its species designation, macroceras.
