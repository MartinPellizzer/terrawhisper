Lomelosia comosa

Northeastern scabious

Lomelosia comosa, commonly known as the "guaco" plant, is a versatile medicinal climber native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and bronchodilatory properties, it is frequently used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, coughs, and asthma, offering a natural approach to managing pulmonary health effectively.

  • Scientific name: Lomelosia comosa
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales, it is situated in the family Caprifoliaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Lomelosia as the species Lomelosia comosa.
