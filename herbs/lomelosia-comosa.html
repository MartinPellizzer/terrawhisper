Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range primarily concentrated within the northern regions of Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found documented in Buryatiya, Chita, and Irkutsk. Its distribution also extends into parts of China, where it is present in the Inner Mongolia region. Furthermore, the species is known to inhabit the Manchuria area of China. Together, these locations define the particular environmental niche where the species thrives.
