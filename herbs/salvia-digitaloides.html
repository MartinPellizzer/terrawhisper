The life history of Salvia digitaloides is characterized by its classification as a hemicryptophyte, a growth form where the perennating buds are located at or just below the soil surface, protected by leaf litter or the substrate itself. Within the spectrum of life forms, it specifically functions as a hemicryptophyte, distinguishing its survival strategy from other categories such as phanerophytes, chamaephytes, cryptophytes, or therophytes. This life history pattern allows the plant to persist through unfavorable seasons by maintaining its regenerative structures at the ground level, ensuring its ability to emerge and resume vegetative growth once environmental conditions become suitable.
