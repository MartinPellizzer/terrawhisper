Hibiscus taiwanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Hibiscus taiwanensis?

Hibiscus taiwanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further classified into the subclass Magnoliidae and belongs to the order Malvales. As a member of the family Malvaceae, it is situated within the genus Hibiscus, specifically identified by its species name, taiwanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Hibiscus taiwanensis has woody, perennial shrub characteristics featuring elliptical to lanceolate leaves with serrated margins and large, trumpet-shaped flowers that typically display shades of pink or red. The leaves are arranged alternately along the stems and possess long petioles connected to a central vein. Its flowers feature a prominent central staminal column which is a defining trait of the Malvaceae family. The plant produces small, capsule-like woody fruits that contain numerous tiny seeds. These structural features allow the species to thrive in its specific subtropical forest habitats.
