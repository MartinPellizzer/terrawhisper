Rosa kweichowensis

Rosa kweichowensis, commonly known as the Kweicho Rose, is a rare medicinal shrub valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its unique floral structure, this plant contains potent bioactive compounds. Researchers are currently exploring its antioxidant properties and potential therapeutic applications for treating inflammatory conditions and supporting overall cellular health.
