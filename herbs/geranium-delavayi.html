Geranium delavayi

What's the taxonomical classification of Geranium delavayi?

Geranium delavayi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Geraniales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Geraniaceae, specifically situated in the genus Geranium, and is identified by its unique species name, delavayi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Geranium delavayi has a perennial growth habit characterized by deeply lobed, palmately divided leaves that typically measure several centimeters in width and possess serrated margins. The foliage is often covered in fine hairs, providing a textured appearance to the plant's base. During its flowering season, the plant produces small, five-petaled flowers that can range in color from pale pink to light violet. These blossoms emerge from slender, upright stems that rise above the dense basal foliage. The reproductive structures include central pistils and surrounding stamens typical of the Geraniaceae family.
