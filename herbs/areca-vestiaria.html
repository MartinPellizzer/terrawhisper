Areca vestiaria

Areca catechu, commonly known as the betel nut palm, is a significant tropical plant prized for its seeds. Widely used in traditional medicine and cultural rituals, the nut possesses stimulant properties. Beyond its psychoactive effects, it holds various medicinal applications, particularly in treating digestive issues and promoting oral health.

  • Scientific name: Areca vestiaria
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Areca catechu (often referred to in botanical contexts as Areca vestiaria), belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Arecales and the family Arecaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Areca.
