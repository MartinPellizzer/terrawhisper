Purple-leaved False-thorn, scientifically known as Albizia antunesiana, is a striking deciduous tree native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its distinctive foliage and medicinal properties, it is widely used in traditional healing. Its bark and leaves are frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues.

Distribution

This plant is native to several distinct regions across the African continent. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is recorded in Burundi, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, it can also be found in Tanzania. Furthermore, its distribution extends into South Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Angola. Together, these locations highlight a broad range spanning from West-Central to Southern Africa.

This plant, Albizia antunesiana, possesses various anatomical features that are utilized in traditional medicine. Below the surface, the extensive roots serve as a vital storage system for the tree, and the root itself is frequently harvested to prepare decoctions for treating various ailments.
