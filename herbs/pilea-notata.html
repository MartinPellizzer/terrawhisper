Pilea notata

What's the taxonomical classification of Pilea notata?

Pilea notata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales. The plant is a member of the family Urticaceae, specifically falling within the genus Pilea, and is identified by its specific species name, notaata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pilea notata has ovate to elliptical leaves that feature distinct, silvery-white patches or variegation against a deep green background. Small, inconspicuous flowers typically emerge from the leaf axils during its blooming period. The overall structure of the plant is compact, often forming a lush, mounded shape as it grows.
