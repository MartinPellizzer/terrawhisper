Astragalus tongolensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Astragalus tongolensis?

Astragalus tongolensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Astragalus and is specifically categorized as the species tongolensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Astragalus tongolenis has a perennial growth habit characterized by trifoliate leaves with small, oval-shaped leaflets and dense, papilionaceous flowers that are typically pink or purple in color. The stems are often woody at the base and exhibit a spreading or ascending growth pattern. Its leaves are arranged alternately along the stem and feature fine pubescence that gives the foliage a soft texture. The inflorescence consists of small clusters or racemes containing multiple pea-like blossoms. Root structures are deep and fibrous, helping the plant stabilize itself in its specific mountain habitat.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is endemic to the high-altitude regions of the Dolpa district in Nepal. It specifically inhabits the alpine zones located within the Himalayan mountain range. These populations are typically found growing in rocky crevices and alpine meadows at significant elevations. The species is restricted to this very narrow geographic range, making it a localized specialist of the Nepalese highlands. Due to this limited distribution, the plant is highly dependent on the specific environmental conditions of its native mountain habitat.
