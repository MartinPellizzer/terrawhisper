Lactuca tatarica

Lactuca tatarica, commonly known as the Tatar lettuce, is a perennial herb native to Central and Eastern Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds like lactucin. Often utilized for its sedative and analgesic properties, this plant plays a significant role in treating insomnia and soothing nervous tension.

  • Scientific name: Lactuca tatarica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Lactuca.
