Apple chile, scientifically known as Capsicum pubescens, is a unique perennial plant distinguished by its hairy leaves and dark, chocolate-colored seeds. Native to the Andes, this cultivar offers a mild, nutty flavor and impressive frost resistance, making it a prized choice for gardeners and culinary enthusiasts seeking specialty heat.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution spanning across several distinct tropical regions. In the Malesia region, it can be found specifically within Jawa. Its presence extends into Mexico, where it inhabits both the Mexico Gulf and the Mexico Southwest areas. Furthermore, the species is documented throughout parts of Central America. Specifically, its range includes the countries of Costa Rica and Guatemala.
