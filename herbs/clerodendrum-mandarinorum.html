Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted across the South-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is also widely distributed throughout Southeast China. Beyond Chinese borders, its habitat extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, Vietnam serves as another key location for its natural occurrence.
