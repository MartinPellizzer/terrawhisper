Clerodendrum mandarinorum

Clerodendrum mandarinorum, commonly known as the Mandarin Clerodendrum, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional herbal practices. Native to East Asia, this plant is widely utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, playing a crucial role in treating various ailments within folk medicine.

  • Scientific name: Clerodendrum mandarinorum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Clerodendrum.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted across the South-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is also widely distributed throughout Southeast China. Beyond Chinese borders, its habitat extends into the Indo-China peninsula.
