English hawthorn

Crataegus laevigata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (910 studies) · ★★★★★

English hawthorn, scientifically known as Crataeagus laevigata, is a deciduous shrub revered in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its heart-protective properties, it is frequently utilized to support cardiovascular health and regulate blood pressure. This resilient plant offers a rich source of bioactive compounds, making it a cornerstone of botanical medicine.

The reproduction of Crataegus laevigata is driven by sexual processes, as both asexual reproduction and self-fertilization are absent. These fruits have an average width of 1.17 cm and contain seeds with a mean mass of approximately 0.1598 g, ranging from a minimum of 0.04554 g to a maximum of 0.26217 g.
