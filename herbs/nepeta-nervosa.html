Nepeta nervosa

Nepeta nervosa, commonly known as nervous catnip, is a perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Characterized by its delicate, serrated leaves and small white flowers, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism. It is often studied for its calming properties and its unique aromatic qualities in botanical applications.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta nervosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Nepeta.
