Rumex patientia

Patience dock

Rumex patientia, commonly known as patience dock, is a perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its lance-shaped leaves and distinctive reddish-brown seeds, it thrives in moist habitats. This plant is frequently utilized for its astringent properties, often helping to soothe digestive upsets and support skin health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Rumex patientia
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic classification places it within the genus Rumex.
