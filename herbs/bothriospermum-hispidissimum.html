What's the taxonomical classification of Bothriospermum hispidissimum?

Bothriospermum hispidissimum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Boraginales. This plant is a representative of the family Boraginaceae, falling specifically under the genus Bothriospermum, and is identified by its unique species designation, hispidissimum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Bothriospermum hispidissimum has a woody, climbing habit characterized by stems covered in dense, stiff, bristly hairs and leaves that are oppositely arranged, elliptical to ovate, and possess prominent venation. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the leaf axils, often appearing in clusters. These blooms eventually give way to small, fleshy berries that serve as the plant's primary fruit.
