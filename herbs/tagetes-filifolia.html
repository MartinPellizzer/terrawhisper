Tagetes filifolia

Tagetes filifolia, commonly known as threadleaf marigold, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its fine, needle-like foliage and delicate yellow blooms, this plant offers significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices for its potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Tagetes filifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Tagetes.
