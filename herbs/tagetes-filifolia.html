Irish-lace, scientifically known as Tagetes filifolia, is a delicate, thread-leaf marigold cherished for its fine, feathery foliage and vibrant blooms. Native to arid regions, this resilient perennial offers unique medicinal properties, traditionally used in herbal remedies to support skin health and provide soothing, aromatic benefits in wellness practices.
