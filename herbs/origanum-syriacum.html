Origanum syriacum

Origanum syriacum, commonly known as Bible hyssop or Syrian oregano, is a fragrant perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Highly valued for its aromatic essential oils, this medicinal plant offers potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a vital component in traditional herbal remedies and natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Origanum syriacum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions within Western Asia. Its distribution begins in the Mediterranean island of Cyprus. It is also widely found across the Levant, specifically in Lebanon, Syria, and Palestine. Furthermore, the species extends into the Sinai Peninsula. Finally, its range reaches northward into the territory of Türkiye.
