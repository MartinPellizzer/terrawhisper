Origanum syriacum, scientifically known as Origanum syriacum, is a fragrant Mediterranean shrub widely celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Commonly called Bible hyssop, this aromatic herb is rich in essential oils like carvacrol. It is traditionally utilized for its potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory benefits in various medicinal applications.

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions within Western Asia. Its distribution begins in the Mediterranean island of Cyprus. Finally, its range reaches northward into the territory of Türkiye.
