Echinacea sanguinea

Echinacea sanguinea, commonly known as the blood-red coneflower, is a striking perennial celebrated for its vibrant, deep crimson blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its immune-boosting properties, offering natural support against seasonal ailments and promoting overall wellness through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Echinacea sanguinea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Echinacea.
