Prunus itosakura

Prunus itosakura, commonly known as the Itosakura or weeping cherry, is a cherished ornamental plant celebrated for its delicate, drooping blossoms. Beyond its aesthetic elegance, traditional botanical practices value its potential medicinal properties, often utilizing its bark or flowers in holistic remedies to promote wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Prunus itosakura
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Prunus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Prunus itosakura, is referred to by various common names across different languages and regions. In Korean, it is called 올벚나무, while in Japanese, it is identified as エドヒガン. Interestingly, certain linguistic contexts utilize the name Prunus spachiana, which is seen in Cebuano (ceb) and Swedish (sv). Additionally, in Persian, the plant is known as ادوهیگان.

This plant is native to specific regions within the continent of Eastern Asia. Its primary geographical distribution is centered around the islands of Japan. Additionally, the species can be found growing naturally throughout the Korean Peninsula. These two locations represent the core environmental range for the plant's existence. Therefore, its habitat is strictly limited to these particular East Asian territories.
