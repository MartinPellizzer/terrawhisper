Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key ecological regions in the Indo-Pacific. Within the Malesia zone, its presence is documented in both the Maluku islands and the Philippines. It also extends into the Papuasia region, specifically inhabiting the island of New Guinea. Furthermore, its range reaches as far south as Australia, where it can be found in Queensland. Together, these locations highlight the plant's significant presence across diverse tropical and subtropical territories.
