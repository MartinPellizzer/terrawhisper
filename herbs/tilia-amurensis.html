Tilia amurensis

Tilia amurensis, commonly known as the Amur Linden, is a deciduous tree cherished for its fragrant, pale yellow blossoms. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, its flowers are used to craft soothing teas that alleviate stress, reduce fever, and calm inflammation, offering a gentle, natural remedy for relaxation and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Tilia amurensis
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Tilia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the Russian Far East. Specifically, it can be found growing in the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye territories. Its range extends southward into the neighboring territories of China. Within China, it is documented to inhabit the regions of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Consequently, the geographical presence of Tilia amurensis spans both the Russian and Chinese borders.
