Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the Russian Far East. Specifically, it can be found growing in the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye territories. Its range extends southward into the neighboring territories of China. Within China, it is documented to inhabit the regions of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Consequently, the geographical presence of Tilia amurensis spans both the Russian and Chinese borders.
