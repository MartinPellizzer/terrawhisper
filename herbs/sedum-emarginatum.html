Sedum emarginatum

Sedum emarginatum, commonly known as the emarginate stonecrop, is a resilient succulent native to various rocky habitats. Valued in traditional herbalism for its potential healing properties, this plant thrives in well-drained soils. It serves as a fascinating subject for those exploring botanical biodiversity and natural medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Sedum emarginatum
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifragales. Finally, it is a member of the family Crassulaceae and falls under the genus Sedum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Sedum emarginatum, is recognized by several different common names across various Chinese linguistic contexts. In standard Chinese (zh), it is frequently referred to as 凹叶景天 (āoyè jǐngtiān), a name also used in Simplified Chinese (zh-hans) and specifically within Mainland China (zh-cn). Additionally, it is known by the names 岩板菜 (yánbǎncài) and 石雀还阳 (shíquè huányáng) in certain regional or traditional contexts.
