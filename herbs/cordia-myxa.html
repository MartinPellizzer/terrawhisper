Assyrian plum, scientifically known as Cordia myxa, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized in traditional medicine. Native to arid regions, its edible fruit and leaves offer therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and digestive issues, this plant remains a botanical treasure.
