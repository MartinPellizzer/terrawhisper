Stephanotis floribunda

Stephanotis floribunda, commonly known as Madagascar jasmine, is a captivating evergreen climber prized for its fragrant, waxy white blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant traditional value in herbal medicine. Its aromatic properties and unique chemical profile make it a subject of interest in botanical pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Stephanotis floribunda
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant, Stephanotis floribunda, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales and the family Apocynaceae. It is further identified by the genus Stephanotis.
