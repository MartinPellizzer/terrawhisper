Trapa manshurica

What's the taxonomical classification of Trapa manshurica?

Trapa manshurica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, the plant is placed in the order Myrtales and the family Lythraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Trapa and its specific species designation, manshurica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Trapa manshurica has floating, rosette-forming leaves that are kidney-shaped or circular, typically measuring 10 to 30 centimeters in diameter with deeply notched bases and serrated margins. These long-petioled leaves form dense mats on the water surface to maximize sunlight absorption. The plant produces small, white, five-petaled flowers that emerge from the center of the leaf rosette. Its most distinctive feature is the woody, nut-like fruit which is triangular in shape and equipped with sharp, protective spines. These spines serve as a defense mechanism against herbivores and help the fruit anchor in aquatic environments.
