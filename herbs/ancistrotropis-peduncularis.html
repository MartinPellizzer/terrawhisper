Ancistrotropis peduncularis

Anciastrotropis peduncularis, commonly known as a member of the Apocynaceae family, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique flowering structure, it possesses bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. Understanding its phytochemical properties is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Ancistrotropis peduncularis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized under the family Fabaceae. Within this botanical hierarchy, it is specifically identified by the genus Anciastroptis.

Common Names

This plant, Anastrotropis peduncularis, is known by various regional names across different languages. In English, it is commonly referred to as the pedunculate milkweed, while in Spanish, it is known as la flor de leche. In Portuguese, the species is called flor de leite, and in the local context of certain regions, it may also be referred to as la flor de la leche.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range that extends from North America through Central America. In Mexico, its distribution is quite extensive, covering the Northwest, Southwest, and Southeast regions of the country. As it moves southward, the species is also found within the borders of Belize. Additionally, the plant reaches further south into the tropical landscapes of Costa Rica. Together, these locations highlight a diverse habitat spanning multiple climatic zones.
