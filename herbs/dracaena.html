Dracaena

Dracaena, commonly known as Dragon Trees, are striking ornamental plants prized for their architectural foliage and resilience. Beyond their beauty in interior design, various species hold significant roles in traditional medicine. They are frequently utilized for their antioxidant properties and ability to support respiratory and digestive health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Dracaena
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Asparagaceae and falls under the genus Dracaena.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Dragon Blood tree, is highly valued for its unique medicinal properties derived from its various structures. The most significant part is the thick, sturdy stem, which serves as the foundation for its dramatic canopy. Within the inner layers of the bark, the tree produces a distinctive, deep red resin that has been used for centuries in traditional medicine and alchemy. This precious plant resin, often referred to as "dragon's blood," is harvested by making incisions in the trunk and is prized for its ability to aid in wound healing and skin treatments.
