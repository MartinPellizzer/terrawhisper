Bergenia purpurascens

Bergenia purpurascens, commonly known as the purple bergenia, is a resilient perennial herb native to high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned for its thick, fleshy leaves and vibrant blooms, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional practices for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and astringent properties, supporting various healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Bergenia purpurascens
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifragales, it is further categorized under the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Bergenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specialized distribution across several distinct mountainous regions of Asia. In China, it is primarily found within the South-Central provinces as well as the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it thrives in the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout the East Himalaya range. Finally, it can be located within the rugged terrains of Nepal.
