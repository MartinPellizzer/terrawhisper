Bergenia purpurascens, scientifically known as Bergenia purpurascens, is a perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its fleshy leaves contain bioactive compounds with potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and skin inflammations.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specialized distribution across several distinct mountainous regions of Asia. In China, it is primarily found within the South-Central provinces as well as the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it thrives in the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout the East Himalaya range. Finally, it can be located within the rugged terrains of Nepal.
