Eustoma russellianum

Eustoma russellianum, commonly known as the Russian bellflower, is a rare medicinal herb found in specific high-altitude ecosystems. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to explore new applications in traditional healing and modern pharmacological developments for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Eustoma russellianum
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Eustoma russellianum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Gentianaceae and falls under the genus Eustoma.

Distribution

This plant is found across several specific regions within the United States. In the Northwestern U.S.A., its presence is noted in the states of Colorado, Montana, and Wyoming. Moving toward the central part of the country, its range extends into the North-Central U.S.A. area. Specifically, it can be located within the borders of Kansas.
