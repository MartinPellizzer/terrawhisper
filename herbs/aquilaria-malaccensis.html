Eagle wood

Aquilaria malaccensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (93 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Eagle wood, scientifically known as Aquilaria malaçensis, is a precious tropical tree highly revered in traditional medicine and perfumery. Renowned for producing agarwood—a resinous, aromatic substance formed under stress—it offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory and sedative properties, making it a cornerstone of ancient healing traditions.

Plant Parts

This plant, Aquilaria malaccensis, is highly valued for various components used in traditional medicine and perfumery. The most precious part is the fragrant, resinous agarwood, which forms within the heartwood when the tree is infected by specific fungi. The stem bark is also utilized for its medicinal properties, often used in decoctions to treat various ailments. Beneath the surface, the roots and the primary root system contain bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's therapeutic profile. Additionally, the leaves are frequently employed in traditional practices for their various healing applications.
