Aquilaria malaccensis

Aquilaria malaćensis, commonly known as agarwood or aloeswood, is a precious medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its fragrant, resinous heartwood, it has been used for centuries in traditional medicine and perfumery. This species holds immense cultural and economic value due to its unique therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Aquilaria malaccensis
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Malvales and the family Thymelaeaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Aquilaria.
