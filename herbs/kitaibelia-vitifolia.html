Kitaibelia vitifolia

Kitaibelia vitifolia, commonly known as "chichicaste" or "vitiifolia," is a climbing medicinal shrub native to Central and South America. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification
