Lycopodium clavatum

Lycopodium clavatum, commonly known as clubmoss, is a perennial herbaceous plant prized in traditional medicine. Historically used to treat digestive issues and respiratory ailments, its spores have also played a role in various topical applications. This ancient botanical holds significant interest for its unique chemical properties and historical therapeutic uses.

  • Scientific name: Lycopodium clavatum
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Lycopodiidae and order Lycopodiales, it is classified under the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, its specific biological identification is within the genus Lycopodium.
