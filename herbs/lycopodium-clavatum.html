Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as running clubmoss, is utilized in its entirety for medicinal purposes, as the whole plants are often harvested to prepare traditional remedies. Although it is technically a lycopod rather than a true moss, the entire structure—including its creeping rhizomes, scale-like leaves, and distinctive spore-bearing strobili—contributes to its biological identity. In various herbal practices, the whole plants are collected and processed to extract the bioactive compounds contained within their complex physical forms.
