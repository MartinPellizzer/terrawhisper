Scientific literature: Very Extensive (235 studies) · ★★★★★

Cucurbita foetidissima, is a versatile medicinal plant native to arid regions of Africa. Commonly known as the stink melon, it is highly valued in traditional medicine. Its fruits, leaves, and seeds possess significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties, offering therapeutic potential for treating various ailments and skin conditions.
