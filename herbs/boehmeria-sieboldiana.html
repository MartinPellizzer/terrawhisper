Boehmeria sieboldiana

Boehmeria sieboldiana, commonly known as Siebold's False Nettle, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, this plant is recognized for its distinctive foliage and medicinal properties. It is frequently studied for its potential bioactive compounds, offering therapeutic benefits in various herbal healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Boehmeria sieboldiana
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Urticaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Boehmeria, which includes the species Boehmeria sieboldiana.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Boehmeria sieboldiana, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently called Siebold's False Nettle, while in Japanese, it is known as Yatsude. In Chinese, the plant is commonly called Yan Hu Die.
