Maytenus amazonica

Maytenus amazonica, commonly known as a medicinal shrub from the Amazon rainforest, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Rich in bioactive alkaloids and terpenoids, this plant is traditionally utilized by indigenous communities for its diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Maytenus amazonica
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Celastrales and falls into the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Maytenus.
