Glycyrrhiza yunnanensis

Glycyrrhiza yunnanensis, commonly known as Yunnan Licorice, is a vital medicinal herb prized in traditional Chinese medicine. Characterized by its distinct root system, this plant offers potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. It is widely utilized to soothe respiratory issues, harmonize herbal formulas, and support digestive health and immunity.

  • Scientific name: Glycyrrhiza yunnanensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Glycyrrhiza, specifically as the species Glycyrrhiza yunnanensis.
