White box, scientifically known as Eucalyptus albens, is a resilient Australian native tree prized for its medicinal properties. Its aromatic leaves contain essential oils with potent antiseptic and decongestant qualities. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, this versatile species offers significant therapeutic potential in both folk medicine and modern pharmacology.
