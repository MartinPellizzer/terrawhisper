Eucalyptus albens

White box

Eucalyptus albens, commonly known as White Gum, is a resilient Australian native valued for its medicinal properties. Its aromatic leaves contain essential oils with potent antiseptic and decongestant qualities. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and skin irritations, this hardy tree remains a vital resource in holistic herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus albens
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae and the genus Eucalyptus.
