Origanum dictamnus

Origanum dictamnus, commonly known as Dittany of Crete, is a legendary perennial herb native to the rocky terrains of Crete. Renowned in ancient herbalism for its potent healing properties, this aromatic plant has historically been revered as a powerful remedy for wounds, digestive ailments, and various reproductive health concerns.

  • Scientific name: Origanum dictamnus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Origanum dictamnus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is placed within the genus Origanum.
