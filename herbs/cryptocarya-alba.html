Cryptocarya alba

Cryptocarya alba, commonly known as peperuco, is a versatile evergreen tree native to South American forests. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and medicinal properties, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments. This botanical species plays a vital role in local ecosystems and traditional pharmacopeias.

  • Scientific name: Cryptocarya alba
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Southern America
