Dahlia pinnata

Garden dahlia

Dahlia pinnata, commonly known as the Pinnate Dahlia, is a striking perennial celebrated for its vibrant, multi-layered blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various parts of the plant have been utilized in traditional medicine to treat diverse ailments and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Dahlia pinnata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is classified in the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dahlia, specifically as the species Dahlia pinnata.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Dahlia pinnata, is recognized by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In German, it is referred to as the Großfiedrige Dahlie, while in Welsh, it is simply known as Dahlia. It holds the name کوکب in Persian, and in Central and Eastern Europe, it is identified as jiřinkka zahradní in Czech, kerti dália in Hungarian, and in some contexts, it is also known as Dahlia in Welsh.
