Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is specifically documented within the borders of Benin. Additionally, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Burkina. The species also inhabits parts of the Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends to the nation of Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Crateva religiosa, utilizes its leaf as a primary component in traditional medicinal practices. The foliage is often harvested for its various bioactive compounds, which are believed to possess anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. In many herbal remedies, the leaves are processed into decoctions or topical applications to treat skin ailments and other systemic conditions.
