Fridericia chica

Fridericia chica, commonly known as Chica or Pata de Vaca, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Fridericia chica
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Bignoniaceae and is identified by the genus Fridericia.
