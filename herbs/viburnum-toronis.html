Viburnum torminale, scientifically known as Viburnum torminale, is a deciduous shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, its berries are rich in antioxidants and tannins. These fruits are often employed to support digestive health and provide nutritional support through their potent bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range within the western regions of the South American continent. Its natural habitat is primarily concentrated in the mountainous areas of Colombia. Furthermore, the species can also be found growing throughout Ecuador. These two countries represent the core geographical distribution for this medicinal variety. Consequently, its presence is limited to these particular western South American territories.
