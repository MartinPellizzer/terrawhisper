Large-leafed dogwood, scientifically known as Cornus macrophylla, is a striking deciduous shrub native to western North America. Renowned for its oversized, textured foliage and delicate white blossoms, this resilient plant offers significant ecological value, providing essential habitats and nourishment for diverse wildlife within its montane forest ecosystems.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In the Russian Far East, its presence can be documented on the island of Sakhalin. Its range extends significantly into Western Asia, specifically within the borders of Afghanistan. Within China, it is found across diverse territories including China North-Central and China South-Central. Additionally, the species reaches a southern extent in the tropical province of Hainan.
