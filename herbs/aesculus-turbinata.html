Aesculus turbinata

Japanese horse chestnut

Aesculus turbinata, commonly known as the Japanese horse chestnut, is a striking deciduous tree valued for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Native to East Asia, its unique foliage and distinctive flower clusters make it a garden favorite, while its bioactive compounds offer intriguing possibilities in traditional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Aesculus turbinata
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aesculus.
