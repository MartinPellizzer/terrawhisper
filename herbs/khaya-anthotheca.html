Khaya anthotheca

East african mahogany

Khaya anthotheca, commonly known as the African Mahogany, is a majestic evergreen tree native to tropical Africa. Beyond its valuable timber, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as skin infections, inflammation, and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Khaya anthotheca
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Meliaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Khaya.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the tropical regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is recorded in the nation of Guinea. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found spanning across Burundi and the Central African Republic. It is also distributed throughout the Congo and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Consequently, its habitat is primarily defined by these specific West and West-Central African territories.
