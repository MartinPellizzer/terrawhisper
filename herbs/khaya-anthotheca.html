East African mahogany, scientifically known as Khaya anthotheca, is a majestic timber tree indigenous to African tropical forests. Beyond its valuable wood, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.
