Atractylodes

Atractylodes, commonly known as Atractylodes or Atractylodes macrocephala, is a vital medicinal herb in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its powerful digestive properties, this rhizomatous plant is used to strengthen the spleen, resolve dampness, and boost energy. It remains a cornerstone in treating gastrointestinal issues and enhancing immunity.

  • Scientific name: Atractylodes
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Atractylodes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found inhabiting the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye territories. Its range extends significantly into China, where it is present in the South-Central regions. Additionally, the species is documented within the Inner Mongolia area of China. Together, these locations define the specific natural habitats where Atractylodes thrives.
