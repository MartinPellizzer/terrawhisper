This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found inhabiting the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye territories. Its range extends significantly into China, where it is present in the South-Central regions. Additionally, the species is documented within the Inner Mongolia area of China. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known in traditional medicine, utilizes its underground structures as the primary source of therapeutic compounds. The most significant part used for medicinal purposes is the rhizomes, which are harvested and processed to extract essential oils and bioactive substances. Whether referred to in its singular form as a rhizome or by its specific botanical designation, Rhizoma Atractylodes, these thickened stems serve as the vital component in various herbal formulations designed to support digestive health.
