What's the taxonomical classification of Caragana bicolor?

Caragana bicolor belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the order Fabales and is a member of the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Caragana and is designated as the species bicolor.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?
