Salvia verticillata

Salvia verticillata, commonly known as whorled sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to East Asia. Characterized by its striking clusters of small, tubular flowers, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its calming properties and potential antioxidant benefits, offering a unique blend of ornamental beauty and therapeutic utility.

  • Scientific name: Salvia verticillata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Salvia.
