Lomatogonium rotatum

Lomatogonium rotatum, commonly known as the "Wheel Plant," is a significant medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This plant serves as a vital natural resource in various ethnobotanical traditions.

  • Scientific name: Lomatogonium rotatum
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Lomatogonium, and its species, rotatum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the Eurasian continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be identified within the territory of Iceland. Moving toward Eastern Europe, the species is documented throughout North European Russia. Its range extends significantly into Siberia, where it is found in the Altay region. Furthermore, the plant is distributed across the Siberian areas of Buryatiya and Chitá.
