Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin and Ghana. Additionally, its presence extends into the Ivory Coast and Nigeria. The species is also documented to occur in Togo. These specific countries represent the known geographical range for this medicinal plant.
