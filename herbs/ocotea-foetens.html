Ocotea foetens

Ocotea foetens, commonly known as Til, is an evergreen tree native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its aromatic, fragrant foliage and dense canopy, it thrives in coastal regions. Beyond its ornamental value, this species holds significant ecological importance, providing essential shelter and resources within diverse forest ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Ocotea foetens
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Ocotea.
