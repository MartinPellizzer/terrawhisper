Reproduction

The reproduction of Ocotea foetens is characterized by a bisexual sexual reproduction system where self-fertilization is absent, relying instead on biotic pollination mechanisms. The flowering period typically commences in June and extends through September. The plant utilizes an insect-based pollination syndrome to facilitate fertilization. These fruits are characterized by a black color and reach a consistent length of approximately 2.5 cm. The reproductive output is prolific, with the fruit containing more than 1000 seeds, each having a mean seed mass of 0.8 g.
