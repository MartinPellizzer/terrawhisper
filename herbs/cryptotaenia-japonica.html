What's the taxonomical classification of Cryptotaenia japonica?

Cryptotaenia japonica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is placed in the order Apiales and within the family Apiaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific genus, Cryptotaenia, and its unique species designation, japonica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cryptotaenia japonica has a perennial growth habit characterized by a rhizomatous root system and compound leaves consisting of multiple leaflets. These leaves are arranged in an alternate pattern along the stems, which remain relatively low to the ground. During its flowering stage, the plant produces small, inconspicuous blossoms that emerge from the leaf axils. The overall structure is compact, making it well-suited for shaded forest floor environments.

According to a study published by "Plant biotechnology (Tokyo, Japan)", Cryptotaenia japonica is consumed as a traditional vegetable and has medicinal applications. The study describes methods using shoot apex explants from seedlings to induce somatic embryogenesis. When these explants were treated with 0.7 M sucrose for 3 or 6 weeks, somatic embryos formed after 7 weeks in stress-free conditions. Approximately 800 somatic embryos were formed from each explant after 2 weeks of culture in Murashige and Skoog liquid medium. These somatic embryos were produced continuously during 37 weeks under the same culture conditions. The resulting artificial seeds achieved a high germination rate of 72%.
