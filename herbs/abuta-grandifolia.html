This plant, Abuta grandifolia, is recognized for the medicinal properties found within its structural components, specifically its bark. The stem barks are often harvested for their bioactive compounds, which are concentrated within the protective outer layer of the plant. By utilizing both the bark and the stem barks, traditional practitioners can extract the essential elements necessary for various therapeutic applications.
