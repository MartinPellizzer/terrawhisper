Nepeta crispa

Nepeta crispa, commonly known as curly catnip, is a charming perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Featuring distinctive crinkled, aromatic foliage and delicate lavender flowers, this hardy plant thrives in sunny gardens. Beyond its ornamental appeal, it is valued for its calming properties and ability to attract beneficial pollinators.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta crispa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic descent places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, belonging to the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Nepeta.
