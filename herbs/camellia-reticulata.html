Camellia reticulata

What's the taxonomical classification of Camellia reticulata?

Camellia reticulata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical structure, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Ericales, it falls under the family Theaceae. Within this family, the plant is identified by the genus Camellia, and its specific classification is completed by the species name reticulata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Camellia reticulata has dark green, elliptical leaves with serrated margins and a glossy texture, complemented by large, multi-petaled flowers that often feature striking white or pink hues. The foliage is densely arranged along woody stems, providing a lush backdrop for its seasonal blooms. These flowers typically possess a prominent central cluster of bright yellow stamens that contrast sharply with the petal color. The plant develops a rounded or spreading growth habit as it matures into a perennial shrub. What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is native to the subtropical regions of southern China, specifically inhabiting the provinces of Fujian, Guangdong, Guangxi, and Zhejiang. It typically thrives in moist, shaded environments such as forest edges and hilly woodlands. The species is most commonly found at low to moderate elevations where the humidity remains consistently high. Due to its specific environmental requirements, its natural range is relatively restricted compared to more widespread ornamental plants.
