Kopsia singapurensis

Kopsia singapurensis, commonly known as the Singapore Kopsia, is a versatile tropical evergreen shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its glossy foliage and fragrant blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional medicinal practices often utilize its various parts to treat ailments, highlighting its importance in regional biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Kopsia singapurensis
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae and the genus Kopsia. Specifically, the species is identified as Kopsia singapurensis.
