What's the taxonomical classification of Myrsine stolonifera?

Myrsine stolonifera belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic descent through the order Ericales, the plant is categorized under the family Primulaceae. Finally, its specific identification is defined by its placement within the genus Myrsine, with the unique species designation stolonifera.
