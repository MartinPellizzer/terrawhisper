Eumachia oleoides

Eumachia oleoides, commonly known as the Chinese hawthorn, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional practices. Rich in organic acids and flavonoids, it is primarily used to promote digestion, alleviate stomach discomfort, and support cardiovascular health. This potent plant offers significant therapeutic potential for managing metabolic and digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Eumachia oleoides
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Gentianaales and the family Rubiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Eumachia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily characterized by a very specific and localized geographical distribution. Its natural occurrence is confined to the unique island environments of the Southwestern Pacific. Specifically, Eumachia oleoides can be found growing within the territory of New Caledonia. This narrow range suggests that the species is highly adapted to the particular climate and soil conditions of this region. Consequently, the plant remains an endemic feature of the New Caledonian landscape.
