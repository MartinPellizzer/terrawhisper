Meistera muricarpa

Meistera muricarpa, commonly known as the forest berry shrub, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments. Scientific research continues to explore its bioactive compounds for potential pharmacological applications and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Meistera muricarpa
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is classified under the family Zingiberaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Meistera and the species Meistera muricarpa.

Common Names

This plant, Meistera muricarpa, is known by various regional names depending on the local language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the berry-fruited meistera, while in Swahili, it is known as mti wa mchele.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in Hainan and throughout the Southeast portion of the country. Its presence extends into Indo-China, where it inhabits the landscapes of both Laos and Vietnam. Additionally, the species is recorded within the Malesia region, specifically located in the Philippines. Together, these locations highlight the diverse tropical and subtropical habitats where the species thrives.
