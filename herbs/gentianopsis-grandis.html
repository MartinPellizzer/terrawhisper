Gentianopsis grandis

What's the taxonomical classification of Gentianopsis grandis?

Gentianopsis grandis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It follows the taxonomic order Gentianales and is a member of the family Gentianaceae. Ultimately, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Gentianopsis, and its species designation, grandis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Gentianopsis grandis has large, trumpet-shaped flowers that measure between 5 and 10 centimeters in diameter and feature five bright blue petals with a distinctively pale or white center. The leaves are thick, leathery, and elliptical in shape, typically growing in basal rosettes with prominent veins. Its stems are relatively short and sturdy, supporting the heavy floral structures during the blooming season. The plant often grows in rocky or alpine environments where its robust foliage can withstand harsh conditions. This morphological structure allows the species to thrive in high-altitude habitats.
