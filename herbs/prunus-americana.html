Prunus americana

Prunus americana, commonly known as the American plum, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to North America. Renowned for its delicate white blossoms and nutritious fruits, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. Its various parts are utilized for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and soothing properties in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Prunus americana
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Prunus.
