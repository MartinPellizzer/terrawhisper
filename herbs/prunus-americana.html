American plum tree

Prunus americana · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (369 studies) · ★★★★★

American plum tree, scientifically known as Prunus americana, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to North America. Valued for its edible fruits and ornamental blossoms, it holds significant medicinal potential. Traditionally, its bark and fruit have been utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health and soothe various ailments.

Distribution

Moving toward the eastern part of the country, the species can also be found in Ontario. Its distribution continues further east into the province of Québec. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the American plum, utilizes its fruits as a primary component in various medicinal and nutritional applications. These small, stone fruits are harvested when ripe and are valued not only for their dietary fiber and vitamin content but also for their traditional use in soothing digestive ailments. While the flesh provides nourishment, the seeds contained within the pit are often handled with caution due to their chemical composition, emphasizing the importance of using the fruit in its appropriate, prepared forms.
