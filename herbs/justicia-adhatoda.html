Malabar nut

Justicia adhatoda · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (318 studies) · ★★★★★

Malaabar nut, scientifically known as Justicia adhatoda, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South Asia. Renowned for its potent bronchodilatory and expectorant properties, it is widely used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to treat respiratory ailments like asthma, bronchitis, and coughs, offering a natural approach to pulmonary health.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans multiple continents and climates. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Sicilia. Moving toward Africa, it can be found in Algeria as well as in the Northeast Tropical region of Ethiopia. The species also extends into the Western Indian Ocean, with populations located in Madagascar. Finally, its distribution reaches into Western Asia, where it is documented in Afghanistan.
