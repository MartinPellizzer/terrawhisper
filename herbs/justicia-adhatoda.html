Justicia adhatoda

Justicia adhatoda, commonly known as Adhatoda or Vasaka, is a powerful medicinal shrub native to India. Renowned for its potent bronchodilatory and expectorant properties, it is widely used in traditional Ayurveda to treat respiratory ailments like asthma, bronchitis, and coughs, thanks to its active alkaloid, vasicine.

  • Scientific name: Justicia adhatoda
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed by its placement in the genus Justicia.
