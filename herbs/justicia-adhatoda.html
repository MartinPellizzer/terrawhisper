Malabar nut

Justicia adhatoda · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (318 studies) · ★★★★★

Malaabar nut, scientifically known as Justicia adhatoda, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South Asia. Renowned for its potent bronchodilatory and expectorant properties, it is widely used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to treat respiratory ailments like asthma, bronchitis, and coughs, offering a natural approach to pulmonary health.
