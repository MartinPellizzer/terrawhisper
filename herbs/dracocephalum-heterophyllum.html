Dracocephalum heterophyllum

Dracocephalum heterophyllum, commonly known as Dragonhead, is a remarkable medicinal herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its unique leaf structure and aromatic properties, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic potential, including its antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory activities in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Dracocephalum heterophyllum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dracocephalum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats spanning across several key regions in Asia. In the northern reaches of its range, it can be found in the Chita region of Siberia. Its distribution continues through Central Asia, specifically within the territories of Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan. Moving toward Western Asia, the species is documented to grow in Afghanistan.
