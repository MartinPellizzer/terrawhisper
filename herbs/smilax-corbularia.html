Smilax corbularia

Smilax corbularia, commonly known as Sarsaparilla, is a perennial climbing vine prized for its medicinal properties. Renowned for its restorative qualities, this plant has historically been used to treat skin conditions, boost vitality, and support kidney health. Its roots contain valuable saponins, making it a staple in traditional herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Smilax corbularia
  • Family: Smilacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Smilax corbularia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Liliales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Smilacaceae and falls under the genus Smilax.
