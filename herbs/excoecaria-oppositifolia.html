Excoecaria oppositifolia

Excoecaria oppositifolia, commonly known as the Milky Euphorbiaceae, is a versatile medicinal shrub found across tropical regions. Renowned for its milky latex, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential in modern herbal medicine studies.

  • Scientific name: Excoecaria oppositifolia
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Excoecaria.

Plant Parts

This plant, Excoecaria oppositifolia, is characterized by several distinctive morphological features, most notably the presence of a milky white latex that flows from the stems and leaves when they are damaged or cut. This viscous substance is a key identification marker for the species and is often associated with the plant's chemical properties. In addition to its specialized latex, the plant possesses opposite leaves, woody stems, and small flowers, all of which contribute to its overall structure and medicinal potential.
