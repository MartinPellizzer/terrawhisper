Swertia purpurascens

Swertiā purpura-scens, commonly known as Swertia, is a significant medicinal herb belonging to the Gentianaceae family. Widely utilized in Ayurvedic medicine, this plant is celebrated for its potent bitter principles. It possesses remarkable therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory, hepatoprotective, and digestive benefits, making it vital for treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Swertia purpurascens
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
