Patrinia rupestris

Patrinia rupestris, commonly known as the rock patri, is a perennial herb native to mountainous regions. Characterized by its delicate white flowers and hardy nature, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional herbalism. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic properties and its role in supporting various biological functions.

  • Scientific name: Patrinia rupestris
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Dipsacales and placed in the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Patrinia.

Distribution

This plant, Patrinia rupestris, exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within various regions of Siberia. It can be found growing in the Altay mountain range, which represents one of its key habitats. Additionally, the species is documented across the Republic of Buryatiya, extending its presence through the eastern territories. Its range continues through the Chita region, showcasing its ability to thrive in these diverse environments. Finally, the plant is also distributed within the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions, completing its broad Siberian footprint.
