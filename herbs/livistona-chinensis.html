Livistona chinensis

Chinese fan palm

Livistona chinensis, commonly known as the Chinese fan palm, is a versatile medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, it is utilized in traditional practices to treat various ailments. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Livistona chinensis
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Arecales, it is classified under the family Arecaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Livistona.

Distribution

Moving toward the southern reaches of the continent, it is found in the KwaZulu-Natal region of Southern Africa. Its range also extends into the Western Indian Ocean, specifically inhabiting the islands of Mauritius and Réunion. Finally, the species is located in East Asia, specifically on the Chinese island of Hainan.
