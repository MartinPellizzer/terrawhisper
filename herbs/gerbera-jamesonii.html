Gerbera jamesonii

Barberton daisy

Gerbera jamesonii, commonly known as the Gerbera daisy, is a vibrant perennial celebrated for its striking, multi-colored blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens and floral arrangements, this plant holds significant potential in ethnobotany, with various species studied for their diverse pharmacological properties and traditional medicinal applications worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Gerbera jamesonii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Gerbera.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across multiple regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically within the borders of Benin. Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is located in Cameroon. The species also populates parts of Northeast Tropical Africa, including Eritrea and Ethiopia. Finally, its range reaches into Southern Africa, specifically within the nation of Eswatini.
