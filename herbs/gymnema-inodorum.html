Reproduction

The reproduction of Gymnema inoctorum is characterized by the development of inflorescences and the subsequent formation of distinct fruits. The inflorescences exhibit a varying length, ranging from a minimum of 0.015 m to a maximum of 0.04 m. Following pollination, the plant produces elongated fruits that show significant dimensional variability. The length of these fruits ranges from a minimum of 8.5 cm to a maximum of 11 cm, with an observed mean length of 9.75 cm.
