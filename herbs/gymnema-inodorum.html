Gymnema inodorum

Gymnema inoctorum, commonly known as gymnema or "sugar destroyer," is a medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains gymnemic acids that temporarily suppress sweetness on the tongue and may help regulate blood glucose levels, making it a significant subject in natural diabetes management research.

  • Scientific name: Gymnema inodorum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, falling into the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Gymnema.
