Nardostachys jatamansi

Nardostachys jatamansi, commonly known as Spikenard, is a precious perennial herb valued in traditional Ayurvedic and Tibetan medicine. Found in the high altitudes of the Himalayas, its aromatic rhizomes possess potent sedative, anti-inflammatory, and neuroprotective properties, making it a vital botanical resource for treating various neurological and emotional disorders.

  • Scientific name: Nardostachys jatamansi
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Dipsacales and the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Nardostachys.
