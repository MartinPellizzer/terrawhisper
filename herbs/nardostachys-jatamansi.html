Indian Nard

Nardostachys jatamansi · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (573 studies) · ★★★★★

Indian Nard, scientifically known as Nardostachys jatamansi, is a highly valued aromatic herb prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent essential oils, this plant serves as a natural sedative and nervine tonic. It is widely utilized to calm the nervous system, reduce anxiety, and promote restorative sleep.

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions across Asia. Within China, its distribution spans the South-Central and North-Central areas. It is also found in the high-altitude terrains of Qinghai and Tibet. Beyond China, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in Assam. Together, these locations define the natural range of this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Nardostachys jatamansi, is a highly valued medicinal herb where various components contribute to its therapeutic properties. The most significant part used in traditional medicine is the rhizomes, which are often referred to simply as the roots due to their underground structure and aromatic potency. These fragrant, woody rhizome segments are harvested and dried for use in various formulations. In addition to the underground structures, the plant features distinctive leaves that characterize its growth habit, and it eventually produces delicate flowers as part of its reproductive cycle.
