This plant, Oreomecon nudicaulis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Papaveraceae and the genus Oreomecon.
