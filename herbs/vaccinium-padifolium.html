Vaccinium padifolium

Madeira blueberry

Vaccinium padifolium, commonly known as the leaf-leaved blueberry, is a resilient shrub native to specific high-altitude regions. Renowned for its nutritious berries and medicinal potential, this plant offers antioxidant properties. Its unique foliage and ecological significance make it a fascinating subject for botanical study and traditional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Vaccinium padifolium
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is further categorized into the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Vaccinium under the specific species name Vaccinium padifolium.

Distribution

This plant is a specialized species found within a very restricted range. Its natural distribution is confined exclusively to the Macaronesian region. Specifically, it is endemic to the island of Madeira. Due to this localized presence, it plays a unique role in the island's specific ecosystem.
