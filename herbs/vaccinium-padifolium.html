Madeira blueberry, scientifically known as Vaccinium padifolium, is an endemic shrub found exclusively in the lush, subtropical forests of Madeira. Renowned for its vibrant berries, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its antioxidant properties, offering significant health benefits and serving as a unique botanical treasure of the Atlantic.

Specifically, it is endemic to the island of Madeira. Due to this localized presence, it plays a unique role in the island's specific ecosystem.
