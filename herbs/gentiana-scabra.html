Gentiana scabra

Gentiana scabra, commonly known as scabrous gentian, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Native to alpine regions, its bitter roots contain unique secoiridoids and xanthones. These bioactive compounds make it a potent digestive tonic, widely utilized to stimulate appetite, improve bile production, and support overall gastrointestinal health.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana scabra
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, it is a member of the Gentianaceae family. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gentiana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of Northern Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found specifically within the Chita region. Its presence extends into the Russian Far East, where it inhabits the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye areas. Additionally, the species is located within the borders of China, specifically in Inner Mongolia. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varied montane and continental environments.
