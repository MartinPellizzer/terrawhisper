Scabrous gentian, scientifically known as Gentiana scabra, is a perennial herb native to the alpine regions of Europe. Renowned for its bitter roots, it is a vital component in traditional herbal medicine, primarily used to stimulate digestion, support liver function, and alleviate various gastrointestinal discomforts through its potent alkaloids.
