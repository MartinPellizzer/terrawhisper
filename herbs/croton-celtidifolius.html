Croton celtidifolius

Croton celtidifolius, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Euphorbiaceae family, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Croton celtidifolius
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Croton, which includes the species Croton celtidifolius.
