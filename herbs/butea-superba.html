Butea superba

Butea superba, commonly known as Palash or Flame of the Forest, is a significant medicinal plant in Ayurveda. Renowned for its vibrant orange flowers, it possesses potent therapeutic properties. It is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including skin disorders, fever, and digestive issues, owing to its antioxidant qualities.

  • Scientific name: Butea superba
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling into the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Butea and the specific species superba.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions in South and Southeast Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in both India and Bangladesh. Its presence extends further into the Indo-China region, covering multiple countries. Specifically, it is documented to inhabit the landscapes of Cambodia and Laos.
