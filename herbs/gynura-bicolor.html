Gynura bicolor

Gynura bicolor, commonly known as the Purple Passion plant, is a striking perennial herb native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its vibrant, velvety purple leaves, it is highly valued in traditional medicine. Often used to treat inflammation and promote healing, this ornamental plant beautifully blends aesthetics with therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Gynura bicolor
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Gynura, specifically the species Gynura bicolor.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Gynura bicolor, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. In Japanese, it is referred to as スイゼンジナ, while in Chinese-speaking regions, it is widely called 紅鳳菜 (zh), 紅鳳菜 (zh-tw), or 红凤菜 (zh-cn). Additionally, in the Pwngul language, it carries the name ljaqacay a qudjidjilj (pwn).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions in East Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range extends further into Eastern Asia, specifically including the islands of Japan. Additionally, the species is documented in the Nansei-shotō archipelago. Together, these locations define the specific natural habitats where the plant thrives.
