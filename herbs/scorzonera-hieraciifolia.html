Scorzonera hieraciifolia

Scorzonera hieraciifolia, commonly known as Hawkweed, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism. Found across temperate regions, this plant contains bioactive compounds with potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. Understanding its phytochemical profile offers valuable insights into its therapeutic applications and its role in supporting holistic wellness.
