Eucalyptus melliodora

Eucalyptus melliodora, commonly known as Pink Honey Myrtle, is a highly valued medicinal and ecological species native to Australia. Renowned for its fragrant blossoms, it produces premium honey and essential oils. These extracts possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a vital resource in traditional and modern phytotherapy.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus melliodora
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtales, and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eucalyptus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the globe. In the Atlantic, it can be found within the Macaronesia region, specifically on the Canary Islands. Its presence extends significantly into East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits both Kenya and Uganda. Furthermore, the species is established within China, covering both the South-Central and Southeast territories. This wide range demonstrates the plant's ability to adapt to various international environments.
