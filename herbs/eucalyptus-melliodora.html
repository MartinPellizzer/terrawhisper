Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the globe. In the Atlantic, it can be found within the Macaronesia region, specifically on the Canary Islands. Its presence extends significantly into East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits both Kenya and Uganda. Furthermore, the species is established within China, covering both the South-Central and Southeast territories. This wide range demonstrates the plant's ability to adapt to various international environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, Eucalyptus melliodora, is highly valued for its medicinal and ecological properties, which are primarily concentrated within its foliage. The leaves contain high concentrations of essential oils, such as eucalyptol, which are frequently harvested for their antiseptic, decongestant, and anti-inflammatory benefits. When the foliage is crushed or distilled, these aromatic compounds are released, making it a vital component in various natural remedies for respiratory ailments.
