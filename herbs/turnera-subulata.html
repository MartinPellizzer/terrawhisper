Turnera subulata

Turnera subulata, commonly known as White Alder or White Turner, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Turnera subulata
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Passifloraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Turnera.
