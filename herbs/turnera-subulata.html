The ecology of Turnera subulata is characterized by its remarkable adaptability to diverse environmental conditions and a broad altitudinal distribution. It thrives across a wide elevational range, spanning from sea level (0 m AMSL) up to a maximum altitude of 1225 m AMSL. This species demonstrates high ecological plasticity, colonizing a variety of distinct biomes and habitats. It is frequently found in anthropogenic areas (Área Antrópica) and within diverse natural formations including the Caatinga (strictu sensu), open fields (Campo Limpo), and the Cerrado (latu sensu). Furthermore, its presence extends to more humid and coastal environments, such as Ombrophilous Forests (Floresta Ombrófila/Pluvial) and Restinga ecosystems, indicating its ability to survive in both semi-arid and high-moisture regimes.
