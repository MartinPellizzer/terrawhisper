Rubia chinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Rubia chinensis?

Rubia chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Gentianaales and into the family Rubiaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Rubia, and its specific species designation, chinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rubia chinensis has a perennial, creeping, or climbing herbaceous habit characterized by quadrangular stems and opposite, ovate to oblong leaves with entire margins. The plant features a robust, woody rootstock that often develops reddish pigments used in traditional medicine. Its leaves are typically arranged in whorls of four, though they can sometimes appear in pairs along the stem. Small, tubular, pink to white flowers emerge from terminal or axillary cymes during the blooming season. The fruit consists of small, fleshy drupes that turn dark red or black upon maturity.
