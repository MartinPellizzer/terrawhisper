Moricandia arvensis

Moriandica arvensis, commonly known as field moricandia, is a resilient herbaceous plant belonging to the Brassicaceae family. Found primarily in open, disturbed habitats, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional medicine utilizes its unique phytochemical properties to address various ailments, showcasing its potential importance in natural therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Moricandia arvensis
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe
