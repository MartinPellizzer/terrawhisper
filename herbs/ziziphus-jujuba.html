Ziziphus jujuba

Chinese jujube

Ziziphus jujuba, commonly known as the jujube or Chinese date, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated in traditional practices. Renowned for its calming properties, it is frequently used to alleviate insomnia, anxiety, and digestive issues. This nutrient-rich fruit offers potent antioxidant benefits, supporting overall neurological and cardiovascular health.

  • Scientific name: Ziziphus jujuba
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Rosales, it is further categorized into the family Rhamnaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ziziphus, which includes the species Ziziphus jujuba.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ziziphus jujuba, is a versatile medicinal species where various components offer unique therapeutic benefits. The large, fleshy fruits are widely consumed for their nutritional value, often used in traditional medicine to calm the nerves. Within each fruit lies a hard, stony seed that contains specific bioactive compounds. These seeds are frequently processed into powders or extracts to treat insomnia and anxiety. Additionally, the serrated green leaves are utilized in topical applications and decoctions to reduce inflammation, making the entire botanical profile highly significant in herbal pharmacology.
