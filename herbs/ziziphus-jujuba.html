Chinese jujube

Ziziphus jujuba · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (315 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese jujube, scientifically known as Ziziphus jujuba, is a versatile medicinal plant deeply rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its calming properties, it is frequently used to treat insomnia, anxiety, and digestive issues. This nutrient-dense fruit offers significant antioxidant benefits, supporting overall neurological health and emotional well-being.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ziziphus jujuba, is a versatile medicinal species where various components offer unique therapeutic benefits. The large, fleshy fruits are widely consumed for their nutritional value, often used in traditional medicine to calm the nerves. Within each fruit lies a hard, stony seed that contains specific bioactive compounds. These seeds are frequently processed into powders or extracts to treat insomnia and anxiety. Additionally, the serrated green leaves are utilized in topical applications and decoctions to reduce inflammation, making the entire botanical profile highly significant in herbal pharmacology.
