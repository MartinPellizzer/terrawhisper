What's the taxonomical classification of Raphistemma pulchellum?

Raphistemma pulchellum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Gentianales, the plant is a member of the family Apocynaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Raphistemma, with the unique species designation pulchellum.
