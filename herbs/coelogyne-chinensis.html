Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Coelogyne chinensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the Orchidaceae family, falling under the genus Coelogyne.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across various regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Its range also extends into the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Additionally, the species is located within the East Himalaya region of the Indian Subcontinent.
