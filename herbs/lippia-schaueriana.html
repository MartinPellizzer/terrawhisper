Lippia schaueriana

Lippia schaueriana, commonly known as Erva-cidreira in Brazil, is a versatile medicinal shrub belonging to the Verbenaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal teas to alleviate digestive issues, reduce anxiety, and combat mild inflammation, offering a natural remedy rooted in folk medicine.

  • Scientific name: Lippia schaueriana
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Lippia.
