Actinidia macrosperma

Actinidia macrospermma, commonly known as the large-seeded kiwi, is a lesser-known medicinal plant belonging to the Vitaceae family. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this climbing vine offers significant therapeutic potential. Research suggests its bioactive compounds may support immune function and combat oxidative stress, marking it as a valuable botanical resource.
