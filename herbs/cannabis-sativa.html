Cannabis sativa

Cannabis sativa, commonly known as hemp or marijuana, is a versatile plant with a rich history in traditional medicine. Renowned for its complex profile of cannabinoids, particularly THC and CBD, it is utilized globally to manage pain, anxiety, and various therapeutic conditions, bridging ancient wisdom with modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Cannabis sativa
  • Family: Cannabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Rosales and the family Cannabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cannabis, specifically the species Cannabis sativa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific presence across several regions within Northern Europe. In the southern reaches of this territory, it can be found growing in Denmark. Moving further north, the species is also distributed throughout Finland. The plant extends its range to the islands of Great Britain and Ireland as well. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the northernmost stretches of Norway.
