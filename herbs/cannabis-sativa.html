This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Plant Parts

This plant, Cannabis sativa, possesses several distinct morphological features that contribute to its medicinal properties. The most recognizable parts are the serrated green leaves, which are often used in various preparations. The plant produces dense inflorescences, or flowering clusters, which serve as the primary source of many bioactive compounds. These compounds are concentrated within microscopic, resin-producing glandular trichomes that coat the surface of the flowers and foliage. Additionally, the robust stem provides structural support for the plant's rapid growth, while the nutrient-rich seeds offer a different profile of uses, often valued for their nutritional and oil content.
