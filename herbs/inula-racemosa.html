Himalayan elecampane, scientifically known as Inula racemosa, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, its potent rhizomes possess remarkable therapeutic properties, primarily used to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant benefits for holistic wellness.
