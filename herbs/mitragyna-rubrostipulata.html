Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Burundi, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its range extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Ethiopia. Additionally, the species is documented across East Tropical Africa in Kenya. Together, these locations highlight a broad presence spanning from central to eastern African territories.
