Often found in disturbed soils, this resilient plant serves as a symbolic emblem of remembrance and botanical beauty worldwide.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the corn poppy, is characterized by its striking and delicate botanical structures. The most visually prominent features are its vibrant, papery petals, which typically exhibit a deep scarlet hue. These petals are an integral part of the large, solitary flowers that bloom atop slender, hairy stems. In herbal and botanical contexts, the medicinal utility often involves the use of the aerial parts, which encompass everything above the ground, including the stems, leaves, and the blossoms themselves.
