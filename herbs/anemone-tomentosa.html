Anemone tomentosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Anemone tomentosa?

Anemone tomentosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is further categorized under the order Ranunculales. It is a member of the family Ranunculaceae, falling into the genus Anemone, and is specifically identified by its species name, tomentosa.
