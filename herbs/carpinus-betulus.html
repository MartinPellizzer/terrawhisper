European hornbeam, scientifically known as Carpinus betulus, is a deciduous tree native to European forests. While primarily valued for its incredibly dense, durable timber, it holds traditional medicinal significance. Its bark and leaves have been historically utilized in folk remedies to treat various ailments, including digestive and inflammatory conditions.

This plant exhibits a wide range of presence across various parts of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing within the territories of Denmark and Great Britain.
