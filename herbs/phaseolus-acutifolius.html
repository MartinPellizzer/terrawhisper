Tepary bean, scientifically known as Phaseolus acutifolius, is a resilient legume native to the Sonoran Desert. Highly adapted to extreme heat and drought, this nutritious plant offers significant dietary benefits. Traditionally valued in indigenous cultures, it serves as a vital, sustainable food source in arid and warming climates.
