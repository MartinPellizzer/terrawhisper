Luffa acutangula

Chinese okra

Luffa acutangula, commonly known as ridge gourd, is a versatile medicinal plant widely used in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its nutritional value and therapeutic properties, it plays a crucial role in managing digestive health, regulating blood sugar, and reducing inflammation, making it a vital component of holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Luffa acutangula
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa
