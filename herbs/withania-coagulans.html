Withania coagulans

Withania coagulans, commonly known as the "coagulating withania," is a significant medicinal herb within the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its unique coagulant properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various healing practices. Its bioactive compounds offer promising therapeutic potential, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Withania coagulans
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Withania, specifically as the species Withania coagulans.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. In Western Asia, its presence is noted within the territories of Afghanistan and Iran. Furthermore, its range extends northward into the neighboring country of Nepal.
