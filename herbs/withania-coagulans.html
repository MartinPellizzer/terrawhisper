Indian rennet

Withania coagulans · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (151 studies) · ★★★★☆

Indian rennet, scientifically known as Withania coagulans, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its unique coagulating properties, it is traditionally used in Ayurvedic medicine to treat various ailments. This potent herb offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in managing inflammation and supporting overall wellness.
