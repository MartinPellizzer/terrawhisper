This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Vigna, specifically as the species Vigna aconitifolia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia and Africa. In Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Eritrea. Its range extends into the Arabian Peninsula, where it can be found in Yemen. Within the Asian continent, it is distributed throughout China, particularly in the South-Central regions. Finally, the species is also established within the Indian Subcontinent, occurring in both Assam and Bangladesh.
