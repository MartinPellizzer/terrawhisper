Harpagophytum procumbens subsp. procumbens

Harpagophytum procumbens subsp. procumbens, commonly known as Devil's Claw, is a medicinal plant native to southern Africa. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory properties, its tuberous roots are widely utilized in herbal medicine to alleviate joint pain, osteoarthritis, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to managing chronic inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Harpagophytum procumbens subsp. procumbens
  • Family: Pedaliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Pedaliaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Harpagophytum, with the subspecies being procumbens.
