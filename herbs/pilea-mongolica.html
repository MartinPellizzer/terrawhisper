Pilea mongolica

Pilea mongolica, commonly known as the Mongolian Pilea, is a versatile medicinal herb native to East Asia. Widely utilized in traditional medicine, it is valued for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential in treating various ailments, making it a subject of botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Pilea mongolica
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Rosalales and placed in the family Urticaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Pilea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Northeast Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be specifically located within the Buryatiya region. Its range extends into the Russian Far East, encompassing Khabarovsk, Primorye, and the Kuril Islands. Moving further south, the species is also well-established within the China South-Central region. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse habitats across these territories.
