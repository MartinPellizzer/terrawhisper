Thymbra nabateorum

Thymbra nabateorum, commonly known as Nabatean thyme, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to the arid landscapes of the Middle East. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this medicinal plant is prized for its essential oils, offering potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties used in traditional healing and natural remedies.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
