What's the taxonomical classification of Sinocrassula yunnanensis?

Sinocrassula yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic descent, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized under the order Saxifragales and the family Crassulaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Sinocrassula, with its specific designation being the species yunnanensis.
