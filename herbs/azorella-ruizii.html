Azorella ruizii

Azorella ruizii, commonly known as the "palo santo" of the high Andes, is a resilient woody shrub native to the páramo ecosystem. Valued in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties, it is often utilized to treat respiratory ailments and inflammatory conditions, offering vital therapeutic benefits in high-altitude regions.
