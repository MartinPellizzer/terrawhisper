Thalictrum ramosum

What's the taxonomical classification of Thalictrum ramosum?

Thalictrum ramosum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ranunculales. The plant is a member of the family Ranunculaceae, specifically placed within the genus Thalictrum, and is identified by its unique species designation, ramosum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Thalictrum ramosum has branched stems that reach heights of 20 to 60 centimeters and bear pinnately compound leaves with three to five leaflets that are broadly ovate or rhombic. The plant produces small, yellowish-green flowers arranged in terminal panicles that emerge from the upper axils of the stems. Each flower typically features five petals and five sepals that are similar in appearance, surrounding several stamens. The base of the plant develops a woody rhizome which helps it establish itself in moist, shaded woodland habitats. These morphological traits distinguish the species from other members of the Ranunculaceae family.
