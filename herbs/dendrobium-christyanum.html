Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is documented within the South-Central provinces as well as on the island of Hainan. The species also extends into the Indo-China peninsula, where it can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of Laos. Furthermore, its habitat includes the lush environments of Thailand. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal orchid is completed by its occurrence throughout Vietnam.
