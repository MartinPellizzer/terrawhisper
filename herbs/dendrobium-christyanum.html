Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is documented within the South-Central provinces as well as on the island of Hainan. The species also extends into the Indo-China peninsula, where it can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of Laos. Furthermore, its habitat includes the lush environments of Thailand. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal orchid is completed by its occurrence throughout Vietnam.

Morphology

The morphology of Dendrobium christyanum is characterized by its growth habit as an obligatory epiphyte, meaning it relies on host structures for physical support rather than growing in soil. While it functions as a climber, it is specifically classified as a self-supporting climber, utilizing its specialized aerial roots to anchor itself firmly to the bark of trees. Furthermore, the plant is an independent organism in terms of its nutritional requirements, acting as a non-parasitic species that does not derive nutrients or water from its host.
