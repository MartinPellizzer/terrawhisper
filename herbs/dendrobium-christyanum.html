Dendrobium christyanum

Dendrobium christyanum, commonly known as the Christyan's orchid, is a stunning epiphytic plant prized for its delicate, fragrant blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its stems are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties and potential healing benefits in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium christyanum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asparagales, it is a member of the Orchidaceae family. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Dendrobium as the species Dendrobium christyanum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is documented within the South-Central provinces as well as on the island of Hainan. The species also extends into the Indo-China peninsula, where it can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of Laos. Furthermore, its habitat includes the lush environments of Thailand. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal orchid is completed by its occurrence throughout Vietnam.
