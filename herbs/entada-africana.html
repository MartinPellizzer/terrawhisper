Entada africana, scientifically known as Entada africana, is a robust woody climber native to African tropical forests. Renowned for its massive, woody seed pods, this multipurpose plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from skin infections to digestive and inflammatory conditions.
