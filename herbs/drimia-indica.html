Indian-squill, scientifically known as Drimia indica, is a perennial bulbous herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource, offering significant therapeutic potential in ethnopharmacological studies worldwide.
