Plant Parts

This plant, Drimia indica, is a geophyte characterized by its unique underground storage structures. The most significant medicinal components are found within its bulbs, specifically the fleshy, specialized bulb that allows the plant to survive dormant periods. These bulbs are connected to a system of roots that anchor the plant and facilitate nutrient uptake from the soil. Above the ground, the plant produces distinct leaves that are essential for photosynthesis, providing the energy necessary to develop the nutrient-rich bulb and the expansive root system.
