Rumex dentatus subsp. dentatus

Rumex dentatus subsp. dentatus, commonly known as dentate dock, is a perennial herb distinguished by its jagged, tooth-like leaf margins. Found in various habitats, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, though careful identification is essential before utilizing its medicinal components.

  • Scientific name: Rumex dentatus subsp. dentatus
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. It is specifically identified as a member of the genus Rumex under the subspecies designation dentatus subsp. dentatus.
