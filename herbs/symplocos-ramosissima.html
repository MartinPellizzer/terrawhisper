What's the taxonomical classification of Symplocos ramosissima?

Symplocos ramosissima belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Symplocaceae, falling under the genus Symplocos, and is specifically identified by its species name, ramosissima.
