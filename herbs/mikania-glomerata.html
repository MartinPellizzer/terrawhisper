Guaco

Mikania glomerata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (50 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Guaco, scientifically known as Mikania glomerata, is a climbing medicinal vine native to the tropical regions of Central and South America. Widely utilized in traditional folk medicine, it is most renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and anti-parasitic properties, particularly in treating skin conditions and various respiratory ailments.
