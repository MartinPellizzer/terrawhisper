Tabernaemontana amygdalifolia

Tabernaemontana amygdalifolia, commonly known as the white periwinkle or almond-leaved crape jasmine, is a versatile medicinal shrub found in tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in various ethnobotanical practices to treat inflammation, wounds, and digestive ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Tabernaemontana amygdalifolia
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae and the genus Tabernaemontana. Specifically, its scientific identity is recognized as Tabernaemontana amygdalifolia.
