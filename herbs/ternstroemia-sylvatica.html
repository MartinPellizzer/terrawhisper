Ternstroemia sylvatica

Ternstroemia sylvatica, commonly known as the forest ternstroemia, is a versatile evergreen shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to various tropical regions, its leaves and bark possess significant bioactive properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential pharmacological benefits, particularly for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Ternstroemia sylvatica
  • Family: Pentaphylacaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is placed in the family Pentaphylacaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Ternstroemia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several key regions across North and Central America. Moving further south, the species reaches into Central America. Specifically, its range includes the nation of El Salvador.
