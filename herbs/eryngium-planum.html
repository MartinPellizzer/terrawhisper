Eryngium planum

Eryngium caeruleum

Eryngium planum, commonly known as slender eryngo, is a striking perennial herb native to Europe. Characterized by its spiky, blue-tinged bracts and delicate white flowers, it thrives in meadows. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant has traditionally been used to support kidney function and alleviate various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Eryngium planum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Eryngium.
