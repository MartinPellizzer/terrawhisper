Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Orchidaceae. Ultimately, it is identified as a member of the genus Dendrobium.

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is documented in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the Assam region. Additionally, the species extends its reach into the East Himalaya.
