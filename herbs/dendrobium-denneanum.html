Dendrobium denneanum

Dendrobium denneanum, commonly known as a species of orchid, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, it is utilized in traditional practices to support holistic wellness. Understanding its biological significance offers valuable insights into the diverse world of herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium denneanum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Orchidaceae. Ultimately, it is identified as a member of the genus Dendrobium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is documented in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the Assam region. Additionally, the species extends its reach into the East Himalaya. Together, these locations highlight a broad geographical distribution across subtropical and montane environments.
