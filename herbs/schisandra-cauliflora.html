Schisandra cauliflora

Schisandra cauliflora, commonly known as Schisandra or Magnolia vine, is a versatile medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its adaptogenic properties, it is traditionally used to enhance vitality, reduce stress, and support cognitive function. This climbing shrub offers powerful antioxidant benefits, promoting overall wellness and longevity.
