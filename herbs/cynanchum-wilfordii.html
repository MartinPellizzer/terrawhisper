Cynanchum wilfordii

Cynanchum wilfordii, commonly known as wilfordii or Qing Feng Teng, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is widely used to support blood circulation, relieve muscle spasms, and provide anti-inflammatory benefits, making it a vital botanical therapeutic agent.

  • Scientific name: Cynanchum wilfordii
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, falling into the family Apocynaceae. Ultimately, its scientific identity is defined by the genus Cynanchum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye territory. Moving into China, the species can be found extensively throughout the Manchuria region. It also occupies significant portions of China South-Central and China North-Central. Furthermore, the plant's range extends into the diverse landscapes of China Southeast.
