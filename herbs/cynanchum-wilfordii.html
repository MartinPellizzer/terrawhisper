, scientifically known as Cynanchum wilfordii, is a highly valued medicinal herb in traditional East Asian medicine. Commonly referred to as Wilford's swallowwort, it is rich in bioactive compounds like coumarins and alkaloids. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye territory. Moving into China, the species can be found extensively throughout the Manchuria region. It also occupies significant portions of China South-Central and China North-Central. Furthermore, the plant's range extends into the diverse landscapes of China Southeast.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Cynanchum wilfordii, utilizes various structures that are of significant interest in traditional medicine. The most pharmacologically important parts are the roots, specifically the fleshy and nutrient-rich tuberous root, which is widely harvested for its medicinal properties. While the stem provides the necessary structural support for the climbing vine, the primary therapeutic value is concentrated within the underground root system.
