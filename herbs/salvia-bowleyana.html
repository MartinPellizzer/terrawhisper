Salvia bowleyana

Salvia bowleyaana, commonly known as Bowlley's sage, is a rare and enchanting perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Characterized by its striking, tubular flowers and aromatic foliage, this botanical treasure is valued in traditional practices for its unique phytochemical properties and its significant role in supporting local biodiversity.
