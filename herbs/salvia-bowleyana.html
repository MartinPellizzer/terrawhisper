What's the taxonomical classification of Salvia bowleyana?

Salvia bowleyaana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Lamiales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Lamiaceae, placing it within the genus Salvia, and it is specifically identified by the species name bowlheyana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Salviva bowleyana has a rhizomatous perennial habit featuring basal rosettes of deeply lobed, rugose, and hairy leaves that typically measure several centimeters in length. The foliage displays a textured surface with prominent veining and can vary in color from bright green to darker shades. From these basal clusters, upright flowering stems emerge to support terminal verticillasters of tubular, bilabiate flowers. These blossoms are characteristically blue to violet and are surrounded by protective calyces. The overall architecture of the plant combines a compact ground-level presence with elongated vertical spikes during its blooming period.
