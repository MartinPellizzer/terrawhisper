Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Eastern Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence is also noted within the tropical landscape of Hainan and the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving further east, the species extends its range into the islands of Japan.
