Blechnum orientale

Blechnum orientale, commonly known as the Oriental hard fern, is a resilient, shade-loving perennial native to East Asia. Renowned for its elegant, leathery fronds and architectural form, this fern thrives in moist, woodland environments. It serves as a lush, decorative addition to temperate gardens and diverse botanical collections.

  • Scientific name: Blechnum orientale
  • Family: Aspleniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Polypodiinae and order Polypodiales, it is classified under the family Aspleniaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Blechnum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Eastern Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence is also noted within the tropical landscape of Hainan and the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving further east, the species extends its range into the islands of Japan. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environmental niches inhabited by this medicinal fern.
