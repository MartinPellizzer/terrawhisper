Viburnum taitoense

Viburnum taitoense, commonly known as the Taitoe shrub, is a resilient medicinal plant native to East Asian landscapes. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support holistic wellness. This shrub offers valuable insights into botanical healing through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Viburnum taitoense
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is categorized within the order Dipsacales and the family Viburnaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Viburnum.

Common Names

This plant, Viburnum taitoense, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic context and specific regional usage. In Chinese, it is referred to as 台东荚蒾 (Tāidōng jiāmí), 对叶油麻根 (Duìyè yóumágēn), and 对叶油麻叶 (Duìyè yóumáyè), while in Taiwan, the traditional Chinese nomenclature includes 臺東莢蒾 (Táidōng jiāxī) and 臺灣莢蒾 (Táiwān jiāxī).
