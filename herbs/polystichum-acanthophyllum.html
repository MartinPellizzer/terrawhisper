What's the taxonomical classification of Polystichum acanthophyllum?

Polystichum acanthophyllum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, its evolutionary lineage continues through the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. It is further categorized under the family Polypodiaceae, falling within the genus Polystichum, and is specifically identified by its species name, acanthophyllum.
