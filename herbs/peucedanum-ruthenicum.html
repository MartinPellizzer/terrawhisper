Peucedanum ruthenicum

Peucedanum ruthenicum, commonly known as Ruthenic umbellifer, is a perennial herb native to the arid landscapes of Central Asia. Belonging to the Apiaceae family, this medicinal plant is valued for its phytochemical properties. It holds significant traditional importance in regional folk medicine for treating various ailments and supporting wellness.
