Yellow rattle, scientifically known as Rhinanthus minor, is a distinctive herbaceous plant famed for its bell-shaped, yellow flowers. Often called the "meadow maker," it plays a vital ecological role by parasitizing grass roots, helping to reduce dominance and promote diverse wildflower habitats within nutrient-rich, semi-natural grasslands and meadows.
