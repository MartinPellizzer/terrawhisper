Cussonia bancoensis

Cussonia bancoensis, commonly known as the Cabbage Tree, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to subtropical regions. Renowned for its robust structure and broad leaves, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the plant are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Cussonia bancoensis
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Araliaceae and falls under the genus Cussonia.

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions across West Tropical Africa. Its geographical distribution includes the country of Ghana, where it grows in local ecosystems.
