Artemisia copa

Artemisia copa, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, belongs to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions. Its unique phytochemical profile makes it a subject of significant pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia copa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Specifically, Artemisia copa is categorized under the genus Artemisia.
