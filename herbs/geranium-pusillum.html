Geranium pusillum

Small-flowered crane's-bill

Geranium pusillum, commonly known as small-flowered crane's-bill, is a delicate perennial herb found in diverse habitats. Renowned for its subtle pink blossoms and finely divided foliage, this plant holds significant medicinal potential. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies for its soothing properties and various therapeutic applications in wellness.

  • Scientific name: Geranium pusillum
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Geraniales, it is further categorized into the family Geraniaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Geranium, specifically the species Geranium pusillum.
