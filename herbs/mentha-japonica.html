Mentha japonica, scientifically known as Mentha japonica, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its refreshing aroma and cooling properties, this plant is widely used in traditional medicine and herbal teas. Its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic benefits, ranging from digestive support to soothing skin irritations.

Distribution

This plant is primarily characterized by a very specific and localized geographical range. It is native to the regions of Eastern Asia, where it thrives in certain environmental conditions. Within this vast continent, its natural habitat is specifically centered in Japan. Because of this limited distribution, it is considered an endemic species to that particular archipelago. Consequently, finding this medicinal plant in the wild typically requires traveling to the islands of Japan.
