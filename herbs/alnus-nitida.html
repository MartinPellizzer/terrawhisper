Alnus nitida

Alnus nitida, commonly known as the Alder nitida or Chilean Alder, is a versatile evergreen tree prized for its rapid growth and adaptability. Native to South America, it serves as an excellent soil stabilizer and nitrogen fixer, making it a vital component in reforestation efforts and riparian ecosystem restoration.

  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
