Pretty Dragon's Tongue, scientifically known as Phyllodium pulchellum, is a distinctive medicinal herb cherished in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its unique, heart-shaped leaves, this plant offers potent therapeutic properties. It is widely utilized to treat various ailments, including skin disorders, inflammation, and digestive issues, showcasing nature's healing power.

Plant Parts

This plant, known for its significant medicinal properties, is primarily utilized through its aerial parts. The most distinctive feature of Phyllodium pulchellum is its large, broadly ovate leaf, which is characterized by its unique, fan-shaped structure. Because the entire above-ground portion of the plant is often harvested for therapeutic use, the leaf serves as the most important component of the aerial part used in traditional medicine.
