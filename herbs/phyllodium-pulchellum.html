Phyllodium pulchellum

Phyllodium pulchellum, commonly known as the "beautiful leaf," is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its heart-shaped leaves, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering valuable phytochemical properties for diverse therapeutic applications in herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Phyllodium pulchellum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Fabales, specifically within the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phyllodium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution pattern across several specific regions in East Asia. In mainland China, it is primarily located within the South-Central and Southeast territories. Finally, the geographical footprint of this medicinal species encompasses the island of Taiwan.
