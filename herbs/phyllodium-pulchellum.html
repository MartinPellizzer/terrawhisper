Pretty Dragon's Tongue, scientifically known as Phyllodium pulchellum, is a distinctive medicinal herb cherished in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its unique, heart-shaped leaves, this plant offers potent therapeutic properties. It is widely utilized to treat various ailments, including skin disorders, inflammation, and digestive issues, showcasing nature's healing power.
