Chinese Ragwort, scientifically known as Sinacalia tangutica, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of China. Valued in traditional medicine, it is primarily utilized for its bioactive compounds to treat inflammation and various ailments. This plant serves as a significant subject in ethnobotanical and pharmacological research.
