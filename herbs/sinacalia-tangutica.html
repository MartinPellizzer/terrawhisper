Sinacalia tangutica

Sinacalia tangutica, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional practices, is a resilient plant native to high-altitude regions. Valued for its unique phytochemical profile, it is utilized in various herbal remedies to treat inflammatory conditions and promote healing, offering a fascinating subject for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Sinacalia tangutica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Sinacalia. Therefore, its complete taxonomic designation is Sinacalia tangutica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in Great Britain and Ireland. Its range extends further north to include the countries of Norway and Sweden. Additionally, the species is present within the territories of Middle Europe.
