What's the taxonomical classification of Sinacalia tangutica?

Sinacalia tangutica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized into the order Asterales. It is a member of the family Asteraceae, falling under the genus Sinacalia, and is specifically identified by its species name, tangutica.
