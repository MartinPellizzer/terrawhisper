Trifolium aureum

Trifolium aureum, commonly known as Golden Clover, is a resilient medicinal herb celebrated for its vibrant yellow blooms. Traditionally used in various holistic practices, this plant is valued for its antioxidant properties and potential anti-inflammatory benefits. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a fascinating subject for natural healing studies.

  • Scientific name: Trifolium aureum
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Europe

Distribution

This plant, known as Trifolium aureum, exhibits a specific distribution across Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Denmark. Furthermore, its presence extends northward into the territories of Norway and Sweden. The species is also documented to inhabit the landscapes of Finland. Finally, its geographical range includes the islands of Great Britain.
