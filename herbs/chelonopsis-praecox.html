Chelonopsis praecox

Chelonopsis praecox, commonly known as the Early Shell-Flower, is a rare medicinal herb revered in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this botanical treasure is frequently utilized to bolster immune function and soothe systemic inflammation, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and long-term vitality.

  • Scientific name: Chelonopsis praecox
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, eventually reaching the family Lamiaceae. Within this hierarchical structure, it is specifically identified by the genus Chelonopsis.
