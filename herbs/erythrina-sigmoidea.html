Erythrina sigmoidea

Erythrina sigmoidea, commonly known as the coral tree, is a medicinal shrub native to various African regions. Renowned for its vibrant red flowers, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Various parts of the plant are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Erythrina sigmoidea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement includes the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, placing it in the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Erythrina as the species Erythrina sigmoidea.

Distribution

This plant is native to several countries located within the region of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its natural habitat includes the nation of Benin. It is also found growing across the territories of Burkina Faso and Guinea-Bissau. Furthermore, the distribution extends into the country of Guinea. Finally, the range of this species reaches as far as Ivory Coast.
