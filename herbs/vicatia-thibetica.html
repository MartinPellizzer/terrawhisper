Vicatia thibetica

Vicatia thibetica, commonly known as Tibetan Vicatia, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. This plant serves as a vital natural resource, offering therapeutic potential for managing inflammation and promoting holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Vicatia thibetica
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Vicatia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across several distinct regions of Asia. In China, its distribution is quite broad, spanning from the South-Central provinces to the high altitudes of Qinghai. It is also notably found within the vast territory of Tibet. Moving beyond Chinese borders, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it can be located within the mountainous terrain of Nepal.
