Strobilanthes cusia

Strobilanthes cusia whole

Strobilanthes cusia, commonly known as the Malabar Melastome, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant blue flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various folk medicines to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues and respiratory concerns.

  • Scientific name: Strobilanthes cusia
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Strobilanthes.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Strobilanthes cusia, is recognized by various common names across different languages and regions. In Chinese, it is referred to as 板蓝 (bǎnlán), while in Taiwan, the specific name 马蓝 (mǎlán) is used. In Thailand, it is known as ห้อม (hǒm), and in Japan, it is called リュウキュウアイ (ryūkyūai). Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast. Its range also extends to the tropical island of Hainan and the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Beyond mainland China, the species is also documented in the country of Japan. Together, these locations highlight the plant's broad geographical presence throughout the eastern part of the continent.
