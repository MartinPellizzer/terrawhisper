Euphorbia ebracteolata

Euphorbia ebracteolata, commonly known as a member of the diverse Euphorbiaceae family, is a medicinal plant valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Often found in specific tropical habitats, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Researchers study its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in treating various inflammatory and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia ebracteolata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
