Wild medlar, scientifically known as Vangueria infausta, is a versatile fruit-bearing tree native to sub-Saharan Africa. Renowned for its nutritional value, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections and inflammation.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Cameroon, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The species also extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically within the territories of Sudan and South Sudan. Furthermore, its range reaches East Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Kenya. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive across diverse tropical environments.
