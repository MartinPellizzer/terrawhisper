Ambrosia psilostachya

Perennial ragweed

Ambrosia psilostachya, commonly known as the Spiked Ragweed, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique flowering spikes, this species possesses bioactive compounds used to address various ailments. Understanding its botanical properties and therapeutic potential offers vital insights into natural pharmacology and ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Ambrosia psilostachya
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ambrosia psilostachya, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Ambrosia.
