Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across several key regions of the African continent. Moving toward the horn of Africa, it is distributed throughout Northeast Tropical Africa. This distribution includes significant populations found in Eritrea, Ethiopia, and Somalia. Plant Parts

This plant, Crinum macowanii, utilizes various components for its biological functions and potential medicinal applications. Extending from these structures are the roots, which anchor the plant firmly into the soil and facilitate water absorption. The foliage consists of broad, strap-like leaves that are essential for photosynthesis.
