Horse-chestnuts, scientifically known as Aesculus, is a genus of deciduous trees celebrated for both their ornamental beauty and therapeutic potential. Beyond their striking blooms, certain species offer potent medicinal properties, particularly in managing venous insufficiency and swelling, making them a significant subject in traditional and modern phytotherapy studies.
