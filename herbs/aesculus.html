Aesculus

Aesculus, commonly known as horse chestnut, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like esculin and saponins, it is primarily used to support vascular health. Its potent anti-inflammatory and anti-edema effects make it a vital remedy for managing venous insufficiency and swelling.

  • Scientific name: Aesculus
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae, it is classified under the genus Aesculus.
