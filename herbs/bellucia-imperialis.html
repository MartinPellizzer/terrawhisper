Bellucia imperialis

Bellucia imperialis, commonly known as the Imperial Bell, is a spectacular tropical tree native to the rainforests of South America. Renowned for its massive, flamboyant crimson flowers, this botanical wonder is highly valued in horticulture. Beyond its aesthetic charm, it serves as a significant element in diverse tropical ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Bellucia imperialis
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Melastomataceae and is specifically identified by the genus Bellucia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of the Americas. In Western South America, its presence is documented within the borders of Bolivia. Additionally, the species can be found across a significant portion of the Brazilian landscape. Specifically, its habitat extends into the northern territories of Brazil. These locations highlight the plant's reliance on the unique environmental conditions found in these specific zones.
