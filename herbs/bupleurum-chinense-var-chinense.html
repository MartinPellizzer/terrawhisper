Plant Parts

This plant, Bupleurum chinense var. chinense, is valued in traditional medicine primarily for its underground structures. The most therapeutically significant portion is the root, specifically the thick, fleshy taproot that develops deep within the soil. In herbal preparations, the dried roots are harvested and processed to be used as a primary medicinal ingredient. While the aerial part of the plant, including its stems and leaves, is part of its biological structure, it is the roots that contain the concentrated active compounds required for its healing properties.
