scientifically known as Bupleurum chinense var. chinense, is a vital medicinal herb deeply rooted in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Commonly called Chai Hu, this plant is celebrated for its ability to soothe the liver, resolve stagnation, and relieve emotional tension, making it an essential remedy for regulating bodily harmony.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Bupleurum and the variety chinense.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several distinct regions of China. It can be found growing throughout the areas of China South-Central and China Southeast. Furthermore, its range extends into the northern territories of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. The species is also widely distributed within the China North-Central region. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse environmental settings where the plant thrives.
