Kalanchoe fedtschenkoi

Kalanchoe fedtschenkoi, commonly known as the "Jade Plant" variety or a succulent medicinal herb, is a resilient species valued for its unique physiological properties. Often utilized in traditional practices, this plant is recognized for its antioxidant potential and its ability to support holistic wellness through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Kalanchoe fedtschenkoi
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Kalanchoe fedtschenkoi, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Saxifragales. Finally, it is a member of the family Crassulaceae and falls under the genus Kalanchoe.
