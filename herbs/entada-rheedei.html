Entada rheedei

African dream herb

Entada rheedei, commonly known as the Sea Bean or River Bean, is a vigorous woody climber native to tropical Asia. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this plant offers diverse therapeutic benefits, particularly for treating skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, making it a significant botanical resource in herbal pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Kaunch seed or Mucuna pruriens, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for its potent therapeutic properties. While the entire plant contains various bioactive compounds, the most significant medicinal component is found within the seed kernels. These kernels are rich in the amino acid L-Dopa, which plays a crucial role in neurological health and is frequently utilized to manage symptoms of Parkinson's disease. Because of this high concentration of active alkaloids, the processing and extraction of the seed kernels are central to the plant's application in herbal pharmacology.
