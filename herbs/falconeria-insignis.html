Falconeria insignis

Falconeria insignis, commonly known as the Silver-Leaf Fern, is a rare medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, it is frequently used to soothe inflammation and support respiratory health. This resilient species thrives in shaded tropical corridors, offering unique bioactive compounds for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Falconeria insignis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Falconeria insignis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It is further organized under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling into the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Falconeria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In China, its presence is noted within the South-Central provinces as well as on the island of Hainan. The species also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be found in the state of Assam. Furthermore, it inhabits parts of Bangladesh, broadening its range within the subcontinent. Finally, its distribution reaches the rugged terrains of the East Himalaya.
