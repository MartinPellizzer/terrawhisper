Kageneckia oblonga

Kageneckia oblonga, commonly known as the "Pau-ferro," is a resilient medicinal tree native to South American landscapes. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, its bark and leaves are utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering bioactive compounds that support healing processes and provide significant ethnobotanical value.

  • Scientific name: Kageneckia oblonga
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling within the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Kageneckia.
