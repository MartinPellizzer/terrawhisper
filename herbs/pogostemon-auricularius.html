Pogostemon auricularius

Pogostemon auricularius, commonly known as the "Ear-leaf Pogostemon," is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique foliage, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and skin inflammations, offering significant ethnobotanical value for natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Pogostemon auricularius
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Pogostemon.
