Asian fly-honeysuckle, scientifically known as Lonicera morrowii, is a versatile medicinal shrub widely utilized in traditional East Asian practices. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is frequently employed to alleviate inflammation, reduce fever, and clear heat from the body, making it a cornerstone in various herbal therapeutic formulations.
