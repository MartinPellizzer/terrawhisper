Lonicera morrowii

Lonicera morrowii, commonly known as Morrow's honeysuckle, is a versatile deciduous shrub valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its fragrant blooms, its leaves and stems possess significant bioactive properties. Often utilized for their anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities, this plant offers promising therapeutic potential in various holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Lonicera morrowii
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lonicera.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of the Eurasian continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is noted specifically within Germany. Moving eastward, it can be found throughout Central European Russia. The species also inhabits Manchuria within the territory of China. Finally, its range extends into Eastern Asia, specifically covering the regions of Japan and Korea.
