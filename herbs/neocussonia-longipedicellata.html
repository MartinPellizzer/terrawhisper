Neocussonia longipedicellata

Neocussonia longipedicellaata, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in traditional herbal practices, holds great potential in ethnobotany. Characterized by its unique morphological features, this species is valued for its diverse bioactive compounds. Research continues to explore its therapeutic properties, particularly its role in treating various ailments naturally.

  • Scientific name: Neocussonia longipedicellata
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Apiales. Finally, it is classified under the family Araliaceae and the genus Neocussonia.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Neocussonia longipedicellata, possesses a very specific and limited geographical range. It is found exclusively within the Western Indian Ocean region. Specifically, its natural habitat is located on the island of Madagascar. Because it is endemic to this single area, it plays a unique role in the local ecosystem. Understanding this precise distribution is essential for the conservation of the species.
