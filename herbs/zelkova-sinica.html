Zelkova sinica

What's the taxonomical classification of Zelkova sinica?

Zelkova sinica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the order Rosales and is a member of the family Ulmaceae. Specifically, it is situated within the genus Zelkova, with its unique biological identity defined by the species name sinica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Zelkova sinica has a deciduous habit characterized by an upright, spreading crown with bark that becomes grayish and finely fissured with age. The leaves are simple, alternate, and typically possess an ovate to elliptic shape with serrated margins and pointed tips. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in catkins, which emerge from the previous year's wood. The fruit consists of small, winged samaras that facilitate wind dispersal during the growing season. This species maintains a robust woody structure that distinguishes it from other members of the Ulmaceae family.
