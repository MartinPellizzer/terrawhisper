Zanthoxylum dissitum

Zanthoxylum dissitum, commonly known as the prickly ash, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to North America. Renowned for its pungent, citrus-like aroma, it contains bioactive alkaloids and essential oils. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat digestive issues and skin ailments, it remains a subject of pharmacological interest.
