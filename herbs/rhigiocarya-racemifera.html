Rhigiocarya racemifera

Rhigiocarya racemifera, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds immense pharmacological potential. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments. Current scientific interest focuses on its antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, highlighting its importance in ethnobotanical research.

  • Family: Menispermaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is further categorized into the family Menispermaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Rhigiocarya and the species Rhigiocarya racemifera.
