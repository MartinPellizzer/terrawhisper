Barleria strigosa

Barleria striosa, commonly known as the "Barleria" or "wild Barleria," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and various infections, showcasing its significant value in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Barleria strigosa
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Barleria striosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Barleria.
