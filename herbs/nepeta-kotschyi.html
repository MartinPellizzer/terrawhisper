Nepeta kotschyi

Nepeta kotschyi, commonly known as Kotchyi catmint, is a resilient perennial herb native to the arid regions of Central Asia. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate flowers, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its calming properties and potential antioxidant benefits, offering unique botanical therapeutic value.
