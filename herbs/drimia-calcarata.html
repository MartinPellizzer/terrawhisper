Drimia calcarata

Drimia calcarata, commonly known as the bladder plant, is a unique medicinal herb native to Southern Africa. Valued in traditional healing practices, this succulent species is recognized for its distinctive physiological properties. It serves as a fascinating subject for ethnobotanical research, offering potential insights into natural therapeutic applications.

  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant, Drimia calcarata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asparagaceae and falls under the genus Drimia.
