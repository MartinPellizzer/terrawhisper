Bridelia grandis

Bridelia grandis, commonly known as the "wild brideia," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and roots are utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Bridelia grandis
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It further falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Phyllanthaceae and is identified by the genus Bridelia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of Guinea and Liberia. Its presence is also well-documented within the borders of Ivory Coast. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the territories of Benin and Nigeria. Consequently, these five nations represent the key geographical areas for its natural habitat.
