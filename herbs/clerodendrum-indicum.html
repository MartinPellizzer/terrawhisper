Turk's-turban, scientifically known as Clerodendrum indicum, is a versatile medicinal shrub widely used in traditional Ayurvedic and Southeast Asian medicine. Characterized by its distinctive flower shape, this plant offers potent therapeutic properties, primarily utilized for treating skin infections, inflammation, and respiratory ailments through its diverse bioactive compounds.
