Clerodendrum indicum

Clerodendrum indicum, commonly known as Hill Glory Bower, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Asia. Widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic and folk medicine, this plant possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and analgesic properties. Its leaves and roots are frequently employed to treat various ailments, including skin infections and fever.

  • Scientific name: Clerodendrum indicum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Clerodendrum.
