Reproduction

The reproduction of Shirakiopsis elliptica is characterized by a specific seasonal cycle, with the flowering period primarily occurring in February. Following pollination, the plant enters its fruiting stage, which typically spans from May through the same month. The fruit produced is classified as a drupe, which is indehiscent in nature; these fruits are relatively small, possessing a mean length of 0.85 cm and a mean width of 0.7 cm. Dispersal is facilitated through a zoochorous syndrome, specifically via endozoochory, meaning the seeds are dispersed through the digestive tracts of animals.
