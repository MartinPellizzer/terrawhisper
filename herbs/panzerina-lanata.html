Panzerina lanata

Panzerina lanaata, commonly known as the tropical medicinal plant, is a versatile species valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to specific forested regions, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Understanding its bioactive compounds offers promising potential for modern pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Panzerina lanata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Panzerina.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Panzerina lanaata, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Chinese, it is known as 绒毛脓疮草 (zh), which is also used in mainland China as 绒毛脓疮草 (zh-cn), while the traditional nomenclature in Taiwan is 絨毛膿瘡草 (zh-tw). In Japanese, the name is transliterated as パンツェーリア・イアナータ (ja).

Distribution

This plant, Panzerina lanaata, exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the vast regions of Siberia. Its presence is notably recorded across the Altai mountain range, representing a key part of its habitat. Furthermore, the species can be found within the administrative boundaries of Buryatiya. The distribution continues through the eastern territories of Chita and the surrounding landscapes. Finally, its range extends into the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions, marking a significant longitudinal spread across the Siberian landscape.
