Pilocarpus jaborandi

Pilarcus jaboranadi, commonly known as Jaboranandi, is a significant medicinal shrub native to Brazil. Renowned for its alkaloid content, specifically pilocarpine, it has been traditionally used to treat various ailments. Today, it remains a crucial botanical source for developing pharmacological treatments, particularly for managing glaucoma and certain neurological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Pilocarpus jaborandi
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pilocarpus.
