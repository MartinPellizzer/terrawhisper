tamarind

Tamarindus indica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (923 studies) · ★★★★★

Tamarind, scientifically known as Tamarindus indica, is a versatile tropical tree celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and minerals, its tangy fruit and leaves are used traditionally to aid digestion, manage blood pressure, and reduce inflammation, making it a vital staple in holistic healing.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in both Egypt and Libya. Its presence extends into the Macaronesia region, specifically within Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species is established in West Tropical Africa. The life history of Tamarindus indica is characterized by its status as a perennial species, allowing it to persist and thrive across many decades, sometimes even centuries.
