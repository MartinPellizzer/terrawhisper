Swertia macrosperma

Swertiia macrosperma, commonly known as Swertia, is a significant medicinal herb belonging to the Gentianaceae family. Widely distributed across the Himalayas, this plant is highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. It is primarily recognized for its potent bitter principles, which possess powerful anti-inflammatory, hepatoprotective, and antipyretic therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Swertia macrosperma
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
