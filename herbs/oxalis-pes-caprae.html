Oxalis pes-caprae

Oxalis pes-caprae, commonly known as Bermuda buttercup, is a resilient perennial herb widely distributed in tropical and subtropical regions. Beyond its vibrant yellow blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various cultures utilize its leaves and roots in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Oxalis pes-caprae
  • Family: Oxalidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Oxalidales. Finally, it is a member of the family Oxalidaceae within the genus Oxalis.
