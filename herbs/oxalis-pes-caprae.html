Bermuda Buttercup, scientifically known as Oxalis pes-caprae, is a perennial herb widely recognized for its bright yellow blooms. While often viewed as a weed, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various cultures have utilized its leaves and tubers in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Bermuda buttercup, is a perennial herb characterized by its distinct morphology. While it does not possess woody twigs like larger shrubs, its growth is driven by underground bulbs that support a lush spread of vegetation. The fresh leaves are heart-shaped and trifoliate, often displaying a vibrant green color that makes them easily identifiable in grassy areas. Periodically, the plant produces delicate, bright yellow flowers that bloom on slender stalks, adding a splash of color to its natural habitat.
