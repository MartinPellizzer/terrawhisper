Plant Parts

This plant, Platanus orientalis, is a large deciduous tree where the leaves serve as a primary and highly recognizable feature. The leaves are large, broad, and palmately lobed, often resembling the shape of a maple leaf with several distinct points radiating from a central base. In terms of medicinal interest, these leaves are sometimes studied for their chemical constituents, though the tree is more commonly valued for its shade and majestic stature in urban landscapes.
