Platanus orientalis

Platanus orientis, commonly known as the Oriental Plane, is a majestic deciduous tree valued for its impressive stature and unique exfoliating bark. While primarily ornamental, its leaves and bark contain tannins and flavonoids, which have historically been utilized in traditional medicine for their anti-inflammatory and astringent properties.

  • Scientific name: Platanus orientalis
  • Family: Platanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Proteales and the family Platanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Platanus.
