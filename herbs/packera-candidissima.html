Packera candidissima

Packera candidissima, commonly known as white ragwort, is a resilient perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Distinguished by its delicate white flower clusters and silvery-green foliage, this plant plays a vital role in local ecosystems, providing nectar for pollinators and contributing to the biodiversity of montane habitats.

  • Scientific name: Packera candidissima
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Packera.
