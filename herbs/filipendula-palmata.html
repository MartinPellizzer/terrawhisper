Filipendula palmata

What's the taxonomical classification of Filipendula palmata?

Filipendula palmata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Rosales. As a member of the family Rosaceae, it is further categorized under the genus Filipendula, with its specific designation being the species palmata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Filipendula palmata has palmately lobed leaves that are typically three to five centimeters wide and consist of several distinct, pointed segments. The foliage is characterized by a deeply divided structure that gives the plant its namesake appearance. These leaves are arranged alternately along the stems and often feature serrated margins. During the flowering stage, the plant produces numerous small, white flowers clustered in dense, terminal panicles. The stems are sturdy and can reach significant heights to support the weight of the blooms.
