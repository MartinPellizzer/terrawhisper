Cyperus capitatus

Cyperus capitatus, commonly known as the spotted sedge, is a versatile medicinal plant found in wetland habitats. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects. This aromatic perennial is highly valued for its ability to support digestive health and soothe skin irritations.

  • Scientific name: Cyperus capitatus
  • Family: Cyperaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Poales and is a member of the family Cyperaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cyperus as Cyperus capitaptus.
