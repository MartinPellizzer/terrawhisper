Isodon

Isodon, commonly known as the "Blackwood" or "Isodon," is a rare medicinal plant prized for its unique therapeutic properties. Traditionally used in various folk medicine practices, its bark and wood are studied for potential healing benefits, offering a fascinating glimpse into the profound connection between botanical diversity and wellness.
