This plant, commonly known as European mistletoe, possesses distinct morphological features across its various components. The leaves are small, leathery, and evergreen, typically appearing in opposite pairs along the branches. The stems are woody and parasitic, allowing the plant to draw nutrients and water from its host tree. Finally, the plant produces small, white, berry-like fruits that are highly characteristic of the species and play a crucial role in its seed dispersal by birds.
