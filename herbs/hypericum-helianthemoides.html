Hypericum helianthemoides

Hypericum helianthemoides, commonly known as the sun rose, is a resilient perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe. Belonging to the Hypericaceae family, this plant is distinguished by its vibrant yellow blossoms. While less famous than its relatives, it holds significant interest in botanical and ecological studies.
