Cissampelos glaberrima

Cissampelos glaberrima, commonly known as a versatile medicinal climber, is highly valued in traditional herbal medicine across tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various infections, offering significant pharmacological potential for developing modern therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Cissampelos glaberrima
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ranunculales and the family Menispermaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cissampelos.
