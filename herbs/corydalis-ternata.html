Corydalis ternata

Corydalis ternata, commonly known as the Ternate Corydalis, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant contains bioactive alkaloids valued for their sedative and analgesic properties. Often found in moist, shaded woodlands, it serves as a fascinating subject for pharmacological and botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis ternata
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Corydalis.
