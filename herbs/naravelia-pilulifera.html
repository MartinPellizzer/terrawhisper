Naravelia pilulifera

What's the taxonomical classification of Naravelia pilulifera?

Naravelia pilulifera belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales. This plant is a representative of the family Ranunculaceae, specifically categorized under the genus Naravelia, with the specific epithet pilulifera.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Naravelia pilulifera has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by a creeping rhizome and stems covered in dense, fine hairs. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the axils of the leaves, often appearing in clusters. The plant is distinguished by its pilose indumentum, which gives the foliage a soft, fuzzy texture to the touch. Its reproductive structures lead to the development of small nutlets that facilitate seed dispersal.
