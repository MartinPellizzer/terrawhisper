Lepisorus ussuriensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Lepisorus ussuriensis?

Lepisorus ussuriensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. This fern is a member of the family Polypodiaceae, falling under the genus Lepisorus, and is specifically identified by its species name, ussuriensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lepisorus ussuriensis has small, triangular to ovate fronds that are typically pinnatifid or once-pinnate with narrow, lanceolate segments. The rhizomes are creeping and covered in brownish scales that protect the growing tips. Each pinna features finely serrated margins and a distinctively thin texture. The sori are located on the underside of the segments, often positioned near the midrib and protected by small indusia. These morphological features allow the plant to thrive in moist, shaded forest environments.

How is this plant cultivated?
