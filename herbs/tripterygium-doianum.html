Tripterygium doianum, scientifically known as Tripterygium doianum, is a climbing woody vine native to China, highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, it contains unique alkaloids used to treat rheumatoid arthritis. Research continues to explore its significant potential in modern pharmacological applications.
