Elwendia persica

Black-caraway

Elwendia persica, commonly known as Persian Elwendia, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Found in unique ecological niches, this botanical treasure offers significant bioactive compounds. It is widely studied for its potential in treating various ailments, making it a vital subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Elwendia persica
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
