Dryopteris sparsa

Dryopteris sparsa, commonly known as the sparse wood fern, is a resilient pteridophyte native to various subtropical and temperate regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, making it a valuable subject in ethnobotanical studies and natural pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Dryopteris sparsa
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, it is further categorized into the family Polypodiaceae.
