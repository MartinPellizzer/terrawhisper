Jatropha spinosa

Jatrophia spinosa, commonly known as Spiny Spurge, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for pharmacological research and modern herbal medicine applications.
