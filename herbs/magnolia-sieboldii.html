Oyama magnolia, scientifically known as Magnolia sieboldii, is a striking deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its large, creamy-white blossoms and glossy foliage, this resilient species thrives in moist, acidic soils. In traditional medicine, various parts are utilized for their therapeutic properties, offering significant botanical interest.
