Magnolia sieboldii

Magnolia sieboldii, commonly known as Siebold's magnolia, is a graceful deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its fragrant, creamy-white blossoms, this medicinal plant has been utilized in traditional practices to soothe the nervous system, alleviate inflammation, and promote overall wellness through its potent, natural bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia sieboldii
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Magnoliales and the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Magnolia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of East Asia. In China, its distribution spans from the South-Central areas to the North-Central provinces.
