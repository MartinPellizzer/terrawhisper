The reproductive cycle of Lepidium perfoliatum is characterized by a flowering period that typically begins in April and concludes in July, though the timing can be variable. The plant utilizes biotic pollination, specifically relying on insects to facilitate the transfer of pollen. Reproduction is primarily sexual, as asexual reproduction is absent, and the species is capable of self-fertilization. Following successful pollination, the plant develops fruit in the form of a capsule. These capsules are dehiscent, meaning they split open to release the seeds.
