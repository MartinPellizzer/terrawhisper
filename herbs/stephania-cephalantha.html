What's the taxonomical classification of Stephania cephalantha?

Stephania cephalantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the order Ranunculales and the family Menispermaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Stephania, and its specific species designation, cephalantha.
