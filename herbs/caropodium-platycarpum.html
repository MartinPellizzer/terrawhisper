Caropodium platycarpum

Caropodium platycarpum, commonly known as Broad-leaved CaroPodium, is a distinctive herbaceous plant native to specific temperate regions. Valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses unique phytochemical properties. This guide explores its botanical characteristics, ecological significance, and potential medicinal applications, providing a foundational overview for researchers and herbal enthusiasts alike.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Apiales and sits in the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Caropodium, specifically as the species Caropodium platycarpum.
