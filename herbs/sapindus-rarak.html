Sapindus rarak

Sapindu rarak, commonly known as the soapberry tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive disorders. Its unique properties offer significant therapeutic potential in both traditional healing and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Sapindus rarak
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Sapindaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Sapindus.
