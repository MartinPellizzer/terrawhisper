Brassica

Brassica, commonly known as cruciferous vegetables, includes nutritional powerhouses like broccoli, kale, and cabbage. Renowned for their potent bioactive compounds, these plants offer significant medicinal benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and anticancer properties. Incorporating Brassica into a balanced diet supports cellular health and promotes long-term disease prevention and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Brassica
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales and family Brassicaceae, it is classified under the genus Brassica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the northern regions of Europe. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Furthermore, its presence extends to the northern reaches of Finland. The species is also documented within the archipelago of Føroyar. Finally, its range includes the island territories of Great Britain and Ireland.
