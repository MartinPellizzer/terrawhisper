Cabbage, scientifically known as Brassica, is a nutritional powerhouse belonging to the Brassicaceae family. Renowned for its high concentrations of vitamins C and K, this versatile plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Its medicinal value lies in supporting digestive health and providing essential compounds for cellular protection.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as mustard or cabbage depending on the specific variety, offers significant nutritional and medicinal value through its various components. While the mature leaves and seeds are frequently utilized for their potent bioactive compounds, the young sprouts are particularly prized for their high concentration of glucosinolates and antioxidants. Consuming these tender shoots provides a concentrated source of vitamins and minerals, making them a functional dietary addition for supporting overall health.
