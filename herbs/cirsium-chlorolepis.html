Cirsium chlorolepis

What's the taxonomical classification of Cirsium chlorolepis?

Cirsium chlorolepis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its classification through the order Asterales, the plant is a member of the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is finalized within the genus Cirsium, resulting in its scientific name, the species chlorolepis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cirsium chlorolepis has erect, unbranched stems that typically reach heights of 30 to 100 centimeters and possess leaves with distinctively green, non-whitened undersides. The foliage consists of deeply lobed, lanceolate leaves that are often spiny along the margins. Unlike many other thistle species, the abaxial leaf surfaces lack the characteristic white tufts of hairs. The inflorescence features solitary, discoid flower heads arranged in terminal clusters. Each flower head is surrounded by involucral bracts that are often tinged with purple or green.
