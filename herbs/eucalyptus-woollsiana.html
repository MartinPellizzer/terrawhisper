Eucalyptus woollsiana

Eucalyptus woollsiana, commonly known as the Woollsia eucalyptus, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to Australia. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it possesses strong antimicrobial and decongestant properties. Often used in traditional remedies to alleviate respiratory issues, this species highlights the profound therapeutic potential of Australian flora.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus woollsiana
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae and the genus Eucalyptus.

Distribution

This plant is native to the Australian continent, where it thrives in specific regional environments. Its geographical range is primarily concentrated within the eastern parts of the country. Specifically, it can be found growing throughout the state of New South Wales. Additionally, the species extends its distribution further north into Queensland. These two states represent the core areas where this medicinal eucalyptus is naturally situated.
