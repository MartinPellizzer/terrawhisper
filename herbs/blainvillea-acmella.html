Blainvillea acmella

Blainvillea acmella, commonly known as the "vetchia" or "blainvillea," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This plant serves as a vital resource in various ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Blainvillea acmella
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by its genus name, Blainvillea, and its species designation, acmella.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct African regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is recorded within the borders of Egypt. Moving toward West Tropical Africa, it can be found in both Benin and Burkina. Its range extends further into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the Central African Republic. Additionally, the species is distributed across the islands of the Gulf of Guinea.
