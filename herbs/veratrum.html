False-hellebore, scientifically known as Veratrum, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Melanthiaceae family. Renowned for its high alkaloid content, it has been utilized historically in traditional medicine. However, due to its extreme toxicity, it must be handled with immense caution and strict professional supervision.
