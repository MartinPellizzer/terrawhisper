Veratrum

Vera-trum, commonly known as False Hellebore, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Melanthiaceae family. Renowned for its high concentration of toxic alkaloids, it has historically been utilized in controlled doses for treating various ailments. However, extreme caution is required due to its significant potential for systemic toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Veratrum
  • Family: Melanthiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Liliales and falls into the family Melanthiaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by its specific genus, Veratrum.
