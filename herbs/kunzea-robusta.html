Kunzea robusta

Rawirinui

Kunzea robusta, commonly known as the Kanuka, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to New Zealand. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate white flowers, it offers significant medicinal value. Its essential oils possess potent antibacterial and antifungal properties, making it a staple in traditional and contemporary natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Kunzea robusta
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Kunzea robusta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Kunzea.

Distribution

This plant is widely recognized for its presence across the diverse landscapes of New Zealand. Its geographical range spans both the North Island and the South Island of the country. In the North Island, it can be found inhabiting various native ecosystems. Similarly, the species is well-established throughout the regions of the South Island. This distribution ensures that the plant is a common sight across much of the New Zealand archipelago.
