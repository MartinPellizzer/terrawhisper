Agastache urticiifolia, scientifically known as Agastache urticiifolia, is a resilient perennial herb native to North America. Often called Giant Hyssop, this aromatic plant is prized for its medicinal properties, particularly in supporting digestive health and soothing respiratory issues. Its fragrant foliage and nectar-rich blooms attract pollinators beautifully.
