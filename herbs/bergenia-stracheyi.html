Bergenia stracheyi, scientifically known as Bergenia stracheyi, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of East Africa. Renowned for its thick, leathery leaves, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues, inflammation, and digestive discomfort through its bioactive compounds.
