Bergenia stracheyi

Bergenia stracheyi, commonly known as the African Bergenia, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of East Africa. Renowned for its thick, leathery leaves and medicinal properties, it is traditionally used in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Bergenia stracheyi
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Saxifrages and is a member of the family Saxifragaceae. Specifically, Bergenia stracheyi is identified by its genus, Bergenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In Central Asia, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Tajikistan. Moving into Western Asia, the species can be found growing in the rugged terrains of Afghanistan. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the plant is distributed across both Nepal and Pakistan. Finally, its range extends to the high altitudes of Tibet in China.
