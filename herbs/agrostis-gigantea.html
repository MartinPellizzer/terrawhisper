Agrostis gigantea

Black bent

Agrostis gigantea, commonly known as Giant Bent, is a perennial grass often utilized in ecological restoration and medicinal landscapes. While primarily valued for its stabilizing root systems, its unique phytochemical properties offer intriguing potential in traditional herbalism. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for exploring its diverse therapeutic applications.

  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe
