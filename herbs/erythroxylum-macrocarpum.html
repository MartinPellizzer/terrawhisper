Erythroxylum macrocarpum

Erythroxylum macrocarpum, commonly known as the large-fruited coca tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to South American tropical forests. Renowned for its alkaloid-rich leaves, it has historically been utilized in traditional healing practices to alleviate fatigue, digestive issues, and altitude sickness, offering unique pharmacological potential in modern ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Erythroxylum macrocarpum
  • Family: Erythroxylaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement puts it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Erythroxylaceae and falls under the genus Erythroxylum.
