Euphorbia parviflora

Euphorbia parviflora, commonly known as the small-flowered spurge, is a versatile medicinal herb found across various tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties, often utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing capabilities. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for exploring its therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia parviflora
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae, falling under the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range extending across several distinct regions. In Western Asia, its presence is documented within the territories of Lebanon and Syria. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found widely throughout India and Bangladesh. Its distribution also reaches the island nation of Sri Lanka.
