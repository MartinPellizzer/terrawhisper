Strong-Spined Medick, scientifically known as Medicago truncatula, is a resilient, prostrate herb native to Africa. Characterized by its distinctive prickly stems and small yellow flowers, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine. It is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating various ailments across various cultures.
