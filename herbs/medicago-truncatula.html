Medicago truncatula · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (11 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Strong-Spined Medick, scientifically known as Medicago truncatula, is a resilient, prostrate herb native to Africa. Characterized by its distinctive prickly stems and small yellow flowers, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine. It is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating various ailments across various cultures.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found in both Austria and Germany. The distribution extends further south to Southwestern Europe, specifically encompassing the Baleares. Finally, its range in the southwestern region also includes the island of Corse.
