Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key mountainous regions in Asia. In China, it can be found inhabiting both the South-Central and North-Central territories. Its presence is also well-documented within the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is native to the East Himalaya region.
