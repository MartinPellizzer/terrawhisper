Cola digitata

What's the taxonomical classification of Cola digitata?

Cola digitata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae, eventually leading to its placement within the genus Cola. Ultimately, the specific identification of this plant is defined by its species name, digitata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cola digitata has a single, unbranched trunk that reaches heights of up to 15 meters and is characterized by a ringed surface formed by old leaf scars. The leaves are large, pinnately compound, and can extend up to 1 meter in length with numerous leaflets arranged along a central rachis. From the crown of the tree, these foliage clusters emerge to create a dense canopy. The plant produces small, yellowish-white flowers that eventually develop into fleshy, globose fruits. These fruits are typically reddish or yellowish and contain several seeds encased in a pulpy aril.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is native to the tropical regions of West and Central Africa, specifically spanning from Senegal and Gambia in the west to the Democratic Republic of the Congo in the east. It thrives primarily within the moist lowland forests and gallery forests of the Guineo-Congolian region. Its distribution is closely linked to areas with high humidity and significant annual rainfall. Cultivation is widespread in countries such as Nigeria, Ivory Coast, and Ghana due to its economic importance.
