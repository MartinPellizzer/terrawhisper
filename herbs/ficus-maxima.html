Ficus maxima

Amäk wäm

Ficus maxima, commonly known as the Giant Fig, is a majestic tropical tree celebrated for its impressive size and medicinal versatility. Renowned in traditional healing, its various parts—including leaves, bark, and sap—offer potent bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues across diverse cultures.

  • Scientific name: Ficus maxima
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Moraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Ficus.
