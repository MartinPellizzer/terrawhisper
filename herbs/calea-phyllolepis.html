Calea phyllolepis

Calea phyllolepis, commonly known as "bitter herb" in some regions, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, this species is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to support digestive health and manage inflammatory conditions, offering significant potential for pharmacological research.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America
