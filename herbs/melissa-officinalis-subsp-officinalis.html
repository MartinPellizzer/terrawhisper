Melissa officinalis subsp. officinalis

Melissa officinalis subsp. officinalis, commonly known as lemon balm, is a perennial herb cherished for its refreshing citrus scent. Belonging to the mint family, this versatile plant has been used throughout history in traditional medicine to alleviate anxiety, improve sleep quality, and support digestive health through its calming properties.

  • Scientific name: Melissa officinalis subsp. officinalis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, this specific subspecies is categorized under the genus Melissa.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as lemon balm, derives its primary medicinal properties and distinctive lemon-like aroma from its fragrant leaves. These leaves are characterized by their bright green color, ovate shape, and serrated edges, often featuring a slightly wrinkled texture. When crushed or steeped, the essential oils contained within the leaf tissue release therapeutic compounds that are widely utilized in herbal remedies to promote relaxation and digestive health.
