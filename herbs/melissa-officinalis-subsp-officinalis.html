Bee herb, scientifically known as Melissa officinalis subsp. officinalis, is a fragrant perennial herb belonging to the mint family. Renowned for its calming properties, it is widely used in traditional medicine to alleviate anxiety and digestive issues. Its bright leaves and citrus scent make it a staple in herbal teas.
