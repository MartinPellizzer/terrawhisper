Juncus

Juncus, commonly known as rushes, comprises diverse aquatic and wetland species valued for their medicinal and ecological importance. Often utilized in traditional remedies to address inflammation and digestive issues, these resilient plants also serve vital roles in water purification and environmental restoration, bridging the gap between nature and healing.

  • Scientific name: Juncus
  • Family: Juncaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Poales. Finally, it is a member of the family Juncaceae and falls under the genus Juncus.
