Cola lizae

Cola lizae, commonly known as the Kola nut, is a vital medicinal plant native to West Africa. Renowned for its high caffeine content, it serves as a powerful stimulant. Beyond energy enhancement, it is traditionally used to alleviate fatigue, improve digestion, and treat various ailments through its potent bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Cola lizae
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cola lizae, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae and the genus Cola.

Distribution

This plant is characterized by a very specific and limited geographical range. It is primarily found within the West-Central Tropical Africa region. More specifically, its documented presence is concentrated in the nation of Gabon. Because its habitat is so localized, it is considered an endemic species to this particular area. Consequently, its natural distribution is tied closely to the unique tropical ecosystems of this part of Africa.
