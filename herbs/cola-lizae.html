Cola nitida, scientifically known as Cola lizae, is a vital medicinal plant native to West Africa. Renowned for its stimulating properties, the plant's nuts contain high levels of caffeine and theobromine. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to enhance energy, improve digestion, and alleviate fatigue naturally.
