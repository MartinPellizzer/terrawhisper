Castanopsis argyrophylla

What's the taxonomical classification of Castanopsis argyrophylla?

Castanopsis argyrophylla belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows a lineage that leads to the order Fagales. It is further categorized under the family Fagaceae, which houses many prominent tree species. Within this family, it is placed in the genus Castanopsis, and its specific taxonomic designation is completed by the species name argyrophylla.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Castanopsis argyrophylla has large, leathery, elliptic to oblong leaves that measure up to 30 centimeters in length and feature a distinct silvery underside due to a dense covering of fine hairs. The upper leaf surface is dark green and glossy, providing a sharp contrast to the pale, pubescent ventral side. Its branches are sturdy and woody, supporting a dense canopy of evergreen foliage. The plant produces small, inconspicuous yellowish flowers that eventually develop into woody, globose fruits. These morphological traits help the species adapt to its specific tropical forest habitat.
