Coca Trujillo

Coca Trujillo, scientifically known as Erythroxylum novogranatense var. truxillense, is a specialized variety of the coca plant native to specific Andean regions. Renowned for its unique alkaloid profile, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance, traditionally utilized by indigenous communities for its stimulating properties and profound medicinal applications in local healing.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Erythroxylaceae and the genus Erythroxylum. Specifically, it is identified as the variety truxillense of the species Erythroxylum novogranatense.

Distribution

This plant is found exclusively within the western regions of South America. Its specific geographical range is concentrated across three distinct countries. Specifically, it occurs naturally throughout the territory of Colombia. The distribution also extends into the nation of Ecuador. Finally, its presence is documented within the borders of Peru.
