Glaucium corniculatum

Glaucium corniculatum, commonly known as the Horned Glaucia, is a rare, succulent perennial native to the Mediterranean. Characterized by its striking, fleshy leaves and distinctive geometric structure, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique phytochemical properties, though it remains primarily celebrated for its botanical rarity.

  • Scientific name: Glaucium corniculatum
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, specifically within the family Papaveraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Glaucium, which includes the species Glaucium corniculatum.
