Manilkara kauki

Manilkara kauki, commonly known as the Bulletwood tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its dense, durable timber, its various parts are also utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments like skin diseases, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering valuable bioactive compounds for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Manilkara kauki
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, falling into the family Sapotaeceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Manilkara.

Common Names

This plant, Manilkara kauki, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In Indonesia, it is referred to as sawo kecik (id), and this name is also used in Javanese (jv). In Japan, the plant is called サワノキ (ja), while in Bangladesh, it is known as Sabo kecik (ban). Additionally, in Malaysia, it is commonly called sawo kecil (ms).
