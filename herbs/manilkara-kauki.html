Wongai Plum, scientifically known as Manilkara kauki, is a versatile Australian rainforest tree valued for its traditional medicinal properties. Indigenous cultures utilize its bark and leaves to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and respiratory issues. This resilient species remains a vital component of ethnobotanical studies and natural wellness.
