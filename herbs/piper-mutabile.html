Piper mutabile

What's the taxonomical classification of Piper mutabile?

Piper mutabile belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Piperales. This plant is further categorized into the family Piperaceae and the genus Piper, ultimately reaching its specific identification at the species level as Piper mutabile.
