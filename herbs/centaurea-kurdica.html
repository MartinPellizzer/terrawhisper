Centaurea kurdica

Centaurea kurdica, commonly known as a medicinal knapweed, is a significant botanical species native to the mountainous regions of the Middle East. Valued in traditional folk medicine, this plant possesses unique phytochemical properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in modern pharmacological treatments.
