Thymus pubescens

Thymus pubescens, commonly known as pubescent thyme, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid, rocky landscapes. Characterized by its fine, velvety hairs, this plant produces aromatic foliage rich in essential oils. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, it offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, supporting respiratory and digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Thymus pubescens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, eventually reaching the genus Thymus.
