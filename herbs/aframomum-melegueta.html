Aframomum melegueta

Aframomum melegueta, commonly known as cardamom pod or grains of paradise, is a highly aromatic medicinal plant native to West Africa. Renowned for its stimulating properties, it is used traditionally to aid digestion, treat respiratory ailments, and combat inflammation, offering a potent blend of culinary spice and therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Aframomum melegueta
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is classified under the family Zingiberaceae and is identified by the genus Aframomum. Specifically, its scientific name is Aframomum melegueta.
