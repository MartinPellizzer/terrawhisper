Japanese Knotweed

Reynoutria japonica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (480 studies) · ★★★★★

Japanese Knotweed, scientifically known as Reynoutria japonica, is a vigorous perennial plant renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Rich in resveratrol and polyphenols, it offers significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. While often viewed as an invasive species, its bioactive compounds hold immense potential for modern pharmacological research and wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Reynoutria japonica, is a vigorous perennial that utilizes specialized underground structures for both storage and propagation. The most significant medicinal components are found in the rhizomes, which consist of thick, fleshy, and creeping rhizomes that grow horizontally beneath the soil surface. These rhizome sections are rich in bioactive compounds like resveratrol, while the extensive network of roots helps anchor the plant firmly in its environment.
