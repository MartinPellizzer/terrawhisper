Reynoutria japonica

Japanese knotweed

Reynoutria japonica, commonly known as Japanese knotweed, is a robust, perennial plant celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its high concentration of resveratrol, it offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient species is widely studied for its potential benefits in supporting metabolic health and cardiovascular wellness.

  • Scientific name: Reynoutria japonica
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Caryophyllales, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Reynoutria.
