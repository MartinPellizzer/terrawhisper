Euphrasia officinalis subsp. pratensis

Euphrasia officinalis subsp. pratensis, commonly known as Eyebright, is a cherished medicinal herb renowned for its ocular benefits. Traditionally used to treat inflammation and irritation, this delicate flowering plant offers potent antioxidant properties. It remains a vital botanical resource in herbalism for supporting eye health and soothing mucous membranes.

  • Scientific name: Euphrasia officinalis subsp. pratensis
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Euphrasia as the subspecies pratensis.
