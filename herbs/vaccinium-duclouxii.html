Vaccinium duclouxii

Vaccinium duclouxii, commonly known as the forest blueberry, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to specific temperate regions. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant offers therapeutic potential in traditional healing. Its nutrient-dense berries and bioactive compounds make it a subject of interest for modern pharmacological research and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vaccinium duclouxii
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ericaceae and falls under the genus Vaccinium.
