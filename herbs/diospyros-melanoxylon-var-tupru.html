Diospyros melanoxylon var. tupru

Diospyros melanoxylon var. tupru, commonly known as Tupru, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical forest ecosystems. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this variety is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering valuable bioactive compounds that continue to interest modern ethnobotanical research and development.

  • Scientific name: Diospyros melanoxylon var. tupru
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Ebenaceae. It is further identified by the genus Diospyros, specifically as the variety tupru.
