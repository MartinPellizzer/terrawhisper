Distribution

This plant exhibits a significant presence across several regions in Northern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Denmark. Its distribution extends further north into the landscapes of Finland and Norway. Additionally, the species is well-established throughout Great Britain. Finally, it is also documented to occur within Ireland.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as mallow, possesses various therapeutic components, but the fruit is a particularly notable part of its anatomy. These fruits are typically small, disc-shaped structures known as schizocarps, which consist of several individual mericarps that can be easily separated. In many medicinal preparations, the chemical constituents found within these seed-bearing structures are utilized for their specific properties, contributing to the overall traditional healing profile of the species.
