Lycoris longituba

Lycoris longituba, commonly known as the longituba spider lily, is a remarkable medicinal plant cherished in traditional practices. Renowned for its unique bioactive compounds, it is utilized to address various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. This resilient perennial offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lycoris longituba
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
