Melastoma malabathricum subsp. normale

Melastoma malabathricum subsp. normale, commonly known as the Malabar Melastome, is a significant medicinal shrub found in tropical ecosystems. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this subspecies is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin conditions, showcasing its vital role in ethnobotanical biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Melastoma malabathricum subsp. normale
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Melastomataceae and the genus Melastoma. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies Melastoma malabathricum subsp. normale.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across Eastern Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central regions and the island of Hainan. It can also be found throughout China Southeast and across the high-altitude terrain of Tibet. Additionally, the species extends its reach to the island of Taiwan. Together, these regions define the specific geographical footprint of this subspecies.
