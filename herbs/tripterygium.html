Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range throughout much of East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central regions and across the mountainous terrain of Tibet. It is also widely distributed in the Manchuria and China Southeast areas. Furthermore, the species extends its reach beyond the mainland into Japan. These varied locations highlight the significant ecological footprint of the Tripterygium genus.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Tripterygium wilfordii, contains various anatomical structures that are utilized for its medicinal properties. The therapeutic components are primarily concentrated in the underground portions, specifically within the root and the root bark. As the plant grows, the vascular tissues, consisting of the xylem and the phloem, facilitate the transport of nutrients and water throughout the system. In the mature specimen, the structural integrity of the plant is supported by the woody part of the root, which serves as a vital reservoir for the active compounds used in traditional pharmacology.
