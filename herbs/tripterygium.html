Tripterygium

Tripterygium, commonly known as Thunder God Vine, is a climbing woody plant prized in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is primarily used to treat inflammatory conditions and autoimmune disorders. While scientifically significant for its analgesic properties, it requires careful handling due to its toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Tripterygium
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Celastrales, specifically within the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tripterygium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range throughout much of East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central regions and across the mountainous terrain of Tibet. It is also widely distributed in the Manchuria and China Southeast areas. Furthermore, the species extends its reach beyond the mainland into Japan.
