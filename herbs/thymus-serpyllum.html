Wild thyme, scientifically known as Thymus serpyllum, is a fragrant, low-growing perennial herb native to Europe. Renowned for its aromatic leaves and delicate pink flowers, it serves as a potent medicinal plant. Rich in essential oils, it offers significant antibacterial, antioxidant, and digestive benefits in traditional herbal remedies.
