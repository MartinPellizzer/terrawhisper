Thymus serpyllum

Thymus serpyllum, commonly known as creeping thyme, is a resilient, low-growing perennial herb cherished for its aromatic foliage and delicate pink blossoms. Widely used in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, making it a valuable natural remedy for respiratory health and digestive wellness in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Thymus serpyllum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Thymus.
