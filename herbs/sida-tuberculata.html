Sida tuberculata

Sida tuberculata, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional practices, is a shrub valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Frequently used in folk medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, this plant offers rich bioactive compounds, making it a subject of interest for pharmacological research.

  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
