Senegal coraltree, scientifically known as Erythrina senelegensis, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Africa. Renowned for its vibrant orange blossoms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts, including its bark and leaves, are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive disorders and infections.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence can be documented within the borders of Benin. It also grows naturally throughout the territories of Burkina and Gambia. Furthermore, the species is found in the regions of Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends into Guinea-Bissau.
