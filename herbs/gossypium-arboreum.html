Gossypium arboreum

Gossypium arborium, commonly known as the tree cotton, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Beyond its commercial fiber, its leaves, bark, and seeds possess significant therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant effects, offering valuable contributions to natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Gossypium arboreum
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gossypium as the species Gossypium arboreum.

Distribution

This plant, Gossypium arboreum, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It is found natively within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species also maintains a presence in Ghana, contributing to the regional flora. Furthermore, its distribution extends into Mali, marking its range across diverse landscapes. Finally, the plant is widely recorded in both Nigeria and Togo, completing its presence in these West Tropical African nations.
