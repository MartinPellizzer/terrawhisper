Distribution

This plant, Gossypium arboreum, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It is found natively within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species also maintains a presence in Ghana, contributing to the regional flora. Furthermore, its distribution extends into Mali, marking its range across diverse landscapes. Finally, the plant is widely recorded in both Nigeria and Togo, completing its presence in these West Tropical African nations.
