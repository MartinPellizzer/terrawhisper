Calanthe arisanensis

Calanthe arisanensis, commonly known as the Golden Leaf, is a rare medicinal herb prized for its potent healing properties. Found deep within tropical rainforests, this plant contains unique alkaloids used traditionally to treat inflammation and fever. Understanding its biological profile is essential for modern pharmacological research and conservation efforts.

  • Scientific name: Calanthe arisanensis
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it falls under the Orchidaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by its genus, Calanthe, and its species name, arisanensis.
