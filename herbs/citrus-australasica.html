Australian finger lime, scientifically known as Citrus australasicia, is a unique citrus species native to subtropical rainforests. Renowned for its distinctive pearl-like juice vesicles, it offers an intense, acidic flavor profile. Beyond its culinary appeal, this "citrus caviar" is celebrated for its high antioxidant content and medicinal properties.
