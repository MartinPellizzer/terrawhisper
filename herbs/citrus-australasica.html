Citrus australasica

Australian finger lime

Citrus australasica, commonly known as the finger lime, is a unique Australian native fruit prized for its medicinal and culinary properties. Its tiny, acidic juice vesicles are packed with antioxidants and vitamins. Traditionally used for skin rejuvenation and dietary wellness, this "citrus caviar" offers powerful natural healing benefits.

  • Scientific name: Citrus australasica
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is further categorized under the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Citrus.
