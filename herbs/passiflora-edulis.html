Passiflora edulis

Passiflora edulis, commonly known as passion fruit, is a tropical climbing vine celebrated for its aromatic, nutrient-dense fruit. Renowned for its unique flavor profile, this medicinal plant offers significant health benefits, including sedative properties and antioxidant support, making it a valuable staple in both culinary and therapeutic applications worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Passiflora edulis
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Passiflora edulis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Passifloraceae. It is ultimately categorized under the genus Passiflora.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple continents and oceanic regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found specifically within the borders of Morocco. Its presence extends to the Macaronesian islands, including both the Azores and the Canary Islands. Furthermore, the species is distributed across West Tropical Africa, appearing in countries such as Benin. Finally, its range also includes the West African nation of Gambia.
