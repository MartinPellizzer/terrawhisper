What's the taxonomical classification of Styrax dasyanthus?

Styrax dasyanthus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the biological lineage of the order Ericales. The plant is further categorized under the family Styracaceae, which places it within the genus Styrax. Ultimately, its unique biological identity is defined by its specific designation as the species dasyanthus.
