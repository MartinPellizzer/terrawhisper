What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Swertia tibetica has opposite, decussate leaves that are elliptical to oblong in shape with entire margins and acute apices. The plant typically grows as a perennial shrub reaching heights of up to 1 meter. Its flowers are small, actinomorphic, and characterized by a distinct pentamerous structure. The corolla is generally white or pale yellowish, providing a subtle contrast to the green foliage. The fruit develops into a capsule that eventually dehisces to release small seeds.
