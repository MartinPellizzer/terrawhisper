Levantnut

Anamirta cocculus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (116 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Levantnut, scientifically known as Anamirta cocculus, is a climbing shrub native to South Asia. Historically used in traditional medicine, its dried berries contain physostigmine, a potent alkaloid. While valued for treating various ailments, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling, as it can be fatal if ingested incorrectly.
