This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range extends further into Eastern Asia, where it is native to both Japan and Taiwan. Additionally, the species reaches into the Indo-China region, specifically occurring in Myanmar.
