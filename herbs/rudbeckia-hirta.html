Rudbeckia hirta

Black-eyed susan

Rudbeckia hirta, commonly known as Black-eyed Susan, is a resilient perennial wildflower celebrated for its vibrant yellow petals and dark central cones. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this hardy plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism, often utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and immune-boosting medicinal properties in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Rudbeckia hirta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Rudbeckia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Black-eyed Susan, is often valued for its vibrant and distinctive flowers. The flowers are the most striking feature of Rudbeckia hirta, characterized by bright yellow ray florets that surround a prominent, dark central cone composed of tiny disc florets. In traditional herbal practices, various components of the plant have been studied, but the flowers are frequently highlighted for their visual appeal and their role in the plant's overall reproductive cycle.
