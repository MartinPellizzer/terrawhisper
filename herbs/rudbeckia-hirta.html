Black-eyed Susan, scientifically known as Rudbeckia hirta, is a resilient perennial celebrated for its vibrant golden petals and dark central cones. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this medicinal plant has historically been used in traditional herbalism to support immune health, treat skin inflammations, and soothe digestive ailments.
