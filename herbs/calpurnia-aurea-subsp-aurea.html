Calpurnia aurea subsp. aurea

Calpurnia aurea subsp. aurea, commonly known as the Golden Calpurnia, is a rare and striking perennial herb native to specialized woodland habitats. Renowned for its brilliant, cup-shaped yellow blooms, this delicate plant holds significant botanical interest, representing a unique evolutionary adaptation within its niche ecological community and medicinal landscape.
