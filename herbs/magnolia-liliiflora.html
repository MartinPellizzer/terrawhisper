Magnolia liliiflora

Magnolia liliflora, commonly known as the lily-flowered magnolia, is a stunning deciduous shrub celebrated for its elegant, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significance in traditional herbal practices. Its fragrant flowers and unique botanical properties make it a fascinating subject for both gardeners and medicinal enthusiasts.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia liliiflora
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is further categorized into the family Magnoliaceae. Ultimately, Magnolia liliflora is identified under the genus Magnolia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Magnolia liliflora, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In Czech, it is called šácholan liliokvětý, while in German, it is referred to as Purpur-Magnolie. Hungarian speakers call it kínai liliomfa, and in Luxembourgish, it is known as Purpurmagnolia. In Japan, the species is commonly identified by the name モクレン.
