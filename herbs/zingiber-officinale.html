Zingiber officinale

Ginger

Zingiber officinale, commonly known as ginger, is a perennial herbaceous plant celebrated globally for its potent medicinal properties. Belonging to the Zingiberaceae family, its aromatic rhizome is widely utilized in traditional medicine to alleviate nausea, reduce inflammation, and aid digestion, making it a cornerstone of holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Zingiber officinale
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, it is further categorized into the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Zingiber, specifically known as Zingiber officinale.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Zingiber officinale, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. In Chinese, it is referred to as 薑 (zh), while in Javanese, it is called Jaé (jv). Its traditional name in Tibetan is བཅའ་ལྒ། (bo), and it is known as imbir lekarski (pl) in Polish. Additionally, in the Basque language, the plant is called Jengibre (eu).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several tropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its distribution includes the Gulf of Guinea Islands. The species is also well-established throughout the Western Indian Ocean. Specifically, it can be found in the Comoros, Mauritius, and Madagascar. Finally, its geographical range extends to the island of Réunion within the same oceanic region.

Plant Parts

This plant, Zingiber officinale, is most commonly valued for its underground stems, which are frequently referred to as the ginger root. These fleshy rhizomes, or more broadly the rhizomes of the plant, serve as the primary source of its medicinal properties and distinctive aroma. Within these structures, high concentrations of essential oils are stored, providing the plant with its characteristic pungent scent and therapeutic benefits. In addition to the rhizomes, the plant also produces long, green leaves that contribute to its overall biological structure.
