Achillea millefolium

Achillea millefolium, commonly known as yarrow, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for centuries in traditional medicine. Characterized by its feathery foliage, this resilient plant offers potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties. It remains a cornerstone in herbalism, widely used to support skin health and digestive wellness naturally.

  • Scientific name: Achillea millefolium
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Achillea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several northern regions of Europe. In the northern reaches of the continent, it can be found growing throughout Denmark and Finland. Its distribution also extends to the rugged landscapes of Iceland and the Faroe Islands. Additionally, the species is commonly documented across Great Britain. These locations collectively highlight the plant's preference for northern European climates.
