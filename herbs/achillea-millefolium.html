common yarrow

Achillea millefolium · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1406 studies) · ★★★★★

Common yarrow, scientifically known as Achillea millefolium, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for its healing properties. Renowned since antiquity, this plant features feathery foliage and clusters of daisy-like flowers. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to support wound healing, reduce inflammation, and soothe digestive discomfort naturally.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as yarrow, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components offer different therapeutic benefits. The feathery, finely divided leaves are highly characteristic and are often used in herbal preparations for their astringent properties. The plant features delicate, white or pinkish flowers that are arranged in dense, flat-topped inflorescences, which serve as a key identifying feature in the field. While the aerial portions are most frequently harvested, the root can also be utilized in specific traditional remedies. Ultimately, many herbalists prefer using the whole plant to ensure a full spectrum of its active phytochemical constituents.
