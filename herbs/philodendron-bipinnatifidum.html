Philodendron bipinnatifidum

Thaumatophyllum bipinnatifidum

Philodendron bipinnatifidum, commonly known as the Selloum or Tree Philodendron, is a striking tropical plant valued for its ornamental beauty. While primarily used in landscaping, various cultures explore its large, deeply lobed leaves for traditional medicinal purposes, often utilizing its unique properties to treat diverse ailments in herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Philodendron bipinnatifidum
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Alismatales, it is further categorized into the family Araceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Philodendron.
