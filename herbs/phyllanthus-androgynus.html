This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across various parts of Asia. In China, it is widely distributed throughout the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Moving towards the Indian Subcontinent, the species is also found in the state of Assam. Furthermore, its presence extends into the neighboring territory of Bangladesh. Together, these locations highlight the plant's preference for diverse subtropical environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, Phyllanthus androgynus, utilizes its leaves as a primary component in traditional medicine. While the fresh foliage is known for its nutritional value, the dried leaf is frequently processed and used to create various therapeutic preparations. These dried leaves are valued for their concentrated bioactive compounds, making them a key medicinal part of the plant used to support overall wellness.
