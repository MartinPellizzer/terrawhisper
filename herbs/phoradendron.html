Phoradendron

Phoradendron, commonly known as mistletoe, is a fascinating parasitic plant found across diverse ecosystems. Renowned for its unique lifecycle and striking evergreen foliage, it holds significant cultural symbolism. Beyond folklore, various species are studied for their bioactive compounds, offering intriguing potential in traditional medicine and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Phoradendron
  • Family: Santalaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
