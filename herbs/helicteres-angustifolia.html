What's the taxonomical classification of Helicteres angustifolia?

Helicteres angustifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is a member of the family Malvaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized under the genus Helicteres, with its specific designation being the species angustifolia.

The study utilized an Illumina high-throughput sequencing platform to generate 121 gigabases of data for analysis. From this raw data, 424,824 unigenes were obtained after trimming and assembly, with 22,430 unigenes determined to be differentially expressed. Research identified three oxidosqualene cyclases, four Cytochrome P450s, and three acyltransferases as candidate genes. Functional verification confirmed that one OSC, named HaOSC1, and one CYP450, named HaCYPi3, catalyze the production of lupeol and oleanolic acid.
