Sterculia setigera

Sterculia setigera, commonly known as the velvet-leaf Sterculia, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and digestive issues. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a vital subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Family: Malvaceae
