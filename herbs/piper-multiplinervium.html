Piper multiplinervium

Piper multiplinervium, commonly known as long pepper or various regional names, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health, alleviate inflammation, and enhance metabolic functions, offering a rich natural resource for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Piper multiplinervium
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, falling into the family Piperaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Piper.
