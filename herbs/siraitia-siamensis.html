Siraitia siamensis

Siraitia siavensis, commonly known as the Siamese climbing plant, is a versatile medicinal climber native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and stems are frequently utilized for their anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, offering a natural approach to treating various ailments and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Siraitia siamensis
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is categorized within the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Siraitia.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning several distinct ecological regions. Within China, its presence is documented across both the South-Central and Southeast territories.
