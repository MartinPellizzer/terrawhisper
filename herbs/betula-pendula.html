Silver birch, scientifically known as Betula pendula, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Its bark, leaves, and sap contain bioactive compounds like betulin and flavonoids. Traditionally used to support kidney health, reduce inflammation, and promote skin healing, it remains a cornerstone in herbal medicine worldwide.
