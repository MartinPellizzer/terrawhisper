Betula pendula

Betula pendula, commonly known as the silver birch, is a versatile deciduous tree prized for its medicinal properties. Its bark, leaves, and sap contain bioactive compounds with diuretic, anti-inflammatory, and antibacterial effects. Traditionally used to support kidney health and skin conditions, this elegant species remains a cornerstone of herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Betula pendula
  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fagales and the family Betulaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Betula, specifically as the species Betula pendula.
