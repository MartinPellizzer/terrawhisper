Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is well-documented throughout the Amur and Khabarovsk regions. It can also be found spanning the coastal and island territories of Primorye and the Kuril Islands. Furthermore, the species extends its range into northern China, specifically within Inner Mongolia. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse habitats across these territories.
