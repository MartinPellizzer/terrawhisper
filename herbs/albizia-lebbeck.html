Siriisa, scientifically known as Albizia lebbeck, is a versatile medicinal tree native to South Asia. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, it is widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Its bark, leaves, and seeds are frequently employed to treat skin ailments, wounds, and various inflammatory conditions.
