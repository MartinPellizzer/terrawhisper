Lepidium apetalum

Lepidium apetalum, commonly known as the "pepperweed" or "mountain pepper," is a resilient medicinal herb native to specific high-altitude regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant has long been utilized in traditional healing practices to address inflammatory conditions and support overall respiratory and digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lepidium apetalum
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lepidium.
