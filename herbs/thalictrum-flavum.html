Thalictrum flavum

Common meadow-rue

Thalictrum flavum, commonly known as Yellow Meadow Rue, is a perennial herbaceous plant distinguished by its delicate, fern-like foliage and bright yellow blossoms. Native to temperate regions, this species thrives in moist habitats, offering unique botanical interest and traditional medicinal potential within various ecological and herbal healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Thalictrum flavum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Ranunculales, falling into the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Thalictrum.
