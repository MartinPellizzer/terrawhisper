Red Plum, scientifically known as Spondias purpurea, is a versatile tropical fruit tree prized for its luscious, tangy flesh. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Its leaves, bark, and fruit are traditionally utilized for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties in various healing practices.
