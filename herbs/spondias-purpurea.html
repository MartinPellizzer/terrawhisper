Spondias purpurea

Spondias purpurea, commonly known as the purple mombin or ambarella, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree celebrated for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, its fruit, leaves, and bark are traditionally used to treat digestive issues, inflammation, and skin conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Spondias purpurea
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Spondias purpurea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Spondias.
