Fagopyrum esculentum

Fagopyrum esculentum, commonly known as buckwheat, is a versatile pseudocereal valued globally for its nutritional density. Rich in antioxidants, rutin, and essential amino acids, it offers significant health benefits, including improved cardiovascular function and blood sugar regulation. This resilient plant remains a vital dietary staple in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Fagopyrum esculentum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe
