Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as buckwheat, utilizes various components for both nutritional and medicinal purposes. The inflorescence consists of small, fragrant white or pinkish flowers that bloom in clusters, attracting pollinators essential for reproduction. The leaf is typically lanceolate or arrowhead-shaped and is often utilized in traditional herbal preparations due to its bioactive properties. Most notably, the seeds are the most widely consumed part, serving as a nutrient-dense pseudocereal that is prized for its high fiber content and ability to support cardiovascular health.
