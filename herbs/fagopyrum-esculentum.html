Buckwheat plant, scientifically known as Fagopyrum esculentum, is a versatile pseudocereal celebrated for its profound nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants like rutin, it supports cardiovascular health, regulates blood sugar, and reduces inflammation. This resilient plant serves as a vital dietary staple and a potent natural remedy.
