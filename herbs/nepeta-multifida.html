Nepeta multifida

Nepeta multifida, commonly known as Split Nepeta, is a charming perennial herb celebrated for its delicate, deeply lobed foliage and clusters of tiny, pale lavender flowers. Belonging to the mint family, this resilient plant thrives in sunny gardens, offering beautiful textures and attracting pollinators like bees and butterflies.

  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant, Nepeta multifida, exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the vast regions of Siberia.
