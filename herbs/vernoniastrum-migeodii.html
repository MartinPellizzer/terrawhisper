Vernoniastrum migeodii

Vernoniastrum migeodii, commonly known as the Silver Leaf Herb, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments and skin infections. This resilient botanical offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Vernoniastrum migeodii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa
