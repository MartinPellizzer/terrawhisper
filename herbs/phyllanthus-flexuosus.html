Phyllanthus flexuosus

Phyllanthus flexuosus, commonly known as the flexuous leaf euphyllanthus, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional herbal medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This plant is frequently utilized to support liver health, manage digestive issues, and promote overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus flexuosus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Malpighiales and placed in the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Phyllanthus.
