Plant Parts

This plant, Malvaviscus arboreus, commonly known as Turk's cap, features distinct morphological characteristics across its various components. The leaves are typically simple, ovate to heart-shaped, and possess serrated margins that provide a lush, green backdrop for the plant. The flowers are perhaps its most striking feature, consisting of bright red, tubular structures that lack petals and instead showcase prominent, protruding stamens. Both the leaves and the flowers are integral to the plant's identity, with the vibrant blossoms acting as a major attractant for pollinators like hummingbirds.
