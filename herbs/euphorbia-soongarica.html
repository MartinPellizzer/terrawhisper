Euphorbia soongarica

Euphorbia soongarica, commonly known as a member of the diverse Euphorbiaceae family, is a medicinal plant valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Often found in specific tropical regions, it is studied for its potential therapeutic applications, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities, making it a subject of significant ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia soongarica
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
