Elsholtzia rugulosa

Elsholtzia rugulosa, commonly known as the rugose elm, is a deciduous tree valued for its medicinal and ornamental properties. Traditionally used in herbal practices, its bark and leaves possess bioactive compounds with potential therapeutic benefits. This resilient species plays a significant role in biodiversity and traditional ecological knowledge worldwide.
