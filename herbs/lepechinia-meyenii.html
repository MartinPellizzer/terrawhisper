Lepechinia meyenii

Lepechinia meyenii, commonly known as the "Chiloé mint," is a fragrant perennial herb native to the temperate forests of Chile. Valued in traditional medicine, this aromatic plant is frequently utilized for its soothing properties, particularly in treating digestive ailments, respiratory issues, and promoting relaxation through its essential oils.

  • Scientific name: Lepechinia meyenii
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification
