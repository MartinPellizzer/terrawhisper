Thymus numidicus

Thymus numidicus, commonly known as Numidian thyme, is a distinctive aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean landscapes of North Africa. Characterized by its resilient structure and potent essential oils, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its antimicrobial, digestive, and antiseptic properties within various regional folk medicine practices.

  • Scientific name: Thymus numidicus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Thymus.
