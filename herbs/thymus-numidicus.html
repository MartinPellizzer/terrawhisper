Numidian thyme, scientifically known as Thymus numidicus, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to North Africa. Renowned for its intense fragrance and therapeutic potency, this medicinal herb is rich in essential oils. It is traditionally utilized for its potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and digestive properties in various natural remedies.
