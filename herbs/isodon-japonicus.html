Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several regions in East Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the North-Central provinces to the South-Central regions. Additionally, its presence extends into the Manchuria region of the country. Beyond mainland China, the species is also widely distributed throughout the Korean Peninsula. Finally, it is naturally occurring within the islands of Japan.
