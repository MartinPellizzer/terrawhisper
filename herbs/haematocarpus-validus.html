Haematocarpus validus

Haematocarpus validus, commonly known as the Bloodwood, is a distinctive tree native to the tropical rainforests of northern Australia. Renowned for its striking, deep-red sap, this species plays a vital role in its ecosystem. It is highly valued in traditional medicinal practices for its potent antimicrobial and healing properties.
