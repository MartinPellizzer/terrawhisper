Abyssinian Dock, scientifically known as Rumex abyssinicus, is a versatile medicinal herb native to the Ethiopian highlands. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and skin conditions.
