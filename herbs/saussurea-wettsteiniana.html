Saussurea wettsteiniana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is part of the order Asterales and falls under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Saussurea, and its unique species designation, wettsteiniana.
