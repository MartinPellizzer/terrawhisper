Uvaria acuminata

Uvaria acuminata, commonly known as a medicinal climber in various tropical regions, belongs to the Annonaceae family. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for developing new natural therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Uvaria acuminata
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Uvaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in the African continent and surrounding islands. In Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in Somalia. It is also widely found throughout East Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Kenya and Tanzania. Moving further south, the species can be located in South Tropical Africa within Mozambique. Finally, its range extends into the Western Indian Ocean, where it is found on the island of Madagascar.
