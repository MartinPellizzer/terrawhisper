Sandy everlasting, scientifically known as Helicrysum arenarium, is a resilient medicinal herb native to coastal regions. Renowned for its striking golden blooms and aromatic foliage, this plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support skin health and promote overall wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Helichrysum arenarium, is characterized by its distinct reproductive structures, most notably its terminal inflorescences. These inflorescences are composed of multiple small, golden-yellow flowers that are clustered together to form a dense, head-like arrangement. The individual flowers are typically surrounded by papery, straw-colored bracts, which are a hallmark of the Helichrysum genus and contribute to the plant's striking visual appearance.
