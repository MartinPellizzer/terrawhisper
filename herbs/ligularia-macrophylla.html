Ligularia macrophylla

Ligularia macrophylla, commonly known as the Giant Leopard Plant, is a striking perennial cherished for its dramatic, large foliage. While primarily used in ornamental landscaping, this plant holds significance in traditional herbal practices. Its robust growth and unique aesthetic make it a captivating addition to any medicinal or garden collection.

  • Scientific name: Ligularia macrophylla
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Ligularia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within Central Asia, its presence is recorded in both Kazakhstan and Kirgizstan. It can also be found in the Xinjiang region of China. Additionally, its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within Pakistan. These locations highlight the varied environmental niches this species inhabits across the continent.
