Spiraea chamaedryfolia

Spiraea chamaedryfolia, commonly known as meadowsweet, is a perennial herb cherished for its fragrant, creamy flower clusters. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it contains salicylic acid, a natural precursor to aspirin. This resilient plant thrives in moist meadows, offering both aesthetic beauty and significant therapeutic potential in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Spiraea chamaedryfolia
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Spiraea chamaedryfolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Its taxonomic lineage concludes with its designation in the genus Spiraea.
