Urera baccifera

Urera baccifera, commonly known as stinging nettle tree, is a medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various systemic disorders. This versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential in holistic and pharmacological wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Urera baccifera
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosaless, falling into the family Urticaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Urera.
