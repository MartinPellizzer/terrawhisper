Populus tremula

Aspen

Populus tremula, commonly known as the aspen, is a deciduous tree valued for its medicinal properties. Traditionally, its bark has been utilized in herbal remedies to treat inflammation and fever. Rich in bioactive compounds, this resilient species continues to be a subject of interest in ethnobotanical and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Populus tremula
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Salicaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Populus.
