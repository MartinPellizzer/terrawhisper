Aspen, scientifically known as Populus tremula, is a deciduous tree valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. Its bark contains salicylates, offering natural anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects. Often utilized to treat fevers and aches, this resilient species remains a significant subject in both ethnobotany and pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant, known as Populus tremula, exhibits a widespread presence throughout the northern reaches of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing naturally across the landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Its distribution also extends westward to include the islands of Great Britain and Ireland. Additionally, the species is well-established within the mountainous and coastal regions of Norway. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various northern habitats.
