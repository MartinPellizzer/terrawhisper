Polygonum emodi

What's the taxonomical classification of Polygonum emodi?

Polygonum emodi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Caryophyllales. This plant is a member of the family Polygonaceae and is specifically identified by its genus, Polygonum, and its species name, emodi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Polygonum emodi has an erect, herbaceous stem that typically grows to a height of thirty to sixty centimeters and features slender, cylindrical branches. The leaves are arranged alternately along the stem and exhibit an ovate to lanceolate shape with smooth or slightly wavy margins. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in axillary clusters, often possessing a delicate white or pale pink coloration. The plant is further characterized by its distinct ocreae, which are membranous sheaths surrounding the nodes of the stem. Its reproductive structures culminate in small, dry achenes that are characteristic of the Polygonaceae family.
