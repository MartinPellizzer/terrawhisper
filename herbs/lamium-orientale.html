Lamium orientale, scientifically known as Lamium orientale, is a charming perennial herb celebrated for its striking variegated foliage and delicate pink blossoms. Often found in shaded woodland settings, this resilient plant is prized by gardeners and herbalists alike for its ornamental beauty and traditional roles in holistic wellness practices.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across multiple continents and regions. In Middle Europe, its presence can be observed within the borders of Germany. Moving toward the African continent, it is also found in Northern Africa, specifically in Tunisia. Its range extends significantly into Western Asia, where it inhabits the regions of Lebanon, Syria, and Palestine. Finally, the distribution continues through Western Asia to include the territory of Türkiye.
