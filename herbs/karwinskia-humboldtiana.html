Karwinskia humboldtiana

Karwinskia humboldtiana, commonly known as "fresadilla," is a remarkable medicinal herb native to Mexico. Valued in traditional folk medicine, it is primarily used for its purported anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties. This plant offers a fascinating glimpse into botanical healing, serving as a vital resource for managing various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Karwinskia humboldtiana
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Rhamnaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Karwinskia, with the species name humboldtiana.
