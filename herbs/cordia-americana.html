Cordia americana

Cordia americana, commonly known as cherry velvet tree, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Americas. Renowned for its soft, velvety foliage, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and digestive issues, offering a rich source of bioactive phytochemicals for healing.

  • Scientific name: Cordia americana
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cordia, specifically as the species Cordia americana.
