Rhaponticum acaule

Rhaponticum acaule, commonly known as the dwarf mountain rhubarb, is a resilient perennial herb native to alpine and subalpine regions. Characterized by its low-growing stature and distinctive leaves, this medicinal plant has historically been utilized in traditional ethnobotany for its unique properties within high-altitude ecosystems and specialized herbal applications.

  • Scientific name: Rhaponticum acaule
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Rhaponticum.
