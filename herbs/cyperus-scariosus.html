Cyperus scariosus

Cyperus scariosus, commonly known as the creeping sedge, is a resilient perennial herb found in wetland ecosystems. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic applications, particularly in treating digestive ailments and supporting overall wellness through its natural compounds.

  • Scientific name: Cyperus scariosus
  • Family: Cyperaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Poales and is a member of the family Cyperaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Cyperus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Mozambique. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found within the borders of Bangladesh. The species also extends into the Malesia region, particularly on the island of Jawa, and reaches into Papuasia via New Guinea.
