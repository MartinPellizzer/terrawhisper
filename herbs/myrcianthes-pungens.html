Myrcianthes pungens

Myrcianthes pungens, commonly known as the prickly myrtle, is a resilient evergreen shrub valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, this plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation and skin ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and herbal applications.

  • Scientific name: Myrcianthes pungens
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtaleales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae and the genus Myrcianthes.
