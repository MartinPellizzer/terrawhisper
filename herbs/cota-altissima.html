Southern Chamomile, scientifically known as Cota altissima, is a versatile medicinal herb native to South America. Renowned for its soothing properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional remedies to alleviate digestive discomfort, reduce inflammation, and calm anxiety. This potent plant offers a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.
