Marrubium alysson

Marrubium alysson, commonly known as White Horehound, is a perennial herb valued for its significant therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this aromatic plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to soothe respiratory ailments, act as an expectorant, and alleviate coughs, offering a natural remedy for lung health.

  • Scientific name: Marrubium alysson
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Marrubium.
