Mesembryanthemum nodiflorum

Slenderleaf iceplant

Mesembryanthemum nodiflorum, commonly known as the nodding ice plant, is a succulent perennial valued in traditional herbal medicine. Native to various arid regions, it possesses unique bioactive properties. This resilient plant is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic applications, particularly in addressing inflammatory conditions and supporting overall skin health.

  • Scientific name: Mesembryanthemum nodiflorum
  • Family: Aizoaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the Aizoaceae family, falling under the genus Mesembryanthemum.
