Miconia albicans

Miconia albicans, commonly known as the white miconia, is a distinctive shrub native to the tropical forests of Central and South America. Characterized by its striking silvery foliage and vibrant berries, this resilient plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem, offering significant medicinal potential in traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Miconia albicans
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Myrtales, specifically within the family Melastomataceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Miconia.
