Vernicia montana

Vernicia montana, commonly known as the tung oil tree, is a versatile medicinal and industrial plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for producing high-quality tung oil, it also holds significant ethnobotanical value. Its seeds and extracts are traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat skin conditions and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Vernicia montana
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is found within the genus Vernicia.
