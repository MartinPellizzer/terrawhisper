Casearia arborea

Casearia arborea, commonly known as the "tree of life" in certain tropical regions, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional ethnobotany. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Casearia arborea
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Salicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Casearia.
