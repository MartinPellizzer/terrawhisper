Primula

Primula, commonly known as primrose, is a diverse genus of flowering perennials celebrated for their vibrant blooms and delicate foliage. Beyond their ornamental charm, many species possess significant medicinal properties, traditionally used in herbal remedies to soothe respiratory issues, reduce inflammation, and support overall wellness through their bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Primula
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Primulaceae and is defined by the genus Primula.
