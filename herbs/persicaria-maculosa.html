Persicaria maculosa

Persicaria maculosa, commonly known as spotted bindweed, is a resilient perennial herb recognized by its distinctive dark spots on trailing leaves. Native to Eurasia, this plant thrives in moist, shaded environments. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it is valued for its antioxidant properties and potential anti-inflammatory therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria maculosa
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae and falls under the genus Persicaria.
