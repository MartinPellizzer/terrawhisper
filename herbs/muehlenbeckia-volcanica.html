Muehlenbeckia volcanica

Muehlenbeckia volcanic, commonly known as volcanic lace vine, is a resilient medicinal plant prized for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific volcanic terrains, this hardy species offers significant phytochemical benefits. Exploring its traditional uses provides valuable insights into natural healing, making it a subject of growing botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Muehlenbeckia volcanica
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae and falls under the genus Muehlenbeckia.
