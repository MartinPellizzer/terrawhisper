Lepidium didymum

Coronopus didymus

Lepidium didymum, commonly known as peppercress, is a versatile annual herb native to various regions. Renowned for its pungent, peppery flavor, it serves as a nutrient-dense edible plant. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, this medicinal herb is traditionally utilized for its anti-inflammatory properties and to support overall digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Lepidium didymum
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Lepidium didymum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Brassicales. Finally, it is a member of the family Brassicaceae and is situated within the genus Lepidium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lepidium didymum, is referred to by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In German, it is commonly called Zweiknotiger Krähenfuß, while in Switzerland, the spelling shifts slightly to Zweiknotiger Krähenfuss. In Japan, the species is known as カラクサナズナ, and among the Quechua-speaking people, it is identified as Kimpi. Additionally, in the Upper Sorbian dialect, it carries the name Suckata ronica.
