Acacia mearnsii

Black wattle

Acacia mearnsii, commonly known as Black Wattle, is a versatile evergreen tree native to Australia. Renowned for its high tannin content in bark, it is globally significant in the leather tanning industry. This resilient species also serves essential roles in reforestation, soil stabilization, and supporting diverse local ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Acacia mearnsii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Acacia. As a member of the genus Acacia, Acacia mearnsii is specifically recognized for its medicinal and industrial properties.

When harvested, the bark provides the essential chemical compounds required for various medicinal preparations, making this specific plant part central to its biological utility.
