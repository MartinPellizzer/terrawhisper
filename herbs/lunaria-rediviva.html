Lunaria rediviva

Lunaria rediviva, commonly known as the Rejuvenating Moonflower, is a rare medicinal herb celebrated for its restorative properties. Renowned in traditional healing for its ability to revitalize vital energies, this luminous plant offers potent antioxidant benefits, supporting cellular regeneration and promoting overall wellness through its unique botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Lunaria rediviva
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales, it falls under the family Brassicaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lunaria.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lunaria rediviva, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Russian, it is called Лунник оживающий, while in Dutch, it is known as wilde judaaspennink. German speakers call it Ausdauerndes Silberblatt, and in Swedish, it is referred to as Månviol. Additionally, in Lithuanian, the plant is known as Daugiametė blizgė.
