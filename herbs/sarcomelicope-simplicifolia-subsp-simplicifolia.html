Sarcomelicope simplicifolia subsp. simplicifolia

Sarcomelicope simplicifolia subsp. simplicifolia, commonly known as a vital medicinal shrub in Neotropical regions, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this subspecies is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices to treat various ailments, offering a rich subject for pharmacological research and biodiversity studies.

  • Scientific name: Sarcomelicope simplicifolia subsp. simplicifolia
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Australasia

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Sarcomelicope simplicifolia subsp. simplicifolia, possesses a very specific and limited geographical range. Its distribution is highly localized rather than widespread across large continents. Within the broader region, its presence is documented exclusively in Australia. Specifically, this subspecies can be found inhabiting the unique environment of Norfolk Island. Consequently, its natural occurrence is restricted to this single oceanic territory.
