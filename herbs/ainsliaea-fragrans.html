Ainsliaea fragrans

Ainsliaea fragrans, commonly known as fragrant china berry, is a deciduous shrub native to East Asian forests. Renowned for its delicate, fragrant white blossoms and distinctive serrated leaves, this botanical species holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its various components are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Ainsliaea fragrans
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Ainsliaea fragrans, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Ainsliaea.
