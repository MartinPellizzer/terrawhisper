Nigerian stylo, scientifically known as Stylosanthes erecta, is a versatile medicinal herb widely utilized in traditional medicine across Africa. Often referred to as "button weed," this plant possesses significant pharmacological properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, making it a valuable resource for treating various skin conditions and digestive ailments.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Buttonweed, is characterized by its distinct morphology where the medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in the aerial parts. The aerial part of Stylosanthes erecta consists of a prostrate to ascending stem, small trifoliate leaves, and tiny, yellow, button-like flower heads that grow close to the ground. In herbal applications, the entire aerial parts are often harvested for their bioactive compounds, as the leaves and flowers are the primary components used in traditional medicinal preparations.
