Neurolaena lobata

Neurolaena lobata, commonly known as the Lobate Neurolae, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its potent bioactive compounds. Renowned in traditional healing practices, it offers therapeutic potential in managing neurological conditions. Understanding its unique chemical profile is essential for unlocking its profound benefits in modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Pacific

This plant, Neurolaena lobata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Neurolaena.
