Bushy fleabane, scientifically known as Neurolaena lobata, is a resilient perennial herb native to various tropical regions. Characterized by its dense, bushy growth and distinctive yellow flower heads, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic and healing properties.

This plant, Neurolaena lobata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Neurolaena.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions of the Pacific Ocean. In the Southwestern Pacific, its presence is noted in the Gilbert Islands and the Phoenix Islands. Moving into the South-Central Pacific, it can also be found within the Society Islands. The Northwestern Pacific also serves as a significant habitat, specifically across the Caroline Islands. Finally, its range extends to include the Mariana Islands, completing its broad oceanic spread.
