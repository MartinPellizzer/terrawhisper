Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted within Nigeria. Moving toward East Tropical Africa, the species can be found in both Kenya and Tanzania. Its range extends further south into South Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Malawi. Finally, the plant is also distributed throughout Mozambique, completing its wide regional reach.
