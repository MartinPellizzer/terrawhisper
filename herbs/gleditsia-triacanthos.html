Gleditsia triacanthos

Honey locust

Gleditsia triacantho, commonly known as the three-thorn honey locust, is a resilient medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Characterized by its formidable thorns and unique foliage, it offers therapeutic potential through its bark and pods. Exploring its phytochemical properties reveals significant applications in holistic healing and natural pharmacology.
