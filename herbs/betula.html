Birch, scientifically known as Betula, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, various parts—including bark, leaves, and sap—are used to support kidney health, skin vitality, and detoxification. This resilient tree remains a cornerstone in traditional herbal medicine globally.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across various regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Denmark and Finland. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as birch, utilizes various components for its biological functions and medicinal potential. The leaves are often harvested for their diuretic properties and can be used to create soothing herbal infusions. The bark, which is highly distinctive due to its papery texture, contains betulin and other compounds that are studied for their anti-inflammatory and skin-healing benefits. Additionally, the pollen produced by the tree plays a critical role in its reproductive cycle, though it is also frequently noted in discussions regarding seasonal allergies.
