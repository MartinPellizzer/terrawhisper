Betula

Birch

Betula, commonly known as birch, is a versatile genus of trees renowned for its therapeutic properties. Beyond its striking bark, various species offer significant medicinal benefits, particularly through their diuretic, anti-inflammatory, and antiseptic qualities. Traditionally used in herbal remedies, birch extracts support kidney health and skin vitality effectively.

  • Scientific name: Betula
  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Betulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Betula.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across various regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Denmark and Finland. Additionally, its presence extends to the island territories of Føroyar and Iceland. The species is also documented throughout the landscape of Great Britain. Together, these locations highlight the plant's preference for northern maritime and continental climates.
