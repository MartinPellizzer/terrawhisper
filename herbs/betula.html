Birch, scientifically known as Betula, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, various parts—including bark, leaves, and sap—are used to support kidney health, skin vitality, and detoxification. This resilient tree remains a cornerstone in traditional herbal medicine globally.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across various regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Denmark and Finland. Additionally, its presence extends to the island territories of Føroyar and Iceland. The species is also documented throughout the landscape of Great Britain. Together, these locations highlight the plant's preference for northern maritime and continental climates.
