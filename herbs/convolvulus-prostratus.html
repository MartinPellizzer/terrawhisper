Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated primarily within the northern regions of the African continent. Its distribution extends across several key countries in Northern Africa, including Algeria and Egypt. Additionally, it can be found throughout the territories of Libya and Morocco. Beyond the mainland, its presence reaches into the Macaronesian islands. Specifically, the species is documented to grow in the archipelago of Cape Verde.
