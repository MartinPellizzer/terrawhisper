Mandarin, scientifically known as Citrus reticulata, is a highly valued citrus fruit celebrated for its vibrant flavor and therapeutic properties. Rich in vitamin C and essential antioxidants, it is widely used in traditional medicine to boost immunity, aid digestion, and promote skin health through its potent bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant is primarily identified by its specific geographical presence in certain regions. It is native to and widely distributed throughout China. More specifically, its habitat is concentrated in the Southeast part of the country. This localized distribution plays a significant role in where the species can be harvested for medicinal purposes. Consequently, the Southeast Chinese landscape serves as a key environment for the growth of Citrus reticulata.
