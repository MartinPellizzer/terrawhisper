Strychnos congolana, scientifically known as Strychnos congolana, is a significant medicinal plant native to African tropical forests. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, particularly strychnine, it is traditionally utilized in various ethnobotanical practices. While biologically powerful, its applications require careful study due to the plant's intense toxicity and pharmacological complexity.
