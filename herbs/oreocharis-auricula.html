Oreocharis auricula

Oreocharis auriculata, commonly known as the auriculate Oreocharis, is a unique medicinal plant native to specialized forest ecosystems. Characterized by its distinctively ear-like leaf bases, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to explore potential therapeutic applications in traditional medicine and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Oreocharis auricula
  • Family: Gesneriaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Oreocharis auriculata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales. Finally, it is classified within the family Gesneriaceae and the genus Oreocharis.
