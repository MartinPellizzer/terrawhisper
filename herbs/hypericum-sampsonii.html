Hypericum sampsonii

Hypericum sampsonii, commonly known as Samson's St. John's Wort, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific rocky terrains. Distinguished by its unique floral structure, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Researchers study its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications, exploring its role in traditional healing and modern pharmacological development.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum sampsonii
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Hypericum sampsonii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Ultimately, it is classified under the genus Hypericum.

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several distinct regions within East Asia. In China, its range extends through the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Beyond the Chinese mainland, it can also be found growing in Japan. Additionally, the species is documented to exist within the island of Taiwan.
