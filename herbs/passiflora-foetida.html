Common passion flower, scientifically known as Passiflora foetida, is a vigorous climbing vine native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive hairy stems and striking, complex blooms, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional healing. It is primarily utilized for its sedative, anti-inflammatory, and calming therapeutic properties.
