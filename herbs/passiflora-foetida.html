Passiflora foetida

Common passion flower

Passiflora foetida, commonly known as stinking passionflower, is a creeping perennial vine native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive, hairy bracts and vibrant flowers, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine. Its various parts are studied for their sedative, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Passiflora foetida
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Passifloraceae and falls under the genus Passiflora.
