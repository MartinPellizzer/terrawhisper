Tetrastigma hemsleyanum

Tetrastigma hemsleyanum, commonly known as the "miracle vine," is a medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds, it is widely studied for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and anti-tumor properties, offering significant potential for developing new treatments for various metabolic and chronic diseases.

  • Scientific name: Tetrastigma hemsleyanum
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Tetrastigma hemsleyanum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Vitales. Finally, it is a member of the family Vitaeaceae and the genus Tetrastigma.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is noted within the South-Central and Southeast provinces. Furthermore, the species can be found growing in the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Beyond the mainland, the plant is also documented in the island territory of Taiwan. These specific locations define the known natural range for Tetrastigma hemsleyanum.
