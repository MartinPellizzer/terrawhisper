Cirsium maackii

Cirsium maackii, commonly known as the Maacki thistle, is a resilient perennial herb native to North American montane regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. Researchers continue to explore its phytochemical potential, focusing on its medicinal applications in treating inflammation and supporting overall metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Cirsium maackii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Cirsium.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. Within the Russian Far East, its presence is documented in both Khabarovsk and Primorye.
