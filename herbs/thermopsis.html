Thermopsis, scientifically known as Thermopsis chinensis, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains potent alkaloids used to treat various ailments. However, due to its high toxicity, it must be handled with extreme caution and precise medicinal expertise.
