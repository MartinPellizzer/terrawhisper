Indian globe thistle, scientifically known as Echinops echinatus, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its unique, spiky blue flower heads. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. Often used to support digestive health and immune function, this resilient plant remains a vital botanical resource.
