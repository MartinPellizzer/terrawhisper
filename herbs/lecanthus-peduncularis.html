Lecanthus peduncularis

What's the taxonomical classification of Lecanthus peduncularis?

Lecanthus peduncularis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the hierarchy of biological classification, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic ranks, the species is organized under the order Rosales and the family Urticaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Lecanthus, and its specific epithet, peduncularis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lecanthus peduncularis has opposite, decussate leaves that are elliptical to oblong in shape, characterized by an entire margin and an obtuse apex. The foliage typically features a smooth, somewhat coriaceous texture and a deep green color on the upper surface. Its stems are woody and can exhibit a square or slightly angular cross-section typical of many members of the Acanthaceae family. The inflorescence is distinguished by its long, slender pedicels which support small, tubular flowers. These flowers are often arranged in dense, terminal spikes or clusters that emerge from the leaf axils.
