White Forsythia, scientifically known as Abeliophyllum distichum, is a versatile medicinal shrub highly valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently used to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and fever. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.
