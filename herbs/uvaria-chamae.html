Uvaria chamae

Uvaria chamae, commonly known as the climber or custard vine, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Uvaria chamae
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Uvaria chamae, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Magnoliales. It is further categorized into the family Annonaceae and the genus Uvaria.

This plant is widely distributed across several nations within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Benin and Burkina.
