Diospyros rhodocalyx

Diospyros rhodocalyx, commonly known as the red-calyx ebony, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Found in tropical regions, its fruits and bark contain bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healthcare applications.
