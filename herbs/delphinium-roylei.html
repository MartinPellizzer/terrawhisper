Delphinium roylei

Delphinium roylei, commonly known as the Royle's Larkspur, is a striking perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its elegant, spurred flowers, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is primarily utilized for its diverse pharmacological properties, including its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial therapeutic effects.
