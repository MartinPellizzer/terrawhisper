Holoptelea integrifolia

Holoptelea integrifolia, commonly known as the smooth-leaved holoptelia, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to South American ecosystems. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this resilient plant offers unique phytochemical properties. Research continues to explore its potential bioactive compounds for pharmacological applications, highlighting its significant role in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Holoptelea integrifolia
  • Family: Ulmaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Ulmaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Holoptelea.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of India and its neighboring territories. Specifically, its presence is documented in the lush environments of Assam and throughout Bangladesh. The species also extends its range into the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya. Additionally, this medicinal plant is known to inhabit the mountainous areas of Nepal.
