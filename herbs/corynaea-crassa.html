Corynaea crassa

Corynaea crassa, commonly known as Palo de Agua, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. This botanical specimen offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Corynaea crassa
  • Family: Balanophoraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Santalales and the family Balanophoraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Corynaea.
