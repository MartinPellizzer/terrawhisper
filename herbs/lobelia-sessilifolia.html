The physiology of Lobelia sessilifolia is characterized by its utilization of the C3 photosynthetic pathway, a common metabolic strategy among most temperate and moist-habitat herbaceous plants. In this pathway, the initial fixation of atmospheric carbon dioxide occurs via the enzyme RuBisCO, which combines CO2 with ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate to form 3-phosphoglycerate. This process occurs within the mesophyll cells, where the plant manages gas exchange through its stomata to balance the intake of carbon for glucose synthesis against the loss of water through transpiration.
