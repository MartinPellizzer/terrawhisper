Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Amaranth, offers therapeutic benefits through various components of its structure. The seed is perhaps the most widely utilized part, serving as a nutrient-dense pseudocereal that is rich in essential amino acids and minerals. Beyond the whole grain, concentrated amaranthus extracts derived from the leaves or the plant's essence are often employed in medicinal formulations to harness their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Together, these parts make the plant a versatile resource in both nutritional science and traditional herbal medicine.
