Thorny weed, scientifically known as Amaranthus, is a versatile plant celebrated in traditional medicine and nutrition. Often dismissed as a common garden pest, its nutrient-rich leaves and seeds offer significant health benefits. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, it plays a vital role in supporting holistic wellness and dietary health.
