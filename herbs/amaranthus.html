Amaranthus

Amaranthus, commonly known as amaranth, is a versatile plant celebrated for its exceptional nutritional profile and medicinal properties. Rich in essential amino acids, antioxidants, and minerals, it serves as both a potent superfood and a therapeutic agent in traditional medicine, offering significant benefits for immunity and overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Amaranthus
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaranthaceae and falls under the genus Amaranthus.
