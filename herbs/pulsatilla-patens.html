What's the taxonomical classification of Pulsatilla patens?

Pulsatilla patens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is part of the order Ranunculales and falls under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Pulsatilla, and its specific species designation, patens.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pulsatilla patens has basal, deeply lobed leaves that are covered in fine silvery hairs and large, bell-shaped violet flowers with prominent yellow stamens. The plant's foliage is divided into multiple narrow segments that create a delicate, feathery appearance near the ground. Each bloom is supported by a slender, pubescent stem that rises from the center of the leafy rosette. As the flowers fade, they are replaced by distinctive, feathery seed heads known as styles that persist on the stalk. These hairy structures serve as an adaptation to help the plant retain moisture and protect itself from temperature fluctuations.

What medicinal compounds this plant contains?

This plant contains protoanemonin, ranunculin, and various glycosides that serve as its primary medicinal components.
