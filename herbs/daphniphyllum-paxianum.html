Daphniphyllum paxianum

Daphniphyllum paxianum, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in certain tropical regions, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic potential, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Scientific interest continues to grow as researchers explore its unique phytochemical properties and bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Daphniphyllum paxianum
  • Family: Daphniphyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Saxifrages, falling into the family Daphniphyllaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Daphniphyllum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Daphniphyllum paxianum, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is commonly called 脉叶虎皮楠 (zh), 脉叶虎皮楠 (zh-hans), or 脉叶虎皮楠 (zh-cn), while in Taiwan, it is specifically written as 脈葉虎皮楠 (zh-tw). Additionally, it is also recognized by the name 海南虎皮楠 (zh).
