Giant milkweed, scientifically known as Calotropis gigantea, is a versatile perennial shrub renowned in traditional medicine. Widely distributed across tropical regions, it features large, waxy flowers and a milky latex. This potent plant is extensively utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties in various holistic healing practices.
