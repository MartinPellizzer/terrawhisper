Datura stramonium

Devil's snare

Datura stramonium, commonly known as Jimsonweed, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. While revered in traditional practices for its sedative and analgesic properties, it contains dangerous tropane alkaloids. Due to its high toxicity, it must be handled with extreme caution and professional medical guidance.

  • Scientific name: Datura stramonium
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Solanaceae. Ultimately, Datura stramonium is identified by its specific genus, Datura.
