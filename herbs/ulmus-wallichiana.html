Ulmus wallichiana

Himalayan elm

Ulmus wallichiana, commonly known as the Himalayan Elm, is a significant medicinal tree native to the mountainous regions of Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic versatility, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues, offering valuable holistic healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Ulmus wallichiana
  • Family: Ulmaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
