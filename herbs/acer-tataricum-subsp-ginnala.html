Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Amur maple, utilizes its leaves as a key component in various traditional medicinal practices. The leaves are characterized by their distinct palmate shape and serrated margins, often undergoing a dramatic color change to vibrant reds and oranges during the autumn season. In botanical and herbal contexts, these leaves are studied for their potential bioactive compounds, contributing to the overall therapeutic profile of the species.
