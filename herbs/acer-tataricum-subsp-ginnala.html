Acer ginnala, scientifically known as Acer tataricum subsp. ginnala, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, its bark and fruits are utilized to support circulatory health and reduce inflammation. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential in various holistic healing practices.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across various regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, it can be found spanning from Germany through Hungary to the area of Czechia-Slovakia. Moving toward the Mediterranean region, its presence extends into Southeastern Europe within Italy. Additionally, the species reaches further north into the Eastern European territories of the Baltic States. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environmental range inhabited by this medicinal species.
