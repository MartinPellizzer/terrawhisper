Heliotropium indicum

Heliotropium indicum, commonly known as Indian Heliotrope, is a versatile medicinal herb widely used in traditional Ayurvedic and folk medicine. Characterized by its small white flowers, this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing effects, making it a vital resource in natural healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Heliotropium indicum
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Heliotropium.
