Rhododendron afghanicum

Rhododendron afghanicum, commonly known as the Afghan rhododendron, is a resilient subalpine shrub native to the high altitudes of Central Asia. Renowned for its striking pink blooms, this hardy species thrives in rocky terrains, offering significant botanical interest and traditional medicinal potential within its unique, mountainous ecological niche.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron afghanicum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales. Finally, it falls under the family Ericaceae and the genus Rhododendron.
