Damiana, scientifically known as Turnera diffusa, is a perennial shrub native to Mexico and Central America. Historically celebrated in traditional medicine, it is primarily utilized as an aphrodisiac and restorative tonic. Today, it remains popular in herbal remedies designed to support emotional well-being and alleviate mild digestive discomfort.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Turnera diffusa and commonly referred to as damiana, derives its therapeutic value primarily from its aerial parts. These above-ground components, which include the stems, leaves, and flowers, contain the essential chemical constituents and volatile oils responsible for its traditional medicinal uses. In many herbal preparations, the dried aerial parts are harvested to create infusions or tinctures, making them the central focus for extracting the plant's bioactive compounds.
