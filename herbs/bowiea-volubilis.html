Climbing onion, scientifically known as Bowiea volubiilis, is a unique succulent climber native to Southern Africa. Characterized by its thick, tuberous roots and delicate, trailing foliage, this plant is prized by collectors. Beyond its ornamental appeal, it holds significant traditional importance in various regional medicinal practices for healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Bowiea volubilis, is characterized as a bulbous plant that stores its essential nutrients and medicinal compounds within its specialized underground structures. The most significant part of the species is its large, fleshy bulbs, which serve as the primary reservoir for the plant's bioactive substances. Because it is a bulbous plant, these bulbs are the main component used in traditional medicinal practices, often requiring careful preparation to harness their therapeutic properties.
