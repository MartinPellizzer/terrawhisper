Crataegus pentagyna

Small-flowered black hawthorn

Crataegus pentagyna, commonly known as the five-leaf hawthorn, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various Asian regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to support cardiovascular health, alleviate digestive issues, and act as a natural tonic to promote overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus pentagyna
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
