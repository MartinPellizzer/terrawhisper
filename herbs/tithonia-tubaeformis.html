Tithonia tubaeformis

Tithonia tubaeformis, commonly known as the Mexican sunflower or tree marigold, is a vibrant medicinal plant native to Mexico. Renowned for its bright orange blooms, it possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing capabilities, making it a valuable resource in traditional herbal medicine and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Tithonia tubaeformis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Tithonia, specifically the species Tithonia tubaeformis.
