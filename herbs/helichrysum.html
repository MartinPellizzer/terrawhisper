Helichrysum

Helichrysum, commonly known as Immortelle or Everlasting, is a remarkable medicinal herb celebrated for its regenerative properties. Renowned for its golden blooms, this plant contains potent antioxidants and anti-inflammatory compounds. It is widely utilized in skincare and aromatherapy to promote healing, soothe skin, and support overall cellular vitality.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Ultimately, it resides in the family Asteraceae under the genus Helichrysum.
