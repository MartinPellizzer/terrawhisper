Combretum aculeatum

Combretum aculeatum, commonly known as the Natal Combretum, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions. This resilient plant offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Combretum aculeatum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic descent places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and order Myrtales, eventually leading to the family Combretaceae. Within this group, it is specifically identified under the genus Combretum.
