Ruellia prostrata

Wild petunia

Ruelliia prostata, commonly known as creeping rue, is a resilient, trailing herbaceous plant native to tropical regions. Characterized by its delicate, serrated leaves and vibrant tubular flowers, it is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, often used to treat inflammation and support digestive health in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Ruellia prostrata
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ruellia prostata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Acanthaceae within the genus Ruellia.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Ruellia prostata, is recognized by various common names across different languages and regions. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted within the Central African Republic. The species also extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Eritrea and Ethiopia. Additionally, it is documented to grow in Somalia. Finally, its range reaches the Northeast Tropical African territories of Sudan and South Sudan.
