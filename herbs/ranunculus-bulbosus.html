Bulbous buttercup

Ranunculus bulbosus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (31 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Bulbous buttercup, scientifically known as Ranunculus bulbosus, is a perennial herbaceous plant characterized by its small, yellow, five-petaled flowers. Native to Europe, it thrives in well-drained soils and grasslands. Historically used in folk medicine, it contains protoanemonin, requiring careful handling due to its potential skin-irritating properties.
