Dioscorea composita

Dioscorea composita, commonly known as the composite yam, is a perennial climbing herb valued in traditional medicine. Native to various subtropical regions, its tubers contain bioactive compounds used to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive issues. This medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential for managing various ailments through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea composita
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Dioscorea composita, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Dioscoreales and the family Dioscoreaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Dioscorea.
