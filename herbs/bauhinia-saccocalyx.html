Bauhinia saccocalyx

Bauhinia saccocalyx, commonly known as the Orchid Tree, is a stunning flowering plant prized for its ornamental beauty and medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in various cultures to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential in holistic herbal medicine.

  • Family: Fabaceae
