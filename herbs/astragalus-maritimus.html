Astragalus maritimus

Astragalus maritimus, commonly known as sea milkvetch, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal environments. Characterized by its distinctive pea-like flowers and ability to thrive in saline soils, this plant plays a vital role in stabilizing dunes. It is increasingly studied for its unique ecological and medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus maritimus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and falls under the genus Astragalus.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Astragalus maritimus, possesses a very specific and limited geographical range. Its natural occurrence is primarily concentrated within the region of Southwestern Europe. Specifically, the species is found inhabiting the unique landscapes of Sardegna. Due to this localized presence, it is considered an endemic feature of that particular Mediterranean island. Understanding this restricted distribution is essential for the conservation and study of the species.
