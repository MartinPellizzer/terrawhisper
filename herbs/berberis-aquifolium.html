Berberis aquifolium

Berberis aquifolium, commonly known as Indian Barberry, is a perennial shrub highly valued in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its bright yellow bark and medicinal berries, it contains potent alkaloids like berberine. It is widely utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties to treat various skin and metabolic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Berberis aquifolium
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and falls under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Berberidaceae, situated within the genus Berberis.

This plant, Berberis aquifolium, is valued in traditional medicine for the various therapeutic properties found within its anatomical structures. Whether used in its whole form or processed as concentrated stem-bark preparations, these components are vital for medicinal efficacy.
