Plant Parts

This plant, Berberis aquifolium, is valued in traditional medicine for the various therapeutic properties found within its anatomical structures. The woody stems provide the structural framework for the shrub, while the thick stem bark serves as a primary source of alkaloids like berberine. Whether used in its whole form or processed as concentrated stem-bark preparations, these components are vital for medicinal efficacy. Additionally, the roots are frequently harvested for their potent bioactive compounds, and the various extracts derived from these different parts are often utilized in pharmacological applications to treat diverse ailments.
