Distribution

This plant, Allophylus africanus, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin. The species is also documented to inhabit the territory of Burkina. Furthermore, its presence extends to the coastal areas of Gambia. Finally, this medicinal plant is distributed throughout Ghana and Guinea-Bissau.
