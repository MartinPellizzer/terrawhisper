Sanguinaria canadensis

Sanguinaria canadensis, commonly known as bloodroot, is a perennial herb native to eastern North America. Characterized by its distinct bright red sap, this plant has a long history in traditional medicine. While used historically for skin conditions and respiratory issues, its potent alkaloids require cautious, expert application.

  • Scientific name: Sanguinaria canadensis
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Sanguinaria canadensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is classified under the family Papaveraceae and the genus Sanguinaria.
