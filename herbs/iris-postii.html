Iris postii

Iris postii, commonly known as Post's Iris, is a rare and enchanting perennial species native to the rugged terrains of Turkey. Renowned for its striking, delicate blooms, this medicinal plant holds significant botanical interest. Research explores its unique phytochemical properties, potentially offering therapeutic benefits within traditional and modern herbal medicine.
