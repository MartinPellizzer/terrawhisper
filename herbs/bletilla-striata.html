Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Bletilla striata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the Orchidaceae family and is situated within the genus Bletilla.

Plant Parts

This plant, Bletilla striata, possesses a specialized underground anatomy essential for its survival and medicinal value. Its structure is characterized by thick, fleshy pseudobulbs that serve as primary storage organs, which are interconnected through a network of creeping rhizomes. These rhizomes facilitate vegetative spread, while the tubers and various fleshy roots allow the plant to store vital nutrients and moisture during dormant periods. Chemically, these parts are highly significant because they are rich in polysaccharide compounds, which are the key bioactive constituents utilized in traditional medicine for their healing properties.
