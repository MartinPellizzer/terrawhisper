Selaginella moellendorffii

Selaginella moellendorffii, commonly known as Moellendorff's spike moss, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its unique phytochemical properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various internal maladies, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Selaginella moellendorffii
  • Family: Selaginellaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Lycopodiidae.
