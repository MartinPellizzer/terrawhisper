Mexican manzaniita, scientifically known as Arctostaphylos pungens, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the coastal regions of Baja California. Characterized by its leathery, pungent leaves and vibrant red berries, this botanical species is valued for its ornamental beauty and potential therapeutic applications within traditional folk medicine practices.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the Southwestern United States. Specifically, its range extends through the states of Arizona, California, Nevada, and Utah. In addition to these western territories, it can also be found in the South-Central region of the country. Its presence is notably documented within the borders of New Mexico.
