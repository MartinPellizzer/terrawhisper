Colombian coca, scientifically known as Erythroxylum novogranatense var. novogranatense, is a distinctive shrub native to the Andean regions. Deeply rooted in indigenous traditions, this botanical variety holds significant ethnobotanical importance. It is primarily valued for its unique phytochemical properties and its historical role in ritualistic and medicinal practices.
