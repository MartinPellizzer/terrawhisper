Tutsan, scientifically known as Hypericum androsaemuon, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean. Belonging to the Hypericaceae family, it is distinguished by its bright yellow, star-shaped flowers. While primarily used in ornamental landscaping, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest for its traditional medicinal properties and unique bioactive compounds.
