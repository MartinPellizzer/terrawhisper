Hypericum androsaemum

Hypericum androsaemum, commonly known as St. John's wort, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Central Asia. Renowned for its vibrant yellow flowers, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various digestive issues through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum androsaemum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Hypericaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Hypericum.
