Boxthorn barberry, scientifically known as Berberis lycium, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the arid regions of North Africa and the Middle East. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant features bright yellow flowers and tart berries, often utilized in traditional remedies to support digestive and inflammatory health.
