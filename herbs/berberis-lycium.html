Berberis lycium

Berberis lycium, commonly known as the Lycium Barberry, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the arid regions of the Middle East. Renowned for its bright yellow flowers and edible berries, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Berberis lycium
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
