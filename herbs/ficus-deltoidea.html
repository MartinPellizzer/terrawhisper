Ficus deltoidea

Ficus deltoidea, commonly known as the Delta Fig, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healthcare applications worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Ficus deltoidea
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Moraceae and the genus Ficus. As a member of the Ficus genus, Ficus deltoidea is identified by its specific taxonomic placement within these hierarchical groups.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions of Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is specifically noted in Thailand. It also has an extensive distribution throughout the Malesia zone, including the island of Borneo. Furthermore, its habitat extends to Jawa and the Malay Peninsula. Finally, the species can be found reaching as far as the Maluku islands.
