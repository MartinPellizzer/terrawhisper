Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions of Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is specifically noted in Thailand. It also has an extensive distribution throughout the Malesia zone, including the island of Borneo. Furthermore, its habitat extends to Jawa and the Malay Peninsula. Finally, the species can be found reaching as far as the Maluku islands.

The reproduction of Ficus deltoidea is characterized by a biotic pollination syndrome, primarily relying on insects to facilitate the transfer of pollen. This insect-mediated process is central to its reproductive cycle, ensuring the successful fertilization required for fruit development.
