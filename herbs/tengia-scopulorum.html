Tengia scopulorum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is situated in the order Lamiales and the family Gesneriaceae. Ultimately, its specific biological identity is defined by its membership in the genus Tengia, with its unique species designation being scopulorum.
