Phlomis betonicoides belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant follows the biological lineage of the order Lamiales. It is further categorized under the family Lamiaceae, a group known for its diverse herbal characteristics. Within this family, it is assigned to the genus Phlomis, and its specific identity is defined by its species name, betonicoides.
