Himalayan Marsh Orchid, scientifically known as Dactylorhiza hatagirea, is a delicate perennial herb native to high-altitude alpine meadows. Renowned for its striking blooms and unique tuberous roots, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Traditionally used in folk medicine, this rare orchid serves as a vital indicator of Himalayan biodiversity.
