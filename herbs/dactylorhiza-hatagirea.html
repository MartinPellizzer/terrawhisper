Dactylorhiza hatagirea

Himalayan marsh orchid

Dactylorhiza hatagirea, commonly known as the Japanese orchid, is a delicate perennial species native to East Asia. Valued in traditional herbal practices, this medicinal plant is recognized for its unique rhizomes. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to explore potential therapeutic applications in supporting holistic health and wellness.

  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
