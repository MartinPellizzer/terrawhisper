Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. Within the vast expanse of Siberia, it can be found in both Buryatiya and throughout West Siberia. Its range extends further east into the Russian Far East, specifically within the Primorye region. Additionally, the species is present in Central Asia, covering parts of Kazakhstan. Finally, its distribution encompasses the mountainous landscapes of Kirgizstan.
