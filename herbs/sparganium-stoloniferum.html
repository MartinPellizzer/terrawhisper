Sparganium stoloniferum

Sparganium stoloniferum, commonly known as stoloniferous spargani, is a unique medicinal plant valued for its bioactive properties. Characterized by its creeping stems, this species is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address various ailments. Research continues to explore its potential pharmacological benefits and its role in supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Sparganium stoloniferum
  • Family: Typhaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Poales, it is situated in the family Typhaceae. Its scientific classification is completed by its assignment to the genus Sparganium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. Within the vast expanse of Siberia, it can be found in both Buryatiya and throughout West Siberia. Its range extends further east into the Russian Far East, specifically within the Primorye region. Additionally, the species is present in Central Asia, covering parts of Kazakhstan. Finally, its distribution encompasses the mountainous landscapes of Kirgizstan.
