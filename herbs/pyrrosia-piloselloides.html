Pyrrosia piloselloides

Pyrrosia piloselloides, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, is a specialized plant valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Found in specific ecological niches, it offers significant therapeutic potential in traditional healing. Understanding its bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its future applications in modern pharmacological research and natural medicine.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, falling into the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Pyrrosiia.
