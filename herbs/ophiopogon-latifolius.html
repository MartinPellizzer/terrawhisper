Ophiopogon latifolius

What's the taxonomical classification of Ophiopogon latifolius?

Ophiopogon latifolius belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant falls under the order Asparagales and is a member of the family Asparagaceae. Ultimately, it is placed within the genus Ophiopogon, with its specific designation being the species latifolius.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ophiopogon latifolius has linear, strap-shaped leaves that are typically dark green, relatively broad compared to other species in its genus, and emerge from a rhizomatous root system. The plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that are usually white or pale blue and emerge from the leaf axils. Following pollination, it develops small, fleshy berries that transition from green to a deep black or dark blue color when mature.
