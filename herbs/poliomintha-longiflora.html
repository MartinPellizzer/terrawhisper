Mexican Oregano, scientifically known as Poliomintha longiflora, is a fragrant perennial herb native to Mexico. Unlike true oregano, it offers a distinct, earthy aroma and pungent flavor. Highly valued in culinary traditions, this versatile plant also boasts significant medicinal properties, often used for its soothing antimicrobial and digestive benefits.
