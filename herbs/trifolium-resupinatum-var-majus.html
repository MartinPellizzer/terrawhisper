Trifolium resupinatum var. majus

Trifolium resupinatum var. majus, commonly known as the creeping clover, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its restorative properties, this variety is often utilized to support digestive health and soothe inflammation. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a significant subject for ethnobotanical study and wellness.
