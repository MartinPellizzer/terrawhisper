Dipsacus fullonum, scientifically known as Dipsacus fullonum, is a distinctive biennial herb renowned for its striking, architectural appearance. Often called Teasel, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine. Its prickly seed heads and robust stems are studied for their unique bioactive properties and potential therapeutic applications.
