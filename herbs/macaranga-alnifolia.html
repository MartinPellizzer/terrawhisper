Macaranga alnifolia

Macaranga alnifolia, commonly known as the Alder-leaf Macaranga, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This botanical species offers significant potential for pharmacological research, particularly in developing natural healing treatments.
