Parapiptadenia rigida

Parapiptadenia rigida, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds profound ethnobotanical value. Belonging to the Rubiaceae family, this woody shrub is prized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Traditional healers utilize its various parts to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions and digestive issues.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Parapiptadenia rigida, is referred to by various names depending on the region and language.
