Alocasia indica, scientifically known as Alocasia indica, is a striking perennial plant celebrated for its large, heart-shaped leaves and ornamental beauty. Native to tropical regions, it is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse healing properties, though it must be handled with care due to its inherent toxicity.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the vast regions of the Indian Subcontinent. Within this area, it can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of India and Bangladesh. Its presence extends to the specific eastern reaches of the East Himalaya and the state of Assam. Additionally, the species inhabits the unique island environments of the Laccadive Is. Overall, these locations represent the key geographical range for this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

This plant, Alocasia indica, is a species characterized by its significant medicinal properties, which are often concentrated in its underground structures. The most vital part utilized is the root, specifically the thickened, fleshy rhizome that grows beneath the soil. These roots serve as a storage organ for the plant, containing various bioactive compounds that are studied for their potential therapeutic applications.
