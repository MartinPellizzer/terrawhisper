Astragalus ernestii

Astragalus erneesti, commonly known as Huang Qi in Traditional Chinese Medicine, is a highly valued medicinal herb. Renowned for its potent immunomodulatory and antioxidant properties, it is frequently used to enhance vitality, support respiratory health, and boost the immune system, making it a cornerstone in various restorative herbal formulations.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus ernestii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Falling under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. It is specifically classified under the genus Astragalus, with its species designation being Astragalus erneesti.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Astragalus erneustii, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is frequently called 梭果黄耆 or 棱果黄蓍, and it is also commonly known as 沙苑子. Under simplified Chinese scripts, it is documented as 梭果黄芪, zh-hans: 梭果黄芪, and zh-cn: 梭果黄芪.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions within China. It can be found growing throughout the South-Central areas of the country. Additionally, its presence extends into the North-Central provinces of China. The species also inhabits the high-altitude landscapes of the Qinghai province. Finally, its geographical range includes the vast territories of Tibet.
