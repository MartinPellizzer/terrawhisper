Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Houseleek or Sempervivum tectorum, is a succulent characterized by its compact rosette formation. The most prominent feature of the plant is its thick, fleshy leaves, which are designed to store moisture and provide resilience in rocky, arid environments. Within these specialized tissues, the plant produces a viscous sap that serves as a vital reservoir for nutrients and water, contributing to its medicinal reputation for soothing various topical ailments.
