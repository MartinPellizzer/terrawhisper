Morinda cochinchinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its evolutionary progression continues through the order Gentianaales, placing it within the family Rubiaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific taxonomic identity as a member of the genus Morinda, with the distinct species name cochinchiensis.
