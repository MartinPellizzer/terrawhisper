Dendrocalamus asper

Rough asper

Dendrocalamus asper, commonly known as the rough-stemmed reed, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Traditionally used in various herbal remedies, it possesses unique bioactive compounds believed to offer anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for exploring its potential in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Dendrocalamus asper
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, placing it in the family Poaceae. Common Names

This plant, Dendrocalamus asper, is known by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In China, it is referred to as 马来甜龙竹 (zh), while in Indonesia, it is commonly called Betung (id). In Malaysia, the species is known as Buluh betong (ms), and among the Sundanese people, it is identified as Awi bitung (su). Additionally, in the Banjar language, it is referred to as Tiying petung (ban).
