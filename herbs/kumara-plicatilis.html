Kumara plicatilis

Kumara plicatiis, commonly known as the pleated kumara, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique structural characteristics, this species offers potent bioactive compounds. It serves as a vital natural resource, providing therapeutic benefits used to support wellness and treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Kumara plicatilis
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Kumara plicatilis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asphodelaceae and is situated within the genus Kumara.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Kumara plicatilis, exhibits a specific and limited geographical range. Its natural habitat is primarily concentrated within the Southern Africa region. More specifically, the species is indigenous to the Cape Provinces. Within these areas, it thrives in particular environmental conditions suitable for its survival. Consequently, its distribution is closely tied to the unique landscapes found in these southern territories.
