Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is well-documented throughout Austria and Germany. It can also be found extending across the territories of Czechia and Slovakia. Additionally, the species inhabits the mountainous landscapes of Switzerland. Finally, its range reaches into Southwestern Europe, specifically on the island of Corse.
