Stipa grandis

What's the taxonomical classification of Stipa grandis?

Stipa grandis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is positioned under the order Poales. It is a part of the family Poaceae, falling under the genus Stipa, and is specifically identified by the species name grandis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Stipa grandis has long, slender, linear leaves that are typically involute and a prominent inflorescence composed of several large, spreading panicles. The stems are upright and can reach significant heights, supporting airy clusters of spikelets. Each spikelet is characterized by a long, twisted awn that aids in seed dispersal through wind and animal contact. The blades of the leaves are often stiff and may exhibit a bluish-green hue depending on the environment. These structural features collectively allow the plant to thrive in open, grassy habitats.
