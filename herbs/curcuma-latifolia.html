Curcuma latifolia

Curcumma latifolia, commonly known as Broad-leaved Turmeric, is a vital medicinal herb celebrated in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, this plant serves as a natural remedy for various ailments, offering therapeutic benefits that support digestive health, skin vitality, and overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Curcuma latifolia
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Curcuma latifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Curcuma.
