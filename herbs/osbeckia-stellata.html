Osbeckia stellata

Osbeckia stellata, commonly known as the star-shaped osbeckia, is a captivating medicinal shrub prized for its vibrant blooms and therapeutic potential. Found in diverse tropical habitats, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Osbeckia stellata
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it is further categorized under the family Melastomataceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Osbeckia, specifically the species Osbeckia stellata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central and Southeast territories, as well as in the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its range also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically appearing in the Assam region. Furthermore, it is documented to inhabit the East Himalaya zone. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environmental settings where the species thrives.
