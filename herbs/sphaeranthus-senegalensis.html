According to a study published by "Journal of ethnopharmacology", the effects of the aqueous extract of Sphaeranthus senegalensis Vaill. were studied on spontaneous motor activity, exploratory behaviour, rota-rod performance and pentobarbital sleeping time in mice. The extract at doses of 50 and 100 mg/kg p.o. produced a reduction in spontaneous motor activity, exploratory behaviour and motor coordination, while also prolonging pentobarbital sleeping time. Phytochemical evaluation showed that glycosides, saponins and tannins were present in the extract. The i.p. LD(50) in mice was 2735.61 and the oral LD(50) was 5000 mg/kg. These results suggest that the aqueous extract of S. senegalensis contains active principles that may be sedative in nature.
