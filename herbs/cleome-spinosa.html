Cleome spinosa

Cleome spinosa, commonly known as Spider Flower, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Characterized by its delicate blooms and spiny stems, it possesses diverse bioactive properties. Often utilized to treat skin ailments and inflammation, this plant offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic applications and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cleome spinosa
  • Family: Cleomaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Cleome spinosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Brassicales. It is further classified within the family Cleomaceae and the genus Cleome.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across the tropical regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented within Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, the species can be found in Cameroon and Gabon. It also inhabits the vast territories of the Congo and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Consequently, its range is concentrated primarily within these central and western tropical zones.
