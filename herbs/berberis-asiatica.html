Berberis asiatica

Berberis asiatica, commonly known as Himalayan Barberry, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots and stems contain potent alkaloids like berberine, offering powerful anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and digestive benefits essential for treating various metabolic and skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Berberis asiatica
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Berberidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Berberis.

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found specifically within the high-altitude areas of Tibet.
