Prunella vulgaris subsp. asiatica

Prunella vulgaris subsp. asiatica, commonly known as Asian Self-heal, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is frequently used to treat respiratory ailments, skin irritations, and minor wounds, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Prunella vulgaris subsp. asiatica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Prunella. Specifically, this medicinal species is identified as Prunella vulgaris subsp. asiatica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution localized within the Russian Far East. Its presence is documented across several key administrative and territorial regions of this area. Specifically, it can be found in the Amur and Khabarovsk regions. The species also extends its range to the Kamchatka Peninsula and the Kuril Islands. Furthermore, its habitat includes the coastal territory of Primorye.
