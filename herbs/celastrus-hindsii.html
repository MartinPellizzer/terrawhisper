Celastrus hindsii

Celastrus hindsii, commonly known as a valuable medicinal shrub in various regional traditions, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering a rich natural resource for pharmacological research and holistic healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Celastrus hindsii
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Celastrales, falling into the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Celastrus and the specific species Celastrus hindsii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across East Asia. Within China, its presence is documented across the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan territories. It also extends into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Beyond the mainland, the species can be found in the Ogasawara-shoto islands of Eastern Asia. Together, these locations define the specific environmental niches where the species thrives.
