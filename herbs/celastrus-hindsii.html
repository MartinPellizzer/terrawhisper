Chinese bittersweet, scientifically known as Celastrus hindsii, is a climbing woody vine native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant contains bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. It is particularly valued for its potential neuroprotective properties and its ability to enhance cognitive function and memory.
