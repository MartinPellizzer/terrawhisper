Hygrophila difformis

Hygrophila difformis, commonly known as Water Wisteria, is a versatile aquatic plant valued in both aquaria and traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its rapid growth and ability to absorb excess nutrients, it serves as a natural water purifier, offering potential medicinal benefits through its unique bioactive chemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Hygrophila difformis
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Hygrophila, specifically as the species Hygrophila difformis.
