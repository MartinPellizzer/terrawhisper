Baru tree, scientifically known as Dipteryx alata, is a majestic legume native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its nutrient-dense seeds, it offers significant medicinal potential, particularly for treating skin ailments and inflammation. This resilient species plays a vital role in ecological conservation and local traditional healing practices.
