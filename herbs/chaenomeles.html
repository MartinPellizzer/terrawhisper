Flowering quince, scientifically known as Chaenomelos, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health and alleviate respiratory ailments. Its nutrient-rich fruits and bark offer therapeutic benefits, making it a valuable botanical resource in herbal medicine.
