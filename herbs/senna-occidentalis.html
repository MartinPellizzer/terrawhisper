Coffee senna

Senna occidentalis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (498 studies) · ★★★★★

Coffee senna, scientifically known as Senna occidentalis, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its potent purgative properties, its leaves and pods are frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat constipation and digestive ailments, offering a natural, bioactive approach to gastrointestinal health and wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the African continent and its surrounding islands. Within Northern Africa, its presence is documented in both Egypt and Libya. The species also extends into the Macaronesian archipelago, specifically inhabiting the Canary Islands and Cape Verde. Furthermore, its range reaches into West Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Benin. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various coastal and tropical environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, Senna occidentalis, is a versatile medicinal species where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaves are perhaps the most frequently studied part due to their significant bioactive compounds, while the roots are often employed in traditional remedies to treat various ailments. Furthermore, the stem serves as a structural foundation for the plant's growth, supporting the development of its reproductive organs. The plant produces both individual seeds and larger quantities of seeds that are vital for its lifecycle and are sometimes utilized in specific traditional formulations.
