Coffee senna

Senna occidentalis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (498 studies) · ★★★★★

Coffee senna, scientifically known as Senna occidentalis, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its potent purgative properties, its leaves and pods are frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat constipation and digestive ailments, offering a natural, bioactive approach to gastrointestinal health and wellness.
