Senna occidentalis

Coffee senna

Sennas occidentalis, commonly known as West African Senna, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent purgative properties, it contains bioactive anthraquinones used to treat constipation. Beyond digestive health, traditional healers utilize this plant to manage skin ailments and various inflammatory conditions effectively.

  • Scientific name: Senna occidentalis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Senna.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the African continent and its surrounding islands. Within Northern Africa, its presence is documented in both Egypt and Libya. The species also extends into the Macaronesian archipelago, specifically inhabiting the Canary Islands and Cape Verde. Furthermore, its range reaches into West Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Benin. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various coastal and tropical environments.
