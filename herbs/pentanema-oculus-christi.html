Pentanema oculus-christi

Pentanema oculus-christi, commonly known as the "Eye of Christ," is a rare medicinal plant revered for its potent healing properties. Deeply embedded in traditional herbalism, this botanical specimen is prized for its unique bioactive compounds, which are believed to support ocular health and provide significant anti-inflammatory benefits.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
