What's the taxonomical classification of Saxifraga divaricata?

Saxifraga divaricata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Saxifragales and is a member of the family Saxifragaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Saxifraga, with its unique identity defined by the species epithet divaricata.
