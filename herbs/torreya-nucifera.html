Torreya nucifera

Torreya nucifera, commonly known as the Japanese Nutmeg-yew, is a rare coniferous evergreen native to East Asian forests. Highly valued for its edible seeds and medicinal properties, it contains unique bioactive compounds. This ancient species plays a vital role in traditional herbal medicine and offers significant ecological importance.
