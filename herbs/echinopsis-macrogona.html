Echinopsis macrogona

Echinopsis macrogona, commonly known as the macrogon cactus, is a striking succulent prized for its impressive floral displays. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this species holds significance in ethnobotany. Researchers explore its unique chemical compounds for potential medicinal properties, investigating its bioactive potential in supporting various traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Echinopsis macrogona
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and the family Cactaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Echinopsis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide and diverse geographical distribution spanning several different continents. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found specifically across Spain and the Baleares islands. Its presence extends into the Macaronesian region, where it grows among the Canary Islands. Moving across the Atlantic, the species is also located in Western South America. Within this South American range, its habitat includes the nations of both Bolivia and Colombia.
