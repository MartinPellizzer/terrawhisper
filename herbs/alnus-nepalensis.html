Nepalese alder

Alnus nepalensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (34 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Nepalese alder, scientifically known as Alnus nepalensis, is a versatile deciduous tree native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its ecological significance, it facilitates nitrogen fixation in soil. Medicinally, various parts are utilized in traditional practices to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues and inflammation.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central areas as well as in the Tibet region. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Additionally, this species is documented to grow in Bangladesh. Finally, it is widely distributed throughout the East Himalaya region.
