Alnus nepalensis

Nepalese alder

Alnus nepellenis, commonly known as the Nepalese Alder, is a resilient deciduous tree native to the Himalayan foothills. Renowned for its ability to enrich soil through nitrogen fixation, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various parts utilized in traditional practices to treat ailments and promote holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Alnus nepalensis
  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales and the family Betulaceae, it is classified under the genus Alnus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Alnus nepalensis, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central areas as well as in the Tibet region. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Additionally, this species is documented to grow in Bangladesh. Finally, it is widely distributed throughout the East Himalaya region.
