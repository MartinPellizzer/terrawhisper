Nepalese alder, scientifically known as Alnus nepalensis, is a versatile deciduous tree native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its ecological significance, it facilitates nitrogen fixation in soil. Medicinally, various parts are utilized in traditional practices to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues and inflammation.
