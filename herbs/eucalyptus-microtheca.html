Bastard-box, scientifically known as Eucalyptus microtheca, is a resilient Australian tree renowned for its medicinal properties. Its aromatic leaves contain essential oils with potent antimicrobial and antiseptic qualities. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, this hardy species remains a vital resource in natural healing and pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as the Coolibah, derives much of its medicinal value primarily from its leaves. The essential oils contained within these leaves are highly aromatic and are frequently harvested for their potent antiseptic and decongestant properties. When processed, these leaf extracts are commonly used in therapeutic applications to alleviate respiratory issues and provide relief from congestion.
