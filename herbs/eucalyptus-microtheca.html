Eucalyptus microtheca

Eucalyptus microtheca, commonly known as the Coolibah, is a resilient Australian tree prized for its medicinal properties. Its aromatic leaves contain essential oils with potent antimicrobial and decongestant qualities. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and skin irritations, this versatile species remains a vital component of natural herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus microtheca
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eucalyptus, specifically as the species Eucalyptus microtheca.
