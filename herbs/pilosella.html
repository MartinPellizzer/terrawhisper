, scientifically known as Pilosella, is a versatile herb often referred to as jewelweed or mouse-ear chickweed. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it possesses potent diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties. This gentle botanical is frequently utilized to support kidney health, soothe skin irritations, and promote overall urinary tract wellness.
