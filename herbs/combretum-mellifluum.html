Combretum mellifluum

Combretum mellifluum, commonly known as the Honeybush, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its nectar-rich flowers, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to respiratory infections, making it a vital resource in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Combretum mellifluum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, eventually being placed in the family Combretaceae. Within this lineage, it is specifically identified as a member of the genus Combretum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Combretum mellifluum, is recognized by several different common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is most frequently referred to as the honeybush. In Afrikaans, it is commonly called heuningbos, while in Portuguese, it is known as carapaçú.
