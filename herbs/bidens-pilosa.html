Bidens pilosa

Beggar's tick

Bidens pilosa, commonly known as black-jack or Spanish needle, is a versatile medicinal herb widely used in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and antioxidant properties, this resilient plant is frequently employed to treat wounds, skin infections, and various digestive ailments across diverse global cultures.

  • Scientific name: Bidens pilosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Bidens, which includes the species Bidens pilosa.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Bidens pilosa, is referred to by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In Quechua, it is called P'irqa, while in Vietnamese, it is known as Xuuyến chi. It is identified in German as Behaarter Zweizahn, and in Nepali, it is called कालोकुरो. Additionally, in Japanese, the plant is commonly known as コセンダングサ.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several European regions. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, its presence is noted in Belgium, Germany, and Poland. Furthermore, the species extends into Southwestern Europe, specifically inhabiting the Baleares. This wide range demonstrates the plant's ability to adapt to various European climates.

Plant Parts

This plant, Bidens pilosa, is a versatile medicinal herb where almost every component offers therapeutic potential. When preparing traditional remedies, practitioners often utilize the whole plant to capture its full spectrum of bioactive compounds. The aerial parts, which include everything above the ground, are commonly harvested for decoctions and infusions. Within these upper structures, the stems provide structural support and contain essential phytochemicals, while the leaves serve as the primary site for nutrient production and medicinal potency. Whether processed as a dried leaf or used in its entirety, the various components of Bidens pilosa contribute significantly to its recognized healing properties.
