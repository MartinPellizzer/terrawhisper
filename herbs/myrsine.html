Myrsine

Myrsine, commonly known as myrtle or myrsine, is a genus of evergreen shrubs and trees within the Primulaceae family. Widely distributed across tropical and temperate regions, these resilient plants are valued for their diverse phytochemical profiles, offering significant potential in pharmacological research for their antioxidant and antimicrobial properties.
