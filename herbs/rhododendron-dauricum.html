Rhododendron dauricum

Rhododendron dauricum, commonly known as the Daurian rhododendron, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude regions of East Asia. Renowned for its striking floral displays, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its unique bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic benefits in various herbal formulations.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron dauricum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is a member of the Ericaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Rhododendron as the species Rhododendron dauricum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the various regions of Siberia. It can be found growing in the Altai mountain range, which forms part of its natural habitat. The species is also documented across the Republic of Buryatia, extending its presence into the eastern territories. Furthermore, its range includes the areas surrounding Chita and the Irkutsk region. Finally, the plant completes its Siberian distribution within the Krasnoyarsk territory.
