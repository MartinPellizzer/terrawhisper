What's the taxonomical classification of Rubus trianthus?

Rubus trianthus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaecae. At the most specific levels of its biological classification, it is identified by the genus Rubus and the species trianthus.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rubus trianthus has erect, unbranched stems that grow up to 1.5 meters tall and are characterized by a lack of thorns or prickles. The leaves are compound and typically consist of three to five leaflets with serrated margins and a slightly hairy surface. These leaflets are dark green on the upper side and often feature a paler, pubescent underside. The plant produces small, white, five-petaled flowers that emerge in clusters from the terminal ends of the stems. Following pollination, the flowers develop into small, aggregate drupelets that turn from green to a deep reddish-black when mature.
