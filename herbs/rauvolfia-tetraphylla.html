Devil-pepper, scientifically known as Rauvolfia tetraphyllla, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent alkaloids, particularly reserpine, this plant has been traditionally utilized to manage hypertension and mental disturbances. Its pharmacological properties make it a vital subject in modern ethnobotanical and therapeutic research.
