Erythroxylum

Erythroxylum, commonly known as the coca plant, is a genus of shrubs native to South America. Historically revered by indigenous cultures for its medicinal and ritualistic properties, it contains alkaloids with profound physiological effects. Today, it remains a subject of significant botanical study and complex legal regulation worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Erythroxylum
  • Family: Erythroxylaceae
  • Native range: Africa
