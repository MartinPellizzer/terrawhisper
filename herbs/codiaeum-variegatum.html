Codiaeum variegatum

Garden croton

Codiaeum variegatum, commonly known as Croton, is a striking ornamental plant celebrated for its vibrant, multicolored foliage. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this species holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its leaves are utilized for their various bioactive properties to treat ailments ranging from skin issues to digestive discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Codiaeum variegatum
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
