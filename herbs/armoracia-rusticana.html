Horseradish, scientifically known as Armoracia rusticana, is a pungent perennial herb celebrated for its powerful medicinal properties. Renowned for its intense, spicy flavor, this root serves as a potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory agent. It is traditionally utilized to support digestive health, clear respiratory congestion, and boost the immune system.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as horseradish, is primarily valued for its pungent and therapeutic properties found within the root. The underground storage organ contains high concentrations of glucosinolates, which react to produce the sharp, sinus-clearing mustard oils characteristic of the species. In traditional medicine, this specific part is harvested and processed to utilize its potent antimicrobial and digestive benefits.
