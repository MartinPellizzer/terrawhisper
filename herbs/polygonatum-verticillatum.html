Whirled Solomon's seal, scientifically known as Polygonatum verticillatum, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Characterized by its whorled leaves and bell-shaped flowers, this plant is valued for its tonic properties. It is frequently used to nourish Yin, promote fluid production, and support overall vitality and wellness.
