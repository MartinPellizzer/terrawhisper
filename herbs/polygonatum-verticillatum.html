Polygonatum verticillatum

Polygonatum verticillatum, commonly known as Solomon's Seal, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Featuring elegant, bell-shaped flowers and arching stems, it is widely utilized in East Asian practices to nourish Yin, moisten lungs, and alleviate dryness. This versatile plant remains a cornerstone of herbal wellness and botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Polygonatum verticillatum
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Polygonatum.
