Air plant, scientifically known as Kalanchoe pinnata, is a versatile succulent renowned for its remarkable healing properties. Often called the "miracle leaf," it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat wounds, inflammation, and respiratory issues. Its thick, fleshy leaves serve as a natural remedy across various global cultures.
