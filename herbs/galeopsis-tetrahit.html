Galeopsis tetrahit, scientifically known as Galeopsis tetrahit, is a perennial herb within the Lamiaceae family, commonly referred to as yellow dead-nettle. Renowned for its historical medicinal uses, this plant possesses notable anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, traditionally employed to support digestive health and alleviate various systemic ailments in herbalism.
