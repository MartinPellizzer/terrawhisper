Galeopsis tetrahit, scientifically known as Galeopsis tetrahit, is a perennial herb within the Lamiaceae family, commonly referred to as yellow dead-nettle. Renowned for its historical medicinal uses, this plant possesses notable anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, traditionally employed to support digestive health and alleviate various systemic ailments in herbalism.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Galeopsis tetrahit or common hedge nettle, utilizes its leaves as a primary component in its medicinal profile. The leaves are characterized by their opposite arrangement on the stem and a serrated margin, typically featuring a rough, hairy texture that distinguishes them from other members of the Lamiaceae family. In herbal medicine, these leaves are harvested and processed to utilize their specific chemical constituents, which are believed to contribute to the plant's traditional uses in supporting nervous system health and alleviating certain inflammatory conditions.
