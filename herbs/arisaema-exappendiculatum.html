What's the taxonomical classification of Arisaema exappendiculatum?

Arisaema exappendiculatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Alismatales, this plant is a member of the family Araceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Arisaema, with its specific designation being the species exappendiculatum.
