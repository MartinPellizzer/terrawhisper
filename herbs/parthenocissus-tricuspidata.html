Parthenocissus tricuspidata

Japanese creeper

Parthenocissus tricuspita, commonly known as three-leaf ivy, is a climbing woody vine prized for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments and skin inflammations, this resilient plant thrives in diverse habitats, offering unique bioactive compounds for contemporary pharmacological research and studies.

  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Europe
