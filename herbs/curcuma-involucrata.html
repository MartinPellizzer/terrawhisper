Distribution

This plant is naturally distributed across several key regions within Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted in the South-Central and Southeast areas. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in Assam. The species also has a significant range within Bangladesh. Finally, its geographical reach extends into the East Himalaya region of the Indian Subcontinent.

Plant Parts

This plant, Curcuma involucrata, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties which are concentrated within its underground rhizome. This thickened, fleshy stem structure serves as the plant's storage organ, containing a dense concentration of bioactive compounds and essential oils. In traditional herbal medicine, the harvested rhizome is often dried and processed into powders or extracts to treat various ailments, making it the most significant part of the plant for therapeutic applications.
