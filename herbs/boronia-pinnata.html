Boronia pinnata

Boronia pinnata, commonly known as the Pinna Boronia, is a distinctive shrub native to the coastal regions of Western Australia. Characterized by its delicate, pinnate leaves and vibrant pink flowers, this species belongs to the Rutaceae family, offering unique botanical beauty and significant interest for collectors and ecologists alike.
