Chaenactis douglasii

Chaenactis douglasii, commonly known as Douglas's plumehead, is a resilient perennial herb native to Western North America. Distinguished by its unique, brush-like flower heads, this plant holds historical significance in traditional ethnobotany. Its hardy nature allows it to thrive in diverse habitats, offering intriguing potential for pharmacological study.

  • Scientific name: Chaenactis douglasii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Chaenactis.
