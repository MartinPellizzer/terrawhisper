Conocarpus lancifolius

Conocarpus lancifolius, commonly known as the lancet-leaf Conocarpus, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This resilient species offers valuable phytochemical compounds that continue to attract scientific pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Conocarpus lancifolius
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Conocarpus lancifolius, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the family Combretaceae and falls under the genus Conocarpus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the arid regions of Northeast Tropical Africa and the Arabian Peninsula. In the Horn of Africa, it can be found growing in the territory of Eritrea. Its presence extends further south into the coastal and inland areas of Somalia. Additionally, the species is documented within the unique landscapes of the Arabian Peninsula, specifically in Yemen. Together, these locations define the primary natural range for Conocarpus lancifolius.
