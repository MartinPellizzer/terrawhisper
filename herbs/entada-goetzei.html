Entada goetzei

Entada goetzei, commonly known as the sea bean or giant woody climber, is a remarkable tropical legume. Renowned for its massive, buoyant seeds and robust climbing vines, this plant plays a vital role in coastal ecosystems. It is increasingly studied for its potential medicinal properties and unique botanical characteristics.

  • Scientific name: Entada goetzei
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa
