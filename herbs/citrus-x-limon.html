Citrus limon, scientifically known as Citrus × limon, is a versatile citrus fruit celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Rich in vitamin C and bioactive flavonoids, it offers powerful antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory benefits. Widely used in traditional remedies, this bright fruit supports immune health and aids digestive wellness.
