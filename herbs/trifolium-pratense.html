red clover

Trifolium pratense · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1245 studies) · ★★★★★

Red clover, scientifically known as Trifolium pratense, is a perennial legume renowned for its medicinal properties. Rich in isoflavones, it is frequently used to alleviate menopausal symptoms and support hormonal balance. This versatile plant also offers antioxidant benefits, making it a valued staple in traditional and modern herbal medicine.
