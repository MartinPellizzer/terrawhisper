Actinidia eriantha

Actinidia eriantha, commonly known as the hairy kiwi, is a deciduous climbing vine native to East Asia. Renowned for its fuzzy, edible fruits, this medicinal plant offers significant health benefits. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, it is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support digestion and enhance overall immunity.

  • Scientific name: Actinidia eriantha
  • Family: Actinidiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
