Musa acuminata

Musa acuminata, commonly known as the wild banana, is a versatile tropical plant valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Beyond its edible fruit, its leaves, stems, and roots are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Musa acuminata
  • Family: Musaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales. Finally, it is a member of the family Musaceae and falls under the genus Musa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several continents and regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence can be noted specifically within Spain. It also inhabits the Macaronesia region, particularly across the Canary Islands. Moving to West Tropical Africa, the species is found in both Guinea and Senegal. Finally, its distribution extends to East Tropical Africa within the borders of Tanzania.
