Plant Parts

This plant, Musa acuminata, offers various components that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The thick, fleshy pseudostem can be processed to extract juices that are often used to treat inflammation or digestive issues. The peel of the fruit is frequently applied topically to soothe skin irritations and minor wounds. Additionally, the large, green leaves are sometimes used in traditional practices to help reduce swelling or as a protective wrap for medicinal poultices. Finally, even the fruit peels themselves are valued for their antioxidant content and can be used to aid in wound healing or to manage skin conditions.
