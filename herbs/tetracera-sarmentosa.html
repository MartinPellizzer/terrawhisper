Sandpaper Vine

Tetracera sarmentosa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (31 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Sandpaper Vine, scientifically known as Tetracera sarmentoosa, is a climbing shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its rough, sandpaper-like leaves, this resilient plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, offering natural therapeutic benefits.
