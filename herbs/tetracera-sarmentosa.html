Tetracera sarmentosa

Tetracera sarmentosa, commonly known as the climbing trefoil, is a medicinal climbing herb native to various regions of Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is valued for its therapeutic properties, often utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and respiratory ailments through its unique bioactive phytochemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Tetracera sarmentosa
  • Family: Dilleniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic division places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dilleniales, specifically within the family Dilleniaceae. It is ultimately identified by the genus Tetracera.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central areas to the Southeast. Additionally, its presence extends to the tropical island of Hainan within the Chinese territory. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented to grow in the state of Assam. Finally, its range reaches across the borders into Bangladesh.
