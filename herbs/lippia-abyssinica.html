Koseret, scientifically known as Lippia abyssinica, is a highly aromatic perennial shrub native to the Ethiopian highlands. Renowned for its distinctive citrus-like fragrance, this medicinal plant is widely used in traditional medicine and culinary practices to enhance flavor, aid digestion, and treat various ailments through its potent essential oils.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several regions of the African continent. The species also inhabits the Central African Republic within this same West-Central zone. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, its range extends into Northeast Tropical Africa.
