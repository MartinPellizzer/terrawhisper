Lippia abyssinica

Lippia abyssinica, commonly known as a potent medicinal shrub, is an indigenous plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to respiratory infections, showcasing its significant importance in ethnomedicine.

  • Scientific name: Lippia abyssinica
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Verbenaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lippia, specifically as the species Lippia abyssinica.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lippia abyssinica, is recognized by various regional names depending on the language and location. In Amharic, it is called ከሴ, while in Tigrinya, it is referred to as kusay. In Oromo, the name used is kasey, and in Korean, it is known as 코서러트. Additionally, in Persian, it is commonly called به‌لیموی حبشی.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found in Burundi, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The species also inhabits the Central African Republic within this same West-Central zone. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, its range extends into Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the borders of Eritrea.

Plant Parts

This plant, Lippia abyssinica, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its lush, green leaves. These leaves contain the essential bioactive compounds and aromatic oils responsible for the plant's therapeutic effects, making them the most significant part used in traditional remedies. Whether used in infusions or topical applications, the leaves serve as the central source of the plant's healing potential.
