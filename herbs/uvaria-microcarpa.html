Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions in Eastern and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is documented in both Hainan and the Southeast regions of the country. The species can also be found on the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Furthermore, its range extends into the Indo-China peninsula, specifically within Vietnam.
