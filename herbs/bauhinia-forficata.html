Bauhinia forficata

Bauhinia forficaata, commonly known as the Orach tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Bauhinia forficata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Bauhinia forficaata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and is categorized under the genus Bauhinia.
