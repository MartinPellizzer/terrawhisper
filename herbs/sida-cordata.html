Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple continents and island groups. In Northeast Tropical Africa, it is specifically documented to grow on the island of Socotra. Its presence extends across the Western Indian Ocean, where it can be found on both Mauritius and Réunion. Within the Arabian Peninsula, the species is located in the nation of Oman. Finally, its range reaches as far east as China South-Central.
