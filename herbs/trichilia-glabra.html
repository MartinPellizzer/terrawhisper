Trichilia glabra

Trichilia glabra, commonly known as the smooth sandalwood or limba tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, its bark, leaves, and seeds are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues and inflammation.

  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
