Vismia cayennensis

Vismia cayennensis, commonly known as a valuable medicinal shrub in Neotropical regions, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Native to the Amazonian rainforest, this plant is traditionally utilized for its bioactive compounds. Research explores its potential pharmacological properties, particularly its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, offering promising avenues for natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Vismia cayennensis
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Vismia cayennensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Hypericaceae and is categorized under the genus Vismia.

Common Names

This plant, Vismia cayennensis, is known by several different common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is referred to as the Cayenne Vismia, while in French, it is called Vismia de Cayenne. In the local Creoles of the Guianas, it is commonly known as Vismia, and in Spanish, it is called Vismia de Cayenne.

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several specific regions within the Caribbean and Northern South America. In the Caribbean, its presence is documented within the islands of Trinidad and Tobago. Moving to the mainland, it can be found throughout the territory of Venezuela. The species also inhabits the Guiana Shield, specifically covering Guyana and Suriname. Finally, its geographical range extends into the region of French Guiana.
