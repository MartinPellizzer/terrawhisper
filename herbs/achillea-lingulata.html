Achillea lingulata

Achillea linguatlata, commonly known as the tongue-leaved yarrow, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific Mediterranean regions. Renowned for its unique foliage and delicate white blooms, this medicinal plant offers traditional therapeutic benefits, primarily utilized in folk medicine for its soothing anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties across various cultures.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is categorized under the genus Achillea.
