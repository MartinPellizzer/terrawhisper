Plant Parts

This plant, Picea abies, commonly known as the Norway spruce, is highly valued in traditional medicine for the various substances it produces. The most significant medicinal components are the resins found within its woody tissues and bark, which possess potent antiseptic and anti-inflammatory properties. When the bark or needles are wounded, the tree produces aromatic exudates that serve as a natural defense mechanism against pathogens. These viscous secretions are often processed or collected in the form of a soothing balm, which is frequently applied topically to treat skin irritations, muscle aches, and respiratory congestion.
