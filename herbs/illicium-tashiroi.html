Illicium tashiroi

Illicium tashiroi, commonly known as the Japanese star anise, is a medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its aromatic fruit, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and inflammation. This plant offers significant pharmacological potential in modern natural therapeutics.

  • Scientific name: Illicium tashiroi
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Austrobaileyales and the family Schisandraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Illicium.
