Helicteres hirsuta

Helicteres hirsuta, commonly known as the hairy helicteres, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering a natural remedy through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Helicteres hirsuta
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Helicteres hirsuta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Malvales and placed in the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Helicteres.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across parts of Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in Hainan as well as throughout the China Southeast region. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Assam. Additionally, the species is known to grow within the borders of Bangladesh. Finally, its range reaches the mountainous terrain of the East Himalaya.
