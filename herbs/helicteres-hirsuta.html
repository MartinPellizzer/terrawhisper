Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across parts of Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in Hainan as well as throughout the China Southeast region. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Assam. Additionally, the species is known to grow within the borders of Bangladesh.
