Carpinus cordata

What's the taxonomical classification of Carpinus cordata?

Carpinus cordata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Fagales and is a member of the family Betulaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Carpinus, with its specific designation being the species cordata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Carpinus cordata has heart-shaped, simple leaves with serrated margins and a prominent pinnate venation pattern. The leaf blades are typically dark green on the upper surface and slightly paler underneath. These leaves are arranged in an opposite pattern along the woody stems. The plant features small, inconspicuous flowers that eventually develop into nutlets attached to three-lobed bracts. Its overall structure consists of a deciduous shrub or small tree with smooth, grayish bark.
