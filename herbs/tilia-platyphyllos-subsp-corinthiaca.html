Tilia platyphyllos subsp. corinthiaca

Tilia platyphyllos subsp. coriinthiaca, commonly known as the Corinthian lime, is a distinguished medicinal subspecies prized for its soothing properties. Renowned for its fragrant blossoms, it is traditionally used in herbal infusions to alleviate anxiety, reduce fever, and promote restful sleep, offering gentle therapeutic benefits for nervous relaxation.

  • Scientific name: Tilia platyphyllos subsp. corinthiaca
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, it is further categorized into the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tilia, specifically as the subspecies corinthiaca.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the southeastern regions of the European continent. Specifically, its presence is documented within the borders of Albania and Bulgaria. Furthermore, the species can be found throughout the diverse landscapes of Greece. Its geographical range extends beyond Europe into the westernmost parts of Asia. In this region, the plant is notably established within Türkiye.
