Euphorbia hylonoma

Euphorbia hylonoma, commonly known as a medicinal shrub, is a unique plant species indigenous to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Scientific research continues to explore its potential pharmacological properties for modern therapeutic applications.

  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae.
