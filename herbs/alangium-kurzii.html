Alangium kurzii

Alangium kurzii, commonly known as the Kurz's Alangium, is a significant medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering immense potential for modern phytotherapy research.

  • Scientific name: Alangium kurzii
  • Family: Cornaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
