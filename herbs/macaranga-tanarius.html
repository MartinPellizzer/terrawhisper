Macaranga tanarius

Macaranga tanarius, commonly known as the Macaranga plant, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This plant holds significant potential for developing new pharmacological treatments.

  • Scientific name: Macaranga tanarius
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Macaranga.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across various parts of Asia. In Eastern Asia, its presence is noted in the Nansei-shotto islands and throughout Taiwan.
