Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across various parts of Asia. In Eastern Asia, its presence is noted in the Nansei-shotto islands and throughout Taiwan. Plant Parts

This plant, Macaranga tanarius, exhibits various parts that are utilized in traditional medicine, most notably its foliage. The large, heart-shaped leaves serve as a primary source of bioactive compounds, and a single leaf can often be applied topically to treat skin irritations or inflammation. Furthermore, the fallen leaves that accumulate on the forest floor are sometimes collected and processed for their medicinal properties, ensuring that every stage of the plant's vegetative life contributes to its therapeutic potential.
