Murdannia loriformis

Murdannia loriformis, commonly known as Cordyceps flower, is a medicinal herb widely utilized in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is frequently studied for its potential to support immune function, combat fatigue, and promote overall cellular health through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Murdannia loriformis
  • Family: Commelinaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Commelinares and the family Commelinaeaecae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Murdannia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central provinces as well as throughout the Southeast. Its presence also extends to the tropical island of Hainan and the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Beyond the Chinese mainland, the species is also native to the islands of Japan. Together, these locations highlight the plant's widespread habitat across the eastern part of the continent.
