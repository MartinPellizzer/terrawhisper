Distribution

This plant is native to the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its distribution can be found within the borders of Benin. It is also widely documented to grow in the country of Ghana. Furthermore, the species is present in Guinea and the Ivory Coast. Finally, its geographical range extends into Liberia.
