Pimpinella anisoides

Pimpinella anisoides, commonly known as a member of the Apiaceae family, is a medicinal herb valued for its diverse therapeutic potential. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to support digestive health and possess antioxidant properties, offering significant interest to modern pharmacology.
