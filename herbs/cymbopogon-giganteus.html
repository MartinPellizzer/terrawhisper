Kachi grass, scientifically known as Cymbopogon giganteus, is a perennial aromatic grass widely recognized for its potent medicinal properties. Native to tropical regions, it is prized in traditional medicine for its essential oils, which offer powerful antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant benefits, making it a vital natural healing resource.

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its natural habitat also extends into the territory of Burkina. Furthermore, the species is documented to exist within the borders of Gambia and Ghana. Plant Parts

This plant, Cymbopogon giganteus, is a perennial grass that is primarily valued for the therapeutic properties found within its leaves. These long, slender, and linear leaves are the most significant part of the plant used in traditional medicine, as they contain essential oils that provide aromatic and healing benefits. When harvested and processed, the leaves serve as the main source for extracting compounds used to treat various ailments, making them the most vital component of the species.
