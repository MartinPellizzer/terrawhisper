Pilocarpus

Pilocarpus, commonly known as pilocarpine-producing shrubs, belongs to the Rubiaceae family. Native to South America, these medicinal plants are primarily harvested for their alkaloid content. Pilocarpine, extracted from their leaves, serves as a vital pharmaceutical agent used extensively to treat glaucoma and stimulate saliva production in dry mouth.

  • Scientific name: Pilocarpus
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Pilocarpus.
