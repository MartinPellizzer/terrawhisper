, scientifically known as Pilocarpus, is a genus of medicinal shrubs native to South America. Renowned for containing the alkaloid pilocarpine, these plants play a vital role in modern pharmacology. They are primarily used to treat glaucoma and manage symptoms of dry mouth, offering significant therapeutic benefits in medicine.
