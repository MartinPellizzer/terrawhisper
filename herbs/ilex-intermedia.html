Ilex intermedia

Ilex intermedia, commonly known as the Intermediate Holly, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its glossy, leathery foliage. Native to parts of Asia, this resilient plant offers ornamental beauty and dense cover for wildlife. In traditional contexts, its hardy nature makes it a staple for ecological landscaping.

  • Scientific name: Ilex intermedia
  • Family: Aquifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Aquifoliales, falling into the family Aquifoliaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Ilex.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ilex intermedia, is referred to by several common names depending on the linguistic context. In Chinese, it is known as 中型冬青, a name that is consistently used across various regional standards including simplified Chinese (zh-cn, zh-hans) and traditional Chinese (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant is native to specific regions within the country of China. Its geographical range is primarily concentrated in the South-Central part of the nation. Additionally, the species can be found growing throughout China Southeast. These particular areas provide the necessary environmental conditions for its natural occurrence. Consequently, the distribution of Ilex intermedia is limited to these distinct East Asian territories.
