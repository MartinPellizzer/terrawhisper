Goodyera foliosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It is specifically categorized within the family Orchidaceae, which characterizes its botanical identity. Ultimately, its scientific classification is completed by its placement in the genus Goodyera and its specific designation as the species foliosa.
