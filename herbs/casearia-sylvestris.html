Casearia sylvestris

Casearia sylvestris, commonly known as "cascão" in Brazil, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South American forests. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Casearia sylvestris
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It further descends through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, eventually placing it in the family Salicaceae. Specifically, Casearia sylvestris is identified by the genus Casearia.
