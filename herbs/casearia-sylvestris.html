Wild sage, scientifically known as Casearia sylvestris, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South American forests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This potent plant continues to be a vital resource in ethnobotanical studies and herbal medicine.
