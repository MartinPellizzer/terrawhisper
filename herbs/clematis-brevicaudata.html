Clematis brevicaudata

Clematis brevicaucula, commonly known as the short-stemmed clematis, is a climbing herbaceous perennial native to specific montane regions. Characterized by its delicate foliage and unique flowering patterns, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional ethnobotany for its potential therapeutic properties and its distinctive role within its natural ecosystem.

  • Scientific name: Clematis brevicaudata
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae, it is specifically identified by the genus Clematis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range stretching across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented within the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Its distribution extends significantly into China, where it can be found in the South-Central areas. Additionally, the species is recorded in the Inner Mongolia region of China.
