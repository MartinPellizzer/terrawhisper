Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range stretching across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented within the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Its distribution extends significantly into China, where it can be found in the South-Central areas. Additionally, the species is recorded in the Inner Mongolia region of China. These diverse locations highlight the wide environmental adaptability of Clematis brevicaunda.
