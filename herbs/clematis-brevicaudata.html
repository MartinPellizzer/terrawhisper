Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range stretching across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented within the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Its distribution extends significantly into China, where it can be found in the South-Central areas. Additionally, the species is recorded in the Inner Mongolia region of China. Reproduction

The reproduction of Clematis brevicaudata is characterized by its bisexual sexual reproduction system. The flowering period for this species is concentrated in the month of July, with both the flowering start and flowering end occurring during this time. The plant produces inflorescences that are quite small, ranging in length from a minimum of 0.01 m to a maximum of 0.015 m. The individual flowers are relatively small, with lengths measuring between 1.5 cm and 2 cm. Following pollination, the plant develops fruits that are uniform in their dimensions; the fruit length is consistent at 0.3 cm (with a minimum, mean, and maximum all measuring 0.3 cm), and the fruit width is also highly stable, maintaining a consistent measurement of 0.2 cm across its minimum, mean, and maximum values.
