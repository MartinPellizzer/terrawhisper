Linum lewisii

Wild blue flax

Linum lewisii, commonly known as Lewis flax, is a charming perennial wildflower native to western North America. Renowned for its delicate, vibrant blue blooms, this species thrives in sunny, open habitats. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds ecological value, supporting pollinators and adding graceful texture to natural landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Linum lewisii
  • Family: Linaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling into the family Linaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Linum.
