Plant Parts

This plant, Eucalyptus rossii, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its aromatic leaves. These leaves contain essential oils, such as cineole, which are often harvested through steam distillation to create treatments for respiratory ailments. When used in therapeutic applications, the extracts from the leaves serve as potent decongestants and antiseptics, making them a vital component in various natural remedies.
