Blackberry lily, scientifically known as Iris domestica, is a resilient perennial native to North America. Renowned for its striking orange flowers and distinctive berry-like seeds, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used by Indigenous cultures, its rhizomes offer various medicinal properties, making it a fascinating subject in herbalism.
