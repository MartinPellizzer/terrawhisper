Japanese Prickly Ash, scientifically known as Zanthoxylum ailanthoides, is a versatile medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its aromatic, numbing properties, it is used in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, inflammation, and skin ailments. Its potent bioactive compounds make it a vital botanical resource.
