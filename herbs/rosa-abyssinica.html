Rosa abyssinica

Rosa abyssinica, commonly known as the Abyssinian rose, is a resilient shrub native to the highlands of East Africa. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its potential therapeutic properties, offering natural remedies that support wellness through its unique botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Rosa abyssinica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Rosa.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Rosa abyssinica, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across Northeast Tropical Africa and parts of the Arabian Peninsula. Within the African continent, it can be found spanning from Eritrea to Ethiopia. Its presence also extends through Somalia and across the regions of Sudan and South Sudan. Beyond Africa, the species reaches into the Arabian Peninsula, specifically within Saudi Arabia. Consequently, its range encompasses a diverse set of territories across these two major geographic regions.
