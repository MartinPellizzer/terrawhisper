Polygala hongkongensis

Polygala hongkongensis, commonly known as Hong Kong Milkwort, is a significant medicinal herb in traditional practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is primarily utilized to clear heat, resolve phlegm, and treat respiratory ailments. This plant offers promising therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology for its anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective properties.

  • Scientific name: Polygala hongkongensis
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Polygalaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Polygala.

Distribution

This plant, known as Polygala hongkongensis, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of China. It can be found growing within the South-Central part of the country. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the Southeast region of China. Its range also extends significantly westward into the Xinjiang province. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various geographical settings across the nation.
