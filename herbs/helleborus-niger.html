Helleborus niger

Helleborus niger, commonly known as the Christmas Rose, is a striking perennial valued for its snowy white blooms during winter. Historically used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, it contains potent compounds. However, due to its toxicity, it must be handled with extreme caution and professional medical guidance.

  • Scientific name: Helleborus niger
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Helleborus niger, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Helleborus.
