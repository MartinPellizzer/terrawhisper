Black hellebore, scientifically known as Helleborus niger, is a striking evergreen perennial celebrated for its dramatic, snow-white blooms. While aesthetically captivating in winter gardens, this plant is highly toxic if ingested. Historically used in traditional medicine, it must be handled with extreme caution due to its potent chemical properties.
