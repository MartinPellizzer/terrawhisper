Rhizophora apiculata

Rhizophora apiculata, commonly known as a specialized mangrove species, plays a vital role in coastal ecosystem stability. This medicinal plant is valued for its unique phytochemical properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits. Researchers explore its bioactive compounds for their antioxidant and antimicrobial capabilities, highlighting its significant importance in traditional pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Rhizophora apiculata
  • Family: Rhizophoraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Rhizophoraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Rhizophora as the species Rhizophora apiculata.
