What's the taxonomical classification of Veronicastrum caulopterum?

Veronicastrum caulopterum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the taxonomic order Lamiales and is a member of the family Plantaginaceae. At the most specific levels of its classification, it is identified by the genus Veronicastrum and the species caulopterum.
