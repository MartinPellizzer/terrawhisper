Alpinia pinnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Alpinia pinnanensis?

Alpinia pinnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Zingiberales. This plant is a member of the family Zingiberaceae, falling under the genus Alpinia, and is specifically identified by its species name, pinnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Alpinia pinnanensis has large, lanceolate leaves that are arranged in an alternate pattern along sturdy, upright rhizomatous stems. The leaf blades feature distinct parallel venation and a smooth, entire margin that extends toward a pointed apex. These perennial herbs typically reach significant heights, supported by thick, fleshy stalks characteristic of the Zingiberaceae family. The inflorescences emerge from the foliage, eventually producing showy flowers with intricate petal structures. Dense, fibrous root systems allow the plant to spread effectively through its tropical habitat.
