Eugenia involucrata

Eugenia involucrata, commonly known as the Surinam cherry, is a tropical evergreen shrub prized for its edible, flavorful fruits. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Its leaves and bark are frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, showcasing its diverse therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Eugenia involucrata
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Eugenia involucrata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Eugenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several distinct regions of South America.
