What's the taxonomical classification of Paspalum notatum?

Paspalum notatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the biological hierarchy further, it is organized under the order Poales and falls within the family Poaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized under the genus Paspalum, with its specific designation being the species notatum.
