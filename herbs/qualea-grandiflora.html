Qualea grandiflora

Qualea grandiflora, commonly known as the "Pau-de-fogo," is a majestic tree native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its striking, vibrant orange blossoms, this species plays a vital ecological role. Beyond its beauty, it holds significant ethnobotanical value, with various parts used in traditional medicine for healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Qualea grandiflora
  • Family: Vochysiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Vochysiaeae and is specifically identified by the genus Qualea.
