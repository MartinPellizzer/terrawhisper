Alternanthera littoralis

Alternanthera achyranthoides

Alternanthera littoralis, commonly known as Rock Knotweed, is a versatile perennial plant cherished for its striking ornamental foliage. While primarily used in landscaping for its vibrant hues, it holds significant place in traditional herbal practices. This resilient species thrives in diverse environments, offering both aesthetic beauty and botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Alternanthera littoralis
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Alternanthera.

Distribution

Moving toward West Tropical Africa, the species is widely distributed across multiple nations. It is found within the territories of Benin, Gambia, and Ghana. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Rock Knotweed, is utilized in various traditional practices primarily through its aerial parts. These above-ground structures include the sprawling, prostrate stems and the small, inconspicuous leaves that characterize its growth habit. Because the medicinal properties are concentrated in these aerial parts, they are typically harvested, dried, and processed for use in infusions or topical applications.
