Plant Parts

This plant, Pinus contorta (commonly known as the Lodgepole pine), is highly valued in traditional and modern medicine primarily for its resinous secretions. The most significant medicinal components are derived from the pitch preparations, which are obtained from the tree's sticky, aromatic resin. These pitch preparations are rich in terpenes and other bioactive compounds, making them useful in topical applications to treat skin conditions, alleviate inflammation, and act as mild antiseptics. Beyond its external uses, the resinous properties of the plant serve as a vital source for various therapeutic extracts used in holistic healing practices.
