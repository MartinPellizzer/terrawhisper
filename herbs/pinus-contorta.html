Pinus contorta

Pinus contorta, commonly known as the Lodgepole pine, is a resilient coniferous species native to western North America. Beyond its ecological importance in forest succession, this plant holds significant medicinal value. Its resin and various extracts are traditionally utilized for their antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and respiratory-supporting properties in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Pinus contorta
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, belonging to the family Pinaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pinus.
