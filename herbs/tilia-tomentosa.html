Tilia tomentosa

Tilia tomentosa, commonly known as the Silver Linden, is a deciduous tree prized for its fragrant, pale-yellow flowers. Renowned in herbal medicine, its blossoms are used to brew soothing teas that alleviate stress, reduce fever, and support digestive health, offering a gentle, calming remedy for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Tilia tomentosa
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, falling within the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tilia.
