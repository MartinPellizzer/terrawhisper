Tilia argentea, scientifically known as Tilia tomentoosa, is a distinctive deciduous tree valued for its medicinal and aesthetic properties. Often called the Silver Lime, its fragrant blossoms are traditionally utilized in herbal infusions to soothe anxiety, reduce inflammation, and alleviate digestive discomfort, making it a staple in traditional pharmacopoeia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Tilia tomentoosa, is characterized by its distinct reproductive structures that are highly valued for their aromatic properties. The inflorescences typically consist of small clusters of flowers that emit a sweet, pleasing scent, making them a primary component in herbal preparations. A defining feature of this species is the presence of prominent, leaf-like bracts that subtend the flower clusters, serving to protect the developing reproductive organs and aiding in the plant's overall identification.
