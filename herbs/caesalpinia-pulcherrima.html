Caesalpinia pulcherrima

Poinciana

Caesalpinia pulcherrimima, commonly known as Pride of Barbados, is a vibrant tropical shrub celebrated for its stunning scarlet blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, utilized in traditional practices to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Caesalpinia pulcherrima
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Fabales, it is specifically situated in the Fabaceae family. Its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Caesalpinia, within which the species Caesalpinia pulcherrimna is categorized.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several key regions. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Egypt. Its range extends to the Macaronesian islands, specifically including Cape Verde. Furthermore, the species is well-established throughout West Tropical Africa. This widespread presence is evidenced by its occurrence in Gambia, Ghana, and the Ivory Coast.
