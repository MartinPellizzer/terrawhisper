Persicaria decipiens

Persicaria decipiens, commonly known as the creeping knotweed, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its trailing stems and delicate foliage, this plant is frequently studied for its bioactive compounds. It holds significant potential in ethnobotany for its diverse medicinal properties and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria decipiens
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it resides in the family Polygonaceae and is identified by the genus Persicaria.
