Kopsia arborea

Kopsia arborea, commonly known as the Kopsia tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional healing to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues, offering valuable insights for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Kopsia arborea
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Kopsia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range extends into the Indo-China region, specifically covering the islands of the Andaman archipelago. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the landscape of Laos. Together, these locations highlight the specific environmental niches where the plant thrives.

Plant Parts

This plant, Kopsia arborea, possesses various anatomical features that are frequently utilized in traditional medicinal practices. The therapeutic properties of the species are often derived from its woody structures, including the stems and the sturdy twigs. The bark is a particularly significant component, with both the internal bark and the outer stem-bark being harvested for their specific bioactive compounds. Additionally, the leaves serve as another vital part of the plant, contributing to its overall medicinal profile through their unique chemical composition.
