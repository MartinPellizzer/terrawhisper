Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range extends into the Indo-China region, specifically covering the islands of the Andaman archipelago. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the landscape of Laos.
