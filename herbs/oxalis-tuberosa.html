Distribution

This plant is native to several distinct regions across the South American continent. In Northern South America, its presence is recorded within the borders of Venezuela. Moving toward the western part of the continent, it can be found growing in Colombia. The species also extends its range into Ecuador and Peru within the western territories. Finally, its distribution reaches further south into the western region of Bolivia.
