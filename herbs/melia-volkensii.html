Melia volkensii

Melia volkensii, commonly known as the African teak, is a vital medicinal tree indigenous to East African drylands. Renowned for its robust timber, it also serves a critical role in traditional medicine. Various parts of the tree are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues.

  • Scientific name: Melia volkensii
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Melia.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Northeast and East Tropical Africa sectors. Specifically, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Ethiopia and Somalia within Northeast Tropical Africa. Moving further south, its range extends into the East Tropical Africa region. Within this eastern zone, the species is notably present in both Kenya and Tanzania. Consequently, its geographical footprint covers a significant portion of the Horn of Africa and its neighboring territories.
