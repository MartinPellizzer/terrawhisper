Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several different continents and regions. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing within the territory of Great Britain. Its presence extends to Southwestern Europe, specifically located in France and the Baleares. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species is also established in Italy. Finally, its range reaches the Northwestern U.S.A., where it is native to Oregon.

This plant, known as the Sequoia, utilizes its distinct structural components to thrive in its natural habitat. The leaf, which appears as small, scale-like structures tightly pressed against the branches, is essential for photosynthesis and capturing sunlight. Supporting this growth is the massive, woody stem, or trunk, which provides the necessary structural integrity and transports nutrients throughout the towering organism.
