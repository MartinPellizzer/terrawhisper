Memecylon edule var. edule

Memecylon edule var. eduled, commonly known as the "Grewia" or "Meme," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to African landscapes. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its various parts are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from gastrointestinal issues to skin inflammations, offering significant ethnobotanical value.

  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
