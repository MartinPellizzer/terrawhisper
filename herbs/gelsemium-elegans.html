Gelsemium elegans

Gelsemium elegans, commonly known as Heartbreak Grass, is a climbing woody vine native to East Asia. Renowned for its striking, bell-shaped flowers, this plant contains potent alkaloids. While historically used in traditional medicine, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling, as it can be fatal if ingested improperly.

  • Scientific name: Gelsemium elegans
  • Family: Gelsemiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Gelsemiaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized within the genus Gelsemium.

Distribution

This plant, Gelsemium elegans, exhibits a specific distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is documented across the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. The species is also naturally found within the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be located in the Assam region. Together, these locations define the primary geographical range of this medicinal species.
