Graceful Jesamine, scientifically known as Gelsemium elegans, is a climbing evergreen vine prized for its striking, bell-shaped flowers. While aesthetically beautiful, this plant contains potent alkaloids, making it highly toxic if ingested. In traditional medicine, it is studied cautiously for its complex pharmacological properties and potential therapeutic applications.

Distribution

This plant, Gelsemium elegans, exhibits a specific distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is documented across the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. The species is also naturally found within the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be located in the Assam region.
