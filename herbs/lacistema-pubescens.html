Lacistema pubescens

Lacistema pubescens, commonly known as the "pajonal" or "huayruro," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Andean regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and digestive issues, offering a rich botanical resource for both traditional and modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Lacistema pubescens
  • Family: Lacistemataceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Malpighiales and the family Lacistemataceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lacistema, of which Lacistema pubescens is a member.
