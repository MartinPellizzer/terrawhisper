Jacobaea leucophylla

Jacobaea leucophylla, commonly known as White-leaf Ragwort, is a striking perennial herb distinguished by its silvery, woolly foliage. Native to Mediterranean climates, this resilient plant thrives in rocky terrains. While primarily valued for its ornamental beauty, its unique physiological adaptations offer intriguing possibilities for botanical and medicinal research.

  • Scientific name: Jacobaea leucophylla
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, specifically residing in the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Jacobaea.
