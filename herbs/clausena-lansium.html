Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it is primarily found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Its presence extends further into the Indo-China peninsula, specifically within the borders of Laos. Additionally, the species is documented to grow throughout Vietnam.
