Clausena lansium

Clausena lansium, commonly known as the "Scented Leaves" or "Lansium," is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, various parts of the plant are traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various systemic infections.

  • Scientific name: Clausena lansium
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae, it is classified under the genus Clausena. Specifically, the medicinal species is identified as Clausena lansium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Clausena lansium, is recognized by several common names across different Chinese linguistic regions. In Simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 黄皮 (zh-hans) and specifically as 黄皮 in mainland China (zh-cn) and Singapore (zh-sg). In Traditional Chinese, the name is written as 黃皮 (zh-hant), which is also the standard designation used in Hong Kong (zh-hk).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it is primarily found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Its presence extends further into the Indo-China peninsula, specifically within the borders of Laos. Additionally, the species is documented to grow throughout Vietnam. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where this medicinal plant thrives.
