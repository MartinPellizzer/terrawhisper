Crotalaria linifolia

Crotalaria linifolia, commonly known as a member of the Fabaceae family, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in managing inflammation, infections, and other systemic health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Crotalaria linifolia
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical presence across several key regions in East Asia. In mainland China, its range extends through the South-Central provinces and into the Southeast. It is also documented to grow in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Furthermore, the species can be found on the tropical island of Hainan. Finally, its distribution includes the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia.
