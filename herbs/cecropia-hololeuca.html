Cecropia hololeuca

Cecropia hololeuca, commonly known as the white-leaved Cecropia, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a vital subject in ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Cecropia hololeuca
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Rosalales, and the family Urticaceae.
