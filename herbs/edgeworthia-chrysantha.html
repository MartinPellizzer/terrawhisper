Edgeworthia chrysantha

Edgeworthia chrysantha, commonly known as Paperbush, is a striking deciduous shrub celebrated for its aromatic, golden-yellow blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this resilient plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its soothing properties and ability to support respiratory health and skin vitality naturally.

  • Scientific name: Edgeworthia chrysantha
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is a member of the Thymelaeaceae family. Its specific taxonomic classification is placed within the genus Edgeworthia.
