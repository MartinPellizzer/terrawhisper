Caryopteris terniflora

What's the taxonomical classification of Caryopteris terniflora?

Caryopteris terniflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. This specific plant is further identified by its genus, Caryopteris, and its species designation, terniflora.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Caryopteris terniflora has an upright, multi-stemmed woody shrub habit characterized by opposite, trifoliate leaves that measure one to two inches long and possess serrated margins. The foliage typically presents a bright green color and develops a slightly textured surface. During its blooming period, the plant produces dense, terminal clusters of small, tubular, blue to lavender flowers. These inflorescences emerge from the ends of the branches to create a striking visual display. The stems transition from a soft, green texture in new growth to a more rigid, woody structure as the plant matures.
