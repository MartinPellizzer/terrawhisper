Globba racemosa

Globba racemosa, commonly known as the dancing ginger, is a charming perennial herb belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Native to tropical regions, it is distinguished by its delicate, colorful flowers and slender stems. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Globba racemosa
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Globba.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central and Southeast territories, as well as in the high-altitude region of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam. Additionally, the species extends its range into the neighboring country of Bangladesh. Together, these locations highlight the specific tropical and subtropical habitats where the species thrives.
