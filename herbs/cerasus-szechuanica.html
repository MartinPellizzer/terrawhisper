What's the taxonomical classification of Cerasus szechuanica?

Cerasus szechuanica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this botanical lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Cerasus, and its specific species designation, szechuanica.
