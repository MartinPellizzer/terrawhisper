Diospyros ferrea

Diospyros ferrea, commonly known as Ironwood, is a robust medicinal plant prized for its potent therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional healing, its bark and leaves are frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. This resilient species offers a rich natural resource for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Diospyros ferrea
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Diospyros ferrea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ebenaceae and the genus Diospyros.

Distribution

This plant, Diospyros ferrea, is primarily located within the West Tropical Africa region. Its natural habitat extends across several different nations, including Benin and Burkina. Furthermore, the species can be found growing in the landscapes of Ghana. It is also distributed throughout the territories of Guinea-Bissau and Guinea. Collectively, these locations define the specific geographical range of this medicinal species.
