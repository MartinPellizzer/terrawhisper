Aglaia mariannensis

Aglaia mariannensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in Southeast Asian ethnobotany, holds immense pharmacological potential. Belonging to the Meliaceae family, this species is valued for its diverse bioactive compounds. Researchers are increasingly exploring its traditional uses to discover novel therapeutic properties for treating various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Aglaia mariannensis
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aglaiia mariannensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Sapindales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Meliaceae and the genus Aglaiia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the specific regions of the Northwestern Pacific. Its geographical presence is notably centered around the Caroline Islands. Additionally, the species extends its range to include the Mariana Islands. These locations represent the documented areas where the plant can be discovered in the wild. Consequently, its distribution is closely tied to these particular island chains in the Pacific Ocean.
