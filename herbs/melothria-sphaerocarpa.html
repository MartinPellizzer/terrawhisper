Distribution

This plant, Melothria sphaerocarpa, is found across several specific regions within West Tropical Africa. Its documented distribution begins in the coastal and inland areas of Benin. It is also known to grow within the borders of Guinea-Bissau. Furthermore, the species can be located throughout the territories of Ivory Coast and Liberia. Finally, its presence is confirmed within the expansive landscapes of Nigeria.

Plant Parts

This plant, Melothria sphaerocarpa, commonly known as strawberry melon or creeping cucumber, is a climbing vine where the most notable botanical feature is the fruit, which contains numerous small, dark seeds. These seeds are contained within a fleshy, spherical berry that transitions from green to a ripe yellowish hue. In medicinal contexts, while the fruit is often the focus of consumption, the seeds serve as the primary reproductive unit, ensuring the propagation of the species across its tropical habitats.
