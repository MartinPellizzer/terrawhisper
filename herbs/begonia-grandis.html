Begonia grandis

What's the taxonomical classification of Begonia grandis?

Begonia grandis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Cucurbitales. This plant is a representative of the family Begoniaceae, specifically situated within the genus Begonia, and is identified by its unique specific epithet, grandi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Begonia grandis has large, asymmetrical, ovate leaves that feature prominent veins and a distinctively textured, slightly hairy surface. These expansive leaves often display deep reddish or bronze hues on their undersides, providing a striking contrast to the green upper surfaces. The plant produces clusters of bright, waxy flowers that typically emerge from the leaf axils. Its stems are thick and succulent, allowing the plant to store significant amounts of moisture within its fleshy tissue. The overall growth habit is bushy and spreading, characterized by its robust foliage and sturdy structural base.
