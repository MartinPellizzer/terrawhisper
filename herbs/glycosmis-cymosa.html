Glycosmis cymosa

Glycosmis cymosa, commonly known as the "Orangeberry" or "Kanimar," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic practices, its leaves, roots, and fruits possess significant therapeutic properties, offering potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant benefits essential for treating various ailments and promoting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Glycosmis cymosa
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Glycosmis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is specifically found throughout the South-Central territories. Its presence is also well-documented across the Indian Subcontinent, where it grows in Assam and throughout Bangladesh. Additionally, the species can be located in the East Himalaya region. Finally, its geographical range extends into the mountainous terrain of Nepal.
