Garcinia pyrifera

Garcinia pyrifera, commonly known as the Malabar gamboge, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Western Ghats. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly xanthones, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various infections, offering immense therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia pyrifera
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Garcinia pyrifera, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales and the family Clusiaceae. Its specific taxonomic classification concludes within the genus Garcinia.
