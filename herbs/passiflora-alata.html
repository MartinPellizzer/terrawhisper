Passiflora alata

Passiflora alata, commonly known as the passionflower, is a remarkable medicinal vine celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally used to alleviate anxiety, promote restful sleep, and soothe nervous tension. Its diverse botanical profile offers significant potential for natural holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Passiflora alata
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Passiflora alata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Passifloraceae and is situated within the genus Passiflora.
