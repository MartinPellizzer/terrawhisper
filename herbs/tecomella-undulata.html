Tecomella undulata

Tecomella undulata, commonly known as the Desert Rose or Palo Verde, is a resilient shrub native to arid regions. Valued for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory issues and skin ailments. This hardy plant exemplifies nature's ability to thrive and provide healing in harsh environments.

  • Scientific name: Tecomella undulata
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
