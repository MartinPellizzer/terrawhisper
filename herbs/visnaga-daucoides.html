Visnaga daucoides

Khella

Visnagia daucoides, commonly known as Visnaga, is a potent medicinal herb native to the Himalayan region. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is primarily utilized in traditional medicine to treat cardiovascular ailments. Its seeds contain bioactive compounds like visnage, which help relax smooth muscles and promote healthy blood circulation.

  • Scientific name: Visnaga daucoides
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is further categorized into the family Apiaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Visnaga, identified by the species name daucoides.
