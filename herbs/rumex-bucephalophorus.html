Rumex bucephalophorus

Rumex bucephalophorus, commonly known as the head-headed dock, is a medicinal herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Polygonaceae family, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and inflammation. Its unique botanical characteristics make it a significant subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe
