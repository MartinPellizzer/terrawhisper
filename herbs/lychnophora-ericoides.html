Lychnophora ericoides

Lychnophora ericoides, commonly known as the heath-like lychnophore, is a resilient shrub native to Southern Africa. Valued in traditional medicine, it is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This hardy species plays a significant role in local ethnobotany, offering therapeutic potential for various skin and digestive ailments.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America
