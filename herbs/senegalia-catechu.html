Senegalia catechu

Acacia catechu

Senegalia catechu, commonly known as Catechu, is a significant medicinal plant widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Extracted from the heartwood, it possesses potent astringent, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties. It is frequently employed to treat gastrointestinal issues, skin inflammations, and oral ailments, serving as a vital natural therapeutic agent.

  • Scientific name: Senegalia catechu
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Senegalia, and its species name, catechu.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across specific regions within Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is notably present in Assam. It also inhabits the landscapes of Bangladesh. Finally, its range extends into the mountainous regions of the East Himalaya.
