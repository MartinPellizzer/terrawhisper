Acacia catechu, scientifically known as Senegalia catechu, is a versatile medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its potent astringent properties, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, gastrointestinal issues, and oral inflammations, offering significant therapeutic benefits through its rich bioactive compounds.
