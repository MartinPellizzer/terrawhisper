Acacia catechu, scientifically known as Senegalia catechu, is a versatile medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its potent astringent properties, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, gastrointestinal issues, and oral inflammations, offering significant therapeutic benefits through its rich bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across specific regions within Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is notably present in Assam. It also inhabits the landscapes of Bangladesh. Finally, its range extends into the mountainous regions of the East Himalaya.

Plant Parts

This plant, Senegalia catechu (commonly known as Khair), is a versatile medicinal resource where various components offer distinct therapeutic benefits. The heartwoods are highly valued for their astringent properties and are frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. The dense wood itself is known for its durability and strength, often used in medicinal preparations, while the pith located within the stem can also be processed for specific applications. Additionally, the resin extracted from the tree is a significant component used in topical and oral treatments, and in some holistic practices, the whole plant is utilized to harness the full spectrum of its bioactive compounds.
