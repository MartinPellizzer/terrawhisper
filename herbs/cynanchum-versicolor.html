Cynanchum versicolor

What's the taxonomical classification of Cynanchum versicolor?

Cynanchum versicolor belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its placement in the order Gentianales, the plant is situated within the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cynanchum and the species versicolor.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cynanchum versicolor has slender, twining stems that produce opposite, ovate to lanceolate leaves with entire margins and prominent venation. The foliage typically exhibits a smooth texture and a deep green color on the upper surface. Small, tubular flowers emerge in clusters, often displaying a range of white to pale pink hues. These blossoms are characterized by a complex structure typical of the Apocynaceae family, featuring a distinct corona. The plant's climbing habit allows it to utilize surrounding vegetation for structural support as it grows.
