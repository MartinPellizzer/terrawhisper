Senna surattensis

Sennasurattensis, commonly known as the Surat Senna, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Valued in traditional pharmacopeia, it is primarily recognized for its potent laxative properties. This botanical species contains bioactive compounds that stimulate digestive health, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Senna surattensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Sennasurattensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and falls under the genus Senna.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Senna surattensis, is referred to by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Poland, it is called strącyniec piłkowany, while in Serbian, it is known as Суратанска сенна. Within Chinese-speaking communities, the plant is identified as 黄槐决明 (zh/zh-hans) and is also called 黃槐決明 in Cantonese (yue).

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions within the West Tropical Africa zone. Its geographical distribution begins in the nation of Benin, where it can be found growing in local habitats. It also extends across the borders into Ghana, marking another key area of its presence. Furthermore, the species is documented within the territories of both Ivory Coast and Togo. Finally, its range includes Nigeria, completing its spread across these West Tropical African countries.
