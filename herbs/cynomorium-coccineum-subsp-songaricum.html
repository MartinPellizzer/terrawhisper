Plant Parts

This plant is a unique holoparasitic species characterized by its lack of chlorophyll and specialized morphological structures. It lacks true leaves and roots, instead developing a thick, fleshy, and succulent stem that emerges from the ground to absorb nutrients from host plants. The most prominent feature of the plant is its large, dense inflorescence, which consists of numerous small, reddish flowers clustered together on the central axis. These succulent stems and specialized inflorescences are the primary parts used in traditional medicinal practices.
