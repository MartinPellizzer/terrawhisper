Schizolobium parahyba

Guapuruvu

Schizolobium parahyba, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, holds profound ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat inflammatory conditions and infections. Understanding its phytochemical properties offers promising pathways for modern pharmacological research and development.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family.
