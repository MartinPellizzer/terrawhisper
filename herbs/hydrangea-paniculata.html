Hydrangea paniculata

Hydrangea paniculata, commonly known as panicle hydrangea, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized for its large, cone-shaped flower clusters. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this resilient plant is studied for its diverse phytochemical properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in traditional medicine through its unique bioactive compounds and antioxidant capacities.

  • Scientific name: Hydrangea paniculata
  • Family: Hydrangeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Cornales, it is further organized into the family Hydrangeaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hydrangea, specifically as the species Hydrangea paniculata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range extending across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found inhabiting the Kuril Islands and Sakhalin. Its distribution also spans much of China, covering the South-Central territories. Furthermore, it is widely documented throughout China's North-Central regions. Finally, the species maintains a presence across the Southeast portion of China.
