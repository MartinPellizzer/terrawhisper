Wrinkle pod mangrove, scientifically known as Cynometrra iripa, is a versatile coastal shrub native to tropical mangrove ecosystems. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant ethnobotanical value for coastal communities.
