Summer savory

Satureja hortensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (635 studies) · ★★★★★

Summer savory, scientifically known as Satureja hortensis, is a versatile aromatic herb prized for its culinary and medicinal properties. Renowned for its peppery flavor, it possesses significant antimicrobial and digestive benefits. Often used to soothe gastrointestinal discomfort, this resilient plant remains a staple in traditional herbal wellness and holistic healing.

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the European continent.
