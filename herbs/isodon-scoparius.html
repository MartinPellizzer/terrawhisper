Isodon scoparius

Isodon scoparius, commonly known as the Japanese Raisin Tree, is a deciduous flowering plant native to East Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, its bark and leaves possess various bioactive properties. This resilient species is often studied for its potential therapeutic applications and its ornamental beauty in diverse landscapes.
