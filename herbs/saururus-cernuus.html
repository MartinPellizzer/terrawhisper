Saururus cernuus

Lizard's tail

Saururus cernuus, commonly known as Lizard's Tail, is a unique perennial herb native to wetland environments. Distinguished by its distinctive spiked white inflorescences, this aquatic plant holds traditional medicinal value. Often utilized in folk remedies, it is studied for its potential bioactive properties and its role in wetland ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Saururus cernuus
  • Family: Saururaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Piperales and the family Saururaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Saururus.
