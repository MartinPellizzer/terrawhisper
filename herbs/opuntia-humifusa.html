Devil's-tongue, scientifically known as Opuntiia humifusa, is a resilient, creeping cactus native to North America. Often found in sandy soils, this low-growing succulent is prized in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. It serves as a vital resource for treating skin ailments and promoting digestive health.
