What's the taxonomical classification of Artemisia sphaerocephala?

Artemisia sphaerocephala belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Asterales. This plant is a prominent representative of the family Asteraceae, characterized by its composite flower structures. Within this family, it is placed in the genus Artemisia, with the specific identification being the species sphaerocephala.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Artemisia sphaerocephala has a perennial growth habit characterized by erect, woody stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters. The leaves are primarily basal and alternate, featuring deeply lobed or pinnatifid margins with a silvery-grey appearance due to dense pubescence. As the plant matures, it produces small, globose flower heads that are clustered tightly at the ends of the stems. These spherical inflorescences are composed of numerous tiny, inconspicuous florets that lack bright petals. The overall texture of the foliage is soft and felt-like, which helps the plant conserve moisture in its native habitat.
