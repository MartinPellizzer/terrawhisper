Artemisia herba-alba

Artemisia herba-alba, commonly known as White Wormwood, is a resilient perennial shrub native to North Africa and the Mediterranean. Renowned for its aromatic silvery foliage, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional pharmacology for its potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, frequently used to treat digestive and inflammatory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia herba-alba
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artemisia, specifically as the species Artemisia herba-alba.
