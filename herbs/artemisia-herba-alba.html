White wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia herba-alba, is a resilient perennial shrub native to North Africa and the Middle East. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant is deeply embedded in traditional folk medicine, frequently utilized for its potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties to treat various ailments.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as White Wormwood, is valued in traditional medicine for its various components, particularly its aerial parts. When harvesting the medicinal material, practitioners often collect the whole aerial parts to ensure a complete profile of its therapeutic properties. The shoots are of particular interest due to their concentration of bioactive compounds, and the primary medicinal potency is derived from the essential oils found within the plant tissues. Ultimately, whether using the entire shoot or processed extracts, the chemical complexity of the plant makes it a significant subject in herbal studies.
