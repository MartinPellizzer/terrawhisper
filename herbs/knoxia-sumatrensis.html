This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. The species also extends its range to the Nansei-shoto islands within Eastern Asia. Furthermore, it can be found growing naturally in Taiwan.
