Jasminum sambac (flower), scientifically known as Jasminum sambac, is a fragrant, evergreen shrub cherished globally for its intoxicating aroma. Renowned in traditional medicine, its blossoms possess potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antibacterial properties. Often used in aromatherapy and herbal teas, this "Arabian jasmine" offers diverse therapeutic healing benefits.
