Jasminum sambac (flower), scientifically known as Jasminum sambac, is a fragrant, evergreen shrub cherished globally for its intoxicating aroma. Renowned in traditional medicine, its blossoms possess potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antibacterial properties. Often used in aromatherapy and herbal teas, this "Arabian jasmine" offers diverse therapeutic healing benefits.

Plant Parts

This plant, Jasminum sambac, utilizes various parts for its medicinal and aromatic properties. The fragrant flowers are the most prominent feature, widely used in perfumery and traditional medicine to soothe the nervous system and alleviate anxiety. The green leaves are also utilized in various herbal preparations for their potential antiseptic and anti-inflammatory qualities. While the roots are less commonly used in modern aromatherapy, they are sometimes employed in traditional practices for their grounding effects. Most importantly, the essential oils extracted from the blossoms provide a concentrated source of therapeutic compounds used to enhance mood, reduce stress, and promote relaxation.
