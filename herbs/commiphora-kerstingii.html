Commiphora kerstingii

Commiphora kerstingii, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in West Africa, belongs to the Burseraceae family. Renowned for its aromatic resin, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues, offering valuable bioactive compounds for pharmacological research and therapeutic applications.

  • Family: Burseraceae
