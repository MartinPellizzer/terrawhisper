Thymus membranaceus

Thymus membranaeus, commonly known as the membrane thyme, is a delicate medicinal herb native to specific Mediterranean regions. Renowned for its potent aromatic oils, it offers significant antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. Traditionally used to support respiratory health and digestion, this resilient plant serves as a valuable botanical remedy.

  • Scientific name: Thymus membranaceus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Thymus as the species Thymus membranceus.

This plant, Thymus membranceus, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as membrane thyme, while in Spanish, it is known as tomillo de membrana. In French, the species is called thym à membrane, and in Portuguese, it is called tomilho de membrana.
