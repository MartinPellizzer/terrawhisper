Salvia circinnata

Salvia circinata, commonly known as the Blue Sage, is a captivating perennial native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its striking, silvery-blue foliage and delicate flowers, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its soothing properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in holistic wellness and natural healing rituals.

  • Scientific name: Salvia circinnata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Salvia.
