Aristolochia maurorum

Aristolochia maurorum, commonly known as creeping birthwort, is a perennial climbing herb native to eastern North America. Characterized by its heart-shaped leaves and tubular, brownish flowers, this plant belongs to the Aristolochiaceae family. While historically used in traditional medicine, it contains toxic aristolochic acids, necessitating extreme caution.

  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
