Cissus cornifolia

Cissus cornifolia, commonly known as climbing vine or various local names, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated in traditional healing. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This climbing species remains a vital resource in ethnobotanical studies worldwide.

  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Africa
