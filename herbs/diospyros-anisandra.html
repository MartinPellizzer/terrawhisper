Diospyros anisandra

Diospyros anisandra, commonly known as the Anisandra tree, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its various parts—including leaves, bark, and fruit—possess significant bioactive properties. These components are frequently utilized to treat diverse ailments, showcasing the plant's profound therapeutic potential.
