Elytranthe arnottiana

Elytranthe arnottiana, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional Southeast Asian practices, holds profound pharmacological importance. Belonging to the Asteraceae family, this plant is celebrated for its bioactive compounds. Researchers increasingly explore its diverse therapeutic potential, particularly for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties in modern medicine.
