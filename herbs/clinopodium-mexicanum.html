Clinopodium mexicanum

Clinopodium mexicanum, commonly known as Mexican mint or Mexican pennyroyal, is a versatile medicinal herb native to various regions. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, this plant offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments, respiratory issues, and inflammatory skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Clinopodium mexicanum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Clinopodium.

Common Names

This plant, Clinopodium mexicanum, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In Spanish, it is commonly referred to as menta de campo or menta silvestre, while in English, it is often called Mexican mint or field mint. In Nahuatl, one of the indigenous languages of Mexico, it is known as xochicuitlatl.
