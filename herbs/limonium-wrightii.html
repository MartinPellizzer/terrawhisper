Limonium wrightii

Limonium wrrightii, commonly known as Wright's Sea Lavender, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal habitats. Thriving in saline environments, this plant is distinguished by its delicate, airy clusters of tiny flowers. It plays a vital role in coastal ecosystems, providing nectar for pollinators and stabilizing sandy soils.

  • Scientific name: Limonium wrightii
  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Limonium wr Wrightii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales and the family Plumbaginaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Limonium.
