Landolphia dulcis

Landolphia dulcis, commonly known as the Milkberry or Madagascar Almond, is a versatile tropical evergreen prized for its medicinal properties. Native to Madagascar, this plant features creamy, edible fruits and lush foliage. Its various parts are traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Landolphia dulcis
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa
