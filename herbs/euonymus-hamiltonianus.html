Euonymus hamiltonianus

Hamilton's spindle

Euonymus hamiltonianus, commonly known as the Hamilton's spindle, is a significant medicinal shrub native to East Asian forests. Renowned in traditional practices, its leaves and stems possess bioactive compounds used to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments. This resilient plant offers valuable botanical insights for modern pharmacological research and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Euonymus hamiltonianus
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Euonymus.
