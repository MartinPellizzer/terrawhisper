Viola tricolor

Wild pansy

Viola tricolor, commonly known as heartsease or wild pansy, is a charming woodland wildflower celebrated in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its vibrant purple, yellow, and white blooms, this resilient plant has long been utilized to support skin health and emotional well-being through its rich antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties.

  • Scientific name: Viola tricolor
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Viola tricolor, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales. It is further classified into the family Violaceae and the genus Viola.
