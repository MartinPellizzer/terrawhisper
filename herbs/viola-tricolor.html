Wild Pansy

Viola tricolor · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (377 studies) · ★★★★★

Wild Pansy, scientifically known as Viola tricolor, is a charming perennial herb renowned for its vibrant, multicolored blooms. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it possesses anti-inflammatory and skin-soothing properties. Often utilized to treat skin conditions and support respiratory health, this resilient wildflower remains a staple in traditional botanical remedies.

This plant, known as Viola tricolor, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales. It is further classified into the family Violaceae and the genus Viola.
