Physalis peruviana

Cape gooseberry

Physalis peruviana, commonly known as the Goldenberry or Cape Gooseberry, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich profile, it offers significant health benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and immune-boosting properties, making it a valuable natural resource in traditional and modern holistic medicine.

  • Scientific name: Physalis peruviana
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Solanales and placed in the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Physalis.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the golden berry or Inca berry, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. Practitioners often utilize the whole plant to prepare infusions, as it contains a variety of bioactive compounds. While the whole plants can be dried for various applications, the most recognizable medicinal components are the fruits. These small, tangy fruits are encased in papery, lantern-like structures known as calyces, which protect the ripening berries. Often, the medicinal essence is concentrated within the pulp of the fruit, making it a vital part of many herbal remedies.
