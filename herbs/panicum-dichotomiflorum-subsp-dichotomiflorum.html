Panicum dichotomiflorum subsp. dichotomiflorum

Panicum dichotomiflorum subsp. dichotomiflorum, commonly known as Dichotomous Panic Grass, is a significant medicinal plant utilized in various traditional healing practices. Valued for its diverse therapeutic properties, this subspecies plays a crucial role in ethnobotanical studies, offering potential applications in treating various ailments through its unique phytochemical profile.

  • Scientific name: Panicum dichotomiflorum subsp. dichotomiflorum
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales and family Poaceae, it is classified under the genus Panicum. Specifically, this medicinal species is identified as the subspecies dichotomiflorum.

Common Names

This plant, Panicum dichotomiflorum subsp. dichotomiflorum, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as panic grass, while in Spanish, it is known as pasto de panicum or zacate de panicum. In Portuguese, it is identified as capim-panicum, and in French, it is called panicum dichotomiflorum.
