Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning across the Caribbean and northern parts of the South American continent. Within the Caribbean region, its presence is documented in both the Dominican Republic and Haiti. Moving toward the mainland, the species can be found in several territories of northern South America. Specifically, it inhabits the regions of French Guiana and Suriname. Finally, its range extends further into the country of Venezuela.

Plant Parts

This plant, Smilax longifolia, is a climbing woody vine that utilizes various structures for survival and medicinal application, most notably its underground rhizo-mes. These thickened, horizontal stems serve as a vital storage organ, allowing the plant to persist through unfavorable seasons and spread through the soil. In traditional herbal practices, these rhizo-mes are often the primary part harvested, as they contain a concentrated density of the bioactive compounds required for therapeutic use.
