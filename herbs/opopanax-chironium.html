Opopanax chironium

Opopanax chironium, commonly known as wild celery or gum elemi, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Native to the Mediterranean, its aromatic resins possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Historically utilized to treat respiratory ailments and digestive issues, this botanical offers significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Opopanax chironium
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Opopanax.
