Crotalaria quinquefolia

Crotalaria quinquefolia, commonly known as rattlebox, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical and subtropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and inflammation. This plant serves as a significant botanical resource in various ethnomedicinal practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Crotalaria quinquefolia
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. It is further identified by the genus Crotalaria, with the specific species name quinquefolia.

Common Names

This plant, Crotalaria quinquefolia, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as rattlebox or pois, while in Spanish, it is known as rátulo or rátulo de sangre. In Portuguese, the plant is called rátulo, and in French, it is identified as crotalaire à cinq feuilles.
