Thermopsis lanceolata

Thermopsis lanceolata, commonly known as lanceleaf lupine, is a perennial herb native to North American mountain ranges. Distinguished by its striking blue-to-purple pea-like flowers, this plant belongs to the Fabaceae family. While visually beautiful, it contains alkaloids that necessitate careful study regarding its traditional medicinal uses and toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Thermopsis lanceolata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is situated within the family Fabaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is found under the genus Thermopsis.
