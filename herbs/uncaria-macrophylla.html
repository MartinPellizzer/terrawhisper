Uncaria macrophylla

What's the taxonomical classification of Uncaria macrophylla?

Uncaria macrophylla belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Gentianaales. This plant is a member of the family Rubiaceae, specifically nested within the genus Uncaria, and is identified by the species name macrophylla.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Uncaria macrophylla has woody, climbing vines characterized by large, elliptical leaves, small yellowish flowers, and distinctive recurved woody spines located along the stem. These hooked thorns facilitate the plant's ability to scramble over other vegetation in its tropical rainforest habitat. The foliage consists of opposite, leathery leaves that provide a dense canopy for the climbing structure. Its inflorescence features small, fragrant blossoms that typically emerge in clusters. The plant also produces small, fleshy fruits that contain the seeds necessary for reproduction.
