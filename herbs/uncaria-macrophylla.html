Uncaria macrophylla

Uncaria macrophylla, commonly known as Cat's Claw, is a powerful medicinal vine native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and immune-boosting properties, this woody climber has been used traditionally by indigenous communities for centuries to treat various ailments, ranging from arthritis to digestive issues and infections.

  • Scientific name: Uncaria macrophylla
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Uncaria macrophylla, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales. It is a member of the Rubiaceae family and falls under the genus Uncaria.
