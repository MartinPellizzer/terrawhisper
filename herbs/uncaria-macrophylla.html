Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Uncaria macrophylla, is a climbing woody vine where the medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in the stem bark. This specific part of the plant is harvested to extract alkaloids, which are utilized in traditional and modern medicine for their purported anti-inflammatory and immune-modulating effects. The bark serves as the essential source for therapeutic compounds, making its careful collection vital for the production of herbal remedies derived from this species.
