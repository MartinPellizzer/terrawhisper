Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of Northern Eurasia. In Middle Europe, its presence has been documented within the borders of Germany. The species also extends deep into Siberia, specifically inhabiting the regions of Buryatiya and Yakutiya. Moving further east, it can be found throughout the Russian Far East. This eastern range specifically includes the Amur region and the Kamchatka Peninsula.

Plant Parts

This plant, Sanguisorba tenuifolia, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its medicinal properties concentrated within the root. The underground rhizomes and roots are the most significant parts used for therapeutic purposes, often being harvested, dried, and processed to extract their astringent compounds. These roots are frequently utilized in herbal formulations to address various ailments, leveraging their natural chemical constituents to provide healing benefits.
