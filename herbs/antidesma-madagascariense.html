Antidesma madagascariense

Antidesma madagascariense, commonly known as a versatile medicinal plant in Madagascar, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this species are utilized in traditional healing practices to treat diverse ailments, offering promising potential for pharmacological research and the development of natural remedies.
