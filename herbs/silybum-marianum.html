Renowned for its ability to support liver detoxification and enhance digestive health, this plant serves as a vital natural remedy. This plant, Silybum marianum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Silybum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific presence across several regions in Northern Europe. Its geographical range extends to the coastal areas of Denmark and the islands of Føroya. Additionally, the species can be found growing throughout Great Britain and Ireland. The distribution also encompasses the northern territories of Norway. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as milk thistle, is characterized by its distinct reproductive structures. The plant produces a variety of small, dry fruits that develop from the flowering heads. Within these structures, each individual seed is contained within a specialized type of fruit known as achenes. These tiny achenes are often equipped with a feathery pappus to aid in wind dispersal. Ultimately, the most medicinally significant part of the plant is found within the seeds, which contain the potent silymarin compounds used for therapeutic purposes.
