This plant, Silybum marianum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Silybum.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as milk thistle, is characterized by its distinct reproductive structures. The plant produces a variety of small, dry fruits that develop from the flowering heads. Within these structures, each individual seed is contained within a specialized type of fruit known as achenes. These tiny achenes are often equipped with a feathery pappus to aid in wind dispersal. Ultimately, the most medicinally significant part of the plant is found within the seeds, which contain the potent silymarin compounds used for therapeutic purposes.
