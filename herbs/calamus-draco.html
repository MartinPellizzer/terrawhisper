Calamus draco

Calamus draco, commonly known as Dragon Calamus, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent aromatic properties, it is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits. This unique botanical offers significant therapeutic potential for treating various ailments through its bioactive compounds.

  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Distribution

This plant is primarily recognized for its specific occurrence within the Malesia botanical region. Specifically, its natural habitat is concentrated on the island of Sumatera. This limited distribution highlights its unique ecological niche within the Southeast Asian archipelago. Because of this localized presence, the species remains a significant component of the regional flora. Understanding this specific range is essential for the conservation and study of the species.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Calamus draco, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its unique exudate rather than its vegetative structures. The most significant part used for therapeutic purposes is the dragon's blood resin, a bright red, sap-like substance that flows from the trunk when the bark is wounded. This resin is prized for its potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, making it a crucial component in various healing salves and medicinal preparations.
