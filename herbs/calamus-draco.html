Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Calamus draco, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its unique exudate rather than its vegetative structures. The most significant part used for therapeutic purposes is the dragon's blood resin, a bright red, sap-like substance that flows from the trunk when the bark is wounded. This resin is prized for its potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, making it a crucial component in various healing salves and medicinal preparations.
