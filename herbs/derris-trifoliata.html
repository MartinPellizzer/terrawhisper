Three-leaf derris, scientifically known as Derris trifoliata, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots contain rotenone, a potent natural compound. This plant is historically valued for its insecticidal properties and its diverse applications in treating various ailments across various cultures.
