Abies pindrow

Abies pindrow, commonly known as Pindrow Fir, is a majestic evergreen conifer native to the Himalayan highlands. Renowned for its high-quality timber and aromatic resin, this medicinal plant holds significant value in traditional practices. Its essential oils are frequently utilized for their soothing, antiseptic, and respiratory-supporting therapeutic properties.

  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
