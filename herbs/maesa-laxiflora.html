Maesa laxiflora

Maesa laxiflora, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb, is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Found in tropical ecosystems, this plant contains bioactive compounds utilized in traditional healing practices. Research explores its potential in treating inflammation and oxidative stress, highlighting its importance in modern ethnobotanical and pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Maesa laxiflora
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Primulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement concludes within the genus Maesa.

Common Names

This plant, Maesa laxiflora, is known by several names depending on the linguistic context, specifically within Chinese terminology. In traditional Chinese nomenclature, it is referred to as 疏花杜茎山 (zh), which is also written as 疏花杜茎山 in its simplified form (zh-hans) and is specifically designated as 疏花杜茎山 in Mainland China (zh-cn).

Distribution

This plant, known as Maesa laxiflora, exhibits a specific geographical range within the Southeast Asian region. Its natural distribution is primarily concentrated within the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Laos. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit various areas across Vietnam. These locations define the known environmental boundaries for this medicinal plant.
