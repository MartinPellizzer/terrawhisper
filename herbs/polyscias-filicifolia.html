Polyscias filicifolia

Polyscias filicifolia, commonly known as the fern-leaved sedge, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique foliage, this species offers therapeutic properties used to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and bioactive potential is essential for exploring its significant role in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Polyscias filicifolia
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized under the order Apiales and the family Araliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Polyscias.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of Papuasia, specifically within New Guinea. Its presence extends significantly into the Southwestern Pacific region, where it can be found in Fiji. Additionally, this medicinal species inhabits the Gilbert Islands located in the same oceanic area. The geographical range further encompasses the islands of New Caledonia. Finally, the distribution of this plant includes the Wallis-Futuna Islands within the Southwestern Pacific.
