Bilimbi, scientifically known as Averrhoa bilimbi, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree prized for its highly acidic, green fruits. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antibacterial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, this versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential globally.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution stretching across several tropical and subtropical regions. Moving toward the Asian continent, its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in Assam. The species is also widely distributed in Eastern Asia, with documented locations in Taiwan. Finally, it inhabits parts of China, specifically in the China Southeast region.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as bilimbi, utilizes various components for its medicinal and culinary properties, most notably its leaves and fruits. The small, green leaves are often used in traditional remedies, sometimes prepared as a decoction to treat skin ailments or fever. Meanwhile, the fruits are highly distinctive due to their cylindrical shape and intense acidity; they are frequently harvested to create juices or topical applications used to soothe inflammation and manage various ailments.
