Bilimbi, scientifically known as Averrhoa bilimbi, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree prized for its highly acidic, green fruits. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antibacterial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, this versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential globally.
