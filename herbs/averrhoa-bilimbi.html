Averrhoa bilimbi

Bilimbi

Averrhoa bilimbi, commonly known as bilimbi or cucumber tree, is a tropical evergreen shrub prized for its small, acidic green fruits. Rich in vitamin C, it is widely used in culinary dishes and traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Averrhoa bilimbi
  • Family: Oxalidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Averrhoa bilimbi, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Oxalidales and the family Oxalidaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Averrhoa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution stretching across several tropical and subtropical regions. Within the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found in both the Chagos Archipelago and the Comoros. Moving toward the Asian continent, its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in Assam. The species is also widely distributed in Eastern Asia, with documented locations in Taiwan. Finally, it inhabits parts of China, specifically in the China Southeast region.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as bilimbi, utilizes various components for its medicinal and culinary properties, most notably its leaves and fruits. The small, green leaves are often used in traditional remedies, sometimes prepared as a decoction to treat skin ailments or fever. Meanwhile, the fruits are highly distinctive due to their cylindrical shape and intense acidity; they are frequently harvested to create juices or topical applications used to soothe inflammation and manage various ailments.
