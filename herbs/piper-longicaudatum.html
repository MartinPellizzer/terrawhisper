Piper longicaudatum

Piper longicaudatum, commonly known as Long Pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Piperaceae family. Renowned for its pungent aroma and warming properties, it is extensively used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, serving as a potent natural remedy for various conditions.

  • Scientific name: Piper longicaudatum
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Piperales and sits in the family Piperaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Piper.

Common Names

This plant, Piper longicaudaudaum, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as Long-tailed Pepper, while in Hindi, it is known as Lamba Pippali. In the Sanskrit language, it is called Pippali, and in Bengali, it is identified as Pipul.
