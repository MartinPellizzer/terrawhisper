Embelia laeta

Embelia laeta, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, belongs to the Embellia genus. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, this plant offers therapeutic potential in traditional medicine. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds for their antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, highlighting its importance in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Embelia laeta
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is situated in the family Primulaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Embelia, which includes the species Embelia laeta.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Embelia laeta, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is known as 酸藤子 (zh) or 酸果藤 (zh), while in mainland China, it is specifically called 酸藤子 (zh-cn). In Taiwan, the plant is identified by the names 正藤木槲 (zh-tw) and 酸果藤 (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions in East Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range also extends to the tropical island of Hainan. Moving further east, the species is documented in the country of Japan. Finally, its geographical presence includes the island of Taiwan.
