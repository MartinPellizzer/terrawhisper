Nymphaea nouchali var. caerulea

Nymphaea nouchalii var. caerulea, commonly known as the Blue Lotus, is a sacred aquatic plant revered for its profound medicinal and spiritual properties. Renowned for its calming effects, it is traditionally used to alleviate anxiety, improve sleep, and enhance cognitive clarity, offering a natural remedy for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Nymphaea nouchali var. caerulea
  • Family: Nymphaeaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Nymphaeales, it is a member of the family Nymphaeaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement concludes within the genus Nymphaea as the variety caerulea.
