Pteris vittata

Pteris vittata, commonly known as the Chinese brake fern, is a remarkable plant celebrated for its hyperaccumulator abilities. Renowned for its capacity to absorb arsenic from contaminated soils, it serves as a vital tool in phytoremediation, offering natural solutions for environmental cleanup and restoring soil health in polluted areas.

  • Scientific name: Pteris vittata
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae. It is further classified under the order Polypodiales and the family Pteridaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pteris, with its specific species name being vittata.
