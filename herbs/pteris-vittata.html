Ladder brake fern, scientifically known as Pteris vittata, is a remarkable fern renowned for its exceptional ability to hyperaccumulate arsenic from contaminated soils. Beyond its ecological significance in phytoremediation, this resilient species holds potential in traditional medicine, offering unique biochemical properties for studying heavy metal detoxification and plant biology.
