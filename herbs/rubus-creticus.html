Rubus creticus

Rubus creticus, commonly known as the Cretan blackberry, is a resilient shrub native to the Mediterranean landscape. Renowned for its vibrant berries and thorny stems, this medicinal plant offers significant antioxidant properties. Traditionally used in folk remedies, it serves as a valuable resource for supporting digestive and inflammatory health.

  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
