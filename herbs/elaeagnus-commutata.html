Elaeagnus commutata

Elaeagnus commutata, commonly known as the Commutate Silverberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing. Rich in bioactive compounds, it offers potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. Exploring its pharmacological properties provides valuable insights into its role in supporting holistic wellness and developing new natural therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Elaeagnus commutata
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the family Elaeagnaceae and the genus Elaeagnus.

This plant, Elaeagnus commutata, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In German, it is referred to as Silber-Ölweide, while in Russian, it is called Лох серебристый. In Lithuanian, the species is known as Amerikinis žilakrūmis, and in Finnish, it goes by the name lännenhopeapennas. Furthermore, in Upper Sorbian, it is identified as Slěborny dźiwi wolijowc.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across the northern regions of Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Denmark and Finland.
