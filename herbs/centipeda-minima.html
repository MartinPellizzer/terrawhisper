Centipeda minima

Sneezweed

Centipeda minima, commonly known as the small centipede plant, is a versatile medicinal herb cherished in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and skin ailments, making it a vital subject for pharmacological research and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Centipeda minima
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Centipeda minima, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is categorized under the genus Centipeda.
