Sneezeweed, scientifically known as Centipeda minima, is a versatile medicinal herb widely utilized in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antibacterial properties, this plant is frequently employed to treat skin conditions, respiratory issues, and various internal ailments, making it a significant subject in pharmacological research.
