Wikstroemia retusa

Wikstroemia retusa, commonly known as the Wikstromia plant, is a significant medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments and inflammation. Scientific research continues to explore its potential therapeutic properties for modern pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Wikstroemia retusa
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Malvales, it is further categorized into the family Thymelaeaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Wikstroemia, specifically as the species Wikstroemia retusa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a distinct geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within Eastern Asia, it can be found specifically in the Nansei-shoto islands. The species is also documented to grow naturally throughout Taiwan. Moving into the Malesia region, the plant extends its range to the Philippines. Consequently, its habitat spans a diverse stretch of island territories across the eastern and southern parts of the continent.
