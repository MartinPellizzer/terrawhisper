Ligusticum canbyi

Ligusticum canbyi, commonly known as Canby's lovage, is a rare perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Native to specific montane regions, this botanical specimen holds significant interest for ethnobotanical research. Its unique chemical profile offers potential therapeutic applications, marking it as a subject of interest in medicinal studies.
