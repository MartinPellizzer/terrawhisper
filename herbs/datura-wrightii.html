Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as sacred datura, is characterized by its large, trumpet-shaped flower that typically blooms in a creamy white or pale violet hue. These nocturnal blossoms are highly fragrant, emitting a strong scent to attract pollinators like hawkmoths during the night. While the entire specimen is potent, the flower serves as one of the most visually striking and chemically significant components of the species.
