Terebinth, scientifically known as Pistacia terebinthus, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its aromatic resin and nutrient-rich fruits, it has been used throughout history in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin conditions, serving as a vital botanical resource.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as the terebinth, utilizes its fruits as a primary component in various traditional medicinal practices. These small, drupaceous fruits, which transition from green to a deep reddish or bluish hue upon ripening, contain resinous properties that have historically been harnessed for their therapeutic potential. In many herbal traditions, the essence or extracts derived from these fruits are valued for their specific chemical compositions used to treat various ailments.
