Lilium lancifolium

Lilium lancifolium, commonly known as the tiger lily, is a striking perennial celebrated for its vibrant, orange trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its bulbs are utilized for their various therapeutic properties, including treating inflammation and supporting digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Lilium lancifolium
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Liliales, it is further categorized into the family Liliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lilium, specifically as the species Lilium lancifolium.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Lilium lancifolium, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for the therapeutic use of its bulbs. These specialized underground structures, referred to as Bulbus Lilii in pharmacopoeia, are harvested from the perennial lilies to extract their medicinal properties. The most critical component for medicinal preparation is the fresh fleshy scaleleaf, which is often processed to maintain its nutritional integrity. By utilizing the succulent flesh of these scales, practitioners can derive various treatments, making the entire bulbous system an essential part of the plant's healing profile.
