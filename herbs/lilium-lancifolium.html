Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Lilium lancifolium, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for the therapeutic use of its bulbs. These specialized underground structures, referred to as Bulbus Lilii in pharmacopoeia, are harvested from the perennial lilies to extract their medicinal properties. The most critical component for medicinal preparation is the fresh fleshy scaleleaf, which is often processed to maintain its nutritional integrity. By utilizing the succulent flesh of these scales, practitioners can derive various treatments, making the entire bulbous system an essential part of the plant's healing profile.
