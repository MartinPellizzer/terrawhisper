Iris unguicularis

Iris unguicularis, commonly known as the Algerian Iris, is a resilient bulbous perennial prized for its striking, fragrant white blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this species holds historical significance in traditional herbal practices, offering unique botanical properties that continue to intrigue researchers studying its potential medicinal applications.

  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Asparagales and falls into the family Iridaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Iris.
