Gentiana lawrencei

Gentiana lawrencei, commonly known as Lawrence's gentian, is a rare and striking perennial wildflower native to specific high-elevation habitats. Renowned for its intense, deep-blue blossoms, this medicinal plant belongs to the Gentianaceae family, historically valued in traditional herbalism for its bitter principles used to support digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana lawrencei
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Gentianaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by its genus, Gentiana.
