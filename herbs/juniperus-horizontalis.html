Juniperus horizontalis, scientifically known as Juniperus horizontalis, is a resilient, low-growing evergreen shrub native to North America. Often used in landscaping for its spreading habit, this hardy conifer offers significant medicinal potential, traditionally valued for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support digestive and urinary tract health.

The physiology of Juniperus horizontalis is characterized by its adaptation to arid and temperate environments, primarily through its specialized metabolic processes and structural adaptations. The plant utilizes a C3 photosynthetic pathway, which involves the direct fixation of carbon dioxide by the enzyme RuBisCO during the Calvin cycle.
