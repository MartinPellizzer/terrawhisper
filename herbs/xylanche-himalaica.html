Xylanche himalaica

Xylanche himalaica, commonly known as Himalayan succulent, is a precious medicinal plant native to high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned for its remarkable rejuvenating properties, it is traditionally used to enhance vitality, boost immunity, and combat oxidative stress, serving as a vital natural resource in traditional Tibetan and Ayurvedic medicine.

  • Scientific name: Xylanche himalaica
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Xylanche.
