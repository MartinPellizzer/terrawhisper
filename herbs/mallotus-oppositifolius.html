Mallotus oppositifolius

Mallotus oppositifolius, commonly known as the "Wild Mallotus," is a significant medicinal shrub found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Mallotus oppositifolius
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
