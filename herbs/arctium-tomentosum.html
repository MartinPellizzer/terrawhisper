Arctium tomentosum

Arctium tomeñosum, commonly known as the velvet burdock, is a resilient medicinal herb celebrated for its potent therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional medicine for its ability to detoxify the blood and support skin health, this plant offers a rich source of antioxidants, making it a valuable botanical remedy.

  • Scientific name: Arctium tomentosum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Asterales, it is categorized under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is classified within the genus Arctium.
