Cotton burdock, scientifically known as Arctium tomentosum, is a distinctive medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Characterized by its fuzzy, woolly leaves, this perennial herb is traditionally used in herbal medicine to support skin health, promote detoxification, and reduce inflammation, offering a unique botanical solution for holistic wellness.
