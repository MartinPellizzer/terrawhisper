Aralia thomsonii

What's the taxonomical classification of Aralia thomsonii?

Aralia thomsonii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. This plant is a member of the family Araliaceae, falling under the genus Aralia, and is specifically identified by the species name thomsonii.
