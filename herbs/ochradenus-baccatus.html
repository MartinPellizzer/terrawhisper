Ochradenus baccatus

Ochradenus jagodowy

Ochradenus baccatus, commonly known as Golden Root, is a revered medicinal plant celebrated for its potent antioxidant properties. Traditionally used in holistic healing, it supports immune function and combats inflammation. This versatile botanical offers a natural approach to wellness, making it a cornerstone in various ancient herbal remedies.

  • Family: Resedaceae
