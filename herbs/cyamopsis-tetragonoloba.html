Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Mali and Sierra Leone. Moving eastward, it can also be found within West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically in Cameroon and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Additionally, the species extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is recorded in Chad. Together, these locations highlight a wide range spanning from the Atlantic coast to the central and northeastern interior.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cyamoopsis tetragona (often referred to in certain contexts as Cyamoopsis tetragona loba), is recognized for its specific morphological characteristics, though it is important to note that the term "tender cluster bean pods" typically refers to the edible legumes of the Cyamopsis tetragonoloba species (Guar). In the context of medicinal or dietary applications, the tender cluster bean pods are the primary parts utilized, valued for their nutritional density and their role in traditional wellness practices. These young, succulent pods are harvested before they become fibrous, ensuring they retain their medicinal potency and ease of consumption.
