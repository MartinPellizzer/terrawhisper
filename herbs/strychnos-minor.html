Strychnos minor

Strychnos minor, commonly known as the small strychnine vine, is a climbing woody plant native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive foliage and small flowers, this species belongs to the Loganiaceae family. While historically studied for its chemical properties, it requires careful handling due to potential alkaloid toxicity.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several key regions of Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in Bangladesh, India, and Sri Lanka.
