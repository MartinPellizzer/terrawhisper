Spiraea media

Spiraea media, commonly known as meadowsweet, is a versatile perennial herb cherished for its delicate, fragrant blossoms. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine for its soothing properties, it contains salicylic acid, offering natural anti-inflammatory relief. This resilient plant thrives in moist environments, adding both beauty and healing potential to landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Spiraea media
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Spiraea media, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Spiraea.
