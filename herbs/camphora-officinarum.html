Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented specifically within Guinea. Its range also extends to West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout the Western Indian Ocean. Within this ocean region, it inhabits the islands of Mauritius, Réunion, and the Seychelles.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as camphor, utilizes various anatomical structures for its therapeutic properties. The aromatic essence is most famously derived from the leaves and the wood, which contain high concentrations of camphor oil. Additionally, the stem barks are often harvested to extract medicinal compounds. In certain preparations, the seed kernel is utilized, and the extracted seed kernel oil serves as a potent base for various topical applications and traditional remedies.
