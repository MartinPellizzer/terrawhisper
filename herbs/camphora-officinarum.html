Camphora officinarum

Camphora officinarum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (617 studies) · ★★★★★

, scientifically known as Camphora officiinarum, is a highly valued medicinal plant prized for its aromatic camphor content. Renowned for its cooling and antiseptic properties, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin irritations, and inflammation, offering significant therapeutic benefits through its potent essential oils.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented specifically within Guinea. Its range also extends to West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout the Western Indian Ocean. Within this ocean region, it inhabits the islands of Mauritius, Réunion, and the Seychelles.
