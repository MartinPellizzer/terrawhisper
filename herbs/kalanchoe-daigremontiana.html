Mother of thousands, scientifically known as Kalanchoe daigremontiana, is a resilient succulent renowned for its rapid reproduction. Characterized by scalloped leaves and tiny plantlets along its edges, this hardy species is studied for its diverse phytochemical properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in traditional medicine and modern pharmacological research.
