Trichosanthes pilosa var. pilosa

Trichosanthes pilosa var. pilosa, commonly known as creeping cucumber, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional systems like Ayurveda. Characterized by its climbing vines and small fruits, this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial activities, making it essential for treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Trichosanthes pilosa var. pilosa
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Trichosanthes, specifically identified as the variety pilosa.
