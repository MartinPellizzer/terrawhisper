Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast coastal areas. Its presence extends further south to the tropical island of Hainan and reaches into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Beyond the mainland, the species is also naturally distributed within the country of Japan. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse Asian landscapes.
