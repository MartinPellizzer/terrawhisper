Helichrysum pamphylicum

Helichrysum pamphylium, commonly known as the everlasting flower, is a resilient perennial prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this aromatic herb is frequently used in skincare and herbal remedies to promote healing, soothe skin irritations, and support overall cellular health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum pamphylicum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Helichrysum.
