This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several distinct regions of East Asia. Within mainland China, it can be found throughout the South-Central provinces and the Southeast regions. Its distribution also extends to the island of Hainan and into the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow in the territory of Taiwan.
