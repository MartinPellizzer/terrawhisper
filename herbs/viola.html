Viola

Viola evans

Viola, commonly known as violets, is a versatile genus of flowering plants cherished for both their beauty and therapeutic potential. Renowned for their delicate blooms, these plants offer various medicinal benefits, including soothing skin irritations, supporting respiratory health, and acting as gentle anti-inflammatory agents in traditional herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Viola
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Violaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Viola.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions within Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Its presence also extends to the island of Iceland and the Faroe Islands, known locally as Føroyar. Additionally, the species is commonly documented throughout Great Britain. These specific locations highlight the plant's affinity for Northern European climates.
