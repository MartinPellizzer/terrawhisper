Zygophyllum simplex

Zygophyllum simplex, commonly known as the simple caltrop, is a resilient succulent native to arid African regions. Distinguished by its fleshy leaves and unique floral structure, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it offers intriguing properties for treating various ailments within desert ecosystems.

  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa
