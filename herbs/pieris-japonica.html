Japanese andromeda

Pieris japonica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (13 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Japanese andromeda, scientifically known as Pieris japonica, is an evergreen shrub celebrated for its cascading clusters of bell-shaped flowers. While prized in landscaping for its ornamental beauty and resilience, it contains grayanotoxins. These compounds make the plant toxic if ingested, requiring careful handling in garden environments.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. It is native to various parts of China, including the South-Central and Southeast territories. Beyond its Asian origins, the species has also established a presence in the Northeastern United States, specifically within Massachusetts. Consequently, the habitat of Pieris japonica spans from temperate Asian forests to parts of North America.
