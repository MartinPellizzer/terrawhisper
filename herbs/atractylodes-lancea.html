southern tsangshu

Atractylodes lancea · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (653 studies) · ★★★★★

Southern tsangshu, scientifically known as Atractylodes lancea, is a vital medicinal herb central to Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its potent rhizomes, it is primarily utilized to strengthen the spleen, resolve dampness, and harmonize digestion. This aromatic plant plays a crucial role in restoring bodily balance and vitality.

This plant, Atractylodes lancea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Atractylodes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented within the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye territories. Moving southward, the species can be found throughout the South-Central regions of China. It also extends its range northward into the Inner Mongolia region of China. Consequently, these diverse locations highlight the wide environmental adaptability of Atractylodes lancea.

Plant Parts

This plant, Atractylodes lancea, is primarily valued for its medicinal rhizome, which serves as the main source of its therapeutic properties. The underground rhizomes are typically harvested, cleaned, and processed into dried rhizomes to ensure stability for long-term storage and use in traditional formulations. The efficacy of the plant is largely attributed to its high concentration of volatile oil, which contains various bioactive compounds. These volatile oils are essential for the plant's pharmacological actions, making the extraction of the rhizome a critical step in its medicinal application.
