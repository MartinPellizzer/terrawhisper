southern tsangshu

Atractylodes lancea · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (653 studies) · ★★★★★

Southern tsangshu, scientifically known as Atractylodes lancea, is a vital medicinal herb central to Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its potent rhizomes, it is primarily utilized to strengthen the spleen, resolve dampness, and harmonize digestion. This aromatic plant plays a crucial role in restoring bodily balance and vitality.
