Viola philippica var. philippica

Viola philippica var. philippica, commonly known as the Philippine Violet, is a delicate perennial herb native to the tropical archipelago. Characterized by its charming violet blooms, this botanical species holds significant ethnobotanical interest. It is traditionally valued in regional folk medicine for its potential therapeutic and healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Viola philippica var. philippica
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, taxonomically identified as Viola philippica var. philippica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Violaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Viola.

Common Names

This plant, Viola philippica var. philippica, is known by several distinct names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Philippine violet, while in Tagalog, it is known as pansit-pansitan.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across diverse regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is recorded within the Amur and Primorye territories. Its range extends significantly into China, where it can be found in the South-Central provinces. Furthermore, the species is documented in the tropical island of Hainan. Finally, the distribution reaches northward into the Inner Mongolia region of China.
