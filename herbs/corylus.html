Corylus

The hazel genus

Corylus, commonly known as hazel, is a versatile genus of deciduous shrubs and trees valued for both nutrition and traditional medicine. Beyond its edible nuts, various parts of the plant have been used in herbal remedies to treat skin irritations, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering a rich botanical heritage.

  • Scientific name: Corylus
  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fagales and the family Betulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Corylus.
