Indian Jalap Turpeth, scientifically known as Operculina turpethum, is a potent medicinal plant valued in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Primarily recognized for its powerful purgative properties, it is used to treat digestive ailments, constipation, and various skin disorders. This climbing herb offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and detoxification.
