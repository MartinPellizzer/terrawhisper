Anise hyssop, scientifically known as Agastache foeniculum, is a perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic, licorice-scented foliage. Prized in herbal medicine for its soothing properties, it is often used to support digestive health and respiratory ease. This versatile plant also serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators.
