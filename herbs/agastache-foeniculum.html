Agastache foeniculum

Anise hyssop

Agastache foeniculum, commonly known as Anise Hyssop, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic, licorice-like scent. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses soothing properties used to aid digestion, alleviate respiratory issues, and reduce inflammation. This fragrant plant is also a vital nectar source for beneficial pollinators.

  • Scientific name: Agastache foeniculum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Agastache. Specifically, Agastache foeniculum represents a unique medicinal species within this taxonomic hierarchy.
