Avena fatua

Common wild oat

Avena fatua, commonly known as wild oats, is a widespread annual grass often categorized as a weed in cereal crops. Beyond its agricultural impact, it holds historical significance in traditional medicine. This resilient plant contains bioactive compounds that offer potential therapeutic benefits, particularly in managing inflammation and supporting digestive health.
