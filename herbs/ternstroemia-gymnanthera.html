Ternstroemia gymnanthera

Ternstroemia gymnanthera, commonly known as the Ternstroemia tree, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Native to various tropical regions, its leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds with potential therapeutic properties. Research explores its antioxidant and antimicrobial effects, highlighting its importance in developing future natural medicines.

  • Scientific name: Ternstroemia gymnanthera
  • Family: Pentaphylacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it falls under the family Pentaphylacaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ternstroemia.

Common Names

This plant, Ternstroemia gymnanthera, is known by several distinct common names depending on the region and language. In Japan, it is referred to as モッコク (mokkoku), while in Korea, it is called 후피향나무 (hupihyangnamu). Across various Chinese-speaking regions, including mainland China (zh-cn), Taiwan (zh-tw), and general Chinese (zh), it is commonly known as 厚皮香 (hòupíxiāng).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. Within China, its presence is specifically noted in the South-Central and Southeast areas. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam. The species also inhabits the territory of Bangladesh. Additionally, its range extends into the mountainous regions of the East Himalaya.
