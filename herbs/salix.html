Willow, scientifically known as Salix, is a versatile genus of shrubs and trees renowned for its medicinal properties. Long used in traditional healing, its bark contains salicin, a natural precursor to aspirin. This potent botanical resource remains fundamental in managing pain, reducing inflammation, and treating various febrile conditions worldwide.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Salix, derives its significant medicinal properties primarily from its cortex, which is the botanical term for the protective outer layer of the stem. In common terminology, this substance is referred to as the bark. Historically, the active compounds found within the willow bark, such as salicin, have been harvested and utilized for their potent analgesic and antipyretic effects to relieve pain and reduce fever.
