Maritime Pine, scientifically known as Pinus pinaster, is a versatile Mediterranean conifer renowned for its therapeutic properties. Highly valued in herbal medicine, its resin and needles provide potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial compounds. Often used to support respiratory health and skin vitality, it remains a cornerstone of botanical pharmacology.
