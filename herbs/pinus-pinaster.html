Pinus pinaster

Pinus pineaster, commonly known as the stone pine, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and antioxidants, its resin and needles are traditionally used to support respiratory health, reduce inflammation, and promote healing, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Pinus pinaster
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, falling under the family Pinaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pinus.
