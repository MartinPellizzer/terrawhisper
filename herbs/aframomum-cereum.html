Distribution

This plant, Aframomum cereum, is primarily distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is specifically documented in the country of Benin, where it grows in local ecosystems. It can also be found spanning across the borders of Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the territories of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau. Finally, its geographical reach includes the nation of Guinea, completing its regional distribution.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as cardamom, utilizes its aromatic seed as the primary part for medicinal and culinary purposes. These small, dark seeds are encased within a protective capsule and are highly valued for their potent essential oils, which possess various therapeutic properties. When harvested and dried, the seeds are frequently used in traditional medicine to aid digestion, alleviate respiratory issues, and act as a natural breath freshener.
