Calophyllum polyanthum

Calophyllum polyanthum, commonly known as a significant medicinal tree in tropical regions, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Research continues to explore its potential pharmacological properties, particularly for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Calophyllum polyanthum
  • Family: Calophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Malpighiales, it is a member of the family Calophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic classification is placed within the genus Calophyllum.

Moving to the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found throughout India and across Bangladesh. The species also inhabits the Assam region and extends into the East Himalaya. Together, these locations define the natural habitat of Calophyllum polyanthum.
