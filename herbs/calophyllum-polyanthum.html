Poonspar Tree, scientifically known as Calophyllum polyanthum, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its dense timber and lush foliage, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various systemic ailments.
