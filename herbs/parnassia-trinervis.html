Parnassia trinervis

What's the taxonomical classification of Parnassia trinervis?

Parnassia trinervis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and falls within the order Celastrales. Furthermore, this plant is a member of the family Celastraceae, specifically positioned within the genus Parnassia, and is identified by its unique species designation, trinervis.
