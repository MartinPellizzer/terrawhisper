Aspidistra elatior

Aspidistra elatior, commonly known as the Cast-iron plant, is a resilient evergreen prized for its tough, glossy foliage. While primarily used as an ornamental plant due to its extreme durability, it holds traditional significance in various cultures, potentially offering medicinal properties through its unique, hardy biological composition.

  • Scientific name: Aspidistra elatior
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Aspidistra.
