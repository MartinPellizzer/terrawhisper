Neurada procumbens

Neurada procumbens, commonly known as the creeping neurada, is a medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various infections. This plant offers significant potential for developing new therapeutic agents in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Neurada procumbens
  • Family: Neuradaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Neuradaeae and falls under the genus Neurada.
