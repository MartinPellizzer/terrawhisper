This plant, known scientifically as Aristolochia fontanesii, possesses a very specific and restricted natural range. Its geographical distribution is limited to the northern part of the African continent. Specifically, this medicinal species can be found growing within the borders of Algeria. Because it is confined to this particular region, it is considered an endemic element of the local flora.
