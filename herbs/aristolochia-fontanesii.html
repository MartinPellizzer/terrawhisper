Aristolochia fontanesii

Aristolochia fontanesii, commonly known as Fontane's birthwort, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Characterized by its unique heart-shaped leaves and tubular flowers, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine. However, its use is strictly cautioned due to the presence of potent, nephrotoxic aristolochic acids.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia fontanesii
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Aristolochiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Aristolochia.
