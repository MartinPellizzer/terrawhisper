Alpinia officinarum

Alpinia officiinarum, commonly known as Galangal, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Renowned for its pungent aroma and spicy flavor, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and antioxidant properties. Traditionally used in Asian medicine, it effectively aids digestion, alleviates nausea, and supports overall digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Alpinia officinarum
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is classified under the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Alpinia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout specific regions of Southeast Asia and East Asia. In China, its presence is noted in Hainan and across China Southeast. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, it can be found growing in Cambodia. The species also inhabits various parts of Myanmar within the same region. Finally, its geographical range extends into Vietnam, completing its documented distribution.
