Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Eastern Asia, its presence is noted within the Nansei-shoto islands and throughout Taiwan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found extensively across India. Furthermore, the species is specifically documented in the Assam region.
