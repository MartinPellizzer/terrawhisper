Mappia nimmoniana

Mappia nimmoniana, commonly known as the Mappia plant, is a significant medicinal species valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional healing practices, it offers various bioactive compounds used to treat diverse ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and pharmacological potential is essential for exploring its role in natural medicine.
