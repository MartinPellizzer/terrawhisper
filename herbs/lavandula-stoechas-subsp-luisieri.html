Lavandula stoechas subsp. luisieri

Lavandula stoechas subsp. luisieri, commonly known as Spanish lavender, is a fragrant perennial native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its striking bracts and aromatic oils, this medicinal plant offers soothing properties. It is traditionally utilized in aromatherapy and herbal remedies to alleviate stress, promote relaxation, and support skin health.

  • Scientific name: Lavandula stoechas subsp. luisieri
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
