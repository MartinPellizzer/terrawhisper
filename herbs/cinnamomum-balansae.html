Cinnamomum balansae

Cinnamomum balansae, commonly known as Balansa's cinnamon, is a precious medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic bark, it contains potent bioactive compounds with significant antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. This species is highly valued in traditional medicine and pharmacology for its diverse therapeutic potential and healing qualities.

  • Scientific name: Cinnamomum balansae
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
