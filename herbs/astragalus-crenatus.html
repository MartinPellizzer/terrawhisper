Milk Vetch, scientifically known as Astragalus crenatus, is a resilient perennial legume valued in traditional herbalism. Primarily found in rocky habitats, this plant is recognized for its medicinal potential, often used to support immune function and vitality. Its unique botanical properties make it a significant subject for ethnobotanical study.
