Lindera benzoin

Lindra benzoin, commonly known as Benzoin, is a fragrant resin derived from the bark of trees in the genus Styrax. Renowned for its distinct balsamic aroma, it has been utilized for centuries in traditional medicine as an antiseptic, expectorant, and soothing agent for skin irritations and respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Lindera benzoin
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Laurales, it falls under the family Lauraceae. Specifically, it is categorized within the genus Lindera, commonly known as Lindera benzoin.
