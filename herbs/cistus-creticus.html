Pink rock-rose, scientifically known as Cistus creticus, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub celebrated for its therapeutic potential. Renowned for its fragrant blooms, this plant is rich in polyphenols and essential oils. It is traditionally used in herbal medicine for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties in skincare.
