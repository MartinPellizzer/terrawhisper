Cistus creticus

Pink rock-rose

Cistus creticus, commonly known as the pink rockrose, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub renowned for its therapeutic potential. Rich in polyphenols and essential oils, this plant is widely studied for its powerful antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable natural resource in traditional and modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cistus creticus
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Cistaceae and the genus Cistus. Therefore, its full scientific designation is Cistus creticus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Cistus creticus, is recognized by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In German, it is referred to as Kretische Ziastrose, while in Turkish, it is commonly called Tüylü laden. Its names also vary in Mediterranean languages, such as estepa de Creta in Catalan and muchju rossu in Corsican, and it is identified in Arabic as قريضة كريتية.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the Mediterranean region. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is noted on the islands of Corse and Sardegna. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species can also be found in Albania. Its range extends further through the Balkan Peninsula to include Bulgaria. Finally, the geographical reach of this medicinal plant encompasses Greece as well.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cistus creticus, exhibits a specialized morphology that is essential to its medicinal properties. The structure of the plant is characterized by its woody stems and a dense covering of leaves. Upon closer inspection of the leaf surfaces, one can observe a high concentration of multicellular glandular trichomes. These specialized hair-like structures are distributed across the leaf and are responsible for secreting the aromatic resins and essential oils that define the plant's therapeutic profile.
