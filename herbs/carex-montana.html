Mountain sedge, scientifically known as Carex montana, is a resilient perennial herb found in rocky, montane habitats. This plant, known as Carex montana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Poales and the family Cyperaceae. It is ultimately categorized under the genus Carex.
