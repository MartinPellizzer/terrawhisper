Crepis sancta

Crepis sancta, commonly known as the San Clemente hawk'sbeard, is a versatile annual herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Frequently found in disturbed habitats and open fields, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse phytochemical properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in various herbal remedies and pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Crepis sancta
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, Crepis sancta is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Crepis.
