Jatropha variegata

Jatropha variegata, commonly known as Variegated Euphorbiaceae, is a striking ornamental shrub valued for its aesthetic foliage and medicinal properties. Widely used in traditional medicine, its leaves and sap possess significant antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory qualities, offering potential therapeutic benefits for treating various skin ailments and inflammatory conditions.
