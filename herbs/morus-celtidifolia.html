Morus celtidifolia

Morus celtidifolia, commonly known as the climbing mulberry, is a versatile medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions. Its leaves and bark offer significant antioxidant benefits, supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Morus celtidifolia
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, it is further categorized into the family Moraceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Morus.
