Juniperus foetidissima

Juniperus foetidissima, commonly known as the stinking juniper, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to arid regions of Asia and Africa. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and medicinal properties, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues through its potent extracts.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus foetidissima
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, falling into the family Cupressaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Juniperus.
