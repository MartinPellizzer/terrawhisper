Stinking Juniper, scientifically known as Juniperus foetidissima, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to arid regions of Asia and Africa. Renowned for its distinctive, pungent aroma, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections, respiratory issues, and digestive disturbances through its bioactive compounds.
