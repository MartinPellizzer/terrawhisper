What's the taxonomical classification of Rosa banksiae?

Rosa banksiae belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Rosa, and its specific species epithet, banksiae.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rosa banksiae has vigorous, arching, deciduous canes that can reach lengths of up to 15 meters and are covered in small, recurved prickles. The foliage consists of pinnate leaves with five to seven ovate, serrated leaflets that provide a lush green backdrop for the blooms. Its most striking feature is the profusion of fragrant, single or semi-double flowers that appear in shades of pale yellow or deep pink. These blossoms are typically produced in large, dense clusters along the stems during the spring season. The overall growth habit is climbing and sprawling, allowing it to drape heavily over structures or trellises.

How is this plant cultivated?
