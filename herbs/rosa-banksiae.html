Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence can be specifically noted within the borders of Spain. Moving eastward, it is found within the Central Asian territory of Uzbekistan. Within China, the species is widely spread across the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast regions. This broad range highlights the plant's ability to inhabit various ecological zones throughout Asia and Europe.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Rosa banksiae or Lady Banks' rose, is primarily celebrated for its profuse and stunning display of blossoms. The flower is the most distinctive medicinal and aesthetic component of the species, typically appearing in shades of pale yellow or white. These fragrant blooms are often produced in large clusters, covering the climbing canes in a dense blanket of petals that serve as the plant's most vital feature for both visual beauty and traditional botanical use.
