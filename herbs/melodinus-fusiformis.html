Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast territories. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, the species can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Cambodia. It also maintains a notable population within the borders of Laos. Furthermore, the plant's range extends into the tropical regions of Thailand.
