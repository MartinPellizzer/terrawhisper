Buddleja thyrsoides

Buddleja thyrsoides, commonly known as the large-leaved butterfly bush, is a striking evergreen shrub native to China. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves are frequently utilized for their therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and skin ailments, offering a potent natural remedy within the diverse Buddleja genus.

  • Scientific name: Buddleja thyrsoides
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Common Names

This plant, Buddleja thyrsoides, is known by several different common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the False Butterfly Bush, a name that highlights its floral resemblance to other members of its genus. In Chinese, the plant is commonly called 醉मद (Zuì mǐn), while in Japanese, it is known as 酔芙蓉 (Suifuyō), both of which poetically allude to the changing appearance or intoxicating nature of its blossoms.
