Stangeria eriopus

Stangeria eriopus, commonly known as the Stangeria fern, is a unique, ancient cycad endemic to South Africa. Often referred to as a "living fossil," this striking plant features distinctive pinnate leaves. Beyond its botanical rarity, it holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices across its habitat.

  • Scientific name: Stangeria eriopus
  • Family: Zamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Cycadidae. Within the order Cycadales, it is classified under the family Zamiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Stangeria.
