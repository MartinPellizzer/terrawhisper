Malus baccata

Siberian crabapple

Malus baccata, commonly known as the Chinese hawthorn, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its nutrient-dense berries, it serves as a natural remedy for digestive ailments, supporting cardiovascular health and blood circulation. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Malus baccata
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
