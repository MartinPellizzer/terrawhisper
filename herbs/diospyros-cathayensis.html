What's the taxonomical classification of Diospyros cathayensis?

Diospyros cathayensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ericales. This plant is a representative of the family Ebenaceae and is further categorized under the genus Diospyros, with its specific designation being the species cathayensis.
