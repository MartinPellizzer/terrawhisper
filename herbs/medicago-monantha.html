Medicago monantha

Medicago monantha, commonly known as a member of the legume family, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers potential therapeutic benefits, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions. Understanding its botanical characteristics is essential for exploring its pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Medicago monantha
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Medicago monantha, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, following the order Fabales. Ultimately, it is a member of the Fabaceae family within the genus Medicago.
