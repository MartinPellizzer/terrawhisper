This plant, Phyllanthus urinaria, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Chamberbitter, possesses various anatomical features that are utilized in traditional medicine. The leaves are small, green, and arranged in two rows along the branches, often housing tiny seed capsules underneath. The stems are slender, branched, and upright, providing the structural support necessary for the plant's rapid growth. Below the surface, the roots serve to anchor the herb and absorb essential nutrients from the soil. Specifically, the entire root system is often harvested in herbal preparations to exploit its therapeutic properties.
