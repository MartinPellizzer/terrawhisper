Anabasis aphylla

Anabasis aphylla, commonly known as the leafless anahesis, is a resilient desert shrub native to the arid regions of the Middle East. Renowned for its unique physiological adaptations, this medicinal plant is studied for its potential bioactive compounds, offering promising insights into traditional herbal remedies and modern pharmacological research.
