Clinopodium nubigenum

Clinopodium nubigenum, commonly known as the cloud-born mint, is a distinctive aromatic herb found in high-altitude montane regions. Valued for its potent essential oils, this plant features unique medicinal properties used in traditional herbalism. Its refreshing scent and bioactive compounds make it a fascinating subject for botanical and therapeutic study.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
