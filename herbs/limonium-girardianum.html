Limonium girardianum

Limonium girardianum, commonly known as a member of the sea lavender family, is a distinctive medicinal plant found in coastal ecosystems. Renowned for its unique floral structure, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Researchers explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in treating various inflammatory and skin conditions.

  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
