Chinese asparagus

Asparagus cochinchinensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (243 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese asparagus, scientifically known as Asparagus cochinchiensis, is a versatile medicinal plant highly valued in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its restorative properties, the tuberous roots are frequently used to nourish the lungs, moisten dryness, and support kidney function, offering a natural remedy for various respiratory and systemic ailments.
