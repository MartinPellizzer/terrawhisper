Cnestis ferruginea

Cnestis ferruginea, commonly known as the rusty cestis, is a distinctive leguminous shrub native to the arid regions of North Africa and the Middle East. Characterized by its woody structure and unique foliage, this resilient plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest for its potential therapeutic properties in traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Cnestis ferruginea
  • Family: Connaraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Oxalidales and the family Connaraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cnestis, within which the species Cnestis ferruginea is situated.
