Catunaregam spinosa

Catunaregam spinosa, commonly known as the thorny catunar, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin disorders, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern healthcare and natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Catunaregam spinosa
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Gentianaales and the family Rubiaceae. Finally, the species is identified by its unique genus, Catunaregam.
