Phacelia campanularia

Phaceliia campanularia, commonly known as the "bush tea" or "wild bell," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin inflammations, offering a natural approach to holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Phacelia campanularia
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant, known scientifically as Phacelia campanularia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
