Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several key regions across East Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central and North-Central territories. Its presence is also documented in the Southeast regions of China and on the island of Hainan. Beyond the Chinese mainland, the species extends its range into the easternmost parts of the continent. Specifically, this medicinal plant is well-established throughout the various landscapes of Japan.
