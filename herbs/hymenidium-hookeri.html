Hymenidium hookeri

Hymeniidium hookeri, commonly known as hooker's seaweed, is a versatile red macroalga valued for its medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like polysaccharides and antioxidants, it exhibits significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing potential. This marine resource offers promising therapeutic applications in modern pharmacology and functional food development.

  • Scientific name: Hymenidium hookeri
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales and family Apiaceae, it is classified under the genus Hymeniidion.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. In Western Asia, its presence is recorded within the borders of Afghanistan. Moving towards the east, it can be found in China, specifically in the South-Central and Tibet regions. The species is also well-documented within the Indian Subcontinent, particularly in the East Himalaya. Additionally, its habitat extends to the mountainous terrains of Nepal.
