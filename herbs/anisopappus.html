Auisopappus

Anisopappus, commonly known as a genus within the Asteraceae family, comprises diverse herbaceous plants found primarily in tropical regions. Often recognized by their distinctive composite flower heads, these botanical specimens hold significant interest for ethnobotanists exploring their potential pharmacological properties and their unique ecological roles within specialized plant communities.

  • Scientific name: Anisopappus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Anisopappus.

Distribution

This plant is found within several distinct regions of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence is documented in the nation of Benin. It also grows across the landscapes of Guinea and the Ivory Coast. Furthermore, the distribution extends to the country of Mali. Finally, its geographical range includes the territory of Sierra Leone.
