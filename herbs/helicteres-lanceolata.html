Helicteres lanceolata

What's the taxonomical classification of Helicteres lanceolata?

Helicteres lanceolata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malvales. This plant is a specific representative of the family Malvaceae, placed within the genus Helicteres, and is identified by its unique species designation, lanceolata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Helicteres lanceolata has lanceolate, simple leaves with acute tips and serrated margins that grow in an opposite arrangement along its woody stems. The plant is a deciduous shrub characterized by its spreading branches and grayish-brown bark. Its flowers are typically bright yellow and possess a distinct tubular structure that transitions into five spreading lobes. Small, globose drupes develop from the ovaries after pollination, serving as the primary fruit type. These morphological features help distinguish the species within its genus.
