Bupleurum polyclonum

Bupleurum polyclonum, commonly known as Chinese Thoroughwort, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, it contains bioactive compounds like saikosaponins, which offer potent anti-inflammatory and hepatoprotective properties. This versatile plant is frequently utilized to soothe the liver and restore bodily equilibrium.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum polyclonum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
