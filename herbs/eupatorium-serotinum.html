Eupatorium serotinum

Eupatorium serotinum, commonly known as latefall boneset, is a perennial herb prized in traditional herbalism. Characterlızed by its delicate white flower clusters, this plant has long been utilized to support immune function and alleviate symptoms of seasonal ailments, offering a natural remedy rooted in deep botanical history and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Eupatorium serotinum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling into the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Eupatorium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Eupatorium serotinum, is recognized by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is most frequently referred to as latefall boneset or late boneset, reflecting its blooming period and historical medicinal uses. In Spanish, the plant is commonly known as huesoset tardío, and in French, it is referred to as éupatoire tardive.

Its presence extends across the Atlantic to Middle Europe, specifically within Germany. Remarkably, the species also reaches as far as the Southern Hemisphere in Queensland, Australia.
