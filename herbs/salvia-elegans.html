Pineapple sage, scientifically known as Salvia elegans, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its vibrant red tubular flowers and unique, fruity aroma. Native to Mexico, this aromatic plant offers a delightful scent reminiscent of ripe pineapples, making it a versatile choice for both ornamental gardens and medicinal purposes.
