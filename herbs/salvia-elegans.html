Salvia elegans

Salvia elegans, commonly known as the elegant sage, is a captivating perennial valued for its striking tubular flowers and aromatic foliage. Native to specific subtropical regions, this medicinal plant is celebrated in traditional wellness practices for its soothing properties, offering both aesthetic beauty and therapeutic potential in natural remedies.
