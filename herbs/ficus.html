The fig genus, scientifically known as Ficus, is a diverse group of flowering plants celebrated for both their fruit and medicinal properties. Beyond nutrition, various species offer therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant effects. Traditionally used in holistic medicine, Ficus species support digestive health and skin wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the fig tree, possesses various parts that are utilized for their unique biological properties. The most distinctive feature is the syconium, a specialized structure that houses the tiny flowers within a fleshy receptacle. These structures eventually develop into the edible fruits, which are widely referred to as figs. The leaves of the Ficus are often large and leathery, serving as a vital component in many traditional medicinal preparations. Additionally, when the stem or leaves are damaged, the plant exudes a milky white latex, a substance that is frequently studied and used in various therapeutic applications.
