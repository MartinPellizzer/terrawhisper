Ficus

Ficus, commonly known as fig trees, encompasses a diverse genus of flowering plants celebrated globally. Beyond their ornamental beauty, many species possess significant medicinal properties. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues and respiratory discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Ficus
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Moraceae, it is classified under the genus Ficus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented in Denmark and Great Britain.
