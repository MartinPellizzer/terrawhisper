Actinodaphne obovata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Laurales. The plant is a member of the family Lauraceae, which contains the genus Actinodaphne, and is specifically identified by the species epithet obovatas.
