Juniperus seravschanica

Juniperus seravschanica, commonly known as the Seravshan juniper, is a resilient coniferous species native to the high-altitude regions of Central Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, its aromatic berries and essential oils possess potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a vital resource for holistic healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus seravschanica
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling into the family Cupressaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Juniperus as Juniperus seravschanica.
