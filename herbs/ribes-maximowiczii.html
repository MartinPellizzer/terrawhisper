What's the taxonomical classification of Ribes maximowiczii?

Ribes maximowiczii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical structure, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Saxifragales and is a member of the family Grossulariaceae. Ultimately, it is defined by its specific genus, Ribes, and its unique species designation, maximowiczii.
