Prunus mahaleb

Prunus mahaleb, commonly known as Mahaleb cherry, is a versatile deciduous shrub valued in both traditional and modern herbalism. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and sedative properties. This resilient plant serves as a vital resource in holistic wellness and natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Prunus mahaleb
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling within the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established under the genus Prunus.
