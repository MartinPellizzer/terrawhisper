Macleaya cordata

Macleaya cordata, commonly known as plume poppy, is a robust perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its large, heart-shaped leaves and striking yellow blooms, this plant contains potent alkaloids. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, its bioactive compounds offer significant pharmacological potential for treating various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Macleaya cordata
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ranunculales, it is a member of the Papaveraceae family. Its specific taxonomic designation falls under the genus Macleaya, specifically the species Macleaya cordata.
