Plant Parts

This plant, Capparis spinosa subsp. rupestris, exhibits distinct morphological features across its various organs. The leaves are typically small, fleshy, and somewhat leathery, often appearing in a scattered arrangement along the thorny stems to help the plant conserve moisture in arid environments. The flowers are large and strikingly beautiful, characterized by long, prominent stamens that create a delicate, feathery appearance when fully opened. Before reaching this stage of bloom, the plant produces numerous flower buttons, which are tight, rounded buds that protect the developing reproductive structures until they are ready to unfurl.
