Capparis spinosa subsp. rupestris

Capparis spinosa subsp. rupestris, commonly known as the Caper bush, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub celebrated for its medicinal properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, this hardy plant offers diverse therapeutic benefits, ranging from digestive support to skin healing, making it a vital asset in traditional herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Capparis spinosa subsp. rupestris
  • Family: Capparaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales, it is classified under the family Capparaceae and the genus Capparis. As a subspecies, Capparis spinosa subsp. rupestris represents a specific taxonomic grouping within this lineage.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across the Mediterranean basin, spanning both Southwestern and Southeastern Europe. In the Southwestern region, it can be found throughout Spain and the Balearic Islands. Its presence also extends to the island of Sardinia. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species is established in Albania. Finally, its range continues through the various territories of Greece.
