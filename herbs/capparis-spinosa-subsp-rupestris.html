Rock Capers, scientifically known as Capparis spinosa subsp. rupestris, is a resilient, drought-tolerant shrub native to Mediterranean ecosystems. Renowned for its edible flower buds and medicinal properties, this hardy plant offers antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. It thrives in rocky terrains, embodying strength and traditional healing through its unique bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across the Mediterranean basin, spanning both Southwestern and Southeastern Europe. In the Southwestern region, it can be found throughout Spain and the Balearic Islands. Its presence also extends to the island of Sardinia. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species is established in Albania. Finally, its range continues through the various territories of Greece.
