Piper boehmeriifolium var. boehmeriifolium

Piper boehmeriifolium var. boehmeriifolium, commonly known as the Boehmeria pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and infections. This botanical specimen plays a significant role in promoting holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Piper boehmeriifolium var. boehmeriifolium
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, falling into the family Piperaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Piper as the specific variety boehmeriifolium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is native to the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is widely distributed across the country of Bangladesh. Finally, it is also documented growing within the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya.
