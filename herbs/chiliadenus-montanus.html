Chiliadenus montanus

Chiliadenus montanus, commonly known as the mountain chiliadenus, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific highland ecosystems. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat inflammatory conditions and support digestive health, offering significant potential for contemporary pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa
