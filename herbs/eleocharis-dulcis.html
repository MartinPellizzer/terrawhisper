Chinese water-chestnut, scientifically known as Eleocharis dulcis, is a versatile aquatic plant prized in traditional medicine and culinary practices. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is used to alleviate inflammation and support digestive health. This nutritious tuber offers significant bioactive compounds, making it a valuable resource in herbal wellness.
