Eleocharis dulcis

Eleocharis dulcis, commonly known as water soldier, is a versatile aquatic plant valued in traditional medicine. Found in freshwater habitats, it is prized for its nutritional properties and therapeutic potential. Studies suggest it may offer antioxidant benefits, supporting holistic wellness through its unique bioactive compounds and restorative herbal qualities.

  • Scientific name: Eleocharis dulcis
  • Family: Cyperaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Poales, and the family Cyperaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Eleocharis.
