African Laburnum, scientifically known as Cassia sieberiana, is a striking deciduous tree native to various African regions. Renowned for its vibrant, cascading yellow blooms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin conditions to digestive issues.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence has been documented in the nation of Benin. It can also be found growing within the borders of Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species is located in Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends to include Guinea-Bissau.
