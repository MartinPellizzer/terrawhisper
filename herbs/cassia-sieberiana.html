Cassia sieberiana

Cassia sieberiana, commonly known as the Sieber's Cassia, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various African landscapes. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Cassia sieberiana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Cassia.
