African Laburnum, scientifically known as Cassia sieberiana, is a striking deciduous tree native to various African regions. Renowned for its vibrant, cascading yellow blooms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin conditions to digestive issues.
