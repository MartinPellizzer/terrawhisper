Erythrina burana

Erythrina burana, commonly known as the Coral Tree, is a striking deciduous shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its vibrant, crimson flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Erythrina burana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Erythrina burana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and the genus Erythrina.
