Duboisia myoporoides

Duboisia myoporoides, commonly known as Soapbush, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Australia. Renowned for its potent alkaloid content, particularly scopolamine and hyoscyamine, it serves as a vital botanical source for pharmaceutical manufacturing. These compounds are essential in producing treatments for motion sickness and various neurological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Duboisia myoporoides
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Duboisia.
