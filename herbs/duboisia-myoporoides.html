Corkwoodtree, scientifically known as Duboisia myoporoides, is a versatile Australian shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Rich in tropane alkaloids like scopolamine and hyoscyamine, it serves as a vital industrial source for pharmaceutical manufacturing. These compounds are essential in treating various neurological and gastrointestinal conditions worldwide.
