Plant Parts

This plant, Corymbia terminalis, is known for producing a distinct medicinal substance known as kino. This kino is a dark, resinous exudate that seeps from the bark and stems of the tree, often appearing as droplets or streaks on the trunk. In traditional medicinal practices, this kino is harvested and utilized for its various properties, typically serving as a topical treatment for skin ailments or as an astringent to help manage internal issues.
