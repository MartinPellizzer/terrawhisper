Anonychium africanum, scientifically known as Anonychium africanum, is a significant medicinal climbing plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammation, offering a natural resource for community healthcare and pharmacological research.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Within this specific zone, its presence is documented in the nation of Benin. It can also be found growing throughout the territories of Burkina and the Gambia. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the country of Ghana. Finally, its geographical range includes the coastal area of Guinea-Bissau.
