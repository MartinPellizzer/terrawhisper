Mutarda carinata

Mutarda carinaata, commonly known as the Carinate Mustard, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. This resilient herb serves as a vital natural resource for holistic wellness and pharmacology.
