What's the taxonomical classification of Commicarpus chinensis?

Commicarpus chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and follows the order Caryophyllales. The plant is a member of the family Nyctaginaceae, specifically falling under the genus Commicarpus, which ultimately defines its classification as the species chinensis.
