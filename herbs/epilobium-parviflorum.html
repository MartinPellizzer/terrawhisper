Small-flowered hairy willowherb, scientifically known as Epilobium parviflorum, is a delicate perennial herb native to various regions. Renowned for its fine, hairy stems and tiny pink blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, its properties have been utilized in herbal medicine to address various inflammatory skin conditions.
