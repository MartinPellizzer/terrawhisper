Epilobium parviflorum

Epilobium parviflorum, commonly known as small-flowered willowherb, is a resilient perennial herb native to diverse landscapes. Valued in traditional herbalism, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. Its medicinal potential lies in its diverse phytochemical profile, offering therapeutic possibilities for various health conditions through its natural, restorative botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Epilobium parviflorum
  • Family: Onagraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtales, and the family Onagraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Epilobium.
