Nepeta menthoides

Nepeta menthoides, commonly known as Minty Nepeta, is a resilient perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic foliage. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this plant offers distinct menthol-like scents. Traditionally valued in herbal practices, it serves as an ornamental treasure, blending therapeutic potential with striking visual appeal in diverse gardens.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta menthoides
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Nepeta.
