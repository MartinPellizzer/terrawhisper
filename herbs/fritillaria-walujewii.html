Fritillaria walujewii

Fritillaria walujewii, commonly known as the Walujewi's fritillary, is a rare medicinal plant indigenous to specific high-altitude regions. Renowned in traditional ethnobotanical practices, it possesses unique bioactive compounds. Scientific investigation continues to explore its potential pharmacological applications, particularly regarding its therapeutic properties in treating various localized ailments.

Common Names

This plant, Fritillaria walujewii, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In Azerbaijani, it is referred to as Valuyev zanbaqcası, while in Chinese, it is commonly called 新疆贝母 or 天山贝母. Specifically, in simplified Chinese (zh-cn), the name is 新疆贝母, whereas in traditional Chinese (zh-tw), it is written as 新疆貝母.
