Alchornea coelophylla

Alchornea coelophylla, commonly known as "clavo de olor" in some regions, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical landscapes. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Alchornea coelophylla
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
