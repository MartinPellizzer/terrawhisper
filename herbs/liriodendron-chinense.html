Liriodendron chinense

Liriodenroden chinense, commonly known as Golden Lotus, is a significant medicinal herb in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, it is primarily used to treat heat-related ailments, clear toxins, and alleviate swelling. This versatile plant offers valuable therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Liriodendron chinense
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Lirodendron.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical presence across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its distribution is notably broad, spanning from the South-Central areas to the North-Central provinces. It is also commonly found throughout the Southeast regions of China. Beyond Chinese borders, the species extends its range into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the territory of Vietnam.
