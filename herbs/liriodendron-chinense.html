Chinese tulip tree, scientifically known as Liorioendron chinense, is a majestic deciduous species valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its large, fragrant blossoms, various parts of the tree are utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues. It remains a significant botanical resource in diverse herbal therapeutic practices.
