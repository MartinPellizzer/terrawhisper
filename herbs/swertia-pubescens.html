Swertia pubescens

Swertia pubescentis, commonly known as Swertia, is a potent medicinal herb renowned in Ayurvedic medicine. Characterized by its small, delicate flowers, this plant contains unique bitter principles like xanthones. It is widely utilized for its hepatoprotective, digestive, and anti-inflammatory properties, playing a vital role in treating various metabolic ailments.
