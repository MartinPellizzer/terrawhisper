Indian sorrel

Oxalis corniculata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (88 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Indian sorrel, scientifically known as Oxalis corniculata, is a versatile perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Characterized by its delicate, heart-shaped leaves and vibrant yellow flowers, it offers a tangy flavor. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant plays a vital role in various herbal remedies.

Plant Parts

This plant, Oxalis corniculata, is a versatile medicinal species where the whole plant is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties. The leaves are the most prominent feature, often consumed as nutritious leafy vegetables in various culinary traditions. Upon closer inspection, the individual leaf parts exhibit a characteristic trifoliate structure, which is essential for its identification. In many herbal preparations, the aerial parts are harvested and dried to create infusions or extracts, ensuring that the bioactive compounds found above the ground are effectively captured for medicinal use.
