Oxalis corniculata

Oxalis corniculata, commonly known as the creeping woodsorrel, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its delicate, heart-shaped leaflets, this plant is widely recognized for its tangy flavor and therapeutic potential, often utilized to address digestive issues and support overall wellness through its bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Oxalis corniculata
  • Family: Oxalidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Oxalidales, it is a member of the family Oxalidaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Oxalis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the northern regions of Europe. In the northernmost reaches, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Finland. Moving slightly south, the species is also documented across the territories of Norway and Sweden. Its presence extends westward to include the islands of Great Britain and Ireland. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's preference for Northern European environments.
