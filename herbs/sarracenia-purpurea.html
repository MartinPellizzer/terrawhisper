Sarracenia purpurea

Sarracenia purpurea, commonly known as the purple pitcher plant, is a fascinating carnivorous perennial native to North America. Characterized by its unique, deep-red tubular leaves, it traps insects in digestive fluids to supplement nutrient-poor soil. This resilient species is a striking marvel of evolutionary adaptation in wetland ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Sarracenia purpurea
  • Family: Sarraceniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is a member of the family Sarraceniaceae. It is specifically classified under the genus Sarracenia, with the species name purpurea.
