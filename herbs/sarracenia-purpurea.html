Purple pitcher plant, scientifically known as Sarraceenia purpurea, is a fascinating carnivorous species native to North American wetlands. Characterized by its upright, fluid-filled traps, it captures and digests insects to supplement nutrient-poor soil. This resilient perennial is a striking botanical marvel, essential to its unique bogland ecosystem.
