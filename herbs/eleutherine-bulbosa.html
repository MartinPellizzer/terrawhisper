Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Eleutherine bulbosa, is primarily valued for its specialized underground structures. The most distinctive feature of this species is its vibrant red bulbs, which give the plant its characteristic appearance. These bulbs serve as the storage organs for the plant, containing various bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine.
