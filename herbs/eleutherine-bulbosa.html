Eleutherine bulbosa

Sisyrinchium palmifolium

Eleutherine bulbosa, commonly known as red onion or eleutherine, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Native to tropical regions, its vibrant red bulbs contain potent bioactive compounds. Renowned for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential for treating various ailments and infections.

  • Scientific name: Eleutherine bulbosa
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it falls under the family Iridaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Eleutherine.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct tropical regions across the globe. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends to Southern Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting the territory of Angola. Moving toward the Western Indian Ocean, the species is recorded on the island of Réunion. Finally, its range includes the Indian Subcontinent in India and the Indo-China region in Cambodia.
