Schleichera oleosa

Schleicherha oleosa, commonly known as the Ceylon Mahua, is a versatile medicinal tree native to South Asia. Renowned for its nutrient-rich seeds and flowers, it offers significant therapeutic benefits. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, this plant serves as a vital resource in holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Schleichera oleosa
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Schleichera.
