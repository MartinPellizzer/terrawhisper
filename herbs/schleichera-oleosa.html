Ceylon-oak, scientifically known as Schleichera oleosa, is a versatile, deciduous tree native to the dry deciduous forests of India. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it offers therapeutic benefits for skin ailments and digestive issues. Additionally, its nutrient-rich seeds provide valuable oils used extensively in traditional Ayurvedic practices.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Bidara tree, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The stem serves as the structural foundation of the tree, supporting its spreading canopy and facilitating the transport of nutrients essential for growth. Additionally, the fruits are a significant part of the plant, often being edible and containing bioactive compounds that contribute to its medicinal profile.
