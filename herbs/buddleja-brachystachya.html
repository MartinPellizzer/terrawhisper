Buddleja brachystachya

Buddleja brachystaqa, commonly known as a member of the butterfly bush family, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional practices. Native to specific subtropical regions, it possesses unique phytochemical properties. Research explores its potential therapeutic applications, particularly for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects, highlighting its significant pharmacological importance.

  • Scientific name: Buddleja brachystachya
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further categorized into the family Scrophulariaceae. Specifically, Buddleja brachystachya is identified by its genus, Buddleja.
