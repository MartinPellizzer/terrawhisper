Linzia glabra

Linzia glabra, commonly known as the smooth linzia, is a fascinating medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Often found in specific tropical ecosystems, this botanical species is studied for its bioactive compounds. Understanding its traditional uses and chemical profile is essential for advancing natural pharmacology and herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Linzia glabra
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Linzia and the specific species name glabra.

Common Names

This plant, Linzia glabra, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as smooth linzia, while in Spanish, it is known as linzia lisa. In Portuguese, the plant is called linzia lisa, and in French, it is referred to as linzia lisse.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Cameroon and the DR Congo. Moving towards East Tropical Africa, the species can be found in Kenya and Tanzania. Additionally, the plant extends its range into South Tropical Africa, specifically within Angola. This diverse spread highlights its ability to thrive in multiple tropical environments.
