Life history

The life history of Linzia glabra is characterized by its classification as a therophyte, a life form defined by its annual lifecycle where the plant completes its entire developmental process from germination to seed production within a single growing season. As a therophyte, it survives unfavorable environmental periods in the form of resistant seeds, which remain dormant in the soil until conditions become optimal for growth. This strategy allows the species to exploit transient periods of moisture and nutrient availability, ensuring that the vegetative structures do not need to persist through harsh seasonal changes. Its biological cycle is marked by rapid growth, flowering, and seed dispersal, effectively bypassing the need for perennial structures such as woody stems or underground rhizomes.
