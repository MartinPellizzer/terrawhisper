Kirkia acuminata

Witsering

Kirkia acuminata, commonly known as the Kirkia tree, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to the arid regions of East Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Kirkia acuminata
  • Family: Kirkiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Sapindales and placed in the family Kirkiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Kirkia, specifically the species Kirkia acuminata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its distribution includes the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward East Tropical Africa, the species can be found within the borders of Tanzania. Its reach extends significantly into South Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the nation of Angola. Finally, the plant is also established in the southern regions of Malawi and Mozambique.
