Vochysia divergens

Vochysia divergens, commonly known as the Golden Vochysia, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical landscapes. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its potent bioactive properties to treat diverse ailments and support holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Vochysia divergens
  • Family: Vochysiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
