Dracocephalum polychaetum

Dracocephalum polychaetum, commonly known as the many-colored dragonhead, is a distinctive perennial herb within the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its vibrant, multi-hued floral spikes, this plant possesses significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its purported antimicrobial, antioxidant, and soothing therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Dracocephalum polychaetum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Dracocephalum polychaetum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Dracocephalum.
