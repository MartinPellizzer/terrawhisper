Garcinia hanburyi

Garcinia hanburyi, commonly known as the Hanbury mangosteen, is a tropical medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its unique bioactive compounds, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Research highlights its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it a subject of great interest in modern pharmacological studies and traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia hanburyi
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling within the family Clusiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Garcinia, specifically the species Garcinia hanburyi.

Plant Parts

This plant, Garcinia hanburyi, is valued for its various exudates which are utilized in traditional medicinal practices. The primary therapeutic components are derived from the resin that flows from the bark and stem, as well as the dry latex that accumulates on the surface of the plant during certain stages of its growth. When processed for storage or specific medicinal formulations, the dried resin is often used to ensure stability and controlled application in treating various ailments.
