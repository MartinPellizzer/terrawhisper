Cressa cretica

Cressa cretica, commonly known as the Cretan cress, is a resilient medicinal herb native to Mediterranean coastal regions. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is frequently studied in ethnobotany for its potential therapeutic benefits, offering promising insights into natural treatments for various inflammatory skin conditions.

  • Scientific name: Cressa cretica
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Cressa.
