Distribution

This plant, known as Cressa cretica, exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within Southwestern Europe. Its presence is documented across several key Mediterranean islands, including the Baleares, Corse, and Sardegna. Beyond these islands, the species can also be found on the mainland in France. Additionally, its range extends further west to include the coastal regions of Portugal. Collectively, these locations highlight its preference for the unique climates of the Southwestern European region.
