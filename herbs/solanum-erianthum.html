Solanum erianthum

Solanum erianthum, commonly known as a member of the nightshade family, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising therapeutic potential for modern drug development and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Solanum erianthum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification concludes with the genus Solanum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Solanum erianthum, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Simplified Chinese (zh-hans), Traditional Chinese (zh-hant), and the Cantonese dialect used in Hong Kong (zh-hk), it is called 假烟叶树 (or 假煙葉樹). In Indonesia, the species is commonly known as Terung teter (id), while in Spanish-speaking contexts, it is referred to as Solanum verbascifolium (es).

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Its presence is also documented throughout the nation of Ghana. Furthermore, the species inhabits parts of Guinea and the Ivory Coast. Finally, its geographical range extends into Liberia.
