Randia echinocarpa

Randia echinocarpa, commonly known as wild indigo or indigo plant, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to subtropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern herbal studies.

  • Scientific name: Randia echinocarpa
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, specifically within the family Rubiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Randia.
