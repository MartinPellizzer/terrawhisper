Chlorophytum borivilianum

Chlorophycum borivilianum, commonly known as Safed Musli, is a highly valued medicinal herb in Ayurveda. Renowned for its potent adaptogenic properties, this plant is primarily used to enhance vitality, boost immunity, and manage stress. Its tuberous roots serve as a natural tonic, supporting reproductive health and overall physical strength.

  • Scientific name: Chlorophytum borivilianum
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is a member of the Asparagaceae family. Finally, its scientific classification is completed by its designation in the genus Chlorophyton.
