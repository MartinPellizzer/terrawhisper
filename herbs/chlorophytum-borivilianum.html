Safed musli, scientifically known as Chlorophytum borivilianum, is a prized medicinal herb native to India. Renowned in Ayurveda as a potent rejuvenator, it is highly valued for its adaptogenic properties. This plant is primarily used to enhance vitality, support reproductive health, and boost overall immunity and physical strength.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Safed Musli, derives its therapeutic value primarily from its underground structures. The medicinal potency is concentrated within the fleshy roots, which are often modified into swollen, starchy tubers that serve as energy storage organs. Within this specialized root tissue, a rich concentration of saponins and alkaloids is found, making the entire root a vital source for traditional medicine. Consequently, most pharmacological applications involve the preparation of concentrated root extracts to harness the plant's various health-promoting properties.
