Sea-buckthorn

Hippophae rhamnoides · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (779 studies) · ★★★★★

Sea-buckthorn, scientifically known as Hippophae rhamnoides, is a resilient shrub celebrated for its nutrient-dense orange berries. Rich in vitamins C, E, and rare omega fatty acids, it offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. Traditionally used in holistic medicine, it supports skin health, immune function, and cardiovascular wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Hippophae rhamnoides, commonly known as sea buckthorn, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties across its entire anatomy. The most prominent medicinal component is the bright orange fruit, specifically the nutrient-dense berries, which are packed with vitamins and antioxidants. Within these berries, the extracted seed oil provides essential fatty acids that are beneficial for skin health and inflammation. Additionally, the leaves contain various bioactive compounds that can be utilized in traditional remedies, making the whole plant a versatile resource in herbal medicine.
