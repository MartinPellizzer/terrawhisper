Hippophae rhamnoides

Hippoe rhamnoides, commonly known as sea buckthorn, is a resilient shrub celebrated for its nutrient-dense orange berries. Rich in vitamins C, E, and rare omega fatty acids, it offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. Widely used in skincare and traditional medicine, this plant promotes healing and cellular regeneration.

  • Scientific name: Hippophae rhamnoides
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Rosales and the family Elaeagnaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hippophae, which includes the species Hippophae rhamnoides.
