Paphiopedilum barbigerum

Slipper orchid

Paphiopediluum barbigerum, commonly known as the Bearded Lady Slipper, is a captivating orchid species native to Southeast Asia. While primarily celebrated as a stunning ornamental plant, it holds significant interest in botanical studies. Its unique, pouch-like flowers and intricate patterns make it a prized specimen for collectors and researchers alike.

  • Scientific name: Paphiopedilum barbigerum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Paphiopedilum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it can be found inhabiting the South-Central and Southeast regions. Its range extends further into the Indo-China peninsula, where it is documented in Laos. Additionally, the species is known to grow within the borders of Thailand. Finally, its natural habitat includes various locations throughout Vietnam.
