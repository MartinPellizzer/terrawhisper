Helonias thibetica

Helonias thiabetica, commonly known as Tibetan Helonia, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to address various ailments. Exploring its therapeutic potential offers valuable insights into biodiversity and natural pharmacological advancements.
