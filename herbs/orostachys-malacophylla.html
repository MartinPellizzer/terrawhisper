Orostachys malacophylla

Orostachys malacophylla, commonly known as the soft-leaf Orostachys, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific highland ecosystems, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to alleviate inflammation and support digestive health, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Orostachys malacophylla
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across the northern landscapes of Russia. Within the vast expanse of Siberia, its presence is noted in the regions of Buryatiya and Chitа. It can also be found throughout the Irkutsk and Yakutiya areas of the Siberian wilderness. Beyond these territories, the species extends its reach into the Russian Far East. Specifically, its distribution includes the Amur region, marking its presence in these diverse habitats.
