Ligustrum vulgare

Ligustrum vulgare, commonly known as Wild Privet, is a deciduous shrub valued for its diverse medicinal properties. Historically used in traditional practices, its leaves and berries contain bioactive compounds with potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects. Understanding its phytochemical profile is essential for exploring its therapeutic applications in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Ligustrum vulgare
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it resides in the family Oleaceae and is identified by the genus Ligustrum.
