What's the taxonomical classification of Meehania fargesii?

Meehania fargesii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It further falls under the order Lamiales and belongs to the family Lamiaceae. At the most specific taxonomic levels, the plant is identified by the genus Meehania and the species fargesii.
