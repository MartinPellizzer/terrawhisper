Artemisia aucheri

Artemisia aucheri, commonly known as Acher's wormwood, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the mountainous regions of Iran. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat digestive ailments and skin conditions, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia aucheri
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Artemisia aucheri, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Artemisia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In Western Asia, it can be found growing within the territories of both Afghanistan and Iran. Its range extends further east into China, specifically inhabiting the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Additionally, the species is present within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically located in the West Himalaya. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's preference for diverse mountainous environments.
