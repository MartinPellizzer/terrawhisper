Pistacia

Pistacia, commonly known as the mastic or terebinth tree, is a versatile genus prized for its medicinal and aromatic properties. Renowned for its resinous extracts, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits. Traditionally used in various cultures, these resilient plants continue to inspire modern therapeutic research and applications.

  • Scientific name: Pistacia
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pistacia.
