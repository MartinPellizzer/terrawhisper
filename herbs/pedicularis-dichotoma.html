Pedicularis dichotoma

What's the taxonomical classification of Pedicularis dichotoma?

Pedicularis dichotoma belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. At the class level, it is categorized as Equisetopsida, falling under the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is a member of the order Lamiales and is situated within the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement in the genus Pedicularis, with its specific designation being the species dichotoma.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pedicularis dichotoma has an erect, unbranched stem that typically reaches heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and is characterized by oppositely arranged, lanceolate leaves with serrated margins. The foliage is primarily basal, forming a rosette, while the cauline leaves are smaller and decrease in size as they ascend the stem. Its inflorescence consists of a dense, terminal spike of tubular, bilabiate flowers that display a range of colors from pink to deep purple. Each individual flower possesses a prominent upper lip that is often hooded or galea-shaped, which is a distinguishing feature of the genus. The plant also features a fibrous root system that allows it to anchor effectively in its alpine or subalpine habitats.
