Mallotus apelta

Mallotus apelta, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, is highly valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammatory conditions, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Mallotus apelta
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Mallotus apelta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Mallotus.
