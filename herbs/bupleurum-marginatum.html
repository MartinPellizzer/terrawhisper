Bupleurum marginatum

Bupleurum marginatum, commonly known as marginate bupleurum, is a resilient perennial herb native to Mediterranean landscapes. Valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties, this plant contains essential bioactive compounds. It is primarily studied for its anti-inflammatory and adaptogenic potential, offering unique botanical benefits for holistic wellness approaches.

  • Scientific name: Bupleurum marginatum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Bupleurum, specifically as the species Bupleurum marginatum.

Common Names

This plant, Bupleurum marginatum, is known by several common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese (zh), it is referred to as both 竹叶柴胡 and 柴胡. Specifically, in Simplified Chinese (zh-cn and zh-hans), the name is written as 竹叶柴胡, while in Traditional Chinese (zh-tw), it is recorded as 竹葉柴胡.
