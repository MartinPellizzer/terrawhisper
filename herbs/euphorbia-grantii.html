African milk bush, scientifically known as Euphorbia grantii, is a striking succulent native to South Africa. Renowned for its dramatic, bright red bracts and architectural form, it is a popular ornamental plant. While visually captivating, it produces a milky latex that requires careful handling due to its irritant properties.
