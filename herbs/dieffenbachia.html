scientifically known as Dieffenbachia, is a popular tropical houseplant celebrated for its striking, variegated foliage. With minimal care, it remains a stunning, statement-making addition to any collection.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several tropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its distribution extends to the Western Indian Ocean, specifically within the Comoros and the Seychelles. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is documented in Bangladesh. Finally, its presence reaches into Malesia, where it grows on the island of Borneo.
