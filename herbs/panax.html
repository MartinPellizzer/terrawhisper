Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide natural range across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing within the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Its distribution extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the Manchuria region. Furthermore, the species is widespread throughout China's North-Central and South-Central areas. Collectively, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of the Panax genus.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as ginseng, is highly valued for its medicinal properties, which are primarily concentrated in its roots. The most sought-after component is the ginseng root, often referred to as the root itself, which is harvested for its potent bioactive compounds. While the medicinal focus remains on the underground parts, the plant also features distinctive green leaves that grow in a palmate arrangement and sturdy stems that support the foliage and eventual flowering structures.
