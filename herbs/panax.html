Panax

Panax, commonly known as Ginseng, is a renowned medicinal herb celebrated for its potent adaptogenic properties. Belonging to the Araliaceae family, these roots are used globally to enhance energy, reduce stress, and boost immune function. Whether used in traditional practices or modern wellness, Panax remains a cornerstone of herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Panax
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide natural range across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing within the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Its distribution extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the Manchuria region. Furthermore, the species is widespread throughout China's North-Central and South-Central areas. Collectively, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of the Panax genus.
