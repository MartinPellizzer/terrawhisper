Simarouba amara

Simarouba amara, commonly known as Marupá, is a versatile medicinal tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat inflammation, fever, and skin ailments. Its bark and leaves offer significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Simarouba amara
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Sapindales and the family Simaroubaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Simarouba.
