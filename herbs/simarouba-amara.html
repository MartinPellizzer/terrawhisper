Bitterwood, scientifically known as Simarouba amar Amara, is a versatile medicinal tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its intense bitterness, the plant's bark and leaves are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat parasitic infections, digestive ailments, and inflammation, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutics.
