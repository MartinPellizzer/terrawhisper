Dipterocarpus littoralis

Dipterocarpus littoralis, commonly known as the coastal dipterocarp, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical coastal ecosystems. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical properties, it is traditionally utilized in various ethnobotanical practices to treat ailments. Research continues to explore its potential therapeutic applications in modern pharmacology and wellness.
