Tilia platyphyllos

Large-leaved lime

Tilia platyphyllos, commonly known as the Large-leaved Linden, is a deciduous tree prized for its therapeutic properties. Its fragrant flowers are widely used in herbal medicine to create soothing teas. Renowned for their calming effects, these blossoms help alleviate anxiety, reduce fever, and support digestive health naturally.

  • Scientific name: Tilia platyphyllos
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Tilia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the large-leaved lime, utilizes several distinct components for its medicinal applications. The broad, heart-shaped leaves are a characteristic feature of the species, but the most therapeutically significant parts are found within the inflorescence. Each individual flower is small and fragrant, contributing to the plant's sweet aroma. These blossoms are uniquely accompanied by specialized, pale-colored bracts that extend from the stem. When harvested, it is typically these flowers and their attached bracts that are dried to create soothing herbal infusions.
