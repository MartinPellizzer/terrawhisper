Large-leaved lime, scientifically known as Tilia platyphyllos, is a deciduous tree prized for its fragrant, heart-shaped leaves and nectar-rich blossoms. Often used in herbal medicine, its flowers produce soothing infusions traditionally utilized to relieve anxiety, aid sleep, and alleviate common respiratory ailments through their gentle, calming properties.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the large-leaved lime, utilizes several distinct components for its medicinal applications. The broad, heart-shaped leaves are a characteristic feature of the species, but the most therapeutically significant parts are found within the inflorescence. Each individual flower is small and fragrant, contributing to the plant's sweet aroma. These blossoms are uniquely accompanied by specialized, pale-colored bracts that extend from the stem. When harvested, it is typically these flowers and their attached bracts that are dried to create soothing herbal infusions.
