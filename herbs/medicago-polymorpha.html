Medicago polymorpha

Burr medick

Medicago polymorpha, commonly known as polymorphous medic, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Fabaceae family. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Its unique chemical profile offers significant potential for developing modern therapeutic treatments and natural remedies.
