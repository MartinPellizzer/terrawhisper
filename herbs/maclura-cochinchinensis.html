Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spread across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast regions. It can also be found in the tropical landscapes of Hainan and the high-altitude environments of Tibet. Expanding beyond mainland China, the species is documented in the island nation of Japan. Collectively, these locations highlight the broad distribution of Maclurura cochinchiensis throughout the eastern part of the continent.
