Maclura cochinchinensis

Maclurura cochinchiensis, commonly known as the Cochineal tree, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its bioactive properties. Native to tropical regions, its bark and fruits contain potent phytochemicals used in traditional remedies to treat inflammation and skin ailments, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and natural therapeutics.

  • Scientific name: Maclura cochinchinensis
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Moraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Maclurura and the species Maclurura cochinchinensis.
