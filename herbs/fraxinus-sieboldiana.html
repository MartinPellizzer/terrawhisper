Fraxinus sieboldiana

Fraxinus sieboldiana, commonly known as Siebold's ash, is a resilient deciduous tree valued for its striking foliage and ecological significance. While primarily ornamental, various parts of the ash tree have historical roots in traditional medicine, often utilized for their potential anti-inflammatory and restorative properties in various herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Fraxinus sieboldiana
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified in the family Oleaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Fraxinus, specifically as the species Fraxinus sieboldiana.

Common Names

This plant, Fraxinus sieboldiana, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In Germany, it is referred to as Siebolds Blumen-Esche, while in Japan, it is called マルバアオダモ. In Norway, the name used is Kinaask, and in South Korea, it is known as 쇠물푸레. Additionally, in China, the plant is commonly called 庐山梣.
