Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. Within China, it is specifically found throughout the South-Central territories. Its presence extends into the Indo-China region, where it inhabits Myanmar and Thailand. Furthermore, the species can be located on the Andaman Islands as well as the Nicobar Islands. This combination of locations highlights a diverse geographical range spanning mainland and island territories.
