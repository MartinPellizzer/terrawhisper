Ugni candollei

Ugni candollei, commonly known as Chilean guava, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to South America. Renowned for its antioxidant-rich berries, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Its fruit and leaves are traditionally utilized for their anti-inflammatory properties, supporting digestive health and providing essential nutrients in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Ugni candollei
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ugni.
