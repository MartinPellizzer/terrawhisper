Euphorbia tithymaloides

Euphorbia tithymaloides, commonly known as Devil's Backbone or Zigzag plant, is a succulent perennial celebrated in traditional medicine. Characterized by its unique, angular stems, this resilient plant offers diverse therapeutic potential, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies and herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia tithymaloides
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, its presence can be identified within the borders of Gambia. Moving toward the continent's center, it is also found in Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically in Chad. Its range extends into South Tropical Africa, where it inhabits both Malawi and Mozambique. Finally, the species reaches the Western Indian Ocean, occurring in the remote Chagos Archipelago.
