Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Devil's Backbone or Zigzag plant, is characterized by its unique succulent structure. The most distinctive feature is its fleshy, bright green leaves that grow in a staggered, zigzag pattern along the stems. Upon any injury to the stems or the leaves, the plant exudes a thick, milky white latex, which contains various bioactive compounds used in traditional medicinal practices.
