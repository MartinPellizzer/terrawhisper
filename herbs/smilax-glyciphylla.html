Smilax glyciphylla

Smilax glyciphylla, commonly known as Sweet Sarsaparilla, is a versatile medicinal climbing vine native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is primarily utilized for its anti-inflammatory and detoxifying properties. Its roots contain bioactive saponins, making it a valuable botanical resource for treating skin ailments and promoting vitality.

  • Family: Smilacaceae
