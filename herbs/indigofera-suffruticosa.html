Indigofera suffruticosa

Indigofera suffruticosa, commonly known as indigo shrub, is a versatile medicinal plant widely utilized in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it possesses significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This shrub serves as a vital natural resource for treating various skin ailments and infections globally.

  • Scientific name: Indigofera suffruticosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Indigofera suffruticosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, following the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and falls under the genus Indigofera.
