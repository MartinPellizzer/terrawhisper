Illicium lanceolatum

Illicium lanceolatum, commonly known as the lanceolate star anise, is a remarkable medicinal shrub native to Southeast Asian forests. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and woody structure, this species contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Illicium lanceolatum
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
