Vigna radiata var. radiata

Vigna radiata var. radiata, commonly known as mung bean, is a nutrient-dense legume celebrated for its significant medicinal properties. Rich in proteins, vitamins, and antioxidants, it serves as a functional food used in traditional medicine to support digestion, detoxify the body, and promote overall metabolic health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Vigna radiata var. radiata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Vigna as the specific variety Vigna radiata var. radiata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across multiple continents. In Northern Europe, its presence can be noted within Great Britain. Its range extends into Eastern Europe, specifically covering South European Russia and Ukraine. Furthermore, the species is found in West Tropical Africa, including the nation of Benin. Finally, its distribution continues through the West Tropical African region of Burkina.
