Vigna radiata radiata, scientifically known as Vigna radiata var. radiata, is a highly nutritious subspecies of the mung bean. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it serves as a potent source of antioxidants, proteins, and essential minerals. This versatile legume is widely utilized in traditional medicine to promote holistic wellness.
