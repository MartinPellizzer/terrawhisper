Cryptolepis sanguinolenta

Cryptolepis sanguinolenta, commonly known as the blood-leaf plant, is a remarkable medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its distinct reddish pigmentation, this species is utilized for its potent bioactive compounds, which offer significant antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a subject of intense pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Cryptolepis sanguinolenta
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cryptolepis sanguinolenta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales. It is further categorized into the family Apocynaceae and the genus Cryptolepis.

This plant, Cryptolepis sanguinolenta, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its natural habitat includes the country of Benin.
