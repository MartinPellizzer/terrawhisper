Cryptolepis sanguinolenta, scientifically known as Cryptolepis sanguinolenta, is a climbing shrub highly valued in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive alkaloids, particularly cryptolepine, it is widely utilized to treat malaria, skin infections, and gastrointestinal ailments. This plant remains a significant subject in modern pharmacological research.

This plant, Cryptolepis sanguinolenta, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its natural habitat includes the country of Benin. Plant Parts

This plant, Cryptolepis sanguinolenta, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic components. The medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in the roots, where the active alkaloids are most potent. Practitioners often utilize the root bark to prepare infusions or decoctions, while the leaves are also recognized for their secondary medicinal contributions. When processed into a concentrated extract, these various elements work synergistically to provide powerful antimalarial and antimicrobial effects. Whether focusing on the deep-seated root system or the foliage, each part contributes to the plant's significant pharmacological profile.
