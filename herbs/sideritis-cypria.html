Sideritis cypria

Sideritis cypria, commonly known as Cypriot dittany, is a perennial herb endemic to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant has been used traditionally to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin inflammations. It remains a valuable botanical resource in herbal medicine and traditional folk remedies.

  • Scientific name: Sideritis cypria
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Sideritis cypria, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Sideritis.
