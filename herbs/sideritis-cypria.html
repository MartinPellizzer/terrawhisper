Cyprus Ironwort, scientifically known as Sideritis cypiia, is a resilient perennial herb endemic to the Mediterranean landscape. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in herbal teas, it is celebrated for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties in holistic medicine.

This plant, Sideritis cypria, is characterized by its distinct morphological features which are often utilized in traditional medicine.
