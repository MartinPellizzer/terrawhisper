Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Within this region, its presence is specifically noted in the nations of Benin and Guinea-Bissau. Moving eastward, its range extends into the West-Central Tropical Africa zone. In this area, it can be found growing in the Central African Republic and Cameroon. Finally, the species also inhabits the unique ecosystems of the Gulf of Guinea Islands.
