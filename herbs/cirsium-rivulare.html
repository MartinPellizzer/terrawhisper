Cirsium rivulare

Cirsium rivulare, commonly known as the Dragon's Blood Thistle, is a striking perennial valued for its dramatic, deep-red flower heads. Thriving in damp, marshy habitats, this resilient plant offers unique aesthetic appeal. While primarily ornamental, its robust presence in wetland ecosystems highlights its significant role in supporting local biodiversity.
