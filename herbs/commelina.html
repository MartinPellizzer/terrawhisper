Commelina

Commelina, commonly known as a vital botanical remedy, holds a distinguished place in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this remarkable plant offers diverse therapeutic benefits, ranging from anti-inflammatory properties to immune support. Exploring its unique characteristics reveals why it remains essential in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Commelina
  • Family: Commelinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Commelinales and the family Commelinaceae. Its taxonomic identification is completed by its classification under the genus Commelina.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Europe. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in Austria and throughout the Czechia-Slovakia region. It can also be found growing within the borders of Germany. Moving toward the coast, the species extends into Southwestern Europe. Specifically, its range encompasses the territories of both Portugal and Spain.
