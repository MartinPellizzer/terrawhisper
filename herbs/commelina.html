Dayflower, scientifically known as Commelina, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its vibrant blue blossoms, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat skin irritations and kidney ailments, this resilient herb offers profound therapeutic potential in natural medicine.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Europe. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented in Austria and throughout the Czechia-Slovakia region. It can also be found growing within the borders of Germany. Moving toward the coast, the species extends into Southwestern Europe. Specifically, its range encompasses the territories of both Portugal and Spain.
