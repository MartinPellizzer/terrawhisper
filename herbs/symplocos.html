Symplocos

Symplocos, commonly known as Lodestone tree, is a versatile genus of flowering plants valued for its diverse medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive alkaloids and flavonoids, various species are traditionally used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This botanical treasure continues to inspire modern pharmacological research and therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Symplocos
  • Family: Symplocaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Ericales and the family Symplocaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Symplocos.
