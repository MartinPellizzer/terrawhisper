This plant, Ferula aucheri, is characterized by its distinct morphological features used in medicinal applications. The plant consists of sturdy stems and complex leaf structures that form its primary vegetative framework. When harvested, the green and white aerial sections are often utilized, representing the visible above-ground portions of the organism.
