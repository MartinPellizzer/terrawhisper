Glyptopetalum sclerocarpum

Glyptopetalum sclerocarpum, commonly known as a versatile medicinal plant, holds significant value in traditional ethnobotanical practices. Renowned for its unique bioactive compounds, this species is frequently utilized to address various ailments. Understanding its phytochemical profile and therapeutic potential is essential for exploring its role in modern pharmacological advancements.

  • Scientific name: Glyptopetalum sclerocarpum
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Glyptopetalum, with the species name sclerocarpum.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically within the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its distribution extends further into the Indo-China peninsula, where it is native to Cambodia. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Laos.
