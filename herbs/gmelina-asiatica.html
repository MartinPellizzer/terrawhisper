Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is commonly found in India, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka. Its presence also extends into the Indo-China region, specifically reaching the Andaman Islands. Furthermore, the species can be identified growing in Cambodia. Together, these locations highlight the diverse habitats where the plant thrives.

Plant Parts

This plant, Gmelina asiatica, possesses various components that are utilized in traditional medicine. The root of the plant is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, often used in decoctions to treat ailments such as fever, inflammation, and digestive issues. Additionally, the leaf serves as another vital part, frequently applied topically or prepared as a paste to soothe skin conditions and reduce swelling. Together, these parts contribute to the plant's reputation as a versatile medicinal resource.
