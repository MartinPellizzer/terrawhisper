Garcinia, scientifically known as Garcinia, is a diverse genus of tropical trees renowned for their potent medicinal properties. Often called "Malabar tamarind," these plants are rich in hydroxycitric acid (HCA). They are widely utilized in traditional medicine to support weight management, enhance digestion, and provide powerful antioxidant benefits.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It is found natively in the country of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species also extends its range into Burkina Faso, contributing to the regional biodiversity. Furthermore, its presence is documented throughout Guinea-Bissau and the neighboring nation of Guinea. Consequently, its geographical footprint is primarily defined by these specific West African territories.
