Garcinia, scientifically known as Garcinia, is a diverse genus of tropical trees renowned for their potent medicinal properties. Often called "Malabar tamarind," these plants are rich in hydroxycitric acid (HCA). They are widely utilized in traditional medicine to support weight management, enhance digestion, and provide powerful antioxidant benefits.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It is found natively in the country of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species also extends its range into Burkina Faso, contributing to the regional biodiversity. Furthermore, its presence is documented throughout Guinea-Bissau and the neighboring nation of Guinea. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Garcinia, offers various components that are utilized for their medicinal and nutritional properties. The most widely recognized part is the fruit, which is often consumed for its unique flavor and bioactive compounds. Within the fruits, the seeds are also present, though they are typically separated from the fleshy pulp. Beyond the fruit, the stem barks are frequently harvested for their concentrated chemical constituents used in traditional remedies. Additionally, the roots of the plant are sometimes employed in medicinal preparations, ensuring that almost every part of the organism serves a specific purpose in holistic health.
