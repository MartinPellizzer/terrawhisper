Dianthus thunbergii

Dianthus thunbergii, commonly known as the Thunberg pink, is a resilient perennial celebrated for its delicate, fragrant blooms. Native to specific rocky terrains, this medicinal plant possesses unique phytochemical properties. It is valued in traditional herbal practices for its potential therapeutic benefits, particularly in soothing inflammation and supporting wellness.
