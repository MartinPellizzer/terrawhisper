Thymus pannonicus

Thymus pannonicus, commonly known as Pannonian thyme, is a resilient perennial herb native to the steppe regions of Central and Eastern Europe. Characteriezed by its aromatic foliage and delicate pink blossoms, this species thrives in sunny, rocky habitats, offering significant ethnobotanical value for its antimicrobial and soothing properties.

  • Scientific name: Thymus pannonicus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Thymus.
