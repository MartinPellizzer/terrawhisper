Thymus pannonicus auct., scientifically known as Thymus pannonicus, is a perennial aromatic herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Native to Central and Eastern Europe, this species is distinguished by its small, fragrant leaves and pinkish flowers. It thrives in dry, sunny habitats, offering significant medicinal and ecological value.
