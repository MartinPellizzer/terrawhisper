Crataegus azarolus var. aronia

Crataegus azarolus var. aronia, commonly known as the aronia hawthorn, is a resilient shrub valued for its medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive antioxidants, its berries and leaves are traditionally used to support cardiovascular health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall wellness, making it a significant plant in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus azarolus var. aronia
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Common Names

This plant, Crataegus azarolus var. aronia, is known by several different names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the white hawthorn, while in Greek, it is known as aspro milti. In Italian, it is called biancospino bianco, and in French, it is referred to as aubépine blanche.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range spanning across several regions of the Mediterranean basin. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is documented in Italy and the island of Sicilia.
