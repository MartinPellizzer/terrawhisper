Soncoya, scientifically known as Annona purpurea, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree cherished for its striking, deep-purple flowers and nutritious fruit. Plant Parts

This plant, Annona purpurea, possesses various components that are utilized in traditional medicinal practices. The large, fleshy leaves are often the primary part used, frequently prepared as infusions or poultices to treat skin ailments or inflammatory conditions. The stems serve as the structural foundation of the plant and can be utilized in decoctions to address digestive issues. Additionally, the seeds contain specific bioactive compounds that are studied for their potential therapeutic properties, though they must be handled with care due to their potency.
