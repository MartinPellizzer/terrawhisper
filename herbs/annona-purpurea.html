Annona purpurea

Annona purpurea, commonly known as the purple custard apple, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree native to the Americas. Valued for its vibrant, dark-skinned fruit, it offers a sweet, creamy pulp. Beyond its culinary appeal, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance, with various parts utilized in traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Annona purpurea
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and falls under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Annona.
