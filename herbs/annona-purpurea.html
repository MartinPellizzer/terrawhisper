Soncoya, scientifically known as Annona purpurea, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree cherished for its striking, deep-purple flowers and nutritious fruit. Native to the Americas, this member of the custard apple family is valued in traditional medicine for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support overall holistic wellness.
