Viburnum odoratissimum

Viburnum odoratissimum, commonly known as Sweet Viburnum, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its lush foliage and fragrant white blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, various species in the Viburnum genus are studied for their bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic properties in traditional medicine and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Viburnum odoratissimum
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales, it is a member of the family Viburnaceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Viburnum.

Common Names

This plant, Viburnum odoratissimum, is known by a variety of common names across different languages and regions. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within China. It can be found throughout China South-Central and the northern reaches of China North-Central. Additionally, its habitat extends to the tropical island of Hainan and the lush landscapes of China Southeast. Beyond the mainland, the species is also documented in Eastern Asia, specifically within Taiwan. These diverse locations highlight the wide regional presence of Viburnum odoratissimum.
