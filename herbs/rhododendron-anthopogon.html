Rhododendron anthopogon

Rhododendron anthopogon, commonly known as the flower-bearing rhododendron, is a captivating medicinal shrub valued for its therapeutic properties. Found in diverse ecosystems, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address various ailments. Its unique botanical characteristics and bioactive compounds make it a subject of significant pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron anthopogon
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Ericales and placed in the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Rhododendron.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several mountainous regions. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the Tibet region. It is also widely found across the Indian Subcontinent, particularly in the East Himalaya. Furthermore, the species is documented growing in the high altitudes of Nepal. Finally, its range extends to the West Himalaya and parts of Pakistan.
