Rhododendron anthopogon, scientifically known as Rhododendron anthopogon, is a distinctive evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and flowers possess unique bioactive properties, often utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, respiratory ailments, and various ailments within local herbal practices.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several mountainous regions. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the Tibet region. It is also widely found across the Indian Subcontinent, particularly in the East Himalaya. Furthermore, the species is documented growing in the high altitudes of Nepal. Finally, its range extends to the West Himalaya and parts of Pakistan.
