Chamaecyparis lawsoniana

Chamaecyparis lawsoniana, commonly known as Leyland cypress, is a versatile evergreen prized for its dense foliage. Beyond landscaping, it holds significant medicinal potential. Rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used for its antimicrobial and antiseptic properties, offering therapeutic benefits in various folk medicine practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Chamaecyparis lawsoniana
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling into the family Cupressaceae.
