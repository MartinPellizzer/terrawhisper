Salvia pratensis

Salvia pratensis, commonly known as meadow sage, is a perennial herb celebrated for its striking violet flowers and aromatic foliage. Native to Europe, this resilient plant has long been valued in traditional herbalism for its calming properties and potential therapeutic benefits, making it a significant subject in botanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Salvia pratensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Salvia.
