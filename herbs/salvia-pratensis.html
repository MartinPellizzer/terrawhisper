It plays a vital role in these human-influenced ecosystems by serving as a significant nectar source for diverse pollinator communities, particularly long-tongued bees and butterflies, which facilitates its reproductive success through entomophily. Its ability to persist in modified landscapes makes it a resilient component of semi-natural grasslands and ruderal habitats subject to periodic human disturbance.
