Artemisia tournefortiana

Artemisia tournefortiana, commonly known as Tournefort wormwood, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant contains diverse bioactive compounds. Traditionally used in various folk medicines, it offers significant therapeutic potential in treating digestive ailments, inflammation, and various respiratory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia tournefortiana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, falling into the family Asteraceae.
