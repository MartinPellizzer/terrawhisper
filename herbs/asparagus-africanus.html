Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its documented presence includes the coastal and inland regions of Ghana and Benin. Furthermore, it can be located within the territories of Burkina Faso and Guinea-Bissau. The species also extends its range into the nation of Gambia.
