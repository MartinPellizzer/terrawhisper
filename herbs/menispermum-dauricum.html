Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the vast regions of Siberia. It can be found growing in the Altai mountain range, which marks one of its key habitats. Additionally, the species is documented throughout the Republic of Buryatia. Its presence extends further into the Chitа region, demonstrating a wide longitudinal spread. Finally, the plant is also distributed across the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk areas, completing its Siberian range.

Plant Parts

This plant, Menispermum dauricum, is a medicinal herb whose therapeutic properties are primarily derived from its underground structures. In traditional pharmacological studies, the roots serve as the fundamental source of bioactive compounds. Specifically, the rhizomes act as the horizontal growing stems that facilitate the plant's spread and storage of nutrients. For the purpose of herbal preparation and extraction, the dry root is frequently utilized to ensure stability and potency in medicinal formulations. Furthermore, in modern biotechnological research, cultured roots are developed in laboratory settings to provide a controlled and sustainable source of specific alkaloids without depleting wild populations.
