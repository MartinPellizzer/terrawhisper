Eucalyptus propinqua

Eucalyptus propinqua, commonly known as the Narrow-leaved Gum, is a resilient Australian native renowned for its medicinal potential. Valued for its aromatic, oil-rich leaves, it possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, this versatile plant offers significant therapeutic benefits in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus propinqua
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement concludes within the genus Eucalyptus.
