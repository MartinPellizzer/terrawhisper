Elephant-Ears, scientifically known as Bergenia crassifolia, is a hardy perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its thick, leathery leaves, this plant holds significant medicinal value. Its rhizomes are widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments like inflammation, digestive issues, and various skin conditions.

Plant Parts

This plant, Bergenia crassifolia, is a perennial herb characterized by its thick, fleshy, and creeping rhizomes that grow horizontally beneath the soil surface. These rhizomes serve as the primary storage organs for the plant, accumulating essential nutrients and bioactive compounds that are highly valued in traditional medicine. Because the medicinal properties are concentrated within these underground structures, the rhizomes are the specific parts typically harvested, cleaned, and processed for therapeutic use.
