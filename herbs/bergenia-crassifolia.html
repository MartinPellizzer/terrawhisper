Bergenia crassifolia

Bergenia crassifolia, commonly known as elephant's ears, is a perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its thick, leathery leaves, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It possesses potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and diuretic properties, frequently used to treat kidney stones and gastrointestinal disorders.

  • Scientific name: Bergenia crassifolia
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Saxifragales and the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Bergenia.
