Zhejiang fritillary

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (301 studies) · ★★★★★

Zhejiang fritillary, scientifically known as Friitillaria thunbergii, is a highly valued medicinal herb in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its ability to moisten the lungs and transform phlegm, it is primarily used to treat dry coughs and respiratory ailments, offering significant therapeutic benefits for pulmonary health and congestion.
