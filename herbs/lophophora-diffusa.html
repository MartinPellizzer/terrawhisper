Peyote, scientifically known as Lophophora diffusa, is a small, button-like cactus native to the Chihuahuan Desert. Renowned for its psychoactive properties, it contains mescaline, a potent alkaloid. Historically central to indigenous spiritual practices, this sacred plant holds profound cultural significance and continues to be studied for its medicinal potential.
