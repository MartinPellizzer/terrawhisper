Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several regions in East and Southeast Asia. In China, it is primarily located within the South-Central and Southeast provinces. Its range extends further south into the Indo-China peninsula. Within this area, the species can be found growing in Cambodia and Laos. Additionally, the plant is documented to inhabit various parts of Thailand.
