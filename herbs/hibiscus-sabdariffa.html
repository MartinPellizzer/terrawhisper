Roselle, scientifically known as Hibiscus sabdariffa, is a vibrant tropical shrub celebrated for its striking, deep-red calyces. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is widely utilized for its potent antioxidant properties, ability to support cardiovascular health, and its ability to assist in managing blood pressure and promoting overall wellness.

Distribution

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Roselle, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties which are concentrated in several specific botanical structures. The most prominent feature used in herbal teas and medicinal preparations is the fleshy calyx, which surrounds the seed capsule. These vibrant, deep-red calyxes are harvested once the fruit has matured, providing the intense pigment and tart flavor characteristic of the species. While the calyces are the primary medicinal component, the leaves are also utilized in various cultures for their nutritional and antioxidant benefits. By utilizing both the nutrient-rich foliage and the potent red calyxes, practitioners can harness the full spectrum of this plant's medicinal potential.
