Berberis bealei, scientifically known as Berberis bealei, is a prominent medicinal shrub native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, particularly in Ayurveda, it contains bioactive alkaloids like berberine. Its roots and bark are widely utilized to treat digestive disorders, skin ailments, and various inflammatory conditions.
