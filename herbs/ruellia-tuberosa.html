Ruellia tuberosa

Minnieroot

Ruellia tuberosa, commonly known as the tuberous rue, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This hardy herb offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Ruellia tuberosa
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ruellia tuberosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Acanthaceae and falls under the genus Ruellia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range that spans across multiple continents and distinct ecological zones. Its distribution extends into the Western Indian Ocean, specifically reaching the islands of the Seychelles. Finally, the plant reaches much further east, appearing in the South-Central regions of China.
