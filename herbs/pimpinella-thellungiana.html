Pimpinella thellungiana

Pimpinella thellungiana, commonly known as a medicinal herb from the Apiaceae family, is a botanical treasure valued for its therapeutic potential. Renowned for its rich bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant pharmacological benefits, particularly in traditional medicine, where its unique phytochemical profile is studied for diverse healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Pimpinella thellungiana
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales. Finally, Pimpinella thellungiana is a member of the family Apiaceae within the genus Pimpinella.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in Northern Asia. Within the territory of Siberia, it can be found specifically in the Chita region. Its range extends into the Russian Far East, where it inhabits both the Amur and Primorye areas. In China, the species is documented across the landscape of Inner Mongolia. Finally, its presence is also noted within the Manchuria region of China.
