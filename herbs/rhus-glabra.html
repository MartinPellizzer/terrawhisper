Rhus glabra

Rhus glabra · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (276 studies) · ★★★★★

Rhus glabra, scientifically known as Rhus glabra, is commonly called smooth sumac. Native to North America, this deciduous shrub is valued for its medicinal properties, particularly its antioxidant-rich berries. Traditionally used to treat digestive issues and skin irritations, it remains a significant plant in ethnobotanical studies and herbalism.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across North America and parts of Europe. In Western Canada, it can be found thriving in the provinces of British Columbia, Manitoba, and Saskatchewan. Its presence extends into Eastern Canada, specifically within the province of Ontario. Additionally, the species is documented in Middle Europe, particularly in Germany. These varied locations demonstrate the plant's ability to adapt to diverse regional environments.
