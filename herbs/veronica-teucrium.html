Veronica teucrium

Veronica teucrium, commonly known as Teucrium speedwell, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Renowned for its delicate floral structures and unique physiological properties, this plant holds significant interest in ethnobotany. Researchers continue to explore its potential bioactive compounds for diverse medicinal and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Veronica teucrium
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
