This plant, Gardenia resinifera, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from the production of gum resin. This substance is obtained as an exudate from the bark and stems of the plant, serving as a vital component in traditional therapeutic practices. The gum resin is harvested for its various bioactive qualities, which are utilized in treating diverse ailments, making this specific plant part the most significant element for pharmacological applications.
