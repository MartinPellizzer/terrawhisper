Pseudostellaria heterophylla

Tai zi shen

Pseudostellaria heterophyllas, commonly known as the variable-leaf star-flower, is a unique medicinal plant valued for its diverse phytochemical properties. Characterized by its distinct heterophyllous foliage, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. Researchers continue to explore its bioactive compounds for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and potential antimicrobial applications in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Pseudostellaria heterophylla
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Caryophyllaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Pseudostellaria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. The species is also widely documented throughout various parts of China, including the South-Central areas. Furthermore, its range extends into the northern territories of China, spanning Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Finally, the plant can be found inhabiting the North-Central regions of China as well.
