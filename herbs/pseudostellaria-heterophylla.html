Tai Zi Shen, scientifically known as Pseudostellaria heterophyllia, is a precious medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its restorative properties, it is frequently utilized to nourish the lungs, tonify the spleen, and replenish vital Qi, making it an essential remedy for enhancing overall systemic vitality.
