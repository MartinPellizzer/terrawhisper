Ipomoea arborescens

Ipomoea arborescens, commonly known as the tree morning glory, is a significant medicinal shrub native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is used in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin disorders and inflammation, making it a valuable botanical resource in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea arborescens
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae and specifically belongs to the genus Ipomoea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several diverse regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in both Guinea and Senegal. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The species also extends into East Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting the territory of Kenya. Finally, its range reaches into South Tropical Africa, where it is located in Zimbabwe.
