Vaccinium iteophyllum

Vaccinium iteophyllum, commonly known as the yellow-leafed blueberry, is a resilient shrub native to specific montane ecosystems. Renowned for its unique foliage and antioxidant-rich berries, this medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Research suggests its bioactive compounds may support metabolic health and provide potent anti-inflammatory benefits.

  • Scientific name: Vaccinium iteophyllum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its placement in the genus Vaccinium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range of distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central areas as well as the Southeast. Its presence also extends into the high-altitude territories of Tibet. Beyond the Chinese borders, the species is documented within the Indo-China region. Specifically, it has been identified as growing in the country of Vietnam.
