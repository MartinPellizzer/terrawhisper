Rheum khorasanicum, scientifically known as Rheum khorasanicum, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Native to high-altitude regions, its roots contain potent anthraquinones. Renowned for their purgative and anti-inflammatory properties, these medicinal roots are essential in various herbal formulations used to support digestive health and detoxification.
