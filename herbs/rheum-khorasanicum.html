Rheum khorasanicum, scientifically known as Rheum khorasanicum, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Native to high-altitude regions, its roots contain potent anthraquinones. This plant, known scientifically as Rheum khorasanicum, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its medicinal properties found within the root.
