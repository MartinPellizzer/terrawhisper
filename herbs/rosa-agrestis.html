Rosa agrestis

Rosa agrestis, commonly known as the wild thorny rose, is a resilient medicinal plant celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its high antioxidant content, it is frequently used in traditional remedies to support immune function, soothe inflammation, and promote skin health through its potent, natural botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Rosa agrestis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is categorized under the genus Rosa.
