Taraxacum

Taraxacum, commonly known as the dandelion, is a versatile perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bitter properties, it serves as a powerful diuretic and liver tonic. Every part of this resilient plant, from its roots to its vibrant yellow blooms, offers unique therapeutic benefits for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Taraxacum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, it is identified by the genus Taraxacum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across various regions within Northern Europe. It can be found growing throughout the landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Its distribution also extends to the remote islands of Føroyar and Iceland. Additionally, the species is well-established across the territory of Great Britain. Together, these locations highlight its widespread reach across the northernmost parts of the continent.
