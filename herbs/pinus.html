The pine genus, scientifically known as Pinus, is a diverse group of coniferous trees valued globally for their medicinal properties. Rich in essential oils, resins, and antioxidants, various species offer therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory and respiratory support. These resilient evergreens remain vital components in traditional and modern herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as Pinus, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The long, slender needles are often harvested to create infusions or extracts rich in vitamins and antioxidants. Within the structure of the tree, the knotwood can be processed for specific chemical compounds, while the most potent medicinal value is frequently found in the essential oils extracted from the plant. These oils are highly valued in aromatherapy and traditional medicine for their antiseptic and calming effects.
