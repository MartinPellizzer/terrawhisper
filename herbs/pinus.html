Pinus

Pinus, commonly known as pine, is a resilient genus of coniferous trees widely distributed across the Northern Hemisphere. Renowned for their needle-like leaves and woody cones, these plants offer significant medicinal value. Their resins, essential oils, and bark contain bioactive compounds used for antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and respiratory therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Pinus
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, it is placed in the family Pinaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Pinus.

This plant, known as Pinus, exhibits a specific distribution across several regions in Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the landscapes of Denmark and Finland.
