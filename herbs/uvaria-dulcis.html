Uvaria dulcis

Uvaria dulcis, commonly known as the Sweet Uvaria, is a climbing shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its delicious, edible fruits, this medicinal plant holds significant value in traditional practices. Its leaves and stems are frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat various ailments and promote holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Uvaria dulcis
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, specifically as a member of the family Annonaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Uvaria.
