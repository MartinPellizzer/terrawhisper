Bulbine latifolia var. latifolia

Bulbine latifolia var. latifolia, commonly known as Bulbine, is a resilient succulent native to South Africa. Renowned for its fleshy leaves and bright yellow flowers, this hardy perennial is highly valued in traditional medicine. It possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties, making it a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Bulbine latifolia var. latifolia
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant, taxonomically identified as Bulbine latifolia var. latifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In the southern reaches of the continent, it can be found growing within the Cape Provinces and the KwaZulu-Natal region. Moving further north, its range extends into the tropical zones of South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is documented to occur within the borders of Malawi and Mozambique. Additionally, the species maintains a presence in the country of Zimbabwe.
