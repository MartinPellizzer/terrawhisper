Broad-Leafed Bulbine

Bulbine latifolia var. latifolia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (12 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Broad-Leafed Bulbine, scientifically known as Bulbine latifolia var. latifolia, is a resilient perennial succulent native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its fleshy, strap-like leaves and delicate yellow blooms, this hardy plant is valued in traditional medicine for its soothing properties, often used to treat skin irritations and minor wounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In the southern reaches of the continent, it can be found growing within the Cape Provinces and the KwaZulu-Natal region. Moving further north, its range extends into the tropical zones of South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is documented to occur within the borders of Malawi and Mozambique. Additionally, the species maintains a presence in the country of Zimbabwe.
