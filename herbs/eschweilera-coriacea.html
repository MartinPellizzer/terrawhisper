Eschweilera coriacea

Eschweilera coriacea, commonly known as the "leather-leaf" tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights.

  • Scientific name: Eschweilera coriacea
  • Family: Lecythidaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is specifically placed in the family Lecythidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eschweilera, which includes the species Eschweilera coriacea.
