What's the taxonomical classification of Lysimachia nutantiflora?

Lysimachia nutantiflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific genus, Lysimachia, and its unique species designation, nutantiflora.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lysimachia nutantiflora has opposite, lanceolate to oblong leaves with entire margins and a herbaceous, upright stem that typically reaches heights of 20 to 50 centimeters. Its most distinctive morphological feature is the nodding, bell-shaped yellow flowers that hang from the leaf axils on slender pedicels. Each blossom consists of five fused petals forming a campanulate corolla. After pollination, the plant develops small, capsule-like fruit that eventually disperse seeds to facilitate new growth.
