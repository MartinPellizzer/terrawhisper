Mitragyna, scientifically known as Mitragyna speciosa, is a tropical evergreen tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its leaves, which contain unique alkaloids, this medicinal plant has been used traditionally for its stimulating and analgesic properties. It remains a subject of significant pharmacological interest in modern botanical research.
