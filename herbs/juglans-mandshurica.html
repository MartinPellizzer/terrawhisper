Juglans mandschurica, scientifically known as Juglans mandshurica, is a deciduous tree native to East Asia, highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its nutrient-dense nut, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and neuroprotective properties. This versatile botanical is frequently utilized to support cognitive health and overall systemic wellness.
