Juglans mandshurica

Juglans mandschurica

Juglans mandshurica, commonly known as the Manchurian walnut, is a versatile medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its nuts and bark are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, respiratory issues, and skin conditions, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Juglans mandshurica
  • Family: Juglandaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Fagales and the family Juglandaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Juglans as the species Juglans mandshurica.
