Aloe debrana

Aloe debrana, commonly known as a resilient succulent, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Native to specific arid regions, it features thick, fleshy leaves filled with soothing gel. This potent extract is widely utilized to heal skin irritations, promote hydration, and support overall wellness naturally.

  • Scientific name: Aloe debrana
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aloe debrana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asphodelaceae and falls under the genus Aloe.
