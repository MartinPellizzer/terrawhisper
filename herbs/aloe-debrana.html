This plant, known as Aloe debrana, possesses a very specific and limited geographical range. Its natural habitat is primarily situated within the Northeast Tropical Africa region. Specifically, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Ethiopia. Due to this localized presence, it is considered an endemic element of the Ethiopian landscape. Understanding this narrow distribution is essential for the conservation of the species in its native environment.

This plant, Aloe debrana, is characterized by its succulent nature, though its medicinal value is primarily concentrated in the fleshy leaves rather than its underground structures.
