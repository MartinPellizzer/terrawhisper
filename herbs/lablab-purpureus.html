Lablab purpureus

Lablab purpureus, commonly known as hyacinth bean, is a versatile legume valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in proteins and antioxidants, it is used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This resilient climber serves as both a vital food source and therapeutic resource.

  • Scientific name: Lablab purpureus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Fabales and falls under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Lablab.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning multiple continents and regions. In Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Italy and the island of Sicilia.
