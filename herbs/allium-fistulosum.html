Allium fistulosum

Welsh onion

Allium fistulosum, commonly known as scallions or bunching onions, is a versatile perennial herb valued in both culinary and medicinal traditions. Rich in organosulfur compounds, it offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This plant is frequently utilized to support digestive health, boost immunity, and promote overall cardiovascular wellness.

  • Scientific name: Allium fistulosum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Allium, specifically as the species Allium fistulosum.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented in Norway. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species is found within the territories of Germany as well as the Czechia-Slovakia region. Its range extends further into Southwestern Europe, where it grows in France. Finally, the geographical spread of this medicinal plant reaches as far as Spain.
