Jack pine, scientifically known as Pinus banksiana, is a resilient coniferous species native to North America. Beyond its ecological role in forest regeneration, it holds medicinal significance. Traditionally, its resin and needles have been utilized for their antiseptic properties and to alleviate respiratory ailments through various topical and aromatic applications.
