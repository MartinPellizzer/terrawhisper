Pinus banksiana

Pinus banksiana, commonly known as the Jack Pine, is a resilient conifer native to North America. Beyond its ecological importance in forest regeneration, it holds medicinal value. Traditionally, its resin and needles have been utilized for their antiseptic and soothing properties, offering natural remedies for respiratory and skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Pinus banksiana
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, it is a member of the family Pinaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Pinus.
