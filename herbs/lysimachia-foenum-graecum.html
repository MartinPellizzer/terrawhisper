Lysimachia foenum-graecum

Lysimachia foenum-graecum, commonly known as fenugreek, is a versatile medicinal herb prized for its nutritional and therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, regulate blood sugar, and enhance lactation. This potent botanical offers diverse applications in both wellness and culinary arts.

  • Scientific name: Lysimachia foenum-graecum
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Lysimachia foenum-graecum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Lysimachia.
