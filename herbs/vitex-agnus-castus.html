monk's pepper

Vitex agnus-castus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (687 studies) · ★★★★★

Monk's pepper, scientifically known as Vitex agnus-castus, is a perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean. Renowned in herbal medicine for centuries, it is primarily used to balance hormones. It serves as a powerful natural remedy for managing PMS symptoms, regulating menstrual cycles, and supporting overall reproductive health.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Chasteberry, utilizes various anatomical structures for its medicinal properties. The most frequently used part is the fruits, which are small, dark, drupe-like berries often referred to as chasteberries. Within these fruits, the seeds contain concentrated bioactive compounds that are essential for therapeutic applications. In addition to the reproductive components, the leaf is also noted for its aromatic qualities and can be used in herbal preparations. Furthermore, the stems provide the structural framework that supports the plant's growth, contributing to the overall biological profile of the species.
