monk's pepper

Vitex agnus-castus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (687 studies) · ★★★★★

Monk's pepper, scientifically known as Vitex agnus-castus, is a perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean. Renowned in herbal medicine for centuries, it is primarily used to balance hormones. It serves as a powerful natural remedy for managing PMS symptoms, regulating menstrual cycles, and supporting overall reproductive health.
