Prunus tenella

Prunus tenella, commonly known as the shadow plum, is a resilient medicinal plant prized for its unique therapeutic properties. Deeply rooted in traditional herbalism, its bark and fruit are utilized to soothe digestive ailments and reduce inflammation. This botanical treasure offers a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Prunus tenella
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Prunus.
