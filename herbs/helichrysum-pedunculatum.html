Helichrysum pedunculatum

Helichrysum pedunculatum, commonly known as the everlasting flower, is a resilient medicinal herb native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is rich in essential oils and antioxidants. It is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support skin health, reduce inflammation, and promote healing.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum pedunculatum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Asterales and falls into the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Helichrysum, with the species name pedunculatum.

This plant is primarily found within the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. Its natural range extends across the various regions of the Cape Provinces.
