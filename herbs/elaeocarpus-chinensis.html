This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Oxalidales, it is classified under the family Elaeocarpaceae. Its taxonomic designation is completed by its placement in the genus Elaeocarpus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across parts of East and Southeast Asia. In China, it is commonly found throughout the South-Central regions. Its distribution also extends into the Southeast territories of China. Moving further south, the species can be located within the Indo-China peninsula.
