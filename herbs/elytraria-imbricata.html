Elytraria imbricata

Elytraria imbricata, commonly known as the "shingle-leaf" plant, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned for its unique overlapping leaf structure, this species is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential through its diverse bioactive compounds and natural healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Elytraria imbricata
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Elytraria, specifically as the species Elytraria imbricata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse and wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it is notably found within Madagascar. Its presence extends into Indo-China, where it grows in Vietnam. Within the Malesia region, the species can be located in the Philippines.
