Cryptocarya rugulosa

Cryptocarya rugulosa, commonly known as a member of the Lauraceae family, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Rich in bioactive phytochemicals, this species holds immense potential for pharmacological research, particularly in developing natural therapies for inflammatory conditions and addressing various metabolic health concerns globally.

  • Scientific name: Cryptocarya rugulosa
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Laurales. Finally, it is a member of the Lauraceae family and falls under the genus Cryptocarya.
