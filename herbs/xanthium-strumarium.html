Xanthium strumarium

Rough cocklebur

Xanthium strumarium, commonly known as cocklebur, is a distinctive annual herb recognized by its prickly, bur-like fruits. While often viewed as a troublesome weed in agricultural fields, this plant holds significant historical importance in traditional medicine, where its various components have been utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Xanthium strumarium
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Xanthium strumarium, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Xanthium.
