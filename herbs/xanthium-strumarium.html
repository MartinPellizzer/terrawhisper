Rough cockleburl, scientifically known as Xanthium strumarium, is a common annual herb found globally in disturbed soils. Renowned for its distinctive prickly seed pods, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation and skin ailments, it remains a subject of pharmacological study.
