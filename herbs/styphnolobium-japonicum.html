Chinese scholartree, scientifically known as Styphnolobium japonicum, is a deciduous tree prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bioactive flavonoids and alkaloids, it is utilized to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and various ailments. This versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential, bridging ancient herbal wisdom with modern pharmacological research.
