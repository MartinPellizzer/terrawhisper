Camellia luteoflora

Camellia luteoflora, commonly known as the Golden Camellia, is a rare medicinal shrub celebrated for its vibrant yellow blooms. Renowned in traditional healing practices, its leaves and flowers possess potent antioxidant properties, often utilized to alleviate inflammation and support respiratory health, making it a valuable botanical treasure for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Camellia luteoflora
  • Family: Theaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is classified under the family Theaceae. It is further identified by its genus, Camellia.
