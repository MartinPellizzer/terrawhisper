What's the taxonomical classification of Indigofera fortunei?

Indigofera fortunei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae, which characterizes its botanical relationships. Finally, the plant is placed within the genus Indigofera, with its specific identity defined by the species epithet fortunei.
