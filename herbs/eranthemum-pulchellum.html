Plant Parts

This plant, Eranthemum pulchellum, utilizes various components for its medicinal applications. While the structure is defined by its upright, herbaceous stems that support the vibrant foliage, the therapeutic potential is often concentrated in the underground systems, specifically the roots. In traditional practices, these roots are harvested and processed into dried roots to ensure stability for long-term storage and use. Additionally, the barks of the plant may be utilized in specific formulations, though much of the botanical interest focuses on the aerial part, which includes the leaves and flowers that are easily accessible for collection and study.
