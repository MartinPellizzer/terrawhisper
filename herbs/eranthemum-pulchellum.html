Eranthemum pulchellum, scientifically known as Eranthemum pulchellum, is a striking perennial shrub renowned for its vibrant blue flowers. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it is primarily found within the South-Central area.
