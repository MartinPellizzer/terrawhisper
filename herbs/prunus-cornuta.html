Prunus cornuta

Himalayan bird cherry

Prunus cornuta, commonly known as the Korean cherry, is a resilient deciduous shrub valued in traditional medicine. Its bark and fruits possess unique bioactive properties, often utilized for their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. This versatile plant serves as a vital botanical resource in holistic approaches to promoting overall wellness.

  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Prunus.
