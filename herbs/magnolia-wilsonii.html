Magnolia wilsonii

What's the taxonomical classification of Magnolia wilsonii?

Magnolia wilsonii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant falls under the order Magnoliales and is a member of the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is defined by its specific genus, Magnolia, and its unique species designation, wilsonii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Magnolia wilsonii has large, elliptic to oblong leaves that measure up to 25 centimeters in length and feature a leathery texture with an entire margin. The foliage is typically dark green on the upper surface and often displays a paler, more matte underside. Its flowers are prominent, solitary, and terminal, appearing in shades of creamy white or pale yellow during the blooming season. These blossoms possess multiple fleshy tepals that surround a central cluster of stamens and carpels. The tree develops into a medium-sized woody shrub or small tree with a spreading canopy.
