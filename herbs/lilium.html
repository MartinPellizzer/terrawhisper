Lilium

The lily genus

Lilium, commonly known as the lily, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its ability to soothe skin irritations and support respiratory health, various species offer potent bioactive compounds. This elegant perennial serves as a bridge between ornamental beauty and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Lilium
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Liliaceae and the genus Lilium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lilium, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Hungarian, it is called liliom, while in Turkish, it is known as zambak. The Arabic language uses the name زنبق, and in Azerbaijani, it is called Zanbaq. Additionally, in Bulgarian, the plant is commonly referred to as крем.
