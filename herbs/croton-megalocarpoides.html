Croton megalocarpoides

Croton megalocarpoides, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, holds significant ethnobotanical value. Belonging to the Euphorbiaceae family, this plant is studied for its diverse phytochemical properties. Researchers explore its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and various skin ailments.
