Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning several regions across the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Gabon and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, the species is also located within Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence is documented in the countries of Djibouti and Eritrea. Finally, the plant completes its recorded range in this area by inhabiting the highlands and lowlands of Ethiopia.
