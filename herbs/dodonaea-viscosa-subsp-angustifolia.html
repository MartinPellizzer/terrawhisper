Dodonaea viscosa subsp. angustifolia

Dodonaea viscosa subsp. angustifolia, commonly known as Hop Bush, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various arid regions. Renowned for its antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, this subspecies is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Dodonaea viscosa subsp. angustifolia
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Sapindales, it is a member of the family Sapindaceae and is specifically placed within the genus Dodonaea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning several regions across the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Gabon and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, the species is also located within Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence is documented in the countries of Djibouti and Eritrea. Finally, the plant completes its recorded range in this area by inhabiting the highlands and lowlands of Ethiopia.
