Pituri, scientifically known as Duboisia hopwoodii, is a significant Australian shrub historically utilized by Indigenous peoples. Rich in nicotine and related alkaloids, it served as a vital stimulant and social tool. Today, it remains a subject of pharmacological interest due to its potent bioactive compounds and traditional medicinal importance.
