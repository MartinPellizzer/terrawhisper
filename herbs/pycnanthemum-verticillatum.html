Pycnanthemum verticillatum, is commonly known as whorled mountain mint, a perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and white flower clusters, this plant serves as a vital pollinator magnet. It possesses traditional medicinal properties, often utilized for its refreshing scent and various therapeutic applications.
