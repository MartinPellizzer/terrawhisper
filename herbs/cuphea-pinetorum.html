Cuphea pinetorum

Cuphea pineatorum, commonly known as the pine-leaf cuphea, is a striking ornamental shrub valued for its delicate, needle-like foliage and vibrant blooms. Native to Mexico, this resilient plant thrives in sunny environments, offering unique aesthetic appeal and potential ethnobotanical interest due to its distinct morphological characteristics and hardy nature.
