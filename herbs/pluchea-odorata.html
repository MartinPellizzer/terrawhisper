Pluchea odorata

Pluchea odorata, commonly known as aromatic pluchea, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its fragrant leaves, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections, showcasing its profound ethnobotanical importance.
