Hemlock, scientifically known as Conium maculatum, is a highly toxic perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its lethal alkaloids, particularly coniine, it has historically played a significant role in toxicology and classical literature. While dangerous, it remains a subject of profound botanical and pharmacological interest.
