Genista tournefortii subsp. tournefortii

Genista tournefortii subsp. tournefortii, commonly known as Tournefort's broom, is a resilient shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this botanical specimen holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is studied for its unique phytochemical profile, offering potential medicinal properties within traditional pharmacological practices.

  • Scientific name: Genista tournefortii subsp. tournefortii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized into the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Genista, specifically as the subspecies Genista tournefortii subsp. tournefortii.

Common Names

This plant, Genista tournefortii subsp. tournefortii, is known by various regional names depending on the language. In English, it is commonly referred to as Tournefort's broom, while in Spanish, it is known as retama de Tournefort or retama de monte. In French, it is called genêt de Tournefort, and in Catalan, it is called genest de Tournefort.
