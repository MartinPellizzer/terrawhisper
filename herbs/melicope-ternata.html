Melicope ternata, commonly known as a member of the Rutaceae family, is a medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic properties. This plant offers significant interest in ethnobotany, particularly regarding its traditional uses in folk medicine.
