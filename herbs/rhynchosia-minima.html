Jumby Bean, scientifically known as Rhynchosia minima, is a small, creeping legume native to tropical regions. Renowned for its striking, eye-like seeds, this plant holds significant traditional value. In various cultures, it is utilized for its medicinal properties, particularly in treating skin ailments and supporting overall wellness through herbal applications.
