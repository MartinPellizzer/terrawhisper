Rhynchosia minima

Rhynchosia minima, commonly known as the small pink pea, is a diminutive annual herb belonging to the Fabaceae family. Native to diverse habitats, this resilient plant is recognized for its delicate, pea-like flowers. While primarily studied for its botanical characteristics, it holds potential interest in traditional medicinal ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Rhynchosia minima
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement includes its position in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and is identified by the genus Rhynchosia.
