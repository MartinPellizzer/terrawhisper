Bassia scoparia

Bassia scoparia, commonly known as burning bush or smoky bush, is a resilient annual herb widely distributed across Eurasia and North America. Valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant offers significant pharmacological potential, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting various aspects of digestive health.

  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe
