Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across various regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Burundi and Rwanda. Its presence continues through the expansive territories of the DR Congo within that same central region. Moving toward Northeast Tropical Africa, the species is also documented in Djibouti. Finally, its geographical range extends into the highland areas of Ethiopia.
