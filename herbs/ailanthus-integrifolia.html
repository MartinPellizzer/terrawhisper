White Siriis, scientifically known as Ailanthes integrifolia, is a versatile medicinal tree celebrated in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its therapeutic bark and leaves, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and anti-diabetic properties. This resilient species serves as a vital natural resource for treating various skin ailments and systemic infections.
