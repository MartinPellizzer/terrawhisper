Chrysanthemum indicum

Chrysanthemum indicum, commonly known as Chrysanthemum weeds or Wild Chrysanthemum, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is widely used to treat inflammation, fever, and eye ailments. This resilient plant offers potent antioxidant benefits, supporting overall wellness through its natural bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Chrysanthemum indicum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Chrysanthemum.
