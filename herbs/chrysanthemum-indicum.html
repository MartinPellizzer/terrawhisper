Indian chrysanthemum

Chrysanthemum indicum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (161 studies) · ★★★★☆

Indian chrysanthemum, scientifically known as Chrysanthemum indicum, is a versatile perennial herb revered in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties, this plant is widely used to treat respiratory ailments, skin issues, and fevers. Its vibrant blooms offer both therapeutic benefits and aesthetic beauty.

Plant Parts

This plant, Chrysanthemum indicum, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties found throughout its structure. The most significant medicinal component is the flowers, which are often harvested when they reach full bloom to ensure maximum potency. Each individual flower contains essential oils and flavonoids that contribute to its calming effects. In addition to the blooms, the green leaves are sometimes utilized in various preparations. Whether processing a single leaf or collecting the entire mass of flos, practitioners focus on preserving the plant's bioactive compounds to treat ailments such as fever and inflammation.
