Chrysanthemum indicum

Indian chrysanthemum

Chrysanthemum indicum, commonly known as Chrysanthemum weeds or Wild Chrysanthemum, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is widely used to treat inflammation, fever, and eye ailments. This resilient plant offers potent antioxidant benefits, supporting overall wellness through its natural bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Chrysanthemum indicum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Chrysanthemum.

Plant Parts

This plant, Chrysanthemum indicum, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties found throughout its structure. The most significant medicinal component is the flowers, which are often harvested when they reach full bloom to ensure maximum potency. Each individual flower contains essential oils and flavonoids that contribute to its calming effects. In addition to the blooms, the green leaves are sometimes utilized in various preparations. Whether processing a single leaf or collecting the entire mass of flos, practitioners focus on preserving the plant's bioactive compounds to treat ailments such as fever and inflammation.
