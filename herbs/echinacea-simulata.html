Echinacea simulata

Echinacea simulata, commonly known as the prairie coneflower, is a resilient perennial native to the North American prairies. Distinguished by its prominent, cone-shaped central disk and delicate pinkish-purple petals, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its potential immune-boosting properties and its role in supporting overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Echinacea simulata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Echinacea simulata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
