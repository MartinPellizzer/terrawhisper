Callicarpa cathayana

Callicarpa cathayana, commonly known as the Beautyberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant purple berries, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Traditional practices utilize its leaves and stems to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, making it a valuable botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Callicarpa cathayana
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
