Black Bryony

Dioscorea communis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (4 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Black Bryony, scientifically known as Dioscorea communis, is a perennial climbing vine native to Europe and North Africa. Characterized by its distinctive dark berries and heart-shaped leaves, this plant contains potent alkaloids. While historically studied for medicinal properties, it is highly toxic and must be handled with extreme caution.
