Dioscorea communis

Dioscorea communis, commonly known as the common yam, is a perennial climbing vine native to Europe and parts of Asia. Belonging to the Dioscoreaceae family, this medicinal plant is valued for its tuberous roots, which contain bioactive compounds traditionally used in various folk remedies to treat diverse health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea communis
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dioscoreales. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy places it in the family Dioscoreaceae and the genus Dioscorea.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Dioscorea communis, is referred to by various local names depending on the region and language. In the Serbian dialects, it goes by the name Bljušt (sr-el) or Бљушт (sr-ec), and in Romanian, it is commonly called Untul pământului.
