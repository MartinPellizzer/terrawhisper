Corydalis crispa, scientifically known as Corydalis crispa, is a perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its bioactive alkaloids, particularly protopine, this plant has been traditionally used for its sedative and analgesic properties. It serves as a significant subject in ethnobotanical research for managing various neurological conditions.
