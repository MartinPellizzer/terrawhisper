Nicandra physalodes

Nicandra physaloides, commonly known as Apple of Peru, is a striking annual herb native to South America. Distinguished by its bell-shaped, bladder-like calyx, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically utilized in traditional medicine for its purported antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, it remains a subject of botanical interest.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Nicandra physaloides, is recognized by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In Portuguese, it is commonly referred to as Joá-de-capote, while in Nahuatl, it is called Capōltomatl. The Quiché name for this species is K'ita ch'illtu, and in French, it is identified as Nicandra physaloides. Additionally, in Spanish, it is simply known as Nicandra.
