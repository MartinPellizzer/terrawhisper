Lawsonia inermis

Lawsonia inermis, commonly known as henna, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its vibrant staining properties and therapeutic benefits. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves possess potent antifungal, antibacterial, and anti-inflammatory qualities. Often used in topical applications, it serves as a natural remedy for skin ailments and hair care.

  • Scientific name: Lawsonia inermis
  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Lythraceae and is identified by the genus Lawsonia.
