Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several key regions in Asia. Within Eastern Asia, its presence is noted in the Nansei-shoto islands as well as throughout Taiwan. Moving to the Indian Subcontinent, it is widely distributed across the country of India. It is also commonly found in the state of Assam, contributing to its regional prevalence. Finally, the species extends its reach into Bangladesh, completing its wide ecological range.
