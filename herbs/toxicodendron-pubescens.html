, scientifically known as Toxicodendron pubescens, is a potent shrub native to the western United States. Commonly called poison scrub oak, it contains urushiol, a skin irritant capable of causing severe allergic reactions. Understanding its growth patterns and chemical properties is essential for safety and ecological study in wild landscapes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution spanning across both Europe and North America. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented within the territories of Germany as well as the region encompassing Czechia and Slovakia. Moving across the Atlantic, the species can be found throughout parts of the North-Central United States. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of Illinois and Kansas. Additionally, its range extends into the state of Missouri within the same North-Central American region.
