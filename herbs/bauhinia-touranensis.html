Bauhinia touranensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Bauhinia touranensis?

Bauhinia touranenensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Fabales, the plant is a member of the family Fabaceae, which places it among the legumes. Finally, it is identified by the genus Bauhinia and is specifically categorized under the species touranenensis.
