Paragynoxys meridana

Paragynoxys meridana, commonly known as the southern medicinal herb, is a versatile botanical species valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Its unique chemical profile makes it a subject of intense interest for developing modern pharmacological treatments and natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Paragynoxys meridana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Paragynoxys.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a highly specific geographical range within the tropical regions of the world. Its occurrence is primarily concentrated in the northern part of the South American continent. Specifically, the documented presence of this species is found within the borders of Venezuela. Due to this localized pattern, the plant is considered native to this particular South American territory. Understanding this limited distribution is essential for researchers studying its natural habitat and ecological requirements.
