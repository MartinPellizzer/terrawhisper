Plant Parts

This plant, Quercus velutina (commonly known as the black oak), utilizes its leaf as a significant component in traditional botanical applications. The leaf of this species is characterized by its distinctively velvety or pubescent texture on the upper surface, which distinguishes it from other oak varieties. In various medicinal contexts, the leaf is valued for its chemical properties, often being used in infusions or decoctions to harness its tannins and other bioactive compounds.
