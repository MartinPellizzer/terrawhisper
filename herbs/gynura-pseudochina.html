Gynura pseudochina

Gynura pseudochina, commonly known as "False Chinese Gynura," is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is frequently utilized to support digestive health, reduce fever, and promote overall wellness through its natural healing potential.

  • Scientific name: Gynura pseudochina
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant, Gynura pseudochina, exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Ghana, Nigeria, and Sierra Leone. Its presence also extends into the West-Central Tropical Africa region. Specifically, documented populations exist in both Burundi and the Central African Republic. Together, these locations highlight the diverse ecological range inhabited by this medicinal species.
