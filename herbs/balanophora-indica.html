What's the taxonomical classification of Balanophora indica?

Balanophora indica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it resides in the order Santalales and is a member of the family Balanophoraceae. Specifically, this plant is categorized under the genus Balanophora, with its unique identification being the species indica.
