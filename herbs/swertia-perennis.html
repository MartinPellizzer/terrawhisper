Felwort, scientifically known as Swertia perennis, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly Ayurveda, it contains bitter glycosides used to stimulate digestion, reduce fever, and treat inflammatory conditions. This potent plant serves as a vital botanical remedy.
