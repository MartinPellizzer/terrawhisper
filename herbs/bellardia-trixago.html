Bellardia trixago

Bellaerdia trixago, commonly known as Triphala, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine. This potent formulation combines three dried fruits—Amalaki, Bibhitaki, and Haritaki—to promote holistic wellness. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it supports digestive health, boosts immunity, and serves as a natural rejuvenator for the body and mind.
