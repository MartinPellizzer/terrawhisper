What's the taxonomical classification of Lysimachia insignis?

Lysimachia insignis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, situated within the genus Lysimachia, and is specifically identified by its species name, insignis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

These blooms are supported by slender pedicels and are often accompanied by inconspicuous bracts.
