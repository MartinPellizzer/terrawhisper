Aframomum alboviolaceum

Aframomum alboviolaceum, commonly known as White-flowered Cardamom, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical African rainforests. Valued in traditional medicine, its rhizomes and leaves possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This aromatic plant offers vast pharmacological potential for treating digestive ailments and managing various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Aframomum alboviolaceum
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Africa
