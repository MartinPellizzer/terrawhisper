Isatis tinctoria subsp. tinctoria

Isatis tinctoria subsp. tinctoria, commonly known as Japanese woad, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its potent antimicrobial properties. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots and leaves contain bioactive compounds used to support immune function, fight infections, and treat respiratory ailments, making it a significant botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Isatis tinctoria subsp. tinctoria
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Isatis, specifically the subspecies tinctoria.
