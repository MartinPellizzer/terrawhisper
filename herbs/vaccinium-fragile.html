What's the taxonomical classification of Vaccinium fragile?

Vaccinium fragile belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Ericales and is a member of the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Vaccinium, and its species designation, fragile.
