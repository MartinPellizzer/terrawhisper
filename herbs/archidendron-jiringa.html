Archidendron jiringa

Archidendroon jiringa, commonly known as the "forest healer" in certain indigenous traditions, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments, serving as a vital natural resource in traditional herbal medicine practices.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
