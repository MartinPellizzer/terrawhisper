Papaver bracteatum

Papaver bracteatum, commonly known as the bracteated poppy, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique pharmacological properties. Renowned for containing high concentrations of alkaloids, it serves as a vital botanical resource in traditional medicine and modern pharmacology, offering significant therapeutic potential for pain management and various neurological treatments.

  • Scientific name: Papaver bracteatum
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Papaver, specifically identified as Papaver bracteatum.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Northern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within Sweden.
