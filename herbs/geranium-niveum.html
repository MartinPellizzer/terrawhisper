Geranium niveum

Geranium niveum, commonly known as the snow geranium, is a delicate perennial herb distinguished by its fine, silvery foliage and charming white blossoms. Native to specific montane regions, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique botanical properties and its aesthetic elegance in medicinal garden landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Geranium niveum
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Geranium.
