Ambrosia artemisiifolia

Ambrosia artemisiifolia, commonly known as ragweed, is a widespread annual herb frequently associated with seasonal allergies. Beyond its role as a potent allergen, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine. It contains bioactive compounds that have been studied for their potential antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and various pharmacological properties.

  • Scientific name: Ambrosia artemisiifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific biological identification is placed within the genus Ambrosia.
