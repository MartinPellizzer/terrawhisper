Tithonia rotundifolia

Tithonia rotundifolia, commonly known as Mexican sunflower, is a vibrant perennial celebrated for its striking orange blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is utilized in traditional medicine for its potential anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties across various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Tithonia rotundifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Tithonia and the species Tithonia rotundifolia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across several continents. In Middle Europe, it can be found within the borders of Germany. Its presence extends into Eastern Europe, specifically throughout South European Russia. The species also thrives in West-Central Tropical Africa, particularly in the DR Congo. Finally, it is distributed across Northeast Tropical Africa, covering both Ethiopia and the Sudan-South Sudan region.
