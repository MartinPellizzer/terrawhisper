Adonis microcarpa

Adonis microcarpa, commonly known as the small-flowered Adonis, is a perennial medicinal herb native to various Eurasian landscapes. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, particularly cardiac glycosides, this plant plays a significant role in traditional pharmacology. It is primarily studied for its potent effects on cardiovascular health and heart regulation.

  • Scientific name: Adonis microcarpa
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Adonis microcarppa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Adonis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a varied geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Middle Europe, its presence can be specifically noted within Germany. Moving towards the Mediterranean, it is found throughout various parts of Southwestern Europe. This includes populations located in France, Portugal, and the Baleares islands. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the island of Sardegna.
