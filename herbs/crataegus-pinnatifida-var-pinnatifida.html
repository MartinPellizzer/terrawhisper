Crataegus pinnatifida var. pinnatifida

Crataegus pinnatifida var. pinnatifida, commonly known as the Hawthorn, is a versatile medicinal plant highly valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its nutrient-rich fruits, it is frequently utilized to support cardiovascular health, aid digestion, and provide antioxidant benefits, making it a vital component in natural therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus pinnatifida var. pinnatifida
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, the specific variety Crataegus pinnatifida var. pinnatifida is identified under the genus Crataegus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Northern Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be specifically found within West Siberia. Its presence extends into the Russian Far East, covering the Amur region as well as Khabarovsk. Furthermore, the species is well-established in the Primorye territory of the Russian Far East. Finally, its range reaches into China, where it is located within Inner Mongolia.
