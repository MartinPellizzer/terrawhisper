Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Caryocar coriaceum, possesses various parts that are utilized for their medicinal and nutritional properties. The large fruits serve as the primary source of interest, particularly the nutrient-dense fruit pulp which is rich in vitamins and essential fatty acids. Extracted from these components is a highly valued fixed oil, which is frequently used in traditional applications for its emollient and healing qualities. Additionally, the bark of the tree is often employed in decoctions, contributing to the plant's diverse range of therapeutic uses in folk medicine.
