Sterculia quadrifida

Peanut tree

Sterculia quadrifidida, commonly known as the Four-lobed Sterculia, is a significant medicinal tree native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its versatile therapeutic properties, its bark, leaves, and seeds are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights.

  • Scientific name: Sterculia quadrifida
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, it is further categorized under the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Sterculia.
