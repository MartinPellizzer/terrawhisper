Sedum alfredi

What's the taxonomical classification of Sedum alfredi?

Sedum alfredi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Saxifragales. It is a member of the family Crassulaceae and is further categorized into the genus Sedum, ultimately reaching its specific identification as the species alfredi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Sedum alfredi has succulent, cylindrical, or slightly flattened leaves that are typically glaucous in color and arranged in dense, overlapping clusters along its stems. The leaves possess a fleshy texture designed for water storage and often exhibit a pale green to bluish-grey hue. Its stems are creeping or decumbent, allowing the plant to form low-growing, mat-like colonies. During the flowering period, the plant produces small, star-shaped blossoms that emerge from terminal clusters. These flowers are generally yellow or greenish-yellow, providing a sharp contrast to the muted tones of the foliage.
