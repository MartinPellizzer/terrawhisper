Alhagi pseudalhagi

Alhagi pseudalhagi, commonly known as camelthorn, is a resilient desert shrub valued in traditional medicine. Widely distributed across arid regions, it possesses significant therapeutic properties. Used to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, this medicinal plant offers promising bioactive compounds for pharmacological research and holistic healing practices.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe
