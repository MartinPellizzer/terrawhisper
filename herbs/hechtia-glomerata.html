Hechtia glomerata

Hechtia glomerata, commonly known as the clustering hechtia, is a resilient succulent native to Mexico. Characterized by its rosette of stiff, serrated leaves, this bromeliad thrives in rocky, arid environments. Beyond its striking ornamental appeal, it plays a vital role in supporting specialized pollinators within its unique desert ecosystem.

  • Scientific name: Hechtia glomerata
  • Family: Bromeliaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Poales. Finally, it is a member of the Bromeliaceae family and is categorized under the genus Hechtia.
