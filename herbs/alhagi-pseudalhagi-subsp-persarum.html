Alhagi pseudalhagi subsp. persarum

Alhagi pseudalhagi subsp. persarum, commonly known as Persian Camelthorn, is a resilient desert shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to arid regions, this subspecies possesses unique phytochemical properties. It is frequently utilized in folk remedies to treat various ailments, showcasing the significant therapeutic potential of desert flora in pharmacology.
