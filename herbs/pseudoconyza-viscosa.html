Sticky knob-bush, scientifically known as Pseudoconyza viscosa, is a resilient, aromatic shrub native to South Africa. Characterized by its distinctive sticky texture and small, clustered flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally used in various cultures to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions through its potent phytochemical properties.
