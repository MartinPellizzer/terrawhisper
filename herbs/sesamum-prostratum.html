Sesamum prostratum

Sesamum prostratum, commonly known as creeping sesame, is a low-growing medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Found across various tropical regions, this plant is prized for its diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, offering significant potential for treating skin ailments and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Sesamum prostratum
  • Family: Pedaliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Sesamum prostratum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Pedaliaceae. Finally, it is identified as a member of the genus Sesamum.
