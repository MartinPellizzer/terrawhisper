Markhamia zanzibarica

Markhamia zanzibariica, commonly known as the Zanzibar Markhamia, is a striking tropical shrub valued for its medicinal properties. Native to East Africa, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and skin conditions, making it a significant botanical resource in regional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Markhamia zanzibarica
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Bignoniaceae.
