Bell-bean

Markhamia zanzibarica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (10 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Bell-bean, scientifically known as Markhamia zanzibarica, is a striking deciduous tree native to East Africa. Renowned for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped orange flowers, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as inflammation, skin infections, and digestive issues.
