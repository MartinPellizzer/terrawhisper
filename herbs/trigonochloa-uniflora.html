Trigonochloa uniflora

Trigonochloa uniflora, commonly known as a unique medicinal grass, is a specialized botanical species valued for its therapeutic potential. Found in specific ecological niches, this plant is studied for its bioactive compounds. Understanding its traditional uses and chemical properties is essential for advancing natural pharmacology and sustainable herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Trigonochloa uniflora
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Poaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Trigonochloa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the African continent. Within West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Benin, Ghana, and Nigeria. Its range also extends into the West-Central Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it is documented to occur in both the Congo and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Consequently, its natural habitat is primarily centered in these tropical African territories.
