Cordia sinensis

Cordia sinensis, commonly known as the Chinese Cordia, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, the plant's leaves and bark are frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues, offering a natural approach to holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cordia sinensis
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Cordia.
