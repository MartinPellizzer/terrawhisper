Guazuma ulmifolia

Guazuma ulmifolia, commonly known as Guázuma or Guazuma, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently used in traditional medicine to treat gastrointestinal issues, inflammation, and skin conditions. Its bark and leaves offer significant bioactive compounds for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Guazuma ulmifolia
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, it is positioned in the family Malvaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Guazuma.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several key regions in the Indian Ocean and the Asian continent. Within the Western Indian Ocean, its presence is noted in both Mauritius and Réunion. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is widely established in India. It also maintains a significant presence across the landscape of Pakistan. Finally, the plant can be found growing within the borders of Sri Lanka.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Guácimo, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties, particularly the stem bark. The medicinal potency is often concentrated within the stem bark, making the extraction of the bark a vital step in processing the plant.
