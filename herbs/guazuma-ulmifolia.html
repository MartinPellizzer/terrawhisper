West Indian-elm

Guazuma ulmifolia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (336 studies) · ★★★★★

West Indian-elm, scientifically known as Guazuma ulmifolia, is a versatile tropical shrub celebrated in traditional medicine. Native to the Caribbean and Central America, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Its leaves and bark are frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and respiratory inflammation.
