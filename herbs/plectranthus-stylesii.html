Plectranthus stylesii

Plectranthus stylesii, commonly known as "Siddha" or "Kalthi" in traditional medicine, is a versatile perennial herb valued for its therapeutic properties. Widely distributed across tropical regions, this plant is frequently utilized in Ayurvedic and folk remedies to treat inflammation, respiratory ailments, and skin conditions, offering significant pharmacological potential.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
