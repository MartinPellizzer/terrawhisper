Bulbine narcissifolia

Bulbine narcissifolia, commonly known as Bulbine or Narcissus Leek, is a resilient succulent native to South Africa. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and promote wound healing, this hardy plant remains a fascinating subject for modern dermatological research.

  • Scientific name: Bulbine narcissifolia
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Bulbine narcissifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asparagales. Finally, it is classified under the family Asphodelaceae and the genus Bulbine.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. Its presence can be specifically noted within the Cape Provinces and the Northern Provinces. Furthermore, it thrives across various regions of Lesotho and the Free State. The species also extends its reach into the territory of Botswana. Together, these areas define the natural geographical range of Bulbine narcissifolia.
