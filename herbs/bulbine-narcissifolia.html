Strap-Leafed Bulbine, scientifically known as Bulbine narcissifolia, is a resilient succulent native to South Africa. Renowned for its fleshy, lance-shaped leaves and delicate yellow blooms, it is frequently used in traditional medicine. This hardy plant is valued for its potential therapeutic properties and its striking, ornamental beauty in gardens.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. Its presence can be specifically noted within the Cape Provinces and the Northern Provinces. Furthermore, it thrives across various regions of Lesotho and the Free State. The species also extends its reach into the territory of Botswana. Together, these areas define the natural geographical range of Bulbine narcissifolia.
