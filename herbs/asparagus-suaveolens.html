Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the countries of Kenya and Tanzania. Its presence also extends into South Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Malawi and Mozambique. Furthermore, the species is documented to occur within the borders of Zimbabwe. Collectively, these locations highlight a significant range spanning both eastern and southern tropical zones.
