Gentiana yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Gentiana yunnanensis?

Gentiana yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant falls under the order Gentianales and is a member of the family Gentianaecae. Specifically, it is situated within the genus Gentiana, which leads to its unique species designation, yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Gentiana yunnanensis has upright, herbaceous stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and feature lanceolate leaves arranged in opposite pairs. The leaves are characterized by an entire margin and a sessile attachment to the stem, often displaying a smooth or slightly textured surface. Its flowers are funnel-shaped or bell-shaped, featuring deep blue or violet corollas with prominent longitudinal veins. The petals are fused at the base to form a tube that transitions into spreading lobes at the apex. These floral structures are supported by a calyx composed of narrow, green sepals. Small, rounded seed capsules develop following pollination to facilitate the plant's reproduction.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is native to the high-altitude regions of Yunnan province in southwestern China. It primarily thrives in the mountainous terrains of the Hengduan Mountains where the climate is cool and moist. These populations are typically found growing in alpine meadows and rocky slopes at elevations ranging from 2,500 to 4,000 meters. The specific environmental conditions of these subtropical highland areas are essential for its natural survival and reproduction.
