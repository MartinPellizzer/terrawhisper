Indian pavetta, scientifically known as Pavetta indica, is a fragrant, evergreen shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its delicate, white, star-shaped flowers, this medicinal plant is widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic practices to treat skin ailments, fever, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal medicine.
