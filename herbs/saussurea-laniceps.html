Saussurea laniceps

Saussurea laniceps, commonly known as the woolly sawtooth, is a rare medicinal herb found in the high-altitude alpine meadows of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is valued for its therapeutic properties, particularly in treating respiratory ailments and regulating bodily functions through its unique bioactive chemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Saussurea laniceps
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is categorized within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Saussurea.
