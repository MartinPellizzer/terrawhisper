What's the taxonomical classification of Arisaema wattii?

Arisaema wattii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and follows the order Alismatales. This plant is a member of the family Araceae, falling under the genus Arisaema, and is specifically identified by its species name, wattii.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause severe health side effects including mouth irritation, a burning sensation, swelling of the lips or tongue, throat irritation, difficulty swallowing, nausea, vomiting, and abdominal pain. These toxic reactions typically occur when the calcium oxalate crystals in the plant come into contact with mucous membranes. The initial contact often leads to immediate localized discomfort and swelling in the oral cavity. If the plant material is ingested, it can trigger systemic gastrointestinal distress such as intense stomach pain and repeated vomiting. The resulting swelling in the throat may also lead to dangerous complications with swallowing.
