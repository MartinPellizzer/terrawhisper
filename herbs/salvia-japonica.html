Salvia japonica

Salvia japonica, commonly known as Japanese sage, is a versatile perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate flowers, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is widely utilized to address inflammation and digestive issues, serving as a vital botanical resource in herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Salvia japonica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Salvia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Salvia japonica, is recognized by various common names across different linguistic and cultural contexts. Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted in the South-Central and Southeast territories. Beyond mainland China, the species can be found growing in Japan. It also has a documented range within the Korean Peninsula. Finally, its geographical reach extends to the island of Taiwan.
