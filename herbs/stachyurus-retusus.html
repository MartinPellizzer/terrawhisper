What's the taxonomical classification of Stachyurus retusus?

Stachyurus retusus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. The plant is further categorized into the family Stachyuraceae, which contains its specific genus, Stachyurus, and ultimately identifies it as the species retusus.
