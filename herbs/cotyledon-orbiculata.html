Cotyledon orbiculata

Dogís ears

Cotyledon orbiculata, commonly known as the pig's ear, is a resilient succulent native to South Africa. Renowned for its thick, fleshy leaves and striking reddish edges, this plant is valued in herbal traditions for its potential medicinal properties, including its use in treating skin irritations and promoting wound healing.

  • Scientific name: Cotyledon orbiculata
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Saxifragales and the family Crasulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cotyledon.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the Mediterranean region, spanning both Europe and Africa. Within Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in the Balearic Islands. Its presence extends to the mainland territories of France, Portugal, and Spain. Furthermore, the species reaches into Northern Africa, specifically inhabiting Tunisia. Together, these locations define its distinct geographical range.
