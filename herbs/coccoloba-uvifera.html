Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Coccoloba uvifera or sea grape, features distinct and highly characteristic leaves that serve as a primary identifier for the species. The leaves are large, leathery, and broadly orbicular in shape, often appearing somewhat kidney-shaped with a rounded or slightly indented apex. They possess a thick, waxy cuticle that helps the plant retain moisture in coastal environments, and they are often characterized by prominent, palmate venation that radiates from the base. In many medicinal applications, these robust leaves are the parts most frequently studied or utilized for their various bioactive properties.
