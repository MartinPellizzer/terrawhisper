Seseli resinosum

Seseli resinosum, commonly known as resinous seseli, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Native to mountainous regions, this plant is distinguished by its aromatic, resinous properties. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it holds significant interest for its unique chemical compounds and potential therapeutic applications in pharmacology.

  • Family: Apiaceae
