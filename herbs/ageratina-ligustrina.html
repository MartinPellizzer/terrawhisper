Ageratina ligustrina

Ageratina ligustrina, commonly known as white snakeroot, is a perennial herb native to the Americas. While it possesses unique botanical characteristics, it is famously noted for containing tremetol, a toxin. This plant is historically significant due to its role in causing milk sickness in humans and livestock.

  • Scientific name: Ageratina ligustrina
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Taxonomically, it is specifically identified under the genus Ageratina, with its species name being ligustrina.
