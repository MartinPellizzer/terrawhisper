Senega altomontana

Senega altomontana, commonly known as Senega snakeroot, is a perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Historically prized in traditional medicine, it contains potent expectorant alkaloids. These compounds help soothe respiratory distress by stimulating mucus secretion, making it a significant botanical subject for treating coughs and bronchial congestion.

  • Scientific name: Senega altomontana
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Polygalaeacea. Finally, it is identified by the genus Senega, specifically as the species Senega altomontana.
