Hedychium yunnanense

Hedychium yunnanense, commonly known as the Yunnanian ginger lily, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for therapeutic use.

  • Scientific name: Hedychium yunnanense
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is classified under the family Zingiberaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Hedychium, with the species name yunnanense.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the diverse landscapes of East and Southeast Asia. Its range extends across various regions of China, specifically encompassing the South-Central territories. Additionally, it can be located within the Southeast portions of the country. Beyond the borders of China, the species is also distributed throughout the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, its presence is documented within the tropical environment of Vietnam.
