Protea cynaroides

King protea

Protea cynaroides, commonly known as the King Protea, is a magnificent South African perennial celebrated for its striking, artichoke-like blooms. Beyond its floral beauty, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional healing to treat various ailments, ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, showcasing its profound botanical significance.

  • Scientific name: Protea cynaroides
  • Family: Proteaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Proteales, it is a member of the Proteaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Protea, where it is known by the species name Protea cynaroides.

Distribution

This plant is native to the southern region of the African continent. Specifically, its natural habitat is confined to the Cape Provinces of South Africa. Within this area, it flourishes in the unique Mediterranean climate characteristic of the fynbos biome. The distribution is highly localized, making it a significant feature of the regional flora. Consequently, this species is a quintessential representative of the Cape's diverse botanical landscape.
