Conker tree, scientifically known as Aesculus hippocasstanum, is a deciduous species prized for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Primarily recognized for its seeds, the plant contains aescin, a compound widely used in pharmacology to treat venous insufficiency, reduce swelling, and improve circulation in patients with chronic vascular issues.
