Aesculus hippocastanum

Aesculus hippocascanum, commonly known as horse chestnut, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Rich in saponins and flavonoids, its seeds are primarily used to treat venous insufficiency and chronic swelling. This botanical powerhouse offers significant support for circulatory health and reducing inflammation in vascular conditions.

  • Scientific name: Aesculus hippocastanum
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Aesculus hippocastanum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Sapindaceae within the genus Aesculus.
