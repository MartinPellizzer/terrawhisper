Scabiosa arenaria

Scabiosa arenaria, commonly known as the sand scabious, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal and sandy habitats. Renowned for its delicate, pincushion-like blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest. It is traditionally studied for its potential medicinal properties, particularly in treating skin ailments and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Scabiosa arenaria
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales, it falls under the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Scabiosa.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Scabiosa arenaria, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is often called sand scabious, while in German, it is known as Sand-Skabiose. In Spanish, the plant is referred to as escabiosa de las arenas, and in French, it is known as scabieuse des sables.

Distribution

This plant, known as Scabiosa arenaria, is primarily distributed across the northern reaches of the African continent. Its presence is recorded in the coastal and inland regions of Morocco and Algeria. Additionally, the species can be found throughout the territories of Tunisia and Libya. The geographical range extends further east to include parts of Egypt. Collectively, these locations highlight its specific adaptation to the North African landscape.
