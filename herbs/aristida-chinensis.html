Aristida chinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Aristida chinensis?

Aristida chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its classification through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed under the order Poales. It is a member of the family Poaceae, specifically falling within the genus Aristida, and is identified by the species name chinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aristida chinensis has an annual growth habit characterized by slender, upright culms that typically reach heights of 10 to 50 centimeters. The leaves consist of narrow, linear blades with scabrous surfaces and sheaths that are often somewhat compressed. Its inflorescence is a terminal panicle containing numerous small, spikelet-like units. Each spikelet features a distinctive lemma that splits into three long, needle-like awns at the apex. These prominent awns facilitate seed dispersal and help the plant anchor itself into the soil.
