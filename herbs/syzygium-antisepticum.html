Syzygium antisepticum

Syzygium antisepticum, commonly known as the clove antiseptic, is a medicinal plant prized for its potent antimicrobial properties. Rich in essential oils like eugenol, it serves as a powerful natural disinfectant. Traditionally used to treat skin infections and oral ailments, this versatile species remains a cornerstone in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium antisepticum
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Syzygium.
