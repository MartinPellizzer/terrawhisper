Actinidia polygama, scientifically known as Actinidia polygama, is a vigorous deciduous vine native to East Asia. Commonly referred to as the mountain kiwi, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional herbalism for its nutrient-dense fruit and bioactive compounds, which are studied for their potential antioxidant and health-promoting properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its range extends to the Kuril Islands and the Primorye region. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central territories. The species also inhabits the northern stretches of China, specifically within Inner Mongolia. Additionally, its presence is noted throughout the Manchuria region.
