What's the taxonomical classification of Astragalus monadelphus?

Astragalus monadelphus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it under the order Fabales. As a member of the family Fabaceae, this plant is further categorized into the genus Astragalus, specifically identifying as the species monadelphus.
