Hypericum papuanum

Hypericum papuanum, commonly known as a unique species of St. John's Wort, is a medicinal plant native to the lush landscapes of Papua New Guinea. Renowned for its distinct botanical features, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering potential therapeutic properties for various traditional healing practices and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum papuanum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling into the family Hypericaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Hypericum.
