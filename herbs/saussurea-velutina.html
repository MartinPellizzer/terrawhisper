Saussurea velutina

What's the taxonomical classification of Saussurea velutina?

Saussurea velutina belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, which is characterized by its composite flower heads, and is specifically placed within the genus Saussurea. Ultimately, its complete scientific designation is defined by its species name, velutina.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Saussurea velutina has a basal rosette of lanceolate leaves that are covered in dense, velvety, white hairs on both surfaces. These leaves feature prominent longitudinal veins and slightly serrated margins that aid in moisture retention. The flowering stem is typically upright and carries a compact inflorescence composed of numerous small, yellow flower heads. Each individual floret is surrounded by involucral bracts that exhibit a soft, felt-like texture. The overall structure of the plant is adapted to withstand harsh, high-altitude environments through its thick pubescence.
