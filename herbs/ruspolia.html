Ruspolia

Ruspolia, commonly known as a precious botanical treasure, belongs to the Apocynaceae family. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this plant is celebrated for its unique bioactive compounds. Often sought for its potential therapeutic properties, Ruspolia offers fascinating insights into natural pharmacology, making it a subject of significant ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Ruspolia
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Ruspolia.
