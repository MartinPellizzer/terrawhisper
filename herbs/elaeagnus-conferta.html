Elaeagnus conferta

Elaeagnus conferta, commonly known as the Sea Olive, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to coastal regions. Renowned for its ability to thrive in saline environments, this plant offers significant ethnobotanical value. Its unique properties and hardiness make it a subject of interest for both traditional medicine and landscaping.

  • Scientific name: Elaeagnus conferta
  • Family: Elaeagnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Rosales and the family Elaeagnaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Elaeagnus.
