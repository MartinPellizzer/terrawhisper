Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the globe. In Middle Europe, its presence can be observed within the borders of Germany. Its range extends southward to Northern Africa, specifically documented in Tunisia. The species is also found within the Macaronesian islands, including both the Canary Islands and Madeira. Finally, its distribution reaches West Tropical Africa, where it grows in the Ivory Coast.
