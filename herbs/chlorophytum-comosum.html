Chlorophytum comosum

Fresa

Chlorophyutum comosum, commonly known as the Spider Plant, is a resilient ornamental plant valued for its air-purifying qualities. Beyond its decorative appeal, it holds significance in traditional medicine. Often used to address inflammation and digestive issues, this versatile plant serves as both a natural home cleanser and a botanical remedy.

  • Scientific name: Chlorophytum comosum
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asparagaceae and is identified by the genus Chlorophytum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the globe. In Middle Europe, its presence can be observed within the borders of Germany. Its range extends southward to Northern Africa, specifically documented in Tunisia. The species is also found within the Macaronesian islands, including both the Canary Islands and Madeira. Finally, its distribution reaches West Tropical Africa, where it grows in the Ivory Coast.
