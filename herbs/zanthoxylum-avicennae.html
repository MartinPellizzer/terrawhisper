Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence also extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, it is documented to inhabit the countries of Laos and Thailand. Therefore, the species thrives across diverse habitats from coastal China to Southeast Asian mainland forests.
