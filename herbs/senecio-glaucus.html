Senecio glaucus

Seneciio glaucus, commonly known as Blue Groundsel, is a resilient succulent perennial native to coastal regions. Characterized by its glaucous, fleshy leaves and striking yellow blooms, this plant thrives in sandy soils. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest for its unique chemical properties and medicinal potential.

  • Scientific name: Senecio glaucus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Senecio.
