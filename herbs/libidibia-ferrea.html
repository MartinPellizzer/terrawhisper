Libidibia ferrea

Libidibia ferrea, commonly known as ironwood, is a resilient medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it is frequently utilized to support vitality and address various physiological imbalances. This robust species offers a rich profile of bioactive compounds essential for holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Libidibia ferrea
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Fabales and placed in the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Libidiibia.
