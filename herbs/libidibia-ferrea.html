Pau ferro, scientifically known as Libidiia ferrea, is a majestic Brazilian tree celebrated for its striking, patterned bark and dense timber. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant value in traditional medicine, where various parts are utilized to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive ailments through potent bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution that spans several distinct tropical regions across the globe. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is commonly found growing in both India and Pakistan. Its presence extends into the Malesia region, specifically documented in Malaya. Furthermore, the species inhabits parts of Papuasia, notably within New Guinea. Finally, its range reaches the Americas, where it can be located in the Northeast region of Brazil.

Plant Parts

This plant, Libidibia ferrea, commonly known as Pau Brasil, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The medicinal value is frequently found within the stem bark, which is often harvested to prepare decoctions, though in some traditional practices, the larger stem barks are specifically processed for their tannins. The botanical structure also includes distinctive fruits that develop into elongated, woody pods. Contained within these structures are the seed units, which complete the plant's reproductive cycle and contribute to the overall botanical profile of this medicinal species.
