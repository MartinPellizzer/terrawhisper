Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range that spans across several distinct regions of East Asia and North America. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Moving south, the species is widely distributed throughout Manchuria in China and across Eastern Asia in Korea. Interestingly, its range extends far beyond its primary Asian habitats to include parts of the North-Central United States, specifically Illinois. This diverse distribution highlights the plant's ability to adapt to varying environmental conditions across different continents.
