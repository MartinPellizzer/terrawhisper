Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of Africa. In Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is documented across the territories of Djibouti and Ethiopia. It is also found within the borders of Somalia, further establishing its range in the northeast. Additionally, the species extends into East Tropical Africa, specifically within Kenya. Consequently, its habitat is primarily limited to these distinct East African nations.
