Evolvulus nummularius

Volvulopsis nummularium

Evolvulus nummularius, commonly known as Kidneyweed, is a creeping perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its small, round leaves and delicate blue flowers, this plant is widely used for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in treating various ailments within diverse herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Evolvulus nummularius
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Evolvulus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Burkina, Ghana, and the Ivory Coast. Moving toward the center of the continent, its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa. Within this central region, the species is documented in Burundi as well as the Central African Republic. Consequently, the presence of Evolvulus nummularius spans a diverse stretch of tropical environments.
