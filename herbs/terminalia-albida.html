Terminalia albida

Terminalia albida, commonly known as the White Terminalia, is a resilient deciduous tree native to various tropical regions. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its bark, leaves, and roots possess significant bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues across diverse ethnobotanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia albida
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Myrtales, it is a member of the family Combretaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Terminalia.
