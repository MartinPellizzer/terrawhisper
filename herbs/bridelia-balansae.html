Bridelia balansae belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical classification, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. This species is further organized under the order Malpighiales and falls within the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Bridelia, with its unique designation being the species balansae.
