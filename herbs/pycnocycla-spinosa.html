Pycnocycla spinosa

Pycnocycla spinosa, commonly known as the thorny acacia in certain regions, is a resilient medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, serving as a vital natural resource in various ethnobotanical practices.

  • Scientific name: Pycnocycla spinosa
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
