Breynia rostrata

Breynia rostrata, commonly known as a member of the Phyllanthaceae family, is a distinctive medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its unique morphological features, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to explore potential therapeutic applications in treating various ailments and enhancing wellness.

  • Scientific name: Breynia rostrata
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Breynia.
