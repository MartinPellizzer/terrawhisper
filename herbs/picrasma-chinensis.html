Picrasma chinensis, scientifically known as Picrasma chinensis, is a medicinal plant native to East Asia, valued primarily for its intense bitterness. Renowned in traditional medicine, its bark contains potent alkaloids like quassinoids, which serve as powerful emetics, febrifuges, and anthelmintics, offering significant therapeutic potential in treating various ailments.
