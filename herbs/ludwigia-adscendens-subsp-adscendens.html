Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is recorded in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Furthermore, it can be found growing in the Nansei-shoto islands. The species also has a documented range within Taiwan. Together, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of this medicinal plant.
