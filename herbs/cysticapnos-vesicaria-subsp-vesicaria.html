Cysticapnos vesicaria subsp. vesicaria

Cysticapnos vesicaria subsp. vesicaria, commonly known as the bladder bladderweed, is a specialized medicinal herb found in unique ecosystems. Renowned for its distinct bladder-like structures, this subspecies contains potent bioactive compounds. Traditional practitioners utilize its unique botanical properties to support holistic healing and address various inflammatory ailments effectively.

  • Scientific name: Cysticapnos vesicaria subsp. vesicaria
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
