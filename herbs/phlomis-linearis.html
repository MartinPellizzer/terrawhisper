Phlomis linearis

Phlomis linearis, commonly known as the Narrow-leaved Sage, is a striking perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its distinctive whorled leaves and tubular yellow flowers, this resilient plant thrives in rocky terrains, offering significant ornamental value and traditional medicinal properties within various botanical healing practices.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
