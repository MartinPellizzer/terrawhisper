Wood Dock

Rumex sanguineus · Accepted scientific name

Wood Dock, scientifically known as Rumex sanguineus, is a perennial herb distinguished by its vibrant, reddish rhizomes. Often found in moist, disturbed habitats, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its astringent and diuretic properties. Its distinctive coloring and resilient nature make it a unique botanical specimen.
