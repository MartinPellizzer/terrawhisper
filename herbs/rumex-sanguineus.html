Rumex sanguineus

Wood dock

Rumex sanguineus, commonly known as blood sorrel, is a perennial herb valued for its distinctive reddish stems and tart, acidic leaves. Rich in oxalic acid and Vitamin C, it serves as both a nutritious edible green and a traditional medicinal plant used to support digestive health and manage inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Rumex sanguineus
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rumex.
