Tree Fuschia, scientifically known as Halleria lucida, is a striking evergreen shrub native to the rainforests of Southeast Asia. Renowned for its vibrant, bell-shaped flowers and glossy foliage, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds, often used to treat various inflammatory conditions.
