Halleria lucida

Halleria lucida, commonly known as the "medicinal plant of the forest," is a significant species in traditional Southeast Asian medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is primarily utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering promising therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research for various healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Halleria lucida
  • Family: Stilbaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Stilbaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Halleria, specifically as the species Halleria lucida.
