Morinda elliptica, scientifically known as Morinda elliptica, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits. Its leaves, roots, and fruits are utilized to treat various ailments, including skin disorders, inflammation, and digestive issues, showcasing its profound healing potential.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is documented in both Myanmar and Thailand. Additionally, the species extends into the Malesia zone, specifically found in Malaya. These locations highlight the plant's preference for the humid, subtropical climates typical of these areas. Consequently, its natural habitat is primarily defined by these interconnected Southeast Asian territories.
