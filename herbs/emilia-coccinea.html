Cupid's-paintbrush, scientifically known as Emilia coccinea, is a vibrant perennial herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its striking, scarlet, brush-like flowers, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing properties across various diverse healing practices.
