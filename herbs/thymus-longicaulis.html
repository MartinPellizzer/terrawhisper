Thymus longicaulis

Thymus longicaulis, commonly known as creeping thyme, is a resilient perennial herb native to rocky, arid regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate flowering stems, this medicinal plant offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support respiratory health and digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Thymus longicaulis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Thymus.
