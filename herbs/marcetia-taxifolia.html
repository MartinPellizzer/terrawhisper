Marcetia taxifolia

Marcetia taxifolia, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds immense botanical interest. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological potential, this species is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices. Research focuses on its bioactive compounds, exploring its therapeutic efficacy in treating various ailments and supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Marcetia taxifolia
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it is specifically situated in the family Melastomataceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Marcetia.
