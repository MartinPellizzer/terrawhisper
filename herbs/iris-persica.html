Iris persica

Persian iris

Iris persica, commonly known as the Persian Iris, is a striking perennial celebrated for its vibrant, bell-shaped blooms. Native to Western Asia, this resilient plant thrives in sunny, well-drained soils. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds traditional significance in botanical studies and regional herbal practices throughout its native habitats.

  • Scientific name: Iris persica
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, it is a member of the family Iridaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Iris.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Iris persica, is referred to by various common names across different languages and regions. In Persian, it is called زنبق ایرانی (fa), while in Arabic, it is identified as سوسن فارسي (ar). In Punjabi, the name used is فارسی ست رنگی (pnb), and in the dialect of Diq, it is known as Kolbıza (diq). Additionally, in Czech, the plant is commonly called kosatec perský (cs).
