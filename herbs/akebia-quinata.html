Plant Parts

This plant, Akebiia quinata, is a woody vine characterized by its climbing stems that support its sprawling growth habit. The most distinctive feature of the species is its large, elongated fruits, which are botanically unique due to their structure. Scientific investigations often focus on the pericarps of Akebiia quinata fruits to isolate bioactive compounds, frequently employing an n-BuOH extract to concentrate specific phytochemicals for medicinal study. Through the analysis of the stem and the fruit layers, researchers continue to uncover the diverse therapeutic potential held within various parts of this medicinal species.
