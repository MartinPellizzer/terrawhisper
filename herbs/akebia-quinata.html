Akebia quinata

Five-leaf akebia

Akebiia quinata, commonly known as Akebia or Chocolate Vine, is a versatile deciduous woody vine native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized for their antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential in treating respiratory ailments and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Akebia quinata
  • Family: Lardizabalaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Lardizabalaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Akebiia.
