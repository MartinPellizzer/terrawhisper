Creeping Psychotria, scientifically known as Psychotria serpens, is a distinctive medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its prostrate, trailing stems and lush foliage, this plant is frequently utilized for its bioactive compounds. It plays a significant role in ethnobotany, offering potential therapeutic benefits for various ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated throughout several regions of Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the Hainan province as well as across China Southeast. Its distribution extends further into Japan, specifically encompassing the Nansei-shoto islands. Additionally, the species is found throughout the territory of Taiwan.
