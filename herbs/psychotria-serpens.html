Psychotria serpens

Psychotria serpens, commonly known as the snake plant, is a fascinating medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique physiological properties, it is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Psychotria serpens
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Psychotria serpens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Gentianales. Finally, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family and falls under the genus Psychotria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated throughout several regions of Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the Hainan province as well as across China Southeast. Its distribution extends further into Japan, specifically encompassing the Nansei-shoto islands. Additionally, the species is found throughout the territory of Taiwan. Together, these locations define the natural habitat of Psychotria serpens within the East Asian landscape.
