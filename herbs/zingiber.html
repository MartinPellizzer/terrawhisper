Ginger, scientifically known as Zingiber, is a versatile rhizomatous herb celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned for its warming effects, it effectively alleviates nausea, aids digestion, and reduces inflammation. Beyond its culinary use, this aromatic plant serves as a powerful natural remedy in traditional and modern medicine.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several tropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands.
