Zingiber

Ginger

Zingiber, commonly known as ginger, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its potent therapeutic properties. Belonging to the Zingiberaceae family, its pungent rhizomes are rich in bioactive compounds like gingerol. Renowned for aiding digestion, reducing inflammation, and alleviating nausea, it remains a cornerstone in traditional and modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Zingiber
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Zingiber.
