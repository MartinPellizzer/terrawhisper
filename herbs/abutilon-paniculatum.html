What's the taxonomical classification of Abutilon paniculatum?

Abutilon paniculatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant is situated in the order Malvales and is a member of the family Malvaceae. Finally, its specific identity is defined by the genus Abutilon and the species paniculatum.
