Commiphora guidottii

Scented myrrh

Commiphora guidottii, commonly known as a species of myrrh, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid African landscapes. Renowned for its aromatic resin, it possesses significant therapeutic potential. Traditionally used to treat inflammation and skin ailments, this plant offers valuable bioactive compounds for modern pharmacological research and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Commiphora guidottii
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Sapindales and the family Burseraceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Commiphora as the species Commiphora guidottii.
