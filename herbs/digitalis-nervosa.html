Digitalis nervosa

Digitalis nervosa, commonly known as Nervy Foxglove, is a perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its striking bell-shaped flowers, this plant contains potent cardiac glycosides. While historically significant in traditional medicine for heart ailments, its extreme toxicity necessitates cautious handling and careful scientific study to prevent poisoning.

  • Scientific name: Digitalis nervosa
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Digitalis nervosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginacea. Finally, it is identified by the genus Digitalis.
