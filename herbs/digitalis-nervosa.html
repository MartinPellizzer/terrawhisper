Digitalis nervosa, scientifically known as Digitalis nervosa, is a perennial herb belonging to the Plantaginaceae family. Native to parts of Europe, this plant is characterized by its bell-shaped flowers. While related to more common foxgloves, it contains potent cardiac glycosides, making it a subject of significant pharmacological interest.
