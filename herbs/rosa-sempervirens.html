Rosa sempervirens

Rosa sempervirens, commonly known as the evergreen rose, is a resilient shrub celebrated for its year-round foliage and delicate blooms. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant offers therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antiseptic qualities. Its unique ability to thrive across seasons makes it a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Rosa sempervirens
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosalales, falling into the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Rosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing across the diverse landscapes of France and Portugal. Its distribution also extends to several significant island territories within this area. Specifically, it is located in the Baleares and Sardegna. Finally, the species is also documented inhabiting the island of Corse.
