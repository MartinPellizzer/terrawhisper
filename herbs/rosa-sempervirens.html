Evergreen rose, scientifically known as Rosa sempervirens, is a resilient, perennial shrub cherished for its year-round foliage and fragrant blooms. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile botanical offers therapeutic benefits, ranging from skin rejuvenation to soothing digestive ailments through its essential extracts.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosalales, falling into the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Rosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing across the diverse landscapes of France and Portugal. Its distribution also extends to several significant island territories within this area. Specifically, it is located in the Baleares and Sardegna.
