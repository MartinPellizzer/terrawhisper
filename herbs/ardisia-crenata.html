Ardisia crenata

Christmas berry

Ardisia crenata, commonly known as Christmas berry, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its vibrant red fruit. Native to tropical regions, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin issues, and digestive irregularities through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Ardisia crenata
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, falling under the family Primulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Ardisia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ardisia crenata, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Finnish, it is simply called ardisia, while in Chinese-speaking regions, its names reflect its traditional usage; it is known as 朱砂根 (Zhūshāgēn) in Simplified Chinese (zh-hans and zh-cn) and as 硃砂根 (Zhūshāgēn) in Traditional Chinese contexts, including those used in Hong Kong (zh-hant and zh-hk).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across different regions of Asia and the Indian Ocean. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found inhabiting the islands of Mauritius, Réunion, and the Seychelles. Moving toward the Asian continent, its range extends into the South-Central regions of China. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the tropical environment of Hainan. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various island and mainland habitats.
