Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifragales, it is a member of the Crassulaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Rhodiola, with the species name yunnanensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. It can also be found growing in the high-altitude terrains of Tibet. Beyond the borders of China, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it is documented to inhabit the region of Assam.
