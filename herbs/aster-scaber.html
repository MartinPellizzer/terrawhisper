Aster scaber

Aster scaber, commonly known as the rough aster, is a resilient perennial wildflower native to North America. Renowned for its textured foliage and vibrant purplish blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various indigenous cultures utilized its properties in traditional medicine to treat ailments and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Aster scaber
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Aster scaber, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Aster.
