Cyclotrichium origanifolium

Cyclotrichium origanifolium, commonly known as Oregano-leaved Cyclotrichium, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued for its unique aromatic properties. Characterized by its serrated, oregano-like foliage, this species is studied for its bioactive compounds. It offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in traditional herbal practices for addressing various inflammatory and digestive ailments.
