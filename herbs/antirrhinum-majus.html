Antirrhinum majus

Antirrhinum majus, commonly known as snapdragon, is a vibrant ornamental plant widely celebrated for its unique, mask-like blooms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, various cultures have traditionally utilized its botanical properties in folk medicine, exploring its potential for treating inflammation and minor ailments through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe
