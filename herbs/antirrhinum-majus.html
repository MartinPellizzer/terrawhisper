Antirrhinum majus

Snapdragons

Antirrhinum majus, commonly known as snapdragon, is a vibrant ornamental plant widely celebrated for its unique, mask-like blooms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, various cultures have traditionally utilized its botanical properties in folk medicine, exploring its potential for treating inflammation and minor ailments through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Antirrhinum majus
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Antirrhinum.
