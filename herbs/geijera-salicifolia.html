Geijera salicifolia

Geijera salicifolia, commonly known as the Willow-leaved Geijer, is a distinctive shrub native to the rainforests of Australia. Renowned for its slender, willow-like foliage, this plant plays a vital role in its ecosystem. While primarily studied for its unique botanical characteristics, it holds significant interest in traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Geijera salicifolia
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is further categorized under the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Geijera.

Common Names

This plant, Geijeria salicifolia, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the Southern Hemisphere. Within the Papuasia region, it can be found growing in New Guinea. Its presence in Australia is widespread, spanning from the coastal areas of New South Wales to the tropical landscapes of Queensland. Additionally, the species extends its range into the western part of the continent in Western Australia. Finally, its habitat reaches into the Southwestern Pacific, specifically within New Caledonia.
