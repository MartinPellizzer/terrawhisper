Dracocephalum

Dracocephalum, commonly known as dragonhead, is a versatile genus of aromatic perennial herbs within the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for their striking, tubular flowers and distinctively fragrant foliage, these plants are valued in traditional medicine for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant potential in modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Dracocephalum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Dracocephalum.
