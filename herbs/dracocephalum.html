Dragonhead, scientifically known as Dracocephalum, is a versatile perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its striking, colorful blooms and aromatic foliage, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. It is widely utilized in traditional practices to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and provide calming therapeutic effects.
