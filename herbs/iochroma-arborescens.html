Iochroma arborescens

Iochroma arborescens, commonly known as the Tree Iochroma, is a stunning ornamental shrub celebrated for its trumpet-shaped, bell-like flowers. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this botanical species holds significant potential in ethnobotanical studies, offering intriguing possibilities for medicinal applications and traditional healing practices within its native tropical habitats.

  • Family: Solanaceae
