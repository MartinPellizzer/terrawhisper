Emerging in early spring, these nodding blooms feature distinctive green-spotted tips. While primarily ornamental, this hardy woodland plant adds a touch of elegance to shady gardens and naturalized landscapes.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Spring Snowflake, is a perennial herb characterized by its distinct flowering structure and underground storage organs. While the visible portions include elegant, bell-shaped white flowers with green-tipped petals and slender green leaves, the most significant medicinal component is found beneath the soil. The fresh bulbs serve as the primary reservoir for nutrients and are the specific part utilized in traditional preparations, as they contain the concentrated bioactive compounds required for its therapeutic applications.
