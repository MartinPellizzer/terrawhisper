Leucojum vernum

Spring snowflake

Leucojum vernum, commonly known as the spring snowflake, is a delicate perennial bulb native to European woodlands. Distinguished by its bell-shaped, white flowers with greenish spots, it emerges in early spring. Traditionally used in herbalism, this enchanting plant symbolizes purity and serves as a charming addition to botanical gardens.

  • Scientific name: Leucojum vernum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Leucojum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Leucojum vernum, is recognized by various local names across different regions. In Poland, it is called Śnieżyca wiosenna, while in France, it is referred to as Nivéole de printemps. In Russia, the species is known as Белоцветник весенний, and in the Frisian language, it goes by the name Maarteblomke. Additionally, in Romania, it is commonly called Lușcă.
