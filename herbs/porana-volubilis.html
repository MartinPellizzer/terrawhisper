Porana volubilis

Porana volubiilis, commonly known as the climbing vine, is a remarkable medicinal plant featured in various traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this climbing species is often utilized for its potential therapeutic benefits, offering significant interest to researchers exploring natural remedies for holistic health and wellness.

  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant, Porana volubilis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Porana.
