Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within Southeastern Europe and Western Asia. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is documented in Greece as well as throughout the Northwestern Balkan Peninsula. Moving into Western Asia, the species can be found growing on the island of Cyprus. Its range further extends across the Levant, specifically within the territories of Lebanon and Syria.
