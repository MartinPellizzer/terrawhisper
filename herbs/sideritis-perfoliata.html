Sideritis perfoliata

Sideritis perfoliata l.

Sideritis perfoliata, commonly known as Perfoliate Dittany, is a resilient medicinal herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its unique leaf structure, this plant has been used traditionally to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin inflammations. Its potent bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Sideritis perfoliata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Sideritis perfoliata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Sideritis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within Southeastern Europe and Western Asia. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is documented in Greece as well as throughout the Northwestern Balkan Peninsula. Moving into Western Asia, the species can be found growing on the island of Cyprus. Its range further extends across the Levant, specifically within the territories of Lebanon and Syria. Finally, the geographical distribution of Sideritis perfoliata includes the region of Palestine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Sideritis perfoliata, is primarily utilized in herbal medicine through its leaves and the overall aerial part. The leaves are characterized by their unique arrangement, often appearing to wrap around the stem, and they contain the essential bioactive compounds responsible for the plant's therapeutic properties. When preparing medicinal teas or extracts, practitioners typically collect the entire aerial part, which includes the stems and foliage, to ensure a complete profile of the plant's beneficial phytochemicals.
