fringed-gentian, scientifically known as Gentianopsis, is a remarkable perennial herb native to the high-altitude alpine meadows of Asia. Renowned for its striking, star-shaped violet flowers and delicate fringed petals, this resilient plant holds significant value in traditional medicine for its potent bitter compounds and therapeutic properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a scattered presence across several distinct regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its range extends from the rugged landscapes of Iceland to the coastal areas of Norway. It can also be found growing within the territories of Great Britain.
