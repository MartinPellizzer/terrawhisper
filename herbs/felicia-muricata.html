Wild Aster, scientifically known as Felicia muricata, is a resilient perennial herb native to southern Africa. Renowned for its vibrant blue daisy-like blooms, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its soothing properties. It serves as a hardy addition to gardens, offering both aesthetic beauty and therapeutic potential.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several different regions of the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Kenya. Its presence extends into South Tropical Africa, specifically within the territories of Angola and Zimbabwe. Moving further south, the species is also distributed throughout Botswana in Southern Africa. Finally, its geographical reach includes the Cape Provinces of the Southern African region.
