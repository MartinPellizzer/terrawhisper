Tamarix gallica

Tamarix anglica

Tamarix gallica, commonly known as French tamarisk, is a resilient, salt-tolerant shrub native to Mediterranean regions. Renowned for its delicate, scale-like foliage and pinkish flower clusters, this plant thrives in saline soils. In traditional medicine, its extracts are valued for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Tamarix gallica
  • Family: Tamaricaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Moving through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is a member of the family Tamaricaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Tamarix.
