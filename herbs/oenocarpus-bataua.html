Oenocarpus bataua

Palm

Oenocarpus bataua, commonly known as Pataua, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the Amazon rainforest. Highly valued for its nutrient-dense oil, it serves as a potent natural moisturizer. Rich in fatty acids and antioxidants, this botanical treasure supports skin health, hair vitality, and overall wellness through traditional applications.

  • Scientific name: Oenocarpus bataua
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Arecales and family Arecaceae, it is classified under the genus Oenocarpus. Specifically, this species is identified by its scientific name, Oenocarpus bataua.

Common Names

This plant is known by several different names depending on the language and region. In Portuguese, it is commonly called Patauá, while in Guaraní, it is referred to as Patagua. The scientific variety is also used as a common name in Spanish and Asturian, appearing as Oenocarpus bataua var. bataua. Additionally, in the Yorùbá language, the plant is known as Ope.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions in the Neotropics. In Central America, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Panamá. Moving into the Caribbean, it can be found inhabiting the islands of Trinidad and Tobago. Its range extends significantly into Northern South America, covering the territory of French Guiana. Finally, the species is also widely documented throughout Guyana and Suriname.
