Moullava digyna

Moullava digyna, commonly known as the Indian medicinal plant, is a significant botanical species valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its therapeutic potential offers promising insights into natural remedies and modern integrative medicine developments.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Moullava digyna, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the Fabaceae family and falls under the genus Moullava.
