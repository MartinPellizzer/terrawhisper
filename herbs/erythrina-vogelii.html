Erythrina vogelii

Erythrina vogelii, commonly known as a medicinal legume, is a significant plant species utilized in traditional healing practices. Primarily found in tropical regions, it is valued for its diverse pharmacological properties. Research focuses on its bioactive compounds, which may offer potential therapeutic benefits for treating various inflammatory and metabolic conditions.
