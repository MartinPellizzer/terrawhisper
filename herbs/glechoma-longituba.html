Glechoma longituba

Glechoma longituba, commonly known as moneywort, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Characterized by its creeping habit and heart-shaped leaves, this plant is frequently utilized for its cooling properties. It is often employed to treat respiratory ailments, reduce inflammation, and support overall detoxification within herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Glechoma longituba
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Glechoma. Specifically, it is identified by the species name Glechoma longituba.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing within the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye territories. Its range also extends significantly into China, where it is documented in the South-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is known to inhabit the Inner Mongolia area of China. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varied northern and central Asian environments.
