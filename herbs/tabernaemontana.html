Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the nation of Benin, contributing to the local biodiversity. Its presence extends to the Gambia, where it inhabits various ecological niches. The species is also documented throughout Ghana, marking another significant area of its range. Finally, its distribution continues through Guinea-Bissau and Guinea, completing its presence across these West African territories.
