Tabernaemontana

Tabernaemontana, commonly known as Crape Jasmine, is a diverse genus of flowering shrubs and trees within the Apocynaceae family. Renowned for their fragrant, white blossoms, these plants are highly valued in traditional medicine. Various species are utilized globally for their diverse bioactive compounds to treat diverse ailments.
