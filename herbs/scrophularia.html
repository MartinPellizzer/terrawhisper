Figwort, scientifically known as Scrophularia, is a versatile genus of flowering plants within the Scrophulariaceae family. Renowned in traditional herbalism, various species have been utilized for centuries to treat skin conditions, swellings, and glandular issues. Today, these resilient plants remain significant subjects in both botanical study and ethnobotany.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as figwort, utilizes its various components for medicinal purposes, though the root is often the most significant part in traditional herbalism. The root of the Scrophularia species is typically harvested and dried to be used in decoctions or tinctures, valued for its historical application in treating glandular swellings and skin conditions. While the stems and leaves also comprise the plant's structure, it is the concentrated properties found within the root that have traditionally drawn interest in botanical studies.
