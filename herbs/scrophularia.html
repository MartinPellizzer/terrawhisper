Scrophularia

Scrophularia, commonly known as figwort, is a versatile genus of flowering plants found globally. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its roots and leaves have historically been used to treat skin ailments, swellings, and glandular issues. This resilient botanical offers a fascinating intersection of natural beauty and ancient medicinal wisdom.

  • Scientific name: Scrophularia
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Scrophulariaceae and the genus Scrophularia.
