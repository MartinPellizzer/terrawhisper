Urtica urens

Urtica urens, commonly known as the stinging nettle, is a perennial herb renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Characterized by fine, stinging hairs, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, allergies, and urinary tract issues. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Urtica urens
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling into the family Urticaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Urtica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across various regions of Northern Europe. Its presence is documented in the coastal areas of Denmark and throughout the landscape of Finland. It can also be found growing within the Faroe Islands and across much of Great Britain. Furthermore, its geographical range extends to the unique environment of Iceland. These locations highlight the plant's affinity for the northernmost territories of the continent.
