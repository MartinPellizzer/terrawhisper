Carduus nutans

Musk thistle

Carduus nutans, commonly known as Musk Thistle, is a striking perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its large, purple flower heads and prickly foliage, this plant belongs to the Asteraceae family. Historically, it has been studied for its unique botanical properties and its significant role in local ecosystems.
