Rostellularia prostrata

Rostellularia prostrata, commonly known as creeping rostellularia, is a resilient, low-growing medicinal herb valued in traditional healing. Often found in tropical regions, this plant is prized for its unique phytochemical properties. It is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to support wellness, offering promising potential for various therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Rostellularia prostrata
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Rostellularia prostata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Rostellularia.
