Calophyllum membranaceum

Calophyllum membranaceuum, commonly known as the Mamra tree, is a versatile tropical evergreen species prized for its medicinal properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory compounds, various parts of the plant are traditionally used to treat skin ailments, promote healing, and support overall wellness through diverse bioactive extracts.

  • Scientific name: Calophyllum membranaceum
  • Family: Calophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is further categorized into the family Calophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Calophyllum.

Common Names

This plant, Calophyllum membranaceuum, is known by several different names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is commonly referred to as 薄叶红厚壳 (zh), 薄叶红厚壳 (zh-cn), or 薄葉紅厚殻 (zh-tw). Additionally, it is identified by the names 横经席 (zh) and 横经席叶 (zh).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the tropical environment of Hainan. Its range also extends to the coastal areas of China Southeast. Furthermore, the species is located within the Indo-China region, specifically in Vietnam. These locations highlight the plant's preference for certain subtropical and tropical habitats.
