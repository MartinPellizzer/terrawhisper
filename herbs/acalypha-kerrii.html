Acalypha kerrii

Acalypha kerrii, commonly known as Kerri's Copperleaf, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for developing modern therapeutic treatments and herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Acalypha kerrii
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
