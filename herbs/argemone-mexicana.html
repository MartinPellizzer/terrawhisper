Argemone mexicana

Mexican prickly poppy

Argemone mexicana, commonly known as Mexican prickly poppy, is a distinctive herbaceous plant native to Mexico and widely naturalized globally. Recognized by its blue-flowered blooms and prickly leaves, it holds significant traditional importance in folk medicine for treating inflammatory conditions, though its potent alkaloids require careful, expert handling.

  • Scientific name: Argemone mexicana
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Papaveraceae. Finally, its specific biological identification is placed within the genus Argemone.
