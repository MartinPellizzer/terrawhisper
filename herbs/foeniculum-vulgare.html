Fennel

Foeniculum vulgare · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1675 studies) · ★★★★★

Fennel, scientifically known as Foeniculum vulgare, is a versatile aromatic herb cherished for both culinary and medicinal purposes. Renowned for its digestive benefits, it effectively relieves bloating and indigestion. Rich in essential oils and antioxidants, this perennial plant serves as a natural remedy for respiratory health and hormonal balance.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Apiales and falls under the family Apiaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is completed by the genus Foeniculum.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as fennel, utilizes various components for its medicinal and aromatic purposes. The most frequently harvested parts are the fruits, which are technically schizocarps that contain the aromatic seeds used in culinary and therapeutic applications. Within these structures, the plant produces concentrated essential oils that provide its distinct scent and bioactive properties. Often, the high-quality essential oil is extracted directly from the dried fruit to create potent tinctures or remedies, making the entire reproductive part a vital resource in herbal medicine.
