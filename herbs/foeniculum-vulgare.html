Foeniculum vulgare

Foeniculum vulgare, commonly known as fennel, is a versatile aromatic herb celebrated in both culinary and medicinal traditions. Renowned for its digestive benefits, it effectively relieves bloating and indigestion. Rich in essential oils and antioxidants, this plant serves as a powerful natural remedy for respiratory health and hormonal balance.

  • Scientific name: Foeniculum vulgare
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Apiales and falls under the family Apiaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is completed by the genus Foeniculum.
