Mentha, scientifically known as Mentha, is a versatile genus of aromatic herbs widely celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its refreshing scent and cooling sensation, these plants are staples in traditional medicine. From soothing digestive issues to relieving headaches, Mentha offers diverse holistic benefits for modern wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Mentha.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a notable presence across several regions within Northern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Its distribution extends further north into the landscapes of Finland and the island of Iceland. Additionally, the species is documented within the territory of the Føroyaar islands. Finally, the geographical reach of this medicinal plant includes the various regions of Great Britain.
