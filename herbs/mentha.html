Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Mentha, is a versatile medicinal species where almost every component offers therapeutic benefits. The most prominent part used in traditional and modern medicine is the lush green leaves, which serve as the primary source of its healing properties. Within these leaves, the plant synthesizes concentrated essential oils that provide its characteristic cooling aroma and potency. This aromatic oil is frequently extracted for use in topical ointments and aromatherapy to soothe digestive issues and headaches. Furthermore, a concentrated leaf extract can be derived for various pharmaceutical applications, ensuring that the entire herb is utilized to promote wellness and relief.
