Mentha, scientifically known as Mentha, is a versatile genus of aromatic herbs widely celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its refreshing scent and cooling sensation, these plants are staples in traditional medicine. From soothing digestive issues to relieving headaches, Mentha offers diverse holistic benefits for modern wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a notable presence across several regions within Northern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Its distribution extends further north into the landscapes of Finland and the island of Iceland. Additionally, the species is documented within the territory of the Føroyaar islands.
