Mentha

Mentha, commonly known as mint, is a versatile genus of aromatic herbs widely celebrated for its refreshing fragrance and medicinal properties. Rich in essential oils like menthol, it is utilized globally to aid digestion, soothe respiratory issues, and provide cooling relief, making it a staple in herbal medicine and aromatherapy.

  • Scientific name: Mentha
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Mentha.
