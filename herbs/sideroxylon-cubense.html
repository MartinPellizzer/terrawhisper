Sideroxylon cubense

Sideroxylon cubense, commonly known as the Cuban Sideroxylon, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Research highlights its potential antioxidant and antimicrobial activities, making it a subject of great ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Sideroxylon cubense
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Ericales and the family Sapotaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Sideroxylon.
