Myricaria rosea

What's the taxonomical classification of Myricaria rosea?

Myricaria rosea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its lineage through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed under the order Caryophyllales. This plant is a member of the family Tamaricaceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Myricaria, with its unique identity defined by the species name rosea.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Myrica rosea has an upright, woody shrub structure characterized by elongated, lanceolate leaves that are densely covered in fine, silvery hairs. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in clusters, often appearing in the leaf axils during the growing season. These flowers eventually give way to small, fleshy drupes that transition in color as they mature.
