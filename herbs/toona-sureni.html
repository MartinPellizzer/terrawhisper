Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central areas as well as on Hainan island. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is known to grow within the borders of Bangladesh. Finally, its range reaches the mountainous terrains of the East Himalaya.
