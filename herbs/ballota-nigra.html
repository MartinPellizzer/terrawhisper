Ballota nigra

Black horehound

Ballota nigra, commonly known as black horehound, is a perennial medicinal herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its distinctive dark leaves and grayish flowers, it has historically been used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, coughs, and digestive issues, offering valuable therapeutic properties in herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Ballota nigra
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Ballota, with the species name nigra.
