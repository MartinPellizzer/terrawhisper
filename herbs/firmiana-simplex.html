Firmiana simplex

Parasol

Firmiana simplex, commonly known as Chinese Parasol Tree, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional practices. Renowned for its large, heart-shaped leaves and woody structure, it offers various therapeutic benefits. Often utilized to treat inflammation, fever, and skin ailments, this resilient species remains a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Firmiana simplex
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa
