Argyreia capitiformis

Argyreiia capitiformis, commonly known as the silver bush, is a resilient shrub native to South Africa. Valued in traditional medicine, its silvery, aromatic foliage is frequently used to treat respiratory ailments and skin irritations. This hardy plant highlights the rich biodiversity and medicinal potential found within fynbos ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Argyreia capitiformis
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
