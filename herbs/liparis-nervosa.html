Liparis nervosa

Liparis nervosa, commonly known as the Nervous Liparis, is a delicate epiphytic orchid native to tropical regions. Characterized by its intricate, vein-like patterns and small, translucent blooms, this species thrives in humid forest canopies. It is highly valued by botanists for its unique morphology and fascinating ecological niche.

  • Scientific name: Liparis nervosa
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, Liparis nervosa is identified by the genus Liparis.
