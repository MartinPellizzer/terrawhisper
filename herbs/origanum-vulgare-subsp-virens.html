Origanum vulgare subsp. virens

Origanum vulgare subsp. virens, commonly known as green oregano, is a potent medicinal herb prized for its therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils like carvacrol, this subspecies offers powerful antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory benefits. It is traditionally used to support digestive health and bolster the body's immune system.

  • Scientific name: Origanum vulgare subsp. virens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is categorized under the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Origanum as the subspecies virens.

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several distinct regions in the Mediterranean and Atlantic areas.
