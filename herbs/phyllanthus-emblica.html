Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Indian gooseberry or Amla, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The most prominent part is the fruit, which is a small, translucent green berry known for its high vitamin C content. Both fresh fruits and dried fruits are frequently used in traditional Ayurvedic formulations to boost immunity and aid digestion. The leaves are also significant, often used in topical applications or infusions due to their antioxidant properties. Additionally, the stem and branches of the tree are sometimes employed in decoctions to address specific health concerns, making the entire structure a vital resource in herbal medicine.
