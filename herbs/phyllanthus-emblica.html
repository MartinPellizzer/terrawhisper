Phyllanthus emblica

Phyllanthus emblica, commonly known as amla or Indian gooseberry, is a cornerstone of Ayurvedic medicine. This nutrient-dense superfruit is exceptionally rich in Vitamin C and antioxidants. Renowned for boosting immunity, enhancing digestion, and promoting hair health, it remains a vital botanical resource for holistic wellness and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus emblica
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found specifically in Mauritius. Its presence in China is widespread, occurring in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Furthermore, the species extends its range into Eastern Asia, specifically inhabiting Taiwan. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environmental settings where the species thrives.
