Polygonatum

Polygonatum, commonly known as Solomon's seal, is a revered genus of perennial herbs valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its rhizomatous roots, it is frequently utilized to nourish yin, moisten lungs, and alleviate dryness. This versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential for supporting vitality and restoring internal balance.

  • Scientific name: Polygonatum
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae, it is classified under the genus Polygonatum.
