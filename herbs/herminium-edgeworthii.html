Herminium edgeworthii

Herminium edgeworthii, commonly known as the Edgeworth's Herminium, is a delicate, shade-loving orchid native to the moist, mountainous regions of Asia. While primarily valued by botanists for its unique morphology, this subtle terrestrial orchid represents the rich biodiversity of forest ecosystems, offering profound insights into specialized ecological niches.

  • Scientific name: Herminium edgeworthii
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the Orchidaceae family, situated within the genus Herminium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found specifically within the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its presence is also widespread throughout the Indian Subcontinent, where it inhabits the East Himalaya and the West Himalaya. Furthermore, the species is documented growing in the mountainous terrain of Nepal. Finally, its geographical range extends into the rugged landscapes of Pakistan.
