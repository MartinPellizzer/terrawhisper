Artemisia macrocephala

Artemisia macrocephala, commonly known as the big-headed wormwood, is a perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia macrocephala
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic details place it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, specifically within the family Asteraceae. It is identified under the genus Artemisia, where it is known by the species name Artemisia macrocephala.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in Northern and Central Asia. In the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found growing in both the Altai and Tuva regions. Moving into Central Asia, the species inhabits the landscapes of Kazakhstan and Tajikistan. Its range also extends further south into the territory of Western Asia. Specifically, this medicinal herb is documented to grow within the borders of Afghanistan.
