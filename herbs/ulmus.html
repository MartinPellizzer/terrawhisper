Ulmus

Ulmus, commonly known as the elm, is a versatile genus of deciduous trees prized for their historical medicinal significance. Traditionally, various species have been utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues, offering valuable bioactive compounds that support overall wellness and holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Ulmus
  • Family: Ulmaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Ulmaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Ulmus.
