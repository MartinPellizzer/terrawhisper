Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is specifically located in the South-Central areas. Its presence extends significantly into the Indo-China region, where it can be found in Laos, Thailand, and Vietnam. Additionally, the species reaches into the Malesia region, specifically inhabiting the Philippines. Together, these locations highlight the diverse habitats where this medicinal variety thrives.
