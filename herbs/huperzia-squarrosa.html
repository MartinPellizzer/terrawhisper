, scientifically known as Huperzia squarrosa, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its potent alkaloid, huperzine A, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to enhance cognitive function, improve memory, and treat neurodegenerative disorders by acting as a powerful acetylcholinesterase inhibitor.
