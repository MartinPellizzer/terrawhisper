Aquarius macrophyllus

Aquarius macrophyllus, commonly known as a vital medicinal plant in tropical ecosystems, is celebrated for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its large, distinctive foliage, this species contains bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to treat inflammation and various ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Aquarius macrophyllus
  • Family: Alismataceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Alismatales and the family Alismataceae. Finally, the medicinal species is identified by its genus, Aquarius, with its specific epithet being macrophyllus.
