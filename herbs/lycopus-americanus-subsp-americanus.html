American buglewort, scientifically known as Lycopus americanus subsp. americanus, is a perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this plant is recognized for its calming properties and its role in supporting digestive and circulatory health, often serving as a gentle remedy for various ailments.

Plant Parts

This plant, Lycopus americanus subsp. americanus, is characterized by its distinctive foliage, where the leaf serves as a primary morphological feature. The leaf is typically arranged in opposite pairs along the square stem, exhibiting an ovate to lanceolate shape with serrated margins and a pointed tip. Often possessing a slightly hairy surface and a prominent vein pattern, the leaf is not only vital for the plant's photosynthetic processes but also contains the concentrated phytochemicals that contribute to its traditional medicinal properties.
