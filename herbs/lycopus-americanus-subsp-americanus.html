Scientific literature: Very Sparse (14 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

American buglewort, scientifically known as Lycopus americanus subsp. americanus, is a perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this plant is recognized for its calming properties and its role in supporting digestive and circulatory health, often serving as a gentle remedy for various ailments.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several major regions of the country. In Western Canada, it can be found throughout the provinces of Alberta and British Columbia. Its presence also extends eastward into the prairie provinces of Manitoba and Saskatchewan. Moving toward the Atlantic coast, the species is documented within Eastern Canada. Specifically, its geographical range includes the island of Newfoundland.
