Long-leaved ground-cherry, scientifically known as Physalis longifolia, is a versatile perennial herb native to parts of Asia and Australia. Renowned for its edible, lantern-like fruit, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. It is traditionally utilized for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support various healing processes.
