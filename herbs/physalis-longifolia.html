Physalis longifolia

Physalis longifolia, commonly known as the Longleaf Groundcherry, is a versatile perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its edible, berry-like fruits enclosed in papery husks, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is increasingly studied for its unique phytochemical properties and potential medicinal applications in traditional wellness.

  • Scientific name: Physalis longifolia
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Solanales, and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Physalis.
