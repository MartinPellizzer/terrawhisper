Dioscorea melanophyma

Dioscorea melanophyma, commonly known as a medicinal yam, is a climbing plant valued for its therapeutic potential. Native to specific tropical regions, it contains bioactive compounds utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Research continues to explore its phytochemical properties and its role in supporting holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea melanophyma
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Dioscoreales. Finally, it is a member of the family Dioscoreaceae and falls under the genus Dioscorea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range of distribution across parts of Asia. In China, it can be found growing throughout the South-Central regions as well as in Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it inhabits the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Bangladesh. Finally, its geographical reach includes the mountainous terrain of the East Himalaya.
