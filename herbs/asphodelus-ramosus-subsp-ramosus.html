Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout several key regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing across the diverse landscapes of France and Spain. Additionally, the species inhabits various Mediterranean islands, including Baleares and Sardegna. Its presence is also documented within the territory of Corse. Consequently, its geographical range is centered around these specific European coastal areas.
