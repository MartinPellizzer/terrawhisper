Asphodelus ramosus subsp. ramosus

Asphodelus ramosus subsp. ramosus, commonly known as the branching asphodel, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Valued in traditional herbalism, this plant features elegant white flowers and fleshy roots. It is historically recognized for its diverse medicinal properties, particularly in treating gastrointestinal issues and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Asphodelus ramosus subsp. ramosus
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asphodelaceae and the genus Asphodeluus. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies ramosus.
