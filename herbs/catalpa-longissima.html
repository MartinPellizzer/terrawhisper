Catalpa longissima, is a striking deciduous tree native to the southeastern United States. Characterized by its large, heart-shaped leaves and cascading clusters of white, bell-shaped flowers, it is prized in traditional herbalism. This species is often studied for its unique phytochemical properties and potential therapeutic applications in medicine.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across several distinct oceanic regions. Within the Malesia region, it can be found growing in the Philippines.
