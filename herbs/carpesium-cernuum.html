Carpesium cernuum

Carpesium cernuum, commonly known as the Velvet Root, is a highly revered medicinal plant celebrated for its potent antioxidant properties. Found primarily in tropical highland regions, it has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to support immune function, reduce inflammation, and promote overall cellular vitality and neurological health.
