Ajuga multiflora

What's the taxonomical classification of Ajuga multiflora?

Ajuga multiflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales. Its taxonomic journey continues through the family Lamiaceae, which places it within the genus Ajuga, ultimately defining it as the specific species multiflora.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ajuga multiflora has a low-growing, creeping growth habit characterized by basal rosettes of ovate to elliptical leaves with crenate margins and a hairy texture. These leaves are typically arranged in a dense mat that spreads via stolons to colonize the ground. The flowering stems emerge from the center of the rosettes, bearing whorls of small, tubular flowers that are usually blue or violet in color. Each flower features a distinct two-lipped corolla typical of the Lamiaceae family. The overall structure of the plant remains compact, making it well-suited for groundcover roles in its native habitats.
