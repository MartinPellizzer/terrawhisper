Sophora pachycarpa

Sophora pachycarpa, commonly known as the thick-fruited sophora, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to address various ailments. Exploring its bioactive compounds offers vital insights into its therapeutic potential and pharmacological applications.
