Hanceola sinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Hanceola sinensis?

Hanceola sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Hanceola and is specifically designated by the species name sinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Hanceola sinensis has woody stems with opposite leaves that are elliptical to lanceolate in shape and possess finely serrated margins. The leaf surfaces are typically smooth with distinct pinnate venation that extends toward the edges. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the leaf axils in clusters during the blooming season. These floral structures are supported by slender pedicels that connect them directly to the main stem. The plant's overall structure remains compact and shrub-like as it matures.
