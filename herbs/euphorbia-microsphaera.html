Euphorbia microsphaera

Euphorbia microsphaera, commonly known as a resilient succulent from the Euphorbiaceae family, thrives in arid landscapes. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses unique bioactive compounds. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential therapeutic properties, specifically for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial capabilities, marking it as a significant botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia microsphaera
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and falls under the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several key regions across Eurasia. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is documented in both Kriti and the European portion of Türkiye. Moving eastward, the species is found within the Caucasus region, specifically throughout the Transcaucasus. Its distribution extends significantly into Central Asia, where it inhabits the territories of Uzbekistan and Tajikistan. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive across varied landscapes from the Mediterranean to the heart of Asia.
