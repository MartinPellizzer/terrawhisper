Pachyrhizus erosus

Pachyrhizus erosus, commonly known as jicama or yam bean, is a versatile tuberous plant valued in traditional medicine. Rich in inulin and antioxidants, it is utilized for its hydrating properties and potential to support digestive health, manage blood sugar, and reduce inflammation, offering significant therapeutic benefits in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Pachyrhizus erosus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Pachyrhizus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent and surrounding islands. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in both Cameroon and Gabon. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, it can also be found in East Tropical Africa, specifically within Tanzania. The species extends into the Western Indian Ocean, where it inhabits the island of Mauritius. Finally, its range includes the large island of Madagascar located within the same ocean region.
