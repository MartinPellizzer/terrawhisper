Arenga pinnata

Areng palm

Arenga pinnata, commonly known as the Sugar Palm, is a versatile tropical plant highly valued in Southeast Asian traditional medicine. Beyond its commercial uses, its various parts offer therapeutic potential, traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin conditions, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance in regional healing.

  • Scientific name: Arenga pinnata
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Arenga pinnata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Arecales and the family Arecaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Arenga.

Its presence extends to various parts of China, including the South-Central, Southeast, and the island of Hainan.
