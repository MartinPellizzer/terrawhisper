Commiphora harveyi

Commiphora harveyi, commonly known as Harvey's Commiphora, is a medicinal shrub valued for its aromatic resin. Widely used in traditional medicine, its extracts possess significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This botanical species serves as a vital resource in ethnopharmacology, offering promising potential for developing new therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Commiphora harveyi
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Commiphora harveyi, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Burseraceae and is placed within the genus Commiphora.
