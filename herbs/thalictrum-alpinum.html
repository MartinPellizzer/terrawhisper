Thalictrum alpinum

Thalictrum alpinum, commonly known as Alpine Meadow Rue, is a delicate perennial herb native to high-altitude European mountain ranges. Characterized by its elegant, fern-like foliage and subtle blooms, this plant thrives in moist alpine meadows, playing a vital role in its fragile ecosystem through its unique botanical presence.
