Centaurothamnus maximus

Centaurothamnus maximus, commonly known as the Giant Centauro, is a remarkable medicinal plant endemic to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it has long been utilized in traditional healing practices to treat inflammatory conditions and boost immunity, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
