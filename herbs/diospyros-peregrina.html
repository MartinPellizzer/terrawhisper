Diospyros peregrina

Diospyros peregrina, commonly known as the wild ebony or black pear, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, its various parts are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, showcasing its profound ethnobotanical importance.
