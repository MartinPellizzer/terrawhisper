Teucrium montanum

Mountain germander

Teucrium montanum, commonly known as the mountain germander, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Teucrium montanum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Teucrium.
