Cotinus coggygria

Cotinus coggygriia, commonly known as the smoke bush, is a versatile ornamental shrub prized for its ethereal, cloud-like foliage. While primarily valued in landscaping for its striking aesthetic, this resilient plant holds traditional significance in various cultures, offering unique botanical properties and decorative potential in herbal applications.

  • Scientific name: Cotinus coggygria
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus name Cotinus.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the smoke bush, possesses distinct morphological features that contribute to its unique appearance. The leaves are typically small, oval, or obovate, often displaying vibrant seasonal colors that range from deep purples to bright greens. During its blooming period, the flowers appear as tiny, inconspicuous structures tucked within large, billowy, smoke-like panicles that give the species its name. While a single leaf may seem delicate, the overall foliage provides a dense texture to the shrub. Furthermore, the woody stems are protected by a textured bark that becomes increasingly rugged as the plant matures.
