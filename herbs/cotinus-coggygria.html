European smoketree, scientifically known as Cotinus coggygria, is a deciduous shrub celebrated for its ethereal, cloud-like plumes. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this resilient plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine. Used for its astringent properties, it offers unique botanical insights into natural remedies and sustainable landscape design.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the smoke bush, possesses distinct morphological features that contribute to its unique appearance. The leaves are typically small, oval, or obovate, often displaying vibrant seasonal colors that range from deep purples to bright greens. During its blooming period, the flowers appear as tiny, inconspicuous structures tucked within large, billowy, smoke-like panicles that give the species its name. While a single leaf may seem delicate, the overall foliage provides a dense texture to the shrub. Furthermore, the woody stems are protected by a textured bark that becomes increasingly rugged as the plant matures.
