Artemisia ciniformis

Artemisia ciniformis, commonly known as Mugwort or Wormwood, is a versatile medicinal herb celebrated in traditional practices. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is frequently utilized to support digestive health, regulate menstrual cycles, and improve circulation. This potent plant offers a rich array of bioactive compounds for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia ciniformis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Artemisia.
