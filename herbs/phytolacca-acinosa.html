American Pokeweed, scientifically known as Phytolacca acinosa, is a perennial herb renowned for its striking appearance and potent medicinal properties. While traditionally utilized in various herbal practices to treat inflammation and skin ailments, it contains toxic compounds, requiring careful handling and expert knowledge to ensure safe and effective application.

Plant Parts

This plant, Phytolacca acinosa, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for the therapeutic properties found within its various components. The roots serve as the primary source of medicinal compounds, often processed as dry roots to ensure stability for long-term use. In pharmacognosy, this part is formally referred to as radix, which is frequently utilized to prepare a potent root extract for topical or internal applications. Beyond the underground structures, the plant also produces distinctive dark berries, which contribute to its overall botanical profile.
