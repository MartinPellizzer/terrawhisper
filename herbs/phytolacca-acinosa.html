Phytolacca acinosa

Phytolacca acinosa, commonly known as Indian pokeweed, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Native to tropical regions, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various infections. While therapeutically potent, careful handling is essential due to its inherent toxicity in concentrated forms.

  • Scientific name: Phytolacca acinosa
  • Family: Phytolaccaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Caryophyllales and the family Phytolaccaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phytolacca, specifically the species Phytolacca acinosa.
