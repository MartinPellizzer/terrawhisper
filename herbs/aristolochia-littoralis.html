Duck

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (5 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Duck, scientifically known as Aristolochia littoralis, is a captivating climbing perennial native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique, heart-shaped leaves and distinctive tubular flowers, this plant holds significant historical interest in traditional medicine. However, users must exercise extreme caution due to the presence of potent nephrotoxic compounds.
