East Asian arrowroot

Pueraria montana var. lobata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (742 studies) · ★★★★★

East Asian arrowroot, scientifically known as Pueraria montana var. lobata, is a versatile medicinal legume widely utilized in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its starch-rich tubers, it is prized for its ability to reduce inflammation, soothe fevers, and support detoxification, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions of Europe and its surrounding islands. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is noted in Italy as well as throughout the Northwest Balkan Peninsula. Moving toward Eastern Europe, the species can be found growing in both Crimea and Ukraine. Beyond the mainland, its range extends into the Atlantic region specifically within the Macaronesia archipelago. Notably, it is also documented as being present in the Azores.
