Heliotropium elongatum

Heliotropium elongatum, commonly known as the elongated heliotrope, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid coastal regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Traditional practices utilize its extracts to treat various ailments, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Heliotropium elongatum
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Boraginales. Finally, it is a member of the family Boraginaceae and falls within the genus Heliotropium.

Common Names

This plant, Heliotropium elongatum, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context.
