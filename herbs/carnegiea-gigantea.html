Carnegiea gigantea

Carnegiea gigantea, commonly known as the Saguaro cactus, is an iconic symbol of the Sonoran Desert. Beyond its majestic stature, this succulent holds medicinal significance in indigenous cultures. Traditionally, its fruit and various components have been utilized for their nutritional and therapeutic properties, embodying the resilience of desert life.

  • Scientific name: Carnegiea gigantea
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Carnegiea gigantea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales. It is further categorized into the family Cactaceae and the genus Carnegiea.
