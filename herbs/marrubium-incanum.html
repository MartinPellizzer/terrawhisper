Silver horehound, scientifically known as Marrubium incanum, is a perennial medicinal herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its soft, silvery foliage, this plant has long been utilized in traditional medicine for its potent expectorant properties, primarily helping to soothe respiratory ailments, alleviate coughs, and support bronchial health.
