Canavalia ensiformis

Canavalia ensiformis, commonly known as jack bean, is a versatile legume valued in traditional medicine. Rich in bioactive compounds, it possesses significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. Often utilized in herbal remedies, this plant offers promising therapeutic potential for treating various ailments, making it a subject of intense pharmacological interest.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa
