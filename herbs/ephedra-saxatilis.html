Ephedra saxatilis

Ephedra saxatilis, commonly known as the rock ephedra, is a resilient, shrubby medicinal plant native to arid, rocky terrains. Renowned for its alkaloid content, particularly ephedrine, it has been utilized in traditional medicine to stimulate respiration and treat various ailments, playing a significant role in ethnobotanical studies worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra saxatilis
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Gnetidae and the order Ephedrales, specifically within the family Ephedraceae. Finally, the species is categorized under the genus Ephedra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within East and South-Central Asia. In China, its presence is noted across the South-Central regions as well as within the high-altitude terrain of Tibet. The species also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be found inhabiting the East Himalaya. Furthermore, its range includes the mountainous landscapes of Nepal. Collectively, these locations define the primary natural habitats where Ephedra saxatilis thrives.
