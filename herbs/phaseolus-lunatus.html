Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Phaseolus lunatus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification concludes within the genus Phaseolus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning both the Macaronesian islands and parts of West Tropical Africa. Within the Macaronesian region, its presence is noted in the Canary Islands and Cape Verde. Moving toward the African continent, the species can be found across several West Tropical African nations. Specifically, it inhabits the territories of Benin and Ghana. Furthermore, its range extends to the coastal region of Guinea-Bissau.

This plant, commonly known as the runner bean (Phaseolus lunatus), is valued in various traditional medicinal practices, with its primary therapeutic components often derived from its seeds. These seeds, which can vary in color and shape, contain a variety of bioactive compounds and nutrients that are utilized for their potential health benefits. When prepared correctly to remove natural toxins, the seeds serve as the central element in herbal applications, often being used to support digestive health or provide essential proteins and minerals.
