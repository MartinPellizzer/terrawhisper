Indian birthwort

Aristolochia indica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (54 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Indian birthwort, scientifically known as Aristolochia indica, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to East Asia. Characterized by its distinctive heart-shaped leaves and tubular flowers, it holds historical significance in traditional medicine. However, modern research warns of its potent nephrotoxicity, cautioning against its use due to severe health risks.
