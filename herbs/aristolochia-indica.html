Aristolochia indica

Indian birthwort

Aristolochia indica, commonly known as Indian Birthwort, is a perennial climbing herb native to various Asian regions. Renowned for its unique tubular flowers and medicinal potential, it has historically been used in traditional practices. However, caution is vital due to the presence of toxic aristolochic acids within the plant.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia indica
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Aristolochiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Aristolochia.
