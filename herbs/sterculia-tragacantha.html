Sterculia tragacantha

Sterculia tragacantha, commonly known as the Gumsterculia, is a significant medicinal tree native to arid regions. Renowned for its versatile therapeutic properties, the plant yields a mucilaginous gum used to treat digestive ailments and inflammations. Its bark and leaves further offer potent antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Sterculia tragacantha
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae and is placed within the genus Sterculia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the region of West Tropical Africa. Within this geographical area, its presence is specifically noted in the nation of Benin.
