Tabernaemontana alternifolia

Tabernaemontana alternifolia, commonly known as the Pinwheel Flower, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its striking white blooms, this plant contains bioactive alkaloids used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, wounds, and digestive ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Tabernaemontana alternifolia
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. It is further identified by its genus, Tabernaemontana, specifically as the species Tabernaemontana alternifolia.
