Artemisia laciniata

Artemisia laciniata, commonly known as a member of the sagebrush family, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its unique lobed foliage, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potent bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic benefits for various inflammatory and digestive conditions.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia laciniata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artemisia.
