Tanacetum parthenium

Tanacetum parthenium, commonly known as feverfew, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is primarily used to alleviate migraines and reduce inflammation. This perennial plant offers a natural approach to managing neurological discomfort and promoting overall vascular health through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Tanacetum parthenium
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, the species is identified by its genus, Tanacetum.
