Sasamorpha borealis

Sasamorpha borealis, commonly known as the northern sasamour, is a perennial herb native to the Arctic and subarctic regions. Traditionally valued for its nutrient-rich rhizomes, this resilient plant offers significant medicinal potential. Its unique chemical profile suggests bioactive properties that may support digestive health and provide antioxidant benefits.

  • Scientific name: Sasamorpha borealis
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Sasamorpha borealis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Its specific classification concludes within the genus Sasamorpha.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across parts of Eastern Asia. It can be found growing in the Russian Far East, specifically on the island of Sakhalin. Additionally, its range extends to the islands of Japan within Eastern Asia. The species is also documented to inhabit various regions throughout Korea. Consequently, its natural habitat is concentrated within these distinct northern and eastern territories.
