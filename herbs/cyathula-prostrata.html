Cyathula prostrata

Cyathula prostrata, commonly known as the "prostrate cyathu", is a unique medicinal plant belonging to the Asteraceae family. Found primarily in specific tropical regions, it is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, often utilized to treat various ailments through its bioactive compounds and natural healing potential.

  • Scientific name: Cyathula prostrata
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and is a representative of the family Amaranthaceae. Ultimately, its scientific identification is defined by the genus Cyathula.

Distribution

This plant is geographically distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the nation of Benin. Its presence also extends to the territories of Burkina and Gambia. Furthermore, the species is documented to inhabit parts of Ghana. Finally, its range includes the coastal area of Guinea-Bissau.
