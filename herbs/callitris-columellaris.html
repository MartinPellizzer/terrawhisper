Callitris columellaris

Callitris arenosa

Callitris columeellaris, commonly known as the Hoop Pine, is a majestic evergreen conifer native to Australia. Beyond its value in timber production, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Traditional knowledge often utilizes various parts of the tree for medicinal purposes, showcasing its versatile role in both ecology and healing.

  • Scientific name: Callitris columellaris
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, it is categorized under the family Cupressaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Callitris.

Distribution

This plant, known as Callitris columellaris, is native to several distinct regions across the Australian continent. Its geographical range extends through the diverse landscapes of New South Wales and Queensland. Additionally, it can be found growing within the Northern Territory. The species also makes a presence in the southern reaches of South Australia. Finally, its distribution includes the state of Victoria, showcasing its broad environmental adaptability.
