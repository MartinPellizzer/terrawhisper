Apocynum pictum

Apoceynum pictum, commonly known as the Madagascar Periwinkle, is a versatile medicinal shrub celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Rich in alkaloids, it has been traditionally utilized in various cultures to manage ailments ranging from hypertension to diabetes. This plant remains a vital subject in pharmacological research for developing modern treatments.

  • Scientific name: Apocynum pictum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Apoceynum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of Central and East Asia. In the Central Asian region, it can be found growing in Kazakhstan, Kirgizstan, and Tadzhikistan. Its range extends into China, where it is present in the Inner Mongolia autonomous region. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the North-Central areas of China. These locations define the primary natural habitats for this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

Its lush green leaves are highly valued in traditional medicine, often being harvested to create a soothing herbal tea used to treat ailments such as fever or inflammation. Through these diverse components, the plant serves as both a material resource and a vital element in holistic healing practices.
