What's the taxonomical classification of Klainedoxa gabonensis?

Klainedoxa gabonenis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic lineage further, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Irvingiaceae. Ultimately, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Klainedoxa, and its unique species name, gabonenis.
