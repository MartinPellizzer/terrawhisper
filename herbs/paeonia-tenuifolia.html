Fern-leaf Peony, scientifically known as Paeonia tenuifolia, is a resilient perennial prized for its delicate, finely divided foliage and striking pink blooms. Historically used in traditional medicine, this hardy species offers therapeutic potential, particularly for its anti-inflammatory and sedative properties, making it a fascinating subject for botanical study.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of Southeastern and Eastern Europe. Within Southeastern Europe, its presence is noted in Bulgaria and Romania. It can also be found throughout the Northwestern Balkan Peninsula. Moving into Eastern Europe, the species inhabits the Crimean Peninsula. Finally, its range extends further north into East European Russia.
