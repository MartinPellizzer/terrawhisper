Gochnatia glutinosa

Gochnatia glutinosa, commonly known as the sticky gochnatia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its resinous leaves and bark, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin inflammations, wounds, and digestive ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Gochnatia glutinosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gochnatia, as seen in the species Gochnatia glutinosa.
