Apodytes dimidiata

White-pear

Apodytes dimidiata, commonly known as the Sandpaper Leaf, is a versatile evergreen climber native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin conditions. This resilient plant holds significant value in both folk medicine and ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Apodytes dimidiata
  • Family: Metteniusaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Metteniusales and the family Metteniusaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Apodytes.
