Petasites formosanus

Petasites formosanus, commonly known as the Formosan butterbur, is a versatile medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to alleviate respiratory ailments and inflammation. Modern research continues to explore its significant therapeutic potential in managing various inflammatory and neurological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Petasites formosanus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Petasites.

Common Names

This plant, Petasites formosanus, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic context and regional usage. In Chinese, it is referred to as 台湾蜂斗菜 (zh) or 台湾蜂斗菜 (zh-cn), while specifically in Taiwan, it is called 臺灣蜂斗菜 (zh-tw). Additionally, the plant is known as 山菊 (zh-tw) and 台湾蜂斗叶 (zh).
