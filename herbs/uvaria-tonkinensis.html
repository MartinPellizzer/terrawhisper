Uvaria tonkinenensis, scientifically known as Uvaria tonkinenensis, is a tropical medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Belonging to the Annonaceae family, it is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds. Researchers study its leaves and stems for potential anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties.

Distribution

This plant is geographically distributed across several distinct regions within Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its range also extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Within this region, the species is documented to grow in Cambodia. Additionally, its natural habitat includes the country of Laos.

Plant Parts

This plant, Uvaria tonkinenensis, is a woody climber characterized by its climbing habit, where the stem serves as the primary structural support used to ascend through surrounding vegetation. The stem is essential for transporting nutrients and water throughout the plant, facilitating the growth of its leaves and reproductive structures. In addition to its functional role in transport, the stem provides the necessary physical framework that allows the plant to thrive in its natural habitat.
