Crocus caspius

Crocus caspius, commonly known as the Caspian crocus, is a rare and striking perennial bulb native to the arid regions of the Caspian Sea. Renowned for its delicate, vibrant blooms, this medicinal plant offers unique phytochemical properties, making it a subject of growing interest in ethnobotanical and pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Crocus caspius
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Crocus caspius, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, falling into the family Iridaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Crocus.
