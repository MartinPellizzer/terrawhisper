According to a study published by "Heliyon", the root of Cyphostemma adenocaule is used in indigenous medicinal systems to manage tumors, helminthiasis, snake envenomation, rabies virus, splenomegaly, and other immunological disorders. The study evaluated the in-vivo immunomodulatory activity of the crude root extract and its solvent fractions using doses of 100, 200, and 400 mg/kg in Swiss albino mice. The crude extract at a dose of 400 mg/kg revealed the highest and statistically significant augmentation of the carbon clearance rate at 0.1100 ± 0.0124. At this same 400 mg/kg dose, the humoral antibody titer reached 96.00 ± 14.31 compared to the vehicle control group. Ultimately, the crude extract and n-butanol fraction showed promising immunostimulant activity.
