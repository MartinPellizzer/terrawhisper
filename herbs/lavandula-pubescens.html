Lavandula pubescens

Lavandula pubescens, commonly known as Pubescent Lavender, is a charming perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean. Characterized by its soft, silvery-grey foliage and fragrant purple blooms, this medicinal plant is prized for its calming essential oils, offering soothing properties to alleviate anxiety, promote restful sleep, and reduce skin inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Lavandula pubescens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus name, Lavandula.
