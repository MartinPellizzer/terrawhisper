Aegopodium podagraria

Ground elder

Aegopodium podagraria, commonly known as goutweed, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, it is primarily used to alleviate symptoms of gout and arthritis. This versatile plant offers a natural approach to managing uric acid levels and joint discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Aegopodium podagraria
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Apiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Apiaceae and the genus Aegopodium.
