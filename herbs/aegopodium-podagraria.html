Ground elder, scientifically known as Aegopodium podagraria, is a perennial herb long valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently used to alleviate symptoms of gout and kidney issues. This versatile plant offers natural therapeutic benefits for supporting urinary health and reducing swelling.
