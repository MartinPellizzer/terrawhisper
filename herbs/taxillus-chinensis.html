Taxillus chinensis

Taxillus chinensis, commonly known as the Chinese Taxillus, is a significant parasitic plant in traditional medicine. Often referred to as a "mistletoe" variety, it is highly valued for its therapeutic properties. Historically used to support vitality and kidney health, this botanical specimen remains a key subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Taxillus chinensis
  • Family: Loranthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Santalales and the family Loranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Taxillus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions in East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in Hainan and throughout China Southeast.
