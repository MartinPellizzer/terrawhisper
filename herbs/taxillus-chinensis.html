Mulberry Mistletoe, scientifically known as Taxillus chinensis, is a revered medicinal herb deeply embedded in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent adaptogenic properties, it is frequently utilized to support hormonal balance, enhance vitality, and bolster the immune system, offering a natural approach to maintaining holistic wellness and longevity.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions in East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in Hainan and throughout China Southeast.
