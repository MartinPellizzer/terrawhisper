Limonium bicolor

Limonium bicolor, commonly known as the bicolor sea lavender, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal regions. Distinguished by its striking, dual-toned floral displays, this plant thrives in saline environments. Beyond its ornamental appeal, it holds significant potential in ethnobotany for its diverse therapeutic and medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Limonium bicolor
  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Limonium bicolor, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales and the family Plumbaginaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Limonium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several diverse regions within China. It can be found growing in the northern territories of Inner Mongolia and the Manchuria region. Additionally, its distribution extends into the central areas of North-Central China. The species is also documented to inhabit the high-altitude landscapes of Qinghai. Finally, its range reaches as far as the various provinces located in China Southeast.
