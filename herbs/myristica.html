Myristica

Myristica, commonly known as nutmeg, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its aromatic seeds and potent therapeutic properties. Renowned for its digestive benefits, it possesses powerful antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial qualities. Often used in traditional medicine, it serves as a natural remedy for sleep, digestion, and skin health.

  • Scientific name: Myristica
  • Family: Myristicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Myristicaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Myristica.
