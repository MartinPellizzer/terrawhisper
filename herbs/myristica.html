Nutmeg genus, scientifically known as Myristica, is a group of aromatic evergreen trees native to tropical regions. Renowned for their highly valued seeds and mace, these plants offer significant medicinal properties, including antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits, making them essential in traditional healing and modern pharmacological studies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its presence extends significantly into the Western Indian Ocean, where it inhabits the Comoros, Mauritius, and Réunion.
