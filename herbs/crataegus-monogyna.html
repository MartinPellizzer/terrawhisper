Crataegus chlorocarpa

Crataegus monogyna · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1112 studies) · ★★★★★

Crataegus chlorocarpa, scientifically known as Crataegus monogyna, is a resilient deciduous shrub widely recognized as the common hawthorn. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this medicinal plant is traditionally used to support cardiovascular health, improve circulation, and alleviate symptoms of anxiety, making it a cornerstone in herbal medicine practices.
