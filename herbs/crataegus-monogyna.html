Crataegus monogyna

Crataegus monogyna, commonly known as common hawthorn, is a deciduous shrub renowned for its therapeutic properties. Historically used in traditional medicine, it is primarily celebrated for supporting cardiovascular health. Rich in flavonoids, this plant helps strengthen heart muscles, regulate blood pressure, and improve overall circulatory function and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus monogyna
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Crataegus.

Distribution

This plant, Crataegus monogyna, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the northern reaches of the European continent. It can be found growing naturally throughout various regions of Northern Europe. Specifically, its presence is documented in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark and Finland. The species also thrives across the islands of Great Britain and Ireland. Furthermore, its geographical range extends into the rugged landscapes of Norway.
