Agastache rugosa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (452 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese giant-hyssop, scientifically known as Agastache rugosa, is a versatile perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic purple spikes, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antibacterial properties. Often used to soothe digestive issues and respiratory ailments, this resilient plant is a staple in holistic wellness.
