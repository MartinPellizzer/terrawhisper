Agastache rugosa

Agastache rugosa, commonly known as Korean Mint, is a highly valued perennial herb in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and purple flower spikes, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits. This versatile plant is widely utilized in herbal remedies to support respiratory health and calm stress.

  • Scientific name: Agastache rugosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Agastache.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within the borders of Great Britain and Sweden. Moving toward the center of the continent, it is also found throughout Middle Europe. Specifically, its range extends through Germany and into the territories of Austria. Finally, the distribution continues through the combined regions of Czechia and Slovakia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Blue Giant Hyssop, offers various components that can be utilized for medicinal purposes. While the roots are sometimes sought after, most traditional uses focus on the leaves and the aerial parts, which contain the essential volatile oils. The stems are sturdy and support the plant's upright growth, but it is the combination of the foliage and flowering structures that provides the most therapeutic value. Ultimately, when preparing herbal remedies, the whole plant is often harvested to capture the full spectrum of its medicinal properties.
