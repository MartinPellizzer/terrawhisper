Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae, it is further categorized under the genus Clematis. Specifically, the variety chinensis represents a distinct botanical classification within this group.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several distinct regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and North-Central areas. Its distribution also extends to the Southeast regions of China and the island of Hainan. Beyond the mainland, the species is documented in the Nansei-shoto islands of Eastern Asia. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse range in which this medicinal variety thrives.
