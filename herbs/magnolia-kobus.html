Kobushi magnolia, scientifically known as Magnolia kobus, is a deciduous flowering tree native to Japan and Korea. Renowned for its elegant, creamy-white blossoms that appear in early spring, this ornamental species is prized in landscaping. Beyond its beauty, it holds traditional significance in various East Asian botanical practices.
