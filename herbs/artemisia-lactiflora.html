Guizhou mugwort, scientifically known as Artemisia lactiflora, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is frequently utilized to regulate menstruation, relieve pain, and promote blood circulation. This versatile botanical serves as a vital component in various therapeutic herbal formulations.
