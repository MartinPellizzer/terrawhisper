Gardenia jasminoides

Gardenia jasminoides, commonly known as Cape Jasmine, is a fragrant evergreen shrub prized for its intoxicatingly sweet, white blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant is traditionally used in herbal medicine to soothe skin irritations, reduce inflammation, and calm the nervous system, offering both aesthetic and therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Gardenia jasminoides
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Gardenia.

Its presence in China is extensive, covering the South-Central areas of the country. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout the Hainan province.
