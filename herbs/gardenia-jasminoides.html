Cape-jasmine, scientifically known as Gardenia jasminoides, is a fragrant evergreen shrub celebrated for its intoxicatingly sweet, white blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant offers diverse therapeutic benefits, traditionally used in various cultures to soothe skin irritations, reduce inflammation, and promote relaxation through its potent aromatic properties.
