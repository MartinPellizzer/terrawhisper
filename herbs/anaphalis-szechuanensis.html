What's the taxonomical classification of Anaphalis szechuanensis?

Anaphalis szechuanenensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales. This plant is a representative of the family Asteraceae, which is further categorized into the genus Anaphalis, with the specific species designation being szechuanenensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

The involucral bracts surrounding the base of the flower head are often distinct and help protect the developing seeds.
