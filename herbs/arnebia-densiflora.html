Arnebia densiflora, scientifically known as Arnebia densiflora, is a perennial herb belonging to the Boraginaceae family. Native to high-altitude regions, this medicinal plant is celebrated in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties, primarily attributed to the presence of bioactive shikonin derivatives in its roots.
