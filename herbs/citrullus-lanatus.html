Citrullus lanatus

Watermelon

Citrullus lanatus, commonly known as watermelon, is more than just a refreshing summer fruit. Beyond its sweet, hydrating flesh, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Rich in lycopene, citrulline, and essential vitamins, it possesses antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and cardiovascular benefits, making it a valuable resource in traditional and modern wellness.

  • Scientific name: Citrullus lanatus
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Cucurbitalees, specifically within the family Cucurbitaeaceace. Finally, it is identified under the genus Citrullus.
