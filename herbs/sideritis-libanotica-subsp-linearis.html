Sideritis libanotica subsp. linearis

Sideritis libanotica subsp. linearis, commonly known as Lebanese mountain tea, is a precious medicinal herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this subspecies is widely utilized in traditional herbalism for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and digestive benefits, offering a natural remedy for respiratory and digestive wellness.
