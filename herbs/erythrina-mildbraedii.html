Distribution

This plant is native to the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its presence is specifically documented within the borders of Ghana. Furthermore, the species can be found growing across the terrain of Guinea. It also extends its range into the coastal regions of the Ivory Coast and Liberia.
