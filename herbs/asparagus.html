Asparagus genus, scientifically known as Asparagus, is a diverse group of herbaceous perennials valued globally. Beyond its culinary fame, various species offer significant medicinal potential. Rich in antioxidants and essential nutrients, these plants are traditionally used to support kidney health, reduce inflammation, and provide restorative tonic properties in herbalism.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the northern regions of Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Denmark.
