Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Maytenus iliiciifolia, utilizes its leaf as a primary component in traditional medicinal practices. The leaves are characterized by their distinctive holly-like shape, featuring spiny margins that distinguish the species. In various therapeutic applications, these leaves are often harvested to prepare infusions or decoctions, as they are believed to contain bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's overall medicinal properties.
