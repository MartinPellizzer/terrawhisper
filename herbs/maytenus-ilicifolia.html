Maytenus ilicifolia, scientifically known as Maytenus iliiciifolia, is a medicinal shrub native to South American forests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant contains bioactive alkaloids and triterpenoids. It is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant effects in treating various ailments.
