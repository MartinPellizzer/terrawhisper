What's the taxonomical classification of Pentaphragma sinense?

Pentaphragma sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the family Pentaphragmataceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Pentaphragma and is specifically designated as the species sinense.
