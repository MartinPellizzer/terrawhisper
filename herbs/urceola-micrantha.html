Urceola micrantha

Urceoella micrantha, commonly known as the "small-flowered urceola," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, it is frequently utilized for its potent bioactive compounds. This plant serves as a natural remedy for various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Urceola micrantha
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae and belongs to the genus Urceoia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across various regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central areas as well as throughout the Southeast. It can also be found growing in the unique environment of Hainan. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Beyond mainland China, its geographical reach includes the Nansei-shotō islands in Eastern Asia.
