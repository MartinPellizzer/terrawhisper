Rhododendron ovatum

Rhododendron ovatum, commonly known as the "Red Rhododendron," is a majestic evergreen shrub native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its vibrant crimson blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from respiratory issues to digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron ovatum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, falling into the family Ericaceae. Finally, its taxonomic position is completed within the genus Rhododendron.

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories.
