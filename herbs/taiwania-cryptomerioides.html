Coffin Tree, scientifically known as Taiwania cryptomerioides, is a rare coniferous species endemic to Taiwan. Traditionally valued in folk medicine, its bark and leaves are utilized for their potential therapeutic properties. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central regions and the high altitudes of Tibet. The species is also native to the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Furthermore, its range extends into the Indo-China peninsula, specifically inhabiting parts of Myanmar. Finally, it can be found growing within the tropical landscapes of Vietnam.
