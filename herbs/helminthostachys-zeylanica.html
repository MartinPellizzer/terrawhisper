Helminthostachys zeylanica

Helminthostachys zeylanica, commonly known as the "fern of the forest," is a rare, primitive fern endemic to the humid tropical regions of Sri Lanka. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this unique plant possesses significant bioactive properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits for treating various ailments through its diverse phytochemical profile.

  • Family: Ophioglossaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
