Slash pine, scientifically known as Pinus elliottii, is a versatile conifer native to the southeastern United States. Renowned for its resinous properties, it holds significant medicinal potential. Historically, its extracts have been utilized for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory qualities, offering valuable insights into natural therapeutic applications and pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Southern Africa, it can be found growing within the Free State province. Its presence extends to the Western Indian Ocean, specifically on the island of Mauritius. In Australia, the species is established in both New South Wales and Queensland. Finally, it is also located within the Southwestern Pacific, specifically in New Caledonia.
