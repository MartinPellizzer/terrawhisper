Brassica oleracea

Brassica oleracea, commonly known as cabbage, kale, or broccoli, is a versatile cruciferous plant valued for its immense nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in glucosinolates, antioxidants, and essential vitamins, it offers significant health benefits, including anti-inflammatory effects, cancer prevention, and enhanced digestive health through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Brassica oleracea
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification concludes within the genus Brassica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide range of distribution across several key regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing throughout Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward Middle Europe, its presence is well-established in Belgium. The species also occupies significant territories within Germany.
