Caesalpinia spinosa, scientifically known as Tara spinosa, is a versatile legume native to the tropical regions of Central and South America. Renowned for its nutrient-rich pods, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine and industry for its exceptional tannin content, used widely in pharmaceuticals and tanning processes.
