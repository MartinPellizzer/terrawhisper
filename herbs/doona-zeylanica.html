Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Doona zeylanica, possesses a very specific and localized geographical distribution. Within the vast expanse of the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is notably restricted to a single island nation. Specifically, this medicinal species is found growing in the natural landscapes of Sri Lanka. Because its range is so limited, it is considered an endemic treasure of that particular region. Therefore, understanding its habitat requires focusing primarily on the unique ecosystems found within Sri Lanka.
