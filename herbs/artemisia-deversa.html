What's the taxonomical classification of Artemisia deversa?

Artemisia deversa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage leading to the order Asterales. This plant is a representative of the family Asteraceae, a diverse group of flowering plants, and is specifically categorized under the genus Artemisia. Within this genus, it is identified as the distinct species deversa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Artemisia deversa has a perennial, woody-based growth habit characterized by narrow, linear leaves that are typically grayish-green and finely divided. The inflorescence consists of small, inconspicuous flower heads arranged in panicles or clusters.
