Echium amoenum

Echium amoenum, commonly known as the beautiful viper's bugloss, is a striking perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its vibrant blue flowers and unique architectural foliage, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its restorative properties and its significant role in supporting local biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Echium amoenum
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Echium amoenum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. It is ultimately identified by its genus, Echium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within the mountainous regions of Eurasia. Its primary distribution includes the North Caucasus area, where it adapts to unique local climates. Additionally, the species can be found throughout the Transcaucasus region. Moving further south, its presence extends into the landscapes of Western Asia. Specifically, the plant is known to inhabit parts of Iran within this broader territory.
