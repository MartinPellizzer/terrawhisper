Ficus dicranostyla

Ficus dicranostyla, commonly known as the Branched Fig, is a versatile medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of this species are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Ficus dicranostyla
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Moraceae and the genus Ficus.
