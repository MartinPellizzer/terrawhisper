Mimosa scabrella

Mimosa scabrella, commonly known as the "sensitive shrub," is a resilient medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Mimosa scabrella
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling into the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Mimosa, specifically as the species Mimosa scabrella.
