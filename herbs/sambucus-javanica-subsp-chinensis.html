Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Dipsacales, it is situated in the family Viburnaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Sambucus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several key regions. In Western Asia, its presence is documented within the borders of Afghanistan. Within China, the species is widely scattered, specifically inhabiting the North-Central and South-Central territories. It also thrives in the Southeast region of the country. Furthermore, its range extends to the island province of Hainan.
