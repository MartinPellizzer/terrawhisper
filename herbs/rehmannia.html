Rehmannia, scientifically known as Rehmannia nana, is a rare, deciduous shrub native to the arid regions of India and Pakistan. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this botanical treasure is prized for its potent therapeutic properties, particularly in treating various ailments through its unique bioactive compounds and restorative herbal qualities.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across various regions of China. It can be found growing in the South-Central areas of the country. Additionally, its presence extends into the northern reaches of Inner Mongolia. The species also inhabits the Manchuria region, showing adaptability to different climates. Finally, its range covers the China North-Central and China Southeast territories as well.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Rehmannia glutinosa, possesses various medicinal components, most notably the root and the leaf. The root is the most significant part used in traditional medicine, typically being processed and used to treat ailments related to kidney function and blood nourishment. While the root serves as the primary therapeutic agent, the leaf is also studied for its biological properties and potential health benefits.
