Erythroxylum cuneatum

Erythroxylum cuneatum, commonly known as the wedge-leaf coca, is a significant medicinal plant native to South America. Belonging to the Erythroxylaceae family, it contains alkaloids used traditionally for their stimulant properties. This species plays a vital role in regional ethnobotany, offering unique pharmacological insights into neurological and physiological wellness.
