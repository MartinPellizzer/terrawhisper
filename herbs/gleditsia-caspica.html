Gleditsia caspica

Gleditsia caspia (emendation)

Gleditsia caspica, commonly known as the Caspian honey locust, is a resilient medicinal plant native to specific Caspian regions. Renowned for its unique therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies. This plant offers significant botanical interest, providing valuable insights into natural treatments and holistic wellness practices.
