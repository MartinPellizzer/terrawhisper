Plant Parts

This plant, Dioscorea membranacea, is characterized by its underground storage organs which serve as its primary medicinal component. Specifically, the therapeutic properties are derived from the rhizomes, which function as the plant's nutrient reservoirs. When harvesting for medicinal use, it is the fleshy, tuberous rhizome that is collected, processed, and utilized for its various bioactive compounds.
