Dioscorea membranacea

Dioscorea membranacea, commonly known as the membrane yam, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal medicine. Native to various subtropical regions, this climbing tuberous herb contains bioactive compounds with potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering promising therapeutic applications for supporting overall health and treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea membranacea
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Dioscoreales and the family Dioscoreaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dioscorea, specifically as the species Dioscorea membranacea.
