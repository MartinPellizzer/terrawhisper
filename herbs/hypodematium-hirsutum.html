What's the taxonomical classification of Hypodematium hirsutum?

Hypodematium hirsutum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, its evolutionary lineage progresses through the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales. This specific fern is situated within the family Hypodematiaceae and is defined by the genus Hypodematium, ultimately reaching its unique biological identification at the species level as hirsutum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Hypodematium hirsutum has a creeping rhizome that produces small, pinnately branched fronds measuring approximately 2 to 10 centimeters in length. These segments are typically dark green and lack a distinct midrib, giving them a delicate and slender appearance. The underside of the foliage features small, circular sori located near the margins, which are protected by a thin, reflexed leaf edge.
