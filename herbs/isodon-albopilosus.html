Isodon albopilosus

Isodon albopilosus, commonly known as the White-haired Isodon, is a valuable medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, it is frequently utilized in traditional practices to treat various ailments. Its unique chemical composition offers significant pharmacological interest for modern research into natural healing properties.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
