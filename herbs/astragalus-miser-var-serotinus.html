Columbia milk-vetch, scientifically known as Astragalus miser var. serotinus, is a resilient perennial legume native to Western North America. Renowned for its ability to thrive in arid landscapes, this plant features distinctive yellow blossoms. While studied for its unique phytochemical properties, it remains a fascinating subject in botanical research.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range across Western North America. In Western Canada, its distribution includes the provinces of Alberta and British Columbia. Moving into the Northwestern United States, it can be found throughout the state of Idaho. The species also populates much of Montana within the same region. Finally, its presence extends into the coastal and inland areas of Washington.
