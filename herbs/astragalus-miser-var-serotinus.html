Astragalus miser var. serotinus

Astragalus miser var. serotinus, commonly known as the late-blooming milkvetch, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, it contains bioactive compounds traditionally used in holistic remedies. Understanding its ecological niche and chemical profile is essential for its therapeutic application.
