Sichuan fritillary, scientifically known as Fritillaria unibracteata, is a precious medicinal herb highly valued in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its ability to clear heat and transform phlegm, it is frequently used to treat respiratory ailments, including persistent coughs and asthma, making it a cornerstone of herbal pharmacology.
