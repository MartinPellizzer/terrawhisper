Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as copal, is primarily valued for the aromatic resin that is harvested from its bark. This sticky, balsamic substance is extracted through traditional tapping methods, where incisions are made in the trunk to allow the exudate to flow and solidify. Beyond the resin, the plant features a woody structure with a canopy of leaves that characterize its tropical forest habitat, but it is the chemically rich secretion from its stem that holds the most significant medicinal and ritualistic importance.
