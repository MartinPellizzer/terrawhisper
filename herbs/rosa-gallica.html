Gallica, scientifically known as Rosa gallica, is a storied medicinal shrub prized for centuries. Often called the French rose, its fragrant petals possess potent anti-inflammatory and antiseptic properties. Traditionally used to soothe skin irritations and calm the nervous system, this versatile botanical remains a cornerstone of herbal apothecary traditions.
