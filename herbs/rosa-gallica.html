Rosa gallica

Gallica

Rosa gallica, commonly known as the French rose, is a perennial shrub celebrated for its fragrant, deep pink blossoms. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its petals are used to create soothing salves and floral waters. This hardy species remains a cornerstone of botanical gardens and natural skincare formulations worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Rosa gallica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rosaceae, falling under the genus Rosa.
