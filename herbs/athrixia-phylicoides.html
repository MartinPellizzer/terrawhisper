Athrixia phylicoides

Athrixia phylicoides, commonly known as the "Bushveld Aloe" or simply "Athrixia," is a resilient medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally used in various indigenous practices to treat ailments ranging from skin conditions to internal inflammations, showcasing remarkable botanical significance.

  • Scientific name: Athrixia phylicoides
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Athrixia, where it is known as Athrixia phylicoides.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of the continent. Within South Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe.
