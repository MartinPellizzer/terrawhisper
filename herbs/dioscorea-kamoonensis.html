Dioscorea kamoonensis

Dioscorea kamoonensis, commonly known as the Kamoonia yam, is a medicinal climbing vine native to parts of Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its tubers contain bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments, including inflammation and fever. This plant represents a significant botanical resource for exploring natural therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea kamoonensis
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dioscoreales, falling into the family Dioscoreaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dioscorea, specifically as the species Dioscorea kamoonensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across various regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central and Southeast territories, as well as in the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Moving towards the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is well-documented in the state of Assam. Additionally, the species extends its range into the neighboring country of Bangladesh.
