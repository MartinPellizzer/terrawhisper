Catharanthus ovalis

Catharanthus ovalis, commonly known as the Madagascar Periwinkle, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued for its potent alkaloids. Historically utilized in traditional healing, it contains bioactive compounds with significant pharmacological potential. Research focuses on its therapeutic applications in treating various ailments, highlighting its importance in modern ethnobotanical studies.

  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianaales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Catharanthus.
