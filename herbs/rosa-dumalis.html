Rosa dumalis

Rosa dumalis, commonly known as the sweetbriar rose, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to North American woodlands. Renowned for its fragrant, pale pink blooms and thorny stems, this medicinal plant has historically been used in traditional remedies to support digestive health and soothe minor skin irritations and inflammations.

  • Scientific name: Rosa dumalis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant, known as Rosa dumalis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Rosales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rosaceae and the genus Rosa.
