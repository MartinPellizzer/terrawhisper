Lathyrus sylvestris

Lathyrus sylvestris, commonly known as the perennial pea, is a resilient wildflower celebrated for its striking violet blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism. It is valued for its nitrogen-fixing properties and potential therapeutic applications in treating various minor ailments and supporting wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lathyrus sylvestris
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Lathyrus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lathyrus sylvestris, is recognized by various regional common names across Europe. In Poland, it is referred to as Groszek leśny, while in France, it is called gesse des bois. Its names also vary in Northern and Eastern Europe, where it is known as metsänätkelemä in Finnish and mets-seahernes in Estonian. Additionally, in Germany, the species is commonly called Wilde Platteerbse.
