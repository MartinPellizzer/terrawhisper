, scientifically known as Coix, is a versatile medicinal plant primarily celebrated for its nutrient-rich seeds, often called Job's tears. Widely used in traditional Asian medicine, it possesses powerful diuretic, anti-inflammatory, and skin-healing properties. This resilient grain serves as both a functional superfood and a potent natural therapeutic agent.
