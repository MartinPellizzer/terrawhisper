Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its distribution is specifically noted in both Austria and Germany. Moving toward the western territories, the species can be found throughout Southwestern Europe, including France and Spain. Additionally, the plant extends its reach into Southeastern Europe, where it is documented in Greece. This diverse range demonstrates its ability to thrive in various European climates and landscapes.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Job's tears, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for the therapeutic properties found within its seed. While the entire plant can be utilized, the dried and processed grain is the most significant component used to prepare decoctions and powders aimed at reducing inflammation and supporting kidney health. These small, pearl-like seeds are harvested once the plant matures, serving as the concentrated source of the medicinal compounds required for various health treatments.
