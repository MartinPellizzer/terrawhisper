Diplopterys pubipetala

Diplopterys pubipepetala, commonly known as Chaliponga, is a perennial vine native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent psychoactive properties, it contains high concentrations of DMT. Traditionally utilized by indigenous shamans in sacred Ayahuasca brews, this plant serves as a profound tool for spiritual healing and visionary experiences.
