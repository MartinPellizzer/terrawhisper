Carissa carandas

Natal plum/christ's thorn

Carissa carandas, commonly known as Christ's Thorn or Karanda, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its edible, vitamin-rich berries, this plant holds significant medicinal value. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions and skin disorders.

  • Scientific name: Carissa carandas
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, specifically being a member of the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed within the genus Carissa.

Its range extends significantly into China, where it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan regions. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout Eastern Asia, including the island of Taiwan. These various locations highlight the wide environmental adaptability of Carissa carandas.
