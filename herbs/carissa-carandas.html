Natal Plum/Christ's Thorn, scientifically known as Carissa macrocarpa, is a versatile, evergreen shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Renowned for its edible, vitamin-rich fruit and fragrant white blossoms, it is traditionally used to treat wounds, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a unique blend of beauty and healing.
