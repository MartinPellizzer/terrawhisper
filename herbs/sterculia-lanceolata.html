Sterculia lanceolata

What's the taxonomical classification of Sterculia lanceolata?

Sterculia lanceolata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Malvales. The plant is a member of the family Malvaceae and is specifically identified within the genus Sterculia, reaching its taxonomic designation at the species level as Sterculia lanceolata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Sterculia lanceolata has large, alternate, imparipinnate leaves consisting of several pairs of lanceolate leaflets with entire margins and prominent venation. The foliage typically exhibits a leathery texture and can reach significant lengths depending on the age of the tree. Its woody stem features a rugged bark that provides structural support for its spreading canopy. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge in clusters, often presenting in shades of pale yellow or cream. The plant eventually produces distinctive woody follicles that split open to reveal bright red or orange seeds.
