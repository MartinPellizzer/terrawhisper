Crataegus songarica

Crataegus songarica, commonly known as the Songarian Hawthorn, is a potent medicinal plant valued for its cardiovascular benefits. Rich in bioactive flavonoids and antioxidants, it is traditionally utilized to support heart health, regulate blood pressure, and improve circulation. This resilient shrub offers significant therapeutic potential in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus songarica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Crataegus songarica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Crataegus.
