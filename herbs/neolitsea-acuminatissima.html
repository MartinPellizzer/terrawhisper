Neolitsea acuminatissima

Neolitsea acuminatissima, commonly known as a significant member of the Lauraceae family, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it offers potential therapeutic benefits, particularly for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Neolitsea acuminatissima
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Neolitsea acuminatissima, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Laurales. Finally, it is a member of the Lauraceae family and the genus Neolitsea.
