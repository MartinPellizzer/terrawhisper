Callicarpa integerrima

Callicarpa integerrima, commonly known as the narrow-leaf beautyberry, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its slender foliage and vibrant berries, it possesses potent bioactive compounds. Research suggests its leaves and fruits offer significant antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a valuable botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Callicarpa integerrima
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
