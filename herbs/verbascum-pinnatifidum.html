Verbascum pinnatifidum

Verbascum pinnatifidum, commonly known as pinnate mullein, is a distinctive perennial herb native to North America. Characterized by its deeply lobed, fern-like foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it is valued for its soothing properties and potential therapeutic applications in herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Verbascum pinnatifidum
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant, Verbascum pinnatifidum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Scrophulariaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with the genus Verbascum.
