Libidibia coriaria

Libidibia coriaria, commonly known as the African locust bean, is a vital medicinal and nutritional plant native to sub-Saharan Africa. Rich in proteins and bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and inflammation, playing a significant role in both local diets and herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Libidibia coriaria
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Libidiibia.
