Vitis sinocinerea

Vitis sinoceanea, commonly known as the Chinese grape vine, is a significant medicinal plant utilized in traditional practices. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it offers therapeutic potential in treating various ailments. Exploring its phytochemical properties provides valuable insights into its role in natural pharmacology and holistic health treatments.

  • Scientific name: Vitis sinocinerea
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Vitales, it falls under the family Vitaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Vitis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Vitis sinocinerea, is referred to by several common names depending on the linguistic context and specific usage. In Chinese, it is frequently called 小叶葡萄 (zh), 小叶葡萄 (zh-hans), or 小叶葡萄 (zh-cn), all of which translate to "small-leaf grape." Additionally, it is known as 秋葡萄茎 (zh) in certain contexts.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the diverse landscapes of East Asia. Within mainland China, its presence is notably concentrated in the South-Central regions. It also thrives across various parts of Southeast China. Furthermore, its natural habitat extends to the island of Taiwan. These specific areas define the core geographical range of Vitis sinoquinaria.
