Phyllanthus acuminatus

Phyllanthus acuminatus, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, is valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Often used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, this plant offers significant bioactive compounds. Exploring its botanical characteristics reveals its profound potential in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus acuminatus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Phyllanthus.
