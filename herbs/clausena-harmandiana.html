Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several regions across Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China peninsula, it can be found growing in Cambodia, Laos, and Myanmar. It is also documented to inhabit various parts of Thailand. Beyond the mainland, its range extends into the Malesia region. Specifically, the species is known to occur on the island of Jawa.
