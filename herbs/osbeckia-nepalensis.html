Osbeckia nepalensis

Osbeckia nepalensis, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in Himalayan regions, is a versatile plant valued in traditional medicine. Characterized by its vibrant flowers and distinctive foliage, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammatory conditions and skin issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance in herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Osbeckia nepalensis
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Osbeckia nepalensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the Melastomataceae family and falls under the genus Osbeckia.
