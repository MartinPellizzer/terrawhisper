Helianthemum asperum

Helianthemum asperum, commonly known as rocky rose, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Characterized by its bright yellow flowers and textured foliage, this plant thrives in sunny, well-drained environments. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it offers unique botanical interest for dry gardens and ecological restoration.
