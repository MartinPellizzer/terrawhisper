Serenoa repens

Saw palmetto

Serenoa repens, commonly known as Saw Palmetto, is a perennial plant native to the southeastern United States. Highly valued in traditional and modern herbal medicine, its fatty acids and sterols are widely studied for supporting prostate health, managing urinary symptoms, and balancing hormonal levels in men naturally.

  • Scientific name: Serenoa repens
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Serenoa repens, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Arecales and the family Arecaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Serenoa.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Saw Palmetto, is valued in traditional medicine primarily for its reproductive structures. The medicinal components are derived from the fruits, which are small, dark, drupe-like structures that develop from the individual fruit of each flower. These fleshy, berry-like parts are harvested and processed to extract the bioactive compounds used to support prostate and urinary health.
