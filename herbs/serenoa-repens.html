Serenoa repens

Serenoa repens, commonly known as Saw Palmetto, is a perennial plant native to the southeastern United States. Highly valued in traditional and modern herbal medicine, its fatty acids and sterols are widely studied for supporting prostate health, managing urinary symptoms, and balancing hormonal levels in men naturally.

  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
