Saw palmetto, scientifically known as Serenoa repens, is a resilient palm native to the southeastern United States. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is widely utilized in herbal supplements to support prostate health, balance hormones, and manage urinary symptoms, offering a natural approach to men's wellness and vitality.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Saw Palmetto, is valued in traditional medicine primarily for its reproductive structures. The medicinal components are derived from the fruits, which are small, dark, drupe-like structures that develop from the individual fruit of each flower. These fleshy, berry-like parts are harvested and processed to extract the bioactive compounds used to support prostate and urinary health.
