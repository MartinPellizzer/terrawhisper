Paeonia peregrina

Paeonia peregrina, commonly known as the peregrine peony, is a perennial herb native to the mountainous regions of Europe and Asia. Renowned for its striking blooms, this medicinal plant contains bioactive compounds used in traditional practices to address inflammatory conditions, support reproductive health, and promote overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia peregrina
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Paeoniaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Paeonia.
