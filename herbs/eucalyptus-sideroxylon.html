Plant Parts

This plant, Eucalyptus sideroxylon, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, which are primarily concentrated within its essential oils. These volatile oils are extracted from various parts of the plant, most notably from the leaves, where they contain high concentrations of bioactive compounds like cineole. When harvested and distilled, these essential oils serve as the primary medicinal component, providing the potent aromatic and antiseptic qualities that characterize the species.
