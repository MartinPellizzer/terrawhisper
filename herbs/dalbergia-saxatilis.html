Dalbergia saxatilis

Dalbergia saxatilis, commonly known as rock rosewood, is a resilient medicinal plant found in rocky terrains. Valued in traditional healing, it possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often utilized in herbal formulations, this species offers significant therapeutic potential for treating various ailments, making it a vital botanical resource.
