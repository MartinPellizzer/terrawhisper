Campuloclinium macrocephalum

Campuloclinium macrocephalum, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant, holds profound importance in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique botanical features, this species offers diverse therapeutic potential. Exploring its phytochemical properties reveals its value in treating various ailments, making it a vital subject for pharmacological and ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Campuloclinium macrocephalum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Campuloclinium and the species Campuloclinium macrocephalum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning two different continents. In Southern Africa, it can be found growing within the provinces of KwaZulu-Natal, the Free State, and the Northern Provinces. Its presence extends across the Atlantic to Mexico, where it occupies specific regional habitats. Within Mexico, the species is specifically located in the Northeast and the Gulf regions. Consequently, this medicinal plant maintains a widespread range that connects distinct ecological zones in Africa and North America.
