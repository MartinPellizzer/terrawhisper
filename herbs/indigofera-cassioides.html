Indigofera cassioides

Indigofera cassioides, commonly known as the Indigo shrub, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Indigofera cassioides
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Indigofera, specifically as the species Indigofera cassioides.
