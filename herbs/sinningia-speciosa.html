Sinningia speciosa

Florist's gloxinia

Sinningia speciosa, commonly known as the "Balsa" or "Brazilian Balsa," is a versatile shrub native to Brazil. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments and inflammation. Beyond its healing potential, its fast-growing, lightweight wood makes it an important resource in many regions.

  • Scientific name: Sinningia speciosa
  • Family: Gesneriaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Gesneriaceae and is categorized under the genus Sinningiia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Sinningiä speciosa, is recognized by various common names across different languages and regions. In Japanese, it is referred to as グロキシニア, while in Korean, it is called 글록시니아. It is known as Krasny zwónčk in Lower Sorbian and is called 大岩桐 in Chinese. Additionally, in Russian, the plant is identified as Синнингия красивая.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse and widespread geographical distribution across several distinct global regions. In Eastern Asia, its presence can be specifically noted within the territory of Korea. Moving toward the South-Central Pacific, it is found inhabiting the Cook Islands. The Caribbean region also serves as a significant habitat, with occurrences documented in both the Dominican Republic and Puerto Rico. Finally, its range extends further south within the Caribbean to include Trinidad and Tobago.
