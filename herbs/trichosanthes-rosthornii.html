What's the taxonomical classification of Trichosanthes rosthornii?

Trichosanthes rosthornii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the taxonomic lineage of the order Cucurbitales. It is further categorized under the family Cucurbitaceae, which places it among the gourd and melon family. Within this family, it is assigned to the genus Trichosanthes, ultimately reaching its specific identification at the species level as Trichosanthes rosthornii.

The medicinal properties are often attributed to its ability to nourish the lungs and calm the body during febrile illnesses. Beyond its role in respiratory care, various traditional preparations focus on its cooling effects to balance internal heat. This historical application reflects a long-standing reliance on the species within regional herbal pharmacopeias.

According to a study published by "Journal of ethnopharmacology", Trichosanthes rosthornii Harms is an origin plant of Trichosanthis Fructus, which serves as an essential traditional Chinese medicine. This medicine is used to treat conditions such as thoracic obstruction, angina, cardiac failure, myocardial infarction, pulmonary heart disease, and some cerebral ischaemic diseases. Information regarding this plant was collected from scientific materials including books, monographs, pharmacopoeia, and electronic databases like SCI finder, PubMed, Web of Science, ACS, Science Direct, Wiley, Springer, Taylor, CNKI and Google Scholar.
