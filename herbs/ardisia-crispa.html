Ardisia crispa

Ardisia crispa, commonly known as the Evergreen Ardisia, is a resilient shrub native to tropical forest understories. Valued in traditional medicine, its glossy leaves and vibrant berries harbor bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties, offering significant potential in modern ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Ardisia crispa
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is specifically situated in the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ardisia.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions of Eastern Asia. Within China, its distribution is specifically noted in the South-Central and Southeast territories.
