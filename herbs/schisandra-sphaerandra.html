Schisandra sphaerandra

Schisandra sphaerandra, commonly known as Schisandra or Magnolia berry, is a versatile medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its adaptogenic properties, it is used in traditional medicine to enhance vitality, reduce stress, and support liver health. This potent botanical offers significant antioxidant benefits for overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra sphaerandra
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
