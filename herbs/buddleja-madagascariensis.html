Plant Parts

This plant, Buddleja madagascariensis, possesses distinct morphological features, most notably its large, lanceolate leaves which play a significant role in its biological functions. These leaves are typically arranged oppositely along the stems and are characterized by their soft, pubescent texture, often feeling velvety to the touch due to a fine covering of hairs. In traditional medicinal practices, the leaves are the primary part utilized, as they are frequently harvested and prepared to harness the plant's various therapeutic properties.
