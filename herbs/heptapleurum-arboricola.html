Heptapleurum arboricola

Heptapleurum arboricola, commonly known as the Tree Aralia, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Native to tropical regions, its leaves and bark are traditionally used to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Heptapleurum arboricola
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It further descends through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, eventually reaching the family Araliaceae. Within this botanical hierarchy, its specific classification is defined by the genus Heptapleurum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a remarkably wide and diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In East Asia, its presence is noted in the Nansei-shoto islands, Taiwan, and the Hainan province of China. Its range extends into the North-Central Pacific, where it can be found in Hawaii. Additionally, the species reaches far westward into the Southeastern United States, specifically within Florida. This scattered occurrence demonstrates the plant's ability to thrive in various subtropical environments.
