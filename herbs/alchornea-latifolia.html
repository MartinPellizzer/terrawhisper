Alchornea latifolia

Alchornea latifolia, commonly known as the "alchornea" or "palo de sangre," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Alchornea latifolia
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae.
