Paeonia qiui

Paeonia qiui, commonly known as Qiui peony, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific mountainous regions. Renowned in traditional herbal medicine, its roots possess unique bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and promote circulation. Understanding its botanical properties is essential for exploring its significant therapeutic potential in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia qiui
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifragales, it is a member of the family Paeoniaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Paeonia.
