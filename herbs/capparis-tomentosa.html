Capparis tomentosa

Capparis tomentoosa, commonly known as the hairy caper, is a resilient, evergreen shrub native to arid regions of Africa and Asia. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is utilized in traditional practices to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Capparis tomentosa
  • Family: Capparaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is part of the order Brassicales and the family Capparaceae. Its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Capparis, where it is known as Capparis tomentoza.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Within this geographical area, it can be found growing in the nation of Benin. Its presence extends further to include the territory of Burkina. Additionally, the species is documented within the borders of Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its natural range encompasses the country of Guinea.
