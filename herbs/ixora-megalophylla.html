Ixora megalophylla

Ixora megalophylla, commonly known as the Large-leaf Ixora, is a striking tropical shrub prized for its vibrant blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional medicine utilizes its leaves and flowers to treat various ailments, highlighting its potential importance in natural pharmacological studies and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ixora megalophylla
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, it is specifically situated in the Rubiaceae family. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ixora, under which the species Ixora megalophylla is categorized.

Common Names

This plant, Ixora megalophylla, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Giant Ixora, while in Spanish, it is known as Ixora gigante. Within local contexts in India, it is called Jathi, and in the Malay language, it is referred to as Bunga Ixora.
