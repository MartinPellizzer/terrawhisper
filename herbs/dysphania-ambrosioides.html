This plant, known scientifically as Dysphania ambrosioides, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Caryophyllales. Plant Parts

This plant, Dysphania ambrosioides, is characterized by its distinct morphological features, most notably its small, serrated leaves that grow along slender, upright stems. When harvested for medicinal purposes, the aerial parts are typically collected, as they contain the concentrated bioactive compounds necessary for therapeutic use. In many traditional practices, the whole plant is utilized to ensure a full spectrum of its healing properties. Furthermore, the presence of volatile oils within these tissues contributes to its aromatic profile and plays a crucial role in its pharmacological efficacy.
