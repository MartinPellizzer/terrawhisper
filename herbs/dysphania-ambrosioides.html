Dysphania ambrosioides

Dysphania ambrosioides, commonly known as "Kalamako" or "Wild Spurge," is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. Its unique chemical profile makes it a subject of growing pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Dysphania ambrosioides
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Dysphania ambrosioides, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaranthaceae and is situated within the genus Dysphania.
