Atractylodes macrocephala

Atractylodes macrocephala, commonly known as Atractylodes or White Atractylodes, is a vital medicinal herb in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its potent rhizomes, it is primarily used to strengthen the spleen, regulate digestion, and dispel dampness. This aromatic plant plays a crucial role in promoting overall digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Atractylodes macrocephala
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Atractylodes.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions in East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into Eastern Asia, where it grows in both Japan and Korea. Additionally, the species is located within the Indo-China region, specifically in Vietnam.
