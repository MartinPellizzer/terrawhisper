Siberian larkspur, scientifically known as Delphiiniuum grandiflorum, is a striking perennial prized for its tall, blue floral spikes. While celebrated for its ornamental beauty in gardens, it possesses significant historical medicinal properties. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the vast regions of Siberia. It can be found growing in the Republic of Buryatiya, where local ecosystems support its survival. The species also inhabits the territories of Chita and the Irkutsk region. Furthermore, its presence extends into the Krasnoyarsk area of the Siberian landscape. Finally, the distribution of Delphinium grandiflorum includes the remote and rugged terrain of Tuva.
