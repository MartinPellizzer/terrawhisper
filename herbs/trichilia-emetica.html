Trichilia emetica

Trichilia emeetica, commonly known as the African Mahogany, is a versatile medicinal tree native to various African regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and seeds possess significant antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This plant serves as a vital natural resource for treating diverse ailments within local communities.

  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa
