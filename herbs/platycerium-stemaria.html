The life history of Platycerium stemaria is characterized by its classification as a hemicryptophyte, a growth form where the perennating buds are located at or just below the soil surface, protected by leaf litter or a thin layer of substrate. This life strategy allows the plant to endure seasonal fluctuations by maintaining its regenerative capacity near the ground level. Within the broader spectrum of plant life forms, it specifically functions as a hemicryptophyte, distinguishing its survival mechanism from other categories such as phanerophytes, chamaephytes, cryptophytes, or therophytes.
