Arum hygrophilum

Arum hygrophilum, commonly known as the water arum, is a distinctive wetland plant valued in traditional medicine. Growing in moist habitats, it possesses unique bioactive properties. While utilized for its therapeutic potential in various cultures, careful attention must be paid to its potency and proper preparation during medicinal use.
