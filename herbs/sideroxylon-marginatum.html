Sideroxylon marginatum

Sideroxylon marginatum, commonly known as the West Indian Satinwood, is a precious tropical tree valued for its dense, fine-grained timber. Beyond its industrial importance, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical value in Caribbean ecosystems.

  • Family: Sapotaceae
