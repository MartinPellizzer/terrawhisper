Elephant apple, scientifically known as Dillenia indica, is a distinctive tropical tree native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned for its large, edible, acidic fruits, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Beyond its culinary uses, this versatile plant offers numerous medicinal properties, widely utilized in traditional healing practices across various cultures.
