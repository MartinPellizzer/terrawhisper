Asarabacca, scientifically known as Asarum europaeum, is a charming, low-growing perennial native to European woodlands. Historically valued in traditional medicine, this plant features distinctive heart-shaped leaves and dark, bell-like flowers. While once used for its aromatic properties, it must be handled with caution due to its chemical potency.
