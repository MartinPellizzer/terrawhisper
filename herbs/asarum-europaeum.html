Asarum europaeum

Asarum europaeum, commonly known as European wild ginger, is a perennial herb native to much of Europe. Characterized by its glossy, heart-shaped leaves, this plant has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries. It possesses mild sedative and warming properties, often used to treat respiratory ailments and digestive discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Asarum europaeum
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, falling into the family Aristolochiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Asarum.
