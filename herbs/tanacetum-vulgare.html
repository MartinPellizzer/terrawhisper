Scientific literature: Very Extensive (472 studies) · ★★★★★

Tansy, scientifically known as Tanacetum vulgare, is a perennial herb recognized by its bright yellow, button-like flowers and aromatic foliage. Historically used in traditional medicine for digestive support and as an anthelmintic, it must be handled with caution due to the presence of potent, toxic thujone compounds.
