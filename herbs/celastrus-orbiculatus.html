Oriental bittersweet, scientifically known as Celastrus orbiculatus, is a vigorous climbing vine native to East Asia. While often considered an invasive species in many ecosystems, it holds traditional significance in herbal medicine. Its bioactive compounds are studied for potential therapeutic properties, particularly regarding cognitive enhancement and neurological health.
