Distribution

This plant exhibits a specialized geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions. In Asia, it is found throughout China, specifically within the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Beyond its native Asian range, the species has established a presence in Middle Europe, notably in Austria. It can also be located within Southeastern Europe, specifically in Romania. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to persist in both its original habitats and introduced European landscapes.
