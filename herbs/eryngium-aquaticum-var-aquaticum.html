Marsh eryngo, scientifically known as Eryngium aquaticum var. aquaticum, is a striking perennial herb native to wetlands. Characterized by its distinctive blue-tinged, spiny bracts and architectural foliage, it thrives in marshes. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine for its unique bioactive properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across both Europe and North America. In Middle Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Germany. Moving to the Northeastern United States, the species can be found in the states of New Jersey, New York, and Pennsylvania. Furthermore, its range extends into the Southeastern United States, particularly in Alabama.
