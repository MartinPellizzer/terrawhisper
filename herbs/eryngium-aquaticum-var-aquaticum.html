Eryngium aquaticum var. aquaticum

Eryngium aquaticum var. aquaticum, commonly known as water eryngo, is a unique perennial herb native to wetland habitats. Characterized by its striking, spiny blue bracts and aquatic adaptability, this plant holds significant botanical interest. It is valued for its distinct morphology and potential medicinal properties within diverse ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Eryngium aquaticum var. aquaticum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Apiales and is a member of the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Eryngium as the variety aquaticum.
