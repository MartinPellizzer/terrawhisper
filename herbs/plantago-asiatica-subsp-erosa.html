Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of China. It can be found throughout the South-Central and North-Central parts of the country. Additionally, the species is documented in the high-altitude landscapes of Tibet and Qinghai. Its presence also extends into the Southeast region of China. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse environmental range inhabited by this subspecies.

Plant Parts

This plant, specifically the subspecies Plantago asiatica subsp. erosa, is primarily characterized by its distinctive leaf morphology, which serves as a key identifying feature. The leaf is typically lanceolate in shape and is distinguished from other members of the genus by its noticeably erose or irregularly toothed margins. These leaves are arranged in a basal rosette, featuring prominent longitudinal veins that run from the base toward the apex, a structural trait that is common among many medicinal Plantago species used in traditional herbal practices.
