Jalisco Dutchman's pipe

Aristolochia foetida · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (13 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Jalisco Dutchman's pipe, scientifically known as Aristolochia foetida, is a perennial herb recognized by its distinctive tubular flowers. While historically used in traditional medicine, modern science warns of its significant nephrotoxicity and carcinogenicity due to aristolochic acids. Caution is essential, as its use poses severe health risks.
