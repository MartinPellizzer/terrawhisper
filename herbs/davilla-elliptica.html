Davilla elliptica

Davilla elliptica, commonly known as the elliptical davilla, is a captivating climbing woody vine native to tropical regions. Characterized by its fragrant, bell-shaped flowers and lush foliage, this ornamental plant is valued for its aesthetic charm and potential medicinal properties, often used in traditional practices to support holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Davilla elliptica
  • Family: Dilleniaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Davilla elliptica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Dilleniales. Finally, it is a member of the family Dilleniaceae and the genus Davilla.
