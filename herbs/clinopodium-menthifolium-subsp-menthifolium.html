Clinopodium menthifolium subsp. menthifolium

Clinopodium menthifolium subsp. menthifolium, commonly known as minty polemonium or mint-leaved calamint, is a fragrant perennial herb. Valued for its aromatic leaves, it possesses significant medicinal properties, including antimicrobial and digestive benefits. This resilient plant thrives in sunny habitats, offering a blend of therapeutic utility and sensory beauty.

  • Scientific name: Clinopodium menthifolium subsp. menthifolium
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, this medicinal subspecies is identified by the genus Clinopodium.
