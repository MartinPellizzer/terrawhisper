Wood calamint, scientifically known as Clinopodium menthifolium subsp. menthifolium, is a resilient perennial herb cherished for its aromatic foliage. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this medicinal plant offers potent antimicrobial and digestive benefits. Its delicate, mint-scented leaves are traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support overall wellness and vitality.
