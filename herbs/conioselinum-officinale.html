Conioselinum officinale

Conioselinum officinale, commonly known as Hemlock Water-dropwort, is a perennial herb native to Europe. While historically studied for its unique chemical properties, it is highly toxic and potentially lethal if ingested. This botanical species requires careful identification to avoid accidental poisoning, as its appearance can mimic edible wild plants.

  • Scientific name: Conioselinum officinale
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
