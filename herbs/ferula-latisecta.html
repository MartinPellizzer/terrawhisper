Ferula latisecta

Ferula latisecta, commonly known as Asafoetida, is a perennial herb renowned for its pungent, resinous oleo-gum-resin. Extracted from the plant's roots, this potent substance is a staple in Ayurvedic and traditional medicine. It is widely utilized for its digestive, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, serving as a vital culinary spice.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
