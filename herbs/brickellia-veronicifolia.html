Brickellia veronicifolia

Brickellia veronicifolia, commonly known as Veronica-leaf Brickellia, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant is recognized for its unique foliage and aromatic properties. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic applications in treating diverse ailments within indigenous healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Brickellia veronicifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Brickellia veronicifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Brickellia.
