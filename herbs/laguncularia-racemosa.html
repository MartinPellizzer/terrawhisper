White mangrove, scientifically known as Laguncularia racemosa, is a versatile coastal shrub prized in traditional medicine. Often found in saline wetlands, its leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds with significant antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This resilient plant offers promising therapeutic potential for treating various skin and systemic ailments.
