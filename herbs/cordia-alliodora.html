Manjack, scientifically known as Cordia alliodora, is a versatile evergreen tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its fragrant, bell-shaped flowers and medicinal properties, it is used in traditional healing to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient species holds significant value in both ethnobotany and ecology.

The reproduction of Cordia alliodora is characterized by a dioecious sexual reproduction system, meaning individual plants are either male or female, and self-fertilization is notably absent.
