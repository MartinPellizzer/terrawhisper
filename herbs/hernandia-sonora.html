Hernandia sonora

Hernandia sonora, commonly known as the Sonora Hernandia, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it has long been utilized in traditional healing practices to treat various ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and natural medicine development.

  • Scientific name: Hernandia sonora
  • Family: Hernandiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Laurales, falling into the family Hernandiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Hernandia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across both North America and the Caribbean. Within Mexico, its presence is documented in several distinct regions, including the Central, Northeast, and Gulf areas. It also thrives in the Southwestern portion of the Mexican territory. Beyond the mainland, the species extends its range into the Caribbean island of Cuba. Consequently, the distribution of Hernandia sonora spans multiple ecological zones across these varied locations.
