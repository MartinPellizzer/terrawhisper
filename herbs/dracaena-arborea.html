Dracaena arborea

Dracaena arborea, commonly known as the Tree Dracaena, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its architectural beauty. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potential therapeutic properties. Its hardy nature makes it an ideal choice for both tropical landscapes and indoor settings.

  • Scientific name: Dracaena arborea
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, falling into the family Asparagaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Dracaena.
