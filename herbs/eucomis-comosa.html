Crown Pineapple Lily, scientifically known as Eucomis comosa, is a striking perennial native to Southern Africa. Distinguished by its unique rosette of small white flowers atop a fleshy stem, it resembles a miniature pineapple. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value in traditional medicinal practices across Africa.
