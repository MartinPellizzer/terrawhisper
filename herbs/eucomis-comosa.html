Eucomis comosa

Eucomis comosa, commonly known as the Pineapple Lily, is a stunning perennial valued for its unique, rosette-shaped flower heads. Native to Southern Africa, this medicinal plant is traditionally used in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammation, offering both botanical beauty and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Eucomis comosa
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Eucomis.
