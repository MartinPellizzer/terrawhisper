Orobanche aegyptiaca

Egyptian broomrape

Orobanche aegyptiaca, commonly known as Egyptian broomrape, is a unique parasitic plant lacking chlorophyll. Traditionally used in Mediterranean folk medicine, it is valued for its potential bioactive compounds. Research explores its ethnopharmacological properties, particularly its role in managing inflammatory conditions and supporting overall metabolic health through specialized phytochemicals.

  • Scientific name: Orobanche aegyptiaca
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Orobanchaceae. Ultimately, it is identified as a member of the genus Orobanche.
