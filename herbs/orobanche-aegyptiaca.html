Egyptian broomrape, scientifically known as Orobanche aegyptiaca, is a parasitic plant historically valued in traditional Mediterranean medicine. Lacking chlorophyll, it relies on host roots for nutrients. It is primarily recognized for its ethnobotanical applications, specifically used in various cultures to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and certain skin conditions.
