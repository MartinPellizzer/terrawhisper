Isodon nervosus

Isodon nervosus, commonly known as the Ashbark, is a deciduous tree native to North American woodlands. Renowned for its exceptionally hard, durable wood, it has historically been valued for tool handles and heavy-duty implements. While primarily prized for timber, its unique botanical characteristics make it a fascinating forest species.
