Amur grape, scientifically known as Vitis amurensis, is a hardy, deciduous vine native to East Asia. Renowned for its resilience in cold climates, this medicinal plant offers diverse therapeutic potential. Its leaves, roots, and fruit are utilized in traditional practices to support cardiovascular health and combat oxidative stress.
