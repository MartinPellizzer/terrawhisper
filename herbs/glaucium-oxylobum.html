Glaucium oxylobum

Glaucium oxylobum, commonly known as the silver-leafed Glaucium, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its unique physiological properties, this plant is studied for its potential therapeutic applications. Understanding its botanical characteristics and chemical composition is essential for exploring its role in traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Glaucium oxylobum
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Glaucium.
