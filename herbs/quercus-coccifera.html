Kermes Oak, scientifically known as Quercus coccifera, is a distinctive Mediterranean evergreen shrub valued for its medicinal and historical significance. Renowned for its resin-rich bark, it has traditionally been utilized in folk remedies and dye production. This resilient species plays a vital role in supporting regional biodiversity and ecosystems.
