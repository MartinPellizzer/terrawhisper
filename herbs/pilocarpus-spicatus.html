Pilocarpus spicatus, scientifically known as Pilocarpus spicatus, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive alkaloids, particularly pilocarpine, this plant plays a crucial role in pharmacology. It is primarily utilized in treating glaucoma and managing certain conditions related to excessive sweating and salivation.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide presence across several key regions of Brazil. Its occurrence is notably documented within the Northeast region of the country. Additionally, the species can be found throughout the Southeast portion of Brazil. It also extends its natural range into the South region. Consequently, these findings highlight a broad geographical distribution across much of the Brazilian territory.
