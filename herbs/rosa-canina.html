Rosa canina

Rosa canina, commonly known as the dog rose, is a resilient wild shrub celebrated for its medicinal properties. Rich in Vitamin C and antioxidants, its hips are frequently used in herbal teas and syrups to boost immunity, support digestive health, and provide potent anti-inflammatory benefits in traditional wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Rosa canina
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rosa, specifically the species Rosa canina.
