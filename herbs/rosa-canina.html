dog-rose

Rosa canina · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (784 studies) · ★★★★★

Dog-rose, scientifically known as Rosa caniina, is a resilient, wild shrub celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its vitamin C-rich hips, it serves as a potent natural remedy for boosting immunity and supporting digestive health. This versatile plant offers significant antioxidant benefits, making it a staple in herbal medicine.
