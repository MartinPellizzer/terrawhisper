Mesembryanthemum cordifolium

Baby sun rose

Mesembryanthemum cordifolium, commonly known as the Heart-leaf Ice Plant, is a resilient succulent prized for its medicinal properties. Native to various regions, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Its unique physiological traits make it a subject of significant pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Mesembryanthemum cordifolium
  • Family: Aizoaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is further categorized into the family Aizoaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Mesembryanthemum.
