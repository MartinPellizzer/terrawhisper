Distribution

This plant exhibits a distinct geographical distribution that spans across several regions of Eurasia. In Eastern Europe, it can be found growing within the territories of East European Russia. Its presence extends significantly into Central Asia, where it inhabits the landscape of Kazakhstan. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout the mountainous and steppe regions of Kirgizstan and Turkmenistan. Finally, its range is completed by its occurrence within the borders of Tadzhikistan.
