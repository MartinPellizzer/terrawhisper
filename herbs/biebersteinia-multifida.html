Biebersteinia multifida

Biebersteinia multifida, commonly known as the split-leaf Biebersteinia, is a rare medicinal herb native to specific regions of Asia. Valued in traditional practices, it possesses unique phytochemical properties. Researchers are currently exploring its potential therapeutic applications, particularly for its bioactive compounds and their roles in supporting overall wellness.

  • Family: Biebersteiniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Biebersteiniaceae, situated within the genus Biebersteinia.
