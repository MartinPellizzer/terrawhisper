Blepharis linariifolia

Blepharis linariifolia, commonly known as the narrow-leaved blepharis, is a versatile medicinal herb found throughout tropical Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions. This resilient plant offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Blepharis linariifolia
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Blepharis linariifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Blepharis.
