Zanthoxylum austrosinense

Zanthoxylum austrosinense, commonly known as the Chinese Prickly Ash, is a versatile medicinal plant native to subtropical forests. Renowned for its bioactive alkaloids and essential oils, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to alleviate digestive issues, reduce inflammation, and provide analgesic relief, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Zanthoxylum austrosinense
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Zanthoxylum.
