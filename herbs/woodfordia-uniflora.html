Woodfordia uniflora

Woodfordia uniflora, commonly known as Fire-flame Bush, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its vibrant red flowers, it plays a vital role in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. The plant is frequently utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and astringent properties to treat various ailments.

  • Family: Lythraceae
  • Native range: Africa
