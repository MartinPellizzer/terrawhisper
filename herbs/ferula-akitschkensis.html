Ferula akitschkensis

Ferula akitschkensis, commonly known as a vital medicinal herb from Central Asian landscapes, holds significant ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this species is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Exploring its phytochemical properties offers promising insights into developing new therapeutic agents for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Ferula akitschkensis
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Ferula as Ferula akitschkensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the regions of Central Asia and East Asia. In Central Asia, its presence is documented across the vast landscapes of Kazakhstan. It can also be found growing within the mountainous territories of Kirgizstan. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the eastern part of the continent. Specifically, it is located within the Xinjiang region of China.
