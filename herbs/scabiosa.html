Scabiosa

Pincushion flower

Scabiosa, commonly known as the pincushion flower, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its therapeutic properties. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it possesses anti-inflammatory and antiseptic qualities. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this resilient perennial offers significant botanical interest, serving as a vital component in various natural healing remedies.

  • Scientific name: Scabiosa
  • Family: Caprifoliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Dipsacales, it is classified under the family Caprifoliaceae. Finally, the medicinal species is categorized under the genus Scabiosa.
