Gentiana dahurica

Gentiana dahuricia, commonly known as Dahurian gentian, is a perennial herb native to the alpine regions of East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its bitter roots contain potent iridoid glycosides. These bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential, particularly for supporting digestive health and reducing systemic inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana dahurica
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, it is a member of the Gentianaceae family. Finally, its taxonomic hierarchy places it within the genus Gentiana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Russia, its presence is noted specifically within the Chita region of Siberia. The species is also widely found throughout China, spanning across the South-Central and North-Central territories. Furthermore, it inhabits the northern stretches of China within Inner Mongolia. Finally, its range extends into the high-altitude landscapes of Qinghai.
