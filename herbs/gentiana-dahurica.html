Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In Russia, its presence is noted specifically within the Chita region of Siberia. The species is also widely found throughout China, spanning across the South-Central and North-Central territories. Furthermore, it inhabits the northern stretches of China within Inner Mongolia. Finally, its range extends into the high-altitude landscapes of Qinghai.

Plant Parts

This plant, Gentiana dahurica, is highly valued in traditional medicine, with its primary therapeutic components derived from its underground structures. The medicinal properties are concentrated in the roots and rhizomes, which are harvested and processed to ensure potency. In pharmaceutical applications, the air-dried roots are most commonly utilized to prepare decoctions and extracts. While the plant produces distinctive flowers and eventually develops seeds for reproduction, these reproductive parts are typically not the focus of its medicinal use, as the most potent bitter glycosides are found within the subterranean portions.
