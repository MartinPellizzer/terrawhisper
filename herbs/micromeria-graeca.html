Greek Savory, scientifically known as Micromeria graeca, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Often found in rocky terrains, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, including its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial activities, making it a subject of significant ethnobotanical interest.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing in the Balearic Islands, known as Baleares. The species also inhabits the island of Corsica and the territory of France. Additionally, its range extends to the coastal areas of Portugal. Finally, its geographical presence includes the Mediterranean island of Sardinia.
