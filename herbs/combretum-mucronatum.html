Combretum mucronatum

Combretum mucronatum, commonly known as the Natal bush or Natal aloe, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Combretum mucronatum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Combretum.
