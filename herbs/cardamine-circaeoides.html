Cardamine circaeoides

Cardamine circaeoides, commonly known as the round-leaved bittercress, is a delicate herbaceous perennial native to diverse woodland habitats. While primarily recognized for its distinctive foliage, this plant holds traditional significance in ethnobotany. Its subtle medicinal properties and nutritional potential make it a fascinating subject for botanical and pharmacological study.

  • Scientific name: Cardamine circaeoides
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central to the North-Central territories. Its presence also extends into the Southeast regions of the country. Moving beyond the mainland, the species is native to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Finally, its range reaches the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam.
