Rhanterium suaveolens

Rhanterium suaveolens, commonly known as the Everlasting Flower, is a resilient shrub native to South America. Renowned for its aromatic properties and striking blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic potential, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant activities.
