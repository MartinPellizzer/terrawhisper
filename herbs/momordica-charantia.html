Momordica charantia

Bitter gourd

Momoerdica charantia, commonly known as bitter melon or bitter gourd, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is widely used to manage blood sugar levels, support digestive health, and provide antioxidant benefits, making it a vital staple in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Momordica charantia
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cucurbitales, it is classified under the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Momordica, with the specific species name being charantia.

Distribution

This plant, Momordica charantia, exhibits a diverse geographical presence across several key regions. In the Macaronesia region, it can be found specifically in Cape Verde. Its distribution extends significantly into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Benin. Furthermore, the species is present in the West Tropical African nations of Burkina and Gambia. Finally, its range continues through the West Tropical African territory of Ghana.

Plant Parts

This plant, Momordica charantia, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The vibrant green fruits are perhaps the most well-known part, often consumed as a vegetable or processed for their hypoglycemic effects. Within each fruit, one can find numerous small, distinctive seeds that are frequently used in traditional remedies. Additionally, the large, lobed leaves are highly valued, as every individual leaf contains bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's overall healing potential.
