Saussurea amara

Saussurea amara, commonly known as bitter saussurea, is a potent medicinal herb indigenous to the high-altitude Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional Tibetan and Chinese medicine, this plant possesses remarkable therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and detoxifying effects, playing a vital role in holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Saussurea amara
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Saussurea.
