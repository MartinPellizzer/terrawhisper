Tall sawwort, scientifically known as Saussurea amaram, is a robust perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent bitter properties used to treat digestive ailments, fever, and inflammation. This resilient plant remains a vital component of herbal therapeutic practices.
