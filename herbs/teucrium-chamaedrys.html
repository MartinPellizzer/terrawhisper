Teucrium chamaedrys

Teucrium chamaedrys, commonly known as germander, is a perennial herb native to Europe. Long valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses astringent and antimicrobial properties. Historically used to support digestive health and treat respiratory ailments, this resilient plant remains a fascinating subject for both botanical enthusiasts and modern medicinal researchers.

  • Scientific name: Teucrium chamaedrys
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Teucrium.
