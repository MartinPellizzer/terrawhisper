Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as germander, is a versatile medicinal herb where various parts are utilized for their therapeutic properties. While the leaves and flowering tops are the primary components used in herbal preparations, they are frequently processed into infusions to extract their beneficial compounds. These infusions are created by steeping the plant material in hot water, allowing the active constituents to dissolve, which provides a gentle method for consuming the herb to support digestive and respiratory health.
