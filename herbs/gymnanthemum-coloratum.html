Gymnanthemum coloratum

Gymnanthemum coloratum, commonly known as the "herb of longevity," is a remarkable medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is traditionally used to support metabolic health, improve glucose regulation, and promote overall vitality through its unique bioactive compounds and natural strength.

  • Scientific name: Gymnanthemum coloratum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is specifically nested in the family Asteraceae. It is further identified by the genus Gymnanthemum, which includes the species Gymnanthemum coloratum.
