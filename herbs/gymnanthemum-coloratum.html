Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. Within the Macaronesia archipelago, its presence is noted in Cape Verde. The species is also found throughout West Tropical Africa, specifically spanning multiple countries. Its documented range includes Benin, Burkina, and the Gambia.
