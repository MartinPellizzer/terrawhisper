Hypericum lanceolatum

Hypericum lanceolatum, commonly known as lanceleaf St. John's wort, is a perennial herb native to New Zealand. Distinguished by its narrow, lance-shaped leaves and vibrant yellow flowers, this botanical species belongs to the Hypericaceae family. It plays a vital role in local ecosystems, providing nectar for various native pollinators.

  • Native range: Africa
