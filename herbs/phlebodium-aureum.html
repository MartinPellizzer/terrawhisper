Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across several tropical and subtropical regions. In the Macaronesian islands, it can be found growing in both the Canary Islands and Madeira. Its range extends significantly into Southern Africa, where it inhabits the Cape Provinces and the KwaZulu-Natal region. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in India. These varied locations highlight its ability to thrive in distinct maritime and continental environments.
