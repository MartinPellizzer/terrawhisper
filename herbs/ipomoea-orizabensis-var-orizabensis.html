Ipomoea orizabensis var. orizabensis

Ipomoea orizabensis var. orizabensis, commonly known as wild morning glory, is a resilient medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, this species is utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering significant therapeutic value in exploring natural treatments for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea orizabensis var. orizabensis
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ipomoea, specifically as the variety orizabensis.
