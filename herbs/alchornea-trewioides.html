Alchornea trewioides

Alchornea trewioides, commonly known as the "forest Alchornea," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This botanical specimen offers significant pharmacological potential for developing new natural therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Alchornea trewioides
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized into the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Alchornea.
