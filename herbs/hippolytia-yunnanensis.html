What's the taxonomical classification of Hippolytia yunnanensis?

Hippolytia yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy places it in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asterales. As a member of the family Asteraceae, this plant is further categorized into the genus Hippolytia, with its specific identification being the species yunnanensis.
