What's the taxonomical classification of Lithocarpus henryi?

Lithocarpus henryi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant falls under the order Fagales and is a member of the family Fagaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lithocarpus and the species henryi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lithocarpus henryi has simple, evergreen leaves that are elliptical to oblong in shape, measuring approximately 5 to 15 centimeters in length, and characterized by a coriaceous texture with entire or slightly serrated margins. The leaf surfaces are typically dark green and glossy on the upper side, while the undersides may appear slightly paler or pubescent. Its woody stems and branches develop a rugged, fissured bark as the tree matures into a large canopy specimen. The reproductive structures consist of inconspicuous flowers that eventually produce hard-shelled acorns held within a cupule. These nuts are a key morphological feature used to identify the species within its forest habitat.
