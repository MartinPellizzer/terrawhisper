Jatropha mollissima

Jatrophia mollisima, commonly known as the "milk bush" or "soapberry," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its succulent leaves and latex, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant pharmacological potential in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Jatropha mollissima
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae and falls under the genus Jatropha.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the South American continent. In Northern South America, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Venezuela. Moving toward the western portion of the continent, the species can also be found in Bolivia. Within Brazil, its range is quite extensive, covering the West-Central territories. Additionally, the plant is widely distributed throughout the Northeast and Southeast regions of Brazil.
