Aria edulis

Aria edulis, commonly known as the edible Aria, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. Exploring its phytochemical profile offers promising insights into modern natural pharmacology and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Aria edulis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed by its genus, Aria.

Common Names

This plant, Aria edulis, is known by various common names depending on the language and region. In Finland, it is referred to as saksanpihlaja (fi), while in Norway, it is commonly called sølvasal (nb), and in South Korea, it is known as 유럽팥배나무 (ko).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the regions of Northern Europe. In the western reaches of this area, it can be found growing in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the Scandinavian Peninsula, the species is also established in Norway and Sweden. Furthermore, its presence extends to the coastal landscapes of Denmark. Consequently, these five nations represent the primary geographical range for Aria edulis.
