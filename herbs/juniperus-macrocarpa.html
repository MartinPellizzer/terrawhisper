Juniperus macrocarpa

Large-fruited juniper

Juniperus macrocarpa, commonly known as large-fruited juniper, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and berry-like cones, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. Its bioactive compounds offer potential antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, making it a subject of ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus macrocarpa
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe
