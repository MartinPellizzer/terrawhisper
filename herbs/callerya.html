Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions of China. It can be found growing in the areas of China South-Central and China North-Central. Additionally, the species is present in the tropical environment of Hainan. Its range also extends into the provinces of China Southeast. Finally, this medicinal plant is documented to grow within the high-altitude terrain of Tibet.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Calathea (often misspelled as Callerya), utilizes its sturdy stem as a structural foundation to support its large, vibrant foliage. While the stems are relatively short and grow close to the ground, they play a crucial role in transporting water and nutrients from the root system to the decorative leaves, ensuring the plant remains hydrated and healthy.
