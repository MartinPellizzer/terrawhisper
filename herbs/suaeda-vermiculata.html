Seablite, scientifically known as Suaeda vermiculata, is a resilient halophytic shrub native to arid coastal regions. Renowned for its ability to thrive in saline soils, this medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its antioxidant properties and diverse pharmacological applications.
