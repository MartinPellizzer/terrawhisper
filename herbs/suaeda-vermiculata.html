Suaeda vermiculata

Seablite

Suaeda vermiculata, commonly known as sea blite or saltwort, is a succulent halophyte frequently found in saline coastal and desert environments. Renowned for its remarkable ability to thrive in high-salinity soils, this medicinal plant is utilized in traditional practices for its antioxidant properties and various therapeutic healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Suaeda vermiculata
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, falling into the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Suaeda, specifically as the species Suaeda vermiculata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in the Mediterranean basin. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Spain. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the species can be found inhabiting Sicilia. Its range extends significantly into Northern Africa, where it is documented in Algeria. Furthermore, the plant is also widely distributed throughout the North African territories of Libya and Egypt.
