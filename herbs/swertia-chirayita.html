Swertia chirayita

Swertia chirayita, commonly known as Chirata, is a potent medicinal herb renowned in Ayurveda for its intense bitterness. Widely used to treat fever, digestive disorders, and skin ailments, it possesses powerful antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and hepatoprotective properties, making it a vital natural remedy for enhancing overall immunity and detoxification.

  • Scientific name: Swertia chirayita
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Swertia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing in the lush landscapes of Assam.
