Zygophyllum propinquum

Zygophyllum propiqnuum, commonly known as the Karoo Sand Mustard, is a resilient succulent native to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Renowned for its ability to thrive in harsh environments, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its profound ethnobotanical significance.

  • Scientific name: Zygophyllum propinquum
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa
