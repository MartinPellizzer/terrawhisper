Phalaris arundinacea

Reed canary grass

Phalaris arundiunacea, commonly known as Arundinaceous Canary Grass, is a resilient botanical species valued for its unique properties. While primarily recognized in ecological studies, its traditional medicinal applications highlight its potential within herbal pharmacology. Understanding its bioactive profile offers fascinating insights into its role in natural wellness and biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Phalaris arundinacea
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales and family Poaceae, it is further classified under the genus Phalaris. This taxonomic hierarchy defines its biological position as a member of the grass family.
