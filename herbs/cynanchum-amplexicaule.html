What's the taxonomical classification of Cynanchum amplexicaule?

Cynanchum amplexicaule belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Gentianaales. This plant is further categorized into the family Apocynaceae, which is the group to which its genus, Cynanchum, pertains. Ultimately, its specific biological identity is defined by its species designation, amplexiCaule.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cynanchum amplexicaule has climbing stems that wrap tightly around host plants, ovate to lanceolate leaves that clasp the stem at the base, and small, tubular, pale flowers arranged in umbel-like cymes. Its foliage is often smooth or slightly pubescent, providing a lush appearance to the climbing vine. The inflorescence consists of multiple tiny blossoms that emerge from the leaf axils.
