Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found inhabiting both the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range extends into the Indo-China region, specifically within the country of Vietnam. Additionally, the species is documented in the Malesia region, specifically on the island of Jawa. Together, these locations highlight the diverse environmental niches occupied by the species.
