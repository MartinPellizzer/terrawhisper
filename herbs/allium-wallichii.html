Himalayan onion, scientifically known as Allium wallichii, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its distinctive culinary aroma and therapeutic properties, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to support digestive health and possesses potent antioxidant and antimicrobial characteristics.
