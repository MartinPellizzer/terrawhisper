Allium wallichii

Allium wallichii, commonly known as Wallich's onion, is a perennial bulbous plant native to the Himalayan regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. Often utilized to treat respiratory ailments and digestive issues, this aromatic species serves as a vital component in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Allium wallichii
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized in the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Allium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several distinct regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central and Southeast areas, as well as the high-altitude terrain of Tibet. Its distribution extends significantly into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically throughout the state of Assam. Additionally, it can be found growing within the East Himalaya region. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in different ecological zones across the continent.
