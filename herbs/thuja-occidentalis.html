Thuja occidentalis

Northern whitecedar

Thuja occidentalis, commonly known as Eastern White Cedar, is a versatile evergreen native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional herbalism to support respiratory health, promote skin healing, and provide antiseptic properties, making it a cornerstone of botanical wellness.

  • Scientific name: Thuja occidentalis
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling into the family Cupressaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Thuja.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Eastern White Cedar, offers various components that are utilized in traditional medicinal practices. The scale-like leaves are frequently harvested to create aromatic infusions, while the fragrant wood is often used in topical applications due to its resinous properties. Medicinal extracts derived from the entire organism are sometimes employed for their antimicrobial qualities. Additionally, the tough outer bark and the viscous plant sap are noted for their concentrated chemical compounds, which have historically been used in various folk remedies.
