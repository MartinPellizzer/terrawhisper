Coreopsis lanceolata

Coreopsis lanceolata, commonly known as lanceleaf coreopsis, is a resilient perennial wildflower native to North America. Distinguished by its vibrant yellow, daisy-like blooms and slender, lance-shaped foliage, this hardy plant thrives in sunny prairies. It is highly valued in landscapes for its drought tolerance and ability to attract pollinators.
