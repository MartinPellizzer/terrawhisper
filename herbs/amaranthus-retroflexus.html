Amaranthus retroflexus

Amaranthus retroflexus, commonly known as redroot pigweed, is a resilient annual herb frequently found in disturbed soils. While often viewed as a common agricultural weed, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Rich in vitamins and minerals, it has been utilized historically in various traditional medicinal practices for its nutritional properties.

  • Scientific name: Amaranthus retroflexus
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Amaranthus retroflexus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Ultimately, it is classified under the genus Amaranthus.
