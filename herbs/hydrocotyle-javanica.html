Hydrocotyle javanica

Hydrocotyle javanica, commonly known as Java pennywort, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical wetlands. Renowned for its bioactive properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its unique phytochemical profile offers significant therapeutic potential in supporting overall wellness and healing.

  • Family: Araliaceae
