Berberis baluchistanica

Barberry

Berberis baluchistanica, commonly known as Baluchistan barberry, is a resilient shrub native to the arid regions of Western Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its roots and bark contain potent alkaloids like berberine, offering powerful anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties used to treat various metabolic and digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Berberis baluchistanica
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Berberis baluchistanica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is classified under the family Berberidaceae and the genus Berberis.
