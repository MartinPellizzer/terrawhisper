Barberry, scientifically known as Berberis baluchistanica, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to the rugged terrains of Balochistan. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly berberine, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support digestive health and manage metabolic functions effectively.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range spanning across parts of Western and Southern Asia. Within the Arabian Peninsula, its presence is documented in the country of Oman.
