Ebenopsis ebano

Ebenopsis ebano, commonly known as Ebony False Acacia, is a resilient tropical legume valued for its dense, dark wood and nitrogen-fixing capabilities. Native to various forested regions, this hardy plant plays a vital role in soil enrichment and ecosystem stability, offering significant potential in ecological restoration and traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Ebenopsis ebano
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, placing it in the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Ebenopsis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across specific regions of North America. In the South-Central United States, its distribution is notably centered within the state of Texas. Moving into Mexico, the species can be found spanning several distinct territories. It is established throughout the Northeast, Gulf, and Northwest regions of the country. Furthermore, the plant extends its range into the Southwest portion of Mexico.
