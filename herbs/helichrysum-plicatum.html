Helichrysum plicatum

Helichrysum plicatum, commonly known as the Everlasting flower, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it possesses remarkable anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and skin-healing properties. Traditionally used in holistic wellness, this botanical treasure offers therapeutic benefits for skincare and inflammatory relief.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum plicatum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. These oils are concentrated within the aerial parts with flowers, making the entire above-ground structure of the plant a source of therapeutic compounds. The most visually striking and chemically significant components are the flowers, which grow in dense clusters. Whether focusing on the individual flower or the collective beauty of the flowers, it is the intricate structure of these blooms that holds the greatest concentration of the plant's healing essence.
