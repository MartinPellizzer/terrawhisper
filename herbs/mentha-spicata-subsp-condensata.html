Mentha spicata subsp. condensata

Mentha spicata subsp. condensata, commonly known as a specialized variety of spearmint, is a fragrant perennial herb valued in herbal medicine. Characterized by its dense growth and aromatic oils, it offers soothing properties. Often utilized in aromatherapy and digestive remedies, this botanical subspecies provides unique therapeutic benefits in natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Mentha spicata subsp. condensata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Mentha. Specifically, this medicinal specimen is identified as the subspecies condensata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across various regions of Europe. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is notably recorded in Sardegna. Moving toward the Balkan region, it can be found in several Southeastern European countries. Specifically, it inhabits the landscapes of Albania, Bulgaria, and Greece. Finally, its range extends into the Southeastern European territory of Italy.
