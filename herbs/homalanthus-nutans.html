Homalanthus nutans

Homalanthus nutans, commonly known as the White Spurge, is a tropical shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to Southeast Asia and the Pacific, its leaves are frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This versatile plant offers significant ethnobotanical potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Homalanthus nutans
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Pacific
