Kalanchoe ceratophylla var. ceratophylla

Kalanchoe ceratophylla var. ceratophylla, commonly known as the "Horned Kalanchoe," is a striking succulent native to Mexico. Renowned for its fleshy, pointed leaves, this resilient plant is prized in traditional herbalism. It is often studied for its potential bioactive compounds and its ability to thrive in arid environments.

  • Scientific name: Kalanchoe ceratophylla var. ceratophylla
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Crassulaceae and the genus Kalanchoe. Specifically, it is identified as the variety ceratophylla.
