Satureja bachtiarica

Saturaja bachtiarica, commonly known as the golden mountain herb, is a potent medicinal plant native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its rich antioxidant properties, it is traditionally used to boost immunity, reduce inflammation, and support digestive health. This resilient botanical offers a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.
