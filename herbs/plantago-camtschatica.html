This plant is primarily found across the diverse landscapes of the Russian Far East. Its natural range includes the rugged terrain of the Kamchatka Peninsula and the vast stretches of the Khabarovsk region. It also thrives within the unique ecosystems of the Kuril Islands. Furthermore, its distribution extends to the Magadan region, where it adapts to specific local climates. Finally, the species is well-established in the Primorye territory, completing its regional presence.
