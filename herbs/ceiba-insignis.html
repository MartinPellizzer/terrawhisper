Ceiba insignis

Ceiba insignis, commonly known as the silk floss tree, is a majestic tropical species valued for its impressive stature and ecological importance. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant medicinal potential, with various parts used in traditional practices to treat ailments ranging from skin irritations to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Ceiba insignis
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ceiba insignis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Ceiba.
