Goniothalamus laoticus

Goniothalamus laoticus, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in Southeast Asian traditional medicine, holds great pharmacological potential. This perennial herb is valued for its bioactive compounds, which are frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Research continues to explore its therapeutic properties, particularly regarding its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant activities.

  • Scientific name: Goniothalamus laoticus
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Annonaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Goniothalamus.
