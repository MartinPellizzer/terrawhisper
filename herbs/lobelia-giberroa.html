Lobelia giberroa

Lobelia gibberoa, commonly known as the slender lobelia, is a striking perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Characterized by its delicate, bell-shaped blue flowers and slender stems, this botanical gem thrives in moist, shaded habitats. It holds significant interest for botanists studying unique alpine flora and biodiversity.

  • Family: Campanulaceae
