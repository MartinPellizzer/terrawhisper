Magnolia hypolampra

Magnolia hypolampra, commonly known as a rare medicinal magnolia, is a distinctive botanical species valued for its unique therapeutic potential. Native to specific subtropical regions, this plant possesses bioactive compounds that offer significant pharmacological interest. Researchers study its traditional uses to uncover new possibilities in natural medicine and healing.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia hypolampra
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Magnolia as the species Magnolia hypolampra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central regions. It is also found throughout the Southeast portions of the country. Additionally, the species can be located on the island of Hainan.
