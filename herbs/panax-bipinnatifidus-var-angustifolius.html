Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of Asia. Within China, it can be found throughout the South-Central regions as well as in Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically inhabiting the state of Assam. Additionally, it is widely distributed across the East Himalaya region. Finally, the species is also documented to grow within the borders of Nepal.
