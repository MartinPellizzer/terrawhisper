Myrothamnus flabellifolius

Myrothamnus flabellifolius, commonly known as the fan-leaved shrub, is a versatile medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammatory conditions and infections. This botanical specimen holds significant potential for pharmacological research and natural wellness applications worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Myrothamnus flabellifolius
  • Family: Myrothamnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Gunnerales. Finally, it is classified within the family Myrothamnaceae under the genus Myrothamnus.
