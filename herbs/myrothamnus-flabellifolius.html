Resurrection Bush

Myrothamnus flabellifolius · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (30 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Resurrection Bush, scientifically known as Myrothamnus flabellifolius, is a remarkable perennial shrub renowned for its ability to survive extreme desiccation. Capable of reviving after intense drought, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant potential in modern pharmacology.
