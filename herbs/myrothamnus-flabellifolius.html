Resurrection Bush

Myrothamnus flabellifolius · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (30 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Resurrection Bush, scientifically known as Myrothamnus flabellifolius, is a remarkable perennial shrub renowned for its ability to survive extreme desiccation. Capable of reviving after intense drought, this resilient plant is valued in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant potential in modern pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several regions across the African continent. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends into East Tropical Africa, where it grows in both Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern reaches, the species is documented in the South Tropical Africa region of Angola. Finally, the plant is also distributed within South Tropical Africa in Malawi.

Attached to these stems are the leaves, which are characterized by their distinct fan-shaped morphology, a feature from which the species derives its specific epithet.
