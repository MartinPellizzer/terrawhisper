Rubia ovatifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Rubia ovatifolia?

Rubia ovatifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is positioned in the order Gentianaales. It is a member of the family Rubiaceae, falling under the genus Rubia, and is specifically identified by its species name, ovatifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rubia ovatifolia has prostrate to ascending woody stems that branch frequently and bear opposite, ovate to elliptical leaves with entire margins. The foliage is typically dark green and can reach lengths of up to five centimeters. Small, tubular flowers emerge from the leaf axils in clusters, displaying a pale pink to white coloration. These blossoms are accompanied by small, fleshy drupes that transition from green to a deep red or black color upon maturation. The entire plant forms a dense, spreading mat through its creeping growth habit.
