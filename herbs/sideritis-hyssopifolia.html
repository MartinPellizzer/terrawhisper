Mountain Tea, scientifically known as Sideritis hyssopifolia, is a fragrant perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, this plant is widely used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. Its antioxidant-rich infusion offers a soothing, revitalizing experience for holistic wellness.
