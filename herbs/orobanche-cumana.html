Orobanche cumana

Orobanche cumana, commonly known as the parasitic broomrape, is a unique medicinal plant found in various arid regions. Although it lacks chlorophyll and survives by parasitizing host roots, it possesses significant bioactive properties. Research suggests its extracts hold potential in traditional medicine for treating various inflammatory and oxidative conditions.

  • Scientific name: Orobanche cumana
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Orobanchaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Orobanche.
