Careya arborea

Slow match tree

Careya arborea, commonly known as the White Mangrove or Carey, is a majestic tropical tree celebrated in Ayurvedic medicine. Featuring large, fragrant white flowers, it holds significant therapeutic value. Its various parts, including bark and leaves, are traditionally utilized to treat ailments such as inflammation, skin disorders, and digestive issues.
