Xeranthemum annuum

Xeranthemum annuum, commonly known as Everlasting Flower, is a striking annual herb cherished for its papery, long-lasting blooms. Native to Mediterranean regions, this resilient plant features vibrant, straw-like bracts. Beyond its ornamental beauty in dried arrangements, it holds traditional significance in botanical studies and diverse ecological landscapes worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Xeranthemum annuum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Xeranthemum.
