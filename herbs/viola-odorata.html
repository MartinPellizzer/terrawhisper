Plant Parts

This plant, Viola odorata, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components offer therapeutic benefits. The fragrant, violet-colored flowers are frequently used to create aromatic infusions, while the heart-shaped leaves can be utilized in topical applications or herbal preparations. Deep within the soil, the roots serve as a potent source of medicinal compounds, often harvested for their specific healing properties. In many traditional practices, the whole plant is collected to ensure a complete profile of its bioactive constituents, and the tiny seed capsules are also noted for their role in the plant's reproductive cycle and potential medicinal utility.
