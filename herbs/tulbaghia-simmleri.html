Tulbaghia simmleri

Tulbaghia simmleri, commonly known as Simmler's garlic, is a striking perennial herb native to Southern Africa. Distinguished by its fragrant, garlic-scented foliage and delicate white flowers, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial properties and its ability to support digestive health and overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Tulbaghia simmleri
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Tulbaghia simmleri, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaryllidaceae and the genus Tulbaghia.
