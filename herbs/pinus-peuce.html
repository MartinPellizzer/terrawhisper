Pinus peuce

Pinus peuce, commonly known as the Weeping Blue Pine, is a majestic conifer native to high-altitude regions of Mexico and Guatemala. Renowned for its striking silvery-blue needles and elegant, pendulous branches, this resilient species thrives in cool climates, offering significant ornamental value and ecological importance in mountain ecosystems.
