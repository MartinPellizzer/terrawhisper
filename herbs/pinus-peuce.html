Macedonian Pine, scientifically known as Pinus peuce, is a majestic evergreen native to the high-altitude mountains of the Balkans. Renowned for its elegant, narrow silhouette, this resilient conifer offers medicinal potential through its resin and needles, which are traditionally valued for their soothing, antiseptic, and restorative botanical properties.
