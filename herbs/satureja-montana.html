The reproduction of Saturaja montana is characterized by a strictly sexual process, as asexual reproduction is entirely absent. Following successful pollination, the plant develops a dry fruit classified as an achene. This fruit is indehiscent, meaning it does not spontaneously split open at maturity to release its seeds.
