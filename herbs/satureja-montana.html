Winter savory, scientifically known as Satureja montana, is a hardy, perennial herb prized for its potent aromatic qualities. Renowned in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and digestive benefits, this robust plant thrives in well-drained soils. Its pungent leaves offer both culinary depth and therapeutic properties essential to holistic wellness.
