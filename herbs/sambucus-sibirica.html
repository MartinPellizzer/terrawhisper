Siberian elder, scientifically known as Sambucus sibirica, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to the harsh climates of Northern Asia. Renowned for its dark berries and fragrant blossoms, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional herbalism for its potent antioxidant properties and ability to support immune health.
