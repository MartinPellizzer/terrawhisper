Sambucus sibirica

Sambucus sibirica, commonly known as Siberian elderberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this plant is traditionally used to support immune health and combat inflammation. Its dark, nutrient-rich berries offer a natural remedy for respiratory ailments and seasonal wellness.

  • Scientific name: Sambucus sibirica
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales, it is further categorized under the family Viburnaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sambucus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Northern Asia and Europe. In Eastern Europe, its presence is notably documented throughout East European Russia. Moving into the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found in the Altay region. The species also inhabits the areas of Buryatiya and Chita within the Siberian landscape. Finally, its range extends further into the Irkutsk region of Siberia.
