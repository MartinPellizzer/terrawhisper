Elephant-bush, scientifically known as Portulacaria afra, is a versatile succulent native to South Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Beyond its healing potential, this resilient plant serves as vital forage for wildlife and a stunning ornamental choice.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Spekboom, is characterized by its distinctive succulent structure, where the most notable medicinal component is its fleshy leaves. These thick, moisture-retaining leaves serve as a vital reservoir for nutrients and water, making them highly resilient in arid environments. In traditional medicinal practices, these fleshy leaves are often utilized due to their unique chemical composition and ability to store essential bioactive compounds.
