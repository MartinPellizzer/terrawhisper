Elephant-bush, scientifically known as Portulacaria afra, is a versatile succulent native to South Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Beyond its healing potential, this resilient plant serves as vital forage for wildlife and a stunning ornamental choice.
