Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several key regions in Asia. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the lush landscapes of Assam. The species also extends into the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya. Finally, its geographical reach concludes with significant populations located in Nepal.
