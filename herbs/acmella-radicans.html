Acmella radicans

Acmeilla radicans, commonly known as the creeping acmeilla, is a versatile medicinal herb prized for its potent therapeutic properties. Native to tropical regions, this resilient plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering natural bioactive compounds that support healing, wellness, and overall holistic health.
