Plant Parts

This plant, Acmella radicans, commonly known as the toothache plant, contains various bioactive compounds distributed throughout its different structures. The roots serve as the foundational anchor and storage organ, absorbing essential nutrients from the soil to support growth. Meanwhile, the aerial parts, which include the leaves, stems, and flowers, are the most frequently utilized in medicinal applications due to their high concentration of spilanthol, a compound known for its numbing and analgesic properties.
