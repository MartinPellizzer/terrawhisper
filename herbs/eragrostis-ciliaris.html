What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Eragrostis ciliaris has an annual growth habit characterized by slender, creeping stems that produce narrow, linear leaves and small, solitary spikelets. The plant typically reaches a height of 10 to 40 centimeters, often forming dense mats in moist environments. Its leaves are typically flat or slightly channeled with a distinct midrib and fine, hair-like margins. The spikelets are small, containing a single floret enclosed by a lemma that features fine, ciliate hairs along the edges.
