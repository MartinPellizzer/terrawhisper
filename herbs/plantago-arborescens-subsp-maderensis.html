Plantago arborescens subsp. maderensis

Plantago arborescens subsp. maderensis, commonly known as the Madeiran plantain, is a rare endemic species found in the Macaronesian islands. This specialized medicinal plant is valued for its unique phytochemical properties. Historically used in traditional herbal remedies, it offers significant potential for pharmacological research and biodiversity conservation efforts.

  • Scientific name: Plantago arborescens subsp. maderensis
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Plantago as the specific subspecies Plantago arborescens subsp. maderensis.
