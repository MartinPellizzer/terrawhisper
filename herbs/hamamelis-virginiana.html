Witch-hazel, scientifically known as Hamamelis virginiana, is a versatile North American shrub renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Primarily utilized in topical formulations, its astringent bark and distilled hydrosols effectively soothe inflammation, reduce swelling, and treat skin irritations, making it a cornerstone in natural dermatological care and herbalism.

This plant, Hamamelis virginiana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Saxifragales. Ultimately, it is a member of the Hamamelidaceae family within the genus Hamamelis.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as witch hazel, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, most notably the leaf and the Hamamelis virginia na bark. While the leaves are often studied for their astringent qualities, the bark is frequently harvested to extract tannins and other bioactive compounds used in topical treatments. Together, these plant parts contribute to the species' traditional reputation for soothing skin irritations and reducing inflammation.
