Hamamelis virginiana

Hamamelis virginiana, commonly known as witch hazel, is a versatile North American shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its astringent qualities, its bark and distilled hydrosols are widely used to soothe skin irritations, reduce inflammation, and treat minor wounds, making it a staple in natural skincare.

  • Scientific name: Hamamelis virginiana
  • Family: Hamamelidaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Hamamelis virginiana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Saxifragales. Ultimately, it is a member of the Hamamelidaceae family within the genus Hamamelis.

This plant is native to specific regions across North America. In Eastern Canada, its range extends through the provinces of New Brunswick and Nova Scotia.
