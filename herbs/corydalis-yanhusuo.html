Corydalis yanhusuo

Yan hu suo

Corydalis yanhusuo, commonly known as Yan Hu Suo, is a significant medicinal herb in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its potent analgesic properties, the rhizome contains bioactive alkaloids that effectively alleviate pain. It is widely utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from headaches to menstrual cramps and abdominal discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis yanhusuo
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Corydalis.

Plant Parts

This plant, Corydalis yanhuosuo, is primarily valued for its underground storage organs which contain its medicinal properties. The most significant part utilized in traditional medicine is the tuber, often referred to in pharmacological contexts as the dried tuber to ensure stability and potency. While some species in the genus may feature a rhizome, the therapeutic focus for this specific species is centered on the fleshy, bulb-like tubers that develop from the root system. In botanical descriptions, one may find references to the rhizoma or the development of the tuber as the plant matures, but it is the harvested and processed dried tuber that serves as the essential component for medicinal preparations.
