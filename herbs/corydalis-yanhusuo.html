Yan Hu Suo

Corydalis yanhusuo · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (106 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Yan Hu Suo, scientifically known as Corydalis yanhusuo, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potent analgesic properties, its tubers contain bioactive alkaloids used to alleviate pain, soothe menstrual cramps, and treat various neurological discomforts. It remains a cornerstone in herbal pain management therapies.

Plant Parts

This plant, Corydalis yanhuosuo, is primarily valued for its underground storage organs which contain its medicinal properties. The most significant part utilized in traditional medicine is the tuber, often referred to in pharmacological contexts as the dried tuber to ensure stability and potency. While some species in the genus may feature a rhizome, the therapeutic focus for this specific species is centered on the fleshy, bulb-like tubers that develop from the root system. In botanical descriptions, one may find references to the rhizoma or the development of the tuber as the plant matures, but it is the harvested and processed dried tuber that serves as the essential component for medicinal preparations.
