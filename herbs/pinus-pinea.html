Stone Pine, scientifically known as Pinus pinea, is a majestic Mediterranean conifer renowned for its edible pine nuts. Beyond its culinary value, this species offers significant medicinal potential, containing bioactive compounds with antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. It remains a vital resource in traditional herbalism and modern pharmacological research.
