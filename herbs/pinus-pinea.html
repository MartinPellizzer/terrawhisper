Pinus pinea

Pinus pinea, commonly known as the stone pine, is a majestic Mediterranean conifer prized for its edible pine nuts. Beyond its culinary value, this resilient species holds significant medicinal potential, offering antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties that contribute to holistic wellness and traditional therapeutic practices across Southern Europe.

  • Scientific name: Pinus pinea
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. It is classified within the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pinus.
