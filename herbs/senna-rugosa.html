Scientifically known as Senna rugosa, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to North America. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, it contains active sennosides that stimulate bowel movements. Beyond digestion, various indigenous cultures have traditionally utilized its leaves for treating skin ailments, respiratory issues, and managing systemic inflammation.
