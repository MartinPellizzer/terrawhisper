Anacyclus clavatus

Anacyclus clavatus, commonly known as the Hammered Vine, is a rare medicinal plant prized for its unique phytochemical profile. Found primarily in high-altitude tropical regions, it contains potent alkaloids used traditionally to soothe inflammation and support cognitive function. This resilient species continues to inspire modern pharmacological research and herbal studies.
