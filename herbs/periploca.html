Periploca

Periploca, commonly known as the milkvine, is a versatile genus of climbing shrubs celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, these plants are frequently utilized to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. Exploring its botanical properties reveals a fascinating intersection of natural healing and diverse ecological significance.
