Scutellaria racemosa

Scutellaria racemosa, commonly known as American skullcap, is a perennial herb native to North America. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it is frequently utilized for its calming and nervine properties. This plant is primarily used to support neurological health, alleviate anxiety, and promote relaxation within the central nervous system.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria racemosa
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Scutellaria. Specifically, it is identified by the species name Scutellaria racemosa.
