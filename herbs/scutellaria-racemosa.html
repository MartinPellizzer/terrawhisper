, scientifically known as Scutelariia racemosa, is a perennial herb native to North America. Commonly called skullcap, it has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to calm the nervous system. Renowned for its sedative and anxiolytic properties, it helps alleviate anxiety, insomnia, and various stress-related neurological conditions.
