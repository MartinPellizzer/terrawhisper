Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across parts of North America. In Eastern Canada, its presence is noted in the provinces of Nova Scotia, Ontario, and Québec. Plant Parts

This plant, Trillium grandiflorum, is a perennial herb known for its distinctive large white flowers, but its medicinal and biological significance often lies beneath the soil. The most vital part of its structure for long-term survival and propagation is the thick, fleshy rhizome. This underground stem serves as a storage organ for nutrients, allowing the plant to endure dormant periods and provide the energy necessary for its dramatic spring growth.
