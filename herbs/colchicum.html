Men-in-a-Boat, scientifically known as Colchicum, is a genus of flowering plants renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Primarily containing the alkaloid colchicine, it is used clinically to treat acute gout and certain inflammatory conditions. However, extreme caution is required due to its high toxicity and narrow therapeutic window.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a notable presence across various regions of Northern Europe. Specifically, its range includes the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. It is also widely documented throughout Great Britain and Ireland. Moving further north, the species can be found growing within the landscapes of Sweden. Finally, its distribution extends into Middle Europe, where it is present in Austria.
