Plant Parts

This plant, Dendrobium densiflorum, is an epiphytic orchid characterized by its specialized growth structure, particularly its stems. These stems function as pseudobulbs, serving as essential storage organs that hold water and nutrients to help the plant survive fluctuations in moisture availability within its tropical habitat. The stems are typically elongated and fleshy, providing the structural support necessary for the plant to anchor itself to host trees while facilitating the upward growth required to reach optimal light levels.
