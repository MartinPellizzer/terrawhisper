Cuscuta europaea

Cuscuta europaea, commonly known as European dodder, is a parasitic plant characterized by its leafless, thread-like stems. While often viewed as a weed that depletes host nutrients, it holds significant ethnobotanical interest. Researchers explore its unique biochemical properties and secondary metabolites for potential therapeutic applications in traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Cuscuta europaea
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Cuscuta europaea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. It is ultimately classified under the genus Cuscuta.
