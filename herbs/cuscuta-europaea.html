Cuscuta europea, scientifically known as Cuscuta europaea, is a parasitic plant characterized by its leafless, thread-like stems. Lacking chlorophyll, it survives by extracting nutrients from host plants. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it possesses unique bioactive compounds that are currently being researched for their diverse pharmacological and therapeutic potential.
