Rhododendron naamkwanense

Rhododendron naamkwanense, commonly known as the Namaqualand Rhododendron, is a resilient shrub endemic to the arid landscapes of South Africa. Renowned for its striking, vibrant blooms, this medicinal plant offers unique phytochemical properties. It serves as a vital subject for botanical research, exploring its potential therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron naamkwanense
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Rhododendron as the species Rhododendron naamkwanense.
