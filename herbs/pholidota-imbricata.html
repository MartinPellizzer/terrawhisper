What's the taxonomical classification of Pholidota imbricata?

Pholidota imbricata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. This species is further categorized under the order Asparagales and is a member of the family Orchidaceae. Within this family, it is identified by the genus Pholidota, and its specific designation is the species imbricata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pholidota imbricaata has a small, fleshy, coral-like morphology characterized by a cap that is roughly spherical to slightly flattened and covered in dense, overlapping, scale-like structures. These imbricate scales give the fruiting body a textured, shingled appearance that distinguishes it from other members of the genus. The stem is typically short, cylindrical, and lacks a distinct ring, supporting the cap from its base. Its color profile often includes shades of brownish-orange or ochre, which helps it blend into its forest floor environment.
