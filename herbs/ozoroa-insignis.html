Distribution

This plant, known as Ozoroa insignis, is found across several regions within West Tropical Africa. Its natural range includes the country of Benin, where it plays a role in the local ecosystem. It is also distributed throughout Burkina, extending its presence further into the West African landscape. In addition to these areas, the species can be located in Ghana and Gambia. Finally, the geographical reach of this medicinal plant extends to Guinea-Bissau.
