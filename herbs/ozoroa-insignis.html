Ozoroa insignis

Ozoroa insignis, commonly known as the Ozoroa tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions, offering valuable ethnobotanical insights.

  • Scientific name: Ozoroa insignis
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ozoroa.
