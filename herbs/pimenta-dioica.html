Allspice, scientifically known as Pimenta dioica, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Caribbean and Central America. Renowned for its aromatic berries, it possesses potent antimicrobial, analgesic, and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used in traditional remedies, it offers significant therapeutic benefits for digestive health and topical pain relief.
