Black Fruited Galanga, scientifically known as Alpinia nigra, is a perennial herb belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Native to Southeast Asia, this aromatic plant is prized in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. Its rhizomes are widely utilized in culinary preparations and therapeutic herbal formulations.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia. In China, it is primarily found throughout the South-Central provinces. Its presence extends into Eastern Asia, specifically within the Kazan-retto and Ogasawara-shoto areas. Furthermore, the species is well-established across the Indian Subcontinent, including the state of Assam. Finally, it can also be located within the borders of Bangladesh.
