Lowveld bead-string, scientifically known as Alchornea laxiflora, is a resilient shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its delicate, drooping white flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments such as skin infections, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions.
