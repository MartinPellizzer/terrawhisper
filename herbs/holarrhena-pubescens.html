Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it is specifically found within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its range extends into East Tropical Africa, where it inhabits both Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern reaches, the species is also present in South Tropical Africa. Specifically, its documented locations in this region include Malawi and Mozambique.
