Yam (vegetable), scientifically known as Dioscorea hispida, is a climbing perennial vine native to East Asia. Often referred to as the bristly yam, it contains bioactive alkaloids used in traditional medicine. While toxic if consumed raw, its processed extracts offer significant therapeutic potential for treating inflammation and pain.
