Ashoka tree, scientifically known as Saraca asoca, is a sacred evergreen species revered in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its vibrant orange blossoms, it holds profound therapeutic significance, particularly in women's healthcare. This potent medicinal plant offers diverse healing properties, offering natural remedies for various hormonal and reproductive wellness needs.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing naturally throughout various parts of India, including the state of Assam. The species also has a significant presence in Bangladesh and throughout the East Himalaya region. Additionally, its range extends to the remote areas of the Laccadive Islands. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in multiple subtropical environments.
