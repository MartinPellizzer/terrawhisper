Impatiens tienchuanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Impatiens tienchuanensis?

Impatiens tienchuankenis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales. The plant is a member of the family Balsaminaceae, falling under the genus Impatiens, and is specifically identified by the species epithet tienchuankenis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Impatiens tienchuanensis has succulent, lanceolate leaves with serrated margins and small, zygomorphic flowers that typically feature orange or reddish petals with yellow markings. The stem is fleshy and herbaceous, often exhibiting a translucent or slightly reddish hue depending on the light exposure. Its inflorescence is characterized by a loose arrangement of blooms that emerge from the leaf axils. The plant develops specialized, explosive seed capsules that are a hallmark of the Impatiens genus. Its distribution is limited to these specific subtropical habitats where humidity remains consistently high.
