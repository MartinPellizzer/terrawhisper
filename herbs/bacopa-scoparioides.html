Bacopa scoparioides

Bacopa scoparioides, commonly known as water hyssop, is a perennial herb native to the moist, tropical regions of Asia. Belonging to the Plantaginaceae family, this aquatic plant is valued in traditional medicine for its restorative properties, often utilized to support cognitive health, reduce stress, and promote overall vitality.

  • Scientific name: Bacopa scoparioides
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae. It is further categorized into the genus Bacopa, specifically identified as Bacopa scoparioides.
