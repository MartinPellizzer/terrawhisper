Chinese Ginger

Boesenbergia rotunda · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (162 studies) · ★★★★☆

Chinese Ginger, scientifically known as Boesenbergia rotunda, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Widely used in traditional Southeast Asian medicine, its rhizomes possess potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. This aromatic herb is highly valued for supporting digestive health and managing various inflammatory conditions.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Round-leaf Ginger, utilizes various structures for its growth and medicinal properties. The most significant part for pharmacological use is the rhizomes, which are thick, aromatic underground stems. Each individual rhizome serves as a storage organ for nutrients, often appearing in clusters where one primary Rhizome may branch out into several smaller ones. Above the ground, the plant features a leafy stalk that supports the foliage, while the structural connection between the leaves and the underground stem is formed by the shoot base.
