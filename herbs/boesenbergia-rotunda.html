Boesenbergia rotunda

Boesenbergia rotunda, commonly known as Round Alpinia, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Native to Southeast Asia, this perennial herb is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds, offering significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties used to treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Boesenbergia rotunda
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Boesenbergia rotunda, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Zingiberales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Zingiberaceae and the genus Boesenbergia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Boesenbergia rotunda, is recognized by various regional names across different languages. In Malaysia, it is commonly referred to as Pokok Temu Kunci, while in Indonesia, it is known as Temu kunci, a name also used in the Dutch language (nl) as Temu kunci. In Javanese (jv), it is called Temu kunci, and in Sundanese (su), it is known simply as Kunci. Additionally, in German (de), it is referred to as Chinesischer Ingwer.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is specifically located in the South-Central areas. Its presence extends significantly into the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be found in India and Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is documented to grow in the state of Assam. Finally, its range reaches further south into the nation of Sri Lanka.
