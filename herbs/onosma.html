Onosma

Onosma, commonly known as stonecrop or gromwell, is a distinctive genus of flowering plants within the Boraginaceae family. Thriving in rocky, Mediterranean landscapes, these resilient perennials are celebrated for their striking, bell-shaped blossoms. Beyond their ornamental beauty, various species hold significant historical importance in traditional herbal medicine practices.

  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe
