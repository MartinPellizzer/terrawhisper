Chaiturus marrubiastrum

Chaiturus marrubiastrum, commonly known as White Horehound, is a potent medicinal herb revered for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its bitter essence, it serves as a powerful expectorant and digestive aid. This versatile plant is widely utilized in traditional herbalism to alleviate respiratory congestion and support overall metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Chaiturus marrubiastrum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
