Fordia cauliflora

Fordia cauliflora, commonly known as the Cauliflora, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to the tropical regions of Brazil. Renowned for its unique stem-flowering habit, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Its various parts are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Fordia cauliflora
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Fordia cauliflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is placed within the genus Fordia.
