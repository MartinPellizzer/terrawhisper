Fragrant rosewood, scientifically known as Dalbergia odorifera, is a precious medicinal and aromatic timber tree native to East Asia. Renowned for its exquisite scent and deep red wood, it holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its extracts are utilized for their calming, antiseptic, and anti-inflammatory therapeutic properties.

Plant Parts

This plant, Dalbergia odorifera, is highly valued in traditional medicine and perfumery for its diverse therapeutic components found throughout its anatomy. The most prized part is the dense, fragrant heartwood, which contains a significant concentration of aromatic volatile oil essential for its medicinal properties. Within its structure, the durable lignum provides a robust foundation, while the botanical pod serves as the vessel for seed dispersal. Additionally, the leaf can be utilized in various preparations, contributing to the overall medicinal profile of this species.
