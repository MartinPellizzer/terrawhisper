Dalbergia odorifera

Dalbergia odorifera, commonly known as rosewood, is a prized medicinal plant celebrated for its aromatic properties. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities. Beyond its therapeutic applications, this species is highly valued for its fragrant, durable timber, making it both biologically and economically remarkable.

  • Scientific name: Dalbergia odorifera
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Dalbergia.

Common Names

This plant, Dalbergia odorifera, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Gan, it is referred to as 黃花梨木, while in Cantonese (yue), it is commonly called 海南黃花梨. Across several Mandarin-based scripts, including Simplified Chinese (zh-cn), Traditional Chinese (zh-tw), and standard Mandarin (zh), it is identified as 降香黄檀.

Distribution

This plant, known as Dalbergia odorifera, exhibits a specific range within its natural habitat. Its geographical presence is notably concentrated in the southeastern regions of China. Within this broader area, it can be found growing across China Southeast. Furthermore, the species is specifically documented to inhabit the island of Hainan. Plant Parts

This plant, Dalbergia odorifera, is highly valued in traditional medicine and perfumery for its diverse therapeutic components found throughout its anatomy. The most prized part is the dense, fragrant heartwood, which contains a significant concentration of aromatic volatile oil essential for its medicinal properties. Within its structure, the durable lignum provides a robust foundation, while the botanical pod serves as the vessel for seed dispersal. Additionally, the leaf can be utilized in various preparations, contributing to the overall medicinal profile of this species.
