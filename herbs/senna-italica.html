Senna italica

Sennas italica, commonly known as Italian Senna, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized for its potent laxative properties. Rich in anthraquinone glycosides, it effectively stimulates bowel movements to treat constipation. Widely utilized in traditional herbal medicine, this plant offers a natural approach to managing digestive health and regularity.

  • Scientific name: Senna italica
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its classification within the genus Senna.
