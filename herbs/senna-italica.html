Cassia obovatica, scientifically known as Senna italicica, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, this plant contains bioactive compounds that stimulate digestive health. Beyond gastrointestinal use, it is studied for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits, making it a vital botanical resource.

Plant Parts

This plant, Senna italica, possesses several components used in traditional medicine, most notably its foliage. The medicinal properties are primarily concentrated within the green leaves, which are harvested to create various herbal preparations. Each individual leaf is typically lanceolate in shape, growing in an opposite arrangement along the slender, woody branches. These branches serve as the structural framework that supports the dense growth of the foliage, allowing for the effective collection of the plant's bioactive elements.
