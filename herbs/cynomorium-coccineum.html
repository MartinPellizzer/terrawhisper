Cynomorium coccineum

Red thumb

Cynomorium coccineum, commonly known as the coral coral or desert cynomorium, is a unique holoparasitic plant found in arid regions. Lacking chlorophyll, it derives nutrients from host roots. Traditionally valued in various medicinal systems, it is utilized for its purported rejuvenating properties and its potential to treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Cynomorium coccineum
  • Family: Cynomoriaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Saxifrages and falls under the family Cynomoriaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Cynomorium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the Mediterranean region of Southwestern Europe. Its presence is documented across several key island territories, including the Baleares, Corse, and Sardegna. Beyond these islands, it can also be found on the mainland portions of the Iberian Peninsula. Specifically, its range extends through the countries of Portugal and Spain. Consequently, the geographical footprint of Cynomorium coccineum is defined by these distinct coastal and island habitats.
