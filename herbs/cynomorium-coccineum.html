Red thumb

Cynomorium coccineum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (27 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Red thumb, scientifically known as Cynomorium coccineum, is a unique holoparasitic plant lacking chlorophyll. Found in arid regions, it emerges from the soil as a fleshy, crimson spike. Highly valued in traditional medicine, it is used to treat various ailments, including anemia, inflammation, and reproductive health issues throughout Asia.
