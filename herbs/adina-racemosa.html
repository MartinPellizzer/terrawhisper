Adina racemosa

Adina racemosa, commonly known as False Sandpaper Tree, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to the moist forests of South Asia. Renowned for its rough, sandpaper-like leaves, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions across diverse cultures.

  • Scientific name: Adina racemosa
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Adina racemosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rubiaceae and is situated within the genus Adina.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions of East Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted in the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species also extends its reach into Japan, where it can be found growing naturally. Additionally, the distribution includes the Nansei-shoto islands within the Eastern Asian region.
