Polygala sibirica

Polygala sibirioca, commonly known as Siberian Milkwort, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its adaptogenic and neuroprotective effects, this plant is frequently utilized to enhance cognitive function, support respiratory health, and alleviate inflammation, offering significant potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Polygala sibirica
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Polygala sibirica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Polygalaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Polygala.

Common Names

This plant, known by various names across different cultures and languages, holds significant medicinal importance. In Russian, it is called Истод сибирский, while in Azerbaijani, it is referred to as Sibir südotusu. Its presence in East Asian tradition is reflected in the Korean name 두메애기풀 and the Chinese name 西伯利亚远志. Additionally, in Bulgarian, the species is known as сибирска телчарка.
