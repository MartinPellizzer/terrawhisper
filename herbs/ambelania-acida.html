Ambelania acida

Ambelaenia acida, commonly known as the Acidic Ambarella, is a versatile tropical plant valued for its medicinal and nutritional properties. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, its fruits and leaves are utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, skin ailments, and inflammation, offering significant therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ambelania acida
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Ambelania.
