Himatanthus bracteatus

Plumeria bracteata

Himatanthus bracteatus, commonly known as the "Manihot" or "Apocynaceae" species, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it contains bioactive alkaloids used traditionally to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various infections, offering vast potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Himatanthus bracteatus
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Himatanthus, specifically as the species Himatanthus bracteatus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the tropical regions of South America. In Northern South America, its presence is documented in French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname. It also extends its range into the country of Venezuela within the same northern sector. Furthermore, the distribution reaches into Western South America, specifically within Ecuador. Consequently, these specific regions define the known geographical footprint of Himatanthus bracteatus.
