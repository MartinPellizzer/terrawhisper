Distribution

This plant is primarily found across the tropical regions of South America. In Northern South America, its presence is documented in French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname. It also extends its range into the country of Venezuela within the same northern sector. Furthermore, the distribution reaches into Western South America, specifically within Ecuador. Consequently, these specific regions define the known geographical footprint of Himatanthus bracteatus.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Devil's's Trumpet or Sandpaper Tree, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, specifically focusing on the protective outer layers of its structure. The stem barks are frequently harvested for their bioactive compounds, which are often processed to create traditional remedies. Furthermore, the bark itself contains a milky latex that is characteristic of the species and is studied for its various pharmacological potential in treating different ailments.
