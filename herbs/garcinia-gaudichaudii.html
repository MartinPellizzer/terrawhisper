Garcinia gaudichaudii

Garcinia gaudichaudii, commonly known as "Garcinia" or "Mangosteen relative," is a medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its rich bioactive compounds, it possesses significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Traditionally used in folk medicine, this species offers promising potential for developing modern therapeutic treatments and natural supplements.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia gaudichaudii
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it falls into the family Clusiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Garcinia.
