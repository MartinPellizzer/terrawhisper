Crotalaria paulina

Crotalaria paulina, commonly known as the rattlepod, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments. This species serves as a vital botanical resource, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Crotalaria paulina
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Crotalaria.

Common Names

This plant, Crotalaria paulina, is known by various regional names depending on the local language. In English, it is commonly referred to as rattlepod. In Spanish, it is known as ratalia or ratalia de monte, while in Portuguese, it is called ratalia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across multiple continents. In the African region, it can be found in Ethiopia within Northeast Tropical Africa and in Kenya throughout East Tropical Africa. Its presence also extends to the southern part of the continent in Zimbabwe. Moving to the Americas, the species is located in Venezuela within Northern South America. Finally, its range includes the western reaches of South America in Colombia.
