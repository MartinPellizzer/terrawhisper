Pfaffia

Pfaffia, commonly known as Brazilian Ginseng, is a powerful adaptogenic herb native to South America. Renowned for its ability to boost energy, balance hormones, and enhance vitality, this medicinal plant supports the body's resilience against stress. It remains a vital staple in traditional herbal medicine for promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Pfaffia
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Pfaffia.

Common Names

This plant is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as Brazilian ginseng, while in Portuguese, it is commonly called ginseng brasileiro. In Spanish, the plant is known as ginseng brasileño, and in French, it is called ginseng brésilien.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical presence across several distinct tropical regions. In Mexico, its distribution spans from the Southwest to the Southeast portions of the country. Moving through Central America, it can be found growing in Costa Rica. The plant also extends into the Caribbean, where it is native to Haiti. Finally, its range reaches the Leeward Islands within the same Caribbean archipelago.
