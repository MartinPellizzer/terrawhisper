Sarcandra glabra

Sarcandra glabra, commonly known as the "Evergreen Sarcandra," is a medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves are utilized for their potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Researchers increasingly study its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in treating various systemic inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Sarcandra glabra
  • Family: Chloranthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Chloranthales and the family Chloranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Sarcandra.
