Scutellaria pinnatifida, scientifically known as Scutellaria pinnatifida, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Known as Ban Lan Gen, it is celebrated for its potent anti-inflammatory, antiviral, and antibacterial properties. Often used to treat fevers and respiratory infections, this plant remains a cornerstone of herbal therapy.
