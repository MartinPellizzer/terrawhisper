Ruta corsica

Ruta corsica, commonly known as Corsican Rue, is a distinctive aromatic herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its intense fragrance and medicinal properties, it has traditionally been used in herbal remedies to support digestive health and circulation. This resilient plant remains a valued botanical treasure in traditional European pharmacopoeia.

  • Scientific name: Ruta corsica
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Sapindales, and the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Ruta.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ruta corsiica, is recognized by various regional names depending on the language. In Arabic, it is referred to as سذاب كورسيكي or سذاب كورسيكى, while in Russian, its name is written as Рута корсиканская. Additionally, in French, this medicinal species is commonly called Rue de Corse.

Distribution

This plant is specifically native to certain regions within Southwestern Europe. Its geographical range is quite limited, as it is primarily found in the Mediterranean area. Specifically, one of its key habitats is located on the island of Corse. Additionally, the species can be found growing in the neighboring region of Sardegna. Therefore, its natural distribution is closely tied to these specific Mediterranean islands.
