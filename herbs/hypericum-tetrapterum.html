Hypericum tetrapterum

Hypericum tetrapterum, commonly known as Four-winged St. John's-wort, is a distinctive perennial herb native to wetlands and marshy habitats. Characterized by its unique winged stems and bright yellow flowers, this plant belongs to the Hypericaceae family and is studied for its diverse phytochemical properties and traditional medicinal uses.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum tetrapterum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hypericum, specifically as the species Hypericum tetrapterum.
