four-winged St. John's-wort, scientifically known as Hypericum tetrapterum, is a distinctive perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Characterized by its unique four-winged seed capsules, this plant belongs to the Hypericaceae family. Often found in moist habitats, it shares traditional medicinal properties with its well-known relative, Hypericum perforatum.
