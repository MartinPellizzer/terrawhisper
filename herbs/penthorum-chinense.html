Penthorum chinense

Penthorum chinense, commonly known as Chinese Penthorum, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to East Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and antioxidant properties. Often utilized to treat skin ailments and internal inflammation, this versatile botanical offers promising therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Penthorum chinense
  • Family: Penthoraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifrages, it is uniquely situated in the family Penthoraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Penthorum.

Common Names

This plant is known by various common names depending on the language and region. In Chinese, it is referred to as 扯根菜, 水杨柳, and specifically in mainland China and Taiwan as 扯根菜 (zh-cn, zh-tw). In Vietnamese, the species is known as Xả căn.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. Within the Russian Far East, it can be found specifically in the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Its range extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the Inner Mongolia region. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout Manchuria. Finally, its presence is also noted in the South-Central areas of China.
