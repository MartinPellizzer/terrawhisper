Melia dubia

Melia dubia, commonly known as Chinaberry or Malabar Neem, is a fast-growing deciduous tree prized for its versatile medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This potent plant offers significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal pharmacology and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Melia dubia
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Melia.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing throughout the diverse landscapes of India and its neighboring nation, Nepal. Specifically, its presence is well-documented in the state of Assam. Additionally, the species extends its reach to the island nation of Sri Lanka. Beyond the subcontinent, it is also located within the Indo-China region, specifically in Myanmar.
