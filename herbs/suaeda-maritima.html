Annual sea blite, scientifically known as Suaeda maritima, is a succulent halophyte commonly found in coastal salt marshes. Renowned for its salt-tolerant nature, this plant plays a vital role in saline ecosystems. Historically used for culinary and medicinal purposes, it offers unique nutritional benefits and antioxidant properties.
