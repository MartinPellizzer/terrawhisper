Suaeda maritima

Annual seablite

Suaeda maritima, commonly known as sea blite, is a resilient succulent herb native to coastal salt marshes. Thriving in saline environments, this plant is rich in essential minerals and vitamins. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it offers potential therapeutic benefits, particularly for managing inflammation and supporting overall metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Suaeda maritima
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Caryophyllales and belongs to the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Suaeda.
