Plant Parts

This plant, Cistanche sinensis, is a unique holoparasitic species that lacks chlorophyll and does not perform photosynthesis. Because it does not need to capture sunlight, it lacks traditional green leaves and instead relies on its specialized fleshy stems for survival. These succulent, thickened stems serve as the primary structure for nutrient and water absorption, which the plant extracts from the roots of its host plants. These fleshy stems are also the part most commonly harvested for medicinal use, as they store the bioactive compounds used in traditional therapies.
