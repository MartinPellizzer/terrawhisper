Chamaeleon gummifer

Chamaeleon gummifer, commonly known as the Chameleon plant, is a remarkable medicinal herb prized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its ability to support holistic wellness, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional remedies to promote healing, balance bodily functions, and enhance overall vitality through its potent compounds.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is specifically categorized under the genus Chamaeleon.
