Koenigia polystachya

Koenigia polysachya, commonly known as "Kowai" or "Indian Climbing Knotweed," is a versatile medicinal herb found across tropical Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This climbing plant serves as a vital resource in various herbal formulations.

  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe
