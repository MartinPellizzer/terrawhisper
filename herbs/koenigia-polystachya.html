Himalayan knotweed, scientifically known as Koenigia polystachya, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. This versatile botanical is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, wounds, and various gastrointestinal disorders naturally.
