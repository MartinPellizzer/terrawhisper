Iris spuria

Iris spuria, commonly known as the Spurious Iris, is a resilient perennial herb celebrated for its striking, colorful blooms. Native to various temperate regions, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Its rhizomes are traditionally utilized in folk medicine for their diverse therapeutic properties, ranging from skin treatments to digestive support.

  • Scientific name: Iris spuria
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asparagales and the family Iridaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Iris.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Iris spuria, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In German, it is called Bastaard-Schwertlilie, while in Vietnamese, it is known as Diên vĩ hoa tím. It is identified as زنبق نمکزar in Persian and as korcs nőszirom in Hungarian. Additionally, in Upper Sorbian, the species is referred to as Přeměnita škleńčica.
