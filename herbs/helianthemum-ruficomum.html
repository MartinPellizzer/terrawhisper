Helianthemum ruficomum

Helianthemum ruficomum, commonly known as the rusty-haired rock rose, is a resilient perennial herb native to Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its delicate yellow blooms and distinctive hairy foliage, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its antioxidant properties and potential anti-inflammatory benefits within diverse medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Helianthemum ruficomum
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Malvales, and the family Cistaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Helianthemum, specifically the species Helianthemum ruficomum.

Common Names

This plant, Helianthemum ruficomum, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the red-haired rockrose. Moving into German, it is identified as der Rotschopf-Sonnenröschen, while in French, it is known as le hélianthème à poils roux. In Spanish, the species is called el heliantemo de pelos rojos, and in Italian, it is referred to as l'eliantemo dai peli rossi.
