Pointed gourd, scientifically known as Trichosanthes dioica, is a versatile medicinal vegetable widely used in Ayurveda. Renowned for its cooling properties, it aids digestion, manages diabetes, and boosts immunity. Rich in essential vitamins and antioxidants, this nutrient-dense plant plays a vital role in holistic wellness and traditional healing.
