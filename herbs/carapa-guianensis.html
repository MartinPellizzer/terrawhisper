Plant Parts

This plant, Carapa guianensis, is highly valued for its diverse medicinal and industrial applications derived from its various anatomical components. The most significant part is the seeds, which are encased within a hard, woody fruit. From these seeds, a rich and potent seed oil is extracted, often referred to simply as the oil of the tree, which is widely used for its anti-inflammatory and skin-healing properties. Whether one refers to the substance as seeds oil or simply the extracted liquid, its value remains immense in traditional medicine. Additionally, the plant produces a delicate flower that plays a vital role in the reproductive cycle, ensuring the continued production of the medicinal kernels.
