Sclerocroton cornutus

Sclerocroton cornutus, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, holds significant ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. Its unique chemical profile offers promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Sclerocroton cornutus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
