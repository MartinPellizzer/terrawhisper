Parthenocissus quinquefolia

Virginia creeper

Parthenocissus quinquefolia, commonly known as Virginia creeper, is a versatile climbing vine native to North America. While primarily recognized for its ornamental beauty and striking seasonal foliage, this plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism. Its various compounds offer intriguing possibilities for research into diverse medicinal and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Parthenocissus quinquefolia
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Europe
