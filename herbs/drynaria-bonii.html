Drynaria bonii

Drynaria bonii, commonly known as the basket fern, is a distinctive epiphytic plant celebrated for its unique rhizome structure. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in herbal medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Drynaria bonii
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae. Within the order Polypodiales and family Polypodiaceae, it is classified under the genus Drynaria. Specifically, Drynaria bonii is a member of this unique botanical lineage.
