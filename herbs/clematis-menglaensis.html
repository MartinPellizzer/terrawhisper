Clematis menglaensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Clematis menglaensis?

Clematis menglaensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Ranunculales, it falls under the family Ranunculaceae. The plant is further categorized into the genus Clematis, specifically identified by its unique species designation, menglaensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Clematis menglaensis has opposite, petiolate leaves that are typically trifoliolate with ovate to lanceolate leaflets that feature entire or slightly serrated margins. The plant exhibits slender, climbing stems that utilize petioles to scramble over surrounding vegetation. Its flowers are characterized by distinct tepals that often present in shades of pale yellow or cream. The reproductive structures include a central cluster of numerous stamens that surround the carpels. Small, hairy styles are present on the developing achenes after fertilization occurs.
