Osbeckia aspera

Osbeckia aspera, commonly known as the rough osbeckia, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This plant holds significant botanical importance for its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Osbeckia aspera
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
