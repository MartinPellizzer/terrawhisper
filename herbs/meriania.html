Meriania

Meriania, commonly known as a versatile medicinal herb, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to address various ailments, ranging from inflammation to digestive issues. Exploring its botanical properties reveals profound potential for modern pharmacological developments and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Meriania
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales. Finally, it resides in the family Melastomataceae under the genus Meriania.

Common Names

This plant belongs to a genus known by several common names depending on the language. In Czech, it is referred to as merianie, while in Chinese, it is identified as 元丹花属 (zh), 元丹花属 (zh-cn), or 元丹花屬 (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across North America, Central America, and the Caribbean. In Mexico, its presence is specifically noted within the southeastern region of the country. Moving through Central America, it can be found growing in Guatemala, Costa Rica, and Panamá. Additionally, the species extends its range into the Caribbean islands, specifically inhabiting Cuba. Together, these locations define the diverse tropical habitats where the plant thrives.
