Spatholobus suberectus

Spatholobus suberectus, commonly known as Dan Shen in certain regional contexts, is a vital medicinal plant prized in traditional pharmacology. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is primarily utilized to promote blood circulation, alleviate pain, and treat various cardiovascular ailments, serving as a cornerstone in many herbal formulations.

  • Scientific name: Spatholobus suberectus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the Fabaceae family, falling under the genus Spatholobus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Assam and the East Himalaya. Additionally, the species is present within the Indo-China region, specifically in Laos. Together, these areas define the diverse geographical footprint of Spatholobus suberectus.

Plant Parts

This plant, Spatholobus suberectus, is a climbing woody species characterized by its distinct morphology. The medicinal properties of the plant are often derived from various structural components, most notably the vine stem which facilitates its climbing habit. The overall structure consists of strong, fibrous stems that support its growth through forest canopies. In many traditional applications, the roots are utilized for their bioactive compounds, while the tough, durable rattans found within the plant structure are characteristic of its climbing nature.
