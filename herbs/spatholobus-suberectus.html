Spatholobus suberectus, scientifically known as Spatholobus suberectus, is a vital medicinal climbing vine primarily utilized in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its blood-invigorating properties, the plant's stems are harvested to treat ailments related to poor circulation, menstrual irregularities, and abdominal pain, offering significant therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in Assam and the East Himalaya. Additionally, the species is present within the Indo-China region, specifically in Laos. Plant Parts

This plant, Spatholobus suberectus, is a climbing woody species characterized by its distinct morphology. The medicinal properties of the plant are often derived from various structural components, most notably the vine stem which facilitates its climbing habit. The overall structure consists of strong, fibrous stems that support its growth through forest canopies. In many traditional applications, the roots are utilized for their bioactive compounds, while the tough, durable rattans found within the plant structure are characteristic of its climbing nature. Additionally, the green, functional leaves contribute to the plant's overall vitality and photosynthetic processes.
