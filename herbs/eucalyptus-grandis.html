Flooded Gum, scientifically known as Eucalyptus grandis, is a fast-growing evergreen tree native to Australia. Renowned for its impressive height and robust timber, it holds significant medicinal value. Its essential oils possess potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and decongestant properties, making it a vital resource in traditional and modern pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, Eucalyptus grandis, offers various components that are utilized for their distinct chemical and structural properties. The leaves are the most prominent part used in medicinal applications, as they are rich in essential oils like cineole, which possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory qualities. Interestingly, the plant exhibits heterophylly, meaning the juvenile leaves differ significantly in shape and chemical composition from the adult foliage, a distinction that is crucial for determining the correct harvest for specific therapeutic uses. Additionally, the wood of the species is valued not only for its structural strength but also for its secondary metabolites, which can contribute to the overall medicinal profile of the tree.
