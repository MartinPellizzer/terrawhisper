Flooded Gum, scientifically known as Eucalyptus grandis, is a fast-growing evergreen tree native to Australia. Renowned for its impressive height and robust timber, it holds significant medicinal value. Its essential oils possess potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and decongestant properties, making it a vital resource in traditional and modern pharmacology.
