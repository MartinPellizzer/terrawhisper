Cardoon, scientifically known as Cynara cardunculus, is a versatile perennial thistle prized for its medicinal and culinary properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like cynarin, it is widely utilized to support liver health, aid digestion, and manage cholesterol levels, making it a significant botanical resource in traditional herbal medicine.
