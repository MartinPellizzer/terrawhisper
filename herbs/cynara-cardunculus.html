Cardoon, scientifically known as Cynara cardunculus, is a versatile perennial thistle prized for its medicinal and culinary properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like cynarin, it is widely utilized to support liver health, aid digestion, and manage cholesterol levels, making it a significant botanical resource in traditional herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the artichoke or cardoon, is a member of the thistle family, and while most of its medicinal value is traditionally derived from the leaves and flower heads, its botanical structure also includes the fruit. The fruit of Cynara cardunculus is a small, dry, one-seeded structure known as an achene, which is produced after the flowering stage. These tiny fruits are typically dispersed by the wind, playing a vital role in the plant's reproductive cycle and the spread of the species across its natural habitats.
