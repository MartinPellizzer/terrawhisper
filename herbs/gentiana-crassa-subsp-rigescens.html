Gentiana crassa subsp. rigescens

Gentiana crassa subsp. rigescens, commonly known as the thick-stemmed gentian, is a resilient perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its bitter roots contain potent bitter principles. These compounds are widely utilized to stimulate digestion, support liver function, and alleviate various gastrointestinal ailments effectively.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana crassa subsp. rigescens
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales. Finally, it is a member of the family Gentianaceae and falls under the genus Gentiana.

Common Names

This plant, Gentiana crassa subsp. rigescens, is known by several different names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the thick-leaved gentian, while in German, it is known as der Dickblättrige Enzian. In Spanish, the species is called la genciana de hojas gruesas, and in French, it is identified as la gentiane à feuilles épaisses.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is primarily concentrated in the South-Central regions. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, the species can also be found in Myanmar. Additionally, it occupies habitats within the territory of Vietnam.
