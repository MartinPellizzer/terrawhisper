, scientifically known as Gentiana crassa subsp. rigescens, is a specialized medicinal perennial native to high-altitude alpine regions. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is primarily concentrated in the South-Central regions. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, the species can also be found in Myanmar. Additionally, it occupies habitats within the territory of Vietnam. These locations highlight the plant's range across diverse mountainous or subtropical landscapes.
