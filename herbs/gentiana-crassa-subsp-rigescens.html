Plant Parts

This plant, Gentiana crassa subsp. rigescens, is characterized by its distinct morphological structure where the medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in the underground portions. The most significant part used for therapeutic purposes is the thick, fleshy roots, which are harvested and processed to extract active compounds. Each individual root serves as a vital storage organ for the plant, providing the necessary nutrients to support the development of the aerial structures. Above the soil, the plant develops sturdy stems that support the foliage and flowers, forming the main framework of the shoots that extend toward the sunlight. In botanical studies and pharmacological testing, specific root samples are carefully collected to ensure the quality and potency of the medicinal extracts derived from this species.
