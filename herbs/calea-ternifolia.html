Calea ternifolia, scientifically known as Calea ternifolia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments, fever, and inflammation. This plant holds significant pharmacological interest for its potential therapeutic properties.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Karuvela or Karuka, possesses various therapeutic properties that are primarily harnessed through its botanical components. Among its many medicinal applications, the leaf extracts are particularly significant, as they are frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as inflammation, skin disorders, and digestive issues. These extracts contain bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's overall pharmacological profile, making the leaves a vital part of its healing potential.
