Calea ternifolia

Calea ternifolia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (51 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Calea ternifolia, scientifically known as Calea ternifolia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments, fever, and inflammation. This plant holds significant pharmacological interest for its potential therapeutic properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across various regions of Mexico. It can be found growing throughout Mexico Central, covering a significant portion of the country's heartland. The species also extends into Mexico Northeast and Mexico Gulf, adapting to the diverse climates found there. Furthermore, its distribution continues into the Mexico Southwest area. Finally, the plant is documented to thrive within the Mexico Southeast region as well.
