Terminalia schimperiana

Terminalia schimperiana, commonly known as the Schimper's Terminalia, is a resilient deciduous tree native to the woodlands of East Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional healing to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical value.

  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa
