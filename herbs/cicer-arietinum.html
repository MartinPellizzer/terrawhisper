Cicer arietinum

Bengal gram

Cicer arietinum, commonly known as the chickpea or garbanzo bean, is a versatile legume prized globally. Beyond its nutritional density, it possesses significant medicinal properties, including antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-diabetic effects. Traditionally used to support digestive health and manage blood sugar, it remains a vital functional food.

  • Scientific name: Cicer arietinum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Cicer.
