Bengal gram

Cicer arietinum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (754 studies) · ★★★★★

Bengal gram, scientifically known as Cicer arietinum, is a vital legume celebrated for both its nutritional density and therapeutic potential. Rich in protein, fiber, and essential minerals, it serves as a functional food. Traditionally, it is utilized in holistic medicine to manage blood sugar and support digestive health.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cicer arietinum, commonly known as the chickpea, utilizes various morphological structures that contribute to its biological development and potential medicinal value. The most prominent part is the seeds, which serve as the primary storage organ for nutrients and are the part most frequently consumed and studied for their nutritional properties. Within each seed, the seed embryonic axes act as the critical structural foundation, containing the fundamental tissues that will eventually differentiate into the root and shoot systems during germination. Additionally, the leaves function as the primary site for photosynthesis, capturing solar energy to drive the plant's growth and the synthesis of various bioactive compounds.
