What's the taxonomical classification of Calamus macrorhynchus?

Calamus macrorhynchus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Arecales, it falls under the family Arecaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Calamus and is specifically distinguished as the species macrorhynchus.
