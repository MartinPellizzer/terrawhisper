Nephrolepis cordifolia

Nephrolepis cordifolia, commonly known as the sword fern, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is frequently used to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive disturbances. This resilient fern offers a natural approach to wellness through its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Nephrolepis cordifolia
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, falling under the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Nephrolepis.
