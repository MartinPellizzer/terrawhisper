Herpetospermum pedunculosum

Herpetospermum pedunculosum, commonly known as the creeping herb or climbing plant, is a unique medicinal species valued in traditional practices. Characterized by its climbing habit and distinct foliage, it offers significant ethnobotanical interest. Exploring its bioactive properties reveals potential therapeutic applications, making it a vital subject for pharmacological study.

  • Scientific name: Herpetospermum pedunculosum
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Herpetospermum pedunculosum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes within the genus Herpetospermum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across parts of East and South Asia. In China, it can be found growing within the South-Central regions as well as in Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is specifically documented in Assam. Furthermore, the species inhabits the rugged landscapes of the East Himalaya. Finally, its range includes the mountainous terrain of Nepal.
