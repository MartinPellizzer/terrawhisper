Vanda coerulea

Blue vanda

Vanda coerulea, commonly known as the Blue Vanda, is a stunning tropical orchid prized for its striking azure blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant traditional value in various cultures, where its unique properties are explored for medicinal purposes, highlighting the profound connection between nature and healing.

  • Scientific name: Vanda coerulea
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Vanda coerulea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asparagales, it is a member of the Orchidaceae family. It is further categorized under the genus Vanda.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions in Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the South-Central area. The Indian Subcontinent hosts various populations, ranging from the state of Assam to the regions of Bangladesh and the East Himalaya. Additionally, its range extends into the Indo-China peninsula, specifically within Laos. Together, these locations define the diverse geographical footprint of Vanda coerulea.
