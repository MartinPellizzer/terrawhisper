Meconopsis simplicifolia

Meconopsis simplicifolia, commonly known as the Himalayan poppy, is a striking perennial native to high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its brilliant yellow blooms, this plant holds significant botanical interest. While primarily admired for its aesthetic beauty, it remains a subject of study within traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Meconopsis simplicifolia
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Meconopsis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several high-altitude regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the mountainous areas of Tibet. Its presence also extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the East Himalaya range. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow throughout the country of Nepal. These locations highlight the plant's preference for the unique climates found in these Himalayan territories.
