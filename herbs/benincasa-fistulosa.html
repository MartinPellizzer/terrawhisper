Round gourd, scientifically known as Benincasa fistulosa, is a versatile creeping vine highly valued in traditional medicine and culinary practices. Often referred to as winter melon, this plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, cooling, and diuretic properties, making it a vital resource for holistic health and wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Benincasa fistulosa and commonly referred to as wax gourd or winter melon, possesses various components used in traditional medicine, though its fruit sap is particularly notable. The fruit itself is a large, fleshy pepo that serves as the primary source of nutritional and therapeutic value, but the sap extracted from the plant's tissues is also recognized for its specific properties. In various herbal practices, the sap and the juice derived from the fruit are utilized for their cooling and hydrating effects, often helping to alleviate inflammation or soothe internal heat within the body.
