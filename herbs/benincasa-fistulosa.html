Benincasa fistulosa

Benincasa fistulosa, commonly known as wax gourd or winter melon, is a versatile medicinal plant widely used in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, the gourd is utilized to treat digestive issues, reduce fever, and alleviate inflammation, offering significant nutritional value and therapeutic benefits in holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Benincasa fistulosa
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Benincasa, with its specific species being fistulosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several continents and climatic zones. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is notably documented in Ghana. Its distribution extends significantly into East Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda. Furthermore, the species reaches into Western Asia, specifically within the borders of Afghanistan. Consequently, the plant thrives in various ecological settings from the African tropics to the arid regions of Asia.
