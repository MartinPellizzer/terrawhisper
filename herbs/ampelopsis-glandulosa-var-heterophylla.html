This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Vitales and the family Vitaeaceae. Specifically, this variety is identified by the genus Ampelopsis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range spanning across several distinct regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is noted throughout the Kuril Islands, Sakhalin, and the Primorye region. Moving southward, the species is also found within the Manchuria region of China. Furthermore, its distribution extends into the South-Central areas of China. Together, these locations highlight the varied habitats where this medicinal variety can be discovered.
