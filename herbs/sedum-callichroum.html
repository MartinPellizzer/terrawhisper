Sedum callichroum

Sedum callichroum, commonly known as a succulent herb, is a resilient plant valued for its unique medicinal properties. Native to specific tropical regions, it is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential, making it a subject of botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Sedum callichroum
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Sedum callichroum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Saxifragales and the family Crassulaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Sedum.
