Plumbago zeylanica

Wild leadwort

Plumbago zeylanica, commonly known as Ceylon Leadwort, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South Asia. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, its roots and leaves possess potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and analgesic properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and various inflammatory conditions through herbal formulations.

  • Scientific name: Plumbago zeylanica
  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Plumbago zeylanica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Plumbaginaceae and the genus Plumbago.
