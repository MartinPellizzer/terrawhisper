Life history

The life history of Selaginella lepidophylla is characterized by a perennial lifecycle, allowing it to persist through varying environmental conditions over multiple years. As a lycophyte, its development is defined by a complex alternation of generations, typically involving a dominant sporophyte stage that undergoes specialized physiological adaptations to survive extreme desiccation. The plant exhibits a remarkable ability to enter a state of metabolic suspension, curling into a tight, brown ball when moisture is scarce and rapidly unfurling into a green, active state upon rehydration. This survival strategy enables the plant to endure prolonged periods of drought in arid habitats, resuming its growth and reproductive processes once favorable hydration levels are restored.
