Resurrection-plant, scientifically known as Selaginella lepidophylla, is a remarkable lycopod capable of surviving extreme dehydration. Known for its ability to "revive" when exposed to water, this desert-dwelling species is studied for its unique physiological resilience and potential medicinal applications in cellular protection and combating oxidative stress in biological systems.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the resurrection plant, is primarily utilized for its dried aerial parts, which consist of the intricate, moss-like fronds that have the unique ability to undergo desiccation and subsequent rehydration. In traditional medicinal practices, these dried stems and leaves are often prepared as a decoction, a process where the plant material is boiled in water to extract its active bioactive compounds. This liquid preparation is then used to harness the plant's purported antioxidant and neuroprotective properties.
