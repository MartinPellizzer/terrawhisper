Rubia akane, scientifically known as Rubiia argyi, is a perennial flowering plant belonging to the Rubiaceae family. Renowned for its medicinal properties, the plant's roots contain potent anthraquinone glycosides. Historically used in traditional medicine, it is primarily utilized for its therapeutic effects on the circulatory and lymphatic systems.
