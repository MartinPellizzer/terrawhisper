Rubia argyi

What's the taxonomical classification of Rubia argyi?

Rubia argyi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and falls into the order Gentianaales. This plant is a member of the family Rubiaceae, which is further refined into the genus Rubiua, ultimately leading to its specific identification as the species argyi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rubia argyi has an erect, perennial herbaceous habit characterized by quadrangular stems that grow in whorls of four to six leaves. The leaves are typically opposite or in whorls, possessing an elliptical to lanceolate shape with entire margins and prominent longitudinal veins. Small, inconspicuous flowers are arranged in dense cymes, often featuring a tubular corolla with four lobes. The plant produces small, fleshy drupes that transition from green to a deep red or black color upon ripening. Its root system is robust and often serves as the primary site for the accumulation of specific secondary metabolites.
