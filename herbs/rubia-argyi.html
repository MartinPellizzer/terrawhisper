Rubia argyi

Rubia akane

Rubia argyi, commonly known as Indian Madder, is a perennial herb valued for its potent medicinal properties. Primarily sourced from its roots, this plant contains alizarin, a key compound used in traditional remedies. It is widely recognized for its effectiveness in treating skin conditions, inflammation, and various digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Rubia argyi
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Gentianales and the family Rubiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rubia.
