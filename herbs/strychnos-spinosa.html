Strychnos spinosa

Strychnos spinosa, commonly known as the Monkey Orange, is a versatile medicinal plant native to African savannas. Renowned for its nutritional fruit and woody bark, it is traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin infections, playing a vital role in indigenous healing and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Strychnos spinosa
  • Family: Loganiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Gentianales and the family Loganiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Strychnos.
