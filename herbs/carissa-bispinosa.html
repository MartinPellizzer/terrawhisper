Forest Num-num, scientifically known as Carissa bispinosa, is a versatile, evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its glossy leaves and white, star-shaped flowers, it serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. Often used in traditional remedies, this resilient plant offers significant value in biodiversity and landscape design.
