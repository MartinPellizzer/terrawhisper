Excoecaria venenata

Excoecaria venenata, commonly known as the Blister Bush, is a toxic shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its milky, caustic sap, it is used in traditional medicine for skin ailments and inflammation. However, its potent irritant properties require extreme caution to prevent severe chemical burns and ocular damage.

  • Scientific name: Excoecaria venenata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Excoecaria.
