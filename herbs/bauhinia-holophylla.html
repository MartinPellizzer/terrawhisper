Bauhinia holophylla

Cow's pow

Bauhinia holophylla, commonly known as the full-leaf orchid tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, promote wound healing, and support digestive health through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Bauhinia holophylla
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Bauhinia. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined as Bauhinia holophyllla.
