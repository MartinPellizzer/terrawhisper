Lycopodium japonicum

Lycopodium japonicum, commonly known as Japanese clubmoss, is a perennial herbaceous plant valued in traditional medicine. Often utilized for its unique chemical properties, it plays a significant role in botanical studies and herbal remedies. This resilient species serves as a fascinating subject for exploring natural therapeutic potential and biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Lycopodium japonicum
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Lycopodiidae, it is organized into the order Lycopodiales and the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Lycopodium.
