Inula japonica

Inula japonica, commonly known as the Japanese elecampane, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties. Rich in inulin and essential oils, this plant is primarily utilized to support respiratory health, alleviate inflammation, and promote digestive wellness, making it a significant botanical in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Inula japonica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Inula.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution stretching across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is noted within the borders of Türkiye in Europe. Moving into Eastern Europe, the species can be found inhabiting the Crimea region. Its range extends significantly into Siberia, where it is documented in the Altay mountains. Furthermore, the plant is distributed throughout other Siberian territories, specifically in Buryatiya and Chita.
