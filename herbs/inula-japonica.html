Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution stretching across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is noted within the borders of Türkiye in Europe. Moving into Eastern Europe, the species can be found inhabiting the Crimea region. Its range extends significantly into Siberia, where it is documented in the Altay mountains. Furthermore, the plant is distributed throughout other Siberian territories, specifically in Buryatiya and Chita.
