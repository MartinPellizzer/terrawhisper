Pinus tabuliformis

What's the taxonomical classification of Pinus tabuliformis?

Pinus tabuliformis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Pinidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae, eventually reaching the genus Pinus. Ultimately, this specific plant is identified by its unique species designation, tabuliformis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pinus tabuliformis has a single-stemmed growth habit characterized by a tall, cylindrical trunk with reddish-brown bark that develops deep longitudinal fissures as it matures. The foliage consists of needle-like leaves arranged in bundles of two, which are typically dark green and exhibit a slightly curved or twisted appearance. Its woody cones are ovoid to conical in shape, featuring thick, woody scales that protect the developing seeds. The tree develops a spreading crown that becomes more irregular and flattened with age, reflecting its specific taxonomic identity. These morphological traits collectively distinguish it from other pine species within its geographical range.
