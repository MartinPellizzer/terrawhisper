Rheum officinale

Rheum officinale, commonly known as Chinese rhubarb, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its potent laxative effects, its roots contain bioactive compounds like anthraquinones. This versatile plant serves as a vital botanical resource for digestive health and various pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Rheum officinale
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae, it is categorized under the genus Rheum.
