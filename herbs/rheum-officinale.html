Tibetan Rhubarb

Rheum officinale · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (478 studies) · ★★★★★

Tibetan Rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum officinale, is a potent medicinal herb cherished in traditional Tibetan and Chinese medicine. Renowned for its powerful purgative properties, its dried roots are primarily utilized to treat gastrointestinal issues, promote digestion, and alleviate constipation, making it a cornerstone in ancient herbal pharmacopeia.
