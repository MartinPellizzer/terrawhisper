Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, it falls within the family Apocynaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Gymnema, which includes the medicinal species Gymnema sylvestre.

Plant Parts

This plant, Gymnema sylvestre, is a woody climber characterized by its distinct morphological features. The primary medicinal components are found within the green leaves, which are typically arranged oppositely along the climbing stem. Each individual leaf is supported by elongated petioles that connect the blade to the main structural axis of the vine. Within the specialized leaf tissue, particularly on the surface of the lamina, lie the unique gymnemic acids responsible for its ability to temporarily suppress the sensation of sweetness.
