Swartzia polyphylla

Swartzia polyphylla, commonly known as the many-leaved Swartzia, is a medicinal plant native to various African regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in ethnobotanical practices to treat various ailments. Scientific interest continues to grow as researchers explore its complex phytochemical profile and potential pharmacological benefits.
