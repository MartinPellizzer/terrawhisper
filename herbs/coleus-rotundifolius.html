Chinese-potato, scientifically known as Coleus rotundifolius, is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its tuberous roots, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and skin conditions. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds and nutritional properties.

Distribution

This plant, known as Coleus rotundifolius, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species can also be found extending through the territories of Burkina and Ghana. Furthermore, its range continues into the neighboring country of Guinea. Finally, the distribution of this medicinal plant includes the coastal landscapes of the Ivory Coast.
