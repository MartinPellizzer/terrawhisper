Coleus rotundifolius

Coleus rotundifolius, commonly known as Indian Borage or Mexican Mint, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic, succulent leaves, this plant offers potent antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties. It is widely utilized to soothe respiratory ailments, aid digestion, and support overall wellness through natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Coleus rotundifolius
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Coleus.

Distribution

This plant, known as Coleus rotundifolius, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species can also be found extending through the territories of Burkina and Ghana. Furthermore, its range continues into the neighboring country of Guinea. Finally, the distribution of this medicinal plant includes the coastal landscapes of the Ivory Coast.
