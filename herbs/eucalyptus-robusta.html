Plant Parts

This plant, Eucalyptus robusta, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties, most notably its leaves. The leaves are highly valued in traditional and modern medicine for their essential oils, which exhibit strong antiseptic, decongestant, and anti-inflammatory effects when inhaled or applied topically. In addition to the foliage, the fruits, often referred to as woody capsules or gumnuts, represent a distinct morphological part of the plant's reproductive structure. While the leaves are the primary source of medicinal compounds, the overall structure of the plant, including its robust canopy and fruit, contributes to its ecological and pharmacological significance.
