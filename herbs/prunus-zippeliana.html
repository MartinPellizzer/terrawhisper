Prunus zippeliana

Big leaf cherry

Prunus zippeliana, commonly known as the Japanese dwarf cherry, is a resilient medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, its fruits and bark offer therapeutic benefits. This species is increasingly studied for its potential role in supporting cellular health and managing inflammatory responses naturally.

  • Scientific name: Prunus zippeliana
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized into the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Prunus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Its presence extends beyond the mainland into the islands of Japan. Additionally, the species is located within the Nansei-shotō archipelago. Together, these areas define the primary natural range of Prunus zippeliana.
