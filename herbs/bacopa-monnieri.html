Bacopa monnieri

Bacopa monnieri, commonly known as Brahmi, is a perennial creeping herb revered in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent nootropic properties, it enhances cognitive functions like memory, focus, and learning. Rich in bacosides, this adaptogenic plant also helps reduce anxiety and protects the brain against oxidative stress and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Bacopa monnieri
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Bacopa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning multiple continents and diverse climates. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is documented in both Portugal and Spain. Moving toward the African continent, it can be found across Northern Africa in Egypt. Its range extends further into West Tropical Africa, specifically within Nigeria. Finally, the species is also located in West-Central Tropical Africa, notably in the Congo.
