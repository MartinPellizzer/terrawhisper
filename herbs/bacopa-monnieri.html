water hyssop

Bacopa monnieri · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (802 studies) · ★★★★★

Water hyssop, scientifically known as Bacopa monnieri, is a perennial creeping herb revered in Ayurvedic medicine. Often found in wetlands, this potent nootropic is celebrated for enhancing cognitive functions, such as memory and focus. Its rich antioxidant properties help reduce stress, making it a vital plant for brain health.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Bacopa monnieri, is a creeping perennial herb where the whole plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine. The medicinal properties are concentrated primarily in the succulent, fleshy leaf structures, which are characteristically small and rounded. Anatomically, the plant consists of a prostrate stem that grows along the ground, often rooting at the nodes to form dense mats. Because the therapeutic compounds are distributed throughout the vegetative growth, practitioners often employ the whole plants or the aerial shoot systems to prepare various herbal decoctions and extracts.
