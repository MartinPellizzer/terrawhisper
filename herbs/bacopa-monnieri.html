water hyssop

Bacopa monnieri · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (802 studies) · ★★★★★

Water hyssop, scientifically known as Bacopa monnieri, is a perennial creeping herb revered in Ayurvedic medicine. Often found in wetlands, this potent nootropic is celebrated for enhancing cognitive functions, such as memory and focus. Its rich antioxidant properties help reduce stress, making it a vital plant for brain health.

The ecology of Bacopa monnieri is characterized by an extraordinary degree of environmental plasticity, allowing it to inhabit a vast spectrum of diverse ecosystems. This species exhibits a wide range of habitat preferences, ranging from aquatic environments (Vegetação Aquática) to specialized terrestrial landscapes such as Restingas, Caatinga (strictu sensu), Cerrado (lato sensu), and various field types including Campo de Altitude, Campo de Várzea, Campo Limpo, and Campo Rupestre. Additionally, it can be found in anthropogenic areas (Área Antrópica) and Ombrophilous Forests (Floresta Ombrófila). This adaptability extends to its vertical distribution across landscapes, as the plant occupies an expansive elevational range. The morphology of Bacopa monnieri is characterized by its growth form as a non-woody herb, specifically classified as a forb. It is a self-supporting species that does not function as a climber, liana, or vine, and it maintains an ascending shoot orientation. While it can exist in various environments, it is characterized here as a terrestrial plant rather than an aquatic or semiaquatic one, and it functions as an independent organism without parasitic tendencies.
