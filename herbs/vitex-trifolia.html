Vitex trifolia

Rara

Vitex trifolia, commonly known as Three-leaved Chastetree, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and flowers possess potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. It is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various inflammatory conditions through natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Vitex trifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Vitex trifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Vitex.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is noted in Tunisia. Moving toward the northeast, it can be found within the borders of Somalia. The species is also widely distributed throughout East Tropical Africa, specifically in Kenya and Tanzania. Finally, its range extends into South Tropical Africa, where it is located in Mozambique.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Vitex trifolia, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties. The fruits are also significant, often harvested once ripe to extract their therapeutic essence. Within these fruits, the seeds contain concentrated elements used in specific preparations. Furthermore, the roots are sometimes utilized in decoctions, ensuring that almost every part of the plant contributes to its holistic healing potential.
