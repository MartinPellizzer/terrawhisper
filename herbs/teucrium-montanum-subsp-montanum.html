Teucrium montanum subsp. montanum

Teucrium montanum subsp. montanum, commonly known as Mountain Germander, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to various high-altitude regions. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, this subspecies is frequently utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating respiratory ailments, skin inflammations, and digestive issues through its bioactive chemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Teucrium montanum subsp. montanum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Teucrium montanum subsp. montanum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Teucrium.

Common Names

This plant, Teucrium montanum subsp. montanum, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as montane germander, while in Afrikaans, it is known as bergteucium. In the local South African languages, it is called makhulu mkhulu in isiZulu and ntsu mkhulu in isiXhosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the regions of Middle Europe. It can be found growing across the landscapes of Austria and Germany. Its presence also extends into the territories of Belgium and Hungary. Furthermore, the species is documented within the combined regions of Czechia and Slovakia. Together, these locations define the primary geographical range for this particular subspecies.

Plant Parts

This plant, Teucrium montanum subsp. montanum, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through the use of its plant aerial parts. These above-ground structures, which include the stems, leaves, and flowers, contain the essential bioactive compounds required for its therapeutic applications. By harvesting the plant aerial parts, practitioners can access the concentrated phytochemicals stored within the foliage and reproductive organs, making this specific portion of the herb the most vital component for medicinal preparation.
