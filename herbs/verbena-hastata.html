Blue vervain, scientifically known as Verbena hastata, is a perennial herb native to Asia, celebrated for its vibrant, slender blue flowers. Traditionally used in various medicinal systems, it is prized for its restorative properties, often employed to support respiratory health, alleviate inflammation, and promote overall vitality and wellness.
