Dryopteris aemula

Dryopteris aeulis, commonly known as the female fern, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal medicine. Native to various forest ecosystems, this fern contains bioactive compounds utilized for their anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. It plays a vital role in treating various ailments through diverse therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Dryopteris aemula
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, specifically within the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed by the genus Dryopteris.
