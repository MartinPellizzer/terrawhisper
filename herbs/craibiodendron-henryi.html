Craibiodendron henryi

Craibiodendron henryi, commonly known as a rare medicinal shrub, holds significant value in traditional botanical practices. Native to specific tropical ecosystems, this plant is prized for its unique phytochemical properties. Researchers continue to explore its bioactive compounds, seeking potential therapeutic applications for managing various health conditions through natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Craibiodendron henryi
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, placing it in the family Ericaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Craibiodendron.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central areas as well as the high-altitude terrains of Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it inhabits the state of Assam and the East Himalaya region. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow within the Indo-China territory, specifically in Myanmar. Together, these locations highlight the plant's presence across diverse mountainous and subtropical landscapes.
