Ajania myriantha

What's the taxonomical classification of Ajania myriantha?

Ajania myriantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Asterales, the plant is a member of the family Asteraceae. More specifically, it is identified by the genus Ajania and is designated as the species myriantha.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ajania myriantha has perennial, subshrubby stems that form dense, spreading mats with small, oval-shaped leaves measuring approximately 1 to 2 centimeters in length. The foliage is characterized by a dark green color and a slightly leathery texture with finely serrated margins. During the flowering season, the plant produces small, yellowish-white flower heads that are held in tight, terminal clusters. These tiny blossoms are accompanied by inconspicuous bracts that contribute to the compact appearance of the inflorescence. The overall growth habit is low-growing and spreading, allowing the plant to cover ground effectively through its woody base and branching structure.
