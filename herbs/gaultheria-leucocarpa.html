Gaultheria leucocarpa

Gaultheria leucocarpa, commonly known as the white-fruited wintergreen, is a resilient perennial shrub native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant contains bioactive compounds that offer anti-inflammatory and analgesic benefits. It serves as a vital botanical resource in traditional healing practices for managing various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Gaultheria leucocarpa
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Ericaceae. It is further identified by its genus, Gaultheria, specifically under the species Gaultheria leucocarpa.
