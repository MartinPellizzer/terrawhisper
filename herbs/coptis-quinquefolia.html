Coptis quinquefolia, scientifically known as Coptis quinquefolia, is a perennial herb native to East Asia, prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its high berberine content, it possesses potent antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. This versatile plant is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments and metabolic disorders.
