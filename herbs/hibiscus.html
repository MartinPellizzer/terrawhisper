Distribution

This plant exhibits a varied geographical distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within the borders of Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is found growing in the landscapes of Austria and Belgium. It also maintains a notable presence throughout the territories of Czechia-Slovakia. Finally, the distribution extends further into the central reaches of Germany.
