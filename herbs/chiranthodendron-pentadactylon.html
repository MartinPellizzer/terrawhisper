Plant Parts

This plant, known for its striking appearance, utilizes its unique flowers as a primary feature for both pollination and medicinal interest. The flowers of Chiranthodendron pentapeadylon are large, pendulous, and characterized by their bright red or orange hues, which serve to attract avian pollinators. These blossoms are distinctively shaped with long, protruding stamens that give the plant its common name, the "hand-flower tree," and they play a vital role in the plant's reproductive cycle.
