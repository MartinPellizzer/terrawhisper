Chiranthodendron pentadactylon

Chiranthodendron pentaacylon, commonly known as the Hand-Flower Tree, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical India. Renowned for its unique, hand-shaped blossoms, it possesses significant therapeutic properties. Traditionally, various parts of the tree are utilized in Ayurvedic medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin disorders to digestive issues.
