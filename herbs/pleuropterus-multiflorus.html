Scientific literature: Very Extensive (760 studies) · ★★★★★

Fallopia multiflora, scientifically known as Pleuropterus multiflorus, is a perennial herb native to East Asia, highly revered in traditional medicine. Commonly called Japanese knotweed, its nutrient-rich rhizomes contain potent compounds like resveratrol, offering significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits used to support circulation, longevity, and overall metabolic health.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae and falls under the genus Pleuropterus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In the Caucasus, it can be found specifically within the Transcaucasus area. Its presence in China is widespread, spanning from the North-Central regions to the Southeast. Furthermore, the species is documented in the South-Central parts of China. Finally, its range extends southward to include the island of Hainan.
