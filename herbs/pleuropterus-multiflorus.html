Fallopia multiflora, scientifically known as Pleuropterus multiflorus, is a perennial herb native to East Asia, highly revered in traditional medicine. Commonly called Japanese knotweed, its nutrient-rich rhizomes contain potent compounds like resveratrol, offering significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits used to support circulation, longevity, and overall metabolic health.

Plant Parts

This plant, Pleuropterus multiflorus, possesses several distinct morphological features that are utilized in medicinal applications. The plant is characterized by its lush green leaves and can be harvested as whole plants for various preparations. Below the surface, the plant develops a complex root system that includes fine, hairy roots and larger, specialized tuberous roots, which serve as the primary storage organs for its bioactive compounds.
