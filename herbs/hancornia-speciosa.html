Hancornia speciosa

Hancornia speciosa, commonly known as the spectacular flowering shrub, is a prized ornamental plant valued for its brilliant, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, this species holds significant interest in ethnobotanical studies due to its unique chemical properties, making it a subject of fascination for both gardeners and researchers.

  • Scientific name: Hancornia speciosa
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant, Hancornia speciosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Gentianales. Finally, it is a member of the Apocynaceae family and is identified by the genus Hancornia.
