Lycium truncatum

Lycium truncatum, commonly known as the Tibetan wolfberry, is a resilient shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Tibetan Plateau. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this medicinal plant features nutrient-dense berries rich in polysaccharides, offering significant therapeutic potential for enhancing immunity and combating oxidative stress in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Lycium truncatum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Lycium, specifically as the species Lycium truncatum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions in East and Central Asia. Within China, it is commonly found in the northern territories of Inner Mongolia. Its presence is also noted throughout the China North-Central region. Furthermore, the species can be located in the expansive Xinjiang province. Beyond the Chinese borders, its habitat extends into the country of Mongolia.
