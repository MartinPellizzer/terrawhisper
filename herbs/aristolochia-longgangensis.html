Aristolochia longgangensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Aristolochia longgangensis?

Aristolochia longgangensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows a taxonomic lineage that places it in the order Piperales. Within this order, the plant is a representative of the family Aristolochiaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Aristolochia, with its specific identification being the species longgangensis.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause severe kidney damage, permanent renal failure, and various forms of cancer due to the presence of toxic aristolochic acids. Consumption leads to progressive nephropathy, which significantly impairs the filtration functions of the kidneys. Patients often develop uropathy, a condition characterized by damage to the urinary tract and bladder. The high levels of carcinogenicity associated with this species increase the long-term risk of developing urothelial carcinomas. These combined side effects represent a profound threat to human renal and urinary health.

The main side effects this plant can have are shown in the list below.

  • Nephrotoxicity (kidney damage)
  • Nephropathy
  • Carcinogenicity (cancer)
  • Uropathy
  • Renal failure
