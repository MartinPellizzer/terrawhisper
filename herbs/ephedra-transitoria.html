Ephedra transitoria

Ephedra transitoria, commonly known as a medicinal shrub, is a significant species within the Ephedraceae family. Renowned for its alkaloid content, particularly ephedrine, it has been utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments and stimulate the central nervous system. Understanding its botanical properties is vital for pharmacological research.
