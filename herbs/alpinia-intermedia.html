Alpinia intermedia

What's the taxonomical classification of Alpinia intermedia?

Alpinia intermedia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Further taxonomic refinement places the plant in the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Alpinia, and its species designation, intermedia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Alpinia intermedia has a rhizomatous growth habit with long, lanceolate leaves that can reach up to 60 centimeters in length and feature parallel venation. The plant produces upright, erect inflorescences that emerge from the leafy shoots to display striking, showy flowers. These blossoms typically feature tubular, white or pale pink petals with prominent yellow or orange markings near the throat. The structure of the flower is designed to attract specific pollinators through its nectar-rich center. Small, globose fruit capsules develop following successful pollination to facilitate seed dispersal.
