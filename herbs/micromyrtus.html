Micromyrtus

Micromyrtus, commonly known as various species of small-leaved myrtles, is a versatile genus within the Myrtaceae family. Native to diverse habitats, these hardy shrubs offer significant medicinal potential. Renowned for their essential oils, they possess antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, making them valuable subjects for traditional and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Micromyrtus
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, falling within the family Myrtaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Micromyrtus.
