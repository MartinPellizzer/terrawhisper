Rumex dentatus

Golden dock

Rumex dentatus, commonly known as Toothed Dock, is a perennial herb distinguished by its serrated leaves and reddish-brown flowering spikes. Found in moist habitats, this plant contains various bioactive compounds. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it is studied for its potential antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and digestive-regulating properties in herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Rumex dentatus
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rumex and the specific species Rumex dentatus.
