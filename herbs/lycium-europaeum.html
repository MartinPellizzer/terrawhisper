Lycium europaeum

Lycium europaeum, commonly known as the European Boxthorn, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, this plant offers therapeutic potential through its berries and bark. It is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support immune function and promote overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lycium europaeum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Solanales, it is further categorized under the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Lycium.
