Canavalia lineata

Cana Valiia lineata, commonly known as the "medicinal climber," is a versatile plant prized in traditional healing systems. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This botanical specimen offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
