Ochrosia glomerata

Ochroosia glomerata, commonly known as the "palo de sangre" or bloodwood, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive reddish sap, this species is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and healing properties, serving as a vital natural remedy.

  • Scientific name: Ochrosia glomerata
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Gentianaales, it is a member of the family Apocynaceae. Its scientific classification is completed by its placement in the genus Ochrosia.

This plant is widely distributed across various regions within the Malesia botanical zone. Specifically, it can be found growing in the lush environments of Borneo and the Philippines.
