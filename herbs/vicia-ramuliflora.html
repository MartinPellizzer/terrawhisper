What's the taxonomical classification of Vicia ramuliflora?

Vicia ramuliflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated in the order Fabales. It is a representative of the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified under the genus Vicia, with the unique species designation ramuliflora.
