Spanish heath, scientifically known as Erica australis, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its needle-like leaves and delicate bell-shaped flowers, this hardy plant thrives in acidic soils. It is valued in traditional medicine for its potential antioxidant and antimicrobial properties in various treatments.
