Rhodiola sachalinensis

Rhodiola sachalinensis, commonly known as a potent adaptogen, is a medicinal herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its ability to combat fatigue and enhance mental clarity, this plant contains unique salidrosides. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to support stress resilience and promote overall physiological balance.

  • Scientific name: Rhodiola sachalinensis
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Crassulaceae. Specifically, Rhodiola sachalinensis is identified as a member of the genus Rhodiola.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of Northeast Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing on the Kuril Islands. It is also native to the island of Sakhalin within the same Russian region. Moving into China, the species is distributed throughout the Inner Mongolia region. Additionally, its range extends into the Manchuria area of China.
