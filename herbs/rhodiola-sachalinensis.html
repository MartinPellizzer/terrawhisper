, scientifically known as Rhodiola sachalinensis, is a resilient perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its adaptogenic properties, this medicinal plant contains unique salidrosides that help the body resist physical and emotional stress, supporting energy levels, cognitive function, and overall metabolic health through its potent bioactive compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of Northeast Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing on the Kuril Islands. It is also native to the island of Sakhalin within the same Russian region. Moving into China, the species is distributed throughout the Inner Mongolia region. Additionally, its range extends into the Manchuria area of China.
