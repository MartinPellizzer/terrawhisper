Bladder hibiscus, scientifically known as Hibiscus trionum, is a unique perennial shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive bladder-like calyx, this plant holds significant medicinal value. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support urinary tract health and address various inflammatory conditions through its bioactive properties.
