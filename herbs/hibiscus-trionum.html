Hibiscus trionum

Hibiscus trionum, commonly known as the "trio" or "three-leaved hibiscus," is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized to support skin health, reduce inflammation, and promote cardiovascular wellness, making it a vital resource in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Hibiscus trionum
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, it is situated in the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Hibiscus.
