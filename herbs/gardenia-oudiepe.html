Gardenia oudiepe

Gardenia oudiepe, commonly known as the Oudiep Pe Gardenia, is a captivating medicinal shrub native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its fragrant white blooms, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, offering potential benefits for skin health, inflammation, and various restorative wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Gardenia oudiepe
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
