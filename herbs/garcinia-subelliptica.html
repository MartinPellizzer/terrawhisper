Garcinia subelliptica

Common garcinia

Garcinia subellipticia, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, belongs to the Clusiaceae family. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it offers diverse therapeutic potential. Research highlights its antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, making it a valuable subject for pharmacological studies aimed at developing natural healing treatments.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia subelliptica
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, specifically within the family Clusiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Garcinia as the species Garcinia subelliptica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of Asia and the Malay Archipelago. In Eastern Asia, its presence is noted in the Nansei-shoto islands as well as throughout Taiwan. Moving into the Malesia region, the species can be found growing on the island of Jawa. Furthermore, its range extends to the various islands within the Philippines. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in specific tropical and subtropical environments.
