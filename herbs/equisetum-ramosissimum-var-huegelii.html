Equisetum ramosissimum var. huegelii

Equisetum ramosissimum var. huegelii, commonly known as Horsetail, is a resilient perennial fern valued in traditional medicine. Rich in silica, it is widely utilized to support bone health, strengthen connective tissues, and promote skin vitality. This botanical powerhouse offers unique therapeutic properties through its mineral-dense, multifaceted medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Equisetum ramosissimum var. huegelii
  • Family: Equisetaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, known scientifically as Equisetum ramosissimum var. huegelii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
