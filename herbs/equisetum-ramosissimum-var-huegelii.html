Scientifically known as Equisetum ramosissimum var. huegelii, is a unique perennial herb belonging to the horsetail family. Renowned for its high silica content, this botanical variety is utilized in traditional medicine for its astringent and diuretic properties, offering therapeutic potential in supporting bone health and skin integrity.
