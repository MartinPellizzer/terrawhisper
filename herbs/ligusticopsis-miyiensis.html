Ligusticopsis miyiensis

Ligusticopsis miyiensis, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, holds significant potential in ethnobotanical studies. Native to specific ecological niches, this plant is valued for its unique phytochemical profile. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds to uncover new therapeutic applications for treating various inflammatory and metabolic conditions.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ligusticopsis, specifically as the species Ligusticopsis miyiensis.
