Burkea africana

Wild seringa

Burkea africana, commonly known as the Wild Syringa, is a resilient deciduous tree native to Southern and East Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of the plant are used in traditional healing to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical value.

  • Scientific name: Burkea africana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Fabales, and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Burkea.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. It can be found growing in the nation of Benin, contributing to the local flora. The species also has a presence in Burkina, extending its reach across the savanna landscapes. In addition to these areas, the plant is documented in Ghana and Guinea. Finally, its geographical range continues into the Ivory Coast, completing its distribution pattern in this part of the continent.
