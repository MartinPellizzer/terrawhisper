Trigonella filipes

Trigonella filipes, commonly known as a member of the fenugreek genus, is a lesser-known medicinal herb valued in traditional phytotherapy. Characterized by its unique climbing habit, this plant offers diverse bioactive compounds. Researchers explore its potential pharmacological properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant activities, to support modern holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Trigonella filipes
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Finally, it is identified by the genus Trigonella, which distinguishes it as the species Trigonella filipes.
