Ephedra equisetina

Ephedra equisetiina, commonly known as Equisetina Ephedra, is a significant medicinal plant native to arid regions. Renowned for its alkaloid content, particularly ephedrine, it has been utilized traditionally to treat respiratory ailments and stimulate metabolism. Modern pharmacology continues to study its bioactive compounds for their potent physiological effects.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra equisetina
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Ephedra equisetiina, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Gnetidae, falling within the order Ephedrales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ephedraceae and the genus Ephedra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Northern Asia. In Siberia, its presence is documented in the Altay and Krasnoyarsk regions. It can also be found throughout the vast landscapes of West Siberia and the mountainous areas of Tuva. Moving toward the eastern reaches, the species extends into the Russian Far East. Specifically, it inhabits the unique ecosystems located within the Primorye territory.
