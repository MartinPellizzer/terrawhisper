Diplazium donianum

What's the taxonomical classification of Diplazium donianum?

Diplazium donianum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Polypodiinae and falls under the order Polypodiales. This fern is a member of the family Aspleniaceae and is specifically identified by its genus, Diplazium, and its species name, donianum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Diplazium donianum has pinnate fronds that reach up to 60 centimeters in length, consisting of a dark, slender stipe and multiple pairs of lateral pinnae. The texture of the foliage is typically thin and membranous with a slightly glossy appearance on the upper surface. Each pinna is further divided into smaller segments that exhibit slightly serrated or wavy margins. The underside of the leaves contains distinct sori arranged in rows between the midvein and the margin. These reproductive structures are often covered by a specialized protective membrane called an indusium.
