Gynostemma compressum

Gymnostemma compressum, commonly known as the "herb of immortality," is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its adaptogenic properties, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to enhance vitality, reduce stress, and support metabolic health, offering significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and longevity.

  • Scientific name: Gynostemma compressum
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Cucurbitaleales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Gymnostemma, specifically as the species Gymnostemma compressum.
