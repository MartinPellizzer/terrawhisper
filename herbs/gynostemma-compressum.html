Gymnema compressum, scientifically known as Gymnema compressum, is a versatile medicinal herb native to India. Renowned for its potent anti-diabetic properties, it is often called "Gurmar" due to its ability to suppress sugar cravings. This plant plays a vital role in traditional Ayurveda for regulating blood glucose levels.
