Lindera pulcherrima

Lindra pulcherrima, commonly known as the beautiful linden or mountain laurel, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Australia. Renowned for its striking foliage and flowers, it is traditionally utilized in various indigenous practices to treat ailments, offering significant botanical interest and potential therapeutic properties in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Lindera pulcherrima
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
