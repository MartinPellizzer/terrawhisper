Summer Grass, bug-seed grass, cockatoo grass, scientifically known as Alloteropsis cimicina, is a resilient perennial grass native to tropical regions. While primarily recognized as a common forage crop for livestock, it holds significant ethnobotanical value, often utilized in traditional medicine for its purported antimicrobial and healing properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Benin, Liberia, and Nigeria. Moving toward the central regions, its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa. Within this central zone, the species can be found in both Burundi and Cameroon. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's spread across diverse tropical environments.
