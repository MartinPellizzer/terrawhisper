Hairy corkwood, scientifically known as Commiphora africanica, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to African landscapes. Renowned for its aromatic resins, this plant offers diverse therapeutic properties. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive discomfort, it serves as a vital resource in holistic and ethnobotanical medicine.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Its natural range also extends into the territory of Burkina. Furthermore, the species is documented in the Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical presence includes the Ivory Coast.
