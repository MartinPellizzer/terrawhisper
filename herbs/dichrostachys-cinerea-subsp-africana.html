Bushveld Sicklebush, scientifically known as Dichrostachys cinerea subsp. africana, is a versatile legume native to African savannas. Renowned for its profound medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional healing to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues, offering significant pharmacological potential.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of the African continent and its islands. In Northern Africa, its presence can be documented within the borders of Egypt. The species also extends into the Macaronesian region, specifically found in Cape Verde. Moving into West Tropical Africa, it is widely distributed across multiple nations. Specifically, this includes populations located in Benin, Burkina, and Gambia.
