Prunus virginiana var. virginiana

Prunus virginiana var. virginiana, commonly known as chokecherry, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to North America. Renowned for its dark, astringent berries, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, it has traditionally been used to support digestive health and alleviate inflammatory conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Prunus virginiana var. virginiana
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Rosales and is a member of the Rosaceae family. Finally, it is identified by the genus Prunus, specifically as the variety virginiana.
