Virginia chokecherry

Prunus virginiana var. virginiana · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (524 studies) · ★★★★★

Virginia chokecherry, scientifically known as Prunus virginiana var. virginiana, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to North America. Renowned for its dark, nutrient-dense berries, it has long been utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues and inflammation, offering a rich source of antioxidants and essential vitamins for wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Rosales and is a member of the Rosaceae family. Finally, it is identified by the genus Prunus, specifically as the variety virginiana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across various regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented in both Norway and Sweden. Moving toward the center of the continent, it can be found within the borders of Germany. The species also extends into Southwestern Europe, specifically inhabiting the landscapes of France.
