Phyllanthus assamicus

Phyllanthus assamicus, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb, is a species native to the lush, subtropical regions of Assam. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising potential for pharmacological research and modern herbal wellness treatments.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus assamicus
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement situates it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range of distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence also extends to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is native to the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Together, these locations define the unique geographical footprint of Phyllanthus assamicus.
