Hyoscyamus niger

Henbane

Hyoscyamus niger, commonly known as Henbane, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its alkaloid content, particularly hyoscyamine, it has been used historically for its sedative and antispasmodic properties. However, due to its high toxicity, it must be handled with extreme caution.

  • Scientific name: Hyoscyamus niger
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, the medicinal species is categorized under the genus Hyoscyamus.
