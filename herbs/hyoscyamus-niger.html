Hyoscyamus niger · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (542 studies) · ★★★★★

Henbane, scientifically known as Hyoscyamus niger, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its high concentrations of tropane alkaloids like hyoscyamine, it has historically been used for its sedative and antispasmodic properties, though its extreme toxicity requires precise, expert-guided pharmaceutical application.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Henbane, is a biennial herb characterized by its various therapeutic and toxic components. The entire plant is classified among various medicinal herbs, though caution is required due to its potency. The leaves are typically large and irregularly shaped, providing a significant surface area for the plant's chemical compounds. For medicinal preparations, the roots are often harvested as they contain high concentrations of alkaloids. Additionally, the seeds, or the single seed produced within its capsules, are frequently utilized in traditional pharmacological applications to harness the plant's sedative and antispasmodic properties.
