Hyoscyamus niger · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (542 studies) · ★★★★★

Henbane, scientifically known as Hyoscyamus niger, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its high concentrations of tropane alkaloids like hyoscyamine, it has historically been used for its sedative and antispasmodic properties, though its extreme toxicity requires precise, expert-guided pharmaceutical application.
