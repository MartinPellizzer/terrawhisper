Thymus thracicus

Thymus thracicus, commonly known as the Thrace thyme, is a resilient aromatic shrub native to the Balkan Peninsula. Distinguished by its delicate leaves and pale blossoms, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its antiseptic properties, offering therapeutic potential in treating respiratory ailments and supporting digestive health.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
