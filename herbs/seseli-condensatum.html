Seseli condensatum

Seseli condensatum, commonly known as a rare member of the Apiaceae family, is a specialized medicinal plant found in specific montane habitats. Valued for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers potential therapeutic benefits. Understanding its botanical characteristics and ecological requirements is essential for exploring its traditional and pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Seseli condensatum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae and the genus Seseli. Specifically, it is identified by the scientific name Seseli condensatum.

Common Names

This plant, Seseli condensatum, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is referred to as the condensed seseli, while in German, it is called dichtes Seselium. In its native or localized habitats, it may also be known as kondensiertes Seseli in the German-speaking botanical tradition.

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several key regions of Eastern Europe and Northern Asia. In Eastern Europe, its range extends through both East European Russia and North European Russia. Moving into the vast territory of Siberia, the species can be found in the Altay region. It also inhabits the area of Buryatiya within the same Siberian expanse. Finally, its geographical presence includes the Chitа region of Siberia.
