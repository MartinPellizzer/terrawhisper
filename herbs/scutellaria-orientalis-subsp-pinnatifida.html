Scutellaria orientalis subsp. pinnatifida

Scutellaria orientalis pinnatifida

Scutellaria orientalis subsp. pinnatifida, commonly known as a specialized subspecies of skullcap, is a resilient medicinal herb valued in traditional pharmacology. Renowned for its bioactive flavonoids, this plant offers significant neuroprotective and anti-inflammatory properties, serving as a vital botanical resource for studying neurological health and natural therapeutic interventions.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria orientalis subsp. pinnatifida
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Scutellaria. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies pinnatifida of the species Scutellaria orientalis.
