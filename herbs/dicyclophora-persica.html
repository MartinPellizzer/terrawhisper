Dicyclophora persica

Dicyclophora persica, commonly known as Persian Dicyclophora, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific regions of Iran. Renowned for its unique botanical characteristics, it holds significant potential in traditional pharmacology. Research continues into its bioactive compounds, exploring therapeutic applications for various ailments and its role in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Dicyclophora persica
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Dicyclophora.

Common Names

This plant, Dicyclophora persica, is known by several different common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Persian dicyclophora, while in German, it is commonly called das Persische Schuppenblümchen. Moving to other linguistic contexts, it is known as la diclyophora persica in French and as la diclyophora persica in Spanish.
