Ulmus rubra

Ulmus rubra, commonly known as the slippery elm, is a cherished medicinal tree prized for its soothing inner bark. Rich in mucilage, it serves as a natural remedy for soothing sore throats, calming digestive inflammation, and supporting skin health, offering a gentle, time-tested approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Ulmus rubra
  • Family: Ulmaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Ulmaceae and the genus Ulmus.
