Red elm, scientifically known as Ulmus rubra, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, its bark and inner layers offer anti-inflammatory and soothing benefits. This resilient species serves as a significant botanical resource for treating various ailments through natural healing applications.
