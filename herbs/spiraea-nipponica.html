Spiraea nipponica

Spiraea nipponica, commonly known as Japanese Spirea, is a resilient deciduous shrub celebrated for its delicate, frothy clusters of pink flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this hardy plant holds significance in traditional herbal practices, valued for its calming properties and its ability to thrive in diverse environments.

  • Scientific name: Spiraea nipponica
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Spiraea.
