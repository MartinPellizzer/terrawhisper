False sandalwood, scientifically known as Ximenia americana, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and gastrointestinal issues. This resilient plant offers significant pharmacological potential for modern drug development and wellness.
