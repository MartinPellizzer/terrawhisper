What's the taxonomical classification of Pedicularis davidii?

Pedicularis davidii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and is a member of the order Lamiales. Further taxonomic refinement places this plant within the family Orobanchaceae, specifically under the genus Pedicularis, with its unique designation as the species davidii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pedicularis davidii has upright, unbranched stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and are adorned with small, lanceolate to linear leaves. The foliage is primarily basal, forming a rosette of leaves with serrated margins that transition into smaller, oppositely arranged leaves along the stem. Its inflorescence consists of a dense, terminal spike containing numerous tubular, bilabiate flowers that display a striking reddish-purple or pinkish hue. Each individual flower possesses a prominent upper lip and a smaller, lobed lower lip, which are characteristic of the genus. The root system is robust and often features rhizomes that assist in the plant's establishment in alpine environments.
