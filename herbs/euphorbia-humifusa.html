Euphorbia humifusa

Euphorbia humifusa, commonly known as the "ground spurge," is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its unique healing properties continue to fascinate researchers exploring natural pharmacological remedies.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia humifusa
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Euphorbia.
