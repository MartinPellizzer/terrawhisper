Physalis philadelphica

Tomatillo

Physalis philadelphica, commonly known as the husk tomato or ground cherry, is a versatile perennial herb native to North America. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds traditional medicinal significance, often utilized for its anti-inflammatory properties and ability to support digestive health through its nutrient-dense, vitamin-rich fruit.
