Limonium virgatum

Limonium virgatum, commonly known as sea lavender, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal wetlands. Renowned for its delicate, airy clusters of violet flowers, this plant thrives in saline environments. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it plays a vital role in stabilizing coastal ecosystems and supporting local biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Limonium virgatum
  • Family: Plumbaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Limonium virgatum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Plumbaginaceae and falls under the genus Limonium.
