African blackwood, scientifically known as Dalbergia melanoxylon, is a highly prized timber tree found across eastern and southern Africa. Beyond its legendary use in musical instrument crafting, various parts of the plant hold significant medicinal value, traditionally utilized in folk remedies to treat ailments ranging from skin conditions to inflammation.
