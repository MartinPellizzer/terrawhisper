Dalbergia melanoxylon

African blackwood

Dalbergia melanoxylon, commonly known as Siamese Rosewood, is a prized medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments and promote wound healing. Its unique chemical profile offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
