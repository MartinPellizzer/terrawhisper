Ferula

Ferula, commonly known as giant fennel, is a diverse genus of aromatic herbs within the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its resinous sap, various species are prized in traditional medicine for their anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. These resilient plants play a crucial role in both herbal pharmacology and ecological biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Ferula
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae, falling within the genus Ferula.
