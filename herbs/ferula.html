Giant Fennel, scientifically known as Ferula, is a versatile perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its impressive stature and aromatic properties, it contains bioactive compounds used extensively in traditional medicine. These include anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial agents, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical research today.

Plant Parts

This plant, belonging to the Ferula genus, is highly valued for its diverse medicinal components distributed throughout its structure. The robust roots and thick stems serve as the foundational base of the organism, providing structural support and storing essential nutrients. The leaves are often used in various traditional preparations, but the most therapeutically significant elements are the chemical constituents extracted from the plant. Specifically, the plant is known for producing aromatic volatile oils and a complex mixture of oleo-gum-resins, which are often harvested from the plant's tissues to be used in medicinal extracts and ointments.
