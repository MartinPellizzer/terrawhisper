Giant Fennel

Ferula · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (49 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Giant Fennel, scientifically known as Ferula, is a versatile perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its impressive stature and aromatic properties, it contains bioactive compounds used extensively in traditional medicine. These include anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial agents, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical research today.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several European regions. In Northern Europe, its presence can be noted within Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is distributed throughout Germany, Hungary, and the Czechia-Slovakia region. Additionally, its habitat extends to the Mediterranean climate of Southwestern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing among the Baleares islands.
