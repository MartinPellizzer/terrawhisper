Salta triflora

Salta triflora, commonly known as a versatile medicinal herb, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, this plant is utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from inflammatory conditions to digestive issues. Understanding its phytochemical composition is essential for exploring its therapeutic potential globally.

  • Scientific name: Salta triflora
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Salta.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several regions of South America. In Western South America, it can be found within the borders of Bolivia. Its presence extends into Brazil, specifically concentrated in the West-Central region. In Southern South America, the species is distributed throughout the Northeast and Northwest areas of Argentina. Finally, its habitat reaches further south into the country of Paraguay.
