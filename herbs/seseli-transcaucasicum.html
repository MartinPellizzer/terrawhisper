Seseli transcaucasicum

Seseli transcaucasicum, commonly known as a medicinal herb from the Apiaceae family, is native to the Caucasus region. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to address various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential, making it a subject of pharmacological interest.

  • Family: Apiaceae

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Seseli, and its specific species name, Seseli transcaucasicum.

Common Names

This plant, Seseli transcaucasicum, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is referred to as Caucasian seseli, while in Russian, it is commonly called сезели транскавказское (sezeli transkavkazskoye). In Turkish, the species is known as Transkafkasya seseli.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the mountainous regions of the Caucasus. Specifically, its presence is well-documented throughout the Transcaucasus area. Beyond this region, its geographical range extends into Western Asia. Within Western Asia, it can be located in the country of Iran. Furthermore, the species is also distributed across the territory of Türkiye.
