Psidium guajava

Psidium guajava, commonly known as guava, is a tropical evergreen shrub celebrated for its remarkable therapeutic properties. Rich in vitamins and bioactive compounds, this medicinal powerhouse offers potent antioxidant, antibacterial, and anti-inflammatory benefits. Traditionally used to treat digestive issues and skin ailments, it remains a cornerstone of natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Psidium guajava
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae, it is classified under the genus Psidium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. Within the Macaronesian archipelago, it can be found growing in the Canary Islands, Cape Verde, and Madeira. Additionally, its range extends into West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it is documented in the nations of Burkina and Gambia. Consequently, this species occupies both Atlantic islands and mainland African territories.
