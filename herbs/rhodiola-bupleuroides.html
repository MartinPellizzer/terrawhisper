Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is primarily found in the South-Central areas as well as throughout Tibet. Its distribution also extends significantly into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species is commonly located throughout the East Himalaya region. Finally, its presence is well-documented across the mountainous landscapes of Nepal.

Plant Parts

This plant, Rhodiola bupleuroides, is characterized by its significant underground structures which are the primary focus of its medicinal value. The plant features a sturdy rhizome that grows horizontally beneath the soil surface, often serving as a storage organ for nutrients. Extending from this structure is the root system, which helps anchor the plant and absorb essential minerals from its alpine habitat. In many medicinal preparations, these subterranean parts are harvested and processed to extract the bioactive compounds responsible for its therapeutic properties.
