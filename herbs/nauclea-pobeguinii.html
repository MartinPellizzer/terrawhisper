Distribution

This plant, known as Nauclea pobeguinii, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the West Tropical African region. It can be found growing naturally within the borders of Burkina. Furthermore, its presence is documented throughout the coastal and inland areas of Ghana. The species also extends its range into Guinea-Bissau and the neighboring nation of Guinea. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the territory of the Ivory Coast.

Plant Parts

This plant, Nauclea poggeana (often referred to in medicinal contexts as Nauclea species), possesses several components utilized in traditional medicine. The stem bark and the outer bark are frequently harvested for their bioactive compounds, often used to treat inflammatory conditions. Additionally, the roots are highly valued for their potent therapeutic properties, often prepared as decoctions to address various ailments. The leaves are also commonly employed in herbal remedies, while the fruits serve as a distinctive part of the plant's reproductive structure, contributing to the overall botanical profile used in ethnobotanical studies.
