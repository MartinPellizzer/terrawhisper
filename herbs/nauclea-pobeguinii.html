Nauclea pobeguinii

Nauclea pobeguinii, commonly known as the African medicinal tree, is a significant botanical species native to West African tropical forests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including malaria and inflammation, offering promising potential for modern drug development and therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Nauclea pobeguinii
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, falling within the Rubiaceae family. Finally, it is identified by the genus Nauclea.
