Anoectochilus formosanus

Anoectochilus formosanus, commonly known as the Formosan orchid, is a fascinating medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its delicate blooms, it is traditionally valued in herbal medicine for its potential therapeutic properties. Researchers continue to explore its bioactive compounds for their significant roles in modern pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Anoectochilus formosanus
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asparagales and is a member of the Orchidaceae family. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Anoectochilus.
