Rhus punjabensis

Rhus punjabensis, commonly known as the Punjab sumac, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various arid regions. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments and skin inflammations. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Rhus punjabensis
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Rhus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning multiple regions across Asia. Within China, it is widely documented in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Its presence is also noted in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Beyond China, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically found in Assam. Together, these locations highlight the plant's diverse natural habitat across the continent.
