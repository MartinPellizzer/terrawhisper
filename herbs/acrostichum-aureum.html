Acrostichum aureum

Acrostichum aureum, commonly known as the Golden Leather Fern, is a robust tropical plant found in coastal wetlands. Renowned for its striking, leathery fronds, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various cultures utilize its extracts for medicinal purposes, traditionally addressing skin ailments, inflammation, and various digestive issues through natural properties.

  • Scientific name: Acrostichum aureum
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae. It is further classified under the order Polypodiales and the family Pteridaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Acrostichum.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented in several coastal and inland nations within this tropical belt.
