Golden leather fern, scientifically known as Acrostichum aureum, is a robust aquatic plant found in tropical wetlands. Renowned for its large, leathery fronds, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various cultures utilize its extracts for medicinal purposes, including treating skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues, showcasing its diverse therapeutic potential.
