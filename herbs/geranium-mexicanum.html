Geranium mexicanum

Geranium mexicanum, commonly known as Mexican Geranium or Rose Geranium, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its aromatic leaves. Renowned for its calming scent, it possesses potent antibacterial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used in aromatherapy and skincare, this resilient perennial offers diverse therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Geranium mexicanum
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae, it is classified under the genus Geranium.

Common Names

This plant, Geranium mexicanum, is known by several different names depending on the region and language. In English, it is most frequently referred to as the Mexican geranium, while in Spanish, it is commonly called geranio mexicano. In French, it is known as géranium du Mexique, and in German, it is called mexikanischer Storchschnabel.
