Amaranthus spinosus

Amaranthus spinosus, commonly known as spiny amaranth, is a resilient annual herb valued for its extensive therapeutic properties. Recognized by its sharp, thorn-like stipules, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant bioactive compounds for various health applications.

  • Scientific name: Amaranthus spinosus
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified as a member of the genus Amaranthus.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across various regions of the European continent.
