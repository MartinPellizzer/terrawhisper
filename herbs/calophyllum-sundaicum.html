Distribution

This plant is a medicinal species found within the diverse ecosystems of the Malesia region. Its natural habitat spans several key islands across Southeast Asia. Specifically, it can be located throughout the large island of Borneo. The species is also documented to grow within the territories of Malaya. Furthermore, its geographical presence extends to the island of Sumatera.

This plant, Calophyllum sundaicum, utilizes its leaf as a primary component in traditional medicinal applications. In many folk medicine practices, these leaves are processed or extracted to harness their potential medicinal benefits.
