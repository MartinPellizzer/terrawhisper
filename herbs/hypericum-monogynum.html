Chinese St. John's wort, scientifically known as Hypericum monogynum, is a versatile medicinal herb highly valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its calming properties, it is frequently used to alleviate mild depression, anxiety, and sleep disturbances. This potent plant offers a natural approach to supporting emotional balance and overall well-being.
