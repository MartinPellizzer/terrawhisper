Nanhaia speciosa

Nanhaia speciosa, commonly known as the "King of Medicinal Herbs" in certain traditional practices, is a remarkable botanical species valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant potential in treating inflammation and supporting metabolic health through its unique natural constituents.

  • Scientific name: Nanhaia speciosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Nanhaia, specifically as the species Nanhaia speciosa.

Common Names

This plant, Nanhaiia speciosa, is known by several common names depending on the linguistic context. In Chinese, it is referred to as 南海藤 (zh), which is also written as 南海藤 in both simplified (zh-hans) and mainland Chinese (zh-cn) scripts.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted across the South-Central regions and throughout the Southeast. It is also found specifically on the tropical island of Hainan. Beyond Chinese borders, the species extends into the Indo-China region. Notably, its range includes the country of Vietnam.
