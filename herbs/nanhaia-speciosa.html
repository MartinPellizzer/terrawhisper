This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Nanhaia, specifically as the species Nanhaia speciosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted across the South-Central regions and throughout the Southeast. It is also found specifically on the tropical island of Hainan. Beyond Chinese borders, the species extends into the Indo-China region. Notably, its range includes the country of Vietnam.
