Illyrian Thistle, scientifically known as Onopordum illyricum, is a striking perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its towering, spiny stalks and robust purple blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties and antioxidant potential.
