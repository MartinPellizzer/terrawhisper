Vachellia flava

Vachellia flava, commonly known as the yellow Vachellia, is a significant medicinal shrub native to various African regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues, owing to its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, falling within the family Fabaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Vachellia.
