Huperzia lucidula

Huperzia lucidula, commonly known as lucidula firmoss, is a perennial clubmoss native to North America. Renowned in pharmacology, it contains the potent alkaloid huperzine A. This compound acts as a highly selective acetylcholinesterase inhibitor, showing significant potential in research for treating cognitive decline and Alzheimer's disease symptoms.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia lucidula
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Lycopodiidae and the order Lycopodiales, it is a member of the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Huperzia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several Canadian provinces. In Western Canada, its presence is noted within the province of Manitoba. Moving to Eastern Canada, it can be found throughout Ontario. The species also inhabits the Atlantic provinces, specifically in New Brunswick. Additionally, its range extends to the islands of Newfoundland and Nova Scotia.
