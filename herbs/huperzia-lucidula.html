Shining firmoss, scientifically known as Huperzia lucidula, is a perennial lycophyte native to North American forests. Renowned in ethnobotany, it contains huperzine A, a potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. This alkaloid is extensively studied for its potential to enhance cognitive function and provide neuroprotective benefits against various neurodegenerative diseases.
