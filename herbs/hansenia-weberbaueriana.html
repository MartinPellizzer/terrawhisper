Notopterygium, scientifically known as Hansenia weberbaueriana, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Often used to treat digestive issues and respiratory ailments, this plant offers profound therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.
