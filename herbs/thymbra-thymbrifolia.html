Thymbra thymbrifolia

Thymbra thymbrifolia, commonly known as Mediterranean thyme or savory, is a fragrant perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, this medicinal plant is rich in essential oils. It is traditionally utilized for its potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and digestive properties in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Thymbra thymbrifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. It is specifically categorized under the genus Thymbra.
