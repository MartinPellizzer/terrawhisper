White lotus, scientifically known as Nymphaea lotus, is a sacred aquatic perennial celebrated for its profound medicinal properties. Renowned in ancient traditions, this exquisite flowering plant possesses calming, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant qualities. It is widely utilized in herbal remedies to support neurological health and promote deep, restorative relaxation.
