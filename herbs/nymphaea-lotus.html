Nymphaea lotus

White lotus

Nymphaea lotus, commonly known as the white water lily, is a captivating aquatic perennial valued for its therapeutic properties. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this plant offers calming effects and anti-inflammatory benefits. Its delicate blooms serve as a symbol of purity while providing essential bioactive compounds for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Nymphaea lotus
  • Family: Nymphaeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Nymphaeales and the family Nymphaeaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its classification under the genus Nymphaea.
