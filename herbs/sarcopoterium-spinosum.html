Sarcopoterium spinosum

Prickly burnet

Sarcopoterium spinosum, commonly known as sweet broom, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this aromatic plant possesses significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive health and manage various inflammatory conditions effectively.

  • Scientific name: Sarcopoterium spinosum
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Rosaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Sarcopoterium.
