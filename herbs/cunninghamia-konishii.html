Cunninghamia konishii

Cunninghamia konishii, commonly known as the Konishi's Cunninghamia, is a medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific subtropical regions, its extracts are traditionally used to address various ailments. Research into its bioactive compounds continues to explore its potential in developing innovative natural pharmacological treatments.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Cunninghamia konishii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is classified under the order Pinales. Finally, it is a member of the family Cupressaceae and the genus Cunninghamia.
