Wissadula periplocifolia

Wissadula periplocifolia, commonly known as the Wissadula plant, is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient species offers valuable phytochemical insights for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Wissadula periplocifolia
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malvaceae and falls under the genus Wissadula.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Wissadula periplocefolia, is recognized by various regional names across different languages. In Chinese, it is referred to as 隔蒴苘 (zh, zh-cn, and zh-tw), while in Bengali, it is commonly known as সাদা নাগমণি (bn). Additionally, in the Fulani language, it is called Kapappi daneehi (ff).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted in the tropical province of Hainan. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found spanning across the states of India and Assam. Its range further extends into the neighboring country of Bangladesh. Finally, the species is also documented to grow within the island nation of Sri Lanka.
