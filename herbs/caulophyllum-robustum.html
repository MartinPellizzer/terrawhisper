Caulophyllum robustum

Caulophyllum robustum, commonly known as Blue Cohosh, is a perennial herb native to North American woodlands. Renowned in traditional herbalism, this plant features striking blue-black berries and distinctive foliage. While historically used to support reproductive health, it must be handled with extreme caution due to its potent pharmacological properties.

  • Scientific name: Caulophyllum robustum
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, known as Caulophyllum robustum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the Berberidaceae family within the genus Caulophyllum.
