Asian blue cohosh, scientifically known as Caulophyllum robustum, is a perennial herb native to North American forests. Recognized by its striking blue berries and delicate white flowers, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional herbalism for its potential benefits in supporting reproductive health and balancing hormonal systems.
