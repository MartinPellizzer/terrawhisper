Hemiboea follicularis

What's the taxonomical classification of Hemiboea follicularis?

Hemiboea follicularis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Lamiales and the family Gesneriaceae. Finally, the plant is situated within the genus Hemiboea, specifically identified as the species follicularis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Hemiboea follicularis has pinnately compound leaves with several pairs of oblong to elliptic leaflets that are arranged oppositely along a central rachis. The leaflets exhibit a leathery texture and possess entire margins with acute or obtuse apices. The plant produces small, actinomorphic flowers that are typically white or pale pink in color. These blossoms emerge from axillary positions and contain five petals that are fused at the base. The reproductive structures include a superior ovary that develops into a characteristic follicular fruit.
