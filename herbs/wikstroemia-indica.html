Tiebush, scientifically known as Wikstroemia indica, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. While highly valued for its therapeutic potential, careful handling is essential due to its inherent toxic properties.
