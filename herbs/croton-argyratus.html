Croton argyratus

Croton argyratus, commonly known as the Silver Croton, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued for its therapeutic properties. Indigenous to various tropical regions, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Croton argyratus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Croton.
