Physiology

The physiology of Croton argylanthus is characterized by a metabolic framework consistent with standard dicotyledonous woody shrubs. The plant utilizes a C3 photosynthetic pathway, meaning carbon fixation occurs via the Calvin cycle within the mesophyll cells, which is typical for most species within the Euphorbiaceae family. Furthermore, Croton argylanthus is not a nitrogen fixer; it lacks the specialized symbiotic relationship with nitrogen-fixing bacteria, such as Rhizobium, required to convert atmospheric nitrogen into usable forms, and therefore relies on the uptake of nitrates and ammonium from the soil substrate to meet its nutritional requirements.
