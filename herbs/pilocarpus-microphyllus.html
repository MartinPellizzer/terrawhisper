Pernambuco jaborandi, scientifically known as Pilocarpus microphyllus, is a medicinal shrub native to Brazil. Renowned for its alkaloid content, particularly pilocarpine, it has played a vital role in pharmacology. This plant is primarily utilized in treating glaucoma and managing certain conditions related to excessive sweating and salivary secretion.
