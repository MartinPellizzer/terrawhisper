Pilocarpus microphyllus

Pilocarpus microphyllus, commonly known as a species of pilocarpine-producing shrub, is a significant medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its alkaloid content, it is primarily utilized in pharmacology to treat glaucoma and stimulate saliva production, playing a vital role in modern treatments for various physiological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Pilocarpus microphyllus
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida.
