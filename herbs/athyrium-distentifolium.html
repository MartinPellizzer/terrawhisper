Athyrium distentifolium

Athyrium distentifolium, commonly known as the broad-leaved lady fern, is a resilient perennial fern found in moist, shaded habitats. While primarily valued for its ornamental beauty in lush landscapes, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical interest, traditionally utilized in various cultures for its unique properties in holistic herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Athyrium distentifolium
  • Family: Aspleniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae. It is further classified under the order Polypodiales and the family Aspleniaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Athyrium.
