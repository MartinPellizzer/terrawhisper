Strychnos

Strychnos, commonly known as the strychnine tree, is a genus of woody plants belonging to the Loganiaceae family. Renowned for producing potent alkaloids, these species play a critical role in pharmacological research. While some species offer medicinal potential, others contain highly toxic compounds that require careful, controlled handling.

  • Scientific name: Strychnos
  • Family: Loganiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, it is a member of the family Loganiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Strychnos.
