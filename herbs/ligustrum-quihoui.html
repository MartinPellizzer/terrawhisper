Ligustrum quihoui

Ligustrum quihoui, commonly known as the privet tree, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its various parts are utilized to treat diverse ailments. This resilient species offers significant pharmacological potential for developing natural therapeutic treatments and wellness solutions.
