Reynoutria

Reynoutria, commonly known as Japanese knotweed, is a resilient perennial celebrated for its remarkable medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like resveratrol, it offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits. Traditionally used to support circulatory health and manage inflammation, this versatile plant remains a significant subject in modern herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Reynoutria
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Reynoutria.
