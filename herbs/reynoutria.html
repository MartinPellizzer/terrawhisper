Knotweed, scientifically known as Reynoutria, is a resilient plant celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Often recognized by its distinctive heart-shaped leaves, it contains bioactive compounds like resveratrol. Traditionally used to support vascular health and reduce inflammation, this versatile species remains a vital subject in modern herbal pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Japanese Knotweed, possesses distinct morphological features used in various medicinal preparations. The most therapeutically significant part is the rhizomes, which are thick, fleshy underground structures that store vital compounds like resveratrol. While these rhizomes extend deep into the soil, they are connected to a complex network of fibrous roots that help anchor the plant. Above the ground, the plant develops sturdy, hollow stems that grow rapidly in height, topped by large, heart-shaped or shield-shaped leaves that facilitate efficient photosynthesis.
