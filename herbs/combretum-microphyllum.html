Combretum microphyllum

Combretum microphyllum, commonly known as the small-leaf bush, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, offering a valuable resource in ethnobotanical studies and natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Combretum microphyllum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Combretum microphyllum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales. Finally, it is a member of the family Combretaceae within the genus Combretum.
