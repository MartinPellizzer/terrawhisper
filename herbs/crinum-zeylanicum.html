Crinum zeylanicum

Crinum zeylanicum, commonly known as the Zeeran Lily, is a perennial medicinal plant native to South Asia. Renowned for its potent alkaloid content, it is widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various parasitic infections, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Crinum zeylanicum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Crinum, within which the species C. zeylanicum is placed.
