Milk-and-wine-lily, scientifically known as Crinum zeylanicum, is a perennial herb prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its striking white blooms and bulbous roots, this plant contains potent alkaloids. It is widely utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and anticancer properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural healing.
