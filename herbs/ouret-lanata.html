Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across the African continent. In Northern Africa, it can be specifically found within the borders of Egypt. Moving toward the West Tropical Africa region, the species is well-established in Benin and Burkina. Its presence continues through Ghana, where it remains a notable part of the local flora. Plant Parts

This plant, known as Ouret lanaata, is a versatile medicinal species where various components are utilized for healing purposes. In many traditional practices, the whole plant is harvested to ensure all active compounds are captured for decoctions. The leaf serves as a primary source of medicinal properties, often used in topical applications or infusions. Additionally, the herb itself is frequently dried and prepared for various therapeutic uses, while the whole aerial parts, including the stems and foliage above the ground, are collected to create potent botanical extracts.
