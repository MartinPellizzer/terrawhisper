Ouret lanata

Ouret lanaata, commonly known as the "tree of life" in certain traditional practices, is a revered medicinal plant celebrated for its diverse healing properties. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory qualities, it serves as a cornerstone in herbal remedies, supporting holistic wellness and vitality through ancient botanical wisdom.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
