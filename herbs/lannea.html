Lannea

Lannea, commonly known as the shrubby medicinal plant, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Found primarily in tropical regions, its various parts are utilized to treat diverse ailments, ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions. Exploring its phytochemical properties offers promising insights into developing modern therapeutic applications.
