Mojave yucca, scientifically known as Yucca schidigera, is a resilient desert shrub native to the Southwestern United States. Renowned for its hardiness, this medicinal plant contains saponins and polysaccharides, traditionally used to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote skin vitality through its potent, bioactive chemical compounds.

This plant is primarily distributed across the arid regions of the Southwestern United States. Within this American territory, it can be found growing in the state of Arizona.
