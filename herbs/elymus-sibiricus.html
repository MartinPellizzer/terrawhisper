Elymus sibiricus

Elymus sibiricus, commonly known as Siberian wild rye, is a resilient perennial grass native to the steppes of Asia. Valued for its ecological endurance, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to support digestive health and reduce inflammation, offering promising botanical properties for modern natural wellness studies.

  • Scientific name: Elymus sibiricus
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, it is further categorized under the family Poaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Elymus.
