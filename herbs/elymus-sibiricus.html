Siberian wildrye, scientifically known as Elymus sibiricus, is a perennial grass native to the vast steppes of Asia. Valued for its resilience in harsh climates, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. It is studied for its nutritional potential and bioactive compounds, offering promising applications in traditional and modern medicine.
