Pinus parviflora var. parviflora

Pinus parviflora var. parviflora, commonly known as the Japanese White Pine, is a revered coniferous species prized for its medicinal and culinary properties. Its nutrient-rich seeds offer significant health benefits, while its resin and needles are traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to promote wellness and holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Pinus parviflora var. parviflora
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales and family Pinaceae, it is classified under the genus Pinus. Specifically, its taxonomic designation is Pinus parviflora var. parviflora.

Common Names

This plant, Pinus parviflora var. parviflora, is known by various regional names depending on the language and culture. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Japanese white pine, while in Japanese, it is known as aoyamaboku or hinoki. In Chinese, it is identified as baiyun, and in Korean, it is called chojong.

This plant is a specific variety of Japanese five-needle pine that possesses a limited natural range. Its geographical distribution is primarily concentrated within the region of Eastern Asia. More specifically, the variety is native to the islands of Japan. Within this territory, it can be found growing in various mountainous habitats. Consequently, its presence is highly characteristic of the unique temperate ecosystems found in this part of the world.
