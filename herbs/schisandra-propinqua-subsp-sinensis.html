Schisandra propinqua subsp. sinensis

Schisandra chinensis subsp. sinensis, commonly known as Schisandra or "five-flavor fruit," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine for its adaptogenic properties, it is used to enhance mental clarity, reduce fatigue, and support liver health through its unique complex of lignans.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra propinqua subsp. sinensis
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Austrobaileyales, it is classified under the family Schisandraceae and the genus Schisandra. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies sinensis within the species Schisandra chinensis.
