Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Schisandra, is valued for the therapeutic properties found within its specific morphological structures. The aerial parts of the plant, which include the woody vines, leaves, and flowers, serve as the primary growth framework and are often studied for their overall botanical characteristics. However, the most significant medicinal component is the fruits, which appear as clusters of small, red, berry-like drupes. These fruits are highly concentrated with bioactive compounds, making them the primary part used in traditional medicine to harness the plant's adaptogenic benefits.
