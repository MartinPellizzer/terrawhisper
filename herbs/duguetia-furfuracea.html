Duguetia furfuracea

Duguetia furfuracea, commonly known as the "velvet leaf" in certain tropical regions, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Characterized by its distinctive textured foliage, this species is frequently utilized for its bioactive properties to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various digestive issues in indigenous medicine.

  • Scientific name: Duguetia furfuracea
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Duguetia.
