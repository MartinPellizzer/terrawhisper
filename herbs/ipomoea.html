Ipomoea

Bush morning glory

Ipomoea, commonly known as morning glory, is a diverse genus of flowering plants celebrated for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond their ornamental beauty, various species hold significant medicinal value in traditional practices, offering diverse pharmacological properties used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various other systemic ailments worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is the genus Ipomoea.
