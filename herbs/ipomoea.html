Bush morning glory, scientifically known as Ipomoea, is a versatile climbing plant celebrated for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, various species possess significant medicinal properties, traditionally used in herbal remedies to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, making it a vital resource in ethnobotany.
