Distribution

This plant is naturally found within specific regions of the Indo-China peninsula. Its geographical range is primarily concentrated in certain parts of Cambodia. Additionally, the species can be located within the borders of Myanmar. These locations represent the documented areas where the plant thrives in the wild.
