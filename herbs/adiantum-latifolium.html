Adiantum latifolium

Adiantum latifolium, commonly known as the broad-leaf maidenhair fern, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, it is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions. Understanding its bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Adiantum latifolium
  • Family: Pteridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, it is further categorized into the family Pteridaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Adiantum.
