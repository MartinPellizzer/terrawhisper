What's the taxonomical classification of Cremanthodium nobile?

Cremanthodium nobile belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Cremanthodium, and its species designation, nobile.
