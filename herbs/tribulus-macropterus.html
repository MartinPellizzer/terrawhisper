Tribulus macropterus

Tribulus macropterus, commonly known as the large-fruited puncture vine, is a resilient medicinal herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various cultures to support reproductive health, enhance vitality, and possess potent antioxidant properties, making it a significant subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Tribulus macropterus
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zygophyllales, it is further categorized into the family Zygophyllaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tribulus as Tribulus macropterus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across the African continent. Within Northern Africa, it can be found growing in the territories of Algeria, Egypt, and Libya. Its presence extends further west into Morocco, marking a significant part of its regional reach. Additionally, the species is distributed within West Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting Mali. Together, these regions define the primary geographical footprint of Tribulus macropterus.
