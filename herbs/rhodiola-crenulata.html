Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several high-altitude regions of Asia. Within China, it is primarily found in the South-Central areas, as well as throughout Qinghai and Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the East Himalaya region. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Nepal. Together, these locations highlight the plant's preference for the rugged mountainous terrains of the eastern Himalayas.

Plant Parts

This plant, Rhodiola crenulata, is characterized by a complex underground structure where the most medicinal components are found. The primary focus of its therapeutic value lies within the rhizomes, which often develop into thick, fleshy roots that store essential nutrients. Extending from these central structures are various roots, including delicate hairy roots that assist in nutrient absorption from the alpine soil. While the stems emerge above the ground to support the foliage, it is the robust development of the roots and their interconnected rhizomes that defines the plant's unique botanical profile.
