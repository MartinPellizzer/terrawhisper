Rhodiola crenulata

Rhodiola crenulata, commonly known as crenulate rhodiola, is a potent medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of China. Renowned for its adaptogenic properties, it helps the body manage stress, enhances cognitive function, and promotes longevity. This resilient plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its antioxidant benefits.

  • Scientific name: Rhodiola crenulata
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Saxifrages, falling into the family Crassulaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Rhodiola.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several high-altitude regions of Asia. Within China, it is primarily found in the South-Central areas, as well as throughout Qinghai and Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the East Himalaya region. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Nepal. Together, these locations highlight the plant's preference for the rugged mountainous terrains of the eastern Himalayas.
