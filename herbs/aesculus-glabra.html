Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across multiple continents. In North America, it can be found growing in the eastern region of Canada, specifically within Ontario. Its range extends significantly into the North-Central United States, where it is documented in Illinois, Iowa, and Kansas. Beyond the American continent, the species also establishes itself in Middle Europe, particularly in Germany. Consequently, the presence of Aesculus glabra reflects a wide environmental adaptability across these various territories.
