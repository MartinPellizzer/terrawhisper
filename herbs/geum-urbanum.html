Wood Avens

Geum urbanum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (38 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Wood Avens, scientifically known as Geum urbanum, is a perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Often found in woodland edges, this plant features delicate yellow flowers and distinctive hooked seeds. Historically valued in traditional herbalism, it possesses astringent properties used to treat various ailments involving inflammation and digestion.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several regions within Northern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Denmark. Its distribution also extends to the northern territories of Finland and Norway. In the western parts of the continent, the species is well-established in both Great Britain and Ireland. Together, these locations highlight the plant's affinity for the Northern European climate.

This plant, commonly known as wood avens, utilizes different components for its medicinal properties. Above the ground, the aerial parts consist of the leaves, stems, and flowers, which comprise the visible structure of the plant and are frequently collected for decoctions or infusions.
