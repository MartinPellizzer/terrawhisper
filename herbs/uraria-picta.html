Uraria picta

Uraria picta, commonly known as "erva-de-ratim" in Brazil, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional folk medicine. Primarily used for its potent diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently employed to treat urinary tract infections, kidney stones, and various inflammatory conditions, offering a natural approach to renal health.

  • Scientific name: Uraria picta
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Uraria.

This plant is primarily found within the diverse ecosystems of West Tropical Africa. Its distribution includes specific populations located in the nation of Benin. Furthermore, it can be identified growing across the territories of Burkina and Gambia. The species is also documented to inhabit parts of Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends to the coastal regions of Guinea-Bissau.
