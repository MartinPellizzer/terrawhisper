Fabiana bryoides

Fabiana bryoides, commonly known as mossy fabiana, is a distinctive evergreen shrub native to the rugged Andean landscapes. Characterized by its dense, cushion-like growth habit and tiny, scale-like leaves, this resilient plant plays a vital role in high-altitude ecosystems, offering unique medicinal properties used in traditional regional healing.

  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
