Canarina eminii

Canarina eminii, commonly known as the "Climbing Cana," is a vigorous evergreen climber native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its stunning, bell-shaped orange flowers, this botanical marvel is prized in ornamental gardening. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, it holds significant ecological value, supporting diverse pollinators within its lush canopy.

  • Scientific name: Canarina eminii
  • Family: Campanulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales and the family Campanulaceae, it is classified under the genus Canarina. Its specific species designation is Canarina eminii.

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the territories of Burundi and Rwanda.
