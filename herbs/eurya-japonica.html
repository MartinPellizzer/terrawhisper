Eurya japonica

East asian eurya

Eurya japonica, commonly known as Japanese Eurya, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its glossy foliage and delicate white flowers, this plant holds significant medicinal value. Traditionally used in various herbal remedies, it offers potential therapeutic benefits, making it a subject of botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Eurya japonica
  • Family: Pentaphylacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Pentaphylacaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Eurya.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across several key regions of Eastern Asia. Its range includes the Nansei-shotō islands and the broader territory of Japan. It is also widely distributed throughout Korea. In mainland Asia, the species can be located in Southeast China. Finally, its geographical reach extends to the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the Assam region.

Plant Parts

This plant, Eurya japonica, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its glossy, dark green leaves. These leaves are small, elliptical, and characterized by their serrated margins, serving as the main component used in various traditional preparations. Due to the presence of specific bioactive compounds within the foliage, the leaves are often processed to extract therapeutic agents used in treating diverse ailments.
