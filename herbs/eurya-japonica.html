Plant Parts

This plant, Eurya japonica, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties derived from its glossy, dark green leaves. These leaves are small, elliptical, and characterized by their serrated margins, serving as the main component used in various traditional preparations. Due to the presence of specific bioactive compounds within the foliage, the leaves are often processed to extract therapeutic agents used in treating diverse ailments.
