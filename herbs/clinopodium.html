Calamint, scientifically known as Clinopodium, is a versatile genus of aromatic herbs within the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its distinctive minty fragrance, these plants offer significant therapeutic potential. Often used in traditional medicine, Clinopodium species possess antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, making them valuable subjects for both herbalism and pharmacology.
