Kalanchoe ceratophylla

Kalanchoe ceratophylla, commonly known as the Horned Kalanchoe, is a striking succulent native to Madagascar. Characterized by its unique, fleshy leaves and distinctive horn-like projections, this resilient plant is prized in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its purported antioxidant properties and its ability to support various healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Kalanchoe ceratophylla
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Saxifrages and the family Crassulaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Kalanchoe, specifically as the species Kalanchoe ceratophylla.
