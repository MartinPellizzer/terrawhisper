Panicum hemitomon

Panicum hemitomon, commonly known as the semi-cut panic grass, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin irritations and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential within herbal pharmacology and natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Panicum hemitomon
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Panicum, specifically as the species Panicum hemitomon.
