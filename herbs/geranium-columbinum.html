Geranium columbinum

Geranium columbinum, commonly known as Columbine Geranium, is a delicate perennial native to North America. Renowned for its striking, deeply lobed foliage and vibrant blooms, this medicinal plant has long been studied for its potential therapeutic properties, offering unique botanical characteristics essential for both gardeners and herbal enthusiasts alike.

  • Scientific name: Geranium columbinum
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Geranium.

Distribution

This plant is native to several distinct regions within Northern Europe. Its geographical presence is notably established across the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. It can also be found growing within the northern landscapes of Finland. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, the plant extends its range into the rugged territories of Norway.
