Minthostachys verticillata

Minthostachys verticillata, commonly known as Muña, is a fragrant perennial herb native to the Andean highlands. Valued in traditional South American medicine, this aromatic plant offers potent digestive, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Its refreshing scent and therapeutic versatility make it a vital component in both herbal remedies and culinary traditions.

  • Scientific name: Minthostachys verticillata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
