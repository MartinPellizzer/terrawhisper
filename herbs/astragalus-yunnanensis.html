Astragalus yunnanensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Astragalus yunnanensis?

Astragalus yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Astragalus, and its unique species designation, yunnanensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Astragalus yunnanensis has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by pinnately compound leaves, trifoliate leaflets, and dense, papilionaceous flowers clustered in racemes. The leaves typically feature several pairs of small, oval leaflets that are arranged symmetrically along a central rachis. Its stems are often woody at the base and may exhibit fine pubescence that covers the foliage and reproductive structures. The flowers are distinctive for their pea-like structure, often displaying shades of purple or white depending on the specific population. Small, legume-type pods develop from the ovaries following pollination to facilitate seed dispersal.
