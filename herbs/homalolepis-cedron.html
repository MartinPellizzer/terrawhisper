Homalolepis cedron

Homalolepis cedron, commonly known as the "cedron" or "mountain cedar," is a significant medicinal shrub found in diverse tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering vast pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Homalolepis cedron
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Sapindales and the family Simaroubaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Homalolepis, specifically the species Homalolepis cedron.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range primarily concentrated within the tropical regions of Central America. It can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of Costa Rica, El Salvador, and Honduras. Additionally, its distribution extends southward through the territory of Panamá. Beyond the mainland, the species is also documented in the Caribbean region. Specifically, it inhabits the islands of Trinidad and Tobago.
