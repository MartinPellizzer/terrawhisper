Artemisia selengensis

Artemisia selengensis, commonly known as a unique member of the wormwood family, is a medicinal plant native to specific regional habitats. Renowned for its complex chemical profile, it contains bioactive compounds studied for their therapeutic potential, including antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, making it a subject of significant botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia selengensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Artemisia as the species Artemisia selengensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Eastern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within Belarus. Moving eastward, the species can be found in the Siberian regions of both Buryatiya and Chita. The plant also extends into the Russian Far East, occupying territories such as Amur. Finally, its range reaches into the Khabarovsk region, completing its widespread distribution.
