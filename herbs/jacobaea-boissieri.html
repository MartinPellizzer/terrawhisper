Jacobaea boissieri

Jacobaea boissiei, commonly known as the Boisssier's ragwort, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific Mediterranean regions. Characterized by its bright, daisy-like yellow flowers and deeply lobed leaves, this plant is studied for its unique phytochemical profile, offering potential insights into traditional herbal medicine and bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Jacobaea boissieri
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Jacobaea.
