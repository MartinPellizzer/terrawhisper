What's the taxonomical classification of Bulbophyllum griffithii?

Bulbophyllum griffithii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Asparagales. This specific plant is a member of the family Orchidaceae, falling within the genus Bulbophyllum, and is identified by its unique species designation, griffithii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Bulbophyllum griffithii has creeping rhizomes that produce small, ovoid pseudobulbs, each bearing a single, apical, lanceolate leaf. The inflorescence is a dense, many-flowered raceme that arises from the base of the pseudobulbs. Its flowers are characterized by small, yellowish or brownish petals and a highly distinctive, mobile labellum that responds to touch. The sepals are often longer than the petals and provide the primary visual structure of the bloom. These morphological traits allow the plant to thrive in its specific epiphytic ecological niche.
