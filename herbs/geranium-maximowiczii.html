Geranium maximowiczii

What's the taxonomical classification of Geranium maximowiczii?

Geranium maximowiczii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific genus, Geranium, and its unique species designation, maximowiczii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Geranium maximowiczii has erect, rhizomatous stems that typically reach a height of 30 to 60 centimeters and are covered in fine hairs. The leaves are palmately lobed with five to seven distinct segments that possess deeply serrated margins. During the flowering season, the plant produces clusters of five-petaled flowers that vary in color from pale pink to deep lavender. These blossoms feature prominent stamens and are supported by long, slender pedicels extending from the leaf axils. The overall structure of the foliage is dense and spreading, forming a lush mound of textured greenery.
