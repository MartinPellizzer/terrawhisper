Bridelia scleroneura

Bridelia scleroneura, commonly known as the "wild olive" in certain regions, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical forests. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves, bark, and roots are utilized to treat diverse ailments, including inflammation, digestive issues, and skin infections, offering significant pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Bridelia scleroneura
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Malpighiales and the family Phyllanthaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Bridelia.

Common Names

This plant, Bridelia scleroneura, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the hard-leaved brideia, while in French, it is known as brideia à feuilles dures. Locally, in Swahili, it is called mkwaju mwekundu, and in various West African contexts, it is identified as kwaye.
