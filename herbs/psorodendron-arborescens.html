Mojave Indigobush, scientifically known as Psorodendron arborescens, is a resilient desert shrub prized for its deep blue blossoms and profound healing properties. Traditionally used by indigenous healers to treat respiratory ailments and skin inflammation, this botanical treasure offers a potent blend of antioxidants, making it a cornerstone of desert ethnobotany.

Distribution

This plant, known as Psorospermadendron arborescens, exhibits a specific range across the arid regions of North America. In the Southwestern United States, its presence is documented within the state of Arizona. It also grows naturally throughout various parts of California. Additionally, the species can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Nevada. Beyond the U.S. border, its distribution extends into the Northwest region of Mexico.
