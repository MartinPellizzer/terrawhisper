Mojave Indigobush, scientifically known as Psorodendron arborescens, is a resilient desert shrub prized for its deep blue blossoms and profound healing properties. Traditionally used by indigenous healers to treat respiratory ailments and skin inflammation, this botanical treasure offers a potent blend of antioxidants, making it a cornerstone of desert ethnobotany.
