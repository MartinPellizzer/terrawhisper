Phaseolus ritensis

Phaseolus ritensis, commonly known as the Riten bean, is a specialized plant species native to specific high-altitude regions. As a member of the Fabaceae family, it plays a vital role in its ecosystem. Renowned for its potential medicinal properties, this plant offers unique bioactive compounds for therapeutic research.

  • Scientific name: Phaseolus ritensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Fabales, it falls under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized under the genus Phaseolus.
