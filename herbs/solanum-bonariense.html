Solanum bonariense

Solanum bonariense, commonly known as a member of the nightshade family, is a botanical species primarily recognized for its unique ecological niche. While less documented in mainstream pharmacology, this plant holds significant potential in ethnobotanical research, offering intriguing possibilities for discovering novel bioactive compounds and diverse medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Solanum bonariense
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Solanales, and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Solanum, which includes the species Solanum bonaeriense.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions in the Mediterranean basin. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found throughout Spain and specifically within the Baleares islands. Its presence extends into Southeastern Europe, where it is documented in Italy. Furthermore, the species is established in Northern Africa, appearing in both Algeria and Tunisia. This range highlights the plant's ability to thrive in diverse Mediterranean coastal environments.
