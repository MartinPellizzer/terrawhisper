Cullen plicatum

Cullen plicatum, commonly known as the "blue-flowered Cullen," is a versatile medicinal herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, digestive ailments, and skin conditions, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Cullen plicatum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cullen, specifically as the species Cullen plicatum.

Distribution

This plant, known as Cullen plicatum, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the North African region. It can be found growing within the borders of Algeria and Egypt. Its range also extends across the territories of Libya and Morocco. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the landscape of Western Sahara. Collectively, these locations define its presence within the northernmost parts of the African continent.
