Rhododendron hainanense

Rhododendron hainanense, commonly known as a rare medicinal shrub, is an endemic species native to the tropical forests of Hainan Island. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in regional practices for its unique phytochemical properties and potential therapeutic applications in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron hainanense
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is classified under the family Ericaceae. Within this family, it is specifically identified by the genus Rhododendron.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within specific regions of East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is documented in the tropical province of Hainan. It also extends across the broader geographical area known as China Southeast. Beyond Chinese borders, the species can be found within the Indo-China region.
