Chrozophora oblongifolia

Chrozophora oblongifolia, commonly known as the slender chrozophora, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and digestive issues, offering valuable bioactive compounds for contemporary pharmacological research.

  • Native range: Africa
