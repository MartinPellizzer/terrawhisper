Thelypteris nipponica

Thelypteris nipponica, commonly known as the Japanese marsh fern, is a resilient pteridophyte native to East Asian wetlands. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential medicinal applications, focusing on its phytochemical profile and its ability to offer diverse therapeutic benefits.
