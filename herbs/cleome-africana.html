Cleome africana

Cleome africana, commonly known as African spiderflower, is a versatile medicinal herb widely used in traditional African medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, the plant is frequently employed to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to gastrointestinal issues, offering a natural resource for holistic healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cleome africana
  • Family: Cleomaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cleome africana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Brassicales and the family Cleomaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Cleome.
