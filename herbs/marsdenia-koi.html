What's the taxonomical classification of Marsdenia koi?

Marsdenia koi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Marsdenia, and its specific species designation, koi.

What are the pharmacological activities of Marsdenia koi?

The chemical constituents within Marsdenia koi interact with the reproductive system to induce uterine contractions. These biological mechanisms can effectively disrupt the maintenance of a developing embryo during the initial stages of gestation.
