Primulina fimbrisepala

Primulina fimbrisepala, commonly known as a rare gem of the Gesneriaceae family, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific subtropical habitats. Characterized by its unique fringed sepals, this species holds significant botanical interest. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in traditional pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Primulina fimbrisepala
  • Family: Gesneriaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Gesneriaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Primulina, specifically as the species Primulina fimbrisepaala.
