Yellow lupin

Lupinus luteus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (117 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Yellow lupin, scientifically known as Lupinus luteus, is a versatile perennial legume valued for its medicinal and nutritional properties. Rich in alkaloids and essential amino acids, it has been traditionally used to support digestive health and manage inflammation. This vibrant plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies worldwide.
