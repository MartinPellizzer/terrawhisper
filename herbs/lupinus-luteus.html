Lupinus luteus

Yellow lupin

Lupinus luteus, commonly known as the yellow lupin, is a versatile perennial legume valued for its striking golden blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant serves medicinal purposes due to its alkaloid content. It is historically utilized in traditional practices to support digestive health and manage various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Lupinus luteus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Lupinus, specifically as the species Lupinus luteus.
