White-flowerring Korean dandelion, scientifically known as Taraxacum coreanum, is a resilient perennial native to the Korean Peninsula. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is prized for its therapeutic properties, often utilized to support liver health, reduce inflammation, and promote detoxification through its nutrient-rich leaves and roots.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted in the Primorye region. Its range extends into China, where it can be found in both Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Furthermore, the species is widely recognized as part of the natural flora in Korea. Together, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of Taraxacum coreanum.
