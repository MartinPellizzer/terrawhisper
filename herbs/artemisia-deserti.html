Artemisia deserti

Artemisia deserti, commonly known as desert sagebrush, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the arid landscapes of North America. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this medicinal plant has been utilized by indigenous cultures for centuries to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and various skin inflammations through traditional remedies.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia deserti
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Artemisia and the species name Artemisia deserti.
