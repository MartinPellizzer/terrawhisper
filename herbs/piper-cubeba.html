Piper cubeba

Cubeb

Piper cubeba, commonly known as Cubeb pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its pungent, aromatic berries, it has been used for centuries in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. This spice offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Piper cubeba
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Piperales and the family Piperaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Piper.
