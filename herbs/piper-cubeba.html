Cubeb, scientifically known as Piper cubeba, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional herbalism. Native to tropical regions, its aromatic berries possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments and digestive issues, this flavorful spice serves as both a culinary staple and therapeutic remedy.
