Reichardia tingitana

False sowthistle

Reichardia tingitana, commonly known as the Tangier Reichardia, is a rare, succulent medicinal plant endemic to the coastal regions of Morocco. Highly valued in traditional folk medicine, it possesses unique phytochemical properties used to treat various ailments, making it a significant subject of interest for pharmacological research and conservation.

  • Scientific name: Reichardia tingitana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Reichardia, with the species name tingitana.
