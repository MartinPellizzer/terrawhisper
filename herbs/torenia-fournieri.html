Torenia fournieri

Torenia fournieri, commonly known as Flesh Flower, is a charming annual prized for its trumpet-shaped blooms and velvety foliage. While primarily grown as an ornamental bedding plant in shady gardens, its vibrant colors and compact habit make it a favorite for containers, adding lush texture to any landscape.

  • Scientific name: Torenia fournieri
  • Family: Linderniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and order Lamiales, it is a member of the Linderniaceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Torenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Asia and Africa. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted specifically within the Congo. Moving across the continent to Asia, it is widely distributed throughout China, including the South-Central and Southeast regions. The plant's reach extends further east to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Finally, its distribution encompasses the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be found in Bangladesh.
