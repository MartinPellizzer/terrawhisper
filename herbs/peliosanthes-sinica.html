What's the taxonomical classification of Peliosanthes sinica?

Peliosanthes sinica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic descent, it falls under the order Asparagales and is a member of the family Asparagaceae. Finally, its specific biological identity is defined by the genus Peliosanthes and the species sinica.
