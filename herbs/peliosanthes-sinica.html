Peliosanthes sinica

Peliosanthes sinica, commonly known as the Chinese liverwort, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional Eastern practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, promote detoxification, and support digestive health, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Peliosanthes sinica
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Peliosanthes sinica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Peliosanthes.
