This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is documented in both Hainan and the Southeast regions of the country. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be found in both India and Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is located within the Indo-China region, specifically encompassing the Andaman Islands. These varied locations highlight its ability to thrive across diverse tropical environments.
