Acanthus ebracteatus subsp. ebracteatus

Acanthus ebracteatus subsp. ebracteatus, commonly known as the ebracteate acanthus, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Characterized by its unique leaf structure, this subspecies possesses bioactive compounds used to address various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential within the diverse botanical landscape of its native habitat.

  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Lamiales and falls into the family Acanthaceae.
