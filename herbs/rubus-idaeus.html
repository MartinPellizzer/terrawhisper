Rubus idaeus

Red raspberry

Rubus idaeus, commonly known as the red raspberry, is a versatile deciduous shrub celebrated for its nutrient-dense fruit. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal value. Rich in antioxidants, vitamins, and polyphenols, it is traditionally used to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Rubus idaeus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Rubus idaeus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Rubus.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Rubus idaeus or the red raspberry, offers various components that are utilized for their therapeutic potential. The young shoots are often studied for their nutritional density and potential antioxidant properties, providing a fresh source of bioactive compounds. The fruits themselves are widely consumed not only for their flavor but also for their high concentration of vitamins and minerals. Furthermore, the berries extract is a highly concentrated form used in medicinal preparations to harness potent polyphenols and anthocyanins, which are valued for their ability to combat oxidative stress in the body.
