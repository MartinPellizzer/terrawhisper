Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Rubus idaeus or the red raspberry, offers various components that are utilized for their therapeutic potential. The young shoots are often studied for their nutritional density and potential antioxidant properties, providing a fresh source of bioactive compounds. The fruits themselves are widely consumed not only for their flavor but also for their high concentration of vitamins and minerals. Furthermore, the berries extract is a highly concentrated form used in medicinal preparations to harness potent polyphenols and anthocyanins, which are valued for their ability to combat oxidative stress in the body.
