Lilium longiflorum

Loa kèn trắng

Lilium longiflorum, commonly known as the Easter Lily, is a perennial flowering plant celebrated for its striking white, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant cultural importance and has been utilized in traditional medicine for its perceived calming properties and ability to soothe various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Lilium longiflorum
  • Family: Liliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Liliaceae and the genus Lilium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Lilium longiflorum, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. In Indonesia, it is referred to as Bakung Paskah (id), while in Japan, it is known as テッポウユリ (ja). In Sweden, the plant is called Basunliljolm (sv), and in Vietnam, it is commonly known as Loa kèn (vi). Additionally, in Ireland, it is identified by the name lile na Cásca (ga).

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions located throughout Eastern Asia. Its primary geographical distribution includes the islands of Japan and the Korean Peninsula. Furthermore, it can be found spreading across the Nansei-shoto island chain. The species also inhabits the remote Ogasawara-shoto archipelago. Finally, its natural range extends southward to include the territory of Taiwan.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Lilium longiflorum, derives its primary structural and storage importance from its underground bulbs. These bulbs serve as the essential organ for nutrient storage, allowing the plant to survive dormant periods and providing the necessary energy for the development of its striking, trumpet-shaped flowers. In many botanical contexts, the bulb is the most significant part of the plant's anatomy, acting as a specialized stem base composed of fleshy, scale-like leaves that protect the embryonic bud.
