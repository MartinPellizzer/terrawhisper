Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Lilium longiflorum, derives its primary structural and storage importance from its underground bulbs. These bulbs serve as the essential organ for nutrient storage, allowing the plant to survive dormant periods and providing the necessary energy for the development of its striking, trumpet-shaped flowers. In many botanical contexts, the bulb is the most significant part of the plant's anatomy, acting as a specialized stem base composed of fleshy, scale-like leaves that protect the embryonic bud.
