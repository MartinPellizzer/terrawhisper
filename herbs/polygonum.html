Polygonum

Polygonum, commonly known as knotweed, is a diverse genus of herbaceous plants valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, various species are utilized to treat skin conditions, promote wound healing, and support cardiovascular health. Its resilient nature makes it a significant subject in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Polygonum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Caryophyllales, it is further categorized into the family Polygonaceae. Ultimately, it is identified under the genus Polygonum.
