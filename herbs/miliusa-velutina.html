Miliusa velutina

Miliusa velutinina, commonly known as a member of the Annonaceae family, is a significant medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian tropical forests. Renowned for its bioactive phytochemicals, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology and natural drug development.
