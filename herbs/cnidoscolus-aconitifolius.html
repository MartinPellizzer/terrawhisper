Cnidoscolus aconitifolius

Cnidoscolus aconitifolius, commonly known as chaya, is a nutrient-dense perennial shrub native to Mexico. Renowned for its exceptional protein, vitamin, and mineral content, this "superfood" offers significant medicinal potential. When properly prepared, it serves as a sustainable dietary staple, providing vital health benefits and supporting overall nutritional wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cnidoscolus aconitifolius
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Cnidoscolus.
