Spinach tree, scientifically known as Cnidoscolus aconitifolius, is a nutrient-dense perennial shrub native to Mexico. Highly valued for its edible, protein-rich leaves, this resilient plant offers significant medicinal benefits, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. It serves as a vital superfood, supporting digestive health and overall nutritional wellness globally.
