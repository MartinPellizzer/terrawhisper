Sambucus nigra

Sambucus nigra, commonly known as elderberry, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Renowned for its potent antioxidant content, it is frequently used to support immune health and alleviate respiratory symptoms. Both the dark berries and fragrant flowers offer therapeutic benefits in traditional herbal medicine worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Sambucus nigra
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Sambucus nigra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Dipsacales. Finally, it is a member of the family Viburnaceae and falls under the genus Sambucus.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions in Northern Europe. In the western parts of the continent, it can be found growing throughout Great Britain and Ireland. Moving further north, the species is also established within the territories of Norway and Denmark. Additionally, its presence extends into the easternmost reaches of the region, including Finland. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse Northern European climates.
