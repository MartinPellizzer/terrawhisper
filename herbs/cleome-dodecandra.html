Cleome dodecandra

Cleome dodecandra, commonly known as the spider flower or cleome, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and inflammation, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Cleome dodecandra
  • Family: Cleomaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Cleome dodecandra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Brassicales. Furthermore, it is a member of the Cleomaceae family and falls under the genus Cleome.

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several distinct global regions. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented within the borders of Germany. Moving toward the Russian Far East, the species is found in the Primorye region. Its reach extends into Eastern Canada, specifically spanning the provinces of Ontario and Québec.
