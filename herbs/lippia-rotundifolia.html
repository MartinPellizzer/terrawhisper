Lippia rotundifolia

Lippia rotundifolia, commonly known as bushy verbena, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, this plant is widely used in traditional medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and skin inflammations. Its potent phytochemical properties offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Lippia rotundifolia
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Lippia rotundifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Verbenaceae and falls under the genus Lippia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of South America. In the western part of the continent, it can be found specifically within the borders of Bolivia. Its presence is also widespread across Brazil, spanning multiple distinct territories. This includes significant populations in the West-Central region and the Northeast region of the country. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the Southeast region of Brazil.
