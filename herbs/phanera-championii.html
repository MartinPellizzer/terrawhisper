Plant Parts

This plant, Phanera championii, is a climbing species characterized by its robust growth habit. The medicinal properties and structural utility of the species are often associated with its specialized anatomy, particularly the vine stem. This stem is a form of rattan, a climbing woody vine that provides the plant with the necessary support to reach higher levels of the forest canopy.
