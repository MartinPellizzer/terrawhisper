Polygonum senticosum

What's the taxonomical classification of Polygonum senticosum?

Polygonum senticosum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it falls into the subclass Magnoliidae and is further categorized under the order Caryophyllales. It is a member of the family Polygonaceae, which places it within the genus Polygonum, and is specifically identified by the species name senticosum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Polygonum senticosum has slender, erect stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and are characterized by a reddish tint and ocreae at the nodes. The leaves are small, lanceolate to narrowly elliptical in shape, and possess entire margins with a smooth texture. Tiny, inconspicuous flowers are arranged in dense terminal clusters or axillary racemes. These blossoms are generally white or pale pink and are supported by delicate pedicels. The plant often develops a branched structure as it matures, spreading slightly from its central base.
