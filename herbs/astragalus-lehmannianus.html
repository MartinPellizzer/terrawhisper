Astragalus lehmannianus

Astragalus lehmannianus, commonly known as a species of milkvetch, is a resilient medicinal plant native to specific high-altitude regions. Valued in traditional herbalism for its bioactive compounds, it offers potential therapeutic benefits. Understanding its unique phytochemical profile is essential for exploring its role in supporting immune health and longevity.
