Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Liliales, it is specifically situated in the family Liliaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Lilium as the subspecies Liliium leichtlinii subsp. maximowiczii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye region. Finally, its habitat includes various parts of Japan, completing its extensive East Asian footprint.
