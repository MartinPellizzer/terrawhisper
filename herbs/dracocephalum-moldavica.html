Moldavian Dragon-head, scientifically known as Dracocephalum moldavica, is a resilient perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Characterized by its aromatic foliage and delicate blue flowers, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional herbalism for its calming properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits for soothing the nervous system and digestion.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Moldavian dragonhead, is a versatile medicinal herb used in various traditional practices. The whole plant possesses distinct therapeutic qualities, though it is most frequently utilized in the form of its dried aerial parts to preserve its chemical potency. The aromatic leaves are particularly rich in bioactive compounds, which serve as the primary source for extracting its fragrant essential oil.
