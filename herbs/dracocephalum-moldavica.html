Dracocephalum moldavica

Dracocephalum moldavica, commonly known as Moldavian dragonhead, is a perennial herb within the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate blue flowers, this medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, playing a vital role in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Dracocephalum moldavica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Dracocephalum.
