Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several key regions in East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is notable in the South-Central provinces and the Southeast coastal areas. It can also be found growing on the tropical island of Hainan. Beyond the borders of China, its range extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, the species is documented to inhabit the landscapes of Vietnam.

Plant Parts

This plant, Eurya ciliata, is primarily recognized for the medicinal properties found within its leaf. The leaves are typically small, glossy, and elliptic in shape, serving as the main component used in various traditional herbal preparations. In many therapeutic applications, the extracts derived from these leaves are utilized for their bioactive compounds, making the leaf the most significant part of the plant for medicinal purposes.
