Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in South and Southeast Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, Avicennia alba can be found growing in both India and Bangladesh. Its presence extends into the Indo-China region, where it is documented in Myanmar and Thailand. Additionally, the species is specifically located around the Andaman Islands. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's preference for coastal or mangrove environments within these tropical territories.
