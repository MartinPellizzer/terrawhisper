Grona triflora

Grona triflora, commonly known as the three-flowered grona, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Grona triflora
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Grona triflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Grona.

Distribution

This plant, known as Grona triflora, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally within the borders of Benin. Its presence is also documented in the country of Gambia. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout the coastal and inland areas of Ghana. Finally, the plant's range extends to Guinea-Bissau and Guinea.
