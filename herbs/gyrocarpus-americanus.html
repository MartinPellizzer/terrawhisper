Gyrocarpus americanus

Helicopter tree

Gyrocarpus americanus, commonly known as the "wild guava" or "bush guava," is a significant medicinal shrub native to the Caribbean and Central America. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat gastrointestinal ailments, skin infections, and inflammatory conditions through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Gyrocarpus americanus
  • Family: Hernandiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Laurales, it is a member of the family Hernandiaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Gyrocarpus.
