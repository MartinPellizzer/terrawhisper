Cycas beddomei

Cycas beddomei, commonly known as Beddome's Cycad, is a critically endangered gymnosperm endemic to the Eastern Ghats of India. While primarily valued for its unique evolutionary significance and biodiversity, this rare plant holds potential in traditional ethnobotany, representing a vital, yet fragile, component of India's natural medicinal heritage.

  • Family: Cycadaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
