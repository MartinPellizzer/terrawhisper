Reproduction

The reproduction of Tithonia diversifolia is characterized by a flowering process that can occur throughout the year, with a notable period starting in April. The plant produces yellow flowers that range in length from 4 cm to 7 cm and possess a width between 5 cm and 10 cm. These blooms utilize a biotic pollination syndrome, specifically relying on insects for successful fertilization. Following pollination, the plant develops an indehiscent fruit known as an achene. These fruits are relatively small, with lengths measuring between 0.4 cm and 0.8 cm. The dispersal of these seeds is categorized as anthropochorous, meaning they are spread via human activity.
