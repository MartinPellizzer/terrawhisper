Tree Marigold, Mexican Sunflower

Tithonia diversifolia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (108 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Tree Marigold, Mexican Sunflower, scientifically known as Tithonia diversifolia, is a vibrant, sun-loving perennial celebrated for its brilliant orange blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this resilient plant holds significant medicinal value, traditionally used in various cultures to treat wounds, skin inflammations, and digestive ailments due to its bioactive properties.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tithonia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Tunisia. Its range extends into Macaronesia, where it can be found on both the Canary Islands and Madeira. Furthermore, the species inhabits parts of West Tropical Africa, including Benin. Finally, its distribution continues through the West Tropical African nation of Guinea.

Plant Parts

This plant, Tithonia diversifolia, is widely recognized for the therapeutic potential found within its various anatomical structures. Most medicinal applications focus on the leaves, which are frequently harvested to prepare potent leaf extracts for topical or systemic use. Beyond just the individual leaf, the entire aerial parts of the plant—including the stems and foliage—are often utilized in traditional medicine to harness a broad spectrum of bioactive compounds. Consequently, the concentrated plant extract derived from these components serves as a vital resource in studying its pharmacological properties.
