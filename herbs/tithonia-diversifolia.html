Tithonia diversifolia

Tithonia diversifolia, commonly known as Mexican sunflower, is a versatile perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, wounds, and digestive ailments. Beyond its medicinal properties, this vigorous plant serves as an excellent organic fertilizer in agroecology.

  • Scientific name: Tithonia diversifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tithonia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Tunisia. Its range extends into Macaronesia, where it can be found on both the Canary Islands and Madeira. Furthermore, the species inhabits parts of West Tropical Africa, including Benin. Finally, its distribution continues through the West Tropical African nation of Guinea.
