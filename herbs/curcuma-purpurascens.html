Curcuma purpurascens

Curcumma purpurascens, commonly known as Purple Turmeric, is a striking medicinal plant celebrated for its vibrant, violet-hued rhizomes. Rich in unique bioactive compounds and antioxidants, this botanical treasure is traditionally utilized in holistic wellness to support anti-inflammatory processes, enhance digestive health, and promote overall systemic vitality and healing.

  • Scientific name: Curcuma purpurascens
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, it falls under the family Zingiberaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Curcuma.
