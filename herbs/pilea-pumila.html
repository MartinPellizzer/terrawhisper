Pilea pumila

What's the taxonomical classification of Pilea pumila?

Pilea pumila belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Rosales and the family Urticaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Pilea, and its specific species name, pumila.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pilea pumila has small, opposite, ovate to elliptical leaves with serrated margins and a slightly hairy texture that grow along thin, creeping stems. The foliage is typically a bright green color and possesses a somewhat delicate, herbaceous appearance. These plants often form low-growing mats due to their decumbent growth habit. The tiny, inconspicuous flowers are produced in axillary clusters, often appearing in late spring or summer. This species is characterized by its compact size and fine-textured leaf structure.
