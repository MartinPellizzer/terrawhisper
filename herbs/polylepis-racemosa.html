Polylepis racemosa

Polylepis racemosa, commonly known as the "medicinal climber," is a significant woody shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is extensively used in traditional Ayurveda to treat skin ailments, inflammatory conditions, and digestive disorders, offering a rich source of bioactive phytochemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Polylepis racemosa
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
