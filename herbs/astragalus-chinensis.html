Astragalus chinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Astragalus chinensis?

Astragalus chinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. It is a member of the family Fabaceae, which places it within the genus Astragalus, ultimately identifying it by its specific species name, chinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Astragalus chinensis has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by erect, branched stems that typically reach heights of 30 to 60 centimeters. The leaves are pinnately compound, consisting of several pairs of small, oval-shaped leaflets arranged along a central axis. Small, papilionaceous flowers emerge in dense clusters or racemes, displaying a pale yellow to whitish color. The root system is robust and consists of a thick, fleshy taproot that serves as a primary storage organ for the plant. Fine hairs often cover the stems and foliage, providing a slightly textured surface.
