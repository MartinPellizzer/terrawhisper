Chinese Milkvetch, scientifically known as Astragalus chinensis, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its immune-boosting and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized to support vitality, enhance respiratory health, and promote longevity. This potent botanical offers significant therapeutic potential in modern holistic wellness.
