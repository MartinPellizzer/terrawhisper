Astragalus chinensis

Astragalus chinensis, commonly known as Astragalus or Milkvetch, is a cornerstone of traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its potent immune-modulating properties, this medicinal herb is frequently used to enhance vitality, combat fatigue, and support respiratory health. Its roots contain bioactive compounds vital for promoting longevity and overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Astragalus chinensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. It is further identified by the genus Astragalus, specifically as the species Astragalus chinensis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated across East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing in the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions. Its distribution also extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the territory of Manchuria. Furthermore, the species is documented within the Inner Mongolia region of China. These combined locations define the primary natural habitat for Astragalus chinensis.
