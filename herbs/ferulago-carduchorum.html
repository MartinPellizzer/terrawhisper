Ferulago carduchorum

Ferulago carduchorum, commonly known as carducho, is a perennial herb native to the arid regions of Mexico. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this plant is utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, often prepared as decoctions to treat various ailments within indigenous healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Ferulago carduchorum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Apiales, falling within the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Ferulago.
