Rhododendron fortunei

Rhododendron fortunei, commonly known as Fortune's Rhododendron, is a striking evergreen shrub celebrated for its profuse, bell-shaped blooms. Native to China, this resilient plant features fragrant, pale pink to white flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds significant value in traditional herbal practices and serves as a lush landscape staple.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron fortunei
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae, it is classified under the genus Rhododendron. Specifically, Rhododendron fortunei represents a distinct species within this taxonomic hierarchy.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several distinct regions of Asia. Within China, its distribution spans the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast provinces. Beyond Chinese borders, it can also be found throughout the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, the species is documented to grow in the country of Myanmar. Additionally, its natural habitat extends into the mountainous regions of Vietnam.
