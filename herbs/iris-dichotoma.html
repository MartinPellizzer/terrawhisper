Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Northern and East Asia. Within the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found specifically in the areas of Buryatiya and Chita. Its range extends into the Russian Far East, particularly within the Amur region. In China, the species is widely distributed throughout the South-Central provinces. Furthermore, the plant is also documented to grow in the northern reaches of Inner Mongolia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Iris dichotoma, is primarily valued in traditional medicine for its medicinal properties found within its underground structures. The most significant part used for therapeutic purposes is the rhizomes, which are thick, fleshy stems that grow horizontally beneath the soil surface. In various botanical and pharmacological texts, these structures are also referred to as the rhizoma, a term used to denote the mass of interconnected roots and stems that store essential nutrients and bioactive compounds.
