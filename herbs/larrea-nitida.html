Larrea nitida

Larrea nitiida, commonly known as the creosote bush, is a resilient shrub native to the arid landscapes of the American Southwest. Renowned for its distinctive resinous scent, this hardy plant possesses significant medicinal properties, traditionally utilized in folk remedies for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant therapeutic qualities.

  • Scientific name: Larrea nitida
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zygophyllales, it is classified under the family Zygophyllaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Larrea.
