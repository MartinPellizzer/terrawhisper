Jasminum pentaneurum

What's the taxonomical classification of Jasminum pentaneurum?

Jasminum pentaneurum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Lamiales. It is a member of the family Oleaceae and falls under the genus Jasminum, specifically identified by the species name pentaneurum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Jasminum pentaneurum has woody, climbing stems with opposite, ovate to lanceolate leaves that typically feature five distinct veins originating from the base. The foliage is characterized by a smooth, entire margin and a glossy texture on the upper surface. Its inflorescences consist of small, fragrant white flowers that emerge from axillary clusters. Each flower possesses a tubular corolla that opens into five spreading lobes. The plant often develops aerial roots to assist in its scandent growth habit through surrounding vegetation.
