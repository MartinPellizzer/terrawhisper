Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated primarily across the North African region. Within Northern Africa, its presence is documented in Algeria, Egypt, and Western Sahara. Additionally, the species extends its range into the Macaronesian islands. This includes populations found on the Canary Islands as well as in Cape Verde. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where the species can be located.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as the African milk bush, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties found within its aerial parts. These above-ground structures consist of thick, succulent, grayish-green stems that are often characterized by a distinct, wavy or "scalloped" appearance. Because the plant stores moisture in these fleshy aerial segments, it is highly adapted to arid environments, and it is from these specialized tissues that the milky, caustic latex is harvested for various traditional applications.
