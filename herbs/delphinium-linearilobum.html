Delphinium linearilobum

Delphinium linearilobum, commonly known as a rare member of the larkspur family, is a specialized medicinal plant indigenous to specific montane regions. Renowned for its unique floral morphology, it contains bioactive alkaloids. Traditionally used in ethnobotany, this species holds significant potential for pharmacological research and future therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Delphinium linearilobum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Delphinium.
