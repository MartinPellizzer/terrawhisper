Pouteria ramiflora

Pouteria ramiflora, commonly known as the White Sapote or simply Sapote, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree prized for its creamy, sweet flesh. Beyond its culinary appeal, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering various medicinal properties used in traditional practices to support wellness and promote overall holistic health.

  • Scientific name: Pouteria ramiflora
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Pouteria ramiflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Sapotaecae and falls under the genus Pouteria.
