Odontonema tubaeforme

Odontonema tubaeforme, commonly known as the Variegated Buttonbush, is a striking tropical shrub prized for its ornamental beauty. Beyond its vibrant, tubular flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in various cultures, it is studied for its potential medicinal properties, particularly regarding its bioactive phytochemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Odontonema tubaeforme
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Lamiales, it is further categorized into the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Odontonema.
