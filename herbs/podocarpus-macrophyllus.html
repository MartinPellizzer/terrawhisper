Podocarpus macrophyllus

Podocarpus macrophyllus, commonly known as the Buddhist Pine, is an evergreen conifer valued for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Traditionally used in various cultures, it possesses antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Research explores its bioactive compounds for therapeutic applications, highlighting its significance in both landscaping and natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Podocarpus macrophyllus
  • Family: Podocarpaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is categorized under the order Pinales. Finally, it is classified under the family Podocarpaceae within the genus Podocarpus.
