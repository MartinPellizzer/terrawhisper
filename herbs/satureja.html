Satureja

Savoury

Satureja, commonly known as savory, is a fragrant perennial herb celebrated for its culinary versatility and potent medicinal properties. Rich in essential oils, it serves as a powerful digestive aid, anti-inflammatory agent, and antioxidant. This resilient plant offers a natural way to support wellness through its aromatic, healing essence.

  • Scientific name: Satureja
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is uniquely identified by its genus, Satureja.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Saturaja or Clausena lansium, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its aromatic properties found within its various botanical components. While the leaves and bark are often utilized in topical applications, the most potent medicinal value is concentrated in its essential oils (EOs). These essential oils are extracted from the leaves and seeds, containing complex chemical compounds that provide antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic effects. Because these EOs are highly concentrated, they serve as the primary bioactive constituents that define the plant's therapeutic potential.
