Orychophragmus violaceus

Orychophragmus violaceus, commonly known as the violet-flowered Orychophraagmum, is a resilient medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant is utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in treating various ailments and promoting overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Orychophragmus violaceus
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Orychophraagmum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the northern reaches of Inner Mongolia and the Manchurian region. It is also found throughout China North-Central and extends into the areas of China Southeast. Beyond the mainland, the species can be located in the eastern part of the continent in Japan. Together, these locations highlight a broad range spanning multiple distinct ecological zones.
