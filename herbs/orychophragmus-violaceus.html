Chinese violet cress, scientifically known as Orychophraagmum violaceum, is a traditional medicinal herb highly valued in East Asian pharmacology. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is frequently utilized to relieve inflammation, soothe coughs, and clear heat from the body, playing a vital role in various holistic healing practices.
