Paullinia yoco

Paullinia yoco, commonly known as Yoco, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to South American rainforests. Renowned for its potent stimulant properties, it is traditionally consumed as a restorative infusion. This botanical treasure is valued in folk medicine for enhancing physical energy, mental clarity, and overall vitality.

  • Scientific name: Paullinia yoco
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
