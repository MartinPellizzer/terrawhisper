Sonchus bulbosus, scientifically known as Sonchus bulbosus, is a perennial herbaceous plant belonging to the Asteraceae family. Commonly found in coastal regions and sandy soils, it features distinctive bulbous roots and yellow flowering heads. This resilient species is recognized in traditional medicine for its diverse bioactive properties and therapeutic potential.
