Lithospermum officinale

Lithospermum officinale, commonly known as gromwell, is a perennial herb prized in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its striking blue berries and small flowers, this plant contains allantoin, a compound renowned for its skin-healing properties. It has historically been used to treat inflammation, wounds, and various dermatological conditions effectively.

  • Scientific name: Lithospermum officinale
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Boraginales, belonging to the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Lithospermum.
