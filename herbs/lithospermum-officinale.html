European gromwell, scientifically known as Lithospermum officiinale, is a perennial herb native to Europe. Renowned for its vibrant blue berries and reddish roots, it has a long history in traditional medicine. Its roots contain naphthodianthrones, which are traditionally used for skin ailments, though modern clinical applications remain limited.
