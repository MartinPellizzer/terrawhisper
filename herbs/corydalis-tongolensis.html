Corydalis tongolensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Corydalis tongolensis?

Corydalis tongolenis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales. This plant is a part of the Papaveraceae family, specifically situated within the genus Corydalis, and is identified by its unique species name, tongolenensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Corydalis tongolensis has a herbaceous growth habit characterized by deeply lobed, pinnatifid leaves and zygomorphic flowers featuring long, nectar-bearing spurs. Its inflorescence consists of several nodding flowers arranged along a central stem, often displaying a pale yellow or cream coloration. The most distinctive morphological feature is the elongated, curved spur extending from the base of the corolla.
