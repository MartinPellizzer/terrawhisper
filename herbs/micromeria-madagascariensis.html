Micromeria madagascariensis

Microceria madagascariensis, commonly known as a medicinal herb in various tropical regions, is a significant plant within the Melastomataceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Micromeria madagascariensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Micromeria.

Common Names

This plant, Micromeria madagascariensis, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as Madagascar Melastoma, while in French, it is known as Melastoma de Madagascar. In Swahili, the plant is identified as Mvule wa mbuga, and in Malagasy, it is called Melastoma madagasikarensis.

This plant exhibits a highly localized geographical distribution within the tropical regions of the world. Specifically, its presence is strictly confined to the Western Indian Ocean.
