Aloe castanea

Cat's-tail aloe

Aloe castanea, commonly known as chestnut aloe, is a distinctive succulent native to specific subtropical regions. Characterized by its unique, robust foliage, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional herbalism. It is frequently utilized for its soothing properties, offering therapeutic benefits for skin ailments and promoting overall topical healing.

  • Scientific name: Aloe castanea
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Aloe castanea, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asphodelaceae within the genus Aloe.

Distribution

This plant has a very specific and limited geographical range within the African continent. It is primarily found in the southern region of Africa, where it thrives in particular ecological niches. Specifically, its population can be located within the borders of Eswatini. Additionally, the species is distributed across the Northern Provinces of South Africa.
