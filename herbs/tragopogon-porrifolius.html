Salsify, scientifically known as Tragopoapon porrifolius, is a versatile perennial herb prized for its edible taproot. Often called the "oyster plant" due to its unique flavor, it offers significant nutritional value. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it serves as a rich source of inulin, supporting digestive health and wellness.
