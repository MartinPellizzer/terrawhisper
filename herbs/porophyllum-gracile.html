Porophyllum gracile

Porophyllum gracile, commonly known as the slender porophyllum, is a versatile medicinal herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments and skin inflammations. Its unique chemical profile offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Porophyllum gracile
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Porophyllum.
