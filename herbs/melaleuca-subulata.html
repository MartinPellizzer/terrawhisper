Melaleuca subulata

Melaleuca subulata, commonly known as the needle-leaved paperbark, is a resilient Australian shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Native to coastal regions, this medicinal plant contains essential oils with potent antiseptic and antimicrobial qualities. It is traditionally used in botanical remedies to support skin health and promote healing.

  • Scientific name: Melaleuca subulata
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, falling into the family Myrtaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Melaleuca.
