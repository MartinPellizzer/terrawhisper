Within these leaves, the specialized leaf tissues contain secretory glands that store these medicinal aromatic compounds. The stems provide a woody structural framework that supports the plant's growth and facilitates nutrient transport. During its reproductive phase, the plant produces delicate flowers, which are often arranged in clusters to attract pollinators.
