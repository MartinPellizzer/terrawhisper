Atractylodes koreana

Atractylodes koreana, commonly known as Korean Atractylodes, is a significant medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this perennial plant is prized for its potent rhizomes. It is primarily utilized to support digestive health, strengthen the spleen, and alleviate various gastrointestinal ailments through its therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Atractylodes koreana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Atractylodes as the species Atractylodes koreanas.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across several regions in East Asia. Within the borders of China, it can be found growing in the Manchuria region. It is also documented to inhabit the North-Central part of China. Moving across the sea, the species is widely distributed throughout the Korean peninsula. Therefore, its natural habitat is primarily concentrated in these specific parts of Eastern Asia.
