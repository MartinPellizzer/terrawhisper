Phlomoides maximowiczii

Phlomoides maximowiczii, commonly known as Maximowicz's phlomoides, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to East Asia. Valued in traditional herbal medicine, it contains bioactive compounds with potential therapeutic properties. This species is primarily studied for its pharmacological significance, particularly in addressing inflammatory conditions and supporting various physiological functions.

  • Scientific name: Phlomoides maximowiczii
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Lamiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Phlomoides.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across specific regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing within the Primorye territory. Its range extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the region of Inner Mongolia. Additionally, the species is documented to occur throughout Manchuria in China. These locations define the distinct geographical footprint of Phlomoides maximowiczii.
