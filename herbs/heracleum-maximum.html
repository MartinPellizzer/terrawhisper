Cow parsnip, scientifically known as Heracleum maximum, is a robust perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its large, umbellifer flowers and broad leaves, this plant holds significant historical medicinal value. However, it requires careful handling due to phototoxic sap that can cause severe skin irritation and burns.
