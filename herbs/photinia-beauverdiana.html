What's the taxonomical classification of Photinia beauverdiana?

Photinia beauverdiana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the evolutionary hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The taxonomic lineage continues through the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, this specific plant is defined by its classification within the genus Photinia, specifically identifying it as the species beauverdiana.

    What side effects this plant can have?

    This plant can cause severe gastrointestinal distress, including intense abdominal pain, vomiting, and diarrhea, if any part of it is ingested. The presence of cyanogenic glycosides within its tissues means that consumption may lead to cyanide poisoning. Symptoms of such toxicity can escalate to include dizziness, headaches, and rapid heart rates. In more extreme cases, systemic poisoning may result in respiratory failure or even death. Localized skin irritation or allergic contact dermatitis may also occur upon physical contact with the foliage.
