Artemisia manshurica

What's the taxonomical classification of Artemisia manshurica?

Artemisia manshurica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. It falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, following the evolutionary lineage of the order Asterales. As a member of the family Asteraceae, it is part of one of the most significant plant families in nature. Specifically, this plant is categorized under the genus Artemisia, with its unique biological identity defined by the species name manshurica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Small, tubular flower heads are arranged in dense, terminal clusters that emerge during the summer months. The plant often develops a woody base as it matures, helping it establish a sturdy presence in its native habitat.
