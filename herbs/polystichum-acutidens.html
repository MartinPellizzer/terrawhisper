What's the taxonomical classification of Polystichum acutidens?

Polystichum acutidens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Polypodiales and the family Polypodiaceae. Ultimately, this specific plant is identified by the genus Polystichum and is designated as the species acutidens.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Polystichum acutidens has lanceolate fronds characterized by serrated margins and a deeply pinnatifid structure that extends along a central, robust rachis. Fine, hair-like scales cover the stipe and the base of the blades, offering a tactile contrast to the smooth leaf surfaces.
