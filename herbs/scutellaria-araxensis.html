Scutellaria araxensis

Scutelariia araxenensis, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, belongs to the Lamiaceae family. Native to specific mountainous regions, this plant is valued for its unique phytochemical profile. Researchers study its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications, particularly in developing natural treatments for inflammatory and oxidative stress-related conditions.
