Mussaenda multinervis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic progression continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it within the order Gentianaales. Furthermore, this plant is a member of the family Rubiaceae, specifically falling under the genus Mussaenda, with its unique biological identity defined by the species multinervis.
