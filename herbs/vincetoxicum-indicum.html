Vincetoxicum indicum

Vincetoxicum indicum, commonly known as Indian swallow-wort, is a perennial climbing herb native to tropical Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains diverse bioactive alkaloids. These compounds offer significant pharmacological potential, particularly for their anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antitumor properties, making it a subject of intense scientific interest.

  • Scientific name: Vincetoxicum indicum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Vincetoxicum indicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Gentianales. Finally, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae within the genus Vincetoxicum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several key regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam and throughout Bangladesh. Its presence also extends significantly into the Western Indian Ocean islands. Specifically, it is documented in the island nations of Mauritius and the Seychelles.
