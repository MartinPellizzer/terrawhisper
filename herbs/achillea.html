Achillea

Achillea, commonly known as yarrow, is a resilient perennial herb celebrated for centuries in traditional medicine. Renowned for its feathery foliage and flat-topped flower clusters, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties. This versatile botanical remains a staple in herbalism for supporting skin health and digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Achillea
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Achillea.
