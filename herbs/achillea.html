Yarrow, scientifically known as Achillea, is a versatile perennial herb celebrated for its remarkable healing properties. Renowned since antiquity, this hardy plant features feathery foliage and delicate flower clusters. Traditionally used to staunch bleeding and soothe inflammation, it remains a cornerstone in herbal medicine for supporting skin and digestive health.
