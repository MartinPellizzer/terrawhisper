Mikania laevigata

Mikania laeviigata, commonly known as the "leafy Mika\" or "climbing plant," is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant potential for contemporary pharmacological research and development.
