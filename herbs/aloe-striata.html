Coral Aloe, scientifically known as Aloe striata, is a striking succulent native to South Africa. Distinguished by its distinctive coral-pink stem and soft, silvery-green leaves, this ornamental plant thrives in well-drained soils. It is highly valued by gardeners for its unique aesthetic appeal and its ability to withstand arid conditions.
