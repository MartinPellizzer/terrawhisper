Geranium

Geranium, commonly known as cranesbill, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential oils, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to support skin health, reduce inflammation, and ease digestive issues. Its aromatic leaves and flowers offer holistic wellness benefits.

  • Scientific name: Geranium
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Geraniales. Furthermore, it resides in the family Geraniaceae and is specifically identified under the genus Geranium.
