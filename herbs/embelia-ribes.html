Vidaunga, scientifically known as Embelia ribes, is a cherished medicinal plant in Ayurvedic tradition. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and antihelmintic properties, it is primarily used to treat intestinal worms and digestive disorders. This versatile herb also supports metabolic health, offering significant therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness and vitality.
