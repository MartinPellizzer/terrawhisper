Embelia ribes

Embelia ribes, commonly known as Vidanga, is a significant medicinal plant prized in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its potent antihelmintic properties, it effectively combats intestinal parasites. Rich in embelin, this botanical extract also offers antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-diabetic benefits, making it a versatile staple in traditional holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Embelia ribes
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Embelia ribes, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Embelia.

Distribution

This plant is distributed across several distinct regions within the vast landscape of China. Specifically, it can be found growing in the South-Central areas as well as the Southeast provinces of the country. Its presence extends to the tropical island of Hainan and the high-altitude terrains of Tibet. Beyond the borders of China, the species is also located within the Indian Subcontinent. More precisely, its range includes the specific region of Assam.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Embelia, utilizes various components for its therapeutic applications. The medicinal value is primarily concentrated in the fruits, which are harvested and processed to obtain the highly potent fruit's pericarp. This outer layer is often ground into a fine fruit powder to be used in traditional formulations. Additionally, the roots are recognized for their biological properties, and the Root itself serves as an important part of the plant's overall medicinal profile.
