Paris forrestii

Paris forrestii, commonly known as Forrest's Paris, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the shaded understories of subtropical forests. Characterized by its striking single flower and dark, fleshy berries, this botanical rarity holds significant interest in ethnobotany and traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties and bioactive compounds.
