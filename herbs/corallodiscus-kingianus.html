What's the taxonomical classification of Corallodiscus kingianus?

Corallodiscus kingianus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, eventually reaching the order Lamiales. Within this order, the plant is a member of the family Gesneriaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Corallodiscus, with the species designation kingianus.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant requires high humidity levels above 70 percent, consistent warmth between 18 and 25 degrees Celsius, and a well-draining orchid bark substrate to prevent root rot.

Because it is an epiphytic species, it thrives when provided with dappled light rather than direct, intense sunlight. Regular misting or the use of a humidifier helps mimic its natural tropical habitat. Watering should occur frequently enough to keep the medium moist but never waterlogged.
