Tartarian Rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum tataricum, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its potent therapeutic properties. Renowned for its high anthraquinone content, this plant is primarily used to support digestive health, acting as an effective natural laxative to stimulate bowel movements and relieve constipation.
