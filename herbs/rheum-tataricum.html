Rheum tataricum

Rheum tatsaticum, commonly known as Tatarian rhubarb, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent rhizomes, it serves as a powerful natural laxative. Rich in anthraquinone glycosides, this plant is widely utilized to support digestive health, stimulate bile production, and promote effective detoxification.

  • Scientific name: Rheum tataricum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Rheum tatricum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae and is specifically identified within the genus Rheum.
