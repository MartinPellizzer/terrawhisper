Tartarian Rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum tataricum, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its potent therapeutic properties. Renowned for its high anthraquinone content, this plant is primarily used to support digestive health, acting as an effective natural laxative to stimulate bowel movements and relieve constipation.

Plant Parts

This plant, Rheum tatricum, is primarily valued for its underground structures, which contain the concentrated medicinal compounds used in traditional treatments. The most significant parts of the plant are the roots and the thick, fleshy rhizomes. These subterranean organs serve as the storage centers for anthraquinone glycosides, making them the essential components harvested for pharmacological applications.
