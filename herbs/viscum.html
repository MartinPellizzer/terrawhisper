Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as mistletoe, is a hemiparasitic species where the medicinal properties are primarily derived from its green parts. The leafy, evergreen shoots constitute the most significant portion used in traditional and alternative medicine, as they contain the bioactive lectins and viscotoxins required for therapeutic applications. Because the plant relies on its green parts to perform photosynthesis while simultaneously drawing water and nutrients from its host tree, these succulent, verdant stems serve as the essential reservoir for its active chemical compounds.
