Cnidium

Cnidium, commonly known as windshield or sea fennel, is a versatile genus of medicinal herbs valued in traditional pharmacopoeia. Renowned for its potent essential oils, these plants offer significant anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and analgesic properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and respiratory issues, Cnidium remains a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Cnidium
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Cnidium.
