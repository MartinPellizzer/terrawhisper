This plant, commonly known as field calyx or goosefoot calyx, is primarily utilized in herbal medicine through its aerial parts. These above-ground structures include the stems, leaves, and flowering inflorescences, which collectively contain the bioactive compounds required for therapeutic use. When preparing decoctions or infusions, the aerial parts are harvested to capture the plant's medicinal properties, making them the most essential component for traditional applications.
