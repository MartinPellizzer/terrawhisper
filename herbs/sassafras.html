Sassafras, scientifically known as Sassafras, is a versatile deciduous tree native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic leaves and roots, it has long been used in traditional medicine and culinary arts. While prized for its unique scent, certain components require careful handling due to evolving safety regulations.
