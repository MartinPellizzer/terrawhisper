Sassafras

Sassafras, commonly known as the aromatic tree of North America, has a storied history in herbal medicine. Renowned for its distinct scent and versatile roots, it has traditionally been used to support digestive health and circulation. Today, it remains a fascinating subject for those exploring natural botanical wellness.

  • Scientific name: Sassafras
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Sassafras.
