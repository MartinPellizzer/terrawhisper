Cotoneaster lindleyi

Cotoneaster lindleyi, commonly known as Lindley's Cotoneaster, is a resilient evergreen shrub prized for its ornamental beauty. While primarily valued in landscaping for its dense foliage and vibrant berries, it holds traditional significance in various herbal practices, offering potential therapeutic benefits through its unique phytochemical properties and antioxidant qualities.
