Apios taiwaniana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is categorized under the order Fabales. It is a member of the family Fabaceae, falling specifically into the genus Apios, with the unique species designation taiwaniana.
