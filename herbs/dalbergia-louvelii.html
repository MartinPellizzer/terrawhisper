Dalbergia louvelii

Dalbergia louvelii, commonly known as Indian Rosewood, is a highly valued medicinal plant cherished in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. This precious timber species is extensively utilized in herbal formulations to treat various skin and systemic ailments.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa
