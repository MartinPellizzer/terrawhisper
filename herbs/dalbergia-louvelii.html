Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as East Indian Rosewood, is highly valued for its medicinal and commercial properties, with its most significant component being the heartwood. The heartwood of Dalbergia louvellii is characterized by its deep, rich color and density, containing concentrated bioactive compounds that contribute to its therapeutic potential. In various traditional medicinal practices, extracts derived from this durable inner wood are utilized for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory qualities, making the heartwood the most essential part of the plant for such applications.
