Rhynchosia tomentosa var. tomentosa

Rhynchosia tomentoza var. tomentoza, commonly known as the velvet-leafed Rhynchosia, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique velvety texture, this plant possesses diverse bioactive compounds. It is frequently studied for its potential therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting wellness.

  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
