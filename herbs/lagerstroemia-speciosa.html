This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in the Comoros. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Banaba, offers various therapeutic benefits derived from different components of its anatomy. The leaves are widely recognized for their ability to help regulate blood sugar levels due to their high concentration of corosolic acid. Additionally, the root extracts are utilized in traditional medicine for their potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, contributing to the plant's overall medicinal profile.
