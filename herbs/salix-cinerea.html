Salix cinerea

Salix cinerea, commonly known as grey willow, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to much of Europe and Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, its bark contains salicin, a precursor to salicylic acid. This compound provides natural analgesic and anti-inflammatory properties, forming the historical foundation for modern aspirin production.

Distribution

This plant, known as Salix cinerea, exhibits a widespread presence across several regions of Northern Europe.
