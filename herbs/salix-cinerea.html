Salix cinerea, scientifically known as Salix cinerea, is a resilient shrub frequently found in damp, marshy habitats. Often called Grey Willow, this medicinal plant has historically been utilized for its soothing properties. Its bark contains salicin, a precursor to salicylic acid, traditionally used to alleviate pain and inflammation.

Distribution

This plant, known as Salix cinerea, exhibits a widespread presence across several regions of Northern Europe. Additionally, the species is well-established within the British Isles, specifically in Great Britain and Ireland.
