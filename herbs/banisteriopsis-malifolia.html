Banisteriopsis malifolia

Banisteriopsis malifolia, commonly known as the "tree of life" in certain Amazonian traditions, is a significant medicinal vine. Renowned for its potent phytochemical properties, it is used by indigenous communities to treat diverse ailments, ranging from inflammation to neurological conditions, offering a rich subject for ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Banisteriopsis malifolia
  • Family: Malpighiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Malpighiaceae within the genus Banisteriopsis.

Common Names

This plant, Banisteriopsis malifolia, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as malifolia, while in Spanish, it is known as malifolia or malifolia de monte. In Portuguese, the plant is called malifolia or malifolia de monte, and in Tupi, it is identified as yva-poti.
