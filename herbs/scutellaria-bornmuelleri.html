Scutellaria bornmuelleri

Scutellaria bornmuelleri, commonly known as a member of the skullcap family, is a medicinal herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, particularly flavonoids, this plant is studied for its potential anti-inflammatory and neuroprotective properties, offering significant promise in traditional medicine and modern pharmacological research.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
