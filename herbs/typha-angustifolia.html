Typha angustifolia

Typha angustifolia, commonly known as narrowleaf cattail, is a versatile aquatic perennial found in wetlands worldwide. Renowned for its ecological significance, this plant offers diverse medicinal applications. Traditional practices utilize its rhizomes and pollen for their anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties, making it a vital resource in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Typha angustifolia
  • Family: Typhaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Typhaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Typha.
