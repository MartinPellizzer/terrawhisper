Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging distribution across various regions of China. It can be found growing throughout the South-Central areas of the country. Additionally, its presence extends into the northern reaches of Inner Mongolia. The species is also documented within the North-Central provinces of China. Finally, its geographical range includes the high-altitude terrains of both Qinghai and Tibet.
