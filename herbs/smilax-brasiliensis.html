Smilax brasiliensis

Smilax brasiliensis, commonly known as Sarsaparilla, is a climbing woody vine native to the tropical regions of South America. Renowned in traditional medicine for its revitalizing properties, this plant contains valuable saponins used to treat skin conditions, promote vitality, and support overall detoxification through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Smilax brasiliensis
  • Family: Smilacaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Liliales and is a member of the family Smilacaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Smilax.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across various regions of Brazil. It is found extensively throughout the North of the country, occupying much of its tropical landscape.
