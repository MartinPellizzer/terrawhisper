Peddiea fischeri

Peddiea fischeri, commonly known as Fischer's peddiea, is a rare and specialized perennial herb native to specific montane habitats. Often found in rocky crevices, this botanical treasure is valued for its unique evolutionary adaptations. Understanding its medicinal potential offers vital insights into the diverse pharmacological properties of high-altitude flora.

  • Scientific name: Peddiea fischeri
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Peddiea fischeri, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malvales. Finally, it is a member of the family Thymelaeaceae and falls under the genus Peddiea.
