Plectocephalus chilensis

Plectocephalus chilensis, commonly known as a medicinal shrub native to Chile, holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Valued in traditional healing practices, this plant is frequently utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Understanding its chemical composition and biological activities is essential for exploring its potential roles in modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Plectocephalus chilensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. It is specifically identified by the genus Plectocephalus.
