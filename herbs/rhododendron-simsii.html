Distribution

This plant is native to several distinct regions across East Asia. Within China, its distribution spans the South-Central and Southeast territories, as well as the high-altitude areas of Tibet. The species can also be found growing in Korea, extending its range further into Eastern Asia. Additionally, it is documented within the Nansei-shotto islands.
