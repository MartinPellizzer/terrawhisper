Prickly Juniper

Juniperus oxycedrus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (64 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Prickly Juniper, scientifically known as Juniperus oxycedrus, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and berry-like cones, this plant is prized in traditional medicine for its antiseptic, diuretic, and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in various herbal remedies.

Plant Parts

This plant, Juniperus oxycedrus, utilizes several of its components for medicinal purposes, most notably its aromatic foliage and reproductive structures. The needle-like leaves are frequently harvested for their essential oils, which possess potent antiseptic properties. The plant produces fleshy, globose fruits that are commonly referred to as berries; these berries are rich in bioactive compounds and are often used in traditional remedies to support digestive and urinary health. Together, the leaves and the berries serve as the primary sources of the plant's therapeutic potential.
