Prickly Juniper, scientifically known as Juniperus oxycedrus, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and berry-like cones, this plant is prized in traditional medicine for its antiseptic, diuretic, and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in various herbal remedies.
