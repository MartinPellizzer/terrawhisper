Lablab purpureus subsp. purpureus

Lablab purpureus subsp. purpureus, commonly known as Lablab or Hyacinth bean, is a versatile legume valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential minerals, it is used in traditional medicine to support digestive health, manage blood sugar, and provide restorative nourishment in various global cultures.

  • Scientific name: Lablab purpureus subsp. purpureus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe
