Arenaria quadridentata

What's the taxonomical classification of Arenaria quadridentata?

Arenaria quadridentata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated under the order Caryophyllales. It is further categorized into the family Caryophyllaceae, falling under the genus Arenaria, with its specific designation being the species quadridentata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Arenaria quadridentata has a perennial growth habit characterized by small, tufted stems that arise from a woody base and bear opposite, linear to lanceolate leaves. Its most distinguishing morphological feature is the four-toothed structure found on the calyx of the flowers. The flowers themselves are small and white, often appearing in terminal clusters.
