What's the taxonomical classification of Platycarya strobilacea?

Platycarya strobilacea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Further taxonomic refinement places the plant in the order Fagales and the family Juglandaceae. Ultimately, it is defined by its specific classification within the genus Platycarya, resulting in its scientific name, Platycarya strobilacea.

The ethyl acetate extract of the infructescence demonstrated the best radical-scavenging activity, ferric reducing power, and antimicrobial activity in vitro. These findings regarding the infructescence suggest its potential for medicinal uses and food preservation.
