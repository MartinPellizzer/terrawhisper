Platycarya strobilacea

Platycarya strobilacea, commonly known as the Chinese Silverleaf, is a distinctive medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Characterized by its unique foliage and reproductive structures, it offers diverse therapeutic potential. Research explores its bioactive compounds, highlighting its importance in herbal pharmacology and its role in supporting various holistic wellness treatments.

  • Scientific name: Platycarya strobilacea
  • Family: Juglandaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fagales, it is further categorized into the family Juglandaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Platycarya.
