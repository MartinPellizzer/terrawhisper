What's the taxonomical classification of Myricaria prostrata?

Myricaria prostrata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Tamaricaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Myricaria, and its unique species designation, prostrata.
