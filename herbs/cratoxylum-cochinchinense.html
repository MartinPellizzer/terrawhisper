Yellow Cow Wood, scientifically known as Cratoxylum cochinchinense, is a tropical tree celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned for its bark, which yields a rich, red dye, the plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various digestive disorders across tropical regions.
