Haplophyllum buxbaumii subsp. buxbaumii

Haplophyllum buxbaumii subsp. buxbaumii, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in traditional healing, holds profound ethnobotanical value. Native to specific tropical regions, this subspecies is prized for its unique phytochemical properties. It is frequently utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising insights for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Haplophyllum buxbaumii subsp. buxbaumii
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Rutaceae and the genus Haplophyllum. Specifically, this medicinal species is identified as the subspecies Haplophyllum buxbaumii subsp. buxbaumii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across the Mediterranean region. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found within the borders of France. Its presence extends to Southeastern Europe, specifically on the island of Kriti and throughout the European portion of Türkiye. The species also occupies territories in Northern Africa, including Algeria. Finally, its range reaches into Tunisia, completing its presence across these various coastal landscapes.
