Capparis decidua

Capparis decidua, commonly known as Karira or Spiny Ficus, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to the arid regions of India. Renowned for its adaptability to desert climates, this medicinal powerhouse offers diverse therapeutic benefits, ranging from digestive support to managing diabetes, making it vital in traditional Ayurvedic medicine.

  • Scientific name: Capparis decidua
  • Family: Capparaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Capparis decidua, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Brassicales and the family Capparaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Capparis.
