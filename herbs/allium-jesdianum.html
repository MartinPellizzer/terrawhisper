Allium jesdianum

Allium jesdianum, commonly known as Jesdia's onion, is a resilient perennial native to the arid landscapes of the American Southwest. Renowned for its distinct culinary flavor and medicinal properties, this wild bulb offers significant nutritional value and has been utilized traditionally for its various therapeutic healing benefits.

  • Scientific name: Allium jesdianum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Allium jesdianum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Allium.
