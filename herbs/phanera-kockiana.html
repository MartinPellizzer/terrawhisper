Phanera kockiana

Phanera kockiana, commonly known as the Kock's Bauhinia, is a remarkable medicinal climbing shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive butterfly-shaped leaves, this plant is deeply embedded in traditional healing practices, valued for its diverse bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments and support holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Phanera kockiana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
