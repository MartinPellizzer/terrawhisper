Chinese Juniper, scientifically known as Juniperus chinensis, is a versatile evergreen conifer native to East Asia. Renowned for its striking architectural form, this hardy species is highly valued in traditional medicine and ornamental landscaping. Its aromatic foliage and berries offer unique therapeutic properties used in various holistic practices.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution that spans across several distinct regions. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented in Great Britain and Norway. Moving eastward, the species is also found within the Russian Far East. Specifically, it inhabits the areas of Khabarovsk, the Kuril Islands, and Primorye. This plant, known scientifically as Juniperus chinensis, utilizes its foliage for various medicinal and aromatic purposes. Whether referring to the collective leaves or a single individual leaf, these green, needle-like structures contain essential oils that are frequently extracted for their therapeutic properties.
