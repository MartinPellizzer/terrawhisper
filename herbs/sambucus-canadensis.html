American black elderberry

Sambucus canadensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (686 studies) · ★★★★★

American black elderberry, scientifically known as Sambucus canadensis, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to North America. Renowned for its dark, nutrient-dense berries, it has been used for centuries in traditional medicine. Today, it is widely celebrated for its potent antioxidant properties and ability to support immune system health.
