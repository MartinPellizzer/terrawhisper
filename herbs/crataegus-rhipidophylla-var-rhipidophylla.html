Scientifically known as Crataegus rhipidophylla var. rhipidophylla, is a specialized variety of hawthorn renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Often utilized in traditional herbalism, this plant offers unique bioactive compounds that support cardiovascular health, aid digestion, and provide antioxidant benefits, making it a valuable subject in ethnobotanical studies.
