Crataegus rhipidophylla var. rhipidophylla

Crataegus rhipidophylla var. rhipidophylla, commonly known as the rugose hawthorn, is a resilient deciduous shrub valued for its medicinal potential. Native to North American woodlands, this variety contains bioactive compounds often utilized in traditional practices to support cardiovascular health, promote digestion, and provide antioxidant benefits through its berries.
