Elephantopus mollis

Elephantopus mollis, commonly known as Elephant's Foot, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat respiratory ailments and skin infections, this resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Elephantopus mollis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Elephantopus.

Distribution

This plant, known as Elephantopus mollis, exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally in the country of Benin. Additionally, its presence extends to the territory of Burkina Faso. The species is also documented across Guinea-Bissau and the nation of Guinea. Finally, its range includes the coastal landscapes of the Ivory Coast.
