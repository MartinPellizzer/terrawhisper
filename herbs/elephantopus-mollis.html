Distribution

This plant, known as Elephantopus mollis, exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally in the country of Benin. Additionally, its presence extends to the territory of Burkina Faso. The species is also documented across Guinea-Bissau and the nation of Guinea. Finally, its range includes the coastal landscapes of the Ivory Coast.
