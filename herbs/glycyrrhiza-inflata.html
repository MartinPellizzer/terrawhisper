Glycyrrhiza inflata

Glycyrrhiza inflata, commonly known as inflated licorice, is a perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its swollen, bladder-like pods, this plant belongs to the Fabaceae family. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, it contains bioactive compounds utilized for their soothing, anti-inflammatory, and various therapeutic properties in holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Glycyrrhiza inflata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Specifically, it is classified under the genus Glycyrrhiza as the species Glycyrrhiza inflata.
