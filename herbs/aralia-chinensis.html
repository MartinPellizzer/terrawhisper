Aralia chinensis

Aralia chinensis, commonly known as China Aralia, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues. This resilient plant offers significant pharmacological potential for modern research into natural healing remedies.

  • Scientific name: Aralia chinensis
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Araliaceae and the genus Aralia. Specifically, its scientific name is Aralia chinensis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Aralia chinensis, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. In Chinese, it is referred to as 楤木 (shènmù), while in Polish, it is called Aralia chińska. The names used in Slavic languages also reflect its origin, being known as Аралия китайская in Russian, Китайись аралия in Komi (koi), and Китайысь аралия in Udmurt (udm).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning from Europe to various regions of Asia. In Northern Europe, its presence can be identified within Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, it is also found growing in Germany. Within its native Asian territory, the species is widely distributed across China South-Central. Additionally, the plant inhabits the Southeast regions of China as well as the island of Hainan.
