Flame Lilies, scientifically known as Gloriosa, is a striking climbing plant celebrated for its brilliant, fire-like blooms. Native to tropical regions, these spectacular perennials feature reflexed petals in vivid shades of red and yellow. While visually breathtaking, they contain potent alkaloids, requiring careful handling due to their toxicity.
