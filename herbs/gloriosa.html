Gloriosa

Gloriosa, commonly known as the Flame Lily, is a stunning climbing plant celebrated for its vibrant, fiery petals. While its striking appearance adds elegance to tropical gardens, it is also a potent medicinal herb. Used carefully in traditional practices, its alkaloids offer significant therapeutic potential despite its high toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Gloriosa
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Liliales, it is specifically situated in the family Colchicaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gloriosa.
