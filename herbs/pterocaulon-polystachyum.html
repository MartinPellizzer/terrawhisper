Pterocaulon polystachyum

Pterocaulon polystachyum, commonly known as a traditional medicinal herb, holds significant value in ethnomedicine. Often utilized in folk remedies, this plant is recognized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds to understand its potential roles in treating various ailments and supporting holistic health.

  • Scientific name: Pterocaulon polystachyum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. It is further identified by its specific genus, Pterocaulon, and its species name, polystachyum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of South America. Within Brazil, its presence is documented in the West-Central, Southeast, and South regions. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, it is also found in the Northeast of Argentina. Furthermore, its range extends into the country of Paraguay.
