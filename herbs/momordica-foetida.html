Momordica foetida

Momoirdica foetida, commonly known as the stinking bitter gourd, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Cucurbitaceae family. Renowned for its pungent aroma, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. Various parts of the plant are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive disorders and inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Momordica foetida
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant, known scientifically as Momordica foetida, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
