Lindera aggregata

Lindra aggregata, commonly known as the Mountain Laurel, is a stunning evergreen shrub native to Australia. Beyond its exquisite, bell-shaped blooms, this medicinal plant holds traditional significance. Its various parts have been utilized in folk remedies, offering unique phytochemical properties that continue to intrigue modern botanical and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Lindera aggregata
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Laurales. Finally, it is a member of the family Lauraceae, falling under the genus Lindra.

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of East Asia. Within China, its range extends through the South-Central and Southeast territories.
