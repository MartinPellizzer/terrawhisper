Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of the Indo-China region. Within this geographical area, it can be found growing in the tropical environments of Cambodia. The species also inhabits various habitats throughout Laos and Myanmar. Furthermore, its presence extends into the forested regions of Thailand. Finally, the natural range of this medicinal plant includes the country of Vietnam.
