Artocarpus elasticus

Artocarpus elasticus, commonly known as the Wild Jack, is a versatile tropical tree valued in traditional medicine. Its various parts, including bark, leaves, and fruit, possess significant bioactive properties. Often used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation, this species represents a vital resource in ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Artocarpus elasticus
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Rosalales, it is a member of the family Moraceae and is specifically identified under the genus Artocarpus.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Artocarpus elasticus, is referred to by various common names across different languages and regions. In Indonesia, it is called Benda (pohon) in Indonesian (id) and bendha in Javanese (jv), while in Minangkabau (min), it is known as Tarok. In Chinese, the species is identified as 馬來波羅蜜 (zh) and 马来波罗蜜 (zh-hans).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, it can be found growing in Myanmar and Thailand. Its presence extends significantly into the Malesia zone, where it inhabits the island of Borneo. Additionally, the species is widely distributed across Jawa. Finally, the plant reaches the Lesser Sunda Islands, completing its diverse range across the archipelago.
