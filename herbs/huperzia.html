Firmoss, scientifically known as Huperzia, is a genus of ancient clubmosses renowned for their unique bioactive properties. Often found in shaded forest floors, these primitive plants contain huperzine A, a potent alkaloid used in neurological research for its ability to enhance memory and protect cognitive functions against decline.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the northern regions of Europe. In the northern reaches of the continent, it can be found growing in Denmark and Finland. Its presence also extends to the island territories of Iceland and the Faroe Islands, known as Føroyar. Furthermore, the species is documented to inhabit parts of Great Britain.
