Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West-Central Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented across several distinct nations, including the Central African Republic and Cameroon. Furthermore, it can be found within the borders of Gabon and the Congo. The species also extends its range to include the territory of Cabinda. Collectively, these locations highlight its localized habitat within the tropical landscapes of West-Central Africa.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Tabernanthe iboga, utilizes various components for its medicinal and psychoactive properties. The most significant part used is the root, specifically the bark found on the root system, which contains high concentrations of ibogaine. Additionally, the leaf of the plant can also be utilized in traditional preparations, though it is less commonly cited than the potent root bark in primary medicinal applications.
