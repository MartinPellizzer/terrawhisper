Scopolia japonica, scientifically known as Scopolia japonica, is a perennial medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its potent alkaloid content, particularly scopoline, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its sedative and analgesic properties, though its high toxicity requires careful handling and expert pharmacological application.
