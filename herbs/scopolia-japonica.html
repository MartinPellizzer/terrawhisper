Scopolia japonica

Scopolia japonica, commonly known as Japanese Scoparia, is a perennial medicinal herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive alkaloids used to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource for developing natural pharmacological treatments and holistic wellness therapies.

  • Scientific name: Scopolia japonica
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Scopolia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Scopolia japonica, is recognized by various names across different languages and regions. In Japanese, it is referred to as ハシリドコロ, while in Korean, it is called 미치광이풀. In Arabic, it is transliterated as سكوبوليا جابونيكా, and in Chinese, it is identified as 东莨菪 (which is also the designation used in simplified Chinese, zh-hans).
