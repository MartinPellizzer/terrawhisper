Tetradium trichotomum

Tetradium trichotomum, commonly known as the mountain magnolia, is a significant medicinal plant native to East Asian forests. Valued in traditional medicine, its bark and leaves are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Tetradium trichotomum
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is further categorized into the family Rutaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Tetradium and the specific species name trichotomum.

This plant, known scientifically as Tetradium trichotomum, is referred to by several different common names depending on the linguistic context and regional usage. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast territories. Its presence also extends to the tropical island of Hainan. Beyond the Chinese borders, the species is located within the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, it has been documented growing in the Assam region.
