Clinopodium acinos, scientifically known as Clinopodium acinos, is a perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Commonly called creeping thyme, this aromatic plant features tiny, fragrant leaves and delicate pink flowers. Often found in damp habitats, it is valued in traditional herbalism for its soothing antimicrobial and digestive properties.
