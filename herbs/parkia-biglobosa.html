African locust-bean

Parkia biglobosa · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (73 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

African locust-bean, scientifically known as Parkia biglobosa, is a multipurpose tree native to the African savannah. Renowned for its nutrient-dense seeds used in fermented condiments, it also holds immense medicinal value. Various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from hypertension to inflammation.

Distribution

This plant, Parkia biglobosa, exhibits a specific distribution pattern within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of Benin. The species is also widely distributed across the territory of Burkina. Furthermore, its presence is documented throughout the nation of Gambia.
