Berberis hsuyunensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Berberis hsuyunensis?

Berberis hsuyunenensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this classification, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Ranunculales and is a member of the family Berberidaceae. At the most specific levels of its biological classification, this plant is identified by the genus Berberis and is specifically designated as the species hsuyunenensis.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is native to the mountainous regions of central and western China, specifically occurring within the provinces of Gansu, Qinghai, Sichuan, and Yunnan. These populations are often found at elevations ranging from 2,000 to 3,500 meters above sea level. The species thrives in the temperate climates characteristic of these specific montane ecosystems.
