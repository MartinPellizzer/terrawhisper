Noccaea caerulescens

Thlaspi caerulescens

Noccaea caerulescens, commonly known as the blue lobelia, is a remarkable medicinal herb native to specific alpine regions. Renowned for its vibrant blue blooms, this plant contains unique bioactive compounds. Historically utilized in traditional practices, it offers significant therapeutic potential in treating respiratory ailments and supporting overall systemic health.

  • Scientific name: Noccaea caerulescens
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Noccaea.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Noccaea caerulescens, is referred to by various common names across different languages and regions. Distribution

This plant, Noccaea caerulescens, is primarily distributed across the northern regions of Europe. Its presence is well-documented throughout Scandinavia, specifically within the borders of Norway and Sweden. The species can also be found in the coastal and inland areas of Finland. Furthermore, its geographical reach extends to Denmark, contributing to its northern European profile. Finally, the plant is also known to inhabit various locations throughout Great Britain.
