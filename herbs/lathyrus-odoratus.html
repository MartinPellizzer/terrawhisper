Lathyrus odoratus

Lathyrus odoratus, commonly known as the Sweet Pea, is celebrated more for its enchanting fragrance and vibrant blooms than its medicinal properties. Historically, various parts were used in traditional remedies to treat ailments like inflammation or skin irritations, though it remains primarily cherished as a stunning ornamental garden flower.

  • Scientific name: Lathyrus odoratus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Lathyrus.
