English laurel

Prunus laurocerasus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (328 studies) · ★★★★★

English laurel, scientifically known as Prunus laurocerasus, is a versatile evergreen shrub valued for its glossy foliage. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, its leaves contain cyanogenic glycosides. While requiring careful handling due to toxicity, it remains a significant species in botanical studies, landscaping, and historical pharmacological research.

Plant Parts

This plant, Prunus laocearusus, is a medicinal species where the primary part of interest for certain applications is its fruit, which develops into small, fleshy drupes. These drupes consist of a fleshy outer layer surrounding a hard, stony endocarp that protects the seed inside. In various traditional medicinal practices, different components of the plant may be utilized, but the chemical constituents found within the drupes are often a focus of botanical study.
