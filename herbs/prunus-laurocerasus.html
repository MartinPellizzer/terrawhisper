English laurel

Prunus laurocerasus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (328 studies) · ★★★★★

English laurel, scientifically known as Prunus laurocerasus, is a versatile evergreen shrub valued for its glossy foliage. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, its leaves contain cyanogenic glycosides. While requiring careful handling due to toxicity, it remains a significant species in botanical studies, landscaping, and historical pharmacological research.
