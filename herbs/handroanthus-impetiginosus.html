Handroanthus impetiginosus

Handroanthus impetiginosus, commonly known as the Golden Bark tree, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its potent therapeutic properties. Found in tropical ecosystems, its bark and leaves contain unique bioactive compounds used traditionally to treat inflammation, infections, and digestive ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Handroanthus impetiginosus
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
