Hedyotis biflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant follows the taxonomic order Gentianaales and is a member of the family Rubiaceae. Ultimately, its specific biological identity is defined by its placement within the genus Hedyotis, with the specific epithet biflora.
