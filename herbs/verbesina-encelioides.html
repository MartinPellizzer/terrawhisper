Verbesina encelioides

Verbesina encelioides, commonly known as the False Sunflower, is a resilient perennial herb native to diverse ecosystems. Valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive properties, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Understanding its chemical constituents is essential for exploring its medicinal applications in treating various inflammatory and systemic conditions.
