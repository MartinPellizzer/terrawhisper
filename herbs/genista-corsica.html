Distribution

This plant is native to specific regions within Southwestern Europe. Its natural habitat is primarily centered around the Mediterranean islands. Specifically, Genista corsica can be found growing in the territory of Corse. Additionally, its distribution extends to the nearby island of Sardegna. Therefore, the species is geographically restricted to these two distinct locations.
