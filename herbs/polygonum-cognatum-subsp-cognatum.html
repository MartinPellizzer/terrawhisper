Polygonum cognatum subsp. cognatum

Polygonum cognatum subsp. cognatum, commonly known as knotweed, is a resilient perennial herb native to various temperate regions. Renowned for its distinctive jointed stems, this subspecies possesses diverse phytochemical properties. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds and antioxidant capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Polygonum cognatum subsp. cognatum
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Polygonaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Polygonum as the subspecies Polygonum cognatum subsp. cognatum.
