Elephantopus scaber

Elephantopus scaber, commonly known as Elephant's Foot, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Often used to treat digestive issues, fever, and skin ailments, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Elephantopus scaber
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Elephantopus scaber, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. It is a member of the Asteraceae family and is specifically categorized under the genus Elephantopus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in both Cameroon and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the eastern side of the continent, it can be found within East Tropical Africa, specifically in Tanzania. Its range also extends into South Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Angola. Finally, the species is also distributed throughout Mozambique in the southern part of the continent.
