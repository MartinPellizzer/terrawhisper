Schisandra chinensis

Magnolia-vine

Schisandra chinensis, commonly known as magnolia vine or schisandra berry, is a versatile medicinal plant revered in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its "five-flavor" profile, it acts as a powerful adaptogen, helping the body resist stress, enhancing mental clarity, and supporting liver health and overall physical vitality.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra chinensis
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Austrobaileyales and the family Schisandraceae. Ultimately, its scientific identity is defined by its placement within the genus Schisandra.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Schisandra chinensis, is recognized by various regional common names across different languages. Distribution

This plant is distributed across several specific regions within the Russian Far East. It can be found growing in the Amur and Khabarovsk territories. Additionally, its natural range extends into the Primorye and Sakhalin regions. Beyond Russia, the species is also located within the Inner Mongolia province of China. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of the species.

Plant Parts

This plant, Schisandra chinensis, is a woody vine characterized by its diverse functional anatomy. The climbing stems provide the structural support necessary for the plant to ascend through forest canopies, with each individual stem often displaying a twisted growth habit. The lush, green leaves are typically used to aid in photosynthesis and contribute to the plant's overall vigor. Most notably, the plant produces distinctive reddish fruits that are highly valued in traditional medicine; each individual fruit is a small, fleshy berry containing seeds that carry the concentrated medicinal properties of the species.
