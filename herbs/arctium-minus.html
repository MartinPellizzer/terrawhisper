Arctium minus

Arctium minus, commonly known as lesser burdock, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and detoxifying properties, it is frequently used to support skin health and blood purification. This versatile plant offers a natural approach to managing various systemic inflammatory conditions.
