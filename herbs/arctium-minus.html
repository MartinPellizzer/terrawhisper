Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as lesser burdock, is particularly noted for its specialized fruits, which are technically small, dry achenes. These fruits are enclosed in a hooked, bur-like structure designed for zoochory, allowing them to cling easily to the fur of animals or the clothing of humans for seed dispersal. While the roots are often the most discussed medicinal component, these unique fruits play a crucial role in the plant's reproductive cycle and are a defining characteristic of the species.
