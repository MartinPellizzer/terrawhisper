Cheesewood, scientifically known as Pittosporum tobira, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its glossy foliage and fragrant white blossoms, it serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. In traditional practices, various plant parts are utilized for their potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory therapeutic properties.

The reproduction of Pittosporum tobira is characterized by a bisexual sexual reproduction system, where individual flowers possess both male and female reproductive organs.
