Pittosporum tobira

Pittosporum tobira, commonly known as Japanese Mock Orange, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its glossy foliage and fragrant white flowers. Beyond its ornamental appeal in landscaping, this resilient plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance, containing bioactive compounds studied for their potential medicinal, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties in traditional practices.

  • Scientific name: Pittosporum tobira
  • Family: Pittosporaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further descending through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is a member of the family Pittosporaceae.
