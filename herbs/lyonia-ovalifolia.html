Lyonia ovalifolia

Lyonia ovalifolia, commonly known as the oval-leafed lyonia, is a distinctive shrub native to the southeastern United States. Characterized by its glossy, elliptical leaves and delicate blooms, this plant holds significant botanical interest. It is often studied for its unique ecological role and potential applications within traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Lyonia ovalifolia
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Lyonia ovalifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with its placement in the genus Lyonia.
