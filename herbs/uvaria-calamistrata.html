Uvaria calamistrata

Uvaria calamistrata, commonly known as a climbing shrub within the Annonaceae family, is a fascinating medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive phytochemicals, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Researchers increasingly explore its potential properties for developing natural therapeutic agents to combat various health conditions.

  • Scientific name: Uvaria calamistrata
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Annonaceae, specifically falling under the genus Uvaria.
