Alangium chinense

Angelica chinensis, commonly known as Dong Quai, is a cornerstone of traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned as a "female ginseng," this perennial herb is prized for its ability to regulate hormonal balance, improve blood circulation, and support reproductive health, offering powerful therapeutic benefits for vitality and overall systemic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Alangium chinense
  • Family: Cornaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cornales, it is classified under the family Cornaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Alangium.
