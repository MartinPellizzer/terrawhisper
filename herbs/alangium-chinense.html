Distribution

This plant is primarily found across various regions within West-Central Tropical Africa. Its geographical range includes the nation of Burundi, where it grows in specific local habitats. It can also be located in the territory of Cabinda and throughout Cameroon. Furthermore, the species is distributed among the various islands within the Gulf of Guinea. Finally, its presence extends eastward to include the country of Rwanda.
