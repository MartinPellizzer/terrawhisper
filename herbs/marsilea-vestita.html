Plant Parts

This plant, Marsilea vestita, is a species of water fern where the medicinal properties are distributed throughout its biological structure. While the plant lacks traditional upright stems, its creeping rhizomes function as the specialized stems that anchor it to the substrate and facilitate nutrient transport. The leaf extract is frequently utilized in various traditional applications due to its concentrated bioactive compounds. Ultimately, because its therapeutic potential is so widespread, the entire plant is often harvested and processed to ensure maximum efficacy in medicinal preparations.
