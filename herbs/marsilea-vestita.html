Marsilea vestita

Marsilea vestita, commonly known as the clothed water clover, is a unique aquatic fern characterized by its distinctive four-leaflet structure. Native to specific wetland habitats, this resilient species plays a vital role in its ecosystem, offering potential medicinal properties and serving as an important subject in botanical pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Marsilea vestita
  • Family: Marsileaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Salviniales. Finally, it is a member of the family Marsileaceae, specifically falling under the genus Marsilea.
