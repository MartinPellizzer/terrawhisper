Rosa elongata

Rosa elongata, commonly known as the elongated rose, is a resilient climbing shrub native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its delicate blooms and thorny stems, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in folk medicine for its potential therapeutic properties, often utilized to support skin health and digestive wellness.
