Rosa elongata, scientifically known as Rosa elongata, is a resilient climbing rose species native to various arid regions. Valued in traditional medicine, it is often utilized for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This hardy shrub offers therapeutic potential through its aromatic flowers and nutrient-rich hips in holistic healing.

Within these structures, the pseudocarps form, acting as the specialized, accessory fruit tissues that protect the reproductive elements.
