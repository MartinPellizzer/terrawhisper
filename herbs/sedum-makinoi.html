Sedum makinoi

What's the taxonomical classification of Sedum makinoi?

Sedum makinoi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Saxifragales. This plant is a member of the family Crassulaceae and is further categorized into the genus Sedum, with its specific classification being the species makinoi.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Sedum makinoi has fleshy, succulent leaves that are typically ovate to elliptical in shape and grow in a dense, low-spreading rosette pattern. These leaves possess a smooth texture and can range in color from bright green to subtle shades of reddish-bronze depending on light exposure. The stems are creeping and tend to form thick mats as they spread across the soil surface. Small, star-shaped flowers emerge from the foliage, often appearing in clusters of pale yellow or greenish tones. This compact growth habit makes the plant highly effective for groundcover applications in rocky or arid environments.
