Gac melon, scientifically known as Momordica cochinchiensis, is a vibrant tropical fruit celebrated for its intense red flesh. Rich in lycopene and beta-carotene, this nutritional powerhouse offers potent antioxidant properties. Traditionally used in Southeast Asian medicine, it supports eye health, skin vitality, and overall immune system function.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as Gac, possesses various components that are utilized for their medicinal and nutritional properties. The most significant parts are the root tubers, which are often referred to as tubers in botanical descriptions, as they store essential nutrients and bioactive compounds. Within the fruit, the large, bright red seeds contain a high concentration of healthy fats, which can be extracted to produce seed oil rich in carotenoids like lycopene and beta-carotene. Each individual seed serves as a vital source of these concentrated oils, making the entire reproductive structure highly valued in traditional medicine.
