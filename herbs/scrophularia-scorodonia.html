Scrophularia scorodonia

Scrophularia scorodonia, commonly known as Common Figwort, is a perennial herb native to European wetlands. Distinguished by its tubular, spotted flowers, this plant has a long history in traditional herbalism. It is often studied for its bioactive compounds, which may offer various therapeutic properties in medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Scrophularia scorodonia
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Scrophularia scorodonia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Scrophularia.
