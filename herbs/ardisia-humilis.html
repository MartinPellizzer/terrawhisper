Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across parts of East and South Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the Hainan province as well as throughout the China Southeast region. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found growing in the state of Assam. It also inhabits the territory of Bangladesh within the same subcontinent. Finally, the plant's range extends further into the mountainous regions of the East Himalaya.
