Solidago chilensis

Solidago chilensis, commonly known as Chilean goldenrod, is a resilient perennial herb native to South American landscapes. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its anti-inflammatory and diuretic properties, often used to support urinary tract health and soothe digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Solidago chilensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Solidago, with its species name being chilensis.
