Ligularia fischeri

Ligularia fischeri, commonly known as Fischer's buttercup, is a striking perennial herb native to the moist, alpine regions of East Asia. Renowned for its large, heart-shaped leaves and vibrant yellow blooms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies for its diverse medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Ligularia fischeri
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Ligularia.
