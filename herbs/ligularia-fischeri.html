Ligularia fischeri

Ligularia fischerii

Ligularia fischeri, commonly known as Fischer's buttercup, is a striking perennial herb native to the moist, alpine regions of East Asia. Renowned for its large, heart-shaped leaves and vibrant yellow blooms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies for its diverse medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Ligularia fischeri
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Ligularia. As a member of this specific lineage, Ligularia fischeri exhibits the characteristic botanical traits associated with its taxonomic hierarchy.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Eurasia. In Northern Europe, its presence can be specifically noted within Great Britain. Moving eastward into Siberia, the species is found in both Buryatiya and Chita. The plant also extends into the Russian Far East, covering the Amur region. Finally, its range reaches as far as Khabarovsk within the same eastern territory.
