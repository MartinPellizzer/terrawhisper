Ichnocarpus frutescens

Ichnocarpus frutescens, commonly known as the climbing milkweed, is a versatile woody climber native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant contains diverse bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, offering significant potential for developing modern therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Ichnocarpus frutescens
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is found within the genus Ichnocarpus.
