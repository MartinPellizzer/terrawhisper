Scientifically known as Hyoscyamus senecionis, is a potent medicinal herb belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its high concentration of tropane alkaloids, particularly hyoscyamine, this plant has historically been utilized for its antispasmodic and sedative properties. It remains a significant subject in pharmacological studies regarding neuromuscular control.
