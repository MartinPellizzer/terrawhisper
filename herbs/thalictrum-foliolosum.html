Thalictrum foliolosum

Leafy meadow-rue

Thalictrum foliolosum, commonly known as the creeping corydalis, is a delicate herbaceous perennial native to East Asian woodlands. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant contains bioactive alkaloids valued for their sedative and analgesic properties. Its lush, fine-textured foliage and subtle blooms make it both medicinal and aesthetically charming.

  • Scientific name: Thalictrum foliolosum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Ranunculales, specifically within the family Ranunculaceae. Ultimately, the species is identified by the genus Thalictrum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically within the South-Central areas as well as in Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is present in India. Furthermore, the species is documented in the specific region of Assam. Finally, it is also known to grow throughout the East Himalaya range.
