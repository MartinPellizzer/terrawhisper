Thuja sutchuenensis

Thuja sutchuemenis, commonly known as Western Red Cedar, is a versatile evergreen prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antiseptic and antimicrobial properties, it is frequently utilized in essential oils and tinctures to support respiratory health, soothe skin irritations, and promote overall immune function through its potent resinous compounds.

  • Scientific name: Thuja sutchuenensis
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
