common bracken

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (446 studies) · ★★★★★

Common bracken, scientifically known as Pteridium aquilinum, is a widespread, perennial fern found across various global climates. While historically used in folk medicine for its purported anti-inflammatory properties, it contains thiaminase and ptaquiloside. Consequently, modern scientific caution is advised due to its potential toxicity and carcinogenic risks.
