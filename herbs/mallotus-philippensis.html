Mallotus philipensis, scientifically known as Mallotus philippensis, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Asia. Widely utilized in Ayurvedic medicine, it is renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. The plant's bark and leaves are frequently employed to treat skin disorders, digestive issues, and various infections.
