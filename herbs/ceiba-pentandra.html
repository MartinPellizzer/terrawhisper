Ceiba pentandra

Java cotton

Ceiba pentandra, commonly known as the Kapok tree, is a majestic tropical giant renowned for its diverse medicinal properties. Beyond its economic value, various parts of this tree—including the bark, leaves, and seeds—are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues across many cultures.

  • Scientific name: Ceiba pentandra
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Malvales, and the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Ceiba.

Distribution

This plant, Ceiba pentandra, exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the West Tropical Africa region. It is documented to grow naturally in the nation of Benin. Furthermore, its presence extends to the territories of Burkina and Gambia. The species also inhabits parts of Ghana within this tropical belt. Finally, its range includes the coastal area of Guinea-Bissau.
