Distribution

This plant, Ceiba pentandra, exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the West Tropical Africa region. It is documented to grow naturally in the nation of Benin. Furthermore, its presence extends to the territories of Burkina and Gambia. The species also inhabits parts of Ghana within this tropical belt. Finally, its range includes the coastal area of Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ceiba pentandra, is a massive tropical tree where various anatomical components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The robust stem serves as the structural foundation of the tree, while the bark is frequently harvested for medicinal preparations. Specifically, the stem barks are often used in traditional remedies to treat various ailments, and the root bark is similarly valued for its unique bioactive compounds. Additionally, the leaves are commonly employed in decoctions or topical applications, ensuring that almost every significant part of the plant contributes to its medicinal profile.
