Corydalis impatiens, scientifically known as Corydalis impatiens, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant contains potent alkaloids used to alleviate pain and soothe neurological symptoms. Its delicate, spreading foliage and unique medicinal properties make it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions in Northern Asia. Within the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found growing in Buryatiya, Chitá, and Irkutsk. Additionally, the species extends its range into the southern Siberian region of Tuva. Beyond the Russian borders, its habitat also encompasses parts of China, specifically within Inner Mongolia. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Corydalis impatiens.
