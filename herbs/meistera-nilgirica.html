Meistera nilgirica

Meistera nilgirica, commonly known as the Nilgiri Meistera, is a rare medicinal shrub endemic to the high-altitude shola forests of the Western Ghats. Valued in traditional wellness practices, this unique plant offers significant therapeutic potential, contributing to biodiversity and providing natural compounds essential for studying modern botanical pharmacology.
