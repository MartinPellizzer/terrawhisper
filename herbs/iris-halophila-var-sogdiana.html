Iris halophila var. sogdiana

Iris halophila var. sogdiana, commonly known as the Sogdian Iris, is a rare medicinal plant native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this variety is utilized in traditional pharmacology for its potential therapeutic properties, offering significant interest for modern research into bioactive botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Iris halophila var. sogdiana
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Iridaeceae and the genus Iris. Specifically, it is identified as the variety sogdiaana of the species Iris halophila.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Iris halophila var. sogdiaana, is referred to by several common names depending on the linguistic context. In Chinese, it is identified by the names 蓝花喜盐鸢尾, 蓝花喜盐鸢尾, and 蓝花喜盐鸢尾 across the language codes zh, zh-hans, and zh-cn, respectively.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the diverse landscapes of Central Asia. Its geographical range extends across the vast territories of Kazakhstan and Kirgizstan. Additionally, it can be located in the arid regions of Turkmenistan. The distribution further encompasses the mountainous areas of Tadzhikistan. Finally, this specific variety is also documented as growing throughout Uzbekistan.
