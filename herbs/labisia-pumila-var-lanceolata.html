Labisia pumila var. lanceolata

Labisia pumila var. lanceolata, commonly known as Kacip Fatimah, is a renowned medicinal herb native to Southeast Asia. Highly valued in traditional Malay medicine, this plant is celebrated for its potent phytoestrogens. It is widely utilized to support female reproductive health, balance hormones, and enhance overall vitality and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Labisia pumila var. lanceolata
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Specifically, Labisia pumila var. lanceolata is identified as a member of the genus Labisia.
