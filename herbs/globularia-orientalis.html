Globularia orientalis

Globularia orientalis, commonly known as Oriental Globularia, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its striking, spherical blue flower heads and silvery-green foliage, this plant thrives in rocky terrains. While primarily ornamental, it holds traditional significance in regional herbalism for its unique botanical properties.

  • Scientific name: Globularia orientalis
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
