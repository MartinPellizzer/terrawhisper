Rockcress, scientifically known as Arabidopsis, is a small flowering plant that serves as the premier model organism in plant biology. Renowned for its rapid life cycle and compact genome, it allows researchers to decode complex genetic processes, driving breakthroughs in understanding plant development, stress responses, and molecular evolution.
