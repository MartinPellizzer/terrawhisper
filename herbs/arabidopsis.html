Arabidopsis

Arabidopsis, commonly known as the model organism of plant biology, is a small flowering plant belonging to the Brassicaceae family. Despite its unassuming appearance, it is indispensable to scientific research, providing critical insights into genetics, development, and environmental responses, serving as a cornerstone for understanding complex plant life processes.

  • Scientific name: Arabidopsis
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Brassicales and sits in the family Brassicaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic classification is placed within the genus Arabidopsis.
