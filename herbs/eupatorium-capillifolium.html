Eupatorium capillifolium

Eupatorium capillifolium, commonly known as Thoroughwort, is a versatile perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its feathery foliage and delicate pink blooms, this plant possesses significant therapeutic properties, often utilized to support respiratory health, reduce inflammation, and promote overall wellness through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Eupatorium capillifolium
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Eupatorium.
