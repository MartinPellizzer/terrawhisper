Gambeya albida

White star apple

Gambeya albida, commonly known as the African mango, is a versatile tree native to the African savannah. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its bark, leaves, and roots are utilized to treat diverse ailments, including inflammation and skin infections. This resilient species remains a vital resource for local medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Gambeya albida
  • Family: Sapotaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ericales and the family Sapotaceae. Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Chrysophyllum albiidum, is recognized by several different names across various regions and languages. Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several countries within the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Benin and Ghana. Its natural habitat also extends through the territories of Guinea and Ivory Coast. Furthermore, the species is documented to occur within Liberia. These locations highlight its significant presence throughout this specific part of the continent.
