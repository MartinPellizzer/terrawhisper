What's the taxonomical classification of Pothos pilulifer?

Pothos pilulifer belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic progression, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant falls under the order Alismatales and is a member of the family Araceae. Within this family, it is categorized under the genus Pothos, with its specific designation being the species pilulifer.
