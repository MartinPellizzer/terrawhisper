Morus cathayana

What's the taxonomical classification of Morus cathayana?

Morus cathayana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Rosales. Further taxonomic refinement places this plant in the family Moraceae, within the genus Morus, and is specifically identified by the species epithet cathayana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Morus cathayana has a woody, deciduous growth habit characterized by spreading branches, ovate to lanceolate leaves that exhibit heterophylly, and small, pendulous, aggregate fruits. The leaves can vary significantly in shape, ranging from entire margins to deeply lobed structures depending on the specific branch. Its bark is typically grayish and develops fine longitudinal fissures as the tree matures. The inflorescences are inconspicuous, featuring small, yellowish flowers that develop into fleshy, dark-colored drupes. These fruits are typically elongated and contain numerous tiny seeds embedded within the pulp.
