Plant Parts

This plant, Siraitia grosvenorii, is highly valued for its medicinal and nutritional properties, which are primarily derived from its unique fruits. The most significant part of the plant is the fruit, often referred to as monk fruit, which is harvested once it reaches maturity. Within each fruit, the fleshy pulp contains the seed, which is the component most frequently used in traditional remedies and sweeteners. Additionally, while the fleshy parts are most common, various studies also investigate the potential therapeutic benefits of the Roots and other components of the plant to understand its full medicinal profile.
