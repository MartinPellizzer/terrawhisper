Sneezewort, scientifically known as Achillea ptarmica, is a perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Distinguished by its delicate white flower clusters, this plant has long been used in traditional medicine. Historically, its name reflects its historical use as an emetic, though it remains valued for various therapeutic properties.
