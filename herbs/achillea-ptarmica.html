Achillea ptarmica

Sneezewort

Achillea ptarmica, commonly known as Sneezewort, is a perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Characterized by its delicate white flower clusters, this plant has long been valued in traditional herbalism. It is primarily recognized for its astringent properties and its historical use in treating respiratory and digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Achillea ptarmica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Achillea.
