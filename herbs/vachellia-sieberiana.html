Vachellia sieberiana

Vachellia sieberiana, commonly known as the Mountain Sieber's tree, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of this species are traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, offering valuable insights into natural ethnobotanical healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Vachellia sieberiana
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Vachellia.
