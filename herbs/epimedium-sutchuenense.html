What's the taxonomical classification of Epimedium sutchuenense?

Epimedium sutchuenense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the evolutionary lineage of the order Ranunculales. The plant is further categorized under the family Berberidaceae, which places it within the genus Epimedium, specifically as the species sutchuenense.
