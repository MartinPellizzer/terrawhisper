Arundo formosana

What's the taxonomical classification of Arundo formosana?

Arundo formosana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized into the order Poales and falls within the family Poaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Arundo and the species name formosana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Arundo formosana has tall, perennial, rhizomatous stalks that reach heights of up to 5 meters and consist of segmented, hollow culms with lanceolate leaves. The leaves are long and narrow, measuring up to 60 centimeters in length, and possess a distinct midrib with parallel venation. These plants feature a dense, spreading rhizome system that allows them to form thick, monotypic stands in riparian habitats. The stems are characterized by prominent nodes and internodes that provide structural support for the upright growth habit. Small, feathery panicles emerge at the top of the stalks to facilitate wind pollination.
