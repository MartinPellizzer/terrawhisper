Justicia patentiflora

Justicia patentiflora, commonly known as the Brazilian nettle, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues. This botanical treasure offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Justicia patentiflora
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Justicia and the specific species patentiflora.
