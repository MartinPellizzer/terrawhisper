Montanoa speciosa

Montanoa speciosa, commonly known as the "tree daisy," is a remarkable medicinal shrub native to Mexico. Renowned for its potent emmenagogue properties, it has been used traditionally to regulate menstrual cycles and support reproductive health. This versatile plant offers significant ethnobotanical value for its diverse therapeutic potential and healing qualities.

  • Scientific name: Montanoa speciosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Montanoa speciosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Montanoa.

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within Mexico. Specifically, it can be found throughout Mexico Central, the Southwest, and the Southeast.
