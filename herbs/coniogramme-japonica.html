Coniogramme japonica

What's the taxonomical classification of Coniogramme japonica?

Coniogramme japonica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae, this plant falls under the order Polypodiales. Further taxonomic refinement places it in the family Pteridaceae, specifically within the genus Coniogramme, with the specific epithet japonica defining the species.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Coniogramme japonica has delicate, bipinnate to tripinnate fronds characterized by thin, membranous, light green pinnae that are frequently lanceolate in shape. The stipe is typically slender and dark colored, often appearing brownish or blackish to provide a sharp contrast against the foliage. Small, dark soral patches are located on the underside of the segments, which serve as the primary reproductive structures for the fern. The margins of the leaflets are often finely serrated or slightly lobed, contributing to the plant's intricate and lacy texture. This morphology allows the fern to thrive in shaded, moist environments where its large surface area can efficiently capture low light levels.
