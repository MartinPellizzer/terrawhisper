Lamium gundelsheimeri, scientifically known as Lamium maculatum, is a versatile perennial often called spotted deadnettle. Characterized by striking silvery foliage and delicate purple flowers, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism. It possesses soothing properties and is frequently utilized for its potential medicinal benefits in topical skin applications.
