Andromeda polifolia

Andromeda polifolia, commonly known as a member of the Ericaceae family, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific subtropical regions. Renowned for its unique leaf structure and potent phytochemical properties, it has been traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments and promote overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Andromeda polifolia
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Andromeda, specifically as the species Andromeda polifolia.
