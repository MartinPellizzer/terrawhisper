Bog rosemary, scientifically known as Andromeda poliifolia, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to acidic peatlands and coastal heaths. Characterized by its leathery leaves and bell-shaped flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. However, it contains toxic compounds, requiring careful handling and precise knowledge for medicinal applications.
