Tasmannia lanceolata

Mountain pepper

Tasmannia lanceolata, commonly known as Mountain Pepper, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the cool temperate rainforests of Australia. Renowned for its pungent, peppery berries, this medicinal plant has long been used by Indigenous cultures for its antimicrobial properties and to alleviate various ailments, including digestive and respiratory issues.

  • Scientific name: Tasmannia lanceolata
  • Family: Winteraceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Tasmannia lanceolata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Canellales and the family Winteraceae. It is further categorized under the genus Tasmannia.
