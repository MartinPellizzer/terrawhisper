Macleaya

Aedes (macleaya)

Macleaya, commonly known as the Plume Poppy, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its architectural foliage and dramatic, upright flower spikes. Native to East Asia, this ornamental plant offers a unique blend of botanical elegance and historical interest, making it a captivating addition to any sophisticated garden landscape.

  • Scientific name: Macleaya
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by the specific genus Macleaya.
