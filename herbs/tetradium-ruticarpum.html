Tetradium ruticarpum

Tetraedium ruticarpum, commonly known as the "canela-de-cobra," is a medicinal shrub native to South American landscapes. Renowned for its potent phytochemical properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various infections, offering a rich natural resource for pharmacological study and healing.

  • Scientific name: Tetradium ruticarpum
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Sapindales and the family Rutaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tetradium.
