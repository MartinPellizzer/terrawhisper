Alstonia macrophylla

Alstonia macrophylla, commonly known as the large-leaved Alstoni, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. This botanical species offers significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Alstonia macrophylla
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Alstonia, specifically as the species Alstonia macrophyllia.

Common Names

This plant, Alstonia macrophylla, is known by several different common names depending on the region and language. In Chinese, it is referred to as 大叶糖胶树 (zh-hans), 大葉糖膠樹 (zh-hant), and 大葉糖膠樹 (zh-hk). In Vietnam, the species is commonly called Sữa lá to (vi), while in Thailand, it is known as ทุ้งฟ้า (th).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically within the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in both India and Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is located on the island nation of Sri Lanka. Together, these areas highlight the diverse habitats where the plant thrives.
