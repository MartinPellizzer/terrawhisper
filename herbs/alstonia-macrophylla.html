Hard Alstonia, scientifically known as Alstonia macrophylla, is a prominent medicinal tree native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is extensively used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from fever and malaria to skin infections, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern healthcare.

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically within the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in both India and Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is located on the island nation of Sri Lanka. Plant Parts

This plant, Alstonia macrophylla, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The medicinal value is found throughout its structure, including the stem bark and the root barks, which are often prepared for decoctions. Additionally, the large, lush leaves are a primary focus of study, with both the collective foliage and the individual leaf serving as significant sources of bioactive compounds used in traditional healing practices.
