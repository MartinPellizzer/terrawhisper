Saxifraga tangutica

What's the taxonomical classification of Saxifraga tangutica?

Saxifraga tangutica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this classification, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic lineage further, it falls under the order Saxifragales and is a member of the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by the genus Saxifraga and is specifically classified as the species tangutica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Saxifraga tangutica has a basal rosette of thick, fleshy, ovate leaves that are typically dark green with distinct reddish margins. These blossoms feature five white petals and prominent central stamens that contrast against the foliage. The overall structure of the plant is compact and adapted to rocky, alpine environments.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is native to the high-altitude alpine regions of western China, specifically within the provinces of Sichuan, Yunnan, Gansu, Qinghai, and Tibet. It primarily inhabits rocky crevices and scree slopes located in mountainous terrain. These populations are typically found at elevations ranging from 2,500 to 4,500 meters above sea level. The species thrives in these specific microclimates where moisture is consistent despite the harsh, rocky substrate.
