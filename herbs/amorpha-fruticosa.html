Amorpha fruticosa

Amorpha fruticosa, commonly known as false indigo bush, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to North American wetlands. Renowned for its striking purple flower spikes, this nitrogen-fixing plant offers significant ecological value. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it possesses bioactive properties with potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory applications.

  • Scientific name: Amorpha fruticosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Amorpha fruticosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and the genus Amorpha.
