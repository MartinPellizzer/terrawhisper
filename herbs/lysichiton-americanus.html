Lysichiton americanus

Western skunk cabbage

Lysichiton americanus, commonly known as western skunk cabbage, is a unique perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Renowned for its ability to generate heat through thermogenesis, it emerges early in spring. While visually striking, its pungent odor and chemical composition necessitate cautious handling and careful medicinal consideration.

  • Scientific name: Lysichiton americanus
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Alismatales and family Araceae, it is classified under the genus Lysichiton.
