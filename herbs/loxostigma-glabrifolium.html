What's the taxonomical classification of Loxostigma glabrifolium?

Loxostigma glabrifolium belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It falls under the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Gesneriaceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its specific genus, Loxostigma, and its unique species designation, glabrifolium.
