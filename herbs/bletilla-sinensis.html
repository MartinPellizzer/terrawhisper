Bletilla sinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Bletilla sinensis?

Blettilla sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Asparagales. It is a member of the Orchidaceae family, specifically situated within the genus Blettilla, and is identified by its unique species name, sinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?
