Jointed flatsedge, scientifically known as Cyperus articulatus, is a perennial sedge valued in traditional medicine across Asia. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it is frequently used to treat digestive ailments, inflammatory conditions, and menstrual irregularities. This versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its diverse bioactive phytochemical properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Algeria and Egypt. Moving toward the Atlantic, it can be found within the Macaronesian islands of Cape Verde. The species also extends into West Tropical Africa, where it is documented in Benin. Plant Parts

This plant, Cyperus articulatus, is a medicinal herb characterized by its distinct structural components. The most therapeutically significant parts are the rhizomes, which are often referred to as the rhizome mass used in traditional remedies. These underground structures are accompanied by a network of fibrous roots that help anchor the plant. Above the ground, the plant features elongated, slender leaves that are typical of the sedge family. Furthermore, the plant is known to contain various EOs (essential oils), which contribute to its aromatic properties and medicinal potency.
