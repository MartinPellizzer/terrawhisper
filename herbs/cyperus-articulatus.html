Jointed flatsedge, scientifically known as Cyperus articulatus, is a perennial sedge valued in traditional medicine across Asia. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it is frequently used to treat digestive ailments, inflammatory conditions, and menstrual irregularities. This versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its diverse bioactive phytochemical properties.
