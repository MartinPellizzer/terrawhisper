Urtica circularis

Urtica circularis, commonly known as the circular nettle, is a fascinating medicinal plant celebrated for its unique healing properties. Characterized by its distinctive leaf structure, this species is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support inflammatory relief and enhance vitality, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and recovery.

  • Scientific name: Urtica circularis
  • Family: Urticaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its lineage through the subclass Magnoliidae and order Rosales, it is placed in the family Urticaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Urtica.

Common Names

This plant, Urtica circularis, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the circular nettle, while in Spanish, it is known as ortiga circular. In French, the plant is called ortie circulaire, and in German, it is identified as kreisförmige Brennnessel.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several regions of South America. In Western South America, its range includes the territory of Bolivia. Its distribution extends into Brazil, specifically within the southern portion of the country. Moving into Southern South America, it can be found throughout Paraguay. Additionally, the species inhabits the northeast and northwest regions of Argentina.
