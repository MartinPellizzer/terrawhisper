Quercus infectoria

Quercus infectoria, commonly known as Aleurates or Oak Gall, is a potent medicinal plant prized for its high tannin content. Historically used in traditional medicine, it possesses significant astringent, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties. Today, it remains a vital natural resource for treating skin conditions and promoting oral health.

  • Scientific name: Quercus infectoria
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fagales, it is a member of the Fagaceae family. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Quercus.
