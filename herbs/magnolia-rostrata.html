Magnolia rostrata

Magnolia rostrata, commonly known as the Beaked Magnolia, is a stunning evergreen tree native to the subtropical forests of Vietnam. Renowned for its distinctive, large, creamy-white blooms and unique elongated petals, this rare species combines ornamental elegance with botanical significance, making it a prized treasure for collectors and enthusiasts.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia rostrata
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, falling into the family Magnoliaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Magnolia.
