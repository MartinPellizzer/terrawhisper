Stereospermum fimbriatum

Stereosperma fimbriatum, commonly known as the "Indian climbing plant," is a versatile medicinal climber native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Various parts of this plant are utilized to treat skin ailments, fever, and digestive issues through holistic applications.

  • Scientific name: Stereospermum fimbriatum
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further categorized into the family Bignoniaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Stereospermum.
