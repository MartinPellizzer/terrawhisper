Heterotis rotundifolia

Heterotis rotundifolia, commonly known as the round-leaved heterotis, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional African medicine. Characterized by its distinctive foliage, this species is frequently utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial activities, offering valuable insights into natural pharmacology and regional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Heterotis rotundifolia
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Myrtaleales and the family Melastomataceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Heterotis, with the specific species being Heterotis rotundifolia.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Heterotis rotundifolia, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In the Ilocano language, it is referred to as Dissotis rotundifolia, while in Malayalam, it is known as വള്ളി അതിരാണി. In Chinese contexts, it is identified as 蔓性野牡丹 (zh and zh-Hans) and is also referred to as Dissotis rotundifolia in Taiwan (tw).

Distribution

This plant, Heterotis rotundifolia, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Furthermore, its presence extends to the coastal and inland areas of Guinea-Bissau. The species is also documented to inhabit parts of Guinea and Sierra Leone. Finally, its range includes the territory of Togo, completing its regional footprint.

Plant Parts

This plant, Heterotis rotundifolia, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its vegetative components, specifically the fresh and dried leaves. When used in their fresh state, the leaves retain their full succulent texture and volatile properties, which are often preferred for immediate applications. Alternatively, the dried leaves serve as a stable, concentrated form that can be easily stored, powdered, or steeped into decoctions, providing a versatile way to harness the plant's therapeutic potential.
