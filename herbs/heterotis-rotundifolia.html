Distribution

This plant, Heterotis rotundifolia, exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Furthermore, its presence extends to the coastal and inland areas of Guinea-Bissau. The species is also documented to inhabit parts of Guinea and Sierra Leone. Finally, its range includes the territory of Togo, completing its regional footprint.

Plant Parts

This plant, Heterotis rotundifolia, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its vegetative components, specifically the fresh and dried leaves. When used in their fresh state, the leaves retain their full succulent texture and volatile properties, which are often preferred for immediate applications. Alternatively, the dried leaves serve as a stable, concentrated form that can be easily stored, powdered, or steeped into decoctions, providing a versatile way to harness the plant's therapeutic potential.
