Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions of Eastern Asia. Within China, it can be found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Its presence also extends northward into the neighboring territories of Korea. Furthermore, the species is documented to grow naturally in Japan. Together, these areas define the specific environmental range where Dendropaunax thrives.
