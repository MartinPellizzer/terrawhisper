Salaacia oblonga, scientifically known as Salaacia oblonga, is a woody climber native to the tropical forests of India and Sri Lanka. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, this potent plant is primarily utilized for its remarkable anti-diabetic properties, helping to regulate blood sugar levels and support overall metabolic health naturally.
