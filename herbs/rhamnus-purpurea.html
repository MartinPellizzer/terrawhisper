Rhamnus purpurea, scientifically known as Rhamnus purpurea, is a deciduous shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its medicinal properties, its bark contains anthraquinone glycosides, traditionally used as a potent stimulant laxative. This versatile plant plays a significant role in both traditional herbalism and modern pharmacological studies.
