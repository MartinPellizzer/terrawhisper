Rhamnus purpurea

Rhamnus purpurea, commonly known as Purple Buckthorn, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to Europe. Renowned for its dark, glossy foliage and distinctive berries, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically used in traditional medicine, its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic properties, making it a subject of botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Rhamnus purpurea
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, it is a member of the Rhamnaceae family. It is further identified by its genus, Rhamnus.
