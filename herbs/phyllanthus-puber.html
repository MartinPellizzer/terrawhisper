Phyllanthus puber

Phyllanthus puber, commonly known as the hairy phyllanthus, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its fine pubescence, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties. It is widely utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial effects, offering therapeutic potential for various skin and digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus puber
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Malpighiales and is a member of the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across the various regions of China. Its presence is recorded within the South-Central areas of the country. Additionally, it can be found growing in the tropical environment of Hainan. The species also extends its reach into the North-Central provinces of China. Finally, its geographical distribution reaches into the high-altitude regions of Tibet.
