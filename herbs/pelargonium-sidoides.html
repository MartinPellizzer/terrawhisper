Pelargonium sidoides

Pelargonium sidoides, commonly known as African geranium, is a powerful medicinal plant native to South Africa. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and antiviral properties, it is frequently used in herbal medicine to support respiratory health. This botanical extract serves as a key ingredient in modern cough and cold remedies.

  • Scientific name: Pelargonium sidoides
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales, it is classified under the family Geraniaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pelargonium.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. It is widely distributed across the Cape Provinces, where it thrives in various ecological niches. Additionally, its range extends into the mountainous regions of Lesotho. The species can also be located within the provinces of KwaZulu-Natal and the Free State. Finally, its geographical presence includes the territory of Eswatini.
