Distribution

This plant is primarily found throughout the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. It is widely distributed across the Cape Provinces, where it thrives in various ecological niches. Additionally, its range extends into the mountainous regions of Lesotho. The species can also be located within the provinces of KwaZulu-Natal and the Free State. Finally, its geographical presence includes the territory of Eswatini.

Plant Parts

This plant, Pelargonium sidoides, is highly valued in herbal medicine for its complex underground structures. The medicinal potency is primarily concentrated in its thick, fleshy tubers, which are often referred to as the roots of the plant. These specialized roots serve as the storage organs for active compounds, and it is from these tubers that potent root extracts are derived for therapeutic use. While the above-ground stems and leaves are characteristic of the species, the most significant pharmacological properties are found within the subterranean parts, making the harvested root the essential component for medicinal preparation.
