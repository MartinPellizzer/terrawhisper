Planchonia careya

Planchonia careya, commonly known as the "Sandalwood Tree" or "Kaimari," is a versatile medicinal plant native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its bark and leaves are traditionally used in Ayurvedic medicine to treat skin diseases, inflammation, and various digestive ailments through natural healing.
