Hexasepalum teres

Hexasepalum teres, commonly known as the Wireleaf, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its unique linear foliage, this plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential, making it a subject of growing interest in ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Hexasepalum teres
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family. It is further categorized under the genus Hexasepalum, specifically as the species Hexasepalum teres.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. In Middle Europe, its presence is noted within the Netherlands. Moving toward the Atlantic, it can be found in the Macaronesian region of Cape Verde. The species also extends into West Tropical Africa, specifically inhabiting Gambia and Guinea-Bissau. Furthermore, its range continues through the West Tropical African nation of Senegal.
