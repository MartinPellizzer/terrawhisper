Small knobwood, scientifically known as Zanthoxylum capense, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to African regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, the plant is used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues. Its aromatic leaves and bark offer significant pharmacological potential.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rutaceae and falls under the genus Zanthoxylum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. Within South Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Mozambique and Zimbabwe. Moving into the Southern African region, its presence is well-documented in the Cape Provinces. It is also widely distributed throughout the KwaZulu-Natal area. Finally, the species extends its range into the Free State province.
