Adelostemma gracillimum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, it is placed under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Within this family, it is defined by the genus Adelostemma, with its specific identification being the species gracillimum.
