Bassia indica

Bassia indica, commonly known as Shova in traditional medicine, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is extensively used in Ayurvedic practices to treat various ailments, including skin disorders, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant potential in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Bassia indica
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Amaranthaceae and is identified by the genus Bassia.
