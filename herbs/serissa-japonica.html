Serissa japonica

Serissa japonica, commonly known as Snowbush or Tiny Star, is a versatile evergreen shrub celebrated for its delicate white flowers. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its leaves are utilized for their potential therapeutic properties and diverse bioactive compounds in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Serissa japonica
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Serissa japonica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales. Finally, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family within the genus Serissa.
