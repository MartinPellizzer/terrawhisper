Ilex bioritsensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Ilex bioritsensis?

Ilex bioritsensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It falls under the order Aquifoliales and is a member of the family Aquifoliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed within the genus Ilex, specifically as the species bioritsensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ilex bioritsensis has small, elliptical to ovate leaves that are typically 1 to 3 centimeters long, featuring an entire margin and a coriaceous, leathery texture. The leaf surfaces are dark green on the upper side and often slightly paler on the underside. These leaves are arranged oppositely or sub-oppositely along the woody stems of the shrub. The plant produces tiny, inconspicuous flowers that eventually develop into small, dark fleshy drupes. This species generally maintains a compact, bushy growth habit suitable for its Mediterranean habitat.
