, scientifically known as Huperzia carinaata, is a significant medicinal clubmoss valued in traditional pharmacology. Primarily recognized for its potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitory properties, it contains huperzine A. This alkaloid is widely studied for its neuroprotective potential, offering promising applications in treating cognitive decline and Alzheimer's disease symptoms.
