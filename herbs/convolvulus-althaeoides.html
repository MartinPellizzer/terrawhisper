Convolvulus althaeoides

Convolvulus althaeoides, commonly known as the Mallow Bindweed, is a distinctive climbing plant belonging to the Convolvulaceae family. Characterized by its delicate, funnel-shaped flowers and trailing habit, this species holds significant interest in ethnobotany. It is often studied for its potential bioactive compounds and traditional medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Convolvulus althaeoides
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified in the order Solanales and the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus name, Convolvulus.
