Malus baccata var. baccata

Malus baccata var. baccata, commonly known as the Chinese hawthorn, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its nutrient-rich fruits, it offers diverse therapeutic benefits, particularly for digestive health. This versatile plant serves as a vital resource in botanical medicine and holistic wellness treatments.

  • Scientific name: Malus baccata var. baccata
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Malus. Specifically, its taxonomic designation is Malus baccata var. baccata.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Malus baccata var. baccata, is recognized by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the Chinese hawthorn or simply hawthorn. Moving into regional dialects, it is known as shanzha in Chinese and hawthorn berry in some English-speaking contexts. In Spanish, it is commonly called espino blanco, while in French, it is identified as aubépine. Furthermore, in Portuguese, it is known as espinheiro-alvar, providing a diverse linguistic landscape for this medicinal species.
