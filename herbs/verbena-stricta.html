Hoary vervain, scientifically known as Verbena stricta, is a hardy, perennial herb native to North America. Characterized by its upright stems and clusters of small, purple flowers, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its restorative properties, often used to support digestive health and calm the nervous system.
