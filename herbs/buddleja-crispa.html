Himalayan butterfly bush, scientifically known as Buddlejia crispia, is a striking perennial shrub native to the high altitudes of the Himalayas. Renowned for its fragrant, panicle-shaped flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and to promote overall wellness.
