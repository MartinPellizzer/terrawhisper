Himalayan butterfly bush, scientifically known as Buddlejia crispia, is a striking perennial shrub native to the high altitudes of the Himalayas. Renowned for its fragrant, panicle-shaped flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies to treat respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and to promote overall wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the butterfly bush or Buddleja crispa, is utilized in traditional medicine primarily through the use of the whole plant. Every part of the organism, from its fragrant terminal panicles and lanceolate leaves to its woody stems and root system, contains a complex array of bioactive compounds that contribute to its therapeutic properties. In various folk medicine practices, the entire herb is often decocted or prepared as a tincture to harness its full medicinal potential, making the whole plant a versatile resource for treating various ailments.
