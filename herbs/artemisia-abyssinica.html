Artemisia abyssinica

Artemisia abyssinicica, commonly known as an important medicinal herb in East Africa, holds significant ethnobotanical value. Native to high-altitude regions, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including gastrointestinal issues and respiratory infections, showcasing its profound biochemical potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia abyssinica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa
