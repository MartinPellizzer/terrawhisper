Madrasthorn, scientifically known as Pithecellobium dulce, is a versatile tropical shrub valued for its extensive medicinal properties. Widely used in traditional practices, its leaves, bark, and roots offer therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing effects. This resilient plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical research and herbal medicine.
