Sea hibiscus, scientifically known as Hibiscus tiliaceus, is a perennial shrub renowned for its large, striking flowers and significant therapeutic potential. Distribution

This plant is primarily found within the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its presence is specifically documented in the nation of Gambia, where it thrives in local ecosystems. It also grows across various regions of Guinea, including Guinea-Bissau. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout Ghana and the Ivory Coast.
