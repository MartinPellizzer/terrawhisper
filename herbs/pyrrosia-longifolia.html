Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae, it is categorized under the order Polypodiales and the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Pyrrosia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, it can be found growing across the South-Central regions as well as on the island of Hainan. Its range extends further into the Indo-China peninsula, where it is documented in various habitats. Specifically, the species is native to the countries of Cambodia and Laos. Additionally, its geographical presence includes the mountainous and tropical areas of Myanmar.

Plant Parts

This plant, Pyrrosia longifolia, is a species of epiphytic fern where the medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in its aerial part. This includes the creeping rhizomes that grow along the substrate, as well as the characteristic long, linear fronds that extend into the air to capture light and moisture. Because the active constituents are located in these above-ground structures, the aerial part is the specific portion harvested for traditional therapeutic applications.
