Plumeria alba

Plumeria alba, commonly known as white frangipani, is a stunning tropical shrub celebrated for its fragrant, waxy white blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, utilized for its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic properties to treat various skin ailments and systemic conditions.

  • Scientific name: Plumeria alba
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Plumeria alba, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Its taxonomic hierarchy concludes with the genus Plumeria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across both Asia and the Caribbean. Within the Indian Subcontinent, Plumeria alba can be found growing in the region of Assam and throughout Bangladesh. Its presence extends into the Caribbean islands, specifically inhabiting the Leeward Islands and Puerto Rico. Additionally, the species is documented in the archipelago of Trinidad and Tobago. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in different tropical environments.
