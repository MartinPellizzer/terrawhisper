Adenophora capillaris

What's the taxonomical classification of Adenophora capillaris?

Adenophora capillaris belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Asterales and into the family Campanulaceae. Finally, the plant is placed within the genus Adenophora, with its specific designation being the species capillaris.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Adenophora capillaris has an erect, perennial herbaceous habit characterized by slender, cylindrical stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 30 centimeters. The leaves are oppositely arranged, possessing an ovate to lanceolate shape with finely serrated margins and acute tips. Small, bell-shaped flowers emerge from the upper leaf axils, displaying a purplish or pale lavender coloration. The inflorescence is often loosely arranged in a terminal panicle or raceme. Its root system consists of a thin, creeping rhizome that facilitates vegetative spread within its habitat.
