Asystasia mysorensis

Asystaesia mysorenensis, commonly known as the Mysore Achyranthes, is a versatile medicinal herb native to India. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently used in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This potent plant offers significant pharmacological potential for modern healthcare applications.
