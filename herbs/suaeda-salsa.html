Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Suaeda salsa, is characterized by its succulent morphology, where the leaves play a vital role in its physiological function. The leaves are typically small, fleshy, and cylindrical or linear in shape, a specialized adaptation that allows the plant to store water and manage salinity in its coastal or saline environments. These succulent leaves are essential for the plant's survival, as they facilitate the accumulation of salts and provide the necessary moisture to endure harsh, salty habitats.
