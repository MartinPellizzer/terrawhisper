Artemisia austriaca

Artemisia austriaca, commonly known as Austrian wormwood, is a perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Native to various European regions, this plant is characterized by its aromatic, silvery-green foliage. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it possesses diverse bioactive compounds used for their potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia austriaca
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Artemisia, specifically as the species Artemisia austriaca.
