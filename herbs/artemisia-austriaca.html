Austrian Mugwort, scientifically known as Artemisia austriaca, is a perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Native to central and southern Europe, this aromatic plant is characterized by its silver-green foliage. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it is studied for its diverse bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic properties.
