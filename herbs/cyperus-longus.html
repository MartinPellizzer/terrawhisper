Cyperus longus

Cypterus longus, commonly known as sweet flag or long cyperus, is a perennial medicinal herb valued in traditional healing systems. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, digestive, and antimicrobial properties. This versatile plant has long been used to treat gastrointestinal issues and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cyperus longus
  • Family: Cyperaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Poales and the family Cyperaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Cypterus.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cyperus longus, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic applications across various forms. The whole plants are often utilized in herbal preparations, though the rhizomes are the primary source of its bioactive properties. To harness its medicinal potency, researchers and herbalists frequently prepare an extract through various solvent processes, allowing for a concentrated dose of its healing compounds. Furthermore, the plant is a significant source of its aromatic essential oil, which is distilled from the rootstock to capture its complex chemical profile. This EO is widely used in aromatherapy and topical formulations due to its intense fragrance and calming physiological effects.
