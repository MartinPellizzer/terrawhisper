Hard Vetch, scientifically known as Vicia monantha, is a resilient legume native to various European landscapes. Characterized by its delicate purple flowers and climbing habit, this plant plays a vital role in nitrogen fixation, enhancing soil fertility. While primarily used in ecological restoration, it holds significant botanical interest.
