Acer

Acer, commonly known as maple, is a diverse genus of trees valued for both ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Beyond its famous syrup, various species offer therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. Historically used in traditional practices, Acer extracts are increasingly studied for their bioactive compounds and health benefits.

  • Scientific name: Acer
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae, it is classified under the genus Acer.
