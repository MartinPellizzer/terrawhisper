Iris schachtii

Iris schachtii, commonly known as the Schacht's Iris, is a resilient perennial native to the rocky terrains of the Middle East. Valued for its striking floral displays and potential ethnobotanical properties, this species offers unique insights into medicinal plant biodiversity, serving as a subject of interest for pharmacological studies.
