Echinops bovei

Echinops bovei, commonly known as the sphere thistle, is a striking medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its unique, globe-like blue flower heads, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional healers utilize various parts of this hardy perennial to treat ailments, showcasing its profound importance in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Echinops bovei
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Echinops.
