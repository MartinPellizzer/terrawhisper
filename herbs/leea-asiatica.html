Leea asiatica

Leea asiatica, commonly known as the Malabar Leea, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient plant offers significant pharmacological potential for developing natural remedies in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Leea asiatica
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Vitales, falling into the family Vitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Leea and the specific species Leea asiatica.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Asian continent. In China, its presence is specifically noted throughout the South-Central territories. Moving to the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found in the state of Assam and across the country of India. It also inhabits the unique landscape of the East Himalaya region. Finally, its geographical range extends into the neighboring nation of Bangladesh.
