What's the taxonomical classification of Eurya weissiae?

Eurya weissiae belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ericales. This plant is specifically categorized under the family Pentaphylacaceae, and its taxonomic hierarchy is completed by its placement in the genus Eurya and its designation as the species weissiae.
