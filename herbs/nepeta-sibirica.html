Siberian Catmint, scientifically known as Nepeta sibirica, is a hardy perennial herb native to the steppes of Central Asia. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate lavender blossoms, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its calming properties and is a favorite in both gardens and herbalism.
