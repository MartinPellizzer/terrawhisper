This plant, Euclea crispa, is frequently valued for its therapeutic potential, particularly through the use of its leaves. These leaves are characterized by their distinctively wavy or "crisped" margins, which give the species its specific epithet. In various traditional medicinal practices, the leaves are harvested and prepared to treat a variety of ailments, often being utilized in decoctions or infusions to address inflammatory conditions or skin issues.
