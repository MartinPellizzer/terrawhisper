Euclea crispa

Blue guarri

Euclea crispa, commonly known as the Wild Date Plum, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin conditions. This versatile plant holds significant ethnobotanical value for its diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Euclea crispa
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ebenaceae and is situated within the genus Euclea.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Euclea crispa, is referred to by various names depending on the language and region. In English, it is commonly called blue guarri, while in Afrikaans, it is known as Bloughwarrie, and in Tsonga, it is called Nhlangula.

Distribution

This plant, Euclea crispa, exhibits a specific geographical distribution throughout the South Tropical Africa region.
