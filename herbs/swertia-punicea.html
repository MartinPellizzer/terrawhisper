Swertia punicea

Swertiia punicea, commonly known as the Punicea Gentian, is a significant medicinal herb valued in traditional pharmacology. Belonging to the Gentianaceae family, this plant is prized for its potent bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized for its bitter properties, supporting digestive health and exhibiting various antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory activities.

  • Scientific name: Swertia punicea
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
