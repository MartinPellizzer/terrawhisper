Coptis omeiensis

Coptis omeiensis, commonly known as Chinese goldthread, is a perennial herb native to high-altitude regions of China. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains potent alkaloids like berberine. These compounds provide significant antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties, making it a vital resource for treating gastrointestinal and metabolic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Coptis omeiensis
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is further classified under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation falls within the genus Coptis.

Common Names

This plant, Coptis omeiensis, is known by several distinct common names depending on the linguistic context.
