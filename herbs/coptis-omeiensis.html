Coptis omeiensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Coptis omeiensis?

Coptis omeiensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. At the class level, it is categorized as Equisetopsida, falling under the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is further organized into the order Ranunculales and is a member of the family Ranunculaceae. Within this family, it is placed in the genus Coptis, and its specific biological identity is defined by the species name omeiensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Coptis omeiensis has a rhizomatous perennial structure featuring basal leaves that are deeply divided into multiple narrow, linear segments. These leaves are typically arranged in a rosette pattern and exhibit a bright green coloration. The plant produces small, solitary flowers that emerge on slender stalks from the center of the foliage. Each flower contains several yellow petals and numerous stamens characteristic of the Ranunculaceae family. The reproductive structures eventually develop into small, globose capsules containing numerous tiny seeds.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

This plant is endemic to the high-altitude regions of central China, specifically within the mountainous provinces of Sichuan and Hubei. It typically inhabits rocky crevices and shaded slopes at elevations ranging from 2,000 to 3,500 meters above sea level. These populations are often found in temperate montane forests where moisture is consistent throughout the growing season. The specific microclimates required for its survival limit its presence to fragmented patches within these mountain ranges. Due to its restricted range, the species maintains a highly localized distribution pattern.
