Coptis omeiensis, scientifically known as Coptis omeiensis, is a perennial medicinal herb native to high-altitude regions in China. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly berberine, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, gastrointestinal issues, and infections, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology.
