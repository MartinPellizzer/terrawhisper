Calanthe davidii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, placing it in the order Asparagales. As a member of the family Orchidaceae, this plant is further categorized under the genus Calanthe, eventually reaching its specific designation as the species davidii.
