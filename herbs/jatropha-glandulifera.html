Jatropha glandulifera

Jatrophia glandulifera, commonly known as the flowering spurge, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional healing practices, its various parts are utilized for their potent bioactive compounds. This plant offers significant pharmacological potential, particularly in treating skin ailments and managing inflammatory conditions naturally.
