Strychnos ignatia, scientifically known as Strychnos ignatii, is a tropical climbing shrub native to the West Indies. Renowned for its potent seeds, commonly called Saint Ignatius beans, this plant contains strychnine and brucine. Historically used in pharmacology, it serves as a powerful stimulant and a significant subject in toxicology.
