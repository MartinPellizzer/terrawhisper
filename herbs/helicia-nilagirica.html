Helicia nilagirica

Helicia nilagirica, commonly known as a rare medicinal shrub, is an endemic species native to the Western Ghats of India. Renowned for its unique botanical properties, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Research focuses on its potential bioactive compounds, offering promising prospects for developing diverse therapeutic applications in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Helicia nilagirica
  • Family: Proteaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Proteales, it is a member of the Proteaceae family. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Helicia.
