Bouea macrophyllla, scientifically known as Bouea macrophyllla, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree native to Southeast Asia. Commonly called the Manilkara or Mango Plum, it is prized for its succulent, sweet-tart fruit. Beyond its culinary appeal, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine for their healing properties.
