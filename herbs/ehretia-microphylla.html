Ehretia microphylla

Ehretiia microphylla, commonly known as the small-leaved Ehratia, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource in various indigenous pharmacological traditions.
