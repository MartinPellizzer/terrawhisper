Salvia

Salvia, commonly known as sage, is a versatile genus of flowering plants cherished for both culinary and medicinal purposes. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently used to support cognitive function, soothe sore throats, and promote digestive health, making it a cornerstone in traditional herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Salvia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling under the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Salvia.
