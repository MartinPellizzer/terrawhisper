Sage, scientifically known as Salvia, is a versatile genus of flowering plants prized for centuries in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently used to support cognitive function, soothe sore throats, and enhance digestive health, making it a staple in holistic wellness.
