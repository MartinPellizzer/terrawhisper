Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions in Africa. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in Algeria, Egypt, and Morocco. Its range extends southward into the West Tropical Africa region as well. Specifically, the species is documented in the countries of Benin and Mali. Therefore, its habitat spans both the northern and western tropical zones of the continent.
