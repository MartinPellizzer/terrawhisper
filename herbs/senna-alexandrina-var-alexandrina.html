Senna alexandrina var. alexandrina

Senna alexandrina var. alexandrina, commonly known as Senna, is a prominent medicinal shrub native to Africa and the Middle East. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, it contains bioactive sennosides. This plant is widely utilized in traditional and modern medicine to effectively treat acute constipation and intestinal issues.

  • Scientific name: Senna alexandrina var. alexandrina
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Senna. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined as Senna alexandrina var. alexandrina.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Senna alexandrina var. alexandrina, is known by several common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is most frequently referred to as Alexandrian senna, while in Arabic, it is commonly called senna (سنا). In French, the plant is known as séné, and in Spanish, it is called sen.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions in Africa. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in Algeria, Egypt, and Morocco. Its range extends southward into the West Tropical Africa region as well. Specifically, the species is documented in the countries of Benin and Mali. Therefore, its habitat spans both the northern and western tropical zones of the continent.
