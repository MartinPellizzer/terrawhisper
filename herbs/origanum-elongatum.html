Origanum elongatum

Origanum elongatum, commonly known as elongated oregano, is a resilient aromatic herb native to Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its distinctive elongated leaves, this plant belongs to the Lamiaceae family. It is highly valued in traditional medicine and culinary practices for its potent essential oils and diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Origanum elongatum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Origanum elongatum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae and the genus Origanum.
