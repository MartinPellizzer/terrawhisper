Distribution

This plant is a specialized subspecies with a very specific and limited range. Its geographical distribution is confined to the Western Asia region. Specifically, it can be found growing within the territories of Lebanon and Syria. Because its habitat is restricted to these two countries, it is considered an endemic taxon to this area. This narrow distribution highlights the unique ecological niche it occupies in the Levantine landscape.
