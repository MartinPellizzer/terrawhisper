Wedelia acapulcensis var. hispida

Wedelia acapulcensis hispida

Wedelia acapulcensis var. hispida, commonly known as "Pata de vaca," is a significant medicinal herb utilized in traditional folk medicine. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently employed to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic health and wellness.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Northern America
