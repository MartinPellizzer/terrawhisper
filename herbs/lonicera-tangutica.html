What's the taxonomical classification of Lonicera tangutica?

Lonicera tangutica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Dipsacales. This plant is a member of the family Caprifoliaceae, falling under the genus Lonicera, and is specifically identified by the species name tangutica.
