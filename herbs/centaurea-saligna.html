Centaurea saligna

Centaurea saliigna, commonly known as the saltwort knapweed, is a resilient medicinal plant native to coastal regions. Characterized by its silvery foliage and vibrant blooms, it possesses unique phytochemical properties. Traditionally utilized for its restorative qualities, this hardy species offers promising potential in modern pharmacological research and holistic wellness.
