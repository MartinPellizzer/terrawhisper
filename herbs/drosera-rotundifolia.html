Round-leaved sundew, scientifically known as Drosera rotundifolia, is a carnivorous perennial plant native to acidic bogs. Distinguished by its sticky, glandular tentacles, it traps insects to supplement nutrients in nitrogen-poor soils. Historically used in traditional medicine, it offers potential therapeutic properties, particularly for soothing respiratory ailments and coughs.
