Drosera rotundifolia

Drosera rotundifolia, commonly known as the round-leaved sundew, is a fascinating carnivorous plant native to peat bogs. Characterized by its glandular, sticky tentacles, it captures and digests insects to supplement nutrients in nitrogen-poor soils. Historically, this resilient species has been utilized in traditional medicine for its various therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Drosera rotundifolia
  • Family: Droseraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it falls into the family Droseraceae.
