Corchorus depressus

Corchorus depressus, commonly known as the low jute, is a versatile medicinal herb found across tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and digestive issues, this plant offers valuable phytochemical benefits for holistic health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Corchorus depressus
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Corchorus depressus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, falling into the family Malvaceae. Ultimately, it is situated within the genus Corchorus.
