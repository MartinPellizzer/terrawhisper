Plant Parts

This plant, known as Euphorbia helioscopia, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components are utilized for therapeutic purposes. Often, practitioners refer to the medicinal efficacy of the whole plants, as the interaction between the leaves, stems, and flowers contributes to its complex chemical profile. In many traditional preparations, the whole plant is harvested to ensure that all bioactive compounds, including the characteristic milky latex, are included in the remedy. Specifically, the aerial part of the plant is frequently collected for drying and processing into infusions or extracts, focusing on the structures above the ground to facilitate ease of collection and standardization of use.
