Euphorbia helioscopia

Euphorbia helioscopia, commonly known as Sun Spurge, is a distinctive medicinal plant characterized by its bright yellow flowers and milky latex. Historically utilized in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory and purgative properties, this species requires careful handling due to its potent, skin-irritating sap and complex chemical composition.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia helioscopia
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and belongs to the genus Euphorbia.
