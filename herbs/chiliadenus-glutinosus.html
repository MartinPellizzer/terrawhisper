Chiliadenus glutinosus

Chiliadenus glutinosus, commonly known as the sticky chiliaden, is a unique medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Characterized by its distinctive resinous texture, this species offers therapeutic potential through its bioactive compounds. Understanding its botanical properties and traditional applications provides vital insights into its role in natural pharmacology.
