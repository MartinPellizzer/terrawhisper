Flueggea suffruticosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Flueggea suffruticosa?

Flueggea suffruticosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant falls under the order Malpighiales. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the family Phyllanthaceae and the genus Flueggea, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species suffruticosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Flueggea suffruticosa has a woody, shrubby habit characterized by slender, branching stems and simple, alternate leaves that are typically elliptic to oblong in shape. The foliage is often leathery in texture and possesses entire margins with prominent pinnate venation. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the axils of the leaves, often appearing in clusters. These flowers eventually develop into small, globose fruits that contain several seeds. The overall structure of the plant is compact, allowing it to thrive in various scrubland environments.

What medicinal compounds this plant contains?

This plant contains various bioactive alkaloids, most notably the specific group of compounds known as Secupyritiines A-C. These secondary metabolites are primarily responsible for the medicinal properties attributed to the species in traditional healing practices. Researchers have identified these alkaloids as key components that contribute to the plant's overall pharmacological potential. Beyond these specific secupyritiines, the chemical profile of Flueggea suffruticosa includes other diverse phytochemicals. Studying these unique chemical constituents helps scientists understand the biological mechanisms behind the plant's therapeutic uses.

According to a study published by "Angewandte Chemie (International ed. in English)", the medicinal plant Flueggea suffruticosa contains three natural Securinega alkaloids known as secupyritinines A-C. These unique polycyclic compounds feature a distinctive 6/6/6/5/6 fused pentacyclic ring system. The molecules are characterized by a highly strained 2-oxa-6-aza[4.4.3]propellane core. The total synthesis of these secupyrinitines A-C was successfully achieved in 14 or 16 steps.
