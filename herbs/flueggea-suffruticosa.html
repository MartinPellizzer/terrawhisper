Flueggea suffruticosa

Flueggea suffruticosa, commonly known as the "wild fluiggia," is a resilient medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, its leaves and roots are frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This plant serves as a vital natural resource in many indigenous healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Flueggea suffruticosa
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is situated in the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Flueggea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In the colder climates of Siberia, it can be specifically found in the Chita region. Moving toward the eastern coast, the species inhabits the Russian Far East, particularly within the Amur and Primorye territories. Its range extends significantly into China, where it is documented throughout the South-Central provinces. Finally, the plant's distribution reaches tropical latitudes in the southernmost island of Hainan.
