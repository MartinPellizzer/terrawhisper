Shrubby Baeckea, scientifically known as Baeckea frutescens, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to Australia. Highly valued for its aromatic foliage, this plant offers diverse medicinal potential. Historically used in traditional healing practices, its essential oils and bioactive compounds provide valuable insights into contemporary pharmacological research and natural wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the Hainan province as well as throughout the China Southeast region. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, the species can be found growing in Cambodia. It also maintains a population within the borders of Laos. Finally, its range extends into Myanmar, completing its regional distribution.

Plant Parts

This plant, Baeckea frutescens, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties through the use of its various aerial parts. When preparing traditional remedies, practitioners often collect the woody twigs and the smaller, slender twig segments to extract essential compounds. Additionally, the abundant green leaves are a key component of the plant's therapeutic profile, where each individual leaf contributes to the overall potency of the herbal preparation.
