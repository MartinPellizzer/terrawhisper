Distribution

This plant is widely distributed throughout the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, it can be found growing in the natural landscapes of Benin. Its presence is also documented within the borders of Burkina. Furthermore, the species inhabits various parts of Ghana and Gambia. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant extends into Guinea-Bissau.
