Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across East Asia, spanning several distinct regions. In the Russian Far East, its presence is noted in the Khabarovsk area and throughout the Kuril Islands. It can also be found within the territories of Primorye and Sakhalin. Furthermore, its distribution extends into China, specifically within the Manchuria region. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive across varying coastal and continental environments.
