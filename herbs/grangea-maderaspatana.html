Grangea maderaspatana

Madras carpet

Graneia maderaspatana, commonly known as madera de patente, is a significant medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin infections and respiratory ailments, offering a natural approach to supporting holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Grangea maderaspatana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Grangea.
