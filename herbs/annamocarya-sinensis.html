Annamocarya sinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Annamocarya sinensis?

Annamocarya sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant falls under the order Fagales. Its taxonomic placement continues through the family Juglandaceae, which serves as the group for its specific genus, Annamocarya, and finally identifies the individual species as sinensis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Annamocarya sinensis has a deciduous tree habit characterized by spreading crowns and a bark that becomes deeply fissured with age. The leaves are pinnately compound, typically bearing 7 to 15 leaflets that are lanceolate in shape with serrated margins. These leaflets possess a smooth texture and are arranged oppositely along the rachis. Small, inconspicuous flowers emerge in clusters, eventually developing into large, woody drupes. These fruits are distinctive for their hard, nut-like endocarp which houses a single seed.
