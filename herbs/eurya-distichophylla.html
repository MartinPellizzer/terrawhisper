What's the taxonomical classification of Eurya distichophylla?

Eurya distichophylla belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Pentaphylacaceae and falls under the genus Eurya, specifically identified by the species name distichophylla.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Eurya distichophylla has small, elliptical to oblong leaves that are arranged in two distinct rows along the stem, a characteristic known as distichous phyllotaxy. These leaves possess entire margins and a leathery texture with a glossy upper surface. The plant typically exhibits a woody structure with branches that support these densely packed foliage layers. Tiny, inconspicuous flowers emerge from the leaf axils, often appearing in small clusters. The overall morphology is defined by this structured, two-ranked leaf pattern which gives the plant a flattened appearance.
