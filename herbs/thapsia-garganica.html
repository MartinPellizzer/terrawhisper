Thapsia garganica

Thapsia garganica, commonly known as Indian ginseng or turtlehead, is a medicinal perennial herb native to the southeastern United States. Historically utilized by Indigenous cultures, it is valued for its roots, which possess significant properties in traditional herbalism, often used to treat various ailments related to the nervous system.

  • Scientific name: Thapsia garganica
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Apiales and is a member of the family Apiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Thapsia.
