Riparian greenbriar, scientifically known as Smilax riparia, is a versatile perennial climber found in moist, shaded habitats. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant has historically been utilized in traditional remedies to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. Its robust vines and distinctive berries make it a significant botanical specimen.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be specifically found within the Primorye territory. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it is documented in the South-Central region as well as on the island of Hainan. Furthermore, the species inhabits the northern reaches of China, including Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. These diverse locations highlight the wide range of habitats the species occupies.
