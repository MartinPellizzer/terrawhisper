Smilax riparia

Smilax riparia, commonly known as bush sarsaparilla, is a vigorous climbing shrub native to riparian habitats. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant contains saponins historically used to treat skin conditions and inflammation. Its sprawling vines and distinctive thorny stems make it a unique feature of wetland ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Smilax riparia
  • Family: Smilacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Smilacaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Smilax.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be specifically found within the Primorye territory. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it is documented in the South-Central region as well as on the island of Hainan. Furthermore, the species inhabits the northern reaches of China, including Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. These diverse locations highlight the wide range of habitats the species occupies.
