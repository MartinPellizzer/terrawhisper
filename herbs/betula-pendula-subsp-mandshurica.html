Betula pendula subsp. mandshurica

Betula pendula subsp. mandshurica, commonly known as the Manchurian Silver Birch, is a resilient deciduous tree valued in traditional medicine. Its bark and leaves contain bioactive compounds like betulin, offering potent anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and antimicrobial properties. This hardy subspecies remains a vital resource for natural therapeutic applications worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Betula pendula subsp. mandshurica
  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the vast territories of Russia. In the Siberian region, its presence is notably documented in Buryatiya, Chita, and Yakutiya. Moving eastward, the species also inhabits portions of the Russian Far East. Specifically, it can be found growing in the Amur region. Additionally, its range extends further toward the Kamchatka Peninsula.
