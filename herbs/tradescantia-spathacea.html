Tradescantia spathacea

Moses-in-the-cradle

Tradescantia spathecea, commonly known as the inch plant or wandering dude, is a versatile creeping perennial. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant medicinal potential in traditional practices. Renowned for its resilience, it is studied for its bioactive compounds, offering promising applications in various natural healing therapies.

  • Scientific name: Tradescantia spathacea
  • Family: Commelinaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Commelinales, placing it in the family Commelinaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Tradescantia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Tradescantia spathacea, is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties, with its most common application involving the consumption of a medicinal tea. To prepare this infusion, the succulent, variegated leaves are harvested and steeped in hot water to extract their beneficial compounds. These leaves, characterized by their green upper surfaces and deep purple undersides, serve as the primary source of the plant's medicinal value when processed into a tea.
