Distribution

This plant exhibits a distinct distribution pattern concentrated across various regions of Northern Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing naturally throughout the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. The species also occupies significant portions of the landscape in Finland. Its presence extends westward to include the islands of Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, the plant completes its northern range by inhabiting the rugged terrains of Norway.
