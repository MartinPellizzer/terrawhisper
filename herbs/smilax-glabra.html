Chinese smilax

Smilax glabra · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (69 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Chinese smilax, scientifically known as Smilax glabra, is a versatile perennial climbing vine native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, its rhizomes are prized for their detoxifying and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat skin ailments and joint pain, this botanical offers significant therapeutic potential.

This plant, Smilax glabra, is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine, with its therapeutic properties primarily derived from its underground structures. The medicinal components are found within the rhizomes, which are harvested and processed to extract their bioactive compounds.
