Smilax glabra

Smilax glabra, commonly known as Sarsaparilla, is a perennial climbing vine valued for centuries in traditional medicine. Renowned for its revitalizing properties, its roots are utilized to treat skin conditions, inflammation, and blood impurities. This potent botanical serves as a vital tonic, promoting overall vitality and systemic detoxification.

  • Scientific name: Smilax glabra
  • Family: Smilacaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Smilax glabra, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Liliales. Finally, it is classified under the family Smilacaceae and the genus Smilax.
