Malva neglecta, scientifically known as Malva neglecta, is a resilient herbaceous plant commonly referred to as common mallow. Often found in disturbed soils, this versatile species has been utilized in traditional medicine for centuries to treat inflammation, soothe digestive issues, and support respiratory health due to its nutrient-rich leaves.
