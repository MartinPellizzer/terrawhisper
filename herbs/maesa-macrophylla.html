Maesa macrophylla

Maesa macrophylla, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices to treat various ailments. Understanding its pharmacological potential offers promising insights into developing new natural therapeutic treatments.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae, it is classified under the genus Maesa. Specifically, the species is identified as Maesa macrophyllia.
