Anthocleista

Anthocleista, commonly known as a versatile medicinal herb, holds significant importance in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers therapeutic benefits ranging from anti-inflammatory to antimicrobial properties. Understanding its botanical characteristics and ethnobotanical uses is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacology.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Anthocleista.
