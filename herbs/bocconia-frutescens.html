Distribution

This plant exhibits a remarkably wide geographical distribution across several distinct oceanic and continental regions. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found specifically on the island of Réunion. Its presence extends to the Indian Subcontinent, where it grows in Sri Lanka. Within the Malesia region, the species is documented in Jawa. Finally, its reach spans the North-Central Pacific in Hawaii and extends to Mexico Central in North America.
