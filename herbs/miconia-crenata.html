Miconia crenata

Miconia crenata, commonly known as the crenate miconia, is a versatile shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctively notched leaves, this medicinal plant is utilized in traditional practices to treat various ailments. It offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in managing inflammation and supporting overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Miconia crenata
  • Family: Melastomataceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Melastomataceae. Specifically, it is a member of the genus Miconia.

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse islands of the Western Indian Ocean. It can be found growing naturally within the archipelago of the Comoros.
