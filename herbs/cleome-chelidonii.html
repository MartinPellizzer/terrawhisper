Cleome chelidonii

Celandine spider flower

Cleome chelidonii, commonly known as a member of the Cleomaceae family, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique foliage and delicate blooms, this plant offers significant pharmacological potential, particularly for its bioactive compounds used in treating various inflammatory and systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Cleome chelidonii
  • Family: Cleomaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Brassicaleales and the family Cleomaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed by the genus Cleome.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in both India and Sri Lanka. Its range extends further into the Indo-China region as well. Specifically, it is documented to inhabit the countries of Cambodia and Laos. Additionally, the species is also distributed throughout Myanmar.
