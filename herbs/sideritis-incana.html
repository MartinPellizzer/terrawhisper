Sideritis incana

Sideritis incana, commonly known as silvery mountain tea, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its soft, silvery foliage, this medicinal plant is prized in traditional herbalism for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties, frequently brewed into soothing, aromatic teas for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Sideritis incana
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sideritis.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the Mediterranean region, spanning both Europe and Africa. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Spain. Moving across the sea to Northern Africa, the species is widely found in Algeria. It also maintains a significant population throughout the territory of Morocco. Finally, its geographical range extends further east to include the country of Tunisia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Silver Sage or Sideritis incana, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its aerial parts. These above-ground components include the stems, leaves, and inflorescences, which are often harvested during the flowering stage to ensure a high concentration of bioactive compounds. The silvery, pubescent texture of the leaves and stems is a distinctive characteristic of these aerial structures, making them the most accessible and effective portions for preparing herbal teas, infusions, and various medicinal extracts.
