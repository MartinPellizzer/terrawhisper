Digitaria abyssinica

Digitaria abyssinicas, commonly known as African finger millet, is a resilient cereal crop valued for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in essential minerals and antioxidants, it plays a vital role in traditional healing practices, often used to manage metabolic health and support overall wellness in various African regions.

  • Scientific name: Digitaria abyssinica
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Digitaria.
