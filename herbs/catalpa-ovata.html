Catalpa ovata

Catalpa ovata, commonly known as the Eastern Catalpa, is a striking deciduous tree prized for its large, heart-shaped leaves and fragrant white blooms. While primarily ornamental, its unique properties have historically garnered interest in traditional medicine, offering potential therapeutic benefits through its diverse chemical compounds and botanical characteristics.

  • Scientific name: Catalpa ovata
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Bignoniaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Catalpa.
