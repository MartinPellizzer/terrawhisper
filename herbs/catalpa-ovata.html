Chinese catalpa

Catalpa ovata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (313 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese catalpa, scientifically known as Catalpa ovata, is a deciduous tree renowned for its large, heart-shaped leaves and fragrant white blossoms. While primarily ornamental, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. Its bark and leaves are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties, offering potential benefits in various herbal remedies.
