Artocarpus chama

Artocarpus chama, commonly known as the breadfruit or wild breadfruit, is a tropical tree prized for its nutritious, starchy fruit. Native to Southeast Asia, this medicinal plant offers various health benefits, including antioxidant properties and digestive support, making it a valuable resource in traditional herbal medicine and regional diets.

  • Scientific name: Artocarpus chama
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Moraceae, it is classified under the genus Artocarpus. Specifically, the medicinal species is known as Artocarpus chama.
