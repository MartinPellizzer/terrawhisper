Jasminum syringifolium

Jasminum syringifolium, commonly known as the Syrian Jasmine, is a fragrant, evergreen shrub prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic blossoms, this plant is frequently utilized for its calming properties, skin-healing benefits, and antiseptic qualities, offering a natural approach to wellness through its potent essential oils.

  • Scientific name: Jasminum syringifolium
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Oleaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Jasminum as Jasminum syringifolium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations across Southeast Asia and the Indian subcontinent. Within the Indian subcontinent, its presence is specifically noted in the state of Assam. Moving into the Indo-China region, the species can be found growing in Myanmar. Its distribution also extends to the tropical landscapes of Thailand. Finally, the plant reaches the remote reaches of the Indo-China region on the Nicobar Islands.
