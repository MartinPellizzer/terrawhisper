Solanum tuberosum

Potato

Solanum tuberosum, commonly known as the potato, is a versatile tuberous plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Beyond its global culinary importance, it possesses significant medicinal potential. Rich in bioactive compounds like phenolic acids and flavonoids, it offers antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties used in traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Solanum tuberosum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is classified under the genus Solanum. Therefore, the medicinal species Solanum tuberosum is defined by this specific hierarchical lineage.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Solanum tuberosum, utilizes various parts that serve different biological and potentially medicinal functions. The stems are perhaps the most distinctive component, as they undergo underground modification to form tubers, which serve as the primary energy storage organs. The leaves are typically green, compound, or deeply lobed, facilitating photosynthesis to support the plant's growth. Additionally, the plant produces small, green fruits that resemble berries; however, unlike the edible tubers, these fruits contain high concentrations of alkaloids and are generally avoided due to their toxicity.
