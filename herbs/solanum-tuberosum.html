Solanum tuberosum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (1254 studies) · ★★★★★

Potato, scientifically known as Solanum tuberosum, is more than just a dietary staple. Beyond its nutritional value, this tuberous plant possesses significant medicinal potential. Rich in bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and wound-healing properties, offering promising therapeutic applications in modern herbal pharmacology.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Solanum tuberosum, utilizes various parts that serve different biological and potentially medicinal functions. The stems are perhaps the most distinctive component, as they undergo underground modification to form tubers, which serve as the primary energy storage organs. The leaves are typically green, compound, or deeply lobed, facilitating photosynthesis to support the plant's growth. Additionally, the plant produces small, green fruits that resemble berries; however, unlike the edible tubers, these fruits contain high concentrations of alkaloids and are generally avoided due to their toxicity.
