Ferula alliacea

Ferula alliacea, commonly known as garlic fennel, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Native to the Mediterranean, it is distinguished by its potent, garlic-like aroma. This medicinal plant is traditionally valued for its antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant potential in natural therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Ferula alliacea
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is categorized under the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification concludes within the genus Ferula.
