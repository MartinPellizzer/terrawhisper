Lithocarpus polystachyus

Lithocarpus polystachyus, commonly known as a species of stone oak, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammatory conditions and digestive issues, offering promising therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research and natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lithocarpus polystachyus
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Fagales and the family Fagaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lithocarpus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several regions of Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in Assam and throughout Bangladesh. Its distribution also extends into the mountainous terrains of the East Himalaya. Moving into the Indo-China region, the species is present in the country of Cambodia. Finally, its habitat includes the forested areas of Laos.
