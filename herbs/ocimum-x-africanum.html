Ocimum × africanum

Ocimum × africanum, commonly known as African basil, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this aromatic herb possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. It is widely utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin infections, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Ocimum × africanum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ocimum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Guinea. Moving toward the center of the continent, it is found within West-Central Tropical Africa in both Cameroon and the DR Congo. The species also reaches into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it inhabits Ethiopia. Finally, its range extends to East Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Kenya.
