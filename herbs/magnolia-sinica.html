Magnolia sinica

Magnolia sinica, commonly known as Chinese Magnolia, is a distinguished medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is primarily utilized to alleviate respiratory ailments, reduce inflammation, and soothe nervous tension. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia sinica
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales, it is positioned in the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Magnolia, specifically as the species Magnolia sinica.

Common Names

This plant is known by several names depending on the language and regional context. In Chinese, it is referred to as 华盖木 (zh), while in Italian (it), Latin (la), Dutch (nl), and Afrikaans (af), it is commonly identified by its botanical designation, Manglietia sinica.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within a specific regional range in East Asia. Its natural habitat is concentrated in the South-Central portion of China. Within this territory, it thrives in local ecosystems that support its medicinal growth. This localized distribution makes it a characteristic species of that particular Chinese landscape. Consequently, researchers focusing on this species primarily look to South-Central China for collection.
