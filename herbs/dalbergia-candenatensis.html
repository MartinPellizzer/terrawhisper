Dalbergia candenatensis

Dalbergia candenatensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal legume, holds profound importance in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant species is utilized to treat various ailments. Exploring its phytochemical profile reveals its potential in developing modern pharmacological treatments and enhancing holistic wellness strategies.

  • Scientific name: Dalbergia candenatensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Fabales and is a member of the Fabaceae family. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Dalbergia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions of Asia. In East Asia, its distribution includes the Nansei-shoto islands. Moving towards the mainland, it can be found throughout Southeast China. The species is also well-established within the Indian Subcontinent, specifically occurring in Bangladesh and India. Finally, its range extends further south to include the island of Sri Lanka.

Plant Parts

This plant, Dalbergia candenatensis, is primarily recognized for its medicinal and structural value derived from its heartwood. The heartwood, which forms the dense, central core of the stem, contains concentrated secondary metabolites that contribute to its therapeutic properties. In various traditional applications, this specific part of the plant is utilized due to its distinct chemical composition, distinguishing it from the softer sapwood.
