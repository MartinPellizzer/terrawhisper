Daphne giraldii

Daphne giraldii, commonly known as Giraldi's Daphne, is a captivating evergreen shrub celebrated for its intense fragrance and elegant blooms. Native to specific Mediterranean habitats, this ornamental plant features clusters of highly scented, tubular flowers. While visually stunning, it requires careful handling due to its potentially toxic botanical properties.

  • Scientific name: Daphne giraldii
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malvales, it is a member of the family Thymelaeaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Daphne.
