Mahorka, scientifically known as Nicotiana rustica, is a potent, high-nicotine species of the tobacco family. Native to South America, this resilient plant is traditionally revered for its intense psychoactive properties and medicinal applications. It serves as a vital cultural cornerstone in various indigenous rituals and historical herbal practices worldwide.
