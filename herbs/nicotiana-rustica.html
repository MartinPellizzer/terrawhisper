The reproduction of Nicotiana rustica is characterized by a biotic pollination syndrome, primarily driven by insects such as butterflies. The flowering period typically begins in July and extends through September. During this phase, the plant produces green flowers that vary in size, with lengths ranging from 1.4 cm to 2.2 cm and widths between 0.2 cm and 0.4 cm. Following successful pollination, the plant develops a capsule-type fruit.
