Nicotiana rustica, commonly known as wild tobacco, is a potent medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its high nicotine content, it has been utilized traditionally for its stimulating and anesthetic properties. Today, it is studied for its complex biochemical profile and its significant roles in ethnobotany.
