Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and fragmented geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the southwestern territory of Spain. It also thrives in the Macaronesian islands, specifically inhabiting both the Canary Islands and Madeira. Moving toward the Southern Hemisphere, the species can be found throughout the Cape Provinces of South Africa. Additionally, its range extends further north along the African coastline to include the KwaZulu-Natal region.
