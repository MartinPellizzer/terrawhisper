Thalictrum sachalinense

Thalictrum sachalinense, commonly known as a rare medicinal herb, belongs to the Ranunculaceae family. Endemic to specific mountainous regions, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties. Researchers are currently investigating its bioactive compounds to unlock potential therapeutic applications in treating various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Thalictrum sachalinense
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Thalictrum sachalinense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Thalictrum.
