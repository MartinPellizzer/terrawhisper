Micranthes atrata

Micranthes atrata, commonly known as the black saxifrage, is a resilient perennial herb native to alpine and subalpine rocky environments. Characterized by its low-growing rosette and dark-tinged foliage, this plant plays a vital role in high-altitude ecosystems, offering unique botanical interest and potential medicinal properties in traditional herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Micranthes atrata
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Micranthes atrata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Saxifrages and the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Micranthes.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the North-Central areas, as well as within the provinces of Qinghai and Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the East Himalaya range. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Nepal. Together, these locations define the primary geographical range of Micranthes atrata.
