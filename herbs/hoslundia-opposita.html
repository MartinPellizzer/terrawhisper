Hoslundia opposita

Hoslundia oppositia, commonly known as the Hophead plant, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is traditionally used in various cultures to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and inflammatory conditions. This plant offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Hoslundia opposita
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Hoslundia.
