This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several major regions. In Northeast Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in both Sudan and South Sudan. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Tree of Heaven or Mahaima, possesses various therapeutic properties, with its root bark being a particularly significant component in traditional medicine. The bark extracted from the roots is often utilized for its potent medicinal qualities, frequently employed to treat ailments related to the digestive system and to reduce inflammation. Because the root bark contains concentrated bioactive compounds, it is carefully harvested to ensure its effectiveness in various herbal formulations.
