Caper bush, scientifically known as Capparis spinosa, is a resilient, thorny shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its edible flower buds, it possesses significant therapeutic potential. Rich in antioxidants and flavonoids, this plant is traditionally used to combat inflammation, aid digestion, and support overall metabolic health.
