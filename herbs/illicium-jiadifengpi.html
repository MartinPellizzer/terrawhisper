Illicium jiadifengpi

Illicium jiadifengpi, commonly known as the dried fruit bark of a specific star anise species, is a potent medicinal herb in traditional pharmacology. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is primarily utilized to dispel wind, relieve pain, and treat respiratory ailments, offering unique therapeutic benefits within herbal medicine.
