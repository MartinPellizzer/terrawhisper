Marsypianthes chamaedrys

Marsypianthes chamaedrys, commonly known as the Chame Dry, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments and skin inflammations. This resilient herb offers significant pharmacological potential, making it a subject of interest in botanical studies.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
