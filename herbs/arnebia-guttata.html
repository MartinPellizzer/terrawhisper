Arnebia guttata

Arnebia guttata, commonly known as the Chinese Boraginaceae, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Native to high-altitude regions, it contains bioactive naphthoquinones. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory and skin-healing properties, it is extensively used in topical treatments to promote wound recovery and soothe skin irritations.

  • Scientific name: Arnebia guttata
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Arnebia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast and diverse landscapes of Northern and Central Asia. In the northern reaches of its range, it can be found growing within the Altai region of Siberia. Moving southward into Central Asia, the species occupies significant territories in Kazakhstan. Its distribution continues through the mountainous terrains of Kyrgyzstan and the arid regions of Turkmenistan. Finally, the plant completes its geographical presence by inhabiting the landscapes of Tajikistan.
