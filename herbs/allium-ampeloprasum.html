Leek, scientifically known as Alliun ampeloprasum, is a versatile perennial vegetable belonging to the Amaryllidaceae family. Renowned for its mild onion-like flavor, it offers significant nutritional value, providing essential vitamins and antioxidants. Beyond culinary uses, leeks possess traditional medicinal properties, often utilized for their anti-inflammatory and digestive benefits.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Allium ampeloprasum, is primarily valued for its edible and medicinal components, specifically the stalks and the base. The most prominent parts used are the leeks, which consist of the elongated, cylindrical green leaves and white stems that are commonly consumed in culinary and therapeutic applications. Additionally, the bulbs located at the base of the plant serve as a concentrated source of nutrients and bioactive compounds, making both the leeks and the bulbs essential elements of its medicinal profile.
