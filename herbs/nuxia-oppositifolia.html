Water Elder, scientifically known as Nuxia oppositifolia, is a resilient aquatic perennial often found in wetland ecosystems. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation and digestive ailments. Its unique ability to thrive in saturated soils makes it a vital component of riparian biodiversity.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Its presence extends significantly into Northeast Tropical Africa as well. Specifically, it is located in the nations of Djibouti, Eritrea, and Ethiopia. Furthermore, the species is also documented to grow in Somalia.
