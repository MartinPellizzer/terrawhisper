What's the taxonomical classification of Rubia wallichiana?

Rubia wallichiana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Gentianales and is a member of the family Rubiaceae. Specifically, this plant is categorized within the genus Rubia, with its unique identification as the species wallichiana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rubia wallichiana has an erect or ascending perennial herb morphology characterized by woody stems that reach heights of up to 60 centimeters and possess four angled ridges. The leaves are arranged in whorls of four at each node and exhibit an elliptical to lanceolate shape with acute tips and entire margins. Small, tubular, white to pinkish flowers emerge from the axils of the leaves, typically appearing in dense clusters. The plant produces small, fleshy drupes that transition from green to a dark red or black color upon maturity. These distinctive structural features help distinguish the species within the Rubiaceae family.
