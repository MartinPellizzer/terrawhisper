Caragana korshinskii

Caragana korshinskii, commonly known as Korshin's Karaana, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to East Asian landscapes. Renowned for its distinctive flowering properties, this plant is studied for its bioactive compounds. It offers significant potential in traditional herbal practices, providing unique phytochemical benefits for various holistic wellness applications.

  • Scientific name: Caragana korshinskii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Fabales, falling within the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Caragana as Caragana korshinskii.

Distribution

This plant, Caragana korshinskii, exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of China. It is commonly found throughout the vast landscapes of Inner Mongolia and the historic territory of Manchuria. The species also extends its range into the northern stretches of China North-Central. Furthermore, its presence is documented in the high-altitude regions of Qinghai. Finally, this medicinal shrub can be located as far west as the Xinjiang province.
