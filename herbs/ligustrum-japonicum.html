L. japonicum, scientifically known as Ligustrum japonicum, is a versatile evergreen shrub valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its leaves and fruits are frequently utilized to support immune health, promote skin vitality, and alleviate various inflammatory conditions through diverse therapeutic applications.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Oleaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Ligustrum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct regions of the world. In East Asia, its presence is noted in Japan and the Nansei-shotō islands. It is also commonly found throughout the Korean peninsula. Moving towards the mainland, the species can be located in Southeast China. Additionally, its range extends much further south to include Lesotho in Southern Africa.
