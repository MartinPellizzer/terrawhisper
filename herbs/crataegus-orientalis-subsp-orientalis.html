Crataegus orientalis subsp. orientalis

Crataegus orientalis subsp. orientalis, commonly known as the Oriental Hawthorn, is a resilient deciduous shrub prized for its medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive flavonoids and polyphenols, it is traditionally used to support cardiovascular health, improve circulation, and provide antioxidant protection, making it a significant subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Crataegus orientalis subsp. orientalis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and family Rosaceae, it is classified under the genus Crataegus. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies orientalis.
