What's the taxonomical classification of Stachyurus yunnanensis?

Stachyurus yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic placement continues through the order Crossosomatales and the family Stachyuraceae. Finally, the plant is defined by its genus, Stachyurus, and its specific species designation, yunnanensis.
