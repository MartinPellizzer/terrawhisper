Epidendrum strobiliferum

Epidendrum strobiliferum, commonly known as a specialized orchid species, holds significant interest in ethnobotany. While primarily admired for its unique floral structure, this plant is studied for its potential bioactive properties. Understanding its chemical constituents offers promising insights into developing natural medicinal compounds for diverse therapeutic applications in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Epidendrum strobiliferum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. As a member of the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Epidendrum and the species strobiliferum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that stretches from North America down through Central America. In the Southeastern United States, its presence is primarily documented within the state of Florida. Moving southward, the species is widely distributed across several regions of Mexico, including the Gulf coast, the Southwest, and the Southeast. The distribution continues into Central America, where it can be found in Belize. Overall, these locations highlight the plant's preference for subtropical and tropical environments.
