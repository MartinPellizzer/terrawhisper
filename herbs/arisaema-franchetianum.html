Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across East and Southeast Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in both the South-Central and Southeast regions. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, the species can be found in Myanmar. Additionally, the plant's distribution extends into the territory of Vietnam. Together, these locations define the natural habitat of Arisaema franchetianum.

Plant Parts

This plant, Arisaema franchetianum, is characterized by a unique morphological structure where the visible above-ground portion is actually a specialized flowering stem known as a spadix, which is enclosed by a leaf-like bract called a spathe. The true stem is modified into a fleshy, underground tuberous structure that serves as a primary storage organ for nutrients. This underground stem allows the plant to survive dormant periods and provides the energy necessary to push the reproductive structures through the soil during the growing season.
