Erythrina crista-galli

Cockspur coral tree

Erythrina crista-galli, commonly known as the Cockspur Coral Tree, is a striking ornamental legume valued for its vibrant red blossoms. Beyond its aesthetic appeal, this species holds significant ethnobotanical importance, with various parts traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Erythrina crista-galli
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Erythrina, with its species name being crista-galli.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent and its surrounding islands. In Northern Africa, its presence has been documented within the borders of Egypt. The species also extends to the Macaronesian region, specifically inhabiting the island of Madeira. Moving into the tropical zones, it can be found in West-Central Tropical Africa in Rwanda. Finally, its range reaches Northeast Tropical Africa, encompassing both Ethiopia and the Sudan-South Sudan area.
