Huperzia javanica

Huperzia javaica, commonly known as Java clubmoss, is a significant medicinal fern prized for its neuroprotective properties. It contains huperzine A, a potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor used to enhance cognitive function and treat memory loss. This ancient plant plays a vital role in traditional medicine and modern neurological research.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia javanica
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Lycopodiidae, it is categorized under the order Lycopodiales and the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Huperzia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within various regions of China, including South-Central and Southeast areas. It also extends its range to the tropical island of Hainan. In the western parts of the continent, the species can be located in Tibet. Furthermore, its geographical distribution reaches into Eastern Asia, specifically covering the Nansei-shoto islands.
