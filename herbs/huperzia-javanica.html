scientifically known as Huperzia javanica, is a clubmoss species native to East Asia, highly valued in traditional medicine. It is a potent source of huperzine A, a natural acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. This alkaloid is widely researched for its ability to enhance cognitive function and protect against neurodegenerative diseases.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found within various regions of China, including South-Central and Southeast areas. It also extends its range to the tropical island of Hainan. In the western parts of the continent, the species can be located in Tibet. Furthermore, its geographical distribution reaches into Eastern Asia, specifically covering the Nansei-shoto islands.
