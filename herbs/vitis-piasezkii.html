What's the taxonomical classification of Vitis piasezkii?

Vitis piasezkii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant falls under the order Vitales and is a member of the family Vitaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Vitis, specifically identified by the species name piasezkii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Vitis piasezkii has woody, climbing vines characterized by large, palmately lobed leaves with deeply incised sinuses and serrated margins. The foliage typically displays three to five distinct lobes that provide a broad surface area for light absorption. Its stems are equipped with specialized tendrils that facilitate upward growth by anchoring the plant to surrounding structures. Small, greenish-white flowers emerge in clusters, eventually developing into fleshy berries used for reproduction. The overall architecture of the plant is adapted for a vigorous liana growth habit in forested environments.
