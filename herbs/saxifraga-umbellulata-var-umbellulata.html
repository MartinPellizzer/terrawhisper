Saxifraga umbellulata var. umbellulata

Saxifraga umbellulata var. umbellulata, commonly known as the umbellate saxifrage, is a delicate perennial herb found in rocky, alpine habitats. Renowned for its unique umbrella-like flower clusters, this resilient plant possesses subtle medicinal properties, historically valued in traditional practices for its potential restorative effects within specialized herbal applications.

  • Scientific name: Saxifraga umbellulata var. umbellulata
  • Family: Saxifragaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Saxifragales and the family Saxifragaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Saxifraga, under the variety umbellulata.

Common Names

This plant, Saxifraga umbellulata var. umbellulata, is known by various regional names across different languages. In English, it is commonly referred to as the umbrella saxifrage, while in French, it is called saxifrage à ombelles. In Spanish, it is known as saxifraga umbelulada, and in Italian, it is called saxifraga umbellulata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, its presence is documented within the mountainous terrain of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species can be found throughout the East Himalaya range. Furthermore, it is also distributed across the diverse landscapes of Nepal.
