Calendula

Calendula · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (5 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Calendula, scientifically known as Calendula, is a vibrant flowering herb celebrated for its remarkable healing properties. Often called pot marigold, these bright orange or yellow blooms are rich in antioxidants. Traditionally used to soothe skin irritations, promote wound healing, and reduce inflammation, it remains a staple in natural wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Great Britain and Ireland.
