Calendula, scientifically known as Calendula, is a vibrant flowering herb celebrated for its remarkable healing properties. Often called pot marigold, these bright orange or yellow blooms are rich in antioxidants. Traditionally used to soothe skin irritations, promote wound healing, and reduce inflammation, it remains a staple in natural wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad presence across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Great Britain and Ireland. Its distribution continues further north into the Scandinavian territories of Norway and Sweden. Additionally, the species is present in Middle Europe, specifically within Austria. These various locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in diverse European climates.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as pot marigold, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, primarily focusing on the vibrant flower heads which are harvested for their soothing oils and extracts. The green leaf is also frequently used in herbal preparations due to its anti-inflammatory qualities, while the root can be incorporated into specific traditional remedies. Together, these parts contribute to the plant's reputation as a powerful natural healer for skin ailments and digestive health.
