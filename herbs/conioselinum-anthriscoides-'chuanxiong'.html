Plant Parts

This plant, known as Conioselinum anthriscoides 'Chuanxiong', is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its underground structures. While the green leaves grow above the soil, the medicinal potency is concentrated within the root system, specifically in the thick, fleshy rhizomes. To prepare the plant for therapeutic use, the rhizomes are harvested and processed into dried rhizomes, which are then utilized in various formulations. Whether referred to as the singular rhizome or the collective rhizomes, these specialized parts serve as the essential source of the plant's healing properties.
