Alchemilla · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (13 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Lady's mantle, scientifically known as Alchemilla, is a versatile perennial renowned for its striking, scalloped foliage that beautifully collects morning dew. Traditionally valued in herbalism for its astringent and anti-inflammatory properties, this resilient plant is widely used to support women's reproductive health and soothe various skin conditions.
