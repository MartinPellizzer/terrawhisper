Alchemilla

Alchemilla, commonly known as Lady's Mantle, is a versatile perennial herb prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its unique, scalloped leaves that collect dew, this plant has long been used to support women's reproductive health, reduce inflammation, and aid in astringent healing, making it a staple in botanical medicine.

  • Scientific name: Alchemilla
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Within this taxonomic hierarchy, it is specifically identified by the genus Alchemilla.
