Dioscorea benthamii

What's the taxonomical classification of Dioscorea benthamii?

Dioscorea benthamii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is further organized under the order Dioscoreales and falls within the family Dioscoreaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed at the genus level as Dioscorea, with its specific designation being the species benthamii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Dioscorea benthamii has climbing stems that emerge from tuberous roots and bear cordate to sagittate leaves with entire margins and acute tips. The foliage typically features prominent venation and a smooth or slightly pubescent surface texture. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in clusters, often appearing in shades of pale green or cream. These inflorescences are supported by slender peduncles that emerge from the leaf axils. The reproductive structures are characterized by their delicate morphology, typical of many species within the Dioscoreaceae family.
