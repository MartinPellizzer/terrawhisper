Gentiana crassicaulis

Gentiana crassicaulis, commonly known as the thick-stemmed gentian, is a striking perennial herb native to the high-altitude alpine meadows of the Himalayas. Renowned for its vibrant blue flowers and robust structure, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional practices for its bitter properties and various therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Gentiana crassicaulis
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is a member of the order Gentianales and the family Gentianaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identification is completed under the genus Gentiana.
