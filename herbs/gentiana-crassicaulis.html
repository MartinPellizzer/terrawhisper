Plant Parts

This plant, Gentiana crassicaulis, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine, where the primary part utilized is the root. The root of this species is typically thick and fleshy, serving as the main storage organ for the plant's bioactive compounds, such as bitter gentianins. In medicinal applications, the root is harvested, dried, and often processed into powders or tinctures to harness its therapeutic properties, which are believed to aid digestion and stimulate appetite.
