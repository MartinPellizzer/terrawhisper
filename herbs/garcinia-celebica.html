Garcinia celebica

Garcinia celebica, commonly known as Celebes mangosteen, is a rare medicinal plant native to Sulawesi, Indonesia. Renowned for its bioactive xanthones, this species offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally valued for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it a subject of intense pharmacological interest and study.

  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
