Mercurialis perennis

Mercurialis perennis, commonly known as dog's mercury, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to Europe. Often forming lush, green carpets in shaded woodlands, it holds a significant place in traditional herbalism. While historically used for various medicinal purposes, it requires cautious handling due to its potent chemical properties.

  • Scientific name: Mercurialis perennis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Mercurialis perennis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, following the order Malpighiales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae within the genus Mercurialis.
