Plant Parts

This plant, Alkanet tinctoria subsp. tinctoria, is highly valued for its distinct coloring properties, which are primarily derived from its specialized anatomy. The most significant part for medicinal and dye purposes is the roots, specifically the deep red root material that contains the pigment alkanet. When preparing extracts, the root is often processed to release its bioactive compounds. In addition to the underground structures, the leaves of the plant are also present and can contribute to the overall botanical profile, though the primary focus in herbal applications remains the Plant Roots.
