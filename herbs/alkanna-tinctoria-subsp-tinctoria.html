Alkanna tinctoria subsp. tinctoria

Alkannna tinctoria subsp. tinctoria, commonly known as Alkanet, is a prized medicinal herb celebrated for its deep crimson pigment. Traditionally used in herbalism, this plant offers potent anti-inflammatory and antiseptic properties. Often prepared as a soothing oil or tincture, it serves as a staple in traditional topical skin treatments.

  • Scientific name: Alkanna tinctoria subsp. tinctoria
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Alkanet tinctoria subsp. tinctoria, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Boraginales. Finally, it is a member of the family Boraginaceae and the genus Alkanna.

Common Names

This plant, Alkanet tinctoria subsp. tinctoria, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as alkanet or dye alkanet, reflecting its historical importance as a coloring agent. In German, it is known as echtes alkannet, while in French, it is called alcanna. The plant also carries the name alkanna in Italian and is referred to as alcaiana in Spanish.
