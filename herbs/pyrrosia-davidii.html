Pyrrosia davidii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Polypodiales and the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, the plant is situated within the genus Pyrrosiia, with its specific designation being the species davidii.
