Phlomoides mongolica

Phlomoides mongolica, commonly known as Mongolian Phlomis, is a perennial herb native to the arid regions of East Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant contains unique bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. Its therapeutic potential lies in its ability to support respiratory health and reduce systemic inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Phlomoides mongolica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It further descends through the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Phlomoides.

Common Names

This plant, Phlomoides mongolica, is known by various common names depending on the linguistic region. In Chinese, it is referred to as 串铃草 (chuàn líng cǎo), with the simplified form 串铃草 being used in mainland China (zh-cn) and the traditional form 串鈴草 being used in Taiwan (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant is native to specific regions within the country of China. Its geographical presence is primarily concentrated in the northern territories. Specifically, it can be found growing across the Inner Mongolia autonomous region. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout the China North-Central area. These locations define the natural habitat where the plant thrives.

Plant Parts

This plant, Phlomoides mongolica, utilizes its underground root as a primary component in traditional medicinal practices. The root serves as a vital storage organ for the plant, accumulating various bioactive compounds that are often extracted for therapeutic purposes. In many pharmacological applications, it is this specific part of the plant that is harvested and processed to harness its potential medicinal properties.
