Juniperus virginiana

Juniperus virginia, commonly known as Eastern Red Cedar, is a resilient evergreen native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and berry-like cones, this plant offers diverse medicinal properties. Historically utilized for its antiseptic and astringent qualities, it remains a significant species in traditional herbal practices and ecology.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus virginiana
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Juniperus virginiana, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae within the order Pinales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Cupressaceae and falls under the genus Juniperus.
