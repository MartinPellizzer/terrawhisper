The life history of Combretum racemosum is characterized by its status as a perennial species, allowing it to persist in its environment over multiple growing seasons. As a phanerophyte, its perennating buds are located high above the ground on woody stems, a structural adaptation that defines its growth habit and survival strategy. This phanerophytic life form, specifically categorized under both general and specific classifications of phanero-growth, ensures that the plant can withstand various environmental stresses by maintaining its vital reproductive and vegetative structures on elevated branches.
