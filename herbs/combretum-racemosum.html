Combretum racemosum

Combretum racemosum, commonly known as the "Wild Plum" or "Bush Plum" in various regions, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Combretum racemosum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it is a member of the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Combretum as the species Combretum racemosum.

This plant is native to several specific regions across the West Tropical Africa zone. Its presence has been documented within the borders of Benin.
