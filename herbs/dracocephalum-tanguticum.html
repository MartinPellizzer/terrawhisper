Life history

The life history of Dracocephalum tanguticum is characterized by its classification as a hemicryptophyte, a life form where the perennating buds are located at or just below the soil surface, protected by leaf litter or the substrate. Within the various botanical life form frameworks, it is specifically identified as a hemicryptophyte, distinguishing it from other growth strategies such as phanerophytes, chamaephytes, or therophytes. This life history strategy allows the plant to survive seasonal dormancy by sheltering its regenerative tissues at the soil level, facilitating its ability to persist through unfavorable environmental conditions and re-emerge during the growing season.
