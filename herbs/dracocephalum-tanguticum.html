Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct mountainous and high-altitude regions. Within China, it can be found spanning from the South-Central provinces to the North-Central areas. Its presence is also well-documented in the high-elevation terrains of Qinghai. Furthermore, the species thrives within the unique environmental conditions of Tibet. Finally, its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the East Himalaya region.
