Alexandrian senna

Senna alexandrina · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (404 studies) · ★★★★★

Alexandrian senna, scientifically known as Senna alexandrina, is a potent medicinal shrub prized for its powerful laxative properties. Primarily harvested from its leaves and pods, it contains active sennosides that stimulate bowel movements. This versatile plant remains a cornerstone in traditional medicine and modern herbal treatments for constipation.
