Senna alexandrina

Sennas alexandrina, commonly known as Senna, is a potent medicinal shrub valued primarily for its powerful laxative properties. Containing bioactive sennosides, it effectively stimulates bowel movements to treat constipation. Widely used in herbal medicine, this plant remains a cornerstone in digestive health treatments and traditional pharmacological practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Senna alexandrina
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Senna alexandrina, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Fabales. Furthermore, it is a member of the Fabaceae family and is situated within the genus Senna.

Distribution

This plant, Senna alexandrina, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the African continent. In the northern region, it is widely found within the countries of Algeria, Egypt, and Morocco. Moving toward the west, its presence extends into the tropical zones of West Africa. Specifically, the species is documented to grow in Benin. Additionally, the plant can be located within the borders of Mali.
