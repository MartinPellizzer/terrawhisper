Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution spanning several distinct regions across the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in the nations of Guinea and Mali. Moving toward the center of the continent, it can be found within West-Central Tropical Africa in both Rwanda and the DR Congo. Additionally, its range extends into East Tropical Africa, specifically within the borders of Kenya. Together, these locations highlight the diverse ecological zones inhabited by this medicinal species.
