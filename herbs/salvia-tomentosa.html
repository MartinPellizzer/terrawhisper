Salvia tomentoosa, scientifically known as Salvia tomentoza, is a striking perennial sage celebrated for its velvety, silver-green foliage and vibrant tubular flowers. Native to arid regions, this resilient plant is prized in traditional herbalism for its therapeutic properties and as an ornamental gem in Mediterranean-style gardens.
