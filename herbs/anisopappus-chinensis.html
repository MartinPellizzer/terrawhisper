Anisopappus chinensis

Anisopappus chinensis, commonly known as a traditional medicinal herb, holds significant value in ethnobotany. Frequently utilized in various herbal remedies, this plant is recognized for its bioactive compounds. Researchers study its pharmacological properties to explore potential therapeutic applications, particularly in addressing inflammatory conditions and supporting overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Anisopappus chinensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Anisopappus, specifically as the species Anisopappus chinensis.

Common Names

This plant, Anisopappus chinensis, is known by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is frequently referred to as 山黄菊 (zh), 旱山菊 (zh), or 萄涧菊 (zh). More specifically, in Mainland China, it is identified as 山黄菊 (zh-cn), while in Taiwan, the name is written as 山黃菊 (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations throughout its natural habitat. Within China, its distribution spans across the South-Central and Southeast regions. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found in both Assam and the East Himalaya. Additionally, the species extends its reach into the Indo-China region, specifically in Cambodia. Together, these areas define the primary geographical footprint of Anisopus chinensis.
