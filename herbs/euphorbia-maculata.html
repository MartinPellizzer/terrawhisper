Euphorbia maculata

Euphorbia maculata, commonly known as spotted spurge, is a resilient annual herb found in various tropical and subtropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive reddish spots on the stems, this plant is recognized in traditional medicine for its diverse pharmacological properties, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing potential in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia maculata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Euphorbia.
