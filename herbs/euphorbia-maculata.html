Blotched spurge, scientifically known as Euphorbia maculata, is a common annual herb recognized by its distinctive dark spots on the leaves. While often viewed as a garden weed, this resilient plant holds significant value in traditional medicine, particularly for its potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties in various cultures.
