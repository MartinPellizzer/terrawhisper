Ilex pedunculosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Ilex pedunculosa?

Ilex pedunculosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is part of the order Aquifoliales and falls under the family Aquifoliaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized within the genus Ilex, specifically identified as the species pedunculosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Ilex pedunculosa has small, evergreen leaves that are typically elliptical or lanceolate in shape with entire or slightly serrated margins and a leathery texture. The foliage is often dark green on the upper surface while the underside may appear slightly paler. Its stems are woody and develop a rugged bark as the shrub matures into a larger form. The characteristic long pedicels, from which the species derives its name, support small, inconspicuous flowers. These structures eventually give way to small drupes that serve as the plant's fruit.
