Euphorbia lagascae

Euphorbia lagascae, commonly known as the Spurge plant, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, it possesses significant antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient plant offers promising therapeutic potential, particularly in treating skin ailments and various inflammatory conditions through bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia lagascae
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic designation is completed within the genus Euphorbia.
