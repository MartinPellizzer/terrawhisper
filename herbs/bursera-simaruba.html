Balsam Tree, scientifically known as Bursera simaruba, is a versatile tropical species native to Central and South America. Renowned for its medicinal properties, the tree produces a fragrant resin used traditionally to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. It remains a vital resource in many indigenous healing practices.
