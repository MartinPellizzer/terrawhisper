Neolitsea konishii

Neolitsea konishii, commonly known as a member of the Lauraceae family, is a significant medicinal plant native to East Asian forests. Valued for its bioactive secondary metabolites, this evergreen shrub offers therapeutic potential in traditional practices, particularly for its antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, making it a subject of pharmacological interest.

  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Neolitsea.
