Buddleja

Buddleja, commonly known as the butterfly bush, is a versatile flowering shrub celebrated for its fragrant, cone-shaped blooms. Renowned for attracting pollinators like butterflies and bees, this hardy plant adds vibrant color to gardens. Beyond its ornamental appeal, certain species offer traditional medicinal properties used in various herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Buddleja
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Buddleja.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Buddleja, is referred to by several common names across different Chinese linguistic contexts. In Simplified Chinese, it is identified as 醉鱼草属 (zh-hans) or 醉鱼草属 (zh-cn), and in the same manner, it is called 醉鱼草属 in Singapore (zh-sg). In Traditional Chinese, the name is written as 醉魚草屬 (zh-hanr), which is also the standard designation used in Hong Kong (zh-hk).
