Jatropha divaricata

Jatrophia divaricata, commonly known as the desert spurge, is a resilient shrub native to arid tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its various parts are utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This hardy plant exemplifies the diverse therapeutic potential found within specialized desert ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Jatropha divaricata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
