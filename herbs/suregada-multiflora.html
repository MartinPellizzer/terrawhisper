False Lime, scientifically known as Suregada multiflora, is a versatile evergreen climber native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its fragrant white flowers and dense foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin diseases, inflammation, and digestive issues.
