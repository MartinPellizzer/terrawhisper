Suregada multiflora

False lime

Suregada multiflora, commonly known as the Climbing Ylang-Ylang, is a versatile medicinal climber prized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its fragrant blossoms, this plant is utilized to treat skin ailments, reduce inflammation, and alleviate digestive issues. Its potent bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Suregada multiflora
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Suregada multiflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and the genus Suregada.
