Dunnia sinensis

Dunnia sinensis, commonly known as Chinese cinnamon or sweet cinnamon, is a versatile medicinal plant native to East Asia. Renowned for its aromatic bark, it possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and digestive benefits. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it remains a vital component in modern phytotherapy.

  • Scientific name: Dunnia sinensis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, falling specifically into the family Rubiaceae. Within this family, it is identified by the genus Dunnia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Dunnia sinensis, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is known as 绣球茜草 or 绣球茜, while in Mainland China, the specific designation used is 绣球茜草 (zh-cn) and 绣球茜草 (zh-hans). In Taiwan, the name is written as 繍球茜草 (zh-tw).
