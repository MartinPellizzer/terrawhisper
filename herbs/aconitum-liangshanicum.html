What's the taxonomical classification of Aconitum liangshanicum?

Aconitum liaangshanicum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae, eventually placing it within the genus Aconitum. Ultimately, the specific identity of this plant is defined by its species designation, liangshanicum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aconitum liangshanicum has herbaceous stems that reach heights of 20 to 50 centimeters, topped by clusters of zygomorphic, helmet-shaped flowers that are typically blue or violet. Its floral structure is characterized by a prominent, hood-like sepal that protects the inner reproductive organs. The root system consists of thickened, tuberous structures that store nutrients for the plant. Small, inconspicuous petals are tucked beneath the enlarged, colorful sepals within the flower head.
