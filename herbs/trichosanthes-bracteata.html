Trichosanthes bracteata

Trichosanthes bracteata, commonly known as the creeping cucumber or snake gourd relative, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, digestive ailments, and skin conditions, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Trichosanthes bracteata
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Cucurbitales and the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Trichosanthes, specifically as the species Trichosanthes bracteata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is specifically documented to grow in the South-Central areas. Its distribution extends significantly into the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be found throughout India and Bangladesh. Additionally, the species is native to the specific terrain of the East Himalaya. Finally, it is also recorded within the Assam region of the Indian Subcontinent.
