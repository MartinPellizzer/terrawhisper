Annona coriacea, scientifically known as Annona coriacea, is a versatile tropical fruit tree native to the Neotropics. Often called "guanábana de monte," this species is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering significant therapeutic potential for various health conditions across South America.
