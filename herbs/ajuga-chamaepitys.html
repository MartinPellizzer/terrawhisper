, scientifically known as Ajuga chamaepitys, is a perennial herb native to Europe and parts of Asia. Commonly called bugleweed, this plant features distinctive whorls of small, tubular flowers. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it possesses various bioactive properties, often studied for its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant therapeutic effects.
