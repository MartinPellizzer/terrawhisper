What's the taxonomical classification of Tolypanthus maclurei?

Tolypanthus maclurei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the order Santalales and is a member of the family Loranthaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identification is defined by the genus Tolypanthus and the species maclurlei.
