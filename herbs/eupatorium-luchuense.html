Eupatorium luchuense

Eupatorium luchuense, commonly known as a vital medicinal herb in regional traditional practices, holds significant pharmacological potential. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various inflammatory and respiratory ailments. Ongoing research aims to unlock its therapeutic secrets for modern clinical applications and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Eupatorium luchuense
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by its genus, Eupatorium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range concentrated within certain regions of Eastern Asia. Its distribution is notably found across the Nansei-shoto islands. Additionally, the species can be located within the territory of Taiwan. These two areas represent the primary habitats where the plant occurs naturally. Consequently, its presence is limited to these specific eastern maritime and island environments.
