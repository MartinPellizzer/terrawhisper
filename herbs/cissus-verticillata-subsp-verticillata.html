Cissus rotundifolia, scientifically known as Cissus verticillata subsp. verticillata, is a climbing medicinal vine prized in traditional healing. Characterized by its distinctive whorled leaves, this plant is frequently utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, offering a natural approach to supporting wellness through its unique phytochemical composition.
