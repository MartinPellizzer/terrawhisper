Cissus verticillata subsp. verticillata

Cissus rotundifolia

Cissus verticillata subsp. verticillata, commonly known as the grape vine or medicinal climber, is a versatile plant valued in traditional medicine. Native to tropical regions, it is frequently utilized for its therapeutic properties, including treating inflammation and supporting digestive health, making it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.
