Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions within the Malesian and Papuasian botanical zones. Within the Malesian realm, it can be found growing on the island of Jawa. Its range further extends through the Lesser Sunda Islands and across the archipelago to Maluku. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit the island of Sulawesi. Finally, its geographical reach extends into Papuasia, specifically within the territory of New Guinea.

Plant Parts

This plant, Clematis pickeringii, is characterized by its climbing habit, which is facilitated by its specialized stem structure. The stem serves as the primary structural support for the vine, allowing it to ascend through surrounding vegetation using petioles that act similarly to tendrils. As the stem grows, it provides the essential vascular framework necessary to transport water and nutrients from the roots to the developing leaves and flowers.
