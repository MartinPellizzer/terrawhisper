Nymphaea odorata

Sweet-scented water lily

Nymphaea odorata, commonly known as the fragrant water lily, is a captivating aquatic plant revered for its striking blooms. Beyond its aesthetic beauty, this species holds significant place in traditional herbalism. Its aromatic properties and botanical compounds are frequently studied for their potential therapeutic and calming medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Nymphaea odorata
  • Family: Nymphaeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Nymphaeales and the family Nymphaeaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Nymphaea.
