Thymelaea hirsuta

Thymelaea hirsuta, commonly known as the hairy daphne, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions. Its unique botanical properties continue to attract scientific interest for therapeutic development.

  • Scientific name: Thymelaea hirsuta
  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malvales, it is classified under the family Thymelaeaceae and the genus Thymelaea. Therefore, its scientific name is Thymelaea hirsuta.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed throughout specific regions of Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing in the Balearic Islands, which are part of the Baleares archipelago. Its presence is also noted in Corsica and the island of Sardegna. Furthermore, the species is documented across various locations in France. Finally, its range extends to the coastal and inland areas of Portugal.
