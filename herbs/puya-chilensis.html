Puya chilensis

Puyas chilensis, commonly known as the Chilean Puya, is a striking endemic plant from the Andean landscapes. Beyond its majestic, spiky appearance, this species holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in folk medicine, its unique properties offer fascinating insights into the healing potential of South America's diverse floral biodiversity.
