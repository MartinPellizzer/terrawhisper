Vangueria madagascariensis

Vangueria madagascariensis, commonly known as the Madagascar Mangrove, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues. Its unique phytochemical profile continues to inspire modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Vangueria madagascariensis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianaales, it is further classified under the family Rubiaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Vangueria, specifically as the species Vangueria madagascariensis.
