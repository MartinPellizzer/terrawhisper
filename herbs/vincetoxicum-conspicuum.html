Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the tropical regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Ghana, Ivory Coast, and Liberia. The species also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found within the borders of Cameroon and Gabon. This range highlights its adaptation to the diverse environments of these equatorial territories.
