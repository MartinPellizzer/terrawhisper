Dipteryx odorata

Dipteryx odorata, commonly known as tonka bean, is a tropical tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its fragrant, almond-like seeds, it holds significant value in perfumery and gastronomy. Beyond its aromatic uses, the plant offers diverse medicinal properties, traditionally utilized for its antioxidant and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Dipteryx odorata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dipteryx, specifically as the species Dipteryx odorata.
