Cumaru, scientifically known as Dipteryx odorata, is a majestic tropical tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it provides a rich, vanilla-like essence used in perfumery and flavoring. Beyond its culinary appeal, this botanical treasure offers significant medicinal properties, valued for its diverse therapeutic potential.

Distribution

This plant, known as Dipteryx odorata, exhibits a specific geographical range across several distinct regions.
