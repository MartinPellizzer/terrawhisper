What's the taxonomical classification of Amorphophallus kiusianus?

Amorphophallus kiuisianus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Alismatales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Araceae, where it is categorized under the genus Amorphophallus, specifically identified as the species kiusianus.
