Lavandula

Lavandula, commonly known as lavender, is a fragrant perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic purple blooms. Renowned in traditional medicine for its calming properties, it is widely used to alleviate anxiety, insomnia, and skin irritations. This versatile plant offers therapeutic benefits through essential oils, teas, and soothing topical applications.

  • Scientific name: Lavandula
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further categorized into the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lavandula.
