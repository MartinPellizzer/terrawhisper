Pulsatilla dahurica

Pulsatilla dahurica, commonly known as Siberian Pasque Flower, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is primarily used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions. This medicinal plant requires careful handling, as its bioactive compounds offer significant healing potential when used correctly.

  • Scientific name: Pulsatilla dahurica
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ranunculales, it falls under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Pulsatilla.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across the northern reaches of Asia. In the vast expanse of Siberia, it can be found growing in the regions of Buryatiya, Chita, and Yakutiya. Moving toward the eastern edge of the continent, its presence extends into the Russian Far East. Within this area, the species is specifically documented in the Amur region. Additionally, the plant's range reaches as far east as the Kamchatka Peninsula.
