Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across the northern reaches of Asia. In the vast expanse of Siberia, it can be found growing in the regions of Buryatiya, Chita, and Yakutiya. Moving toward the eastern edge of the continent, its presence extends into the Russian Far East. Within this area, the species is specifically documented in the Amur region. Additionally, the plant's range reaches as far east as the Kamchatka Peninsula.
