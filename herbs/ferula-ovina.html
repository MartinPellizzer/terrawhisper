Ferula ovina

Ferula ovina, commonly known as sheep's fennel, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Native to the Mediterranean region, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It contains bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments, including inflammation, digestive issues, and respiratory conditions through its potent extracts.
