Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asparagaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Cordyline.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several tropical and subtropical regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Its presence extends to the Western Indian Ocean, where it inhabits the Comoros. Within Asia, the species is located in the Indian subcontinent, particularly in Assam, as well as in China Southeast. Additionally, its range includes the specific Chinese island of Hainan.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Ti plant, utilizes its vibrant and elongated leaves as its primary medicinal component. These leaves are highly valued in various traditional healing practices, where they are often prepared as poultices or decoctions to treat ailments such as inflammation, skin irritations, and digestive issues. The diverse colors and textures of the foliage not only serve an aesthetic purpose but also make the leaves easily identifiable for their specific therapeutic applications in herbal medicine.
