Ligustrum lucidum

Ligustrum lucidum, commonly known as Chinese Privet, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to East Asia. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. Its leaves and berries are frequently utilized for their diverse pharmacological properties, offering potential therapeutic benefits in various healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Ligustrum lucidum
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Oleaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Ligustrum.
