Broad-leaf-privet, scientifically known as Ligustrum lucidum, is a versatile evergreen shrub valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its leaves and fruits are utilized to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and skin conditions. This resilient plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Ligustrum lucidum, contains various components that are utilized for their therapeutic potential. The small, dark berries produced by the species are classified as its fruits, with the individual fruit often being harvested once it reaches a state of ripeness. Specifically, the mature fruit is sought after for its concentrated bioactive compounds, which are often processed into a dried fruit form to ensure stability and ease of storage. Ultimately, these components are frequently processed into a potent LL extract, which serves as the primary medium for medicinal applications and standardized supplementation.
