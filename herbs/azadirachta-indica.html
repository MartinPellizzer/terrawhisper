Azadirachta indica

Azadirachta indica, commonly known as Neem, is a versatile medicinal powerhouse native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned for its potent antibacterial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties, every part of this "village pharmacy" tree—from leaves to bark—offers therapeutic benefits, making it a cornerstone of traditional Ayurvedic medicine and holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Azadirachta indica
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Azadirachta indica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales and the family Meliaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Azadirachta.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Azadirachta indica, exhibits a specific presence across various regions of West Tropical Africa. Within this part of the continent, its distribution includes the nation of Burkina. It is also found in the coastal and inland areas of the Gambia. Furthermore, the species spans across Ghana and the territory of Guinea-Bissau. Finally, its geographical range extends into the country of Guinea.
