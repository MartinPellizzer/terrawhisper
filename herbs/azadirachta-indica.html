Neem, scientifically known as Azadirachta indica, is a versatile medicinal powerhouse native to the Indian subcontinent. Often called the "village pharmacy," this hardy evergreen tree offers profound therapeutic benefits. Renowned for its potent antibacterial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties, neem serves as a cornerstone in traditional Ayurvedic medicine.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Azadirachta indica, exhibits a specific presence across various regions of West Tropical Africa. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Neem, utilizes various components for its diverse medicinal properties. The leaves are the most frequently used part, often prepared as a paste or infusion, while even a single leaf can provide potent antibacterial benefits. The seeds are highly valued for their oil, which is rich in bioactive compounds, and the bark is often employed in traditional remedies to treat skin conditions and inflammation. Additionally, the fruits, known as nimbolant, contribute to the plant's overall therapeutic profile, making every part of the Azadirachta indica essential to its reputation as a "pharmacy in a tree."
