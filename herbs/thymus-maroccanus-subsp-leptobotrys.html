Thymus maroccanus subsp. leptobotrys

Thymus maroccanus subsp. leptobotrys, commonly known as Moroccan thyme, is a rare, aromatic shrub endemic to the Atlas Mountains. Renowned for its potent essential oils, this medicinal plant offers significant antioxidant and antimicrobial properties. It is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to support respiratory health and alleviate various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Thymus maroccanus subsp. leptobotrys
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Thymus. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies leptobotrys of the species Thymus maroccanus.
