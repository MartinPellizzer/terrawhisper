Distribution

This plant is characterized by a very restricted geographical range within the African continent. Its natural habitat is located exclusively in the northern part of Africa. Specifically, the distribution of this subspecies is limited to the country of Morocco. Because it is endemic to this specific region, it cannot be found growing wild in any other part of the world. Consequently, its survival is closely tied to the unique environmental conditions found within the Moroccan landscape.
