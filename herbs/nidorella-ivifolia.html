Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across multiple continents and diverse climates. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Portugal. Moving to the African continent, it can be found in South Tropical Africa, specifically in Zimbabwe. The species is also well-established in Southern Africa, where it inhabits Botswana and the Cape Provinces. Additionally, its range extends into the mountainous regions of Lesotho.
