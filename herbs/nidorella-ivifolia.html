Nidorella ivifolia

Nidorella ivifolia, commonly known as the velvet-leaf herb, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its potent therapeutic properties. Native to tropical forest understories, it contains unique bioactive compounds used in traditional healing to treat inflammation and digestive ailments, offering a fascinating subject for modern pharmacological research and study.

  • Scientific name: Nidorella ivifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Nidorella, which includes the species Nidorella ivifolia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across multiple continents and diverse climates. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Portugal. Moving to the African continent, it can be found in South Tropical Africa, specifically in Zimbabwe. The species is also well-established in Southern Africa, where it inhabits Botswana and the Cape Provinces. Additionally, its range extends into the mountainous regions of Lesotho.
