Rheum lhasaense

Rheum lhasaense, commonly known as Tibetan rhubarb, is a precious medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, its rhizomes are harvested for their potent purgative and detoxifying properties, playing a vital role in treating digestive ailments and regulating bodily heat effectively.

  • Scientific name: Rheum lhasaense
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Rheum lhasaense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Caryophyllales and the family Polygonaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rheum.
