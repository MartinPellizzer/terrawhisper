Panax sokpayensis

Panax sokpayensis, commonly known as Himalayan ginseng, is a rare medicinal perennial native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional Tibetan medicine, this potent botanical is highly valued for its adaptogenic properties, used to bolster immunity, reduce fatigue, and restore vital energy in various healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Panax sokpayensis
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
