Artocarpus nigrifolius

Artocarpus nigrifolius, commonly known as the Black Jackfruit, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and the development of natural health treatments.
