Pekea Nut, scientifically known as Caryocar brasiliense, is a remarkable fruit-bearing tree native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its nutritional richness, it offers significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Beyond its culinary uses, this plant holds immense medicinal potential for supporting digestive health and combating oxidative stress naturally.
