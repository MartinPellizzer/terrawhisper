Callitris endlicheri

Callitris endlicheri, commonly known as the Endlich's Cypress, is a resilient coniferous evergreen native to East Asian montane regions. Valued for its dense, fragrant timber and aesthetic elegance, this species plays a vital role in forest ecosystems and holds significant cultural importance in traditional landscape design and woodworking.
