Sesamum radiatum

Sesamum radiatum, commonly known as a medicinal variety of sesame, is a significant botanical species valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique radial flowering patterns, this plant offers diverse bioactive compounds. It is primarily studied for its potential therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Sesamum radiatum
  • Family: Pedaliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Pedaliaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Sesamum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution that spans across both Macaronesia and West Tropical Africa. Within the Macaronesian islands, its presence is noted in Cape Verde. Moving into West Tropical Africa, the species can be found inhabiting various regions including Benin and Burkina. Furthermore, the plant extends its range through the coastal and inland territories of Gambia. Finally, its distribution reaches as far as Ghana, illustrating its presence across multiple West African nations.
