What's the taxonomical classification of Androsace umbellata?

Androsace umbellata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Ericales and into the family Primulaceae. Finally, the plant is situated within the genus Androsace, with its specific designation being the species umbellata.
