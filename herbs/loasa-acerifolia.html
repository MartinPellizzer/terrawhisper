Loasa acerifolia

Loasa acerifolia, commonly known as a member of the Loasaceae family, is a striking medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant blooms and unique foliage, it is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies to treat skin ailments and inflammatory conditions, showcasing nature's diverse healing potential.

  • Scientific name: Loasa acerifolia
  • Family: Loasaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Loasa acerifolia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Cornales. Finally, it is a member of the family Loasaceae and the genus Loasa.
