Camphora philippinensis

Camphora philippinenesis, commonly known as Philippine camphor, is a rare and significant medicinal plant endemic to the Philippines. Highly valued for its aromatic properties, its essential oils possess potent antiseptic and anti-inflammatory qualities. This precious species remains a vital subject for botanical research and traditional healing practices in Southeast Asia.

  • Scientific name: Camphora philippinensis
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Camphora philippinenis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its specific genus, Camphora.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and Southeast Asia. Within the region of Eastern Asia, its presence is recorded in Taiwan. Moving into the Malesia zone, it is found natively in the Philippines. These two locations represent the primary areas where the species can be located in the wild. Consequently, its habitat is limited to these specific island territories.
