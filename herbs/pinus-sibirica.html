Pinus sibiriica, scientifically known as Pinus sibirica, is a majestic coniferous tree native to the Siberian taiga. Renowned for its nutrient-dense pine nuts and resinous extracts, it holds significant medicinal value. Its needles and seeds are traditionally utilized for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and immune-boosting properties in herbal medicine.
