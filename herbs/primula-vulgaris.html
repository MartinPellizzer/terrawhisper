Primula vulgaris

Primula vulgaris, commonly known as the primrose, is a charming perennial herb native to Europe. Characterized by its delicate yellow flowers and rosette of crinkled leaves, this woodland plant signals spring's arrival. Historically used in traditional medicine, it possesses mild sedative and expectorant properties, valued for soothing respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Primula vulgaris
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Primula vulgaris, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Primula.
