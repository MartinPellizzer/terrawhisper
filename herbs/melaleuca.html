Melaleuca

Kajeput

Melaleuca, commonly known as tea tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Australia. Renowned for its powerful antimicrobial and antifungal properties, its essential oil is a staple in natural healing. Used to treat skin conditions and respiratory issues, this resilient shrub offers profound therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Melaleuca
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtales, and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Melaleuca.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Europe and its surrounding islands. In Northern Europe, its presence can be noted within the territory of Great Britain. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it is found in the Baleares of Southwestern Europe and throughout Italy in Southeastern Europe. The plant also inhabits the Northwest Balkan Peninsula, marking another significant area in Southeastern Europe. Finally, its range extends to the Macaronesian region, specifically including the Canary Islands.
