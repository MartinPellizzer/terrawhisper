Kajeput, scientifically known as Melaleuca, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its powerful antimicrobial properties. Native to Australia, its aromatic leaves yield essential oils used to treat skin infections, respiratory issues, and inflammation. This resilient shrub remains a cornerstone in natural healing and therapeutic aromatherapy worldwide.
