Quercus marilandica var. marilandica

Quercus marilandica var. marilandica, commonly known as the Maryland Oak, is a distinctive medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent tannins and bioactive compounds, this variety offers significant therapeutic potential. It is frequently utilized for its astringent properties to treat various inflammatory and skin-related conditions.

  • Scientific name: Quercus marilandica var. marilandica
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales and the family Fagaceae, it is classified under the genus Quercus. Its specific taxonomic designation is Quercus marilandica var. marilandica.
