Acer triflorum

Acer triflorum, commonly known as the three-flowered maple, is a deciduous tree native to East Asia. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significance in traditional medicine. Its bark and various parts are utilized in herbal remedies to treat ailments, showcasing the profound intersection of botany and therapeutic practice.

  • Scientific name: Acer triflorum
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. It is further identified by the genus Acer, specifically as the species Acer triflorum.
