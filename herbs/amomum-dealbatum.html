Amomum dealbatum

Amomum dealbatum, commonly known as white cardamom, is a precious medicinal plant native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it serves as a potent remedy in traditional medicine. It is widely utilized to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and inflammation, offering significant therapeutic benefits for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Amomum dealbatum
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Amomum.
