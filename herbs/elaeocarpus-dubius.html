Elaeocarpus dubius

Elaeocarpus dubius, commonly known as the Dubious Elaeocarpus, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical rainforests. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies. Its diverse phytochemical properties offer promising potential for developing therapeutic treatments, highlighting its importance in ethnobotanical research and pharmacology.

  • Family: Elaeocarpaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
