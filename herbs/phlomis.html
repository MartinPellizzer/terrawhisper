Phlomis

Phlomis, commonly known as sage balm or Jerusalem sage, is a resilient perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Characterized by its velvety, textured leaves and architectural flower spikes, this plant offers diverse therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits in various holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, the specific medicinal genus to which it belongs is Phlomis.
