Jerusalem Sages, scientifically known as Phlomis, is a versatile genus of perennial herbs celebrated for their architectural beauty and medicinal properties. Renowned for their velvety, textured foliage and striking tubular flowers, these resilient plants offer therapeutic benefits, traditionally used to support respiratory health and soothe various inflammatory conditions.
