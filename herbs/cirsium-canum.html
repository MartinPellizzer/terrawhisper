Cirsium canum

Cirsium canum, commonly known as the yellow thistle, is a striking perennial herb native to North America. Characterized by its vibrant, golden-yellow flower heads and prickly foliage, this plant plays a vital role in local ecosystems, providing essential nectar for pollinators while offering unique ethnobotanical potential in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cirsium canum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it falls under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Cirsium as the species Cirsium canum.
