Liquidambar formosana

Sweet gum

Liquidambar formosana, commonly known as the Formosan sweetgum, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its aromatic resin, it possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Historically used to treat skin ailments and respiratory issues, this species remains a significant subject in modern ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Liquidambar formosana
  • Family: Altingiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Saxifragales and the family Altingiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Liquidambar.
