Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several distinct Mediterranean and Atlantic regions. Within Southwestern Europe, its presence is recorded in both the Balearic Islands and Sardegna. Its range extends into Southeastern Europe, specifically within the territory of Sicilia. Moving toward the southern coast, the species can also be found in Northern Africa, particularly in Tunisia. Finally, its habitat includes the Macaronesian region, specifically encompassing the Canary Islands.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Selenicereus, is often valued for its unique botanical structures, particularly its specialized fruit. These fruits, which are frequently referred to as dragon fruits or pitaya, develop from large, nocturnal flowers and are characterized by their vibrant skin and flesh packed with tiny, edible seeds. In many traditional medicinal practices, the juice or pulp of the fruit is utilized for its high antioxidant content and hydrating properties.
