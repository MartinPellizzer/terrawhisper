Lagenaria siceraria

Bottle gourd

Lagenaria siceraria, commonly known as the bottle gourd, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in essential nutrients, it is traditionally used to treat digestive issues, reduce inflammation, and support kidney health. This climbing vine remains a vital staple in holistic wellness and nutrition.

  • Scientific name: Lagenaria siceraria
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Cucurbitales. Finally, it resides in the family Cucurbitaceae under the genus Lagenaria.

From these vines, a large fruit develops, often growing to a significant size; these individual fruits can be harvested for both nutritional and medicinal use.
