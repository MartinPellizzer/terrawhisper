Ipomoea aquatica

Water spinach

Ipomoea aquatica, commonly known as water spinach or kangkong, is a highly nutritious leafy green vegetable native to tropical regions. Widely cultivated in Asia, this aquatic plant is prized for its rapid growth and versatile culinary uses. Beyond nutrition, it possesses significant medicinal properties, including antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea aquatica
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Ipomoea.
