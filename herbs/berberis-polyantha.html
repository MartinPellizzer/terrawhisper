Berberis polyantha

Berberis polyantha, commonly known as the many-flowered barberry, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its ornamental beauty and medicinal properties. Native to various regions, it features striking yellow blossoms and vibrant foliage. In traditional medicine, its roots and bark are utilized for their potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial benefits.

  • Scientific name: Berberis polyantha
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is further organized into the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Berberis.
