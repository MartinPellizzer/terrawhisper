Mentha suaveolens

Mentha suaveolens, commonly known as Sweet Mint, is a fragrant perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its refreshing aroma and culinary versatility, this plant offers significant medicinal properties. It is traditionally utilized to soothe digestive issues, alleviate respiratory discomfort, and provide calming aromatherapy benefits.

  • Scientific name: Mentha suaveolens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Mentha.
