Plant Parts

This plant, Papaver somniferum, is highly valued for the specific components harvested from its structure for medicinal purposes. The most critical parts used in pharmaceutical production are the mature seed capsules, which are harvested once they have dried on the stem. From these capsules, a milky, dry latex is extracted, containing potent alkaloid compounds that serve as the primary source for various analgesic medications.
