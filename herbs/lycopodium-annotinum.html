Spinulum annotinum, scientifically known as Lycopodium annotinum, is a perennial clubmoss native to temperate forests. Often called Stagwort Clubmoss, this resilient plant thrives in acidic, moist soils. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine for its unique properties, it serves as a fascinating subject for both botanical study and ethnobotanical research.
