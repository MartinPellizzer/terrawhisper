Ipomoea lacunosa

Ipomoea lacunosa, commonly known as the lacunose morning glory, is a versatile medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This resilient climber offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea lacunosa
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales, it is classified under the family Convolvulaceae and the genus Ipomoea.

Common Names

This plant, Ipomoea lacunosa, is known by various regional names across different languages and cultures. In Sweden, it is referred to as Vit praktvinda (sv), while in Wales, it carries the name Tegwch-y-bore gwyn (cy). The Finnish language identifies it as kaljuelämänlanka (fi), and it is known in Taiwan as 白星牽牛 (zh-tw). Additionally, in Chinese, it is called 瘤梗甘薯 (zh).
