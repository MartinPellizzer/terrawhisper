Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China peninsula, it can be found growing in Cambodia, Laos, Thailand, and Vietnam. Beyond the mainland, its presence extends into the Malesia region. Specifically, it is documented to inhabit the island of Jawa. This range highlights the plant's ability to thrive in various tropical environments across the region.
