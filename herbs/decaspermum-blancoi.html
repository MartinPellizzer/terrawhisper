Decaspermum blancoi

Decaspermum blancoi, commonly known as the Philippine citrus or local lime, is a significant medicinal shrub native to the Philippines. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin infections, offering a potent natural remedy for holistic wellness.
