Vitis heyneana var. heyneana

Vitis heyneana var. heyneana, commonly known as a wild grape variety, is a significant medicinal climber found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to digestive issues, offering immense potential for modern therapeutic research.
