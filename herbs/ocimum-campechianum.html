Ocimum campechianum

Ocimum campechianum, commonly known as wild basil, is a versatile medicinal plant prized for its aromatic properties. Rich in essential oils, it possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory qualities. Traditionally used in various cultures, this species offers significant therapeutic potential in treating respiratory ailments and promoting overall digestive wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ocimum campechianum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ocimum.
