Deparia boryana

Deparia boryana, commonly known as the Borrya fern, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to specific forest ecosystems. Valued for its unique phytochemical properties, it has long been utilized in traditional healing practices. Understanding its botanical characteristics and therapeutic potential is essential for exploring its role in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Deparia boryana
  • Family: Aspleniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, specifically within the family Aspleniaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Deparia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily located within the tropical regions of West and West-Central Africa. Its distribution begins in West Tropical Africa, where it can be found in Ghana and Ivory Coast. The species also grows across the West Tropical Africa region within the borders of Liberia and Nigeria. Furthermore, its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within Cameroon. Together, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of Deparia boryana.
