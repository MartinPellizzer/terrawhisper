Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across different continents. In the Indo-China region, its presence has been specifically documented in Thailand. Moving to North-Central U.S.A., the species can be found growing in the state of Illinois. It also inhabits the plains of Kansas and Missouri within the same American region. Finally, its range extends further south into the territory of Oklahoma.

Plant Parts

This plant, Phyllanthus carolinensis, is characterized by its unique foliage, where the medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in the leaf. The leaves are typically small, green, and arranged in a manner that often mimics the appearance of a compound leaf, contributing to the plant's distinct architectural structure. In traditional medicinal practices, these leaves are the most frequently utilized part of the plant for preparing various therapeutic remedies.
