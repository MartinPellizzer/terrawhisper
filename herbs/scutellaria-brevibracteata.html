Scutellaria brevibracteata

Scutelariia brevibracteata, commonly known as a traditional medicinal herb, is a significant plant species valued in herbal medicine. Primarily utilized in East Asian practices, it is recognized for its bioactive compounds that offer anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, serving as a vital natural resource for supporting various therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria brevibracteata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Scutellaria.
