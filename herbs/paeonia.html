Peony, scientifically known as Paeonia, is a versatile medicinal plant revered for centuries in traditional healing. Renowned for its striking blooms, its roots contain bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, pain, and menstrual disorders. This resilient perennial serves as a cornerstone in herbal pharmacology for promoting overall wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Peony, possesses various parts that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The most significant therapeutic component is found in the roots, specifically the fleshy taproot which is harvested and processed to extract its active compounds. Beyond the underground structures, the cortex or bark of the plant can also be of interest in certain botanical studies. The stem provides the necessary structural support for the leaves and flowers, while the seed oil extracted from its seeds offers unique fatty acid profiles that are often studied for their topical applications.
