Paeonia

Kirigo peony

Peonia, commonly known as peony, is a versatile flowering plant celebrated for both its ornamental beauty and significant medicinal properties. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its roots contain bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, alleviate menstrual discomfort, and support circulatory health, making it a cornerstone of botanical therapeutic practices.

  • Scientific name: Paeonia
  • Family: Paeoniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Paeoniaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Paeonia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Peony, possesses various parts that are utilized for their medicinal properties. The most significant therapeutic component is found in the roots, specifically the fleshy taproot which is harvested and processed to extract its active compounds. Beyond the underground structures, the cortex or bark of the plant can also be of interest in certain botanical studies. The stem provides the necessary structural support for the leaves and flowers, while the seed oil extracted from its seeds offers unique fatty acid profiles that are often studied for their topical applications.
