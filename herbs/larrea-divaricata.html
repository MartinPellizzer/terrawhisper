Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions of the South American continent. In Western South America, its presence is noted in both Bolivia and Peru. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, it can be found throughout various parts of Argentina. Specifically, its range covers the Northeast, Northwest, and South regions of the country. This extensive coverage demonstrates the plant's ability to thrive in diverse South American environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Jarilla, utilizes various structures for its medicinal properties, most notably its small, resinous leaves and its delicate flowers. The leaves are characterized by a thick, waxy coating that helps the plant retain moisture in arid environments, and they are the primary part used in traditional infusions to treat inflammatory conditions. Additionally, the flowers, which typically appear in shades of yellow, contribute to the plant's reproductive cycle and are often studied for their specific chemical compositions.
