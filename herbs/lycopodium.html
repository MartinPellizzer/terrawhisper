Lycopodium

Lycopodium, commonly known as clubmoss, is a fascinating genus of ancient vascular plants. Often used in traditional medicine and botanical studies, these non-flowering species are distinguished by their spore-bearing cones. Renowned for their unique evolutionary history, they offer significant interest in both ecological research and historical herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Lycopodium
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
