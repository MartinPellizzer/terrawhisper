Cupaniopsis macropetala

Cupaniopsis macropetala, commonly known as the medicinal shrub of the tropical rainforest, is a botanical treasure valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating inflammation and infections, making it a subject of growing interest in ethnopharmacology.
