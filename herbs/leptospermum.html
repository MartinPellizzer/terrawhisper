Tea Trees, scientifically known as Leptospermum, is a diverse genus of shrubs and trees native to Australia and New Zealand. Renowned for their potent essential oils, these plants possess remarkable antimicrobial and healing properties. Often used in traditional medicine, they remain a cornerstone of natural skincare and holistic wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several continents and oceanic regions. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented within Great Britain. The species is also found across the Macaronesian islands, specifically in the Azores and Madeira. Moving toward the African continent, it is located in East Tropical Africa. Within this region, its range extends to include both Kenya and Uganda.
