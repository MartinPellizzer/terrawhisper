Leptospermum

Leptospermum, commonly known as Manuka, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to New Zealand and Australia. Renowned for its potent antibacterial properties, its honey and essential oils are widely utilized in natural healing. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic benefits, supporting skin health and strengthening the immune system effectively.

  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Europe
