Pseudodictamnus hirsutus subsp. hirsutus

Pseudodictamnus hirsutus subsp. hirsutus, commonly known as hairy dictamnus, is a resilient perennial herb native to East Asian montane regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this species contains bioactive alkaloids used for their potent anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, offering significant potential in modern pharmacological research for treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Pseudodictamnus hirsutus subsp. hirsutus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. It is further identified by the genus Pseudodictamnus, specifically as the subspecies hirsutus.

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range spanning parts of Southwestern Europe and Northern Africa. Within the European region, its presence is documented across the Balearic Islands and Corsica. It can also be found inhabiting various territories throughout Spain and Portugal.
