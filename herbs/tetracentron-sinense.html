Tetracentron sinense

Tetracentron sinense, commonly known as the Chinese tea plant, is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it offers significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often utilized in herbal formulations, this plant serves as a vital resource for promoting holistic wellness and therapeutic healing.

  • Scientific name: Tetracentron sinense
  • Family: Trochodendraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Trochodendrales, it is classified under the family Trochodendraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Tetracentron.
