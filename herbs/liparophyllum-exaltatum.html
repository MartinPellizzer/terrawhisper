Liparophyllum exaltatum

Liparophyllum exaltatum, commonly known as the Golden Starleaf, is a rare medicinal herb prized for its potent bioactive compounds. Native to high-altitude ecosystems, this remarkable plant offers significant therapeutic potential in treating inflammation and oxidative stress, making it a vital subject for modern pharmacological research and traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Liparophyllum exaltatum
  • Family: Menyanthaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Menyanthaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Liparophyllum, specifically the species Liparophyllum exaltatum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several distinct regions within the Australian continent. It can be found growing naturally throughout the state of New South Wales. Its range also extends northward into the tropical and subtropical areas of Queensland. Furthermore, the species inhabits the temperate landscapes of Victoria. Finally, its geographical presence reaches as far south as the island of Tasmania.
