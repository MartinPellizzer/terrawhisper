Annona montana

Annona montana, commonly known as soursop, is a tropical evergreen tree native to the Americas. Renowned for its delicious, tangy fruit, it holds significant value in traditional medicine. Research suggests its bioactive compounds may offer antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and potential anticancer properties, making it a subject of intense scientific interest.

  • Scientific name: Annona montana
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales, it is classified under the family Annonaceae. Finally, Annona montana is identified by its specific genus, Annona.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several tropical regions. In Central America, it is commonly found throughout Costa Rica, Honduras, and Panamá.
