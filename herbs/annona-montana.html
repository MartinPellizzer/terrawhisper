Mountain soursop, scientifically known as Annona montana, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree celebrated for its nutritional and medicinal properties. Renowned for its tangy, creamy pulp, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to support immune health, reduce inflammation, and manage oxidative stress through its rich antioxidant content.
