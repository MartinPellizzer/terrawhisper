Minthostachys andina

Minthostachys andina, commonly known as Andean mint, is a resilient medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Andes. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to soothe digestive issues, alleviate respiratory discomfort, and provide relief from tension through its refreshing, herbal essence.

  • Scientific name: Minthostachys andina
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
