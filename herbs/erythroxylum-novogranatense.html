Colombian coca

Erythroxylum novogranatense · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (288 studies) · ★★★★★

Colombian coca, scientifically known as Erythroxylum novogranatense, is a distinctive shrub native to the Andean regions. Renowned for its unique alkaloid profile, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. It has been utilized traditionally for centuries in indigenous practices for its stimulating properties and its profound cultural and medicinal heritage.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several specific regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central areas as well as across the Southeast.
