Erythroxylum novogranatense

Erythroxylum novogranatense, commonly known as a relative of the coca plant, is a significant medicinal species native to the Neotropical regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, it contains various alkaloids with potent physiological effects. Understanding its chemical properties and ecological role is essential for exploring its potential pharmacological applications.

  • Scientific name: Erythroxylum novogranatense
  • Family: Erythroxylaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Erythroxylaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Erythroxylum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several specific regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is noted in the South-Central areas as well as across the Southeast. It can also be found growing on the tropical island of Hainan. Beyond the mainland, the species is distributed throughout Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Finally, its geographical reach extends into the Indo-China region, specifically within Vietnam.
