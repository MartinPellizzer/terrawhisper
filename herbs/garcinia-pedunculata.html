Garcinia pedunculata

Garcinia pedunculata, commonly known as the Malabar tamarind, is a significant medicinal plant native to the Western Ghats. Renowned for its antioxidant and antimicrobial properties, it is widely used in traditional Ayurveda. The plant's fruit and bark offer therapeutic benefits, particularly in managing digestive issues and promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia pedunculata
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling under the family Clusiaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Garcinia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution spanning across parts of Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central regions as well as in Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is native to the state of Assam. It also inhabits the territory of Bangladesh within the same subcontinent. Furthermore, its presence is noted throughout the East Himalaya region.
