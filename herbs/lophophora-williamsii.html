Lophophora williamsii

Peyote

Lophophora williamsii, commonly known as peyote, is a small, slow-growing cactus native to the Chihuahuan Desert. Renowned for its potent psychoactive alkaloid, mescaline, this sacred plant holds profound spiritual significance in indigenous cultures. It is widely studied for its complex pharmacological properties and its profound impact on human consciousness.

  • Scientific name: Lophophora williamsii
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
