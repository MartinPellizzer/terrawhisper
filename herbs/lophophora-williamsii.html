Peyote, scientifically known as Lophophora williamsii, is a small, spineless cactus native to the Chihuahuan Desert. Renowned for its psychoactive properties, it contains mescaline, a potent alkaloid used in sacred indigenous rituals. This slow-growing perennial is currently threatened by overharvesting, making its conservation a critical global priority.
