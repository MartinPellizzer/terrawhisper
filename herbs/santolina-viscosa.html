Santolina viscosa

Santolina viscosa, commonly known as Sacred Lavender or Cotton Lavender, is a perennial aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean and parts of Asia. Renowned for its silvery-grey foliage and yellow blooms, this medicinal plant is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin inflammations.

  • Scientific name: Santolina viscosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Santolina.
