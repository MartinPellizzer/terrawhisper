Santolina viscosa, scientifically known as Santolina viscosa, is a perennial aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean and parts of Asia. Renowned for its silver-gray foliage and bright yellow flowers, this medicinal herb is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and various skin inflammations.
