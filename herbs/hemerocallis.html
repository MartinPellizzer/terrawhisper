Hemerocallis

Daylilies

Hemerocallis, commonly known as the daylily, is a versatile perennial valued for its stunning blooms and medicinal potential. Traditionally used in various cultures, it offers diverse therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. Exploring its botanical benefits reveals a fascinating intersection of ornamental beauty and holistic wellness applications.

  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales and the family Asphodelaceae, it is classified under the genus Hemerocallis.
