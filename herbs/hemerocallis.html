Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the daylily, is valued in various traditional practices for the medicinal properties found within its aerial parts. The aerial parts, which include the long, strap-like green leaves and the prominent flowering stalks, serve as the primary components used in herbal preparations. These above-ground structures are often harvested to create decoctions or infusions, as they contain the bioactive compounds necessary for their therapeutic effects.
