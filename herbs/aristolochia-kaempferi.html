Aristolochia kaempferi

Aristolochia kaempferi, commonly known as Japanese birthwort or Kaempferia, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its unique medicinal properties, it is frequently used to treat inflammation and respiratory ailments. This plant remains a significant subject of study in botanical pharmacology and herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Aristolochia kaempferi
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Piperales, falling into the family Aristolochiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aristolochia.
