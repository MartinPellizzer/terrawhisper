Michelia maudiae

What's the taxonomical classification of Michelia maudiae?

Michelia maudiae belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further classified into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales. This plant is a member of the family Magnoliaceae and is specifically situated within the genus Michelia, reaching its final taxonomic designation at the species level as maudiae.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Michelia maudiae has an evergreen habit characterized by large, elliptic to oblong leaves that are coriaceous in texture and possess entire margins. The foliage displays a deep green color on the upper surface with a lighter underside, often featuring prominent pinnate venation. Its inflorescence consists of fragrant, solitary flowers that emerge from the leaf axils during the blooming season. These flowers typically feature multiple layers of creamy white to pale yellow petals and numerous stamens arranged in a central cluster. The plant also develops woody, indehiscent fruits that eventually split to reveal bright red seeds.

According to a study published by "Molecules (Basel, Switzerland)", the leaves of Michelia maudiae contain abundant essential oils that can be used in medicinal fields. This essential oil contains specific compounds such as valeranone and nerolidol. The essential oil of Michelia maudiae demonstrated the best antioxidant activity with an IC50 value of 1283.58~6258.32 μg/mL. Furthermore, the LC50 value for its antibacterial activity was 0.03~2.28 mg/mL. Overall, the essential oil of Michelia maudiae had the best comprehensive performance.
