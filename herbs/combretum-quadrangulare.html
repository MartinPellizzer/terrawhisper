Combretum quadrangulare, scientifically known as Combretum quadrangulare, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Widely revered in traditional medicine, it is most famous for its ability to support bone health, enhance athletic performance, and promote weight management through its unique bioactive compounds and antioxidant properties.
