Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of Southern Africa. Within South Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Mozambique. In the Southern African region, it can be found growing throughout the Cape Provinces. It also inhabits the coastal and inland areas of KwaZulu-Natal. Finally, the species extends its range into the Northern Provinces of the same region.
