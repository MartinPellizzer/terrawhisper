Lomatia hirsuta

Lomatia hirsuta, commonly known as the hairy lomatia, is a distinctive shrub native to the diverse ecosystems of Australia. Characterized by its textured, pubescent foliage, this resilient plant plays a vital role in its habitat, offering unique botanical interest and contributing to the rich biodiversity of its native landscape.

  • Scientific name: Lomatia hirsuta
  • Family: Proteaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic hierarchy places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Proteales. Finally, it is a member of the family Proteaceae and is categorized under the genus Lomatia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several regions of the South American continent. In the western part of the continent, it can be found growing in Ecuador and Peru. Moving toward the southern reaches, its range extends into the southern regions of Argentina. The species is also well-established within the central and southern territories of Chile. Consequently, its habitat spans a diverse longitudinal stretch from the equatorial west to the southern tip of the landmass.
