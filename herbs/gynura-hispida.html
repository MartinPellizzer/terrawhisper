Gynura hispida

Gynura hispida, commonly known as the bristly gooseherb, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, promote wound healing, and support digestive health, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Gynura hispida
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. It is specifically identified by its genus, Gynura.
