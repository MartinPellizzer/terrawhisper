Euphorbia fischeriana

Euphorbia fischeriana, commonly known as a unique member of the Euphorbiaceae family, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse bioactive compounds. Native to specific tropical regions, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia fischeriana
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling into the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified within the genus Euphorbia as the species Euphorbia fischeriana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of East Asia. In Russia, its presence is specifically noted within the Chitá region of Siberia. Moving into China, it can be found spanning from Inner Mongolia to the Manchuria area, as well as across North-Central China. The species also extends its range into the territory of Mongolia. Collectively, these locations highlight a broad habitat stretching across northern continental landscapes.
