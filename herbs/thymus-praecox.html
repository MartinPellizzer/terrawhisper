Creeping thyme, scientifically known as Thymus praecox, is a versatile, low-growing perennial prized for its aromatic foliage and delicate blossoms. Renowned for its antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, this hardy herb is traditionally used in herbal medicine to support respiratory health, soothe digestive issues, and promote overall wellness naturally.
