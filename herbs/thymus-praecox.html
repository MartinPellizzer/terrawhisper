Thymus praecox

Thymus praecox, commonly known as creeping thyme, is a resilient, low-growing perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic foliage and delicate blossoms. Widely used in traditional medicine for its antiseptic and digestive properties, this versatile plant thrives in sunny landscapes, offering both medicinal benefits and beautiful groundcover for gardens.

  • Scientific name: Thymus praecox
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Thymus.
