Sulla coronaria

Sulla coronaaria, commonly known as the Crowned Sulla, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its unique floral structure, this plant offers therapeutic potential in treating various ailments. Understanding its botanical properties and bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its role in natural healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Sulla coronaria
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Sulla.
