What's the taxonomical classification of Bridelia tomentosa?

Bridelia tomentoza belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales. This plant is a representative of the family Phyllanthaceae, specifically situated within the genus Bridelia, with its unique biological identity defined by its specific epithet, tomentosa.

According to a study published by "Toxicology reports", the leaf of Bridelia tomentoosa is used as a traditional medicinal plant for treating diverse ailments. The study utilized a methanol extract of Bridelia tomentoosa leaf (BTL) to investigate its protective effects in Sprague-Dawley rats. HPLC analysis of the BTL extract confirmed the existence of tannic acid, gallic acid, salicylic acid, and naringin. These components provided in-vitro antioxidant potentialities through scavenging properties against DPPH, nitric oxide, hydrogen peroxide, and hydroxyl radicals. The BTL extract showed dose-dependent significant protective effects on various serum markers including alanine aminotransferase, aspartate aminotransferase, alkaline phosphatase, γ-glutamyl-transferase, and lactate dehydrogenase. Furthermore, the BTL extract increased levels of hepatic antioxidant enzymes such as superoxide dismutase, catalase, and glutathione peroxidase.
