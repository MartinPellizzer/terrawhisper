Litsea rubescens

Litsea rubescens, commonly known as a significant medicinal species in tropical regions, offers a rich profile of bioactive compounds. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Understanding its chemical constituents is essential for exploring its pharmacological properties and future medicinal applications.
