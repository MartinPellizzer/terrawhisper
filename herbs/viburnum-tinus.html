Viburnum tinus

Laurustinus

Viburnum tinus, commonly known as Laurustinus, is a versatile evergreen shrub prized for its glossy foliage and fragrant white blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this hardy plant holds traditional medicinal value, often utilized in herbal remedies for its potential antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and soothing properties in various botanical applications.

  • Scientific name: Viburnum tinus
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Dipsacales and placed in the family Viburnaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Viburnum.
