Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Dipsacales and placed in the family Viburnaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Viburnum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence is noted within the territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it is also found throughout parts of Southwestern Europe. Specific locations within this southern region include the Balearic Islands, Corsica, and mainland France. Consequently, the distribution of Viburnum tinus stretches from the Atlantic coasts to the warmer Mediterranean landscapes.
