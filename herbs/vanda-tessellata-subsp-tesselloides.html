Vanda tessellata subsp. tesselloides

Vanda tesselata subsp. tesselloides, commonly known as a subspecies of the Vanda orchid, is a remarkable botanical specimen valued for its aesthetic and potential medicinal properties. Found in diverse tropical habitats, this orchid is studied for its unique bioactive compounds, offering significant insights into traditional herbal remedies and pharmacology.
