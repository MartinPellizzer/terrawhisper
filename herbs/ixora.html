Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented across several distinct nations, including Benin and Burkina. Additionally, the species can be found extending through the territory of Gambia. The range also encompasses parts of Ghana within the same tropical zone. Finally, the distribution reaches into Guinea-Bissau, completing its regional profile.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Ixora, is frequently studied for its various therapeutic properties, with the flower being one of its most distinctive and utilized components. These vibrant, tubular blooms are often concentrated in dense clusters, providing not only aesthetic beauty to gardens but also serving as a focal point for traditional medicinal preparations. In various herbal practices, extracts derived from the flower are sometimes employed due to their specific bioactive compounds.
