Dischidia bengalensis

Dischidid bengalensis, commonly known as a medicinal succulent, is a unique plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive morphology, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional practitioners utilize various parts of this species to treat diverse ailments, highlighting its growing importance in natural pharmacology and botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Dischidia bengalensis
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized into the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Dischidid.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, its presence is documented in the state of Assam and throughout the country of Bangladesh. It can also be found growing in the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya and across the landscapes of Nepal. Beyond the Indian Subcontinent, its range extends into the Indo-China region, specifically within Myanmar. Together, these areas define the diverse geographical footprint of Dischidia bengalensis.
