Ephidras, scientifically known as Ephedra, is a diverse genus of gymnosperm shrubs found in arid regions worldwide. Renowned for its historical use in traditional medicine, it contains alkaloids like ephedrine. These compounds are widely utilized in modern pharmacology for treating respiratory ailments, stimulating alertness, and managing blood pressure.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Gnetidae and the order Ephedrales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ephedraceae and is represented by the genus Ephedra.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific range of locations across the European continent. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing in the landscapes of Austria and Switzerland. The species also inhabits the regions of Czechia-Slovakia and Hungary within the same central zone. Moving toward the Mediterranean, its presence extends into Southwestern Europe. Specifically, it is documented to grow within the Balearic Islands.
