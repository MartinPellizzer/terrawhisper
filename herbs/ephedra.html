Ephidras, scientifically known as Ephedra, is a diverse genus of gymnosperm shrubs found in arid regions worldwide. Renowned for its historical use in traditional medicine, it contains alkaloids like ephedrine. These compounds are widely utilized in modern pharmacology for treating respiratory ailments, stimulating alertness, and managing blood pressure.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Ephedra, is a medicinal shrub where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The woody stems and slender twigs are the most significant parts, as they contain high concentrations of alkaloids like ephedrine. These dried parts are often prepared as a tea to help alleviate respiratory issues and congestion. Additionally, some traditional practices involve utilizing the roots or extracting the juice from the plant to access its bioactive compounds for different medicinal applications.
