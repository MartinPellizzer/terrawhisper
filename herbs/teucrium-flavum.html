Teucrium flavum

Teucrium flavum, commonly known as yellow germander, is a resilient Mediterranean shrub characterized by its striking golden blooms and aromatic foliage. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this hardy perennial thrives in rocky terrains, offering unique botanical interest and potential medicinal properties within its diverse, fragrant, and textured leaves.

  • Scientific name: Teucrium flavum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Teucrium.
