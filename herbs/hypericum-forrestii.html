Hypericum forrestii

Hypericum forrestii, commonly known as Forrest's St. John's Wort, is a specialized perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of China. Distinguished by its delicate yellow blooms, this botanical species belongs to the Hypericaceae family, offering unique phytochemical properties that intrigue researchers studying traditional medicine and natural bioactive compounds.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Hypericum forrestii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
