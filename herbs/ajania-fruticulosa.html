Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Asia. In the northern reaches of its range, it can be found in the Tuva region of Siberia. Moving southward into Central Asia, its presence extends through both Kazakhstan and Tajikistan. The species also inhabits parts of Western Asia, specifically within the country of Iran. Finally, its distribution continues into China, where it is located in Inner Mongolia.
