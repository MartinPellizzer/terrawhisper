Scientific literature: Very Extensive (240 studies) · ★★★★★

, scientifically known as Passiflora quadrangularis, is a vigorous climbing vine native to tropical regions. Often called the Giant Passionflower, this medicinal plant is renowned for its large, striking blooms and therapeutic potential. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures to promote relaxation, alleviate anxiety, and support digestive health.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence can be identified in Guinea-Bissau. The species also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is found in Cameroon and Gabon. Additionally, it inhabits the Gulf of Guinea Islands within the West-Central Tropical region. Finally, its range reaches East Tropical Africa, specifically in Kenya.
