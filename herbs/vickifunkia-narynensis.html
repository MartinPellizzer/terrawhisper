Vickifunkia narynensis

Vickifunkia narynensis, commonly known as the Yellow Flowering Vicki, is a stunning evergreen shrub prized for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blossoms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this botanical gem holds significant potential in traditional medicine, offering unique phytochemical properties that warrant scientific exploration for their therapeutic and healing capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Vickifunkia narynensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is placed within the genus Vickifunkia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of Central and East Asia. Within the territory of Central Asia, it can be found growing in Kazakhstan. Its presence is also documented in the mountainous landscapes of Tajikistan. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the Xinjiang region of China. These three locations represent the primary known habitats for this medicinal species.
