Glaucium

Glaucium, commonly known as Blue Globe Thistle, is a striking medicinal plant prized for its unique spherical blooms. Renowned in traditional herbalism, it possesses potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Beyond its aesthetic charm, this resilient perennial offers therapeutic potential in treating various ailments, making it a botanical treasure.

  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe
