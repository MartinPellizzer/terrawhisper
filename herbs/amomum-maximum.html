Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several major Asian regions. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central and Southeast territories. Additionally, it can be found growing in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is well-established within the borders of India. Finally, its range extends further south to include the island nation of Sri Lanka.

Plant Parts

This plant, Amomum maximum, is a species within the ginger family, and its medicinal properties are primarily derived from its underground structures. The root, specifically the rhizome, serves as the most significant part used in traditional applications. These thickened, aromatic rhizomes are harvested and processed to extract essential oils and bioactive compounds that contribute to the plant's therapeutic potential.
