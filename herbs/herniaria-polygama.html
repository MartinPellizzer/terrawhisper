Herniaria polygama

Herniaria polygama, commonly known as Polygonum or Herniaria, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently used to support kidney health and treat urinary tract issues. This resilient plant offers a natural approach to promoting holistic bodily wellness.

  • Scientific name: Herniaria polygama
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Caryophyllaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Herniaria.
