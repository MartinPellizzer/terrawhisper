Polystichum sinense

What's the taxonomical classification of Polystichum sinense?

Polystichum sinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it falls under the subclass Polypodiinae and is organized within the order Polypodiales. This plant is a representative of the family Polypodiaceae, specifically categorized under the genus Polystichum, with the specific designation of the species sinese.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Polystichum sinense has large, leathery, pinnate fronds that typically measure between 30 and 100 centimeters in length and feature lanceolate leaflets with serrated margins. The stipe is stout and often covered in dark, brownish scales that persist throughout the plant's growth. Each pinna is arranged symmetrically along a central rachis, which provides structural support for the dense foliage. Small, kidney-shaped sori are positioned on the undersides of the leaflets, protected by thin membranes called indusia. The overall texture of the plant is firm and glossy, reflecting its adaptation to shaded, moist forest environments.
