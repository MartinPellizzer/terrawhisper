Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, falling under the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hura, specifically as the species Hura crepitans.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans across several continents. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found within the borders of Benin, Guinea, and Guinea-Bissau. Its presence also extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically within the Central African Republic. Interestingly, the species is not limited to Africa, as it also inhabits the Southeastern United States. Within that American region, the plant is specifically documented in the state of Florida.
