Corymbia ficifolia

Red flowering gum

Corymbia ficifolia, commonly known as the Red Flowering Gum, is a stunning Australian native tree prized for its vibrant crimson blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it holds traditional significance. This resilient species thrives in diverse environments, offering ecological value to pollinators while serving as a breathtaking centerpiece in botanical landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Corymbia ficifolia
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, falling into the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Corymbia.

Its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it is located in Rwanda. In the Northeast Tropical region, the species is established in Ethiopia. Finally, its range reaches East Tropical Africa, encompassing both Kenya and Tanzania.
