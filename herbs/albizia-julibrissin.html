Albizia julibrissin

Acacia julibrissin

Albizia julibrissin, commonly known as the Persian silk tree, is a graceful flowering plant valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its delicate, feathery foliage and pink pom-pom blooms, it is often utilized in herbal practices to promote relaxation, reduce anxiety, and support cardiovascular health through its unique bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Albizia julibrissin
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, placing it in the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Albizia.
