Osmanthus fragrans

Osmanthus fragrans, commonly known as sweet osmanthus or sweet olive, is a fragrant evergreen shrub cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its intoxicatingly sweet aroma, it is used to treat respiratory ailments, soothe emotions, and support digestive health. This versatile plant offers both aesthetic beauty and therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Osmanthus fragrans
  • Family: Oleaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Lamiales and the family Oleaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Osmanthus.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed throughout various regions of China, including the South-Central and North-Central areas. It can also be found growing in the tropical environment of Hainan. Furthermore, the species is commonly located across China Southeast. Beyond mainland China, its presence extends to Eastern Asia, specifically in Japan.
