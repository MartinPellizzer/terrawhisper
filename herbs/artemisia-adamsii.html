Artemisia adamsii

Artemisia adamsii, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in Southern Africa, belongs to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and respiratory infections. Its unique chemical composition offers promising potential for modern pharmacological research.
