Artemisia adamsii

Artemisia adamsii, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in Southern Africa, belongs to the Asteraceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and respiratory infections. Its unique chemical composition offers promising potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia adamsii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its scientific classification places it within the genus Artemisia as the species Artemisia adamsii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Northern Asia. Within the vast expanse of Siberia, it can be found specifically in the areas of Buryatiya and Chita. Its range extends significantly into China, where it inhabits both Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Additionally, the species is present within the borders of Mongolia. Together, these locations define the typical habitat where this medicinal species thrives.
