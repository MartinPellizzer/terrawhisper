What's the taxonomical classification of Pronephrium nudatum?

Pronephrium nudatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Polypodiinae and falls under the order Polypodiales. Taxonomically, it is a member of the family Aspleniaceae, situated within the genus Pronephrium, and is specifically identified by the species name nudatum.
