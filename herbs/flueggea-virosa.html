Flueggea virosa

White-berry bush

Flueggea virosa, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical Africa, holds great ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this shrub are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Flueggea virosa
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
