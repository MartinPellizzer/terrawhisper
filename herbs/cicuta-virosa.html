Cicuta virosa, scientifically known as Cicuta virosa, is a potent perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Often mistaken for edible wild parsnips, this plant is highly toxic due to its concentrated cicutoxin. Understanding its identification is crucial, as ingestion can lead to severe, potentially fatal neurological distress.
