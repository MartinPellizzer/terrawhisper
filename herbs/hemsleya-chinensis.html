Hemsleya chinensis

Hemsleya chinensis, commonly known as Chinese Hemsley, is a significant medicinal herb in traditional practices. Belonging to the Araliaceae family, this plant is valued for its bioactive compounds. Often used to address inflammatory conditions and digestive issues, it represents a vital component in natural pharmacology and botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Hemsleya chinensis
  • Family: Cucurbitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cucurbitales, it is classified under the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hemsleya, specifically as the species Hemsleya chinensis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Hemsleya chinensis, is recognized by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is frequently referred to as 雪胆 (zh) or 雪胆 (zh-cn), and in Traditional Chinese characters, it is written as 雪膽 (zh-tw). Additionally, it is also known by the name 罗锅底 (zh).
