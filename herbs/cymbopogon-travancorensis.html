Cymbopogon travancorensis

Cymbopegon travaurencensis, commonly known as Travancore lemongrass, is a rare medicinal grass indigenous to the Western Ghats of India. Renowned for its potent aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic medicine to treat digestive ailments, skin infections, and fever, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Cymbopogon travancorensis
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Cymbopogon.
