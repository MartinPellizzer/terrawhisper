Philippine violet, scientifically known as Barleria cristata, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized for its vibrant purple blooms. Widely used in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots possess potent anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and diuretic properties. It serves as a natural remedy for various ailments, including skin infections and respiratory issues.
