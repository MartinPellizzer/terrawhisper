Ottelia alismoides belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established through its placement in the genus Ottelia, with its unique designation being the species alismoides.
