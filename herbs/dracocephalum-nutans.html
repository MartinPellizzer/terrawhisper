Dracocephalum nutans

Dracocephalum nutans, commonly known as Nodding Dragonhead, is a charming perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Characterized by its distinctive drooping, violet-blue flowers, this aromatic plant thrives in sunny, rocky habitats. It is highly valued in traditional herbal medicine and serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators.

  • Scientific name: Dracocephalum nutans
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Dracocephalum.
