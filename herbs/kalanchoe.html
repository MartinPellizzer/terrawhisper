Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution primarily concentrated within the Mediterranean region. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found inhabiting the various islands of the Baleares. Its presence extends to the rugged landscapes of Corse and the coastal areas of Sardegna. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed across the mainland territories of Spain and Portugal. Together, these locations define the key environmental niches where this medicinal plant thrives.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Kalanchoe, is a succulent characterized by its unique morphology and versatile medicinal properties. While the roots serve to anchor the plant and absorb essential nutrients from the soil, it is primarily the fleshy, thick leaves that are most valued for their therapeutic potential and ability to store water. In many traditional healing practices, all parts of the plant are recognized for their bioactive compounds, though the most concentrated medicinal benefits are often found within the aerial parts that grow above the ground.
