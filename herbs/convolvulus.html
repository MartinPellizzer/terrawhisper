Bindweed, scientifically known as Convolvulus, is a versatile genus of climbing plants celebrated for their trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its reputation as a garden interloper, Convolvulus holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, its various species offer fascinating insights into the complex relationship between botany and healing.
