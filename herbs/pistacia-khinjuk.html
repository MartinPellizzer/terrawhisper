Plant Parts

This plant, Pistacia chinensis (often referred to in various contexts as Pistacia chinjuk), is highly valued for its medicinal properties derived from its exudates. The primary therapeutic components are obtained from the oleo gum resin and the gum that exude from the bark of the tree. These substances are harvested through careful tapping of the trunk and are utilized in traditional medicine for their various healing applications, ranging from digestive support to topical treatments.
