Pistacia khinjuk

Pistacia khinjuk, commonly known as the Chinese Pistachio, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is utilized to treat various ailments. Beyond its medicinal value, this resilient plant is widely valued for its vibrant ornamental foliage.

  • Scientific name: Pistacia khinjuk
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
