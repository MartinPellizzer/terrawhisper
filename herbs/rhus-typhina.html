Rhus typhina

Staghorn sumac

Rhus typhina, commonly known as staghorn sumac, is a striking deciduous shrub celebrated for its velvety red stems and dramatic autumn foliage. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this resilient plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine, valued for its high antioxidant content and tart, vitamin-rich berries used in various remedies.

  • Scientific name: Rhus typhina
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Rhus typhina, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae.
