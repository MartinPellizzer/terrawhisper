Piper latifolium

Piper latifolium, commonly known as the broad-leaved pepper, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for developing new therapeutic treatments in modern herbal pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Piper latifolium
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Pacific

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions of the Pacific Ocean. In the Southwestern Pacific, its presence is documented on the Santa Cruz Islands and throughout Vanuatu. Moving into the South-Central Pacific, the species can also be found within the Cook Islands. Furthermore, its range extends to the Marquesas Islands in the same central region. Finally, the plant is known to inhabit the Society Islands, completing its broad geographical spread.
