Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several key regions of the Pacific Ocean. In the Southwestern Pacific, its presence is documented on the Santa Cruz Islands and throughout Vanuatu. Moving into the South-Central Pacific, the species can also be found within the Cook Islands. Furthermore, its range extends to the Marquesas Islands in the same central region. Finally, the plant is known to inhabit the Society Islands, completing its broad geographical spread.

Plant Parts

This plant, Piper latifolium, possesses various components that are utilized in traditional medicinal practices. While the entire underground structure is often studied, specific focus is frequently placed on the rhizome for its bioactive properties. The roots serve as a vital part of the plant's anatomy, and specialized root extracts are often prepared to harness their therapeutic potential. Additionally, the plant may produce a resin that contributes to its chemical profile, while the root system as a whole provides the foundation for these valuable medicinal constituents.
