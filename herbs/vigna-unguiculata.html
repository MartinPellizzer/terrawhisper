Vigna unguiculata

Cowpea

Vigna unguiculata, commonly known as cowpea, is a versatile legume highly valued in traditional medicine and nutrition. Beyond its role as a staple food source, it offers significant therapeutic potential. Rich in bioactive compounds, this resilient plant is utilized to support metabolic health, combat oxidative stress, and provide essential nutrients.

  • Scientific name: Vigna unguiculata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and is identified by the genus Vigna.
