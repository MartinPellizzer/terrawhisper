Distribution

This plant, known as Vigna unguiculata, exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Eastern Europe, it can be found growing within the territory of Ukraine. Its presence extends significantly into Northern Africa, where it is established in Algeria, Egypt, and Libya.
