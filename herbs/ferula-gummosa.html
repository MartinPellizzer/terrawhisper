Galbanum, scientifically known as Ferula gumosa, is a highly aromatic resin harvested from the stalks of perennial herbs in the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its distinct, green, earthy scent, it has been used since antiquity in perfumery and traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties.

This plant, known as Ferula gummosa, exhibits a specific and limited range of occurrence across two major regions.
