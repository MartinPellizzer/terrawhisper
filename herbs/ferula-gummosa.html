Ferula gummosa

Ferula gummosa, commonly known as Asafoetida or Hing, is a perennial herb prized for its pungent, aromatic resin. Extracted from the roots of the Ferula genus, this medicinal plant has been used for centuries in traditional Ayurvedic and Unani medicine to treat digestive issues, respiratory ailments, and inflammatory conditions.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
