Erythrina rubrinervia

Erythrina rubrinervia, commonly known as the coral coral tree, is a striking medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant red flowers, this species is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse pharmacological properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, making it a significant subject of ethnobotanical study.
