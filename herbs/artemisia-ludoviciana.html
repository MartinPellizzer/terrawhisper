Artemisia ludoviciana

Artemisia ludoviciana, commonly known as big sagebrush, is a perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its aromatic, silvery foliage, this plant contains various bioactive compounds. Traditionally used in folk medicine for its antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, it remains a subject of interest in modern ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia ludoviciana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Artemisia.
