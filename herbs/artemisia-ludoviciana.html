Silver wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia ludoviciana, is a perennial herb native to North America. Characterized by its silvery, aromatic foliage, this resilient plant belongs to the Asteraceae family. Historically valued for its medicinal properties, it is often studied for its essential oils and potential antioxidant effects.
