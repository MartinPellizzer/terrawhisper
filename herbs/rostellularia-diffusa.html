Rostellularia diffusa

Rostellularia diffusa, commonly known as the "fringe-flower," is a delicate perennial herb valued for its unique aesthetic and potential medicinal properties. Native to specific tropical regions, this plant features distinctive blooms and is traditionally utilized in various herbal practices to support holistic wellness and promote natural healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Rostellularia diffusa
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, specifically falling into the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rostellularia and the species name diffusa.
