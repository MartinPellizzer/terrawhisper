Celastrus flagellaris

Celaastrous flagellaris, commonly known as the whip bush, is a versatile medicinal shrub found in diverse ecosystems. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation and skin ailments. Its unique chemical profile offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Celastrus flagellaris
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Celastrus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Eastern Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted within the Primorye territory. The species is also widely distributed throughout China, spanning both the Manchuria region and China North-Central. Furthermore, its range extends across the Korean Peninsula. Finally, the plant can be found within the various islands of Japan.
