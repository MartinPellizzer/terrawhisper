Weinmannia trichosperma

Weinmannia trichosperma, commonly known as "Canelo," is a revered medicinal tree native to the Andean forests. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, its bark is widely utilized in traditional South American medicine to treat respiratory ailments, reduce inflammation, and combat infections, serving as a vital natural remedy.
