Tabernaemontana elegans

Tabernaemontana elegans, commonly known as the elegant pinwheel flower, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, its leaves and bark possess significant bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections, inflammation, and digestive issues, showcasing its therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Tabernaemontana elegans
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, falling into the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Tabernaemontana.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Tabernaemontana elegans, is referred to by various names across different languages and regions. In German, it is called der Zierlicher Krötenbaum, while in Afrikaans, it is commonly known as the Paddaboom. Its names in Chinese include the Traditional Chinese 蟾蜍樹 and the Simplified Chinese 蟾蜍树, both of which translate to "toad tree." Additionally, in Arabic, it is identified as ايليجانس تابيرنايمونتانا.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in Somalia. Its presence extends into East Tropical Africa, specifically within the countries of Kenya and Tanzania. Moving southward, the species is also documented in South Tropical Africa. Within this southern region, the plant is located in both Malawi and Mozambique.
