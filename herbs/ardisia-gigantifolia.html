Ardisia gigantifolia

Ardisia gigantifolia, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, is a plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Found in tropical regions, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Its unique phytochemical profile makes it a subject of growing interest in contemporary pharmacological research and studies.

  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is a member of the family Primulaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Ardisia.
