Pedicularis croizatiana

What's the taxonomical classification of Pedicularis croizatiana?

Pedicularis croizatiana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales. This specific plant is a member of the family Orobanchaceae, falling under the genus Pedicularis, and is identified by its unique species designation, croizatiana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pedicularis croizatiana has upright, glandular-pubescent stems that typically reach heights between 10 and 40 centimeters and bear dense, terminal clusters of tubular, bilabiate flowers. The leaves are primarily basal and deeply pinnatifid, often divided into narrow, linear segments that give the foliage a delicate appearance. Each individual flower features a prominent, hooded upper lip and a smaller, spreading lower lip, often displaying colors ranging from pink to deep magenta. The calyx is tubular and hairy, providing structural support to the developing seed capsules. These capsules eventually split to release numerous small, lightweight seeds characteristic of the genus.
