Prosthechea livida

Prosthechea livida, commonly known as the Livid Orchid, is a captivating epiphytic plant native to tropical regions. While primarily admired for its delicate, pale blossoms, this species holds significant interest in ethnobotany. Its unique biological properties offer fascinating potential for exploring traditional medicinal applications within diverse forest ecosystems.
