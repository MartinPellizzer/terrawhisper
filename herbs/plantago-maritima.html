Sea Plantain, scientifically known as Plantago maritima, is a resilient perennial herb native to coastal environments. Thriving in salty, sandy soils, this plant is distinguished by its fleshy leaves and adaptability. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it offers unique bioactive properties, making it a subject of botanical interest.
