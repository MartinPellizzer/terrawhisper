Swertia japonica

Swertiia japonica, commonly known as Japanese Gentian, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional medicine. Belonging to the Gentianaceae family, it is prized for its bitter principles, which stimulate digestion and support liver health. This potent botanical serves as a vital remedy for promoting metabolic wellness and vitality.
