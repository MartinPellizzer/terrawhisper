Ilex dipyrena

Ilex dipyrena, commonly known as the Madagascar holly, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the unique ecosystems of Madagascar. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in herbal remedies to address various ailments, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural therapeutic applications in modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Ilex dipyrena
  • Family: Aquifoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Aquifoliales and the family Aquifoliaceae, it is classified under the genus Ilex.
