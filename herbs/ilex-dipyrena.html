Himalayan holly, scientifically known as Ilex dipyrena, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its glossy foliage and bright berries, this medicinal plant offers diverse therapeutic potential, traditionally utilized in folk medicine to support various health functions and holistic wellness.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions within East and South Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central territories as well as the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it can be found specifically in the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species thrives within the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the mountainous landscapes of Nepal.
