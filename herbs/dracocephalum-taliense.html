Dracocephalum taliense

What's the taxonomical classification of Dracocephalum taliense?

Dracocephalum taliense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Lamiales. This plant is a member of the family Lamiaceae, situated within the genus Dracocephalum, and is specifically identified by the species name taliense.
