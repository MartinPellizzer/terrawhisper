Illicium merrillianum

Illicium merrillianum, commonly known as the Merrillian star anise, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it contains bioactive compounds used in traditional healing. This species is increasingly studied for its potential therapeutic applications in pharmacology and natural wellness treatments.

  • Scientific name: Illicium merrillianum
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Illicium merrillianum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling into the order Austrobaileyales. Finally, it is a member of the family Schisandraceae and is situated within the genus Illicium.
