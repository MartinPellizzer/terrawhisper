Illicium difengpi

Illicium difengpi, commonly known as the dried fruit of the star anise shrub, is a vital component in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its warming properties, it is primarily used to treat abdominal pain, vomiting, and digestive stagnation. This potent botanical serves as a key remedy for invigorating circulation.

  • Scientific name: Illicium difengpi
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Austrobaileyales. It is further categorized into the family Schisandraceae and the genus Illicium.
