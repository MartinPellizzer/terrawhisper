According to a study published by "Yao xue xue bao = Acta pharmaceutica Sinica", four compounds were isolated from the traditional Chinese medicine Difengpi, which consists of the barks of Illicium difengpi K.I.B. et K.I.M. These four triterpene acids (I-IV) were identified using physico-chemical constants and various spectral analyses including UV, IR, EIMS, 1HNMR, 13CNMR, NOEDE and 2D NMR. These specific triterpene acids (I-IV) were isolated from the Illicium genus for the first time.
