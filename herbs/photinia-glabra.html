Photinia glabra

What's the taxonomical classification of Photinia glabra?

Photinia glabra belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. At the class level, it is situated in Equisetopsida, which falls under the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic lineage further, it is part of the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Photinia, and its specific species name, glabra.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Photinia glabra has an evergreen shrub or small tree structure characterized by elliptical, leathery leaves that measure approximately 5 to 15 centimeters in length and feature finely serrated margins. The foliage often transitions from a bright red color in new growth to a deep, lustrous green as the leaves mature. Its woody stems are typically smooth in younger specimens but develop a more textured bark as the plant ages. Small, white, five-petaled flowers emerge in terminal clusters during the spring season to attract pollinators. These blooms are eventually followed by small, fleshy red drupes that serve as a primary feature of the plant's reproductive morphology.
