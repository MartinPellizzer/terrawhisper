Amnesia

Cannabis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (451 studies) · ★★★★★

Amnesia, scientifically known as Cannabis, is a versatile medicinal plant renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in cannabinoids like THC and CBD, it is widely utilized to manage chronic pain, anxiety, epilepsy, and nausea. As research advances, its complex chemical profile offers profound potential for modern neurological treatments.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Cannabaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Cannabis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a notable presence across several regions within Northern Europe. In the coastal and mainland areas of Denmark, it can be found growing in various environments. It also extends its range northward into the expansive landscapes of Finland. Moving westward, the distribution includes the island territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, the plant's reach continues through the rugged terrain of Norway.
