Hypericum veronense

Hypericum veronense, commonly known as a rare member of the St. John's wort family, is a distinctive medicinal shrub native to specific Mediterranean habitats. Valued for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in traditional applications for soothing skin irritations and supporting neurological wellness.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum veronense
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Hypericum veronense, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the Hypericaceae family and falls under the genus Hypericum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several regions within Middle Europe. Specifically, it can be found growing in the mountainous or forested areas of Austria. Its presence also extends through the territories of Czechia and Slovakia. Furthermore, the species is documented within the borders of Germany and Hungary. Finally, its geographical range reaches into the landscapes of Poland.
