Ixora undulata

Ixora undulata, commonly known as the wavy-leaved Ixora, is a captivating evergreen shrub prized for its lush foliage and vibrant blooms. Native to tropical regions, this ornamental species is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, offering potential benefits in treating various ailments through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Ixora undulata
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is a member of the Rubiaceae family. It is scientifically classified under the genus Ixora, specifically as the species Ixora undulata.
