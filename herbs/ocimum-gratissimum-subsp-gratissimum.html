Ocimum gratissimum subsp. gratissimum

Ocimum gratissimum subsp. gratissimum, commonly known as African basil or Clove basil, is a highly aromatic perennial herb. Renowned for its distinct spicy scent, it holds significant medicinal value across Africa and Asia. It is widely utilized for its potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties in traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Ocimum gratissimum subsp. gratissimum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Ocimum as the subspecies Ocimum gratissimum subsp. gratissimum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several key regions across the Atlantic and African continents. Within the Macaronesian islands, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. Moving to the mainland, it is widely distributed across West Tropical Africa. In this region, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Benin, Burkina, and Gambia. Furthermore, its range extends through the coastal territories of Ghana.
