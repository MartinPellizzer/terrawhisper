Erigeron canadensis

Canadian horseweed

Erigeron canadensis, commonly known as horseweed or fleabane, is a resilient annual herb found globally in disturbed soils. While often viewed as a persistent agricultural weed, it possesses significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various cultures have utilized its bioactive compounds for their medicinal properties, including anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects.

  • Scientific name: Erigeron canadensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
