Bauhinia forficata subsp. pruinosa

Bauhinia forficaata subsp. pruinosa, commonly known as the "pata de vaca," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South America. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to manage diabetes, inflammation, and kidney ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal medicine and biochemical research.
