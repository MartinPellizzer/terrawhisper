Ribbon-leaved water-plantain, scientifically known as Alisma gramineum, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Frequently found in wetlands and marshes, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diuretic and anti-inflammatory properties. Its delicate, grass-like foliage and white flowers make it a unique aquatic botanical.
