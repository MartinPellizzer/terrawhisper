Alisma gramineum

Ribbon-leaved water-plantain

Alisma gramineum, commonly known as plantain speedwell, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Frequently utilized in traditional medicine, this aquatic plant is valued for its ability to treat urinary tract issues and promote diuresis. Its delicate foliage and medicinal properties make it a significant botanical species.

  • Scientific name: Alisma gramineum
  • Family: Alismataceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Alismatales, it is classified under the family Alismataceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Alisma.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Alisma gramineum, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. In Traditional Chinese contexts, it is identified as 草澤瀉 (zh-hant) and similarly as 草澤瀉 (zh-hk) in Hong Kong.
