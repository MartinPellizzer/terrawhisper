Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the English daisy, is valued for its therapeutic properties which are primarily derived from its various morphological structures. The most distinctive feature of Bellis perennis is its composite flowers, which consist of white ray florets surrounding a central disc of yellow tubular florets. In herbal medicine, the medicinal potency is often concentrated within the aerial parts, which encompass the entire portion of the plant growing above the soil, including the leaves and the flowering stems.
