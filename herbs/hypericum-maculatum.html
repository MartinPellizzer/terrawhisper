Imperforate St John's-wort, scientifically known as Hypericum maculatum, is a perennial herb distinguished by its yellow, star-shaped flowers and distinctive dark spots on its leaves. Native to Europe, this resilient species is often studied for its unique chemical properties and its ecological role within diverse meadow habitats.
