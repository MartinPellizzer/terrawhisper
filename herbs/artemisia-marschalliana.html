Artemisia marschalliana

Artemisia marschalliana, commonly known as wormwood, is a resilient perennial herb native to Eurasia. Renowned for its aromatic, silvery foliage, this plant belongs to the Asteraceae family. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it possesses bioactive compounds often studied for their potential antimicrobial, digestive, and antioxidant therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia marschalliana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. It is specifically identified by its genus, Artemisia.
