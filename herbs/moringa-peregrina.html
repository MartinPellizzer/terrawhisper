Scientific literature: Sparse (54 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Wild drumstick tree, scientifically known as Moringa peregrina, is a rare, slow-growing perennial endemic to the Socotra Archipelago. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this resilient species produces nutrient-dense seeds and leaves used traditionally to treat various ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential and immense ecological value to its unique habitat.
