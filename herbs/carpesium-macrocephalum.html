Carpesium macrocephalum

Carpesium macrocephalum, commonly known as the Great-headed Carpese, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its potent therapeutic properties. Native to tropical rainforest ecosystems, this botanical species contains unique bioactive compounds used in traditional healing to treat inflammation, enhance immunity, and support overall metabolic health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Carpesium macrocephalum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further classified under the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Carpesium, specifically as Carpesium macrocephalum.
