Spatholobus sinensis, scientifically known as Spatholobus sinensis, is a significant medicinal climbing vine widely utilized in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its blood-regulating properties, the plant's stems and roots are harvested to treat various ailments, including menstrual irregularities, stasis, and pain, offering valuable therapeutic potential in herbal pharmacology.
