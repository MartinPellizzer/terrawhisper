Spatholobus sinensis

Spatholobus sinensis, commonly known as Sinomenii vine, is a significant medicinal plant in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is primarily utilized to treat rheumatoid arthritis, nerve pain, and blood stasis. This climbing woody vine offers valuable therapeutic potential in modern pharmacological research and holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Spatholobus sinensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Spatholobus, specifically as the species Spatholobus sinensis.

Common Names

This plant, Spatholobus sinensis, is known by several common names across different linguistic contexts. In Chinese, it is frequently referred to as 红血藤 (zh) and specifically as 红血藤 (zh-cn) and 紅血藤 (zh-tw) in mainland China and Taiwan, respectively. Additionally, it is also known by the name 华密花豆 (zh).
