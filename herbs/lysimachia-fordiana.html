Lysimachia fordiana

What's the taxonomical classification of Lysimachia fordiana?

Lysimachia fordiana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the taxonomic order Ericales. The plant is further categorized within the family Primulaceae, specifically under the genus Lysimachia, with its specific designation being the species fordiana.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lysimachia fordiana has erect, woody stems that reach heights of 30 to 60 centimeters and bear opposite, lanceolate leaves with entire margins. Each flower possesses five distinct petals that surround a central cluster of stamens. The plant also develops small, capsule-like fruits that contain numerous tiny seeds.
