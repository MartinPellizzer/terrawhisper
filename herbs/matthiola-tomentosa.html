Matthiola tomentosa

Matthiola tomentoosa, commonly known as velvet poppy, is a striking medicinal plant prized for its soft, hairy foliage and vibrant blooms. Often utilized in traditional wellness practices, this species offers unique phytochemical properties. Its distinctive texture and therapeutic potential make it a fascinating subject for botanical and pharmacological study.
