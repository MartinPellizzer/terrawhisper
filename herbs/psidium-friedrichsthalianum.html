Psidium friedrichsthalianum

Psidium friedrichsthalianum, commonly known as the Brazilian guava, is a significant medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal medicine. Rich in bioactive compounds like polyphenols and flavonoids, it possesses potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile species offers promising therapeutic potential for treating various digestive and inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Psidium friedrichsthalianum
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification
