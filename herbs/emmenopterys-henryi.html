Emmenopterys henryi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. This plant is further categorized into the family Rubiaceae, which defines its botanical relationships. Within this family, it is placed in the genus Emmenopterys, ultimately identifying it as the specific species henryi.
