, scientifically known as Nyssa sylvatica, is a distinctive deciduous shrub valued for its unique medicinal properties. Commonly referred to as Black Tupelo, this resilient plant offers bioactive compounds traditionally used to support respiratory health and skin vitality. Its deep pigments and nutrient-rich berries make it a botanical treasure.
