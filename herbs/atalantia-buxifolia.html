Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is documented in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. The species also extends its range to the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Additionally, the plant can be found in the Indo-China region, specifically in Laos.
