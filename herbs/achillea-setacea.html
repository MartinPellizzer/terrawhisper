Bristly yarrow, scientifically known as Achillea setacea, is a perennial herb native to North America. Distinguished by its coarse, hairy foliage and clusters of white flowers, this resilient plant thrives in sunny, open habitats. Historically valued for its healing properties, it remains a fascinating subject in botanical studies.
