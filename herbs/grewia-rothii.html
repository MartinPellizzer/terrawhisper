Grewia rothii

Grewia rothii, commonly known as the Velvet Grewia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Grewia rothii
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing in the state of Assam and throughout much of India. Its presence extends to the lush landscapes of Bangladesh and the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya. Beyond the subcontinent, the species also inhabits the Indo-China region, specifically within Myanmar. Together, these locations define the natural range of this medicinal species.
