Celastrus paniculatus

Celastrus paniculatus, commonly known as the Bittersweet Staff Vine, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Asia. Renowned in traditional practices, its leaves, bark, and roots possess significant therapeutic properties. Often utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments, this plant offers a rich source of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Celastrus paniculatus
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and order Celastrales, it is categorized under the family Celastraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification falls within the genus Celastrus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several specific regions of Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central provinces as well as the Southeast regions. Its presence is also noted on the island of Hainan within the Chinese territory. Moving towards Eastern Asia, the species is documented to grow in Taiwan. Finally, its distribution extends to the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the state of Assam.
