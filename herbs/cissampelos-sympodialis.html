Cissampelos sympodialis

Cissampelos sympodialis, commonly known as the woody vine or "Kandari," is a significant medicinal plant in traditional Ayurvedic and folk medicine. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, fever, and digestive issues, offering promising potential for modern therapeutic developments in various healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Cissampelos sympodialis
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
