Eriocephalus africanus

Eriocephalus africanus, commonly known as a vital medicinal herb in various African cultures, plays a significant role in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and infections, offering a natural approach to wellness rooted in deep ethnobotanical heritage.

  • Scientific name: Eriocephalus africanus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
