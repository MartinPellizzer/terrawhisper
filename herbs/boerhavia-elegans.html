Boerhavia elegans

Boerhavia elegans, commonly known as Punarnava in Ayurvedic traditions, is a versatile medicinal herb celebrated for its restorative properties. Renowned for its ability to "renew" the body, this plant plays a vital role in supporting kidney health, reducing inflammation, and managing edema, making it a cornerstone of herbal wellness.

  • Scientific name: Boerhavia elegans
  • Family: Nyctaginaceae
  • Native range: Africa
