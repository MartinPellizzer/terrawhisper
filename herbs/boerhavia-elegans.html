Boerhavia elegans, scientifically known as Boerhavia elegans, is a versatile medicinal herb widely recognized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Often referred to as Punarnava, this plant possesses remarkable rejuvenating properties. It is primarily utilized for its diuretic, anti-inflammatory, and hepatoprotective qualities, offering significant therapeutic benefits for kidney and liver health.
