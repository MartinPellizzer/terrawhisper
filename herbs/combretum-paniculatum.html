Combretum paniculatum

Combretum paniculatum, commonly known as the Tropical Bushwillow, is a versatile medicinal plant native to African savannas. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, various parts of this shrub are used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance in herbal healing.

  • Scientific name: Combretum paniculatum
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa
