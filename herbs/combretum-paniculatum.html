Forest flamecreeper, scientifically known as Combretum paniculatum, is a striking woody climber native to African savannas. Renowned for its vibrant, fiery orange blooms, this resilient plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts are utilized in herbal medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations.
