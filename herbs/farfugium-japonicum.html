Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans across several distinct regions. In Europe, its presence is noted specifically within the island of Sicilia. It also has a significant distribution across the Macaronesian islands, including the Azores, the Canary Islands, and Madeira. Moving toward Asia, the species is found within the South-Central regions of China. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varied subtropical and island environments.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as leopard plant, utilizes its thick, fleshy rhizome as a primary storage organ for nutrients and energy. These underground rhizomes are crucial for the plant's perennial survival, allowing it to persist through dormant seasons and spread horizontally to form dense groundcovers. In various traditional medicinal practices, the rhizome is the specific part harvested and processed to extract its bioactive compounds for therapeutic use.
