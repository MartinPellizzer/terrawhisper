Eryngium cymosum

Eryngium cymosum, commonly known as the Blue Sea Holly or African Eryngium, is a resilient medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Valued in traditional medicine, its roots and foliage are utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical importance and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Eryngium cymosum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Eryngium, which includes the species Eryngium cymosum.
