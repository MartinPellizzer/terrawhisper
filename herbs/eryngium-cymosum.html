Eryngium cymosum, scientifically known as Eryngium cymosum, is a resilient perennial herb native to Southern Africa. Often referred to as "Devil's Bit," this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It is primarily utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory and wound-healing properties, playing a vital role in various folk remedies.
