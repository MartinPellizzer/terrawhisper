Zang qie, scientifically known as Anisodus tangutiensis, is a traditional medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Tibetan Plateau. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, it is primarily utilized for its potent alkaloid content, offering significant therapeutic benefits in treating pain, inflammation, and various respiratory ailments.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several distinct regions within China. Its presence is noted in the South-Central and North-Central territories of the country. Furthermore, it can be found growing in the high-altitude areas of Qinghai and Tibet. Beyond the borders of China, its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, the species is also documented to inhabit the mountainous landscapes of Nepal.

Plant Parts

This plant, Anisodus tanguticus, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for the therapeutic compounds found within its subterranean structures. The primary medicinal component is derived from the roots, which are carefully harvested for their bioactive properties. More specifically, researchers and herbalists often focus on the hairy roots that develop during the growth process, as these structures are rich in alkaloids.
