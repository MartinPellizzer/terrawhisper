Zang qie, scientifically known as Anisodus tangutiensis, is a traditional medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Tibetan Plateau. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, it is primarily utilized for its potent alkaloid content, offering significant therapeutic benefits in treating pain, inflammation, and various respiratory ailments.
