Anisodus tanguticus

Anisodus tangutiensis, commonly known as the Tibetan bellflower, is a significant medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Tibetan Plateau. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, it contains potent alkaloids used to treat inflammation, pain, and respiratory ailments, offering valuable therapeutic properties for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Anisodus tanguticus
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Anisodus.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several distinct regions within China. Its presence is noted in the South-Central and North-Central territories of the country. Furthermore, it can be found growing in the high-altitude areas of Qinghai and Tibet. Beyond the borders of China, its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent. Specifically, the species is also documented to inhabit the mountainous landscapes of Nepal.
