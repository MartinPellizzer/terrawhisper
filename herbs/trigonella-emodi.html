Trigonella emodi

What's the taxonomical classification of Trigonella emodi?

Trigonella emodi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Fabales. This plant is a member of the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Trigonella and the species emodi.
