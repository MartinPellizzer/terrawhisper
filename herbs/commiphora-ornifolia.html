Commiphora ornifolia

Commiphora ornifolia, commonly known as the Ornate Myrrh, is a resilient shrub native to the arid regions of Africa and Arabia. Renowned for its aromatic resin, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Commiphora ornifolia
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Burseraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Commiphora.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Commiphora ornifolia, possesses a very specific and limited geographical range. Its natural habitat is primarily confined to the unique ecosystems found within Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, the species is endemic to the island of Socotra. This isolated location provides the distinct environmental conditions necessary for its survival. Consequently, the distribution of this medicinal species is highly localized to this single archipelago.
