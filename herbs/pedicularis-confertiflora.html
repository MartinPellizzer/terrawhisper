Pedicularis confertiflora

What's the taxonomical classification of Pedicularis confertiflora?

Pedicularis confertiflora belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Lamiales. It is a member of the family Orobanchaceae, which contains the genus Pedicularis, and is specifically identified by its species name, confertiflora.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pedicularis confertiflora has a basal rosette of leaves that are typically lanceolate or oblong and an upright stem bearing a dense, spike-like inflorescence of tubular, bilabiate flowers. The leaves are often deeply lobed or pinnatifid, providing a textured appearance to the lower portion of the plant. Its flowers exhibit a characteristic hooded upper lip and a broader, three-lobed lower lip, often displaying hues of pink, red, or purple. The inflorescence is notably compact, with many individual flowers clustered tightly along the upper portion of the central stalk.
