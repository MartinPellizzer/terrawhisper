Sorghum bicolor

Jowar

Sorghum bicolor, commonly known as sorghum or milo, is a versatile cereal grain celebrated for its nutritional density and resilience. Beyond its dietary importance, this robust plant holds significant medicinal potential, offering bioactive compounds with antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and anti-diabetic properties that contribute to various traditional and modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Sorghum bicolor
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, it is a member of the family Poaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Sorghum, specifically as the species Sorghum bicolor.
