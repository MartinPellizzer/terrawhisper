Distiller's acacia, scientifically known as Vachellia leucophloea, is a versatile medicinal plant native to African savannas. Renowned for its gum arabic production, it also holds significant therapeutic value. Traditionally, its bark and resin are utilized to treat various ailments, showcasing its profound importance in ethnobotanical and pharmaceutical applications.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the white-bark tree, is highly valued in traditional medicine for the various therapeutic properties found within its structure. Most notably, the medicinal compounds are concentrated in the bark, which is harvested for its intense bitter principles. Depending on the preparation method and the specific species variation, different layers of the barks may be utilized to treat ailments such as malaria, intestinal parasites, and various inflammatory conditions.
