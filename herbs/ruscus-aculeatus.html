Ruscus aculeatus

Ruscus aculeatus, commonly known as Butcher's-broom, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to European woodlands. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains saponins that support venous circulation. Traditionally used to alleviate leg heaviness and hemorrhoids, this versatile plant remains a valuable subject in modern phytotherapy and botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Ruscus aculeatus
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Ruscus aculeatus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling into the order Asparagales.
