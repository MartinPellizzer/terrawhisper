Butcher's broom, scientifically known as Ruscus aculeatus, is a resilient evergreen shrub valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its ability to support venous circulation, it is frequently utilized to alleviate symptoms of varicose veins, hemorrhoids, and leg swelling, offering a natural approach to managing vascular health and inflammation.
