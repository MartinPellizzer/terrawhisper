Butcher's broom, scientifically known as Ruscus aculeatus, is a resilient evergreen shrub valued in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its ability to support venous circulation, it is frequently utilized to alleviate symptoms of varicose veins, hemorrhoids, and leg swelling, offering a natural approach to managing vascular health and inflammation.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ruscus aculeatus, possesses a specialized morphology where its essential medicinal properties are often concentrated in its underground parts. The plant utilizes a sturdy rhizome as its primary means of spreading and storing nutrients, which functions as a complex root-rhizome system. While it possesses a traditional root structure to anchor itself and absorb moisture from the soil, the most distinctive anatomical feature is the aerial part, which consists of cladodes that mimic leaves to facilitate photosynthesis.
