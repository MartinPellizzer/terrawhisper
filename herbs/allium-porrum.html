Garden Leek, scientifically known as Allium porrum, is a versatile perennial herb belonging to the Amaryllidaceae family. Renowned for its mild, onion-like flavor, it serves as a nutritional powerhouse. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, this culinary staple offers significant medicinal benefits, supporting digestive health and boosting immune function.

Plant Parts

This plant, Allium porrum, commonly known as leek, is a versatile medicinal and culinary herb where the whole plant is often studied for its nutritional and therapeutic properties. The most prominent feature is the white bulb, which forms at the base and serves as a concentrated source of various nutrients. Emerging from this base is the pseudostem, a structural part created by the tightly overlapping leaf bases that provides upright support. The long, blade-like leaves extend upward, contributing to the plant's overall biomass and chemical profile. Additionally, the seeds produced by the plant can be utilized in specific herbal applications, ensuring that every component of the organism plays a role in its biological function.
