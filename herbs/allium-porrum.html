Allium porrum

Allium porrum, commonly known as leek, is a versatile perennial plant prized for its culinary and medicinal properties. Belonging to the Amaryllidaceae family, it is rich in antioxidants and sulfur compounds. Traditionally used to support digestion and respiratory health, this nutritious vegetable offers significant wellness benefits through regular consumption.

  • Scientific name: Allium porrum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Allium porrum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Asparagales. Finally, it is a member of the Amaryllidaceae family and falls under the genus Allium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is well-documented in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the center of the continent, it can be found growing throughout Germany in Middle Europe. Its range extends further south into Southwestern Europe, where it is commonly located in France. Finally, the distribution continues into the Mediterranean climate of Spain within the same southwestern region.
