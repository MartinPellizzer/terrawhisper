Cymbidium macrorhizon

Cymbidium macrorhizon, commonly known as the large-root orchid, is a prized medicinal plant in traditional Asian medicine. Renowned for its rejuvenating properties, its rhizomes are frequently used to nourish the lungs, soothe coughs, and enhance vitality. This resilient orchid serves as a vital botanical resource in holistic healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Cymbidium macrorhizon
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae and the genus Cymbidium.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be specifically found throughout the South-Central areas.
