Pycnanthemum torreyi, scientifically known as Pycnanthemum torreyi, is a resilient perennial herb native to North American prairies. Often called Torreya Mountain Mint, this aromatic plant is prized for its dense, white flower clusters. It serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators and offers significant therapeutic potential in herbal medicine.
