Gymnema montanum

Gymnema montanum, commonly known as "sugar destroyer," is a medicinal herb native to India. Renowned for its unique ability to temporarily suppress sweet tastes, it is widely used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to manage blood glucose levels, support metabolic health, and combat cravings, making it a powerful botanical ally.

  • Scientific name: Gymnema montanum
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Gentianaales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its classification in the genus Gymnema.
