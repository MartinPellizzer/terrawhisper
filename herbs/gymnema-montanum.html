Gymnema montanum, scientifically known as Gymnema montanum, is a medicinal climbing shrub native to the tropical forests of India. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine as "Gurmar," or "sugar destroyer," it is highly valued for its unique ability to suppress sweet tastes and help regulate blood glucose levels naturally.
