Herniaria glabra

Smooth rupturewort

Herniaria glabra, commonly known as smooth rupturewort, is a low-growing perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its diuretic and lithontriptic properties, it is frequently used to support kidney function and aid in the expulsion of bladder stones. This resilient plant thrives in various habitats across Eurasia.

  • Scientific name: Herniaria glabra
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Caryophyllales and the family Caryophyllaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Herniaria.
