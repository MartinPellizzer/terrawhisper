Delphinium oliverianum

Delphinium oliverianum, commonly known as the olive-leaf delphinium, is a rare medicinal herb found in specific high-altitude regions. Valued in traditional practices, this plant possesses unique phytochemical properties. Understanding its botanical characteristics and therapeutic potential is essential for exploring its role in natural pharmacology and holistic wellness treatments.

  • Scientific name: Delphinium oliverianum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ranunculales, it falls under the family Ranunculaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Delphinium.

Distribution

This plant, Delphinium oliverianum, exhibits a specific distribution across the region of Western Asia. It can be found growing within the borders of Iran, marking one part of its natural range. Additionally, the species is located throughout the territory of Iraq. Its presence extends further north to include the areas of Lebanon and Syria. Finally, the geographical spread of this medicinal plant reaches into Türkiye.
