Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of the Eastern Hemisphere. In mainland Asia, its presence is recorded throughout Southeast China. The species also extends to the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Moving toward the Australian continent, it can be found in Queensland and specifically on Norfolk Island. Finally, its range reaches into the Southwestern Pacific, notably inhabiting the Gilbert Islands.
