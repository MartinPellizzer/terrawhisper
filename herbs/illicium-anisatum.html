Illicium anisatum

Japanese star anise

Illicium anisatum, commonly known as Japanese star anise, is a distinctive evergreen shrub native to East Asia. While visually similar to the edible Chinese star anise, it is highly toxic if ingested. This potent medicinal plant is primarily studied for its unique bioactive compounds and significant pharmacological potential in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Illicium anisatum
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Austrobaileyales, falling into the family Schisandraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Illicium.
