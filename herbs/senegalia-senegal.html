Senegalia senegal

Senegalia senegal, commonly known as gum arabic tree, is a vital medicinal and economic plant native to arid African regions. Renowned for its drought resistance, it produces valuable exudates used in diverse industries. Traditionally, its bark and pods are utilized in herbal remedies to treat inflammation and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Senegalia senegal
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement within the genus Senegalia, specifically the species Senegalia senegal.
