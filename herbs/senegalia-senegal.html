Gum Arabic

Senegalia senegal · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (430 studies) · ★★★★★

Gum Arabic, scientifically known as Senegalia seneleg, is a versatile exudate harvested from the acacia trees of the Sahel. Renowned for its unique adhesive and stabilizing properties, this natural substance serves critical roles in food, pharmaceuticals, and cosmetics, offering a sustainable, plant-based solution for diverse industrial applications worldwide.
