, scientifically known as Dionaea muscipula, is the captivating Venus flytrap, a carnivorous plant native to the wetlands of North and South Carolina. Renowned for its rapid, snap-trap mechanism, it captures insects to supplement nutrient-poor soil, representing one of nature's most extraordinary evolutionary adaptations and botanical marvels.

Distribution

This plant, known as Dionaea muscipula, exhibits a specific and varied distribution across different regions of the United States.
