Uvaria siamensis

Uvaria siamensis, commonly known as the Siamese Uvaria, is a climbing shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional practices to treat various ailments. This plant is characterized by its distinctive foliage and small, fragrant flowers, offering significant ethnobotanical value.

  • Scientific name: Uvaria siamensis
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Uvaria siamensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Magnoliales. It is further classified into the family Annonaceae and the genus Uvaria.
