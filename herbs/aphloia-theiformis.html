Aphloia theiformis

Aphloia theiformis, commonly known as "mãe-da-lua," is a versatile evergreen shrub native to South America. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is widely used in traditional healing to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues. Its antioxidant-rich leaves offer significant therapeutic potential in modern herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Aphloia theiformis
  • Family: Aphloiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Crossosomatales and is a member of the family Aphloiaceae. Within this family, it is specifically identified by the genus Aphloia.

Common Names

This plant, Aphloia theiformis, is known by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In several Chinese dialects and classifications, including Simplified Chinese (zh-hans), Traditional Chinese (zh-hant), Cantonese (zh-hk), and Singaporean Mandarin (zh-sg), it is referred to as 单果树科 (or 單果樹科). However, specifically in Mainland China (zh-cn), it is commonly known as 茶红木.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is noted in the countries of Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern regions, it is also found within South Tropical Africa. Specifically, the species inhabits the territories of Malawi and Mozambique. Finally, its range extends into the country of Zimbabwe.
