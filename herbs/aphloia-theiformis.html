Albinoberry, scientifically known as Aphloia theiiformis, is an evergreen shrub native to the subtropical forests of New Zealand. Revered in traditional Māori medicine, this resilient plant offers significant antioxidant properties. Its leaves are frequently utilized in herbal infusions, providing wellness benefits and contributing to the rich biodiversity of ecosystems.
