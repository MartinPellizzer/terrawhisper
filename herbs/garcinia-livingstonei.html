Garcinia livingstonei

Garcinia livingstonei, commonly known as the African mangosteen, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its nutritional value and therapeutic properties, its fruits and bark are traditionally used to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and inflammation, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
