African mangosteen, scientifically known as Garcinia livingstonei, is a versatile tropical fruit tree native to West Africa. Renowned for its edible, fleshy pulp, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research.
