Bidens alba

Bidenia alba, commonly known as Blackjack, is a resilient medicinal herb native to the Americas. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healing remedies.

  • Scientific name: Bidens alba
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Bidens, specifically as the species Bidens alba.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of the world. In the Macaronesia region, it can be found specifically on the island of Madeira. Moving to Western Asia, its presence is noted in the East Aegean Islands as well as throughout the Lebanon-Syria area. Its range also extends into Eastern Asia, where it is located in the Kazan-retto region. Finally, the distribution includes the remote Ogasawara-shoto islands within the same eastern territory.
