Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and antioxidant properties, this plant serves as a natural remedy for skin ailments, wound healing, and various infections, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic healthcare practices.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Bidens, specifically as the species Bidens alba.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions of the world. In the Macaronesia region, it can be found specifically on the island of Madeira. Moving to Western Asia, its presence is noted in the East Aegean Islands as well as throughout the Lebanon-Syria area. Its range also extends into Eastern Asia, where it is located in the Kazan-retto region. Finally, the distribution includes the remote Ogasawara-shoto islands within the same eastern territory.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as White Bidens, is a medicinal herb where various components offer different therapeutic benefits. The vibrant yellow flowers are a distinctive feature, often used in traditional remedies, while the green leaves are frequently studied for their bioactive compounds. The stems provide the structural support for the plant's growth, connecting the foliage to the nutrient-absorbing roots situated underground. In many medicinal preparations, the whole herb is utilized, ensuring that the synergistic effects of the leaves, stems, flowers, and roots are captured in a single treatment.
