Paphiopedilum dianthum

Orchid

Paphiopediluum dianthum, commonly known as the Dianthus Lady Slipper, is a captivating orchid species prized for its intricate, pouch-like blooms. While primarily celebrated in horticulture for its ornamental beauty, its unique biological properties offer fascinating potential for ethnobotanical study, bridging the gap between aesthetic splendor and natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Paphiopedilum dianthum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Asparagales, it is a member of the Orchidaceae family. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Paphiopedilum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found in several distinct regions across Asia. In China, its presence is noted throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Moving into the Indo-China peninsula, the species also inhabits the country of Laos. Additionally, it can be located within the borders of Vietnam.
