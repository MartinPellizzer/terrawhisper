Hoja santa, scientifically known as Piper auritum, is a prominent medicinal plant native to the tropical Americas. Renowned for its large, heart-shaped leaves and distinct aroma, it is traditionally used to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and respiratory issues, serving as a vital cornerstone in many indigenous healing practices.
