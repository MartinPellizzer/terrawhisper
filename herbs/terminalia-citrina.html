Terminalia citrina

Terminalia citrina, commonly known as the Lemon Terminalia, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and respiratory infections, offering a natural approach to holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia citrina
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Terminalia.
