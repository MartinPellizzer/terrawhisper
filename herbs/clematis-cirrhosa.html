Clematis cirrhosa

Clematis cirrhosa, commonly known as the climbing clematis, is a resilient woody vine prized for its delicate, star-shaped blooms. Native to various temperate regions, this medicinal plant has been traditionally utilized in herbal practices to support respiratory health and soothe inflammation, offering a unique blend of beauty and healing.

  • Scientific name: Clematis cirrhosa
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Clematis cirrhosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales. It is a member of the Ranunculaceae family and falls under the genus Clematis.
