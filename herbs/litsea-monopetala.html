Litsea monopetala

Litsea monopetala, commonly known as the Indian Bay Leaf, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it possesses potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties. Widely used in traditional medicine, it helps improve digestion, supports respiratory health, and aids in managing metabolic disorders.

  • Scientific name: Litsea monopetala
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Laurales, it is a member of the Lauraceae family. Finally, its taxonomic classification is completed under the genus Litsea.
