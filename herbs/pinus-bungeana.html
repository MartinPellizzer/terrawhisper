What's the taxonomical classification of Pinus bungeana?

Pinus bungeanana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Pinidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Pinus, and its specific species, bungeanana.
