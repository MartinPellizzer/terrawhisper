Ulmus davidiana japonica, scientifically known as Ulmus davidiana var. japonica, is a resilient deciduous tree native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its bark and leaves possess significant therapeutic potential. Often utilized for their anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, this species offers valuable insights into natural healing practices.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Rosales and the family Ulmaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Ulmus as the variety japonica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across Northern Asia and the Russian territories. Within Siberia, it can be found growing in the regions of Buryatiya and Chita. Its presence extends into the Russian Far East, specifically inhabiting the Amur area. The species is also documented in the Khabarovsk region of the same territory. Finally, its range reaches the maritime reaches of the Kuril Islands.
