Salsola collina

Salsola collina, commonly known as the dune saltwort, is a resilient halophytic shrub found in arid, coastal environments. Valued in traditional medicine for its unique chemical composition, this plant offers therapeutic potential. It is primarily studied for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support various physiological healing processes.

  • Scientific name: Salsola collina
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Caryophyllales and the family Amaranthaceae. Within this hierarchy, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Salsola.
