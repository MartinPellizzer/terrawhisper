Yucca baccata, scientifically known as Yucca baccata, is a resilient perennial shrub native to the arid landscapes of North America. Renowned for its tough, sword-like leaves and creamy bell-shaped flowers, this plant offers diverse medicinal properties, traditionally used to support respiratory health and treat various inflammatory conditions.
