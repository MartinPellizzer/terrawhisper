Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, it is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory properties and to support digestive wellness.

This plant is primarily found across the Mediterranean region. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Spain. Moving across the sea, its range extends into Northern Africa.
