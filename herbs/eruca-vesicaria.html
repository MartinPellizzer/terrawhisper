Eruca vesicaria

Eruca vesicaria, commonly known as wild rocket or garden rocket, is a versatile aromatic herb belonging to the Brassicaceae family. Renowned for its peppery flavor, it serves both culinary and medicinal purposes. Rich in antioxidants and essential nutrients, this plant is traditionally used to support digestive and anti-inflammatory health.

  • Scientific name: Eruca vesicaria
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Brassicales. Finally, it is a member of the family Brassicaceae and falls under the genus Eruca.
