What's the taxonomical classification of Ardisia corymbifera?

Ardisia corymbifera belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ericales. This plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, falling specifically within the genus Ardisia, and is identified by its unique species name, corymbifera.
