Nautochilus, scientifically known as Ocimum, is a versatile genus of aromatic herbs celebrated in traditional medicine worldwide. Renowned for its diverse species, including holy basil, it offers potent antibacterial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties. These plants are essential in herbal remedies, promoting wellness through their unique therapeutic essence.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Ocimum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several European regions. In Northern Europe, its presence can be observed in Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, it is commonly found throughout Germany. The species also inhabits Southeastern Europe, specifically within the territories of Bulgaria and Romania.
