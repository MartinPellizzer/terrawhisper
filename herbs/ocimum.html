Ocimum

Ocimum, commonly known as basil, is a versatile genus of aromatic plants celebrated in traditional medicine worldwide. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is widely used to alleviate stress, boost immunity, and support digestive health, making it a cornerstone of holistic healing and culinary wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ocimum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Ocimum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse range of habitats across several European regions. In Northern Europe, its presence can be observed in Great Britain. Moving into Middle Europe, it is commonly found throughout Germany. The species also inhabits Southeastern Europe, specifically within the territories of Bulgaria and Romania. Finally, its distribution extends into Eastern Europe, covering parts of South European Russia.
