Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as basil, possesses several components that contribute to its therapeutic value. The most frequently utilized parts are the aromatic leaves, as each individual leaf contains concentrated bioactive compounds. Small, delicate flower clusters often emerge from the top of the plant, while the sturdy stem provides the structural support necessary for growth. Most importantly, the plant is highly valued for its essential oils, which are extracted from the foliage to provide its distinct scent and medicinal potency.
