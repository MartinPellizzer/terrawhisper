Arisaema tortuosum

Whipcord cobra lily

Arisaema tortuosum, commonly known as the twisted cobbler's fig, is a striking perennial herb native to the Himalayan regions. Characterized by its unique, contorted spadix and mottled leaves, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional systems for its bioactive compounds, often used to treat various ailments through careful application.

  • Scientific name: Arisaema tortuosum
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Arisaema tortuosum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Alismatales. Finally, it is a member of the family Araceae and the genus Arisaema.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is primarily found in the South-Central areas as well as in Tibet. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it inhabits the state of Assam. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout the East Himalaya region. Ultimately, the plant is commonly recorded across various parts of India.
