Persian asafoetida, scientifically known as Ferula persica, is a highly aromatic resin derived from the roots of this perennial herb. Renowned in traditional medicine for its digestive and antimicrobial properties, it offers a potent, pungent profile used globally as both a culinary spice and a therapeutic healing agent.
