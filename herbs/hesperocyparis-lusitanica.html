Hesperocyparis lusitanica

Hesperocyparis lusitaniica, commonly known as the Portuguese Cypress, is a resilient evergreen prized for its aromatic properties. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this botanical species offers unique bioactive compounds. Exploring its phytochemical profile reveals significant potential for therapeutic applications, particularly in treating respiratory ailments and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Hesperocyparis lusitanica
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, it is classified under the family Cupressaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Hesperocyparis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution that spans across several continents. In Northern Europe, its presence can be noted within Great Britain. Moving toward Southwestern Europe, the species is found throughout both Portugal and Spain. Its range extends further east into Southeastern Europe, specifically within Italy. Finally, the plant reaches Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is located in Eritrea.
