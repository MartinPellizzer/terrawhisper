Mexican cedar, scientifically known as Hesperocyparis lusitaniica, is a resilient evergreen prized for its aromatic qualities. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, its essential oils and resins offer potential therapeutic benefits, including antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant remains a significant subject in both ethnobotany and modern pharmacology.
