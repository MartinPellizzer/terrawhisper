Scientifically known as Khaya, is a genus of African trees highly valued for both timber and medicinal properties. Often referred to as African Mahogany, various species are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as malaria, skin infections, and digestive issues, showcasing their significant therapeutic importance in herbal healing.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions within West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the territories of Benin and Burkina. Its presence extends further to the nation of Gambia, highlighting its regional importance. Additionally, the species is documented within the borders of Ghana. Finally, the geographical range of Khaya includes the country of Guinea-Bissau.
