Lespedeza bicolor

Lespedeza bicolor, commonly known as the bicolor lespedeza, is a distinctive perennial legume native to North America. Characterized by its striking two-toned flowers, this medicinal plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically utilized in traditional remedies, it serves as a fascinating subject for studying natural bioactive compounds and botanical diversity.
