Ligularia hodgsonii

Ligularia hodgsonii, commonly known as the giant leopard plant, is a striking perennial prized for its massive, heart-shaped leaves. Native to moist, high-altitude regions, it holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its cooling properties and potential to treat inflammation and various digestive ailments.

  • Scientific name: Ligularia hodgsonii
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is situated in the family Asteraceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ligularia, which includes the species Ligularia hodgsonii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across the northern hemisphere. In Northern Europe, its presence is specifically noted in Sweden. The species is also found within the Russian Far East, inhabiting both the Kuril Islands and Sakhalin. Furthermore, its range extends significantly into China, covering both the South-Central and North-Central territories. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various temperate climates.
