Japanese Hop, scientifically known as Humulus scandens, is a perennial climbing vine native to East Asia. Distinguished by its serrated leaves and aromatic properties, it belongs to the Cannabaceae family. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this plant offers unique therapeutic potential, particularly for its calming and restorative botanical qualities.
