Japanese Hop

Humulus scandens · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (13 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Japanese Hop, scientifically known as Humulus scandens, is a perennial climbing vine native to East Asia. Distinguished by its serrated leaves and aromatic properties, it belongs to the Cannabaceae family. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, this plant offers unique therapeutic potential, particularly for its calming and restorative botanical qualities.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of the European continent. In Northern Europe, its presence is documented within the borders of Denmark and Sweden. Plant Parts

This plant, Humulus scandens, is characterized by its climbing growth habit, which is facilitated by its long, slender stem that utilizes specialized structures to ascend surrounding vegetation. The foliage consists of a distinct leaf structure, typically featuring serrated margins and a characteristic shape that distinguishes it from other members of the Cannabaceae family. Together, the climbing stem and the patterned leaves allow the plant to maximize its exposure to sunlight within its natural habitat.
