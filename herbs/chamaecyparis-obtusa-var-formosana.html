Chamaecyparis obtusa var. formosana

Chamaecyparis obtusa var. formosana, commonly known as Taiwan Hinoki, is a precious evergreen conifer highly valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic, antimicrobial essential oils, it is utilized to support respiratory health, soothe skin conditions, and promote relaxation through its unique, therapeutic woody essence and calming properties.

  • Scientific name: Chamaecyparis obtusa var. formosana
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is categorized under the order Pinales and the family Cupressaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Chamaecyparis as the variety formosana.

This plant is specifically native to the Eastern Asian region. Within this part of the world, its range is restricted to the island of Taiwan. This narrow geographical distribution indicates that the variety is endemic to this particular location.
