Adinandra nitida

Adinandra nitida, commonly known as the "Forest medicinal plant," is a significant evergreen species native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering promising potential for modern drug development due to its bioactive compounds.
