Digitalis davisiana

Digitalis davisiana, commonly known as Davis's foxglove, is a striking perennial native to the mountainous regions of China. Renowned for its bell-shaped, cream-to-yellow flowers, this species belongs to the Scrophulariaceae family. While containing potent cardiac glycosides, it serves as a significant subject in botanical and pharmacological studies.
