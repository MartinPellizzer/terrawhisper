Hypericum gentianoides

Hypericum gentianoides, commonly known as Gentian St. John's Wort, is a perennial herb native to mountainous regions. Distinguished by its vibrant yellow, star-shaped flowers, this botanical species belongs to the Hypericaceae family. While less famous than its relatives, it holds significant interest in traditional herbalism and ecological studies.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum gentianoides
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Malpighiales and is a member of the family Hypericaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Hypericum.
