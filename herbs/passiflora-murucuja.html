Passiflora murucuja

Passiflora murucuja, commonly known as passionfruit, is a vibrant tropical vine celebrated for its aromatic, tangy fruit. Rich in antioxidants and vitamins, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to promote relaxation, alleviate anxiety, and support digestive health, offering a natural way to enhance overall wellness and tranquility.

  • Scientific name: Passiflora murucuja
  • Family: Passifloraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Passiflora murucuja, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Passifloraceae and the genus Passiflora.
