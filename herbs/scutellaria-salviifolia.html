Scutellaria salviifolia

Scutelariia salviiifolia, commonly known as Sage-leaf Scutellaria, is a perennial herb distinguished by its aromatic, sage-like foliage. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this plant is prized in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds, which offer potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and neuroprotective properties, making it a subject of significant pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria salviifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Scutellaria. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined as Scutellaria salviiifolia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Scutellaria salviiifolia, is recognized by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as sage-leaved skullcap, a name that directly describes its distinctive foliage and botanical classification. In Spanish, it is commonly called escutelaria de hoja de salvia, and in Portuguese, it is known as escutélaria de folha de sálvia.
