Iris pseudacorus

Yellow iris

Iris pseudacorus, commonly known as yellow flag iris, is a perennial wetland plant distinguished by its vibrant golden blooms. Native to Europe and Asia, this resilient species thrives in marshes and riverbanks. Historically utilized in traditional medicine, it possesses bioactive properties, though careful handling is essential due to its toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Iris pseudacorus
  • Family: Iridaceae
  • Native range: Europe
