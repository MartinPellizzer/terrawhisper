Yellow iris, scientifically known as Iris pseudacorus, is a striking perennial native to European wetlands. Renowned for its vibrant, lemon-yellow blooms, this moisture-loving plant thrives in marshes and stream banks. While aesthetically beautiful, it contains irritating substances, requiring careful handling when used in traditional medicinal or ornamental contexts.
