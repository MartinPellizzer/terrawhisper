Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across several distinct regions. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is noted in Albania, Greece, and Italy. Furthermore, it can be found throughout the Northwestern Balkan Peninsula. Beyond the European continent, the species extends its range into West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it has been documented growing within the borders of Burundi.

Plant Parts

This plant, Rumex nepalenis var. nepalenis, is primarily utilized in traditional medicine through its leaf, which contains various bioactive compounds. The leaf of this species is characterized by its distinct shape and venation, serving as the main site for the accumulation of phytochemicals used in therapeutic applications. In many ethnobotanical practices, the leaf is processed or applied topically to harness its medicinal properties, making it the most significant part of the plant for healing purposes.
