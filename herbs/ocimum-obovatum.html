Cat's whiskers, scientifically known as Ocimum obovatum, is a versatile medicinal shrub belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its distinctive, narrow leaves and aromatic properties, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic benefits globally.
