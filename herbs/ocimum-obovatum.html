Cat's whiskers, scientifically known as Ocimum obovatum, is a versatile medicinal shrub belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its distinctive, narrow leaves and aromatic properties, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic benefits globally.

Distribution

This plant, Ocimum obovatum, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally within the borders of Benin. The species also inhabits various territories in Burkina. Furthermore, its presence is documented throughout Ghana and Guinea. Finally, this medicinal plant is distributed within the Ivory Coast.
