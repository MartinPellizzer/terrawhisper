Duchesnea chrysantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, the plant falls under the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Duchesnea and the species chrysantha.
