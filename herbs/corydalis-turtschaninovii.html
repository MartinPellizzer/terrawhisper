Corydalis turtschaninovii

Corydalis turtschaninovii, commonly known as the turtle's corydalis, is a perennial herb native to Eastern Europe. Distinguished by its delicate foliage and vibrant blooms, this species belongs to the Papaveraceae family. It is scientifically studied for its complex alkaloids, which offer significant potential in traditional and modern pharmacology.
