Zygophyllum bruguieri

Zygophyllum bruguieri, commonly known as the Desert Caltrop, is a resilient succulent native to arid African regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections and digestive issues, thanks to its unique bioactive compounds and ability to thrive in harsh environments.

  • Scientific name: Zygophyllum bruguieri
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Zygophyllales and the family Zygophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Zygophyllum, which includes the species Zygophyllum bruguieri.

Common Names

This plant, Zygophyllum bruguieir, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic context. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Bruguiere's caltrop, while in French, it is known as le caltrop de Bruguière. In Arabic, the plant is called حجر السواني (Hajar al-Sawani).

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast landscapes of Northern Africa. Specifically, its presence is noted within the borders of Algeria and Egypt. It also occupies significant territories throughout Libya and Morocco. Furthermore, the species can be found extending into the region of Western Sahara. Together, these locations define its specific geographical range across the African continent.
