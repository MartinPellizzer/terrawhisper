Alchemilla speciosa

Alchemilla speciosa, commonly known as Lady's Mantle, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its distinctive scalloped, velvety leaves, this plant is renowned for its astringent properties. It is frequently utilized in botanical remedies to support women's reproductive health and promote skin vitality through its healing essence.

  • Scientific name: Alchemilla speciosa
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Alchemilla.
