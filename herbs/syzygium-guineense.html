Syzygium guineense

Syzygium guineense, commonly known as the African waterberry, is a versatile evergreen tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned in ethnobotany, its leaves, bark, and fruits possess significant therapeutic properties. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions and skin infections.

  • Scientific name: Syzygium guineense
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Syzygium, specifically identified as the species Syzygium guineense.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin. The species is also documented in the country of Burkina. Additionally, its geographical range extends into Gambia and Ghana. Finally, this medicinal plant is also located within Guinea-Bissau.
