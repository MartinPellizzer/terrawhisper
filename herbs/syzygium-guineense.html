Water-pear, scientifically known as Syzygium guineense, is a versatile evergreen tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant—including leaves, bark, and fruit—are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as digestive issues, skin infections, and inflammatory conditions across many cultures.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin. The species is also documented in the country of Burkina. Additionally, its geographical range extends into Gambia and Ghana. Finally, this medicinal plant is also located within Guinea-Bissau.
