Melaleuca citrina, scientifically known as Melaleuca lutea, is a distinctive Australian shrub celebrated for its potent essential oils. Often referred to as Lemon-scented Tea Tree, this medicinal plant is prized in aromatherapy and traditional healing for its powerful antimicrobial, antiseptic, and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a versatile natural remedy.
