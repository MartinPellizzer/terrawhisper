Melaleuca lutea

Melaleuca citrina

Melaleuca lutea, commonly known as Lemon-scented Tea Tree, is a distinctive Australian shrub prized for its aromatic properties. Native to subtropical regions, this plant produces fragrant foliage rich in essential oils. It is widely utilized in traditional and modern medicine for its potent antimicrobial, antiseptic, and soothing therapeutic qualities.

  • Scientific name: Melaleuca lutea
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Myrtales, it falls under the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Melaleuca.
