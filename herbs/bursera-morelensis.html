Bursera morelensis

Bursera morelensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in arid regions, holds great importance in traditional ethnobotany. Native to specific dry ecosystems, this plant is utilized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Researchers study its unique chemical compounds to unlock potential applications in modern pharmacology and natural healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, Bursera morelensis, is valued for its specific medicinal components, most notably its bark and the dried exudate it produces. The bark is often utilized in traditional practices for its various therapeutic properties, while the dried exudate, a resinous substance that seeps from the stem, is collected and processed for its unique chemical constituents. Together, these parts serve as the primary sources for extracting the bioactive compounds essential to its medicinal applications.
