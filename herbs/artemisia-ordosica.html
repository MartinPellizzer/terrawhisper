Artemisia ordosica

Artemisia ordosica, commonly known as Ordos mugwort, is a resilient perennial herb native to the arid regions of Northern China. Renowned in traditional medicine, it is valued for its potent bioactive compounds. This plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various respiratory ailments through herbal decoctions.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia ordosica
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Artemisia, and its specific species name, Artemisia ordosica.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Artemisia ordosica, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Chinese, it is widely known as 油蒿 (zh), 油蒿 (zh-cn), and 油蒿 (zh-hans), while it is specifically called 黑沙蒿 (zh-tw) in Taiwan. Additionally, it is sometimes referred to as 油蒿白草 (zh).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution across specific regions of East and Central Asia. In China, it can be found growing throughout the Inner Mongolia autonomous region. Its presence is also noted across the broader China North-Central area. Furthermore, the species extends its range into the Xinjiang region of China. Finally, its habitat includes the expansive landscapes of Mongolia.
