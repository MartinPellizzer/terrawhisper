Dendrobium hercoglossum

Dendrobium hercoglossum, commonly known as the tongue-shaped dendrobium, is a captivating epiphytic orchid native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its striking, long-lasting blooms, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its purported restorative properties, specifically to support respiratory health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium hercoglossum
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales and is a member of the Orchidaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Dendrobium as Dendrobium hercoglossum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across various regions of East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Its range extends further into the Indo-China peninsula, covering significant territories in Cambodia. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Laos. These diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in multiple subtropical environments.
