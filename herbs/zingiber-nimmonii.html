Zingiber nimmonii

Zingiber nimmonii, commonly known as Nimmon's Ginger, is a rare medicinal herb native to the Himalayan foothills. Belonging to the Zingiberaceae family, this plant is prized in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds. It is primarily utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and digestive-enhancing properties in various herbal formulations.
