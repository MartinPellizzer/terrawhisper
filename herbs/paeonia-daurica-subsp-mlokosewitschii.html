Paeonia daurica subsp. mlokosewitschii

Paeonia daurica subsp. mlokosewitschii, commonly known as the Balkan Peony, is a rare medicinal perennial native to specific mountainous regions. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, this subspecies contains bioactive compounds utilized in traditional remedies to reduce inflammation, soothe nervous tension, and support overall vitality through its unique phytochemical profile.
