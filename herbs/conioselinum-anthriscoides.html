Conioselinum anthriscoides

Conioselinum anthriscoides, commonly known as the False Hemlock, is a perennial herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Native to mountainous regions, this plant is characterized by its delicate white umbels. While historically studied for its complex chemical profile, it requires careful identification due to its resemblance to toxic species.
