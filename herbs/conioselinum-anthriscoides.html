Sichuan lovage, scientifically known as Conioselinum anthriscoidum, is a vital medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional East Asian medicine. Renowned for its warming properties, it is frequently used to invigorate blood circulation, alleviate pain, and dispel stagnation, making it a cornerstone ingredient in various therapeutic herbal formulations.
