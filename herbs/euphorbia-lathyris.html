Euphorbia lathyris

Caper spurge

Euphorbia lathyris, commonly known as Caper Spurge, is a perennial plant prized for its medicinal latex. Historically utilized for treating skin conditions and various ailments, its potent sap requires careful handling. This species belongs to the Euphorbiaceae family and serves as a significant subject in traditional pharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia lathyris
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it resides in the family Euphorbiaceae and is identified by the genus Euphorbia.
