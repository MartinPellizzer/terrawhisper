What's the taxonomical classification of Euodia lepta?

Euodia lepta belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its hierarchical classification further, it falls under the order Sapindales and is a member of the family Rutaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Euodia, and its species designation, lepta.
