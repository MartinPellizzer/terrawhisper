Dune wattles, scientifically known as Acacia ligulata, is a resilient shrub native to the coastal regions of Australia. Characterized by its striking yellow blooms and spreading habit, this species plays a vital role in stabilizing sand dunes, preventing erosion, and supporting local biodiversity through its unique ecological contributions.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across both Western Asia and the Australian continent. Within Western Asia, its presence has been documented specifically in Cyprus. In Australia, the species is widely spread across several different states and territories. It can be found in the northern regions of the Northern Territory, as well as in the eastern state of Queensland. Additionally, its range extends to the southern parts of the country, including South Australia and Victoria.
