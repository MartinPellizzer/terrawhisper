Vassobia breviflora

Vassobia breviflora, commonly known as the short-flowered vassobia, is a resilient shrub native to the arid regions of South America. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant possesses unique medicinal properties. It is frequently studied for its potential bioactive compounds, offering promising insights into natural treatments for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Vassobia breviflora
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed within the genus Vassobia.
