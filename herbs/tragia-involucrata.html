Tragia involucrata

Tragia involucrata, commonly known as the "velvet leaf" or "stinging nettle" of the Fabaceae family, is a medicinal herb renowned for its therapeutic properties. Widely used in traditional Ayurvedic and folk medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing capabilities, making it a vital resource for natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Tragia involucrata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
