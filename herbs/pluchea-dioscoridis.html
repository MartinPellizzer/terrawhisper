Pluchea dioscoridis

Pluchea dioscoridis, commonly known as a versatile medicinal herb, belongs to the Asteraceae family. Found across arid regions, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. It is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural pharmacology.
