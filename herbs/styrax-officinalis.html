Styrax officinalis, scientifically known as Styrax officinalis, is a deciduous shrub prized for its aromatic resin. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, it possesses antiseptic and anti-inflammatory properties. Often found in Mediterranean climates, this plant serves as a vital component in various topical treatments and traditional healing practices worldwide.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range across several Mediterranean regions. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is noted within the borders of France. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, it can be found growing in Albania and Greece. The species also extends its distribution into Italy. Furthermore, its habitat includes the island of Kriti within the Southeastern European region.

Plant Parts

This plant, Styrax officinalis, is a medicinal shrub known for its various therapeutic properties, though the primary part used for its aromatic resin is the bark. In terms of its reproductive structures, the plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that eventually develop into a fruit containing a single seed. While the resinous exudate from the stem is the most common component used in traditional medicine, the seed serves as the fundamental unit for the plant's propagation and life cycle.
