Chinese mugwort

Artemisia argyi · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (211 studies) · ★★★★★

Chinese mugwort, scientifically known as Artemisia argyi, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is frequently used in moxibustion therapy to stimulate healing. This plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly for its anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial biological activities.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution spanning several distinct regions across Eurasia. In Southeastern Europe, its presence is documented within the borders of Romania. Its range extends into Eastern Europe, where it can be found throughout both Central European Russia and East European Russia. The species is also widely distributed across Ukraine. Finally, its geographical footprint reaches into Siberia, specifically within the Altay region.

Plant Parts

This plant, Artemisia argyi, is valued for its therapeutic properties which are derived from various botanical components. The medicinal efficacy is often concentrated in the leaves, specifically referred to as Folium in pharmacognosy. To ensure optimal potency, practitioners often focus on the young leaves, which contain higher concentrations of active compounds compared to the older growth. Therefore, preparations frequently utilize a mixture of young and mature leaves to balance the chemical profile, though the root also serves as a significant part of the plant's biological structure.
