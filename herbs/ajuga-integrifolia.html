Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Burundi and Rwanda. Its presence is also well-documented in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward Northeast Tropical Africa, the species extends its range into Eritrea. Finally, it is also known to inhabit the highlands and landscapes of Ethiopia.
