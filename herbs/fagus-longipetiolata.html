What's the taxonomical classification of Fagus longipetiolata?

Fagus longipetiolata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant further falls into the order Fagales and is a member of the family Fagaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed within the genus Fagus, specifically defined as the species longipetiolata.
