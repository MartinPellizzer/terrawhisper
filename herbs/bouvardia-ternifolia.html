Distribution

This plant is found across several distinct regions of North America. In the Southwestern United States, its presence is specifically noted in Arizona. Moving through the South-Central United States, it can be located in both New Mexico and Texas. Its range extends significantly into Mexico, where it inhabits the central regions. Finally, the species is also distributed throughout Northeastern Mexico.
