Artemisia pallens

Artemisia pallens, commonly known as pale wormwood, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean and parts of Asia. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine. It is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties, offering diverse therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia pallens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, Artemisia pallens is categorized within the genus Artemisia.
