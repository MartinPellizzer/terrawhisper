Colchicum schimperi

Colchicum schimperi, commonly known as a medicinal species within the Colchicaceae family, is a plant primarily found in East Africa. Renowned for its potent phytochemical properties, it is traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies. However, due to its high alkaloid content, careful handling is essential to avoid toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Colchicum schimperi
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Colchicaceae and the genus Colchicum.
