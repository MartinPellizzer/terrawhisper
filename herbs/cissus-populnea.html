Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Cassia populnea, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Within this part of the continent, it can be found growing in the nation of Benin. Its presence is also documented in the territory of Burkina. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into the countries of Gambia and Ghana. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes Guinea-Bissau.
