Croton flavens

Croton flavens, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical ecosystems, holds profound ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this species is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Understanding its phytochemical properties is essential for exploring its potential applications in modern pharmacology and therapeutics.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Croton.
