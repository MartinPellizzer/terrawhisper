Colchicum bivonae

Colchicum bivonae, commonly known as a rare medicinal species, is an endemic plant primarily found in the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its anti-inflammatory properties. Scientific study focuses on its alkaloid content for potential therapeutic applications in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Colchicum bivonae
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Colchicum bivonae, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Liliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Colchicaceae and the genus Colchicum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Colchicum bivonae, is recognized by various regional names across different languages. In Bulgaria, it is called турски мразовец, while in the Czech Republic, it is referred to as ocún skvrnitý. In Greece, the name used is Κολχικό του Μπιβόνα, and in Turkey, it is known as öksüzoğlan. Additionally, in Chinese, the plant is commonly identified as 杂色秋水仙.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several regions of Europe. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is noted within the island of Sardegna. Moving toward the Mediterranean and Balkan areas, it can be found in Southeastern Europe. Specifically, this species inhabits the territories of Albania, Bulgaria, and Greece. Finally, its range extends into the Southeastern European regions of Italy.
