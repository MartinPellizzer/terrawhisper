Phlomis crinita subsp. mauritanica

Phlomis criinita subsp. mauritanica, commonly known as a specialized variant of the sage family, is a resilient medicinal herb native to specific Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its unique morphological features, this subspecies is valued in traditional herbalism for its potential bioactive properties, offering therapeutic promise in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Phlomis crinita subsp. mauritanica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phlomis, specifically as the subspecies Phlomis crinita subsp. mauritaica.
