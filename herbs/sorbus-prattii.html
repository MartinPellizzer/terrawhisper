Sorbus prattii

What's the taxonomical classification of Sorbus prattii?

Sorbus prattii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Rosales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Rosaceae, falling under the genus Sorbus, and is specifically identified by the species name prattii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Sorbus prattii has pinnately compound leaves featuring 7 to 15 serrated leaflets that are elliptic to lanceolate in shape. Its woody stems are characterized by a grayish bark that becomes more textured as the plant matures. During the fruiting season, the plant produces clusters of small, berry-like pomes that transition from green to red or orange.
