Thymus alternans

Thymus alternans, commonly known as alternative thyme, is a distinctive aromatic herb native to specific Mediterranean regions. Characterized by its unique leaf morphology, this species offers potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties. It serves as a vital subject in ethnobotanical studies, highlighting the plant's diverse potential in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Thymus alternans
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Thymus.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence can be documented within the territories of Czechia and Slovakia.
