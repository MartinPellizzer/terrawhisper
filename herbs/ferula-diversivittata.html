Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast landscapes of Central Asia. Its natural habitat extends through the territory of Kazakhstan, where it can be found in specific ecological niches. Additionally, the species is documented within the borders of Kirgizstan and Turkmenistan. The range further continues into the mountainous and arid regions of Tadzhikistan. Finally, the geographical presence of this medicinal species is completed by its occurrence in Uzbekistan.
