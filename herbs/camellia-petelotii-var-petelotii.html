Camellia petelotii var. petelotii

Camellia petelotii var. petelotii, commonly known as "Chay Hu" in traditional Chinese medicine, is a vital medicinal shrub valued for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its ability to resolve phlegm, relieve coughs, and clear heat, this plant plays a significant role in treating respiratory ailments and promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Camellia petelotii var. petelotii
  • Family: Theaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
