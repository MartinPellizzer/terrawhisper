Timonius pedunculatus

Thymus pedunculatus, commonly known as pedunculate thyme, is a resilient perennial herb native to rocky, mountainous terrains. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, this plant contains essential oils with potent antimicrobial properties. Traditionally used in folk medicine, it serves as a natural remedy for respiratory ailments and digestive wellness.

  • Family: Rubiaceae
