Senega molluginifolia

Senega molluginifolia, commonly known as Senega, is a perennial herb valued for its potent medicinal properties. Traditionally utilized in herbal medicine, particularly for respiratory ailments, it serves as a powerful expectorant. This plant's roots contain bioactive compounds that help soothe coughs and clear congestion, making it a significant botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Senega molluginifolia
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygalaceae and falls under the genus Senega.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several distinct regions of South America. In Western South America, its presence is documented in both Bolivia and Ecuador. The species also extends into Brazil, where it can be found in the Southeast and South regions. Additionally, its range reaches into Southern South America, specifically within the Northeast of Argentina. Consequently, this medicinal plant occupies a diverse range of habitats across the continent.
