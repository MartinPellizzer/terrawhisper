Often used to treat digestive issues and inflammation, Coleus serves as a fascinating bridge between aesthetic gardening and natural herbal remedy.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Northern Africa, its presence is noted within the borders of Egypt. Its range extends to the Macaronesian islands, specifically including Madeira. Within West Tropical Africa, the species can be found in Benin and Burkina. Additionally, the plant is documented as growing in Ghana.
