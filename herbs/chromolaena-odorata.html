Chromolaena odorata

Bitter bush

Chromolaena odorata, commonly known as Siam weed or Christmas bush, is a vigorous perennial shrub widely distributed across tropical regions. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, it is extensively utilized in traditional medicine to accelerate wound healing, control bleeding, and treat various skin infections due to its antimicrobial qualities.

  • Scientific name: Chromolaena odorata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Chromolaena.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several regions in the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Benin and Guinea. The species is also widely distributed throughout the Ivory Coast and Nigeria. Furthermore, its presence extends into West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, it has been documented growing within the territory of Cabinda.
