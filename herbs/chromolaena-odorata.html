Bitter bush

Chromolaena odorata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (318 studies) · ★★★★★

Bitter bush, scientifically known as Chromolaena odorata, is a resilient, fast-growing shrub widely recognized for its potent medicinal properties. Primarily used in traditional medicine to accelerate wound healing and control bleeding, this plant possesses significant antibacterial and anti-inflammatory qualities, making it a vital resource in various herbal treatments.
