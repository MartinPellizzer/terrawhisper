Rhodiola chrysanthemifolia

Rhodiola chrysanthemifolia, commonly known as golden root or rose root, is a resilient perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its adaptogenic properties, this medicinal plant has been utilized in traditional medicine to enhance mental clarity, reduce fatigue, and bolster the body's natural resistance to physical stress.

  • Scientific name: Rhodiola chrysanthemifolia
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Crassulaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rhodiola.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Rhodiola chrysanthemifolia, is recognized by several different common names depending on the linguistic context and regional usage. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, its presence is documented within the South-Central provinces as well as the Qinghai region. It is also found extensively throughout the plateau of Tibet. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, the species inhabits the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya. Furthermore, its geographical range extends into the mountainous landscapes of Nepal.
