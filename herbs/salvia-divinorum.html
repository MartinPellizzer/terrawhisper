Salvia divinorum

Salvia divinorum, commonly known as divine sage, is a legendary perennial herb from Mexico. Historically revered by Mazatec shamans for its profound psychoactive properties, it has been used in sacred rituals to induce visionary states. Today, it remains a subject of intense botanical study and spiritual fascination worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Salvia divinorum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Salvia divinorum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, falling into the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Salvia.
