What's the taxonomical classification of Coelogyne punctulata?

Coelogyne punctulata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Asparagales. This plant is a member of the family Orchidaceae, placing it among the diverse orchids, and is specifically identified by the genus Coelogyne and the species punctulata.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Coelogyne punctulata has sympodial pseudobulbs that are ovoid in shape, relatively small, and bear two thin, leathery leaves at their apex. These pseudobulbs are typically spaced apart on creeping rhizomes that allow the plant to spread across its substrate. The inflorescence consists of a raceme that produces several small, fragrant flowers characterized by a distinctively spotted labellum. Each petal and sepal features a yellowish or greenish base color adorned with numerous tiny, dark purplish spots. The structure of the lip is often trilobed, providing a specialized platform for pollinators.
