Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of Asia and Europe. In Eastern Europe, its presence is noted within the Baltic States. Moving toward the Russian Far East, the species can be found in the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye regions.
