The ecology of Erythroxylum impressum is characterized by its specialized adaptation to the Savana Amazônica, a distinct transitional landscape within the broader Amazonian biome. This species thrives within these open, mosaic-like ecosystems, where environmental conditions differ significantly from the dense, closed-canopy rainforests nearby. Its distribution is specifically tied to lower-lying terrains, maintaining a mean elevational range of approximately 110 m AMSL (meters above sea level). This specific altitudinal positioning and habitat preference suggest a reliance on the unique soil compositions and light availability patterns characteristic of the Amazonian savanna, which influence its growth patterns and ecological niche.
