Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as black cumin, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, which are primarily derived from the nigella seed. These small, matte black seeds are the most significant part of the plant used in traditional medicine and culinary practices. Beyond the seeds, the plant produces delicate white or pale blue flowers and fine, feathery foliage, but it is the concentrated oils and compounds found within the seed that provide its most potent medicinal benefits.
