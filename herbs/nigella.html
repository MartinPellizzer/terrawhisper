Nigella

Nigelella, commonly known as black cumin or kalonji, is a versatile medicinal plant cherished in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, its tiny, dark seeds offer diverse health benefits, ranging from supporting digestive wellness to boosting immunity and promoting overall metabolic health across various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Nigella
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Nigella.
