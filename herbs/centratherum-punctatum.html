Centratherum punctatum

Centratherum punctatum, commonly known as Blue Dagger or Bluebell, is a perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its striking blue flowers, this plant possesses significant therapeutic potential, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. It serves as a vital resource in herbal pharmacology for treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Centratherum punctatum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Centratherum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning across different regions. In the Macaronesian islands, it can be found specifically in Madeira. Its distribution also extends significantly into West Tropical Africa. Within this African region, the species is documented in Benin, Ghana, and Nigeria. Additionally, the plant is known to grow in Togo, completing its documented presence in West Tropical Africa.
