Brazilian Buttonflower, scientifically known as Centratherum punctatum, is a resilient perennial herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant, yellow, button-like blooms, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine. It possesses significant therapeutic potential, particularly for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties used in herbal remedies.
