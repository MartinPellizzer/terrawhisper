Rumex hydrolapathum, scientifically known as Rumex hydrolapathum, is a perennial herb widely recognized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Commonly called Yellow Dock, this plant thrives in moist habitats. Its roots are highly valued for their cooling, astringent, and detoxifying properties, playing a vital role in treating various digestive and skin ailments.
