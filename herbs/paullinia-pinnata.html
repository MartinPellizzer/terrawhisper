Paullinia pinnata

Paullinia pinnata, commonly known as the climber or woody vine, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, offering valuable insights for contemporary pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Paullinia pinnata
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Sapindales and the family Sapindaceae. Finally, the medicinal species is identified by the genus Paullinia.
