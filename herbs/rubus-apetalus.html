Common Bramble, scientifically known as Rubus apetalus, is a versatile deciduous shrub celebrated for its nutritious berries. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, its leaves and fruits are valued for their antioxidant properties, supporting digestive health and reducing systemic inflammation.
