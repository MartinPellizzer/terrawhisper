Plant Parts

This plant, Rubus apetalus, commonly known as the Himalayan blackberry, offers therapeutic potential through its various components, most notably when utilizing the leaves extract. The leaves extract is often prepared through infusion or decoction to harness the plant's natural tannins and flavonoids, which are believed to possess astringent and anti-inflammatory properties.
