Rubus apetalus

Rubus apetalus, commonly known as the Himalayan raspberry, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the mountain regions of Asia. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it supports immune function and digestive health. Traditionally used to alleviate inflammation, its nutrient-rich berries offer significant therapeutic benefits in holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Rubus apetalus
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic progression places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, specifically within the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Rubus, where its species designation is Rubus apetalus.
