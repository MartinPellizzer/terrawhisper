Opuntia leucotricha

Opuntia leucotricha, commonly known as the silver prickly pear, is a resilient succulent native to arid regions. Renowned for its distinctive white, powdery coating, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Traditionally, its pads and fruits are utilized for their antioxidant properties and ability to support digestive and metabolic health.

  • Scientific name: Opuntia leucotricha
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales and the family Cactaceae, it is classified under the genus Opuntia.
