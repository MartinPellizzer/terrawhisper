Tassel Hyacinth, scientifically known as Muscari comosum, is a captivating ornamental plant prized for its unique, whimsical appearance. Characterized by striking, drooping clusters of blue flowers topped with bright orange sterile florets, it adds texture to gardens. Beyond its beauty, it holds historical significance in traditional herbal practices.
