Alpinia kwangsiensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Alpinia kwangsiensis?

Alpinia kwangsiensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further organized into the order Zingiberales and falls within the family Zingiberaceae. At the most specific levels of its classification, it is identified as part of the genus Alpinia, with its unique designation being the species kwangsiensis.

Scientific interest continues to grow regarding the bioactive properties found within this specific plant part.

According to a study published by "Zhong yao cai = Zhongyaocai = Journal of Chinese medicinal materials", the rhizome of Alpinia kwangsiensis is used for identification through microscopic characteristics. The identification involves observing the root transverse section by paraffin tissue section, free-hand section, and powder. Specific features used include the cell structure of the epidermis and endodermis, the existence of nonglandular hair, and the lignification degree of vascular bundle fiber. Researchers also examine the number and existence style of vascular bundle fiber, as well as the number and size of tube. Powder identification is further aided by the shape of starch grain and the existence of spiral vessel.
