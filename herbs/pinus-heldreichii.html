Pinus heldreichii

Pinus leucodermis

Pinus heldreichii, commonly known as the Bosnian pine, is a resilient coniferous species native to the Balkan Peninsula. Renowned for its longevity and ability to thrive in rocky, high-altitude terrains, it holds significant ecological value. Beyond its impressive stature, the species is studied for its unique biochemical properties.

  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae, it is classified under the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pinus.
