Cota palaestina

Cota palaestina, commonly known as Palestinian Cota, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its unique aromatic properties, this plant has been utilized in traditional folk medicine to treat various ailments. Its bioactive compounds offer significant therapeutic potential, making it a subject of botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Cota palaestina
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Cota.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Cota palaestiina, is recognized by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Arabic, it is referred to as كوتا فلسطينية, while in Hebrew, it is known by the names קחוון ארצישראלי and קחוון שחוור מוצים. Additionally, the names Rumian palestyński are used in both Polish and Dutch contexts to identify this medicinal species.
