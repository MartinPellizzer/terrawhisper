Palestinian Chamomile, scientifically known as Cota palaestiina, is a resilient medicinal herb native to the Eastern Mediterranean. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally used to soothe digestive issues, reduce inflammation, and alleviate anxiety. Its aromatic essence and potent phytochemicals make it a vital botanical treasure.
