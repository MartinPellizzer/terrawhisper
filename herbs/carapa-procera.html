Carapa procera

Carapa procera, commonly known as Carapa or Mahagony, is a versatile medicinal tree native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive seed oil, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and insecticidal properties. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments and infections, this plant remains a vital resource in ethnobotanical research.

  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Africa
