Plant Parts

This plant, Carapa procera, is highly valued in traditional medicine due to the therapeutic properties found throughout its various structures. The leaves are frequently utilized for their medicinal qualities, often applied or processed to treat skin ailments. The bark and the dense wood of the tree are also significant, often incorporated into decoctions or topical preparations for their healing potential. Furthermore, the seeds are a vital source of vegetable oil, which is widely extracted for its powerful anti-inflammatory and insecticidal properties. Finally, the roots serve as another important component, often used in traditional remedies to address various systemic health concerns.
