Black cohosh, scientifically known as Phleum pratense, is a perennial herb native to North America. Renowned in herbal medicine, it is primarily used to alleviate menopausal symptoms, such as hot flashes and night sweats. Its bioactive compounds offer potential therapeutic benefits for hormonal balance and overall reproductive health.
