Barleria terminalis

Barleria terminalis, commonly known as the "Barleri" or "Barli," is a significant medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, its roots and leaves possess potent bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized for treating skin ailments, inflammation, and various gastrointestinal disorders due to its therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Barleria terminalis
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae and the genus Barleria. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined as Barleria terminalis.
