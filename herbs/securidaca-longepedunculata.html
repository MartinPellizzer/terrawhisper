Securidaca longipedunculata, scientifically known as Securidaca longepedunculata, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Africa. Often called the "violet tree," it is prized in traditional medicine for treating various ailments, including inflammation, pain, and infections. Its roots and leaves contain bioactive compounds essential to diverse ethnobotanical practices.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence has been documented in the nation of Benin. Plant Parts

This plant, Securidaca longepedunculata, possesses various therapeutic components that are utilized in traditional medicine. The most significant parts used are the roots, which are often processed to extract their potent medicinal properties. Specifically, the root bark is frequently harvested for its specialized chemical constituents, while the whole root is sometimes decocted for treatment. In addition to the subterranean structures, the leaves are also employed in various herbal preparations, though the medicinal strength is most often concentrated within the Root system.
