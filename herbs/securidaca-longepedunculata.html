Securidaca longepedunculata

Securidaca longipedunculata

Securidaca longepedunculata, commonly known as the Violet Tree, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Africa. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, various parts of the plant are used in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation and pain to infections, making it a vital botanical resource.

  • Scientific name: Securidaca longepedunculata
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Fabales and placed in the family Polygalaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Securidaca.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Securidaca longepedunculata, is referred to by various common names across different linguistic and regional contexts. Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence has been documented in the nation of Benin. It is also found growing within the borders of Burkina Faso. Furthermore, the species can be located in the Gambia and Ghana. Finally, its geographical range extends into Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, Securidaca longepedunculata, possesses various therapeutic components that are utilized in traditional medicine. The most significant parts used are the roots, which are often processed to extract their potent medicinal properties. Specifically, the root bark is frequently harvested for its specialized chemical constituents, while the whole root is sometimes decocted for treatment. In addition to the subterranean structures, the leaves are also employed in various herbal preparations, though the medicinal strength is most often concentrated within the Root system.
