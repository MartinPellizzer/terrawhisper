Acalypha guatemalensis

Acalypha guatemalensis, commonly known as the "copperleaf" or "guatemalan acalypha," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its unique foliage, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant therapeutic potential in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Acalypha guatemalensis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. Its scientific identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Acalypha.
