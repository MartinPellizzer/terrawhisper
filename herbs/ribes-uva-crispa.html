Ribes uva-crispa

Ribes uva-crispa, commonly known as the gooseberry, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its nutrient-dense berries. Rich in Vitamin C, antioxidants, and dietary fiber, it supports immune function and digestive health. Historically used to treat inflammation and skin ailments, this shrub remains a staple in traditional herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Ribes uva-crispa
  • Family: Grossulariaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Ribes uva-crispa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Saxifragales. Finally, it is a member of the family Grossulariaceae and is placed in the genus Ribes.
