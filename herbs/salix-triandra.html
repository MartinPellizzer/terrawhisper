Salix triandra

Salix triandra, commonly known as the Almond-leaved Willow, is a versatile deciduous shrub native to various temperate regions. Renowned for its distinctive lanceolate leaves, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, its bark has been utilized in traditional medicine for its natural salicin content, providing potent anti-inflammatory properties.
