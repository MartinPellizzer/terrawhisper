Moraea sisyrinchium

Moraea sisyrinchium, commonly known as the Yellow Iris, is a striking perennial herb native to Southern Africa. Beyond its vibrant aesthetic appeal, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices, it is studied for its unique biochemical properties and potential therapeutic applications in holistic healing.
