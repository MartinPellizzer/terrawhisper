Poa pratensis

Smooth meadow-grass

Poa pratensis, commonly known as Kentucky bluegrass, is a widely distributed perennial grass valued globally for turfgrass production. Beyond its landscaping utility, it holds significance in traditional herbal practices. This resilient species is studied for its nutritional potential and bioactive compounds, offering interesting possibilities within the realm of ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Poa pratensis
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales. Finally, its taxonomic lineage places it in the family Poaceae and the genus Poa.
