Smyrnium olusatrum

Alexanders

Smyrnium olusaentrum, commonly known as Black Fennel, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and bioactive compounds, this plant is utilized to support digestive health, reduce inflammation, and provide antioxidant benefits, making it a significant botanical in herbal pharmacology.
