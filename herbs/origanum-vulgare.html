Origanum vulgare

Origanum vulgare, commonly known as wild marjoram or oregano, is a versatile perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it has been utilized for centuries in traditional medicine. Rich in bioactive compounds, it offers potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties essential for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Origanum vulgare
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is placed within the genus Origanum, specifically known as Origanum vulgare.
