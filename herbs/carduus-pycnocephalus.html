Italian thistle, scientifically known as Carduus pycnocephalus, is a striking perennial herb native to the Mediterranean. Recognized by its vibrant purple flower heads and spiny foliage, it holds historical significance in traditional medicine. Often studied for its bioactive compounds, this plant offers intriguing potential for modern pharmacological research.
