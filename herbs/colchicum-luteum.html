Indian colchicum, scientifically known as Colchicum luteum, is a perennial herb renowned for its potent medicinal properties. Widely used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, it contains bioactive alkaloids essential for treating various ailments. This plant is particularly valued for its therapeutic potential in managing inflammatory conditions and complex systemic disorders.
