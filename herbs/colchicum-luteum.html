Colchicum luteum

Colchicum luteum, commonly known as the yellow autumn crocus, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its striking yellow blooms, this plant contains potent alkaloids like colchicine. While medically significant for treating inflammatory conditions, it is highly toxic and must be handled with extreme caution.

  • Scientific name: Colchicum luteum
  • Family: Colchicaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Liliales, it is further categorized into the family Colchicaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established under the genus Colchicum.
