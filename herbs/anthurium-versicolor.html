Anthurium versicolor

Anthurium versicolor, commonly known as the colorful anthurium, is a striking tropical plant celebrated for its vibrant, heart-shaped spathes. While primarily prized as an ornamental wonder for its exotic beauty, it holds significant potential in traditional medicine, offering unique bioactive properties that fascinate researchers and botanical enthusiasts alike.

  • Scientific name: Anthurium versicolor
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Anthurium versicolor, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Alismatales. Finally, it is classified within the family Araceae under the genus Anthurium.
