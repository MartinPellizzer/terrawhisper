Boerhavia repens

Boerhavia repens, commonly known as Punarnava, is a versatile medicinal herb celebrated in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its profound rejuvenating properties, it is primarily used to support kidney function, reduce inflammation, and combat edema. This resilient plant offers powerful restorative benefits, promoting overall vitality and detoxification within the body.

  • Scientific name: Boerhavia repens
  • Family: Nyctaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the Nyctaginaceae family. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Boerhavia.
