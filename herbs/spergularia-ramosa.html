Spergularia ramosa

Spergularia ramosa, commonly known as branched pearlwort, is a small, herbaceous plant belonging to the Caryophyllaceae family. Often found in sandy soils or disturbed habitats, this resilient species is recognized by its delicate, branching stems. While primarily studied in botanical research, it holds potential interest in traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Spergularia ramosa
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Caryophyllaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Spergularia.
