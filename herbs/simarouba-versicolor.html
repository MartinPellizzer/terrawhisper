Simarouba versicolor, scientifically known as Simarouba versicolor, is a medicinal tree native to the tropical regions of the Americas. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly quassinoids, this species is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues, malaria, and inflammatory skin conditions.
