Simarouba versicolor

Simarouba versicolor, commonly known as Marupá, is a significant medicinal tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and analgesic properties. This versatile plant plays a crucial role in ethnobotanical practices and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Simarouba versicolor
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Simaroubaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Simarouba.
