Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical presence across several key regions in Africa. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found specifically within Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, its range extends through the territory of Cabinda. The distribution continues through Cameroon and Gabon within this same central region. Finally, its occurrence is also documented in the Democratic Republic of the Congo.
