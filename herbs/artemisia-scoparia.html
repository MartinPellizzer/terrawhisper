Artemisia scoparia

Artemisia scoparia, commonly known as broomworm sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to various arid regions. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and distinctive woody structure, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional medicine for its potential antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia scoparia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Artemisia.
