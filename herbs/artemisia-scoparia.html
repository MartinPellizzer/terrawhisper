Red-stemmed wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia scoparia, is a resilient perennial herb native to diverse terrains. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and distinct reddish stems, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally utilized in various cultures for its potent antimicrobial properties and diverse medicinal applications.
