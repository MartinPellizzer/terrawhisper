The reproduction of Tradescantia fluminenensis involves both sexual and asexual mechanisms, with asexual reproduction being a present and viable pathway for the species. When reproducing sexually, the plant features bisexual flowers that are white in color, measuring between 0.8 cm and 1.2 cm in length.
