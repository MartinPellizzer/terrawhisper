Tradescantia fluminensis

Tradescantia fluillenis, commonly known as the creeping spiderwort, is a delicate herbaceous perennial valued in traditional herbal medicine. Native to tropical regions, this plant is celebrated for its lush foliage and subtle healing properties. It is often utilized in various ethnobotanical practices to support wellness and promote vitality.

  • Scientific name: Tradescantia fluminensis
  • Family: Commelinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Commelinales and the family Commelineae, it is classified under the genus Tradescantia. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is recognized as Tradescantia flueminenis.
