Sumatra benzointree, scientifically known as Styrax benzoin, is a medicinal tree prized for its fragrant oleoresin. Extracted from the bark, benzoin is widely used in traditional medicine for its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and expectorant properties. It serves as a soothing ingredient in topical ointments and various aromatic therapeutic preparations.

Plant Parts

This plant, Styrax benzoin, is highly valued for the aromatic substances derived from its trunk, which are harvested through a process of wounding the bark. The primary medicinal and fragrant components obtained are the benzoin gum and benzoin resin. The benzoin gum is a soft, sticky substance that exudes from the tree, while the benzoin resin is the more solidified, balsamic material collected after the exudate has partially dried. Both of these plant parts are widely utilized in traditional and modern medicine for their antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and expectorant properties.
