Styrax benzoin

Styrax benzoin, commonly known as Benzoin, is a fragrant resin-producing tree prized in traditional medicine and perfumery. Extracted from its bark, this aromatic substance possesses potent antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and expectorant properties. It is widely utilized in topical salves and aromatherapy to soothe skin irritations and respiratory ailments.

  • Scientific name: Styrax benzoin
  • Family: Styracaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it falls under the family Styracaceae. Finally, it is specifically categorized within the genus Styrax.
