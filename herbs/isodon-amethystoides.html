Isodon amethystoides

Isodon amethystoides, commonly known as the Amethyst Oak, is a rare and distinctive deciduous tree native to North America. Renowned for its striking purple-tinged wood and unique bark, this botanical treasure holds significant interest in ethnobotany. Its specialized ecological niche makes it a subject of vital conservation study.

  • Scientific name: Isodon amethystoides
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Isodon.
