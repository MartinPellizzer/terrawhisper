Tropical Sensitive Pea, scientifically known as Chamaecrista absus, is a versatile legume native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and seeds possess diuretic, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This resilient plant is frequently utilized in herbal remedies to treat various ailments, highlighting its significant ethnobotanical importance worldwide.
