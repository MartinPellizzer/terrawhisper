Chamaecrista absus

Chamaecrista absus, commonly known as sicklepod, is a versatile medicinal legume found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. This plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and natural remedies.

  • Scientific name: Chamaecrista absus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Fabales and is a member of the Fabaceae family. Finally, its taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Chamaecrista.
