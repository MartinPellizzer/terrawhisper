Asparagus filicinus

Asparagus filicinus, commonly known as the ferny asparagus, is a resilient climbing plant prized for its delicate, foliage-like stems. While primarily ornamental, it holds historical significance in traditional herbalism. This study explores its botanical characteristics, ecological role, and potential medicinal applications, highlighting its unique bioactive properties and importance.

  • Scientific name: Asparagus filicinus
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic details place it in the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Asparagales, and the family Asparagaceae. It is ultimately identified by the genus Asparagus.
