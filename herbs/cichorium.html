Chicory, scientifically known as Cicorium, is a resilient perennial herb cherished for its multifaceted medicinal and culinary properties. Renowned for supporting digestive health and liver function, it contains valuable inulin. From its vibrant blue flowers to its bitter roots, this plant offers profound benefits in traditional herbal wellness.
