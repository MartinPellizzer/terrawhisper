Gymnosporia senegalensis

Maytenus senegalensis

Gymnosporia senelegensis, commonly known as the Senegal Gymnosporia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to African savannas. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of this plant are traditionally utilized to treat skin ailments, gastrointestinal issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Gymnosporia senealensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. Finally, its taxonomic classification concludes with the genus Gymnosporia.
