Derris robusta belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. According to a study published by "Phytochemistry", the twig extract of Derris robusta (DC.) Benth. was used to isolate twelve isoflavones. These compounds included three previously undescribed isoflavones, derrisrobustones A-C, and one previously undescribed natural isoflavone, derrisrubsone D, along with eight known isoflavones. What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause severe rotenone toxicity, which manifests through symptoms such as nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, dizziness, headache, muscle weakness, hypotension, respiratory distress, and convulsions. Exposure to the chemical compounds within the plant disrupts cellular respiration, leading to significant physiological distress. These toxic effects can range from mild gastrointestinal discomfort to life-threatening neurological and respiratory failure. Because the rotenone acts as a potent metabolic poison, the onset of these symptoms can occur rapidly following ingestion or skin contact. Monitoring for these specific signs is critical when accidental exposure to the plant is suspected.
