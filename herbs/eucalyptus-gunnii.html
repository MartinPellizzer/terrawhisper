Eucalyptus gunnii

Eucalyptus gunnii, commonly known as Snow Gum, is a resilient evergreen native to high-altitude Australian regions. Highly valued for its therapeutic properties, its aromatic leaves are rich in cineole. This essential oil is widely utilized in traditional and modern medicine to treat respiratory congestion and alleviate inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Eucalyptus gunnii
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Eucalyptus.
