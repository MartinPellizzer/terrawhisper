Cinnamomum bejolghota

Cinnamomum bejolghota, commonly known as the wild cinnamon or Beyal, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South Asia. Renowned for its aromatic bark and leaves, it possesses potent antimicrobial, antioxidant, and anti-inflammatory properties. It is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic medicine to treat digestive issues and various skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Cinnamomum bejolghota
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Cinnamomum bejolghota, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Cinnamomum.
