Pepper, scientifically known as Capsicum, is a versatile genus of flowering plants prized for their pungent fruit. Beyond culinary uses, Capsicum offers significant medicinal benefits. Rich in vitamins and capsaicin, it serves as a powerful anti-inflammatory agent, effective in managing pain relief, enhancing circulation, and supporting digestive health.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Capsicum, is highly valued for its medicinal properties, which are primarily concentrated within its specialized structures. The most significant part used is the fruit, though various species produce different types of fruits ranging from sweet to intensely spicy. The characteristic heat of these pepper fruits is derived from specific pungent ingredients, most notably capsaicin, which serves as a powerful anti-inflammatory agent. Interestingly, the highest concentration of these active compounds is often found within the placenta tissues, which are the internal structures connected to the seeds that deliver the intense spicy sensation.
