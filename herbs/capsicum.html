Pepper, scientifically known as Capsicum, is a versatile genus of flowering plants prized for their pungent fruit. Beyond culinary uses, Capsicum offers significant medicinal benefits. Rich in vitamins and capsaicin, it serves as a powerful anti-inflammatory agent, effective in managing pain relief, enhancing circulation, and supporting digestive health.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Ultimately, the medicinal plant is identified by the genus Capsicum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse presence across several European regions. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the center of the continent, its range extends into Middle Europe. Specifically, it is distributed throughout Austria and the region of Czechia-Slovakia. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the country of Germany.
