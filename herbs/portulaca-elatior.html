Portulaca elatior

Portulaca oleracea, commonly known as purslane, is a resilient succulent valued for its remarkable nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in omega-3 fatty acids, antioxidants, and essential vitamins, this versatile plant offers significant anti-inflammatory and healing benefits. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to support skin health and digestion.

  • Scientific name: Portulaca elatior
  • Family: Portulacaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Caryophyllales and the family Portulacaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Portulaca.
