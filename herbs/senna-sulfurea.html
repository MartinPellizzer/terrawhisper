Smooth Senna, scientifically known as Senna sulfurea, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent laxative properties, it contains bioactive compounds like sennosides. Beyond digestive health, this plant is traditionally valued in herbal medicine for its antioxidant benefits and its role in supporting detoxification.
