Chelonopsis lichiangensis

Chelonopsis lichiangensis, commonly known as the Golden Shell Fern, is a rare medicinal plant endemic to high-altitude tropical forests. Renowned for its unique antioxidant properties, it is traditionally used in indigenous practices to alleviate inflammation and support respiratory health, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Chelonopsis lichiangensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Chelonopsis lichiangensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic position is defined by its placement within the genus Chelonopsis.
