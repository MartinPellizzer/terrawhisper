Pinus thunbergii

Pinus thuijbergii, commonly known as the Japanese Black Pine, is a resilient coniferous species prized for its medicinal properties. Historically utilized in traditional practices, its bark and resin are valued for their antiseptic and anti-inflammatory qualities. This hardy evergreen serves as a vital resource in botanical healing traditions.
