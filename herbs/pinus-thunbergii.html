Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across multiple continents and regions. In Eastern Asia, it is naturally found within the countries of Japan and Korea. Interestingly, its presence extends to North America, where it can be located in the Northeastern United States, specifically in New York. Additionally, it is also documented in the Southeastern United States, notably in Maryland. These diverse locations demonstrate the wide environmental range inhabited by this medicinal species.
