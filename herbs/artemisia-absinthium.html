Artemisia absinthium

Wormwood

Artemisia absinthium, commonly known as wormwood, is a perennial herb celebrated for its intense bitterness and potent medicinal properties. Historically utilized to treat digestive ailments and intestinal parasites, this aromatic plant contains thujone. Today, it remains a significant botanical interest in both traditional herbalism and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia absinthium
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Artemisia, with the species name absinthium.

When preparing medicinal extracts or tinctures, the entire herb is often utilized to ensure a full spectrum of bioactive compounds.
