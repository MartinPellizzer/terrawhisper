Wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia absinthium, is a perennial herb celebrated for its potent medicinal properties and distinctively bitter flavor. Renowned for its ability to support digestive health and expel intestinal parasites, this aromatic plant has played a significant role in traditional herbalism and historical spirit production for centuries.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as wormwood, is primarily valued for its medicinal properties found within its aerial parts. The most significant therapeutic components are concentrated in the aromatic leaves, which are characterized by their silvery-green color and bitter taste. When preparing medicinal extracts or tinctures, the entire herb is often utilized to ensure a full spectrum of bioactive compounds. Furthermore, the plant is a rich source of essential oil, which contains the potent volatile compounds responsible for its distinct fragrance and pharmacological effects.
