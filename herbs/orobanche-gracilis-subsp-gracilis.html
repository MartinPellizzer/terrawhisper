Orobanche gracilis subsp. gracilis

Orobanche gracilis subsp. gracilis, commonly known as the slender broomrape, is a specialized parasitic plant lacking chlorophyll. Lacking leaves, it survives by extracting nutrients directly from host plant roots. While primarily studied in botanical research, its unique biological mechanisms offer fascinating insights into parasitic interactions within medicinal ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Orobanche gracilis subsp. gracilis
  • Family: Orobanchaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Orobanchaceae and the genus Orobanche. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies gracilis within the species Orobanche gracilis.

Distribution

This plant is a parasitic species primarily found across several regions of Middle Europe. Its distribution includes specific territories such as Austria and Germany. It can also be located within the borders of Czechia and Slovakia. Furthermore, the species is present in Hungary, contributing to its regional presence. Finally, its geographical range extends into the mountainous landscapes of Switzerland.
