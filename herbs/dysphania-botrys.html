Dysphania botrys

Dysphania botrys, commonly known as the grape-like dysphania, is a versatile medicinal herb belonging to the Acanthaceae family. Frequently found in tropical regions, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, offering diverse therapeutic benefits for various skin and digestive ailments.
