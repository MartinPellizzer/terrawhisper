Plant Parts

This plant, Levisticum officinale, commonly known as lovage, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components offer therapeutic benefits. The thick, aromatic roots are frequently utilized in traditional medicine for their digestive and diuretic properties. The large, serrated green leaves are not only edible but also contain bioactive compounds used to support kidney function. Additionally, the plant produces small, ribbed fruits that can be harvested for their concentrated potency. Most notably, the essential oil extracted from the plant is highly valued for its intense aroma and its ability to serve as a powerful antimicrobial and carminative agent in herbal preparations.
