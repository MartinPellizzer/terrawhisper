Callicarpa nudiflora

Callicarpa nudiflora, commonly known as a member of the beautyberry family, is a distinctive medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique foliage and berry clusters, this plant offers significant pharmacological potential, particularly for its bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments in diverse herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Callicarpa nudiflora
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Callicarpa. Specifically, it is identified as the species Callicarpa nudiflora.
