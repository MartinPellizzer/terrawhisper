Hypericum linariifolium

Hypericum linariifolium, commonly known as the flax-leaved St. John's wort, is a distinctive perennial herb native to specific Mediterranean regions. Characterized by its slender, lanceolate leaves and vibrant yellow blossoms, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique bioactive compounds and potential therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum linariifolium
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Hypericaceae. It is further identified by the genus Hypericum, specifically as the species Hypericum linariifolium.
