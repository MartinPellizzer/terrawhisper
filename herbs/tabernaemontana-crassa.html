Tabernaemontana crassa

Tabernaemontana crassa, commonly known as a member of the Apocynaceae family, is a significant medicinal shrub valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its bioactive alkaloids, it is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and gastrointestinal issues. Exploring its phytochemical properties offers promising insights into modern pharmacological developments.

  • Family: Apocynaceae

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Specifically, its presence has been documented within the borders of Ghana. It is also found growing in the coastal and forest areas of Ivory Coast. Furthermore, the species extends into the territories of Liberia and Sierra Leone. Finally, its geographical range includes the large nation of Nigeria.
