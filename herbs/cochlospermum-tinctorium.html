Cochlospermum tinctorium

Cochlospermum tinctorium, commonly known as the Yellow Silk Cotton, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, it possesses significant therapeutic properties. Traditionally used in folk medicine, its various parts offer potential applications in treating inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Cochlospermum tinctorium
  • Family: Bixaceae
  • Native range: Africa
