Guaiacum officinale

Roughbark lignum vitae

Guaiacum officinale, commonly known as Guaiacum or Lignum Vitae, is a renowned medicinal tree native to the Caribbean. Historically prized for its resin, it has been utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, rheumatism, and inflammatory conditions, offering potent antioxidant and antimicrobial properties essential to holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Guaiacum officinale
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Zygophyllales, falling within the family Zygophyllaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Guaiacum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Guaiacum officinale, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide and scattered geographical distribution across several oceanic and continental regions. Within the Middle Atlantic Ocean, its presence can be noted on the remote island of Ascension. Moving toward Asia, it is found within the Indo-China region, specifically in Vietnam. Its Pacific range extends from the Mariana Islands in the Northwestern Pacific to the islands of Hawaii in the North-Central Pacific. Finally, its reach includes the Southeastern United States, where it grows in Florida.
