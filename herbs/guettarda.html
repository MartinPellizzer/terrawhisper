Guettarda

Guettarda, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb in various traditional healing practices, holds significant importance in natural pharmacology. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and skin ailments, offering a natural approach to wellness through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Guettarda
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, it is situated in the family Rubiaceae. Its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Guettarda.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide range of occurrence across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Kenya and Tanzania. Its presence extends into South Tropical Africa, where it is specifically noted in Mozambique. Moving further south, the species is also distributed throughout the KwaZulu-Natal region of Southern Africa. Additionally, it inhabits the Western Indian Ocean, specifically on the island of Aldabra.
