Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide range of occurrence across several distinct tropical and subtropical regions. In East Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Kenya and Tanzania. Its presence extends into South Tropical Africa, where it is specifically noted in Mozambique.
