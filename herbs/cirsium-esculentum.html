Cirsium esculentum

Cirsium esculentum, commonly known as edible thistle, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its nutritional and therapeutic properties. Found in various wild habitats, its roots and leaves offer significant dietary benefits. Traditionally used in folk medicine, it serves as a natural remedy, supporting overall wellness through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Cirsium esculentum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae, falling under the genus Cirsium.
