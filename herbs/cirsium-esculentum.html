Ground thistle, scientifically known as Cirsium esculentum, is a resilient perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned for its nutritional and medicinal properties, this plant features distinctive prickly leaves and vibrant purple flowers. Traditionally used in various cultures, it offers significant antioxidant benefits and serves as a valuable dietary staple.
