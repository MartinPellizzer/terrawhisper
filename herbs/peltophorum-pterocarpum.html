Peltophorum pterocarpum

Yellow ponciana

Peltophorum pterocarpum, commonly known as the Yellow Flamboyant or Copperpod, is a majestic tropical tree valued for its medicinal properties. Traditionally used in various cultures, its bark, leaves, and flowers possess antioxidant and anti-inflammatory qualities, offering potential therapeutic benefits for treating skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Peltophorum pterocarpum
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa
