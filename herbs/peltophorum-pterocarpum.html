Distribution

This plant is naturally distributed across several regions within West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence has been documented in the nation of Burkina. It is also found spreading through the coastal and inland areas of Gambia. Furthermore, the species inhabits significant portions of Ghana and Guinea. Finally, its geographical range extends into the territories of Liberia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Yellow Flamboyant, utilizes various components for its distinct characteristics and medicinal potential. Its bipinnate leaves provide a lush, feathery canopy that aids in photosynthesis and contributes to the plant's shade-providing properties. The bright, showy yellow flowers are a hallmark of the species, attracting pollinators and serving as a key visual indicator of its blooming season. Once pollinated, the plant produces flattened, woody pods that house the seeds, facilitating its reproductive cycle. Additionally, the dense wood of the tree is notable for its durability and has historically been studied for its structural and potential bioactive qualities.
