Tempsky's Sand Crocus, scientifically known as Romulea tempskyana, is a striking perennial native to New Zealand. Renowned for its vibrant purple blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used by Māori for its medicinal properties, it is often applied topically to treat skin irritations, wounds, and inflammation.
