Romulea tempskyana

Romulea tempskyana, commonly known as the New Zealand Rock Lily, is a native bulbous perennial prized for its medicinal properties. Traditionally used by Māori, this plant contains bioactive compounds thought to possess anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial qualities. It remains a fascinating subject of study for natural pharmacology and ethnobotanical research.
