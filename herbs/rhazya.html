Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Rhazyia pinnata, is a medicinal shrub where the root serves as a primary component for pharmacological applications. The root of the plant contains various bioactive alkaloids that are extracted for their therapeutic properties, often used in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and fever. Because the medicinal potency is concentrated within this underground structure, careful harvesting of the root is essential for ensuring the quality and efficacy of its derivative treatments.
