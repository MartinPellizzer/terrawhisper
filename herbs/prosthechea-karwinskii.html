Prosthechea karwinskii

Prosthechea karwinskii, commonly known as the Karwin's Walking Dendrobium, is a fascinating orchid native to Central America. While primarily celebrated by botanists and horticulturists for its resilient nature and striking blooms, it holds significant place in traditional ethnobotany, where various orchid species are explored for their unique medicinal properties.
