Plant Parts

This plant, Prosthechea karwinskii, is a miniature epiphytic orchid characterized by a distinct morphological structure. It features thickened, fleshy pseudobulbs that serve as vital storage organs for water and nutrients, helping the plant survive periods of drought. From these pseudobulbs, several narrow, lance-shaped leaves emerge, providing the necessary surface area for photosynthesis. During its blooming period, the orchid produces delicate, fragrant flowers that are typically small and white with subtle yellowish or greenish tints, making it a prized specimen for collectors.
