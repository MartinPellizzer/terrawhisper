What's the taxonomical classification of Euonymus yunnanensis?

Euonymus yunnanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic hierarchy continues through the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae, eventually leading to its placement within the genus Euonymus. Finally, its specific identity is defined by its species designation, yunnanensis.
