Symphoricarpus orbiculatus, scientifically known as Symphoricarpus orbiculatus, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to North America. Often called Coralberry, it features striking, cluster-like red fruits. While primarily ornamental, its berries have historical significance in folk medicine, traditionally utilized for their potential tonic and restorative properties in various cultures.
