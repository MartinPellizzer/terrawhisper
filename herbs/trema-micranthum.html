Trema micranthum

Trema micrantum, commonly known as the "Small-flowered Tremma," is a significant medicinal plant found in various tropical regions. Valued in traditional healing practices, it possesses unique bioactive properties. Research suggests its leaves and extracts may offer therapeutic benefits, particularly for their antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial potential in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Trema micranthum
  • Family: Cannabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
