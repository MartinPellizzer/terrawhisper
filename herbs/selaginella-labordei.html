Selaginella labordei

Selaginella labordei, commonly known as a species of spike moss, is a fascinating medicinal plant valued in traditional herbal practices. Often found in moist, shaded habitats, it possesses unique bioactive properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its potential pharmacological applications, particularly for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and various therapeutic benefits.
