Coleus neochilus

Coleus neochilus, commonly known as the Indian Coleus, is a versatile medicinal herb celebrated in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat respiratory ailments, skin disorders, and digestive issues, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Coleus neochilus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Coleus.

Common Names

This plant, Coleus neochilus, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Plectranthus or the Coleus. In Spanish, it is frequently called mentha de monte or menta de monte. For those speaking Hindi, it is known as Kapur Kachri, while in Telugu, it is referred to as Kanchu.

This plant is found across several distinct regions within the African continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Mozambique, Zambia, and Zimbabwe.
