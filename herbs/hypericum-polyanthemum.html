Hypericum polyanthemum

Hypericum polyanthemum, commonly known as a unique member of the St. John's wort family, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to support neurological health, reduce inflammation, and promote skin healing and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum polyanthemum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and order Malpighiales, it is a member of the family Hypericaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Hypericum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Hypericum polyanthemum, is referred to by various names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is commonly called St. John's wort, a name shared with many members of its genus. In Spanish, it is known as Hierba de San Juan, reflecting its traditional medicinal uses. In Portuguese, the plant is referred to as Erva de São João, while in French, it is called Millepertuis.
