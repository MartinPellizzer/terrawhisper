Vernonia blanda

What's the taxonomical classification of Vernonia blanda?

Vernonia blanda belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is situated under the order Asterales. It is a prominent representative of the family Asteraceae, a large group of flowering plants, and is specifically categorized under the genus Vernonia. The scientific name of this particular species is completed by its specific epithet, blanda.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Vernonia blanda has erect, unbranched or sparingly branched stems that reach heights of 0.5 to 2 meters and are topped with numerous discoid flower heads. The leaves are primarily lanceolate to oblong in shape, featuring entire margins and an alternate arrangement along the stem. These foliage structures are typically dark green and possess a somewhat leathery texture. Each inflorescence consists of small, tubular purple or violet florets clustered tightly together. The plant also produces a specialized involucral bract that surrounds the base of each flower head.
