Pulsatilla cernua var. koreana

Pulsatilla cerulea var. koreanan, commonly known as Korean Pasque Flower, is a rare medicinal herb native to East Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory and sedative properties. This delicate perennial is prized for its unique bioactive compounds, used to treat various neurological and skin conditions.
