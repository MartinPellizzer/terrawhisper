Nicotiana alata

Nicotiana alata, commonly known as Flowering Tobacco, is a captivating annual plant celebrated for its fragrant, trumpet-shaped blossoms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant botanical importance. Traditionally studied for its chemical properties, it serves as a vital subject in pharmacological research regarding its diverse bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Nicotiana alata
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Nicotiana and the species N. alata.
