Siparuna thecaphora

Siparuna thecaphora, commonly known as Siparu, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This botanical powerhouse offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Siparuna thecaphora
  • Family: Siparunaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Siparuna thecaphora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Laurales. Finally, it is a member of the family Siparunaceae within the genus Siparuna.

Common Names

This plant, Siparuna thecaphora, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the False Cacao. Within its native habitats, it is known as Siparuana in Indonesian and as Siparuina in Malay. Additionally, it is recognized by the name Siparuana in the local vernacular of certain areas.

This plant, Siparuna thecaphora, exhibits a wide geographical presence across various regions of Mexico. It can be found growing throughout the central part of the country in Mexico Central.
