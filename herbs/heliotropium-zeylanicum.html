Heliotropium zeylanicum

Heliotropium zeylanicum, commonly known as Ceylon Heliotrope, is a significant medicinal herb native to South Asia. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, it possesses potent therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory and analgesic effects. This versatile plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, offering valuable insights into natural pharmacological healing.

Distribution
