Centaurea parviflora

Centaurea parviflora, commonly known as small cornflower, is a delicate medicinal herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Found primarily in Mediterranean climates, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its diverse therapeutic properties, including its potential anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects, used to support various holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Centaurea parviflora
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Centaurea parviflora, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Asterales. Finally, it is classified under the family Asteraceae within the genus Centaurea.
