Scleropyrum pentandrum

Scleropyrum pentandrum, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, holds immense value in traditional ethnobotany. Native to specific tropical regions, this plant is prized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Researchers study its bioactive compounds to unlock potential treatments for various ailments, highlighting its vital role in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Scleropyrum pentandrum
  • Family: Santalaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Santalales, and the family Santalaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Scleropyrum, specifically as the species Scleropyrum pentaandrum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence extends significantly into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is native to India. Additionally, the species is recorded within the borders of Sri Lanka. Together, these locations highlight the plant's diverse habitat across both mainland China and South Asia.
