Moringa's, scientifically known as Moringa oleifera, is a nutritional powerhouse often called the "miracle tree." Renowned for its exceptional density of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants, every part of this plant is edible. It serves as a vital superfood, offering potent anti-inflammatory properties and significant health benefits for holistic wellness.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Brassicales, it is classified under the family Moringaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Moringa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse presence across several distinct regions in Africa. In Northern Africa, it is found within the borders of both Egypt and Libya. Its distribution extends to the Macaronesia region, specifically in Cape Verde. Furthermore, the plant is established in West Tropical Africa, including the nation of Benin. Finally, its geographical range encompasses the landlocked country of Burkina Faso.
