Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse presence across several distinct regions in Africa. In Northern Africa, it is found within the borders of both Egypt and Libya. Its distribution extends to the Macaronesia region, specifically in Cape Verde. Furthermore, the plant is established in West Tropical Africa, including the nation of Benin. Finally, its geographical range encompasses the landlocked country of Burkina Faso.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Moringa, is a nutritional powerhouse where almost every component offers unique health benefits. The nutrient-dense leaves are perhaps the most widely consumed part, often dried and processed into tea leaves to create a potent, antioxidant-rich infusion. Each individual leaf serves as a concentrated source of vitamins and minerals, making them a staple in medicinal diets. Beyond the foliage, the delicate white flowers are edible and can be used to enhance the flavor of various dishes, while the seeds provide essential oils and can even be used for water purification.
