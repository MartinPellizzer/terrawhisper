Eugenia astringens

Eugenia astringens, commonly known as Cambará, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South American forests. Renowned for its potent astringent and antioxidant properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin inflammations, digestive issues, and wounds. This botanical powerhouse offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Eugenia astringens
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtales, and the family Myrtaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Eugenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical presence across several key regions of Brazil. Specifically, its natural range extends into the diverse landscapes of the Brazil Northeast. Furthermore, the species can be found throughout the fertile areas of the Brazil Southeast. It is also widely distributed across the temperate climates of the Brazil South. Consequently, these three distinct regions represent the primary locations where this medicinal species thrives.
