Plant Parts

This plant, known as Eugenia astringens, is highly valued in traditional medicine for the specific therapeutic properties found within its fruits. These small, fleshy berries are often harvested and processed to utilize their high concentration of tannins and bioactive compounds. When prepared as decoctions or extracts, the fruits serve as a key medicinal component, particularly for their astringent qualities which are used to treat various gastrointestinal issues and inflammatory conditions.
