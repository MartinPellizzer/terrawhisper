Maytenus spinosa

Maytenus spinosa, commonly known as the "maguey" or "espinilla" in various regional dialects, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to parts of South America. Renowned for its bioactive properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Maytenus spinosa
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Celastrales, it falls under the family Celastraceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Maytenus.
