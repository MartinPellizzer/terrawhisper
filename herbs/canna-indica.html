Canna indica

Canna indica, commonly known as Indian Shot, is a vibrant perennial valued for its striking tropical blooms and therapeutic potential. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant is traditionally used in various cultures to treat inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues, offering a rich intersection of beauty and medicinal utility.

  • Scientific name: Canna indica
  • Family: Cannaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Zingiberales and is a member of the Cannaceae family. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Cannna.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing in Austria.
