Indian shot

Canna indica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (235 studies) · ★★★★★

Indian shot, scientifically known as Canna indica, is a versatile perennial valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this plant offers therapeutic properties, particularly for treating inflammation, digestive issues, and kidney ailments. Its rhizomes are frequently utilized in various herbal remedies to promote holistic healing and wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Canna indica, is a versatile medicinal species characterized by several distinct anatomical features. The most significant part utilized for therapeutic purposes is the rhizome, a thick, fleshy underground stem that stores nutrients and contains many of the plant's active compounds. These interconnected rhizomes are often processed into powders or extracts to treat various ailments. Above the ground, the plant displays large, prominent leaves that are broad and lanceolate, contributing to the plant's lush appearance while also playing a role in its overall biological vitality.
