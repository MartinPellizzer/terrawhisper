Yellow Hedge Barleria, scientifically known as Barleria prionitis, is a deciduous shrub native to the Indian subcontinent. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this plant features vibrant yellow flowers and versatile medicinal properties. Its leaves, roots, and bark are widely utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various digestive disorders.
