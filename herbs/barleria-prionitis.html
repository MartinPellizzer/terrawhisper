Barleria prionitis

Yellow hedge barleria

Barleria prionitis, commonly known as Vajradanti in Ayurveda, is a versatile medicinal shrub prized for its therapeutic properties. Renowned for its significant dental benefits, this plant is extensively used to treat gum diseases, toothaches, and inflammation. Its roots and leaves offer potent bioactive compounds essential in traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Barleria prionitis
  • Family: Acanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Acanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Barleria, specifically as the species Barleria prionitis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several distinct tropical regions. In Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Eritrea and Ethiopia. Its presence extends significantly into the Western Indian Ocean islands as well. Specifically, it is documented in the diverse landscapes of Madagascar and Mauritius. Additionally, the species is also recorded in the territory of Réunion.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Barleria prionitis, utilizes its leaves as a significant component in traditional medicinal practices. The leaves are typically lanceolate in shape and are frequently employed in various herbal formulations to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and even certain types of fever. When processed, these leaves can be used in topical applications or decoctions, showcasing the diverse therapeutic potential held within the plant's foliage.
