Vismia baccifera

Vismia baccifera, commonly known as "Pau de mico" in Brazil, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical forests. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat skin inflammations, wounds, and digestive ailments. Modern research continues to explore its potent antioxidant and antimicrobial potential.

  • Scientific name: Vismia baccifera
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, specifically within the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Vismia.
