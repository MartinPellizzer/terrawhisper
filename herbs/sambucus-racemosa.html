Sambucus racemosa

Red-berried elder

Sambucus racemosa, commonly known as Red Elderberry, is a deciduous shrub native to North America. Highly valued in traditional herbalism, its berries and flowers possess potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. While medicinal, careful preparation is essential, as raw components contain compounds that require processing to ensure safe consumption.

  • Scientific name: Sambucus racemosa
  • Family: Viburnaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Dipsacales and the family Viburnaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Sambucus.
