Atlas cedar, scientifically known as Cedrus atlantica, is a majestic evergreen conifer native to the Atlas Mountains. Renowned for its impressive stature and aromatic timber, this medicinal plant offers therapeutic properties. Traditionally, its essential oils are utilized for their antimicrobial and soothing effects, supporting holistic wellness and natural healing.
