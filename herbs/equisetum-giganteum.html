Southern Giant Horsetail, scientifically known as Equisetum giganteum, is a striking perennial herb native to tropical regions. Characterized by its tall, segmented stems and unique silica content, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in various cultures, this plant offers fascinating medicinal potential for supporting bone health and skin integrity.
