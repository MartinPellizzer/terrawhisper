Equisetum giganteum

Equisetum giganteum, commonly known as the Giant Horsetail, is a striking perennial fern found in tropical and subtropical regions. Renowned for its impressive height and segmented stems, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its high silica content, often used to support bone health, skin integrity, and detoxification.

  • Scientific name: Equisetum giganteum
  • Family: Equisetaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Equisetidae and the order Equisetales, it is placed in the family Equisetaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its classification within the genus Equisetum.
