Simarouba berteroana

Simarouba berteroana, commonly known as Marupá, is a versatile medicinal tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, particularly quassinoids, it is traditionally utilized for its antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antipyretic properties. This remarkable species remains a vital resource in ethnobotanical research and natural therapeutics.

  • Scientific name: Simarouba berteroana
  • Family: Simaroubaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindales, it is situated in the family Simaroubaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Simarouba.
