Achillea gypsicola

Achillea gypsicola, commonly known as a rare member of the yarrow family, is a specialized medicinal plant native to specific rocky habitats. Renowned for its unique chemical profile, it holds significant ethnobotanical potential. Research into its bioactive compounds offers promising insights for developing new natural therapeutic treatments.

  • Scientific name: Achillea gypsicola
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
