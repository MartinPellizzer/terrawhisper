Saxifraga sanguinea

What's the taxonomical classification of Saxifraga sanguinea?

Saxifraga sanguinea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Saxifrages (Saxifragales) and is a member of the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, the plant is placed within the genus Saxifraga, with its specific designation being the species sanguinea.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Saxifraaga sanguinea has a low-growing, tufted perennial habit characterized by basal rosettes of fleshy, rounded leaves that often feature reddish margins. During the flowering season, the plant produces upright, branched stems that rise significantly above the foliage. The flowers are small and five-petaled, displaying a striking deep red or crimson color that gives the species its name. This morphological structure allows the plant to thrive in thin soils and crevice habitats.
