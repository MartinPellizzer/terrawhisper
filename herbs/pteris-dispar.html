What's the taxonomical classification of Pteris dispar?

Pteris dispar belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Polypodiales and the family Pteridaceae, eventually placing it within the genus Pteris, where it is specifically identified as the species dispar.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Pteris dispar has pinnate fronds that typically measure between 30 and 60 centimeters in length, characterized by a dark green color and a distinctively uneven or irregular shape. The individual pinnae are often lobed or deeply incised, contributing to the plant's complex and textured appearance. Along the margins of the segments, small sori are located on the underside, which are essential for the plant's reproductive cycle. The stipe, or leaf stalk, is usually stout and may exhibit a brownish or reddish hue near the base. These structural features collectively define the unique morphological profile of this fern species.
