Adonis dentata

Adonis dentata, commonly known as the Toothed Adonis, is a perennial medicinal herb native to various Mediterranean regions. Characterized by its distinctive serrated leaves, this plant contains bioactive glycosides. Traditionally, it has been utilized in herbal medicine for its potential cardiac properties and its role in treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Adonis dentata
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Adonis.
