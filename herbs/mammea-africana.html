Mammea africana

Mammea africana, commonly known as the African Mammea, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional healing practices, its diverse chemical compounds offer significant therapeutic potential. From treating skin ailments to addressing internal inflammation, this species serves as a vital resource in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Mammea africana
  • Family: Calophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Calophyllaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Mammea, specifically as the species Mammea africana.
