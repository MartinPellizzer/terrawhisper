Tanacetum balsamita

Tanacetum balsamita, commonly known as lemon balm or sweet chamomile, is a fragrant perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its uplifting citrus aroma, this plant offers soothing properties for digestive health and anxiety relief. Its versatile leaves serve as a gentle remedy for calming the mind and body.

  • Scientific name: Tanacetum balsamita
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Following the order Asterales, it is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Tanacetum.
