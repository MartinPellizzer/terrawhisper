Balsamita vulgaris

Tanacetum balsamita · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Extensive (176 studies) · ★★★★☆

Balsamita vulgaris, scientifically known as Tanacetum balsamita, is a fragrant perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Often called sweet chamomile, it is cherished for its aromatic, lemon-scented leaves. Traditionally used in herbal medicine, this plant offers soothing properties, supporting digestive health and promoting relaxation through its gentle essence.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as sweet chamomile, is highly valued in herbal medicine primarily for its volatile essential oils. These oils are concentrated within the aromatic foliage and flowering tops, containing complex chemical compounds that provide the plant with its characteristic sweet, balsamic scent. When extracted, these essential oils serve as the primary therapeutic component, utilized in various applications for their calming, antispasmodic, and antimicrobial properties.
