Clinopodium gilliesii

Clinopodium gilliesiii, commonly known as Gillies' Calamint, is a resilient aromatic herb native to specific Mediterranean and montane regions. Valued for its essential oils, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential. It is traditionally studied for its antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, serving as a vital subject in ethnobotanical research.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
