Distribution

This plant, Eugenia puniciifolia, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the Caribbean and northern reaches of the South American continent. Within the Caribbean region, its presence is documented in Cuba as well as the islands of Trinidad and Tobago. Moving into Northern South America, the species can be found spanning several distinct territories. Specifically, it inhabits the lush landscapes of French Guiana and Guyana. Finally, its geographical range extends further through the country of Suriname.
