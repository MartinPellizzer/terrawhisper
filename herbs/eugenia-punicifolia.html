Eugenia punicifolia

Eugenia puniciifolia, commonly known as the pomegranate shrub, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional healing. Rich in bioactive compounds like polyphenols and flavonoids, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Often used to treat digestive and skin ailments, it remains a significant subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Eugenia punicifolia
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Myrtales and placed in the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is established within the genus Eugenia.

Distribution

This plant, Eugenia puniciifolia, exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the Caribbean and northern reaches of the South American continent. Moving into Northern South America, the species can be found spanning several distinct territories. Specifically, it inhabits the lush landscapes of French Guiana and Guyana.
