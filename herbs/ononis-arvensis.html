Rest harrow, scientifically known as Ononis arvensis, is a resilient perennial herb belonging to the Fabaceae family. Characterized by its small, pea-like flowers and woody base, this plant thrives in dry, calcareous soils. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it possesses notable anti-inflammatory properties and is used to treat various ailments.
