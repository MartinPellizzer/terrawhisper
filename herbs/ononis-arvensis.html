Ononis arvensis

Rest harrow

Ononis arvensis, commonly known as yellow gromwell, is a distinctive herbaceous plant belonging to the Boraginaceae family. Characterized by its vibrant yellow blooms and fine, hairy foliage, it thrives in diverse habitats. Historically valued in traditional medicine, this resilient species offers unique botanical properties and significant ecological importance.

  • Scientific name: Ononis arvensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Ononis.
