Pink sage bush, scientifically known as Ocimum labiatum, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to Africa. Renowned for its aromatic, serrated leaves, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin infections. This potent plant serves as a vital natural remedy in herbalism.
