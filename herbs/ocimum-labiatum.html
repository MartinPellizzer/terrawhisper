Ocimum labiatum

Pink sage bush

Ocimum labiatum, commonly known as the broad-leaved basil, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin infections, and digestive issues, offering a natural wealth of bioactive compounds for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Ocimum labiatum
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and family Lamiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Ocimum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ocimum labiatum, is referred to by several different names depending on the language and region. In English, it is commonly called the pink sage bush or the shell bush, while in Afrikaans, it is known as both Pienksalie and Orthosiphon labiatus. Additionally, in Arabic, the plant is identified as حبق شفوي.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the southern regions of the continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is notably recorded in Mozambique. Moving into Southern Africa, it can be found inhabiting the KwaZulu-Natal region. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout Eswatini. Finally, its range extends to the Northern Provinces within Southern Africa.
