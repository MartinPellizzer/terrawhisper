Aegle marmelos

Bengal quince

Aegle marmelos, commonly known as Bael, is a sacred medicinal tree revered in Ayurvedic tradition. Renowned for its potent therapeutic properties, the plant's leaves, bark, and fruit are utilized to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and fever. It serves as a vital natural remedy for promoting holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Aegle marmelos
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Aegle marmelos, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae within the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Rutaceae and falls under the genus Aegle.
