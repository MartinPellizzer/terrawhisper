Bengal quince

Aegle marmelos · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (901 studies) · ★★★★★

Bengal quince, scientifically known as Aegle marmelos, is a versatile medicinal tree native to the Indian subcontinent. Revered in Ayurveda, its fruits, leaves, and bark possess potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and digestive properties. This sacred plant offers significant therapeutic potential in treating gastrointestinal disorders and managing various metabolic conditions naturally.
