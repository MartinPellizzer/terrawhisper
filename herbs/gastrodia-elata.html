Gastrodia elata

Orchid(orchidaceae)

Gastrodia elata, commonly known as Tianma, is a precious medicinal orchid highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Often referred to as "heavenly herb," it is primarily utilized to treat dizziness, headaches, and neurological disorders. This rare, parasitic plant is prized for its ability to calm the liver and nourish the brain.

  • Scientific name: Gastrodia elata
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asparagales, it is further categorized into the family Orchidaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Gastrodia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found in the Khabarovsk region and throughout the Kuril Islands. Its distribution continues through the Primorye territory and the island of Sakhalin. Beyond Russia, the species is also located within the South-Central regions of China. Collectively, these areas define the primary natural habitats for this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Gastrodia elata, is a unique orchid that relies on its specialized underground structures for survival and nutrient storage. The most significant medicinal component is the fleshy rhizome, which develops into a thickened, starch-rich tuber used extensively in traditional medicine. Within this complex rhizoome system, the plant stores high concentrations of essential compounds, most notably polysaccharide chains that contribute to its therapeutic properties. Because these rhizomes serve as the primary energy reservoir for the plant, they are the most valued part for harvesting and medicinal preparation.
