Orchid(orchidaceae), scientifically known as Gastrodia elaata, is a unique, holoparasitic orchid native to East Asia. Lacking chlorophyll, it derives nutrients from underground fungi. Highly valued in Traditional Chinese Medicine, this tuberous plant is prized for its purported ability to enhance cognitive function, boost vitality, and promote longevity.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across parts of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found in the Khabarovsk region and throughout the Kuril Islands. Its distribution continues through the Primorye territory and the island of Sakhalin. Beyond Russia, the species is also located within the South-Central regions of China.
