Artemisia chinensis, scientifically known as Artemisia stelleriana, is a perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Primarily native to East Asia, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant potential in pharmacological research.
