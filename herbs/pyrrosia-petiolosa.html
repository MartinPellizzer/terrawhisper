Pyrrosia petiolosa

Pyrrosia petiolosa, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various traditional practices, is a fascinating botanical species valued for its therapeutic potential. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant is often studied for its bioactive compounds, offering promising insights into natural remedies for diverse health and wellness applications.

  • Scientific name: Pyrrosia petiolosa
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic descent places it in the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, specifically within the family Polypodiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Pyrrosiia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented within the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Its range extends significantly into China, where it can be found throughout the South-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is established in the northern parts of China, specifically within Inner Mongolia. Finally, it is also widely distributed across the Manchuria region.

In pharmacognosy, the dried leaves of this species are formally referred to as Pyrrosiae Folium or Folium Pyrrosiae.
