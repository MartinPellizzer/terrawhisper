Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented within the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Its range extends significantly into China, where it can be found throughout the South-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is established in the northern parts of China, specifically within Inner Mongolia. Finally, it is also widely distributed across the Manchuria region.
