What's the taxonomical classification of Myricaria squamosa?

Myricaria squamosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It falls under the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Tamaricaceae. Finally, the plant is categorized under the genus Myricaria, with its specific identifier being the species squamosa.
