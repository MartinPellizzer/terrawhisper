Dioscorea deltoidea

Dioscorea deltoidea, commonly known as a wild medicinal yam, is a climbing plant valued for its therapeutic properties. Found in various tropical regions, its tubers are traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation and digestive issues. This species remains a significant subject of ethnobotanical research and pharmacological interest.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea deltoidea
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Dioscoreales and the family Dioscoreaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Dioscorea.
