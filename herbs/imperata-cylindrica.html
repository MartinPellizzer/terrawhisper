Imperata cylindrica

Imperata cylindrica, commonly known as cogon grass, is a resilient perennial herb found globally in tropical and subtropical regions. While often classified as a stubborn invasive weed, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various cultures utilize its roots and rhizomes in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, fever, and gastrointestinal issues.

  • Scientific name: Imperata cylindrica
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is found within the genus Imperata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific presence within the region of Southwestern Europe. Its geographical range includes the islands of Baleares and Corse. Furthermore, it can be found across the mainland territory of France. The distribution extends southward to include the country of Portugal. Finally, its occurrence is also documented on the island of Sardegna.
