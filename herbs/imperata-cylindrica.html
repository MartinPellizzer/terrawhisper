Imperata cylindrica

Satin tail

Imperata cylindrica, commonly known as cogon grass, is a resilient perennial herb found globally in tropical and subtropical regions. While often classified as a stubborn invasive weed, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various cultures utilize its roots and rhizomes in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, fever, and gastrointestinal issues.

  • Scientific name: Imperata cylindrica
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is found within the genus Imperata.

Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Imperata cylindrica, is recognized by various common names across different languages and regions. In the Pamonese language, it is referred to as Ilib, while in Javanese, it is known as Alang-alang. It is called Eurih in Sundanese and is identified as 띠 in Korean. Additionally, in Russian, the plant is commonly called Императа цилиндрическая.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific presence within the region of Southwestern Europe. Its geographical range includes the islands of Baleares and Corse. Furthermore, it can be found across the mainland territory of France. The distribution extends southward to include the country of Portugal. Finally, its occurrence is also documented on the island of Sardegna.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as cogon grass, possesses several significant underground parts that are frequently utilized in traditional medicine. The most vital component is the rhizomes, which are thick, creeping stems that store nutrients and are highly valued for their therapeutic properties. While the plant develops a network of fine roots to anchor itself, it is specifically the root system that contains the concentrated bioactive compounds. When prepared as a root extract, the essence of the plant can be applied to treat various ailments, making the entire subterranean structure essential to its medicinal profile.
