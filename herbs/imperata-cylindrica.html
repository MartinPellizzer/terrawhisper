Satin Tail

Imperata cylindrica · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Sparse (50 studies) · ★★☆☆☆

Satin Tail, scientifically known as Imperata cylindrica, is a resilient perennial grass widely distributed across tropical and subtropical regions. Renowned for its invasive nature, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Various cultures utilize its rhizomes in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as inflammation, fever, and digestive issues.
