Sennoside, scientifically known as Senna afillia, is a medicinal shrub native to arid regions. Renowned for its potent purgative properties, it contains anthraquinone glycosides that stimulate bowel movements. Traditionally used to treat constipation, this plant serves as a vital natural remedy in various herbal pharmacopeias worldwide.
