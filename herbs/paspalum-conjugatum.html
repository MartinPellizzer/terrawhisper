Paspalum conjugatum

Paspalum conjugatum, commonly known as ajungi or colonial paspalum, is a versatile creeping grass found across tropical regions. While primarily valued in landscaping, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Traditional medicine utilizes various parts of this plant to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its unique therapeutic potential in folk healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Paspalum conjugatum
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Poales. Finally, it is classified under the family Poaceae and the genus Paspalum.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution throughout the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing within the borders of Benin. Furthermore, its presence extends into the territory of Burkina. The plant is also documented to inhabit parts of Ghana and Guinea. Finally, its range reaches as far as the Ivory Coast.
