Kaempferia angustifolia

Kaempferia angustifolia, commonly known as aromatic ginger, is a perennial herb native to Southeast Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, its fragrant rhizomes contain essential oils with potent anti-inflammatory and digestive properties. This versatile plant is widely utilized in herbal remedies to soothe gastrointestinal discomfort and promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Kaempferia angustifolia
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is classified under the family Zingiberaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Kaempferia, which includes the medicinal species Kaempferiia angustifolia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Kaempferiia angustifolia, is recognized by several distinct common names across different regions and languages. In Indonesia, it is widely referred to as Temu Rapet (id) and is also known as Kunci Pepet (jv) in Javanese. Moving to Vietnam, the plant is called Địa liền lá hẹp (vi), while in Thailand, it is identified as ว่านเฒ่าหนังแห้ง (th).

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several distinct regions within the Asian continent. Within the Indian Subcontinent, its presence is recorded in India, Bangladesh, and the East Himalaya. The species also extends into the Indo-China region, where it can be found in Cambodia. Additionally, its geographical range encompasses the country of Myanmar. Collectively, these areas represent the primary habitats where this medicinal species thrives.

Plant Parts

This plant, Kaempferia angustifolia, commonly known as Sandalwood Chia, is primarily utilized for its therapeutic properties derived from the rhizome. As a member of the ginger family, the underground rhizome serves as the storage organ for the plant, containing a high concentration of essential oils and bioactive compounds that provide its distinct aromatic profile. In traditional medicine, it is this specific part of the plant that is harvested, dried, and processed to create various medicinal preparations used to support digestive health and alleviate inflammation.
