Kitagawia praeruptora

Kitagawia praeruptora, commonly known as "the plant of the broken clouds," is a significant medicinal herb native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. Researchers are increasingly exploring its bioactive compounds for potential therapeutic applications in managing various modern inflammatory conditions.
