Marjoram, scientifically known as Origanum majorana, is a perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic leaves and therapeutic properties. Widely used in culinary traditions, it also serves as a potent medicinal plant. Renowned for its antioxidant, antimicrobial, and calming effects, marjoram supports digestive health and promotes relaxation and wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Marjoram, is a fragrant herb valued for its various therapeutic properties derived from its different components. Most of the medicinal value is concentrated in the aerial parts, which encompass the stems and foliage above the ground. Specifically, the aromatic leaves are the primary source of the plant's potent essential oils. When these essential oils are extracted through distillation, they provide a concentrated form of the plant's bioactive compounds, making the essential oil a highly sought-after ingredient in aromatherapy and herbal medicine.
