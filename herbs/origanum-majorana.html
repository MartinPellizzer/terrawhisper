Marjoram, scientifically known as Origanum majorana, is a perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic leaves and therapeutic properties. Widely used in culinary traditions, it also serves as a potent medicinal plant. Renowned for its antioxidant, antimicrobial, and calming effects, marjoram supports digestive health and promotes relaxation and wellness.
