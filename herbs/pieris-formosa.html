Pieris formosa

Pieris formosa, commonly known as Andromeda or Lily-of-the-Valley shrub, is a striking evergreen ornamental. While celebrated for its cascading white flowers and vibrant foliage, it contains grayanotoxins. These toxic compounds necessitate cautious handling, as ingestion can lead to significant physiological effects, marking it as both beautiful and dangerous.

  • Scientific name: Pieris formosa
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification
