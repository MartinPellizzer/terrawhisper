Petasites hybridus

Common butterbur

Petasiites hybridus, commonly known as butterbur, is a perennial herb prized for its potent medicinal properties. Primarily recognized for its ability to alleviate allergic rhinitis and migraines, this plant contains bioactive compounds that offer significant therapeutic benefits. Understanding its traditional uses and chemical profile is essential for modern herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Petasites hybridus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales, it is further categorized into the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Petasites.
