Common butterbur, scientifically known as Petasites hybridus, is a perennial herbaceous plant prized for its potent medicinal properties. Historically used to treat various ailments, it is most recognized today for its effectiveness in managing migraines and seasonal allergies, offering a natural alternative for those seeking relief from inflammatory conditions.
