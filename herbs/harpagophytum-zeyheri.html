Harpagophytum zeyheri

Harpagophytum zeyheri, commonly known as Devil's Claw, is a medicinal plant native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its potent anti-inflammatory properties, it is widely utilized in traditional and modern herbal medicine to alleviate joint pain, arthritis, and digestive discomfort, offering a natural approach to managing various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Harpagophytum zeyheri
  • Family: Pedaliaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant, Harpagophytum zeyheri, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Lamiales and the family Pedaliaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Harpagophytum.
