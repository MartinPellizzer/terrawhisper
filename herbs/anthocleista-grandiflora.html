Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence has been documented in the countries of Burundi, Cameroon, and Rwanda. It also maintains a significant range within the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Beyond West-Central regions, the species extends into Northeast Tropical Africa. This broader distribution encompasses the territories of both Sudan and South Sudan.
