Iris anguifuga

What's the taxonomical classification of Iris anguifuga?

Iris anguifuga belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Iridaeceae, placing it within the genus Iris, and it is specifically identified by its species designation, anguifuga.
