Exostema caribaeum

Exostemma caribaeum, commonly known as "pau-de-fumo," is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues and inflammation. This woody species holds immense potential for pharmacological research and sustainable herbal medicine development.
