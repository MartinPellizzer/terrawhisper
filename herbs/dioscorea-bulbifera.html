Air Potato

Dioscorea bulbifera · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (344 studies) · ★★★★★

Air Potato, scientifically known as Dioscorea bulbifera, is a perennial climbing vine native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive aerial tubers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. While often considered an invasive species, many cultures utilize its tubers as a nutritional food source and for traditional medicinal purposes.

Regarding the dispersal of these reproductive units, the plant utilizes an autochorous syndrome, relying on self-dispersal mechanisms to distribute its seeds into the surrounding environment.
