Air Potato

Dioscorea bulbifera · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (344 studies) · ★★★★★

Air Potato, scientifically known as Dioscorea bulbifera, is a perennial climbing vine native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive aerial tubers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. While often considered an invasive species, many cultures utilize its tubers as a nutritional food source and for traditional medicinal purposes.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as air potato, possesses unique morphological features that are utilized in various medicinal and culinary contexts. The most distinctive parts are the aerial bulbils, which grow along the stems and serve as a method of reproduction. Below the surface, the plant develops starchy tubers and specialized rhizomes that store energy and nutrients. While the whole plant is often studied for its various bioactive compounds, specific attention is frequently given to the chemical properties found within both the underground tuber and the hanging bulbils.
