Beach almond, scientifically known as Terminalia catapla, is a versatile tropical tree widely distributed along coastal regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of the plant, including bark and leaves, are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues and inflammatory conditions.
