Chinese rhubarb

Rheum tanguticum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (47 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Chinese rhubarb, scientifically known as Rheum tanguticum, is a potent medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of China. Valued in traditional medicine, its roots contain unique anthraquinones used to treat constipation, inflammation, and digestive ailments. This perennial plant remains a vital component in various specialized herbal formulations.
