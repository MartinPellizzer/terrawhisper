Melaleuca alternifolia

Melaleuca alternifolia, commonly known as tea tree, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Australia. Renowned for its powerful antimicrobial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties, its essential oil is widely used in aromatherapy and skincare. This potent botanical serves as a natural remedy for treating skin infections and promoting healing.

  • Scientific name: Melaleuca alternifolia
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Myrtales, and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Melaleuca.
