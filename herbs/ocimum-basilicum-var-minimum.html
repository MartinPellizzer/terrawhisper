Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct oceanic and continental regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it is widely distributed across both India and Sri Lanka. Furthermore, the species can be found in the South-Central Pacific, specifically within the Tuamotu Archipelago. This wide dispersal illustrates the plant's ability to inhabit various tropical environments across the globe.
