Nepeta binaloudensis

Nepeta binaloudensis, commonly known as Binualoud Catnip, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to soothe nervous tension and alleviate digestive discomfort, offering a natural, botanical approach to promoting overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta binaloudensis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Nepeta.
