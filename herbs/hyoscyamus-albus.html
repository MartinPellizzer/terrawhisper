Hyoscyamus albus, scientifically known as Hyoscyamus albus, is a perennial herb belonging to the Solanaceae family. Native to the Mediterranean, this plant contains potent tropane alkaloids like hyoscyamine. While historically used for its sedative and antispasmodic properties, its high toxicity requires extreme caution and professional medical supervision.
