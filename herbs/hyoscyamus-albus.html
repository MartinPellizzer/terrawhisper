Hyoscyamus albus

Hyoscamus albus

Hyoscyamus albus, commonly known as Henbane, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its high concentration of tropane alkaloids like hyoscyamine, it has historically been used for its sedative and antispasmodic properties. However, its extreme toxicity requires precise handling and expert medical supervision.

  • Scientific name: Hyoscyamus albus
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales, it falls under the family Solanaceae. Its specific taxonomic identification is defined by the genus Hyoscyamus, which includes the species Hyoscyamus albus.

This plant exhibits a diverse range of locations throughout the European continent. In Middle Europe, its presence is noted across Germany as well as the region encompassing Czechia and Slovakia. Moving toward the Mediterranean, the species is distributed across several islands in Southwestern Europe, including the Baleares. It can also be found on the island of Corse within the same southwestern territory. Finally, the plant's range extends across the mainland of France.
