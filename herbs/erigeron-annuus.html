Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Erigeron annuus, is characterized by its distinctive floral structure, which serves as a primary point of identification. The flowers are arranged in numerous small, composite heads that typically feature a central disc of yellowish florets surrounded by a fringe of numerous, fine, white ray florets. These delicate blossoms are often clustered together, creating a soft, feathery appearance atop the plant's stems, and they play a crucial role in the plant's reproductive cycle and its overall medicinal profile.
