Aster sphaerotus

What's the taxonomical classification of Aster sphaerotus?

Aster sphaerotus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales. This plant is a representative of the family Asteraceae, placing it among the sunflowers and daisies. Within this family, it is categorized under the genus Aster, and its specific biological identity is defined by the species name sphaerotus.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Aster sphaerotus has a perennial life cycle characterized by erect, woody stems that typically reach heights of 30 to 60 centimeters and feature narrow, linear to lanceolate leaves with entire margins. The foliage is often sparsely distributed along the stem and possesses a grayish-green hue due to fine pubescence. Its inflorescences consist of solitary, terminal flower heads that are relatively small compared to other aster species. Each head contains numerous disc florets that transition from yellow to a brownish color as they mature. The involucral bracts are arranged in several overlapping rows, providing a protective structure for the developing seeds.
