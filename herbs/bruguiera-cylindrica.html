Bruguiera cylindrica

Bruguiera cylindrica, commonly known as the large-leafed mangrove, is a vital species within coastal estuarine ecosystems. Renowned for its ecological importance, this woody plant offers significant medicinal potential. Traditional healers utilize various parts of the tree to treat ailments, highlighting its valuable role in ethnobotanical studies and pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Bruguiera cylindrica
  • Family: Rhizophoraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant exhibits a widespread geographical distribution across several distinct regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is specifically noted in the Hainan province. It is also commonly found throughout the Indian Subcontinent, spanning countries such as India and Bangladesh. Together, these locations highlight the plant's preference for diverse coastal and subtropical environments.
