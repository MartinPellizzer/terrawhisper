Cape Speckled Aloe, scientifically known as Aloe microsstigma subsp. microsstigma, is a charming, small-statured succulent native to South Africa. Characterized by its distinctive spotted leaves and delicate orange-to-yellow blooms, this resilient species is highly valued by collectors for its compact growth habit and striking, decorative aesthetic in xeriscaping.
