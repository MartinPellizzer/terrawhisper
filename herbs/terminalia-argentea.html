Terminalia argentea

Terminalia argentea, commonly known as the Silver Terminalia, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential for modern pharmacological research and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia argentea
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Combretaceae. It is further categorized into the genus Terminalia, where its specific species is identified as Terminalia argentea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution throughout much of South America. In the western part of the continent, it can be found within the borders of Bolivia. Its presence is also heavily concentrated in Brazil, where it spans multiple distinct regions. Specifically, the species is documented across the North, Northeast, Southeast, and West-Central areas of Brazil. Together, these locations highlight the significant range occupied by this medicinal species.
