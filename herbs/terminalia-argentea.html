Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution throughout much of South America. In the western part of the continent, it can be found within the borders of Bolivia. Its presence is also heavily concentrated in Brazil, where it spans multiple distinct regions. Specifically, the species is documented across the North, Northeast, Southeast, and West-Central areas of Brazil. Together, these locations highlight the significant range occupied by this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

This plant, Terminalia argentea, possesses various components that are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The leaves are equally important, as they are frequently used in decoctions or infusions to treat various ailments.
