California corn lily, scientifically known as Veratrum californicum var. californicum, is a striking perennial native to Western North America. Characterized by its elegant, bell-shaped flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance. Historically utilized by Indigenous communities, its alkaloids possess potent medicinal properties, though careful handling is essential due to toxicity.
