Veratrum californicum var. californicum

Verratrum californicum var. californicum, commonly known as California false hellebore, is a perennial herb native to western North America. While valued in traditional ethnobotany for its potent alkaloids, it is highly toxic if ingested. This plant requires careful handling due to its powerful physiological effects on the human nervous system.

  • Scientific name: Veratrum californicum var. californicum
  • Family: Melanthiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Liliales, it is classified under the family Melanthiaceae and the genus Veratrum. Specifically, the variety californicum identifies this particular specimen of Veratrum californicum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the northwestern regions of the United States. Within this broad area, its range extends into the state of Colorado. It can also be found growing throughout the landscapes of Idaho and Oregon. Furthermore, the species is present across the terrain of Washington. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the mountainous regions of Wyoming.
