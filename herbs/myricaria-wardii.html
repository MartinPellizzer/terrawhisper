Myricaria wardii

Myricaria wardii, commonly known as the Ward's Myricaria, is a resilient, deciduous shrub native to the arid, riverine landscapes of Central and East Asia. Valued in traditional medicine for its therapeutic potential, this hardy plant thrives in challenging environments, offering unique phytochemical properties used to treat various ailments.
