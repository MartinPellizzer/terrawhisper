Ullucus tuberosus, scientifically known as Ullucus tuberosus, is a nutritious Andean tuber prized for its unique texture and vibrant colors. Primarily cultivated in South America, this resilient crop offers essential vitamins and minerals, serving as a vital staple in traditional diets and a functional ingredient in modern nutrition.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions in the South American continent. In Northern South America, it can be found growing in Venezuela. Its presence extends significantly into Western South America, where it inhabits the landscapes of Colombia and Ecuador. Furthermore, it is commonly distributed throughout the mountainous areas of Peru. Finally, the species completes its regional range by growing in Bolivia.
