What medicinal compounds this plant contains?

This plant contains high concentrations of tropane alkaloids, specifically hyoscyamine, scopolamine, and atropine. These medicinal compounds act as anticholinergic agents by blocking acetylcholine receptors in the nervous system. Hyoscyamine is frequently used to treat gastrointestinal spasms and reduce abdominal cramping. Scopolamine is often utilized for its ability to prevent motion sickness and induce sedation. Because these substances are highly potent, they must be carefully regulated to avoid toxic effects on the human body.

What side effects this plant can have?

This plant can cause severe anticholinergic toxicity resulting in symptoms such as nausea, dizziness, dry mouth, tachycardia, blurred vision, constipation, dilated pupils, confusion, hallucinations, urinary retention, delirium, and anxiety. Ingestion of its alkaloids disrupts the autonomic nervous system, leading to an increased heart rate and difficulty urinating. The neurological impact often manifests as cognitive impairment, characterized by sudden confusion or intense anxiety. In more extreme cases, a person may experience frightening hallucinations or a state of total delirium. These various side effects demonstrate the high level of toxicity present within the plant.
