Cutleaf groundcherry, scientifically known as Physalis angulata, is a versatile annual herb belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its edible, husk-covered berries, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is widely studied for its potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties in various therapeutic applications.
