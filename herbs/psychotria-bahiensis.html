Psychotria bahiensis

Psychotria bahiensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub in tropical regions, holds great ethnobotanical value. Primarily recognized for its bioactive alkaloids, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments. Understanding its pharmacological properties is essential for exploring its potential in modern therapeutic developments.

  • Family: Rubiaceae
