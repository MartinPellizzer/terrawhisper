What's the taxonomical classification of Selaginella nipponica?

Selaginella nipponica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Lycopodiidae. Following its taxonomic progression, it falls under the order Selaginellales and the family Selaginellaceae. Finally, the plant is placed within the genus Selaginella, with its specific designation being the species nipponica.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Selagineella nipponica has a creeping rhizome structure with small, scale-like leaves that are arranged in four rows along the narrow, dichotomously branching stems. The plant exhibits a lycopsid morphology where the microphylls are densely packed to cover the stem surface. Each leaf contains a single, unbranched vascular vein running through its center.
