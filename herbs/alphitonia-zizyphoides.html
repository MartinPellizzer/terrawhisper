Alphitonia zizyphoides

Alphitonia zizyphoides, commonly known as the "Zizyphus-leafed Alphitonia," is a versatile medicinal shrub valued in traditional herbalism. Native to various tropical regions, it is frequently utilized for its potent bioactive compounds. This plant serves as a natural remedy for treating skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions.

  • Family: Rhamnaceae
