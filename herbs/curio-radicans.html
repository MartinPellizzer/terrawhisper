This plant is primarily found throughout the diverse landscapes of Southern Africa. Its native range extends across the various Cape Provinces, where it thrives in specific local climates. Additionally, it can be located within the mountainous regions of Lesotho. The species is also distributed through parts of Namibia, showing its adaptability to different terrains.
