Lupinus caudatus

Lupinus caudatus, commonly known as the tailed lupin, is a striking perennial herb native to South America. Distinguished by its tall, upright spikes of vibrant blue or purple flowers, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique phytochemical properties and its ability to thrive in diverse ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Lupinus caudatus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Lupinus, and its specific species name, Lupinus caudatus.
