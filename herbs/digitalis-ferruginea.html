Rusty foxglove, scientifically known as Digitalis ferruginea, is a perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Distinguished by its rust-colored, hairy leaves and bell-shaped flowers, it contains potent cardiac glycosides. While historically significant in pharmacology for treating heart conditions, its high toxicity requires extreme caution during medicinal use.
