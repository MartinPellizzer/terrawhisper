Broomrape, scientifically known as Cistanche salsa, is a parasitic medicinal plant native to arid regions of China. Highly valued in traditional medicine, it is renowned for its ability to combat fatigue, enhance fertility, and improve cognitive function. This "desert ginseng" offers potent antioxidant properties and significant neuroprotective benefits.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cistanche salsa, is a parasitic species that lacks chlorophyll, meaning it does not possess traditional green leaves; instead, its most prominent feature is its thick, fleshy, yellowish-brown stems that emerge directly from the soil. These succulent stems serve as the primary structure for transporting nutrients gathered from the roots of host plants. From these robust stems, the plant produces large, showy flowers that are typically yellowish or brownish in color, which are essential for its reproductive cycle.
