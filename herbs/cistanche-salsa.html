Broomrape, scientifically known as Cistanche salsa, is a parasitic medicinal plant native to arid regions of China. Highly valued in traditional medicine, it is renowned for its ability to combat fatigue, enhance fertility, and improve cognitive function. This "desert ginseng" offers potent antioxidant properties and significant neuroprotective benefits.
