Phytolacca icosandra

Phytolacca icosandra, commonly known as a member of the Phytolaccaceae family, is a medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is frequently utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. Exploring its phytochemical profile reveals significant potential for developing diverse therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Phytolacca icosandra
  • Family: Phytolaccaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is further categorized into the family Phytolaccaceae.
