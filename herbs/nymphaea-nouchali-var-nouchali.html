Nymphaea nouchali var. nouchali

Nymphaea nouchalii var. nouchalii, commonly known as the Blue Water Lily, is a revered aquatic plant in Ayurvedic medicine. Renowned for its cooling properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin disorders, and nervous system ailments. This sacred bloom holds significant spiritual and therapeutic value in Eastern healing traditions.

  • Scientific name: Nymphaea nouchali var. nouchali
  • Family: Nymphaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
