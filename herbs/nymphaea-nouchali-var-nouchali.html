Plant Parts

This plant, Nymphaea nouchali var. nouchali, commonly known as the blue water lily, is highly valued in traditional medicine, where its large, fragrant flowers serve as a primary source of therapeutic properties. The flowers are characterized by their striking blue or violet petals and are frequently utilized in various herbal formulations to treat ailments such as inflammation, fever, and skin disorders. Beyond their aesthetic beauty, the medicinal potency of the flowers is often harnessed through infusions or extracts to provide calming and cooling effects for the body.
