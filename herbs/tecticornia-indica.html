Tecticornia indica

Tecticornia indica, commonly known as saltwort, is a specialized halophyte native to coastal and saline environments. Renowned for its remarkable ability to thrive in high-salinity soils, this succulent plant plays a vital role in ecosystem stability and is increasingly studied for its unique medicinal properties and antioxidant potential.

  • Scientific name: Tecticornia indica
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Tecticornia.
