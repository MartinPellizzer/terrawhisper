Vachellia tortuosa

Vacchellia tortuosa, commonly known as the crooked vacchellia, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to arid African regions. Renowned for its complex chemical profile, it contains bioactive alkaloids used in traditional healing. This plant serves as a vital resource for treating various ailments, highlighting its significant ethnobotanical importance.

  • Scientific name: Vachellia tortuosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Vachellia.
