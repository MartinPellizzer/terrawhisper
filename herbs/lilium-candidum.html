Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Lilium candidum, is characterized by its striking reproductive and storage structures. The most visually prominent features are the large, fragrant white flowers, which are composed of elegant, spreading petals that give the blossom its classic lily appearance. Beyond its ornamental beauty, the medicinal value of the species is often associated with its underground bulbs, which serve as the plant's primary nutrient storage organs and are frequently utilized in traditional herbal preparations.
