Cassia alata

Senna alata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (259 studies) · ★★★★★

Cassia alata, scientifically known as Senna alata, is a versatile medicinal shrub renowned for its potent therapeutic properties. Commonly called the candlestick senna, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin conditions like psoriasis and fungal infections, while also serving as a natural, effective laxative for digestion.
