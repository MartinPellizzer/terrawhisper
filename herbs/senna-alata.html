Senna alata

Sennat alatas, commonly known as senna or cassia, is a medicinal plant renowned for its potent laxative properties. Primarily harvested from its leaves and pods, it contains sennosides, which effectively stimulate bowel movements. Widely used in traditional and modern medicine, it remains a vital natural remedy for constipation.

  • Scientific name: Senna alata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Senna.

This plant is widely distributed across the region of West Tropical Africa. Specifically, its presence is documented within the borders of Benin.
