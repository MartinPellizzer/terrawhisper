Neltuma glandulosa

Neltuma glandulosa, commonly known as the glandular nettle, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its unique bioactive properties. Characterized by its distinctive glandular structures, this species plays a vital role in traditional healing practices, offering significant therapeutic potential in treating various inflammatory conditions and supporting overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Neltuma glandulosa
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized into the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Neltuma, specifically the species Neltuma glandulosa.
