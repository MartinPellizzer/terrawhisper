Honey Mesquite, scientifically known as Neltuma glandulosa, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory capabilities, it is traditionally used to support digestive health and skin healing. This resilient species offers a natural resource for holistic wellness and herbal remedies.

Distribution

This plant, Neltuma glandulosa, exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Libya. Moving toward West Tropical Africa, the species can be found in Guinea-Bissau and Mali. The plant's range also extends to the coastal and desert regions of Mauritania. Finally, its distribution continues southward into the territory of Nigeria.
