Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across specific regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing in both the Amur and Primorye territories. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the South-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is documented within the Inner Mongolia area of China. Finally, its range also encompasses the Manchuria region, completing its notable distribution pattern.
