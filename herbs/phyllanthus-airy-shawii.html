This plant, Phyllanthus airy-shawii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Malpighiales. Ultimately, it is a member of the family Phyllanthaceae within the genus Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing in the diverse landscapes of India and its neighbor, Bangladesh. Its presence is also documented throughout the various terrains of Nepal. Specifically, the species thrives in the lush environments of Assam. Additionally, it is known to inhabit the elevated areas of the West Himalaya.
