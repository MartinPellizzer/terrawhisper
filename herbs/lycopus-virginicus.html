Virginia water horehound

Lycopus virginicus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (331 studies) · ★★★★★

Virginia water horehound, scientifically known as Lycopus virginicus, is a perennial herb native to North American wetlands. Characterized by its distinctive square stems and small, white, tubular flowers, this plant has long been valued in traditional herbalism for its astringent properties and potential roles in supporting respiratory and digestive health.
