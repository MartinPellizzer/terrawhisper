Epimedium grandiflorum

Epimedium grandiflorum, commonly known as Horny Goat Weed, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional Chinese medicine, its bioactive compound, icariin, is widely studied for its potential to support sexual health, improve bone density, and enhance circulation, making it a popular botanical supplement worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium grandiflorum
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Berberidaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Epimedium and the specific species grandiflorum.
