Davilla nitida

Davilla nitida, commonly known as the "climbing vine" or "Davilla," is a fascinating tropical woody climber native to South America. Renowned for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently studied for its diverse bioactive compounds and traditional medicinal properties in holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Davilla nitida
  • Family: Dilleniaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Davilla nitiida, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Dilleniales. Finally, it is a member of the family Dilleniaceae and is situated within the genus Davilla.

Common Names

This plant, Davilla nitida, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as the nitid davilla, while in Portuguese, it is commonly called trepadeira-de-fogo. In Spanish, the plant is known as trepadora de fuego.
