Protium

Protium, commonly known as Breu or Copal, is a medicinal tree native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its aromatic resin, it possesses powerful anti-inflammatory, antiseptic, and healing properties. Traditionally used to treat skin ailments and respiratory issues, this botanical treasure offers significant potential for modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Protium
  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Sapindales and the family Burseraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Protium.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Protium, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties which are concentrated in several distinct anatomical components. The most significant part is the resin, a viscous substance that flows from the bark when the trunk is wounded. This resin is primarily composed of various oleoresins, which contain the complex aromatic compounds and bioactive molecules responsible for its medicinal efficacy. Additionally, the leaves of the plant are often utilized in traditional practices, contributing to the overall pharmacological profile of the species.
