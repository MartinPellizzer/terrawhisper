Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Protium, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties which are concentrated in several distinct anatomical components. The most significant part is the resin, a viscous substance that flows from the bark when the trunk is wounded. This resin is primarily composed of various oleoresins, which contain the complex aromatic compounds and bioactive molecules responsible for its medicinal efficacy. Additionally, the leaves of the plant are often utilized in traditional practices, contributing to the overall pharmacological profile of the species.
