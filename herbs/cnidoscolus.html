Cnidoscolus

Cnidoscolus, commonly known as chaya or tree spinach, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Americas. Renowned for its nutrient-dense leaves, it is used in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This resilient shrub offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Cnidoscolus
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Euphorbiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Cnidoscolus.

Distribution
