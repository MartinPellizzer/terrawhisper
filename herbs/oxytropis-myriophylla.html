What's the taxonomical classification of Oxytropis myriophylla?

Oxytropis myriophyllas belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the hierarchical structure of botanical classification, it is placed in the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Oxytropis, and its unique species designation, myriophylla.

Researchers study these chemical constituents to better understand the pharmacological potential inherent in the plant's chemical profile. According to a study published by "Chemical & pharmaceuctical bulletin", eight compounds were isolated from Oxytropis myriophylla. These compounds were identified through spectral analyses as (6R,9R)-roseoside (1), (6R,9S)-roseoside (2), adenosine (3), myriophylloside B (4), myriophylloside C (5), myriophylloside D (6), myriophylloside E (7), and myriophylloside F (8). The plant contained five new flavonoids, specifically numbered 4 through 8. Additionally, the 3 known compounds were isolated from this plant for the first time.
