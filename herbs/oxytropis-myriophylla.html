Oxytropis myriophylla

Oxytropis myriophylla, commonly known as the many-leaved milkwort, is a rare perennial legume native to specific arid regions. Distinguished by its intricate, multi-foliate leaves, this plant holds significant botanical interest. Researchers explore its unique phytochemical profile for potential therapeutic properties, highlighting its value in traditional and modern ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Oxytropis myriophylla
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, Oxytropis myriophyllas, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Fabales. Finally, it is a member of the family Fabaceae and the genus Oxytropis.
