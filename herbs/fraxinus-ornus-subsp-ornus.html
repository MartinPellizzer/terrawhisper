Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence can be found within the territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species is also established in Austria. Its range continues through Belgium, which marks another part of its central European territory. Finally, the distribution extends into the region of Czechia-Slovakia.

Plant Parts

This plant, known as the European Manna Ash (Fraxinus ornus subsp. ornus), is characterized by its pinnate leaf structure, which consists of several pairs of leaflets arranged along a central axis. These leaves are typically lanceolate in shape with finely serrated margins, providing a lush, green canopy that is essential to the plant's physiological processes. In traditional herbal studies, while other parts of the tree may be examined, the morphology of the leaf remains a primary identifying feature for distinguishing this specific subspecies in the field.
