Plant Parts

This plant, Pueraria mirifica, is characterized by its unique morphological structure, primarily centered around its specialized underground storage organs. The most pharmacologically significant part is the large, fleshy tuber, which is a modified tuberous root used to store nutrients and bioactive phytoestrogens. Surrounding the central mass, one may encounter the tuberous cortex, the outer layer of the root system that contributes to its overall structure. Above the ground, the plant features a climbing stem that supports its growth, bearing young leaves that are essential for photosynthesis and the plant's developmental cycle.
