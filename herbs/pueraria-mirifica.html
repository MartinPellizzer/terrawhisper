Pueraria mirifica

Pueraria mirifica, commonly known as White Kwao Krua, is a medicinal plant native to Thailand. Renowned for its phytoestrogenic properties, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to support hormonal balance, skin health, and vitality. This potent botanical serves as a natural remedy for managing various female wellness concerns.

  • Scientific name: Pueraria mirifica
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is a member of the Fabaceae family. Its taxonomic classification concludes with the genus Pueraria, specifically identified as Pueraria mirifica.

This plant is primarily recognized for its specific regional presence within the Indo-China area. Its natural habitat is most notably concentrated in the country of Thailand.
