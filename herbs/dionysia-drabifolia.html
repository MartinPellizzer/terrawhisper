Dionysia drabifolia

Dionysia drabifolia, commonly known as the Himalayan Dionysia, is a rare, slow-growing medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Valued in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties, this perennial plant plays a vital role in treating various ailments, though it remains a conservation priority.

  • Scientific name: Dionysia drabifolia
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Ericales, it is further categorized into the family Primulaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Dionysia.

This plant, known scientifically as Dionysia drabifolia, possesses a very specific and limited geographical range. Its natural occurrence is primarily centered within the region of Western Asia.
