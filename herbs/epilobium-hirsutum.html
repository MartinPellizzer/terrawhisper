Epilobium hirsutum

Great willowherb

Epilobium hirsutum, commonly known as Great Willowherb, is a resilient perennial herb prized in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its hairy stems and pinkish-purple flowers, this plant is often utilized for its anti-inflammatory and soothing properties, traditionally supporting digestive health and skin vitality through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Epilobium hirsutum
  • Family: Onagraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Myrtales, it is classified under the family Onagraceae. Finally, it is categorized within the genus Epilobium.
