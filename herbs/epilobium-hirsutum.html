Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as hairy willowherb, is characterized by its distinctive aerial parts which are utilized in various medicinal preparations. The overground part of Epilobium hirsutum consists of erect, leafy stems that are densely covered in fine, soft hairs, giving the plant its specific epithet. These harvested components, including the leaves and flowering tops, contain the bioactive compounds essential for its therapeutic properties.
