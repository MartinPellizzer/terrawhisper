Cyclamen repandum

Cyclamen repandum, commonly known as the branched cyclamen, is a perennial herb native to European woodlands. Characterized by its distinctive heart-shaped leaves and delicate pink flowers, this plant holds historical significance in traditional medicine. While aesthetically pleasing, it contains bioactive compounds that require careful handling and expert knowledge.

  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Europe
