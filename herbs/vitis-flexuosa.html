Vitis flexuosa

What's the taxonomical classification of Vitis flexuosa?

Vitis flexuosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Vitales. This plant is a member of the family Vitaceae, specifically falling under the genus Vitis, with its unique identity defined by the species name flexuosa.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Vitis flexuosa has woody, climbing vines characterized by serrated, palmately lobed leaves and clusters of small, greenish-yellow flowers. The stems are often covered in fine hairs and possess tendrils that allow the plant to ascend toward sunlight. Its leaf structure typically features three to five distinct lobes with coarsely toothed margins. During the fruiting season, the plant produces small, ellipsoid berries that transition from green to a dark purple or black hue when ripe. The root system is robust, providing stability as the vine spreads through forest understories.
