Scientifically known as Aconitum palmatum, is a striking perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Characterized by its distinctive palmate leaves and hooded blue flowers, this species belongs to the Ranunculaceae family. While visually captivating, it contains potent alkaloids, requiring extreme caution in medicinal applications.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key mountainous regions in Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted within the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the lush terrain of Assam. The species also inhabits the vast stretches of the East Himalaya and the rugged landscapes of the West Himalaya. Finally, its range extends significantly into the mountainous country of Nepal.
