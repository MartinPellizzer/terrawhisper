Inula aucheriana

Inula aucherianana, commonly known as Golden Inula, is a resilient perennial herb cherished for its vibrant, sun-kissed yellow blooms. Native to Mediterranean landscapes, this botanical treasure is valued in traditional medicine for its potent anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, offering a natural approach to supporting holistic wellness and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Inula aucheriana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic placement puts it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is categorized under the genus Inula.
