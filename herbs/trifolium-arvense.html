Trifolium arvense

Trifolium arvense, commonly known as creeping clover, is a delicate herbaceous plant belonging to the Fabaceae family. Characterized by its small, white, pea-like flowers and trifoliate leaves, it thrives in meadows and disturbed soils. Historically utilized in traditional herbalism, it holds significant interest in botanical and ecological studies.

  • Scientific name: Trifolium arvense
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Trifolium.
