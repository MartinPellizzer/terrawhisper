Ludwigia octovalvis

Mexican primrose-willow

Ludwigia octovalvis, commonly known as primrose-willow, is a versatile medicinal herb found in tropical wetlands. Renowned for its bioactive properties, it is traditionally used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. This resilient plant offers significant pharmacological potential, making it a subject of growing interest in ethnobotany.

  • Scientific name: Ludwigia octovalvis
  • Family: Onagraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it falls under the family Onagraceae. Finally, it is identified by its scientific genus name, Ludwigia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions. In the Macaronesian islands, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. Moving to the mainland, it is found throughout various parts of West Tropical Africa. This includes significant populations within the territories of Benin, Burkina, and Gambia. Finally, its range extends further along the coast to include Ghana.
