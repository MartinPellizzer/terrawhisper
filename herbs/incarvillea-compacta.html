Incarvillea compacta

Incarvillea compacta, commonly known as the Himalayan Incarvillea, is a stunning perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various medicinal practices to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to respiratory issues.

  • Scientific name: Incarvillea compacta
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant, known scientifically as Incarvillea compacta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
