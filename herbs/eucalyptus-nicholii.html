Narrow-leaved Peppermint, scientifically known as Eucalyptus nicholii, is a distinctive Australian shrub prized for its aromatic properties. Characterized by slender, oil-rich leaves, it offers significant medicinal potential. Traditionally utilized for its invigorating essential oils, this plant serves as a natural remedy for respiratory relief and soothing topical applications.
