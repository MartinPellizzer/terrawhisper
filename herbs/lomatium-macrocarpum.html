Bigseed lomatium, scientifically known as Lomatium macrocarpum, is a perennial herb native to the western United States. Renowned in traditional indigenous medicine, this plant features large, distinctive seeds and umbrella-like white flowers. It is frequently utilized for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties to support respiratory and immune health.
