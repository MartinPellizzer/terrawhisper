Stachys lavandulifolia

Stachy's lavandulifolia, commonly known as Lavender-leaf Lamiaceae, is a remarkable medicinal herb valued for its aromatic properties. Renowned for its calming effects, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional remedies to alleviate stress and promote relaxation. Its unique, lavender-like foliage makes it a distinctive botanical treasure in herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Stachys lavandulifolia
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Stachys, specifically as the species Stachys lavandulifolia.
