Tragia spathulata

Tragia spatulata, commonly known as the Spathulate Tragia, is a medicinal herb belonging to the Euphorbiaceae family. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and inflammation, offering significant potential for pharmacological research and natural healing.
