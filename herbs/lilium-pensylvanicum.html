Candlestick lily, scientifically known as Lilium pensylvanicum, is a charming woodland perennial native to eastern North America. Characterized by its upright stems and nodding, orange-red tubular flowers, this striking plant thrives in shaded, moist environments. It serves as a vital component of forest ecosystems and a visual delight.
