Anaphalis aureopunctata

Anaphalis aureopunctata, commonly known as the golden-spotted Anaphalis, is a remarkable medicinal herb native to various subtropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant floral displays, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, offering potent bioactive compounds used in traditional remedies to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various respiratory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Anaphalis aureopunctata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Anaphalis aureopunctata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and the genus Anaphalis.
