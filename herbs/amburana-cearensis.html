Amburana cearensis

Amburana cearensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, holds immense ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments. Research continues to explore its therapeutic potential, particularly regarding its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties.

  • Scientific name: Amburana cearensis
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Amburana.
