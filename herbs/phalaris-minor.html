Little seed canary grass, scientifically known as Phalaris minor, is a widespread annual grass often classified as a significant weed in wheat crops. Primarily found in arid and semi-arid regions, it competes aggressively for nutrients and moisture, posing a substantial threat to agricultural productivity and overall grain yield quality.
