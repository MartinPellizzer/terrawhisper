Phalaris minor

Little seed canary grass

Phalaris minor, commonly known as little canary grass, is a widespread annual grass frequently encountered as a significant weed in wheat crops. Despite its status as a competitor in agriculture, it holds medicinal potential due to its unique nutritional profile and bioactive compounds used in traditional herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Phalaris minor
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Phalaris minor, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Poales and the family Poaceae. It is specifically classified under the genus Phalaris.
