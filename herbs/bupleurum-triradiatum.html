Bupleurum triradiatum

What's the taxonomical classification of Bupleurum triradiatum?

Bupleurum triradiatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Apiales. As a member of the family Apiaceae, it is further defined by its placement in the genus Bupleurum, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species triradiatum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Bupleurum triradiatum has a perennial rootstock that produces upright, erect stems topped with small, yellowish-green umbels of tiny flowers. The leaves are typically arranged in a pseudo-whorl near the top of the stem and feature distinctively three-lobed or triradiate segments. These leaf segments are often narrow and linear, giving the foliage a delicate and divided appearance. The flowering structures consist of multiple small pedicels radiating from a central point to form a flat-topped cluster. Throughout its growth cycle, the plant maintains a relatively compact and herbaceous structure.
