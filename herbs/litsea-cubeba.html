Litsea cubeba

Litsea cubeba, commonly known as Indonesian bay leaf or Mayang, is a versatile tropical evergreen shrub prized for its aromatic properties. Native to Southeast Asia, its essential oils offer potent antimicrobial and antifungal benefits. Traditionally used in culinary applications and herbal medicine, it remains a valuable botanical resource worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Litsea cubeba
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Laurales, it is classified under the family Lauraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Litsea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across various regions of East Asia. Within China, it can be found growing in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Its range also extends into the high-altitude areas of Tibet. Additionally, the species is naturally distributed throughout parts of Eastern Asia, specifically in Japan.
