Saussurea obvallata

Saussurea obvallata, commonly known as Brahma Kamal, is a magnificent medicinal herb found in the high-altitude Himalayas. Renowned for its sacred status and striking blue-rimmed bracts, this rare plant holds significant value in Ayurveda. It is traditionally utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and various healing therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Saussurea obvallata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Saussurea, where its species designation is obvallata.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across various high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central areas as well as within the Qinghai province. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the Tibet region. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species inhabits the state of Assam. Additionally, it is widely distributed across the East Himalaya range.
