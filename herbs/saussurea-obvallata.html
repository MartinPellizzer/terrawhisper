This plant exhibits a specific distribution across various high-altitude regions of Asia. In China, it can be found growing in the South-Central areas as well as within the Qinghai province. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the Tibet region. Within the Indian Subcontinent, the species inhabits the state of Assam.
