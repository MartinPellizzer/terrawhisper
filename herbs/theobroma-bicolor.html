Theobroma bicolor, scientifically known as Theobroma bicolor, is a unique tropical species within the cacao family. Often called "mocambo," this plant produces distinctive, small fruits containing nutrient-rich seeds. Beyond its culinary potential, it holds significant medicinal interest due to its diverse bioactive compounds and traditional therapeutic applications.
