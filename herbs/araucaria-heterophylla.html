This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, it is further categorized into the family Araucariaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Araucarica.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a scattered geographical distribution across several distinct oceanic and continental regions. In the Middle Atlantic Ocean, its presence can be noted on the remote island of Ascension. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it is found within the borders of Bangladesh. The species also reaches the Pacific, specifically inhabiting the Norfolk Islands near Australia. Finally, its range extends to the northern parts of New Zealand.
