Nepeta supina subsp. supina

Nepeta supina subsp. supina, commonly known as Catmint, is a versatile perennial herb prized for its aromatic foliage and delicate lavender blooms. Belonging to the mint family, this subspecies is valued in traditional medicine for its calming properties, offering mild sedative effects to soothe anxiety and promote relaxation.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta supina subsp. supina
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Nepeta. Specifically, it is identified as the subspecies supina within the species Nepeta supina.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the western regions of Eurasia. It can be found growing naturally across the North Caucasus mountain range. Additionally, its presence extends throughout the Transcaucasus area. The species also inhabits various parts of Western Asia, specifically within Türkiye. These distinct locations define the primary natural habitat for this particular subspecies.
