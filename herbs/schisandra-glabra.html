Schisandra glabra

Schisandra glabra, commonly known as Schisandra or magnolia vine, is a perennial woody vine prized in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its "five-flavor" berries, it serves as a potent adaptogen. This botanical powerhouse helps balance the body, reduces fatigue, and supports cognitive function by mitigating the effects of stress.

  • Scientific name: Schisandra glabra
  • Family: Schisandraceae
  • Native range: Northern America
