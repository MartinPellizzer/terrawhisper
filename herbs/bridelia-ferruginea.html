Bridelia ferruginea

Bridelia ferruginea, commonly known as the rusty brideia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Bridelia ferruginea
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. Specifically, Bridelia ferruginea is categorized within the genus Bridelia.

Common Names

This plant, Bridelia ferruginea, is known by various regional names depending on the linguistic landscape. In English, it is commonly referred to as the Rusty Bridelia, while in Hindi, it is known as Lohaphal. In Tamil, the species is called Kanni, and in Kannada, it is referred to as Kanni.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing naturally in the country of Benin. Additionally, the species is present in Burkina and Guinea. Its range also extends through the coastal areas of the Ivory Coast. Finally, the distribution includes the territory of Liberia.
