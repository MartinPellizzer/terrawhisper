Chrysanthellum americanum

Chrysanthellum americanum, commonly known as "carneiro" or "erva-de-carneiro," is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions. This plant holds significant promise in contemporary ethnopharmacology and natural medicine research.

  • Scientific name: Chrysanthellum americanum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Chrysanthellum and the specific species name americanum.
