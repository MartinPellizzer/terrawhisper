Renowned for its large, trumpet-shaped flowers and spiny seed capsules, this plant contains powerful alkaloids. While utilized in traditional medicine, its extreme toxicity requires cautious handling and expert pharmacological knowledge.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Devil's Trumpet, contains various components that are utilized in traditional medicine, though they must be used with extreme caution due to their toxicity. The whole plant is known to contain potent alkaloids, making every part potentially hazardous if mishandled. The large, velvety leaves are often used in topical applications to treat inflammatory conditions, while the trumpet-shaped flowers are visually striking and carry a heavy scent. The small, dark seeds are particularly concentrated with active compounds and are frequently cited in pharmacological studies. Structurally, the plant is supported by sturdy stem segments that provide the necessary framework for its rapid growth and upright posture.
