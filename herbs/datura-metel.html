Datura metel

Datura metel, commonly known as Devil's Trumpet or Moonflower, is a potent medicinal shrub belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its large, trumpet-shaped flowers, it contains bioactive alkaloids like scopolamine. While traditionally used in various healing practices, its intense toxicity requires extreme caution and expert pharmacological guidance.

  • Scientific name: Datura metel
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is classified under the genus Datura. Specifically, Datura metel represents a distinct species within this botanical hierarchy.

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution that spans across multiple continents and diverse climates. In Middle Europe, its presence can be noted within the borders of Germany. The species also extends into Eastern Europe, where it grows throughout South European Russia. Finally, its range reaches into Northern Africa, with documented occurrences in both Algeria and Egypt.
