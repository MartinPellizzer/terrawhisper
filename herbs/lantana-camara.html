Lantana camara

West indian lantana

Lantana camara, commonly known as Lantana or West Indian Lantana, is a versatile flowering shrub widely used in traditional medicine. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties, offering therapeutic potential in treating inflammation, skin ailments, and various infections through its diverse phytochemical constituents.

  • Scientific name: Lantana camara
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Lantana.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across several continents. In Southeastern Europe, its presence can be observed in countries such as Italy. Moving to the African continent, it thrives in West Tropical Africa within Guinea and extends into West-Central Tropical Africa in the DR Congo. The species also inhabits East Tropical Africa, specifically in regions like Kenya. Finally, its range reaches Eastern Asia, where it is found in the Nansei-shoto islands.

Plant Parts

This plant, Lantana camara, is a versatile medicinal species where various components offer distinct therapeutic benefits. The whole plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to prepare decoctions for treating various ailments. Specifically, the leaves are known for their antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, and a single crushed leaf can often be applied topically to soothe skin irritations. The vibrant, multi-colored flowers are also significant, often used in herbal preparations to address different health concerns. Furthermore, the roots of the plant are employed in various folk remedies to manage systemic issues, making every part of this species valuable in ethnobotany.
