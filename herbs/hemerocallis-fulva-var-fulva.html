Hemerocallis fulva var. fulva

Hemerocallis fulva var. fulva, commonly known as the orange daylily, is a resilient perennial valued for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal importance in traditional practices, where its rhizomes are utilized for their purported anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and restorative healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Hemerocallis fulva var. fulva
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, falling into the family Asphodelaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hemerocallis as the variety Hemerocallis fulva var. fulva.
