Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the northern hemisphere. In Northern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Finland. Moving toward Eastern Europe, the species can be found throughout North European Russia. Its range extends significantly into Siberia, where it inhabits the Altay region. Furthermore, the plant is documented in the Siberian territories of both Buryatiya and Chitа.

Plant Parts

This plant, Dryopteris fragrans, is a fern species characterized by its distinct morphological features used in botanical studies. The medicinal properties are primarily derived from the aerial parts, which include the lush green fronds that extend above the ground. Within these fronds, specialized leaves known as sporophylls are present, which are responsible for the production and dispersal of spores. Additionally, the root system provides the necessary stability and nutrient absorption required for the plant to thrive in its natural habitat.
