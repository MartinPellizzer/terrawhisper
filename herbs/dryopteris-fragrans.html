Dryopteris fragrans

Dryopteris fragrans, commonly known as the fragrant wood fern, is a distinctive pteridophyte valued for its aromatic properties. Characterized by its lush, spreading fronds and unique scent, this plant thrives in moist, shaded environments. It serves as a fascinating subject for botanical study and holds potential in traditional herbalism.

  • Scientific name: Dryopteris fragrans
  • Family: Polypodiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Polypodiinae and the order Polypodiales, falling into the family Polypodiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Dryopteris.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several distinct regions of the northern hemisphere. In Northern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Finland. Moving toward Eastern Europe, the species can be found throughout North European Russia. Its range extends significantly into Siberia, where it inhabits the Altay region. Furthermore, the plant is documented in the Siberian territories of both Buryatiya and Chitа.
