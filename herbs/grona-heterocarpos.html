Grona heterocarpos

Grona heterocarpos, commonly known as the False Sandalwood, is a resilient Australian shrub valued for its medicinal properties. Traditionally used by Indigenous communities, its leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds with potential antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory effects. This versatile plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Grona heterocarpos
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Grona.
