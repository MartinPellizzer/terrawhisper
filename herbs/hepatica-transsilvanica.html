Hepatica transsilvanica

Hepatica transsilvaniica, commonly known as Transylvanian liverwort, is a delicate perennial herb native to Eastern European woodlands. Characterized by its distinctive lobed leaves and early spring blooms, this medicinal plant has long been valued in traditional herbalism for its potential therapeutic properties in supporting liver health and vitality.

  • Scientific name: Hepatica transsilvanica
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant, Hepatica transsilvaniica, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta.
