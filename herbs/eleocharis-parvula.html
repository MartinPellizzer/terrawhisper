Eleocharis parvula

Dwarf spikerush

Eleocharis parvula, commonly known as the dwarf waterweed, is a subtle aquatic plant found in freshwater ecosystems. While primarily valued for its ecological role in stabilizing habitats, it holds emerging interest in ethnobotany. Understanding its unique chemical properties offers potential insights into its traditional medicinal applications and therapeutic benefits.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poales, falling into the family Cyperaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eleocharis.
