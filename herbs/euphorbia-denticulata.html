Euphorbia denticulata

Euphorbia denticulata, commonly known as the denticulate spurge, is a resilient medicinal plant valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its distinctively toothed leaves, this species contains bioactive compounds often utilized for their anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. It remains a significant subject in botanical studies and ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia denticulata
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Its taxonomic designation is completed by its placement in the genus Euphorbia.
