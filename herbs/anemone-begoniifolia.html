Anemone begoniifolia

What's the taxonomical classification of Anemone begoniifolia?

Anemone begoniifolia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ranunculales, it is further categorized under the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Anemone, and its specific species designation, begoniifolia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Anemone begoniifolia has asymmetrical, heart-shaped leaves that resemble those of a begonia, featuring a distinctively irregular base and a pointed tip. The leaf margins are often serrated or wavy, providing a textured appearance to the foliage. These leaves are typically arranged in a basal rosette pattern, which helps the plant maximize light absorption near the ground. The texture of the foliage can range from smooth to slightly hairy depending on the specific environmental conditions. Underlying the foliage, the stems are relatively slender and support the spreading growth habit of the plant.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?
