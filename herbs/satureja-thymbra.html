Satureja thymbra

Saturajeya thymbra, commonly known as wild thyme, is a versatile medicinal herb valued for its potent therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils, it possesses significant antibacterial, antifungal, and antioxidant qualities. Traditionally used to support digestive health and respiratory relief, this aromatic plant remains a vital resource in holistic medicine.
