Cinnamon, scientifically known as Cinnamomum, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for its potent therapeutic properties. Rich in antioxidants and anti-inflammatory compounds, it is widely used to regulate blood sugar, support digestive health, and combat infections. This aromatic bark remains a cornerstone in both traditional healing and modern wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as cinnamon, is primarily valued for its medicinal and culinary properties derived from the cortex. The bark, or cortex, is harvested from the inner layer of the stems and branches, where it is carefully peeled and dried to form the characteristic rolled quills used in various treatments. This specific part of the plant contains high concentrations of essential oils and bioactive compounds that contribute to its warming and antimicrobial effects.
