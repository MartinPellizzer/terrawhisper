Monoon longifolium

Monoon longifoilium, commonly known as a versatile medicinal plant, holds significant importance in traditional herbal medicine. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is frequently utilized to treat various ailments. Understanding its botanical characteristics and bioactive compounds is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacological applications.

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Annonaceae. Taxonomically, it is identified under the genus Monoon as Monoon longifolium.
