Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Magnoliales, it is a member of the family Annonaceae. Taxonomically, it is identified under the genus Monoon as Monoon longifolium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several key regions across Asia and Africa. In the West-Central Tropical Africa region, it can be specifically located in Cameroon. Its presence extends into China, where it is found in both the South-Central areas and on the island of Hainan. Moving toward Eastern Asia, the species is also documented in Taiwan. Finally, its range includes the Indian Subcontinent, particularly within the state of Assam.
