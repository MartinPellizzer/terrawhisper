Pyrostegia venusta

Pyrostegia venusta, commonly known as the Celestial Bloom, is a rare medicinal herb prized in traditional ethnobotany. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this exquisite plant is frequently utilized to alleviate inflammation and support cognitive function. Its unique bioactive compounds offer promising potential for modern pharmaceutical research and holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Pyrostegia venusta
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
