Reproduction

The reproduction of Pyrostegia venusta is characterized by a prolonged flowering period that can span nearly the entire year, beginning as early as January and extending through to December. During this cycle, the plant produces vibrant orange flowers that can reach a maximum length of 7.5 cm. This fruit is classified as dehiscent, meaning it opens at maturity to release its seeds.
