Brassica macrocarpa

Brassica macrocarpa, commonly known as the large-fruited mustard, is a distinctive member of the Brassicaceae family. Valued for its robust growth and nutritional profile, this plant offers significant medicinal potential. Its bioactive compounds provide therapeutic benefits, making it a subject of interest for traditional healing and modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Brassica macrocarpa
  • Family: Brassicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Brassicales and the family Brassicaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Brassica.

Distribution

Its natural occurrence is primarily concentrated within the region of Southeastern Europe. Specifically, this medicinal species is found growing in the unique landscapes of Sicilia.
