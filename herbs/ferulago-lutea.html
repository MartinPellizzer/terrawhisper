Ferulago lutea

Ferulago lutea, commonly known as the yellow fennel, is a perennial herb native to Mediterranean landscapes. Distinguished by its vibrant yellow umbels and aromatic foliage, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Researchers study its bioactive compounds, exploring potential therapeutic applications in traditional medicine for its diverse phytochemical properties.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Ferulago.
