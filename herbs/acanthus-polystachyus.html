Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Burundi, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the northeast, its presence extends into the diverse environments of Ethiopia. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the territories of both Sudan and South Sudan. Collectively, these locations highlight a distribution pattern that spans both West-Central and Northeast Tropical Africa.
