Allium cepa

Allium cepa, commonly known as the onion, is a versatile bulbous plant widely used in culinary and medicinal practices. Rich in bioactive compounds like quercetin and sulfur, it possesses potent antibacterial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant properties. Historically, it has been utilized to support respiratory health and bolster immune function.

  • Scientific name: Allium cepa
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asparagales and the family Amaryllidaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its genus, Allium.
