Balanites maughamii

Torchwood

Balanites maugamii, commonly known as the Desert Date, is a resilient medicinal plant native to arid African regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and digestive issues, thanks to its rich concentration of bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Balanites maughamii
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Zygophyllales and the family Zygophyllaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Balaenites.

Common Names

This plant is known by several different names depending on the language and region. In English, it is commonly referred to as Torchwood, while in Afrikaans, it can be called both Groendorring and Fakkelhout. In Arabic, the species is known as هجليج موغامي, and in Zulu, it is called uGobandlovu.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across the African continent, spanning both East and South Tropical Africa. In the East Tropical region, its presence is recorded in Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, its range extends into South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Malawi and Mozambique. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Zambia.
