Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across the African continent, spanning both East and South Tropical Africa. In the East Tropical region, its presence is recorded in Kenya and Tanzania. Moving toward the southern part of the continent, its range extends into South Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found inhabiting the landscapes of Malawi and Mozambique. Additionally, the species is documented to grow within the borders of Zambia.
