Heterotheca subaxillaris

Camphorweed

Heterotheca subaxillaris, commonly known as Goldenbush, is a resilient perennial herb native to North America. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in folk medicine, its chemical constituents offer potential therapeutic benefits, particularly for treating skin inflammations and supporting digestive health.

  • Scientific name: Heterotheca subaxillaris
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Heterotheca.
