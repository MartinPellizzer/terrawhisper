Euploca marifolia

Euploca marifolia, commonly known as the sea-leafed Euploca, is a significant medicinal herb native to the coastal regions of India. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly Ayurveda, this plant is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, specifically its ability to support reproductive health and treat various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Euploca marifolia
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further classified under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Euploca.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across parts of Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted in the Hainan province. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found spanning across India and Nepal. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout the Assam region.
