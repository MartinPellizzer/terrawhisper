Paeonia delavayi

Paeonia delaivayi, commonly known as Delavay's peony, is a rare and precious medicinal perennial native to the high-altitude regions of China. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its roots contain potent bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, alleviate pain, and promote blood circulation, making it a significant botanical treasure.
