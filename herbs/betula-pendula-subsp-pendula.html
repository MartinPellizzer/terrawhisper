Eurasian weeping birch

Betula pendula subsp. pendula · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (3 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Eurasian weeping birch, scientifically known as Betula pendula subsp. pendula, is a graceful deciduous tree revered for its elegant, pendulous branches. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant medicinal value. Its bark and leaves contain bioactive compounds used traditionally to support detoxification, reduce inflammation, and promote urinary health.
