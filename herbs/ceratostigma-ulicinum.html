What's the taxonomical classification of Ceratostigma ulicinum?

Ceratostigma ulicinum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its hierarchical lineage, it falls under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Caryophyllales and is a member of the family Plumbaginaceae. Ultimately, its specific biological identity is defined by its placement within the genus Ceratostigma, specifically as the species ulicinum.

These blossoms are supported by slender pedicels and contrast sharply against the greenery.
