Crepis incana

Crepis incana, commonly known as hoary hawk's-beard, is a perennial herb distinguished by its soft, silvery-tomentose foliage. Native to mountainous regions, this plant plays a vital role in alpine ecosystems. Traditionally valued in herbal studies, it offers unique botanical characteristics essential for understanding biodiversity and regional medicinal flora.

  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe
