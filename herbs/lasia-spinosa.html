Spiny Lasia, scientifically known as Lasia spinosa, is a robust, prickly shrub native to the tropical wetlands of Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its various parts—including leaves and roots—possess significant therapeutic properties. It is widely utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive disorders through herbal formulations.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions of East Asia. Within mainland China, it is widely distributed throughout the South-Central and Southeast areas. Its presence also extends to the tropical island of Hainan and the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Furthermore, the species can be found growing in the island territory of Taiwan. Overall, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in various Chinese and Eastern Asian environments.
