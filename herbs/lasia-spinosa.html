Lasia spinosa

Lasia spinosa, commonly known as the Spiny Lasia, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Native to subtropical regions, it is characterized by its distinctive prickly leaves and stems. This plant is widely utilized for its potent bioactive compounds to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Lasia spinosa
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Alismatales, falling into the family Araceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Lasia.

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions of East Asia. Within mainland China, it is widely distributed throughout the South-Central and Southeast areas.
