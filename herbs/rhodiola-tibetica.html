Rhodiola tibetica

Rhodiola tibetica, commonly known as Tibetan Rhodiola, is a resilient medicinal herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned for its potent adaptogenic properties, this plant is traditionally used to enhance physical endurance, combat fatigue, and support mental clarity amidst the extreme environmental stresses of alpine ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Rhodiola tibetica
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
