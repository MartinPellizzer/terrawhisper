Amorphophallus paeoniifolius

Amorphophallus paeoniifolius, commonly known as Elephant Foot Yam, is a versatile medicinal tuber valued in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Rich in dietary fiber and essential nutrients, it serves as a potent remedy for digestive issues, inflammation, and blood sugar regulation, making it a vital plant for holistic wellness and nutrition.

  • Scientific name: Amorphophallus paeoniifolius
  • Family: Araceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Aliasmaetales and the family Araceae, it is classified under the genus Amorphophallus.
