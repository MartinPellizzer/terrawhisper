Oxalis sepium subsp. sepium

Oxalis sepium subsp. sepium, commonly known as wood sorrel, is a perennial herb distinguished by its delicate, heart-shaped leaflets. Often found in shaded, moist woodlands, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its tangy, citrus-like flavor and its potential therapeutic properties in managing various mild ailments.

  • Scientific name: Oxalis sepium subsp. sepium
  • Family: Oxalidaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Oxalidales, it is classified under the family Oxalidaceae and the genus Oxalis. Specifically, the subspecies sepium represents a distinct taxonomic level within this group.
