What's the taxonomical classification of Smilax sieboldii?

Smilax sieboldii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Liliales. This plant is a member of the family Smilacaceae, specifically categorized within the genus Smilax, with its unique designation being the species sieboldii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Smilax sieboldii has climbing stems equipped with sharp, woody thorns and large, leathery leaves that are typically arranged in an alternate pattern. During the flowering season, small, greenish-white blossoms emerge from the leaf axils to facilitate pollination. These flowers eventually develop into small, fleshy berries that transition from green to a deep red or black color when mature.
