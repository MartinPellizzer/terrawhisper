Distribution

This plant, known as Astragalus hoaantchy, exhibits a specific geographical distribution within the country of China. It can be found growing across the vast regions of Inner Mongolia. Furthermore, its presence extends into the North-Central part of China. The species is also documented to inhabit the high-altitude landscapes of Qinghai. Together, these locations define the primary natural range for this medicinal plant.

Plant Parts

This plant, Astragalus mongholicus (often referred to in medicinal contexts as a variety of Astragalus), relies on its specialized anatomy for its therapeutic properties. The most significant part utilized in traditional medicine is the root, which is harvested and dried for use in various herbal formulas. This woody, tuberous structure serves as the primary storage organ for the plant, concentrating the bioactive compounds and polysaccharides that contribute to its immune-boosting reputation.
