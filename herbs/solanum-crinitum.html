Solanum crinitum

Solanum crinitum, commonly known as the creeping nightshade, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its unique properties make it a significant subject in ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Solanum crinitum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Ultimately, Solanum crinitum is identified under the genus Solanum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning across several distinct tropical regions. Within the Indo-China territory, it can be found specifically in Myanmar. Its presence extends to the Caribbean, where it inhabits Trinidad and Tobago. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout Northern South America. In this South American region, it is documented in French Guiana, Guyana, and Suriname.
