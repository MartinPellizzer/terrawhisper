Rumex spinosus

Rumex spinosus, commonly known as spiny dock, is a hardy perennial herb distinguished by its prickly, erect stems and lance-shaped leaves. Found in disturbed soils and wetlands, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory properties and its ability to treat various skin ailments and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Rumex spinosus
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Europe

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several distinct regions of Europe. In Middle Europe, its presence is documented within Germany. Moving toward the Mediterranean, it can be found in the Southwestern European territories of the Baleares and Corse. The species is also established in the coastal landscapes of Portugal. Finally, its geographical range extends to the island of Sardegna in Southwestern Europe.
