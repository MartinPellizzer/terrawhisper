Soybean, scientifically known as Glycine max, is a versatile legume renowned for its exceptional nutritional and medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds like isoflavones, it offers significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and cardioprotective benefits. This powerhouse crop plays a vital role in supporting hormonal balance and promoting overall metabolic health.

This plant, known scientifically as Glycine max, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Glycine max, is primarily valued for its nutrient-dense seeds, commonly referred to as soya seeds. Within these seeds, the large, fleshy cotyledons serve as a vital storage organ, packing the plant with essential proteins and fats. Furthermore, the plant plays a crucial role in nitrogen fixation through the specialized nodules found on its roots, which interact with soil bacteria to enrich the environment.
