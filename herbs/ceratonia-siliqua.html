Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Ceratonia siliqua, is a versatile evergreen that utilizes various components for its medicinal and nutritional value. The dark green, pinnate leaves are a defining feature of its foliage, while the reproductive cycle involves the dispersal of fine pollen grains to facilitate fertilization. Once matured, the tree produces distinctive, elongated carob pods that are highly valued in both culinary and herbal applications. Whether studied as a single pod or harvested in large quantities as pods, these structures serve as the primary source of the plant's bioactive compounds.
