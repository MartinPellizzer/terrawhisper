Ceratonia siliqua

Locust bean

Ceratonia siliqua, commonly known as the carob tree, is a resilient Mediterranean evergreen prized for its nutrient-dense pods. Beyond its culinary uses, it offers significant medicinal potential. Rich in antioxidants and dietary fiber, carob is traditionally used to support digestive health, manage blood sugar, and provide essential nutritional support.

  • Scientific name: Ceratonia siliqua
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Ceratonia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Ceratonia siliqua, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. In Chinese, it is called 长角豆 (zh), while in Berber (Tamazight), it is referred to as caroubî (wa). In Poland, the species is known as szarańczyn strąkowy (pl), and in the Basque Country, it is called algarrobo (eu). Finally, in French, the plant is commonly known as caroubier (fr).
