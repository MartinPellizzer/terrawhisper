Hyoscyamus reticulatus

Hyoscyamus reticulatus, commonly known as Henbane, is a potent medicinal herb belonging to the Solanaceae family. Renowned for its high concentration of tropane alkaloids like hyoscyamine, it has historically been used for its sedative and antispasmodic properties. However, its extreme toxicity requires careful handling and precise pharmacological application.

  • Scientific name: Hyoscyamus reticulatus
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae, it is classified under the genus Hyoscyamus. This specific taxonomic hierarchy identifies the medicinal species Hyoscyamus reticulum.
