Egyptian henbane

Hyoscyamus reticulatus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (6 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Egyptian henbane, scientifically known as Hyoscyamus reticulatus, is a potent medicinal plant belonging to the Solanaceae family. Rich in tropane alkaloids like hyoscyamine, it has historically been used for its sedative and antispasmodic properties. However, due to its high toxicity, it requires extremely careful handling and professional administration.
