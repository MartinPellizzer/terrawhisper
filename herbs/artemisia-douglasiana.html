Douglas' sagewort, scientifically known as Artemisia douglasiana, is a versatile perennial shrub native to the coastal regions of western North America. Renowned for its aromatic, silver-green foliage, this plant has long been utilized in traditional herbal medicine for its antimicrobial properties and its ability to support digestive health.
