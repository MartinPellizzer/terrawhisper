Helianthemum rotundifolium

Helianthemum rotundifolium, commonly known as the round-leaved rockrose, is a resilient perennial shrub native to Mediterranean landscapes. Renowned for its delicate yellow blooms, this medicinal plant contains bioactive compounds valued in traditional herbalism. It is frequently studied for its potential antioxidant properties and its role in supporting holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Helianthemum rotundifolium
  • Family: Cistaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Malvales and the family Cistaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Helianthemum.

Distribution

This plant, Helianthemum rotundifolium, exhibits a distinct distribution across the Mediterranean region. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within the borders of Spain. The species also extends extensively into Northern Africa, where it can be found in Algeria. Furthermore, it inhabits the territories of Libya and Morocco. Finally, its geographical range is completed by its occurrence in Tunisia.
