Lycium dasystemum

Lycium dasystemum, commonly known as a species of wolfberry, is a medicinal shrub valued in traditional herbal practices. Native to specific arid regions, this plant contains bioactive compounds believed to support vitality and immune function. Its unique botanical properties make it a subject of interest for pharmacological research and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lycium dasystemum
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Lycium, as part of the species Lycium dasystemum.

Distribution

This plant, Lycium dasystemum, is found across several distinct regions within Central Asia. Its natural habitat includes the vast territories of Kazakhstan and Kirgizstan. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout the landscapes of Turkmenistan and Tadzhikistan. It can also be located within the borders of Uzbekistan. Collectively, these five countries represent the primary geographical range for this medicinal species.
