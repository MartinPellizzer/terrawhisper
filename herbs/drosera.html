Drosera

Sundews

Drosera, commonly known as sundews, are fascinating carnivorous plants renowned for their glistening, sticky tentacles. These botanical wonders thrive in nutrient-poor wetlands, capturing insects to supplement their diet. Beyond their captivating beauty, various species hold significant medicinal potential, traditionally used for their antimicrobial and soothing properties in herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Drosera
  • Family: Droseraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Droseraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Drosera.
