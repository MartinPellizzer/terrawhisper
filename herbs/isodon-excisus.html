Isodon excisus

Isodon excisuus, commonly known as the Black Locust tree, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it possesses significant antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This robust species offers unique therapeutic potential, making it a subject of growing interest in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Isodon excisus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it falls under the family Lamiaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Isodon.

Distribution

This plant is native to several specific regions across East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found growing in the Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye territories. Its range also extends significantly into China. Specifically, it is distributed throughout the Manchuria region and China North-Central. This combination of locations defines its natural geographical footprint.
