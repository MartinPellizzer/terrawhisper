Wikstroemia chamaedaphne

Wikstroemia chamaedaphne, commonly known as the Wikstroemia shrub, is a significant medicinal plant found in various subtropical regions. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for developing modern therapeutic treatments.

  • Family: Thymelaeaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
