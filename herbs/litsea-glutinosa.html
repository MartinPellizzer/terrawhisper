Litsea glutinosa

Litsea glutinosa, commonly known as Indian bay leaf, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South and Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it possesses significant therapeutic properties, including anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant effects. Traditionally used in holistic medicine, it serves as a vital natural remedy.

  • Scientific name: Litsea glutinosa
  • Family: Lauraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Litsea.
