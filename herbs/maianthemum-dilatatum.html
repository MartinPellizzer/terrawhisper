Maianthemum dilatatum

False lily of the valley

Maianthemum dilatatum, commonly known as the native peppermint, is a distinctive perennial herb native to Australia. Renowned for its aromatic, mint-like foliage, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its soothing properties. It thrives in moist environments, offering both aesthetic beauty and therapeutic potential in herbal applications.

  • Scientific name: Maianthemum dilatatum
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Maianthemum dilatatum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. It is specifically identified under the genus Maianthemum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several northern regions. In Northern Europe, its presence is specifically noted within Great Britain. Moving eastward into Siberia, the species can be found in the Chita region. Its range extends significantly into the Russian Far East, covering areas such as Amur and Khabarovsk. Additionally, the plant is also distributed throughout the Kamchatka Peninsula.
