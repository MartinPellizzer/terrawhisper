Maianthemum dilatatum

Maianthemum dilatatum, commonly known as the native peppermint, is a distinctive perennial herb native to Australia. Renowned for its aromatic, mint-like foliage, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its soothing properties. It thrives in moist environments, offering both aesthetic beauty and therapeutic potential in herbal applications.
