Dendrobium officinale

Dendrobium officinale, commonly known as the "Golden Cane Orchid," is a highly valued medicinal herb in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its restorative properties, this orchid is used to nourish Yin, support lung health, and boost immunity. It serves as a potent natural tonic for enhancing overall vitality and longevity.

  • Scientific name: Dendrobium officinale
  • Family: Orchidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Orchidaceae and the genus Dendrobium. Specifically, Dendrobium officinale represents a distinct species within this botanical hierarchy.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of Eastern Asia. In China, its presence is specifically noted in the South-Central and Southeast territories. The species also extends its range to include the islands of Japan and the Nansei-shoto archipelago. Furthermore, it can be found growing within the natural landscapes of Taiwan. Together, these locations define the primary geographical footprint of Dendrobium officinale.
