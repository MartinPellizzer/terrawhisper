Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is classified under the family Asparagaceae and is identified by the genus Dracaena.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of East and South Asia. In China, its presence is recorded in the South-Central regions as well as on the island of Hainan. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam. The species also inhabits the territory of Bangladesh within the same subcontinent. Finally, its range extends into the mountainous terrain of the East Himalaya.

Plant Parts

This plant, Dracaena angustifolia, is highly valued for its various anatomical components used in traditional medicine and industry. The most significant part is the red resin, often referred to as dragon's blood, which is extracted from the plant's trunk. This viscous resin is prized for its various therapeutic applications. Additionally, the sturdy wood of the plant can be utilized, while the underground structures, specifically the roots and rhizomes, often contain bioactive compounds essential for medicinal preparations.
