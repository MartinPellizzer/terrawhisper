Erythrophleum fordii

Erythrophleum fordii, commonly known as the "medicinal shrub" in various tropical regions, is a significant botanical species valued for its therapeutic potential. Traditionally used in folk medicine to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments, this plant offers promising pharmacological properties that continue to attract scientific interest in natural medicine.
