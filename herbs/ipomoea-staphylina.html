Ipomoea staphylina

Ipomoea staphylina, commonly known as the cluster morning glory, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant possesses significant bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized for its therapeutic potential in treating various ailments, offering valuable insights into natural pharmacology and herbal healing.

  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
