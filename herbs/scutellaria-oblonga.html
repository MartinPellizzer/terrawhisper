Scutellaria oblonga

Scutelariia oblonga, commonly known as Huang Qin in Traditional Chinese Medicine, is a perennial herb valued for its potent bioactive compounds. Rich in baicalin, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. This versatile medicinal plant is frequently utilized to treat respiratory infections, heat-related illnesses, and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Scutellaria oblonga
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Scutellaria.
