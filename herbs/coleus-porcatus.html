Coleus porcatus

Coleus porcatus, commonly known as Coleus, is a vibrant medicinal plant prized for its striking foliage and therapeutic properties. Widely used in traditional medicine, it possesses antioxidant and anti-inflammatory qualities. Often cultivated for ornamental beauty, this versatile herb offers significant potential in holistic wellness and natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Coleus porcatus
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Coleus.
