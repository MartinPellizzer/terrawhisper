Kikul oil plant, scientifically known as Salvadora persica, is a versatile shrub native to arid regions of the Middle East and Asia. Renowned for its aromatic seeds, it produces high-quality essential oils used extensively in traditional medicine, perfumery, and flavoring, offering significant therapeutic benefits and unique olfactory properties.
