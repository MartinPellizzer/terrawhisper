Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Salvadora, is a hardy shrub where various components offer significant medicinal value. The roots are often utilized for their therapeutic properties, while the stem serves as a vital structural support for the plant's growth. In many traditional practices, the root itself is processed to extract essential compounds used in dental hygiene. Additionally, the small fruit produced by the plant can be studied for its nutritional and bioactive content, and even the slender twigs are frequently employed as natural toothbrushes to maintain oral health.
