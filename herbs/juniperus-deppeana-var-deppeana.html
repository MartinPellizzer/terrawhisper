Juniperus deppeana var. deppeana

Juniperus depapeana var. depapeana, commonly known as the Depapean Juniper, is a resilient coniferous species native to Mexico. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and berry-like cones, this medicinal plant has been traditionally utilized in folk remedies to support digestive health and possess potent antimicrobial and antioxidant properties.

  • Scientific name: Juniperus deppeana var. deppeana
  • Family: Cupressaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Pinidae and order Pinales, eventually reaching the family Cupressaceae. Within this lineage, it is specifically identified by the genus Juniperus.
