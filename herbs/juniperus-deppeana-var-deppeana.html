Alligator juniper, scientifically known as Juniperus depapeana var. depapeana, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the arid landscapes of the American Southwest. Renowned for its rugged, alligator-like bark, this hardy plant plays a vital role in desert ecosystems, offering medicinal potential and providing essential habitat for wildlife.
