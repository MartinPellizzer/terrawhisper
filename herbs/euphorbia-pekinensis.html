Euphorbia pekinensis

Euphorbia pekinensis, commonly known as Peking spurge, is a perennial herb valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is primarily utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various internal maladies. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique phytochemical properties and medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia pekinensis
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Euphorbia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across East Asia. In the northernmost reaches of its habitat, it can be found within the Russian Far East, specifically in the Primorye region. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the landscapes of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout the South-Central parts of China. Finally, its range reaches as far south as the island province of Hainan.
