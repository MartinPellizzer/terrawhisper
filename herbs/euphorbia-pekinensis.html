Euphorbia pekinenensis Boiss. (1860) non Rupr. (1859), scientifically known as Euphorbia pekinesis, is a perennial herbaceous plant belonging to the Euphorbiaceae family. Native to East Asia, this medicinal species is highly valued in traditional practices for its bioactive compounds, which offer significant therapeutic potential in treating various inflammatory conditions.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across East Asia. In the northernmost reaches of its habitat, it can be found within the Russian Far East, specifically in the Primorye region. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the landscapes of Inner Mongolia and Manchuria. Furthermore, the species is distributed throughout the South-Central parts of China. Finally, its range reaches as far south as the island province of Hainan.

Plant Parts

This plant, Euphorbia pekinenensis, is a medicinal species where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. The most common medicinal preparation involves the radix, which refers to the roots of the plant. This root system is harvested and processed to serve as a primary ingredient in traditional remedies. In addition to the underground structures, the leaf and the entire aerial part of the plant may also be collected for different applications, ensuring that the whole biological profile of the herb can be utilized in botanical medicine.
