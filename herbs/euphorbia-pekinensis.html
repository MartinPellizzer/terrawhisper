Euphorbia pekinenensis Boiss. (1860) non Rupr. (1859), scientifically known as Euphorbia pekinesis, is a perennial herbaceous plant belonging to the Euphorbiaceae family. Native to East Asia, this medicinal species is highly valued in traditional practices for its bioactive compounds, which offer significant therapeutic potential in treating various inflammatory conditions.
