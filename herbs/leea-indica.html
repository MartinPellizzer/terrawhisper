Bandicoot berry, scientifically known as Leea indica, is a versatile shrub native to tropical Asia. Renowned for its vibrant red berries and delicate flowers, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditional medicine utilizes its leaves and roots to treat various ailments, showcasing its profound importance in holistic healing practices.
