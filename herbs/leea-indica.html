Leea indica

Bandicoot berry

Leea indica, commonly known as the Bandicoot Berry, is a versatile tropical shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to Asia and Oceania, it features vibrant red berries and distinctive foliage. Its leaves and roots are frequently utilized for their antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic properties to treat various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Leea indica
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Vitales, falling into the family Vitaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Leea.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various parts of the Indian Subcontinent. Within this region, it can be found growing natively throughout the diverse landscapes of India. Its presence also extends to the tropical settings of the Laccadive Islands. Additionally, the species is well-established in the environmental conditions of Sri Lanka. These locations highlight the specific geographical range occupied by this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Bandicoot Berry, utilizes its leaves for various medicinal purposes. The leaves of Leea indica are typically characterized by their serrated margins and ovate shape, often serving as the primary part used in traditional remedies. When prepared as a decoction or a paste, these leaves are frequently employed to treat skin inflammations, wounds, and even digestive ailments, demonstrating the functional importance of the foliage in the plant's therapeutic profile.
