Solanum stramoniifolium var. stramoniifolium

Solanum stramoniifolium var. stramoniifolium, commonly known as a specialized variety of nightshade, is a medicinal plant valued for its unique bioactive properties. Found in specific ecological niches, it contains potent alkaloids used in traditional healing practices to address various ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Solanum stramoniifolium var. stramoniifolium
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales, falling into the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Solanum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it is documented to grow in India, specifically in the state of Assam. Its presence extends across the Atlantic to the Caribbean, where it can be found in Trinidad and Tobago. In Central America, the species is also established in both Nicaragua and Panamá.
