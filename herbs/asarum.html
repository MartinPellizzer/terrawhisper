Wild ginger, scientifically known as Asarum, is a genus of perennial herbs prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it contains essential oils with potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat digestive issues and respiratory ailments, this resilient plant remains a significant botanical treasure.
