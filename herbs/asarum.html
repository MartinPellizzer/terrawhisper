Asarum

Asarum, commonly known as Wild Ginger, is a genus of perennial herbs cherished in traditional medicine. Renowned for its aromatic rhizomes, it possesses potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. Historically used to treat digestive issues and respiratory ailments, this botanical treasure remains a significant subject in ethnopharmacological studies worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Asarum
  • Family: Aristolochiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Piperales and the family Aristolochiaceae. Specifically, it is identified under the genus Asarum.
