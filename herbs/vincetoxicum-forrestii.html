Vincetoxicum forrestii

Vincetoxicum forrestii, commonly known as Forrestier's vincetoxicum, is a rare medicinal plant valued for its unique phytochemical profile. Belonging to the Apocynaceae family, it contains diverse alkaloids with significant therapeutic potential. Researchers study this species for its bioactive compounds, which may offer promising applications in modern pharmacological developments.
