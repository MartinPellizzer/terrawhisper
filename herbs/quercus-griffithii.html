What's the taxonomical classification of Quercus griffithii?

Quercus griffithii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Fagales. As a member of the family Fagaceae, it falls under the genus Quercus, and its specific biological designation is identified by the species name griffithii.

The plant produces small, inconspicuous flowers that eventually develop into nut-like acorns. These acorns are typically contained within a shallow, scaly cupule.
