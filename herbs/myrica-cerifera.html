Myrica cerifera, scientifically known as Myrica cerifera, is commonly called wax myrtle or bayberry. This resilient evergreen shrub is celebrated for its aromatic berries, which produce a unique, flammable wax. Historically utilized for candle making and traditional medicine, it remains a vital component of coastal wetland ecosystems.
