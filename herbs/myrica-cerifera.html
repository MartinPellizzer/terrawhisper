Myrica cerifera

Myrica cerifera, commonly known as wax myrtle, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to the southeastern United States. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and resinous berries, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is traditionally used in medicinal preparations to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Myrica cerifera
  • Family: Myricaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Fagales and the family Myrricaceae, specifically falling within the genus Myrica.
