Stereospermum colais

Stereosperma colais, commonly known as the "Tree of Life" in certain tropical regions, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory qualities, it is traditionally used in herbal remedies to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and various systemic infections.
