Phyllanthus ussuriensis

Phyllanthus ussuriensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb, holds a vital place in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its diverse pharmacological properties, this plant is frequently utilized for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory effects. Exploring its botanical characteristics and therapeutic potential offers valuable insights into natural medicine's expanding capabilities.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus ussuriensis
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Phyllanthus, specifically as the species Phyllanthus ussuriensis.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented in both the Khabarovsk and Primorye territories. Moving into China, it can be found throughout the Manchuria region. Furthermore, its range extends into the China North-Central areas. Finally, the species is also established within the China South-Central provinces.
