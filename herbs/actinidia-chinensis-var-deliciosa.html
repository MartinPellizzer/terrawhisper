Actinidia chinensis var. deliciosa

Actinidia chinensis var. deliciosas, commonly known as the Hayward kiwifruit, is a nutrient-dense medicinal plant celebrated for its high vitamin C and antioxidant content. Renowned for supporting digestive health and boosting immunity, this vine produces fleshy fruits essential in traditional and modern dietary therapies to promote overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Actinidia chinensis var. deliciosa
  • Family: Actinidiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Actinidia chinensis var. deliciosia, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ericales and the family Actinidiaceae. It is further categorized under the genus Actinidia.
