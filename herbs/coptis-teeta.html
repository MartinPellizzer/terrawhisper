Indian goldthread, scientifically known as Coptis teeta, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive properties. Renowned for its high berberine content, this plant is frequently utilized to treat digestive ailments, inflammation, and infections, offering a natural approach to holistic healing and wellness.
