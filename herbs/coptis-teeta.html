Coptis teeta

Coptis teeta, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in traditional Eastern practices, holds profound pharmacological importance. Renowned for its rich alkaloid content, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments. Scientific research continues to explore its potent bioactive compounds for modern therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Coptis teeta
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Coptis teeta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Coptis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. Within China, it can be found specifically in the South-Central areas as well as in Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it grows in Assam and the East Himalaya. Additionally, the species is distributed throughout the Indo-China region, particularly in Myanmar. These diverse locations highlight the varied habitats where this medicinal plant thrives.
