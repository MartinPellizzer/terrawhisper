Common knotgrass, scientifically known as Polygonum aviculare, is a resilient, low-growing annual herb found globally in disturbed soils. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, it possesses astringent and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to support skin health and wound healing, this versatile plant remains a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.
