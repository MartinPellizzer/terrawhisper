Polygonum aviculare

Polygonum aviculare, commonly known as knotgrass, is a resilient, creeping herb found globally in disturbed soils. Valued in traditional medicine, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Often used to support skin health and digestive wellness, this versatile plant offers significant therapeutic potential in natural healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Polygonum aviculare
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Specifically, it is a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. It is further categorized under the family Polygonaceae within the genus Polygonum.
