Lathyros quinquenervius belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Fabales. This plant is a member of the family Fabaceae, which places it within the genus Lathyros, specifically identified by the species name quinquenervius.

What side effects this plant can have?

Consuming these compounds can lead to immediate gastrointestinal distress including nausea and vomiting. Individuals who eat the plant may also experience intense abdominal pain and persistent diarrhea. These toxic effects occur because the chemical compounds interfere with the body's ability to utilize oxygen at a cellular level. Therefore, any part of the plant should be avoided to prevent these dangerous health complications.
