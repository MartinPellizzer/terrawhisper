Brazilian pepper tree, scientifically known as Schinus terebinthifolia, is a versatile medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is traditionally used to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and respiratory conditions. This resilient species offers significant therapeutic potential in modern ethnobotanical research.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Brazilian peppertree, utilizes various components for its medicinal and practical applications. The small, reddish-pink fruits are highly valued and are often used as a spice or for their aromatic properties. Additionally, the medicinal potential of the species is often associated with the leaves, particularly when preparing a traditional infusion or decoction, sometimes referred to in specific contexts as a "Raddi leaf" preparation, to harness its bioactive compounds.
