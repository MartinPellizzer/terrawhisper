Schinus terebinthifolia

Schinus terebinthifolia, commonly known as the Brazilian peppertree, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to South America. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and berries possess potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This resilient plant offers significant therapeutic potential for treating various ailments through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Schinus terebinthifolia
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Sapindales and the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Schinus.
