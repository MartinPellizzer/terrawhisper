Dichanthium foveolatum

Dichanthium fooveolatum, commonly known as velvet grass, is a resilient perennial herb valued in traditional medicine. Found primarily in tropical regions, it possesses significant bioactive properties. This plant is frequently utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, serving as a vital resource in ethnobotanical studies and herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Dichanthium foveolatum
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
