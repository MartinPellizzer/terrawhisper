Helicia cochinchinensis

What's the taxonomical classification of Helicia cochinchinensis?

Helicia cochinchinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Proteales and falls within the family Proteaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Helicia, reaching its unique designation at the species level as cochinchinenis.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Helicia cochinchinensis has woody stems with elliptical to oblong leaves that possess entire margins and prominent pinnate venation. The foliage is typically dark green and leathery in texture, reflecting its evergreen nature. Small, actinomorphic flowers emerge from the axils, displaying white petals and numerous stamens. These blossoms eventually develop into fleshy, rounded drupes that serve as the plant's primary fruit structure. The overall architecture of the plant forms a dense shrub or small tree characterized by a spreading canopy.
