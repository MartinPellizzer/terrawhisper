Agave sisalana

Agave sisalana, commonly known as sisal, is a perennial succulent native to Mexico. Renowned for its long, tough fibers, it is a vital industrial resource used in rope and textile production. Beyond its commercial utility, this resilient plant holds significant botanical importance due to its hardiness and rapid growth.

  • Scientific name: Agave sisalana
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asparagales, falling within the family Asparagaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Agave.
