What's the taxonomical classification of Microlepia hancei?

Microlepia hancei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following the order Polypodiales, the plant is a member of the family Dennstaedtiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Microlepia and the species hancei.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Microlepia hancei has bipinnate-pinnatifid fronds that typically measure between 20 and 60 centimeters in length with stipes that are covered in brownish scales. The pinnules are characterized by oblong to lanceolate shapes and feature finely serrated margins. These leaves are arranged in a basal rosette and possess a distinctively textured surface due to the presence of minute hairs. The venation pattern within the leaflets is delicate and serves to distribute nutrients across the expanded blade. Small sori are located on the underside of the pinnules, often protected by a thin indusium.
