This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Myrtales, it is a member of the family Myrtaceae. Specifically, it is categorized under the genus Calytrix as Calytrix ecalycata.
