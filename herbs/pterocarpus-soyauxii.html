Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern centered primarily within the tropical regions of Africa. In West Tropical Africa, its presence has been documented in Nigeria. Moving into the West-Central Tropical Africa zone, the species can be found in the Central African Republic and Cameroon. It also inhabits the territories of Congo and Cabinda within this same central region. Overall, these locations define the known geographical range for Pterocarpus soyauxii.
