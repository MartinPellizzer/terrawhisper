Monocarpia maingayi

Monocarpia maingayi, commonly known as a vital medicinal herb, holds significant value in traditional healing practices. Found in specific ecological niches, this plant is prized for its unique phytochemical properties. Research into its bioactive compounds offers promising potential for developing new therapeutic treatments and addressing various health conditions naturally.

  • Scientific name: Monocarpia maingayi
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Monocarpia maingayi, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Magnoliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Annonaceae and falls under the genus Monocarpia.

Common Names

This plant, Monocarpia maingayi, is known by various regional names across different linguistic groups. In English, it is commonly referred to as the single-carp plant, while in Chinese, it is known as dan ye cao. In the local context of Vietnam, it is called thuy thuc.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range spanning across several distinct regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, its presence is recorded in Thailand. Moving into the Malesia zone, it can be found throughout the Malay Peninsula. Additionally, the species is distributed across the island of Sumatera. These locations highlight the tropical habitats where the plant naturally thrives.
