Achillea atrata

Black yarrow

Achillea atrata, commonly known as the dark yarrow, is a perennial herb distinguished by its deep green foliage and delicate white flower clusters. Native to mountainous regions, this resilient plant belongs to the Asteraceae family and is valued in traditional herbalism for its potential therapeutic and medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Achillea atrata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Asterales, it is a member of the family Asteraceae. Its taxonomic identity is further specified under the genus Achillea, known as Achillea atrata.
