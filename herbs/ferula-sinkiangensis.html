Ferula sinkiangensis

Ferula sinkianensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb from the Apiaceae family, is native to the arid regions of China. Renowned for its therapeutic potential, this plant contains bioactive compounds utilized in traditional medicine to address various ailments, offering promising pharmacological properties for modern scientific research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ferula sinkiangensis
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Ferula.
