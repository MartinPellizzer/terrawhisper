Aesculus parviflora, scientifically known as Aesculus parviflora, is the delicate dwarf buckeye, native to the mountains of East Asia. Renowned for its stunning, weeping panicles of white flowers, this ornamental plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique properties and its ability to thrive in shaded woodland environments.

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range that spans across both Europe and North America. In Middle Europe, it can be found growing within the borders of Germany. Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the dwarf buckeye, features distinctive foliage that plays a role in its botanical identification. The leaf of Aesculus parviflora is characterized by its palmate structure, typically consisting of five to seven leaflets that radiate from a single point at the end of the petiole. These leaflets are generally ovate in shape with serrated margins, providing a lush, green canopy that contributes to the overall aesthetic and biological function of the shrub.
