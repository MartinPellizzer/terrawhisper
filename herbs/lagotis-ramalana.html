What's the taxonomical classification of Lagotis ramalana?

Lagotis ramalana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant is placed in the order Lamiales and belongs to the family Plantaginaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Lagotis, and its unique species designation, ramalana.
