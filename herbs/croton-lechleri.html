Croton lechleri

Sangre de grado

Croton lechleri, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, is valued for its diverse therapeutic potential. Often utilized in traditional medicine, this plant contains bioactive compounds believed to possess anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties, offering significant interest for pharmacological research and natural healing practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Croton lechleri
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Croton.

Plant Parts

This plant, Croton lechleri, is renowned for its unique biological characteristics, most notably the production of a distinctive red sap that flows from its bark. This liquid, often referred to as "dragon's blood," is a potent resin that is harvested for its numerous therapeutic properties. While the medicinal value is primarily concentrated in this viscous substance, the overall health and chemical composition of the plant are supported by its lush green leaves. Each individual leaf serves as a vital component of the organism, contributing to the synthesis of the compounds found within the sap.
