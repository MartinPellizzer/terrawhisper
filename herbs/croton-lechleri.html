Sangre de Grado, scientifically known as Croton leclyceri, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to the Amazon rainforest. Renowned for its deep red, latex-like sap, it possesses potent healing properties. Traditionally used to treat wounds, inflammation, and infections, this botanical treasure remains a cornerstone of natural holistic medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, Croton lechleri, is renowned for its unique biological characteristics, most notably the production of a distinctive red sap that flows from its bark. This liquid, often referred to as "dragon's blood," is a potent resin that is harvested for its numerous therapeutic properties. While the medicinal value is primarily concentrated in this viscous substance, the overall health and chemical composition of the plant are supported by its lush green leaves. Each individual leaf serves as a vital component of the organism, contributing to the synthesis of the compounds found within the sap.
