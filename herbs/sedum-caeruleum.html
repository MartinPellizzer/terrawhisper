Sedum caeruleum

Sedum caeruleum, commonly known as Sky Blue Stonecrop, is a resilient perennial succulent native to North America. Characterized by its striking star-shaped blue flowers, this plant thrives in rocky, well-drained environments. While primarily valued in landscaping for its drought tolerance, it holds historical significance in traditional herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Sedum caeruleum
  • Family: Crassulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Saxifragales, it is classified under the family Crassulaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Sedum.
