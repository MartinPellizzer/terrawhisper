Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several distinct regions of the African continent and its surrounding islands. Within the Macaronesia archipelago, its distribution includes the islands of Cape Verde. Moving to the mainland, it is found throughout West Tropical Africa, specifically in Guinea, Liberia, and Sierra Leone. Additionally, its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it inhabits the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Together, these locations illustrate a broad geographical footprint spanning from island chains to central tropical forests.
