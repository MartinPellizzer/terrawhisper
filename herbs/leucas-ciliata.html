Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found specifically within the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is native to the state of Assam. Additionally, the species is widely distributed throughout Bangladesh. Finally, it is also recorded growing within the East Himalaya region.

Plant Parts

This plant, Leucas ciliata, possesses distinct morphological features in its vegetative structures that are often utilized in traditional medicine. The leaf is typically characterized by its opposite arrangement, an ovate to lanceolate shape, and a slightly serrated margin, serving as a primary site for various bioactive compounds. Supporting the foliage is the stem, which is generally erect, quadrangular in cross-section, and often covered with fine hairs, providing the necessary structural integrity for the plant's growth.
