Ipomoea murucoides

Ipomoea murucoides, commonly known as the "murucoides" or various local names, is a versatile medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Often found in tropical regions, this species is traditionally utilized to treat diverse ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential for developing natural remedies and supporting holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Ipomoea murucoides
  • Family: Convolvulaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Solanales. Ultimately, Ipomoea murucoides is a member of the Convolvulaceae family and falls under the genus Ipomoea.
