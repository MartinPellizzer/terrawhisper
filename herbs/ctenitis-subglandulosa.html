Ctenitis subglandulosa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida, it is situated under the subclass Polypodiinae and falls within the order Polypodiales. Its taxonomic lineage continues through the family Polypodiaceae and the genus Cteniitis, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species subglandulosa.
