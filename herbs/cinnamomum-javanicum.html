Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse tropical landscapes of the Malesia region. Within this vast area, it can be found growing in the lush forests of Borneo. Its presence is also well-documented throughout the island of Jawa. Furthermore, the species inhabits various parts of Malaya. Finally, its geographical range extends to the fertile lands of Sumatera.
