Meconopsis quintuplinervia

Meconopsis quintuplinervia, commonly known as the five-lined poppy, is a rare and striking medicinal plant native to high-altitude regions. Characterized by its distinctive foliage, this perennial offers significant ethnobotanical interest. Researchers study its unique chemical constituents for potential therapeutic applications, highlighting its importance in traditional healing practices.

  • Scientific name: Meconopsis quintuplinervia
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Papaveraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Meconopsis.
