Smallanthus uvedalia

Smallanthus uvedalia, commonly known as tocotopherine or tocotrienol-rich bran, is a medicinal plant prized for its potent antioxidant properties. Often derived from the seeds of the bittertoothed herb, it serves as a concentrated source of tocotrienols, which support cardiovascular health, neuroprotection, and overall cellular vitality in various therapies.

  • Scientific name: Smallanthus uvedalia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Smallanthus.
