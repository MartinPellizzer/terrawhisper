Cyclamen

Cyclamen, commonly known as sowbread, is a versatile genus of flowering plants prized for their striking, upturned blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, various species hold historical significance in traditional medicine. Used to treat respiratory ailments and skin conditions, these plants offer a fascinating blend of aesthetics and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Cyclamen
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Primulaceae and the genus Cyclamen.

Common Names

This plant, belonging to the genus Cyclamen, is known by various names across different Chinese linguistic contexts. In Simplified Chinese, it is referred to as 仙客来属, which is the standard designation used in mainland China (zh-cn) and Singapore (zh-sg). In Traditional Chinese, the name is written as 仙客來屬, a form commonly used in Taiwan (zh-hans) and Hong Kong (zh-hk). Regardless of the specific script or regional variation, these names all identify this ornamental and medicinal genus.
