Sowbreads, scientifically known as Cyclamen, is a versatile genus of flowering plants cherished for their striking, reflexed petals and decorative foliage. While primarily ornamental, various species possess traditional medicinal properties used in folk healing. However, caution is essential, as many parts of the plant contain toxic compounds.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving into Middle Europe, the species is also present in Belgium. Its range extends further east to include the areas of Austria and the Czechia-Slovakia region. Together, these locations define the primary habitats for this medicinal flora.
