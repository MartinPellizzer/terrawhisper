Puccinellia maritima

Puccinellia maritima, commonly known as sea couch, is a resilient salt-tolerant grass native to coastal salt marshes. Valued for its ability to thrive in saline environments, it plays a crucial role in stabilizing shorelines. While primarily ecological, its unique biochemical properties offer intriguing potential for specialized medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Puccinellia maritima
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Poaales, belonging to the family Poaceae. Within this family, it is specifically categorized under the genus Puccinellia.
