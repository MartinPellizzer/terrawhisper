Saltmarsh grass, scientifically known as Puccinellia maritima, is a resilient halophyte essential to coastal ecosystems. Thriving in saline environments, it stabilizes mudflats and provides critical habitat for diverse fauna. Beyond its ecological role, this hardy grass holds significant potential in traditional medicine and pharmacological research for its bioactive compounds.
