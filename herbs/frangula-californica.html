Frangula californica, scientifically known as Frangula californica, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to the coastal regions of western North America. Renowned for its medicinal potential, this plant contains anthraquinone compounds often utilized in traditional herbal remedies to support digestive health and promote natural laxative effects within the body.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution that spans several distinct regions across the Pacific and the United States. Within the North-Central Pacific, it can be found growing in Hawaii. Moving to the Northwestern U.S.A., its range extends into the state of Oregon. Its presence is also notable throughout the Southwestern U.S.A., specifically covering the states of Arizona, California, and Nevada. Consequently, the species occupies a wide variety of habitats across these western territories.

Plant Parts

This plant, Frangula californica, is characterized by its distinctive foliage, where the leaf serves as a primary component for identification and potential medicinal study. The leaves are typically elliptical or oblong in shape, featuring an entire margin and a somewhat leathery texture that helps the shrub thrive in coastal environments. In the context of traditional or botanical uses, the chemical compounds often concentrated within the leaf tissue are of particular interest to researchers studying the plant's bioactive properties.
