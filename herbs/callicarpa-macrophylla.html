Callicarpa macrophylla

Callicarpa macrophylla, commonly known as the large-leaf beautyberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its vibrant purple berries, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering a rich source of bioactive compounds for therapeutic use.

  • Scientific name: Callicarpa macrophylla
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Callicarpa.
