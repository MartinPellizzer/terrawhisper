Mikania cordata

Mikania cordata, commonly known as Heartleaf Climbing Vine, is a vigorous tropical climber native to Central and South America. Renowned for its cordate leaves, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, promote wound healing, and address various inflammatory conditions through its bioactive properties.

  • Scientific name: Mikania cordata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Mikania.
