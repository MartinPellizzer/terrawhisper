Stachys inflata

Stachy's inflata, commonly known as Chinese Bugleweed, is a versatile medicinal herb deeply rooted in traditional East Asian practices. Renowned for its cooling properties, this plant is frequently utilized to treat inflammation, clear heat, and soothe respiratory ailments, offering a natural approach to promoting holistic wellness and balance.
