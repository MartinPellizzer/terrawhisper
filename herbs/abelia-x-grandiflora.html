Plant Parts

This plant, Abelia × grandiflora, is primarily valued in ornamental gardening for its profuse and delicate flowers. These blossoms appear in clusters, typically featuring trumpet-shaped petals that range in color from pure white to soft pink. While the plant is most famous for its aesthetic floral display, the flowers are the central component of its visual appeal, providing continuous color throughout much of the growing season.
