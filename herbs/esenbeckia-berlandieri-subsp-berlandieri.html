Esenbeckia berlandieri subsp. berlandieri

Esenbeckia berlandieri subsp. berlandieri, commonly known as the Berlandieri's Senna, is a significant medicinal shrub native to Mexico. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and inflammatory conditions, offering valuable insights for modern pharmacological research.
