Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Southeast Asia. Within the Indo-China region, it can be found growing in Cambodia, Myanmar, and Thailand. Its presence in the Indo-China zone also extends to the Nicobar Islands. Furthermore, the species is distributed within the Malesia region, specifically occurring on the island of Borneo. Together, these locations highlight the plant's diverse habitat across diverse tropical territories.
