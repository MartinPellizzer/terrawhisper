Lepidium meyenii · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (619 studies) · ★★★★★

Maca, scientifically known as Lepidium meyenii, is a resilient cruciferous plant native to the high altitudes of the Peruvian Andes. Renowned for its potent adaptogenic properties, this "superfood" has been used for centuries to enhance vitality, balance hormones, and support overall energy levels and reproductive health naturally.
