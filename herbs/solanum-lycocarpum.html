Wolf apple, scientifically known as Solanum lycocarpum, is a prominent medicinal fruit native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including digestive issues and inflammation. This nutrient-rich plant offers significant potential for pharmacological research and sustainable nutritional development.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as lobeira or wolf apple, is highly valued for its medicinal properties derived from various components. The most significant part used in traditional medicine is the fruit, which undergoes distinct changes in composition as it matures. The unripe fruits are often used in specific preparations, while the ripe fruits are widely consumed or processed due to their nutritional and therapeutic potential. Ultimately, whether utilizing the unripe fruits or the ripe fruits, the entire fruit serves as a vital resource in folk remedies.
