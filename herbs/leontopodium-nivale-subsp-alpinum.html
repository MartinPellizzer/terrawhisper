Alpine Edelweiss, scientifically known as Leontopodium nivale subsp. alpinum, is a resilient perennial herb found in high-altitude rocky terrains. Renowned for its woolly, star-shaped white bracts, this rare botanical treasure holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its potential antioxidant and anti-inflammatory medicinal properties.
