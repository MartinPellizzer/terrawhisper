Ceriops decandra

Ceriops decandra, commonly known as the mangrove buttonwood, is a significant coastal plant found in tropical Indo-Pacific regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this mangrove species are utilized in traditional practices to treat ailments such as inflammation, skin infections, and digestive issues through bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Ceriops decandra
  • Family: Rhizophoraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic details place it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, specifically within the family Rhizophoraceae. Finally, its scientific classification is completed under the genus Ceriops, with the specific species being Ceriops decandra.
