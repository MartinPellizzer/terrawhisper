Ensete superbum

Ensete superbum, commonly known as the Ethiopian false banana, is a vital perennial plant native to East Africa. Beyond its role as a staple food source, it holds significant medicinal potential. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized to treat ailments, offering unique biochemical properties for pharmacological study.
