couch grass

Elymus repens · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (396 studies) · ★★★★★

Couch grass, scientifically known as Elymus repens, is a perennial plant valued in traditional herbalism for its diverse medicinal properties. Often considered a persistent weed, its rhizomes possess diuretic and anti-inflammatory qualities. Historically used to support kidney function and digestive health, it remains a subject of botanical interest.

Plant Parts

This plant, known commonly as couch grass, possesses a highly resilient structure characterized by its extensive underground system and vigorous growth. The primary medicinal and structural components include the creeping rhizome, which spreads through the soil to form a dense network, and the aerial part, which consists of the leafy green shoots that emerge above the ground. Together, these parts allow the plant to thrive in various environments and serve as the basis for its various botanical applications.
