Couch grass, scientifically known as Elymus repens, is a perennial plant valued in traditional herbalism for its diverse medicinal properties. Often considered a persistent weed, its rhizomes possess diuretic and anti-inflammatory qualities. Historically used to support kidney function and digestive health, it remains a subject of botanical interest.
