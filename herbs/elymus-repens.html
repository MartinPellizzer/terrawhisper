Elymus repens

Couch grass

Elymus repens, commonly known as couch grass or quackgrass, is a resilient perennial herb found globally. While often viewed as a persistent weed in agriculture, it holds significant medicinal value. Traditionally, its rhizomes have been utilized in herbal medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, digestive issues, and various skin ailments.

  • Scientific name: Elymus repens
  • Family: Poaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Poales, it is classified under the family Poaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Elymus.
