Polygonatum odoratum

Polygonatum odoratum, commonly known as Solomon's seal, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine. Characterized by its elegant, arching stems and bell-shaped white flowers, it thrives in woodland settings. Historically used to treat inflammation and promote healing, this versatile plant remains a fascinating subject of botanical study.

  • Scientific name: Polygonatum odoratum
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
