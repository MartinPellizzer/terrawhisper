Angular Solomon's-Seal, scientifically known as Polygonatum odoratum, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine for its restorative properties. Characterized by arching stems and delicate, bell-shaped white flowers, this woodland plant has historically been used to support respiratory health, nourish yin, and promote overall vitality and healing.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as fragrant Solomon's seal, is characterized by its distinctive underground structure. The medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in the rhizomes, which serve as the plant's storage organs. These fleshy, creeping rhizome structures allow the plant to spread horizontally through the soil. Because of this rhizomatous growth habit, the plant forms dense colonies over time. When harvesting the roots for therapeutic use, one is actually collecting these thickened, nutrient-rich horizontal stems that anchor the plant and store its vital energy.
