What's the taxonomical classification of Lithocarpus paniculatus?

Lithocarpus paniculatus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant is a member of the order Fagales and falls under the family Fagaceae. It is specifically categorized within the genus Lithocarpus, eventually reaching its unique designation as the species paniculatus.
