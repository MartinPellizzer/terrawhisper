What's the taxonomical classification of Begonia wilsonii?

Begonia wilsonii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the taxonomic order Cucurbitales. This plant is a representative of the family Begoniaceae, specifically placed within the genus Begonia, with its unique classification completed by its specific designation as the species wilsonii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?
