Matricaria, scientifically known as Matricaria, is a revered medicinal herb celebrated for its calming properties. Commonly called chamomile, this delicate flowering plant offers soothing relief for digestive issues, skin irritations, and sleep disturbances. Its gentle essence makes it a versatile staple in traditional wellness and modern herbal remedies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the northern reaches of Europe. It can be found growing in the landscapes of Denmark and Finland. Additionally, its presence extends to the island of Iceland. The species is also documented within the territory of Great Britain. Finally, its geographical range includes the Faroe Islands, known as Føroyar.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as chamomile, is most valued for its delicate, daisy-like flower. The medicinal properties are primarily concentrated within the flower heads, which consist of bright yellow central discs surrounded by slender white petals. When harvested, these flowers are often dried to create soothing teas and topical remedies, making the flower the most essential component of the plant for therapeutic use.
