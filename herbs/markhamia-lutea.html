Markhamia lutea, scientifically known as Markhamia lutea, is a striking deciduous tree native to tropical Africa. Renowned for its vibrant, trumpet-shaped yellow flowers, it is highly valued in traditional medicine. Various parts of the plant are utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to inflammatory conditions.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Ghana and Ivory Coast. Its presence extends further east through Togo and into Nigeria. Additionally, the species reaches into West-Central Tropical Africa.
