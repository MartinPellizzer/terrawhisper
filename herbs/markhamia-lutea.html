Markhamia lutea

Markhamia lutea, commonly known as the Yellow Markhamia, is a striking deciduous tree celebrated for its brilliant, trumpet-shaped yellow flowers. Native to tropical Africa, this ornamental species is valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, often utilized to treat various ailments through its potent bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Markhamia lutea
  • Family: Bignoniaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is further categorized into the family Bignoniaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Markhamia, which includes the specific species Markhamia lutea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found growing within the borders of Ghana and Ivory Coast. Its presence extends further east through Togo and into Nigeria. Additionally, the species reaches into West-Central Tropical Africa. Specifically, one documented location for this medicinal plant is Burundi.
