Artemisia giraldii var. giraldii

Artemisia giraldii var. giraldii, commonly known as a rare medicinal wormwood, is a distinctive botanical variety valued for its unique phytochemical profile. Found in specific ecological niches, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in traditional herbal medicine, owing to its potent bioactive compounds and antioxidant properties.
