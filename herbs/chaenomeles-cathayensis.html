Chaenomeles cathayensis

Chaenomeles cathayensis, commonly known as Chinese Melia or Chaeno, is a deciduous shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asia, its leaves and bark possess potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat fever, inflammation, and various skin ailments, it remains a significant subject in ethnopharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Chaenomeles cathayensis
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
