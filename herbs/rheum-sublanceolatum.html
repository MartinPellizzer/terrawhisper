Plant Parts

This plant, Rheum lanceolatum, is highly valued in traditional medicine, particularly for the therapeutic properties found within its underground structures. The medicinal efficacy of the species is primarily concentrated in the rhizoma and the radix, which are harvested and processed to extract bioactive compounds. While the radix refers to the main taproot system that anchors the plant, the rhizoma consists of the thickened, horizontal stem-like structures that store nutrients and essential medicinal constituents. Together, these parts serve as the primary source for pharmacological applications.
