Bupleurum wenchuanense

Bupleurum wenchuaneense, commonly known as a specialized member of the Apiaceae family, is a medicinal herb native to specific high-altitude regions. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this plant is studied for its potential therapeutic properties, offering promising insights into traditional herbal medicine and modern pharmacological applications.
