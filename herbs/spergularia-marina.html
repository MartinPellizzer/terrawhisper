Spergularia marina

What's the taxonomical classification of Spergularia marina?

Spergularia marina belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Caryophyllales. This plant is further categorized into the family Caryophyllaceae, specifically within the genus Spergularia, and is identified by the species name marina.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Spergularia marina has prostrate, succulent stems that form low-growing mats and leaves that are linear, fleshy, and oppositely arranged. Its flowers are small and white, featuring five distinct petals that are often slightly notched at the tips. The fruit consists of small capsules that contain numerous tiny, dark seeds designed for dispersal by wind or water.
