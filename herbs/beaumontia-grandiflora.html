Beaumontia grandiflora

Beaumontia grandiflora, commonly known as the Devil's Mandarin, is a spectacular evergreen climbing vine native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its large, fragrant, trumpet-shaped white flowers, this vigorous climber adds dramatic tropical flair to gardens, though its rapid growth and potent scent require careful placement and management.

  • Scientific name: Beaumontia grandiflora
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Beaumontia.
