Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as moneywort, is characterized by its distinctive foliage, where the leaf serves as a primary feature for identification. The leaf is typically small, oval to elliptical in shape, and possesses a smooth, entire margin with a slightly glossy texture. These leaves are arranged oppositely along the creeping stems, providing a lush, green groundcover that helps the plant spread effectively across moist soil.
