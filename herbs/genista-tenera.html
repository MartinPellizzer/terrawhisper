Genista tenera

Genista tenera, commonly known as the tender broom, is a resilient shrub native to specific Mediterranean and temperate regions. Valued in traditional ethnobotany, this plant features delicate yellow blooms and dense foliage. It is primarily studied for its bioactive compounds, offering significant potential in developing natural therapeutic applications.
