Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical presence across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its distribution includes the countries of Ghana and Nigeria. Moving into West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found in Cameroon as well as throughout the Gulf of Guinea Islands. The species also extends its range into East Tropical Africa, specifically within Tanzania. Collectively, these locations demonstrate that the plant spans multiple tropical zones from west to east.
