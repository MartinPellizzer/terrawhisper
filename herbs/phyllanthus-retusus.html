Plant Parts

This plant, Phyllanthus retusus, utilizes its small, green leaves as a primary component in various traditional medicinal applications. These leaves are characterized by their simple, elliptical shape and are often harvested to prepare decoctions or infusions used to treat ailments such as inflammation, fever, and digestive issues. In many herbal practices, the chemical constituents found within the leaf tissue are valued for their potential antioxidant and antimicrobial properties.
