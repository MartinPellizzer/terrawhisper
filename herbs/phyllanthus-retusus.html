Phyllanthus retusus

Phyllanthus retusus, commonly known as the "leaf flower," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical coastal regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This plant is widely studied for its potential therapeutic applications in treating various ailments and promoting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus retusus
  • Family: Not available
  • Native range: Africa

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution that spans across different continents. In the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in Mauritius and Réunion. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the South-Central regions. Furthermore, the species is also documented throughout China Southeast. Finally, its range reaches the high-altitude areas of Tibet.
