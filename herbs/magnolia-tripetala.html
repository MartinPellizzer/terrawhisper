Magnolia tripetala

Magnolia tripetala, commonly known as the Three-Petalled Magnolia, is a distinctive medicinal plant native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used in Ayurvedic practices to treat respiratory ailments and inflammatory conditions. This resilient species holds significant value in both herbal medicine and biodiversity.
