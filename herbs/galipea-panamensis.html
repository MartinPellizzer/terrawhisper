Galipea panamensis

Galipea panamensis, commonly known as Panamanian Galipea, is a rare medicinal plant native to the tropical forests of Panama. Belonging to the Acanthaceae family, this botanical treasure is valued in traditional medicine for its unique phytochemical properties, offering significant potential for developing new treatments in pharmacology and herbal healing.
