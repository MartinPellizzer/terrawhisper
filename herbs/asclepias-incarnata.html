Swamp milkweed, scientifically known as Asclepias incarnata, is a striking perennial native to North American wetlands. Renowned for its vibrant pink blooms, this essential plant supports biodiversity by providing vital nectar for pollinators and serving as a critical host for monarch butterfly larvae, making it a cornerstone of ecological restoration.
