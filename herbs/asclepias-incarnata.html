Asclepias incarnata

Asclepia incarnata, commonly known as Swamp Milkweed, is a striking perennial native to North American wetlands. Renowned for its vibrant pink clusters, this medicinal plant serves as a vital nectar source for pollinators. Traditionally used in herbalism, it holds significant ecological importance for supporting monarch butterfly populations and local biodiversity.

  • Scientific name: Asclepias incarnata
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Asclepia incarnata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Gentianaales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Asclepias.
