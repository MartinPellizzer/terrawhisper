Codonopsis ovata

Codonoopsis ovata, commonly known as the "Moonflower" or "Codonoopsis," is a captivating perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its striking, bell-shaped white blossoms, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its rhizomes are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties and unique botanical characteristics.
