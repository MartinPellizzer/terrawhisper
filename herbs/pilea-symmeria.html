Pilea symmeria

What's the taxonomical classification of Pilea symmeria?

Pilea symmeria belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this botanical lineage, it is positioned in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it is part of the order Rosales and falls under the family Urticaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its genus, Pilea, and its specific species designation, symmeria.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant requires bright, indirect sunlight, consistently moist but well-draining soil, and high humidity levels to thrive.

You should place it in a spot that avoids direct afternoon sun to prevent leaf scorch. Using a peat-based potting mix helps maintain the necessary moisture while allowing excess water to escape through drainage holes. Mist the foliage regularly or use a pebble tray to keep the surrounding air humid.

Avoid letting the soil dry out completely, as this can cause the delicate leaves to wilt.
