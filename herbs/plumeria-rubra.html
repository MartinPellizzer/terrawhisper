Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several continents and tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is noted specifically in Senegal. Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Plumeria rubra, possesses various components that are utilized in traditional medicine. The large, green leaves are frequently employed for their topical healing properties, and sometimes a single leaf is used in decoctions to treat specific ailments. When the stem or a branch is damaged, it exudes a thick, white milky juice, which is more formally referred to as latex. This sticky latex is often studied for its bioactive compounds, while the bark of the tree is also noted in various ethnobotanical practices for its potential therapeutic applications.
