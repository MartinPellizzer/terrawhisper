Plumeria rubra

Plumeria rubra, commonly known as Frangipani, is a tropical flowering plant celebrated for its fragrant, waxy blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices. It is frequently utilized for its antiseptic, anti-inflammatory, and analgesic properties to treat skin ailments and various internal conditions.

  • Scientific name: Plumeria rubra
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its specific biological identity is defined by the genus Plumeria.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several continents and tropical regions. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is noted specifically in Senegal. Its range extends into West-Central Tropical Africa, where it can be found around the Gulf of Guinea Islands. Within the Western Indian Ocean, the species is located in the Comoros. Finally, in Asia, the plant is distributed across South-Central China as well as the island of Hainan.
