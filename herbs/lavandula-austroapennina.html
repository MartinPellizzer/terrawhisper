Lavandula austroapennina

Lavandula austroapennina, commonly known as Alpine Lavender, is a resilient perennial prized for its aromatic properties. Native to high-altitude regions, this medicinal herb offers soothing therapeutic benefits. Its essential oils are frequently utilized in aromatherapy to reduce anxiety, promote restful sleep, and alleviate mild skin irritations naturally.

  • Scientific name: Lavandula austroapennina
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Lavandula.
