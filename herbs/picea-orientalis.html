Picea orientalis

Picea orientalis, commonly known as the Oriental spruce, is a majestic evergreen native to the Caucasus Mountains. Renowned for its elegant, drooping branches and fragrant needles, this botanical treasure offers significant medicinal potential. Its resins and essential oils are traditionally valued for their antimicrobial and soothing therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Picea orientalis
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Pinidae. Within the order Pinales and the family Pinaceae, it is classified under the genus Picea.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Picea orientalis, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In Polish, it is called Świerk kaukaski, while in Udmurt, it is known as Кавказысь кыз. Russian speakers call it Ель восточная, and in French, it is referred to as Épicéa d'Orient. Additionally, in Serbian (Cyrillic), it is known as Kavkaska smrča.

Finally, its distribution reaches further west into Belgium, completing its regional spread.
