Caucasian Spruce, scientifically known as Picea orientalis, is a majestic evergreen native to the Caucasus Mountains. Renowned for its dense, conical form and fragrant needles, this resilient conifer offers significant medicinal potential. Its essential oils and resinous extracts are traditionally utilized for their antimicrobial and therapeutic properties.
