Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Zingiber ottensii, is a species of ginger that is primarily valued for its medicinal properties found within its underground structures. The most significant part used for therapeutic purposes is the rhizomes, which are thick, fleshy, horizontal stems that grow beneath the soil surface. These rhizomes serve as the plant's nutrient storage organs and contain a concentrated array of bioactive compounds, making them the primary component harvested for extracting essential oils and medicinal extracts.
