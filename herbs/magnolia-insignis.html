Magnolia insignis

Magnolia insignis, commonly known as the rare Magnolia, is a distinguished medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific tropical regions, its bark and flowers are traditionally utilized to treat various ailments. This botanical treasure offers significant potential for pharmacological research and holistic wellness applications.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia insignis
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Magnoliales and the family Magnoliaceae, it is classified under the genus Magnolia. Specifically, Magnolia insignis represents a distinct species within this botanical lineage.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its range extends through the South-Central and Southeast territories. It can also be found growing in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is specifically documented in Assam. Finally, its habitat includes the rugged terrain of the East Himalaya.
