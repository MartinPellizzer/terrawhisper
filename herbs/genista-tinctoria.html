Genista tinctoria

Genista tinctoria, commonly known as Dyer's Greenweed, is a perennial shrub native to Europe. Renowned historically for its vibrant yellow blooms, it was prized by dyers for producing lasting pigments. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this botanical species holds significant place in traditional herbalism and ecological biodiversity studies.

  • Scientific name: Genista tinctoria
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Genista tinctoria, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Genista.
