Dyer's Greenweed, scientifically known as Genista tinctoria, is a perennial shrub native to Europe, renowned for its historical significance in textile dyeing. Beyond its utility in producing vibrant yellow pigments, this medicinal plant has long been valued in traditional herbalism for its various therapeutic properties and unique botanical characteristics.
