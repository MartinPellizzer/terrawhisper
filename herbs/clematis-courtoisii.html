Clematis courtoisii

What's the taxonomical classification of Clematis courtoisii?

Clematis courtoisii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales. This plant is a representative of the family Ranunculaceae, specifically situated within the genus Clematis, and is identified by its unique species designation, courtoisii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Clematis courtoisii has opposite, petiolate leaves that are typically composed of three to five ovate or lanceolate leaflets with entire or slightly serrated margins. The plant exhibits a climbing growth habit, utilizing its leaf petioles to twine around supporting structures. Its flowers are characterized by four to six pale to creamy white tepals that form a star-like shape. The reproductive structures include numerous central stamens that provide a delicate texture to the bloom. Small, hairy or glabrous stems support the foliage and floral development throughout the growing season.
