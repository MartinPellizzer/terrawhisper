Casearia tardieuae

Casearia tardieuae, commonly known as a member of the Salicaceae family, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical properties, it is traditionally utilized in various folk remedies. Scientific interest focuses on its potential bioactive compounds, which offer promising therapeutic applications in pharmacology.
