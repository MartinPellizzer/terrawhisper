Sorghum

Sorghum · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (29 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Sorghum, scientifically known as Sorghum, is a versatile cereal grain celebrated for its profound medicinal properties. Rich in antioxidants and essential nutrients, it aids digestion, regulates blood sugar, and combats inflammation. Historically used in traditional medicine, this hardy plant offers significant health benefits for managing various chronic metabolic conditions.
