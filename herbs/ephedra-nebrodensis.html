Ephedra nebrodensis

Ephedra nebrodensis, commonly known as a desert shrub, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its unique alkaloid content. Found in arid environments, it has been traditionally used for its stimulant and bronchodilatory properties. Modern pharmacological studies continue to explore its potential in treating respiratory and cardiovascular conditions.

  • Scientific name: Ephedra nebrodensis
  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Gnetidae. Within the order Ephedrales, it is classified under the family Ephedraceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Ephedra as Ephedra nebrodensis.
