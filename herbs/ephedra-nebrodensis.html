Plant Parts

This plant, Ephedra nebrodensis, is primarily utilized for its medicinal properties through its aerial parts. These above-ground structures consist of slender, photosynthetic, jointed stems that lack true leaves, a characteristic adaptation of the genus. Because the therapeutic alkaloids are concentrated within these green, wiry stems, the aerial parts are the specific components harvested and processed for pharmacological use.
