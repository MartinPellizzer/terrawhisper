Rubus chingii var. suavissimus

Rubus chingii var. suavissimus, commonly known as Sweet Raspberry, is a highly valued medicinal plant in Traditional Chinese Medicine. Renowned for its antioxidant properties, it is primarily used to support kidney function, nourish the blood, and combat fatigue. This potent berry offers significant therapeutic benefits for holistic wellness.
