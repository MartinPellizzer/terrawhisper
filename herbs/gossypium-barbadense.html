Gossypium barbadense

Creole cotton

Gossypium barbadense, commonly known as the cotton plant, is a versatile medicinal species valued in traditional healing. Beyond its industrial importance, its various parts possess bioactive properties used to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Exploring its phytochemical profile reveals significant potential for developing new therapeutic applications.

  • Family: Malvaceae

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gossypium.

Common Names

This plant, Gossypium barbadense, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In Haiti, it is referred to as Pye koton, while in Catalan, it is called cotoner Pima. It is identified as västindisk bomull in Swedish, Amerikaanse katoen in Dutch, and is known as Barbados-Bomuld in Danish.
