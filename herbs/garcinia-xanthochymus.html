Plant Parts

This plant, Garcinia xanthochymus, commonly known as the yellow mangosteen, offers various parts that are utilized for their medicinal and nutritional properties. The bright yellow fruits are widely consumed for their tangy flavor and are a rich source of vitamins and bioactive compounds. The seeds contained within the fruit also hold potential therapeutic value due to their specific chemical constituents. For traditional medicinal purposes, the bark and the roots are frequently employed, often used in decoctions to treat various ailments. Additionally, the sturdy wood of the tree is noted for its durability, though its medicinal application is less common than that of the other components.
