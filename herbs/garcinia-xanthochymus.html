Garcinia xanthochymus

Garcinia xanthochymus ex

Garcinia xanthochymus, commonly known as the yellow mangosteen, is a tropical fruit-bearing tree valued for its diverse medicinal properties. Rich in bioactive compounds, its leaves, fruit, and bark are traditionally used in folk medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant potential for therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia xanthochymus
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Under the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls into the order Malpighiales and the family Clusiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Garcinia, which includes the species Garcinia xanthochymus.

Plant Parts

This plant, Garcinia xanthochymus, commonly known as the yellow mangosteen, offers various parts that are utilized for their medicinal and nutritional properties. The bright yellow fruits are widely consumed for their tangy flavor and are a rich source of vitamins and bioactive compounds. The seeds contained within the fruit also hold potential therapeutic value due to their specific chemical constituents. For traditional medicinal purposes, the bark and the roots are frequently employed, often used in decoctions to treat various ailments. Additionally, the sturdy wood of the tree is noted for its durability, though its medicinal application is less common than that of the other components.
