Dolichos kilimandscharicus

Dolichos kilimandscharicus, commonly known as a vital medicinal herb of the East African highlands, offers remarkable therapeutic potential. Native to the Kilimanjaro region, this plant is traditionally utilized by local communities to treat diverse ailments, ranging from digestive issues to inflammatory conditions, showcasing its profound ethnobotanical significance.

  • Scientific name: Dolichos kilimandscharicus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is further categorized into the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Dolichos.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, its presence is documented in Burundi, Rwanda, and the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the eastern part of the continent, it is also found within Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, this species can be located in Ethiopia.
