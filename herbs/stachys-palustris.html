Stachys palustris

Marsh hedgenettle

Stachys palustris, commonly known as marsh woundwort, is a perennial herb native to wetlands and damp habitats. Renowned in traditional herbalism, its leaves possess astringent and anti-inflammatory properties. Often used to treat skin irritations and minor wounds, this plant remains a fascinating subject for both botanical and medicinal study.

  • Scientific name: Stachys palustris
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. It is specifically identified by the genus Stachys.
