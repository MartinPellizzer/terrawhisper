Milkwort, scientifically known as Polygala, is a diverse genus of flowering plants valued in traditional medicine for centuries. Renowned for its neuroprotective and adaptogenic properties, it is frequently used to support cognitive function, soothe the nervous system, and alleviate respiratory issues, offering a rich botanical resource for holistic wellness.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Its presence is also documented across the island of Great Britain and throughout Ireland. Furthermore, the species extends to the northern territories of Finland. Finally, the plant is known to inhabit the Faroe Islands, also referred to as Føroyaar.
