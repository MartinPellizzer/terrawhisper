Polygala

Polygala, commonly known as milkwort, is a versatile genus of flowering plants celebrated in traditional medicine. Renowned for its diverse phytochemical profile, it possesses significant neuroprotective, anti-inflammatory, and adaptogenic properties. Often used to enhance cognitive function and soothe the nervous system, Polygala remains a vital subject in ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Polygala
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Polygalaeacee and specifically belongs to the genus Polygala.
