Wrightia tinctoria subsp. rothii

Wrightia tinctoria rothii

Wrightia tinctoria subsp. rothii, commonly known as the Indian Oleander, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent phytochemical properties, it is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic practices to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and various infections, serving as a vital resource in ethnobotanical healing.

  • Scientific name: Wrightia tinctoria subsp. rothii
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae. Specifically, this medicinal subspecies is identified by the genus Wrightia.
