Genipap

Genipa americana · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (383 studies) · ★★★★★

Genipap, scientifically known as Genipa americana, is a versatile tropical fruit tree native to Central and South America. Renowned for its medicinal properties, the plant provides bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and skin conditions. Its unique juice also serves as a natural, long-lasting pigment for traditional body art.

The reproduction of Genipa americana is characterized by a biotic pollination syndrome, primarily driven by insects and specifically facilitated by bees.
