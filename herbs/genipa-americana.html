Genipa americana

Genipa americana, commonly known as Genipap, is a versatile tropical tree native to the Americas. Renowned for its fruit, which yields a potent blue-black dye, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in medicine, its extracts exhibit promising antioxidant, antimicrobial, and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a subject of scientific interest.

  • Scientific name: Genipa americana
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized into the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Gentianales, falling under the family Rubiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is established within the genus Genipa.
