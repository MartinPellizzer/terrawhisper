Genipap

Genipa americana · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (383 studies) · ★★★★★

Genipap, scientifically known as Genipa americana, is a versatile tropical fruit tree native to Central and South America. Renowned for its medicinal properties, the plant provides bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation and skin conditions. Its unique juice also serves as a natural, long-lasting pigment for traditional body art.

Plant Parts

This plant, Genipa americana, commonly known as genipap, utilizes various parts for its diverse medicinal and cultural applications. The large, glossy green leaves are often studied for their phytochemical properties, while the fleshy fruits are highly valued for their juice, which serves as both a natural dye and a source of bioactive compounds. Additionally, the bark of the tree is frequently employed in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, showcasing the holistic utility of the entire organism.
