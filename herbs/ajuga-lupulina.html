Ajuga lupulina, scientifically known as Ajuga lupulina, is a creeping perennial herb native to Europe and Asia. Often found in damp, marshy habitats, this member of the mint family features whorls of small, tubular flowers. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it offers unique botanical interest for herbal enthusiasts.
