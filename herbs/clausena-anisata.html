Distribution

This plant, known as Clausena anisata, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Within this area, it can be found growing in the country of Benin. Its presence is also documented throughout the nation of Ghana. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed across Guinea and the Ivory Coast. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant extends into Mali.

Plant Parts

This plant, Clausena anisata, possesses several components that are utilized for their various medicinal properties. The stems provide the structural framework for the shrub, and the stem bark is often harvested to prepare decoctions used for their potent antimicrobial or anti-inflammatory effects. Additionally, the fruit serves as another vital part of the plant, contributing to its overall chemical profile used in holistic healing practices.
