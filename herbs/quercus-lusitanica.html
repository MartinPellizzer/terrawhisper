Scrub Gall Oak, scientifically known as Quercus lusitaniica, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its dense foliage and tannin-rich acorns, this plant plays a vital role in local ecosystems. Traditionally, its bark and galls have been utilized in various medicinal and tanning applications.
