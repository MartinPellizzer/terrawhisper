Quercus lusitanica

Quercus lusitanica, commonly known as the Lusitanian oak, is a resilient evergreen species native to the Iberian Peninsula. Renowned for its robust timber and ecological significance, this medicinal plant offers traditional therapeutic potential. Its leaves and bark are studied for bioactive compounds that support holistic wellness and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Quercus lusitanica
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Europe
