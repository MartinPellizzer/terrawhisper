Duranta erecta

Angel whisper

Duranta erecta, commonly known as Golden Dewdrop or Skyflower, is a versatile evergreen shrub celebrated for its vibrant blue flowers and cascading yellow berries. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant value in traditional medicine, where its leaves and stems are utilized for their diverse therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Duranta erecta
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Duranta erecta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Lamiales and the family Verbenaceae. Finally, it is classified under the genus Duranta.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Duranta erecta, is recognized by various common names across different regions and languages. In Indonesia, it is referred to as Sinyo nakal in both Indonesian (id) and Javanese (jv), while in Japan, it is called デュランタ (ja). In Kerala, India, it is known by its Malayalam name മാണിക്ക്യചെമ്പഴുക്ക (ml), and in Vietnam, it is commonly called Thanh quan (vi).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several continents and diverse climatic zones. In Southeastern Europe, its presence can be specifically noted in Sicilia. Its range extends into Northern Africa, where it is found within the borders of Algeria. Moving toward the tropical regions, the species is located in Guinea within West Tropical Africa. Finally, it inhabits West-Central Tropical Africa, specifically documented in both Burundi and the Central African Republic.

Plant Parts

This plant, Duranta erecta, is highly valued in traditional medicine, where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties.
