Nauclea orientalis

Nauclea orientalis, commonly known as the Oriental Nauclea, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical Asian forests. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from inflammation to fever, offering valuable biochemical potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Nauclea orientalis
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further organized into the order Gentianaales and the family Rubiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is found within the genus Nauclea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across various regions of South and Southeast Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in both Bangladesh and Sri Lanka. Its distribution extends significantly into the Indo-China region as well. Specifically, it is documented in the countries of Cambodia and Laos. Finally, the species also inhabits the tropical landscapes of Myanmar.
