Solidago rugosa var. rugosa

Solidago rugosa var. rugosa, commonly known as Rough Goldenrod, is a resilient perennial native to North American wetlands and prairies. Distinguished by its deeply wrinkled leaves and vibrant yellow flower clusters, this plant plays a vital role in supporting pollinators and holds significant historical value in traditional herbal medicine.

  • Scientific name: Solidago rugosa var. rugosa
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Solidago, specifically as the variety rugosa.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide-ranging geographical distribution across several continents. In Northern Europe, it can be found growing in both Great Britain and Norway. Its presence extends into Middle Europe, specifically within the territory of Switzerland. Moving toward the Mediterranean, the species is also documented in Southwestern Europe, particularly in Portugal. Finally, its range reaches East Asia, where it is located in Southeast China.
