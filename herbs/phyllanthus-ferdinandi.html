Phyllanthus ferdinandi

Phyllanthus ferdinandi, commonly known as the Lemon Aspen, is a unique rainforest shrub native to Australia. Highly valued for its tart, citrus-like fruit, this medicinal plant is rich in antioxidants. It is traditionally utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory properties and its ability to support overall immune system health.

  • Scientific name: Phyllanthus ferdinandi
  • Family: Phyllanthaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is further categorized into the family Phyllanthaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Phyllanthus.

Distribution

This plant is a native species primarily found within the diverse landscapes of Australia. Its geographical range extends across several distinct regions of the continent. Specifically, it can be located throughout the state of New South Wales. The species is also known to inhabit parts of Queensland, thriving in its unique climate. Additionally, its distribution reaches into the tropical environments of the Northern Territory.
