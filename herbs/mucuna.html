Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions. Within the Macaronesia archipelago, its presence is noted in Cape Verde. Moving to the mainland, it is widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the territories of Benin and Burkina. Additionally, its range extends through the coastal nations of Ghana and Guinea-Bissau.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Velvet Bean, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, most notably its seed and flower. The seeds are highly valued in traditional medicine due to their significant concentration of L-Dopa, a precursor to dopamine that helps manage neurological conditions. Additionally, the flowers of the Mucuna plant are often studied and used for their unique bioactive compounds, contributing to the plant's overall therapeutic profile.
