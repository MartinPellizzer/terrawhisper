Cynanchum viminale

Cynanchum viminale, commonly known as climbing milkweed, is a perennial climbing shrub native to various regions of Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this plant is prized for its diverse therapeutic properties. It is frequently utilized to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and various systemic disorders through its bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Cynanchum viminale
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae and the genus Cynanchum.

Distribution

This plant, known as Cynanchum viminale, is found across several regions within West Tropical Africa. Its geographical range specifically includes the nation of Benin.
