Morphology

The morphology of Goniothalamus macrophyllus is characterized by a woody growth form that functions primarily as a self-supporting climber, exhibiting characteristics of both a liana and a vine. While its structural development allows it to act as a self-supporting entity, it possesses the capacity for climbing, transitioning between obligatory and facultative support mechanisms. Although it can manifest in shrub-like or tree-like growth forms, it is fundamentally categorized as a terrestrial plant rather than an epiphyte. This woody species can reach a maximum plant height of approximately 5 meters, establishing a robust physical presence within its habitat.
