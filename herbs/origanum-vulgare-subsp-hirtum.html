Plant Parts

This plant, Origanum vulgare subsp. hirtum, derives much of its therapeutic potency from its specific chemical constituents, most notably the essential oils found within its tissues. These essential oils are primarily concentrated in the glandular trichomes located on the leaves and flowering stems, where they serve as a defense mechanism for the plant. When harvested for medicinal use, these volatile oils provide the characteristic aromatic profile and contain bioactive compounds such as carvacrol and thymol, which are responsible for the plant's antimicrobial and antioxidant properties.
