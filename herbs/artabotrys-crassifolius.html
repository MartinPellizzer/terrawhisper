Artabotrys crassifolius

Artabotrys crassifolius, commonly known as the thick-leaved Artabotrys, is a climbing woody vine native to tropical regions. Valued in traditional medicine, this plant possesses diverse bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized for its potential therapeutic effects, particularly in treating various ailments through its unique phytochemical constituents and medicinal applications.
