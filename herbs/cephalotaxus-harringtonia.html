Cephalotaxus harringtoniana, scientifically known as Cephalotaxus harringtonia, is a significant medicinal evergreen shrub prized for its therapeutic potential. Renowned for containing bioactive alkaloids, particularly harringtonine, this plant plays a crucial role in pharmacology. Researchers extensively study its derivatives for their potent anti-tumor and antimicrobial properties in modern medicine.
