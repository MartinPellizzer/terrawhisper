Cephalotaxus harringtonia

Cephalotaxus harringtoniana

Cephalotaxus harringtonia, commonly known as Harrington's Yew, is a significant medicinal plant valued for its complex alkaloids. It serves as a vital precursor in the synthesis of vinca alkaloids, such as vincristine and vinblastine. These compounds are essential in modern oncology for treating various types of cancer worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Cephalotaxus harringtonia
  • Family: Cephalotaxaceae
  • Native range: Europe
