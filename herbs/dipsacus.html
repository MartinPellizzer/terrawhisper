Dipsacus

Dipsacus, commonly known as teasel, is a distinctive genus of flowering plants in the Caprifoliaceae family. Characterized by prickly, cone-shaped flower heads and architectural foliage, these resilient perennials thrive in meadows. Beyond their ornamental value, various species hold historical significance in traditional medicine for their diverse therapeutic potential.

  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Dipsacales and the family Caprifoliaceae. It is ultimately classified under the genus Dipsacus.
