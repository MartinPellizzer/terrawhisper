Bush Tea

Lippia alba · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (343 studies) · ★★★★★

Bush Tea, scientifically known as Lippia alba, is a versatile perennial shrub native to tropical regions of South America. Widely utilized in traditional medicine, it possesses significant antimicrobial, sedative, and antispasmodic properties. Often brewed as a fragrant herbal infusion, this plant offers a natural approach to promoting relaxation and wellness.

Plant Parts

This plant, Lippia alba, is a versatile medicinal shrub where various components offer distinct therapeutic benefits. The most significant part is the leaves, which serve as the primary source of the plant's potent essential oils, known for their calming and antimicrobial properties. While the stem provides the structural framework for the shrub's growth, the delicate, fragrant flowers contribute to the plant's biological cycle and aromatic profile. Additionally, the roots are often utilized in traditional medicine, completing a botanical profile where every part plays a role in its medicinal value.
