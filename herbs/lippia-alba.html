Lippia alba

Lippia alba, commonly known as lemon verbena or West Indian vervain, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to South America. Renowned for its aromatic leaves, it possesses potent antimicrobial, sedative, and anti-inflammatory properties. Traditionally used in folk medicine, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential for treating anxiety and infections.

  • Scientific name: Lippia alba
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales, it is classified under the family Verbenaceae and belongs to the genus Lippia.
