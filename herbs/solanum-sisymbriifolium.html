Solanum sisymbriifolium

Yellow-berried nightshade

Solanum sisymbriifolium, commonly known as the Cheese Weed, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued in traditional healing practices. Characterized by its unique leaf structure, this plant offers diverse bioactive compounds. Exploring its pharmacological potential reveals significant therapeutic possibilities, making it a subject of great interest for ethnobotanical research.

  • Scientific name: Solanum sisymbriifolium
  • Family: Solanaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Common Names

This plant, scientifically known as Solanum sisymbriifolium, is recognized by various regional and linguistic names across the globe. In Welsh, it is referred to as Codwarth-yr-ychen coch, while in French, it is known as morelle de Balbis. The Swedish common name is Blek taggborre, and in Chinese, it is called 蒜芥茄. Furthermore, in the Guaraní language, it is identified as Ñuatĩ pytã.
