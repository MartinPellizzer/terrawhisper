Dioscorea spongiosa

Dioscorea spongiosa, commonly known as the sponge yam, is a climbing tuberous plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its nutritional value and medicinal properties, this species is utilized in traditional medicine to address various ailments. Its unique bioactive compounds offer significant potential for pharmacological research and therapeutic applications.

  • Scientific name: Dioscorea spongiosa
  • Family: Dioscoreaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Dioscoreales and the family Dioscoreaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Dioscorea as Dioscorea spongiosa.
