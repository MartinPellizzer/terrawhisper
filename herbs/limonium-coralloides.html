Limonium coralloides

Limonium coralloides, commonly known as a rare endemic species, is a unique medicinal plant native to specific coastal regions. Valued for its distinct phytochemical profile, it holds significant potential in traditional healing practices. Scientific exploration continues to uncover its therapeutic properties, offering promising insights into natural pharmacological advancements.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions of Northern and Central Asia. Within the vast territory of Siberia, it can be found specifically in the Altay, Tuva, and West Siberia regions. Its range also extends southward into Central Asia, where it is documented in Kazakhstan. Additionally, the species is present in the Xinjiang region of China. Collectively, these locations highlight the diverse environmental niches inhabited by this medicinal species.
