Vitex bojeri

Vitex bojeri, commonly known as Chasteberry, is a versatile medicinal shrub celebrated for its hormonal balancing properties. Widely used in herbal medicine to support reproductive health, it helps regulate menstrual cycles and alleviate PMS symptoms. This potent botanical offers a natural approach to managing endocrine wellness and hormonal equilibrium.

  • Scientific name: Vitex bojeri
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Vitex bojeri, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae and the genus Vitex.
