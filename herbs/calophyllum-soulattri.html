Calophyllum soulattri

Calophyllum soulattri, commonly known as the fragrant Calophyllum, is a significant medicinal tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is traditionally utilized in various holistic practices. The plant's leaves and bark contain bioactive compounds frequently studied for their potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial therapeutic benefits.

  • Scientific name: Calophyllum soulattri
  • Family: Calophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Calophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Calophyllum.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Calophyllum soulattri, is recognized by various regional names depending on the local language. In Vietnam, it is referred to as Còng trắng (vi), while in Sulawesi, it is commonly called bintangur (su). In Javanese, the name used is solatri (jv), and it is known by two distinct names in Indonesia: kapur naga (id) and gamer (id).
