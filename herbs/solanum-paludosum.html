Concurrently, its presence in Restinga habitats indicates an ability to tolerate sandy, nutrient-poor soils and the saline influence of coastal ecosystems. This dual habitat distribution suggests a versatile ecological strategy that allows the species to navigate both the moisture-stressed landscapes of the interior and the coastal, well-drained sandy terrains of the littoral zones.
