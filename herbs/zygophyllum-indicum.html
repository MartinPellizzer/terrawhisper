Zygophyllum indicum

Zygophyllum indicum, commonly known as the Indian Calotropis or Sand Lily, is a resilient succulent shrub native to arid regions. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves and roots possess potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering valuable therapeutic potential for treating skin ailments and various inflammatory conditions in indigenous practices.

  • Scientific name: Zygophyllum indicum
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Zygophyllales and the family Zygophyllaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by its genus, Zygophyllum.
