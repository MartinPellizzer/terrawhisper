Laserpitium latifolium

Broad-leaved sermountain

Laserpitium latifolium, commonly known as broad-leaved laserwort, is a distinctive medicinal herb belonging to the Apiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it has been utilized in traditional medicine to address digestive ailments and inflammatory conditions. This plant offers significant therapeutic potential through its unique bioactive phytochemical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Laserpitium latifolium
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Apiales and the family Apiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Laserpitium.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Laserpitium latifolium, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. German speakers call it Breitblättriges Laserkraut, and in France, it is identified as Laser à feuilles larges. Additionally, in Poland, the plant is commonly known as Okrzyn szerokolistny.
