Coriandrum sativum

Coriandrrum sativum, commonly known as coriander or cilantro, is a versatile aromatic herb valued globally. Beyond its culinary uses, it serves as a potent medicinal plant. Rich in antioxidants and essential oils, it aids digestion, supports heart health, and possesses anti-inflammatory properties, making it a staple in traditional medicine.

  • Scientific name: Coriandrum sativum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Coriandrum, identifying it as Coriandrum sativum.
