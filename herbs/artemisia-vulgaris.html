Artemisia vulgaris

Common wormwood

Artemisia vulgaris, commonly known as mugwort, is a perennial herb celebrated in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties. Renowned for aiding digestion, regulating menstrual cycles, and enhancing dream recall, this aromatic plant has been utilized across various cultures for centuries to promote holistic wellness and cognitive clarity.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia vulgaris
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Artemisia, specifically as the species Artemisia vulgaris.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Artemisia vulgaris, is recognized by various common names across different languages and cultures. In Polish, it is referred to as bylica pospolita, while in French, it is called Armoise commune. Its regional names also vary, such as Bifut in the Frisian language and Sinsioło in Venda. Additionally, in Chinese, it is identified as 北艾.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing within the borders of Denmark and Finland. Additionally, its presence extends to the Faroe Islands, which are known as Føroyar. The species is also commonly documented throughout Great Britain. Finally, its geographical range includes the island of Ireland.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as mugwort, is a versatile medicinal herb where various components contribute to its therapeutic properties. The most frequently utilized parts are the green, aromatic leaves and the flowering tops, which are often harvested when the plant is in bloom to ensure maximum potency. Beyond these structural parts, the plant is highly valued for its rich concentration of essential oils, which provide its characteristic scent and carry many of its bioactive compounds. Because it is classified among various medicinal herbs, the integration of its foliage and floral elements makes it a significant subject in traditional herbalism.
