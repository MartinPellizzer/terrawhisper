Ephedra alata subsp. alenda

Ephedra alata subsp. alenda, commonly known as a vital medicinal shrub, is an endemic species highly valued in traditional pharmacology. Renowned for its rich alkaloid content, particularly ephedrine, it is utilized to treat respiratory ailments and enhance physical stamina, offering significant therapeutic potential within modern herbal medicine research.

  • Family: Ephedraceae
  • Native range: Africa
