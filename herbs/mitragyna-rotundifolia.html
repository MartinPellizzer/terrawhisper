Mitragyna rotundifolia, scientifically known as Mitragyna rotundifolia, is a tropical evergreen tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its bioactive alkaloids, particularly mitragynine, this plant has gained significant global attention for its unique pharmacological properties, offering potential therapeutic applications in pain management and various neurological studies.
