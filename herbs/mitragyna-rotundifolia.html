Mitragyna rotundifolia, scientifically known as Mitragyna rotundifolia, is a tropical evergreen tree native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its bioactive alkaloids, particularly mitragynine, this plant has gained significant global attention for its unique pharmacological properties, offering potential therapeutic applications in pain management and various neurological studies.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several distinct regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is primarily concentrated in the South-Central areas. Moving into the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found in Assam, Bangladesh, and the East Himalaya region. The species also extends into the Indo-China territory, specifically inhabiting the Andaman Islands. Together, these locations highlight the broad natural distribution of Mitragyna rotundifolia.
