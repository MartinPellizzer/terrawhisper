Eurasian black bindweed, scientifically known as Fallopia convolvulus, is a resilient climbing perennial found across Europe and Asia. While often viewed as a persistent garden weed, it possesses significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of this plant have been utilized in folk medicine for their unique bioactive properties.
