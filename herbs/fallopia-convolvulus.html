Fallopia convolvulus

Fallopia convolvulus, commonly known as field bindweed, is a vigorous climbing perennial often viewed as a persistent weed. Beyond its agricultural nuisance, this plant holds historical significance in traditional herbalism. It is studied for its unique phytochemical profile, offering potential therapeutic properties within the diverse Fallopia genus.

  • Scientific name: Fallopia convolvulus
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae and falls under the genus Fallopia.
