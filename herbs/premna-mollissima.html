Premna mollissima

Premna mollisima, commonly known as soft-leaved premna, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammatory conditions, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its unique botanical properties offer significant potential for modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Premna mollissima
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Premna. As a member of this specific lineage, Premna mollisima represents a unique botanical specimen within its taxonomic hierarchy.
