What's the taxonomical classification of Shortia sinensis?

Shortia sinensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Following its descent through the order Ericales, the plant is placed into the family Diapensiaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Shortia, with the specific epithet sinensis denoting its unique species designation.
