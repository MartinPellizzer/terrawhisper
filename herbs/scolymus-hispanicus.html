Scolymus hispanicus

Scolymus hispanicus, commonly known as Spanish golden thistle, is a resilient, prickly perennial native to the Mediterranean region. Highly valued in traditional herbalism, this plant is rich in bioactive compounds. It is primarily utilized for its potent anti-inflammatory and digestive properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in natural medicine.

  • Scientific name: Scolymus hispanicus
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Scolymus hispanicus, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and falls under the genus Scolymus.
