coltsfoot

Tussilago farfara · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (631 studies) · ★★★★★

Coltsfoot, scientifically known as Tussilago farfarra, is a perennial herb renowned for its traditional medicinal properties. Primarily used to soothe respiratory ailments, it acts as a powerful expectorant. This plant, characterized by its distinctive yellow flowers, has been utilized for centuries in herbal remedies to alleviate persistent coughs.
