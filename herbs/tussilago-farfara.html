Tussilago farfara

Tussilago farfarra, commonly known as coltsfoot, is a perennial herb cherished in traditional medicine for its respiratory benefits. Renowned for its soothing properties, it has long been used to treat coughs, bronchitis, and sore throats. This versatile plant offers natural relief, making it a staple in herbal healing traditions.

  • Scientific name: Tussilago farfara
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Tussilago farfara, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Asterales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Asteraceae within the genus Tussilago.
