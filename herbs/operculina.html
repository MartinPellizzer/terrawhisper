Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Operculina tuberosa, is highly valued in traditional medicine primarily for its specialized underground structures. The most significant medicinal component of the species is its large, fleshy storage roots, which serve as the primary reservoir for bioactive compounds. These thickened roots are harvested and processed to extract the therapeutic properties required for various herbal remedies, making them the most vital part of the plant for medicinal applications.
