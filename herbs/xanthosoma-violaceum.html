What's the taxonomical classification of Xanthosoma violaceum?

Xanthosoma violaceum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Aliasmaetales, this plant is a member of the family Araceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Xanthosoma and the species violaceum.
