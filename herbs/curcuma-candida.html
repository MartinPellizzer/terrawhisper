Curcuma candida

Curcuma candida, commonly known as White Turmeric, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Zingiberaceae family. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. This rhizomatous herb offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties, supporting overall holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Curcuma candida
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is a member of the Zingiberaceae family. Finally, it is classified under the genus Curcuma.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Curcuma candida, is recognized by several different common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In English, it is frequently referred to as white turmeric, a name that highlights the distinct color of its rhizomes compared to its more famous relative. In Hindi, the plant is commonly called safed haldi, which directly translates the characteristic color of the root. Furthermore, in various regional contexts within India, it may also be identified by its local vernacular names that distinguish it from the standard yellow variety.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. In East Asia, its presence is noted within the South-Central regions of China. The species is also found across the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within Bangladesh. Furthermore, it extends into the Indo-China peninsula, covering parts of Cambodia and Myanmar. Finally, its geographical range includes the tropical landscapes of Thailand.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Curcuma candida, utilizes its underground rhizoes as the primary medicinal component. These thickened, fleshy rhizomes serve as the storage organs for the plant, concentrating various bioactive compounds and essential oils that contribute to its therapeutic properties. In traditional herbal medicine, it is specifically these rhizomes that are harvested, dried, and processed to extract their healing potential.
