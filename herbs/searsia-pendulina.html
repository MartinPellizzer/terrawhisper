Searsia pendulina

White karee

Searsia pendulina, commonly known as the Fever Tree, is a distinctive deciduous tree native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its striking, peeling bark and spreading canopy, it plays a vital ecological role. In traditional medicine, various parts are utilized for their potent healing properties and significant cultural importance.

  • Scientific name: Searsia pendulina
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Sapindaales, falling into the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Searsia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Searsia pendulina, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In English, it is widely called the White Karee, while in Afrikaans, it is known as Witkarree. Its names in other languages include Rhus pendulina in Vietnamese and 垂枝漆 in Chinese.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several regions within Southern Africa. It can be found growing throughout the various Cape Provinces. Additionally, its range extends northward into the country of Namibia. The species is also well-established within the Free State province. Together, these areas define the primary geographical footprint of the species.
