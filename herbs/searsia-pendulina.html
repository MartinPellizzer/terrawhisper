White Karee, scientifically known as Searsia pendulina, is a versatile, evergreen shrub native to Southern Africa. Highly valued for its medicinal properties, it is traditionally used to treat ailments ranging from stomach upsets to skin conditions. Its hardy nature and dense foliage also make it an excellent ornamental landscape plant.
