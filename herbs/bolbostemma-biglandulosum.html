Bolbostemma biglandulosum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Cucurbitaleales and is a member of the family Cucurbitaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Bolbostemma and the species name biglandulosum.
