Phlomoides rotata

Phlomoides rotaata, commonly known as the wheel-leaf phlomoides, is a distinctive medicinal herb native to specific rocky terrains. Renowned for its unique foliage and therapeutic properties, this plant is studied for its bioactive compounds. It holds significant potential in traditional healing practices and modern pharmacological research for various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Phlomoides rotata
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phlomoides, specifically the species Phlomoides rotaata.
