Cornus sericea

Cornus sericea, commonly known as Red-osier Dogwood, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized for its striking, bright red stems. Native to North America, this resilient plant thrives in wetlands. Beyond its ornamental beauty, it offers significant ecological value, providing essential food and habitat for various local bird species.
