Red osier, scientifically known as Cornus sericea, is a resilient deciduous shrub prized for its striking, crimson-colored stems. Thriving in wetlands and moist soils, it serves as a vital component of riparian ecosystems, providing nutrient-rich forage and essential habitat for diverse wildlife while offering significant traditional medicinal properties.
