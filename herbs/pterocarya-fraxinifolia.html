Caucasian wingnut, scientifically known as Pterocarya fraxinifolia, is a rare, deciduous tree native to the Caucasus region. Renowned for its striking, large pinnate leaves, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various parts of the plant have been utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation and support overall wellness.
