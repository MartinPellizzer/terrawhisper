Pterocarya fraxinifolia

Caucasian wingnut

Pterocarya fraxinifolia, commonly known as the Chinese Ash, is a rare deciduous tree valued in traditional medicine. Native to East Asian riverbanks, its bark and leaves possess significant bioactive properties. Researchers study its potential for anti-inflammatory and antioxidant applications, highlighting its importance in both ecological and therapeutic contexts.
