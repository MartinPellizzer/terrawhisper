Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the rugged landscapes of Northern and Central Asia. In the Siberian region, its presence is specifically noted within the Altai mountains. Moving into Central Asia, it can be found growing throughout the territory of Kazakhstan. The species also inhabits various parts of Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan. Finally, its geographical range extends into the diverse terrains of Uzbekistan.
