Lusitanian Heath, scientifically known as Erica lusitaniica, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its bell-shaped, fragrant flowers, it plays a vital role in stabilizing soil and supporting local pollinators. This hardy plant is highly valued in traditional herbalism for its diverse medicinal properties.
