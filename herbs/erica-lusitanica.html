Erica lusitanica

Erica lusitaniica, commonly known as the Portuguese Heath, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its delicate, bell-shaped flowers and dense foliage, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its potential antioxidant properties and its ability to support various holistic wellness practices.

  • Scientific name: Erica lusitanica
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Erica.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence is noted in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward the south, it can also be found throughout much of Southwestern Europe. Specifically, this species is native to the territories of France, Portugal, and Spain. These diverse locations highlight its ability to thrive in different European climates.
