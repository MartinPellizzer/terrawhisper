Rhododendron auriculatum

Rhododendron auriculatum, commonly known as the Ear-leaved Rhododendron, is a striking medicinal shrub native to the Himalayan highlands. Renowned for its vibrant blooms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, its various parts are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from fever to digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron auriculatum
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Rhododendron.
