What's the taxonomical classification of Adenophora wawreana?

Adenophora wawreana belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales. The plant is further categorized into the family Campanulaceae, within which it is placed in the genus Adenophora, ultimately reaching its specific designation as the species wawreana.
