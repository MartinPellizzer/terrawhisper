Baccharoides guineensis

Baccharoides guineeensis, commonly known as the Guinea Baccharoides, is a significant medicinal plant native to tropical African regions. Valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and bark are frequently utilized to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues, offering promising ethnobotanical insights for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Baccharoides guineensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Its specific taxonomic designation is placed within the genus Baccharoides.
