What's the taxonomical classification of Ilex godajam?

Ilex godajam belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic descent, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Aquifoliales and falls within the family Aquifoliaceae. Finally, its specific classification is defined by the genus Ilex and the species godajam.
