Nepeta parnassica

Nepeta parnasica, commonly known as Parnassus nepeta, is a perennial herb within the Lamiaceae family. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate white flowers, this plant has been traditionally utilized in folk medicine for its sedative and antispasmodic properties, offering a natural approach to soothing various nervous ailments.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta parnassica
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Nepeta. Specifically, Nepeta parnassica represents a distinct species within this botanical lineage.

This plant, Nepeta parnasica, is known by various names depending on the region and language. In French, the plant is called Népite de Parnasse, and in Italian, it is referred to as Nepeta del Parnasso.
