Chenopodiastrum murale

Chenopodium murale, commonly known as black nightshade or goosefoot, is a versatile medicinal plant used in traditional healing. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is frequently employed to support digestive health and skin vitality. This resilient herb offers significant therapeutic potential within various global herbal practices.

  • Scientific name: Chenopodiastrum murale
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe
