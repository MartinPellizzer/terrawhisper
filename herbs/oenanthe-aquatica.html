Oenanthe aquatica

Fineleaf water dropwort

Oenanthe aquatica, commonly known as water dropwort, is a perennial herb found in wetland habitats. While its roots possess unique medicinal properties in traditional practices, the plant is highly toxic if ingested. Understanding its botanical characteristics and chemical potency is essential for distinguishing its potential uses from its extreme danger.

  • Scientific name: Oenanthe aquatica
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically categorized under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae and is identified by the genus Oenanthe.
