Stephania viridiflavens

Stephania viridiflavens, commonly known as a significant medicinal climber, belongs to the Menispermaceae family. Native to tropical regions, this plant is highly valued in traditional ethnobotany. It contains unique bioactive alkaloids, which are being extensively researched for their potential therapeutic properties, particularly in treating inflammatory and neurological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Stephania viridiflavens
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, specifically within the family Menispermaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Stephania as Stephania viridiflavens.
