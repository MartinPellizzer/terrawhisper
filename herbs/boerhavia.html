Spiderlings, scientifically known as Boerhavia, is a versatile genus of medicinal herbs found in tropical regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, particularly Boerhavia diffusa, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, diuretic, and hepatoprotective properties. These resilient plants offer significant therapeutic potential for treating liver ailments, skin disorders, and various systemic inflammations.
