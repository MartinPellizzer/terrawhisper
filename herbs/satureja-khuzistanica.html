Satureja khuzistanica

Saturaj khuizistanica, commonly known as Khuzistan savory, is a distinctive aromatic herb native to the arid regions of Iran. Renowned for its potent essential oils, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat digestive ailments, respiratory issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential in pharmacology.
