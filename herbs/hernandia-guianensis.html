Hernandia guianensis

Hernandia guianenis, commonly known as a versatile medicinal plant, holds significant importance in traditional healing practices. Native to tropical regions, this species is prized for its diverse therapeutic properties. Researchers continue to explore its phytochemical composition to uncover potential applications in treating various ailments and developing new pharmacological treatments.

  • Scientific name: Hernandia guianensis
  • Family: Hernandiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Laurales and the family Hernandiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Hernandia, specifically as the species Hernandia guianenis.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions in the Caribbean and Northern South America. Within the Caribbean, its presence is specifically noted in the islands of Trinidad and Tobago. Moving into the mainland, it can be found throughout the coastal and interior territories of Guyana and Suriname. Its range also extends further west to include the nation of Venezuela. Finally, the species is documented within the lush landscapes of French Guiana.
