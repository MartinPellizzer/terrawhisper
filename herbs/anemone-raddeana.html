Anemone raddeana, scientifically known as Anemone raddeana, is a resilient perennial herb native to the Himalayan highlands. Renowned for its delicate white blossoms and hardy nature, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, offering unique bioactive properties essential to regional botanical heritage.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is well-documented within the Amur and Khabarovsk regions. It can also be found throughout the Primorye territory and on the island of Sakhalin. Beyond the Russian borders, the species extends its distribution into the Manchuria region of China. Consequently, its habitat is defined by these interconnected areas of the North Pacific rim.
