Bruguiera gymnorhiza

Bruguiera gymnorhiza, commonly known as the large-leafed manilora, is a significant mangrove species found in tropical Indo-Pacific coasts. Beyond its ecological role in stabilizing shorelines, this plant holds medicinal potential. Traditional practices utilize its bark and leaves to treat various ailments, highlighting its importance in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Bruguiera gymnorhiza
  • Family: Rhizophoraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is classified under the family Rhizophoraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Bruguiea.

This plant exhibits a wide geographical range across several regions of the African continent. In Northeast Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in both Djibouti and Somalia. Its presence extends into East Tropical Africa, specifically within the countries of Kenya and Tanzania.
