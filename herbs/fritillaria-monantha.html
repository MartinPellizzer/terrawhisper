Fritillaria monantha

What's the taxonomical classification of Fritillaria monantha?

Fritillaria monantha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is positioned in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Liliales. This plant is a member of the family Liliaceae, specifically placed within the genus Fritillaria, and is identified by its specific species name, monantha.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Friillaria monantha has a single, bell-shaped flower that hangs from a slender stem, typically emerging from a bulbous underground structure. The solitary blossom features a pale, yellowish-green corolla often marked with distinct dark spots or brownish stripes. Its leaves are narrow and lanceolate, arranged sparsely along the stem to minimize energy expenditure during flowering. The stem itself is relatively thin and upright, supporting the weight of the lone bloom. These morphological traits collectively distinguish it from other species within the genus that often produce multiple flowers per stem.

What is the geographical distribution of this plant?

It primarily inhabits alpine and subalpine zones located at elevations between 3,000 and 4,500 meters. The species thrives in moist, rocky slopes and grassy meadows where seasonal snowmelt provides sufficient moisture. Its distribution is highly fragmented and restricted to specific microclimates within these mountain ranges.
