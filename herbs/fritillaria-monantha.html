Fritillaria monantha

Fritillaria monantha, commonly known as the mountain fritillary, is a delicate perennial herb native to high-altitude regions. Renowned in traditional herbal medicine, this species is valued for its unique bioactive compounds. It plays a significant role in botanical studies, offering potential therapeutic benefits for various respiratory and inflammatory conditions.
