Swertia brownii

Swertiä brownii, commonly known as Swertia, is a significant medicinal herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, this plant possesses potent bioactive compounds. It is widely utilized for its bitter properties, offering therapeutic benefits for digestive health, fever reduction, and managing various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Swertia brownii
  • Family: Gentianaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Swertia brownii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Gentianales. Finally, it is a member of the Gentianaceae family and is categorized under the genus Swertia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the borders of Burundi and Rwanda. The species also extends its range into the vast territory of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Moving toward the northeast, it is documented growing in the highlands of Ethiopia. Finally, its geographical presence reaches into East Tropical Africa, specifically within Kenya.
