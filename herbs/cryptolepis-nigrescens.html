Cryptolepis nigrescens, scientifically known as Cryptolepis nigrescens, is a medicinal shrub native to West Africa. Renowned for its potent bioactive alkaloids, particularly cryptolepine, this plant is traditionally used to treat malaria, skin infections, and inflammatory conditions. Its significant pharmacological properties continue to drive modern scientific research and drug development.

Distribution

This plant, Cryptolepis nigrescens, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence can be documented within the borders of Benin and Burkina Faso. Additionally, the species is found throughout the territory of Ghana. The geographical range extends further into Guinea-Bissau as well. Finally, the plant is also naturally occurring within the country of Guinea.

The environmental dynamics of these forested areas near the shoreline create a unique niche, integrating the moisture-retaining properties of the forest canopy with the salt-influenced air of the coastal zone.
