Cryptolepis nigrescens

Cryptolepis nigrescens, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in tropical regions, holds profound ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from skin inflammations to digestive issues. Ongoing research continues to explore its potential pharmacological applications in modern medicine.

  • Scientific name: Cryptolepis nigrescens
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is a member of the family Apocynaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Cryptolepis.

Common Names

This plant, Cryptolepis nigrescens, is known by various common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as West African Quassia, while in French, it is called Quassia d'Afrique. In local contexts, such as in Ghana, it is commonly known as Asante Herb or Asante Medicine.

Distribution

This plant, Cryptolepis nigrescens, is primarily distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence can be documented within the borders of Benin and Burkina Faso. Additionally, the species is found throughout the territory of Ghana. The geographical range extends further into Guinea-Bissau as well. Finally, the plant is also naturally occurring within the country of Guinea.
