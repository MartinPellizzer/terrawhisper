Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Erythrina mulungu, is highly valued in traditional medicine for its various therapeutic properties derived from specific anatomical structures. The most significant components used for medicinal preparations are the stem barks, which are often decocted to create extracts used for their calming and sedative effects. Specifically, the thick stem bark is carefully harvested to ensure the potency of the active compounds. In addition to the stem barks, the vibrant flowers are also recognized for their role within the plant's biological profile, though the bark remains the primary focus for those seeking its psychoactive benefits.
