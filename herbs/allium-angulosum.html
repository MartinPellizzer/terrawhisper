Allium angulosum

Allium angulosum, commonly known as wild garlic or angular garlic, is a versatile perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned for its pungent aroma and culinary utility, this plant offers significant medicinal benefits, including antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, making it a valuable subject in traditional herbal medicine practices.
