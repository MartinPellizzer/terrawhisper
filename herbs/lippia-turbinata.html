Lippia turbinata

Lippia turbinata, commonly known as wild basil or African basil, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to sub-Saharan Africa. Renowned for its aromatic foliage, it is widely used in traditional medicine to treat ailments such as digestive issues, fever, and inflammation, offering significant bioactive potential for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Lippia turbinata
  • Family: Verbenaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales and is a member of the family Verbenaceae. Within this family, it is specifically identified under the genus Lippia.
