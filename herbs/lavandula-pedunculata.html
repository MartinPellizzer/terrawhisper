Spanish lavender, scientifically known as Lavandula pedunculata, is a resilient, aromatic shrub native to the Mediterranean. Renowned for its striking violet blooms and silver-green foliage, it is widely used in herbal medicine for its calming properties, antiseptic qualities, and ability to soothe skin irritations and reduce anxiety effectively.
