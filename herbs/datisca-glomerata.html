Datisca glomerata

Datisca glomerata, commonly known as White Datura, is a perennial herbaceous plant belonging to the Berberidaceae family. Native to Europe and parts of Asia, it is characterized by its striking white flowers. While historically used in traditional medicine, it contains potent alkaloids, requiring careful handling due to its toxicity.
