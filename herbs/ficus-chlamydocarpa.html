Ficus chlamydocarpa

Ficus chlamydocarpa, commonly known as a medicinal fig, is a significant plant species valued in traditional herbal medicine. Primarily found in tropical regions, it possesses unique bioactive properties. Researchers explore its various extracts for potential therapeutic applications, focusing on its ability to provide antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial benefits.

  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales, it is classified under the family Moraceae and the genus Ficus. Specifically, it is identified by the species name Ficus chlamydocarpa.
