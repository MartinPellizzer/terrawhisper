Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, it can be found throughout the South-Central and Southeast territories. Its range also extends into the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, the species is present in the state of Assam. Finally, it is widely distributed across the East Himalaya region.
