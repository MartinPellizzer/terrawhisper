What's the taxonomical classification of Bulbostylis densa?

Bulbostylis densa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the lineage, the plant is part of the order Poales and falls under the family Cyperaceae. Finally, it is identified within the genus Bulbostylis, specifically as the species densa.
