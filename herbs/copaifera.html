Copaifera

Copaifera, commonly known as Copaiba, is a versatile medicinal tree native to tropical South America. Renowned for its aromatic oleoresin, this plant offers powerful anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties. Historically used in traditional medicine, its potent oils are now widely studied for treating skin conditions and systemic inflammation.

  • Scientific name: Copaifera
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Copaifera.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within West Tropical Africa. Specifically, it can be found growing in the nation of Ghana. Its presence extends further into the coastal and inland areas of Guinea-Bissau. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit various parts of Guinea. Finally, its geographical range includes the territories of Ivory Coast and Liberia.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Copaiba, is highly valued for its therapeutic properties derived primarily from its unique biological secretions. The most significant medicinal component is the oleoresin, a viscous substance produced within the tree. To harvest this liquid, specialized incisions are made into the trunk, allowing the precious oleoresins to flow out for collection. This substance, extracted directly from the woody structure, serves as the foundation for various traditional and modern medicinal applications.
