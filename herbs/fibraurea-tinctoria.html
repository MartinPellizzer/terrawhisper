Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in South and Southeast Asia. Within the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found throughout India, with a specific presence noted in Assam. Its range extends into the Indo-China region, where it is documented in Cambodia. Additionally, the species is known to inhabit the landscapes of Laos. Finally, its geographical reach includes the territory of Myanmar.

Plant Parts

This plant, Fibraurea tinctoria, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, including its woody stems and the singular central stem that supports its climbing structure. The medicinal value is often concentrated in the root, specifically within the thick root bark which is harvested for its bioactive compounds. Additionally, the lush green leaves and the overall network of stems contribute to the plant's physiological profile, making the entire root and foliage system significant in botanical studies.
