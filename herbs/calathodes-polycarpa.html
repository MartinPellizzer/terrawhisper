What's the taxonomical classification of Calathodes polycarpa?

Calathodes polycarpa belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the taxonomic lineage of the order Ranunculales. This plant is a representative of the family Ranunculaceae, falling under the genus Calathodes, and is specifically identified by its species name, polycarpa.
