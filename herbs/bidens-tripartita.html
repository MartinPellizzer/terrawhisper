The reproduction of Bidens tripartita is primarily sexual, characterized by bisexual flowers that facilitate the reproductive process. The flowering period typically begins in July and concludes in September, though the timing can be variable. Pollination is achieved through biotic means, specifically involving insects, with bees serving as a key pollinator. While self-fertilization is present within the species, the plant relies on these insect visitors to ensure successful fertilization. Following pollination, the plant produces indehiscent fruits categorized as achenes. The seeds contained within these fruits are quite small, possessing a mean mass of approximately 0.00276 g, with individual seed masses ranging from a minimum of 0.0011 g to a maximum of 0.003081 g.
