Three-lobe beggartick, scientifically known as Bidens tripartita, is a resilient annual herb valued in traditional medicine. Often found in wetland habitats, this plant is recognized by its distinctive trifoliate leaves. It is widely studied for its diverse bioactive compounds, offering potential therapeutic benefits in managing inflammation and oxidative stress.
