Bidens tripartita

Three-lobe beggartick

Bidens tripartita, commonly known as three-lobe bidens, is a versatile medicinal herb valued in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. Its unique botanical properties make it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies and natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Bidens tripartita
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Bidens.
