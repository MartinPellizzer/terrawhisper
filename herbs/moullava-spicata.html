Moullava spicata

Moullava spicata, commonly known as the spiked medicinal herb, is a versatile botanical treasure valued in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating inflammatory conditions and supporting digestive health through its unique phytochemical profile and natural healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Moullava spicata
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and is specifically identified by the genus Moullava.
