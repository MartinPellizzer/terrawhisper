Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across the African continent. In East Tropical Africa, its presence is noted within the borders of Tanzania. Moving toward the southern regions, it is found throughout several South Tropical African nations. Specifically, its habitat includes the territories of Malawi and Mozambique.
