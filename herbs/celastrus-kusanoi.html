What's the taxonomical classification of Celastrus kusanoi?

Celastrus kusanoi belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant's taxonomic progression continues through the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae, ultimately placing it within the genus Celastrus. Finally, its specific identity is defined by the species designation kusanoi.
