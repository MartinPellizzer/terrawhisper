Strychnopsis thouarsii

Strychnopsis thouarsii, commonly known as the "thousardier" or "thousardi," is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical African forests. Renowned in traditional ethnobotany, its various parts are utilized to treat diverse ailments, including skin infections, inflammation, and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research.
