Peperomia pellucida

Peperomia pellucidum, commonly known as shiny bush or peperomia, is a succulent medicinal herb cherished in traditional medicine. Characterized by its translucent, fleshy leaves, this plant offers diverse therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, and antioxidant properties. It is widely utilized to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive discomfort.

  • Scientific name: Peperomia pellucida
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Piperaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Peperomia.
