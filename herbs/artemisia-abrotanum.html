Artemisia abrotanum

Artemisia abrotanum, commonly known as Abrotanum, is a perennial herb belonging to the Asteraceae family. Renowned in traditional medicine, this aromatic plant contains essential oils used historically for digestive support and as an anthelmintic. Its distinct fragrance and potent bioactive compounds make it a significant subject in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia abrotanum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Asterales. Finally, it is a member of the family Asteraceae and is identified by the genus Artemisia.
