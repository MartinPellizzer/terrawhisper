Aglaia roxburghiana

Aglaiia roxburghiana, commonly known as the Roxburghia Aglai, is a significant medicinal evergreen tree native to South and Southeast Asia. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its various parts, including bark and leaves, possess potent bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various digestive disorders effectively.
