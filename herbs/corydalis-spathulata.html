Corydalis spathulata

What's the taxonomical classification of Corydalis spathulata?

Corydalis spatulatha belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Ranunculales. This plant is a member of the family Papaveraceae, which places it within the genus Corydalis, specifically identifying it as the species spatulatha.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Corydalis spathulata has deeply lobed, spatulate leaves that are typically bright green and arranged in a basal rosette or along ascending stems. The foliage features prominent venation and distinctively shaped margins that define its delicate appearance. Its flowering stems bear zygomorphic, tubular flowers that often transition from yellow to orange or reddish hues. These blooms are characterized by a long, nectar-producing spur extending from the rear of the corolla. The plant's overall structure is herbaceous and relatively low-growing, suited for moist, shaded environments.
