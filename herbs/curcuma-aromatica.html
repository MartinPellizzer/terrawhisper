Curcuma aromatica

Curcumma aromatica, commonly known as aromatic turmeric, is a precious medicinal plant prized for its potent bioactive compounds. Renowned for its strong, spicy fragrance, this rhizomatous herb offers significant anti-inflammatory and antioxidant benefits. It serves as a vital natural remedy in traditional medicine for supporting holistic wellness and healing.

  • Scientific name: Curcuma aromatica
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized under the order Zingiberales and the family Zingiberaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Curcuma.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several distinct regions in Asia. In China, its distribution is widespread, spanning from the South-Central provinces to the Southeast regions. It can also be found in the tropical environment of Hainan and the high-altitude terrains of Tibet. Beyond China, the species extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the Assam region. Together, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive in varied ecological zones.
