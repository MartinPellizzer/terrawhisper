Cynara cornigera

Cynara cornigera, commonly known as the artichoke thistle, is a striking perennial plant native to South America. Renowned for its dramatic, silvery-green foliage and large, purple flower heads, it serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. Historically, its unique botanical properties have been explored for various traditional healing applications.

  • Scientific name: Cynara cornigera
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and is specifically identified by the genus Cynara.
