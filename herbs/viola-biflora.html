Viola biflora

Twoflower violet

Viola biflora, commonly known as the two-flowered violet, is a delicate perennial herb native to North America. Characterized by its unique ability to produce two flowers per stem, this charming wildflower thrives in moist, shaded woodland habitats, offering subtle beauty and ecological value to its native forest ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Viola biflora
  • Family: Violaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Violaceae and is situated within the genus Viola.
