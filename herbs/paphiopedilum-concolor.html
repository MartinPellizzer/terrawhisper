What's the taxonomical classification of Paphiopedilum concolor?

Paphiopediluum concolor belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asparagales. This plant is a member of the family Orchidaceae, specifically situated within the genus Paphiopedilum, and is identified by the species name concolor.
