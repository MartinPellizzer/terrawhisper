Magnolia dealbata

Magnolia dealbata, commonly known as the white magnolia, is a graceful deciduous shrub prized for its large, fragrant white blooms. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this species holds historical significance in traditional herbal practices, where its bark and flowers are studied for their potential therapeutic and calming medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Magnolia dealbata
  • Family: Magnoliaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated in the order Magnoliales and the family Magnoliaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement within the genus Magnolia.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Magnolia dealbata, is referred to by various common names depending on the linguistic and regional context. In Nahuatl, it is identified as Ēlōxōchicuahuitl (also documented as Eleoxochitl), while in Persian, it is known as مگنولیا نقره‌ای or ماجنوليا ديلباتا. Additionally, in Spanish-speaking contexts, it is sometimes referred to as Magnolia macrophylla var. dealbata.
