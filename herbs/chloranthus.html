Chloranthus

Chloranthus, commonly known as the "dragon herb" in traditional medicine, is a genus of flowering plants prized for its therapeutic properties. Often found in moist, shaded forest habitats, these resilient species contain bioactive compounds used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and fever, making them essential in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Chloranthus
  • Family: Chloranthaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Chloranthales and the family Chloranthaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Chloranthus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is documented in the Amur, Kuril Islands, and Primorye regions. Moving southward, the species is widely established throughout South-Central China. Its range also extends to the tropical environment of Hainan. Consequently, these diverse locations highlight the plant's ability to inhabit various climates across the continent.
