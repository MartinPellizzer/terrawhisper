Ophiorrhiza japonica belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ophiorrhiza and the species japonica.
