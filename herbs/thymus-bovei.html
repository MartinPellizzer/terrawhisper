Thymus bovei

Thymus bovei, commonly known as the cattle thyme, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the arid regions of East Africa. Characterized by its woody structure and aromatic foliage, this species belongs to the Lamiaceae family and holds significant ethnobotanical value for its potential antimicrobial and therapeutic medicinal properties.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
