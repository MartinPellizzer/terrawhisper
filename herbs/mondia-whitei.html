White's-ginger

Mondia whitei · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (35 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

White's-ginger, scientifically known as Mondia whitei, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the African savannah. Renowned for its aromatic roots, it is traditionally used to enhance libido, treat digestive issues, and boost vitality. This potent botanical holds significant ethnobotanical value and continues to attract modern pharmacological interest.

Plant Parts

This plant, Mondia whitei, is a climbing shrub whose medicinal properties are primarily concentrated in its specialized anatomical structures. The most significant part used in traditional medicine is the large, fleshy roots, which are harvested for their various bioactive compounds. In addition to the main taproot system, the plant develops adventitious roots that emerge from the stem to assist in climbing and anchoring itself to host trees. Furthermore, while the roots are the primary focus of extraction, the fruit also serves as a distinct reproductive part of the plant, contributing to its overall biological profile.
