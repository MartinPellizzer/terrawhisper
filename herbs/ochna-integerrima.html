This plant, known scientifically as Ochna integerrima, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales. It is further classified into the family Ochnaceae and the genus Ochna.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it can be found in the Hainan province as well as across China Southeast. Its presence extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically located in Assam and Bangladesh. Furthermore, the species is documented within the Indo-China region, including the Andaman Islands. These varied locations highlight the plant's preference for these particular tropical and subtropical environments.
