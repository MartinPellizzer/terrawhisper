Ochna integerrima

Ochna integerrima, commonly known as the Wild Almond, is a resilient shrub valued in traditional medicine. Native to various African regions, its leaves and roots are utilized to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations. This plant serves as a vital botanical resource in ethnopharmacological studies.

  • Scientific name: Ochna integerrima
  • Family: Ochnaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Ochna integerrima, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is categorized under the order Malpighiales. It is further classified into the family Ochnaceae and the genus Ochna.
