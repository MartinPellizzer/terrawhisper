Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several key regions in Asia. Within China, it is notably found in the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan provinces. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, where it is documented in the state of Assam. Additionally, the species can be located within the borders of Bangladesh. These specific locations highlight the subtropical and tropical habitats preferred by this medicinal plant.
