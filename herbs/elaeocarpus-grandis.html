Blue quandong, scientifically known as Elaeocarpus grandis, is a versatile tropical tree highly valued in traditional medicine. Native to Southeast Asia, its various parts, including the bark and leaves, possess significant therapeutic properties. It is widely utilized to treat ailments such as inflammation, skin conditions, and digestive issues.
