Leucas martinicensis, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb, belongs to the Fabaceae family. Found in specific ecological niches, this plant is valued for its unique bioactive compounds. Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range stretching across several distinct regions. Within the Macaronesia archipelago, its presence is specifically noted in Cape Verde. Moving towards the mainland, it is widely distributed throughout West Tropical Africa. Its documented habitats include the nations of Benin, Burkina, and Gambia. Additionally, the species can be found inhabiting the coastal and inland areas of Ghana.
