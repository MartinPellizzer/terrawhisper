Euphorbia characias wulfenii, scientifically known as Euphorbia characias subsp. wulfenii, is a striking Mediterranean perennial celebrated for its architectural foliage and lime-green bracts. While primarily ornamental, it holds traditional significance in herbal studies, requiring careful handling due to its milky, caustic latex which possesses potent bioactive properties.
