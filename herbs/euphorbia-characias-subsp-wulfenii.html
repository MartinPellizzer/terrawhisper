Euphorbia characias subsp. wulfenii

Euphorbia characias wulfenii

Euphorbia characias subsp. wulfenii, commonly known as Wulfen's Mediterranean Spurge, is a striking perennial prized for its architectural foliage. While primarily ornamental, this plant holds traditional significance in herbal studies. Its unique milky latex contains bioactive compounds, though extreme caution is required due to its potent skin irritant properties.

  • Scientific name: Euphorbia characias subsp. wulfenii
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, it is a member of the family Euphorbiaceae. Ultimately, it is classified under the genus Euphorbia as the subspecies wulfenii.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated primarily within the Mediterranean region. In Southwestern Europe, its presence is noted within the borders of France. Moving toward Southeastern Europe, the subspecies can be found across Albania and Greece. It also maintains a significant presence in the Italian peninsula. Furthermore, its range extends to the island of Kriti, completing its regional footprint.
