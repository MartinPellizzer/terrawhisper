Haloxylon salicornicum

Haloxylon salicornicum, commonly known as Sea-Glass Willow, is a resilient halophyte found in coastal salt marshes. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, this medicinal plant offers potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties. Traditionally used in maritime folk medicine, it serves as a vital resource for developing modern therapeutic treatments.

  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Africa
