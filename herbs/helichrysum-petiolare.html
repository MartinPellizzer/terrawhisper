Helichrysum petiolare

Helichrysum petiolare, commonly known as the Rock Everlasting, is a resilient perennial shrub prized for its aromatic foliage. Native to diverse terrains, this medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential. Renowned for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is widely utilized in traditional remedies to support skin health and wellness.

  • Scientific name: Helichrysum petiolare
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Helichrysum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution across several regions of Europe. In Northern Europe, its presence can be documented in both Great Britain and Ireland. Moving toward Middle Europe, the species is also found within the borders of Austria. Its range extends into Southwestern Europe, specifically inhabiting the island of Corse. Finally, the distribution continues further west into the country of Portugal.

Plant Parts

This plant, Helichrysum petiolare, is highly valued in aromatherapy and herbal medicine primarily for its chemical composition rather than its physical structure. While the entire aerial portion of the plant can be used, its most significant medicinal component resides within the essential oils extracted from its flowers and foliage. These oils contain a complex profile of bioactive compounds that provide therapeutic benefits, making the plant a vital resource for creating aromatic extracts used to support skin health and emotional well-being.
