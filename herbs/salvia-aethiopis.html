Salvia aethiopis

African sage

Salvia aethiopis, commonly known as African sage, is a resilient perennial herb native to various African landscapes. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and distinctive flowering spikes, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. It is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its profound therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Salvia aethiopis
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae, it is classified under the genus Salvia.
