Terminalia neotaliala

Terminalia neotaliala, commonly known as the Blackwood, is a majestic rainforest tree native to Australia. Valued for its high-quality timber and ecological importance, this species plays a vital role in forest ecosystems. In traditional medicine, various parts of the tree are utilized for their diverse therapeutic and healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Terminalia neotaliala
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Myrtales, falling within the family Combretaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Terminalia.
