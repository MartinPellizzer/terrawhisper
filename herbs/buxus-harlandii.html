Buxus harlandii

What's the taxonomical classification of Buxus harlandii?

Buxus harlandii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Buxales. This plant is a member of the family Buxaceae, situated within the genus Buxus, and is specifically identified by the species name harlandii.
