Arbutus pavarii

Arbutus pavarii, commonly known as the Chinese strawberry tree, is a significant medicinal shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, particularly arbutin, it is extensively utilized in traditional medicine to treat urinary tract infections, skin conditions, and inflammatory ailments, offering promising therapeutic potential in pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Arbutus pavarii
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Africa

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, falling into the family Ericaceae.
