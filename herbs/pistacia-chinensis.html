Pistacia chinensis

Pistacia chinensis, commonly known as Chinese mastic tree, is a versatile medicinal plant revered in traditional practices. Renowned for its resinous properties, it offers significant therapeutic benefits, including anti-inflammatory and antioxidant effects. This resilient species serves as a vital natural resource for treating various ailments across diverse cultures.

  • Scientific name: Pistacia chinensis
  • Family: Anacardiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Sapindales, and the family Anacardiaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Pistacia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning several significant regions across Asia. In Central Asia, its presence is notably documented within Tajikistan. Moving toward the Caucasus region, it can be found throughout the Transcaucasus area. Its range extends into Western Asia, specifically within the borders of Afghanistan. Finally, in China, the species is distributed across both the South-Central provinces and the island of Hainan.
