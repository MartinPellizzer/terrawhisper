Cissampelos capensis

Cisampelos capeensis, commonly known as the Cape Climber, is a fascinating climbing shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it has traditionally been used in various herbal remedies. This resilient plant features distinctive foliage and serves as a vital component in ethnobotanical studies regarding natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Cissampelos capensis
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is classified under the family Menispermaceae. It is specifically identified by its genus, Cissampelos.
