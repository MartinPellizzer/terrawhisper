Artemisia terrae-albae

Artemisia terrae-albae, commonly known as White Earth Wormwood, is a distinctive medicinal herb prized for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific arid regions, this plant offers potent antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits. Traditional practitioners utilize its aromatic leaves to support digestive health and alleviate various inflammatory conditions effectively.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia terrae-albae
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
