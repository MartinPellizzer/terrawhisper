Lithocarpus pachylepis

Lithocarpus pachylepis, commonly known as a species of stone oak, is a significant medicinal plant found in diverse forest ecosystems. Renowned for its unique phytochemical profile, it offers potential therapeutic benefits. Research focuses on its bioactive compounds, which may provide valuable applications in traditional medicine and modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Lithocarpus pachylepis
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Fagaceae. Specifically, it is identified by its genus, Lithocarpus, and its species name, pachylepis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within East and Southeast Asia. In China, it can be found spanning across the South-Central regions. It is also widely distributed throughout the Southeast portions of China. Beyond Chinese borders, the species extends into the Indo-China peninsula. Specifically, its range includes the territory of Vietnam.
