Flacourtia indica

Flacourtia indica, commonly known as the Indian Screw Tree, is a versatile tropical evergreen tree found across coastal regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, its leaves, bark, and fruits are utilized in traditional medicine to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, offering significant ethnobotanical value.

  • Scientific name: Flacourtia indica
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Malpighiales, and the family Salicaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Flacourtia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated within the tropical regions of the African continent. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found growing in the countries of Burundi, Congo, and Rwanda. Its presence is also well-documented within the vast territory of the Democratic Republic of the Congo. Furthermore, the species extends its reach into Northeast Tropical Africa. Specifically, this particular region includes the nation of Chad as part of its natural habitat.
