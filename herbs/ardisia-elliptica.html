Inkberry, scientifically known as Ardisia elliptica, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its glossy, elliptical leaves and small, delicate flowers, it serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat diverse ailments and inflammations.

Plant Parts

This plant, Ardisia elliptica, is characterized by several distinct morphological features used in its study and application. The leaves are typically dark green and glossy, providing a lush appearance to the shrub. Each individual leaf is elliptic in shape with an entire margin, serving as a primary site for photosynthesis. Furthermore, the plant produces a small, fleshy fruit that often turns a deep red or black color when ripe, which is a key identifying characteristic of the species.
