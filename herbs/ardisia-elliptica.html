Ardisia elliptica

Ardisia elliptica, commonly known as the Evergreen Ardisia, is a versatile tropical shrub prized for its glossy, elliptical foliage and delicate bell-shaped flowers. Renowned in traditional medicine, various parts of this plant are utilized for their therapeutic properties, offering potential benefits in treating inflammation and supporting overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Ardisia elliptica
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is organized into the order Ericales and the family Primulaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Ardisia, specifically as the species Ardisia elliptica.
