Inkberry, scientifically known as Ardisia elliptica, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to Southeast Asia. Renowned for its glossy, elliptical leaves and small, delicate flowers, it serves both ornamental and medicinal purposes. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat diverse ailments and inflammations.
