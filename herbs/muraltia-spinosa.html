Muraltia spinosa

Muraltia spinosa, commonly known as the Thorny Muraltia, is a resilient, deciduous shrub native to tropical regions. Characterized by its distinctive thorny branches and delicate flowers, this medicinal plant is highly valued in traditional practices for its diverse therapeutic properties, particularly in treating skin ailments and various inflammatory conditions.

  • Scientific name: Muraltia spinosa
  • Family: Polygalaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Muraltia spinosa, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Fabales. Furthermore, it is a member of the family Polygalaceae and is categorized under the genus Muraltia.
