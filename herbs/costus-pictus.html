Costus pictus

Costus pictus, commonly known as the Crepe Ginger, is a stunning tropical perennial celebrated for its striking, paper-like flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, often utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and healing properties within various holistic wellness traditions worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Costus pictus
  • Family: Costaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales and the family Costaceae, it is classified under the genus Costus.
