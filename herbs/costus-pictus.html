Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Crepe Ginger, utilizes various components for its medicinal and biological properties. The large, lanceolate leaves are a prominent feature, often studied for their ability to help regulate blood sugar levels. Below the surface, the thick, fleshy rhizomes serve as the primary storage organs and are frequently used in traditional remedies due to their concentrated bioactive compounds. Whether examining the structural integrity of a single leaf or the nutrient-rich underground stems, each part of Costus pictus contributes to its unique profile in herbal medicine.
