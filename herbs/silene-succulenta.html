Silene succulenta

Catchfly

Silene succulenta, commonly known as the succulent campion, is a distinctive perennial herb native to rocky, mountainous regions. Characterized by its fleshy, moisture-retaining leaves and delicate, star-shaped flowers, this resilient species thrives in challenging terrains, offering unique botanical interest and potential medicinal properties within its specialized ecological niche.

  • Scientific name: Silene succulenta
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Silene succulenta, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Caryophyllaceae and the genus Silene.
