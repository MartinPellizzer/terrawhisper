Persian-hyssop

Thymbra capitata · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (692 studies) · ★★★★★

Persian-hyssop, scientifically known as Thymbra capitata, is a fragrant perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern regions. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this aromatic herb is highly valued for its essential oils. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine and culinary practices for its therapeutic properties.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various regions of Southwestern Europe. Its presence is specifically noted in the Balearic Islands, known as Baleares. It can also be found growing in the island of Corsica. Furthermore, the species inhabits the coastal and inland areas of Portugal. Finally, its geographical range extends to Sardegna and throughout Spain.
