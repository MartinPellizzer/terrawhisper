Persian-hyssop, scientifically known as Thymbra capitata, is a fragrant perennial shrub native to the Mediterranean and Middle Eastern regions. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this aromatic herb is highly valued for its essential oils. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine and culinary practices for its therapeutic properties.
