Jasminum yunnanense

What's the taxonomical classification of Jasminum yunnanense?

Jasminum yunnanense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. This plant is a member of the order Lamiales and falls under the family Oleaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized within the genus Jasminum, with its specific identification being the species yunnanense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Jasminum yunnanense has woody, climbing stems that produce opposite, elliptical to ovate leaves and fragrant, salverform white flowers with five spreading lobes. The foliage typically consists of dark green, glossy leaflets that can grow up to 10 centimeters in length. Its flowering structure features clusters of tubular corollas that emerge from the leaf axils during the warmer months. The plant's stems are highly flexible, allowing it to twine around supports or other vegetation to ascend toward sunlight. This shrubby climber often develops a dense canopy of greenery as its sprawling branches mature.
