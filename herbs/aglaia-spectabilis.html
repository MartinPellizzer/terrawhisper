Aglaia spectabilis

Aglaia spectabilis, commonly known as the fragrant Aglaiia, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Renowned for its aromatic properties, it is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat various ailments. This species holds significant ethnobotanical importance, offering promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Aglaia spectabilis
  • Family: Meliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Meliaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Aglaiia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across parts of Asia, primarily concentrated within the Indian Subcontinent. It can be found growing in several key regions, including India and Bangladesh. Furthermore, its range extends into the East Himalaya and the state of Assam. Beyond the subcontinent, the species is also documented in the South-Central region of China. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's preference for these specific subtropical environments.
