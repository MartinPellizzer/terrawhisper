Banksia attenuata

Banksia attenuata, commonly known as the candle banksia, is a majestic evergreen shrub native to Western Australia. Characterized by its striking, elongated flower spikes and serrated leaves, it plays a vital role in its ecosystem, providing essential nectar for birds and insects while stabilizing sandy coastal soils.

  • Scientific name: Banksia attenuata
  • Family: Proteaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Proteales, it is further categorized under the family Proteaceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Banksia.
