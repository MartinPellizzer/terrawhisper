Bush tomato, scientifically known as Solanum centrale, is a resilient desert shrub native to Australia. Highly valued by Indigenous communities, this nutrient-dense fruit offers unique savory flavors and significant medicinal properties. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, it serves as both a functional food and a traditional remedy for wellness.
