Yarrow, scientifically known as Achillea crithmifolia, is a resilient perennial herb celebrated for its aromatic foliage and delicate white blooms. Often found in rocky terrains, this medicinal plant has a long history of use in traditional healing to support wound recovery, reduce inflammation, and promote overall skin health.
