Achillea crithmifolia

Achillea crithmifolia, commonly known as the creeping yarrow, is a resilient perennial native to Eastern Asia. Distinguished by its creeping rhizomes and delicate white flower clusters, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its soothing properties, offering unique botanical charm to both healing gardens and natural landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Achillea crithmifolia
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae and the genus Achillea.
