Hypericum monanthemum

What's the taxonomical classification of Hypericum monanthemum?

Hypericum monanthemum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Following its taxonomic hierarchy, it is placed in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Malpighiales, the plant is categorized under the family Hypericaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Hypericum and is specifically designated as the species monanthemum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Hypericum monanthemum has opposite, oblong leaves that measure 2 to 5 centimeters in length and small, five-petaled yellow flowers with numerous prominent stamens. The plant grows from a creeping rhizome, forming dense colonies in shaded, moist habitats. Its stems are typically square in cross-section and can reach heights of up to 30 centimeters. The bright yellow petals often feature tiny black dots, a characteristic common to the genus. Following flowering, the plant produces small, capsule-like seed pods that facilitate reproduction.
