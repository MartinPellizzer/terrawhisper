Cyclospermum leptophyllum

Cyclospermum leptophyllum, commonly known as the slender-leaved starfruit, is a distinctive medicinal herb valued in traditional practices. Characterized by its delicate, elongated foliage, this plant offers diverse therapeutic potential. Often utilized for its unique bioactive properties, it serves as a fascinating subject for ethnobotanical research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Cyclospermum leptophyllum
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is specifically nested within the family Apiaceae. Its taxonomic identity is further defined by its placement in the genus Cyclospermum.
