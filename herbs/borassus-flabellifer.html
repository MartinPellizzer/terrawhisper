Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the Palmyra palm, possesses a unique reproductive structure where the male and female flowers are produced on separate inflorescences. The male flowers are small, numerous, and clustered within a woody spathe, serving as a vital component of the palm's biological cycle. These male flowers are essential for the pollination process, as they produce the pollen necessary to fertilize the female flowers, ultimately ensuring the production of the palm's characteristic fruits and seeds.
