Borassus flabellifer

Borassus flaellifer, commonly known as the Palmyra palm, is a versatile tropical plant revered for its medicinal properties. Every part of this resilient tree offers therapeutic potential, from its nutrient-rich fruit and sap to its fibrous leaves. It is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments and support wellness.

  • Scientific name: Borassus flabellifer
  • Family: Arecaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Arecales and the family Arecaceae, it is classified under the genus Borassus. Its specific scientific name is Borassus flabellifer.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide and varied geographical distribution across several major regions. In West Tropical Africa, it can be found extending as far north as Mauritania. Its presence is also noted in Northeast Tropical Africa, specifically on the island of Socotra. Within the Asian continent, the species is located in South-Central China and across parts of the Indian Subcontinent, including Assam and Bangladesh. These diverse locations highlight the broad ecological range of this medicinal species.
