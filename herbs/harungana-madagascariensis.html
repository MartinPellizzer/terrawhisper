Dragon's-blood-tree, scientifically known as Harungana madagascariensis, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to Madagascar. Renowned for its vibrant, resinous sap, it has been utilized in traditional healing for centuries. This versatile species offers significant therapeutic potential, particularly in treating wounds and inflammation through its unique phytochemical properties.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the West Tropical Africa region. It can be found growing in the territories of Benin and Burkina.
