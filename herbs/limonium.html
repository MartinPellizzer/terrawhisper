Sea lavender, scientifically known as Limonium, is a resilient genus of flowering plants cherished for its vibrant, papery blooms. Thriving in coastal environments, these salt-tolerant perennials offer significant medicinal potential, particularly in traditional practices for treating inflammation and respiratory ailments, while adding ethereal beauty to any botanical collection.
