Saussurea salicifolia, is a resilient perennial herb native to the high-altitude alpine regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, particularly within Tibetan practices, this botanical species is valued for its therapeutic properties. It is often utilized to treat inflammatory conditions and respiratory ailments, showcasing remarkable medicinal potential.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread presence across several key regions within the vast landscape of Siberia. Specifically, its habitat includes the mountainous terrain of the Altai region. It is also found throughout the territory of Buryatia, stretching across the eastern parts of the continent. The distribution continues through the Chitа area, marking its presence in southeastern Siberia. Furthermore, the species is documented within the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk regions, completing its extensive geographical range.
