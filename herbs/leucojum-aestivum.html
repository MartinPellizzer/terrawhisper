Leucojum aestivum

Leucojum aestivum, commonly known as summer snowflake, is a perennial bulbous plant cherished for its delicate, bell-shaped white flowers. While primarily ornamental in gardens, it holds historical significance in traditional herbalism. This resilient woodland species thrives in moist soils, adding elegance and seasonal charm to temperate landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Leucojum aestivum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asparagales, it is a member of the family Amaryllidaceae. Its scientific classification concludes with the genus Leucojum, which identifies the species Leucojum aestivum.
