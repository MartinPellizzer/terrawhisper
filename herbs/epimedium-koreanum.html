Epimedium koreanum

Epimedium koreanum, commonly known as Korean Horny Goat Weed, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive flavonoids like icariin. These compounds are widely studied for their potential to support reproductive health, enhance vitality, and promote overall bone and cardiovascular wellness.

  • Scientific name: Epimedium koreanum
  • Family: Berberidaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Epimedium koreanum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the Berberidaceae family and falls under the genus Epimedium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions within Eastern Asia. In the northernmost reaches of its range, it can be found in the Primorye region of the Russian Far East. Its presence extends significantly into China, spanning both the Manchuria and China Southeast territories. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed throughout the Korean Peninsula in Eastern Asia. Finally, its geographical footprint includes the islands of Japan, completing its reach across the Eastern Asian landscape.
