Korean barrenwort, scientifically known as Epimedium koreanum, is a perennial herb native to the forest floors of East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this elegant plant features delicate, star-shaped flowers. It is highly valued for its therapeutic properties, particularly in supporting kidney health and balancing bodily energies.
