What's the taxonomical classification of Damnacanthus indicus?

Damnacanthus indicus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Gentianales and into the family Rubiaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Damnacanthus, and its unique species designation, indicus.
