Peumus boldus

Árnica

Peumus boldus, commonly known as boldo, is a medicinal shrub native to the central regions of Chile. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this evergreen plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine to support digestive health, stimulate bile production, and alleviate liver discomfort, offering a natural approach to wellness.

  • Scientific name: Peumus boldus
  • Family: Monimiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Laurales and the family Monimiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Peumus.
