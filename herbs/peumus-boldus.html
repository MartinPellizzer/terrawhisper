Árnica, scientifically known as Peumus boldus, is a native Chilean shrub celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Primarily utilized in traditional medicine, its leaves contain boldine, a potent alkaloid. This medicinal plant is widely recognized for supporting liver health, aiding digestion, and acting as a soothing digestive tonic.
