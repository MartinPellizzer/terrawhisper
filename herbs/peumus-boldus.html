Árnica, scientifically known as Peumus boldus, is a native Chilean shrub celebrated for its therapeutic properties. Primarily utilized in traditional medicine, its leaves contain boldine, a potent alkaloid. This medicinal plant is widely recognized for supporting liver health, aiding digestion, and acting as a soothing digestive tonic.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Boldo, utilizes various components for its medicinal properties, though the primary therapeutic focus is on its foliage. While the woody stems provide the structural support necessary for the shrub to grow, the most significant parts are the leaves, which contain the essential bioactive compounds. Specifically, the Leaves are harvested and processed to obtain concentrated Leaf Extracts, which are frequently used in herbal medicine to support digestive health and liver function.
