What's the taxonomical classification of Oresitrophe rupifraga?

Oresitrophe rupifraga belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. Moving through its hierarchical lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant is further categorized under the order Saxifragales and is a member of the family Saxifragaceae. Finally, it is defined by its specific taxonomic placement within the genus Oresitrophe and the species rupifraga.

According to a study published by "Heliyon", 1965 different metabolites were identified in the various tissues of Oresitrophe rupifraga Bunge. The study investigated metabolites in bulblets, leaves, and rhizomes to understand its medicinal benefits. Comparisons between bulblets and leaves revealed the differential expression of 461 metabolites, with bulblets having 49 higher and 412 lower metabolites than the leaves. The comparison between rhizomes and leaves showed 423 differentially expressed metabolites, while the comparison between bulblets and rhizomes showed 249. Rhizomes showed 123 upregulated and 300 downregulated metabolites, and bulblets showed an increase in 18 metabolites and a decrease in 231 metabolites compared to the rhizomes.
