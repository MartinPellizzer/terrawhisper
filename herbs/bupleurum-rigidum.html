Stiff-leaved Hare's Ear, scientifically known as Bupleurum rigidum, is a perennial herb native to East Asian landscapes. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds like saikosaponins. These properties offer significant anti-inflammatory and hepatoprotective benefits, making it a valuable botanical resource for supporting liver health and immune function.
