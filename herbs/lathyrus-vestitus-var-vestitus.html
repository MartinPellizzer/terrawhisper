Lathyrus vestitus var. vestitus

Lathyrus vestitus var. vestitus, commonly known as the clothed pea, is a specialized medicinal legume valued for its unique phytochemical profile. Native to specific ecological niches, this variety offers therapeutic potential through its bioactive compounds, providing traditional practitioners with a natural resource for treating various ailments and promoting wellness.

  • Scientific name: Lathyrus vestitus var. vestitus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae and the genus Lathyrus. Specifically, it is identified as the variety vestitus within the species Lathyrus vestitus.

This plant exhibits a specific range across several regions in North America. In the Northwestern United States, its presence is noted within the state of Oregon. Moving southward, it can also be found throughout the Southwestern United States, specifically in California. The distribution extends further south into the country of Mexico.
