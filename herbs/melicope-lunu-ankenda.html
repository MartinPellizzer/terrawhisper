Melicope lunu-ankenda

Melicope lunu-ankenda, commonly known as a significant medicinal herb in Sri Lankan traditional medicine, holds immense therapeutic value. Belonging to the Rutaceae family, this plant is widely utilized for treating various ailments, including skin diseases and inflammatory conditions. Its unique bioactive compounds make it a vital subject for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Melicope lunu-ankenda
  • Family: Rutaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
