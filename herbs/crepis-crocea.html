What's the taxonomical classification of Crepis crocea?

Crepis crocea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further organized into the order Asterales. This plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, placing it among the diverse composite flowers, and is specifically identified under the genus Crepis, with its unique biological designation being the species crocea.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Crepis crocea has a perennial growth habit characterized by a basal rosette of long-petioled leaves and erect, leafless flowering stems that reach heights of up to 30 centimeters. Its inflorescence consists of a single yellow flower head supported by a scape, which is covered in fine, spreading hairs. The individual florets are ligulate, forming a bright yellow composite head typical of the Asteraceae family.
