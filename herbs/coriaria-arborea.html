Tree Tutu, scientifically known as Coriaria arborea, is a distinctive evergreen shrub endemic to New Zealand. Renowned for its striking clusters of small, dark berries, it holds significant ethnobotanical importance. While traditionally used by Māori for various purposes, its berries contain toxic compounds, requiring careful handling and knowledge.
