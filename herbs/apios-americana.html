, scientifically known as Apios americana, is the native North American groundnut, a highly nutritious tuberous perennial. Once a dietary staple for Indigenous peoples, this legume offers a starchy, nutty flavor. It is valued by foragers and gardeners alike for its edible tubers and its ability to thrive in wetlands.
