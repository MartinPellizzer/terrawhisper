Commiphora berryi

Commiphora berryi, commonly known as the Berry Gum tree, is a resilient shrub native to the arid regions of East Africa. Renowned for its aromatic resin, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, digestive issues, and inflammatory conditions, offering significant therapeutic potential.

  • Family: Burseraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
