Geranium pratense

Geranium pratense, commonly known as meadow cranesbill, is a perennial herb cherished for its striking blue flowers. Native to temperate regions, this resilient plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its anti-inflammatory and astringent properties. It remains a beautiful addition to gardens and a subject of botanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Geranium pratense
  • Family: Geraniaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Geraniales and the family Geraniaceae, it is classified under the genus Geranium.
