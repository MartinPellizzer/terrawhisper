Parkia biglandulosa

Parkia biglandulosa, commonly known as the velvet tamarind, is a versatile medicinal tree native to tropical regions. Renowned in traditional Ayurvedic medicine, its various parts—including bark, leaves, and fruit—offer potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it a vital natural resource for treating diverse ailments and promoting wellness.
