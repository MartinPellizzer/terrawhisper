Coleus abyssinicus

Coleus abyssinicus, commonly known as the Ethiopian Coleus, is a fragrant medicinal herb native to East Africa. Distinguished by its dark, aromatic foliage, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat respiratory ailments, skin conditions, and digestive issues, offering a rich tapestry of bioactive phytochemical properties.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Africa
