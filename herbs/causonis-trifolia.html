Causonis trifolia

Slender water vine

Causonis trifolia, commonly known as creeping milkweed, is a perennial climbing herb native to tropical regions. Characterized by its succulent stems and delicate white flowers, this plant is valued in traditional medicine for its diverse therapeutic properties, including its potential anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and wound-healing capabilities in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Causonis trifolia
  • Family: Vitaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Vitales, specifically within the family Vitaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Causonis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Causonis trifolia, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In English, it is frequently called the slender water vine or threeleaf Cayratias. In Chinese, the plant is identified as 三叶乌蔹莓 (zh), with the specific regional variants being 三叶乌蔹莓 in simplified characters (zh-cn) and 三葉烏蘞莓 in traditional characters (zh-tw).

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range across several key regions in Asia. Within China, its presence is specifically noted in the South-Central areas. The species is widely distributed throughout the Indian Subcontinent, including major regions such as India and Bangladesh. It can also be found in the unique terrains of Assam and the East Himalaya. Consequently, its habitat spans from the central parts of China to various diverse landscapes across the Indian Subcontinent.
