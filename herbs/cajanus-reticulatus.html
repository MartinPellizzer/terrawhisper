Cajanus reticulatus

Cajaunus reticulatus, commonly known as the Net-veined Oleander, is a striking ornamental shrub valued for its intricate foliage and vibrant blooms. While celebrated for its aesthetic beauty in tropical landscapes, it contains potent cardiac glycosides, requiring careful handling and respect due to its significant medicinal and toxicological properties.

  • Scientific name: Cajanus reticulatus
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Cajanus, and its specific species name, reticulatus.
