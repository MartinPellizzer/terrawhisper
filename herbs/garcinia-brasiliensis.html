Garcinia brasiliensis

Garcinia brasilensis, commonly known as the Brazilian mangosteen, is a tropical medicinal plant native to South America. Renowned for its rich concentration of xanthones and bioactive compounds, it offers significant antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile species is increasingly studied for its potential therapeutic applications in modern pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Garcinia brasiliensis
  • Family: Clusiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and order Malpighiales, it is further organized into the family Clusiaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Garcinia.
