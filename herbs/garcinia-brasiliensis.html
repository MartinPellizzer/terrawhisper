Garcinia bracolensis, scientifically known as Garcinia bracolensis, is a tropical plant celebrated for its potent medicinal properties. Renowned for containing bioactive compounds like xanthones, it is widely used in traditional medicine to combat inflammation, oxidative stress, and various bacterial infections, offering significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology.
