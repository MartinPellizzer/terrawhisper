Black spruce, scientifically known as Picea mariaana, is a resilient coniferous tree valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Rich in essential oils and antioxidants, it is traditionally used in herbal medicine to support respiratory health, soothe skin irritations, and promote relaxation through its potent, aromatic, and healing essences.
