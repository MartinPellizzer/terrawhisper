Picea mariana

Black spruce

Picea mariana, commonly known as black spruce, is a resilient coniferous evergreen native to northern boreal forests. Renowned for its ability to thrive in acidic, nutrient-poor soils, this hardy species provides essential habitat and medicinal potential, traditionally utilized in various cultures for its aromatic resins and healing properties.

  • Scientific name: Picea mariana
  • Family: Pinaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Pinidae and the order Pinales, falling into the family Pinaceae. Finally, the medicinal species is identified by its genus, Picea.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Picea mariana, is referred to by various names depending on the region and language. In Poland, it is commonly called Świerk czarny, while in Russian, it is known as Ель чёрная. The Udmurt name for this species is Сьӧд кыз, and in French, it is identified as épinette noire. Additionally, in Turkey, it is known by the name Kara ladin.
