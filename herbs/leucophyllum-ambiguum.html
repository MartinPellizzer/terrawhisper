Leucophyllum ambiguum

Leucophyllum ambiguum, commonly known as the Mexican Silverleaf, is a resilient shrub prized for its striking silvery foliage and vibrant purple blooms. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical importance, traditionally utilized in various herbal remedies for its diverse medicinal properties and ability to thrive in arid environments.

  • Scientific name: Leucophyllum ambiguum
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Lamiales and the family Scrophulariaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Leucophyllum and the specific species ambiguum.
