Ajuga decumbens

Ajuga decumbens, commonly known as a member of the bugleweed family, is a medicinal plant valued for its diverse pharmacological properties. Often found in specific regional habitats, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to address various ailments, showcasing the rich biodiversity and therapeutic potential of indigenous flora.

  • Scientific name: Ajuga decumbens
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Lamiales and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Ajuga.
