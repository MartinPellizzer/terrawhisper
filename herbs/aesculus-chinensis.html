Aesculus chinensis

Aesculus chinensis, commonly known as the Chinese horse chestnut, is a deciduous tree prized in traditional medicine. Its seeds contain aescin, a compound valued for treating venous insufficiency and reducing inflammation. This botanical powerhouse offers significant therapeutic potential for improving circulation and managing swelling in various vascular conditions.

  • Scientific name: Aesculus chinensis
  • Family: Sapindaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Sapindales, it is classified under the family Sapindaceae and belongs to the genus Aesculus.
