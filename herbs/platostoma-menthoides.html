Platostoma menthoides

Platostoma menthoides, commonly known as Mintleaf Plectranthus, is a versatile medicinal herb prized for its aromatic properties. Belonging to the Lamiaceae family, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and skin inflammations, offering significant therapeutic potential through its rich bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
