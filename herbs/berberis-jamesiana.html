Berberis jamesiana

Berberis jamesiana, commonly known as James's barberry, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to North American montane regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it contains alkaloids like berberine. Traditionally used by Indigenous cultures, this plant offers significant therapeutic potential in treating digestive ailments and supporting metabolic health naturally.

  • Family: Berberidaceae
