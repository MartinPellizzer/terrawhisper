Vinca minor

Common periwinkle

Vinca minor, commonly known as periwinkle, is a versatile evergreen groundcover prized for its glossy leaves and delicate blue flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this medicinal plant has a long history in traditional herbalism, often utilized for its purported properties in supporting circulation and cognitive health.

  • Scientific name: Vinca minor
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Gentianales, it is classified under the family Apocynaceae and is identified by the genus Vinca.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as periwinkle, is a low-growing evergreen characterized by its spreading habit and glossy leaves. While the foliage and small, five-petaled flowers are the most visually striking features, the underground structure is also significant. The plant develops a system of roots that can be described as having hairy roots, which assist in anchoring the stems to the soil and facilitating the absorption of essential nutrients and water from the ground.
