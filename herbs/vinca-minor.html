Common periwinkle, scientifically known as Vinca minor, is a resilient, evergreen groundcover prized for its glossy leaves and delicate blue flowers. Beyond its ornamental beauty in gardens, this traditional medicinal plant has long been studied for its bioactive alkaloids, offering potential therapeutic applications in various pharmacological treatments and herbal remedies.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as periwinkle, is a low-growing evergreen characterized by its spreading habit and glossy leaves. While the foliage and small, five-petaled flowers are the most visually striking features, the underground structure is also significant. The plant develops a system of roots that can be described as having hairy roots, which assist in anchoring the stems to the soil and facilitating the absorption of essential nutrients and water from the ground.
