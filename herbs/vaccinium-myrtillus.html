Vaccinium myrtillus

Whort

Vaccinium myrtillus, commonly known as bilberry, is a versatile woodland shrub prized for its nutrient-dense fruit. Rich in anthocyanins and antioxidants, it is traditionally used to support eye health, enhance circulation, and combat oxidative stress. This potent medicinal plant offers significant therapeutic potential for modern wellness and holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Vaccinium myrtillus
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Ericales and the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is identified by its genus, Vaccinium.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Vaccinium myrtillus, exhibits a widespread distribution across several regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing naturally throughout the landscapes of Denmark and Finland. The species also extends its reach to the islands of Føroyaar and Iceland. Furthermore, its habitat encompasses various parts of Great Britain. Together, these locations highlight the plant's preference for the northernmost territories of the continent.
