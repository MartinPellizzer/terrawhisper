Gleditsia japonica

Gleditsia japonica, commonly known as the Japanese honey locust, is a deciduous tree valued for its unique botanical characteristics. Beyond its ornamental appeal, this species holds significant traditional importance. In various medicinal practices, its components are explored for their potential therapeutic properties, offering intriguing insights into natural healing traditions.

  • Scientific name: Gleditsia japonica
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered in Fabales and falls under the family Fabaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Gleditsia.
