Green mistletoe, scientifically known as Ileostylus micranthus, is a fascinating hemiparasitic plant valued in traditional medicine. Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across several islands in the Oceania region. Within Australia, its presence is limited to the Norfolk Island territory. It is also widely documented across the North Island of New Zealand. Furthermore, the species can be found throughout the South Island of New Zealand. These locations define the primary geographical range for this medicinal species.

This plant, Ileostylus micranthus, utilizes various components for its medicinal applications, most notably its stems, leaves, and dried leaves.
