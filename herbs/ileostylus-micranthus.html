Ileostylus micranthus

Ileostylus micranthus, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, holds profound importance in traditional healing practices. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is frequently utilized to treat various ailments, ranging from inflammatory conditions to digestive issues. Understanding its pharmacological potential offers vital insights into natural therapeutic developments.

  • Scientific name: Ileostylus micranthus
  • Family: Loranthaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It falls under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Santalales, specifically within the family Loranthaceae. Taxonomically, it is identified under the genus Ileostylus, which includes the species Ileostylus micrantus.
