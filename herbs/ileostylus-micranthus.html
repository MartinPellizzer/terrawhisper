Green mistletoe, scientifically known as Ileostylus micranthus, is a fascinating hemiparasitic plant valued in traditional medicine. Often found in tropical regions, it is utilized for its bioactive compounds to treat various ailments, including inflammation and fever. Understanding its botanical properties offers significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.
