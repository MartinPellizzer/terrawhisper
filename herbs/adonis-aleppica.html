Adonis aleppica

Adonis aleppica, commonly known as Aleppo Adonis, is a perennial herbaceous plant belonging to the Ranunculaceae family. Native to the Mediterranean region, it features striking yellow flowers. Traditionally used in folk medicine, this plant contains bioactive glycosides, making it a subject of interest for its diverse pharmacological potential.

  • Scientific name: Adonis aleppica
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified within the order Ranunculales and the family Ranunculaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Adonis.
