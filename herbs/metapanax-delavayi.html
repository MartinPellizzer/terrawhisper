Metapanax delavayi

Metapanax delaavayi, commonly known as Choti Ajwain, is a significant medicinal herb native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this plant is extensively used in traditional Ayurvedic medicine to treat digestive disorders, respiratory issues, and inflammation, serving as a vital natural remedy for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Metapanax delavayi
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Further taxonomic descent places it in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, falling under the family Araliaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Metapanax and the specific species name delaayi.
