What's the taxonomical classification of Rubus fockeanus?

Rubus fockeanus belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. The plant follows the order Rosales and is a member of the family Rosaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Rubus and the specific species epithet fockeanus.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Rubus fockeanus has erect or arching stems that are densely covered in reddish-brown prickles and possess small, simple, serrated leaves arranged in an alternate pattern. The leaves are typically trifoliate or five-foliate with ovate leaflets that feature distinctively dentate margins. These plants produce small, fragrant white flowers with five petals and numerous stamens that emerge from terminal clusters. Following pollination, the plant develops aggregate fruits that transition from green to a dark red or black color when fully ripe. The overall structure is characterized by a woody, scrambling growth habit typical of the Rosaceae family.
