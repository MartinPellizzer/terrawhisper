Silene nocturna

Silene nocturna, commonly known as the Night-blooming Catchfly, is a captivating nocturnal wildflower celebrated for its enchanting, pale blossoms. Thriving in specific habitats, this species relies on nighttime pollinators to ensure survival. Beyond its aesthetic allure, it holds significant botanical interest for researchers studying specialized pollination and plant biology.

  • Scientific name: Silene nocturna
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Caryophyllales and the family Caryophyllaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Silene.
