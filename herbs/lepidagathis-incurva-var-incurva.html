Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across parts of Asia, spanning from the Indian Subcontinent to various regions of China. In the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found growing in the state of Assam and throughout Bangladesh. Moving toward East Asia, its range extends into the South-Central regions of China. It is also documented to inhabit the Southeast portions of the country. Additionally, the species is present on the tropical island of Hainan.
