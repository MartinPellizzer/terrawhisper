Chinese Clubmoss

Huperzia chinensis · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (1 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Chinese Clubmoss, scientifically known as Huperzia chinensis, is a perennial lycopod valued in traditional medicine for its cognitive benefits. It contains huperzine A, a potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. This alkaloid is widely researched for its ability to enhance memory, improve focus, and protect neurons against neurodegenerative conditions like Alzheimer's disease.
