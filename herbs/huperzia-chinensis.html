Huperzia chinensis

Huperzia chinensis, commonly known as Chinese clubmoss, is a significant medicinal plant prized for its potent neuroprotective properties. It is the primary natural source of huperzine A, a powerful acetylcholinesterase inhibitor. This alkaloid is widely researched for its potential to enhance cognitive function and treat neurodegenerative diseases.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia chinensis
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Lycopodiidae and order Lycopodiales, it is a member of the family Lycopodiaceae. Finally, it is specifically identified by the genus Huperzia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern within the vast landscape of China. It is primarily documented across the regions of China South-Central. Additionally, its presence extends into the territories of China North-Central. These two distinct zones represent the core areas where the species can be found in the wild. Consequently, its geographical range is concentrated within these specific central parts of the country.
