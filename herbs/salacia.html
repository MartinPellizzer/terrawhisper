Salacia

Salacia, commonly known as Salaak, is a powerful medicinal climber native to the tropical forests of India and Sri Lanka. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, it is highly valued for its potent anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, making it an effective natural remedy for managing arthritis, joint pain, and metabolic disorders.

  • Scientific name: Salacia
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is categorized under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Celastrales and the family Celastraceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Salacia.
