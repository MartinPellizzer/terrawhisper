Fringed rue, scientifically known as Ruta chalepensis, is a perennial herb belonging to the Rutaceae family. Characterized by its distinctive feathery foliage and bright yellow flowers, this medicinal plant is traditionally valued in various cultures for its therapeutic properties, often utilized in herbal remedies to support digestive and inflammatory health.
