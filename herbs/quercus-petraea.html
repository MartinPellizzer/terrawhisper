Quercus petraea

Quercus petraea, commonly known as the Scrape Oak, is a resilient deciduous species valued for its historical and medicinal properties. Often found in rocky terrains, its bark and wood contain bioactive compounds like tannins. These constituents offer significant antioxidant and antimicrobial benefits, making it a subject of ethnobotanical interest.

  • Scientific name: Quercus petraea
  • Family: Fagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Fagales and the family Fagaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Quercus.
