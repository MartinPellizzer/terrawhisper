Eriobotrya deflexa

Eriobotrya deflexa, commonly known as the loquat, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Its leaves and fruits possess significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antibacterial properties. Widely utilized to support respiratory health and digestive wellness, this resilient species offers a wealth of bioactive compounds for natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Eriobotrya deflexa
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further organized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosales, falling into the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Eriobotrya.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern across several key regions in East and Southeast Asia. Within China, it can be found in the southern island province of Hainan as well as across China Southeast. Its range also extends to the island of Taiwan in Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is documented within the Indo-China region, specifically in Vietnam. These locations highlight its preference for the subtropical environments found in these territories.

Plant Parts

This plant, Eriobotrya deflexa, commonly known as the loquat, utilizes its distinctive leaves as a significant medicinal component. These leaves are large, leathery, and lanceolate in shape, often featuring a fuzzy or pubescent texture on the underside which helps in moisture retention. In traditional medicine, the dried leaves are frequently prepared as decoctions or poultices to treat various ailments, including respiratory issues like asthma and coughs, as well as skin inflammations.
