Theobroma cacao

Theobroma cacao, commonly known as the cacao tree, is a fascinating medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Beyond its famous role in chocolate production, it contains potent bioactive compounds like flavonoids and methylxanthines. These elements offer antioxidant, neuroprotective, and cardiovascular benefits, making cacao a powerhouse of natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Theobroma cacao
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Theobroma cacao, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Theobroma.

Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Theobroma cacao, is widely distributed across the West Tropical Africa region. Within this area, it is prominently cultivated in the nation of Ghana. Its geographical presence also extends to the country of Guinea. Furthermore, significant populations of this species are found throughout Ivory Coast and Nigeria. Finally, the plant's distribution reaches into Togo, completing its regional presence in West Tropical Africa.
