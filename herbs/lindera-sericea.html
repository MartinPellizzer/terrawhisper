Lindera sericea

Lindra seriicea, commonly known as the Mountain Pepper, is a versatile evergreen shrub native to Australia's temperate rainforests. Renowned for its aromatic, pungent leaves, it is widely used in traditional medicine and culinary arts. Its potent antioxidant properties and antimicrobial qualities make it a valuable resource in natural wellness.
