Plant Parts

This plant, Aconitum hemsleyanum var. hemsleyanum, is a member of the Ranunculaceae family, and its primary medicinal value is concentrated within its specialized underground structures. The most significant part of the plant used in traditional practices is the roots, which typically develop into tuberous, fleshy organs that store essential alkaloids. These roots are carefully harvested and processed, as they contain the potent chemical compounds responsible for the plant's therapeutic and toxicological properties.
