Poison Bulb, scientifically known as Crinum asiaticum, is a striking perennial herb celebrated for its large, lily-like white flowers. Native to tropical Asia, this plant is widely utilized in traditional medicine for its various therapeutic properties, though its toxic alkaloids require careful handling and expert knowledge during medicinal application.
