Tarchonanthus parvicapitulatus

Tarchonanthus parvicapitulatus, commonly known as a member of the Asteraceae family, is a significant medicinal shrub valued for its therapeutic properties. Native to specific arid regions, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat various ailments, leveraging its unique bioactive compounds for their potent pharmacological potential.
