Knobweed, scientifically known as Hyptis capitata, is a versatile medicinal herb native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its distinctive flowering heads, this plant is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. Its unique phytochemical profile offers promising potential for modern therapeutic applications.
