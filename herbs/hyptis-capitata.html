Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In Eastern Asia, its presence is specifically noted within the Nansei-shoto islands. Moving toward the Indian Subcontinent, it can be found in both Assam and Bangladesh. The species also extends into the Indo-China region, encompassing the Andaman Islands. Finally, its distribution reaches into the mainland area of Cambodia.
