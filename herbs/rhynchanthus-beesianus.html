Rhynchanthus beesianus

Rhynchanthus beesianus, commonly known as the "miracle plant," is a rare, parasitic medicinal herb endemic to the Western Ghats of India. Renowned for its potent rejuvenating properties, it is traditionally used in Ayurveda to enhance cognitive functions, boost memory, and combat fatigue, offering significant potential in neuroprotective therapies.

  • Scientific name: Rhynchanthus beesianus
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zingiberales, it is classified under the family Zingiberaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Rhynchanthus.
