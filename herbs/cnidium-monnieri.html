Cnidium monnieri, scientifically known as Cnidium monnieri, is a versatile medicinal herb prized in traditional pharmacopoeia. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, this plant contains bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments. It holds significant therapeutic potential in modern research for managing oxidative stress and inflammation.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the vast territory of Siberia. Specifically, it can be found growing in the Buryatiya region. Its presence also extends to the areas of Chita and Irkutsk. Furthermore, the species inhabits the Krasnoyarsk territory. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes the remote landscapes of Yakutiya.
