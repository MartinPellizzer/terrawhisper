Cnidium monnieri

Cnidium monnieri, commonly known as Chuan Xiang Zi in Traditional Chinese Medicine, is a versatile medicinal herb. Renowned for its potent essential oils, it is widely utilized to treat digestive ailments, relieve pain, and combat respiratory issues. This aromatic plant plays a crucial role in holistic therapeutic practices worldwide.

  • Scientific name: Cnidium monnieri
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Apiales, it is classified under the family Apiaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Cnidium and the species Cnidium monnieri.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several key regions within the vast territory of Siberia. Specifically, it can be found growing in the Buryatiya region. Its presence also extends to the areas of Chita and Irkutsk. Furthermore, the species inhabits the Krasnoyarsk territory. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes the remote landscapes of Yakutiya.
