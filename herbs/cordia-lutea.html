Cordia lutea

Cordia lutea, commonly known as the Golden Cordia or Yellow Cordia, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its vibrant yellow blooms, this plant is highly valued in traditional medicine for its anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and wound-healing properties, offering significant therapeutic potential for holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Cordia lutea
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Pacific

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cordia, specifically as the species Cordia lutea.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide and diverse geographical distribution spanning several oceanic and continental regions. Within the North-Central Pacific, it can be found growing in the islands of Hawaii. Its presence extends to the South-Central Pacific, specifically within the Marquesas. Moving toward the Americas, the species is established across Western South America, including Colombia and Ecuador. Finally, the plant is also documented in the Galápagos archipelago.
