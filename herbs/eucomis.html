Eucomis

Eucomis, commonly known as the pineapple lily, is a striking medicinal plant native to southern Africa. Renowned for its unique rosette of flowers, it has historically been used in traditional practices to treat ailments like indigestion, inflammation, and skin irritations. This ornamental gem offers both beauty and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Eucomis
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asparagales and the family Asparagaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Eucomis.
