Black gram, scientifically known as Vigna mungo, is a nutrient-dense legume widely cultivated across Asia. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is rich in protein, fiber, and essential minerals. Traditionally used in Ayurveda, it supports digestion, boosts energy levels, and aids in managing various metabolic and systemic health conditions.
