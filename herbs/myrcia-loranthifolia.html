Myrcia loranthifolia

Myrcia loranthifolia, commonly known as the Mistletoe tree, is a versatile medicinal plant valued in traditional practices. Native to tropical regions, it possesses significant therapeutic potential, often utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties. This botanical specimen offers unique phytochemical compounds that continue to inspire modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Myrcia loranthifolia
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Myrcia loranthifolia, is referred to by various names depending on the region and language. In English, it is commonly called the bay rose, while in Spanish, it is known as laurel de monte or laurel de costa. In Portuguese, it is frequently identified as louro-da-serra, and in the local context of some regions, it may be referred to as mirtáceo.
