Plant Parts

This plant, Myrcia loranthifolia, is commonly utilized in traditional medicine, with its therapeutic properties frequently derived from the leaf. The leaves are typically characterized by their elliptical or oblong shape and leathery texture, serving as the primary component in various herbal decoctions and infusions. In many regional practices, these leaves are processed to extract bioactive compounds used to address ailments such as inflammation, digestive issues, or skin conditions, making the leaf the most significant medicinal part of the species.
