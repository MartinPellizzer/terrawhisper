Piper wallichii

Piper wallichii, commonly known as Long Pepper, is a versatile medicinal plant belonging to the Piperaceae family. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, its pungent fruit offers potent antioxidant, antimicrobial, and digestive benefits. Widely used to treat respiratory ailments and enhance metabolism, this aromatic spice remains a cornerstone of traditional healing.

  • Scientific name: Piper wallichii
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Piperales, falling into the family Piperaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Piper, specifically as the species Piper wallichii.
