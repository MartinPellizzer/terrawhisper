Angelica anomala

Eumenol angelica

Angelica anomala, commonly known as the Himalayan Angelica, is a perennial herb native to the high-altitude regions of the Himalayas. Renowned in traditional medicine, it contains bioactive compounds used to treat digestive issues and respiratory ailments. This potent botanical is highly valued for its unique therapeutic and aromatic properties.

  • Scientific name: Angelica anomala
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, it is specifically situated in the family Apiaceae. It is further identified by its genus, Angelica, as part of the species Angelica anomala.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of Northern Asia. In Siberia, its presence is specifically noted in the Chita region. The species also extends into the Russian Far East, where it can be found in Amur, Khabarovsk, and Primorye. Furthermore, its range reaches into China, particularly within the Inner Mongolia region. Together, these locations define the natural habitat and spread of Angelica anomala.
