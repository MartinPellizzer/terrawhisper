Eugenia bergii

Eugenia bergii, commonly known as the Berg's Eugenia, is a versatile medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat various ailments, including inflammation and digestive issues. This resilient species offers significant pharmacological potential for modern herbal medicine development.

  • Scientific name: Eugenia bergii
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae in the order Myrtales, falling into the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is situated within the genus Eugenia as the species Eugenia bergii.
