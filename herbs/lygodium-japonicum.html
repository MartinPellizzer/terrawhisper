Lygodium japonicum

Japanese climbing fern

Lygodium japonicum, commonly known as Japanese climbing fern, is a vigorous creeping plant native to East Asia. While often viewed as an invasive species in many regions, it holds significant traditional importance in various medicinal practices, where its unique botanical properties are utilized for their diverse therapeutic potential and healing qualities.

  • Scientific name: Lygodium japonicum
  • Family: Schizaeaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Lygodium japonicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Polypodiinae, it is classified under the order Schizaeales and the family Schizaeaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by its genus, Lygodium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across multiple continents. In Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa.
