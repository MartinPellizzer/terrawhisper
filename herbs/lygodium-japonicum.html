Japanese climbing fern, scientifically known as Lygodium japonicum, is a creeping pteridophyte often found in moist, shaded forest environments. While primarily recognized for its distinct climbing habit and delicate foliage, this plant holds intriguing potential in traditional ethnobotany, offering unique biochemical properties for further medicinal and pharmacological exploration.
