Japanese climbing fern, scientifically known as Lygodium japonicum, is a creeping pteridophyte often found in moist, shaded forest environments. While primarily recognized for its distinct climbing habit and delicate foliage, this plant holds intriguing potential in traditional ethnobotany, offering unique biochemical properties for further medicinal and pharmacological exploration.

This plant, known scientifically as Lygodium japonicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad and diverse geographical distribution across multiple continents. In Africa, its presence is specifically noted within the Cape Provinces of Southern Africa. Its range extends significantly into Asia, where it is found throughout various regions of China. Specifically, it inhabits the South-Central, North-Central, and Southeast areas of the country. Additionally, the species can be found on the tropical island of Hainan.
