Giant salvinia, scientifically known as Salvinia molestia, is a highly invasive floating aquatic fern. Characterized by its rapid growth and hairy leaves, it forms dense mats that obstruct waterways, deplete oxygen levels, and disrupt local ecosystems. Controlling this aggressive species is critical for maintaining healthy, functional aquatic environments globally.
