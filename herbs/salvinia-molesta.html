Salvinia molesta

Giant salvinia

Salvinia molestla, commonly known as water fern or giant salvinia, is a free-floating aquatic plant native to Brazil. Renowned for its rapid growth, it forms dense mats that transform freshwater ecosystems. While ecologically disruptive, it holds potential in biotechnology and phytoremediation due to its unique structural and biological properties.
