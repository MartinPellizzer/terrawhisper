Artemisia leucodes

Artemisia leucodes, commonly known as White Wormwood, is a resilient perennial herb native to arid and semi-arid regions. Characterized by its distinctive silvery-white foliage, this plant belongs to the Asteraceae family. It is valued in traditional medicine for its potent bioactive compounds and unique ecological role in dry landscapes.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia leucodes
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Asterales, it is classified under the family Asteraceae. Specifically, Artemisia leucodes is a member of the genus Artemisia.
