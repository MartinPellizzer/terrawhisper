Japanese Buttercup

Ranunculus japonicus · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Sparse (17 studies) · ★☆☆☆☆

Japanese Buttercup, scientifically known as Ranunculus japonicus, is a perennial herb native to East Asia. Characterized by its vibrant yellow flowers and glossy foliage, this plant holds significant place in traditional medicine. It is primarily utilized for its anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties, though careful handling is required due to toxicity.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across various expansive regions within Siberia. It can be found growing in the Buryatiya region of this vast territory. Additionally, the species is documented in the areas of Chita and Irkutsk. Its presence extends further into the southern reaches of Tuva. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal plant includes the northern expanse of Yakutiya.
