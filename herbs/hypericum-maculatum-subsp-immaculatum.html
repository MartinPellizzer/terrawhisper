Hypericum maculatum subsp. immaculatum

Hypericum maculatum subsp. immaculaatum, commonly known as Spotted St. John's Wort, is a distinctive perennial herb. Native to specific European regions, this subspecies is valued for its bright yellow blooms. In traditional herbalism, members of the Hypericum genus are studied for their unique bioactive compounds and therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Hypericum maculatum subsp. immaculatum
  • Family: Hypericaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is further categorized into the family Hypericaceae. Finally, it is identified under the genus Hypericum as the subspecies Hypericum maculatum subsp. immaculatum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily found across specific regions of Southeastern Europe. It can be located within the borders of Bulgaria and Greece.
