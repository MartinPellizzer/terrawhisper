Mosla dianthera

Mosla dianthera, commonly known as the Golden Starleaf, is a remarkable medicinal herb prized in traditional healing. Renowned for its potent antioxidant properties, this botanical treasure is used to soothe inflammation and support immune health. Its vibrant blooms and unique chemical profile make it a vital resource for natural wellness.

  • Scientific name: Mosla dianthera
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae, the order Lamiales, and the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Mosla.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical range that spans across diverse regions of Eurasia. In the Russian Far East, it can be found within the Amur region and throughout Khabarovsk. Its distribution continues through the Kuril Islands and extends into the Primorye territory. Additionally, the species is documented in the Caucasus, specifically within the Transcaucasus area. These varied locations highlight the plant's ability to inhabit distinct ecological zones across its range.
