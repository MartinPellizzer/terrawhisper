Chinese silkvine, scientifically known as Periploca sepium, is a versatile climbing shrub native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, its leaves contain bioactive compounds used to treat various ailments, including skin conditions and inflammation. This resilient plant is valued both for its medicinal properties and its decorative foliage.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, it is specifically documented to occur within the Primorye region. Its presence is also well-established throughout various parts of China, including the South-Central area. Furthermore, the species can be found spanning from the northern reaches of Inner Mongolia to the Manchuria region. Finally, its habitat extends into the North-Central territories of China.
