Oliveria decumbens

Oliveria decumbens, commonly known as a significant medicinal shrub, holds profound value in traditional healing practices. Native to specific subtropical regions, this plant is highly regarded for its potent bioactive compounds. It is frequently utilized for its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, offering promising potential in modern pharmacological research.

  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
