Scleranthus uncinatus

Scleranthus uncinatus, commonly known as the Hooked Scleranthus, is a remarkable medicinal plant valued for its unique therapeutic properties. Native to specific subtropical regions, it contains potent bioactive compounds used in traditional healing to treat inflammation and skin ailments, offering significant potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Scleranthus uncinatus
  • Family: Caryophyllaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales. Finally, it is a member of the family Caryophyllaceae and falls under the genus Scleranthus.
