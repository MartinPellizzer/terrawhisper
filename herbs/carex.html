Carex

Carex, commonly known as sedges, represents a diverse genus of perennial plants found in wetlands and diverse ecosystems worldwide. While primarily valued for ecological stability and erosion control, certain species offer medicinal potential. Traditionally, various sedges have been utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation and digestive issues.

  • Scientific name: Carex
  • Family: Cyperaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Poales, it is further categorized into the family Cyperaceae. Ultimately, it is identified by the genus Carex.
