Vachellia nilotica

Gum arabic tree

Vacchellia nilotica, commonly known as the Nile Acacia or Nilotica, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to African savannas. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, the plant is used in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, skin infections, and inflammation, offering valuable phytochemicals for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Vachellia nilotica
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fabales, it is classified under the family Fabaceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Vacchellia.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of Northern Africa. It can be found growing in the landscapes of Algeria and Egypt. Additionally, its presence extends to the territories of Libya and Morocco. The species also reaches the Macaronesian islands, specifically appearing in Cape Verde. Together, these locations define the primary geographical range of this medicinal species.
