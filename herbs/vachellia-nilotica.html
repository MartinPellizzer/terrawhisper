Gum arabic tree, scientifically known as Vachellia nilotica, is a versatile medicinal plant native to Africa and the Middle East. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its resin, bark, and leaves are utilized in traditional medicine to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments, offering significant pharmacological potential worldwide.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across various regions of Northern Africa. It can be found growing in the landscapes of Algeria and Egypt. Additionally, its presence extends to the territories of Libya and Morocco. The species also reaches the Macaronesian islands, specifically appearing in Cape Verde. Together, these locations define the primary geographical range of this medicinal species.

Plant Parts

This plant, Vacchellia nilotica, possesses various anatomical components that are utilized for their diverse medicinal properties.
