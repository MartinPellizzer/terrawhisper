Ononis hirta

Ononis hirta, commonly known as Sabaphyllum or "grass of God," is a versatile medicinal herb native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer significant potential for modern therapeutic research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ononis hirta
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
