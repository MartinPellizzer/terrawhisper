Paper mulberry, scientifically known as Broussonetia papyrifera, is a versatile deciduous shrub prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its anti-inflammatory and antioxidant properties, various parts of the plant are used to treat skin ailments, respiratory issues, and digestive disorders, offering significant therapeutic potential in holistic healing practices worldwide.

Plant Parts

This plant, Broussonetiia papyrifera, is a versatile medicinal species where various components offer different therapeutic benefits. The large, heart-shaped leaves are frequently utilized in traditional medicine for their anti-inflammatory properties, while the roots serve as a vital source of bioactive compounds. Specifically, the root bark is often harvested for its concentrated medicinal potency, and the entire root structure can be processed for decoctions. In some traditional practices, the whole plants are even employed to ensure a comprehensive application of the specimen's healing properties.
