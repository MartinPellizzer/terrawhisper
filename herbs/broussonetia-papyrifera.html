Broussonetia papyrifera

Paper mulberry

Broussonetiia papyrifera, commonly known as paper mulberry, is a versatile medicinal plant deeply rooted in traditional Eastern medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, its leaves and roots are utilized to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and respiratory issues. This resilient species offers significant pharmacological potential for modern holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Broussonetia papyrifera
  • Family: Moraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Rosales and the family Moraceae, it is classified under the genus Broussonetiia.

Plant Parts

This plant, Broussonetiia papyrifera, is a versatile medicinal species where various components offer different therapeutic benefits. The large, heart-shaped leaves are frequently utilized in traditional medicine for their anti-inflammatory properties, while the roots serve as a vital source of bioactive compounds. Specifically, the root bark is often harvested for its concentrated medicinal potency, and the entire root structure can be processed for decoctions. In some traditional practices, the whole plants are even employed to ensure a comprehensive application of the specimen's healing properties.
