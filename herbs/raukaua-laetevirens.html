Raukaua laetevirens

Raukaua laetevirens, commonly known as the Emerald Shrub, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to remote tropical highlands. Renowned for its vibrant, waxy foliage, it possesses potent bioactive compounds. Traditional healers utilize its crushed leaves to treat inflammation and skin ailments, offering a unique natural remedy for wellness.

  • Scientific name: Raukaua laetevirens
  • Family: Araliaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
