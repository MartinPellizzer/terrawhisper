Mentha cervina

Mentha cervina, commonly known as deer mint, is a distinctive perennial herb belonging to the Lamiaceae family. Characterized by its aromatic foliage and delicate flowers, this plant thrives in moist, shaded environments. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, it offers unique therapeutic properties and serves as a fascinating subject for botanical study.

  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Europe
