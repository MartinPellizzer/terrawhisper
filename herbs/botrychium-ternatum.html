Botrychium ternatum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Its taxonomic progression continues through the order Ophioglossales and the family Ophioglossaceae. Ultimately, this plant is identified by the genus Botrychium and is specifically designated as the species ternatum.
