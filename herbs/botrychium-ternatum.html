Sceptridium ternatum, scientifically known as Botrychium ternatum, is commonly called the ternate moonwort. This delicate, shade-loving fern is distinguished by its unique, three-parted leaf structure. Often found in moist, woodland habitats, it holds significant value in ethnobotany and serves as a fascinating indicator of healthy, undisturbed forest ecosystems.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted on the island of Sakhalin. Within China, the species is widely distributed across the Manchuria region. It can also be found throughout the North-Central areas of the country.
