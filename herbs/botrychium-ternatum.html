Botrychium ternatum

Sceptridium ternatum

Botrychium ternatum, commonly known as three-flowered moonwort, is a delicate, inconspicuous fern found in woodland habitats. Distinguished by its unique trifoliate leaf structure and tiny, inconspicuous flowers, this ancient species relies on complex fungal relationships in the soil to thrive, playing a vital role in forest ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Botrychium ternatum
  • Family: Ophioglossaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized into the subclass Ophioglossidae and the order Ophioglossales, falling under the family Ophioglossaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Botrychium.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical range across several key regions in East Asia. In the Russian Far East, its presence is specifically noted on the island of Sakhalin. Within China, the species is widely distributed across the Manchuria region. It can also be found throughout the North-Central areas of the country. Finally, the distribution extends further south into the South-Central and Southeast provinces of China.
