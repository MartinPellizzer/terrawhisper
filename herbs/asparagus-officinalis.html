Asparagus officinalis

Asparagus plant

Asparagus officinalis, commonly known as garden asparagus, is a perennial plant valued both culinary and medicinal properties. Renowned for its diuretic effects, it supports kidney function and detoxification. Rich in vitamins and antioxidants, this plant has historically been used to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and various urinary tract ailments.

  • Scientific name: Asparagus officinalis
  • Family: Asparagaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered as Asparagales and placed in the family Asparagaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Asparagus.
