Curcuma mutabilis

Curcumma mutabilis, commonly known as Double Curcuma, is a remarkable medicinal plant prized for its unique, dual-colored rhizomes. Renowned in traditional wellness practices, it offers potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. This versatile plant serves as a valuable natural resource for supporting holistic health and promoting overall physical vitality.

  • Scientific name: Curcuma mutabilis
  • Family: Zingiberaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It falls under the order Zingiberales and is a member of the family Zingiberaceae. Specifically, it is identified by the genus Curcuma.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Curcuma mutabilis, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and local language. In English, it is frequently called the Double Curcuma or the Variegated Turmeric. Within different linguistic contexts, it is known as Temu Kuning in Indonesian and Temu Putih in Malay. Furthermore, it is identified as Kunyit Putih in parts of Southeast Asia and is sometimes referred to as White Turmeric in various English-speaking botanical circles.
