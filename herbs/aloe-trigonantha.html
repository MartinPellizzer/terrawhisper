Aloe trigonantha

Aloe trigonantha, commonly known as the Three-angled Aloe, is a striking succulent native to the arid regions of Southern Africa. Characterized by its distinctively angular, fleshy leaves and vibrant flowering spikes, this resilient plant is valued both for its unique botanical geometry and its potential roles in traditional medicinal practices.

  • Scientific name: Aloe trigonantha
  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Africa
