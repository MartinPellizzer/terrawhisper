Asplenium pekinense

What's the taxonomical classification of Asplenium pekinense?

Asplenium pekinense belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Polypodiinae. Following the taxonomic hierarchy further, it falls under the order Polypodiales and is a member of the family Aspleniaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Asplenium and is specifically designated as the species pekinense.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Asplenium pekinense has large, dark green, pinnate fronds that typically measure between 20 and 60 centimeters in length with a prominent, dark brown to blackish stipe. The pinnae are arranged alternately along the rachis and possess oblong to lanceolate shapes with slightly wavy or entire margins. Each individual leaflet features finely serrated edges and a distinct midrib that tapers toward the tip. The fertile part of the plant is characterized by small, circular sori located on the underside of the pinnules, often protected by a thin membrane. These structural characteristics allow the fern to thrive in shaded, moist environments.
