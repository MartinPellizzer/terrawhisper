Hibiscus micranthus

Hibiscus micranthus, commonly known as the Small-flowered Hibiscus, is a resilient shrub native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its delicate blooms, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional healing. It is frequently utilized for its antioxidant properties and its ability to support digestive and skin health.

  • Scientific name: Hibiscus micranthus
  • Family: Malvaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malvales and the family Malvaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Hibiscus.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across multiple regions of the African continent. In Northern Africa, it can be found growing in the territories of Algeria, Egypt, and Morocco. Its presence also extends into the West Tropical Africa region, specifically within the borders of Burkina. Additionally, the species is documented to inhabit parts of Ghana. These locations highlight the wide environmental range that this medicinal plant occupies.
