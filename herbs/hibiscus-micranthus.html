Tiny White Wild Hibiscus, scientifically known as Hibiscus micranthus, is a resilient shrub native to Africa. Renowned for its delicate, small white blossoms, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally, various parts of the plant are utilized in folk medicine to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to skin inflammations.
