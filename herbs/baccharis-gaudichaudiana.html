Baccharis gaudichaudiana

Baccharis gaudichaudiana, commonly known as various local names in South American regions, is a versatile medicinal shrub. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, this plant is traditionally utilized for its anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial properties. It serves as a vital botanical resource in treating skin ailments and digestive irregularities.

  • Scientific name: Baccharis gaudichaudiana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is further categorized under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Baccharis, specifically as the species Baccharis gaudichaudiaana.

This plant exhibits a specific distribution pattern concentrated within the southern regions of the South American continent. In Brazil, its presence is primarily recorded throughout the southern portion of the country. Its range extends into Argentina, where it can be found in both the northeast and northwest territories.
