Vachellia hockii

Vachellia hockii, commonly known as the African milkweed, is a significant medicinal plant native to various African regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues. Its bioactive compounds offer promising potential for modern pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Vachellia hockii
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It is classified under the order Fabales and the family Fabaceae, eventually falling into the genus Vachellia.

Common Names

This plant, Vachellia hockii, is known by various regional names depending on the local language. In English, it is commonly referred to as Hock's vacchellia, while in Swahili, it is known as mshubiri mwekundu. In Arabic, the plant is called al-khuzama.

Distribution

This plant, known as Vachellia hockii, exhibits a specific distribution across the West Tropical Africa region. Its presence is documented within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. The species also extends its range into Burkina Faso, contributing to the regional biodiversity. Furthermore, the plant can be found growing throughout the territories of Ghana and Guinea. Finally, its geographical reach includes the coastal and inland areas of the Ivory Coast.
