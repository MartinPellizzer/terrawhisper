Cat's Whisker, scientifically known as Volkameria glabra, is a striking tropical shrub celebrated for its fragrant, long-exerted stamens. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized in various cultures for its potential medicinal properties, including its reputed ability to treat ailments related to the skin.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several key regions of the African continent. In West Tropical Africa, its presence is specifically noted in Sierra Leone. Moving toward the center of the continent, it can be found within the Democratic Republic of the Congo in West-Central Tropical Africa. Its range extends into Northeast Tropical Africa, where it is located in Somalia. Finally, the species is also widely distributed across East Tropical Africa, specifically within Kenya and Tanzania.
