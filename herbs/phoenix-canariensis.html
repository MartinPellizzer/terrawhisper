The reproduction of Phoenix canariensis is primarily characterized by its sexual processes, as the species is dioecious, meaning male and female flowers are borne on separate individuals, while asexual reproduction is noted as absent. The flowering period typically begins in December and concludes in May, though the timing can be variable.
