Berula erecta

Berula erecta, commonly known as Lesser Water Parsley, is a perennial herb native to wetland habitats. Belonging to the Apiaceae family, this plant thrives in marshes and stream margins. Historically valued for its edible leaves and potential medicinal properties, it serves as a vital component of diverse aquatic ecosystems.

  • Scientific name: Berula erecta
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Apiales, falling into the family Apiaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by its placement in the genus Berula.
