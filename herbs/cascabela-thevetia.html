Cascabela thevetia

Cascabela thevetia, commonly known as Yellow Oleander, is a striking evergreen shrub prized for its bright, trumpet-shaped flowers. While aesthetically beautiful in tropical landscapes, it is highly toxic if ingested. In traditional medicine, its potent alkaloids are studied for their cardiovascular effects and potential pharmacological applications in treating various ailments.

  • Scientific name: Cascabela thevetia
  • Family: Apocynaceae
  • Native range: Europe
