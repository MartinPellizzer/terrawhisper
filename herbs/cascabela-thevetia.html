Be-still tree

Cascabela thevetia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (436 studies) · ★★★★★

Be-still tree, scientifically known as Cascabela thevetia, is a striking evergreen shrub prized for its bright yellow blooms. While its medicinal potential is widely studied, the plant contains potent cardiac glycosides, making it highly toxic if mishandled. Always exercise extreme caution when exploring its pharmacological properties and traditional uses.

Plant Parts

This plant, Cascabela thevetia, utilizes various anatomical components for its medicinal applications, though it is important to note that many parts contain potent cardiac glycosides. The large, lanceolate leaves are frequently studied for their bioactive compounds, and when processed into a fine leaf powder, they can be used in specific topical or traditional preparations. The woody stem bark also contains concentrated alkaloids used in various botanical extracts. Furthermore, the plant produces distinctive elongated fruit, which is encased in a thick fruit rind that serves as a protective layer for the seeds. While these parts are chemically significant, their use requires extreme caution due to the plant's inherent toxicity.
