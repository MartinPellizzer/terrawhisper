Cascabela thevetia

What's the taxonomical classification of Cascabela thevetia?

Cascabela thevetia belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving through its higher taxonomic ranks, it falls under the order Gentianales and the family Apocynaceae. Finally, the plant is identified by its specific genus, Cascabela, and its species name, thevetia.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cascabela thevetia has an evergreen shrub or small tree morphology characterized by lanceolate, leathery leaves that are arranged alternately along slender, woody branches. The foliage typically measures between 10 and 20 centimeters in length and features smooth margins with a glossy, dark green upper surface. Its flowers are funnel-shaped and bright yellow, emerging from terminal clusters to create a striking visual display. The plant produces elongated, narrow drupes that transition from green to a dark, mature color. These structural components combine to form a dense, bushy habit that can reach heights of up to six meters in optimal conditions.

How is this plant cultivated?

This plant, commonly known as yellow oleander, thrives in tropical and subtropical climates when planted in well-draining soil with full sun exposure.

It requires regular watering during its establishment phase but is notably drought-tolerant once its root system is mature.
