Be-still tree

Cascabela thevetia · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Very Extensive (436 studies) · ★★★★★

Be-still tree, scientifically known as Cascabela thevetia, is a striking evergreen shrub prized for its bright yellow blooms. While its medicinal potential is widely studied, the plant contains potent cardiac glycosides, making it highly toxic if mishandled. Always exercise extreme caution when exploring its pharmacological properties and traditional uses.
