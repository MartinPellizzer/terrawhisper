Parastrephia lucida

Parastrephia lucida, commonly known as the "maguey de monte," is a resilient medicinal shrub native to Mexico. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally used to treat respiratory ailments, inflammation, and digestive issues. This botanical specimen offers significant potential for pharmacological research and holistic wellness applications.

  • Scientific name: Parastrephia lucida
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America
