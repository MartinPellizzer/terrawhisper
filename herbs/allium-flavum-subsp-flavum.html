Allium flavum subsp. flavum

Allium flavum subsp. flavum, commonly known as Yellow Onion, is a distinctive perennial herb native to Central Asia. Characterized by its vibrant yellow, umbel-shaped flowers and aromatic bulbous roots, this subspecies is valued in traditional medicine for its antimicrobial properties and its potential role in supporting cardiovascular health.

  • Scientific name: Allium flavum subsp. flavum
  • Family: Amaryllidaceae
  • Native range: Europe
