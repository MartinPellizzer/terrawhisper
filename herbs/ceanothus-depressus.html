Ceanothus depressus

Ceaunothus depressus, commonly known as the low-growing broom, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to specific arid regions. Valued in traditional healing practices, this plant possesses unique bioactive properties. It is primarily utilized for its potential anti-inflammatory and antimicrobial effects, offering significant promise for natural pharmacological research and development.

  • Scientific name: Ceanothus depressus
  • Family: Rhamnaceae
  • Native range: Northern America
