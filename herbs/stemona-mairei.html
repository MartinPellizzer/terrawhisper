What's the taxonomical classification of Stemona mairei?

Stemona mairei belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this group, it is categorized under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving further down the taxonomic hierarchy, it falls under the order Pandanales and is a member of the family Stemonaceae. Finally, the plant is situated within the genus Stemona, specifically identified by the species epithet mairei.
