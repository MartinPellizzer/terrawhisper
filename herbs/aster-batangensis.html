Aster batangensis

Aster batangensis, commonly known as the Batang Aster, is a resilient perennial herb native to specific high-altitude regions. Renowned for its medicinal properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional healing practices to treat inflammatory conditions and digestive ailments, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness and botanical therapy.

  • Scientific name: Aster batangensis
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known as Aster batangensis, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Asterales and the family Asteraceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Aster.
