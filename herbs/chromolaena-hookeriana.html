Chromolaena hookeriana

Chromolaena hookerianana, commonly known as a medicinal shrub in various tropical regions, is a plant valued for its diverse therapeutic properties. Often utilized in traditional medicine, it possesses significant bioactive compounds known for their antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and wound-healing capabilities, making it a subject of interest for pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Chromolaena hookeriana
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the order Asterales and is a member of the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Chromolaena.

Common Names

This plant, Chromolaena hookerianana, is known by various regional names across different linguistic groups. In English, it is commonly referred to as the hookerianana chromolaena, while in Swahili, it is called mchanga mweusi. Additionally, in Hausa, the plant is identified as kwanon baki.
