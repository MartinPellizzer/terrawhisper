This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Distribution

This plant exhibits a widespread distribution throughout several key regions of Asia. In China, it can be found across the South-Central, Southeast, and Hainan areas. Its presence extends to the island of Taiwan within Eastern Asia. Additionally, the species is documented in the Indian Subcontinent, specifically in the Assam region. These diverse locations highlight its ability to thrive in various subtropical environments.
