Coin-leaf willow, scientifically known as Salix nummularia, is a resilient, low-growing shrub native to northern Europe and Asia. Characterized by its distinctive, rounded leaves, this hardy plant thrives in damp habitats. Historically valued in traditional medicine, it contains salicin, offering natural anti-inflammatory and analgesic properties for various ailments.
