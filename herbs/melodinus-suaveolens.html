Meloideus suaveolens belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Finally, its taxonomic identity is completed by its classification within the genus Meloideus and its specific designation as the species suaveolens.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Melodinus suaveolens has a prostrate, creeping growth habit characterized by slender, trailing stems and small, ovate to lanceolate leaves. These reproductive structures are generally pale in color and possess a subtle fragrance that contributes to the species' specific epithet.
