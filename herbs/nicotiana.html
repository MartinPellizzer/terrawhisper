Nicotiana

Nicotiana, commonly known as tobacco, is a genus of approximately 74 species within the Solanaceae family. While widely recognized for its commercial cultivation, many species possess significant medicinal properties. Historically, various parts of the plant have been utilized in traditional practices for their potent alkaloids and diverse pharmacological effects.

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified within the order Solanales and the family Solanaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identification is placed within the genus Nicotiana.
