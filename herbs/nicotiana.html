Plant Parts

This plant, Nicotiana (commonly known as tobacco), possesses specialized anatomical features that play significant roles in its biological processes. One of its most distinctive characteristics is the presence of multicellular trichomes, which are tiny, hair-like structures covering the leaves and stems that serve to protect the plant from herbivory and reduce water loss. Additionally, the flowers of the Nicotiana species produce nectar, a sugar-rich fluid secreted by specialized glands to attract various pollinators, ensuring the plant's reproductive success.
