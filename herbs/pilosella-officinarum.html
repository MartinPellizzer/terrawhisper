Pilosella officinarum

Hieracium pilosella

Pilosella officiinarum, commonly known as Devil's Claw or Mouse-ear Hawkweed, is a perennial herb prized in traditional herbalism. Renowned for its diuretic and astringent properties, it is frequently utilized to support kidney health, alleviate urinary tract discomfort, and promote fluid balance, offering a natural approach to holistic wellness.

  • Scientific name: Pilosella officinarum
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
