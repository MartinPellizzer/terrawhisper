Hieracium pilosella, scientifically known as Pilosella officinarum, is a perennial herb renowned in traditional herbalism for its diuretic properties. Often called mouse-ear hawkweed, this plant is frequently utilized to support kidney health and manage fluid retention. Its delicate, hairy leaves and yellow blooms make it a distinctive botanical specimen.
