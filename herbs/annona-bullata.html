Annona bullata

Annona bullata, commonly known as the bullata custard apple, is a significant medicinal plant native to various tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering valuable bioactive compounds for modern pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Annona bullata
  • Family: Annonaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Annona bullata, belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Magnoliales. Finally, it is a member of the family Annonaceae and falls under the genus Annona.
