Tinospora bakis

Tinospora bakis, commonly known as a potent medicinal herb, is a climbing shrub valued in traditional medicine. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it exhibits significant antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. This plant is frequently utilized to support immune health and manage various metabolic disorders through its therapeutic potential.

  • Scientific name: Tinospora bakis
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Ranunculales and is a member of the family Menispermaceae. Specifically, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Tinospora.
