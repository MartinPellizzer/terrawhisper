Carya illinoensis, scientifically known as Carya illinoinensis, is the hickory tree prized for its nutrient-dense nuts. Beyond its culinary value, traditional medicine utilizes its bark and leaves for their astringent and anti-inflammatory properties. This resilient species serves as a vital botanical resource in both ecological and therapeutic contexts.
