Alnus pendula

Alnus pendula, commonly known as the European alder, is a graceful deciduous tree native to Europe. While primarily valued in landscaping for its elegant, weeping form, it holds traditional significance in herbalism. Its bark and roots contain bioactive compounds that have historically been explored for various medicinal applications.

  • Scientific name: Alnus pendula
  • Family: Betulaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Fagales, it is classified under the family Betulaceae and belongs to the genus Alnus.
