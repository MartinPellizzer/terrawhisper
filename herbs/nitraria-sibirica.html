Nitraria sibirica

Nitraria sibirica, commonly known as the Siberian Nitraria, is a resilient, salt-tolerant shrub native to arid Central Asian regions. Highly valued in traditional medicine, this plant offers therapeutic potential due to its unique phytochemical properties, often utilized for its antioxidant effects and its ability to support various physiological functions.

  • Scientific name: Nitraria sibirica
  • Family: Zygophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Zygophyllales, it is classified under the family Zygophyllaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Nitraria.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the vast expanse of Siberia. Its presence is specifically documented in the Altay region, where it thrives in local ecosystems. Furthermore, the species can be found inhabiting the territory of Buryatiya. It also stretches across the eastern landscapes of the Chita region. Finally, its geographical range extends through the Irkutsk and Krasnoyarsk areas of Siberia.
