Hellenia speciosa

Hellenia speciosa, commonly known as Indian Mulberry, is a versatile medicinal plant prized in traditional Ayurvedic practices. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it offers significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and antimicrobial properties. This robust shrub is widely utilized in holistic medicine to support immune health and manage various systemic ailments.

  • Scientific name: Hellenia speciosa
  • Family: Costaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Zingiberales, it is further categorized into the family Costaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Hellenia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across two major regions. Within the Western Indian Ocean, it can be found growing in the Comoros, Mauritius, and Réunion. Additionally, its presence extends to East Asia, specifically covering parts of China. In this region, it is documented in China South-Central as well as on the island of Hainan. Consequently, the species thrives in both isolated oceanic islands and significant mainland territories.
