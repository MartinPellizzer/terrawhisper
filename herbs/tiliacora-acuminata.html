Tiliacora acuminata

Tiliacora acuminata, commonly known as Balsam pear or Kraton, is a climbing woody vine native to Southeast Asian rainforests. Highly valued in traditional medicine, its leaves and fruits possess potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties, making it a vital botanical resource for treating various ailments and promoting wellness.

  • Scientific name: Tiliacora acuminata
  • Family: Menispermaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical
