Salvia africana-caerulea (informal name), scientifically known as Salvia africana, is a remarkable perennial shrub native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its striking blue flowers and potent aromatic properties, this medicinal plant is traditionally utilized in various cultures to treat ailments ranging from digestive issues to respiratory infections and inflammation.
