Aconitum kusnezoffii

Aconitum kusnezoffii, commonly known as Kusnezoff's monkshood, is a potent perennial herb belonging to the Ranunculaceae family. Renowned for its striking blue flowers, it contains highly toxic alkaloids. While historically utilized in traditional medicine for pain and inflammation, its extreme toxicity requires precise processing to ensure therapeutic safety.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum kusnezoffii
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Aconitum kusnezoffii, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is further classified within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, falling under the order Ranunculales. Finally, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae and the genus Aconitum.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions of Northern Asia. Within the vast expanse of Siberia, it can be found inhabiting the territories of Buryatiya and Chitа. It also grows throughout the Irkutsk region and extends into the northern reaches of Yakutiya. Furthermore, its presence is documented within the Russian Far East, specifically in the Amur region. Consequently, these locations define the primary geographical range for this medicinal species.
