Sesamum indicum

Sesamum indicum, commonly known as sesame, is a versatile oilseed plant cherished for millennia. Renowned for its nutrient-dense seeds, it serves as a vital source of healthy fats, proteins, and antioxidants. Beyond nutrition, this botanical powerhouse offers significant medicinal benefits, including anti-inflammatory properties and support for cardiovascular health.

  • Scientific name: Sesamum indicum
  • Family: Pedaliaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Sesamum indicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Pedaliaceae and is placed in the genus Sesamum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across various regions of the European continent. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in Spain. Its presence extends into Southeastern Europe, where it is documented in Bulgaria and Greece. Furthermore, the species is located in the European portion of Türkiye. Finally, its range encompasses the Northwestern Balkan Peninsula.
