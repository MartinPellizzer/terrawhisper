Taxonomical Classification

This plant, known scientifically as Sesamum indicum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae within the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Pedaliaceae and is placed in the genus Sesamum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific distribution across various regions of the European continent. In Southwestern Europe, it can be found growing in Spain. Plant Parts

This plant, Sesamum indicum, is a versatile medicinal and nutritional resource where various components offer distinct benefits. The flowers are often used in traditional practices, while the lush green leaves serve as a source of vitamins and minerals. Most notably, it is classified as a high-value oilseed, primarily valued for the nutrient-dense seeds it produces. Within these seeds, the presence of seed storage globulins plays a crucial role in providing essential proteins, making the plant a vital component in both dietary and therapeutic applications.
