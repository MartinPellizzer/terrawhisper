Malus toringo

Malus toringo, commonly known as the Toringo Crabapple, is a resilient deciduous tree prized for its ornamental beauty and medicinal potential. Beyond its delicate blossoms, various parts of the plant are utilized in traditional practices to support wellness, offering unique bioactive compounds that contribute to holistic health and healing.
