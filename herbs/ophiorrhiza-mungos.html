Ophiorrhiza mungos

Ophiorrhiza mungos, commonly known as White Ophiorrhiza, is a significant medicinal herb native to South Asia. Renowned in Ayurvedic medicine, this plant possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. It is widely utilized to treat various ailments, including fever, skin disorders, and gastrointestinal issues, serving as a vital therapeutic resource.

  • Scientific name: Ophiorrhiza mungos
  • Family: Rubiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Tropical

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, situated within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. It is further classified under the order Gentianaales and the family Rubiaceae. Ultimately, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Ophiorrhiza.
