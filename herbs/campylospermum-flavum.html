Campylospermum flavum

Campylospermum flavum, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical ecosystems, holds profound ethnobotanical value. Renowned for its diverse bioactive compounds, this species is utilized in traditional healing practices to address various ailments. Understanding its phytochemical properties is essential for exploring its potential in modern pharmacological developments.

  • Scientific name: Campylospermum flavum
  • Family: Ochnaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Ochnacaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Campylospermum, specifically the species Campylospermum flavum.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of West Tropical Africa. Its presence is documented within the borders of Benin, where it thrives in local ecosystems. Furthermore, the species can be found extending into the territories of Burkina and Ghana. The geographical range also encompasses the coastal and inland regions of Guinea-Bissau. Finally, the plant is established within the country of Guinea, completing its regional distribution.
