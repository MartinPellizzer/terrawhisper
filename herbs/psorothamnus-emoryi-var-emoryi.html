Psorothamnus emoryi var. emoryi

Psorothamnus emoryi var. emoryi, commonly known as Emory's indigo bush, is a resilient desert shrub native to the Southwestern United States. Valued in ethnobotanical traditions, this plant possesses unique chemical properties. Its specialized adaptations to arid environments make it a fascinating subject for studying natural medicinal compounds and survival.
