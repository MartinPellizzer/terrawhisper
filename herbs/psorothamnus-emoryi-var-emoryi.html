Dyebush, scientifically known as Psorothamnus emoryi var. emoryi, is a resilient desert shrub native to the southwestern United States. Renowned for its striking yellow blooms and deep-rooted survival, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value, traditionally utilized by indigenous communities for its unique medicinal properties and natural pigment qualities.
