Persicaria nepalensis

Persicaria nepellenis, commonly known as Himalayan knotweed, is a charming perennial valued for its striking pink flowers. Native to the Himalayan regions, this resilient plant is prized in traditional herbal practices for its diverse therapeutic properties, offering potential medicinal benefits in treating various ailments through its unique bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Persicaria nepalensis
  • Family: Polygonaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is a member of the family Polygonaceae. Ultimately, its scientific identity is defined by its genus, Persicaria.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Persicaria nepalenensis, is referred to by various common names depending on the region and language. Additionally, in Japan, it is commonly called タニソバ.
