Plant Parts

This plant, Persicaria nepellenis, is a medicinal herb where various components are utilized for their therapeutic properties. In many traditional applications, the whole plants are harvested to ensure all active constituents are captured, though often the whole herb is specifically prepared for decoctions and infusions. The structural foundation of the plant consists of sturdy stems that support the lush growth, while the broad, green leaves serve as a primary site for concentrated medicinal compounds.
