Trichocentrum ascendens

Trichocentrum ascendens, commonly known as the climbing orchid, is a remarkable medicinal plant native to tropical regions. Renowned for its bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in various herbal remedies to treat respiratory issues and inflammation. Exploring its phytochemical properties offers promising potential for modern pharmacological advancements and holistic healing.

  • Family: Orchidaceae

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, placing it within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae.
