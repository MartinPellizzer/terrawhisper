Veronica

Veronica, commonly known as speedwell, is a diverse genus of flowering plants cherished in both gardens and traditional herbalism. Renowned for their delicate, blue-hued blossoms, these resilient perennials offer significant medicinal potential. Historically, various species have been utilized to support respiratory health and soothe skin inflammations naturally.

  • Scientific name: Veronica
  • Family: Plantaginaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Lamiales and the family Plantaginaceae, it is classified under the genus Veronica.
