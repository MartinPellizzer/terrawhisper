Aconitum richardsonianum var. pseudosessiliflorum

Aconitum richardsonianum var. pseudosessiliflorum, commonly known as a subspecies of Richardson's monkshood, is a striking perennial herb. Renowned for its distinctive blue-violet flowers, this plant contains potent alkaloids. While historically significant in botanical studies, it is highly toxic, requiring extreme caution and expert knowledge when handled or studied.

  • Scientific name: Aconitum richardsonianum var. pseudosessiliflorum
  • Family: Ranunculaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ranunculales, it is a member of the family Ranunculaceae. Specifically, it is identified as a variety within the genus Aconitum.
