Red gromwell, scientifically known as Lithospermum erythrorhizon, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its vibrant red roots, it contains bioactive naphthodianthrones. These compounds offer potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and skin-healing properties, making it a vital ingredient in various dermatological treatments and holistic healing practices.
