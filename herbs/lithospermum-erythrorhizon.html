red gromwell

Lithospermum erythrorhizon · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (131 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Red gromwell, scientifically known as Lithospermum erythrorhizon, is a perennial herb prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its vibrant red roots, it contains bioactive naphthodianthrones. These compounds offer potent anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and skin-healing properties, making it a vital ingredient in various dermatological treatments and holistic healing practices.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution across several key regions in Northern and Eastern Asia. In the vast expanse of Siberia, it can be found specifically in the areas of Chita and Yakutiya. Moving toward the Russian Far East, the species is also established in the Amur and Primorye regions. Its range extends significantly further south into the territory of China, particularly within the China South-Central region. Collectively, these locations highlight the plant's ability to thrive across diverse continental landscapes.

Plant Parts

This plant, known for its significant pharmacological properties, relies primarily on its specialized underground structures for medicinal use. The most vital components are the roots, which are harvested to obtain their potent bioactive compounds. Once processed, the dried root is often utilized in various traditional preparations to ensure stability and potency. These characteristic red roots are highly valued in herbal medicine, specifically because they serve as the primary source for concentrated red pigment extracts. Ultimately, the medicinal value of the root lies in these deep, colorful tissues that hold the essence of the plant's healing potential.
