Shirakiopsis indica

Shirakiopsis indica, commonly known as the "Tree of Life" in certain traditional contexts, is a versatile medicinal plant native to the Western Ghats. Renowned for its potent pharmacological properties, its bark and leaves are widely utilized in Ayurvedic practices to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and various digestive disorders.

  • Scientific name: Shirakiopsis indica
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
