Lamprocapnos spectabilis

Lamprocapnos spectabilis, commonly known as Shooting Star, is a delicate perennial wildflower celebrated for its unique, backward-pointing blooms. While primarily admired for its aesthetic elegance in woodland habitats, this plant holds significant botanical interest. Understanding its growth patterns and ecological role is essential for enthusiasts of medicinal flora.

  • Scientific name: Lamprocapnos spectabilis
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida.
