Syrian catnip, scientifically known as Nepeta curviflora, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Renowned for its aromatic foliage and delicate, curved flowers, it belongs to the mint family. This plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its calming properties and its unique attraction to felids.
