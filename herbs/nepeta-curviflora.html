Nepeta curviflora

Nepeta curviflora, commonly known as the curved-flowered catmint, is a distinctive perennial herb native to Southern Africa. Characterized by its unique, arched floral structures and aromatic foliage, this species belongs to the Lamiaceae family. It is valued in botanical studies for its specialized morphology and potential medicinal properties.

  • Scientific name: Nepeta curviflora
  • Family: Lamiaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. Following its placement in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Lamiales, it is a member of the family Lamiaceae. Ultimately, it is categorized under the genus Nepeta.
