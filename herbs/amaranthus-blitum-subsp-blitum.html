Plant Parts

This plant, Amaranthus blitum subsp. blitum, is characterized by its versatile morphology, where various parts contribute to its medicinal profile. The shoots are prominent, often exhibiting a branched structure that supports the overall growth of the herb. Attached to these stems are numerous green leaves, each leaf typically possessing an ovate or lanceolate shape with smooth margins. Together, these components form a lush, leafy structure that is commonly harvested for its nutritional and therapeutic properties.
