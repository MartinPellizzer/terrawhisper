Amaranthus blitum subsp. blitum

Amaranthus blitum subsp. blitum, commonly known as Slender Amaranth, is a resilient annual herb found in tropical and subtropical regions. Valued for its nutritional density, this plant offers significant medicinal potential, containing antioxidants and bioactive compounds used traditionally to support digestive health and reduce inflammation in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Amaranthus blitum subsp. blitum
  • Family: Amaranthaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Caryophyllales, it is classified under the family Amaranthaceae. Finally, it is placed in the genus Amaranthus as the subspecies Amaranthus blitum subsp. blitum.

Common Names

This plant, Amaranthus blitum subsp. blitum, is known by a variety of common names depending on the region and language. In English, it is frequently referred to as slender amaranth or amaranth. In Spanish, it is commonly called bledo or bledo de campo, while in Portuguese, it is known as bledo. Additionally, in French, it is referred to as amarante.
