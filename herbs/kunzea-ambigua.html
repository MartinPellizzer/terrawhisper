Kunzea ambigua

Kunzea ambigua, commonly known as the Kunzea or bushy kunzea, is a resilient evergreen shrub native to Australia. Renowned for its delicate, creamy-white flower clusters, this medicinal plant offers significant ethnobotanical value. Its unique chemical properties suggest potential therapeutic uses, making it a fascinating subject for botanical research.

  • Scientific name: Kunzea ambigua
  • Family: Myrtaceae
  • Native range: Australasia

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Myrtales and the family Myrtaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Kunzea.
