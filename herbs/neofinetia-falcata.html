What's the taxonomical classification of Neofinetia falcata?

Neofinetia falcata belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it follows the biological lineage of the order Asparagales. This plant is a distinguished representative of the family Orchidaceae, falling under the genus Neofinetia, with its specific designation being the species falcata.
