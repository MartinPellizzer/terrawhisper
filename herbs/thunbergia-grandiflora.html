Bengal trumpet vine, scientifically known as Thunbergia grandiflora, is a vigorous, evergreen climbing shrub native to Brazil. Renowned for its large, bell-shaped, creamy-white flowers, this ornamental climber thrives in tropical climates. Beyond its stunning aesthetic appeal, it holds significant botanical interest for its rapid growth and lush foliage.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical range that spans several distinct regions across the Atlantic. In the Macaronesian islands, it can be found specifically in Madeira. Its distribution extends to West Tropical Africa, where it is recorded in Gambia. Within West-Central Tropical Africa, the species is present in both Cameroon and Gabon. Additionally, it inhabits the remote Middle Atlantic Ocean on Ascension Island.
