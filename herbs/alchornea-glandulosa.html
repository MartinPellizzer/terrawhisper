Alchornea glandulosa

Alchornea glandulosa, commonly known as the "palo de sangre," is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat skin ailments, inflammation, and gastrointestinal issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for modern scientific research.

  • Scientific name: Alchornea glandulosa
  • Family: Euphorbiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Malpighiales and the family Euphorbiaceae.
