Dalbergia monetaria, scientifically known as Dalbergia monetaria, is a precious tropical hardwood valued for its exquisite grain and medicinal properties. Often found in Southeast Asian forests, this species is highly prized in traditional medicine for its therapeutic potential, while its rare, ornamental timber remains a luxury in woodworking.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a diverse geographical distribution spanning across two major global regions. In West-Central Tropical Africa, it can be found within the territories of Equatorial Guinea and Gabon. Its presence is also noted throughout the Gulf of Guinea Islands in that same African region. Shifting to North America, the species is distributed across various parts of Mexico. Specifically, it inhabits the Mexico Gulf area as well as the Mexico Southeast region.
