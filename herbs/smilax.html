Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution focused within the Mediterranean region. It can be found growing across various parts of Southwestern Europe, including France and Portugal. Additionally, the species is present on several significant islands such as Corse and Sardegna. Its range also extends to the Baleares, further establishing its presence in the archipelago. Overall, these locations highlight the plant's preference for the unique climates of the Southwestern European coastlines.

Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as Sarsaparilla, is highly valued in traditional medicine for the therapeutic properties found within its anatomy. While the entire vine may be studied, the most significant medicinal component is the root. This underground part is harvested, dried, and processed to extract active compounds that are believed to support blood purification and skin health.
