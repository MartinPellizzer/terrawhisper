What's the taxonomical classification of Euonymus semenovii?

Euonymus semenovii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, this plant is organized under the order Celastrales. It is a member of the family Celastraceae, falling specifically within the genus Euonymus, and is identified by its unique species designation, semenovii.

These blossoms eventually develop into distinctive four-lobed capsules that split open to reveal bright orange or red fleshy seeds.
