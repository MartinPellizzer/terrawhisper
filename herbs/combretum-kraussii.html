Combretum kraussii

Combretum kraussii, commonly known as the River Bushwillow, is a resilient deciduous tree native to Southern Africa. Renowned for its medicinal properties, various parts of this plant are utilized in traditional healing to treat ailments ranging from skin infections to digestive issues, showcasing its significant ethnobotanical value and ecological importance.

  • Scientific name: Combretum kraussii
  • Family: Combretaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Myrtales and the family Combretaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Combretum.

Distribution

This plant is widely distributed across several key regions within the southern part of the continent. In South Tropical Africa, its presence is notably documented in Mozambique. Moving into the southern reaches, it can be found throughout the Cape Provinces. The species also inhabits the lush landscapes of KwaZulu-Natal and the territory of Esватини. Finally, its geographical range extends into the Northern Provinces of Southern Africa.
