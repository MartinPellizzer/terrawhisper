Tephroseris palustris

What's the taxonomical classification of Tephroseris palustris?

Tephroseris palustris belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida, it is situated in the subclass Magnoliidae and falls under the order Asterales. The plant is a member of the family Asteraceae, which places it within the genus Tephroseris, and is specifically identified by its species name, palustris.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Tephroseris palustris has a perennial herbaceous habit characterized by erect stems that typically reach heights of 10 to 40 centimeters and bear many small, discoid flower heads. The leaves are primarily basal and lanceolate to linear in shape, often featuring serrated margins and a somewhat fleshy texture. The stems are sparsely clothed in fine, spreading hairs that distinguish them from similar species. Each flower head is composed of numerous yellow tubular florets clustered on a common receptacle. The involucral bracts are arranged in multiple series and are often tinged with purple or green.
