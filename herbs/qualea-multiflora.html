Qualea multiflora

Qualea multiflora, commonly known as “jacarandá-do-campo,” is a remarkable medicinal tree native to the Brazilian Cerrado. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally utilized to treat inflammation, digestive issues, and skin ailments. This resilient species offers significant pharmacological potential for modern therapeutic research and natural healing.

  • Scientific name: Qualea multiflora
  • Family: Vochysiaceae
  • Native range: Southern America
