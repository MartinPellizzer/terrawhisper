Plant Parts

This plant, commonly known as the red spider lily, relies on its underground bulbs for both survival and medicinal application. The bulbs serve as the primary storage organs, containing the concentrated alkaloids and bioactive compounds that are often the focus of pharmacological studies. Because the bulb acts as a nutrient reservoir that allows the plant to persist through dormant periods, it is the most significant part used in traditional preparations.
