This life cycle allows the species to exploit seasonal favorable conditions and evade unfavorable periods, such as extreme drought or cold, by persisting in the environment solely through its seed bank. Within the broader spectrum of plant life forms, its developmental trajectory aligns with the therophyte designation, distinguishing it from perennial forms like phanerophytes or hemicryptophytes that maintain permanent aboveground or bud-protected structures.
