Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Piper lanatum (commonly referred to as Devil's Club), is a significant medicinal species where various components hold therapeutic value, though the bark is one of the most frequently utilized parts in traditional healing practices. The inner bark is often harvested and prepared into decoctions or tinctures to treat ailments such as digestive issues, inflammation, and skin conditions. While other parts of the plant like the roots and leaves may also be used, the bark remains a central element in its medicinal application.
