Sichuan teasel, scientifically known as Dipsacus asper, is a perennial herb highly valued in traditional Chinese medicine. Renowned for its therapeutic properties, it is frequently used to nourish the liver, kidney, and blood. This botanical specimen plays a vital role in treating various ailments, including weakness and dizziness.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across several distinct regions within East and Southeast Asia. In China, its presence is noted in the South-Central and Southeast territories, as well as in the high-altitude regions of Tibet. Its range extends into the Indian Subcontinent, specifically within the Assam region. Additionally, the species can be found throughout the Indo-China peninsula. Finally, its geographical reach includes the country of Myanmar.
