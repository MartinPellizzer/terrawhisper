Primula veris subsp. veris

Cowslip

Primula veris subsp. veris, commonly known as cowslip, is a charming perennial wildflower native to Europe. Renowned for its bright yellow, bell-shaped blossoms, this plant holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in herbal medicine to soothe respiratory ailments and calm nerves, it remains a symbol of springtime vitality.

  • Scientific name: Primula veris subsp. veris
  • Family: Primulaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Ericales, it is classified under the family Primulaceae and the genus Primula. Specifically, this taxon is identified as the subspecies Primula veris subsp. veris.
