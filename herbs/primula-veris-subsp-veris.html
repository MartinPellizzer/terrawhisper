Cowslip, scientifically known as Primula veris subsp. veris, is a charming perennial wildflower distinguished by its bright yellow, bell-shaped blossoms. Traditionally valued in herbal medicine, this plant is renowned for its soothing properties, particularly in supporting respiratory health and calming the nervous system through its unique bioactive compounds.
