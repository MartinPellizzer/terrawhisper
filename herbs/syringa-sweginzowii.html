What's the taxonomical classification of Syringa sweginzowii?

Syringa sweginzowii belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. As a member of the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is situated under the order Lamiales. This plant is a part of the family Oleaceae, falling under the genus Syringa, and is specifically identified by its species name, sweginzowii.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

The floral structure features a long corolla tube that terminates in four spreading lobes. Small, globose drupes serve as the fruit, often appearing in the following growing season.
