Pachylecythis pisonis

Pachylecythis pisonis, commonly known as a significant medicinal plant in tropical regions, possesses unique therapeutic properties. Traditionally utilized for its bioactive compounds, it offers potential in treating various ailments. Understanding its phytochemical profile is essential for exploring its pharmacological applications and integrating it into modern natural medicine practices.

  • Scientific name: Pachylecythis pisonis
  • Family: Lecythidaceae
  • Native range: Southern America

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. It is further classified under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales. Finally, it is a member of the Lecythidaceae family, specifically falling under the genus Pachylecythis.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions of the Americas. In the Caribbean, its presence is specifically noted within the islands of Trinidad and Tobago. Moving toward the continent, it can be found across Western South America, including both Colombia and Peru. Within Brazil, its range extends significantly into the West-Central territories. Additionally, the species is documented as growing within the Northeast region of Brazil.
