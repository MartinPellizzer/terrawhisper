Peganum nigellastrum

Peganum nigellashtrum, commonly known as Black Seed or Nigella Sativa, is a versatile medicinal plant celebrated for centuries. Rich in bioactive compounds like thymoquinone, it offers potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties. Traditionally used in diverse cultures, this remarkable botanical supports immune health and metabolic wellness globally.

  • Scientific name: Peganum nigellastrum
  • Family: Nitrariaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Peganum nigellasthrum, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, it is classified under the order Sapindales. Finally, it is a member of the family Nitrariaceae and the genus Peganum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution spanning several distinct regions across Asia. In the northern reaches, it can be found growing within the Buryatiya region of Siberia. Its presence extends significantly into China, where it inhabits the areas of Inner Mongolia and Xinjiang. Furthermore, the species is widely distributed across the China North-Central territories. Finally, the plant's range reaches into the China Southeast region, completing its expansive habitat.
