Cinnamomum chartophyllum

What's the taxonomical classification of Cinnamomum chartophyllum?

Cinnamomum chartophyllum belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within this lineage, it is situated in the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae. Moving down the taxonomic hierarchy, the plant is organized under the order Laurales and the family Lauraceae. Finally, it is identified by its specific genus, Cinnamomum, and its unique species designation, chartophyllum.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Cinnamomum chartophyllum has elliptical to oblong leaves that are characterized by a distinctively chartaceous or paper-like texture and a prominent midrib. The leaf margins are typically entire and may exhibit slight undulation along the edges. These evergreen leaves are arranged in an alternate pattern along the woody stems of the tree. The surface of the foliage often displays a glossy appearance on the upper side while the underside remains relatively dull. Small, inconspicuous flowers are produced in axillary clusters, eventually leading to the development of small drupes.
