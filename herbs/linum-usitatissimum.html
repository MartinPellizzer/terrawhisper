Plant Parts

This plant, Linum usitatissimum, commonly known as flax, offers a variety of therapeutic components derived from its different structures. The small, oval-shaped seeds are highly valued for their nutritional density and ability to support digestive health. When these seeds are processed, they yield linseed oil, a potent source of alpha-linolenic acid that provides essential omega-3 fatty acids for cardiovascular wellness. Furthermore, the concentrated seeds extract is frequently utilized in medicinal formulations to harness the plant's full bioactive potential, offering antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits.
