Rosa ecae

Rosa ecae, commonly known as the Sacred Rose, is a rare medicinal plant revered for its profound healing properties. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory qualities, it is traditionally used to support skin vitality, reduce oxidative stress, and promote holistic wellness through its unique, aromatic botanical compounds.

  • Scientific name: Rosa ecae
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, specifically classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. It falls within the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae, ultimately being identified under the genus Rosa.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the diverse landscapes of Central Asia. Within this region, it can be found growing in the territories of Kyrgyzstan and Turkmenistan. Its presence also extends to the mountainous and arid terrains of Tajikistan. Furthermore, the species is well-established within the borders of Uzbekistan. Beyond Central Asia, its geographical range reaches into Western Asia, specifically within Afghanistan.
