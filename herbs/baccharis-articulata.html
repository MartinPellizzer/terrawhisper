Baccharis articulata

Baccharis articulata, commonly known as the stickbush or shrubby baccharis, is a resilient woody plant native to various regions of the Americas. Renowned for its medicinal properties, it is frequently utilized in traditional folk medicine to treat inflammation, skin ailments, and digestive issues, offering significant pharmacological potential for research.

  • Scientific name: Baccharis articulata
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Following the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Asterales and placed in the family Asteraceae. Ultimately, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by its membership in the genus Baccharis.
