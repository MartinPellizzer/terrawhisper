Phytolacca americana

Phytolacca americana, commonly known as pokeweed, is a perennial herbaceous plant native to North America. Renowned for its deep purple stems and dark berries, it holds a dual legacy: while traditionally used in folk medicine for various ailments, its potent compounds require careful handling due to inherent toxicity.

  • Scientific name: Phytolacca americana
  • Family: Phytolaccaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Caryophyllales, it is further categorized into the family Phytolaccaceae. Finally, its taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Phytolacca.
