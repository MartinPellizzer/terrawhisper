African wormwood

Artemisia afra · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (117 studies) · ★★★☆☆

African wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia afra, is a versatile perennial shrub native to various regions of Africa. Renowned for its aromatic, pungent foliage, this medicinal plant has long been utilized in traditional healing to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and malaria, making it a cornerstone of herbal medicine.

Plant Parts

This plant, known scientifically as Artemisia afra, is primarily valued for its therapeutic properties found within its aerial parts. The most significant medicinal components are concentrated in the leaves, which are often harvested to prepare traditional remedies. Each individual leaf contains essential oils that contribute to the plant's aromatic profile. In addition to the plant leaves, the stems also form part of the harvested material, though the foliage remains the most potent element of the species.
