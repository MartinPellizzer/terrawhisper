Zi cao

Arnebia euchroma · Accepted scientific name

Scientific literature: Moderate (132 studies) · ★★★☆☆

Zi cao, scientifically known as Arnebia euchroma, is a perennial herb renowned in traditional Chinese medicine for its potent therapeutic properties. Rich in shikonin derivatives, this plant is widely utilized to treat inflammation, skin infections, and promote wound healing, making it a vital component in various dermatological treatments.
