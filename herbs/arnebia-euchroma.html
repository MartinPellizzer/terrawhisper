Arnebia euchroma

Arnebia euchroma, commonly known as Alkanet or Iranian Alkanet, is a perennial medicinal herb prized in traditional medicine. Renowned for its deep red roots, it possesses significant anti-inflammatory, antioxidant, and wound-healing properties. Often used in dermatological treatments, this plant serves as a vital natural remedy in various cultures.

  • Scientific name: Arnebia euchroma
  • Family: Boraginaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Boraginales and the family Boraginaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic identity is defined by the genus Arnebia.

Distribution

This plant is primarily distributed across the vast landscapes of Central Asia. It can be found growing naturally within the borders of Kazakhstan. Its presence is also documented throughout the mountainous and arid regions of Kirgizstan. Furthermore, the species inhabits various territories in Turkmenistan and Tadzhikistan. Finally, the geographical range of this medicinal herb extends into Uzbekistan.
