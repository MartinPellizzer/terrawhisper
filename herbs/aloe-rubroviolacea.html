Aloe rubroviolacea

Aloe rubroviolacea, commonly known as the Red-Violet Aloe, is a striking succulent prized for its deep, pigment-rich foliage. Beyond its ornamental beauty, this medicinal plant is valued in traditional practices for its soothing properties, offering potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory benefits through its unique, nutrient-dense gel and bioactive compounds.

  • Family: Asphodelaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate
