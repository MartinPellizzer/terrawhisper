Cereus hildmannianus

Cereus hildmanniaus, commonly known as the Hilmann's Cereus, is a striking nocturnal cactus native to South American landscapes. While primarily prized by collectors for its magnificent, large white blooms, its unique biological properties offer fascinating potential in ethnobotany, representing a fascinating intersection of ornamental beauty and traditional medicinal interest.

  • Scientific name: Cereus hildmannianus
  • Family: Cactaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized in the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, falling into the family Cactaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Cereus and the specific species Cereus hildmannianus.
