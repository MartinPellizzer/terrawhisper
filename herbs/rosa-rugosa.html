Rugosa rose, scientifically known as Rosa rugosa, is a hardy, salt-tolerant shrub prized for its fragrant, vibrant blooms and medicinal properties. Rich in Vitamin C and antioxidants, its hips are used in herbal remedies to support immunity, skin health, and digestion, making it a versatile botanical treasure.
