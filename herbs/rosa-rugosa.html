It is highly tolerant of salt spray, high wind exposure, and sandy, nutrient-poor soils, allowing it to dominate maritime landscapes. In addition to coastal regions, it can colonize disturbed sites, forest edges, and riparian zones, often acting as a pioneer species in areas undergoing ecological succession.
