Rosa rugosa

Rugosa rose

Rosa rugosa, commonly known as the rugosa rose, is a resilient, salt-tolerant shrub prized for its fragrant blooms and vibrant hips. In traditional medicine, its nutrient-rich rose hips are utilized for their high vitamin C content, offering potent antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and immune-boosting properties to support overall wellness.

  • Scientific name: Rosa rugosa
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is positioned in the order Rosales and the family Rosaceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Rosa.

Common Names

Distribution

This plant exhibits a notable presence across several regions of Northern Europe. It can be found growing in the coastal and inland areas of Denmark. Furthermore, its distribution extends to the northern landscapes of Finland. The species is also well-established within the territories of Great Britain and Ireland. Finally, it continues its northern reach into the rugged terrain of Norway.
