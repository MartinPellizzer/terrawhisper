Hydrangea

Hortensia

Hydrangea, commonly known as bigleaf or mophead hydrangea, is a versatile flowering shrub celebrated for its breathtaking, voluminous blooms. While primarily prized in landscaping for their color-changing petals, various species hold traditional medicinal significance, utilized in herbal practices to support kidney health and manage inflammation through their bioactive compounds.

  • Scientific name: Hydrangea
  • Family: Hydrangeaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Cornales, it is classified under the family Hydrangeaceae and belongs to the genus Hydrangea.
