Diospyros bipindensis

Diospyros bipindensis, commonly known as the ebony tree, is a significant medicinal plant found in tropical regions. Renowned for its dense timber, it also holds ethnobotanical value. Various parts of the tree are utilized in traditional medicine to treat diverse ailments, showcasing its profound importance in natural pharmacology.

  • Scientific name: Diospyros bipindensis
  • Family: Ebenaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. It is further categorized under the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, falling into the family Ebenaceae. Finally, its taxonomic placement is completed within the genus Diospyros.
