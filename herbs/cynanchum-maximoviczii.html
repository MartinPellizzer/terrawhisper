Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution across parts of Eastern Asia and the Russian Far East. It can be found growing within the Kuril Islands, which are part of the Russian Far East. Additionally, its range extends to Sakhalin, another significant region in the Russian Far East. Moving further into Eastern Asia, the species is also widely distributed throughout Japan. Together, these locations define the natural habitat where this medicinal plant thrives.
