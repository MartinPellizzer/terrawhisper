Mesua ferrea

Mesua ferrea, commonly known as Ceylon ironwood, is a majestic evergreen tree native to South Asia. Renowned for its fragrant, waxy white flowers and dense, durable timber, it holds significant value in traditional Ayurvedic medicine. Its various parts are utilized for their potent antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antioxidant therapeutic properties.

  • Scientific name: Mesua ferrea
  • Family: Calophyllaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae and order Malpighiales, it is a member of the family Calophyllaceae. Finally, it is placed under the genus Mesua, specifically known as Mesua ferrea.
