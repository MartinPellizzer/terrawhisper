Indian Rose Chestnut, scientifically known as Mesua ferrea, is a magnificent evergreen tree native to South Asia. Renowned for its fragrant, snow-white blossoms and dense timber, it holds significant value in Ayurveda. This versatile medicinal plant is widely utilized for its antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, and antimicrobial properties in traditional healing.

This plant exhibits a broad distribution across several key regions in Asia. In China, its presence is noted specifically in the South-Central and Southeast territories.
