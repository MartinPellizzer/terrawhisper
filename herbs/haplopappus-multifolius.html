Haplopappus multifolius

Haplopappus multifolius, commonly known as the many-leaved haplopappus, is a resilient medicinal shrub native to specific arid regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this plant is traditionally utilized in herbal remedies to address various ailments, offering valuable bioactive compounds that contribute to holistic wellness and natural healing.
