Myoporum bontioides

Myoporum bontioides, commonly known as the Boerhaavia or various local medicinal names, is a significant shrub valued in traditional healing. Found in specific ecological zones, this plant possesses diverse bioactive properties. It is frequently utilized for its therapeutic potential in treating various ailments, highlighting its importance in ethnobotanical studies.

  • Scientific name: Myoporum bontioides
  • Family: Scrophulariaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant, Myoporum bontioides, belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta. It is classified under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae, falling within the order Lamiales. Finally, it is a member of the family Scrophulariaceae and the genus Myoporum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several regions in Eastern Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in Hainan as well as throughout China Southeast. Its range extends to the island of Taiwan, which is a key part of its habitat. Additionally, the species is present in Japan and the Nansei-shotō islands. Together, these locations define the specific geographical footprint of Myoporum bontioides.
