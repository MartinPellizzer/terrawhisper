Distribution

This plant exhibits a wide distribution across several regions in Eastern Asia. In China, it can be found specifically in Hainan as well as throughout China Southeast. Its range extends to the island of Taiwan, which is a key part of its habitat. Additionally, the species is present in Japan and the Nansei-shotō islands.
