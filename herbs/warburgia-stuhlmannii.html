Warburgia stuhlmannii

Warburgia stuhlmannii, commonly known as the Pepper-bark tree, is a vital medicinal plant native to African forests. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and antifungal properties, its bark is traditionally used to treat respiratory infections, digestive issues, and skin ailments, making it a cornerstone of ethnomedicine and pharmacological research.

  • Scientific name: Warburgia stuhlmannii
  • Family: Canellaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Canellales, it is classified under the family Canellaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Warburgia.
