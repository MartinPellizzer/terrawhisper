Phlomis anisodonta

Phlomis anisodonta, commonly known as the anise-leaved sage, is a distinctive perennial herb native to the Mediterranean region. Characterized by its aromatic, serrated foliage and striking hooded flowers, this plant is valued in traditional herbalism for its unique phytochemical properties and its potential role in supporting various therapeutic applications.
