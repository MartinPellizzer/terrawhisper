Cimicifuga nanchuanensis belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta. This plant is a specific representative of the family Ranunculaceae, falling under the genus Cimicifuga, with its unique biological identity defined by the species designation nanchuanensis.
