Pterocarpus santalinus

Red sandalwood

Pterocarpus santalinus, commonly known as the Santa Linus tree, is a significant medicinal plant native to specific tropical regions. Renowned for its diverse therapeutic properties, this species is frequently utilized in traditional medicine to treat various ailments, offering valuable bioactive compounds that contribute to modern pharmacological research and herbal treatments.
