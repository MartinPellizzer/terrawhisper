Red sandalwood, scientifically known as Pterocarpus santalinus, is a highly prized medicinal plant endemic to the Eastern Ghats of India. Renowned for its deep red heartwood, it possesses potent anti-inflammatory, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. Traditionally used in Ayurveda, it serves as a vital component in various therapeutic formulations.
