Populus deltoides

Eastern cottonwood

Populus deltoides, commonly known as the Eastern Cottonwood, is a majestic deciduous tree native to North America. While primarily valued for its rapid growth and ecological importance, its bark and sap have historically been utilized in traditional medicine for their soothing properties and potential anti-inflammatory effects in various herbal remedies.

  • Scientific name: Populus deltoides
  • Family: Salicaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Malpighiales, it is further categorized into the family Salicaceae. Its specific taxonomic designation is within the genus Populus, known as Populus deltoides.
