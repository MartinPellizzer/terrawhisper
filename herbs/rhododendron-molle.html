Rhododendron molle

Rhododendron molle, commonly known as the soft rhododendron, is a resilient deciduous shrub native to East Asia. Renowned for its delicate, bell-shaped flowers, it holds significant ethnobotanical value. Traditionally used in various folk medicines, this plant is valued for its unique chemical properties and striking ornamental beauty.

  • Scientific name: Rhododendron molle
  • Family: Ericaceae
  • Native range: Asia-Temperate

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Ericales, it is situated in the family Ericaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Rhododendron.
