Cydonia oblonga

Cydonia oblonga, commonly known as the quince, is a deciduous shrub celebrated for its aromatic, golden fruits. Beyond its culinary appeal, this plant holds significant medicinal value in traditional practices, utilized for its astringent and anti-inflammatory properties to support digestive health and soothe various respiratory ailments effectively.

  • Scientific name: Cydonia oblonga
  • Family: Rosaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Rosalales, it is further categorized into the family Rosaceae. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is defined by the genus Cydonia.
