Quince, scientifically known as Cydonia oblonga, is a deciduous shrub celebrated for its fragrant blossoms and aromatic, golden fruit. Beyond its culinary uses, this medicinal plant offers therapeutic benefits, traditionally utilized for its digestive properties and astringent qualities, making it a valuable addition to holistic wellness and natural remedies.
