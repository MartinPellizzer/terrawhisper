Helleborus cyclophyllus

Helleborus cyclophyllus, commonly known as the Round-leaved Hellebore, is a resilient perennial prized in traditional herbalism. Characterized by its distinctive foliage, this plant contains potent bioactive compounds. While historically used for various therapeutic purposes, it requires careful handling due to its natural toxicity, demanding expert botanical knowledge.
