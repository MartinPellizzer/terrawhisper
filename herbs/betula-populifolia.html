Betula populifolia, scientifically known as Betula populifolia, is the American white birch, a resilient deciduous tree native to North America. Renowned for its distinctive peeling bark, it offers significant ethnobotanical value. Historically, various parts of the tree have been utilized in traditional medicine for their soothing, therapeutic properties.
