Piper aduncum

Piper aduncum, commonly known as peppertree or wild pepper, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to tropical regions. Renowned for its potent antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, it is widely utilized in traditional medicine to treat digestive ailments, skin infections, and respiratory issues, offering significant therapeutic potential for holistic healing.

  • Scientific name: Piper aduncum
  • Family: Piperaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Magnoliidae. Within the order Piperales, it is classified under the family Piperaceae. It is further identified by its genus, Piper, and its specific species name, Piper aduncum.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a broad geographical distribution across several distinct oceanic and tropical regions. Within the Malesia zone, it is commonly found in Malaya and the islands of Jawa. Its presence extends further into the Lesser Sunda Islands and even reaches the remote Christmas Island. Additionally, the species can be located in the Middle Atlantic Ocean on Ascension. These diverse locations highlight the wide environmental adaptability of Piper aduncum.
