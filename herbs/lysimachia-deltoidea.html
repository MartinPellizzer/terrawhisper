What's the taxonomical classification of Lysimachia deltoidea?

Lysimachia deltoidea belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta. Within the class Equisetopsida and the subclass Magnoliidae, it is placed in the order Ericales. The plant is a member of the family Primulaceae, falling under the genus Lysimachia, and is specifically identified by its species name, deltoidea.

What are the morphological characteristics of this plant?

Lysimachia deltoidea has opposite, lanceolate to ovate leaves that are entire in margin and sessile or short-petioled along an upright, pubescent stem. Its inflorescence consists of small, yellow, five-petaled flowers that emerge from the leaf axils. These blossoms are characterized by a central cluster of stamens and a somewhat spreading corolla shape.
