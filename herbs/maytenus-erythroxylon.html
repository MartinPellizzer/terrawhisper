Maytenus erythroxylon

Maytenus erythroxylon, commonly known as the "Pau-ferro" or "Ironwood," is a versatile medicinal plant native to South American forests. Renowned for its potent antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, it is traditionally utilized in folk medicine to treat skin conditions, digestive ailments, and various inflammatory processes throughout the tropical regions.

  • Scientific name: Maytenus erythroxylon
  • Family: Celastraceae
  • Native range: Southern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae within the phylum Streptophyta and is classified under the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Celastrales, it is further categorized into the family Celaestraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Maytenus.
