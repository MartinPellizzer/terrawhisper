Tree wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia arborescens, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in Ayurvedic medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation.
