Artemisia arborescens

Tree wormwood

Artemisia arborescens, commonly known as the Tree Wormwood, is a perennial shrub native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its potent medicinal properties, this plant contains diverse bioactive compounds. It is traditionally utilized in Ayurvedic medicine to treat ailments ranging from fever and digestive issues to inflammatory conditions and skin disorders.

  • Scientific name: Artemisia arborescens
  • Family: Asteraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it falls under the order Asterales and is a member of the Asteraceae family. Finally, its specific taxonomic placement is within the genus Artemisia.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated throughout Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing in the Balearic Islands, which are part of the Baleares archipelago. The species is also present on the island of Corsica and across the mainland of France. Additionally, its range extends to the coastal regions of Portugal. Finally, the plant is documented inhabiting the territory of Sardegna.
