Tree wormwood, scientifically known as Artemisia arborescens, is a versatile medicinal shrub native to the Himalayan regions. Renowned for its potent bioactive compounds, it is traditionally used in Ayurvedic medicine to treat respiratory ailments, digestive issues, and inflammation. This hardy plant offers significant therapeutic potential in modern pharmacology.

Distribution

This plant exhibits a specific geographical distribution concentrated throughout Southwestern Europe. It can be found growing in the Balearic Islands, which are part of the Baleares archipelago. The species is also present on the island of Corsica and across the mainland of France. Additionally, its range extends to the coastal regions of Portugal. Finally, the plant is documented inhabiting the territory of Sardegna.

Plant Parts

This plant, Artemisia arborescens, is valued for its therapeutic properties, which are primarily derived from its chemical constituents found in specific sections of its anatomy. The most common source for medicinal extraction is the ground aerial parts, which include the leaves and stems that grow above the soil surface. These parts are rich in bioactive compounds, most notably the essential oil, which contains the concentrated aromatic and medicinal volatile components responsible for the plant's pharmacological effects.
