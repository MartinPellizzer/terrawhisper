Distribution

This plant, known scientifically as Aconitum genuiculatum, possesses a very specific and limited range of occurrence. Its natural habitat is primarily concentrated within the unique ecosystems of East Asia. Specifically, the species is indigenous to the South-Central region of China. Within these mountainous or hilly terrains, it thrives in the local environmental conditions. Consequently, its geographical distribution is characterized by this singular regional focus.

Plant Parts

This plant, Aconitum geniculatum, is characterized by its potent medicinal properties, which are primarily concentrated in the root. This underground organ serves as the primary site for the accumulation of bioactive alkaloids, which are the chemical compounds responsible for the plant's pharmacological effects. In traditional herbal medicine, the processed root is the part most frequently utilized, though it requires careful preparation due to its high toxicity.
