Ligusticum porteri

Ligusticum porteri, commonly known as Porter's Lovage, is a perennial herb native to specific mountainous regions. Renowned for its aromatic properties, this medicinal plant contains essential oils believed to support digestive health and reduce inflammation. Its unique phytochemical profile makes it a valuable subject in traditional herbal medicine studies.

  • Scientific name: Ligusticum porteri
  • Family: Apiaceae
  • Native range: Northern America

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida.
