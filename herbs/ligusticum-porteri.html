Osha, scientifically known as Ligusticum porteri, is a perennial herb native to the mountain ranges of the American Southwest. Highly revered in traditional Native American medicine, this aromatic plant is often called "Beargrass" due to its historical use by bears. It is widely valued for its potent antimicrobial properties.
