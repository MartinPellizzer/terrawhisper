, scientifically known as Huperzia saururus, is a perennial clubmoss native to East Asia. Renowned in traditional medicine, this ancient lycopod contains unique alkaloids, most notably huperzine A. These bioactive compounds are widely studied for their potent acetylcholinesterase inhibitory effects, offering significant potential in treating cognitive decline and Alzheimer's.
