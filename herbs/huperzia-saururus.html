Huperzia saururus

Huperzia saururus, commonly known as the creeping clubmoss, is a perennial lycophyte native to East Asia. Valued in traditional medicine, this ancient plant contains bioactive alkaloids that exhibit significant neuroprotective properties. It is primarily studied for its potential to enhance cognitive function and treat various neurological disorders.

  • Scientific name: Huperzia saururus
  • Family: Lycopodiaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and the phylum Streptophyta, falling under the class Equisetopsida and subclass Lycopodiidae. Within the order Lycopodiales and family Lycopodiaceae, it is specifically classified under the genus Huperzia. As the species Huperzia saururus, it represents a distinct lineage within this botanical hierarchy.
