Corydalis solida

Spring fumewort

Corydalis solida, commonly known as fumewort, is a perennial herb valued in traditional medicine for its bioactive alkaloids. Native to Europe, this plant features delicate, lobed leaves and pale violet flowers. It is primarily utilized for its sedative and analgesic properties, offering significant potential in treating various neurological conditions.

  • Scientific name: Corydalis solida
  • Family: Papaveraceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta and class Equisetopsida. Within the subclass Magnoliidae, it is ordered under Ranunculales and sits in the family Papaveraceae. Finally, it is identified by the genus Corydalis.

Common Names

This plant, known scientifically as Corydalis solida, is referred to by various common names depending on the language and region. In German, it is called Gefingerter Lerchensporn, while in Polish, it is known as Kokorycz pełna. French speakers call it Corydale à bulbe plein, and in Frisian, it is referred to as Helmblom. Additionally, in Lithuanian, the plant is identified as Paprastasis rūtenis.
