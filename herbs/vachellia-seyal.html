Vachellia seyal

Vacchellia seyal, commonly known as the African milk tree, is a succulent shrub native to East Africa. Renowned for its milky latex, this resilient plant is deeply embedded in traditional medicine. It is widely utilized to treat various ailments, including skin infections, inflammatory conditions, and various digestive disturbances.

  • Scientific name: Vachellia seyal
  • Family: Fabaceae
  • Native range: Africa

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified under the phylum Streptophyta within the class Equisetopsida.
