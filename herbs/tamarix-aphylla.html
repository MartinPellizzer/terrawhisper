Tamarix aphylla

Tamarisk

Tamarix aphylla, commonly known as the Aphyllous Tamarisk, is a resilient, leafless shrub adapted to arid and saline environments. Renowned for its drought tolerance, this medicinal plant is utilized in traditional practices for its antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties, offering significant therapeutic potential in treating various skin and systemic conditions.

  • Scientific name: Tamarix aphylla
  • Family: Tamaricaceae
  • Native range: Europe

Taxonomical Classification

This plant belongs to the kingdom Plantae and is classified within the phylum Streptophyta and the class Equisetopsida. As a member of the subclass Magnoliidae and the order Caryophyllales, it is placed in the family Tamaricaceae. Finally, it is categorized under the genus Tamarix.

Distribution

Its range extends significantly into Northern Africa, where it can be found in Morocco and Algeria.
